# Chapter 81: Publicly Slandering Zhu Shuzhen

---

How could An Mingxuan fall behind? Youqi Jushi's ci was brought by them—how could he let everyone slander it?

---

He stood up: "Since Young Master Cao mentioned 'discussing ci based on ci itself,' let me set an example for everyone. Dongpo Jushi evaluated Wang Youcheng's landscape poetry as 'having painting within the poetry.' I'd like to borrow this to evaluate Youqi Jushi's ci—it truly is 'having painting within the ci.' However, this painting is not that painting. The painting in Wang Youcheng's poetry is often a complete landscape painting, while Youqi Jushi's painting consists of several scenes. The first scene shows lovers pausing, admiring together the beautiful sight of mist teasing dew; the second scene shows lovers hand in hand, yet encountering plum rain drizzle; the third scene shows them sheltering from rain, with no one around, the lovers intimate and close, both innocent and beautiful..."

---

Hearing "both innocent and beautiful," Chen Haoyu felt disdainful inwardly: "Innocent and beautiful, my ass! Beautiful your head! I think Zhu Shuzhen is just a woman with improper behavior! But, hehe, the more improper she is, the more mistakes I can catch. Heaven is truly helping me!"

---

After Chen Haoyu finished thinking, he continued listening to An Mingxuan's praise.

---

"The fourth scene shows the reluctant parting, the woman returning to her boudoir, recalling the feelings of their meeting. Such scenes full of artistic conception are pleasing to the eye and mind!"

---

Someone was moved: "Young Master An's analysis is wonderfully indescribable. When I return home, I'll definitely paint four pictures."

---

An Mingxuan cupped his hands: "I leave it to you!"

---

After An Mingxuan finished speaking, he returned to the table with Dongfang Chenze and Cao Ban and sat down.

---

Chen Haoyu whispered: "Why do you guys speak up for Zhu Shuzhen? I think she's just improper! She actually dares to publicly write about improper behavior in her ci for everyone to appreciate—she must have something wrong with her brain!"

---

Dongfang Chenze whispered a reminder: "You came in with the three of us, so you should be on our side. Besides, does Young Master Chen have the nerve to talk about Youqi Jushi? What about you harassing the female tea master at the tea house..."

---

Chen Haoyu said: "Stop! Stop! A true hero doesn't brag about past exploits! I'm a man—how can Zhu Shuzhen compare to me?"

---

Cao Ban emphasized: "Past exploits? That happened this very summer!"

---

Chen Haoyu stopped talking. He didn't dare show his usual arrogance either. After all, there was no one on his side at the poetry gathering. Better to keep a low profile and listen more, seeing if anyone else would criticize Zhu Shuzhen. He really hoped a few more would come forward.

---

Lord Sheng asked: "I wonder what Zixiao's insights are?"

---

When Youqi Jushi's ci was hung up, Lin Zixiao had already seen that Youqi Jushi had a new romance. She had just divorced his nephew Lin Jue, and was already touring the lake with another man—showing that during her marriage, her heart already belonged to someone else.

---

The more Lin Zixiao thought about it, the more dissatisfied he became. But he couldn't voice his dissatisfaction. On one hand, he truly liked Youqi Jushi's ci; on the other hand, he had to give face to the prefect.

---

Since Lord Sheng mentioned him, Lin Zixiao had to speak. He stood up: "Since we're discussing ci based on ci itself, let me share my humble views. Youqi Jushi's ci often excels in the word 'delicacy,' which is inseparable from her being a woman. 'Annoyed by mist, teased by dew' expresses the complex feelings of a young girl in spring, complaining that heaven doesn't provide fair weather, yet immersed in the beautiful sentiments of a rendezvous. 'A moment of plum rain drizzle'—in terms of the ci itself—establishes the time and also describes the weather condition, further filling the ci with artistic beauty. One can imagine the complex feelings of anxiety and joy when this young couple encounters rain, and also imagine the scene of them finding shelter, full of youthful vitality. Reading it, I can't help but recall my own past times."

---

Lin Zixiao didn't want to continue speaking, only because he didn't want to touch upon the line "sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed." Youqi Jushi married into the Lin family, unwilling to consummate the marriage with his nephew, and upon divorce, immediately fell into another man's arms—how could he have the mood to touch upon the following lines? He sat back down and continued drinking tea.

---

Seeing this situation, Chen Haoyu worried that no one wanted to criticize Zhu Shuzhen. Someone had to take the lead. He didn't understand poetry and ci, but criticizing people—who doesn't know how to do that! So he stood up and walked to the middle.

---

"Everyone, let me say a few words too. My father often taught my sister, 'A woman's virtue need not be exceptional in talent'—meaning a woman doesn't need to have extraordinary talent..."

---

Cao Ban sighed: "Chen Haoyu actually understands a line from Admonitions for Women—that's not easy!"

---

An Mingxuan said: "He already said it was from listening to his father. His father educates his sister all day long. Hearing it so much, it'd be strange if he didn't remember."

---

Cao Ban said: "That's true too."

---

Dongfang Chenze reminded: "You two stop talking. It looks like he's going to criticize Youqi Jushi."

---

The two also felt something was off and listened carefully.

---

"Youqi Jushi having talent already violates what Admonitions for Women says. She even displays her talent in poetry and ci—that's even more wrong! She even writes better than the talented gentlemen here—that's even more wrong! If she writes, then let her write, but she writes about matters that violate feminine virtue—that's even more wrong! 'Sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed'—tsk tsk tsk, this scene, how indecent it must be!" Chen Haoyu walked in front of the child. "How old are you?"

---

The child replied: "Nine years old."

---

Chen Haoyu continued: "Good boy, go back. This place isn't suitable for you."

---

The child insisted: "Why isn't it suitable? I'm very good at appreciating poetry and ci. Great-grandfather invited me."

---

Chen Haoyu asked: "Who is your great-grandfather?"

---

The child pointed at Lord Sheng.

---

Lord Sheng spoke: "If Young Master Chen has finished speaking, please sit to the side."

---

"I haven't finished yet!" How could Chen Haoyu let go of such a good opportunity to criticize Zhu Shuzhen? Worried others would interrupt, he quickly said: "Such a young child, coming to the poetry gathering full of passion for poetry and ci, yet being corrupted by Zhu Shuzhen's 'sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed'—the young and innocent heart being hurt is truly pitiful."

---

Chen Haoyu pretended to be the pitiful child, raising his fist: "I propose we all together—bring down Zhu Shuzhen! Bring down Zhu Shuzhen!"

---

Everyone present stared wide-eyed at Chen Haoyu, finding him very strange.

---

The child's somewhat young voice came: "Brother, I haven't been corrupted. Youqi Jushi said 'sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed' is very normal. I also often sleep in my clothes in Great-grandfather's arms."

---

Chen Haoyu explained: "Child, you sleeping in your great-grandfather's arms has no problem at all. Zhu Shuzhen is a woman. Sleeping in a man's arms is immoral."

---

The child said again: "Brother, I'm a boy, and Qingqing is a girl. Yesterday I played with her, and she even fell asleep on my lap. Is she immoral too? But I don't feel that way at all."

---

Chen Haoyu said: "You're children, so it doesn't matter."

---

"Why must it matter when Youqi Jushi is an adult? And why must it be measured by morality and immorality?" Faced with the child's question, Chen Haoyu couldn't answer. With nothing more to say, he could only return to his seat.

---

Dongfang Chenze said: "Young Master Chen, you're not welcome at our table."

---

Chen Haoyu had publicly slandered Zhu Shuzhen and thought of a way to get revenge on her. In a great mood, he didn't want to argue with Dongfang Chenze and the others: "I've had enough fun too. I'm heading home. You all continue." With that, he left the poetry gathering.

---

The three listened to others appreciate Youqi Jushi's new ci, then followed along appreciating works recommended by others, before leaving satisfied.

---

When parting outside the pleasure quarters, Dongfang Chenze worriedly said: "Today everyone appreciating Youqi Jushi's ci deliberately avoided 'sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed.' I'm really worried the three of us did something bad. Once this ci spreads, I'm afraid it will ruin Youqi Jushi's reputation."

---

Cao Ban said: "I'm worried too."

---

An Mingxuan proposed: "Why don't we find Youqi Jushi another day, explain today's poetry gathering to her, let her be mentally prepared, and apologize to her while we're at it."

---

Dongfang Chenze nodded in agreement: "It's settled then. Tomorrow we'll tell Brother Qiao and invite Youqi Jushi to the Qiao family's Youlin Villa. When the two of them meet again and are in a good mood, Youqi Jushi won't blame us too much."

---

After the three finished discussing, they took their carriages and returned home separately.
