第251章 仪王就职司理院
## Chapter 251: Prince Yi Takes Office at the Judicial Office

之前临安的司理参军将朱晞颜巴结得甚好，一直想给他升职，苦于没有好的借口。仪王看上司理参军的活儿，朱晞颜再高兴不过，他可光明正大把人调走。
朱晞颜也仔细思量过，以后狱讼之事他一概不过问，仪王想怎么着都行，谁让现在的临安城仪王最大。贵人别没事挑他的毛病便万事大吉，他头上的乌纱帽也能戴得长久些。
白风驾着马车来到司理院，看到有一群人队列整齐，站在门口等待。他先跳下马车，将步梯放好：“王爷，到了。”
申简辰从马车里出来，踩着步梯走下。
那群人见状，上前行礼。
一身儒生装扮，外形文雅的中年汉子带头说：“我等在此恭候贵人多时，仪王能来，司理院蓬荜生辉。”
申简辰瞧瞧眼前之人：“你们是？”
“见到贵人太过激动，竟然忘记介绍。”儒生装扮的中年汉子说，“小人是司理院的师爷，名唤韩谷；这位是温都头温信，后面全是司理院的差役。”
申简辰想起《水浒传》中的武松也曾担任过都头，不由对眼前的温都头产生兴趣：“不知温都头功夫如何？”
温都头回道：“回仪王，尚可。”
申简辰想试试对方的身手，待温信话音刚落，迅速打出一拳。温信反应敏捷，头向一边侧去，顺利躲开。
The previous Judicial Investigator of Lin'an had flattered Zhu Xiyan so well that the Prefect had long been itching to promote him, lacking only a clean excuse. Prince Yi taking a fancy to the post was a gift from heaven—Zhu Xiyan could transfer his protégé right out in the open. And he had already thought the rest through: from now on, he wouldn't poke his nose into a single criminal case. Prince Yi could run the courts however he liked—he was the biggest name in Lin'an now, after all. So long as the prince didn't go hunting for faults in him, everything was golden and his official's hat would sit safe on his head.

其他人见二人动起拳头，都向一旁闪开，将地方让给两位切磋之人。白风最喜看王爷与人打斗，在一旁津津有味地瞧着。
When Bai Feng drove the carriage up to the Judicial Office, a neat line of staff stood waiting outside the gates. Bai Feng hopped down and set out the footstool. "We're here, Your Highness."

很快温信由被动转为主动，以极快的速度挥出右拳。申简辰身体灵活地旋转，避开的同时使出一记勾拳打向温信的下巴，温信侧身躲避。两人你来我往，拳影交错，每一拳都蕴含着力量。最终申简辰使出一招连环拳，对方终于招架不住，连连后退。
Shen Jianchen stepped down from the carriage, and the crowd surged forward to bow.

再说温信也不敢赢贵人，于是拱手认输：“仪王拳法精妙，在下佩服佩服。”
A refined middle-aged man dressed like a scholar stepped ahead as spokesman. "We have been awaiting Your Highness's arrival. Your presence brings boundless luster to our humble Judicial Office!"

申简辰收起拳头：“温都头的拳法也不赖，看来能成为都头的人都不简单，不知温都头酒量如何？”
Shen Jianchen looked them over. "And you are?"

温信说：“不瞒仪王，在下的酒量至今未逢敌手。”
"Forgive me—so thrilled to meet Your Highness I forgot our introductions!" the scholar said. "I am Han Gu, clerk to the Judicial Office. Beside me is Chief Constable Wen Xin, and behind us are all the constables of the office."

申简辰说：“甚好！有时间你我斗斗酒，痛饮一回！”
Remembering Wu Song serving as a chief constable in *Water Margin*, Shen Jianchen eyed Wen Xin with sudden curiosity. "How are your martial arts, Chief Constable Wen?"

大家都豪爽地笑起来，气氛变得轻松许多。
"Passable, Your Highness," Wen Xin answered.

韩谷本以为来了位需供着的大神，却没想到如此亲民，拳法也厉害。
Wanting to test his mettle, Shen Jianchen threw a lightning punch the instant the words left Wen Xin's mouth. Wen Xin reacted in a blink, snapping his head aside to slip the blow.

总不能让贵人一直待在外面，韩谷赶快请仪王进入司理院堂中歇息，再将公务详情一一说明。
Everyone else scrambled back to clear a ring for the spar, while Bai Feng—who loved nothing more than watching his master trade blows—folded his arms to enjoy the show.

白风叫两名差役帮忙搬东西，住处早已安排好，温信带他们直接朝府衙后面拐去。
Wen Xin flipped from defense to offense, whipping a blistering right fist forward. Shen Jianchen spun inside the reach, ducked clear, and fired an uppercut toward Wen Xin's jaw; Wen Xin twisted clear in turn. Fists blurred back and forth, every strike packed with solid force, until Shen Jianchen unleashed a rapid chain of punches that finally forced Wen Xin staggering backward.

后面有一院子，主要供司理参军居住。挨着院子的东面是都头和师爷的住处，西边则是差役们群居之处。
Not that Wen Xin would have dared beat a prince anyway. He cupped his hands in concession. "Your Highness's boxing is extraordinary! I concede in total admiration."

司理院的人亦可选择回家住，只需每日按时前来点卯，尽心尽力办好差事即可。不过一旦有案情，往往没日没夜，大家渐渐形成大部分时间住在司理院的习惯。
Shen Jianchen lowered his fists. "Your own fists aren't half bad either, Chief Constable. No wonder you made chief constable. How well do you hold your liquor?"

首要需解决吃饭的问题，司理院也安置有后厨，安排专人负责采买做饭，做出来的饭食自然比不上幽林别居厨子的手艺，更无法与临安各大酒楼的名厨相比较，好在家常饭的味道较浓厚，吃到肚内也舒服。
"If Your Highness will forgive my boasting, I've yet to meet my match at the wine table."

申简辰在堂内坐好。
"Splendid! When we get a spare evening, you and I will drink till we drop!"

师爷韩谷不敢坐下，站着说道：“仪王殿下替官家体察民情，我等甚为感动。昨日知府大人派人前来叮嘱，往后临安刑狱诉讼之事不必上报，一切由仪王您裁夺。司理院从未有过此等殊荣，都是托殿下的福。”
Hearty laughter rippled through the courtyard, melting the stiffness in a heartbeat. Han Gu had braced himself for a haughty royal deity who had to be waited on hand and foot; instead, here was a down-to-earth prince with wicked fists.

对方一阵夸赞，搞得申简辰甚是不自在：“韩师爷还是坐下给我说说司理参军日常的事务。”
Mindful not to keep the prince standing outside, Han Gu hurried to invite him into the main hall for a rest and a briefing on official duties. Bai Feng enlisted two constables to haul in the trunks, and Wen Xin showed them straight to the rear quarters already prepared for the prince.

韩谷依然不敢坐下，生平第一次见身份如此高贵之人，总担心哪里做得不对：“小的还是站着回话心里舒坦些。”
Behind the court hall lay a private courtyard reserved for the Judicial Investigator. Immediately to its east were the quarters for the chief constable and clerk, and to the west the barracks for the constables. Staff were free to sleep at home so long as they made morning roll call and did their jobs, though active cases often meant working day and night, so most ended up living right at the Judicial Office. A back kitchen with a dedicated buyer and cook handled meals—nothing like the chef at Youlin Retreat or Lin'an's grand taverns, of course, yet the hearty home-style cooking sat warm and satisfying in the belly.

申简辰不再强求，认真听韩师爷说些什么。
Shen Jianchen took his seat in the main hall. Clerk Han dared not sit, staying on his feet as he spoke. "We are deeply moved that Your Highness would step down to look into the people's hardships on His Majesty's behalf. Yesterday the Prefect sent word that from now on, criminal cases and lawsuits in Lin'an need not be referred upward—everything rests solely on Your Highness's judgment. The Judicial Office has never known such an honor, all thanks to Your Highness."

“参军平日要忙的事不少。首先需审理临安牢狱里的犯人，将陈年冤案理一理，各类卷宗殿下每日必然要抽时间阅览；其次新的案子也需受理，老百姓时常会有人前来报案，司理院只有受理的份儿，一旦接下便需帮百姓排忧解难。若受理的案件大些，面上还有光，可有时老百姓邻里、夫妻之间闹些小矛盾也会来报官，直接拉低我们的档次，不受理还不行。”
The deluge of flattery made Shen Jianchen squirm. "Sit down, Clerk Han, and walk me through the Judicial Investigator's day-to-day work."

申简辰笑道：“老百姓将朝廷官员称为‘父母官’，不就是希望官员为百姓做主？百姓之事无小事，哪怕夫妻、邻里之间的纠纷依然是大事。”
Han Gu stayed rooted on his feet—his first time ever dealing with someone of such lofty blood, and he was terrified of putting a foot wrong. "I feel far more at ease speaking on my feet, Your Highness."

“仪王教训得对，小的谨记在心。”
Shen Jianchen stopped pressing and leaned in to listen.

“还有呢？”
"An Investigator has plenty on his plate," Han Gu began. "First, there are the prisoners already in Lin'an's jail—old unresolved cases and appeals need sorting through, so Your Highness will have to set aside time each day to read through the dossiers. Second, new complaints arrive constantly. Whenever commoners come to file a case, the Judicial Office has no choice but to accept it and resolve their grievances. Big cases at least bring prestige, though half the time neighbors or husbands and wives show up squabbling over petty nonsense—drags down the dignity of the court, yet we still can't turn them away."

“参军还需时常到百姓中走动，替朝廷和临安官员做些慈善。”
Shen Jianchen chuckled. "The common folk call officials 'father-and-mother officials' because they expect us to stand up for them. Nothing that weighs on the people is petty—even domestic or neighborly disputes matter."

申简辰心想，昏君和腐 败的官员竟然也会做慈善？他的历史知识有限，穿越前的历史课上老师也并未提及。
"Your Highness speaks true wisdom. I will take it to heart."

“慈善？是官家允许的？”
"What else?"

韩谷诧异，眼前之人不是官家的儿子吗？怎么连官家重视慈善之事都不知？又不敢多问，只得费些口舌解释一番。
"The Investigator must also go out among the people now and then to oversee charity work on behalf of the court and Lin'an's officials."

“崇宁年间官家便下旨命各地建居养院、安济坊、漏泽园，目的在于让鳏寡孤独者，有居养之法；疾而无医者，有安济之所；贫而不葬者，有安葬之地。官家重视慈善，一直施行至今，各地官员无不尽心竭力，因为官家将此事作为考核官员升迁的重要依据。”
Charity? Shen Jianchen blinked. That incompetent emperor and his corrupt mandarins actually ran public charities? His history classes back in the modern world had never mentioned that. "Charity? Ordered by His Majesty?"

申简辰心想：“昏君能有这么好？”紧接着问：“如此重大之事为何知府大人不亲力亲为，反而交给小小的司理参军？”
Han Gu looked startled—wasn't this man His Majesty's own son? How could he not know about His Majesty's prized welfare system? Still, he dared not question him and launched into a patient explanation.

韩谷犹豫着，不敢开口。
"Back during the Chongning reign, His Majesty decreed that every prefecture establish three institutions: Juyang Yuan—poorhouses to shelter the widowed, orphaned, and destitute; Anji Fang—charity infirmaries for the sick who cannot afford doctors; and Louze Yuan—pauper cemeteries for the poor who cannot afford burial. His Majesty puts great store by these charities and has kept them going ever since. Local officials bend over backward over them because His Majesty uses them as a key yardstick for promotions."

“放心大胆讲，本王既不会治你的罪，也不会讲出去，就当听听他人的故事。”
*Could that useless emperor actually be that decent?* Shen Jianchen wondered, before asking aloud, "If it's so important, why isn't the Prefect handling it himself instead of dumping it on a lowly Judicial Investigator?"

仪王如此说，韩谷轻松一些，再说面前的人虽是仪王，又是节度使，但也是司理参军，他有义务将实情告知，否则治他一个知情不报之罪，也担待不起。
Han Gu hesitated, lips pressed shut.

“按说各地的居养院、安济坊、漏泽园均是知州或知府亲自管辖，可临安的知府大人政绩已捞够，这等烫手的山芋还是赶快丢给别人好，您之前的司理参军之所以能被知府大人赏识，就是因为他将烫手的山芋处理得好。”
"Speak freely," Shen Jianchen assured him. "I won't punish you, and not a word leaves this room. Pretend you're just telling me a story."

“烫手的山芋？怎么个说法？”
That loosened Han Gu's shoulders. Besides, the man in front of him wasn't just Prince Yi and Military Commissioner—he was now the Judicial Investigator himself. Keeping the truth from him could get Han Gu slapped with concealing official facts.

“一个居养院就需许多银子来养，何况再加上安济坊和漏泽园？朝廷每年拨下来的银两由上到下经过各人之手，到了地方所剩无几，总不能让知府大人自己掏腰包？他刚担任知府那会儿位置还未坐稳，除了需打通人脉，还需多多捞政绩，保住头上的乌纱帽，必然亲力亲为。殿下，您猜猜他用的银子从哪里来？”
"By rights, the Juyang Yuan, Anji Fang, and Louze Yuan fall under the Prefect's direct charge," Han Gu admitted. "Trouble is, Prefect Zhu has already racked up all the merit he needs, so he couldn't wait to offload such a hot potato. Your predecessor won the Prefect's favor precisely because he knew how to juggle that hot potato so neatly."

“除了朝廷分派下来所剩无几的部分，难道大部分让临安的官员一起承担？”
"A hot potato? Explain."

“殿下只猜对一半，一大半钱财由陈家承担，朱陈两家联姻的背后都是利益捆绑，陈家有钱帮知府大人稳住乌纱帽，知府大人利用职权帮陈家稳固临安首富的地位，因此临安一大半的银子都被陈家赚了。”
"Running a single Juyang Yuan burns through heaps of silver, let alone an Anji Fang and a Louze Yuan on top of it. The annual welfare funds the court sends down pass through layer after layer of sticky fingers; by the time anything reaches the prefecture, there's barely a scrap left. You don't expect the Prefect to dip into his own pocket, do you? Back when he first took office and still needed to secure his seat, build connections, and pile up political merit to keep his hat, he did run them hands-on. Care to guess where the silver came from, Your Highness?"

提到朱陈两家联姻，申简辰自然想到千里寻，难道她也无形中成为朱陈两家利益捆绑的纽带？还未听出“烫手的山芋”，申简辰无心多想，继续问：“这跟烫手的山芋有什么关系？”
"Aside from whatever crumbs trickled down from the court, did Lin'an's officials chip in together?"

“殿下别急，听我慢慢道来。知府大人靠陈家和依附他的官员捞够政绩，也稳住在临安的地位，也许是看开，或是累了，不想再往上爬，没必要继续捞政绩，干脆捞钱，上面拨下来的善款层层盘剥，到达临安没剩多少，但也不少，最后一个子儿也到不了居养院、安济坊和漏泽园。他便把这三处交给司理参军管理，意思再明显不过。”
"Half right, Your Highness. More than half the money came straight out of the Chen family's pockets. The marriage alliance between the Zhus and the Chens is all about mutual gain—the Chens have the silver to keep Prefect Zhu's hat firm on his head, and Prefect Zhu uses his office to keep the Chens perched atop Lin'an as its richest clan. That's why half the silver in Lin'an flows into Chen coffers."

“什么意思？”
Mention of the Zhu-Chen alliance sent Shen Jianchen's thoughts straight back to Qianli Xun. Had she unwittingly become just another link chaining the two families' interests together? Pushing the ache down, he asked, "And how does that tie into the hot potato?"

“就是别当回事。”
"Bear with me, Your Highness. Once Prefect Zhu piled up enough merit through the Chens and his clique and locked down his grip on Lin'an, he either saw through the game or grew tired of climbing. With no urge to seek higher office, he stopped chasing merit and started chasing silver instead. Whatever welfare money survives the journey down from the capital still adds up to a tidy sum—and not a single copper of it reaches the poorhouses, infirmaries, or cemeteries anymore. Handing all three over to the Judicial Investigator was his way of sending a clear message."

“难道朝廷不会派人来查？”
"Which is?"

“唉，查有什么用？听说知府大人朝中有人，无人敢查，即使有人来查，也不怕。”
"Don't bother taking them seriously."

“陈家难道也不再出善款？”
"Doesn't the court send inspectors to check?"

“这陈家倒也不错，善款继续出，只是没有以前那么多。知府大人都不管，陈家也没必要多费心。其实，要我说不管也好。”
"Ha—what good are inspections? Word is Prefect Zhu has powerful backers at court. Nobody dares audit him, and even if someone did, he wouldn't break a sweat."

“为何？”
"So the Chen family stopped donating too?"

“老百姓中流行着一个谚语：不养健儿，却养乞儿；不理好人，却理病人；不管活人，只管死尸。一针见血，指出官家设立居养院、安济坊、漏泽园的弊端。”
"To give the Chens their due, they still pitch in—just nowhere near as much as before. If the Prefect doesn't care, why should they work themselves ragged? Truth be told, Your Highness, leaving them alone might be for the best."

申简辰听后，颇为感慨，昏君做个好事都会成为坏事，怪只怪这个时代如同根部腐朽的大树，土里的养分已无法通过根部传遍整个树干。
"Why?"

“如此看来，这三个地方对知府大人来讲的确是烫手的山芋，不想管又不得不管，不愿花钱又不得不花钱，花了反而为社会滋生一大批蛀虫，不花又担心朝廷知道。你刚说之前的司理参军将烫手的山芋处理得好，不妨向本王说说他是如何做的？”
"There's a saying going around among the common folk: *Instead of feeding able soldiers, they feed beggars; instead of minding healthy folk, they mind the sick; instead of caring for the living, they care for corpses.* Hits the nail right on the head about how rotten His Majesty's Juyang Yuan, Anji Fang, and Louze Yuan have become."

“朝廷的善款不可能拨到司理院，参军每个月的俸禄也不多，更不可能自己掏腰包，司理院是个穷衙门，银子都不够自己用的，哪管得了那三处？还好有陈家。之前的司理参军想到一个好办法，将原来的居养院、安济坊和漏泽园里的人全部赶出去，该怎么样就怎样。最多由陈家出些银子设个粥棚和医病的棚子，那些个被救济之人只要有口粥吃，有个医病的人就行，死了也有个乱坟岗埋即可。上面一旦差人来查，知府大人会提前得到消息，以前的参军才会临时将那三个地方重新布置，再让人住进去，当然银子也是由陈家出。”
Shen Jianchen let out a heavy breath. Even when that inept emperor tried to do something good, it curdled into rot—because the whole era was a massive tree rotten to its roots, unable to carry a drop of nourishment up the trunk.

“难道老百姓不会乱说？”
"No wonder those three places turned into a hot potato for the Prefect," he murmured. "Doesn't want to manage them, yet can't drop them outright; hates spending money on them, yet spends it only to breed a swarm of parasites—and stops spending only to risk the court finding out. You said my predecessor handled the hot potato well. How did he do it?"

“老百姓哪敢？司理院的杀威棒厉害着呢。”
"None of the imperial funds ever make it to the Judicial Office," Han Gu said, "and the Investigator's own salary is meager—no way he could pay out of pocket. Our yamen is dirt-poor; we barely scrape by on our own expenses, let alone run three charities. Thank heaven for the Chens. The previous Investigator hit on a neat trick: he evicted everyone from the Juyang Yuan, Anji Fang, and Louze Yuan and let them fend for themselves. At most, the Chens funded a plain congee shed and a makeshift sick-tent—just enough gruel to keep the destitute breathing, a doctor to hand out herbs, and a potter's field to dump the dead. Whenever word leaked ahead of time that an imperial inspector was coming, the old Investigator would scramble to refurbish all three buildings and herd people back inside for show—on the Chen family's dime, naturally."

韩谷一番介绍，申简辰算是了解到一些关于临安官府的情况。他来担任司理参军一职，难道朱晞颜不怕他的腐败之举被发现？
"And the townsfolk don't blab?"

申简辰仔细想想，也就明白，朱晞颜有什么好怕的？昏君的政策看似爱民，实则漏洞百出，他申简辰即使以仪王的身份诟病政策不好，也怪不到朱晞颜的头上，最终只能与官家对着干。
"Who'd dare? The beating rods of the Judicial Office don't joke around."

哪怕不诟病政策，只查贪污一事，办了朱晞颜，再顺藤摸瓜，摸出来的也不知会有多少个腐烂的大瓜，也许最后一个还得摸到官家的头上。
Han Gu's briefing pulled the curtain right back on Lin'an's officialdom. And why hadn't Zhu Xiyan worried that Prince Yi might expose his corruption? Thinking it over, Shen Jianchen saw the answer plain as day. What did Zhu Xiyan have to fear? The emperor's own welfare decree looked benevolent on paper yet leaked like a sieve in practice. Even if Shen Jianchen criticized the system as Prince Yi, none of the mud would stick to Zhu Xiyan—it would only pit Shen Jianchen against His Majesty. And if he bypassed policy and went straight after embezzlement, pulling the thread on Zhu Xiyan would unravel an endless vine of rotten gourds stretching all the way to the Dragon Throne itself.

申简辰不是怕事，是不想在毫无意义的事情上浪费时间，他决定既然已接下司理参军一职，不如在老百姓中做些更有意义的事。
Shen Jianchen wasn't afraid of trouble; he just refused to burn his energy on a lost cause. Since he had taken the Investigator's chair, he would rather do real, tangible good down among ordinary people.

正想着，温信进来报告：“仪王，竹里村的村长前来报案。”
Just as that thought settled, Wen Xin stepped in to report. "Your Highness, the headman of Zhuli Village is here to report a crime."

听到有案情，申简辰兴致倍增，在桌案前坐正：“把人叫进来。”
A real case! Shen Jianchen's eyes brightened as he squared his shoulders behind the desk. "Bring him in."

韩谷赶紧坐在师爷的位置，准备记录案情。
Han Gu hurried to the clerk's desk and picked up his brush.

温信到门口向外喊道：“进来吧，参军有话问你。”
Wen Xin called out through the doorway, "Come on in—the Investigator wants to question you."

村长小心走进堂内，原来是千里寻住过的竹林中的那位村长，他曾经来过司理院，也见过以前参军。新参军看着面生，他不敢多问，一走进跪在地上：“参军在上，小的是竹里村的村长，一大早去忙，结果在离村子不远的溪水旁发现一具女尸，便赶过来报案。”
The village headman stepped in with wary steps—the very same headman from the bamboo grove where Qianli Xun had once stayed. He had visited the Judicial Office before and knew the old Investigator; seeing a young stranger behind the desk, he kept his curiosity to himself and dropped straight to his knees. "Your Honor, I am the headman of Zhuli Village. I went out to work first thing this morning and found a woman's corpse beside the stream not far from the village, so I rushed straight here to report it."

申简辰虽没做过断案之事，但这方面的剧追过不少，经验还是有一些：“可破坏现场？”
Shen Jianchen had never tried a real murder case, though he had binge-watched enough detective dramas in his old life to know the basics. "Has the crime scene been disturbed?"

村长回道：“毕竟是死了人，大家都不敢靠近，现场自然完好。”
"A dead body's bad luck, Your Honor—nobody dared go near it. The scene's untouched."

申简辰即刻下令：“韩师爷、温都头随我去现场，记得带上仵作。”
Shen Jianchen barked his orders at once. "Clerk Han, Chief Constable Wen, come with me to the scene—and bring the coroner!"
