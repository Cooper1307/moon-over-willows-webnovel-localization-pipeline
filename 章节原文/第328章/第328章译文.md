## Chapter 328: Kneeling Without Budging to Save Her Life

After two full days with Prince Yi missing, Haoyu and Bai Feng rushed to Xihua Gate—barred from the inner palace, leading Haoyu to drop onto his own knees outside the main palace gates in solidarity while Bai Feng waited till dark to vault the palace walls with warm water, pastries, and a cloak.

Chilled to the bone with numb knees and cracked lips, Shen Jianchen gulped down the warm water yet pushed back both pastries and cloak lest guards spot help and ruin his plea—and outside the gate, Haoyu stubbornly refused them too, sitting rigid through freezing night wind watched by morning court ministers.

By day three, hearing his thirteenth son swore to starve to death on the stones rather than rise without a pardon, Zhao Ji finally relented and summoned Liang Shicheng. Knowing a father wouldn't let an imperial prince die over a eunuch's pride, Liang Shicheng pivoted slickly: spare her life, lock her in prison for a few years—*and* since Mengrong had lost her wits, grant the poetess to Prince Yi as secondary consort!

Delighted to dress his clemency in literary flair, Zhao Ji laid down a clever test: if Zhu Shuzhen composed an astonishing **wordless ci lyric** without a single written character, he'd spare her life!

Stepping out to tell half-conscious Shen Jianchen her life was saved—watching the prince collapse flat on the flagstones—Liang Shicheng had guards haul him out to Bai Feng's waiting carriage, where battered, limping Haoyu shared ointment, water, pastries, and tearful relief as they rolled back to Prince Yi's Manor.

Meanwhile, inside her cell, handed brush and paper by two baffled eunuchs, Qianli Xun smiled—hearing how Prince Yi had knelt three brutal days for her—and effortlessly drew page-wide clusters of **circles and ink dots**, reviving Zhu Shuzhen's legendary *Circle Ci* to save her own neck.
