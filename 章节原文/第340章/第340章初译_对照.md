第340章 仪王临安传死讯
## Chapter 340: News of Death Reaches Lin’an

净光法师一番开解，申简辰没有了轻生的念头，他带着悲伤来到陈府外。
陈浩宇听下人来报仪王找他，人就站在门外，他带着愤怒和不安匆忙走出陈府，顾不上仪王一副落寞失魂的样子，上前就是一拳：“我听白风说我娘子在燕京被金人抓走，都怨你！谁让你把她带去燕京？”
申简辰并不还手，任由陈浩宇打，又是几拳落在他身上。
陈浩宇抓起仪王的衣领：“白风说你去救她，人呢？为何只有你一人回来？”
“她……死了。”
陈浩宇一把推开仪王：“你胡说！”
“她被葬在燕京城外，我亲眼见过……”
“你胡说！一定是你不想将她还给我，才骗我！”
“我没骗你，我比谁都希望她活着。”
陈浩宇见仪王双眼红肿，情绪低沉，原来的一张俊脸仿佛一下子苍老了许多，不得不相信他所说，举起的拳头再也打不下去，蹲在地上哭起来：“呜呜……！”
哭了一会儿，陈浩宇起身往回走，边走边说：“我回去准备一下，亲自去趟燕京，把娘子的尸骨带回临安，葬在陈家的墓地……”
“她已不是陈家人，你没有资格带回她的尸骨。”
陈浩宇停下脚步转过身，迅速上前，再次抓住仪王的衣领：“我是被圣旨所逼不得已解除婚姻，在我心里她永远是我娘子！”
“不管你心里怎么想，她已是我的人。”
“什么意思？难道你已经把圣旨的事告诉她？”
申简辰希望死后的千里寻只属于他一人：“是的，我已告诉她。她如释重负，对我说，因有婚姻，她不得不一再委屈跟着你，婚姻没了，终于可以和喜欢的人在一起。她是心甘情愿跟我去燕京，而不是回临安找你。”
“你骗我！告诉你，金军正在南下，很快会攻打汴梁，朝堂上那位还不知会如何，到时圣旨作废，她照样是我娘子！”
“无论官家如何，都晚了，因为……因为在燕京时，我已与她圆房……”
话还未说完，陈浩宇一拳打在仪王的脸上，本就因痛苦而沧桑的脸上又多出一处瘀青。
“你胡说！你胡说！”陈浩宇的声音近似歇斯底里。
“该说的话我已说，你比我幸运，起码她生前陪过你一段时日，而我与她单独相处的时间太短。你竟然还不知足？还要在她死后跟我抢？你不觉得我很可怜吗？”申简辰的眼角有泪水滚落下来。
陈浩宇被触动，不想再与仪王争论什么，转身像霜打的茄子一般向陈府内走。
到门口时，他停住脚步，背对着仪王说：“我不再跟你抢，抢又有何用？她又不会活过来。”
直到陈浩宇落寞的背影消失在眼前，申简辰才想到离开。该去哪里？他忽然觉得人生失去方向。以前有千里寻的地方便有人生，现在她不在，方向没了，前面的路不知该如何走？
汴梁的王府已变卖，他不会再回去，过不了多久，汴梁也会被金人霸占。想到幽林别居，虽在那里住过，可幽林别居依然不是他的家。
唯有竹屋可称作家，从萌生建造竹屋的想法时，那竹屋便是为他和千里寻建造的。
申简辰以徒步的方式向城外走，没有了心爱之人，他何必追赶时间？时间需慢慢消磨，消磨的过程可排遣掉内心空落落的不适感。
刚走出城外，一辆马车停在面前，乔文仙从车上下来。
“听说你回来，正准备去找你。”
“你怎知我回来？”
Stepping away from Master Jingguang’s counsel with suicide put behind him, Shen Jianchen walked heavy-hearted to the gates of Chen Manor.

“幽林别居的下人在街上看到你，说你往陈府方向去。我准备去陈府找你，还好没白跑，半路上遇见。”
Hearing Prince Yi waited outside, Chen Haoyu rushed out full of dread and rage. Barely glancing at the prince’s gaunt, haunted face, Haoyu swung a fist straight at him. “Bai Feng told me my wife was captured by Jin troops in Yanjing! It’s all your fault—who told you to take her there?!”

面对文渊，申简辰不想绷着，整个人松懈下来，上前抱住文渊，哭起来：“呜呜！文渊，她没了，我永远不可能再见到她，呜呜……！”
Shen Jianchen stood still and took blow after blow without raising a hand. Haoyu grabbed his lapels and shook him hard. “Bai Feng said you went to save her! Where is she? Why did you come back alone?!”

文渊一边安慰一边说：“看到你的样子我已猜出是这等结果，要是我当初不顾家人反对带她远走他乡也不会有今日之事。别难过，随我回幽林别居。”
“She… is dead.”

自仪王与仪王妃回汴梁后，乔文仙携带一妻一妾住进幽林别居。不久苗知瑜生下一个儿子，夏果生下一个女儿，两个孩子均两岁多，已经能够满地跑。
Haoyu shoved him backward. “Liar!”

乔家的长辈见儿子儿女双全甚是满意，更庆幸当年没有选择朱家姑娘，否则今日跟着揪心的会是乔家。
“She lies buried outside Yanjing’s walls. I saw her grave with my own eyes.”

乔文仙不这么想，他的心里挂念着淑真，希望她一切顺利。
“You’re lying just to keep her from me!”

来到幽林别居，沐浴后，换上文渊准备的衣裳，简单用过饭后，整个人精神焕发许多，可申简辰的眼睛里依然写着“落寞”二字。
“I didn’t lie. Nobody wanted her alive more than I.”

乔文仙没有让苗知瑜和夏果以及孩子前来拜见仪王，他情绪不佳，看到自己儿女双全肯定会更难过，等缓过几日再说。
Seeing Prince Yi’s swollen red eyes, sunken cheeks, and face aged years overnight, Haoyu felt his raised fist go limp. Slowly crouching onto the flagstones, he buried his face and wept aloud.

两人在屋内的桌边对面而坐，一旁的火盆烧得很旺，屋里暖烘烘的。
After a long moment, Haoyu pushed himself up and turned back toward the gate. “I’ll pack right now and ride to Yanjing myself to bring my wife’s bones home to the Chen family graveyard.”

从燕京一路走来体验过的寒冷在这一刻已被暖意驱散。身体的寒冷是没了，心里的却还在。
“She belongs to the Chen family no more. You have no right to fetch her bones.”

乔文仙为仪王斟满酒酿，也为自己满上：“白风带邹嬷嬷回到临安时听他说，你把汴梁的王府和名下的财产全部变卖才凑够银两，唉！我自愧不如。”
Whirling back, Haoyu seized Shen Jianchen’s collar again. “I only dissolved our marriage under an Imperial Decree! In my heart, she is my wife forever!”

“没有她要钱财何用？”申简辰端起酒杯一饮而尽，“现在她没了，钱财对我来讲更是毫无意义。”
“No matter what’s in your heart, she became mine.”

“你我两年多没见，很多事情发生变化，朝廷眼看着大势已去，只怕天下要沦为金人的。”
“What do you mean? Did you tell her about the decree?!”

“金人没有资格夺得天下，最多与大宋分庭抗争，最终照样被灭，只可惜你我等不到那一日。”
Wanting Qianli Xun to belong solely to him even in death, Shen Jianchen lied straight to his face. “Yes. I told her. Relief washed over her—she confessed she had only suffered staying with you out of marital duty, and once free, she could finally be with the man she truly loved. She followed me to Yanjing of her own accord, never intending to come back to Lin’an for you.”

“你怎知？”
“You’re lying! Jin armies are marching south on Bianliang right now—who knows what becomes of the man on that throne? Once that decree turns into scrap paper, she’s my wife all over again!”

申简辰露出难得的笑容：“猜的。”
“No matter what becomes of His Majesty, it’s too late. In Yanjing… she and I had already consummated our union.”

乔文仙端起酒杯：“不管你猜的是对是错，反正猜到我心里，既然那一日你我等不到，不如现在与我干了这杯，提前为那一日到来庆贺。”
Before the final word fell, Haoyu cracked a fist across Shen Jianchen’s jaw, painting fresh purple over his weary face. “Liar! Liar!” Haoyu’s voice broke near hysteria.

申简辰为自己斟满，端起酒杯与文渊干了。
“I’ve said my piece,” Shen Jianchen whispered as tears spilled down his own cheeks. “You’re luckier than me—at least you had her beside you while she lived. My time alone with her was far too brief. Still not satisfied? Still fighting me for her even after death? Don’t you feel even a shred of pity for me?”

乔文仙放下酒杯问道：“往后你如何打算？”
Stung silent, Haoyu dropped his fists and trudged back through Chen Manor’s gates like frostbitten stalks. Stopping just across the threshold with his back turned, he muttered, “I won’t fight you anymore. What’s the use fighting when she can’t come back alive?”

申简辰沉默一会儿说：“我会回到属于我和她的竹屋，在那里了此残生。”
Only after Haoyu’s slumped back vanished inside did Shen Jianchen turn away—suddenly adrift with nowhere to go. His Bianliang manor was sold, Secluded Forest Villa belonged to another, and soon Bianliang might fall to Jin hands anyway. Only the bamboo house outside town felt like home—every bamboo beam built for him and Qianli Xun.

想起净光法师的话，又强调道：“我会以她的名义行善积德，当大家说起她时，仿佛她一直在我身边。”
Walking out past the city gates with nowhere to hurry, letting empty miles eat away the hollow ache inside, he spotted a carriage stopping ahead—and Qiao Wenxian stepped down.

乔文仙很感动：“算我一个好吗？”
“I heard you returned and was just coming to find you.”

“你已夺走她的初恋，这等事还要来跟我抢？”
“How did you know?”

“夺走她初恋的人不是我，是徐公子。”
“Servants from Secluded Forest Villa spotted you heading toward Chen Manor. I drove straight there and caught you on the road.”

“在我看来就是你。”
Before Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen dropped all pretense and collapsed onto his friend’s shoulder sobbing aloud. “Wenyuan… she’s gone! I’ll never see her again!”

“好吧，我不跟你抢。”
Clapping his shoulder gently, Wenxian blamed himself for not eloping with her back then, then guided the prince back to Secluded Forest Villa.

“现在多好，既没有人跟我抢，梦容也不会前来阻拦，我可以毫无顾忌跟她在一起，可她……”申简辰说着又流下泪水。
Since Prince Yi had returned to Bianliang two years ago, Wenxian had moved into the villa with his wife and concubine. Miao Zhiyu had borne him a son and Xiaguo a daughter—both now lively toddlers past two racing across the yards. Elder Qiaos couldn’t have been happier, thanking Heaven they hadn’t chosen Zhu Shuzhen and brought this heartbreak upon Qiao Manor—though Wenxian himself had prayed for her safety every day.

“仪王妃和喜兰的事我也听白风说起过，你要节哀。”
After a hot bath, fresh clothes, and supper, Shen Jianchen looked less haggard, though his eyes remained hollow. Knowing better than to parade his own happy toddlers before a grieving man, Wenxian kept his family tucked away for a few days and sat across a table beside a glowing brazier pouring warm fermented rice wine.

申简辰抬起头，忍住再次要流下的泪水：“等明年开春时，我会再去趟燕京，把她们的尸骨全部带回临安。”
Winter’s road-chill melted out of Shen Jianchen’s bones, leaving only the frost inside his chest.

乔文仙不愿仪王太过伤心，打算将话题扯到临安之事：“自从梁内侍将临安搞得乌烟瘴气后，东方诗会没举办过几次被新任的知府大人取缔。东方尘泽只能像从前一样私下搞些小型诗会。后来东方尘泽和安明轩各自成亲，有了家室后，办诗会的次数变得更少。加之政局不稳，人心惶惶，文人们都无心附庸风雅，临安诗坛再无往日气象。”
“When Bai Feng brought Matron Zou back, he told me you sold your Bianliang manor and everything in your name for ten million taels,” Wenxian sighed, filling both cups. “I couldn’t hold a candle to you.”

“没有了她，临安的诗会失去不少风采，又何必再搞？”
“Without her, what good was silver?” Shen Jianchen drained his cup in one swallow. “And now she’s gone, silver means even less.”

转移话题失败，乔文仙继续提及其他：“倒有件高兴事，今年中秋节曹班带叶安雅回到临安，两人的儿子已两岁多，看在孙子的份上，曹家人接纳了叶安雅。几位好友又能聚到一起，实乃人生乐事，改日我把大家约来陪你说说话。”
“Over two years apart, and everything has shifted. Our court looks spent—I fear the whole realm may fall to the Jin.”

“若中秋节那日把她从狱中接出，立刻送她回临安，也没有后面的事，都怪我贪恋与她单独在一起的日子。”
“The Jin aren’t fit to rule the whole realm,” Shen Jianchen said. “At best they’ll split the map with Song for a time, then meet ruin themselves. Shame neither of us will live to see that day.”

转移话题再次失败，乔文仙仍旧不放弃：“你知道吗？蒋爷的夫人去年生孩子时难产，蒋爷本想保住大人，他夫人不同意，硬要保住孩子。儿子是有了，夫人却撒手人寰，想来也挺可怜。”
“How can you be so sure?”

想到天目山之事，申简辰可怜不起来：“佛家讲善恶有报，还真没错。”
A faint, rare smile touched Shen Jianchen’s lips. “Just a guess.”

乔文仙以为转移话题成功，脸上刚露出笑容，却听仪王说：“她那么善良，处处为他人着想而委屈自己，为何落得如此下场？善恶有报又在哪里？”
“Right or wrong, you spoke straight to my heart!” Raising his cup, Wenxian toasted that distant day, and Shen Jianchen drank with him.

第三次转移话题失败，乔文仙收起笑容，又想到一个话题：“善恶有报还真有，林觉的妾室春金巧去年生了怪病，怎么都医不好，今年夏天离开人世。大家私底下议论，商妓出身本就卑贱，仗着出卖姿色被临安的贵妇参拜，结果无福消受，一命呜呼。”
Setting his cup down, Wenxian asked what he planned next.

申简辰批评道：“什么时候临安才子变成长舌妇？竟议论起别家的家长里短来？”
“Go back to the bamboo house built for her and me, and live out the rest of my days there,” Shen Jianchen answered softly, remembering Master Jingguang’s counsel. “I’ll do charity and build merit in her name, so whenever folks speak of her, it’ll feel like she’s still right beside me.”

“哪是我想议论，不过是因为你离开临安的时间长，给你说说两年多的变化。”
Moved, Wenxian asked, “Count me in?”

“林觉现在如何？”
“You already stole her first love. Trying to steal charity from me too?”

转移话题终于成功，乔文仙开玩笑道：“还有脸说我？你不也关心别人家的家长里短？”
“Young Master Xu took her first love, not me.”

“不说，我可走了。”申简辰装作起身的样子。
“As far as I’m concerned, it was you.”

乔文仙赶紧拦住：“别着急走，两年多没见，你我需多聊会儿。今日不许走，晚上住在幽林别居，明日我亲自送你回乡下的竹屋。”
“Fine, I won’t fight you over it.”

见仪王继续坐好，乔文仙说：“林觉最善于巴结上面的官员，诸军都统制的位置坐得相当安稳，还好本性不算坏，做事倒也知分寸。春金巧的死对他影响极大，不仅不再纳妾，也没去过青楼，安心跟白梅若过日子。白梅若给他生下一个女儿，跟我那两个孩子一般大。”
“Think how absurd life is,” Shen Jianchen sighed as fresh tears brimmed. “Nobody left to fight me now, Mengrong no longer there to block us, nothing standing in our way—and she’s gone.”

“真羡慕你们，都已为人父母，可我和她却……”
“Bai Feng told me about Princess Consort Yi and Xilan too. Accept my condolences.”

仪王又将话题扯了回去，乔文仙转移话题完全失败，他还是努力在脑中搜索新的话题。
Blinking tears back, Shen Jianchen promised himself that come spring, he would ride back to Yanjing and bring their bones home to Lin’an.

还未想出时，南双进来说：“仪王、公子，林都统前来拜见。”
Eager to pull him out of grief, Wenxian shifted to Lin’an gossip: after Liang Shicheng tainted the city, the new Prefect shut Dongfang’s poetry banquet down, leaving Dongfang Chenze holding rare quiet gatherings; then Chenze and An Mingxuan both married, political dread killed everyone’s appetite for poetry, and Lin’an’s literary circle lost all its old sparkle.

乔文仙说：“他肯定是听到你回来的消息才来拜见，要不要见？”
“Without her, Lin’an’s poetry world lost half its soul anyway,” Shen Jianchen murmured. “Why bother hosting?”

申简辰说：“见见又何妨？大家都是老相识，再说那会儿他跟着我也做过不少好事。”
Attempt one failed. Wenxian tried happier news: at Mid-Autumn, Cao Ban brought Ye Anya back to Lin’an with their two-year-old son, winning the Cao family’s acceptance for the grandson’s sake—and their old group could gather again soon.

乔文仙向南双说：“请林都统进来。”
“If I’d sent her straight back to Lin’an on Mid-Autumn when she walked out of prison, none of this would have happened,” Shen Jianchen choked. “I got greedy wanting her to myself.”

南双出去后，林觉掀开厚厚的帘子进来。外面冷，满面凉气，一进屋子热气扑面而来，冷热交替的过程中，林觉的脸颊和鼻头变得通红。
Attempt two failed. Wenxian dug deeper: Master Jiang’s wife died in childbirth last year insisting the midwives save her baby boy—pitiable in its own way.

林觉上前行礼：“拜见仪王殿下。”
“Buddhism calls it karma—good and evil both get repaid,” Shen Jianchen muttered coldly, recalling Mount Tianmu.

申简辰淡淡地说：“今日没有仪王，只有朋友，坐下喝两杯。”
Thinking he had succeeded at last, Wenxian broke into a smile—until Shen Jianchen added, “She stayed gentle and put everyone before herself, suffering every wrong. Why did she end up like this? Where was karma for her?”

林觉不敢，跪在地上：“下官今日前来当着仪王的面亲自忏悔，哪怕要了下官的命也在所不惜。”
Attempt three failed. Wiping away his smile, Wenxian tried one more piece: karma *did* strike—Lin Jue’s concubine Chun Jinqiao caught a strange sickness last year that no doctor could cure, and died this summer. People whispered a courtesan’s low birth couldn’t bear the blessing of Lin’an noblewomen bowing to her.

“何悔之有？”
“Since when did Lin’an’s young scholars turn into gossiping busybodies?” Shen Jianchen chided.

“若下官当日没有将她从陈府带走，就没有后面许多事，都是下官的错，要杀要剐任凭仪王处置。”
“Only catching you up on two years away!”

申简辰冷笑两声：“哼哼！若她不愿意，就凭你能轻易将她带走？你的命对我来讲一文不值，若你舍得性命，不如去汴梁，帮你的官家抵抗金人，死在战场总比死在我的手上更有价值。”
“How is Lin Jue doing?”

“仪王不说，下官也这么打算。下官已向知府大人请命，明日带兵开拔汴梁前去支援。”
Success at last! Wenxian grinned. “Calling me a busybody when you’re curious about other folks’ homes yourself?”

“既如此又何必假惺惺前来让我要了你的命？”
“Keep teasing and I’m walking out.”

“下官不过是为表达忏悔之意。”
Laughing him back into his chair to stay the night before riding to the bamboo house tomorrow, Wenxian told him Lin Jue kept his post as Commander-in-Chief of All Garrisons by currying favor smoothly, though he knew boundaries. Jinqiao’s death sobered him completely: no more brothels, no more concubines, living steadily with Bai Meiruo and their newborn daughter—the same age as Wenxian’s two kids.

“她从来没怪过你，起来吧，坐下说话。”
“Envious of all of you becoming fathers… while she and I…”

“谢仪王不杀之恩。”林觉起身，端把椅子，在桌边坐好。
Back to square one. Before Wenxian could hunt for a new topic, Nan Shuang stepped in announcing Commandant Lin at the gate. Since he’d heard Prince Yi was back and had done good work under him before, Shen Jianchen agreed to see him.

乔文仙为林觉放好一只酒杯，把酒斟满。
Pulling back the thick winter door curtain, Lin Jue entered with cheeks and nose bright red from outside cold meeting indoor brazier warmth. Dropping straight to his knees, he confessed his guilt.

三人一边聊往事，一边饮酒，直到天色暗下，仪王要休息，林觉才离开。
“No prince here today, just old acquaintances—sit and drink,” Shen Jianchen said flatly.

乔文仙吩咐南双伺候仪王，他则回到自己屋中。
Lin Jue refused to rise. “If I hadn’t marched to Chen Manor to take her away that day, none of what followed would have happened! Take my life if you wish!”

刚踏进门，被一妻一妾堵住。
“Your life isn’t worth a copper to me,” Shen Jianchen sneered. “If you’re so eager to die, march to Bianliang and fight the Jin for your Emperor. Dying on a battlefield beats dying by my hand.”

苗知瑜问：“仪王都回来了，幽栖居士为何没见人影？”
“Even without Your Highness saying so, I already petitioned the Prefect—I lead troops north to relieve Bianliang tomorrow!”

夏果问：“我听南双说小姐人没了，是不是真的？”
“Then spare me the hollow act begging for death.”

乔文仙埋怨道：“我刚把仪王哄好，你们又来。”
“I came only to ask forgiveness.”

“看来是真的。”夏果哭道，“呜呜，小姐是好人，为何好人不长命？”
“She never blamed you. Get up and sit down.”

苗知瑜比夏果好些，上前把夏果扶到椅子跟前坐好：“妹妹别难过，文渊不是还没给出答案？”
Thanking him for sparing his life, Lin Jue pulled up a chair while Wenxian poured wine, and the three drank over old days until dusk called them to rest.

夏果又充满希望：“到底如何？”
When Wenxian finally returned to his own room, Miao Zhiyu and Xiaguo cornered him at the door, demanding why Prince Yi had come back alone—was the rumor from Nan Shuang true?

乔文仙叹口气：“仪王说淑真和仪王妃被葬在汴京城外，明年春天会把她们的尸骨迁回临安。”
“I barely calmed Prince Yi down, and now you two start!” Wenxian groaned.

得到确切答复，夏果更加伤心：“小姐，你怎么就这么没了？呜呜……！”
Xiaguo burst into sobs over good people dying young while Zhiyu helped her into a chair hoping Wenxian would deny it—only for Wenxian to sigh out the truth that Shuzhen and Princess Consort Yi lay buried outside Yanjing until spring.

苗知瑜也流下几滴泪：“没了幽栖居士，从此后临安的诗坛再无生气。”
Xiaguo wailed uncontrollably; Zhiyu shed quiet tears for Lin’an’s dead poetry scene.

文渊发誓道：“从今日起我不会再参加诗会，也不再参加任何文人的活动，除非淑真死而复生。”
Standing straight, Wenxian swore a solemn oath: “From this day on, I will attend no more poetry banquets and no literary gatherings—unless Shuzhen rises from the dead.”

听到这话，两个女人都哭出声来，既为离世的人悲伤，亦为离世的人在文渊心中的地位而悲伤。
At that vow, both women wept harder still—grieving both for the dead poetess and for how deep she still stood in their husband’s heart.
