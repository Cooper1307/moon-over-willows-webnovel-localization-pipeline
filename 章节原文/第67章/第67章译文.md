# Chapter 67: Early Arrival and Unexpected Encounters

After arriving at West Lake, Qianli Xun headed straight for the Lake-viewing Pavilion, but didn't see Wenyuan anywhere. She had come too early. There was still one hour before the hour of wei. By Qianli Xun's familiar timekeeping method, there were still two hours.

A full two hours—how should she pass the time? She might as well take a walk around West Lake with Kuqiao. The pavilion where she and Wenyuan agreed to meet was close to Bai Causeway. She'd stroll along Bai Causeway, enjoying the scenery while killing time.

Kuqiao saw her young lady turn away from the Lake-viewing Pavilion and quickly caught up. She felt her young lady was very different today. Normally carefree and easygoing, today her walk showed a gentle grace—truly like her pre-amnesia self.

Kuqiao secretly rejoiced: "Young Master Qiao is really something. Without saying a word, he's brought the young lady back to who she was before losing her memory."

Qianli Xun stepped onto Bai Causeway, walking while admiring the weeping willows along West Lake's shore. Two lines of poetry came to mind, and she recited them aloud: "East of the lake, I never tire of wandering, along the white sand dyke where green willows cast their shade."

Kuqiao praised: "Young lady, you're amazing! After just a few steps, you can compose such beautiful poetry!"

Qianli Xun smiled gently: "How could I have written this? These are lines from 'Spring Journey by Qiantang Lake' by the Tang Dynasty poet Bai Juyi."

"It's normal for you to remember Xiangshan Jushi's poetry. It's said that Bai Causeway is related to him."

"You know quite a lot. That's impressive!"

Praised by her young lady, Kuqiao was delighted and became more talkative: "You told me all this before. I also know about Su Causeway—it was built by Dongpo Jushi of our dynasty. The scenery there is nice too. Why not visit Su Causeway after finishing Bai Causeway?"

"Better not. Walking the entire Bai Causeway would take at least an hour, and walking back another hour. I'm afraid Wenyuan would be anxious waiting. I've decided—I'll walk halfway along Bai Causeway, then turn back. I'll hide near the Lake-viewing Pavilion. I want to see with my own eyes how anxious Wenyuan gets waiting."

"That's a good idea, young lady. You got up early this morning, dressed up carefully to meet Young Master Qiao, skipped lunch just to see him, and came so early. You should definitely see with your own eyes whether he'll be anxious to see you too."

Qianli Xun turned her head and stared at Kuqiao. When had this girl become a tapeworm in her stomach, knowing her thoughts so clearly?

Kuqiao thought her young lady was going to scold her and quickly admitted her mistake: "I said something wrong. Please don't scold me."

Qianli Xun didn't scold Kuqiao. Instead, she laughed: "You said nothing wrong. Why would I scold you?"

"I thought you'd find me too talkative."

"You are too talkative, but I'm in a good mood today, so I won't hold it against you."

Qianli Xun continued admiring the beautiful scenery of West Lake. More captivating than the scenery were the couples passing by from time to time. On the lake, pairs of people rowed small boats, playing in the water, their faces beaming with the joy of love.

The old Qianli Xun, upon seeing this, would always feel disdain and mock girls in love as a bunch of love-brained fools and idiots.

But now she found herself envying the girls in love. She also wanted to hold Wenyuan's hand, stroll along Bai Causeway then Su Causeway, and then row a boat and play in the water...

Just as she was thinking this, she caught the smell of roasting meat. Qianli Xun looked around: "Where is this smoke coming from?"

Kuqiao pointed not far away: "Young lady, look—the smoke is coming from over there."

Qianli Xun looked over. On the lakeshore, where Bai Causeway was wider, there was a natural patch of grassland surrounded by four weeping willows. Under the trees, a group of people had gathered—looks like a bunch of rich young masters. They had somehow brought four round stoves and were having a barbecue. Waves of roasting meat smell drifted over, making Qianli Xun even hungrier. Thinking carefully, it must be lunchtime.

Qianli Xun suppressed her hunger and criticized: "West Lake is a 5A-level scenic spot! How dare these people hold a barbecue here? Is no one in charge?"

Kuqiao asked puzzled: "Young lady, what is a 5A-level scenic spot?"

"It means West Lake has beautiful scenery, and we should protect it, not be like that group, having a barbecue here and destroying the natural environment."

Kuqiao seemed to understand: "You're absolutely right, young lady!"

Qianli Xun's sense of public morality was aroused: "Come, let's go over and criticize that group. Tell them to pack up their stoves right away..."

Kuqiao pulled her young lady back: "Better not go. Don't you see Young Master Chen is over there?"

Qianli Xun looked carefully—and sure enough, it was Chen Haoyu. He sat under a willow tree, one lackey holding a plate of roasted meat, another feeding him with long chopsticks, while Chen Haoyu wore an expression of pure enjoyment.

"Why do I run into him everywhere?"

Kuqiao advised: "Although you're not afraid of Young Master Chen, today is your first date with Young Master Qiao. Less trouble is better than more trouble."

Thinking of the date, Qianli Xun had to give up: "Forget it. I'll let him off today. Next time I catch him doing something bad, I'll deal with him then!"

Chen Haoyu's appearance made Qianli Xun not want to continue strolling. She decided to turn back the way she came.

On her way back, five young ladies walked toward her. Miao Zhiyu was among them. She spotted Zhu Shuzhen at first glance and went up to greet her: "If I'd known Youqi Jushi was also coming to tour the lake, I should have invited you."

Qianli Xun thought—how could she run into all sorts of people today? Miao Zhiyu admired Wenyuan. She absolutely couldn't let her know about the date with Wenyuan: "If I'd known you were coming, I should have invited you. I've been strolling all morning and I'm tired now. Have you just arrived, Young Lady Miao?"

Miao Zhiyu looked at the friends beside her: "I made an appointment with a few close friends to have tea at Yongtai Teahouse, and we also had lunch there. After tea and a full meal, we took a walk—it helps digest the food and lets us enjoy the beautiful scenery."

Qianli Xun was puzzled: "Yongtai Teahouse is a place for tea. Why are they selling food now?"

A young lady explained: "Today Young Lady Miao is treating us. She ordered lunch in advance from Juxiang Tower. The staff from Juxiang Tower delivered it to Yongtai Teahouse at the agreed time, so we could enjoy it."

Qianli Xun thought: "So the Song Dynasty also has food delivery."

Seeing Zhu Shuzhen stay silent, Miao Zhiyu proactively said: "On my way here, I ran into Young Master Dongfang and his group drinking and gathering at the Lake-viewing Pavilion on the north side of West Lake. Strangely, I didn't see Young Master Qiao at all. Does Youqi Jushi know why Young Master Qiao didn't come?"

Qianli Xun had been worried about hurting Miao Zhiyu's feelings and reluctant to tell the truth. But since the other party was probing directly in front of so many people, why should she consider her feelings?

"Of course I know. Wenyuan and I have an appointment today. I arrived a bit early and strolled along Bai Causeway. Judging by the time, he should be arriving soon. You all enjoy your stroll. I need to hurry to meet Wenyuan. Excuse me."

After speaking, Qianli Xun left with Kuqiao.

Miao Zhiyu stood there stunned, envy and sorrow overwhelming her.

A young lady commented: "I heard Youqi Jushi just divorced the Military Training Officer. She's already with Young Master Qiao so quickly. It seems the rumors were true. Could her divorce have been because of Young Master Qiao?"

Another said: "I think so too. Otherwise, how could she divorce yesterday and be invited to tour West Lake today? Sigh, we unmarried girls are still eagerly waiting for our parents' orders and the matchmaker's words. Who knows when that will be? Youqi Jushi has not only experienced marriage, but also snatched away Young Master Qiao, whom all the young ladies in Lin'an admire. We can't help but feel inferior."

Miao Zhiyu didn't want to listen anymore: "Youqi Jushi is indeed enviable. It's just that the Qiao family is currently choosing a bride for Young Master Qiao. As far as I know, the Qiao family will never choose a woman who has been divorced. The real show has yet to begin. Let's go. We shouldn't waste the beautiful scenery of West Lake."

Miao Zhiyu's words were meant for others to hear, but also to comfort herself. The young ladies stopped discussing and continued enjoying the scenery.

When they passed the spot where Chen Haoyu and his group were having a barbecue, Miao Zhiyu wore a look of disgust and ignored them. The other young ladies also walked quickly, worried about being noticed by Young Master Chen.

The more you worry about something, the more it happens. The rich young masters having a barbecue with Chen Haoyu spotted the young ladies. Some whistled, others teased: "Beautiful ladies! Come join us for barbecue! The meat is freshly roasted! It smells great!"

One young lady walked quickly while cursing softly: "Playboys!"

Two rich young masters were about to go over and grab them, but Chen Haoyu stopped them: "Those girls may have some looks, but they're boring. Besides, their fathers' ranks are all higher than yours. Be careful—if they go home and complain, your fathers will have a hard time. Today we just enjoy ourselves and don't bother with women. After we finish eating, we'll take a boat ride."

With Young Master Chen speaking, the two rich young masters had to give up. They continued grinning, turning the roasting meat through the smoke and fire.
