第217章 老人心思需要猜
## Chapter 217: Guessing an Elder's Mind

说话艺人说完故事离开后，夏老太太心满意足，提出要求：“光说一次有什么用？以后让说话艺人每日都来。”
千里寻说：“奶奶放心，我已叮嘱过，往后说话艺人每日这个时间来给您老讲故事。”
老人笑得合不拢嘴。
二婶包氏用酸酸的语气说：“真没看出来，浩宇的媳妇比浩宇还会哄老太太开心。真羡慕大嫂有这么个会哄人的儿媳妇，哪像我家这位……”
包氏瞪一眼身旁的儿媳妇叶安雅：“以后多向淑真学学，要不浩东把你娶回来有什么用？”
叶安雅不敢言语，任由婆婆批评。
After the storyteller wrapped up and left, Old Madam Xia leaned back in pure contentment and laid down an order. "One session isn't nearly enough. Have him come every day from now on."

老太太虽不满也不好说什么。
"Don't worry, Grandmother," Qianli Xun said. "I already arranged for him to come at this same hour every day."

千里寻想观察观察再说，穿越前宅斗剧看多了，自然知道后宅之事不是用拳头就能解决的，需多动脑子。
Old Madam Xia beamed from ear to ear.

崔氏与他的儿子陈浩飞则权当看热闹，再说他们早已习惯叶安雅被婆婆批评，见怪不怪。
Second Aunt Madam Bao chimed in with a tart edge. "Who would have guessed Haoyu's bride is even better at buttering up Old Madam Xia than Haoyu himself? I do envy Eldest Sister-in-law for landing such a clever daughter-in-law, unlike the one in my house..." She shot a glare at Ye Anya beside her. "Take a lesson from Shuzhen! Otherwise what use was Haodong marrying you?"

包氏继续说：“怪只怪我家浩东从早到晚都在陈家的绸缎庄、茶庄、盐庄、瓷器行、码头，还有各大客栈、酒楼忙活，哪像浩宇和他媳妇，时间一大把，只顾着哄老太太开心。老太太您可不能顾此失彼，否则浩东会伤心。”
Ye Anya kept her head lowered and said nothing. Old Madam Xia frowned at the jab, yet held her tongue.

夏老太太听出对方话中有话，脸上笑容顿失：“不用你拿话提醒我，我心里有杆秤，谁人品、能力如何，谁对陈家贡献大，我都跟明镜似的。”
Qianli Xun stayed quiet and watched. Having binged enough household-intrigue dramas in her previous life, she knew inner-courtyard politics required brains, not fists.

崔氏见二嫂为自己儿子立功，也不愿落下：“我家浩飞自然比不上他的两位堂兄，一个会筹谋，一个会玩儿，还会讨老太太欢心。只怪他一门心思用在读书上，明年就该参加会试，会试要是通过，那可是要去汴梁参加殿试。浩飞，赶紧给你奶奶背背《尔雅》。”
Madam Cui and her son Chen Haofei treated the scolding as routine background noise; they had watched Madam Bao pick on Ye Anya too many times to blink.

老太太说：“行了，不用背，背了我也听不懂。浩飞是好孩子，好好学，给咱们陈家考个功名回来，就是对陈家最大的贡献。”
Madam Bao pressed on. "The trouble is, my Haodong slaves from dawn till dusk across the Chen silk house, tea house, salt shop, porcelain warehouse, docks, inns, and taverns—unlike Haoyu and his wife, who have all the time in the world to hang around amusing Old Madam Xia. Don't favor one at the expense of the other, Mother, or Haodong will be heartbroken."

陈浩飞说：“是，奶奶。”
Catching the barbed hint, Old Madam Xia let her smile drop. "Save your reminders. I keep a clear scale in my head. Who has character, who has ability, and who contributes most to the Chen household—I see every bit of it crystal clear."

老太太吩咐道：“都回去吧，我要跟浩宇的媳妇说几句话。”
Not wanting Second Sister-in-law to claim all the credit for her son, Madam Cui jumped right in. "My Haofei can't compare with either of his older cousins—one schemes for business, the other knows how to play and win Grandmother's favor. He puts his whole heart into his books instead. Next year he sits the metropolitan examination, and once he passes, he'll ride to Bianliang for the palace exam! Haofei, recite a passage from the *Erya* for your grandmother."

千里寻目送大家离开，想想刚才二婶和三婶说过的话，完全能体会到老太太为何会喜欢陈浩宇这个只会哄她开心却一无是处的孙子。
"No need," Old Madam Xia waved it off. "Even if he recites it, I won't understand a word. Haofei is a good boy. Study hard and bring an official title back to the Chen family—that will be the greatest contribution of all."

待众人离开后，老太太才说：“淑真，扶我进屋。”
"Yes, Grandmother," Chen Haofei answered.

千里寻将老太太扶起，搀扶的过程更感觉出她腿脚实在不利索。不由想到老祖宗，一百二十岁的人不知比眼前这位强多少倍，怜悯疼爱之情油然而生。边搀老太太边说：“奶奶，陈浩宇上次说有位大夫能治腿脚疾病，改日我把人给您找来。”
Old Madam Xia dismissed the crowd. "All of you head back now. I want a private word with Haoyu's wife."

老太太笑道：“浩宇的话你也信？他不过是哄我开心而已，陈家有的是钱，什么大夫请不来？早请过，我这腿脚无人可医。”
Watching them file out, Qianli Xun weighed the veiled competition from Second and Third Aunts. Suddenly it made total sense why Old Madam Xia doted on Chen Haoyu despite his good-for-nothing streak: everyone else came with an agenda, while he just showed up to make her laugh.

千里寻带着疑惑，扶老太太在榻上坐好，自己也脱去鞋子，盘腿坐在老人对面：“从今日起奶奶不再生我的气？”
Once the yard cleared, Old Madam Xia held out her hand. "Shuzhen, help me inside."

老太太笑道：“我何时生过你的气？”
Supporting her across the threshold, Qianli Xun noticed how stiff and unsteady the old woman's legs felt. Compared with the Old Ancestor back at Zhu Manor, who moved sprightly at a hundred and twenty, Old Madam Xia seemed frail indeed, stirring a sudden rush of pity and fondness in Qianli Xun's chest. "Grandmother, Chen Haoyu mentioned a physician who treats leg ailments. I'll track him down for you one of these days."

千里寻回想片刻，立刻醒悟：“原来奶奶是故意装作生我的气？早知道如此，我也不用费尽心思讨您欢心。”
Old Madam Xia chuckled. "You believe anything Haoyu says? He only says that to cheer me up. With the Chen fortune, what physician couldn't we hire? We've tried them all; my legs are beyond curing."

没想到老太太却说：“在这陈府，我终于遇到你这么个明白人，知道我寻事、生气，不过是想让你讨我欢心而已，可我那几个儿子儿媳没一个懂我的心思。”
Puzzled, Qianli Xun settled Old Madam Xia onto the kang couch, kicked off her own shoes, and sat cross-legged opposite her. "So you aren't angry with me anymore, Grandmother?"

千里寻这才恍然大悟，原来老太太每次见到陈浩宇的爹娘又是埋怨又是生气，不过是想让他们讨好她，哄她开心而已，却适得其反，她千里寻不过是歪打正着。
Old Madam Xia laughed. "When was I ever angry with you?"

“奶奶应该将您的喜好直接告诉各位长辈，还有我们这些晚辈。”
Qianli Xun froze a second before the penny dropped. "You were pretending to be mad the whole time? Had I known that, I wouldn't have worked so hard trying to win you over!"

老太太叹口气：“唉，说出来多没意思，凡是真正对我有心之人，自然知道该如何去做。就像你，愿意在我身上花心思，讨我欢心。往后你在这陈府，有我罩着，没人敢欺负你。”
"In this entire manor, I've at last found one person with sense," Old Madam Xia said. "You realized I picked fights and fumed just to get you to fuss over me. None of my sons or daughters-in-law ever figured out what I wanted."

千里寻故意说：“我嫁到陈府这段时日，除老太太您，还没人欺负过我，再说我也不怕，谁让我拳头厉害。”
Everything clicked at once. Every time Old Madam Xia snapped at Chen Haoyu's parents, she was only fishing for attention and affection—yet it had driven them away instead. Qianli Xun had stumbled onto the answer by sheer accident.

“呦呦呦，看把你能的。”夏老太太笑道，接着说，“不过，奶奶就喜欢你这样的女子，天不怕地不怕，像当年的我，可惜啊，我老了。”
"You ought to just tell the elders and us younger ones straight out what you like, Grandmother."

“奶奶，您骗我吧？我来陈家闹时您看上去对我很不满意，怎么可能喜欢我？”千里寻既开心，又不敢相信。想起敬茶那日奶奶的态度，哪像喜欢她的样子？
Old Madam Xia sighed. "Where's the joy in spelling it out? Anyone who truly cares finds out on their own—just like you, putting real thought into cheering me up. From now on, I have your back in Chen Manor. Nobody dares bully you."

老太太解释道：“你来闹事那日显然不想嫁给浩宇，我又何必同意？再说你那个表现，我老婆子笑脸相迎岂不是太不配合？况且我心疼孙子，怕你嫁过来真的揍他，想着干脆配合你把这婚事给搅黄了。”
Qianli Xun grinned wickedly. "Since marrying in, Grandmother, nobody except you has even tried to bully me. Not that I'd care—my fists do plenty of talking."

千里寻觉得夏老太太深不可测，表面一套，心里又是一套，让人难以捉摸，难怪她的儿子和媳妇们猜不透她的心思。
"Oh, listen to you brag!" Old Madam Xia laughed, her eyes crinkling. "Still, I like a girl like you—fearless of heaven and earth, just like me when I was young. Pity I'm old now."

说起婚前来陈家大闹一事，千里寻想起老太太那日提到父亲逼迫陈浩宇娶她一事，于是问道：“奶奶那日说是我父亲逼迫陈浩宇娶我，不知是何缘故？”
"You're teasing me, Grandmother," Qianli Xun said, half pleased and half skeptical. "You looked furious with me when I came raising a storm before the wedding, and at the tea ceremony too. How could you possibly have liked me?"

夏老太太立刻谨慎起来，孙媳妇还不知，看来知府大人有意瞒着她。只怪自己那日一生气没管好嘴，差点儿暴露。今日更不能说，凭孙媳的脾气和拳头，要是知道恐怕浩宇以后真没好日子过，还是赶快掩饰。
"You made it obvious that day you didn't want to marry Haoyu, so why would I beam and welcome you? Putting on a smiling face while you raged wouldn't have played along with your act! Besides, I worried you'd actually beat my grandson black and blue once you married in, so I figured I might as well help you blow up the engagement."

“没有的事儿，那日被你气得瞎说，别多想。”
Qianli Xun marveled at how deep Old Madam Xia's waters ran—one face outside, another inside. No wonder her own sons and daughters-in-law stayed baffled.

千里寻从老太太的表情中看不出异样，也就不再多问：“奶奶，您觉得千里寻才子写的故事怎么样？”
Thinking back to that pre-wedding showdown reminded Qianli Xun of one slip Old Madam Xia had let drop. "Grandmother, that day you mentioned my father forced Chen Haoyu to marry me. Why did he do that?"

“好！写得太好了！奶奶从来没听过这么新奇的故事。”老太太夸赞有加，接着疑惑道，“你刚才说写故事的人是才子，我怎么觉得像是位姑娘写的？”
Old Madam Xia stiffened at once. Her granddaughter-in-law still did not know—meaning Prefect Zhu had kept it under wraps. She cursed her own loose tongue from that day. With this girl's temper and fists, spilling the truth now would doom Haoyu for good. She scrambled to cover it up. "Nothing of the sort! You had me so flustered that day I just blabbed nonsense. Don't give it another thought."

被老太太夸奖，千里寻很欢喜，穿越前网暴残留的不快已荡然无存。接着她又佩服老太太的敏锐：“何以见得？”
Seeing no crack in the old lady's poker face, Qianli Xun let it drop. "Grandmother, how did you like Master Qianli Xun's story?"

老太太用肯定的语气说：“讲姑娘家的故事，肯定是姑娘写的。”
"Marvelous! Best tale I've heard in years—never heard anything so fresh!" Old Madam Xia beamed, then tilted her head. "You called the author a male scholar, yet why does it sound to me like a young woman wrote it?"

千里寻想到应对之语：“此言差矣，唐代诗仙李太白有《清平乐》，诗圣杜子美有《佳人》，诗佛王右丞有《洛阳女儿行》，写的全是女子，可他们都是响当当的男儿。”
Delighted by the praise—every last trace of her pre-transmigration cyberbullying blues gone—Qianli Xun also marveled at the old woman's sharp nose. "What makes you think that?"

孙媳妇书本上的知识懂得多，老太太更是乐得不行，心想，浩宇从小不怎么碰书本，也不知道哪辈子修来的福分，竟娶到一位才女：“不愧是江南女词人，懂得就是多。”
"A story all about a young lady's feelings? Had to be written by a woman," Old Madam Xia declared with conviction.

又被老太太夸赞，千里寻一高兴，想出讨老太太欢心的新招数：“奶奶，我认识一位负鼓盲翁，改日把他带来，给您唱蔡伯喈的故事。”
Qianli Xun had a comeback ready in a heartbeat. "Not necessarily! The Tang Poet Immortal Li Bai wrote 'Qingping Yue,' the Poet Sage Du Fu wrote 'The Fair Lady,' and the Poet Buddha Wang Wei wrote 'Song of the Daughter of Luoyang'—every one about women, yet all three were red-blooded men!"

老太太的头点得跟拨浪鼓似的：“好好好！”
Impressed by her granddaughter-in-law's learning, Old Madam Xia beamed all the brighter, wondering what karma her book-hating grandson had piled up in a past life to land such a gifted bride. "Worthy of the title Poetess of Jiangnan! You know everything."

两人越聊越开心，快到中午时，老太太要用饭，千里寻才离开。
Flushed with pleasure, Qianli Xun thought of another treat. "Grandmother, I know a blind drummer who sings ballads. I'll invite him over one of these days to perform the tale of Cai Bojie for you."

带着红豆走出老太太的院子没几步，被陈浩飞挡住：“二嫂好！”
Old Madam Xia bobbed her head like a pellet drum. "Yes, yes, yes!"

千里寻刚见过，当然认得：“原来是三弟，找我何事？”
The two talked right up until noon, when Qianli Xun excused herself so the old lady could eat.

陈浩飞说：“我想求二嫂件事，您一定要答应我。”
Barely a few paces beyond Old Madam Xia's gate with Hongdou in tow, Chen Haofei stepped into her path. "Greetings, Second Sister-in-law!"

“还是先说事情，我再衡量衡量要不要答应你。”跟陈浩飞不熟，千里寻不敢轻易应下。
Qianli Xun recognized him from the courtyard. "Third Brother-in-law. Need something?"

“我虽过乡试，可写诗作词差些，在临安也毫无名气。曾受邀参加过一些诗会，都是去凑凑热闹，从来没有机会带新的诗词前去，现在闻名江南的幽栖居士成为我的二嫂，怎么着也得给我送一两首诗词，好让我在诗会上有表现的机会。求求二嫂送我一两首吧？”
"I have a favor to beg of you, Second Sister-in-law. Please don't turn me down!"

原来是为此事，帮朱淑真宣传诗词，千里寻来者不拒：“我当什么事，写个把诗词不在话下，以后你想要随时找我。”
"Tell me what it is first, and I'll weigh whether to say yes." Hardly knowing him, she would not sign any blank checks.

“现在就想要。”
"I passed the provincial exams, yet my poetry is weak and nobody in Lin'an knows my name. Whenever I'm invited to poetry gatherings, I just tag along to fill a seat because I never have fresh verses to present. Now that the renowned Youqi Jushi is my second sister-in-law, could you spare me a poem or two so I can shine at the next salon? Please?"

“那就跟上。”
Promoting Zhu Shuzhen's poetry was right up her alley. "I thought it was something tough! Writing a couple of lyrics is nothing. Come ask anytime you need more."

陈浩飞高兴不已，跟上二嫂往墨香苑而去。
"I'd love them right now!"

当他们走到墨香苑门口时，叶安雅正站在门口等候。
"Follow me, then."

千里寻上前问：“大嫂怎么站在这里？身边也不带个丫鬟？”
Thrilled, Chen Haofei trailed her straight toward Moxiang Courtyard.

叶安雅说：“我正在等妹妹，想与你聊会儿。浩飞怎么也跑来？”
Approaching the gate, they spotted Ye Anya waiting outside.

陈浩飞上前说：“大嫂好，二嫂答应送我两首诗词，让我跟着前来。”
"Eldest Sister-in-law, why are you standing out here without even a maid?" Qianli Xun asked.

“原来如此。”叶安雅笑道，“大名鼎鼎的幽栖居士进入陈家的门，倒是给你带来方便，以后参加诗会不会空手而去。”
"I was waiting for you, hoping we could chat," Ye Anya said. "What brings Haofei along?"

陈浩飞也笑道：“那是自然，谁让我们成一家人。”
"Greetings, Eldest Sister-in-law," Chen Haofei said. "Second Sister-in-law promised to gift me two lyrics, so I came to fetch them."

大家说着笑着走进墨香苑，苦荞已将饭摆好，千里寻不着急用，先坐在桌前给浩飞写诗作词。此时正值春季，便选了两首与春季相关的词，一首《西江月·春半》，一首《蝶恋花·送春》。
"So that's it," Ye Anya smiled. "Having the celebrated Youqi Jushi marry into the Chen household certainly smooths your path—no more showing up empty-handed to poetry banquets."

洋洋洒洒写完之后，给了浩飞。陈浩飞接过，感谢不已，高兴离去。
"Of course! We're family now," Chen Haofei laughed.

已到用饭时间，千里寻邀请叶安雅留下与自己一同用饭。
Chatting and laughing, they stepped into Moxiang Courtyard, where Kuqiao had already laid out lunch. Setting aside the meal for a moment, Qianli Xun sat at her desk and penned two spring lyrics for Chen Haofei: "Xijiang Yue: Mid-Spring" and "Dielianhua: Bidding Farewell to Spring."

叶安雅没有拒绝，这个点儿等弟妹，不留下用饭说不过去。
Accepting the crisp sheets with effusive thanks, Chen Haofei hurried off on cloud nine.

两人在饭桌前坐好，不过是四菜一汤，加上主食。
Since lunch sat steaming on the table, Qianli Xun invited Ye Anya to dine with her. Having waited right at mealtime, Ye Anya could not refuse and took a seat.

千里寻穿越而来渐渐习惯古人的生活，对饭食已没有那么新奇，倒是对眼前的大嫂很感兴趣，不知前来找她所为何事？
The spread was modest—four dishes, one soup, and rice. By now Qianli Xun had grown used to Song meals and found far more interest in her sister-in-law, wondering what had brought Ye Anya to her door.

苦荞给二人盛好汤和主食后，站在一旁伺候。
Kuqiao ladled soup and served rice for both women before stepping back to attend them.

千里寻说：“在陈家只有大嫂是与我年纪相仿的女子，以后你我要常来往。”
"You're the only woman near my age in Chen Manor, Eldest Sister-in-law," Qianli Xun said. "We should spend time together often."

叶安雅并不客气，拿起勺子喝口汤后说道：“我也是这么想的，所以今日才来找你聊天儿。”
Ye Anya raised her spoon for a sip of soup without standing on ceremony. "My thoughts exact—that's why I came over to talk today."

千里寻夹一道菜放入大嫂的碗中：“我刚嫁到陈家什么都不了解，有时间大嫂要多给我讲讲，省得我在陈家说错话，做错事，惹长辈不开心。”
Qianli Xun placed a morsel of food into Ye Anya's bowl. "I just married in and don't know half the ins and outs of this household. Fill me in whenever you have time, so I don't put my foot in my mouth and upset the elders."

“听说你根本不愿嫁到陈家，洞房那晚跟浩宇也未圆房，他整日逛荡在外你也不管，竟然愿意在长辈身上花心思，实在让人费解。”
"Word is you never wanted to marry into the Chen family," Ye Anya remarked, "didn't consummate the marriage on your wedding night, and don't care where Haoyu wanders off to. Yet you still put real effort into pleasing the elders. I can't quite figure you out."

“我是不愿嫁给陈浩宇，可命运捉弄，到最后不得不嫁。既然嫁过来，就把自己当陈家人看待，想在这里安心过日子。”
"I didn't want to marry Chen Haoyu, true, yet fate twisted my arm and here I am. Since I married in, I treat myself as part of the Chen family and mean to live here in peace."

淑真提到“不得不嫁”让叶安雅想到仪王抢亲之事，原来仪王也看上朱家姑娘，她想不通为何朱家不愿把女儿给仪王，却偏偏许给浩宇这样的浪荡子？
Those words—"fate twisted my arm"—reminded Ye Anya of Prince Yi raiding the wedding procession. Prince Yi himself had wanted the Zhu daughter; why had the Zhu family turned down a prince to hand her to a rake like Haoyu?

不由感慨道：“在外人看来，妹妹与我能嫁到陈家算是幸运，其中的酸甜苦辣也只有你我知道。浩宇整日不着家，也不知在哪里浪荡，我家那位表面看着比浩宇强，其实……”
She let out a quiet sigh. "Outsiders think you and I hit the jackpot marrying into the Chen family, yet only we know the bitter along with the sweet. Haoyu stays out day and night, heaven knows where, and while my husband looks respectable on the surface, in truth..."

叶安雅欲言又止，千里寻听出些心酸来，赶紧又夹些菜，放入大嫂碗中：“尝尝这道菜，还不错。”
Ye Anya bit off the rest of the sentence. Hearing the ache behind her words, Qianli Xun placed another dish in her bowl. "Try this. It's pretty good."

叶安雅吃完菜后，继续说：“我很羡慕妹妹，又厉害又有主见，既得了浩宇的财产，还能把这院子收拾成自己喜欢的样子，而我……”
After a bite, Ye Anya went on. "I envy you, Sister—tough, independent, holding Haoyu's properties in your hands, remaking this courtyard just the way you please. Whereas I..."

千里寻赶紧解释：“我能如此也是公爹和婆婆人好，陈家各位长辈纵着我，不愿跟我一般见识，更有像大嫂这样愿意与我吐露心声的妯娌。”
"I only got away with that because Father-in-law and Mother-in-law are decent people, the elders indulge me, and I have a sister-in-law like you willing to speak from the heart," Qianli Xun said.

叶安雅被夸得“噗嗤”笑出声来：“你这张嘴还真甜，难怪能把老太太搞定。我听说那日老太太带着我婆婆还有三婶前来寻事，你怼得有理有据，她们三人最后无话可说只能离开，你这张嘴啊不仅能口吐芬芳，还能吐出各种道理来，实在厉害！”
Ye Anya burst into a laugh. "What a honeyed tongue—no wonder you won Old Madam Xia over so fast! I heard that when Old Madam Xia marched in with my mother-in-law and Third Aunt to pick a fight, you argued them into a corner with solid logic until none of the three had a comeback left. That mouth of yours doesn't just compose fine verses—it shoots out airtight arguments too. Impressive!"

“大嫂谬赞。”
"You flatter me, Eldest Sister-in-law."

千里寻与叶安雅说说笑笑吃着饭，她觉得陈家人多又热闹，充满温馨的气息，唯一美中不足的便是嫁人不淑，若陈浩宇是她喜欢的人那该多好。
Laughing over lunch, Qianli Xun felt the warmth of a large, lively family—spoiled only by the wrong husband. If only Chen Haoyu were the man she actually loved.

想到喜欢之人，自然想起申简辰。他跟梦容应该还在回汴梁的路上，等到他们再回到临安时，大家会唤梦容仪王妃。
That thought drifted straight to Shen Jianchen. He and Mengrong had to be on the road to Bianliang right now. When they returned to Lin'an, everyone would address Mengrong as Princess Consort Yi.

千里寻默默在心中祝福，希望仪王与他的王妃能过上幸福的生活。
In silence, Qianli Xun sent them her blessing, wishing Prince Yi and his princess consort a happy life together.
