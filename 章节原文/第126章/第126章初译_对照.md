第126章 话本才子美名传
## Chapter 126: Fame of the Storyteller Scholar Spreads

正如安明轩所言，中秋诗宴中的文人并未因流言轻视幽栖居士的诗，大家反而赞赏有加。
Just as An Mingxuan predicted, the literati at the Mid-Autumn Poetry Banquet refused to let street gossip cloud their judgment, pouring praise on Youqi Jushi's poems instead.

有人说，幽栖居士没有被流言打倒，竟然还能写出：“莫做寻常看，嫦娥亦解愁”这等浪漫的诗句，实在难得。
One scholar marveled that no storm of rumors could break Youqi Jushi's spirit, letting her pen a line as transcendent as "Do not regard it with a casual eye: even Chang'e knows what sorrow means."

更有人说，市井之人只顾嚼舌，却不知幽栖居士以笑面对，竟然在中秋之夜玩起月来，此等心态令人羡慕。
Another praised how, while market folk wagged tongues, Youqi Jushi met the world with a smile and enjoyed the Mid-Autumn moon—a breadth of spirit to envy.

尤其是“兔影寒犹弄，蟾蜍老更坚”二句，“寒”字与“老”字写出现实境况不佳，可玉兔面对中秋之夜的寒冷依然在捣药，蟾蜍虽老，却老当益壮，无不展现出可敬可佩的精神力量。
Above all, the couplet "Through chill the jade hare works its pestle still; grown old, the moon toad stands yet stronger still" drew raves: "chill" and "old" mirrored harsh reality, yet the jade hare pounded its elixir undaunted by autumn frost, and the moon toad grew firmer with age, radiating an indomitable grace.

二位公子也各自展现品诗才华，闹到凌晨，实在扛不下去，才离开中秋诗宴，准备回家睡觉。其他文人继续饮酒、赏月、赋诗、作词、品鉴，打算热闹整个通宵。
Both young masters showed off their critical flair too, reveling until the small hours before exhaustion sent them home to bed, while other scholars kept drinking, gazing at the moon, trading verse, and debating until dawn.

二人刚出酒楼，被林子孝叫住：“二位公子！留步！”
Stepping out of the tavern, they heard Lin Zixiao hail them: "Hold on, you two young masters!"

见是林子孝，两人恭敬道：“林家二叔好，不知拦住我们二人，有何指教？”
Recognizing him, both bowed: "Greetings, Second Uncle Lin. What brings you to stop us?"

林子孝直言不讳：“二位可见过幽栖居士？”
Lin Zixiao spoke straight: "Have either of you seen Youqi Jushi?"

二人摇摇头：“未见本尊。”
Both shook their heads: "Not in person."

“她的诗你们如何得来？”
"Then where did you get her poems?"

曹班说：“这就不劳烦二叔挂怀。”
Cao Ban smiled: "No need for Second Uncle to worry over that."

“我倒也不挂怀，只想二位帮我捎个话。她毕竟在林家待过一段时日，以前也算是一家人，现在被人议论成那样，听说跟乔家的事也黄了，又被知府大人赶出家门，一个女儿家在外生活不容易。今日读她的诗，我算看出，幽栖居士不是一位能够被困难打倒之人，令人钦佩。”
"I'm not prying—just want to ask you to pass a message. She lived under the Lin roof once, so we were family. Hearing how folk smear her, how the Qiao match fell through, and how the Prefect cast her out—life's hard for a young woman on her own. Reading her poems tonight showed me Youqi Jushi isn't someone hardship can break. I admire her."

二人被林子孝的话感动。
Lin Zixiao's words moved both young masters.

安明轩说：“林家二叔有什么话尽管说，我等定帮您转达。”
An Mingxuan said, "Tell us whatever you'd like to pass along, Second Uncle Lin, and we'll deliver it."

“麻烦二位告诉幽栖居士，不管别人怎么议论她，我和林家人绝不会在背后议论她一分一毫。再转告她，谣言就像一阵风，很快会过去，等下一阵风来，自然消失。”
"Tell Youqi Jushi this: no matter what outsiders say, neither I nor anyone in the Lin family will ever breathe a single bad word behind her back. Tell her too that rumors are like a gust of wind—here one moment, blown clean away the next time the wind shifts."

曹班调侃道：“您可做得了林家的主？”
Cao Ban teased, "Can you speak for the whole Lin clan?"

“我大哥不在，我便是林家族中长辈，哪个小辈敢在我面前说个‘不’字？”
"With my eldest brother away, I'm the senior elder of the Lin house—which junior dares say 'no' to me?"

二位公子相视一笑：“我二人定当转达，林家二叔，回见。”
Sharing a smile, both young masters promised, "We'll deliver every word. Farewell, Second Uncle Lin."

接下来的日子，千里寻未踏出望月轩半步，除吃喝拉撒休息外，只顾写话本，偶尔写首朱淑真的诗词消遣消遣。
In the days that followed, Qianli Xun did not set foot outside Wangyue Pavilion. Between eating, sleeping, and daily routines, she poured herself into writing huaben scripts, jotting down a poem or ci of Zhu Shuzhen's now and then to pass the hours.

仪王派白风来过两次，每次来除找苦荞玩耍以外，就是告知文渊的境况。第一次说文渊还被关着，不让走出屋子；第二次说乔家同意文渊可以走出屋子，但不能离开院子。
Prince Yi sent Bai Feng over twice, both times visiting Kuqiao while delivering updates on Wenyuan. The first time, Wenyuan was still locked indoors; the second, the Qiaos let him step out into his courtyard, though not beyond its gate.

千里寻听后，担心的同时，有些许轻松。能走出屋子已是进步，很快文渊会走出院子，再走出乔府，然后来望月轩找她。她决定耐心等待着文渊突然出现在面前。
Worry mingled with relief in Qianli Xun: stepping out into his yard meant progress. Soon he would step past the gate, out of Qiao Manor, and straight to Wangyue Pavilion. She decided to wait with patience for the day he showed up at her door.

没有将文渊等来，倒是等来三位公子。
Wenyuan did not arrive; instead, the three young masters paid a call.

东方尘泽的家人经过调查，自家儿子跟幽栖居士清清白白，没有任何越矩之事，便逼他认下一门亲，待亲事定后，才放他走出家门。
After Dongfang Chenze's parents investigated and confirmed their son had kept spotless propriety with Youqi Jushi, they pressured him into accepting a betrothal, releasing him only once the match was sealed.

千里寻深感歉意：“我的事，竟然牵连到东方公子，实属抱歉。”
Guilt tugged at Qianli Xun: "Getting Young Master Dongfang dragged into my mess—I'm truly sorry."

东方尘泽趁机提出要求：“幽栖居士光口头抱歉有何用？来点实惠的。”
Dongfang Chenze seized the opening: "Empty apologies don't count, Youqi Jushi—give me something real!"

“东方公子请说，只要我能力所及，必然答应。”
"Name it, Young Master Dongfang—anything within my power, you've got it."

“以后幽栖居士写的诗词必须第一个给我。”
"From now on, every new poem or ci you write comes to me first."

“好说好说，以后我写好诗词会让丫鬟送到府上。”
"Deal! I'll send a maid over to your manor whenever I finish one."

“不必，我让化雨定期来取。”
"No need—I'll have Huayu come collect them on a schedule."

安明轩说：“东方兄得这么大的便宜，再被多关几日也值得。”
An Mingxuan laughed: "Scoring a bargain like that, Brother Dongfang—being locked up a few extra days would've been worth it!"

曹班说：“东方兄还要多谢幽栖居士，要不是因为她的事，你能这么快与喜欢的姑娘定亲？”
Cao Ban added, "Brother Dongfang ought to thank Youqi Jushi! Without this fuss, would you have gotten betrothed to the girl you love so fast?"

千里寻露出近期以来难得的笑容：“什么？东方公子竟然与喜欢的姑娘订亲？怎么回事？说来听听。”
A rare, bright smile broke across Qianli Xun's face: "What? Young Master Dongfang got engaged to the girl he loves? Tell me everything!"

安明轩帮忙解释：“那姑娘是乔兄的大妹妹，东方兄与她早已郎有情妾有意，一直不敢对父母提起，没想到家里竟然帮他到乔府提亲，乔家一口答应，岂不是美事？”
An Mingxuan filled her in: "It's Brother Qiao's eldest younger sister! Brother Dongfang and she have had feelings for each other ages without daring to tell their parents—who knew his parents would march straight to Qiao Manor to propose, and the Qiaos said yes on the spot! Isn't that grand?"

千里寻替东方尘泽感到高兴，既有父母之命媒妁之言，又有两情相悦，实属不易。可安明轩的话语中提到乔文仙，莫名的伤感不由袭上心头。
Qianli Xun felt genuine joy for Dongfang Chenze—parental blessing and mutual love, a rare prize! Yet mention of Qiao Wenxian sent a quiet pang through her chest.

曹班见状，赶紧岔开话题：“中秋那晚，得到幽栖居士的两首五律，我们二人拿到中秋诗宴，众人都赞不绝口。临走时，林家二叔还让我们二人给你捎话来着。”
Catching the shadow on her face, Cao Ban shifted gears fast: "On Mid-Autumn night, An Mingxuan and I got hold of your two new five-character poems and brought them to the Mid-Autumn Poetry Banquet—everyone raved! On our way out, Second Uncle Lin even asked us to pass you a message."

安明轩接着说：“林家二叔说，不管别人怎么议论，他与林家绝不会在背后议论一分一毫。还说谣言就像一阵风，很快会过去，等下一阵风来，自然消失。”
An Mingxuan picked it up: "Second Uncle Lin said no matter how folk gossip, neither he nor the Lin family will ever badmouth you behind your back—and that rumors are just a passing gust of wind, gone the second the next breeze blows."

“让林家二叔费心，有机会见到他，我定当面感谢。”
"How kind of Second Uncle Lin. Next time I see him, I'll thank him in person."

千里寻被林子孝的话感动，感恩命运让她遇到许多善良又关心她的人，林子孝是，眼前的三位公子也是，还有苗知瑜，也不知她有没有见到文渊？
Warmth swelled inside Qianli Xun at Lin Zixiao's kindness, grateful fate had placed so many good, loyal people around her: Lin Zixiao, these three young masters, and Miao Zhiyu too—she wondered if Miao Zhiyu had seen Wenyuan yet?

“你们近期可见过苗姑娘？”
"Have any of you seen Lady Miao lately?"

东方尘泽说：“我一直被关着，才获自由，她的境况一概不知。”
Dongfang Chenze said, "I just got let out of lockup—no idea how she's doing."

曹班回道：“我倒是在街上遇见过一次，跟一群闺中密友在一起，看上去心情不错。”
Cao Ban replied, "I spotted her on the street once with a cluster of female friends; looked in good spirits."

安明轩说：“没有你和乔兄，诗会都举行不起来，苗姑娘除诗会时跟我们见上一面，离开诗会都不怎么跟我等往来。她的境况我们很难得知。”
An Mingxuan said, "Without you and Brother Qiao, our poetry salons fell apart. Lady Miao only mixed with us at salons anyway, so we haven't heard much."

“没关系，我只是随口问问，毕竟大家因诗会结识。”
"No matter—just asking in passing since we all met through salons."

东方尘泽劝道：“幽栖居士没事不要老窝在望月轩，出去走走，也可去钱塘江观潮，近期去的人挺多。”
Dongfang Chenze urged, "Don't stay cooped up inside Wangyue Pavilion all day, Youqi Jushi—go out for a breath of air! Even head down to watch the Qiantang River tidal bore; crowds have been flocking there lately."

“有机会我定去看看。”千里寻口中答应，其实她哪有心情去江边观潮。
"I'll go take a look when I get the chance." Qianli Xun agreed out of politeness, though she had zero mood for tide-watching.

曹班突然提到另一个话题：“你们最近有没有听说一位叫千里寻的才子？”
Out of the blue, Cao Ban changed the subject: "Have you guys heard of a scholar named Qianli Xun lately?"

安明轩说：“当然听说过，写话本的，故事写的很吸引人，听的人很多，我也去听过，确实不错，现在全临安城的说话艺人都在说他写的故事，大家听了一遍又一遍，都翘首期盼后面的情节。”
An Mingxuan lit up: "Of course! Writes huaben scripts—gripping plots! Huge crowds flock to hear them; I went myself and loved it! Right now every storyteller in Lin'an is performing his story, and listeners come back again and again dying for the next installment!"

这大概是千里寻近期听到的最令她欣慰之事。
That was the sweetest news Qianli Xun had heard in weeks.

东方尘泽说：“我一直被关在家里，因我家堂弟酷爱听书，回来给我讲千里寻写的故事，的确曲折动人。可惜对这位才子只闻其名，不见其人，有机会定要结识结识。”
Dongfang Chenze chimed in: "While I was locked indoors, my cousin—a storytelling fanatic—kept retelling Qianli Xun's tale, and it really is full of twists! Shame everyone knows his name while nobody's seen his face—I'd love to meet him one day."

曹班说：“恐怕东方兄会失望，千里寻甚是神秘，每次写好话本，是由身边的丫鬟送到说话艺人那里，连说话艺人都从来没见过本尊。”
Cao Ban laughed: "Prepare for disappointment, Brother Dongfang! Qianli Xun is mysterious as a ghost—sends every new script through a maid, so not even the storytellers have ever laid eyes on him!"

东方尘泽说：“如此神秘，更勾起我的兴起，非把他挖出来不可。”
Dongfang Chenze's eyes sparkled: "That just makes me itch to dig him out even more!"

千里寻赶紧劝道：“依我看，千里寻才子应该不喜被人关注，我等还是尊重他的选择，只需听他的故事即可。他写的本子我也听过，甚是喜欢，有机会我再去听听。”
Qianli Xun jumped in fast: "If you ask me, Master Qianli Xun likely hates the spotlight. Better respect his wishes and just enjoy his stories! I've listened to his scripts too and loved them—I'll go listen again when I can."

三位公子惊讶地看着幽栖居士。
All three young masters gaped at Youqi Jushi.

东方尘泽问：“幽栖居士以前可瞧不上下里巴人的玩意儿，怎么突然来了兴致？”
Dongfang Chenze blinked: "Youqi Jushi used to turn her nose up at lowbrow stuff—since when did you take a shine to it?"

“人总是会变，以前是我小瞧下里巴人之作，现在我以为只要老百姓喜欢，就是好东西，什么阳春白雪，什么下里巴人，分得那么清楚，有何意义？”
"People change! I used to look down on lowbrow works, too. Now I figure whatever ordinary folk love is good work—highbrow 'Spring Snow' versus lowbrow street art, what's the point drawing such snobbish lines?"

穿越前的千里寻写网络小说，那个时代，精英文人对网络小说褒贬不一，在她看来，精英文人说什么都没用，最有用的是时代和老百姓的选择。
Back before crossing over, Qianli Xun wrote web fiction—a world where elitist critics argued over whether web novels counted as literature. In her book, elitist opinions meant nothing; the verdict of time and everyday readers was what lasted.

就像她穿越而来的宋代，朱淑真的诗词在精英文人中传颂，要不是因谣言四起，老百姓哪会关心她？即使如此议论，也只记住一句词：“和衣睡倒人怀”。
Just like here in the Song dynasty: Zhu Shuzhen's verse lived among elite scholars, yet common folk never spared her a thought until rumors broke—and even then only knew one line, "falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed."

而千里寻不一样，老百姓之所以议论她，只因她写的故事写到老百姓心里。
Qianli Xun was different: ordinary folk talked about her because her stories spoke straight to their hearts.

幽栖居士一番话，令三位公子佩服不已。
Her words left all three young masters nodding in genuine admiration.

大家又聊一会儿，才互相分别，望月轩又恢复寂静。
After chatting a bit longer, they took their leave, returning quiet to Wangyue Pavilion.

千里寻继续回闺房写话本，老百姓喜欢，她更有创作动力，也希望用写话本来消磨时光，忘掉不快，等待更美好的一天到来。
Back at her desk writing huaben scripts, knowing readers loved her work fired up her pen—giving her something meaningful to fill the hours, push sorrow aside, and wait for brighter days ahead.
