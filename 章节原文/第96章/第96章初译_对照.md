第96章 来到校场把话训
## Chapter 96: Addressing the Troops on the Drill Ground

乔守仁见仪王不言语，以为他也不愿乔家的儿媳妇是个悍妇，不由心中窃喜。
Seeing Prince Yi fall silent, Qiao Shouren assumed the prince had no wish to see a shrew become the Qiao family's daughter-in-law either, and secret glee warmed his chest.

乔文仙急了，放下茶盏：“仪王，你怎么不说话？”
Panic seized Qiao Wenxian. He set down his tea bowl. "Prince Yi, why aren't you saying anything?"

申简辰看着文渊渴求的眼神，想道：“若千里寻在此，肯定也会用同样渴求的目光看着我，我爱她，却无法得到她，不如成全。”
Looking into Wenyuan's pleading eyes, Shen Jianchen thought, "If Qianli Xun were here, she'd be looking at me with that exact same longing. I love her, yet I can't have her. Better to give them my blessing."

申简辰定定心神，努力说道：“陈家公子的做派和名声，外公应该也听说过。朱家姑娘教训他，一来，代替哥嫂教训晚辈；二来，肯定是陈家公子做出该打之事，朱家姑娘才会出手。说明朱家姑娘坚持正义，不惧邪恶，如若是我遇到，定然也要管一管。能打得过陈家公子的姑娘，更应是文渊娶妻的不二人选。外公试想，乔家产业不少，文渊手无缚鸡之力，若再娶一位柔弱的当家主母，以后如何撑得起这份家业？还望外公仔细斟酌。”
Shen Jianchen steadied his mind and forced himself to speak. "Grandfather must have heard of Young Master Chen's conduct and reputation. When Miss Zhu taught him a lesson, first, she was disciplining a junior on behalf of her brother and sister-in-law; second, Young Master Chen must have done something that begged for a beating before she lifted a hand. That proves Miss Zhu stands up for justice and fears no bully. Had I been there, I would have stepped in too. A young woman who can beat Young Master Chen in a fight is all the more the one and only match for Wenyuan. Think about it, Grandfather: the Qiao family holds vast estates, and Wenyuan hasn't the strength to truss up a chicken. If he marries some frail mistress of the household on top of that, how will they ever hold this estate together in the future? Please give it careful thought, Grandfather."

乔守仁想起曾派人去乡下收租，遇到地痞流氓之事，收租的下人回来，不仅租没收到，下人的腿也被打得残废。
Qiao Shouren recalled a time he had sent servants to the countryside to collect rent, only for them to run into local thugs. The rent collector came back empty-handed, his leg beaten until it was crippled.

再瞧瞧正在品茶的儿子，虽不是仪王所说的“手无缚鸡之力”，但要让他一人撑起这份偌大的家业，的确辛苦，如若娶个厉害的女子也不错。
Glancing back at his son sipping tea, he admitted that while Wenyuan might not be quite "too weak to truss up a chicken" as Prince Yi put it, carrying such a massive family enterprise alone would indeed be a heavy burden. Marrying a tough, capable woman might not be a bad idea after all.

再说仪王苦口婆心，亲自前来，说的没有多少道理也就罢了，可偏偏至情至理，再不同意，岂不是不给仪王面子？
Besides, Prince Yi had come in person and argued with such earnest conviction. If his words had been hollow, that would be one thing, yet every point made complete sense. Turning him down now would be a slap in Prince Yi's face.

“仪王说得不无道理，我无话可说，同意便是。”
"Prince Yi makes a compelling case. I have no further objection. I agree."

乔文仙一口喝完剩下的茶，还未放盏，急切地问：“父亲！您同意到朱府提亲？”
Qiao Wenxian gulped down the rest of his tea and asked before even setting the bowl down, "Father! You agree to send a marriage proposal to Zhu Manor?"

乔守仁点点头：“我同意了，不过，你先别急，朱家姑娘和离不久，现在上门提亲不甚合适，等过上一两个月，我再带上你母亲去朱府拜见知府大人。”
Qiao Shouren nodded. "I agree. Hold your horses, though. Miss Zhu only went through her mutual divorce a short while ago, so calling on them to propose right now would look unseemly. Give it a month or two, and then I'll take your mother to Zhu Manor to pay a call on the Prefect."

文渊起身，向父亲和仪王行礼：“感谢仪王！感谢父亲！”
Wenyuan rose and bowed to both his father and Prince Yi. "Thank you, Prince Yi! Thank you, Father!"

申简辰却高兴不起来，好不容易找到千里寻，刚找到没多久，还未告诉她仪王正是申简辰，就要轻易失去。
Shen Jianchen, however, could feel no joy. After all the trouble it took to find Qianli Xun, he had just found her, had not even told her that Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen, and now he was about to lose her just like that.

离开乔府，申简辰想起千里寻拜托之事，他不打算跟文渊回幽林别居，两人在街头分开。
Leaving Qiao Manor, Shen Jianchen remembered the favor Qianli Xun had asked of him. With no plans to go back to Youlin Retreat alongside Wenyuan, he parted ways with him on the street.

今日有车夫赶车，用不上白风，他陪王爷坐在马车里。
Since a coachman was driving today, Bai Feng was not needed up front and rode inside the carriage with His Highness.

见王爷一副失落的样子，白风不敢打扰，静静坐在一旁，心里想着苦荞。想到在朱府的马车里向苦荞表白之事，觉得自己既大胆又好笑，不由笑出声来。
Seeing how downcast His Highness looked, Bai Feng dared not disturb him. He sat in silence at his side, thinking of Kuqiao. Recalling how he had confessed his feelings to Kuqiao inside Zhu Manor's carriage, he found himself both bold and ridiculous, and a laugh slipped out before he could stop it.

听到笑声，申简辰才注意白风：“你笑什么？”
The laugh drew Shen Jianchen's attention to Bai Feng. "What are you laughing about?"

“没……没什么。”白风不好意思说出。
"N-nothing." Bai Feng felt too embarrassed to say.

“刚才你跟南双去玩了？”
"Did you go hang out with Nan Shuang just now?"

“他带我在乔府转了转。”
"He showed me around Qiao Manor."

“你饿不饿？”
"Are you hungry?"

“是有些饿，王爷为何不在乔府吃过饭再走？我看乔府今日做了东坡肉。”
"A bit. Why didn't Your Highness stay for lunch at Qiao Manor before leaving? I saw they made Dongpo pork today."

申简辰努力挤出笑容：“我以为你现在只对幽栖居士身边的丫鬟感兴趣，竟然还想着东坡肉。”
Shen Jianchen forced a smile. "I thought you only had eyes for Youqi Jushi's maid now. Didn't expect you to still be thinking about Dongpo pork."

“两个我都感兴趣。”
"I'm interested in both."

“待会儿办完事，带你下馆子。”
"Once I wrap up my errand, I'll take you out to eat."

“好啊好啊！”白风很开心，又问道，“王爷，什么时候幽栖居士还去幽林别居？”
"Yes, yes!" Delighted, Bai Feng asked, "Your Highness, when is Youqi Jushi coming back to Youlin Retreat?"

“这个嘛……我不大清楚。”申简辰故意逗白风，希望逗逗他，自己心里就不会那么难受。
"Well... hard to say." Shen Jianchen teased Bai Feng on purpose, hoping a little banter might ease the ache in his own chest.

“唉，我现在真想和南双换换，乔公子与幽栖居士约会，他就能见到苦荞，可我却没这福气。”
"Sigh. Right now I'd love to trade places with Nan Shuang. Whenever Young Master Qiao goes out with Youqi Jushi, he gets to see Kuqiao, while I have no such luck."

申简辰装作开心的样子：“要不赶明儿你跟南双换换？”
Putting on a cheerful front, Shen Jianchen said, "How about we swap you with Nan Shuang tomorrow?"

“那怎么行！我是王爷的贴身侍卫，还要跟王爷学拳法，刚才不过是感慨一下。”
"No way! I'm Your Highness's personal bodyguard, and I still have to learn martial arts from you. I was just sighing over my luck."

“快到七夕节，到时文渊会约幽栖居士逛街，我也去，你就能见到想见的人。”
"Qixi Festival is almost here. Wenyuan is taking Youqi Jushi out to stroll the streets, and I'm going too, so you'll get to see the person you've been missing."

白风开始扳手指算日子，笑道：“还有五日。”
Bai Feng counted the days on his fingers and broke into a grin. "Five days left."

马车来到练兵署。
The carriage rolled up to the Military Training Office.

练兵署位于城西，由林觉主管，他的上面还有统领、统制、副都统制，诸军都统制，最后是节度使大人。
Located in the west of the city, the Military Training Office fell under Lin Jue's charge. Above him stood the Commandant, the Division Commander, the Vice Commander-in-Chief, the Commander-in-Chief of All Forces, and at the very top, the Lord Military Commissioner.

林觉之所以敢越级直接找节度使大人，无非是因和离一事与他攀上关系，再走得近些，其他官员也可对他忌惮几分。如此想来，与朱淑真和离倒是因祸得福。
The only reason Lin Jue dared bypass the chain of command to approach the Military Commissioner himself was the tie he had forged through his mutual divorce. Growing closer to him would also make other officials think twice before crossing Lin Jue. Looked at that way, divorcing Zhu Shuzhen had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.

申简辰走下马车，眼前是练兵署的大门，谈不上气派倒也庄严，上面写着“练兵署”三个大字。
Shen Jianchen stepped down from the carriage. Before him stood the gate of the Military Training Office, not grand yet solemn enough, with three large characters reading "Military Training Office" inscribed above.

白风上前对门口站的士兵说：“节度使大人来找林练兵官，去告诉他出来迎接。”
Bai Feng walked up to the soldier stationed at the gate. "The Lord Military Commissioner is here to see Training Officer Lin. Go tell him to come out and receive His Lordship."

士兵听到是节度使大人，立刻拜道：“节度使大人稍候，小的这就去报告练兵官。”
Hearing it was the Military Commissioner, the soldier dropped into a bow at once. "Please wait a moment, Your Lordship. I will report to the Training Officer right away."

士兵去后没过多久，林觉匆忙出来：“不知大人前来，望恕罪！”
Moments after the soldier hurried off, Lin Jue rushed out. "I did not know Your Lordship was coming! Please forgive my failure to greet you!"

申简辰说：“我也是偶然想起你上次请我给士兵训话，便过来看看。”
Shen Jianchen said, "I just happened to recall how you invited me to address the troops last time, so I thought I'd drop by for a look."

林觉更觉荣耀，他随口一说，仪王竟然记在心里，攀上这棵大树，以后可平步青云。
Lin Jue felt even more honored. He had only tossed out a casual invitation, yet Prince Yi had taken it to heart. With a patron this mighty behind him, his rise through the ranks was assured.

统领的位置刚刚空缺下来，林觉有心爬上去。本想求朱晞颜帮忙，可因夏果一事，昨日他明显故意躲着他。仪王前来，正好给林觉送来一个好机会，定要好好表现一番。
The post of Commandant had just fallen vacant, and Lin Jue had his heart set on climbing into it. He had meant to ask Zhu Xiyan for help, yet over the whole Xiaguo affair, Zhu Xiyan had gone out of his way to dodge him yesterday. Prince Yi's arrival handed Lin Jue a golden opportunity; he had to put on a stellar show.

“节度使大人，里面请，进去喝杯茶，我再带您去校场。”
"Please come inside, Lord Military Commissioner. Have a cup of tea first, and then I'll escort you to the drill ground."

“喝茶就不必了，直接去校场，看完士兵，我还有事要与练兵官说。”
"Skip the tea. Take me straight to the drill ground. After I see the troops, I have something to discuss with you."

林觉内心更加窃喜，仪王有事与他说，说明已把他当成自己人：“好，我这就带节度使大人去校场，这边请。”
Secret delight surged in Lin Jue's chest. If Prince Yi had business to discuss with him, that meant the prince already viewed him as one of his own. "Very well, I'll take Your Lordship straight to the drill ground. This way, please."

校场离练兵署几步之遥，到达之后，林觉将仪王请上验兵台，吩咐校尉将士兵聚集而来。
The drill ground lay only a few steps from the Military Training Office. Upon arriving, Lin Jue invited Prince Yi up onto the reviewing platform and ordered a captain to assemble the soldiers.

临安的士兵真不少，黑压压一片。
Lin'an had quite a force: a dense, dark sea of soldiers stretching across the ground.

林觉先向仪王行礼：“节度使大人，我先给士兵们打个招呼，您再训话。”
Lin Jue bowed to Prince Yi first. "Lord Military Commissioner, let me address the troops first, and then you can give your speech."

申简辰点点头。
Shen Jianchen nodded.

只见林觉上前一步，大声说道：“诸位！你们肩负重任，保护临安百姓的安全，官家尽知，派仪王前来担任节度使一职，足以体现官家对大家十分关怀，现在有请节度使大人给大家训话！”
Lin Jue stepped forward and boomed, "Men! You shoulder a heavy duty, guarding the safety of Lin'an's people. His Majesty knows this well. Sending Prince Yi to serve as our Military Commissioner shows just how much His Majesty cares for every one of you. Now, let us welcome the Lord Military Commissioner to address the ranks!"

林觉退后，申简辰只能上前一步。想到昏庸的官家，他什么话都不愿说，可面对一张张充满期待的面孔，他内心的热情还是被点燃。
Lin Jue stepped back, leaving Shen Jianchen no choice but to step forward. Thinking of that incompetent emperor drained him of any desire to speak, yet facing row after row of expectant faces lit a spark of fire inside him.

“诸位，我是赵朴，官家的十三子仪王，也是临安上任不久的节度使。从今日起，大家要记住，你们既是临安的士兵，也是大宋的士兵。有你们在，大宋终有一日不用再与金人联合攻打辽国，我们靠自己的实力便可夺取燕云十六州！”
"Men, I am Zhao Pu, Prince Yi, thirteenth son of His Majesty, and Lin'an's new Military Commissioner. From this day on, remember this: you are not just soldiers of Lin'an—you are soldiers of the Great Song! With men like you, the day will come when the Song no longer needs to ally with the Jin to strike the Liao. By our own strength alone, we will reclaim the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun!"

“燕云十六州！燕云十六州！燕云十六州！”
"The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun! The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun! The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun!"

申简辰清楚历史不可逆转，金宋联合是步臭棋，可他总不能在庄重的场合说些丧气话。鼓励的话语已说，还需说些警示之语。
Shen Jianchen knew history could not be undone, and the Song-Jin alliance was a disastrous blunder, yet he could not pour cold water on a solemn gathering like this. Now that he had stirred their spirits, he needed to leave them with a word of warning as well.

“金人表面与大宋联合，实则心怀鬼胎，我们要时刻警惕，不要被金人迷惑，用其所用，防其所防。只有不断提高征战沙场的能力，方可保家卫国！”
"On the surface, the Jin ally with the Great Song, yet beneath it they harbor sinister designs. We must stay on our guard at every moment and never let the Jin fool us: use them where they serve us, and guard against them where we must. Only by sharpening our prowess on the battlefield can we protect our homes and defend our nation!"

“保家卫国！保家卫国！保家卫国！”
"Protect our homes and defend our nation! Protect our homes and defend our nation! Protect our homes and defend our nation!"

申简辰说完，退后一步。
Finished speaking, Shen Jianchen took a step back.

林觉又行礼，向前一步：“大家都必须把节度使的话牢牢记在心里，听到没！”
Lin Jue bowed again and stepped forward. "Carve every word of the Military Commissioner's speech into your hearts—do you hear me?!"

“听到！听到！听到！”
"Yes, sir! Yes, sir! Yes, sir!"

“好！继续操练！”
"Good! Back to your drills!"

士兵们又开始操练。
The soldiers resumed their drills.

申简辰在士兵的操练声中，下了验兵台，走出校场。
Amid the clamor of the drilling troops, Shen Jianchen stepped down from the reviewing platform and walked out of the drill ground.

诸军都统制、副都统制和统制三位大人迎面走来。
Three senior officers came striding toward him: the Commander-in-Chief of All Forces, the Vice Commander-in-Chief, and the Division Commander.

申简辰见过他们，心想自己本因有私事要办，不得已来校场一趟，没想到这么快走漏风声，引得他们纷纷而来。
Shen Jianchen had met them before. He had only come to the drill ground because he had private business to settle; who would have guessed word would leak so fast and bring all three rushing over?

三位官员匆忙迎上来，鞠躬行礼。
The three officers hurried up to greet him, bowing low.

诸军都统制带头说：“节度使大人前来，应该让人先打声招呼，我等好提前迎接您，也不至于怠慢了贵人。”
The Commander-in-Chief of All Forces spoke first. "When the Lord Military Commissioner pays a visit, you ought to send word ahead so we can come welcome you in advance and avoid neglecting so honored a guest."

申简辰说：“我也是偶然起意，来练兵署走走，谁知也将你们几个惊动。”
Shen Jianchen said, "I just dropped by the Military Training Office on a whim. I didn't mean to stir up all three of you."

诸军都统制说：“练兵官派人告知我等，我们才匆忙赶到，大人若不嫌弃，可到我等办理公务之处坐坐，那里舒服些，站在这里太辛苦。”
The Commander-in-Chief said, "The Training Officer sent a runner to notify us, so we hurried right over. If Your Lordship doesn't mind, why not step into our headquarters for a seat? It's far more comfortable than standing out here."

申简辰拒绝道：“不了，我今日找练兵官有些私事要谈，改日再到各位办公之地坐坐。”
Shen Jianchen turned them down. "No need. I have some private matters to discuss with the Training Officer today. I'll pay a visit to your headquarters another day."

林觉感到面上有光，就凭仪王这句话，明日三位统制定会找他谈话，看来成为统领是板上钉钉了。
Lin Jue beamed with pride. On the strength of that single remark from Prince Yi, the three senior commanders were bound to summon him tomorrow. The Commandant's post was as good as in the bag.

果不其然，三位统制互相看看，心领神会。
Sure enough, the three commanders exchanged a knowing look.

诸军都统制又说：“既然仪王找练兵官有事，我等也不敢多打搅，只是有一事需向大人禀明，统领的位置暂时空缺，我们三人都有意推举林练兵官，不知大人意下如何？”
The Commander-in-Chief added, "Since Prince Yi has business with the Training Officer, we won't intrude any further. There is just one matter we must report to Your Lordship: the post of Commandant is vacant at the moment, and all three of us intend to recommend Training Officer Lin. What are Your Lordship's thoughts?"

刚猜是明日，没想到这么快！林觉喜出望外，与仪王走得近还是好处多多。
He had just guessed tomorrow; he never dreamed it would happen this fast! Overjoyed, Lin Jue thought how rewarding it was to stay close to Prince Yi.

申简辰瞅着林觉高兴的样子，心想：“没想到我这一来，倒帮他升了官儿，也好，一会儿劝他放弃夏果也好说些。”
Eyeing Lin Jue's delight, Shen Jianchen thought, "Didn't expect my visit to land him a promotion. Fine by me—that'll make it all the easier to talk him into giving up Xiaguo in a moment."

他对三位大人说：“一切由你们定夺。”
He told the three officers, "I leave it all to your discretion."

诸军都统制又带头说：“下官谨遵节度使大人的安排，您忙，我等退下。”
The Commander-in-Chief led the reply once more: "We shall abide by the Lord Military Commissioner's decision. We won't keep you from your business. We take our leave."

三人退去后，申简辰对林觉说：“我的贴身侍卫喜欢吃聚香楼的饭菜，也到饭点儿，林练兵官能否陪我一起去？”
After the three withdrew, Shen Jianchen turned to Lin Jue. "My personal bodyguard loves the food at Juxiang Tower, and it's just about mealtime. Care to join me, Training Officer Lin?"

这等好事林觉岂能拒绝？满口应下：“能陪仪王用饭，下官不胜荣幸！”
How could Lin Jue turn down an honor like that? He agreed without missing a beat. "Dining with Prince Yi is the highest honor this humble officer could ask for!"
