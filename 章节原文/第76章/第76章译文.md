# Chapter 76: Crafting a Dream to Express His Heart

Early in the morning, after getting up, Qiao Wenxian ran straight to Prince Yi's courtyard. How could he give up so easily before sharing his happiness with Prince Yi?

---

As soon as he entered the courtyard, he saw Prince Yi practicing boxing.

---

The exquisite boxing made Qiao Wenxian temporarily forget about sharing his happiness. He stood aside, lost in thought: "Is this truly the same Prince Yi as before? Aside from his appearance, there's not a single point of resemblance. He's been ill for years—when did he have time to practice boxing? Can being brought back to life actually change a person? The world is truly full of wonders."

---

Baifeng was attending the prince nearby. Seeing Young Master Qiao arrive, he bowed and said: "Young Master Qiao."

---

Qiao Wenxian asked: "Why didn't you go to Xisan Hutong today?"

---

"The prince said there's no need to go anymore."

---

"Have you found Qianlixun already?"

---

"Not yet."

---

Qiao Wenxian thought to himself: Perhaps Prince Yi has suddenly come to his senses, realizing that someone met in another world couldn't possibly be found in this one?

---

Shen Jianchen finished his set of boxing and gathered his qi. He knew all too well why Wenyuan had come. What must be faced has to be faced eventually.

---

"I'm having breakfast here this morning." Seeing that Prince Yi had finished practicing, Qiao Wenxian stepped forward and grabbed his arm, then said to Baifeng, "Get Nanshuang and bring our breakfast from the kitchen."

---

"Yes." Baifeng ran off to find Nanshuang. Every day he went to Xisan Hutong, he never had a chance to play with Nanshuang. Starting today, he finally had the time.

---

"I still need to go to the yamen shortly. I'm afraid I can't keep you company for long." Though Shen Jianchen had prepared himself to listen to Wenyuan show off about his romance, he couldn't help but say this.

---

Wenyuan let go of Prince Yi: "Oh? You've actually become diligent about governance. That's a good thing. Don't worry, I won't take up much of your time—just the duration of a breakfast. Later you go back to the yamen, and I'll return to the Qiao residence."

---

"Oh, when you go back, send my regards to your grandfather."

---

"Of course. Do you know why I'm going back?"

---

"How would I know?" Shen Jianchen said as he walked toward the room.

---

Qiao Wenxian followed. Once Prince Yi was seated, he went straight to the tea table and began preparing tea for the two of them: "This time I'm going back to tell my father and mother, asking them to go to the Zhu family to propose marriage."

---

"Propose marriage? Isn't...isn't it a bit too hasty?" Shen Jianchen felt as if his heart had been pricked by a needle, a slight pang of pain, yet his surface remained calm.

---

Qiao Wenxian focused on making tea, not observing Prince Yi: "Not hasty at all. I've liked Shuzhen for several years. Now that she's finally agreed, I need to strike while the iron is hot and marry her back to the Qiao residence quickly. If I delay, I'm afraid someone better might steal her away."

---

Shen Jianchen watched Wenyuan whisking the tea continuously in the cup. Each stroke felt like a blow to his aching heart: "Are you so insecure?"

---

"It's not insecurity—it's that Shuzhen is too outstanding, and too many people like her. She's just recently divorced. Once the news spreads, there will surely be no shortage of people coming to propose marriage. So I need to hurry and inform my parents, let them know about this, go to the Zhu family to settle the engagement, and preferably have the wedding this year."

---

"Wedding? Has Zhu Shuzhen agreed?" Though Shen Jianchen already knew the answer, he couldn't help but ask.

---

"Of course she agreed. Yesterday she even said..." Qiao Wenxian felt too embarrassed to continue.

---

Enduring the heartache, Shen Jianchen pressed: "What else did she say?"

---

Rich tea foam had already risen in the cup. Qiao Wenxian picked it up, placed it in front of Prince Yi, and boldly said: "She also said she wants to have many children with me. She said these things in front of everyone, which made me quite embarrassed."

---

Seeing Wenyuan say "embarrassed" while looking thoroughly pleased, Shen Jianchen picked up his tea cup and drained it in one gulp.

---

Qiao Wenxian said: "Why aren't you tasting it again? That's not fitting for a prince."

---

Baifeng and Nanshuang came in carrying breakfast and set it up for the two of them. Breakfast was simple—a bowl of Seven-treasure Vegetable Porridge, two meat buns, and a tea egg. After setting everything out, the two servants went out to the courtyard to play.

---

Qiao Wenxian sat down and began enjoying his breakfast.

---

Shen Jianchen took a sip of porridge and asked tentatively: "Wenyuan, if someone were to snatch Youqi Jushi away from you, what would you do?"

---

Qiao Wenxian bit into a bun. Hearing this, he hurriedly swallowed: "I...I'm afraid of exactly that happening, which is why I need to propose marriage as soon as possible."

---

Shen Jianchen's question was less a test than a reflection of his true feelings.

---

But given the current situation, he had no hope at all. The food before him had lost all appeal.

---

"I really have no appetite. You enjoy your meal here. I'm heading to the yamen."

---

Before Qiao Wenxian could react, Prince Yi had already risen and walked out of the room, calling out: "Baifeng! Come with me to the yamen!"

---

Qiao Wenxian sighed: "Prince Yi seems troubled. What's wrong with him? Could he be having a spat with Princess Jiahe?"

---

Qiao Wenxian finished breakfast with confusion, then went to Princess Jiahe's boudoir.

---

Nanshuang called inward: "Princess, Young Master Qiao has come to visit."

---

After a moment, Xilan accompanied the princess out.

---

"What brings Young Master Qiao to see me?"

---

"Prince Yi seems to be in a bad mood today. I don't know the reason, so I came to ask."

---

Qiao Mengrong remembered kissing her brother last night. Could it be that her brother was angry because of that kiss?

---

"Thank you for caring about my brother, Young Master Qiao. He's fine—it's just that Mengrong did something wrong. I heard Young Master Qiao went on a date with Youqi Jushi yesterday. How enviable."

---

Qiao Wenxian comforted her: "How could the princess do anything wrong? Even if you did, Prince Yi wouldn't take it to heart. The princess and Prince Yi share mutual affection, plus the Imperial Marriage Decree—it makes Wenyuan extremely envious."

---

Qiao Mengrong reflected on "mutual affection." When her brother was ill, that was indeed the case. Now she felt "mutual affection" seemed to be drifting further and further away from her.

---

Qiao Wenxian noticed something was off with Princess Jiahe and quickly said: "I still have matters to attend to, so I won't disturb you further."

---

"Take care, Young Master Qiao." After Qiao Wenxian left, Qiao Mengrong asked: "Xilan, do you think my brother is angry with me? Disgusted that I kissed him under the Sacred Willow last night?"

---

"The princess overthinks. In this servant's view, the prince would be delighted, not angry."

---

Hearing Xilan's explanation, Qiao Mengrong felt much more at ease.

---

"Then tell me, do outsiders see my brother and me as sharing mutual affection?"

---

Though Xilan could no longer see the light of affection for the princess in the prince's eyes, she still replied: "Of course. The prince will only share mutual affection with the princess. Besides, with the Imperial Marriage Decree, the prince will marry the princess sooner or later."

---

"The Imperial Marriage Decree, yes. What am I still worried about? My brother will marry me sooner or later. But why can't I feel happy? Am I asking for too much?"

---

Xilan couldn't answer the princess's question and could only remain silent.

---

Qiao Wenxian left Youlin Bieju and took a carriage toward the Qiao residence in the city.

---

As soon as he entered the Qiao residence, he headed straight for his father Qiao Shouren's courtyard.

---

Qiao Shouren was sitting in the courtyard tasting tea, with servants standing by to attend him. Seeing his son return, he was very pleased and called out: "Come sit here and taste tea with me."

---

A servant filled Qiao Wenxian's cup. He picked it up and took a sip: "Father is drinking Jiuqu Hongmei."

---

Qiao Shouren praised: "You're truly my son—you understand tea. One sip and you know. Everyone in Lin'an loves drinking West Lake Longjing, but I specifically prefer Jiuqu Hongmei because of its mellow flavor and stomach-warming properties. My stomach isn't good, so drinking a bit of it both quenches thirst and benefits my health."

---

Qiao Wenxian asked: "Is Father's stomach any better?"

---

"Much better. How has Prince Yi been lately?"

---

"He's a prince. Wherever he goes, no one dares to slight him. Naturally, he's doing well."

---

"With you at the villa accompanying Prince Yi, I can rest completely at ease."

---

After enough small talk, Qiao Wenxian wanted to speak his mind directly but felt too embarrassed.

---

He suddenly remembered hearing about the old story of Master Defu (Zhao Mingcheng) and Yian Jushi (Li Qingzhao) when he went to Laizhou, and inspiration struck.

---

When Master Defu (Zhao Mingcheng) was young, to express his feelings and prompt his father to propose marriage to the Li family, he fabricated a dream containing three sentences, each holding a word riddle. Why not borrow this idea?

---

"Father, last night I had a dream. In it, a divine being told me: 'Wenyuan, it's time for you to marry.' I thought about it—he's right. I've reached the age for marriage. So I asked the divine being: 'Do you know whom I'll marry?' The divine being smiled and said three sentences. I can't quite figure them out at the moment. Perhaps Father could help me guess."

---

Qiao Shouren had been worrying about his son's marriage selection. Hearing it was a dream about marriage, he immediately became enthusiastic: "Tell me about it."

---

Seeing his father had taken the bait, Qiao Wenxian was quite satisfied. He spoke while watching his father's expression: "The divine being said: 'Speech combined with office; peace with its roof removed; herbs stripped of their green heads.'"

---
