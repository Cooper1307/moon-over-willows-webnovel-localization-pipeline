## Chapter 273: Matching Wits with Granduncle Lu at the Gate

Granduncle Lu didn't buy a word. "Covering for your wife is one thing, Haoyu—taking the rap for everything is pathetic! Never mind last year—just yesterday somebody handed out free pamphlets trashing her all over town! My own servant brought one home! What decent woman spends her days mooning over poems and courting scandal? Disgraceful!"

Knowing full well Haoyu *had* spread last year's rumors, Haodong kept his mouth glued shut and enjoyed the show, silently guessing Princess Consort Yi stood behind yesterday's pamphlets—having forgotten every warning from his passing acquaintance Yan Chi.

"What pamphlets?! Who's trashing my wife?!" Haoyu bristled, pivoting to storm off to the Judicial Office.

"No need to report it—someone already handled it!" Chen Wenxin's crisp voice broke in. Sent by Zhu Xiyan to warn Shuzhen to quit writing poetry, she had watched the showdown from the curb before stepping in.

"Sis! Perfect timing—calm Granduncle Lu down!" Haoyu cheered.

Greeting her sister-in-law, Chen Wenxin tried steering the elder indoors, only for Granduncle Lu to brush her off like poured-out water—"Keep out of Chen family business!"

Stepping smoothly in front of her furious sister-in-law, Qianli Xun smiled coldly. "Perfect—let's settle this right here, Granduncle Lu. Claiming I ruined Haoyu's shot at the inheritance is sheer comedy! Even if he wanted it—which he doesn't—his wild reputation before we ever got engaged ruled him out long ago. Grandmother and my in-laws chose me knowing full well those rumors were hogwash—are you calling Old Madam Xia and my father-in-law fools? I married in to live a quiet life; neither Haoyu nor I cares a copper about inheriting the Chen empire!"

"Spot on—couldn't care less!" Haoyu backed her right up.

Flashing Haoyu an approving grin, Qianli Xun added, "Since we don't care, stop waving the inheritance in our faces—keep harping on it and folks might think Haoyu's eyeing the prize and stir up resentment."

Catching the needle aimed straight at him, Haodong sneered inwardly yet relished hearing them renounce the prize.

Glancing at Haodong, Granduncle Lu grunted, "Glad you two know your place. Fine—if Grandmother accepts you, I won't meddle in Jianglin's branch. Still!" He jabbed a finger at the six kneeling girls. "Standing around gawking while your husband's women weep at the gate—explain *that*!"

Time to cure this old busybody of meddling. "Private household matter—better stay out of it, Granduncle."

"I'm a clan elder! I have every right to meddle!"

*Walked right into it!* "Then since Granduncle insists on pitching in, how about pitching in for real?" Stepping before the six girls, Qianli Xun sized them up. "Haoyu acted like a scoundrel—honestly he deserves a thousand cuts." (Haoyu stuck out his tongue silently.) "I'd even let him make you concubines, only he flat-out refuses and I won't force him. So tell me—aside from being his concubines, what path do you want? Whatever's in my power, I'll make happen."

Their tears dried up fast; clearly concubinage was dead. One girl spoke up: "We're terrified nobody will marry us now. If Second Young Madam finds us decent husbands, we swear we'll never bother you again!"

"What sort of husbands? Age, wealth, background?"

"If his family's rich, we won't be picky about age," another replied promptly. "If he's ordinary, then around our age and sturdy!" Every head nodded in unison.

"Brilliant!" Qianli Xun pivoted straight onto Granduncle Lu. "Hear that, Granduncle? One, two, three, four, five, six—take your pick! Concubines or wives, you've got plenty of silver so they won't mind your age! As an elder stepping in to help juniors, solve a few for us right now!"

Haoyu bit down hard on a laugh; Chen Wenxin burst out snickering; even Haodong had to look away hiding a grin while Granduncle Lu turned purple, sputtering, "You—you—humph!" and stormed straight into Chen Manor without waiting for an arm.

Haodong hurried after him, satisfied enough: his paid agitators had made his sister-in-law burn every bridge with Granduncle Lu—and his second trap was already ticking.

Chen Wenxin flashed a thumbs-up. "Masterful!"

"Your brother's the real master—dumped a giant mess in my lap!"

Poking Haoyu's forehead, Wenxin sighed, "All your old stunts, and Shuzhen's stuck cleaning up!" Too mortified to squeak, Haoyu stared at his boots.

Turning back to the six girls, Qianli Xun promised, "I'll find you all decent husbands—stop causing scenes. Get up before folks think I'm bullying servants." Thanking her warmly, the six rose and dispersed.

After Chen Wenxin swore Butler Wang and the gate servants to silence so Old Madam Xia wouldn't fret, she led Shuzhen indoors—leaving Haoyu trailing gloomily behind, slapping his own cheek in disgust as the door of Suxin Thatched Hall clicked shut right in his nose.

Inside, Kuqiao stepped out to whisk tea while Qianli Xun pressed Wenxin about the pamphlets.

"Father-in-law heard Prince Yi sent Commander Lin and the Judicial Office to sweep the streets, burn the pamphlets, and detain the printer—though they let them go right away."

Relief tangled with cold dread in Qianli Xun's stomach: Mengrong would only hate her twice as hard once she learned Brother burned her booklets!

"Father-in-law told me to warn you to quit writing poems," Wenxin added gently.

"Quit writing and people out to get me will just invent new excuses."

"Sounds like you know who printed them?"

"How would I know? Did my father find out—or did Lin Jue tell him?"

"Neither—Lin Jue claimed he knew nothing."

Qianli Xun exhaled, relieved her family stayed in the dark about Mengrong's murderous grudge.

"Strange," Wenxin mused, "ever since you drifted apart from Princess Consort Yi, Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo have been practically glued to her—well, Qiaos are imperial kin, and Lin Jue wants a ladder up. Still, funny seeing you so mad at Haoyu today!"

"I'm always mad at him!"

"Not like this! Back when he lived in brothels you didn't blink—today six maids made you slam the door in his face! Means you care about him now. Look at who he is *today*, Shuzhen—he's changing for you."

Qianli Xun nodded quietly, then pitched her fix for the six maids. "Could you have my brother ask Lin Jue to match those six girls with unmarried soldiers under his command? I can't approach Lin Jue myself without setting off Bai Meiruo, and Haoyu has zero pull with him—whereas Lin Jue won't refuse Prefect Zhu's son."

"Scheming to use Zhu connections to clean up your husband's mess?" Wenxin laughed brightly. "Since you're sticking up for my little brother, I'll hold a knife to your brother's throat if I have to till it's done!"
