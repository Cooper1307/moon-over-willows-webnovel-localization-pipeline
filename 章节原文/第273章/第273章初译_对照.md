第273章 大门外巧怼陆爷
## Chapter 273: Outwitting Granduncle Lu at the Gate

谁知陈陆根本不相信：“浩宇，我知道你疼媳妇，也不至于什么事都往自己身上揽。去年的事不提也罢，就昨日，还有人在街上免费发放书卷，我的下人拿回来一卷，上面全是骂她的话。一个女人家做什么不好？偏偏没事写诗作词，矫揉造作，招惹是非，实在不雅！”
陈浩东明明知道谣言是浩宇散播，并不帮着说话，而是一副看热闹的样子。
陆爷提到书卷时，他猜测应是仪王妃派人做下的事，她连除掉朱淑真的心都有，什么事做不出来？陈浩东心中更喜，早已把那位萍水相逢的颜兄交代的话忘到九霄云外。
陈浩宇急了：“什么书卷？为何要骂我娘子？”
千里寻不愿大家关注书卷之事：“书卷之事不必理会，不过是别有用心之人诋毁我而已。”
“不行！我不能让娘子的声誉再被人诋毁，现在就去报官！”
陈浩宇说着便打算去司理院。
传来姐姐陈文心的声音：“不必报官！此事已有人出面解决！”
原来朱晞颜已知书卷之事，让陈文心前来劝说女儿往后不要再写诗作词。陈文心刚到，正好瞧见眼前的一幕，在一旁看了一会儿，才开口说话。
陈浩宇见到姐姐甚是开心：“姐，你来得正好，快劝劝陆爷，别让他为难淑真。”
千里寻上前行礼：“让嫂嫂见笑。”
陈文心扶住淑真：“你啊，何必跟老人家计较？”
陈文心放开淑真，走到陈陆面前，行完礼说道：“陆爷，您别跟淑真计较，我扶您进去。”
陈陆并不给面子，嫁出去的姑娘泼出去的水，他根本不会当回事：“二少夫人眼中没我，我自然要跟她计较，你还是靠边站，不要管陈家的事。”
陈文心气不打一处来，想骂两句，被千里寻拦住。
Chen Lu refused to believe a word of it. “Haoyu, I know you dote on your wife, yet you needn’t take the blame for everything onto yourself. Never mind last year’s mess—just yesterday people were handing out free pamphlets on the street! My servant brought one home, and every page was packed with abuse aimed at her. Of all things a woman could do, why must she sit around writing poetry and ci lyrics, putting on pretentious airs and stirring up scandals? How indecent!”

“嫂嫂，我来。”
Chen Haodong knew full well Haoyu had started the old rumors himself, yet he held his tongue and stood back to enjoy the show.

陈文心不再阻拦，不如让淑真替她好好教训一下陆爷。
When Granduncle Lu mentioned the pamphlets, Haodong guessed Princess Consort Yi’s people were behind it. A woman ruthless enough to want Zhu Shuzhen dead would stop at nothing. Delight bubbled inside him, and the warning from his passing acquaintance Brother Yan flew straight out of his mind.

千里寻上前说：“正好，我也想跟陆爷计较。那我就跟您掰扯掰扯。您说我影响陈浩宇继承陈家产业，真是荒唐至极！且不说陈浩宇并无此心，即使有，凭他以前的做派陈家长辈也瞧不上。那时朱陈两家还未订婚，此事与我何干？后来老太太与我公婆愿意定下这门亲事，足以说明他们是明理之人，知道外面关于我的传言子虚乌有，难道陆爷连老太太和我公婆也要怀疑？我嫁到陈家只想安安稳稳过日子，至于陈浩宇是否继承陈家产业，我并不在乎。”
Chen Haoyu bristled. “What pamphlets? Why are they slandering my wife?”

陈浩宇立即应和道：“我与娘子一样，都不在乎。”
Unwilling to draw any more eyes to the pamphlets, Qianli Xun cut in. “Ignore them. Just someone with an ax to grind trying to smear me.”

千里寻对陈浩宇露出满意的笑容：“既然我们都不在乎，又何来他娶了我更别想继承陈家产业的说法？还请陆爷收回此想法，千万不要再提，只怕您提多了，有人会以为陈浩宇觊觎陈家产业，心中不满。”
“No way! I won’t let anyone trash my wife’s reputation again—I’m going straight to report this to the authorities!” Turning on his heel, Chen Haoyu made to head for the Judicial Office.

陈浩东听出这是内涵他，很是不屑。不过弟妹刚才说的话他甚是舒心，最好别觊觎陈家产业，没什么好果子吃。又一想谁知她是不是口是心非？还是继续听陆爷如何教训她。
His sister Chen Wenxin’s voice rang out. “No need to report it! Someone has already stepped in and handled it!”

陈陆瞧一眼身旁的陈浩东，才说道：“你们两口子有自知之明再好不过，老太太都不嫌弃你，我又何必管江林房中的事？只是替浩宇惋惜啊。”
Zhu Xiyan had caught wind of the pamphlets and sent Chen Wenxin over to warn his daughter to quit writing poetry. Arriving right in the middle of the standoff, she had watched from the sidelines for a moment before speaking up.

陈浩宇趁机说：“陆爷不必替我惋惜，娶到淑真我高兴着呢。”
Relief lit Chen Haoyu’s face. “Sister, thank goodness you’re here! Hurry and talk Granduncle Lu down before he gives Shuzhen any more grief.”

“你既护着媳妇，不认为她拖你的后腿，我也不好说什么，此事到此为止。不过……”陈陆扫视一遍跪在地上的丫鬟，“你作为浩宇的娘子，对她们置之不理，在一旁看热闹，又作何解释？”
Qianli Xun stepped forward with a curtsy. “I’m sorry you had to witness this spectacle, Sister-in-law.”

千里寻觉得这位陆爷真是多管闲事，不如打击打击他，让他明白爱管闲事不是什么好习惯。
Chen Wenxin caught her elbow. “Really, why bother locking horns with an old man?” Letting go of Shuzhen, she walked over to Chen Lu, curtsied, and offered her arm. “Granduncle Lu, don’t hold a grudge against Shuzhen. Let me help you inside.”

“此乃我的家事，陆爷最好别管。”
To Chen Lu, a married-out daughter was spilled water, not worth his regard. He brushed her off. “Second Young Madam shows me zero respect, so of course I’m taking issue with her. Step aside and keep your nose out of Chen family affairs.”

陈陆被成功气到：“我乃族中长辈！晚辈的家事我也管得！”
Fury flared in Chen Wenxin’s chest. She opened her mouth to snap back, only for Qianli Xun to stop her.

此话正中下怀，千里寻立即说：“陆爷既然要管，不如来点儿实惠的？”
“Leave this to me, Sister-in-law.”

陈陆不解：“什么意思？”
Chen Wenxin stepped back—better to let Shuzhen give the old coot a proper tongue-lashing on her behalf.

千里寻没有立即回答，走到几位跪着的丫鬟面前，将她们个个瞧一遍：“陈浩宇以前做事荒唐，冒犯你们是他不对，依我的意思，他就应该被千刀万剐……”
Qianli Xun stepped up to face him. “Suits me fine—I’ve got a bone to pick with Granduncle Lu too, so let’s lay it all out. Claiming I ruined Chen Haoyu’s chances of inheriting the Chen businesses is pure nonsense! Even setting aside the fact that Chen Haoyu has zero interest in it, with his old track record the elders wouldn’t have looked twice at him anyway. Back then the Zhus and Chens weren’t even betrothed—what did any of that have to do with me? Later, Old Madam and my parents-in-law agreed to this match because they’re sensible people who knew the gossip about me was groundless. Is Granduncle Lu saying he doubts Old Madam and my in-laws too? I married into the Chen house to live a quiet life. Whether Chen Haoyu inherits the family business or not couldn’t matter less to me.”

娘子的话吓得陈浩宇吐吐舌头，不敢言语，还是继续听娘子说些什么。
Chen Haoyu chimed in at once. “Same here—I couldn’t care less either.”

“我也想让他纳你们为妾，可他不乐意，我又不愿为难他。你们说，除了给他做妾，还愿意选择哪条路？只要在我能力范围之内，自然尽心尽力帮忙解决。”
Qianli Xun shot him a pleased smile. “Since neither of us cares, where does this talk about his marrying me ruining his inheritance come from? Please take that notion back and quit bringing it up, Granduncle Lu. Keep harping on it, and folks might get the wrong idea that Chen Haoyu is eyeing the family business and grow resentful.”

几位丫鬟面面相觑，早已哭得差不多，刚刚流过的眼泪也被擦干，头脑清楚许多。很显然想给陈公子做妾已不可能，不如图点别的。
Chen Haodong caught the jab aimed straight at him and scoffed under his breath. Still, his sister-in-law’s words pleased him—good, let them stay clear of the inheritance, because fighting him for it wouldn’t end well. Then again, who knew if she meant a word she said? Better keep listening to Granduncle Lu put her in her place.

一位丫鬟带头说：“我们几个最担心以后嫁不出去，若少夫人能帮着解决终身大事，我们保证以后再也不会前来闹事。”
Chen Lu shot a sideways glance at Chen Haodong before answering. “Good that the two of you know your place. If Old Madam doesn’t look down on you, why should I meddle in Jianglin’s branch? I just feel it’s a pity for Haoyu.”

千里寻继续问：“你们对未来夫君有什么要求？比如年纪、家世等等。”
Chen Haoyu jumped right in. “Save your pity, Granduncle Lu. I’m thrilled to be married to Shuzhen.”

另一位丫鬟带头说：“若家中不缺银子，其他不计较。若家境一般，还需与我们年纪相仿，力气大些。”
“Since you’re determined to shield your wife and don’t think she’s dragging you down, I’ve nothing more to say—we’ll drop it.” Chen Lu’s eyes swept over the kneeling maids. “Still, as Haoyu’s wife, how do you explain standing back and enjoying the show while ignoring these girls?”

其他丫鬟无不点头认可。
What a busybody, Qianli Xun thought. Time to knock him down a peg and teach him not to poke his nose where it didn’t belong. “This is my household business, Granduncle Lu. Best stay out of it.”

“如此甚好！”千里寻面向陈陆，“陆爷听到了吧？我现在帮着夫君解决难题，陆爷作为长辈喜欢管小辈的闲事，不如帮个忙？”
Right on cue, Chen Lu bristled. “I am a clan elder! I have every right to meddle in a junior’s household affairs!”

陈陆依然十分疑惑：“什么意思？”
Walked straight into the trap. Qianli Xun smiled. “Since Granduncle Lu insists on stepping in, why not offer something practical?”

千里寻故意把几个丫鬟数一遍：“一、二、三、四、五、六，共六人，陆爷随便挑，帮着解决几人，做妾也好，登堂入室也好，反正陆爷不缺银子，她们不会计较你的年纪，作为长辈帮晚辈解决难题是应该的，也算闲事没白管！”
Chen Lu blinked. “What do you mean?”

娘子捉弄陆爷，陈浩宇觉得好玩儿，可面对长辈他不好笑出声来。
Instead of answering him right away, Qianli Xun walked up to the kneeling maids and looked each one over. “Chen Haoyu acted like a fool in the past, and wronging you was his fault. If it were up to me, he’d deserve death by a thousand cuts...”

陈文心觉得解气，噗嗤笑了一声。
Hearing that, Chen Haoyu stuck his tongue out in fright and kept his mouth shut to hear what came next.

就连陈浩东也被逗乐，长辈是他请来的，总不能笑他，故意看向一边缓解缓解情绪。
“I’d even let him take you as concubines,” she went on, “except he refuses, and I’m not about to force him. Tell me: aside from becoming his concubines, what other path would you choose? Anything within my power, I’ll do my best to arrange.”

陈陆被气得说不出话：“你……你……，哼！”
The maids exchanged glances. By now their crying had tapered off, their cheeks were dry, and their heads were clear. Becoming Young Master Chen’s concubines was off the table; might as well angle for the next best thing.

不用陈浩东扶，陈陆自顾转身往陈府里走。
One maid spoke up for the group. “What worries us most is never finding husbands. If Young Madam can arrange good marriages for us, we swear we’ll never come back to make trouble.”

陈浩东忍着笑快步跟上。
Qianli Xun asked, “What do you look for in a future husband? Age, family background, that sort of thing?”

这些丫鬟能来闹事，是陈浩东私下派人蛊惑又塞些银子的，才有了今日的热闹。
Another maid spoke up. “If his family has silver, nothing else matters. If his family is ordinary, he should be close to our age and strong.”

丫鬟们算是起到作用，起码弟妹得罪了陆爷，浩宇变得再好，没有陆爷支持，许多长辈也会放弃支持他。
Every other maid nodded in agreement.

唯一令他不满的是，看上去浩宇的娘子已想到解决难题的办法。没关系，他还有下一招，走着瞧！
“Splendid!” Qianli Xun pivoted toward Chen Lu. “Heard that, Granduncle Lu? I’m right in the middle of solving my husband’s headache. Since you’re so fond of meddling in your juniors’ affairs, why not lend a hand?”

陈文心竖起大拇指：“你真厉害！佩服佩服！”
Chen Lu stayed baffled. “What are you getting at?”

面对嫂嫂的夸奖，千里寻根本高兴不起来：“还是你弟弟最厉害，招惹来这么大的麻烦事，还得我出面解决。”
Qianli Xun pointed and counted them off one by one. “One, two, three, four, five, six—six girls in all. Take your pick, Granduncle Lu! Settle a few of them yourself, whether as concubines or proper wives. You’re never short on silver, so they won’t mind your age. An elder helping a junior out of a jam is only right—and that way all your meddling won’t go to waste!”

陈文心上前用手指在陈浩宇的额头上点了一下：“你啊，以前净不干好事，现在还得淑真替你擦屁股。”
Watching his wife tease Granduncle Lu made Chen Haoyu fight to keep a straight face out of respect for his elder.

陈浩宇肠子都快悔青，又不知该如何安慰娘子，此时任何话都毫无意义，只能沉默不语。
Chen Wenxin let out an amused snort of pure satisfaction. Even Chen Haodong bit back a chuckle, turning his head away to keep from laughing at the elder he himself had invited.

千里寻对六位丫鬟说：“我答应帮你们解决终身大事，以后不许再来闹事，听到没？”
Chen Lu spluttered, purple in the face. “You—you—hmph!”

丫鬟们齐声回道：“听到了！”
Without waiting for Chen Haodong’s arm, he spun around and stalked into Chen Manor. Chen Haodong suppressed his grin and hurried after him.

“起来吧！再跪下去，别人还以为我欺负下人。”
It was Chen Haodong who had slipped those maids silver and goaded them into making a scene today. They had done their job well enough: at the very least, his sister-in-law had alienated Granduncle Lu, and without Granduncle Lu’s backing, plenty of other elders would drop Haoyu no matter how much he reformed. The only snag was that Haoyu’s wife already looked close to sorting out the mess. No matter—he had another move waiting up his sleeve.

丫鬟们拜谢后，都起身离开。
Chen Wenxin gave her a thumbs-up. “Brilliant! Hats off to you!”

千里寻拉起嫂嫂：“嫂嫂，到我屋里坐坐，陪我说说话。”
Qianli Xun could not summon a smile. “Your brother’s the brilliant one—stirring up a mess this big and leaving me to clean it up.”

陈文心说：“等等，我去给他们叮嘱一番。”
Chen Wenxin reached out and poked Chen Haoyu’s forehead. “You—never did a shred of good before, and now Shuzhen has to wipe your backside for you.”

陈文心走到王管家等人身边：“今日之事奶奶与各位老爷可知？”
Regret gnawed at Chen Haoyu’s guts, yet no words could soothe his wife right now. He kept his mouth shut.

王管家回道：“并不知。”
Qianli Xun turned to the six maids. “I’ve given my word to arrange marriages for you. No more coming here to make scenes, understood?”

“不知甚好，你们在府里不要乱说话，听到没？”
“Understood!” they chorused.

“大小姐放心，我等有分寸。”
“Get up, then. Keep kneeling and people will think I’m bullying the help.”

陈文心这才放心地拉起淑真的手进入府内。
The maids thanked her and rose to leave.

陈浩宇被落在后面，心情落寞。经过今日一事，他的努力全部泡汤，以后想让娘子心中有他，恐怕难上加难。
Qianli Xun tugged her sister-in-law’s sleeve. “Come sit in my room and chat with me, Sister-in-law.”

他在自己脸上扇了一巴掌，懊悔又有什么用？还是跟上娘子，找机会再讨好她。
“Hold on—let me have a word with them first.” Chen Wenxin walked over to Butler Wang and the gatekeepers. “Do Grandmother and the Masters know about today’s scene?”

进入墨香苑后，陈浩宇想与她们一起走进素心草堂，结果脚还未踏入，门“砰”的一声被关上。他向后退了一步，失落地向后院走去，还是等姐姐走后，再向娘子请罪。
“No, Miss,” Butler Wang answered.

丫鬟们的事让千里寻肚子里窝着火，顺手将陈浩宇关在门外。
“Keep it that way. Not a loose word inside the manor, hear me?”

陈文心不请自坐，笑道：“你啊，进步不少。”
“Rest assured, Eldest Miss. We know our place.”

“嫂嫂这话说得好不应景，我正生气着呢！”没瞧见苦荞，千里寻喊道：“苦荞！你在吗？”
Reassured, Chen Wenxin took Shuzhen’s hand and walked inside.

苦荞正在里屋忙着收拾屋子，听到小姐的呼唤，从里面出来：“小姐今儿回来得早？少夫人也来了，我给您做茶。”
Left trailing behind, Chen Haoyu felt his spirits plummet. All his hard work over the past weeks had gone up in smoke; winning a place in his wife’s heart would be ten times harder now. He slapped his own cheek—what good did regret do? Better trail after her and look for a chance to get back in her good graces.

千里寻唤苦荞本也是此意，苦荞既然心领神会，她也没什么好吩咐的。
Inside Moxiang Courtyard, he made to follow them into Suxin Thatched Hall, only for the door to slam right in his face. He took a step back and trudged off toward the rear courtyard, resigned to apologizing once his sister left.

心里纠结书卷之事，还是向嫂嫂问个明白。
Still simmering over the maids, Qianli Xun had shut him out on instinct.

她走到嫂嫂身边：“刚在大门外，嫂嫂说书卷之事已有人出面解决，不知谁这么好心？”
Chen Wenxin sat down without waiting to be asked and laughed. “My, you’ve come a long way.”

“你坐下，听我慢慢给你说。”
“Not the time for jokes, Sister-in-law—I’m fuming!” Not seeing her maid, Qianli Xun called out, “Kuqiao! Are you there?”

千里寻坐下后，用期待的目光盯着嫂嫂。
Busy tidying the inner room, Kuqiao stepped out at her call. “Back so soon, Miss? Young Madam is here too—I’ll whisk some tea right away.”

“听公爹说，仪王派林统制和司理院的人一起将大部分书卷收回，一把火给烧了，还抓了书局的管事和分发书卷的人，他们也没犯什么大事，很快又给放了。”
That was all Qianli Xun wanted; since Kuqiao read her mind, there was nothing more to order. Still uneasy over the pamphlets, she walked over to her sister-in-law. “Outside the gate, you said someone already dealt with the pamphlets. Who was kind enough to step in?”

原来是仪王，千里寻感激的同时，根本高兴不起来，只怕梦容知道破坏计划的人是她的哥哥，更不会原谅她。
“Sit down and I’ll tell you the whole thing.”

“你怎么了？”淑真没有露出高兴的神情，是意料之外的事，陈文心不明所以，继续说，“公爹让我转告你，以后别再写诗作词。”
Qianli Xun sat and watched her in anticipation.

千里寻失落地说：“即使我不写诗作词，想找我麻烦的人还会有其他办法，挡也挡不住。”
“Father-in-law told me Prince Yi sent Commander Lin and men from the Judicial Office to round up most of the pamphlets and burn the lot. They arrested the bookshop manager and the men handing them out too, though since it wasn’t a major crime, they released them soon after.”

“听这话，你应该知道书卷之事与何人有关？”
Prince Yi again. Gratitude mixed with dread in Qianli Xun’s chest—if Mengrong learned her own brother had wrecked her scheme, she would hate her even more.

千里寻掩饰道：“我怎么会知道？要是知道还能让那人为非作歹？”
“What’s wrong?” Surprised that Shuzhen did not look cheered at all, Chen Wenxin added, “Father-in-law also told me to tell you to stop writing poetry and ci lyrics from now on.”

“也是，你的拳头甚是厉害，要是知道还不得把那人打得屁滚尿流。”陈文心说完，又觉不对，“仪王已审讯过书局的管事，应该知道是谁人所为，为何没有把那人抓起来？”
Qianli Xun’s shoulders slumped. “Even if I quit writing, anyone out to make my life miserable will find another way. There’s no stopping them.”

千里寻担忧地问：“我爹可知是谁人所为？”
“Sounds like you know who’s behind those pamphlets.”

“公爹并不知，他也不好直接问仪王。”
Qianli Xun covered up at once. “How could I know? If I knew, would I just sit back and let them run wild?”

“林觉亲自去抓的，肯定知道些什么，难道他没对我爹说起？”
“True—with those fists of yours, you’d have beaten them silly.” Chen Wenxin paused, a thought crossing her mind. “Wait. Prince Yi questioned the bookshop manager, so he must know who ordered them printed. Why didn’t he arrest whoever it was?”

“公爹问过林统制，他只说一概不知。”
Qianli Xun asked in alarm, “Does Father know who did it?”

千里寻松口气，她不想家人知道梦容因仪王对她充满怨恨，甚至想让她消失。自己惹的事，不必家人替她担忧。
“No, and he couldn’t well ask Prince Yi straight out.”

“我怎么感觉你好像很担心公爹知道是谁？”
“Lin Jue made the arrests himself, so he must know something. Didn’t he tell Father?”

千里寻继续掩饰：“哪有？嫂嫂不要瞎想。”
“Father-in-law asked Commander Lin, and he claimed he knew nothing.”

陈文心突然说道：“自从你与仪王妃疏远，苗知瑜和白梅若倒与仪王妃来往密切。也能理解，乔家本来就是皇亲国戚，不来往才不正常。林统制则希望利用他娘子与贵人走近，好继续往上爬。”
Qianli Xun breathed out in relief. She did not want her family finding out that Mengrong despised her over Prince Yi—enough to wish her dead. Troubles of her own making were not for her family to shoulder.

嫂嫂的话令千里寻担心不已，难道嫂嫂知道了什么？
“Why do I get the feeling you’re terrified Father-in-law might find out who it is?”

“嫂嫂怎么突然提起此事？”
“Not at all! Stop imagining things, Sister-in-law.”

“不过是随口说说。”
Chen Wenxin switched tack out of the blue. “Ever since you drifted apart from Princess Consort Yi, Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo have grown thick with her. Makes sense, I suppose—the Qiaos are imperial kin, so it’d be stranger if they didn’t socialize. And Commander Lin clearly wants to use his wife to get closer to the royals and climb higher.”

千里寻松口气。
Qianli Xun’s heart skipped a beat. Did her sister-in-law know something? “Why bring that up all of a sudden?”

“想想去年仪王妃还是郡主时与你那个亲密劲儿，真是羡煞旁人，谁知说变就变，都怪……”
“Just passing chatter.”

陈文心话到嘴边，不敢吐出那两个字，毕竟人家是贵人，背后埋怨贵人是大不敬。
Qianli Xun exhaled.

“嫂嫂不要再提及此事，已经过去，应该向前看。”千里寻说这话，与其说是宽慰嫂嫂，不如说是宽慰自己。
“To think how close you and the Princess Consort were last year when she was still Princess Jiahe—everyone envied you two! Who knew things could sour overnight, all because of...” Chen Wenxin swallowed the last two words; complaining about royals behind their backs was asking for trouble.

陈文心笑道：“是得向前看，你今儿生浩宇的气，挺让人意外。”
“Drop it, Sister-in-law. What’s past is past—we have to look forward.” Saying it comforted herself as much as her sister-in-law.

“我生他的气，是常有之事，嫂嫂何来意外？”
Chen Wenxin chuckled. “Look forward, yes! Still, seeing you lose your temper with Haoyu today took me by surprise.”

“不一样，以前你何时因其他女子生过他的气？刚嫁过来时，他整日待在青楼，你跟个没事人似的。现在倒好，几个丫鬟就搞得你把他关在门外，说明你开始在乎他。”
“I get mad at him all the time. What’s surprising about that?”

嫂嫂的一番话，更让千里寻明白她的确开始在乎陈浩宇。
“It’s different! When did you ever get mad at him over other women before? Right after the wedding he spent all day in brothels and you didn’t bat an eye. Now a handful of maids has you slamming the door in his face—that means you’re starting to care about him.”

“淑真，你别在乎浩宇以前的事儿，多看看现在的他，他为你改变，证明他真心待你，也说明他本质上并不坏。”
Her sister-in-law’s words drove the truth home: she really was starting to care about Chen Haoyu.

嫂嫂的劝解之语不无道理，千里寻点点头表示认可。 丫鬟的事还未解决，不如求嫂嫂帮忙。
“Don’t dwell on Haoyu’s past, Shuzhen. Look at who he is now. Changing for your sake proves he’s sincere—and shows he’s not bad at heart.”

“嫂嫂，我有一个办法可以解决几个丫鬟的终身大事，你能不能帮我个忙？”
Unable to fault the logic, Qianli Xun nodded. The six maids still needed husbands, so she might as well ask her sister-in-law for help. “Sister-in-law, I’ve got a way to settle those maids’ marriages. Could you do me a favor?”

“跟我还客气，尽管说。”
“No need to stand on ceremony with me. Name it.”

“林觉手底下的兵不少，应该有很多人没成亲，总会有人不嫌弃那些丫鬟，我想请他出面帮忙解决此事。碍于我跟他以前的关系，不好亲自去见他，更怕见了被白梅若知道搞得他家宅不宁。恳请嫂嫂帮我转告哥哥，让他出面，给林觉说说。”
“Lin Jue commands plenty of soldiers, and lots of them must still be unmarried. Some of them won’t mind those maids’ pasts, so I want to ask him to help match them up. Given my history with him, I can’t go see him myself—and if Bai Meiruo caught wind of it, she’d raise a storm in his house. Could you ask my brother to step in and put in a word with Lin Jue?”

“为何不让浩宇去？”
“Why not send Haoyu?”

“他跟林觉没什么交情，恐怕林觉不会给他面子。相反由哥哥出面，他多少会顾及知府大人的面子，肯定不会果断拒绝。”
“He has no pull with Lin Jue—Lin Jue wouldn’t give him the time of day. If my brother goes instead, Lin Jue will at least respect Father’s position as Prefect and won’t turn him down flat.”

“你倒谋算得好，用朱家人的关系替自己夫君擦屁股。”
“Clever little scheme—using the Zhu family’s connections to clean up your husband’s mess.”

“陈浩宇也是嫂嫂的弟弟，难道嫂嫂不希望他好？”
“Chen Haoyu is your younger brother too! Don’t you want things to go well for him?”

“呵呵呵……”陈文心笑得很开心，“看在你如此为浩宇考虑，就是拿刀架在脖子上，也要逼你哥哥把这件事情办成功。”
Chen Wenxin laughed in delight. “Seeing how hard you’re looking out for Haoyu, I’d hold a knife to your brother’s throat if I had to make him pull this off!”
