## Chapter 5: Love at First Sight, No Easy Divorce

林觉之所以会到朱府提亲，只因偶然一次参加临安知府朱晞颜，正是他现任的岳父大人，举办的赏花钓鱼宴时，与朱淑真有过一面之缘。自此，她的美貌就像刻在他的心里，挥之不去。
Lin Jue had gone to Zhu Manor to propose for one simple reason: at a Flower-Viewing and Fishing Banquet hosted by Prefect of Lin'an Zhu Xiyan—now his father-in-law—he had caught a single glimpse of Zhu Shuzhen. From that day on, her beauty etched itself into his heart and refused to fade.

记得那是一年的春天，百花盛开。朱晞颜差人给族叔林子孝送来请帖，邀请他参加赏花钓鱼宴。林子孝的诗画虽谈不上震惊临安，但在临安城文人中也占有一席之地。而朱晞颜作为临安知府也是位好诗词歌赋的文人，要不怎么会把女儿培养成为女词人。
That spring, every flower in the city had burst into bloom. Zhu Xiyan sent an invitation to his clan uncle Lin Zixiao for the Flower-Viewing and Fishing Banquet. Though Lin Zixiao's poetry and painting did not shake all of Lin'an, he held a respected place among the city's literati. And Prefect Zhu himself loved verse—how else could he have raised his daughter into a renowned female ci poet?

林子孝接到请帖时，林觉正在一旁，便请族叔带他到赏花钓鱼宴上走一遭，体验一下文人风骚。林子孝说，像这种场合临安没有名头的文人根本没有机会参加，何况林觉只是个武夫。谁让林子孝有面子，能让知府大人瞧得上，带个把人去应该没问题。
Lin Jue happened to be beside his clan uncle when the invitation arrived and begged to tag along for a taste of scholarly elegance. Lin Zixiao told him lesser-known scholars could not even get through the gate, let alone a military man. Still, Lin Zixiao had enough face with the Prefect to bring one guest along.

就这样林觉参加了赏花钓鱼宴。这种宴会，除了赏花、钓鱼、品茶、听曲儿、宴饮外，最重要的就是赋诗作词，展示文人才华。
So Lin Jue attended the banquet. Aside from viewing blossoms, fishing, sipping tea, listening to music, and feasting, the main event was composing poetry and ci to showcase literary brilliance.

林觉玩了一会儿，当文人们以“春”为主题赋诗时，他毫无兴致。直到朱淑真出现，他眼前一亮，被深深吸引，内心爱意萌生，平生对附庸风雅的文词诗句最是愚笨的他，竟然把朱淑真做的那首词记在心间，现在依然能吟诵而出:
After amusing himself for a while, Lin Jue lost all interest once the scholars began writing poems on "spring"—until Zhu Shuzhen stepped forward. His eyes lit up, love struck on the spot, and this man who had zero head for affecting literary airs somehow memorized the entire ci she composed that day. Even now he could recite every word:

迟迟春日弄轻柔，花径暗香流。清明过了，不堪回首，云锁朱楼。
Slow spring daylight toys with gentle grace, along the flower path a secret fragrance drifts. Qingming has passed; I cannot bear to look back, where clouds lock in the crimson tower.

午窗睡起莺声巧，何处唤春愁？绿杨影里，海棠亭畔，红杏梢头。（《眼儿媚·迟迟春日弄轻柔》）
Waking by the noon window to clever oriole songs, where do they summon this spring sorrow? Amid green willow shadows, beside the crabapple pavilion, atop the red apricot boughs. ("Yan'er Mei: Slow Spring Daylight Toys with Gentle Grace") [TN: Translator's rendering]

恐怕这会成为他一生唯一记住的一首词，这就是爱情的力量。
It would likely remain the only ci he ever memorized in his life—such was the power of love.

只可惜娶了朱淑真后，曾经美好的想象在现实面前不堪一击。新婚之夜，她不愿圆房，甚至手拿剪刀做出不要命的样子。林觉爱着她，也就放弃了，想着刚成亲她不习惯，以后会慢慢接纳自己，毕竟两人已是夫妻。
What a pity that once he married her, every sweet daydream shattered against reality. On their wedding night, she refused to consummate the marriage and even held scissors to her own throat. Out of love, Lin Jue backed off, telling himself she only needed time to adjust—they were man and wife now.

事实却相反，他的理解并未换来朱淑真的改变，她不仅不愿与他同桌吃饭，甚至连句话都不愿与他讲，整日不是在屋里写诗作词，就是出去约好友郊游玩乐，甚至还有文人才子在其中。
Instead, his patience changed nothing. She refused to eat at the same table, wouldn't speak a word to him, and spent every day either shut inside writing verse or going on outings with friends—male scholars included.

林觉再也不想忍下去。有一日，冲进竹兰居，逼迫朱淑真与他圆房。这女人力气不大，却懂得如何保护自己，一脚踢在林觉的要害处，他疼了好一会儿，一气之下开始打她。
At last Lin Jue snapped. One day he stormed into Zhulan Residence and tried to force himself on her. Frail as she was, she knew where to strike—one kick to his groin doubled him over in agony, and fury drove him to hit her.

﻿被打后，她那个可怜样儿，让林觉既解气、兴奋，又找回丈夫的自尊，于是家暴朱淑真就成为林觉每隔一段时间要做的事情，尤其是想要与她圆房时。后来干脆将青楼女子带回家，当着她的面亲热，换来的依然是朱淑真的冷漠，这冷漠他看着更来气。
Seeing her battered and helpless gave Lin Jue a twisted rush of relief and restored his wounded pride as a husband. Beating Zhu Shuzhen became a regular habit, especially whenever she refused his bed. Later he went further, hauling brothel courtesans home to fondle right before her eyes—yet her icy indifference only stoked his rage higher.

今日就是在这种情况下又打了她。没成想用的力气大了些，打着打着，一把推去，她撞到柱子上晕死过去。林觉还担心好一阵儿，毕竟打死人要偿命的。谢天谢地，终于醒来，可却变了个人，样貌身段还是朱淑真，其他完全不一样。
Today had started the exact same way, only he had used too much strength and shoved her into a pillar until she collapsed. Panic had gripped him for a long stretch—killing someone meant paying with his own life. Thank heaven she had woken up, though she seemed a whole new person: same face, same figure, everything else turned upside down.

有一次朱淑真被打，仅仅提出和离，还被他一巴掌打回去，自此再也没提过。没想到眼前这个朱淑真竟然提出让休了她？林觉既诧异又满心不乐意。
Once before, after a beating, Zhu Shuzhen had mentioned heli—a divorce by mutual consent—only to get slapped down so hard she never breathed the word again. Now this woman demanded he unilaterally repudiate her? Shock and stubbornness knotted in Lin Jue's chest.

林觉又说出那句话：“你不是朱淑真。”
He repeated the same line: "You aren't Zhu Shuzhen."

千里寻说：“你老强调这句话干嘛？难道真想让所有人知道朱淑真因你家暴而亡？让官府把你抓起来？”
Qianli Xun rolled her eyes. "Why do you keep harping on that? Want everyone to know you beat Zhu Shuzhen to death so the yamen locks you up?"

“官府不会抓我，因为所有人都能证明，你跟朱淑真长得一模一样。”
"The yamen won't arrest me. Everyone can swear you look identical to Zhu Shuzhen."

“那不就结了，我就是朱淑真，你还怀疑什么？”
"Then that settles it! I am Zhu Shuzhen. What's left to doubt?"

“她不会提出让我休了她。”
"She would never ask me to write a bill of divorce."

千里寻找椅子坐下：“那是以前，现在她的想法已改变。”
Qianli Xun pulled up a chair and sat. "That was then. She changed her mind."

“难道你不知一个女人被丈夫休了是什么下场？”
"Don't you know what happens to a woman cast out by her husband?"

“不管是什么下场，总比和你一起过日子强。”
"Whatever happens beats spending another day with you."

“总之，我不同意！”
"Either way, I refuse!"

林觉态度强硬，又怎能难倒千里寻：“你不同意，没关系，我把你天天关在家里，不给你吃，不给你喝，也不让你上茅房，屋子里也不放夜壶，关你十天十夜，看你同不同意。”
His stubbornness didn't faze Qianli Xun one bit. "Refuse all you want. I'll just lock you in here day after day—no food, no water, no latrine, no chamber pot. Ten days and nights of that, let's see if you still refuse!"

“你妄想！我是练兵官，天天要操练临安士兵，只要一天不去，就会有人来找我。”
"Keep dreaming! I'm the Training Officer—I drill Lin'an's troops every day. Miss one shift and people come looking!"

“这个容易，大不了我让下人到你上班的地方……”
"Easy fix. I'll send a servant down to your workplace..."

“上班？”
"Workplace?"

千里寻赶紧改口：“到你练兵的地方给你告个假，就说你病了，而且是很严重的病，需要在家待个十天半月，估计你的上司不会不同意。”
Qianli Xun corrected herself fast. "To your drill ground to call in sick for you—say you caught something nasty and need ten or fifteen days in bed. Your superior won't say no."

“你这个狠毒的女人！即便如此，林府的下人也不会随便被你差遣！”
"Vicious woman! Even so, the servants of Lin Manor won't take orders from you!"

“下人好说，使银子就行，有钱能使鬼推磨，再说刚才那两个下人可是听话得很，连银子都没使，乖乖把你关起来。”
"Servants are easy—money talks. Even ghosts turn the millstone for silver. Besides, those two guys just now didn't even need a bribe to haul you in here and lock the door!"

林觉气得不行：“这两个家伙！我迟早会把他们赶出去！”
Lin Jue seethed. "Those two fools! I'll throw them both out sooner or later!"

看这情况，眼前的朱淑真是铁了心要离开他。打又打不过，干脆服软：“跟你夫妻一场，也算缘分，我们好合好散，除被我休了这条路，还有第二条路，就是和离，到时你离开我传出去名声也好听些。”
Seeing she was dead set on leaving him and knowing he couldn't beat her in a fight, he gave ground. "We've been man and wife—that counts as fate. Let's part on clean terms. Besides a bill of divorce, there's a second way: mutual divorce. Leave that way, and your reputation won't suffer as much."

千里寻并不了解林觉，毕竟刚认识他不久，但仅从打女人这一条判断，不是什么好东西，他的建议还需认真考量：“你能这么好？”
She barely knew Lin Jue, yet any man who hit women was rotten to the core; his offer warranted suspicion. "Since when are you this nice?"

“你晕死后醒来，变成母夜叉，把你放在家里，我会寝食难安，早些把你安顿走，我也好再娶一位娘子过舒坦日子。不过，和离之事我们两个说了不算，必须将你的父母以及有身份的长辈请来，当然林家也会叫来长辈，再把媒人请来，当着所有人的面，征得所有人同意，把财产分割清楚，在和离的文书上签字按下指印，再由媒婆呈送官府，你我的姻缘才能结束。”
"You wake up from a faint turned into a she-demon—keeping you under my roof will cost me my sleep and my appetite. Send you packing sooner, and I can marry a new wife and live in peace. Still, mutual divorce isn't up to just the two of us. We have to summon your parents and respected elders from both sides, plus the matchmaker. Everybody has to agree in person, divide the property, sign and affix our thumbprints to the divorce papers, and have the matchmaker file them at the yamen. Only then is our marriage over."

千里寻心想，古代离个婚怎么这么麻烦，还得长辈同意。既然已穿越成古人，还是入乡随俗吧。
Qianli Xun thought, Getting divorced in ancient times is such a hassle—even the elders have to sign off! Still, when in Rome...

“我同意，那就和离。”
"Deal. Mutual divorce it is."

“能不能看在我出了这么好的主意，帮我松开绳子？”
"Seeing as I gave you such a good idea, how about untying me?"

千里寻起身，拿起旁边的布子，走到床前，狠劲儿塞进林觉的嘴里。
Qianli Xun stood, grabbed a cloth nearby, stepped to the bed, and shoved it straight into Lin Jue's mouth.

林觉怎么反抗都没用。
No matter how he thrashed, he couldn't stop her.

千里寻指着他说：“本来我想关你好几日，看在你出了这么好一个主意，给你把时间缩短一下，只关你半天一晚上，明日一早再放你出去，这是对你殴打朱淑真的惩罚。”
She pointed a finger at him. "I was going to lock you up for days, so consider this your reward for the bright idea: just half a day and one night. I'll let you out tomorrow morning. Call it payback for beating Zhu Shuzhen."

千里寻说完，转身离开。
With that, she turned and walked out.

林觉的嘴被堵着，不能大骂她，只能心里骂道：“走着瞧！你以为和离那么容易，到时长辈们的唾沫星子都能把你淹死，还有你爹娘，他们未必同意！哼！到时你就知道想离开林家没那么容易！”
Gagged and unable to curse her aloud, Lin Jue raged in his head: Just you wait! Think mutual divorce is that easy? The elders will drown you in lectures—and your parents won't necessarily agree either! Hmph! You'll soon learn walking out of the Lin family isn't that simple!

千里寻回到竹兰居，苦荞刚好将茶做好，还热着，见小姐回来，将茶盏端上：“小姐，喝茶。”
Back at Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao had just finished making tea, still steaming hot, and presented the tea bowl. "Tea, Miss."

千里寻接过，看到茶汤色泽鲜白，还有一层细腻的泡沫，跟她喝过的茶很不一样：“这是茶吗？”
Qianli Xun took it and blinked at the bright white tea liquor topped with fine foam—nothing like any tea she had ever drunk. "This is tea?"

“是我刚刚为小姐点的茶。”
"Whisked tea I just prepared for you, Miss."

“点茶？”千里寻听过点茶，第一次见，只知这茶做起来不简单，她端起茶盏，一口气喝下，口感绵密细腻，甘甜润滑，“不错！没想到你这丫头竟然会点茶。”
"Whisked tea?" She had heard of Song whisked tea, never seen it, and knew it took skill. Lifting the bowl, she drained it in one go—creamy, smooth, sweet, and silky. "Not bad! Didn't know you had this skill."

苦荞接过茶盏：　“是小姐您以前教我的，您喜欢喝点的茶，又不想总自己点，就教会我，要不像点茶这种功夫，我作为下人是没机会学的。”
Kuqiao took the empty bowl. "You taught me yourself, Miss! You love whisked tea, didn't feel like whisking it every time, so you trained me. Otherwise a servant like me would never get the chance to learn."

“原来是我教你的？看来以后得倒过来，换你教我。”千里寻说完，到处找床，她太累了，想躺一会儿。
"I taught you? Guess we'll have to flip roles and let you teach me." Looking around for a bed, Qianli Xun felt exhaustion drag at her limbs. "Where's the bed? I need to lie down."

苦荞放下茶盏：“小姐，跟我来。”
Kuqiao set the bowl aside. "Right this way, Miss."

千里寻跟着苦荞向里面拐去，一张闺床出现在眼前，她二话不说，躺了上去。
Following Kuqiao around an inner partition, Qianli Xun spotted a curtained bed and flopped straight onto it without a word.

苦荞走到床边：“小姐，宽了衣裳您再睡。”
Kuqiao hurried over. "Miss, take off your outer robes before you sleep."

千里寻坐起来，准备自己动手脱掉外衣，刚穿越过来，一切还不习惯。
Qianli Xun sat up to undress herself—new to this era, she wasn't used to being waited on.

“小姐，您下床站着，我帮您脱。”
"Stand beside the bed, Miss, I'll help you."

千里寻照做。在苦荞帮她宽衣之际，问道：“为什么点茶这种功夫下人没机会学？”
Qianli Xun complied. As Kuqiao untied her robes, she asked, "Why wouldn't servants get a chance to learn whisked tea?"

苦荞边帮小姐宽衣边说：“点茶的功夫是像小姐这样的千金小姐，以及豪门贵族人士必备的技能之一，也只有像小姐这样的人才有闲情逸致在一盏茶上用功夫。市井小民，还有我们这些下人，既没闲情逸致，也没那机会，我是沾了小姐的光。”
"Whisking tea is a refined art expected of noble ladies like you and high-ranking families," Kuqiao explained as she folded the garments. "Only people of your standing have the leisure to fuss over a single bowl of tea. Common street folk and servants like us have neither the leisure nor the chance. I only learned because of your favor."

外衣脱掉后，千里寻一身轻松，穿着中衣躺入被中：“我太累了，天没黑就睡，等天黑了也不要叫我，我要一直睡到明天天亮。”
Down to her inner tunic, Qianli Xun slipped under the quilt with a sigh of relief. "I'm worn out. Going to bed before sundown—don't wake me after dark. I'm sleeping straight through till morning."

说完没多久，千里寻已进入梦乡。
Moments later, she was fast asleep.

苦荞放下床幔，轻轻走出去，把门带好。
Kuqiao let down the bed curtains, stepped out on light feet, and pulled the door shut behind her.
