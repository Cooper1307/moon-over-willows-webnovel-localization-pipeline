# Chapter 17: Three Taels and a Black Eye

Impatient by nature, once Qianli Xun decided to return to Lin Manor, she moved at once—not because she missed the place, but to feel out Lin Jue's hand and lock down every detail for the divorce. After going through hoops to summon a heavy hitter like the Old Ancestor, she wasn't leaving anything to chance.

She had Kuqiao order a carriage. The Lin coachman had long since gone back, so Zhu Manor's driver had to make the run. Soon they were rolling back toward Lin Manor.

Halfway there, Qianli Xun felt her forehead cut itch—nearly healed. That reminded her of the punch she had landed on Lin Jue; his black eye couldn't have faded yet, and he had already tattled to Zhu Xiyan about it. What if he waved that bruise around at the divorce meeting to muddy the waters?

"Kuqiao, got silver on you?"

"Not much—just five taels I packed for you, Miss."

"Enough for medicine?"

"Depends what kind."

"Lin Jue's eye is still bruised from my punch. I want to buy ointment to clear it up."

"Plenty for that! Wait—why are you being nice to Son-in-law all of a sudden?"

"Remember this, Kuqiao: sometimes being nice to someone is just a way to get what you want."

Lifting the window curtain, Qianli Xun scanned the street for a pharmacy.

Reading her mind, Kuqiao said, "No need to look, Miss—just up ahead is Jixiang Pharmacy. Best trauma salves in all of Lin'an. The ointment I put on your forehead came from there."

At Jixiang Pharmacy, Kuqiao called a halt and helped her mistress step down.

Stepping inside, a rich scent of dried herbs washed over them. Wooden shelving lined every wall, row after row stacked with jars and vials of every shape.

An apothecary clerk stepped out from behind the counter with a welcoming grin. "What medicine can I fetch for you today, young lady?"

Glancing around the shop, Qianli Xun asked, "Someone took a punch to the eye and has a nasty bruise—got anything that clears it up fast?"

The clerk beamed. "Came to the right place! Can't brag about everything, yet for bruises like that, dab this on tonight and by tomorrow it's gone without a trace!"

He reached up and pulled down a small porcelain vial. "Right here."

Qianli Xun took it. "We'll take it. Hold this, Kuqiao." Kuqiao stepped up to tuck it away.

"One hundred wen for this vial," the clerk said.

"Hold on, you'll get your money. I need a second medicine—see this cut on my forehead..."

Before she could finish, the clerk cut in, "Your cut is practically healed, miss! Don't waste coin on more salve—we run an honest shop and never trick customers into buying what they don't need."

Qianli Xun raised her voice. "Who said anything about healing it?! I want to make this wound look worse—without leaving a scar! Got anything like that?"

Never having met a buyer asking to harm herself, the clerk frowned. "We sell medicine to heal and save lives, never potions to hurt people! Don't put me in a bind, miss."

Qianli Xun held out her hand. "Kuqiao—the silver!"

Kuqiao pulled out the money pouch. Qianli Xun fished out one tael of silver and set it on the counter. "Got any?"

Eyeballing the silver, the clerk stood firm. "No."

Qianli Xun laid down a second tael. "Got any?"

Unmoved: "No!"

A third tael hit the wood. "Got any?"

Before the clerk could refuse again, the shopkeeper brushed aside the back curtain, grinned ear to ear, and scooped up all three taels. "Yes! Of course we do!"

Pocketing the silver, the shopkeeper pulled a tiny porcelain vial from beneath the counter and handed it over. "Miss, this is Corrosive Paste. Just a tiny dab on your cut will make it flare up looking fierce. Whenever you want it healed fast, just dab on the first salve you bought."

"Thanks, boss!" Turning to the clerk, Qianli Xun teased, "Take notes from your boss—gotta stay flexible in business!"

Handing the vials and remaining change to Kuqiao, she stepped back out to the carriage for Lin Manor.

Kuqiao frowned down at the two vials. "Three whole taels—highway robbery! You shouldn't have dropped silver one tael at a time, Miss—both vials together aren't worth a single string of cash!"

"If I hadn't, would the boss have pulled out Corrosive Paste?"

"Still don't get what you're thinking—spending all that silver just to buy ointment to hurt yourself!"

"Sometimes hurting yourself a little is the smartest move you can make. You'll see soon enough."

Back inside Lin Manor, Qianli Xun glanced around hoping to catch sight of Lin Jue's two brothers, yet only busy servants crossed the paths.

Heading west back to her shared courtyard, she learned from servants that Lin Jue wasn't back yet and went straight to Zhulan Residence. With divorce looming, she needed a full accounting of her own assets before Lin Jue could pull a fast one.

Barely seated—not even waiting for tea—she asked, "Kuqiao, who manages my dowry?"

Setting the two new vials into a round bamboo medicine basket, Kuqiao answered, "You poured your whole heart into poetry and never bothered with accounts, Miss—Matron Wei handles all of it for you."

"Matron Wei?"

"Your wet nurse! When Madam gave birth to you, she lacked enough milk and hired Matron Wei to nurse you. Afterward Matron Wei stayed to raise you, trusted completely by Madam, and came with you when you married to manage your dowry. Whenever you need cash, just ask Matron Wei—never mind Son-in-law."

"Why haven't I seen her around?"

"She took servants out to the countryside these past few days to collect rent. Once she's done, she'll be back and you'll meet her."

One of her own people holding the purse strings—zero worry of Lin Jue skimming her assets. "I'm beat from running around all day—going to grab a nap. Tell me the second Lin Jue gets back."

After helping her mistress lie down, Kuqiao closed the door and went to check on Son-in-law.

Lin Jue's page boy promised to notify her the moment his master returned. Reassured, Kuqiao returned to Zhulan Residence and watered the bamboo and orchids—normally sweeping maids' work, just something to pass the time.

She had just finished watering when the page boy dashed over. "Sister Kuqiao, Master is back!"

Thanking him, Kuqiao slipped inside to the bedside and whispered, "Miss, Son-in-law's back."

Too wired over the Old Ancestor agreeing to appear, Qianli Xun hadn't fallen asleep, only resting with shut eyes. Hearing Kuqiao, she sat right up and parted the drapes. "Right on time! Hand me the bruise ointment."

Slipping on her clothes, smoothing her hair without pinning ornaments, and pocketing the vial Kuqiao handed her, she strode straight out of Zhulan Residence to Lin Jue's quarters and shoved the door open.

Having just changed clothes with a servant's help, Lin Jue spun around at the noise.

Spotting the bruise still circling his left eye, Qianli Xun smiled inwardly—good thing she had bought this ointment so he couldn't weaponize that punch at the divorce meeting.

"Back from Zhu Manor so soon, Wife? Couldn't get a single elder to show up, huh? If not, settle down in Lin Manor—I won't mistreat you."

"Ha, hate to burst your bubble—if I hadn't lined someone up, why would I come back early? You tattled to my dad first, fine, I won't hold it against you. I'm a softhearted person, unlike you. Figuring we were husband and wife once and I clocked you pretty hard, I felt bad—so I picked up some top-grade ointment passing Jixiang Pharmacy."

She tossed the vial straight into his chest. "Put it on right away, before your troops at the drill ground find out your wife gave you a shiner and cost you your face."

Still smitten with Zhu Shuzhen, seeing her talk nonstop and even buy him medicine sent Lin Jue's imagination racing.

"Well? Put it on!" Eager to make that bruise vanish fast, Qianli Xun frowned as he just clutched the vial in a daze.

Impatient, she stepped up, snatched the vial back, popped the lid, scooped thick salve onto her finger, and smeared it right over the bruise around his left eye.

Snickering at the sight, the servants slipped out the door on quiet feet.

As Qianli Xun finished dabbing and pulled her hand back, Lin Jue caught her wrist. "Why are you suddenly so nice to me?"

Yanking her wrist free, she snorted. "Didn't I just tell you? Soft heart. Don't read into it."

Setting the vial on the table, she added, "Keep applying it tomorrow—don't waste my goodwill."

Turning to go, she remembered the real reason she came and spun back to find him still staring dumbstruck. "Hey! Let's nail down the time and place for the mutual divorce right now."

"Mutual divorce" snapped him cold awake. "Day after tomorrow, the Hour of the Snake, North Courtyard reception hall."

"You know I'm not the old Zhu Shuzhen—remember nothing, don't even know who our matchmaker was. You're in charge of summoning her."

"No problem."

"How many elders are coming on your side?"

"Eight. More wanted to come, had to hold them back."

"What? Eight?!" Qianli Xun's heart dipped—one elder against eight! "I've only got one, you're bringing eight—how's that fair? Can't you bring just one too?"

"Can't help it—Lin clan elders are huge in number and eager to help." Stepping closer to study her face, Lin Jue murmured, "Why bother divorcing me? Even if you aren't the old Zhu Shuzhen, I like you even better now. Why not stay and build a life with me?"

Qianli Xun looked him up and down. Definitely not her type—even if she wasn't sure what her type was, it sure wasn't Lin Jue, especially after their rocky start.

"Save your breath—this divorce is locked in. Only one elder on my side or not, I'm fighting this through!"

With that, she marched out.

Lin Jue let out a cold laugh. "You're the one who should save your breath!"
