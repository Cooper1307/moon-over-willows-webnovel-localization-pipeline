# Chapter 17: Three Taels and a Black Eye

---

千里寻天生急性子，说回林府必须立刻回去。并不是她想念林府，而是想回去探听林觉的虚实，好为和离之事做好万全准备。费了老鼻子劲儿请了一尊大神，还不得回去好好筹谋筹谋。

Qianli Xun was impatient by nature. The moment she said she was returning to the Lin residence, she meant it—no delay. It wasn't that she missed the place. She wanted to gauge Lin Jue's reaction and prepare thoroughly for the mutual divorce. She'd gone through all that trouble to bring back a heavyweight ally. Time to plan carefully.

---

她吩咐苦荞找车夫把马车套上。林府的车夫早已回去，只能辛苦朱府的车夫再跑一趟。苦荞安排好后，搀扶小姐上了马车，行走在回林府的路上。

She told Kuqiao to find a coachman and hitch the carriage. The Lin family's coachman had already gone back, so they'd have to borrow one from the Zhu residence. Kuqiao arranged it, then helped her miss into the carriage. They set off for the estate.

---

千里寻忽觉额头的伤口有些发痒，估计是快好了。这一痒，让她想起打林觉的那一拳，他眼睛周围的淤青估计一时半会儿好不了，他因此在朱晞颜面前告了她一状，如果和离时再以此说事，岂不麻烦？

Qianli Xun noticed her forehead wound itching. Probably healing. The itch reminded her of the punch she'd thrown at Lin Jue. The bruise around his eye wouldn't fade anytime soon. He'd already complained about it to Zhu Xiyan. If he used it as evidence during the divorce, that would be trouble.

---

"苦荞，你身上可带银子？"

"Kuqiao, do you have any silver on you?"

---

"多的没有，只替小姐装了五两。"

"Not much. Just five taels for you, Miss."

---

"买药应该够吧？"

"That should be enough for medicine."

---

"那得看小姐买什么药？"

"Depends on what medicine you're buying, Miss."

---

"林觉的眼睛周围被我打得淤青，我想买药帮他治治。"

"Lin Jue's eye is bruised from my punch. I want to buy medicine to treat it."

---

"这种药花不了多少银子，尽够。不过，小姐怎么突然对姑爷这么好？"

"That won't cost much. More than enough. But why are you suddenly being so nice to the young master?"

---

"苦荞，你记着，有时看似对别人好，其实是另有所图。"

"Kuqiao, remember this—sometimes looking like you're doing someone a favor is just a way to get what you want."

---

千里寻掀开帘子，向马车外瞧，希望能找到一家药房。

Qianli Xun lifted the carriage curtain and peered outside, hoping to spot a pharmacy.

---

苦荞猜出小姐的心思："小姐不用找，马车再往前走一会儿，有家吉祥药房，他们家跌打损伤药是全临安城最好的，我给小姐涂的药就是从吉祥药房买的。"

Kuqiao guessed her miss's intention. "No need to look, Miss. A little further down the road, there's Jixiang Pharmacy. Their bruise and injury remedies are the best in all of Lin'an. The ointment I used on you came from there."

---

到达吉祥药房门口时，苦荞让车夫把车停下，她扶着小姐下了车。

When they reached Jixiang Pharmacy, Kuqiao had the coachman stop and helped her miss down.

---

走进吉祥药房，一股草药之气扑面而来。再仔细观察，药房四周靠墙的地方全部放着木制货架，一层一层的架子上摆着各式各样的瓶瓶罐罐。

They stepped inside. The sharp scent of medicinal herbs hit them immediately. Wooden shelves lined every wall, layer upon layer, loaded with all kinds of bottles and jars.

---

药房的伙计见有顾客光临，从柜台后面走出来，笑脸相迎："姑娘，想买什么药？"

A clerk came out from behind the counter with a welcoming smile. "What can I help you find, Miss?"

---

千里寻向药房四周瞅瞅："有人的眼睛周围被打得淤青，有没有一涂就好的药？"

Qianli Xun glanced around the pharmacy. "Someone got a black eye from a punch. Got anything that makes it disappear overnight?"

---

伙计笑道："姑娘可是找对地方，吉祥药房别的药不敢说，这类药还真有，只要头一天往那淤青处一涂，保准第二天淤青全消。"

The clerk beamed. "You've come to the right place, Miss. For bruises, Jixiang Pharmacy has just the thing. Apply it on the first day, and by the next morning, the bruise will be completely gone."

---

伙计说完从货架上取下一个小瓷瓶："就是这瓶药。"

He pulled a small porcelain bottle from the shelf. "This is the one."

---

千里寻接过药瓶："就它了。苦荞，拿着。"苦荞上前接过药瓶。

Qianli Xun took it. "This'll do. Kuqiao, take it." Kuqiao stepped forward and accepted the bottle.

---

伙计说："这瓶药，一百个铜钱。"

"That'll be a hundred copper coins," the clerk said.

---

"你别急，钱不会少你的，我还得买一种药，你看我额头的伤……"

"Hold on. I need something else. See this wound on my forehead—"

---

千里寻话还未说完，药房的伙计插嘴道："姑娘额头的伤快好了，不用再浪费钱买药，我们是良心商家，不能欺骗姑娘花冤枉钱。"

Before she could finish, the clerk cut in. "Your forehead wound is almost healed, Miss. No need to waste silver on medicine. We're honest merchants—we won't cheat you into spending money you don't need to."

---

千里寻急了："谁说要买药治伤！我想让这伤口变得更厉害，但不能毁我的容貌，你们药房可有此药？"

Qianli Xun got impatient. "Who said I want medicine to heal it? I want to make this wound look worse. But not so bad that it scars my face. Do you have anything like that?"

---

伙计第一次碰到买药伤害自己的人，为难道："我们药房卖药只为救死扶伤，从不卖伤人伤己的药，姑娘这不是为难我吗？"

The clerk had never encountered a customer who wanted medicine to harm herself. He looked troubled. "Miss, we sell medicine to heal people, not to hurt them. You're putting me in a difficult position."

---

千里寻伸出手："苦荞，把银子拿来！"

Qianli Xun held out her hand. "Kuqiao, give me the silver."

---

苦荞从怀里掏出钱袋，放到小姐手里。千里寻接过，取出一两银子放在柜台上："有没有？"

Kuqiao pulled out the money pouch and placed it in her miss's hand. Qianli Xun took out one tael of silver and set it on the counter. "Do you have it or not?"

---

伙计看看银子，坚持道："没有。"

The clerk glanced at the silver and held firm. "No."

---

千里寻又拿出一两，继续放在柜台上："有没有？"

Qianli Xun placed another tael on the counter. "Do you have it or not?"

---

伙计依然毫不动容："没有！"

The clerk remained unmoved. "No!"

---

千里寻又拿出一两："有没有？"

She added a third tael. "Do you have it or not?"

---

伙计刚要说没有，掌柜掀开帘子从里面出来，笑嘻嘻地拿过三两银子："有！当然有！"

The clerk opened his mouth to refuse again. Just then, the pharmacy owner pushed through a curtain from the back room. He snatched up the three taels of silver with a sly grin. "Yes! Of course we do!"

---

收好银子后，掌柜从柜台下面拿出一个小瓷瓶，递给千里寻："姑娘，这里装的是腐肌膏，只要在您额头的伤口上涂一点点，那伤口会变得严重，如果想让伤口尽快好，再涂上您刚买的那瓶药即可。"

After pocketing the silver, the owner produced a small porcelain bottle from beneath the counter and handed it to Qianli Xun. "Miss, this is Fuji Ointment. Just dab a tiny bit on your forehead wound, and it'll look much worse. When you want it to heal fast, apply the other medicine you just bought."

---

"多谢掌柜。"千里寻高兴道，然后批评伙计，"你啊，以后要多向掌柜学习，做生意需头脑灵活。"

"Thank you, sir." Qianli Xun beamed. Then she turned to the clerk. "You—learn from your boss. Running a business takes a sharp mind."

---

千里寻把药和剩下的银子给了苦荞，两人走出吉祥药房，继续乘坐马车回林府。

Qianli Xun handed the medicines and the remaining silver to Kuqiao. The two left Jixiang Pharmacy, got back in the carriage, and continued toward the estate.

---

苦荞端详着两瓶药："花了三两银子，实在亏。小姐就不该把银子一两一两地往出拿，这两瓶药最多一吊钱。"

Kuqiao studied the two bottles. "Three taels spent—what a waste. You shouldn't have pulled out the silver one tael at a time, Miss. Both bottles together were worth maybe a string of coins."

---

"我要不这么做，药房的掌柜能拿出腐肌膏？"

"If I hadn't done it that way, do you think the owner would've brought out the Fuji Ointment?"

---

"真想不通小姐是怎么想的，花这么多钱就是为买瓶伤害自己的药膏。"

"I really can't figure out your thinking, Miss. Spending all that silver just to buy medicine that hurts yourself."

---

"有时候看似伤害自己，其实全是为自己好，到时你就知道。"

"Sometimes what looks like hurting yourself is actually looking out for yourself. You'll understand soon enough."

---

回到林府，千里寻不由向四处瞧瞧，既然里面也住着林觉的两位哥哥，说不定能碰到。可瞧了半天，只有下人来回忙碌走动的身影。

Back at the Lin residence, Qianli Xun glanced around. Lin Jue's two older brothers also lived here. Maybe she'd run into one of them. But after looking for a while, she only spotted servants bustling about their chores.

---

千里寻只好向西拐去，回到她与林觉居住的院子，问过下人，才知道林觉还未归来，便直奔竹兰居。想起嫁妆，马上要离婚，必须把属于她的财产了解清楚，否则到时被林觉算计都不自知。

She headed west instead. Back to the courtyard she shared with Lin Jue—the Zhulan Residence. She asked a servant and learned Lin Jue hadn't returned yet. She went straight to the main room. Thinking about her dowry—the divorce was coming, and she needed to know exactly what belonged to her. Otherwise, Lin Jue might swindle her without her even realizing it.

---

千里寻刚坐下，连茶都没来得及喝，急切地问："苦荞，我的嫁妆平时都是怎么管的？"

She'd barely sat down before asking, "Kuqiao, how has my dowry been managed all this time?"

---

苦荞拿出一个放药的圆形竹筐，把新买的两瓶药放进去："小姐一心扑在诗词上，根本不管这些事，都是韦嬷嬷帮小姐管着。"

Kuqiao pulled out a round bamboo basket for storing medicine and placed the two new bottles inside. "Miss, you've always been focused on poetry. You never bothered with these things. Nanny Wei's been handling it all for you."

---

"韦嬷嬷？"

"Nanny Wei?"

---

"她是您的奶娘，夫人刚生下小姐，奶水不够，找了韦嬷嬷喂养小姐，后来她就留在小姐身边照顾您，深得夫人信任，您嫁人时，也跟着过来，负责管理嫁妆之事。小姐用钱只需问韦嬷嬷要，不用理会姑爷。"

"She's your wet nurse. Right after you were born, Madam didn't have enough milk, so Nanny Wei was brought in to nurse you. She stayed by your side ever since and earned Madam's full trust. When you married, she came along to manage your dowry. Whenever you need money, just ask Nanny Wei—no need to deal with the young master."

---

"我怎么没见到她？"

"Why haven't I seen her?"

---

"这几日她带下人到乡下收租子，收完租便回来，小姐到时自然见到。"

"She's been collecting tenant rent in the countryside these past few days. She'll be back once the collection is done. You'll see her then, Miss."

---

原来是自家人管理嫁妆，千里寻不用担心林觉算计："我要睡一会儿，今儿跑来跑去有些累，林觉回来，告诉我一声。"

So her own people were managing the dowry. Qianli Xun relaxed. No need to worry about Lin Jue scheming. "I'm going to take a nap. Running around today wore me out. Wake me when Lin Jue gets back."

---

苦荞伺候小姐躺好，关了房门出去打探姑爷的消息。

Kuqiao helped her miss lie down, closed the door, and went out to gather news about him.

---

姑爷身边的小厮说，主君一旦回来，第一个告诉苦荞。她放心回到竹兰居，给院子里的兰花和竹子浇水。这些事本应该是洒扫丫鬟的职责，她闲来无事，用此打发时间。

The page boy beside Lin Jue said he'd let Kuqiao know the moment he returned. Satisfied, she went back to watering the orchids and bamboo in the courtyard. That was supposed to be the sweeping maid's job, but with nothing better to do, she used it to pass the time.

---

刚给院子的植物浇完水，姑爷身边的小厮跑来报告："苦荞姐姐，主君回来了。"

She'd just finished watering the plants when the page boy came running. "Sister Kuqiao, the young master is back!"

---

苦荞谢过后，进屋走到床边，轻声说："小姐，姑爷回来了。"

Kuqiao thanked him and hurried inside. She walked to the bedside and whispered, "Miss, the young master is back."

---

因老祖宗答应在和离时出面，千里寻太过兴奋，没有一挨枕头就睡着，一直躺着闭目养神。听到苦荞的声音，立刻爬起来，掀开床幔："等的就是他，把治淤青的药给我拿来。"

Qianli Xun hadn't actually fallen asleep. The Old Ancestor had agreed to speak for her, and she'd been too excited to sleep. She'd been lying there with her eyes closed, resting. At Kuqiao's voice, she sat right up and threw open the bed curtain. "Just who I've been waiting for. Bring me the bruise medicine."

---

千里寻穿好衣裳，简单整理好头发，并未簪发饰，拿好苦荞取出的药瓶，急急匆匆出了竹兰居，来到林觉居住的屋外，一把推开门。

Qianli Xun dressed, pinned her hair up simply—no ornaments—and took the medicine bottle Kuqiao handed her. She rushed out of the Zhulan Residence and headed straight for Lin Jue's quarters. She pushed the door open with one hand.

---

林觉刚在下人的伺候下换完衣裳，听到门响，习惯性地转过身。

Lin Jue had just finished changing clothes with a servant's help. Hearing the door, he turned out of habit.

---

千里寻看到他左眼周围的淤青还在，心想，多亏自己想得周到，及时买好药，否则和离时，她打的这一拳说不定会带来麻烦。

Qianli Xun saw the bruise around his left eye was still there. Good thing she'd thought ahead and bought the medicine. Otherwise, that punch might cause trouble during the divorce.

---

"娘子这么快就从朱府回来？难道一个长辈都没请到？如果没请到，安心待在林府，我不会亏待你。"

"You're back from the Zhu residence already, Wife? Couldn't find a single elder to speak for you? If that's the case, just stay at the Lin residence. I won't treat you badly."

---

"呵呵，让你失望，要是没请到，我也不会这么早回来。你恶人先告状，我不跟你计较，我这个人呢，善良，跟你不一样，想着我们毕竟夫妻一场，也算缘分，把你打得这么重，于心不忍，经过吉祥药房时，买了一瓶好药。"

"Ha! Disappointed, are you? If I hadn't found anyone, I wouldn't be back this early. You ran to Zhu Xiyan to complain first. I'm not holding a grudge. I'm kind—unlike you. We were husband and wife for a while, after all. I feel bad about hitting you so hard. I stopped at Jixiang Pharmacy on the way and bought some good medicine for you."

---

千里寻把药瓶扔到林觉怀里："赶紧涂上，省得你去练兵，被下属知道是我打的，搞得你面子上挂不住。"

She tossed the bottle into Lin Jue's arms. "Apply it. No need to let your subordinates at training camp find out I gave you that black eye and make you lose face."

---

林觉本就爱着朱淑真，现在她变得跟以前完全不同，话又多，还给自己买药，他不禁浮想联翩。

Lin Jue was already in love with Zhu Shuzhen. Now she'd become completely different—talkative, and even buying him medicine. He couldn't help letting his imagination run wild.

---

"怎么还不涂？"千里寻希望他尽快涂上，想让那淤青快些消散，可林觉摸着怀里的药，愣在那里。

"Why aren't you applying it?" Qianli Xun wanted him to put it on right away, so the bruise would fade fast. But Lin Jue just stood there, fingering the bottle, frozen.

---

千里寻干脆过去又从他怀里拿了药瓶，打开，给手指上倒些粘稠的药膏，帮林觉涂在左眼周围的淤青之处。

Fine. Qianli Xun walked over, took the bottle back from his hands, uncorked it, poured some thick ointment onto her finger, and applied it to the bruise around his left eye.

---

一旁的下人见二人如此，偷乐着，悄悄走出屋外。

The servants watching from the side exchanged knowing smiles and quietly slipped out.

---

当千里寻涂完药膏，要收回手时，被林觉抓住手腕："你为什么突然对我这么好？"

When Qianli Xun finished and was about to pull her hand back, Lin Jue grabbed her wrist. "Why are you suddenly being so good to me?"

---

千里寻抽回手："我刚才不都说过？我这个人善良，你可别瞎想。"

Qianli Xun pulled her hand free. "Didn't I just say? I'm kind. Don't read into it."

---

她把药瓶放在桌上："记得明日继续涂，别辜负我一番心意。"

She set the bottle on the table. "Remember to apply it again tomorrow. Don't waste my effort."

---

刚要走，想起重要的事，转过身，见林觉依然呆呆地看着她："喂！和离的具体时间和地点不如现在定下来？"

She was about to leave when she remembered something important. She turned back. Lin Jue was still staring at her blankly. "Hey! Why don't we settle the time and place for the divorce right now?"

---

听到"和离"二字，林觉清醒过来："后日巳时在林府北院的会客厅。"

Hearing the word "divorce," Lin Jue snapped out of it. "The day after tomorrow. The hour of Si—around mid-morning. The reception hall in the north courtyard of the Lin residence."

---

"你也知道，我不是以前的朱淑真，什么都记不得，也不知道媒人是谁，和离时媒人由你负责请。"

"You know I'm not the Zhu Shuzhen you knew. I can't remember everything. I don't even know who the matchmaker was. You'll be in charge of hiring one for the divorce."

---

"没问题。"

"No problem."

---

"你那里共有几位长辈出面？"

"How many elders are coming from your side?"

---

"八位，本来还有更多长辈要来，都被我拦住。"

"Eight. There would've been more, but I turned them away."

---

"什么？八位？"千里寻很担心，自己只请了一位，从数量上已败下阵来，"我才请一位，你请八位，不公平，能不能你也只请一位？"

"What? Eight?" Qianli Xun was worried. She only had one. In numbers, she was already losing badly. "I've only got one, and you've got eight. That's not fair. Can you also bring just one?"

---

"没办法，林家长辈实在太多，也太热情。"林觉走近，看着朱淑真，"要不别跟我和离？你虽然不是以前的朱淑真，看着却比以前更好，不如跟我好好过日子？"

"Can't be helped. The Lin family has too many elders, and they're all too enthusiastic." Lin Jue stepped closer, studying Zhu Shuzhen's face. "How about—don't get a divorce after all? You may not be the Zhu Shuzhen I married, but you're even better than before. Why not just settle down and be my proper wife?"

---

千里寻也瞅着林觉，他并不是她喜欢的类型，其实她也不知道自己到底喜欢什么样的类型，反正不是林觉这种。再说她刚穿越而来，对方没给她留下多少好印象。

Qianli Xun studied him too. He wasn't her type at all. Actually, she didn't even know what her type was. But it definitely wasn't Lin Jue. Besides, she'd just transmigrated here, and he hadn't exactly left a good first impression.

---

"你别妄想，这婚我跟你是离定了。虽然我这里长辈只有一位，但我也会为之努力。"

"Don't even dream about it. This marriage—I'm getting out of it. Even if I only have one elder on my side, I'll make it work."

---

千里寻说完，扬长而去。

With that, Qianli Xun turned and strode out.

---

林觉冷笑道："是你不要妄想才对！"

A dark sneer curled Lin Jue's lips. "You're the one who should stop dreaming."
