整体来看，第17章的翻译**依然非常出色，情节推进非常干脆！** 

这一章的看点是女主用现代人的小聪明“反向套路”药房掌柜买毁容药，以及她跑去给渣男涂药以掩盖家暴证据的“心机”操作。你的翻译将女主这种雷厉风行、绝不吃亏的性格展现得淋漓尽致。

针对你的审校需求，以下是几个细节上的优化建议，主要集中在**“商业对话的地道化”**、**“中式口语的替换”**以及**“微表情描写的张力”**上：

### 1. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 17: A Trip to Jixiang Pharmacy*
*   **点评**：比较平淡。网文标题需要带点悬念或趣味性。女主去药房不是为了治病，而是去买“毁容药”做局的。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 17: Scheming at the Pharmacy**（药房里的算计）或 **Chapter 17: A "Cure" for a Black Eye**（带引号的Cure，暗示女主买药背后的心机）。

### 2. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P1 极具现代网文感的俚语（费了老鼻子劲儿）**：`She'd gone through all that trouble...`
    *   **优化**：原译意思准确，但“老鼻子劲儿”这种极具夸张色彩的口语，可以用更地道的英语口语来还原。
    *   **修改**：`She had busted her ass to bring back a heavyweight ally.` (busted her ass 是非常美式、大女主风格的口语，表示费尽九牛二虎之力)。
*   **P15 药铺老板的推销术（一涂就好）**：`Got anything that makes it disappear overnight?` / `Apply it on the first day, and by the next morning...`
    *   **优化**：古代药房伙计推销神药，英文里有一个绝佳的词叫 **miracle cure / overnight cure**。
    *   **修改**：`"Is there an overnight cure?"` / `"We have just the miracle cure for that."`
*   **P19 道德绑架的反击（良心商家）**：`We're honest merchants—we won't cheat you...`
    *   **优化**：伙计的话里带着一点商家的自傲。
    *   **修改**：`"We run an honest business here. We wouldn't dare swindle you out of your hard-earned money."`
*   **P28 商业互吹（头脑灵活）**：`Business requires a flexible mind.`
    *   **问题**：flexible mind 稍显中式。英文夸商人懂变通、精明，通常用 **business savvy** 或者 **sharp**。
    *   **修改**：`"You should learn from your boss. Running a business takes a sharp mind."`
*   **P43 丫鬟的偷笑（偷乐着）**：`The servants watching from the side snickered to each other and quietly slipped out.`
    *   **优化**：Snicker 带有嘲笑的意思。下人们看到男女主亲密涂药，是带着一种暧昧的偷笑/憋笑。
    *   **修改**：`The attending servants exchanged knowing smiles and quietly slipped out of the room.` （knowing smiles = 会心的微笑）。
*   **P54 渣男的冷笑**：`Lin Jue sneered coldly.`
    *   **优化**：sneered 本身就带有 cold 的意思（嘲笑/冷笑），加 coldly 有点语义重复。
    *   **修改**：`A dark sneer curled Lin Jue's lips.` （一个阴暗的冷笑爬上林觉的嘴角，画面感更强）。

### 3. 句子是否过长？节奏与标点是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **P32 吐槽花钱（一两一两地拿）**：`You shouldn't have pulled out the silver one tael at a time...`
    *   **优化**：读起来稍微有点拖沓。可以优化为更清脆的抱怨。
    *   **修改**：`"You shouldn't have flashed those silver taels one by one, Miss."`
*   **P49 渣男的调戏（好好过日子）**：`Why not just live with me properly?`
    *   **优化**：渣男其实是在PUA/引诱女主放弃离婚。
    *   **修改**：`"Why not just settle down and be my proper wife?"`

### 🌟 综合精修版英文参考 (Polished Version)

以下是吸收了上述建议后的精修版，调整了买药讨价还价的喜剧节奏，并强化了结尾处男女主交锋时的火药味：

**Chapter 17: A "Cure" for a Black Eye**

Qianli Xun was impatient by nature. The moment she said she was returning to the Lin residence, she meant it—no delays. 
It wasn't that she missed the place. She simply wanted to gauge Lin Jue's reaction and prepare her defenses for the divorce negotiations. She had busted her ass to secure a heavyweight ally. It was time to head back and plot her next move.
She instructed Kuqiao to find a coachman and hitch the carriage. Since the Lin family's coachman had long gone back, they had to borrow one from the Zhu estate. Once everything was arranged, Kuqiao helped her miss into the carriage, and they set off for the Lin residence.
During the ride, Qianli Xun felt a sudden itch on her forehead. *Must be healing,* she thought. The itch immediately reminded her of the punch she had thrown at Lin Jue. The black eye she had given him wouldn't fade anytime soon. He had already used it to play the victim in front of Zhu Xiyan. If he paraded that bruise around as evidence during the divorce proceedings, it would cause unnecessary trouble.
"Kuqiao, do you have any silver on you?"
"Not much, Miss. I only brought about five taels."
"That should be enough to buy some medicine, right?"
"Depends on what kind of medicine you're looking for, Miss."
"Lin Jue's eye is bruised black and blue from my punch. I want to buy an ointment to treat it."
Kuqiao blinked in surprise. "Oh, that won't cost much at all. More than enough. But... why are you suddenly being so nice to the young master?"
"Kuqiao, remember this," Qianli Xun smiled slyly. "Sometimes, doing someone a 'favor' is just a stepping stone to getting exactly what you want."
Lifting the carriage curtain, Qianli Xun peered out into the bustling streets, keeping an eye out for an apothecary.
Guessing her miss's intention, Kuqiao chimed in, "No need to look, Miss. Just a little further down this road is Jixiang Pharmacy. Their bruise and injury remedies are the best in all of Lin'an. The ointment I used on your forehead came from them."
When the carriage rolled up to Jixiang Pharmacy, Kuqiao signaled the driver to stop and helped her miss down.
Stepping inside, the sharp, earthy scent of medicinal herbs instantly hit them. Wooden shelves lined every inch of the walls, stacked floor-to-ceiling with an endless array of bottles and jars.
Spotting a customer, a clerk hurried out from behind the counter with a welcoming smile. "What can I help you find, Miss?"
Qianli Xun glanced around the shop. "Someone took a fist to the eye and got a nasty bruise. Got any miracle cures that make it disappear overnight?"
The clerk beamed. "You've come to the right place, Miss! We have just the cure for that. Apply it on the first day, and by the next morning, the bruise will be completely gone." He pulled a small porcelain bottle from a shelf. "This is the one."
"This'll do. Kuqiao, take it." Qianli Xun nodded. Kuqiao stepped forward and accepted the bottle.
"That will be one hundred copper coins," the clerk said.
"Hold on. I need something else." Qianli Xun pointed to the cut on her forehead. "See this wound—"
Before she could finish, the clerk cut her off politely. "Your wound is already scabbing over, Miss. There is no need to waste silver on more medicine. We run an honest business here; we wouldn't dare swindle you out of your money."
Qianli Xun grew impatient. "Who said I wanted medicine to heal it? I want something that will make this wound look *much worse*—inflamed and terrifying—but without actually leaving a permanent scar. Do you have anything like that?"
The clerk froze. He had never encountered a customer actively looking to maim themselves. "Miss... we sell medicine to heal people, not to harm them. You are putting me in a very difficult position."
Qianli Xun held out her hand. "Kuqiao, the silver."
Kuqiao pulled the money pouch from her sleeve and placed it in her miss's palm. Qianli Xun pulled out a single tael of solid silver and slammed it on the counter. "Do you have it or not?"
The clerk eyed the silver but held his ground. "No."
*Clack.* Qianli Xun placed a second tael next to the first. "Do you have it?"
The clerk swallowed hard but remained firm. "No!"
*Clack.* A third tael hit the wood. "Do you have it?!"
The clerk opened his mouth to refuse again, but the curtain to the back room suddenly whipped open. The pharmacy owner practically flew out, his face stretched into a greedy, ear-to-ear grin. His hands shot out and swept the three taels off the counter in a blur. 
"Yes! Of course we do!"
Pocketing the silver, the owner ducked under the counter and produced a tiny porcelain vial, handing it to Qianli Xun. "Miss, this is Fuji Ointment. Dab just a tiny speck onto your wound, and it will look horrifyingly severe. When you're ready for it to heal, simply apply the bruise ointment you just purchased, and it will clear right up."
"Thank you, boss." Qianli Xun smiled in satisfaction. She then shot a look at the dumbfounded clerk. "You should learn from your boss. Running a successful business requires a sharp mind."
Handing the medicines and the remaining silver back to Kuqiao, the two left the pharmacy and continued their journey back to the Lin estate.
In the carriage, Kuqiao stared mournfully at the two tiny bottles. "Three whole taels spent... what a waste! You shouldn't have flashed those silver pieces one by one, Miss. Both of these bottles combined are worth a string of copper coins at most!"
"If I hadn't done it that way, do you honestly think the owner would have brought out the Fuji Ointment?"
"I just can't understand how your mind works, Miss. Spending a fortune just to buy a poison to torture yourself."
"Sometimes, what looks like self-harm is actually the ultimate self-preservation. You'll understand soon enough."

Upon arriving back at the Lin residence, Qianli Xun glanced around the courtyards. Since Lin Jue's two older brothers lived on the estate, she thought she might bump into one of them. But after looking around, she only spotted servants scurrying about their chores.
Giving up, she headed west toward the Zhulan Residence. After questioning a passing servant, she learned Lin Jue hadn't returned yet, so she headed straight into the main room. 
As the divorce loomed closer, a crucial thought struck her: her dowry. She needed to know exactly what belonged to her. Otherwise, Lin Jue might try to swindle her out of her own wealth.
She barely sat down before demanding, "Kuqiao, how has my dowry been managed all this time?"
Kuqiao pulled out a round bamboo basket and placed the two new medicine bottles inside. "Miss, you've always poured your entire soul into your poetry. You never bothered with worldly matters. Nanny Wei has been managing all of your assets."
"Nanny Wei?"
"She's your wet nurse. When Madam first gave birth to you, she didn't have enough milk, so Nanny Wei was hired. She stayed by your side ever since and earned Madam's complete trust. When you married, she came as part of your dowry entourage to manage your finances. Whenever you need money, you just ask Nanny Wei. You never had to deal with the young master for funds."
"Why haven't I seen her?"
"She took some servants down to the countryside to collect tenant rent a few days ago. She'll be back as soon as the collection is done. You'll see her then."
Knowing her dowry was entirely controlled by her own loyal staff, Qianli Xun breathed a sigh of relief. No need to worry about Lin Jue embezzling her money. "I'm going to take a nap. Running around all day wore me out. Wake me the second Lin Jue gets back."
Kuqiao helped her miss into bed, closed the door, and went out to bribe the servants for information.
Lin Jue's personal pageboy promised to alert Kuqiao the moment his master returned. Satisfied, Kuqiao returned to the Zhulan Residence and began watering the orchids and bamboo in the courtyard—a chore meant for the sweeping maids, but she needed something to kill time.
She had just finished watering the plants when the pageboy came sprinting into the courtyard. "Sister Kuqiao! The master is back!"
Kuqiao thanked him, hurried inside, and approached the bed. "Miss," she whispered. "The young master has returned."
Qianli Xun hadn't actually fallen asleep. Knowing the Old Ancestor was going to back her up had kept her buzzing with adrenaline; she had just been resting her eyes. At Kuqiao's words, she shot up and threw open the bed curtains. "Just the man I've been waiting for. Give me the bruise ointment."
She threw on her outer robe, pinned her hair up simply without any ornate hairpins, grabbed the medicine bottle, and marched straight to Lin Jue's quarters.
Reaching his room, she pushed the door open without knocking.
Lin Jue, who was just changing his robes with the help of a servant, turned around in surprise.
Qianli Xun noted that the bruise around his left eye was still vividly dark. *Good thing I bought this,* she thought. *Otherwise, that punch might have given him the upper hand during the negotiations.*
"Back from the Zhu residence so soon, Wife?" Lin Jue smirked. "Couldn't find a single elder brave enough to speak for you? Since that's the case, just stay here quietly in the Lin residence. I promise I won't treat you badly."
"Ha! Disappointed, are we? If I hadn't secured someone, I wouldn't be back this early," Qianli Xun retorted. "You ran to Zhu Xiyan to play the victim first. But I'm not holding a grudge. I'm a kind-hearted person, unlike you. We are husband and wife, after all. I actually started feeling guilty about hitting you so hard, so I stopped at Jixiang Pharmacy and bought some top-tier medicine for you."
She tossed the porcelain bottle squarely into his chest. "Apply it immediately. We can't have your subordinates at the training camp finding out your wife gave you a black eye. You'd lose all your precious 'face.'"
Lin Jue had always been infatuated with Zhu Shuzhen. Seeing her act so completely different now—talkative, fiery, and actually buying him medicine—his mind began to race with wishful delusions. 
"Why aren't you putting it on?" Qianli Xun demanded. She wanted him to apply it immediately so the bruise would vanish. But Lin Jue just stood there, clutching the bottle, staring at her in a daze.
Losing her patience, Qianli Xun marched over, snatched the bottle back, popped the cork, and squeezed a dab of the thick ointment onto her finger. Without asking, she reached up and began rubbing it directly onto the bruise around his eye.
Seeing the master and his normally estranged wife acting so intimately, the attending servants exchanged knowing smiles and quietly slipped out of the room.
As Qianli Xun finished spreading the ointment and pulled her hand back, Lin Jue suddenly grabbed her wrist. His eyes burned with an intense, complicated emotion. "Why are you suddenly treating me so well?"
Qianli Xun yanked her hand free, wiping her fingers on a cloth. "Didn't I just tell you? I'm kind-hearted. Don't go reading into it."
She set the bottle firmly on the table. "Make sure you reapply it tomorrow. Don't let my silver go to waste."
She turned to leave, but suddenly remembered her main objective. She spun back around. Lin Jue was still staring at her, looking utterly bewildered. "Hey! While we're here, why don't we lock in the time and place for the divorce negotiations?"
The word "divorce" snapped Lin Jue out of his trance. His face hardened. "The day after tomorrow. The hour of Si (9-11 AM). The main reception hall in the north courtyard."
"As you know, my memory is completely gone. I have no idea who our original matchmaker was. You will be in charge of hiring a matchmaker to officially witness the divorce."
"No problem."
"How many elders are you bringing to mediate?"
"Eight," Lin Jue replied coolly. "There would have been more, but I turned the rest away."
"What?! Eight?!" Qianli Xun panicked internally. She only had the Old Ancestor. In terms of numbers, she was going to be absolutely crushed. "I only have one person! You having eight is completely unfair. Can't you just bring one as well?!"
"It can't be helped. The Lin clan is large, and my uncles are... very enthusiastic." Lin Jue took a slow step closer, his eyes raking over her face. "How about this... why don't we just call off the divorce? You may not be the old Zhu Shuzhen, but to be honest, I like you much better this way. Why not just settle down and be my proper wife?"
Qianli Xun stared right back at him. He was absolutely not her type. Honestly, she didn't even know what her "type" was, but she knew for a fact it wasn't a wife-beating hypocrite. Besides, the terrible first impression he had made upon her transmigration was etched into her soul.
"Don't even dream about it. This marriage is over. Even if I only have one elder backing me up, I will tear this marriage contract to shreds."
Without another word, Qianli Xun turned and strode out the door.
Watching her retreating figure, a dark sneer curled Lin Jue's lips. "No... *you* are the one who should stop dreaming."
