针对你的审校需求，以下是几个细节上的优化建议，主要集中在**“现代俚语的地道化”**、**“古代黑话的翻译”**以及**“动作描写的节奏感”**上：

### 1. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 20: Encountering the Chen Dandy at the Teahouse*
*   **点评**：比较四平八稳。在网文里，女主暴揍纨绔子弟是一个非常经典的高潮桥段，标题可以更具动感。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 20: Beating Up the Wealthy Dandy** 或者 **Chapter 20: A Brawl at the Teahouse**。

### 2. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P19 女主的霸气自称（老娘）**：`take a punch from this queen!`
    *   **优化**：`this queen` 听起来像是在美剧里说 "yas queen"，稍显滑稽。在武侠/古装语境中，女性豪放地自称“老娘”，更地道的翻译是 **your grand-aunt** (姑奶奶) 或者是极其霸气的 **me** 配合前缀。但考虑到下一句纨绔子弟真的喊了“姑奶奶”，这里我们可以用更有江湖气的表达。
    *   **修改**：`"Chen Haoyu! Let's see if you can take a punch from me!"` (配合后面直接跳下去打脸，动作感更强)。
*   **P24 求饶的俚语（姑奶奶饶命）**：`Mighty lady, spare my life!`
    *   **优化**：`Mighty lady` 听起来像是在称呼超级英雄。中文里的“姑奶奶”是地痞流氓求饶时的经典用词，相当于承认对方辈分高、自己惹不起。
    *   **修改**：`"Ouch! Auntie, spare me! Have mercy!"`（Auntie 在这种语境下能完美传达“姑奶奶”那种滑稽的认怂感）。
*   **P32 俗语的翻译（好汉不吃眼前亏）**：`A wise man avoids danger when it's right in front of him.`
    *   **优化**：直译稍微有点长。英文中有一个完美对应“好汉不吃眼前亏/识时务者为俊杰”的谚语。
    *   **修改**：`Discretion is the better part of valor.`
*   **P2 娱乐场所的翻译（勾栏瓦肆）**：`entertainment quarters—gambling dens and brothels`
    *   **点评**：这个翻译**非常准确且地道！** 完美解释了古代的勾栏瓦肆。
*   **P37 装逼的翻译（本公子不跟你计较）**：`this young master won't hold it against you.`
    *   **优化**：他在挽尊。
    *   **修改**：`"I'll be the bigger man and let this go."`（我大人有大量，不跟你计较，更能体现纨绔子弟死要面子的可笑）。

### 3. 句子是否过长？标点与节奏是否合乎网文习惯？

**Chapter 20: Beating Up the Wealthy Dandy**

An Mingxuan had just finished whisking the tea and returned to his seat. Setting his bowl down, he scoffed, "If that dandy from the Chen family doesn't cause a scene every few days, he must get physically uncomfortable. But for him to actually cause trouble at the Yongtai Teahouse today... how unusual."
Cao Ban nodded in agreement. "True. He's a regular at the pleasure quarters—gambling dens and brothels. I certainly didn't expect him to suddenly develop the refined taste to visit a teahouse."
Miao Zhiyu's face twisted with utter disdain. "Hmph! Worthless dandies like him should be dragged off by the magistrate and locked in a cell for a few months. That would teach him how to behave."
Listening to them, Qianli Xun was completely lost. "Who exactly is this dandy you're all talking about?"
The four of them stopped and stared at her in sheer surprise.
Qianli Xun immediately realized her question had exposed a massive blind spot. The real Zhu Shuzhen definitely knew who this guy was.
"Doesn't Youqi Jushi know him?" Dongfang Chenze asked slowly.
Qianli Xun scrambled to cover her tracks. "There are so many useless dandies roaming the streets these days. How am I supposed to know which specific one you're talking about?" But the moment the words left her mouth, she knew it sounded like a flimsy excuse, only waving a massive red flag.
Seeing the situation turning awkward, Kuqiao quickly stepped up to save her miss. "Young Masters, Lady Miao, you have all seen the nasty wound on my Miss's forehead. Ever since that injury, her memory has been foggy. She struggles to remember certain people and events. Please forgive her."
The four friends looked at her, their surprise deepening into concern.
"Does Youqi Jushi still remember *us*?" Dongfang Chenze asked, voicing the group's sudden anxiety.
"I remember some! I remember some!" Qianli Xun quickly reassured them. "Otherwise, why would I have come straight to this poetry gathering?"
Dongfang Chenze breathed a sigh of relief. "That is good. Since Youqi Jushi cannot recall the Chen family's young master, allow me to refresh your memory. The Chen family is the wealthiest household in Lin'an. Their heir is named Chen Haoyu. He is an absolute wastrel, spending his days loitering in brothels or gambling dens. Relying on his family's vast wealth, he runs around with a pack of lackeys, bullying commoners wherever he goes. And it seems today, he has bullied his way into this very teahouse."
Qianli Xun absolutely despised men like that. Her fists literally itched. "I'm going down there to teach him a lesson!"
"Wait, don't!" Dongfang Chenze blocked her path. "The Chen family is connected to the Zhu family by marriage! Chen Haoyu's older sister is your sister-in-law, Youqi Jushi. If you go down there and publicly humiliate him, he will definitely run and complain to your brother and sister-in-law. Furthermore, Chen Haoyu knows martial arts, and his lackeys are all thugs. You are a fragile woman. If you go down there, you will only get hurt."
Just as they were arguing, an attendant from the teahouse burst into the room, his face pale with panic. "Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, Young Master An! You are all prominent figures in Lin'an, please, I beg you to help our teahouse! Young Master Chen saw that our female tea master was beautiful and insisted on harassing her. When she refused his advances, he ordered his men to tear the downstairs apart!"
Before the three gentlemen could even open their mouths, Qianli Xun was already on her feet. "I'm going down!"

The three young masters and Miao Zhiyu had no choice but to rush out of the private room after her. They peered over the wooden railing of the second floor.
The patrons who had been drinking tea downstairs had completely vanished. The entire ground floor was a disaster zone. Vases were smashed, elegant floral arrangements trampled into the floorboards. Expensive calligraphy and paintings had been ripped from the walls and were being actively stomped on by Chen Haoyu's thugs. The main tea-whisking table was flipped upside down, scattering precious tea powder and shattered ceramics everywhere.
Sitting right in the middle of the wreckage was Chen Haoyu. He had forcibly dragged the beautiful female tea master onto his lap and was pinning her there tightly.
Qianli Xun squinted at him. The guy actually had decent, aristocratic features, but his vile actions made him look like a complete monster.
"Since your stubbornness just cost this teahouse everything they own, you might as well give in to me," Chen Haoyu sneered, harassing the struggling woman. "Just entertain me for one night, and this young master promises to treat you very, very well."
"In your dreams!" the tea master spat, trying to shove him away. "Everyone knows that once a woman is played with by you, she ends up sold to a filthy brothel!"
Enraged by her rejection, Chen Haoyu barked an order to his men. "Keep smashing!" 
Immediately, his seven or eight lackeys resumed tearing the teahouse apart.
Qianli Xun had seen enough. "Let her go!" she roared.
The entire ground floor froze. Everyone looked up at the second-floor balcony.
Chen Haoyu burst into laughter. "I was wondering who dared to interrupt. If it isn't my dear relative by marriage! What was her name again? Ah, right... Zhu Shuzhen! Scurry back to your little room and keep writing your sad little poems. Don't meddle in adult affairs. Considering our families are in-laws, I'll let your disrespect slide."
"But I don't want to let your disrespect slide!" Qianli Xun shot back.
Chen Haoyu sneered mockingly. "And what exactly are you going to do about it? Compete with me in writing poetry? What a pity, I despise that flowery garbage. The only people who treat poetry like treasure are those three useless 'scholars' standing next to you! They have zero actual skills, so they pretend to be refined and write little songs. They aren't even real men!"
Insulted, Cao Ban lost his temper. "Chen Haoyu! You spend your days terrorizing innocent women! You are worse than pigs and dogs!"
Chen Haoyu laughed dismissively. "You call yourself a refined scholar of Lin'an, yet you open your mouth to spew insults about pigs and dogs. How uncivilized. You're ruining your pristine reputation, Cao."
"Chen Haoyu," An Mingxuan warned coldly, "this is a respectable teahouse, not one of the brothels you frequent. That tea master possesses extraordinary skills; she is not a woman of the night. Release her at once, or I will report you to the magistrate. You will not escape punishment."
"Hmph!" Chen Haoyu scoffed. "Your father is nothing but a lowly Intendant! Since when is it your turn to bark orders at me? Go ahead and report me! See if I care!"
"You may not fear the local authorities," Dongfang Chenze interjected smoothly, "but do you not fear the Emperor?"
"The Emperor is miles away in the capital! Why would His Majesty care which woman I choose to bed? Your threats are pathetic, Dongfang." Chen Haoyu smirked, leaning his face uncomfortably close to the struggling tea master.
The woman shrank back in absolute disgust.
"You just mocked us for writing poetry, claiming it makes us less than men," Dongfang Chenze continued, his voice dangerously calm. "That statement alone insults the Emperor. Our current Sovereign is not only a master calligrapher but an expert in poetry and song. If I were to write a letter to my friend in the capital, who would deliver it to Prince Yi, who would then present it directly to the Emperor... your entire Chen family would be annihilated simply because you couldn't control your arrogant mouth."
For a second, Chen Haoyu hesitated, before his arrogance returned. "Dongfang Chenze, the 'friend' you're talking about is Qiao Wenxian, isn't it? His nephew, Prince Yi, is a bedridden weakling who hasn't spoken a single word of influence in front of the Emperor in his entire life! You think I don't know that? Stop trying to bluff me."
Qianli Xun's patience officially snapped. "Chen Haoyu! Let's see if you can take a punch from me!" 
Using her *qinggong* (lightfoot technique), she vaulted directly over the second-floor railing. She plummeted toward the ground floor, landed squarely in front of Chen Haoyu, and drove a brutal, bone-shattering punch straight into his face.
*Smack!*
Chen Haoyu howled in pain. His grip loosened, and the female tea master instantly scrambled away to safety. Clutching his rapidly swelling face, Chen Haoyu stared at her in shock. "What the—?! Since when did you hit like that?!"
Up on the balcony, the scholars who had known Zhu Shuzhen for years stood with their jaws completely unhinged. They could not believe their eyes. Was this truly Youqi Jushi?!
Humiliated, Chen Haoyu screamed at his men. "Get her! Beat her into the ground right now!"
Seven or eight burly lackeys rushed forward, forming a tight circle around Qianli Xun. Standing in the center, her eyes darted alertly, tracking their movements. The moment they lunged at her in unison, Qianli Xun moved like lightning. She ducked under a swinging fist, slipped outside their encirclement, and slammed a palm into the first thug's chest, sending him flying. Before the rest could even process what had happened, she unleashed a devastating flurry of chained punches and sweeping kicks. 
Within seconds, every single lackey was crashing to the floor, writhing and groaning in agony. "Ah! My ribs! It hurts!"
Seeing his men decimated, Chen Haoyu gritted his teeth and charged her himself. He didn't even make it past two moves. Qianli Xun easily parried his strike, grabbed his wrist, and brutally twisted his arm up behind his back. 
"If I ever catch you bullying innocent people again, I'll beat you so hard your own mother won't recognize you!" she hissed in his ear.
Despite the pain, Chen Haoyu remained stubborn. "You're assaulting me! Wait until I tell Zhu Jingyuan and my sister! They'll make you pay for this!"
"Hmph! My brother and sister-in-law are the most reasonable people in the world. Once they find out what you did, they'll probably help me beat you!" Qianli Xun cranked his arm a few inches higher.
"Ouch! Ouch! Auntie, spare me! Have mercy!" Chen Haoyu finally shrieked, his knees buckling.
"Sparing you is easy," Qianli Xun demanded. "First, apologize to the tea master. Second, fully compensate the owner for everything you broke. Third, you are permanently banned from the Yongtai Teahouse!"
Chen Haoyu had never expected Zhu Shuzhen to possess such terrifying strength. Knowing he couldn't fight back, and realizing that discretion was the better part of valor, he gritted his teeth and caved. "Fine! I agree to your terms!"
Qianli Xun released his arm and shoved him violently toward the tea master. "Apologize! Now! Or my fist is going straight into your other eye!"
Trembling, Chen Haoyu bowed stiffly to the woman. "I am sorry. I will never harass you again."
The tea master didn't even want to look at him. She turned her head away in sheer disgust.
Fishing a heavy pouch of silver from his robes, Chen Haoyu slammed it onto the only table left standing. "Zhu Shuzhen, look closely. I am leaving the compensation right here." He immediately turned to leave.
"Hold it!" Qianli Xun barked. "I don't know if that's enough to cover the damages yet. Nobody leaves until the money is counted."
The teahouse attendant nervously scurried over, opened the pouch, and did a rapid count. "It's enough, Miss! There's actually extra!"
Chen Haoyu sneered, adjusting his disheveled robes to regain some dignity. "The Chen family is the richest in Lin'an. Silver is the one thing we never lack. Distrusting me like this is a massive insult. But... seeing as your martial arts are surprisingly decent, I'll be the bigger man and let this slide."
He kicked one of his groaning lackeys. "What are you idiots still on the floor for?! Get up! We're leaving! I'm never stepping foot in this dump again!"
Just as he reached the doors, he paused and threw one last glare over his shoulder. "Zhu Shuzhen... you better pray you never fall into my hands." With that pathetic final threat, he swaggered out into the street.
Once the coast was clear, the female tea master, the owner, and all the attendants rushed forward, bowing profusely to thank Qianli Xun.
The scholars and Miao Zhiyu slowly descended the stairs, staring at Zhu Shuzhen as if looking at a complete stranger.
Miao Zhiyu was the first to speak, her voice trembling. "Youqi Jushi... when did you secretly master martial arts? I have never seen you like this. If you are this incredibly skilled... why did you arrive at our gatherings covered in bruises so many times? Is the Military Training Officer truly that much stronger than you?!"
Qianli Xun froze, having absolutely no idea how to explain that one. She could only remain silent, offering an incredibly awkward, strained smile.
Dongfang Chenze chuckled, stepping in to break the tension. "Youqi Jushi truly hides her depths well. Not only is she a genius at poetry, but her martial prowess is genuinely terrifying. You have forced us men to look at you with entirely new respect. No wonder Brother Qiao..."
He trailed off, his words laden with a hidden meaning Qianli Xun couldn't even begin to decipher. 
Miao Zhiyu, however, caught it immediately and looked alarmed. "What about Young Master Qiao?!"
Realizing he had spoken out of turn, Dongfang Chenze quickly waved his fan. "It is nothing, nothing. Today's gathering was quite thrilling, but the teahouse clearly needs time to clean up this disaster. I believe it is time we took our leave."
With the excitement over, the group gathered their maids and pageboys and departed the ruined teahouse. Along the way, they strolled by the breathtaking scenery of the West Lake, eventually bidding their farewells and returning to their respective homes.
