# Chapter 20: Take a Punch from This Queen

An Mingxuan had finished making tea and returned to his seat, setting down the tea bowl. "That Chen family dandy stirs up trouble every now and then. If he doesn't cause a scene for a while, he gets uncomfortable. Today he's actually causing trouble at Yongtai Teahouse. How unusual."

Cao Ban added, "True. He's a regular at the entertainment quarters—gambling dens and brothels. I didn't expect him to have the refined taste to visit a teahouse today."

Miao Zhiyu was utterly disdainful. "Hmph! Dandies like him should be arrested by the authorities and locked up in prison for a while. That would teach him to behave."

Qianli Xun was quite puzzled. "Who exactly is this dandy you're talking about?"

The four of them stared at Zhu Shuzhen in surprise.

Qianli Xun immediately realized this question had given her away. Zhu Shuzhen must surely know this dandy.

Dongfang Chenze asked, "Doesn't Youqi Jushi know him?"

Qianli Xun tried to cover up. "There are too many dandies in this world. How would I know which one you're talking about?" As soon as she finished speaking, she realized this was like waving a red flag—only drawing more suspicion.

Kuqiao saw the situation was awkward and quickly explained on her miss's behalf. "Three young masters, Lady Miao, you've all seen the wound on my miss's forehead. Because of this injury, she can't remember some people and things. Please forgive her."

The four of them looked surprised again.

Dongfang Chenze asked on everyone's behalf, "Does Youqi Jushi still remember us?"

Qianli Xun replied, "I remember some, remember some. Otherwise, why would I come to the poetry gathering?"

Dongfang Chenze continued, "That's good, that's good. Since Youqi Jushi can't remember the Chen family young master, let me tell you. The Chen family is the richest in Lin'an. The young master's name is Chen Haoyu. He's good for nothing—spending his days either loitering in brothels or gambling dens. Relying on his family's wealth, he often leads a group of lackeys around, bullying people everywhere. Today he's bullying his way into the teahouse."

Qianli Xun usually hated this kind of person the most. "Let me go teach him a lesson!"

Dongfang Chenze stopped her. "The Chen family is connected to the Zhu family by marriage — Chen Haoyu's sister married into the Zhu family. She's your brother's wife, Youqi Jushi. If you go teach him a lesson, be careful he doesn't run crying to your brother and sister-in-law. Besides, Chen Haoyu knows some martial arts, and so do the men he brings along. You're a woman alone. If you go, you'll only get the worst of it."

As they spoke, the teahouse attendant came in, his face full of worry. He pleaded, "Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, Young Master An, you're all famous young masters in Lin'an. Please help the teahouse. Young Master Chen saw that the female tea master was beautiful and insisted on bothering her. When she wouldn't give in, he caused a huge scene downstairs."

Before the three young masters could speak, Qianli Xun stood up. "I'll go!"

The three young masters and Miao Zhiyu could only follow her out of the private room. They peered down from the second floor.

The tea guests downstairs had already disappeared. Everywhere was a mess. The flower arrangements on the tables were thrown on the ground. The calligraphy and paintings on the walls were torn—some thrown on the ground and trampled under the feet of the men Chen Haoyu brought. The tea-making table was overturned. Tea powder and tea utensils were scattered all over the floor.

Chen Haoyu sat on a chair, holding a female tea master on his lap, gripping her tightly.

Qianli Xun studied Chen Haoyu carefully. He looked the part of a proper gentleman, but his actions were a different story entirely.

They heard Chen Haoyu messing with her. "Because of you, the whole teahouse has taken a beating. Just give in to me. As long as you spend one night with me, I won't treat you badly."

The female tea master said, "Don't even think about it! Everyone knows that after being played by you, the only fate is to be sold to a brothel."

Chen Haoyu shouted orders loudly. "Keep smashing!" As soon as he finished speaking, the seven or eight lackeys he brought started destroying the teahouse again.

Qianli Xun really couldn't watch anymore. She shouted, "Let her go!"

Everyone glanced upstairs.

Chen Haoyu smirked. "I thought it was someone. It's actually the relative by marriage. What's her name again? Right... Zhu... Zhu Shuzhen. Go back quickly. Keep writing your poetry. Don't cause trouble. Since the Zhu and Chen families are connected by marriage, I'll let it slide this time."

Qianli Xun said, "But I want to take issue with you!"

Chen Haoyu sneered. "What will you use to take issue with me? Do you want to compete with me in writing poetry? It's a pity I despise that stuff the most. Only those three young masters beside you treat poetry like treasure. They have no other skills. They have no choice but to pretend to be refined, writing poetry and ci. They're not like real men!"

Cao Ban was angered and couldn't help cursing. "Chen Haoyu! You bully decent women every day. You're worse than pigs and dogs!"

Chen Haoyu chuckled. "You're called a famous young master in Lin'an, but you open your mouth and talk about pigs and dogs. How uncivilized. It damages your good reputation."

An Mingxuan said, "Chen Haoyu, this is a teahouse, not the brothels you often visit. The female tea master has extraordinary tea-making skills. She's not a brothel woman. Let her go, or I'll report you to the authorities. You'll be in for it."

Chen Haoyu sneered. "Hmph! Your father is just an intendant. When is it your turn to make a scene here? If you've got the guts, go report me. I'm not afraid at all!"

Dongfang Chenze asked, "You're not afraid of the authorities, but are you afraid of the Emperor?"

"The Emperor is far away in Bianliang. How would he have time to care about which woman I like? Young Master Dongfang's question is really ridiculous." Chen Haoyu leaned in close, his lips nearly touching the female tea master's ear.

The female tea master dodged to the side in disgust.

Dongfang Chenze continued, "You just said we're not like real men for writing poetry and ci. Those words have already offended the Emperor. The current Emperor not only writes well — he's skilled in poetry, ci, and songs. If I write a letter to my friend in Bianliang, have him deliver it to Prince Yi, and Prince Yi presents it to the Emperor, your Chen family will pay the price for your reckless words."

"Dongfang Chenze, the friend you're talking about must be Qiao Wenxian, right? His nephew Prince Yi is a sickly weakling. He can't say a word in front of the Emperor. You think I don't know? Stop trying to scare me." Chen Haoyu looked extremely arrogant.

Qianli Xun couldn't stand it anymore. "Chen Haoyu! If you've got the guts, take a punch from this queen!" She vaulted from the second floor using qinggong, landed right in front of Chen Haoyu, and drove a solid punch into his face.

Chen Haoyu was hit hard. His hand loosened, and the female tea master quickly escaped. Chen Haoyu covered half his face with his hand. "Ha! When did you become so powerful?"

Everyone upstairs who knew Zhu Shuzhen stood with their jaws dropped. They couldn't believe the person before them was Youqi Jushi.

Chen Haoyu ordered the lackeys beside him. "Get her! Beat her down for me right now!"

Seven or eight lackeys came forward and formed a circle. Qianli Xun stood in the middle, watching them alertly. When they all threw punches at her, Qianli Xun was agile. She skillfully dodged and slipped outside the circle. She knocked one man to the ground with a sharp blow. Before the others could react, she unleashed a flurry of punches and kicks, sending all the lackeys crashing to the floor. They lay on the ground one by one, shouting, "That hurts! That hurts!"

Seeing this, Chen Haoyu personally charged in. After only two moves, Qianli Xun twisted his arm behind his back. "From now on, if this queen catches you bullying people again, I'll beat you every single time!"

Chen Haoyu talked tough. "You're bullying me. Be careful, I'll tell Zhu Jingyuan and my sister. Let them teach you a lesson."

"Hmph! My brother and sister-in-law are the most reasonable people. I'm afraid once they find out, they'll help me get you!" Qianli Xun twisted Chen Haoyu's arm tighter.

Chen Haoyu was in so much pain he kept shouting, "Ouch! Auntie, spare me! Have mercy!"

Qianli Xun said, "If you want me to spare your life, it's easy. First, apologize to the female tea master. Then compensate the teahouse. And from now on, don't come to Yongtai Teahouse!"

Chen Haoyu didn't expect Zhu Shuzhen to be so strong. He couldn't fight back. A wise man avoids danger when it's right in front of him. He could only agree. "Fine, I agree to your terms."

Qianli Xun let go and pushed Chen Haoyu in front of the female tea master. "Apologize quickly! Or this queen's fists will do the talking!"

Chen Haoyu was scared and could only say to the female tea master, "I'm sorry. I won't mess with you anymore."

The female tea master didn't want to pay attention to Chen Haoyu at all. She turned her head away in disgust.

Chen Haoyu took out a bag of silver and put it on a tea table that hadn't been kicked over. "Zhu Shuzhen, take a good look. I've put the money to compensate the teahouse right here." After speaking, he was about to leave.

"Wait!" Qianli Xun stopped him. "I don't know if the money is enough. Let me check before you go."

The teahouse attendant went over and opened the money bag. He roughly counted it. "It's enough. There's even extra."

Chen Haoyu grinned. "The Chen family is the richest in Lin'an. Silver is the one thing we never lack. You distrust me like this? That's the greatest disrespect to me. Considering your martial arts are impressive, I'll let it slide."

Then he said to all the lackeys, "What are you still standing there for! Let's go! I won't be coming back to this dump!"

Just as he reached the door, Chen Haoyu turned back. "Zhu Shuzhen, you better watch out next time." With that, he strode out with a swagger.

The female tea master, the teahouse owner, and the attendants all came to thank her.

The young masters and Miao Zhiyu upstairs also walked down the stairs, staring at Zhu Shuzhen as if she were a stranger.

Miao Zhiyu asked first, "When did Youqi Jushi secretly learn martial arts? I've never seen you like this before. Since you're so skilled, why did you come with injuries so many times? Is the Military Training Officer even more powerful than you?"

Qianli Xun didn't know how to answer. She could only stay silent, smiling awkwardly.

Dongfang Chenze said, "Youqi Jushi hides her talents well. Not only does she write poetry and ci well, her martial arts are also impressive. You make us men look at you with new respect. No wonder Brother Qiao..."

Dongfang Chenze's words had deeper meaning. Qianli Xun couldn't guess what it was. But Miao Zhiyu was very concerned. "What about Young Master Qiao?"

Dongfang Chenze didn't dare to say directly. "It's nothing. Today's poetry gathering was enjoyable enough. The teahouse has been through enough today and needs to be repaired. Let's go."

Everyone left the teahouse with their maids and page boys. Along the way, they admired the beauty of West Lake for a while. After saying goodbye outside West Lake, they each returned home.
