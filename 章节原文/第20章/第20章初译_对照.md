第20章 茶楼偶遇陈纨绔
## Chapter 20: Running Into the Chen Dandy at the Teahouse

安明轩做完茶已回到他的位置，放下茶盏：“陈家的纨绔子弟隔一段时间不出来闹一回，就不舒服，今儿竟然闹到永泰茶楼，真是稀奇。”
Back at his seat, An Mingxuan set down his bowl. "That Chen family dandy can't go two weeks without causing a scene—odd he dragged it into Yongtai Teahouse today."

曹班接着说：“也是，他可是勾栏瓦肆，赌场妓馆的常客，没想到今儿竟然有雅兴来茶楼。”
Cao Ban chimed in, "Right—he practically lives in Goulan Wasi, gambling dens, and brothels. Since when does he have the taste for a teahouse?"

苗知瑜十分不屑：“哼！这等纨绔子弟，就应该被官府抓起来在狱中关些时日，也就老实了。”
Miao Zhiyu sneered in disgust. "Hmph! A dandy like that belongs locked up in the yamen jail for a spell to teach him manners."

千里寻很是不解：“你们说的纨绔子弟到底是谁？”
Confused, Qianli Xun asked, "Who is this dandy you're talking about?"

四人惊讶地瞧着朱淑真。
All four stared at her in astonishment.

千里寻立刻意识到这个问题出卖了自己，朱淑真定然认识这位纨绔子弟。
Oops—she'd just given herself away! Zhu Shuzhen clearly knew this guy.

东方尘泽问：“幽栖居士不是认得他吗？”
"Don't you recognize him, Youqi Jushi?" Dongfang Chenze asked.

千里寻努力掩饰：“这个世道，纨绔子弟太多，我怎么知道你们说的是哪位？”刚说完，又意识到这话只不过是此地无银三百两而已。
Qianli Xun tried to bluff: "Plenty of dandies roam the streets—how should I know which one you mean?" The words barely left her lips before she realized how hollow the excuse sounded.

苦荞见情势不对，赶紧替小姐解释：“三位公子，苗姑娘，你们也看到我家小姐额头上有伤，因为这伤，有些人和事她记不得，还请见谅。”
Sensing danger, Kuqiao jumped in fast: "Young masters, Lady Miao—you saw the cut on Miss's forehead. That blow left her forgetting quite a few faces and events. Please forgive her."

四位又露出惊讶的表情。
Fresh astonishment rippled across all four faces.

东方尘泽帮大家询问：“幽栖居士可还记得我们？”
Speaking for the group, Dongfang Chenze asked, "Do you still remember us, Youqi Jushi?"

千里寻回道：“记得一些，记得一些，要不怎么跑来参加诗会？”
"Bits and pieces—why else would I show up for the salon?"

东方尘泽继续说：“那便好，那便好。既然幽栖居士记不得陈家公子，我给幽栖居士说说。陈家是临安首富，陈公子名叫陈浩宇，不学无术，整日不是流连青楼就是赌坊，仗着家里有钱，还经常带着一群小弟到处欺负人，今儿又欺负到茶楼里来。”
"Good, good! Since you don't recall Young Master Chen, let me fill you in. The Chens are the richest family in Lin'an. Their son Chen Haoyu is an idle good-for-nothing who haunts brothels and gambling houses, throws his wealth around, and drags thugs along to bully folks—now bringing his mess into a teahouse."

千里寻平日最讨厌这种人：“待我去教训教训他！”
People like that made Qianli Xun's blood boil. "Let me go teach him a lesson!"

东方尘泽阻止道：“陈家与朱家联姻，陈浩宇的姐姐正是幽栖居士的嫂嫂，你去教训他，小心他在你哥嫂面前告状，再说陈浩宇有些拳脚功夫，带的那帮人也是，你是弱女子，去了只有吃亏的份儿。”
Dongfang Chenze stopped her at once. "The Chens are your in-laws—Chen Haoyu's older sister is your sister-in-law! Cross him and he'll tattle to your brother and sister-in-law. Worse, he and his cronies know rough brawling—a delicate lady like you would only get hurt!"

说话间，茶楼的伙计进来，满面愁容，求道：“东方公子，曹公子，安公子，你们都是临安城有名的公子，麻烦你们帮帮茶楼，陈公子见女茶师长得美貌，非要调 戏，调 戏不成，在楼下大闹起来。”
Right then the teahouse waiter rushed in, wringing his hands. "Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, Young Master An—you're respected gentlemen of Lin'an, please save our teahouse! Young Master Chen took a fancy to our female tea performer, tried forcing himself on her, and when she resisted, started smashing the place downstairs!"

没等三位公子说话，千里寻站起来：“我去！”
Before the three men could open their mouths, Qianli Xun shot to her feet. "I'm going!"

三位公子与苗知瑜只能跟着出了雅间，他们从二楼向下瞧去。
The three scholars and Miao Zhiyu had no choice but to trail out of the booth and peer down over the second-floor railing.

只见楼下的茶客已不见踪影，到处一片狼藉。桌上的插花被扔在地上；墙上的字画有些被撕烂，有些扔在地上，被陈家公子带的人踩在脚下；做茶的桌子被掀翻，茶粉和茶具也都撒落一地。
Every customer downstairs had fled, leaving utter wreckage behind. Flower arrangements lay smashed across the floor; wall scrolls hung ripped or trampled under thugs' boots; the tea table lay overturned, tea powder and utensils scattered everywhere.

陈浩宇坐在一个椅子上，将一位女茶师搂坐在腿上，紧紧箍住。
Seated on a chair, Chen Haoyu had pinned a female tea performer across his lap, gripping her tight.

千里寻仔细瞧陈浩宇，长得倒人模狗样，做下的事与那长相实在不匹配。
Qianli Xun sized him up—handsome enough on the outside, rotten to the core inside.

只听陈浩宇调戏道：“因为你整个茶楼都遭殃，你就从了我，只要陪我一晚上，本公子不会亏待你。”
Chen Haoyu leered at the struggling woman. "The whole teahouse is paying for your stubbornness! Give in already—spend one night with me and I'll treat you right."

女茶师说：“你休想！谁不知道被你玩弄，只有被卖到青楼的下场。”
"In your dreams!" the tea performer spat. "Everyone knows girls you toy with end up sold to brothels!"

陈浩宇大声命令道：“继续给我砸！”话音刚落，只见他带的七八个小弟又在茶楼内破坏起来。
"Keep smashing!" Chen Haoyu barked. Seven or eight cronies resumed wrecking tables and chairs.

千里寻实在看不下去，大喊道：“放开她！”
Unable to stomach another second, Qianli Xun shouted from above: "Let her go!"

所有人向楼上瞧去。
Every head snapped upstairs.

陈浩宇笑道：“我当是谁，原来是亲家姑娘，叫什么来着，对朱……朱淑真，快回去，好好做你的诗，别瞎胡闹，看在朱陈两家是亲家，我不跟你计较。”
Chen Haoyu broke into a mocking grin. "Well, well—who do we have here? My dear in-law! What was your name again... oh right, Zhu—Zhu Shuzhen! Go back upstairs and write your little poems, don't meddle! Out of respect for our families being in-laws, I'll let you off."

千里寻说：“可我想跟你计较！”
"Too bad I'm not letting you off!" Qianli Xun snapped back.

陈浩宇冷笑道：“你用什么和我计较？难道想和本公子比做诗？只可惜本公子最瞧不上那玩意儿，也只有你身边那三位公子把诗词当个宝，他们呀，没别的本事，不得已才附庸风雅，吟诗作词，实在不像男人！”
Chen Haoyu laughed cold. "And how do you plan to take me on? A poetry duel? Spare me—I have zero use for that junk! Only those three softies beside you treat verse like treasure because they can't do anything else—affecting literary airs and reciting lyrics like a bunch of sissies!"

曹班被气到，不由骂道：“陈浩宇！你整日欺负良家妇女，连猪狗都不如！”
Stung, Cao Ban cursed back: "Chen Haoyu! Preying on respectable women every day—you're lower than pigs and dogs!"

陈浩宇笑道：“还号称临安有名的公子，张口就说猪狗，实在不文雅，有损你的美名。”
Chen Haoyu smirked. "Calls himself a famed scholar of Lin'an and talks of pigs and dogs—not very refined! Mind your precious reputation!"

安明轩说：“陈浩宇，这是茶楼，不是你常去的烟花柳巷之地，女茶师身怀茶艺绝技，不是青楼女子，你还是放了她，否则我告到官府，有你好果子吃。”
An Mingxuan stepped forward. "Chen Haoyu, this is a teahouse, not your usual brothel haunt! This tea performer is a skilled artisan, not a courtesan. Release her now, or I report this to the yamen and watch you pay!"

陈浩宇冷笑道：“哼！你爹不过是个提举，什么时候轮到你在这里大呼小喝，有本事你去告，本公子根本不怕！”
"Hmph! Your dad's just an Intendant—who gave you leave to bark at me?" Chen Haoyu sneered. "Go ahead and report me! Think I'm scared?"

东方尘泽问：“你不怕官府，可怕官家？”
Dongfang Chenze spoke cool and steady: "Not scared of the local yamen—what about His Majesty?"

“官家远在汴梁，哪有时间管我喜欢个把女人？东方公子的问题实在可笑。”陈浩宇将嘴巴靠近女茶师。
"His Majesty sits way off in Bianliang—think he has time to care if I fancy a girl or two? Don't make me laugh, Dongfang Chenze!" Chen Haoyu leaned his face toward the tea performer.

女茶师厌恶地躲闪到一边。
She twisted away in revulsion.

东方尘泽继续说：“你刚说我们吟诗作词不像男人，此话已得罪官家，当今的官家不仅字写得好，诗词歌赋无一不通，如若我给汴梁的朋友写封信，让他送到仪王手中，再由仪王呈给官家，到时你们陈家会因你口出狂言而遭殃。”
"You just sneered that men who compose poetry aren't real men," Dongfang Chenze pressed on. "That insults His Majesty himself—our sovereign excels at calligraphy, poetry, and lyrics alike! One letter from me to my friend in Bianliang, passed to Prince Yi and straight to His Majesty, and your entire Chen clan pays for your loose tongue!"

“东方尘泽，你说的朋友该不会是乔文仙吧？他那外甥仪王是个病秧子，在官家面前根本说不上话，你以为我不知？少在这里吓唬我。”陈浩宇的样子十分嚣张。
"Talking about Qiao Wenxian? His nephew Prince Yi is a sickly prince who has zero pull before His Majesty! Think I don't know? Quit trying to bluff me!" Chen Haoyu swaggered even harder.

千里寻已看不下去：“陈浩宇！有本事吃老娘一拳！”她用轻功从二楼跳到一楼，一拳打在陈浩宇的脸上。
Done listening, Qianli Xun snapped: "Chen Haoyu! Eat my fist!" Using light-foot skill, she vaulted clean over the second-floor railing, landed below, and drove a punch square into Chen Haoyu's face!

陈浩宇被打疼，手一松，女茶师迅速逃离。陈浩宇用手捂着半边脸：“吆喝！你什么时候这么厉害？”
Yelping in pain, he loosened his grip; the tea performer tore free and bolted. Clutching half his face, Chen Haoyu stared. "Whoa! Since when can you hit like that?!"

楼上认识朱淑真的人也都张大嘴巴，怎么都无法相信眼前的人是幽栖居士。
Upstairs, every friend who knew Zhu Shuzhen stood with jaws on the floor—was that really Youqi Jushi?!

陈浩宇吩咐身边的小弟：“给我上！立刻把她给我打趴下！”
"Get her!" Chen Haoyu barked at his thugs. "Beat her flat right now!"

七八个小弟上来，围成一圈。千里寻站在中间，警惕地看着他们。当他们伸出拳头一起打过来时，千里寻身手敏捷，巧妙躲开，来到圈外，将一人打倒在地，趁其他人还没反应过来，迅速用连环拳和脚上功夫，将那些小弟全部打倒在地。他们一个个倒在地上直喊：“疼死我了！”
Seven or eight toughs closed in around Qianli Xun. Calm and sharp, she tracked every stance. The instant fists flew from every angle, she slipped outside their ring with agile footwork, dropped one man flat, and before the rest could pivot, unleashed a blur of rapid punches and kicks that put every last thug on the floor groaning, "Ow! It hurts!"

陈浩宇见状，亲自上阵，没打两下，被千里寻扭转臂膀：“以后老娘再见你欺负人，见一次打一次！”
Chen Haoyu jumped in himself—two moves later, Qianli Xun twisted his arm behind his back. "Catch you bullying anyone again, and I beat you every single time!"

陈浩宇嘴硬道：“你欺负我，小心我告诉朱景元和我姐，让他们替我收拾你。”
Still talking tough, Chen Haoyu hissed, "Dare touch me? Watch me tell Zhu Jingyuan and my sister to deal with you!"

“哼！我哥哥和嫂嫂最通情达理，只怕他们知道后会帮我收拾你！”千里寻又将陈浩宇的手臂拧紧。
"Hmph! My brother and sister-in-law are fair people—they'll probably help me thrash you!" She wrenched his arm higher.

陈浩宇疼得不行，直喊：“哎吆！姑奶奶饶命！”
Agony shot up his shoulder. "Ow! Mercy, Granny, mercy!"

千里寻说：“想让我饶命容易，先给女茶师道歉，再给茶楼赔钱，还有，往后不准来永泰茶楼！”
"Easy! First, apologize to the tea performer. Second, pay for the wrecked teahouse. Third, never set foot in Yongtai Teahouse again!"

陈浩宇没想到朱淑真的力气如此大，他难以反抗，好汉不吃眼前亏，只能答应：“好，我答应你。”
Outmatched by her iron grip and knowing better than to fight a losing battle, Chen Haoyu folded. "Fine, fine—I agree!"

千里寻松开手，一把将陈浩宇推到女茶师面前：“赶紧道歉！否则老娘的拳头打得就是你！”
Letting go, Qianli Xun shoved him straight toward the tea performer. "Apologize now—or my fist talks next!"

陈浩宇害怕，只能对女茶师说：“对不起，以后我不会再调戏你。”
Cowed, Chen Haoyu muttered, "I'm sorry. I won't harass you again."

女茶师根本不想理会陈浩宇，厌恶地扭过头。
Disgusted, the tea performer turned her head away without a glance.

陈浩宇从身上掏出一袋银子放在一旁没有被踢翻的茶桌上：“朱淑真，你看清楚，本公子把赔茶楼的银子放在这里了。”说完就要走。
Pulling a pouch of silver from his belt, Chen Haoyu tossed it onto an upright table. "See that, Zhu Shuzhen? Payment for the teahouse right there." He turned to leave.

“慢着！”千里寻阻止道，“银子够不够我还不知道，等验完再走。”
"Hold it!" Qianli Xun halted him. "Haven't checked if it's enough yet—stay put till they count!"

茶楼的伙计过去把钱袋打开，大概数数：“够，还多了。”
A waiter opened the pouch and did a quick count. "Enough—more than enough!"

陈浩宇笑道：“陈家是临安首富，最不缺的就是银子，你如此不信任我，就是对我最大的不敬。看在你功夫了得，本公子不跟你计较。”
Chen Haoyu forced a cocky laugh. "The Chens are the richest clan in Lin'an—silver's the one thing we're never short on! Doubting my word is an insult. Seeing how tough your fists are, I'll let it slide."

然后对所有小弟说：“还愣着干什么！走！这破地方以后本公子不会再来！”
Glaring at his bruised thugs, he barked, "What are you lying around for? Move! Never coming back to this dump anyway!"

刚走到门口，陈浩宇又回过头：“朱淑真，以后可别栽到我手里。”说完扬长而去。
At the doorway he looked back over his shoulder. "Watch yourself, Zhu Shuzhen—don't ever fall into my hands!" With that, he swaggered off.

女茶师、茶楼的掌柜、伙计纷纷前来感谢。
The tea performer, shopkeeper, and waiters crowded around pouring out gratitude.

楼上的公子和苗知瑜也都从楼梯走下来，用陌生的目光盯着朱淑真。
Upstairs, the three scholars and Miao Zhiyu came down the steps, staring at Zhu Shuzhen as if meeting a stranger.

苗知瑜先问：“幽栖居士什么时候悄悄学了功夫？以前从未见过你这样。你既如此厉害，为何多次带伤而来，难道练兵官比你还厉害？”
Miao Zhiyu asked first, "Youqi Jushi, when on earth did you learn martial arts? Never saw anything like that before! If you're this skilled, why did you always come to salons covered in bruises—is the Training Officer even stronger?"

千里寻不知如何回答，只能保持沉默，尴尬地笑着。
At a loss for an excuse, Qianli Xun kept silent behind an awkward grin.

东方尘泽说：“幽栖居士深藏不露，不仅诗词写得好，功夫也了得，令我等男儿刮目相看，难怪乔兄他……”
Dongfang Chenze stepped in with a smile. "Youqi Jushi hides her depths well—gifted in verse and deadly in combat, putting us men to shame! No wonder Brother Qiao..."

东方尘泽话中有话，千里寻无法猜出，倒是苗知瑜关心不已：“乔公子怎么了？”
His trailing words puzzled Qianli Xun, yet Miao Zhiyu caught fire at once: "What about Young Master Qiao?"

东方尘泽不便明说：“没什么，今日诗会也算尽兴，茶楼遭此一劫也需修整，我们还是走吧。”
Choosing discretion, Dongfang Chenze waved it off. "Nothing. Today's salon was thrilling enough, and the teahouse needs time to tidy up—let's call it a day."

大家带着丫鬟和小厮离开茶楼，沿途又欣赏一会儿西湖美景，在西湖外道别后各自归家。
Leading their maids and pages out of the teahouse, they strolled a bit longer along scenic West Lake before parting ways outside the lakeside path.
