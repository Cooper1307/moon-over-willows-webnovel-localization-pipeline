## Chapter 158: A Springtime Invitation

接下来的冬日过得倒也平静。
千里寻去过几次幽林别居，只为陪伴梦容，互相解闷儿。遇到仪王，相处起来与往日大不相同，起码从内心来讲，已没有距离感，他们之间的聊天儿如同老朋友一般，事实上本就是老朋友。
过春节那日，朱晞颜竟然破天荒地派人将女儿叫回朱府。千里寻没有拒绝，乖乖回去。回去后避免提到退婚之事，她不想惹得父亲不高兴，破坏新年气氛，一切等年后再说。
再没见过文渊与苗知瑜，也许是他们有意避开，也许是命运安排大家最好不见，无论如何，他们出现或不出现，对千里寻来讲都已经无所谓。
开始写新话本，还要训练十名下人练习千里拳，千里寻忙得不亦乐乎。
叫红豆的丫鬟已练得一身拳法，千里寻有意将她带在身边。出门时，身边带着三个丫鬟，实在有面儿。
正月十五以后，冷过一段时日，天气开始回暖。
当枯草焕发生机，神柳的枝条上冒出新芽时，乔家竟然派人送来请帖。
千里寻接过打开一看，原来乔家要搞捶丸比赛，邀请她参加。
正当犹豫之际，嫂嫂陈文心来到望月轩，没有让下人禀报，自顾进入。在下人的眼中，陈文心是家人，家人无须禀报。
陈文心一进屋便问：“淑真，你可收到乔家的请帖？”
紧接着看到淑真手中正拿着请帖：“看来你已收到，乔家真是过分，给公爹和婆婆送也就罢了，还给你送，是几个意思？千万不要去！”
千里寻将请帖放在桌上，迎上去：“嫂嫂难道是为此事而来？”
“那还用说？婆婆知道乔家邀请众人去捶丸，担心也邀请你，让我过来瞧瞧。”
“嫂嫂坐下说。”
千里寻拉陈文心在椅子上坐下，吩咐苦荞做两盏茶。
陈文心坐下，继续拉开话匣子：“乔老爷的夫人爱捶丸，隔上一两年搞那么一次，烦都烦死，要不是因为他们家是皇亲国戚，又租用陈家的捶丸场地，我才懒得去！”
千里寻故意开玩笑：“我猜嫂嫂肯定是因乔家租用陈家的场地，担心乔家把场地给搞坏，才跑去监督。”
“你嫂嫂我有那么闲吗？以前没嫁到朱家，是代表陈家去，现在嫁到朱家，公爹婆婆都不去，派我和你哥去，没办法，只能走一趟。我虽不喜欢乔家，面儿上的功夫还是不能少。”自从乔家伤害了淑真，陈文心便对乔家更加不满。
千里寻开始犹豫要不要去。
去吧，不想面对乔家人以及文渊和苗知瑜，尽管已见过他们，也检验过面对他们的勇气，但在千里寻看来，能不见还是不要再见。
不去吧，又担心别人觉得她故意逃避，反而更显得她还未放下与文渊的感情。
陈文心见淑真不说话又问道：“你是怎么想的？去还是不去？”
没等千里寻回答，韦嬷嬷正好进来：“为何不去？我想问姑娘，若是别家邀请，你会不会去？”
穿越前，千里寻打过几次高尔夫球，因千里拳功力影响，学得很快，而且打得不错。
后来写网络小说因情节需要，她专门了解过古代的捶丸，发现两者之间很相似，既然有机会实战，何必想那么多。
“当然去，去了可以好好玩玩儿。可现在是乔家邀请，我……”
韦嬷嬷坐下：“依我看，乔家与别家没有什么区别，再说姑娘已有新的婚约，没必要再纠结。”
嬷嬷的话简单明了，千里寻不再犹豫：“嬷嬷说的有道理，乔家既然敢送来请帖，我便敢去。”
韦嬷嬷赞道：“这就对了！”
陈文心笑道：“呵呵，我看婆婆的担心都是多余，有韦嬷嬷在，这种小事无需我们操心，三言两语便解决。”
The rest of winter rolled by in calm peace. Qianli Xun visited Youlin Retreat several times to keep Mengrong company and chase off winter boredom. Crossing paths with Prince Yi felt nothing like before: zero awkward walls, bantering easy like the old friends they truly were.

韦嬷嬷提到与陈浩宇的婚约，千里寻又高兴不起来，嫂嫂在此，她不好说什么。上次跟陈浩宇闹过后，一直未见过他，也不知陈家老太太现在是否还想耗着？
On New Year's Day, Zhu Xiyan broke his own silence and sent word summoning his daughter home to Zhu Manor. Qianli Xun went without fuss, steering clear of any mention of canceling the betrothal so she would not sour the holiday; plenty of time to fight after New Year! Neither Wenyuan nor Miao Zhiyu crossed her path once. Whether they avoided her on purpose or fate kept their orbits apart, Qianli Xun could not care less anymore. Between drafting a brand-new huaben and drilling ten household servants in Qianli Fist, her days stayed packed and lively. A maid named Hongdou picked up Qianli Fist so sharp that Qianli Xun promoted her to personal attendant; stepping out flanked by three maids felt pretty grand.

“嫂嫂，陈家谁会去？”
After the Lantern Festival came one last cold snap before spring warmth crept back. Just as pale grass turned green and fresh buds broke along the Divine Willow's boughs, a messenger arrived from Qiao Manor bearing an invitation card. Opening it, Qianli Xun blinked: the Qiaos were hosting a tournament of chuiwan, a Song-dynasty lawn game played with clubs and balls, and inviting her!

“怎么？难道想浩宇了？”陈文心故意开着玩笑。
Mid-hesitation, Sister-in-law Chen Wenxin walked straight into Wangyue Pavilion unannounced; servants treated her as family. Past the threshold, she blurted, "Shuzhen, did the Qiaos send you an invitation too?" Spotting the card right in Shuzhen's hand, she bristled. "They did! The nerve of those Qiaos—inviting Father-in-law and Mother-in-law is bad enough, dragging you in too?! Whatever you do, don't go!"

“嫂嫂别瞎说，我只是找陈浩宇有事要问。”
Setting the card down, Qianli Xun stepped up smiling. "Came all this way just over this, Sister-in-law?"

“放心，定是浩宇与我的两位堂弟去。陈家的捶丸场地，不过是陈家的生意而已，我爹娘从不喜欢捶丸，肯定不去。别的长辈见我爹娘不去，也都不会去。”
"Need you ask? Mother-in-law heard the Qiaos were throwing a chuiwan match, feared they'd invite you, and sent me straight over!"

姑娘与陈家公子定亲，韦嬷嬷一直未发表过任何意见，也从未与姑娘畅聊过。当她听说此事时，便知姑娘定然不满，可她也理解朱晞颜的不易。
"Sit down and talk." Leading Chen Wenxin to a chair, she told Kuqiao to whisk two bowls of tea.

朱家老爷顾着家族的面子，岂能让朱家的姑娘永远嫁不出去被人笑话？韦嬷嬷两边都理解，两边则都不说什么。
Settling in, Chen Wenxin rattled on. "Madam Qiao adores chuiwan and throws a match every year or two—drives me crazy! If they weren't imperial kin renting the Chen family's chuiwan grounds, I wouldn't set foot near it!"

苦荞又多做一盏茶，为三人奉上。韦嬷嬷端起一盏，品完一口，问道：“夏果去了哪里？该不会又在丫鬟房读书？”
Qianli Xun teased, "Bet you're only going to guard Chen family turf so the Qiaos don't wreck the lawn!"

苦荞不敢言语。
"Think I have that much spare time?! Before marrying into the Zhu house I went for the Chens; now Father-in-law and Mother-in-law skip out and dump it on your brother and me! Can't stand the Qiaos, yet appearances still matter." Ever since the Qiaos jilted Shuzhen, Chen Wenxin's disgust ran twice as deep.

千里寻说：“是我让她读的，这丫头挺聪明，多读书，没什么不好。”
Qianli Xun weighed both sides. Go, and she would face Qiao elders plus Wenyuan and Miao Zhiyu; even knowing she could keep her cool, skipping awkward reunions sounded nicer. Skip it, though, and folks might whisper she was hiding out still heartbroken over Wenyuan.

陈文心先批评道：“淑真，这就是你的不对，怎么能把自个丫鬟当主子供着？夏果既是你的贴身丫鬟，就应该每日像苦荞一样在屋里伺候你。”
"Well? What's your call—going or skipping?" Chen Wenxin pressed.

“少夫人说得没错。”韦嬷嬷放下手中的茶盏，“夏果因自己长相好，早已不安分，姑娘再让她读些书，知道得多，会更加不安分，哪有心思伺候主子？”
Before Qianli Xun answered, Matron Wei walked in speaking crisp. "Why skip? Ask yourself, young lady: if any other house invited you, would you go?"

千里寻虽已观察出夏果的不安分，可她并不觉得有什么错：“嬷嬷关心我，我打心里感激，至于夏果，您就别再理会她，还是想想要不要陪我去捶丸。”
Back in her old world, Qianli Xun had played golf a few times; thanks to Qianli Fist coordination, she picked it up fast and hit crisp. Researching ancient chuiwan later for a web novel, she had marveled how close it mirrored golf, and getting to play live sounded like a blast. "Course I'd go—fun game! Only because it's the Qiaos..."

姑娘如此说，韦嬷嬷不再与夏果计较：“姑娘都不计较，我还理会个什么劲儿。捶丸那日，我当然要陪姑娘去，我虽不会捶丸，却可代表望月轩与各家的夫人们聊聊，拉拉关系。”
Taking a seat, Matron Wei settled it in one line. "The Qiaos are no different from any other house! Besides, you have a new betrothal now; nothing left to fret over!"

陈文心一脸羡慕：“真希望我身边也有个像韦嬷嬷一样的老人儿，走到哪里都让人踏实。”
Crisp and clear! Hesitation gone, Qianli Xun grinned. "Matron's right! If the Qiaos have the nerve to send a card, I've got the nerve to show up!"

“少夫人可别羡慕，您是福窝里长大的，生得好嫁得好。哪像姑娘，她生得虽好，感情婚姻之路却……”韦嬷嬷不觉伤感起来。
"That's the spirit!" Matron Wei approved.

千里寻被韦嬷嬷感染，也伤感起来，不想言语。
Chen Wenxin chuckled. "Mother-in-law fretted for nothing! With Matron Wei here, two sentences clear any headache!"

陈文心装作生气的样子提醒：“嬷嬷这么说，我可生气了，淑真已与浩宇定亲，难道我们陈家不好？难道淑真嫁到陈家不会享福？”
Hearing mention of the Chen betrothal dimmed Qianli Xun's smile again, yet with Sister-in-law right here, she held her tongue. She had not seen Chen Haoyu since their Juxiang Tower flop; she wondered if Old Madam Xia was still stalling. "Who's going from the Chen house, Sister-in-law?"

韦嬷嬷了解姑娘的心思，知道她不想嫁到陈家，装作刚刚想起的样子：“看我这脑子，只想以前不顺心的事，忘了朱陈两家亲上加亲，希望姑娘嫁到陈家一切能好起来。”
"Ooh, missing Haoyu already?" Chen Wenxin teased.

“那还用说？淑真，你说呢？”陈文心瞧向淑真，眼中充满询问。
"Quit teasing, Sister-in-law! I just need to ask him something."

千里寻不想让嫂嫂失望，可让她说出肯定的话，又难以启齿，只好端起茶盏品起茶来，想到转移话题这招，才放下茶盏：“嫂嫂的捶丸技术如何？”
"Relax, Haoyu and my two male cousins will be there. That chuiwan field is just Chen business; my parents hate chuiwan and never attend, and none of the other elders go without them."

淑真未接话，陈文心猜到她还是不愿嫁给浩宇，也不好追问下去：“还行，不过比你强。”
Matron Wei had stayed silent on Shuzhen's Chen betrothal until now, knowing full well her young lady hated it while also seeing Prefect Zhu's bind: guarding family honor meant refusing to leave his daughter unmarried as a laughingstock. Understanding both sides, Matron Wei kept quiet.

“嫂嫂可与我比过捶丸？”
Kuqiao served three fresh bowls of whisked tea. Taking a sip, Matron Wei asked, "Where's Xiaguo? Hiding in the maids' quarters reading again?"

“那倒没有，你以前很少捶丸，偶尔玩过一次，被我看到，技术可不怎么样。”
Kuqiao kept mum. Qianli Xun answered easy, "My idea. Bright girl—no harm reading."

嫂嫂说的是以前的朱淑真，现在的朱淑真并非如此：“嫂嫂有所不知，现在的我捶丸技术精进不少，到时你我可得好好比比。”
Chen Wenxin jumped in scolding. "That's on you, Shuzhen! Pampering a maid like a young lady—she's your personal maid and belongs right here waiting on you like Kuqiao!"

“那是自然。”陈文心端起茶盏，一口气喝完，“行了，话已说完，茶也喝过，该走了，婆婆在家还等着我回话。”
"Young Madam speaks truth!" Matron Wei set down her tea bowl. "A pretty face already left Xiaguo restless; stuffing her head with books only makes her twice as ambitious, with zero heart left for serving!"

千里寻与韦嬷嬷将陈文心送至门口，目送她乘马车离开。
Noticing Xiaguo's ambition herself yet seeing zero sin in wanting more out of life, Qianli Xun waved it off. "Appreciate you looking out for me, Matron, yet let Xiaguo be. More important, coming with me to the chuiwan match?"

韦嬷嬷主动邀请：“姑娘，去我屋中坐会儿？”
Seeing her mistress drop it, Matron Wei shrugged. "If you don't mind, why should I fret? Course I'm coming! I won't swing a club myself, yet representing Wangyue Pavilion to mingle with the madams keeps doors open!"

千里寻猜到嬷嬷应该有话想与她说：“嬷嬷请我，岂能不去？”
Chen Wenxin sighed envious. "Wish I had a rock like Matron Wei by my side; puts your mind at ease everywhere you go!"

韦嬷嬷吩咐苦荞不必陪着。
"Don't envy us, Young Madam. You grew up wrapped in blessings, born lucky and wed lucky! Unlike our young lady: lovely as a flower, yet her road in marriage and love..." Emotion caught in Matron Wei's throat, casting quiet gloom over Qianli Xun too.

苦荞明白，嬷嬷不想别人在一旁打扰，应该是想与小姐说些不愿被别人听到的话。
Feigning mock anger to lift the room, Chen Wenxin cut in, "Hey, Matron, watch out or I get mad! Shuzhen's engaged to Haoyu—calling our Chen house bad luck?! Won't Shuzhen live like a queen with us?"

来到韦嬷嬷房中，虽是春季，天气回暖，韦嬷嬷还是怕冷，依然燃着火盆，只是放的炭火没有冬季那么多。
Knowing her young lady's real dread yet playing smooth, Matron Wei tapped her brow. "Look at my memory, stuck brooding over past storms and forgetting our two houses sealing double kinship! Praying everything blooms bright once our young lady marries into the Chens!"

两人坐在火盆旁，火盆上架着壶，里面正煮着茶。身边没有丫鬟伺候，韦嬷嬷亲自动手伺候姑娘。
"Need you ask? Right, Shuzhen?" Chen Wenxin looked over hopeful.

壶中冒出热气，韦嬷嬷提起壶，给姑娘和自己各倒一盏：“老吃苦荞做的茶，姑娘也尝尝我煮的毛尖茶。”
Hating to crush her sister-in-law yet unable to force a fake yes, Qianli Xun took a slow sip of tea before changing gears. "How's your chuiwan game, Sister-in-law?"

千里寻端起茶盏，品了一口：“不错，茶味芬芳，好像有股熟悉的味道，是什么……”
Catching the dodge—plain she was still unwilling—Chen Wenxin did not push. "Decent; beats yours anyway!"

“熟栗子香。”韦嬷嬷笑着也品了一口。
"When'd we ever play?"

“对！是这个味道。”
"Never played you. You used to hate chuiwan; I caught you swinging once ages ago, looked pretty rough!"

千里寻想起小时候，父亲给她第一次买熟栗子的情景……
"That was the old me! I improved tons lately—wait till we match up on the lawn!"

那时，有家人在旁，吃每一颗栗子，都觉得无比甜美。
"You're on!" Draining her bowl clean, Chen Wenxin stood. "Message delivered, tea drunk. I'm off home before Mother-in-law paces a hole in the floor!"

长大后，她赚了钱，每年都买，可每次吃到嘴里，再也吃不出童年时的记忆，倒是这毛尖茶的味道让她的记忆回到穿越前的童年时期。
After seeing Chen Wenxin off to her carriage, Matron Wei invited, "Step into my room for a spell, young lady?"

“姑娘在想什么？”
Sensing a heart-to-heart ahead, Qianli Xun smiled. "Lead the way, Matron!" Matron Wei signaled Kuqiao to stay back; understanding private talk awaited, Kuqiao stayed put.

“没想什么？嬷嬷把我叫进来，应该不只是为品茶。”
Even with spring warming outdoors, chill-prone Matron Wei still kept a low charcoal brazier glowing with a kettle steeping tea overhead, pouring two bowls herself without calling servants. "Sick of Kuqiao's whisked tea—try my steeped Maojian tea!"

“自从朱陈两家亲上加亲，我还没与姑娘好好聊过，不如今日说说此事。”韦嬷嬷在姑娘面前没有必要藏着掖着，开门见山才能体现她们之间亲密的关系。
Taking a sip, Qianli Xun blinked. "Delicious—fragrant with something super familiar... what is that?"

千里寻放下茶盏，其实她早想与韦嬷嬷聊聊，担心嬷嬷会像家人一样对陈家满意，故而一直未提起。今日既然提到，她根本不想隐瞒真实想法。
"Roasted chestnuts," Matron Wei smiled, sipping hers.

“嬷嬷，我不想嫁给陈浩宇，他是什么样的人，嬷嬷也领教过，也知道我与他不合，你帮我出出主意，好让陈家退了这门亲事。”
"Yes! Roasted chestnuts!" An instant memory flashed back to childhood: her father buying her very first bag of hot roasted chestnuts, every kernel tasting pure magic surrounded by family. Buying them as an adult after earning her own money never tasted the same, yet one sip of Song Maojian tea carried her straight back to childhood.

韦嬷嬷无比心疼自家姑娘，嫁给林家前有喜欢的人，却因徐家搬走，感情无疾而终。后又和离，好不容易跟乔家公子有情，又遇流言蜚语，好好的亲事被破坏，又偏偏要嫁给一个连林觉都比不上的人。
"Lost in thought, young lady?"

“姑娘有没有想过，如果退不了婚，必须嫁到陈家，你该如何？”
"Nothing. Guess you didn't invite me in just for tea."

“没想过，我只想以后不涉足感情和婚姻，就住在望月轩，有嬷嬷和苦荞陪伴，也挺好。”
"Since the Zhu-Chen betrothal landed, we haven't had one real talk. Let's speak open today." Nothing stayed hidden between them; straight talk proved their bond.

“怎么不提夏果？”
Setting down her bowl, Qianli Xun spoke plain; she had held back earlier fearing Matron Wei favored the Chens too. "I don't want to marry Chen Haoyu, Matron! You've seen firsthand what he is and how we clash. Help me find a way to make the Chens cancel this match!"

千里寻沉默不语，夏果那样子，心根本不在望月轩，迟早会离开。
Matron Wei's heart ached raw for her girl: lost her first sweetheart when the Xus fled, endured a brutal marriage and divorce, fell for Young Master Qiao only for rumors to smash it, and now saddled with a spoiled dandy worse than Lin Jue! "Ever think what you'll do if breaking it off fails and you have to marry into the Chen house?"

“看来姑娘心中明得跟镜子一般，也知道夏果靠不住，可我看苦荞也不一定能陪你，她跟仪王身边的侍卫关系不一般，那孩子每隔几日在望月轩门外等她，我都碰见好几回。”
"Haven't! I'm done with love and marriage—happy living solo right here in Wangyue Pavilion with you and Kuqiao!"

“大不了我把苦荞嫁出去，和嬷嬷在一起也不错。”
"Notice you skipped Xiaguo."

韦嬷嬷幸福地笑着：“我老了，怎能陪姑娘一辈子？还得找个可心的人陪你才行。姑娘还是想想若退不了婚，该怎么办？”
Qianli Xun stayed mute; everyone knew Xiaguo's heart lay far past Wangyue Pavilion's gates.

“若……若退不了婚……”千里寻还未想好，她不知该如何回答。
"Clear-eyed as a mirror, young lady; you see Xiaguo won't stay! Hate to tell you, Kuqiao won't stay forever either. She and Prince Yi's guard have something going; I've caught that boy waiting outside Wangyue Pavilion's gate for her plenty of times!"

“姑娘不用回答我，只需你想清楚便可。结果无非两种，嫁或不嫁。为不嫁努力的同时，还需做好最坏的打算，省得最坏的结果出现，搞得心慌意乱。”
"Fine, I'll marry Kuqiao off happy and stay with you!"

从韦嬷嬷的话中，千里寻算是听出来，想要退婚不是那么容易，估计比与林觉和离还难：“嬷嬷说得极是。”
Matron Wei smiled warm. "Old bones like mine can't walk beside you forever; you still need a true soul to share your years! Think through the worst case: only two paths exist, marrying or not marrying. Fight tooth and nail not to wed, yet prepare for the worst too so panic never catches you flat-footed!"
