## Chapter 158: Chapter 158: A Springtime Invitation

The rest of winter rolled by in calm peace. Qianli Xun visited Youlin Retreat several times to keep Mengrong company and chase off winter boredom; crossing paths with Prince Yi felt totally different now—zero awkward walls, bantering easy like the old friends they truly were.

On New Year's Day, Zhu Xiyan broke his own silence and sent word summoning his daughter home to Zhu Manor. Qianli Xun went without fuss, steering clear of any mention of canceling the betrothal so she wouldn't sour the holiday—plenty of time to fight after New Year! Neither Wenyuan nor Miao Zhiyu crossed her path once; whether they avoided her on purpose or fate simply kept their orbits apart, Qianli Xun couldn't care less anymore. Between drafting a brand-new huaben and drilling ten household servants in Qianli Fist, her days stayed packed and lively. A maid named Hongdou picked up Qianli Fist so sharp that Qianli Xun promoted her to personal attendant—stepping out flanked by three maids felt pretty grand!

After the Lantern Festival came one last cold snap before spring warmth crept back. Just as pale grass turned green and fresh buds broke along the Divine Willow's boughs, a messenger arrived from Qiao Manor bearing an invitation card! Opening it, Qianli Xun blinked: the Qiaos were hosting a tournament of chuiwan, a Song-dynasty lawn game played with clubs and balls—and inviting HER?!

Mid-hesitation, Sister-in-law Chen Wenxin walked straight into Wangyue Pavilion unannounced—servants treated her as family. Barely past the threshold, she blurted: "Shuzhen—did the Qiaos send you an invitation too?" Spotting the card right in Shuzhen's hand: "They actually DID! The nerve of those Qiaos—inviting Father-in-law and Mother-in-law is bad enough, dragging YOU in too?! Whatever you do, don't go!"

Setting the card down, Qianli Xun stepped up smiling: "Came all this way just over this, Sister-in-law?"

"Need you ask? Mother-in-law heard the Qiaos were throwing a chuiwan match, feared they'd invite you, and sent me straight over!"

"Sit down and talk." Leading Chen Wenxin to a chair, she told Kuqiao to whisk two bowls of tea.

Settling in, Chen Wenxin rattled on: "Madam Qiao adores chuiwan and throws a match every year or two—drives me crazy! If they weren't imperial kin renting the Chen family's chuiwan grounds, I wouldn't set foot near it!"

Qianli Xun teased: "Bet you're only going to guard Chen family turf so the Qiaos don't wreck the lawn!"

"Think I have that much spare time?! Before marrying into the Zhu house I went for the Chens; now Father-in-law and Mother-in-law skip out and dump it on your brother and me! Can't stand the Qiaos, yet appearances still matter." Ever since the Qiaos jilted Shuzhen, Chen Wenxin's disgust ran twice as deep.

Qianli Xun weighed both sides. Go, and she'd face Qiao elders plus Wenyuan and Miao Zhiyu—even knowing she could keep her cool, skipping awkward reunions sounded nicer. Skip it, though, and folks might whisper she was hiding out still heartbroken over Wenyuan!

"Well? What's your call—going or skipping?" Chen Wenxin pressed.

Before Qianli Xun answered, Matron Wei walked in speaking crisp: "Why skip? Ask yourself, young lady: if any other house invited you, would you go?"

Back in her old world, Qianli Xun had played golf a few times—thanks to Qianli Fist coordination, picking it up fast and hitting crisp! Researching ancient chuiwan later for a web novel, she had marveled how close it mirrored golf; getting to play live sounded like a blast! "Course I'd go—fun game! Only because it's the Qiaos..."

Taking a seat, Matron Wei settled it in one line: "Qiaos are no different from any other house! Besides, you have a new betrothal now—nothing left to fret over!"

Crisp and clear! Hesitation gone, Qianli Xun grinned: "Matron's right! If the Qiaos have the nerve to send a card, I've got the nerve to show up!"

"That's the spirit!" Matron Wei approved.

Chen Wenxin chuckled: "Mother-in-law fretted for nothing! With Matron Wei here, two sentences clear any headache!"

Hearing mention of the Chen betrothal dimmed Qianli Xun's smile again—yet with Sister-in-law right here, she held her tongue. Hadn't seen Chen Haoyu since their Juxiang Tower flop—wonder if Old Madam Xia was still stalling? "Who's going from the Chen house, Sister-in-law?"

"Ooh—missing Haoyu already?" Chen Wenxin teased.

"Quit teasing, Sister-in-law! Just need to ask him something."

"Relax, Haoyu and my two male cousins will be there. That chuiwan field is purely Chen business—my parents hate chuiwan and never attend, and none of the other elders go without them."

Matron Wei had stayed silent on Shuzhen's Chen betrothal until now—knowing full well her young lady hated it while also seeing Prefect Zhu's bind: guarding family honor meant refusing to leave his daughter unmarried as a laughingstock. Understanding both sides, Matron Wei kept quiet.

Kuqiao served three fresh bowls of whisked tea. Taking a sip, Matron Wei asked: "Where's Xiaguo? Hiding in the maids' quarters reading again?"

Kuqiao kept mum. Qianli Xun answered easy: "My idea—bright girl, no harm reading."

Chen Wenxin jumped in scolding: "That's on you, Shuzhen! Pampering a maid like a young lady—she's your personal maid and belongs right here waiting on you like Kuqiao!"

"Young Madam speaks truth!" Matron Wei set down her tea bowl. "Pretty face already left Xiaguo restless; stuffing her head with books only makes her twice as ambitious—zero heart left for serving!"

Noticing Xiaguo's ambition herself yet seeing zero sin in wanting more out of life, Qianli Xun waved it off: "Appreciate you looking out for me, Matron—let Xiaguo be. More important, coming with me to the chuiwan match?"

Seeing her mistress drop it, Matron Wei shrugged: "If you don't mind, why should I fret? Course I'm coming—won't swing a club myself, yet representing Wangyue Pavilion to mingle with the madams keeps doors open!"

Chen Wenxin sighed envious: "Wish I had a rock like Matron Wei by my side—puts your mind at ease everywhere you go!"

"Don't envy us, Young Madam—you grew up wrapped in blessings, born lucky and wed lucky! Unlike our young lady: lovely as a flower, yet her road in marriage and love..." Emotion caught in Matron Wei's throat, casting quiet gloom over Qianli Xun too.

Feigning mock anger to lift the room, Chen Wenxin cut in: "Hey, Matron, watch out or I get mad! Shuzhen's engaged to Haoyu—calling our Chen house bad luck?! Won't Shuzhen live like a queen with us?"

Knowing her young lady's real dread yet playing smooth, Matron Wei tapped her brow: "Look at my memory—stuck brooding over past storms and forgetting our two houses sealing double kinship! Praying everything blooms bright once our young lady marries into the Chens!"

"Need you ask? Right, Shuzhen?" Chen Wenxin looked over hopeful.

Hating to crush her sister-in-law yet unable to force a fake yes, Qianli Xun took a slow sip of tea before changing gears: "How's your chuiwan game, Sister-in-law?"

Catching the dodge—clearly still unwilling—Chen Wenxin didn't push: "Decent—beats yours anyway!"

"When'd we ever play?"

"Never played you—used to hate chuiwan; caught you swinging once ages ago, looked pretty rough!"

"That was the old me! Improved tons lately—wait till we match up on the lawn!"

"You're on!" Draining her bowl clean, Chen Wenxin stood: "Message delivered, tea drunk—off home before Mother-in-law paces a hole in the floor!"

After seeing Chen Wenxin off to her carriage, Matron Wei invited: "Step into my room for a spell, young lady?"

Sensing a heart-to-heart ahead, Qianli Xun smiled: "Lead the way, Matron!" Matron Wei signaled Kuqiao to stay back; understanding private talk awaited, Kuqiao stayed put.

Even with spring warming outdoors, chill-prone Matron Wei still kept a low charcoal brazier glowing with a kettle steeping tea overhead, pouring two bowls herself without calling servants. "Sick of Kuqiao's whisked tea—try my steeped Maojian tea!"

Taking a sip, Qianli Xun blinked: "Delicious—fragrant with something super familiar... what is that?"

"Roasted chestnuts," Matron Wei smiled sipping hers.

"Yes! Roasted chestnuts!" Instant flash back to childhood—her father buying her very first bag of hot roasted chestnuts, every kernel tasting pure magic surrounded by family! Buying them as an adult after making money never tasted the same—yet one sip of Song Maojian tea carried her straight back to childhood!

"Lost in thought, young lady?"

"Nothing—guess you didn't invite me in just for tea."

"Since the Zhu-Chen betrothal landed, we haven't had one real talk—let's speak open today." Nothing hidden between them; straight talk proved true bond.

Setting down her bowl, Qianli Xun spoke plain—she had held back earlier fearing Matron Wei favored the Chens too: "I don't want to marry Chen Haoyu, Matron! You've seen firsthand what he is and how we clash—help me find a way to make the Chens cancel this match!"

Matron Wei's heart ached raw for her girl: lost her first sweetheart when the Xus fled, endured a brutal marriage and divorce, fell for Young Master Qiao only for rumors to smash it, and now saddled with a spoiled dandy worse than Lin Jue! "Ever think what you'll do if breaking it off fails and you HAVE to marry into the Chen house?"

"Haven't! Done with love and marriage—happy living solo right here in Wangyue Pavilion with you and Kuqiao!"

"Notice you skipped Xiaguo."

Qianli Xun stayed mute; everyone knew Xiaguo's heart lay far past Wangyue Pavilion's gates.

"Clear-eyed as a mirror, young lady—you see Xiaguo won't stay! Hate to tell you, Kuqiao won't stay forever either: she and Prince Yi's guard have something going—caught that boy waiting outside Wangyue Pavilion's gate for her plenty of times!"

"Fine, I'll marry Kuqiao off happy and stay with YOU!"

Matron Wei smiled warm: "Old bones like mine can't walk beside you forever—you still need a true soul to share your years! Think through the worst case: only two paths exist, marrying or not marrying. Fight tooth and nail not to wed, yet prepare for the worst too so panic never catches you flat-footed!"

Reading between Matron Wei's calm lines, canceling this betrothal looked even tougher than divorcing Lin Jue. "Wise words, Matron."

"Hear you're promoting Hongdou to your side?"

Qianli Xun nodded. "Picks up Qianli Fist fast—criminal waste leaving her scrubbing laundry!"

"Good call—once Xiaguo leaves and Kuqiao weds, you'll have someone loyal close at hand."

"Always thinking ten steps ahead for me, Matron."

"Just eaten a few extra bowls of rice and walked more miles—you'll see just as clear at my age."

"Never catch up to you, Matron!"

Every word from Matron Wei radiated cozy heat like the charcoal brazier, melting away spring's last chill. Even without a silver bullet to break the betrothal, Qianli Xun walked out feeling warmed to the bone.
