# Chapter 61: Dividing Property and Discussing Mutual Divorce

Tomorrow was the day of the mutual divorce. Qianli Xun didn't need to return to the Lin residence anymore. Madam Xiang invited Nanny Wei to live with her—they were about the same age and had plenty to talk about. Qianli Xun, meanwhile, returned to the remote garden with her two maids.

After the banquet, the Zhu residence returned to peace, but Qianli Xun's heart was too excited to settle. After tomorrow, she could finally live the life of Qianli Xun.

"Kuqiao, bring me some wine!"

There was no response from Kuqiao. She remembered that Xiaguo had knelt for half a shichen, her knees red and raw. She had sent Kuqiao to apply medicine to Xiaguo's wounds.

Since no one was bringing wine, she had to find another way to express her feelings.

Zhu Shuzhen was a female poet, so naturally Qianli Xun thought of writing poetry. She searched her memory, but her mind was filled with sorrowful, melancholic verses. Zhu Shuzhen never seemed to have been happy.

Qianli Xun walked to the window and gazed at the moon outside. The bamboo grove was in the direction where the moon rose. She couldn't help but think of the bamboo grove, grateful for the good luck it had brought her.

Such a beautiful bamboo grove had been taken away by Chen Haoyu. Qianli Xun couldn't accept this. In the future, she had to find a way to get the bamboo grove back, build her own bamboo house there, live with the one she loved, and grow old together.

Naturally, she thought of Wenyuan. After tomorrow, they could enjoy their love together without restraint. A smile involuntarily appeared on Qianli Xun's face.

One of Zhu Shuzhen's poems appeared in her mind. It was a rare poem—deep yet not sorrowful—quite fitting for her current mood.

So she walked to the desk, sat down, spread out the paper, picked up her brush, and wrote:

A hundred tall stalks, their noble joints brushing the clouds,
A thousand acres—who would envy the Wei River streams?
Let sparrows and swallows come and chatter noisily,
The humble bamboo awaits the phoenix to rest.
("Quatrain on Bamboo")

After finishing, Qianli Xun softly recited the poem, feeling it with her heart, and murmured to herself: "Zhu Shuzhen, I've already waited for the 'phoenix' on your behalf. He is Wenyuan."

Just as she put down the brush, Kuqiao walked in, holding a medicine bottle. Seeing her young lady writing poetry, she leaned over to take a look: "This new poem of yours is quite different from your previous ones."

Qianli Xun asked deliberately: "Tell me, what's different?"

Kuqiao walked aside to put down the medicine bottle and said: "The poems Young Lady wrote before made people feel sad when reading them, but this one doesn't."

Qianli Xun continued to ask: "What feeling does this poem give you?"

Kuqiao thought for a moment: "Hmm... it makes me feel that the bamboo in Young Lady's writing is very proud and aloof. The sparrows and swallows chirp noisily beside it, but it pays them no mind, for it awaits the phoenix's arrival."

Qianli Xun praised: "I didn't expect you to have learned to appreciate poetry, girl."

"I often follow Young Lady to poetry gatherings, so I've picked up a little, though it's just superficial knowledge." Kuqiao walked in front of her young lady, bowed, and said: "Congratulations, Young Lady. Tomorrow you'll be leaving the young master."

"Thinking that I'll regain my freedom after tomorrow, I'm so happy I don't know what to do. How about having some more wine?"

"Better not. Young Lady has already drunk quite a lot at the banquet today. Drinking more might harm your health."

Qianli Xun thought Kuqiao had a point, so she gave up the idea of drinking: "Is Xiaguo's injury serious?"

"Young Lady doesn't need to worry. I've already applied medicine to her knees. She'll be fine soon." Kuqiao remembered seeing Shen Jianchen at the banquet. "Young Lady, today at the banquet I saw the young master who went to the bamboo grove looking for Qianli Xun, but I didn't see his attendant. He seemed to have a good relationship with Young Master Qiao."

"This person is very extraordinary. He's a prince. I heard from my father that he's Prince Yi."

"Ah! A prince? I didn't expect a prince to enjoy listening to storytellers. Could he be the son born to Consort Qiao?"

"My father didn't mention that, but seeing how close he is with Wenyuan, he probably is."

"Since he's a prince, why doesn't Young Lady admit to him that you wrote the storytelling scripts?"

Qianli Xun thought for a moment and said: "Forget it. Your young lady is a famous female poet from Jiangnan. If people found out I'd been secretly writing storytelling scripts, wouldn't that embarrass me?"

"Actually, the storytelling scripts Young Lady writes are very interesting. Every time I take them to Kujue, I read them through first. Compared to your poetry, I like the storytelling scripts better."

"Of course the stories I write are good. Anyone who reads them will like them."

"Why not make them public? Maybe everyone will praise Young Lady for being talented, able to write both poetry and storytelling scripts."

Qianli Xun wanted to leave her own mark in this unfamiliar era, so that her time travel wouldn't be in vain: "Forget it. The stories in the storytelling scripts belong only to Qianli Xun. How could I publish them under Zhu Shuzhen's name?"

Kuqiao was already used to her young lady occasionally treating herself as two different people: "Whatever Young Lady wants is fine. I understand, and I'll keep Young Lady's secret."

"Don't even tell Xiaguo, understand?"

"I understand." Kuqiao was full of worry. "Young Lady, I'm afraid Xiaguo already knows. When we were at the bamboo grove, several times when you were writing storytelling scripts, she was attending nearby."

"Don't worry. She can barely read a few characters. She has no idea what I was writing. We need to be more careful in the future, though, since you've already started teaching her to read."

"I'll be even more careful from now on."

"Things have been busy lately, so there's no time to write storytelling scripts. When we have free time, I'll continue writing. After it's done, you'll be responsible for delivering it to the storyteller."

"Yes, Young Lady."

...

In the main hall of the Zhu residence, people from both the Zhu and Lin families had gathered. With the prefect presiding, the Lin family didn't dare say a word. Lin Zixiao still sat in the seat of honor representing the Lin family elders, with Zhu Xiyan sitting on the other side.

Today, Qianli Xun only needed to remain silent. Everything had someone to make decisions for her. She didn't need to rush to the front to fight for her own interests.

The two families first discussed the division of property.

Zhu Xiyan said: "Since this is a mutual divorce, the property should be divided according to the rules. The Zhu family has no objections."

Matchmaker Yan said: "According to the rules, the property of the Military Training Officer and the young madam should be divided equally. But the Military Training Officer's property isn't as much as the dowry the young madam brought. If it's divided equally, wouldn't the young madam be at a disadvantage?"

Zhu Xiyan said: "It doesn't matter."

With the prefect present, how could Lin Jue dare to take any of the dowry? He quickly said: "I don't want a single coin of the dowry. Let Shuzhen take it all with her. Since this is a mutual divorce, let's part on good terms."

Qianli Xun looked at Lin Jue. He seemed very dejected. At this moment, she realized that this guy really did like Zhu Shuzhen, and his unwillingness to divorce was truly out of genuine feelings. But who told him he didn't know how to win a female poet's heart? Thinking carefully, how the marriage reached this point couldn't be explained by right or wrong. The key was that the two people weren't compatible.

Lin Zixiao said: "I agree with what my nephew Lin Jue said. He lacks the fortune to keep Youqi Jushi, so how can he divide the dowry? The children are separating, but the two families still need to maintain relations. The Lin family also doesn't want to create a rift with the Zhu family over property. Youqi Jushi didn't live a good life after marrying into the Lin family. I've made my decision—of my nephew Lin Jue's property, whatever Youqi Jushi wants, she can take it."

Lin Jue and the Lin family elders began to get nervous, worried that the Zhu family really would take away their property.

Zhu Xiyan said: "The Lin family is so generous. How can the Zhu family be so rude as to take advantage? Since that's the case, my daughter won't take a single coin of Lin Jue's property. She'll only take her own dowry."

The Lin family and Lin Jue all breathed a sigh of relief, saying one after another: "Let's do as the prefect says."

Matchmaker Yan laid out the divorce documents: "Since both families have agreed on the property division, please sign the divorce documents and press your fingerprints."

Qianli Xun stood up first, walked to the documents, picked up the brush, and as she wrote Zhu Shuzhen's name, she said in her heart: "Zhu Shuzhen, I've already finalized the divorce for you. Are you as happy as I am?"

She dipped her right thumb in the ink paste and pressed a red fingerprint on the document. At this moment, how could her feelings be described by just the word "happy"?

Lin Jue was the second to sign and press his fingerprint. At the moment of pressing his fingerprint, he felt unwilling. Was he really going to let Zhu Shuzhen go just like this? Everything was settled, and there was no way to turn back. He felt strange. He had clearly made up his mind, but when it came time for the actual divorce, he felt strangely uncomfortable.

Then Zhu Xiyan and Lin Zixiao also signed and pressed their fingerprints on the documents.

Matchmaker Yan picked up the documents and looked them over: "Alright, the divorce is finalized. May you both be well in the future. I need to rush to the government office to deliver the divorce documents, so I won't disturb you any longer. You two families can chat some more."

Everyone watched Matchmaker Yan's departing figure, each person's feelings vastly different.
