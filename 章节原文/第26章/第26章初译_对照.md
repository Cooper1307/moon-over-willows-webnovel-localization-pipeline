第26章 有缘才做三件事
# Chapter 26: Three Trials for the Fated

---

正殿后面除供香客烧香跪拜的殿阁外，两边是寮房。

Behind the main hall, beyond the pavilions where pilgrims burned incense and prostrated themselves, lay rows of monastic quarters on either side.

---

女尼掀开一个房间的布帘：“两位请。”

A young nun pulled back the curtain of one room. "After you."

---

千里寻认为主持应该就在里面，可走进去后，除了桌椅茶具，空无一人。

Qianli Xun stepped inside, expecting to find the abbess waiting. Instead, the room was empty—furnished with nothing but a set of tea tables, chairs, and brewing utensils.

---

女尼说：“二位在此稍侯，我去请慈玄法师。”

"Please wait here," the nun said. "I'll go fetch Venerable Cixuan."

---

千里寻强调道：“小师傅，我找净光法师，不找慈玄法师。”

Qianli Xun quickly clarified, “Sister, I'm looking for Abbess Jingguang—not Master Cixuan.”

---

女尼没有说话，掀开帘子走出去。

Without a word, the nun lifted the curtain and slipped out.

---

千里寻只得找椅子坐下，耐心等候。

With no other choice, Qianli Xun found a chair and sat down to wait.

---

大约过了一刻钟，布帘被掀开，进来一名年纪较大的女尼，手拿佛珠：“阿弥陀佛，让施主久等。”

About a quarter of an hour passed before the curtain rustled open again. An elder nun entered, her hands slowly rolling a string of prayer beads. "Amitabha. Forgive the wait."

---

千里寻起身问：“您是慈玄法师？”

Qianli Xun stood immediately. "Are you Master Cixuan?"

---

“正是。”

"I am."

---

“我想拜见净光法师，请她为我指点迷津。”

"I wish to meet Abbess Jingguang and ask her to guide me through a great crisis in my life."

---

“住持她老人家不随便接见外人……”

"The abbess does not receive outsiders lightly..."

---

千里寻急了：“不随便接见外人？为何刚才的小师傅要带我进来？”

Qianli Xun grew anxious. "Does not receive outsiders? Then why did that young nun bring me in here?"

---

“她刚刚剃度出家，在正殿负责敲击木鱼，有些规矩不清楚，我已批评过她。”

"She was only recently tonsured and assigned to strike the wooden fish in the main hall. She is unfamiliar with our rules. I have already reprimanded her."

---

千里寻直接傻眼，本以为很顺利，原来是这么回事。进都进来，总不能白跑一趟。

Qianli Xun was left speechless. What she thought had been a smooth encounter was just a novice's mistake. But now that she was inside, she couldn't leave empty-handed.

---

“求法师通融，要不是遇到人生难题，我也不敢随便打扰住持。”

"I beg for your compassion, Master. If I hadn't hit a dead end in life, I would never dare disturb Her Eminence."

---

“请施主下山去吧。”

"Please go back down the mountain."

---

千里寻给苦荞使使眼色，苦荞拿出一两银子塞给慈玄法师：“请法师笑纳。”

Qianli Xun shot Kuqiao a sharp look. Catching the hint, Kuqiao quickly produced a piece of silver weighing one tael and pressed it toward the elder nun. "Please accept this small token, Master."

---

“出家人不贪图世间财物，施主还是收回去吧。”

"Those who have left the secular world do not covet material wealth. Please take it back."

---

慈玄面无表情，给人难以接近之感，苦荞只得把银子收回，用目光向小姐请示。

The older nun's face remained an unreadable mask, radiating a cold, unapproachable air. Kuqiao was forced to pull the silver back, casting a helpless look toward her lady.

---

千里寻以为对方嫌银子给得少，又给苦荞使眼色。

Thinking the nun simply found the amount too small, Qianli Xun signaled her maid once more.

---

苦荞从怀里拿出一袋银子，把那一两也放进去，再把钱袋递到慈玄面前：“法师还是笑纳吧。”

Kuqiao pulled a heavy silver pouch from her robe, stuffed the single tael back inside, and held out the entire pouch. "Please, Master, reconsider and accept it."

---

慈玄依旧面无表情，苦荞只好又拿回银子。

The elder nun remained as cold as stone. Kuqiao had no choice but to retract the money once again.

---

千里寻见钱无用，立刻跪在地上，苦荞也跟着跪下。

Seeing that money was useless, Qianli Xun dropped directly to her knees. Kuqiao hastily knelt beside her.

---

“今日不见到净光法师，我绝不下山，恳请慈玄法师通融。”

"If I don't see Abbess Jingguang today, I will not step foot off this mountain!" Qianli Xun declared firmly. "I earnestly beg Master Cixuan to grant me an exception!"

---

千里寻下跪倒是让慈玄感到意外，好言劝道：“施主起来下山去吧，住持她年事已高，不愿再为世俗之人预知未来。昨日一位临安高官前来想见住持，也被我请下山去。”

The sudden kneel caught the elder nun off guard. Her tone softened slightly as she tried to persuade: "Madam, please get up and leave. She is advanced in years and no longer wishes to divine the future for those bound to the secular world. Just yesterday, a high-ranking official from Lin'an came seeking an audience, and I turned him away as well."

---

“我不起来，也不下山，净光法师不见我，我就永远跪在这里，反正回到现在的家里还不如待在寺庙，再不济我也剃度出家，不怕见不到住持。”

“I'm not getting up, and I'm not leaving！” Qianli Xun insisted, her resolve unyielding. “If Abbess Jingguang refuses to see me, I'll kneel right here forever! Besides, going back to that house is worse than staying in a temple anyway! If push comes to shove, I'll shave my head and become a nun right now—let's see if she can avoid me then！”

---

千里寻十分坚决，搞得慈玄法师为难起来。

Her fierce determination clearly put the gatekeeper in a tight spot.

---

正犹豫间，进来一名女尼，年纪看上去与慈玄相仿，在慈玄耳边悄悄说句话，很快又出去。

Just as she hesitated, another middle-aged nun slipped into the room. She whispered a few quick words into the elder nun's ear and swiftly departed.

---

慈玄面色缓和许多：“起来吧，看在你与住持有缘，请跟我来。”

The older nun's expression softened considerably. "Get up, then. It seems you share a karmic tie with the abbess. Follow me."

---

终于有进展，千里寻很开心，起身跟着走出寮房，心想这回肯定能见到住持尊容，刚才慈玄都说了，她与住持有缘。

*Finally, a breakthrough!* Overjoyed, Qianli Xun scrambled to her feet and followed the elder nun out of the room. She was confident she would finally meet the legendary abbess—after all, she had just admitted they shared a karmic tie.

---

千里寻跟着慈玄穿过一个石门，向东边拐去，走了没多久，来到两个山洞跟前。

They passed through an arched stone gate and turned east down a quiet path until they arrived before two natural mountain caverns.

---

慈玄掀开左边山洞的布帘：“施主，请。”

The elder nun drew back the cloth hanging over the entrance of the left cavern. "Please, madam."

---

千里寻带着苦荞走进后，并未看到任何人影。

Qianli Xun stepped inside with Kuqiao trailing behind, but once again, no one was there.

---

迎面是尊佛像，前面放着一个很大的功德箱；旁边有个茶桌，上面摆着做茶的器具和材料；左边有一个书桌，笔墨纸砚放置得整整齐齐；再瞧两面墙壁，挂满唐诗，与佛像显得很不相称，不过字倒写得圆润饱满，不失风骨。

A Buddha statue faced the entrance, a fat merit box at its feet. To the right: a tea table, laid out with pots and canisters. To the left: a writing desk—brushes, inkstone, paper—arranged with care. And the walls—covered, top to bottom, in calligraphic scrolls of Tang Dynasty poetry. Odd company for a Buddha. But the calligraphy was good. Round strokes, strong bones. Whoever wrote these knew what they were doing.

---

千里寻无心欣赏书法与诗，问道：“住持呢？”

Qianli Xun had no patience to appreciate the art. "Where is the abbess?"

---

“住持在右边的山洞，施主想见住持，缘是第一步，还需完成三件事，得到住持允许，方可与她相见。”

"She is in the right cavern," the elder nun explained. "A karmic tie is merely the first step for anyone seeking an audience. You must complete three tasks and receive the abbess's approval before you may see her."

---

慈玄法师的话让千里寻感到前路障碍重重，好不容易走到这一步，还需接着往下走。

Hearing this, Qianli Xun felt a headache coming on. The road ahead was practically an obstacle course. But having come this far, she couldn't back out now.

---

“请法师说说是哪三件事？”

"Pray tell, Master, what are these three tasks?"

---

“第一件事很简单，只需将施主身上的钱财以及所有值钱之物统统放入功德箱。”

"The first task is simple: place all the money and valuables on your person into the merit box."

---

千里寻心想：“确实简单，她刚才不为钱财所动，原来是为将我洗劫一空。算了，不跟她计较！”想到这里，千里寻叫声：“苦荞。”

Qianli Xun scoffed internally. *Simple? She wasn't moved by money earlier because she wanted to rob me clean in one go! Whatever, I won't argue with her.*

"Kuqiao," Qianli Xun called out.

---

苦荞赶紧把身上带的银子全部拿出，放入功德箱中。

Kuqiao hurriedly pulled out all the silver she was carrying and dropped it into the wooden box.

---

千里寻又将玉镯，耳坠，以及头上的金钗统统取下，放入功德箱内。

Qianli Xun followed suit, taking off her jade bracelet, her earrings, and the gold hairpin holding up her hair, tossing them all into the merit box.

---

“法师，第一件事已做完，快说第二件吧。”

"Master, the first task is complete. Tell me the second."

---

慈玄法师说：“你那丫鬟身上还有贵重之物。”

The elder nun glanced at the maid. "Your servant still has valuables on her."

---

苦荞为难道：“是小姐要见住持，又不是我，为何还要我身上唯一值钱的银镯子？”

Kuqiao's face fell. “My lady is the one seeking the abbess, not me. Why do you need the only valuable thing I own—my silver wristband？”

---

千里寻走到苦荞身边，抓起她的右手，掀开袖子，果然手腕上有个银镯子，强行取下：“回头我送你好几个。”

Qianli Xun stepped over, grabbed Kuqiao's right arm, and pulled back her sleeve. Sure enough, a silver bracelet encircled her wrist. Qianli Xun yanked it off without hesitation. "I'll give you several more later!"

---

苦荞不再为难：“小姐说话可要算话。”

Kuqiao stopped protesting. "You must keep your word, my lady!"

---

千里寻把银镯放进功德箱：“放心，你家小姐我一诺千金。”

Qianli Xun tossed the silver bracelet into the box. "Relax. My word is gold. Always."

---

慈玄满意道：“这第二件需施主在墙上挑一首诗，将解诗的内容写在纸上，由我呈给住持，她老人家满意，方可进行第三件，如若她老人家不满意，也请即刻下山。”

The gatekeeper nodded, satisfied. "For the second task, you must choose a poem from these walls, write your interpretation of it on paper, and allow me to present it to the abbess. If she is pleased with your insight, you may proceed to the final task. If she is unsatisfied, you must leave the mountain immediately."

---

解诗对千里寻来讲并非难事，她再次环顾两面墙上的唐诗。

Interpreting poetry was second nature to Qianli Xun. She scanned the wall covered in Tang poems once more.

---

以前的千里寻对王右丞的诗并不感兴趣，不知为何此刻的她被那首《竹里馆》吸引住。她靠近墙边，细心琢磨诗句，内心文思如泉涌，转过身，走到书桌前坐下，提笔写下所思所想。

The old Qianli Xun had never cared much for Wang Youcheng's poetry, but for some reason, her eyes were drawn to his *Bamboo Lodge*. She stepped closer, carefully pondering the subtle lines. A wave of poetic inspiration surged through her mind. Turning around, she sat at the writing desk, picked up the brush, and began pouring her thoughts onto the paper.

---

苦荞见小姐写得非常专注，心中暗喜：“现在的小姐看上去跟从前没有任何区别。”

Watching her lady write with such intense focus, Kuqiao felt a quiet wave of relief. *The lady right now looks no different from how she used to be.*

---

千里寻用小楷写了整整一大张纸，终于写完，搁下笔：“法师，辛苦您拿给住持阅览。”

Qianli Xun filled an entire sheet of paper in tiny, precise characters. Setting the brush down, she blew on the wet ink. "Master, please take this to the abbess for her review."

---

苦荞过去把纸张拿了，送到慈玄法师手中。慈玄接过，简单折起后，掀开布帘离开。

Kuqiao stepped forward, picked up the paper, and handed it to the elder nun. Accepting the sheet, she folded it casually, drew back the curtain, and stepped out.

---

没多久，慈玄空手而归：“恭喜施主，住持对您解的诗很满意，可以进行第三件。”

Before long, the elder nun returned empty-handed. "Congratulations. She is very pleased with your commentary. You may proceed to the third task."

---

千里寻早已预料到结果，别的她不敢说，解个把诗倒不在话下：“第三件是什么？”

Qianli Xun had expected as much. She might not excel at everything, but breaking down a poem was a walk in the park. "What is the third task?"

---

“第三件是为住持做盏茶，住持品后若满意，便可与她老人家见面，若不满意还请施主下山。”

"The third task is to prepare a bowl of whisked tea for the abbess. If she enjoys the taste, she will meet with you. If not, please take your leave."

---

做茶是苦荞的强项，千里寻还未学会：“可否让我的丫鬟来做？”

Tea-making was Kuqiao's specialty; Qianli Xun hadn't learned the intricate art yet. "Can my maid prepare it instead?"

---

“不行，是你求事，又不是你的丫鬟，必须施主亲自动手。”

"No," the elder nun replied flatly. "It is you who seeks guidance, not your maid. You must prepare it with your own hands."

---

“能不能让我的丫鬟在一旁指点。”

"Can she at least guide me from the side?"

---

“也不行，在一旁指点跟她做没什么区别。”

"No. Guidance from the side is no different from doing it for you."

---

千里寻只能离开书桌，来到茶桌跟前，看到一桌子的茶具，不知该如何下手。努力回忆苦荞做茶的过程，好像需先温盏。千里寻拿起一个茶盏，放到面前，从旁边壶里倒些热水，再将盏双手捧着晃几下，倒还有些意思。晃完，四处瞧瞧，不知道该把盏中的水倒在何处。

Sighing, Qianli Xun walked over to the tea table. Staring at the intimidating array of teaware, she didn't even know where to begin. She wracked her brain, trying to recall how Kuqiao usually did it. *Right, warming the bowl first!*

Qianli Xun picked up a tea bowl, set it down, and poured hot water into it from the kettle. Holding the bowl with both hands, she swirled the water around a few times. *Hey, this is kind of fun.* But after swirling, she looked around, stumped as to where to discard the hot water.

---

苦荞急得在心里提醒：“废水盂就在旁边，倒里面就行！”

*The slop basin is RIGHT THERE! Just pour it in there!* the maid screamed in her heart.

---

结果看到小姐盯着地面瞧了一会儿，直接把水倒在地上，苦荞一脸无奈。

To her absolute dismay, she watched her lady stare at the floor for a moment—and pour the hot water directly onto the ground. Kuqiao pressed her hand to her forehead in despair.

---

千里寻把盏放好，接下来该怎么做？对，放茶粉！她拿过茶粉盒，用小木勺给盏中放一勺茶粉，感觉有些少，又一连放了好几勺。

Qianli Xun set the warmed bowl back down. *What's next? Ah, the powdered tea!* She grabbed the tea caddy, used a small bamboo spoon to scoop out a bit of green powder, and dropped it into the bowl. Feeling it wasn't enough, she dumped several more overflowing scoops inside.

---

苦荞又在心里提醒：“多了！放多了！”

*Too much! TOO MUCH!* Kuqiao shrieked silently.

---

接下来该注入热水，千里寻记得苦荞做茶时，分好几次将水注入，到底多少次，她不记得。

Next came pouring the hot water. Qianli Xun remembered Kuqiao adding water in multiple controlled stages, though she couldn't remember how many.

---

常言道事不过三，那就三次吧。既然三次要把盏倒满，第一次不能多也不能少。千里寻按照自己的判断，将水加入，她认为不多不少，再倒两次，则刚刚好。

*Well, as the saying goes, good things come in threes. Three pours it is!* Since three pours needed to fill the bowl, her first pour couldn't be too little or too much. Qianli Xun added water according to her own flawed logic, confident she had nailed the proportion.

---

可急坏一旁的苦荞，继续在心里提醒：“水倒多了！倒多了！”

The maid was practically sweating bullets. *Too much water! WAY too much!*

---

千里寻拿起一旁的茶筅，在盏中开始搅动。

Qianli Xun picked up the bamboo tea whisk and began sloshing it around in the bowl.

---

“击拂，击拂，不是胡乱搅！”苦荞不忍心看下去，她捂住眼睛。做茶的方法曾是小姐教她的，现在的小姐竟然一点儿都不会。捂了一会儿，还是担心小姐，又把手放下，见小姐仅倒三次水，再胡乱搅几下，茶算是做好。

*Whisk! You WHISK! That's not—oh, Buddha, that's stirring soup—* Kuqiao slapped both hands over her eyes. Her lady used to be the one who taught *her* how to brew tea, yet now she looked like she had never seen a whisk in her life!

After a moment, anxiety got the better of Kuqiao, and she peeked through her fingers. She watched in horror as her lady stopped after just three hasty pours of water and a few chaotic stirs. The tea was apparently... "done."

---

“七次注汤，在小姐这里变成三次注汤，做出的能是茶吗？”苦荞继续着心里的话语，瞧一眼旁边的慈玄法师，她一脸严肃，眼睛虽盯着鼻端，可也让苦荞不敢妄动。

*Seven pourings. SEVEN. The miss had done three. Three!* And that... that thing in the bowl... calling it "tea" was an insult to tea leaves everywhere. Kuqiao stole a glance at the stone-faced nun beside her. Not a flicker of emotion. Eyes fixed on the tip of her nose. Kuqiao didn't dare make a single sudden move.

---

千里寻看着盏中的茶，跟苦荞做的无法相比，简直一个天上一个地下，茶汤中零散地躺着些许细沫，让人一看，产生不了一点儿品茶的欲望。

Qianli Xun stared down at her creation. Compared to Kuqiao's tea, this was night and day. A few pathetic flecks of foam floated lazily on the murky surface, a sight so unappetizing it killed any desire to take even a single sip.

---

能做到这种程度，千里寻算是尽力。她已想好，如果住持因此不愿见她，就冲进隔壁的山洞，跪在住持面前哭诉，不相信打动不了她。打定主意后，吩咐道：“苦荞，把茶盏端给法师。”

Well, this was the absolute best she could do. Qianli Xun had already formulated a contingency plan: if the old lady rejected her because of this awful tea, she would simply charge straight into the neighboring chamber, drop to her knees before the old woman, and weep her heart out until she yielded.

Mind made up, she commanded, "Kuqiao, present the tea to the Master."

---

苦荞上前端起茶盏，瞧那茶汤，心想：“这哪是给人喝的？”

Kuqiao stepped forward and picked up the tea bowl. Looking at the swampy liquid inside, she groaned internally. *Is this even fit for human consumption?*

---

慈玄接过，也瞧了瞧茶汤，眉头不由皱起，没说一句话，出了山洞。

The nun took the bowl and glanced at the contents. Her brow twitched slightly. Silently, she turned and ducked out through the doorway.

---

千里寻起身对苦荞说：“我做的茶住持肯定瞧不上，一会儿我要冲进隔壁的山洞。”

Qianli Xun immediately stood up and whispered to Kuqiao, "The abbess is definitely going to despise that tea. In a moment, I'm going to rush into the next chamber."

---

苦荞很担忧：“能行吗？”

Kuqiao was terrified. "Will that really work?"

---

“怎么不行？世间之事，有时太遵守规矩反而办不成，必须出其不意，才能取得意想不到的效果。待会儿慈玄法师回来，只要说让我们下山，你我假装同意乖乖走出山洞，趁她不注意，你帮我拽住她，我瞅准机会冲进去。”

"Why not? Sometimes following rules too strictly gets you nowhere. You need to catch them off guard to get unexpected results. When Master Cixuan comes back and tells us to leave, we'll pretend to agree and walk out obediently. Then, while she's not looking, you grab her, and I'll rush in."

---

苦荞点头同意。

Kuqiao nodded nervously.

---

很快慈玄法师掀开布帘进入，还未张口，千里寻立刻说：“法师不用说，肯定因为我做的茶不好，住持不愿见我，我这就带丫鬟下山。”说完顺便向苦荞使了个眼色。

Moments later, the curtain stirred. The elder nun stepped in. Before she could speak, Qianli Xun cut in quickly: "No need to say it, Master! My tea was terrible, so the abbess refuses to see me. I'll take my maid and leave at once." As she spoke, she gave Kuqiao a hard elbow nudge.

---

慈玄法师笑道：“住持允许施主进山洞与她见上一面。”

To her shock, the stone face cracked. The elder nun smiled gently. "The abbess has granted you permission to enter her chamber and meet with her."

---
