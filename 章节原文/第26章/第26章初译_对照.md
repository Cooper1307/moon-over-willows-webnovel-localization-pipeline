第26章 有缘才做三件事
## Chapter 26: Fate and Three Trials

正殿后面除供香客烧香跪拜的殿阁外，两边是寮房。
Behind the main hall stood smaller worship halls flanked on both sides by monastic living quarters.

女尼掀开一个房间的布帘：“两位请。”
The young nun lifted the cloth curtain of one room. "Please step inside."

千里寻认为主持应该就在里面，可走进去后，除了桌椅茶具，空无一人。
Expecting the abbess to be waiting, Qianli Xun entered—only to find an empty room with a table, chairs, and tea set.

女尼说：“二位在此稍侯，我去请慈玄法师。”
"Wait a moment here," the young nun said. "I'll fetch Master Cixuan."

千里寻强调道：“小师傅，我找净光法师，不找慈玄法师。”
"Little Master, I asked for Master Jingguang, not Master Cixuan!" Qianli Xun corrected her.

女尼没有说话，掀开帘子走出去。
Without answering, the young nun ducked back past the curtain.

千里寻只得找椅子坐下，耐心等候。
Nothing to do but pull up a chair and wait.

大约过了一刻钟，布帘被掀开，进来一名年纪较大的女尼，手拿佛珠：“阿弥陀佛，让施主久等。”
About a quarter hour later, the curtain lifted and an older nun entered holding prayer beads. "Amitabha. Forgive the wait, benefactor."

千里寻起身问：“您是慈玄法师？”
Rising, Qianli Xun asked, "You're Master Cixuan?"

“正是。”
"I am."

“我想拜见净光法师，请她为我指点迷津。”
"I came to beg an audience with Master Jingguang—hoping she'll point the way through my troubles."

“住持她老人家不随便接见外人……”
"The Abbess does not receive outside visitors lightly..."

千里寻急了：“不随便接见外人？为何刚才的小师傅要带我进来？”
Qianli Xun flared in alarm. "Not lightly? Then why did that young nun bring me back here?!"

“她刚刚剃度出家，在正殿负责敲击木鱼，有些规矩不清楚，我已批评过她。”
"She only recently took vows and tends the wooden fish in the main hall; she didn't know the rules. I have already reprimanded her."

千里寻直接傻眼，本以为很顺利，原来是这么回事。进都进来，总不能白跑一趟。
Stumped—so much for smooth sailing! Having climbed all the way up here, she wasn't leaving empty-handed.

“求法师通融，要不是遇到人生难题，我也不敢随便打扰住持。”
"Please make an exception, Master! I wouldn't dare trouble the Abbess if I weren't facing a crisis of a lifetime."

“请施主下山去吧。”
"Please head back down the mountain, benefactor."

千里寻给苦荞使使眼色，苦荞拿出一两银子塞给慈玄法师：“请法师笑纳。”
Qianli Xun signaled Kuqiao with her eyes. Kuqiao slipped out one tael of silver and pressed it toward Master Cixuan. "A small offering, Master—please accept."

“出家人不贪图世间财物，施主还是收回去吧。”
"Monastics do not covet worldly wealth. Take it back, benefactor."

慈玄面无表情，给人难以接近之感，苦荞只得把银子收回，用目光向小姐请示。
Master Cixuan's face stayed cold as stone; pulling the silver back, Kuqiao glanced at her mistress for orders.

千里寻以为对方嫌银子给得少，又给苦荞使眼色。
Thinking one tael looked stingy, Qianli Xun gave another nod.

苦荞从怀里拿出一袋银子，把那一两也放进去，再把钱袋递到慈玄面前：“法师还是笑纳吧。”
Kuqiao drew the entire silver pouch, dropped the single tael back inside, and offered the whole bag. "Please accept our sincere offering, Master."

慈玄依旧面无表情，苦荞只好又拿回银子。
Not a muscle twitched on Master Cixuan's face; Kuqiao tucked the pouch away again.

千里寻见钱无用，立刻跪在地上，苦荞也跟着跪下。
Seeing silver fail, Qianli Xun dropped straight to her knees, Kuqiao dropping right beside her.

“今日不见到净光法师，我绝不下山，恳请慈玄法师通融。”
"I'm not leaving this mountain until I see Master Jingguang! Please grant this favor, Master Cixuan!"

千里寻下跪倒是让慈玄感到意外，好言劝道：“施主起来下山去吧，住持她年事已高，不愿再为世俗之人预知未来。昨日一位临安高官前来想见住持，也被我请下山去。”
Surprised by the abrupt kneel, Master Cixuan spoke milder: "Rise and go home, benefactor. The Abbess is advanced in years and no longer reads fortunes for laypeople. Just yesterday a senior Lin'an official came seeking her, and I sent him down the mountain too."

“我不起来，也不下山，净光法师不见我，我就永远跪在这里，反正回到现在的家里还不如待在寺庙，再不济我也剃度出家，不怕见不到住持。”
"I'm not getting up and I'm not going down! If Master Jingguang won't see me, I'll kneel here forever—going back to that house is worse than staying in a nunnery anyway! Push me and I'll shave my head right here; then you can't keep me from seeing the Abbess!"

千里寻十分坚决，搞得慈玄法师为难起来。
Her iron stubbornness put Master Cixuan in a real bind.

正犹豫间，进来一名女尼，年纪看上去与慈玄相仿，在慈玄耳边悄悄说句话，很快又出去。
Just as she hesitated, another nun of about the same age slipped in, whispered a single line in Cixuan's ear, and slipped straight back out.

慈玄面色缓和许多：“起来吧，看在你与住持有缘，请跟我来。”
Master Cixuan's stony face softened at once. "Rise. Fate links you to the Abbess—follow me."

终于有进展，千里寻很开心，起身跟着走出寮房，心想这回肯定能见到住持尊容，刚才慈玄都说了，她与住持有缘。
Breakthrough! Beaming, Qianli Xun climbed up and followed her out of the quarters—sure she was about to meet the Abbess face to face now that fate had spoken.

千里寻跟着慈玄穿过一个石门，向东边拐去，走了没多久，来到两个山洞跟前。
Passing through a stone archway and turning east, they soon came upon two mountain caves side by side.

慈玄掀开左边山洞的布帘：“施主，请。”
Master Cixuan lifted the curtain over the left cave. "Please enter, benefactor."

千里寻带着苦荞走进后，并未看到任何人影。
Leading Kuqiao inside, Qianli Xun found not a single soul.

迎面是尊佛像，前面放着一个很大的功德箱；旁边有个茶桌，上面摆着做茶的器具和材料；左边有一个书桌，笔墨纸砚放置得整整齐齐；再瞧两面墙壁，挂满唐诗，与佛像显得很不相称，不过字倒写得圆润饱满，不失风骨。
Straight ahead stood a Buddha statue fronted by a large donation box; beside it sat a tea table stocked with whisking gear and supplies; to the left stood a study desk with the Four Treasures of the Study neatly lined up; and along both walls hung Tang poems—odd decor next to a Buddha, though written in rich, vigorous calligraphy.

千里寻无心欣赏书法与诗，问道：“住持呢？”
Paying no mind to art or verse, Qianli Xun asked, "Where's the Abbess?"

“住持在右边的山洞，施主想见住持，缘是第一步，还需完成三件事，得到住持允许，方可与她相见。”
"In the right-hand cave. Fate is only step one—to meet the Abbess, you must pass three tests and win her approval."

慈玄法师的话让千里寻感到前路障碍重重，好不容易走到这一步，还需接着往下走。
Hoop after hoop! Having come this far, she wasn't turning back now.

“请法师说说是哪三件事？”
"Name the three tests, Master."

“第一件事很简单，只需将施主身上的钱财以及所有值钱之物统统放入功德箱。”
"First is simple: place every coin and every valuable item on your person into the donation box."

千里寻心想：“确实简单，她刚才不为钱财所动，原来是为将我洗劫一空。算了，不跟她计较！”想到这里，千里寻叫声：“苦荞。”
Simple indeed! Qianli Xun thought wryly: Refused my silver earlier just to clean me out completely here! Fine, whatever! "Kuqiao!" she called.

苦荞赶紧把身上带的银子全部拿出，放入功德箱中。
Kuqiao emptied every last coin they had brought straight into the donation box.

千里寻又将玉镯，耳坠，以及头上的金钗统统取下，放入功德箱内。
Qianli Xun stripped off her jade bangle, earrings, and gold hairpins and dropped them all inside.

“法师，第一件事已做完，快说第二件吧。”
"Test one done, Master—what's number two?"

慈玄法师说：“你那丫鬟身上还有贵重之物。”
"Your maid still wears something of value," Master Cixuan pointed out.

苦荞为难道：“是小姐要见住持，又不是我，为何还要我身上唯一值钱的银镯子？”
Kuqiao balked. "Miss is the one seeing the Abbess, not me—why take my one and only silver bracelet?!"

千里寻走到苦荞身边，抓起她的右手，掀开袖子，果然手腕上有个银镯子，强行取下：“回头我送你好几个。”
Stepping over, Qianli Xun grabbed Kuqiao's right wrist, pushed back her sleeve, and slipped the silver bracelet off. "I'll buy you a whole stack later!"

苦荞不再为难：“小姐说话可要算话。”
Relieved, Kuqiao grumbled, "You'd better keep your word, Miss!"

千里寻把银镯放进功德箱：“放心，你家小姐我一诺千金。”
Dropping the bracelet into the box, Qianli Xun promised, "A promise worth a thousand gold!"

慈玄满意道：“这第二件需施主在墙上挑一首诗，将解诗的内容写在纸上，由我呈给住持，她老人家满意，方可进行第三件，如若她老人家不满意，也请即刻下山。”
Satisfied, Master Cixuan moved on: "Second: pick any poem on the wall, write out your interpretation on paper, and I will present it to the Abbess. If she approves, you advance to test three; if not, leave the mountain at once."

解诗对千里寻来讲并非难事，她再次环顾两面墙上的唐诗。
Analyzing poetry? Easy! She scanned the Tang poems lining both walls.

以前的千里寻对王右丞的诗并不感兴趣，不知为何此刻的她被那首《竹里馆》吸引住。她靠近墙边，细心琢磨诗句，内心文思如泉涌，转过身，走到书桌前坐下，提笔写下所思所想。
Before transmigrating, Qianli Xun hadn't cared much for Wang Wei—yet right now his "The Bamboo Pavilion" pulled her eyes like a magnet. Stepping close to study the lines, ideas surged into her head; turning to the desk, she sat down and poured every thought onto paper.

苦荞见小姐写得非常专注，心中暗喜：“现在的小姐看上去跟从前没有任何区别。”
Watching her mistress write with deep focus, Kuqiao smiled inside: Right now she looks identical to the old Miss!

千里寻用小楷写了整整一大张纸，终于写完，搁下笔：“法师，辛苦您拿给住持阅览。”
Filling a full sheet in crisp small regular script, Qianli Xun set down her brush. "Please deliver this to the Abbess, Master."

苦荞过去把纸张拿了，送到慈玄法师手中。慈玄接过，简单折起后，掀开布帘离开。
Kuqiao carried the sheet over to Master Cixuan, who folded it neat and stepped past the curtain.

没多久，慈玄空手而归：“恭喜施主，住持对您解的诗很满意，可以进行第三件。”
Minutes later Cixuan returned empty-handed. "Congratulations, benefactor—the Abbess approved your essay. Ready for test three."

千里寻早已预料到结果，别的她不敢说，解个把诗倒不在话下：“第三件是什么？”
No surprise there—literary essays were her bread and butter. "What's test three?"

“第三件是为住持做盏茶，住持品后若满意，便可与她老人家见面，若不满意还请施主下山。”
"Whisk a bowl of tea for the Abbess. If she approves upon tasting, you may meet her; if not, descend the mountain."

做茶是苦荞的强项，千里寻还未学会：“可否让我的丫鬟来做？”
Whisking tea was Kuqiao's specialty—Qianli Xun hadn't learned a lick yet! "Can my maid make it?"

“不行，是你求事，又不是你的丫鬟，必须施主亲自动手。”
"No. You seek the audience, not your maid. You must prepare it with your own hands."

“能不能让我的丫鬟在一旁指点。”
"Can my maid stand beside me and talk me through it?"

“也不行，在一旁指点跟她做没什么区别。”
"No—that is no different from her making it herself."

千里寻只能离开书桌，来到茶桌跟前，看到一桌子的茶具，不知该如何下手。努力回忆苦荞做茶的过程，好像需先温盏。千里寻拿起一个茶盏，放到面前，从旁边壶里倒些热水，再将盏双手捧着晃几下，倒还有些意思。晃完，四处瞧瞧，不知道该把盏中的水倒在何处。
Leaving the desk for the tea table, Qianli Xun stared blankly at the array of tools. Straining to recall Kuqiao's moves—first, warm the tea bowl! Picking up a bowl, she poured in hot water from the kettle and swirled it in both hands—kind of fun! Done swirling, she looked around clueless where to dump the rinse water.

苦荞急得在心里提醒：“废水盂就在旁边，倒里面就行!”
Kuqiao screamed inside her head: The waste water bowl is RIGHT THERE! Dump it in there!

结果看到小姐盯着地面瞧了一会儿，直接把水倒在地上，苦荞一脸无奈。
Instead, her mistress glanced at the floor and splashed the water straight onto the ground, leaving Kuqiao staring in despair.

千里寻把盏放好，接下来该怎么做？对，放茶粉！她拿过茶粉盒，用小木勺给盏中放一勺茶粉，感觉有些少，又一连放好几勺。
Setting the bowl down—next step: tea powder! Opening the caddy, she scooped one wooden spoonful in, thought it looked skimpy, and dumped several more big scoops on top.

苦荞又在心里提醒：“多了！放多了！”
Too much! Way too much! Kuqiao wailed silently.

接下来该注入热水，千里寻记得苦荞做茶时，分好几次将水注入，到底多少次，她不记得。
Next, hot water! She remembered Kuqiao adding water in rounds—how many rounds though?

常言道事不过三，那就三次吧。既然三次要把盏倒满，第一次不能多也不能少。千里寻按照自己的判断，将水加入，她认为不多不少，再倒两次，则刚刚好。
Things come in threes—so three rounds it is! If three pours had to fill the bowl, round one needed a hefty splash. Going by gut, she poured in a third of a bowl.

可急坏一旁的苦荞，继续在心里提醒：“水倒多了！倒多了！”
Too much water! You flooded it! Kuqiao nearly fainted inside.

千里寻拿起一旁的茶筅，在盏中开始搅动。
Grabbing the bamboo whisk, Qianli Xun started stirring in circles.

“击拂，击拂，不是胡乱搅！”苦荞不忍心看下去，她捂住眼睛。做茶的方法曾是小姐教她的，现在的小姐竟然一点儿都不会。捂了一会儿，还是担心小姐，又把手放下，见小姐仅倒三次水，再胡乱搅几下，茶算是做好。
Whisk back and forth—not stir like soup! Unable to watch another second, Kuqiao clapped both hands over her eyes. Miss herself taught me how to whisk tea—how did she forget every single step?! Peeking through her fingers, she saw her mistress finish in three pours and a few clumsy stirs.

“七次注汤，在小姐这里变成三次注汤，做出的能是茶吗？”苦荞继续着心里的话语，瞧一眼旁边的慈玄法师，她一脸严肃，眼睛虽盯着鼻端，可也让苦荞不敢妄动。
Seven infusions turned into three splashes—can you even call that tea?! Kuqiao lamented silently, glancing at stone-faced Master Cixuan, whose strict posture kept the maid rooted in place.

千里寻看着盏中的茶，跟苦荞做的无法相比，简直一个天上一个地下，茶汤中零散地躺着些许细沫，让人一看，产生不了一点儿品茶的欲望。
Qianli Xun peered down at her creation: worlds apart from Kuqiao's creamy froth, just murky liquid dotted with pathetic specks of foam nobody in their right mind would drink.

能做到这种程度，千里寻算是尽力。她已想好，如果住持因此不愿见她，就冲进隔壁的山洞，跪在住持面前哭诉，不相信打动不了她。打定主意后，吩咐道：“苦荞，把茶盏端给法师。”
Oh well—she'd given it her best shot. Plan B was already set: if the Abbess turned her away over bad tea, she'd storm straight into the next cave, drop to her knees, and cry until the Abbess caved! Mind made up, she ordered, "Kuqiao, hand the bowl to Master."

苦荞上前端起茶盏，瞧那茶汤，心想：“这哪是给人喝的？”
Lifting the bowl and peeking inside, Kuqiao thought: Who on earth could drink this?!

慈玄接过，也瞧了瞧茶汤，眉头不由皱起，没说一句话，出了山洞。
Master Cixuan took it, glanced at the brew, knitted her brows without a word, and walked out.

千里寻起身对苦荞说：“我做的茶住持肯定瞧不上，一会儿我要冲进隔壁的山洞。”
Rising, Qianli Xun whispered to Kuqiao, "No way the Abbess likes my tea—in a minute I'm rushing straight into the next cave!"

苦荞很担忧：“能行吗？”
"Will that even work?" Kuqiao worried.

“怎么不行？世间之事，有时太遵守规矩反而办不成，必须出其不意，才能取得意想不到的效果。待会儿慈玄法师回来，只要说让我们下山，你我假装同意乖乖走出山洞，趁她不注意，你帮我拽住她，我瞅准机会冲进去。”
"Sure it will! Playing by the rules gets you nowhere sometimes—you've got to catch folks off guard! The second Master Cixuan tells us to leave, we play nice and step outside; the instant she looks away, you grab her arm and I bolt straight inside!"

苦荞点头同意。
Kuqiao nodded.

很快慈玄法师掀开布帘进入，还未张口，千里寻立刻说：“法师不用说，肯定因为我做的茶不好，住持不愿见我，我这就带丫鬟下山。”说完顺便向苦荞使了个眼色。
Moments later Master Cixuan parted the curtain. Before she could open her mouth, Qianli Xun jumped in: "Don't bother saying it, Master—my tea was awful, the Abbess won't see me, we'll pack up and go!" She shot Kuqiao the signal glance.

慈玄法师笑道：“住持允许施主进山洞与她见上一面。”
A faint smile broke across Master Cixuan's face. "The Abbess grants you leave to enter her cave for an audience."

真是喜出望外，那么差的一盏茶住持竟然不嫌弃？看来她老人家在茶方面要求不高，既如此又何必搞这一出？反正能见面是好事，既免去千里寻采取莽撞的方式达到目的，也保住朱淑真大家闺秀的风范。
Jackpot! The Abbess accepted that swamp water?! Guess she wasn't picky about tea—then why make her whisk it in the first place? Never mind! Getting in without causing a scene saved both the effort and Zhu Shuzhen's ladylike image.
