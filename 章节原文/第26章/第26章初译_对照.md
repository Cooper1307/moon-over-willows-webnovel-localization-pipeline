# Chapter 26: Three Tasks for the Destined

---

正殿后面除供香客烧香跪拜的殿阁外，两边是寮房。

Behind the main hall, besides the pavilions for pilgrims to burn incense and kneel in worship, there were monastic quarters on both sides.

---

女尼掀开一个房间的布帘："两位请。"

The nun lifted the curtain of one room. "Please, you two."

---

千里寻认为主持应该就在里面，可走进去后，除了桌椅茶具，空无一人。

Qianli Xun thought the abbess should be inside. But after walking in, she found no one—only tables, chairs, and tea utensils.

---

女尼说："二位在此稍侯，我去请慈玄法师。"

The nun said, "Please wait here. I'll go fetch Master Cixuan."

---

千里寻强调道："小师傅，我找净光法师，不找慈玄法师。"

Qianli Xun emphasized, "Young sister, I'm looking for Master Jingguang, not Master Cixuan."

---

女尼没有说话，掀开帘子走出去。

The nun said nothing. She pulled back the curtain and walked out.

---

千里寻只得找椅子坐下，耐心等候。

Qianli Xun had no choice but to find a chair and wait patiently.

---

大约过了一刻钟，布帘被掀开，进来一名年纪较大的女尼，手拿佛珠："阿弥陀佛，让施主久等。"

About a quarter of an hour passed before the curtain was lifted. An older nun entered, holding prayer beads. "Amitabha. I've kept the donor waiting."

---

千里寻起身问："您是慈玄法师？"

Qianli Xun stood and asked, "Are you Master Cixuan?"

---

"正是。"

"I am."

---

"我想拜见净光法师，请她为我指点迷津。"

"I wish to meet Master Jingguang and ask her to guide me through my confusion."

---

"住持她老人家不随便接见外人……"

"The abbess doesn't casually receive outsiders..."

---

千里寻急了："不随便接见外人？为何刚才的小师傅要带我进来？"

Qianli Xun grew anxious. "Doesn't casually receive outsiders? Then why did the young nun just now bring me in?"

---

"她刚刚剃度出家，在正殿负责敲击木鱼，有些规矩不清楚，我已批评过她。"

"She was just tonsured and became a nun. She's responsible for striking the wooden fish in the main hall. She doesn't know some of the rules. I've already reprimanded her."

---

千里寻直接傻眼，本以为很顺利，原来是这么回事。进都进来，总不能白跑一趟。

Qianli Xun was stunned. She thought it was going smoothly, but it turned out like this. Since she'd already come in, she couldn't make the trip for nothing.

---

"求法师通融，要不是遇到人生难题，我也不敢随便打扰住持。"

"I beg you to make an exception, Master. If I hadn't encountered a difficult situation in life, I wouldn't dare disturb the abbess casually."

---

"请施主下山去吧。"

"Please go down the mountain, donor."

---

千里寻给苦荞使使眼色，苦荞拿出一两银子塞给慈玄法师："请法师笑纳。"

Qianli Xun signaled Kuqiao with her eyes. Kuqiao took out one tael of silver and offered it to Master Cixuan. "Please accept this, Master."

---

"出家人不贪图世间财物，施主还是收回去吧。"

"Those who have left the world don't covet worldly possessions. Please take it back, donor."

---

慈玄面无表情，给人难以接近之感，苦荞只得把银子收回，用目光向小姐请示。

Cixuan's expression was blank, giving off an unapproachable vibe. Kuqiao had to take the silver back and looked to her miss for instructions.

---

千里寻以为对方嫌银子给得少，又给苦荞使眼色。

Qianli Xun thought the other party found the silver too little, so she signaled Kuqiao again.

---

苦荞从怀里拿出一袋银子，把那一两也放进去，再把钱袋递到慈玄面前："法师还是笑纳吧。"

Kuqiao took out a silver pouch from her bosom, put that one tael in too, and handed the money pouch to Cixuan. "Please accept it, Master."

---

慈玄依旧面无表情，苦荞只好又拿回银子。

Cixuan remained expressionless. Kuqiao had to take the silver back again.

---

千里寻见钱无用，立刻跪在地上，苦荞也跟着跪下。

Seeing that money was useless, Qianli Xun immediately knelt on the ground. Kuqiao followed and knelt too.

---

"今日不见到净光法师，我绝不下山，恳请慈玄法师通融。"

"Today, if I don't meet Master Jingguang, I absolutely won't go down the mountain. I earnestly beg Master Cixuan to make an exception."

---

千里寻下跪倒是让慈玄感到意外，好言劝道："施主起来下山去吧，住持她年事已高，不愿再为世俗之人预知未来。昨日一位临安高官前来想见住持，也被我请下山去。"

Qianli Xun's kneeling actually surprised Cixuan. She advised kindly, "Madam, please get up and leave. The abbess is advanced in years and doesn't wish to predict the future for worldly people anymore. Yesterday, a high official from Lin'an came to see the abbess, and I also asked him to leave."

---

"我不起来，也不下山，净光法师不见我，我就永远跪在这里，反正回到现在的家里还不如待在寺庙，再不济我也剃度出家，不怕见不到住持。"

"I won't get up, and I won't leave. If Master Jingguang doesn't see me, I'll kneel here forever. Anyway, going back to my current home is worse than staying in the temple. If it comes to it, I'll get tonsured and become a nun. I'm not afraid of not seeing the abbess."

---

千里寻十分坚决，搞得慈玄法师为难起来。

Qianli Xun was very resolute, which put Master Cixuan in a difficult position.

---

正犹豫间，进来一名女尼，年纪看上去与慈玄相仿，在慈玄耳边悄悄说句话，很快又出去。

Just as she was hesitating, another nun entered. She looked about the same age as Cixuan. She whispered something in Cixuan's ear and quickly left.

---

慈玄面色缓和许多："起来吧，看在你与住持有缘，请跟我来。"

Cixuan's expression softened considerably. "Get up. Since you're destined with the abbess, please follow me."

---

终于有进展，千里寻很开心，起身跟着走出寮房，心想这回肯定能见到住持尊容，刚才慈玄都说了，她与住持有缘。

Finally, there was progress. Qianli Xun was very happy. She stood up and followed Cixuan out of the monastic quarters, thinking that this time she would definitely see the abbess's face. Cixuan had just said she was destined with the abbess.

---

千里寻跟着慈玄穿过一个石门，向东边拐去，走了没多久，来到两个山洞跟前。

Qianli Xun followed Cixuan through a stone gate and turned east. After walking a short distance, they arrived at two caves.

---

慈玄掀开左边山洞的布帘："施主，请。"

Cixuan lifted the curtain of the left cave. "Please, Madam."

---

千里寻带着苦荞走进后，并未看到任何人影。

Qianli Xun walked in with Kuqiao but didn't see anyone.

---

迎面是尊佛像，前面放着一个很大的功德箱；旁边有个茶桌，上面摆着做茶的器具和材料；左边有一个书桌，笔墨纸砚放置得整整齐齐；再瞧两面墙壁，挂满唐诗，与佛像显得很不相称，不过字倒写得圆润饱满，不失风骨。

Facing them was a Buddha statue. In front of it was a very large merit box. Beside it was a tea table with tea-making utensils and materials. On the left was a desk with brush, ink, paper, and inkstone neatly placed. The two walls were hung with Tang poetry. It seemed quite incongruous with the Buddha statue, but the calligraphy was rounded and full, not losing its character.

---

千里寻无心欣赏书法与诗，问道："住持呢？"

Qianli Xun had no mind to appreciate the calligraphy and poetry. She asked, "Where's the abbess?"

---

"住持在右边的山洞，施主想见住持，缘是第一步，还需完成三件事，得到住持允许，方可与她相见。"

"The abbess is in the right cave. For the donor to see the abbess, destiny is the first step. You still need to complete three things. Only after getting the abbess's permission can you meet her."

---

慈玄法师的话让千里寻感到前路障碍重重，好不容易走到这一步，还需接着往下走。

Master Cixuan's words made Qianli Xun feel there were many obstacles ahead. She had worked hard to get to this step. She had to continue.

---

"请法师说说是哪三件事？"

"Please tell me, Master, what are the three things?"

---

"第一件事很简单，只需将施主身上的钱财以及所有值钱之物统统放入功德箱。"

"The first thing is simple. Just put all the money and valuables on your person into the merit box."

---

千里寻心想："确实简单，她刚才不为钱财所动，原来是为将我洗劫一空。算了，不跟她计较！"想到这里，千里寻叫声："苦荞。"

Qianli Xun thought, "Indeed simple. She wasn't moved by money just now. It turns out it was to rob me clean. Forget it, I won't argue with her!" Thinking this, Qianli Xun called, "Kuqiao."

---

苦荞赶紧把身上带的银子全部拿出，放入功德箱中。

Kuqiao quickly took out all the silver she carried and put it into the merit box.

---

千里寻又将玉镯，耳坠，以及头上的金钗统统取下，放入功德箱内。

Qianli Xun also took off her jade bracelet, earrings, and the golden hairpin on her head, putting them all into the merit box.

---

"法师，第一件事已做完，快说第二件吧。"

"Master, the first thing is done. Quickly tell me the second."

---

慈玄法师说："你那丫鬟身上还有贵重之物。"

Master Cixuan said, "Your maid still has valuables on her."

---

苦荞为难道："是小姐要见住持，又不是我，为何还要我身上唯一值钱的银镯子？"

Kuqiao said with difficulty, "It's the miss who wants to see the abbess, not me. Why do you still want the only valuable thing on me—my silver bracelet?"

---

千里寻走到苦荞身边，抓起她的右手，掀开袖子，果然手腕上有个银镯子，强行取下："回头我送你好几个。"

Qianli Xun walked to Kuqiao's side, grabbed her right hand, lifted her sleeve, and sure enough, there was a silver bracelet on her wrist. She forced it off. "I'll give you several more later."

---

苦荞不再为难："小姐说话可要算话。"

Kuqiao stopped protesting. "Miss, you must keep your word."

---

千里寻把银镯放进功德箱："放心，你家小姐我一诺千金。"

Qianli Xun put the silver bracelet into the merit box. "Don't worry. Your miss keeps her word."

---

慈玄满意道："这第二件需施主在墙上挑一首诗，将解诗的内容写在纸上，由我呈给住持，她老人家满意，方可进行第三件，如若她老人家不满意，也请即刻下山。"

Cixuan said satisfactorily, "For the second thing, the donor needs to pick a poem from the wall, write the interpretation on paper, and I'll present it to the abbess. If she's satisfied, you can proceed to the third thing. If she's not satisfied, please leave immediately."

---

解诗对千里寻来讲并非难事，她再次环顾两面墙上的唐诗。

Interpreting poetry wasn't difficult for Qianli Xun. She looked around at the Tang poetry on the two walls again.

---

以前的千里寻对王右丞的诗并不感兴趣，不知为何此刻的她被那首《竹里馆》吸引住。她靠近墙边，细心琢磨诗句，内心文思如泉涌，转过身，走到书桌前坐下，提笔写下所思所想。

The old Qianli Xun wasn't interested in Wang Youcheng's poetry. She didn't know why, but at this moment, she was attracted by "Bamboo Lodge." She approached the wall and carefully pondered the lines. Her literary thoughts surged like a spring. She turned around, walked to the desk, sat down, picked up the brush, and wrote down her thoughts.

---

苦荞见小姐写得非常专注，心中暗喜："现在的小姐看上去跟从前没有任何区别。"

Kuqiao saw her miss writing very focused and was secretly pleased. "The miss now looks no different from before."

---

千里寻用小楷写了整整一大张纸，终于写完，搁下笔："法师，辛苦您拿给住持阅览。"

Qianli Xun wrote a whole large sheet of paper in small regular script. Finally finished, she put down the brush. "Master, thank you for taking this to the abbess for review."

---

苦荞过去把纸张拿了，送到慈玄法师手中。慈玄接过，简单折起后，掀开布帘离开。

Kuqiao went over and took the paper, delivering it to Master Cixuan's hands. Cixuan accepted it, folded it simply, lifted the curtain, and left.

---

没多久，慈玄空手而归："恭喜施主，住持对您解的诗很满意，可以进行第三件。"

Not long after, Cixuan returned empty-handed. "Congratulations, donor. The abbess is very satisfied with your poetry interpretation. You can proceed to the third thing."

---

千里寻早已预料到结果，别的她不敢说，解个把诗倒不在话下："第三件是什么？"

Qianli Xun had already expected the result. She didn't dare say anything about other things, but interpreting a poem was no problem. "What's the third thing?"

---

"第三件是为住持做盏茶，住持品后若满意，便可与她老人家见面，若不满意还请施主下山。"

"The third thing is to make a bowl of tea for the abbess. If she's satisfied after tasting it, you can meet her. If not, please leave, Madam."

---

做茶是苦荞的强项，千里寻还未学会："可否让我的丫鬟来做？"

Making tea was Kuqiao's strength. Qianli Xun hadn't learned it yet. "Can my maid do it?"

---

"不行，是你求事，又不是你的丫鬟，必须施主亲自动手。"

"No. It's you who's seeking, not your maid. The donor must do it personally."

---

"能不能让我的丫鬟在一旁指点。"

"Can my maid guide me from the side?"

---

"也不行，在一旁指点跟她做没什么区别。"

"That won't work either. Guiding from the side is no different from her doing it."

---

千里寻只能离开书桌，来到茶桌跟前，看到一桌子的茶具，不知该如何下手。努力回忆苦荞做茶的过程，好像需先温盏。千里寻拿起一个茶盏，放到面前，从旁边壶里倒些热水，再将盏双手捧着晃几下，倒还有些意思。晃完，四处瞧瞧，不知道该把盏中的水倒在何处。

Qianli Xun could only leave the desk and come to the tea table. Seeing a table full of tea utensils, she didn't know where to start. She tried hard to recall Kuqiao's tea-making process. It seemed she needed to warm the bowl first. Qianli Xun picked up a tea bowl, put it in front of her, poured some hot water from the pot beside, then held the bowl with both hands and swirled it a few times. It actually felt somewhat interesting. After swirling, she looked around, not knowing where to pour the water in the bowl.

---

苦荞急得在心里提醒："废水盂就在旁边，倒里面就行!"

Kuqiao anxiously reminded her in her heart, "The waste water bowl is right beside you. Just pour it in there!"

---

结果看到小姐盯着地面瞧了一会儿，直接把水倒在地上，苦荞一脸无奈。

But she saw her miss stare at the ground for a while, then pour the water directly on the ground. Kuqiao looked helpless.

---

千里寻把盏放好，接下来该怎么做？对，放茶粉！她拿过茶粉盒，用小木勺给盏中放一勺茶粉，感觉有些少，又一连放了好几勺。

Qianli Xun put the bowl back. What should she do next? Right, add tea powder! She took the tea powder box, used a small wooden spoon to put one spoonful of tea powder in the bowl. It felt a bit little, so she added several more spoonfuls in succession.

---

苦荞又在心里提醒："多了！放多了！"

Kuqiao reminded her in her heart again, "Too much! You put too much!"

---

接下来该注入热水，千里寻记得苦荞做茶时，分好几次将水注入，到底多少次，她不记得。

Next, she should pour in hot water. Qianli Xun remembered that when Kuqiao made tea, she poured water in several times. How many times exactly? She couldn't remember.

---

常言道事不过三，那就三次吧。既然三次要把盏倒满，第一次不能多也不能少。千里寻按照自己的判断，将水加入，她认为不多不少，再倒两次，则刚刚好。

As the saying goes, things shouldn't exceed three. Then three times it is. Since three times would fill the bowl, the first time couldn't be too much or too little. Qianli Xun added water according to her own judgment. She thought it was neither too much nor too little. Two more pours would be just right.

---

可急坏一旁的苦荞，继续在心里提醒："水倒多了！倒多了！"

This made Kuqiao anxious. She continued reminding in her heart, "Too much water! Too much!"

---

千里寻拿起一旁的茶筅，在盏中开始搅动。

Qianli Xun picked up the tea whisk beside and started stirring in the bowl.

---

"击拂，击拂，不是胡乱搅！"苦荞不忍心看下去，她捂住眼睛。做茶的方法曾是小姐教她的，现在的小姐竟然一点儿都不会。捂了一会儿，还是担心小姐，又把手放下，见小姐仅倒三次水，再胡乱搅几下，茶算是做好。

"Whisk it! Whisk it! Don't just stir randomly!" Kuqiao couldn't bear to watch. She covered her eyes. The tea-making method was once taught by her miss. Now the miss didn't know any of it. After covering for a while, she still worried about her miss, so she put her hands down. She saw her miss only pour water three times, then stir randomly a few times. The tea was considered done.

---

"七次注汤，在小姐这里变成三次注汤，做出的能是茶吗？"苦荞继续着心里的话语，瞧一眼旁边的慈玄法师，她一脸严肃，眼睛虽盯着鼻端，可也让苦荞不敢妄动。

"Seven infusions became three infusions with the miss. Can what's made even be called tea?" Kuqiao continued her inner monologue. She glanced at Master Cixuan beside. Cixuan had a serious face. Though her eyes were fixed on the tip of her nose, it still made Kuqiao not dare to move rashly.

---

千里寻看着盏中的茶，跟苦荞做的无法相比，简直一个天上一个地下，茶汤中零散地躺着些许细沫，让人一看，产生不了一点儿品茶的欲望。

Qianli Xun looked at the tea in the bowl. It couldn't be compared to what Kuqiao made. It was like the difference between heaven and earth. Scattered fine foam lay in the tea liquor. At one glance, it didn't arouse any desire to taste tea.

---

能做到这种程度，千里寻算是尽力。她已想好，如果住持因此不愿见她，就冲进隔壁的山洞，跪在住持面前哭诉，不相信打动不了她。打定主意后，吩咐道："苦荞，把茶盏端给法师。"

Being able to do it to this extent, Qianli Xun had tried her best. She had already decided that if the abbess didn't want to see her because of this, she would rush into the cave next door, kneel in front of the abbess, and cry out her story. She didn't believe she couldn't move her. After making up her mind, she ordered, "Kuqiao, take the tea bowl to the Master."

---

苦荞上前端起茶盏，瞧那茶汤，心想："这哪是给人喝的？"

Kuqiao stepped forward and picked up the tea bowl. She looked at the tea liquor and thought, "How can this be for people to drink?"

---

慈玄接过，也瞧了瞧茶汤，眉头不由皱起，没说一句话，出了山洞。

Cixuan accepted it. She also looked at the tea liquor. Her brows couldn't help but frown. Without saying a word, she left the cave.

---

千里寻起身对苦荞说："我做的茶住持肯定瞧不上，一会儿我要冲进隔壁的山洞。"

Qianli Xun stood up and said to Kuqiao, "The abbess definitely won't appreciate the tea I made. In a moment, I'm going to rush into the cave next door."

---

苦荞很担忧："能行吗？"

Kuqiao was very worried. "Will it work?"

---

"怎么不行？世间之事，有时太遵守规矩反而办不成，必须出其不意，才能取得意想不到的效果。待会儿慈玄法师回来，只要说让我们下山，你我假装同意乖乖走出山洞，趁她不注意，你帮我拽住她，我瞅准机会冲进去。"

"Why not? In worldly matters, sometimes following the rules too strictly gets nothing done. You must take people by surprise to achieve unexpected results. Later, when Master Cixuan comes back and tells us to leave, you and I will pretend to agree and walk out of the cave obediently. While she's not paying attention, you help me hold her back. I'll seize the opportunity to rush in."

---

苦荞点头同意。

Kuqiao nodded in agreement.

---

很快慈玄法师掀开布帘进入，还未张口，千里寻立刻说："法师不用说，肯定因为我做的茶不好，住持不愿见我，我这就带丫鬟下山。"说完顺便向苦荞使了个眼色。

Soon, Master Cixuan lifted the curtain and entered. Before she could speak, Qianli Xun immediately said, "No need to say it, Master. It must be because the tea I made was bad that the abbess doesn't want to see me. I'll take my maid and leave now." As she spoke, she conveniently signaled Kuqiao with her eyes.

---

慈玄法师笑道："住持允许施主进山洞与她见上一面。"

Master Cixuan smiled. "The abbess allows you to enter the cave and meet her."

---

真是喜出望外，那么差的一盏茶住持竟然不嫌弃？看来她老人家在茶方面要求不高，既如此又何必搞这一出？反正能见面是好事，既免去千里寻采取莽撞的方式达到目的，也保住朱淑真大家闺秀的风范。

It was truly an unexpected joy. The abbess actually didn't mind such a bad bowl of tea? It seemed the old lady didn't have high requirements for tea. If so, why go through all this?
