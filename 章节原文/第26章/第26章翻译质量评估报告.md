## 四、翻译亮点

1. **点茶场景的内心独白**：
   - 原文：苦荞在心里提醒："废水盂就在旁边，倒里面就行！""多了！放多了！""水倒多了！倒多了！"
   - 译文：Kubiao anxiously reminded her in her heart, "The waste water bowl is right beside you. Just pour it in there!" / "Too much! You put too much!" / "Too much water! Too much!"
   - 准确传达苦荞的焦急和无奈，节奏感强

2. **千里寻的坚决态度**：
   - 原文："今日不见到净光法师，我绝不下山"
   - 译文："Today, if I don't meet Master Jingguang, I absolutely won't go down the mountain."
   - 语气坚决，符合人物性格

3. **慈玄的原则性**：
   - 原文："出家人不贪图世间财物，施主还是收回去吧。"
   - 译文："Those who have left the world don't covet worldly possessions. Please take it back, donor."
   - 宗教用语准确，语气庄重

## 五、需修改项汇总

### 必须修改（3处）：

   - 建议：**Young sister**

   - 建议：**Your miss keeps her word.** 或 **A promise from your miss is as good as gold.**

It seemed quite incongruous with the Buddha statue, but the calligraphy was rounded and full, not losing its character.
