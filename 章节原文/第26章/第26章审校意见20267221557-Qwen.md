## 一、标题

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Three Tasks for the Destined* |
| **修改后** | *Three Trials for the Fated*（或保留现译，已合格） |

## 三、表达正确性与地道性（前后对比）

### 1. "小师傅"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *"Young sister, I'm looking for Master Jingguang, not Master Cixuan."* |
| **修改后** | *"Sister, I'm looking for Master Jingguang—not Master Cixuan."* |
| **理由** | "Young sister" 在英文中 = 妹妹。英文修道院传统中称 *Sister*。破折号比逗号更能传达"不是X是Y"的强调 |

---

### 2. "与住持有缘"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *"Since you're destined with the abbess, please follow me."* |
| **修改后** | *"It seems you share a karmic tie with the abbess. Get up. Follow me."* |
| **理由** | "destined with" 语法搭配不成立。"karmic tie"（缘）是佛教英文标准用语。拆成三个短句更符合慈玄"惜字如金"的性格 |

---

### 3. "事不过三"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *As the saying goes, things shouldn't exceed three. Then three times it is.* |
| **修改后** | *Three's the limit—or so the saying went. Fine. Three pours it is.* |
| **理由** | "things shouldn't exceed three" 是字面直译，英文读者无法理解。"Three's the limit" 是自然英文表达 |

## 六、其他细节修正

| 修改前 | 修改后 | 理由 |
|--------|--------|------|
| *monastic quarters* | *cells*（或 *small rooms*） | "monastic quarters" 偏建筑学术语 |
| *Please, you two.* | *After you.* | "Please, you two" 像老师对学生 |
| *I've kept the donor waiting.* | *Forgive the wait.* | 更自然、更有高僧简洁感 |
| *giving off an unapproachable vibe* | *radiating do-not-approach energy* | "vibe" 偏口语/现代，与古风不搭；"energy" 同样偏现代但更有画面 |
| *Her literary thoughts surged like a spring.* | *Words flooded in—unbidden, unstoppable.* | "literary thoughts surged like a spring" 是"文思如泉涌"的直译，英文不自然 |
| *writing very focused* | *writing with such focus* | 语法修正（focused 不能直接做副词） |
| *small regular script* | *tiny, precise characters* | "small regular script" 是书法术语（小楷），网文读者不需要 |
| *The tea was considered done.* | *The tea was... "done."* | 加引号传达"这也算做完了？"的反讽 |
| *It didn't arouse any desire to taste tea.* | *Looking at it, you'd sooner drink the rinse water.* | 更具体、更有幽默感 |

| 标点/网文节奏 | **★★☆☆☆** | 内心戏格式错误；感叹号/破折号严重不足 |

| 读者吸引力 | ★★★★☆ | 做茶喜剧结构好，但笑点爆发力被翻译削弱 |
