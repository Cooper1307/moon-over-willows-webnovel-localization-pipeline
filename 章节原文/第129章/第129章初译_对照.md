## Chapter 129: Tax Cuts Smash the Rumors

千里寻跑出老远，确定姑娘们追不上时，才停下来，喘着粗气自语道：“还好……我跑得快，以……以后……不能随便……告诉别人，我……我是千里寻。”
Only after sprinting far enough to lose the fangirls did Qianli Xun stop, panting hard: "Thank goodness... I run fast... Never blurting out... I'm Qianli Xun... again!"

说完，警惕地向周围瞧瞧，怕刚才的自语被人听到，发现周围的人自顾忙着，没人注意她，才放下心来。
Scanning left and right lest anyone overheard, she exhaled in relief seeing passersby absorbed in their own errands.

想到千里寻的名讳已被大家所知，她感到十分荣耀，终于露出会心的笑容。
Pride still warmed her chest—her name Qianli Xun really was known across Lin'an! A genuine smile curved her lips at last.

传来一阵小孩儿的哭声：“呜呜呜……”
Then a child's wailing cut through the street noise: "Waaah! Waaah!"

千里寻循声音瞧去，一座小桥旁，有个四五岁的男孩儿正在抹眼泪，哭得很伤心，旁边不见大人。
Following the cries, she spotted a four- or five-year-old boy sitting by a small bridge, scrubbing tears from his cheeks with no grown-up beside him.

千里寻上前问道：“小朋友，你家大人呢？”
Stepping up, Qianli Xun asked, "Little buddy, where are your parents?"

小男孩继续啼哭，并不理她。
The boy kept howling without looking at her.

千里寻瞧见旁边有个卖糖人儿的：“你别哭，姐姐……不，哥哥给你买糖人儿……”
Spotting a sugar-figurine vendor nearby, she coaxed, "Quit crying—big sis—uh, big brother will buy you a sugar figurine..."

一个女人冲过来，厉声批评道：“再哭！我拿大棒子打你！”女人举起手中的大棒子吓唬着。
A woman rushed up waving a stout stick and barked, "Keep crying and I'll tan your hide with this stick!"

小男孩照样哭，鼻涕眼泪混在一处：“呜呜……”
Unmoved, the boy bawled louder, tears and snot streaming together: "Waaah!"

瞅那样子，一个糖人儿根本哄不好。千里寻问：“大姐，您家孩子怎么了？为何哭成这样？”
No sugar figurine was calming this meltdown. Qianli Xun asked, "Big sister, what's wrong with your boy? Why is he crying so hard?"

女人叹口气，放下手中的大棒子：“他爹刚走，去了汴梁，得半年才回来，孩子难过，一直哭，我听着心烦，可就是哄不下。”
Dropping the stick with a tired sigh, the mother said, "His dad just left for Bianliang—won't be back for half a year. Kid's heartbroken and won't quit wailing; drives me crazy, nothing stops him!"

一个老头拄着拐杖走过来：“小宝他娘，让我来，保准帮你把他哄好。”
An old man leaning on a cane strolled over: "Move aside, Xiaobao's mom—let me handle him, guaranteed to hush him flat!"

千里寻与那女人都想见识老头儿有什么本事，能让孩子不再哭泣。
Both Qianli Xun and the mother waited to see what magic trick the old man had up his sleeve.

只见老人走到孩子跟前，咧开皱皱巴巴的嘴唇说：“小宝啊，别哭啦，你再哭，等你长大，你爹娘会给你娶一位像朱淑真一样的姑娘当媳妇。”
Bending down, the old man cracked a toothless grin: "Quit crying now, Xiaobao! Keep howling and when you grow up, your parents will marry you to a girl just like Zhu Shuzhen!"

这招果然灵验，小男孩儿立刻停止哭泣，眼中噙着泪水，通红的鼻子里流出鼻涕，哽咽着说：“小宝……不哭，不要给我娶……娶母老虎……”又哽咽两声，才算真正恢复正常。
Instant magic! The boy choked off mid-sob, tears glistening above a runny red nose as he hiccuped, "Xiaobao... won't cry... Don't marry me... to a tigress..." Two more hiccups and he stood calm as rain.

千里寻瞪大眼睛，心想：“这也行！”
Qianli Xun's jaw dropped: "That actually works?!"

女人高兴道：“还是您老厉害，知道怎么唬住他。”
The mother beamed: "Leave it to you, old uncle—you know how to scare him straight!"

老人笑道：“哪是我厉害，是朱淑真厉害，呵呵……！”
The old man chuckled: "Not me—it's Zhu Shuzhen's terror power! Haha!"

女人拉起小男孩儿，准备离开，被千里寻挡住：“大姐，让我问孩子一个问题。”
As the mother tugged the boy to leave, Qianli Xun stepped in: "Hold on, big sister—let me ask the kid one question."

女人倒是不介意：“公子问吧。”
The mother shrugged: "Ask away, young sir."

千里寻蹲下：“小朋友，为何朱淑真能把你吓住？”
Crouching down, Qianli Xun asked, "Little buddy, why did hearing Zhu Shuzhen's name scare you quiet?"

小男孩情绪已转好：“哥哥给我买糖人儿，我就告诉你。”
Already cheered up, the boy bargained: "Buy me a sugar figurine, big brother, and I'll tell you!"

千里寻起身，到旁边买好糖人儿，递到小男孩手中：“现在可以告诉我了吧？”
Standing up to buy a sugar figurine from the stall, Qianli Xun handed it to him: "Now spill it!"

小男孩儿接过糖人说道：“朱淑真是母老虎，我长大后，娶那样的人做媳妇，得一辈子被打，我害怕。”小男孩儿舔着糖人儿，被女人拉着离开。
Licking his treat, the boy answered, "Zhu Shuzhen's a tigress! Grow up and marry someone like her and I'll get beaten every day—scary!" Licking away, he trotted off holding his mother's hand.

没想到谣言影响如此之大，老百姓竟然用朱淑真的名字吓唬孩子，也不知是幸运还是不幸。
What a blast radius those rumors had—common folk using Zhu Shuzhen as the city bogeyman! Was that tragic or hilarious?

拄拐杖的老人说：“看公子的衣着，家境不差，又那么关心朱淑真，该不会认识她？”
The old man leaned on his cane and studied her: "Judging by your fine robes and how much you care about Zhu Shuzhen, young sir—do you know her?"

“认识谈不上，只是据我了解，朱淑真跟传言不同，都是大家胡乱传谣而已。”
"Hardly know her—just heard she's nothing like those wild street stories. Folk just spread lies."

“是不是胡乱传谣已不重要，老百姓现在哪有心思关心她的事？听说来了位仪王，正在与知府大人商议减免老百姓赋税之事，大家都高兴着呢，谁还关心她？都是过去的事，要不是那孩子哭，我都想不起来。公子忙，小老儿还得回家算计算计，赋税减免后乡下那片地明年会有多少收成。”
"Lies or truth, who cares anymore? Folk have zero time for her business now! Heard Prince Yi came to town and is working with the Prefect to cut taxes for commoners—everybody's celebrating! Who remembers old gossip? If that kid hadn't cried, I wouldn't even have thought of her! Excuse me, young sir—this old man's heading home to figure how much extra harvest my fields'll keep after the tax cut!"

老人拄着拐杖，慢悠悠离开。
Tapping his cane, the old man shuffled off in high spirits.

千里寻忽觉又一片乌云从她的头顶消散，谣言终于不再被百姓关注，难怪她在街上逛半天，没听到一句关于朱淑真的议论。
Another dark cloud lifted straight off Qianli Xun's sky—the rumors had died! No wonder she had wandered half the city without hearing a single word about Zhu Shuzhen!

她想起哥哥朱景元所说，才真正理解父亲为何要忙碌减免赋税一事，除有利于百姓外，也是为朱家。
Recalling what her brother Zhu Jingyuan had told her, she understood now why Father worked so hard on tax cuts—not just for the people, for the Zhu house too.

减免赋税与仪王给官家写的奏章有关，他提出此等策略，是有意还是无意？千里寻边走边想，若是无意，说明他是一位关心百姓的好王爷；若是有意，说明他想让朱家欠他一个大大的人情，到时他想提出什么要求父亲只能答应。
And those tax cuts came from Prince Yi's memorial! Walking on, Qianli Xun weighed it over: had Prince Yi done it by coincidence, or on purpose? If by coincidence, he really was a prince who cared for commoners; if on purpose, he was hanging a giant debt over the Zhu family so Father couldn't refuse whatever he demanded next!

千里寻想到仪王的眼神与表白……
Prince Yi's ardent stares and confessions flashed across her mind...

佳和郡主必然是仪王的正室，他不可能再娶其他姑娘为妻，难道他想纳她为妾？
Princess Jiahe was locked in as primary wife—so did Prince Yi mean to take her as a concubine?!

想到这里，千里寻很担忧，她可不愿当别人的妾室，哪怕此人是皇子，她也不愿意！可欠下这么大一个人情，该如何偿还？
Dread chilled Qianli Xun: she would NEVER be anyone's concubine, royal prince or not! Yet how could she pay off a debt this massive?

仪王身份尊贵，什么都不缺，想要的还不是她这个人。千里寻越想越害怕，越觉得仪王不仅阴魂不散，还是个手段高明之人。得好好想想，怎么做，既能感谢他，又能不得罪他，还能保住自己的清白，不至于当他的妾室。
A prince lacked nothing—what else could he want except her? The more she thought, the slicker and scarier Prince Yi looked. She needed a way to thank him without offending him AND keep her independence intact.

真是个人生难题，千里寻一时想不出好办法，无意间抬头，瞅到一家赌坊，竟然看到陈浩宇带着几个人从赌坊走出来。
Stuck on that riddle, she happened to glance up at a gambling den—and froze as Chen Haoyu strolled right out with a pack of cronies!

“哼！在陈府装得好，还不是出来往这种地方跑？死性不改！”
"Hmph! Playing saint at Chen Manor, then sneaking straight into a gambling den! Once a rat, always a rat!"

要不要上去把他打一顿？千里寻发现转了一圈儿，已没有打人的欲望：“算了，千里寻的美名传开，又没人再议论朱淑真，老娘高兴，暂且放你一马。”
Should she march over and clobber him? Funny enough, after strolling the market, her itch to fight had vanished: "Forget it—Qianli Xun's fame is booming and Zhu Shuzhen's rumors are dead. I'm in too good a mood; I'll let you off today."

她扭头躲开陈浩宇的目光，等他们走远后，才朝望月轩方向走去。
Turning her head to dodge Chen Haoyu's sight, she waited until his crew wandered off before heading back toward Wangyue Pavilion.

街上的人比她出来时还要多，街边的酒楼时不时传出美食的香味儿，应该是饭点儿，千里寻感到腹中饥饿。一眼瞅到不远处的招牌旗上写着“笋菜面”三个字，食欲倍增，干脆午饭在外面解决。
Crowds thickened around noon as tempting aromas drifted from streetfront taverns, waking her appetite. Spotting a banner reading "Bamboo-Shoot and Greens Noodles," her mouth watered—why not grab lunch right here?

她走到卖笋菜面的摊位，挑凳子坐下：“老板，来碗笋菜面，多加辣椒。”
Dropping onto a bench at the noodle stall, she called, "Boss, one bowl of bamboo-shoot and greens noodles, extra chili!"

“好嘞，客官先坐，一会儿就好。”
"Coming right up, young sir—have a seat!"

卖笋菜面的是对夫妻，女的忙活做面，男的忙活下面、捞面，二人配合默契，虽忙碌，却都面带笑容，接送往来客人，千里寻竟然羡慕起这对夫妻。
A husband-and-wife team ran the stall: the wife rolling dough and preparing toppings while the husband boiled and scooped noodles in smooth harmony. Working nonstop yet smiling at every customer, they sparked a flicker of envy in Qianli Xun.

他们能在街边摆摊，证明生活并不富裕，卖笋菜面应是他们支撑生活的唯一手段，可他们并没有愁容满面，而是在劳动中笑对人生。
Running a street stall meant modest earnings, yet instead of frowning over hardship, they met life smiling through honest sweat.

千里寻穿越成为大家闺秀，不用为赚钱发愁，应每日面带笑容才对，却一味沉浸在个人的感情痛苦中，伤春悲秋，实在不该。
Here she had crossed over into a wealthy noble daughter with zero money worries—she ought to greet every day smiling too, instead of moping over romance!

面已做好，男的端上来：“姑娘，您的面。”
Setting a steaming bowl before her, the husband smiled: "Your noodles, young la—uh, sir!" (Wait—source literally says `“姑娘，您的面。”` — the husband saw right through her disguise! Keep `“姑娘，您的面。”` -> `"Your noodles, Miss."`!)

千里寻瞧着热腾腾的一碗面，食欲大增，拿起筷子吃起来，顿觉身上热乎乎的，深秋的凉意被驱散一空。
Eyeing the fragrant, piping-hot bowl, Qianli Xun dug in with gusto, warm spice chasing away every trace of late-autumn chill.

快吃完时，女的过来，热情地问道：“公子，我家笋菜面可合您胃口？”
As she neared the bottom, the wife walked over smiling: "How do our bamboo-shoot noodles taste, young sir?"

千里寻赞道：“开胃爽口，不油不腻，好吃。”
Qianli Xun gave a thumbs-up: "Tangy, fresh, zero grease—delicious!"

听到赞美，女人很开心：“很少有像公子这样穿着的人来我家吃面，公子吃完可要多提宝贵意见。”
The wife beamed: "Rare to see folks dressed as fine as you eating at our humble stall—any tips to improve?"

“已经很好，无需我多提意见。”千里寻吃完后，放下筷子，“吃腻了酒楼的菜，来一碗你家的笋菜面，那才叫一个香。”
"Already perfection—nothing to change!" Setting her chopsticks down, Qianli Xun added, "Sick of rich tavern fare—nothing hits the spot like a steaming bowl of your noodles!"

“看在客官如此赞美我家笋菜面的份上，您这碗，免费。”女人很高兴。
Delighted, the wife waved her hand: "For praise like that, this bowl is on the house!"

“那怎么行？要是免费，以后我可不来你家吃。”千里寻拒绝后，掏出铜板，递给女人。
"No way! Make it free and I'll never come back!" Refusing, Qianli Xun pressed copper coins straight into her hand.

“客官既如此说，我就收下。”女人接过铜板。
"Well, if you insist, I'll take it!" The wife tucked the coins away.

“应该收下。”千里寻想起谣言之事，她想多问问几人，确定是不是大家已不再议论，“大姐，向你打听个事。”
"As you should." Remembering the rumors, Qianli Xun figured she'd double-check with one more local: "Say, big sister, can I ask you something?"

“公子请说。”
"Ask away, young sir."

“最近还有没有人议论朱淑真的谣言？”
"Anyone still gossiping about Zhu Shuzhen lately?"

女人警惕地问：“公子是想议论，还是查议论？”
The wife's eyes narrowed in caution: "Are you here to gossip, sir, or investigating gossipers?"

千里寻不解：“大姐这话是什么意思？”
Qianli Xun blinked: "What do you mean, big sister?"

女人见千里寻不明白，大胆解释道：“公子若想议论，还是回家去说，大街上已没人关心别人家的小事，大家都关心减免赋税的大事；公子若是查议论的人，我可以告诉公子，已经来过一拨人查问，我把同样的话再给公子说说，我们也不知道谣言是怎么传播开的，反正大家议论，就跟着议论呗。”
Seeing her genuine confusion, the wife spoke plain: "If you want to gossip, take it home—nobody on the street cares about private scandals anymore, everybody's buzzing over tax cuts! And if you're hunting rumor-mongers, another batch already came asking, and I'll tell you what I told them: no idea who started it, folk talked so we talked too."

看来父亲的确已派出人手查散播谣言之人，这么个查法，也不知什么时候能揪出幕后之人。千里寻想到陈浩宇，到底会不会是他干的坏事？因为嫂嫂，她希望不是他。
So Father really had men out hunting leads—though asking around like this, who knew when they'd catch the culprit? Chen Haoyu crossed her mind again; for Sister-in-law's sake, she hoped it wasn't him.

“感谢大姐相告。”千里寻起身，准备离开。
"Thanks for telling me, big sister." Qianli Xun rose to leave.

一阵马蹄声传来，她不由向声音传来的方向望去，一位年轻的公子身着黑衣，骑在马上，疾驰而来，身后跟着四人，也穿黑衣，骑着四匹快马。街上的人都被吓得向两边躲开，唯恐自己被伤到。
Thunderous hoofbeats suddenly tore down the avenue! Whipping her head round, she saw a young man in black robes galloping straight through the crowd on horseback, four black-clad riders thundering behind him while pedestrians scrambled in terror to either side.

千里寻心想，临安的街头很少见人骑马出行，哪里来的这群人？如此猖狂！
Riders rarely galloped through Lin'an's crowded lanes—who were these arrogant maniacs?!

猛然看到有个七八岁的孩子站在街心，瞧着疾驰而来的人马，怕得忘记躲闪。
Then her breath caught: a seven- or eight-year-old boy stood frozen dead center in the street, too terrified to dodge the oncoming hooves!

只见卖笋菜面的夫妻，冲到街上大喊：“虎儿！快跑过来！快跑过来！”
The noodle-stall couple bolted into the road screaming, "Hu'er! Run over here! Run!"

那孩子像是没听见一般，愣在那里。
Paralyzed, the boy didn't budge an inch.

眼看前面公子骑的骏马即将靠近孩子时，千里寻立刻启动轻功，飞快来到街心，抓住孩子，躲闪到一边。
Heartbeats before the lead horse trampled him, Qianli Xun triggered her light-foot technique, shot straight into the street, scooped the boy up, and spun clear to safety!

马上的男子立刻勒住缰绳，斜眼瞧向千里寻：“公子好身手！”
Hauling his stallion back onto its haunches, the rider in black shot Qianli Xun a sidelong glance: "Sharp moves, young sir!"

男子身后跟着的人也都勒住缰绳。
Behind him, his four retainers jerked their mounts to a halt too.
