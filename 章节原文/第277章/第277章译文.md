## Chapter 277: Prince Yi Steps In to Try the Case

Blocking Chun Jinmo's path with an outstretched arm, Qianli Xun spoke briskly. "Don't worry—no fists needed for your kind, just plain sense. Nothing in this world is perfect, Madam Jiang. You're gorgeous, stuck in a brothel, yet still lucked out better than most—dallying with Haoyu, then landing primary wife to Master Jiang. As for me, sure my husband's past dragged headaches home, yet I've got a wonderful grandmother, kind in-laws, and family who back me—and right now Haoyu treats me with his whole heart. Who cares about tomorrow's guesses? Nobody knows the future—not even you. Meanwhile, you call her 'little sister' yet bolt the second the yamen's called so you don't get burned—pretty fake sisterhood!"

Hit dead center on her hypocrisy, Chun Jinmo shrieked "Liar!" before Yao Qingyan snapped at her to clear out before the Investigator hauled *her* in too. Feeding gullible Jinlan one last empty pledge to file an imperial petition for her if needed, Chun Jinmo hurried off.

Back at the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen had just heard Kujue's joyful report—he'd picked a quiet northern grove beside a waterfall for the bamboo cottage—and dispatched Bai Feng with silver to oversee construction (letting him swing by to see Kuqiao first) when Han Gu announced Chen Haodong requesting a house-call investigation over Second Young Master's scandal.

Worried for Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen swept Han Gu, Wen Xin, constables, Bai Feng, and Kujue straight over to Chen Manor's reception hall.

Spotting Qianli Xun's quiet sorrow the instant he entered made Shen Jianchen ache—yet he forced his gaze away, taking the Investigator's seat while Bai Feng slipped Kuqiao out the back door to greet her brother Kujue.

Kneeling side by side with Jinlan still trussed, Haoyu gave a blunt account: last spring he spotted Jinlan washing clothes by a creek, had thugs lock her in the Chen country villa, got summoned back to town by buddies before nightfall, and found her gone the next day—never laid a finger on her.

Jinlan sobbed back: tied up in the dark room, a man unlocked the door after nightfall, raped her in pitch blackness, knocked her out, and had her bagged and sold to a Yangzhou brothel—who else could it be besides Chen Haoyu?!

"If it was pitch black, how are you sure it was him?" Shen Jianchen pressed.

"Who else kidnapped me?!"

When Jinlan tried headbutting Haoyu, Wen Xin blocked her with his scabbard. Seeing Qianli Xun's pained silence, Shen Jianchen's voice turned stern, pressing Haoyu for alibi witnesses. Haoyu named Scarface and the young gentlemen who called him back to town.

Deciding the case needed formal court interrogation, Shen Jianchen remanded both Haoyu and Jinlan to the Judicial Office for tomorrow's hearing.

As constables marched them out, Chun Jinlan shot one quick, instinctive glance straight at Chen Haodong—who met her eyes without flinching.

Catching that fleeting exchange sharpened Shen Jianchen's instincts at once: Eldest Young Master Chen was knee-deep in this. Pushing past Qianli Xun without daring pause to comfort her—too many eyes watching—he vowed silently to dig out the truth for her sake.
