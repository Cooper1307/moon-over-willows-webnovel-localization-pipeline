## Chapter 277: Prince Yi Takes the Case

Heart sore and furious with Chen Haoyu, Qianli Xun had meant to hold her tongue—yet Chun Jinmo had pushed too far. Not only had she dragged a troublemaker in to wreck the Chens’ peace, she had honeyed her up as “Sister” only to dump her the second the law was called. Qianli Xun despised snakes like that. Had Chun Jinmo slunk off quiet, she would have let her go; since she chose to run her mouth, Qianli Xun wasn’t letting her walk away clean.

Chun Jinmo kept strolling ahead with her maid as if deaf. Qianli Xun strode ahead and planted herself right in her path. “Want out? You go through me first.”

Forced to halt, Chun Jinmo sneered, “What, going to hit me with those fists?”

“For your kind, I don’t need fists. My tongue is plenty.”

“The great Poetess of Jiangnan stooping to curse in public? People will laugh.” Trying to sidestep past her before getting shredded in front of everyone, Chun Jinmo bumped right into Qianli Xun’s outstretched arm.

“Madam Jiang is short on common sense, so let me teach you a little before you go.”

Cornered, Chun Jinmo raised her chin. “What grand lesson haven’t I heard that I need you preaching to me?”

“Not enough, clearly—or you’d know nothing in this world is ever perfect.” Shaking off her gloom, Qianli Xun spoke clear and steady. “Take yourself, Madam Jiang: pretty face, graceful figure, trapped in a brothel—yet still luckier than most courtesans. You caught Chen Haoyu’s eye, and then Master Jiang married you as his primary wife.”

Hearing his name stung Chen Haoyu; back in Chunxiang Brothel he had only chased Chun Jinmo’s looks and maidenhood. How foolish he had been—if regret medicine existed, he’d swallow a whole bottle.

Her words gave Chun Jinmo a flicker of pride; she was luckier than most, except she hadn’t ended up with Young Master Chen. She glanced at him—his eyes were glued to his wife alone.

“I married into the Chen family,” Qianli Xun went on. “Sure, my husband wasn’t much to brag about and stirred up piles of trouble, yet I’ve got a wonderful grandmother, kind parents-in-law, and elders who care for me—more than enough for one lifetime. As for Chen Haoyu, whatever he does is his business. Even if his change of heart only lasts a spell, so what? Right here and now, he’s sincere with me. Who can predict the future? Not even Madam Jiang can guarantee what tomorrow brings.”

Pain pulsed in his arm, yet Chen Haoyu spoke loud before everyone. “Rest easy, Wife—my heart for you will never change.”

A public vow couldn’t lift Qianli Xun’s mood. With a wry smile, she pressed on. “So every word you spat just now means nothing—and my household affairs aren’t for outsiders to fret over. If you love meddling so much, why bolt instead of staying to defend your ‘sister’ when the officials arrive? Scared the trouble will splash onto you, aren’t you? Makes that sweet ‘Sister’ sound mighty fake.”

Already bruised by Chen Haoyu’s vow and now stripped bare in front of everyone, Chun Jinmo flushed purple and barked, “Lies!”

Her point made, Qianli Xun stepped aside and returned to the pavilion.

Yao Qingyan snapped, “Get out already—stick around and the Judicial Investigator might haul you in for questioning too!”

Outnumbered on Chen turf, Chun Jinmo swallowed her rage and turned to Chun Jinlan. “Don’t fall for her tricks, Sister—she’s trying to drive a wedge between us. You know what I’ve got lined up. If the yamen sides with Chen Haoyu, I’ll spend every last copper appealing all the way to the throne for you!”

Touched, Chun Jinlan said, “I know your heart, Sister. Go on—no sense staying here to be insulted.”

*Gullible fool bought every fake word—no wonder Chen Haoyu’s crowd sold her off and Chen Haodong played her like a fiddle.*

“Good—then I’ll leave with an easy mind.” Chun Jinmo hurried out with her maid without looking back.

Meanwhile, at the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen was poring over dossiers when Kujue arrived from Zhuli Village. Having picked out the site and hired builders for the bamboo cottage, he had come into town first thing to report.

He described the plot on the north edge of the bamboo grove—few villagers around, secluded and quiet, with a waterfall right in view once the cottage doors swung open.

Pleased, Shen Jianchen told Bai Feng to take silver and accompany Kujue back to Zhuli Village to oversee construction, freeing him from constant attendance.

Thrilled at the chance to meet Kuqiao’s parents and build ties with them, Bai Feng missed Kuqiao fiercely after days apart and seized his chance. “Your Highness, Kujue made it all the way into town—at least let me take him to see Kuqiao once!”

Shen Jianchen was about to nod when Han Gu stepped in. “Your Highness, Eldest Young Master Chen is outside reporting a case at Chen Manor that requires the Judicial Office in person.”

“Where is he?”

“Waiting outside.”

“Show him in.”

Chen Haodong had been at the tea shop when the manor’s runner fetched him. Delighted as he was, dragging the Judicial Office in risked exposing himself—until he worked out a foolproof angle on the ride over.

Stepping into the hall, Chen Haodong bowed low. “This commoner pays respects to Your Highness, Prince Yi.”

Fearing it touched Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen asked at once, “What happened at Chen Manor?”

“It concerns my second brother, Your Highness. He got entangled with a girl in the past who insists he defiled her and sold her to a brothel. Second Brother denies it flat-out. Unable to settle it, my family sent me to request Your Highness come to the manor.”

Sure enough, it touched her. Shen Jianchen rose at once. “Clerk Han, fetch Chief Constable Wen and a squad of constables—we’re riding to Chen Manor.”

Bai Feng panicked. “What about Kujue, Your Highness?”

“Bring him along—we’re headed to the same place!” Striding out, Shen Jianchen led the way while Bai Feng grabbed Kujue and trotted happily behind.

At Chen Manor, all three Chen Masters had been summoned home, gathered with the family in the reception hall. Everyone rose to bow as Prince Yi entered, even Old Madam Xia leaning on a maid.

Chen Jianglin spoke first. “An ugly scandal broke out at home, and hauling the girl to the Judicial Office seemed unseemly, so we troubled Your Highness to make the trip.”

Shen Jianchen’s gaze swept the room and found Qianli Xun in an instant. One glance told him she hurt more than anyone there.

*Hurting over Chen Haoyu—has she fallen for him?* He had no right to ache over that now. For her sake, he would handle this case with utmost care. Warning himself not to linger, he pulled his gaze away before anyone noticed.

“No trouble, Master Chen. As Judicial Investigator, handling cases on-site is routine. Please sit—let me hear the facts before making any ruling.”

He took the main seat, Han Gu sat at a desk to transcribe, Wen Xin stood at his elbow, and the constables guarded the doors.

The instant the prince sat, Bai Feng slipped beside Kuqiao and murmured, “Someone’s outside looking for you.”

Hesitating, Kuqiao whispered to her miss, “Bai Feng says someone’s outside for me. May I step out a moment?”

Kuqiao wasn’t needed here, and she hadn’t seen Bai Feng in days. Qianli Xun nodded and whispered back, “Constables are at the front—slip out the back door.” Kuqiao agreed and slipped out with Bai Feng.

Once the prince settled, Wen Xin barked, “Principals of this case, step forward and kneel to state the facts!”

Chen Haoyu rose first and knelt; maids guided the bound Chun Jinlan forward to kneel beside him.

“Why is she bound?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“After she stabbed Haoyu,” Chen Jianglin replied, “my mother and the women feared she’d hurt someone else and tied her up.”

“Leave her bound, then.” Glancing at Chen Haoyu’s arm—blood had soaked through the bandage, no light wound—Shen Jianchen got down to specifics. “Chen Haoyu, tell me in full how you know this woman.”

Chen Haoyu answered straight. “Last spring, I went out to the countryside for fun and saw her washing clothes by a creek. Struck by her looks, I had my men abduct her and lock her in our country residence, meaning to go back that night... except a few friends sent word inviting me back into town to drink. So I headed back to the city. When I returned to the country house the next day, she had already escaped. I never touched a hair on her head.”

Chun Jinlan cried out, “Don’t believe his one-sided lies, Your Lordship! He ruined me and sold me to a Yangzhou brothel—he destroyed my honor and drove me from home!”

“What is your name?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“This humble woman is called Chun Jinlan now.”

“Tell me everything that happened after you were locked up.”

“I was washing clothes by the creek when someone threw a sack over my head. Strong men hauled me into a carriage and tied me up. No idea how far we rode before they pulled the sack off inside a fine room. Young Master Chen walked in, told me to stay put till he came back that night, and locked the door on his way out. Tied hand and foot, terrified, I curled up on the bed. After dark, in pitch blackness where I couldn’t see a thing, the lock clicked open, someone came in, and forced himself on me—it had to be Chen Haoyu!”

Shen Jianchen frowned. “You just said it was pitch black and you couldn’t see a thing. How can you be sure it was Chen Haoyu?”

“Who else could it be? Why else did he abduct me? Worst of all, afterward he knocked me out cold—when I woke up, I was stuffed inside a sack in a moving carriage, and when they let me out I was in a Yangzhou brothel!”

“Liar!” Chen Haoyu snapped. “You escaped and now you’re framing me!”

Furious, Chun Jinlan scrambled up despite her ropes and lunged to ram into him. Wen Xin flashed forward, blocking her with his scabbard. “Stay on your knees! If you’ve been wronged, His Lordship will see justice done.”

Cowardly at the steel, she sank back down. “All I beg is for Your Lordship to make Chen Haoyu take me as his concubine—otherwise I’ll never give him peace!”

Shen Jianchen glanced again at Qianli Xun. Eyes cast down, face composed, yet the misery rolling off her stabbed right through him. His tone hardened despite himself. “Chen Haoyu!”

Everyone jumped, thinking the prince was about to erupt. Startled, Chen Haoyu answered quick, “Present, Your Lordship.”

Softening his edge, Shen Jianchen asked, “You say you never touched her or sold her. Do you have witnesses?”

“Yes! Scarface and the rest of my crowd can vouch for me, and so can the young masters who invited me back to town that night!”

Sensing something murky underneath, Shen Jianchen turned to Chen Jianglin. “Master Chen, we’ll stop here for today. I’m taking both of them back to the Judicial Office and summoning the witnesses tomorrow for a full hearing.”

“Whatever Your Highness decides.”

Constables filed in. “On your feet,” Wen Xin ordered. “You’re staying at the Judicial Office a few days.”

Chen Haoyu and Chun Jinlan rose and walked out. Passing Chen Haodong, Chun Jinlan shot him a fleeting glance.

Chen Haodong met her eyes without flinching. *Picked the right girl to make a scene—let’s see how Second Brother wriggles out of this! Still, to keep my own neck clean, I’d better go see Scarface tonight.*

That brief exchange of looks did not escape Shen Jianchen’s sharp eyes. Suspicion sparked at once: was Eldest Young Master Chen mixed up in this? Suspicions needed proof, though.

Rising, Shen Jianchen led Han Gu and Wen Xin out as the Chens stood to bow. “Safe journey, Your Highness!”

Passing right by Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen longed to stop and offer a word of comfort, yet walked straight past. Singling her out would only bring her grief; the best thing he could do was clean up this mess for her.
