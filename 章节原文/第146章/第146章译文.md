## Chapter 146: Old Madam Xia Decides to Drag It Out

After Qinggua changed his dressings, Chen Haoyu insisted on heading straight back to Chen Manor; Qinggua hitched the carriage and drove him home.

Still sore from healing bruises, Chen Haoyu shed his usual swagger as he walked with care toward his parents' courtyard—already picturing the sweet domestic scene waiting inside.

Truth be told, Chen Haoyu envied his parents' marriage more than anything, wishing he too might meet one woman to grow old beside—only for Heaven to stick him with Zhu Shuzhen instead!

Sure enough, stepping inside, he found Father and Mother engrossed in a chess game, having eyes only for each other.

Waving a maid silent before she could announce him, Chen Haoyu stood watching with quiet envy—sore bruises making him uncommonly mellow today.

"Every single game you let me win—takes all the fun out!" Yao Qingyan teased, beaming.

Making another generous move, Chen Jianglin smiled: "I don't yield an inch to anyone in this world—only to my wife."

"Well, if you insist, I won't hold back—don't pout when you lose!"

"When have I ever been petty or mad at my wife? Once we finish this game, I'm taking you shopping! Our Chen tailor shop hired a master tailor who's already made two new outfits for you, and our cosmetics shop got fresh stock—packed and waiting for us to pick up."

"Why not have a servant deliver them instead of trekking out ourselves?"

"Where's the fun in delivery? I want to take my wife out for fresh air and show you around the shops myself!"

Father had to be the slickest wife-whisperer alive, Chen Haoyu thought—would he ever meet a woman he wanted to spoil like that?

Dropping her winning stone, Yao Qingyan cheered: "I win!"

Chen Jianglin smiled warm: "Your smile is my win."

Seeing the match conclude, Chen Haoyu stepped forward: "Father, Mother—I need to talk to you."

Glancing up, Chen Jianglin's face snapped stern: "Where did you vanish to carousing these past few days?!"

"Didn't go carousing—just stayed at our east-city house."

"Behaving yourself like that?!" Chen Jianglin pressed hard.

Yao Qingyan nudged him: "He's an engaged man now—don't talk to him like a little boy."

"Fair point, Wife." Softening at once, Chen Jianglin gestured: "Sit down and talk with your mother and me."

Chen Haoyu lowered himself onto a chair with care.

Yao Qingyan frowned: "Why do you look off today?"

He would die before admitting Prince Yi had pounded him flat!

"N-nothing... Heard Father and Mother betrothed me to Miss Zhu. We clash like mortal enemies—marrying her means daily hell! Please go break off this betrothal..."

Sending Qinggua outside first, Chen Jianglin exploded the second the door shut: "Nonsense! How do you break off a sealed betrothal—ruining the girl's name?! You pulled that dirty stunt wrecking her reputation—if you don't marry her, who will?! Blame your own reckless hide!"

Yao Qingyan added, "Miss Zhu is stunning and brilliant—marrying her is three lifetimes of luck! Treasure her and never mention breaking it off again."

With both parents shutting him down, panic spiked in Chen Haoyu: "Father! Mother! I don't want to marry a girl I don't love!"

Yao Qingyan chuckled: "Oh? Got a girl you love already? Bring her home for your father and me to inspect—if she's decent, take her as a concubine!"

Chen Haoyu shook his head: "Not yet. I just can't stand Zhu Shuzhen—we'll never last under one roof!"

Irritation crossed Chen Jianglin's brow: "Love doesn't matter! Your mother and I never even laid eyes on each other before our wedding, and look at us now! Go on—quit pestering us. This betrothal stays."

Seeing zero crack in his parents, he pivoted to Grandmother: "Enjoy your chess, Father and Mother—won't bother you anymore."

Noticing his stiff, ginger posture as he rose, Yao Qingyan asked in concern: "Are you hurt somewhere, Haoyu? Why are you moving with such care?"

A lightbulb flashed over Chen Haoyu's head! "I am hurt, Father and Mother—Zhu Shuzhen beat me! Found out I spread the rumors, lost her mind, and pounded me black and blue! Hid out at Yongning Residence healing so I wouldn't make you cry—ask Qinggua if you don't believe me!"

Jumping up, Yao Qingyan pulled his collar back to inspect his back—covered in fading purple and yellow bruises! "Heavens above! How did you get beaten like this?! Really by Miss Zhu?! How does a girl hit that hard?!"

Putting on a pitiful whimper, Chen Haoyu lied: "You don't know, Mother—she knows martial arts and hits like a hammer! Marry her and I'll be getting battered by my wife every single day!"

Skeptical, Yao Qingyan shook her head: "I've met Miss Zhu—slender and delicate, nothing like a fighter! Wenxin never mentioned martial arts either. Didn't you get in a street brawl and blame Miss Zhu just to wiggle out of marrying her?!"

Chen Jianglin snorted: "Bet my fortune on it! And even if Miss Zhu did beat you—bravo to her! Nobody in this house can handle you; a tough wife is just what you need to whip you straight!"

"Father—am I even your real son?!" With that bluff flopping too, panic rose higher.

Yao Qingyan called toward the door: "Qinggua!"

Stepping in at her call, Qinggua received orders: "Walk Young Master back to his room and rub our manor's finest bruise salve over his injuries."

"Right away!" Stepping up, Qinggua reached out to steady his master's arm.

Chen Haoyu shrugged him off: "Don't touch me—I can walk! Wait till I marry that shrew, though—I won't even be able to crawl out of bed!"

Once their son stomped out, worry lingered on Yao Qingyan's face: "Husband, should we ask Wenxin back to check? What if Miss Zhu really is violent and Haoyu suffers?"

Chen Jianglin patted her hand: "Relax, Wife. Look at that brat—lying through his teeth! Picked a fight somewhere else and pinned it on Miss Zhu to kill the betrothal."

Having suspected the same, Yao Qingyan nodded: "True... still, who on earth did he cross to get beaten that bad? I'll go look after him in his room and pry the truth out."

"Go ahead—just looking at that rascal spikes my temper. While you're busy, I'll go talk silk-shop expansion with Jiangshu." Walking his wife to the door, Chen Jianglin headed for his second brother's yard.

Right as Chen Haoyu reached his own courtyard gate, Old Madam Xia arrived tapping her cane, beaming ear to ear: "Heard you came home, Haoyu, so Grandmother rushed straight over! Seven or eight days away and you've lost weight!"

Stepping forward to steady her arm: "Come inside out of the cold, Grandmother."

Supporting her weight through the doorway pulled his sore shoulder, wringing a sharp hiss between his teeth: "Tss..."

Alarm flashed in Old Madam Xia's eyes: "What's wrong? Where does it hurt? Show Grandmother!"

Time to deploy Grandmother against the betrothal! Putting on his saddest puppy eyes: "Grandmother, do you love me most in the world?"

"Goes without saying!"

"I got beaten black and blue all over—seven whole days later and I'm still sore! That's how brutal it was!"

"What heartless villain touched my grandson?! Tell Grandmother—I'll have your father drag him here and tear him apart!"

Never daring to breathe Prince Yi's name, Chen Haoyu spun his lie: "Nobody else—your future granddaughter-in-law, Miss Zhu!"

Behind them, Qinggua muffled a snicker—Young Master would tell any whopper alive to dodge this wedding!

Baffled, Old Madam Xia frowned: "How could a big strapping lad like you get beaten by a young lady?"

"You don't know, Grandmother—she knows kung fu! Hits like iron; I couldn't even fight back!"

"Why on earth would she beat you?" Old Madam Xia still struggled to picture it.

Unwilling to mention the rumors in front of Qinggua and the maids: "Come inside and I'll tell you, Grandmother."

Catching his master's wink, Qinggua ushered both inside, held every maid outside, and shut the door firm.

Once Old Madam Xia sat, Chen Haoyu spun fast: "Zhu Shuzhen found out I spread rumors about her, went ballistic, dragged me into a dark shack a few days ago, and beat me half to death! Look for yourself, Grandmother..."

Parting his lapel, he revealed his purple-mottled chest, sending horror straight to Old Madam Xia's heart!

"Where does a girl get strength like that?! Beating my grandson like a criminal—how dare we bring her under this roof?! No way—I'm marching straight to talk to your parents!"

Just as Old Madam Xia rose, Yao Qingyan pushed the door open and hurried to steady her elbow: "What brings Mother over here?"

"Can't I visit my own grandson?!" Sitting back down, Old Madam Xia barked, "Good timing—go tell Jianglin Miss Zhu is out! Look what she did to my grandson!"

Yao Qingyan walked back to shut the door tight first—only a handful knew about Chen Haoyu spreading the rumors, and neither Zhus nor Chens wanted it spreading further.

Once the door stood closed, she returned to Old Madam Xia's side: "Like it or not, Mother, he has to marry her—Haoyu brought it on himself pulling that wicked stunt."

"Look at what she did to my boy! Bring her home and she'll flip Chen Manor upside down!" Old Madam Xia would not budge—Haoyu was the apple of her eye, untouchable!

Yao Qingyan knew explaining was useless; Old Madam Xia believed every fairy tale out of her favorite grandson's mouth.

"Wenxin praises Miss Zhu to the skies, Mother—she can't be half as wild as Haoyu claims!"

"Don't care! Look at my grandson's bruises—how could I rest easy?! If we can't break the betrothal, then drag it out! Until Miss Zhu tames that temper, no wedding!"

Chen Haoyu pleaded, "I'd still rather break it off, Grandmother—tell Father and Mother to cancel it with the Zhus, please?!"

Old Madam Xia patted his hand: "Grandmother wants to cancel it too, sweetie, yet canceling right after sealing it won't fly—your mother's right, you did mess up and owe amends. Plus she's the Prefect's daughter, not easy to dump overnight. Stalling is our best play right now."

Yao Qingyan fretted: "Stalling won't work either, Mother—New Year is right around the corner, barely two months until the wedding date! How do we stall?"

"Leave that to me—I'll have a plan ready when the time comes." Old Madam Xia looked downright smug.

"Dragging it out will only anger the Prefect, Mother—and we'll still end up marrying her anyway. Better wed on schedule and keep everyone smiling!"

"Quit lecturing me! Haoyu's no weakling, yet a girl beat him raw! One henpecked son letting his wife walk over him is plenty—I refuse to watch a granddaughter-in-law wrap my grandson around her little finger too!"

That jab froze Yao Qingyan silent—how could her mother-in-law keep preaching to her sons to cherish their wives, yet keep sniping that Jianglin spoiled her too much? A mother-in-law's mind was an unsolvable riddle.

"Whatever Mother decides."

After all that acting, scoring only a stalling tactic felt thin to Chen Haoyu, though nothing better existed. Letting out a sigh, he muttered: "Wish I had one more gorgeous older sister!"

Old Madam Xia blinked: "What for?"

"Then His Majesty could take her into the palace, making the Chens imperial kin! Wouldn't have to fear any Prefect—break any engagement we want, marry whoever we please! How awesome would that be?!"

Yao Qingyan tapped his forehead: "Quit talking crazy! Think being imperial relatives is paradise? The Zhus may not dare touch the Qiaos, yet bad blood is already brewed between them."

Tugging her son toward the inner bed: "Go lie down—sent Qinggua for our manor's best salve. Top-notch medicine sitting right at home and you go hiring street doctors!"

Walking inside, she called toward the door: "Qinggua! Get in here and put salve on Young Master!"

Pushing open the door, Qinggua bowed to Old Madam Xia and hurried in to tend his master.

A maid stepped in to escort Old Madam Xia back; satisfied from seeing her boy, she headed off to rest.
