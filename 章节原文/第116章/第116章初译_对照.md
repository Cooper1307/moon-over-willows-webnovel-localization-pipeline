第116章 裁缝铺后会文渊
## Chapter 116: Meeting Wenyuan After the Tailor Shop

离开茶肆，千里寻心情愈加沉重，大家如此议论她，乔家取消提亲实在正常不过，她与文渊该怎么办？
Leaving the teahouse with an even heavier heart, Qianli Xun saw why the Qiao family had canceled the proposal—with the city talking like this, what choice did she and Wenyuan have?

走着走着，看到一家裁缝铺，想到身上的男装实在不适合，不如现做两身，以备不时之需。
Walking on, she spotted a tailor shop. Her borrowed servant's outfit fit poorly; better have two proper sets of men's clothes tailored in case she needed them again.

她走进裁缝铺内，店主迎上来：“小哥是买成衣还是现做衣裳？”
Stepping inside, the shopkeeper greeted her: "Ready-made or custom-tailored, young sir?"

“现做。”
"Custom-tailored."

“男装在左边，小哥这边请。”
"Men's wear is on the left—right this way, sir."

千里寻毕竟是女子，不由自主瞧向右边，女装区的成衣的确不错。瞧着瞧着，无意中发现苗知瑜与一位闺秀正在挑衣裳。
Still a woman at heart, Qianli Xun glanced over to the right, where the women's ready-made robes looked lovely—and right there, browsing fabrics with a female friend, stood Miao Zhiyu!

还好没被她们注意到，千里寻赶紧扭过头，走到男装区。
Before they noticed her, Qianli Xun whipped her head away and ducked into the men's section.

过来一名伙计，负责量尺寸：“麻烦小哥把胳膊抬起来。”
An apprentice stepped up with a measuring tape: "Please raise your arms, young sir."

千里寻乖乖地抬起胳膊，背对着女装区，怕被苗知瑜发现。
Qianli Xun lifted her arms obediently, keeping her back turned to the women's side so Miao Zhiyu wouldn't spot her.

苗知瑜倒未注意男装区，与闺中密友边挑衣裳边聊天儿。
Unaware of anyone on the men's side, Miao Zhiyu chatted with her confidante while sorting through silks.

“知瑜，这件款式不错，很适合你。”
"This style is lovely, Zhiyu—suits you down to the ground."

“太过素雅，再看看别的。”
"Too plain. Let's look at others."

“你不是向来喜欢素雅？怎么突然喜好有所改变？”
"Didn't you always prefer understated pieces? Why the change in taste?"

“我想试试艳丽些的，也许更适合我。”
"Thought I'd try brighter colors—might suit me better."

闺中密友又说：“东方公子若组织诗会，你一定叫上我。”
Her friend added, "If Young Master Dongfang holds another poetry salon, you have to bring me along!"

“你又不懂诗词，去了也没意思。”
"You don't even care for poetry—you'd be bored."

“诗词我不感兴趣，我只想认识东方公子。”
"I don't care about poetry; I just want to meet Young Master Dongfang!"

苗知瑜叹口气：“唉！恐怕东方公子的诗会再也组织不起来。”
Miao Zhiyu let out a sigh. "I doubt Young Master Dongfang will hold any more salons."

“为何？”
"Why?"

“幽栖居士出这么大的事，他哪有心情？”
"With everything hitting Youqi Jushi, how could he be in the mood?"

“又为何？”
"Why would that stop him?"

“诗会没有幽栖居士，没有乔公子，举办了又有什么意思？”
"Without Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao, what's the point of holding a salon?"

“原来如此。说起幽栖居士，没想到她是那样的女子，真是人不可貌相。”
"Oh, right. Speaking of Youqi Jushi—who knew she was that kind of woman? Never judge a book by its cover!"

苗知瑜边挑衣裳边说：“你别跟他人瞎胡议论，幽栖居士可不是谣言中说的那种人。”
Browsing robes, Miao Zhiyu spoke up: "Don't repeat wild street talk. Youqi Jushi is nothing like those rumors claim."

听到这话，千里寻对苗知瑜生出好感，能在众人诋毁之时还替她说话，人品必然不错。
Warmth surged in Qianli Xun toward Miao Zhiyu—defending her while the whole city spat on her name showed real character.

伙计边量尺寸边疑惑：“明明是个女子，却要扮成男子，还买男装，真是奇怪。”
Taking measurements, the tailor's apprentice puzzled in silence: clearly a young woman, yet dressed as a man ordering men's robes—how odd!

尺寸已量完，伙计说：“小哥，尺寸已记下，您再挑挑款式。”
Finishing up, the apprentice said, "Got your measurements down, sir. Now pick out your styles."

千里寻不着急，装作认真挑选款式的样子，尽量避开与苗知瑜对视，却忍不住竖起耳朵听她们聊天儿。
Taking her time, Qianli Xun pretended to inspect patterns while keeping her back to Miao Zhiyu, ears pricked wide open.

闺中密友说：“好像你很了解她似的。”
The friend teased, "You talk like you know her inside out."

“我跟幽栖居士虽谈不上是闺中密友，可常常在诗会中碰面，对她的事再清楚不过。谣言说她是母老虎，实在过分！她参加诗会时，常常带伤而来，都是被林觉打的。后来，她虽变得厉害，可对朋友最温和不过。至于其余难听的话，更不符合实际。她和离前，每次来诗会，与东方公子他们相处最懂男女有别，行为从未逾矩。即使乔公子对她有情，她也从未有过任何……”
"Youqi Jushi and I aren't bosom friends, sure, yet we met at salon after salon, so I know her well. Calling her a tigress is outrageous! She used to show up to salons covered in bruises from Lin Jue's beatings. Later she grew tougher, yet she's always warm to friends. As for the rest of that filth, it's pure lies. Before her mutual divorce, she was scrupulous about proper distance around Young Master Dongfang and the others—never crossed a line once! Even when Young Master Qiao had feelings for her, she never..."

苗知瑜想起到朱家参加宴会时，那时幽栖居士还未和离，已与乔公子眉来眼去，她因此吃醋不已。
Miao Zhiyu stopped short, recalling the Zhu Manor banquet before the divorce when Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao had traded tender glances—sparking green jealousy inside her.

“怎么，难道幽栖居士还是有问题？”闺中密友见苗知瑜忽然停下，故意问。
"Well? So Youqi Jushi isn't spotless after all?" her friend prodded at the pause.

苗知瑜强调道：“她跟乔公子在一起是和离后的事，总之，外面的谣言都是诋毁。”
Miao Zhiyu insisted, "She and Young Master Qiao only got together after her divorce. Point is, those street rumors are all slander."

千里寻很想上前抱住苗知瑜，感谢她替她说话，瞧到自己身上的男装，还是忍住，继续装作挑选款式的样子，认真听着。
Qianli Xun almost marched over to hug Miao Zhiyu in thanks, then remembered her men's disguise and held back, keeping her ears glued to their chat.

闺中密友继续说：“知瑜，我估计也只有你替幽栖居士说话。你真善良，应了那句善有善报。我听说乔家又开始准备为乔公子选亲，你的机会来了。”
Her friend went on: "Zhiyu, you're probably the only soul left defending Youqi Jushi! Such a kind heart—and kindness brings good karma! Heard the Qiao family started bride selection for Young Master Qiao all over again—your chance has come!"

苗知瑜露出笑容，接着担忧道：“也不知乔公子现在可好？”
A smile curved Miao Zhiyu's lips before worry crept in: "I wonder how Young Master Qiao is holding up right now..."

闺中密友建议道：“别在这里瞎担心，不如找机会去见见他。”
Her friend urged, "Stop fretting here—find a chance to go see him!"

苗知瑜点头同意。
Miao Zhiyu nodded in agreement.

二人又挑选一会儿，终于选好衣裳。
After browsing a bit longer, they settled on their robes.

掌柜命人取下，包好后，将二位客人送出门。
The shopkeeper wrapped their purchases and saw both ladies out the door.

千里寻根本无心挑选，随便选两个款式，付完定金，走出裁缝铺。
With no heart left for shopping, Qianli Xun picked two styles at random, paid the deposit, and walked out.

在外面逛荡许久，也该回去，收拾收拾去见文渊，只有见到他，才知接下来的路该如何走。
Having wandered long enough, it was time to head back and get ready to meet Wenyuan—only face to face would they know what path lay ahead.

……
...

回到望月轩，千里寻换回女装，带上两个丫鬟乘坐马车，赶往神柳下面见乔文仙。
Back at Wangyue Pavilion, Qianli Xun changed back into women's robes, took both maids, and rode out to meet Qiao Wenxian beneath the Divine Willow.

夏果很开心，又能见到乔公子。
Xiaguo beamed at the thought of seeing Young Master Qiao again.

苦荞一直观察小姐，发生这么大的事，小姐还能够坦然面对，令她钦佩。小姐沉默不语，苦荞也保持沉默，心中担忧着小姐与乔公子的未来。
Kuqiao watched her mistress in quiet awe—facing such a storm with calm courage was no small thing. Seeing her mistress silent, Kuqiao stayed silent too, fretting over what lay ahead for Miss and Young Master Qiao.

乔文仙早已来到神柳旁，站在树下，向淑真必然出现的方向眺望，满脸期待。
Qiao Wenxian had arrived beneath the Divine Willow long before, staring down the path where Shuzhen was sure to appear, longing stamped across his face.

神柳的叶子已发黄，一阵秋风吹过，时不时落下枯黄的柳叶，掉落在乔文仙的身上。南双见状，上前将柳叶从公子身上取下，扔在草丛中。
The willow's leaves had turned yellow; gusts of autumn wind shook withered leaves down onto Qiao Wenxian's shoulders, and Nan Shuang kept stepping up to brush them off into the grass.

发黄的柳叶与正在发黄的草混合在一起，分不清哪个是柳叶，哪个是草叶。周围的植物逐渐失去生气，如同乔文仙的心情一样凄凉。
Yellow willow leaves mingled with yellowing grass until neither could be told apart. All around them, nature drained of life—matching the bleak chill in Qiao Wenxian's chest.

昨晚他没睡好，思索一晚上与淑真的未来，仍旧未思索出结果。他更担心淑真会就此舍他而去。
He had lain awake all night searching for a future with Shuzhen and finding none, terrified above all that she might walk away from him.

期待已久的身影终于出现在眼前，正缓慢向他走来，乔文仙凄凉的表情中露出些许喜色。
At last the figure he longed for appeared, walking up the path at an unhurried pace, bringing a spark of light to his desolate face.

他已等不及，快步走上前，一把将心爱的人抱入怀里……
Unable to wait another second, he strode forward and pulled the woman he loved straight into his arms...

苦荞拉起夏果朝南双走去。
Kuqiao tugged Xiaguo over to join Nan Shuang.

夏果很不情愿，又没有任何理由待在一旁，只能不舍地回头再瞧几眼乔公子，他看上去很憔悴，夏果一阵心疼。
Xiaguo dragged her feet, casting longing glances back over her shoulder at Young Master Qiao; seeing how haggard he looked made her heart ache.

被文渊抱住，千里寻也伸手抱住他，这一抱，她忍不住流下泪来：“文渊，我已在临安城臭名昭著，难道你不嫌弃我？”
Held tight in Wenyuan's arms, Qianli Xun wrapped her arms around him too, and tears spilled over at last: "Wenyuan, my name is dirt across all of Lin'an—don't you despise me?"

“我对你再清楚不过，谣言岂能影响我？”
"I know you better than anyone. How could rumors ever sway me?"

千里寻把文渊抱得更紧：“谢谢你信任我。”
Qianli Xun hugged him tighter. "Thank you for trusting me."

“以后不许对我说谢谢的话。”
"Never say thank you to me again."

“好，我不会再说。”千里寻沉默一会儿，终于问出一路思索的问题，“以后，我们……我们该怎么办？”
"All right, I won't." After a quiet beat, she asked the question she had turned over the whole way here: "What... what do we do from here?"

乔文仙没有立刻回答，他放开淑真，深情地盯着她的面庞：“你要等我，相信我，我会说服爹娘，让他们同意再次到朱家提亲。”
Qiao Wenxian did not answer at once. Letting her go, he gazed straight into her eyes with deep tenderness: "Wait for me. Trust me—I'll persuade my parents to propose to the Zhu family again."

千里寻垂下眼帘：“我当然相信你，可仪王告诉我，你的父亲宁可舍弃性命也不愿到朱府提亲。”
Qianli Xun dropped her gaze. "Of course I trust you. Only Prince Yi told me your father would sooner give up his life than propose to Zhu Manor."

“父亲可以舍弃性命，我也可以……”
"If Father can stake his life, so can I—"

“不！千万不要！”千里寻担心不已，“如若你这样，我宁可不要这段姻缘……”
"No! Never say that!" Panic gripped Qianli Xun. "If it comes to that, I'd rather give up this marriage..."

乔文仙用手捂住淑真的嘴巴，不愿她继续说下去：“别这么说，我答应你，会珍爱自己，你也要相信我，等着我。”
Qiao Wenxian pressed a hand over her mouth to stop her words: "Don't talk like that. I promise I'll take care of myself—just believe in me and wait for me."

“好，我等你，只要你坚持，我便坚持，你若放弃……”
"All right, I'll wait. As long as you hold on, I'll hold on. If you ever give up—"

“我不会放弃。”
"I'll never give up."

“我也不放弃。”
"Neither will I."

两人正沉浸着体会各自坚守的话语时，传来仪王的声音：“你们两个人信誓旦旦又有何用？”
Right as they drank in each other's vows, Prince Yi's voice sliced in: "What good do all your grand vows do?"

千里寻心想，说仪王没眼色，还真没眼色。她与文渊约会，他又跑来凑热闹。正要回怼两句，瞧到佳和郡主跟在一旁，才控制好情绪，上前拜道：“仪王，佳和郡主。”
Qianli Xun rolled her eyes—talk about zero tact! Intruding right on her date with Wenyuan! Ready to snap back, she spotted Princess Jiahe walking beside him, swallowed her temper, and stepped up to bow: "Prince Yi, Princess Jiahe."

乔梦容上前扶住幽栖居士：“我与哥哥前来，有没有打扰到你们？”
Qiao Mengrong hurried up to steady her: "Did Brother and I interrupt you two?"

千里寻心想，还用问？打扰得实在厉害！嘴上却说：“不打扰，不知郡主与仪王前来有何指教？”
"Need you ask? Huge interruption!" Qianli Xun raged inside, while smiling aloud: "Not at all. What brings Princess and Prince Yi out here?"

申简辰主动说：“没什么指教，我想出来走走，邀梦容陪我一起。谁知走着走着，来到此处。”
Shen Jianchen spoke up: "Nothing in particular. Felt like a walk and brought Mengrong along, and somehow our feet led us right here."

千里寻心里骂道：“我看你就是故意！把准了我不敢当众揭下你的面具，才如此没羞没臊，破坏我的好事！”
Qianli Xun cursed him in her mind: "On purpose, my foot! Banking on me not tearing your mask off in public so you can shamelessly wreck my date!"

千里寻拉起文渊：“我们去那边聊，把这里留给仪王与佳和郡主。”
She tugged Wenyuan's sleeve: "Let's go talk over there and leave this spot to Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe."

乔文仙点头同意：“仪王，我与淑真去那边。”
Qiao Wenxian nodded: "Prince Yi, Shuzhen and I will step over that way."

申简辰拦道：“还是别，既然大家遇到，不如一起聊？再说你们两个待了一阵儿，重要的话也该说完，再说下去，又提那些伤心事，多没意思，不如我们四个一起，说点儿别的，你们也可开心开心。”
Shen Jianchen stepped in front of them: "Why bother? Since we ran into each other, let's all chat together! You two have been out here a while—must've said everything important already. Keep dwelling on sad stuff and you'll only feel worse. Let the four of us talk about something brighter to cheer you up!"

乔文仙认为仪王说的有道理：“淑真，该说的我已说，不如大家一起？”
Qiao Wenxian saw sense in that: "Shuzhen, we've said our piece—why not join them?"

千里寻正要反对，佳和郡主拉起她的手：“幽栖居士，好久不见你，我有很多话想跟你聊，不如今晚留在幽林别居，跟我聊一晚上。”
Before Qianli Xun could refuse, Princess Jiahe took her hand: "Youqi Jushi, I haven't seen you in ages and have so much to tell you! Why not stay the night at Youlin Retreat and talk all night with me?"

千里寻向远处瞧去，太阳快要落山，入秋后，白天正逐渐缩短，再聊一会儿天色自然越来越暗。发生这么多事，她正想找人好好聊聊，发泄发泄不满，佳和郡主无疑是最佳人选。
Glancing toward the horizon, Qianli Xun saw the sun sinking low; autumn days shrank fast, and night would fall soon. After all the storms lately, she did crave a good vent, and Princess Jiahe was the best confidante around.

“好吧，既如此，我在幽林别居留宿一晚。”
"All right—then I'll stay the night at Youlin Retreat."

淑真同意住下，乔文仙很开心，也不急于一时，他把仪王拉到一边。
Hearing Shuzhen agree to stay cheered Qiao Wenxian up; with no rush now, he pulled Prince Yi aside.

千里寻则跟佳和郡主绕着池塘，边走边聊。
Qianli Xun walked around the pond arm in arm with Princess Jiahe, chatting as they strolled.

白风一见到苦荞便粘着她；南双见夏果不理睬他，去找喜兰说话；留夏果一人只顾关切地注视乔公子。
Bai Feng stuck to Kuqiao like glue the second he saw her; ignored by Xiaguo, Nan Shuang wandered off to chat with Xilan; and Xiaguo stood alone, eyes fixed in anxious care on Young Master Qiao.
