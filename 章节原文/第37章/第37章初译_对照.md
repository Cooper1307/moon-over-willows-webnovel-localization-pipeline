# 第37章 宴会结束回王府

皇宫的宴会终于结束，申简辰与乔文仙乘坐马车回仪王府时，天色已渐渐暗下。

The palace banquet finally came to an end. By the time Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian rode back to Prince Yi's residence in their carriage, dusk was settling in.

---

一天的皇宫经历，申简辰无心回味，只玩味着临安节度使的职位。

Shen Jianchen had no interest in reliving the day's palace experience. His mind was occupied with one thing—his new position as Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

---

临安，他本就要去，现在更有正当理由去，岂不是天遂人愿？足以说明他与临安有缘，难道千里寻真的在那里？想到这些申简辰对临安更加充满向往。

Lin'an—he was going there anyway, and now he had legitimate reason. Wasn't this fate working in his favor? It proved he was destined for Lin'an. Could Qianli Xun really be there? These thoughts filled him with even greater longing.

---

当他与文渊踏进王府，乔梦容第一个迎上来："你们终于回来，我在家里替哥哥担心一整天。"

As soon as he and Wenyuan stepped into the residence, Qiao Mengrong was the first to greet them. "You're finally back! I've been worried about you all day."

---

乔文仙笑道："佳和郡主多虑，仪王不仅一点儿事没有，还被官家亲口封为临安节度使。"

Qiao Wenxian smiled. "Your Highness needn't worry. Not only is Prince Yi perfectly fine, but the Emperor himself has appointed him Military Commissioner of Lin'an."

---

乔梦容喜出望外："哥哥被陛下赏识，往后仪王府终于不会再被人轻视。"

Qiao Mengrong was overjoyed. "Now that the Emperor recognizes you, no one will look down on Prince Yi's residence anymore."

---

乔梦容既兴奋又幸福的表情令申简辰产生莫名的内疚："我与文渊口渴，大家不要站在这里，进屋喝口茶再说。"

The excited, happy expression on Qiao Mengrong's face filled Shen Jianchen with inexplicable guilt. "Wenyuan and I are thirsty. Let's not stand here—come inside for some tea."

---

乔梦容说："都怪我太担心哥哥，才把你们堵在院子里。快进屋，我让丫鬟泡壶散茶。喜兰，快去安排。"

"It's my fault for worrying so much," Qiao Mengrong said. "I blocked you in the courtyard. Please, come inside. I'll have the maid brew some loose-leaf tea. Xilan, go prepare it."

---

"是，郡主。"喜兰笑着离开。

"Yes, Your Highness." Xilan left with a smile.

---

三人进入屋内，坐了没多时，喜兰端着茶壶和两个茶盏进来，给仪王与乔公子斟好茶，站在郡主身边，听他们聊天儿。

The three went inside. They hadn't been seated long when Xilan entered with a teapot and two tea bowls. She poured tea for Prince Yi and Young Master Qiao, then stood beside the princess, listening to their conversation.

---

乔梦容说："哥哥，刚泡的散茶，有些烫，你等会儿再喝。"

"Brother, the tea is freshly brewed and still hot," Qiao Mengrong said. "Wait a bit before drinking."

---

申简辰点点头。

Shen Jianchen nodded.

---

乔文仙看着乔梦容充满爱意的眼神调侃道："我看我还是走吧，继续待下去，怕自己显得多余。"

Watching the loving gaze Qiao Mengrong directed at her brother, Qiao Wenxian teased, "I think I should leave. If I stay any longer, I'll feel like a third wheel."

---

乔梦容因为太高兴，一直盯着哥哥，自觉失态。她把目光从仪王身上移开，害羞道："乔公子向来喜欢开玩笑，你怎么会显得多余？在宫中乔公子肯定帮过哥哥，梦容在此替哥哥谢过。"乔梦容说着起身行谢礼。

Qiao Mengrong had been staring at her brother out of happiness and now realized her impropriety. She shifted her gaze away from Prince Yi and said shyly, "Young Master Qiao always likes to joke. How could you be a third wheel? You must have helped my brother at the palace. Please accept my thanks on his behalf." She stood and bowed.

---

乔文轩赶快站起来回礼："佳和郡主折煞乔某，仪王哪里需要我帮忙？他文武双全，靠自己的本事赢得官家赞赏，与我毫无关系。"

Qiao Wenxian quickly stood and returned the bow. "Your Highness flatters me. How could Prince Yi need my help? He excels in both literary and martial arts, winning the Emperor's praise entirely on his own merit. I had nothing to do with it."

---

申简辰说："你们两个能不能别这样，太见外。"

"Can you two stop being so formal?" Shen Jianchen said.

---

"哥哥说的对，我听你的。"

"You're right, Brother. I'll listen to you."

---

乔梦容不再客气，继续坐下，思忖乔文仙刚才说的话。他说哥哥文武双全，武，她已见识过，至于文，哥哥曾说，写诗作词太费心力，他身体虚弱，不能在诗词方面费心思，故而从未涉猎。

Qiao Mengrong sat back down, no longer standing on ceremony. She pondered what Qiao Wenxian had just said. He claimed her brother excelled in both literary and martial arts. She had already witnessed his martial prowess, but as for literary talent—her brother had once said that writing poetry and composing ci required too much mental effort. With his weak constitution, he couldn't afford to expend energy on such things, so he had never dabbled in them.

---

不由问道："乔公子，能否给我说说哥哥今日的表现，也让我高兴高兴。"

She couldn't help asking, "Young Master Qiao, could you tell me about my brother's performance today? I'd love to hear about it."

---

申简辰阻止道："没什么好说的，都是些奉承巴结之语。"

"There's nothing worth mentioning," Shen Jianchen interrupted. "Just a lot of flattery and sycophancy."

---

乔文仙也已坐下，反对道："仪王此言差矣，奉承巴结之语你确实说过不少，可也有真才实学，就拿你做的词来讲，真是不简单，我给佳和郡主念念……"

Qiao Wenxian, now seated again, objected. "Your Highness is wrong. You did say quite a bit of flattery, but you also displayed genuine talent. Take the ci you composed, for instance—it was truly remarkable. Let me recite it for Her Highness..."

---

词并非申简辰所作，他必须阻止："作词的人不是我，另有其人。"

The ci wasn't composed by Shen Jianchen at all. He had to stop this. "I didn't write that ci. Someone else did."

---

乔文轩质问道："不是你还能是谁？我可是第一次从你口中听到这样的词句？"

Qiao Wenxian challenged. "If not you, then who? It's the first time I've ever heard such words from your mouth."

---

申简辰只能说："我死后在另一个世界学的，是一位伟人所作。"

Shen Jianchen could only say, "I learned it in another world after I died. It was written by a great man."

---

乔文仙根本不认可仪王所说："就知道你会这么说，另一个世界我没见过，既然第一次从你口中听说，只会认为是你所作。"

Qiao Wenxian didn't believe a word. "I knew you'd say that. I've never seen this 'other world' of yours. Since I first heard these words from you, I can only assume you wrote them."

---

乔梦容已按耐不住："乔公子，快念念，我一定要认真记下。"

Qiao Mengrong couldn't contain herself any longer. "Young Master Qiao, please recite it! I want to memorize it carefully."

---

申简辰拦不住文渊，只能由着他。毛爷爷的词借助他来宋代走一遭，想着也挺好玩儿。

Shen Jianchen couldn't stop Wenyuan, so he let him be. It was rather amusing to think that Grandpa Mao's ci was traveling through him to the Song Dynasty.

---

估摸着茶水温度已合适，乔文仙端起来喝了几口，放下茶盏，起身，酝酿好情绪，念道："风雨送春归，飞雪迎春到。已是悬崖百丈冰，犹有花枝俏。俏也不争春，只把春来报。待到山花烂漫时，她在丛中笑。"

Judging the tea had cooled enough, Qiao Wenxian took a few sips, set down his bowl, and stood. He gathered his emotions and recited:

> *Wind and rain escort Spring's return,*  
> *Flying snow welcomes Spring's arrival.*  
> *Though a hundred-foot cliff is clad in ice,*  
> *There remains a sprig of blossoms, elegant and nice.*  
> *Elegant, yet she vies not for Spring's grace,*  
> *But only heralds Spring's embrace.*  
> *When mountain flowers bloom in full array,*  
> *She will smile amongst them, hidden away.*

---

乔梦容认真品味句子，不由陷入回忆……

Qiao Mengrong savored the words carefully and couldn't help falling into memory...

---

有一年冬季，天气寒冷，哥哥病情加重，厚厚的被子和一个火盆根本不管用。乔梦容吩咐侍卫再给屋子里搬进一个火盆，她亲自抱来一床被子给哥哥盖上，他才感觉好些，渐渐睡去。

One winter, the weather was bitterly cold, and her brother's condition had worsened. The thick quilts and single brazier weren't helping at all. Qiao Mengrong ordered the guards to bring another brazier into the room, and she personally carried over an extra quilt to cover him. Only then did he feel better and gradually fall asleep.

---

外面正下着鹅毛大雪，乔梦容披上红色斗篷，带着喜兰走出屋外，踩着刚积了一层的冬雪来到园子里，一株梅花正迎雪开放。

Outside, thick, feathery snowflakes were falling. Qiao Mengrong draped herself in a red cloak and led Xilan out of the room. Stepping onto the fresh layer of winter snow, they made their way to the garden, where a plum tree was blooming defiantly against the snow.

---

冬天哥哥根本不出来，他很少看到冬梅开放的景象，乔梦容吩咐喜兰折几枝梅花。

Her brother never came out in winter, so he rarely saw the winter plums in bloom. Qiao Mengrong told Xilan to pick a few branches.

---

回到屋里，给花瓶中放些水，她把花枝亲手插入后坐在床边欣赏熟睡中的哥哥。

Back inside, she filled a vase with water and arranged the branches herself. Then she sat by the bed, admiring her sleeping brother.

---

哥哥睁开眼时，闻到一股清幽之香："怎么会有股梅花的香气？"

When her brother opened his eyes, he caught a faint, delicate fragrance. "Why does it smell like plum blossoms?"

---

"是我让喜兰折了几枝梅花插在瓶中。"

"I had Xilan pick a few branches and arrange them in the vase."

---

"快扶我起来瞧瞧。"

"Help me up so I can see."

---

乔梦容将哥哥扶起，再让喜兰搬个凳子放在床边，把花瓶放在凳子上，哥哥便可以尽情欣赏。

Qiao Mengrong helped her brother sit up, then had Xilan bring a stool and place it beside the bed. She set the vase on it so her brother could admire the flowers to his heart's content.

---

神奇的是，当梅花见到哥哥时，竟然有一朵即刻开放。乔梦容认为定是喜兆，预示着哥哥一定会好起来。

Miraculously, when the plum blossoms "saw" her brother, one of them immediately bloomed open. Qiao Mengrong took it as an auspicious sign, a harbinger that her brother would surely recover.

---

哥哥却说："梦容，在我眼中，你就是那朵开放的梅花，是我心中的梅花，让我拥有活下去的勇气，要不是因为你，哥哥早已坚持不下去……"

But her brother said, "Mengrong, in my eyes, you are that blooming plum blossom—the plum blossom in my heart that gives me the courage to live. If it weren't for you, I would have given up long ago..."

---

想到这里，乔梦容眼中含着泪，透过泪光，瞧着眼前已脱离病体的哥哥，他写这首词时会不会是因为想起了过往？

Thinking of this, tears welled up in Qiao Mengrong's eyes. Through her tear-blurred vision, she gazed at her brother, now free from illness. Had he written this ci because he was remembering the past?

---

申简辰注意到乔梦容眼睛发红，问道："梦容，你怎么了？"

Shen Jianchen noticed Qiao Mengrong's reddened eyes and asked, "Mengrong, what's wrong?"

---

乔文仙也向佳和郡主瞧去，意识到他不能再待下去："二位，乔某还有事，失陪。"说完迅速离开。

Qiao Wenxian also glanced at the princess and realized he couldn't stay any longer. "I have other matters to attend to. Please excuse me." With that, he quickly left.

---

乔梦容才说道："哥哥，你写的词我很喜欢，不管你写的时候心里想些什么，在我看来这首词是哥哥写给我的。"

Only then did Qiao Mengrong speak. "Brother, I really love the ci you wrote. No matter what you were thinking of when you wrote it, to me, it's a poem you wrote for me."

---

申简辰一阵无语，既不能过多解释，又不忍伤害乔梦容，他保持沉默，端起茶盏，用喝光茶水的动作掩饰无语的表情。

Shen Jianchen was speechless. He couldn't explain too much, nor could he bear to hurt Qiao Mengrong. He stayed silent, picked up his tea bowl, and drained it to hide his expression.

---

趁喝茶时，迅速调整好情绪，放下茶盏，笑道："梦容，今日参加宴会我有些累，想回屋早些睡，你不困吗？"

Taking advantage of drinking tea, he quickly composed himself. Setting down the bowl, he smiled. "Mengrong, I'm a bit tired from the banquet today. I want to go back and rest early. Aren't you sleepy?"

---

乔梦容很失望，要搁以前，哥哥绝对会做出肯定的回应，而现在的哥哥只想回避她的感情。

Qiao Mengrong was disappointed. In the past, her brother would have definitely responded positively. But now, he only wanted to avoid her feelings.

---

"我送哥哥回屋休息。"

"Let me walk you back to your room."

---

申简辰站起来："不用，我自己可以。"

Shen Jianchen stood. "No need. I can manage on my own."

---

乔梦容也站起来，目送哥哥往出走的背影，更加失落，病着时希望他好起来，可好起来后，哥哥却不再依赖她。

Qiao Mengrong also stood, watching her brother's retreating figure. She felt even more dejected. When he was ill, she hoped he would recover. But now that he was well, he no longer depended on her.

---

"哥哥！"

"Brother!"

---

申简辰站住，转过身："还有事？"

Shen Jianchen stopped and turned. "Something else?"

---

"什么时候去临安任职？"

"When are you leaving for Lin'an to take up your post?"

---

"明日还要到宫中领取任职的文书和大印，需耽搁一日，计划后天动身。"

"Tomorrow I need to go to the palace to collect the appointment documents and official seal. It'll take a day. I plan to set off the day after."

---

"哥哥可愿意带上我？"

"Would you be willing to take me with you?"

---

"你想一起去临安，哥哥没有拒绝的理由，跟着便是。"

"If you want to go to Lin'an, I have no reason to refuse. Just come along."

---

乔梦容露出笑容："多谢哥哥！"

Qiao Mengrong smiled. "Thank you, Brother!"

---

"我们是一家人，不用对我客气。"申简辰说完回屋去了。

"We're family. No need to be polite with me." With that, Shen Jianchen went back to his room.

---

"一家人"从哥哥的口中说出，乔梦容感到无比温馨，说明在哥哥心里她依然很重要。

Hearing the word "family" from her brother's mouth filled Qiao Mengrong with warmth. It meant she was still important in his heart.

---

申简辰跟乔文仙已商量好，到宫中办完事，收拾好行囊启程南下。

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian had already made plans: after finishing business at the palace, they would pack their bags and head south.

---

天亮后，他来到宫中，梁师成将盖有官家大印的文书和临安节度使的大印给了。

At daybreak, he went to the palace. Liang Shicheng handed him the documents bearing the Emperor's seal and the official seal of the Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

---

"临安是个好地方，仪王到那里想要了解什么，尽管找临安知府朱晞颜。去年朝廷虽派人打败方腊，招安了淮南盗，可往南去的路上流寇不断，仪王定要一路谨慎。"

"Lin'an is a fine place. If Your Highness needs to know anything once there, just seek out Prefect Zhu Xiyan. Last year, although the court sent forces to defeat Fang La and offered amnesty to the Huainan bandits, the roads south are still plagued by roaming bandits. Your Highness must be cautious on the journey."

---

"不知淮南盗被招安后现状如何？"

"I wonder what happened to the Huainan bandits after the amnesty?"

---

"淮南盗被招安后朝廷妥善安置，可这些盗贼贼心不死，又聚集作乱，原来投降并非出于本意，一周前已被大将折可存镇压，首领宋江也被杀死，其他要犯都一一被斩杀。"

"After the amnesty, the court properly resettled the Huainan bandits. But these thieves never changed their ways—they gathered again and caused trouble. It turned out their surrender was never sincere. A week ago, they were suppressed by General Zhe Kecun. The leader, Song Jiang, was killed, and the other key offenders were executed one by one."

---

"死了！"申简辰本想有机会见见他曾在书中看到的人物，竟如此无缘。

"Dead!" Shen Jianchen had hoped for a chance to meet the figure he'd read about in books. Fate was truly cruel.

---

梁师成无法理解仪王的失落："当然死了，跟朝廷作对没有什么好结果。"

Liang Shicheng couldn't understand Prince Yi's disappointment. "Of course he's dead. There's no good ending for those who oppose the court."

---

离开皇宫，申简辰坐在马车上，沉浸在无法见到宋江的失落中。

Leaving the palace, Shen Jianchen sat in the carriage, immersed in the disappointment of never meeting Song Jiang.

---

马车经过闹市，汴梁街市的喧闹声将他从失落中唤醒。他向车窗外瞧去，街市繁华热闹，人影穿梭，有贵家妇女，有行脚僧人，还有说话艺人，有抬着轿子从身边经过的，有骆驼拖着人或物品慢悠悠走过大街的……

The carriage passed through the bustling market, and the noisy clamor of Bianliang's streets roused him from his melancholy. He looked out the window. The streets were lively and prosperous, with people bustling about—noblewomen, traveling monks, storytellers, sedan chairs passing by, and camels slowly dragging people or goods across the wide streets...

---

申简辰看着看着，眼前出现了一家店铺，写着"赵太丞家"，原来是家医铺。不由想起白风，这家伙还没主动去仪王府找他，不如进赵太丞家打听打听。

As he watched, a shop came into view with the sign "Zhao Taicheng's House"—it was a medical clinic. He couldn't help thinking of Bai Feng. The rascal still hadn't come to find him at the residence. Might as well stop by and ask around.

---

刚有此想法，听到一阵哭闹声从医铺传出："呜呜……！我娘被你们治死了！还我娘！还我五两银子！呜呜……！"

Just as the thought crossed his mind, a wailing cry came from the clinic. "Waaah! You killed my mother with your treatment! Give her back! Give me back my five taels of silver! Waaah!"

---

申简辰让车夫把马车停下，他走下马车，进入赵太丞家。

Shen Jianchen told the driver to stop the carriage. He stepped down and entered Zhao Taicheng's clinic.

---

白风正坐在地上又哭又闹，身边站着医铺的伙计和一些前来诊治的病人。

Bai Feng was sitting on the ground, crying and making a scene. Around him stood the clinic's attendants and some patients who had come for treatment.

---

申简辰上前问："白风，你不去找我，怎么在这里胡闹？"

Shen Jianchen stepped forward and asked, "Bai Feng, why are you causing trouble here instead of coming to find me?"

---

白风像见到救星，立刻爬起来，拽住他："呜呜！哥哥，你是仪王府的侍卫总管，帮帮我，赵太丞家的大夫把我娘治死了，还不给我还钱，呜呜……！"

Bai Feng, seeing a savior, immediately scrambled up and grabbed him. "Waaah! Brother, you're the head guard at Prince Yi's residence. Help me! The doctor here killed my mother with his treatment and won't refund my money! Waaah!"

---

听到是仪王府的人，大夫从里面出来，拱手道："不知阁下是仪王府的什么人？"

Hearing that he was from Prince Yi's residence, the doctor came out from inside and bowed. "May I ask what your position is at Prince Yi's residence?"

---

申简辰说："如果我说我就是仪王，你可相信？"

Shen Jianchen said, "What if I told you I am Prince Yi himself? Would you believe it?"

---

大夫立刻拜道："仪王殿下，这孩子把他娘带来时人已病入膏肓，根本治不好……"

The doctor immediately bowed deeply. "Your Highness Prince Yi, when this boy brought his mother here, she was already beyond help. There was no cure..."

---

白风哭着强辩："既然治不好，为何还要我五两银子？"

Bai Feng argued through his tears. "If you couldn't cure her, why did you take five taels of silver from me?"

---

大夫继续解释："我看你一片孝心，想着也许能救回人，就帮你娘治治，治她这病的药材不便宜，五两银子都是少的，诊金我还没问你要。既诊了脉，也开了药，回到家里，药还没吃到嘴里，人就没了，怎能怪我？"

The doctor continued explaining, "Seeing your filial piety, I thought I might be able to save her, so I treated your mother. The herbs for her illness aren't cheap—five taels was already a bargain. I didn't even charge you the consultation fee. I took her pulse and prescribed the medicine, but by the time you got home, she passed before taking even a single dose. How can you blame me?"

---

"我娘死了虽不能怪你，但你开的药我娘都没吃上，凭什么不把银子还给我？"白风不依不饶。

"I'm not blaming you for my mother's death, but since she never took the medicine you prescribed, why won't you refund my silver?" Bai Feng was relentless.

---

申简辰算是听明白，白风是想要回五两银子："大夫，这孩子抓的药还能不能再退回去？"

Shen Jianchen finally understood—Bai Feng just wanted his five taels back. "Doctor, can the herbs this boy bought be returned?"

---

大夫为难道："都是按照药方抓的，不同药材按量混在一起，退是不能再退。不过看在仪王的面子上，药退不退无所谓，我退给这孩子二两银子，让他回去好安葬他娘，您看如何？"

The doctor looked troubled. "The herbs were prepared according to the prescription, with different ingredients mixed in specific amounts. They can't be returned. But for His Highness's sake, whether the herbs are returned or not doesn't matter. I'll refund this boy two taels of silver so he can properly bury his mother. How does that sound?"

---

白风依然不满意，还想强辩，被申简辰拦住："如此甚好。"

Bai Feng was still dissatisfied and wanted to argue further, but Shen Jianchen stopped him. "That's fine."

---

医铺把二两银子退给白风后，申简辰一把拽起白风，出了赵太丞家，上了马车。

After the clinic refunded two taels to Bai Feng, Shen Jianchen pulled the boy up and led him out of Zhao Taicheng's clinic and into the carriage.

---

白风揣好二两银子问："你果真是仪王？"

Bai Feng tucked away the two taels and asked, "Are you really Prince Yi?"

---

"当然。"

"Of course."

---

白风凑近，瞧了又瞧："原来王爷长这样，跟我没什么区别，就是穿戴好些。"

Bai Feng leaned in, looking him over and over. "So this is what a prince looks like. There's no difference from me, except you dress better."

---

"你家住哪里？我送你回去。"

"Where do you live? I'll take you home."

---

"娘都没了，哪里还有家？不回也罢。"

"With my mother gone, where's home? No point going back."

---

"难道你不打算回去安葬好你娘？"

"Aren't you going back to bury your mother?"

---

"昨日已安葬。"

"I buried her yesterday."

---

"家里可有其他人？"

"Do you have any other family?"

---

"我爹在我出生时就死了，剩我和娘，她现在也走了，我已无家可归。本想今日要到五两银子去仪王府找你，没想到又碰上，看来我与王爷十分有缘。"

"My father died when I was born. It was just my mother and me. Now she's gone too. I have no home. I planned to get five taels today and go find you at the residence, but I didn't expect to run into you again. Seems we have quite the connection, Your Highness."

---

"既如此，现在就去仪王府，以后跟着我。"

"In that case, let's go to Prince Yi's residence now. From now on, you'll follow me."

---

"好的，王爷。"白风立刻转悲为喜，他又有了新家。

"Yes, Your Highness." Bai Feng instantly turned from grief to joy. He had a new home again.
