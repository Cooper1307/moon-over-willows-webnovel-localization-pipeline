## Chapter 37: Chapter 37: Returning from the Palace Banquet

皇宫的宴会终于结束，申简辰与乔文仙乘坐马车回仪王府时，天色已渐渐暗下。
By the time the palace banquet wrapped up and Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian rolled back to Prince Yi's Manor, twilight had settled over the city.

一天的皇宫经历，申简辰无心回味，只玩味着临安节度使的职位。
Skipping over every pompous palace detail, Shen Jianchen rolled one gleaming prize around in his head: Military Commissioner of Lin'an!

临安，他本就要去，现在更有正当理由去，岂不是天遂人愿？足以说明他与临安有缘，难道千里寻真的在那里？想到这些申简辰对临安更加充满向往。
He was already heading to Lin'an—now he held an official imperial mandate to live there! Talk about fate opening every door! Did that mean Qianli Xun truly waited in Lin'an? Anticipation tugged him south harder than ever.

当他与文渊踏进王府，乔梦容第一个迎上来：“你们终于回来，我在家里替哥哥担心一整天。”
Stepping through the manor gate alongside Wenyuan, Qiao Mengrong hurried straight up to meet them: "Back at last! I worried sick at home all day, Brother!"

乔文仙笑道：“佳和郡主多虑，仪王不仅一点儿事没有，还被官家亲口封为临安节度使。”
Qiao Wenxian grinned: "Worried for nothing, Princess Jiahe! Not only did Prince Yi walk out without a scratch, His Majesty named him Military Commissioner of Lin'an on the spot!"

乔梦容喜出望外：“哥哥被陛下赏识，往后仪王府终于不会再被人轻视。”
Pure joy lit Qiao Mengrong's face: "His Majesty finally recognizes Brother's worth! Nobody will ever look down on Prince Yi's Manor again!"

乔梦容既兴奋又幸福的表情令申简辰产生莫名的内疚：“我与文渊口渴，大家不要站在这里，进屋喝口茶再说。”
Her radiant happiness stirred another pang of guilt in Shen Jianchen. "Wenyuan and I are parched—let's not stand in the yard, head inside for tea."

乔梦容说：“都怪我太担心哥哥，才把你们堵在院子里。快进屋，我让丫鬟泡壶散茶。喜兰，快去安排。”
"Silly me blocking you in the courtyard out of worry! Come inside—I'll have maids steep loose-leaf tea! Xilan, hurry!"

“是，郡主。”喜兰笑着离开。
"Yes, Princess!" Xilan dashed off grinning.

三人进入屋内，坐了没多时，喜兰端着茶壶和两个茶盏进来，给仪王与乔公子斟好茶，站在郡主身边，听他们聊天儿。
Moments after the three settled inside, Xilan returned with a teapot and two tea bowls, poured for Prince Yi and Young Master Qiao, and stood beside her mistress listening.

乔梦容说：“哥哥，刚泡的散茶，有些烫，你等会儿再喝。”
"Freshly steeped loose-leaf tea, Brother—still scalding, let it cool a bit," Qiao Mengrong cautioned.

申简辰点点头。
Shen Jianchen nodded.

乔文仙看着乔梦容充满爱意的眼神调侃道：“我看我还是走吧，继续待下去，怕自己显得多余。”
Catching Qiao Mengrong's melting gaze locked onto her brother, Qiao Wenxian teased: "Think I'd better bow out before I feel like a third wheel!"

乔梦容因为太高兴，一直盯着哥哥，自觉失态。她把目光从仪王身上移开，害羞道：“乔公子向来喜欢开玩笑，你怎么会显得多余？在宫中乔公子肯定帮过哥哥，梦容在此替哥哥谢过。”乔梦容说着起身行谢礼。
Flushing hot as she realized she had been staring nonstop, Qiao Mengrong lowered her eyes shyly: "Always teasing, Young Master Qiao—how could you ever be in the way? You surely looked out for Brother at the palace today; allow Mengrong to thank you on his behalf!" Rising, she offered a formal curtsy.

乔文轩赶快站起来回礼：“佳和郡主折煞乔某，仪王哪里需要我帮忙？他文武双全，靠自己的本事赢得官家赞赏，与我毫无关系。”
Jumping up to return the bow, Qiao Wenxian protested: "You overwhelm me, Princess Jiahe! Prince Yi needed zero help from me—skilled in letters and arms alike, he won His Majesty's favor entirely on his own!"

申简辰说：“你们两个能不能别这样，太见外。”
"Quit being so stiff, you two—acts like strangers," Shen Jianchen cut in.

“哥哥说的对，我听你的。”
"Right as always, Brother—I'll listen to you."

乔梦容不再客气，继续坐下，思忖乔文仙刚才说的话。他说哥哥文武双全，武，她已见识过，至于文，哥哥曾说，写诗作词太费心力，他身体虚弱，不能在诗词方面费心思，故而从未涉猎。
Sitting back down, Qiao Mengrong turned over Wenyuan's phrase: skilled in letters and arms alike! She had witnessed his martial skill—yet in letters, Zhao Pu used to say poetry drained his energy too much for his frail frame and never wrote a line.

不由问道：“乔公子，能否给我说说哥哥今日的表现，也让我高兴高兴。”
Curious, she asked: "Tell me what Brother did today, Young Master Qiao—let me celebrate too!"

申简辰阻止道：“没什么好说的，都是些奉承巴结之语。”
"Nothing worth telling—just a pile of bootlicking flattery," Shen Jianchen tried shutting it down.

乔文仙也已坐下，反对道：“仪王此言差矣，奉承巴结之语你确实说过不少，可也有真才实学，就拿你做的词来讲，真是不简单，我给佳和郡主念念……”
Seated again, Qiao Wenxian countered: "Don't sell yourself short, Prince Yi! Plenty of flattery, sure—yet real talent too! Take that ci lyric you recited—utterly extraordinary! Let me recite it for Princess Jiahe..."

词并非申简辰所作，他必须阻止：“作词的人不是我，另有其人。”
Not his poem—Shen Jianchen moved to stop him: "I didn't write that lyric—someone else did!"

乔文轩质问道：“不是你还能是谁？我可是第一次从你口中听到这样的词句？”
"Who else could've written it?" Qiao Wenxian demanded. "First time in my life I ever heard those lines!"

申简辰只能说：“我死后在另一个世界学的，是一位伟人所作。”
Cornered, Shen Jianchen shrugged: "Learned it in the other world after I died—written by a great man."

乔文仙根本不认可仪王所说：“就知道你会这么说，另一个世界我没见过，既然第一次从你口中听说，只会认为是你所作。”
Disbelieving as ever, Qiao Wenxian smirked: "Knew you'd say that! Never seen this 'other world'—first heard it from your lips, so as far as I'm concerned you wrote it!"

乔梦容已按耐不住：“乔公子，快念念，我一定要认真记下。”
Bursting with curiosity, Qiao Mengrong pleaded: "Recite it, Young Master Qiao—I want to memorize every word!"

申简辰拦不住文渊，只能由着他。毛爷爷的词借助他来宋代走一遭，想着也挺好玩儿。
Unable to muzzle Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen gave up—letting Grandpa Mao's masterpiece visit the Song dynasty sounded kinda fun anyway!

估摸着茶水温度已合适，乔文仙端起来喝了几口，放下茶盏，起身，酝酿好情绪，念道：“风雨送春归，飞雪迎春到。已是悬崖百丈冰，犹有花枝俏。俏也不争春，只把春来报。待到山花烂漫时，她在丛中笑。”
Checking the tea temperature, Qiao Wenxian took a few sips, set down his bowl, rose, gathered feeling, and recited aloud: "'Wind and rain escorted Spring's departure, / Flying snow welcomes Spring's return. / On the ice-clad rock rising high and sheer / A flower blooms sweet and fair. / Sweet and fair, she craves not Spring for herself alone, / To be the harbinger of Spring she is content. / When the mountain flowers are in full bloom / She will smile mingling in their midst.' [TN: Official Foreign Languages Press translation]"

乔梦容认真品味句子，不由陷入回忆……
Savoring every word, Qiao Mengrong drifted straight back into memory...

有一年冬季，天气寒冷，哥哥病情加重，厚厚的被子和一个火盆根本不管用。乔梦容吩咐侍卫再给屋子里搬进一个火盆，她亲自抱来一床被子给哥哥盖上，他才感觉好些，渐渐睡去。
One brutal winter, her brother's illness worsened; thick quilts and one brazier couldn't warm him. Ordering guards to haul in a second brazier, she fetched another quilt herself until he drifted off to sleep.

外面正下着鹅毛大雪，乔梦容披上红色斗篷，带着喜兰走出屋外，踩着刚积了一层的冬雪来到园子里，一株梅花正迎雪开放。
Outside, heavy snow drifted down like goose feathers. Throwing on a red cloak, she stepped into the garden with Xilan through ankle-deep snow—where one winter plum tree bloomed brave against the frost.

冬天哥哥根本不出来，他很少看到冬梅开放的景象，乔梦容吩咐喜兰折几枝梅花。
Unable to step outdoors in winter, her brother rarely beheld winter plum blossoms; Qiao Mengrong had Xilan snap several branches.

回到屋里，给花瓶中放些水，她把花枝亲手插入后坐在床边欣赏熟睡中的哥哥。
Back inside, she filled a vase with water, arranged the plum twigs by hand, and sat beside his bed watching him sleep.

哥哥睁开眼时，闻到一股清幽之香：“怎么会有股梅花的香气？”
Waking to a crisp, delicate fragrance, Zhao Pu murmured: "Where is plum blossom scent coming from?"

“是我让喜兰折了几枝梅花插在瓶中。”
"I had Xilan break a few winter plum branches for a vase."

“快扶我起来瞧瞧。”
"Help me sit up to see!"

乔梦容将哥哥扶起，再让喜兰搬个凳子放在床边，把花瓶放在凳子上，哥哥便可以尽情欣赏。
Propping him up while Xilan set a stool by the bedside, she placed the vase right before his eyes.

神奇的是，当梅花见到哥哥时，竟然有一朵即刻开放。乔梦容认为定是喜兆，预示着哥哥一定会好起来。
Like magic, the instant the branches met his gaze, one tight bud unfurled open before them! Joyful, Qiao Mengrong took it for a heavenly omen predicting his recovery.

哥哥却说：“梦容，在我眼中，你就是那朵开放的梅花，是我心中的梅花，让我拥有活下去的勇气，要不是因为你，哥哥早已坚持不下去……”
Yet Zhao Pu whispered: "In my eyes, Mengrong, you are that blooming plum—the plum flower in my heart giving me courage to hold on. Without you, Brother would have given up long ago..."

想到这里，乔梦容眼中含着泪，透过泪光，瞧着眼前已脱离病体的哥哥，他写这首词时会不会是因为想起了过往？
Tears misted Qiao Mengrong's eyes; gazing through shimmering blur at the healthy man seated across from her, she wondered: had memories stirred when he recited that poem?

申简辰注意到乔梦容眼睛发红，问道：“梦容，你怎么了？”
Spotting her reddened eyes, Shen Jianchen asked: "What's wrong, Mengrong?"

乔文仙也向佳和郡主瞧去，意识到他不能再待下去：“二位，乔某还有事，失陪。”说完迅速离开。
Glancing at the princess, Qiao Wenxian read the room fast: "Business calls—excuse me, you two!" Stepping briskly out, he left them alone.

乔梦容才说道：“哥哥，你写的词我很喜欢，不管你写的时候心里想些什么，在我看来这首词是哥哥写给我的。”
Only then did Qiao Mengrong speak: "I adore your lyric, Brother. Whatever was in your heart when you spoke it, to me you wrote it for me."

申简辰一阵无语，既不能过多解释，又不忍伤害乔梦容，他保持沉默，端起茶盏，用喝光茶水的动作掩饰无语的表情。
Struck speechless—unable to shatter her dream yet refusing to lie—Shen Jianchen kept silent, lifting his tea bowl to drain the rest and hide his awkwardness.

趁喝茶时，迅速调整好情绪，放下茶盏，笑道：“梦容，今日参加宴会我有些累，想回屋早些睡，你不困吗？”
Collecting himself behind the cup, he set it down with an easy smile: "Exhausted from the palace banquet, Mengrong—heading to bed early. Aren't you sleepy?"

乔梦容很失望，要搁以前，哥哥绝对会做出肯定的回应，而现在的哥哥只想回避她的感情。
A sharp sting of letdown pierced her—the old Zhao Pu would have answered with tender devotion, whereas this brother dodged her heart every single time.

“我送哥哥回屋休息。”
"Let me walk you back to your room."

申简辰站起来：“不用，我自己可以。”
Rising, Shen Jianchen waved it off: "No need—I'll head back myself."

乔梦容也站起来，目送哥哥往出走的背影，更加失落，病着时希望他好起来，可好起来后，哥哥却不再依赖她。
Standing to watch his retreating back, ache deepened in her chest: sick, she had prayed for his health; healthy, he no longer leaned on her.

“哥哥！”
"Brother!"

申简辰站住，转过身：“还有事？”
Halting, he turned back: "Something else?"

“什么时候去临安任职？”
"When do you leave for Lin'an to take office?"

“明日还要到宫中领取任职的文书和大印，需耽搁一日，计划后天动身。”
"Have to pick up my imperial commission and seal at the palace tomorrow—one day delay, then we set out the day after."

“哥哥可愿意带上我？”
"Will you take me along, Brother?"

“你想一起去临安，哥哥没有拒绝的理由，跟着便是。”
"If you want to see Lin'an, how could Brother say no? Come right along."

乔梦容露出笑容：“多谢哥哥！”
Brightness bloomed back on her face: "Thank you, Brother!"

“我们是一家人，不用对我客气。”申简辰说完回屋去了。
"We're family—no need to thank me." With that, he headed off to his quarters.

“一家人”从哥哥的口中说出，乔梦容感到无比温馨，说明在哥哥心里她依然很重要。
"We're family"—hearing those words from his lips warmed Qiao Mengrong straight through; clearly she still held a vital place in his heart!

申简辰跟乔文仙已商量好，到宫中办完事，收拾好行囊启程南下。
Plans set with Qiao Wenxian, they would handle palace paperwork first, pack up, and head south.

天亮后，他来到宫中，梁师成将盖有官家大印的文书和临安节度使的大印给了。
Next morning at court, Liang Shicheng handed over the imperial commission stamped with the sovereign's seal alongside the official seal of Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

“临安是个好地方，仪王到那里想要了解什么，尽管找临安知府朱晞颜。去年朝廷虽派人打败方腊，招安了淮南盗，可往南去的路上流寇不断，仪王定要一路谨慎。”
"Lin'an is a fine post—need local guidance, turn straight to Prefect Zhu Xiyan," Liang Shicheng advised. "Though imperial armies crushed Fang La last year and granted amnesty to the Huainan bandits, straggler outlaws still roam southern roads—travel with care, Prince Yi."

“不知淮南盗被招安后现状如何？”
"How did the Huainan bandits fare after amnesty?" Shen Jianchen asked.

“淮南盗被招安后朝廷妥善安置，可这些盗贼贼心不死，又聚集作乱，原来投降并非出于本意，一周前已被大将折可存镇压，首领宋江也被杀死，其他要犯都一一被斩杀。”
"The court settled them properly, yet rebel hearts never die—they rose up again! Clearly their surrender was fake from the start. A week ago General Zhe Kecun crushed them, killing their leader Song Jiang and executing every ringleader."

“死了！”申简辰本想有机会见见他曾在书中看到的人物，竟如此无缘。
"Dead?!" Hoping to meet one legendary character from the novels he knew, Shen Jianchen felt a dull thud of loss.

梁师成无法理解仪王的失落：“当然死了，跟朝廷作对没有什么好结果。”
Baffled by the prince's gloom, Liang Shicheng scoffed: "Of course dead! Defying the throne brings only one end."

离开皇宫，申简辰坐在马车上，沉浸在无法见到宋江的失落中。
Leaving the palace inside his carriage, Shen Jianchen brooded over missing Song Jiang.

马车经过闹市，汴梁街市的喧闹声将他从失落中唤醒。他向车窗外瞧去，街市繁华热闹，人影穿梭，有贵家妇女，有行脚僧人，还有说话艺人，有抬着轿子从身边经过的，有骆驼拖着人或物品慢悠悠走过大街的……
Soon the lively roar of Bianliang's market avenues pulled him from his gloom. Peering out the window, he took in the teeming streetscape: noble ladies, wandering monks, storytellers, sedan chairs threading through pedestrians, camels lumbering down the boulevard loaded with goods and riders...

申简辰看着看着，眼前出现了一家店铺，写着“赵太丞家”，原来是家医铺。不由想起白风，这家伙还没主动去仪王府找他，不如进赵太丞家打听打听。
Then a familiar shop sign caught his eye: "Grand Physician Zhao's Clinic"—an apothecary! Wait—that reminded him of Bai Feng! The kid still hadn't shown up at Prince Yi's Manor; why not check inside?

刚有此想法，听到一阵哭闹声从医铺传出：“呜呜……！我娘被你们治死了！还我娘！还我五两银子！呜呜……！”
Just as the thought struck, loud wails burst right out of the clinic door: "Sob...! You killed my mom! Give back my mom! Give back my five taels of silver! Sob...!"

申简辰让车夫把马车停下，他走下马车，进入赵太丞家。
Halting the carriage, Shen Jianchen hopped down and stepped inside Grand Physician Zhao's Clinic.

白风正坐在地上又哭又闹，身边站着医铺的伙计和一些前来诊治的病人。
Sprawled on the floor howling sat Bai Feng, ringed by clinic clerks and waiting patients.

申简辰上前问：“白风，你不去找我，怎么在这里胡闹？”
Stepping right up, Shen Jianchen asked: "Bai Feng! Why are you making a scene here instead of reporting to me?"

白风像见到救星，立刻爬起来，拽住他：“呜呜！哥哥，你是仪王府的侍卫总管，帮帮我，赵太丞家的大夫把我娘治死了，还不给我还钱，呜呜……！”
Spotting his savior, Bai Feng scrambled up and latched onto his sleeve: "Sob! Big Brother—you're Captain of the Guard at Prince Yi's Manor, help me! This doctor killed my mom and won't give back my silver! Sob...!"

听到是仪王府的人，大夫从里面出来，拱手道：“不知阁下是仪王府的什么人？”
Hearing "Prince Yi's Manor," the physician stepped out from the back bowing low: "May I ask your honored position in Prince Yi's Manor, sir?"

申简辰说：“如果我说我就是仪王，你可相信？”
"What if I told you I AM Prince Yi?" Shen Jianchen replied calm.

大夫立刻拜道：“仪王殿下，这孩子把他娘带来时人已病入膏肓，根本治不好……”
The doctor dropped into a deep bow at once: "Prince Yi! When this lad brought his mother in, she was already beyond saving..."

白风哭着强辩：“既然治不好，为何还要我五两银子？”
Bai Feng cried back: "If you couldn't save her, why take my five taels?!"

大夫继续解释：“我看你一片孝心，想着也许能救回人，就帮你娘治治，治她这病的药材不便宜，五两银子都是少的，诊金我还没问你要。既诊了脉，也开了药，回到家里，药还没吃到嘴里，人就没了，怎能怪我？”
"Moved by your filial devotion, I still tried my utmost!" the doctor defended himself. "Her condition needed costly herbs—five taels barely covered ingredients, I didn't even charge a consultation fee! Checked her pulse, weighed the herbs—she passed away at home before taking a single dose! How is that my fault?!"

“我娘死了虽不能怪你，但你开的药我娘都没吃上，凭什么不把银子还给我？”白风不依不饶。
"Her dying isn't your fault—yet she never swallowed a drop of your medicine! Why keep all the silver?!" Bai Feng refused to budge.

申简辰算是听明白，白风是想要回五两银子：“大夫，这孩子抓的药还能不能再退回去？”
Catching the gist—Bai Feng just wanted his five taels back—Shen Jianchen asked: "Doctor, can you take back the herbs he filled?"

大夫为难道：“都是按照药方抓的，不同药材按量混在一起，退是不能再退。不过看在仪王的面子上，药退不退无所谓，我退给这孩子二两银子，让他回去好安葬他娘，您看如何？”
The doctor sighed awkwardly: "Mixed together by prescription weight—impossible to separate and resell. Still, out of respect for Prince Yi, herbs or no herbs, I'll refund the boy two taels to pay for his mother's burial—fair enough?"

白风依然不满意，还想强辩，被申简辰拦住：“如此甚好。”
Still bristling to argue more, Bai Feng got cut off by Shen Jianchen: "More than fair."

医铺把二两银子退给白风后，申简辰一把拽起白风，出了赵太丞家，上了马车。
The moment the clinic handed over two taels of silver, Shen Jianchen hauled Bai Feng by the arm out the door and straight into his carriage.

白风揣好二两银子问：“你果真是仪王？”
Stashing the two taels safe, Bai Feng squinted up at him: "You're REALLY Prince Yi?"

“当然。”
"Of course."

白风凑近，瞧了又瞧：“原来王爷长这样，跟我没什么区别，就是穿戴好些。”
Leaning close for a long look, Bai Feng muttered: "Huh—princes look just like regular folks, just fancier threads!"

“你家住哪里？我送你回去。”
"Where do you live? I'll drop you home."

“娘都没了，哪里还有家？不回也罢。”
"Mom's gone—what home? No point going back."
