### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 现代穿越者的幽默内心独白（对应第16段：借用毛主席诗词）
> **【原文】** 申简辰拦不住文渊，只能由着他。毛爷爷的词借助他来宋代走一遭，想着也挺好玩儿。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen couldn't stop Wenyuan, so he let him be. It was rather amusing to think that Grandpa Mao's ci was traveling through him to the Song Dynasty.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ***Well, there’s no stopping him now,*** Shen Jianchen thought with a resigned wry chuckle. ***I suppose letting Chairman Mao’s poetry take a little joyride through the Song Dynasty via my mouth isn't the worst thing in the world.***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    **网文斜体规范！** 将主角的内心情感活动严格写为**斜体 (Italics)**。用 "take a little joyride through the Song Dynasty via my mouth"（借我的嘴在宋代游历一番）替代平淡的 "traveling through him"，极大地展现了现代穿越者特有的幽默与洒脱。

#### 2. “骨科”误会与尴尬防线的心理拉扯（对应第27、28段：关于诗词给谁写的）
> **【原文】** 乔梦容才说道："哥哥，你写的词我很喜欢，不管你写的时候心里想些什么，在我看来这首词是哥哥写给我的。" 申简辰一阵无语，既不能过多解释，又不忍伤害乔梦容，他保持沉默，端起茶盏，用喝光茶水的动作掩饰无语的表情。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Only then did Qiao Mengrong speak. "Brother, I really love the ci you wrote. No matter what you were thinking of when you wrote it, to me, it's a poem you wrote for me."
    Shen Jianchen was speechless. He couldn't explain too much, nor could he bear to hurt Qiao Mengrong. He stayed silent, picked up his tea bowl, and drained it to hide his expression.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "Brother," Qiao Mengrong murmured softly, her eyes brimming with undeniable affection. "I love the poem you wrote. No matter what was on your mind when you composed it, in my heart, it will always be a poem you dedicated to me."
    Shen Jianchen was utterly speechbound. ***How on earth do I even respond to that?!*** He couldn't set the record straight without shattering her heart into a million pieces. Swallowing his awkwardness, he silently raised his tea bowl and gulped down the brew to mask his deadpan expression.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    强化乔梦容对哥哥的执念与申简辰内心的“尬到窒息”。加入直接内心吐槽 ***How on earth do I even respond to that?!***，用 "gulped down the brew to mask his deadpan expression"（咕噜咕噜喝茶来掩饰自己的面瘫脸）替代平淡的 "hide his expression"，大大提升了网文的喜剧效果。

#### 3. 闻知宋江之死时的失落与朝廷残酷（对应第41、42段）
> **【原文】** "死了！"申简辰本想有机会见见他曾在书中看到的人物，竟如此无缘。 梁师成无法理解仪王的失落："当然死了，跟朝廷作对没有什么好结果。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    "Dead!" Shen Jianchen had hoped for a chance to meet the figure he'd read about in books. Fate was truly cruel.
    Liang Shicheng couldn't understand Prince Yi's disappointment. "Of course he's dead. There's no good ending for those who oppose the court."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ***"Dead?!"*** Shen Jianchen’s jaw dropped slightly. He had genuinely held out hope for a chance to meet the legendary outlaw chief he’d grown up reading about in history and fiction. What a cruel twist of fate.
    Liang Shicheng mistook his reaction and snorted softly, completely unable to grasp Prince Yi’s melancholy. "Of course he’s dead, Your Highness. Opposing the Imperial Court never ends in anything less than a blade to the neck."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. “死了！”翻译为更具惊讶感和冲击力的 ***"Dead?!"***。用 "legendary outlaw chief" 代替 "figure"，凸显宋江在男主心中的“英雄/绿林好汉”定位。
    2. 梁师成的台词升级：把平淡的 "no good ending" 改为符合宋代奸臣酷吏口吻的 "anything less than a blade to the neck"（脖子上挨一刀/身首异处），突出了封建朝廷的冷酷无情。

#### 4. 小泼皮白风医馆闹事的泼辣画面感（对应第51、52段）
> **【原文】** 白风正坐在地上又哭又闹... 白风像见到救星，立刻爬起来，拽住他："呜呜！哥哥，你是仪王府的侍卫总管，帮帮我，赵太丞家的大夫把我娘治死了，还不给我还钱，呜呜……！"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Bai Feng was sitting on the ground, crying and making a scene... Bai Feng, seeing a savior, immediately scrambled up and grabbed him. "Waaah! Brother, you're the head guard at Prince Yi's residence. Help me! The doctor here killed my mother with his treatment and won't refund my money! Waaah!"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Bai Feng was sprawled on the clinic floor, wailing at the top of his lungs and thrashing his legs. Spotting Shen Jianchen, the boy’s eyes lit up as if seeing his messiah. He scrambled to his feet and lunged forward, clutching Shen Jianchen’s sleeve.
    "Brother! You’re the head guard from Prince Yi’s estate, right?!" Bai Feng howled through crocodile tears. "Help me! This quack physician’s bad medicine killed my mother, and now he won't give back my five taels of silver! Waaah!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    描写白风“撒泼耍赖”的动作："sprawled on the clinic floor, wailing at the top of his lungs and thrashing his legs"（撒滚撒泼、哭天抢地），指责大夫时使用 "quack physician's bad medicine"（庸医的毒药/劣药），形象跃然纸上，极具街头孤儿的狡黠与可怜。

#### 5. 医馆大夫自辩的古风老练表达（对应第55段）
> **【原文】** 大夫继续解释："我看你一片孝心，想着也许能救回人，就帮你娘治治，治她这病的药材不便宜，五两银子都是少的，诊金我还没问你要..."

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    The doctor continued explaining, "Seeing your filial piety, I thought I might be able to save her, so I treated your mother. The herbs for her illness aren't cheap—five taels was already a bargain. I didn't even charge you the consultation fee..."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    The physician offered a helpless sigh. "Moved by the boy's filial devotion, I attempted the impossible to save her. The rare herbs required for her ailment were extremely costly—five taels barely covered the ingredients, and I didn't even charge a single copper for my consultation fee!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    “赵太丞家”是宋代汴梁赫赫有名的官方御医御用诊所，大夫说话应带有老练自负的口吻。将 "bargain"（便宜货/划算）改为 "barely covered the ingredients"（刚好够药材成本），用 "didn't even charge a single copper" 代替 "didn't charge you"，更契合宋代老医者的语气。
