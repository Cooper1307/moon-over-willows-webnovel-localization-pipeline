# 第37章 深度审校报告（Phase 2）

## 一、重复用词排查

### 修改点 1：「said」高频重复（全章出现约 9 次）

**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong **said**, "It's my fault for worrying so much..."
> Shen Jianchen **said**, "Can you two stop being so formal?"
> Shen Jianchen **said**, "What if I told you I am Prince Yi himself?"
> The doctor **continued explaining**, "Seeing your filial piety..."
> Shen Jianchen **said**, "That's fine."
> Bai Feng tucked away the two taels and **asked**, "Are you really Prince Yi?"
> Shen Jianchen **said**, "Where do you live?"
> Shen Jianchen **said**, "In that case, let's go to Prince Yi's residence now."

**修改后：**
> "It's my fault for worrying so much," Qiao Mengrong **admitted**.
> "Can you two stop being so formal?" Shen Jianchen **cut in**.
> "What if I told you I am Prince Yi himself?" Shen Jianchen **offered**.
> The doctor **pressed on**, "Seeing your filial piety..."
> "That's fine." Shen Jianchen **waved it off**.
> Bai Feng tucked away the two taels. "Are you really Prince Yi?"
> "Where do you live?" Shen Jianchen **asked**.
> "In that case, let's go to Prince Yi's residence now. From now on, you'll follow me."

**修改理由：** "said"在全章出现约9次，远超阈值（>3次/章）。根据原文情绪信号替换为多样化对话动词或直接用动作替代标签。

---

### 修改点 2：「Qiao Mengrong was / Qiao Mengrong + 情绪形容词」结构重复

**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong **was overjoyed**. "Now that the Emperor recognizes you..."
> Qiao Mengrong **was disappointed**. In the past, her brother would have definitely responded positively.
> Qiao Mengrong **also stood**, watching her brother's retreating figure. She **felt even more dejected**.

**修改后：**
> Joy lit up Qiao Mengrong's face. "Now that the Emperor recognizes you..."
> Qiao Mengrong's smile faltered. In the past, her brother would have said yes without hesitation.
> Qiao Mengrong rose as well, watching her brother's retreating figure. The distance between them felt wider than the room.

**修改理由：** "was + 情绪形容词"是典型中式叙述（"乔梦容喜出望外""乔梦容很失望"的直译），缺乏画面感。改为具象化动作/身体反应，避免"告诉读者情绪"而改为"展示情绪"。

## 二、中式英语 / 不地道表达

### 修改点 3：「Taking advantage of drinking tea」

**修改前：**
> **Taking advantage of drinking tea**, he quickly composed himself. Setting down the bowl, he smiled.

**修改后：**
> He hid behind the rim of his tea bowl, buying himself three seconds. When he set it down, the smile was already in place.

**修改理由：** "Taking advantage of drinking tea"是"趁喝茶时"的逐字直译，英文中不存在此结构。改为具象化动作（"hid behind the rim"），同时保留原文"用喝茶掩饰"的意图。

---

### 修改点 4：「The excited, happy expression on Qiao Mengrong's face filled Shen Jianchen with inexplicable guilt」

**修改前：**
> **The excited, happy expression on Qiao Mengrong's face** filled Shen Jianchen with **inexplicable guilt**.

**修改后：**
> Qiao Mengrong was *glowing*—eyes bright, cheeks flushed, barely containing herself. And for reasons Shen Jianchen couldn't name, it made him feel like a fraud.

**修改理由：** ① "The excited, happy expression"是"既兴奋又幸福的表情"的直译，英文中不会用两个形容词堆叠修饰expression；② "inexplicable guilt"是"莫名的内疚"的直译，改为更口语化的"like a fraud"，贴合穿越者冒用他人身份的语境。

---

### 修改点 5：「Qiao Mengrong had been staring at her brother out of happiness and now realized her impropriety」

**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong **had been staring at her brother out of happiness** and now **realized her impropriety**.

**修改后：**
> Qiao Mengrong caught herself—she'd been staring. Her cheeks warmed. She pulled her gaze away.

**修改理由：** ① "out of happiness"是"因为太高兴"的直译，英文不用此结构；② "realized her impropriety"是"自觉失态"的直译，过于书面化；③ 改为碎片句+身体反应，更符合网文节奏。

---

### 修改点 6：「Bai Feng instantly turned from grief to joy」

**修改前：**
> "Yes, Your Highness." Bai Feng **instantly turned from grief to joy**. He had a new home again.

**修改后：**
> "Yes, Your Highness." The tears hadn't even dried, but Bai Feng was already grinning. Just like that—he had a home again.

**修改理由：** "turned from grief to joy"是"转悲为喜"的成语直译，英文中不自然。改为具象画面（泪未干已在笑），更有冲击力。

---

### 修改点 7：「Shen Jianchen had no interest in reliving the day's palace experience」

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen **had no interest in reliving the day's palace experience**. His mind was occupied with one thing—his new position as Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

**修改后：**
> The palace? The banquet? The Emperor's poetry? Shen Jianchen couldn't have cared less. One word circled his mind like a hawk: *Lin'an*. Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

**修改理由：** ① "had no interest in reliving the day's palace experience"是"无心回味一天的皇宫经历"的直译，冗长且书面化；② 改为碎片式反问+否定，节奏更快；③ "like a hawk"是轻微润色，基于原文"玩味"的意象（反复盘旋思考）。

---

### 修改点 8：「In the past, her brother would have definitely responded positively」

**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong was disappointed. In the past, her brother **would have definitely responded positively**. But now, he only wanted to avoid her feelings.

**修改后：**
> Qiao Mengrong's smile dimmed. Before—the *real* before—her brother would have said yes. Would have teased her, pulled her along, made her laugh. Now he just... sidestepped.

**修改理由：** ① "responded positively"是"做出肯定的回应"的直译，过于正式/商务化；② "avoid her feelings"是"回避她的感情"的直译；③ 改为更口语化、更有情感层次的表达，用"sidestepped"一个词概括回避。

## 三、句子过长 / 节奏问题

### 修改点 9：乔梦容回忆段（梅花往事）节奏偏平

**修改前：**
> One winter, the weather was bitterly cold, and her brother's condition had worsened. The thick quilts and single brazier weren't helping at all. Qiao Mengrong ordered the guards to bring another brazier into the room, and she personally carried over an extra quilt to cover him. Only then did he feel better and gradually fall asleep.

**修改后：**
> One winter. Bitter cold. Her brother's breathing had turned shallow, rattling.
>
> Two quilts. One brazier. Not enough. Never enough.
>
> She ordered another brazier. Carried a third quilt herself—refused to let the guards do it. Tucked it around his shoulders, smoothed the edges. Only then did his breathing ease. Only then did his eyes close.

**修改理由：** ① 原文是情感回忆的高潮铺垫，需要节奏感；② 原译是标准叙述句，缺乏情绪张力；③ 用碎片句（"Two quilts. One brazier. Not enough."）模拟回忆时的画面闪回；④ "refused to let the guards do it"基于原文"亲自抱来"的语义强化角色性格。

---

### 修改点 10：大夫辩解段过长

**修改前：**
> The doctor continued explaining, "Seeing your filial piety, I thought I might be able to save her, so I treated your mother. The herbs for her illness aren't cheap—five taels was already a bargain. I didn't even charge you the consultation fee. I took her pulse and prescribed the medicine, but by the time you got home, she passed before taking even a single dose. How can you blame me?"

**修改后：**
> The doctor pressed on, "I saw a son's devotion. Thought maybe—*maybe*—I could pull her back. So I tried. The herbs alone cost more than five taels. Five was a *bargain*. I didn't even charge the consultation fee.
>
> I took her pulse. Wrote the prescription. But by the time you carried her home—" He spread his hands. "She was gone before the first dose touched her lips. How is that my fault?"

**修改理由：** ① 原译是单段长引语（超70词），不符合网文移动端阅读习惯；② 拆分为两段，中间加入动作（"He spread his hands"）制造停顿；③ "pull her back"比"save her"更口语化，符合大夫急切辩解的语气。

---

### 修改点 11：梁师成长段信息交代

**修改前：**
> "After the amnesty, the court properly resettled the Huainan bandits. But these thieves never changed their ways—they gathered again and caused trouble. It turned out their surrender was never sincere. A week ago, they were suppressed by General Zhe Kecun. The leader, Song Jiang, was killed, and the other key offenders were executed one by one."

**修改后：**
> "After the amnesty, the court resettled them. Gave them land. Gave them a chance." Liang Shicheng's tone turned cold. "They spat on it. Regrouped. Started raiding again. Turned out the surrender was never real—just a breath between kills.
>
> A week ago, General Zhe Kecun put them down. Song Jiang—dead. The rest of the ringleaders—dead. Every last one."

**修改理由：** ① 原译是平铺直叙的信息交代，缺乏情绪层次；② 梁师成作为宦官，语气应有轻蔑和冷酷感；③ "dead"重复三次形成节奏锤击，比"executed one by one"更有冲击力；④ 拆分段落，加入语气描写。

## 四、吸引力与节奏强化

### 修改点 12：开篇首句缺乏钩子

**修改前：**
> The palace banquet finally came to an end. By the time Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian rode back to Prince Yi's residence in their carriage, dusk was settling in.

**修改后：**
> Dusk swallowed the carriage whole.
>
> The palace banquet was over. The gold bowls were packed. The Emperor's praise had faded to polite silence. And Shen Jianchen sat in the swaying dark, turning one word over and over in his mind like a coin: *Lin'an*.

**修改理由：** ① 原开篇"The palace banquet finally came to an end"是平铺直叙；② 改为意象化开头（"Dusk swallowed"），制造氛围；③ 用三个短句回顾本章收获，然后聚焦到核心悬念（Lin'an），为下章铺垫。

---

### 修改点 13：白风哭闹场景缺乏画面感

**修改前：**
> Just as the thought crossed his mind, a wailing cry came from the clinic. "Waaah! You killed my mother with your treatment! Give her back! Give me back my five taels of silver! Waaah!"
>
> Shen Jianchen told the driver to stop the carriage. He stepped down and entered Zhao Taicheng's clinic.
>
> Bai Feng was sitting on the ground, crying and making a scene. Around him stood the clinic's attendants and some patients who had come for treatment.

**修改后：**
> Then—a wail. Raw, ugly, the kind that made strangers flinch.
>
> "You *killed* her! You killed my mother! Give her back! Give me back my five taels! *Waaah—!*"
>
> Shen Jianchen knocked twice on the carriage wall. The driver pulled up. He stepped down into the street noise and pushed through the clinic's doorway.
>
> Bai Feng. On the ground. Legs splayed, face streaked with snot and tears, fists pounding the flagstones. A ring of attendants and patients stood around him, arms crossed, expressions ranging from pity to irritation.

**修改理由：** ① 原译"a wailing cry came from the clinic"是叙述性交代，缺乏冲击力；② 改为先声夺人（"Then—a wail"）；③ 白风的动作描写从"sitting on the ground, crying and making a scene"升级为具象画面（"fists pounding the flagstones"）；④ 围观者的反应增加场景真实感。

---

### 修改点 14：「死了！」——宋江之死的冲击

**修改前：**
> "Dead!" Shen Jianchen had hoped for a chance to meet the figure he'd read about in books. Fate was truly cruel.

**修改后：**
> "Dead."
>
> The word landed flat. Shen Jianchen stared at Liang Shicheng. Song Jiang. *The* Song Jiang. The man he'd read about in a previous life, in a world of paperbacks and late-night reading lamps. Gone. Killed a week ago. By a general whose name he'd also read in a book.
>
> Fate didn't just close the door. It bricked it up.

**修改理由：** ① 原译"Fate was truly cruel"是"竟如此无缘"的直译，过于概括；② 穿越者得知书中人物已死，应有更复杂的内心冲击——用碎片句模拟震惊；③ "bricked it up"比"truly cruel"更有画面感和现代口语感，符合穿越者内心声音。

---

### 修改点 15：结尾缺乏余韵

**修改前：**
> "Yes, Your Highness." Bai Feng instantly turned from grief to joy. He had a new home again.

**修改后：**
> "Yes, Your Highness."
>
> The tears hadn't dried. But Bai Feng was already grinning—wide, gap-toothed, ridiculous.
>
> The carriage rolled on. Two taels in his pocket. A dead mother behind him. A new home ahead.
>
> He didn't look back.

**修改理由：** ① 原结尾"He had a new home again"是叙述性总结，缺乏余韵；② 改为具象画面+三个碎片名词短语（"Two taels / A dead mother / A new home"）制造节奏感；③ "He didn't look back"作为收尾，暗示角色性格（向前看、不沉溺），比直接告诉读者"他很高兴"更有力。

## 五、标点与网文格式

### 修改点 16：内心独白未使用斜体

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen thought happily, I'm a soccer expert. I've practiced keeping the ball in the air. With my Shen Fist skills, playing this kind of ball is a piece of cake.

（注：此段在第36章，但第37章中申简辰的内心活动以叙述形式呈现，如：）

> His mind was occupied with one thing—his new position as Military Commissioner of Lin'an.

**修改后（第37章中的内心活动处理）：**
> One thing. Only one thing. *Military Commissioner of Lin'an.* The words tasted like a key turning in a lock.

**修改理由：** 工作流要求内心独白用斜体。本章申简辰的内心活动多以叙述形式呈现（"His mind was occupied with..."），在关键情绪点应转为斜体内心独白格式，增强代入感。

### 修改点 17：乔梦容回忆中的对话缺少段落分隔

**修改前：**
> When her brother opened his eyes, he caught a faint, delicate fragrance. "Why does it smell like plum blossoms?"
> "I had Xilan pick a few branches and arrange them in the vase."
> "Help me up so I can see."

**修改后：**
> When her brother opened his eyes, he caught a faint, delicate fragrance.
>
> "Why does it smell like plum blossoms?"
>
> "I had Xilan pick a few branches. Arranged them in the vase myself."
>
> "Help me up. I want to see."

**修改理由：** ① 对话之间需要空行分隔（网文移动端阅读规范）；② "Help me up so I can see"改为两个短句"Help me up. I want to see."更符合病中虚弱的说话节奏。

## 六、其他零散修正

### 修改点 18：「Shen Jianchen couldn't stop Wenyuan, so he let him be」

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen couldn't stop Wenyuan, so he let him be. It was rather amusing to think that Grandpa Mao's ci was traveling through him to the Song Dynasty.

**修改后：**
> Shen Jianchen gave up trying to stop him. Fine. Let Wenyuan recite.
>
> *Grandpa Mao's words, hitching a ride through my mouth into the Song Dynasty.* He almost laughed. The old poet would've appreciated the absurdity.

**修改理由：** ① "so he let him be"是"只能由着他"的直译，改为更口语化的"Fine. Let Wenyuan recite."；② 内心活动转为斜体；③ "traveling through him"改为"hitching a ride through my mouth"更生动、更符合穿越者痞气。

---

### 修改点 19：「Qiao Mengrong couldn't contain herself any longer」

**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong **couldn't contain herself any longer**. "Young Master Qiao, please recite it! I want to memorize it carefully."

**修改后：**
> Qiao Mengrong leaned forward, hands clasped. "Young Master Qiao—*please*. Recite it. Every word. I want to write it down, memorize it, keep it."

**修改理由：** "couldn't contain herself any longer"是"已按耐不住"的直译。改为具象动作（leaned forward, hands clasped）+ 递进式短句（"write it down, memorize it, keep it"），体现角色的急切和珍视。

---

### 修改点 20：「Shen Jianchen was speechless」

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen **was speechless**. He couldn't explain too much, nor could he bear to hurt Qiao Mengrong. He stayed silent, picked up his tea bowl, and drained it to hide his expression.

**修改后：**
> Shen Jianchen's mouth opened. Closed. Opened again. Nothing came out.
>
> He couldn't explain. Couldn't tell her the truth. And he couldn't—*wouldn't*—hurt her. So he picked up his tea bowl and drained it, letting the hot liquid buy him silence.

**修改理由：** ① "was speechless"是"一阵无语"的直译，过于概括；② 改为动作分解（mouth opened / closed / opened）展示"无语"的状态；③ "couldn't...couldn't...couldn't—wouldn't"递进结构增强情感层次。
