第147章 两人筹谋两人忧
## Chapter 147: Two Plotting, Two Worrying

青瓜为公子上药，姚轻琰在一旁将儿子身上的伤尽数看遍，的确严重。打得这么重，竟然小命还在，实在难得。
As Qinggua rubbed ointment across her son's torso, Yao Qingyan saw the full extent of the bruising—fierce enough it was a miracle nothing had broken!

“青瓜，老实告诉我，公子身上的伤是谁打的？”
"Tell me the truth, Qinggua—who gave Young Master these bruises?"

青瓜边上药边支支吾吾不敢言语。
Applying salve, Qinggua stammered, unable to meet her eye.

陈浩宇说：“我都说了，是朱淑真，娘为何不信？还要问青瓜？”
Chen Haoyu jumped in: "Already told you it was Zhu Shuzhen, Mother—why interrogate Qinggua?!"

“我才不信是朱家姑娘打的，肯定是你在外得罪什么人，老实告诉娘是谁打的？我让你爹出面处理此事。”
"Don't buy Zhu Shuzhen for one second! Who did you cross outside? Tell Mother straight and I'll have your father handle it!"

“娘爱信不信，反正就是朱淑真打的。”
"Believe it or don't—it was Zhu Shuzhen."

姚轻琰从儿子口中问不出所以然，便不再继续追问，目睹青瓜一点点涂完药，为儿子穿好衣裳后，把青瓜叫到一边。
Getting nowhere with her stubborn son, Yao Qingyan waited until Qinggua finished dressing him, then pulled the boy aside.

“青瓜，你今儿不给我说出公子身上的伤是谁打的，我就把你送回老家。”
"Tell me who beat Young Master right now, Qinggua, or I'll pack you back to your home village!"

青瓜立刻跪下：“夫人，千万不要送我回老家，……其实我也不知道公子是被谁打的，我在城外找到他时，公子已被人打得遍体鳞伤。”
Qinggua hit his knees at once: "Please don't send me home, Madam! Honest, I don't know who beat him either—by the time I tracked Young Master down outside the city, he was already beaten black and blue!"

陈浩宇从床上下来：“娘，您别吓唬青瓜，他真的不知道。”
Hopping off bed, Chen Haoyu protested, "Quit scaring Qinggua, Mother—he really doesn't know."

姚轻琰只好说：“起来吧，以后跟着公子，小心伺候，别让他再被人伤到。”
Relenting, Yao Qingyan told him, "Get up. Watch closer over Young Master from now on and don't let him get hurt again."

青瓜谢过夫人后起身，一高兴说道：“夫人有所不知，抓公子的人很厉害，我还没反应过来，公子就被他抓上马车……”
Scrambling up relieved, Qinggua's loose tongue slipped: "You didn't see it, Madam—the guy who snatched Young Master moved like lightning! Before I could blink, he yanked Young Master straight into a carriage..."

陈浩宇踢青瓜一脚：“滚！滚出去！尽瞎说！”
Chen Haoyu kicked Qinggua's shin: "Get out! Quit making up fairy tales!"

青瓜意识到失言，边往出走边改口：“夫人，我是说着玩的。”
Catching his slip, Qinggua scrambled for the door calling back: "Just joking, Madam!"

因踢青瓜，又一阵疼痛，陈浩宇忍着，尽量不被母亲看出：“娘，您就别瞎问，儿子还能骗您？爹不是说要带您去取衣裳还有胭脂水粉吗？您赶紧去吧，省得爹等得着急。”
Swinging his leg woke a sharp twinge in his ribs, which Chen Haoyu masked fast: "Stop playing detective, Mother—would I lie to you? Didn't Father promise to take you shopping for robes and rouge? Go already before Father gets impatient!"

儿子显然是赶人，姚轻琰不好再待下去：“近期不准再往外跑，好好在家休养，听到没？”
Taking the clear hint he was shooing her out, Yao Qingyan warned: "Stay indoors healing—no sneaking out, hear me?"

“听到了，听到了，赶紧去吧。”陈浩宇将姚轻琰往外推。
"Heard you, heard you—have fun!" Chen Haoyu nudged her right out the door.

……
...

陈家的好药的确管用，不出三日，陈浩宇身上的伤已痊愈，精神头一起来，在陈府待不下去，打算出去玩儿。
Chen Manor's top-grade salve worked miracles: within three days Chen Haoyu's bruises cleared, his vigor snapped back, and boredom drove him right back out the gate.

为报复朱淑真，发誓吃素半年，半年未到，报复已结束，自己的幸福还可能被搭进去，实在没必要坚持下去。
Swearing off women for six months had been his revenge vow against Zhu Shuzhen; revenge was over and his own bachelorhood was on the chopping block—zero point keeping celibate now!

上次打算开荤，还没踏入春香楼，被仪王的侍卫给绑了，真是晦气！不如今日出去开荤，顺便驱赶驱赶晦气。
Last time he aimed for Chunxiang Brothel, Prince Yi's guard had bagged him right outside the door—rotten luck! Today he'd go celebrate and wash the bad luck off.

陈浩宇决定开荤前，先去趟望月轩，与朱淑真通通气，商量接下来该怎么对付两家人。
First, though, better swing by Wangyue Pavilion to sync plans with Zhu Shuzhen against both their families.

他带青瓜刚走出陈府大门，瞧见常跟着他的小弟在外面晃荡，显然在等他。
Stepping out Chen Manor's gate with Qinggua, he spotted his usual pack of cronies loitering outside waiting for him.

“青瓜，你回去吧，不必跟着我。”
"Head back indoors, Qinggua—don't tag along."

青瓜失望道：“公子，我想去春香楼。”
Qinggua pouted: "Young Master, I want to see Chunxiang Brothel!"

“本公子仔细一想，你实在不适合，还是老老实实待在陈府，等我回来再跟着我。”
"Thought it over—not your scene. Stay home at Chen Manor and wait on me when I get back."

“公子为什么说我不适合？我可是想去得很。”
"Why isn't it my scene? I'm dying to go!"

“问那么多干吗？本公子就不想带你，你要想去自己去，不要跟着我！”
"Quit arguing—said I'm not taking you! Wanna go, go on your own dime!"

“去春香楼那种地方得花不少银子，我可舍不得，要不公子把银子给我，我自己去。”
"Chunxiang Brothel costs a fortune—I can't afford that! Give me the silver and I'll go solo!"

还没等公子答应，小弟们瞧见陈浩宇，都靠拢过来：“陈公子，可见着您，我们想您想得天天晚上睡不着觉。”
Before Chen Haoyu could answer, his cronies swarmed over grinning: "Young Master Chen! Missed you so much we couldn't sleep at night!"

陈浩宇已顾不上青瓜：“你们几个不就是想跟着本公子到处干坏事，还不用自己花银子，放心，本公子好久没干坏事，手痒痒，这就带你们去。”
Forgetting Qinggua completely, Chen Haoyu grinned: "Just itching to raise hell on my tab, aren't you?! Good news—my hands are itching too! Come on!"

青瓜眼睁睁看着公子坐上马车离开，瞧着那几个他没见过的小弟，不满道：“一看都是些混吃混喝的地痞流氓，我跟他们的确不是一类人，难怪公子不喜欢带我，还是老实待在陈府，别想那些乱七八糟的事。”
Watching his master climb into a carriage surrounded by those loud ruffians, Qinggua curled his lip: "Bunch of freeloading street thugs—glad I'm nothing like them! Better off staying cozy inside Chen Manor anyway."

陈浩宇先来到望月轩，跟小弟们一下马车，在门口碰到化雨。
Stopping first at Wangyue Pavilion, Chen Haoyu hopped out with his pack and bumped straight into Huayu at the gate.

“喂！你是不是东方尘泽身边的跟班？叫什么来着？”
"Hey! Aren't you Dongfang Chenze's little sidekick—what's your name?"

化雨上前见礼：“陈公子好，我是跟着我家公子，名叫化雨。”
Huayu bowed: "Greetings, Young Master Chen. I serve my master—name's Huayu."

“你为何在望月轩门口？”
"What's got you hanging outside Wangyue Pavilion?"

“来替我家公子讨要幽栖居士的诗词。”
"Collecting new poems from Youqi Jushi for my master."

“东方尘泽脑子是不是有问题？整日就喜欢朱淑真写的那些没用的玩意儿。”
"Is Dongfang Chenze loose in the head? Obsessing all day over Zhu Shuzhen's useless scribbles!"

正说着，苦荞拿着手稿出来：“化雨，一共两首，都是我家小姐现写的。”
Right then Kuqiao walked out holding folded sheets: "Here you go, Huayu—two brand-new pieces Miss just wrote."

化雨上前接过手稿，折好揣进怀里：“感谢苦荞姐姐。”
Taking and tucking them safely inside his robe: "Thanks, Sister Kuqiao!"

“我家小姐麻烦你给东方公子捎句话，如果哪里举行诗会，记得来邀请她，小姐整日待在望月轩，闷得慌。”
"Miss also asked you to pass word to Young Master Dongfang—if any poetry salon pops up, invite her! Cooped up in Wangyue Pavilion all day is driving her nuts."

“苦荞姐姐放心，我定将幽栖居士的话转达给我家公子。”
"Rest assured, Sister Kuqiao—I'll tell my master word for word!"

得到幽栖居士的佳作，化雨很满意，笑着离开。
Pleased with his prize, Huayu smiled and hurried off.

苦荞早已瞧见陈浩宇，懒得理他，准备回去，被陈浩宇叫住。
Spotting Chen Haoyu and wanting zero part of him, Kuqiao turned back inside—only for Chen Haoyu to yell after her:

“喂！你这丫头！看见本公子也不理会，如此没礼貌！赶紧的，给我把朱淑真叫出来，本公子有话跟她说。”
"Hey, little maid! Walking right past me without a greeting—no manners! Move it and summon Zhu Shuzhen; I need to talk to her!"

苦荞很不情愿地答道：“陈公子稍候。”
Scowling reluctantly, Kuqiao replied: "Wait here, Young Master Chen."

等待朱淑真的过程中，一个小弟问：“陈公子，您今日来找朱淑真，该不会是找她麻烦？”
While waiting, one crony asked, "Here to settle scores with Zhu Shuzhen today, Young Master Chen?"

陈浩宇笑道：“你们几个以后得经常跟我见面，否则都不了解本公子的情况。告诉你们，本公子与朱淑真已订亲……”
Chen Haoyu smirked: "You boys need to keep up—haven't heard the news? Zhu Shuzhen and I just got engaged..."

“什么什么？竟然有这等事？”几位小弟感到又意外又不解。
"WHAT?! No way!" Every crony's jaw dropped.

看着他们惊讶的样子，陈浩宇有些得意：“想不到吧？本公子怎么可能愿意？朱淑真也不乐意，所以本公子现在与她也算是一根绳上的蚂蚱，今日找她来商议商议怎么对付两家长辈。”
Enjoying their shock, Chen Haoyu chuckled: "Wild, right? Like hell I want her—and she hates it too! So we're two grasshoppers tied to one string now; came over to cook up a plan against our parents."

一位小弟说：“婚事定得蹊跷，陈公子能不能给我们几个详细说说？”
One crony probed: "Sounds fishy—how on earth did that betrothal happen, Young Master Chen?"

“去去去！你们几个没资格知道，滚远些！别影响我跟朱淑真商量正事！”
"Buzz off! Above your pay grade—stand back so you don't ruin our strategy talk!"

几个小弟都听话地躲到远处，谁让陈浩宇是他们的大财神，得罪不起。
Knowing better than to cross their meal ticket, the cronies scattered down the lane.

千里寻很快出来，瞧见陈浩宇，大声问道：“陈浩宇，事情办得如何？”
Qianli Xun marched out soon after, demanding loud: "Well, Chen Haoyu—how'd it go?"

陈浩宇一副得意的样子：“本公子出马，怎么说也八九不离十。”
Puffing out his chest: "When I step up, consider it almost done!"

千里寻兴致倍增：“你家长辈同意退婚？”
Hope flared in Qianli Xun: "Your elders agreed to break off the betrothal?!"

“退婚倒没同意……”
"Well, not break it off..."

“没同意！你那么得意干什么？等同意了再来找老娘。”千里寻说完准备转身离开。
"Not break it off—and you're grinning like that?! Come back when they agree!" Spinning on her heel, she started back inside.

陈浩宇一把将她抓住：“着急干吗？我的话还没说完。”
Chen Haoyu caught her sleeve: "Hold your horses—haven't finished talking!"

千里寻甩开陈浩宇：“别抓着老娘，有什么话赶紧说，我的时间很宝贵。”
Shaking his hand off: "Hands off! Talk fast—my time is precious!"

“我虽没说动家人同意退婚，可也有进展，我奶奶说既然婚退不了就耗着……”
"Couldn't get them to cancel it outright, yet I scored real progress: Grandmother ordered them to stall it..."

“耗着？什么意思？”千里寻十分不解。
"Stall it? What's that mean?" Qianli Xun frowned.

“意思就是到成亲的日子，我奶奶应该会想办法拖着，让婚事不能如期举行。”
"Means when our wedding date rolls up, Grandmother's going to drag her feet and block the ceremony."

“拖着有什么用？最终还不得嫁到陈家？”
"What good does stalling do if I still end up married to you eventually?!"

“怎么没用？陈家拖着，你这边再搞些事情出来，你爹没办法，说不定拖着拖着这婚自然就退了。”
"Plenty of good! My family stalls from our side while you raise hell from yours—push your dad long enough and the whole betrothal collapses on its own!"

“我搞些事情？怎么搞？”
"Me raise hell? How?"

“我给你出个主意，你可不能打我，你要是打我，我就不说，转头就走，让你以后嫁给我天天受苦。”
"Got a plan—just swear you won't hit me! Hit me and I walk right now, leaving you stuck married to me in misery!"

千里寻举起拳头：“说不说？不说我可真打了。”
Qianli Xun cocked a fist: "Spill it or eat knuckles right now!"

陈浩宇抬手将千里寻紧握的拳头压下去：“别动不动举拳头，哪像个大家闺秀。”
Reaching over, Chen Haoyu gently pushed her raised fist down: "Stop waving fists around—nothing like a noble lady!"

“挪开你的臭手！赶紧说！”
"Get your dirty paw off me and talk!"

陈浩宇乖乖将手挪开，千里寻也收了拳头。
Pulling his hand back fast as she lowered her fist, Chen Haoyu began:

“前一段时间，我被人打，受了重伤……”
"A few days ago I got jumped and beaten half to death..."

陈浩宇话还未说完，千里寻笑道：“呵呵……，没想到临安城内还有人和老娘一样讨厌你，改日可得结识结识。”
Before he finished, Qianli Xun burst out laughing: "Haha! Glad to know someone else in Lin'an hates your guts as much as I do—gotta meet that hero someday!"

“你能不能好好听人说话？不要随便打断我？”陈浩宇很不满，继续讲，“我被人打得受伤，就骗家人是你打的……”
"Will you let me finish without interrupting?!" Scowling, he pressed on: "While nursing my bruises, I told my family YOU beat me up..."

千里寻又举起拳头，对准陈浩宇：“你怎能随意污蔑老娘？”
Her fist shot straight back toward his nose: "How dare you frame me?!"

陈浩宇向一旁躲开：“我要不这么说，怎能说动家人？我奶奶又怎能想到耗着的办法？”
Ducking back a step: "How else was I supposed to freak my family out?! That's why Grandmother decided to stall!"

千里寻又放下拳头：“看在你是为了退婚，老娘暂且饶了你，继续讲！”
Lowering her fist again: "Since it's for breaking off this wedding, I'll let it slide. Go on!"

“我奶奶说，朱家姑娘什么时候把性子改了，什么时候成亲。你要做的就是别改你的性子，以后当着众人的面，尤其当着你爹的面见我就打，保准这婚结不了。”
"Grandmother swore no wedding until Miss Zhu fixes her violent temper! So all you have to do is stay wild—every time you see me in public, above all in front of your dad, beat me on sight! Betrothal dies guaranteed!"

“放心，我天生的性子，改不了，在众人面前打你，求之不得。只是得制造些能当着我爹的面打你的机会。”
"Piece of cake! Born with this temper, never changing—and beating you in public is pure pleasure! Just need chances right in front of my dad."

“不着急，以后我会帮你制造机会。”
"Leave setting up the stage to me!"

“看在你如此配合的份儿上，老娘不与你计较污蔑我的事，不过你得告诉我是谁打的你，我想与他结识结识。”
"Since you're playing ball, I'll forgive the framing. Tell me who beat you up, though—I want to shake their hand!"

陈浩宇不愿将仪王说出：“问那么多干什么？跟你又没关系，本公子还有正事，不跟你瞎耽误工夫。”
Refusing to breathe Prince Yi's name: "None of your business! Got real fun waiting—later!"

陈浩宇不再继续耽搁下去，带着小弟们离开。
Waving off, Chen Haoyu rounded up his cronies and strolled off.

“哼！你不告诉老娘，老娘迟早会知道。”
"Hmph—keep secrets all you want, I'll find out sooner or later!"

今日也算是收到好消息，陈家既然愿意拖着，千里寻自然配合。
Still, today counted as good news: if the Chens meant to stall, Qianli Xun would happily pile on fuel!

她已想好，以后不谈恋爱，不结婚，做喜欢做的事，再交几个知心好友，若有一日喜欢小孩子，便收养一个，等年迈走不动时，也可有人照顾床前。
Her mind was set for life now: no romance, no marriage, pour into her passions, cherish close friends, and maybe adopt a child one day if she wanted company in old age.

陈浩宇的堂哥陈浩东听说堂弟与朱家的亲事，既不解又担心，来到父亲陈江树的房中。
Meanwhile inside Chen Manor, hearing his cousin Haoyu had gotten betrothed to the Zhus stirred both puzzle and alarm in Chen Haodong, who went straight to his father Chen Jiangshu's room.

陈江树刚从商铺回来，换了衣裳，茶还没喝一口，见儿子进来，问道：“你今儿怎么有空来我这里？为何不在铺子待着好好学习做生意？”
Just home from inspecting shops and still waiting on his first sip of tea, Chen Jiangshu looked up: "Free today? Why aren't you at the shop learning the ropes?"

“爹不用担心，儿子学着呢。陈家这么大的产业，儿子不敢不用心。儿子今儿来是听说大伯给浩宇定下朱家的亲事，不知父亲可知？”陈浩东在父亲面前表现出毕恭毕敬的样子。
"Studying hard, Father—I'd never slack on an empire this big. Came over because I heard Eldest Uncle betrothed Haoyu to the Zhu family—did Father know?" Standing stiff and humble, Chen Haodong played the model son.

陈江树喝口茶，放下茶盏：“这么大的事，我岂能不知？两家亲上加亲，你大伯看上去很高兴。”
Taking a sip and setting his cup down, Chen Jiangshu nodded: "How could I miss news that big? Doubling our bond with the Zhus—your Eldest Uncle looks thrilled."

“我怎么听说那日知府大人绑了浩宇，手里还拿着鞭子冲进陈府，之后这婚事就定下，爹不觉得蹊跷？”
"Yet servants saw the Prefect haul Haoyu in bound in ropes with a whip right before the betrothal got sealed—doesn't that smell off, Father?"

“是听下人说了，也觉纳闷，侧面向你大伯打听过，他含糊其辞，说不出所以然，也就没再多问。”
"Heard the rumors and wondered too. Probed your Eldest Uncle gently, got dodgy answers, so I dropped it."

“爹，您说朱家姑娘被传成那样，谁家还愿意娶？为何大伯会同意给自家儿子娶那样的？会不会是浩宇做下什么错事，被朱家抓住把柄，大伯才不得不同意？”
"Think about it, Father: with Miss Zhu's reputation dragged through the mud, who else wanted her? Why did Eldest Uncle jump at her? Bet Haoyu screwed up bad, handed the Zhus dirt on him, and forced Eldest Uncle's hand!"

“因为什么与我们无关，关键是，浩宇娶朱家姑娘对我们来讲利弊如何。”
"Why doesn't matter—all that matters is whether Haoyu marrying Miss Zhu helps or hurts US."

“爹考虑的是，按说与朱家结亲对浩宇继承陈家产业有利，可朱家姑娘在外名声不好，浩宇娶她似乎又不利。儿子还听说，朱家姑娘拳头比男子厉害，还与浩宇结下梁子，这两人要是成亲，以后可有好戏看。”
"Dead right, Father! On paper, tying closer to the Prefect boosts Haoyu's claim to inherit the Chen empire—yet Miss Zhu's rotten name cuts the other way! Plus I heard Miss Zhu packs a meaner punch than most men and already has bad blood with Haoyu—watch sparks fly if they actually wed!"

“没想到我儿考虑问题越来越全面。”陈江树赞赏完，问道，“你怎么知道朱家姑娘跟浩宇结下梁子？”
"Sharp analysis—my boy's seeing the whole board now!" Beaming, Chen Jiangshu asked, "How'd you learn Miss Zhu and Haoyu have bad blood?"

陈浩东笑道：“爹有所不知，浩宇身边那些跟他鬼混的人均是些见钱眼开之人，儿子稍微使些钱财，他们什么话都对我讲。”
Chen Haodong smirked: "Father wouldn't know—those freeloaders trailing Haoyu sell their mothers for a coin! Grease a palm and they sing like birds."

“懂得使手段达到目的，不错，算我没白教你。我问你，你是想浩宇与朱家的亲事成还是不成？”
"Smart using tools to get intel—didn't waste my lessons on you! Tell me: do you want Haoyu's wedding to happen or blow up?"
