第276章 金兰怒刺少夫人
## Chapter 276: Jinlan Strikes at Young Madam

两人逛得累，想着还要从西边走到东边，面露难色。正犹豫间，陈府的下人抬着两顶轿子过来停在旁边，她们立刻面露笑容。
春金沫问：“轿子可是给我们乘坐的？”
下人回道：“正是，二少夫人担心二位腿脚走累，让下人抬着你们过去。”
两人对视一笑，各自走上一顶轿子。下人抬起轿子，腿脚倒也利索，不多会儿来到离东边亭子不远处，将轿子放下。
下人说：“二位，到了。”
两人走下轿子，向四周瞧瞧，根本没有亭子。
春金兰不满：“哪有什么亭子？该不会是骗我们？”
下人回道：“二位顺着这条小路直走，该拐弯的时候拐弯，自然看到亭子。”
小路正好容下两人，她们肩并肩走着。
春金沫叹道：“今日你我终于见识到什么是富贵人家。”
春金兰问：“难道钱塘帮都比不上陈家？”
“钱塘帮不过是黑帮，有钱也谈不上体面，陈家则不一样，既有钱又体面，何况钱塘帮哪有陈家有钱？”
Footsore from walking, both women grimaced at the trek from the west wing all the way east—until servants trotted up bearing two sedan chairs and set them down beside them. Smiles bloomed on their faces at once.

“姐姐后悔了？”
“Are those sedan chairs for us?” Chun Jinmo asked.

“后悔谈不上，只是不甘心，凭什么我真心待他，他却甩下我另娶他人？”
“Yes,” the servant replied. “Second Young Madam worried your feet might tire, so she sent bearers to carry you.”

“我也不甘心。”
Exchanging smug smiles, they climbed aboard. The bearers moved briskly and soon set them down near the eastern garden. “We’re here, ladies.”

“今日就看妹妹的。”
Stepping out, they looked around and saw no pavilion in sight.

亭子已出现在眼前，二人不能再聊下去。她们顺着小路，靠近亭子，还是那些人，只是多了陈浩宇而已，其他人都围着亭中的石桌坐着，唯独陈浩宇站着。
Chun Jinlan frowned. “Where’s the pavilion? Are you playing tricks on us?”

坐着的人中并没有叶安雅，她心里惦记与曹班的约会，没有心思关注浩宇的事儿，趁众人去亭子，随便找个理由离开。
“Follow this footpath straight ahead and take the bend,” the servant said. “You’ll see it right away.”

包氏等着看热闹，懒得理会儿媳妇，走了也好，省得在眼前她看着来气。
Walking shoulder to shoulder down the narrow path, Chun Jinmo sighed. “Today we’ve finally seen what real wealth looks like.”

再次见到陈公子，春金沫百感交集，走上前：“陈公子，我终于见到你。”
“Even the Qiantang Gang doesn’t measure up?” Chun Jinlan asked.

陈浩宇瞧向娘子，她将头扭向一边，显然不高兴，陈浩宇下意识向后退一步：“听说你已嫁给蒋爷，还是别这么对我说话。”
“The Qiantang Gang is an underworld outfit—money doesn’t buy class. The Chens have both wealth and prestige, and the gang doesn’t have half their money anyway.”

春金沫不再继续表达感情，将春金兰拉到陈浩宇面前：“陈公子可还记得她？”
“Do you regret it, Sister?”

陈浩宇当然有印象：“我记得你，只是忘记叫什么，怪我当初不懂事，从乡下抢了你。只记得把你锁在陈家乡下的宅子里，后来你不知怎么就不见了，我以为你逃走，懒得追究，今儿为何又跑来？”
“Not regret—just resentment. I gave him my heart, so why did he dump me and marry somebody else?”

听到此话，春金兰怒不可遏，扑向陈浩宇，揪住他的衣裳，眼中含着泪：“你害我好惨！竟然装作不知？我被你糟蹋，卖到扬州的青楼，我本也是好人家的姑娘，被你害得生不如死！你今日要么纳我为妾，要么你死！要么你的娘子死！要么我死在你面前！”
“I resent it too.”

凭千里寻的功力，本可以冲上去帮陈浩宇解围，可她并不想帮他，自己造下的孽，还需自己承受，他人都替代不了。
“Today’s all on you, Sister.”

姚轻琰受不了，想上前训斥春金兰，被夏老太太阻止。老太太虽疼孙子，可他惹下大事，老人家心寒，也想让孙子吃些苦头。
The pavilion came into view, cutting their chatter short. The same family members sat around the stone table, only now Chen Haoyu stood in their midst—and Ye Anya was missing, having slipped away on a flimsy excuse to salvage her rendezvous with Cao Ban. Eager for the drama ahead, Madam Bao hadn’t bothered stopping her.

包氏心中欢喜不已，希望再闹得大些。不过，面上还要装出关心浩宇的样子，故意训斥道：“这是陈府，岂容你如此无礼？”雷声大雨点小，屁股连石凳都没挪开。
Seeing Young Master Chen again stirred a storm in Chun Jinmo’s chest. She stepped forward. “Young Master Chen, we meet at last.”

崔氏见老太太拦住大嫂，也熄了火，更庆幸自己有个好儿子。
Catching his wife turning her head away in displeasure, Chen Haoyu took an instinctive step back. “I hear you married Master Jiang. Better not talk to me like that.”

春金沫站在一旁像看热闹一般，根本没有宽慰劝解春金兰的意思，相反春金兰闹得越厉害她越开心，谁让春金兰做了她想做又做不出的事。
Dropping the sentiment, Chun Jinmo pulled Chun Jinlan forward. “Do you still remember her, Young Master Chen?”

陈浩宇觉得与娘子失望的表情相比，春金兰拽住他怒骂根本不算什么，他继续辩解：“你胡说！我根本没动过你！也没有把你卖到青楼！”
Recognition flickered across his face. “I remember you, just forgot your name. My fault for acting wild back then and snatching you from the countryside. All I recall is locking you in our country residence—next thing I knew you vanished, so I figured you escaped and didn’t bother chasing you. What are you doing turning up now?”

春金兰像疯了一样，双手往陈浩宇脖子上掐：“你竟然不承认！我……我当着众人的面掐死你！”
Fury exploded in Chun Jinlan. She lunged at him, fistfuls of his robe in her grip, tears spilling over. “You ruined my life and dare play dumb?! You defiled me and sold me to a Yangzhou brothel! I was a decent girl, and you made my life a living hell! Today either you take me as your concubine, or you die—or your wife dies—or I die right in front of you!”

陈浩宇忍了一会儿，脖子被掐得难受，一脚将春金兰踢开，一边揉着脖子一边骂：“你个泼妇！只要是本公子做过的事没有不敢承认的！”
With her skills, Qianli Xun could have stepped in and bailed him out in a blink, yet she didn’t lift a finger. You reap what you sow—nobody else could pay his debts for him.

春金兰被踢倒在地，嚎啕大哭：“呜呜……！陈浩宇！你就是个畜生！夺去我的贞洁，呜呜！还让人把我装进麻袋，卖到扬州青楼，呜呜！我还清楚地记得，卖我的人脸上有道疤痕，你称呼他疤子，呜呜……！”
Yao Qingyan started up to scold the girl, only for Old Madam Xia to hold her back. Much as she doted on her grandson, this mess chilled her heart; letting him take a few knocks would do him good.

春金沫终于说话：“陈公子，疤子经常跟着你混，既是他把金兰带到扬州，必然与你有关，不如老实承认，把金兰放在身边当个妾室，对大家都好。”
Overjoyed inside, Madam Bao put on a show of outrage without budging an inch from her stone stool. “This is Chen Manor! How dare you act so wild!”

脖子舒服一些，陈浩宇放下手，走到春金兰身边，又把她仔细瞧一遍：“我陈浩宇玩儿过的女人都印象深刻，而你，实在没有动过你的任何印象。比如春金沫，我去春香楼只找她，没什么不好承认的，即使当着我娘子的面，也不会否认。”
Seeing the Old Madam restrain her eldest sister-in-law, Madam Cui stayed put, gladder than ever for her own sensible son.

千里寻十分不屑，心中骂道：“头一次见有人做下错事，还把承认错误当成一种荣耀！”
Chun Jinmo stood off to the side watching like a spectator at a play—the wilder Chun Jinlan got, the happier she was, since Jinlan was unleashing everything she herself never dared.

春金兰继续哭着：“呜呜！你既不承认，我也不想活了！”她快速爬起来，向亭中的柱子冲去……
Compared to the disappointment on his wife’s face, Chun Jinlan’s tirade meant nothing to Chen Haoyu. He insisted, “You’re talking nonsense! I never laid a finger on you, and I never sold you to any brothel!”

众人被眼前的情景吓住，陈浩宇也不知所措。
Screaming like a madwoman, Chun Jinlan wrapped both hands around his throat. “Still denying it?! I’ll strangle you right in front of everyone!”

千钧一发之际，千里寻迅速上前抓住春金兰的手臂，一把将她拽回，用另一只手箍住她的上身：“想死在陈家没那么容易！”
He endured it a few seconds until his airway tightened, then kicked her away and rubbed his neck. “You crazy shrew! If I did something, I own up to it!”

春金沫趁机向春金兰使眼色，只见春金兰趁千里寻不注意时，从头上拔出簪子向箍着手臂刺去……
Sprawled on the paving stones, Chun Jinlan wailed, “Waaah! Chen Haoyu, you’re an animal! You stole my virtue, stuffed me in a sack, and sold me to a Yangzhou brothel! I still remember the man who sold me—big scar across his face, and you called him Scarface!”

陈浩宇第一个发现，担心娘子受伤，立刻冲过去，下意识将自己的手臂伸了出去，春金兰手中的簪子正好戳在他的手臂上，只见鲜血流出。
Chun Jinmo jumped in. “Scarface always ran with you, Young Master Chen. If he hauled Jinlan to Yangzhou, you had to be behind it. Better own up and take Jinlan as a concubine—it’s best for everyone.”

姚轻琰再也忍不住冲到儿子跟前，扶着他：“浩宇！呜呜！快去拿药！”
His throat easing, Chen Haoyu dropped his hand, walked over, and studied Chun Jinlan’s face. “I remember every woman I’ve ever touched, and I have zero memory of touching you. Take Chun Jinmo—when I went to Chunxiang Brothel, I only saw her. I’m not afraid to admit it, not even in front of my wife!”

两位婶婶也到跟前，问他疼不疼。
Qianli Xun rolled her eyes inside. *First time I’ve seen a guy brag about owning up to his sleazy track record like it’s a badge of honor!*

老太太腿脚慢些，被丫鬟扶着过去，看到簪子戳进孙子的手臂内，心疼不已，大吼道：“够了！”
Chun Jinlan sobbed louder. “If you won’t admit it, I don’t want to live!” Scrambling up, she charged headfirst at a pavilion pillar.

要不是陈浩宇，那簪子必然戳在千里寻的手臂上，她虽谈不上感激，却也为之动容。一时无法松开春金兰，否则她又不知会做出什么事来，只能用目光照拂陈浩宇受伤的手臂。
Gasps erupted; Chen Haoyu froze.

只见他狠狠心将簪子从手臂拔出来，一股鲜血涌出来，陈浩宇忍着痛被姚轻琰扶到一边。
In a split second, Qianli Xun shot forward, caught Chun Jinlan’s arm, yanked her back, and pinned her upper body against her. “Think you can die in Chen Manor that easy? Not on my watch!”

陈公子受伤，春金沫多少感到难过，可也羡慕女词人，如此看来，陈公子对她的确全心全意。如若有人前来刺她，陈公子多半会置之不理。
Chun Jinmo flashed a look. Taking advantage of Qianli Xun’s focus, Chun Jinlan plucked a hairpin from her bun and jabbed straight at the arm pinning her.

夏老太太对身边的丫鬟说：“把绳子取来！”
Chen Haoyu spotted it first. Terrified his wife would get stabbed, he threw himself forward and thrust his own arm in the way. The hairpin sank straight into his flesh, blood welling at once.

丫鬟赶紧去拿绳子。
Yao Qingyan rushed to her son’s side in tears. “Haoyu! Bring medicine—hurry!” Both aunts crowded in asking if it hurt.

老太太走到春金兰身边：“我在一旁观察半天，算是看出来，你今儿不闹出个人命绝不会罢休。”
Hobbling over on a maid’s arm, Old Madam Xia saw the pin jutting from her grandson’s arm and roared in heartbreak, “Enough!”

春金兰嘴上并不饶人：“我也可以不闹出人命，只要把我放在陈公子身边当妾室。”
If not for Chen Haoyu, that pin would have buried itself in her own arm. Hard as it was to call it gratitude, a tremor stirred in Qianli Xun’s chest. Unable to let go of Chun Jinlan lest she lash out again, she kept her eyes locked on his bleeding arm.

“呸！”夏老太太啐道，“即使真是我孙儿做下错事，我宁可将他赶出陈府也绝不会把你这号人放在他身边！”
Gritting his teeth, Chen Haoyu yanked the hairpin out. Blood spurted, and Yao Qingyan helped him aside.

春金兰说：“那你就等着！要么我死！要么陈浩宇，或者我身后这位，随便死一个都可解我心头之恨！”
Watching him bleed stung Chun Jinmo a little, yet envy stung sharper—clearly Young Master Chen cared for the poetess with his whole heart. If someone stabbed at her, he wouldn’t have budged.

丫鬟已将绳子拿来，夏老太太吩咐道：“淑真，把她绑起来。”
Old Madam Xia barked at a maid, “Fetch rope!” Turning to Chun Jinlan, she said, “I’ve watched you long enough to know you won’t quit until somebody ends up dead.”

千里寻一只手接过绳子，苦荞过来帮忙，三下五除二将春金兰绑了。
“I won’t kill anyone,” Chun Jinlan shot back, “so long as you make me Young Master Chen’s concubine!”

春金兰大吼道：“你们陈家仗着有钱为非作歹，欺负人！小心我告到官府，让官老爷把你们都抓起来！”
“Bah!” Old Madam Xia spat. “Even if my grandson did wrong you, I’d sooner kick him out of Chen Manor than put someone like you beside him!”

千里寻绑完，揉揉手掌心：“瞎说什么？你像疯子一样胡闹，都快闹出人命，把你绑起来都是轻的，官老爷即使来，抓的也是你！”
“Then wait and see! Either I die, or Chen Haoyu dies, or the woman behind me dies—any one of us will satisfy my grudge!”

春金沫在一旁看了许久，也有些许担忧，尤其是春金兰被绑起来什么都做不了，她总得说些什么：“老太太将妹妹绑起来是什么道理？难道天下连王法都没有？”
When the maid ran back with rope, Old Madam Xia ordered, “Tie her up, Shuzhen.”

夏老太太气不打一处来：“你口口声声称她为妹妹，你算哪门子姐姐？哪有姐姐看着妹妹闹事在一旁置之不理？我看八成是你撺掇她来闹事。”
Taking the rope one-handed, Qianli Xun trussed Chun Jinlan up in seconds with Kuqiao’s help.

“老太太错怪我，我是可怜妹妹才陪她来。”
Chun Jinlan yelled, “Rich bullies throwing your weight around! Watch me sue you at the yamen and have the magistrate lock you all up!”

“哼！可怜？说得好听！我刚才也大略听明白，你那妹妹认为浩宇害了她，浩宇并不承认，此事陈家人无法解决，不如报官，让官府来查，若查出错在浩宇，我们陈家认栽，任凭官府处置；若浩宇并未做过这位姑娘说的那些事，还请你们远离陈家。”
Rubbing her palms, Qianli Xun scoffed, “Talk sense! Carrying on like a lunatic and almost killing someone—tying you up is letting you off light! If the magistrate shows up, you’re the one getting hauled in!”

春金兰人被绑着，嘴上功夫依然强硬：“报官就报官！我还怕了不成？”
Growing nervous now that Chun Jinlan was bound helpless, Chun Jinmo felt compelled to chime in. “What right does Old Madam have to tie up my sister? Is there no law in the land?”

在丫鬟的搀扶下，夏老太太坐回石凳：“去给王管家说一声，让他安排下人把三位老爷找回来，再告诉大公子让他去趟司理院，就说陈家出了案子，需参军大人到府中走一趟。”
Old Madam Xia snapped, “Calling her ‘sister’ every other word—what kind of sister stands by watching while she raises hell? Ten to one you put her up to this!”

下人已将药拿来，姚轻琰正在一旁帮浩宇上药，儿子被簪子刺得严重，她没有心情关心老太太说些什么。
“You wrong me, Old Madam—I only came out of pity for her.”

崔氏听到老太太要请司理参军上门，担心地叮嘱道：“母亲，现在的司理参军可是仪王，我们怎么敢请贵人上门替陈家办案？”
“Pity? Sounds noble enough! From what I’ve gathered, your sister accuses Haoyu of ruining her, and Haoyu denies it. Our family can’t settle this—better report it to the yamen and let the officials investigate. If Haoyu is guilty, the Chens will accept whatever punishment the law hands down. If he didn’t do it, stay far away from Chen Manor!”

老太太解释道：“无妨，仪王既然主动担任司理参军，必然不会心胸狭窄，介意来百姓家中办案，况且他又不是没来过。再说我们总不能绑着她亲自去司理院，她毕竟也没犯法。”
Bound tight, Chun Jinlan kept her chin up. “Call the officials, then! Think I’m scared?”

崔氏强调道：“她伤了浩宇。”
Supported back onto her stone stool, Old Madam Xia gave orders. “Tell Butler Wang to fetch all three Masters home, and tell Eldest Young Master to go to the Judicial Office—say Chen Manor has a case that needs the Judicial Investigator to come in person.”

夏老太太叹道：“要不是浩宇当初做下傻事，岂有今日之事？”
Medicine arrived, and Yao Qingyan bent over her son’s arm, too distraught to pay attention to anything else.

春金兰说道：“老太太总算说句明事理的话，要不是陈浩宇当初抓我，就不会有后来的事，我今日也不会前来闹事，所以罪魁祸首就是陈浩宇！”
Hearing the Old Madam summon the Judicial Investigator, Madam Cui warned in worry, “Mother, the Judicial Investigator right now is Prince Yi! How dare we summon a royal to handle a family case?”

伤口上好药，也用布子绑好，依然很疼，陈浩宇忍着痛，不想说一句话。从昨日到今日，以前惹下的事终于结出恶果，还是在他全心全意爱上娘子的时候。看来人真的不能做坏事，恶报迟早会显现。
“No harm done,” Old Madam Xia replied. “Since Prince Yi took the post of his own accord, he won’t be petty about visiting a commoner’s house—besides, it’s not his first visit here. And we can’t very well haul a tied-up woman to the Judicial Office when she hasn’t been charged.”

他悄悄瞧向娘子，她没有继续坐下，站在一旁，既不说话，也懒得理他，低眼垂眉，像是有心事的样子，难道是因为一会儿仪王会来？
“She stabbed Haoyu!” Madam Cui pointed out.

陈浩宇觉得肯定是这样。
Old Madam Xia sighed. “If Haoyu hadn’t pulled such foolish stunts back then, none of this would’ve happened.”

他没有亲眼见过仪王与朱淑真在一起时亲密的样子，可他却莫名其妙地感觉出她心中的那个人一定是仪王，而不是乔文仙。
“Now there’s a sensible word!” Chun Jinlan sneered. “If Chen Haoyu hadn’t abducted me, none of the rest would’ve followed. He’s the culprit!”

他很担心娘子会因为这两日的事想弃他而去，毕竟她和离过一次，会不会也会像第一次一样想方设法离开他？
His arm salved and bandaged, Chen Haoyu endured the throbbing pain in silence. Yesterday and today, every ugly seed from his past was bearing bitter fruit—right when he had fallen head over heels for his wife. Doing wrong really did catch up with you in the end.

陈浩宇越想越担忧，却不敢上前与娘子说话。
He stole a glance at his wife. Standing off to the side with eyes lowered and brows drawn, ignoring him altogether, she looked deep in thought. Was it because Prince Yi was coming?

春金沫见陈府的下人已按照老太太说的去办，待会儿陈家的老爷们会回来，连官府的人也要来，她实在不想待下去，事情惹大，只怕会把自己给搭进去。于是对春金兰说：“妹妹，姐姐现在已为人妻，不比从前，只能陪你到这里，家中还有事，我需回去一趟。”
It had to be. He had never seen Prince Yi and Zhu Shuzhen act intimate with his own eyes, yet something in his gut told him the man in her heart was Prince Yi, not Qiao Wenxian. Dread tightened his chest: after these two days, would she want out? She had walked away from one marriage via mutual divorce already—would she find a way to leave him too? He couldn’t muster the nerve to say a word to her.

然后凑近春金兰耳边：“无论你做什么，姐姐都支持你，即使你今日动不了她，迟早有人会解决她。”
Seeing the servants hurry off to fetch the Chen Masters and the authorities, Chun Jinmo decided it was time to bail before the scandal dragged her under. She told Chun Jinlan, “Sister, I’m a married woman now, not free like before. This is as far as I can stay—I’ve got things waiting at home.” Leaning close to her ear, she murmured, “Whatever you do, I back you. Even if you can’t touch her today, someone else will finish her off soon enough.”

春金兰回道：“有劳姐姐，你快些回去，我一人足以应对。”
