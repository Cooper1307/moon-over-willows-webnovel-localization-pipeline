## Chapter 276: Jinlan Strikes at Second Young Madam

Seeing the two women tired from walking across the massive estate, Chen servants arrived with twin sedan chairs sent thoughtfully by Second Young Madam to carry them to the east pavilion.

Stepping down along the narrow garden path, Chun Jinmo sighed at how refined Chen wealth made even the Qiantang Gang look crude. Reaching the pavilion, they found the whole family seated around the stone table—minus Ye Anya, who had slipped away to fret over Cao Ban—with Chen Haoyu standing alone in the center.

Seeing Haoyu's wife glaring away, Haoyu stepped back when Chun Jinmo greeted him warmly. "You're Master Jiang's wife now—watch your tone."

Dragging Chun Jinlan forward, Chun Jinmo challenged, "Remember her?"

Haoyu squinted. "Yeah, forgot your name. Stupid mistake back then—snatched you from a village and locked you in our country house. When I came back you'd run off, so I dropped it. Why show up now?"

With a shriek of fury, Chun Jinlan lunged at his robes. "Playing dumb after ruining me and selling me to a Yangzhou brothel?! Today either you take me as concubine, or you die, your wife dies, or I die right here!"

Qianli Xun stayed planted in her seat—his karma, his mess—and Old Madam Xia held Yao Qingyan back to let her spoiled grandson face the music, while Madam Bao cheered silently behind a token scold.

"You're lying! I never touched you and never sold you!" Haoyu protested until Jinlan's fingers locked around his throat, forcing him to kick her clear.

Wailing on the stones, Jinlan shrieked, "You beast! Your own lackey Scarface shoved me in a sack and sold me to Yangzhou!"

"Scarface ran with you—admit it and take her in!" Chun Jinmo egged on.

Rubbing his throat, Haoyu shook his head. "I remember every woman I ever slept with—like Chun Jinmo, I won't deny her even in front of my wife! Yet I have zero memory of touching *you*!"

*Bragging about owning up to sleaze—unbelievable!* Qianli Xun rolled her eyes.

"Fine—since you deny it, I'll end it right now!" Springing up, Chun Jinlan sprinted headfirst toward a stone pillar!

In a blur, Qianli Xun shot forward, caught her arm, and locked her tight from behind—"Nobody dies on Chen grounds!"

Catching Chun Jinmo's sharp wink, Jinlan whipped a hairpin straight out of her bun and stabbed back at Qianli Xun's arm!

Spotting the glint first, Haoyu lunged and threw his own forearm straight across the strike—the pin sank deep into his flesh, spilling crimson blood!

Yao Qingyan burst forward sobbing for medicine while Old Madam Xia roared, "Enough!"

Moved despite herself—that pin had been aimed straight at her—Qianli Xun held Jinlan pinned while Haoyu gritted his teeth and yanked the bloody hairpin out himself. Even Chun Jinmo felt a stab of jealous shock seeing Haoyu take a blade for his wife.

"Fetch rope!" Old Madam Xia ordered. Ignoring Jinlan's screeching threats of murder or suicide, Qianli Xun and Kuqiao trussed her tight in three seconds flat.

When Chun Jinmo dared complain about "royal law," Old Madam Xia cut her down cold: "Call yourself her sister while encouraging her to spill blood? Fine—since she blames Haoyu and he denies it, let the yamen investigate! If Haoyu's guilty, we take our punishment; if he's innocent, get out of our sight! Summon the three masters home, and send Eldest Young Master Haodong to the Judicial Office to invite the Investigator here!"

Not minding that the Investigator was Prince Yi—he'd handled Steward Fang's case right here before—Old Madam Xia stood firm.

Silently nursing his bandaged arm, Haoyu stole bleak glances at his wife's hushed, downcast face, terrified his old sins would drive her to divorce him just like she'd left Lin Jue.

Realizing the yamen was coming and refusing to get dragged down, Chun Jinmo murmured false encouragement into Jinlan's ear, made her excuses—throwing in a threat of Master Jiang's backing—and sneered parting venom at Qianli Xun: "Think Haoyu's reformed forever? Men always get bored and stray back out—enjoy your empty bed!"

"Shut your trap, Chun Jinmo!" Haoyu roared. "My heart for my wife will never change!"

"Call me *Madam Jiang*," Chun Jinmo snapped bitterly, swaying toward the exit—until Qianli Xun's ringing voice halted her:

"Courtesy goes two ways, Madam Jiang—I haven't said *my* piece yet!"
