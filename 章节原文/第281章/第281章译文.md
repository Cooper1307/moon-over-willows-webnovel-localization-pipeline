## Chapter 281: Two Contradictory Testimonies

Next morning, Shen Jianchen reopened court with Chen Jianglin and Chen Haodong watching from the gallery—glad Qianli Xun hadn't come to endure the ugly details.

Three witnesses knelt: Scarface, plus two young masters from Haoyu's old barbecue-and-chuiwan crowd.

The two young masters testified clearly: on the third day of the third month last year—the roast-meat dandy's birthday—they summoned Haoyu back from the country estate, and he caroused with them in the entertainment quarters straight through dawn!

Jinlan shrieked that Haoyu's wife had bribed them, earning a sharp clapper crack and a twenty-board warning from Shen Jianchen.

Then Scarface took the floor—confessing he'd sold Jinlan to Yangzhou (nearly getting mauled by a lunging Jinlan before constables pinned her down), yet swearing blind that Haoyu had slipped back out to the country house *in the middle of the night*, assaulted Jinlan, and ordered him to bag and sell her—claiming the other lackeys had already gone home leaving only him!

Catching the glaring clash—and trusting the disinterested partygoers far more than the human trafficker—Shen Jianchen asked Haoyu whether servants lived at that country villa. Across the gallery, Chen Haodong's tea bowl jittered, spilling drops down his sleeve as he silently assured himself the servants had all been transferred and the lone estate steward had been dead drunk.

Suspending the trial to hunt hard evidence, Shen Jianchen watched Chen Jianglin bow in thanks—then turned a sharp probe straight onto tense Chen Haodong. "Which witness do you think is lying?"

"Brotherhood makes me hope Scarface is lying!" Haodong bluffed smoothly.

"Good—then I'll dig up proof Scarface lied. Ever heard the saying *trouble starts inside one's own walls*, Master Chen? Fits great clans just as well as imperial houses."

Riding home in silence, Chen Jianglin mulled that cryptic warning—then dismissed it. With Haofei aiming for officialdom and Haoyu disqualified by scandal, Haodong already had the inheritance in the bag; why would he sabotage his own cousin?
