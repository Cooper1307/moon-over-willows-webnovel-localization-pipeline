第281章 两种矛盾的证词
## Chapter 281: Contradictory Testimony

第二天，温都头差人将证人找来带到司理院。一切就绪后，申简辰继昨日之后再次审理陈浩宇的案子。
陈家来了陈江林和陈浩东二人，他们坐在差役身后专为旁听之人准备的位置。
千里寻未出现，申简辰觉得甚好，省得她听到更多细节心里难过。
陈浩宇与春金兰被差役带进来，乖乖跪在地上。
温信大声说：“将证人带进来！”
只见两名差役带着三名证人走进来，有疤子，还有两名与陈浩宇一起在捶丸场和西湖烤过肉的公子。
证人都跪下，一起说：“拜见大人！”
申简辰先问两位公子：“去年春天可有一日你们将陈浩宇从乡下叫回城里？”
曾在捶丸场调戏过红豆的炙肉男先开口：“记得记得，那天我生日，三月初三，想把朋友们聚到一起。听说陈公子去了乡下的宅子，没他不热闹，便派人将他从乡下叫回来。”
申简辰继续问：“他跟你们待了一晚上？”
另一位公子说：“回大人，那晚我们在勾栏瓦肆玩了一个通宵，陈公子一直与我们在一起玩儿到天亮，还在瓦子里睡了一会儿才离开。”
两位公子的证词显然对陈浩宇有利，春金兰一心认为是女词人买通证人，急切地说：“大人，您不能听他们一面之词，他们一定是拿了陈浩宇的银子，才向着他说话！”
The next morning, Chief Constable Wen brought in the witnesses, and Shen Jianchen reopened Chen Haoyu’s hearing.

陈浩宇强辩道：“我昨晚被关着，哪有机会去找他们？”
Chen Jianglin and Chen Haodong attended from the spectators’ seats behind the constables. Qianli Xun stayed away—just as well, Shen Jianchen thought, sparing her the ugly details.

春金兰说：“你娘子有的是时间和机会！难道她不会帮你收买人心？”
Constables escorted Chen Haoyu and Chun Jinlan in to kneel.

有人诋毁千里寻，申简辰决不允许，他将惊堂木一拍“啪！”：“若再吵闹下去，一人二十大板！”
“Bring in the witnesses!” Wen Xin called.

春金兰吓得不敢再言语。
Two constables led three men forward: Scarface, plus two young masters who had grilled meat by West Lake and played chuiwan with Chen Haoyu. All three knelt. “Greetings, Your Lordship!”

申简辰又问疤子：“你是疤子？”
Shen Jianchen questioned the two young masters first. “One day last spring, did you send for Chen Haoyu to return from the countryside?”

疤子回道：“小的是。”
The barbecue enthusiast who had once flirted with Hongdou spoke up. “Remember it clear as day! My birthday, the third day of the third month. Wanted the gang together, heard Young Master Chen was out at his country house, and figured it wouldn’t be lively without him—so I sent someone to fetch him back.”

“经常跟着陈浩宇混？”
“Did he stay with you all night?”

“以前是，现在陈公子不愿带我们，没再跟他混。”
The second young master answered. “Yes, Your Lordship! We partied all night in the entertainment quarters—Young Master Chen stayed with us till dawn and even napped there before heading off.”

“可认得跪着的女子？”
With their testimony favoring Chen Haoyu, Chun Jinlan was sure the poetess had bought them off and burst out, “Don’t believe them, Your Lordship! They must’ve taken Chen Haoyu’s silver to lie for him!”

疤子早已看到春金兰，老实地点点头：“认得，还是我把她卖到扬州青楼。”
“I was locked up all night!” Chen Haoyu shot back. “When did I get a chance to see them?”

疤子进入堂内的一刻，春金兰就充满仇恨，因大老爷在，暂时忍了。此时疤子承认，她再也忍不下去，正好疤子跪在她身旁，冲过去用拳头砸在疤子的身上。
“Your wife had plenty of time and chances! Wouldn’t she buy them off for you?”

“你害我好惨！我要杀了你！”
Shen Jianchen would not let anyone smear Qianli Xun. Crack! His gavel slammed the desk. “Another outburst and you each get twenty strokes of the paddle!”

疤子不敢动手，直往一旁躲，还是被她打在身上。那女人劲儿不大，不是很疼，可春金兰跟疯了一样，竟然把头伸过来要咬他，还好上来两名差役将她拉开。
Chun Jinlan clammed up in fright.

差役把春金兰按在地上。
Shen Jianchen turned to Scarface. “You’re Scarface?”

申简辰说：“春金兰，你再胡闹，我就让人把你绑起来。”
“Yes, Your Lordship.”

春金兰忍着怒气不敢再闹腾，两名差役才又回到原位站好。
“Used to run with Chen Haoyu?”

申简辰问道：“疤子，你为何要把她卖到扬州青楼？”
“Used to. Young Master Chen won’t hang with us anymore.”

疤子开始胡说：“陈公子见她美貌，没忍住把她给糟蹋了，可她脾气不好，实在不好玩儿，陈公子极其嫌弃，将她打晕，让我拉到扬州给卖了。”
“Recognize the woman kneeling here?”

申简辰又问：“可是去年三月初三那晚？”
Scarface nodded straightaway. “Sure do—I’m the one who sold her to the Yangzhou brothel.”

疤子肯定道：“正是，我清楚地记得，陈公子说三月初三带我们去乡下放风筝。我们当时正在放风筝，发现她在河边洗衣裳，陈公子吩咐哥几个悄悄把她抓进宅子里。”
Seething since Scarface walked in, Chun Jinlan snapped at his admission. Since he knelt right beside her, she threw herself sideways and rained fists on him. “You ruined my life! I’ll kill you!”

春金兰虽恨疤子，可他的证词显然对她有利，怒气便消了许多。
Not daring to hit back, Scarface dodged and took the weak blows—until she lunged to sink her teeth into him and two constables hauled her off and pinned her down.

又换陈浩宇着急，他冲着疤子说：“本公子平日待你不薄，你怎么满嘴谎言？明明那日我回城里，叮嘱你们几个帮我看着她，等我第二日回来再……，该不会是你做下龌龊事，又把人给卖了，赖到我身上？”
“One more stunt, Chun Jinlan, and you go back in ropes,” Shen Jianchen warned. She bit back her rage, and the constables stepped back.

疤子继续胡诌：“陈公子是待我不薄，我也记着您的好，可一码归一码，公堂之上我不能欺瞒大人，需照实说。再说陈公子您抢来的人，我怎么敢动？”
“Scarface, why did you sell her to a Yangzhou brothel?”

春金兰趁机说：“大人，疤子亲自将我卖到扬州青楼，他的证词最可信。”
Scarface launched right into his lie. “Young Master Chen saw how pretty she was, couldn’t hold back, and forced himself on her. Only she had a nasty temper and wasn’t any fun, so he got disgusted, knocked her out, and told me to haul her to Yangzhou and sell her.”

炙肉男疑惑道：“不对啊，明明那晚陈公子跟我们在一起，难道他有分身术？”
“Was that the night of the third day of the third month last year?”

三个证人，两种说辞，其中肯定有人做了假证。
“Sure was! Remember clear as day—Young Master Chen took us out to fly kites that day, spotted her washing clothes by the creek, and had the boys snatch her into the country house.”

申简辰仔细观察，认真思索，一个人说谎往往是为了对自己有利的目的。
Much as she despised Scarface, his words backed her up, cooling her anger.

他认为两位公虽不着调，证词却十分可信，只因此事与二人没有利益牵扯，他们没有必要编谎言欺骗人。而疤子与此事息息相关，还是他亲自赶马车将春金兰卖到扬州，说不定真如陈浩宇所说，那晚是疤子做下龌龊事赖到陈浩宇身上。
Now Chen Haoyu panicked and snapped at him, “I never treated you shabby—why spout lies?! That day when I went back to town, I told you boys to watch her till I returned the next day! Don’t tell me you did the dirty deed yourself, sold her off, and pinned it on me?!”

“疤子，你刚才的证词中提到‘我们’看来跟着陈浩宇的还有其他人？”
Scarface kept spinning. “You treated me decent, Young Master Chen, and I haven’t forgotten—yet facts are facts, and I can’t lie in court! Besides, how would I dare touch a girl you snatched?”

司理参军心思细腻，疤子提醒自己说话需谨慎：“是有其他人，不过陈公子回城后，其他人都回家了，只留我一人在宅子里。”
Chun Jinlan jumped in. “He sold me to Yangzhou with his own hands, Your Lordship—his word counts most!”

“这么说，你也承认陈浩宇被人叫回城里？”
The barbecue young master scratched his head. “Hold on—Young Master Chen was with us all night! Can he split himself in two?”

“承认，当然承认，只是陈公子惦记被抓的姑娘，大半夜又回来，办完事，才吩咐我把人给卖了。”
Three witnesses, two clashing stories—somebody was lying.

申简辰又转向陈浩宇：“陈浩宇，乡下的宅子里可还有下人？”
Shen Jianchen weighed their motives. Flighty as the two young masters were, they had no stake in the case and zero reason to perjure themselves. Scarface, on the other hand, was neck-deep in it and had driven the girl to Yangzhou himself—maybe he really had done the deed and blamed Haoyu.

仪王的问题吓得陈浩东手中的茶盏晃了一下，茶水溅到衣衫上。他赶紧将茶盏放好，抖了抖衣衫，掩饰好情绪，尴尬地向一旁的大伯笑笑。心想，那日天够晚，宅子里的下人也都被调走，唯一留下的管家也睡得跟个死猪一样，他做下的事除了疤子，应该无人知晓。
“Scarface, you said ‘we’ earlier. So other men came with Chen Haoyu?”

陈江林一直认真听证词，关键还在疤子，若有人能证明疤子的话是假，儿子便可无事，仪王的问题也正问到他的心里。
Wary of the sharp investigator, Scarface chose his words with care. “Yes, others came, yet after Young Master Chen returned to town, the rest went home—leaving just me at the house.”

陈浩宇回道：“陈家所有在外的宅子都有下人，乡下的宅子也不例外。”
“So you admit Chen Haoyu got called back to town?”

申简辰对众人说：“三位证人的证词出现矛盾，今日还无法定案，先审到这里，待司理院找到相关证据后再继续审理。”
“Sure do—only Young Master Chen couldn’t stop thinking about the girl, came back in the dead of night, finished his business, and ordered me to sell her.”

春金兰摸摸藏在袖子里的匕首，今日状况不明，还不到启用它的时候。
Shen Jianchen shifted to Chen Haoyu. “Were there any servants at that country house?”

差役将陈浩宇和春金兰带下去后，三位证人也都离去。
The question jolted Chen Haodong’s hand; tea sloshed from his bowl onto his sleeve. Setting the bowl down quick, he brushed his robe and flashed his uncle an awkward smile. *Relax—the servants had all been pulled away that night, and the one caretaker left slept like a dead pig. Nobody knows except Scarface.*

陈江林与陈浩东起身拜别仪王。
Chen Jianglin listened with bated breath; if someone could punch a hole in Scarface’s story, his son walked free.

陈江林说：“辛苦仪王殿下为我这不成器的儿子操心，若他真的做下错事，任凭仪王处置，我绝不姑息。”
“Every outside Chen residence has servants,” Chen Haoyu replied. “That country house included.”

陈浩宇如何申简辰不会放在心上，他只为千里寻担忧，若陈浩宇真的有事，她岂不是刚嫁人就得守寡？为了她好，他也希望陈浩宇无事。
Shen Jianchen addressed the hall. “With conflicting witness accounts, no verdict can be reached today. Adjourned until the Judicial Office gathers further proof.”

“常言道知子莫若父，我相信陈老爷心中对此事已有定论，只是还需寻找有力的证据。”
Chun Jinlan felt the hidden dagger in her sleeve—still too unclear to play her trump card.

陈江林佩服仪王，年纪轻轻竟然能看透他的心思：“殿下所言极是。”
Once constables led Chen Haoyu and Chun Jinlan away and the witnesses left, Chen Jianglin and Chen Haodong rose to bow.

申简辰瞧向身边的陈浩东，他恭恭敬敬，一言不发，眼神中却透出紧张的情绪来，不如探探虚实：“陈大公子可了解陈浩宇？”
“Thank you for taking trouble over my wayward son, Your Highness,” Chen Jianglin said. “If he truly committed this crime, punish him by law—I will not shield him.”

仪王冷不丁发问，搞得陈浩东紧张不已：“浩宇是我堂弟，我自然对他十分了解。”
Shen Jianchen cared nothing for Chen Haoyu himself, only for Qianli Xun—if Haoyu swung for this, she’d be widowed right after her wedding. For her sake, he hoped Haoyu was clean.

申简辰继续追问：“既然了解，你认为哪个证词是假？”
“No one knows a son like his father,” Shen Jianchen said. “I believe Master Chen already knows the truth in his heart; we just need solid proof.”

陈浩东装作毫不犹豫的样子：“仪王既然给小人机会，小人斗胆说一说，我与浩宇手足情深，自然向着他，满心希望是疤子作假证，害我二弟。”
Impressed by the young prince’s insight, Chen Jianglin bowed. “Your Highness speaks true.”

申简辰故意顺着对方：“很好，既然陈大公子如此希望，我会差人好好找出证据证明疤子是作假证。”
Shen Jianchen turned his eyes on Chen Haodong—standing there pious and silent, yet tension leaked from his gaze. Time to poke him. “Does Eldest Young Master Chen know Chen Haoyu well?”

陈浩东更加紧张：“仪王不必将小人的话当回事，小人所说只是亲情使然。”
Caught off guard, Chen Haodong stiffened. “Haoyu is my cousin—of course I know him well.”

“怎么？你不希望我找到证据证明疤子的证词是假？”
“Then whose testimony do you think is false?”

仪王的反问更让陈浩东不知所措：“小人不是这个意思……”
Chen Haodong put on an earnest front. “Since Your Highness asks, I’ll speak bold: Haoyu and I are like brothers, so of course I side with him and hope with all my heart that Scarface is lying to frame him.”

“你不必紧张，我只是随便问问。”申简辰瞧向陈江林，“陈老爷可听过一个词：祸起萧墙？”
Shen Jianchen played right along. “Splendid! Since you hope for that so much, I’ll have my men dig up proof that Scarface perjured himself.”

陈江林一时不明白，还是回道：“听过。”
Panic tightened Chen Haodong’s throat. “Pay no mind to my words, Your Highness—I only spoke out of family feeling.”

“这个词不仅适用于皇家，普通百姓家也适用，尤其像陈家这种大户人家。行了，你们回去吧，本王还要继续寻找证据。”
“What, don’t you want me to prove Scarface lied?”

仪王明确发话，二人躬起身，后退几步后才离开。
Flustered, Chen Haodong stammered, “That isn’t what I meant...”

走出司理院的途中，陈江林一直思忖，仪王为何要提“祸起萧墙”四个字？还说也适用陈家？难道他想提醒什么？
“Relax—just asking.” Shen Jianchen looked back at Chen Jianglin. “Master Chen, have you heard the saying ‘calamity starts inside one’s own walls’?”

直到与浩东一起坐上马车，看到这位大侄子忧心忡忡，心中自问：“难道仪王的意思浩宇之事与浩东有关？”
Baffled, Chen Jianglin answered, “I have.”

很快陈江林否认了他的看法，怎么可能？八竿子打不着的事。他们兄弟平日看着十分和谐，况且浩东没有任何理由给浩宇制造麻烦。
“It doesn’t apply just to imperial houses—ordinary households too, most of all wealthy clans like the Chens. Run along now; I still have evidence to hunt down.”

接着又觉不对，还是有理由，陈家产业的继承人要在他们兄弟中产生。
Dismissed, both men bowed and backed out of the hall.

又一想，浩飞一心考功名，对继承产业不感兴趣。浩宇破事一大堆，以前吊儿郎当，族中长辈也瞧不上。前日陈陆来府中，当着他的面将浩宇和他娘子数落了一番，如此看来大家根本不可能选浩宇。
All the way out, Chen Jianglin chewed over those words. *Calamity inside one’s own walls—applicable to the Chens?* Climbing into the carriage beside his anxious nephew, a wild thought flashed across his mind: *Was Prince Yi hinting Haodong had a hand in Haoyu’s mess?*

陈家产业的继承人只能是浩东，连陈江林自己也这么认为，浩东应该能感受到，如此好的形势，他怎么可能做出伤害浩宇之事？
He brushed it off almost at once. Absurd! The cousins got along fine, and why on earth would Haodong sabotage Haoyu? Well—for the business inheritance, maybe. Yet Haofei cared only for imperial exams, and Haoyu’s wild past had already sunk him with the elders—just look at how Chen Lu chewed out Haoyu and his wife the other day. Haodong already had the inheritance locked up, and he knew it. Why risk hurting Haoyu?

最终陈江林得出结论：贵人有时说话也没多大意思，听听得了，千万不能太当真。
In the end Chen Jianglin concluded royals just tossed out cryptic remarks for show—best not to overthink it.
