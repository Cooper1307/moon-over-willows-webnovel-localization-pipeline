## Chapter 101: A Lively Qiqiao Banquet

Having repaid her debt to Prince Yi, the four parted ways outside Qiaotou Wa.

Qiao Wenxian took Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe straight back to Qiao Manor for the Qiqiao Banquet, while Qianli Xun hurried back to Zhu Manor.

Inside the carriage, Qianli Xun studied Kuqiao without blinking...

Flushing under her mistress's stare, Kuqiao confessed at once: "Don't look at me like that, Miss. I do like Bai Feng, and I wasn't trying to hide it from you today."

"When did this start?"

"The last time I went to West Third Alley, he blocked my carriage, talked my ear off, and gave me a clay figurine. That's when I agreed."

"One clay figurine won you over? Are you even my maid? If you want clay figurines, I can buy you piles of them."

"It wasn't about the figurine! I like Bai Feng, and besides, he's Prince Yi's guard with a bright future ahead. From what I've seen so far, he treats me pretty well."

Qianli Xun let out an easy laugh at last. "Now that sounds more like my maid—didn't let love go straight to your head."

Kuqiao grew bold: "Funny, Miss, you seem like the one who let love go to her head."

"You dare talk back to your own mistress? Watch me tickle you!"

Qianli Xun tickled Kuqiao until she burst into giggles, shrieking over and over, "Miss! Mercy! Spare me!"

Mid-tickle, Qianli Xun caught sight of Xiaguo forcing a smile beside them to play along. She paused. "What's wrong with you? You look out of sorts."

"Both Miss and Sister Kuqiao have someone you love, while I..."

Qianli Xun laughed. "Didn't you say you have someone too? Tell me who!"

How could Xiaguo dare admit it? She covered it up: "I just made that up on the spot to turn Nan Shuang down. Don't take it to heart, Miss."

"I see. Well, no need to envy us. You're pretty—just work on cultivating your inner self, and when the time is right, I'll make sure to find you a fine match."

Xiaguo looked curious. "Miss, how do you cultivate your inner self?"

"Your inner self comes down to two things: learning and character. You already know most characters, so flip through the books in my room whenever you're free. As the saying goes, a mind steeped in poetry and books gives a person natural grace. Over time, cultivation follows. As for character, remember four words: Right Thought, Right Mindfulness. Keep them in mind in everything you do."

Xiaguo seemed to grasp it and nodded. "I'll remember."

"I'll also have Kuqiao teach you how to whisk tea. Once an opening comes up, I'll pick out a good family for you."

Panic struck Xiaguo. "Miss doesn't need to pick a family for me! I only want to follow you. When you marry into the Qiao family, I'll follow you right there."

Kuqiao scolded her: "Xiaguo, which word out of your mouth is true and which is false? One second ago you envied Miss and me for having sweethearts, and the next you refuse when Miss offers to find you a good match. What's going on?"

Qianli Xun jumped in to explain for her: "She just wants to find someone she likes on her own, same as you. Still, Xiaguo, you can look on your own, but he has to pass my inspection first. If I say no, neither of you is allowed to marry—hear me? That goes for Bai Feng too!"

Kuqiao protested, "Bai Feng is a great guy. Miss doesn't need to worry."

"Ever heard 'like master, like servant'? I know what kind of man Prince Yi is better than you do. Stay sharp! Until you know Bai Feng inside and out, no holding hands, no..." Qianli Xun bit off the rest. "Anyway, nothing too intimate, or you'll live to regret it."

"Ah! So many rules, Miss—what if Bai Feng and I already held hands?"

"Fine, I'll allow holding hands, and nothing else—hear me?"

"Loud and clear, Miss. Don't worry."

Running Qianli Fist Manor had given Qianli Xun the habit of fussing over her disciples, and she had brought that habit straight into the Song dynasty, worrying over her two personal maids' love lives without even trying.

Back at Zhu Manor, they headed straight for the Qiqiao Banquet.

The servants had all returned, filling Zhu Manor with lively cheer once more. Lanterns and silk festoons glowed everywhere, brightest of all where the banquet tables stood, and the entire household had gathered there.

By the time Qianli Xun arrived, the Qiqiao Banquet stood laid out, though no one had taken their seats yet.

Matron Wei spotted her first and hurried over to take her hand. "At last you're back, Young Lady—everyone was waiting on you." Turning toward the hall, she called out, "Master, Madam, the Young Lady is back!"

Zhu Xiyan, Madam Xiang, Zhu Jingyuan, and Chen Wenxin stepped out of the hall, lining up in order before the incense table.

A servant lit three sticks of incense and handed them to Zhu Xiyan. Holding them, he bowed three times to the moon while the rest followed suit, then planted the three sticks one by one into the censer.

Only after the offering did everyone take their seats to enjoy the Qiqiao Banquet.

Once the masters finished bowing, the maids offered their prayers, followed by the male servants.

Afterward, the maids picked up needles and thread to try threading them by moonlight. Kuqiao and Xiaguo joined in, laughing and having the time of their lives.

Meanwhile, the male servants placed offerings and the Four Treasures of the Study on a table holding a clay statue. A maid cradling Laibao knelt down and helped him kowtow, chanting three times: "Eldest grandson of the Zhu house, Laibao, prays for wit and wisdom!"

Everyone in the Zhu family burst into wide smiles. After praying for him, the maid handed Laibao back into Chen Wenxin's arms, and the male servants took their turns kneeling before the altar to pray for cleverness.

Eyeing the clay statue, Qianli Xun asked Matron Wei beside her, "Who is that statue modeled after?"

Matron Wei answered, "That's the Cowherd. When boys and men kneel to him, they pray for intelligence."

Eating her way through the Qiqiao Banquet, Qianli Xun marveled at the wisdom of the ancients, who gave even an ordinary festival such an uplifting meaning. Not like the era she had crossed from, where a fine title like "Cowherd" (niulang) had taken on a sleazy meaning—male escort! What a decline in morals. Looked at from that angle, was history moving forward or backward? Nobody could say for sure.

Just like human life itself—maybe the healthiest state was being like little Laibao. Growing up looked like gaining wisdom, yet hearts grew tangled and complicated until people marched toward death in that tangle. Growing up looked like progress, yet it was a step backward too.

Qianli Xun realized one Qixi Festival was turning her into a philosopher, brooding over history and the meaning of life.

Kuqiao's thrilled voice rang out: "Whoa! I caught a spider!"

The maids crowded around, and Lutao brought over a small wooden box. "I hunted forever and couldn't find one—you're so lucky! Take this box, and tomorrow you have to let me see the web it spins so I can borrow some of your good luck!"

Kuqiao took the box. "Thanks, Sister Lutao. Tomorrow I'll let you peek first when I open it."

The masters shared in Kuqiao's cheer; even Zhu Xiyan wore a broad smile. For one thing, keeping a stern face on Qixi Festival brought bad luck. For another, his daughter had asked Prince Yi to step in and settle Xiaguo's trouble, proving Prince Yi looked kindly on the Zhu family—with Prince Yi as his backing, his seat as Prefect of Lin'an stood solid as Mount Tai.

Best of all, he had heard the Qiao family meant to propose marriage to the Zhu family. Becoming in-laws with imperial relatives was a windfall beyond his dreams. He even regretted marrying Shuzhen off to the Lin family in a rush—had he known Young Master Qiao had feelings for her, he would never have done it. Thank goodness everything was turning out right. In high spirits, Zhu Xiyan drank several extra cups with his son, Zhu Jingyuan.

On Qixi night, the servants stayed up reveling late, while Qianli Xun turned in early.

In the middle of the night, she had a dream. A man stood with his back to her. Mistaking him for Qiao Wenxian, she shouted, "Wenyuan!"

When the man turned around, it was Shen Jianchen. She ran up and grabbed him. "Shen Jianchen, where have you been? Did you cross over too?"

Shen Jianchen reproached her: "Why didn't you come look for me?"

"Where was I supposed to look?"

"You have someone you love now—have you forgotten me already?"

"I do have someone I love, but I haven't forgotten you."

"Then tell me: do you like him, or do you like me?"

Qianli Xun let go of his hand. "I never said yes to you! Same answer as always: I'll marry you when pigs climb trees!"

Shen Jianchen vanished, replaced by Prince Yi staring at her with a teasing gleam: "Youqi Jushi, I love you!"

Qianli Xun swung a punch straight at him—and jolted herself awake, eyes flying open to find her arm thrust straight into the air.

Dawn had not broken yet, and pitch darkness filled the yard outside. Qianli Xun lowered her arm, rolled over, and tried to fall back asleep, yet sleep refused to come. Thinking back on that absurd dream, she could not help laughing.

Dreaming of Shen Jianchen left her missing him. Where on earth was he? When would she ever cross paths with him again? And if they met and he found out about Wenyuan, would it break his heart?

Then again, maybe Shen Jianchen had already built a new life somewhere else and forgotten all about her. Why borrow trouble worrying over nothing? Better to get some sleep.
