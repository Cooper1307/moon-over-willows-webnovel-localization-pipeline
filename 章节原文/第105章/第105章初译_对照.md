第105章 焚烧书稿受家法
## Chapter 105: Burning the Manuscripts and Facing Family Punishment

屋内的手稿被洗劫一空，全部堆积在院内，一个下人掏出火折子，吹出火苗，打算点着时，苦荞冲出去，一把将火折子打落在地。火碰到冰凉的石砖，立刻熄灭。
Stripped bare from the room, the manuscripts lay heaped in the courtyard. A servant pulled out a tinder tube, blew a spark into flame, and bent to light the pile—only for Kuqiao to dash forward and knock it straight from his hand. The spark hit the cold stone tiles and died at once.

“这些手稿都是小姐多年的心血，怎能被你们随意烧掉？”
"These manuscripts are years of Miss's heart and soul! How dare you burn them like trash?"

夏果从丫鬟房出来，拦在书稿前：“想烧书稿，先烧了我再说！”
Xiaguo ran out of the maids' quarters and threw herself in front of the stack. "If you want to burn the manuscripts, burn me first!"

见此情景，其他下人不敢随意动手。
Confronted by the two maids, the servants hesitated to move.

一位劝道：“拦也没用，老爷吩咐的，必须今日将小姐写的诗词全部焚烧，一首都不许留，我们也没办法。”
One urged, "Blocking us won't help. Master's orders—every poem Miss ever wrote has to burn today, not a single piece spared. Our hands are tied."

焚烧书稿之事不可逆转，千里寻没想过要阻拦，再说该留下的还会留下。
The burning could not be stopped, and Qianli Xun had no wish to stop it. Whatever was meant to survive would survive anyway.

焚书坑儒毁灭不了儒家文化，一把火又岂能烧掉朱淑真的诗词？与文字相关的玩意儿，最大的妙处就在于容易刻在脑中，凭记忆便可薪火相传。
Burning books and burying scholars had never wiped out Confucianism; how could one bonfire erase Zhu Shuzhen's verse? The beauty of words was how they etched themselves into memory, passing from mind to mind like an undying flame.

千里寻从屋内走出，显得异常冷静：“你们两个不要阻拦，既然是我爹的意思，烧就烧吧。”
Qianli Xun stepped out of the room, eerily calm. "Step aside, you two. If it's Father's wish, let them burn."

苦荞与夏果离开堆积的书稿，站到小姐身边，目睹下人捡起火折子，又将火苗吹出，把纸点着。纸见到火，疯狂燃烧，火势越来越大。
Kuqiao and Xiaguo moved away from the mound and stood beside their mistress as the servant retrieved the tinder tube, blew it to life, and touched flame to paper. Fire caught with a fierce crackle, leaping higher by the second.

纸张一点一点在火焰中化为灰烬，千里寻的内心也一点点萌生出希望，仿佛朱淑真的命运正在逐渐被火苗吞噬，剩下的只会属于千里寻。
Watching sheet after sheet crumble into ash, a strange hope took root inside Qianli Xun—as if Zhu Shuzhen's tragic fate were being devoured by the flames, leaving only Qianli Xun's own path behind.

正当她体验火焰和灰烬带来的快感时，朱晞颜怒气冲冲进来，一巴掌打在女儿的脸上。
Just as she felt a dark rush of relief watching paper turn to ash, Zhu Xiyan stormed into the yard and slapped her hard across the face.

“从小我教你写诗作词，不是让你拿来辱没门风，你倒好，写出淫词滥句，败坏自己的名声不说，连带朱家也跟着被整个临安城的人非议，以后谁还会来朱府提亲！我作为临安知府，因为你！没脸见临安的官员与百姓！”
"I taught you poetry and ci from childhood—not so you could drag our family honor through the mud! And look what you've done: writing lewd lines, ruining your own name, and leaving the entire Zhu family mocked by all of Lin'an! Who will ever come propose at Zhu Manor now?! As Prefect of Lin'an, I can't even show my face before officials or commoners because of you!"

千里寻捂住被打疼的脸，不想做任何反抗，这一关总得过，过了便好。
Cupping her stinging cheek, Qianli Xun offered no resistance. This storm had to be weathered; once it passed, it would be over.

苦荞与夏果替小姐担心，可面对老爷的愤怒，她们不敢有任何言语。
Kuqiao and Xiaguo trembled for their mistress, yet before their master's wrath neither dared utter a peep.

“呵呵……！烧得好，统统烧光，一个字都不许留着！”朱晞颜盯着火焰，难过地笑道，接着吩咐下人，“去！到屋里把所有与写诗作词有关的统统搬出来烧掉！”
"Haha! Burn them! Burn every last scrap—leave not a single character!" Staring into the fire, Zhu Xiyan laughed a bitter laugh, then barked at the servants, "Go! Haul out everything in her room tied to writing poetry and ci and burn it all!"

下人为难道：“老爷，难道笔也要烧？”
A servant hesitated: "Master, even the brushes?"

朱晞颜大声命令：“烧！还有屋子里的书，也都搬出来！”
Zhu Xiyan roared, "Burn them! Drag out every book in the room too!"

下人们又进入屋内，把笔和所有书全部搬出来，扔进大火中。
The servants filed back inside, hauling out brushes and every single book to hurl into the blaze.

千里寻放下捂住脸的手，在心中说：“烧吧，把所有与朱淑真有关的都烧掉吧。”
Lowering her hand from her cheek, Qianli Xun murmured in her heart: "Burn it all. Burn everything that belonged to Zhu Shuzhen."

朱晞颜厉声说：“跪下！”
Zhu Xiyan thundered, "Kneel!"

千里寻很不情愿地跪下，苦荞和夏果也跟着跪下
Against her will, Qianli Xun dropped to her knees, with Kuqiao and Xiaguo kneeling right beside her.

朱晞颜命令道：“拿鞭子来！”
Zhu Xiyan ordered, "Bring the whip!"

千里寻这才注意到朱晞颜走进小院时，身后跟着一位手拿鞭子的下人。听到命令，那下人将鞭子递到老爷跟前。
Only then did Qianli Xun notice a servant carrying a whip trailing behind Zhu Xiyan. At the command, the servant stepped forward and offered it.

朱晞颜接过鞭子：“你们两个给我闪开！”
Zhu Xiyan gripped the handle. "You two, get out of the way!"

苦荞与夏果被吓坏，赶紧站起来，躲到一边。
Terrified, Kuqiao and Xiaguo scrambled to their feet and backed off.

只见朱晞颜使出全身力气，狠狠打下一鞭……
Putting all his strength into his arm, Zhu Xiyan swung the whip down with ruthless force...

千里寻只觉背上被抽打得火辣辣的疼。
A searing stripe of fire tore across Qianli Xun's back.

刚要打下第二鞭，韦嬷嬷扶着向夫人走进来，向夫人伤心地哭着。
Just as he raised his arm for a second strike, Matron Wei hurried in supporting Madam Xiang, who was sobbing her heart out.

“呜呜……！老爷！淑真怎么能经得起如此鞭打？您就饶了她吧！……呜呜……！”
"Waaah! Husband! How can Shuzhen survive a whipping like this? Please spare her! Waaah!"

朱晞颜又狠狠打下一鞭……
Zhu Xiyan brought the whip slashing down a second time...

向夫人立刻扑到女儿的背上：“呜呜……老爷要打就先打死我！以前的女儿被林觉打得失忆，难道老爷连失忆后的女儿都不愿给我留下？呜呜……！”
Madam Xiang threw herself straight over her daughter's back. "Waaah! If you want to beat her, beat me to death first! Lin Jue already beat our old daughter into amnesia—won't you even leave me this daughter who came back to us?! Waaah!"

朱晞颜的鞭子无法再落下，他以为如果女儿没有失忆，也许不会写出“和衣睡倒人怀”的句子。
Zhu Xiyan's arm froze in midair. If his daughter had never lost her memory, perhaps she would never have written "falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed."

朱晞颜将鞭子扔在地上，对向夫人说：“你起开，我有话与淑真说。”
He flung the whip to the ground and told Madam Xiang, "Get up. I have words for Shuzhen."

韦嬷嬷将夫人扶到藤椅上坐下，向夫人继续哭着抹眼泪。
Matron Wei helped Madam Xiang onto a rattan chair, where she kept weeping and dabbing her eyes.

朱晞颜看着跪在地上忍着疼痛的女儿，想起那日她抱着他的脖子在他脸上亲了一口的情景，那时他已完全从心底接受了这个不一样的女儿。
Gazing down at his daughter kneeling in silent pain, Zhu Xiyan remembered the day she had thrown her arms around his neck and kissed his cheek—the moment he had accepted this changed daughter with his whole heart.

可她却写出有失女德的句子，被整个临安城的人唾骂，搞得朱家声誉扫地，若继续将她留在朱府，以后朱家的子孙都会受其牵连。
Yet she had written shameless lines, earning the scorn of all Lin'an and dragging the Zhu name into the dirt. Keeping her under his roof would taint every generation to come.

“淑真，你走吧，以后不要再回朱府，朱家就当没有你这个女儿。”
"Leave, Shuzhen. Never set foot in Zhu Manor again. From this day on, the Zhu family has no daughter like you."

千里寻虽在朱府待的时间不长，可亲情已萌生，朱淑真的父亲突然赶她离开，她的眼角还是滚落下不舍的泪水。
Though Qianli Xun had not spent long in Zhu Manor, real family warmth had grown there. Hearing Zhu Shuzhen's father cast her out sent reluctant tears spilling down her cheeks.

向夫人急了：“老爷！你把淑真赶出去，她以后一个人怎么过？呜呜……！”
Madam Xiang panicked: "Husband! If you throw Shuzhen out, how will she survive all alone?! Waaah!"

千里寻双目含泪，果断地说：“父亲放心，从今日起，我搬出朱府。朱府不认我，可在我心里，这里永远是我的家，您和娘，还有哥哥嫂嫂永远是我的亲人。”
Eyes glistening with tears, Qianli Xun spoke with firm resolve: "Rest easy, Father. Starting today, I move out of Zhu Manor. Even if Zhu Manor disowns me, in my heart this will always be my home, and you, Mother, Brother, and Sister-in-law will always be my family."

女儿的话令向夫人哭得更伤心：“呜呜……！我家淑真的命怎么就这么苦啊！呜呜……！”
Her words made Madam Xiang sob even harder: "Waaah! Why is my Shuzhen's life so cruel?! Waaah!"

朱晞颜忍住泪水，往院外走，撂下一句话：“天黑前搬出朱府，否则我会派人把你的东西全部扔出去！”
Swallowing back his own tears, Zhu Xiyan strode toward the gate, tossing one parting order over his shoulder: "Be out of Zhu Manor before dark, or I'll have your belongings dumped in the street!"

其他下人都跟着朱晞颜出了小院。
The other servants trailed out of the courtyard behind him.

火还在继续燃烧，随着灰烬越来越多，火势变得缓和许多。
The fire kept crackling, sinking lower as the heap of ash grew deeper.

在两位丫鬟的搀扶下，千里寻努力爬起来，有意装作轻松的样子。
Leaning on her two maids, Qianli Xun struggled to her feet and put on a brave, easy front.

“娘，您别哭，爹打我两下，根本不算什么，再说我离开朱府，又不会走远，依然会待在临安，娘想要见我，可以去望月轩。”
"Don't cry, Mother. Two lashes from Father are nothing. Besides, I'm not going far—I'll still be right here in Lin'an. Anytime Mother wants to see me, just come to Wangyue Pavilion."

韦嬷嬷也说：“夫人放心，有我在，姑娘离开朱府生活不会差。”
Matron Wei added, "Rest easy, Madam. With me watching over her, our Young Lady won't live poorly outside Zhu Manor."

“淑真的生活我不担心，以前的嫁妆够她用，大不了我再拿出些田产铺子给她带上，我只担心她以后没个长辈做主，婚事该怎么办？”向夫人又是一阵难过。
"I'm not worried about her livelihood—her old dowry is plenty, and I can hand over more estates and shops if need be. I just worry about her marriage, with no elders left to stand behind her!" Fresh grief shook Madam Xiang.

面对突如其来的变故，千里寻很痛心，却强撑着安慰向夫人：“娘不必担心，人来世间又不是只有嫁人这一件事，还有很多事可做。若没人要我，我就一个人过，做自己喜欢的事。”
Her own heart aching from the sudden blow, Qianli Xun forced herself to comfort Madam Xiang. "Don't worry, Mother. A person doesn't come into this world just to get married—there's plenty else to do. If nobody wants me, I'll live on my own and do whatever makes me happy."

向夫人继续抹眼泪：“话虽如此，女子生来是要嫁人的。”
Madam Xiang wiped her eyes. "Easy to say, yet a woman is born to wed."

韦嬷嬷宽慰道：“我以后就替您和老爷当姑娘的长辈，她的婚事我定会放在心上。”
Matron Wei soothed her: "From now on, I'll serve as Young Lady's elder in place of you and Master. I'll keep her marriage close to heart."

向夫人才放心地点点头，对两位丫鬟说：“你们两个还不赶快扶小姐进屋给她背上上些药。”
Only then did Madam Xiang nod in relief, turning to the two maids: "Hurry up and help Miss indoors to put medicine on her back!"

安慰好向夫人，千里寻才感受到背部一阵刺痛，在两位丫鬟的搀扶下进屋上药。
Only after soothing Madam Xiang did sharp stings flare across Qianli Xun's back. Supported by both maids, she went inside for medicine.

韦嬷嬷也扶着向夫人离开。
Matron Wei helped Madam Xiang back to her quarters as well.

苦荞帮小姐把衣裳脱下，看到背部两道鞭打的伤痕，还时不时渗出血来：“老爷怎么下这般狠手？”
Helping her mistress out of her robe, Kuqiao stared at the two angry welts oozing blood across her back. "How could Master strike so hard?!"

夏果把药瓶拿来，她的心里还想着乔公子：“小姐，您与乔公子该怎么办？”
Xiaguo brought over the salve bottle, her mind still stuck on Young Master Qiao. "Miss, what will happen with you and Young Master Qiao now?"

今日发生的事情太多，千里寻还没腾出心情考虑她与文渊之事。夏果提起，她想了想说道：“等我伤好后，跟文渊见一面，再作决定。”
Too much had piled up today for Qianli Xun to sort out her future with Wenyuan yet. At Xiaguo's question, she paused and said, "Once my wounds heal, I'll meet Wenyuan and decide."

苦荞接过药瓶，边给小姐上药边说：“乔公子是乔家独苗，只要乔公子愿意跟小姐在一起，乔家的长辈也没办法。”
Taking the bottle, Kuqiao dabbed salve onto her mistress's back. "Young Master Qiao is the sole heir of the Qiao line. As long as he wants to be with you, his elders won't be able to stop him."

夏果转悲为喜：“苦荞姐姐说这话我爱听。”
Xiaguo perked up at once: "Now that's what I love to hear, Sister Kuqiao!"

刚为小姐上完药，一群丫鬟进来，为首的说：“小姐，老爷吩咐，让我们过来帮您收拾东西，即刻离开朱府。”
No sooner had the medicine been applied than a troop of maids filed in. Their leader said, "Miss, Master ordered us to help pack your things and see you out of Zhu Manor at once."

夏果生气道：“老爷不是说天黑前吗？离天黑还早，你们急什么？”
Xiaguo bristled: "Didn't Master say before dark? It's nowhere near dusk—what's your rush?!"

为首的丫鬟又说：“小姐，老爷吩咐，我等只能听从。”
The lead maid replied, "Master's orders, Miss. We have to obey."

朱晞颜着急把她赶出去，无非是为维护朱家声誉，当所有人知道朱家已将无品无德的女儿赶出家门，才会认为朱家家风甚严，也才可保朱家子孙后代不被他人诟病。
Zhu Xiyan was in a rush to throw her out just to protect the Zhu name: once all of Lin'an saw the family expel an "unvirtuous" daughter, folk would praise the Zhu household's strict standards and spare future generations from gossip.

千里寻觉得甚是讽刺，她认真奉行净光法师教的“正思正念”四字，却因品行遭人诟病，又被父亲赶出家门，她自信从未做过任何违背道德之事。
Qianli Xun found it rich with irony. She had followed Master Jingguang's "Right Thought, Right Mindfulness" to the letter, never doing a single immoral deed—yet here she was, slandered for loose conduct and thrown out of her own father's house.

到底哪里不对？要说是因为一句“和衣睡倒人怀”，千里寻难以相信一句词会有这么大威力。再说普通百姓很少喜欢欣赏诗词，这句词最多只会在文人雅士中流传，不至于闹得人尽皆知。
Where had it all gone wrong? One line—"falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed"—could never carry that kind of blast radius on its own. Common folk didn't follow poetry; that line would have stayed among literati, not swept the whole city.

看来关键在流言，显然是别有用心之人故意编排。编排的人得多恨她，才诌出那些话来。
The real weapon was the smear campaign, spun up by someone with a grudge—someone who hated her enough to invent filth out of thin air.

提到“恨”字，千里寻想到陈浩宇，她穿越而来唯一合不来之人便是他，难道流言与他有关？
At the word "hate," Chen Haoyu popped straight into her head. He was the one person she had clashed with since crossing over. Could he be behind the rumors?

千里寻猛然想起在陈浩宇说过，过不了多久，送她一份大礼……
Then she remembered Chen Haoyu's parting taunt—promising to send her a "big gift" before long...

“姑娘，您在想什么？”为首的丫鬟问道。
"Young Lady, what are you thinking about?" the lead maid asked.

千里寻回过神来：“哦，没什么，既然是我爹吩咐，你们收拾吧。”
Qianli Xun snapped back. "Oh, nothing. If it's my father's orders, go ahead and pack."

丫鬟们开始翻箱倒柜帮忙收拾整理，无须苦荞和夏果插手。她们头一次不用亲手为小姐整理衣物，清闲在一旁，也头一次体会到原来不是所有的清闲都令人愉快。
The maids busied themselves emptying chests and wardrobes without needing Kuqiao or Xiaguo to lift a finger. For the first time, the two personal maids stood idle while others packed their mistress's clothes—learning for the first time that not every spell of idleness felt sweet.
