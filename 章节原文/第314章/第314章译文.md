## Chapter 314: Delivering Banknotes to Liu'an Villa

Chilled awake by autumn wind once the moon cleared the willow's crest, Shen Jianchen walked home to find anxious Bai Feng waiting outside the gate after catching a rare earful from Mengrong for wandering off.

Inside, Mengrong hugged him in relief—then froze when he casually announced he'd lost his Military Commissioner post and left the Judicial Office thanks to **Liang Shicheng** coming to Lin'an! Behind his turned back, a sharp gleam lit Mengrong's eyes. Pretending she only wanted to bribe Liang Shicheng into restoring Brother's post—which Shen Jianchen flatly vetoed—she quietly enlisted Matron Zou the next morning to track down where Liang Shicheng was staying.

Within an hour, Matron Zou learned from Bai Meiruo (whose weathervane husband Lin Jue had already jumped ship to butter up the eunuch!) that Liang Shicheng resided at north town's sprawling **Liu'an Villa**.

Telling Shen Jianchen she was off to a noble ladies' tea—firmly turning down his offer to escort her—Mengrong packed **twenty thousand taels** in banknotes (nearly her whole savings) and rode with Xilan to Liu'an Villa.

Kept waiting at the gate while servants announced her, she marched through a mansion grander than Prince Yi's palace back in Bianliang only to find Liang Shicheng lounging half-undressed with loose hair on a daybed while maids massaged his legs and fed him grapes—not even rising to greet an emperor's daughter-in-law!

Swallowing humiliation, Mengrong asked about her letter from months ago, presented the brocade box of banknotes through Xilan, and asked him to restore Brother's post as well.

Eyeing Mengrong's graceful form with greasy hunger, Liang Shicheng ordered Xilan outside—then stood barefoot, bolted the heavy doors shut, and pushed the box of banknotes straight back into Mengrong's arms.

"Keep your silver—I'm drowning in silver! Help you? Gladly—so long as you give me what *I* want. I may not be a whole man, yet I adore women—especially *married* women. Keep me happy once today, and I'll do anything you ask."
