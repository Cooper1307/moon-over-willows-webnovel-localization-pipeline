## Chapter 183: One Step Short of Discovering Her Best Friend

Pulling up outside Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen spotted Lin Jue speaking with Butler Li at the gate. Already dark out—what brought Lin Jue knocking at this hour?

Hearing carriage wheels, Butler Li looked over. "What timing—His Highness is just back."

Stepping up to the carriage, Lin Jue bowed low. "Your Highness Prince Yi, this humble official has an urgent matter to report."

Sensitive business did not belong outside the gate. Alighting, Shen Jianchen ushered Lin Jue into his main courtyard chamber. Too respectful to sit—and treating the report with gravity—Lin Jue remained standing.

Setting his personal turmoil aside for the moment, Shen Jianchen asked, "What urgent matter? Speak freely, Division Commander Lin."

"This humble official has discovered Jurchens operating inside Lin'an lately."

"Jurchens? How did you learn this?" Shen Jianchen sharpened to full alert at once; if Lin Jue came in person, these were no ordinary Jurchen traders.

"Since last year, after the Jurchens seized the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun from the Liao, they offered to return only seven—Yan, Ji, Tan, Jing, Shun, Zhuo, and Yi—while demanding a million strings of cash from the Song as replacement tax for Yanjing AND stripping away the entire local population! Such shameless terms! I ordered my men to keep close tabs on foreign merchants, especially Jurchens. Sure enough, a band of six or seven Jurchens entered Lin'an—no merchants at all. They gallop horses through crowded markets, terrorizing citizens. On the night of the Qiao wedding, a group of Jurchens even raided the Shao Escort Agency, throwing the whole compound into chaos. My patrol discovered it while making rounds—yet oddly, the Shao family refused yamen involvement."

Shen Jianchen commended him first. "Your patriotism does you credit, Division Commander Lin. Shame that while you and I care for the empire, His Majesty keeps giving ground—feeding Jurchen greed without end! I hear the Jurchens just jacked up the price again, demanding two hundred thousand taels of silver in army provisions to return the Western Capital, and His Majesty paid without blinking. All His Majesty cares about is the hollow glory of 'recovering Yanyun.' Keep watching that Jurchen crew, and I will investigate them quietly from my end."

Flushed with pride at Prince Yi's praise, Lin Jue held his tongue over the prince's sharp criticism of His Majesty—though in his own chest, he too resented how softly the court coddled the Jurchens, fearing disaster down the road.

Not until after Lin Jue took his leave did the servant who had shadowed Prince Yi return to Youlin Retreat.

Inside her room, Qiao Mengrong had been pacing on pins and needles since her brother's cold departure, spinning darker and darker scenarios until dusk fell and Xilan brought word he was home. She had started out to confront him, only to pause when Xilan reported Division Commander Lin was inside with him. Just as she rose to go over, the tailing servant returned. Sitting back down, she demanded at once, "What did you see? Where did Brother go?"

Dropping to one knee, the servant reported, "I tailed His Highness straight to Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake..."

Yongtai Teahouse—at least it was a scholars' haunt, not a brothel! Wait: did that mean Brother's beloved was a literary beauty? Her chest tightened right back up. "What else did you see?"

"I hid outside watching the door. Right around dusk, I saw Youqi Jushi walk out—and moments later, His Highness walked out too. Your Highness, could Prince Yi have been taking tea with Youqi Jushi?" Head bowed low, the servant dared not look up at her darkening face.

Hearing Shuzhen had been at the teahouse—leaving right before her brother—sent a storm surging through Qiao Mengrong's chest. Could the woman Brother loved be Shuzhen?!

Seeing her mistress frozen mute, Xilan prompted, "See anything else?"

"That is all I saw."

"Dismissed." Once the servant exited, Xilan murmured with caution, "Your Highness... could it really be Youqi Jushi?"

Replaying every moment Shuzhen and her brother had shared in front of her, Qiao Mengrong clung to doubt and grabbed at a lifeline. "Maybe Shuzhen just happened to be at Yongtai Teahouse! Or even if they arranged to meet, maybe she went to talk sense into Brother on my behalf! I went crying to her yesterday, and Shuzhen is so loyal to her friends—she must have been trying to persuade him for me!"

"Put like that, it does make sense," Xilan agreed.

Unable to sit still another second, Qiao Mengrong rose and hurried toward the main courtyard to ask her brother straight to his face.

Stepping through the gate, she saw him practicing fist forms in the lamplit yard—lithe, crisp, brimming with power. Deep down, she already knew he was not her old brother—aside from the body, nothing matched—yet admitting her old brother was gone forever left her whole heart's devotion with nowhere to land.

"Bro... Brother..."

Hearing the tremor in her voice, Shen Jianchen halted his stance and turned. In the lantern light, her pale, tear-filled eyes gazed straight at him with raw pleading. Unable to bear meeting them, he turned his face away. "It is late, Mengrong. Get some rest."

Stepping close, Qiao Mengrong nearly reached out to hug him, remembered his cold warning earlier, and pulled her hands back. "Did Brother just come back from Yongtai Teahouse?"

Startled—so she had put a tail on him—Shen Jianchen saw no point denying it. "Yes."

"To meet Shuzhen?" she pressed, voice taut with dread.

"Yes. I went to meet Youqi Jushi."

He said it straight out and met her eyes; tears spilled down her cheeks as her lips trembled. "Could it be... could the woman Brother loves be... Shuzhen?"

Part of him longed to say yes, yet with their marriage decree unresolved, he kept Qianli Xun shielded. "Since you went to see her, you can guess why she called me out—only to lecture me on your behalf. Shame her powers of persuasion need work; my mind has not changed."

A rush of relief loosened the vice around Qiao Mengrong's heart. Dabbing her cheeks with her handkerchief, she steadied her voice. "I have thought it through, Brother. I accept that you have someone else in your heart. Once we marry, bring her into the manor—I will live in harmony with her and run your inner quarters well. Tell me who she is now so I can learn her tastes and get on good terms ahead of time."

Any ordinary man would have cheered at such generosity—yet this was Shen Jianchen, whose heart and side had room for Qianli Xun alone. "Mengrong, I see you as my own little sister, and I want you to see me only as your brother. Where in this world does a brother marry his sister? Can you let go of our betrothal?"

Even after she bent this low, he still hid the woman's name—and dared ask her to give up their betrothal! Pain curdled into bitter hatred, and strangely, hatred brought ice-cold calm. "I fear I cannot grant Brother's wish. His Majesty decreed our marriage—how could anyone cast it aside?"

"If you agree to let go, I will ride to Bianliang and beg His Majesty face-to-face to cancel the decree."

Last year, the morning after Shuzhen stayed overnight at Youlin Retreat, Brother had promised to marry her; just two days ago he had agreed to honor the decree—and now, straight after Yongtai Teahouse, he wanted to cancel it outright?! Whoever this faceless rival was, she had Brother wrapped tight around her finger! Mengrong swore she would dig three feet under the earth to drag her into the light.

"Canceling an ordinary betrothal is hard enough, let alone an imperial decree," she answered flatly. "Wake up, Brother, and stop wasting your breath."

Seeing her calm face despite the moisture in her eyes, Shen Jianchen mistook it for resignation and hoped she was coming around to staying brother and sister. Reaching out, he clasped her hands warmly like an elder brother. "Just make peace with staying brother and sister, Mengrong, and leave the rest to me. I will cherish, protect, and care for you like a true brother."

Warmth radiated from his broad palms, yet she wanted a lover, not a brother. Drawing her hands free, she dropped a crisp curtsy. "I am tired and going back to rest."

Without waiting for a reply, she strode swiftly out of the courtyard, reached her chambers, and threw herself sobbing onto her bed.

Xilan stood helpless—with His Highness talking about canceling the imperial betrothal itself, what comfort remained? How heartless a man could be! When he lay sick, he leaned on Her Highness and promised the moon; the second he got well, he tossed every vow aside with that ridiculous tale about not being the old Prince Yi—who on earth would buy that?!

Once her tears ran dry, Qiao Mengrong wiped her face and sat ramrod straight. "You saw and heard with your own eyes today, Xilan—Brother betrayed me first. From here on, whatever I do is justified. Am I right?"

"Of course," Xilan nodded.

"Good. Two tasks lie ahead of me now: first, wait for word advancing our wedding date; second, find out who holds Brother's heart—even if I have to dig three feet underground! I will confront her face-to-face and drive her clear of Brother!"

"Understood. I will see it arranged."

Meanwhile, back at Wangyue Pavilion after Yongtai Teahouse, Qianli Xun replayed her talk with Shen Jianchen and judged she had left no loopholes. Done meddling in Mengrong's love life—the more she tangled in it, the worse it knotted. Time to dive back into her huaben—starting with that field trip to a gambling house tonight!

"Kuqiao, fetch Hongdou and pull out my men's clothes."

"Going to a gambling den right now, Miss? Isn't it too late?"

"Nighttime is when the real action starts!"

Fetching the disguises and calling Hongdou in, Kuqiao soon watched the pair emerge looking like two fresh-faced young dandies.

"Bring a pouch of silver, Hongdou," Qianli Xun instructed. "Your mistress knows zero about gambling—we will head home the moment we lose the whole bag."

"Got it!" Hongdou stowed the silver.

After seeing the pair into their carriage, Kuqiao sighed aloud at the gate. "First fighting an engagement, then writing huaben, then lecturing Prince Yi, now off to a gambling den! Miss never sits still a minute! If writing huaben is this much fuss, why not stick to poetry—instead of only tossing off verses right before Huayu shows up?"

"Muttering to yourself again?" Matron Wei's voice floated from behind.

Spinning around with a sheepish grin, Kuqiao answered, "Nothing, Matron! What brings you out?"

"Where is Miss sneaking off to at this hour?"

"A gambling house," Kuqiao confessed.

"A young lady going to a place like that?" Matron Wei frowned.

"Not to gamble for real! She is writing stories that need a gambling scene, does not know how they run, so she went for 'field research.'"

"Always an excuse with you. Think I did not already figure out Miss writes huaben on the sly—and that the famed writer Qianli Xun the whole city raves about is probably our young mistress?"

"Hush, Matron! Miss does not want a soul knowing!"

"Nobody here but you and me—what is there to fear?"

Scanning the empty courtyard before looping an arm through Matron Wei's, Kuqiao marveled in a whisper as they walked inside, "Nothing ever slips past your eyes! Xiaguo worked right in Miss's room and never guessed, yet you barely step inside and know everything—uncanny!"

Matron Wei only chuckled all the way down the walkway.
