## Chapter 189: Storming Zhu Manor to Stop the Wedding

When Zhu Xiyan's messenger delivered the wedding invitation to Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen had been down beneath the Divine Willow watching Bai Feng try to hoist the piglet up the bark.

While Bai Feng fretted over the stubborn pig, Shen Jianchen stayed cool as breeze. "No rushing—take it slow."

Bai Feng grumbled inside: *Easy for His Highness to flap his lips! A dignified royal guard armed with top fist arts reduced to coaching a pig up a tree—if word leaks out, I will be a laughingstock!*

Bored after a short while, Shen Jianchen told him, "Keep drilling it. I am heading back to the retreat." Qianli Xun had arrived beneath the willow shortly after he left.

Back in his study, Qiao Mengrong stepped slowly inside holding a crimson wedding card. "Brother, while you were out, Prefect Zhu sent an invitation—tomorrow Shuzhen marries Young Master Chen and invites us both. Looks like... Shuzhen's push to cancel the betrothal ended in failure."

She weighted those last words on purpose, driving home the quiet warning that breaking his betrothal to her was just as impossible.

Staring at the red card in her hand, Shen Jianchen neither reached for it nor uttered a word—simply spun on his heel and bolted straight out the door.

Assuming her pointed phrasing had angered him, Qiao Mengrong sagged in dismay. "Xilan, is Brother mad at me?"

"Your Highness only mentioned Youqi Jushi's failed annulment—nothing aimed at His Highness. Why would he be mad?" Xilan tried soothing her.

"I did say it wanting Brother to see he cannot easily break an imperial betrothal..."

"Even I did not catch that meaning, so maybe His Highness missed it too."

"How can you compare your wit to Brother's?" Mengrong chided faintly.

"Forgive my loose tongue, Your Highness."

Softening at once, Mengrong sighed. "Enough. Come back to my room."

"Should I send someone to tail His Highness?" Xilan asked.

"No," Mengrong decided after a pause. "He was clearly furious catching a tail last time—sending another will only put him on guard."

"Ask the driver when he returns, then?"

"No. Servants love gossip; keep fewer tongues wagging." Noticing Bai Feng absent from the courtyard, she frowned. "Where has Bai Feng vanished to these last two days?"

"He bought a piglet, Your Highness—probably down under the Divine Willow right now training it to climb trees."

"Training a pig to climb a tree?! Whatever for?!"

"All I know is His Highness ordered it."

Sharp instinct flashed across Qiao Mengrong's face, icing her soft tone with resentment. "The instant Bai Feng gets back, summon him to my room. I have questions."

"Yes, Your Highness."

Qianli Xun had sworn she would never marry Chen Haoyu—so why was the wedding suddenly tomorrow?! Determined to get the truth face-to-face, Shen Jianchen whipped his carriage straight into Lin'an, racing through crowded lanes to Wangyue Pavilion.

Matron Wei met him at the gate; one look at his frantic face sealed her suspicions about his heart, and she sighed inwardly: *Kind-hearted Miss refuses to hurt the princess, leaving Prince Yi crushed—pray he does not tear the roof off.* Dropping a curtsy, she said, "Please step inside, Your Highness."

"Is Youqi Jushi home? I need to see her."

"My young mistress stepped out an hour ago."

"Where did she go?"

Matron Wei shook her head. "She left no word."

"When will she be back?"

Another shake. "Hard to say."

"Is she truly marrying into the Chen family tomorrow?"

Reading the desperate disbelief on his face, Matron Wei confirmed softly, "Indeed she is."

A hollow ache slammed Shen Jianchen so hard he could not even squeeze out a farewell; turning silently, he climbed back into his carriage as Matron Wei bowed after him.

Minutes down the street, realization struck: she had to be at the Divine Willow! Ordering the driver straight out the city gate toward the foothills, his carriage thundered out into the falling dusk at the exact moment Qianli Xun's carriage rolled in through the gate—neither glancing out the curtains, passing inches apart on opposite roads.

Reaching the Divine Willow under moonlight, Shen Jianchen searched every shadow and found emptiness. Cold dread crept down his spine—were they truly barred from ever being together? Laying a palm on the thick bark, he whispered, "Divine Willow, you dragged Qianli Xun and me into this era—why deny us our shot? Did you send me across a thousand years just to lose her for good?"

Only wind whispered through the leaves.

Dropping his hand in bitter frustration, he muttered, "Always silent, cold as stone—waste of breath talking to you! If she is not here, back to Wangyue Pavilion—I refuse to believe I cannot see her tonight!"

Back at Wangyue Pavilion, the gates stood barred tight. Pounding the wood himself until a sleepy servant cracked it open and dropped in shock before Prince Yi, Shen Jianchen demanded, "Has Youqi Jushi returned?!"

"Master ordered Miss to depart from Zhu Manor tomorrow, Your Highness—she must have reached Zhu Manor by now."

Hard lead at last! Back into the carriage and racing straight for Zhu Manor—criss-crossing the city all night just to see her face, get the truth, and smash this wedding!

Arriving at Zhu Manor, Shen Jianchen stormed straight through the side gate. Terrified servants dared not block him and led him into the reception hall, where he stood without sitting and barked, "Bring Youqi Jushi to me right now!"

Bewildered why a fuming prince demanded the daughter instead of the Prefect, the servant scurried off to fetch her—while another had already alerted Zhu Xiyan mid-dowry-inventory. Unsurprised yet panicked that Prince Yi meant to wreck tomorrow's ceremony, Zhu Xiyan dropped the lists and summoned the entire family to the reception hall to form a united wall.

Back in her old Zhu Manor courtyard, Qianli Xun sat staring at the crimson bridal gown hung by the wall in dull gloom. Was she really walking down the aisle tomorrow? *You already said yes—quit waffling!* Just then a servant called outside: "Miss, Prince Yi is here demanding to see you!"

Qianli Xun shot to her feet and stepped outside. "Where is he?"

"In the reception hall."

A sudden jolt of warmth spiked through her before worry drowned it out—had he come to stop tomorrow's wedding?

Hurrying out beside her, Kuqiao fretted, "What do we do, Miss?"

Letting out a long breath, Qianli Xun forced out words she hated speaking. "Meet soldiers with generals, block floods with earth. Even if he is Prince Yi, he cannot sway my decision."

Kuqiao's heart ached for her mistress: forced to marry a rake worse than Lin Jue, barred from every man she cared for—first losing Young Master Qiao, now losing a prince.

Crossing the Zhu garden to the reception hall, Qianli Xun froze: her father, mother, elder brother, and sister-in-law all knelt on the flagstones outside the hall doorway! So her family knew Prince Yi's heart too—and knelt here begging him to let her go. Bai Feng's harsh words beneath the willow echoed sharp in her ears.

Steadying her pulse, she walked straight to Madam Xiang and reached for her arms. "Rise, Mother—Father, Brother, Sister-in-Law, get up! I will handle my own mess."

"Kneel down too!" Zhu Xiyan barked. "Until Prince Yi promises to let you go, none of us rises!"

Refusing to defy her husband, Madam Xiang instead tugged her daughter's sleeve. "Kneel quick and beg Prince Yi not to ruin the wedding!"

"Kneel if you want—I am not kneeling for this!" Qianli Xun pulled free. "I will talk to Prince Yi face-to-face."

Just as she stepped toward the doorway, Prince Yi's voice rang out: "There you are at last—made me hunt half the night."

Lifting her eyes in the courtyard lantern glow, she saw his silhouette—no longer Zhao Pu, simply Shen Jianchen. Never had she wanted more to run straight into his arms, yet she choked it back, eyes stinging red behind a cold scoff. "What stunt is Your Highness pulling, making my whole family kneel in the dirt begging you?"

Wasting zero breath, Shen Jianchen cut straight to the bone. "I chased all over town tonight and still tracked you down—proof that if you fight hard enough, no wall holds! Why give up so easy and wed Chen Haoyu tomorrow? Say the word and fight alongside me to break this betrothal!"

Kneeling nearby, Chen Wenxin held her breath—tonight proved her chuiwan hunch dead right! When on earth had Prince Yi fallen for Shuzhen?! One wrong slip and the Chens would cross imperial wrath!

Keeping her frosty mask up, Qianli Xun retorted, "People change their minds! I refused before, now I accept—what is so weird? And why on earth should I team up with Your Highness?"

Zero heed for the kneeling Zhu family, Shen Jianchen declared, "Because I love you and want you by my side!"

Thrilled, flushed, panicked, and furious all at once, Qianli Xun snapped, "Watch your loose tongue, Prince Yi—eat my fist!"

Her punch flew straight out; Shen Jianchen caught it smoothly. Her blows held less sting than usual yet kept sharp force—pouring her silent plea through every strike for him to stop throwing himself against a stone wall. Meeting strike for strike, Shen Jianchen answered right back through his palms: *Never giving you up.*

Watching from their knees, the Zhu family trembled—terrified Shuzhen might offend the prince or get hurt, while forced at last to admit this fierce fighter was nothing like the frail daughter they used to know.

Hurrying into the courtyard and taking in the kneeling family and sparring pair at a glance, Matron Wei dropped straight to her knees too. "What good does trading fists with my young mistress do, Your Highness?!"

Never fighting in earnest, both dropped their hands at once.

Bowing low from her knees, Matron Wei spoke steadily. "Master, Madam, and my young mistress all know Your Highness's heart. With things at this pass, take one step back so everyone can find peace."

"Step back? YOU all find peace—what about me?!" Snatching Qianli Xun's hand, Shen Jianchen locked eyes with her. "Come with me right now! Tonight! We leave Lin'an, I ditch this princely title, and with our skills we will thrive anywhere in this dynasty!"

When Wenyuan had offered to elope, she had hardened her heart and walked away; hearing Shen Jianchen ask tonight made her heart stutter. Say yes, and the wide sky opened before them—none of these cages truly belonged to her anyway...

"Has Your Highness spared one thought for what happens to Princess Jiahe if you carry my mistress off?!"

Without Matron Wei's cutting question, Qianli Xun might well have nodded and bolted straight out of Zhu Manor with him. Hit dead center in her softest spot, she pulled her hand free. "Go back, Your Highness. My mind to marry into the Chen family is cast in iron."

Shen Jianchen seized her hand back tight—and truth be told, she barely even tried pulling free. "Do not fret over Mengrong, Matron Wei—I will see her well cared for..."

Before he finished, a trembling, familiar voice shattered the night:

"Brother... so the one in your heart... really is... Shuzhen..."

Standing at the archway, cheeks glistening with tears, was Qiao Mengrong. Every drop of warmth drained from Qianli Xun's veins; ripping her hand free of Shen Jianchen's grip, she burned with shame for daring to dream of running away with him.

Swaying under the blow, Qiao Mengrong's knees gave out—

In one blur of motion, Shen Jianchen lunged forward and caught her before she hit the stones.
