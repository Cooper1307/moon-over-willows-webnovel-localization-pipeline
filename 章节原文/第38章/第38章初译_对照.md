第38章 终于得见韦嬷嬷
## Chapter 38: Meeting Matron Wei at Last

千里寻已在竹林度过七八日，每日不是欣赏竹林美景，就是阅读苦蕨找来的话本。
她越读越兴奋，终于在这个时代找到用武之地，她可以把曾经利用网络写过的故事改编成话本，供百姓娱乐。
说写就写，千里寻提笔在纸上书写起来……
一直写了几十张纸，终于撂下笔。
夏果帮小姐整理纸张，边整理，边瞅着纸张上的字看。
“小姐真厉害，能写这么多字。”
“你可认得？”
“只认得几个。”
千里寻甚觉可惜，夏果长得如此貌美，如若生在富贵人家，定是琴棋书画样样精通。
“以后我让苦荞教你识字。”
夏果开心道：“谢谢小姐！”
“苦荞呢？”
“苦荞姐姐回旧屋给小姐做午饭。”
千里寻透过门望向外面的天空，慨叹道：“时间过得真快，都晌午了。”
苦荞做好午饭，用竹篮提回竹屋，伺候小姐吃过饭后，又开始收拾，这是她每日的生活主题。
千里寻吩咐她把写好的话本交给苦蕨，麻烦苦蕨拿到集市，随便找位说话艺人给了。
苦荞收拾好后，提上竹篮，拿着写好的话本往旧屋而去。
等苦荞再回来时，身边多一位妇人，两个丫鬟和两个小厮。
苦荞先进竹屋禀报：“小姐，您猜我把谁带来？”
千里寻继续翻着已看完的话本：“谁？”
“韦嬷嬷，其他地方的租子已收完，今日来到竹林，听说小姐在，赶来拜见。”
韦嬷嬷掌握着朱淑真的经济大权，又是她的奶娘，千里寻早想见她，没成想在竹林给碰上。
千里寻放下书：“快！有请！”
Seven or eight peaceful days flew by in the bamboo grove, Qianli Xun splitting her hours between admiring emerald bamboo vistas and devouring the huaben storybooks Kujue brought back. Excitement climbed with every page: she had found her true craft in this era! She could adapt her old modern web novels into Song-dynasty huaben scripts for common folk to enjoy!

苦荞出去说道：“韦嬷嬷，小姐让您进去。”
No sooner thought than done—she picked up her brush and began writing nonstop. Dozens of pages later, she set down the brush at last.

韦嬷嬷是位五十岁左右的妇人，人看上去慈祥中不失威严，威严中藏着精明。她进入竹屋，先行礼。苦荞拿个垫子放在小姐对面，韦嬷嬷很习惯地坐下。
Stacking the sheets neat, Xiaguo stole admiring looks at the crisp lines of ink. "You're amazing, Miss, writing so many characters!"

“没想到在这里遇见姑娘，苦荞在路上已将姑娘的情况告诉我，早知道我晚些出来收租，也不至于你被姑爷打得失忆。”
"Can you read them?"

说着韦嬷嬷流出两滴眼泪，她用巾帕沾去泪水，继续说：“和离之事没有成功，姑娘不要灰心，等回到林府，我再劝说姑爷，他若还不同意，我们搬出林府，临安城空着的宅子多的是，姑娘买一处就是，何必在林府受姑爷的气。”
"Only a few."

韦嬷嬷一番话，千里寻听着舒畅无比，这才是家人该说的话，亲切感油然而生，也理解为何朱淑真愿意把财政大权交给她。
What a pity, Qianli Xun thought: blessed with looks like Xiaguo's, born into a wealthy home she'd have mastered zither, chess, calligraphy, and painting alike! "I'll have Kuqiao teach you to read from now on."

“嬷嬷不必担心，我和离之心坚决，也正为此努力，如若林觉还不同意，就听你的，搬出林府。”
Xiaguo lit up. "Thank you, Miss!"

姑娘说话比以前底气更足，也更有力度。韦嬷嬷意识到，她真的变了。变了也好，省得像以前一样任人欺负。
"Where's Kuqiao?"

“姑娘如此说，我深感欣慰。”
"Sister Kuqiao went back to the old cottage to cook your lunch."

“嬷嬷前来收租可有地方住？”
Glancing out the doorway at the high sun, Qianli Xun sighed, "Time flies—already noon!"

“村长已安排好竹屋，离姑娘不远，每年来都住那里。”
Soon Kuqiao returned carrying a bamboo basket of lunch, waited on Miss as she ate, and cleared up—her steady daily rhythm. Qianli Xun told her to pass the finished huaben script to Kujue and have him hand it to any storyteller at the market. Packing the basket and clutching the manuscript, Kuqiao headed back to her family's old cottage.

“有村长安排，我就放心。”
When Kuqiao returned, an older woman walked beside her flanked by two maids and two page boys. Slipping indoors first, Kuqiao grinned, "Guess who I brought, Miss!"

夏果泡好茶，给韦嬷嬷端来，放在桌上。
Still flipping through a finished storybook, Qianli Xun looked up. "Who?"

韦嬷嬷抬头看一眼夏果：“这丫鬟我怎么没见过？”
"Matron Wei! Done collecting rents elsewhere, she came here for the bamboo grove, heard you were staying here, and hurried right over!"

夏果赶紧说：“嬷嬷好，我是小姐新收的丫鬟，名叫夏果。”
Holder of Zhu Shuzhen's purse strings and her loyal wet nurse—Qianli Xun had been eager to meet her, never expecting to cross paths right here in the grove! Tossing the book aside, she called, "Quick—invite her in!"

“夏果，你到竹屋外等着，我与姑娘说些事。”
Stepping back out, Kuqiao announced, "Miss invites you inside, Matron Wei!"

夏果很听话，到竹屋外站在韦嬷嬷带来的丫鬟和小厮身旁。
Around fifty, carrying a blend of warmth, authority, and sharp judgment, Matron Wei stepped inside and bowed low. Kuqiao set a cushion across from her mistress, and Matron Wei settled down at ease. "Never dreamed I'd meet you out here, young lady! Kuqiao caught me up on the walk over; had I known, I would've put off rent collection instead of leaving you to get beaten into amnesia by Son-in-law!"

竹屋内韦嬷嬷说：“姑娘怎么能给身边收这么漂亮的丫鬟？”
Two tears slipped down her cheeks; dabbing them with a handkerchief, she pressed on crisp, "Don't lose heart over the failed divorce, young lady. Once back in Lin Manor, I'll talk to Son-in-law myself—and if he still refuses, we move right out! Plenty of vacant houses across Lin'an; buy one of our own instead of swallowing his garbage!"

千里寻不解：“嬷嬷为何如此说？”
Music to Qianli Xun's ears! Now this was real family! Instant warmth surged through her chest, showing at once why Zhu Shuzhen trusted this woman with her whole dowry. "Don't worry, Matron. My resolve to divorce hasn't budged an inch, and if Lin Jue drags his feet, we'll do just what you said and move out!"

“大家闺秀身边的丫鬟就应该像苦荞这样，长相不差但也不出挑，夏果长相太过出众，恐带出去，大家只顾看她，而不顾姑娘，岂不会强压姑娘一头？”
Hearing steel and grit in her voice, Matron Wei saw the change plain—good! Better strong than pushed around like before. "Hearing you speak like that puts my heart at ease."

“嬷嬷多虑，我不在乎这些，再说若把她带出去，有好人家的公子看上，也算给她寻个好去处。”
"Do you have a place to stay while collecting rent, Matron?"

“姑娘既这样想，我也不多说什么，就是担心夏果的长相会给姑娘带来麻烦。”
"Village head set up a bamboo cottage close by, same place I stay every year."

苦荞插嘴道：“嬷嬷说我长得不好看，我不怪您。不过您说得没错，夏果一出现，小姐确实遇到麻烦，多亏小姐拳头厉害，才不怕地痞流氓。”
"Good, glad you're settled."

“在我眼里，你长得刚刚合适，比那夏果强多了！呵呵……”韦嬷嬷笑完继续说，“我听苦荞说，姑娘被姑爷打晕后醒来，竟然神奇般地会拳法，真替姑娘高兴，以后看姑爷还敢打你!”
Just then Xiaguo brought fresh tea over and set it on the table. Glancing up at the striking girl, Matron Wei paused. "Don't recognize this maid."

韦嬷嬷是第一个知道朱淑真醒来后会拳法没有惊讶甚至高兴的人，更让千里寻感到亲切：“我有事与嬷嬷商量。”
Xiaguo curtsied quick. "Good day, Matron. I'm Xiaguo, Miss's newly hired maid."

“姑娘请说。”
"Wait outside the cottage a moment, Xiaguo. Private business with my young lady."

“在竹林生活这段时日，我发现竹林的百姓生活挺不容易，想着今年的租子能不能减免些？”
Obedient, Xiaguo stepped out beside Matron Wei's own servants.

苦荞在一旁听了，甚是高兴。
Once alone indoors, Matron Wei spoke blunt: "Why hire a maid THIS gorgeous, young lady?"

“姑娘与我想到一处去，可如今这竹林很快就不是姑娘的，今年的租子没有办法再收。”
Qianli Xun blinked. "What's wrong with that, Matron?"

“我因失忆，名下到底有多少田产，根本不记得。一到竹林，苦荞说我是竹林的主人，才知道我名下有这份田产，竹民们见我也是东家长东家短的，嬷嬷为何要说很快竹林不是我的？”
"A noble lady's personal maids ought to look neat yet modest like Kuqiao! Xiaguo is way too eye-catching; bring her out in public and folks stare at the maid instead of the mistress! Won't she steal your thunder?"

“姑娘不是因为失忆不记得名下有多少田产，而是姑娘根本不关心田产之事，都交由我负责，等回城里，我定向姑娘一一细说。至于竹林，本就是姑娘的。我前来收租，找到村长，他告诉我竹林的田根在陈家公子手中，他要按照原价收回竹林，也会帮村民把今年的租子一并给了。”
"Overthinking it, Matron. I don't care about stuff like that! Besides, if some good young gentleman spots her and takes a shine to her, all the better for her future."

千里寻搞不清田根是什么，听到韦嬷嬷提起陈家公子，猜想定是陈浩宇搞鬼：“为何我是竹林的东家，陈家又能插一杠子进来？”
"If that's your view, I won't argue—just worried her face will invite trouble for you."

韦嬷嬷解释道：“当年朱家买竹林时，与售卖之人签的是活卖契约，朱家可以自由使用和收租，但田根还在以前的主人手中，他有随时按照原价收回的权利。”
Kuqiao piped up, "Calling me plain, Matron, I'll forgive you! Still, you hit the nail on the head: the second Xiaguo showed up, trouble hit—lucky Miss's fists sent those local thugs packing!"

千里寻总算听明白：“难道出售竹林的是陈家？”
"To my eyes you look just right, beats Xiaguo by a mile! Haha..." Laughing warm, Matron Wei went on, "Kuqiao told me you woke up knowing martial arts—thrilled for you! Let's see Son-in-law dare lay a finger on you now!"

“不是，另有其人，我估计是陈家看上这片竹林，从卖主手中出高价买了田根，陈家是临安首富，有的是钱。想不通的是，竹林收成一般，朱家和陈家还是亲家，陈家怎么会这么干？”
First person in this whole dynasty to cheer rather than freak out over her fighting skills—Qianli Xun warmed to Matron Wei even faster! "Got something to discuss with you, Matron."

千里寻气道：“肯定是陈浩宇想要报复我。”
"Go ahead, young lady."

“报复？”韦嬷嬷不解。
"Living here these days, I see how tough these bamboo villagers have it. Can we cut their rent this year?"

苦荞解释道：“嬷嬷一直在乡下收租，很多事情不清楚，小姐两次阻止陈公子欺负良家妇女，定是他怀恨在心，才想出这招对付小姐。”
Beside them, Kuqiao beamed in delight.

“原来如此，姑娘做得没错，既然陈公子想要竹林，给他便是，其他田产够姑娘用，少个竹林不算什么。”
"Great minds think alike—except this bamboo grove won't belong to you much longer, so we can't collect rent this year anyway."

千里寻更加喜欢韦嬷嬷，她的心胸和想法与自己实在相投。可她不想轻易饶了陈浩宇，必须给他些颜色瞧瞧，再让他吐出些东西来。
"Wait—with amnesia I didn't even know what estates I own! Only found out this grove was mine when Kuqiao told me and everyone started calling me Proprietor! Why won't it be mine anymore?"

“他想用手中的田根收回竹林，那我就会会他。”
"Not amnesia; you never bothered tracking estates before and left everything to me. Once back in town I'll walk you through every single ledger. As for this grove, it was yours—until I went to collect rent and the village head told me Young Master Chen now holds the land root! He plans to redeem the grove at original price and cover this year's village rent out of pocket too!"

聊完后，韦嬷嬷在竹屋里转了一圈儿，十分满意，将苦荞夸赞一番。
Unsure what "land root" meant yet catching "Young Master Chen," Qianli Xun saw red—Chen Haoyu's dirty trick! "Wait—if I'm the owner, how does the Chen family butt in?"

拜别姑娘，出了竹屋，见夏果恭敬地站在外面，上前仔细瞧了瞧，用温和而有力度的语气说：“以后跟着姑娘出去，心里要明白自己是伺候姑娘的人，不能喧宾夺主，要守好丫鬟的本分，记住没？”
Matron Wei explained, "When the Zhu family bought this grove, they signed a conditional sale contract with the original owner, giving the Zhus use and rent rights while the land root stayed with the seller, letting him buy it back at original cost anytime."

夏果老实答道：“嬷嬷放心，我会牢记在心。”
Light dawned. "Was the seller the Chen family?"

“回去吧，好生伺候姑娘。”韦嬷嬷带着丫鬟和小厮离开。
"No, someone else. My guess is the Chens paid top silver to buy the land root off the seller! Richest house in Lin'an, swimming in cash! Weird part is, this grove yields next to nothing, plus Zhus and Chens are in-laws—why pull a stunt like this?"

夏果回到屋内，见小姐又在看书，不敢打扰，进里面去找苦荞。
Qianli Xun fumed, "Because Chen Haoyu wants revenge on me!"

苦荞忙着绣花，她蹭到苦荞身边：“苦荞姐姐，小姐让你教我识字，你教教我呗？”
"Revenge?" Matron Wei frowned.

苦荞边绣花边说：“其实啊，我们做丫鬟的，识不识字并不重要，重要的是把主子伺候好。”
Kuqiao filled her in: "Out collecting rents, you missed it, Matron—Miss busted up Young Master Chen harassing innocent girls twice! He's nursing a grudge and pulled this to spite her!"

“可小姐让你教我，关键是我也想学，再说苦荞姐姐是丫鬟不也会识字？”
"Aha! Good on you for stepping in, young lady! If Young Master Chen wants the bamboo grove, let him have it—your other estates leave you rolling in silver anyway; losing one grove is pocket change."

“好吧，既然小姐吩咐，我教你便是，你每日把小姐写废的纸拿来，我按照上面的字教你。”
Matron Wei's cool head matched Qianli Xun's own vibe, yet no way was she letting Chen Haoyu walk off smug without bleeding a little! "Wants to use the land root to reclaim the grove? Fine—let me meet him face to face!"

“谢谢苦荞姐姐。”
Chatting done, Matron Wei toured the cozy cottage, praised Kuqiao's tidy work, and bade farewell. Stepping outside, she looked Xiaguo over standing dutiful by the doorway and delivered a mild yet steel-edged warning: "When you go out serving our young lady, remember your place: never outshine your mistress, and keep a maid's proper bounds. Clear?"

千里寻看话本一直保持一个动作，很不舒服，她放下书，起身走出竹屋，打算活动活动。
Xiaguo bowed low. "Yes, Matron. I'll engrave it on my heart."

苦蕨正站在外面犹豫要不要进去。
"Go back in and wait on her well." Gathering her attendants, Matron Wei departed.

千里寻走下竹屋的台阶，来到苦蕨跟前：“你怎么站在这里？”
Stepping inside to find Miss reading again, Xiaguo slipped to the inner room where Kuqiao sat embroidering and sidled close. "Sister Kuqiao, Miss asked you to teach me to read—will you teach me?"

别看苦蕨长得健壮，却是个害羞之人，一开口说话就紧张，尤其是面对像朱淑真这等大家闺秀。
Keeping her needle moving, Kuqiao remarked, "Truth is, reading or not doesn't matter for maids; serving our mistress well is what counts."

“东……东家，你写的……我给了说话艺人，他喜欢，直说好，让……继续，继续写。”
"Still, Miss told you to, and I really want to learn! Besides, you're a maid and you know how to read!"

千里寻笑道：“紧张什么？我又不是老虎！”
"Fine, since Miss ordered it. Every day collect Miss's discarded practice sheets, and I'll teach you from those characters."

苦蕨用手挠挠头，害羞道：“东家是贵人，我见贵人……紧张。”
"Thank you, Sister Kuqiao!"

“我在竹林这段时间，送手稿的事就麻烦你，放心，不会白跑，我会让苦荞给你跑路费。”
Stiff from sitting over storybooks, Qianli Xun set her volume down and stepped outdoors to stretch. Right outside hovered Kujue, hesitating whether to knock. Walking down the bamboo steps, Qianli Xun smiled, "What are you standing out here for?"

“白跑我乐意。”
Sturdy as Kujue was, shyness tied his tongue around refined ladies like Zhu Shuzhen. "Pr... Proprietor, gave your pages to a storyteller... he loved it, praised it nonstop, begs you to keep... keep writing!"

“你可真老实，回去忙吧。”
Qianli Xun laughed. "Why so jumpy? I'm not a tiger!"

苦蕨得到允许扭头跑走。
Scratching his head sheepish, Kujue mumbled, "Proprietor's a grand lady—I get nervous around bigwigs..."

千里寻正想再回竹屋，余光瞥到陈浩宇带着人朝竹屋方向走来，心想：“我不找你，你倒送上门来，老娘正好与你聊聊竹林之事。”
"While I'm staying here, I'll trouble you to deliver scripts—not for free, Kuqiao will pay your errand fee."

陈浩宇身边除四个地痞流氓外，还多出一位，从步履来看，身手不错，看来他今日有备而来。
"Happy to run 'em free!"

千里寻仔细瞧那位高手，长得虎背熊腰，袒胸露背，身上的肌肉十分发达，让人不由产生畏惧。她能否打得过对方，还不好判断，只有交过手之后才能得知。
"Too honest for your own good! Go on back to work." Excused, Kujue spun around and trotted off.

陈浩宇走近，冷笑道：“喂！朱淑真，竹林已经是我的，你赶紧离开！”
Turning back toward her cottage, Qianli Xun caught movement out of the corner of her eye: Chen Haoyu swaggering straight toward her with an entourage! Didn't even have to hunt him down—he marched right to her door! Beside his usual four street thugs strode a newcomer whose steady gait screamed martial master; Chen Haoyu had brought heavy muscle today. Sizing up the giant—broad shoulders, bare brawny chest rippling with thick muscle, intimidating build—Qianli Xun couldn't tell who'd win until fists met.

千里寻不屑道：“陈浩宇，田根虽在你手中，可你实在缺乏契约精神。当初朱家与竹林主人签过活卖契约，需用原价把竹林赎回，钱还没给老娘，就说竹林是你的，实在可笑！也有失富家公子的身份！”
Strutting close, Chen Haoyu sneered, "Hey, Zhu Shuzhen! This bamboo grove belongs to me now—pack up and get out!"

“银子很快就到，银子一到，马上给本公子搬出竹林，顺便把你刚收的丫鬟给本公子留下！”
Qianli Xun scoffed right back. "Holding the land root and still zero clue how contracts work, Chen Haoyu! The Zhu family signed a conditional sale contract—you have to redeem it at original price first! Haven't paid me a single coin and you're claiming the land? Hilarious—some rich young master!"

“放你的臭狗屁！即使银子到了，老娘也不会立刻搬走，告诉你，老娘要在竹林住满一个月，一个月后，你想挽留，老娘都不稀罕！”
"Silver's on the way! Second it arrives, haul yourself out of my grove—and leave that new maid behind for me!"

“啧啧啧，一个大家闺秀，张口闭口臭狗屁，实在影响形象，昨日听我姐说你跟林觉闹和离，像你这样粗鲁的大家闺秀谁还敢要？我要是你的夫君，早把你休了！”
"Stuff your garbage! Even after you pay, I'm not budging an inch till my full month is up—after that, you couldn't beg me to stay!"

千里寻知道对方是想故意激怒她，她稳住情绪，保持理智，现在还不是动手的最佳时机，一旦动手，想要牵制对方会难上加难。
"Tsk, tsk, a noble lady tossing out street curses—wrecking your classy image! Heard from my sister yesterday you're fighting Lin Jue for divorce. Acting this crude, who'd even want you? Were I your husband, I'd have cast you out ages ago!"

“陈浩宇，你不就是想要竹林，老娘给你，但你必须把与竹林相关的人请齐全，再答应老娘几个条件，竹林定双手奉上！”
