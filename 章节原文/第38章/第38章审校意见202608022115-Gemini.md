### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 人名格式统一与穿越者心态（对应第1段：网文改编话本）
> **【原文】** 每日不是欣赏竹林美景，就是阅读苦蕨找来的话本。她把曾经利用网络写过的故事改编成话本，供百姓娱乐。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Each day, she either admired the bamboo scenery or read the vernacular stories that **Ku Jue** had found for her. ...adapt the stories she had once written **online** into vernacular tales...
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Day in and day out, when she wasn't admiring the emerald scenery, she was engrossed in the popular storybooks **Kujue** had scavenged for her. ...adapt the **web novels she used to write in her past life** into popular tales for the local commoners.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. **拼写修正**：将 "Ku Jue" 统一为术语表规范的无空格连写 **Kujue**（同理，"Ku Qiao" 统一修正为 **Kuqiao**）。
    2. “网络写过的故事”翻译为 "web novels she used to write in her past life"，更加彰显女主现代穿越者（网文作者）的独特身份感。

#### 2. 韦嬷嬷霸气护短的台词升级（对应第16、17段：劝离与搬家）
> **【原文】** 韦嬷嬷流出两滴眼泪... 和离之事没有成功，姑娘不要灰心... 搬出林府，临安城空着的宅子多的是，姑娘买一处就是，何必在林府受姑爷的气。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    As she spoke, Nanny Wei shed a few tears... "The **mutual divorce** didn't succeed. Don't be discouraged... When we return to the Lin Manor, **I'll persuade your husband again**... Why suffer your husband's temper at the Lin Manor?"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Two tears welled in Nanny Wei's eyes. Dabbing them away with a silk handkerchief, she continued fiercely, "Do not lose heart over the **failed divorce**, My Lady. Once we return to the Lin residence, **I’ll give that son-in-law a piece of my mind!** If he still refuses an **amicable divorce**, we’ll simply pack up and move out. Lin'an is full of vacant estates—you can easily buy one for yourself. Why stay and suffer under his roof?"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    “和离”统一使用术语表规范 **amicable divorce**。“我再劝说姑爷”翻译为更具护短魄力的英文俚语 "I’ll give that son-in-law a piece of my mind!"（看我不狠狠训那个姑爷一顿！），让韦嬷嬷这个护主心切的奶娘形象瞬间鲜活起来。

#### 3. 宋代地契法律术语的地道化（对应第35-37段：活卖契约与田根）
> **【原文】** 朱家买竹林时，与售卖之人签的是活卖契约，朱家可以自由使用和收租，但田根还在以前的主人手中，他有随时按照原价收回的权利。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Nanny Wei explained, "When the Zhu family bought the bamboo forest years ago, they signed a **conditional sale contract** with the seller. The Zhu family could freely use it and collect rent, but the **land root** remained with the previous owner..."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "When the Zhu family originally bought this forest, they signed a **redeemable deed**," Nanny Wei explained. "Our family got full rights to use the land and collect rent, but the ultimate **land-root title** remained with the original seller, giving him the right to buy it back at the original price whenever he wished."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    中国古代古代土地买卖中的“活卖契约”在英美法系和历史译著中，最地道的对应表达是 **redeemable deed**（可回购的契约/典当式契约）；“田根”翻译为 **land-root title**，逻辑表达比单译 "land root" 更加严谨自然。

#### 4. 害羞少年与女主内心独白（对应第54、57段：苦蕨结巴与迎敌心理）
> **【原文】** "东……东家，你写的……我给了说话艺人，他喜欢..."
> 千里寻心想："我不找你，你倒送上门来，老娘正好与你聊聊竹林之事。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    "P-Pproprietor... what you wrote... I gave it to a storyteller. He liked it..."
    ...She thought, *I wasn't looking for you, but you've come to my door. **This queen** will have a chat with you about the bamboo forest.*
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "L-Landlady... the story you wrote..." Kujue stammered, blushing. "I gave it to the storyteller in town. He... he loved it!"
    ...***Well, well,*** she thought with a cold smirk. ***I wasn't looking for you, but you walked right into my trap. Yours truly will be happy to settle this bamboo forest business with you.***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. **术语规范**：老娘统一翻译为术语表规范的 **Yours truly**。
    2. **斜体排版**：心理活动全部严格转为无引号的**斜体 (Italics)**，增强第一视角爽感。

#### 5. 主角交锋与口角对骂（对应第62、63段：放你的臭狗屁！）
> **【原文】** "放你的臭狗屁！即使银子到了，老娘也不会立刻搬走，告诉，老娘要在竹林住满一个月..."

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    "**Bullshit!** Even if the silver arrives, **this queen** won't move out immediately. Let me tell you—**this queen** will stay in the bamboo forest for a full month..."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "**Like hell I will!**" Qianli Xun scoffed. "Even when your silver arrives, **Yours truly** won't budge an inch right away. Hear this—I am staying in this bamboo forest for a full month!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    "Bullshit!" 在英文中通常用于反驳谎言（“瞎说/吹牛！”）。这里女主是在坚决拒绝对方的威胁要求，用英文暴脾气口语 "**Like hell I will!**"（想得美/老娘偏不！）更加精准地展现了千里寻不甘示弱的硬核气场。
