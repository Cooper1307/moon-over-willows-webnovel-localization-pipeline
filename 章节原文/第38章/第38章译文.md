# Chapter 38: Finally Meeting Nanny Wei

Qianli Xun had spent seven or eight days in the bamboo forest. Each day, she either admired the bamboo scenery or read the vernacular stories that Ku Jue had found for her.

The more she read, the more excited she became. She had finally found her place in this era—she could adapt the stories she had once written online into vernacular tales for the people's entertainment.

No sooner said than done. Qianli Xun picked up her brush and began writing on paper...

She wrote dozens of pages before finally setting down her brush.

Xiaguo helped her mistress organize the papers, glancing at the characters as she worked.

"You're amazing, Young Miss. You can write so many characters."

"Can you read them?"

"Only a few."

Qianli Xun felt it was a pity. Xiaguo was so beautiful—if she had been born into a wealthy family, she would surely have been skilled in zither, chess, calligraphy, and painting.

"From now on, I'll have Ku Qiao teach you to read."

Xiaguo said happily, "Thank you, Young Miss!"

"Where is Ku Qiao?"

"Sister Ku Qiao went back to the old house to prepare lunch for you."

Qianli Xun looked through the door at the sky outside and sighed, "Time flies so fast. It's already noon."

Ku Qiao finished preparing lunch, carried it back to the bamboo house in a bamboo basket, and served her mistress. After the meal, she began cleaning up—this was her daily routine.

Qianli Xun instructed her to give the finished manuscript to Ku Jue, asking him to take it to the market and give it to any storyteller he could find.

After cleaning up, Ku Qiao picked up the bamboo basket and headed to the old house with the finished manuscript.

When Ku Qiao returned, she was accompanied by a middle-aged woman, two maids, and two young servants.

Ku Qiao entered the bamboo house first to report, "Young Miss, guess who I've brought?"

Qianli Xun continued flipping through the finished manuscript. "Who?"

"Nanny Wei. She's finished collecting rent from the other properties and came to the bamboo forest today. Hearing you were here, she rushed to pay her respects."

Nanny Wei controlled Zhu Shuzhen's finances and was also her wet nurse. Qianli Xun had wanted to meet her for a long time—she never expected to run into her in the bamboo forest.

Qianli Xun put down her book. "Quick! Invite her in!"

Ku Qiao went out and said, "Nanny Wei, the Young Miss invites you in."

Nanny Wei was a woman around fifty years old. She looked kindly yet authoritative, with shrewdness hidden beneath her stern demeanor. She entered the bamboo house and bowed first. Ku Qiao placed a cushion opposite the Young Miss, and Nanny Wei sat down quite naturally.

"I never expected to meet you here. Ku Qiao told me about your situation on the way. If I had known, I would have delayed collecting rent, and you wouldn't have been beaten into amnesia by your husband."

As she spoke, Nanny Wei shed a few tears. She dabbed them away with her handkerchief and continued, "The mutual divorce didn't succeed. Don't be discouraged, Young Miss. When we return to the Lin Manor, I'll persuade your husband again. If he still doesn't agree, we'll move out. There are plenty of empty residences in Lin'an City. You can just buy one. Why suffer your husband's temper at the Lin Manor?"

Nanny Wei's words made Qianli Xun feel incredibly comfortable. This was the kind of talk family should have. A sense of warmth arose naturally, and she understood why Zhu Shuzhen was willing to entrust her with financial control.

"Don't worry, Nanny. I'm determined to get the mutual divorce, and I'm working toward it. If Lin Jue still doesn't agree, I'll listen to you and move out of the Lin Manor."

The Young Miss spoke with more confidence and force than before. Nanny Wei realized she had truly changed. It was good that she had changed—no more being bullied like before.

"Hearing you say that, I'm deeply relieved."

"Do you have a place to stay while collecting rent?"

"The village head has already arranged a bamboo house for me. It's not far from you. I stay there every year when I come."

"With the village head arranging it, I can rest assured."

Xiaguo brewed tea and brought it to Nanny Wei, placing it on the table.

Nanny Wei glanced up at Xiaguo. "I've never seen this maid before."

Xiaguo quickly said, "Hello, Nanny. I'm the Young Miss's new maid. My name is Xiaguo."

"Xiaguo, wait outside the bamboo house. I have some matters to discuss with the Young Miss."

Xiaguo obediently went outside and stood beside the maids and servants Nanny Wei had brought.

Inside the bamboo house, Nanny Wei said, "How could you take such a beautiful maid into your service?"

Qianli Xun was puzzled. "Why do you say that, Nanny?"

"A maid beside a young lady of a distinguished family should be like Ku Qiao—good-looking but not outstanding. Xiaguo is too beautiful. I'm afraid if you take her out, everyone will only look at her and ignore you. Wouldn't that overshadow you?"

"You worry too much, Nanny. I don't care about such things. Besides, if I take her out and a young master from a good family takes a liking to her, that would be finding her a good future."

"Since you think that way, I won't say more. I'm just worried Xiaguo's beauty might bring you trouble."

Ku Qiao interjected, "Nanny says I'm not good-looking. I don't blame you. But you're right—ever since Xiaguo appeared, the Young Miss has indeed encountered trouble. Fortunately, the Young Miss's fists are formidable, so she's not afraid of hooligans."

"In my eyes, you look just right—much better than that Xiaguo! Hehe..." After laughing, Nanny Wei continued, "I heard from Ku Qiao that after your husband knocked you unconscious, you miraculously knew martial arts when you woke up. I'm truly happy for you. Now let's see if your husband dares to hit you again!"

Nanny Wei was the first person who, upon learning that Zhu Shuzhen knew martial arts after waking up, showed no surprise but instead expressed happiness. This made Qianli Xun feel even closer to her. "I have something to discuss with you, Nanny."

"Please speak, Young Miss."

"Living in the bamboo forest these past days, I've found that the people here have a hard life. I was wondering if we could reduce or exempt this year's rent?"

Ku Qiao, listening nearby, was very pleased.

"You and I think alike, Young Miss. But now, this bamboo forest will soon no longer be yours. There's no way to collect this year's rent."

"Because of my amnesia, I don't remember at all how much property I own. As soon as I arrived at the bamboo forest, Ku Qiao told me I was the owner, and only then did I learn I had this property. The bamboo people see me and talk about this and that—why do you say the bamboo forest will soon not be mine?"

"It's not because of amnesia that you don't remember your property—it's because you never cared about such matters and left everything to me. When we return to the city, I'll explain everything to you in detail. As for the bamboo forest, it was originally yours. When I came to collect rent, I found the village head. He told me the land root of the bamboo forest is in the hands of Young Master Chen. He wants to redeem the bamboo forest at the original price and will also help the villagers pay this year's rent."

Qianli Xun didn't understand what a land root was. Hearing Nanny Wei mention Young Master Chen, she guessed it was Chen Haoyu's doing. "Why am I the owner of the bamboo forest, yet the Chen family can stick their nose in?"

Nanny Wei explained, "When the Zhu family bought the bamboo forest years ago, they signed a conditional sale contract with the seller. The Zhu family could freely use it and collect rent, but the land root remained with the previous owner. He has the right to redeem it at the original price at any time."

Qianli Xun finally understood. "Was it the Chen family who sold the bamboo forest?"

"No, it was someone else. I suspect the Chen family took a fancy to this bamboo forest and bought the land root from the seller at a high price. The Chen family is the wealthiest in Lin'an—they have plenty of money. What I can't figure out is that the bamboo forest's yield is average, and the Zhu and Chen families are related by marriage. Why would the Chen family do this?"

Qianli Xun said angrily, "It must be Chen Haoyu trying to get revenge on me."

"Revenge?" Nanny Wei was puzzled.

Ku Qiao explained, "Nanny has been collecting rent in the countryside and doesn't know about many things. The Young Miss stopped Young Master Chen from bullying decent women twice. He must have held a grudge and came up with this trick to deal with the Young Miss."

"I see. You did nothing wrong, Young Miss. Since Young Master Chen wants the bamboo forest, just give it to him. Your other properties are enough for you. Losing one bamboo forest is nothing."

Qianli Xun liked Nanny Wei even more. Her mindset and ideas were truly in sync with her own. But she didn't want to let Chen Haoyu off easily. She had to teach him a lesson and make him spit something out.

"He wants to use the land root in his hands to take back the bamboo forest? Then I'll meet with him."

After their chat, Nanny Wei looked around the bamboo house and was very satisfied, praising Ku Qiao profusely.

Taking leave of the Young Miss, she exited the bamboo house and saw Xiaguo standing respectfully outside. She stepped forward to examine her carefully and said in a gentle yet firm tone, "From now on, when you follow the Young Miss out, you must remember in your heart that you are here to serve her. Don't overshadow the mistress. Keep to your duties as a maid. Remember?"

Xiaguo answered honestly, "Don't worry, Nanny. I'll keep it firmly in mind."

"Go back and serve the Young Miss well." Nanny Wei left with her maids and servants.

Xiaguo returned inside and saw the Young Miss reading again. Not daring to disturb her, she went inside to find Ku Qiao.

Ku Qiao was busy embroidering. Xiaguo sidled up to her. "Sister Ku Qiao, the Young Miss told you to teach me to read. Can you teach me?"

Ku Qiao said while embroidering, "Actually, for us maids, whether we can read or not doesn't matter. What matters is serving our mistress well."

"But the Young Miss told you to teach me, and I really want to learn. Besides, Sister Ku Qiao, you're a maid but you can read."

"Alright, since the Young Miss ordered it, I'll teach you. Bring me the Young Miss's discarded paper every day, and I'll teach you the characters on it."

"Thank you, Sister Ku Qiao."

Qianli Xun had been reading in the same position for too long and felt uncomfortable. She put down her book, got up, and walked out of the bamboo house to stretch.

Ku Jue was standing outside, hesitating whether to go in.

Qianli Xun walked down the steps of the bamboo house and came to Ku Jue. "Why are you standing here?"

Despite his sturdy build, Ku Jue was a shy person. He got nervous whenever he spoke, especially in front of a young lady from a distinguished family like Zhu Shuzhen.

"P-Pproprietor... what you wrote... I gave it to a storyteller. He liked it, kept saying it was good, and asked... to c-continue writing."

Qianli Xun laughed. "Why are you so nervous? I'm not a tiger!"

Ku Jue scratched his head shyly. "The proprietor is a noble person. When I see noble people... I get nervous."

"While I'm in the bamboo forest, I'll trouble you with delivering manuscripts. Don't worry—I won't let you run for nothing. I'll have Ku Qiao give you travel expenses."

"I'd be happy to run for free."

"You're truly honest. Go back to your work."

Having received permission, Ku Jue turned and ran off.

Qianli Xun was about to return to the bamboo house when she caught sight of Chen Haoyu leading a group of people toward the bamboo house out of the corner of her eye. She thought, *I wasn't looking for you, but you've come to my door. This queen will have a chat with you about the bamboo forest.*

Besides four hooligans, Chen Haoyu had brought along an extra person. From his stride, he seemed quite skilled. It appeared he had come prepared today.

Qianli Xun examined the expert carefully. He was built like a bear, with his chest and back exposed, his muscles well-developed, inspiring fear in onlookers. Whether she could defeat him was hard to judge—she would only know after they crossed hands.

Chen Haoyu approached and sneered, "Hey! Zhu Shuzhen, the bamboo forest is already mine. Leave immediately!"

Qianli Xun said disdainfully, "Chen Haoyu, although the land root is in your hands, you truly lack contractual spirit. Back then, the Zhu family signed a conditional sale contract with the bamboo forest owner. They need to redeem it at the original price. You haven't given this queen the money yet, and you're already claiming the bamboo forest is yours? Ridiculous! You're also losing the dignity of a wealthy young master!"

"The silver will arrive soon. Once it does, move out of the bamboo forest immediately. And leave the maid you just took in for me!"

"Bullshit! Even if the silver arrives, this queen won't move out immediately. Let me tell you—this queen will stay in the bamboo forest for a full month. After a month, even if you want to keep me, this queen won't be interested!"

"Tsk tsk tsk, a young lady from a distinguished family, cursing left and right—it really damages your image. Yesterday, I heard from my sister that you're demanding a mutual divorce from Lin Jue. Who would dare want a rude young lady like you? If I were your husband, I would have divorced you long ago!"

Qianli Xun knew he was trying to provoke her. She steadied her emotions and kept her rationality. This wasn't the best time to act—once they started fighting, it would be much harder to restrain him.

"Chen Haoyu, you just want the bamboo forest, right? This queen will give it to you. But you must gather all the people related to the bamboo forest and agree to a few conditions from this queen. Then the bamboo forest will be handed over with both hands!"

"Fine! Then tomorrow, at the village head's house. Be there or be square. As for your conditions, we'll talk about them then." Chen Haoyu finished speaking and turned to leave with his men, waving his hand behind him as he walked.

Qianli Xun cursed in her heart, *You spoiled brat!*
