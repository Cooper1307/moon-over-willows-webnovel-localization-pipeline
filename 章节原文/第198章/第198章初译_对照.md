第198章 一早迎来乔公子
## Chapter 198: An Early Morning Visit from Young Master Qiao

第二天一大早，乔文仙急急匆匆来到幽林别居。婚后，他还是第一次来。
他直奔仪王的院子，进入屋内。仪王刚刚用过早饭，丫鬟正收拾碗筷，仪王则看似悠闲地品着茶。
申简辰见文渊来访，甚是热情：“我还以为你有了妻妾，把我忘了，以后不会再来幽林别居。”
乔文仙很客气地拜过仪王，没有说话，坐到一旁，面无表情。
申简辰吩咐道：“白风，倒茶。”
白风提起茶壶，为乔公子倒上一盏：“乔公子，请用茶。”
南双给白风使眼色，白风放下茶壶，跟南双走出屋子，两人自乔公子婚宴后一直未见，想好好聊聊。
申简辰问：“你怎么不说话？”
乔文仙盯着仪王，反问道：“仪王为何昨日没去参加陈家的婚宴？”
昨日抢亲之事肯定已传开，从文渊的表情判断，应该为此事而来。他既然敢抢亲，也不怕向文渊承认自己的感情。
“你既已知，不必我多说。”
“什么时候的事？”
“要说很久很久以前不大合适，应该是很久很久以后，我喜欢上她，喜欢上那个有趣的灵魂。”
乔文仙听不明白：“仪王难道想说你在另一个世界就已喜欢上淑真？”
“可以这么理解。”
乔文仙认为仪王不过是想找个冠冕堂皇的理由，就像曾在官家面前想要讽刺官家是昏君时编的理由一样。
乔文仙忍不住不难过，听得出仪王是在他与淑真分手之前就喜欢上她。
“你帮我和淑真时可否真心？”
“我还不知她是幽栖居士时，自然乐意帮忙。后来……，我就不怎么乐意。”
乔文仙很迷惑，仪王口中的她与淑真有何区别？他无心思虑太久，顺着仪王的话，担忧地问：“什么时候仪王开始不乐意？”
“你与幽栖居士第一次在西湖幽会时，我便知她正是我喜欢的人。”
乔文仙根本不愿接受仪王的理由：“仪王真会编故事，为自己偷偷喜欢上淑真找出这么一个无法令人信服的理由，还不如直接说你是在我与淑真分手前已偷偷喜欢上她，我还会觉得仪王是个爽快之人。”
“是，我是在你未与她分手之前就喜欢上她。”
得到仪王肯定的回答，乔文仙更加难过：“那你为何还要帮忙劝说我爹答应到朱家提亲？”
申简辰叹口气：“唉，我虽喜欢她，可她那时满心满眼都是你，我希望她幸福，心里再不愿意，还是会劝说外公。”
“老实告诉我，是不是第二次劝说我爹时，你根本没尽全力？”乔文仙的眼中充满担忧，他怕仪王的答复是肯定的。
只听仪王回道：“不完全是……”
乔文仙闭上眼睛体会内心之痛，他失去爱情，难道还会失去朋友？他睁开双眼，盯着仪王：“那就是……是了？”
“你也知道，外公以死相逼，我毫无办法。加之我心中有她，自然庆幸老天替我找到一个不帮你的理由……”
乔文仙已听不下去，站起来，走到仪王跟前，抓住他的衣领：“我与家人抗争的那段时日，你是不是故意不帮我和淑真传送消息？”
At first light the next morning, Qiao Wenxian hurried to Youlin Retreat—his very first visit since his wedding—and strode straight into Prince Yi's courtyard. Inside, maids were clearing breakfast dishes while Prince Yi sat sipping morning tea at ease.

申简辰不由自主站起来，盯着文渊泛红的眼神。
Shen Jianchen greeted him warmly. "Thought wife and concubine had wiped me clean out of your mind and you would never darken my door again!"

文渊的劲儿不算大，申简辰毫无感觉，让文渊发泄发泄也是好的：“是，你与她已没有希望，我希望我是那个能与她在一起的人。”
Giving a stiff, formal bow without a word, Qiao Wenxian took a side seat with an expressionless face.

“你一直都有婚约，比我好不到哪里去！有什么资格跟她在一起？”乔文仙将仪王胸前的衣裳抓得更紧。
"Pour tea, Bai Feng," Shen Jianchen instructed. Serving a bowl to Young Master Qiao, Bai Feng caught Nan Shuang's discreet signal and stepped outside with him to catch up after days apart.

“有没有资格，对你我来讲已不重要，她已嫁给陈浩宇，你我只能接受命运安排。”申简辰既是安慰文渊，也是安慰自己。
"Why the silent treatment?" Shen Jianchen asked.

“命运安排”几个字让乔文仙变得清醒，淑真已不属于他，他连一点儿希望都没有，又何必如此可笑地埋怨仪王？
Fixing him with a steady stare, Qiao Wenxian countered, "Why did Your Highness skip the Chen wedding feast yesterday?"

乔文仙松开仪王胸前的衣裳，抱着仪王哭起来：“呜呜……！你若是把她抢走，也比她跟着陈浩宇强！呜呜……！”
Word of the raid was clearly all over town—and by Wenyuan's face, that was why he came. Bold enough to snatch a bride, Shen Jianchen saw no reason to dodge now. "Since you already know, why make me repeat it?"

申简辰用双手轻拍文渊的脊背：“男儿有泪不轻弹，别动不动就哭，让人看见笑话。情势逼得她与你分手，而后又无法选择我，都是命运捉弄。我该努力的已努力，差点将梦容的命给搭进去，也于事无补。不早不晚，官家诏书正好昨日送达，让我半月内回汴梁完婚，一切仿佛有人操纵一般。尼姑庵的法师对我讲‘随缘而不攀缘’，以前我过于攀缘，不如试试随缘，也许有一日可美梦成真，毕竟法师送我一句话：守得云开见月明。”
"When did it start?"

仪王一长串话，乔文仙没听进几句，倒是止住他的哭声，其中有一句让他琢磨不已：“你刚说淑真无法选择你，是什么意思？”
"Saying a long, long time ago is not quite right—more like a long, long time in the future. I fell for her—for that bright soul inside."

申简辰猜到文渊不过是担心淑真已完全放下他们之间的感情，倒不如让他清醒些，安心接受现实。
Baffled, Qiao Wenxian frowned. "Are you telling me you loved Shuzhen in another world?"

“她与梦容是闺中密友，不愿伤害梦容，才多次拒绝我的感情，也才心甘情愿嫁到陈家……”
"You could put it that way."

乔文仙放开仪王，泪眼中充满痛苦：“你的意思是她已爱上你？”
Dismissing it as another slick fairy tale—just like the yarn Prince Yi once spun mocking His Majesty—Qiao Wenxian felt bitter grief rise: plain as day, Prince Yi had fallen for her BEFORE they even broke up! "When you offered to help Shuzhen and me, were you ever sincere?"

千里寻只说过刚穿越而来时心里有过他，至于现在，申简辰不是很确定，但他希望如此，更希望文渊不要再念着他爱的人。
"Before I realized she was Youqi Jushi, yes. Afterward... not so much."

“是的，她已爱上我。”
Confused by his distinction between "her" and Shuzhen, Qiao Wenxian pressed past it. "When did you stop being willing?"

乔文仙一把推开仪王：“你胡说！”
"The very first time you and Youqi Jushi met secretly by West Lake, I realized she was the woman I loved."

然后痛苦地往出走，口中不断重复着“你胡说”三个字。他没有心情寻找南双，失魂落魄地坐上马车离开幽林别居。
Refusing to buy the tall tale, Qiao Wenxian scoffed painfully. "Always spinning fancy stories to dress up stealing a friend's girl! Better just admit straight out you fancied her before we broke up—at least I would respect your candor!"

申简辰没有追出去，文渊迟早要面对幽栖居士爱上别人的事实，不如现在让他彻底清醒过来。
"Fine. Yes—I loved her before you two broke up."

白风与南双离开后，爬上木质高台，边聊天儿，边欣赏远处的风景。
Hearing the flat admission twisted the blade deeper. "Then why did you still try talking my father into proposing to the Zhus?!"

“你跟苦荞好着没？”
Shen Jianchen let out a low sigh. "Even though I loved her, her whole heart and eyes belonged to you back then. I wanted her happy—gritted my teeth and leaned on Grandfather anyway."

“那还用问？”白风对自己的感情十分自信，转而问，“你呢？跟那个叫幼凡的怎么样？”
"Tell me the truth," Qiao Wenxian pressed, dread burning in his eyes. "The second time you spoke to my father—did you hold back?"

“幼凡倒挺乖，也喜欢跟我聊天儿，可我……，算了，不说了，都是过去的事。”
"Not entirely..."

白风猜到南双的意思：“我警告你，夏果已是乔公子身边的人，你可不能再想着人家。”
Squeezing his eyes shut against the stab—losing his love AND his friend both?!—he snapped them open. "So... you DID hold back?!"

“我知道，你放心，我再笨也不会抢公子的人。”南双从高处瞧见自家公子已走到大门口，“我家公子怎么不叫我就离开？改日跟你聊，我要去追我家公子。”
"You saw Grandfather threatening to kill himself—what could I do? And yes, loving her as I did, part of me thanked Heaven for handing me an excuse not to help you..."

南双快步走下木质高台，向公子追去。追到门口，公子的马车已离开，他在后面喊了好几声，公子都没有回应。还好幽林别居的车夫与他相熟，把马车赶过来，送他追赶自家公子。
Snapping at last, Qiao Wenxian leaped up, strode straight across, and seized Prince Yi by the collar! "While I was starving myself fighting my family—did you deliberately refuse to carry messages between Shuzhen and me?!"

白风则走下木质高台，去找王爷。刚来到正院外，王爷正好走出来。
Rising calmly to meet his bloodshot gaze—feeling barely a tug from the scholar's grip, letting him vent—Shen Jianchen owned it flat. "Yes. Your road with her was dead, and I hoped I would be the man who ended up with her."

申简辰并未理会白风，拐向佳和郡主的闺房小院。白风很自然地跟上。
"You have an imperial betrothal—you are no better off than me! What right did you have to be with her?!" Qiao Wenxian twisted the silk tighter.

诏书就像一剂良药，乔梦容只觉伤口比昨日好许多，她一早起来精神焕发，早饭也吃下不少。
"Right or no right means nothing to either of us now," Shen Jianchen replied quietly. "She married Chen Haoyu. All either of us can do is bow to fate." Comforting Wenyuan and himself in the same breath.

本想一早去见哥哥，听喜兰说乔公子来访，便安心坐在书桌前随手拿本诗集看，诗集里收录了不少本朝人的诗作。
"Bow to fate" hit like cold water. Shuzhen was gone—zero hope left—so what joke was he playing brawling with Prince Yi? Loosening his fists from the collar, Qiao Wenxian collapsed against Prince Yi's chest and wept aloud: "Wuuu! Even YOU carrying her off would have been better than handing her to Chen Haoyu! Wuuu!"

她随意翻看，以此消磨时光，安心等待乔公子离开。翻着翻着，看到一首绝句《对景漫成》，被题目吸引，再看诗句，充满情趣之美，不由念出声来：“半窗残照一帘风，小小池亭竹径通。枫叶醉红秋色里，两三行雁夕阳中。”
Patting his shaking back gently, Shen Jianchen murmured, "Real men do not shed tears easy—stop bawling before someone laughs. Circumstances forced her to leave you, then barred her from choosing me—fate toys with us all. I threw everything I had at it, nearly cost Mengrong her life, and still came up empty. Right on cue yesterday arrived His Majesty's decree ordering me back to Bianliang within half a month to wed—like invisible strings pulling every move. The nun up the hill told me: *Follow fate, never force it.* I forced things too hard before; maybe letting fate steer will turn a dream true someday—she also told me *Wait through the clouds, and you will see the bright moon.*"

念完后，乔梦容仔细品着：“真是一幅残阳夕照，秋高气爽的美景图，‘帘’字用得甚好，一下子将秋风与闺阁之情联系起来；‘通’字也用得好，将池塘、亭阁台榭、竹中的小径串在一起，还能写出竹中小径笔直幽深的特点；‘醉’字极其生动，将枫叶写活，仿佛是位娇羞的闺阁女子沉醉在爱情中红了脸；‘两三行’用得最妙，轻快松弛中渲染出高空的景象。”
Half the speech drifted past Qiao Wenxian, yet it dried his tears—and one phrase snagged sharp in his ears: "Wait—what did you mean saying Shuzhen was 'barred from choosing you'?!"

喜兰赞道：“只听诗句，觉不出有多美，被郡主这么一品，倒让奴婢感觉出诗的好来。”
Guessing Wenyuan dreaded hearing Shuzhen had truly moved on, Shen Jianchen figured a clean cut would finally set him free. "Because she and Mengrong are closest friends. Refusing to hurt Mengrong, she turned me down over and over and willingly married into the Chen family..."

乔梦容谦虚道：“诗不好，我也品不出好来，也不知是谁写的？”乔梦容向诗句后面瞧去，“幽栖居士”四个小字映入眼帘。
Pulling back with pain flashing through wet eyes, Qiao Wenxian whispered, "You mean... she fell in love with you?"

她顿时失去兴趣，将书扔到一边：“我也有眼拙的时候，刚才一番赞叹，她的诗根本配不上！”
Qianli Xun had only admitted feelings right after crossing over; how deep they ran today, Shen Jianchen could not be 100% certain—yet he hoped so, and wanted Wenyuan to stop chasing ghosts. "Yes. She fell in love with me."

因为生气，伤口不由疼起来，她将手放在左腹处，眉头紧皱：“喜兰，好好在里面翻翻，凡是有她的诗，全部撕掉。”
Shoving Prince Yi back hard, Qiao Wenxian choked out, "Liar!" Spinning away, he stumbled blindly out the doorway muttering "Liar! Liar!" under his breath—forgetting even Nan Shuang as he boarded his carriage in a daze and rattled out of Youlin Retreat. Shen Jianchen did not chase him; sooner or later Wenyuan had to face reality and move forward.

又一想，撕掉会留下痕迹，瞧着也会想起：“不，把书拿出去烧掉！”
Up on the wooden watch-platform outside, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang had been trading updates on Kuqiao and Youfan ("Quit eyeing Xiaguo—she is your master's woman now!" / "Don't worry, I am not that stupid!") when Nan Shuang spotted his master bolting out the gate solo. Sprinting down the stairs too late to catch the departing carriage, he hitched a ride from a friendly Youlin Retreat driver to chase him down.

喜兰已猜到刚才的诗是谁人所写，见郡主难受，想上前安慰，又被吩咐烧书，只能顾一头，还是先把书给烧掉，省得郡主看到难受。
Coming down to find his master, Bai Feng saw Prince Yi stepping out toward Princess Jiahe's courtyard and fell into step behind him.

她拿起书，到院子里打开火折子，将书点着，目睹整本书在火中化为灰烬。
Inside her quarters, the imperial decree had acted like miracle medicine on Qiao Mengrong: her wound felt worlds lighter, breakfast went down clean, and while waiting for Qiao Wenxian to leave before seeing Brother, she browsed a volume of contemporary Song verse at her desk.

当喜兰再次进入屋内时，郡主的脸色好了很多。她回道：“郡主，书已烧毁。”
Flipping pages, her eye caught a quatrain titled *"Impromptu Upon a View"* (*Duijing Mancheng*). Drawn by its charm, she read it aloud:

乔梦容挪开捂住伤口的手：“我以前收集过不少关于她的诗词，你今日在我房中认真搜寻一番，全部找出来，统统烧掉，我不想在自己的屋里看到任何与她有关的字迹。”
"Half a window of dying light, a single curtain of wind; A modest pond pavilion where a bamboo path leads through. Maple leaves flush drunk with red amid the autumn hues; Two or three lines of wild geese cross the evening sun." [TN: Translator's rendering]

“是，郡主。”喜兰忙着去搜寻。
Savoring every character, Qiao Mengrong praised it brightly: "What a crisp autumn sunset picture! 'Curtain' links the autumn breeze right to a lady's boudoir longing; 'leads through' threads pond, pavilion, and bamboo grove together while sketching a straight, deep trail; 'drunk' brings the maple leaves alive like a shy maiden blushing in love; and 'two or three lines' sweeps wide, airy height across the sky!"

喜兰正忙时，申简辰走进来。
"Hearing the lines alone I missed half of it," Xilan marveled, "yet once Your Highness unpacks them, it glows!"

﻿见王爷到来，喜兰停下手头之事，上前拜见：“王爷。”她不好再去忙，怕王爷问起原因，便站立一旁伺候。
"Only fine poetry yields fine reading—wonder who wrote it?" Glancing at the signature line, four tiny characters jumped out: *Youqi Jushi*.

哥哥到来，乔梦容开心许多，她身上有伤，并未起身，主要是担心哥哥看到她站起来时艰难的样子，会推迟回汴梁的时日。
Every spark of delight died on the spot as she flung the book aside! "Even my eyes go blind sometimes—wasting praise on verse she does not deserve!" Rage pulled sharp pain across her bandaged side; clutching her left flank with knotted brows, she barked, "Xilan, flip through that book and tear out every single poem by her—no! Take the whole book outside and burn it!"

“我正要去看望哥哥，没想到哥哥就来。”
Guessing the author at once, Xilan scooped the volume out into the yard, struck a fire-starter, and watched it curl to black ash before reporting back to find her mistress's breathing calmer.

申简辰关切地问：“今日感觉如何？”
Dropping her hand from her side, Qiao Mengrong ordered coldly, "I collected stacks of her poems and lyrics before. Search my entire room today, pull out every single one, and burn the lot! I refuse to look at a single brushstroke tied to her under my roof!"

乔梦容掩饰道：“好多了，只盼早些跟哥哥回到汴梁。”
"Yes, Your Highness."

申简辰已放平心态，决定随了这段缘分：“等你伤好，就回汴梁完婚。”
Just as Xilan began rummaging shelves, Shen Jianchen walked in. Stopping her search to curtsy politely, Xilan stepped back in silence lest His Highness ask what she was clearing out.

乔梦容面露喜色：“我的伤无碍，随时可以出发。”
Brightening at her brother's arrival—staying seated lest rising awkwardly tempt him to delay their trip to Bianliang—Qiao Mengrong smiled. "I was just about to visit Brother, and here you are!"

申简辰犹豫片刻：“官家在诏书中要求半月内回汴梁，时间来得及，你再养两日，两日后出发。”
"How does the wound feel today?"

得到哥哥确定的答复，乔梦容更加开心：“哥哥放心，两日后我的伤会彻底痊愈。”
"Much better! Cannot wait to ride back to Bianliang with Brother."

申简辰勉强挤出微笑，向喜兰吩咐：“好生照顾郡主。”
Heart settled on following fate, Shen Jianchen nodded. "Once you mend, we head back to Bianliang for the wedding."

正要离开时，被梦容叫住：“哥哥要去哪里？”
"It barely hurts—we can leave anytime!"

申简辰没有回头：“临安城内有位叫蒋爷的人，听说是黑道，他得罪过我，打算前去会会。”
After a brief pause, he said, "His Majesty gave us half a month; plenty of time. Rest two more days—we depart the day after tomorrow."

只要哥哥不是去见朱淑真，乔梦容便放心：“哥哥小心，早去早回。”
"Two days from now I will be good as new!" Mengrong beamed.

哥哥带着白风离开后，乔梦容对喜兰说：“你继续忙吧，统统找出来，哪怕只有‘幽栖居士’四个字，也要烧毁，我不想再让她出现在我的生活中。”
Forcing a thin smile, Shen Jianchen told Xilan to tend her well and turned to go—only for Mengrong to call out, "Where is Brother heading?"

“是。”喜兰答应完，继续忙活。
Without turning back, he answered, "There is an underworld boss in Lin'an called Master Jiang who crossed me. Going to pay him a visit."

乔梦容又暗自伤神，回忆曾与淑真躺在一张床上互诉衷肠；回忆与她围坐在火炉旁说些闺中私密之事。
Long as he wasn't sneaking off to Zhu Shuzhen, she felt at peace. "Be careful and come back early, Brother."

以后恐怕再也找不到一位能与她互诉衷肠之人。
Once he left with Bai Feng, Mengrong turned ice-cold to Xilan. "Keep searching—even if a sheet only bears the four characters 'Youqi Jushi,' burn it! Wipe her clean out of my life!"

她恨朱淑真，不仅仅因为她抢走哥哥的心，还因为她亲手葬送了她重视的友情。
"Yes, Your Highness."

喜兰已收拾出一堆书籍，从外面叫来几个下人将书搬出屋外，一把火点着，火势比烧一本书时大了许多。
