## Chapter 344: Grieving in a Crumbling Era

Flattening every blocking gate guard, Qianli Xun blasted Wanyan Chi's twisted "love" speech—stopping dead in her tracks only when he called after her in sudden decency: *"Prince Yi brought ten million taels for you! I lied you were dead and robbed him—go find him!"*

Tears spilling down her cheeks, she walked straight out into the winter streets without looking back: better let Shen Jianchen believe her dead and move on clean, while Wanyan Chi quietly sent Lu Yuanfeng to trail and protect her from afar.

Back in Lin'an, the fake death news broke Old Madam Xia's frail heart, and the Old Ancestor at Zhu Manor peacefully passed two days later—leaving Haoyu shattered inside Suxin Thatched Hall, refusing to let graying Matron Wei or weeping Kuqiao move back to Zhu Manor while young Chen Haofei resigned his corrupt capital post to learn Chen trade.

Meanwhile, history roared south: Emperor Zhao Ji panicked and dumped the throne onto weeping **Zhao Huan** (opening the **first year of Jingkang**), while hero **Li Gang** rallied Bianliang's defenders against Wanyan Zonghan until appeasers bought a fragile truce by ceding Zhongshan, Hejian, and Taiyuan!

Spring thawed the frost, yet Haoyu still sat drinking in Moxiang Courtyard reciting Zhu Shuzhen's ***Busuanzi: Ode to the Plum Blossom***:

*"One bough leans sideways deep in the bamboo grove, / lending the quiet woods a stillness deeper still. / After rain, pure beauty no brush could ever paint— / sparse shadows stretched across the shallow water. / A flute plays the Little Chanyu tune clean through, / stirring old sorrows back to mind once more. / Faint fragrance drifts across the gentle breeze, / while chill moonlight wraps the blossoms cold."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Unable to stomach seeing his master waste away indoors, Qinggua talked him into visiting Qiaotou Wa (where the storyteller was sick)—then pulled out his ace: **Xiangrong** at Huayun Pavilion, who sang all of Second Young Madam's lyrics set to Dongfang Chenze's melodies!
