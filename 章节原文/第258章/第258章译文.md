## Chapter 258: Persuading Prince Yi at the Judicial Office

Concubine Xia's syrupy purr made even Nan Shuang go weak at the knees, let alone his master. Qiao Wenxian had a natural soft spot for that coaxing tone—every time she spoke like that, memories of Shuzhen curling in his lap surfaced, whispering that she loved him, wanted to marry him, and wanted to bear him heaps of children.

A warm smile broke across his face, and his voice gentled. "All right." Giving Xiaguo a brief embrace, he strode out with Nan Shuang.

"Young Master doesn't stand a chance against your voice, Concubine Xia," Aimo giggled.

Watching Wenyuan vanish down the path, Xiaguo turned to her maid. "Take notes. Every man melts for a woman who knows how to coax and pout. Precious few women can hook a man without it like Miss used to. You and I both saw how madly in love Wenyuan was with her—and look at him now! One sweet purr from me and he's putty in my hands. That old flame is ancient history."

Aimo glanced toward the east wing. "That one never coaxes or pouts—so why does Young Master still spend plenty of nights in her room?"

Xiaguo's smile soured. "Word is she's skilled in *that* department."

"Which department?" Aimo blinked.

Xiaguo leaned close to her ear. "Bedroom skills."

Aimo's cheeks flamed scarlet, cutting off every word.

"Ask around on the quiet," Xiaguo ordered. "Find out where to hire a matron who knows all those tricks and bring her here to teach me."

Aimo couldn't fathom why Concubine Xia needed tutoring when Young Master already bedded her half the week, yet she dared not question her and replied, "Yes, Concubine Xia."

Back at Youlin Retreat, Matron Zou reported Master Qiao's lukewarm brush-off.

A wave of bitterness hit Qiao Mengrong—all because she lacked a towering patron at court, even the Qiaos dared shrug her off! She thought of Imperial Attendant Liang; sure, Liang Shicheng listened to her now and then, yet calling him a rock-solid backer was stretching the truth.

"Even though the Qiaos won't lift a finger," Matron Zou added, "Master Qiao did toss out a promising lead."

"What lead?"

"The Qiantang Gang."

"Who are they?"

"An underworld syndicate, Master Qiao says—they take silver to kill."

The gloom cleared from Qiao Mengrong's face. "Good. While you were out, I thought it over. First, I want to ruin her life—stir up trouble, torment her, and make every day miserable. Step one is stripping her of that 'Poetess of Jiangnan' title, which is already underway. Next, we need someone inside Chen Manor making her life hell. Once I've watched her suffer to my heart's content, making her vanish for good won't be too late."

"Well reasoned, Your Highness. Still, how do we stir up trouble inside Chen Manor? Someone of your exalted station can't very well march over there to mess with that vixen."

"Forgot already, Matron? You're the one who suggested borrowing someone else's blade."

Realization lit Matron Zou's eyes. "Whom does Your Highness have in mind?"

"Chen Haodong."

"A Chen?"

Qiao Mengrong nodded. "Eldest Young Master Chen. Madam Miao's mother, Madam Qin, invited Second Madam Bao to play madiao several times and pried out plenty of gossip. Put the pieces together, and it's clear Chen Haodong has his eye on inheriting the Chen empire—and Chen Haoyu's sudden reformation and foray into family business has him sweating bullets."

"If that tip holds water, Chen Haodong could be very handy indeed. Your Highness really does need a capable man outside running errands for you."

"Miao Zhiyu is eager to serve me—she wouldn't feed me lies."

"Then I'll have someone look into Eldest Young Master Chen and arrange a quiet chat with him."

"I'll leave it in your hands, Matron."

Over at the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen sat alone in the Investigator's tiny rear courtyard, sipping fermented rice wine from a square wooden table as dark thoughts drifted through his head. The small yard had been swept clean, yet the scenery left plenty to be desired—the flowerbeds had gone wild with weeds, lending the humble yamen a scrappy sort of life.

Chief Constable Wen had ridden into the hills to arrest Sun Er; with any luck he'd be back tomorrow. Once this murder case wrapped up, Shen Jianchen figured he ought to return to Youlin Retreat and visit Mengrong. Since he had come back to life inside Zhao Pu's body, looking after Mengrong was only right—still, consummating the marriage...

Just as he brooded aimlessly, Qiao Wenxian strolled through the gateway looking radiant, not a trace left of the hollowed-out wretch who had starved himself last year. Shen Jianchen marveled inwardly: how could a man who swore undying love transform so completely? People's hearts really did shift—though when his own might shift, he hadn't the foggiest clue.

"Drinking all alone like a brooding hermit? Where's the fun in that! Bai Feng, fetch another cup!" Qiao Wenxian dropped into a chair without waiting for an invite. "Leave it to you—a full-blooded imperial prince playing clerk at the Judicial Office!"

Bai Feng brought a second cup, jerked his chin toward the doorway at Nan Shuang, and the two young attendants slipped out to wander the grounds.

Shen Jianchen set his cup down. "Which eye saw me brooding? Just passing a slow afternoon with a drink. What about you—shouldn't you be home keeping your wife and concubine happy instead of dropping in on me?"

Qiao Wenxian poured himself a cup and topped off the prince's. "Matron Zou came by Qiao Manor today and brought up you and Princess Jiahe. My father sent me over to talk sense into you, so I figured I'd inspect your new digs. This Judicial Office is a dump compared to Youlin Retreat—move back home already."

"So they sent you as a lobbyist?"

"What else?"

"You already gave me the speech once. Why waste your breath again?"

"Last time I only skimmed the surface. Today I want to dig deeper."

Shen Jianchen lifted his bowl and took a sip. "Another lecture on how you let go and embraced your wife and concubine? Save it—you and I aren't built the same."

Having his prepared script guessed on line one threw Qiao Wenxian off stride, so he flipped his opening straight for shock value. "Wrong! I never truly let Shuzhen go."

Bullseye. Shen Jianchen set his cup down and went still.

"I'll never forget her," Qiao Wenxian confessed quietly. "All I can do is bury her deep inside my heart and swallow my pride to accept women I don't love. That's the only way the people around me smile and the house stays warm. Otherwise, I've already lost Shuzhen—and making two other women and my parents miserable on top of it only piles sin on sin."

"Are you happy?"

"How could anyone be truly happy apart from the woman he loves? Still, I've learned that every woman has her own kind of charm. Since I can't have the joy of being with the one I love, I might as well accept what other women offer. Xiaguo loves to pout and coax—one sweet whine from her and I melt, plus she even looks a bit like Shuzhen. Miao Zhiyu isn't the pouting sort, yet once we're in bed she knows far more tricks than I do, and—much to my surprise—I actually enjoy myself..."

Shen Jianchen cut in with a grimace. "Why are you telling me about your bedroom affairs?"

"Don't you get my point?"

"What point?"

"Men and women are simple at the end of the day. Once you consummate your marriage with Princess Jiahe, you'll discover how sweet she is—and just like me, you won't want to stop! Once leads to twice, twice leads to a hundred times; you're content, and she's overjoyed. Since you can't have the woman in your heart, lock her away deep inside like I did and embrace the wife right beside you. That's the only way everyone comes out in one piece."

It wasn't that Shen Jianchen lacked normal desires or had never thought of taking Mengrong to bed—he simply couldn't step over the moral line inside his own head. Bedding a woman he didn't love felt wrong to the bone, especially when he had only ever viewed Mengrong as a little sister.

"She calls me *Brother*," he said grimly. "In my head she's my little sister. How could I lay hands on my own sister? I'd feel lower than a beast."

"Back when you were sick, you loved her—and now you've married her! She stopped being your sister ages ago; she's your wedded wife! Touching your own wife is Heaven's law, not some taboo. On the contrary, *refusing* to touch her leaves her frantic and insecure—and before you know it, that bitterness might spill straight onto Shuzhen!"

The memory flashed red before Shen Jianchen's eyes: Mengrong slapping Qianli Xun across the face on her wedding day and shrieking, *"Zhu Shuzhen! I hate you!"* Followed by her icy threat the day they returned to Lin'an: *"You still can't let her go—and the harder you hold onto her, the more I hate her!"*

Only now did it sink in how his stubborn devotion to Qianli Xun was twisting the knife inside Mengrong—and putting Qianli Xun in the crosshairs.

Throwing back his entire cup in one gulp, Shen Jianchen set the vessel down with a heavy *thud*. Every word scraped out of his throat like gravel. "Wenyuan... I'll listen to you. Once I close this murder case I just picked up, I'll go back to Youlin Retreat and... consummate my marriage with Mengrong."

Getting the prince to cave brought Qiao Wenxian no triumph at all. First he had bowed his neck to reality, and now he had talked his best friend into bowing too. Against the weight of the real world, even an imperial prince had to bend the knee.

Refilling both cups, Qiao Wenxian raised his. "That's the right move... She's married now, and word is Chen Haoyu really has straightened up. Let's both lock her in our hearts and wish her well in silence—so long as she's happy, we'll be fine. Come on, bottoms up—to the life we're forced to bow to!"

Shen Jianchen clinked his cup against his friend's, and both drank deep.

Setting down his empty cup, Qiao Wenxian sighed. "Tell me—is there anyone on earth who doesn't have to bow to life and gets to live completely free? What about His Majesty? Is he the one man alive who never has to bend?"

Still reeling from his own pledge to consummate his marriage—how hollow and laughable his bold oath to stay chaste for Qianli Xun felt now!—Shen Jianchen heard the mention of His Majesty. Wasn't His Majesty the very hammer of reality that had forced this wedding decree on him and driven him away from the woman he loved? As for that incompetent sovereign, sooner or later history would force *him* onto his knees too—and part of Shen Jianchen almost wished that day would hurry up and arrive.

"Every human being faces a reality he has to bow to—His Majesty included," Shen Jianchen said darkly. "A rotting court and the turning tide of an era will grind him lower than dust."

Unable to foresee the storm history held in store, Qiao Wenxian chalked it up to his friend blowing off steam. Ever since coming back from the dead, the prince loved trashing his own father's court—good thing he had the sense to watch who was in the room, or his head would have rolled ten times over.

Meanwhile, after touring the Judicial Office grounds together, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang returned to perch on a pair of stone stools outside the Investigator's courtyard gate.

"This place is pretty bare-bones," Nan Shuang observed. "Can Prince Yi really stomach living here?"

"My master isn't picky—eats whatever's served, sleeps wherever there's a bed. Doesn't bother him a bit."

"Whoa—a noble prince as easygoing as me?"

"Quit comparing yourself to His Highness—you're not even in the same league."

Unfazed, Nan Shuang flashed a cheeky grin. "Hey, tell me something—have you and Kuqiao done *that* yet?"

"Done *what*?" Bai Feng blinked.

Nan Shuang leaned over and whispered straight into his ear, then leaned back grinning ear to ear.

Bai Feng scowled. "You two aren't even married yet! Bit early for that, isn't it?"

"Young Master says I can marry Youfan next year."

"Didn't you tell me he was making you wait two years?"

"Well, somebody caught me and Youfan sneaking around in the maids' quarters and snitched to Young Master—so today he caved and promised next year!"

Bai Feng jabbed a finger at him. "You're a year younger than me and already this impatient! And what's Youfan thinking—isn't she worried about getting taken advantage of?"

"Hey, I wasn't the impatient one—*she* was!"

Bai Feng's eyes went wide. "What?! Call yourself a man, pinning the blame on a girl?"

Nan Shuang leaned back in, dropping his voice to a conspiratorial whisper. "Before my Young Madam married in, the Miao family hired some veteran matron who taught her every trick in the book. Youfan was right there waiting on her and picked up a fair bit. Ever since Young Madam used those moves to wrap Young Master around her finger, Youfan has to attend outside the bed curtains every night—gets all worked up listening and drags me off to practice! I'm red-blooded flesh and bone—how am I supposed to say no to that?"

"Red-blooded my foot!" Bai Feng scoffed. "You're just a little lecher with zero willpower!"

Nan Shuang kept grinning. "Oh yeah? Swear you could resist?"

"Of... of course I could!"

"What if Kuqiao came tempting you?"

"Kuqiao isn't some loose girl, and I'm not careless like you!"

"Sure, sure, you're a saint—and you're missing out on heaven! Next time you see Kuqiao, drag her somewhere quiet and try it once. Guarantee you'll never forget it!"

Bai Feng shoved him sideways. "Shut your dirty mouth! Think I'm like you? Don't go crossing any lines out there, kid—I work for the Judicial Office now, so watch yourself or I'll lock you up!"

"Relax, I'm loyal to a fault—Youfan's the only one for me."

"With that sleazy look in your eye today, trouble's bound to find you sooner or later."

"What trouble? I'm practically married to her already!"

Listening to him, Bai Feng decided he was probably just worrying too much. Still, much as he scolded Nan Shuang, the boy's gossip left an undeniable itch in his chest, making him eager to marry Kuqiao soon. Remembering her asking him to wait dimmed his mood again. What rough patch could Youqi Jushi possibly be facing that made Kuqiao hold off on his proposal? He couldn't figure it out for the life of him.
