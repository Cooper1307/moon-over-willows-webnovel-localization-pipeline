## Chapter 203: Two Requests for a Poetry Gathering

Property transfer wrapped up, Matron Wei returned to the newlyweds' courtyard to find her mistress standing outside looking glum.

"Transfer is done, Miss—smooth sailing, just waiting on Steward Fang to register it at the yamen."

Weighed down by unfamiliar Chen walls, Old Madam Xia's morning ambush, and yesterday's heartbreak, Qianli Xun brightened a shade at the smooth news—at least Chen Haoyu's parents treated her well. "Thank you, Matron."

"Did morning tea go smoothly?" Matron Wei asked gently.

Kuqiao blurted straight out, "Father-in-Law and Mother-in-Law were lovely, Matron, yet Chen Old Madam ordered Miss to kneel and smashed her tea bowl on the floor! Outrageous!"

Far from bristling, Matron Wei let out a warm chuckle. "Thought it was real trouble! Just Old Madam Xia? Trivial—coaxing an elderly lady is child's play for Miss!"

That single word—"coaxing"—clicked a light straight on in Qianli Xun's head, chasing the clouds off her brow. "Trust Matron to nail it in one word! Elders are just big kids craving sweet talk—whoever coaxes them wins them over! Now I get why Old Madam Xia adores Chen Haoyu: the guy pours out syrupy butter nonstop!"

Pleased to see her cheer return, Matron Wei nodded warmly. "Even if Miss did not marry for love, putting thought into winning over Chen elders shows you accept your new home and mean to live well here. Wherever your heart settles, no wall holds."

Touched yet skipping sappy thanks, Qianli Xun pivoted to decorating. "This courtyard is smaller than Wangyue Pavilion yet bigger than my old Zhu Manor garden. Walking around, I saw plenty of flowers yet none I like! Besides redoing the hall, I want my favorite blooms planted—and I named the whole courtyard and every single room! Written on paper for Kuqiao to hand you."

"Just heading to Wangyue Pavilion to haul your furniture over," Matron Wei agreed effortlessly. "Once the hall is set, I will call Chen Manor's gardeners to redo the yard just how you like."

Hugging her arm, Qianli Xun smiled. "Matron never turns down a single wish of mine!"

"Reasonable wishes—and living in a house that feels wrong never brings peace." Accepting a thick stack of room names from Kuqiao ("Even the servants' quarters got fancy names!" Kuqiao grinned), Matron Wei skimmed them. "I will swing by a plaque carver on the way to Wangyue Pavilion—hung by tomorrow!" Rounding up Hongdou and the nine trained hands, she set off to haul furniture.

Meanwhile, bolting from Youlin Retreat earlier that morning, Qiao Wenxian had refused to go back to Qiao Manor—driven by one burning need: see Shuzhen face-to-face and ask straight out! Once Nan Shuang caught up to his carriage, they stopped briefly outside Chen Manor's closed gate before turning straight for Dongfang Manor.

Inside his study, Dongfang Chenze sat at his zither setting one of Youqi Jushi's ci lyrics to music for Xiangrong to sing at his next gathering, strumming and singing aloud: *"Slanting winds and fine rain bring a spring chill. / Before the wine cup, / I recall old joys. / Once beside the pear blossoms, / Lonely tears streamed down..."*

Stepping inside, Qiao Wenxian recognized Shuzhen's *"Jiangchengzi: Admiring the Spring"*—the exact lyric that had filled him with bright dreams back in Bianliang, now ashes in his mouth.

Alerted by Huayu, Dongfang Chenze lifted his hands from the strings and saw his friend's red-rimmed eyes. "Brother Qiao! Rare honor—sit while I whisk tea myself!"

Catching his arm, Qiao Wenxian shook his head. "No mood for tea. Sit and listen to business."

Seated together, Dongfang Chenze braced himself. "Speak."

"Host a poetry gathering tomorrow."

Much as Dongfang Chenze loved salons, tomorrow?! "Planned one soon, yet tomorrow is wildly rushed—push it back a few days?"

"No. Tomorrow. I cannot wait another day."

Reading the raw storm on his face, Dongfang Chenze asked softly, "Why the urgent rush?"

"I need to see Shuzhen."

Blunt honesty put Dongfang Chenze in a tight bind. "You and Youqi Jushi both have new lives now, Brother Qiao—better not stir old embers."

"Do not misunderstand—I only want ONE last private talk to ask a few questions, then I let go for good."

"Ask questions, and I fear your heart only burns hotter."

"My word on it—the very last time. After tomorrow, I never disturb her again."

Seeing genuine finality beneath the ache—maybe closure really would set both free—Dongfang Chenze relented. "First time helping you like this, and the LAST—never again!"

A tired smile touched Qiao Wenxian's face. "Thank you, Brother Dongfang." Taking his leave shortly after, he left Dongfang Chenze dropping his zither to seek counsel from An Mingxuan and Cao Ban on pulling off a discreet salon overnight.

Stepping out his front gate with Huayu, he froze at a carriage pulling up driven by Bai Feng—Prince Yi! Having struck out in Mihun Alley, Shen Jianchen had come straight here seeking the exact same pretext to see Qianli Xun one last time before leaving for Bianliang!

Bowing outside the cab, Dongfang Chenze asked, "Calling on my father for official business, Your Highness?"

"Not Master Dongfang—YOU, Young Master Dongfang. Step inside for a word."

Climbing in without surprise, Dongfang Chenze sat opposite the prince. "At Your Highness's service."

"Simple task," Shen Jianchen smiled smoothly. "Host a poetry gathering tomorrow—keep the guest list small, yet make certain Youqi Jushi attends."

Having heard all about yesterday's raid—and secretly understanding, having briefly admired Youqi Jushi himself before backing off—Dongfang Chenze blinked at getting the EXACT SAME demand twice in one hour!

Reading his hesitation, Shen Jianchen tossed a heavy silver pouch onto his lap. "Expenses are on me. Word is you want your poetry salon famous across Lin'an—help me tomorrow, and once I return from Bianliang, I will put Prince Yi's name behind you and make your gathering number one in the city!"

Silver, dream fulfilled, AND a royal patron—impossible to refuse! Pocketing the pouch gratefully, Dongfang Chenze hesitated before confessing honestly: "One tiny snag, Your Highness... right before you arrived, Brother Qiao came asking the exact same thing—swearing it is his final private meeting with Youqi Jushi. Should we push Your Highness's gathering back a day?"

Guessing Wenyuan wanted final closure straight from her lips so he could move on, Shen Jianchen stayed cool. "Funny how Wenyuan and I think alike. No need to delay—I trust Young Master Dongfang to choreograph it smoothly. Send location details to Bai Feng before dusk."

Left with zero escape, Dongfang Chenze bowed out, checked Cao Manor, tracked Cao Ban and An Mingxuan down outside a storytelling hall at Qiaotou Wa, and dragged both aboard a rented tea boat on the river to brainstorm.

"Both Brother Qiao AND Prince Yi hit me up today demanding a poetry salon tomorrow—just as cover to see Youqi Jushi!" Dongfang Chenze spilled while An Mingxuan poured tea.

"Wild!" Cao Ban gasped. "You guessed it, or they admitted it?"

"Admitted it straight to my face! Tell me how the hell I pull this off!"

An Mingxuan ticked off three hurdles cleanly: "Cannot turn down Prince Yi, so it happens tomorrow. One: pick a secluded spot nobody frequents. Two: bar Lady Miao and Princess Jiahe—and keep them clueless it exists. Three: craft a slick excuse to lure Youqi Jushi out on day two of her marriage without tipping off the Chens."

"Wait—after yesterday's raid, if Brother Qiao and Prince Yi both show up chasing Youqi Jushi, won't they brawl?!" Cao Ban worried.

"Already warned Prince Yi," Dongfang Chenze said. "He didn't blink—told me to stagger it."

"Then schedule Brother Qiao FIRST, Prince Yi SECOND!" Cao Ban proposed. "As for location—Yongtai Teahouse is out (Lady Miao haunts it), Goulan Wasi is too crowded. Must be outside city walls—what about Chen Haoyu's Zuiyun Tower?! Deserted and dead quiet!"

"What if Chen servants there rat out Youqi Jushi to Chen Haoyu?" Dongfang Chenze worried.

"Only two staffers there, busy with their own jobs—keep it discreet and nobody notices!"

Location locked, roles divided: "Some poetry gathering!" An Mingxuan sighed; "A poetry gathering in name only!" Cao Ban summed up as the three split off to put every cog in place.
