第118章 同榻而卧聊聊天
## Chapter 118: Pillow Talk Into the Night

佳和郡主热情邀请同榻而卧，千里寻不好拒绝也不愿拒绝。
With Princess Jiahe's warm invitation to share her bed, Qianli Xun neither wanted nor had the heart to refuse.

两日来发生太多事，她需要找个人聊聊发泄发泄，佳和郡主无疑是最佳人选。
Too much had piled up over the past two days; she needed a sympathetic ear to vent to, and Princess Jiahe was the ideal confidante.

两个女孩子睡前还没聊够，躺下后，继续开聊，她们如同两个长久失联的好友，一见面有聊不完的话题。当然，关键话题还是关乎个人感情之事。
Not finished talking before bed, the two girls kept right on chatting beneath the quilts like long-lost sisters catching up—with romance, of course, taking center stage.

“淑真……”乔梦容不再客气地称呼幽栖居士，而是直呼其名，“我听哥哥说，乔家取消提亲，你有没有想过以后跟乔公子该怎么办？”
"Shuzhen..." Dropping the formal "Youqi Jushi" for her given name, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Brother told me the Qiao family called off the proposal. Have you thought about what you and Young Master Qiao will do next?"

“文渊说他不会放弃，我也不会放弃。”
"Wenyuan said he won't give up, and neither will I."

“你们打算怎么做？”
"What's your plan?"

“不知道。”千里寻认为穿越后的时代除科技不够发达外，最大的问题就是恋爱婚姻不得自由，“如果没有父母之命，媒妁之言，我与文渊的难题会迎刃而解。”
"No idea." Aside from primitive technology, Qianli Xun decided the worst flaw of this era was zero freedom in love and marriage. "If there were no parents' orders and matchmakers' words, Wenyuan's and my troubles would vanish in a snap."

“很难想象父母不管儿女的婚事，也不需要媒人出面，会是什么样子？”
"Hard to picture a world where parents stay out of their children's marriages and nobody needs a matchmaker—what would that even look like?"

千里寻转向乔梦容：“年轻人自由恋爱，父母基本上不插手，除非到谈婚论嫁，父母才出面，会尊重年轻人的想法。还有很多人不愿意恋爱结婚，父母急得不行，恨不得在大街上随便拉个人把婚给结了。”
Qianli Xun rolled over to face Qiao Mengrong. "Young people fall in love freely while parents stay hands-off, only stepping in when it's time to talk wedding plans—and even then respecting what their kids want. Plenty of folks don't even want to date or marry, driving their parents so crazy they'd drag any stranger off the street to get them hitched!"

“这么有趣，说的你好像经历过一样。”
"Sounds so wild—you talk like you've lived through it!"

“我梦想着有这么一个时代，也相信未来会出现这样一个时代。”
"I dream of an age like that, and I believe one day it'll come."

千里寻幻想着带领文渊回到更先进的时代，享受恋爱的自由。她觉得人真是奇怪，拥有自由时，不认为有多重要，一旦失去，才知原来在先进时代经历的生活有多么来之不易。
Qianli Xun pictured bringing Wenyuan back to the modern world to taste the freedom to love. Funny how human beings worked: when you had freedom, you took it for granted; only after losing it did you realize how precious that modern life had been.

乔梦容想到哥哥：“淑真，你认识我和哥哥已有一段时日，你觉得哥哥爱我吗？”
Thoughts drifting to Prince Yi, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Shuzhen, you've known Brother and me a while now—do you think Brother loves me?"

仪王花心的样子出现在脑海中，他怎么可能对佳和郡主一心一意？千里寻无法说出实情：“你跟仪王天天在一起，他爱不爱你，难道感觉不出？”
Prince Yi's flirtatious glances flashed across Qianli Xun's mind. How could he be devoted to Princess Jiahe? Yet she couldn't bear telling her the blunt truth: "You're with Prince Yi every day—can't you feel whether he loves you?"

乔梦容叹口气：“唉！哥哥病着时，我很清楚他心里只有我，自从病好后，以前的事想不起来，连我与他的感情都忘记，我现在不是很确定他对我的感情……”
Qiao Mengrong sighed. "Back when Brother was sick, I knew for a fact he had room only for me. Since he recovered, he can't remember the past—he even forgot our love. Now I'm not so sure how he feels..."

“什么？仪王失忆过？”千里寻产生莫名的希冀，仪王该不会与申简辰有什么关系？
"Wait—Prince Yi had amnesia?!" A strange spark of hope leaped in Qianli Xun: could Prince Yi have some tie to Shen Jianchen?

“是的，哥哥失忆，以前的事情都不记得。”
"Yes. Brother lost his memory and remembers nothing from before."

“他是怎么失忆的？”
"How did he lose his memory?"

“哥哥本来已病入膏肓，咽下最后一口气，我正哭得伤心，他却奇迹般地活过来，可什么都不记得。邹嬷嬷说，哥哥到鬼门关走一趟，阎王不要他，又回来，因喝过孟婆汤，以前的记忆全无。”
"Brother was at death's door and breathed his last breath. While I was sobbing my heart out, he miraculously came back to life, remembering nothing. Matron Zou said Brother wandered to the Gates of Hell, King Yama sent him back, and because he'd drunk Mengpo's Soup, his past memories were wiped clean."

“原来仪王是起死回生。”千里寻轻声说着。因她有过神奇的经历，对“鬼门关”和“孟婆汤”的说法深信不疑。
"So Prince Yi came back from the dead." Qianli Xun murmured under her breath. After her own wild crossing-over, tales of the Gates of Hell and Mengpo's Soup sounded believable enough.

她放弃了仪王可能是申简辰的想法，也能够理解为何仪王有婚约还会对她放电，原来是不记得以前的感情。
She dropped the notion that Prince Yi could be Shen Jianchen—and at last understood why an engaged prince kept flirting with her: he had forgotten his old love.

可再不记得，与佳和郡主的婚约实实在在，因婚约也不应对其他女子动情。
Still, forgetting or not, his engagement to Princess Jiahe stood solid as rock, giving him zero right to flirt with other women.

“哥哥现在依然对我很好，却不像以前那么热情，你说我该怎么办？”
"Brother still treats me well, just without the warmth he used to have. What should I do?"

乔梦容的问题使千里寻陷入沉思，每个人的人生都有不幸，佳和郡主与仪王有婚约，在外人眼中看似完美，实则不尽然，只有身处其中，才能品出酸甜苦辣。
Qiao Mengrong's question plunged Qianli Xun into silence. Every life held its hidden thorns: to outsiders, Princess Jiahe's imperial betrothal to Prince Yi looked flawless, yet only the one inside tasted its bittersweet ache.

她与文渊没有婚约，又因外力受阻，心却连在一起，千里寻希望她与文渊的心力最终能突破外力限制。
She and Wenyuan had no betrothal and faced walls on every side, yet their hearts beat as one. Qianli Xun prayed their shared devotion would smash through every barrier.

“淑真，你睡着了吗？”
"Shuzhen, are you asleep?"

“哦，没有，我是在想你跟仪王有婚约，还是官家赐婚，仪王娶你不容置疑。比起很多女子，已经很幸福，起码能嫁给喜欢的人。至于仪王的变化，不必太在意，还记得之前我教你的吗？”
"Oh, no. Just thinking—you and Prince Yi have an imperial betrothal from His Majesty, so he's guaranteed to marry you. Compared to most women, you're already lucky, marrying the man you love. Don't dwell on how he changed—remember the advice I gave you before?"

乔梦容早已忘记：“什么？”
Qiao Mengrong had already forgotten. "What advice?"

“我再说一遍，可要记清楚。嫁给仪王后，你肯定是正室，时刻把正室的地位巩固好，不要在乎仪王纳妾，他想纳谁让他纳，你大度些，他才会对你印象好。不要把对仪王的感情看得太重，看得越重对你越不利，要时刻保持理智，记住没？”
"I'll repeat it, so listen close! Once you marry Prince Yi, you're the primary wife. Guard your authority as primary wife tight, and don't sweat him taking concubines—let him take whoever he wants, and your graciousness will win his favor. Don't invest too much heart in him either; the heavier your heart leans on him, the worse off you'll be. Keep a cool head—got it?"

这回乔梦容听进心里：“你说得没错，我不能把对哥哥的感情看得太重，要保持理智。不过，我总觉得哥哥不是那种会纳妾的人。”
This time the words sank in: "You're right—I can't put too much of my heart on Brother; I have to stay levelheaded. Still, I don't think Brother's the type to take concubines."

千里寻很想告诉乔梦容，仪王是如何对她放电，还是忍住，只怕说出会影响两人之间的关系。于是编道：“告诉你，我亲眼见仪王盯着别的女子看。”
Qianli Xun itched to tell her how Prince Yi kept tossing sparks her own way, yet held back lest it poison their friendship. Instead she invented a tale: "Listen, I saw Prince Yi staring at another girl with my own eyes."

乔梦容立刻说不出话来，半天哽咽道：“你……你在……哪里见到？”
Qiao Mengrong froze, choking out after a long beat: "W-where... did you see that?"

千里寻搜寻记忆，该说哪一次？想到七夕节那日，逛街时，街上姑娘最多，随便挑一位即可。
Racking her memory for a scene, Qianli Xun landed on Qixi Festival—crowds of pretty girls everywhere!

“七夕那日，我们去逛街，你跟仪王走在前面，我无意中瞧见他看旁边经过的姑娘，看得相当仔细，眼神很不对劲，反正当时我感觉他就是个色狼。”
"On Qixi, when we went strolling. You and Prince Yi walked ahead, and I caught him eyeing a passing girl up and down—creepy look in his eyes, total lecher vibes!"

乔梦容回想那日的情景，很快破涕为笑：“哦，是那日，你误会哥哥，他是看一位女子来着，是我让哥哥看的，那女子手中的摩睺罗太像哥哥小时候，他便多看了几眼。”
Thinking back to that day, Qiao Mengrong burst into relieved laughter through her tears: "Oh, that! You misunderstood Brother! He did look at a girl, because I told him to—the Mohouluo clay doll in her hand looked just like Brother when he was little, so he looked twice!"

千里寻本想胡乱邹个场景，提醒佳和郡主多个心眼儿，没承想抛出去的球，又被踢回来，只好说：“可能是我看错，反正你还是小心些，以免嫁给他后……啊……啊……失望。”
Trying to spin a caution only to get her serve smashed straight back in her face, Qianli Xun muttered, "Guess I saw wrong, then. Stay sharp anyway, so after you marry him you won't... *yawn*... end up disappointed."

千里寻开始打哈欠，困意渐渐袭来。
Yawns rolled over Qianli Xun as sleep pulled at her lids.

“从没有人对我说这些话，谢谢你关心我。”
"Nobody's ever talked to me like this—thank you for caring about me."

“我是……关心你，希望你能……能有个好归宿，你……放心，我绝对不会……不会破坏……你们……的感……”千里寻断断续续说完，已进入梦乡。
"Of course I... care about you... want you to have a happy home... Don't worry, I'll never... come between... the two of..." Murmuring broken phrases, Qianli Xun drifted fast asleep.

乔梦容还清醒着，因千里寻说得含糊不清，并未听清她后面说些什么。好友已入睡，没有聊天儿的对象，乔梦容翻过身，也逐渐入睡。
Still wide awake, Qiao Mengrong missed her friend's mumbled last words. With her confidante asleep, she turned onto her side and slipped off to sleep too.

……
...

两个女孩子昨晚聊得太久，天亮时，还继续睡着。
Having talked into the wee hours, both girls slept right past dawn.

丫鬟们很知趣，没有打扰她们。
Tactful, the maids left them to sleep undisturbed.

两人睡得正酣时，乔守仁带着几名力气大的下人，手拿棍子和绳子，闯进幽林别居，那架势一看就是要绑人。
Right as they slept soundest, Qiao Shouren stormed into Youlin Retreat with a crew of brawny servants carrying clubs and ropes—plainly here to haul someone away in bonds.

李管家迎上去：“老爷。”
Butler Li hurried out: "Master!"

乔守仁厉声问道：“文渊住哪里？”
Qiao Shouren barked, "Where is Wenyuan's courtyard?"

李管家知道事态不好，已没机会通知自家公子：“我带老爷过去。”
Sensing disaster with zero chance to tip off his young master, Butler Li bowed: "This way, Master."

到达乔文仙居住的院子，乔守仁一脚踹开院门，站在院中：“文渊！你给我出来！”
Reaching Qiao Wenxian's yard, Qiao Shouren kicked the gate wide open and roared into the courtyard: "Wenyuan! Get out here!"

乔文仙晚上没睡好，又起个大早，依旧在翻书，越是心急，越从书上翻不出所以然来。听到父亲的声音，打开门走出去。
Having lain awake most of the night and risen at dawn, Qiao Wenxian sat flipping books in vain frustration when his father's shout shook the yard. Opening the door, he stepped out.

从眼前的架势判断，父亲显然是想把他绑回乔府：“父亲不是让我老实待在别居吗？为何带他们前来？”
One glance at the clubs and ropes told him Father meant to haul him back to Qiao Manor in bonds. "Didn't Father order me to stay at the retreat? Why bring this mob?"

“为何带他们？得问你自己！我让你待在别居，一来陪伴仪王，二来修身养性，可你却把不该留的人留在别居，今日我把你绑回乔府，你就老老实实在乔府待着！”
"Ask yourself why! I left you at the retreat to keep Prince Yi company and calm your mind—and here you go letting a woman who has no business here sleep overnight! Today I'm tying you up and hauling you back to Qiao Manor to stay put!"

听父亲的话，昨晚已有人到乔府打小报告。也难怪，幽林别居的下人都是父亲安排，必然有他的心腹，乔文仙怪自己太大意。
A spy among the retreat's staff must have run straight to Qiao Manor last night! No surprise—Father had picked every servant here. Qiao Wenxian kicked himself for getting careless.

还没等乔文仙再次说话，下人们蜂拥而上，手举大棒子，将他团团围住。乔文仙不会功夫，无法反抗。又上去几个下人，将他的手脚紧紧控制，用绳子捆绑住全身。
Before he could utter another word, the servants swarmed in with raised clubs. Knowing no martial arts, Qiao Wenxian couldn't fight back; several big men pinned his arms and legs and trussed him tight with rope.

乔文仙努力挣扎，一点儿用都没有：“父亲！你以为把我绑起来就会改变我的想法，告诉你，我依然爱淑真，非她不娶！”
Struggling in vain, Qiao Wenxian shouted, "Father! Think tying me up will change my heart?! Listen well—I still love Shuzhen, and I'll marry her or nobody!"

乔守仁怒道：“给我把公子抬上马车！”
Qiao Shouren barked, "Carry Young Master onto the carriage!"

南双跪在地上：“老爷，您就饶了公子吧，公子够可怜……”
Nan Shuang threw himself to his knees: "Have mercy on Young Master, Master—he's miserable enough!"

乔守仁训道：“你个小兔崽子，别以为我不知道，在中间帮文渊与朱家姑娘传递消息，今日也把你绑起来，一并关入乔府！”
Qiao Shouren snapped, "You little rascal—don't think I don't know you've been running messages between Wenyuan and Miss Zhu! Tie him up too and lock them both in Qiao Manor!"

下人听到，立刻把南双也给捆了。
At the command, the servants trussed Nan Shuang up as well.

乔守仁正要将捆绑好的儿子带走，听到仪王的声音：“外公何必如此对待文渊？”
Just as Qiao Shouren moved to haul his bound son out, Prince Yi's voice cut across the yard: "Why treat Wenyuan like this, Grandfather?"

原来李管家将老爷带到公子的住处后，看出老爷前来会对公子不利，赶快跑去报告仪王，。
After leading Master to Young Master's courtyard and seeing trouble brewing, Butler Li had bolted straight to warn Prince Yi.

申简辰起得早，正在院中教白风练习拳法，见李管家行色匆匆而来，问后才知文渊之事，匆忙带领白风前来。
Up at dawn coaching Bai Feng in martial arts, Shen Jianchen heard Butler Li's panicked report and rushed straight over with Bai Feng.

白风本想上前把捆绑乔公子的人打一顿，被王爷阻拦：“对付他们不需要用我教你的拳法。”
Bai Feng had itched to jump in and clobber the men tying Young Master Qiao, only for Prince Yi to hold him back: "No need to use the fists I taught you on them."

申简辰说完，才对乔守仁喊出话来。
With that, Shen Jianchen had called out to Qiao Shouren.
