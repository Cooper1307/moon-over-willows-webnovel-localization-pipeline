## Chapter 343: Stripped of Honor, Too Ashamed to Go Home

Waking before dawn once the acupoint wore off, Qianli Xun huddled weeping in the corner while smug Wanyan Chi gloated over being her first man—swearing he'd stay faithful and take her back to Lin'an someday, until she yanked the whole quilt off him in fury to freeze him out. Leaving sheepishly at sunrise promising never to touch her uninvited again, he gave her free run of the manor.

Strolling out at noon with Huilanna into the stark northern winter, she paused by a round pond strewn with red maple leaves beside withered chrysanthemums and bright yellow courtyard citrus, murmuring Zhu Shuzhen's ***Writing My Thoughts in Early Winter***:

*"My eyes meet the round pond's winter scene— / lotus withered, chrysanthemums gone wild. / Chill winds invade my midnight pillow; / biting frost makes me shrink from morning rouge. / Crimson maple leaves flip across the river gale; / yellow citrus hangs before the courtyard hall. / I brush a poem hoping to ease this gloom, / yet facing the view only doubles my grief."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Clapping warmly from behind, Wanyan Chi met her blistering hatred by handing her the real **antidote pill** outright—*"Kill me if you hate me that much, just lead my mother to my grave!"*

Swallowing the pill and feeling power surge back into her fists, Qianli Xun smashed a full-strength punch straight into his unguarded chest, sending him kneeling coughing blood—yet froze inches from his skull when weeping Huilanna clung to her knees pleading for the generous prince who'd saved her father's life, surrounded by loyal guards swearing blood vengeance across generations if he died.

Dropping her fist in disgusted defeat—too ashamed now to face either Haoyu or Prince Yi—she marched straight toward the front gates to vanish on her own.
