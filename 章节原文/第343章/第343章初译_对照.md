第343章 无爱失贞家难归
## Chapter 343: Defiled Without Love, No Way Home

身上的穴位被解开时，完颜池早已得手。
When the seal on her acupoints was released, Wanyan Chi had already had his way.

天还没亮，千里寻只穿着贴身衣物，缩在床榻的角落哭泣，伤心程度不亚于第一次失恋时。不同的是那时她不会哭出声来，而现在她一直出声哭泣：“呜呜……！”
Dawn had not come. Qianli Xun sat in her underthings, huddled in a corner of the bed, crying. Her grief was no less than it had been after her first heartbreak. Then she had not cried aloud. Now she could not stop: "Wah...!"

她努力守护的贞洁既没有给她所爱之人，也没有给与她有婚姻关系的陈浩宇，却被一个金人夺走。她越想越委屈，越想越恨，哭的也越发厉害。
The chastity she had guarded so hard had gone neither to the man she loved nor to Chen Haoyu, the man she was married to. A Jin man had taken it. The more she thought, the more wronged she felt, the more she hated, and the harder she cried.

完颜池坐在床榻上傻乐：“嘿嘿，没想到本王竟是你第一个男人，嘿嘿……”
Wanyan Chi sat on the bed, grinning like a fool. "Heh, heh. Who would have thought I was your first man? Heh, heh..."

“呜呜！滚！滚！呜呜……！”千里寻边哭边喊。
"Wah! Get off! Get off! Wah...!" Qianli Xun shouted between sobs.

完颜池并不滚，这一晚，美人的心依旧没被他征服，反而是他，成为美人的第一个男人而倍加珍惜。他把被子盖在美人的身上：“小心冻着。”
He did not get off. All night, the beauty's heart had stayed unconquered. Instead, he had become her first man, and that made him cherish her all the more. He drew the quilt over her. "Don't catch cold."

千里寻掀开盖在身上的被子：“我就是冻死，也不想再见到你！呜呜……！”
Qianli Xun flung the quilt back. "I'd rather freeze to death than see you again! Wah...!"

完颜池既开心又担心：“别哭了，从今往后你是本王的人，本王会对你加倍好，等大金拿下汴梁，带你回会宁，本王发誓，有了你以后，身边不会再多一个女人。”
Wanyan Chi was pleased and worried at once. "Stop crying. From now on you're mine. I'll treat you twice as well. When Jin takes Bianliang, I'll take you back to Huining. I swear it: once I have you, there'll be no other woman by my side."

“呜呜！我不会跟你去会宁，我要回临安，呜呜……！”
"Wah! I won't go to Huining with you. I want to go back to Lin'an. Wah...!"

“好好，你不想去会宁，本王陪你待在燕京，等战事结束后，再带你去临安，正好拜见岳父岳母。”
"All right, all right. If you don't want Huining, I'll stay with you in Yanjing. When the fighting ends, I'll take you to Lin'an. We can call on your father and mother then."

千里寻委屈到说不出一句话，大家若知道她与一个金人圆房，不知该如何看待她？还是不回去得好。
Qianli Xun was too wounded to speak. If people learned she had consummated with a Jin man, what would they think of her? Better not to go back at all.

完颜池见怎么哄都没用，干脆躺在床上：“你继续哭，本王睡会儿。”
Wanyan Chi saw that coaxing did no good. He lay down. "Cry all you like. I'll sleep a while."

完颜池躺下继续说：“真想不通，本王这么优秀，多少女人想跟本王，她们要被本王这样，都不知乐成什么样，你却委屈，好像本王做错了什么事……”
He lay there and went on. "I can't understand it. I'm such a fine catch. So many women want me. If I did this to them, they'd be over the moon. But you're wronged, as if I'd done something wrong..."

完颜池说着困意袭来，伴着美人的哭声进入梦乡。
As he spoke, sleep came over him. He drifted off to the sound of the beauty's crying.

千里寻哭着哭着感到冷意袭来，拽过被子盖在自己身上。
Qianli Xun cried until cold crept over her. She dragged the quilt onto herself.

哭声越来越小，她开始思考接下来的人生。
Her sobs died down. She began to think about the life ahead of her.

她怨自己没有勇气结束生命，既如此还要生活下去，难道必须当完颜池的妃子？她根本不乐意。
She hated herself for not having the courage to end it. Since she had to go on living, did that mean she must become Wanyan Chi's consort? She wanted no part of it.

她的身体还清晰地记着他对待她的感觉，毫无快乐可言，只有屈辱，还有一种珍惜了多年的宝贝被贼人夺走的恨！因恨千里寻把被子全部拉到自己身上：“冻死你！”
Her body still remembered how he had handled her. No pleasure in it. Only shame. And the hatred of a treasure she had guarded for years, stolen by a thief! Hate made her yank the whole quilt to her side. "Freeze, then!"

被子带来的暖意像微醺的酒酿，她忍不住打起盹来，靠在榻边，带着屈辱的情绪睡去。
The quilt's warmth was like wine going to her head. She dozed off against the edge of the bed, still wrapped in shame.

天快亮时，完颜池被冻醒，发现身上的被子被美人拉去裹着，她靠在床榻边睡得正酣。
Near dawn, cold woke Wanyan Chi. The beauty had pulled the quilt off him and wrapped herself in it. She was deep asleep, propped against the side of the bed.

完颜池坐起来，面带笑容欣赏了一会儿美人的睡姿，自语道：“你睡着的样子也这么美，不哭不闹多好，本王扶你躺下。”
Wanyan Chi sat up and watched her sleep, smiling. "Even asleep you're lovely. Much better when you're not crying or fighting. Let me lay you down properly."

完颜池伸手去抱美人，想将她平放在床上，好睡得舒服些。
He reached to lift her, meaning to lay her flat on the bed so she would sleep more comfortably.

千里寻在梦中感受到有一双万恶之手向她袭击而来，立刻惊醒，发现完颜池已将她抱住。
In a dream, Qianli Xun felt a pair of wicked hands come at her. She woke with a start to find Wanyan Chi holding her.

“你要做什么？”
"What are you doing?"

“这样睡不舒服，本王想帮你躺好。”
"This position isn't comfortable. I want to help you lie properly."

“别碰我！”千里寻挣脱开来，继续靠在床榻边。
"Don't touch me!" She broke free and leaned back against the bed's edge.

完颜池很无奈：“放心，以后不经过你同意，本王不会随便碰你。本王去别的屋子睡，好好休息。记着，本王是爱你才迁就你，不是怕你。”
Wanyan Chi had no answer to that. "Don't worry. From now on I won't touch you without your leave. I'll sleep in another room. Rest well. Remember: I give in to you because I love you, not because I fear you."

“哼！等我功力恢复，你只会怕我。”
"Hah! Once my skill comes back, you'll be the one afraid."

“恢复了本王也不怕，因为本王是你的第一个男人。”
"Even then I won't fear you. I'm your first man."

“还不快滚！”
"Then why don't you get out!"

“这就滚，你跟本王有了第一次，应该还会想有第二次甚至无数次，想本王了差人来唤，本王随时奉陪。”
"I'm going. We've had a first time. You'll want a second, and then countless more. When you miss me, send someone to call. I'll come whenever you wish."

“滚！滚！”千里寻大喊着，声音近似歇斯底里。
"Get out! Get out!" Qianli Xun screamed, her voice near hysteria.

完颜池则心花怒放，吹着口哨离开。
Wanyan Chi's heart bloomed. He whistled as he left.

确定完颜池离开后，千里寻才安心躺下，眼泪流个不停。
Only when she was sure he was gone did Qianli Xun lie back. Tears kept running.

睡着后，还有一滴泪从眼角滚落下来，若有人看到会认为梦中的她正在哭泣。
After she fell asleep, another tear rolled from the corner of her eye. Anyone seeing it would have thought she was crying in her dream.

睡到午时醒来，在丫鬟的伺候下梳洗完毕，简单用过午饭，她要求道：“我想出去走走。”
She woke near noon. The maids helped her wash and dress. She ate a simple lunch, then asked, "I want to go out for a walk."

会兰娜说：“小王爷吩咐过，姑娘已自由，想去哪里都可以。”
Huilanna said, "The Young Prince gave orders. You're free, miss. You may go wherever you like."

被关了许久，每日都渴望自由，可这自由是用她的贞洁换来，想着都心痛。
She had been shut up so long, had longed for freedom every day. And this freedom had cost her chastity. The thought made her chest ache.

“今日阳光不错，晒晒太阳，我的心情也许会好些。”
"The sun's good today. A little sun might lift my mood."

会兰娜取来厚厚的斗篷给她披上，陪她走出屋外。
Huilanna brought a thick cloak and draped it over her, then went out with her.

眼前的冬季既熟悉又陌生。熟悉的是冬日的寒冷，连高空挂着的红日都无法驱赶走寒意；陌生的是，北方的冬日冷得刺骨，也更显萧瑟，王府里全是光秃秃的树木，连一株梅花都没有，毫无生气。
The winter before her was familiar and strange. Familiar: the cold, which the red sun hanging high in the sky could not drive off. Strange: the northern cold bit to the bone and looked more barren still. The manor was all bare trees, not one plum branch, nothing alive.

千里寻踩踏着落叶，想起幽林别居，也到处栽种着树木，比燕京王府的树木还多，她曾在冬日踩踏着落叶走过，此时回忆起却比这里的树木更令她心动。
Qianli Xun walked over fallen leaves. She thought of Youlin Retreat, where trees grew everywhere, more than in this prince's manor in Yanjing. She had once walked through fallen leaves there in winter. Remembering those trees now stirred her more than these did.

她带着情绪自语道：“王府看着挺大，景致却不怎么样。”
She said to herself, bitterness in her voice, "The manor looks big enough. There's nothing to see."

会兰娜说：“现在冬季，没有好景致也正常。奴婢听说姑娘是从南边来的，自然见过不少好景致，王府里光秃秃的树木居多，肯定与江南水乡没法比。”
Huilanna said, "It's winter. No wonder there's no fine view. I hear you come from the south, miss. You must have seen many fine views. The bare trees here can't compare with the water towns of Jiangnan."

提到“水乡”，会兰娜想起一处景致来：“我带姑娘去王府的另一处，姑娘绝对喜欢。”
At the words "water towns," Huilanna remembered a place. "Let me take you to another part of the manor. You'll love it, miss."

“哪一处？”
"Which part?"

会兰娜怕照实说姑娘不乐意去，只说：“姑娘随我去便知。”
Afraid the truth would make the girl refuse, Huilanna said only, "Come with me and see."

为了心情好些，千里寻点头答应，跟在会兰娜身后。
To lift her mood, Qianli Xun nodded and followed.

燕京的王府很大，绕了一大圈，会兰娜才停下：“姑娘，到了。”
The prince's manor in Yanjing was vast. They walked a long way around before Huilanna stopped. "We're here, miss."

千里寻瞧去，有一圆形的池子，池子里是枯萎的荷花，池水的表面散落着枫叶。岸边栽种着几棵枫树，满树原本红彤彤的枫叶已尽显凋零。
Qianli Xun looked. A round pond, withered lotus in it, maple leaves scattered across the water. A few maples grew on the bank; their red leaves, once a blaze, were now falling away.

离枫树不远处，栽种着一片菊花，可惜也枯萎。菊花的旁边是三棵橘树，绿意盎然，为毫无生命气息的冬季增色不少。绿色的叶子中间时不时挂着一颗颗黄橘。橘在北方应为枳，难以下咽，王府栽种橘树应该仅仅为观赏。
Not far from the maples stood a bed of chrysanthemums, also withered, poor things. Beside them, three orange trees, green and thriving, gave the lifeless winter some color. Yellow oranges hung here and there among the leaves. In the north the fruit should have been the bitter trifoliate orange, barely edible. The manor must have planted these trees only to look at.

此情此景，加上无所安放的心境，千里寻想起朱淑真的一首诗《初冬书怀》，有感而发：
The sight, and a heart with nowhere to rest, brought Zhu Shuzhen's poem "Writing My Heart in Early Winter" to Qianli Xun's mind. It fitted, and she recited it:

“触目圆池景，荷枯菊已荒。风寒侵夜枕，霜冻怯晨妆。江上风翻赤，庭前橘带黄。题诗欲排闷，对景倍悲伤。”
[TN: Translator's rendering] Before my eyes, the round pond: lotus withered, chrysanthemums gone to waste. Wind and cold bite through the night pillow; frost makes me dread the morning mirror. On the river, wind rolls up red leaves; before the courtyard, the oranges are yellow. I write a poem to push the gloom away, but facing this scene, sorrow doubles.

传来一阵拍手的声音，千里寻顺着声音看去，完颜池一边拍手一边朝她走来。
A burst of clapping came. Qianli Xun turned toward it. Wanyan Chi was walking to her, clapping.

她向会兰娜露出询的目光。
She shot Huilanna a questioning look.

会兰娜解释道：“此处景致好些，小王爷就住在旁边。”
Huilanna explained, "The view is better here. The Young Prince lives just next door."

完颜池走近后，停下拍手的动作：“没想到本王喜欢的人还会作诗，更令本王刮目相看。”
Wanyan Chi came close and stopped clapping. "I never guessed the woman I like could write poetry. You've made me see you in a new light."

千里寻不由自主想起昨晚他在床上对待自己的情景，既厌恶又羞愧，转身打算离开。
Qianli Xun could not help remembering how he had used her in bed the night before. Revulsion and shame. She turned to leave.

完颜池堵在面前：“既然想逛，本王陪你。”
Wanyan Chi stepped into her path. "Since you wanted a walk, I'll go with you."

“有你陪伴，我会更难受，盼着药劲儿尽快消失，好恢复如初，杀了你再回临安。”
"With you beside me I'll feel worse. I only wish the drug would wear off soon, so I can be myself again, kill you, and go back to Lin'an."

“你这么恨本王？”
"You hate me that much?"

“我恨不得扒了你的皮！吃了你的肉！啃了你的骨！”
"I want to flay you! Eat your flesh! Gnaw your bones!"

完颜池伤感道：“你竟如此恨本王？既如此，本王把解药给你，现在就动手！”
Wanyan Chi looked hurt. "You hate me so? Then I'll give you the antidote. Right now!"

完颜池从怀里掏出一瓶药，倒出一粒：“会兰娜，把药丸拿给她吃。”
He took a bottle from his robe and tipped out one pill. "Huilanna, give her the pill."

会兰娜掏出干净的巾帕，接过小王爷手中的药丸，送至姑娘面前。
Huilanna took a clean handkerchief, accepted the pill from the Young Prince, and held it out to the girl.

千里寻盯着那丸药，甚是怀疑，完颜池该不会又使什么奸诈计谋害她？
Qianli Xun stared at it, suspicious. Was Wanyan Chi up to another trick?

完颜池见美人犹豫要不要吃下，宽慰道：“本王不会再骗你。”
He saw her hesitate. "I won't lie to you again."

千里寻依然不信：“你会愿意早些送死？”
She still did not believe him. "You'd want to die sooner?"

“能死在你的拳下，乐意之至。杀了本王后，替本王找到娘亲，把她带到本王坟前，也好让本王死而无憾。”
"To die under your fist? Gladly. After you kill me, find my mother and bring her to my grave. Then I can die without regret."

“好！老娘定会满足你！”
"Good! I'll see you get your wish!"

千里寻拿起药丸，放入口内吞下。
Qianli Xun took the pill, put it in her mouth, and swallowed.

没过多久，全身的肌肉变得松快起来，她试着攥紧拳头，完颜池果然没骗她，她的功力已恢复。
Before long, her muscles loosened. She tried clenching her fist. He had told the truth. Her skill had returned.

想到昨晚的屈辱，千里寻将力量集中在拳头，使出一拳，向完颜池冲去。
The shame of the night before rose in her. She drove power into her fist and lunged at Wanyan Chi.

完颜池出拳做出应战的准备，当美人的靠近他时，他却收起动作，迎接美人袭击而来的拳头。
Wanyan Chi threw up a fist, ready to fight. But as the beauty closed in, he dropped his guard and met her incoming fist with his chest.

对方突然收起所有防备，千里寻感到意外，她袭击而出的拳头已收不回，关键她从未想过要收回，一拳落在完颜池的胸口。
He had given up all defense without warning. Qianli Xun was taken aback. Her fist was already on its way. The truth was, she had never thought of pulling it back. It landed on his chest.

完颜池口吐鲜血，跪在地上，却面带笑容：“一拳……还要不了……本王的命，再……再来……再来一拳。”
Blood sprayed from Wanyan Chi's mouth. He fell to his knees, smiling. "One punch... won't... kill me. Again... come again... one more."

千里寻从来没遇到过主动求死之人，她变得犹豫。
Qianli Xun had never met a man who begged for death. She faltered.

王府的侍卫聚拢而来，手执刀剑准备保护小王爷。
The manor guards gathered, blades drawn, ready to protect the Young Prince.

完颜池命令道：“都……都……退到一边！她是……本王的人，杀……本王……本王……心甘情愿……不许……拦……”
Wanyan Chi gave the order. "All... all of you, stand aside! She's... mine. If she kills me... I... consent... don't... stop her..."

如此好的机会杀死夺走自己贞洁之人，千里寻不再犹豫：“既然你主动送死，老娘现在送你归西！”
Here was her chance to kill the man who had taken her chastity. She faltered no longer. "You ask for death. I'll send you on your way now!"

完颜池说：“能成为……你的第一个……男人，本王……死而无憾。”
Wanyan Chi said, "To be... your first man... I die... content."

千里寻更加气愤：“现在就去死！”
That made her angrier. "Then die!"

千里寻继续出拳向完颜池袭击而去，拳头快要击打在完颜池的脑门上时，她的腿被会兰娜从身后抱住。
She struck again. Her fist was inches from his forehead when Huilanna caught her leg from behind.

“姑娘！您不能杀小王爷！他是好人！”
"Miss! You can't kill the Young Prince! He's a good man!"

拳头的力度冲击着气流扑面而来，完颜池不由闭上眼睛。听到会兰娜的为他求情，他睁开眼，美人的拳头就在眼前。
The force of the punch pushed air into his face. Wanyan Chi shut his eyes. Hearing Huilanna plead for him, he opened them. The beauty's fist hung right before him.

“会兰娜……别……拦着，牡丹花下死……做鬼也……风流，我……求之不得。”
"Huilanna... don't... stop her. To die beneath the peonies... even as a ghost, to die a lover. I... ask for nothing better."

“不！小王爷！没有您，我阿爹早已病死，是您请巫医救了他，还分文不取，又让我到王府当下人，每月挣的钱够养活爹娘，您就是我全家的恩人，我不能眼睁睁看着恩人死在我面前。姑娘，您有任何气，尽管撒在我身上，一拳打死我也行，我不过是个奴婢，死不足惜，可若您杀了王爷，天下就少了一位有能力帮助可怜人的王爷。您不知道，在会宁小王爷乐善好施，救济穷人，老百姓都对小王爷夸赞有加，若姑娘杀了小王爷，只怕会被人唾骂。”
"No! Young Prince! Without you, my father would have died of sickness long ago. You fetched a shaman-healer and saved him, and took no payment. You let me work here in the manor. The wages feed my parents. You're the blessing of my whole family. I can't stand by and watch my benefactor die before me. Miss, if you're angry, take it out on me. Kill me with one punch if you like. I'm only a slave; my life is worth nothing. But if you kill the prince, the world loses a man with the power to help the poor. You don't know: back in Huining, the Young Prince gave to the needy and relieved the poor. People sang his praises. If you kill him, they'll curse you for it."

会兰娜的一番言论直击千里寻的肺腑，她的拳头不由软下来，她仍旧撑住，继续用拳头对准完颜池：“被金人唾骂是宋人的荣耀。”
Huilanna's words struck home. Qianli Xun's fist softened. She kept it raised, still pointed at Wanyan Chi. "Being cursed by Jin people is an honor to a Song."

周围的侍卫见状全部放下刀剑，跪在地上，齐声求道：“求姑娘放了小王爷！”
The guards saw it and laid down their blades. They knelt and said with one voice, "Please spare the Young Prince, miss!"

千里寻环顾四周，厉声问道：“你们为何跪下？”
Qianli Xun looked around. "Why are you kneeling?" she demanded.

带头的侍卫说：“姑娘若杀了小王爷，我等会为他报仇！”
The guard in front said, "If you kill the Young Prince, we'll avenge him."

千里寻十分不屑：“哼！老娘岂会怕你们？”
She sneered. "Hah! As if I feared you?"

带头的侍卫继续说：“我等的确不是姑娘的对手，但我等会将报仇之事牢记在心，代代相传，杀不了姑娘，只要您的家人和后辈在，此仇亦可报得。姑娘拳头厉害，不敢保证您的家人和后辈也会像您一样厉害。”
The guard went on. "We're no match for you, it's true. But we'll pass the duty down, from son to son. If we can't kill you, we'll have your kin and your descendants. Your fist is strong. There's no guarantee theirs will be."

“老娘今日偏要杀了他！看你们能将我怎样？”千里寻口中说着狂言，拳头却还停留在原地。
"I'll kill him today! Let's see what you can do!" The boast left her mouth, but her fist stayed where it was.

完颜池趁机说：“别……吓唬她，我死了……谁都不许……报仇！”
Wanyan Chi seized the moment. "Don't... frighten her. If I die... nobody... is to avenge me!"

千里寻的拳头彻底泄气，她收了回去：“你的命先给老娘留着，哪一日老娘能下得去狠手，再来索取！”
All the strength went out of her fist. She drew it back. "Keep your life for now. When I can bring myself to strike hard, I'll come and claim it."

小王爷的命保住，会兰娜松口气放开手，见姑娘往回走，爬起来向小王爷行礼后迅速跟上。
With the Young Prince's life safe, Huilanna let go. She saw the girl walk away, scrambled up, bowed to the Young Prince, and hurried after her.

完颜池整个人松懈下来，笑着自语道：“就知道……你……不忍心杀本王。”
Wanyan Chi went limp. "I knew... you... couldn't bring yourself to kill me," he said, smiling.

千里寻恨自己，既不忍心杀掉别人眼中的好人，又不愿接受他的感情，还让对方夺去她的贞洁，接下来她该如何是好？千里寻打算离开燕京的王府。
Qianli Xun hated herself. She could not kill a man others called good. She could not accept his love. And she had let him take her chastity. What was she to do now? She decided to leave the prince's manor in Yanjing.

离开后该去哪里？回临安的陈家？她已失去贞节，回去后如何面对陈浩宇？
Where would she go? Back to the Chen family in Lin'an? She had lost her virtue. How could she face Chen Haoyu?

要不去汴梁找仪王？不，她去了会打扰梦容的好日子，说不定又惹得她旧病复发，再说她已失去贞洁，已经没有资格念着心中的感情。
Or to Bianliang, to Prince Yi? No. She would only disturb Mengrong's good life, and might bring on her old illness again. Besides, she had lost her chastity. She had no right to nurse the feelings in her heart.

实在不行回临安的朱府，她立即否认，回朱府与回陈家有何区别？
If worst came to worst, back to Zhu Manor in Lin'an? She rejected the thought at once. How was Zhu Manor any different from the Chen house?

到底该去哪里？她无法做决定，反正她不想再见到完颜池，只要看不到他即可，也许她会渐渐忘记不愉快的经历。
Where then? She could not decide. All she knew was that she did not want to see Wanyan Chi again. As long as she never saw him, perhaps the ugly memory would fade.

千里寻回到临时住的屋子，想要收拾包袱就此离去，四下环顾，没有一样东西属于她，有什么好收拾的？
Qianli Xun went back to the room she had been given and started to pack so she could leave. She looked around. Nothing there belonged to her. What was there to pack?

她转身向屋外走去。
She turned and walked out.

会兰娜跟上去问：“姑娘要去哪里？”
Huilanna hurried after her. "Where are you going, miss?"

“告诉完颜池，老娘要离开王府，让他不许拦着。”
"Tell Wanyan Chi I'm leaving the manor. He's not to stop me."

千里寻说完大踏步往门口走。
She finished speaking and strode toward the gate.

会兰娜则急忙赶着去向王爷禀报。
Huilanna rushed off to report to the prince.
