### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 台词被中断时的英文标点规范（对应第7、8段：夏果插话）
> **【原文】** 夏果说："我若跟了陈公子只有被卖的份儿！……" 韦嬷嬷厉声斥责："夏果！哪有你说话的份儿？赶紧把嘴闭上！"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Xiaguo said, "If I followed Young Master Chen, I'd only end up being sold!**...**"
    Nanny Wei sharply rebuked, "Xiaguo! When did it become your place to speak? Shut your mouth immediately!"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "If I went with Young Master Chen, I'd just end up sold off to some awful place—" Xiaguo blurted out.
    "Xiaguo!" Nanny Wei snapped sharply, **cutting her off**. "Is it your place to speak when the masters are talking? Shut your mouth this instant!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. **标点规范**：在英文小说中，台词突然被打断**必须使用 Em-dash（—）**，而不是省略号（...，省略号表示声音渐小或犹豫）。
    2. 增加了 "cutting her off" 等动作衔接，强化了韦嬷嬷治家严厉的尊严。

#### 2. 武侠招式与以柔克刚的打斗描写（对应第31、38-40段：爆衣与比武）
> **【原文】** 高手走出竹棚... 使出内力，将上衣震碎... 他的拳脚如狂风暴雨般袭来... 千里寻灵活地躲避着，运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚，努力化解对方的攻势。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    The expert walked out... **Using his inner qi, he shattered his upper garment**, and the pieces fell to the ground. His muscles rippled...
    His fists and feet came like a storm... Qianli Xun dodged nimbly, **using her exquisite martial arts skills to overcome hardness with softness**, trying to deflect his attacks.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    The martial arts expert stepped out into the open courtyard. **Flexing his internal *qi*, he ruptured his upper shirt into rags** that fluttered to the ground. His massive, rock-hard muscles rippled in the sunlight, striking dread into the onlookers.
    ...The brute launched a fierce offensive. His punches and kicks rained down like a relentless typhoon, each strike packing pulverizing power. Qianli Xun ducked and weaved with fluid grace, **employing the soft-style counter techniques of Qianli Fist to redirect his brute force, yielding to his momentum to dismantle his guard.**
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. 动态强化：将“震碎上衣”描写为 "ruptured his upper shirt into rags"，赋予看客强烈的视觉冲击感。
    2. 武侠精修：把“以柔克刚”翻译为符合千里拳（Qianli Fist）设定的 "employing the soft-style counter techniques... to redirect his brute force"，画面感和武侠网文爽感瞬间拉满。

#### 3. 粗犷壮汉认输的喜剧反差（对应第49-51段：收徒与拒徒）
> **【原文】** 那高手努力爬起来，跪在地上："师傅在上，受徒弟一拜！" 千里寻在另一个时代有很多男徒弟，没有一个像他这般长相粗鲁的，此人她根本瞧不上："起来吧，我可不想收你做徒弟。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    The expert struggled to get up and knelt on the ground. "Master, please accept a bow from your disciple!" 
    Qianli Xun **had many male disciples in another era**, but none looked as rough as him. She had no interest in this person at all. "Get up. I don't want to take you as a disciple."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    The defeated brute struggled to his feet and dropped to both knees. "Master! Please accept your disciple's bow!"
    Qianli Xun had trained plenty of male disciples **in her past life**, but none looked quite this terrifyingly rough. She waved her hand dismissively. "Get up. I have zero interest in taking you as my disciple."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    用 "past life"（前世/上辈子）替代平淡的 "another era"。将女主内心的嫌弃（此人她根本瞧不上）表现得更具现代人的吐槽趣味："I have zero interest in taking you as my disciple."

#### 4. 提出胜负三个条件的强硬台词（对应第28段）
> **【原文】** "如若我赢了，第一，不准向竹民收租；第二，不准再打夏果的主意；第三，以后见我要问好行礼，然后赶紧从我面前滚开。你看如何？"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    "If I win, first, you won't collect rent from the bamboo people; second, you won't have any more ideas about Xiaguo; third, from now on, when you see me, you'll greet me politely and then quickly get out of my sight. How does that sound?"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "If I win, I have three conditions," Qianli Xun said coolly. "First, **you will waive all rent for the villagers permanently**. Second, **you will never harass Xiaguo again**. Third, whenever you see me in the future, **you will bow, greet me respectfully, and scram from my sight.** What do you say?"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    修饰了语气和句式。用 "waive all rent"（免除所有租金）、"never harass Xiaguo again"（绝不再骚扰夏果）、"scram from my sight"（从我视线里滚蛋）增强了女主立规矩时的硬核气场。

#### 5. 韦嬷嬷地道口语与贴心告诫（对应第62段：少管闲事）
> **【原文】** 韦嬷嬷叮嘱道："……不过我必须嘱咐姑娘，往后少管闲事，你再厉害，也帮不了天下所有需要帮助的人，要先把自己照顾好。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Nanny Wei instructed, "...But I must advise you, Young Miss—**stay out of other people's business from now on**. No matter how capable you are, you can't help everyone in the world who needs help. You need to take care of yourself first."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "I’ve certainly witnessed your formidable skill today, My Lady," Nanny Wei said affectionately, gently patting her hand. "...But I must urge you—**stop sticking your neck out in other people's business**. No matter how strong your fists are, you can't carry the weight of the whole world on your shoulders. You must put your own safety first."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    “少管闲事”在英文中针对“爱替别人打抱不平”的情境，最地道的习惯表达是 "**stop sticking your neck out**"（别再为别人的事冒风险/出头）。用 "My Lady" 统一称呼，并补充了慈爱拍手的微表情描写，显得温馨地道。
