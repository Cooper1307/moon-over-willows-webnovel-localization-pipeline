# Chapter 39: Expert Duel, New Contract

The village head's bamboo house was probably the best in the entire village—large, sturdy, with a spacious courtyard.

A bamboo canopy had been set up in the courtyard, with tables and chairs underneath. Chen Haoyu had arrived early with his men, sitting under the canopy waiting. The village head had already brewed tea and stood nearby with a smile.

Before long, Qianli Xun arrived with her two maids and Nanny Wei. From a distance, she saw that Chen Haoyu still had the same five people from yesterday. She glanced a few more times at the martial arts expert, thinking, *Chen Haoyu must be afraid of being beaten by me, so he brought in someone with high martial arts skills.*

The village head stepped forward to greet them. "Proprietor and Nanny Wei, please this way!"

Qianli Xun and Nanny Wei chose seats at a table, while the two maids stood beside their mistress.

Chen Haoyu stared at Xia Guo. "It's your good fortune that I've taken a liking to you. What's so good about following her? With your looks, it's a waste to serve her as a maid."

Xia Guo said, "If I followed Young Master Chen, I'd only end up being sold!—"

Nanny Wei sharply rebuked, "Xia Guo! When did it become your place to speak? Shut your mouth immediately!"

Xia Guo clamped her mouth shut and dared not say another word.

Qianli Xun looked at Chen Haoyu's lecherous expression with great displeasure. "Chen Haoyu, I told you to gather all the relevant people. Why are you the only one here?"

Chen Haoyu shifted his gaze away from Xia Guo. "What, these men don't count?"

Qianli Xun emphasized, "I meant people related to the bamboo forest."

Chen Haoyu said, "Do we need to invite your father and my father? That's completely unnecessary. The land root is in my hands, so I own the bamboo forest. Your father gave the bamboo forest as dowry to you, so you have the right to use it and collect rent. As long as you and I are here, everyone is present."

Although Qianli Xun despised Chen Haoyu, she had to admit he was right, so she stopped arguing with him.

Nanny Wei listened patiently to Chen Haoyu's words, then spoke up, "Young Master Chen, there's something I don't understand. Please enlighten me."

"Please ask, Nanny Wei. I'll answer without reservation."

"Although the Zhu family isn't as wealthy as the Chen family, they're not poor either. When our young mistress married, the worst item in her dowry was this bamboo forest. I really can't figure out why Young Master Chen specifically wants the bamboo forest?"

Chen Haoyu laughed, "It's just because someone talked big, saying the bamboo forest was her territory. I want to teach her a lesson and make her understand that as long as I want it, her territory can become mine."

Qianli Xun was about to explode, but Nanny Wei stopped her with a look. She had to suppress her anger and carefully listen to how Nanny Wei would deal with Chen Haoyu.

"So Young Master Chen is acting on impulse. Our young mistress isn't that narrow-minded. Considering the two families are related by marriage, and she calls your sister sister-in-law, she won't hold a grudge. She only hopes that Young Master Chen will follow the contract, return the original price, and pay a year's rent on behalf of the bamboo people. Our young mistress definitely won't say no."

It was going too smoothly. Chen Haoyu couldn't be happy at all. He'd been called narrow-minded and hadn't achieved his goal. He was unwilling to accept this. He'd invited an expert to back him up, so he at least needed to spar with Zhu Shuzhen to salvage his face after two failures. Besides, he hadn't gotten that maid Xia Guo yet—how could he let this matter rest so easily?

"I'm never vague when it comes to money. Two boxes of silver are right over there, totaling one thousand taels. The original price of the bamboo forest plus the bamboo people's rent is more than enough, with plenty left over!..."

Before Chen Haoyu finished speaking, the village head immediately bowed and flattered, "Young Master Chen, I thank you on behalf of all the bamboo people in the village. You're a living bodhisattva! Someday, we'll make a wooden plaque for you with the words 'Aiding the imperiled and assisting the destitute.' How does that sound?"

Chen Haoyu chuckled, "You can decide what to write. But for now, village head, step aside. I have matters to discuss with Zhu Shuzhen."

The village head, all smiles, respectfully stepped aside.

Chen Haoyu glanced at Xia Guo and said to Zhu Shuzhen, "Zhu Shuzhen, at the same time I buy back the bamboo forest, you must give me that maid."

Qianli Xun suppressed her anger. "Dream on! You can have the bamboo forest, but don't even think about touching a single hair on my maid's head!"

"Today I specifically want her. What can you do about it?"

"Today I specifically won't give her to you. What can you do about it?"

Chen Haoyu laughed loudly, "Hahaha! Aren't you so great? If you're so capable, fight the fighter beside me. If you win, I'll let that maid go. If you lose, obediently give me the maid along with everything else."

Qianli Xun glanced at the man again. He was burly and thick-muscled, his face all hard, brutal flesh. He was definitely not someone to mess with—once he started fighting, he'd be a deadly opponent. But Qianli Xun wasn't about to show weakness. Accepting the challenge was, she knew, the only right move.

"In that case, I'll spar with the fighter beside you. If I win, you must agree to three demands."

"Speak. I'll listen and then decide whether to agree."

"If I win, first, you won't collect rent from the bamboo people; second, you won't have any more ideas about Xia Guo; third, from now on, when you see me, you'll greet me with a bow and then quickly get out of my sight. How does that sound?"

For the first demand, Chen Haoyu didn't take it seriously—the bamboo forest didn't earn much money, so whether he collected rent or not didn't matter. For the second, he'd already said that if Zhu Shuzhen won, he'd let the maid go, so that wasn't a big problem either. The third demand angered him—wasn't this insulting him? Since he was born, Chen Haoyu had never suffered such great humiliation.

He was about to explode, but when he glanced at the fighter beside him and then looked at Zhu Shuzhen—frail and weak, she'd definitely lose once they fought—he immediately turned his anger into joy. "Fine! I agree!"

The fighter stepped out into the open. Channeling his inner qi, he tore his upper garment to shreds that fell to the ground. His muscles rippled, intimidating everyone who saw them.

Kuqiao thought, *How could the Young Miss possibly defeat him?* Xia Guo was also extremely worried, afraid the Young Miss would get hurt because of her.

Nanny Wei leaned close and whispered, "Young Miss, why not just give Xia Guo to Young Master Chen? From what I understand, the women Young Master Chen has played with—some were kept as maids, some were given silver and sent home. As for being sold to brothels, no one has seen it, so it's probably just rumors. With Xia Guo's looks, she'd likely be kept as a maid. You don't need to put yourself in danger for her."

Qianli Xun also whispered, "Don't worry, Nanny. Some people look fierce but aren't actually that strong. Besides, I'm not just doing this for Xia Guo. Chen Haoyu swallowed the bamboo forest, so I need to make him spit something out. It'll fight for our dignity and benefit the bamboo people. Why not do it?"

With that, Qianli Xun stood up, walked out of the bamboo canopy, and came before the fighter. Standing together, the contrast in their appearances was even more obvious.

Chen Haoyu sneered, "Zhu Shuzhen, it's not too late to admit defeat now."

Qianli Xun didn't pay him any mind. Before she could even think about how to throw a punch, he attacked with powerful force and fierce aggression. His fists and feet came like a storm, each blow carrying devastating force. Qianli Xun dodged nimbly, using yielding technique to turn his force aside and deflect his attacks.

A flicker of surprise crossed his eyes. This seemingly frail woman actually had such superb martial arts skills. He became vigilant and went all out to fight.

Despite Qianli Xun's efforts to dodge and counterattack, his strength was like a mountain. Each confrontation made her feel pressured and exhausted. It seemed that relying solely on technique was not enough to defeat such a strong and powerful opponent.

Nanny Wei and the others had their hearts in their mouths, sweat beading on their foreheads.

Seeing Zhu Shuzhen at a disadvantage, Chen Haoyu was naturally pleased. He thought, *My martial arts aren't great, but there are plenty of people stronger than you in the world. Can't I even handle you?*

Qianli Xun didn't give up. She quickly adjusted her tactics, using the gaps in his attacks to counter with flexible footwork and precise punches. She turned his strength into a weakness, exploiting his unstable center of gravity, striking his vital points again and again. He gradually began to falter.

Nanny Wei and the others finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Chen Haoyu became nervous. He never expected that beneath Zhu Shuzhen's frail exterior lay such unexpected strength.

The village head prayed in his heart, *Please let the Proprietor win, so all rent is exempted and the whole village can live well.*

He was beaten back several steps. Qianli Xun seized the chance and struck, hitting him in the abdomen. He fell to the ground. Qianli Xun withdrew her fist, panting heavily, with a look of triumphant joy on her face.

The fighter struggled to his feet and dropped to his knees. "Master, please accept a bow from your disciple!"

Qianli Xun had many male disciples in another era, but none looked as rough as him. She had no interest in this person at all. "Get up. I don't want to take you as a disciple."

He stood up. "Even if you're unwilling to take me as a disciple, I still recognize you as my master. My name is Lu Yuanfeng. We'll meet again!" With that, Lu Yuanfeng left, enduring his pain.

Chen Haoyu was completely dumbfounded. Not only had the man he'd brought lost, but he'd also recognized Zhu Shuzhen as his master. In all his life, Chen Haoyu had never been so humiliated.

Qianli Xun said, "Village head, bring paper and brush. I'm going to write a new contract right here, and have Young Master Chen and I both press our thumbprints."

The village head cheerfully set out the paper, brush, and red ink paste. Qianli Xun stepped forward, finished writing the contract, signed her name, put down the brush, and pressed her thumbprint. She turned to Chen Haoyu. "Your turn. Three conditions, no backing out on any of them."

Chen Haoyu stood up sullenly, walked over, picked up the brush, signed his name, and also pressed his thumbprint. "Zhu Shuzhen, don't think you'll be this lucky every time. One day you'll fall into my hands, and I'll make you pay dearly." With that, he took the contract and was about to leave with his hooligans.

The village head ran forward to stop him, bowed and asked, "Young Master Chen, is the rent for the bamboo forest really completely exempted from now on?"

Chen Haoyu said irritably, "It's all written in the contract. Do you think I'd renege on it? Now hurry up and get out of the way!"

The village head said excitedly, "Good, good, I'll get out of the way. Young Master Chen, take care!" Then he thanked Nanny Wei and the old Proprietor profusely.

Qianli Xun said, "Village head, don't just thank us. Find four people to carry those two boxes of silver to the carriage at the village entrance. Nanny Wei is returning to the city today."

"Don't worry, old Proprietor. I'll handle it properly." The village head immediately found four villagers to carry the two boxes to the village entrance. Beside the carriage at the village entrance stood the maids and servants. Two servants took the boxes of silver and placed them securely in the carriage.

After handling the bamboo forest matter, there was no need for Nanny Wei to stay any longer. Qianli Xun saw her off to the village entrance.

Nanny Wei instructed, "I've truly seen how capable you are, Young Miss. I can rest assured knowing you're staying in the bamboo forest. Young Master Chen probably won't trouble you again. But I must advise you, Young Miss—stay out of other people's business from now on. No matter how capable you are, you can't help everyone in the world who needs help. You need to take care of yourself first."

"You're right, Nanny. I'll be more careful from now on."

Nanny Wei looked at her young mistress with great affection. "You've changed from before, Young Miss, but your kind nature hasn't changed. That's wonderful."

After seeing Nanny Wei off, Qianli Xun returned to the bamboo house. During her time in the bamboo forest, she hadn't yet felt any impact on her fate, but her heart had become increasingly peaceful. The quieter she grew, the more her imagination stirred. She planned to devote herself to writing vernacular stories in the days to come.
