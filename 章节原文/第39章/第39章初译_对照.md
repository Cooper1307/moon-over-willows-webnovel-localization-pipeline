# 第39章 高手对决立新约

村长家的竹屋应该是全村最好的，又大又结实，院子也宽敞。

The village head's bamboo house was probably the best in the entire village—large, sturdy, with a spacious courtyard.

---

院子里搭着竹棚，下面摆着桌椅。陈浩宇带人早早过来，坐在竹棚下等待。村长已将茶泡好，站在一旁陪着笑脸。

A bamboo canopy had been set up in the courtyard, with tables and chairs underneath. Chen Haoyu had arrived early with his men, sitting under the canopy waiting. The village head had already brewed tea and stood nearby with an obsequious smile.

---

不多时，千里寻带着两个丫鬟和韦嬷嬷前来。远远瞧见陈浩宇的身边还是昨天的五人，她又多看了高手几眼，心想，陈浩宇定是怕被她打，才找来一位武功高强之人。

Before long, Qianli Xun arrived with her two maids and Nanny Wei. From a distance, she saw that Chen Haoyu still had the same five people from yesterday. She glanced a few more times at the martial arts expert, thinking, *Chen Haoyu must be afraid of being beaten by me, so he brought in someone with high martial arts skills.*

---

村长迎上去："东家和韦嬷嬷这边请！"

The village head stepped forward to greet them. "Proprietor and Nanny Wei, please this way!"

---

千里寻和韦嬷嬷挑桌椅坐下，两个丫鬟站在主人身旁。

Qianli Xun and Nanny Wei chose seats at a table, while the two maids stood beside their mistress.

---

陈浩宇瞧着夏果说："本公子看上你是你的福气，跟着她有什么好？你这容貌给她当丫鬟实在可惜。"

Chen Haoyu stared at Xiaguo and said, "It's your good fortune that I've taken a liking to you. What's so good about following her? With your looks, it's a waste to serve her as a maid."

---

夏果说："我若跟了陈公子只有被卖的份儿！……"

Xiaguo said, "If I followed Young Master Chen, I'd only end up being sold!..."

---

韦嬷嬷厉声斥责："夏果！哪有你说话的份儿？赶紧把嘴闭上！"

Nanny Wei sharply rebuked, "Xiaguo! When did it become your place to speak? Shut your mouth immediately!"

---

夏果闭紧嘴巴，不敢再言语。

Xiaguo clamped her mouth shut and dared not say another word.

---

千里寻瞧着陈浩宇色眯眯的样子，甚是不满："陈浩宇，我让你把相关人请齐全了，怎么只有你来？"

Qianli Xun looked at Chen Haoyu's lecherous expression with great displeasure. "Chen Haoyu, I told you to gather all the relevant people. Why are you the only one here?"

---

陈浩宇将目光从夏果身上移开："难道我身后这几位不是人吗？"

Chen Haoyu shifted his gaze away from Xiaguo. "Are the people behind me not human?"

---

千里寻强调道："我是说与竹林相关之人。"

Qianli Xun emphasized, "I meant people related to the bamboo forest."

---

陈浩宇说："难道还要把你爹和我爹请来？大可不必，田根在我手中，我拥有竹林所有权；你爹把竹林当嫁妆给你，你拥有竹林的使用权和收租权，只要你我前来，人就算到齐。"

Chen Haoyu said, "Do we need to invite your father and my father? That's completely unnecessary. The land root is in my hands, so I own the bamboo forest. Your father gave the bamboo forest as dowry to you, so you have the right to use it and collect rent. As long as you and I are here, everyone is present."

---

千里寻虽讨厌陈浩宇，可也承认他说得没错，便不再与他计较。

Although Qianli Xun despised Chen Haoyu, she had to admit he was right, so she stopped arguing with him.

---

韦嬷嬷耐心听完陈浩宇所说，忍不住说道："陈公子，我有一事不明，还请解惑。"

Nanny Wei listened patiently to Chen Haoyu's words, then couldn't help but say, "Young Master Chen, there's something I don't understand. Please enlighten me."

---

"韦嬷嬷请问，本公子知无不言。"

"Please ask, Nanny Wei. I'll answer without reservation."

---

"朱家虽没陈家有钱，但也不差，我家姑娘嫁人时，所有嫁妆里最差的就是这片竹林，实在想不通陈公子为何偏偏看上竹林？"

"Although the Zhu family isn't as wealthy as the Chen family, they're not poor either. When our young mistress married, the worst item in her dowry was this bamboo forest. I really can't figure out why Young Master Chen specifically wants the bamboo forest?"

---

陈浩宇笑道："只因某人口出狂言，说竹林她的地盘，本公子想教训教训她，让她明白，只要本公子想，她的地盘可变成本公子的。"

Chen Haoyu laughed, "It's just because someone talked big, saying the bamboo forest was her territory. I want to teach her a lesson and make her understand that as long as I want it, her territory can become mine."

---

千里寻正想发作，被韦嬷嬷用眼神阻止，只好忍了，用心听韦嬷嬷如何对付陈浩宇。

Qianli Xun was about to explode, but Nanny Wei stopped her with a look. She had to suppress her anger and carefully listen to how Nanny Wei would deal with Chen Haoyu.

---

"原来陈公子是为一时之气。我家姑娘可没那么心胸狭窄，她看在两家是亲家，又把陈公子的姐姐叫一声嫂嫂的份上，不与你计较，只希望陈公子按照契约要求，归还原价，再把一年的租子替竹民们给了，我家姑娘定然不会说一个不字。"

"So Young Master Chen is acting on impulse. Our young mistress isn't that narrow-minded. Considering the two families are related by marriage, and she calls your sister sister-in-law, she won't hold a grudge. She only hopes that Young Master Chen will follow the contract, return the original price, and pay a year's rent on behalf of the bamboo people. Our young mistress definitely won't say no."

---

如此顺利，陈浩宇根本高兴不起来，既被人说成心胸狭窄，又没达成目的，他心有不甘。请了一位高手前来撑场面，总得跟朱淑真过两招，挽回两次失败的颜面；再说夏果那丫头还没得手，岂能轻易了结此事？

It was going too smoothly. Chen Haoyu couldn't be happy at all. He'd been called narrow-minded and hadn't achieved his goal. He was unwilling to accept this. He'd invited an expert to back him up, so he at least needed to spar with Zhu Shuzhen to salvage his face after two failures. Besides, he hadn't gotten that maid Xiaguo yet—how could he let this matter rest so easily?

---

"本公子在钱上从不含糊，两箱银子摆在旁边，共一千两，竹林的原价加竹民的租子，尽够，还富余了呢！……"

"I'm never vague when it comes to money. Two boxes of silver are right over there, totaling one thousand taels. The original price of the bamboo forest plus the bamboo people's rent is more than enough, with plenty left over!..."

---

陈浩宇还没说完，村长立刻行礼，奉承道："陈公子，我替全村竹民谢过您，您就是活菩萨在世，改日我们全村人给您做个匾额，上写'扶危济困'四个字，如何？"

Before Chen Haoyu finished speaking, the village head immediately bowed and flattered, "Young Master Chen, I thank you on behalf of all the bamboo people in the village. You're a living bodhisattva! Someday, we'll make a wooden plaque for you with the words 'Aiding the imperiled and assisting the destitute.' How does that sound?"

---

陈浩宇"呵呵"笑道："写什么你们定，不过这会儿，村长先靠边站，我跟朱淑真有事商量。"

Chen Haoyu chuckled, "You can decide what to write. But for now, village head, step aside. I have matters to discuss with Zhu Shuzhen."

---

村长满脸堆笑，恭敬地站到一边。

The village head, his face full of obsequious smiles, respectfully stepped aside.

---

陈浩宇瞅了一眼夏果，对朱淑真说："朱淑真，我把竹林买回来的同时，你必须把那丫头给我。"

Chen Haoyu glanced at Xiaguo and said to Zhu Shuzhen, "Zhu Shuzhen, at the same time I buy back the bamboo forest, you must give me that maid."

---

千里寻忍住怒火："你妄想！竹林可以给你，我的丫鬟，别想碰一根毫毛！"

Qianli Xun suppressed her anger. "Dream on! You can have the bamboo forest, but don't even think about touching a single hair on my maid's head!"

---

"我今日偏要她，你能把我怎样？"

"Today I specifically want her. What can you do about it?"

---

"我今日偏不给你，你又能把我怎样？"

"Today I specifically won't give her to you. What can you do about it?"

---

陈浩宇大笑："哈哈哈……！你不是很厉害吗？有本事跟我身边的高手打一架，如果你打得赢他，我就放过那丫头，如果你打不赢，还是乖乖连那丫头一起给我。"

Chen Haoyu laughed loudly, "Hahaha! Aren't you so great? If you're so capable, fight the expert beside me. If you win, I'll let that maid go. If you lose, obediently give me the maid along with everything else."

---

千里寻又朝高手瞧去，他身体健壮，肌肉发达，一脸横肉，绝不是善茬，一旦动起手来，是个要命的家伙。可千里寻不甘示弱，她认为只有接受挑战才是此时最正确的选择。

Qianli Xun glanced at the expert again. He was robust, well-muscled, with a face full of fierce flesh. He was definitely not someone to mess with—once he started fighting, he'd be a deadly opponent. But Qianli Xun wasn't willing to show weakness. She believed accepting the challenge was the most correct choice at this moment.

---

"既如此，我就与你身边的高手切磋切磋，要是我赢了，必须答应三个要求。"

"In that case, I'll spar with the expert beside you. If I win, you must agree to three demands."

---

"说吧，本公子听听，再决定要不要答应你。"

"Speak. I'll listen and then decide whether to agree."

---

"如若我赢了，第一，不准向竹民收租；第二，不准再打夏果的主意；第三，以后见我要问好行礼，然后赶紧从我面前滚开。你看如何？"

"If I win, first, you won't collect rent from the bamboo people; second, you won't have any more ideas about Xiaguo; third, from now on, when you see me, you'll greet me politely and then quickly get out of my sight. How does that sound?"

---

第一个要求，陈浩宇并未当回事，竹林挣不了几个钱，收不收租无所谓；第二个，他已说过，朱淑真要是赢了，就放了那丫头，故而也没多大问题；第三个将他气着，这不是侮辱人吗？他陈浩宇自生下来，从未受过如此大的羞辱。

For the first demand, Chen Haoyu didn't take it seriously—the bamboo forest didn't earn much money, so whether he collected rent or not didn't matter. For the second, he'd already said that if Zhu Shuzhen won, he'd let the maid go, so that wasn't a big problem either. The third demand angered him—wasn't this insulting him? Since he was born, Chen Haoyu had never suffered such great humiliation.

---

本想发作，瞧到身旁的高手，再瞧瞧朱淑真，柔柔弱弱，一旦打起来，必输无疑，立刻转怒为喜："好！本公子答应你！"

He was about to explode, but when he glanced at the expert beside him and then looked at Zhu Shuzhen—frail and weak, she'd definitely lose once they fought—he immediately turned his anger into joy. "Fine! I agree!"

---

高手走出竹棚，来到宽敞处，使出内力，将上衣震碎，纷纷落在地上，身上的肌肉跳动着，让人看了都害怕。

The expert walked out of the bamboo canopy and came to a spacious area. Using his inner qi, he shattered his upper garment, and the pieces fell to the ground. His muscles rippled, intimidating everyone who saw them.

---

苦荞心想，小姐怎么可能打得过他？夏果也担忧不已，怕小姐因她被伤到。

Ku Qiao thought, *How could the Young Miss possibly defeat him?* Xiaguo was also extremely worried, afraid the Young Miss would get hurt because of her.

---

韦嬷嬷凑近，小声说："姑娘，不如把夏果给了陈公子？据我了解，陈公子玩弄过的女子，有的放在身边当丫鬟，有的给些银子打发回家，至于卖到妓院，没人见过，估计是谣传而已。像夏果这等容貌，应该会留在身边当丫鬟，姑娘犯不着为她以身涉险。"

Nanny Wei leaned close and whispered, "Young Miss, why not just give Xiaguo to Young Master Chen? From what I understand, the women Young Master Chen has played with—some were kept as maids, some were given silver and sent home. As for being sold to brothels, no one has seen it, so it's probably just rumors. With Xiaguo's looks, she'd likely be kept as a maid. You don't need to put yourself in danger for her."

---

千里寻也小声说："嬷嬷不必担心，有些人看着厉害，实则不然，再说我不光为夏果，陈浩宇吃下竹林，得让他吐些东西出来，既为我们争口气，也惠及竹民，何乐而不为？"

Qianli Xun also whispered, "Don't worry, Nanny. Some people look fierce but aren't actually that strong. Besides, I'm not just doing this for Xiaguo. Chen Haoyu swallowed the bamboo forest, so I need to make him spit something out. It'll fight for our dignity and benefit the bamboo people. Why not do it?"

---

说完，千里寻站起来，走出竹棚，来到高手面前。两人站在一起，外形对比更加明显。

With that, Qianli Xun stood up, walked out of the bamboo canopy, and came before the expert. Standing together, the contrast in their appearances was even more obvious.

---

陈浩宇冷笑道："朱淑真，现在认输还来得及。"

Chen Haoyu sneered, "Zhu Shuzhen, it's not too late to admit defeat now."

---

千里寻并未理会，当她还未想到如何向对方出拳时，高手以强劲的力量和凶猛的攻势进攻而来，他的拳脚如狂风暴雨般袭来，每一拳每一脚都蕴含着毁灭性的力量。千里寻灵活地躲避着，运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚，努力化解对方的攻势。

Qianli Xun didn't pay him any mind. Before she could even think about how to throw a punch, the expert attacked with powerful force and fierce aggression. His fists and feet came like a storm, each punch and kick containing destructive power. 千里寻灵活地躲避着，运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚，努力化解对方的攻势。

Qianli Xun dodged nimbly, using her exquisite martial arts skills to overcome hardness with softness, trying to deflect his attacks.

---

高手眼中闪过一丝惊讶，这个看似弱不禁风的女子，竟然有着如此高超的拳法技巧，他暗自警惕，全力以赴应战。

A flash of surprise crossed the expert's eyes. This seemingly frail woman actually had such superb martial arts skills. He became vigilant and went all out to fight.

---

尽管千里寻努力躲闪并还击，高手的力量如同山岳一般，每一次对抗让她感到压力和疲惫。看来单凭技巧根本无法战胜身强力壮的对手。

Despite Qianli Xun's efforts to dodge and counterattack, the expert's strength was like a mountain. Each confrontation made her feel pressured and exhausted. It seemed that relying solely on technique was not enough to defeat such a strong and powerful opponent.

---

韦嬷嬷等人心提到嗓子眼儿，额头上冒着汗珠。

Nanny Wei and the others had their hearts in their mouths, sweat beading on their foreheads.

---

陈浩宇见朱淑真处于弱势，当然高兴，心想："本公子武功不济，天下比你厉害的人多的是，还收拾不了你？"

Seeing Zhu Shuzhen at a disadvantage, Chen Haoyu was naturally pleased. He thought, *My martial arts aren't great, but there are plenty of people stronger than you in the world. Can't I even handle you?*

---

千里寻没有放弃，迅速调整战术，利用高手攻势的间隙，以灵活的步伐和精准的拳法反击，将高手的力量化为他的弱点，利用高手重心不稳，一次一次打在他的要害，高手逐渐力不从心。

Qianli Xun didn't give up. She quickly adjusted her tactics, using the gaps in the expert's attacks to counter with flexible footwork and precise punches. She turned the expert's strength into his weakness, exploiting his unstable center of gravity, hitting his vital points again and again. The expert gradually became overwhelmed.

---

韦嬷嬷等人终于松口气。

Nanny Wei and the others finally breathed a sigh of relief.

---

陈浩宇紧张起来，没想到朱淑真柔弱的外表下藏着让人意想不到的力量。

Chen Haoyu became nervous. He never expected that beneath Zhu Shuzhen's frail exterior lay such unexpected strength.

---

村长则在心中祈祷："保佑东家赢，租子全免，全村过好日子。"

The village head prayed in his heart, *Please let the Proprietor win, so all rent is exempted and the whole village can live well.*

---

高手被打得倒退几步，千里寻趁机使出一拳，击中高手的腹部，高手倒在地上。千里寻收了拳头，喘着粗气，脸上露出胜利的喜悦。

The expert was beaten back several steps. Qianli Xun seized the opportunity and threw a punch, hitting the expert in the abdomen. The expert fell to the ground. Qianli Xun withdrew her fist, panting heavily, with a look of triumphant joy on her face.

---

那高手努力爬起来，跪在地上："师傅在上，受徒弟一拜！"

The expert struggled to get up and knelt on the ground. "Master, please accept a bow from your disciple!"

---

千里寻在另一个时代有很多男徒弟，没有一个像他这般长相粗鲁的，此人她根本瞧不上："起来吧，我可不想收你做徒弟。"

Qianli Xun had many male disciples in another era, but none looked as rough as him. She had no interest in this person at all. "Get up. I don't want to take you as a disciple."

---

高手站起来："你不愿收我为徒，我还认你当师傅，我叫陆远峰，后会有期！"陆远峰说完，忍痛离开。

The expert stood up. "Even if you're unwilling to take me as a disciple, I still recognize you as my master. My name is Lu Yuanfeng. We'll meet again!" With that, Lu Yuanfeng left, enduring his pain.

---

陈浩宇直接傻眼，自己找的高手不仅输了，还认朱淑真当师傅，他长这么大，从未如此丢脸过。

Chen Haoyu was completely dumbfounded. Not only had the expert he brought lost, but he'd also recognized Zhu Shuzhen as his master. In all his life, Chen Haoyu had never been so humiliated.

---

千里寻说："村长，拿纸笔来，我要当场写下新契约，请陈公子与我共同按下指印。"

Qianli Xun said, "Village head, bring paper and brush. I'm going to write a new contract right here, and have Young Master Chen and I both press our thumbprints."

---

村长兴高采烈把纸笔和印朱摆上，千里寻上前写完契约，签了名，放下笔，按下指印，面向陈浩宇："该你了，三个条件，一个都不许反悔。"

The village head cheerfully set out the paper, brush, and red ink paste. Qianli Xun stepped forward, finished writing the contract, signed her name, put down the brush, and pressed her thumbprint. She turned to Chen Haoyu. "Your turn. Three conditions, no backing out on any of them."

---

陈浩宇悻悻地站起来，走过去，拿起笔签了名，也按下指印："朱淑真，别以为你每次都这么幸运，有一天栽到我手里，定让你吃不了兜着走。"说完，拿了契约，带着地痞流氓就要离开。

Chen Haoyu stood up sullenly, walked over, picked up the brush, signed his name, and also pressed his thumbprint. "Zhu Shuzhen, don't think you'll be this lucky every time. One day you'll fall into my hands, and I'll make you pay dearly." With that, he took the contract and was about to leave with his hooligans.

---

村长跑上前拦住，行礼问道："陈公子，以后竹林的租子真的全免了？"

The village head ran forward to stop him, bowed and asked, "Young Master Chen, is the rent for the bamboo forest really completely exempted from now on?"

---

陈浩宇没好气地说："契约上都写着呢，本公子能赖你？还不赶紧滚开！"

Chen Haoyu said irritably, "It's all written in the contract. Do you think I'd renege on it? Now hurry up and get out of the way!"

---

村长兴奋地说："好好，我滚，陈公子慢走！"然后对韦嬷嬷和老东家又是一番千恩万谢。

The village head said excitedly, "Good, good, I'll get out of the way. Young Master Chen, take care!" Then he thanked Nanny Wei and the old Proprietor profusely.

---

千里寻说："村长不要光谢我们，找四个人把那两箱银子搬到村口的马车上，韦嬷嬷今日要回城里。"

Qianli Xun said, "Village head, don't just thank us. Find four people to carry those two boxes of silver to the carriage at the village entrance. Nanny Wei is returning to the city today."

---

"老东家放心，我定办妥。"村长立刻找来四个村民，抬起两个箱子往村口去。村口的马车旁站着丫鬟和小厮，两个小厮接了放银两的箱子在马车里搁好。

"Don't worry, old Proprietor. I'll handle it properly." The village head immediately found four villagers to carry the two boxes to the village entrance. Beside the carriage at the village entrance stood the maids and servants. Two servants took the boxes of silver and placed them securely in the carriage.

---

处理完竹林之事，韦嬷嬷没有必要再待下去，千里寻将她送到村口。

After handling the bamboo forest matter, there was no need for Nanny Wei to stay any longer. Qianli Xun saw her off to the village entrance.

---

韦嬷嬷叮嘱道："姑娘的厉害我算是见识到，你在竹林待着我也放心，陈家公子应该不会再找你麻烦。不过我必须嘱咐姑娘，往后少管闲事，你再厉害，也帮不了天下所有需要帮助的人，要先把自己照顾好。"

Nanny Wei instructed, "I've truly seen how capable you are, Young Miss. I can rest assured knowing you're staying in the bamboo forest. Young Master Chen probably won't trouble you again. But I must advise you, Young Miss—stay out of other people's business from now on. No matter how capable you are, you can't help everyone in the world who needs help. You need to take care of yourself first."

---

"嬷嬷说的是，我以后会注意。"

"You're right, Nanny. I'll be more careful from now on."

---

韦嬷嬷充满怜爱地看着自家姑娘："姑娘变得跟以前不同，善良的品质仍旧没变，真好。"

Nanny Wei looked at her young mistress with great affection. "You've changed from before, Young Miss, but your kind nature hasn't changed. That's wonderful."

---

千里寻送走韦嬷嬷，继续回到竹屋。在竹林待的这段时日，还未感受到对命运有何影响，内心却越来越安静，越是安静越能激发文思，她打算接下来的时日潜心写话本。

After seeing Nanny Wei off, Qianli Xun returned to the bamboo house. During her time in the bamboo forest, she hadn't yet felt any impact on her fate, but her heart had become increasingly peaceful. The more peaceful she was, the more it stimulated her literary thoughts. She planned to devote herself to writing vernacular stories in the days to come.
