第39章 高手对决立新约
## Chapter 39: A Duel of Masters and a New Contract

村长家的竹屋应该是全村最好的，又大又结实，院子也宽敞。
The village head's bamboo house stood biggest and sturdiest in the whole village, fronting a spacious yard.

院子里搭着竹棚，下面摆着桌椅。陈浩宇带人早早过来，坐在竹棚下等待。村长已将茶泡好，站在一旁陪着笑脸。
Beneath a shaded bamboo arbor set with tables and chairs, Chen Haoyu sat waiting early with his crew while the village head hovered pouring tea with a fawning grin.

不多时，千里寻带着两个丫鬟和韦嬷嬷前来。远远瞧见陈浩宇的身边还是昨天的五人，她又多看了高手几眼，心想，陈浩宇定是怕被她打，才找来一位武功高强之人。
Soon Qianli Xun arrived with Matron Wei and both maids. Spotting the same five men beside Chen Haoyu from afar, she studied the brawny fighter again—clearly brought as muscle against her fists.

村长迎上去：“东家和韦嬷嬷这边请！”
The village head bustled forward: "Right this way, Proprietor and Matron Wei!"

千里寻和韦嬷嬷挑桌椅坐下，两个丫鬟站在主人身旁。
Qianli Xun and Matron Wei took seats across from Chen Haoyu while Kuqiao and Xiaguo stood behind their mistress.

陈浩宇瞧着夏果说：“本公子看上你是你的福气，跟着她有什么好？你这容貌给她当丫鬟实在可惜。”
Leering straight at Xiaguo, Chen Haoyu purred: "Taking a fancy to you is pure luck—what future is there trailing after her? Criminal waste of that pretty face working as a maid!"

夏果说：“我若跟了陈公子只有被卖的份儿！……”
Xiaguo flared back: "Go with Young Master Chen just to end up sold off?!..."

韦嬷嬷厉声斥责：“夏果！哪有你说话的份儿？赶紧把嘴闭上！”
Matron Wei cut her off sharp: "Xiaguo! Who gave you leave to speak? Shut your mouth!"

﻿夏果闭紧嘴巴，不敢再言语。
Xiaguo clamped her lips shut at once.

千里寻瞧着陈浩宇色眯眯的样子，甚是不满：“陈浩宇，我让你把相关人请齐全了，怎么只有你来？”
Disgusted by Chen Haoyu's sleazy stare, Qianli Xun barked: "Told you to bring every relevant party, Chen Haoyu—why only you?"

陈浩宇将目光从夏果身上移开：“难道我身后这几位不是人吗？”
Dragging his eyes off Xiaguo, Chen Haoyu smirked: "What, these five behind me don't count as people?"

千里寻强调道：“我是说与竹林相关之人。”
"I meant people legally tied to this grove!"

陈浩宇说：“难道还要把你爹和我爹请来？大可不必，田根在我手中，我拥有竹林所有权；你爹把竹林当嫁妆给你，你拥有竹林的使用权和收租权，只要你我前来，人就算到齐。”
"Expected me to drag your dad and my dad out here? Waste of time! I hold the land root—meaning ownership; your father gave you the grove as dowry—meaning usage and rent rights. With you and me sitting here, everybody who counts is present!"

千里寻虽讨厌陈浩宇，可也承认他说得没错，便不再与他计较。
Loathe him as she did, he had a point; Qianli Xun let it drop.

韦嬷嬷耐心听完陈浩宇所说，忍不住说道：“陈公子，我有一事不明，还请解惑。”
Listening quietly till now, Matron Wei spoke up: "One question puzzles me, Young Master Chen—care to enlighten an old woman?"

“韦嬷嬷请问，本公子知无不言。”
"Ask away, Matron Wei—happy to oblige."

“朱家虽没陈家有钱，但也不差，我家姑娘嫁人时，所有嫁妆里最差的就是这片竹林，实在想不通陈公子为何偏偏看上竹林？”
"The Zhu house isn't as wealthy as the Chens, yet hardly poor. Out of every estate in our young lady's dowry, this bamboo grove yields the least—why on earth set your sights on this patch?"

陈浩宇笑道：“只因某人口出狂言，说竹林她的地盘，本公子想教训教训她，让她明白，只要本公子想，她的地盘可变成本公子的。”
Chen Haoyu grinned smug: "Only because somebody ran her mouth boasting this grove was 'her turf'! Figured I'd teach her that anytime I snap my fingers, her turf turns into MY turf!"

千里寻正想发作，被韦嬷嬷用眼神阻止，只好忍了，用心听韦嬷嬷如何对付陈浩宇。
Catching Matron Wei's warning glance, Qianli Xun swallowed her comeback and let the veteran handle him.

“原来陈公子是为一时之气。我家姑娘可没那么心胸狭窄，她看在两家是亲家，又把陈公子的姐姐叫一声嫂嫂的份上，不与你计较，只希望陈公子按照契约要求，归还原价，再把一年的租子替竹民们给了，我家姑娘定然不会说一个不字。”
"Ah—just over wounded pride! Our young lady isn't nearly so petty. Out of regard for our families being in-laws—and calling your sister Sister-in-law—she won't hold it against you! Simply pay back the original purchase price per the contract and cover this year's village rent, and our young lady hands over the grove without a murmur."

如此顺利，陈浩宇根本高兴不起来，既被人说成心胸狭窄，又没达成目的，他心有不甘。请了一位高手前来撑场面，总得跟朱淑真过两招，挽回两次失败的颜面；再说夏果那丫头还没得手，岂能轻易了结此事？
Going this smooth left Chen Haoyu irritated—called petty to his face AND cheated out of revenge! He had hired a martial ace just to settle scores for two humiliating beatings—plus he still wanted Xiaguo! No way was this ending quiet!

“本公子在钱上从不含糊，两箱银子摆在旁边，共一千两，竹林的原价加竹民的租子，尽够，还富余了呢！……”
"Never stingy with coin! Two chests sitting right there—one thousand taels of silver! Covers original purchase price plus village rent with change to spare!..."

陈浩宇还没说完，村长立刻行礼，奉承道：“陈公子，我替全村竹民谢过您，您就是活菩萨在世，改日我们全村人给您做个匾额，上写‘扶危济困’四个字，如何？”
Before he finished, the village head dropped into a bowing scrape: "On behalf of every bamboo villager, thank you, Young Master Chen! A living Bodhisattva! Soon we'll carve you an inscribed board reading 'Aiding the Imperiled and Assisting the Destitute'—how's that?!"

陈浩宇“呵呵”笑道：“写什么你们定，不过这会儿，村长先靠边站，我跟朱淑真有事商量。”
Chen Haoyu chuckled: "Carve whatever you please—for now step aside, got business with Zhu Shuzhen!"

村长满脸堆笑，恭敬地站到一边。
Grinning obsequious, the village head backed off.

陈浩宇瞅了一眼夏果，对朱淑真说：“朱淑真，我把竹林买回来的同时，你必须把那丫头给我。”
Cutting his eyes back to Xiaguo, Chen Haoyu told Zhu Shuzhen: "I buy back the grove—AND you hand over that maid!"

千里寻忍住怒火：“你妄想！竹林可以给你，我的丫鬟，别想碰一根毫毛！”
Holding down her fury, Qianli Xun snapped: "Keep dreaming! Take the grove—touch one hair on my maid and see what happens!"

“我今日偏要她，你能把我怎样？”
"What if I insist on taking her today? What are you gonna do about it?!"

“我今日偏不给你，你又能把我怎样？”
"And what if I refuse to give her up? What are YOU gonna do about it?!"

陈浩宇大笑：“哈哈哈……！你不是很厉害吗？有本事跟我身边的高手打一架，如果你打得赢他，我就放过那丫头，如果你打不赢，还是乖乖连那丫头一起给我。”
Chen Haoyu roared with laughter: "Hahaha! Think you're tough, right? Got the guts to spar my master fighter here? Beat him, and I leave the maid alone! Lose, and you hand her right over along with the grove!"

千里寻又朝高手瞧去，他身体健壮，肌肉发达，一脸横肉，绝不是善茬，一旦动起手来，是个要命的家伙。可千里寻不甘示弱，她认为只有接受挑战才是此时最正确的选择。
Sizing up the scarred, muscle-bound giant with mean eyes—clearly a deadly brawler—Qianli Xun refused to blink. Backing down wasn't an option.

“既如此，我就与你身边的高手切磋切磋，要是我赢了，必须答应三个要求。”
"Done! I'll spar your fighter—and when I win, you accept three terms!"

“说吧，本公子听听，再决定要不要答应你。”
"Let's hear 'em before I agree!"

“如若我赢了，第一，不准向竹民收租；第二，不准再打夏果的主意；第三，以后见我要问好行礼，然后赶紧从我面前滚开。你看如何？”
"When I win: first, never collect rent from these bamboo villagers again! Second, drop every scheme on Xiaguo! Third, every time you see me from now on, bow polite, say hello, then scram out of my sight! Deal?"

第一个要求，陈浩宇并未当回事，竹林挣不了几个钱，收不收租无所谓；第二个，他已说过，朱淑真要是赢了，就放了那丫头，故而也没多大问题；第三个将他气着，这不是侮辱人吗？他陈浩宇自生下来，从未受过如此大的羞辱。
Term one meant peanuts—this grove made pocket change anyway. Term two he had already offered. Term three boiled his blood—total public humiliation! Never in his gilded life had anyone insulted him like that!

本想发作，瞧到身旁的高手，再瞧瞧朱淑真，柔柔弱弱，一旦打起来，必输无疑，立刻转怒为喜：“好！本公子答应你！”
About to explode, he glanced at his massive hired bruiser, then at slender Zhu Shuzhen—one punch would fold her flat! Fury flipped straight into a vicious grin: "Deal! You're on!"

高手走出竹棚，来到宽敞处，使出内力，将上衣震碎，纷纷落在地上，身上的肌肉跳动着，让人看了都害怕。
Stepping out of the arbor into open dirt, the bruiser flexed his inner force—rip!—shredding his shirt to scraps as knotted muscles twitched like coiled iron, sending shivers through everyone watching.

苦荞心想，小姐怎么可能打得过他？夏果也担忧不已，怕小姐因她被伤到。
No way Miss beats a monster like that! Kuqiao panicked while Xiaguo trembled in dread lest Miss get hurt on her account.

韦嬷嬷凑近，小声说：“姑娘，不如把夏果给了陈公子？据我了解，陈公子玩弄过的女子，有的放在身边当丫鬟，有的给些银子打发回家，至于卖到妓院，没人见过，估计是谣传而已。像夏果这等容貌，应该会留在身边当丫鬟，姑娘犯不着为她以身涉险。”
Leaning close, Matron Wei whispered: "Young lady, maybe let him have Xiaguo? From what I know, girls Chen Haoyu fancies either stay in his household as maids or get sent home with silver—brothel rumors are likely talk. Pretty as Xiaguo is, he'd keep her around as a maid; no sense risking injury over her!"

千里寻也小声说：“嬷嬷不必担心，有些人看着厉害，实则不然，再说我不光为夏果，陈浩宇吃下竹林，得让他吐些东西出来，既为我们争口气，也惠及竹民，何乐而不为？”
Whispering back, Qianli Xun winked: "Relax, Matron—flashy muscles don't always win fights! Besides, it isn't just for Xiaguo—Chen Haoyu swallowed our grove, time to make him spit something back out for our pride and the villagers' sake!"

说完，千里寻站起来，走出竹棚，来到高手面前。两人站在一起，外形对比更加明显。
Rising, she stepped out past the arbor facing the giant—the contrast between slender lady and hulking brute couldn't look starker.

陈浩宇冷笑道：“朱淑真，现在认输还来得及。”
Chen Haoyu jeered: "Still time to forfeit, Zhu Shuzhen!"

千里寻并未理会，当她还未想到如何向对方出拳时，高手以强劲的力量和凶猛的攻势进攻而来，他的拳脚如狂风暴雨般袭来，每一拳每一脚都蕴含着毁灭性的力量。千里寻灵活地躲避着，运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚，努力化解对方的攻势。
Ignoring him, before Qianli Xun even threw a probing jab, the bruiser surged forward like an avalanche—fierce punches and sweeping kicks packing bone-crushing power! Slipping aside with crisp footwork, Qianli Xun flowed into classic Qianli Fist deflections, turning raw force aside with supple finesse.

高手眼中闪过一丝惊讶，这个看似弱不禁风的女子，竟然有着如此高超的拳法技巧，他暗自警惕，全力以赴应战。
Shock flickered across the fighter's eyes—this frail-looking woman wielded razor-sharp skill! Dropping cockiness at once, he poured full force into his attack.

尽管千里寻努力躲闪并还击，高手的力量如同山岳一般，每一次对抗让她感到压力和疲惫。看来单凭技巧根本无法战胜身强力壮的对手。
Even dodging and countering clean, colliding with mountain-heavy power taxed her arms and stamina—pure finesse alone wouldn't topple brute bulk.

韦嬷嬷等人心提到嗓子眼儿，额头上冒着汗珠。
Matron Wei, Kuqiao, and Xiaguo watched with hearts jammed in their throats, sweat glistening on their brows.

陈浩宇见朱淑真处于弱势，当然高兴，心想：“本公子武功不济，天下比你厉害的人多的是，还收拾不了你？”
Seeing Zhu Shuzhen pressed back, Chen Haoyu crowed inside: Might not fight well myself, plenty of heavyweights out there who can crush you flat!

千里寻没有放弃，迅速调整战术，利用高手攻势的间隙，以灵活的步伐和精准的拳法反击，将高手的力量化为他的弱点，利用高手重心不稳，一次一次打在他的要害，高手逐渐力不从心。
Refusing to fold, Qianli Xun switched gears fast—reading gaps between his heavy swings, darting inside his reach with lightning footwork and razor-accurate counters that turned his own momentum against him, throwing his balance off and hammering exposed pressure points until the giant began to flag!

韦嬷嬷等人终于松口气。
Exhales of relief escaped Matron Wei's group.

陈浩宇紧张起来，没想到朱淑真柔弱的外表下藏着让人意想不到的力量。
Panic crept into Chen Haoyu's eyes—who knew fragile porcelain hid iron springs underneath?!

村长则在心中祈祷：“保佑东家赢，租子全免，全村过好日子。”
Meanwhile the village head prayed silent: Heaven let Proprietor win—rent-free forever, happy days ahead!

高手被打得倒退几步，千里寻趁机使出一拳，击中高手的腹部，高手倒在地上。千里寻收了拳头，喘着粗气，脸上露出胜利的喜悦。
Staggering back two steps off balance, the giant left his gut wide open—Qianli Xun shot in and drove a crisp palm-backed punch straight into his solar plexus, dropping him flat in the dirt! Drawing back her fists, breathing hard, a triumphant grin lit her face.

那高手努力爬起来，跪在地上：“师傅在上，受徒弟一拜！”
Struggling up off the dust, the bruiser dropped straight onto both knees before her: "Master above—accept your disciple's bow!"

千里寻在另一个时代有很多男徒弟，没有一个像他这般长相粗鲁的，此人她根本瞧不上：“起来吧，我可不想收你做徒弟。”
Having trained plenty of male students in her old world—none looking this rough around the edges—Qianli Xun waved him off: "Get up! Not taking apprentices!"

高手站起来：“你不愿收我为徒，我还认你当师傅，我叫陆远峰，后会有期！”陆远峰说完，忍痛离开。
Climbing to his feet, the fighter declared firm: "Refuse me or not, I still call you Master! My name is Lu Yuanfeng—till we meet again!" Nursing his aching ribs, Lu Yuanfeng limped away.

陈浩宇直接傻眼，自己找的高手不仅输了，还认朱淑真当师傅，他长这么大，从未如此丢脸过。
Chen Haoyu stood frozen like a wooden post—not only had his ace lost, he'd bowed down calling Zhu Shuzhen Master! Biggest humiliation of Chen Haoyu's entire life!

千里寻说：“村长，拿纸笔来，我要当场写下新契约，请陈公子与我共同按下指印。”
"Village Head! Brush, ink, and paper!" Qianli Xun called brisk. "Drawing up a new contract right here—Young Master Chen and I sign and thumbprint together!"

村长兴高采烈把纸笔和印朱摆上，千里寻上前写完契约，签了名，放下笔，按下指印，面向陈浩宇：“该你了，三个条件，一个都不许反悔。”
Beaming from ear to ear, the village head rushed out paper, brush, and red ink paste. Qianli Xun drafted the terms crisp, signed her name, pressed her thumbprint, and turned to Chen Haoyu: "Your turn—all three conditions, zero backing out!"

陈浩宇悻悻地站起来，走过去，拿起笔签了名，也按下指印：“朱淑真，别以为你每次都这么幸运，有一天栽到我手里，定让你吃不了兜着走。”说完，拿了契约，带着地痞流氓就要离开。
Seething yet cornered, Chen Haoyu stalked up, scrawled his name, and slammed down his thumbprint. "Don't think luck stays with you forever, Zhu Shuzhen—the day you fall into my hands, you'll beg for mercy!" Snatching his copy, he turned to leave with his thugs.

村长跑上前拦住，行礼问道：“陈公子，以后竹林的租子真的全免了？”
Scampering up to block him with a bow, the village head beamed: "Young Master Chen—village rent is really waived forever?!"

陈浩宇没好气地说：“契约上都写着呢，本公子能赖你？还不赶紧滚开！”
"Written right on the contract—think I'd stiff you?! Get out of my face!" Chen Haoyu snarled.

村长兴奋地说：“好好，我滚，陈公子慢走！”然后对韦嬷嬷和老东家又是一番千恩万谢。
"Yes, yes, getting out of your face! Safe travels, Young Master Chen!" Then he spun round showering bows and blessings upon Matron Wei and their former proprietor.

千里寻说：“村长不要光谢我们，找四个人把那两箱银子搬到村口的马车上，韦嬷嬷今日要回城里。”
"Save the speeches, Village Head," Qianli Xun directed. "Grab four men to carry those two silver chests down to the carriage at the village entrance—Matron Wei heads back to town today."

“老东家放心，我定办妥。”村长立刻找来四个村民，抬起两个箱子往村口去。村口的马车旁站着丫鬟和小厮，两个小厮接了放银两的箱子在马车里搁好。
"Done, former Proprietor!" Calling four sturdy villagers at once, he had both chests hauled out to the road where Matron Wei's page boys loaded them safe aboard her carriage.

处理完竹林之事，韦嬷嬷没有必要再待下去，千里寻将她送到村口。
With grove business wrapped clean, Matron Wei prepared to depart; Qianli Xun walked her out to the village edge.

韦嬷嬷叮嘱道：“姑娘的厉害我算是见识到，你在竹林待着我也放心，陈家公子应该不会再找你麻烦。不过我必须嘱咐姑娘，往后少管闲事，你再厉害，也帮不了天下所有需要帮助的人，要先把自己照顾好。”
Matron Wei clasped her hand with warm advice: "Witnessed your fighting prowess firsthand now—feel much safer leaving you out here, doubt Young Master Chen dares bother you again. Still, listen to an old woman: meddle less in other folks' fights! Strong as you are, you can't rescue everyone under Heaven—take care of yourself first."

“嬷嬷说的是，我以后会注意。”
"Good advice, Matron—I'll keep it in mind."

韦嬷嬷充满怜爱地看着自家姑娘：“姑娘变得跟以前不同，善良的品质仍旧没变，真好。”
Gazing at her with fond pride, Matron Wei smiled: "Changed as much as you have, your kind heart stayed pure—wonderful!"

千里寻送走韦嬷嬷，继续回到竹屋。在竹林待的这段时日，还未感受到对命运有何影响，内心却越来越安静，越是安静越能激发文思，她打算接下来的时日潜心写话本。
Seeing Matron Wei off, Qianli Xun strolled back to her bamboo cottage. Whether this bamboo grove had worked mystical luck yet or not, her mind felt calmer and clearer every single day—fueling fresh creative flow! She resolved to spend the remaining weeks pouring herself into writing huaben scripts.
