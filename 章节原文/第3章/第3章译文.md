## Chapter 3: Awakening to Abuse in a Baffling New World

Dragged along by a mysterious force, Qianli Xun dared not open her eyes. The sheer speed and terror left her no room to process a thing; her mind went blank until the force flung her out with brutal strength...

A voice reached her ears: "Miss, wake up, please wake up! Sob... Son-in-law, did you beat my Miss to death? Sob..."

Qianli Xun wondered in silence, Miss? Son-in-law? Am I dreaming? No—I, Qianli Xun, died in that earthquake. Did I end up in another world? The afterlife? Is it going to be terrifying?

Her eyes stayed shut, yet through her eyelids she sensed bright light around her. Did the afterlife have daylight too? She might as well take a look.

Straining to lift her eyelids, Qianli Xun saw a maid in period clothing kneeling beside her in tears.

The moment the maid saw her open her eyes, grief flipped to joy. "Thank goodness! Miss, you're still alive!"

Qianli Xun asked, "Am I dead? Is this the afterlife? Who are you?"

The maid insisted, "Miss, you aren't dead, you're alive! I'm your maid, Kuqiao!"

"My maid? Kuqiao?" Qianli Xun had written several transmigration web novels, and this setup felt straight out of her own chapters. "Did I transmigrate? I didn't really die?"

Her spirits lifted at the thought, and only then did she notice she was sprawled on the floor. Kuqiao helped her up. A sharp throb shot through her forehead; she touched it and found blood on her fingertips. Since childhood, nobody had ever laid a violent hand on Qianli Xun. Anger flared—until she remembered her body lying in a pool of blood during the earthquake. Coming back to life was already a gift. Most of her fury drained away.

Steady on her feet, she saw a man and a woman standing before her. The man looked to be in his twenties—average features, broad shoulders, clearly a martial artist—with irritation written all over his stare. Beside him stood a flashy woman with slicked hair and a coy posture, sizing Qianli Xun up with open disdain.

Both wore period clothes whose exact names Qianli Xun had no idea about, coming as she did from another era. She glanced around. Far removed from modern decor, sure, yet the furnishings spoke of a well-off household.

She looked down at herself—period clothes too—and felt her head: coiled hair, gold hairpins. She really had transmigrated.

Qianli Xun pulled Kuqiao aside. "You just said you're my maid, and your name is Kuqiao?"

Eyes wide, Kuqiao nodded. "Miss, did Son-in-law hit you so hard you lost your wits? Why are you asking me that?"

"Kuqiao, tell me—who am I?"

"Miss, he really did beat the sense out of you. You don't even know who you are!"

Instead of answering right away, Kuqiao marched up to the man. "Son-in-law, Master Zhu is the Prefect of Lin'an—his rank is far above yours! How dare you lay a hand on my Miss! Just wait till I go back to Zhu Manor and tell Master to punish you!"

Unfazed by the threat, the man sneered, "Once she married into the Lin family, she became a Lin. I'm disciplining my wife. A married daughter is like water poured out—even the Prefect of Lin'an has no right to meddle."

Qianli Xun grew more bewildered and uneasy by the second. Married into the Lin family? Was the body she had landed in married to that guy? Setting everything else aside, she tugged Kuqiao back. "Hurry up and tell me who I am!"

Looking at her poor mistress, Kuqiao answered, "Your name is Zhu Shuzhen, daughter of Zhu Manor."

"Zhu Shuzhen? The Song-dynasty female ci poet Zhu Shuzhen?" Qianli Xun had read plenty of Zhu Shuzhen's poems, loved them, and knew most by heart. "What year is it?"

The woman beside the man piped up in a sugary voice, "Training Officer, looks like you really knocked her silly. She doesn't know who she is or what era she's in. Let me tell her."

She sashayed over to Qianli Xun. "This is the fourth year of the Xuanhe reign of the Great Song."

Qianli Xun thought to herself, The fourth year of Xuanhe of the Great Song—it really is the Song dynasty, and I really transmigrated into the poet Zhu Shuzhen. Back in my era, almost no records survived about her—unknown birth and death dates, people couldn't even agree whether she lived in the Northern or Southern Song. Since it's the fourth year of Xuanhe, Northern Song it is...

The woman drawled on, "Miss Zhu, since you married the Training Officer, you ought to do your wifely duty. Not to lecture you, mind you, but he's a red-blooded man. Keeping a beauty like you at home where he can look but not touch—of course he loses his temper and beats you!"

"What? Beats me?"

Qianli Xun stared at the man. Kuqiao called him Son-in-law, and this woman said Miss Zhu had married the Training Officer—so he was Zhu Shuzhen's husband. From what Qianli Xun remembered reading, Zhu Shuzhen had married at twenty. Had transmigration just shaved almost ten years off her age?

A spark of delight flashed, then soured. Qianli Xun had stayed single right up to thirty, only to land in a twenty-year-old married body—her hard-kept chastity gone down the drain, and stuck with a husband who beat her to boot!

She marched straight up to the man. "You're Zhu Shuzhen's husband? What's your name? What do you do?"

The man stared back. After he had punched, kicked, and shoved his wife into a pillar until she blacked out, she had woken up looking odd and speaking with far more backbone than before. Suspicion gnawed at him, yet he had watched the whole thing happen with his own eyes—nothing strange had occurred, and her face was unmistakably Zhu Shuzhen's. Keeping up his macho front, he ignored her and turned his head away.

The woman stepped in to explain. "He's your husband, Lin Jue, the famous Training Officer in charge of drilling every soldier in Lin'an. You should count yourself lucky you married Officer Lin!"

Training Officer? Qianli Xun mulled it over. The historical notes she knew said Zhu Shuzhen had married a minor clerk, hated the match, and pined away in misery. Looking at this cocky Training Officer who treated his wife like dirt, even Qianli Xun despised him—let alone Zhu Shuzhen, one of the Twin Jewels of Ci Poetry.

Qianli Xun decided to teach Lin Jue a lesson on Zhu Shuzhen's behalf, starting with the woman beside him. "Since I married him, I'm his primary wife. Where did you crawl out from? Are you a concubine he took?"

Lin Jue and the woman gaped. Fragile, quiet Zhu Shuzhen—since when did she talk so tough and blunt?

Kuqiao rushed to explain. "Miss, she's a commercial courtesan Son-in-law brings home from Chunxiang Brothel—Chun Jinqiao. He drags her here all the time just to spite you. Today he brought her back again, and when you still ignored him, he started hitting and kicking you."

Fury surged through Qianli Xun. She loathed men who hit women, and this bastard had the nerve to haul a courtesan right into the house.

"Lin Jue, call yourself a man? Beating your wife over a brothel girl? If you've got the guts, step into the courtyard and fight me!"

Doubt churned in Lin Jue's gut. How is she nothing like the old Zhu Shuzhen?

Even if her personality had flipped after fainting, he figured she could not have picked up new skills out of thin air. She must have lost her memory and forgotten how strong he was. Better give her another beating to jog her memory and remind her of a wife's duties.

"Fine, then. Your husband will spar with you. This time I won't knock you cold—just teach you a little lesson."

They moved out to the courtyard. Servants of Lin Manor soon gathered around, curious. Usually their master shut the doors before beating his wife; why had the fight spilled outside?

Lin Jue scoffed, "Zhu Shuzhen, I'll go easy on you. You strike first."

Qianli Xun clenched her fists. "You offered Zhu Shuzhen the first move—don't blame me for not holding back!" With that, she threw a punch.

Lin Jue brushed it off. Delicate as Zhu Shuzhen was, what force could her punch hold? One light shove would drop her.

He raised a palm to brush her fist aside. The instant skin met skin, crushing force slammed into his hand, jolting his heart. How did she get this strong out of nowhere?

Before he could recover, Qianli Xun's other fist smashed into his eye, blooming into an instant bruise. A swift kick swept him off his feet. She pinned his torso under one knee and locked both his arms behind him. "With skills this pathetic, you dare call yourself a Training Officer? Kuqiao!"

Startled yet thrilled to see her mistress turn fierce—no more getting bullied!—Kuqiao snapped back crisp and clear, "Here!"

"Bring me a rope!"

Kuqiao dashed off and returned moments later with thick hemp rope.

Qianli Xun took it, trussed Lin Jue up in seconds, and pointed at two male servants standing nearby. "You two, get over here. Haul him into a room and lock him up. Give him food and water, nothing else—nobody lets him out!"

Obediently, the two servants hauled Lin Jue to his feet.

Tied up tight, Lin Jue still ran his mouth. "Watch out, or I'll report you to the local yamen for assaulting your husband!"

"Go ahead—I'll report you for failing your duty as a husband!"

"You aren't Zhu Shuzhen!" Lin Jue burst out with the doubt eating at him.

Qianli Xun stepped close and murmured in his ear, "You'd better believe I'm Zhu Shuzhen. Otherwise that means you beat her to death. That's a human life—take it to the yamen and see who lands in a cell."

Lin Jue clamped his mouth shut as the servants dragged him off and locked him inside a room.

Qianli Xun turned her gaze on Chun Jinqiao, strode over, and cracked a heavy slap across her face. "That one's for Zhu Shuzhen. Now get out of here and crawl back to your brothel!"

Chun Jinqiao swung back to return the slap, only for Qianli Xun to block her arm cold. Overmatched by sheer strength, she knew better than to stay; clutching her stinging cheek, she swayed out of Lin Manor in tears.
