# 第60章 和离之事成定局
# Chapter 60: The Mutual Divorce Becomes Final

---

乔梦容想起哥哥病着时，有一次，身体稍微好些，要求丫鬟为他穿戴整齐，他要牵着她的手，在仪王府的花园里走走。

Qiao Mengrong remembered when her brother was ill. Once, when he was feeling a bit better, he had the maid dress him properly. He wanted to hold her hand and walk through the gardens of Prince Yi's mansion.

---

哥哥拉着她的手，步履艰难，却也努力走着，不让乔梦容和丫鬟搀扶，说他要好好体验恋爱的感觉。乔梦容既心疼哥哥，又不愿违背他的心愿，任凭他牵着手。

Her brother held her hand, his steps unsteady, yet he struggled forward, refusing to let Qiao Mengrong or the maid support him. He said he wanted to truly experience the feeling of being in love. Qiao Mengrong felt heartache for her brother but didn't want to go against his wishes, so she let him hold her hand.

---

他们好不容易走到王府内一大片种满薰衣草的园中，哥哥将她的手拽得更紧："梦容，等我好了，一定要抱着你，在这片薰衣草中转无数个圈，告诉它们，我会爱你一生一世。"

They finally made it to a large garden in the mansion filled with lavender. Her brother squeezed her hand tighter: "Mengrong, when I'm well, I will definitely hold you and spin you around in countless circles in this lavender field. I'll tell every flower that I will love you for all eternity."

---

乔梦容幸福地笑着："哥哥那样转我，岂不是会把我转晕？"

Qiao Mengrong smiled happily: "If you spin me like that, Brother, won't you make me dizzy?"

---

"晕了才好，倒在我怀里，我会永远抱着你。"

"Good. If you're dizzy, you'll fall into my arms, and I will hold you forever."

---

马车外传来乔文仙的声音："累死我了！快停下来！我要上马车歇歇！"

Outside the carriage came Qiao Wenxian's voice: "I'm exhausted! Stop! I need to get back in the carriage and rest!"

---

乔梦容的回忆被乔公子的声音打断，外面已没有街市上嘈杂的人声，应该出了城。她轻轻叹口气，埋怨自己要求太多，只要哥哥身体健康比什么都重要。

Qiao Mengrong's memories were interrupted by Young Master Qiao's voice. Outside, there were no longer the noisy sounds of the market streets. They must have left the city. She sighed softly, scolding herself for wanting too much. As long as her brother was healthy, that was more important than anything.

---

宴会结束后，千里寻并未回林府，她匆匆去找韦嬷嬷，想尽快了解韦嬷嬷有没有与父亲谈过。

After the banquet ended, Qianli Xun did not return to the Lin residence. She hurried to find Nanny Wei, wanting to know as soon as possible whether Nanny Wei had spoken with her father.

---

当她带着苦荞与夏果来到向夫人居住的院子时，韦嬷嬷正好从屋内出来，先批评夏果。

When she arrived at Madam Xiang's courtyard with Kuqiao and Xiaguo, Nanny Wei happened to be coming out of the room. She first criticized Xiaguo.

---

"夏果，我让你在院中待着，你却跑到宴会上，如此不懂礼数，不适合待在姑娘身边，从哪里来回哪里去吧。"

"Xiaguo, I told you to stay in the courtyard, but you ran off to the banquet. You're so lacking in propriety that you're not fit to stay by the young lady's side. Go back to where you came from."

---

夏果立刻跪下："嬷嬷恕罪，我在院中无聊，又担心小姐，才跑到宴会上，再说我也没犯什么大错，嬷嬷又何必针对我？"

Xiaguo immediately knelt down: "Nanny, please forgive me. I was bored in the courtyard and worried about the young lady, so I ran to the banquet. Besides, I didn't make any big mistake. Why must you single me out?"

---

韦嬷嬷说："针对你？你也配？如若你像苦荞一样是个安分的丫头，我也不说什么，可你天生不安分……"

Nanny Wei said: "Single you out? You're not even worth it. If you were a well-behaved maid like Kuqiao, I wouldn't say anything. But you've always been unruly by nature..."

---

韦嬷嬷还未说完，千里寻劝道："嬷嬷不必理会夏果，我还有重要的事问你。"

Before Nanny Wei could finish, Qianli Xun interceded: "Nanny, don't bother with Xiaguo. I have something important to ask you."

---

姑娘已发话，韦嬷嬷不好再批评下去："罚你在门外跪上半个时辰，好好想想错在哪里。苦荞，你在旁边守着，姑娘由我来伺候。"

Since the young lady had spoken, Nanny Wei couldn't continue criticizing: "You're to kneel as punishment outside the gate for half a shichen. Think carefully about what you did wrong. Kuqiao, you watch over her. I'll attend to the young lady."

---

千里寻虽可怜夏果，可也不能驳了韦嬷嬷的面子，毕竟这位嬷嬷无论做任何事都是为她考虑。

Although Qianli Xun felt sorry for Xiaguo, she couldn't undermine Nanny Wei's authority. After all, this nanny always considered things for her sake, no matter what she did.

---

二人进屋后，千里寻问起韦嬷嬷见父亲之事，韦嬷嬷将具体过程一一对姑娘说了。

After the two entered the room, Qianli Xun asked Nanny Wei about meeting her father. Nanny Wei told the young lady the whole process in detail.

---

林觉已同意和离，千里寻当然高兴，可父亲的态度依然坚决，又让千里寻高兴不起来。

Lin Jue had already agreed to the mutual divorce. Of course, Qianli Xun was happy about that. But her father's attitude remained firm, which made Qianli Xun unhappy again.

---

银杏从外面进来："小姐，韦嬷嬷，老爷请你们去厅堂。"

Yinxing came in from outside: "Young lady, Nanny Wei, the master invites you to the main hall."

---

韦嬷嬷问："是单叫我与姑娘，还是也有其他人？"

Nanny Wei asked: "Is it just Nanny Wei and the young lady, or are there others?"

---

银杏回道："夫人已过去，大公子和少夫人也去了。"

Yinxing replied: "Madam has already gone. The eldest young master and the young madam have also gone."

---

韦嬷嬷判断道："看来有大事，不妨过去听听，趁人都在，我再与老爷提提和离之事。"

Nanny Wei judged: "It seems something big is happening. We might as well go listen. While everyone is there, I'll bring up the matter of the mutual divorce with the master again."

---

来到院中，韦嬷嬷看一眼跪在地上的夏果，没有说一句话，与姑娘往厅堂而去。

Coming to the courtyard, Nanny Wei glanced at Xiaguo kneeling on the ground. Without saying a word, she and the young lady headed toward the main hall.

---

夏果显得很委屈，见韦嬷嬷走远，问道："苦荞姐姐，你说说，韦嬷嬷为何看我不顺眼？"

Xiaguo looked very wronged. Seeing Nanny Wei walk far away, she asked: "Sister Kuqiao, tell me, why does Nanny Wei dislike me so much?"

---

"我哪里知道？"

"How would I know?"

---

"她以前可这么对待过你？"

"Did she ever treat you like this before?"

---

"那倒没有。"

"No, she didn't."

---

"哼！她肯定是嫌我长得好看！我长得好看，难道有错吗？"

"Hmph! She must resent that I'm good-looking! Is it wrong that I'm good-looking?"

---

"你别瞎想，肯定不是因为这个。"

"Don't think nonsense. It's definitely not because of that."

---

"肯定是！她嫌我长得好看，小姐把我带出去，怕我抢了小姐的风头。小姐还没说什么，韦嬷嬷凭什么这么对待我？"

"It definitely is! She resents that I'm good-looking. The young lady took me out, afraid I'd steal the young lady's spotlight. The young lady hasn't said anything, so why is Nanny Wei treating me like this?"

---

苦荞劝道："你别胡乱想，至于什么原因，我也不清楚，但我知道韦嬷嬷是个很厉害的人，一般连我们自己都还没发现的问题，她一眼就能看出，她罚你，肯定是你有问题。"

Kuqiao advised: "Don't think wildly. As for the reason, I'm not clear either. But I know Nanny Wei is a very sharp person. Usually, even problems we haven't discovered ourselves, she can see at a glance. If she punished you, there must be something wrong with you."

---

夏果更加不满："苦荞姐姐什么意思？难道你伺候小姐从来都不会出错？"

Xiaguo became even more dissatisfied: "What do you mean, Sister Kuqiao? Have you never made a mistake while attending the young lady?"

---

苦荞想想说："出错没出错我都不记得，不过韦嬷嬷的确从来没罚过我。"

Kuqiao thought and said: "I don't remember whether I made mistakes or not. But Nanny Wei has indeed never punished me."

---

"哼！"夏果很不服气。

"Hmph!" Xiaguo was very unconvinced.

---

千里寻与韦嬷嬷来到厅堂时，其他人已坐在里面，向夫人示意她们尽快落座。

When Qianli Xun and Nanny Wei arrived at the main hall, the others were already seated inside. Madam Xiang signaled them to take their seats quickly.

---

朱晞颜坐在主位，一脸严肃，与宴会上的表现判若两人。

Zhu Xiyan sat in the seat of honor, his face serious, looking like two different people from his performance at the banquet.

---

人已到齐，朱晞颜咳嗽一声，开口说："把你们叫来，有两件大事要说。第一件，今日宴会中来了一位贵人，不知你们可注意到？"

Everyone had arrived. Zhu Xiyan coughed once and began to speak: "I've called you all here to discuss two important matters. First, a noble guest came to today's banquet. Did you all notice?"

---

大家十分不解。

Everyone was very confused.

---

朱景元在府衙办差，很快明白父亲的意思："爹，您说的贵人可是临安节度使？"

Zhu Jingyuan worked at the prefecture office and quickly understood his father's meaning: "Father, is the noble guest you're referring to the Military Commissioner of Lin'an?"

---

朱晞颜点点头："没错，是临安节度使，他今日出现在宴会，竟然无人知晓，你们可知道临安节度使是谁？"

Zhu Xiyan nodded: "That's right, the Military Commissioner of Lin'an. He appeared at the banquet today, yet no one knew. Do you all know who the Military Commissioner of Lin'an is?"

---

向夫人小心问："是谁？"

Madam Xiang asked cautiously: "Who is it?"

---

朱晞颜回道："官家的十三子，仪王。"

Zhu Xiyan replied: "The thirteenth son of the emperor, Prince Yi."

---

大家被惊到，面面相觑。

Everyone was shocked and looked at each other.

---

朱晞颜继续说："都仔细想想，在宴会中有没有说过分的话，做过分的事。"

Zhu Xiyan continued: "Think carefully. Did any of you say anything excessive or do anything excessive at the banquet?"

---

千里寻问："爹，你说的仪王到底是宴会中哪一位？我可一点儿印象都没有。"

Qianli Xun asked: "Father, who exactly is the Prince Yi you're talking about at the banquet? I don't have any impression at all."

---

向夫人问："该不会是跟着乔公子来的那位申公子？"

Madam Xiang asked: "Could it be Young Master Shen who came with Young Master Qiao?"

---

朱晞颜点点头。

Zhu Xiyan nodded.

---

千里寻心想："原来他是仪王，还是临安节度使。"

Qianli Xun thought to herself: "So he's Prince Yi, and also the Military Commissioner of Lin'an."

---

陈文心说："仔细想想，我与婆婆，还有淑真说话做事均符合礼仪要求，应该不会得罪仪王。"

Chen Wenxin said: "Thinking carefully, Mother-in-law and I, along with Shuzhen, all spoke and acted according to propriety. We shouldn't have offended Prince Yi."

---

朱景元说："儿子与仪王一直没打过照面，谈不上有什么差错。"

Zhu Jingyuan said: "I never came face to face with Prince Yi, so there's no question of any mistake."

---

朱晞颜点点头："那便好，还有一位，大家以后也要谨慎对待，仪王身边有位姑娘，是佳和郡主，以后跟她说话也需恭敬，记住没？"

Zhu Xiyan nodded: "That's good. There's another person you all must be cautious with in the future. There's a young lady by Prince Yi's side—she's Princess Jiahe. When speaking with her in the future, you must also be respectful. Remember?"

---

大家回道："记下了。"

Everyone replied: "We remember."

---

千里寻回忆她与佳和郡主聊天儿的情景，没有什么不对。心想："没想到乔姑娘是佳和郡主，跟我说话谦和有礼，一点儿没有郡主的架子。"

Qianli Xun recalled her conversation with Princess Jiahe. Nothing was wrong. She thought to herself: "I didn't expect Young Lady Qiao to be Princess Jiahe. She spoke to me so gently and politely, without any airs of a princess at all."

---

朱晞颜又咳嗽一声，看向女儿："接下来该说第二件事。"

Zhu Xiyan coughed again and looked at his daughter: "Now it's time to discuss the second matter."

---

接触到父亲的目光，千里寻纳闷，为何父亲看向她？难道第二件事与她有关？

Meeting her father's gaze, Qianli Xun was puzzled. Why was her father looking at her? Could the second matter be related to her?

---

果不其然，父亲唤她的名字："淑真。"

Sure enough, her father called her name: "Shuzhen."

---

千里寻回道："在。"

Qianli Xun replied: "Yes."

---

朱晞颜说："你既然跟林觉过不下去，和离就和离吧……"

Zhu Xiyan said: "Since you can't get along with Lin Jue, then let's just have the mutual divorce..."

---

朱晞颜话还未说完，千里寻已忍不住兴奋激动的心情，起身抱住朱晞颜："谢谢爹！你是天下最好的爹！"

Before Zhu Xiyan could finish speaking, Qianli Xun couldn't contain her excitement. She stood up and hugged Zhu Xiyan: "Thank you, Father! You are the best father in the world!"

---

女儿长这么大，第一次搂抱父亲，朱晞颜十分不适应："回去坐好，听我把话说完。"

His daughter had grown up and was hugging her father for the first time. Zhu Xiyan was very uncomfortable: "Go back and sit properly. Let me finish speaking."

---

千里寻只得放开，坐回原位。其他人也都无不替她感到高兴。

Qianli Xun had to let go and sit back in her seat. Everyone else was also happy for her.

---

朱晞颜继续说："景元，明日把族中所有长辈请到朱府，就说是我的意思，在这厅堂，给淑真把和离的事办了。"

Zhu Xiyan continued: "Jingyuan, tomorrow invite all the clan elders to the Zhu residence. Say it's my wish. In this main hall, handle the mutual divorce matter for Shuzhen."

---

朱景元回道："好的，爹。"

Zhu Jingyuan replied: "Yes, Father."

---

朱晞颜发话道："今日就到这里，散了吧。"

Zhu Xiyan announced: "That's all for today. Dismissed."

---

一家人目送朱晞颜离开，千里寻将每个人抱了一遍，表达开心之情。由此看来，竹林的确给她带来好运。

The family watched Zhu Xiyan leave. Qianli Xun hugged everyone, expressing her joy. It seemed the bamboo grove had indeed brought her good luck.
