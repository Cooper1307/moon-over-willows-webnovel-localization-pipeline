# Chapter 88: Can't Shake Off the Fly Speck

---

Arriving at her brother's courtyard, as soon as she entered the gate, she heard Laibao babbling. Chen Wenxin was playing with the baby in the courtyard with a maid. Laibao sat in a small wooden crib, drooling and laughing at the same time.

---

The maid wiped the drool from the corners of Laibao's mouth with a handkerchief, while her other hand shook a pellet drum — ding-ding, dong-dong.

---

Seeing Shuzhen come, Chen Wenxin welcomed her: "I thought after the divorce, you'd look down on your brother and me and wouldn't want to visit our courtyard."

---

Qianlixun teased: "Sister-in-law, that's a strange thing to say. What does the divorce have to do with looking down on you and brother?"

---

Chen Wenxin explained: "When you couldn't get a divorce, you needed us, so you'd come to your brother and me for help. Now that you've successfully divorced, you don't need us anymore, so naturally you won't come."

---

Qianlixun remembered Kuqiao once saying this sister-in-law had a sharp tongue. Sure enough, her words today made people uncomfortable.

---

But Qianlixun felt her sister-in-law wasn't a bad person, so she didn't take it to heart: "Here I am now, aren't I? That shows you're overthinking. Even if I hadn't come, it would be because something kept me busy, not what you said."

---

"Good then." Chen Wenxin took Shuzhen's hand. "Come, sit over there with me and chat for a while."

---

They went to a tree on the opposite side, where a table, chairs, and tea snacks were set out. The two sat down smiling.

---

"I heard you toured West Lake with Young Master Qiao the other day."

---

"Did you hear it from Mother?"

---

"Mother-in-law didn't tell me. I heard it from Haoyu."

---

Qianlixun fell silent. Just hearing Chen Haoyu's name made her feel uncomfortable all over.

---

After playing with Laibao, Zhu Jingyuan said: "Wenxin, I'll go inside and keep my brother-in-law company for a while. You and Shuzhen can chat here."

---

Chen Wenxin was puzzled: "It's uncanny! I haven't said anything — how do you know Haoyu is here?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan laughed: "Do I need you to tell me? Last time he came, he said he'd give Laibao a silver bracelet. When I left this morning, it wasn't there yet. Now it's on — if it wasn't him, who else could it be?"

---

Chen Wenxin remained puzzled: "Maybe he's already left?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan said: "Every time he comes for this, he always goes inside to admire the goldfish I raise. He won't leave until he's admired them for a while."

---

"You sure know him well. He is here, admiring your goldfish inside." Chen Wenxin finished, then turned to Shuzhen. "Let's ignore them and chat about our things."

---

Hearing that Chen Haoyu was here, Qianlixun frowned and thought: "I was in a good mood today, and Chen Haoyu just had to show up. It makes me want to storm in, drag him out, and give him a beating — revenge for falling in the water!"

---

"Sister, what's wrong? You look unhappy. Did my words just now hurt you? Are you angry with me?"

---

"No... how could I be angry with you? I'm angry with your brother. When I was touring West Lake, he deliberately rammed my boat. I lost my balance and fell into the water, soaked from head to toe — like a drowned rat."

---

"That boy is too wild. Someday when I visit my parents, I'll have a serious talk with Father and teach him a lesson."

---

"How can you call it just wild behavior? Twice I've seen him bully respectable women. My maid Xiaguo was rescued right from your brother's hands."

---

"That's outrageous! My parents scold and yell at him for it too, but we can't watch him every moment. Besides, with Grandmother protecting him, there's nothing we can do. Though he's never done anything wrong to me as his sister."

---

"You're a woman too. If you were bullied by some lecher out there, how would you feel?"

---

"I'd want to kill someone."

---

"Your brother is a lecher out there. He even bullied the tea house's female tea master and kissed her in public."

---

"That's going too far! Just wait till I get my hands on him!" Chen Wenxin stood up, walked to the door, and shouted loudly: "Chen Haoyu! Get your butt out here!"

---

Chen Haoyu was inside admiring fish with his brother-in-law. He didn't know Zhu Shuzhen was also in the courtyard. Hearing his sister's shout, he immediately walked out.

---

"Sister, if you want me, just call. Why shout so loud? You might scare Laibao." Then he spotted Zhu Shuzhen. She sat in a chair with a smug smile.

---

Chen Wenxin went up and twisted Chen Haoyu's ear: "Confess honestly — did you bully respectable women again? Watch out, or I'll tell Father and have him beat you with a big stick!"

---

Chen Haoyu's ear was twisted by his sister. He yelped in pain: "Ow! Ow! Sister, how can you listen to others and misunderstand your own brother?"

---

"Misunderstand? What do you do out there? Think I don't know? I spare you criticism out of love, and you dare bully Shuzhen and even knock her into the water? Now apologize to her in front of me!"

---

"Ow! Apologize to her? Impossible!"

---

Zhu Jingyuan followed out. Seeing this, he stepped forward to mediate: "He's still a kid. Being wild is normal. He'll grow out of it. Let go — his ear's already red."

---

Chen Wenxin couldn't bear to twist her brother's ear for too long either. With her husband's persuasion, she immediately let go: "I'll stop twisting your ear, but you must apologize to Shuzhen."

---

Chen Haoyu was extremely unwilling, but since his brother-in-law had spoken up for him, he pretended to apologize and walked to Zhu Shuzhen: "Zhu Shuzhen, sorry. That day I shouldn't have knocked you into the lake and turned you and Qiao Wenxian into drowned rats. But you should thank me. Without me, your date with Qiao Wenxian would've been boring. Think of it as adding some spice to your plain date — so you'll have something to remember."

---

The apology was clearly half-hearted. Qianlixun stood up and cursed: "Spice, my foot! You ruined my perfectly good date. Now every time I remember it, I'll have to think of you, you pest. Today I'll let it go for brother's and sister-in-law's sake, but next time I run into you, I won't forgive you!"

---

Zhu Jingyuan criticized: "Shuzhen, you're a young lady. How can you speak so rudely? Be more careful."

---

Chen Haoyu became even more smug: "Hear that? You're a young lady. Don't be so rude. Even my brother-in-law can't stand it. Hurry back to your boudoir and look in the mirror — see if you still look like a proper lady."

---

Seeing Shuzhen fuming, Chen Wenxin said sharply: "Chen Haoyu! Shut up and get out! From now on, you're not allowed in the Zhu residence without Shuzhen's permission! Got it?"

---

Zhu Shuzhen was angry. Chen Haoyu was even more gleeful: "Hit me if you dare! Let me tell you, before long, I'll send you a big gift!" With that, Chen Haoyu ran off quickly.

---

Qianlixun clenched her fists and wanted to chase after him, but Chen Wenxin held her back: "Don't stoop to his level. I think you two have incompatible horoscopes. Better to see each other less in the future."

---

Qianlixun loosened her fists and calmed down: "Sister-in-law, if it weren't for your sake, I'd have beaten Chen Haoyu so hard he'd be picking his teeth off the ground today!"

---

Chen Wenxin said: "Shuzhen, you're completely different from before. Your speaking style was never like this. When you were in the Zhu residence, you barely even acknowledged me — when did you ever pay attention to Haoyu? I never expected you'd end up feuding with him now. I don't know if that's good or bad."

---

Zhu Jingyuan asked: "Shuzhen, why don't you get along with Haoyu at all? Before, when you two met, you didn't bother each other."

---

"What 'didn't bother each other'? In my memory, you two barely met alone. Even when you did, with Shuzhen's proud attitude, when did she ever take Haoyu seriously? Haoyu once told me there's an ice beauty in the Zhu family — just being near you makes him feel cold and gives him the shivers. Hehe..." Chen Wenxin's laughter dissolved Qianlixun's inner dissatisfaction.

---

She had wanted to chat about daily life or women's matters with her sister-in-law, but Chen Haoyu ruined it. Chen Haoyu was like a fly speck she couldn't shake off — always ruining her good moments. Just when life was becoming somewhat happy, someone who kept causing trouble had to appear.

---

If Qianlixun hadn't stepped in when Chen Haoyu bullied the tea master; if she hadn't stepped in when he bullied Xiaguo — maybe none of this would have happened.

---

But when she saw Chen Haoyu bullying people, if she didn't step in, her conscience wouldn't let her rest. It seemed Master Jingguang was right — a person's fate is determined by their own actions.

---

Qianlixun was no longer in the mood to chat with her sister-in-law. She went to the small wooden crib to play with Laibao: "Laibao, Auntie came to see you. When you grow up, be a big official like Grandpa — whatever you do, don't be like your uncle."

---

Laibao giggled twice, as if he understood and was responding.

---

Qianlixun praised: "Laibao is such a good baby!"

---

Zhu Jingyuan and Chen Wenxin exchanged glances and showed helpless smiles.

---

After playing with Laibao, Qianlixun said goodbye to her brother and sister-in-law. On the way back to the small courtyard, she thought about what Chen Haoyu had said: "Before long, I'll send you a big gift!"

---

What did it mean? She couldn't figure it out. She decided to be more careful from now on — that guy definitely had no good intentions.
