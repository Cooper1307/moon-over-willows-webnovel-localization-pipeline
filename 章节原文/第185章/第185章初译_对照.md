第185章 为逼婚巧设赌局
## Chapter 185: A Rigged Wager to Force a Wedding

陈浩宇正在赌坊玩斗鸡，下的赌注有赢有输，见陆远峰迟迟未到，心有埋怨，却无可奈何。担心遇到蒋爷寻事，便不想再玩下去，对身边的小弟说：“你们玩儿，本公子困了，回去休息。”
疤子拦道：“才玩儿一会儿，陈公子就要走，多没意思。”
“去去去！少拦本公子。”陈浩宇推开疤子，进入屋内。
赌坊的伙计迎上来：“陈公子，您是想玩牌九还是掷骰子？”
“本公子累了，准备回去。”
陈浩宇边说边往外走，还未走到门口，只见赌坊的门被推开，真是冤家路窄，蒋爷带人出现在面前。
“怎么？陈公子这么快就要走？”
陆远峰还未到，陈浩宇不敢太强势，露出笑脸：“是蒋爷来得晚，我有事，先行一步。”
蒋爷身后的两名壮汉立刻上前堵住陈浩宇，一脸凶神恶煞的样子。
Back in the gambling house's rear yard, Chen Haoyu had won a few cockfights and lost a few, grumbling over Lu Yuanfeng's no-show. Nervous about bumping into Master Jiang unprotected, he called it a night. "Keep playing, boys—I am beat and heading home."

陈浩宇继续笑道：“蒋爷，快让你身边的人闪开，别挡我的道。”
Scarface stepped in front of him. "Barely been here a spell, Young Master Chen—leaving so soon spoils the fun!"

蒋爷一挥手，两名壮汉闪到一边。陈浩宇想走，却被蒋爷挡住：“陈浩宇，今儿你我之间做个了断，如何？”
"Move it! Quit blocking my way!" Pushing Scarface aside, Chen Haoyu stepped back into the main hall.

陈浩宇装作若无其事的样子：“了断？什么了断？”
An attendant stepped up. "Pai gow or dice next, Young Master Chen?"

“陈公子真是贵人多忘事，那我就提醒提醒你，爷爷我还是放不下春金沫，今晚就以她为赌注，你若赢了，我以后离春金沫远远的，你若输了，她就是我的人，你以后连她一根手指都不许碰。”
"Done for the night. Heading home."

蒋爷的提议，陈浩宇觉得尚可，他也不想与蒋爷再耗下去：“好！我同意！不过不能以春金沫为赌注，她毕竟是人。”
Before he reached the front doors, they swung wide—talk about bad luck! Master Jiang strode in flanked by his gang.

陈浩宇的话正中蒋爷的心思：“陈公子倒是位有情有义之人，不用春金沫做赌注也可以。考虑到春金沫在爷爷心中的地位，能够替换她的赌注不能太差，否则爷爷可不认账。”
"Leaving so early, Young Master Chen?"

陈浩宇十分爽快：“那就由蒋爷开个价。”
With no Lu Yuanfeng in sight, Chen Haoyu forced a smooth grin. "Master Jiang arrived late—I have business and must run."

蒋爷一挥手，他身后的人立刻把赌坊的门关上，其他人都堵在门口，不允许任何人出去。赌徒们也无心再赌博，一个个都伸长脖子看热闹。
Two burly thugs stepped forward to block the exit, glaring murder.

只听蒋爷说：“让我开价，你可别后悔。”
Keeping his grin pinned on, Chen Haoyu said, "Tell your boys to step aside, Master Jiang—do not block my road."

陈浩宇觉得不就是给钱吗？陈家有的是钱，还怕满足不了蒋爷：“蒋爷尽管开口，别犹豫。”
With a wave, Master Jiang parted them—only to block Chen Haoyu himself. "Let us settle things between us once and for all tonight, Chen Haoyu. Deal?"

“好！陈公子就是爽快！我可说了。”见陈浩宇中计，蒋爷更是开心，“若用陈家家产做赌注，爷爷我以后绝不再惦记春金沫。”
Playing dumb, Chen Haoyu blinked. "Settle what?"

陈浩宇带的小弟此时也凑过来，听到蒋爷所说，一人小声议论：“蒋爷的胃口大得离谱，陈家富甲一方，也得吃得下才行，陈公子肯定不会答应。”
"Short memory for a rich young master! Let me jog it: I still cannot get Chun Jinmo out of my head. Put her on the table tonight—you win, I steer clear of Chun Jinmo forever; you lose, she is mine and you never lay a finger on her again."

疤子小声回道：“看着吧，好戏还在后头。”
Fair enough, Chen Haoyu thought—he was sick of dodging Master Jiang too. "Deal! Only no betting Chun Jinmo herself—she is a living person, not chips."

陈浩宇果然不同意：“不行！怎能随便拿我们陈家的家产做赌注？还是换一个。”
Walking straight into Master Jiang's script! "Spoken like a man of honor! Fine—we leave Chun Jinmo off the slip. Still, given how high she ranks in my heart, whatever replaces her cannot be cheap, or no deal."

蒋爷给身边两名手下使了使眼色，两名手下立刻冲上去将陈浩宇拿住，其中一名拔出刀架在陈浩宇的脖子上。
"Name your stake, Master Jiang!" Chen Haoyu agreed breezily.

陈浩宇被控制，脖子向一边闪去，躲开明晃晃的刀刃：“有话好好说，别动刀动枪的。待会儿我身边的高手来了，小心你们吃不了兜着走。”
At Master Jiang's flick of a wrist, his gang slammed the gambling house doors shut and blocked every exit. Gamblers dropped their dice and craned their necks for the show.

蒋爷指着赌坊的伙计：“去，到外面挂上休息的牌子，高手看到，肯定会原路返回。赌坊接下来的损失都由陈公子赔偿。”
"Let me name the stake, and do not cry foul later," Master Jiang warned.

伙计立刻答道：“是，东家。”
Just silver—the Chens swam in silver! "Spit it out already!"

陈浩宇这才知道原来这家赌坊是黑帮的产业，后悔只顾玩，没把赌坊背后的人了解清楚。
"Bold! I like it! Then hear my terms:" Grinning as the trap snapped shut, Master Jiang declared, "Wager the Chen family fortune—and I swear off Chun Jinmo forever!"

伙计去门外将休息的牌子挂好，进入赌坊内回道：“东家，已挂好。”
Chen Haoyu's lackeys huddled close, one whispering, "Master Jiang's appetite is insane—even if he won the richest house in Lin'an, could he swallow it? Young Master Chen will never agree!"

蒋爷向周围的人说：“诸位，继续玩，我跟陈公子有些个人恩怨需解决，不会影响大家。”
"Just watch," Scarface murmured back. "The good part is coming."

大家比刚才轻松一些，还是没有人继续，谁让看热闹更有意思。
Sure enough, Chen Haoyu barked, "No way! How could I bet the whole Chen family fortune?! Pick something else!"

蒋爷并不去管众人，靠近陈浩宇：“陈家的家产与春金沫请陈公子任选其一。”
Master Jiang flicked his eyes; two thugs grabbed Chen Haoyu on the spot, and one pressed a gleaming broadsword flat against his neck.

陈浩宇谈不上多喜欢春金沫，也觉得与陈家的家业比起来春金沫显然逊色许多，可她是目前他接触过的女子中最满意的一位，重要的是自己的女人被别人的男人强行抢走，有失颜面，加之春金沫曾说过对他死心塌地，若陈浩宇把她让给蒋爷，她会一头撞死。
Pulling his chin clear of the cold steel, Chen Haoyu yelled, "Talk like civilized men—put the blade away! When my bodyguard gets here, you will regret this!"

于是陈浩宇一口回绝：“不选！”
Master Jiang pointed at the house attendant. "Go hang the 'Closed' sign outside. When his bodyguard sees it, he will turn straight around. Charge tonight's lost business to Young Master Chen's tab!"

大刀已靠近他的脖子，拉出一道血印。
"Yes, Proprietor!" the attendant obeyed.

陈浩宇感到脖子处一阵刺痛，他的嘴角轻轻咧开，脑中进行激烈的斗争，到底该选哪个？
Only now did it hit Chen Haoyu that this whole gambling house belonged to the Qiantang Gang—cursed himself for playing blind without checking who owned the roof!

蒋爷说：“陈公子若不愿用陈家的家产做赌注，那就还用春金沫，若我赢，陈公子以后不许再找她。也是，一个商妓怎么比得上你们陈家的家产重要。”
Once the attendant returned reporting the sign hung, Master Jiang called to the room, "Keep playing, folks! Just a private matter between Young Master Chen and me—do not let us spoil your evening." Nobody went back to their tables; watching a rich heir get squeezed beat dice hands down.

陈浩宇觉得春金沫很可怜，无人能帮，若以她为赌注，输了，必然被蒋爷糟蹋，估计她连死的心都有。而陈家的家产多到他陈浩宇都不清楚有多少，蒋爷若赢，未必吃得下。再说有陈家长辈在，蒋爷想得手恐怕不容易。况且输赢未定，保不准赢的人是他陈浩宇。
Stepping right into Chen Haoyu's face, Master Jiang murmured, "The Chen fortune, or Chun Jinmo—pick one."

想好后，开口应道：“我同意！”
Chun Jinmo was nowhere near worth the Chen family empire, yet she was the one woman who suited him, handing his own girl over to another man would humiliate him across Lin'an, and Chun Jinmo had sworn she would smash her head against a wall rather than fall into Master Jiang's clutches.

蒋爷问：“同意哪个？”
"Neither!" Chen Haoyu snapped.

“用……用陈家的家产做赌注。”
The broadsword nudged closer, slicing a thin red line across his skin.

看热闹的人都唏嘘不已，有人小声议论：“陈公子真不愧是败家子。”
Stinging pain flashed across his neck as his mind raced in panic.

蒋爷听到陈浩宇的选择，心中吁口气，总算选到他心里：“好！陈公子为女人愿意以陈家家产做赌注，令蒋某开了眼，诸位也都做个见证。”
"Unwilling to bet the Chen fortune?" Master Jiang cooed. "Then stick with Chun Jinmo—I win, and you never visit her again. Right enough—what is one brothel girl next to a family empire?"

很快陈浩宇后悔刚才说过的话，想收回为时已晚。
Poor Chun Jinmo had no one to protect her—bet her and lose, and Master Jiang would break her. Meanwhile the Chen estate was so vast even Chen Haoyu could not tally it all; could a street boss actually collect with all the Chen elders standing guard? And besides—who said he would lose?!

蒋爷走到赌桌前，陈浩宇也被带到蒋爷对面。拿刀的人收起大刀，依然守在他身旁。
Gritting his teeth, Chen Haoyu blurted, "I agree!"

赌坊的伙计拿来提前准备好的两张字据，放在陈浩宇面前：“陈公子，请您签字，再按下指印。”
"Agree to which?"

旁边已有人递上笔和印朱，陈浩宇总算是看出，对方早有准备，看来自己八成是跳进蔣爷挖的坑里。
"Wager... wager the Chen family fortune!"

他想反悔，准备逃走，给围观的小弟使眼色，让他们前来帮忙。疤子跟那些小弟一个个都摇头，不敢向前，像个缩头乌龟。
Gasps and whistles rippled across the hall. "True prodigal son!" someone muttered.

陈浩宇这才明白，关键时候，他们根本靠不住。他身边站着两名带刀的壮汉，门口也守着蒋爷的人，想逃没那么容易，陈浩宇只能自认倒霉。
Exhaling in quiet triumph—bull's-eye!—Master Jiang clapped. "Willing to stake a whole empire for a woman! You opened my eyes today, Young Master Chen. Everyone here, stand witness!"

“蔣爷，还是先商量好条件。”
Regret slammed into Chen Haoyu two seconds later, far too late to swallow the words back.

蔣爷见陈浩宇不愿签字，不满道：“有什么好商量的？纸上都写着，你若输，陈家财产归我。”
Escorted across the gaming table from Master Jiang while the blade-wielding guard stood right behind his chair, Chen Haoyu stared as an attendant laid two pre-written contracts before him alongside brush and red ink paste. "Sign your name and press your thumbprint, Young Master Chen."

陈浩宇提出要求：“我想再加一条。”
Pre-written contracts?! He had walked blind into an ambush! Casting a frantic glance at his lackeys for help, he saw Scarface and the rest shaking their heads and shrinking back like turtles. Useless fair-weather parasites! Boxed in by armed thugs inside a locked den, he had zero escape.

蒋爷想听听他到底会提什么要求：“说！”
"Hold on—let us settle terms first!"

“若我赢，以后蒋爷不准再欺负我，我被人欺负时，钱塘帮还得派人帮我。”
"What terms?" Master Jiang barked. "Written plain as day: you lose, Chen property is mine."

面对陈浩宇的要求，蔣爷没有任何犹豫：“好，我答应你。”
"I am adding one clause!"

陈浩宇这才提笔在两张字据上写下名字，并用食指沾了印朱，按下指印。
"Speak!"

伙计将字据收好：“东家，可以开始。”
"If I win, you never bully me again—and whenever anyone messes with me, the Qiantang Gang steps in to back me up!"

陈浩宇又问：“蔣爷是要跟我推牌九还是掷骰子？”
Without blinking, Master Jiang nodded. "Done!"

蔣爷不想太浪费时间：“爷爷我喜欢快些赢你，我们还是玩关扑，一把定输赢。”
Dipping the brush, Chen Haoyu signed both slips and stamped his red thumbprint.

“关扑就关扑，要九纯十纯。”陈浩宇觉得铜钱越多，蔣爷赢的可能性越小。
"Ready to begin, Proprietor," the attendant announced as he collected the contracts.

没想到蔣爷一口答应：“就按你说的来，我要是掷出九纯十纯，你小子可就输了。”
"Pai gow or dice?" Chen Haoyu asked.

蔣爷的爽快引起陈浩宇怀疑，当伙计将放有十枚铜钱的小木盒放在蔣爷面前时，他大喊：“慢着！总得让我与在场的诸位验一下里面的铜钱是否动过手脚。”
"Why waste all night? I prefer winning quick—guanpu, one throw takes all!"

蔣爷冷笑道：“陈公子竟然不信任我？既如此，让你查验一番。”
"Guanpu it is—and you need nine or ten of a kind!" More coins meant slimmer odds for Master Jiang.

蔣爷向伙计使使眼色，伙计心领神会，拿起盒子那一刻，神不知鬼不觉已换成另外一个，走到陈浩宇面前打开：“陈公子，请过目。”
"Agreed!" Master Jiang answered without batting an eye. "If I throw nine or ten matching, you lose."

陈浩宇用手指在盒子里拨拉一会儿，的确是十枚，每个铜钱正反两面都有，才放心说：“可以开始。”
His readiness set off alarm bells. As the attendant set a small wooden box holding ten copper coins before Master Jiang, Chen Haoyu shouted, "Wait! Let me and everyone here inspect those coins for tampering!"

蔣爷朝围观的赌徒瞧一遍，目光停留在疤子身上：“为让陈公子输得心服口服，就选陈公子身边的人来摇铜钱。你，过来，陈公子的输赢就由你来决定。”
Master Jiang sneered. "Do not trust me? Fine—inspect away." Catching his boss's glance, the attendant deftly swapped boxes in one sleek sleight of hand before opening it in front of Chen Haoyu. "See for yourself, Young Master."

陈浩宇当然乐意，这样蔣爷想耍手段都难：“疤子，还不赶紧过来。”
Stirring the coins with a finger—ten ordinary coins, heads and tails clean—Chen Haoyu breathed easier. "Start."

疤子笑嘻嘻走到赌桌旁：“陈公子，我要是摇不出九纯十纯的，您是不是就赢了。”
Master Jiang swept his eyes across the crowd and pointed straight at Scarface. "So Young Master Chen loses without a single gripe, let one of his own boys shake the box! You—step up and decide your master's fate!"

“那是当然。”
Thrilled—no way Master Jiang could rig his own lackey!—Chen Haoyu waved him over. "Get up here, Scarface!"

“那您保准赢，我连五纯六纯都没摇出过，何况九纯十纯？您瞧好了。”
Grinning up to the table, Scarface asked, "If I fail to roll nine or ten matching, Young Master wins, right?"

陈浩宇更是开心：“你若摇不出，出了赌场，本公子奖励你十两银子。”
"Damn right!"

疤子很自信，十两银子稳赚不赔，他伸出手：“把木盒给我。”
"Then it is in the bag! I never even rolled five or six matching in my life—let alone nine or ten! Watch this!"

伙计早已偷偷将盒子更换过，放在疤子手中。
"Miss nine or ten, and I hand you ten taels of silver outside!" Chen Haoyu promised.

疤子双手握紧木盒，上上下下，左左右右，晃好几回，才打开盒子的盖子，将里面的铜钱一个不落倒在桌面。
Easy silver! Scarface held out his hands. "Pass the box!"

众人向桌面瞧去，都惊叹道：“十浑纯！”
Slipping the rigged box back in with seamless sleight of hand, the attendant placed it in Scarface's palms.

陈浩宇只觉脑袋嗡嗡作响，差点儿晕倒。
Gripping tight, Scarface rattled the box up, down, left, and right before popping the lid and dumping all ten coins across the table.

有位小弟大声说：“疤子，你这是帮陈公子还是害陈公子？”
The entire hall gasped in one voice: "All ten matching!"

疤子很委屈：“我可是按照以前输的方法摇，谁知手气这么好。陈公子，您可不能怪我。”
A roar echoed inside Chen Haoyu's skull; his knees nearly buckled.

陈浩宇哪有心情理会疤子，他自语道：“我不信！我不信！我要再验验铜钱！”
"Scarface, are you helping Young Master or burying him?!" a lackey yelled.

他正要过去抓铜钱时，赌坊的伙计手更快，立刻将十枚铜钱收起来，装入木盒中，递给蒋爷。
"I shook it the exact loser's way I always do—who knew my luck would turn golden tonight?! Do not blame me, Young Master!" Scarface whined.

蒋爷接过木盒，目的到达，一脸取胜的姿态：“大家亲眼目睹，十浑纯，陈公子还有什么好说的？愿赌服输吧！”
Dazed, Chen Haoyu muttered, "No way! Impossible! Let me check those coins again!"

陈浩宇依然不愿认输，他此刻清醒许多，赌注可是陈家所有的财产，他岂不是坐实败家子的名声？
Before his fingers touched the wood, the attendant swept all ten coins back into the box and handed it to Master Jiang.

“不行！再摇一回！”
Cradling the box in triumph, Master Jiang grinned. "Every eye here saw all ten matching! What excuse is left, Young Master Chen? Pay up like a man!"

蒋爷根本不同意：“刚说过一把定输赢，陈公子这么快就忘记？”
"No! Shake again!" Panic sobered Chen Haoyu—he had just gambled away the entire Chen empire!

伙计拿出一张字据，走到陈浩宇跟前，塞进他的怀里：“陈公子拿好。”
"One throw takes all—short memory again?" Master Jiang scoffed.

陈浩宇接过字据想要撕掉。
The attendant shoved one contract into Chen Haoyu's chest. "Keep your copy safe, Young Master."

蒋爷阻止道：“我这里也有一份，你即使撕毁也没用。如若不想我亲自带人去陈府讨要财产，最好老老实实拿着，回去告诉陈家长辈，以后陈家的一切就是蒋爷我的。”
Chen Haoyu gripped it ready to shred it to bits.

陈浩宇撕也不是不撕也不是，直接揉成一团塞进怀里：“哼！想要陈家的财产没那么容易。”
"I hold the twin copy—tearing yours changes nothing," Master Jiang warned. "Unless you want me marching my whole gang up to Chen Manor to collect, take it home and tell your elders everything the Chens own now belongs to Master Jiang!"

他准备离开赌坊，惹下这么大的祸事，得回去禀告爹娘，他们一定有办法处理。
Stuck neither tearing nor folding, Chen Haoyu crushed it into a ball and jammed it inside his robe. "Hmph! Think grabbing Chen property is that easy?!" Turning toward the door, he raced to get home and confess to his parents—they would find a way out!

刚走到门口，被蒋爷叫住：“陈公子慢走，还有一事需说明。”
At the threshold, Master Jiang called out, "Hold on, Young Master Chen—one last word."

陈浩宇站住，并未回头：“蒋爷还有什么事？”
Halting without turning around, Chen Haoyu snapped, "What now?"

蒋爷走到他身边：“爷爷我并非贪图陈家财产，就是想捉弄你，灭一灭你的气焰。回去告诉陈家人，只要你娶了朱家姑娘，陈家的财产我一分一毫不取。”
Stepping beside him, Master Jiang lowered his voice. "Truth be told, I have no appetite for Chen property—just wanted to knock that cocky grin off your face. Go tell your family: marry Miss Zhu, and I will not touch a single coin of Chen wealth."

听到这个要求，陈浩宇沉重的心情略有轻松，毕竟有了保住陈家财产的方法。他转头看向蒋爷：“你如此大费周章，难道只是让我娶朱家姑娘？”
A glimmer of air hit Chen Haoyu's lungs. Spinning around, he stared. "All this setup—just to force me to marry Miss Zhu?!"

“陈公子果然聪明，朱家姑娘的厉害我可是领教过，你若娶她，以后恐怕没有机会再去春香楼，春金沫不就是爷爷我的？”蒋爷说完，继续叮嘱，“限你三日内娶朱家姑娘，若不娶，爷爷我可就拿着字据找陈家大老爷。”
"Sharp lad! I got a firsthand taste of Miss Zhu's fists. Once you marry her, you will never sneak near Chunxiang Brothel again—leaving Chun Jinmo free and clear for me!"
