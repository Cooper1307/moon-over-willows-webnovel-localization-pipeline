## Chapter 155: Sister-in-Law Visits Her Maiden Home

Next morning dawned bright and mild, so Chen Wenxin asked Madam Xiang's leave to visit her parents, then boarded a carriage back to Chen Manor with Yuegui and the wet nurse carrying little Laibao.

Watching her beaming daughter-in-law ride home stirred a quiet ache in Madam Xiang. When would her own daughter return beaming with a grandchild in her arms? New Year loomed right around the corner; Shuzhen had lived outside Zhu Manor for ages now, and frequent visits to Wangyue Pavilion could not match having her daughter safe under her own roof. With the Zhu-Chen betrothal settled, Madam Xiang had almost begged Master several times to bring Shuzhen home, only to lose her nerve before his stony face. Once Zhu Xiyan mentioned in passing that Shuzhen would come back to Zhu Manor to leave in her bridal sedan on her wedding day. That thrilled Madam Xiang while tipping her off he meant not one day earlier, killing any hope of bringing her home ahead of time.

Arriving at Chen Manor, Chen Wenxin walked straight to her parents' rooms, finding only Yao Qingyan inside. Lighting up at sight of Laibao, Yao Qingyan scooped her grandson from the wet nurse's arms and kissed his chubby cheek. "Growing like a weed! Almost walking age!"

"Practicing at home; just needs someone holding his hands," Chen Wenxin smiled, taking a seat. "Where's Father? Not keeping you company?"

"Off chasing business. Expanding Chen ventures into other prefectures, so he's seldom home these days." Bouncing Laibao playful, she coaxed, "Say Grandma!"

"Gwa-ma... Gwa-ma..." Babbling unclear yet adorable, Laibao earned showered kisses across both cheeks.

Chen Wenxin teased, "Going this wild over a daughter's son, wonder what you'll do holding your own son's baby!"

"Need you ask? I only envy your mother-in-law getting a grandson first! Praying Shuzhen marries in fast and gives Haoyu a boy—or a girl, Mother adores both!" Remembering something urgent, Yao Qingyan told the wet nurse and Yuegui, "Take Laibao inside the inner room to play a bit; I need a private word with Wenxin."

Once they withdrew, Chen Wenxin leaned forward. "What's on your mind, Mother?"

"Your brother again!" Yao Qingyan let out a heavy sigh. "We're thrilled sealing his match, yet Haoyu hates it—swears Shuzhen is a tigress who'll beat him black and blue! Turned up covered in bruises last time trying to swear to your dad and me Shuzhen did it! We didn't buy it, yet he put a bug in my ear. Could Shuzhen look gentle outside while hiding a she-demon underneath?"

Leaning close with a secretive glint, Chen Wenxin whispered, "Let me tell you a secret, Mother—never breathe a word outside!"

Yao Qingyan nodded curious. "What secret?"

"Shuzhen got amnesia! Wiped out every memory of the past!"

"Amnesia?! How on earth?!"

"Lin Jue beat her unconscious. She woke up remembering nothing and turned into a whole different person!"

"Into a she-demon?!"

"Nothing like that! Just dropped her frosty airs—chats warm with me calling 'Sister-in-law' left and right, super pleasant to be around!"

"Well, that's lovely. Any other changes?"

Pausing to think, Chen Wenxin grinned. "Oh, once during dinner with Father-in-law, she split a solid dining table clean in half with one bare punch!"

"Good heavens! Strength like that?! Guess Haoyu has some reason to sweat! Wait—how did Haoyu even know she packs punches like that?"

"No idea! All I know is those two clash like fire and water, snapping at each other on sight, probably why those ugly rumors blew up! Funny thing is, before they seldom spoke two words; now at least they bicker nonstop!"

Relief broke into laughter on Yao Qingyan's face. "Now it all makes sense! Haoyu just spun tall tales because they lock horns—nothing rotten in Shuzhen's heart! Seldom spoke before, bickering nonstop now... hehe, proves amnesia sparked real fate between them! Couples who squabble grow closest!"

Watching her mother beam, Chen Wenxin teased, "Grinning ear to ear—aren't you worried strong-fisted Shuzhen will beat Haoyu once she moves in?"

"Your father already said nobody in this house can rein Haoyu in. Bringing home a fierce wife to keep him in line might cure his wild ways! Only snag is Old Madam Xia. She dotes on Haoyu blind, swears Shuzhen bruised him, and wants the match broken! Daren't cross the Prefect to his face, so she plans on stalling the Zhus out!"

"Leave it to Grandma! How's she stalling?"

"No clue; bet she's got a scheme cooking! Your dad and I nod along to keep peace while praying nothing derails the wedding."

Chen Wenxin jumped up. "I'm marching straight to Grandma to tell her Haoyu played her!"

Pulling her back down, Yao Qingyan shook her head. "Think Grandma lived all these decades without seeing through Haoyu's lies? She just loves her grandson too much to bear seeing him scraped!"

Sinking back into her seat, Chen Wenxin groused, "If you ask me, Grandma's gone senile!"

Right on cue, Chen Haoyu sauntered through the door. "Talking trash about Grandma behind her back, Sister? Careful or I tattle straight to her!" Out roaming town hunting ways to wreck his betrothal, he had dropped by Zhu Manor first, heard Young Madam had gone home to Chen Manor, and hurried straight back.

Putting on a stern elder-sister glare, Chen Wenxin barked, "Where've you been gallivanting now?! Almost a married man and still wild as ever!"

Dropping beside his mother, Chen Haoyu grinned. "Where else? Ran over to Zhu Manor looking for you, only to hear you came home!"

"Pull wool over Grandma's eyes, not mine!" Stepping over, Chen Wenxin pinched his ear hard. "Confess—brothel or gambling den?!"

"Ow! Ow! Real sister or torture master?! Attacking me the second I walk in!"

Releasing his ear and sitting back, she snapped, "Spill it—what do you want with me?"

Rubbing his red ear, he whined to Yao Qingyan, "Mother, scold Sister! Grown man getting his ear twisted; I'll lose all my face if word gets out!"

Yao Qingyan offered zero backup. "Do one decent thing outside and your sister wouldn't touch you!"

"Playing favorites, Mother!"

"Zero shame asking Mother to cover for you!" Chen Wenxin snapped impatient. "Out with it—why were you looking for me?"

Keeping his real scheme hid, Chen Haoyu put on a pious mask. "About to become a proper Zhu son-in-law, so I figured I'd ask what my future father-in-law's up to lately to find a chance to impress him!"

Mother and daughter traded startled stares. What flipped overnight?

Smelling a rat at once, Chen Wenxin narrowed her eyes. "Serious?"

"Need you ask?!"

"Really willing to marry Shuzhen, son?" Yao Qingyan pressed.

"Willing, willing!"

"Then march over to Grandma first and tell her to drop her stalling games!" Doubt still nagged Yao Qingyan.

"Relax, I'll talk Grandma round soon enough."

Still skeptical, Chen Wenxin probed, "Think I buy that? What are you really digging into Father-in-law's schedule for?"

"Told you—impressing him!"

"Want to impress Father-in-law, visit Zhu Manor anytime. Why pump me?"

He could not put on a show solo at Zhu Manor when Zhu Shuzhen was barred from the gate! Spinning fast, he grinned. "Where's the glory at home? Impressing my future father-in-law in front of all his fellow officials gives him real face!"

Clueless what scheme her brother had cooking yet sure even he would not dare make a scene before Lin'an's officials, Chen Wenxin relented. "Heard from your brother-in-law that with New Year coming, the prefecture office booked Juxiang Tower tomorrow for an officials' banquet. Do yourself a favor and stay clear so you don't shame both houses!"

Jackpot! Beaming inside, Chen Haoyu promised smooth, "Relax, Sister. Wild as I get, I'd never shame both families! Keep chatting; I'm off to Grandma's to tell her to quit stalling!"

He had scarce warmed his chair before bolting right back out.

"Two words and gone again—that boy!" Yao Qingyan scolded.

"Believe him, Mother?"

"Not a word! Just feeding us sugar. You shouldn't have told him your father-in-law's plans!"

"Relax, Mother. Give him ten extra guts and he still wouldn't dare cause trouble in front of every Lin'an official!"

Skipping Old Madam Xia altogether, Chen Haoyu bolted straight out of Chen Manor toward Wangyue Pavilion. Arriving at the gate, he refused to step inside and had the gate boy summon Zhu Shuzhen out.

Jogging to her room, the page boy called, "Miss, Young Master Chen is at the gate. He won't come in and asks you to step out."

Just finished dressing to visit Youlin Retreat, Qianli Xun guessed at once he had cooked up a break-off scheme. Reading her mind, Kuqiao lifted the heavy winter curtain. "Tell Young Master Chen Miss will be out in a bit."

"Skip the message—I'm heading right out!" Qianli Xun stepped past him.

Walking solo to the gate and seeing Chen Haoyu standing alone without attendants, fresh off some mischief no doubt, she scowled. "Coming to my door—cooked up a plan?"

"Of course! Think I came over to whisper sweet nothings?!"

"Spill it and get lost!"

Her fierce bark convinced him twice over he could not marry this woman. "Free tip: quit snarling at me. Doesn't hurt me, just makes you uglier!"

"Talk business or eat my fist!"

"Speaking of hitting me, I've got the golden stage for you—just don't hit for real!"

"What stage?"

"Tomorrow Lin'an officials booked Juxiang Tower. Your dad's the Prefect, guaranteed to be there! Let's put on a brawl right in front of the entire prefecture office so everyone sees we're oil and water! Shamed enough, maybe your dad pushes to cancel the match himself!"

Qianli Xun hesitated. Trashing Zhu Xiyan's face before every official sounded harsh, and pushing him into backing down might backfire. "Terrible idea! My dad guards his face with his life; making a scene before every official leaves him nowhere to hide! Plus my name just recovered—trying to start fresh gossip over me?!"

"I meant to stage it inside Zhu Manor, except your dad banned you from the door! I sweated getting this hot tip from my sister and you shoot it down. Fine, what's your brilliant plan?!"

Thinking fast, Qianli Xun countered, "Skip Zhu Manor and skip the officials! No day like today. Right this afternoon we ambush outside Zhu Manor's side gate before the Hour of the Monkey when Dad gets off office duty, and stage our brawl right in front of him!"

"Deal! Done—see you there!" Pivoting off toward Yongning Residence, Chen Haoyu planned on grabbing lunch and a quick nap before heading over to wait for Zhu Xiyan.
