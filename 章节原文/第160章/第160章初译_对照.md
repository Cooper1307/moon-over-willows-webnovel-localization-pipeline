第160章 捶丸聊天各顾各
## Chapter 160: Chapter 160: Chuiwan and Idle Talk, Each Mind Elsewhere

千里寻思忖着文渊刚才的表现，想不出所以然。
传来梦容的声音：“淑真，原来你坐在这里，我一进来到处找你，终于在这儿找到。”
众人起身拜见郡主：“佳和郡主。”
“你们别对我这么客气，朱家少夫人，韦嬷嬷，我能不能把淑真带走？”乔梦容上前已拉住淑真。
陈文心与韦嬷嬷相视一笑，主动说：“郡主相邀，我们二人岂有阻拦之理？”
“谢谢二位。”乔梦容拉起淑真就要离开。
苦荞与红豆想跟上，被小姐阻止：“在此处候着，不用跟来。”
二人齐声回道：“是。”
梦容拉着淑真出了捶丸场，边走边说：“我不喜欢捶丸，你陪我走走。”
“不喜欢，为何要来？”
“乔家邀请哥哥，哥哥不好不来，他到哪里，我当然得跟着。”
自从知道仪王是申简辰后，千里寻不再批判他是花心大萝卜，也不再认为他不懂得知恩图报，反而更加怜惜梦容。
“梦容，仪王起死回生后，以前的事都不记得，你有没有想过他不再是以前的仪王？而是另外一个人。”
“另外一个人？”梦容想起哥哥刚刚起死回生后，说他不是赵朴的话，可她根本不愿承认，“怎么可能，哥哥还是以前的哥哥，只是失忆而已。”
如此认为倒也不错，千里寻觉得自己又何必替梦容操心，只要她不接受申简辰，仪王才会永远是梦容心中的好哥哥。
“我胡乱说的，你别往心里去，仪王人不错，你们以后一定能幸福。”
梦容站住，扭过头，好奇地看着淑真：“你为何突然对哥哥印象变好？”
“唉，我这人识人不准，后来渐渐发现还是你最了解仪王。”
以前淑真说过的话，乔梦容虽未完全听进去，多少还是受到影响，更担心哥哥身边会出现其他女子。现在淑真改变看法，乔梦容彻底轻松下来。
她们在园林内边走边聊，周围的植物渐渐焕发生气，许多桃树叶子还未长出，已满树桃花，满眼尽是春光烂漫。
陈浩东陪同颜池从另一条小径走来。
“颜兄，后面正举行捶丸赛，要不要去玩玩？”
“我有要事，等办完事再陪你玩儿。”
颜池一眼瞅到走来的两位姑娘，其中一位正是大街上偶然遇到之人，心想：“踏破铁鞋无觅处，得来全不费功夫。”
“陈兄，她们你可认识？”
陈浩东向两位姑娘瞧去：“认得，左边那位是佳和郡主，右边是临安知府的千金朱淑真。”
“朱淑真。”颜池重复一遍，“没想到她叫这个名字。”
陈浩东从颜池的表情中看出他对朱家姑娘颇有兴趣：“颜兄瞧上她？”
“什么都逃不过你的眼睛。”颜池毫不掩饰。
陈浩东叹口气：“可惜啊，她已与我家堂弟定亲，再过一个月，就要举办婚礼。”
“那有什么？我喜欢的人，即使跟别人生了娃娃，迟早我也会把她搞到手。”
“颜兄好气魄！”
“等她嫁给你的堂弟，你可得帮我盯紧她。”
陈浩东不解：“为何？”
“别让她生病离开人世，等我忙完正事，自然会从你堂弟手中将她抢走。”
颜池说完，拐向另一条小径，向园林外而去。
陈浩东将颜池送出后，拐回来，见两位姑娘依旧在散步，便上前打招呼。
“佳和郡主，朱家姑娘，二位要是逛累，可以去那边品茶歇息，陈家园林内的下人若有不周到之处，还请二位及时向我反馈，我好吩咐管事改进。”
二位姑娘愣住，她们从未见过眼前之人，从他所说的话中判断，应该是陈家人。
梦容试探地问：“你是？……”
“在下名叫陈浩东，是浩宇的堂兄。”
对方的身份已确定，与他不熟，没必要多言语。
乔梦容以郡主的身份说：“不必客气，你尽管去忙，不用理会我们。”
明显是赶人的话，陈浩东本也只是打声招呼，让贵人知道有他这号人物。贵人既然赶人，他自然乖乖退下：“在下谨遵郡主吩咐。”
二人继续散步，欣赏春色，聊着闺中密友之间的话题。
乔家举办捶丸赛，为给乔家撑脸面，申简辰早早到来，他与梦容被安排在最利于观看比赛的位置。
Still turning over Wenyuan's strange stunt without making heads or tails of it, Qianli Xun heard Mengrong's cheerful voice call out. "There you are, Shuzhen! Hunted everywhere since arriving—found you at last!"

自进入，四处寻找千里寻的身影。捶丸场地倒挺大，依地势而建，四周有木质凉亭，供来客观看比赛。申简辰将场地瞧了个遍，都没看到千里寻的身影。
Everyone rose with bows. "Princess Jiahe."

正当无聊之际，见她与哥嫂一起到来，甚是开心。
"Quit being so stiff with me! Young Madam Zhu, Matron Wei, mind if I kidnap Shuzhen?" Already tugging Shuzhen's hand, Qiao Mengrong beamed.

“梦容，你的闺中密友来了。”
Trading warm smiles, Chen Wenxin and Matron Wei answered together, "How could we ever block Princess Jiahe's invitation?"

梦容也瞧去，的确是淑真：“哥哥，我去找淑真。”
"Thank you both!" Leading Shuzhen out, Qianli Xun waved Kuqiao and Hongdou back when both moved to follow. "Wait right here; no need to come." Both maids stayed put.

申简辰阻止道：“等会儿再去，他们刚来，乔家还得寒暄几句。”
Stepping out of the chuiwan lawn into the quiet gardens, Qiao Mengrong linked arms. "Can't stand chuiwan. Walk with me!"

果不其然，淑真坐下没多久，乔家人前去打招呼。直到见文渊去而离开后，申简辰已坐不住，告诉梦容可以去找他的闺中蜜友。
"Hate chuiwan, why even come?"

自从与千里寻相认，申简辰既开心又不开心。
"The Qiaos invited Brother, so he couldn't say no, and wherever Brother goes, I tag along!"

开心的是，千里寻的确对他与以往不同，与他说话放得更开，行礼也没以前那么谨慎，也不再误解他，让他感受到浓浓的朋友情谊。
Knowing Prince Yi was really Shen Jianchen had wiped out every bit of Qianli Xun's old resentment toward him as a fickle prince, leaving only deeper pity for Mengrong. "Mengrong... after Prince Yi revived forgetting everything before, did you ever wonder if he isn't the old Prince Yi anymore, if he's someone else altogether?"

不开心的是，他与千里寻的关系并未得到实质性进展，她在神柳下说过的话，还回荡在耳边。因为梦容，他无法放开手脚去爱千里寻，心里却难以放下她，无时无刻不念着。
"Someone else?" Flashing back to Brother's very first words upon waking—"I'm not Zhao Pu"—she pushed the thought miles away. "Impossible! Brother is still my Brother, just lost his memories!"

梦容去找千里寻，她们两个出了捶丸场，应该是去园林处赏景，迟迟不见回来。申简辰更坐不住，该找什么正当理由与千里寻搭话？
Better she believed that, Qianli Xun decided; long as she never knew about Shen Jianchen, Prince Yi stayed her devoted brother forever. "Just talking nonsense; don't mind me! Prince Yi's a good man, and you two are bound to be happy."

为难之际，钱氏身边的丫鬟站出来大声向众人说：“诸位！场上的捶丸爱好者均已热身，现在进入正式比赛环节，我家夫人说两人一队，男女不限，大家可以自由组队，彩头均由我家夫人出。第一场！翡翠玉镯一只！”
Stopping short to peer curious into Shuzhen's face, Mengrong asked, "How'd your opinion of Brother flip overnight?"

有下人用盘子端着一只翡翠玉镯，成色不错，对于在场的富贵人家来讲，并不会将彩头放在眼里，大家只图个乐子。
"Bad judge of character before. Came to see you know Prince Yi best after all."

申简辰倒开心，不如借此邀请千里寻出战。他对身边的白风说：“你不必跟着，我去找郡主。”
Though Mengrong had brushed off Shuzhen's past doubts, they had left quiet thorns worrying another woman might steal Brother away; hearing Shuzhen's praise now lifted every cloud clean. Strolling deeper through the blooming estate, spring pulsed everywhere, peach trees bursting into pink clouds even before green leaves unfolded.

白风明白，王爷的凉亭需有人守着，否则别人会以为王爷与郡主已离开。他便老实待着，向苦荞那边瞧去。
Down an intersecting walk approached Chen Haodong alongside Yan Chi. "Chuiwan tournament's underway out back, Brother Yan. Care to play a round?"

幽栖居士被郡主邀请已离开，苦荞没有跟着，倒便宜了白风，主人不在，他可以肆意与苦荞打招呼。
"Urgent errands waiting. I'll play with you once business is done." Suddenly Yan Chi froze, eyes locking straight onto the two ladies strolling ahead—one the exact woman from his street clash! Talk about wearing out iron sandals hunting in vain, only to stumble across treasure with zero effort! "Know those two ladies, Brother Chen?"

苦荞见白风向她挥手，也偷偷抬手回应，被韦嬷嬷瞧见。
Glancing over, Chen Haodong replied, "Left is Princess Jiahe; right is Prefect of Lin'an's daughter, Zhu Shuzhen."

“这小伙子不错，算你有眼光。”
"Zhu Shuzhen," Yan Chi rolled the name on his tongue. "So that's her name."

得到韦嬷嬷夸赞，苦荞红在脸上，喜在心里。
Reading clear intrigue on his friend's face, Chen Haodong grinned. "Caught Brother Yan's eye?"

韦嬷嬷瞧见身后的夏果，立刻拉下脸，刚要批评，陈浩宇走来，她不好再说什么。
"Nothing slips past you!" Yan Chi owned it brazen.

陈浩宇手拿球杆，满脸笑容，站在凉亭下，朝上面说：“姐！你跟我组队吧！”
Chen Haodong sighed. "Tough break—betrothed to my cousin Haoyu! Wedding's in one month."

陈文心笑着埋怨道：“你什么时候来的？也不知道过来跟我打个招呼，都是快成家的人，还这么不懂礼数？”
"So what? Anyone I want, even after she bears another man's kids, ends up mine sooner or later!"

“都是自家人，计较那么多干吗？快下来，跟我捶丸。”
"Bold spirit, Brother Yan!"

陈文心站起来，脱下披风，身边的丫鬟主动将襻膊替她绑好，再将球杆取了，走下凉亭，跟着亲弟弟来到比赛场地。
"Once she marries your cousin, keep sharp eyes on her for me."

他们的对手是乔文仙的二妹妹文秀与本家的一名公子，场上开始比赛，负责比赛的人将球放在球基处，两队开始轮流将球打向指定的球窝。
"Why?"

捶丸赛相比较马球赛而言，没那么激烈。看客们目的不在捶丸，而是借助捶丸赛，互相走动走动，寒暄寒暄，关系有了，或许大事也会办成。因此，看比赛的人少，互相走动的人多。
"Make sure she stays healthy and alive. Soon as my business wraps, I'm snatching her straight out of your cousin's hands!" Turning down a side trail, Yan Chi headed toward the exit.

场上捶丸的人并不理会他人是否关注，完全沉浸在比赛中，也只有他们能够感受到比赛的激烈。
After seeing Yan Chi off, Chen Haodong doubled back, spotted the two ladies still strolling, and stepped up bowing. "Princess Jiahe, Miss Zhu, if you tire of walking, tea pavilions await right over there! Should any servant in our Chen gardens fall short, tell me at once so I instruct the steward!"

韦嬷嬷无心看比赛，少夫人一离开，便对夏果说：“刚才少夫人在，我不便说什么，趁这空挡，我替姑娘向你传句话，乔家公子向姑娘提出纳你为妾，乔家老爷也同意，姑娘当然不会阻止，你如愿以偿，乔家举行婚礼那日，也会来望月轩用轿子把你抬走。”
Startled—neither recognized him, though his words pegged him as a Chen—Mengrong probed polite, "And you are...?"

夏果已知是这样的结果，亲耳听到，还是欣喜不已：“谢小姐，谢嬷嬷。”
"Chen Haodong, Haoyu's older cousin."

“不用谢我和姑娘，还是谢你自己会筹谋，三言两语激得乔公子说出纳你的话，只怕乔公子这会儿正后悔。”
Strangers—no need for small talk! Pulling gentle royal rank, Qiao Mengrong waved. "Don't stand on ceremony; tend your duties without minding us." Taking the crisp dismissal smooth, Chen Haodong bowed out. "At Princess Jiahe's command." Both girls resumed strolling amid spring blossoms, trading warm girl talk.

韦嬷嬷的话，夏果并未当回事，只要能跟喜欢的人在一起，什么都无所谓，再说乔公子无论家世，还是人品才学比林觉不知强多少倍。
Meanwhile, back at the chuiwan grounds, having arrived early to lend the Qiaos royal prestige, Shen Jianchen sat with Mengrong in the best viewing pavilion scanning the rolling field for Qianli Xun. Nothing turned up until she arrived at last alongside her brother and sister-in-law!

正想着，瞧见不远处，林觉身边多了一位女子，二人正走向对面的凉亭。
"Look, Mengrong, your best friend's here!"

夏果问道：“嬷嬷，林练兵官身边的女子是谁？”
Spotting Shuzhen, Mengrong jumped up. "Going to grab Shuzhen, Brother!"

“还练兵官呢！他已连升两级，现在是林统制，身边那位是新娶的夫人。”
"Give them a minute; the Qiaos need to greet them first." Sure enough, Qiao elders stepped up, and only after Wenyuan strode over and stalked off did restless Shen Jianchen let Mengrong go fetch her.

苦荞忍不住问：“新娶的夫人？是哪家姑娘？”
Since revealing his identity to Qianli Xun, joy and ache tugged him in two. He felt thrilled she talked breezy and real with him like old times, zero stiffness or misunderstandings, yet ached because her iron line beneath the Divine Willow had not budged an inch. With Mengrong standing between them, he could not chase her in the open, yet could not tear her out of his heart for a single breath.

“听说是隔了好几门子的亲戚，反正不是临安人。”
Watching Mengrong lead Qianli Xun off into the gardens and linger long made him antsy. What decent excuse got him over to talk? Right on cue, Madam Qian's maid stepped forward calling loud: "Attention all players! Warm-ups done—official matches start now! Madam sets teams of two, men or women mixed free, prizes courtesy of Madam! Round One prize: one fine emerald jade bangle!" A servant held up a glowing jade tray, pocket change to wealthy guests, pure sport.

“他们看上去挺不错，林统制应该不会再将春金巧带回府中了吧？”
Perfect opening to invite Qianli Xun onto the lawn! Telling Bai Feng, "Stay put, I'm going to fetch Princess," Shen Jianchen strolled off. Knowing an empty royal pavilion looked like they had departed, Bai Feng held the post—and seized the golden moment with both masters away to grin and wave right across the grass at Kuqiao! Catching Kuqiao sneaking a shy wave right back, Matron Wei smiled warm. "Solid lad—good eyes you've got!" Turning bright red to her ears, Kuqiao beamed inside.

苦荞不由为小姐感伤，林觉都已另娶，看上去还很幸福，自家小姐虽有婚约，可小姐并不中意，时刻想着退婚之事。
Then Matron Wei's gaze hit Xiaguo standing behind her and iced straight over, cut off before scolding as Chen Haoyu strolled up gripping a wooden club and grinning ear to ear beneath the pavilion steps. "Sis! Team up with me!"

韦嬷嬷说：“没听过有一句话吗？……”
"When'd you slink in without even saying hello?!" Chen Wenxin laughed scolding. "Almost a married man and still zero manners!"

夏果接道：“狗改不了吃屎！”
"Family—skip the rules! Come down and swing clubs with me!"

韦嬷嬷第一次对夏果露出笑容：“不错，倒说了句我爱听的话。”
Standing up, Chen Wenxin slipped off her cloak while her maid bound her wide sleeves tight with a sleeve-tying cord. Grabbing her club, she trotted down onto the lawn beside her brother. Facing Qiao Wenxian's younger sister Wenxiu paired with a Qiao cousin, both sides teed off from the starting mark taking turns driving balls toward the flagged hole. Far calmer than polo, chuiwan served most guests as a social playground; few kept score, crowds strolling pavilion to pavilion sealing friendships and deals, while players on the grass drowned out everything in fierce focus.

钱氏和米氏正与秦夫人坐在一处，苗知瑜挨母亲坐着，面对未来的婆婆，更表现出矜持。
Indifferent to the match, once Young Madam left, Matron Wei turned crisp to Xiaguo. "Didn't speak in front of Young Madam earlier. Passing Miss's word straight to you now: Young Master Qiao asked for you as concubine, Master Qiao consented, and Miss won't block you. Got your wish—on the Qiao wedding day, their sedan chair comes to Wangyue Pavilion for you."

钱氏看未来的儿媳妇，是越看越顺眼，米氏也笑得合不拢嘴。
Even knowing the outcome, hearing it confirmed made Xiaguo beam. "Thank you, Miss! Thank you, Matron!"

大家拉了一会儿家常话，秦夫人向朱家的凉亭瞅去，不见朱家姑娘。
"Save your thanks for your own scheming, winding Young Master Qiao up with two slick lies till he blurted it out! Bet he's kicking himself blind right now!"

“亲家可听说朱家将那位女词人许给陈家？”
Brushing off the jab—long as she won the man she wanted, nothing else mattered, and Young Master Qiao's lineage, grace, and learning crushed Lin Jue by a hundred miles—Xiaguo's eye caught a familiar man escorting a young woman toward an opposite pavilion. "Matron, who's that lady beside Training Officer Lin?"

钱氏说：“怎能没听说？也不懂知府大人是怎么想的，陈家虽好，可陈家二公子却不成器，把女儿嫁给他，恐怕还得闹到和离的份儿。”
"Training Officer? Jumped two ranks—Division Commander Lin now! And that's his new bride."

米氏搭腔道：“我猜肯定是因为朱家姑娘名声不好，知府大人才会同意。”
Kuqiao blinked curious. "New bride? From which house?"

“不管因为什么，只要别影响文渊便好。”
"Distant relative from outside Lin'an, word says."

钱氏的话令秦夫人心中欢喜。
"They look happy enough. Guess Division Commander Lin quit hauling Chun Jinqiao home?" A quiet sting pricked Kuqiao for Miss: even Lin Jue had remarried smiling while Miss fought tooth and nail to escape an unwanted match.

钱氏又瞅瞅苗知瑜，向丫鬟吩咐：“去把公子寻来。”
Matron Wei scoffed. "Ever heard the old saying?..."

丫鬟领命而去。
Xiaguo finished crisp, "A dog can't quit eating filth!"

一时失去理智，问淑真要了夏果，等清醒过来，乔文仙后悔不已。不愿与任何人交流，一个人坐在捶丸场角落的石头上，垂头丧气。
For the very first time, a genuine smile cracked Matron Wei's face toward Xiaguo. "Fair point—said one line I like at last!"

南双因公子主动要纳夏果为妾，不愿与公子说话，站在一旁，黯然伤神。
Across the lawn, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi sat chatting warm with Madam Qin while poised Miao Zhiyu stayed demure beside her mother, drawing fond grins from her future mother-in-law and beaming smiles from Concubine Mi. Glancing over toward the Zhu pavilion and noticing Miss Zhu absent, Madam Qin asked offhand, "Hear the Zhus betrothed that ci poet to the Chen family, in-law?"

钱氏身边的丫鬟走过来：“公子，夫人叫你过去。”
"Who hasn't heard?" Madam Qian sniffed. "No clue what Prefect Zhu's thinking. Wealthy house, sure, yet their second son's a hopeless scamp! Marry her over there and they'll end up in mutual divorce all over again!"

乔文仙不言语。
Concubine Mi chimed in, "Bet Prefect Zhu settled just because her reputation's shot!"

丫鬟又强调一遍：“公子，夫人叫你过去。”
"Whatever their reason, long as she stays clear of Wenyuan!" Madam Qian's words warmed Madam Qin straight through. Glancing fond at Miao Zhiyu, Madam Qian told a maid, "Go fetch Young Master over."

乔文仙才站起来，没有回答，径直朝钱氏的凉亭走去。南双再难过也得跟上，他已决定，要彻底把夏果从心里清除掉。
Meanwhile, cooling down after his rash fit demanding Xiaguo, bitter regret choked Qiao Wenxian. Shunning every guest, he sat hunched alone on a boulder at the lawn's edge while heart-sore Nan Shuang stood silent beside him, refusing to speak after Master claimed the very girl Nan Shuang liked.

丫鬟对自家公子的表现并不感到奇怪，自从与苗家定亲后，公子一直如此。
Madam Qian's maid hurried up. "Young Master, Madam summons you."

乔文仙走到钱氏的凉亭内，行礼道：“母亲找我何事？”
Silence. Repeating once more—"Young Master, Madam calls for you"—she watched him rise mute and stalk straight toward Madam Qian's pavilion, glum Nan Shuang trailing behind swearing to wipe Xiaguo clean from his heart. Used to Young Master's bleak gloom ever since the Miao betrothal, the maid did not bat an eye.

钱氏笑道：“知瑜也来了一会儿，园林内风景不错，你带她去逛逛。”
Stepping into Madam Qian's pavilion, Qiao Wenxian bowed stiff. "You called for me, Mother?"

乔文仙没立刻回应，而是说道：“有一事需告知母亲，儿子决定纳幽栖居士身边的丫鬟夏果为妾，父亲已点头答应。”
Madam Qian smiled warm. "Zhiyu's been sitting long enough. Lovely spring gardens out there—walk her around!"

钱氏愣了一下，瞧一眼米氏。
Skipping any reply to that, Qiao Wenxian announced flat, "One matter to report, Mother: your son has decided to take Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo as concubine, and Father already consented."

米氏尴尬地笑道：“我当什么呢，纳个把妾室没什么，秦夫人，您说呢？”
Frozen a split second, Madam Qian glanced over at Concubine Mi, who forced an awkward chuckle. "Thought it was big news! Taking a concubine or two is nothing, right, Madam Qin?"

秦夫人当然不爽，可乔家是皇亲国戚，能看上自家女儿，苗家也算高攀，她只能露出笑容：“男子纳几个妾室，无伤大雅，只盼妾室能像米姨娘这般好相处，我家知瑜嫁过去不至于被欺负。”
Burning inside yet swallowing pride before imperial kin—marrying into the Qiao house lifted the Miaos high—Madam Qin pasted on a polite smile. "No harm in a gentleman taking a concubine or two. Only hope she's sweet-tempered as Concubine Mi so my Zhiyu won't suffer grief!"

钱氏说：“知瑜嫁过来，是乔家未来的主母，谁敢欺负，我第一个饶不了她。”
"Zhiyu enters as future head matriarch of Qiao Manor," Madam Qian declared firm. "Whoever dares cross her answers to me first!"

苗知瑜还未过门，就有人前来抢夫君，她心中着实不快。听了钱氏的话，心情稍微好些。
Stung sour having another woman claim a slice of her groom before she even crossed his threshold, Miao Zhiyu felt Madam Qian's iron promise soothe at least a fraction of her chill.
