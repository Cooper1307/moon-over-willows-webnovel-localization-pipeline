## Chapter 160: Chapter 160: Chuiwan and Idle Talk, Each Mind Elsewhere

Still turning over Wenyuan's strange stunt without making heads or tails of it, Qianli Xun heard Mengrong's cheerful voice call out: "There you are, Shuzhen! Hunted everywhere since arriving—found you at last!"

Everyone rose with bows: "Princess Jiahe."

"Quit being so stiff with me! Young Madam Zhu, Matron Wei—mind if I kidnap Shuzhen?" Already tugging Shuzhen's hand, Qiao Mengrong beamed.

Trading warm smiles, Chen Wenxin and Matron Wei answered together: "How could we ever block Princess Jiahe's invitation?"

"Thank you both!" Leading Shuzhen out, Qianli Xun waved Kuqiao and Hongdou back when both moved to follow: "Wait right here; no need to come." Both maids stayed put.

Stepping out of the chuiwan lawn into the quiet gardens, Qiao Mengrong linked arms: "Can't stand chuiwan—walk with me!"

"Hate chuiwan, why even come?"

"Qiaos invited Brother, couldn't say no—and wherever Brother goes, I tag along!"

Knowing Prince Yi was really Shen Jianchen had wiped out every bit of Qianli Xun's old resentment toward him as a fickle prince—leaving only deeper pity for Mengrong. "Mengrong... after Prince Yi revived forgetting everything before, did you ever wonder if he isn't the old Prince Yi anymore—if he's someone else altogether?"

"Someone else?" Flashing back to Brother's very first words upon waking—"I'm not Zhao Pu"—she pushed the thought miles away: "Impossible! Brother is still my Brother—just lost his memories!"

Better she believed that, Qianli Xun decided; long as she never knew about Shen Jianchen, Prince Yi stayed her devoted brother forever. "Just talking nonsense—don't mind me! Prince Yi's a good man; you two are bound to be happy."

Stopping short to peer curious into Shuzhen's face, Mengrong asked: "How'd your opinion of Brother flip overnight?"

"Bad judge of character before—came to see you know Prince Yi best after all."

Though Mengrong had brushed off Shuzhen's past doubts, they had left quiet thorns worrying another woman might steal Brother away; hearing Shuzhen's praise now lifted every cloud clean!

Strolling deeper through the blooming estate, spring pulsed everywhere—peach trees bursting into pink clouds even before green leaves unfolded!

Down an intersecting walk approached Chen Haodong alongside Yan Chi. "Chuiwan tournament's underway out back, Brother Yan—care to play a round?"

"Urgent errands waiting—play with you once business is done." Suddenly Yan Chi froze, eyes locking straight onto the two ladies strolling ahead—one the exact woman from his street clash! Talk about wearing out iron sandals hunting in vain, only to stumble across treasure effortless! "Know those two ladies, Brother Chen?"

Glancing over, Chen Haodong replied: "Left is Princess Jiahe; right is Prefect of Lin'an's daughter, Zhu Shuzhen."

"Zhu Shuzhen," Yan Chi rolled the name on his tongue. "So that's her name."

Reading clear intrigue on his friend's face, Chen Haodong grinned: "Caught Brother Yan's eye?"

"Nothing slips past you!" Yan Chi owned it brazen.

Chen Haodong sighed: "Tough break—betrothed to my cousin Haoyu! Wedding's in one month."

"So what? Anyone I want, even after she bears another man's kids, ends up mine sooner or later!"

"Bold spirit, Brother Yan!"

"Once she marries your cousin, keep sharp eyes on her for me."

"Why?"

"Make sure she stays healthy and alive—soon as my business wraps, I'm snatching her straight out of your cousin's hands!" Turning down a side trail, Yan Chi headed toward the exit.

After seeing Yan Chi off, Chen Haodong doubled back, spotted the two ladies still strolling, and stepped up bowing: "Princess Jiahe, Miss Zhu—if you tire of walking, tea pavilions await right over there! Should any servant in our Chen gardens fall short, tell me at once so I instruct the steward!"

Startled—neither recognized him, though his words pegged him as a Chen—Mengrong probed polite: "And you are...?"

"Chen Haodong, Haoyu's older cousin."

Strangers—no need for small talk! Pulling gentle royal rank, Qiao Mengrong waved: "Don't stand on ceremony; tend your duties without minding us." Taking the crisp dismissal smooth, Chen Haodong bowed out: "At Princess Jiahe's command." Both girls resumed strolling amid spring blossoms, trading warm girl talk.

Meanwhile, back at the chuiwan grounds, having arrived early to lend the Qiaos royal prestige, Shen Jianchen sat with Mengrong in the best viewing pavilion scanning the rolling field for Qianli Xun—nothing until she arrived at last alongside her brother and sister-in-law!

"Look, Mengrong—your best friend's here!"

Spotting Shuzhen, Mengrong jumped up: "Going to grab Shuzhen, Brother!"

"Give them a minute—Qiaos need to greet them first." Sure enough, Qiao elders stepped up—and only after Wenyuan strode over and stalked off did restless Shen Jianchen let Mengrong go fetch her.

Since revealing his identity to Qianli Xun, joy and ache tugged him in two: thrilled she talked breezy and real with him like old times, zero stiffness or misunderstandings; aching because her iron line beneath the Divine Willow hadn't budged an inch—with Mengrong standing between them, he couldn't chase her open, yet couldn't tear her out of his heart for a single breath!

Watching Mengrong lead Qianli Xun off into the gardens and linger long made him antsy—what decent excuse got him over to talk? Right on cue, Madam Qian's maid stepped forward calling loud: "Attention all players! Warm-ups done—official matches start now! Madam sets teams of two, men or women mixed free, prizes courtesy of Madam! Round One prize: one fine emerald jade bangle!" A servant held up a glowing jade tray—pocket change to wealthy guests, pure sport!

Perfect opening to invite Qianli Xun onto the lawn! Telling Bai Feng "Stay put—going to fetch Princess," Shen Jianchen strolled off. Knowing an empty royal pavilion looked like they had departed, Bai Feng held the post—and seized the golden moment with both masters away to grin and wave openly across the grass at Kuqiao! Catching Kuqiao sneaking a shy wave right back, Matron Wei smiled warm: "Solid lad—good eyes you've got!" Turning bright red to her ears, Kuqiao beamed inside.

Then Matron Wei's gaze hit Xiaguo standing behind her and iced straight over—cut off before scolding as Chen Haoyu strolled up gripping a wooden club and grinning ear to ear beneath the pavilion steps: "Sis! Team up with me!"

"When'd you slink in without even saying hello?!" Chen Wenxin laughed scolding. "Almost a married man and still zero manners!"

"Family—skip the rules! Come down and swing clubs with me!"

Standing up, Chen Wenxin slipped off her cloak while her maid bound her wide sleeves tight with a sleeve-tying cord; grabbing her club, she trotted down onto the lawn beside her brother! Facing Qiao Wenxian's younger sister Wenxiu paired with a Qiao cousin, both sides teed off from the starting mark taking turns driving balls toward the flagged hole. Far calmer than polo, chuiwan served most guests as a social playground—few kept score, crowds strolling pavilion to pavilion sealing friendships and deals, while players on the grass drowned out everything in fierce focus!

Indifferent to the match, once Young Madam left, Matron Wei turned crisp to Xiaguo: "Didn't speak in front of Young Madam earlier—passing Miss's word straight to you now: Young Master Qiao asked for you as concubine, Master Qiao consented, and Miss won't block you. Got your wish—on the Qiao wedding day, their sedan chair comes to Wangyue Pavilion for you."

Even knowing the outcome, hearing it confirmed made Xiaguo beam: "Thank you, Miss! Thank you, Matron!"

"Save your thanks for your own scheming—winding Young Master Qiao up with two slick lies till he blurted it out! Bet he's kicking himself blind right now!"

Brushing off the jab—long as she won the man she wanted, nothing else mattered, and Young Master Qiao's lineage, grace, and learning crushed Lin Jue by a hundred miles!—Xiaguo's eye suddenly caught a familiar man escorting a young woman toward an opposite pavilion: "Matron, who's that lady beside Training Officer Lin?"

"Training Officer? Jumped two ranks—Division Commander Lin now! And that's his new bride."

Kuqiao blinked curious: "New bride? From which house?"

"Distant relative from outside Lin'an, word says."

"Look happy enough—guess Division Commander Lin quit hauling Chun Jinqiao home?" A quiet sting pricked Kuqiao for Miss—even Lin Jue had remarried smiling while Miss fought tooth and nail to escape an unwanted match!

Matron Wei scoffed: "Ever heard the old saying?..."

Xiaguo finished crisp: "A dog can't quit eating filth!"

For the very first time, a genuine smile cracked Matron Wei's face toward Xiaguo: "Fair point—finally said one line I like!"

Across the lawn, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi sat chatting warm with Madam Qin while poised Miao Zhiyu stayed demure beside her mother—drawing fond grins from her future mother-in-law and beaming smiles from Concubine Mi. Glancing over toward the Zhu pavilion and noticing Miss Zhu absent, Madam Qin asked casual: "Hear the Zhus betrothed that ci poet to the Chen family, in-law?"

"Who hasn't heard?" Madam Qian sniffed. "No clue what Prefect Zhu's thinking—wealthy house, sure, yet their second son's a hopeless scamp! Marry her over there and they'll end up in mutual divorce all over again!"

Concubine Mi chimed in: "Bet Prefect Zhu settled purely because her reputation's shot!"

"Whatever their reason, long as she stays clear of Wenyuan!" Madam Qian's words warmed Madam Qin straight through. Glancing fondly at Miao Zhiyu, Madam Qian told a maid: "Go fetch Young Master over."

Meanwhile, cooling down after his rash fit demanding Xiaguo, bitter regret choked Qiao Wenxian; shunning every guest, he sat hunched alone on a boulder at the lawn's edge while heart-sore Nan Shuang stood silent beside him, refusing to speak after Master claimed the very girl Nan Shuang liked.

Madam Qian's maid hurried up: "Young Master, Madam summons you."

Silence. Repeating once more—"Young Master, Madam calls for you"—she watched him rise mute and stalk straight toward Madam Qian's pavilion, glum Nan Shuang trailing behind swearing to wipe Xiaguo clean from his heart. Used to Young Master's bleak gloom ever since the Miao betrothal, the maid didn't bat an eye.

Stepping into Madam Qian's pavilion, Qiao Wenxian bowed stiff: "You called for me, Mother?"

Madam Qian smiled warm: "Zhiyu's been sitting long enough—lovely spring gardens out there, walk her around!"

Skipping any reply to that, Qiao Wenxian announced flat: "One matter to report, Mother: your son has decided to take Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo as concubine, and Father already consented."

Frozen a split second, Madam Qian glanced over at Concubine Mi, who forced an awkward chuckle: "Thought it was big news! Taking a concubine or two is nothing—right, Madam Qin?"

Burning inside yet swallowing pride before imperial kin—marrying into the Qiao house lifted the Miaos high—Madam Qin pasted on a polite smile: "No harm in a gentleman taking a concubine or two—only hope she's sweet-tempered as Concubine Mi so my Zhiyu won't suffer grief!"

"Zhiyu enters as future head matriarch of Qiao Manor," Madam Qian declared firm. "Whoever dares cross her answers to me first!"

Stung sour having another woman claim a slice of her groom before she even crossed his threshold, Miao Zhiyu felt Madam Qian's iron promise soothe at least a fraction of her chill.
