## Chapter 325: Changing Winds After Prince Yi Leaves

Lingering five days in Lin'an once his bruises healed, Liang Shicheng gutted every honest official loyal to Prince Yi—sparing Master Jiang after he gifted three brothel courtesans posing as married wives, and showering massive rewards on his star sycophants: promoting Lin Jue two full ranks to **Commander-in-Chief of All Garrisons** and throwing a lavish banquet hailing **Chun Jinqiao** as a "model virtuous woman," forcing nauseated Bai Meiruo to sing her praises publicly (her persistent nausea promptly revealing she was one month pregnant—saving her primary-wife seat even as Lin Jue granted Jinqiao equal footing indoors).

Meeting at Yongtai Teahouse—both pregnant now—Bai Meiruo and Miao Zhiyu gleefully tore into shameless Chun Jinqiao and her spineless husband, realizing how shallow their old jealousy of Youqi Jushi had been and pledging to become her true bosom friends the day she returned safely to Lin'an.

Meanwhile, across ten icy days on the road to Bianliang, Shen Jianchen ignored blank-staring Mengrong completely—making Haoyu nervous that the prince might try stealing his wife once they reached the capital.

The very night they settled into Prince Yi's Manor (Shen Jianchen insisting Haoyu stay there), Shen Jianchen summoned Matron Zou and Xilan behind closed doors and forced out the entire truth—from Mengrong's earliest plots all the way to Liang Shicheng's sickening needle assault at Liu'an Villa!

Flipping his desk in roaring fury—*"I'll gut Liang Shicheng with my own hands!"*—Shen Jianchen handed down his frigid verdict: he'd keep feeding and treating Mengrong out of debt to the past, yet never step foot in her courtyard again.

Strolling through dry autumn leaves to cool his head before tomorrow's palace appeal, he passed Bai Feng stealing a shy kiss from Kuqiao in the dark—and bumped straight into sleepless Chen Haoyu.
