## Chapter 315: Mengrong Defiled by Imperial Attendant Liang

The brocade box slipped from her hands and clattered onto the floor. Qiao Mengrong backed away in revulsion. “Back in Bianliang, you loved silver well enough!”

Liang Shicheng stalked closer. “Back then you weren’t married—you had zero appeal to me. I only took your silver to keep you calm. Now you’re wed, prettier than ever, and you walked right into my lap begging for help. Think I’d let a prize like you slip away?” Reaching out, he stroked Qiao Mengrong’s cheek.

She slapped his hand away in disgust, her voice shaking. “I… I am Princess Consort Yi! How dare you make… make such a filthy demand of me!”

“I don’t even put Prince Yi in my eyes—why would I care about his consort?”

“I’m leaving! I don’t want your help anymore!” Qiao Mengrong bolted toward the door.

Liang Shicheng cut straight across her path. “Think I’d ever let a chance this good walk out the door?”

Desperate, she threatened him. “Touch me, and my brother… my brother will never let you off!”

“You won’t tell Prince Yi—you’re terrified he’ll look at you with disgust if he finds out. Just you and me know. No third soul will ever hear a word, so why put up this coy act?”

Putting every ounce of strength into her arms, Qiao Mengrong shoved Liang Shicheng aside and lunged for the door. Quick as a snake, he grabbed her arm and wrenched her straight into his chest.

“You’re not escaping my grip.”

She thrashed to break free, yet one arm clamped tight around her throat while his other hand groped across her body, pinning her helpless.

Tears welled hot in her eyes. “Please… let me go…”

“While Prince Yi lay sick, I worried you’d wind up living a chaste widow’s life—looking all pure and untouched like ice. I even considered having someone take your maidenhead first so you’d taste sweeter for me later. Who knew Prince Yi would suddenly recover and you’d beg to move up the wedding? How could I say no? Two words from me swayed His Majesty, and you got your wedding right on cue. That night you married, I lay awake thrilled half to death, picturing you as my own bride. Care to guess why I came all the way down to Lin’an?”

Never in her wildest nightmares had Qiao Mengrong imagined the man had been stalking her all along. Fury at her own blindness burned her throat—she had walked step by step straight into the wolf’s jaws.

Sick with dread and regret, she couldn’t choke out a word.

“All for you! I whispered in His Majesty’s ear, spiced up everything Prince Yi was doing in Lin’an, got His Majesty steaming mad, and got myself sent down here to clean house. I knew the second you heard I was in town, you’d come running to me. So restoring Prince Yi’s post? Nothing more than a single word from my lips.”

Knowing she was the very reason her brother lost his post tore fresh tears down Qiao Mengrong’s cheeks. “How did you know what my brother was doing in Lin’an?”

“Hehehe… I’ve got eyes inside Lin’an’s prefecture office writing me letters on everything happening here. Did you think I wouldn’t know?”

Hands roaming wildly over her body, Liang Shicheng trapped her as Qiao Mengrong stared hopelessly at the bolted door and scanned the room—no escape anywhere.

Blaming herself twisted straight into murderous hatred for Zhu Shuzhen. That woman had driven her corner by corner into this hell. Zhu Shuzhen had to die!

“Can you truly kill her for me?”

Hearing the fight go out of her voice, Liang Shicheng kissed her cheek. “Keep me happy today, and I guarantee I’ll scatter her soul to the winds.”

“Don’t lie to me!”

“Why would I ever lie to a beauty who’s haunted my dreams?”

Qiao Mengrong squeezed her eyes shut as two heavy tears rolled down her cheeks. “Fine… I agree. Only swear to me you’ll never breathe a single syllable of today to my brother.”

Loosening his grip, Liang Shicheng stripped off her outer robe. “Relax. Prince Yi will never know a thing.”

With her outer garment pooling on the floor, he stepped back, walked to the table, lifted a wine pot in one hand and a cup in the other, poured himself wine, and barked, “Get in here!”

Four women in skimpy, revealing clothes stepped out at once.

Liang Shicheng ordered, “Strip every stitch off her—leave her stark naked—and make her dance for me. Nothing I enjoy more than sipping wine while watching women dance.”

Tears streaming down her face, Qiao Mengrong stood numb while the four women peeled off her clothes, then forced herself to sway and twist like them in a degrading dance.

Liang Shicheng didn’t even sit; prowling around her with a leering grin, he drank wine and feasted his eyes.

When the pot ran dry, he flung pot and cup crashing onto the floor. At once the four women stopped, grabbed Qiao Mengrong, and pinned her flat onto the boards.

Part of her wanted to fight, yet everything had already gone too far—she’d been stripped bare before his eyes anyway. All she could do was shut her eyes tight.

“Hold her arms and legs down steady,” Liang Shicheng ordered, stalking closer. “I’m no whole man, true, yet I’ve got my own way of finding bliss. I’m going to kiss you from head to toe, and everywhere my lips touch, I’ll drive a needle in. The worse it hurts, the louder you scream, the happier I get—and I won’t stop until I reach pure heaven.”

At the word “needle,” Qiao Mengrong’s eyes flew open. Seeing Liang Shicheng looming over her like a demon clutching a steel needle between his fingers, cold sweat broke out across her skin. “Please! Spare me! Let me go! Wuu…”

Crouching beside her, Liang Shicheng spoke in a gentle, chilling purr. “We’ve come this far—why on earth would I stop now? Let this needle show you the might of a man. Scream when it stings; the louder, the better.”

“You… monster!”

“Curse all you like! I used to be a proper boy till palace life left me neither man nor woman. Living in a twisted world, I learned to twist along with it. If you want someone to blame, curse His Majesty, not me.”

With that, Liang Shicheng pressed his lips to Qiao Mengrong’s shoulder—followed instantly by a piercing stab that tore a shriek from her throat: “Aaah!”

Closing his eyes to savor the rush of her scream, Liang Shicheng leaned down, kissed another spot, and ruthlessly drove the needle in again.

Outside, hearing her mistress’s cries, Xilan pounded frantically on the door. “Your Highness! What’s wrong? Open the door! Open up!”

A guard stepped over and pressed a naked blade against Xilan’s neck. “Keep yelling and I’ll slit your throat!”

The razor-sharp edge grazed her skin; one slip would draw blood. Terrified, Xilan froze.

“Wait quietly in the courtyard and stay out of the Lord Attendant’s fun!”

Forced back at swordpoint, Xilan retreated into the yard while the guard sheathed his blade and kept watch over her.

Piercing screams continued pouring through the walls, each louder than the last, twisting Xilan’s stomach into knots. Hearing the guard’s words about “fun,” she guessed well enough what awful fate Princess Consort Yi was suffering inside.

Crouching on the flagstones, Xilan clapped both hands over her ears, yet her mistress’s shrieks cut straight through anyway.

After what felt like ages, the screaming died down. A little while later, the door bolt finally slid back.

Xilan scrambled up and stared at the doorway.

Princess Consort Yi stumbled out. Her clothes were buttoned neat enough, yet her hair hung tangled, her face looked white as chalk, and her eyelids burned red and swollen—she swayed like a ghost ready to collapse.

Xilan rushed up to catch her. “Your Highness! What happened?”

Qiao Mengrong mumbled blankly, “I want to go home. Take me home right now…”

“Right away, Your Highness.” Seeing her mistress shaken to her wits’ end, Xilan’s heart ached with dread.

Halfway across the yard, one of the scantily clad women trotted up behind them holding out the brocade box. “The Lord Attendant says Princess Consort Yi performed wonderfully—and he doesn’t want a single one of your silver notes.”

“I want to go home, I want to go home…” Qiao Mengrong kept repeating.

Snatching the box, Xilan half-carried her mistress out of Liu’an Villa.

Inside the carriage, Xilan smoothed down her mistress’s tangled hair, weeping softly. “Had I known, I never would have brought Your Highness to meet Imperial Attendant Liang.”

Tear streaks staining her deathly pale cheeks, Qiao Mengrong murmured in a hollowed voice, “Don’t tell Brother.”

Wiping her eyes, Xilan promised, “Never. If we told him, I’m afraid… His Highness might turn away from you in disgust.”

Qiao Mengrong longed to wail out loud, yet fearing the coachman would hear, she bit down and swallowed the sobs—shaking violently from head to toe inside.

Panic-stricken, Xilan wrapped her arms around her mistress, mistress and maid clutching each other and trembling together inside the swaying carriage.

Back at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong forced herself to walk steadily. Supported by Xilan, she stopped outside the courtyard where her brother slept. After a long pause, she whispered, “Take me to my old courtyard.”

Understanding why, Xilan didn’t argue a word.

Once inside her old room, Qiao Mengrong finally let out a whimper of pain and told Xilan to fetch salve for her skin.

When Xilan helped her out of her clothes and saw dozens of tiny needle punctures speckling her shoulders and back, her jaw nearly hit the floor. She had assumed Imperial Attendant Liang had simply forced himself on her—never imagining the depraved cruelty behind those screams.

“Don’t… don’t breathe a word,” Qiao Mengrong choked out. “Not even to Matron Zou.”

Weeping, Xilan nodded and dabbed salve over every red puncture mark. “Your Highness, never go near Imperial Attendant Liang again for that poetess’s sake. He’s pure scum!”

“Never again. Not ever. He gave me his word he’ll kill her—she has to die! Otherwise what did I endure all this for…”

Unable to finish, Qiao Mengrong ignored Xilan’s hands still tending her back, flung herself face-down onto the bed, buried her face in the pillow, and broke into racking sobs.

When night fell and Mengrong still hadn’t returned, Shen Jianchen grew uneasy and started toward the main gate to check.

Just then Xilan walked up and curtsied. “Your Highness, Her Highness came back feeling unwell and wishes to rest quietly in her old courtyard for a few days.”

Shen Jianchen frowned in concern. “Let me go look at her.”

Xilan stepped in his path. “Her Highness is already asleep, Your Highness. Go tomorrow.”

Suspicion flickered across his mind. “Did something happen to her while she was out?”

Xilan lied smoothly. “Nothing at all—just a common women’s ailment, a discharge issue that leaves her uncomfortable. She wants to sleep in separate quarters for a few days until she feels better, then she’ll keep you company again.”

“In that case I won’t disturb her. Take good care of her.”

“Yes, Your Highness.”

Breathing a quiet sigh of relief, Xilan curtsied and slipped away.

...

The very next day, Liang Shicheng turned up at Youlin Retreat dressed in crisp, formal robes to pay a call on Prince Yi.

Shen Jianchen received him from the seat of honor with a thundercloud on his face.

Offering the briefest bow, Liang Shicheng helped himself to a chair uninvited, showing open contempt for the prince.

“Does Your Highness know why I’ve come calling?”

“Spare me the riddles, Imperial Attendant Liang. State your business and be quick about it.”

“First, congratulations, Your Highness.”

“What is there to congratulate?”

“Your Highness may keep serving as Military Commissioner of Lin’an. Take up the post calmly for now—once I return to the capital, I’ll put in plenty of good words before His Majesty, and he’s sure to rescind his verbal decree.”

A key post stripped away on a crooked attendant’s whim, then handed right back the next day—how far gone was the emperor sitting on the dragon throne? Shen Jianchen had zero desire to lift a finger for an incompetent sovereign.

“Hmph! So that’s all. Save yourself the trouble, Imperial Attendant Liang. I am Prince Yi—my title as an imperial prince outranks some petty Military Commissioner of Lin’an ten times over. Think I care about a minor post? Carry word back to Bianliang for my imperial father: I prefer staying a leisurely prince.”

Prince Yi’s blunt refusal caught Liang Shicheng off guard; apparently, Princess Consort Yi’s begging yesterday had been entirely her own idea.

“How remarkably detached Your Highness is—truly admirable. Very well, the Lin’an Military Commissioner post can go to someone else. Still, once back in Bianliang, I’ll praise Your Highness to His Majesty anyway—seeing how fortunate you were to wed such an extraordinary princess consort.”

Shen Jianchen sharpened his gaze. “What is that supposed to mean?”

“Oh, nothing—only how touching her devotion was while you lay sick. A woman like that is rare in this world. For her sake, naturally I’ll help Your Highness.” Smoothly covering his slip, Liang Shicheng stood and bowed. “Your servant takes his leave.”

Making no sense of the man’s cryptic remark, Shen Jianchen kept his dark scowl. Once Liang left, he instructed Bai Feng, “Tell Butler Li: if that man shows up again, say I’m not home.”

“Yes, Your Highness.” Bai Feng hurried off to pass the order along.

When word reached the inner quarters that Imperial Attendant Liang had come to Youlin Retreat, Xilan mentioned it to her mistress. Lying in bed, Qiao Mengrong went pale with terror and pointed straight at the door.

“Hurry! Shut the doors! Don’t let him inside!”

Xilan scrambled to bolt both the courtyard gate and the bedchamber door before rushing back to soothe her. “He only came to see His Highness, Your Highness—he won’t bother you.”

Panic clawed at Qiao Mengrong. “What if he tells Brother what happened yesterday? Go find out right now what he wanted with Brother!”

Agreeing, Xilan hurried outside and bumped right into Bai Feng.

“Bai Feng! Where are you off to?”

Hearing his name, Bai Feng stopped and turned. “Looking for Butler Li.”

Stepping closer, Xilan asked, “I heard Imperial Attendant Liang came to see His Highness. Her Highness is worried and sent me to ask what it was about.”

“Tell Her Highness not to worry. Imperial Attendant Liang just came to ask His Highness to take back the Military Commissioner post, and His Highness turned him down flat, so he left. His Highness even told me to have Butler Li turn Liang away next time he comes calling.”

Nothing about yesterday—thank Heaven! Xilan let out a huge breath. “Go on then.”

Once Bai Feng trotted off, Xilan hurried back to the small courtyard to tell Princess Consort Yi.

Her fear easing, Qiao Mengrong lay back down. So her brother couldn’t care less about the Military Commissioner title—meaning half the reason she had sacrificed her own body had been thrown away for nothing. Hot tears spilled down her cheeks.

All at once, sharp cramps tore through her lower belly, doubling Qiao Mengrong over.

Alarmed, Xilan rushed to the bed. “What’s wrong, Your Highness?”

“I don’t know—my belly hurts terribly! Rub it for me!”

Throwing back the quilt to massage her mistress’s stomach, Xilan froze at the sight: dark red blood was pooling from beneath Princess Consort Yi, soaking straight through her trousers and the mattress pad.

Xilan shrieked in terror, “Mother of Heaven! Where did all this blood come from?! I’m getting His Highness!”
