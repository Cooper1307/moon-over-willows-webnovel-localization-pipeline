## Chapter 315: Mengrong Falls Prey to Imperial Attendant Liang

The brocade box crashed to the floor as Mengrong stumbled back in terror, stammering her royal title—only for Liang Shicheng to laugh coldly that he didn't fear Prince Yi, let alone his wife, and snag her arm before she reached the door.

Pinned against his chest, she heard the horrifying truth: he had lusted after her back in Bianliang when she was unwed, pushed His Majesty to move her wedding up solely so she'd become a married woman, and even slandered Prince Yi to get himself sent to Lin'an knowing she'd walk straight into his trap!

Trapped behind bolted doors with escape impossible, Mengrong channelled every shred of terror and ruin straight into venom for Zhu Shuzhen. *"You swear you'll kill her—and never breathe a word to Brother?!"*

"Word of honor—she'll vanish without a trace!"

Tears spilling down her cheeks, Mengrong closed her eyes and surrendered.

Clapping for four scantily clad women, Liang Shicheng ordered them to strip Mengrong completely bare and force her to dance while he drank—then hold her limbs down as he pulled out a fine sewing needle, kissing her trembling flesh and jabbing deep wherever his lips touched, reveling in every agonizing shriek!

Outside, Xilan hammered the door frantically until a palace guard pressed a blade to her throat and forced her to crouch with hands over her ears listening to her mistress's screams.

At last the doors opened. Pale, disheveled, and barely able to stand, Mengrong stumbled out muttering *"Take me home..."* as a maid tossed back her untouched box of banknotes. Back at Youlin Retreat, refusing to enter Brother's courtyard lest he spot the clustered needle punctures across her shoulders and back, she hid in her old side courtyard sobbing into her pillow while Xilan gently dabbed ointment on every tiny wound—swearing Xilan to silence even from Matron Zou, and telling Shen Jianchen she merely had a mild women's ailment.

Next morning, Liang Shicheng breezed into Youlin Retreat offering to restore Shen Jianchen's Military Commissioner post—only for Shen Jianchen to disdain the petty sinecure flatly and bar Butler Li from ever letting the eunuch inside again!

Learning Brother didn't even *want* the post made Mengrong weep bitterly over half her sacrifice going to waste—until a sudden razor-sharp cramp doubled her over on the mattress, and Xilan pulled back the quilt to find crimson blood soaking clean through her clothes and bedding!
