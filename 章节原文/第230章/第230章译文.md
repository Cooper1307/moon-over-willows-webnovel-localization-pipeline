## Chapter 230: Qinggua Seduces Chouya

Catching Yao Qingyan's panicked look, Chen Jianglin cleared his throat to reassure both his wife and his daughter-in-law. "Since marrying in, Shuzhen helped us three brothers finally understand Mother's heart. That proves she is thoughtful and deeply kind."

"Deeply kind" echoed Matron Wei's exact words from yesterday, easing the knot in Yao Qingyan's chest. Right—Shuzhen isn't cruel; why am I panicking?

"Nothing is impossible to a willing heart," Chen Jianglin went on. "I trust you, Shuzhen. Even if you can't reform him, I won't blame you or regret it. Either he shapes up and we all rejoice, or he doesn't and I wash my hands of him—every child walks his own fate. All I ask is that after I'm gone, whoever takes the helm of the Chen family leaves Haoyu a warm bowl of rice, and that you don't abandon him."

Fair and heartfelt words—leaving Qianli Xun unable to keep bristling. Chen Haoyu was the villain, not these elders; no sense taking her rage out on them.

Chen Jiangshu and Madam Bao perked up at Chen Jianglin's phrasing—clearly Eldest Brother had already written Haoyu off as heir, and Haofei cared only for books, leaving the Chen fortune practically handed to Haodong! Both hid pleased smiles.

Chen Jiangmu jumped in. "What talk is that, Eldest Brother? What kind of house are the Chens that Haoyu would ever be down to a plain bowl of rice? Even if he plays around for life, he'll eat fine meats and fish whenever he walks home!"

"Exact right!" Madam Cui echoed. "How could Eldest Brother, Sister-in-law, and Mother let Haoyu go hungry? Between his own share, his cousins looking out for him, and his wife—Shuzhen holds her own dowry plus all Haoyu's personal properties, richer than almost any lady in Lin'an! Haoyu won't suffer a day!"

Watching her granddaughter-in-law's cool face, Old Madam Xia sensed she still had little real interest in reforming Haoyu and felt a dull pang. Annoyed by Third Son and his wife jumping straight to dividing estates, she cut them off. "Enough! I'm not dead yet, and you're already planning Haoyu's welfare after the elders are gone! As the saying goes, 'A worthy wife outweighs ten thousand acres of fertile land.' Haoyu earned blessings in a past life to marry Shuzhen—with a wife like her, why fret over his future?"

Worthy wife, maybe—too bad I got stuck with a grudge against Chen Haoyu that turned me into everyone's favorite tigress! Qianli Xun thought.

Madam Bao scoffed silently: Worthy wife? Pure tigress!

Madam Cui snickered inside: Sweet face, lethal fists—never heard of a "worthy wife" tying her husband up and locking him in a back room! Old Madam Xia's bar for worthy wives sure dropped low!

"Shuzhen," Old Madam Xia continued, "if you think well of your father-in-law, mother-in-law, and me, leave those old rumors in the past. Good or bad, yesterday is gone—people have to look forward. Focus purely on disciplining Haoyu from now on; no need to come pay morning respects or worry about keeping me entertained. The storyteller knows the path by heart now, and this big family has plenty of people to keep me company. No need to report your methods either—handle him as you please. Go on back now."

Not wanting Shuzhen to vent further resentment and give Second and Third Sister-in-law more fodder to gloat over, Old Madam Xia closed the session. Qianli Xun curtsied and left.

Walking back, she felt the trip had been pointless: Matron Wei had already cleared the elders' approval, yet the sour lump still sat in her chest. Better to stay away from the main courtyard until her temper cooled, and focus instead on creative "discipline" for Chen Haoyu.

Earlier that morning, after Hongdou delivered breakfast to Boya Study and they ate, Chen Haoyu shot Qinggua a look.

Catching his cue, Qinggua edged up to Hongdou as she stacked bowls. "Could you do me a favor and call Chouya over here?"

"What do you want Chouya for?" Hongdou asked.

"Don't get the wrong idea—I don't like her! Just need to ask her something."

Having noticed Chouya's crush on Qinggua ages ago, Hongdou happily seized the chance to pass Qinggua off onto her and get him off her own back. "Hang on, I'll send her over. She can't come inside though—she'll have to talk through the window."

"Window works fine!" Qinggua escorted her to the door until the padlock snapped shut again.

"Why pick Chouya?" Chen Haoyu asked.

Qinggua turned back. "You handed me a brutal mission, Young Master—Chouya's my only shot! With a nickname like 'Ugly Girl,' she's way easier to charm."

"What's the other maid's name?"

"Amei."

"Amei—means pretty, right? Must be a looker."

"Pretty plain actually, nowhere near Hongdou. She's Asheng's younger sister."

"Brother and sister both slaving under Zhu Shuzhen—hopeless."

Qinggua disagreed. "You grew up rich, Young Master, so you don't know how hard poor folks have it. Poor families celebrate landing a servant post in a wealthy house, especially under a good mistress like Young Madam. Both siblings get to learn martial arts from her—half the manor envies them!"

"Shoo, shoo! Quit singing Zhu Shuzhen's praises in front of me! You're my man—don't let her buy you off!"

"Relax, Young Master. Loyal to you to the bone."

Right then a voice floated outside the boarded window. "Qinggua? Sister Hongdou said you wanted me?"

Chouya was not truly hideous—just plump with narrow, squinting eyes, less pleasing to look at than the other maids, which had saddled her with the nickname Chouya, literally "Ugly Girl."

"She's here! Go turn on the charm!" Chen Haoyu whispered, dropping into a chair to watch the show.

For his master—and for the promise of winning Hongdou later—Qinggua braced himself and leaned close to the shutters. "Chouya, Young Madam locked me up in here, and it's been sixteen hours since I last saw you. I begged Hongdou to call you just so I could hear your voice."

Chen Haoyu's brows shot up. Who knew Qinggua could spin smooth lies without blinking? Gotta watch this kid from now on—and ugh, what syrupy cheese!

A bashful flutter answered from outside. "I thought you only had eyes for Sister Hongdou! Never dreamed you had feelings for me... truth is, I like you too."

Qinggua froze stiff—fake flirting had reeled in real feelings! Struck dumb, he stood gaping.

Thrilled, Chen Haoyu scrambled over and hissed in his ear, "Say something! She's into you—this is in the bag!"

Nudged forward, Qinggua stammered, "Then... we feel the same way about each other..."

"Get to the point!" Chen Haoyu hissed.

"I'm dying to see you, yet I'm trapped in here! Could you unlock the door for me?"

Chouya hesitated. "I want to see you too, yet Miss locked you up. What if I beg her to let you out when she gets back?"

"No use—Young Madam will never let me out! Who knows how long I'll rot in here without seeing your face? Chouya, I miss you."

"I miss you too!"

"I didn't even do anything wrong, yet she threw me in here keeping us apart..."

"Wait right there, Qinggua! I'll sneak the key and let you out!"

Hearing her footsteps hurry off, master and servant slapped palms in triumph.

"So who sneaks out first, Young Master—me or you?"

"Me, obviously! Let's see—should I run straight to Grandmother and my parents, or bolt outside? Forget the elders, I'll bolt straight out of the manor and hide somewhere Zhu Shuzhen can never find me! Deal!"

Minutes later the padlock rattled. Both sprang to the door ready to dash—only for Chouya to slip inside and slam the door shut behind her, cutting off every inch of escape.

The instant she saw Qinggua, Chouya threw her arms right around him. "Qinggua! I'm so happy you actually like me too!"

Pinned in her bear hug, Qinggua stiffened, forcing himself to endure for freedom's sake.

While Chouya was distracted, Chen Haoyu reached quietly for the door latch—only for Chouya to pivot, still hugging Qinggua, and block the door solid. "Going somewhere, Young Master?"

Impressed by Zhu Shuzhen's sharp maids, Chen Haoyu tried a bluff. "Don't let me out and I'll order Qinggua to dump you! He's my servant—he obeys my every word!"

Releasing the hug, Chouya clasped Qinggua's hand tight. "Don't try scaring me, Young Master. Qinggua and I love each other—nobody can tear us apart!"

Chen Haoyu switched to sweet-talking. "Let me walk out, and I give my blessing to you two!"

"Your blessing doesn't matter," Chouya replied flatly. "I'm only taking Qinggua out. You stay right in here, Young Master."

"Let go of my hand a second, Chouya," Qinggua said. "Let me whisper a word to Young Master."

Obediently, she let go. Leaning to Chen Haoyu's ear, Qinggua breathed, "Hang tight a bit longer, Young Master. Let me get out first and I'll figure out a way to spring you from outside!"

Chen Haoyu nodded and waved to Chouya. "Fine, take Qinggua. I'll stay right here enjoying my lockup."

Watching the door open, sunlight flash in and vanish, and the padlock snap shut again, Chen Haoyu rushed to the crack yelling, "Qinggua! Don't forget to go straight to Old Madam Xia!"

"Count on me, Young Master!" Qinggua called back.

Outside, Chouya clung tight to his arm. Helpless, Qinggua coaxed, "Hurry and put the key back before anyone notices it's gone!"

Only then did she let go. "While Miss is out, go hide in the west garden pavilion and wait for me! I'll slip the key back to Asheng in the West Courtyard first!"

The second Chouya hurried off, Qinggua sighed in relief and sprinted straight out of Moxiang Courtyard toward Old Madam Xia's rooms—nearly colliding halfway with Qianli Xun and Kuqiao walking back! Diving behind a rockery until they passed, he popped back out and bolted to Old Madam Xia's courtyard.

Inside, after Shuzhen left, the family had moved back to chatting while waiting for the storyteller; all three brothers had cleared their morning to stay and listen with their mother.

Bursting past an empty yard straight into the hall, Qinggua threw himself to the floor squeezing out loud wails. "Old Madam! Save Young Master! Young Madam locked him up! Won't let him breathe free air! Boo-hoo!"

Chen Jiangmu frowned. "Haoyu's wife went too far! Mother, since we're all here, lead us over and tell her to let him out!"

"Yes, Mother, go help Haoyu!" Madam Cui urged.

Old Madam Xia lifted one hand to halt them. "Quit wailing, Qinggua. Go tell Haoyu that from today on, neither his parents nor I will meddle in his courtyard. Sink or swim, he's on his own."

Shocked dry of tears, Qinggua gaped before stammering, "You can't abandon him, Old Madam! Young Madam's fists are lethal—Young Master's life is in danger!"

Secretly thrilled, Chen Jiangshu and Madam Bao kept smooth poker faces and stayed dead silent—let Haoyu take his beating!

Turning to Chen Jianglin, Qinggua begged, "Master, he's your own flesh and blood! You can't stand by!"

"Tell Haoyu I'm thrilled she locked him up," Chen Jianglin said coldly. "Why would I rescue him? He made his bed—let him lie in it."

Desperate, Qinggua swung toward Yao Qingyan. "Madam—"

Hardening her heart, Yao Qingyan cut him off. "Not another word! Live or die, Haoyu answers to his wife now. Don't bother me with Moxiang Courtyard's business again!"

"Servants!" Old Madam Xia snapped. Two men stepped in. "Escort Qinggua out!"

Dragged outside just as the storyteller arrived, Qinggua lingered until the clapper sounded and laughter rose within, then trudged off with a heavy sigh.
