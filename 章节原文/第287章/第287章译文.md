## Chapter 287: Cracking the Case Open

Chen Haoyu’s hearing resumed, with Chen Jianglin, Chen Haodong, and Chen Haofei attending from the gallery.

Chen Haodong came in braced this time—knowing Prince Yi would try exposing his hand, he had his defenses locked tight.

Constables brought in Chen Haoyu and Chun Jinlan to kneel, then flanked the hall.

Shen Jianchen opened. “Yesterday’s investigation turned up new leads. Bring in Butler Wang of Chen Manor.”

Butler Wang entered and knelt. “Your Lordship, I told Chief Constable Wen everything I know last night.”

“Repeat it in front of everyone.”

Butler Wang recited clear. “On the third day of the third month last year, I personally sent orders pulling every servant out of the country house because Chen Manor’s city residences needed a thorough cleaning and ran short on hands.”

“Who gave you that order?”

“Eldest Young Master.”

“Did the Chen Masters know?”

“Eldest Young Master always handles those chores—no need to inform the Masters.”

*Still gunning for me,* Chen Haodong thought. *Good thing transferring servants proves nothing on its own.*

Shen Jianchen turned to him. “Care to explain why you pulled every servant out on that exact day, Eldest Young Master?”

Rising to bow, Chen Haodong answered smooth. “Chen Manor deep-cleans its town properties every year, Your Lordship. Landing on the third day of the third month was pure chance—how could I know Haoyu had locked a girl up there that day?”

Shen Jianchen gave a cool smirk. “Sit—I’ll have more questions shortly.”

Chen Haodong sat, uneasy yet unafraid—he had his firewall in place.

Dismissing Butler Wang, Shen Jianchen called, “Bring in Aye and Scarface!”

Both entered and knelt. Seeing a new witness—one of the country-house maids—tightened Chen Haodong’s nerves. *No way she recognized me in that cloak!*

Shen Jianchen warned Scarface first. “Last chance to tell the truth, Scarface. Once proof shows you perjured yourself, your penalty doubles.”

Scarface stuck to his script. “Every word I spoke is true, Your Lordship! Young Master Chen did it all; I was just his lackey.”

“Which Young Master Chen?” Shen Jianchen asked sharp. “Three of them sit in this hall—mind you don’t misspeak.”

Scarface gulped. “Second... Second Young Master Chen.”

Turning to Aye, Shen Jianchen said, “Tell everyone what you witnessed on the night of the third day of the third month last year.”

“Yes, Your Lordship. When I got back from home that night, every servant was gone and the caretaker was dead drunk. I went to let the girl Second Young Master snatched go—only to see Scarface enter the room, so I hid behind the tree to wait. Screams came from inside, so I figured Scarface was violating her. Then Scarface hauled her out in a sack—and right after, a second person walked out wrapped head to face in a black cloak!”

Chen Haodong exhaled a little. *Missed one maid on leave—lucky she never saw my face.*

“Was that cloaked man Chen Haoyu?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“No!” Aye said flat-out. “Second Young Master never sneaks around for this sort of thing—plus that man stood shorter than Second Young Master, right around... around Eldest Young Master’s height!”

Jolted cold, Chen Haodong barked to cover, “Watch your mouth, girl! This is a court of law—don’t go comparing random people!”

Aye flinched. “Forgive me, Eldest Young Master—spoke out of turn in my nerves.”

Chen Haodong appealed straight to the bench. “Don’t put stock in a jittery maid’s chatter, Your Lordship! Likely there was no cloaked man at all—just her eyes playing tricks in the dark!”

“Believe me, Your Lordship!” Aye insisted. “My eyes didn’t fool me!”

“Hold your conclusions, Eldest Young Master—I’ll judge her credibility myself.” Turning to Chen Haoyu, Shen Jianchen asked, “Describe returning to the house the next day.”

Seeing the tide turn in his son’s favor lifted the boulder off Chen Jianglin’s chest, though Haodong’s jumpy reaction left him baffled.

“I got back around noon the next day,” Chen Haoyu explained. “Wondered a second why no servants were around, then brushed it off and went straight to the room. The girl was gone, and Scarface and the boys were nowhere to be found. Figured she’d escaped and the boys hid out of fear I’d chew them out, so I dropped it and forgot—never even brought it up when I saw them again.”

Chun Jinlan’s world spun—had Scarface been the one who ruined her? All she remembered from that pitch-black room was someone throwing a cloth over her face, untying her body only to lash her ankles together, pinning her down with brute strength while she screamed her lungs out, tearing her clothes open, and taking her in silence—not a single word, only heavy, coarse breathing in her ear.

“Lies!” Chun Jinlan shrieked. “The maid’s lying, Your Lordship! Chen Haoyu snatched me—who else could it be?!”

“I decide who’s lying, not you!” Shen Jianchen snapped.

Cowed, she felt for the dagger in her sleeve. Things were tilting bad—one threat on her own life might flip the board.

Shen Jianchen bored into Scarface. “Well, Scarface? Your explanation?”

“The maid’s making it up, Your Lordship! I didn’t touch her!”

“Who was the man in the black cloak?”

“Who else? Young Master Chen!”

Chen Haodong’s heart lurched. *Is Scarface folding?!*

“Which Young Master Chen?”

“Second... Second Young Master Chen.”

Chen Haodong exhaled—worth every tael of that hundred!

Next, constables brought in the country-house caretaker, who kowtowed low.

“Where did the wine you drank that night come from?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“Scarface brought it, Your Lordship. Said we were old acquaintances from his trips with Second Young Master and offered a drink since the place was empty. I love my wine, so I said yes—stuff must’ve been strong, knocked me cold after just a few cups.”

“Not strong wine—drugged wine.” Shen Jianchen snapped his eyes back to Scarface. “Confess! Why did you drug the caretaker?”

Scarface flopped flat. “I swear I meant well, Your Lordship—just sharing a drink! How could I know he couldn’t hold his liquor?!”

“Stubborn, eh? Twenty strokes of the paddle!”

Constables pinned Scarface down and brought the heavy bamboo down right across the court. Screaming bloody murder, Scarface broke on the tenth stroke. “Mercy, Your Lordship! I’ll talk! I’ll talk!”

Shen Jianchen raised a hand, halting the blows. “One lie and you get ten more.”
