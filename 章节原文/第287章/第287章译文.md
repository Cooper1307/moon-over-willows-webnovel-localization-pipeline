## Chapter 287: Cracking Open the Case

Court reconvened with Chen Jianglin, Chen Haodong, and Chen Haofei seated in the gallery.

First, Butler Wang confirmed under oath that *Chen Haodong* alone had ordered every servant pulled out of the country villa on the third day of the third month—which Haodong smoothly chalked up to annual spring cleaning.

Next came Scarface and Aye. Scarface stuck stubbornly to blaming Second Young Master—until Aye described the black-cloaked figure emerging behind Scarface, adding innocently, "Shorter than Second Young Master—about Eldest Young Master's height!"

Haodong snapped immediately—"Watch your mouth on a courtroom floor, girl!"—trying to brush off her eyes playing tricks in the dark, yet Shen Jianchen shut him down coolly.

Haoyu recounted arriving next noon to an empty house and assuming Jinlan escaped, while devastated Jinlan recalled only a suffocating blindfold, brutal hands, and heavy silent breathing in pitch black—still screeching Haoyu was to blame as her fingers brushed the hidden dagger in her sleeve.

Finally, the estate steward testified that *Scarface* had poured him strangely knock-out wine that night! Caught flat-footed in a blatant lie, Scarface got pinned down for twenty heavy court boards—and shrieked for mercy after ten!
