## Chapter 346: A Storyteller Brings Miraculous News

Waking in Xiangrong's bed, Haoyu vowed no second wedding yet promised lifelong loyalty to her alone—and within weeks, news of Xiangrong's pregnancy pulled Haoyu straight back into leading the Chen empire, though he still refused Matron Wei's offer to vacate Moxiang Courtyard.

Out in Zhuli Village, bearded, weathered Shen Jianchen lived simply while nervous Prefect Li Ke—whose patron Liang Shicheng had just been strangled on Emperor Zhao Huan's orders!—showered him with tribute silver that Shen Jianchen poured right into village *"Qianli Feasts"* (making everyone shout *"Qianli Xun!"* ten times just to hear her name alive in the air), promising Bai Feng his own bamboo house so he could finally marry Kuqiao and bring Matron Wei over too.

Slipping into town to Qiaotou Wa's storytelling hall after lunch with his wine gourd, Shen Jianchen froze dead in his tracks when the storyteller announced a **brand-new huaben from Master Qianli Xun**, smuggled out of Yanjing just ten days ago—with promises of more to come!

*"She's alive! She's alive!"* Bursting back to his bamboo cottage in dizzy joy, he crashed straight into Qiao Wenxian bearing twin proof: a scholar returning from Yanjing had spotted a brand-new lyric by Youqi Jushi hung in a Yanjing teahouse, signed **sixth day of the first month, first year of Jingkang**!

Cooling Shen Jianchen's impulse to rush blindly north without leverage, Wenxian swore all his friends to keep her survival quiet from newly expectant Haoyu so Shen Jianchen finally got his fair shot! Shaving clean while weeping Matron Zou combed his hair, Shen Jianchen mounted up and galloped straight to the **Shao Escort Agency**—holding the one trump card Wanyan Chi could never refuse.
