## Chapter 259: Volunteering to Run the Congee Shed

晚上，陈浩宇从绸缎庄回来，按时在门外报告。
“娘子，我回来了，今日我进步很大，伙计的活儿已十分熟悉……”
话还未说完，苦荞从里面把门打开：“姑爷，小姐请您进来。”
陈浩宇受宠若惊，几日的努力终于有成果：“我这就进去。”
走进后发现娘子并未正襟危坐，而是站着，像是专门等他一般，更是心花怒放。
“娘子，没想到这么晚，你还在等我，我好感动。”
搁往常，千里寻必然将陈浩宇的话怼回去。既打算学着接受他，怼他并不合适。
“你忙一整天很辛苦，苦荞刚做好一盏茶，把它喝了。”
苦荞已将茶盏端到姑爷面前：“姑爷，小姐算好时间，让我提前做好等你回来喝，你刚刚在外面说话时，我也刚做好。”
陈浩宇二话不说，立刻端起来一口喝下，再将空茶盏递给苦荞。
好心情的作用下，陈浩宇只觉一盏茶下肚，浑身舒坦：“带着娘子心意的茶喝起来感觉就是好！”
千里寻说：“你累了一天，赶紧休息吧。”
又是给他准备茶，又是关切的语气，陈浩宇以为娘子已经接纳他，不免想入非非：“娘子，我能不能今晚住在你这里？”
青瓜和苦荞的脸上都露出诧异的表情，更别说千里寻。她试着学习接受对方，刚刚开始，甚是不习惯，对方就提出如此要求，千里寻立刻原形毕露。
“陈浩宇！你满脑子都想些什么？老娘刚对你好些，就得寸进尺！你在青楼里干的那些个龌龊事，老娘想起来就觉恶心！还想跟老娘睡一个屋子，下辈子吧！”
陈浩宇意识到火候还未到，他不小心用力猛了些，还是赶紧服软：“娘子别生气，怪我太喜欢你，心急了些，你继续对我好，以后我不敢再提无礼的要求。”
That evening, Chen Haoyu came home from the silk emporium and made his usual check-in outside her door. "Wife, I'm back! Made huge progress today—I've pretty much got the hang of working the counter..."

对方态度端正，千里寻也不好一直生气，语气缓和许多：“走吧，别杵在这儿烦我。”
Before he could finish, Kuqiao pulled the door open from inside. "Young Master, Miss invites you in."

“这就走，这就走，祝娘子晚上做个好梦，最好梦里有我。”陈浩宇担心娘子再生气，迅速带着青瓜离开。
Catching his breath in delighted shock—days of dogged effort had finally paid off!—Haoyu blurted, "Coming right in!"

回后院的路上，青瓜一直“嘿嘿”笑着。
Stepping inside, he found Qianli Xun standing there rather than sitting stiffly at her table, looking as though she had been waiting just for him. Joy burst through his chest. "Wife, you stayed up waiting for me this late? I'm so touched!"

陈浩宇站住，转过身：“你笑什么？”
Any other evening, Qianli Xun would have snapped back a cutting remark. Since she had resolved to try accepting him, biting his head off didn't fit the plan. "You worked hard all day. Kuqiao just whisked a fresh bowl of tea—drink it."

青瓜忍住笑声：“没什么。”
Kuqiao presented the bowl to him. "Young Master, Miss timed it so I'd have this ready right as you got back. You were talking outside just as I finished."

“不说小心我踢你。”
Without a second's hesitation, Haoyu drained the entire bowl in one gulp and handed it back to Kuqiao. Riding high on pure joy, he felt warmth spread from head to toe. "Tea made with my wife's care hits different!"

“公子怎么还想踢我？您又不是不知道我现在躲避的本领超强。”
"You've been on your feet all day," Qianli Xun said. "Go get some rest."

“赶紧说，否则今晚去西院睡觉。”
Hot tea waiting for him, soft words of concern—convinced his wife had finally thawed, Haoyu let hope run away with his tongue. "Wife... could I sleep in here with you tonight?"

“好好好，我说。”青瓜说着又乐起来，“嘿嘿嘿，我是笑少夫人，她明显是想对公子您好些，可却不知道怎么对公子好。”
Qinggua and Kuqiao both froze in shock—and Qianli Xun blew straight through her new resolve, showing her true claws in half a heartbeat.

“她给我准备了茶，还说些关心的话，难道不对吗？”
"Chen Haoyu! What filth is rattling around inside that skull?! I act halfway civil for five seconds and you push for a mile! Every nasty thing you did in those brothels still turns my stomach! Sleep in the same room with me? Wait for your next lifetime!"

“公子觉得够吗？嘿嘿嘿……”
Realizing the iron wasn't hot yet and he had jumped the gun, Haoyu backpedaled fast. "Don't be mad, Wife—my fault for liking you so much I got ahead of myself! Keep treating me nice, I swear I won't push my luck again!"

“是不够，不过需慢慢来。你想想，她以前跟我可是死对头，又知道是我散播她的谣言，能像今日这般对待我，已是不易。”陈浩宇脸上露出高兴的神色，“说明她想跟我好好过日子，我也需努力努力，明日也该去永泰茶楼拜师学艺。”
With him backing down so meekly, Qianli Xun couldn't stay steamed and dialed her tone down a notch. "Get out of here. Stop standing around getting on my nerves."

青瓜不再继续笑，公子一本正经想入非非的样子，让他觉得继续笑下去，太不尊重少夫人。
"Leaving right now, leaving right now! Sweet dreams, Wife—hope I'm in them!" Afraid of setting her off again, Haoyu whisked Qinggua right out the door.

姑爷走后，苦荞边为小姐宽衣边说：“您开始表现挺好，后面怎么又发起火来？”
Halfway back toward the rear quarters, Qinggua couldn't stop snickering under his breath.

“你还问我？他提那要求搁谁谁不生气。”外衣已脱掉，千里寻身着睡衣躺在床上，将薄薄的蚕丝被盖在身上。
Haoyu spun around. "What's so funny?"

苦荞边挂衣裳边说：“姑爷的要求确实唐突，可仔细一想也没什么不对，毕竟已成亲，在外人看来，他要求住在素心草堂再正常不过。”
"Nothing!" Qinggua choked back a laugh.

“你竟然帮他说话？”
"Spill it or you're getting a kick."

苦荞走到床边：“我不是帮姑爷，是帮小姐，想提醒您以后别老对姑爷发火，要不真成姑爷说的母……”
"Still trying to kick me, Young Master? You know my dodge footwork is untouchable now!"

“母老虎？”
"Talk, or you're sleeping in the West Courtyard tonight."

苦荞点点头：“再把变好的姑爷给吓跑，他纳一两个妾室，墨香苑会变得鸡飞狗跳，到时恐怕小姐不得安生。”
"All right, all right, I'll talk!" Qinggua broke into another grin. "Hehehe—I'm laughing at Young Madam! She's clearly trying to be nice to you, only she has no clue *how* to be nice to a man!"

“我倒希望她找妾室，可陈浩宇不乐意。”
"She made me tea and checked if I was tired—what's wrong with that?"

苦荞又替小姐将蚕丝被盖好：“我怎么觉着小姐说这话有些得意？”
"Think that's enough, Young Master? Hehehe..."

“你哪只眼睛看到我得意了？目前他说的话还让我得意不起来。你家小姐我都想好了，无论陈浩宇纳不纳妾都得跟他过日子。他要是纳妾日子还好过些，有妾室陪他省去我不少麻烦；可他要不纳妾，我可就难了。”
"No, it's not enough, though we have to take it slow. Think about it—we used to be sworn enemies, and she knows I spread those rumors about her. Treating me even this well is already huge." A bright grin spread across Haoyu's face. "Means she's willing to give our marriage a shot. I need to step up my game too—tomorrow I'm heading to Yongtai Teahouse to find a tea master and learn to whisk tea."

“这有什么难的？小姐只要拿出当初跟乔公子在一起的劲头来，不就万事大吉？”
Seeing his master grinning earnestly over such tiny crumbs stopped Qinggua's chuckles; laughing any further felt disrespectful to Young Madam.

千里寻早已放下文渊，苦荞提起并不会再次引起她的伤感之情。
Inside Suxin Thatched Hall, Kuqiao helped Qianli Xun out of her outer robe. "You started out doing so well, Miss—why did you snap at him at the end?"

“那时我喜欢文渊自然愿意跟他亲近，可我根本不喜欢陈浩宇，估计拉他的手全身都会起鸡皮疙瘩，恋爱的劲头用在他身上不合适。”
"You have to ask? Who wouldn't snap at a stunt like that?" Climbing into bed in her sleeping tunic, Qianli Xun pulled a light silk quilt up to her chin.

苦荞将床幔放下，心里替小姐担忧，什么时候她才能从心底真正愿意接纳姑爷？
Hanging up the robe, Kuqiao mused, "Sure, Young Master was abrupt, yet think about it—you're man and wife. To any outsider, him asking to sleep in Suxin Thatched Hall is the most natural thing in the world."

第二天，说话艺人家中有事，告了假，陈家的女人都陪着老太太聊天儿，男人们则一个个去忙生意上的事。
"Taking his side now?"

千里寻依然没有见到叶安雅，自从上次她打听过和离之事后，人就像消失一般。
Stepping beside the bed, Kuqiao shook her head. "I'm not on Young Master's side—I'm on *yours*. Just reminding you not to bark at him every turn, or you really will turn into the 'tigress' he used to call you. Scare off a reformed husband and he might take a concubine or two, turning Moxiang Courtyard into a hornet's nest—then *you* won't have a moment's peace!"

本来千里寻想去叶安雅的院子看她，想来想去还是算了，那位堂兄本就喜欢打老婆，若她去看望，恐怕哪个下人告状，又让大嫂被白白打一顿。
"I'd *love* it if he took a concubine," Qianli Xun grumbled, "only Chen Haoyu refuses."

﻿她又不敢多问，担心二婶子不高兴。
Tucking the silk quilt snug around her mistress, Kuqiao eyed her slyly. "Why do you sound a little pleased saying that, Miss?"

千里寻本是天不怕地不怕的人，穿越而来面对复杂的妯娌关系，竟也变得瞻前顾后，这恐怕就是现实。
"Which eye saw me pleased? Nothing he says makes me pleased yet! Still, I've thought it through: concubines or not, I'm stuck living with Chen Haoyu. If he took a concubine to keep him busy, it'd save me heaps of trouble; if he won't, I'm the one left on the hook!"

当大家聊得无话可说时，包氏建议：“母亲，不如我们打马吊吧？”
"What's so hard about that? Just bring out the same spark you had back when you were with Young Master Qiao, and everything's solved!"

夏老太太说：“我不喜欢那玩意儿，你们若喜欢你们打，我在一旁看。”
Qianli Xun had long since moved past Wenyuan; hearing his name raised not even a flicker of heartache. "Back then I *liked* Wenyuan, so getting close came natural. I don't like Chen Haoyu—just holding his hand would probably give me goosebumps from head to toe. Romance tactics don't work when the feelings aren't there."

姚轻琰说：“打马吊我听说过，从未玩过。”
Drawing the bed curtains closed, Kuqiao sighed in quiet worry. When would her mistress's heart ever open up to her husband?

崔氏说：“我是会些，可光我会人也不够，起码得四个人。”
The next day, the storyteller sent word he had a family matter and couldn't come. The Chen women gathered in Old Madam Xia's hall to chat while the men went off to mind family businesses.

包氏转向淑真：“淑真，你可会打马吊？”
Once again, Ye Anya was nowhere to be seen; ever since asking Qianli Xun about mutual divorce, she might as well have vanished off the face of the earth. Qianli Xun considered walking over to Anya's courtyard to check on her, then thought better of it—with a cousin-in-law who loved beating his wife, one gossipy servant running her mouth could earn Anya another senseless thrashing. Nor did she dare ask aloud lest Second Aunt Bao bristle. Fearless as Qianli Xun used to be, navigating a sprawling clan of in-laws had taught her to tread on eggshells—such was reality.

千里寻摇摇头：“我不会。”
When conversation hit a lull, Madam Bao suggested, "Mother, how about a game of madiao?"

包氏说：“我听说莱州知州的夫人也是女词人，人家打马吊可是一流，你既是女词人，可要多向她学习学习。”
Old Madam Xia waved a hand. "Never cared for that game. You girls play if you like—I'll watch."

易安居士喜欢打马吊，千里寻也是听说过，还知道她给后世留有《打马赋》，但要让她学习打马吊是万万不可。她非常讨厌这种游戏，要知道打马吊可是麻 将的前身。
"I've heard of madiao, never played," Yao Qingyan said.

她穿越前的时代打麻 将的游戏很流行，尤其在她小时候。
"I know a bit," Madam Cui offered, "only we need at least four players."

记得邻居家有个小妹妹，她十岁那年，那孩子八岁。小妹妹的爸妈超喜欢打麻将，经常没事把孩子锁在家里跑出去找人打麻将。有一次小妹妹想翻窗出去，结果从十二楼掉下，当场死亡。
Madam Bao turned to Shuzhen. "Do you play madiao, Shuzhen?"

当时十岁的千里寻正在楼下跟小朋友玩耍，小妹妹就掉在她们面前，她永远也忘不了可怕的一幕。从那时起，她便十分讨厌打麻将的人。
Qianli Xun shook her head. "Don't know how."

此时包氏建议她学习，千里寻一百个不乐意：“易安居士爱好独特，那是她的事，我是打死都不会学那玩意儿。”
"I hear the wife of the Prefect of Laizhou is a poetess too, and a wizard at madiao," Madam Bao remarked. "Fellow poetess like you ought to learn from her!"

包氏甚觉无趣。
Qianli Xun knew well enough that Yi'an Jushi loved gambling games and had even left behind her *"Rhapsody on Dama"*, yet learning madiao was completely out of the question. She despised the game—madiao was the ancestor of mahjong! Back in her own era, mahjong had been everywhere, especially during her childhood. She still remembered a little girl next door—eight years old when Qianli Xun was ten. Her parents were obsessed with mahjong, constantly locking the child alone inside their apartment to go play. One day the little girl tried climbing out a window and plunged twelve stories to her death, hitting the pavement right in front of ten-year-old Qianli Xun and her playmates. She had never shaken that horror—and she had loathed mahjong players ever since.

包氏院中有个丫鬟前来，向老太太及众人行礼后，对包氏说：“二夫人，秦夫人差人来邀请您去打马吊，正在外头候着。”
Bristling at Madam Bao's nudge, Qianli Xun replied flatly, "Yi'an Jushi can keep her hobbies. You couldn't pay me to touch that game."

包氏立刻满脸堆笑：“母亲，我能不能去玩玩儿？”
Madam Bao lost interest at once.

夏老太太说：“想去就去吧，只是你与那秦夫人走得近，定要管住自己的嘴，不要什么都说，她可是个爱嚼舌根的人，把没有的事都能说得天花乱坠。”
Just then a maid from Madam Bao's courtyard entered, bowed to Old Madam Xia and the room, and reported, "Second Madam, Madam Qin sent someone inviting you over for madiao—they're waiting outside."

包氏强调道：“母亲放心，儿媳只喜欢跟她打马吊，从未说过别的事。”
Madam Bao's face lit up. "Mother, may I go play a few rounds?"

夏老太太点点头：“去吧。”
"Go on if you like," Old Madam Xia said, "only watch your tongue around Madam Qin. That woman's a notorious gossip who spins mountains out of molehills."

包氏前脚出去，陈江林后脚进来，还未行礼，老太太问道：“你怎么回来了？”
"Don't worry, Mother—I only play madiao with her, never breathe a word about family affairs."

陈江林这才行礼：“母亲，有一事需征求您的意思。”
Old Madam Xia gave a nod. "Off you go."

“坐下说。”
No sooner had Madam Bao stepped out than Chen Jianglin walked in. Before he could even bow, his mother asked, "What brings you home at this hour?"

姚轻琰让出榻上的位置，坐到淑真身旁椅子上。
Chen Jianglin bowed. "Mother, there's a matter I need your ruling on."

陈江林上前坐下，开门见山：“我想把陈家搭的施粥棚给撤了。”
"Sit and speak."

“为何？”
Yao Qingyan yielded her seat on the daybed and moved to a chair beside Shuzhen. Chen Jianglin sat down and came straight to the point. "I want to shut down the congee shed our family runs."

“搭建施粥棚，本是想让可怜人有个填饱肚子的地方，可也养了许多懒汉懒妇。到陈家施粥棚混饭的人越来越多，甚至有些力气大的懒汉为了一碗粥，还欺负老弱病残。现在是懒汉懒妇居多，真正的可怜人则是少数。所以儿子认为还是撤了，省得养一大群蛀虫出来，再说知府大人现在也不过问此事。”
"Why?"

夏老太太想了一会儿说：“撤起来容易，只是撤了那些个真正需要的人该怎么办？知府大人过不过问不重要，重要的是陈家既挣了百姓的银子，也要懂得回馈百姓。”
"We put up that congee shed so poor folk wouldn't starve, yet it's bred a mob of lazy layabouts—men and women both. More freeloaders crowd in every day; beefy loafers even shove aside the elderly, sick, and crippled just to grab a bowl of gruel. Now parasites outnumber the truly needy. Better shut it down than bankroll a pack of leeches—besides, Prefect Zhu doesn't even bother checking on it anymore."

“母亲说得极是，陈家回馈百姓的方式很多，比如修桥修路，逢年过节布施医药物资，茶庄、米庄、香料庄、绸缎庄，还有各大酒楼、茶肆、店铺等不知解决了多少人的生计，官府收集善款时陈家总是出得最多，回馈百姓已不少，不在乎一个施粥棚。”
Old Madam Xia mulled it over. "Shutting it down is easy enough, yet what happens to the folk who genuinely need it? Whether the Prefect cares or not doesn't matter—the Chen family earns its silver from the common people, so we owe it to give back."

“话虽如此，一旦撤去，那些个流浪街头的老弱病残怎么活？”
"You're right, Mother, and we already give back plenty: repairing bridges and roads, distributing medicine and grain every festival, plus our tea emporiums, rice shops, spice stores, silk emporiums, taverns, and teahouses keep thousands fed. Whenever the yamen passes the hat for charity, our family always pledges the most. We've done our share—one congee shed won't tip the scales."

“母亲心善，见不得他人受苦，可是天下可怜人多得是，陈家哪管得过来？”
"Even so, tear it down and how will the crippled and elderly wandering the streets stay alive?"

“是管不过来，能管多少管多少。别的地方我们管不上，临安城的可怜人必须管。”
"Your heart is too soft, Mother—you can't bear seeing anyone suffer. Still, the world is overflowing with misery; the Chen family can't save them all."

陈江林见母亲坚决，便不再提撤去施粥棚一事：“那就不撤了，大不了儿子花钱雇些打手，将那些混饭的懒汉懒妇驱逐出去，让他们知道陈家的粥棚不是供养懒人之地。”
"We can't save the whole world, so we save who we can. Folks elsewhere are beyond our reach, yet the destitute right here in Lin'an we *must* look after."

“此法甚好，需找个可靠的人看管。”
Seeing his mother wouldn't budge, Chen Jianglin dropped shutting it down. "Then we'll keep it open. Worst case, I'll hire some toughs to chase the lazy freeloaders off so they learn our congee shed isn't a free meal ticket for idlers."

“儿子也是这么想，可我、二弟、三弟、浩东都忙着陈家的生意脱不开身，浩宇刚刚学做生意，不好分身，容儿子想想，不行从兴隆阁找个人前去。”
"Now that's a solid plan—only put someone reliable in charge."

千里寻认真听着两人的对话，觉得此事自己最适合不过，立刻将昨日窝在陈府的想法抛到九霄云外，兴致勃勃地说：“若长辈不嫌弃，淑真主动请缨管理施粥棚。”
"My thought too. Only Second Brother, Third Brother, Haodong, and I are all tied up with business, and Haoyu's just starting his training and can't spare the time. Let me think—if nothing else, I'll pull someone from Xinglong Pavilion."

陈江林反对道：“家里这么多男人，哪需要你一个女人家抛头露面？”
Listening closely, Qianli Xun felt her pulse quicken—this job had her name written all over it! Instantly forgetting yesterday's vow to hole up indoors, she spoke up brightly. "If the elders don't mind, Shuzhen would like to volunteer to manage the congee shed!"

崔氏应和道：“是啊淑真，大哥自有安排，你就别瞎掺和。”
"Nonsense!" Chen Jianglin objected at once. "With a house full of men, why on earth would a woman go showing her face out in public?"

姚轻琰也说：“淑真啊，你要是觉得在家待着无趣，可以去逛逛街，管理粥棚的事，你不适合。”
Madam Cui chimed right in. "Exactly, Shuzhen—Eldest Brother will handle it. Don't go poking your nose in."

千里寻强调道：“没有比我更适合的人，我手底下有十个功夫了得的下人，省得公爹再另找打手。我的厉害各位长辈见识过，再说我是自家人，公爹和奶奶大可放心。”
"Shuzhen," Yao Qingyan added gently, "if you're bored cooped up at home, go browse the shops. Running a congee shed is no job for you."

陈江林还是不大情愿：“你毕竟是女子，让你出去忙活，别人会笑话我们陈家没有男人，要让知府大人知道，恐怕也得批评我。”
Qianli Xun stood her ground. "Nobody is better suited than me! I've got ten crack martial-arts servants under my command—saves Father-in-law from hiring extra thugs. You've all seen what I can handle myself, and since I'm family, Grandmother and Father-in-law won't have to worry a wink."

千里寻虽有写话本的本事，可写话本不是窝在家里就是窝在家里，时间一长恐怕窝得发霉，也需出去透透气，体验体验生活，找找新的灵感。再说她一身功夫，窝在陈府没有用武之地，也该出去活动活动筋骨。
Chen Jianglin still balked. "You're a lady! Send you out running errands in the street and folks will laugh that the Chen family has no men left—and if Prefect Zhu hears of it, he'll chew my ear off!"

“陈家的男人忙得都是大事，这等小事女子出面处理最合适不过。我已是陈家人，我爹即使知道，他也管不着。再说我爹哪会关注一个施粥棚？公爹尽管把心放在肚子里。”
Writing huaben was great, sure, yet staying cooped up indoors week after week would make anyone go moldy; she needed fresh air, real life, and new story material—plus her fists were itching for a proper workout! "The Chen men have big enterprises to run; letting a woman handle a small charity like this makes total sense. I'm a Chen now—even if my father heard, he couldn't boss me around, and honestly, when has my father ever spared a glance for a congee shed? Put your mind at ease, Father-in-law!"

夏老太太算是看出来，淑真是铁了心想要管理施粥棚，不如成全她：“女子又如何？我当初还不是一个人管理陈家产业，又把你们三个拉扯大，天天抛头露面的？我们陈家的女人没那么多穷讲究，淑真想去尽管让她去。”
Seeing the iron set in Shuzhen's eyes, Old Madam Xia decided to back her. "So what if she's a woman? Didn't I run the whole Chen empire by myself while raising you three boys, showing my face outdoors every single day? Chen women don't fuss over prissy rules! If Shuzhen wants to run it, let her!"

千里寻立刻说：“淑真谢奶奶成全！”
"Thank you for backing me, Grandmother!" Qianli Xun beamed.

母亲都同意，陈江林不敢违背母亲，也只好同意：“儿子听从母亲安排。”
With his mother laying down the law, Chen Jianglin dared not push back and bowed his head. "I'll follow Mother's wishes."

千里寻赶紧说：“谢过公爹。”
"Thank you, Father-in-law!" Qianli Xun chirped.

两个儿媳妇心里再不乐意，也不好说什么。
However sour Yao Qingyan and Madam Cui felt inside, neither dared utter another word.
