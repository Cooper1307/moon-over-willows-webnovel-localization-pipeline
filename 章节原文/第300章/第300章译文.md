## Chapter 300: Finding the Right Matchmaker

Shao Zhengye flatly threw the blood contract back in Chen Haodong's face—even laughing off Haodong's attempt to drop Princess Consort Yi's name, praising Prince Yi's sterling character and ordering his son Shao Zhize to show him out.

Stung, Haodong played his reckless trump card: *"Don't play saint—I know about **Yan Chi**'s father!"*

In half a heartbeat Shao Zhize's iron grip clamped around Haodong's windpipe until he wheezed for mercy, confessing Yan Chi had merely passed through the Chen chuiwan field! Releasing him with a frost-cold warning to steer clear of Yan Chi forever, Shao Zhengye showed the shaken heir-hopeful out—then resolved to ride over with fine wine tomorrow to fulfill his own pre-Bianliang pledge to call on Prince Yi at the Judicial Office.

Meanwhile, remembering her promise to introduce Master Jiang to Prince Yi yet unwilling to stir gossip or bump into Mengrong, Qianli Xun penned a concise note and sent Hongdou to the silk emporium—where Haoyu sat clicking an abacus like a seasoned accountant (and Qinggua chatted merrily with Hongdou about saving clerk wages for Chouya!)—asking Haoyu to deliver it to the Judicial Office.

Presenting his wife's letter to Shen Jianchen in the humble yamen yard, Haoyu bristled slightly at how eagerly the prince agreed to visit Master Jiang personally—and even more when Shen Jianchen kept him back for a candid word: *"I'd trade places with you in a heartbeat—treat her right, or I won't hesitate to step in!"*

Forgetting royal rank completely, Haoyu shot back boldly: "You won't get the chance! Take care of Princess Consort Yi and stop eyeing my wife—want to see her bleed like on my wedding day again?!"

Realizing he'd let his guard slip, Shen Jianchen exhaled wryly—"Fair point, past is past"—and after seeing Haoyu out, mounted his horse solo and rode straight to the Qiantang Gang.

Hearing Prince Yi waited outside alone, Master Jiang bolted out *barefoot* to kneel in the dirt—"Too thrilled to grab shoes!"—overjoyed that asking Youqi Jushi had brought royalty straight to his door!
