第180章 白风嫂嫂望月轩
## Chapter 180: Bai Feng and Sister-in-Law at Wangyue Pavilion

用过午饭后，白风还真跑来找苦荞。
当看门的小厮告诉苦荞仪王身边的白侍卫找她，苦荞兴高采烈地跑出去，白风就站在他们见面的老地方。
一看到苦荞，白风先解释：“昨日我陪王爷去乔家……”
“不必解释，我都清楚。”
“你真是善解人意。”白风把苦荞拉到一边。
苦荞很疑惑：“外面没人，你又何必把我拉得更偏？”
“我有重要的事情对你说，最好不要被望月轩其他下人听到。”白风显得很谨慎。
苦荞向四周瞧了一遍，小声问：“什么事？”
Sure enough, right after lunch, Bai Feng showed up looking for Kuqiao. The moment the gate boy brought word, Kuqiao skipped outside to their usual meeting spot.

“就昨日，王爷去朱府要求知府大人把幽栖居士放在他身边……”
"Yesterday I had to attend the Qiao wedding with His Highness—" Bai Feng began at once.

白风还未说完，苦荞惊得张大嘴巴：“啊！有这等事？”
"No need to explain, I already figured."

“我也被吓坏，才明白原来王爷心里真正喜欢的人是幽栖居士，佳和郡主多可怜。”
"You are always so understanding." Taking her arm, Bai Feng pulled her deeper into the shadows.

白风替佳和郡主鸣不平，苦荞很不满意：“我家小姐才可怜，被仪王无缘无故喜欢上，要是郡主知道仪王心里的人是我家小姐，肯定不会再来望月轩。”
"Nobody is out here—why drag me off to the corner?" Kuqiao asked, puzzled.

“听你这话，郡主来过望月轩？”
"I have something huge to tell you, and no other servant from Wangyue Pavilion can overhear."

“是啊，一大早就来，哭得跟个泪人儿似的，还问我家小姐知不知道仪王心里的人是谁，我都替小姐为难。”
Scanning the empty lane, Kuqiao dropped her voice. "What is it?"

“你不用为难，我已为他们想清楚。”
"Yesterday, His Highness marched straight into Zhu Manor and demanded Prefect Zhu hand Youqi Jushi over to him..."

苦荞苦笑道：“你能为他们想清楚？”
Kuqiao's jaw dropped before he finished. "What?! Really?!"

白风得意道：“小瞧了吧，让你见识见识我的聪明。你仔细想想，我家王爷与你家小姐能不能在一起？”
"Scared the daylights out of me too! That was when it finally hit me—Youqi Jushi is the woman His Highness really loves. Poor Princess Jiahe!"

苦荞先摇摇头，后又点点头。
Hearing him take the princess's side ruffled Kuqiao's feathers. "My mistress is the pitiful one, getting pestered by Prince Yi out of nowhere! If Her Highness ever learns Prince Yi loves my mistress, she will never set foot in Wangyue Pavilion again!"

“为何又摇头又点头的？”
"Wait—Her Highness came here?"

“摇头是因为我家小姐与你家王爷各自有婚约，他们想要在一起根本不可能；点头是因为我家小姐根本不想嫁到陈家，她一直为退婚努力，现在虽有些松懈，但我相信她还会努力，如果退婚，仪王再用他的身份强行要求老爷把小姐给他，老爷肯定同意。”
"First thing this morning! Cried her eyes out and asked Miss if she knew who Prince Yi loved! I was sweating buckets for Miss!"

“所以，关键还在幽栖居士，只要她老老实实嫁到陈家，不就什么事都没有。”
"No need to sweat—I already figured out the whole puzzle for them."

苦荞生气道：“白风，你说的什么话？我家小姐才不愿嫁给陈公子，他连林觉都比不上，小姐若嫁给他岂不是从一个火坑跳到另一个更大的火坑？那她还和离个什么劲儿！”
Kuqiao gave a dry laugh. "YOU figured it out?"

“你小声些，免得别人听去。”
"Underestimating me? Watch my brains at work! Think about it: can my prince and your mistress ever end up together?"

“你走吧，我不想理你。”
Kuqiao shook her head, then nodded.

“我错了，给你赔不是，不要因为别人的事情影响我们之间的关系。”
"Why both shake and nod?"

“哪能是别人？是我家小姐！她是除我家人以外，我最亲的人。难道你也把仪王看作别人？”
"Shake because both of them have betrothals, so being together looks impossible. Nod because Miss hates marrying into the Chen family and fought hard to break it off—she eased up lately, yet I bet she tries again. If that match breaks, and Prince Yi pulls royal rank on Master, Master is bound to cave."

“我今日是怎么了，老说错话，你原谅我好不好？”
"Which means the whole thing hinges on Youqi Jushi! Long as she marries Young Master Chen like a good girl, problem solved!"

苦荞也不想跟白风继续闹别扭，很快平复情绪：“我原谅你。”
Temper flaring, Kuqiao snapped, "What kind of rotten talk is that, Bai Feng?! Miss cannot stand Young Master Chen—he is not even half the man Lin Jue was! Marrying him jumps from one fire pit into a deeper one! What was the whole point of her divorce?!"

白风拉起苦荞的手：“这就对嘛，我们隔几日才能见一面，不要一见面就生气。要不你给幽栖居士请个假，跟我一起去逛街？”
"Keep your voice down before someone hears!"

“不行，郡主来后，小姐吃过午饭，一直坐着发呆，看上心情不好，我出来时她才提笔写话本，这个时候我不能随便离开。” 
"Beat it! I am not talking to you!"

白风很失望：“好吧，看来我只能一个人去趟猪市。”
"My fault, my fault—I apologize! Do not let other people's messes come between us."

苦荞很是不解：“为何要去猪市？”
"Who are you calling 'other people'? That is my mistress—closer to me than anyone outside my own family! Do you call Prince Yi 'other people' too?"

“王爷让我买头猪，训练爬树，你说奇怪不奇怪？”
"What is wrong with my mouth today? Always saying the wrong thing! Forgive me, alright?"

“你确定仪王的脑子没有问题？”
Unwilling to stay mad at Bai Feng for long, Kuqiao softened. "Fine, forgiven."

白风故意装作生气的样子：“竟敢如此说我家王爷，小心挨板子。”
Taking her hand, Bai Feng grinned. "That is my girl. We only see each other every few days—no sense wasting it fighting. Ask Youqi Jushi for the afternoon off and stroll the market with me?"

苦荞笑道：“你打一个试试！”
"No can do. Since Her Highness left, Miss sat staring blankly right through lunch in a foul mood and just picked up her brush to write her huaben. I cannot walk out on her now."

“我怎么舍得打你。”
Bai Feng slumped in disappointment. "Alright then. Guess I am heading to the pig market alone."

苦荞想起小姐叮嘱的事：“回去后告诉仪王，我家小姐请仪王酉时到西湖边的永泰茶楼相见。”
"Pig market?! What for?"

“都这样了，幽栖居士还要约王爷？”
"His Highness ordered me to buy a pig and train it to climb trees. Weird or what?"

“你太小瞧我家小姐，她见仪王肯定是劝说他要好好对待郡主。”
"Are you sure Prince Yi has not bumped his head?"

“这样便好。”
Bai Feng put on a mock scowl. "Talk smack about my prince and watch out for a spanking!"

两人正说着，听见马车的声音，原来是朱家少夫人前来。
Kuqiao giggled. "Lay a finger on me—I dare you!"

白风已见过苦荞，没必要再耽搁下去，便与苦荞分别，拐到一旁离开。
"Like I could ever bear to."

月桂跟着陈文心从马车上下来，打趣道：“白侍卫见到我们来就跑，该不会做下什么错事？”
Remembering her mistress's errand, Kuqiao added, "Tell Prince Yi when you get back: my mistress invites him to Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake at the Hour of the Rooster."

苦荞的脸立刻变红：“姐姐少拿我开玩笑。”
"Even after all this, Youqi Jushi still wants to meet him?"

月桂继续打趣：“谁拿你开玩笑？我说的是白侍卫。”
"Stop underestimating Miss! She is going to lecture him into treating Her Highness right."

苦荞更是害羞，不再言语。
"Thank goodness for that."

陈文心说：“行了，别再打趣她。苦荞，淑真在做什么？”
Just then carriage wheels rattled up—Young Madam Zhu had arrived! Having seen Kuqiao already, Bai Feng bade a swift farewell and ducked around the corner.

“回少夫人，小姐正在书桌前坐着写……”苦荞差点说漏嘴，只怪月桂搞得她害羞，丧失警惕，还好反应快些，“写诗作词。”
Stepping down from the carriage behind Chen Wenxin, Yuegui teased, "Guard Bai bolts the second we pull up—up to something naughty?"

“真不愧是女词人，午饭刚过也不歇着，我去找她聊会儿。”陈文心说着就往里走。
Kuqiao flushed bright red. "Quit teasing me, Sister Yuegui!"

苦荞赶紧上前，似有拦截的架势：“少夫人不用急，我先小跑赶去告诉小姐，您缓缓的。”
"Who is teasing you? I meant Guard Bai!"

苦荞转身撒开腿跑进望月轩。
Blushing deeper, Kuqiao clamped her mouth shut.

陈文心纳闷道：“苦荞今儿是怎么了？这么奇怪。”
"Enough teasing her," Chen Wenxin laughed. "What is Shuzhen up to, Kuqiao?"

月桂笑道：“估计是被我打趣的。”
"Miss is at her desk writing..." Catching her slip just in time—Yuegui's ribbing had dropped her guard—Kuqiao swerved smoothly: "...writing poems and lyrics!"

陈文心说：“我看也是。”
"A poetess through and through—not even resting after lunch! Let me go chat with her." Chen Wenxin started inside.

苦荞跑进小姐的闺房，见小姐还在奋笔疾书，气喘吁吁地说：“小姐，少夫人来看您。”
Kuqiao hopped in front to slow her down. "No rush, Young Madam! Let me dash ahead and tell Miss—walk slow!" Turning tail, she sprinted straight into the courtyard.

千里寻放下笔：“慌什么，我又没干什么坏事。”
Chen Wenxin blinked. "What got into Kuqiao today? Acting so jumpy."

苦荞走到跟前，边整理手稿边说：“还不是因为小姐不想让别人知道您在写话本。”
"Probably embarrassed from my teasing," Yuegui chuckled.

“难为你。”千里寻从桌上拿来一张空白的花笺纸，开始写朱淑真的一首诗。
"Must be."

当苦荞将手稿刚刚放进书架上的木盒里，陈文心带着月桂已进入：“淑真，别写了，陪嫂嫂聊聊天儿。”
Bursting into her mistress's room out of breath while Qianli Xun scribbled away, Kuqiao panted, "Miss, Young Madam is here!"

千里寻猜到嫂嫂前来肯定是因她昨日大闹陈家一事。她放下笔，起身走到嫂嫂跟前，拉起她的手：“嫂嫂好久没来看我，正好陪我说说话。”
Setting down her brush, Qianli Xun raised an eyebrow. "What are you panicking over? It is not like I am doing anything crooked."

陈文心故意埋怨：“还有脸说我，为什么不主动去看我？”
Sweeping the huaben pages together, Kuqiao reminded her, "Miss does not want anyone knowing you write huaben!"

两人笑着坐下后，千里寻提醒道：“嫂嫂又不是不知道，爹不让我回朱府。”
"Good thinking." Pulling out a fresh sheet of ornamental paper, Qianli Xun began copying one of Zhu Shuzhen's poems.

“我看是你不想回去，公公嘴上严厉，你要真回去，他也不会说什么。”
Just as Kuqiao slid the manuscript into a wooden box on the shelf, Chen Wenxin walked in with Yuegui. "Put the brush down, Shuzhen, and chat with your sister-in-law."

嫂嫂说到她的心里。
Guessing her sister-in-law had come over yesterday's raid on Chen Manor, Qianli Xun stood and took her hand. "You have not visited in ages, Sister-in-Law—stay and talk awhile."

离开朱府时的确不舍，真正离开后，越来越喜欢望月轩，反而不愿再回去。朱府的园子是朱淑真居住之所，而望月轩只有千里寻住过。要过千里寻的生活，和离是第一步，离开朱府则是第二步。
"Listen to you blaming me—why do you never come visit me?"

“怎么不说话？”陈文心见淑真沉默，不由问道。
Laughing as they sat down, Qianli Xun reminded her, "You know Father barred me from setting foot in Zhu Manor."

千里寻不再品味自己的心境，说起拉家常的话：“娘可好？”
"More like you do not want to go back! Father-in-law talks stern, yet if you actually showed up, he would not say a word."

“婆婆好着呢，不用你操心，我只问你昨日为何跑到陈家闹？”陈文心迅速扯到正题。
Her sister-in-law hit the nail on the head. Leaving Zhu Manor had hurt at first, yet living at Wangyue Pavilion had grown so sweet she had no wish to go back. The garden at Zhu Manor belonged to Zhu Shuzhen; Wangyue Pavilion belonged solely to Qianli Xun. Divorcing Lin Jue had been step one toward her own life, and moving out of Zhu Manor was step two.

想起昨日之事，面对嫂嫂，千里寻还真有些不好意思：“嫂嫂知道还问。”
"Why so quiet?" Chen Wenxin asked.

“你呀！一个大家闺秀，还是江南女词人，又是砸石头，又是劈椅子的，像什么样？传出去不被人笑话才怪。”陈文心埋怨中充满关切。
Pulling back from her thoughts, Qianli Xun switched to family chatter. "How is Mother?"

千里寻在嫂嫂面前没必要隐瞒真实想法：“笑话就笑话，我才不怕，只要别让我嫁给陈浩宇就行。”
"Mother-in-law is fine—stop changing the subject and tell me why you stormed my parents' house yesterday!" Chen Wenxin cut straight to the chase.

陈文心也心疼淑真，自家弟弟不成器，临安城内谁家大家闺秀能瞧上？若真有瞧上的十有八九是冲着陈家的钱财而来。淑真无论品貌还是家世都好，嫁给浩宇实在是亏大发。
Faced with her sister-in-law, Qianli Xun felt a twinge of sheepishness. "Why ask when you already know?"

作为嫂嫂自然希望淑真能嫁给称心之人；可作为陈家人，又希望淑真能嫁到陈家，与浩宇好好过日子。
"Honestly, you! A noble daughter and Jiangnan's celebrated poetess—smashing rocks and splintering chairs! What kind of spectacle is that? Word gets out and half the city will laugh!" Reproach mingled with genuine affection in her voice.

“淑真，有些男子生下来就好，走的也是正道；有些男子则需先走弯道，最终才会拐到正道上。我觉得浩宇是第二种，你需对他有耐心，说不定你嫁给他后，他会很快走上正道。”
No need to hide behind polite masks with Chen Wenxin. "Let them laugh—I do not care, so long as I do not have to marry Chen Haoyu."

嫂嫂语重心长的话，千里寻并未听进心里：“陈浩宇是哪种人跟我没关系，再说我也没有义务为改变陈浩宇而嫁给他，改变他是陈家长辈的事。”
Chen Wenxin ached for her too. Her younger brother was a hopeless rake; what respectable lady in Lin'an would want him unless chasing Chen money? Shuzhen had beauty, brains, and family—marrying Haoyu was a raw deal by any measure. As a sister-in-law, she wanted Shuzhen to find true joy; yet as a Chen daughter, she still hoped Shuzhen would marry in and straighten Haoyu out.

陈文心见淑真油盐不进，不想再好言相劝：“你既如此想，我也无话可说。今日我回娘家，爹娘告诉我，你与浩宇的婚事推迟一段时日，等一切筹备好后，按照重新约定的时间举行，公爹已同意。”
"Shuzhen, some men are born steady and walk the straight path from day one; others have to wander down crooked roads before turning straight. I believe Haoyu is the second kind. Have a little patience with him—once you marry him, he might turn around faster than you think."

千里寻急了：“你们陈家不是想耗着吗？怎么又变？”
Her heartfelt plea bounced straight off Qianli Xun. "What kind of man Chen Haoyu is has nothing to do with me, and I have zero obligation to marry him just to fix him. Raising him is the Chen elders' job."

“那是奶奶的想法，我爹娘只是表面上顺着奶奶，实际上已跟公爹商议好对策。你呀，什么也别想，老实嫁到陈家。”
Seeing she could not budge Shuzhen an inch, Chen Wenxin dropped the persuasion. "Think what you like—I will save my breath. I went home to visit today, and my parents told me your wedding with Haoyu is postponed for a spell until preparations are perfected, then goes ahead on a new date. Father-in-law already agreed."

嫂嫂得意的表情千里寻讨厌不起来，但也需怼回去：“嫁又能怎样？你弟弟都给我说过，即使成亲，我们也是各过各的。”
Qianli Xun sat bolt upright. "Wait—was your family not planning to stall forever? Why did it change?!"

“没事，没事，只要你愿意嫁就好。”陈文心不想再聊下去，怕再说下去，姑嫂的关系会受到影响，她站起来，“淑真，你忙，我该回去，改日再来看你。”
"Stalling was Grandmother's notion. My parents only humored her to her face while settling the real plan with Father-in-law. Stop scheming, girl, and get ready to be a Chen bride."

嫂嫂匆忙离开，千里寻觉得好笑，嫂嫂也太小瞧她，她才不会因为此事对嫂嫂有看法。想到婚事只是推迟而已，又没心情再笑下去。
Unable to stay mad at her sister-in-law's smug grin, Qianli Xun still lobbed a comeback. "Even if I marry him, so what? Your own brother told me we will live our own separate lives."

千里寻发现自己知道婚期只是推迟一段时日后，虽不高兴，可不高兴的情绪已没以前那么强烈，肯定是因为她已想清楚若必须嫁到陈家该如何做。她更从心底佩服韦嬷嬷。
"Fine, fine—just as long as you walk down the aisle." Afraid pushing further might strain their sisterly bond, Chen Wenxin rose. "Keep busy, Shuzhen. I will head back and visit another day."

她累了，不愿再理会取消婚约之事。该做的已做，接下来只能听天由命。不嫁当然好，嫁了也有好处，起码可以让申简辰死心。
Watching her sister-in-law beat a hasty retreat, Qianli Xun chuckled—Chen Wenxin underestimated her if she thought a disagreement could sour their bond. Yet once the chuckle faded, realizing the wedding was only postponed wiped away her humor.

﻿不管什么结果，她只需往好处想。
Oddly enough, though the news annoyed her, the panic and fury were nowhere near as fierce as before—because she had already mapped out how to survive if she ended up at Chen Manor. Respect for Matron Wei's wisdom deepened in her chest. She was tired of fighting the engagement. She had done all she could; let fate take the wheel from here. Staying unwed was great; marrying had its silver lining too—at least it would force Shen Jianchen to give up. Whichever way it fell, she would look on the bright side.
