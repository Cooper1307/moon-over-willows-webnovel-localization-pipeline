第250章 宴会之后的筹谋
## Chapter 250: Scheming After the Banquet

两位主人聊他们的话题，白风与南双许久未见，坐在池塘边也聊着两人关心的事。
“你见过苦荞？”
“当然，今儿一早见过她。”
“告诉你件事，幼凡喜欢上我。公子说等我再长两岁，让我娶幼凡过门。你呢？什么时候娶苦荞？”
白风想了一会儿：“王爷已成亲，按说也该我，我找机会向王爷提提，他应该会同意。”
“我觉得你跟喜兰在一起会更方便一些，可你偏偏喜欢苦荞，恐怕要娶苦荞没那么容易。”
白风十分不解：“什么意思？”
南双向公子那边瞧一眼，王爷与公子聊得甚欢，并未注意他们，放心说道：“我听幼凡说，郡主，不，现在应该称仪王妃，十分讨厌幽栖居士，恐怕你想娶苦荞，仪王妃会阻拦。”
白风疑惑道：“幼凡怎么知道？”
“你回汴梁前，我家少夫人开始与仪王妃走得近，每回都带着幼凡，她什么事都清楚。”
白风并未当回事：“那是仪王妃与幽栖居士之间的事儿，怎么会牵连到我跟苦荞？况且仪王妃人很好，才不会阻拦我跟苦荞。再说我是王爷的人，王妃也管不上。”
“话虽如此，我还是建议别着急娶苦荞，等幽栖居士和仪王妃之间的恩怨了结后再向仪王提及此事。”
白风犹豫着：“谢谢你提醒我，我会视情况而定。”
乔文仙与仪王在神柳下畅聊后，两人又回幽林别居畅饮一回，天黑时他才离开。
忙了一日伙计的事儿，陈浩宇多少学到些本事，连青瓜也跟着进步不少。眼瞅着天黑，想起学做茶一事，今日没腾出时间，只能等待明日。
回到陈府的墨香苑，陈浩宇见素心草堂的门关着，里面的灯也熄灭，才知自己回来得晚，再晚也需向娘子禀报一声。
他靠近素心草堂的木门，声音稍微放大：“娘子，我回来了，今儿忙一整天，秋管事先带我了解绸缎庄，然后让我从伙计做起，我才发现伙计也不好当。今日常跟我混的那些个人跑来勾引我，我都没上当，以后我会老老实实的，谁让我喜欢上娘子。”
汇报半天，没有将朱淑真吵醒，隔壁悦宁屋的门却被打开，红豆探出脑袋：“姑爷，大晚上的您吵什么？小姐已经睡下，您也赶紧休息吧。”
红豆说完又把门关上。
陈浩宇批评道：“这丫头，怎么对你家姑爷说话呢？”
青瓜说：“公子，赶紧回去睡吧，来来回回招呼客人，我的腿都累酸了。”
陈浩宇很不舍地瞧着木门：“不理我是吧，等我说多了，总有一日你会理我。”
While their two masters talked, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang sat by the pond catching up after their long separation.

两人向后院走去，走到半道，碰见丑丫。
"Did you see Kuqiao?" Nan Shuang asked.

“姑爷回来了，我能不能跟青瓜说句话？”
"Of course. Saw her first thing this morning."

陈浩宇竟然羡慕起青瓜，一回来就有人关心，而他用心汇报，娘子理都不理。
"Got news for you—Youfan has taken a shine to me. Young Master says once I'm two years older, he'll let me marry her. What about you? When are you marrying Kuqiao?"

“随便说，时间不能太长。”
Bai Feng mulled it over. "His Highness is married now, so I suppose it's my turn. I'll find a good moment to bring it up with him—he's bound to agree."

“谢姑爷。”
"Honestly, pairing off with Xilan would've been way simpler for you. Of all people, you had to pick Kuqiao. Marrying her might not go so smooth."

丑丫高兴地把青瓜拉到一边，将手中的一块糕点塞到青瓜手里：“专门给你留的，味道很好。”
Bai Feng frowned. "What do you mean?"

青瓜虽然不情愿，还是接过吃起来。
Nan Shuang stole a glance toward their masters—Prince Yi and Qiao Wenxian were deep in conversation, paying them no mind—and lowered his voice. "Youfan told me that Princess Jiahe—no, Princess Consort Yi now—hates Youqi Jushi's guts. If you try to marry Kuqiao, the Princess Consort might step in and block it."

陈浩宇瞧一眼，羡慕不已，什么时候娘子也能这么对待他？是不是他也应该找一个喜欢自己的？很快，他否认了这一想法。
"How would Youfan know that?"

喜欢他的人实在好遇，他喜欢的还是破天荒头一遭。他陈浩宇就是这么贱，就是喜欢他喜欢的人，再难也要迎难而上。再说都娶了她，还有什么好担心的？朱淑真迟早会被他搞定。
"Right before you left for Bianliang, my Young Madam started getting chummy with the Princess Consort, and Youfan tags along every time. She knows all the dirt."

门外传来陈浩宇的声音时，千里寻刚有些迷糊，被那家伙吵醒。本想起身披上外衣，打开门上前教训一顿，但听到陈浩宇当了一日伙计，应该很辛苦，想着他的确与往日不同，便打消教训的念头。
Bai Feng brushed it off. "That's between the Princess Consort and Youqi Jushi—why would it spill over onto me and Kuqiao? Besides, the Princess Consort has a gentle heart; she wouldn't get in our way. And I belong to Prince Yi's staff anyway, not hers."

暂时睡不着，干脆躺着胡思乱想。先构思一会儿明日的话本该写什么故事更为合适，接着不由自主想起个人感情。想要的目前来讲已不可能，不想要的真实地摆在眼前，接还是不接？人生最为难的事情莫过于此。
"Even so, take my advice and hold off on proposing. Wait until the bad blood between Youqi Jushi and the Princess Consort blows over before you ask His Highness."

千里寻又想起净光法师说的“正思正念”，面对人生难题，正确的想法又是什么？
Bai Feng hesitated. "Thanks for the heads-up. I'll play it by ear."

思量来思量去，接受陈浩宇，老老实实当他的娘子才是最正确的念头，可她现在还无法接受。看来人生中总有一些正确的道路人不乐意踏上，还需正确的道路与心意相合。
After their long talk under the willow, Qiao Wenxian and Shen Jianchen returned to Youlin Retreat for a round of drinks, parting ways only after nightfall.

为什么人需有的正思正念经常会与心意不相合？也许人生是一场修行，人需时时改变心意，才能踏上正确的道路，只是这修行之路对千里寻来讲实在艰难。
Over at the silk emporium, a full day playing clerk had taught Chen Haoyu a fair few tricks, and even Qinggua had picked up plenty. Seeing darkness settle outside, Haoyu remembered he still meant to learn tea-whisking, though today had slipped away; it would have to wait until tomorrow.

……
Returning to Moxiang Courtyard in Chen Manor, he found Suxin Thatched Hall shut and dark. He was late—still, late or not, he had to check in with his wife. Stepping close to the wooden door, he raised his voice a notch. "Wife, I'm back! Worked hard all day. First Steward Qiu gave me the tour, then put me up front as a clerk—turns out being a clerk isn't easy at all. Those loafers I used to run with showed up trying to lure me away, and I didn't fall for it once. I'm going to keep my nose clean from now on—what else can I do when I'm head over heels for you?"

既然是陈家出钱，临安的婚宴必然安排在醉云楼。
His long report got no stir from Zhu Shuzhen, though the door of Yuening Room next door creaked open and Hongdou poked her head out. "Young Master, what's with all the racket at this hour? Miss is already asleep. Go get some rest yourself." With that, she shut the door in his face.

醉云楼虽在儿媳名下，毕竟还是陈家的产业，陈江林作为家主，有权利随时启用，而且无需向儿媳打招呼。因此，千里寻对婚宴在醉云楼举办并不清楚。
"Hey—is that any way to talk to your master?" Haoyu grumbled.

醉云楼比京城任何一家酒楼布置得都豪华，配仪王的身份再合适不过；加之离钱塘江近，景色宜人，既可博仪王欢心，亦可令临安官员舒心。
"Come on, let's go to bed, Young Master," Qinggua groaned. "Running back and forth waiting on shoppers all day has my legs aching."

从仪王回汴梁那日，陈江林就差人将各大酒楼的名厨和有经验的伙计以高价请来，加上原有的名厨和总管，人员算是齐备。接下来准备采买之物，既有后厨要用的，也有布置婚宴场地需要的。
Haoyu cast a wistful look at the closed wooden door. "Keep ignoring me, fine. Sooner or later, after I talk enough, you'll answer."

申简辰来过醉云楼两次，谈不上熟悉，也不陌生。尤其是第二次来时，更令他印象深刻。
Halfway down the path to the back courtyard, Chouya stepped out to meet them. "You're back, Young Master. Might I have a quick word with Qinggua?"

二楼的隔间里，他与千里寻单独在一起，也许那是他们最后一次单独说话，以后想有这样的机会都难。
A stab of envy hit Haoyu. The second Qinggua walked through the gate, someone was waiting to fuss over him, while Haoyu poured his heart out at a closed door and got total silence. "Go ahead, just don't take long."

当申简辰带着梦容走下马车，出现在醉云楼大门外时，朱晞颜携带有身份的官员和家眷迎出来。大家先向两位贵人行礼问安，然后祝二位百年好合。
"Thank you, Young Master!" Beaming, Chouya tugged Qinggua aside and pressed a pastry into his hand. "Saved this just for you. It's delicious."

最令乔梦容喜欢的是，大家都很知趣，称她为“仪王妃”。
Reluctant as he looked, Qinggua still accepted it and took a bite.

接着仪王与各位官员去了男宾区，乔梦容则被向夫人牵着来到女宾区。她一路瞧着宴会的布置，豪华而不失典雅，心中甚是满意。
Watching from a few paces off, Haoyu sighed in envy. When would his wife ever dote on him like that? Maybe he should just find some girl who already liked him—no, he squashed that thought on the spot. Finding women who threw themselves at him was easy; falling in love himself was a once-in-a-lifetime miracle. That was just how perverse Chen Haoyu was: he only wanted the woman he loved, no matter how steep the climb. Besides, she was already his wife—what was there to worry about? Sooner or later, he would win Zhu Shuzhen over.

来到主桌位，桌上的美食不比汴梁的婚宴差，看来临安官员颇费心思。
Inside Suxin Thatched Hall, Qianli Xun had just been drifting off when Haoyu's voice outside woke her. Her first impulse was to throw on a robe, fling the door open, and chew him out—until she heard he had spent the whole day working as a shop clerk. He must be exhausted, and he really wasn't the same brat he used to be. She let the urge to lecture him fade.

乔梦容坐下，各位女眷才敢坐下，她体会到在汴梁无法体会的优越感。
Wide awake now, she lay in the dark letting her mind wander. First she mapped out a plot for tomorrow's huaben chapter, then her thoughts drifted, unbidden, back to matters of the heart. The man she wanted was out of reach; the man she did not want stood right in front of her, real and earnest. Accept him, or push him away? Life offered few riddles harder than this.

在汴梁时，皇家女眷众多，没有多少人会将她放在眼里，就连官家身边的近臣，她都需畏惧三分。可在这里，她成为所有女眷中身份极高之人，大家都得把她放在眼里。
Master Jingguang's words came back to her—*Right Thought and Right Mindfulness*. Faced with a knot like this, what counted as the "right" thought? Turning it over from every angle, accepting Chen Haoyu and settling down as his wife was obviously the right path—yet her heart still balked at walking it. Sometimes the right road and a person's own heart pointed in opposite directions. Why did they clash so often? Perhaps life was a kind of spiritual discipline, forcing you to reshape your own heart step by step until it fit the right path. For Qianli Xun, though, that discipline felt brutally hard.

她的身边坐着向夫人，朱淑真的亲娘，她谨慎地陪着笑脸，生怕一不小心得罪贵人。可她的女儿却胆大妄为走进哥哥的心里，还将她看重的友情踩得稀巴烂。等着吧，报复很快会到来。
Since the Chen family was footing the bill, Lin'an's wedding banquet for Prince Yi was bound to be held at Zuiyun Tower. Even though the tavern stood under his daughter-in-law's name, it remained Chen family property; as family head, Chen Jianglin could commandeer it whenever he pleased without asking her permission, so Qianli Xun had no idea the banquet was taking place there.

各位官员的女眷拜过仪王妃后，又巴结奉承一回；邹嬷嬷则看准时机，吩咐喜兰将丫鬟们叫进来。
Zuiyun Tower outshone even the grandest taverns in the capital—more than worthy of Prince Yi's rank—and its riverside setting along the Qiantang River promised both to delight the prince and put Lin'an's officials at ease. The very day Prince Yi had departed for Bianliang, Chen Jianglin had hired top chefs and seasoned waiters from every major establishment at steep wages to bolster Zuiyun Tower's existing kitchen and management, then stocked every delicacy and decoration needed for the feast.

只见进来七八位丫鬟，一个个手里托着木盘，盘中整齐摆放着锦盒，大家都不明所以。
Shen Jianchen had visited Zuiyun Tower twice before—neither a stranger nor a regular—and the second visit was seared into his memory. Upstairs in that private booth, he and Qianli Xun had spoken alone—perhaps for the very last time. Opportunities like that were gone for good.

邹嬷嬷解释道：“诸位夫人，仪王妃跟随殿下来到临安，承蒙照拂，自今日起，她愿与诸位成为朋友，希望在座的女眷有事没事去幽林别居坐坐，聊聊天儿，帮她解解闷儿。今日仪王妃带来见面礼，礼物不贵重，重在交个朋友。”
When Shen Jianchen escorted Qiao Mengrong from their carriage outside Zuiyun Tower's gates, Zhu Xiyan led Lin'an's titled officials and their families out to greet them. Everyone bowed and offered wishes for a hundred years of wedded bliss—and what delighted Qiao Mengrong most was how quick they all were to address her as "Princess Consort Yi."

只见丫鬟们从主桌起，一一将礼物给了。
Prince Yi moved on with the officials to the men's banquet hall, while Madam Xiang took Qiao Mengrong's hand to guide her to the ladies' wing. Drinking in the opulent yet tasteful decor along the way, Mengrong felt a quiet glow of satisfaction. At the head table, the spread rivaled the imperial wedding feast back in Bianliang; Lin'an's officials had spared no effort.

有些女眷打开，看到是鸽子蛋大小的珍珠，甚是惊讶喜悦，也觉出仪王妃真诚交友的心意来。
Not a single lady dared take her seat until Qiao Mengrong sat first, giving her a rush of supremacy she had never tasted in Bianliang. Back in the capital, imperial women swarmed the palace; hardly anyone gave a mere commandery princess a second glance, and even His Majesty's favorite courtiers had to be treated with deference. Here in Lin'an, she stood at the very pinnacle of every woman in the room, and every eye had to answer to her.

所有女眷拿到礼物后，均起身齐声拜谢：“感谢仪王妃垂爱！”
Right beside her sat Madam Xiang—Zhu Shuzhen's own mother—wearing a careful, anxious smile lest she give the slightest offense. Yet that woman's daughter had had the gall to worm her way into Brother's heart and trample the friendship Mengrong had treasured into the dirt. *Just wait,* Mengrong thought. *Payback is coming soon.*

宴会快结束时，喜兰分别在苗知瑜和白梅若耳边说了几句。
After the wives and daughters offered their bows and a round of flattery, Matron Zou chose her moment and signaled Xilan to summon the maids. Seven or eight servants filed in bearing lacquered trays neatly arranged with brocade boxes, drawing puzzled looks around the hall.

陈文心看在眼里，心想：“听说仪王妃与这二位走得近，看来没错。唉，以前她与淑真才是闺中密友，都怪那位挨千刀的仪王，偏偏喜欢上我家淑真，搞得朱陈两家紧张不说，淑真与仪王妃的友情想要修复都难。”
"Ladies," Matron Zou announced, "since arriving in Lin'an with His Highness, the Princess Consort has been grateful for your hospitality. From this day on, she hopes to count you all as friends, and welcomes you to drop by Youlin Retreat whenever you have a spare moment to chat and help pass the time. Today she has brought a small token of greeting—nothing grand, just a mark of friendship."

男宾区内，自然也是巴结奉承，申简辰甚觉无趣。人在江湖混，有些事身不由己，即使再不乐意，依然需笑脸应付。
Starting from the head table, the maids presented a box to every guest. Several ladies lifted their lids to reveal pearls the size of pigeon eggs, gasping in delighted shock and feeling certain of the Princess Consort's sincere desire to befriend them. As soon as every guest held her gift, the entire hall rose and bowed in unison. "We thank Princess Consort Yi for her gracious favor!"

宴会终于结束，申简辰将乔梦容送回幽林别居，他则收拾行李，继续乘坐马车前往司理参军的衙门报到。
As the feast neared its close, Xilan leaned down to whisper briefly into Miao Zhiyu's ear, then Bai Meiruo's.

哥哥收拾行李，乔梦容心里不好受，也并未前去阻拦。待会儿会有人来，哥哥走了，她正好光明正大住进哥哥的院子，在那里接见朋友，安排明日之事。
Chen Wenxin caught it all from her seat. *So the rumors about the Princess Consort growing close to those two are true,* she thought with a sigh. *Shuzhen used to be her closest friend. Blast that damned Prince Yi—of all women, why did he have to fall for Shuzhen? Now both the Zhu and Chen families are walking on eggshells, and Shuzhen's friendship with the Princess Consort may never recover.*

宴会上仪王妃身边的丫鬟悄悄耳语后，苗知瑜和白梅若心中甚喜，她们还未抽出时间到幽林别居拜访贵人，贵人竟然主动邀请她们，实在幸运。
Over in the men's hall, flattery and toasts dragged on just as tediously, boring Shen Jianchen half to death. Still, living in this world meant swallowing plenty of things you hated and plastering on a polite smile anyway.

宴会结束后，二人不约而同来到幽林别居。她们的马车刚停下，两人通过马车的窗户瞧见白侍卫赶着仪王的马车离开，她们连上前打招呼的机会都没有。
When the banquet finally wrapped up, Shen Jianchen saw Qiao Mengrong back to Youlin Retreat, then packed his trunks and rode off in his carriage to report for duty at the Judicial Investigator's yamen.

二人走下马车，进入幽林别居。喜兰正在半路上等着，将她们带进正院。
Watching Brother pack stung Qiao Mengrong, yet she did not lift a finger to stop him. Guests were on their way; with Brother gone, she could move right into his main courtyard in the open, receive her allies there, and put tomorrow's scheme into motion.

眼前的院子比上次来时见到的院落更大。也是，郡主已成仪王妃，自然与仪王住在一处。
Thrilled by Xilan's quiet invitation at the banquet—they hadn't even found a chance to call on the royal bride yet, and here she was summoning them herself!—Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo both hurried straight to Youlin Retreat after the feast. Just as their carriages pulled up, both caught sight through their windows of Guard Bai driving Prince Yi's carriage away, leaving no chance to greet him.

她们一起进入前厅，先行礼拜见贵人：“仪王妃万福。”
Stepping down, the two women entered Youlin Retreat, where Xilan stood waiting along the path to escort them into the main courtyard. It was far grander than the side courtyard they had visited before—natural enough, now that Princess Jiahe was Princess Consort Yi and shared quarters with the prince.

乔梦容客气道：“两位快请坐，喜兰，上茶。”
Entering the front hall together, they bowed low. "Blessings upon Princess Consort Yi."

二人各自落座，喜兰将茶给二人摆上后继续站在郡主身后伺候。
"Please, sit," Qiao Mengrong said with smooth courtesy. "Xilan, bring tea."

不等二人说话，乔梦容主动说：“我想明日在幽林别居举办一场诗会，将二位请来，是想让你们帮我出出主意。”
Once they were seated and Xilan had set down their cups before taking her post behind her mistress, Qiao Mengrong wasted no time. "I plan to host a poetry salon here at Youlin Retreat tomorrow. I asked the two of you over to help me plan it."

二人心领神会，明白仪王妃的目的并不在诗会。
Both women caught her drift at once—poetry was hardly the real point.

白梅若先说：“不知王妃举办诗会想请哪些人？”
Bai Meiruo spoke first. "Whom does Your Highness intend to invite?"

乔梦容说：“人不在多，关键在精，该请的人一定要请。”
"Quality over quantity," Qiao Mengrong replied. "The right people must be there."

白梅若继续说：“幽栖居士一定要请。”
"Youqi Jushi, of course," Bai Meiruo prompted.

乔梦容点点头：“不仅要请她，还需品品她的诗词才好。”
Qiao Mengrong nodded. "Not only must we invite her—we should critique her verses too."

三人不约而同露出笑容。
All three women broke into knowing smiles.

苗知瑜说：“既然是品幽栖居士的诗词，不如我献上一首。昨日文渊让南双到陈府向幽栖居士讨得一首词，我觉甚好，诗会中定会令仪王妃满意。”
Miao Zhiyu leaned forward. "If we're critiquing Youqi Jushi's poetry, let me contribute a piece. Yesterday Wenyuan sent Nan Shuang over to the Chen Manor to ask her for a new lyric. I find it quite... fitting. It's sure to please Your Highness at tomorrow's gathering."

乔梦容说：“我相信苗夫人的眼光，到时还请苗夫人多带些闺中密友前来，一定要告诉她们如何品好幽栖居士的词。”
"I trust Madam Miao's judgment," Qiao Mengrong said. "Bring several of your close female friends along, too—and be sure to brief them beforehand on just how to critique Youqi Jushi's work."

苗知瑜说：“王妃放心，我定会安排妥当。”
"Leave it to me, Your Highness. I'll arrange everything."

白梅若想到一个好主意：“诗会中，我们必然高谈阔论，甚是难得，不如让人将这些高谈阔论记录下来，刊印成册，免费发放，相信要不了多久，幽栖居士的‘美名’更会广为人知。”
A bright spark crossed Bai Meiruo's face. "Our discussions at the salon are bound to be full of brilliant insights—what a waste if they vanished into thin air! Why not have everything written down, printed into booklets, and handed out free all over town? Before long, Youqi Jushi's 'illustrious reputation' will be known far and wide."

乔梦容听后大加赞赏：“白夫人的主意甚好，你们二人都识文断字，苗夫人更是才女，此事交由二位，事成之后，定有奖赏。”
Qiao Mengrong's eyes lit with approval. "An inspired idea, Madam Bai. Both of you are well-read, and Madam Miao is a true literary talent. I'll entrust this task to you two—once it's done, you'll be handsomely rewarded."

三人筹谋一番后，乔梦容才与二位拉起家常：“我离开这段时日，不知临安有没有什么趣事发生？”
With the scheme settled, Qiao Mengrong eased into casual chatter. "Has anything amusing happened in Lin'an while I was away?"

苗知瑜说：“趣事倒没有，不过，我听闺中密友议论过一件事。”
"Nothing amusing," Miao Zhiyu replied, "though my friends have been buzzing over one piece of gossip."

乔梦容敏锐地感觉出与朱淑真有关：“什么事？不妨说来听听。”
Sensing it involved Zhu Shuzhen, Qiao Mengrong perked up. "What gossip? Tell me."

苗知瑜说：“听说幽栖居士亲自带人冲进春香楼，把陈公子绑回陈府后给关起来。”
"Word is Youqi Jushi personally led servants straight into Chunxiang Brothel, tied up Young Master Chen, hauled him back to Chen Manor, and locked him up."

白梅若应和道：“我也听说，她一个大家闺秀竟然跑到那种地方找夫君，真不害臊。”
Bai Meiruo chimed in with a sniff. "I heard the same! A proper lady from a respectable family barging into a place like that to drag her husband home—how shameless!"

苗知瑜继续说：“昨日我回娘家，听我母亲说幽栖居士十分厉害，竟然把陈公子给管教过来，也不知她使了什么手段。”
"And when I visited my parents' house yesterday," Miao Zhiyu added, "my mother told me Youqi Jushi is quite formidable—she actually whipped Young Master Chen into shape, though nobody knows what tricks she used."

乔梦容问：“秦夫人如何得知？”
"How did Madam Qin hear about that?" Qiao Mengrong asked.

苗知瑜回道：“我母亲喜欢打马吊，陈家二爷的娘子包氏偶尔也喜欢打马吊，会与我母亲约在一起。昨日两人又在一起玩儿，我母亲正是从她口中得知。我母亲还说，包氏提起陈浩宇变化之事，看上去并不高兴。”
"My mother loves playing madiao, and Second Master Chen's wife, Madam Bao, plays with her now and then. They got together for a game yesterday, and Madam Bao spilled it all. My mother also noticed Madam Bao looked none too thrilled when she mentioned Chen Haoyu turning over a new leaf."

乔梦容问：“为何？”
"Why?"

苗知瑜说：“我母亲猜测可能包氏担心陈浩宇变好会影响自家儿子继承陈家家业。”
"Mother guessed Madam Bao is worried Chen Haoyu shaping up might threaten her own son's shot at inheriting the Chen fortune."

乔梦容心生一计：“辛苦苗夫人向秦夫人转告，多约包氏打几回马吊，将陈家的事情了解清楚，也许以后会有用。”
A fresh plot crystallized in Qiao Mengrong's mind. "Please pass word to Madam Qin for me—ask her to invite Madam Bao for a few more games of madiao and sound out everything going on inside the Chen Manor. It may prove useful down the line."

“是。”苗知瑜答应着，心下思量，幽栖居士得罪仪王妃，恐怕以后没有好日子过。
"Yes, Your Highness," Miao Zhiyu agreed, thinking to herself that after crossing Princess Consort Yi, Youqi Jushi's good days were numbered.
