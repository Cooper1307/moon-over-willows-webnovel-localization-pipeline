## Chapter 334: The Jin Army Marches into Yanjing

Halting Physician Liu at the door, Qianli Xun offered an impromptu quatrain on the white chrysanthemums:

*"Swirling autumn hues gather tender morning dew; / fierce west winds polish crisp petals white as frost. / Never dismiss them as common blooms by the eastern hedge— / their cool purity once distilled the fragrance of Guanghan's Moon Palace."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Dazzled—and thrilled upon learning she was *the* Youqi Jushi—Physician Liu happily delayed his move to Yangzhou, coming to Yunlai Inn next morning and administering three days of cranial acupuncture. Though still dreamy and forgetful, Mengrong stopped raving and received a six-month herbal prescription for full recovery before Physician Liu hurried south.

Seeing Mengrong stable, Qianli Xun prepared to return to Lin'an—ignoring Bai Feng's blurted *"Your Highness, the edict—!"* cut sharp by Shen Jianchen—until Mengrong's clear, gentle voice called after her: *"Shuzhen, don't go! Walk the streets of Yanjing with me?"*

Overjoyed to hear her old friend—even though Mengrong still insisted Shen Jianchen was an imposter while her real Brother waited in Heaven—Qianli Xun linked hands with her and strolled out through Yanjing's fleeing crowds, sharing candied hawthorn skewers and tender forgiveness while Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng trailed protectively behind.

Suddenly thunderous hoofbeats shook the central avenue! Herding Bai Feng, Xilan, and Mengrong into an abandoned side-street house, Shen Jianchen and Qianli Xun vaulted onto the roofs to watch history unfold: **Wanyan Zongwang**, Prince of Wei, marched unopposed down Main Street as Yanjing's Song officials knelt offering surrender—until one defiant Song commander laughed loud behind them: *"Pack of traitors! I'll die fighting before I kneel!"*
