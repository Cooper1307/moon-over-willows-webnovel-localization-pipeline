第334章 金人领兵入燕京
## Chapter 334: The Jin Army Marches into Yanjing

刘妙手甚是喜爱：“这孩子懂我，送的菊花不俗，放到马车上，路上也可解闷儿。”
学徒答应完，退出门外去忙活。
刘妙手端好茶壶站起来：“二位若无诗词奉上，我可就走了，还要去接家里人一起南下，眼看着金人要打进来，我可不想待在燕京送死。”
申简辰问道：“外面看着一派祥和，你怎知金军要攻打大宋？”
刘妙手解释道：“道途听说，防患于未然，再说我早想举家搬到南边。好友在扬州为我置办好田产，现在不走更待何时？你们既没有诗词奉上，还是另请高明。”
刘妙手说着要往出走。
千里寻拦道：“神医且慢！”
刘妙手端着紫砂茶壶转过身：“二位还有事？”
千里寻有礼道：“刚才那盆白菊长得甚是喜人，让人过目难忘，不如我以‘白菊’为题，赋诗一首，赠予神医？”
刘妙手表现出极高的兴趣：“甚好！姑娘念来听听，若做得好，我答应你晚离开燕京几日，专程为病人诊治，若做得不好，只能由我的徒弟留下来帮忙诊治。”
“神医定会满意。”千里寻回想了一会儿诗句，念道，“回旋秋色漙情露，凌厉西风洁嫩霜。莫作东篱等闲看，清新曾结广寒香。”
刘妙手很快将诗句记在心间，心中默念一回，赞道：“姑娘才情斐然，白菊到了你的诗中，娇艳欲滴，含情怒放，还有种傲然秋风的精神，最妙的莫过于‘广寒香’既写出白菊开放的季节，还写出了它超脱世俗高洁清雅的一面。姑娘此等才华足以在诗坛占据一席之地。”
申简辰忍不住说：“神医有所不知，您面前这位姑娘正是江南女词人朱淑真。”
刘妙手将眼前的姑娘上下打量一番：“你就是闻名江南的女词人？号称幽栖居士？”
千里寻说：“正是。还请神医看在我千里迢迢而来甚是辛苦，帮我的朋友诊治。”
“没想到我今日有幸见到江南女词人，真是幸甚至哉！看在幽栖居士的面子上，我答应你们在燕京留几日。不过今日还不能帮病人诊治，我需将家人安顿好，让他们先去扬州，明日亲自登门。不知幽栖居士与公子暂住何处？”
申简辰回道：“云来客栈。”
“公子怎么称呼？”
“鄙人姓申”
“还请申公子与幽栖居士先回，明日一早我会亲自去云来客栈拜访。”
第二日，刘妙手果不食言，一早提着医箱来到云来客栈。
为乔梦容把完脉后，将她仔细观察一番，肯定地说：“这病除了我还真没有第二个人能医好。”
一旁的人听了无不高兴。
刘妙手又说：“我先为病人施针三日，需住在客栈，费用嘛……”
“神医尽管住下，费用不必忧心。”申简辰立刻向白风吩咐，“到楼下让小二给神医找间客房。”
白风领命下楼去安排。
神医在客栈住下后，每日按时为乔梦容在头部施诊。
乔梦容呆呆傻傻，倒也明白有人给她医病，乖乖配合，不哭不闹。
千里寻瞧着神医施诊的过程，既充满期待，又担心梦容好起来还会恨她。她决定，三日后直接从燕京回临安。她瞧向申简辰，他正专注地看神医施针。
千里寻在心中对他说：“三日后，你我就要分别，以后还不知何时能见？不管我有多么舍不得你，还是衷心希望梦容痊愈后，你能与她白头偕老。”
申简辰感觉到有双眼睛盯着自己，他看向千里寻，见她匆忙间移开眼神，心中偷乐：“偷偷看我，还不承认你喜欢我？”
三日施针结束时，梦容看上去比之前好了许多，不再要像生花，也不说些大家听不懂的话，只是呆呆的，眼神看上去还不怎么灵动。
刘妙手写好药方叮嘱道：“用这方子吃上半年，病人便可痊愈。”
任务已完成，刘妙手拿了诊金，匆忙南下追赶家人。
千里寻坐在床边，抓起梦容的手：“你很快会好起来，可我没办法见证那一天，今日我要回临安，回自己的家，你要好好的，希望我们再见时，依然像以前一样是闺中密友。”
乔梦容呆呆地坐在床上，没有丝毫反应。
千里寻放下梦容的手，起身走到申简辰面前：“我走了，你照顾好梦容。”
申简辰一把抓住她的手臂：“你……这么着急离开我？”
千里寻没有挣脱，淡淡地说：“梦容的病已有着落，是该回去的时候，否则家里人等得着急。”
“你……舍不得陈浩宇？”申简辰不顾白风和喜兰在一旁，直白地问道。
“我既嫁给他，应该回到临安的陈家。”
白风替王爷感到着急，埋怨王爷为何不说圣旨的事？若说了幽栖居士说不定会留下。可看到坐在床上的仪王妃，白风的情绪稳定一些，幽栖居士若留下，仪王妃的病即使好了，恐怕还会再犯。
可若幽栖居士回到临安跟陈公子在一起，岂不是违抗圣旨？白风忍不住脱口而出：“王爷，圣旨……”
“闭嘴！没你说话的份儿！”申简辰很不情愿地放开千里寻，“想走就走吧，过不了多久我还会去临安找你。”
“何必如此？”千里寻狠了狠心，继续向外走。
刚要跨出房门，身后传来梦容的声音：“淑真！你别走！好吗？”
梦容说话的声音太过正常，与好着时没有两样。千里寻激动之余停下脚步，转身快步走到床边坐下。
乔梦容用稍显呆滞的眼神看着她：“淑真，你别走，第一次来燕京，陪我逛街，好吗？”
千里寻忍不住流下眼泪：“好，我不走，陪你逛街。”
Liu Miaoshou beamed over the flowers. “That boy knows my taste—fine blooms. Set them in the carriage to brighten the road south.”

王妃说话像正常人一般，喜兰很开心：“王爷！王妃她好了！”
Once the apprentice carried the pot outside, Liu Miaoshou rose with his teapot. “If neither of you has a verse to offer, I must go fetch my family and head south. With the Jin about to attack, I have no wish to stay in Yanjing and die.”

王爷异常平静，喜兰的兴奋显得毫无意义。
“Everything looks peaceful out there,” Shen Jianchen noted. “How do you know the Jin will strike the Song?”

乔梦容看向仪王：“你为何长得跟我哥哥一模一样？只可惜，你不是哥哥，哥哥去了天上，他在那里等着我。”
“Word along the road. Better safe than sorry—and I’ve meant to move my household south for years. A friend already bought farmland for me in Yangzhou; if not now, when? Without a poem, go seek someone else.”

听到这话，白风和喜兰感到失落，仪王妃并未彻底好。看来还需用神医开的药好好调养，方可彻底痊愈。
“Wait, Great Physician!” Qianli Xun stepped in his path. “Those white chrysanthemums were so lovely they stay in the mind. Let me compose a poem on ‘White Chrysanthemum’ as a gift for you.”

申简辰倒觉得现在的梦容无比正常，好感萌生许多，他走到跟前：“我虽不是你的哥哥，只要你乖乖的，我会替他照顾你一辈子。”
Intrigued, Liu Miaoshou paused. “Good! Recite it. If it passes muster, I’ll stay in Yanjing a few extra days to treat your friend personally. If not, my disciple stays behind instead.”

“谢谢你。”乔梦容脸上露出笑容，看向淑真，“我不是躺在床上，就是待在屋里，好闷，现在就陪我逛街？”
“You won’t be disappointed,” Qianli Xun promised, recalled the lines, and recited:

千里寻点头答应：“听你的。”
“Swirling autumn hues hold thick the tender dew;

喜兰伺候仪王妃换好衣裳，扶着她走出客房外。
The keen west wind keeps pure the young frost.

乔梦容挣脱喜兰，上前拉起淑真的手：“我想和你牵着手逛街。”
Count it no common bloom beside the eastern hedge—

千里寻乖乖答应：“好。”
Fresh and clean, it once gathered the Moon Palace’s fragrance.” [TN: Translator’s rendering.]

两人不顾他人，牵着对方的手，一起走下楼，走出客栈。
Turning the lines over silently, Liu Miaoshou burst into praise. “What rare talent! Under your brush, white chrysanthemums gleam heavy with dew, glowing with tender feeling against the autumn wind—and that closing phrase, ‘the Moon Palace’s fragrance,’ captures both the season and their unworldly purity! Talent like yours deserves a seat of honor in the world of letters.”

喜兰快步跟上。
Unable to hold back, Shen Jianchen spoke up. “Perhaps you don’t know, Physician Liu—the young lady before you is none other than the southern poetess Zhu Shuzhen.”

白风请示道：“王爷，要不要跟上？”
Liu Miaoshou looked her up and down in wonder. “You are the famed poetess of Jiangnan—Youqi Jushi herself?”

申简辰说：“远远跟着，好护她们周全。”
“The very same,” she replied. “Please look kindly on our long journey and treat my friend.”

白风提醒道：“幽栖居士多厉害，哪需要我们保护？”
“To meet the southern poetess today is rare fortune! For Youqi Jushi’s sake, I’ll linger a few days in Yanjing. Today I must see my family off toward Yangzhou first; tomorrow morning I will call on you myself. Where are you staying?”

申简辰瞪了白风一眼，没说一句话，也下了楼。
“Yunlai Inn,” Shen Jianchen answered, giving his surname as Shen when asked.

白风自语道：“别以为我不知道，是王爷自己想跟幽栖居士逛街。”
True to his word, Liu Miaoshou arrived at Yunlai Inn first thing the next morning with his medicine chest. Taking Mengrong’s pulse and studying her closely, he declared with certainty, “Besides me, nobody alive could cure this.”

步行来到街上，申简辰一直跟在身后，保持一定的距离。他顾不上关注大街上背着包袱出城的人，一心放在前面两位女子的身上。
Joy lit everyone’s faces. Liu Miaoshou asked for a room at the inn to perform three days of acupuncture, and Shen Jianchen told Bai Feng to cover every expense and book him quarters downstairs.

她们牵着手，比往常任何时候都显得亲密，画面实在美好。不用看正面便知两人正在开心地笑。此刻她们的关系已恢复到从前，申简辰希望时光永远停留在这一刻。
Over the next three days, Liu Miaoshou applied needles to Mengrong’s head on schedule. Dull as she was, she seemed to sense she was being treated and sat quietly without crying or fussing.

白风没有王爷那么多闲情逸致，他更关注街上背着包袱匆忙出城的人。他还看到小摊小贩的数量减少了一半，有一些店铺也已关门，如此看来燕京城要发生大事。
Watching, Qianli Xun felt hope mixed with dread that a healed Mengrong might hate her anew. She resolved to head straight back to Lin’an once the three days ended. Glancing at Shen Jianchen’s absorbed profile, she prayed silently that after they parted in three days, Mengrong would recover and the two of them would grow old together in peace.

白风拦住一位背着行李的妇人：“大婶，为什么很多人像逃难似的？到底发生了什么？”
Sensing her eyes on him, Shen Jianchen looked over—only for her to snap her gaze away, making him grin to himself that she still wouldn’t admit her feelings.

大婶急切说：“金人要进城了，快逃吧，只怕到时连命都没了。”大婶说完，匆匆离开。
By the end of the third day, Mengrong no longer begged for silk flowers or babbled nonsense, though her gaze remained vacant. Leaving a prescription to be taken for six months until full recovery, Liu Miaoshou collected his fee and hurried south after his family.

白风靠近仪王：“王爷，金人要来，还是尽快离开燕京吧？”
Sitting on the bed, Qianli Xun took Mengrong’s hand. “You’ll recover soon, though I can’t stay to see that day. I’m going home to Lin’an today. Take care of yourself—when we meet again, let’s still be the closest of friends.”

“今日陪她们逛街，明日回。”申简辰并不当回事，他实在不想破坏眼下的气氛。
Mengrong sat blankly without a twitch. Letting go, Qianli Xun stood before Shen Jianchen. “I’m leaving. Look after Mengrong.”

白风不好再说什么，继续跟着。他想不通，街上卖东西的人越来越少，仪王妃和幽栖居士有什么好逛的？还那么起劲儿？
He caught her arm at once. “In such a rush to leave me?”

千里寻感受着两年多来久违的友情，心思并未放在时不时擦肩而过逃亡百姓的身上。梦容说着时而正常，时而充满傻气的话语，她都爱听，只因那些话语没有一句充满怨恨。
She didn’t pull free, speaking evenly. “Her recovery is underway. It’s time I went home so my family stops worrying.”

“为什么街上很多人背着包袱？像是要离开？”
“Can’t bear to stay away from Chen Haoyu?” he asked bluntly right in front of Bai Feng and Xilan.

千里寻不想梦容害怕，编了一个善意的谎言：“估计都是像我们一样暂时来燕京的人，办完事打算回家。等你逛完街，我们也快些离开。”
“I married him. My place is at the Chen manor in Lin’an.”

“不，我不想走，我喜欢燕京。”
Sick with impatience over his master keeping silent about the decree, Bai Feng blurted out, “Your Highness, the Imperial Decree—”

“为什么？燕京比不上临安，更比不上汴梁。”
“Shut your mouth! Nobody asked you!” Reluctantly, Shen Jianchen let go of her arm. “Go if you must. Before long, I’ll come to Lin’an for you.”

“因为有你陪我。”
“Why bother?” Hardening her heart, Qianli Xun walked toward the door.

千里寻被感动：“恐怕往后我陪你的时间并不多。”
Just as she reached the threshold, Mengrong’s voice rang out behind her—clear and normal as before her illness. “Shuzhen! Don’t leave… all right?”

“为何？”
Joy froze Qianli Xun in place. Turning back, she hurried to the bed. Mengrong looked up with slightly sluggish eyes. “Don’t go, Shuzhen. It’s our first time in Yanjing—come walk the streets with me, please?”

千里寻站住，盯着梦容郑重地说：“因为我不想破坏属于你的幸福。”
Tears sprang to Qianli Xun’s eyes. “All right. I won’t go. I’ll walk with you.”

乔梦容一脸委屈：“我总感觉好久好久没见到你，你好像做了一件让我伤心的事，我很难过，可又想不起来是哪件事。”
Delighted, Xilan cried out that the Princess Consort was cured, though Shen Jianchen stayed sober when Mengrong looked right at him and murmured, “Why do you look identical to my brother? Too bad you aren’t him. Brother went up to heaven, waiting for me there.”

千里寻担心梦容会想起往事，小心问：“若我真的做过让你伤心的事，你会原谅我吗？”
Seeing she still needed the six months of medicine, Bai Feng and Xilan sank back down, yet Shen Jianchen stepped forward gently. “Even if I am not your brother, as long as you’re good, I’ll look after you for him my whole life.”

乔梦容宛然一笑：“一定是因为我病了，才会胡思乱想，你怎么会做伤害我的事？即使你真的做了，我也会原谅你。因为在我的记忆中，你是我唯一的闺中密友。”
Mengrong smiled, thanked him, and told Shuzhen she was sick of beds and locked rooms and wanted to go out right now.

千里寻的双眼再次湿润。
Once Xilan dressed her, Mengrong slipped away from her maid and laced her fingers through Qianli Xun’s. “I want to hold hands while we walk.”

乔梦容靠近她，悄悄说：“身后那人根本不是我哥哥，还要冒充他，你说他是不是骗子？”
Trailing with Xilan, Bai Feng asked whether they should follow. Telling him to keep a safe distance to guard them, Shen Jianchen ignored Bai Feng’s teasing reminder about Youqi Jushi’s fists and headed down behind them.

千里寻含泪说：“他是仪王赵朴，是你的哥哥。”
Out on the street, Shen Jianchen kept pace far enough back, ignoring streams of citizens hurrying toward the city gates with bundles, eyes fixed solely on the two women ahead walking hand in hand like old times—wishing time could freeze right there.

“连你也骗我。”乔梦容说完，看到不远处一个卖糖葫芦的，“我想吃糖葫芦，我们一人买一个吧？”
Less dreamy, Bai Feng noticed half the stalls gone and shops shuttered. Stopping a woman lugging baggage, he learned Jin troops were marching into the city!

不等淑真答应，乔梦容像个快乐的孩子一般，跑到卖糖葫芦的跟前。喜兰追上去，付了银子。
“Your Highness, the Jin are coming—let’s leave Yanjing now!” Bai Feng urged.

卖糖葫芦的人将两串糖葫芦递给乔梦容，她拿着一串咬了一口：“真甜。”
“We walk with them today and leave tomorrow,” Shen Jianchen dismissed him, unwilling to shatter the moment.

然后举起另一串朝淑真招呼：“快过来！这个给你！”
Puzzled as Bai Feng fell silent, Qianli Xun gently fibbed when Mengrong asked why folks carried bundles, claiming they were out-of-towners heading home.

千里寻走上前，接过糖葫芦，也吃上一口，赞道：“你买的糖葫芦就是好吃。”
“I don’t want to leave,” Mengrong murmured. “I like Yanjing because you’re with me.”

乔梦容朝后面的申简辰瞥上一眼：“我怎么感觉他老是看你，他会不会是喜欢上你？”
Moved, Qianli Xun stopped and spoke earnestly. “I won’t have much time to stay by you from now on, because I don’t want to spoil your happiness.”

千里寻担心梦容试探她，迅速解释：“怎么会？难道你忘了，我已经成亲。”
Looking hurt, Mengrong whispered, “I feel like I haven’t seen you in ages… like you did something that broke my heart, though I can’t remember what.”

“成亲？”乔梦容像是回想着以前的事，“你什么时候成亲的？我怎么一点儿印象都没有？我只记得乔公子喜欢你，难道你嫁给他？”
Tense, Qianli Xun asked softly, “If I really did hurt you once, would you forgive me?”

原来神医扎针的效果是让梦容忘记很多事，难怪她会如此真诚地对待她。
Mengrong smiled sweetly. “Only my sickness making me imagine things. How could you ever hurt me? Even if you did, I’d forgive you—in my memory, you’re my only bosom friend.”

“想什么呢？陪我继续逛街。”乔梦容拉起淑真的手，两人边吃糖葫芦边逛。
Tears blurred Qianli Xun’s eyes again. Leaning closer, Mengrong whispered that the man behind them was an impostor pretending to be her brother, then brightened at a vendor selling candied hawthorns and darted over like a child while Xilan paid.

当他们来到街市中心时，四周传来马蹄声。
Biting one skewer (“So sweet!”), Mengrong held the second out to Shuzhen, then glanced back at Shen Jianchen. “Why does he keep staring at you? Could he be falling for you?”

千里寻心想：“不好！难道金军已入城？”
“How could that be? Did you forget I’m married?”

只见街上的行人开始四处逃窜，小商贩快速收起摊位，商铺也立刻关了大门。
“Married? When did you marry? All I remember is Young Master Qiao liking you—did you marry him?”

申简辰走上前，指着另一条街：“那边的街道没有马蹄声，应该安全，我们尽快过去。”
Realizing the physician’s needles had wiped parts of her memory clean—explaining her sudden warmth—Qianli Xun let Mengrong pull her along down the avenue eating candied hawthorns.

几人快步走到另一条街，来到一个废弃的屋子跟前，申简辰吩咐道：“白风，你和喜兰带仪王妃在废弃的屋子里躲会儿。”
Then thunderous hooves shattered the market square!

白风问：“王爷要做什么？”
Jin troops inside the city! Pedestrians screamed and scattered; peddlers grabbed stalls and bolted; heavy shop doors slammed shut.

“这么大的历史事件，我又岂能错过？”申简辰转向千里寻，“要不要一起去瞧瞧？”
Shen Jianchen rushed up, pointing toward an empty side street free of hoofbeats. Hurrying down it to an abandoned house, he ordered Bai Feng and Xilan to hide inside with Mengrong.

千里寻点头同意：“说不定我们两个还能一起干掉几个金人。”
“What are you doing, Your Highness?” Bai Feng asked.

白风不好再阻拦，推开废弃屋子的门。乔梦容一脸懵懵懂懂，却也知道发生了大事，任由众人安排。
“Think I’d miss witnessing a moment of history this huge?” Shen Jianchen asked Qianli Xun. “Care to watch with me?”

见三人已躲好，千里寻和申简辰一起用轻功跳上街边的屋顶，他们蹲在屋顶向主街瞧去。
“Might even knock off a few Jin soldiers while we’re at it,” she grinned.

金军的大部队不费一兵一卒已进入主街。
As the other three slipped behind the door, Shen Jianchen and Qianli Xun vaulted cleanly onto the rooftops overlooking the main avenue.

管理燕京的官员和将领拿着降书来到主街跪下，手举降书，口称：“魏王在上，下官愿携上下官员与燕京将领归顺金国。”
Below, the Jin vanguard marched straight in without shedding a drop of blood. Yanjing’s Song officials and commanders dropped to their knees in the street, lifting a surrender document. “Your servant presents the officials and garrison commanders of Yanjing in submission to the Jin!”

坐在马背上的完颜宗望很是满意：“若我大金所到之处宋人皆如你一般知趣，岂不是天下太平？燕京本就是金人从辽国手中夺得，又被大宋的皇帝要回去，给就给了，谁让盟约在先。只可惜是我大金的国土要也要不走，今日照样还得回到金人的手中。”
Mounted on horseback, Wanyan Zongwang smiled down. “If every Song town knew its place like you, the world would know peace! We took Yanjing from the Liao and returned it to your Emperor out of treaty honor—yet land that belongs to Great Jin returns to Jin hands sooner or later.”

有金人跳下马从官员的手中接过降书，送至完颜宗望手中。他展开仔细瞧一遍：“态度诚恳，言辞恳切，既已是金国子民，都起来吧。”
A Jin soldier took the document up to Wanyan Zongwang, who skimmed it and bade the new subjects of Jin rise.

申简辰小声骂道：“一群卖国贼！”
“Pack of traitors!” Shen Jianchen hissed on the roof.

千里寻说：“官家昏庸，他们不投降才怪。”
“With an emperor that muddled, no shock they surrender,” Qianli Xun muttered.

这时，大宋官员的身后传来一阵笑声：“哈哈哈……！尔等都是一群卖国贼！我今日就是战死在此！也绝不投降！”
Just then a wild laugh rang out behind the kneeling officials: “Hahaha! Nothing but traitors, every last one of you! Better to die fighting right here than ever surrender!”
