第272章 陈府门外遇麻烦
## Chapter 272: Trouble Waiting Outside Chen Manor

两人用完饭，又逛一会儿消消食，逛得差不多时，终于逛累，才租辆马车回陈府。
今日陪娘子逛，没有时间去永泰茶楼跟女茶师学习做茶，就当是体验生活。
女茶师说过，做茶的方法基本传授给他，还需多体验生活，将生活的体验融入茶技中，做出的茶会更加高级。
陈浩宇不大懂，不过以此为理由不去找女茶师倒也冠冕堂皇。
马车到达陈府南面离门口不远处时，一群丫鬟装扮的女子排成一排站在大门外。
陈浩宇从马车里探出脑袋一瞧，全是他以前从乡下抢来的。心中纳闷，不是把她们安置在陈家的其他宅子里吗？怎么一个个都跑到这里来？
因担心娘子生气，陈浩宇匆忙间找出理由：“娘子，我突然想起来还要到永泰茶楼跟女茶师学习做茶，要不你陪我一起去？”
千里寻敏锐地觉察出不对劲，也向马车外瞧，虽不知发生了什么，大略已猜到与陈浩宇有关。
“今日逛得累，不必再去学习做茶。不如与我下马车，看看外面到底发生了什么？”
已无法避免，陈浩宇只能答应：“娘子别生气，我这就跟你一起下去。”
“看来那些人与你有关？”
从陈浩宇的表情能判断出，外面的人是来找他。
千里寻想看看到底发生了什么，她起身走下马车。
陈浩宇担心不已，没敢尾随娘子，继续躲在马车里。
千里寻没有急着上前打扰，她想站在原地观察一会儿再做打算。
那些丫鬟一个个长得不敢说花容月貌，也颇具姿色。
千里寻朝马车里小声批判：“好色之徒！”
陈浩宇小声回道：“娘子批评得是。”
王管家带着几名下人从陈府走出来，因着急应对丫鬟们，并未注意到不远处的马车和少夫人。
他来到丫鬟们面前劝道：“大家回去吧，二公子不在府中，即使在也不会见你们，能在陈府当丫鬟已经不错，难道还想登堂入室不成？赶紧回其他宅子去，该干啥干啥。”
丫鬟们纷纷说：
“今日不见到陈公子我们绝不走！”
“他把我们抢来不能就这么算了！”
“就是！不能就这么算了！”
一个丫鬟哭着从队伍中走出来：“呜呜，王管家有所不知，二公子把我们抢来，已将我们……，呜呜，他只顾自己娶娘子，我们往后可怎么办？呜呜……”
许多丫鬟跟着哭起来，陈府外立刻一片哭声。
王管家与几个下人左哄右哄不见成效，干脆站在一旁干瞪眼。
刚对陈浩宇产生的好感立刻去大半，千里寻很想将陈浩宇从马车里揪出来让丫鬟们一个个将他狠狠揍一顿。
毕竟已开始承认作为陈家二少夫人的身份，千里寻警告过自己，往后做事不能太鲁莽。
她向马车里说：“陈浩宇，你要是承认自己是男人就下来，当着我的面将此事解决。”
娘子的话，陈浩宇必须听。何况他要不出面处理就不是男人，以后岂不是要在娘子面前矮半截？
他从车里下来，掏出碎银，给了车夫：“赶紧走，别在一旁看热闹。”
车夫接过银子，已对热闹不感兴趣，把马车赶走。
众人听到赶马车的声音，才向这边瞧来。
还没等王管家上前行礼，众位哭闹的丫鬟一起涌上去，将陈浩宇围住。
作为正主的千里寻竟然被挤到一边。
她心里更不爽，倒要见识一下陈浩宇怎么处理眼前的麻烦事。哼！自己拉的屎，还是自己擦屁股得好！
众位丫鬟哭哭啼啼：
“陈公子，你是不是不要我了？呜呜……”
“陈公子，你不是说以后会把我放在身边做妾室吗？为何成亲了不兑现承诺？呜呜……”
“陈公子，我把自己都给你了，你要是不要我，我就不想活了，呜呜……”
“陈公子，你既把我抢来，就应该对我负责，否则你抢个什么劲儿啊？呜呜……”
“陈公子，我不图别的，只想跟着你，你可千万不要不管我，呜呜……”
“陈公子，听说二少夫人是只母老虎，你这么久没来找我，该不会是她把你管得太严，呜呜……”
陈浩宇抢来的丫鬟们你一言我一语，加上哭声，搞得陈府大门外甚是热闹。
陈浩宇人被围着，眼睛却看向娘子，担心自己的努力因此情此景而泡汤。娘子的脸色并不好看，却也没要走的意思，她应该是想看他如何解决麻烦。
如何解决？陈浩宇根本不知，除了后悔仍是后悔，总得说些什么。
“别哭了！再哭我可谁都不理！”
这话果真管用，丫鬟们都不再大声哭泣，抹着眼泪小声抽泣。
After lunch they strolled a bit more to walk off the meal before hiring a carriage back to Chen Manor. Skipping today's tea lesson had a convenient excuse—the tea master had said real tea artistry required soaking up real life.

“都站好了！”
Nearing Chen Manor's south gate, a whole row of maids stood lined up outside. Peeking out the window, Haoyu recognized every last face—girls he had snatched from the countryside back in his wild days! Wait—weren't they tucked away in the Chen family's side estates? Why were they camped at the front gate?!

丫鬟们还真听话，整整齐齐站成一排，继续抹眼泪。
Panic hit. "Wife! Just remembered I've got a tea lesson at Yongtai Teahouse—come along with me?"

陈浩宇指着丫鬟们：“你！你！你！还有你！向前一步！”
Sensing trouble instantly, Qianli Xun leaned out to look. "Too tired to watch you whisk tea. Come step down and see what's going on."

被指的四个丫鬟都向前迈出一步。
Cornered, Haoyu groaned, "Don't get mad, Wife..."

陈浩宇训斥道：“你们四个本公子一根汗毛未动，凑什么热闹？今日我就把卖身契还给你们，从哪里来回哪里去，别在这里缠着本公子。”
"So they're here for *you*." Stepping out of the carriage while Haoyu stayed huddled inside, she eyed the pretty row of maids and muttered back through the window, "Shameless skirt-chaser!"

四个丫鬟哭起来：“陈公子！求求你留下我们吧？我们已习惯在陈府当丫鬟，回乡下会不适应，呜呜……”
"Guilty as charged, Wife," Haoyu whimpered from inside.

“别哭啦！”
Unaware of the carriage nearby, Butler Wang hurried out with servants trying to shoo the girls off. "Go back to the side estates! Second Young Master isn't home—and wouldn't see you if he were! Being maids in a rich house is good enough; don't dream of climbing higher!"

四个丫鬟立刻停止哭声。
"Not budging till we see Young Master Chen!"

陈浩宇接着训斥：“只要你们四个乖乖的，别瞎闹事，留你们继续当丫鬟没什么不可以，但需给我记好了，本公子不纳妾，别再有这种想法，听到没？”
"He snatched us—can't just walk away!"

四个丫鬟齐声说：“听到了。”
One maid stepped out bawling, "You don't understand, Butler Wang! Second Young Master snatched us and... *sob*... took our innocence! Now he marries a fine wife and leaves us ruined—what happens to us?!"

“听到了还不滚！”
Half the row burst into wails, turning the south gate into a weeping chorus while Butler Wang stood helpless.

四个丫鬟迅速离开，不再参与闹事。
Half the warm feelings Qianli Xun had just built for Haoyu evaporated on the spot. Swallowing the urge to drag him out for them to beat senseless—she was Second Young Madam Chen now, no acting rash—she rapped on the carriage door. "Chen Haoyu! If you call yourself a man, get down here and handle this right in front of me!"

剩下的丫鬟听到陈浩宇不愿纳妾，伤心不已，可不敢哭出声来。
Refusing meant losing every shred of manhood before his wife. Climbing out, Haoyu tossed silver to the driver—"Beat it, show's over!"—sending the carriage rattling away.

陈浩宇走到跟前，一脸愧疚，拱手向剩下的丫鬟行礼：“几位，我陈浩宇当初不懂事，冒犯各位。可我已有娘子，又不打算纳妾，只能委屈大家。你们出个价，多少银子我都给，只要断了给我当妾室的想法即可。”
At the sound, the wailing crowd spun around and swarmed straight onto Haoyu, shoving the actual wife clean to the sidelines!

千里寻心中冷笑道：“哼！有钱就了不起？你以为女子的贞洁能用钱买回来？好色之徒！”
*Fine—let's see how you wipe your own mess!* Qianli Xun crossed her arms darkly.

几位丫鬟并不买账，都跪下哭道：“我等已失贞于陈公子，往后没有人愿意再要我们，要那么多银子有何用？还望陈公子把我们留在身边伺候，呜呜……”
"Young Master Chen, are you dumping us?! *Sob*..."

陈浩宇用几乎乞求的语气说：“你们还是要银子吧？银子多实惠。”
"You promised to make me a concubine—why break your word?!"

几位丫鬟坚持道：“我们不要银子，就要待在陈公子身边，呜呜……”
"I gave myself to you—if you abandon me I'll kill myself!"

这时过来一辆马车停在陈府外，陈浩东扶着族中的一位长辈从车上下来。
"Word is Second Young Madam's a fierce tigress keeping you locked down—is that why you vanished?!"

这位长辈与夏老太太年纪相仿，名唤陈陆，在陈家的产业中占有一定份额。陈家选择产业的继承人，他持有重要的一票，而且此人在其他族人中影响较大。
Pinned under the shrieking storm, Haoyu kept darting nervous glances at his wife's chilly face. Seeing she wasn't storming off—she wanted to see him handle it—he barked, "Shut it! Cry one more tear and I won't deal with any of you!"

陈浩东从钱塘帮办完事专程将陈陆接来，为的就是让他看到堂弟不堪的一面。
The wails shrank to sniffles. "Line up!" They snapped into a neat row.

果然不负陈浩东一番筹谋，陈陆被扶着来到陈浩宇面前：“浩宇啊！听说你刚好些，我还替你高兴，又惹下这等事，陈家是指望不上你。唉！家门不幸！以后要好好跟浩东学！”
Jabbing a finger down the line, he barked, "You, you, you, and you—step forward!" Four girls stepped out. "I never laid a finger on any of you four—what are you jumping on the bandwagon for?! I'm handing back your deeds of sale today—go home to your villages and stop pestering me!"

陈浩宇赔着笑脸：“陆爷批评得对，您快进去，别让这事儿污了您的眼。”
All four burst into fresh tears. "Please don't kick us out, Young Master! We're used to serving in the Chen estates—we can't go back to the dirt!"

陈陆叹口气不再说话。
"Stop crying! Behave yourselves and you can stay on as maids—only get it through your heads I'm *never* taking concubines! Got it?!"

陈浩东装作关心的样子：“要是处理不了，交给大哥，不就是银子的事儿。”
"Yes, Young Master!" They scrambled off at once.

陈浩宇说：“东哥，还真不是银子的事儿，你赶紧扶陆爷进去，我自己的事儿自己处理。”
Turning to the six remaining girls, Haoyu bowed with genuine shame. "I was a reckless fool back then and wronged you all. Yet I have a wife now and won't take concubines. Name your price—whatever silver you want, I'll pay, just give up being my concubines."

千里寻没见过陈陆，对方留着山羊胡，看上去年纪不小，陈浩宇还称呼他陆爷，应该是位有身份的长辈，作为陈家二少夫人，不能失了礼数，便上前拜见：“陆爷，晚辈这厢有礼。”
*Think silver buys back a girl's honor? Filthy flirt!* Qianli Xun scoffed silently.

陈陆将陈家二少夫人上下瞧一遍，没好气地说：“浩宇与你订亲我老大不乐意，可我做不了主。你既嫁给浩宇，应该尽做娘子的职责，他遇到此等事，你却站在一旁看热闹，成何体统？浩宇娶了你，更别想继承陈家产业！”
All six dropped to their knees weeping. "You already took our virtue—no decent man will marry us now! What good is silver? Just keep us beside you!"

陈浩东窃喜不已，扶着陈陆往府内走。
"Come on—silver's way more practical!" Haoyu practically begged.

千里寻最讨厌这种什么都不了解，胡乱批评人的长辈，见到必然要怼回去：“陆爷留步！”
"We don't want silver—we want to stay with you!"

陈陆停下来，转过身：“二少夫人有何指教？”
Right then a carriage halted nearby, and Chen Haodong stepped out assisting an elderly clansman with a goatee—Chen Lu, an elder of Old Madam Xia's generation who held a major share in the Chen enterprises and swung heavy clout over the heir vote. Haodong had fetched him straight from his errands specifically to catch Haoyu knee-deep in scandal.

千里寻走上前，继续行礼：“指教谈不上，陆爷刚才的话晚辈听着甚不顺耳，需辩解几句。”
"Haoyu!" Granduncle Lu tutted in disgust. "Just heard you were straightening up, and here's another disgrace! Hopeless—pure family misfortune! You ought to learn from Haodong!"

“你这晚辈，不想着处理自己夫君惹下的丑事，还想跟长辈辩解？实在可笑，我可没工夫听。”
Haoyu forced an apologetic grin. "You're right, Granduncle Lu. Go on inside before this eyesore spoils your day."

陈浩东扶着陆爷继续向府内走去。
"Need me to settle this with silver, little brother?" Haodong offered smoothly.

千里寻大胆挑衅道：“陆爷刚说自己做不了主，看来你在陈家族人中并不重要，又何必倚老卖老？作威作福？一见面毫无道理批评晚辈？”
"Silver won't fix this one, Brother Dong—help Granduncle inside, I'll handle my own mess."

陈浩东更是开心，弟妹实在给力，竟然敢得罪手中握有重要一票的陆爷，设计逼浩宇娶她此时算是起到良好的效果。还需故意装作好意帮忙说话的样子：“陆爷，弟妹不懂事，您老别跟她计较。”
Mindful of manners toward an elder, Qianli Xun stepped forward and curtsied. "Greetings, Granduncle Lu."

陈陆被成功气到：“她既不懂事，我更要替陈家好好教育她。”
Granduncle Lu eyed her up and down with disdain. "I was dead set against Haoyu marrying you, only nobody asked my leave. Now that you're his wife, standing around gawking while he's knee-deep in scandal—what kind of wife are you? With you married to him, Haoyu can forget ever inheriting the Chen estates!"

陈陆彻底转过身：“我早听说你厉害，果然脾气不小，我倒要听听你如何替自己辩解。”
Gleaming inside, Haodong started steering the old man indoors—until Qianli Xun's sharp voice halted them.

陈浩宇担心娘子吃亏，满脸堆笑地解释道：“陆爷，您错怪我娘子，她是要管来着，我不让，自己惹的事儿怎能让娘子替我擦屁股？否则别人会笑话我不是男人。”
"Hold on, Granduncle Lu."

“以为我不知？跟你爹一样天生怕老婆，一心维护自己的娘子。从现在起，你靠边站，不准说一句话，我只听她说。”陈陆指着那几个丫鬟，“老实跪着，不许哭出声来！”
"What do you want?" the elder snapped.

丫鬟们吓得更是忍住哭声。
"Just a word in my own defense—your remarks didn't sit right with me."

陈陆又指着王管家和几名下人：“你们几个好好站在这里，一个都不许进去替她找帮手，听到没？”
"Too lazy to clean up your husband's disgrace yet bold enough to talk back to elders? Absurd—I haven't got time for this!"

王管家很为难，不敢说一句话。
"Since Granduncle Lu admitted he can't call the shots anyway," Qianli Xun jabbed boldly, "looks like you don't carry much weight in the clan—so why strut around throwing your age at a junior you just met?"

陈陆这才面向二少夫人：“浩宇的娘子，该你说话了。”
Haodong nearly laughed out loud—his fiery sister-in-law insulting the swing-vote elder straight to his face! Putting on a righteous peacemaker act, he chimed, "Don't mind her, Granduncle—she's young and brash."

千里寻早已等不及，一肚子话给他准备着：“陆爷既不愿我嫁给陈浩宇，为何当初不出面阻止？您作为长辈应该知道父母之命媒妁之言，婚姻我也做不了主，嫁给陈浩宇不是我的错，现在却拿没有意义的话来批评我，实在毫无道理可言！”
Hooked straight into rage, Granduncle Lu whirled back. "Brash? Then I'll educate her on the Chen clan's behalf! Speak up—let's hear your 'defense'!"

陈浩宇心中难过，难道娘子还认为嫁给他是一种错误？他看看跪在地上的丫鬟，如此看来娘子嫁给他的确是错误的，乔文仙和仪王从来没做过此等事。
Haoyu jumped in grinning nervously. "You've got her wrong, Granduncle! She offered to handle this—I stopped her! What kind of man makes his wife wipe his own messes?"

陈陆辩解道：“我已说过，我做不了主，难道你白白长了一双耳朵？”
"Save it—henpecked just like your father! Step aside and shut your mouth! You six—kneel still and quit sniveling! Butler Wang, nobody goes inside for backup! Now talk, Haoyu's wife!"

“依晚辈看，不尽然，以陆爷的年纪和说话的口气在陈家族人中地位应该不低，否则堂兄也不可能请您来，又是搀又是扶的。”
Locked and loaded, Qianli Xun stepped forward. "If Granduncle Lu hated my marrying Haoyu, why didn't you block it beforehand? You know marriage goes by parents' orders—I didn't choose it, so blaming me for marrying him makes zero sense!"

千里寻怼人的话中多少有些奉承的意味，说得陈陆面露自豪。
Haoyu winced—did she still view marrying him as a mistake? Looking at the six kneeling girls, maybe it *was*.

“你这晚辈倒也有些眼色，即使我在族人中地位不低，可浩宇娶媳妇的事我也不好参与，才让你这么顺利嫁到陈家。浩宇本来就跟浩东没法比，你这一嫁更是令他与陈家的产业毫无瓜葛。”
"Already told you nobody asked me—are your ears decorative?!"

陈浩东刚慨叹过弟妹厉害，能从他又搀又扶看出陆爷的地位。陆爷的话又令他兴奋不已，弟妹最厉害的还是帮了他，呵呵……
"Hardly," she parried smoothly with a sliver of flattery. "Judging by your age and how carefully Cousin Haodong escorts you arm in arm, you clearly hold high rank in the clan."

陈浩宇忍不住又替娘子说话：“陆爷说的不对，我娘子无可挑剔，是我的问题，我不争气，还好陈家有东哥。”
Puffing up slightly, Granduncle Lu sniffed, "At least you have sharp eyes. High rank or not, I don't meddle in Eldest Brother's son's wedding—which is how you slipped in. Haoyu never held a candle to Haodong anyway, and marrying *you* sealed him clean out of the inheritance!"

“无可挑剔？哼！她的问题大了！陈家产业继承人的条件里有一条规定，继承人娶的娘子必须出自清白人家，贤良淑德，名声要好。而你这位娘子不仅和离过，去年满城人议论她难道你不知？”
"Granduncle's wrong!" Haoyu burst out. "My wife is flawless—I'm the useless one, thank goodness we've got Brother Dong!"

陈浩宇此时更加悔恨，不该散播娘子的谣言，最终害人害己。还好他对陈家产业没有兴趣，害己已不重要，重要的是害得娘子被人诟病。显然陆爷并不知道他是散播谣言之人，不如当众承认，也省得他怪罪娘子。
"Flawless?! Ha! Clan law states the heir's wife must come from an unblemished house, virtuous and spotless in reputation! Yours is divorced—and last year half the city was gossiping about her!"

“陆爷，去年的谣言是我让人胡乱散播的，我娘子根本不是那样的人。”
Remorse tore through Haoyu. Never mind losing an inheritance he didn't want—watching his wife smeared for *his* crime burned like fire! "Granduncle Lu, *I'm* the one who paid people to spread those rumors last year! My wife is completely innocent!"
