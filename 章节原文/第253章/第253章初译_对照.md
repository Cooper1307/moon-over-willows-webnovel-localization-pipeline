第253章 希望满满赴诗会
## Chapter 253: Heading to the Poetry Salon Full of Hope

一早陈浩宇在门外道完别离开后，千里寻用过早饭，简单装扮完，换上一身素淡的衣裙，先去婆婆房中告假。
既是仪王妃邀请参加诗会，必然不能阻拦，姚轻琰只有同意的份儿：“仪王妃主动邀请，怠慢不得，我也替你感到高兴。”
婆婆的话语中流露出长辈也一直担心她与梦容的关系，毕竟梦容在仪王抢亲那日受了伤，任何人都会认为从此一对闺中密友形同陌路。
千里寻也无时无刻不在担心与梦容的关系无法恢复到从前，梦容主动邀请她参加诗会，才暂时放下忧虑。
得到长辈允许，千里寻拜别后，赶往幽林别居。
马车里，苦荞一直观察小姐，她脸上挂着笑容。苦荞能够感受到小姐并不是因为可能会遇见仪王而高兴。
“小姐，郡主人真好，举办诗会，还主动邀请您参加，给两人一个重修旧好的机会，明摆着不再计较以前的事，我真替小姐和郡主感到高兴。”
此刻千里寻觉得委屈自己嫁给陈浩宇是最正确的选择，在心里默默告诫自己，今后不要再去念着申简辰，也不要再与他相见。
“还称呼郡主？应该称仪王妃。”
“对，应该是仪王妃，我可得记牢，不能给小姐丢脸。”
到达后，马车刚在幽林别居门口停下，李管家迎上来：“少夫人来了，仪王妃正在里面候着，其他人也都到齐。”
不等苦荞先下车，千里寻迅速跳下马车：“我出发得不晚，大家怎么都到了？倒显得我不积极。梦容，不，仪王妃会不会怪我？”
李管家并不知情，安慰道：“仪王妃性情最好，少夫人又不是不知，她怎么会怪您？”
是啊，梦容向来性情好，那么大的事她都不计较，主动邀请她参加诗会，又怎么会计较这等小事？千里寻轻松许多。
苦荞已从马车上下来，跟着小姐往里走，李管家也跟在后面。
苦荞趁机悄悄问：“李管家，白风可在？”
“白侍卫不在，他跟着仪王去了司理院。”
Right after Chen Haoyu said his morning farewell outside her door and left for work, Qianli Xun ate breakfast, dressed simply in a muted, understated gown, and went to ask her mother-in-law's leave.

“司理院？是什么地方？他为何要去那里？”
Since Princess Consort Yi herself had sent the invitation, Yao Qingyan could hardly say no. "When Princess Consort Yi invites you in person, you must not keep her waiting. I'm truly glad for you."

“仪王要当司理参军，估计是喜欢审理案子，王爷去哪里白侍卫都得跟着。”
Her mother-in-law's relief showed how worried the elders had been about the rift with Mengrong—after Prince Yi's wedding crash left Mengrong wounded, anyone would assume the two bosom friends had become strangers for good. Qianli Xun had fretted over the same thing every waking day, and Mengrong's personal invitation had lifted that stone right off her chest.

苦荞瘪起嘴：“哼，这么大的事他都不告诉我，等下次见到他，我一定要好好批评。”
With Yao Qingyan's blessing, she took her leave and rode to Youlin Retreat.

两人的对话千里寻一字不落听进耳朵里，申简辰不在，她失落之余也有轻松，可他跑去做司理参军，又是几个意思？
Inside the carriage, Kuqiao studied her mistress's smiling face and sensed at once that this glow had nothing to do with hoping to glimpse Prince Yi. "Miss, Princess Jiahe really has a heart of gold—hosting a poetry salon and inviting you herself to mend fences! Clearly she's put the past behind her. I'm so happy for the two of you."

唉！想那么多干什么？他是仪王想做什么便做什么，跟自己一点儿关系都没有。
In that moment, Qianli Xun felt that swallowing her pride to marry Chen Haoyu had been the wisest choice she could have made. Silently, she vowed to stop thinking about Shen Jianchen and stop seeing him altogether. "Still calling her Princess Jiahe? It's Princess Consort Yi now."

幽林别居对千里寻来讲并不陌生，她沿着小路准备往梦容的小院拐去，被李管家拦住。
"Right—Princess Consort Yi! Better drill that into my head so I don't embarrass you, Miss."

“少夫人走错了，两位贵人回来后住一处，诗会在仪王住的院子里举行。”
The instant their carriage halted outside Youlin Retreat, Butler Li stepped out to greet them. "Second Young Madam has arrived. Princess Consort Yi is waiting inside—all the other guests are already here."

千里寻收住脚步：“瞧我这记性，他们都已成亲，仪王妃怎么可能还住在以前的小院儿？”
Without waiting for Kuqiao to step down first, Qianli Xun hopped straight out of the carriage. "I didn't leave late—how did everyone else beat me here? Makes me look rude! Will Mengrong—I mean, Princess Consort Yi—be upset?"

她改变方向，一边走着，一边想起申简辰说过守身如玉的话来。
In the dark about any tension, Butler Li smiled reassuringly. "You know as well as anyone what a sweet temper the Princess Consort has. Why would she be upset with you?"

心想：“你若真的为我守身如玉，我岂不是更加对不起梦容？我心里虽然还念着你，却也希望你跟梦容能相守一生。你们能住在一起，看来你对她不错，难怪梦容会原谅我，主动邀请我参加诗会。申简辰，我们一起努力忘记彼此吧。”
True enough. Mengrong had always been gentle—if she could forgive something as huge as that wedding mess and invite her over, why would she sweat a trifle like arriving last? Qianli Xun's shoulders loosened.

心中想着事，不知不觉已来到正院门外。
Kuqiao climbed down and trailed behind her mistress, with Butler Li following along. Seeing her chance, Kuqiao leaned over to murmur, "Butler Li, is Bai Feng here?"

李管家说：“我就送到这里，少夫人自行进去，院中自有下人招呼。”
"Guard Bai isn't here—he went with Prince Yi to the Judicial Office."

待李管家去后，苦荞推开院门，一阵“嘎吱”声，传达出千里寻对友情的期待。
"Judicial Office? What's that? Why'd he go there?"

她小心走进去，院子里竟然异常安静，没有一丝热闹的气息，她甚至怀疑梦容为了见她找了诗会的借口而已。早知如此，她应主动前来向她忏悔。
"Prince Yi took on the post of Judicial Investigator—must have a taste for solving cases. Wherever His Highness goes, Guard Bai has to follow."

刚走没几步，喜兰从屋里出来：“幽栖居士可来了，众人正在里面等着，都盼您快些到来，尤其是仪王妃。”
Kuqiao stuck out her lower lip. "Humph! Something that big and he didn't even tell me! Wait till next time I see him—I'm giving him an earful."

原来还有其他人，千里寻有些许失望。失望过后，仔细品喜兰说话的语气和表情，很正常，没有什么异样，足以说明梦容对她的心思还和以前一样。
Every word reached Qianli Xun's ears. Knowing Shen Jianchen wasn't here brought a twinge of emptiness mixed with genuine relief—though what on earth was he doing playing Judicial Investigator? Oh, forget it! He was Prince Yi; he could do whatever he pleased, and none of it was her business anymore.

千里寻快走几步，踏进屋内，只觉眼前一片红色。不是诗会吗？怎么布置得跟新婚似的？仔细一想，梦容与申简辰不就是新婚，如此布置再正常不过。
Familiar with Youlin Retreat's paths, she started turning down the lane toward Mengrong's old side courtyard, only for Butler Li to stop her. "Wrong way, Second Young Madam. Since returning, Their Highnesses live together—the salon is in Prince Yi's main courtyard."

一片红光过后，清楚地看到梦容身着一身王妃的服饰，端坐在正位，面色平静中带着威严，见她走进，只是略微抬眼瞧来。
Qianli Xun halted with a sheepish smile. "Look at my memory. They're married now—of course the Princess Consort wouldn't still live in her old side courtyard."

那表情令千里寻产生从未有过的距离感，准备了一肚子的话说不出来，只能站在原地沉默不语。
Changing course, she remembered Shen Jianchen's vow to stay chaste for her. *If you really did keep yourself untouched for me, wouldn't I owe Mengrong an even heavier debt? Even if a piece of my heart still aches for you, I truly want you and Mengrong to grow old together. Moving into the same courtyard means you're treating her right—no wonder she forgave me and invited me today. Shen Jianchen, let's both try our hardest to forget each other.*

梦容则微启双唇：“幽栖居士不愧是江南女词人，架子就是大，这么多人早早到来，你却是最后一个，该不会根本没把我放在眼里？”
Lost in thought, she reached the gate of the main courtyard before she knew it.

从昨日接到请帖到刚才进入幽林别居，所有美好的畅想被梦容冷冷的表情和语气击得粉碎。
"I'll leave you here, Second Young Madam," Butler Li said. "Go right on in—servants inside will see to you."

千里寻缓了缓心绪，瞧瞧在座的人。左边坐着苗知瑜和她的一位闺中密友，还有一个位置空出，应该是给她留的。右边坐着白梅若与两位没见过的女子，用脚趾头都能猜到应该也是苗知瑜的闺中密友。
Once he withdrew, Kuqiao pushed the wooden gate open. Its low creak carried all of Qianli Xun's quiet hope for restored friendship. Stepping inside, she blinked at the eerie hush hanging over the courtyard—not a shred of party chatter anywhere. For half a heartbeat, she wondered if Mengrong had only used a poetry salon as an excuse to see her alone. Had she known, she would have come knocking first to beg forgiveness.

千里寻敏锐地感觉出今日诗会不是那么简单，既然来了，该面对的还需面的。仪王妃已发话，她再不接显得不守规矩。
A few paces in, Xilan emerged from the hall. "At last Youqi Jushi has arrived! Everyone's waiting inside, eager to see you—especially Princess Consort Yi."

千里寻不由自主躬身行礼：“请仪王妃海涵，紧赶慢赶还是最后一个，好在没有迟到。”
So there *were* other guests. A flicker of disappointment passed over her, though studying Xilan's calm tone and smooth expression reassured her—nothing looked amiss, so Mengrong's feelings must be just as warm as before.

“既然你没迟到，我也不与你计较。在座诸位来时都为我送上祝福，幽栖居士却一句祝福的话都没有，难道不希望我与仪王百年好合？”
Quickening her step across the threshold, Qianli Xun met a blaze of wedding red. Wait—wasn't this a poetry salon? Why was the hall decked out like a bridal chamber? Well, Mengrong and Shen Jianchen *were* newlyweds, so red hangings made sense enough.

现实总是与想象中有差距，千里寻失落的同时，无法埋怨梦容，她受过的剑伤虽好，内心的伤口应该没有那么容易愈合，怪只怪命运捉弄。
As her eyes adjusted to the scarlet glow, she saw Qiao Mengrong seated in the place of honor, arrayed in full Princess Consort regalia. Her face held a cool, regal stillness; as Qianli Xun entered, she lifted her eyes by no more than a fraction.

“回仪王妃，百年好合怎么能够？我时刻在心中祝福您与仪王相濡以沫，白头偕老，生生世世不离不弃。”
That single look opened a chasm between them Qianli Xun had never felt before. Every warm word she had rehearsed dried up in her throat, leaving her rooted in place.

“哼！心中祝福，说得轻巧，谁知你心中作何想法？希望幽栖居士不要口是心非，更希望你时刻记着你已嫁人，不要再惦记不该惦记的人。”
Qiao Mengrong parted her lips. "Youqi Jushi truly lives up to her name as the Poetess of Jiangnan—what grand airs! Everyone else arrived bright and early, yet you're dead last. Or did you simply not think me worth respecting?"

乔梦容的意思再明显不过，千里寻又岂能不知？她心里即使想过申简辰也不能承认。并不是虚伪，是怕再次伤害梦容。
Every bright hope Qianli Xun had nursed from yesterday's card straight up to this threshold shattered under that glacial tone.

“我心中一直放着一位闺中密友，不管她如何看待我，我绝不会做对不起她的事，对她我永远不会口是心非，心中只惦记着她，希望她越来越好。只要她好……”
Steadying her breathing, she swept her gaze around the room. To the left sat Miao Zhiyu and one of her close friends, with a single empty seat beside them—clearly meant for her. To the right sat Bai Meiruo alongside two unfamiliar young women who, even a fool could tell with her eyes shut, had to be more of Miao Zhiyu's circle.

“不必多言！”乔梦容认为朱淑贞不过是巧舌雌黄，若真的心里有她，为何要偷偷勾引哥哥？她稳住情绪，“既然来了，落座吧，我的诗会怎么能少了江南女词人？”
Today's poetry salon was no friendly tea party, that much was crystal clear. Still, she was already here; no sense flinching from what had to be faced. Princess Consort Yi had spoken, and staying mute would only look defiant.

千里寻行完礼，坐在左边的空位上。那位置是下位中的下位，很显然在梦容心中她已不重要。千里寻一阵落寞，心中自问：“她既不看重我？为何邀请我前来参加诗会？”
Qianli Xun bowed low. "Please forgive me, Princess Consort Yi. I hurried as fast as I could and still ended up last—thankfully, not past the appointed hour."

她抬眼再次瞧一遍在场的人，她们个个脸上露出不怀好意的笑容，尤其是白梅若。由此看来诗会也许会成为批判她的大会，不如找理由溜掉？
"Since you aren't technically late, I won't hold it against you," Mengrong said. "Still, every lady here offered me her wedding blessings upon arriving, yet Youqi Jushi hasn't uttered a single word of congratulation. Don't you wish Prince Yi and me a hundred years of harmony?"

正想着，门从外面被下人关上。
Reality hit colder than she had feared, yet Qianli Xun couldn't bring herself to resent Mengrong. The sword wound on her shoulder might have healed, but the scar on her heart clearly hadn't. Blame fate's cruel jokes. "How could a mere hundred years be enough, Your Highness? In my heart I pray every day that you and Prince Yi share every joy and hardship, grow white-haired together, and never part through life after life."

梦容连溜掉的机会都不给她，既如此还是做好被众人批判的准备。只要梦容心中的怨气能消，被人骂两句也没什么，去年被那么多人骂，不都挺过来，今日就这几人，又能有多大力度。
"Humph! Easy enough to claim you pray 'in your heart'—who knows what you're really thinking in there? I only hope Youqi Jushi isn't speaking sweet words while harboring foul thoughts, and that you keep firmly in mind that you are a married woman now. Stop coveting what doesn't belong to you."

苦荞站在小姐身后很是担忧，仪王妃说话的语气和表情跟以前大不相同，不仅有距离感，还充满情绪。恐怕小姐今日在劫难逃，怎么办？
Every barb struck straight at the mark, and Qianli Xun caught every syllable. Even if Shen Jianchen still crossed her thoughts, she could never admit it—not out of hypocrisy, but because she refused to knife Mengrong again. "There is one dear friend I have always carried in my heart. No matter how she views me, I would never betray her, never lie to her, and only ever wish her happiness. So long as she is happy—"

都怪那个杀千刀的仪王，放着那么好的妹妹不好好爱，偏偏招惹自家小姐，才搞成今日局面。他倒好，自己远远躲开当什么司里参军，害得白风也跟着去，错过一次见他的机会。
"Save your breath!" Qiao Mengrong cut in. Silver-tongued hypocrisy—if she truly cared about her, why had she seduced Brother behind her back? Reining in her temper, she gestured to the empty chair. "Since you're here, sit. How could my poetry salon go on without the Poetess of Jiangnan?"

苦荞担忧小姐，必然观察她的表情，发现小姐失落之后倒安静坦然，她心里也平静许多。
After bowing again, Qianli Xun took the lone seat on the left—the lowest spot at the lowest end of the room, signaling plain as day how little she meant to Mengrong now. A hollow chill crept into her chest. *If she despises me this much, why invite me at all?* Looking around the circle again, she met smug, venomous smiles on every face—Bai Meiruo's sharpest of all. This wasn't a salon; it was a public lynching. Should she invent an excuse and slip away?

从后门进来五位幽林别居的丫鬟，手中端着笔墨纸砚，分别放在其他五人的桌上。
Right on cue, servants pulled the main doors shut from outside.

千里寻心中纳闷，第一次见诗会如此操作，既如此操作为何她面前的桌上不给摆同样的物件？
Mengrong wasn't even giving her an exit. Fine—might as well brace herself for the verbal firing squad. If letting them tear into her drained some of Mengrong's bitterness, what were a few insults? She had survived an entire city slandering her last year; how much bite could half a dozen noblewomen have?

乔梦容向苗知瑜使眼色。
Standing behind her mistress, Kuqiao's stomach knotted. Princess Consort Yi's icy tone and hostile stare were nothing like the sweet girl she used to be. Miss was walking straight into an ambush! Curse that rotten Prince Yi—ignoring such a devoted little sister to chase after Miss and landing everyone in this mess, while he hid away playing Judicial Investigator and dragged Bai Feng off with him so she didn't even get to see her sweetheart! Watching her mistress's profile, though, Kuqiao saw the initial hurt settle into quiet composure, and her own nerves steadied a notch.

苗知瑜迅速领会，第一个开口说话：“幽栖居士不必疑惑，今日主要品鉴你的诗词，大家必然佳句频出，不记下来实在浪费。此等劳烦事，不必幽栖居士亲自动笔，我等代劳即可。”
Five maids filed through the rear door carrying the Four Treasures of the Study—brushes, inksticks, paper, and inkstones—and set them on the tables before the other five guests, skipping Qianli Xun's table altogether.

千里寻再笨也能听出，她们是要记录今日品诗的内容。已猜出她们品诗的话语定是以批判为主，将批判的内容记下来到底意欲何为？难道也要像陈浩宇传播她谣言一样宣扬出去？
Qianli Xun frowned. Strange setup for a poetry salon—and if they were writing, why give everyone else brushes and paper except her?

面前的人要是别人，搁千里寻的性格，必然大骂一顿冲出去，凭她的拳头谁能拦得住？可谁让请她的人是梦容，谁让她的的确确惦记过她的哥哥，一个人终要为自己的错误付出代价。
Qiao Mengrong shot Miao Zhiyu a glance.

不如听听她们到底要说些什么，再做决断。
Miao Zhiyu picked up the cue at once. "No need to look puzzled, Youqi Jushi. Today we are here specifically to critique your poetry. Brilliant commentary is bound to pour forth from everyone present, and failing to record it would be a pity. Naturally we wouldn't trouble you to take notes yourself—we'll handle the writing."

苗知瑜对身后的幼凡说：“把幽栖居士写的新词拿出来。”
Even a dimwit could read between those lines: they were going to transcribe every word of their hatchet job. Why write it all down? Were they planning to broadcast it all over town, just like Chen Haoyu had spread rumors about her? If anyone else had pulled this stunt, Qianli Xun's temper would have snapped on the spot—she'd have cursed them sideways and punched her way straight out the door. Who here could stop her fists? Yet Mengrong was the host, and she *had* coveted Mengrong's brother. Sooner or later, a person had to pay for her own mistakes. Better sit tight, hear what they had to say, and decide her move after.

幼凡手中早已拿着一个卷轴，只见她点点头，转向苦荞：“既是幽栖居士的词，你帮我一起展开。”
Miao Zhiyu nodded to the maid behind her. "Youfan, bring out Youqi Jushi's new ci."

众目睽睽下，苦荞无法拒绝，点头答应，与幼凡走到中间，将卷轴展开。
Youfan already held a rolled scroll. Stepping forward, she turned to Kuqiao. "Since this is Youqi Jushi's lyric, help me unroll it."

苦荞认得这首词，正是小姐新写的《减字木兰花·春怨》。南双跑来替乔公子讨要诗词，她便将这首给了。
With every eye locked on them, Kuqiao couldn't refuse. She stepped to the center of the hall beside Youfan and stretched the scroll wide. She recognized it at once—her mistress's new *"Jianzi Mulan Hua: Spring Resentment"*, the very piece she had handed Nan Shuang when Young Master Qiao asked for a fresh lyric.

苗知瑜说：“这首词是幽栖居士新写的，文渊派南双从幽栖居士那里讨要来不久，还热乎着。我无意中从文渊的书桌上发现，誊抄来，正好拿到今日诗会，供大家品鉴。”
"This is Youqi Jushi's latest work," Miao Zhiyu announced. "Wenyuan sent Nan Shuang to beg it from her just the other day—the ink's barely dry! I happened to spot it on Wenyuan's desk, copied it out, and brought it along today for everyone to appreciate."

乔梦容说：“既是苗夫人拿来，就由苗夫人第一个品鉴，帮大家开个场，也好让诸位知道今日该如何品鉴幽栖居士的佳作。”
"Since Madam Miao brought it," Qiao Mengrong said, "you shall open the critique—show everyone how to properly appraise Youqi Jushi's masterpiece."

“谨遵仪王妃吩咐。”苗知瑜起身，走到卷轴跟前，念了两句，“独行独坐，独唱独酬还独卧。”
"As Your Highness commands." Miao Zhiyu rose, stepped up to the scroll, and read the opening lines aloud: *"I walk alone, sit alone, sing alone, drink alone, and sleep alone."* [TN: Translated by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping]

念完，转向朱淑贞：“幽栖居士，恕我冒昧，这两句只有十一个字，一个‘独’字竟然出现五次，难道幽栖居士才尽词穷？”
Turning toward Zhu Shuzhen, she arched a brow. "Forgive my bluntness, Youqi Jushi, but those opening lines contain eleven characters, and the single character 'alone' (独) shows up five times! Has the great Youqi Jushi finally run dry of talent and words?"

千里寻正想解释，重复不仅能将内心的孤独感表达得淋漓尽致，而且每个“独”字程度不同，表达的意思也是同中有异。嘴巴刚刚张开，话还未出口，被苗知瑜的话给打了回去。
Qianli Xun opened her mouth to explain how the repetition drove home an inescapable loneliness, with each "alone" carrying a distinct shade and deepening intensity—yet Miao Zhiyu cut straight across her before a syllable escaped.

“我看幽栖居士一定是才尽词穷，像幽栖居士这等有名的女词人，随便写两句，哪怕是重复，别人都会认为是佳句，而我等即使佳句频出，别人也不会拿我们写的诗词当回事。”
"Oh, she must have run dry! Still, for a celebrity poetess like Youqi Jushi, people will hail any sloppy repetition as genius, whereas no matter how many sparkling lines the rest of us write, nobody spares us a glance."

苗知瑜坐回桌前，继续说：“我诗兴大发，仿幽栖居士的句子也写两句，大家听听，给品评品评：喜行喜坐，喜唱喜酬还喜卧。大家觉得如何？”
Returning to her chair, Miao Zhiyu smirked. "In fact, inspiration just struck me! I composed a parody in her exact style—tell me what you think, ladies: *I walk in joy, sit in joy, sing in joy, drink in joy, and sleep in joy.* Well? How does it sound?"

苦荞心中十分不满：“好个屁！跟我家小姐写的句子比差远了！原来她们是想借着诗会批判我家小姐，早知如此，就不该来。”
*Sounds like hot garbage!* Kuqiao raged in silence. *Doesn't hold a candle to my mistress's lines! So they really did drag Miss here just to tear her apart—we never should've come!*

其他几人边笑边提笔在纸上记录着。
Giggling, the other women dipped their brushes and began scribbling on their sheets.

千里寻发现梦容的脸上露出满意的笑容，本来可借此空档说两句，梦容的笑容搞得她更加失落，失落得一句话也不想说。
Qianli Xun glanced up and caught a gleam of pure gratification on Mengrong's face. She could have jumped in right then to shut Miao Zhiyu down, yet seeing that satisfied smile on Mengrong's lips drained every word out of her.

白梅若放下笔：“依我看还是苗夫人的句子更好，起码让人觉得喜庆，人生总须往好处想，不能老是悲悲切切的，好像别人总是欠着她似的。幽栖居士，你说是也不是？”
Bai Meiruo set down her brush. "If you ask me, Madam Miao's version is far superior. At least it sounds cheerful! People ought to look on the bright side of life instead of moping and weeping all day as if the whole world owes them a debt. Wouldn't you agree, Youqi Jushi?"
