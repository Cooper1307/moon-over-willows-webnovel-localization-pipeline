## Chapter 117: Xiaguo's Confession Rejected

Xiaguo kept her eyes locked on Young Master Qiao. Hearing her mistress agree to stay overnight had brought a spark of smiles back to his worn face as he drew Prince Yi beneath the willow tree to talk.

Qiao Wenxian had pulled Prince Yi aside for one reason only: to beg him for a plan.

"Neither Shuzhen nor I will give up, yet I have no idea what to do next. Can Prince Yi think of a way out for me?"

Facing the desperation on Wenyuan's haggard face, Shen Jianchen could not bear to crush him—yet remembering Qianli Xun's vow to hold on stabbed his own chest. "Is Youqi Jushi really refusing to give up? No matter how hard it gets?"

"She told me herself, and it filled me with hope! I have to do something worthy of her devotion—I just don't know what!" Gloom clouded Qiao Wenxian's brow.

Shen Jianchen tested gently, "Have you ever thought you and Youqi Jushi might simply not be fated to end up together?"

Before he even finished, Qiao Wenxian cut him off: "Impossible! We will be together—this is just a rough patch."

"In that case, I've got an idea." Qianli Xun loved Wenyuan and meant to stand by him; grinding his teeth, Shen Jianchen forced himself to help them.

Qiao Wenxian pressed: "What idea? Tell me!"

Wenyuan's pitiful look wrung the plan straight out of Shen Jianchen: "Take Youqi Jushi and leave Lin'an to build a life somewhere else. Zhu Manor already cast her out, so nobody will stop her. Once the rice is cooked and you two have a child, a few years down the road Grandfather will have no choice but to accept it—you're the Qiao family's only son and heir, so he'll have to bring you home. Happy ending, right?"

Qiao Wenxian shook his head at once: "No! How could I do that? Folk are already slandering Shuzhen—if I elope with her, her reputation will be ruined forever! Besides, a marriage without parents' orders and matchmakers' words earns no blessings. I won't let Shuzhen suffer with me. Isn't there another way?"

Hearing Wenyuan reject elopement sent a secret surge of relief through Shen Jianchen. He had half dreaded Wenyuan taking his advice and whisking Qianli Xun away—forcing him to chase after them until he couldn't even hide his feelings anymore. Truth be told, if not for hurting Qiao Mengrong and Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen would never have bothered hiding how he felt about Qianli Xun at all.

Shen Jianchen shook his head. "No better way."

"No, there has to be a better way—I need to think harder."

Crushed under heavy thoughts, Qiao Wenxian dragged his feet over to the boulder beneath the Divine Willow, sank down against the trunk, and stared up at the clear autumn sky—crisp and bright overhead, yet bleak inside his chest.

Gazing across the water, Shen Jianchen saw Qianli Xun and Qiao Mengrong reach the far bank and disappear into the woods toward Youlin Retreat.

"Let's head back, Wenyuan. Youqi Jushi and Mengrong already went into the woods."

Only then did Qiao Wenxian heave himself up and trudge off toward the far bank without waiting for Prince Yi.

Watching his heavy steps, Shen Jianchen thought, "Wenyuan's great in every way except he overthinks everything. If it were me, I'd drop it all and run off to the ends of the earth with the woman I love."

Seeing Young Master Qiao depart, Xiaguo trailed behind him and Prince Yi, with the rest following along.

At Youlin Retreat's gate, Shen Jianchen had Bai Feng drive Kuqiao back to Wangyue Pavilion to inform Matron Wei that Princess Jiahe and Youqi Jushi had hit it off so well the princess had invited her to stay the night and return in the morning.

Any excuse to spend time with Kuqiao delighted Bai Feng. Whipping up the carriage, he laughed and joked with Kuqiao all the way to Wangyue Pavilion.

After reporting her mistress's overnight stay to Matron Wei, Kuqiao rushed to add lest the matron keep her behind: "It's Miss's first time sleeping away from home—I'm worried and need to hurry back to wait on her."

Matron Wei hated the idea of her mistress staying out overnight, yet with Princess Jiahe hosting, she couldn't object. "Folks are wagging tongues everywhere lately; if word leaks that you stayed out overnight, they'll spin even worse filth! Be careful coming back tomorrow so nobody spots you."

Promising she would, Kuqiao rode back to Youlin Retreat with Bai Feng. After stealing a little more playtime with him, she hurried off to Princess Jiahe's courtyard to tend her mistress.

Tonight her mistress was sharing a bed with the princess—at Princess Jiahe's enthusiastic insistence, since she had never shared a bed with a close friend before and wanted to try it.

By the time Kuqiao arrived, Xiaguo and Xilan had already removed their mistresses' makeup and outer robes, leaving nothing left for Kuqiao to do.

Xilan settled Kuqiao and Xiaguo into the maids' quarters. With an easy conscience, Kuqiao drifted off in minutes.

Xiaguo, however, tossed wide awake—not worrying over her mistress, but over Young Master Qiao. Never had she slept so close to him, and she longed to draw closer still.

Slipping out of bed, she dressed in the dark, tidied her hair, and tiptoed out of the maids' quarters.

Out in the yard, she heard her mistress and the princess chatting indoors. Uninterested, Xiaguo crept out through the courtyard gate.

On entering Youlin Retreat earlier, she had memorized the layout and the exact route from Princess Jiahe's yard to Young Master Qiao's quarters.

Finding his courtyard took no effort. Outside the gate, she spared a quick glance at the wooden viewing platform, then peered over the low wall—warm lamplight still glowed inside.

Stepping up to the gate, she found it unlatched and nudged it open with a soft creak.

Nan Shuang's voice rang out: "Who's there?"

Xiaguo froze, answering in a timid voice: "It's me."

Stepping outside, Nan Shuang recognized Xiaguo by moonlight and lit up, thinking she had come for him: "What brings you here?"

Xiaguo replied, "I have something to tell Young Master Qiao."

Nan Shuang's excitement fizzled on the spot.

From indoors came Qiao Wenxian's voice: "Who's outside, Nan Shuang?"

In a flat, disappointed tone, Nan Shuang called back, "Young Master, it's Youqi Jushi's maid, Xiaguo."

Thinking Shuzhen had sent word, Qiao Wenxian answered, "Let her in."

"Hear that? Young Master says go in."

Nan Shuang watched Xiaguo walk straight past him through the door and trailed right in behind her.

Unable to sleep, Qiao Wenxian had been poring over books at his desk in hopes of finding a way out, turning up nothing. Xiaguo coming by meant maybe Shuzhen had hit on an idea!

"What message did your mistress send?"

Her mistress hadn't sent any message—realizing Young Master Qiao didn't even see her stabbed Xiaguo's heart.

"Why do you look upset?" Dread pricked Qiao Wenxian—could Shuzhen be thinking of giving up on them, despite their vows earlier today?

Gathering her courage, Xiaguo said, "What I have to tell Young Master Qiao can't have anyone else listening."

Qiao Wenxian told Nan Shuang, "Step outside for a minute and shut the door."

Scowling, Nan Shuang tramped outside and pulled the door shut, muttering, "Big deal! So you're a little pretty—what's pretty get you? Still a maid's fate! Think a pretty face is going to marry you to a gentleman like my master?!"

Inside, Qiao Wenxian pressed: "What word did your mistress send?"

Xiaguo dropped to her knees. "Miss didn't send any message. I came because I wanted to see you, Young Master. Watching you grieve over the ruined proposal broke my heart, so I came to check on you..."

Disappointment crashed over Qiao Wenxian, mixed with annoyance: "If that's all, go back."

"Hear me out, Young Master—then I'll leave."

"No need. Leave."

Refusing to waste her one shot to confess, Xiaguo blurted out, "I want Young Master to know that besides Miss, I hold you in my heart too! I don't ask you to love me the way you love Miss—just let me stay by your side and wait on you once you two are together, and I'll be content! And if things with Miss don't work out, please take me in—I'll wait on Young Master for the rest of my life..."

"Shut up! Get out! Get out!" Rage and impatience flared in Qiao Wenxian as he shouted, "Nan Shuang!"

Hearing his master's shout, Nan Shuang pushed the door open to find Xiaguo kneeling on the floor and his master furious, baffled at what had happened.

"Nan Shuang, escort Xiaguo out!"

Rejected, Xiaguo felt crushed yet unrepentant—at least she had spoken her heart. From tonight on, every time Young Master Qiao laid eyes on her, he would remember what she had confessed.

Rising to leave, she froze when Qiao Wenxian called out, "Hold on!"

Hope flared as she spun back—had Young Master Qiao changed his mind?!

Instead, his voice fell cold as stone: "I'll pretend tonight never happened. You forget it too."

Without another word, Xiaguo turned and walked out.
