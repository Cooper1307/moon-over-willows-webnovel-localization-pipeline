# 第86章 竹兰居内救夏果

---

韦嬷嬷一把拉住姑娘："犯不着为此生气，老爷本就将夏果许给练兵官，她既被绑走，也符合常理。"

Nanny Wei grabbed the young lady: "No need to get angry over this. The master already promised Xiaguo to the training officer. Since she was tied up and taken away, it's reasonable."

---

接着又批评小厮："我当什么事，何必慌慌张张来报告姑娘？还不赶紧退下！"

Then she scolded the servant boy: "I thought it was something important. Why rush to report to the young lady in such a panic? Hurry up and leave!"

---

小厮本以为做了件好事，没承想被嬷嬷批评，赶紧退去。

The servant boy thought he had done a good deed, but didn't expect to be scolded by the nanny. He hurried away.

---

向夫人也说道："嬷嬷说得对，不要管了，那是别人家的事，好不容易离开林家，又何必再上林家的门？如若你身边缺丫鬟，娘将银杏给你。"

Madam Xiang also said: "The nanny is right. Don't get involved. That's another family's business. You barely left the Lin family. Why go back to their door? If you need a maid, I'll give you Yinxing."

---

"我身边不缺丫鬟，苦荞一人足矣。当时从陈浩宇手中救夏果，就是不想她被人欺负，现在却被林觉欺负，我岂能不管？"千里寻想起林觉要夏果主要因夏果的身段与她有几分相似，更觉厌恶，"不行！我得去看看，否则心里会不安。"

"I don't lack maids. Kuqiao is enough. When I saved Xiaguo from Chen Haoyu, it was because I didn't want her to be bullied. Now she's being bullied by Lin Jue. How can I not get involved?" Qianlixun remembered that Lin Jue wanted Xiaguo mainly because her figure was somewhat similar to hers, and felt even more disgusted. "No! I have to go see, or I won't be at ease."

---

韦嬷嬷见阻拦不住，只好说："既然姑娘想去，我陪你走一趟。"

Seeing she couldn't stop her, Nanny Wei had to say: "Since the young lady wants to go, I'll accompany you."

---

向夫人无奈地说："罢了罢了，要去就去吧，你若把夏果带回来，还是赶紧把她打发走，放在身边净给你招惹麻烦。"

Madam Xiang said helplessly: "Fine, fine. If you want to go, go. But if you bring Xiaguo back, hurry up and send her away. Keeping her around will only cause you trouble."

---

千里寻在韦嬷嬷和苦荞的陪伴下，来到林府。

Accompanied by Nanny Wei and Kuqiao, Qianlixun arrived at the Lin residence.

---

下了马车，一进大门，不顾下人阻拦，轻车熟路往西院匆忙走去，半路上碰见春金巧迎面走来。

Getting off the carriage, she entered the main gate. Ignoring the servants' attempts to stop her, she hurried toward the west courtyard as if she knew the way well. Halfway there, she ran into Chun Jinqiao coming toward her.

---

"吆！朱家姑娘来了，怎么？舍不得林府？"

"Oh! The Zhu family's young lady is here. What? Can't bear to leave the Lin residence?"

---

韦嬷嬷不爽道："哪有你问话的份儿？"

Nanny Wei said displeasedly: "What business is it of yours to ask?"

---

春金巧根本不把韦嬷嬷放在眼里："我与你家姑娘说话，你搭什么茬？"

Chun Jinqiao didn't even bother to look at Nanny Wei: "I'm talking to your young lady. What are you butting in for?"

---

千里寻说："你整日往林府跑，太阳都老高，才离开，也没见林觉娶你，甚至连个妾室都没混上，你不觉得很可悲吗？"

Qianlixun said: "You run to the Lin residence every day. The sun is already high when you leave. Lin Jue hasn't married you, or even made you a concubine. Don't you think that's pathetic?"

---

被戳到痛处，春金巧装作不在意的样子："我不在乎这些，只要能跟练兵官在一起便好，他娶谁跟我都没关系。听说，他刚抓了一个丫鬟回来，准备纳她为妾，就关在你以前住的竹兰居。"

Hit where it hurt, Chun Jinqiao pretended not to care: "I don't care about that. As long as I can be with the training officer, it doesn't matter who he marries. I heard he just caught a maid and brought her back. He's planning to make her a concubine. She's locked up in the Zhulan Residence where you used to live."

---

春金巧说完，瞪了韦嬷嬷一眼，扭着腰肢离开。

After finishing, Chun Jinqiao glared at Nanny Wei and swayed her hips as she left.

---

千里寻笑道："装什么装，不在意又何必给我透露夏果关在哪里？"

Qianlixun laughed: "Why pretend? If you don't care, why tell me where Xiaguo is locked up?"

---

她们进入西院，直奔竹兰居。已有几个下人守在竹兰居门口，将千里寻挡住。

They entered the west courtyard and headed straight for Zhulan Residence. Several servants were already guarding the door, blocking Qianlixun.

---

千里寻说："还敢拦老娘？怎么？想找打？"

Qianlixun said: "You dare block me? What? Looking for a beating?"

---

下人知道她的厉害，求道："少夫人别为难我们几个，我们自知不是您的对手，可我们也得听主君的。"

The servants knew how formidable she was. They pleaded: "Young madam, don't make things difficult for us. We know we're no match for you, but we have to obey the master."

---

千里寻生气道："不要再称呼我少夫人！既然知道老娘的厉害，还不赶紧闪开？否则我的拳头打的就是你们！"

Qianlixun said angrily: "Don't call me young madam anymore! Since you know how formidable I am, hurry up and get out of the way! Otherwise, my fists will be on you!"

---

几个下人乖乖让开。

The servants obediently stepped aside.

---

千里寻冲进去，见门紧紧关着，一脚踢开。

Qianlixun rushed in. Seeing the door tightly closed, she kicked it open.

---

夏果坐在地上，身上被绳子绑着，口里塞着布子，见小姐前来，努力挣扎，流下高兴而又伤心的泪水。

Xiaguo sat on the ground, tied up with rope, her mouth stuffed with cloth. Seeing her young lady come, she struggled hard, shedding tears of joy and sadness.

---

苦荞上前取掉夏果口中的布子，她"哇"地一声哭出声来："呜呜……！我以为小姐以后不会再管我，呜呜……！小姐您终于来了！呜呜……！"

Kuqiao went forward and removed the cloth from Xiaguo's mouth. She burst into tears: "Wuwu...! I thought the young lady wouldn't care about me anymore. Wuwu...! Young lady, you finally came! Wuwu...!"

---

苦荞又帮夏果把身上的绳子解开。

Kuqiao helped Xiaguo untie the ropes from her body.

---

夏果跪在地上："小姐救我！呜呜……！"

Xiaguo knelt on the ground: "Young lady, save me! Wuwu...!"

---

千里寻生起怜悯之心："放心，我这就带你走。"

Qianlixun felt pity: "Don't worry. I'll take you away now."

---

韦嬷嬷在一旁不知该说些什么。

Nanny Wei stood aside, not knowing what to say.

---

门外传来林觉的声音："想走，没那么容易！"

Lin Jue's voice came from outside the door: "Want to leave? It's not that easy!"

---

千里寻转过身，发现林觉带来许多士兵，他们已将院子团团围住。

Qianlixun turned around and found that Lin Jue had brought many soldiers. They had surrounded the courtyard.

---

林觉站在院中："朱淑真，你现在已不是林府的人，跑到这里来抢我的人，我有很多理由可以把你抓起来！"

Lin Jue stood in the courtyard: "Zhu Shuzhen, you're no longer part of the Lin family. You come here to snatch my person. I have many reasons to arrest you!"

---

千里寻走出屋门："林觉，你搞搞清楚，夏果是老娘的人，是你把老娘的人抢走，老娘还没找你算账，你却倒打一耙，真有你的。"

Qianlixun walked out of the room: "Lin Jue, get this straight. Xiaguo is my person. You snatched my person. I haven't settled accounts with you yet, and you turn around and accuse me. You've got some nerve."

---

"知府大人已答应将她许给我，她就是我的人。"

"The prefect has already promised her to me. She's my person."

---

"哼！有什么证据？我爹有没有给你签字按手印？有没有把夏果的卖身契给你？都没有！告诉你，夏果的卖身契在我手中，她是老娘的人，老娘没同意把她给你，你凭什么强抢民女？"

"Hmph! What proof? Did my father sign and give you a deed? Did he give you Xiaguo's deed of sale? No! Let me tell you, Xiaguo's deed of sale is in my hands. She's my person. I didn't agree to give her to you. What right do you have to forcibly take a civilian woman?"

---

林觉被怼得哑口无言："那日，知府大人红口白牙允诺我，你也在场，难道想反悔？"

Lin Jue was rendered speechless: "That day, the prefect promised me in plain words. You were there too. Are you trying to go back on your word?"

---

"告诉你，老娘那日什么都没听到，何谈反悔？"千里寻瞅瞅围着的士兵，"你今日竟然把临安士兵带来私用，等我回去在我爹面前告你一状，我爹虽不管临安军政，但他与新来的节度使关系要好，绝对会将此事报告节度使大人。也不怕告诉你，我跟节度使的关系也不错，他要是知道你私用士兵强抢民女，定然不会饶恕！"

"Let me tell you, I didn't hear anything that day. How can I go back on my word?" Qianlixun looked at the surrounding soldiers. "You actually brought Lin'an soldiers here for personal use. Wait until I go back and report you to my father. My father doesn't manage Lin'an's military affairs, but he has a good relationship with the new military commissioner. He will definitely report this to the commissioner. I'm not afraid to tell you, I also have a good relationship with the commissioner. If he knows you're using soldiers for personal affairs and forcibly taking a civilian woman, he definitely won't forgive you!"

---

林觉还真害怕起来："此等小事，又何必对知府大人提起，更不必打扰节度使大人。"

Lin Jue actually became afraid: "This is such a small matter. Why mention it to the prefect? Even less need to disturb the military commissioner."

---

千里寻见对方害怕，带着众人打算离开。

Seeing the other party was afraid, Qianlixun led the group and prepared to leave.

---

林觉又堵住："你可以走，把夏果留下。"

Lin Jue blocked them again: "You can leave, but leave Xiaguo behind."

---

"老娘的话你是不是没听明白？夏果是我的人，你抢是没用的，不如好好想想重新娶妻之事，不要盯着老娘的人不放，再有下次，老娘的拳头绝不轻饶。"千里寻又看看周围的士兵，"即使你带这么多人来，老娘打他们也不在话下。"

"Didn't you understand what I said? Xiaguo is my person. It's no use you snatching her. You'd better think about remarrying. Don't keep eyeing my person. If there's a next time, my fists won't let you off easily." Qianlixun looked at the surrounding soldiers again. "Even if you bring so many people, I can still beat them."

---

朱淑真用节度使吓唬他，真的动起拳头来，又打不过，林觉只能让开，眼睁睁看着朱淑真将夏果从眼前带走，既不舍，又憋屈。

Zhu Shuzhen used the military commissioner to scare him. If it came to fists, he couldn't beat her. Lin Jue could only step aside and watch helplessly as Zhu Shuzhen took Xiaguo away from right in front of him. He was reluctant but also frustrated.

---

夏果坐在马车里一直哭，苦荞哄她半天不管用，干脆不再理会。

Xiaguo kept crying in the carriage. Kuqiao tried to comfort her for a long time but it didn't work, so she just ignored her.

---

韦嬷嬷很不满："哭什么哭！遇到姑娘这样的主子，你就偷着乐吧，要不是姑娘，你早都被陈公子糟蹋了。"

Nanny Wei was very dissatisfied: "What are you crying for? You should be happy to have a young lady like this as your master. If it weren't for the young lady, you would have been ruined by Young Master Chen long ago."

---

夏果哭得更厉害："我怎么这么命苦！呜呜……！"

Xiaguo cried even harder: "Why is my fate so bitter? Wuwu...!"

---

韦嬷嬷说："这女子，有些人长得好是好事，有些人长得好不见得是好事。"

Nanny Wei said: "This girl, for some people being good-looking is a good thing. For others, being good-looking isn't necessarily a good thing."

---

夏果继续哭着："我也不想长这样，可爹娘生下我就这样，难道是我的错？呜呜……！"

Xiaguo continued crying: "I didn't want to look like this either. But my parents gave birth to me looking like this. Is it my fault? Wuwu...!"

---

千里寻说："我看林觉对你还未死心，他必然会找我爹，你还是先别回朱府，继续住在新宅子，以后尽量别出来，采买之事吩咐其他人即可。"

Qianlixun said: "I think Lin Jue hasn't given up on you. He will definitely look for my father. You'd better not return to the Zhu residence for now. Continue living in the new residence. Try not to go out in the future. For shopping, just have others do it."

---

夏果哭着点点头。

Xiaguo nodded while crying.

---

先把夏果送回望月轩，马车继续回往朱府。

First, they sent Xiaguo back to Wangyue Pavilion. The carriage continued back to the Zhu residence.

---

到达朱府门口，刚走下马车，南双迎面走来："真是巧，我刚到，就在门口碰到幽栖居士。"

Arriving at the Zhu residence gate, they had just gotten off the carriage when Nanshuang came toward them: "What a coincidence. I just arrived and ran into Youqi Jushi at the gate."

---

南双前来，肯定是文渊有事要转达，千里寻自然开心："文渊找我有事？"

Nanshuang coming here definitely meant Wenyuan had something to convey. Qianlixun was naturally happy: "Does Wenyuan have something for me?"

---

"我家公子邀请姑娘明日去幽林别居，东方公子、曹公子和安公子都过去。"

"Young Master invites the young lady to Youlin Villa tomorrow. Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, and Young Master An will all be there."

---

"难道又要举行诗会？"

"Are they holding another poetry gathering?"

---

"是不是诗会我不大清楚，反正公子让我前来传话，邀请姑娘前去。"

"I'm not sure if it's a poetry gathering. Young Master just asked me to come and deliver the message, inviting the young lady."

---

"告诉文渊，明日不见不散。"

"Tell Wenyuan, I'll be there tomorrow, rain or shine."

---

南双说完，行了礼，目送三人往朱府走，趁其他人不注意，上前拉住苦荞，使了个眼色。

After Nanshuang finished speaking, he bowed and watched the three walk toward the Zhu residence. Taking advantage of the others' inattention, he went forward, pulled Kuqiao, and gave her a look.

---

苦荞领会，悄悄跟着他走到侧门的一边，问道："有什么事？"

Kuqiao understood and quietly followed him to one side of the side gate. She asked: "What's the matter?"

---

"泥人的事。"

"It's about the clay figurine."

---

"怎么了？"

"What about it?"

---

"我把泥人帮你还给白风，他看上去一点儿都不高兴，我怀疑他喜欢上你。"

"I helped you return the clay figurine to Baifeng. He didn't look happy at all. I suspect he likes you."

---

"什么？喜欢我？"

"What? Likes me?"

---

南双点点头："就像我家公子喜欢你家姑娘一样。"

Nanshuang nodded: "Just like my young master likes your young lady."

---

苦荞一阵脸红："别胡说，他怎么可能喜欢我？我才刚和他认识。"

Kuqiao blushed: "Don't talk nonsense. How could he like me? I just met him."

---

"苦荞姐姐难道没听过一个词？"

"Does Sister Kuqiao not know a word?"

---

"什么？"

"What?"

---

"一见钟情。"

"Love at first sight."

---

"你年纪不大，懂得倒挺多，赶紧回去吧，小姐一会儿找不到我，会着急。"

"You're not old, but you know a lot. Hurry back. If the young lady can't find me in a while, she'll worry."

---

苦荞红着脸进了朱府，追上小姐与韦嬷嬷。

Kuqiao entered the Zhu residence with a red face and caught up with the young lady and Nanny Wei.

---

千里寻早已注意到苦荞被南双拉走，又瞧她的脸色通红，问道："你的脸怎么那么红？南双对你说了什么？"

Qianlixun had already noticed Kuqiao being pulled away by Nanshuang. Seeing her red face, she asked: "Why is your face so red? What did Nanshuang say to you?"

---

苦荞不好意思道："没说什么，我脸红是因追小姐走得太快，热的。"

Kuqiao said embarrassedly: "Nothing. My face is red because I walked too fast chasing the young lady. It's from the heat."

---

韦嬷嬷的心思不在苦荞身上，还在纠结夏果之事："姑娘，要是练兵官找到老爷，说起今日之事，恐怕老爷会生气，姑娘有没有想到应对之策？"

Nanny Wei's mind wasn't on Kuqiao. She was still worried about Xiaguo's matter: "Young lady, if the training officer finds the master and talks about today's matter, I'm afraid the master will be angry. Have you thought of a solution?"

---

千里寻想想说："嬷嬷放心，一路上我已想好，我爹只是口头答应，他完全可以否认，只要他否认，此事便万事大吉。"

Qianlixun thought and said: "Nanny, don't worry. I've already thought it through on the way. My father only promised verbally. He can completely deny it. As long as he denies it, everything will be fine."

---

"可老爷是知府，一诺千金，况且当时两家的族人都在，岂可随意否认？"

"But the master is the prefect. A promise is worth a thousand gold. Besides, both families' clansmen were there at the time. How can he deny it at will?"

---

"难就难在这里，不想了，走一步看一步，不行我就去求节度使大人帮忙，我看林觉即使敢得罪我爹，也不敢得罪临安节度使。"

"That's the difficult part. Forget it. We'll take it one step at a time. If it doesn't work, I'll go ask the military commissioner for help. I think even if Lin Jue dares to offend my father, he won't dare offend the Lin'an military commissioner."

---

"只怕节度使大人不愿理会这等小事。"

"I'm afraid the military commissioner won't want to bother with such a small matter."

---

千里寻不能确定仪王是否答应帮她，不管他能否答应，明日去幽林别居还是要试上一试。

Qianlixun wasn't sure if the Prince of Yi would agree to help her. Whether he could agree or not, she still had to try tomorrow when she went to Youlin Villa.

---
