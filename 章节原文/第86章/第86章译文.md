# Chapter 86: Rescuing Xiaguo from Zhulan Residence

Matron Wei grabbed the young lady: "No need to get angry over this. The master already promised Xiaguo to the training officer. Since she was tied up and taken away, it's reasonable."

Then she scolded the servant boy: "I thought it was something important. Why rush to report to the young lady in such a panic? Hurry up and leave!"

The servant boy thought he had done a good deed, but didn't expect to be scolded by the Matron. He hurried away.

Madam Xiang also said: "The Matron is right. Don't get involved. That's another family's business. You barely left the Lin family. Why go back to their door? If you need a maid, I'll give you Yinxing."

"I don't lack maids. Kuqiao is enough. When I saved Xiaguo from Chen Haoyu, it was because I didn't want her to be bullied. Now she's being bullied by Lin Jue. How can I not get involved?" Qianli Xun remembered that Lin Jue wanted Xiaguo mainly because her figure was somewhat similar to hers, and felt even more disgusted. "No! I have to go see, or I won't be at ease."

Seeing she couldn't stop her, Matron Wei had to say: "Since the young lady wants to go, I'll accompany you."

Madam Xiang said helplessly: "Fine, fine. If you want to go, go. But if you bring Xiaguo back, hurry up and send her away. Keeping her around will only cause you trouble."

Accompanied by Matron Wei and Kuqiao, Qianli Xun arrived at the Lin residence.

Getting off the carriage, she entered the main gate. Ignoring the servants' attempts to stop her, she hurried toward the west courtyard as if she knew the way well. Halfway there, she ran into Chun Jinqiao coming toward her.

"Oh! The Zhu family's young lady is here. What? Can't bear to leave the Lin residence?"

Matron Wei said displeasedly: "What business is it of yours to ask?"

Chun Jinqiao didn't even bother to look at Matron Wei: "I'm talking to your young lady. What are you butting in for?"

Qianli Xun said: "You run to the Lin residence every day. The sun is already high when you leave. Lin Jue hasn't married you, or even made you a concubine. Don't you think that's pathetic?"

Hit where it hurt, Chun Jinqiao pretended not to care: "I don't care about that. As long as I can be with the training officer, it doesn't matter who he marries. I heard he just caught a maid and brought her back. He's planning to make her a concubine. She's locked up in the Zhulan Residence where you used to live."

After finishing, Chun Jinqiao glared at Matron Wei and swayed her hips as she left.

Qianli Xun laughed: "Why pretend? If you don't care, why tell me where Xiaguo is locked up?"

They entered the west courtyard and headed straight for Zhulan Residence. Several servants were already guarding the door, blocking Qianli Xun.

Qianli Xun said: "You dare block me? What? Looking for a beating?"

The servants knew how formidable she was. They pleaded: "Young madam, don't make things difficult for us. We know we're no match for you, but we have to obey the master."

Qianli Xun said angrily: "Don't call me young madam anymore! Since you know how formidable I am, hurry up and get out of the way! Otherwise, my fists will be on you!"

The servants obediently stepped aside.

Qianli Xun rushed in. Seeing the door tightly closed, she kicked it open.

Xiaguo sat on the ground, tied up with rope, her mouth stuffed with cloth. Seeing her young lady come, she struggled hard, shedding tears of joy and sadness.

Kuqiao went forward and removed the cloth from Xiaguo's mouth. She burst into tears: "Wuwu...! I thought the young lady wouldn't care about me anymore. Wuwu...! Young lady, you finally came! Wuwu...!"

Kuqiao helped Xiaguo untie the ropes from her body.

Xiaguo knelt on the ground: "Young lady, save me! Wuwu...!"

Qianli Xun felt pity: "Don't worry. I'll take you away now."

Matron Wei stood aside, not knowing what to say.

Lin Jue's voice came from outside the door: "Want to leave? It's not that easy!"

Qianli Xun turned around and found that Lin Jue had brought many soldiers. They had surrounded the courtyard.

Lin Jue stood in the courtyard: "Zhu Shuzhen, you're no longer part of the Lin family. You come here to snatch my person. I have many reasons to arrest you!"

Qianli Xun walked out of the room: "Lin Jue, get this straight. Xiaguo is my person. You snatched my person. I haven't settled accounts with you yet, and you turn around and accuse me. You've got some nerve."

"The prefect has already promised her to me. She's my person."

"Hmph! What proof? Did my father sign and give you a deed? Did he give you Xiaguo's deed of sale? No! Let me tell you, Xiaguo's deed of sale is in my hands. She's my person. I didn't agree to give her to you. What right do you have to forcibly take a civilian woman?"

Lin Jue was rendered speechless: "That day, the prefect promised me in plain words. You were there too. Are you trying to go back on your word?"

"Let me tell you, I didn't hear anything that day. How can I go back on my word?" Qianli Xun looked at the surrounding soldiers. "You actually brought Lin'an soldiers here for personal use. Wait until I go back and report you to my father. My father doesn't manage Lin'an's military affairs, but he has a good relationship with the new military commissioner. He will definitely report this to the commissioner. I'm not afraid to tell you, I also have a good relationship with the commissioner. If he knows you're using soldiers for personal affairs and forcibly taking a civilian woman, he definitely won't forgive you!"

Lin Jue actually became afraid: "This is such a small matter. Why mention it to the prefect? Even less need to disturb the military commissioner."

Seeing the other party was afraid, Qianli Xun led the group and prepared to leave.

Lin Jue blocked them again: "You can leave, but leave Xiaguo behind."

"Didn't you understand what I said? Xiaguo is my person. It's no use you snatching her. You'd better think about remarrying. Don't keep eyeing my person. If there's a next time, my fists won't let you off easily." Qianli Xun looked at the surrounding soldiers again. "Even if you bring so many people, I can still beat them."

Zhu Shuzhen used the military commissioner to scare him. If it came to fists, he couldn't beat her. Lin Jue could only step aside and watch helplessly as Zhu Shuzhen took Xiaguo away from right in front of him. He was reluctant but also frustrated.

Xiaguo kept crying in the carriage. Kuqiao tried to comfort her for a long time but it didn't work, so she just ignored her.

Matron Wei was very dissatisfied: "What are you crying for? You should be happy to have a young lady like this as your master. If it weren't for the young lady, you would have been ruined by Young Master Chen long ago."

Xiaguo cried even harder: "Why is my fate so bitter? Wuwu...!"

Matron Wei said: "This girl, for some people being good-looking is a good thing. For others, being good-looking isn't necessarily a good thing."

Xiaguo continued crying: "I didn't want to look like this either. But my parents gave birth to me looking like this. Is it my fault? Wuwu...!"

Qianli Xun said: "I think Lin Jue hasn't given up on you. He will definitely look for my father. You'd better not return to Zhu Manor for now. Continue living in the new residence. Try not to go out in the future. For shopping, just have others do it."

Xiaguo nodded while crying.

First, they sent Xiaguo back to Wangyue Pavilion. The carriage continued back to Zhu Manor.

Arriving at Zhu Manor gate, they had just gotten off the carriage when Nan Shuang came toward them: "What a coincidence. I just arrived and ran into Youqi Jushi at the gate."

Nan Shuang coming here definitely meant Wenyuan had something to convey. Qianli Xun was naturally happy: "Does Wenyuan have something for me?"

"Young Master invites the young lady to Youlin Retreat tomorrow. Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, and Young Master An will all be there."

"Are they holding another poetry gathering?"

"I'm not sure if it's a poetry gathering. Young Master just asked me to come and deliver the message, inviting the young lady."

"Tell Wenyuan, I'll be there tomorrow, rain or shine."

After Nan Shuang finished speaking, he bowed and watched the three walk toward Zhu Manor. Taking advantage of the others' inattention, he went forward, pulled Kuqiao, and gave her a look.

Kuqiao understood and quietly followed him to one side of the side gate. She asked: "What's the matter?"

"It's about the clay figurine."

"What about it?"

"I helped you return the clay figurine to Bai Feng. He didn't look happy at all. I suspect he likes you."

"What? Likes me?"

Nan Shuang nodded: "Just like my young master likes your young lady."

Kuqiao blushed: "Don't talk nonsense. How could he like me? I just met him."

"Does Sister Kuqiao not know a word?"

"What?"

"Love at first sight."

"You're not old, but you know a lot. Hurry back. If the young lady can't find me in a while, she'll worry."

Kuqiao entered Zhu Manor with a red face and caught up with the young lady and Matron Wei.

Qianli Xun had already noticed Kuqiao being pulled away by Nan Shuang. Seeing her red face, she asked: "Why is your face so red? What did Nan Shuang say to you?"

Kuqiao said embarrassedly: "Nothing. My face is red because I walked too fast chasing the young lady. It's from the heat."

Matron Wei's mind wasn't on Kuqiao. She was still worried about Xiaguo's matter: "Young lady, if the training officer finds the master and talks about today's matter, I'm afraid the master will be angry. Have you thought of a solution?"

Qianli Xun thought and said: "Matron, don't worry. I've already thought it through on the way. My father only promised verbally. He can completely deny it. As long as he denies it, everything will be fine."

"But the master is the prefect. A promise is worth a thousand gold. Besides, both families' clansmen were there at the time. How can he deny it at will?"

"That's the difficult part. Forget it. We'll take it one step at a time. If it doesn't work, I'll go ask the military commissioner for help. I think even if Lin Jue dares to offend my father, he won't dare offend the Lin'an military commissioner."

"I'm afraid the military commissioner won't want to bother with such a small matter."

Qianli Xun wasn't sure if the Prince of Yi would agree to help her. Whether he could agree or not, she still had to try tomorrow when she went to Youlin Retreat.
