# Chapter 86: Rescuing Xiaguo from Zhulan Residence

---

Nanny Wei grabbed the young lady: "No need to get angry over this. The master already promised Xiaguo to the training officer. Since she was tied up and taken away, it's only reasonable."

---

Then she scolded the servant boy: "I thought it was something important. Why rush to report in such a panic? Hurry up and leave!"

---

The servant boy thought he had done a good deed, but didn't expect to be scolded. He hurried away.

---

Madam Xiang also said: "The nanny is right. Don't get involved. That's another family's business. You barely escaped the Lin family — why go back? If you need a maid, I'll give you Yinxing."

---

"I don't lack maids. Kuqiao is more than enough. When I saved Xiaguo from Chen Haoyu, it was because I didn't want her bullied. Now she's being bullied by Lin Jue. How can I just stand by?" Qianlixun remembered that Lin Jue wanted Xiaguo mainly because her figure resembled hers. The thought disgusted her even more. "No! I have to go check, or I won't be at ease."

---

Seeing she couldn't stop her, Nanny Wei had to say: "Since you insist on going, I'll come with you."

---

Madam Xiang said helplessly: "Fine, fine. Go if you must. But if you bring Xiaguo back, send her away quickly. Keeping her around will only stir up trouble."

---

Accompanied by Nanny Wei and Kuqiao, Qianlixun arrived at the Lin residence.

---

Getting off the carriage, she entered the main gate. Ignoring the servants trying to stop her, she hurried toward the west courtyard like she owned the place. Halfway there, she ran into Chun Jinqiao coming the other way.

---

"Well, well! If it isn't the Zhu family's young lady. What? Can't bear to leave the Lin residence?"

---

Nanny Wei said displeasedly: "What business is it of yours to ask?"

---

Chun Jinqiao didn't even glance at Nanny Wei: "I'm talking to your young lady. What are you butting in for?"

---

Qianlixun said: "You come running to the Lin residence every day, leave only when the sun's already high, and yet Lin Jue still hasn't married you — can't even get you a concubine's spot. Don't you find that pathetic?"

---

Hit where it hurt, Chun Jinqiao pretended not to care: "I don't care about titles. As long as I can be with the training officer, it doesn't matter who he marries. I heard he just dragged a maid back and locked her up in Zhulan Residence — the place you used to live in. He's planning to make her his concubine."

---

With that, Chun Jinqiao glared at Nanny Wei and swayed her hips as she left.

---

Qianlixun laughed: "Why pretend? If you didn't care, why tell me exactly where Xiaguo is locked up?"

---

They entered the west courtyard and headed straight for Zhulan Residence. Several servants were already guarding the door, blocking Qianlixun's way.

---

Qianlixun said: "You dare block me? Looking for a beating?"

---

The servants knew how formidable she was. They pleaded: "Please don't make things difficult for us. We know we're no match for you, but we have to follow the master's orders."

---

Qianlixun said angrily: "Stop calling me 'young madam'! Since you know how tough I am, then get out of my way! Otherwise my fists will find you!"

---

The servants obediently stepped aside.

---

Qianlixun rushed in. Seeing the door tightly shut, she kicked it open.

---

Xiaguo sat on the ground, bound with rope, a cloth stuffed in her mouth. Seeing her young lady arrive, she struggled hard, tears of joy and sorrow streaming down her face.

---

Kuqiao went forward and pulled the cloth from Xiaguo's mouth. She wailed: "Wuwu...! I thought you'd never come for me anymore... Wuwu...! You finally came! Wuwu...!"

---

Kuqiao helped untie the ropes from Xiaguo's body.

---

Xiaguo knelt on the ground: "Please save me! Wuwu...!"

---

Pity rose in Qianlixun's heart: "Don't worry. I'm taking you with me right now."

---

Nanny Wei stood to the side, not knowing what to say.

---

Lin Jue's voice came from outside the door: "Want to leave? Not that easy!"

---

Qianlixun turned around. Lin Jue had brought a squad of soldiers. They had already surrounded the courtyard.

---

Lin Jue stood in the middle of the yard: "Zhu Shuzhen, you're no longer part of the Lin family. You come here to snatch my person — I have plenty of reasons to arrest you!"

---

Qianlixun walked out of the room: "Lin Jue, get this straight. Xiaguo belongs to me. You're the one who snatched my maid. I haven't settled accounts with you yet, and now you turn around and blame me. You've got some nerve."

---

"The prefect has already promised her to me. She's mine."

---

"Hmph! What proof do you have? Did my father sign anything? Did he give you Xiaguo's deed of sale? Nothing! Let me tell you — Xiaguo's deed of sale is in my hands. She's my person. I never agreed to give her to you. What right do you have to abduct an innocent woman?"

---

Lin Jue was rendered speechless, then said: "That day, the prefect promised me with his own words. You were there too. Are you trying to go back on it?"

---

"Let me tell you, I didn't hear a thing that day. How can I go back on something I never agreed to?" Qianlixun glanced at the surrounding soldiers. "You actually brought Lin'an soldiers here for personal use. Wait till I go back and file a complaint to my father. He may not manage Lin'an's military affairs, but he's close to the new military commissioner. He'll report this for certain. And I'm not afraid to tell you — I'm on good terms with the commissioner too. If he finds out you're using soldiers to abduct innocent women, he won't let you off!"

---

Lin Jue actually grew afraid: "This is such a small matter. No need to bring it up to the prefect, let alone trouble the military commissioner."

---

Seeing him back down, Qianlixun led the group and prepared to leave.

---

Lin Jue blocked them again: "You can go. Leave Xiaguo behind."

---

"Are you hard of hearing? Xiaguo is mine. Snatching her won't work. You'd better focus on finding a new wife instead of eyeing what's mine. If there's a next time, my fists won't hold back." Qianlixun looked at the soldiers around them. "Even with all these men, I can still take them on."

---

Qianlixun had scared him with the military commissioner's name. In a fistfight, he couldn't beat her anyway. Lin Jue could only step aside and watch as Zhu Shuzhen took Xiaguo right from under his nose — reluctant to let go, yet seething with frustration.

---

Xiaguo kept crying in the carriage. Kuqiao tried comforting her to no avail, then gave up.

---

Nanny Wei was fed up: "What are you crying for? You should count yourself lucky to have a mistress like our young lady. If it weren't for her, you'd have been ruined by Young Master Chen long ago."

---

Xiaguo cried even harder: "Why am I so unlucky! Wuwu...!"

---

Nanny Wei said: "Girl, for some people, being pretty is a blessing. For others, it's a curse."

---

Xiaguo kept sobbing: "I didn't ask to look this way. My parents gave me this face. Is it my fault? Wuwu...!"

---

Qianlixun said: "I don't think Lin Jue has given up on you. He'll definitely go to my father. Don't go back to the Zhu residence for now. Stay at the new house. Try not to go out. For shopping, just send someone else."

---

Xiaguo nodded through her tears.

---

First they sent Xiaguo back to Wangyue Pavilion. Then the carriage continued to the Zhu residence.

---

They had just arrived at the Zhu residence gate and stepped off the carriage when Nanshuang came toward them: "What a coincidence. I just got here and ran into Youqi Jushi right at the gate."

---

Nanshuang coming here meant Wenyuan had a message. Qianlixun was naturally pleased: "Does Wenyuan need something from me?"

---

"My young master invites you to Youlin Villa tomorrow. Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, and Young Master An will all be there."

---

"Another poetry gathering?"

---

"I'm not sure if it's a poetry gathering. My young master just asked me to deliver the invitation."

---

"Tell Wenyuan — I'll be there tomorrow, rain or shine."

---

Nanshuang bowed and watched the three women walk toward the Zhu residence. Then, when no one was looking, he slipped up to Kuqiao and gave her a signal.

---

Kuqiao caught on and quietly followed him to one side of the side gate. "What is it?"

---

"It's about the clay figurine."

---

"What about it?"

---

"I returned the clay figurine to Baifeng for you. He didn't look happy at all. I think he likes you."

---

"What? Likes me?"

---

Nanshuang nodded: "Just like my young master likes your young lady."

---

Kuqiao blushed: "Don't be silly. How could he like me? I barely know him."

---

"Has Sister Kuqiao never heard of a certain phrase?"

---

"What?"

---

"Love at first sight."

---

"You're young but you sure know a lot. Hurry back — if the young lady can't find me, she'll worry."

---

Kuqiao entered the Zhu residence with a flushed face and caught up with Qianlixun and Nanny Wei.

---

Qianlixun had already noticed Kuqiao being pulled away by Nanshuang. Seeing her red face, she asked: "Why is your face so red? What did Nanshuang tell you?"

---

Kuqiao said embarrassedly: "Nothing. My face is red because I walked too fast catching up. It's the heat."

---

Nanny Wei's mind wasn't on Kuqiao. She was still worried about Xiaguo's situation: "Young lady, if the training officer goes to the master about today's incident, I'm afraid the master will be angry. Have you thought of a plan?"

---

Qianlixun said: "Nanny, don't worry. I've been thinking about it on the way back. My father only promised verbally. He can deny it completely. As long as he denies it, we're in the clear."

---

"But the master is the prefect. His word is his bond. Besides, both families' clansmen were present. He can't just deny it casually."

---

"That's the hard part. Forget it — we'll cross that bridge when we come to it. If push comes to shove, I'll ask the military commissioner for help. Even if Lin Jue dares to offend my father, he won't dare offend the Lin'an military commissioner."

---

"I'm afraid the military commissioner won't bother with such a small matter."

---

Qianlixun wasn't sure if the Prince of Yi would agree to help. Whether he would or not, she had to try tomorrow at Youlin Villa.
