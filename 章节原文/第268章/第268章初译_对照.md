第268章 三位公子访仪王
## Chapter 268: Three Young Masters Call on Prince Yi

第二天一大早，南双手拿书卷急匆匆来到乔文仙的屋子：“公子！不得了！有人写书骂幽栖居士！”
乔文仙接过，先看了看封面上的八个字，再翻开书浏览几行，又急又气：“从哪里得来？”
“从乔府的下人手里得的，说是昨日有人在西城的街上到处发，免费的。”
“府中多少人有？”
“没仔细算过，反正好几个人都有。”
“去！把他们手里的书卷收了，有多少收多少，全部烧毁！”
“好，我这就去办！”
南双刚出去，苗知瑜走进来：“文渊，你让南双去做什么？他为何急匆匆的样子？”
乔文仙将书卷递到苗知瑜面前：“你瞧，有人诋毁淑真，把一首好好的词说得一无是处，看着就来气。”
乔文仙依然称幽栖居士为淑真，苗知瑜虽已习惯，心里多少有些不爽。她装作不知道的样子，将书卷接入手中，并未翻开，担忧地问：“是过分，你打算怎么做？”
乔文仙面露难色：“我不知道，还是先把府内下人手中的书卷没收后烧毁再说。”
苗知瑜轻松许多，将书卷放在桌上，装作怀疑的样子：“真是奇怪，为何总有人诋毁幽栖居士？该不会她真的有问题？”
乔文仙并未将苗知瑜的问题听进心里，猛然说道：“不对！《减字木兰花·春怨》刚写出来她就给了我，连东方尘泽他们都不知道，怎么会传出去？”
苗知瑜开始紧张：“我听说陈家三公子陈浩飞经常向幽栖居士讨要诗词，说不定幽栖居士把这首词又写一遍送给他，是他拿到诗会传扬出去。”
乔文仙摇摇头：“淑真送他人的诗词从来不会重复。”
他用怀疑的目光看向苗知瑜：“该不会是你从我这里看到后传扬出去？”
苗知瑜庆幸，文渊没有将她想得够坏，不如顺水推舟。
她装作可怜而又后悔的样子：“都怪我，从你的桌案上看到这首词，爱不释手，便记在心里，向我的闺中密友宣扬一番，也许是这样传了出去，没想到被别有用心的人给利用，反而拿来诋毁幽栖居士。”
乔文仙安慰道：“怎么能怪你？你也是好心，只是不知淑真得罪了何人？竟然如此诋毁她？难道不怕知府大人计较此事？”
文渊实在好哄，苗知瑜本想回应，外面传来丫鬟的声音。
“少夫人，白夫人前来拜访。”
苗知瑜吩咐道：“先带白夫人到院外的亭子里等我，好生伺候，我很快就到。”
丫鬟回道：“是，少夫人。”
乔文仙一脸不悦：“为何你现在与林觉的娘子走得近？”
苗知瑜趁机表达不满：“只因我二人同病相怜，共同话题多些，才不由自主走得近。”
乔文仙明白苗知瑜的意思，反问道：“你已嫁给我，我也心甘情愿接纳你，又何必计较往事？”
“难道只有往事？我看不尽然，你仍亲切地称呼她淑真，还时常讨要她的诗词，会让我觉得你心里还有她。”
苗知瑜的语气充满询问，又试图肯定着她的猜测。因着急见白梅若，不等文渊回应，带着幼凡离开。
乔文仙正不知该如何应对，见苗知瑜离开，松口气：“看来我还得感谢白夫人，否则你会问得人心烦。”
苗知瑜来到亭子，白梅若已坐在亭中等她。走近后开玩笑道：“一大早的，白夫人跑来看我？该不会是想我了？”
白梅若没有心情开玩笑：“好我的苗夫人，出大事了，你还有心情跟我开玩笑？”
苗知瑜在白梅若对面坐下：“出什么大事？”
白梅若向四周瞧一遍，只有两人的贴身丫鬟，才放心说：“书卷之事仪王已知，他派人把书卷当街收回，我夫君还帮着把书局的管事给抓了，江南女词人的美名根本毁不了。”
苗知瑜担忧地问：“仪王妃可知此事？”
白梅若试探道：“不清楚，也许已经知道。不如你我去趟幽林别居？仪王妃若知道，问问她下一步该怎么办？若她不知，趁机告诉她，省得贵人埋怨我们两个办事不力。”
苗知瑜想想，认为对方说得有道理：“你先去府外等我，我给公婆说一声就来。”
亭中的石凳还未坐热，二人又朝两个方向走去。
白梅若多少感到庆幸，林觉的父母早亡，她少去与公婆相处的麻烦，想走出林府随时都可以。而苗知瑜不一样，想走出大门，还得请示公婆，他们若不同意，想出去都难。
苗知瑜请示公婆的过程相当顺利，毕竟是去拜访贵人，阻挡不得，再说儿媳妇与贵人走得近对乔家只有好处。
两人在乔府外汇合，坐上各自的马车赶往幽林别居。
First thing next morning, Nan Shuang burst into Qiao Wenxian's room waving a pamphlet. "Young Master! Disaster! Someone printed a book trashing Youqi Jushi!"

……
Snatching it, Qiao Wenxian scanned the eight words on the cover, flipped through several lines, and bristled with rage. "Where did you get this?"

一大早，申简辰没有打扰梦容，起床的动作尽量放轻。
"From servants around Qiao Manor—somebody handed them out free in the west quarter yesterday!"

旁边伺候的两名丫鬟过来为他整理衣装，像是能体会到王爷的心思一般，也是轻手轻脚。
"How many servants have one?"

穿戴整齐后，简单用过早饭，带着白风回司理院。
"Didn't count—at least half a dozen!"

依旧是翻阅卷宗，翻着翻着终于有个卷宗引起他的兴趣。
"Collect every last copy in the manor and burn them all right now!"

“去把韩师爷唤来。”
"Right away!"

白风领命而去。
Nan Shuang had barely bolted out when Miao Zhiyu strolled in. "What's got Nan Shuang sprinting off like that, Wenyuan?"

不一会儿韩谷进来，白风依然在王爷身边站好。
Handing her the booklet, Qiao Wenxian fumed, "Look! Somebody's smearing Shuzhen—ripping a gorgeous lyric to shreds! Makes my blood boil!"

申简辰收起手中的卷宗递给白风：“拿给韩师爷。”
Hearing him still call her *Shuzhen* pricked Miao Zhiyu, though she masked it well, taking the booklet unopened with a look of concern. "How awful! What are you going to do?"

白风接过，送到韩师爷手中。韩谷打开浏览一遍，原来是邵家镖局的案子。
"Not sure yet—at least burn every copy floating around our house first."

他将卷宗收好，亲自放回仪王面前的桌案上，开口说：“此人名唤彭景辛，是邵家镖局的镖师，之所以入狱是因强奸邵家姑娘，邵家便告到司理院。彭景辛被抓后，认罪倒快，至今已在狱中关押将近二十年。”
Relaxing inwardly, Miao Zhiyu laid it on the table with an innocent frown. "Odd how people are always trashing Youqi Jushi. Could there actually be some truth to it?"

申简辰瞧向桌上的一本《宋律》：“律法规定强奸罪处以绞刑，为何彭景辛依然活着？”
Barely listening, Qiao Wenxian froze. "Wait a minute! She handed me *Jianzi Mulan Hua: Spring Resentment* the second she wrote it—even Dongfang Chenze hasn't seen it! How on earth did it leak out?!"

“回仪王，大宋律法规定绞刑可用钱赎，彭家有钱，赎了他的命。”
Miao Zhiyu's pulse jumped. "I hear Third Young Master Chen Haofei begs poems from her all the time—maybe she copied it out for him and he shared it at a salon?"

“原来如此。”申简辰不好置喙古代的律法，心中却也着实不满，凭什么有钱连死刑都可豁免？很快他从不满的情绪中走出，继续问，“赎命的钱应该不少，大概需要多少？”
"Shuzhen never gifts the same poem twice." His gaze swung sharp onto Miao Zhiyu. "Wait—did *you* see it on my desk and pass it around?"

“绞刑大约需赎铜一百二十斤。”
Relieved he hadn't guessed half the truth, Miao Zhiyu rolled straight with it, putting on a tearful, remorseful face. "It's all my fault! I saw it on your desk, fell in love with it, memorized it, and recited it to my girlfriends! That must be how it leaked into some schemer's hands to smear her!"

申简辰迅速在脑中换算，这笔数目放在他穿越而来的时代不算什么，可在这个时代，的确不是一笔小数目，普通人家承受不起。
"How could I blame you? You meant well!" Qiao Wenxian soothed her. "Still, who on earth did Shuzhen offend to pull a stunt like this—aren't they scared of Prefect Zhu?"

“彭家有钱，彭景辛为何还要去邵家当镖师？”
*So easy to fool,* Miao Zhiyu thought—just as a maid announced from the doorway, "Young Madam, Madam Bai is here to call."

仪王的问题将韩谷难住：“案子已过去将近二十年，小的实在不知。令人欣慰的是，死刑免除后，邵家不再计较，每年邵正邺都会去狱中探望。邵家不缺钱，将狱卒上下打点，彭景辛住的牢房倒也干净，每次邵正邺探望时，两人还会在狱中对饮，聊上几句。”
"Escort her to the garden pavilion and serve tea—I'll be right out."

申简辰总觉得此案子疑点重重，不如改日亲自到邵家问个明白。
Qiao Wenxian frowned. "Why are you hanging around Lin Jue's wife lately?"

一位差役进来报告：“大人，司理院外来了三位公子，前来拜见您。”
Seizing the opening, Miao Zhiyu poked back, "Birds of a wounded feather share plenty to talk about."

一定是东方尘泽等人，申简辰想起回汴梁前的承诺，估计是为此事而来：“请他们进来。”
"You married me and I accepted you willingly—why drag up old history?"

差役退出后，韩谷说：“既然有朋友前来拜访，小人不便在此打扰殿下，先行退去，殿下若有任何差遣尽管吩咐。”
"Is it really just history? You still call her *Shuzhen* and keep begging her for poems—makes me wonder if she's still sitting in your heart!" Eager to meet Bai Meiruo, she didn't wait for an answer, sweeping out with Youfan and leaving Qiao Wenxian exhaling in relief: *Guess I owe Madam Bai a thank-you for sparing me an interrogation.*

申简辰点点头，示意韩谷退下。
Waiting in the pavilion, Bai Meiruo cut straight past Miao Zhiyu's playful greeting. "How can you joke when disaster just hit?!" Checking that only their two personal maids stood near, she dropped her voice. "Prince Yi found out! He confiscated every pamphlet off the streets, and my husband arrested the print manager! Our plan to wreck her reputation is dead!"

韩谷出去没多久，东方尘泽、曹班和安明轩走进，三人满面春风，一起行礼：“仪王殿下千岁！千岁！千千岁！”
"Does Princess Consort Yi know?" Miao Zhiyu asked sharply.

申简辰笑道：“行了，许久未见，你们三个倒变得客套起来，一见面说些奉承话，还不赶快自行落座。”
"No idea—maybe! Why don't we ride out to Youlin Retreat together? If she knows, we ask for new orders; if she doesn't, we tell her straight before she blames us for botching it."

三人不再客气，各自坐下。
"Good thinking. Wait outside the gate while I tell my in-laws."

奉承话还是不能少，东方尘泽第一个开口：“仪王殿下回到临安，我们三人本想早些来拜见，怕贵人旅途劳累，便等您多休息几日，再前来叨扰。谁知您竟屈尊担任司理参军，令我等刮目相看。听说您一上任，三下五除二破获一起杀人案，更是令我等钦佩不已。”
Bai Meiruo silently blessed her luck—with Lin Jue's parents long dead, she walked out whenever she pleased, while Miao Zhiyu still had to ask permission. Fortunately, calling on royalty drew instant approval from Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian, and the two wives rode off toward Youlin Retreat in separate carriages.

曹班接着夸道：“我还听说仪王让知府大人尽快解决临安流浪的百姓，帮他们安一个家，再帮他们寻个谋生的手段，实乃仁义之举。”
Over at Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen slipped out of bed at dawn without waking Mengrong; two maids tiptoed to dress him, and after a quick breakfast he rode with Bai Feng back to the Judicial Office.

昨日才安排下去的事，曹班这么快已知道，估计是从他那位担任经略使的父亲口中得知，足以说明昨日去府衙见知府大人的事已在地方官员中传开。传开也好，正好让临安官员知道他申简辰作为仪王赵朴关心的是什么，往后他们当官做事才不会偏离正途。
Paging through old dossiers, one case file caught his eye. "Fetch Clerk Han."

申简辰看向曹班，不由想起神柳下无意中偷看到的情景，本以为满腹经纶，记忆力极好的他不会为情所困，没想到跟自己一样可怜，喜欢上难以得到之人。
When Han Gu hurried in, Shen Jianchen passed him the scroll. Glancing over it, Han Gu recognized the Shao Escort Agency case and laid it back on the bench.

“曹公子可有喜欢之人？”
"The prisoner is Peng Jingxin, an escort guard for the Shao agency," Han Gu explained. "Jailed nearly twenty years ago when the Shaos charged him with raping a daughter of their house. He confessed right away."

大家正奉承着，仪王脱口而出的问题将众人搞蒙。只有白风清楚是怎么回事，那日在神柳下偷听时，他就在王爷身旁。
Glancing at his copy of the *Song Penal Code*, Shen Jianchen asked, "Statute mandates strangulation for rape—why is Peng Jingxin still alive?"

三位公子努力回过神来，都面露惆怅的表情，看得出东方尘泽和安明轩早已知道曹班的隐情。
"Song law lets convicts redeem a strangulation sentence with copper, Your Highness. Peng's family had silver and bought his life."

面对仪王的问题，曹班不得不回：“有，可想要走到一起却很难。”
Shen Jianchen grimaced inwardly—money buying your way off the gallows! Setting his irritation aside, he asked, "How much to redeem a life?"

“再难也要坚持下去，希望曹公子得偿所愿。”申简辰已屈服现实，他不愿别人像他一样。更确切地说他心甘情愿屈服于现实的同时，对曾执着的感情依然抱有一丝希望。
"Roughly one hundred and twenty *jin* of redemption copper."

曹班回道：“感谢仪王鼓励，我会努力坚持。”
Not pocket change by any means—far beyond ordinary folk. "If Peng's family had money like that, why was he working as an escort guard for the Shaos?"

刚刚充满阳光的气氛因仪王的问题而消散，这样下去不好说出举办诗会之事。
"Twenty years back, Your Highness—before my time. Oddly enough, once his death sentence was commuted, the Shaos held no grudge. Every single year Shao Zhengye visits him in jail, greases the jailers' palms so his cell stays spotless, and even shares wine and a chat with him inside."

安明轩挤出笑容：“两位都已奉承过仪王，也得给我说些奉承话的机会。”
*Something smells mighty fishy here,* Shen Jianchen thought. He'd pay the Shao household a visit soon.

另两位公子的脸上顿时露出笑容。
Just then a constable stepped in. "Three young gentlemen are outside asking to see Your Honor."

申简辰第一次觉得奉承话也可活跃气氛，他并未阻拦，任由安公子口吐芬芳。
Dongfang Chenze and his crew—come to collect on his pre-Bianliang promise, no doubt. "Show them in." Han Gu bowed out tactfully, and moments later Dongfang Chenze, Cao Ban, and An Mingxuan strode in beaming, dropping into grandiose bows. "Long live Your Highness Prince Yi! A thousand years!"

“仪王身份尊贵，却当司理参军，说明您心有百姓，是个真正为百姓做事的好王爷。听我爹说今年知府大人不打算再举办宴会，用省下的银子修建……修建什么村来着？”
"Cut the court flattery and sit," Shen Jianchen laughed. "Haven't seen you three in weeks and suddenly you're bowing like courtiers?"

东方尘泽提醒道：“懒人村。”
Laughing, the three took seats, though Dongfang Chenze still delivered his warm opening tribute. "We wanted to call the day you got back, yet hated disturbing your rest. Never dreamed you'd stoop to take Judicial Investigator—and crack a murder case in a flash right off the bat!"

“对！懒人村！仪王不理临安政务则罢，一旦理会瞬间扭转风气，是临安之福，百姓之福。”
"And pushing Prefect Zhu to build homes and livelihoods for Lin'an's homeless vagrants!" Cao Ban chimed in. "Truly benevolent!"

对方夸赞得稍有过火，申简辰阻止道：“再说下去，小心我让白风用胶把你们三个的嘴给封住。”
Word of yesterday's meeting with Zhu Xiyan had already traveled through official circles—probably via Cao Ban's father, Military Commissioner Cao. Good; let every mandarin in Lin'an see where Prince Yi stood. Looking at Cao Ban, Shen Jianchen suddenly remembered the hushed embrace beneath the Divine Willow—this brilliant bookworm was trapped in the same painful love triangle as himself.

三人对视一笑，安明轩立刻扭转话锋
"Got someone special in your heart, Young Master Cao?"

：“不过我等也担心，知府大人不再举办宴会，该不会以后连诗会都无法举行？”
The abrupt curveball froze all three scholars mid-smile—only Bai Feng knew why. Recovering, all three wore shadowed looks; clearly Dongfang Chenze and An Mingxuan already knew Cao Ban's secret.

终于引到诗会，东方尘泽很满意，让他直接提醒仪王，还真不好意思，安兄转移话锋，引得实在巧妙。
"I do," Cao Ban admitted quietly. "Only being together is terribly hard."

他们三个的心思申简辰早已猜到，不如直接说出想法。
"Hold on no matter how hard," Shen Jianchen said. "I hope you get what your heart wants." Having surrendered to reality himself, he hated watching another man fold—and truth be told, a tiny stubborn spark still glowed inside his own chest.

“诗会当然要继续，只要不借诗会之名行奢侈之风便好，何况东方公子每次举办诗会都是自己掏腰包，更与奢侈二字无关，这样的诗会应大力提倡。本王决定自掏银两，帮东方公子举办一场更大的诗会，再利用仪王的身份帮你招来临安所有名流，让你的诗会成为临安第一。”
"Thank you for the encouragement, Your Highness. I won't give up."

仪王果然没有忘记承诺，三位公子喜上眉梢，东方尘泽更是感激不尽：“多谢仪王！”
Seeing the room turn gloomy before they'd even pitched their poetry salon, An Mingxuan slapped on a sunny grin. "Hey, you two got your turn flattering His Highness—my turn!"

申简辰继续叮嘱：“怎么举办，规格如何，邀请哪些人，需要多少银两，都由你们自己定，有什么需要直接告诉白风，总之必须是临安有史以来最大的诗会。”
Everyone chuckled, breaking the chill, and Shen Jianchen let him roll.

三人齐声回道：“谨遵仪王叮嘱。”
"An imperial prince taking Judicial Investigator proves your heart truly beats for commoners! My father says Prefect Zhu canceled every official banquet this year to fund rebuilding... what was that village called?"

申简辰想起书卷一事，虽说已收回不少，应该还有许多流落在外，若有人以此做文章，恐怕影响更加恶劣。
"Lazybones Village," Dongfang Chenze supplied.

“还有一事，需要你们利用诗会解决。”
"Right, Lazybones Village! One finger lifted by Your Highness and Lin'an's entire officialdom snaps straight!"

三人面面相觑，不知仪王想说的是什么。
"Keep laying it on that thick and I'll have Bai Feng glue your three mouths shut," Shen Jianchen warned with a grin.

东方尘泽说：“仪王请讲。”
Trading amused glances, An Mingxuan pivoted smoothly. "Still, with Prefect Zhu canceling banquets, we do worry whether poetry salons might get axed too..."

“昨日有人在街上免费发放书卷，上面全是诋毁幽栖居士的话语，不知你们可知此事？”
*Smooth setup,* Dongfang Chenze cheered inwardly.

三人摇摇头，曹班说：“从未听说。”
Reading them like open books, Shen Jianchen came straight out with it. "Of course salons continue—so long as they aren't excuses for luxury. Young Master Dongfang pays out of his own pocket anyway, which deserves full support. I'll fund a grander salon for you myself and lend Prince Yi's name to pull in every notable scholar in Lin'an—making your salon number one in the city."

桌上正好留有一本，申简辰让白风拿给三位过目。
