## Chapter 139: Uncovering the Mastermind at Last

First thing next morning, Shen Jianchen waited in his carriage outside Wangyue Pavilion for Qiao Mengrong. Last night had shown him Wenyuan would never forget Youqi Jushi—carrying her in his heart for life.

What about Qianli Xun? Would she carry Wenyuan in her heart forever too?

Peering out the carriage window as Wangyue Pavilion's household saw Qiao Mengrong off, he saw only Matron Wei and a cluster of maids, no Qianli Xun—was she still heartbroken?

The second Qiao Mengrong climbed inside and sat down, Shen Jianchen blurted out, "How is Youqi Jushi?"

Remembering Shuzhen's warning last night, Qiao Mengrong eyed him with caution: "Why is Brother so concerned about Shuzhen?"

Shen Jianchen scrambled to cover: "Not for her—for Wenyuan! Saw last night he'll never get over Youqi Jushi, probably carry her in his heart his whole life, which'll wreck his marriage later. Just wanted to know where Youqi Jushi's head is—if she's going to pine for him forever too."

Satisfied by his excuse, Qiao Mengrong dropped her guard: "She hurts, sure, yet she's sorted it all out—won't stay tangled up over Young Master Qiao anymore..."

A faint, hidden smile tugged at Shen Jianchen's mouth.

"Though she also swore off men and romance for good—said love hurts too much, so she's living solo from now on."

His smile died on the spot. Qianli Xun was a woman of her word—melting her heart now would be ten times harder.

A long sigh slipped out: "Sigh..."

"Why sighing, Brother?"

Covering smooth: "Just a shame watching two people in love torn apart."

Thinking of themselves, Qiao Mengrong smiled soft: "Brother and I are so lucky."

Without replying, Shen Jianchen felt a heavy reluctance as the carriage pulled away from Wangyue Pavilion.

For Qianli Xun, this winter promised to bite twice as cold. She made up her mind to keep her braziers roaring, hole up in Wangyue Pavilion, and wait for spring.

Her half-year mark wasn't even close, yet her story with Wenyuan had already hit the final page—arriving out of nowhere, ending just as abrupt.

Asking herself honestly: had she stopped loving Wenyuan overnight? No. Yet she ordered herself to act like she had—fake it long enough and habit turned act into truth.

No romance? Time to pour into her career! Besides writing huaben scripts, she meant to turn Wangyue Pavilion into a second Qianli Fist Manor.

Where to find students? Posting flyers to recruit apprentices wasn't realistic right now; better pick servants inside Wangyue Pavilion with decent martial aptitude and train them to keep busy.

She knew Kuqiao and Xiaguo too well—zero martial bones between them.

Gathering the whole staff and sizing them up, she picked ten: three maids and seven male servants.

"From today on, drop your chores and train in Qianli Fist with me—clear?"

Overjoyed, all ten shouted in unison: "Clear!"

"Good! Follow me to the back courtyard!"

Matron Wei didn't interfere—poor Young Lady had suffered enough; whatever kept her smiling went.

Since returning from beneath the Divine Willow, Xiaguo hadn't dared speak a word to her mistress, terrified Miss would pack her back to the countryside like Matron Wei threatened.

Walking toward the back courtyard alongside Kuqiao, Xiaguo rushed ahead and dropped to her knees: "Beat me or scold me all you want, Miss—just don't send me away!"

Qianli Xun halted: "What are you kneeling for? Ground's freezing—you'll catch cold!"

"Not getting up until Miss promises not to send me away!"

"When did I ever say I was sending you away?"

"Miss didn't say it, yet I messed up—sooner or later you'll kick me out..."

Recalling Xiaguo's crush on Wenyuan—too numb to care yesterday—now that Xiaguo brought it up, a prickle of annoyance did surface. Having her own maid moon over her boyfriend would irk anyone.

"How long has this been going on?"

Xiaguo held nothing back: "Ever since before your divorce, Miss, when you met Young Master Qiao by West Lake—that's when I fell for him."

The very day Qianli Xun had fallen for Qiao Wenxian at first sight—how could she forget?

"Started pretty early, didn't you."

"I figured once Miss married Young Master Qiao, I'd get to wait on him—maybe even become his concubine someday..."

Kuqiao snapped, "Xiaguo, shut your mouth!"

Swallowing her annoyance—what was there left to guard now that she and Wenyuan were finished?—Qianli Xun said, "Keep talking."

"The night Miss stayed at Youlin Retreat, I snuck over and confessed to Young Master Qiao, and he turned me down. Yesterday he turned me down again. I could've hidden it from you, Miss, yet you've treated me too well to lie—so I worked up the nerve to confess today. Please don't kick me out!"

Qianli Xun wanted to scold her, then stopped—who the girl crushed on was her own business! Still, she needed to say something to show she was over Wenyuan for good.

"You're my maid—where's your backbone?! Throwing yourself at a man and begging to be a concubine?! Quit embarrassing me out there!"

Walking on toward the back courtyard, she paused two steps later: "Still kneeling? Get up and come to the back yard with Kuqiao!"

"Thank you, Miss!" Jumping up thrilled, Xiaguo hurried after her.

From then on, between drilling her ten martial students and writing huaben scripts, Qianli Xun's days filled tight and brisk. Before long, Wenyuan stopped crossing her mind during daylight—and not thinking meant not hurting.

At night alone in bed he still crept back, though the sting had dulled to a quiet wistfulness—what a shame such a lovely romance had ended so soon.

Her ten trainees had decent enough build, though short of true master potential. Qianli Xun didn't mind—she was keeping busy, not looking for a grandmaster heir to Qianli Fist.

Throwing themselves into practice with fierce energy, all ten nailed the basics within a month.

Pleased, Qianli Xun moved them onto real combat moves—envisioning seven male guards watching her gates and three bodyguard maids trailing her everywhere. What noble lady in Lin'an could top that entourage?!

On Laba Festival, while Zhu Xiyan sat eating Laba porridge, Lin Jue arrived with news: they had caught a rumor-monger.

Changing into plain clothes with no servants, Zhu Xiyan followed Lin Jue to an abandoned house tucked in a lonely corner of town.

Soldiers guarded the door, swinging it open as the Prefect and Commandant Lin stepped up.

Inside lay a man trussed tight with rope, a rag stuffed in his mouth.

Lin Jue signaled a soldier to yank the gag out.

The instant his mouth cleared, the prisoner yelled, "Mercy, Your Lordship! I didn't spread any rumors—somebody framed me!"

Lin Jue sneered: "My men don't nab folks without proof! Refuse to confess and you'll taste a stout cudgel."

"I didn't do anything, Your Lordship—what's there to confess?!"

Lin Jue barked, "Men! Give him a round of warning strokes!"

A soldier strode in hefting a thick wooden staff while two others pinned the man face-down on the floor; heavy blows rained down as the prisoner howled in agony.

Offering Zhu Xiyan a chair, Lin Jue assured him, "Wait a minute, Lord Prefect—we'll pry the truth right out of him."

Zhu Xiyan nodded, more impressed with Lin Jue by the minute.

Twenty strokes in, the prisoner broke, shrieking, "Mercy, Your Lordship! I'll talk! I'll talk!"

Raising a hand, Lin Jue halted the beating; the soldier lowered the club and stepped back.

"If I talk, Your Lordship has to promise to spare me!"

Zhu Xiyan said, "Talk first; I'll decide once I hear it."

Panting, the man spilled: "I was just following orders—paid to walk around reading off those rumor sheets!"

Zhu Xiyan pressed: "Who gave you orders?"

"Our boss!" Naming the gang boss, he added the boss was probably lounging at Chunxiang Brothel right now.

Lin Jue dispatched men to Chunxiang Brothel at once; moving fast, they hauled back the exact gang boss who had met Chen Haoyu in the alley.

Dragged into the dark abandoned room, one look at his underling's blood-soaked rear and the soldier's thick club killed any urge to lie—he dropped straight to his knees.

"I confess everything, Your Lordship—just spare my hide! I only took silver to do a job; I've got zero grudge against Miss Zhu!"

Face dark as thunder, Zhu Xiyan demanded, "Spit it out—who paid you to pull this filthy stunt?!"

The gang boss swallowed: "Truth be told, he's even tied to the Zhu house..."

Hearing a tie to his own family spiked Zhu Xiyan's rage: "Who?!"

"Young Master Chen—Chen Haoyu!"

Shock shot Zhu Xiyan straight to his feet: "Are you sure you're not lying?!"

"Give me ten extra guts and I wouldn't dare lie to you, Your Lordship! Young Master Chen held a grudge against Miss Zhu, cooked up this smear campaign for revenge, had his own people write out the sheets for us, and paid us heaps of silver!"

Forced to believe it, Zhu Xiyan sank back into his chair in dead silence.

"Every word's the gospel truth, Your Lordship—nothing personal from us, please forgive us!" the boss begged.

Unwilling to widen the scandal—and knowing these hired thugs didn't merit death—Zhu Xiyan waved a hand.

Lin Jue snapped at both men: "His Lordship is merciful and letting you off—get lost before he changes his mind!"

Scrambling up, the boss hoisted his untied underling and started for the door.

Zhu Xiyan barked: "Hold on!"

Both froze in terror, dropping back to their knees: "Mercy, Your Lordship!"

Zhu Xiyan warned them cold: "Walk out that door and breathe not one word of today to a living soul—above all not to Young Master Chen—or I will show no mercy!"

The boss swore, "Today never happened!"

Lin Jue roared, "Still kneeling?! Scram!"

Scrambling up again, the boss dragged his underling out into the lane.

With the culprit turning out to be Chen Haoyu, Lin Jue sensed the tricky bind and asked, "Shall I arrest Young Master Chen, Lord Prefect?"

Without a blink, Zhu Xiyan ordered steel-firm: "Grab him! Keep the Chen family in the dark. Much obliged, Commandant Lin—and keep your lips sealed tight on this."

"Rest easy, Lord Prefect—not a syllable leaks from me."

All the way back to Zhu Manor, Zhu Xiyan couldn't wrap his head around it: as far as he knew, Chen Haoyu and Shuzhen never even crossed paths—why on earth did that brat hate Shuzhen enough to wreck her name and taint the whole Zhu house?!

Once he got that little menace in his hands, he'd grill the truth out of him AND teach him the discipline the Chens failed to give him!
