# Chapter 90: Can't Help But Stare at Her

---

Arriving at Youlin Villa at the agreed time, Qiao Wenxian led Nanshuang to welcome them at the gate. Before Shuzhen could get off the carriage, he had already come to the front of the carriage, extended his hand, and waited to help her down.

---

Qianlixun pushed open the door, leaned out, saw Wenyuan's outstretched hand, and smiled knowingly. She took his hand naturally and stepped down gracefully.

---

Wenyuan was reluctant to let go and held her hand even tighter. Qianlixun certainly wouldn't pull away on her own — she wanted to be held by Wenyuan every moment.

---

"Has everyone arrived?"

---

"Everyone's here. Only you were missing."

---

"I didn't expect everyone to be so enthusiastic. Is it another poetry gathering?"

---

"Not today. Dongfang Chenze and the others wanted to come to Youlin Villa to have fun, and they made me bring you along."

---

Qianlixun thought of Miao Zhiyu: "Was Young Lady Miao invited?"

---

"Young Lady Miao only likes attending poetry gatherings. For things like having fun, there's no need to invite her. Even if invited, she wouldn't come."

---

Qianlixun remembered Miao Zhiyu's probing words: "That's not certain. If you, Wenyuan, were to invite her personally, I think she would definitely come."

---

Qiao Wenxian detected a hint of jealousy and leaned close to Shuzhen's ear: "What? Are you jealous?"

---

Qianlixun pushed Wenyuan's head aside with her other hand: "Don't talk nonsense."

---

Being able to make the woman he loved jealous made Qiao Wenxian feel delighted. He held Shuzhen's hand even tighter and led her into the villa.

---

From the strength of Wenyuan's grip, Qianlixun could feel his affection, which made her heart even sweeter.

---

Kuqiao and Nanshuang followed behind. Seeing Young Master Qiao and her young lady so close, Kuqiao felt a pang of envy. Remembering Baifeng's confession yesterday, she asked: "Nanshuang, where's Baifeng? Why haven't I seen him?"

---

"Baifeng is accompanying the Prince of Yi."

---

"Will he come with the prince later?"

---

"Of course. And Princess Jiahe too."

---

Kuqiao smiled understandingly.

---

Nanshuang noticed and asked curiously: "Why is Sister Kuqiao so concerned about Baifeng? You're not falling for him too, are you?"

---

Kuqiao was too embarrassed to admit it: "You're just a kid! What do you know? Stop making things up!"

---

Arriving outside the small courtyard where Qiao Wenxian lived, Qianlixun was attracted by a wooden platform: "It's so high. How was it built? I want to go up and take a look."

---

"I'll take you up." Qiao Wenxian led Shuzhen by the hand and climbed the platform step by step.

---

Reaching the top, when the entire Youlin Forest came into view, Qianlixun sighed: "It's so beautiful! We're actually standing above the dense forest! Wenyuan, you stay in such a beautiful place every day. How enviable."

---

Qiao Wenxian looked at Shuzhen affectionately: "No need to feel that way. As long as you marry me, you can stay here every day too."

---

Only the two of them were on the platform. With such a wonderful atmosphere, such beautiful scenery, and such a beautiful person, Qiao Wenxian couldn't help but want to kiss Shuzhen...

---

Qianlixun could feel Wenyuan's breath approaching her. She grew nervous, yet also looked forward to her first kiss. Then out of the corner of her eye, she caught sight of the Prince of Yi. He stood below the wooden platform, looking up at them.

---

He appeared at the wrong time again! Ruining her good moment for the second time. Being watched by him, Qianlixun was in no mood to enjoy the sweet kiss Wenyuan was about to give her. She quickly turned around: "Wenyuan, I didn't expect we could see the Divine Willow in the distance from here."

---

Not having kissed the one he loved, Qiao Wenxian wasn't sad. Shuzhen had already pulled her hand away when she turned around just now. He stepped forward and grabbed her hand again: "You're not allowed to let go of me easily."

---

Qianlixun whispered: "Don't you see the Prince of Yi watching us from below? With him staring, how can I be at ease with you..."

---

Qiao Wenxian deliberately asked: "With me what?"

---

"With you... that."

---

"What?"

---

"Just stop asking. Let's get the Prince of Yi to leave first."

---

"What are you afraid of? Kuqiao and Nanshuang are also below."

---

"How can that be the same? Kuqiao and Nanshuang are sensible — they won't look if they don't have to. But the Prince of Yi stares straight at us, like watching a show. He really has no manners. He doesn't even know to look away."

---

"Don't talk about the Prince of Yi like that. He's a very good person. I need his help with many things. Even your divorce — he was the one who persuaded Lin Jue and the prefect. Otherwise, how could the divorce have gone so smoothly?"

---

Qianlixun had always thought it was heaven's will, and with Nanny Wei stepping in, her father Zhu Xiyan had shown mercy. She never expected the Prince of Yi had helped her: "What? My divorce had something to do with him?"

---

Qiao Wenxian briefly explained how the Prince of Yi had helped at the banquet. Qianlixun suddenly understood. She looked at the Prince of Yi again — he was still looking up at them.

---

"Wenyuan, come down with me and thank the Prince of Yi."

---

Qianlixun pulled her hand back and was the first to walk down the wooden platform.

---

The wooden platform was high, and it took time to descend. Shen Jianchen had been watching Qianlixun walk down gracefully. When she reached his side, she curtsied elegantly: "Greetings, Your Highness the Prince of Yi."

---

Shen Jianchen had known yesterday that Wenyuan would invite Qianlixun to come. Today, he certainly wouldn't go to the prefect's office. He had been waiting in his room. When he estimated the time was about right, he brought Baifeng to the courtyard where Wenyuan lived, only to see Qianlixun climbing the wooden platform.

---

The intimacy between Qianlixun and Wenyuan made his heart ache, but he couldn't help watching. When Qianlixun turned around, he finally breathed a sigh of relief — at least they hadn't kissed. Jealousy toward Wenyuan rose in his heart, but reason wouldn't let him do something foolish out of jealousy.

---

As Qianlixun walked down the steps toward him, Shen Jianchen fantasized that she would throw herself into his arms. Instead, he heard her polite yet distant greeting.

---

"I've said before, Youqi Jushi doesn't need to be so formal when seeing me."

---

"Today I must show my respect. Even if the Prince of Yi asked me to kneel, I wouldn't say a word of complaint."

---

"Why say that?"

---

"Wenyuan just told me that the divorce went so smoothly thanks to the Prince of Yi's help. Otherwise, it wouldn't have been so smooth."

---

"It was just a simple favor. Nothing to mention."

---

"For the Prince of Yi, it was just a trifling matter. But for me, it was full of difficulties. How about I kneel and kowtow three times with resounding thuds to the Prince of Yi?"

---

As Qianlixun spoke, she was about to kneel.

---

Shen Jianchen stepped forward and supported her: "I don't need you to do that. If you want to thank me, just give me an answer."

---

Qianlixun showed a confused expression: "What answer?"

---

Shen Jianchen reminded her: "I asked you a question last time. You haven't given me the answer yet."

---

Qianlixun couldn't remember at all: "What question?"

---

Shen Jianchen let go of Qianlixun. He was somewhat angry.

---

"Youqi Jushi should think carefully about my question and prepare the answer. I'll be waiting at any time."

---

The Prince of Yi looked unhappy. Qianlixun didn't dare say more and watched as the Prince of Yi walked into the courtyard where Wenyuan lived.

---

Baifeng followed the prince over. At a glance, he saw Kuqiao. He came close. With their masters present, the two could only communicate with their eyes.

---

Seeing them exchanging glances, Nanshuang was very dissatisfied but didn't dare speak. Only after the Prince of Yi entered the courtyard did Baifeng reluctantly leave.

---

Qiao Wenxian didn't understand: "Shuzhen, when did the Prince of Yi ask you a question?"

---

"I don't remember." Qianlixun recalled in her mind the scenes of her interactions with the Prince of Yi. "Oh! I remember. That night I came to Youlin Villa. It must have been then that the Prince of Yi asked me. But what he asked, I don't recall at all."

---

"It's fine. Don't trouble yourself over it. The Prince of Yi was helping you, and also helping me. With me thanking him, you don't need to worry."

---

"Is that really okay?"

---

"Of course. Relax. With me here, leave everything to me."

---

She had been thinking about how to repay the great favor she owed the Prince of Yi. With Wenyuan saying this, Qianlixun felt much more relaxed. But then she became troubled again. She had come here today wanting to ask the Prince of Yi for help regarding Xiaguo. Now how could she bring herself to open her mouth?

---

Just as she was troubled, she saw Princess Jiahe approaching with her maid. Qianlixun left Wenyuan behind and went to greet Princess Jiahe, thinking that getting along well with her might allow her to trouble the Prince of Yi again to help with Xiaguo's matter.

---

"Greetings, Princess Jiahe."

---

Qiao Mengrong took her hand: "I'm very happy that Youqi Jushi could come today. Even happier than Young Master Qiao."

---

"Why?"

---

"They're all men. It's not interesting being the only woman. With you here, I won't feel lonely."

---

With Shuzhen's hand held by Princess Jiahe, Qiao Wenxian had no way to intervene and could only follow helplessly behind them.

---

The courtyard where Wenyuan lived wasn't very large. Inside, four trees were enclosed, distributed on both sides of the path. Following the path straight led into the house. The outer hall of the house was quite large, with tables and chairs already set up. Dongfang Chenze, Cao Ban, and An Mingxuan all sat on the right. The left was obviously reserved for the other three.

---

The Prince of Yi sat in the main seat. From the moment Qianlixun entered, his gaze had been fixed on her.

---

Because Qianlixun and Princess Jiahe were holding hands and standing very close, others thought the Prince of Yi was gazing at the princess. Even Princess Jiahe herself thought so, feeling delighted in her heart.

---

Princess Jiahe sat at the table near the Prince of Yi. Qianlixun sat in the middle, with Wenyuan sitting beside her.

---

After Qianlixun was seated, Shen Jianchen didn't dare look at her anymore, mainly worried that Wenyuan would be suspicious, and even more worried about hurting Princess Jiahe. So he withdrew his gaze.

---

The servants of Youlin Villa entered, set up the wine and dishes, filled everyone's cups, and then withdrew.

---

Qiao Wenxian said: "Your Highness, Brother Dongfang brought his own dancers. Why don't we enjoy a dance first?"

---

Shen Jianchen nodded.

---

Dongfang Chenze looked at Huayu behind him. Huayu understood his master's intention and quickly went out to arrange the dance performance.

---
