## Chapter 236: Roses to Speak the Heart

直到阿升把博雅斋的门从外面关好，传来上锁的声音，陈浩宇才从傻乐中清醒过来：“又锁门？肯定是怕本公子跑去春香楼，还不快些承认喜欢我？放心，你现在赶本公子去春香楼，本公子也不会去。”
青瓜摸摸公子的额头：“没发热啊？怎么说起胡话来？”
陈浩宇一把将青瓜的手打到一边：“瞎摸什么？本公子怎么可能发热？”
“公子，您认为少夫人把门锁起来是喜欢您？怕您去春香楼？”
“要不然呢？”
青瓜担心道：“该不会软招有后遗症？要不您怎么说起胡话来？”
“什么胡话？是真心话。”
“难道公子以后真的不再去春香楼？”
陈浩宇手撑着下巴，脸上露出美滋滋的表情：“春香楼有什么意思？本公子玩腻了，现在只想守在家里，守在墨香苑。”
青瓜更加确定公子喜欢上少夫人：“真好，以后我们就有好日子过。”
“你怎么变化这么快？刚才还认为我说胡话，这会儿又高兴？”
青瓜做好躲避的准备，小心问：“公子该不会是喜欢上少夫人？”
陈浩宇并未介意：“有这么明显吗？竟然都被你瞧出来。没想到本公子喜欢上母老虎，说出去谁会信？连我自己都觉得像是做梦。”
“是像做梦，我也感觉不真实。”青瓜倒上一盏茶，为公子放在桌上，“您白天还对少夫人骂骂咧咧，到晚上就喜欢上她，会不会太快了些？”
“难道你没听过一个词：瞬息万变？”
青瓜想到公子白天说的梦话：“公子，您认真想想，会不会以前已喜欢上少夫人，只是您自己还没意识到。”
陈浩宇仔细回忆，自从永泰茶楼被朱淑真打，又在竹林偶遇，从此两人真正结下梁子，他有事没事揣摩如何报复她，朱淑真三个字整日在脑子里打转，此前他从未对哪个女子如此动过心思。
难道她就是这样住进他的心里？他只是一直被报复之心迷惑而不自知？直到今日因被她折磨感到委屈流下一滴眼泪，才真正明白自己的心意？看来是这样。
“没想到你还能看懂本公子，以前怎么没发现你有这本事？”
又被公子夸，青瓜觉得荣耀，老实交代：“您白天趴在书桌上睡着，梦里唤少夫人的名字，还说您喜欢她，我就站在您身边，听得一清二楚。”
陈浩宇更是美滋滋的，他终于真心喜欢上一位姑娘，还鬼使神差地把她娶回家：“青瓜，从明日起，你家公子我要改变，让朱淑真对我刮目相看。”
青瓜提醒道：“公子不用等到明日，您今日的软言软语说得不错，我发现少夫人都快招架不住。”
Only when Asheng pulled Boya Study's door shut and snapped the padlock did Chen Haoyu snap out of his dreamy grin. "Locking me in again? Scared I'll sneak off to Chunxiang Brothel—just admit you like me already! Relax, even if you chased me out to the brothel with a broom, I wouldn't go!"

“嘿嘿嘿……，我终于找到对付她的法宝，真没想到，原来她吃这一套，以后我可得好好利用软招，直到把她软成娇滴滴的美娇娘，倒在本公子的怀里，她就不会总用拳头对付我，说不定还会用拳头保护我，嘿嘿嘿……”
Qinggua pressed the back of his hand to his master's forehead. "No fever... why are you talking crazy?"

陈浩宇畅想着朱淑真躺在他怀里的情景，笑得哈喇子都快流出来。
Chen Haoyu swatted his hand away. "Quit poking me! Why would I have a fever?"

第二天，阿升和三宝早早来到博雅斋，把门打开，叫醒陈浩宇。
"You think Young Madam locks your door because she likes you and worries you'll visit brothels?"

陈浩宇从未起过这么早，本想大骂两句，想到自己的感情，立刻来了精神，在青瓜的伺候下穿好衣裳，收拾完毕，各自给脸上绑块布，用来遮盖口鼻。
"What else?!"

来到素心草堂门外，阿升轻轻敲了敲门。
"Does the soft-touch plan cause brain damage? Otherwise why the gibberish?"

门被打开，苦荞手提马桶，放在门口，一句话没说，又把门关上。
"Not gibberish—God's honest truth!"

一股臭气袭来，阿升和三宝捂住鼻子，把头偏向一边。陈浩宇和青瓜虽绑着布子，可一点儿用处都没有，只觉一阵恶心。
"You're seriously done with Chunxiang Brothel?"

陈浩宇心想：“拉这么臭的屎，跟你的外表太不相配，算了，谁让本公子喜欢上你，这点儿小毛病忽略不计。”
Propping his chin on his palm with a dreamy smile, Chen Haoyu sighed. "What's fun about Chunxiang Brothel? Sick of it! All I want now is staying home right here in Moxiang Courtyard."

“青瓜，愣着干什么？还不赶快提了马桶去倒？”
Certain now, Qinggua beamed. "Awesome! Good days ahead for us!"

公子命令，青瓜不敢不照做，忍着臭气，拎起马桶，往茅厕方向而去。
"Flipped fast, didn't you? Calling me crazy one second, grinning the next!"

阿升和三宝被熏得不行，懒得再去理会。
Bracing to dodge just in case, Qinggua ventured, "Young Master... did you fall for Young Madam?"

青瓜走远后，臭气才渐渐消散。
Far from snapping, Chen Haoyu blinked. "That obvious? Even you spotted it? Me falling for the tigress—tell anyone and they'll call me insane! Feels like a dream even to me."

屋内，苦荞点燃香料开始熏屋子，边熏边说：“太臭了，难道明日您还要这样？”
"Sure feels unreal!" Pouring tea for his master, Qinggua marvelled, "Cursing her all morning, smitten by night—isn't that a bit fast?"

千里寻早已穿好衣裳，坐在茶桌前盯着茶具发呆，听到苦荞的问题，回道：“当然，再忍一日。”
"Ever heard the phrase 'shifting in a heartbeat'?"

“我现在只想祈祷姑爷今日事事都做得好，否则我不得天天一大早出去搞些臭粪放在马桶里，恐怕以后素心草堂变成臭粪草堂。”
Remembering the naptime mumble, Qinggua asked, "Think about it, Young Master—maybe you liked her ages ago without realizing it?"

苦荞的牢骚千里寻听到心里，她也觉受罪，那股子臭气熏得她到现在胃里都不舒服。她并未回应苦荞，继续想着内心之事。
Replaying the past made everything click: ever since she beat him at Yongtai Teahouse and crossed paths in the bamboo grove, he had obsessed day and night over getting back at her, her name looping nonstop inside his head—more thought than he had ever spent on any other woman. Had she snuck into his heart behind his grudge, until today's tear tore off the blindfold? Had to be!

一早起来，小姐就坐在茶桌旁一言不发。开始苦荞没当回事，现在才觉出不对来，手拿熏香，上前问道：“小姐，您看上去不高兴，是不是哪里不舒服？”
"Didn't realize you could read me so clear!"

千里寻才开口问：“苦荞，你觉得我报复陈浩宇对还是不对？”
Beaming at yet another compliment, Qinggua confessed, "You muttered Young Madam's name in your sleep this afternoon saying you liked her—heard every word."

苦荞毫不犹豫：“当然对！姑爷散播谣言，就应该受到惩罚。”
Chen Haoyu glowed brighter—falling in love for the first time in his life, and by sheer crazy luck already married to her! "Starting tomorrow, Qinggua, I'm turning over a new leaf to blow Zhu Shuzhen's socks off!"

“为何我已开始报复，却一点儿都不开心？”
"No need to wait till tomorrow—your soft talk today already had her reeling!"

“那是因为小姐心善，见不得别人遭罪。”
"Hehehe! Found her kryptonite at last! Keep laying on the soft touch until she melts into a sweet little bride curling right into my arms—no more punching me, maybe even using those fists to guard me! Hehehe!" Picturing it nearly had him drooling.

千里寻品味着内心，真的是因为她善良吗？她觉得不尽然：“让陈浩宇倒马桶，臭到他的同时，我们也被臭到，所以说报复他人，自己势必会受到反噬，也不知值不值得？”
At dawn, Asheng and Sanbao unlocked the door and roused him. Normally waking this early would have set him cursing; energized by love, he hopped straight up, dressed with Qinggua's help, and both tied strips of cloth across their noses and mouths.

小姐冷不丁说这段话，苦荞听明白一半：“难道您不想再报复姑爷？”
Outside Suxin Thatched Hall, Asheng tapped the door. Kuqiao cracked it open, set a chamber pot on the threshold without a word, and slammed it shut.

“想，怎能不想？”千里寻沉默一会儿，继续说，“只是不知是否值得？因为我一点儿高兴的感觉都没有。”
A pungent stench slammed out like a wall. Asheng and Sanbao pinched their noses and turned away; even through cloth masks, Chen Haoyu and Qinggua gagged.

苦荞不知该如何回应，屋子熏得差不多，估摸着姑爷等人已离开，便放下熏香，把门打开，再将窗户开得大些。
Whoa, stinky business doesn't match that pretty face at all—never mind, love overlooks small flaws! Chen Haoyu mused, barking, "Quit freezing, Qinggua! Grab the pot and haul it to the latrine!"

用过早饭，大概过了两刻钟，陈浩宇开始继续跑圈，今日他跑得心甘情愿，乐在其中。竟然神奇地发现原来跑步也不错，而且越跑越轻松。
Unable to refuse, Qinggua held his breath, grabbed the handle, and bolted toward the latrine while Asheng and Sanbao backed off gasping until the air cleared.

跑着跑着，半道上又碰见东哥，没等陈浩东说话，他边跑边打招呼：“东哥，我得完成跑步任务，不能跟你聊，否则还得倒马桶，回见！”
Inside, Kuqiao waved a lit censer around the hall grumbling, "Reeks to high heaven! Are we really doing this again tomorrow?!"

陈浩东目睹堂弟与三个下人跑远，心中纳闷：“浩宇怎么看上去很开心？难道我昨日出的主意泡汤？看来八成是这样。哼！你竟然被罚还这么开心，让人更来气，我得再想想办法，怎么能让你被母老虎欺负得更惨些。”
Already dressed and staring blankly at her tea set, Qianli Xun answered, "One more day."

经过花匠居住之地时，陈浩宇向一片玫瑰园瞧去，之前经过这里时毫无感觉，今日经过突然产生了想法，对身旁陪跑的青瓜吩咐道：“你去给花匠说说，给我准备一束玫瑰花。”
"Pray Young Master nails every chore today, or I'll be sneaking out at dawn every morning scooping stinky slop into that pot until Suxin Thatched Hall turns into Stinky-Slop Hall!"

有机会不用陪公子跑步，青瓜当然乐意，迅速撤离，向花匠居住的屋子走去。跟花匠说明来意，花匠自然屁颠屁颠地搞了一束玫瑰花，担心刺扎到手，找来一个普通的瓷瓶，装些水，放进去。
Truth be told, Qianli Xun's own stomach still rolled from the fumes. Watching her mistress sit stone-silent since waking, Kuqiao grew concerned and stepped up with the censer. "You look down, Miss—feeling sick?"

青瓜心里细数，公子才跑完五圈，不如趁机躲起来，免得再陪公子跑步。青瓜就近找到一个隐蔽的木廊，将花瓶放在长凳上，他则靠着木柱呼呼大睡。
"Kuqiao," Qianli Xun asked quietly, "am I right or wrong taking revenge on Chen Haoyu?"

直到一坨鸟粪掉在脸上，青瓜才被惊醒，用手摸摸脸，放在鼻子跟前闻了闻，臭烘烘的。在旁边拽了几片植物的叶子，将手和脸上的鸟粪擦干净，瞧瞧太阳，估摸时间差不多，抱起瓷瓶，赶到主道上等公子。
"Right, of course! He spread those rumors—he deserves punishment!"

等了不多会儿，陈浩宇与阿升、三宝跑过来，此处是目的地，最后一圈终于跑完。陈浩宇累得不行，二话不说，一屁股坐在路边的石头上。
"Then why don't I feel happy one bit?"

阿升夸赞道：“姑爷今日表现不错。”
"Because you have a soft heart, Miss, and hate watching anyone suffer."

陈浩宇并未说什么，抬眼看到青瓜怀里抱着一瓶玫瑰花走过来。
Probing her own chest, she felt it ran deeper than mere softness. "Making him empty a chamber pot stank us out right along with him. Hurting someone bounces straight back onto yourself. Wonder if it's even worth it?"

他的脸上露出笑容：“花不错，瓶子成色差些，一会儿回去把博雅斋的绿色瓶子换上。”
Half grasping her drift, Kuqiao asked, "Thinking of calling off the payback?"

歇得差不多，陈浩宇回到博雅斋，给玫瑰花换了瓶子，亲自抱在怀里，来到素心草堂门口，吩咐道：“你们两个还怕我跑了不成？老老实实站外面，别跟进来，有青瓜陪我即可。”
"I want payback—of course I do!" Qianli Xun paused. "Just wondering if it's worth it when it brings me zero joy."

阿升与三宝只好站在门外。
Not knowing how to reply, Kuqiao waited until the incense masked the odor and the men departed, then threw open the doors and windows for fresh air.

陈浩宇抱着瓶子走进屋。
After breakfast, Chen Haoyu hit the main lane for his ten laps—cheerful and willing today, surprised to find running actually felt brisk and energizing once he stopped fighting it.

苦荞正在茶桌前认真做茶，并未注意到。千里寻在写话本，十分专注，也不知有人走进来。
Halfway through, he spotted Chen Haodong ahead and waved without breaking stride. "Gotta finish my laps, Brother Dong—no time to chat or it's chamber-pot duty again! Catch you later!"

陈浩宇怀抱一瓶玫瑰花，乐滋滋地观察母老虎，怎么看怎么顺眼，开始后悔往日总是与她对着干。
Staring after his beaming cousin, Chen Haodong scowled: Why's he grinning while being punished? Yesterday's advice bombed? Irritating—need a nastier way to make the tigress wreck him!

苦荞做好茶，端着茶盏过来，一眼瞧到姑爷抱着一瓶玫瑰花盯着小姐傻笑。
Passing the gardeners' quarters, Chen Haoyu spotted a patch of blooming roses—never noticed them before, yet inspiration struck today. "Qinggua, go ask the gardener to cut me a bouquet of roses!"

“姑爷，您什么时候进来的？”
Thrilled to dodge five more laps, Qinggua peeled off at once. The gardener happily snipped a lush bunch and tucked them into a plain ceramic vase of water to guard against thorns. Counting laps in his head, Qinggua figured he had time to kill, ducked into a shaded corridor, set the vase on a bench, and leaned against a pillar for a nap—until a splat of bird droppings hit his cheek! Wiping his face and hands clean with torn leaves, he checked the sun, grabbed the vase, and hurried back to the main road just as Chen Haoyu finished his tenth lap and dropped gasping onto a roadside boulder.

陈浩宇收起傻乐的表情：“进来一会儿，见你家小姐在忙，所以站着等会儿。”
"Solid run today, Young Master," Asheng commended.

千里寻这才搁下笔，抬起头：“你来做什么？”
Spotting Qinggua trotting up with the roses brought a grin to Chen Haoyu's face. "Gorgeous blooms—vase is cheap though. Swap it into the celadon vase in Boya Study once we get back."

陈浩宇说：“给你送花。”
After catching his breath and switching the blooms into fine green porcelain, Chen Haoyu carried the vase himself to Suxin Thatched Hall, waving Asheng and Sanbao back at the door. "Scared I'll bolt? Wait outside—Qinggua's enough."

千里寻早已瞧见陈浩宇怀里的玫瑰花，再听这话，甚是诧异，一时愣住。
Stepping inside, he found Kuqiao engrossed in whisking tea and Qianli Xun bent absorbed over her writing, neither noticing him. Hugging his vase of roses, he stood gazing fondly at her, wondering why on earth he had ever picked fights with her.

陈浩宇吩咐身边的青瓜：“去，给少夫人把花放在桌上。”
Turning with the fresh tea bowl, Kuqiao jumped at the sight of Young Master hugging roses and grinning goofy at her mistress. "When did you walk in, Young Master?"

当青瓜捧着一瓶花快要走近时，千里寻立刻清醒过来，赶紧把手稿扣在桌上，目睹青瓜将花瓶放在桌上，又回到陈浩宇身边。
"A moment ago. Saw your mistress busy, so I waited."

苦荞看到这波操作，惊得忘记放下茶盏。
Setting down her brush, Qianli Xun looked up. "What do you want?"

穿越前，给千里寻送过花的男子不少，她并不会被一束花搞得心花怒放。只是陈浩宇的举动太出乎意料，一时想不通：“陈浩宇，你想做什么？”
"Brought you flowers."

陈浩宇话还未说，已笑得十分灿烂：“嘿嘿嘿……”
Eyes widening at the vase of roses, Qianli Xun froze speechless while Chen Haoyu nodded to Qinggua. "Set them on Young Madam's desk."

“笑什么笑？赶紧说话！”
Snapping awake as Qinggua approached, Qianli Xun flipped her huaben manuscript face down just before the vase landed beside it, while Kuqiao stood gaping with the tea bowl still frozen in midair.

陈浩宇这才忍住笑：“已经这么明显，难道你瞧不出来？”
Plenty of guys had sent Qianli Xun flowers in her modern life—hardly enough to turn her head—yet seeing Chen Haoyu pull this baffled her completely. "What game are you playing, Chen Haoyu?"

“明显什么？再不说，立刻滚出去！”
He broke into a goofy grin. "Hehehe..."

“你生起气来也这么好看，既然想让本公子直说，我就厚着脸皮当着两个下人的面直说了。朱淑真，我喜欢你，嘿嘿嘿……”陈浩宇表白完，又乐得不行。
"Quit grinning like an idiot and speak!"

听到姑爷的表白，苦荞更是惊讶，手中依然端着茶盏，两只眼睛瞪得老大。
Reining in his laugh, he said, "This obvious and you still can't tell?"

千里寻认为定是陈浩宇耍的把戏，并未当真：“陈浩宇，你以为用这种招数，老娘就不会再报复你？想多了吧？”
"Tell what?! Spit it out or get out!"

陈浩宇并不生气，也不用再念咒语：“你这么认为也正常，毕竟我们两个斗了那么久，以后你慢慢就知道，我真的喜欢上你，嘿嘿，没想到我竟然娶了自己喜欢的人，乔文仙和仪王都没这福气，嘿嘿嘿……”
"Even mad you look gorgeous! Since you want it blunt, I'll swallow my pride and say it right in front of the servants: Zhu Shuzhen, I like you! Hehehe!"

陈浩宇傻笑的样子令千里寻心里发毛：“难道他真的喜欢上我？不管是真是假，都不能当真。”
Kuqiao's eyes popped wide, tea bowl still hovering in her hands.

千里寻大声说：“别笑了！”
Certain it was a scam, Qianli Xun scoffed. "Think a stunt like this makes me drop my payback? Dream on!"

陈浩宇很听话，立刻停止笑声。
Unbothered—no inner chant even needed anymore—he grinned easy. "Makes sense you doubt me after all our battles. Give it time and you'll see I mean it! Hehe, who'd have guessed I'd end up married to the woman I love—beats Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi by miles! Hehehe!"

苦荞这才将茶盏给小姐放在桌上。
His goofy beaming gave Qianli Xun chills: Wait—is he actually serious? True or fake, never buy it! "Stop laughing!" she barked.

千里寻端起茶盏掩饰不自在，可不能总端着，她一饮而尽，轻轻放下茶盏：“按照昨日所说，你今日需向青瓜道歉，现在当着我的面完成任务。”
He zipped his lips instantly.

本以为陈浩宇昨日答应向青瓜道歉只是一时假装同意而已，今日再听到肯定会极其不爽，没想到他竟答应得相当干脆。
Kuqiao finally set the tea bowl down. Qianli Xun downed it in one gulp to hide her flustered nerves, then pointed sharp. "Per yesterday's schedule, apologize to Qinggua right now in front of me!"

“好，我这就道歉。”陈浩宇转向青瓜，“青瓜，你家公子我经常欺负你，是我不对，今日当着少夫人的面给你说声对不起，以后再也不会欺负你。但你需明白，我虽欺负你，心里其实早已把你看成我身边唯一的跟班。”
Expecting him to bristle now that the moment arrived, she blinked as he agreed without hesitation.

“唯一的跟班”将青瓜感动得流下眼泪：“公子，没想到我对您这么重要，早知如此，您怎么欺负我，我都受着，呜呜……”
"Done!" Turning to Qinggua, Chen Haoyu spoke earnestly. "Qinggua, I've bullied you nonstop and I was wrong. In front of Young Madam today, I'm sorry—never laying a finger on you again. Just know that rough as I was, in my heart you've always been my one true right-hand man."

“说你贱，你还真贱。本公子既然话已说出，以后绝不会打你一拳，踢你一脚。”陈浩宇道完歉，转过身，“已道完歉，你可满意？”
"One true right-hand man" burst Qinggua's tear ducts wide open. "I didn't know I meant so much to you, Young Master! Kick me all you want—I'll gladly take it! Boo-hoo!"

千里寻悄悄提醒自己，一切不过是陈浩宇假装而已，千万不能当真。也不知是谁给他出了这么一个让她难以招架的主意：“好，今日的第二个任务算你顺利通过。”
"Talk about a glutton for punishment! My word stands—never throwing another punch or kick at you." Turning back to Qianli Xun, he asked, "Apology delivered. Satisfied?"

陈浩宇骄傲道：“第一个任务我也顺利完成，阿升还夸我，以后你让我做什么我就做什么，绝不说一句反对的话。不过……，今日那马桶真的很臭，嘿嘿嘿！可我不嫌弃，嘿嘿嘿……”
Reminding herself not to fall for this bewildering new act, she answered stiffly, "Fine. Task two passed."

陈浩宇又独自瞎乐。
"Nailed task one too—even Asheng praised me! From now on, whatever you say goes without a peep of complaint! Oh—that chamber pot this morning sure reeked, hehehe, yet I don't even mind! Hehehe!"

千里寻不想再听下去：“要是没事，尽快离开，不要在这里烦我。”
Unable to listen to one more chuckle, Qianli Xun waved him out. "Done here? Scram and quit annoying me!"

“好嘞！这就走！背书去喽！嘿嘿嘿……”陈浩宇兴高采烈地离开。
"Right away! Off to memorize my books! Hehehe!" Chen Haoyu bounced out on air.
