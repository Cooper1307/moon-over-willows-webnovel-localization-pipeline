## Chapter 235: Soft Touch, Soft Touch, Use the Soft Touch

Stepping into Suxin Thatched Hall, Chen Haoyu breathed in the crisp scent of ink and paper—and for the first time in his life found it pleasant.

Zhu Shuzhen sat sipping tea at her desk. Without a scowl on her face, she really was striking—no mystery why Prince Yi lost his head over her, when even Chen Haoyu felt his own pulse skip. Shaking his head, he scolded himself: Snap out of it!

"Young Master is here, Miss," Hongdou announced.

Setting down her bowl, Qianli Xun dismissed her. "Got it. Go tend your chores."

Unable to peel his eyes off her, Chen Haoyu mused silently: If the tigress softened up a bit, she'd be a catch—gifted with a brush, lethal with her fists. Best of all, if anyone messed with me outside, I'd have a wife who could flatten them!

"What are you gawking at, Chen Haoyu?"

Her sharp bark snapped him back to earth. "Came to report to you—who else am I supposed to look at, Kuqiao or Qinggua?"

"Skip the banter. What did you memorize?"

"Too short on time—didn't get any memorized."

"None?! I gave you over an hour and you didn't learn one line? Not a single poem?!"

Chen Haoyu nodded sheepishly.

"Kuqiao! Fetch the bamboo ruler!"

Kuqiao handed over the disciplinary ruler. Rising, Qianli Xun stepped in front of him. "Hold out your palm!"

Shocking even himself, Chen Haoyu held his hand straight out like a chastised schoolboy.

His docile obedience caught her flat-footed. "Since when do you turn meek?"

Launching straight into his soft approach, Chen Haoyu smiled. "No more fighting you. Whatever you order, I'll obey."

Instant buzzkill. Qianli Xun tossed the ruler back to Kuqiao and returned to her desk. "Not wasting energy smacking your hand—need it intact to clean my room."

Score! The soft touch worked like magic! Behind his back, Chen Haoyu flashed a hidden thumbs-up to Qinggua, making the servant beam with pride at his first-ever praise.

"Wipe down every speck of dust inside and out of this hall," Qianli Xun ordered. "Qinggua doesn't lift a finger—and stay at least three feet clear of my desk."

"Dusting's a cinch—I'll do every bit myself! Letting Qinggua rinse the rag is fine, right?"

Unnerved by this sudden personality swap, Qianli Xun snapped, "Less mouth, more dusting!" and went back to writing her huaben, ignoring him.

Rolling up his sleeves with Qinggua's help, Chen Haoyu took a damp cloth and started wiping shelves, sneaking glances toward her desk. Lost in writing, she looked nothing like a tigress.

He remembered spotting her on West Lake last year nestled gentle as silk in Qiao Wenxian's arms. Back then he couldn't fathom why a fierce woman melted for a scholar with zero strength; now it clicked—she loved him. Now she was Mrs. Chen; if she ever fell for Chen Haoyu, she'd turn soft as a kitten too!

Then gloom hit: how could he compete with Qiao Wenxian's literary polish and pretty face, or Prince Yi's royal rank and martial prowess? He had nothing!

Glancing at plain-faced Qinggua—built for Chouya yet chasing Hongdou anyway—sparked an idea. He beckoned Qinggua close and murmured, "Quick question: if everybody told you you're way out of Hongdou's league, would you still chase her?"

"Course! Who cares what outsiders think? I like her—that's all that matters!"

Chen Haoyu flashed a second thumbs-up, sending Qinggua soaring. Right—screw overthinking! For all their pedigree, she still ended up married to me! Proof Heaven picked me for her—dive in and win her over!

Fired up, he scrubbed every beam, shelf, and chair for a full hour until the room shone. Kuqiao swiped a testing finger across a sill and nodded impressed. "Aside from haunting brothels, you dust just as clean as a maid, Young Master!"

"Watch that tongue—your young master is done with brothels for good! I'm a married man now; hanging out in brothels invites gossip and hurts my wife's feelings."

Glancing toward Zhu Shuzhen—who ignored the speech completely—he watched her lay down her brush. "Qinggua, fetch Asheng."

While Kuqiao stowed the manuscript and Qinggua went for Asheng, Qianli Xun flipped open a book and read absorbed, leaving Chen Haoyu staring in wonder. What's so gripping in those pages? If he wanted her respect, he needed some book learning—starting tomorrow, he'd actually study!

Asheng stepped in and bowed. "Your orders, Miss?"

"Report on Chen Haoyu's running."

"Eight and a half laps total, Miss. Stopped twice—once to watch a commotion, once to chat with Eldest Young Master in a pavilion."

Qianli Xun's face turned stern. "Two tasks passed out of four. Starting tomorrow, Chen Haoyu, you empty my chamber pot two days straight. Clear?"

Hot anger flared in his chest—and he hammered it down with an inner chant: Soft touch, soft touch, use the soft touch! Soft touch, soft touch, use the soft touch!

Miracle—the rage melted. Breaking into a sunny smile, he said, "Whatever Wife says goes! I listen to Wife!"

Qianli Xun froze, goosebumps rising. "Don't call me that!"

"Teach me what to call you then, Wife!"

"Still saying it?!"

"Shuzhen."

"Swallow the wrong pill? Put the surname back on!"

"Zhu Shuzhen."

Same three syllables, yet delivered in a warm tone totally unlike before. Was he faking some fresh scheme? One day of punishment couldn't rewrite a man overnight! Hard resistance she could smash; this slippery sweetness threw her off balance. Do not fall for it!

"Kuqiao, bring his pay."

Kuqiao placed the coins into his palm. "One tael of silver and fifty wen, Young Master. Miss honors her word, and expects you to take your punishment tomorrow."

Deciding to double down on the soft touch—already feeling strange new warmth toward her anyway—Chen Haoyu pressed the money straight back into Kuqiao's hand and faced Qianli Xun. "Since this is punishment, how can I pocket wages? Keep every copper—I accept every penalty willingly."

Both maid and mistress stared stunned.

"Miss, what do I do with it?" Kuqiao stammered.

Refusing to bend to his act, Qianli Xun countered, "Take it to the West Courtyard and split it among the servants—treat it as silver Chen Haoyu earned for them!"

Catching the bait meant to make him blow up, Chen Haoyu chanted silently twice more—Soft touch, soft touch!—and beamed wide. "Listen to Young Madam! Split it up, and I'll earn even more tomorrow so everyone eats well! Don't forget a share for my boy Qinggua!"

"Thank you, Young Master!" Qinggua cheered.

Thoroughly thrown off stride, Qianli Xun hurried onto tomorrow's slate. "Besides emptying my chamber pot, complete your full ten laps and memorization. Tomorrow's chore: sweep the entire main courtyard, inspected by Asheng—fail and earn another chamber-pot day. As for your apology..." Her gaze slid to Qinggua. "Apologize to Qinggua in front of every servant in Moxiang Courtyard."

Panicked his master would snap, Qinggua rushed in, "No need, Young Madam! Bullying other servants was wrong, bullying me is fine!"

One silent chant later, Chen Haoyu smiled easy. "Don't talk like that, Qinggua. Tomorrow I'll apologize proper."

Keeping her mask icy, Qianli Xun's eyes caught the angry welt still darkening his cheek. "Kuqiao, bring a jar of bruise salve."

Kuqiao fetched ointment from the inner room and handed it over. "Apply once tonight, Young Master, and the bruise clears by morning."

Thrilled—her first sign of care!—Chen Haoyu clutched the jar like treasure while Qianli Xun waved them off gruffly. "Get back to Boya Study and quit clogging my air!"

Once they left, Kuqiao ventured, "Miss, doesn't Young Master seem like a whole new man?"

"Illusion! Pure illusion!" Qianli Xun scoffed. "Nobody reforms in two days. Just some new trick—watch him show his true colors in a couple days! Besides, my revenge isn't done—don't want him reforming yet!"

"He's your husband, Miss. Him turning good only helps you."

"You don't want me getting payback?"

"If anyone else spread those lies, I'd slap them senseless myself! Yet he's your husband, Miss—I don't want you going through another divorce! If he shapes up and treats you right, isn't that the best of both worlds?"

"Even if he turns into a saint, it's nothing to me." Keeping polite peace was her max; falling for him was out of the question.

After dark in Boya Study, Qinggua dabbed salve across his master's bruised cheek, beaming. "Soft words worked wonders, Young Master! Young Madam couldn't even stay mad at you!"

"Smart plan, kid—once my life's back to normal, I'll reward you big."

"Skip rewards—just keep your promise and help me win Hongdou!"

"Hongdou's clearly not into you—why not switch? Chouya's practically throwing herself at you!"

Setting the jar down, Qinggua stood firm. "Don't want easy—I want Hongdou."

"Fine, fine, once I win Zhu Shuzhen over, I'll play matchmaker for you and Hongdou."

Left alone with his thoughts, Chen Haoyu realized today had flipped his entire world upside down. He would never forget the first woman who ever made him shed a tear—an offhand tavern vow suddenly come true. Falling for a woman felt dizzy and sweet; every sharp quirk of his tigress suddenly looked adorable. A goofy grin stretched across his face, spilling into a silly chuckle. "Hehe..."

Staring at him, Qinggua shook his head: Emptying a chamber pot tomorrow morning, and he's sitting here giggling like a fool!
