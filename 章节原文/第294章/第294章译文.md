## Chapter 294: Bringing Her Husband to the Poetry Salon

That evening Haoyu reported outside her door that he was moving into the counting house in two days, his tea lessons were flying, and the Chens now owned Yongtai Teahouse—getting invited right inside (while shooing Qinggua off to Chouya) and playfully critiquing Kuqiao's foam and grind until she sulked and went right back to re-grinding finer powder!

Handing him the salon invitation, Qianli Xun surprised even herself by asking him to take the day off and accompany her so tongues wouldn't wag—making Haoyu light up like fireworks!

Next morning, Qianli Xun dressed plain-faced with zero rouge in crisp, understated robes, only to blink at Haoyu strutting out in dazzling crimson silk so everyone would instantly recognize the Poetess of Jiangnan's husband! Spotting the pair leaving together sent maid Yingxue sprinting straight to Yao Qingyan, Chen Jianglin, and Old Madam Xia—delighting the elders right as Chen Jianglin settled the heir election for next month.

Meeting Chen Haofei at the south gate—who quickly got talked into sharing one lively carriage with the couple—Qianli Xun chatted warmly with her young brother-in-law about his upcoming Bianliang palace exams, urging him never to turn into a corrupt mandarin.
