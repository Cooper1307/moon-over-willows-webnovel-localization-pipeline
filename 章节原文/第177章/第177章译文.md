## Chapter 177: Master Jiang Fails to Pick a Fight

Chen Haoyu stepped out of Chun Jinmo's room with Lu Yuanfeng right behind him. Scarface and his lackeys hurried over. "Young Master Chen, Master Jiang even tied up the brothel madam! Looks like he will not leave without Miss Jinmo tonight."

Brimming with swagger, Chen Haoyu waved them off. "Stand aside! See the master standing behind me? If I do not flatten Master Jiang tonight, I will write my surname upside down!"

Scarface and the lackeys parted to clear his path.

Strutting to the top of the stairs, Chen Haoyu looked down onto a wrecked hall. Scared patrons had bolted, tables and chairs lay overturned, and the stage stood empty. The brothel madam sat bound to a chair with rope while the other courtesans had vanished upstairs, leaving the entire ground floor packed with Master Jiang's thugs.

"Failed to bully me in the street, so you crash Chunxiang Brothel instead, Master Jiang?" Chen Haoyu jeered as he descended. "Not scared my master here will plant another fist right on your face? Heh!"

The bruise Qianli Xun had left on Master Jiang's cheek still throbbed; Chen Haoyu's taunt pushed him over the edge. "You got lucky this afternoon, Chen Haoyu, yet tonight your luck runs dry! Watch your worthless neck! Be smart and call Chun Jinmo down to wait on me—catch me in a good mood and I might spare your hide."

"Please have pity on me, Young Master Chen, and give Jinmo up!" the bound madam wailed. "Blame my greedy eyes for chasing silver! Mercy, Master Jiang—I am not holding out, Young Master Chen will not let her go! Tell you what, I will find you a fresh young maiden instead—how about it?"

"I want nobody else—only Chun Jinmo!" Master Jiang barked.

The moment Chen Haoyu hit the ground floor, thugs closed a tight ring around him and Lu Yuanfeng.

Paid to clear trouble, Lu Yuanfeng murmured by Chen Haoyu's ear, "Relax, Young Master. Not one of these clowns is a match for me."

Chen Haoyu smiled easily. "Better call your boys off, Master Jiang, and let us talk like civilized men."

"Hand over Chun Jinmo and call me Granddaddy a few times, and I will talk civilized all night!"

"Silver you can have; the woman—wait for your next life! No, not even then!"

Fuming, Master Jiang backed off a step. "Take him! Tie Chen Haoyu up!"

As the mob closed in, Lu Yuanfeng eyed them with cold scorn. "Know what is good for you and back off. With me standing here, nobody touches a hair on Young Master Chen's head."

The thugs rushed in with raised fists. Shielding Chen Haoyu, Lu Yuanfeng met them head-on—one punch dropped a cluster, a sweeping kick sent another bunch flying, and within minutes every single one of Master Jiang's thugs lay groaning on the floor.

Dropping his fists without even breaking a sweat, Lu Yuanfeng declared, "Young Master Chen's safety is under my watch from now on. Keep your eyes open and stay clear of him—hear me?!"

Stunned, Master Jiang stared with fresh respect. "Hero, how have I never seen a fighter of your caliber before?"

Lu Yuanfeng answered with a cold snort.

Undeterred, Master Jiang pressed on. "No friendship starts without a bout! Let us get acquainted—I run Lin'an's underworld, folks call me Master Jiang. How do I address you, Hero?"

Spotting the poaching attempt, Chen Haoyu jumped in. "He is hired muscle on my payroll! Quit trying to butter up my bodyguard, Master Jiang!"

Ignoring him, Master Jiang pitched straight to Lu Yuanfeng. "Hero, wasting skills like yours trailing after Chen Haoyu leads nowhere. Why not throw in with me?"

"I never mix with underworld scum," Lu Yuanfeng scoffed.

Delighted, Chen Haoyu jeered, "Heard him! Now take your crew and get lost!"

Just then, boots thundered through the doorway as a squad of soldiers surged in behind Lin Jue, sword strapped at his hip. The instant the madam had been tied up, Chun Jinqiao had sent word to Lin Jue, who had arrived at top speed.

Face flipping straight to smooth courtesy at the sight of soldiers, Master Jiang greeted him, "Well now! What good wind blows Division Commander Lin here—and with such a big escort?"

Lin Jue swept a cold look over the thugs. "Instead of staying put at your Qiantang Gang, why are you tearing up Chunxiang Brothel?"

Unwilling to cross government troops, Master Jiang hurried to smooth it over. "A misunderstanding! Pure misunderstanding! I only came to settle a private score with Young Master Chen."

"Then why is the brothel madam tied to a chair?"

"What are you standing around for?!" Master Jiang barked at his men. "Untie Madam at once!"

Two thugs scrambled to undo the knots. Freed at last, the madam thanked Lin Jue profusely and scurried out of the way.

Lin Jue turned a disdainful gaze on Chen Haoyu. "Wedding right around the corner, Young Master Chen, and you are still haunting Chunxiang Brothel?"

Chen Haoyu sneered right back. "Brand-new groom yourself, Division Commander Lin, and here you are too!"

Both men let out a simultaneous "Hmph!" and looked away.

Turning back to Master Jiang, Lin Jue asked, "What private score do you two have? Let us hear it."

Master Jiang stepped close. "All over Chun Jinmo! I fancy her something fierce, yet Young Master Chen hogs her all to himself and blocks my path. Infuriating, right?"

"Infuriating indeed," Lin Jue agreed. "Still, settle your grudges out on the street—do not wreck Chunxiang Brothel, spoil everyone's fun, or drag bystanders in. Take Chen Haoyu outside and beat him to any shape you please, and I promise I will not lift a finger."

"With Division Commander Lin's word, my heart is at ease! Enjoy your night—see you around!" Master Jiang rounded up his battered crew and cleared out.

With order restored, Lin Jue dismissed his troops. With patrons long fled, the courtesans shut their doors for the night, leaving only Chen Haoyu, Lin Jue, and Lu Yuanfeng downstairs.

Trouble over, Chen Haoyu turned to head back up to Chun Jinmo—only for Lin Jue to stop him. "Not heading home yet, Young Master Chen?"

Chen Haoyu paused. "Division Commander Lin sure loves sticking his nose in."

Stepping closer, Lin Jue sneered. "How is Zhu Shuzhen marrying you any different from being with me? Just like me, you have zero taste for fancy literary games and live at Chunxiang Brothel—only your family has deeper pockets and you are a few years younger."

"Cannot help my irresistible charm," Chen Haoyu boasted. "Had Prefect Zhu desperate to shove his daughter at me."

Leaning in with a mocking smirk, Lin Jue murmured, "Watch out that rumor business does not blow open, or you will be eating more than you can chew."

"Save your concern for yourself, Division Commander Lin," Chen Haoyu murmured back. "Decide whether you are sleeping at Chunxiang Brothel tonight or crawling home to Lin Manor."

Just then Chun Jinqiao tripped down the steps and hooked her arm through Lin Jue's. "Stay the night with me, Commander?"

Without a second's hesitation, Lin Jue grinned. "Since I am already here, why not?"

Brushing past Chen Haoyu with Chun Jinqiao on his arm, Lin Jue started up the stairs.

Chen Haoyu hollered at his back, "Watch out, Division Commander Lin—you might end up divorced a second time!"

Pausing on the steps, Lin Jue tossed a laugh over his shoulder. "My current wife beats Zhu Shuzhen ten times over—gentle and sensible. Eat your heart out, kid!" Chuckling, he went upstairs with Chun Jinqiao.

Lu Yuanfeng looked at his employer. "Young Master Chen, am I staying the night at Chunxiang Brothel too?"

Already eager to get back to Chun Jinmo, Chen Haoyu halted. "Of course! What if that gang comes back after you leave?" Waving at the madam, he called, "Madam, send a girl down for my friend here!"

"Coming right up—plenty of beauties to spare!" Happy to earn back business, the madam shouted upstairs, "Jinchan!"

"Coming! What do you need, Madam?" a voice floated down.

"A gentleman downstairs needs company—get down here quick!"

Swaying her hips down the staircase, Jinchan took Lu Yuanfeng's arm. "Let me wait on you tonight, sir?"

Though unused to brothel ways, Lu Yuanfeng went along with his employer's treat and let himself be tugged upstairs.

Danger passed and everything squared away, Chen Haoyu whistled his way up the steps one by one.
