## Chapter 187: At Last Agreeing to Marry into the Chen Family

Before Qianli Xun could step out to greet them, Matron Wei ushered her family inside. Spotting Chen Haoyu right there, Chen Wenxin teased at once, "Seeing Qinggua outside, I guessed you sneaked over to see Shuzhen! Could not wait till tomorrow—had to whisper sweet nothings to your bride today?"

"Quit teasing, Sis—I came to discuss other business with her," Chen Haoyu muttered.

Her sister-in-law's words confirmed the shocking news. Keeping her temper reined in, Qianli Xun stepped forward and took Madam Xiang's hands. "Mother, what brings you, Brother, and Sister-in-Law here?"

"Your wedding, of course!" Madam Xiang smiled. "Your father wants you riding out from Zhu Manor tomorrow, so we came to fetch you home."

"I am not going back."

"Quit acting stubborn, Shuzhen," Zhu Jingyuan urged. "You are a Zhu daughter—not riding out from Zhu Manor will make us a laughingstock."

"I do not want to marry into the Chen family."

"Want to or not, you have to," Chen Wenxin said firmly. "Both families have already dispatched invitations—within two hours every notable household in Lin'an will have one in hand. Father-in-law even invited Prince Yi! Would you let guests gather with no bride and shame Father-in-law in front of Prince Yi? Be good and come home with us."

At the mention of Prince Yi, Qianli Xun fell mute. She had already thought of this: marrying into the Chen family would give Mengrong her brother back.

Noting her mistress's silence—meaning her refusal was no longer rock solid—and seeing Young Master Chen clearly eager for a private word, Matron Wei stepped in smoothly. "Madam, Miss and Young Master Chen seem to have matters to discuss. Let me walk you around Wangyue Pavilion for a bit; once Miss thinks it through, she will come home to Zhu Manor."

Trusting Matron Wei as always, Madam Xiang nodded. "Very well. I have visited several times without ever strolling the grounds—good weather to stretch our legs."

Taking Zhu Jingyuan's arm, Chen Wenxin pulled him along too. "Let us go stroll as well."

As the group trailed out with their maids, only Qianli Xun, Chen Haoyu, and Kuqiao remained in the hall—Kuqiao silently fretting over every twist landing against her mistress's wishes.

Watching Zhu Shuzhen sit brooding with knitted brows, Chen Haoyu chuckled. "Believe me now? No dodging this wedding, so let us figure out how we are going to coexist."

Ignoring him, Qianli Xun sank deep into thought. Facing this crossroads, what should she choose? Even having mapped out how to survive at Chen Manor, facing the actual wedding tomorrow rattled her—what if history repeated itself, turning all her struggle to divorce Lin Jue into a joke? Master Jingguang's "Right Thought and Right Mindfulness" echoed in her mind. Yield to please everyone else at her own expense, or bolt to the ends of the earth and throw both houses into chaos? Unlike letting go of Wenyuan or refusing Shen Jianchen for Mengrong's sake, where her gut knew the answer straight away, this choice felt foggy and impossible.

Watching her stay dead silent, panic crept up Chen Haoyu's spine. With her martial skills, skipping town tonight would be child's play—and if she vanished and broke the wedding, Master Jiang would march straight into Chen Manor with the contract, branding him the clan's eternal villain! Even if the elders somehow salvaged the estate, he would never lift his head in Chen Manor again.

"Go shut the door," Chen Haoyu told Kuqiao.

Kuqiao balked.

"Just shut it—scared I will hurt her? Like I even could! I have something dead serious to tell Zhu Shuzhen and cannot let anyone outside hear."

Struck by his sudden earnestness, Kuqiao walked over and pulled the door closed. When she turned back around, her jaw nearly hit the floor: Young Master Chen had dropped straight to both knees on the flagstones!

Desperate to get his contract back from Master Jiang, Chen Haoyu had thrown every ounce of pride out the window.

Startled clean out of her philosophical dilemma, Qianli Xun shot him a scornful glare. "Have you lost your mind, Chen Haoyu? Kneeling to me for what?!"

"Until you agree to wed me tomorrow, I am not getting up!"

"Hold on—weren't you fighting tooth and nail NOT to marry me? Why beg me now?!"

Cornered, Chen Haoyu spilled the entire story of last night's gambling den ambush from start to finish.

Qianli Xun's temper exploded. "YOU screw up and expect ME to wipe your backside?!"

"Have a heart and marry me!" Chen Haoyu pleaded. "I swear your life after marriage will be identical to right now! Do whatever you please inside Chen Manor—I will not stop you! Even if you fall for someone else, I will set you free whenever you want—deal?!"

Rising to circle around him, Qianli Xun scoffed, "Worthless as you are, you never looked half-wit stupid—how on earth did you fall into gambling with Master Jiang and stake your whole family's wealth?! Sharp on the surface, hollow inside!"

Swallowing the insult, Chen Haoyu begged on. "Had a short circuit in my brain and fell straight into Master Jiang's trap! Mock me all you want—I am past caring about face! Just agree to marry me! Name any terms you like and I sign every single one!"

Dropping back into her chair, Qianli Xun shook her head. "One thing baffles me: how does marrying ME equal the entire Chen family fortune? Is Master Jiang's brain flooded with water too?"

"Master Jiang figures you are so fierce you will lock me down and keep me clear of Chunxiang Brothel, handing Chun Jinmo straight to him!"

"A brothel girl outweighs the entire Chen family empire in Master Jiang's eyes?! His skull is not flooded with water—it is stuffed with junk!"

"Just say yes! Marrying me costs you not a hair! Tell you what—every scrap of property in my name now, plus anything I inherit later, I transfer straight into YOUR name!"

A spark of interest cut straight through Qianli Xun's reluctance. "How much property is in your name?"

"Never tallied it all—way more than yours! Pure profit, zero loss—turn down a deal this sweet and you will kick yourself later!"

"You fool around all day—who runs your properties?"

"My father's stewards. Zero hassle for you."

"Hand it all to me and what do you live on?"

"Which is why we negotiate: you take all the assets, and pay me a monthly allowance."

Odd how the prospect of serious wealth cheered her right up—she admitted to herself money beat relying on fickle men any day. At last she had found a deal that served her own interest while satisfying everyone else!

"If you knew today was coming, Chen Haoyu, why snatch my bamboo grove in the first place? Now you are begging to hand it right back!"

Catching the shift in her tone, Chen Haoyu lit up. "Does that mean you agree to marry me tomorrow?!"

"Get off your knees—let us negotiate how we live after the wedding."

Scrambling up with a grin, he repeated, "Same promise: name any terms you want!"

"Your words—no whining once I lay them down!"

"Not a peep!"

"First: once I enter Chen Manor, I do not share quarters with you. I get my own separate courtyard..."

Chen Haoyu winced. "That part is tricky..."

Qianli Xun's brow snapped together. "Said any terms, and you backpedal on item one?! Sincere my foot!"

"Calm down! As my wife you have to move into my courtyard—yet hear me out: the whole courtyard is yours, the main hall is yours, and I will fix up a side room for myself! Besides, I stay out most nights anyway, so the whole place is practically your private kingdom!"

"Fair enough—done. Second: zero interference with my attending banquets or poetry gatherings, zero blocking my friendships with scholars and poets, and pay no heed to outside gossip."

"I do not even care if you fall for another guy—why would I care about poetry salons? Agreed, agreed!"

"Third: the very day after the wedding, every asset under your name transfers into mine, and any future assets go straight to me too."

Wincing for half a beat before bowing to the inevitable, Chen Haoyu gritted out, "Agreed!"

She considered adding a fourth clause, then shrugged—freedom and wealth both locked down, what more did she need? "Three terms. Done."

"Your list is done—my turn!"

"Go ahead."

"Every month I get to draw one hundred taels of silver from the shops..."

Qianli Xun shot upright. "One hundred taels?! What on earth are you doing spending a hundred taels a month?!"

"Plenty of things! Less than a hundred barely covers pocket change!"

"No way—far too much! Cut it down!"

"Not much at all! Some months I spend two hundred!"

"Two hundred taels?! Textbook prodigal son!"

"Fine, ninety!"

"No!"

"Eighty!"

"No!"

Already feeling the squeeze before the wedding even started, Chen Haoyu forced himself to swallow it for the contract's sake. "You shoot down every number—how much will you give me?"

"Twenty taels!"

Chen Haoyu's eyes bugged out. "Twenty taels?! What am I, a beggar?!"

"Twenty taels, take it or leave it! Unhappy? I know ten ways to vanish before tomorrow's wedding!"

Cornered, Chen Haoyu figured he could sign now to snag Master Jiang's contract and wheedle more silver later. "Fine! Deal! Only you never pry into where I go or what I do, and you never meddle when I take concubines!"

"Could not care less about your sordid hobbies. Kuqiao, bring brush, paper, and red ink paste!"

Setting writing supplies on the side table, Kuqiao watched her mistress draft every clause cleanly and press her thumbprint.

Stepping up to press his own, Chen Haoyu paused. "Wait—can I add one tiny clause?"

"Add my foot! Everything is already inked—why slip in extras?!"

Putting on puppy eyes, he pleaded, "I just handed you every copper to my name—cannot I ask one tiny favor?"

"Spit it out quick."

"If anyone ever bullies me, you have to step in and protect me."

Qianli Xun shut him down flat. "Protect you, my ass! Drop that request before I march out to 'protect' you and end up pounding you myself!"

Giving up, Chen Haoyu stamped his thumbprint.

"Lock this up tight, Kuqiao," Qianli Xun instructed. "The second Chen Haoyu breaks a single line, pull it out."

"Same goes if you break it!" Chen Haoyu shot back. Crisis averted—all that remained was getting through tomorrow, grabbing Master Jiang's copy, and torching it! Calling out "See you tomorrow!" he darted out, scooped up Qinggua, and raced back to Chen Manor to play groom.

With her wedding set for tomorrow, Qianli Xun felt a quiet tug toward the Divine Willow—sitting beneath its boughs and talking things out might clear the heaviness in her chest. "Kuqiao, go tell Madam I agreed to marry into the Chen family and will return to Zhu Manor before dark. I am stepping out for a bit."

"Want me to come with you, Miss?"

"I want some quiet by myself."

"Alright, I will go tell Madam."

Once Kuqiao left, Qianli Xun stepped out of Wangyue Pavilion alone and rode a carriage toward the Divine Willow.
