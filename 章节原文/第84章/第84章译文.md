# Chapter 84: Plotting to Slander Zhu Shuzhen

---

Leaning on Qiao Wenxian's shoulder, Qianlixun felt a sense of security she had never experienced before. The more secure she felt, the sleepier she became, and she gradually fell asleep.

---

Qiao Wenxian didn't dare move. He sat quietly, even his breathing becoming cautious. He was afraid that if he breathed too hard, he would wake Shuzhen.

---

In the carriage, besides the sound of wheels rolling over the road, there was only Qiao Wenxian's cautious breathing.

---

The Qiao family's carriage followed behind. Kuqiao and Nanshuang sat opposite each other.

---

Kuqiao held the clay figurine in her hand, wondering all along: Why would the prince's personal guard give her a clay figurine?

---

"Nanshuang, what's the name of the guard by the Prince of Yi's side?"

---

"Baifeng."

---

"Baifeng." Kuqiao repeated the name.

---

"Why does Sister Kuqiao ask about Baifeng?"

---

"He gave me a clay figurine for no reason. It's confusing."

---

"No wonder the clay figurine in Sister Kuqiao's hand looked familiar. It turns out he gave it to you. I asked him for it several times, but he wouldn't part with it. He said he brought it from Bianliang, and his mother bought it for him when he was little. Hmph! Baifeng is too inconsiderate! When I go back, I'll definitely have a good talk with him about whether he still wants to be my friend!"

---

So the clay figurine was that important. Kuqiao gave it to Nanshuang: "Since it's so important, help me return it to him."

---

Nanshuang took it and tucked it into her bosom.

---

The carriage arrived at the side gate of the Zhu residence. Kuqiao and Nanshuang got off first. After waiting for a while, they didn't see their masters get off. They didn't dare make a sound and continued to wait patiently outside the carriage.

---

Qianlixun hadn't woken up yet. She was sleeping soundly. Qiao Wenxian didn't want to disturb her. More precisely, he was enjoying the feeling of his beloved woman leaning on him as she drifted into sleep.

---

Qianlixun dreamed of an earthquake. She dreamed of the moment Shen Jianchen was taken away by the power of the divine willow, calling her name: "Qianlixun! Qianlixun!" She actually heard the voice clearly in her dream and shouted back: "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!" But Shen Jianchen disappeared before her eyes.

---

Qianlixun woke up calling Shen Jianchen's name.

---

Qiao Wenxian held her in his arms again: "Did you have a nightmare? Don't be afraid. I'm here."

---

Qianlixun nestled in Wenyuan's arms: "With Wenyuan here, of course I'm not afraid."

---

"Who is Shen Jianchen? Why were you calling his name in your dream?" Qiao Wenxian remembered that the Prince of Yi had once used Shen Jianchen as a pseudonym.

---

How could she explain to Wenyuan about transmigrating to this era? Even if she told him, he wouldn't believe it. She was afraid he would be disappointed to learn she wasn't the real Zhu Shuzhen. Having finally found someone she loved, she didn't want to lose him easily.

---

"Oh, he's someone I saw in my dream. I dreamed of an earthquake, and he disappeared in it. I was so scared that I couldn't help but shout his name."

---

Qiao Wenxian thought: one is the prince's pseudonym, the other is a name Shuzhen happened to dream of. Perhaps it was just a coincidence. He didn't think much of it.

---

"I see. You worried me half to death. I thought you had another man in your heart."

---

"How could that be? There's only Wenyuan in my heart."

---

Hearing this, Qiao Wenxian held Shuzhen even tighter, as if afraid someone would snatch her away.

---

Hearing voices from inside the carriage, Kuqiao said: "Young lady, it's too late. Let's go back."

---

The two reluctantly got off the carriage and parted with reluctance.

---

Qiao Wenxian watched Shuzhen walk into the Zhu residence. Only after her figure disappeared from his sight did he board the Qiao family's carriage and leave.

---

That night, while Qianlixun was enjoying her love, Chen Haoyu was plotting a major scheme.

---

He sent people to find the leaders of various petty thugs in Lin'an City and gathered them at a residence in the eastern part of the city owned by the Chen family. The residence was uninhabited but fully equipped with maids and servants. The furnishings and supplies were complete. Occasionally, Chen Haoyu would bring people to stay there for a period.

---

In the main courtyard of the residence, Chen Haoyu sat in a chair with everyone standing before him.

---

One of the underlings said: "Young Master Chen has invited everyone here to give important instructions. Now please let Young Master Chen address us."

---

Chen Haoyu cleared his throat: "I've invited you all here because I want you to help me deal with someone..."

---

One of the leaders said: "Just say it, Young Master Chen. Whether it's killing, arson, or beating someone up, we dare to do it all."

---

Chen Haoyu said: "No need for beating, let alone killing or arson. I just need you all to lead your brothers and spread rumors everywhere. In a month, I want the entire Lin'an City, even the country folk, to know about it."

---

One of the leaders asked: "What kind of rumors? Is it worth all this trouble, Young Master Chen?"

---

Chen Haoyu waved his hand.

---

One of the underlings unfolded the paper in his hand and read: "Zhu Shuzhen is a shrew who forced her husband's family into divorce. She had affairs, exchanging secret glances with strange men during her marriage. After the divorce, she shamelessly slept in a man's arms in public and even wrote 'sleeping in a man's arms fully clothed.' She has lost all feminine virtue. Such a person deserves to be pointed at by a thousand fingers and cursed by ten thousand people!"

---

After the underling finished reading, he put away the paper and asked: "Everyone, have you memorized it?"

---

The thug leaders looked at each other, quite confused.

---

Someone asked: "Young Master Chen, the Chen and Zhu families are in-laws. Slandering the Zhu family's daughter like this, won't it affect the relationship between your two families?"

---

Chen Haoyu said: "I've thought this through carefully before asking you to do this. The rumors are spread by you, so they have nothing to do with me, and even less to do with the Chen family. Once the rumors spread, they'll have nothing to do with you either. Spread them with confidence."

---

One of the leaders praised: "Young Master Chen is truly brilliant!"

---

Another asked with a grin: "For doing this kind of job, how will Young Master Chen reward us?"

---

Chen Haoyu said: "I've always been generous. Don't you know that? After the job is done, there will be heavy rewards, and I'll take you to Chunxiang Brothel for a visit."

---

The thug leaders were all pleased: "Rest assured, Young Master Chen. We'll definitely handle this to your satisfaction. In a month, we'll make sure the Zhu family daughter's scandal is known to everyone."

---

Another leader said with difficulty: "Young Master Chen, the rumor content is too long. We can't memorize it, let alone the brothers under us. Could you read it again? Let us memorize it properly."

---

"I knew you idiots would have trouble. I've prepared everything." Chen Haoyu waved his hand again. Two underlings carried out a wooden box and placed it on the ground. Opening the lid, it was full of written papers. "It's all written here. Just take it. Have your people memorize it properly. If you really can't remember, just take it out and read it when spreading the rumors."

---

The leaders praised: "Young Master Chen has thought of everything."

---

The leaders carried the wooden box, bowed to Chen Haoyu, and left.

---

At this moment, an underling returned from outside and reported: "Young Master Chen, the training officer is at Chunxiang Brothel right now."

---

Chen Haoyu mocked: "After Lin Jue's divorce, no one restrains him. He's been going to Chunxiang Brothel quite often."

---

The underling said: "You're wrong, Young Master. He was quite frequent even before the divorce."

---

"Frequent? How come I didn't notice?"

---

"Young Master likes young virgins and basically never goes to Chunxiang Brothel. But we go there often and have run into the training officer many times. He even often brings Chun Jinqiao back to the Lin residence."

---

"Wow! Is that so? Wouldn't that make Zhu Shuzhen furious?" Chen Haoyu gloated.

---

"Definitely furious. Otherwise, why would they divorce?"

---

Chen Haoyu stood up: "Come with me to Chunxiang Brothel to meet Lin Jue!"

---

Chunxiang Brothel was Lin'an's famous pleasure quarter, where courtesans gathered. At night, business thrived. Some came to listen to songs and watch performances, some sought emotional comfort, and all kinds of people came and went.

---

After the divorce, with no shrewish wife at home, Lin Jue could go wherever he wanted. He truly experienced the benefits of being unmarried.

---

The elders of the clan urged him to remarry quickly, but he was in no hurry. He wanted to win over Xiaguo first.

---

The reason he kept pursuing Xiaguo, besides her slight resemblance to Zhu Shuzhen, was more importantly that he didn't want Zhu Shuzhen to leave him so easily without any loss.

---

Although Chun Jinqiao wasn't a famous courtesan at Chunxiang Brothel, she had some charm. She was dancing on stage with several other courtesans. As her old flame, how could Lin Jue not show up? Of course he would enjoy the feast for his eyes.

---

The men of all ages watching the performance below the stage stared at the beauties on stage with beaming faces, each one's lustful nature fully exposed.

---

Chen Haoyu brought his men into Chunxiang Brothel. An attendant came forward: "Young Master Chen honors us with his presence! This way, please!"

---

The madam of Chunxiang Brothel also came forward: "Oh my! Young Master Chen is a rare guest. Please, sit inside!"

---

Chen Haoyu was led to a seat. The madam sat beside him: "Is Young Master Chen here to watch the show, or is there a girl you're looking for?"

---

Chen Haoyu pointed at Lin Jue: "Neither. I'm here to find him."

---

The madam looked and smiled: "In that case, Young Master Chen is busy. I won't disturb you. If you need anything, just let me know. I'll satisfy everything."

---

The madam of Chunxiang Brothel got up and left to attend to other guests.
