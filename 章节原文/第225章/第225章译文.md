## Chapter 225: Matron Wei's Clever Reassurance

After pounding the door and shouting himself hoarse to zero effect, Chen Haoyu needed a punching bag. Spinning around, he laid eyes on Qinggua standing there blinking innocently, and saw red.

"I sent you to fetch Old Madam Xia and my parents! How the hell did you end up locked in here too?"

"Don't blame me, Young Master! Young Madam's too sharp—caught me in the front courtyard, saw straight through me, and tossed me in here."

"You idiot, couldn't you make up a lie?"

"I did! She didn't buy it, said I should keep you company." Qinggua edged closer. "Young Master, why not just play along from now on? If she says east, don't go west. Fighting her gets you nowhere and drags me down with you—"

Already boiling, Chen Haoyu snapped and threw a fierce kick. "Daring to lecture me now? I'll kick you silly!"

Qinggua glided sideways with effortless ease; Chen Haoyu missed empty air and nearly toppled over. Another kick—missed. Three, four more kicks—Qinggua slipped past every single one.

Furious, Chen Haoyu ditched kicking and locked Qinggua in a basic grappling hold. "Keep dodging! Where'd you pick up that trick?"

"Mercy, Young Master! Mercy! Young Madam taught me Phantom Sidestep!"

"Zhu Shuzhen again!" Releasing him, Chen Haoyu waited half a second before booting Qinggua square in the backside. "Screw her!"

Stumbling forward onto his knees, unhurt, Qinggua sat back on the floor. "Stop taking it out on me, Young Master. Beating me won't get you out of here—better figure out how to escape."

Mad as he was, Chen Haoyu knew none of this was Qinggua's fault. The real trick was getting word to his parents and grandmother; once the elders intervened, Zhu Shuzhen would have to unlock the door.

Dropping into a cushioned chair, he asked, "Which servant in this courtyard are you closest with?"

Qinggua puffed up with pride. "All of them! While you were gone, I drilled in the West Courtyard with them every day—just Phantom Sidestep, mind you, since Young Madam said I lack the bone structure for fist forms. Hanging out daily made us tight. Actually, Young Master... between us, I fell for Hongdou, and I think she likes me back!" His ears turned pink.

Chen Haoyu snorted. "Hongdou falling for a mug like yours? Quit daydreaming! Tell me which of the ten servants you're tightest with so we can get him to sneak out a message."

Wounded by the dig, Qinggua pouted. "You always look down on me! First maid I ever like and you mock me. Look how well Young Madam treats her servants—you ought to take notes from her!"

"Since when does a lackey lecture his master? Talk business or I'll smack you!"

Qinggua pursed his lips. "I get along fine with all ten, problem is they answer to Young Madam. None of them take orders from me."

Chen Haoyu jumped up and grabbed the front of Qinggua's tunic. "All that breath wasted for nothing? Fine—your punishment is to stand at the door and yell, 'Zhu Shuzhen, I'm divorcing you!' Don't stop until I say!" Shoving him against the door, he barked, "Yell!"

Sprawled by the threshold, Qinggua whimpered, "Young Master, she married you, not me! How can I shout that? And a servant calling his mistress by her full name—"

Chen Haoyu lifted a boot over him. "Yell, or I stomp you into meat pie!"

"I'll yell! I'll yell! Hold your temper!" Scrambling up, Qinggua leaned against the wood and muttered toward the crack, "Zhu Shuzhen, I'm divorcing you."

"Louder!"

"Zhu Shuzhen, I'm divorcing you!"

"Louder!"

Squeezing his eyes shut, Qinggua bawled at the top of his lungs, "Zhu Shuzhen! I'm divorcing you!" Over and over he shouted.

Satisfied, Chen Haoyu strolled into the inner bedroom and flopped down for a nap.

Right across the back yard in Jingyi Hall, Matron Wei sat calm and alert, listening to every sound—including Qinggua's howling. Rising to her feet, she murmured, "Time to pay Old Madam Xia a visit."

Over in Old Madam Xia's rooms, her three daughters-in-law sat chatting with her when a maid burst in gasping, "Old Madam! Old Madam! Terrible news!"

"Mind your tongue," Old Madam Xia scolded. "What's got you flustered? Slow down and speak plain."

Catching her breath, the maid reported, "I just saw Second Young Madam march Second Young Master through the gate tied up in ropes! Looks like he's in for rough treatment!"

Both Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan felt their hearts clench in worry.

Thrilled that her son's scheme had worked, Madam Bao feigned shock. "Outrageous! A bride barely through the door tying up her own husband! You have to step in, Mother, or folks will think Chen men let women walk all over them!"

Madam Cui piled on. "Eldest Sister-in-law, you need to put your foot down and teach that Zhu girl how a daughter-in-law behaves! Wild as Haoyu is, disciplining him is up to you and Eldest Brother, not her!"

Old Madam Xia tamped down her own unease. "Quit fanning the flames, you two. I gave Shuzhen authority to discipline Haoyu, so I trust her. However she handles him, neither Qingyan nor Jianglin is to interfere—and neither will I."

Itching to rush over and check, Yao Qingyan bit her lip and held still. "I'll obey you, Mother."

With both women too distracted to chat, Madam Bao and Madam Cui saw the fun was over and took their leave together.

Once outside the gate, Madam Bao sniffed, "Heaven knows what Eldest Brother and Sister-in-law saw in that Zhu girl—poetess or not, foul temper and stained name."

"Drop the act, Second Sister-in-law," Madam Cui retorted. "Haoyu marrying poorly only helps Haodong. Go laugh behind closed doors."

"What talk is that? Even if Haoyu landed a saint, he couldn't hold a candle to my Haodong, who learned trade from his father and uncles since childhood. Not our fault Haoyu only chases pleasure and your Haofei ignores business—leaving all the heavy lifting to my Haodong."

Stung by the swipe at her son, Madam Cui stiffened. "I have chores to attend to. Excuse me."

Madam Bao sneered after her retreating back. "Generations of Chen merchants, yet instead of teaching her son trade, she daydreams of him winning an official post in the capital. Good luck with that!"

With news this juicy, she hurried off to report to her son—barely glancing at Matron Wei walking toward Old Madam Xia's courtyard as they crossed paths.

Inside, Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan sat in taut silence, each wrestling with her heart while the maids stood mute in the heavy air.

At last a maid announced, "Old Madam, Eldest Madam, Matron Wei from Second Young Madam's courtyard requests an audience."

Color rushed back to both women's faces. "Show her in at once!" Old Madam Xia ordered.

Matron Wei stepped in and bowed.

Old Madam Xia waved for a chair. "Since Shuzhen married in, this is your first visit to my quarters."

Reading the anxiety etched across both faces—bold enough to delegate discipline, yet softhearted the moment it began—Matron Wei replied smoothly, "My oversight, Old Madam. I beg your forbearance."

"Understandable," Old Madam Xia said with a forced smile. "Running Shuzhen's entire courtyard keeps you busy enough."

Deciding to set their hearts at ease right away, Matron Wei said, "Thank you for understanding, Old Madam. Now that I have a free moment, I came to pay respects and speak with you about Miss and Young Master."

Both women blurted in unison, "Is Haoyu all right?"

"Look how anxious you both are—worried Miss is abusing him?"

"Not abusing," Yao Qingyan hedged, "I just hate seeing him suffer."

Old Madam Xia sighed. "I spoiled him from the cradle and can't bear seeing him take the smallest hardship. I know it hurt him, yet I can't stop fretting."

"All the more reason you were wise to leave his discipline to Miss," Matron Wei said. "Right now she has him locked in the back courtyard—windows boarded, door padlocked, servants bringing him meals and water..."

Both women gasped together, "How can she do that?"

"Disciplining him is fine, yet isn't locking him up too harsh?" Yao Qingyan fretted. "How can Haoyu stand that?"

Seeing their panic, Matron Wei played her real card. "Earlier today, Madam, you stopped me to persuade Miss to discipline Young Master—which she had zero intention of doing. Why did she suddenly change her mind? Not because I talked her into it, but because she just learned something."

"Learned what?" Old Madam Xia asked.

"That Young Master was the one who spread all those rumors about her last year."

Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan traded a dismayed look. "You can't wrap fire in paper forever," Old Madam Xia sighed. "Just as well she found out—though poor Haoyu is in for rough days now. Please go back and coax Shuzhen: discipline is fine, just beg her not to go too heavy-handed."

"Haoyu acted like a scoundrel ruining her name," Yao Qingyan fretted. "I'm terrified she'll lose control and injure him!"

"Since you entrusted Young Master to Miss, trust her completely," Matron Wei reassured them. "I watched Miss grow up. Her heart is upright and deeply kind—"

"Wenxin told me she turned fierce after losing her memory, nothing like before!" Yao Qingyan cut in.

"Fierce on the surface, yes, yet her core kindness hasn't changed one bit. Haven't you both felt that since she married in?"

Both women nodded.

"Even furious at him for ruining her match with Qiao Manor, Miss would never genuinely hurt him with her fists—just teach him a sharp lesson. Put your minds at rest and pretend you know nothing. Think of it as him off gallivanting again! Heaven knows how many scrapes he got into outside without you knowing, and he still came home bouncing on both feet."

Remembering the bruises he had dragged home and Master Jiang's rigged wager—who knew how many other close calls they had never heard of—both women exhaled.

Yao Qingyan steeled herself. "Matron Wei is right. We'll pretend he's out roaming."

Still uneasy, Old Madam Xia asked, "Wait—from what you said, Shuzhen locked him up out of anger over the rumors, not to reform him. Won't that miss the whole point?"

"Rest easy, Old Madam," Matron Wei smiled. "Whatever her motive, her methods are identical, so the result will be the same. Just wait for Miss to whip a fine grandson into shape for you—and who knows, just as Eldest Madam said, bang two stones together enough and sparks might really fly!"

At that, both women chuckled, their gloom lifting away.

Before leaving, Matron Wei gave one final warning. "While Miss disciplines Young Master, neither of you must step in. If he sends pleas for help, harden your hearts and ignore them. Soften even once, and all Miss's effort goes down the drain."

Bracing themselves internally, both women nodded in agreement.
