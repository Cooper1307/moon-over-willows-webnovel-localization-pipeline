- **"Qianli Xun笑着给他解释"**：对照版中"笑着"未翻译，应译为"smiled and explained"。
- **"The boy can be taught!"**：对"孺子可教"的翻译略显生硬，可优化为"You're a quick learner!"或"You can be taught!"

#### 问题2：重复用词
- **"Qiao Wenxian"重复12次**：部分可用代词"he"替代。
- **"Youqi Jushi"重复15次**：部分可用"she"或"Zhu Shuzhen"替代。
- **"wine cup"重复8次**：可简化为"cup"或"goblet"。

#### 问题3：句式优化
- **长句需拆分**：
  - 原文："The 'Qu Li' says, 'The turtle is for bu divination, the stalks are for shi divination.' This shows that 'bu' is related to divination, so 'the fall of divination coins' should be talking about divination."
  - 建议拆分为更短的句子，适合移动端阅读。

#### 问题4：口语化程度
- 部分对话略显正式：
  - "I advise you not to waste your time on me in the future" → "Don't waste your time on me anymore"
  - "It's a pity you came too late" → "Too bad you're late"
