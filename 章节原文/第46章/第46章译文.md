# Chapter 46: Listening in Silence to Appreciate the Masterpiece

Seeing zero trace of Qianli Xun among the three scholars, Shen Jianchen let his eyes roam the room's decor.

Dominating one wall was a grand circular window carved in openwork like a spreading tree, framing crisp river and mountain views beyond.

Beside the window sat an empty head seat—among equal friends nobody pulled rank, so everyone took side seats along both flanks.

Against the opposite wall stood a long brocade-draped console lined with fine porcelain vases and miniature bonsai beneath hanging calligraphy scrolls lending quiet elegance.

Thinking back to yesterday's run-in with Chen Haoyu—handsome shell over zero brains—decorating with lofty paintings felt comically phony! A sardonic smirk tugged Shen Jianchen's mouth.

While he observed the room, Qiao Mengrong observed him.

First time seeing Brother mingle with friends beyond Wenyuan, his quiet composure captivated her—even that brief curve of his lip, plainly amused rather than warm.

What went on inside his head? Impossible to guess.

One sweet comfort: seated right beside her was Miao Zhiyu, a textbook Jiangnan beauty even Mengrong admired—yet Brother didn't spare her a single glance! Either immune to stray eyes, or carrying Mengrong alone in his heart!

A soft, pleased smile lit Qiao Mengrong's face.

Qiao Wenxian opened the discussion: "Hear we're reading Youqi Jushi's work today—her newest?"

"Brand-new!" Dongfang Chenze confirmed. "Wanted to invite her too, pity she's off staying in the countryside—rumor says cooling off after her divorce fell through."

Qiao Wenxian sighed bitter: "One step too late—could've stood by her!"

Miao Zhiyu echoed with a sigh: "Men take wives and concubines at will, yet once a woman marries wrong, starting over feels impossible! Long as the Training Officer refuses, Youqi Jushi has zero exit."

Cao Ban protested quick: "Known us years, Lady Miao—don't you know we live by 'seeking one devoted heart, never parting till hair turns white'? [TN: Translator's rendering]"

"True—you gentlemen differ from the rest," Miao Zhiyu smiled, glancing sideways toward Qiao Wenxian. "As Youqi Jushi's true confidant, Young Master Qiao will surely uncover rare depths for us today!"

"With your own brilliance and years of sisterhood with Youqi Jushi, Lady Miao, you know her soul far deeper than Wenyuan—eager to hear your reading first!" Qiao Wenxian deflected polite.

An Mingxuan groaned playful: "Quit trading bows, you two! Bring out Youqi Jushi's gem already, Brother Dongfang—we're dying to see it!"

Teasing them, Dongfang Chenze grinned: "Patience—let me tell you how I scored it first!"

"Spill it already!" Cao Ban urged.

"Strolling through Donghe Wa the other day, I ran into a literary gathering admiring a brand-new lyric by Youqi Jushi—brought by Training Officer Lin's own Second Uncle, Lin Zixiao! Afterward I treated Lin Zixiao to drinks; knowing I'm friends with her, he opened up! Turns out on her divorce day Prefect Zhu never showed—only the Zhu clan's century-old Old Ancestor appeared, moved straight out of seclusion by this very lyric! Moved too, Lin Zixiao backed the divorce, so Youqi Jushi gifted him the scroll! Begged him for the original, wouldn't budge—had to borrow ink and copy it word for word!"

Heart aching harder, Qiao Wenxian sighed: "With Prefect Zhu opposing it, breaking free of Lin Jue gets ten times harder! Imagine how crushed and alone she felt when not one elder stepped up—desperate enough to drag out an Old Ancestor who never leaves her shrine!"

Hearing them hash over Zhu Shuzhen again left Shen Jianchen yawning inside—aside from Wenyuan's crush, zero interest! His mind drifted to Bai Feng staking out West Third Alley today—wonder if the courier turned up?

"Show us the lyric already, Young Master Dongfang!" Miao Zhiyu urged.

"Huayu!" Dongfang Chenze called.

Stepping before the main seat, Huayu unrolled a long scroll; too wide for one pair of hands, Nan Shuang hopped over to hold the far end taut.

Glancing up, Shen Jianchen froze—three bold characters jumped straight off the paper: "The Heartbreak Riddle"! Wait—Zhu Shuzhen?! Suddenly a vivid memory from Qianli Fist Manor slammed straight back into him...

Walking into Qianli Fist Manor's courtyard one sunny day, he had spotted Qianli Xun sitting engrossed in a book—looking nothing like the tough girl calling herself "I, the boss" every sentence!

Standing right before her for a full minute without her noticing, he finally asked: "What book's got you glued?"

Only then did she look up: "What do you want? Can't you see I'm busy?"

"Long holiday coming up—want to road-trip to Tibet with me?"

Flipping her book facedown on the patio table, she stood: "Too slow! Already promised my apprentices a beach trip!"

Pivoting on a dime, he beamed: "Awesome! I'll bring my apprentices too!"

Eyeing his goofy grin, Qianli Xun lectured earnest: "Shen Jianchen, I know you're chasing me—save your breath! Don't want romance, don't want marriage, just want to grow Qianli Fist and live my own life! Quit wasting time on me!"

Dodging her rejection smooth, he pointed at the book cover—"Heartbreak Lyrics" (Duanchang Ci): "Whose book are you reading?"

"You hate poetry anyway—why ask?"

Hated poetry, sure—yet Qianli Xun loved it! Picking up the book and flipping pages like a serious scholar to change the subject, he remarked: "Classical verse—Heartbreak Lyrics, and the page you're on is titled 'The Heartbreak Riddle'! Who wrote something this gloomy?"

"Got eyes—read the cover!"

Flipping to the front: "Ah, Zhu Shuzhen—Song female ci poet! Only ever heard of Li Qingzhao, never Zhu Shuzhen."

"Shows how clueless you are."

Thrilled to keep her chatting: "Tell me about Zhu Shuzhen!"

"Pass—hate playing lute for a cow."

"Then explain 'The Heartbreak Riddle' you were just reading!"

Softening a notch, she took the book back, pulled a tissue from the box, folded it neat over the footnotes, and grinned: "Fine—guess the riddles!"

"Thought it was a poem—why riddles?"

"Hides ten number riddles! Read the first two lines and take a guess!"

Holding the book open with one hand pinning the tissue down, she leaned in.

Leaning right beside her—closer than ever before, breathing in her crisp scent—Shen Jianchen barely focused on two words and drew a total blank!

Laughing, she walked him through the first clues until the pattern clicked and he cracked every remaining number fast, earning a rare grin: "Not totally hopeless!"

Even better, in a high mood she actually agreed to merge their beach trips! Didn't win her heart that week—yet that memory stayed etched forever!

Across the table, Qiao Mengrong watched Brother drop his eyes right after reading the scroll, staring into his wine cup with a tender, secret smile...

What memory made him smile like that?!

A cold ache pinched her chest—ignoring Miao Zhiyu, ignoring HER too, locked inside a private world! Was he picturing another woman?!

Recalling the fortune-teller's stall—Brother looking for an "old friend"... WAS THAT FRIEND A WOMAN?! Yet Zhao Pu had never known another girl in his life! Could he really not be Prince Yi Zhao Pu?!

Refusing to follow that dark trail, she snapped back as An Mingxuan's exclamation rang out!

Mirroring Lin Zixiao's reaction, An Mingxuan cried: "Genius! Pure genius—weaving character riddles seamless into a ci!"

Cao Ban jumped in: "Every mind here cracks those riddles in seconds—let's skip riddle-guessing and critique the lyric itself!"

Everyone nodded agreement.

"I'll drain a cup to kick off!" Down went Dongfang Chenze's wine as he began: "Written while fighting for mutual divorce, every stroke radiates iron resolve! 'Heartbreak' anchors the theme—never mentions the word once in the body, yet bleeds heartbreak line after line, above all 'from this day on lean no more on a foe'! How dark must her married days be to brand Lin Jue an enemy? Small wonder—since her wedding, she showed up at every salon bruised! Breaks our hearts watching!"

Miao Zhiyu chimed in: "No need to fret quite as much now—at Yongtai Teahouse last time we saw Youqi Jushi knows serious martial arts! Sent Young Master Chen running for his life! Bet she only took Lin Jue's blows before to build grounds to leave—hard to believe we knew her years without guessing she trained in secret!"

Hearing Zhu Shuzhen knew martial arts piqued Shen Jianchen's admiration—kind of curious to meet Wenyuan's idol someday!

Immersed in her pain till now, Qiao Wenxian blinked stunned at Miao Zhiyu's news: "Since when does she know martial arts?!"

Thrilled he addressed her directly, Miao Zhiyu answered bright:

"Hides her depths well! Ask her yourself once she's back from the countryside! Allow Zhiyu to share humble thoughts next."

Draining her pomegranate wine—light, sweet, zero harsh bite—she spoke wistful:

"Woman to woman, her anguish cuts bone-deep. Heart pledged to one love, barred from him, forced to obey parents' orders and matchmakers' words only to land with the wrong man—bitterness and finality blaze between every character! When even a woman of Youqi Jushi's gifts and backbone ends up trapped like this, what hope lies ahead for someone like me?" Her eyes drifted straight onto Qiao Wenxian.

Catching "heart pledged to one love," Qiao Wenxian worried—still hung up on Young Master Xu? He vowed to help break her free AND wipe Xu clean from her heart!

Cao Ban teased playful: "Joining our salons all these years, Lady Miao—if your eye's fallen on anyone here, speak up fast so we march him straight to Miao Manor with a matchmaker!"

Blushing crimson to her ears, Miao Zhiyu snapped shyly: "Quit talking nonsense, Young Master Cao!"—her eyes sneaking right back to Qiao Wenxian!

Noticing her blush, Cao Ban pivoted smooth: "My cup next—time to unpack allusions!"

Downing his wine, he eyed the scroll: "The Book of Rites states tortoise shells serve for 'bu' divination and milfoil stalks for 'shi'—hence 'golden coins drop for divination'! The Zuo Commentary cites 'Wangsun' as noble scion, echoed in Wang Wei's 'a noble lord may linger'—meaning aristocrats like us, Lin Jue included! And Zheng Xuan's Han commentary on the Book of Songs defines 'zaobai' as distinguishing right from wrong—echoed in 'Why ask right from wrong?' Weaving strict character riddles and classical allusions without losing raw emotion proves Youqi Jushi's literary mastery towers over all of us!"

Every classical citation from Cao Ban drew awe—Shen Jianchen stared impressed: Did this guy swallow an entire library?!

An Mingxuan groaned laughing: "Stared ten minutes spotting zero allusions, figured you'd finally sit quiet—and you still pull three out of thin air! Hats off!"

"Read a few extra shelves with a decent memory—forgive the showing off!" Cao Ban grinned.

"Shut your trap and I'll forgive you—next time keep allusions secret so we don't look dumb!" Laughing, An Mingxuan sipped his wine: "Good vintage—worth two cents from me! Nobody turns to divination until backed against a wall—'golden coins drop' lays bare her utter helplessness! Crucially, do 'he,' 'noble lord,' 'heartless one,' and 'foe' point to one man? Worth debating! To me, 'he' and 'noble lord' mean the lost sweetheart she pines for, whereas 'heartless one' and 'foe' mean the husband she longs to escape—so I disagree with Brother Cao lumping Lin Jue under 'noble lord'!"

Holding back till last, Qiao Wenxian emptied his cup: "My turn next."

All eyes leaned forward—Wenyuan always saw deeper than anyone else.
