# Chapter 46: Listening in Silence to Appreciate the Masterpiece

Shen Jianchen couldn't see anything remarkable from the talented young men, so he began observing the decoration of the private room.

The most conspicuous feature was the large circular window. A tree was carved in the circle in an openwork style. Through the window, one could see the river and mountain scenery outside.

The main seat was closest to the window, but no one sat there. Because they were all friends with equal status, without distinction between high and low, they all chose seats on both sides.

There was a long rectangular table against the wall, covered with brocade, which should feel smooth and soft to the touch. In the middle was a row of porcelain vases, or exquisite potted plants. Calligraphy and paintings hung on the walls, filling the private room with a cultural atmosphere.

Shen Jianchen recalled the situation of meeting Young Master Chen yesterday. Although he looked decent, he was obviously a dandy with no learning, yet he knew how to decorate with calligraphy and paintings, which really made people feel he was hypocritical. Shen Jianchen couldn't help but show a contemptuous smile at the corner of his mouth.

Her brother was observing the surroundings of the private room, while Qiao Mengrong was observing her brother.

It was her first time seeing him get along with friends other than Wenyuan. His silent appearance fascinated her. The corner of his mouth was slightly curved, as if smiling, but it didn't seem to be because he was happy.

What was he thinking about? Qiao Mengrong couldn't guess.

What relieved her was that the Miss Miao next to her was gentle and lovely, a standard beauty of Jiangnan. Even she, a woman, was moved, yet her brother hadn't even glanced at her. This showed that he wasn't the type to be fickle, or perhaps because he had her in his heart, he was blind to other women.

Thinking of this, Qiao Mengrong showed a knowing smile.

Qiao Wenxian asked, "I heard that today we'll be appreciating Youqi Jushi's ci poetry. Is it her latest work?"

Dongfang Chenze said, "Of course. I also wanted to invite Youqi Jushi, but unfortunately she went to the countryside. I heard it's because her mutual divorce failed and she went to the countryside to relax."

Qiao Wenxian sighed, "It's all my fault for coming back a step too late. Otherwise, I could have helped her."

Miao Zhiyu also sighed, "Alas, you men can have three wives and four concubines, but once we women marry the wrong husband, it's extremely difficult to restart our lives. As long as the Military Training Officer doesn't agree, it's simply impossible for Youqi Jushi to escape her marriage."

Cao Ban said, "Miss Miao has known us for quite a long time. Don't you know that we are those who 'wish for a heart that beats as one, never to part till our hair turns white'?"

"You are indeed different from other men." Miao Zhiyu couldn't help but look at Qiao Wenxian. "Young Master Qiao regards Youqi Jushi as a soulmate. Today you must appreciate it and bring out a different charm for us."

Qiao Wenxian replied, "Miss Miao is talented and has known Youqi Jushi for many years. As a fellow woman, you must understand her better than Wenyuan. I will listen carefully to your appreciation."

An Mingxuan said, "You two stop praising each other and being modest. Please, Young Master Dongfang, hurry up and bring out Youqi Jushi's masterpiece so we can take a look."

Dongfang Chenze began to be mysterious, "I can bring it out, but first let me tell you how I got this ci poem."

Cao Ban urged, "Hurry up and tell us. We're all waiting."

Dongfang Chenze said, "One day I went to Donghe Wasi and encountered a gathering of literati. They were appreciating a new ci poem written by Youqi Jushi, which was brought by Lin Jue's second uncle, Lin Zixiao. After it ended, I actively invited Lin Zixiao for a few drinks. He knew I was familiar with Youqi Jushi, so he agreed to the invitation. From him I learned that on the day of Youqi Jushi's mutual divorce, the Prefect didn't come at all. Only an Old Ancestor from the Zhu family came. Youqi Jushi wrote the new ci poem to persuade the Old Ancestor to come. Lin Zixiao was touched by the poem and agreed to the mutual divorce. Youqi Jushi then gave the poem to him. I asked Lin Zixiao to give me the original, but he didn't agree. I could only borrow brush and ink to copy it again."

Qiao Wenxian sighed again, "If the Prefect doesn't agree to the mutual divorce, it will be even more difficult for Youqi Jushi to leave Lin Jue. I can imagine how sad she must have been when no one from the Zhu family was willing to come forward to preside over the mutual divorce. How helpless she must have been to think of bringing out the Old Ancestor who never appears."

Shen Jianchen listened to everyone talking about Zhu Shuzhen without much interest. If it weren't for Wenyuan liking her, he would be even lazier to pay attention. He was thinking about whether Bai Feng would encounter the servant delivering the manuscript at West Third Alley today.

Miao Zhiyu urged, "Young Master Dongfang, please hurry up and bring out Youqi Jushi's ci poem."

Dongfang Chenze called out, "Huayu!"

Huayu walked to the main seat and unrolled the scroll in his hands. It was somewhat long, and one person couldn't hold it alone. Nanshuang came forward to help, and the two held the two sides of the scroll tightly, one on the left and one on the right.

Shen Jianchen looked over, and the three characters "Heartbreak Riddle" came into view. Thinking of Zhu Shuzhen's literary name, he remembered the time he went to Qianli Fist Manor...

As soon as he entered the courtyard of Qianli Fist Manor, Qianli Xun was sitting in the yard, holding a book and reading seriously. Her focused appearance didn't look like someone who called herself "yours truly" at every turn.

Shen Jianchen stood in front of her for a while, but she didn't notice at all. Until Shen Jianchen asked, "What book are you reading? So focused?"

Qianli Xun's eyes finally left the book, "What are you here for? Can't you see yours truly is busy?"

Shen Jianchen said, "The long vacation is about to start. I wanted to invite you to travel to Tibet together."

Qianli Xun put the book down on the table beside her and stood up, "Too bad you're late. I've already made an appointment with my disciples to go see the ocean."

Shen Jianchen immediately changed his mind, "That's great! I'll bring my disciples too!"

Seeing Shen Jianchen's silly happy expression, Qianli Xun advised earnestly, "Shen Jianchen, I know you want to pursue me, but I don't want to date or get married. I just want to carry forward Qianli Fist and do some things I like. Don't waste your time on me anymore."

Shen Jianchen didn't respond to Qianli Xun's words. He glanced at the book on the table, which had the three characters "Heartbreak Verses" written on it, "Whose book are you reading?"

Qianli Xun was too lazy to explain, "You don't like it anyway, so why ask?"

Shen Jianchen really didn't like it, but who made Qianli Xun like it. He went over and picked up the book, pretending to read it seriously, hoping to make Qianli Xun forget what she just said by talking about the book, "So it's a book of ancient poetry. The book is called 'Heartbreak Verses'. The ci poem you're reading is called 'The Heartbreak Riddle'. Who's the author? Why is it so sad?"

"Can't you read it yourself?"

Shen Jianchen turned to the book's cover, "Oh, so it's Zhu Shuzhen, also a female ci poet of the Song Dynasty. I only knew there was a female ci poet named Li Qingzhao in the Song Dynasty. I've never heard of Zhu Shuzhen."

"That just shows how ignorant you are."

Shen Jianchen was happy to have successfully diverted the topic, "Tell me about Zhu Shuzhen?"

"Forget it. I'm too lazy to play the lute to a cow."

"Then tell me about 'The Heartbreak Riddle' you're reading."

Qianli Xun was somewhat interested. She took the book from Shen Jianchen's hands, pulled out a tissue from the paper box on the table, folded it in half, and covered the annotations on the book, "Since that's the case, let me have you guess the riddle."

"Weren't you going to talk about the ci poem? Why are we guessing riddles now?"

"This ci poem contains a riddle of ten numbers. Read the first two lines and try to guess."

Qianli Xun held the book, pressing the tissue with one hand.

Shen Jianchen leaned close. It was the first time he was this close to Qianli Xun. He could even smell the fragrance from her. His heart was on Qianli Xun. He casually read the two lines and couldn't figure it out.

Qianli Xun smiled and explained the riddle hidden in the ci poem to him. Only then did he suddenly realize, quickly grasp the pattern, and smoothly guess the remaining riddles. He finally won praise, "You're a quick learner!"

More importantly, Qianli Xun was so happy that she actually agreed to travel with him and take the disciples out. Although that trip didn't win him the beauty's heart, it was a memory Shen Jianchen could never forget in his lifetime.

Qiao Mengrong noticed that after her brother finished reading Youqi Jushi's ci poem, he lowered his eyes and kept staring at the wine cup on the table, occasionally showing a smile.

What was he thinking of? Why was he smiling?

Qiao Mengrong felt disappointed. Although her brother didn't pay attention to Miss Miao, he also didn't throw any attentive glances at her. He was just immersed in his own world. Was he thinking of some other woman in his heart?

Qiao Mengrong remembered the fortune-telling. Her brother was looking for a friend. Could it be a woman? But her brother never knew any woman other than her. Could it be that what her brother said before was true—that he wasn't Prince Yi Zhao Pu?

Qiao Mengrong didn't dare and didn't want to think further. Her thoughts were broken by An Mingxuan's voice.

An Mingxuan's reaction was exactly the same as Lin Zixiao's when he first saw "The Heartbreak Riddle", "Wonderful! It's really wonderful! Youqi Jushi integrated the word riddle into the ci poem. Only she could think of this."

Cao Ban said, "With the talent of everyone present, we can definitely guess the word riddle of this ci poem. I suggest we don't spend our energy on the word riddle. After all, we're here to appreciate the ci poem, not participate in a riddle-guessing competition."

Everyone agreed.

"Let me drink first to kick things off." Dongfang Chenze picked up his wine cup and drank it all in one gulp. He put down the cup and appreciated it, "When Youqi Jushi wanted a mutual divorce, she wrote this ci poem. Between the lines, it reveals her determination to divorce. The two characters 'heartbreak' are the main theme. Not a single sentence in the poem mentions heartbreak, but every sentence doesn't depart from heartbreak. Especially 'from now on, don't rely on your enemy'. How miserable must her married life have been for her to regard Lin Jue as an enemy? And how could she not treat him as an enemy? After Youqi Jushi got married, every time she came to the poetry gathering, she was injured, which made our hearts ache."

Miao Zhiyu continued, "You don't need to worry about her too much in the future. Last time at Yongtai Teahouse, we learned that Youqi Jushi actually knows martial arts. She beat Young Master Chen into fleeing. I guess it was because she wanted to leave the Military Training Officer that she deliberately let him hit her. But we've known her for many years, and we didn't know she secretly practiced martial arts..."

Hearing that Zhu Shuzhen knew martial arts, Shen Jianchen was impressed. He thought to himself that he would have a chance to meet the person Wenyuan was so fond of.

Qiao Wenxian had been immersed in the ci poem, savoring Zhu Shuzhen's pain. He was in a bad mood. Hearing what Miao Zhiyu said, he was very surprised, "How come I've never heard she knows martial arts?"

Qiao Wenxian took the initiative to talk. How could Miao Zhiyu miss this opportunity?

"So Youqi Jushi is a low-key person, hiding her skills deep. When she comes back from the countryside, Young Master Qiao can ask her in person. Next, Zhiyu will embarrass herself in front of everyone and share my understanding."

Miao Zhiyu also picked up her wine cup and drank the wine. The taste was indeed good, sweet but not greasy, with a light pomegranate fragrance. Aftertasting it had a special flavor.

"As a woman, I can best understand Youqi Jushi's difficulties. She originally had someone in her heart, but couldn't be with the one she loved. She could only submit to her parents' arrangement and the matchmaker's words. It's a pity she married the wrong person. The feelings of resentment and determination are evident between the lines. A talented and individualistic woman like Youqi Jushi is still like this. I wonder how I will fare in the future?" Miao Zhiyu looked at Qiao Wenxian.

Qiao Wenxian pondered the "someone in her heart" that Miao Zhiyu mentioned. Could it be that Shuzhen was still thinking of Young Master Xu? He must help her end this marriage this time he came back, and also make her forget Young Master Xu.

Cao Ban said, "Miss Miao often attends Young Master Dongfang's poetry gatherings and is familiar with us. If you take a fancy to any of us, you must say so early, so we can persuade him to go to the Miao residence to propose."

Miao Zhiyu's face instantly turned red, "Young Master Cao, don't talk nonsense." Her eyes still couldn't help but glance at Qiao Wenxian.

Seeing Miao Zhiyu's embarrassment, Cao Ban quickly changed the topic, "I'll also drink a cup and talk about the use of allusions."

After finishing his wine, Cao Ban looked at the scroll, "The 'Qu Li' says, 'The turtle is for bu divination, the stalks are for shi divination.' This shows 'bu' is related to divination. Therefore, 'the fall of divination coins' refers to divination. The 'Zuo Zhuan, 16th Year of Duke Ai' says, 'If the Wang Sun can pacify the State of Chu and rectify the royal court, then he can be protected.' Wang Youcheng also said 'the Wang Sun can stay'. Therefore, the term 'Wang Sun' in 'resent the Wang Sun' refers to noble young men like you and me. Lin Jue is naturally included. The 'Shi, Da Ya, Sang Rou' has the verse, 'If you can't speak, why think of fear and taboo.' In the Eastern Han Dynasty, Zheng Xuan explained, 'The wise man sees the right and wrong of this matter. It's not that he can't distinguish between black and white, but he can't speak to the king.' 'Black and white' has the same meaning as right and wrong. The 'black and white' in Youqi Jushi's 'no need to ask of black and white' also has this meaning. Integrating the riddle into the ci poem, expressing it with special words, and not departing from the main theme—this shows that Youqi Jushi's literary foundation is completely above ours."

Every time Cao Ban spoke, everyone was amazed. This time was no exception. Shen Jianchen was completely convinced. This guy must have a book in his brain, speaking at will, and they were all book titles he had never heard of.

An Mingxuan said, "I've been staring for a long time and haven't spotted a single allusion. I thought your talent would be useless today, but I didn't expect you to spot some allusions again. Admired! Admired!"

Cao Ban smiled, "Everyone, Cao is not talented, but I've read a bit more books, and with good memory, I spotted a few allusions. I hope you'll forgive me."

"Hehe, as long as you shut your mouth, I'll forgive you. If you spot more allusions, don't say them, or else you'll make us look unlearned." An Mingxuan deliberately criticized with a smile, then picked up his wine cup and took a sip, "The wine is good. For the sake of the good wine, I'll say a few words too. Only when a person reaches a dead end will they go for divination. 'The fall of divination coins' shows how helpless Youqi Jushi was in this marriage. Are the 'person', 'Wang Sun', 'enemy', and 'foe' in the poem referring to the same person? It's worth exploring. I think the 'person' and 'Wang Sun' should be the person Youqi Jushi is thinking of, while the 'enemy' and 'foe' are the people she wants to escape from. So Brother Cao just said 'Wang Sun' also refers to Lin Jue, but I disagree."

After holding back for a long time, Qiao Wenxian finally picked up his wine cup and finished the wine, "Next, let me appreciate it."

Everyone was looking forward to it, because every time Qiao Wenxian would say something different.
