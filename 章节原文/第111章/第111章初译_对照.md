第111章 夏果偶遇陈公子
## Chapter 111: Xiaguo Spots Young Master Chen

苦荞进入厅堂，见小姐手里拿个木盒，站着发呆。
Entering the hall, Kuqiao found her mistress standing in a daze with a wooden box in her hands.

“小姐怎么了？难道被仪王吓住？”
"What's wrong, Miss? Did Prince Yi scare you?"

千里寻对仪王的做法并不认同，也不会接受他的感情。可不得不承认，她内心对仪王生出感激之情。
Qianli Xun neither approved of Prince Yi's ways nor meant to accept his feelings, yet she could not deny a warm spark of gratitude toward him.

乔家放弃提亲，令她多少产生自我怀疑。仪王是第一个来望月轩的人，还是身份尊贵之人，他的到来，说的那些话，还有礼物，让千里寻重拾自信。
The Qiao family calling off the proposal had stirred a sliver of self-doubt. Prince Yi was the first visitor to set foot in Wangyue Pavilion—and a royal prince at that. His visit, his words, and his gift had restored her footing.

“他怎会吓住我？只是想，我可能是第一个收王爷礼物的草民。”
"Why would he scare me? I'm just thinking I might be the first commoner to get a gift from a prince."

“小姐不打开看看吗？听白风说很有趣。”
"Aren't you going to open it, Miss? Bai Feng said it's funny."

千里寻打开木盒，不由露出笑容：“竟然送这玩意儿，哪像一位王爷的做派！”
Qianli Xun lifted the lid and broke into a smile. "He actually gave me this—what kind of prince pulls a stunt like this?!"

“小姐，您笑了，仪王送的礼物肯定有趣，也让我瞧瞧。”
"You smiled, Miss! Whatever Prince Yi gave you must be hilarious—let me see!"

千里寻拿起来，举到苦荞面前：“瞧，就是这玩意儿。”
Qianli Xun lifted the figurine up before Kuqiao. "Look, it's this."

苦荞看到泥人娃娃光着屁股，做出撒尿的动作，还一脸得意与享受，立刻捂住眼睛：“仪王怎么送这东西，羞死人！”
Seeing the bare-bottomed clay boy mid-pee with a smug, blissful grin, Kuqiao slapped her hands over her eyes. "Why would Prince Yi give something like that—how shameless!"

千里寻把泥人放进木盒，盖好盖子：“你想得太多，小孩子多单纯可爱，你要用单纯的思想看他。”
Qianli Xun tucked the figurine back inside and shut the lid. "Your mind's in the gutter! Little kids are pure and adorable—look at it with pure eyes."

“小姐说得有道理，是我不对。”苦荞放下捂住眼睛的手，接过木盒，“我帮您收起来。”
"Miss has a point; my bad." Lowering her hands, Kuqiao took the box. "I'll put it away for you."

千里寻忽然觉得仪王不错，只可惜他付错情。
For half a second Qianli Xun thought Prince Yi wasn't half bad, just barking up the wrong tree.

又转念一想：“不错个头！明明有婚约，还想勾引老娘，今日来此，不过是想乘虚而入，想得美！”
Then she caught herself: "Not half bad, my foot! He's engaged and still trying to seduce me—coming here today just to swoop in while I'm down! Dream on!"

“苦荞，把仪王送的礼物压到箱底，我不想再看到。”
"Kuqiao, bury Prince Yi's gift at the bottom of a trunk. I don't want to see it again."

苦荞纳闷，小姐刚才还挺高兴，怎么突然改变态度？
Kuqiao blinked—one second ago Miss was smiling, why the sudden flip?

“好的，一会儿回屋，我把它压到不常打开的箱子里。”
"Yes, Miss. I'll stash it at the bottom of a trunk we never open."

“记住，不能在白风跟前提起此事。”
"And don't breathe a word of this to Bai Feng."

“小姐放心，我虽跟白风好，也知什么话该说，什么话不该说。”
"Don't worry, Miss. Close as I am to Bai Feng, I know what to say and what to keep shut."

“那便好。”
"Good."

主仆二人走出厅堂，准备回屋，在院子里碰到夏果。
Stepping out of the hall toward her room, mistress and maid ran into Xiaguo in the courtyard.

韦嬷嬷派夏果出去买些日用之物，她刚买回来，放下东西，匆匆往小姐闺房方向走，可巧半路遇上。
Matron Wei had sent Xiaguo out to buy household supplies. Having dropped off her parcels, she was hurrying to her mistress's room when they crossed paths.

“小姐，您猜我在外面碰到谁？”
"Miss, guess who I saw outside!"

“谁？”千里寻很好奇。
"Who?" Qianli Xun asked.

“陈公子！陈浩宇！”
"Young Master Chen! Chen Haoyu!"

本以为此人已从她命运中消失，没想到又被夏果碰到。
Just when she thought he had vanished from her life, Xiaguo had spotted him.

“怎么会在这里碰到？”
"How did you run into him around here?"

“我买完东西往回走，快到望月轩时，见到一伙人，往不远处的一所宅子走去，好像叫永宁居。本来我没当回事，因好奇随意瞧一眼，结果借着永宁居门口挂的大灯笼，看清为首的正是陈浩宇。”
"On my way back, almost at Wangyue Pavilion, I saw a bunch of men heading into a manor nearby—the plaque read Yongning Residence. I didn't think much of it, just glanced out of curiosity, and under the big lanterns by the gate I saw Chen Haoyu leading the pack!"

千里寻边向闺房走，边念叨：“永宁居？难道是陈家的宅子？夏果，你去把韦嬷嬷找来。”
Walking toward her room, Qianli Xun muttered, "Yongning Residence? A Chen family property? Xiaguo, go fetch Matron Wei."

夏果答应而去。
Xiaguo hurried off.

回到闺房，苦荞把木盒压入箱底，开始做茶。
Back in her chamber, Kuqiao stashed the wooden box at the bottom of a trunk and began whisking tea.

千里寻坐在书桌前等待韦嬷嬷，她无心提笔，更无心翻阅桌案上的书籍。讨厌的人又出现在她身边，还离得那么近，令她内心难安。
Sitting at her desk waiting for Matron Wei, Qianli Xun had no heart to pick up a brush or open a book. Having her worst nemesis popping up right next door left her uneasy.

韦嬷嬷与夏果进来，一进屋，说道：“过来的路上，夏果都给我说了，陈公子出现在周围不足为奇，陈家有钱，到处有宅子，估计永宁居就是陈家的。”
Matron Wei walked in with Xiaguo, speaking before even sitting: "Xiaguo told me on the way over. Seeing Young Master Chen around here is no surprise—the Chen family is rich and owns houses all over Lin'an. Yongning Residence is bound to be theirs."

“嬷嬷坐，苦荞，看茶。”
"Have a seat, Matron. Kuqiao, serve tea."

苦荞刚做好一盏，给韦嬷嬷端上，继续给小姐做茶，夏果凑到跟前学习。
Kuqiao had just finished a bowl and brought it to Matron Wei before starting another for her mistress, with Xiaguo hovering close to watch.

韦嬷嬷品完一口茶，放下茶盏：“姑娘要是嫌弃望月轩离陈家宅子近，改日我再到其他地方找找，大不了把这处卖掉，另买一处。”
Taking a sip, Matron Wei set the bowl down. "If you dislike being near a Chen house, Young Lady, I can hunt elsewhere another day—worst case we sell this place and buy another."

刚搬来，还未适应新环境，又谋着离开，千里寻内心更感凄凉。
She had barely moved in; uprooting again so soon felt too dreary.

仔细想来，买卖宅子说起来容易，行动起来也是件麻烦事。嬷嬷管理她名下产业已够辛苦，再为琐事操心，岂不累坏？
Besides, buying and selling houses sounded easy, yet handling it was a huge chore. Matron Wei already worked hard enough managing her properties—no need to pile more hassle on her.

“不必，既然命运安排我住进望月轩，就在这里安定下来，我不想再漂泊。陈浩宇的确讨厌，不过陈家既然房产多，他不至于老住这里，估计也是偶尔过来。再说我何必怕他？应该是他怕我才对。”
"No need. If fate put me in Wangyue Pavilion, I'll put down roots here—I'm tired of drifting. Chen Haoyu is obnoxious, sure, yet with so many Chen properties he won't live here full-time; he probably just drops by now and then. Besides, why should I fear him? He's the one who ought to fear me."

讨厌陈浩宇的心理，悄悄滋长起担忧见到陈浩宇的想法，千里寻不愿继续受这种情绪困扰，她要努力调整。
Disliking Chen Haoyu had almost morphed into dreading him—Qianli Xun refused to let that mindset take hold.

哼！陈浩宇算老几！碰见又如何？要是敢横，大不了再揍他一顿！
Hmph! Who did Chen Haoyu think he was? So what if they crossed paths? If he acted cocky, she'd just beat him up again!

“姑娘能如此想，再好不过。”韦嬷嬷又端起茶盏品起茶来。
"That's the spirit, Young Lady." Matron Wei lifted her tea bowl for another sip.

不一会儿苦荞又做好一盏，给姑娘放在桌上。千里寻又与韦嬷嬷闲聊一会儿，才收拾睡去。
Before long Kuqiao served her mistress a fresh bowl. After chatting with Matron Wei a while longer, Qianli Xun turned in for the night.

近期，临安城最高兴的莫过于陈浩宇，计划成功，大仇得报。
Right now, nobody in Lin'an felt happier than Chen Haoyu—plan executed, sweet revenge achieved.

最近他没事总带几个小弟在街上闲逛，听听谣言传播的情况，一点一点见证谣言如何逐渐波及开来，到今日算是达到预期效果。
Over the past few days he had strolled the streets with his lackeys, listening to the rumors snowball bit by bit until today they hit peak blast.

一高兴，吩咐小弟把各处的混混头目叫来，论功行赏。任务是在永宁居布置的，还在永宁居论功行赏。
Thrilled, he summoned the gang bosses from every quarter back to Yongning Residence—where he had handed out the job—to reward them.

永宁居的厨子做好一桌子酒菜，陈浩宇坐在主位，几个混混头目围坐桌边。
The Yongning Residence cook laid out a lavish feast. Sitting in the host's chair, Chen Haoyu presided over the gang bosses crowding the table.

身边的小弟拿出几袋银子，一一扔到混混头目的怀里。拿到银子，他们个个喜笑颜开。
A lackey pulled out pouches of silver and tossed one into each boss's lap, lighting up greedy grins all around.

“谢陈公子！下次还有这等好事，尽管找我们。”
"Thanks, Young Master Chen! Anytime another sweet gig like this comes up, call us first!"

陈浩宇说：“本公子今日开心，你们吃好喝好，完了，再让人带你们去春香楼。”
Chen Haoyu declared, "I'm in a great mood today—eat and drink your fill, and afterward my boys will take you all over to Chunxiang Brothel!"

“陈公子豪爽！跟着陈公子有吃有喝，还有得玩儿，人生夫复何求！”
"Generous as ever, Young Master Chen! Food, drink, and fun—what more could a man ask from life!"

“来！干了这杯！”陈浩宇举起酒杯与众人干了。
"Come on, bottom's up!" Chen Haoyu raised his cup and drank with the crowd.

一位头目饮完酒，嬉笑道：“陈公子这招真够损的……”
Draining his cup, one boss grinned: "Young Master Chen's scheme was nasty as—"

小弟提醒道：“怎么说话呢？”
A lackey barked, "Watch your mouth!"

那位头目立刻改变语气：“真够厉害的，搞得朱家姑娘被全城人骂得面目全非，以后想嫁人都难！”
The boss pivoted on a dime: "—brilliant as hell! Got the whole city tearing Miss Zhu's name to shreds—good luck finding a husband now!"

又一位说：“我听说乔家本来今日上门提亲，结果被陈公子搅黄，嘿嘿，朱家姑娘还被蒙在鼓里，关键是要想找到幕后主使，如同大海捞针。”
Another chimed in: "Heard the Qiao family was set to propose today and you blew it sky-high! Heheh, Miss Zhu's still in the dark, and trying to trace the mastermind? Like fishing a needle out of the sea!"

陈浩宇笑道：“呵呵……！本公子总算出了口恶气，全城人都替本公子骂她，看她以后还敢在本公子面前横！”
Chen Haoyu crowed, "Haha! Got that venom off my chest at last! The whole city's cursing her out for me—let's see her act cocky in front of me again!"

旁边的小弟说：“不止，也传到乡下，乡下百姓也骂着呢！”
A lackey beside him added, "Not just the city—it's reached the countryside too! Peasants out there are cursing her too!"

一桌人更是高兴，喝得更欢，吃得更起劲。
Cheers rose louder around the table as everyone drank harder and dug in deeper.

正高兴着，一个小弟从外面匆匆回来，边往里走，边喊：“陈公子！不好了！不好了！”
Right in the middle of the celebration, a lackey burst in shouting, "Young Master Chen! Disaster! Disaster!"

陈浩宇见状，骂道：“怎么说话呢？本公子打败朱淑真，正好着呢？哪里来的不好？”
Chen Haoyu barked, "Watch your mouth! I just crushed Zhu Shuzhen—life's grand! What disaster?!"

小弟说：“回陈公子，我在春香楼碰到林觉，偶然听到他给春金巧说知府大人让他彻查传播谣言之人。”
The lackey panted, "I bumped into Lin Jue at Chunxiang Brothel and overheard him telling Chun Jinqiao that His Lordship the Prefect ordered him to hunt down whoever started the rumors!"

几个头目开始紧张，有人说：“陈公子，该怎么办？会不会被查出来？”
Tension snapped across the gang bosses' faces. One stammered, "What do we do, Young Master Chen? Will they trace it to us?"

“怕什么？根本查不到！”陈浩宇放下酒杯，指着所有人，“你们给我听好，自己先不要心虚，装作什么都不知，回去把给你们的写好的纸张都烧了，一张都不许留。”
"What's there to panic about? They won't find a thing!" Setting down his cup, Chen Haoyu pointed around the table. "Listen up: don't act guilty, play dumb, and go home and burn every single rumor sheet I gave you—don't leave a scrap!"

有头目说：“那些纸扔的扔，丢的丢，都没了，有的还被弟兄们上茅房给用了。”
One boss piped up, "Those sheets got tossed or lost already—some of the boys even used them in the latrine!"

“既然没了，不必去管，该怎样就怎样，就当啥事没有，今儿犒赏完你们，以后我不叫别来找我。”
"Gone? Even better! Carry on like normal, act like nothing happened. After tonight's reward, don't come looking for me unless I call."

陈浩宇又与大家吃喝起来，直到凌晨，才各自散去。
Chen Haoyu kept drinking and feasting with them until the small hours before they broke up.

其他人去了春香楼，陈浩宇则在永宁居留宿，他打算明日去朱府看望姐姐，顺便打探消息。
The rest headed for Chunxiang Brothel while Chen Haoyu stayed the night at Yongning Residence, planning to visit his sister at Zhu Manor tomorrow to fish for news.
