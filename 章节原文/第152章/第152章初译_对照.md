第152章 冬晴无雪配拳法
## Chapter 152: Chapter 152: Clear Winter Sky Paired with Boxing

南双担心公子喝醉，没有找来真正的酒，端上两瓶桂花酿。乔文仙开始一杯一杯地饮着。
千里寻写完朱淑真的《念奴娇·冬晴无雪》，搁下笔，无意中接触到文渊投来的目光。他一边饮桂花酿，一边看向她，目光中充满埋怨、期待和不舍。
千里寻不知如何回应，躲闪开来：“诸位，淑真在此献丑，今日是入冬以来难得的晴朗之日，便写下‘冬晴无雪’一词，不敢奢谈品鉴，只要能帮大家解解闷儿，我便心满意足。”
申简辰在心里笑道：“多此一举！你又何必替幽栖居士谦虚？人家可是名留后世的女词人。”
东方尘泽说：“幽栖居士太过自谦，我等今日来，除了仪王与郡主盛情邀请不胜荣幸外，就是奔着你的诗词而来。幽栖居士的诗词哪能只有解闷儿的功效？还是我等必需的精神食粮。”
两位公子无不认同。
苗知瑜虽失落，也点头认可。
乔文仙顾不上其他，一味盯着淑真，她有意避开他的目光，令他心酸。
乔梦容很是期待淑真到底写些什么。
陈浩宇则一副不屑的样子，一只母老虎舞文弄墨，被大家赞赏，实在可笑。
申简辰命令道：“来人，将幽栖居士的词展示给众人。”
两名丫鬟取了纸张，站在中间，向众人展示。
安明轩主动将词为大家念一遍，他念的过程中，申简辰越听越觉得熟悉，再次回忆起穿越前的经历。
那是一年一度的拳法交流活动，千里寻表演千里拳时，新编一套并非用于格斗的拳法，将拳法与古诗词相融合，她每表演一招，便吟诵一句，再配上古筝演奏，真是美轮美奂。
拳法的阳刚之美，加上诗词和古筝的阴柔与典雅，将众人深深吸引，最终千里寻以独特的表演取胜，获得评委和观众一致认可。
当时申简辰在台下看得呆住，除千里寻的美貌外，主要是她的一招一式仿佛为词句而创。申简辰在心中惊叹她是如何做到？
他仔细品味词句，觉得无非是为表达对冬雪的向往，与拳法毫无关系，可千里寻却能在二者之间找到联系，申简辰佩服不已。
表演结束后，他在台下堵住千里寻，拿出让徒弟刚买好的奶茶：“累了吧？我给你买的奶茶。”
“老娘最讨厌喝奶茶。”千里寻没有理会，继续往出走。
申简辰跟上：“你不喝我喝，不过你得给我解释，刚才念的那首词下阕是什么意思，我没听明白。”
千里寻站住，盯着正在吸奶茶的申简辰，觉得十分可笑：“你喝奶茶的动作与你的气质实在不搭。”
“别管我的气质，你刚才表演时气质实在绝，赶紧给我解释下阕的意思。”申简辰继续吸着奶茶。
千里寻不满道：“老娘最讨厌别人问古诗词是什么意思，这个问题一听就不懂，诗词重在一个‘品’字，只要一个字一个字，一句一句慢慢品，便能品出其中滋味。”
千里寻说完，又要走，又被申简辰堵住：“你我都表演完，不远处有家咖啡馆儿，去那里坐会儿，你教我如何逐字逐句品诗词。”
“现在是下午，老娘从来不在这个时间段喝咖啡，晚上睡觉容易失眠。”千里寻说完扬长而去，留下申简辰一人怅然若失。
此时回忆起来，依然怀念当日的时光，千里寻表演过的拳法申简辰还依稀记得，不如趁此机会表演一番，也好向她表明真实身份。
安明轩念完后，申简辰说道：“幽栖居士的词，使我兴致大增，想为各位表演一番。”
在座诸位不知仪王想干什么，还是装出兴致满满的样子。
曹班说：“我等能欣赏到仪王的表演，真是三生有幸。”
其他人都无不认同。
“将琴抬上来！”
听到仪王的命令，已有下人抬上一张古琴，挑合适的位置放好。
“梦容，可否为哥哥演奏？”
“当然，不知哥哥想听哪首？”
“你常弹的那首。”
“好，我就为哥哥演奏《佩兰》曲。”
申简辰本不想这么快暴露身份，朱家与陈家的亲事，逼得他不得不暴露。
以前不暴露是担心千里寻拒绝他，果然他的真情并未打动她。
现在想暴露，是因他觉得一旦千里寻知道他是申简辰，肯定高兴，毕竟两人有穿越前的友情，还有共同穿越的经历，也算患难与共，千里寻定会对他另眼相待，说不定因为特殊的关系愿意接受他的感情。那么，他就有正当理由要求陈家退婚
乔梦容已走到琴旁坐好，开始演奏。
Worried his master might get drunk, Nan Shuang skipped strong liquor and brought two flagons of sweet osmanthus wine instead. Cup after cup, Qiao Wenxian drank on in silence.

琴音一起，申简辰起身来到大家面前，瞧着纸张上的词句，跟着琴曲的节奏，边吟诵，边使出拳法……
Setting down her brush after finishing Zhu Shuzhen's "Niannujiao: Clear Winter Sky, No Snow," Qianli Xun happened to meet Wenyuan's gaze. Sipping osmanthus wine, his eyes burned with quiet reproach, yearning, and refusal to let go. At a loss how to respond, she looked away fast. "Shuzhen embarrasses herself before you all. Today brings a rare clear winter sky, so I wrote 'Clear Winter Sky, No Snow.' Hardly worthy of high critique; if it helps pass an idle hour, I'm content."

大家都被仪王的表演吸引，心中赞叹他竟能想出这种品鉴诗词的方法，不用说一句话，用拳法表达对词句的理解，实在妙哉！
Shen Jianchen chuckled to himself. Why act modest on Youqi Jushi's behalf? She's a poet remembered for centuries!

当仪王刚开始表演时，千里寻想起穿越前她曾将拳法与诗词结合的做法，没想到这个时代也有人与她的想法一致。
"Far too humble, Youqi Jushi!" Dongfang Chenze protested. "Honored as we are by Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe's invitation, your poetry is what drew us here! Far more than passing an hour, your verse feeds our spirits!"

仪王刚打出第一招时，千里寻只觉十分熟悉，不就是她的拳法招式？继续往下看，越来越确定。
Cao Ban and An Mingxuan echoed warm agreement; downcast as she felt, Miao Zhiyu nodded too. Qiao Wenxian ignored every voice, eyes fixed on Shuzhen, wincing inside every time she avoided his stare. Qiao Mengrong leaned forward eager to read every character, while Chen Haoyu sneered in silence at a tigress swinging a brush and winning applause.

不同的是，仪王打出的招数虽有千里拳的痕迹，用的内力却是她熟悉的申拳。
"Hold up Youqi Jushi's lyric for everyone to see," Shen Jianchen directed. Two maids lifted the sheet center stage while An Mingxuan volunteered to recite it aloud.

“难道仪王就是申简辰？”
As An Mingxuan read line after line, a jolt of recognition struck Shen Jianchen, carrying him straight back before the earthquake. At an annual martial-arts showcase, Qianli Xun had unveiled a brand-new performance form blending Qianli Fist with classical poetry: reciting one line for every move to the strains of a live guzheng. Martial vigor fused with lyrical poise held the whole arena spellbound, sweeping both judges and audience off their feet. Seated below, Shen Jianchen had stared transfixed—not just by her beauty, by how every turn of her wrist seemed born straight from the words. Pondering the lyric back then, it looked like a simple wish for winter snow with zero link to boxing, yet she had bridged the two with pure magic.

千里寻在心中自问，不由激动万分，真想上前揪住他问个究竟。可身边有其他人在，尤其是梦容，冒失追问，既不合礼数，也会惹得梦容伤心。
Right after her set, he had cornered her backstage holding milk tea his apprentice had just bought. "Tired? Got you milk tea."

千里寻忍住，尽量让自己平静下来，避免一时冲动做下错事。
"I hate milk tea," Qianli Xun snapped, brushing past.

她继续分析，仪王懂得申拳的功力并不能以此判定他就是申简辰，毕竟她曾在街上打败的延迟男也懂申拳，看来申拳在这个时代依然有踪迹可循。
Falling into step beside her, he said, "Fine, I'll drink it. Just explain what the second half of that poem meant; it flew right over my head."

关键是仪王竟然能打出她的招数，那可是她费尽心思根据朱淑真的《念奴娇·冬晴无雪》创造的拳法，不为格斗，只为表演的同时展示千里拳的内功。以此断定，仪王八成就是申简辰。
Stopping to eye him slurping through the straw, she smirked. "Drinking milk tea clashes hard with your whole image."

想到此处，千里寻有些坐不住，想尽快去神柳下与仪王相约。
"Forget my image. Your aura onstage blew everyone away! Explain the second stanza!"

申简辰表演拳法的同时，会偶尔向千里寻瞟来，发现她露出惊喜而又疑惑的表情，便知目的已达成。
"Nothing annoys me more than people asking what a poem 'means'!" Qianli Xun scoffed. "Proves you don't get poetry at all! You don't dissect verse; you savor it word by word, line by line, till the taste sinks in!"

“这下你应该比我更渴望到神柳下相会。”申简辰想着，口中继续吟出：“应念陇首寒梅，花开无伴，对景真愁绝。”每念一句，都以相应的拳法配合……
She started walking off again, and he stepped ahead blocking her. "We're both done onstage, and there's a coffee shop right down the block. Sit with me and teach me how to savor poetry word by word."

千里寻忽然之间想明白很多事，仪王起死回生，是因为申简辰穿越而来所致，他失忆也与此有关，还有他看自己时充满爱意的眼神，以及表达爱意的话语，都是因为他只有申简辰的记忆。
"It's afternoon. I never drink coffee in the afternoon unless I want insomnia all night!" Striding off without looking back, she had left him standing hollow-hearted.

仪王表演完最后一个动作，梦容也停下琴音，二人配合默契。乔梦容觉得她与哥哥天衣无缝的配合，足以说明他们真的是天生的一对儿。
Reliving that afternoon warmed his chest, and he still remembered most of her performance form. What better way to reveal who he really was right here and now?

乔梦容坐回位置，发现淑真一脸怔住的表情，悄悄问：“你被哥哥的表演吸引住了？”
The moment An Mingxuan finished reading, Shen Jianchen spoke up. "Youqi Jushi's lyric fires my spirit! Let me perform something for everyone."

千里寻哄骗道：“我是被你的琴音折服。”
Puzzled over what Prince Yi had in mind, everyone put on eager smiles. "Witnessing a performance by Prince Yi is a lifetime honor!" Cao Ban cheered, echoed around the hall.

梦容笑了笑，继续坐好。
"Bring in the guqin!" At his command, servants carried in a guqin and placed it at a prime spot. Turning to his sister, Shen Jianchen asked, "Play for Brother, Mengrong?"

东方尘泽见仪王已落座，赞道：“仪王的表演令我等钦佩，没想到幽栖居士的词也能与拳法相合，实在妙！尤其‘不放玉花飞堕地，留在广寒宫阙。’仪王的拳法似乎让人感受到‘玉花飞堕’的场景，弥补了‘留在广寒宫阙’带给人的失落和惆怅。”
"Of course! Which piece, Brother?"

东方尘泽赞美仪王时，千里寻朝仪王看了一眼，瞧不出任何申简辰的痕迹，就像自己，换副皮囊，是千里寻，又不是千里寻。
"Your favorite."

申简辰觉出千里寻的目光，他心中暗喜，早知如此，应早些告诉她，也许早已美人在怀。
"Then 'Orchid Pendant' it is!"

安明轩接着说：“仪王的拳法，既是对幽栖居士词作的解析，同时也弥补冬晴无雪带给人的遗憾，真是此时无声胜有声。鄙人接着仪王的拳法，用我这张嘴品鉴一番。”
Shen Jianchen had not meant to expose his identity so soon, yet the Zhu-Chen betrothal forced his hand. Before, he held back fearing rejection, and sure enough, his feelings as Zhao Pu had not won her over. Now he gambled that learning he was Shen Jianchen would thrill her: old ties from their own era, crossing time together through life and death—surely she would look at him with fresh eyes and give him a chance! Once she accepted him, he would have every right to make the Chens cancel the match.

安明轩又朝纸张瞧去，思索一会儿：“幽栖居士的词倒应景，冬日下过一场雪，说句心里话，我还没玩够，连西湖的断桥残雪还未来得及欣赏，真希望再来一场雪，可日日不是阴天，就是冬日高挂，尤其今日，是入冬以来少的晴朗之日。幽栖居士的词算是满足我对冬日雪景的美好想象。鄙人认为最妙处在于用‘玉花’和‘玉盐’描绘想象中的冬雪，既写出冬雪像玉一样晶莹剔透，还写出像花一样美的形态，也写出像盐一样色泽洁白，搞得我越来越希望再下一场雪。”
Taking her seat at the guqin, Qiao Mengrong plucked the opening notes. Rising into the center facing the hanging sheet, Shen Jianchen matched his breath to the melody, reciting verse while flowing into boxing forms. Every guest sat riveted, marveling at such an inventive way to interpret poetry: saying not a word of critique, letting crisp martial motion embody the soul of every phrase.

苗知瑜虽未怎么扭头看身旁的乔公子，可心无时无刻不在他的身上。乔公子不像往日参加别的诗会时滔滔不绝，他自顾沉默，苗知瑜明白，他心里还念着幽栖居士。
From the very first note, Qianli Xun recalled combining boxing with poetry back in her old life, amused that someone in the Song dynasty shared the same idea. Then Prince Yi threw his opening stance, and her heart skipped a beat. That was her choreography! Move after move confirmed it beyond a shadow of a doubt: every form traced Qianli Fist's exact showcase routine, powered underneath by the unmistakable force of Shen Fist.

失落之余，不时瞧向幽栖居士，她倒安然自若，自来到诗会，并未与乔公子眉来眼去。苗知瑜心安许多，见安明轩讲完，立刻说：
Was Prince Yi none other than Shen Jianchen?!

“我最喜‘应念陇首寒梅，花开无伴，对景真愁绝。’寒冬之际，寒梅与冬雪乃绝配，可冬日，寒梅独开无雪，寂寞无伴，也令赏景之人无比惆怅……”
Shock and joy surged so hard she almost bolted forward to grab his sleeve and demand the truth, catching herself only at sight of the crowded room, above all Mengrong. Making a scene here would violate etiquette and break Mengrong's heart. Fighting her pulse steady, she forced herself to think cool. Knowing Shen Fist alone proved nothing, since that "Delayed" guy she had beaten on the street used Shen Fist too, meaning Shen Fist had roots in this era. What sealed it was that choreography: a non-combat showcase she had invented from scratch to pair with Zhu Shuzhen's "Clear Winter Sky, No Snow"! Eight or nine parts out of ten, Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen! Restless now, she could hardly wait to meet him beneath the Divine Willow.

苗知瑜体会了一会儿自己内心的感情：“孤独的寒梅，如同此时此刻的我，花开无伴。……”
Stealing glances her way between turns, Shen Jianchen caught the flash of stunned joy in her eyes and smiled inside. Bet you're racing to the Divine Willow faster than me now! Chanting aloud—"Pity the winter plum upon the ridge, blooming alone without a mate, grieving deep before this view"—he matched every phrase with crisp, flowing strikes.

她转向乔文仙：“乔公子，你说呢？”
Every mystery snapped into place for Qianli Xun: Prince Yi's revival, his amnesia, the tender heat in his eyes, even his bold confessions. All because Shen Jianchen sat inside Zhao Pu's body carrying only Shen Jianchen's memories!

乔文仙根本没有心思鉴赏诗词，他除研究淑真的表情，就是喝闷酒，还好是桂花酿，对他来讲，根本喝不醉。
On the final note, Prince Yi closed his stance in exact sync with Mengrong's last chord. Returning to her seat glowing with pride—such harmony proved Brother and she were a match made in Heaven—Qiao Mengrong noticed Shuzhen staring dazed and whispered, "Spellbound by Brother's performance?"

苗知瑜的问话，乔文仙没听进耳朵里，并未做出任何反应。
Recovering fast, Qianli Xun teased back, "Spellbound by your guqin playing!" Mengrong smiled content and settled back.

陈浩宇瞧见，心里冷笑道：“哼！落花有意，流水无情，以后嫁给心上人，也没好日子过。”
Once Prince Yi sat down, Dongfang Chenze broke into praise. "Inspired beyond words, Your Highness! Who knew Youqi Jushi's lyric paired so well with boxing, above all 'It will not let jade blossoms flutter down to earth, holding them back inside the Palace of Vast Cold'! Your sweeping strokes made 'jade blossoms' seem to snow right before our eyes, soothing the ache of snow locked up in heaven!"

鉴赏诗词环节，陈浩宇甚觉无趣，想要离开，见仪王兴致勃勃，还亲自上场表演一番，他不敢提出离开的请求，耐心坐好，忍受大家说些无聊的话。
While Dongfang spoke, Qianli Xun stole a long look at Prince Yi. Not a physical trace of Shen Jianchen's old face remained, just as she wore another skin herself: Qianli Xun, yet not Qianli Xun. Catching her eyes on him, Shen Jianchen beamed inside: Should have told her ages ago!

东方尘泽见气氛尴尬，赶紧说：“这首词还有一处甚妙，上阕让人产生失落的情绪，下阕则给人希望，告诉我们终有一日和羹金鼎手，把玉盐飘撒。因此，苗姑娘不必伤感，你这枝寒梅，终会等到被‘玉盐’装饰之日。”
An Mingxuan picked up next. "Prince Yi's boxing both interprets the poem and fills the gap left by a snowless winter—silence speaking louder than words! Allow me to follow with plain speech." Studying the sheet a beat, he went on, "Spot-on for the season! One winter snowfall wasn't near enough; I haven't even seen Lingering Snow on Broken Bridge at West Lake yet, and day after day brings gray clouds or bright winter sun, above all today's clear sky. Youqi Jushi's lyric grants my dream of winter snow! Purest touch lies in calling snow 'jade blossoms' and 'jade salt'—clear as jade, delicate as flowers, pure white as salt—making me pray even harder for snow!"

苗知瑜转过头，回礼道：“感谢东方公子安慰，希望正如你所言。”
Without turning much toward Young Master Qiao beside her, Miao Zhiyu's whole heart tracked his every breath. Usually lively and eloquent at salons, today he sat locked in mute gloom, still hung up on Youqi Jushi. Yet glancing across at Youqi Jushi, seeing her poised and distant without trading one flirtatious look with Young Master Qiao eased Miao Zhiyu's nerves. As An Mingxuan finished, she spoke up. "My favorite line is 'Pity the winter plum upon the ridge, blooming alone without a mate, grieving deep before this view.' Winter plum and snow belong together; blooming alone without snow leaves both flower and viewer aching..." Lingering on her own quiet sting, she added, "A lonely winter plum, just like me right now, blooming without a mate... wouldn't you agree, Young Master Qiao?"

大家鉴赏幽栖居士的词，千里寻不便多说，只端坐着细细听大家的品鉴。
Having zero mind for poetry—either watching Shuzhen's face or pouring himself cups of harmless sweet osmanthus wine—Qiao Wenxian did not even register her question and made no reply.

苗知瑜的话使得她更加谨慎，低眉垂目，不敢有一丝松懈，怕稍加松懈，会不由自主看向文渊。
Catching the snub, Chen Haoyu scoffed inside: Hmph, falling petals pine for flowing water that couldn't care less; marrying the man you chase won't bring happy days! Bored stiff by poetry talk, itching to bolt yet scared to offend an enthusiastic Prince Yi, Chen Haoyu stayed glued to his chair enduring the chatter.

曹班忍了半晌，终于发话：“诸位，我说两句……”大家知道，只要曹班一开口必然谈及典故。
Stepping in to rescue the awkward hush, Dongfang Chenze smiled. "Yet the genius of this lyric turns in the second half! Where the opening brings letdown, the close brings hope: someday the hand stirring the golden cauldron will scatter down jade salt! Take heart, Lady Miao; your winter plum will be sure to greet its dusting of jade salt!"

“幽栖居士这首词，我看出一处用典，‘梁园燕客，夜明不怕灯灭。’梁园始建于西汉，建成一时轰动天下，成为人们向往的胜地，冬日时梁园被白雪覆盖，万树着银，翠玉相映，景色更加迷人，故有‘梁园雪霁’之称。幽栖居士引用此典故，无非是想描绘出想象中雪景之美，给人以美好的希望。”
Turning with a gracious nod, Miao Zhiyu thanked him. "Kind words, Young Master Dongfang. May it come true as you say."

申简辰时不时观察千里寻，又不敢一直观察，担心被大家看出他的心思。他发觉千里寻从刚才猜出他是申简辰时的激动已变得平静，应该是因众人在此，不方便表露情绪。
Holding her tongue while everyone critiqued her work, Qianli Xun kept her eyes lowered with care after Miao Zhiyu's pointed words, refusing to let her gaze stray once toward Wenyuan.

他能想象到，若众人不在，她一定会焦急地追问：“快告诉我，你是不是申简辰？”
Having waited his turn, Cao Ban cleared his throat, signaling another classical citation. "I spotted one rich allusion here: 'Feasting guests in Liang Garden fear not the lamps going out in the bright night.' Built in the Western Han, Liang Garden dazzled the empire; blanketed in winter snow, silver boughs gleaming beside jade pines, it earned the famed name 'Snow Clearing at Liang Garden.' Invoking it paints imagined snow in bright splendor, leaving readers full of hope."

想到此处，申简辰心中愉悦，结果发现梦容正看向他，掩饰道：“梦容，你也鉴赏鉴赏。”
Stealing quick glances at Qianli Xun without giving himself away, Shen Jianchen saw her initial jolt settle back into poised calm—only holding back because of the crowd. Alone, he knew she would already be firing questions: Tell me straight, are you Shen Jianchen? Amused by the thought, a faint smile curved his lips right as he caught Mengrong watching him. Covering smooth, he prompted, "Share your thoughts too, Mengrong."

乔梦容正在研究哥哥的表情，他嘴角露出笑容，显然心情不错，哥哥开心她也开心，既然哥哥让她鉴赏，她不好继续沉默。
Watching Brother's happy grin warmed Mengrong's heart; invited to speak, she did not stay silent.
