# Chapter 33: Choosing a Disciple and Returning to the Manor—Qiao Wenxian

Years of Shen Fist had honed both his hands and his footwork. Shen Jianchen tore through the crowded street at blazing speed, closing fast on the pickpocket. Ducking into a quiet lane and then a dead-end alley, the boy ran himself ragged; glancing back to see his pursuer cruising without even breaking a sweat, he gave up and doubled over gasping, "Stop... stop chasing... I quit... here, take the pouch!"

Halting calm without a heavy breath, Shen Jianchen took the money pouch and sized up the lad: fifteen or sixteen, tattered garments, grimy face, yet crisp, handsome features underneath. "Pickpocket, huh?"

"Who's a thief?! Don't slander me!"

"Not a thief? Then why steal his money pouch?"

"Didn't steal—I borrowed!"

Cheeky kid! Shen Jianchen smirked. "Borrowed? Tell that to the man screaming 'thief' down the whole street! If snatching and running isn't stealing, what is?"

Plopping straight onto the dirt unfazed, the boy shot back, "Believe me or not. I'll sit right here till he catches up and you can ask him yourself!"

With the owner not here yet, and zero chance the lad could slip past him, Shen Jianchen asked, "What's your name?"

"Why should I tell you?"

"Because I outran you."

"Hmph, fast legs, I'll give you that. Fair enough: Bai Feng."

"Bai Feng—good name. Stand up and drop into a horse stance so I can see."

"Weird guy! Why should I squat in a horse stance for you? No way!"

Reaching down, Shen Jianchen yanked Bai Feng straight up, pressed one palm down on his shoulder, tapped both legs apart with his foot into a textbook horse stance, and struck an acupoint freezing him in place. Stepping back to inspect his build, delight lit his eyes: Top-grade martial bones! Worth every step of today's stroll—found myself a prime apprentice!

Fuming, Bai Feng yelled, "What's wrong with you?! I already handed over the pouch! Why freeze me like a statue?! Let me go!"

"Punishment for swiping someone's pouch. When the owner gets here, if he says let you go, I let you go."

Right then the middle-aged man stumbled into the alley, wheezing hard. "Thank... thank you, young master... for catching him..."

Handing over the pouch, Shen Jianchen watched the man collapse onto the ground catching his breath before scolding, "Bai Feng, you rascal! Want to borrow coin, ask proper! Why grab my whole pouch and run? If not for this gentleman, you'd have wiped me out!"

"You two know each other?" Shen Jianchen asked.

"Hear that?!" Bai Feng crowed. "Not stealing—taking! Better yet, borrowing!"

Shen Jianchen snapped sharp, "Taking or borrowing without permission IS stealing!" Silenced, Bai Feng glared in stubborn defiance.

Catching his breath and climbing up, the man explained, "I know this boy, young master. He asked to borrow coin; when I opened my pouch to count some out, he snatched the whole bag and bolted."

Bai Feng shot back bitter, "Hmph! Five hundred wen wasn't near enough! My mother needs five taels of silver to save her life, and you only offered five hundred copper coins! Sure I grabbed your bag, and I swore I'd earn it back and repay you! Stingy miser!"

"My coin doesn't blow in on the wind either!" the man argued. "I sweat for every copper to feed my whole family. Lend you all of it and what do my wife and kids eat?!"

"My mother's dying at Grand Physician Zhao's Clinic right now!" Tears burst down Bai Feng's grimy cheeks. "Sob...! She's gravely ill! The doctor wants five taels to treat her, or she'll die! Sob...!"

Softening with pity, the man fished one tael from his pouch and held it out. "This is all I can spare. Hope you understand."

Frozen by the acupoint, Bai Feng watched Shen Jianchen catch the silver and press it right back into the man's hand. "Keep your silver. I'll pay for Bai Feng's mother's treatment."

Relieved, the man beamed as he stowed his coin. "Bless you, bless you! You met a true savior today, Bai Feng! Got chores at home, I'm off!"

Once the man left, Shen Jianchen tapped Bai Feng's acupoint free. "My silver isn't free. From now on, you follow me."

Shaking out stiff legs from holding that horse stance, Bai Feng asked wary, "Why should I follow you?"

"For one tael of silver every month."

Doing quick math—a sweet deal!—Bai Feng grinned and thrust out an open palm. "Deal! Hand over the five taels now!"

Cashless himself, Shen Jianchen said, "My maid holds the silver. Come back to the Foot Shop with me."

Leaving the quiet alley for the noisy street, they threaded through crowds back toward where he had left Mengrong, spotted Prince Yi's carriage parked outside the nearest Foot Shop, and stepped inside. Sure enough, weary shoppers sat resting over tea and light wine—not free, of course—while waiters bustled around refilling cups. They spotted Qiao Mengrong seated at a corner table beside a tea bowl, with Xilan standing watch behind her.

Rising to greet him, Qiao Mengrong smiled. "Back at last, Brother! You kept me waiting!" Eyeing the scruffy boy behind him, she asked, "Who is he?"

Without answering, Shen Jianchen told Xilan, "Give him five taels of silver."

Reluctant yet bound by princely command, Xilan produced a pouch holding five taels. "Here! Take it!"

Clutching the pouch, Bai Feng beamed with gratitude. "Thank you, young master! My mother's saved!"

He spun to dash off, only for Shen Jianchen to catch his collar and murmur low in his ear, "Remember: once your mother's settled, report to Prince Yi's Manor and ask for me. Break your word, and I'll dig three feet underground to drag you back!"

Half warm, half menacing—and "Prince Yi's Manor" stopped Bai Feng cold! Holy cow, is this guy Captain of the Guard at Prince Yi's Manor?! Hit the jackpot today—wants to hire me as a manor guard! Grinning ear to ear, he swore, "No need to dig! Why pass up a gig like this? Soon as Mom's cured, I'm coming straight to Prince Yi's Manor!"

Shen Jianchen let go. "Go on!" Bai Feng bolted straight out the door toward Grand Physician Zhao's Clinic.

Doubts cleared and a prime apprentice recruited, today's stroll had paid off big. Ready to wrap up, and figuring Mengrong's feet must ache after hours walking—plus Prince Yi's Manor sat far out in the suburbs—Shen Jianchen suggested taking the carriage home. Delighted—healthy enough to walk back without breaking stride, he only rode for her sake!—Qiao Mengrong glowed inside.

Aboard the carriage, she sat close beside him and rested her head against his shoulder. Before, frail Zhao Pu had always leaned on her; trading places now filled her with bliss as she felt the solid strength beneath her cheek. Half raising a hand to nudge her upright, Shen Jianchen paused midair and let his arm drop. Fine, let a little sister lean on her brother's shoulder; no harm done.

Thoughts drifted straight back to Qianli Xun and the fortune-teller's reading: "Even standing inches apart, you won't recognize each other..." Worry about recognition later; find her first! Head south, the old man said, yet the south stretched vast. Where on earth to start?

Turning that riddle over all the way back to Prince Yi's Manor, he stepped through the gate to a waiting maid: "Your Highness, Princess, Young Master Qiao has returned from Laizhou!"

"Understood," Qiao Mengrong said. "Where is he? I'll take Brother to meet him."

"Waiting in the reception hall, Your Highness."

"Mengrong, what did I call Young Master Qiao before?" Shen Jianchen asked.

"His name is Qiao Wenxian, courtesy name Wenyuan. You always called him Wenyuan."

Whew, Shen Jianchen thought—long as I don't have to call a guy my own age Uncle!

Entering the reception hall, they found a man in crisp white robes seated in a chair, who rose and bowed at once. "Prince Yi. Princess Jiahe."

Shen Jianchen almost stared slack-jawed. Face fair as carved jade, smooth skin glowing with health; deep, clear eyes shining like starlit pools of quiet intellect; straight nose, clean lips curving into a warm, confident smile; tall, balanced, upright carriage that turned heads on sight. Two phrases jumped straight into Shen Jianchen's head: elegant and refined—handsome as Pan An!

"Heard news of you the instant I entered Bianliang and raced straight back to see for myself! Truly healthy—Sister's spirit in Heaven must watch over you!" Clasping Prince Yi's hands to look him over, Qiao Wenxian beamed. "Casting off that frail frame brought out pure manly vigor—what a blessing!" Wrapping Prince Yi in a hearty embrace, he added, "Sturdy build too, steady as Mount Tai! Marvelous!"

Warmth surged in Shen Jianchen's chest; gaining a true friend and kinsman in this strange century felt wonderful. Returning the hug, he smiled, "Wenyuan, every illness is gone, yet my memory's wiped clean. If Mengrong hadn't told me, I wouldn't even know what to call you."

Letting go, Qiao Wenxian waved it off. "Forget what's gone; health matters most! Stuck in bed all those years barely leaving the manor, nothing worth keeping anyway except that one childhood trip out when you picked up a little sister! Toss the rest!"

"He forgot that too, though Matron Zou and I told him everything," Qiao Mengrong smiled.

"Good, good! Quit standing by the door; let's sit and talk!" Qiao Wenxian guided Prince Yi inside while Qiao Mengrong sent Xilan to fetch tea.

Once all three sat down, Qiao Wenxian pitched an idea: "Now that you're healthy, Prince Yi, why not travel south to Lin'an with me to meet your mother's family? Seeing you hale like this will make them overjoyed!"

"Lin'an? Is that to the south?" Shen Jianchen sat straight up.

"Straight south! Stunning scenery—come along and I'll walk you around West Lake, guaranteed worth every mile!"

"Deal! I'm going to Lin'an with you!" Just wondering where in the vast south to search for Qianli Xun, and Qiao Wenxian dropped Lin'an right into his lap! Had to be Heaven guiding his steps.

Watching her brother's eager grin, Qiao Mengrong recalled the fortune-teller's stall, and unease crept into her chest: Who on earth is Brother looking for?

A maid set fresh tea before the three and withdrew. Steading her mood, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Heading home to Lin'an for good, Young Master Qiao?"

"About time. Been away almost a year, plus someone I love lives there. High time I went back to see her."

"Oh? Young Master Qiao already holds someone in his heart—how many fond ladies will weep at that!"

"Only sorrow is, she's already married."

"If she's wed, better to let go, Young Master Qiao."

"You don't know, Princess Jiahe: her husband shares none of her soul and beats her all the time! Never saw her wounds with my own eyes, yet reading my friend's letters tears my heart out. I'm going back to urge her into mutual divorce."

"Bold indeed, Young Master Qiao. I admire your spirit."

Knowing nothing of Wenyuan's past, Shen Jianchen sat quiet and listened.

Just then a guard stepped in to report: "Your Highness, two letters just arrived from Lin'an for Young Master Qiao." A maid took them and delivered them to Qiao Wenxian.

Breaking open the first, Qiao Wenxian flared in anger: "Just talking about her husband beating her, and here's another letter saying he did it again! No way I leave her suffering another day in that living hell!" Watching his fierce reaction, Qiao Mengrong wondered what sort of married woman held Qiao Wenxian this enthralled. Reading further, relief lit his face: "Says she's pushing for mutual divorce—right in line with my plan! I'm leaving for Lin'an first thing tomorrow morning; get back early and maybe I can help!"

Opening the second envelope, he unfolded a large sheet holding a handwritten lyric, a single drop of black ink staining the character "you" (伊). Reading silent, he murmured, "'Through farewell tears, I gaze at green hills... Yet waking finds sorrow and resentment waiting just as before...' How bitter must her heart be to write lines like these?! Forget tomorrow—I'm leaving for Lin'an right now!"

Shen Jianchen sprang to his feet too. "I'll pack right now and ride with you, Wenyuan!"

Qiao Mengrong rose beside him. "I'm coming with you, Brother!"

Just as all three sealed their plan, a guard stepped in to announce: "Your Highness, an Imperial Attendant from the palace awaits outside."

All three walked out to meet a lone eunuch, not the same one as before. Bowing low, the eunuch beamed, "Prince Yi truly looks the picture of health—and Young Master Qiao is here too, wonderful! I bring His Majesty's invitation summoning both of you to a palace banquet tomorrow."

Opening the card for a quick glance, Shen Jianchen cursed the timing under his breath for derailing today's departure. Still, knowing enough court sense never to refuse His Majesty, he inclined his head: "Tell Imperial Father that Wenyuan and I will attend on time tomorrow."
