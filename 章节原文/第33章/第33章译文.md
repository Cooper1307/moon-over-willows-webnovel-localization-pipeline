# Chapter 33: Choosing a Disciple and Returning to the Manor—Qiao Wenxian

---

Years of practicing Shen Fist had given Shen Jianchen not only powerful hand techniques but also exceptional leg strength. His running speed was extremely fast as he quickly weaved through the crowd, chasing after the young thief.

---

The thief turned into a less crowded alley, running desperately forward, then into another empty lane until he was gasping for breath. Looking back and seeing how easily Shen Jianchen was chasing him, he realized he couldn't outrun him. He stopped, bent over, panting heavily: "Don't... don't... don't chase me anymore. I... I... I can't... can't run anymore. The money pouch... here... take it!"

---

Shen Jianchen also stopped, his expression unchanged. He took the money pouch and carefully examined the thief before him. The boy looked about fifteen or sixteen, his clothes tattered and his face dirty, but his features were quite delicate: "Are you a thief?"

---

"Who... who's a thief? Don't slander people!"

---

"If you're not a thief, why did you steal someone else's money pouch?"

---

"I didn't steal it—I borrowed it!"

---

Shen Jianchen thought this young thief was quite good at making excuses: "Borrowed? Who are you trying to fool? That man just shouted 'thief' at you in front of everyone. If it wasn't stealing, what was it?"

---

The thief sat on the ground, appearing quite calm: "Believe it or not, it's up to you. I'll sit here. When he arrives, just ask him and you'll know."

---

The person who lost the money hadn't arrived yet, so there was no way to verify. But with Shen Jianchen here, the thief couldn't escape anyway.

---

"What's your name?"

---

"Why should I tell you?"

---

"Because I can catch up to you."

---

"Hmph, your legwork is indeed impressive. Since I can't beat you, I might as well tell you. My name is Bai Feng."

---

"Bai Feng—not a bad name. Can you stand up and assume the horse stance so I can take a look?"

---

"You're really strange. Why should I assume the horse stance for you? I won't listen to you."

---

Shen Jianchen reached out, pulled Bai Feng up with one hand, pressed down on his shoulder with the same hand, and kicked both of his legs with his feet. Bai Feng immediately fell into the horse stance. Then he pressed Bai Feng's acupoints, stood aside to observe carefully, and praised in his heart: "His bone structure is indeed good! This trip to the market was worth it—I've found a good disciple."

---

Bai Feng complained unhappily: "You're really unreasonable. I already gave you the money pouch. Why are you treating me like this? Let me go!"

---

"This is your punishment. Who told you to do something bad and steal someone's money pouch? When the owner arrives and tells me to let you go, then I will."

---

Just as he finished speaking, the middle-aged man arrived, panting heavily: "Thank you... thank you, young master... for helping... helping me catch... him."

---

Shen Jianchen gave the money pouch to the middle-aged man. The man took it and sat down heavily on the ground. After catching his breath, he said: "Bai Feng, you brat. If you wanted to borrow money, why not just borrow it properly? Why did you grab my money pouch and run? If it weren't for this young master, you would have taken all my money."

---

Shen Jianchen asked: "You two know each other?"

---

Bai Feng said: "See? I didn't steal! I took it! Or more accurately, I borrowed it!"

---

Shen Jianchen scolded sternly: "Without someone's permission, what's the difference between taking, borrowing, and stealing?"

---

Bai Feng had no reply, his face full of defiance.

---

The middle-aged man had mostly recovered and stood up: "Young master, I know this kid. He originally said he wanted to borrow money. I took out my money pouch to let him take some, but he grabbed the whole pouch and ran."

---

Bai Feng complained unhappily: "Hmph! Even though you were willing to lend to me, it wasn't nearly enough. My mother's medical treatment needs five taels of silver, and you only agreed to lend me five hundred wen in copper coins. Even though I grabbed your money pouch, I also promised to earn money and pay you back later! You're just a miser!"

---

The middle-aged man defended himself: "My money didn't blow in from the wind either. It was all earned through hard work. I still have to support my family. If I lent it all to you, what would my family do?"

---

"But my mother is still sick at Grand Physician Zhao's residence!" Bai Feng burst into tears. "My mother has a serious illness. The doctor said it would cost five taels of silver to treat her. If we don't treat her soon, she'll die!"

---

The middle-aged man felt pity. He took out one tael from his money pouch and handed it to Bai Feng: "I can't give you too much. I hope you can understand."

---

Bai Feng's acupoints were still pressed, so he couldn't move. He watched as the silver was taken by Shen Jianchen, who then returned it to the middle-aged man: "Take the silver back. I'll pay for the silver needed to treat Bai Feng's mother."

---

The middle-aged man took the silver back happily: "That's good! That's good! Bai Feng, you've met a good person. This uncle has matters at home, so I'll be going now."

---

After the man put away the silver and left, Shen Jianchen pressed Bai Feng's acupoints again: "My silver doesn't come for free. From now on, you must follow me."

---

Having held the horse stance for a long time, Bai Feng's legs were somewhat numb. As he moved them around, he asked: "Why should I follow you?"

---

Shen Jianchen said: "Because you can get one tael of silver every month."

---

Bai Feng calculated carefully and thought it was a great deal: "Fine! I agree to follow you. Now give me the five taels of silver." Bai Feng held out his hand in a gesture demanding the silver.

---

Shen Jianchen didn't carry silver on him: "The silver is with my maid. Follow me to the rest tavern to get it."

---

Shen Jianchen led Bai Feng out of the quiet alley and into the bustling market. Weaving through the crowd, he looked around carefully and spotted the nearest rest tavern. The manor's carriage was parked beside it, so he headed in that direction.

---

Entering the rest tavern, it was indeed a place for people to rest. It also provided tea and wine, though of course not for free. There were quite a few people inside, and waiters kept running around refilling tea and pouring wine.

---

They easily found Qiao Mengrong. She was sitting at a table in the corner with a cup of tea in front of her, while her maid Xilan stood beside her.

---

Seeing her brother, Qiao Mengrong stood up and went to greet him: "Brother, you're finally here. I've been waiting for you." Then she noticed the young man behind her brother and asked: "Brother, who is he?"

---

Shen Jianchen didn't answer. He directly said to Xilan: "Give him five taels of silver."

---

Xilan was very reluctant, but since the prince had given the order, she couldn't refuse. She took out a money pouch containing five taels of silver and handed it to Bai Feng: "Here! Take it!"

---

Bai Feng took it, extremely grateful: "Thank you, young master. My mother is saved."

---

As he was about to leave, Shen Jianchen grabbed him and whispered in his ear: "Remember, after you settle your mother's matters, come find me at Prince Yi's Manor. Don't break your promise, or I'll search every corner of the earth to find you."

---

The words, both warm and threatening, made Bai Feng feel quite comfortable. But upon hearing the words "Prince Yi's Manor," he was almost stunned. He thought to himself: "Oh my! Prince Yi's Manor—could this person be the head of the guards at Prince Yi's Manor? I've really met a noble person today. Could he want me to become a guard at the manor?" So he happily said: "No need to dig up three feet of earth. With such a good opportunity, how could I not go? After my mother's illness is cured, I'll definitely come to Prince Yi's Manor."

---

Shen Jianchen let go of him: "Go!"

---

Without another word, Bai Feng ran out of the rest tavern and headed toward Grand Physician Zhao's residence.

---

This trip to the market was very fruitful. He had not only resolved the doubts in his heart but also met a young man with good bone structure. Shen Jianchen was very satisfied and didn't want to continue shopping.

---

Considering that Qiao Mengrong had been walking for a long time and her legs must be tired, and that Prince Yi's Manor was in the suburbs and quite far away, Shen Jianchen voluntarily suggested taking the carriage.

---

Qiao Mengrong was very happy. Her brother didn't look tired at all. If he were to walk back to the manor, it would definitely be no problem for him. The only reason he was taking the carriage was entirely out of concern for her.

---

Boarding the carriage, Qiao Mengrong deliberately sat beside her brother and naturally leaned her head on his shoulder. In the past, it was always her brother leaning on her like this. Now that it was reversed, she felt incredibly happy, carefully experiencing her brother's suddenly powerful shoulder.

---

Shen Jianchen raised his hand, wanting to lift the head resting on his shoulder, but stopped halfway and lowered it again. He thought, let her lean. After all, there's nothing wrong with a sister leaning on her brother.

---

He fell back into thoughts of Qianli Xun, recalling the fortune-teller's words: "The 'Cun' (inch) character at the bottom indicates that even if you meet the person you seek, within your heart you still won't be able to recognize each other..." He couldn't worry about that now—he'd find her first. The old man said she was in the south, so he might as well search in the south. But the south was vast with many places. Where exactly should he go?

---

Returning to Prince Yi's Manor with questions on his mind, they had just entered when a maid reported: "Your Highness, Commandery Princess, Young Master Qiao has returned from Laizhou."

---

Qiao Mengrong said: "I see. Where is Young Master Qiao? I'll take Brother to see him."

---

The maid replied: "He's waiting for Your Highness in the reception hall."

---

Shen Jianchen asked: "Mengrong, how did I used to address Young Master Qiao?"

---

Qiao Mengrong replied: "Young Master Qiao's name is Qiao Wenxian, and his courtesy name is Wenyuan. Brother usually called him Wenyuan."

---

Shen Jianchen thought to himself: "Good, good. As long as I don't have to call him Uncle, any form of address is fine."

---

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Mengrong entered the reception hall. A man dressed in white was sitting in a chair. Seeing them enter, he stood up and bowed: "Prince Yi, Commandery Princess Jiahe."

---

Shen Jianchen was almost stunned into a daze.

---

The man before him had a face as white as jade, with full and smooth skin radiating a healthy glow. His eyes were deep and bright, like stars hiding endless wisdom, yet revealing a restrained and profound temperament. His nose was straight and slender, his lips red and teeth white. The slightly upturned corners of his mouth outlined a confident and charming smile. His tall figure and healthy physique, with his even and upright posture, were truly captivating.

---

Two phrases popped into Shen Jianchen's mind: "Elegant and refined, handsome as Pan An."

[TN: Pan An (潘安) was a famous beauty from the Western Jin Dynasty (265-316 CE), known for his exceptional good looks. The phrase "handsome as Pan An" is a classical Chinese idiom used to describe a man of extraordinary beauty.]

---

"As soon as I entered Bianliang, I heard about Prince Yi's situation and hurried back to see for myself. Prince Yi has indeed recovered his health—surely Sister is watching over us from heaven." As Qiao Wenxian spoke, he stepped forward and grabbed Prince Yi's hand, looking him up and down. "Prince Yi has shed his sickly body and now displays the heroic spirit of a man. This is truly cause for celebration."

---

Qiao Wenxian then hugged Prince Yi: "Your body has also become strong and sturdy, steady as Mount Tai. Good, this is really too good!"

---

Being hugged by Qiao Wenxian, a warm current flooded Shen Jianchen's heart. Having come to this unfamiliar era, gaining another family member and friend made him feel very happy. He couldn't help but extend his arms and hug Qiao Wenxian back: "Wenyuan, although I'm completely free of illness, I don't remember anything. If Mengrong hadn't told me, I wouldn't even know how to address you."

---

Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi: "If you don't remember, then you don't remember. What's most important is that your health has recovered. Besides, the old you lived on a sickbed and rarely left the manor, so there wasn't much worth remembering. Just remember that when you were young, you went out once and picked up a sister. Everything else can be forgotten."

---

Qiao Mengrong said: "Brother doesn't remember this either, but Nanny Zou and I have already told him everything."

---

"That's good then." Qiao Wenxian pulled Prince Yi. "Let's not stand at the door talking. Let's sit down."

---

Qiao Mengrong quickly had Xilan order the maids to serve tea.

---

After the three of them were seated, Qiao Wenxian said: "Since Prince Yi's health has recovered, why not come with me to Lin'an to see your relatives there? They'll surely be very happy to see you like this."

---

Shen Jianchen asked: "Lin'an? Is that in the south?"

---

Qiao Wenxian replied: "Exactly. The scenery is quite pleasant. If you go, I'll take you to visit West Lake. It will definitely be worth the trip."

---

"Good! Then I'll go to Lin'an with you." Shen Jianchen was just worrying about where in the south to look for Qianli Xun when Qiao Wenxian appeared and mentioned Lin'an. He felt it must be heaven's arrangement.

---

Qiao Mengrong beside them recalled the scene of the divination. Seeing her brother's eager and joyful expression, sorrow crept into her heart. She continued to ask herself: "Who exactly is Brother looking for?"

---

The maids brought the brewed tea and served it to the three of them. After they withdrew, Qiao Mengrong composed herself and asked: "Does Young Master Qiao plan to return to Lin'an?"

---

"I should go back. I've been away from home for almost a year. Besides, there's someone I like there—I should go back and see her."

---

"Oh, Young Master Qiao already has someone he likes. Won't that make the girls who like Young Master Qiao sad?"

---

"It's a pity she's already married."

---

"Since she's already married, Young Master Qiao should let go."

---

"Commandery Princess Jiahe doesn't know this, but the husband she married doesn't share her interests and often beats her. Although I haven't seen her injuries with my own eyes, reading what my friend wrote in the letter has already broken my heart. I've decided to persuade her to seek an amicable divorce from her husband when I return this time."

[TN: "和离" (amicable divorce) refers to a mutual agreement between husband and wife to end their marriage, which was different from being "cast out" (被休), where the wife was unilaterally dismissed by the husband's family.]

---

"Young Master Qiao's thoughts are truly bold. Mengrong admires them."

---

Shen Jianchen just listened to the two of them chatting from the side. He blamed himself for not knowing Wenyuan well enough and not knowing what to talk about with him.

---

Just then, a guard came to report: "Your Highness, we just received two letters from Lin'an, both for Young Master Qiao."

---

The attending maid beside them took the letters and delivered them to Qiao Wenxian.

---

Qiao Wenxian opened one of them. After reading it, he was first angry: "We were just talking about her being beaten by her husband, and the letter mentions this again. I absolutely cannot let her stay in that hot bed any longer!"

[TN: "火炕" (hot bed/fire pit) is used metaphorically here to describe a miserable and painful situation, referring to her abusive marriage.]

---

Qiao Mengrong observed Qiao Wenxian's expression, wondering what kind of woman, even though already married, could still make Qiao Wenxian react this way.

---

Then he saw Qiao Wenxian's face break into a smile as he continued: "The letter says she plans to seek an amicable divorce from her husband. This is exactly what I was thinking. I'll return to Lin'an early tomorrow morning. If I go back sooner, I might still be able to help her."

---

Qiao Wenxian opened the other letter and took out a neatly folded piece of paper. Unfolding it, the paper was quite large, with a poem written on it. A drop of ink had fallen on the character "yi" (she/he). He read the poem silently to himself, then said aloud: "'Mingling parting tears with gaze on verdant hills,' 'Alas, upon waking, grief and sorrow linger still.' How much bitterness must be in her heart to write such words? No, I'm returning to Lin'an right now."

---

Shen Jianchen immediately stood up: "I'll go pack right away and leave with Wenyuan."

---

Qiao Mengrong also stood up: "I'll accompany Brother."

---

Just as the three of them reached an agreement, a guard came to report: "Your Highness, an imperial attendant from the palace has arrived and is waiting outside."

---

The three of them went out together to greet him, only to see one imperial attendant—not the same one from last time.

---

The imperial attendant stepped forward to pay respects to Prince Yi: "Prince Yi has indeed completely recovered from his illness. You look very spirited. Young Master Qiao is also here—how wonderful. I bring His Majesty's invitation, inviting you both to attend the banquet at the palace tomorrow."

---

Shen Jianchen took the invitation and opened it to glance through. In his heart, he complained that His Majesty's banquet was really sudden, preventing him from departing for Lin'an today.

---

Shen Jianchen still had some historical knowledge. An invitation from His Majesty couldn't be refused, so he could only say: "Please report back to Father that Wenyuan and I will attend on time tomorrow."
