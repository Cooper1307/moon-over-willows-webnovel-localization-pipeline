## 四、表达正确性与地道性

### 1. 追逐段开篇

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Years of practicing Shen Fist had given Shen Jianchen not only powerful hand techniques but also exceptional leg strength. His running speed was extremely fast as he quickly weaved through the crowd, chasing after the young thief.* |
| **修改后** | *Shen Fist trained the legs as much as the fists. Years of it. And right now, those legs ate up the distance between him and the thief like it was nothing. He wove through the crowd—left, right, left—gaining with every stride.* |
| **理由** | ① 现译是"说明文"式开头（"had given him not only X but also Y"），缺乏追逐的紧迫感；② "His running speed was extremely fast" 是典型中式英语（"速度极快"的直译）；③ 修改后用碎片句 + 节奏感（"left, right, left"）模拟追逐的动态 |

### 2. 白风喘气求饶

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *"Don't... don't... don't chase me anymore. I... I... I can't... can't run anymore. The money pouch... here... take it!"* |
| **修改后** | *"Don't—don't—DON'T chase me! I can't—I CAN'T—" He bent double, hands on knees, gasping. "The pouch. Take it. Just—stop."* |
| **理由** | 现译的省略号过多（7 个），在英文网文中显得拖沓。用破折号 + 大写 + 动作描写（bent double, hands on knees）传达"跑不动了"的狼狈感，节奏更快 |

## 八、吸引力评估

**做得好的：**
- 追逐段开篇节奏快，迅速建立冲突 ✓
- 白风"借不是偷"的反复狡辩有喜剧节奏 ✓
- 申简辰"强制马步"展示武力值 ✓
- 乔文仙白衣登场的视觉冲击 ✓
- 结尾宫廷邀请制造"计划被打断"的悬念 ✓
- 乔文仙对"她"（朱淑真/千里寻）的牵挂为后续埋线 ✓

**可加强：**
- **白风是本章最有趣的新角色**，但他的"无赖少年"气质在翻译中被削弱。现译让他说话太"正常"。应加入更多少年特有的"理直气壮的无赖感"：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *"Why should I tell you?"* |
| **修改后** | *"Why?" He tilted his chin up. Defiant. Like a cat that had been caught but refused to look guilty.* |

---

- **乔文仙读词（《江城子》）是本章情感最重的段落**——他读到了千里寻/朱淑真写的词，墨滴在"伊"字上。现译处理偏平：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *He read the poem silently to himself, then said aloud: "'Mingling parting tears with gaze on verdant hills,' 'Alas, upon waking, grief and sorrow linger still.' How much bitterness must be in her heart to write such words? No, I'm returning to Lin'an right now."* |
| **修改后** | *He read it once. Twice. His thumb traced the ink stain on the character 伊. "She." His voice went quiet. "'With parting tears, I gaze on green mountains.' 'Upon waking—grief. Still grief. Always grief.'" He folded the paper. Carefully. As if it might break. "How much does it hurt," he murmured, "to write something like this?" <br><br> He stood. "I'm leaving. Now. Tonight."* |
| **理由** | ① 加入"读了两遍""手指描过墨痕"的动作，展示他的心疼；② "Carefully. As if it might break." 传达他对这张纸（=她的心）的珍视；③ "How much does it hurt" 比 "How much bitterness must be in her heart" 更个人化、更痛；④ "I'm leaving. Now. Tonight." 碎片句传达急切 |
