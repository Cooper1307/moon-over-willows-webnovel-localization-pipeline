# 第33章 选徒回府乔文仙
# Chapter 33: Choosing a Disciple and Returning to the Manor—Qiao Wenxian

---

长期练习申拳，除手上有功夫外，脚上的功力也比普通人厉害，申简辰奔跑的速度极快，迅速穿过人群，向小贼追去。

Years of practicing Shen Fist had given Shen Jianchen powerful hands and—more useful right now—exceptional leg strength. He sprinted, weaving through the crowd as he closed in on the young thief.

---

小贼拐进一条人少的小巷，奋力向前奔跑，接着又进入无人的巷子，跑得上气不接下气。回头见申简辰追得轻松，怕是跑不过对方，便停下来，弯着腰，口喘粗气："别……别……别追了，我……我……我不……不跑了，钱袋……给……给你！"

The thief turned into a less crowded alley, running desperately forward, then into another empty lane until he was gasping for breath. Looking back and seeing how easily Shen Jianchen was chasing him, he realized he couldn't outrun him. He stopped, bent over, panting heavily: "Don't... don't... don't chase me anymore. I... I... I can't... can't run anymore. The money pouch... here... take it!"

---

申简辰也停下，面不改色，接过钱袋，仔细瞧眼前的小贼，大约十五六岁的模样，身上的衣裳很破烂，脸上脏兮兮的，五官倒十分清秀："你是小偷？"

Shen Jianchen also stopped, his expression unchanged. He took the money pouch and carefully examined the thief before him. The boy looked about fifteen or sixteen, his clothes tattered and his face dirty, but his features were refined: "Are you a thief?"

---

"谁……谁是小偷？别污蔑人！"

"Who... who's a thief? Don't slander people!"

---

"你不是小偷，为何偷别人的钱袋？"

"If you're not a thief, why did you steal someone else's money pouch?"

---

"我不是偷，是借！"

"I didn't steal it. I borrowed it. There's a difference!"

---

申简辰觉得这个小贼倒挺能狡辩："借？骗谁呢？刚才那人当着所有人喊你小贼，不是偷又是什么？"

Shen Jianchen thought this young thief was good at making excuses: "Borrowed? Who are you trying to fool? That man just shouted 'thief' at you in front of everyone. If it wasn't stealing, what was it?"

---

小贼坐在地上，显得很坦然："信不信随你，我坐在这里，等他来了，一问便知。"

The thief sat on the ground, appearing calm: "Believe it or not, it's up to you. I'll sit here. When he arrives, just ask him and you'll know."

---

丢钱的人还未到，无法辨别，反正有他在此，小贼根本逃脱不了。

The person who lost the money hadn't arrived yet, so there was no way to verify. But with him there, the thief couldn't escape anyway.

---

"你叫什么？"

"What's your name?"

---

"凭什么告诉你？"

"Why should I tell you?"

---

"凭我能追上你。"

"Because I caught you."

---

"哼，你的脚力确实厉害，既比不过你，告诉你也无妨，我叫白风。"

"Hmph, your legs are truly something. Since I can't outrun you anyway, I might as well tell you. I'm Bai Feng."

---

"白风，名字还不错，你能不能站起来，蹲个马步让我瞧瞧？"

"Bai Feng—not a bad name. Can you stand up and assume the horse stance so I can take a look?"

---

"你这人真奇怪，凭什么让我蹲马步？我才不听你的。"

"You're really strange. Why should I assume the horse stance for you? I won't listen to you."

---

申简辰伸出手，一把将白风拽起，用一只手把他的肩膀压下，用脚分别在两腿处踢两下，白风立刻呈马步状。接着又点了白风的穴位，站在一旁仔细瞧，心里赞道："根骨确实不错！这趟街逛得值，碰到一个好徒弟。"

Shen Jianchen reached out, pulled Bai Feng up with one hand, pressed down on his shoulder with the same hand, and kicked the backs of his knees. Bai Feng immediately dropped into the horse stance. Then he pressed Bai Feng's acupoints, stood aside to observe carefully, and praised inwardly: ***What exceptional martial aptitude! This trip to the market was worth it—I've found a good disciple.***

---

白风不满道："你这人真没道理，我把钱袋已给你，凭什么这样对待我？快放开我！"

Bai Feng grumbled: "You're really unreasonable. I already gave you the money pouch. Why are you treating me like this? Let me go!"

---

"这是对你的惩罚，谁让你不干好事，偷别人的钱袋？等失主来，他让我放你，我再放。"

"This is your punishment. Who told you to do something bad and steal someone's money pouch? When the owner arrives and tells me to let you go, then I will."

---

刚说完，中年男人才赶到，气喘吁吁地说："谢谢……谢谢公子……帮我……帮我追到……他。"

Just as he finished speaking, the middle-aged man arrived, panting heavily: "Thank you... thank you, young master... for helping... helping me catch... him."

---

申简辰把钱袋给了中年男人，他拿过钱袋一屁股坐在地上，缓了一会儿说："白风，你这小子，借钱就好好借，为何拿了我的钱袋就跑？要不是这位公子，我的钱都被你拿走了。"

He gave the money pouch to the middle-aged man. The man took it and sat down heavily on the ground. After catching his breath, he said: "Bai Feng, you brat. If you wanted to borrow money, why not just borrow it properly? Why did you grab my money pouch and run? If it weren't for this young master, you would have taken all my money."

---

申简辰问："你们认识？"

Shen Jianchen asked: "You two know each other?"

---

白风说："听到没？我不是偷！是拿！更准确地说，是借！"

Bai Feng said: "See? I didn't steal! I took it! Or more accurately, I borrowed it!"

---

申简辰厉声训斥："不经过他人允许，拿和借跟偷有什么区别？"

Shen Jianchen scolded sternly: "Without someone's permission, what's the difference between taking, borrowing, and stealing?"

---

白风无言以对，满脸不服气。

Bai Feng had no reply, his face full of defiance.

---

中年男人基本缓过来，站起身："公子，我认识这孩子，他本说借钱，我把钱袋拿出来给他取，结果他一把将钱袋抢走。"

The middle-aged man had mostly recovered and stood up: "Young master, I know this kid. He originally said he wanted to borrow money. I took out my money pouch to let him take some, but he grabbed the whole pouch and ran."

---

白风不满道："哼！你虽愿意借给我，可根本不够，我娘医病需五两银子，而你只答应借给我五百文铜钱，我虽抢你的钱袋，可我也答应以后挣钱还你！你就是个小气鬼！"

Bai Feng retorted: "Hmph! Even though you were willing to lend to me, it wasn't nearly enough. My mother's medical treatment needs five taels of silver, and you only agreed to lend me five hundred wen in copper coins. Even though I grabbed your money pouch, I also promised to earn money and pay you back later! You're just a miser!"

---

中年男人辩解道："我的钱也不是大风刮来的，都是辛苦赚的，还要养家糊口，都借给你，我一家老小该怎么办？"

The middle-aged man defended himself: "My money didn't blow in from the wind either. It was all earned through hard work. I still have to support my family. If I lent it all to you, what would my family do?"

---

"可我娘还在赵太丞家病着呢！"说着白风哭起来，"呜呜……！我娘得重病，大夫说需花费五两银子，才给我娘医治，再不治，她会死掉！呜呜……！"

"But my mother is still sick at Grand Physician Zhao's!" Bai Feng burst into tears. "Wuwu...! My mother—she's gravely ill. The physician said it would cost five taels of silver to cure her. If she isn't treated soon, she'll die! Wuwu...!"

---

中年男人产生怜悯之心，从钱袋里取出一两，递到白风面前："我不能给你太多，希望你能理解。"

The middle-aged man felt pity. He took out one tael from his money pouch and handed it to Bai Feng: "I can't give you too much. I hope you can understand."

---

白风被点穴位，不能动弹，目睹银子被申简辰接到手中，又还给中年男人："银子你还是拿回去，白风娘治病需要的银两我来出。"

Bai Feng, his acupoints still sealed and unable to move, watched Shen Jianchen take the silver and return it to the man: "Take the silver back. I'll pay for the silver needed to treat Bai Feng's mother."

---

中年男人接过银子高兴道："那就好！那就好！白风啊，你遇到好人，大叔家里还有事，先走了。"

The middle-aged man took the silver back happily: "That's good! That's good! Bai Feng, you've met a good person. This uncle has matters at home, so I'll be going now."

---

男人装好银子走后，申简辰又点了白风的穴位："我的银子不白给，以后你必须跟着我。"

After the man put away the silver and left, Shen Jianchen pressed Bai Feng's acupoints again: "My silver doesn't come for free. From now on, you must follow me."

---

马步蹲得时间长，白风的腿有些麻，边活动边问："我凭什么跟着你？"

Having held the horse stance for a long time, Bai Feng's legs were somewhat numb. As he moved them around, he asked: "Why should I follow you?"

---

申简辰说："凭你每月可以拿到一两银子。"

Shen Jianchen said: "Because you can get one tael of silver every month."

---

白风仔细盘算，觉得很划算："好！我答应你，现在把五两银子给我。"白风伸出手，一副要银子的姿态。

Bai Feng calculated carefully and thought it was a great deal: "Fine! I agree to follow you. Now give me the five taels of silver." Bai Feng held out his hand in a gesture demanding the silver.

---

申简辰身上没有带银两："银子在丫鬟那里，你跟我去脚店拿。"

He didn't carry silver on him: "The silver is with my maid. Follow me to the rest tavern to get it."

---

申简辰带着白风离开冷清的小巷，来到热闹的街市，穿过人群，找到离开之地，向周围仔细瞧了瞧，发现一家最近的脚店，旁边停着王府的马车，便朝脚店的方向走去。

He led Bai Feng out of the quiet alley and into the bustling market. Pushing through the crowd, he found a way clear, glanced around, and spotted the nearest rest tavern, with the manor's carriage parked beside it. He headed toward it.

---

进入脚店后，果然是供人休息之地，还提供茶和水酒，当然不是免费。里面人不少，不时有伙计跑来跑去添茶倒水或上酒。

Entering the rest tavern, it was indeed a place for people to rest. It also provided tea and wine, though of course not for free. There were several people inside, and waiters kept running around refilling tea and pouring wine.

---

很顺利找到乔梦容，她正坐在角落的一个桌旁，面前放着一盏茶，旁边站着丫鬟喜兰。

They easily found Qiao Mengrong. She was sitting at a table in the corner with a cup of tea in front of her, while her maid Xilan stood beside her.

---

乔梦容看到哥哥，起身迎上去："哥哥，你可来了，让我好等。"接着瞧见哥哥身后的小子，问道："哥哥，他是谁？"

Seeing her brother, Qiao Mengrong stood up and went to greet him: "Brother, you're finally here. I've been waiting for you." Then she noticed the young man behind her brother and asked: "Brother, who is he?"

---

申简辰没有回答，直接对喜兰说道："给他五两银子。"

Shen Jianchen didn't answer. He directly said to Xilan: "Give him five taels of silver."

---

喜兰很不情愿，王爷已发话，不能不遵从，拿出一个装着五两银子的钱袋递给白风："给你！拿着！"

Xilan was reluctant, but since the prince had given the order, she couldn't refuse. She took out a money pouch containing five taels of silver and handed it to Bai Feng: "Here! Take it!"

---

白风接过，十分感激："感谢公子，我娘有救了。"

Bai Feng took it, extremely grateful: "Thank you, young master. My mother is saved."

---

说着就要走，被申简辰一把拉住，在他耳边小声说："记住，处理好你娘的事，来仪王府找我，不得食言，否则我挖地三尺也会把你找到。"

As he was about to leave, Shen Jianchen grabbed him and whispered in his ear: "Remember, after you settle your mother's matters, come find me at Prince Yi's Manor. Don't break your promise, or I'll search every corner of the earth to find you."

---

既温暖又充满威胁的话语让白风很舒服，听到"仪王府"三个字，他快要傻眼，心里嘀咕："妈呀！仪王府，这人该不会是仪王府的侍卫总管？我今儿可是遇到贵人，难道他想让我到仪王府当侍卫？"于是高兴地说："不用挖地三尺，这么好的事，我怎么会不去？等治好我娘的病，一定去仪王府。"

Warm and threatening at once, the words left Bai Feng oddly at ease. But the moment he heard "Prince Yi's Manor," he nearly froze. ***Oh my! Prince Yi's Manor—could this man be the head of the guards there? I've really stumbled onto a noble person today. Does he want me as a guard at the manor?*** He said eagerly: "No need to dig up three feet of earth. With an opportunity this good, how could I not go? Once my mother is well, I'll come straight to Prince Yi's Manor."

---

申简辰将手放开："去吧!"

Shen Jianchen let go of him: "Go!"

---

白风二话没说，撒腿跑出脚店，往赵太丞家而去。

Without another word, Bai Feng ran out of the rest tavern and headed toward Grand Physician Zhao's residence.

---

这趟逛街收获颇丰，既解决心中疑惑，又遇到一位根骨不错的小子，申简辰很满意，不想再逛下去。

This trip to the market was fruitful. He had not only resolved the doubts in his heart but also met a young man with exceptional martial aptitude. Shen Jianchen was satisfied and didn't want to continue shopping.

---

考虑到乔梦容逛了许久，腿脚肯定已累，加之仪王府在郊区，比较远，申简辰主动要求乘坐马车。

Considering that Qiao Mengrong had been walking for a long time and her legs must be tired, and that Prince Yi's Manor was in the suburbs and far away, he volunteered to take the carriage.

---

乔梦容很开心，哥哥看上去一点儿都不累，他若徒步回王府肯定没有任何问题，之所以乘坐马车，完全是关心她的缘故。

Qiao Mengrong was delighted. Her brother didn't look tired at all. If he were to walk back to the manor, it would definitely be no problem for him. The only reason he was taking the carriage was entirely out of concern for her.

---

登上马车，乔梦容有意坐在哥哥身边，很自然地靠在他的肩头。以前总是哥哥这样靠着她，现在换回来，她感到无比幸福，认真体会着哥哥突然之间变得十分有力的肩膀。

Boarding the carriage, Qiao Mengrong deliberately sat beside her brother and naturally leaned her head on his shoulder. In the past, it was always her brother leaning on her like this. Now that it was reversed, she felt incredibly happy, carefully experiencing her brother's suddenly powerful shoulder.

---

申简辰抬起手，想要扶起靠在肩膀上的脑袋，刚到半空又放下。心想，靠就靠吧，反正妹妹靠靠哥哥也没什么不妥。

Shen Jianchen raised his hand, wanting to lift the head resting on his shoulder, but stopped halfway and lowered it again. ***Let her lean,*** he thought. ***After all, there's nothing wrong with a sister leaning on her brother.***

---

他又陷入对千里寻的思念之中，回想着算卦老先生的话："底下一个'寸'字，说明您与所找之人即使见到，方寸之间又无法相认……"管不了那么多，先找到人再说。老先生说在南边，不妨去南边找找，可南边很大，地方很多，到底该去哪里？

He fell back into thoughts of Qianli Xun, recalling the fortune-teller's words: "The 'Cun' (inch) character at the bottom indicates that even if you meet the person you seek, within your heart you still won't be able to recognize each other..." He couldn't worry about that now—he'd find her first. The old man said she was in the south, so he might as well search in the south. But the south was vast with many places. Where exactly should he go?

---

带着一路上的疑问回到仪王府，一进门，丫鬟来报："王爷，郡主，乔公子从莱州回来了。"

Returning to Prince Yi's Manor with questions on his mind, they had just entered when a maid reported: "Your Highness, Commandery Princess, Young Master Qiao has returned from Laizhou."

---

乔梦容说："知道了，乔公子人在哪里？我带哥哥去见他。"

Qiao Mengrong said: "I see. Where is Young Master Qiao? I'll take Brother to see him."

---

丫鬟回道："正在会客厅等王爷。"

The maid replied: "He's waiting for Your Highness in the reception hall."

---

申简辰问："梦容，不知我以前如何称呼乔公子？"

Shen Jianchen asked: "Mengrong, how did I used to address Young Master Qiao?"

---

乔梦容回道："乔公子名唤乔文仙，字文渊，哥哥平常称呼他文渊。"

Qiao Mengrong replied: "Young Master Qiao's name is Qiao Wenxian, and his courtesy name is Wenyuan. Brother usually called him Wenyuan."

---

申简辰心想："还好还好，只要不叫他舅舅，怎么称呼都无所谓。"

***Good, good,*** Shen Jianchen thought. ***As long as I don't have to call him Uncle, any form of address is fine.***

---

申简辰与乔梦容来到会客厅，椅子上坐着一位身着白衣的男子，见他们进来，起身行礼："仪王，佳和郡主。"

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Mengrong entered the reception hall. A man dressed in white was sitting in a chair. Seeing them enter, he stood up and bowed: "Prince Yi, Commandery Princess Jiahe."

---

申简辰快要看呆。

Shen Jianchen was almost stunned into a daze.

---

眼前的男子面庞洁白如玉，皮肤丰满而光滑，散发着健康的光泽；他的眼睛深邃明亮，像是藏着无尽智慧的星辰，又透露出内敛而深邃的气质；鼻子挺直修长，唇红齿白，微微上扬的嘴角勾勒出一抹自信而迷人的微笑；他的身材高挑，体魄健康，均匀挺拔的身姿不禁让人为之倾倒。

The man before him had a face pale as jade, his skin full and smooth, luminous with the glow of good health. His eyes were deep and bright—like stars holding endless wisdom—yet gave off a calm, fathomless air. His nose was straight and finely shaped; his lips, red against white teeth, curved in a confident, charming smile. Tall and well-built, he stood with an easy, upright grace that was genuinely captivating.

---

申简辰的脑中蹦出两个词语："风度翩翩，貌若潘安。"

Two phrases popped into Shen Jianchen's mind: "Elegant and refined, handsome as Pan An."

[TN: Pan An (潘安) was a famous beauty from the Western Jin Dynasty (265-316 CE), known for his exceptional good looks. The phrase "handsome as Pan An" is a classical Chinese idiom used to describe a man of extraordinary beauty.]

---

"我一进汴梁听人说起仪王之事，赶紧回来看个究竟，仪王果然已恢复健康，定是姐姐在天有灵。"乔文仙说着，上前抓住仪王的手，将他上下瞧了一遍，"仪王脱去病体，尽显男子英气，实在可喜可贺。"

"As soon as I entered Bianliang, I heard about Prince Yi's situation and hurried back to see for myself. Prince Yi has indeed recovered his health—surely Sister is watching over us from heaven." As Qiao Wenxian spoke, he stepped forward and grabbed Prince Yi's hand, looking him up and down. "Prince Yi has shed his sickly body and now displays the heroic spirit of a man. This is truly cause for celebration."

---

乔文仙又一把将仪王抱住："身板也变得硬朗，稳如泰山，好，真是太好了！"

Qiao Wenxian then hugged Prince Yi: "Your body has also become strong and sturdy, steady as Mount Tai. Good, this is really too good!"

---

申简辰被乔文仙这么一抱，一股暖流涌入心头，来到这个陌生的时代，多了一位亲人和朋友，他感到很幸福，不由伸出双臂也将乔文仙抱住："文渊，我虽疾病全无，但什么都不记得，要不是梦容告诉我，都不知道该如何称呼你。"

Being hugged by Qiao Wenxian, a warm current flooded Shen Jianchen's heart. Having come to this unfamiliar era, gaining another family member and friend made him feel happy. He couldn't help but extend his arms and hug Qiao Wenxian back: "Wenyuan, although I'm completely free of illness, I don't remember anything. If Mengrong hadn't told me, I wouldn't even know how to address you."

---

乔文仙放开仪王："不记得就不记得，身体恢复健康才最重要，再说以前的你生活在病榻上，难得走出府外，也没什么值得记住的事，只要记住你小时候出去过一趟，捡回来个妹妹这一件事即可，其他皆可忘记。"

Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi: "If you don't remember, then you don't remember. What's most important is that your health has recovered. Besides, the old you lived on a sickbed and rarely left the manor, so there wasn't much worth remembering. Just remember that when you were young, you went out once and picked up a sister. Everything else can be forgotten."

---

乔梦容说："这件事哥哥也不记得，不过我与邹嬷嬷已把一切告诉哥哥。"

Qiao Mengrong said: "Brother doesn't remember this either, but Matron Zou and I have already told him everything."

---

"那便好。"乔文仙拉起仪王，"我们别站门口叙话，还是坐下说。"

"That's good then." Qiao Wenxian pulled Prince Yi. "Let's not stand at the door talking. Let's sit down."

---

乔梦容赶快让喜兰吩咐丫鬟上茶。

Qiao Mengrong quickly had Xilan order the maids to serve tea.

---

三人坐定后，乔文仙说："仪王既然身体恢复健康，不如跟我去趟临安，见见临安的亲人，他们见到你现在这样，肯定会很开心。"

After the three of them were seated, Qiao Wenxian said: "Since Prince Yi's health has recovered, why not come with me to Lin'an to see your relatives there? They'll surely be delighted to see you like this."

---

申简辰问："临安？可在南边？"

Shen Jianchen asked: "Lin'an? Is that in the south?"

---

乔文仙回道："正是，景色相当宜人，如若你去了，我带你去西湖走走，绝对不枉此行。"

Qiao Wenxian replied: "Exactly. The scenery is pleasant. If you go, I'll take you to visit West Lake. It will definitely be worth the trip."

---

"好！那我就跟你去趟临安。"申简辰正愁到南边什么地方去找千里寻，乔文仙就出现，还提到临安，他冥冥中认为定是上天的安排。

"Good! Then I'll go to Lin'an with you." Shen Jianchen was just worrying about where in the south to look for Qianli Xun when Qiao Wenxian appeared and mentioned Lin'an. He felt it must be heaven's arrangement.

---

一旁的乔梦容想起算卦时的情景，再看哥哥渴望而又欢喜的表情，愁绪不由袭上心头，她继续在心中自问："哥哥到底要找什么人？"

Qiao Mengrong beside them recalled the scene of the divination. Seeing her brother's eager and joyful expression, sorrow crept into her heart. She continued to ask herself: "Who exactly is Brother looking for?"

---

丫鬟把泡好的茶端上来，给三人放好，退下后，乔梦容收拾好心情，问道："乔公子打算回临安？"

The maids brought the brewed tea and served it to the three of them. After they withdrew, Qiao Mengrong composed herself and asked: "Does Young Master Qiao plan to return to Lin'an?"

---

"是该回去，离开家都快一年，再说那里有我喜欢的人，也该回去看看她。"

"I should go back. I've been away from home for almost a year. Besides, there's someone I like there—I should go back and see her."

---

"哦，乔公子已有喜欢之人，岂不是令喜欢乔公子的女子伤心？"

"Oh, Young Master Qiao already has someone he likes. Won't that make the girls who like Young Master Qiao sad?"

---

"只可惜她已嫁人。"

"It's a pity she's already married."

---

"既已嫁人，乔公子还是放下为好。"

"Since she's already married, Young Master Qiao should let go."

---

"佳和郡主有所不知，她嫁的丈夫与她志趣不相投，还经常打她，我虽未亲眼目睹她受伤的样子，可读了朋友信中所说，已揪心不已，决定此次回去，劝她与丈夫和离。"

"Commandery Princess Jiahe doesn't know this, but the husband she married doesn't share her interests and often beats her. Although I haven't seen her injuries with my own eyes, reading what my friend wrote in the letter has already broken my heart. I've decided to persuade her to seek a mutual divorce from her husband when I return this time."

[TN: "和离" (mutual divorce) refers to a mutual agreement between husband and wife to end their marriage, which was different from being "cast out" (被休), where the wife was unilaterally dismissed by the husband's family.]

---

"乔公子的想法真是大胆，令梦容佩服。"

"Young Master Qiao's thoughts are truly bold. Mengrong admires them."

---

申简辰只顾在一旁听两人聊天儿，只怪他不了解文渊，不知该与他聊些什么。

Shen Jianchen just listened to the two of them chatting from the side. He blamed himself for not knowing Wenyuan well enough and not knowing what to talk about with him.

---

这时有侍卫前来报告："王爷，刚收到两封从临安寄来的信，都是给乔公子的。"

Just then, a guard came to report: "Your Highness, we just received two letters from Lin'an, both for Young Master Qiao."

---

旁边伺候的丫鬟接过，送至乔文仙手中。

The attending maid beside them took the letters and delivered them to Qiao Wenxian.

---

乔文仙打开其中一封，看后先是气愤："刚说到她被丈夫毒打，信中又提此事，我决不能让她在火炕再待下去！"

Qiao Wenxian opened one of them. After reading it, he was first angry: "We were just talking about her being beaten by her husband, and the letter mentions this again. I absolutely cannot let her stay in that living hell a day longer!"


---

乔梦容观察乔文仙的表情，想不通什么样的女子都已嫁人，还能让乔文仙有如此表现？

Qiao Mengrong observed Qiao Wenxian's expression, wondering what kind of woman, even though already married, could still make Qiao Wenxian react this way.

---

接着又看到乔文仙面露笑容，继续说："信中说，她打算与丈夫和离，与我想到一处，明日一早我便回临安，早些回去，说不定还能帮到她。"

Then he saw Qiao Wenxian's face break into a smile as he continued: "The letter says she plans to seek a mutual divorce from her husband. This is exactly what I was thinking. I'll return to Lin'an early tomorrow morning. If I go back sooner, I might still be able to help her."

---

乔文仙又拆开另一封书信，从中取出被叠得整整齐齐的一张纸，打开后，纸张挺大，上面写着一首词，"伊"字上滴着一滴墨痕。他将词在心里读了一遍，口中说道："'和别泪，看青山'，'争奈醒来，愁恨又依然'，她心里该有多苦，才能写出这样的词，不行，我现在就回临安。"

Qiao Wenxian opened the other letter and took out a neatly folded sheet of paper. Unfolding it, the page was large, with a ci poem written on it and a single drop of ink staining the character "yi" (she). He read it over once in his mind, then said aloud: "'Mingling parting tears with gaze on verdant hills,' 'Alas, upon waking, grief and sorrow linger still.' How much pain must she have borne, to write such words? No—I'm returning to Lin'an right now."

[TN: The two ci lines above are the translator's rendering of the original ci (和别泪，看青山 / 争奈醒来，愁恨又依然); no authoritative published translation was found, so they are rendered here.]

---

申简辰立刻站起来："我这就去收拾一番，跟文渊一起走。"

Shen Jianchen immediately stood up: "I'll go pack right away and leave with Wenyuan."

---

乔梦容也站起来："我陪哥哥一起去。"

Qiao Mengrong also stood up: "I'll accompany Brother."

---

三人刚达成一致，侍卫前来报道："王爷，宫中的内侍来了，在外面等候。"

Just as the three of them reached an agreement, a guard came to report: "Your Highness, an imperial attendant from the palace has arrived and is waiting outside."

---

三人一起迎出去，只看到一名内侍前来，不是上次那位。

The three of them went out together to greet him, only to see one imperial attendant—not the same one from last time.

---

内侍上前拜见仪王："仪王殿下果然疾病全无，看上去很精神。乔公子也在，真是太好了，我带来官家的请帖，邀请二位明日参加宫中举办的宴会。"

The imperial attendant stepped forward to pay respects to Prince Yi: "Prince Yi has indeed completely recovered from his illness. You look spirited. Young Master Qiao is also here—how wonderful. I bring His Majesty's invitation, inviting you both to attend the banquet at the palace tomorrow."

---

申简辰接过请帖，打开浏览一遍，心中埋怨官家举办宴会实在突然，搞得他无法今日出发去临安。

He took the invitation and glanced through it. In his heart, he complained that His Majesty's banquet was really sudden, preventing him from departing for Lin'an today.

---

申简辰还是有些历史常识，官家邀请，无法拒绝，只好说："请内侍回禀父皇，明日我与文渊会按时参加。"

He still had some historical knowledge. An invitation from His Majesty couldn't be refused, so he could only say: "Please report back to Father that Wenyuan and I will attend on time tomorrow."
