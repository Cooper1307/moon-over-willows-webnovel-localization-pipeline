## Chapter 120: Awkwardness of Misplaced Hearts

两个丫鬟来到闺房门口，喜兰向屋内说：“郡主，日头老高，您该起床，再不起，只怕王爷一会儿来，您还睡着。”
Reaching the bedchamber door, Xilan called inside, "Princess, the sun is high—time to rise! Sleep any longer and His Highness might arrive to catch you still in bed."

喜兰真是懂郡主的心思，听到哥哥可能会来，乔梦容顿时清醒，从床上爬起来。
Xilan knew her mistress inside out: at the mention of Prince Yi visiting, Qiao Mengrong snapped wide awake and scrambled up.

“我怎么只顾睡觉，忘记哥哥可能会过来，他要是过来见我还睡着，岂不笑话？淑真，你也赶紧起来，难道你不想见乔公子？”
"How did I sleep so late, forgetting Brother might drop by? If he catches me still in bed, how embarrassing! Wake up too, Shuzhen—don't you want to see Young Master Qiao?"

乔文仙如同千里寻的兴奋剂，她也坐起来：“是该起床，昨日我跟文渊没说几句，你与仪王过来，之后只顾跟你聊，今日我要与他好好说说话。”
Qiao Wenxian's name worked like instant adrenaline on Qianli Xun, who sat bolt upright: "Right! Barely got two words in with Wenyuan yesterday before you and Prince Yi showed up, and then I talked with you all night—today I'm going to have a proper chat with him."

“你们进来吧！”乔梦容向门外吩咐完，又笑着问，“难道嫌弃我与哥哥打扰你们？”
"Come in, you two!" calling to the maids outside, Qiao Mengrong added with a smile, "Holding a grudge that Brother and I interrupted you yesterday?"

“哪敢？你们两个来不叫打扰，那叫锦上添花！跟你聊一晚，心情好很多，正能量满满。哪怕现在站在大街上，别人当面指责我，我都不会当回事，因为有身份尊贵的郡主把我当朋友，这份荣耀足以抵挡所有攻击而来的流言蜚语！”
"Not a chance! You two weren't interrupting—you were icing on the cake! Talking with you all night lifted a ton off my chest and pumped me full of positive energy. Even if folk yelled insults in my face on the street right now, I wouldn't flinch—having a royal princess as my bosom friend is armor enough against every rumor in town!"

丫鬟已进来，各自伺候主人穿衣梳洗。
Entering the room, the maids helped each mistress dress and wash up.

千里寻问：“苦荞呢？怎么没见人影？”
Qianli Xun asked, "Where's Kuqiao? Haven't seen her."

夏果边帮小姐穿衣裳边说：“有白风在，苦荞姐姐怎么可能闲着？”
Helping her mistress into her robe, Xiaguo said, "With Bai Feng around, when does Sister Kuqiao ever sit still?"

乔梦容已穿好衣裳，刚坐到梳妆台前，听到夏果提起白风，心想，苦荞不见，说明白风来过，白风既来，定少不了哥哥，难道哥哥来过？
Already dressed and seated at the vanity, Qiao Mengrong froze at the mention of Bai Feng. If Kuqiao was gone, Bai Feng had come—and wherever Bai Feng went, Prince Yi wasn't far behind! Could he be here right now?

她已坐不住，想要起身，见头发不整，急忙说：“喜兰，快帮我收拾，说不定哥哥就在门外。”
Unable to sit still, she started to rise, caught her untidy hair in the mirror, and urged, "Hurry and do my hair, Xilan—Brother might be right outside the gate!"

千里寻笑道：“郡主是长了千里眼吗？一眼能看到门外？”
Qianli Xun laughed: "Since when do you have clairvoyant eyes, Princess, seeing straight through walls?"

“我能感觉到哥哥站在门外。”乔梦容也不知自己何来如此肯定的感觉。
"I can just feel Brother standing right outside." Even Qiao Mengrong couldn't explain where that sudden certainty came from.

喜兰很快为郡主整理完头发。
Xilan pinned up her hair in record time.

乔梦容匆匆起身，快步来到院中，又快步走到院门旁，在心里默默祈祷：“如若哥哥站在门外，说明他心里有我。”
Jumping up, Qiao Mengrong hurried across the yard to the gate, whispering a silent prayer: "If Brother really is standing outside, it proves he has me in his heart."

喜兰要去开门，被乔梦容阻止，她要亲自验证强烈的感觉，要让祈祷成真。
Waving Xilan back from the latch, she meant to test her prayer with her own hands.

乔梦容小心翼翼上前将院门慢慢打开，她很害怕哥哥不在，开门的一刻闭上双眼。门打开后，她的呼吸变得紧张……
Heart pounding, she eased the wooden gate ajar, squeezing her eyes shut for fear of finding the lane empty...

传来喜兰的声音：“王爷，您果真站在门外！”
Xilan's voice rang out: "Your Highness, you really are waiting right outside!"

乔梦容欣喜地睁开双眼，哥哥高大魁梧的身影就在眼前，真实得不能再真实，看来哥哥心中真的有她。
Qiao Mengrong's eyes flew open to find his tall, sturdy figure standing right before her, real as daylight—he really did hold her in his heart!

这一刻，乔梦容埋怨自己不该怀疑哥哥，他若心中没她，为何要站在门外候着？她忍不住又一次抱住哥哥。
Scolding herself for ever doubting him—why else would he wait outside her gate?—she rushed forward and threw her arms around him once more.

“哥哥，你在门外站了多久？”
"How long has Brother been waiting out here?"

没有被心中所想之人拥抱，申简辰倍感失落。最近梦容很爱搂抱他，以后还是少给她搂抱的机会。
Embraced by the wrong woman, emptiness sank into Shen Jianchen's chest. Mengrong was getting way too fond of hugging him lately; he'd have to keep his distance better.

“我……我没站多久，怎么只有你一人起来？幽栖居士难道还睡着？”
"Not... not long. Why are you the only one up? Is Youqi Jushi still asleep?"

“淑真已起床，正在梳妆。不知为何，我感觉到哥哥在门外等我，匆忙出来，哥哥果然在，你说我们是不是心灵相通？”
"Shuzhen's up, just finishing her hair. Somehow I sensed Brother waiting outside, hurried out, and here you are! Doesn't that mean our hearts beat as one?"

申简辰没有回答，他望着院门内，希望看到喜欢的人出现在眼前。
Without answering, Shen Jianchen looked past her into the courtyard, longing for the figure he had waited all morning to see.

瞧了一会儿，她向他走来，申简辰的脸上露出笑容，同时为自己感到悲哀。
A moment later, she came walking out—bringing a smile to Shen Jianchen's lips and a pang of sorrow to his heart.

千里寻来到门口，瞧见佳和郡主搂抱住仪王，而仪王双手耷拉着，没有丝毫拥抱郡主的意思。当她走近时，又瞧见仪王那双直勾勾的眼睛，两只眼珠子都快从眼眶里蹦出来。
Reaching the gate, Qianli Xun saw Princess Jiahe clinging tight to Prince Yi while his arms hung limp at his sides—and the instant she approached, his eyes locked onto her so hard they looked ready to pop from his sockets.

千里寻在心里悄悄批判道：“身在福中不知福！有美人在怀，还敢用那种眼神看老娘！”
Qianli Xun scoffed inside: "Blind to the blessing in his own arms! Holding a beauty and still ogling me like that!"

她边想边走出院门，经过二位身边时，有礼道：“二位忙，我去找文渊。”
Stepping through the gate past them, she bowed with polite distance: "I'll leave you two—off to see Wenyuan."

﻿千里寻说完就要离开。
With that, she turned to go.

“幽栖居士！等等！”申简辰迅速喊道，他将乔梦容扶开，“梦容，我有话与幽栖居士说。”
"Wait, Youqi Jushi!" Shen Jianchen called out at once, easing Qiao Mengrong off his chest. "Mengrong, I have words for Youqi Jushi."

因为欢心，乔梦容很听话：“哥哥尽管说，我回避就是。”
Floating on air, Qiao Mengrong agreed: "Go ahead, Brother—I'll step aside."

“你不用回避，又不是什么秘密。”
"No need to step aside. It isn't a secret."

哥哥的坦诚又使得乔梦容欢心，她点点头。
His openness warmed Qiao Mengrong's heart even more, and she nodded.

仪王真是多事，未婚妻在此，竟然敢公然叫住她！他既然不避讳，千里寻觉得她也没有必要回避，不如趁此机会让郡主知道仪王的真实面目。
What a busybody—calling her back right in front of his fiancée! Since he didn't bother hiding, Qianli Xun saw no reason to either; maybe this was her chance to expose his true colors to the princess!

“仪王有何吩咐？”
"What does Prince Yi want?"

仪王向她靠近几步，又用深情的目光盯着她。
Stepping closer, Prince Yi fixed her with that same deep, ardent gaze.

千里寻瞥一眼仪王身后的佳和郡主，更觉她可怜。她已下定决心，在此时此刻揭露仪王的真面目，刚要说：“仪王，你怎么能当着梦容的面勾引我？”
Glancing at Princess Jiahe behind him, pity surged in Qianli Xun. She made up her mind to blow his cover right here and now—her mouth opened to say, "Prince Yi, how dare you flirt with me right in front of Mengrong?!"

话还未出口，却听仪王说：“幽栖居士恐怕很难再见到文渊。”
Before a syllable came out, Prince Yi spoke: "I'm afraid Youqi Jushi won't be seeing Wenyuan anytime soon."

“什么意思？”对方所说直接将千里寻想要揭露的话语狠狠打回去。
"What do you mean?" His words knocked her accusation straight back down her throat.

“今日一大早，幽栖居士睡得正香时，外公带人前来，将文渊五花大绑带回乔府。”
"At crack of dawn while you were sound asleep, Grandfather showed up with men, trussed Wenyuan up like a bundle, and hauled him back to Qiao Manor."

“什么？怎么会发生这种事？你不是王爷吗？为何不拦着？为何不派人把我叫醒？我去拦也可以！”
"What?! How could that happen?! Aren't you a prince—why didn't you stop him?! Why didn't you send someone to wake me? I would've stopped him myself!"

申简辰第一次见为爱情而痛苦的千里寻，穿越前的她根本不是这样的人，是否会有一天，她也为他如此难过？
For the first time, Shen Jianchen saw Qianli Xun cut raw by love—nothing like the woman he knew before crossing over. Would she ever hurt like this over him?

“我拦过，外公用匕首对着自己的脖子，显然他为此事连性命都不顾，我也无能为力。再说把你唤醒又有何用？难道幽栖居士想觍着脸求乔家娶你？”
"I tried stopping him. Grandfather pressed a dagger to his own neck—he'd die before budging, so my hands were tied. Besides, what good would waking you do? Did Youqi Jushi mean to throw away her pride and beg the Qiao family to marry her?"

申简辰将话说得难听，无非是想让千里寻明白她与文渊已不可能，希望她放下。只有她放下文渊，也许有那么一天，心中才会对他生出情感来。
Shen Jianchen spoke harsh on purpose, hoping to snap her out of illusions about Wenyuan—only if she let Wenyuan go might a spark ever open for him someday.

哥哥的话不入耳，乔梦容担心淑真心中不快，小心批评道：“哥哥怎能如此说淑真？”
Wincing at his harsh tone, Qiao Mengrong chided him soft: "How can Brother talk to Shuzhen like that?"

夏果也很难过，乔公子被五花大绑肯定不好难受，小姐与乔公子没有希望，她的希望也会变得渺茫。
Heartache gripped Xiaguo too—poor Young Master Qiao trussed up in ropes! If Miss's hopes died, hers grew dimmer too.

她见小姐明明难过，却努力控制好情绪，用稍显平静的语气说：“我虽爱文渊，可也不会觍着脸求乔家娶我，只是想不通，我有那么差吗？乔家何至于为拆散我们将文渊五花大绑带走？”
Watching her mistress swallow her grief and keep her voice steady, Xiaguo admired her nerve: "Much as I love Wenyuan, I'd never throw away my pride begging the Qiaos to take me. I just can't fathom it—am I really that awful, that the Qiao family had to tie Wenyuan up just to tear us apart?"

乔梦容走到幽栖居士面前：“淑真，你不差，只是世事弄人。”
Qiao Mengrong stepped up beside her: "Nothing's wrong with you, Shuzhen—it's just cruel fate."

“谢谢你梦容，我该回去。”千里寻很感激佳和郡主，走之前叮嘱仪王，“仪王，您与梦容很幸运，有一纸婚约在手，希望您懂得珍惜眼前人。”
"Thank you, Mengrong. I'd better head home." Grateful for her friend's kindness, Qianli Xun left Prince Yi a pointed parting line: "You and Mengrong are lucky to hold a betrothal in your hands, Prince Yi—treasure the one right in front of you."

千里寻带着夏果向幽林别居外走去，她表情凝重，眼中蓄满泪水，稍不注意便会从脸颊上滚落下来。她在心中默念着：难道我与文渊已不可能？
Leading Xiaguo toward the retreat's gate, Qianli Xun kept her face rigid, eyes brimming with tears one blink away from spilling. "Are Wenyuan and I really finished?" she whispered inside.

她回想昨日的情景，在神柳下，他们互相诉说不会放弃的话。千里寻决定，只要文渊不给她传来想要放弃的讯息，她绝对不会放弃。
Then yesterday beneath the Divine Willow flashed back—their matching vows never to give up. Her jaw set: until Wenyuan himself sent word he was giving up, she would never quit.

千里寻带着伤痛离开，申简辰心中不安：“梦容，我出去一趟。”
Watching Qianli Xun walk away in pain tore at Shen Jianchen: "Mengrong, I have to step out."

“哥哥要去哪里？”
"Where is Brother going?"

“文渊被绑到乔家，我很担心，想去乔家再次劝说外公。”
"I'm worried about Wenyuan hauled back in ropes—I'm going to Qiao Manor to try persuading Grandfather again."

“已到午饭时间，哥哥用完饭再去。”
"It's lunchtime already—eat before you go."

申简辰揪心千里寻，哪里吃得下饭：“少一顿，无妨。”
Sick with worry for Qianli Xun, how could he swallow food? "Missing one meal won't hurt."

刚走出一步，又觉对不起梦容，转过身，走到梦容跟前，将她抱住：“梦容，你放心，哥哥会娶你，会给你一个满意的婚礼。”
Taking a step away, guilt tugged him back toward Mengrong. Turning around, he wrapped his arms around her: "Rest easy, Mengrong—I will marry you and give you a wedding to make you proud."

失忆后的哥哥第一次拥抱她，又一次主动说要娶她的话，简直是意外之喜，一直以来藏在心底的阴云立刻烟消云散，变得阳光明媚。
His very first embrace since waking from amnesia—and another promise to wed her! A windfall of joy swept every lingering cloud from her heart, leaving pure sunshine.

“你就是我的哥哥。”
"That's my Brother!"

申简辰迅速放开梦容，追着千里寻而去。
Releasing her after a beat, Shen Jianchen hurried off after Qianli Xun.

乔梦容还沉浸在喜悦中：“喜兰，你看到吗？哥哥终于主动抱我，他的怀抱既有力又温暖。”
Still floating in bliss, Qiao Mengrong sighed, "Did you see that, Xilan? Brother hugged me on his own at last—his arms are so strong and warm!"

喜兰也替郡主高兴：“奴婢看得清清楚楚，王爷还说要给郡主一个满意的婚礼。”
Xilan beamed for her mistress: "Saw every bit of it, Princess! His Highness even promised you a wedding to make you proud!"

“后天就是中秋节，离明年中秋节还有整整一年，时间过得太慢，真希望明日就能嫁给哥哥。”
"Mid-Autumn is the day after tomorrow—a whole year left until next Mid-Autumn! Time crawls so slow; I wish I could marry Brother tomorrow!"

千里寻懒得寻苦荞，带着夏果上了幽林别居的马车，麻烦车夫送她一程。
With no patience to hunt down Kuqiao, Qianli Xun boarded a Youlin Retreat carriage with Xiaguo and asked the driver to take her home.

申简辰快步走到大门口时，正好碰到白风与苦荞，他叫住白风：“幽栖居士已走，你还带着人家的丫鬟，太不像话！”
Striding up to the front gate, Shen Jianchen caught Bai Feng strolling back with Kuqiao and snapped, "Youqi Jushi already left, and here you are keeping her maid—outrageous!"

白风委屈道：“是王爷让我带苦荞去玩儿的，现在又来怪我。”
Bai Feng protested: "Your Highness told me to take Kuqiao off to play, and now you blame me!"

听到小姐离开，苦荞说：“我不能跟你继续玩儿，要去找我家小姐。”苦荞说着就往外走。
Hearing her mistress had left, Kuqiao cried, "I can't play anymore—I have to catch up to Miss!" and started out the gate.

“别着急，我送你一程。”申简辰拦住苦荞，又吩咐白风，“愣着干什么？还不快去套辆马车，你来赶车。”
"Hold on, I'll give you a ride." Stopping Kuqiao, Shen Jianchen barked at Bai Feng, "What are you standing around for? Go hitch a carriage and drive!"

白风走出大门，很快套好马车，载着王爷与苦荞去追幽栖居士。他感到纳闷，王爷到底是自己追幽栖居士还是为帮苦荞？
Hurrying out, Bai Feng hitched a carriage in no time, driving Prince Yi and Kuqiao after Youqi Jushi—wondering the whole way whether His Highness was chasing Youqi Jushi for himself or helping Kuqiao.

若是王爷追，没有任何道理，若是帮苦荞，还有些道理。可他第一次见一位身份尊贵之人帮一个丫鬟做这种芝麻绿豆大的小事。
Chasing Youqi Jushi made zero sense; helping a maid made slightly more, yet who ever saw a royal prince running tiny errands for a maid?

很快，白风已想通，仪王是个不一样的王爷，在别人看来不可能的事，发生在他身上实属正常。
Before long Bai Feng shrugged it off—Prince Yi was unlike any other prince alive; impossible things were just Tuesday for him.
