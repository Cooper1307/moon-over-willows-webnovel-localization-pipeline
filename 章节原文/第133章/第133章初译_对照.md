第133章 大雪迎来乔家人
## Chapter 133: Heavy Snow Brings the Qiao Elders

时间一点一点过去，已过立冬，寒冬的冷气越发刺骨，大家都换上冬季衣裳。千里寻还是没等到文渊前来，难道仪王没有将她的话转达给文渊？
Days slipped past the Start of Winter, bringing biting cold that sent everyone into heavy winter coats. Still no sign of Wenyuan—had Prince Yi never passed along her message?

白风每隔几日会来望月轩，均是在大门外与苦荞相见。苦荞很懂小姐的心思，几次向白风打探乔公子的情况，白风只说不清楚，王爷已好久没去过乔府。
Every few days Bai Feng stopped outside the gate to see Kuqiao. Reading her mistress's heart, Kuqiao kept fishing for news of Young Master Qiao, only for Bai Feng to shrug that His Highness hadn't visited Qiao Manor in ages.

千里寻从充满期待到慢慢失望，再到努力逼迫自己什么都不要去想。可一点用处都没有，总是自觉或不自觉地想起文渊。
Hope faded to disappointment, and disappointment turned into Qianli Xun ordering herself to stop thinking about him—useless, since Wenyuan drifted straight back into her mind at every turn.

哥嫂来过一次，她试图从哥嫂的口中打探文渊的消息，他们则劝她不要再想乔家公子，其余一概未提。
Her brother and sister-in-law visited once; when she tried fishing for word of Wenyuan, they only urged her to forget him and said not a word more.

七八日未提笔写话本，只因又怕写缠绵悱恻的爱情。既然没有事情可打发时间，为转移内心的痛苦，不如继续动笔，毕竟有那么多听众等着。
For seven or eight days she hadn't touched her huaben script, dreading writing tender love scenes. With empty hours stretching ahead, she forced herself back to the desk to distract herself—and for all those waiting listeners.

千里寻坚持提笔接着上次的内容，写下红香与玄英爱情甜蜜的故事，可写着写着，她竟然改变初衷，给她们之间添加了一段虐恋。
Picking up where she left off on Hongxiang and Xuanying's sweet romance, her brush veered off course on its own, plunging them straight into bitter heartbreak.

听众还未被虐到，她竟边写边流泪，趁着写作，也把积压在内心为文渊为自己流的眼泪通通流出来。
Before a single listener had a chance to weep, tears streamed down Qianli Xun's own cheeks—crying out all the grief she had bottled up for Wenyuan and herself.

苦荞递上丝帕：“小姐今儿写的故事肯定感人，把您都感动得哭了。”
Handing over a silk handkerchief, Kuqiao murmured, "Today's chapter must be a tearjerker, Miss, making even you cry."

千里寻接过丝帕，将脸上和眼睛周围的泪水沾干净：“是感人，我能被自己写的情节感动，听书的人肯定也会被感动。”
Dabbing her wet cheeks and eyes, Qianli Xun managed a nod: "It is. If my own scene makes me cry, listeners will bawl their eyes out too."

夏果掀开帘子，惊喜地喊道：“小姐，下雪了！快出来瞧瞧！”
Pushing aside the door curtain, Xiaguo burst in beaming: "Miss, it's snowing! Come out and see!"

夏果进屋为小姐取下厚披风。
Hurrying inside, Xiaguo reached for her mistress's thick winter cape.

千里寻充满好奇，毕竟是她穿越而来遇到的第一场雪。
Curiosity stirred in Qianli Xun—her very first snowfall since crossing over!

苦荞很机灵，趁夏果不注意，把手稿收起来，从旁边拿本书，装模作样走到书架旁：“小姐别急，我帮您把书放好，再给您拿手炉。”
Quick-witted, Kuqiao swept the manuscript out of sight while Xiaguo's back was turned, grabbed a book to walk casually toward the shelf, and said, "No rush, Miss—let me put this book away and grab your hand warmer."

千里寻会心笑道：“不着急，我等你。”
Qianli Xun tossed her a knowing smile: "Take your time."

当夏果走到书桌跟前时，桌上已无纸张。她平日不怎么关心小姐写些什么，反而喜欢小姐坐在书桌前忙碌，因为这时，她都不用近身伺候，可以拿本书躲在丫鬟房，边读边想象与乔公子在一起的情景。
By the time Xiaguo turned to the desk, not a loose page remained. Never caring what her mistress scribbled, Xiaguo actually loved her desk hours—freeing her to grab a book, hole up in the maids' quarters, and daydream about Young Master Qiao.

“小姐，我把披风给您披上。”
"Let me put your cape on, Miss."

千里寻放下手中的丝帕，站起来，任由夏果为她披好披风。披风是藕粉色，又厚实又暖和，帽子的周围毛茸茸的，摸上去很舒服。
Setting down the handkerchief, Qianli Xun stood as Xiaguo draped the plush lotus-pink cape over her shoulders, its furry hood soft and snug.

苦荞把手炉拿来，递给小姐。
Kuqiao hurried back with a warm brass hand warmer.

千里寻接过，手炉的热气通过手掌传遍全身，顿觉暖洋洋的，暂时将文渊没来之事搁置一边。
Heat radiated straight from her palms through her whole body, easing the chill—and pushing Wenyuan's absence aside for a breath.

“走吧，我们去赏雪。”
"Come on, let's watch the snow."

夏果掀开帘子，随着一股凉气袭来，千里寻已看到外面飘着鹅毛大雪。她迈开脚走出去，站在院中，雪刚下不久，还没将望月轩覆盖。
As Xiaguo lifted the curtain, crisp winter air rushed in along with huge goose-feather snowflakes drifting down. Stepping into the yard, Qianli Xun saw the snow had only just begun, not yet carpeting Wangyue Pavilion white.

苦荞替小姐把披风上的帽子戴好，乖乖地站立一旁，跟夏果欣赏飘落而来的雪花。
Kuqiao pulled the furry hood up over her mistress's head, then stood beside Xiaguo admiring the falling flakes.

千里寻抬起头，望着空中的雪花，大片大片向地面落下；她又看向地面，很多雪花刚落在地上便消失得无影无踪。
Tipping her face skyward, Qianli Xun watched fat flakes float down in sheets, then looked down as flake after flake melted into nothing the instant it kissed the stones.

她慨叹雪花的生命如此短暂，难道它们从天上落下只为消失？
How brief a snowflake's life—drifting down from heaven just to vanish?

她想到自己，穿越前的她已死亡，就像雪花，本以为从天空落下会更精彩，谁知却是奔向死亡，一场地震将她拥有的一切断送。
She thought of herself before crossing over—dead like a melted flake, thinking life was climbing toward its peak only for an earthquake to snuff everything out in a blink.

有幸穿越而来，获得新生，她很珍惜，珍惜到甚至担心是死后的自己做的一场离奇的梦。直到遇见文渊，她才确定不是梦。
Grateful for this second shot at life, she treasured it so fierce she sometimes feared it was only a bizarre dying dream—until meeting Wenyuan made it feel undeniable.

为什么文渊明明已自由，却不来找她？难道一切真的只是梦？如果是梦为什么她看到的如此真切？苦荞和夏果真实不虚，还有眼前飘落的雪花，还有手炉，里面热气满满。
Then why, if Wenyuan walked free now, hadn't he come? Was this all a dream after all? Yet everything felt too real: Kuqiao and Xiaguo beside her, cold flakes brushing her nose, the hot brass hand warmer burning against her palms.

千里寻十分珍惜来之不易的生命，珍惜到想要走好每一步。和离是第一步，走得艰难，结果倒也圆满。与文渊的感情是第二步，本以为万无一失，却困难重重，难到无人能够帮助自己，难到她只能窝在望月轩等待上天掉下一个大大的馅饼……
Treasuring this hard-won life, she wanted every step to count. Her mutual divorce had been step one—rocky, yet a clean victory. Falling for Wenyuan had been step two—looking airtight until walls closed in from every side, leaving her trapped in Wangyue Pavilion waiting on a miracle from the sky...

韦嬷嬷冒雪走来，见自家姑娘站在雪中，批评道：“你们两个丫头，平时我是怎么教你们的？怎能让姑娘在外面待着？小心着凉！”
Striding in through the snow, Matron Wei scolded both maids: "How do I train you two?! Letting Young Lady stand out in the snow—want her catching cold?!"

两个丫鬟笑而不语。
Both maids ducked their heads with sheepish grins.

千里寻边欣赏飘落的雪花，边说：“嬷嬷莫怪她们，是我要出来赏雪。”
Watching flakes whirl down, Qianli Xun said, "Don't scold them, Matron—I wanted to watch the snow."

韦嬷嬷不再批评，倒也没说让姑娘进屋的话：“姑娘，门外来了两个人，你要不要见见？”
Dropping the lecture without dragging her indoors, Matron Wei said, "Two guests are at the gate asking for you, Young Lady—want to see them?"

千里寻不解道：“嬷嬷今日说话奇怪，还没说来人是谁？却问我见不见？”
Qianli Xun blinked: "Odd way to ask, Matron—haven't even told me who they are!"

韦嬷嬷说：“乔家人。”
"The Qiaos."

“难道是文渊？”千里寻充满期待。
"Wenyuan?!" Hope leaped in her chest.

韦嬷嬷为难道：“不是乔家公子，是乔家老爷和夫人。”
Matron Wei hesitated: "Not Young Master Qiao—Master Qiao and Madam Qiao."

千里寻感到失望：“他们来做什么？”
Disappointment dropped heavy in her stomach: "What do they want?"

韦嬷嬷回道：“并未对我说起，只说不见到姑娘今日就不离开。我看未必是好事，姑娘不见也罢，他们想在外面等多久，让他们等去。”
"Wouldn't say—only swore they won't leave today until they see you. Smells like bad news to me; skip seeing them if you want, let them freeze outside as long as they like."

千里寻犹豫片刻：“嬷嬷尽管请他们进来，我在会客厅等着，不管他们为何而来，起码我能得知文渊的近况。”
Pausing a beat, Qianli Xun decided: "Show them into the reception hall, Matron. Whatever they came for, at least I'll hear how Wenyuan is."

夏果听到是乔家来人，充满期待，小姐多久没见到乔公子，她就有多久没见到，她对乔公子的思念并不比小姐差，便与苦荞紧跟小姐来到会客厅。
Hearing Qiao Manor visitors had arrived lit a spark in Xiaguo too—she missed Young Master Qiao every bit as much as Miss did—and she followed close behind with Kuqiao into the reception hall.

到达会客厅，千里寻脱掉披风，放下手炉，正襟危坐。
Slipping off her cape and setting down her hand warmer, Qianli Xun sat upright in the host's seat.

说心里话，她多少有些紧张，要见她的二位差一点成为她的婆婆和公爹，也不知他们前来拜访有何目的？难不成想亲自求她嫁给文渊？
Truth be told, nerves prickled inside her: the two elders coming through that door had almost become her father- and mother-in-law. Why show up now? Surely not to beg her to marry Wenyuan?

千里寻立刻否认了后面的想法，乔家已定下苗知瑜，怎么可能再让文渊娶她？而她又万万不愿给他人当妾室。
She killed that fantasy in half a second—he was already betrothed to Miao Zhiyu, and she'd rather die than be a concubine.

带着忐忑的心情终于等来两位长辈。
Heart thumping, she waited as the two elders approached.

在屏风外，乔守仁与钱氏脱掉厚披风，交给一旁的丫鬟。韦嬷嬷带他们拐过屏风，向里走来。
Behind the entry screen, Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian handed their snowy capes to waiting maids before Matron Wei led them around into the hall.

千里寻犹豫要不要站起来迎接，以显示她是知礼之人，可想到走来的二位切断她与文渊一切往来，气就不打一处来，干脆坐着，拿出主人的款儿来。
Qianli Xun wavered over standing up out of politeness—then remembered these two had severed every thread between her and Wenyuan! Temper flaring, she stayed seated in full host's pride.

谁知乔守仁与钱氏话还未说，竟然跪下。这倒把千里寻吓住，她已坐不住，只好站起来，惊得说不出一句话。
Before a single word left their mouths, Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian dropped straight to their knees! Shock jolted Qianli Xun straight out of her chair, struck speechless.

韦嬷嬷见情势不妙，上前扶道：“二位这是做什么？想折我家姑娘的寿吗？什么话都没说，就跪下？快起来！”
Alarmed, Matron Wei rushed forward to haul them up: "What stunt is this?! Trying to curse our Young Lady's lifespan—kneeling before saying a word?! Get up at once!"

二人不愿起身。
Neither elder budged.

乔守仁说：“我们二人前来，是想请朱家姑娘放了我家文渊，若朱家姑娘不答应，我二人绝不起身。”
Qiao Shouren spoke: "We came to beg Miss Zhu to let our Wenyuan go. Unless Miss Zhu agrees, we will not rise."

韦嬷嬷气道：“二位说的什么话！我家姑娘自搬进望月轩，几乎没出过门，何来放不放过乔公子？再说你们乔家已与苗家定亲，还来求我家姑娘做什么？若再不起来，我可不管什么皇亲国戚，直接拿大棒子赶人！”
Fury exploded in Matron Wei: "What garbage is that?! Since moving to Wangyue Pavilion our Young Lady has barely stepped out the door—who's holding Young Master Qiao hostage?! Your house already engaged him to the Miaos—why come hounding our Young Lady?! Get up right now, imperial kin or not, or I'll grab a broomstick and chase you out!"

钱氏哭道：“呜呜……！我家文渊为了朱家姑娘连命都不要，你说我们不来求她，又能求谁？呜呜……！”
Madam Qian burst into sobs: "Waaah! Our Wenyuan is throwing his own life away over Miss Zhu—who else could we beg?! Waaah!"

千里寻已忍不住，问道：“文渊他……他怎么了？”
Qianli Xun couldn't hold back: "Wenyuan... what happened to him?!"

乔守仁说：“自从与苗家定亲，文渊一直闹着退婚。婚姻大事，岂可由他说了算？我当然不同意。想着他闹一阵儿慢慢会好，谁知近日他开始用绝食逼迫我们，到今天已绝食三日，他的身体怎能受得了！我和他母亲都跪着求他，他说除非朱家姑娘说放弃的话，他才会同意娶苗家姑娘。姑娘，我们两个年纪加起来已超过百岁，跪在这里，求求你，放了我家文渊，否则乔家就要绝后了！”
Qiao Shouren's voice cracked: "Ever since the Miao betrothal, Wenyuan has raised hell demanding we break it off! How can a son dictate his own marriage? Of course I refused. Thought he'd blow off steam and settle down—instead he started starving himself to force our hand! Three full days without food—how can his body take it?! His mother and I even knelt begging him, and he swore only if Miss Zhu herself tells him she's giving up will he marry Miss Miao! Miss Zhu, between us two old bones we're past a hundred years old—kneeling here begging you, spare our Wenyuan, or the Qiao line dies out!"

乔守仁说完，不由老泪纵横，钱氏更是哭得伤心。
Old tears streamed down Qiao Shouren's weathered cheeks as Madam Qian wept harder still.

千里寻此刻才明白，文渊没来找她，不是因他放弃她，而是正在用他的方式努力挣扎，争取他们的未来。千里寻立刻原谅了文渊，更加心疼他的付出。
Clarity crashed over Qianli Xun: Wenyuan hadn't abandoned her—he had been fighting for their future in the only way he knew! Every ounce of blame dissolved into raw heartache for him.

“你们起来吧，被长辈跪，我承受不起。”
"Please stand up. Having elders kneel to me is more than I can bear."

乔守仁坚持道：“姑娘若不答应放了文渊，我二人就长跪不起！”
Qiao Shouren held firm: "Until Miss Zhu promises to let Wenyuan go, we stay on our knees!"

本以为世上最可怕的是刀剑，今日才领略到守礼的话语和行为才真正逼得人无路可退。
She had thought blades were the deadliest weapons in this era—only now did she learn polite humility and filial duty could back a person further into a corner than any sword.

韦嬷嬷生气道：“你们二人如此逼迫我家姑娘，是何道理？乔家公子绝食是乔家的事，与我家姑娘何干？你们还是回去好好劝说自家儿子，赶紧跟苗家成亲，不要再想着我家姑娘，什么皇亲国戚，我家姑娘可不稀罕！”
Matron Wei snapped in rage: "Bullying our Young Lady like this—where's the justice?! Your son starving himself is Qiao family business, nothing to do with our Young Lady! Go home and lecture your own son to marry Miss Miao and forget us—who cares about your imperial-relative prestige?!"

钱氏哭着说：“呜呜……！嬷嬷别用这话噎我们，朱家姑娘不答应放了文渊，我与老爷绝不起来，呜呜……！哪怕你用大棒子打我们，也绝不起来！呜呜……！”
Madam Qian wailed: "Waaah! Save your lectures, Matron—until Miss Zhu lets Wenyuan go, Master and I won't rise! Beat us with clubs and we still won't rise! Waaah!"

本以为等文渊半年太短，才过两个多月，情势逼得她不得不放弃。
Half a year had felt too short; barely two months in, fate had forced her hand.

想嫁给文渊已不可能，除乔家与苗家订婚一事阻挡外，乔家长辈已从心里对她生起的怨气，也令千里寻不敢向前踏出半步。爱依然在，面对现实，不得不放弃。
Marrying Wenyuan was dead—not just because of the Miao engagement, but because the bitter grudge these Qiao elders now bore against her left zero room to step forward. Love still burned, yet reality demanded she let go.

她不想在乔家人面前表现得痛苦难过，努力平复心情：“若二位再跪下去，我绝不答应放弃文渊。”
Refusing to show a crack of pain in front of the Qiaos, she steadied her breath: "Stay on your knees one second longer, and I swear I will never let Wenyuan go."

钱氏立刻停止哭声，与乔守仁四目相对，互相搀扶着爬起来。
Madam Qian choked off mid-sob, traded a glance with Qiao Shouren, and both elders scrambled to help each other up.
