## Chapter 335: Princess Consort Yi Perishes in Yanjing

Watching the lone Song general fight like a cornered tiger against swarming Jin spearmen until a lance pierced his back, Qianli Xun and Shen Jianchen dropped straight from the eaves, blasted the attackers clear, and bounded away across the roofs carrying the bleeding hero—spotting **Wanyan Chi** and **Lu Yuanfeng** galloping up behind Wanyan Zongwang to lead the pursuit!

Returning briefly to the abandoned house to send Bai Feng rushing Mengrong and Xilan back to the inn via a side alley, they barely caught breath before Jin boots pounded outside.

Down the twisting alley, Bai Feng slaughtered one blocking squad of seven or eight Jin troops—only to hit a fresh wall of soldiers at the far mouth! Screaming for Xilan to run with paralyzed Mengrong, Bai Feng fought until hacked down bleeding across the cobbles. Seconds later a Jin blade ran straight through Xilan's back (*"Run, Your Highness!"*), leaving weeping Mengrong cornered, hauled into an empty house, and tossed onto a wooden cot as two leering Jin soldiers brawled over who went first—until the lower-ranked soldier stabbed his superior in the back!

Staring at the bloodied sword left on the table as flashes of Liang Shicheng's needle and her wedding-day blade tore across her mind, Mengrong stepped calmly off the cot, lifted the steel with a soft smile—*"Brother, Mengrong is coming to find you..."*—and drove it straight into her own belly, dying clean before the soldier turned around.

Dragging himself bleeding down the alley, Bai Feng collapsed sobbing over Xilan's corpse, then found Mengrong lifeless inside the empty house.

Meanwhile, bounding out the abandoned house's back wall with the wounded Song commander, Qianli Xun and Shen Jianchen cleared one Jin squad—only for a whistling arrow to slam straight through the commander's heart mid-leap!
