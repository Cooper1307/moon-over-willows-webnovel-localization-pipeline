## Chapter 286: Clues Buried Beneath Fallen Petals

After Prince Yi left the country villa the previous night, Wen Xin had questioned the whole staff while eyeing a thick camphor tree outside the locked bedroom—learning gardeners always swept fallen petals around its trunk as fertilizer.

One constable then uncovered a goldmine: a young maid named Aye had skipped the city transfer because she'd taken leave to visit her sick mother—and had inexplicably returned two days late!

Summoning Aye and the estate steward yielded only nervous stammering—until Wen Xin probed the dirt beneath the camphor petals with his scabbard and unearthed a tiny carved wooden peach-heart charm on a faded string! Holding it up before the entire assembled staff, another maid recognized it instantly as Aye's lost keepsake.

Cornered by Wen Xin's sharp reconstruction—returning after dark to an empty house, stumbling onto something terrifying, ducking behind the camphor tree, and dropping her charm—Aye collapsed to her knees confessing everything!

Kneeling before Shen Jianchen in the Judicial Office that morning, Aye spilled the entire night: returning at dusk to find the manor deserted and the steward passed out drunk, she had crept toward the bedroom hoping to free Haoyu's captive—only to see Scarface walk in and hear the girl screaming! Hiding behind the camphor tree, she watched Scarface haul the girl out in a sack—and moments later saw a *second* man step out of that exact room, head and face wholly shrouded in a black cloak!

"Could it have been Second Young Master Haoyu?" Shen Jianchen pressed.

"Never! Second Young Master never hides his face when he brings girls here—always brazen as daylight! Had to be someone else!" Ready to clear an innocent man even though she disliked Haoyu's old ways, Aye agreed to testify in open court.
