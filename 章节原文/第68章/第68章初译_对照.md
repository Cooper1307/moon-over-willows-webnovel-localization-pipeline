# 第68章 甜蜜约会定私情

---

千里寻朝相约的望景亭而去，快要到达亭子时，看到文渊已站在亭中，一旁的南双手中提着食盒。

Qianli Xun headed toward the agreed-upon Wangjing Pavilion. As she approached, she saw Wenyuan already standing inside the pavilion, with Nanshuang holding a food carrier beside him.

---

食盒勾起腹内的馋虫，千里寻更感到饥饿，依然忍住，心里喜道：“没想到文渊也提前到了，还这么贴心，给我带来吃的。”

The food carrier stirred up her appetite, making Qianli Xun feel even hungrier. She suppressed it, thinking happily: "I didn't expect Wenyuan to arrive early too. How thoughtful of him to bring me food."

---

她想象着食盒中的美味，悄悄躲在离亭子不远的大石后面，石头不算太高，挡不住视线，不影响她观察文渊。

Imagining the delicacies inside the food carrier, she quietly hid behind a large rock not far from the pavilion. The rock wasn't too tall, so it didn't block her view or prevent her from observing Wenyuan.

---

苦荞边乐边跟着小姐躲起来。

Kuqiao followed her young lady with a smile, hiding along with her.

---

文渊背对着大石，从那背影能清晰地感受到他又兴奋又焦急又期待的心情。

Wenyuan had his back to the rock. From his posture, she could clearly sense his mixture of excitement, anxiety, and anticipation.

---

千里寻看了一会儿，实在坚持不下去，肚子咕咕叫，加之想要早些与文渊说话，便从大石后面现身，朝那背影唤了声：“文渊！”

After watching for a while, Qianli Xun couldn't hold out any longer. Her stomach grumbled, and she wanted to talk to Wenyuan sooner, so she stepped out from behind the rock and called out to his figure: "Wenyuan!"

---

乔文仙兴奋地转过身，上前拉起她的手：“淑真，没想到我们两个都早来了一些。”

Qiao Wenxian turned around excitedly, stepped forward, and took her hand: "Shuzhen, I didn't expect both of us to arrive a bit early."

---

苦荞心想：“哪里是一些？我家小姐可是早到了一个时辰！”

Kuqiao thought to herself: "A bit early? My young lady arrived a whole two hours early!"

---

千里寻被文渊拉到亭中，乖乖坐在石桌跟前。

Qianli Xun was pulled into the pavilion by Wenyuan and obediently sat down at the stone table.

---

“我怕你匆忙赶来，在家没吃饱，专门买来三种小吃，还热着呢。”乔文仙转向南双，“把小吃和桂花酿摆上。”

"I was worried you might rush here without eating properly at home, so I specially bought three kinds of snacks. They're still warm." Qiao Wenxian turned to Nanshuang: "Set out the snacks and osmanthus wine."

---

南双把食盒放在桌上，打开，将三盘小吃摆上，又拿出一瓶桂花酿，再摆上酒杯，还有两个瓷盘和筷子。

Nanshuang placed the food carrier on the table, opened it, and set out three plates of snacks. He then took out a bottle of osmanthus wine, along with wine cups, two porcelain plates, and chopsticks.

---

苦荞瞅一眼精美的食物，再看看手里一直拿的布袋，叹道：“糕点白带了，还是乔公子厉害。”

Kuqiao glanced at the exquisite food, then looked at the cloth bag in her hand, and sighed: "I brought pastries for nothing. Young Master Qiao is really something else."

---

面前的三盘小吃，千里寻觉得甚是好看，其中一道大约能猜出名字：“这道应是糯米藕，其它两道我不知名字，你能否告诉我？”

Looking at the three plates of snacks before her, Qianli Xun found them quite beautiful. She could roughly guess the name of one dish: "This should be glutinous rice lotus root. I don't know the names of the other two. Could you tell me?"

---

乔文仙想起淑真失忆之事，坐下耐心解释：“是桂花糯米藕，这道叫葱花肉，这道是刺毛肉圆。”

Remembering Shuzhen's amnesia, Qiao Wenxian sat down and patiently explained: "This is osmanthus glutinous rice lotus root. This one is called scallion pork, and this one is fuzzy meatball."

---

“名字好，颜色也好，形状更好，味道肯定不错。”千里寻拿起筷子就要吃，想起花了心思的妆容，总得让文渊瞧瞧再说，她放下筷子，“文渊，你有没有觉得我今日有什么不同？”

"Great names, great colors, and even greater shapes. The taste must be wonderful too." Qianli Xun picked up her chopsticks, about to eat, but remembered her carefully applied makeup. She should let Wenyuan see it first. She put down her chopsticks: "Wenyuan, do you notice anything different about me today?"

---

乔文仙仔细瞧着朱淑真装扮娇美的容颜，他看得呆住……

Qiao Wenxian carefully looked at Zhu Shuzhen's beautifully adorned face. He was transfixed...

---

南双已将食盒收拾好，苦荞拉起他走到亭子外面。

Nanshuang had already tidied up the food carrier. Kuqiao pulled him outside the pavilion.

---

南双埋怨道：“苦荞姐姐为何要拉我出来？”

Nanshuang complained: "Why did you pull me out, Sister Kuqiao?"

---

“没看到我家小姐跟你家公子那眼神，亲密着呢，你我还是躲远些，别影响他们。”

"Didn't you see the look between my young lady and your young master? They're being intimate. We should stay away and not disturb them."

---

南双这才醒悟过来：“还是苦荞姐姐心细。”

Only then did Nanshuang realize: "Sister Kuqiao is really thoughtful."

---

苦荞打开布袋：“我带的糕点与你家公子带的吃食比起来差远了，我家小姐肯定不会再稀罕，还是我们两个享用吧。”

Kuqiao opened the cloth bag: "The pastries I brought are nothing compared to what your young master brought. My young lady won't care about them anymore. Let's enjoy them ourselves."

---

南双把食盒放在地上，直接伸手从布袋里取一块糕点，咬了一口：“朱府的糕点实在好吃，多给我吃几块。”

Nanshuang put the food carrier on the ground and directly reached into the cloth bag to take a pastry. He took a bite: "The pastries from the Zhu residence are really delicious. Give me a few more."

---

“放心，够你吃的。”苦荞边吃边瞧向亭子内，小姐与乔公子看上去真像一对神仙眷侣。

"Don't worry, there's enough for you to eat." Kuqiao ate while looking toward the pavilion. Her young lady and Young Master Qiao looked like a match made in heaven.

---

被文渊盯着，千里寻感到脸上发烫，她竟然不知不觉红了脸：“你为何盯着我看？”

Being stared at by Wenyuan made Qianli Xun feel her face burning. She had actually blushed without realizing it: "Why are you staring at me?"

---

“因为你好看。”

"Because you're beautiful."

---

“你有没有听过'女为己悦者容'？”

"Have you ever heard of 'a woman adorns herself for the one who delights in her'?"

---

乔文仙温柔地提醒道：“是'女为悦己者容'。”

Qiao Wenxian gently corrected: "It's 'a woman adorns herself for the one who delights in her.'"

---

“我给改了，'悦己'不如'己悦'，试想女子若不喜欢男子，又怎会为他梳妆打扮？文渊便是我心悦之人，故而，我一早起来梳妆打扮，只为见己悦之人。”

“I've changed it. 'Being delighted in' is not as good as 'delighting in someone.' Think about it—if a woman doesn't like a man, why would she dress up for him? Wenyuan is the one my heart delights in. So I got up early this morning to dress up, just to see the one who delights me.”

---

“你怎样我都喜欢。”

"I like you no matter how you look."

---

“难道我蓬头垢面，眼角带着眼屎，你也喜欢？”

"Even if I were unkempt and disheveled, with eye boogers in the corners of my eyes, you'd still like me?"

---

乔文仙被逗乐：“一来你绝不会蓬头垢面；二来，你即使不特意装扮依旧貌美；三来你我若成婚，每天清晨起床，你眼角的眼屎我定会看到，你既是我的人，那眼屎也是我的，我岂有嫌弃之理？”

Qiao Wenxian was amused: "For one thing, you'd never let yourself look unkempt. For another, you're beautiful even without trying. And for a third, if we were married, every morning when we wake up, I'd definitely see the eye boogers in the corners of your eyes. Since you're mine, even your eye boogers are mine. How could I possibly find them disgusting?"

---

“没想到你这么会说情话，会不会哄过很多女孩子？”

"I didn't expect you to be so good at sweet talk. Have you coaxed many girls?"

---

“我只心仪过你一人。”

"You're the only one I've ever been fond of."

---

千里寻更害羞，低下头，目光接触到美食，肚子又开始叫唤，既然自己什么样文渊都喜欢，又何必在乎涂得恰到好处的唇脂，还是开吃吧。

Qianli Xun became even more shy. She lowered her head. Her gaze met the food, and her stomach started grumbling again. Since Wenyuan liked her no matter how she looked, why worry about the perfectly applied lip rouge? Time to eat.

---

她拿起瓶子，给文渊和自己面前的杯子各倒些桂花酿：“你陪我一起吃。”

She picked up the bottle and poured some osmanthus wine into both Wenyuan's cup and her own: "Eat with me."

---

千里寻先拿起筷子吃了起来，边吃便夸赞：“不错！很好吃！”

Qianli Xun picked up her chopsticks and started eating, praising as she ate: "Delicious! Really good!"

---

乔文仙品着桂花酿，面带微笑，瞧着心爱的女子，她的吃相实在不像一位大家闺秀，可贵在真实、可爱。

Qiao Wenxian sipped his osmanthus wine with a smile, looking at his beloved. Her eating manner was truly unlike that of a refined young lady, but what was valuable was her authenticity and cuteness.

---

“你怎么不吃？”千里寻边吃边问。

"Why aren't you eating?" Qianli Xun asked while eating.

---

乔文仙放下酒杯：“我吃过了，要是你吃不完，我会吃你剩下的。”

Qiao Wenxian put down his wine cup: "I've already eaten. If you can't finish, I'll eat your leftovers."

---

“你竟然愿意吃我剩下的？”

"You're actually willing to eat my leftovers?"

---

乔文仙没有回答，拿起筷子，夹了一个刺毛肉圆，送到她口边：“咬一大口。”

Qiao Wenxian didn't answer. He picked up his chopsticks, picked up a fuzzy meatball, and brought it to her lips: "Take a big bite."

---

千里寻张开嘴巴，咬了大大一口，幸福地嚼着。

Qianli Xun opened her mouth, took a big bite, and chewed happily.

---

文渊的筷子中只夹着小小的一块儿，只见他毫不犹豫放入口中，津津有味的吃着。

Wenyuan only had a tiny piece on his chopsticks. Without hesitation, he put it in his mouth and ate with relish.

---

千里寻立刻断定，文渊是可与她相伴终生之人。她咽下口中的食物：“文渊，我想嫁给你。”

Qianli Xun immediately concluded that Wenyuan was the one she could spend her life with. She swallowed the food in her mouth: "Wenyuan, I want to marry you."

---

突如其来的表白和肯定，乔文仙喜出望外，他抬起一只手，抓住淑真纤细而又柔软的手指：“我此生非你不娶，今日回去，我就向爹娘表明，让他们到朱府提亲。”

The sudden confession and affirmation took Qiao Wenxian by surprise. He raised one hand and grasped Shuzhen's slender and soft fingers: "I will marry no one but you in this life. Today, when I return, I'll make it clear to my parents and have them come to the Zhu residence to propose."

---

“我是不是有点太着急？”

"Am I being too hasty?"

---

“不是你太着急，是我。你刚刚接受我，可我对你早已有情，又怎能不着急？我要趁你喜欢我，把你娶进门，让你成为我的人，省得整日担心你被别人抢走。”

“It's not you who's hasty—it's me. You've just accepted me, but I've had feelings for you for a long time. How could I not be hasty? I want to marry you while you like me, make you mine, so I don't have to worry all day about someone else stealing you away.”

---

千里寻担忧地问：“你的家人会不会嫌弃我离过一次婚？”

Qianli Xun asked worriedly: "Will your family mind that I've been divorced once?"

---

“家人嫌弃不重要，重要的是我不嫌弃，即使家人嫌弃，我也会想方设法说服他们。”

"It doesn't matter if my family minds. What matters is that I don't mind. Even if they do, I'll find a way to persuade them."

---

文渊的态度很肯定，千里寻自然对他充满信任：“文渊，有你真好，我以后不会觉得心无所依，也不会觉得不踏实，更不会觉得我与这个时代没有关系。”

Wenyuan's attitude was very certain. Qianli Xun naturally trusted him completely: "Wenyuan, it's so good to have you. From now on, I won't feel adrift, I won't feel unsettled, and I won't feel like I have no connection to this era."

---

乔文仙虽不大理解淑真为何会觉得她与这个时代没有关系，依然关切地说：“放心，我会永远陪在你身边。”

Although Qiao Wenxian didn't quite understand why Shuzhen felt she had no connection to this era, he still said with concern: "Don't worry, I'll always be by your side."

---

他们又深情对视了一会儿，千里寻才抽回手，继续享用美食。她把三盘食物吃得精光，没有给文渊留下吃剩食的机会，最后又喝了三杯桂花酿，才算真正享用完毕。

They gazed at each other affectionately for a while longer before Qianli Xun withdrew her hand and continued enjoying the food. She finished all three plates completely, not leaving Wenyuan any chance to eat the leftovers. Finally, she drank three more cups of osmanthus wine before truly finishing her meal.

---

乔文仙看到她嘴角沾有食物，拿出丝帕，为她轻轻擦去。

Qiao Wenxian saw food stuck at the corner of her mouth. He took out a silk handkerchief and gently wiped it away for her.

---

千里寻安心享受完文渊的照顾，建议道：“带我去坐船吧。”

After comfortably enjoying Wenyuan's care, Qianli Xun suggested: "Take me boating."

---

乔文仙点头答应。

Qiao Wenxian nodded in agreement.

---

糕点已吃完，苦荞提醒道：“他们吃完了，过去收拾吧。”

The pastries were finished. Kuqiao reminded: "They're done eating. Let's go clean up."

---

两个下人又进入亭中，将盘子、桂花酿等物品收拾到食盒中，目睹两位主人牵起对方的手，朝湖边租船的方向走去。

The two servants entered the pavilion again, collecting the plates, osmanthus wine, and other items into the food carrier. They watched their two masters take each other's hands and walk toward the lake to rent a boat.

---

他们租了一条不大不小的船只，上面有一个小房屋，可容一人休息。最惬意的莫过于外面放着圆形桌凳，可坐在那里边饮酒边赏美景。

They rented a medium-sized boat with a small cabin on it that could accommodate one person for rest. The most pleasant part was the round table and stools outside, where one could sit, drink, and enjoy the beautiful scenery.

---

乔文仙吩咐南双把剩下的桂花酿拿出来。

Qiao Wenxian instructed Nanshuang to bring out the remaining osmanthus wine.

---

南双照办，又摆上桂花酿和酒杯。

Nanshuang complied, setting out the osmanthus wine and wine cups again.

---

千里寻与乔文仙在桌旁坐下，南双与苦荞则坐在船尾。

Qianli Xun and Qiao Wenxian sat at the table, while Nanshuang and Kuqiao sat at the stern of the boat.

---

划船的人启动船只，在水的波动中，船只缓缓向水中行进，徜徉于水中的感觉十分美妙。

The boatman started the boat. Amid the rippling water, the vessel slowly moved forward into the lake. The feeling of drifting on the water was truly wonderful.

---

文渊将桂花酿给二人斟满，端起酒杯与心爱的女子互相碰杯后喝下。

Wenyuan filled both their cups with osmanthus wine. He raised his cup, clinked it with his beloved's cup, and they both drank.

---

此时，千里寻的内心比口中的桂花酿还要甜美。

At that moment, Qianli Xun's heart was even sweeter than the osmanthus wine in her mouth.
