## 三、具体问题与修订建议

### 高优先级

#### 1. "女为己悦者容"逻辑表达优化
- **原译**："I've changed it. 'Delighting in oneself' is not as good as 'oneself delighting.'"
- **问题**：逻辑关系不够清晰，读者可能难以理解"悦己"与"己悦"的区别
- **建议**："I've changed it. 'Being delighted in' is not as good as 'delighting in someone.' Think about it—if a woman doesn't like a man, why would she dress up for him?"
- **理由**：更清晰地表达主动与被动的逻辑差异

#### 2. "一来...二来...三来..."结构优化
- **原译**："First, you would never be unkempt and disheveled. Second, even without special makeup, you're still beautiful. Third, if we were married..."
- **问题**：结构略显生硬，缺乏流畅感
- **建议**："For one thing, you'd never let yourself look unkempt. For another, you're beautiful even without trying. And for a third, if we were married..."
- **理由**：更口语化，符合情话的自然流露

#### 3. "shichen"处理
- **原译**："a whole shichen early"
- **问题**：未解释时间单位，西方读者可能困惑
- **建议**："a whole two hours early"
- **理由**：转换为现代时间单位，更直观

### 中优先级

#### 4. "food carrier"重复使用
- **问题**：本章"food carrier"出现多次，略显重复
- **建议**：部分替换为"tiered food box"或直接说"the food"
- **理由**：增加词汇多样性

#### 5. "osmanthus wine"重复
- **问题**：同样重复出现
- **建议**：部分替换为"the sweet wine"或"the fragrant wine"
- **理由**：避免单调

#### 6. "prickly meatball"可优化
- **原译**："prickly meatball"
- **问题**："prickly"可能让读者联想到"多刺的"而非"毛茸茸的"
- **建议**："fuzzy meatball"或"rice-crusted meatball"
- **理由**：更准确传达外观特征

### 低优先级

#### 7. "divine couple"可更自然
- **原译**："looked like a perfect divine couple"
- **建议**："looked like a match made in heaven"
- **理由**：更地道的英文表达

#### 8. 部分对话可更简洁
- **例**："I didn't expect you to be so good at sweet talk. Have you coaxed many girls?"
- **建议**："You're surprisingly good at sweet talk. Have you done this before?"
- **理由**：更口语化，节奏更快
