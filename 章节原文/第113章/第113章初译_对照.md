第113章 陈家上线弄风云
## Chapter 113: Enter the House of Chen

天色已晚，哥哥未归，乔梦容难以入眠，一直坐在闺房内等待。她知哥哥是去帮助乔公子，可这么晚未归，会不会在外面被哪位女子绊住？毕竟临安的姑娘个个长得水灵。
Night grew deep with no sign of Prince Yi returning, and Qiao Mengrong could not sleep a wink, waiting in her chamber. She knew he had gone to help Young Master Qiao, yet staying out this late—what if some girl in town had caught his eye? Lin'an was full of fresh, pretty young women.

一顿胡思乱想，搞得内心难以平静，越不平静越担心，越担心越不平静，她坐立难安。
Her thoughts spiraled, stirring restlessness that fed deeper worry, leaving her pacing on pins and needles.

直到传来白风的声音：“佳和郡主，仪王来看您。”
At last Bai Feng's voice sounded outside: "Princess Jiahe, Prince Yi is here to see you."

等不及喜兰开门，乔梦容急忙推开木门。哥哥出现在面前，她像很久没见到哥哥似的，扑进他的怀里：“哥哥出去许久，害我担心不已。”
Before Xilan could reach the door, Qiao Mengrong flung it wide. Seeing him standing there, she threw herself into his arms as if they had been parted for months: "Brother was gone so long—you worried me to death!"

真是怕什么来什么，申简辰越担心乔梦容抱住他，越偏偏被她抱住，他还没有任何拒绝的理由。
What he dreaded most always came true: the more he feared Qiao Mengrong hugging him, the faster she rushed into his arms, leaving him zero excuse to push her away.

“我一个大男人，又有功夫，不会有事，以后不许再瞎担心。”
"I'm a grown man who knows martial arts—nothing's going to happen to me. Quit worrying over nothing."

乔梦容继续抱住哥哥，不愿放开：“哥哥以后不管多晚出去，都带上我，好吗？”
Clinging tight, Qiao Mengrong refused to let go: "From now on, no matter how late Brother goes out, take me along, all right?"

申简辰岂能愿意？
How could Shen Jianchen agree to that?

“我出去办事，带上你十分不便，我答应你，只要是逛街，都带上你。”
"Bringing you along on errands isn't convenient. I'll promise you this, though: whenever I go strolling the streets, I'll take you."

哥哥没有一口回绝，乔梦容很欣慰：“哥哥说话算话。”
Relieved he hadn't turned her down flat, Qiao Mengrong smiled: "Brother keeps his word."

“当然。”申简辰不想再让梦容抱下去，故意说，“在外面被文渊抱，现在又被你抱，难道大家都觉得我身板硬朗，想把我当大树一样靠靠？可我这棵大树也会被人靠累。”
"Of course." Eager to slip out of her embrace, Shen Jianchen teased, "First Wenyuan hugs me outside, and now you're hugging me in here—does everyone think my frame is sturdy as a big tree to lean on? Even a big tree gets tired of being leaned on."

乔梦容放开哥哥，害羞道：“我抱哥哥跟乔公子不一样，我是要嫁给哥哥的人。”
Qiao Mengrong released him with a shy blush: "Me hugging Brother isn't the same as Young Master Qiao—I'm the woman going to marry you."

乔梦容总是强调婚约，好像害怕他忘掉似的。申简辰能理解，可并不喜欢听，因每次听到，会令他觉得人生黯淡无光。
She kept bringing up their engagement as if terrified he might forget. Shen Jianchen understood why, yet hearing it always seemed to drain the color out of his life.

“梦容，我很累，想回屋休息，你也尽快休息吧。”
"I'm beat, Mengrong—I need to get some sleep. Turn in soon too."

哥哥说完，转身离开。
With that, he turned and left.

乔梦容站在院内，盯着哥哥离开的背影，直到那背影消失在眼前，才失落地问：“喜兰，有没有觉得哥哥不喜欢我提到婚约？”
Standing in the yard watching his back vanish into the dark, Qiao Mengrong murmured in low spirits, "Xilan, doesn't it feel like Brother hates it whenever I bring up our engagement?"

“郡主多虑，王爷出去得久，肯定是累了。”
"Princess is overthinking things—His Highness was out late and must just be worn out."

“你说得有些道理。”
"You have a point."

第二天一早，陈浩宇爬起床，在永宁居用过早饭，想到知府大人调查谣言传播一事，打算先回陈府一趟，再去朱府找姐姐打探消息。
First thing the next morning, Chen Haoyu crawled out of bed, ate breakfast at Yongning Residence, and—mindful of the Prefect hunting the rumor-mongers—decided to drop by Chen Manor first before visiting his sister at Zhu Manor to fish for news.

陈家祖上无人当官，生意倒做得遍布各地，作为临安首富，财力不是一般富裕家庭能媲美。
No ancestor of the Chen clan had ever held public office, yet their businesses spanned the empire; as the wealthiest household in Lin'an, their fortune dwarfed ordinary rich families.

到了这一辈，陈家兄弟共三人，老大陈江林，老二陈江树，老三陈江木。陈家的家业主要由老大陈江林主管，老二老三辅佐。
In this generation stood three Chen brothers: the eldest, Chen Jianglin; the second, Chen Jiangshu; and the third, Chen Jiangmu. Chen Jianglin ran the family empire, assisted by his two younger brothers.

陈浩宇和陈文心乃陈江林所出，陈江树也有一儿子名唤陈浩东，老三陈江木也有一子，名叫陈浩飞。
Chen Haoyu and Chen Wenxin were Chen Jianglin's children; Chen Jiangshu had a son named Chen Haodong; and Chen Jiangmu had a son named Chen Haofei.

陈家年纪最大的便是夏氏夏老太太，已过花甲之年，在家中养尊处优，享受天伦之乐。她最心疼浩宇，只因浩宇长相酷似爷爷，老人把对死去老伴儿的情全部寄托在孙子身上，宠得陈浩宇纨绔异常。
The eldest soul in the house was Old Madam Xia, past sixty and living in pampered comfort. She doted on Haoyu above all others because he was the spitting image of her late husband, pouring all her love for her departed spouse onto her grandson until she spoiled him into an incorrigible rake.

按照往常的做派，陈浩宇能不着家就不着家，考虑到知府大人查传播谣言之人，担心查到自己头上，干脆近期老实些，回家留个好印象，一旦查出，还有家人出面说合。
Normally Chen Haoyu avoided home like the plague, yet with the Prefect investigating the rumors, he figured he'd better play the dutiful son at home for a stretch to build goodwill—just in case the trail reached him and he needed family cover.

全临安城的人都知道，陈家的府邸最大，里面的建筑和布置，在临安没有哪家能比得上。
Everyone in Lin'an knew Chen Manor was the largest estate in town, its halls and gardens unmatched by any other family.

陈浩宇一进陈府大门，碰见的丫鬟小厮都高兴地打招呼：“公子终于回来！”
The second Chen Haoyu stepped inside Chen Manor's gate, maids and page boys beamed in greeting: "Young Master is home at last!"

陈浩宇只调侃丫鬟：“几日不见，又变漂亮！”
Chen Haoyu only flirted with the maids: "Gone a few days and you've gotten even prettier!"

丫鬟既高兴又不好意思。
The maids giggled, flattered yet shy.

正往母亲姚轻琰房中走去，碰见贴身小厮青瓜。
Heading toward his mother Yao Qingyan's courtyard, he ran right into his personal page boy, Qinggua.

说是贴身小厮，实则不然，陈浩宇只允许他在陈府内跟着伺候，不准走出陈府以外。就连名字青瓜也是陈浩宇随口起的，只因他进府时，陈浩宇手中正好拿着一根青瓜在吃。
Personal page boy in name only—Chen Haoyu only let him tag along inside Chen Manor, never beyond the front gate. Even the name Qinggua ("Cucumber") had been slapped on him on a whim because Chen Haoyu happened to be crunching a cucumber when the lad entered service.

“公子可回来！我终于有人可伺候！”
"Young Master is back! I have someone to wait on at last!"

“你怎么这么贱！就喜欢伺候人？”
"Why are you such a glutton for punishment? Love waiting on people that much?"

“别人我不稀罕，只喜欢伺候公子。”
"Nobody else—I only like waiting on Young Master!"

“别以为我不知道，爹娘把你安插在我身边，是想让你盯着我。”
"Don't think I don't know Father and Mother planted you beside me to spy on me."

“公子误会，小的虽是老爷和夫人安排，心却在公子身上，只求公子以后出去带上我，我也想跟着公子出去玩玩。”
"Young Master wrongs me! Master and Madam placed me here, sure, yet my heart belongs to Young Master. Just take me along when you go out—I want to go have fun with you too!"

陈浩宇站住：“可是真心话？”
Chen Haoyu halted. "Mean that?"

青瓜也站住：“比真金白银还真。”
Qinggua stopped too. "Truer than solid gold and silver!"

“既如此，你还在陈府跟着我。”陈浩宇继续往姚轻琰房中而去。
"Then keep following me around Chen Manor." Chen Haoyu strode on toward Yao Qingyan's rooms.

青瓜失落道：“说半天，公子还是不愿我跟您出去。”
Qinggua slumped: "All that talk and Young Master still won't let me go out with you."

陈浩宇并不理会，继续往里走。
Ignoring him, Chen Haoyu kept walking.

陈府很大，姚轻琰住的院子虽是主院，可从大门进入，还需走一段距离。
Chen Manor was vast; even though Yao Qingyan lived in the main courtyard, reaching it from the front gate took quite a trek.

走着走着，碰到二伯的儿子陈浩东。
Halfway there, he bumped into his second uncle's son, Chen Haodong.

陈浩宇主动打招呼：“东哥要去哪里？”
Chen Haoyu greeted him first: "Where are you off to, Brother Dong?"

“浩宇回来了。”陈浩东招呼道，然后把他拉到一边，“你整日混在外面，吃喝玩乐，听说还去赌坊，小心大伯揍你。”
"Haoyu's home!" Returning the greeting, Chen Haodong pulled him aside. "Out carousing day and night, even hitting gambling dens—watch out Eldest Uncle doesn't tan your hide!"

“东哥放心，我是什么样，我爹又不是不知道，他已习惯，根本不会当回事。”
"Relax, Brother Dong. Father knows how I am—he's used to it and won't bat an eye."

“听说你在外面搞了许多黄花大姑娘，中意的放在西城的宅子里当丫鬟，不中意的卖到各大青楼，有没有这回事？”
"Heard you've been messing with young virgins out there, keeping the ones you fancy as maids in your west-city house and selling the rest to brothels—true?"

“污蔑！纯属污蔑！倒是有几个放在西城的宅子里当丫鬟，可卖到青楼这回事，还真没有，也不知是谁到处散播谣言，搞得大家都这么认为。”
"Slander! Pure slander! Sure, I put a few in the west-city house as maids, yet selling girls to brothels? Never happened! No idea who's spreading lies to make everyone think that."

“老实告诉我，那几个丫鬟该不会都被你……”陈浩东的脸上露出调侃的表情。
"Level with me—those maids over there, haven't you already..." A teasing smirk curled Chen Haodong's lip.

陈浩宇毫不掩饰：“那还用说？”
Chen Haoyu didn't even blink: "Goes without saying!"

陈浩东嘴角露出一丝轻蔑，很快又笑道：“弟啊，以后别再这样，陈家这份家业，大伯迟早会交到你手上，得赶紧浪子回头，好好珍惜老天赐给你的机会。”
A flash of scorn crossed Chen Haodong's mouth before turning back into a warm grin. "Little brother, knock it off already. Eldest Uncle is going to hand the Chen empire to you sooner or later—turn over a new leaf and treasure the gift Heaven dropped in your lap."

“别！陈家又不是只有我一个男儿，有东哥，还有浩飞，你们两个够了，我只求自由自在，吃喝玩乐。不说了，还要去找我娘，回见！”
"Pass! I'm not the only male heir—there's you and Haofei, plenty between you two! All I want is freedom, good food, and fun. Gotta run see Mother—catch you later!"

陈浩宇带着青瓜，继续往主院走。
With Qinggua at his heels, Chen Haoyu headed on toward the main courtyard.

陈浩东对着堂弟的背影冷笑道：“浪荡公子，一身纨绔习气，陈家的家业交到你手上岂不玩完？不过，你这样儿倒也好。”
Watching his cousin's back, Chen Haodong let out a cold sneer. "A hopeless rake soaked in dandy habits—hand the Chen fortune to you and it'd go up in smoke! Still, staying just as you are suits me fine."

姚轻琰一生最大的幸福便是嫁给陈江林，不是因为他有钱，而是因为他对她一心一意。
Yao Qingyan's greatest joy in life was marrying Chen Jianglin—not for his fortune, but for his undivided devotion.

陈江林从不纳妾室，平时除忙自家生意外，只顾陪她，甚至把陪她看得比生意还重要，不知令多少临安贵妇羡慕。
Chen Jianglin had never taken a concubine. Outside running the family empire, he spent all his hours by her side, even putting her ahead of business deals, earning the envy of half the noblewomen in Lin'an.

姚轻琰最大的不幸便是生下陈浩宇，不学无术，整日浪荡。她对儿子既不满，又不忍心打他，再说有婆婆护着，她也毫无办法。
Yao Qingyan's greatest sorrow was bearing Chen Haoyu, a shiftless ne'er-do-well who drifted outside all day. Frustrated as she was, she never had the heart to beat him—and with her mother-in-law shielding him, her hands were tied anyway.

家中唯一能狠下心收拾陈浩宇的只有陈江林，可陈江林孝顺，只要家中老太太发话，他只能停手，加之疼爱妻子，她稍加阻拦，手中的棍子难以落下。
The only soul in the manor tough enough to discipline Chen Haoyu was Chen Jianglin, yet he was a filial son who dropped the rod whenever Old Madam Xia spoke, and a doting husband who stayed his hand the instant his wife pleaded.

因此，纵得陈浩宇更是无法无天，想在外浪荡多久便浪荡多久。反正陈家有的是宅子，不怕他没地方住，有的是商铺，不怕他没银子支取。
So Chen Haoyu grew wilder by the day, staying out as long as he pleased—the Chen clan had endless estates to sleep in and endless shops to draw silver from.

夫妻二人正在屋内闲聊，丫鬟映雪进来报告：“老爷，夫人，公子回来，在外面候着。”
Husband and wife were chatting indoors when the maid Yingxue stepped in to announce, "Master, Madam, Second Young Master is home and waiting outside."

陈江林正对着夫人眉开眼笑，听到不成器的儿子回来，立刻拉下脸：“让他进来！”
Beaming at his wife a second ago, Chen Jianglin's face snapped grim at mention of his wayward son. "Send him in!"

姚轻琰说：“老爷别这样，儿子好不容易回来，你老给他脸色看，他会越来越不喜欢待在家里。”
Yao Qingyan chided him: "Don't be like that, Husband. Our son comes home once in a blue moon—keep glaring at him and he'll hate coming home even more."

陈江林立刻露出笑容：“你说的有道理。”
Chen Jianglin broke into a smile at once. "Fair point."

陈浩宇一进屋，扑进母亲怀里：“娘，儿子好想您。”
Bursting through the door, Chen Haoyu threw himself into his mother's arms: "Mother, I missed you so much!"

“又骗我，你要是想娘，就不该在外面鬼混。”姚轻琰语气中略带批评，可眼神却很温柔。
"Sweet-talking me again! If you missed your mother, you wouldn't be out carousing." Though her words scolded, Yao Qingyan's eyes brimmed with warmth.

“我不骗您，这次回来儿子打算安心待在家里。”
"Cross my heart—this time I'm staying home for good!"

“真是太阳打西边出来，你能在家待上两日我就烧高香了。”
"The sun must be rising in the west! If you last two whole days at home, I'll burn incense in thanks."

陈江林还是不由板起脸：“别一回来，就往你娘身上贴，老实站好，听我问话。”
Unable to hold back, Chen Jianglin darkened his brow: "Stop hanging on your mother the second you walk in! Stand up straight and answer my questions."

当着儿子的面，老爷的面子还是要给的，姚轻琰说：“快去站好，好好回答你爹的问题。”
Giving her husband face in front of their son, Yao Qingyan said, "Go stand straight and answer your father."

陈浩宇老实站好：“爹，您问吧。”
Chen Haoyu snapped upright. "Ask away, Father."

陈江林语气严厉：“老实交代，最近在外面都干些什么？”
Chen Jianglin spoke in a stern tone: "Confess—what have you been up to out there?"

“吃吃喝喝玩玩之类，也没干什么坏事。”
"Eating, drinking, having fun—nothing bad."

“难道没欺负良家妇女？没逛青楼？没去赌坊？”
"Not harassing decent girls? Not visiting brothels? Not haunting gambling dens?"

父亲的问题一个个问在点子上，陈浩宇不敢承认：“爹，儿子贪玩儿，喜欢不着家，但也不可能去那些地方，肯定有人诬陷我。”
Every question hit dead center, yet Chen Haoyu dared not admit a word. "Father, I love having fun and staying out, sure, yet I'd never touch places like that—somebody's framing me!"

“就你那样儿，还怕被人诬陷！”
"With a reputation like yours, who'd need to frame you?!"

说着陈江林气不打一处来，想站起来扇儿子一巴掌，被夫人的眼神阻止。他努力平复心情：“听你姐姐说，你连朱家姑娘都欺负，是也不是？”
Tempers flaring, Chen Jianglin moved to stand and box his son's ears, only for a look from his wife to stop him. Steeling his breath, he said, "Your sister told me you even picked on Miss Zhu—true or not?"

陈浩宇一脸不满：“是她欺负我好不好？”
Chen Haoyu pouted: "She's the one who picked on me, all right?!"

“朱家姑娘是有名的女词人，才华横溢，以后见她要恭敬些，多跟人家学习，爹不图你满腹经纶，哪怕你以后娶一位像朱家姑娘那样知书达理，又有才华的姑娘回来，我也心满意足。”
"Miss Zhu is a famed female ci poet brimming with talent! Treat her with respect when you see her and learn from her! I don't expect you to turn into a grand scholar—even if you just marry a refined, talented girl half like Miss Zhu someday, I'll die happy."

陈浩宇很不屑：“爹，您哪只眼睛看到她知书达理？您出去听听，外面的人全在骂她，难听死了。”
Chen Haoyu curled his lip: "Which eye of yours sees her as refined, Father? Step outside and listen—the whole city's cursing her out! Filthy stuff!"

“市井之谈岂能当真？我又不是没见过朱家姑娘？她是什么样的人我难道不知？再说你姐姐经常在我们面前夸她，我与你娘可以怀疑别人，也不能怀疑你姐姐！”
"Since when do we trust street gossip? Haven't I met Miss Zhu myself? Don't I know what kind of girl she is? Besides, your sister praises her to the skies—your mother and I might doubt anyone else, never your sister!"

姚轻琰笑道：“你爹说得对，你姐姐很少夸人，能入她眼之人肯定错不了。”
Yao Qingyan smiled. "Your father's right. Your sister rarely praises anyone; someone who wins her over can't be bad."

正说着，陈文心带丫鬟抱着莱宝进来……
Right on cue, Chen Wenxin walked in with a maid carrying Laibao...
