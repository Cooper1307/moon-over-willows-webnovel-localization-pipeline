## Chapter 145: United by a Common Goal

千里寻到达朱府时，陈家刚刚离开。
Qianli Xun arrived at Zhu Manor just after the Chen procession departed.

她跳下马车，顾不上形象，冲进朱府，下人们也不去阻拦，毕竟是朱家姑娘，再被赶出去，也是朱家人。
Vaulting from her carriage without a thought for poise, she charged straight through the gate—none of the servants daring to block her since, cast out or not, she was still Master's daughter.

千里寻直奔正屋，瞧到坐在榻上的朱晞颜，顾不上行礼，质问道：“父亲为何瞒着我与陈家订亲？也不问问我是否愿意！”
Storming straight into the main hall to find Zhu Xiyan lounging on his couch, she skipped bowing and demanded, "Why did Father seal a betrothal with the Chen family behind my back—without even asking if I agree?!"

朱晞颜对女儿的无礼甚是不满，放下手中的书：“何须问你？只要两家大人说定即可，你只管等开春后嫁过去就是。”
Annoyed at her brashness, Zhu Xiyan set down his book: "Why ask you? Both families' elders agreed—all you have to do is wait till spring and climb into the wedding sedan."

“父亲将女儿强行嫁给林觉，最后闹到和离，难道还想让女儿再和离一次？”
"Father pushed me into marrying Lin Jue and look how that ended in divorce—want to push me straight into a second divorce?!"

“你嫁给林觉时，我征求过你意见，你是同意的，我也没想到，最后闹到和离；现在你因流言坏名声在外，以后还想嫁什么好人家？陈家愿意上门提亲，那是你的造化，不偷着乐，还跑来质问我，是何道理？”
"When you married Lin Jue I asked your consent and you said yes—nobody foresaw a divorce! Now rumors ruined your name across Lin'an; what fine house would come calling? Having the Chens propose is pure fortune—instead of thanking your lucky stars, you storm in grilling me?!"

“我宁可不嫁人，也不会嫁给陈浩宇！”
"I'd rather stay single till I die than marry Chen Haoyu!"

“你嫁不嫁人，要嫁给谁，不是由你说了算，婚事已定下，没有反悔的余地，闹也没用。”
"Whether you marry—or whom you marry—isn't up to you! The betrothal is sealed, zero room to back out—raising hell won't change a thing!"

“婚事定下，是你们的事，我坚决不嫁，到时只怕爹抬到陈家的是我的尸体！”
"You sealed it, not me! I refuse to marry him—come spring, the only thing Father will deliver to Chen Manor is my corpse!"

只听“啪”的一声，朱晞颜用手拍在桌上，桌上的茶盏被震翻，里面的茶水流了一桌。
With a sharp crack, Zhu Xiyan slammed the table so hard his tea bowl flipped, spilling hot tea across the wood.

“你别用死吓唬我，你就是死了，也是陈家的鬼！”
"Don't threaten me with death! Even dead, your ghost belongs to the Chen family!"

陈文心与朱景元搀扶向夫人进来，见父女两个吵得凶，都不敢大声说话。
Right then Chen Wenxin and Zhu Jingyuan walked in steadying Madam Xiang; hearing father and daughter roaring at each other silenced them all.

向夫人把女儿拉到一边：“坐下，跟你爹好好说话。”
Madam Xiang hurried to tug her daughter aside: "Sit down and speak calm with your father."

千里寻根本不愿坐，继续生着气。
Too furious to sit, Qianli Xun stood rigid, chest heaving.

朱晞颜不想再听女儿反对订婚的话，从榻上下来，对向夫人说：“你好好劝劝她。”然后走出门。
Refusing to listen to another word against the match, Zhu Xiyan stepped off his couch, told Madam Xiang, "Talk some sense into her," and marched out the door.

公爹不在，陈文心才敢开口说话：“淑真，我们陈家可是临安首富，嫁过去后不会亏待你。”
Once Father-in-law cleared out, Chen Wenxin dared speak: "Shuzhen, the Chen family is the wealthiest house in Lin'an—marry over and you'll never suffer a day!"

“嫂嫂这是什么话？好像我是爱钱之人，我说的是你那弟弟，我跟他实在不合，为何偏偏要让我嫁给他？”千里寻过去抓住陈文心的手，“嫂嫂，你人最好，能不能回陈府，劝说您父母，让他们取消婚约？”
"What kind of talk is that, Sister-in-law—acting like I'm after money?! It's your brother I'm talking about! We mix like oil and fire—why force me to marry him?!" Grabbing Chen Wenxin's hands, Qianli Xun pleaded, "You're the kindest soul, Sister-in-law—please go back to Chen Manor and beg your parents to call off the betrothal!"

向夫人批评道：“说的什么话？女子被退婚，以后很难在人面前抬起头，听你爹的话，安心嫁到陈家。”
Madam Xiang scolded her: "Watch what you say! A woman dropped from a betrothal can never lift her head in public again! Obey your father and marry into the Chen house in peace."

朱景元也劝道：“淑真，浩宇只是顽劣些，等成亲后，肯定会好，再等你们将来有个孩子，他当上爹，为给孩子做表率，自然会痛改前非。”
Zhu Jingyuan joined in: "Haoyu's just a bit wild, Shuzhen—once wed he'll settle down, and once you have kids he'll straighten right up to set a good example."

全家没人站在自己这边，千里寻放开嫂嫂的手，生气道：“娘，嫂嫂，我上次回来问两家是否要订亲，娘说没这回事，嫂嫂也说不知道，该不会都是故意骗我？”
Not a single soul in the room on her side! Dropping her sister-in-law's hands, Qianli Xun glared: "Mother, Sister-in-law—last time I came asking about a Chen betrothal, Mother swore there was nothing and Sister-in-law swore she knew nothing! Were you both in on fooling me?!"

“当时我真不知，今日才明白，原来公爹怕坏事，故意瞒着婆婆，还让我抄书，不过是为防止我回到陈府得知消息，忍不住告诉你。还别说，公爹这事处理得相当好。”陈文心笑着说完，安慰道，“你放心，以后嫁给浩宇，他若欺负你，只管来找我，我替你收拾他。”
"I really had no clue back then! Only realized today Father-in-law kept Mother in the dark and buried me in copying books so I wouldn't visit Chen Manor and leak it to you! Gotta hand it to Father-in-law—slick move!" Laughing, Chen Wenxin patted her hand: "Relax! Once you marry Haoyu, if he ever dares give you grief, come straight to me and I'll tan his hide for you!"

千里寻将三位亲人扫视一遍，他们都面带笑容，显然对两家结亲之事十分满意，看来只剩下自己一人孤军奋战。
Scanning all three relatives—every single face beaming over the match—Qianli Xun realized she stood all alone.

她想到陈浩宇，他自然反对，要不怎么第一个跑到望月轩告诉她订亲之事？当时误解他，不如现在去寻他，让他劝说陈家退婚。
Then Chen Haoyu flashed into mind! He hated this match just as fierce—why else had he sprinted to Wangyue Pavilion to tip her off?! She had misjudged him earlier; time to go hunt him down and get him working on the Chens to break it off!

“娘、哥哥、嫂嫂你们忙，我还有事。”千里寻带着苦荞迅速离开。
"Excuse me, Mother, Brother, Sister-in-law—got urgent business." Calling Kuqiao, Qianli Xun bolted straight out.

向夫人望着女儿出去的背影叹道：“希望淑真不要再闹出事来，顺利嫁到陈家，以后的日子也好有个着落。”
Watching her daughter rush off, Madam Xiang sighed: "Pray Shuzhen doesn't stir up fresh trouble and settles into the Chen house without a hitch so she has a real anchor in life."

“婆婆说的是，之前我偶尔瞎想过，若是淑真能嫁给浩宇那该多好，没想到竟然美梦成真，呵呵，浩宇也不知几辈子修来的福气，竟然能娶到江南第一女词人。”陈文心满脸笑容。
"Right you are, Mother! Truth be told, I used to daydream how lovely it'd be if Shuzhen married Haoyu—never dreamed it'd come true! Haha, Haoyu must've saved a kingdom in a past life to land Jiangnan's foremost female ci poet!" Chen Wenxin beamed.

向夫人见儿媳对这段姻缘满意，也甚是欣慰：“陈家不愧是大户人家，眼界与别家不同，不会将市井之人的议论当回事。”
Seeing her daughter-in-law thrilled comforted Madam Xiang too: "The Chens really have the breadth of a grand family—paying zero heed to street gossip."

朱景元说道：“娘，文心嫁到朱家，就是朱家人，别人不了解淑真，她还能不了解？她只要一回娘家，就对家人说起淑真的好，陈家当然不会把外人的话当回事。”
Zhu Jingyuan smiled: "Once Wenxin married into the Zhu house, she became one of us—nobody knows Shuzhen better than her! Every visit home she sings Shuzhen's praises, so of course the Chens ignore outsiders' chatter."

向夫人对这个儿媳更是满意：“说起来，淑真失忆后才与你来往甚密，难道以前你也替她说话？”
Pleased with her daughter-in-law all the more, Madam Xiang remarked, "Come to think of it, Shuzhen only grew close to you after losing her memory—you spoke well of her even before that?"

陈文心解释道：“那是自然，以前淑真对我爱理不理，只因才高，不愿与我等俗人往来，但人极好。婆婆您不知道，我身边的丫鬟月桂一次走路没踩稳，脚扭伤，正好碰见淑真，她便让苦荞把月桂扶起来，先去她的小院，涂上药，再让苦荞把月桂送回来。月桂是我从娘家带回来的，从小与我一起长大，关系自然亲近些，总是在我面前对淑真赞不绝口，我自然对她印象好。”
Chen Wenxin explained, "Of course! Before amnesia Shuzhen kept aloof out of sheer poetic pride, yet her heart was pure gold. Mother wouldn't know—once my maid Yuegui slipped and sprained her ankle right in front of Shuzhen. Shuzhen had Kuqiao help Yuegui into her own courtyard, rub medicine on her ankle, and escort her all the way home! Yuegui grew up beside me at Chen Manor, so hearing her praise Shuzhen nonstop won me right over."

向夫人夸道：“原来如此，也是淑真幸运，遇到你这样的好嫂嫂。”
Madam Xiang praised warm: "So that's why! Shuzhen truly struck gold getting a sister-in-law like you."

一路上，千里寻寻思如何才能找到陈浩宇，自上次见过，再没遇见他。他既不愿与她订亲，为何今日这么大的事不见人影？千里寻决定到永宁居碰碰运气。
Rolling along in her carriage, Qianli Xun wondered where to track down Chen Haoyu—had not seen hide or hair of him since his warning, not even today on his own betrothal day! Might as well try Yongning Residence first.

到达永宁居门口，千里寻下了马车后，吩咐车夫先回去，不用在外面等，毕竟离得不远。
Stepping out before Yongning Residence, Qianli Xun sent her driver home—no need waiting when Wangyue Pavilion stood just down the block.

她瞧着“永宁居”三个大字，正要上前敲门，一位下人朝这边走来。
Looking up at the plaque reading "Yongning Residence," she raised her hand to knock just as a servant walked up the lane.

来人正是青瓜，出去给公子买药，大夫开的治伤药快用完，得提前买些备上。
It was Qinggua returning from the apothecary with fresh salves—the physician's ointments were running low and needed restocking.

也不知是哪个杀千刀的把公子打成那样，过去一周，才好一小半儿。还好，脸上的伤倒是痊愈，至于身上，只要不脱衣裳别人也发现不了。
Who knew what ruthless bastard had pounded his master so hard—a whole week later and barely half healed! At least his face looked normal again, and winter clothes hid the bruises across his torso.

青瓜见有位漂亮姑娘站在门口，担心是公子在外惹的烂桃花，上前小心问：“姑娘找谁？”
Spotting a gorgeous young lady standing at the gate—worried she might be some romantic fling Young Master picked up outside—Qinggua approached cautious: "Who is Young Lady looking for?"

千里寻见青瓜手中拎的药包和药瓶，心想，看来永宁居有人病得不轻，希望不是陈浩宇。
Seeing packets and bottles of medicine in Qinggua's hands, Qianli Xun wondered who inside was sick—better not be Chen Haoyu!

“陈浩宇在这里住过，我找他。”
"Chen Haoyu stays here—I'm looking for him."

青瓜这才认出眼前的漂亮姑娘正是朱家姑娘：“不知姑娘找我家公子做什么？”
Recognition dawned on Qinggua—this beauty was Miss Zhu! "May I ask what business Young Lady has with my master?"

苦荞说：“我家小姐找你家公子有事商议，如果人在，麻烦通报一声。”
Kuqiao spoke up: "My mistress has something to discuss with your master. If he's in, go announce her."

青瓜犹豫着：“姑娘还是说说到底什么事？我好回我家公子。”
Qinggua hesitated: "Could Young Lady tell me what it's about so I can report?"

苦荞生气道：“你个下人问那么多干什么？只管通报便是！”
Kuqiao snapped, "Since when does a servant cross-examine guests?! Just go announce her!"

青瓜只觉眼前的丫鬟说话如此厉害，估计朱家姑娘也不是好惹的，再说两家要订亲，以后她嫁给公子，就是自己的女主人，得罪不起，还是老实相告为好。
Wincing at how sharp even the maid's tongue was, Qinggua figured Miss Zhu had to be twice as fierce—and since she was about to become his future Young Madam whom he dared not offend, better just tell her the truth.

“我家公子在，稍等片刻，我进去通报。”
"My master is inside—please wait a moment while I announce you."

经过几日治疗，陈浩宇已无需躺在床上，可以到处活动。
After several days of treatment, Chen Haoyu was off bedrest and roaming the yard again.

身上的伤还未痊愈，想去春香楼开荤需等些时日，只好耐心在永宁居待着，等身上肌肤完好无损，再继续以前浪荡的生活。
Still too bruised to visit Chunxiang Brothel, he had no choice but to sit tight in Yongning Residence until every mark faded before resuming his wild streak.

他正在屋外逗鸟，鸟被关在笼子里叽叽喳喳地叫。青瓜拎着一大堆药从外面匆匆回来，看上去很着急。
Busy outside teasing a chirping songbird in its cage, he watched Qinggua rush in weighed down with medicine packets, looking frantic.

“买个药，急什么？难道急着投胎？”
"Buying medicine—what's the rush, racing to reincarnate?"

“公子，未来的少夫人要见您，人正在外面候着。”
"Young Master, your future Young Madam is at the gate asking to see you!"

“什么未来的少夫人？把话说明白些。”
"Future Young Madam who?! Speak plain!"

“是朱家姑娘，今日是两家订亲的日子，她不就是未来的少夫人？”
"Miss Zhu! Today's your betrothal day—doesn't that make her future Young Madam?!"

陈浩宇仔细想想正是今日，一着急，一脚踢在青瓜的身上：“闭上你的臭嘴！把人给我叫进来！”
Realizing today was the seventh day, Chen Haoyu swung a sharp kick into Qinggua's shin: "Shut your filthy mouth and bring her in!"

因劲儿使得较大，身上的伤口疼起来，陈浩宇努力忍着。
Twisting too hard sent fresh pain stabbing across his bruised ribs; Chen Haoyu clenched his teeth to hide it.

青瓜捂着被公子踢疼的地方，对一旁的下人说：“没听见吗？快去把人叫进来！”
Rubbing his shin, Qinggua barked at a nearby servant: "Deaf?! Go bring her in!"

下人匆匆而去。
The servant sprinted off.

疼痛有所缓解后，青瓜把公子扶到椅子上坐下：“公子，您在，我把药放好，再来伺候您。”
Once the throb eased, Qinggua helped his master into a chair: "Sit tight, Young Master—I'll put this medicine away and come right back."

千里寻来到院中，永宁居的确比望月轩大，布置得更精巧奢华。
Stepping into the courtyard, Qianli Xun noted Yongning Residence dwarfed Wangyue Pavilion, decked out with lush, pricey flair.

陈浩宇一眼瞅到朱淑真，装出悠闲舒适的样子：“朱淑真，今日是两家定亲的日子，是不是让你给搅黄了？专程来向本公子报喜？”
Spotting Zhu Shuzhen walk in, Chen Haoyu leaned back putting on breezy nonchalance: "Well, Zhu Shuzhen—today's betrothal day! Blew the whole thing up already and rushed over to bring me the good news?"

“让你失望了，我晚去一步，两家已将婚事定下。”
"Hate to disappoint you—I arrived one step too late. Both families already sealed the betrothal."

“什么？都已定下？枉我高看你一回，本以为你可以把订亲之事搅黄，没想到你这么无用，难道你想嫁给本公子不成？”
"WHAT?! Sealed already?! And here I gave you credit thinking you'd nuke it in two seconds—turns out you're useless! Don't tell me you actually WANT to marry me?!"

“闭上你的臭嘴！老娘嫁给谁都不会嫁给你！你既不愿意，为何今日躲在永宁居不前去阻拦？”
"Shut your rotten mouth! I'd marry a turnip before marrying you! If you hate it so much, why were you hiding out in Yongning Residence instead of stopping it today?!"

“一言难尽，本公子近几日受重伤，需要治疗，才老实躲在这里。你倒好，大活人一个，为何不及时前去阻止？”
"Long story—got hurt bad a few days back and had to hole up here healing! What's YOUR excuse, walking around healthy as an ox?!"

“我爹欺骗我，说两家根本不会订亲，我信以为真，谁知竟然在今日瞒着我把亲事定下，我来找你，是想让你回陈府劝说陈家人尽快退婚。”
"My dad lied straight to my face swearing there was no betrothal, and I fell for it! Then he sealed it behind my back today! I came to tell you to get back to Chen Manor and talk your family into breaking it off right now!"

“你就不怕退婚对你影响不好？”
"Not worried a broken engagement will smear your name?"

“哼！流言蜚语已让我名声大臭，还怕退婚？你尽管劝说，在老娘看来，退婚后被人嘲笑，也比嫁给你强！”
"Hmph! Rumors already dragged my name through every gutter in Lin'an—what's a broken betrothal? Go talk them round! Being laughed at beats marrying you ten times over!"

陈浩宇听着刺耳，却不愿反驳，谁让他也不愿娶眼前的母老虎。朱淑真提到流言，他谨慎地问：“你爹没告诉你散播流言的人是谁？”
Her jab stung, yet sharing the exact same wish kept him from snapping back. At mention of the rumors, he probed cautious: "Didn't your dad tell you who started the rumors?"

千里寻谨慎地问：“你问这干吗？难道流言是你散播的？”
Suspicion flashed in Qianli Xun's eyes: "Why ask? Don't tell me YOU started them?!"

如此看来，朱晞颜并未告诉女儿他陈浩宇正是散播流言之人。
Aha—Zhu Xiyan had not told his daughter Chen Haoyu was the culprit!

真是老奸巨猾！既不想得罪陈家，还想给女儿找个下家，害得自己还未遇到喜欢的姑娘，就得娶一只母老虎。
Sly old fox—keeping peace with the Chens while offloading his daughter onto him, saddling him with a tigress before he even met a girl he liked!

“怎么可能是我散播的？我是觉得散播流言之人十分可恶，如果抓到他，你会怎么做？”
"Me?! No way! Just saying whoever spread them is pure scum—if you ever caught him, what would you do?"

“如果抓到他，老娘抽了他的筋，剥了他的皮，然后再将他暴揍一顿。”千里寻边说边来气，仿佛散播谣言之人正在眼前。很快她缓和下来，“估计根本抓不到，现在我已不在意此事，反正老百姓也不再议论。对了，你准备什么时候回家劝说你父母？”
"Catch him and I'd yank out his tendons, peel off his hide, and beat him flat!" Fire blazed in Qianli Xun as if the rat stood right before her, before cooling down: "Doubt they'll ever catch him anyway, and I don't care anymore since folk stopped talking. Back to business: when are you going home to talk your parents round?"

“为了我的幸福，现在就回去。”
"For my own future? Heading back right this second!"

陈浩宇答应得爽快，千里寻第一次觉得他还不错，不由关心道：“你的伤好些了吗？”
Surprised at his swift cooperation, Qianli Xun felt Chen Haoyu was not hopeless for once, even asking: "How are your injuries holding up?"

“还没完全好，但不碍事，放心，过不了几日，你就会收到退婚的好消息。”陈浩宇信誓旦旦。
"Not all healed yet, no big deal. Relax—in a few days you'll get the sweet news that our betrothal is off!" Chen Haoyu promised loud and sure.

千里寻当然开心：“好，我在望月轩等你的好消息。”
Smiling in relief: "Great—I'll wait for your good news at Wangyue Pavilion."

两个人第一次以缓和的态度对话，只因二人有共同的目标。
For the very first time, the two arch-enemies spoke without throwing a single punch—united by one shared mission.
