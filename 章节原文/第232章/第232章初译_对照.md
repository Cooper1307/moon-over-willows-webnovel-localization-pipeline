第232章 求助无果跑圈圈
## Chapter 232: No Rescue, Just Laps

青瓜找老太太替公子出头失败而归后，不敢进入墨香苑，又将与丑丫相约一事抛之脑后，干脆在陈府的园子里转悠转悠，等天黑后，再回墨香苑。
转着转着，来到西边，一抬头，瞥见丑丫正站在亭子里，想起相约之事。心中念道：“真是偷鸡不成蚀把米，没帮成公子，把自己给搭进去。还是赶快离开，只要我不去约会，丑丫自然明白我的心思。”
青瓜打算往墨香苑的方向走，准备在外面找个地方躲起来，瞅准机会回去向公子说明情况。
他来到墨香苑门口，瞅来瞅去，两边的翠竹实在好，藏在后面不容易被人发现。
刚藏起来没多久，竟然见公子走出来，身边跟着阿升和三宝。青瓜心想，既然公子被少夫人放出来，说明偷跑出来已无事，还躲个什么劲儿？
他从翠竹后面跳出来：“公子，少夫人放您出来了？”
青瓜出去半天，没见长辈来解救，陈浩宇认为青瓜失职，刚要用脚去踢，想到青瓜会幻影闪移，踢了也是白踢，不得已收回脚：“你早早出来，只顾自己自由自在，却不顾本公子，看我不揍你！”
陈浩宇伸出拳头要去打青瓜。
青瓜躲到一边：“公子这不出来了吗？就别生气了。”
陈浩宇没打着，收起拳头：“出来有什么用？还不是被他俩跟着？”
青瓜瞧了瞧阿升和三宝，笑道：“总比关在屋里强。”
“少废话，陪本公子去找老太太告状！”陈浩宇说着就要走。
青瓜拦住：“公子还是别去，我已经去过，老太太说以后公子院子里的事她一概不管；大老爷说巴不得您被关起来，还说您是自作自受；夫人说您是死是活是少夫人的事，以后少拿墨香苑的事烦她。”
陈浩宇认为青瓜胡说，一把推开他，向奶奶的院子而去。
说话艺人已离开，老二老三也带着各自夫人回屋；陈江林还想再陪老太太，又与姚轻琰多坐会儿。
三人正说话，丫鬟来报：“老太太，二公子被放出来，正朝这边走来。”
老太太吩咐道：“去，把大门关上，顺便告诉他，以后不要再来烦我。”
“是。”丫鬟领命而去。
老太太观察儿媳，她面露担忧之色，便耐心叮嘱道：“你们两个回去，也把院门关好，不要心软，心稍微一软，恐怕会前功尽弃。”
老太太摸摸自己的胸口：“我这个心啊，难受啊，为了浩宇好，得忍住。”
姚轻琰也努力忍着，不说话。
陈江林说：“都怪儿子没把浩宇教育好，让母亲和轻琰跟着为难。成败在此一举，我们都得狠下心来。”
老太太和姚轻琰都点头认可。
陈浩宇来到奶奶的院子门口，见院门紧闭，他命令青瓜上前敲门。
青瓜按照吩咐做了，门没开，里面传出丫鬟的声音：“二公子还是回去吧，以后你的事老太太不会再管。”
Turned away empty-handed from Old Madam Xia's rooms, Qinggua dared not return to Moxiang Courtyard—and clean forgot his date with Chouya—instead wandering the manor gardens planning to sneak home after dark.

陈浩宇气道：“你个下人敢这么跟本公子说话，小心我明日让奶奶把你发卖了！”
Strolling into the west wing, he looked up to spot Chouya waiting inside the pavilion and froze. Oh no—tried helping Young Master and got myself hooked instead! Better ghost her; once I no-show, she'll get the hint!

里面传出声音：“奴婢哪敢？是老太太吩咐我这么说的。”
Creeping back toward Moxiang Courtyard to wait for an opening, he slipped behind the lush green bamboo flanking the gate—and barely seconds later watched Chen Haoyu stride out with Asheng and Sanbao trailing behind!

陈浩宇装出哭腔，向里喊道：“奶奶！您不能不管我！母老虎虽把我放出来，可她狠着呢！不仅派两个人时时刻刻跟着我，还罚我做好多事情，最过分的是竟然让我给她倒马桶！呜呜！您若不管我，恐怕孙儿连命都没了！呜呜……！”
If Young Madam let him out, sneaking out didn't matter anymore! Qinggua jumped out from the stalks. "Young Master, Young Madam released you?"

听到让儿子倒马桶，姚轻琰忍不住说：“淑真怎么能让浩宇做这种事？浩宇从小连洗脸水都没自己倒过，如何能做粗鄙之事？”
Fuming over waiting half the day with zero rescue, Chen Haoyu cocked a boot—remembered Phantom Sidestep, pulled his foot back, and swung a fist instead. "Sneaking out to enjoy yourself while leaving me locked up? I'll clobber you!"

老太太也有些忍不住：“使不得！使不得！”
Qinggua hopped clear. "You're out now, Young Master—let it go!"

刚站起来想出去，犹豫后又坐下：“唉，忍着吧，去，给我拿些棉花来，把耳朵堵住。”
Chen Haoyu lowered his fist. "What good is being out with these two glued to my tail?"

丫鬟拿来棉花，帮老太太把耳朵堵住。
"Still beats staring at four walls," Qinggua grinned.

孙子的哭声还是会传进耳朵，她只能继续狠下心来。
"Quit yakking and come with me to Grandmother's!" Chen Haoyu started off.

老太太如此，姚轻琰不好再说什么，心中告诫自己：“忍一忍就好。”
Qinggua stepped in front of him. "Save your steps, Young Master—I already went! Old Madam Xia said she's staying out of your courtyard for good; Master said he's thrilled you're locked up and you asked for it; Madam said live or die, you're Young Madam's problem now!"

只有陈江林泰然自若：“吃得苦中苦方为人上人，浩宇就是吃得苦少，才养成纨绔的习性，再这么下去，恐怕还比不上掏茅厕的人，倒马桶都是便宜他。”
Convinced Qinggua was talking trash, Chen Haoyu shoved past him and marched straight to Old Madam Xia's gate.

没见里面有动静，陈浩宇继续装哭：“呜呜！奶奶！朱淑真就是为了报复我，昨日她还让丫鬟红豆扇了我两巴掌，到现在脸还疼，呜呜……！您不管我，难道想让我死在她的手中不成？就刚才，她还用拳头打我，到现在我的脸上还有淤青，她连药都不愿给我涂，还说以后我的月钱全部取消！呜呜……！”
Inside, after the storyteller left and Second and Third Brothers retired with their wives, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan lingered chatting with Old Madam Xia when a maid hurried in. "Old Madam, Second Young Master is out and headed this way!"

陈浩宇又装哭半晌，喊了半晌，里面竟一丝声音没有，才意识到奶奶真的不再管他。
"Shut the courtyard gate," Old Madam Xia commanded. "Tell him to quit bothering me."

他不再装哭，大声向里喊道：“奶奶既然不愿替我做主！我去找我娘！”
"Yes, Old Madam."

里面传出丫鬟的声音：“大老爷和夫人就在老太太房中，二公子不必去找，他们让我告诉你，二公子还是好好回去接受少夫人管教。”
Seeing distress flicker across Yao Qingyan's face, Old Madam Xia added sternly, "Lock your own courtyard gate when you go back too. Don't go soft—soften one inch and everything collapses." She pressed a hand to her chest. "Hurts right here, believe me, yet we have to endure for Haoyu's own good."

所有的希望落空，陈浩宇才觉出朱淑真的厉害，尤其是收买人心这招，使得实在高明。
Yao Qingyan bit her lip and stayed silent.

阿升提醒道：“姑爷，该跑步了，如果不跑恐怕您得给小姐倒马桶。”
"My fault for failing to raise him right and putting you both through this," Chen Jianglin said. "Make or break right here—we all have to hold firm." Both women nodded.

陈浩宇使出一拳打向阿升：“你个下人！敢管本公子？”
Arriving to find the gate bolted shut, Chen Haoyu had Qinggua knock. A maid answered through the wood: "Go back, Second Young Master. Old Madam won't meddle in your affairs anymore."

阿升挡住姑爷的拳头：“姑爷还是省省吧。”
"A lowly maid talking back to me?" Chen Haoyu roared. "Wait till I have Grandmother sell you off tomorrow!"

陈浩宇不甘心，又去打三宝。三宝迅速躲开：“姑爷，没用的。”
"This servant wouldn't dare—Old Madam ordered me to say it."

家人不管，两个下人又打不过，一生气又向青瓜踢去……
Switching tactics, Chen Haoyu put on a shrill wail toward the gate. "Grandmother! You can't abandon me! Sure the tigress unlocked my room, yet she's cruel as a demon! Assigned two thugs to tail me nonstop, piled chores on my head—and worst of all, she's forcing me to empty her chamber pot! Boo-hoo! Save me or I'm a dead man!"

青瓜迅速闪开，因一时没思想准备，着急中不小心推公子一把。陈浩宇向一旁趔趄，差点儿倒在地上。
At "empty her chamber pot," Yao Qingyan flinched. "How can Shuzhen make Haoyu do something that filthy? He's never even poured his own washbasin!"

“青瓜！你是不是不想活了？敢推本公子？”
Old Madam Xia surged half off her couch. "Unthinkable! Unthinkable!" Then she froze and sank back down. "Hold firm... bring me cotton to plug my ears!"

青瓜意识到做错事，赶紧道歉：“我不是故意的，一时失手，公子别往心里去。”
A maid stuffed cotton into the old lady's ears, yet her grandson's howling still bled through, forcing her to clench her teeth. Seeing Old Madam Xia hold the line, Yao Qingyan swallowed her plea. Only Chen Jianglin sat calm as stone. "No pain, no gain. Spoiling him turned him rotten—at this rate he'd be worth less than a night-soil collector! Emptying a chamber pot is getting off easy."

被自己的下人推，陈浩宇哪能不介意？上前打算抓住青瓜揍一顿，以泄心头之愤。
Getting zero response, Chen Haoyu bawled louder. "Grandmother! Zhu Shuzhen's just out for revenge! Had her maid Hongdou slap my face twice yesterday—still throbs! Just now she punched me right in the cheek, bruised black and blue without even giving me ointment, and canceled my allowance too! Boo-hoo!"

结果被阿升挡住：“姑爷还是不要再耽搁时间，快些跑步吧。”
Still dead silence. Realizing Grandmother meant business, he dropped the sobbing and shouted, "Fine! If Grandmother won't back me, I'm going to my mother!"

青瓜心中一阵感激，否则今日绝对会被公子一顿乱拳攻击。
The maid's voice drifted back through the gate. "Master and Madam are right inside with Old Madam, Second Young Master. They told me to say: go back and take Young Madam's discipline like a man."

无人替他做主，连下人也打不过，陈浩宇第一次感到无助，不由蹲在地上。
Every lifeline snapped. For the first time, Chen Haoyu felt the full chill of Zhu Shuzhen's skill at winning hearts.

青瓜见状，凑到跟前，关切地问：“公子，您怎么了？”
"Time for your laps, Young Master," Asheng reminded him. "Skip them and it's chamber-pot duty."

结果被陈浩宇一巴掌打在脸上。
Chen Haoyu threw a punch at Asheng. "A servant bossing me around?!"

青瓜捂住脸：“公子，您又欺负我！”
Asheng blocked it clean. "Save your breath, Young Master."

憋在心中的气通过一巴掌得到宣泄，陈浩宇站起来：“本公子不欺负你，欺负谁？难道欺负他们两个？”
Swinging at Sanbao next, he hit empty air as Sanbao ducked clear. "No use, Young Master."

青瓜一脸委屈。
Abandoned by family and outmatched by servants, he vented his frustration by booting toward Qinggua—who glided sideways so fast in panic that he accidentally shoved Chen Haoyu off balance, nearly sending him sprawling.

想到臭烘烘的马桶，陈浩宇仿佛都已闻到臭气，一阵恶心。好汉不吃眼前亏，还是乖乖跑步。
"Qinggua! Tired of breathing?! Daring to shove me?!"

“喂！陈府这么大，本公子怎么跑？”
"Accident! Slipped, Young Master—don't hold it against me!"

阿升说：“我在前面带路，三宝后面跟着，姑爷在我俩中间，跟着我跑就行。”
Boiling at being pushed by his own servant, Chen Haoyu lunged to pummel him, only for Asheng to step square between them. "Don't waste time, Young Master. Start running."

陈浩宇不愿放过青瓜，命令道：“你！过来陪本公子一起跑！”
Qinggua breathed silent thanks as Chen Haoyu—beaten on every front—squatted helplessly on the cobblestones.

青瓜为难道：“公子，我不喜欢跑步，太累。”
Stepping closer in concern, Qinggua asked, "Are you all right, Young Master?"

陈浩宇批评道：“你看看人家，不是我的下人却陪我跑，你是我的下人却当缩头乌龟，赶紧的，否则我可不帮你追姑娘。”
Smack! Chen Haoyu slapped his cheek.

为了红豆，青瓜豁出去：“公子都愿意跑，我哪敢说个不字，陪您就是。”
Clutching his face, Qinggua yelped, "Picking on me again, Young Master!"

陈府的主道上，阿升在前，三宝断后，中间青瓜陪着公子，大家迈开腿跑起来。
The slap let off just enough steam for Chen Haoyu to rise. "Who else can I pick on—them?"

陈府挺大，主道修得宽敞，倒也适合跑步。
Qinggua swallowed his grievance.

只是主道上有来来往往的下人，往西边去时，还有许多兴隆阁的伙计，动不动会碰到。
Then a vivid whiff of a steaming chamber pot flashed through Chen Haoyu's mind, making his stomach churn. A smart man picks his battles; better run the laps. "Hey! Chen Manor's massive—where am I supposed to run?"

前后两人看得紧，陈浩宇根本没有机会逃走，硬着头皮向前跑。下人们指指点点地议论，从未有过的憋屈堵在胸口。因跑步需喘气，他懒得骂那些下人，
"I'll set the pace in front, Sanbao brings up the rear, you run right between us," Asheng said.

到达西边的主道，来往的伙计不比下人，也不怕他，主动打招呼调侃：“二公子跑着呢？什么时候开始学会锻炼身体？”
Refusing to suffer alone, Chen Haoyu pointed at Qinggua. "You! Run with me!"

陈浩宇并不理会，他与青瓜跑得气喘吁吁，哪有心情理会？边跑边向阿升问：“能……能不能……歇会儿？”
"Come on, Young Master, I hate running—too tiring!"

阿升头也不回，继续向前跑：“还没跑上一圈儿，姑爷就喊累，您可是要跑十圈儿，坚持住，再跑跑自然会觉得轻松。”
"Look at them—not even my servants and they're running with me, while my own man acts like a coward! Get moving or forget my help chasing Hongdou!"

身后传来一个丫鬟的声音：“青瓜！你给我站住！”
For Hongdou, Qinggua bit the bullet. "If you're running, Young Master, I'm right beside you!"

声音够大，够气愤，所有人不由停下，向后瞧。
Down Chen Manor's wide main avenues they jogged: Asheng leading, Sanbao trailing, Chen Haoyu and Qinggua panting between them. Passing manor servants and Xinglong Pavilion clerks gawked and whispered, sending a burn of humiliation up Chen Haoyu's neck, though he barely had breath left to curse them.

陈浩宇更是感谢这声音，累得不行，正好歇会儿。
Rounding the west road, bold clerks even called out teasingly, "Jogging, Second Young Master? Taking up fitness now?"

原来是丑丫，她在亭子里等了半天青瓜，没见人来，慢慢明白过来，青瓜不过是利用她的感情而已。越想越气，不想再等下去，应该找他问个明白。
Wheezing hard, Chen Haoyu gasped at Asheng, "Can... can we... take a breather?"

刚走出亭子，来到主道，瞧见青瓜跟着姑爷跑步，丑丫没忍住，大声喊起来。
Without looking back, Asheng kept striding. "Not even one lap down and you're winded, Young Master? Nine more to go—push through and you'll catch your second wind!"

丑丫一脸怒气把陈浩宇逗乐：“青瓜，你完蛋了。”
Then an angry female voice boomed from behind: "Qinggua! Halt right there!"

青瓜小声说：“都是为了公子我才出卖色相勾引丑丫，该怎么办？公子快帮我出个主意。”
Loud and furious enough to freeze all four men in their tracks—music to Chen Haoyu's ears as he bent over gasping for air.

陈浩宇小声回道：“好汉不吃眼前亏，实在不行，先哄哄她。”
Storming down the lane came Chouya. After waiting half the morning in the pavilion, she had realized Qinggua played her for a fool and marched out hunting answers—spotting him jogging right down the main road.

青瓜认为此法可行，赔着笑脸走过去：“丑丫，让你久等，不是我不去亭子，你也看到，少夫人把公子放出来，要求我陪公子一起跑步。”
Seeing Chouya's thunderous face cracked Chen Haoyu up. "You're toast, Qinggua!"

丑丫一脸怀疑：“真的？”
"I only seduced her for your sake, Young Master!" Qinggua hissed. "Save me—what do I do?"

青瓜继续嬉皮笑脸：“那还能有假？不信你问我家公子。”
"Play smart—sweet-talk her out of it for now!" Chen Haoyu whispered back.

丑丫说：“你跟姑爷一个鼻孔出气，我才不问他，要问就问三宝。三宝你告诉我，是不是小姐让青瓜陪着姑爷跑步？”
Plastering on a sheepish grin, Qinggua trotted over. "Sorry to stand you up, Chouya! Wasn't skipping the pavilion—Young Madam let Young Master out and ordered me to run laps with him!"

三宝照实说：“小姐只让姑爷跑步，是姑爷刚才完拉着青瓜陪他一起跑。”
Chouya squinted in suspicion. "Really?"

丑丫转向青瓜：“你又骗我！”
"Cross my heart! Ask Young Master!"

丑丫已攥紧拳头，青瓜很害怕：“我没骗你，不管是公子还是少夫人，哪个的吩咐我都不敢违抗。”
"You two breathe out of the same nostril—why would I ask him? Sanbao! Did Miss order Qinggua to run with Young Master?"

“那你说喜欢我的话呢？是不是骗我？”丑丫并不避讳旁边有人，大胆问出心中的疑惑。
"Miss only ordered Young Master to run," Sanbao answered straight. "Young Master dragged Qinggua along himself."

陈浩宇一脸看热闹的表情，跑步太无趣，眼前发生的事无疑是一道开胃菜。
Chouya whipped back toward Qinggua, fists balling tight. "Lying to me again!"

阿升本以为青瓜喜欢红豆，还一直担心不已，现在终于放心。
Panicked, Qinggua stammered, "Not lying! Servant like me can't say no to Young Master or Young Madam!"

青瓜迅速想到两种回答的结果。若承认说谎欺骗丑丫，肯定免不了被她暴揍一顿；若假装没骗她，以后可别想再摆脱丑丫。一辈子与被揍一次相比较，还是一辈子更重要。
"What about saying you liked me?" Chouya demanded right in front of everybody. "Was that a lie too?"

青瓜刚要承认是欺骗丑丫，却听丑丫说：“你若是欺骗我，我就天天跟着你，让你娶不了别人，最终只能娶我！你也别想逃走，逃到哪里我就跟到哪里！”
Chen Haoyu grinned like a spectator at a street opera—far more fun than jogging. Meanwhile Asheng exhaled in secret relief, having worried Qinggua was after Hongdou.

陈浩宇心想：“本公子都替青瓜感到为难。”
Qinggua weighed his odds in half a second: admit lying and take a bruising right now, or keep lying and get stuck with her forever. One beating beat a lifetime sentence—about to confess, he froze at Chouya's next threat: "If you lied to me, I'll stalk you every single day so no other girl comes near you until you have to marry me! Run to the ends of the earth and I'll follow!"

三宝说：“青瓜，丑丫对你一片真心，千万不要骗她。”
Even Chen Haoyu winced for him.

阿升说：“丑丫，你放心，青瓜对待感情还是很认真的。”
"Chouya's sincere, Qinggua—don't play games with her," Sanbao chimed in.

青瓜又在心中衡量着，还是继续欺骗能好些，好汉不吃眼前亏，以后再寻机会说明：“丑丫，我说过的话当然出自真心，你快些回去，我还要陪公子跑步，否则公子完成不了任务，得替少夫人倒马桶，你想想，我家公子怎么可能做这种事情，到时吃亏的还是我。”
"Relax, Chouya, Qinggua takes feelings seriously," Asheng piled on.

对方没有否认，丑丫一阵高兴，本想上前抱住青瓜，眼前有其他人，还是忍住：“好，你继续陪姑爷跑步，我先回去。”
Recomputing on the fly—survive today, wriggle out later—Qinggua forced a bright smile. "Of course I meant every word, Chouya! Head back now—I have to finish running with Young Master, or he fails his task and has to empty Young Madam's chamber pot, which means I'll end up doing the dirty work!"

丑丫害羞着迅速跑开。
Glowing because he hadn't denied it, Chouya checked an urge to hug him in front of company. "All right, keep running with Young Master. I'll go back!" Blushing bright pink, she scampered off.

阿升说：“姑爷，热闹看完了，该继续跑步。”
"Show's over, Young Master," Asheng announced. "Back to running."

陈浩宇对刚才的热闹意犹未尽，可好戏已结束，还得跑步。他无力反抗，只能忍着心中的不满，继续跑在陈府的主道上。
Reluctant to see the entertainment end, Chen Haoyu swallowed his grumbling and jogged back down Chen Manor's main avenue.
