# Chapter 47: Appreciating Poetry and Discussing Privacy

Gazing at "The Heartbreak Riddle" felt like beholding Zhu Shuzhen herself; Qiao Wenxian had kept silent only to picture her face and feel her ache through each stroke.

"Outwardly Youqi Jushi appears cool and reserved—far from it! Beneath the surface her passion runs storm-deep! Reading 'The Heartbreak Riddle' lets me feel the churning waves inside her: her defiance, her anguish..." Pausing a beat, he went on, "Utter helplessness bleeds from the opening—no path to the life she longs for or the person in her heart, left tossing coins to question Heaven! Hate blooms only where love lived: 'resenting' confirms she loved deep, only to be left behind..."

Assuming "heartless one" also pointed to Young Master Xu, gloom shadowed Wenyuan's eyes—Shuzhen still pined for him! Voice softening with ache, he continued, "A refined daughter of noble halls, soft-spoken, always choosing delicate words—yet here she drops the harsh word 'cursing' (詈)! Not just to fit the riddle for 'four'—brutal reality drove her past breaking into raw curses! Reading that breaks my heart and screams how cruelly mismatched her marriage is!"

Miao Zhiyu sighed soft. "A woman adapts to the husband she's handed. Trapped with a crude brute like the Training Officer, Youqi Jushi had no choice but to harden."

Gazing fond at her brother, Qiao Mengrong counted her blessings: growing up beside Brother, betrothed to him, assured of sweet harmony forever.

Sympathetic toward Zhu Shuzhen's awful match though he was, Shen Jianchen felt a twitch of discomfort: private life was private! Putting raw personal wounds into verse for crowds to dissect? Dressed up as "literary appreciation," this felt like highbrow gossip! Thank heaven Zhu Shuzhen wasn't sitting here watching strangers rip open her scabs and rub salt in them! Partly on her too—why pour intimate heartache into poems for her own peers and centuries of future readers to gossip over? Sad indeed! If he ever met Zhu Shuzhen in person, Shen Jianchen resolved to tip her off: quit spilling personal drama in public poems!

Warming to Miao Zhiyu's remark with a quick smile, Qiao Wenxian wrapped up: "'Regretting' cuts two ways—regretting losing her poise in harsh words, and regretting obeying her parents to step into that bridal sedan—yet showing us she fights tooth and nail not to let a broken match warp her soul! 'Upper union, no lower tie' exposes marriage's bleakest tragedy: binding two minds with zero bond! And her closing sweep—why ask right from wrong?—captures the saddest state on earth: a dead heart. While hope flickers, even blades leave tangled threads; once the heart dies, not a shred remains."

Silence hung heavy after Wenyuan finished; aside from Shen Jianchen, every soul grieved for Zhu Shuzhen.

Dongfang Chenze broke the hush. "Nobody reads Youqi Jushi's heart like Brother Qiao—spot-on! Any insights from Young Master Shen or Miss Qiao?"

Lifting her wine cup with poise, Qiao Mengrong smiled. "Your readings cover every nuance and echo everything I could wish to say. Allow me to toast your brilliance!" Draining her cup, she glanced toward her brother.

Uninterested in dissecting strangers' private lives—and burning to chase his lead on Qianli Xun—Shen Jianchen shifted gears. "Honored to meet you gentlemen! Poetry critique isn't my trade; tagged along with Wenyuan mainly to ask one question: do any of you three write huaben scripts for storytellers?"

All three shook their heads at once. "We catch storytelling now and then, yet never stoop to writing lowbrow scripts," An Mingxuan replied.

"Ever heard of a scholar named Qianli Xun?" Shen Jianchen pressed.

"Never heard the name," Cao Ban shook his head.

"Wait—this Qianli Xun writes scripts for storytellers?" Dongfang Chenze asked.

"Right! Do you know him, Young Master Dongfang?" Hope flared sharp!

Dongfang Chenze shook his head. "Never met or heard of him, though fellows selling scripts to storytellers are most often down-on-their-luck exam failures scraping by! Want to track down writers like that, check Dongren Street—that's where broke scholars cluster."

A cold jolt hit Shen Jianchen! Not only might Qianli Xun have crossed into a man—she might be living hand to mouth as a broke scholar while he lived high as an imperial prince surrounded by silk and a devoted sister! Unacceptable! He had to track Qianli Xun down fast and lift her straight into the good life!

"Much obliged for the tip, Young Master Dongfang!"

Qianli Xun... who is that? Qiao Mengrong wondered quiet, yet hearing Brother call him a male scholar melted every knot in her chest! Long as Brother wasn't chasing another woman, she'd cheer him on finding any friend! Still steeped in Zhu Shuzhen's sorrow, Qiao Wenxian paid zero attention to Prince Yi's hunt.

Seeing the discussion close, Huayu and Nan Shuang rolled up the scroll. Stepping beside his master, Nan Shuang whispered, "Master, you've got another brand-new lyric by Youqi Jushi too!"

Right! Distracted by "The Heartbreak Riddle," Qiao Wenxian had clean forgotten his own treasure! Turning to the group, he said, "I also happen to have another new lyric by Youqi Jushi—just a discarded draft, hope nobody minds!"

"Mind?! Everybody knows crowds camp outside Lin Manor's gate just hoping for her scraps! Bring it out fast!" Dongfang Chenze urged.

Shen Jianchen smirked inside—fighting over literal trash? Until he remembered Wenyuan's own page had scavenged it out of pure devotion: love made smart men do crazy things!

Pulling the sheet from his vest, Nan Shuang held it open; every eye spotted the black ink blot staining the character "you" (伊).

Miao Zhiyu spoke up first. "Strange... the calligraphy doesn't look quite like Youqi Jushi's hand."

Dongfang Chenze frowned closer. "Looking sharp, it really doesn't! Felt a flicker of doubt copying Lin Zixiao's scroll the other day too—hearing Lady Miao say it confirms my hunch!"

"Yeah... definitely off, can't put a finger on how," Cao Ban muttered.

An Mingxuan pinpointed it: "Youqi Jushi's strokes flow supple and soft; this hand tries mimicking her style, yet can't capture that softness—raw force punches straight through!"

Panicking lest they doubt his prized scoop, Nan Shuang protested fast: "Gentlemen, Lady Miao—I camped outside Lin Manor's gate for this, and Sister Kuqiao handed it straight to me! Guaranteed genuine—don't doubt it!"

Qiao Wenxian stepped in steady. "No doubt Nan Shuang brought genuine work; the voice and piercing sorrow belong to her alone! Truth is, my own brows furrowed seeing this hand back in Bianliang. Returning to Lin'an, hearing from Nan Shuang she toughened up and from Lady Miao she knows martial arts—a harsh marriage transforms a person, and handwriting shifts right along with spirit!"

"Wait—just remembered!" Dongfang Chenze snapped his fingers. "Kuqiao told us the Training Officer beat her into amnesia, forgetting heaps of things! Small wonder her brushwork changed!"

"Amnesia?!" Alarm flashed across Qiao Wenxian's face. "Does she still remember us?!"

"Claimed she remembers bits—doubtful though, she didn't even recognize Chen Haoyu!"

A sharp twinge hit Wenyuan—what if she forgot him too?! Then silver lining flashed: means she forgot Young Master Xu too! Brightening fast, he murmured, "Wiping the slate clean might actually be a blessing for her!"

Listening to their chatter over Youqi Jushi's twist, Shen Jianchen just pitied the talented poetess—bad match, brutal husband, knocked into amnesia—and admired her grit for holding back her martial skills instead of punching back just to get out clean!

Just then the steward and chef entered carrying steaming platters. "Dishes are up, young masters and ladies! Take your time—Young Master Chen said stay as long as you please, even overnight; guest rooms are ready!"

"Time to eat first—we'll study Youqi Jushi's new lyric right after!" Dongfang Chenze announced. Tucking the draft safe back inside his vest, Nan Shuang stepped aside.

Platters laid, the chef rolled in a tiered wooden serving cart overflowing with lavish courses. Eyes popping at dishes he'd never seen, Nan Shuang jumped in helping set plates—feasting his eyes on every course and plotting how hard he'd brag to Bai Feng tonight! At Miao Zhiyu's nod, her maid lifted a wine pot pouring rounds while Xilan grabbed the second pot to help.

Every dish arranged, the steward bowed. "Enjoy! Anything less than perfect, tell the chef and he'll remake it on the spot!"

"Much obliged to both of you. Go rest, we'll call if needed," Dongfang Chenze smiled. Steward and chef withdrew.

Eyeing a banquet lavish enough to rival—even top—imperial fare, Shen Jianchen marveled how insanely rich the Chen clan must be!

Cao Ban laughed. "Living like an emperor, Chen Haoyu! Never even seen half these dishes—eating his feast makes me feel guilty calling him a dandy next time!"

An Mingxuan scoffed. "Bought off by one spread? No backbone! I'm eating every bite and still calling him a dandy! Besides, this settles his favor with Brother Dongfang—we owe him zilch!"

Cheering agreement, everyone clinked cups and dug in. Attendants stood serving until their masters neared full, then took seats to finish the mountain of untouched delicacies—Nan Shuang wishing Bai Feng were there for this feast!

Dinner done, they brought out Zhu Shuzhen's lyric for another long discussion before wrapping up the salon, strolling downstairs to enjoy the riverbank breeze a spell, then boarding carriages home—unseemly staying overnight with two unmarried ladies along. A total bust for Shen Jianchen; he decided right then to skip future poetry salons with Wenyuan, not out of dislike for verse, just tired of watching polite society dissect people's private pain.
