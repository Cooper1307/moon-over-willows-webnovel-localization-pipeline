### 2.2 待改进问题

#### 问题1：重复用词
- **"Qiao Wenxian"重复12次**：部分可用代词"he"替代。
- **"Youqi Jushi"重复18次**：部分可用"she"或"Zhu Shuzhen"替代。
- **"appreciate/appreciation"重复15次**：可替换为"analyze"、"interpret"、"discussion"等。

#### 问题2：句式优化
- **长句需拆分**：
  - 原文："The character 'regret' is used extremely well. It not only shows Youqi Jushi regretting losing the demeanor of a distinguished lady and using coarse language, but also shows her regret in obeying her parents' orders and getting into the bridal sedan to marry into the Lin family."
  - 建议拆分为更短的句子，适合移动端阅读。

#### 问题3：口语化程度
- 部分对话略显正式：
  - "I feel honored to meet you all" → "It's an honor to meet you all"
  - "Please don't despise it" → "Please don't laugh at it"

#### 问题4：文化细节
- **"下里巴人"**：翻译为"lowbrow works"准确，但可考虑加注解释其与"阳春白雪"的对比。
- **"藕断丝连"**：翻译为"the lotus root silk will still connect"保留了意象，但可考虑更简洁的表达如"lingering ties will remain"。
