# 第47章 品鉴诗词谈隐私
# Chapter 47: Appreciating Poetry and Discussing Privacy

乔文仙见《断肠谜》就像见到朱淑真本人，之所以一直沉默，不过是想通词句想象朱淑真的模样，感受她的无奈。

Qiao Wenxian felt as if he were seeing Zhu Shuzhen herself when he looked at "The Heartbreak Riddle." The reason he had been silent all along was that he wanted to imagine Zhu Shuzhen's appearance through the words and feel her helplessness.

---

“从外表看，幽栖居士是位清高寡言的女子，实则不然，她内心情感十分丰富，有时甚至如同排江倒海般激烈。我品《断肠谜》，能感受出她内心的波澜以及对现状的不满，还有她的痛苦……”

"From the outside, Youqi Jushi seems like a proud and silent woman, but that's not the case. Her inner emotions are extremely rich, sometimes even as intense as a surging river. When I appreciate 'The Heartbreak Riddle,' I can feel the waves in her heart, her dissatisfaction with the current situation, and her pain..."

---

乔文仙停了一会儿，继续说：“从词中能看出，她很无助，不知该通过何种方式获得理想的生活以及见到心中所想之人，只能靠起金钱卦，向上天询问。人有爱才有恨，一个‘恨’说明她心中爱着某人，可那人却舍她而去……”

Qiao Wenxian paused for a moment, then continued: "From the ci poem, we can see that she was very helpless, not knowing what way to obtain the ideal life or to meet the person in her heart. She could only cast money divination coins and ask heaven. Where there is love, there is hate. The word 'hate' shows that she loves someone in her heart, but that person has left her..."

---

乔文仙认为诗中的“冤家”指的是徐家公子，由此看来淑真心里还想着他。

Qiao Wenxian believed that the "enemy" in the poem referred to Young Master Xu. From this, it seemed that Shuzhen still had him in her heart.

---

乔文仙露出失落的表情，品道：“她是大家闺秀，原本说话软言细语，写诗作词只会用雅致的字眼，可这首词竟然用了一个‘詈’字，我想不仅仅是为设计字谜，定是生活将她逼得动了粗口……，读到这里，我感到心酸，更觉得幽栖居士所嫁非人。”

Qiao Wenxian showed a disappointed expression and appreciated: "She was a lady from a distinguished family. Originally, she spoke in soft and gentle words, and when writing poetry and ci, she would only use elegant words. But this ci poem actually used the character 'li' (to curse). I think it's not just for designing the word riddle. It must be that life forced her to use coarse language... Reading this, I feel heartache and even more that Youqi Jushi married the wrong person."

---

苗知瑜不由叹道：“唉！女子嫁什么样的夫君，就会变成什么样的人，面对练兵官那样粗俗之人，幽栖居士只能改变自己。”

Miao Zhiyu couldn't help but sigh, "Alas! Whatever kind of husband a woman marries, she will become that kind of person. Facing someone as vulgar as the Military Training Officer, Youqi Jushi could only change herself."

---

乔梦容注视着哥哥，她觉得自己很幸运，从小生活在哥哥身边，又与他有婚约，婚后只会与哥哥甜蜜幸福。

Qiao Mengrong gazed at her brother. She felt very lucky to have grown up beside her brother since childhood and to have a marriage contract with him. After marriage, she would only be sweet and happy with her brother.

---

申简辰也感慨朱淑真婚姻不幸。可他认为个人的生活是隐私，不该写在诗词中，供这么多人品鉴。

Shen Jianchen also lamented Zhu Shuzhen's unfortunate marriage. But he believed that personal life was privacy and shouldn't be written in poetry and ci for so many people to appreciate.

---

美其名曰“品鉴”，实则是借着高雅之名议论他人的八卦。多亏朱淑真没来，她若来了，亲身经历自己的伤疤再次被揭开，还揉搓来揉搓去，像是怕她忘掉似的，得有多难受。

They called it "appreciation" in name, but in reality, they were gossiping about others under the guise of elegance. Fortunately, Zhu Shuzhen didn't come. If she had come, experiencing her scars being reopened again and again, rubbed back and forth as if afraid she would forget, how uncomfortable would that be?

---

想来也怪朱淑真，谁让她喜欢把个人的生活写在诗词中？被她同时代的人议论也就罢了，还会被一代又一代的人拿来谈论，真是可悲可叹啊！

Thinking about it, it was also Zhu Shuzhen's fault. Who told her to like writing about her personal life in poetry and ci? Being discussed by people of her own time was bad enough, but she would also be talked about by generation after generation. It was truly lamentable and sigh-worthy!

---

申简辰决定，有机会见到朱淑真本人一定给她提个醒，不要再把个人的生活写在诗词中，被人当八卦一样议论，实在不雅。

Shen Jianchen decided that if he had the chance to meet Zhu Shuzhen in person, he would definitely remind her not to write about her personal life in poetry and ci anymore. Being discussed like gossip was really indecent.

---

苗知瑜的话说到乔文仙心里，他向她投以微笑，仍旧品道：“‘悔’字用得极妙，既写出幽栖居士自悔有失大家闺秀的风范而动了粗口，也写出她后悔听从父母之命登上花轿嫁到林家，更让我们这些品鉴者在失落之余看到她努力保持心性，不愿被一段不符合理想的婚姻所改变。‘有上交无下交’说明婚姻的可悲之处在于娶了或嫁了没有共同志趣之人。最后，她肯定地说出，不要问什么对与错，人世间最悲哀的莫过于心死。心不死，用刀剑斩断还会藕断丝连，心若死了，就一点机会都没有。”

Miao Zhiyu's words touched Qiao Wenxian's heart. He threw her a smile and continued appreciating: "The character 'regret' is used extremely well. It not only shows Youqi Jushi regretting losing the demeanor of a distinguished lady and using coarse language, but also shows her regret in obeying her parents' orders and getting into the bridal sedan to marry into the Lin family. It also lets us who appreciate see her effort to maintain her nature despite disappointment, unwilling to be changed by a marriage that doesn't match her ideals. 'Having superiors but no inferiors' shows that the tragedy of marriage lies in marrying someone without common interests. Finally, she says with certainty, don't ask about right and wrong. The greatest sorrow in the human world is the death of the heart. If the heart doesn't die, even if cut with swords and knives, the lotus root silk will still connect. If the heart dies, there's no chance at all."

---

乔文仙品鉴完，除了申简辰，其他人都无不替朱淑真感伤。

After Qiao Wenxian finished appreciating, except for Shen Jianchen, everyone else felt sad for Zhu Shuzhen.

---

东方尘泽说：“还是乔兄最了解幽栖居士，解析得很到位，令我等佩服。不知申公子与乔姑娘有何见解？”

Dongfang Chenze said, "Brother Qiao is the one who understands Youqi Jushi best. The analysis is very thorough and admirable. I wonder what insights Young Master Shen and Miss Qiao have?"

---

乔梦容端起酒盅：“诸位的品鉴既全面又独到，也已将梦容想说的都说了，我以水酒回敬大家，表达我的赞叹之情。”

Qiao Mengrong picked up her wine cup: "Everyone's appreciation is both comprehensive and unique, and you've already said everything I wanted to say. I'll toast everyone with this wine to express my admiration."

---

乔梦容饮完，放下酒盅看向哥哥。

Qiao Mengrong finished drinking, put down the cup, and looked at her brother.

---

申简辰不想把他人的隐私拿来不断琢磨，再说他心中一直纠结找千里寻之事：“能够与各位相识，我倍感荣幸，品鉴诗词我并不擅长，之所以跟文渊前来，是想问大家一个问题，不知三位公子平日是否给说话艺人供书稿？”

Shen Jianchen didn't want to keep pondering over others' privacy. Besides, he had always been troubled by the matter of finding Qianli Xun: "I feel honored to meet you all. I'm not good at appreciating poetry and ci. The reason I came with Wenyuan is to ask everyone a question. I wonder if the three young masters usually provide manuscripts to storytellers?"

---

三位公子都摇摇头。

The three young masters all shook their heads.

---

安明轩说：“我们三位偶尔听听说书，从来不写下里巴人之作。”

An Mingxuan said, "The three of us occasionally listen to storytelling, but we never write lowbrow works."

---

申简辰又问：“你们可认识一位名叫千里寻的才子？”

Shen Jianchen asked again, "Do you know a talented young man named Qianli Xun?"

---

曹班说：“从未听说过。”

Cao Ban said, "I've never heard of him."

---

东方尘泽说：“申公子提到的千里寻可是给说话艺人供书稿的才子？”

Dongfang Chenze said, "Is the Qianli Xun that Young Master Shen mentioned the talented young man who provides manuscripts to storytellers?"

---

“正是，难道东方公子知道他？”申简辰充满希望。

"Exactly. Does Young Master Dongfang know him?" Shen Jianchen was full of hope.

---

东方尘泽否认道：“我不认识也没听说过，不过给说话艺人供稿的文人往往是些科举无门的落魄书生，靠此维持生计，要想找这类人人，得去东仁街，那里是落魄书生聚集之地。”

Dongfang Chenze denied, "I don't know him and have never heard of him. But the literati who provide manuscripts to storytellers are often down-and-out scholars who have no way to pass the imperial examinations. They rely on this to make a living. To find such people, you need to go to Dongren Street. That's where down-and-out scholars gather."

---

听到这话，申简辰不免担忧。千里寻穿越而来，成为男子不说，还可能是落魄书生，生活可想而知。而他却十分幸运成为王爷，不仅锦衣玉食，还有一位温柔痴情的妹妹。

Hearing this, Shen Jianchen couldn't help but worry. Qianli Xun had transmigrated and not only become a man, but might also be a down-and-out scholar. One could imagine his life. Yet he himself was extremely lucky to become a prince, not only eating fine food and wearing fine clothes, but also having a gentle and infatuated sister.

---

不行！一定要找到千里寻，改变他的生活，让他和自己一样过上好日子。

No! He must find Qianli Xun and change his life, letting him live as well as he does.

---

“感谢东方公子告知。”

"Thank you for telling me, Young Master Dongfang."

---

千里寻是谁？乔梦容在心里琢磨着，哥哥为何要找此人？

Who is Qianli Xun? Qiao Mengrong pondered in her heart. Why was her brother looking for this person?

---

不过听哥哥刚才所说，要找之人是位才子，乔梦容还是松了一口气，只要哥哥心里没有其他女子，想找谁她都无不支持。

But hearing what her brother just said, the person he was looking for was a talented young man. Qiao Mengrong still breathed a sigh of relief. As long as her brother didn't have other women in his heart, she would support him no matter who he wanted to find.

---

乔文仙还沉浸在对朱淑真情感的体验中，仪王问起千里寻之事他并不关心。

Qiao Wenxian was still immersed in the experience of Zhu Shuzhen's emotions. He didn't care about the prince asking about Qianli Xun.

---

化雨与南双见大家已赏完词，将卷轴收起。

Huayu and Nanshuang saw that everyone had finished appreciating the ci poem and rolled up the scroll.

---

南双走到公子跟前，悄悄在他耳边说：“公子，你也有幽栖居士新写的词。”

Nanshuang walked to his young master and whispered in his ear, "Young master, you also have Youqi Jushi's new ci poem."

---

乔文仙才想起，他埋怨自己因纠结于《断肠谜》中“问苍天，人在何方？”忘记了朱淑真的另一首词。

Only then did Qiao Wenxian remember. He blamed himself for being so entangled in "Ask the heavens, where is the person?" in "The Heartbreak Riddle" that he forgot about Zhu Shuzhen's other ci poem.

---

他向众人说：“我也有幽栖居士另一首新词，不过，是她写废的手稿，大家不要嫌弃。”

He said to everyone: "I also have another new ci poem by Youqi Jushi, but it's a discarded draft. Please don't despise it."

---

东方尘泽说：“怎会嫌弃？我等早都听说，经常有人躲在林府大门外，专等着捡拾幽栖居士写废的手稿。乔兄还是尽快拿出来吧，好让我们一饱眼福。”

Dongfang Chenze said, "How could we despise it? We've long heard that there are often people hiding outside the Lin family's main gate, waiting specifically to pick up Youqi Jushi's discarded drafts. Brother Qiao, please bring it out quickly so we can feast our eyes on it."

---

申简辰觉得可笑，写废的手稿都有人当宝贝，不是脑子有问题又是什么？又觉得不对，文渊得到的手稿正是南双捡拾来的，他的脑子倒没有任何问题，只因太喜欢朱淑真，才会出现让人难以理解的行为。

Shen Jianchen found it ridiculous. People treasuring discarded drafts—weren't they out of their minds? Then he thought it was wrong. The draft Wenyuan got was exactly what Nanshuang picked up. His mind wasn't having any problems. It was just because he liked Zhu Shuzhen so much that he exhibited behavior that others couldn't understand.

---

南双从怀里掏出捡拾的手稿，当着众人的面展开。大家朝手稿看去，注意到掉在“伊”字上的黑色墨迹。

Nanshuang took out the picked-up draft from his bosom and unfolded it in front of everyone. Everyone looked at the draft and noticed the black ink stain dripping on the character "yi" (she).

---

苗知瑜第一个说话：“我怎么觉得笔迹与幽栖居士的不太像。”

Miao Zhiyu spoke first: "Why do I feel the handwriting is not quite like Youqi Jushi's?"

---

东方尘泽也说：“仔细看确实不太像，那日林子孝拿的手稿我在誊抄时，也有所怀疑，倒没多想，苗姑娘既如此认为，证明我的怀疑没错。”

Dongfang Chenze also said, "Looking carefully, it's indeed not quite like. That day when Lin Zixiao brought the draft, I also had some doubts when I was copying it, but I didn't think much about it. Since Miss Miao thinks so, it proves my suspicion was right."

---

曹班说：“嗯……是有些不一样，只是说不清楚哪里不一样。”

Cao Ban said, "Hmm... it is a bit different, but I can't clearly say what's different."

---

安明轩说：“幽栖居士的笔迹偏柔和，而这笔迹明显是想要模仿，可又模仿不出柔美的笔韵，反而刚劲外露。”

An Mingxuan said, "Youqi Jushi's handwriting tends to be soft and delicate, but this handwriting is obviously trying to imitate, yet it can't imitate the soft and beautiful rhythm. Instead, it exudes a rigid strength."

---

南双以为几位公子怀疑不是幽栖居士所写，赶紧解释：“三位公子，苗姑娘，这可是我守在林府门口好不容易得来的，还是幽栖居士的贴身丫鬟苦荞姐姐亲手送给我的，绝对是真迹！你们千万不要瞎怀疑！”

Nanshuang thought the several young masters suspected it wasn't written by Youqi Jushi and hurriedly explained: "Three young masters, Miss Miao, this was obtained by me after guarding the Lin family gate with great effort. It was personally given to me by Sister Kuqiao, Youqi Jushi's personal maid. It's definitely authentic! Please don't suspect blindly!"

---

乔文仙说：“南双拿到的肯定是幽栖居士的笔迹，词中所写的内容以及蕴含的悲戚之情，能判断出是她的词。我在汴梁第一眼看到这首词，也对笔迹产生过怀疑。回到临安，听南双说她变得厉害，又听苗姑娘说她会功夫，一段婚姻让她变得跟以往不同，人变了，笔迹变化亦属正常。”

Qiao Wenxian said, "What Nanshuang got is definitely Youqi Jushi's handwriting. The content written in the ci poem and the sorrowful emotions contained in it can determine it's her ci. When I first saw this ci poem in Bianliang, I also had doubts about the handwriting. After returning to Lin'an, I heard from Nanshuang that she had become very different, and then I heard from Miss Miao that she knew martial arts. A marriage made her different from before. Since the person changed, it's normal for the handwriting to change as well."

---

东方尘泽说：“我想起来，苦荞说幽栖居士被练兵官打得失忆，很多事情记不起来，这么想来，笔迹变化再正常不过。”

Dongfang Chenze said, "I remember now. Kuqiao said Youqi Jushi was beaten by the Military Training Officer until she lost her memory and couldn't remember many things. Thinking about it this way, it's perfectly normal for the handwriting to change."

---

“失忆？”乔文仙十分担忧，“她可还记得我们？”

"Lost her memory?" Qiao Wenxian was very worried. "Does she still remember us?"

---

东方尘泽回道：“她说记得一些，可我看够呛，她连陈浩宇都不认识。”

Dongfang Chenze replied, "She said she remembers some, but I think it's doubtful. She didn't even recognize Chen Haoyu."

---

乔文仙一阵失落，也许她也把他忘了。又转念一想，岂不是也忘记徐公子？立刻又觉得开心：“忘记以前的人和事，对她有来讲也许不错。”

Qiao Wenxian felt a wave of disappointment. Perhaps she had also forgotten him. Then he thought again—wouldn't that mean she also forgot Young Master Xu? He immediately felt happy again: “Forgetting the people and things of the past might be good for her.”

---

众人谈论幽栖居士的变化，并未引起申简辰过分在意。他只觉这位江南才女实在不幸，所嫁非人不说，还遭受家暴，又搞得失忆。也佩服她明明会功夫，为离开丈夫硬生生忍着，宁可被打得失忆都不还一拳。

Everyone was discussing the changes in Youqi Jushi, which didn't attract Shen Jianchen's excessive attention. He only felt that this talented woman of Jiangnan was truly unfortunate. Not only did she marry the wrong person, but she also suffered domestic violence and even lost her memory. He also admired her for clearly knowing martial arts, yet enduring it to leave her husband, willing to be beaten until she lost her memory without throwing a single punch.

---

总管与厨师进来，手中端着盘子，盘内上面放着菜肴。

The manager and chef came in, holding plates with dishes on them.

---

总管招呼道：“各位公子、姑娘，菜已做好，我与厨师给大家摆上，你们可以慢慢享用，陈公子说过，想待到什么时候都可以，晚上住这里也行，客房都预备着。”

The manager greeted: "Young masters and young ladies, the dishes are ready. The chef and I will set them up for you. You can enjoy them at your leisure. Young Master Chen said you can stay as long as you want. You can even stay the night. The guest rooms are all prepared."

---

东方尘泽说：“既然饭菜已好，我们先享用，享用完后再品幽栖居士的新词。”

Dongfang Chenze said, "Since the food is ready, let's enjoy it first, and then appreciate Youqi Jushi's new ci poem."

---

南双收了手稿，整理好装进怀里。

Nanshuang collected the draft, organized it, and put it in his bosom.

---

盘子里的菜摆完后，厨师出去推进来一个木制的架子，上面放满菜肴。

After the dishes on the plates were set up, the chef went out and pushed in a wooden rack filled with dishes.

---

南双见都没见过，主动上前帮忙摆放。每端起一道菜，他都会用眼神认真照顾一番，吃不到嘴里，看看也过瘾。南双心想，回去后在白风面前有的炫耀。

Nanshuang had never seen such things before and took the initiative to go forward and help set them up. Every time he picked up a dish, he would carefully look at it with his eyes. He couldn't eat it, but just looking at it was satisfying. Nanshuang thought to himself that he would have something to show off in front of Bai Feng when he went back.

---

苗知瑜给身后的丫鬟示意，丫鬟端起桌上的酒壶，给大家斟满。喜兰见了，也拿起另一只酒壶帮忙。

Miao Zhiyu signaled to the maid behind her. The maid picked up the wine pot on the table and filled everyone's cups. Xilan saw this and also picked up another wine pot to help.

---

美味佳肴摆放完毕后，总管有礼道：“大家可以享用，有什么不满意的尽管告诉厨师，他会立刻重做，直到诸位满意为止。”

After the delicious dishes were set up, the manager said politely: "You can enjoy them. If there's anything unsatisfactory, please tell the chef. He will immediately remake it until you are all satisfied."

---

东方尘泽说：“辛苦总管与厨师，你们去忙吧，需要时定会打扰。”

Dongfang Chenze said, "Thank you for your hard work, manager and chef. You can go attend to your duties. We will definitely bother you when needed."

---

总管与厨师退去。

The manager and chef withdrew.

---

申简辰瞧着面前一桌菜肴，内心慨叹，陈公子竟然比他这位王爷吃得还要好，陈家不知得多有钱。

Shen Jianchen looked at the table of dishes in front of him and sighed inwardly. Young Master Chen actually ate better than him, a prince. The Chen family must be incredibly rich.

---

曹班说：“陈浩宇的日子过得真够奢侈，这些菜肴，我见都没见过，吃了他这么好的菜，下次见面都不好意思喊他纨绔子弟。”

Cao Ban said, "Chen Haoyu's life is truly luxurious. I've never even seen these dishes. After eating such good food from him, I'll feel embarrassed to call him a dandy next time we meet."

---

安明轩批评道：“一桌菜就把你收买，太没骨气，我可是菜照吃，纨绔子弟照样喊，再说他是感谢东方兄，东方兄与他互不相欠，我们与他也互不相欠。”

An Mingxuan criticized: "A table of dishes has already bought you over. You have no backbone. I'll eat the dishes as usual and still call him a dandy. Besides, he's thanking Brother Dongfang. Brother Dongfang and he are even, and we and he are also even."

---

安明轩的说法得到大家认同，众人举杯互相敬酒后，开始享用美食。

An Mingxuan's statement was agreed upon by everyone. After toasting each other, everyone began to enjoy the delicacies.

---

身边的丫鬟、小厮和书童要为主人服务，只有看的份儿。主人们吃得差不多时，他们才有机会坐下享用。菜又多，量又足，主人们根本吃不完，有他们参与，美味才不会被浪费。南双替白风感到可惜，如果他在，又能有好好美餐一顿的机会。

The maids, pages, and book boys beside them had to serve their masters and could only watch. Only when the masters had eaten enough did they have the chance to sit down and eat. There were so many dishes and the portions were so large that the masters couldn't finish them all. With their participation, the delicacies wouldn't be wasted. Nanshuang felt sorry for Bai Feng. If he were here, he would have another chance to have a good meal.

---

享用完美食，把朱淑真的词拿出来又品鉴一番，诗会算是真正结束。来到楼下欣赏一会儿周围的风景，才打道回府。毕竟有两位姑娘跟着，不好在外留宿。

After enjoying the delicacies, they took out Zhu Shuzhen's ci poem and appreciated it again. The poetry gathering was truly over. They came downstairs to appreciate the surrounding scenery for a while before heading home. After all, there were two young ladies with them, so it wasn't appropriate to stay overnight outside.

---

这一日对申简辰来讲毫无意义，他打算以后不再跟文渊参加此类诗会，倒不是因为他不喜欢诗词，而是因为他不想像今日一样，借品鉴诗词谈论他人的隐私。

This day was meaningless to Shen Jianchen. He decided not to attend such poetry gatherings with Wenyuan in the future. It wasn't because he didn't like poetry and ci, but because he didn't want to be like today, discussing others' privacy under the guise of appreciating poetry and ci.
