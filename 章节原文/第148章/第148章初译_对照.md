第148章 商量生意办诗会
## Chapter 148: Debating Trade and Planning a Poetry Salon

陈浩东来到春香楼外，不一会儿，从里面出来一位商妓，身着冬装，依然掩饰不住浑身上下的媚态。
Waiting outside Chunxiang Brothel, Chen Haodong watched a courtesan emerge moments later, heavy winter silk failing to hide her sultry sway.

那商妓抬手向陈浩东打招呼，迎上去：“陈大公子，我在楼上瞧见您站在外面，急忙下来迎接，外面冷，要不跟我进去快活快活？”商妓说完去拉陈浩东的胳膊。
Waving as she sidled close, she cooed, "Spotted you from upstairs, Eldest Young Master Chen, and hurried right down! Freezing out here—come inside and let me warm you up?" Her fingers reached for his sleeve.

陈浩东向后退去：“大街上，金蝉姑娘还是不要离我太近，明日亥时直接去老地方。”
Stepping back sharp, Chen Haodong warned, "Not out on a public street, Miss Jinchan—meet me at our usual spot tomorrow at the hour of hai."

金蝉娇嗔着声音，不满道：“陈大公子真会装，这一点不如您那堂弟，他可是明目张胆花大价钱买了一位黄花大姑娘，这会儿正快活着呢。”
Pouting with a playful whine, Jinchan teased, "Always putting on airs, Eldest Young Master—nothing like your cousin! He just dropped a small fortune buying a fresh virgin's first night and is living it up upstairs right now!"

“我怎能与他相比？需小心谨慎。你来得正好，帮我把他身边跟着的人叫出来。”
"I'm not him—I play careful. Good timing, though: go fetch one of the guys tagging along with him."

“陈家二公子身边跟了好几位，大公子找哪个？”
"Second Young Master brought half a crowd—which one?"

“脸上有刀疤的。”
"The one with the knife scar across his cheek."

“哦，是疤子，等着，我去替您叫。”
"Oh, Scarface! Wait right here."

“要保密，不许被陈浩宇知道。”
"Keep it quiet—don't let Chen Haoyu catch wind."

“放心，他正快活，顾不上其他。”
"Relax, he's way too busy upstairs to notice a thing."

金蝉进入春香楼后没多久，陈浩宇身边的一位小弟出来，见到陈浩东，立刻上前拜见。
Moments after Jinchan ducked inside, one of Chen Haoyu's cronies stepped out and hurried up to bow to Chen Haodong.

“大公子有何吩咐？”
"What can I do for Eldest Young Master?"

陈浩东拿出一袋银子：“给你的，把你知道的都告诉我。”
Tossing him a pouch of silver: "Yours. Spill everything you know."

疤子接过钱袋，笑嘻嘻地说：“我知道陈公子与朱家姑娘一个不想娶，一个不想嫁。”
Weighing the silver with a greasy grin, Scarface whispered: "First off, neither Young Master Chen nor Miss Zhu wants this wedding."

“你可知为何两家会订亲？”
"Do you know why the two families got betrothed?"

“本来陈公子不想告诉我们，结果到春香楼一高兴，我再哄哄他，便全说了。”
"Young Master Chen kept mum at first—then once wine flowed at Chunxiang Brothel and I flattered him right, he spilled the whole pot!"

“因为什么？”
"Why?"

“陈公子散播谣言之事被知府大人知道，把他抓起来，逼他娶朱家姑娘。”
"Prefect Zhu found out Young Master Chen paid for those smears against Miss Zhu, nabbed him, and forced him to marry her!"

“散播谣言？难道朱家姑娘的谣言是他散播的？你为何之前不告诉我？”
"Wait—he started the rumors about Miss Zhu?! Why didn't you tell me sooner?!"

“是陈公子散播的，我还是帮凶，之所以没告诉大公子，是因为此事闹得太大，知府大人又在查，所以不敢告知任何人，现在也算有个了结，不用再藏着掖着。”
"His idea, and I helped run it! Kept my mouth shut while it was hot and the Prefect was hunting heads—now that it's settled, no harm talking."

“原来如此。”打听到这么重大的消息，陈浩东十分满意，“以后记着多打听，银子少不了你的。”
"Aha!" Delighted with that goldmine, Chen Haodong patted his arm: "Keep your ears peeled—plenty more silver where that came from!"

疤子很乐意：“陈大公子放心，您那堂弟好哄着呢，只要把他哄得开心，拿我们当真兄弟，毫无保留。”
Scarface grinned: "Count on me, Eldest Young Master! Your cousin's easy to work—stroke his ego and he treats us like blood brothers, hiding nothing!"

陈浩东叮嘱疤子继续将陈浩宇哄好，打探到有用的消息，随时告诉他。
Charging Scarface to keep buttering Chen Haoyu up and bring any fresh intel straight to him, Chen Haodong dismissed the thug.

陈浩东回到陈府，又来到父亲居住的院子。
Returning to Chen Manor, Chen Haodong went straight to his father's courtyard.

陈江树正打算去找大哥商议开拓绸缎庄生意之事，被陈浩东挡在门口。
Just stepping out to visit his eldest brother about expanding their silk trade, Chen Jiangshu paused as his son blocked the doorway.

“爹，儿子打听到重大消息。”
"Huge news, Father!"

陈江树转身走进里屋，陈浩东紧随其后。
Chen Jiangshu turned back into the inner room, Chen Haodong stepping close on his heels.

“什么重大消息？”
"What huge news?"

“父亲可知朱家姑娘的流言是谁人散播？”
"Guess who started all those rumors about Miss Zhu?"

“谁？”
"Who?"

“浩宇。”
"Haoyu!"

陈江树恍然大悟：“难怪那日知府大人将浩宇五花大绑，估计是知道此事。哼！终于明白大哥为何会同意与朱家订亲，他是被逼无奈，自家儿子惹下大麻烦，总得想办法解决。”
Everything clicked in Chen Jiangshu's head: "No wonder the Prefect hauled Haoyu in bound in ropes! Hmph—no mystery anymore why Eldest Brother agreed to this match! Pure damage control for his idiot son's mess!"

陈浩东笑道：“呵呵……，看来这门亲事不会成为浩宇继承产业的筹码，反而是他想甩掉的累赘。”
Chen Haodong chuckled: "Haha, looks like this wedding won't give Haoyu a single bargaining chip for the inheritance—just a millstone he's dying to shake off!"

“呵呵……”陈江树也笑道，“我儿不用再理会浩宇的亲事，把心用在生意上，在陈家长辈面前留下好印象，未来陈家的产业自然不会旁落他人。”
Chen Jiangshu laughed right along: "Forget Haoyu's wedding then! Pour your heart into the business, win over the clan elders, and the Chen fortune lands straight in your lap!"

陈浩东依然充满忧虑：“我听说奶奶对这门亲事也满意，她可是陈家长辈之一，对产业的继承人有否决权，她疼爱浩宇，肯定愿意浩宇成为继承人，难道她就不怕婚事影响浩宇的前程？”
Still, a shadow lingered in Chen Haodong's eyes: "Heard Grandmother approves of this match too—and she holds veto power over the heir! Spoiling Haoyu the way she does, wouldn't she worry this wedding hurts his shot?"

陈江树帮儿子仔细分析：“你奶奶喜欢浩宇不假，可面对陈家产业她一点儿都不含糊，我猜她同意浩宇娶朱家姑娘，肯定是知道传播谣言之人是他的心肝宝贝，不敢得罪朱家，只好答应。她能答应，也说明浩宇在她心中并非继承产业的最佳人选。”
Breaking it down cool for his son, Chen Jiangshu said, "Your grandmother dotes on Haoyu, sure, yet when it comes to the Chen empire she's sharp as a razor. Bet she only signed off because she knew her darling boy started the rumors and dared not cross the Prefect—and signing off proves even she knows Haoyu isn't cut out to lead the clan!"

“爹说得有道理，儿子以后定会更加努力。”父亲的话令陈浩东高兴了一会儿，很快又忧虑道，“可儿子听说浩宇跟朱家姑娘联合起来，想说服长辈退婚，只怕会闹出大事，将婚事搅黄。”
"Makes total sense, Father—I'll work twice as hard!" Cheered for a beat, another snag crossed his mind: "Wait—heard Haoyu and Miss Zhu teamed up to push both families into breaking the betrothal! What if they raise a storm and blow it up?"

陈江树想想说：“没想到朱家姑娘想法独特，并不把姑娘家的名声当回事。也对，名声都坏成那样，再坏些，也没什么。按说两家大人定下亲事，不可能出现退婚之事。可也需以防万一，先按兵不动，暗地里悄悄关注此事，如若出现不利的迹象，再采取行动不迟。”
Chen Jiangshu mused: "Bold girl, that Miss Zhu—doesn't even care about a maiden's reputation! Fair enough, once mud sticks, what's a little more? A betrothal sealed by both houses doesn't get undone on a whim—still, stay alert. Sit tight and watch from the shadows; if trouble brews, we step in."

“儿子记下。”
"Understood, Father."

“去吧，我还要找你大伯商议正事，再侧面打听浩宇的亲事。”
"Run along—I'm off to talk silk expansion with Eldest Brother and sound out Haoyu's wedding on the side."

儿子走后，陈江树来到大哥居住的院子。
After his son left, Chen Jiangshu walked over to his eldest brother's courtyard.

陈江林正在陪姚轻琰说话，两人聊得甚是开心，姚轻琰不时露出笑容。一旁的丫鬟也被两人感染，充满歆羡之情。
Inside, Chen Jianglin sat chatting warm with Yao Qingyan, drawing bright laugh after bright laugh while attending maids looked on in quiet envy.

有丫鬟进来报告：“老爷，二爷来访。”
A maid stepped in to announce: "Master, Second Master is here to see you."

陈江林说：“我陪夫人说会儿话，都要被打搅，什么时候将这一身责任交出去，每天陪夫人到处逛逛，时时刻刻陪你说话，那该多好。”
Chen Jianglin groaned with a smile: "Can't even chat with my wife without interruptions! Wish I could hand this empire off tomorrow and spend every day strolling and talking with you!"

姚轻琰十分理解丈夫：“老爷是忙大事的人，一闲下来就陪我，我已心满意足。二叔找你肯定有正事，我先退下。”
Smiling warm, Yao Qingyan stood: "You've got big responsibilities—giving me every spare hour is already heaven. Second Brother must have business; I'll leave you two."

陈江林点点头：“还是夫人体贴，等我与江树谈完事，再陪夫人说话。”
Chen Jianglin nodded: "Always so thoughtful! Once Jiangshu and I wrap up, I'm right back to you."

姚轻琰起身，带着丫鬟从后门出去，她打算在府内到处走走，顺便去看望婆婆。
Slipping out the rear door with her maid, Yao Qingyan went for a walk around the manor and a visit with her mother-in-law.

陈江树进屋后，不用大哥招呼，自然坐在他身旁的椅子上。丫鬟很懂事，很快端上一盏茶。
Entering without standing on ceremony, Chen Jiangshu took the chair beside his brother as a sharp maid brought out a bowl of tea.

等丫鬟退去，陈江林问：“二弟找我可是为拓展绸缎庄生意之事？”
Once the maid withdrew, Chen Jianglin asked, "Here about expanding our silk shops, Second Brother?"

“大哥真是料事如神，是为此事。”陈江树端起茶盏，品了一口。
"Reads minds as ever, Eldest Brother! Right on target." Taking a sip of tea, Chen Jiangshu smiled.

“哪是我料事如神？近期陈家生意上拓展绸缎庄的生意头等是大事，又是你负责，你这人有个毛病，找我从不聊家常，只说正事。我当然猜到你是为此事而来，怎么？已经想好在哪里拓展？”
"No mind-reading needed—silk expansion is our top move right now, and you never drop by for small talk, only business! Picked your target markets yet?"

陈江树放下茶盏：“还是大哥了解我，呵呵，弟确实已想好，所以来找大哥商议。”
Setting down his tea bowl: "Nobody knows me like Eldest Brother! Yes, mapped it out and came to run it past you."

“说来听听。”
"Let's hear it."

“陈家生意已拓展到各地，但都在大宋的范围之内，我们可否大胆些，再往大宋的北边和东北方想想。”
"We've already covered every corner of the Great Song—what if we push bold, northward and northeast?"

“你想将生意拓展到辽与金的领地？”
"Into Liao and Jin territory?"

陈江树点点头：“据弟了解，辽和金的百姓对大宋的丝绸很感兴趣，若是拓展到那些地方，生意必然好。”
Chen Jiangshu nodded: "Word is folk in both Liao and Jin go crazy for Song silk—open shops up there and profits will soar!"

陈江林沉默一会儿：“陈家生意之所以能做大，除资金雄厚，还在于一个‘稳’字，大宋北边局势不稳，只怕刚拓展过去，会血本无归。”
After a thoughtful pause, Chen Jianglin shook his head: "What built the Chen empire wasn't just deep pockets—it was one word: steady. The northern frontier is sitting on a powder keg; open up there and we risk losing our shirts."

“大哥为何会如此考虑？”陈江树很不理解，觉得大哥是多虑。
Baffled, thinking his brother overcautious, Chen Jiangshu asked, "Why so worried, Eldest Brother?"

“大宋与金结下海上之盟，目的是为共同对付辽，辽必然成为金囊中之物，故而北边与西北方局势不稳，陈家的生意不适合往那里拓展，你可以考虑一下西夏和吐蕃。”
"The Great Song just struck the Alliance Conducted at Sea with the Jin to smash the Liao. Once the Jin swallow the Liao, the whole north and northwest will burn with war—no place for Chen shops! Look toward Western Xia and Tubo instead."

想法被大哥否定，陈江树不满。大哥是主事人，他不好说什么，只得点头同意：“大哥考虑得极是，就按大哥说的办。”
Stung at getting shot down yet unable to defy the family head, Chen Jiangshu forced a nod: "Wise as ever, Eldest Brother—we'll go with Western Xia and Tubo."

正事已说完，不见二弟离开，陈江林无法与夫人聊天儿，催道：“二弟可还有事？”
Business done yet seeing his brother lingering—keeping him from his wife—Chen Jianglin nudged, "Anything else, Second Brother?"

陈江树大胆问出订婚一事：“大哥为浩宇定下朱家姑娘，弟甚是不解，朱家姑娘在外名声不好，浩宇若娶她，岂不被人笑话？”
Venturing onto thin ice, Chen Jiangshu asked, "Betrothing Haoyu to Miss Zhu puzzles me, Eldest Brother. With her name dragged through the mud, won't Haoyu marrying her make us a laughingstock?"

“二弟过虑，朱家姑娘不仅相貌好，又有才学，浩宇能娶到她也算幸运。至于外面议论朱家姑娘的谣言，我从来不信。文心对她的小姑子常常赞不绝口，我不信别人，还能不信她？若真成亲后，被人笑话，也无妨，自己的日子自己过，与他人无关。”
"Don't sweat it, Second Brother. Miss Zhu is gorgeous and brilliant—Haoyu's lucky to get her! As for street rumors, I don't buy a syllable. Wenxin raves about her sister-in-law nonstop; if I can't trust my own daughter, who can I trust? Even if outsiders laugh after the wedding, who cares—we live our own lives, not theirs."

大哥一脸真诚，陈江树无话可劝，本也不想劝。心想：“浩宇犯下大错，想挽回都难，大哥如此说，不过是怕得罪知府大人。哼！我还是耐心等着看浩宇的笑话吧。”
Facing his brother's genuine calm, Chen Jiangshu dropped it—he was not trying to stop it anyway! "Haoyu dug his own grave and Eldest Brother's just covering for him before the Prefect—sit back and enjoy the circus!" he sneered inside.

“大哥的眼界和心胸难有人及，弟受教。事已讲完，弟去忙拓展绸缎庄生意之事。”
"Nobody matches Eldest Brother's vision and generosity—lesson taken! Off to plan our silk expansion."

“去吧，有什么事，随时与我沟通。”陈江林说完又想起什么，“慢着。”
"Go on—keep me posted." Pausing, Chen Jianglin added: "Wait a second."

陈江树刚站起来要走，又停下：“大哥有何吩咐？”
Halting mid-rise: "Yes, Eldest Brother?"

“近期浩东表现不错，好几位长辈在我面前提起过，没事让他过来陪我说说话。”
"Haodong's been shining lately—several clan elders praised him to me. Tell him to drop by and chat with me whenever he's free."

大哥如此说，明显是要培养浩东，陈江树更是喜出望外：“浩东是陈家子弟，又在年轻一辈中年长些，自然要给弟弟们带个好头。弟回去，定将大哥的话转达给他，让他没事来陪大哥聊聊家常，这孩子最擅长与人拉家常。”
Clear signal Eldest Brother meant to groom Haodong! Elation surged in Chen Jiangshu: "Haodong's the eldest of his generation, duty-bound to set an example for his younger cousins! I'll pass word right away—that boy loves talking shop and family with you."

“二弟有个好儿子令大哥羡慕，浩宇与浩东比起来差远了，都是二弟教育得好，教育孩子这一点，你比大哥强。”
"Envy you raising a son like that, Second Brother—Haoyu isn't half the man Haodong is! You beat me hands down at raising kids."

“怎能怪大哥？老太太宠着，大哥也没办法。”
"Hardly your fault, Eldest Brother—Mother spoils him rotten; your hands are tied."

“说得也是。”
"True enough."

陈江树不想再与大哥聊下去：“大哥，弟先退下。”
Eager to carry the triumph home, Chen Jiangshu bowed: "I'll take my leave, Eldest Brother."

陈江林点点头，看着二弟离去的身影，想着儿子的婚事，姚轻琰已将母亲的意思转达给他。耗着！亏母亲想得出。
Nodding him out, Chen Jianglin's mind drifted back to his own son's wedding—and Yao Qingyan telling him Mother wanted to stall it! What a stunt!

母亲年纪大，他不愿违背母亲的意思，可要耗着婚事，陈江林老大不愿意，又不好前去劝说老人家，只好装作默认。反正离成亲还有几个月，到时说不定老太太的想法会改变。
Unwilling to cross his aging mother straight-on yet hating the idea of stalling, he had stayed quiet for now—months remained before spring, plenty of time for Old Madam Xia to cool down.

近期申简身心难安，主要因朱家与陈家的婚事。
Meanwhile, Shen Jianchen had not known a moment's peace since learning of the Zhu-Chen betrothal.

忧虑难以向人启齿，憋在心里十分难受，难道眼睁睁看着千里寻嫁给一个浪荡公子？申简辰绝对不允许这种事情发生。
Unable to breathe a word to anyone, agony gnawed at him inside—how could he stand by watching Qianli Xun marry a worthless rake?! Over his dead body!

可他身为王爷，总不能无缘无故阻止别人家的亲事。关键是千里寻根本不愿接受他，若她愿意，他会利用王爷的身份逼迫陈家退婚，再说服乔梦容，让她答应纳淑真为妾。他相信梦容定会答应。
Yet even a royal prince could not smash another family's betrothal without cause—and Qianli Xun would not give him an inch! Had she said yes, he would have used royal power to kill the Chen match and talked Qiao Mengrong into accepting Shuzhen—sure Mengrong would agree.

千里寻不愿意，搞得申简辰无计可施。他又不愿什么都不做就这么等下去，他决定举行一场诗会。
With Qianli Xun shutting him out cold, he stood cornered—yet refused to sit idle! An idea hit: host a poetry salon!

他被自己的想法逗乐，不得已才参加诗会的他，竟然会想到用这种方式见日思夜想之人。他决定要好好利用这次诗会，把该请的人都请到。
He laughed at himself—the guy who dreaded poetry salons now throwing one just to see the woman occupying every waking thought! And he would invite every player on the board!

申简辰来到乔梦容居住的院子，身边并未带白风。今日白风又跑去望月轩与苦荞约会，申简辰羡慕不已。为何他身边的侍卫想去望月轩就去？而自己堂堂王爷，却不能随心所欲。
Strolling over to Qiao Mengrong's courtyard alone—Bai Feng had run off to Wangyue Pavilion again to see Kuqiao, leaving Shen Jianchen green with envy that his own bodyguard strolled to Wangyue Pavilion whenever he pleased while a royal prince stood banned!

哥哥前来，乔梦容很开心，哥哥现在每日都来看她，也不常走出幽林别居，能看得出，他身边并无别的女子。
Qiao Mengrong beamed seeing him walk in. Lately he visited every day and rarely left Youlin Retreat, proving no other woman waited outside.

哥哥现在进入她的闺房，也从不让人禀报，乔梦容觉得失忆的哥哥终于把她当成更亲的人看待。
Even better, he no longer waited to be announced before entering her bedchamber—proof his amnesiac heart was warming closer to her!

乔梦容正在写诗，她停下笔：“哥哥，你今日想吃什么？我让厨房安排。”
Setting down her brush mid-poem, she smiled: "What does Brother feel like eating today? I'll tell the kitchen."

申简辰并未回答，而是问道：“你在写什么？”
Skipping the question, he asked, "What are you writing?"

“随便写两句诗。”乔梦容担心哥哥要看。
"Just scribbling two lines of verse." Shy, she hoped he would not ask to read it.

申简辰并不感兴趣：“你好好写，等举行诗会时，让大家也品品你的诗。”
Uninterested, Shen Jianchen said, "Keep at it—let everyone admire your poem at the poetry salon!"

“难道哥哥要举行诗会？”
"Brother is hosting a poetry salon?!"

“我打算后日在幽林别具举办一场诗会，把想请的人都请来。”
"Day after tomorrow right here at Youlin Retreat—inviting the whole crowd."

“也请淑真吗？”
"Inviting Shuzhen too?"

“当然要请幽栖居士，她可是江南女词人，我的诗会怎能少得了她？”
"Of course Youqi Jushi! She's Jiangnan's foremost female poet—can't hold a salon without her!"

“太好了，我又能与淑真见面。不过我写的诗只能自己读读，不适合拿到诗会中显摆，尤其是在淑真面前。”
"Wonderful! I get to see Shuzhen again! Though my little verses are just for myself—no way I'm showing them off in front of Shuzhen!"

申简辰犹豫一会儿：“我担心幽栖居士不愿来，要不你出面帮哥哥请她？”
Shen Jianchen hesitated a beat: "Afraid Youqi Jushi might turn me down—could you invite her for me?"

乔梦容自信道：“没问题，我不用出面，只需差喜兰去，她定会答应。”
Qiao Mengrong smiled with confidence: "Easy! Don't even have to go myself—just send Xilan and she'll say yes on the spot!"

“好，幽栖居士交给你，其他人哥哥来安排。”
"Deal—Youqi Jushi's on you, I'll handle everyone else."

“太好了，幽林别居好久没热闹过，我要吩咐下人好好安排，保准让哥哥满意。”
"Yay! Youlin Retreat hasn't buzzed with life in ages—I'll make the servants prepare everything spotless so Brother is proud!"

梦容的善良与毫不犹豫的支持，令申简辰内心愧疚，他决定以后加倍对梦容好，当然也只是哥哥对妹妹的好而已，即使以后不得已娶她，婚后他也只会把她当妹妹看待。
Watching Mengrong's sweet, instant devotion stabbed Shen Jianchen with fresh guilt. He vowed to spoil her twice as much for life—even if forced to wed her, he would cherish her like a treasured little sister forever.
