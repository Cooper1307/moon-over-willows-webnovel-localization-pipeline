# Chapter 22: The Divorce Showdown

All night, Qianli Xun suffered from the wound on her forehead. She hadn't expected the Corrosive Paste to be so potent. To get some sleep, she had no choice but to apply the other medicine. Only then did she finally drift off.

Early the next morning, she looked in the mirror and saw the wound had improved considerably. Qianli Xun took out the Corrosive Paste again and applied more to the wound. After breakfast, she picked out a pretty outfit, got ready, and took Kuqiao toward the north courtyard.

Halfway there, Kuqiao saw her miss stop. She knew Qianli Xun had gotten lost again and had to walk ahead to lead the way.

Compared to the Zhu residence, the Lin estate was in much worse shape. No one had bothered to design or maintain the grounds. Only the constant coming and going of servants had worn a path through the bare dirt. One good rain and it would turn into a muddy mess.

As the path widened and grew easier underfoot, Qianli Xun spotted a cluster of buildings. This had to be the north courtyard.

Just as they were about to reach the courtyard gate, a servant came up to them. "Young Madam, the Lin family members have all arrived. They're waiting inside."

Hating the title "Young Madam," Qianli Xun ignored the servant and walked straight in. Ahead lay the reception hall. She could already see people sitting inside.

Qianli Xun stepped across the courtyard stones, one slow step after another. Her heart pounded with equal parts excitement and dread. After today, she might finally escape this meaningless marriage. But if it fell through — what then?

She stepped inside the reception hall. It was quite spacious. A painting of peonies hung on the wall ahead, the flowers blooming in lush clusters. Before it stood a long, narrow table holding porcelain and an incense burner. The burner was empty—likely just for show.

In front of the long table was a square table, with chairs on both sides. On the left sat an elder, stroking his beard, looking refined and gentle.

Qianli Xun's gaze left the main seat and looked toward the left side seat. Four people sat in the front row, four in the back. Lin Jue was sitting in the front row, in the chair closest to the main seat.

Lin Jue noticed the wound on Zhu Shuzhen's forehead had worsened far more than yesterday. He guessed she'd done something to it herself. She was willing to hurt herself just to get away from him. A bitter, nameless feeling rose in his chest.

Qianli Xun looked to the right. The Old Ancestor hadn't arrived yet. Four chairs sat there, three of them empty. Only one held a woman. Assuming it must be some Zhu family elder who'd come to help, she went forward eagerly. "Thank you, Elder, for speaking on my behalf. May I ask how to address you?"

The woman smiled. "Young Madam doesn't recognize me? I'm the matchmaker between you and the Military Training Officer. You can call me Matchmaker Yan. The seats for the Zhu family elders are all empty, so I'm borrowing one."

Qianli Xun was speechless. Awkwardly, she sat in an empty chair, feeling only the throbbing pain in her forehead.

"Youqi Jushi, why is there a wound on your forehead?"

Someone among the Lin family elders actually called her "Youqi Jushi." Qianli Xun thought this person must be a poetry lover.

She dabbed around the wound with a silk handkerchief, showing a pained expression. Looking toward the main seat, she said in a weak, pitiful tone, "Elder, this wound on my forehead was caused by Lin Jue. You don't know—he often strikes me. Because of this injury, I can't remember anything. I don't even know how to address you."

She heard that elder say, "Lin Jue, nephew, to marry Youqi Jushi is a blessing cultivated over many lifetimes. Yet you don't cherish it. How could you hit her? Beat her until she lost her memory? No wonder Youqi Jushi wants a divorce from you!"

Lin Jue said anxiously, "Second Uncle, there are many things you don't know. You can't just listen to her side of the story."

Lin Jue's second uncle was none other than Lin Zixiao. He often attended poetry gatherings organized by Zhu Xiyan and greatly admired Zhu Shuzhen's talent. For his nephew to marry such a talented woman was, in his view, a stroke of extraordinary fortune.

Now that things had come to divorce, he was completely against it. He ordered, "Lin Jue, nephew, right now in front of your clan uncles and two older brothers, kneel before Youqi Jushi. Admit your mistakes and beg her forgiveness! Then swear you'll never raise a hand against Youqi Jushi again!"

Lin Jue didn't want a divorce, nor did he want to kneel before Zhu Shuzhen in public. In his view, it wasn't entirely his fault things had come to this.

Lin Jue stood up. "Second Uncle, please calm down. I'll admit my mistakes when the time is right. This isn't the moment. For today's divorce proceedings, so many from the Lin family have come, yet not a single soul from the Zhu family. Uncles, Big Brother, Second Brother—what do you think that means?"

One of them spoke up. "Could it be that Prefect Zhu doesn't approve of the divorce at all?"

Lin Jue nodded. "Big Brother hit the nail on the head. That's exactly it."

A clan uncle advised, "Niece-in-law, since your father disagrees and no elders from the Zhu family have shown up, stop this nonsense. Go back to the west courtyard and live properly."

If Qianli Xun didn't speak now, wouldn't she be forced into submission? She endured the pain from the Corrosive Paste. "My parents aren't coming, but that doesn't mean other Zhu family elders won't come. The time hasn't arrived yet. Why are you in such a hurry? Can't you wait a little longer?"

Lin Zixiao said, "Let's give Youqi Jushi some time. Let's wait patiently."

The Lin family elders all agreed. Lin Jue could only sit back in his seat, saying resentfully, "If no one arrives by the hour of Si—nine in the morning—the divorce fails. And you'll never bring it up again."

Qianli Xun ignored Lin Jue, thinking about how to make better use of the wound. Otherwise, she'd bought the medicine and endured the pain for nothing.

"Elders, Lin Jue has injured me like this. If I continue living with him, I'm afraid I'll lose my life. So I must have this divorce. I hope the elders will support me."

An elder said, "Niece-in-law, Lin Jue hitting you is wrong. But when a man beats his wife, the woman must have done something wrong too. Please think about where you went wrong."

Qianli Xun was furious. She jumped up, about to curse the elder who spoke. But then she heard a voice from outside.

"That's wrong. No matter what the woman does, if a man beats her, the man is in the wrong. Besides, our Zhu family's daughter is educated and proper. What fault could she possibly have?"

Those words doused Qianli Xun's anger in an instant. Having someone in your corner — now that was power. She and everyone else turned toward the door. The Old Ancestor was coming, prayer beads in hand, walking at a steady pace. Matron Xiuzhen trailed behind without so much as offering an arm. Who would have thought a hundred-and-twenty-year-old would walk with such sure footing?

Qianli Xun went forward to greet her. "Thank you, Old Ancestor, for speaking on my behalf."

When the Lin family heard it was the Old Ancestor, they all stood up to welcome her.

Lin Zixiao took the lead. "We respectfully welcome the Old Ancestor. It is an honor to see you in person today. A great fortune for us juniors."

Matchmaker Yan, standing to the side, also stood up. Everyone in Lin'an knew the Zhu family had a long-lived Old Ancestor. Today she was fortunate enough to meet her. Naturally, Matchmaker Yan was extremely respectful.

The Old Ancestor walked into the reception hall and scanned the Lin family members. "You youngsters dare to gang up and bully my granddaughter? I represent the Zhu family to stand up for her and demand an explanation."

Although Lin Jue stood up with the elders to welcome her, he felt disdain inside. "So many elders from the Lin family have come, but only you from the Zhu family. This clearly shows Prefect Zhu disagrees with the divorce. Since the Old Ancestor has come, why not help persuade my wife to drop this divorce nonsense?"

The Old Ancestor stared at Lin Jue for a moment. "You are my granddaughter's husband?"

Lin Jue replied, "Yes."

The Old Ancestor turned back to face her granddaughter. The wound on her forehead was much worse than last time. She looked at Lin Jue again. "You beat my granddaughter and even brought a merchant courtesan home. How can our Zhu family let her continue living with you? Yes, only I came from the Zhu family. But can't I alone represent all the Zhu family elders?"

Lin Zixiao said, "Yes, of course. Old Ancestor, please don't mind the young ones. Please, take the main seat."

The other Lin family elders all agreed.

At this moment, Qianli Xun felt the weight of having a powerful elder on her side. She went forward to help the Old Ancestor, guiding her to the main seat on the right. Then she meekly returned to her own seat, savoring the security of such backing.

Once the Old Ancestor sat on the right, Lin Zixiao didn't dare sit on the left. He ordered a servant nearby to arrange a chair for him in the side seat.

The Old Ancestor said, "You are someone of status among the Lin family elders. Just sit next to me."

Lin Zixiao said modestly, "Since the Old Ancestor has invited me to sit, I shall obey. I just didn't expect you to know of me."

"I don't know you, but my eyes can observe. Everyone sit down. Let's discuss these two children's divorce calmly."

At the Old Ancestor's order, everyone dutifully took their seats.

Lin Zixiao spoke first. "Since the Old Ancestor has graced us with her presence, let me lay out the wishes of the Lin family. We all believe Lin Jue marrying Youqi Jushi is the greatest blessing our ancestors could have bestowed. As for the divorce — the Lin family sees it as unwise. We hope the young couple can make their marriage work."

The Old Ancestor faced her granddaughter. "What is your attitude? You might as well explain it to the elders."

Qianli Xun had already made her position clear, but the Old Ancestor was giving her the floor. She seized the chance to lay it all out.

"Old Ancestor, elders. Although I've lost my memory and can't remember how Lin Jue treated me before, the pain is still here. After being battered unconscious by him and waking up, the first thing I saw was him being very intimate with a merchant courtesan. How could I endure such humiliation? I even asked him to discard me. Perhaps it was his conscience that gave him the idea of a mutual divorce, leading to today's situation. I'm not even afraid of being discarded—how much less a mutual divorce?"

The Old Ancestor fingered her prayer beads, her eyelids lowered, listening carefully to her granddaughter. After her granddaughter finished, she continued, "As the saying goes, better to demolish ten temples than destroy one marriage. But our Zhu family granddaughter has truly suffered. We can't just let her stay in that fire pit. Today I want to help her break this marriage. I hope the Lin family won't obstruct it. Let these two children part amicably."

Before the Lin family elders could speak, Lin Jue hurriedly said, "Old Ancestor, there's something you don't know. If she had consummated the marriage on our wedding night, I wouldn't have gone out to find a merchant courtesan. If she hadn't ignored me and gone mingling with poets and scholars, I wouldn't have hit her."

He paused, then continued, "I can guarantee in front of the Old Ancestor, the Lin family elders, and my two brothers—as long as Shuzhen doesn't leave me, I'll never beat her again. If she agrees to let me take a concubine, I swear I will never visit a merchant courtesan again. I am even willing to let her freely attend her poetry gatherings. All I ask is that she remembers she's married and doesn't go chasing after those flowery poets."

The Lin family elders all nodded after hearing this.

Lin Zixiao said, "Old Ancestor, it's clear that Lin Jue is deeply reluctant to let go of Youqi Jushi. Since he has a heart to reform, why not give them a chance? Besides, a divorced woman's reputation always takes a hit. Youqi Jushi is known as the female ci poet of Jiangnan. This old man would hate to see any stain on that reputation."

Qianli Xun began to admire Lin Jue. Although he was a martial artist, at crucial moments he could speak in a way that won people's hearts. It seemed he had made thorough preparations, wanting to extinguish her desire for divorce in front of the elders. How could she let him have his way? This marriage must end! If applying the Corrosive Paste didn't work, then why continue enduring the pain?

"Kuqiao, give me the medicine to treat the wound."

Kuqiao took out the medicine bottle, squeezed out some ointment, and applied it to her miss's wound. A cool feeling spread across her skin, and the burning pain faded fast. Her mind cleared at once.

Qianli Xun stood up and bowed. "Old Ancestor, elders. My decision to divorce is final. Lin Jue and I are mismatched in talent and at odds in every interest. Living together is like chewing ash — bitter and pointless. No matter what he says or how many promises he makes, I will not change my mind. He claims I attend poetry gatherings and cozy up to scholars — pure slander! It proves he neither trusts me nor understands me. What's the point of staying with such a man?"

The Old Ancestor stopped fingering her prayer beads. "Xiuzhen, take it out. Show it to the young master. If he can guess the riddle's answer, I'll persuade my granddaughter not to think about divorce anymore. If he can't guess it, please return my granddaughter's freedom."

Matron Xiuzhen took out folded paper from her bosom and carefully unfolded it. On it was exactly "The Heartbreak Riddle" that Qianli Xun had written from memory.

Matron Xiuzhen walked up to Lin Jue and handed it to him. "Please examine it, Young Master. After examining, say the answer in front of everyone."

Lin Jue took it. He recognized all the characters on it and could read every line. But the answer eluded him.
