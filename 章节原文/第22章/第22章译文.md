# Chapter 22: The Divorce Showdown

All night long, the wound on Qianli Xun's forehead burned like fire—Corrosive Paste packed a real punch. Just to catch some sleep, she had to dab on a bit of the healing salve before dozing off.

First thing in the morning, checking the mirror showed the cut much calmer—so she dabbed fresh Corrosive Paste right back on! After breakfast she picked out a fine dress, tidied up, and set off with Kuqiao toward the North Courtyard.

Halfway there she halted again, lost as ever; Kuqiao stepped ahead to guide.

Lin Manor paled beside Zhu Manor's landscaped beauty—barely designed at all, with dirt paths worn down purely by servants' feet, bound to turn to mud in the first rain.

Where the lane widened into neat stone paving, rows of buildings rose ahead—the North Courtyard.

Near the gate, a servant hurried up. "Young Madam, the Lin family has all arrived and awaits inside."

Detesting the title "Young Madam," Qianli Xun brushed right past him into the yard, heading straight toward the reception hall where figures already sat visible inside.

Stepping across the courtyard flagstones, excitement and nerves churned in her gut—excitement at winning freedom from a meaningless marriage today, nerves over what she'd do if it fell through.

Crossing the threshold, she took in the spacious hall. A painting of clustered peonies dominated the facing wall above a narrow long table holding porcelain vases and an empty incense burner kept purely for show.

Before it stood a square table flanked by seats of honor. On the left sat an elder stroking his beard with refined poise.

Shifting her gaze to the left side-seats, she counted two rows of four—eight seats in all, with Lin Jue sitting front row closest to the head seat.

Spotting how much worse Zhu Shuzhen's forehead cut looked than yesterday, Lin Jue knew at once she had tampered with it. Willing to hurt herself just to get away from him—a bitter twist tightened his chest.

Qianli Xun looked to the right side: the Old Ancestor hadn't arrived yet. Four chairs—three empty, one occupied by a middle-aged woman. Thinking some sympathetic Zhu elder had shown up on her own, Qianli Xun hurried over with a grateful bow. "Thank you for coming to support me, Elder! How should I address you?"

The woman chuckled. "Don't recognize me, Young Madam? I'm the matchmaker who arranged your marriage to the Training Officer—Matchmaker Yan! All the Zhu seats were empty, so I borrowed one."

Speechless, Qianli Xun sank awkwardly into an empty chair as sharp stings pulsed across her forehead.

"Youqi Jushi, what happened to your forehead?"

Someone among the Lin elders called her Youqi Jushi—must be a poetry lover!

Dabbing around the cut with her silk handkerchief and putting on a fragile, pained look, she turned toward the head seat. "Elder, this wound is Lin Jue's handiwork! You don't know—he beats me all the time, and this blow wiped my memory clean. Forgive me for not knowing your name."

The elder flared up at once: "Nephew Lin Jue! Marrying Youqi Jushi took blessings built across lifetimes, and instead of cherishing her you raise your hand and beat the memory out of her? No wonder she wants a mutual divorce!"

"Second Uncle, you don't know the whole story—don't listen only to her side!" Lin Jue protested fast.

This Second Uncle was Lin Zixiao, frequent guest at Zhu Xiyan's poetry banquets and ardent admirer of Zhu Shuzhen's talent—to him, his nephew landing such a poetess was sheer fortune.

Dead set against letting the marriage crumble, he barked, "Lin Jue! Kneel right now before your clan uncles and two brothers, admit your fault to Youqi Jushi, beg her forgiveness, and swear never to lay a finger on her again!"

Neither willing to divorce nor willing to kneel in public—he felt blame ran both ways—Lin Jue rose instead.

"Calm your anger, Second Uncle—I'll admit my faults when the time is right, not now. Look around: every Lin elder turned out today, yet not a single soul from the Zhu family showed up! Uncles, eldest brother, second brother—what does that tell you?"

One man spoke up: "Does the Prefect oppose the divorce?"

"Hit the nail on the head, Eldest Brother—that's it!" Lin Jue confirmed.

A clan uncle chimed in: "Nephew's wife, since your own father disagrees and no Zhu elder came, quit making a scene and head back to the West Courtyard to live in peace."

Refusing to get steamrolled, Qianli Xun braced through the burn of Corrosive Paste. "My parents aren't coming—doesn't mean other Zhu elders aren't! It's not even the appointed hour yet—what's the rush? Can't you wait?"

"Let's grant Youqi Jushi some time and wait in patience," Lin Zixiao agreed.

The other elders nodded. Lin Jue sank back into his seat, grumbling, "If nobody shows by the Hour of the Snake, the divorce is dead—and you never bring it up again."

Ignoring him, Qianli Xun played up her wound—she hadn't paid three taels and endured this stinging for nothing.

"Elders, look what Lin Jue did to me! Stay with him and I'll lose my life! I have to divorce him—please back me up!"

Another elder spoke up: "Nephew's wife, sure Lin Jue shouldn't hit you—yet when a man strikes his wife, the wife shares blame too. Look inside yourself and ask what you did wrong."

Fury exploded in Qianli Xun; she shot to her feet ready to rip into him—when a clear voice floated in from outside.

"Nonsense! Whatever a woman's flaws, the instant a man raises a hand to hit a woman, the fault is his! Doubly so when a daughter of our Zhu house is educated and refined—what fault could she possibly have?"

Every ounce of anger melted into pure relief—nothing beat having heavy backup! Every head turned toward the doorway as the Old Ancestor strolled in, rolling prayer beads, Matron Xiuzhen walking behind without even needing to steady her—a hundred and twenty years old and striding firm as iron!

Qianli Xun rushed forward. "Thank you for coming on my behalf, Old Ancestor!"

Hearing "Old Ancestor," every Lin in the room scrambled to their feet.

Lin Zixiao led the bow: "Greetings to the Old Ancestor! Beholding your esteemed presence today honors all of us juniors!"

Matchmaker Yan jumped up too; everybody in Lin'an knew of the Zhu clan's century-old matriarch, and meeting her face to face commanded deep awe.

Stepping into the hall, the Old Ancestor swept a cool gaze over the Lin men. "Ganging up on my granddaughter? I'm here representing the Zhu family to back her—and demand answers."

Rising with the rest yet scoffing inside, Lin Jue pushed back: "Crowds of Lin elders came today, while the Zhu house sent only you—proof enough Father-in-law opposes this divorce! Since you're here, Old Ancestor, please help persuade my wife to drop this divorce talk."

The Old Ancestor fixed her eyes on him for a beat. "You're my granddaughter's husband?"

"I am."

Turning to her granddaughter—whose cut looked far nastier than when they last met—then back to Lin Jue, the Old Ancestor said cool and crisp, "You batter my granddaughter and haul brothel courtesans into the house, and you expect the Zhu family to let her stay? True, I came alone—do I not outweigh every elder in the Zhu house?"

"You do, of course you do!" Lin Zixiao hastened to smooth things over. "Pay no mind to the lad's rudeness, Old Ancestor—please take the seat of honor!"

The other Lin elders echoed their agreement.

Glowing with pride at having real backing, Qianli Xun guided the Old Ancestor to the right-hand seat of honor, then resumed her own chair ready to let the matriarch work.

Once the Old Ancestor sat right, Lin Zixiao dared not stay seated beside her on the left and signaled a servant to fetch him a side chair.

"You hold standing among the Lin elders—sit beside me," the Old Ancestor instructed.

Lin Zixiao bowed humbly. "Since the Old Ancestor commands, I obey—never dreamed you even knew of me."

"I don't, yet these eyes know how to read people. Everyone sit—let's discuss these two children's mutual divorce calmly."

At her word, the entire hall sat.

Lin Zixiao opened: "Now that the Old Ancestor is here, allow me to state the consensus of our Lin clan and Nephew Lin Jue: marrying Youqi Jushi into our home is our ancestors' greatest blessing. None of us approve a divorce—we hope the young couple stays together."

Turning to her granddaughter, the Old Ancestor asked, "Where do you stand? State it plain before the elders."

Even having made herself clear already, Qianli Xun seized the floor.

"Old Ancestor, esteemed elders—though amnesia erased how Lin Jue treated me before, the pain lingers! Waking up after he knocked me cold, the very first thing I saw was him cozying up to a brothel courtesan! How could any woman swallow that humiliation? I demanded a bill of divorce; maybe conscience pricked him and he suggested mutual divorce instead, bringing us here today. If I didn't even fear repudiation, why would I hesitate over mutual divorce?"

Eyelids lowered, rolling her beads, the Old Ancestor listened to every word before speaking: "Folks say tear down ten temples sooner than break one marriage—yet my Zhu granddaughter suffers so bitterly, how can we leave her trapped in a fiery pit? Today I intend to break this match for her. I hope the Lin clan steps aside and lets these two part in peace."

Before any elder could answer, Lin Jue jumped in: "Old Ancestor, you don't know the truth! Had she consummated our marriage on our wedding night, I never would've sought courtesans! Had she not treated me like air and carried on with young scholars outside, I never would've hit her! Tell you what—right here before you, my clan uncles, and both my brothers, I swear: if Shuzhen stays, I'll never strike her again! Let me take one concubine and I'll never touch another courtesan, and she can attend all the poetry salons she likes—long as she remembers she's married and doesn't flirt with scholars!"

Heads nodded all down the Lin row.

Lin Zixiao chimed in: "Old Ancestor, you can see how much Lin Jue hates to lose Youqi Jushi. Since he vows to mend his ways, why not grant him a chance? Besides, mutual divorce still smudges a woman's name—I would hate to see any blemish touch the Poetess of Jiangnan."

Qianli Xun had to hand it to Lin Jue—brute or not, the guy knew how to play a room! Clearly he had rehearsed this speech to kill her divorce cold. No way was she letting him win! And since Corrosive Paste wasn't moving the needle, why keep suffering?

"Kuqiao—put the healing salve on my cut."

Kuqiao pulled out the vial and dabbed cooling ointment over the wound; the burn faded at once, clearing her head.

Rising with a bow, Qianli Xun spoke loud and clear: "Old Ancestor, elders—nothing can shake my resolve to divorce! Lin Jue and I share neither mind nor taste; living together tastes like chewing wax. No matter how many vows he makes, my mind is set. Calling my poetry salons 'flirting with scholars' is pure slander—proof he neither trusts nor understands me! What point is there staying married to a man like that?"

The Old Ancestor's hand stopped on her beads. "Xiuzhen, bring it out and show Son-in-law. If he solves the riddle inside, I will talk my granddaughter out of divorcing. If he cannot, grant my granddaughter her freedom."

Matron Xiuzhen drew a folded sheet from her sleeve and opened it with care—Qianli Xun's handwritten "The Heartbreak Riddle."

Stepping before Lin Jue, she handed it over. "Examine this, Son-in-law, and announce the riddle's answer before everyone."

Lin Jue took the sheet. He knew every character and could read every line—yet as for any riddle, he hadn't the foggiest clue!
