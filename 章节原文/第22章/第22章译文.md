# Chapter 22: Elders Gather to Discuss the Divorce

All night, Qianli Xun suffered from the wound on her forehead. She hadn't expected the Fuji Ointment to be so potent. To get some sleep, she had no choice but to apply some of the other medicine. Only then did she gradually fall asleep.

Early the next morning, she looked in the mirror and saw the wound had improved considerably. Qianli Xun took out the Fuji Ointment again and applied more to the wound. After breakfast, she picked out a pretty outfit, got ready, and took Kubiao toward the north courtyard.

Halfway there, Kubiao saw her miss stop. She knew Qianli Xun had lost her direction again and had to walk ahead to lead the way.

Compared to the Zhu residence, the Lin residence's scenery was much worse. It looked like no one had bothered to design or maintain it properly. Only because servants came and went had a path been trampled into the original dirt. If it rained, it would definitely be muddy and hard to walk.

As the road became wider and easier to walk, Qianli Xun saw many buildings. This must be the north courtyard.

Just as they were about to reach the courtyard gate, a servant came up to them. "Young Madam, the Lin family members have all arrived. They're waiting inside."

Because she disliked the title "Young Madam" so much, Qianli Xun ignored the servant and walked straight in. Facing her should be the reception hall. She could already see some people sitting inside.

Qianli Xun stepped on the stone bricks in the courtyard, walking inward step by step. She was both excited and worried. Excited because after today, she would very likely escape this meaningless marriage. Worried because if it didn't work out, what would she do?

Reaching the entrance of the reception hall, she stepped inside. It felt quite spacious. On the wall facing her hung a painting of peonies, the flowers blooming and clustering together. In front was a long table, not very wide, with porcelain and an incense burner on it. The burner was empty—probably just for decoration.

In front of the long table was a square table, with chairs on both sides. On the left sat an elder, stroking his beard, looking refined and gentle.

Qianli Xun's gaze left the main seat and looked toward the left side seat. Four people sat in the front row, four in the back. Lin Jue was sitting in the front row, in the chair closest to the main seat.

Lin Jue saw that the wound on Zhu Shuzhen's forehead had become much worse than yesterday. He guessed she must have done something to it. For her to leave him, she was even willing to hurt herself. Lin Jue felt an indescribable bitterness inside.

Qianli Xun looked to the right. The Old Ancestor hadn't arrived yet. There were four chairs, three empty. Only one had a woman sitting in it. She thought it must be some elder from the Zhu family who knew about her situation and had come to help. Excitedly, she went forward to greet her. "Thank you, Elder, for speaking on my behalf. May I ask how to address you?"

The woman smiled. "Young Madam doesn't recognize me? I'm the matchmaker between you and the Military Training Officer. You can call me Matchmaker Yan. The seats for the Zhu family elders are all empty, so I'm borrowing one."

Qianli Xun was speechless. Awkwardly, she sat in an empty chair, feeling only the throbbing pain in her forehead.

"Youqi Jushi, why is there a wound on your forehead?"

Someone among the Lin family elders actually called her "Youqi Jushi." Qianli Xun thought this person must be a poetry lover.

She dabbed around the wound with a silk handkerchief, showing a pained expression. Looking toward the main seat, she said in a weak, pitiful tone, "Elder, this wound on my forehead was caused by Lin Jue. You don't know—he often beats me. Because of this injury, I can't remember anything. I don't even know how to address you."

She heard that elder say, "Lin Jue, nephew, to marry Youqi Jushi is a blessing cultivated over many lifetimes. Yet you don't cherish it. How could you lay hands on her? Beat her until she lost her memory? No wonder Youqi Jushi wants a divorce from you!"

Lin Jue said anxiously, "Second Uncle, there are many things you don't know. You can't just listen to her side of the story."

Lin Jue's second uncle was none other than Lin Zixiao. He often attended poetry gatherings organized by Zhu Xiyan and greatly admired Zhu Shuzhen's talent. For his nephew to marry such a talented woman was, in his view, a stroke of extraordinary fortune.

Now that things had come to divorce, he was completely against it. He ordered, "Lin Jue, nephew, right now in front of your clan uncles and two older brothers, kneel before Youqi Jushi. Admit your mistakes and beg her forgiveness! Then swear you'll never lay hands on Youqi Jushi again!"

Lin Jue didn't want a divorce, nor did he want to kneel before Zhu Shuzhen in public. In his view, things had come to this point not entirely because of his fault.

Lin Jue stood up. "Second Uncle, please calm down. I'll admit my mistakes when it's time. It's not the right moment yet. Today for the divorce, so many people from the Lin family have come, but not a single person from the Zhu family. Uncles, and you, Big Brother and Second Brother—what do you think this means?"

One of them said, "Could it be that Prefect Zhu fundamentally disagrees with the divorce?"

Lin Jue agreed. "Big Brother is very right. That's exactly it."

A clan uncle advised, "Niece-in-law, since your father disagrees and no elders from the Zhu family have come forward, stop making trouble. Go back to the west courtyard and live your life properly."

If Qianli Xun didn't speak now, wouldn't she be forced into submission? She endured the pain from the Fuji Ointment. "My parents aren't coming, but that doesn't mean other Zhu family elders won't come. The time hasn't arrived yet. Why are you in such a hurry? Can't you wait a little longer?"

Lin Zixiao said, "Let's give Youqi Jushi some time. Let's wait patiently."

The Lin family elders all agreed. Lin Jue could only sit back in his seat, saying resentfully, "If no one arrives by the hour of Si, the divorce fails. And you'll never bring it up again."

Qianli Xun ignored Lin Jue, thinking about how to make better use of the wound. Otherwise, she'd bought the medicine and endured the pain for nothing.

"Elders, Lin Jue has injured me like this. If I continue living with him, I'm afraid I'll lose my life. So I must have this divorce. I hope the elders will support me."

An elder said, "Niece-in-law, Lin Jue beating you is wrong. But when a man beats his own woman, the woman must have done something wrong too. Please think about where you went wrong."

Qianli Xun was furious. She jumped up, about to curse the elder who spoke. But then she heard a voice from outside.

"That's wrong. No matter what the woman does, if a man lays hands on her, the man is in the wrong. Besides, our Zhu family's daughter is educated and proper. What fault could she possibly have?"

These words instantly extinguished Qianli Xun's anger. She immediately felt the importance of having someone backing her up. She and everyone else looked outside. It was the Old Ancestor, holding prayer beads, walking slowly toward them. Xiu Zhen Nanny followed behind, not even offering to support her. She hadn't expected a 120-year-old to walk with such steady steps.

Qianli Xun went forward to greet her. "Thank you, Old Ancestor, for speaking on my behalf."

When the Lin family heard it was the Old Ancestor, they all stood up to welcome her.

Lin Zixiao led the way. "We respectfully welcome the Old Ancestor. Today we are honored to see your true appearance. It is our good fortune."

Matchmaker Yan, standing to the side, also stood up. Everyone in Lin'an knew the Zhu family had a long-lived Old Ancestor. Today she was fortunate enough to meet her. Naturally, Matchmaker Yan was extremely respectful.

The Old Ancestor walked into the reception hall and scanned the Lin family members. "You young ones have gathered to bully my granddaughter. I represent the Zhu family to back her up and demand an explanation."

Although Lin Jue stood up with the elders to welcome her, he felt disdain inside. "So many elders from the Lin family have come, but only you from the Zhu family. This clearly shows Prefect Zhu disagrees with the divorce. Since the Old Ancestor has come, why not help persuade my wife to drop this divorce nonsense?"

The Old Ancestor stared at Lin Jue for a moment. "You are my granddaughter's husband?"

Lin Jue replied, "Yes."

The Old Ancestor turned back to face her granddaughter. The wound on her forehead was much worse than last time. She looked at Lin Jue again. "You beat my granddaughter and even brought a merchant courtesan home. How can our Zhu family let her continue living with you? Yes, only I came from the Zhu family. But can't I alone represent all the Zhu family elders?"

Lin Zixiao said, "Yes, of course. Old Ancestor, please don't mind the young ones. Please, take the main seat."

The other Lin family elders all agreed.

At this moment, Qianli Xun felt even more strongly the importance of having an elder's support. She went forward to help the Old Ancestor, guiding her to the main seat on the right. Then she obediently returned to her own seat, comfortably enjoying the feeling of having the Old Ancestor backing her up.

Once the Old Ancestor sat on the right, Lin Zixiao didn't dare sit on the left. He ordered a servant nearby to arrange a chair for him in the side seat.

The Old Ancestor said, "You are someone of status among the Lin family elders. Just sit next to me."

Lin Zixiao said modestly, "Since the Old Ancestor has asked this junior to sit, I will obey. I just didn't expect the Old Ancestor to know me."

"I don't know you, but my eyes can observe. Everyone sit down. Let's discuss these two children's divorce calmly."

At the Old Ancestor's order, everyone obediently sat down.

Lin Zixiao led the way. "Since the Old Ancestor has come, let me explain the wishes of the Lin family juniors and Lin Jue, nephew. We all believe that for Lin Jue to marry Youqi Jushi is a blessing from our ancestors. Regarding the divorce, the Lin family juniors all think it inappropriate. We hope the young couple can continue living their lives together."

The Old Ancestor faced her granddaughter. "What is your attitude? You might as well explain it to the elders."

Although Qianli Xun had already made her attitude clear, the Old Ancestor was giving her another chance. She might as well take this opportunity to explain further.

"Old Ancestor, elders. Although I've lost my memory and can't remember how Lin Jue treated me before, the pain is still here. After being beaten unconscious by him and waking up, the first thing I saw was him being very intimate with a merchant courtesan. How could I endure such humiliation? I asked him to divorce me. Perhaps it was his conscience that gave him the idea of a mutual divorce, leading to today's situation. I'm not even afraid of him divorcing me—how much less a mutual divorce?"

The Old Ancestor fingered her prayer beads, her eyelids lowered, listening carefully to her granddaughter. After her granddaughter finished, she continued, "As the saying goes, better to demolish ten temples than destroy one marriage. But our Zhu family granddaughter has truly suffered. We can't just let her stay in that fire pit. Today I want to help her break this marriage. I hope the Lin family won't obstruct it. Let these two children part amicably."

Before the Lin family elders could speak, Lin Jue hurriedly said, "Old Ancestor, there's something you don't know. If she had consummated the marriage on our wedding night, I wouldn't have gone out to find a merchant courtesan. If she hadn't ignored me and gone flirting with literary talents, I wouldn't have beaten her."

He paused, then continued, "I can guarantee in front of the Old Ancestor, the Lin family elders, and my two brothers—as long as Shuzhen doesn't leave me, I'll never beat her again. As long as she agrees to let me take a concubine, I also guarantee I won't find a merchant courtesan again. I'll allow her to freely attend various poetry gatherings. All I ask is that she remembers she's married and doesn't casually flirt with literary talents."

The Lin family elders all nodded after hearing this.

Lin Zixiao said, "Old Ancestor, it's clear that Lin Jue nephew is very reluctant to let go of Youqi Jushi. Since he has a heart to reform, why not fulfill their wishes? Besides, when a woman divorces, it will more or less affect her reputation. Youqi Jushi has the reputation of the female ci poet of Jiangnan. This old man doesn't want any blemish on that reputation."

Qianli Xun began to admire Lin Jue. Although he was a martial artist, at crucial moments he could speak in a way that won people's hearts. It seemed he had made thorough preparations, wanting to extinguish her desire for divorce in front of the elders. How could she let him have his way? This marriage must be divorced! If applying the Fuji Ointment didn't work, then why continue enduring the pain?

"Kubiao, give me the medicine to treat the wound."

Kubiao took out the medicine bottle, poured some ointment, and applied it to her miss's wound. It was cool, and the burning pain quickly disappeared. Her mind immediately felt much clearer.

Qianli Xun stood up, bowed, and said, "Old Ancestor, elders. Shuzhen's determination to divorce is unshakable. Lin Jue and I are not matched in talent, not aligned in interests. Living together is like chewing wax. No matter how much Lin Jue says or how many guarantees he makes, I won't change my mind about the divorce. Lin Jue says I attend poetry gatherings and flirt with literary talents—that's pure slander! It fully shows he doesn't trust me and can't understand me! What's the point of continuing to live with such a person?"

The Old Ancestor stopped fingering her prayer beads. "Xiuzhen, take it out. Show it to the young master. If he can guess the riddle's answer, I'll persuade my granddaughter not to think about divorce anymore. If he can't guess it, please return my granddaughter's freedom."

Nanny Xiu Zhen took out folded paper from her bosom and carefully unfolded it. On it was exactly "The Heartbreak Riddle" that Qianli Xun had written from memory.

Nanny Xiu Zhen walked up to Lin Jue and handed it to him. "Please examine it, Young Master. After examining, say the answer in front of everyone."

Lin Jue took it. He recognized all the characters on it and could read every line. But to say the answer—he really couldn't figure it out.
