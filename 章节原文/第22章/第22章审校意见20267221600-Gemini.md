### 1. 致命的拼写错误（重要！）
*   **纠错**：本章中，丫鬟苦荞的名字再次被错误拼写成了 **Kubiao**（P2, P3 等多处）。我在下文的精修版中已为你全部修正回 **Kuqiao**。

### 2. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 22: Elders Gather to Discuss the Divorce*
*   **点评**：比较平铺直叙，像是在写会议通知。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 22: The Divorce Showdown**（离婚大对决）或者 **Chapter 22: The Matriarch Silences the Room**（老太君镇压全场）。

### 3. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P19 中式比喻的转换（祖上烧了高香）**：`a blessing from our ancestors`
    *   **优化**：“祖上烧了高香”意译为“祖先的庇佑”是准确的，但英文中形容这种“天大的福分/祖坟冒青烟”，可以用更强烈的表达。
    *   **修改**：`"the greatest blessing our ancestors could have bestowed upon us."` 或者 `"a stroke of luck that comes once in a lifetime."`
*   **P39 形容枯燥的比喻（如同嚼蜡）**：`Living together is like chewing wax.`
    *   **优化**：“味同嚼蜡”在中国文化里很常见，但在英语里 `chewing wax` 不太常用。英语中表达生活枯燥、毫无感情，常用 **like chewing ashes** 或 **dry as dust / utterly dead**。
    *   **修改**：`"Living with him is as suffocating as chewing on ashes."`
*   **P32 俗语的翻译（宁拆十座庙，不毁一桩婚）**：`better to demolish ten temples than destroy one marriage.`
    *   **优化**：这个直译非常棒！为了让西方读者更顺畅地理解，可以稍微润色一下节奏。
    *   **修改**：`"There is an old saying: it is a lesser sin to tear down ten temples than to destroy a single marriage."`
*   **P33 渣男的无耻发言（允许她参加诗会）**：`I'll allow her to freely attend various poetry gatherings.`
    *   **优化**：渣男是在施舍恩惠，语气应该更傲慢/大度。
    *   **修改**：`"I am even willing to let her freely attend her poetry gatherings..."`

### 4. 句子是否过长？标点与节奏是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **P33 长句的切分**：`As long as she agrees to let me take a concubine, I also guarantee I won't find a merchant courtesan again.`
    *   **优化**：渣男一边保证不嫖娼，一边要求纳妾，这是极其无耻的。可以切短句子，突出这种荒谬感。
    *   **修改**：`"If she agrees to let me take a formal concubine, I swear I will never visit a brothel again."`
*   **P20 老太君霸气护犊子**：`"You young ones have gathered to bully my granddaughter."`
    *   **优化**：老祖宗一出场就是血脉压制，语气要霸气外露。
    *   **修改**：`"You youngsters dare to gang up and bully my granddaughter?"`

The paths were merely dirt trails trampled flat by the endless coming and going of servants. If it rained, the entire courtyard would undoubtedly turn into a muddy, impassable swamp.
As the path eventually widened and became paved, a cluster of grand buildings came into view. This had to be the north courtyard.
Just as they approached the main gates, a servant hurried forward. "Young Madam, the Lin family elders have all arrived. They are waiting for you inside."
Utterly despising the title "Young Madam," Qianli Xun ignored the servant and strode straight inside. Facing her was the main reception hall, and she could already see a crowd of people seated within.

Reaching the threshold of the reception hall, she stepped inside. The space was cavernous. Hanging on the central wall facing her was a massive scroll painting of clustered, blooming peonies. Beneath it sat a long, narrow altar table displaying fine porcelain and an empty incense burner—clearly placed there purely for decoration.
In front of the long altar was a square table with chairs arranged on either side. Seated on the left side of the room was an elder gently stroking his beard, exuding an aura of refined, scholarly grace.
