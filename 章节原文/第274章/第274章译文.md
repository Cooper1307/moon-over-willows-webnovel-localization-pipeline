## Chapter 274: One Headache Settled, Another Arrives

Returning at dusk from posting guards at Lazybones Village and inspecting its soil with Magistrate Linshui, Lin Jue spotted Zhu Jingyuan's carriage waiting right outside Lin Manor.

Having landed a clerical post in the prefectural yamen through Zhu Xiyan, Zhu Jingyuan was someone Lin Jue still treated with brisk courtesy. Climbing into the carriage at his invitation, Lin Jue heard his pitch—matching six pretty maids with decent dowries to unmarried soldiers—and guessed the truth in two seconds.

"Chen Haoyu's castoffs, right? Left Shuzhen stuck wiping up his mess?"

"Sharp as ever, Commander Lin," Zhu Jingyuan admitted. "Six fresh young girls plus dowries from my sister—your lads won't lose out."

*Look at you, Zhu Shuzhen!* Lin Jue sneered silently. *Ditched me only to play mop-up crew for a playboy! Realizing your ex-husband wasn't so bad after all? Watch me knock this out cleanly!* "Tell Shuzhen to get their dowries ready."

Delighted, Zhu Jingyuan promised him a thank-you feast at Juxiang Tower whenever his schedule cleared.

Next morning, after reporting Lazybones Village's soil survey with Zhu Xiyan at the Judicial Office—Magistrate Linshui confirmed the hills only grew weeds and wild greens, leading Prince Yi to order the villagers to farm wild greens commercially while Lin Jue oversaw six months of building earthen cottages and cave dwellings—Lin Jue saw Prefect Zhu off, then doubled back alone to Prince Yi's courtroom.

"One private matter concerning Zhu Shuzhen, Your Highness." Knowing the prince still cared deeply for her, Lin Jue recounted Chen Haoyu's six ruined maids and Shuzhen's request for soldier husbands, pretending to seek the prince's approval.

*She's already cleaning up his messes,* Shen Jianchen sighed inwardly. All he could do now was help her quietly from the shadows. "Easy favor for you—grant it. And pour your heart into Lazybones Village."

Pleased at scoring points with both powerful patrons, Lin Jue headed straight to the barracks to handpick six sturdy bachelors.

Right after lunch that afternoon, a drill officer arrived at Chen Manor's south gate with six strapping, honest-looking soldiers—none of whom minded the girls' pasts once dowries and pretty faces were on the table. Qianli Xun summoned the six maids by carriage; within ten minutes every soldier eagerly claimed a blushing bride, bowing deep in thanks as Qianli Xun promised to send their dowries to the barracks tomorrow.

"Finally—peace and quiet!" Qianli Xun exhaled, relieved when Kuqiao confirmed Butler Wang had sworn no more compromised maids lingered in any other Chen estate.

Yawning toward an afternoon nap, Qianli Xun froze as a gaudy carriage rolled up—one Kuqiao recognized immediately from Chun Jinqiao's old visits to Lin Manor: Chunxiang Brothel!

Out stepped two flashily dressed courtesans—one of them Chun Jinmo!

*Chen Haoyu, you utter bastard!* Qianli Xun raged inside. *Leave all your ticking landmines at home while you hide out at the silk emporium!* Pivoting to ignore them and walk inside, she froze as Chun Jinmo called mockingly after her:

"Why run away, Second Young Madam? Where's all that swagger from when you raided Chunxiang Brothel to haul Young Master Chen out? Did he finally tame you?"

Spinning back, Qianli Xun snapped, "What do you want? You aren't welcome here—clear out."

"I came to talk business," Chun Jinmo purred. "Not for myself—I'm over being some lowly Chen concubine. Master Jiang redeemed me and made me his *primary wife*! My birth surname is Jiang—call me Madam Jiang."

"Smart move, Madam Jiang. Then why roll up in a brothel carriage?"

"To ask Second Young Madam to be generous like Commander Lin's wife and redeem my little sister here as Young Master Chen's concubine."

Eyeing the striking, venom-glaring courtesan beside Chun Jinmo, Qianli Xun cursed Haoyu ten more ways inside before snapping, "Dragging some stranger up to tell me to copy Bai Meiruo? Hilarious. No time for clowns—bye!"

The second Qianli Xun turned her back, steel flashed—the strange woman lunged straight at her with a dagger!
