第274章 解决一个麻烦事
## Chapter 274: One Headache Settled

林觉派人守好懒人村，又带领临水县县令勘察完周围的土质后，从山里回到临安城，天色已晚，他打算等明日再向知府大人和仪王汇报懒人村的情况。
骑着马刚到林府外，听到有人喊他：“林统制可回来！让我久等！”从声音判断出是朱景元。
林觉跳下马，过来一名下人将马牵了。他才循声看去，朱家的马车停在那里，朱景元从马车里探出个脑袋。
朱景元靠着知府大人的关系在府衙谋了个文职，没有官阶，说白了就是打杂的，大家都是看在知府大人的面子上才会把他放在眼里，也因这层关系林觉不敢怠慢。
他走上前：“难道朱兄一直在外面候着？”
对方没有官职不能称他为大人，还好以前是他的妹夫，“朱兄”倒称呼得顺口。
“不算太久，大概等了两刻钟。你现在是大忙人，替仪王和知府大人忙些大事，人实在难找，我便想了到林府门口堵你的法子，还真管用。”
“朱兄既然来，去府里坐坐。”
“我就不进府打扰，烦请林统制上马车来与我一叙。”
林觉想不通朱景元找他何事？能耐心堵他，应该不是小事。带着好奇的心情登上马车，在朱景元对面坐好。
“我猜朱兄找我定有要事，不妨直说。”
“不是府衙的事，是私事，此事非林统制莫属。”
林觉更加好奇：“朱兄请讲。”
“你是统制，管着临安的士兵，说句话肯定比以前当练兵官时更管用。再说是好事，士兵们不会不同意。”
林觉疑惑道：“请朱兄明说。”
“不是什么大事，我妹妹身边有六名丫鬟担心自己嫁不出去，托她找个好人家。她想来想去还是林统制手下的士兵最适合。她一个妇道人家不好主动前来拜访，便让她嫂嫂向我转达。你也知道我疼妹妹，因此替她专程来找林统制。”
林觉算是听明白，朱淑真想给丫鬟找夫婿。
什么样的丫鬟她着急出手？林觉稍加思忖，便了然于胸。
“恐怕是陈浩宇的丫鬟吧？该不会被陈浩宇糟蹋后一脚踢开，朱淑真不得不为他擦屁股，才想到我？”
被对方猜中，朱景元也没必要再隐晦表达。
After posting guards at Lazybones Village and guiding the County Magistrate of Linshui to survey the surrounding soil, Lin Jue rode back into Lin’an after dusk, planning to report to the Prefect and Prince Yi the next day.

“林统制厉害，一猜就中。浩宇以前的确做过许多荒唐事，搞得淑真还得替他出面解决。我听文心说，六个丫鬟长得挺水灵，士兵们肯定满意，再说我妹妹多少也会给她们准备些嫁妆，你手下的士兵肯定不会吃亏。”
As he reined in outside Lin Manor, a familiar voice hailed him. “Commander Lin, back at last! You kept me waiting!”

林觉心中不免冷笑：“朱淑真啊朱淑真，你瞧不上我，费尽心思跟我和离，又能如何？嫁给陈浩宇还得替人家擦屁股，日子不好过啊！该不会现在觉出前夫的好来？才派你的哥哥来找我？既如此，我就帮你，让你知道我比陈浩宇强多了！”
It was Zhu Jingyuan. Lin Jue dismounted, handed his reins to a servant, and looked toward a parked Zhu family carriage where Zhu Jingyuan was poking his head out.

“请朱兄转告淑真，让她把嫁妆准备好。”
Through the Prefect’s pull, Zhu Jingyuan held a low-ranking clerical post at the prefectural yamen—no official grade, just an errand-runner—yet everyone showed him face out of respect for his father, and Lin Jue was no exception.

林觉爽快答应，朱景元开心不已：“我替妹妹谢过林统制，明日在聚香楼设宴，以表谢意。”
He stepped over. “Has Brother Zhu been waiting out here long?” Having no official title, Jingyuan could not be called “Lord,” and since he had once been his brother-in-law, “Brother Zhu” came out easy enough.

“朱兄跟我不必客气，再说明日一大堆事，根本没有心情去聚香楼，先记在账上，等闲了我们再一起畅饮。”
“Not too long—about half an hour. You’re a busy man these days handling big projects for Prince Yi and the Prefect, impossible to track down, so I figured I’d stake out your front gate. Worked like a charm!”

“好！”
“Since you’re here, Brother Zhu, come inside for a cup of tea.”

第二日，林觉在府衙向朱晞颜汇报完懒人村的情况后，二人一起来到司理院。
“I won’t impose. Why not hop into the carriage and chat a minute?”

仪王在司理院的堂内接见他们。
Wondering what warranted ambushing him at his gate, Lin Jue climbed aboard and sat across from him. “Must be something urgent, Brother Zhu. Speak straight.”

有朱晞颜在，林觉不能主动说话，只需跟在身后行礼。
“Nothing to do with the yamen—a personal favor, and only Commander Lin can pull it off.”

朱晞颜开口说：“殿下，昨日林统制带人已将懒人村的村口把守，无人再敢往出跑。临水县县令也详细勘察过村中的土质，发现那里的土质只适合长杂草和野菜，确实不适合种植粮食。”
His curiosity piqued, Lin Jue said, “I’m listening.”

申简辰想想说：“既如此，不如顺其自然，发动懒人村的人清除杂草，开垦土地，大量种植野菜，还请知府大人帮着寻找销路。”
“As Commander, you oversee Lin’an’s garrison. Your word carries far more weight now than back when you were a drill officer. Besides, it’s a good thing—your soldiers won’t say no.”

朱晞颜再不乐意，也得答应：“下官记下。关于在懒人村建造土屋和窑洞一事，下官也已安排下去，交由林统制负责，大概需要半年的时间方可竣工。”
“Come out with it, Brother Zhu.”

“有劳知府大人与林统制，此事办好利国利民，也不枉二位为官一场。说不定此事之后，临安官府会得到百姓夸赞。”
“Nothing huge. Six maids around my sister are worried they’ll never get married and begged her to find them good matches. After thinking it over, she figured the soldiers under your command are the best fit. As a married woman, she couldn’t come calling in person, so she had her sister-in-law pass the request to me. You know how I dote on my sister, so I came straight to you.”

朱晞颜对百姓夸赞并不奢求，只求多一事不如少一事。
Lin Jue caught on in a heartbeat. Zhu Shuzhen wanted husbands for maids—and which maids would she be in such a hurry to marry off? A second’s thought gave him the answer.

仪王刚来时什么都不理会，令他舒心不已；成完婚再次回到临安，却要做事。
“Chen Haoyu’s maids, I bet? He had his way with them and tossed them aside, leaving Zhu Shuzhen to clean up his mess—and she thought of me?”

本以为他担心没事做会憋出病来，选择司理参军一职不过是为怡情悦性，没想到竟认真起来，给府衙添了这么大的麻烦，不仅要动用人力还要花银子，以后还不知会搞出多少名堂来。
Guessed dead-on, Zhu Jingyuan dropped the tact. “Sharp as ever, Commander Lin. Haoyu did plenty of foolish things in the past, and now Shuzhen has to sort them out. Wenxin tells me all six girls are fresh and pretty—your men won’t be disappointed. Plus my sister will put together dowries for them, so your soldiers come out ahead.”

“下官不敢居功，都是仪王的功劳。”
Lin Jue sneered inside. *Zhu Shuzhen, oh Zhu Shuzhen! You looked down on me and bent over backward for a mutual divorce, and for what? Marrying Chen Haoyu only to wipe up his messes—life isn’t so sweet now, is it? Starting to see how much better your ex-husband was? Fine, I’ll help you—just to show you I’m ten times the man Chen Haoyu is!*

“辛苦知府大人和林统制，此事若成，我定向官家替二位请功。”
“Tell Shuzhen to get their dowries ready,” he said.

“为朝廷做事，实乃下官的本分。”
Delighted by his prompt agreement, Zhu Jingyuan beamed. “Thank you on my sister’s behalf, Commander Lin! Tomorrow I’ll host a banquet at Juxiang Tower to repay you.”

“好了，去忙吧。”
“No need for ceremony, Brother Zhu—tomorrow I’m swamped and in no mood for banquets. Put it on a tab, and we’ll drink our fill once things quiet down.”

二人离开后，在司理院门口分别。
“Deal!”

林觉装作去忙懒人村的事，估摸着知府大人走远，扭转马头又来到司理院。
The next morning, after briefing Zhu Xiyan at the prefectural office on Lazybones Village, Lin Jue accompanied him to the Judicial Office.

他将马拴好，又径直走入堂内。
Prince Yi received them in the main hall. With Zhu Xiyan present, Lin Jue stayed silent behind him after bowing.

“仪王殿下，下官还有一事禀明。”
Zhu Xiyan spoke first. “Your Highness, yesterday Commander Lin posted guards at the entrance to Lazybones Village so no one slips out. The County Magistrate of Linshui also inspected the soil in detail and confirmed it can only grow weeds and wild vegetables, not grain.”

申简辰放下手中的卷宗，露出疑惑的表情，林觉要说的事八成不能当着朱晞颜的面讲，要不怎么去而又返？
Shen Jianchen thought it over. “Then work with nature instead of against it. Have the villagers clear the weeds, till the plots, and cultivate wild vegetables on a large scale—and I’ll trouble the Prefect to help secure buyers.”

“林统制有什么话尽管说。”
Reluctant as he was, Zhu Xiyan had to agree. “Understood. As for building earthen cottages and cave dwellings in the village, I’ve already put Commander Lin in charge. Construction should take about half a year.”

“事关朱淑真。”
“Thank you both for your trouble,” Shen Jianchen said. “Pull this off and it will benefit both court and commoner—worthy of your offices. The people of Lin’an might even sing the yamen’s praises.”

林觉本想自己将昨日朱景元说的事解决即可，今日见到仪王，又想告诉他。
Zhu Xiyan cared nothing for popular praise; he just wanted a quiet life. When Prince Yi first arrived and ignored everything, Zhu Xiyan had been ecstatic. Yet since returning from his wedding, the prince had turned dead serious—piling work on the yamen, burning manpower and silver, with no telling what else he would cook up next.

仪王心中放不下朱淑真，林觉最清楚不过。将朱淑真的事告诉仪王，方便他通过此事了解到对方的境况，才会认为他林觉是为仪王心中之人办事，自然对他的印象会更好，也才会从心底把他当成自己人。
“I dare not claim credit,” Zhu Xiyan said. “All credit belongs to Your Highness.”

仪王果然急切地问：“什么事？”
“Both of you have worked hard. Once this succeeds, I will petition His Majesty to commend you.”

“也不是什么大事，陈浩宇以前喜欢抢些黄花大姑娘放在陈家其他宅子里当丫鬟，有些已失身。现在朱淑真想给失了身的丫鬟找婆家，偏偏想到临安士兵，求到下官名下，下官正犹豫要不要答应，想听听殿下的意思。”
“Serving the court is merely our duty.”

千里寻开始为陈浩宇解决麻烦事，看来她已接纳他。申简辰在心中为自己和千里寻叹口气，他唯一能做的便是在背后默默帮她。
“Good. Go see to your duties.”

“此事对林统制来讲十分容易，答应便是。”
Outside the Judicial Office, the two parted ways. Pretending to ride off to Lazybones Village, Lin Jue waited until the Prefect was well down the road, then wheeled his horse back to the Judicial Office, tied it up, and walked straight back into the hall.

“是。”
“Your Highness, I have one more matter to report.”

“辛苦林统制，懒人村的事一定要尽心竭力办好。”
Shen Jianchen set down his dossier, intrigued. Whatever Lin Jue had to say couldn’t be spoken in front of Zhu Xiyan. “Speak freely, Commander Lin.”

“仪王殿下安排的事，下官自当尽心竭力。”
“It concerns Zhu Shuzhen.”

林觉拜别仪王放心离开。他需先去趟练兵署，挑选几名士兵去陈府，然后再去懒人村忙活。
Lin Jue could have handled Zhu Jingyuan’s request on his own, yet seeing Prince Yi today had given him an idea. Nobody knew better than Lin Jue that Prince Yi still could not let go of Zhu Shuzhen. Bringing him word of her let the prince keep tabs on her life—and showed that Lin Jue looked out for the woman in his heart, earning his trust as an insider.

平白无故添了懒人村的烦心事，林觉只能认栽，谁让知府大人用他用的顺手。他感到麻烦的同时又为自己将两个大人物搞定而高兴。
Sure enough, Prince Yi leaned forward. “What happened?”

今日千里寻没有去施粥棚，陪着长辈聊了一会儿，又开始写话本。一直写到午时，苦荞将饭摆上才停下笔。
“Nothing major. Chen Haoyu used to abduct unwed girls and stash them as maids in outside Chen residences, and some lost their virtue to him. Now Zhu Shuzhen wants to marry those girls off, settled on Lin’an’s garrison soldiers, and came to me for help. I wasn’t sure whether to agree and wanted to hear Your Highness’s thoughts.”

用过午饭后，本打算睡一觉，下人前来报告：“一位练兵官带着六名士兵在陈府南门外候着，口口声声说是林统制派他们来见陈家二少夫人。”
Qianli Xun was cleaning up Chen Haoyu’s messes—which meant she had accepted him. Shen Jianchen sighed inside for both of them. All he could do now was help her from the shadows.

一早哥哥差人送来消息，说林觉满口应下，没想到这么快人就送上门来。
“That’s easy enough for you, Commander Lin. Agree to it.”

午饭后的困意全无，千里寻简单整理衣装，在苦荞的陪同下来到大门外。
“Yes, Your Highness.”

练兵官见状上前行礼：“您可是二少夫人？”
“And put your full effort into Lazybones Village.”

千里寻点点头：“正是。”
“I will spare no effort on any task Your Highness assigns.”

练兵官说：“按照林统制的吩咐，人我已带来，二少夫人瞧瞧，可满意？”
Lin Jue bowed out with an easy mind, heading first to the Military Training Office to pick six soldiers to send to Chen Manor before riding on to Lazybones Village. Getting saddled with the village project was a nuisance, sure, yet locking in favor with two powerful men more than made up for it.

千里寻瞧向六人，长相十分普通，身体够强壮，配那六个丫鬟倒也合适。就是不知道他们可忌讳六个丫鬟已不是处子之身。
Qianli Xun skipped the congee shed that morning, chatted with the elders a while, and worked on her huaben tale until noon when Kuqiao set out lunch.

“练兵官，请那边说话。”
Right after lunch, just as she planned to nap, a servant came to report. “A drill officer is waiting outside the south gate with six soldiers, saying Commander Lin sent them to see Second Young Madam.”

练兵官跟着来到一边，问道：“二少夫人可满意？”
Her brother had sent word that morning that Lin Jue had agreed on the spot, yet she hadn’t expected them this fast. Sleepiness vanished. Smoothing her clothes, she walked out to the front gate with Kuqiao.

“满意，不知他们可嫌弃六名丫鬟……？”
The drill officer saluted. “Are you Second Young Madam?”

练兵官显然明白她的意思：“二少夫人放心，林统制已将丫鬟的情况说明，他们都不嫌弃，只想娶个好看的，再说二少夫人您还给嫁妆，何乐而不为？”
“I am.”

“既如此，我现在差人把丫鬟们带来，若看对眼，当场牵走。”
“Per Commander Lin’s orders, I’ve brought the men. Take a look—do they meet your approval?”

“林统制让我把他们带过来，也是这意思。”
Qianli Xun sized up the six men: plain-featured, sturdy builds—well matched for the six maids. The only question was whether they minded that the girls weren’t virgins. “Step aside with me for a word, officer.”

千里寻向苦荞点点头，苦荞会意，走到门口对下人说了几句话，那下人套好马车离开。
Once out of earshot, the officer asked, “Are you satisfied, Second Young Madam?”

苦荞为小姐端把椅子，让她坐下耐心等待。
“Very. Do they mind that the six maids...?”

大约不到三刻钟，马车终于回来。
The officer caught her meaning at once. “Rest easy, Second Young Madam. Commander Lin laid out the maids’ circumstances upfront. None of them mind—they just want pretty wives. And with you providing dowries too, why wouldn’t they jump at the chance?”

六名丫鬟从马车里走下的那一刻，士兵们一个个直直地盯着她们瞧，像没见过漂亮姑娘似的。
“Good. I’ll send for the maids right now. If they hit it off, they can leave together on the spot.”

见此情形，千里寻总算放心。她站起来，走到六位丫鬟面前：“瞧见没，他们六个，你们可满意？”
“That’s what Commander Lin had in mind.”

六个丫鬟害羞地点点头。
Qianli Xun nodded to Kuqiao, who spoke to a gatekeeper; the servant hitched a carriage and drove off. Kuqiao brought out a chair so her miss could wait in comfort.

千里寻说：“既然满意，自己选，选好了可要跟着人家走。”
Less than three quarters of an hour later, the carriage rolled back up. The moment the six maids stepped down, the soldiers’ eyes glued onto them as though they had never seen pretty women before.

丫鬟们都点点头，表示同意。
Relieved, Qianli Xun rose and walked over to the maids. “See those six? Happy with them?”

“开始选吧。”
All six nodded in shy approval.

丫鬟们还未出手，士兵们已经过来，一人拉上一个，都说着：“我看上你了。”
“Then take your pick. Once you choose, you’re going home with him.”

丫鬟们一个个害羞地低下头。
The maids nodded again. “Go ahead, choose.”

如此顺利，千里寻也替丫鬟们感到高兴：“既然已选好，你们先回去与家人商议成亲事宜，明日我会差人将嫁妆送到练兵署。”
Before the maids could move, the soldiers surged forward, each taking a girl by the hand. “I pick you!”

练兵官大声说：“还不赶快谢过二少夫人！”
The maids hung their heads, blushing crimson.

众人齐声说道：“感谢二少夫人！”
Thrilled it went off without a hitch, Qianli Xun said, “Since you’re all paired up, head home and arrange the weddings with your families. Tomorrow I’ll have the dowries delivered to the Military Training Office.”

众人离开后，千里寻深深吐口气：“总算清静！”
“Well? Thank Second Young Madam!” the drill officer barked.

接着又担忧起来：“苦荞，你说陈府其他宅子里还有没有这样的丫鬟？”
“Thank you, Second Young Madam!” they all chorused.

苦荞笑道：“小姐放心，没有了。”
Once they left, Qianli Xun blew out a long breath. “Peace and quiet at last!” Then a fresh worry hit her. “Kuqiao, are there any more maids like that stashed in other Chen residences?”

“你怎么知道？”
Kuqiao smiled. “None at all, Miss.”

“昨晚我早已替小姐问过王管家，他在陈府当管家，自然对其他宅子最清楚不过。”
“How do you know?”

“还是你有心。”
“I already asked Butler Wang last night. He runs the manor—nobody knows the outside properties better than him.”

一轻松，午饭后的困意又袭来，千里寻打了一个哈欠：“我回屋躺会儿。”
“Good thinking.”

有马车的声音传来，千里寻下意识地循声看去，马车的外形装扮较艳丽，不像来自官宦人家。
With the weight off her shoulders, post-lunch drowsiness crept back in. Yawning, Qianli Xun turned to go rest when the rattle of carriage wheels caught her ear. Looking back on reflex, she saw a flashy carriage decked out far too garishly for any official family.

苦荞议论道：“春香楼的马车怎么会来陈府？”
“What’s a carriage from Chunxiang Brothel doing at Chen Manor?” Kuqiao wondered aloud.

“春香楼？你怎么知道？”
“Chunxiang Brothel? How can you tell?”

“以前在林府时我见春金巧乘坐过。”
“I saw Chun Jinqiao ride in one back at Lin Manor.”

千里寻有种不祥的预感，一事解决，难道又来一事？
A bad feeling prickled Qianli Xun’s spine. One mess cleared, and another rolling up to the door?

当两名衣着艳丽的女子从车上走下时，千里寻看得清楚，其中一名正是春金沫。
Two gaudily dressed women stepped down—one of them unmistakable: Chun Jinmo.

她不由心中骂道：“陈浩宇！你个混蛋！老娘嫁给你是想跟你过安稳日子，却给老娘留下这么多隐患！你倒好，一大早跑到绸缎庄，躲得远远的，让老娘在家里替你忙这些个破事！”
Qianli Xun cursed in her head. *Chen Haoyu, you bastard! I married you for a quiet life, and look at the land mines you left behind! And you? Sneaking off to the silk shop first thing in the morning to dodge it all while I stay home mopping up your garbage!*

千里寻在心中骂完，不想理会走来的二位，准备转身继续回陈府。
Ignoring the pair approaching her, she turned back toward the manor gate.

身后却传来春金沫的声音：“少夫人为何见我姐妹二人前来不予理会？”
Chun Jinmo’s voice rang out behind her. “Why walk away without a word when my sister and I came all this way, Young Madam?”

千里寻根本不想搭理，继续朝府中走。
Qianli Xun kept walking.

“哼！没想到少夫人竟然怕我们？跑到春香楼带走陈公子的气焰怎么没了？难道是当了几日陈公子的夫人，被他制服不成？”
“Hmph! Don’t tell me Young Madam is scared of us? Where did all that swagger go from when you stormed into Chunxiang Brothel to haul Young Master Chen off? A few days as his wife, and has he already tamed you?”

对方一再挑衅，千里寻不能再逃避，她转过身：“你来做什么？陈府不欢迎你！知趣的尽快离开。”
With her taunting like that, walking away wasn’t an option. Qianli Xun spun around. “What do you want? You’re not welcome at Chen Manor. Be smart and get lost.”

春金沫说道：“陈府欢不欢迎我并不重要，我来是找二少夫人有事商议。”
“Whether Chen Manor welcomes me or not doesn’t matter,” Chun Jinmo said. “I came to discuss business with Second Young Madam.”

千里寻没好气地说：“如果是商议给陈浩宇当妾室的事儿，恐怕你要失望，不是我不乐意，是陈浩宇根本不愿纳妾。”
“If it’s about becoming Chen Haoyu’s concubine,” Qianli Xun snapped, “save your breath. Not that I’d stop him—he’s the one who refuses to take concubines.”

“少夫人果然聪明，一下子看透我来此的目的。不过，不是我想给陈公子当妾室。自从你把陈公子从春香楼带走，我也想通，即使陈公子为我赎身，到陈家也只是个贱妾的身份。而跟着蒋爷就不一样，他已为我赎身，我现在可是他的正室。”
“Sharp as ever, seeing straight through my visit. Only it’s not me who wants to be his concubine. After you hauled Young Master Chen out of Chunxiang Brothel, I came to my senses. Even if he bought my freedom, I’d only ever be a lowly concubine in Chen Manor. With Master Jiang it’s different—he redeemed my contract and made me his primary wife.”

“算你明智，做出正确的选择，我现在是否该称呼你一声春夫人？”
“Smart choice. Should I call you Madam Chun now, then?”

“我不姓春，只是到春香楼被冠了此姓，我本姓江，请称呼我江夫人。”
“My surname isn’t Chun—the brothel pinned that on me. My family name is Jiang. Call me Madam Jiang.”

“哼！江夫人不好好在钱塘帮待着，却乘坐春香楼的马车？不知来此何事？”
“Hmph! Why isn’t Madam Jiang staying put at the Qiantang Gang instead of rolling up in a Chunxiang Brothel carriage? What are you after?”

“我身边这位妹妹少夫人应该没见过，今日带她来，是想请少夫人学学林统制的夫人，大度些，替她赎身，放在陈公子身边当个妾室。”
“You haven’t met this sister beside me, Young Madam. I brought her today hoping you’d take a page from Commander Lin’s wife, show some generosity, buy out her contract, and install her as Young Master Chen’s concubine.”

千里寻朝春金沫身边的女子瞧去，长相与春金沫不相上下，一身装扮，怎么看都是青楼女子，她没有见过此人，可从她的眼神中能读出憎恨的情绪。
Qianli Xun looked at the woman beside Chun Jinmo—just as striking, dressed head to toe like a courtesan, a complete stranger, yet raw hatred burned in her eyes.

心中再次骂道：“陈浩宇！你个混蛋！在青楼里到底招惹了多少女子？等你回来老娘非跟你算账不可！”
*Chen Haoyu, you absolute scoundrel! How many women did you string along in the brothels? Wait till you get home—I’m settling every last score!*

“你随随便便找个人来，就想让老娘学白梅若，实在可笑，老娘没空跟你浪费时间，失陪！”
“Dragging some random woman up to my door and expecting me to imitate Bai Meiruo? Laughable! I haven’t got time to waste on you. Excuse me.”

千里寻正要走，那女子手中立刻出现一把匕首，向她刺来……
She turned to leave—and a gleaming dagger flashed in the stranger’s hand, lunging straight at her.
