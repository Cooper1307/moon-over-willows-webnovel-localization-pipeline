第157章 当众表演亦失败
## Chapter 157: Chapter 157: Public Performance Flops Too

每年春节前府衙官员都要找地方聚聚，在饭桌上总结总结一年的功绩，重要的是饭桌上大家的关系会更近一层，便于以后更好地开展工作。
申简辰自然被邀请，接到请帖时，满心不愿到吃吃喝喝的环境中应承，怎奈人在江湖，身不由己。
与千里寻在神柳下相认后，对方还是拒绝，搞得他一不高兴转身离开，离开又十分后悔，为何不与她多聊会儿？
本来盼着她按时来陪梦容，趁机再与她多多相处，可苦荞前来，说自家小姐临时有事，明日才来。
“明日”这两个字说起来多容易，可对于期待见到想见之人的申简辰来讲，却十分有力度。
别说他，就连梦容也甚觉失落：“回去告诉淑真，她要是明日再不来，我就去望月轩找她。”
苦荞回道：“我定将郡主的话一字不落说与我家小姐。”
申简辰决定还是去参加聚会，不为别的，只为能从朱晞颜或者他儿子口中听到关于千里寻的信息，哪怕他们只会提到“淑真”这个名讳，他也会觉得心满意足。
苦荞从幽林别居回来，将郡主的话回了。
千里寻笑道：“看把梦容急的，明日我办完事，肯定去幽林别居陪她，大不了陪她住三五日。”
“小姐只能住两日。”
“为何？”
“两日后就是春节。”
“看我这记性，好吧，两日就两日。”
官员们聚会放在午时，申简辰踩着点儿赶来，既不显得为一顿饭过于积极，也不会让人觉得他身为王爷竟然不守时。
宴席安排在聚香楼一层，只因一层够开放，方便众人交流。里面摆着十几张桌子，每张桌子都坐得满满当当。
当申简辰带白风走进聚香楼时，众位大人迎上来，行礼高呼：“仪王殿下！”
王爷被这么多老家伙拜，白风感到十分荣耀。
“诸位在知府大人的带领下，为临安政事操持，十分辛苦，我替官家感谢诸位，大家尽快落座，吃好喝好。”申简辰说着他不喜欢的客套话。
众人不再多礼，各自归座，朱晞颜则将仪王请到最尊贵的位置。
申简辰大略瞧了一下，这一桌坐的全是从五品以上。
接着朱晞颜代大家请仪王讲两句，申简辰拒绝，他不想再说些没有营养的话。
朱晞颜通过仪王的表情和语气判断他并非客气，便不再强求，也更放心大胆当着仪王的面向诸位官员总结府衙一年以来的卓越贡献。
申简辰并未仔细听，但也听到一些内容，无非是夸赞炫耀之辞，即使提到问题，也都无伤大雅，有些问题仔细思索，竟然也可成为功绩之一。
终于讲完，大家开吃，然后有舞伎前来献舞。舞乐声响，大家觥筹交错，个个脸上挂满笑容。
互相敬酒是少不了的，官员们三三两两一起前来敬酒，申简辰应接不暇，还好他酒量够大，否则会被众人喝趴下。
白风一直站在王爷身边，现在的他除拳脚功夫长进以外，最大的变化就是见到美食不再像以前一样流哈喇子，因而无需心猿意马，全部注意力都能放在王爷身上。
大家络绎不绝向王爷敬酒，白风很担心，本想替王爷挡酒，见王爷面不改色，应付自如，便知王爷酒量比他想象的要大得多。
大家敬得差不多时，林觉端着酒盅前来：“仪王，下官敬您，您随意，下官干了。”
申简辰见林觉换上新官服，想起他已被升为统制。之前的统制被朱晞颜随便找个理由同级调走，向申简辰汇报时，他猜到是因林觉查找散播流言之人有功，便点头默认。
“恭喜林统制官升一级。”申简辰举起酒杯。
“下官定不辜负仪王、知府大人和诸位大人信任，以后会加倍用心做好每件事。”林觉说完一饮而尽。
申简辰也一饮而尽。
正当二人饮完，林觉打算向知府大人敬酒时，陈浩宇闯进来：“救命啊！救命啊！要杀人啦！”
舞乐立刻停下，陈浩宇躲在舞伎身后。
众位官员瞧去，只见朱家千金冲进来：“陈浩宇！你躲得了初一，躲不了十五，陈家若不退婚！我见你一次打一次！”
二人绕着舞伎追赶起来……
见到喜爱之人，申简辰自然欢喜，心里乐道：“没想到你竟想出这种办法，只要是为退婚，我自然乐意帮你一把。”
林觉心里冷笑道：“哼！离开我又能如何？陈家虽比我林家有钱，可陈浩宇哪一点能比得上我？估计你的肠子都该悔青了。”
其他官员面面相觑，表示不理解，然后都看起热闹来。
朱晞颜心里清楚，他们昨日未成功，今日又闹到此处，不过是想逼迫他，他绝不能让两个年轻人得逞。
“胡闹！你们两个还不赶紧退下！”
知府大人的声音还是将他们镇住，两人都站住，不再追逐。
陈浩宇哪能随意放过这么好的机会：“朱伯伯，您也看到，朱淑真还没过门儿就这么对待我，要是嫁到陈家，我肯定没有好日子过！”
Every year before New Year, prefecture officials gathered over a banquet to review the year's achievements and—just as vital—loosen up over wine to grease the wheels for the year ahead. Of course Shen Jianchen received an invitation. Though loath to sit through hollow banquet chatter, holding office meant playing the part.

陈浩宇指着昨日被打得乌青的脸：“诸位大人都瞧瞧，是朱淑真打的，再这样下去，我小命不保……”
After revealing himself beneath the Divine Willow only to get turned down again, he had stormed off in hurt, then kicked himself all the way back for not lingering to talk longer. Hoping she would show up on schedule to stay with Mengrong so he could spend more time beside her, instead Kuqiao arrived saying Miss had urgent business and would come tomorrow. "Tomorrow" rolled easy off the tongue, yet felt endless to a man aching to see her, leaving Mengrong pouting too. "Tell Shuzhen if she skips tomorrow, I'm marching straight to Wangyue Pavilion!"

令所有人没想到的是，陈浩宇竟然跪下：“朱伯伯，我当着众人的面给您跪下，求您答应我取消朱陈两家婚约，晚辈将感激不尽！”
"I'll repeat every word to Miss, Princess," Kuqiao promised.

千里寻心里不由赞叹：“陈浩宇干起这种事情来倒十分机灵。”
So Shen Jianchen decided to attend the officials' banquet after all, if only to catch stray mentions of Qianli Xun from Zhu Xiyan or his son; even hearing the name "Shuzhen" would warm his day.

她也跟着跪下：“爹，陈浩宇不学无术，是青楼和赌坊的常客，还欺负良家妇女，这样的人女儿就是当一辈子老姑娘也不愿嫁！”
Back at Wangyue Pavilion, Kuqiao relayed Princess Jiahe's warning. Qianli Xun laughed. "Look how impatient Mengrong gets! Soon as tomorrow's stunt wraps, I'm heading straight to Youlin Retreat—stay three or five days if she wants!"

瞅到周围官员看热闹的样子，朱晞颜气不打一处来，还是努力忍住：“此乃家事，你们两个先回去，等宴饮结束再说。景元！”
"Only two days, Miss."

朱景元知道父亲的意思，从人群中走出，来到二人跟前：“你们两个快起来，跟我回去。”
"Why?"

两人不情愿，朱景元先去扶自家妹妹：“淑真，你是姑娘家，更要注意形象，赶紧起来。”
"New Year's Day is in two days!"

千里寻还是不愿意，向申简辰投去求助的目光。
"What a memory I've got! Fine, two days it is!"

申简辰接收到目光，见到心中之人本就开心，帮她义不容辞。
With the banquet set for noon, Shen Jianchen arrived right on the dot, neither looking too eager for free food nor showing up rude and late. Hosted across Juxiang Tower's open ground floor to let guests mingle easy, over a dozen round tables sat packed brim to brim. The instant Shen Jianchen stepped inside with Bai Feng, every official rose bowing in unison: "Greetings, Prince Yi!" Watching gray-bearded bigwigs bow low to his master swelled Bai Feng's chest with pride.

“知府大人，不如将二位带到楼上的雅间，问清楚缘由，再决定如何处理。”
"Under Prefect Zhu's leadership, you gentlemen have toiled all year for Lin'an's welfare," Shen Jianchen delivered the polite lines he hated. "On His Majesty's behalf, my thanks. Take your seats and feast well!"

仪王主动发话，朱晞颜当然得听：“下官遵命。”
Returning to their tables, everyone watched Zhu Xiyan escort Prince Yi to the head table, reserved for senior officials of vice-fifth rank and above. Invited by Zhu Xiyan to offer opening remarks, Shen Jianchen declined flat, refusing to spout hot air. Reading genuine reluctance in his tone, Zhu Xiyan did not push, stepping forward himself to summarize the prefecture's sterling record before Prince Yi. Half tuning out, Shen Jianchen caught the drift: glowing self-praise where even minor hiccups somehow got spun into triumphs.

千里寻主动起身；陈浩宇见朱淑真不再跪着，也站起来。
Speeches done at last, platters rolled out and dancing girls took the floor to bright music as cups clinked and smiles beamed around the hall. Wave after wave of officials stepped up in twos and threes to toast Prince Yi, keeping him busy. Good thing his liquor tolerance ran deep! Standing watchful beside his master—martial skills sharper by the month and no longer drooling over gourmet dishes—Bai Feng kept sharp focus on Prince Yi, itching to block toasts until he noticed His Highness did not even flush.

二人跟着朱晞颜上二楼雅间，朱景元自然也跟上去。
As toasts tapered off, Lin Jue stepped up holding his cup. "Your Highness, this humble officer toasts you. Sip at your ease; I drink mine dry!" Noticing Lin Jue's fresh uniform, Shen Jianchen recalled his promotion to Division Commander: Zhu Xiyan had shifted the former commander sideways on a tidy pretext after Lin Jue cracked the rumor case, and Shen Jianchen had nodded off on it.

音乐响起，舞伎再次舞动起来，一切恢复正常。官员们除议论议论刚才的事，继续饮酒攀谈，拉关系。
"Congratulations on your promotion, Division Commander Lin," Shen Jianchen said, raising his cup.

申简辰坐了一会儿，再也坐不住，起身也向二楼雅间而去，白风紧跟上王爷。
"This officer will never fail the trust of Your Highness, Prefect Zhu, and fellow lords. I'll double my efforts on every duty!" Down went Lin Jue's cup, Shen Jianchen draining his in return.

众官员瞅着仪王向楼上走去，心中疑惑，都悄悄议论着。
Right as Lin Jue turned to toast Prefect Zhu, Chen Haoyu burst through the front doors screaming at the top of his lungs: "Help! Help! Murder!!"

林觉只觉这位王爷不喜欢操心临安军政，倒喜欢管朱家的家事，实在令人费解。
Music cut off dead as Chen Haoyu ducked behind the startled dancers. Every official spun round to see Miss Zhu charge straight through the entrance yelling, "Hide today, can't hide forever, Chen Haoyu! Till the Chens cancel this betrothal, I beat you every time I lay eyes on you!" Round and round the dancers they went in full chase!

来到二楼的一个雅间，门上写着一句诗“葡萄美酒夜光杯”，申简辰示意白风推开门。
Delight sparked in Shen Jianchen at seeing her. What a wild scheme! Anything to kill that betrothal, count him in! Meanwhile Lin Jue smirked cold inside: Left me for this?! Richer house or not, Chen Haoyu can't hold a candle to me; bet you're kicking yourself raw! Around the room, baffled officials traded wide eyes before leaning back to enjoy free theater.

白风上前将门推开，朱晞颜正在怒批二人：“今日官员都在，你们这么闹，让我的脸往哪里搁？……”
Knowing exact what these two pulled—flopping yesterday, now hauling their circus right here to force his hand—Zhu Xiyan refused to budge. "Outrageous! Halt at once and get out!"

想要继续批评时，见仪王进来，赶紧起身拜见：“仪王怎么也上来？”
The Prefect's thunder froze both mid-step. Seizing his cue, Chen Haoyu cried, "Look for yourself, Uncle Zhu! Not even married yet and Zhu Shuzhen beats me like this; marry her into the Chen house and my life is over!" Pointing straight at yesterday's purple bruise, he shouted, "Look, esteemed lords! Zhu Shuzhen gave me this! Keep this up and she'll kill me..." Dropping straight onto both knees before the whole room, he wailed, "Uncle Zhu, right here before every lord I beg you on my knees: cancel the Zhu-Chen betrothal, and I'll owe you forever!"

“我最喜多管闲事，故而上来瞧瞧。”
Sharp mover, Qianli Xun cheered inside, dropping right onto her own knees beside him. "Father! Chen Haoyu is an idle loafer who haunts brothels and gambling dens and preys on respectable girls! I'd sooner stay an old maid for life than marry a man like him!"

朱晞颜想起上次仪王在他面前说过的话，那时他怀疑仪王对自家姑娘有意思，现在看来多半如此。
Steaming under every amused official gaze, Zhu Xiyan clamped his rage down tight. "Domestic business! Leave at once; we settle this after the banquet! Jingyuan!"

“朱家的家事竟然惊动仪王，是下官疏于管教，还请仪王莫要怪罪。”
Stepping out of the crowd at once, Zhu Jingyuan hurried over. "Get up, both of you, come home with me!" Reaching for his sister first, he urged, "Mind your image, Shuzhen. Stand up!"

“我为何要怪罪知府大人？”申简辰挑椅子坐下，“刚才我大概听明白，他们二人前来不过是对两家的亲事不满，既然不满，又何必硬把他们拴在一起？”
Refusing to budge, Qianli Xun shot an urgent SOS glance straight at Shen Jianchen.

千里寻对申简辰投去赞赏的目光，有仪王帮忙，说不定退婚之事能成。
Catching her eye—thrilled just seeing her, eager to jump in—Shen Jianchen spoke smooth. "Prefect Zhu, why not take these two up to an upstairs private room, hear their side proper, and decide?"

朱晞颜立刻跪下，朱景元也跟着跪下。
With Prince Yi speaking, Zhu Xiyan bowed crisp. "As Your Highness commands."

只听朱晞颜说：“请王爷不要插手下官的家事。……”
Qianli Xun hopped up at once, Chen Haoyu scrambling up right behind her as both trailed Zhu Xiyan and Zhu Jingyuan upstairs. Music struck back up, dancers resumed twirling, and officials returned to drinks and hushed gossip.

千里寻没想到父亲会来这招；陈浩宇也觉准岳父实在老奸巨猾，棋高一着。
Unable to sit still a minute longer, Shen Jianchen rose and headed upstairs too, Bai Feng right on his heels. Watching Prince Yi leave stirred quiet whispers around the tables, Lin Jue frowning puzzled why a prince uninterested in military administration poked so hard into Zhu family drama.

朱晞颜继续说：“淑真年纪不小，许多像她这个年纪的姑娘已儿女双全，何况她还离过婚？名声之事仪王也尽知，又被乔家所欺，陈家愿意结亲已是不错……”
Reaching an upstairs room labeled "Fine Grape Wine in Luminous Jade Cups," Shen Jianchen nodded to Bai Feng to push open the door. Inside, Zhu Xiyan was tearing into both culprits. "Every official in town downstairs and you pull a stunt like this—where do I put my face?!..." Cutting short as Prince Yi stepped in, he rose bowing. "What brings Your Highness upstairs?"

“知府大人不必担忧，这桩婚事没了，朱家姑娘会有更好的……”
"Born busybody; thought I'd check in," Shen Jianchen answered breezy.

仪王话中有话，朱晞颜又怎能听不出？还是坚持道：“下官不敢奢望更好的，毕竟淑真名声在外，再说陈浩宇也该承担起他应当承担的责任，还望仪王应允！”
Recalling Prince Yi's loaded hints once before, Zhu Xiyan felt his old hunch lock in: Prince Yi really did fancy his daughter! "Troubling Your Highness with private family mess—my failure in discipline. Forgive this humble official!"

千里寻纳闷，为何父亲要说“陈浩宇也该承担起他应当承担的责任”？
"Why blame you, Prefect Zhu?" Taking a chair smooth, Shen Jianchen went on, "Caught the gist downstairs: neither of them wants this match. Why force two unwilling kids together?"

申简辰被噎得半天说不出话，他站起来，走到千里寻身边：“幽栖居士，可否与我到隔壁雅间一叙？”
Qianli Xun flashed Shen Jianchen a grateful look; with Prince Yi leaning in, maybe this match would die today!

千里寻自然同意：“仪王邀请，淑真没有拒绝的道理。”
Without blinking, Zhu Xiyan dropped straight onto both knees, Zhu Jingyuan dropping right beside him. "Your Highness, I beg you not to intervene in this humble official's family affairs..."

陈浩宇一脸疑惑，目送二人出去，他想不通，为何仪王会邀请朱淑真到隔壁雅间？
Whoa—Qianli Xun had not seen that coming, while Chen Haoyu marveled to himself at his slick future father-in-law outfoxing a prince!

白风很懂事，负责乖乖守在雅间外。
"Shuzhen is no longer young," Zhu Xiyan pressed steady. "Half the women her age already raise sons and daughters. Worse, she is divorced, her reputation scarred as Your Highness knows well, and jilted by the Qiaos! Having the Chen house still willing to wed her is fortune enough..."

一进门，申简辰立刻说：“你也看到，知府大人态度强硬，不如今日答应跟我在一起，我好以此为理由要求两家退婚。”
"No need to worry, Prefect Zhu. Drop this match, and Miss Zhu has far brighter prospects waiting..."

千里寻毫不犹豫：“我绝不会做令梦容难过之事，既然仪王的身份也帮不了我，我再想别的办法。”
Catching every hidden barb yet standing iron-firm, Zhu Xiyan answered, "I dare not hope higher given Shuzhen's notoriety! Besides, Chen Haoyu owes her the responsibility he brought on himself. Grant this humble father's plea, Your Highness!"

申简辰很想不管不顾当着众人的面承认他喜欢朱家姑娘，不！是千里寻，可他又不愿违背千里寻的意愿。
Wait—why did Dad say Chen Haoyu "owes her responsibility"? Qianli Xun blinked confused.

﻿沉默半晌，艰难说出：“好，你既如此想，我便不再强求。”
Stopped cold by Zhu Xiyan's kneel, Shen Jianchen rose and stepped beside Qianli Xun. "Might Youqi Jushi step into the next room with me for a private word?"

申简辰走出雅间，并未进“葡萄美酒夜光杯”，不进，便已表明态度。
"Honored to oblige, Your Highness," Qianli Xun agreed at once. Watching them walk out left Chen Haoyu baffled: why pull Zhu Shuzhen aside alone? Outside the adjoining room, discreet Bai Feng took watch by the door.

仪王去隔壁时，朱晞颜与儿子朱景元已起身。朱晞颜猜到仪王对淑真的心思，担心他们在隔壁会有亲密的举动，见女儿进来，担忧地问：“仪王呢？”
The instant the door clicked shut, Shen Jianchen plunged in. "You saw how stubborn your dad is! Say yes to being with me right now, and I march back in calling off their wedding!"

“下楼了，他以后不会再管朱家的事。”
Qianli Xun did not hesitate a split second. "I'm never doing anything that breaks Mengrong's heart! If even a prince's rank can't budge him, I'll find another way!"

朱晞颜终于放心，按说女儿能跟仪王在一起再好不过，可谁让朱陈两家之事在先，最好不要再有意外发生，他实在折腾不起。
Every urge screamed at Shen Jianchen to throw caution away and proclaim his love for Qianli Xun before the whole world, yet he refused to trample her wish. Standing silent a heavy stretch, he forced out quiet, "Fine. If that's your stand, I won't push." Stepping out of the room, he bypassed "Fine Grape Wine in Luminous Jade Cups" straight for the stairs, his silent answer plain.

朱晞颜指着女儿及未来的女婿：“告诉你们，趁早断了退婚的心思，无论你们做什么，我都不会让步！”
Already back on their feet inside, anxious over what might happen next door, Zhu Xiyan exhaled sharp when his daughter walked back alone. "Where's Prince Yi?"

说完甩袖走下一楼，他发现仪王已离开。不在也好，没有仪王在，大家才能畅所欲言。
"Headed downstairs. Done meddling in Zhu business."

“你们两个在这里好好想想，不要再异想天开。”朱景元批评完，下楼继续陪父亲。
Relief flooded Zhu Xiyan. Sure, marrying an imperial prince sounded grand on paper, yet with the Chen match sealed first, fresh upheaval was the last risk he could afford. Pointing sharp at daughter and future son-in-law alike, he declared, "Hear me clear: bury every dream of canceling this wedding! No stunt you pull will move me one inch!" Flicking his sleeve, he marched downstairs, pleased to find Prince Yi already departed so everyone could drink free.

有什么好想的，还得继续努力。千里寻说：“喂！靠你了，回家让你奶奶认真耗着。”
"Sit here and cool your heads. Quit chasing fantasies!" Zhu Jingyuan scolded before following his father down.

“还用你说？”陈浩宇露出神秘的表情，“你刚跟仪王在隔壁说什么？他该不会是看上你了？”
Nothing to cool down over—keep fighting! Turning to Chen Haoyu, Qianli Xun prodded, "Your turn now. Go home and make sure your grandma keeps stalling hard!"

"Need you tell me?" Leaning in with a sly grin, Chen Haoyu poked, "What'd Prince Yi whisper to you next door? Tell me he isn't sweet on you!"
