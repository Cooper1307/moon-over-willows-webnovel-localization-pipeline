整体来看，第6章的翻译质量**非常出色**！译者非常熟悉海外网文（Web Novel）的语境，尤其是在处理“穿越（Transmigration）”、“系统/超自然力量”、以及现代人在古代醒来时的内心吐槽时，用词非常精准且生动。

比如把“拍戏的地方”翻译成 **movie set**，把“苦了吧唧”意译为 **Just the smell alone was enough to make him gag**，这些都极其地道，极大地增强了小说的网文感和阅读爽感。

针对你的审校维度，本章在**“情感强度的传达”、“词汇的丰富度（避免重复）”以及“特定历史称谓的处理”**上，还有一点优化的空间。

以下是具体的审校意见与优化建议：

### 1. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P6 削弱了原意**：`"Remember, take good care of Mengrong."`
    *   **问题**：原文是“替我好好爱梦容”。`take good care of` 只是“照顾”，削弱了影子对男主提出的“浪漫/爱情”要求。正因为要求他去“爱”，男主才会紧接着反驳“我有喜欢的人”。
    *   **修改**：`"Remember, love Mengrong in my stead."` 或 `"cherish Mengrong for me."`
*   **P11 逻辑微调**：`"Did I really enter that shadow's body?"`
    *   **问题**：原文“到了那影子的身体里”，直译会有一点语病（影子本身没有身体，是影子留下的肉身）。
    *   **修改**：`"Did I really enter the body that shadow left behind?"`
*   **P30 / P47 称呼习惯**：`Sovereign`
    *   **问题**：“官家”确实是宋代对皇帝的特有称呼，翻译成 Sovereign 在学术上没问题。但在网文语境中，Sovereign 略显生硬且缺乏“天威”的压迫感。海外读者更习惯于直接看懂他是最高统治者。
    *   **修改**：在日常对话中，建议直接用 **the Emperor** 或者 **His Majesty**。比如 P47 欺君之罪翻译成 `the crime of deceiving the Emperor` 会让读者更有代入感。
*   **P50 阶级感的体现**：`This servant will make the trip at once!`
    *   **问题**：原文下人自称“小的”。在极度恐惧（怕被判欺君之罪）的情况下，用 `This servant` 略显平淡。
    *   **修改**：用 **This lowly one** 或者直接体现他连滚带爬的状态。

### 2. 句子是否过长？表达是否过于重复？

*   **词汇重复**：`maidservant` 在本章中出现了 7 次。
    *   **优化**：在英文小说中，为了行文流畅，通常会交替使用 **maid, servant girl, handmaid**。
*   **P9 动作描写稍显拖沓**：`struck him down, sending him falling straight into the corpse on the bed...`
    *   **优化**：灵魂坠入肉身是一个非常有冲击力的瞬间，用词可以更利落：`A beam of light struck him, sending him plunging straight into the corpse on the bed.`
*   **P22 句式优化**：`The beautiful woman holding him brought Shen Jianchen no joy at all.`
    *   **优化**：稍微有些中式主谓结构。改为 `Being embraced by this beautiful woman brought Shen Jianchen no joy whatsoever.` 更加地道。

### 3. 标点使用是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **完美！** 译者对斜体字（*Italics*）用于内心独白（Inner thoughts）的掌握非常精准，这是英文网文的标配。
*   译注 `[TN: ...]` 的使用也很恰当，有效补充了文化背景。

### 4. 是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **非常有吸引力。** 男主作为现代大公司 CEO 醒来后，那种“不想喝苦药”、“非常享受丫鬟伺候穿鞋”的理直气壮，把现代人穿越后的反差萌写得很生动。

### 🌟 综合精修版英文参考 (Polished Version)

以下是吸收了上述建议后的精修版，重点提升了句子的流畅度、动作的张力以及对话的古代阶级感，你可以直接比对使用：

**(P1-7: The Death and the Encounter)**
On the outskirts of Bianliang—the Eastern Capital—stood Prince Yi's Manor. This was the residence of Zhao Pu, the thirteenth son of the reigning Emperor, Zhao Ji. His mother, Consort Qiao, had died shortly after giving birth to him. Raised by a wet nurse, Zhao Pu had been frail and sickly since childhood. Deemed unfit for any serious responsibility, he was the least favored among the Emperor's many sons.
On this day, Zhao Pu could hold on no longer. He closed his eyes and drew his final breath.
Qiao Mengrong, Nurse Zou, and the prince's personal maids wept bitterly beside his bed.
Shen Jianchen faintly heard the sound of weeping just as the power of the Millennial Divine Willow abruptly vanished. Still floating in midair, shaken to his core, he stared at the people in ancient clothing before him in absolute bewilderment. *I'm dead. Why am I on a movie set? Where is Qianli Xun?*
The man lying motionless on the bed exerted a mysterious gravitational pull on him. Fortunately, the pull was mild, allowing Shen Jianchen to steady himself and resist being drawn in.
Suddenly, he watched a shadow detach itself from the corpse. Emitting a soft golden glow, it drifted over to him and spoke: "Our fates are tied to this flesh. I am leaving—you may inhabit it. Remember, love Mengrong in my stead."
Being handed such a request by a total stranger thoroughly annoyed Shen Jianchen. "Who is Mengrong? Why should I love her for you? I already have someone I love—Qianli Xun!"
But the shadow couldn't hear him.

**(P8-21: The Awakening)**
A brilliant golden ray pierced down from higher up in the sky, accompanied by beautiful celestial music that filled the soul with boundless joy. The shadow drifted upward, drawn toward the light.
The radiance and the music were so mesmerizing that Shen Jianchen struggled to float up after them. But before he could even get close, a beam of light struck him, sending him plunging straight down into the corpse on the bed. The sensation was terrifying, akin to falling into a bottomless abyss.
When the falling finally stopped, Shen Jianchen heard the weeping again. But this time, it was vastly different. While he was floating, the voices had seemed close yet muffled. Now, the crying was deafeningly clear, ringing right in his ears and grinding on his nerves. 
"Stop crying!" he barked.
The weeping ceased instantly. Shen Jianchen realized the weightless sensation was gone; instead, an inexplicable, solid sense of grounding filled his entire being. *Did I really enter the body that shadow left behind?* he thought. *Might as well open my eyes and find out.*
He tentatively opened his eyes and, sure enough, a bright world greeted him. Hovering above him were three female faces. One was dressed as a maid from antiquity. The second was a kindly-looking woman in her forties. The third was a strikingly beautiful and elegant young lady, bearing the distinct aura of nobility.
Seeing Prince Yi miraculously awaken, Qiao Mengrong threw herself at him in overwhelming joy. "Brother! I thought I would never see you again, but you've come back to life! *Sob*...!"
"Quickly!" Nurse Zou urged. "Fetch His Highness's medicinal pills and have him take one before something happens again!"
A maid hastily retrieved a brocade box from a nearby table. Opening it, she used a silk handkerchief to pinch a single pill and presented it to Qiao Mengrong.
Stopping her tears, Qiao Mengrong gently released the prince. Holding the pill between her slender, delicate fingers, she brought it to his lips. "Brother, quickly take this. It'll do you good."
Shen Jianchen hadn't even figured out what was going on yet—how could he just accept random medicine? Besides, he hated bitter medicine more than anything. Just the smell alone was enough to make him gag, let alone swallow it.
With the pill right at his nose, the pungent, bitter stench was unbearable. He swatted her hand away and sat up in one fluid motion. "I'm not sick. Why would I take that garbage?"
Her brother had never been rough with her before. Rubbing her stinging hand, Qiao Mengrong was stunned. His voice boomed with strength, and the way he sat up showed none of his usual sickly frailty. Forgetting her pain and shock, she turned around. "Nurse Zou, could it be that a trip to the Gates of Hell has completely purged Brother's illness?"
Knowing a bit of medicine, Nurse Zou grabbed Prince Yi's wrist to check his pulse. "His Highness is cured!" she cried out, trembling with excitement. "Cured! He is truly cured!"
Hearing this, the maids in the room broke into delighted smiles.

**(P22-37: Discovering His Identity)**
Qiao Mengrong was the happiest of all. She threw her arms around Prince Yi again. "Brother! The Heavens must have been moved by my sincerity to bring you back from the dead and restore your health!"
Being embraced by this beautiful woman brought Shen Jianchen no joy whatsoever. He knew full well he was occupying a stranger's body. This was no movie set—it was a completely different era. Right now, he only cared about one thing: who was he?
He gently but firmly pushed Qiao Mengrong away. "Who are you? Who am I? And where is this?"
Everyone froze. Qiao Mengrong, in particular, was devastated—her brother's questions clearly meant he didn't remember her.
Nurse Zou, being the most seasoned in the room, quickly stepped in to mediate. "Do not panic, Princess Jiahe. I reckon His Highness took a trip to the underworld, and they forced him to drink Mengpo's Soup of Forgetfulness before sending him back. The drawback is that it wipes one's memories clean. The blessing is that it cures all mortal diseases."
Qiao Mengrong finally breathed a sigh of relief. "If Nurse Zou says so, then my mind is at ease. Brother's health is what matters most. As for his memories, there's no rush—we will help him recover them slowly. And if he truly can't remember... then we'll just let his new life start from today."
Seeing them discuss folklore instead of answering his questions, Shen Jianchen grew impatient. "You still haven't answered me."
"Don't worry, Brother, I'll tell you everything," Qiao Mengrong soothed. "You are Prince Yi. Your name is Zhao Pu, and this is Prince Yi's Manor..."
Before she could finish, Shen Jianchen cut in eagerly, "I'm a prince? The Emperor's son? What's my father's name?"
"You are a prince, the thirteenth son of the Emperor. His Majesty's true name is not something we subjects are permitted to speak aloud, but I can tell you that the current year is the fourth year of the Xuanhe era in the Great Song Dynasty." As she spoke, Qiao Mengrong watched Prince Yi's expression carefully. He still looked utterly lost.
Shen Jianchen hadn't expected that dying and coming back to life would land him the prestigious status of an imperial prince. "You call me 'Brother'—does that make you a princess?"
"Your Highness," Nurse Zou explained, "this is Princess Jiahe. You found her on the side of the road and brought her home as a younger sister when you were eight years old. The Emperor later bestowed her the title of Princess Jiahe. The two of you grew up together as childhood sweethearts. In fact, His Majesty has already issued a decree for your marriage..."
"What? An arranged marriage?" Shen Jianchen took a closer look at this "sister" he supposedly picked up off the street. Compared to Qianli Xun, she exuded a much softer, more delicate femininity. Remembering what the shadow had told him, he blurted out, "Is your name Mengrong?"
"You remember my name, Brother!" Qiao Mengrong beamed.
Her bubbling enthusiasm failed to move him. Pointing at Nurse Zou, he asked, "And who is she?"
"This is Nurse Zou," Mengrong explained. "She is the wet nurse who raised you. Nurse Zou and I are your closest family in this world."
"A wet nurse? What about my birth mother?" Shen Jianchen reasoned that no matter how close a wet nurse was, she couldn't replace a biological mother. He needed the full picture.
Nurse Zou sighed heavily. "I was originally a maid in Consort Qiao's maiden home. After the Consort entered the imperial palace, I was married off. Tragically, when the Consort gave birth to Your Highness in her twenties, she passed away soon after. I had given birth just two months prior to that. My baby only nursed from me for two months before falling gravely ill and passing away. My husband, broken by grief, soon followed the child to the grave. The Qiao family then sent me to the palace to care for you in the Consort's stead. Raising you is what gradually pulled me out of the despair of losing my own family."

**(P38-55: Assuming the Role)**
The tale was heart-wrenching, and Shen Jianchen decided not to pry any further, afraid of unearthing even more tragedies. He had grasped the basic relationships between the original owner and the people in this room; the rest could wait. Since he had commandeered this man's body to live again, it was time to figure out how to navigate this new life.
With this in mind, he refused to sit in bed any longer. He threw off the brocade quilt and swung his legs over the edge. 
The very moment his feet touched the floor, two maids rushed over, crouching down to slip his shoes on for him. Meanwhile, Mengrong and Nurse Zou stepped aside, standing with utmost respect and deference.
Shen Jianchen thoroughly enjoyed the sensation. Even as the CEO of a massive modern corporation, he had never experienced this level of personal servitude. *Being a prince in the Song Dynasty isn't half bad,* he mused.
Mengrong had mentioned it was the "fourth year of Xuanhe," but Shen Jianchen was practically illiterate when it came to history and had no desire to study it now. He had absolutely no clue what "Xuanhe" meant.
His thoughts drifted to Qianli Xun. If she were here, she would have instantly deciphered the significance of that era name. They had died together in the earthquake and were swept away together by the Millennial Divine Willow. Since he had arrived in the Song Dynasty, had she been sent to this era too?
Once his shoes were on, Shen Jianchen stepped out of the room. Looking up at the vast sky hanging over the manor, he asked silently, *Qianli Xun... where are you?*
Just then, a servant rushed back into the courtyard. Upon seeing the supposedly dead prince standing right in front of him, the man's legs turned to jelly. "A ghost...!"
"What ghost!" a maid snapped. "That is His Highness, Prince Yi!"
The servant collapsed to his knees, trembling. "I—I just returned from the Imperial Palace after reporting His Highness's passing! The moment I step back into the manor, His Highness is alive again! Haven't I just committed the capital crime of deceiving the Emperor?!"
Qiao Mengrong and Nurse Zou emerged from the room.
"Make another trip," Qiao Mengrong commanded. "Explain to the palace that His Highness walked to the Gates of Hell and returned, and that his illness has been entirely cured."
The servant frantically kowtowed. "Congratulations on your rebirth, Your Highness! This lowly one will make the trip at once!" With that, he scrambled to his feet and sprinted out the gates.
Qiao Mengrong approached the prince softly. "Brother, are you hungry? I can have the kitchen prepare something for you."
Shen Jianchen focused on his new body but felt no hunger pangs. "I'm not hungry. I want to walk around the estate. Waking up with no memory makes this place feel entirely foreign to me. If I'm going to live here, I need to learn my way around."
"I'll accompany you, Brother."
Shen Jianchen didn't object, nor did he wait for her. He simply started strolling down the manor's stone-paved paths alone, taking in his surroundings with sharp curiosity.
With her maids in tow, Qiao Mengrong trailed behind Prince Yi, staring at her brother's back with a gaze that was equal parts thrilled and bewildered.
Watching them go, Nurse Zou turned to the maid beside her. "Go tell the kitchen to begin preparing His Highness's meal. Once he tires from his walk, it will be the perfect time to serve it. It has been an eternity since His Highness last left his sickbed to stroll the grounds. This truly is a day for celebration... The Princess has finally seen the end of her bitter days."
