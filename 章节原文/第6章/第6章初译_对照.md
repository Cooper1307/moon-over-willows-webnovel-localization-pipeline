第6章 穿越成为赵仪王
## Chapter 6: Awakening as Prince Yi

东京汴梁的郊区，有座仪王府，是当朝官家赵佶的十三子赵朴的府邸。赵朴的母亲是乔贵妃，只可惜乔贵妃刚生下儿子就撒手人寰。赵朴被奶娘带大，从小体弱多病，不堪重任，在众多皇子中最不受官家重视。
In the environs of Bianliang, the Eastern Capital, stood Prince Yi's Manor—the residence of Zhao Pu, the thirteenth son of the current Sovereign, Zhao Ji. Zhao Pu's mother was Consort Qiao, though, tragically, she had passed away shortly after giving birth to him. Raised by his wet nurse, Zhao Pu had been chronically ill and frail since childhood, unfit for any heavy responsibilities, making him the least favored among the Sovereign's many princes.

这日，赵朴再也坚持不下去，闭上眼睛，咽下最后一口气。
On this particular day, Zhao Pu could hold on no longer; he closed his eyes and drew his final breath.

乔梦容和奶娘邹嬷嬷，以及赵朴的贴身丫鬟哭得甚是伤心。
Qiao Mengrong, Nurse Zou, and Zhao Pu's personal maids wept bitterly.

神柳的力量在申简辰隐约听到哭声时猛然消失。他飘在空中，惊魂未定，眼前身着古装之人令他迷惑：“我死了，怎么会来到拍戏的地方？千里寻呢？”
Just as Shen Jianchen faintly heard the sound of weeping, the power of the Millennial Divine Willow abruptly dissipated. Still floating in midair and shaken to his core, he found himself bewildered by the sight of people dressed in ancient clothing before him. *I'm dead, so why have I ended up on a movie set? Where is Qianli Xun?*

床上躺着的人向他发出莫名的引力，还好引力不大，他能稳住不被吸引而去。
The figure lying on the bed exerted a mysterious gravitational pull on him. Fortunately, the pull was mild, allowing him to steady himself and avoid being drawn in.

接着看到有影子从那人的身体里剥离出来，散发着金光，飘到他面前：“你与我的肉身有缘，我离开，你可以住进去，记住，替我好好爱梦容。”
Then, he watched as a shadow detached from the body. Emitting a golden glow, it drifted over to him and spoke. "You share a destiny with my mortal vessel. I am leaving, so you may inhabit it. Remember, love Mengrong well in my stead."

无缘无故被不认识的人嘱托，申简辰很生气：“梦容是谁？我凭什么要替你好好爱她？我有喜欢的人，她是千里寻！”
Annoyed by this sudden entreaty from a complete stranger, Shen Jianchen said, "Who is Mengrong? Why should I love her for you? I already have someone I love, and her name is Qianli Xun!"

对方根本听不到他的声音。
The shadow could not hear his voice at all.

更高的空中发出一道金光，美妙的仙乐响起，让人听了畅快无比，影子朝金光飘去。
A brilliant ray pierced through the heavens from above, accompanied by an exquisite celestial melody that filled the soul with joy. The shadow drifted upward toward the divine glow.

金光和仙乐实在太美，申简辰努力向上飘，还未到跟前，被一束金光打落，不偏不倚掉落进躺在床上的尸身中，整个过程仿佛是掉进无穷无尽的深渊。
The light and the celestial music were so beautiful that Shen Jianchen struggled to float upwards toward them. But before he could get close, a flash of radiance struck him down, sending him falling straight into the corpse on the bed. The sensation was akin to falling into a bottomless abyss.

不再往下掉时，申简辰又听到哭声，与刚才飘在空中时听到的区别甚大。在空中时，总觉得那些人看似很近，哭声却若隐若现。而此时真真切切，回荡在耳边，让人心烦，他大声说：“别哭了！”
When the falling sensation finally ceased, Shen Jianchen heard the crying once more, though it felt vastly different from before. When he had been floating in the air, the people seemed close, yet their wails had felt faint and indistinct. Now, the sobbing was painfully real, echoing sharply in his ears and grating on his nerves. "Stop crying!" he shouted.

声音果然停止，申简辰觉得自己不再有轻飘飘的感觉，莫名的踏实感充满全身，心想：“难道我真的到了那影子的身体里？不妨睁开眼睛试试。”
The weeping instantly ceased. Shen Jianchen realized he no longer felt weightless; instead, an inexplicable sense of groundedness filled his entire being. *Did I really enter that shadow's body?* he thought. *I might as well open my eyes and see.*

他试着睁开双眼，果然是一个明亮的世界，眼前还多出三个脑袋，都是女的。一个是古代丫鬟的装扮；一个是大约四十岁的女人，面容慈祥；一个长相俊俏，端庄典雅，一副大家闺秀的模样。
He tentatively opened his eyes and, sure enough, was greeted by a brightly lit world. Looming over him were three faces, all female. One was dressed as an ancient maidservant; the second was a kindly-looking woman of about forty; and the third was a pretty, elegant girl who embodied the image of a noble young lady.

乔梦容见仪王竟然奇迹般醒来，高兴地扑上去：“哥哥，我以为以后再也见不到你，没想到，你又活过来！呜呜……！”
Seeing that Prince Yi had miraculously awakened, Qiao Mengrong threw herself at him joyfully. "Brother! I thought I would never see you again, but you've come back to life! *Sob*...!"

邹嬷嬷急切地说：“赶紧把王爷的药丸拿来，给他服下，别一会儿又出事！”
Nurse Zou said urgently, "Quickly, fetch His Highness's medicinal pills and have him take one! We can't let anything else happen to him!"

丫鬟匆忙从一旁的桌上拿来一个锦盒，打开，用丝帕捏了一丸药，送到乔梦容跟前。
The maidservant hastily grabbed a brocade box from a nearby table. Opening it, she used a silk handkerchief to pinch a single pill and presented it to Qiao Mengrong.

乔梦容不再哭泣，放开仪王，用水葱般的手指捏住药丸，递到仪王嘴边：“哥哥，快吃下这丸药，它对你的身体有益。”
Ceasing her tears, Qiao Mengrong released Prince Yi. With slender, delicate fingers, she took the pill and offered it to his lips. "Brother, quickly take this pill. It will do your body good."

申简辰还未搞明白是怎么回事，就被人喂药，他怎能随便接受？再说他平时最讨厌这些苦了吧唧的药，闻见味儿都想吐，何况吃下去？
Shen Jianchen had yet to grasp the situation, so how could he blindly accept medicine being fed to him? Moreover, he absolutely despised bitter concoctions; just the smell alone was enough to make him gag, let alone swallowing it.

药丸在嘴边，离鼻子很近，一股难闻的药味儿让他受不了，不由伸手将药丸打落，顺势坐起来：“我没病，吃那玩意儿干什么？”
With the pill hovering near his lips and terribly close to his nose, the repulsive herbal smell became unbearable. He swatted the pill away and sat up. "I'm not sick. Why would I take that?"

哥哥第一次对她无礼，乔梦容揉着被打疼的手背，很是诧异。哥哥声如洪钟，坐起来的状态也不像以前病病怏怏。她顾不上疼痛和诧异：“邹嬷嬷，难道哥哥到鬼门关走一趟，疾病全消？”
It was the first time her brother had ever been rude to her. Massaging the back of her stung hand, Qiao Mengrong was surprised. Her brother's voice resonated, and the way he sat up bore none of his former sickly frailty. Setting aside her pain and shock, she asked, "Nurse Zou, could it be that by visiting the Gates of Hell, Brother's illness has been entirely cured?"

邹嬷嬷懂些医术，抓起仪王的手腕，摸了摸脉，激动地说：“王爷好了！好了！真的好了！”
Possessing some knowledge of medicine, Nurse Zou seized Prince Yi's wrist to check his pulse. "His Highness is cured!" she said excitedly. "He's cured! He is truly cured!"

旁边的丫鬟们听到也都高兴不已。
Hearing this, the maidservants standing nearby were likewise overjoyed.

乔梦容更是开心，抱住仪王：“哥哥，肯定是上天被我的诚心感动，才让你死而复生，身体也恢复正常。”
Qiao Mengrong was the most elated of all. She threw her arms around Prince Yi again. "Brother! It must be that the Heavens were moved by my sincerity, allowing you to return from the dead and restoring your health completely!"

申简辰被眼前这位美女抱着，没有一丝开心，他清楚自己住进一个陌生的身体里，眼前的情景并不是拍戏，而是另一个时代，他此时最想搞清楚他是谁。
Being embraced by the beautiful woman before him brought Shen Jianchen no joy. He was aware that he was inhabiting a stranger's body, and the scene unfolding around him was no movie set—it was an entirely different era. In this moment, all he wanted was to find out who exactly he was.

他用手扶开乔梦容，问道：“你是谁？我又是谁？这是哪里？”
Gently pushing Qiao Mengrong away, he asked, "Who are you? And who am I? Where is this?"

众人听到仪王的发问都愣住，尤其是乔梦容，哥哥的问题明显是不记得她。
Everyone froze upon hearing Prince Yi's questions. Qiao Mengrong, in particular, was distressed; her brother's words made it clear that he had forgotten her.

还是邹嬷嬷有经验：“郡主放心，王爷从鬼门关走一趟回来，据我估计是喝过孟婆汤后才被送回来，这孟婆汤坏处是能让人忘记生前之事，好处便是可消百病。”
It was the experienced Nurse Zou who spoke up. "Rest assured, Princess Jiahe. I suspect that when His Highness visited the underworld, he was made to drink Mengpo's Soup before being sent back. [TN: In Chinese mythology, Mengpo's Soup or the Soup of Oblivion is given to souls in the underworld to make them forget their past lives before reincarnation]. The drawback of the soup is that it erases one's past memories, but its blessing is that it cleanses all ailments."

乔梦容终于松口气：“邹嬷嬷这样说，我便放心。哥哥身体健康才最重要，至于记忆，不急，我们帮他慢慢恢复，实在恢复不了，就让他的记忆从现在开始……”
Qiao Mengrong finally let out a breath of relief. "If Nurse Zou says so, then my heart is at ease. Brother's health is the most important thing. As for his memories, there's no rush; we can help him recover them slowly. And if he truly cannot remember, then let his memories begin anew from this moment..."

这些人不回答问题，只顾言其他，申简辰显得不耐烦：“你们还没回答我的问题。”
Seeing them talk among themselves rather than answering his questions, Shen Jianchen grew impatient. "You still haven't answered me."

乔梦容说：“哥哥别急，我一一告诉你。哥哥是仪王，大名赵朴，这里是仪王府……”
"Don't worry, Brother, I'll explain everything to you," Qiao Mengrong said. "You are Prince Yi, and your formal name is Zhao Pu. This is Prince Yi's Manor..."

没等乔梦容说完，申简辰急切地问道：“我是王爷？是皇帝的儿子？我爹叫什么？”
Before Mengrong could finish, Shen Jianchen eagerly asked, "I'm a prince? The emperor's son? What's my father's name?"

“哥哥是王爷，是官家的十三子，官家的名讳不是我等能提的，我可以告诉哥哥现在是大宋宣和四年。”乔梦容边说，边观察仪王的表情，他看上去还是一脸懵。
"You are a prince, yes—the Sovereign's thirteenth son. The Sovereign's name is not something we are permitted to speak aloud, but I can tell you that the current year is the fourth year of the Xuanhe era in the Great Song Dynasty." As she spoke, Qiao Mengrong watched Prince Yi's expression; he still looked confused.

申简辰没想到自己死一回，再活过来，竟然成为皇帝的儿子，倒也身份尊贵：“你叫我哥哥，难道你是公主？”
Shen Jianchen never expected that dying and coming back to life would turn him into the emperor's son. It was quite an esteemed status. "You keep calling me 'Brother'—does that make you a princess?"

邹嬷嬷解释道：“王爷，这位是郡主，是王爷八岁时从路边捡回来的妹妹，后来被官家封为佳和郡主，跟王爷从小一起长大，青梅竹马，而且王爷和郡主已被官家赐婚……”
Nurse Zou stepped in to explain. "Your Highness, this is the Princess Jiahe. You found her on the side of the road and brought her home as a younger sister when you were eight years old. The Sovereign later bestowed upon her the title of Princess Jiahe. The two of you grew up together, and furthermore, the Sovereign has already arranged a betrothal for your marriage..."

“什么？赐婚？”申简辰仔细瞅瞅眼前这位被捡回来的妹妹，与千里寻相比更显女子的温柔。他想起影子说的话，不由问道：“你叫梦容？”
"What? A betrothal?" Shen Jianchen looked closely at the adopted sister before him. Compared to Qianli Xun, she exuded a more gentle femininity. Recalling the shadow's words, he asked, "Is your name Mengrong?"

乔梦容兴奋道：“哥哥记着我的名字！”
"You remember my name, Brother!" Qiao Mengrong beamed.

看到乔梦容兴奋的样子，申简辰提不起兴趣，指着邹嬷嬷问：“她是谁？”
Her enthusiasm left him unmoved. Pointing toward Nurse Zou, Shen Jianchen asked, "And who is she?"

乔梦容解释道：“这位是邹嬷嬷，是把哥哥带大的奶娘，我和邹嬷嬷是哥哥在这个世界上最亲的人。”
"This is Nurse Zou," Qiao Mengrong explained. "She is the wet nurse who raised you. Nurse Zou and I are your closest people in this world."

“奶娘？我亲娘呢？”申简辰觉得奶娘再亲怎能亲得过亲娘？还需问清楚。
"Wet nurse? What about my real mother?" Shen Jianchen reasoned that no matter how close a wet nurse was, she could not replace a biological mother. He needed to know.

邹嬷嬷叹口气：“哎，我本是乔贵妃在娘家时的丫鬟，贵妃进宫后，我就嫁了人。谁知贵妃二十多岁生下王爷后撒手人寰。我比贵妃早两个月生下孩子，那孩子才吃下我两个月的奶，后来生一场大病，死了，他爹因伤心过度也随那孩子而去。乔家就把我送到宫里替贵妃照顾王爷，有了王爷，我也渐渐走出失去孩子的痛苦。”
Nurse Zou let out a sigh. "I was originally a maidservant in Consort Qiao's maiden home. After the Consort entered the imperial palace, I married. Who could have foreseen that when the Consort gave birth to Your Highness, she would pass away shortly after? I had given birth two months before she did. My baby only nursed from me for two months before falling gravely ill and dying, and his father, overcome with grief, followed him into the grave. The Qiao family then sent me to the palace to care for you in the Consort's stead. Having you helped me gradually recover from the sorrow of losing my own child."

邹嬷嬷说得感人，申简辰不想再问下去，怕问出更多让人伤感的事情。目前他已清楚眼前这几位与正主的关系，其他的以后再慢慢了解。既然借用正主的身体活过来，还是想想新的生活该怎么过。
Though Nurse Zou's tale was moving, Shen Jianchen did not press further, afraid of unearthing more sad history. He now understood the relationship between the body's original owner and the people before him; everything else could be learned later. Since he had borrowed this man's body to return to life, he might as well start figuring out how to live this new existence.

想到这里，他不愿继续坐在床上，掀开身上的锦被，刚把脚放在地上，两个丫鬟立刻过来，蹲下给他穿鞋。而那位唤他“哥哥”的梦容和邹嬷嬷都闪到一边，看上去十分恭顺。
With that in mind, he did not want to remain in bed. Throwing off the brocade quilt, he put his feet on the floor. The moment his feet touched the ground, two maidservants immediately came over, crouching down to put on his shoes. Meanwhile, Mengrong—the one who called him "Brother"—and Nurse Zou stepped aside, appearing quite respectful.

申简辰甚是享受这种感觉，自己虽曾是大公司CEO，可也从来没享受过这等待遇，在宋代当个王爷倒也不错。
Shen Jianchen enjoyed the feeling. He had been a CEO of a large company, but he had never experienced such treatment. Being a prince in the Song Dynasty was not bad.

那位梦容说现在是宣和四年，申简辰不懂历史，也不愿在这些事情上下功夫，所以根本搞不清楚宣和四年是个什么玩意儿。
Mengrong had mentioned it was the "fourth year of Xuanhe," but Shen Jianchen knew nothing of history and had no interest in learning, so he had no idea what the "fourth year of Xuanhe" meant.

他想起千里寻，如果她在身边，“宣和四年”定能被她品出意味来。他们一起在地震中丧生，一起被神柳的力量带走，他来到宋代，她是否也来到这个时代？
His thoughts turned to Qianli Xun. If she were by his side, she would undoubtedly have found meaning in the phrase. They had perished together in the earthquake and been swept away together by the power of the Millennial Divine Willow. Since he had arrived in the Song Dynasty, could she have come to this era as well?

穿好鞋后，申简辰走出屋外，抬头望着仪王府的上空，在心中问道：“千里寻，你在哪里？”
With his shoes on, Shen Jianchen stepped outside. Looking up at the sky above Prince Yi's Manor, he asked silently, *Qianli Xun, where are you?*

一名下人匆匆从外面回来，看到王爷出现在眼前，吓得腿脚发软：“鬼啊！……”
A servant hurried back from outside. Upon seeing the prince standing before him, the man's legs went weak with fear. "A ghost!..."

一个丫鬟批评道：“什么鬼！是仪王！”
"What ghost?" one of the maidservants scolded. "That is Prince Yi!"

下人跪在地上：“我刚到皇宫报了王爷离世的消息，一进王府大门，王爷又活过来，我岂不是犯了欺君之罪？”
The servant knelt on the ground. "I just went to the Imperial Palace to report His Highness's passing! The moment I return through the manor gates, His Highness is alive again! Haven't I committed the crime of deceiving the Sovereign?" [TN: Deceiving the sovereign was considered a capital offense in ancient China, punishable by execution.]

乔梦容和邹嬷嬷也走出来。
Qiao Mengrong and Nurse Zou came outside as well.

乔梦容吩咐道：“你再去一趟，向宫里说明，王爷到鬼门关走了一趟，又回来，而且疾病全无。”
"Make another trip," Qiao Mengrong said. "Explain to the palace that His Highness went to the Gates of Hell but was sent back, and that his illness has been entirely cured."

下人先磕头：“恭喜王爷获得新生，小的这就再多跑一趟。”说完，起身跑了出去。
The servant kowtowed. "Congratulations to Your Highness on your rebirth! This servant will make the trip at once!" With that, he got up and ran out.

乔梦容走到仪王身边：“哥哥，你饿不饿？我让厨房给你做些吃的。”
Qiao Mengrong approached Prince Yi. "Brother, are you hungry? I can have the kitchen prepare something for you."

申简辰努力感受着新的身体，并没有饥饿的感觉：“我不饿，我想在王府走走，刚醒来，什么都不记得，这王府看着也陌生，以后要在这里生活，得熟悉熟悉。”
Shen Jianchen took stock of his new body but felt no hunger. "I'm not hungry. I want to walk around the manor. I just woke up and don't remember anything, and this place looks unfamiliar. If I'm going to live here, I need to get familiar with it."

“我陪着哥哥。”
"I will accompany you, Brother."

申简辰并未拒绝，也未等乔梦容，一个人踏着王府的石板路，好奇地瞧着周围的一切。
Shen Jianchen did not refuse, but he did not wait for her either. Walking alone along the manor's stone paths, he looked around with curiosity.

乔梦容带着丫鬟跟在仪王身后，用既兴奋又陌生的目光观察着哥哥的背影。
With the maidservants behind her, Qiao Mengrong followed Prince Yi, watching her brother's back with a gaze mixed of excitement and unfamiliarity.

邹嬷嬷给身边的丫鬟说：“去吩咐厨房给王爷准备好饭食，他逛累了正好将饭摆上。很久了，王爷一直躺在病榻上，都没在王府内走动过，现在真是可喜可贺，郡主总算是熬出头。”
Nurse Zou leaned over and whispered to a passing maidservant, "Go instruct the kitchen to have His Highness's meal prepared. Once he tires of walking, it'll be the perfect time to serve it. It has been so long since His Highness last left his sickbed to stroll the manor grounds; this truly is a blessing worth celebrating. The Princess Jiahe has finally seen the end of her suffering."
