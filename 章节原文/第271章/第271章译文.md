## Chapter 271: A Stroll, a Date, and Lunch Together

Haoyu refused to let go of her hand, pulling her from stall to stall with boyish glee. Qianli Xun stayed quieter than usual—still figuring out what to chat about on a date with him—yet laughed aloud whenever some quirky trinket caught her eye. By noon, her fingers were getting damp and cramped in his sweaty grip, though she endured it rather than kill his mood until her stomach saved her.

"I'm starving. Let's grab lunch." Eating meant he *had* to let go!

"Juxiang Tower's right around the corner—let's eat there!"

The syllable *Juxiang* instantly recalled the day he'd slipped and called it *Chunxiang Brothel*. All his gross brothel past flashed back, and she yanked her hand free, marching ahead alone.

Haoyu jogged right after her. "Hey, what's wrong? Don't like Juxiang Tower? We can pick somewhere else!"

"Who said I don't want Juxiang Tower? Aren't I walking straight toward it?"

"Then why are you mad?"

Stopping dead, she glared at him. "I hate guys who accidentally call Juxiang Tower *Chunxiang Brothel*! I hate the old you!"

Haoyu blinked—then beamed and pulled her into a hug. "I'm thrilled you actually care! Means I'm finally in your heart!"

Qianli Xun didn't shove him off right away, startled by her own reaction. Back when he camped out at brothels all week, she couldn't have cared less—now a passing memory actually stung! That meant she *was* starting to care about him. Hard to stay furious after realizing that. "All right, all right, people are staring—let go! My stomach's growling!"

When he reached for her hand again, she tucked it behind her back. "Your palm's drenched in sweat—gross."

"Fair enough—no hand-holding, just walk right next to me so everybody knows you're my wife!"

"Fine."

Shoulder to shoulder—with Haoyu edging back in whenever an inch of air opened between them—they reached Juxiang Tower's entrance just as Prince Yi walked straight out, followed by Bai Feng, Han Gu, Wen Xin, and constables escorting two brawling waiters, one nursing a knife gash on his arm from fighting over a girl.

The instant Shen Jianchen stepped through the doors, his gaze instinctively snapped straight onto Qianli Xun standing tight against Haoyu.

Feeling his eyes on her, Qianli Xun tensed and instinctively seized Haoyu's hand—forgetting for half a second even to curtsy.

Over the moon, Haoyu locked his fingers tight around hers and stepped forward bowing. "Fancy meeting Your Highness here! My wife and I came for lunch—would you honor us by joining our table?"

Shen Jianchen's gaze dropped from her face to their joined hands. Swallowing the sharp ache, he kept his voice smooth. "Official business awaits—I won't hold you up. Enjoy your meal, Young Master Chen, Madam." Stepping aside to clear the entrance, he led his staff to yield the way.

Haoyu bowed with her hand still clasped, Qianli Xun dipping a silent curtsy—yet as her foot crossed the threshold, Prince Yi spoke behind them.

"A moment, Youqi Jushi."

Turning together, Haoyu stayed silent while Qianli Xun kept her eyes lowered. "Yes, Your Highness?"

Masking how much that icy formality stung, Shen Jianchen said evenly, "I hear the bamboo grove outside town is back under your name?"

"Yes."

"I'm quite fond of that grove. Last time a case took me out there, I asked Kujue to pick a scenic spot and build me a bamboo cottage. Hope you don't mind my borrowing a corner of your land."

She'd once thought of building a cottage there herself—better drop that idea now. "That Your Highness favors the grove honors both the villagers and me. Need anything, just say the word."

"Good. Once it's finished, you and Young Master Chen must drop by as my guests."

"We wouldn't miss it, Your Highness!" Haoyu chimed in briskly. Satisfied, Shen Jianchen marched off with his prisoners.

Inside, the bustling ground floor had already bounced back from the waiters' brawl. Qianli Xun slipped her hand free for lunch while Haoyu led her toward the stairs—"My private booth's on the second floor"—only for a waiter to step in apologetically.

"Forgive me, Young Master Chen—since you haven't dropped by since your wedding, the lease lapsed and somebody else booked your booth."

Keeping his temper checked in front of his wife, Haoyu snapped, "Got tied up! Go tell whoever's in there to clear out—silver's no issue."

"Can't touch him—it's Master Jiang! Mean customer, we can't afford to cross him."

"Scared of crossing him and not scared of crossing *me*?!"

"We daren't cross either of you—there's just only one booth, and all the others upstairs are full too!"

Seeing Haoyu's fist cock back, Qianli Xun caught his wrist. "Downstairs is fine—don't blow up over a table."

Instantly beaming, Haoyu seized her hand again. "Wife's right—I won't sweat it! Come on, open table over there."

Sitting face to face, he clasped her hand in *both* of his. "Having you beside me even fixed my temper—you're everything to me."

Ignoring his grip, Qianli Xun finally voiced the puzzle chewing at her. "Chen Haoyu, why on earth do you like me? I'm divorced, three years older than you, and used to beat you black and blue—are you a masochist?"

"Masochist? What an ugly word! I just love you—no rhyme or reason! I'm the luckiest guy alive marrying the very woman I fell for—hehehe!"

Envy touched her—he really *was* lucky. What about her? Time would tell.

Watching the notorious playboy grin like a love-struck pup, the waiter stepped over marveling at what marriage did to a man. "What'll you two have?"

"Whatever my wife wants!" Haoyu beamed, releasing her hand at last.

Wanting a taste of real romance—remembering how sweet her first picnic with Wenyuan had felt—Qianli Xun ordered the exact three dishes from that memory: "Osmanthus glutinous rice lotus root, minced pork with scallions, and fuzzy meatballs."

"Starving, huh? Going straight for the hearty plates!" Haoyu grinned. "Add West Lake vinegar fish, shredded chicken with Hangzhou chrysanthemum, and two crisp greens—my usuals."

While waiting, Haoyu propped his chin in his hands staring at her. "So pretty."

"Humph! Bet if I were ugly you wouldn't look twice."

"Well, yeah."

"Knew it—total shallow flirt!"

Sitting straight, he protested, "Not true! Plenty of pretty girls out there, yet I only want *you*."

"Oh, like you didn't chase plenty before!"

Guilt flashed across his face. "My fault, okay? Never actually loved any of them—just messing around. You're the first woman I've ever felt real love for."

"Just messing around—and you say it proud!"

"Quit digging up my old dirt, Wife? Give a reformed guy a break—I swear I'm done fooling around forever."

Oddly enough, the more she tried opening her heart to him, the sharper his past pricked her. When the waiters set the dishes down, Qianli Xun picked up a fuzzy meatball, bit off half, and suddenly asked, "Chen Haoyu, would you eat my leftovers?"

"Of course!" Without a second's hesitation, he leaned over and munched down the other half straight from her chopsticks.

A real flutter of date-night sweetness warmed her chest. "I might look decent now, yet half the time I can't be bothered washing up or fixing my hair—mind that?"

"Never. Love you however you look."

"I burp, grind my teeth, pass gas, and wake up with crust in my eyes—still don't mind?"

"Not a bit—I do all that too! You're so spotless I worry I'm out of my league; flaws make us a matched pair."

"And if your friends insist you bring me to a banquet, would you fuss over how I dress?"

"You're always sharp at banquets—why worry? Even plain-faced without a hairpin you'd outshine every woman in the room."

A bright laugh broke out of her. "Fine—you pass! Eat up before it gets cold."

Grinning, Haoyu loaded West Lake vinegar fish and side dishes into her bowl before digging in himself.
