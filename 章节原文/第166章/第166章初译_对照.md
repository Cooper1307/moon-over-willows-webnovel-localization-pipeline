第166章 炙肉男轻薄红豆
## Chapter 166: The Skewer-Roasting Rake Harasses Hongdou

千里寻与乔梦容正要拐向别处，青瓜用盘子端着几串炙肉兴冲冲跑过来：“二位等等！”
两人只好站住。
青瓜上前先对郡主行礼：“佳和郡主，朱家姑娘，我家公子与朋友在那边烤肉串，瞧见你们过来，让我端几串让你们品尝。”
两人向青瓜说的方向瞧去，并未看到任何人影。
千里寻问：“在哪里？怎么看不到人影？”
青瓜笑道：“那边地势低，走到跟前才能看到。”
“哦，原来如此。”玩了许久，千里寻也觉饥饿，将肉串拿过，递给梦容一些，“陈浩宇请客，我们不吃白不吃。”
青瓜嘱咐道：“两位吃完，把竹签放到盘子里，我还要端过去。”
青瓜说完一直盯着两位姑娘。
被他人盯着，两人不好下嘴。
千里寻故意问：“你那双眼睛直勾勾地盯着肉串，难不成不想给我和郡主吃？”
“怎么会？我这就转过身，二位放心吃。”
青瓜转过身后，两位姑娘开吃起来，边吃边夸赞肉串烤得不错。
Just as Qianli Xun and Qiao Mengrong turned to head the other way, Qinggua came bounding up with a platter of sizzling meat skewers. "Wait up, ladies!"

青瓜趁机夸道：“我家公子可会烤肉串，以后朱家姑娘嫁到陈家等着享福吧。”
Forced to halt, they watched Qinggua bow to the princess first. "Princess Jiahe, Miss Zhu—my young master and his friends are roasting skewers over there. Seeing you walk by, he sent me over with a few for you to taste."

千里寻并不会将青瓜的话当回事，咬一口肉串，边嚼边说：“陈浩宇是什么样的人，我见识过，你没必要在我面前替他说好话，说再多都没用。”
Both girls peered in the direction he gestured, yet saw nobody at all. "Where?" Qianli Xun asked. "There is not a soul in sight."

青瓜替自家公子解释：“我家公子是不着调，那是因为没人能管住他，我听公子说姑娘很厉害，他好像很怕您，等您嫁过来，有人管他，我家公子肯定会改掉以前的臭毛病。”
"The ground dips down over there," Qinggua grinned. "You only spot them once you walk right up to the rim."

“你都说陈浩宇不着调，我可不愿嫁给一个不着调的人，再说，我有什么义务替陈家管教他？”
"Aha." Having been active for hours, Qianli Xun felt her stomach rumble. She took the skewers and passed half to Mengrong. "Chen Haoyu is treating—waste not, want not."

青瓜之所以说那些话，只是没话找话而已，朱家姑娘既然如此说，他不再多言，再说公子还嫌弃朱家姑娘是母老虎。青瓜觉得有趣，这俩人都不怎么样，还互相瞧不上。
"When you finish, please set the bamboo skewers back on the platter so I can carry them back," Qinggua reminded them—then stayed planted right there, staring straight at the two girls.

吃完肉串，千里寻接过梦容手中的竹签，递给青瓜：“转过来吧，竹签拿去，今日陈浩宇算是做了一件好事，替我和郡主感谢他。”
Under his unblinking gaze, neither felt comfortable taking a bite.

青瓜接过竹签：“那是自然，二位忙，我要去向公子回话。”
"Why are your eyes glued to these skewers?" Qianli Xun asked on purpose. "Do you grudge Her Highness and me a taste?"

千里寻继续与梦容踏青，她们随意走在宽敞的草地上，春风时不时拂面而来，温馨而舒适，两个姑娘各自体会着各自的心思。
"Never! I will turn right around—eat in peace, ladies."

乔梦容依然纠结哥哥刚才的表现，她又朝几位公子处望去，哥哥坐在那里，没有离开的意思。
Once Qinggua spun around, the two dug in, praising how well-seasoned the roast meat was.

“淑真，不知为何，我总觉得哥哥心里装着另一个人。”
Seizing the opening, Qinggua boasted over his shoulder, "My young master is a wizard at roasting skewers! Once Miss Zhu marries into the Chen family, you are in for the good life."

女子向来心思细腻，能被梦容猜到实属必然，况且申简辰总是时不时流露出来，梦容不怀疑那才不正常。
Taking a bite and chewing, Qianli Xun brushed it right off. "I have seen firsthand what sort of man Chen Haoyu is. Save your breath—no amount of sweet talk on his behalf will move me."

千里寻努力帮仪王掩饰：“应该不会，你别瞎想。”
"My master acts wild only because nobody has ever kept him in line," Qinggua argued. "I hear him say Miss is fierce and he seems scared stiff of you. Once you marry in and rein him in, he will drop all his bad habits for sure."

“我没有瞎想，哥哥刚刚起死回生时我就猜到。我陪他去街市，遇到卦摊，他便上前占卜，还说是寻人，当时我猜他想寻的一定是个女子。”
"Even you admit he is hopeless. Why on earth would I marry a hopeless rake? And since when is it my duty to raise the Chen family's son for them?"

原来申简辰刚刚穿越而来就想着找她，千里寻内心生起异样的情愫，可乔梦容伤感的表情令她无法再继续体会下去。
Qinggua had only been making conversation; hearing her sharp retort, he shut his mouth—especially since his master called Miss Zhu a tigress behind her back anyway. Funny pair, Qinggua mused: neither one was much of a prize, yet both looked down their noses at the other.

“你肯定是想多了。”
Finishing her meat, Qianli Xun collected Mengrong's empty sticks and held them out. "Turn around and take your skewers. Chen Haoyu did one decent thing today—pass on our thanks from Her Highness and me."

“后来我才知道哥哥要找的人是千里寻，那位写话本的才子，我才放心。可我着实想不通，哥哥为找那人先是占卜，来到临安又派白风守在西三胡同，既然知道话本才子就在临安，为何哥哥后来又不找了？”
"Will do! Enjoy your walk; I must report back to my master."

虽然千里寻已知申简辰为寻她颇费一番心思，可被梦容以怀疑的口吻叙述一番，她竟然感动起来，忘记接梦容的话。
Qianli Xun and Mengrong continued their stroll across the wide meadow, a soft spring breeze brushing their cheeks, each lost in her own thoughts.

“淑真，你怎么了？”
Still gnawed by her brother's strange behavior, Qiao Mengrong glanced back toward the young gentlemen; her brother sat right where he was, showing no sign of coming over. "Shuzhen, I do not know why, yet I keep feeling there is another person in Brother's heart."

梦容的声音让千里寻变得更加清醒：“哦，没什么。我觉得想不通就不要想，反正你与仪王有官家赐婚，他必然会娶你。”
A woman's intuition was sharp, and with Shen Jianchen letting his feelings leak out every other turn, it would have been a miracle if Mengrong had not noticed.

“话虽如此，我还是很担心，怕哥哥根本不想娶我。”
Qianli Xun scrambled to cover for him. "No way—do not go inventing worries."

这个话题太过麻烦，千里寻不愿再聊，她怕再聊下去，会愈加愧疚：“梦容，你看，大好春光，你我应该忘记所有不快，好好享受，相信明天一切会好！”
"I am not inventing anything. I suspected it the moment Brother came back from death's door. I went to the market with him, and the instant he saw a fortune-teller's stall, he stopped to ask for a divination about finding someone. I guessed right then he was looking for a woman."

乔梦容露出笑容：“淑真，你真好，总能给我带来积极健康的情绪。”
So Shen Jianchen had started searching for her the very instant he transmigrated. A warm, tangled flutter stirred in Qianli Xun's chest, yet one look at Mengrong's downcast face snuffed it out. "You are overthinking this."

被梦容夸奖，千里寻内心无法真正高兴起来，如果梦容知道仪王心里的人是她，肯定不会再觉得她好。
"Later I learned he was searching for Qianli Xun, that famed huaben writer, and I breathed easier. Yet I still cannot fathom why—after casting lots in Bianliang and posting Bai Feng outside West Third Alley in Lin'an, knowing the writer was right here in the city—Brother gave up searching out of the blue."

“梦容，我永远不会做对不起你的事。”
Even though Qianli Xun already knew how hard Shen Jianchen had hunted for her, hearing the story told through Mengrong's uneasy questions moved her so much she forgot to reply.

“怎么突然说这个？”乔梦容露出疑惑的表情。
"Shuzhen? What is wrong?"

千里寻掩饰道：“谁让你说让我感动的话。”
Mengrong's voice jolted her awake. "Oh, nothing. I just think you should stop racking your brain over puzzles with no answers. You and Prince Yi have an Imperial Marriage Decree; he is bound to marry you."

乔梦容露出朋友之间最真诚的笑容，拉起淑真，两人用心享受着春日的阳光……
"Even so, I keep worrying Brother does not want to marry me at all."

这片草地大部分比较平坦，只有一处洼地，倒成为陈浩宇的好去处。他提前让人将炉子放入，炭火烧好，再将肉串穿好。捶丸结束后，与约好的两位公子前来炙肉。
This topic was walking on thin ice, and pressing further would only pile more guilt on her head. "Look at this glorious spring day, Mengrong! Let us toss every gloomy thought aside and enjoy ourselves. Tomorrow will turn out bright!"

青瓜一手握着竹签，一手端着空盘子，下到低洼处。
A smile broke across Qiao Mengrong's face. "You are wonderful, Shuzhen. You always bring such bright, uplifting cheer."

“公子，朱家姑娘与郡主把肉串全都吃完，直夸味道好，还让我代她们感谢您。”
The compliment sat heavy on Qianli Xun's conscience. If Mengrong ever learned who occupied Prince Yi's heart, she would never call her wonderful again. "Mengrong, I will never do anything to betray you."

陈浩宇坐在毛毡上，一边吃肉串，一边说：“我是看在郡主的面子上才让朱淑真品尝，否则她哪有机会吃到我亲自烤的肉。”
"Why say that out of the blue?" Mengrong asked, puzzled.

青瓜放下盘子和竹签，站在一旁赔着笑：“公子，我在朱家姑娘面前夸您，说您最会炙肉……”
"Blame yourself for saying something so touching," Qianli Xun deflected.

“滚！本公子这么完美，需要你夸！”陈浩宇一脸不屑。
Smiling with pure, open trust, Qiao Mengrong took her hand, and the two soaked in the spring warmth side by side.

青瓜并未离开，他知道公子只是习惯用“滚”骂他而已，他唯一能做的就是闭嘴。
Most of the meadow lay flat, save for one sunken hollow that served Chen Haoyu's purposes well. He had servants haul in a brazier, kindle the charcoal, and thread the skewers ahead of time; once the chuiwan match wrapped up, he met two cronies there to roast meat.

坐在陈浩宇对面的一位公子说：“陈兄一直奉行放荡不羁，还说不碰到喜欢的人绝不成亲，结果怎样？还不是被订婚？”
Empty platter in one hand and bamboo sticks in the other, Qinggua hopped down into the hollow. "Young Master, Miss Zhu and Her Highness ate every single skewer! They raved about the flavor and sent their thanks."

“订婚又能把我怎样？哪怕真的娶了朱淑真，我该怎样还怎样，谁都改变不了本公子。”陈浩宇为自己满上一杯酒，端起来，“喝！人生得意须尽欢，莫使金樽空对月。”
Sprawled on a felt rug munching a skewer, Chen Haoyu scoffed. "I only shared on Princess Jiahe's account. Otherwise Zhu Shuzhen would never get a taste of my handiwork."

对面的公子端起酒杯，还未饮，先用戏谑的口吻夸赞：“呵！陈兄竟然开始吟诗，难道是因为要娶女词人，为与其相匹配，趁机恶补？”
Setting the platter down, Qinggua grinned and fawned. "I talked you up to Miss Zhu too, told her nobody roasts meat better than—"

陈浩宇饮完酒，怼道：“能不能好好说话？本公子吟这两句，三岁孩童都知道，你敢说你不知？”
"Scram! Does perfection like me need your bragging?" Chen Haoyu sneered.

“我错了，自罚一杯。”对面的公子也将酒饮下。
Qinggua stayed put, knowing "scram" was just his master's verbal tic; all he needed to do was zip his lip.

正在炙肉的公子翻了一遍肉串，撒了一遍香料，才开始说话：“朱家姑娘也是个美人儿，每次见到，我都神魂荡漾，只可惜敢看不敢上手，陈兄有福气，令人羡慕不已，洞房花烛夜之后，可得跟我们说说她的好……”
A young rake seated opposite Chen Haoyu chuckled. "Brother Chen always swore he would stay footloose and fancy-free, never marrying unless he met true love—and look at you now, betrothed all the same!"

陈浩宇骂道：“滚！狗嘴里吐不出象牙！”
"What can a betrothal do to me? Even if I end up marrying Zhu Shuzhen, I will live just as I please. Nobody changes this young master!" Chen Haoyu filled his cup and raised it high. "Drink up! 'When one has one's way in life, one must enjoy it to the full. The golden wine cup must not face the moon unfilled.'" [TN: Translated by Chan Sin-wai]

“陈兄都开始护媳妇儿，少见少见。”
Cup raised, the friend mocked him before taking a sip. "Whoa! Brother Chen reciting poetry? Cramming in advance so you can keep up with your poetess bride?"

“护个屁！你说那些话，被她听去，小心你的狗命！”
Draining his cup, Chen Haoyu shot back, "Can you speak like a normal person? Every three-year-old knows those two lines—dare tell me you do not?"

炙肉公子抬头向上望望：“都已走远，听不见。没想到陈公子这么怕朱淑真，只怕婚后得了怕老婆的美名，呵呵……！”
"My bad! Penalty cup." The friend downed his wine too.

“笑你个头！本公子根本不会娶她。”
The other crony tending the grill flipped the skewers, shook spice over them, and leered. "Miss Zhu is a real looker, though. Sets my heart fluttering every time I see her—shame I only dare look and not touch. Brother Chen is a lucky dog! After the wedding night, you have to tell us all about how she—"

“担心得了怕老婆的美名？”
"Get lost!" Chen Haoyu barked. "Nothing decent ever comes out of a dog's mouth!"

“去你的！本公子要娶，也得娶个黄花大姑娘，她都结过一次婚，根本配不上我，要不是因为他爹是知府大人，我爹娘也不会同意。”
"Look at Brother Chen sticking up for his bride! Now that is a rare sight."

炙肉公子继续翻着肉串，用慨叹的语气玩笑道：“我可不嫌弃，为何知府大人不来找我？”
"Stick up for her, my foot! If she caught wind of what you just said, you could kiss your worthless hide goodbye!"

陈浩宇懒得再理会，拿起一串肉，大口嚼起来。
The griller glanced up toward the rim of the hollow. "They are long gone—cannot hear a thing. Who knew Young Master Chen was scared stiff of Zhu Shuzhen? Come your wedding, you will earn the grand title of henpecked husband! Hahaha!"

对面的公子嚼完一串肉，凑近陈浩宇：“其实你不必担心，我听说林觉虽娶了朱淑真，可并未得手，要不他怎么老往春香楼跑，还把春金巧往林府带，嘿嘿……！”
"Laugh all you want—I am not marrying her in the first place!"

陈浩宇继续解释：“所以说朱淑真更娶不得，她就是母老虎，林觉都无法得手，我娶了恐怕也难。”
"Scared of earning that henpecked title?"

“她爹可是临安知府，估计你这婚不好退。”
"Drop dead! When I marry, it will be a fresh young maiden. She has been married once already—she is nowhere near my league. If her father were not the Prefect, my parents would never have agreed."

“我都想好了，实在退不了，就把她娶回去，大家井水不犯河水，各过各的。”
Flipping the meat, the griller sighed in mock envy. "I would not turn her down. Why did the Prefect not come knocking at my door?"

“原来陈兄什么都想好了。”
Ignoring him, Chen Haoyu grabbed another skewer and bit in.

“人无远虑必有近忧，所以得考虑长远些。”
Chewing through his own skewer, the first crony leaned close. "You need not worry anyway. Word has it Lin Jue married Zhu Shuzhen yet never even laid a finger on her—why else did he haunt Chunxiang Brothel and drag Chun Jinqiao straight into Lin Manor? Heh!"

“少见，陈兄不是提倡及时行乐，最讨厌远虑那一套？怎么跟以前不一样？”
"Which proves all the more she is poison," Chen Haoyu shot back. "She is a tigress! Even Lin Jue could not tame her, so what chance do I have?"

“没办法，谁让这婚约耽误本公子及时行乐的宗旨，所以不得不远虑一下。”
"Her father is the Prefect of Lin'an. Breaking off that match will not be easy."

“嘿嘿，原来如此。”
"I have it all mapped out. If I truly cannot break it off, I will marry her home and we will keep out of each other's hair—each living our own life."

炙肉公子感到尿急：“青瓜，你来炙肉，我去方便一下。”
"Look at Brother Chen, planning every move ahead."

“遵命！”青瓜换了那位公子，认真炙起肉来。
"If a man takes no thought for the distant future, sorrow waits close at hand. Got to look ahead."

炙肉公子走出洼地，四下瞧瞧，都是放风筝的人，在此处方便不太合适。
"Miracles never cease! Since when did the champion of living for the moment start preaching foresight?"

他又向别处瞧瞧，看到一个小土坡，步行过去，躲到后面，方便起来……
"No choice—this betrothal threatens my whole creed of living for the moment, so I had to spare it a thought."

一泡尿过后，全身无比舒适，整理好衣裳，往回走。
"Heh, fair enough."

不再尿急，心情变得轻松，又朝放风筝的人瞧去，一个姑娘的身影映入他的眼帘，红扑扑的脸蛋甚是可爱，再配上一张笑脸，实在令人心动。再看那穿着，应该是哪家的丫鬟。
Feeling nature call, the grilling crony stood up. "Take over the skewers, Qinggua. I need to relieve myself."

炙肉公子心绪摇荡，心想，既是丫鬟，他要是得手，应该没人管，说不定主人家还会把她许给自己。
"Right away!" Qinggua stepped in and tended the meat.

于是大胆靠近那丫鬟……
Climbing out of the hollow, the young rake scanned the meadow. Kite-flyers dotted the grass, hardly a private spot. Spotting a low earthen mound nearby, he strolled behind it and took care of business.

丫鬟正是红豆，小姐与郡主散步，她一个人放风筝，越放越熟练，越熟练越开心，只顾体会风筝带来的快乐，根本没发现有人在偷看她。
Relieved and cheerful, he adjusted his robe and headed back. Glancing toward the kite-flyers, his gaze snagged on a young girl—rosy cheeks, bright grin, utterly charming. Judging from her dress, she was some household's maid. His heart leaped. Just a maid! If he got his hands on her, who would fuss? Her master might even gift her to him.

炙肉公子走近后，问道：“喂！你是谁家的丫鬟？”
Bold as brass, he sauntered over.

红豆瞧了一眼，对方色眯眯的样子，实在讨厌，她赶紧躲开。
It was Hongdou. While her mistress walked with the princess, she had flown the kite alone, growing handier and happier by the minute, wrapped up in the fun without noticing the lecher creeping up.

炙肉公子靠过去：“放风筝多没意思，跟哥哥去炙肉，吃完炙肉再去哥哥府上，只要让哥哥开心，哥哥让你有享不完福。”
"Hey there!" he called out as he closed in. "Which household do you serve?"

红豆又朝一边躲开。
One look at his greasy leer disgusted Hongdou, and she sidestepped away.

炙肉公子继续跟上：“哥哥一眼瞧上你，来，让哥哥香一个。”
He pressed closer. "What fun is flying a kite? Come join Big Brother for roast meat, then come back to my estate. Keep Big Brother happy and you will live in clover."

炙肉公子噘起嘴向红豆脸上亲去……
Hongdou sidestepped again.

红豆忍无可忍，无心再放风筝，小姐教的拳法不能白白浪费，一拳过去，打在炙肉公子的脸上，再一脚将炙肉公子踢得向后跌倒在地。
He trailed right after her. "Big Brother fell for you at first sight! Come here and give me a kiss!" Puckering his lips, he lunged for her cheek.

此时，飘在空中的风筝才乖乖落在草地上。
Pushed past her limit, Hongdou forgot the kite. Why waste the fist techniques Miss had drilled into her? She swung a punch straight into his face, then planted a kick that sent him sprawling backward onto the grass. Only then did the forgotten kite flutter down onto the turf.

“哎吆！哎吆……！”炙肉公子捂着已乌青的半张脸直喊疼，努力爬起来，大骂道，“你个臭丫头！本公子瞧得上你，是你的福气，竟然敢打本公子！快说！你是谁家丫鬟？让你的主人好好修理你！”
"Ow! Owww!" Clutching half a face already swelling purple, the rake scrambled up and cursed. "You little wretch! Having this young master take a shine to you is your blessing, and you dare hit me?! Speak up! Whose maid are you? I will have your master flay you alive!"

草地太大，很多人并未注意到这里发生的事，就连千里寻也只顾与郡主边走边聊，自己的丫鬟发生了什么，她并不知道。
The meadow was vast, so few noticed the scuffle; engrossed in talking with Mengrong, Qianli Xun had no idea what had happened to her maid.

因低洼处离得近，倒惊动陈浩宇。他爬上来，仔细瞧那丫鬟，觉得脸熟，猛然想起正是朱淑真身边新带的丫鬟，赶紧上前劝：“去去去！快去放你的风筝！一个丫鬟拳头也这么厉害，真是有其主必有其仆。”
Down in the nearby hollow, however, Chen Haoyu heard the commotion and scrambled up the slope. One look at the maid rang a bell—Zhu Shuzhen's new girl! He rushed over and waved her off. "Go on, shoo! Back to your kite! Even her maid packs a wallop—like mistress, like servant!"

红豆捡起风筝，瞪了一眼炙肉公子：“以后再调 戏我，打得你脑袋开花！”
Retrieving her kite, Hongdou glared daggers at the rake. "Try getting fresh with me again and I will crack your skull open!"

“陈兄，你认识那丫头？”
"You know that brat, Brother Chen?"

陈浩宇瞧着红豆的背影：“你调 戏谁不成，偏偏调 戏朱淑真的丫鬟，吃亏了吧？”
Watching Hongdou's departing back, Chen Haoyu shook his head. "Out of everyone to grope, you pick Zhu Shuzhen's maid. Got what was coming to you, huh?"

“不行，我要找朱淑真算账！”
"No way—I am going to settle accounts with Zhu Shuzhen!"

“算你个头！只怕你去找她，再被她揍一顿！”
"Settle accounts, my foot! Go find her and you will only catch a second beating!"

“啊！陈兄，你真可怜，还要娶她，我今日算是明白你为何想方设法退婚。”
"Ah! Poor Brother Chen, stuck marrying her. Now I see why you are desperate to break it off."

“理解我就好，这女子还需温柔，朱淑真一点儿都不温柔。刚才那丫头若温柔些，估计你已得手，哪会受这苦？”
"Glad you get it. A woman ought to be gentle, and Zhu Shuzhen does not have a gentle bone in her body. If that maid had been the gentle sort, you would have had your way instead of eating dirt."

“难道我就这么吃哑巴亏？”
"So I just swallow this loss in silence?"

“不吃亏，就得再吃拳头，你好好想想。”
"Swallow the loss, or swallow another fist. Take your pick."

炙肉公子故作认真地想想：“算了，我还是吃亏吧。”
The rake pretended to weigh his options. "Forget it. I will swallow the loss."

“这就对了，走，我们继续炙肉。”
"Smart move. Come on, back to our skewers."

陈浩宇与炙肉公子回到低洼处，无非再解释一番脸上乌青的来历，再被嘲笑一番，再感慨一番朱家姑娘娶不得之类的话。
Descending back into the hollow, they spent the next stretch explaining the bruised cheek, enduring fresh rounds of mockery, and sighing all over again that no man in his right mind should marry Miss Zhu.
