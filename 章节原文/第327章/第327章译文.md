## Chapter 327: Sentenced to Beheading in Ten Days

Haoyu rushed straight into her arms weeping, chains clanking as she hugged him back—forcing Shen Jianchen to turn away in quiet pain while listening to Haoyu assure her the whole Chen family stood proud behind her.

Ordering the hesitant jailer to unlock her heavy iron shackles on his own authority, Shen Jianchen asked softly if she knew the deeper reason behind Liang Shicheng's summon—and when Qianli Xun quietly asked *"How is Mengrong?"*, he answered simply that her wrongdoing had cost her her sanity. Watching the silent, private code passing between them baffled and stung Haoyu all the way home, though Shen Jianchen flatly refused to spill Mengrong's shame.

Returning to the manor just as the imperial physician confirmed Mengrong's illness was likely hereditary and utterly incurable, Shen Jianchen coldly ordered Matron Zou to stop all treatment—shocking sympathetic Bai Feng (who didn't know Mengrong's crimes, sparking a brief spat with loyal Kuqiao).

Eleven agonizing days dragged by before Shen Jianchen returned to the palace—only to get hit with a devastating thunderbolt from a minor eunuch: Liang Shicheng had returned yesterday, marched straight to the Court of Judicial Review, and secured an instant death sentence—**beheading in ten days**!

Confirmed helpless by Chief Minister Chen—only His Majesty's personal pardon could halt the blade—Shen Jianchen rode home looking like a ghost, dropping the sentence onto Haoyu, Kuqiao, and Qinggua in a burst of sobs.

*"Only one chance left: His Majesty's pardon."* Refusing to let Bai Feng or Haoyu follow, Shen Jianchen mounted up, rode straight into the palace, and dropped onto his knees on the stone courtyard directly outside **Zichen Hall**—vowing silently to kneel until His Majesty relented or he dropped dead on the flagstones.

Inside, Emperor Zhao Ji brushed sleek slender-gold calligraphy while Liang Shicheng purred flattery, casually waving off his thirteenth son kneeling outside: *"Let him kneel till his legs give out!"*
