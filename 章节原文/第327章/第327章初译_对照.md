第327章 十日后要被斩首
## Chapter 327: Sentenced to Behead in Ten Days

陈浩宇顾不上其他，上前抱住娘子：“娘子。”眼泪不由夺眶而出。
千里寻很自然地抱住陈浩宇，手上的铁链跟着叮叮当当地响：“没想到还能见到你，家里人可好？”
二人抱在一起，申简辰心里不舒服，又没有任何理由将他们分开，他转过身不去看他们。只要不看，心里应该会舒服一些，可却清清楚楚听到二人的对话。
“家里人都很好，只是担心你。”
“都怪我，惹下事，搞得家人不得安宁。”
“家里人和我从来没怪过你，相反，因为此事全家人更认为我娶了位好娘子，来汴梁前一再叮嘱我一定要将娘子救出来。”
从陈浩宇的话中，千里寻听出其中的意味。正因为她被梁师成定罪，才向众人证明她的清白。陈家人，包括陈浩宇在内才会高看她。
挨到陈浩宇身上的包袱，千里寻问：“你怎么还背着包袱？难道里面是给我带的东西？牢里什么都用不上，也不让用。”
陈浩宇放开娘子，将背着的包袱取下，放在铺着草席的床上：“不过是吃的和用的，狱卒看在仪王的面子上允许将东西留下。”
陈浩宇本想继续抱住娘子，娘子却拖着铁链，向仪王行礼：“殿下为草民之事费心费力，草民无以为报，只能以礼相谢。”
申简辰转过身：“你我之间何必言谢？今日见过你，总算放心。暂时还没法救你出去，需等梁师成回到汴梁后大理寺才能启动你的案子，到时我会竭尽全力将你救出。暂且在狱中待些时日，我会叮嘱狱卒对你多加照顾。”
仪王的言辞中每一句饱含着个人情感，陈浩宇心中不爽。需依靠仪王救娘子，他不敢得罪贵人，又不能憋着不说话：“仪王若真想让狱卒照顾我娘子，不如先让人把她手脚上的铁链去掉，也好松快些。”
申简辰二话不说向外喊道：“来人！”
开门的狱卒过来：“仪王殿下有何吩咐？”
“把幽栖居士手上和脚上的铁链打开。”
狱卒为难道：“她是重犯，若出意外，小的担不起责任。”
“出了什么事由我担着，你尽管打开。”
狱卒无奈，仪王的话不得不听，上前掏出钥匙，将千里寻手上和脚上的铁链打开，起身说道：“殿下，探望的时间快到，您有什么话需快些说。”
被狱卒催促，申简辰与陈浩宇反而不知该说些什么。
千里寻活动活动手脚，轻松地说：“这下松快许多，今日我终于体会到有位王爷做朋友的好处。”
陈浩宇再次上前抱住娘子：“你真傻，要是早些跟我去乡下躲避，也不至于有今日。”
千里寻轻拍着陈浩宇的背部：“躲起来不过是迟几日蹲大牢，说不定还会连累陈家。现在不挺好？只要家里都好，陈家的生意没有受到影响，我吃点儿苦不算什么。再说都是我惹下的事，无论什么结果都需我承担。”
申简辰问：“难道你知道梁师成要求见你的原因？”
除了梁师成好色，陈浩宇想不出还会有什么原因。仪王的问题显然包含着其他说法，他放开娘子：“梁师成好色成性，除贪恋娘子美色外，还能有什么原因？”
千里寻并未回答陈浩宇，而是向仪王发问：“难道你已知？”
申简辰点点头：“你被送到汴梁后我才得知。”
“梦容可好？”
“她做错事，已尝到恶果，精神变得失常。”
“精神失常？怎么会这样？”
陈浩宇在跟前，申简辰不好将详细经过说明：“我刚说过，她做错事，因错误的行为才导致精神失常。”
千里寻不好再问下去。
显然娘子和仪王都知道其他原因是什么，他们不明说，陈浩宇很是着急。想问时，外面的狱卒再次提醒：“仪王殿下，时间到。”
申简辰看一眼千里寻，目光中充满心疼和不舍，他没有说道别的话，转身走出牢房。
陈浩宇第三次抱住娘子：“还有什么原因？能不能告诉我？”
千里寻不愿说出梦容的事，再次轻拍陈浩宇的背部：“哪有其他原因？仪王走了，你也快去，狱卒等着锁牢房的门。”
陈浩宇恋恋不舍地放开娘子：“你先在狱中待几日，我和仪王定会救你出去。”
狱卒继续催促：“时间已到，还请公子快些出来。”
陈浩宇终于放开娘子的手，走出牢房，目睹牢房的门被锁上，狠狠心，转身离开。
牢狱外，陈浩宇向仪王追问：“刚才殿下与我娘子提到的其他原因是什么？”
申简辰装作不知：“什么原因？你的问题倒把我搞糊涂。”
仪王往马车旁走，陈浩宇紧跟上。
两人在马车里坐定后，陈浩宇仍不死心，继续追问：“殿下装傻充愣，我可不傻，你明明问我娘子是不是知道梁师成要见她的原因，快告诉我，到底还有什么原因？”
申简辰根本不愿说，他要替梦容保留最后一点儿尊严，除此之外，还有其他。
Chen Haoyu brushed everything else aside and swept his wife into an embrace, tears spilling from his eyes. Qianli Xun hugged him back naturally, the iron chains clinking between them.

刚才在牢房里，他与千里寻之间的对话只有他们听得懂，陈浩宇无法领会。申简辰很享受这种感觉，又岂会将实情告知从而破坏只属于他和千里寻之间的感觉？
“I never thought I’d see you here,” she said. “Is everyone at home well?”

“即使有，我也不会告诉你，还是不要再问。”
Standing beside them, Shen Jianchen felt a dull ache in his chest. Having no right to part them, he turned his back so he wouldn’t have to look, though every word reached his ears clearly.

人家是王爷，救娘子还要仰仗他，陈浩宇不好再追问下去。想到刚才在牢房里，娘子与仪王之间仿佛只有他们二人的那段对话，心里老大不舒服。
“Everyone is fine, just sick with worry over you.”

回到仪王府时，白风正送宫中的太医往外走。
“It’s my fault for stirring up trouble and costing the family their peace.”

太医见到仪王，也是恭敬行礼。
“Nobody blames you—not me, not anyone at home. Quite the opposite. Because of this, everyone respects you even more as a worthy wife. Before I left for Bianliang, they told me over and over to bring you back safe.”

仪王并未向太医问起仪王妃的病情，陈浩宇心里有不好的预感，恐怕仪王又会将目标盯向自己的娘子。
Qianli Xun caught the deeper meaning behind his words. By standing up to Liang Shicheng and taking his false charge, she had proved her innocence to the Chen household beyond doubt. Feeling the bundles against his shoulders, she asked, “Why carry packs in here? You can’t use anything inside a prison.”

倒是白风，送走太医后，快步跟上仪王，主动汇报诊断的结果。
Haoyu let go, slipped the bundles off, and laid them on the straw mat. “Just food and daily things. Out of respect for Prince Yi, the jailer let them stay.”

“王爷，宫中的太医医术的确好，诊治完，将仪王妃得病的原因和病症说得一字不差。唉，只可惜太医也治不好，还说仪王妃的病容易遗传，恐怕是王妃的家人有此病史，才过继到王妃身上。若仪王妃生下孩子，多半也会染上此病，有朝一日受到外因诱发同样会犯病。”
He reached to hug her again, only for Qianli Xun to step back, chains dragging, and drop into a formal bow before Shen Jianchen. “Your Highness has spent great care and effort on this humble subject’s behalf. I have no way to repay you save this bow of gratitude.”

陈浩宇在一旁听了，很想笑白风，两句话的事，却说那么多。见仪王面色凝重，他不敢露出笑意，否则会被认为是幸灾乐祸。
Shen Jianchen turned around. “Why speak of thanks between us? Seeing you with my own eyes sets my mind at ease. I cannot get you out just yet—the Court of Judicial Review won’t open your case until Liang Shicheng returns to Bianliang. Once he arrives, I will do everything in my power to free you. Bear with it a few days more. I will see that the jailers look after you.”

正好来到路口处，陈浩宇打算回房歇息，行礼道：“殿下，我回房了。”
Every word carried personal devotion, souring Haoyu’s mood. Needing the prince’s help yet unable to swallow his pride completely, he cut in. “If Your Highness wants the jailers to care for my wife, start by taking off her wrist and ankle chains so she can breathe easier.”

申简辰点头回礼后，朝另一边拐去，边走心中边想着赵朴与梦容的事。
Without hesitation, Shen Jianchen called out for the guard. When the jailer stepped up, the prince ordered him to unlock Youqi Jushi’s shackles.

梦容是赵朴在路边捡回来的妹妹，根本没见过她家里的人，无法得知梦容的家人有没有同样的病史。太医既如此说，搞不好梦容的家人真的有此类病史。
“She is a capital prisoner, Your Highness,” the jailer hedged. “If something happens, this lowly servant cannot bear the blame.”

他想起在柳岸别馆的屋顶偷看梁师成用针扎春金巧的场景……
“If anything happens, I will bear it. Unlock them.”

同样的经历，春金巧无事，梦容却出事。她不像商妓会应付男人，自然会害怕，便诱发出遗传的疾病，加上痛失孩子，又知道自己不能再有身孕，病情自然加重。
Powerless to refuse a prince, the jailer took out his keys, freed her wrists and ankles, and stood up. “Your time is almost up, Your Highness. Say what you must quickly.”

申简辰难以想象当时梁师成用针在梦容的身上扎下时她痛苦的样子，他也不愿去想，冷冷地说：“转告邹嬷嬷，既然治不好，便不要再治。”
Hurried by the guard, both men fell strangely tongue-tied. Qianli Xun rolled her shoulders and flexed her ankles. “Much better! Today I finally see the perks of having a prince for a friend.”

王爷变得如此冷漠，白风一时难以接受，愣愣地站在原地，忘记进屋伺候王爷。
Haoyu pulled her back into his arms. “You fool. If you had fled to the countryside with me earlier, none of this would have happened.”

苦荞正好过来：“白风，你愣着做什么？”
She patted his back gently. “Hiding would only have delayed my arrest and dragged the Chen family down too. Isn’t this better? As long as everyone at home is safe and the family business untouched, a little hardship means nothing. I brought this on myself, and I’ll answer for it.”

白风才回过神来，把苦荞拉到一边：“自从仪王妃病了，我总感觉王爷开始嫌弃她，仪王妃真可怜。”
“Wait—do you know why Liang Shicheng demanded to see you?” Shen Jianchen asked.

小姐不让将仪王妃害她的事告诉任何人，苦荞从未向白风提起过。白风可怜仪王妃，苦荞很想向他说明，话到嘴边，想起小姐的话，不敢说出口，只好表现出不满：“是可怜，可我家小姐更可怜。”
To Haoyu, the answer seemed obvious—Liang’s lechery. Hearing an unspoken layer in the prince’s question, he let go of his wife. “Liang Shicheng is a shameless lecher after her looks. What other reason could there be?”

“幽栖居士再怎么着也是正常人，王爷和陈公子正想尽办法营救。可仪王妃的病已治不好，王爷也不再去看望她，两相对比，还是仪王妃可怜。”
Instead of answering Haoyu, Qianli Xun looked at Shen Jianchen. “You already found out?”

“可怜之人必有可恨之处。”
He nodded. “Only after they sent you off to Bianliang.”

“苦荞，你怎能这么说？”
“How is Mengrong?”

白风不知实情，一再维护仪王妃，苦荞很虽生气也能理解：“我才懒得管仪王妃的事，今日仪王去狱中见我家小姐，我想进去问问小姐的境况。”
“She did wrong and reaped the bitter fruit. She has lost her mind.”

苦荞丢下白风，朝仪王的屋子走去。
“Lost her mind? How did that happen?”

白风追上去拦住她：“王爷心情不好，不要去打扰，想了解情况可以去问陈公子。”
With Haoyu standing right there, Shen Jianchen kept his answer guarded. “As I said, she acted wrongly, and the consequences broke her mind.”

苦荞拍拍自己的脑袋：“看我这脑子，一心想着你，光想到问仪王，竟忘记我家姑爷也去了，这就去问他。”
Taking the hint, Qianli Xun asked no more.

苦荞迅速跑开，白风才进屋陪王爷。
Watching the two of them trade coded words he couldn’t follow left Haoyu frantic. Before he could press for answers, the jailer called time. Shen Jianchen rested one long look on Qianli Xun, full of pain and reluctance, then walked out in silence.

……
Haoyu hugged her a third time. “What other reason? Tell me.”

七八日的时间本不算长，可对申简辰和陈浩宇来讲度日如年。
“What other reason could there be?” she replied, patting his back. “His Highness already left. Hurry out so the jailer can lock up.”

好不容易过了八日，申简辰到宫中打探梁内侍的消息，结果说他还需三日才能回到汴梁。
Haoyu pulled away at last, promising that he and Prince Yi would free her, and stepped out as the cell door clanged shut.

然后又是度日如年地等待。
Outside the prison, Haoyu chased after Shen Jianchen. “What was that other reason you and my wife spoke of?”

三日后，申简辰又去宫中，既没见到官家也没见到梁内侍，只有一位小内侍前来传话。
“What reason? You’re baffling me.”

“仪王殿下，内侍大人让我转告您，女词人谋杀朝廷官员罪大恶极，大理寺已将此案审理，十日后斩首。还请殿下以后不要再来宫中为此事打扰官家和内侍大人。”
Haoyu followed him right into the carriage and pressed harder. “Don’t play dumb with me, Your Highness. I’m no fool. You asked straight out whether she knew why Liang Shicheng summoned her. Tell me what else happened!”

听到这样的结果，申简辰差点晕倒。他踉跄着走出宫外，骑上马，往大理寺而去。
Shen Jianchen refused to speak, both to guard the last shred of Mengrong’s dignity and to keep that secret thread shared solely with Qianli Xun intact. “Even if there were one, I wouldn’t tell you. Stop asking.”

来到大理寺，大理寺卿陈大人恭敬地接待了仪王。
Dependent on the prince to save his wife, Haoyu swallowed his frustration, though that private exchange inside the cell left a bitter taste in his mouth.

还未等仪王说明来由，陈大人主动说：“殿下肯定是为女词人的事而来，昨日梁内侍回到京城，先来到大理寺，要求将女词人处死，下官不敢不从。再说她谋杀的人可是内侍大人，官家也因此勃然大怒，她必须死。”
Back at Prince Yi’s Manor, Bai Feng was just seeing an imperial physician out the gate. The doctor bowed respectfully to the prince, who walked straight past without asking a word about his wife—sending a chill down Haoyu’s spine.

申简辰责怪自己为何要晚进宫一日？他依然不愿放弃，请教道：“她不能死，还请陈大人帮本王出个主意。”
Once the doctor left, Bai Feng hurried after Shen Jianchen to report. “The palace physicians truly know their craft, Your Highness! He named the exact cause and symptoms of the Princess Consort’s illness down to the word. Sadly, he can’t cure her either. He said this sickness tends to run in bloodlines—someone in her family likely passed it down to her, and any child she bore might inherit it and break down under outside strain too.”

陈大人叹口气：“唉，除了官家开口饶她不死，没有其他更好的办法。”
Listening nearby, Haoyu almost laughed at how Bai Feng turned two sentences into a speech, though he kept a straight face lest Prince Yi think him gloating. At the path fork, Haoyu bowed and headed back to his room.

申简辰谢过陈大人后，离开大理寺，一路上都是一副垂头丧气的模样。
Shen Jianchen turned down the opposite path, brooding over Zhao Pu’s sister. Zhao Pu had picked Mengrong up off the roadside as a child; nobody knew her birth family or their ailments. If the imperial physician spoke the truth, perhaps her bloodline carried that flaw after all.

白风骑马跟着王爷，他也很难过，担心苦荞知道结果会哭得死去活来。
He pictured Liang Shicheng driving needles into Chun Jinqiao at Liu’an Villa. Both women endured the same torture, yet Jinqiao shrugged it off while Mengrong—unversed in handling men—shattered from terror, waking the dormant illness, followed by losing her unborn child and learning she might never conceive again. Shen Jianchen refused to dwell on her agony during those hours and spoke coldly to Bai Feng. “Tell Matron Zou: since it cannot be cured, stop all treatment.”

回到仪王府，两人刚走到正院的中央，陈浩宇带着青瓜和苦荞迎上来。仪王失落的表情让他产生不好的预感，还是充满希望地问：“殿下，事情办得如何？”
Shocked by the prince’s chill, Bai Feng stood frozen until Kuqiao walked up and asked what was wrong. Pulling her aside, he murmured, “Ever since the Princess Consort fell ill, His Highness acts like he wants nothing to do with her. Poor lady.”

申简辰照实说：“昨日梁师成回到汴梁，已让大理寺定案，十日后要将幽栖居士斩首。”
Bound by her mistress’s order never to tell a soul about Mengrong’s plots, Kuqiao held her tongue, though anger flared in her chest. “Pitiable, yes. My miss is far worse off.”

陈浩宇只觉双腿一软，差点倒下，还好被青瓜扶住。
“At least Youqi Jushi has her wits about her, and His Highness and Young Master Chen are moving heaven and earth to save her. Princess Consort Yi can never be cured, and His Highness won’t even visit her. Who’s more to be pitied?”

“什么？斩首？”陈浩宇硬撑着走上前，抓住仪王，“你不是王爷吗？不是官家的儿子吗？为何连个人都救不了？呜呜……，娘子！”
“Anyone so pitiful must have something hateful in her to bring it on.”

苦荞已泪流满面，伤心地哭起来：“呜呜……，小姐，呜呜……”白风走到苦荞身边，抱住她，让她靠着自己哭个够。
“Kuqiao! How can you say that?”

青瓜也十分难过，问道：“仪王，难道二少夫人真的没救了？”
Though frustrated that Bai Feng didn’t know the truth, Kuqiao understood his ignorance and changed the subject, heading toward the prince’s rooms to ask after her mistress. Bai Feng caught up and told her to ask Chen Haoyu instead, since Prince Yi was in a black mood. Laughing at herself for thinking only of Bai Feng and forgetting her young master had gone too, Kuqiao darted off.

申简辰说：“只有一个办法。”
Seven or eight days crawled past like years. At last Shen Jianchen rode to the palace for word, only to learn Liang Shicheng needed three more days. Another three agonizing days crept by. When Shen Jianchen went back, neither His Majesty nor Liang saw him; a junior attendant came out instead.

众人都用泪眼充满希望地盯着仪王。
“His Majesty’s Attendant bade me inform Your Highness: the poetess’s crime of attempting to murder a court official is heinous. The Court of Judicial Review has tried and closed the case. She will be beheaded in ten days. Do not trouble His Majesty or the Attendant over this matter again.”

陈浩宇追问道：“快说，还有什么办法？”
Nearly collapsing on the spot, Shen Jianchen staggered out to his horse and galloped to the Court of Judicial Review.

申简辰说：“只要官家开口饶她不死，她便可获救。”
Chief Minister Chen received him respectfully and answered before the prince spoke. “Your Highness must be here for the poetess. Imperial Attendant Liang arrived in the capital yesterday and came straight to this court demanding her execution. We dared not disobey. Besides, attempting to murder him infuriated His Majesty. She must die.”

众人听后，又开始哭起来，这哪是什么办法？纯粹是再次告诉大家已无人能救。
Blaming himself for waiting an extra day to check the palace, Shen Jianchen begged the minister for any way out. Chief Minister Chen only sighed: nothing short of His Majesty’s own word could spare her.

“白风，照顾好仪王府和陈公子，不许跟着我。”申简辰命令完，转身向门外走。
Shen Jianchen rode back to the manor crushed, with Bai Feng trailing gloomily behind, dreading how Kuqiao would take the news.

白风上前拦住：“王爷要去哪里？”
In the main courtyard, Haoyu, Qinggua, and Kuqiao rushed up. One look at the prince’s face foretold disaster, yet Haoyu clung to hope. “How did it go, Your Highness?”

申简辰没有回头：“去宫中找官家，请他同意饶幽栖居士不死。”
“Liang Shicheng reached Bianliang yesterday and had the court close the case. Youqi Jushi is set to be beheaded in ten days.”

陈浩宇立刻说：“我跟你一起去！”
Haoyu’s knees buckled; Qinggua barely caught him. Haoyu lurched forward and grabbed Shen Jianchen’s robe. “Beheaded?! Aren’t you an imperial prince? Aren’t you His Majesty’s son? How can you fail to save one person?!”

“你没有资格进宫，去了起不到任何作用，安心待在仪王府，等我的消息。”申简辰再次走出仪王府，骑上马向皇宫而去。
Kuqiao burst into loud sobs, collapsing against Bai Feng’s chest as he held her tight. Pale with grief, Qinggua asked if any hope remained.

到达皇宫后，他直接来到紫宸殿外，不由分说跪在地上。申简辰已决定，他要跪到官家同意为止。
“There is only one way,” Shen Jianchen said. “If His Majesty speaks the word to spare her life, she lives.”

若官家不同意，他便跪死在紫宸殿外。千里寻要被斩首，他活着还有什么意义？
Fresh tears broke out; to them, asking His Majesty for mercy sounded no different from saying nobody could save her.

早已有人将此事报告给梁内侍。
“Bai Feng, look after the manor and Young Master Chen. Do not follow me.” Shen Jianchen turned for the gate, telling Haoyu—who begged to come—that a commoner had no right inside the palace and should wait at the manor.

梁师成到殿外瞧了一眼，心中冷笑道：“现在知道我的厉害，打我那日怎么就没想到你也有下跪的一天？哼！还扬言女词人若有个三长两短，让我死无葬身之地。十日后她就要被斩首，你是王爷，又能奈我何？”
Riding straight into the palace, Shen Jianchen walked to the stone steps outside Zichen Hall and dropped to his knees. He had made his choice: he would kneel without rising until His Majesty relented—or until death took him, since life without Qianli Xun held no meaning anyway.

紫宸殿内，赵佶正站在桌前写字，一旁有宫女伺候。
Word soon reached Liang Shicheng. Stepping outside to gloat over the kneeling prince, Liang sneered to himself: had the prince imagined this day when beating him in Lin’an? Ten days from now the poetess’s head would roll, prince or no prince.

梁师成走进来，禀告道：“官家，仪王殿下正跪在殿外。”
Inside Zichen Hall, Zhao Ji stood at a long table practicing calligraphy while palace maids waited beside him. Liang entered and reported that Prince Yi was kneeling outside.

赵佶继续写字：“为女词人的事？”
“For the female poet?” Zhao Ji asked, never pausing his brush.

梁师成回道：“正是。”
“Yes, Your Majesty.”

“喜欢跪就让他跪着，跪累了自然会回去。”赵佶越发写得轻松。
“If he likes kneeling, let him kneel. Once his knees ache enough, he’ll go home.”

梁师成凑到跟前，赞道：“官家的字写得挺拔秀丽，独具韵味，笔画瘦劲，似铁画银钩，刚硬中尽显细腻与灵动。”
Liang leaned closer in praise. “Your Majesty’s brushstrokes stand tall and slender, keen as iron hooks and silver cords, uniting steel strength with nimble grace!”

赵佶沉浸在溢美之词中，根本没将仪王跪在殿外之事放在心上。
Basking in flattery, Zhao Ji dismissed his kneeling son from his mind.
