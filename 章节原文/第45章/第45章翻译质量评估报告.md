### 2.2 待改进问题

#### 问题1：重复用词
- **"Qiao Mengrong"重复12次**：部分可用代词"she"或"the princess"替代。
- **"brother"重复15次**：乔梦容对话中的"哥哥"翻译为"brother"过于频繁，可部分替换为"you"或直接省略。
- **"prince"重复10次**：可部分替换为"he"或"Shen Jianchen"。

#### 问题2：中英混杂错误
- **"练习拳法"未翻译**：对照版中出现"practicing拳法"，应译为"practicing martial arts"或"practicing Shen Fist"。
- **"shichen"需注释**：半个时辰译为"half a shichen"对英文读者不够友好，可译为"about an hour"或加注。

#### 问题3：句式优化
- **长句需拆分**：
  - 原文："The interior decoration could be described with the word 'luxurious': In the first-floor entrance hall was a large rosewood table, surrounded by various exquisite potted landscapes."
  - 建议拆分为更短的句子，适合移动端阅读。

#### 问题4：口语化程度
- 部分对话略显正式：
  - "It's my great honor to be able to appreciate poetry with Young Master Qiao again" → "It's such an honor to appreciate poetry with you again, Young Master Qiao"
  - "I've missed you to death" 可更口语化为 "I've missed you like crazy"
