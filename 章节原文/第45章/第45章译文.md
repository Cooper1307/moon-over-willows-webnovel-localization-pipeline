# Chapter 45: Talented Scholars and Beautiful Ladies at Drunken Cloud Pavilion

Night fell clear and calm, a sea of stars framing a luminous half-moon. With a real lead on Qianli Xun at last, warm comfort eased Shen Jianchen's chest. Peering out the carriage window, he caught the silhouette of the Divine Willow in the distance, silver moonlight turning its sweeping fronds slender and graceful. Quiet in his mind drifted the line: "The moon rises above the willow tips; our tryst is set past dusk." [TN: Translator's rendering] Romance stirred, only to catch on the bitter worry that the soul stirring it might now wear a male scholar's body. Relief and melancholy tangled together.

As the carriage rolled beneath Youlin's canopy hiding the willow from view, Shen Jianchen pulled his gaze inward and rode silent across from Wenyuan, who tactful as ever rested with shut eyes.

Stepping down at Youlin Retreat, Butler Li hurried out to greet them. "Home at last, Prince Yi and Young Master! Princess Jiahe has been asking after you nonstop!"

Qiao Wenxian grinned teasing. "Speak accurate, Butler Li—Princess Jiahe asks after Prince Yi alone! Poor bachelor me, nothing like Prince Yi having a beauty waiting everywhere he turns!"

Butler Li chuckled. "High time you thought of marriage too, Young Master! Lady Miao seems a fine match—heard you got back from Bianliang and called in person today! Missed you, left saying she'll visit another day."

"Who's Lady Miao? Come clean!" Shen Jianchen teased.

Dodging fast, Qiao Wenxian nudged him. "Better hurry see Princess Jiahe and cure her lovesickness!"

Bantering, they strolled inside the grounds. Butler Li remembered one more message: "Oh, Young Master Dongfang sent word inviting you to Zuiyun Tower tomorrow to appreciate lyrics—new work by Youqi Jushi, begs you not to miss it!"

"Got it. Go tend your work, Butler Li; we know our way from here." Bowing, Butler Li withdrew.

Turning to Prince Yi, Qiao Wenxian invited, "Care to tag along to tomorrow's poetry salon?"

Wait—poetry salon meant scholars! If Qianli Xun had transmigrated into a male scholar, maybe she'd show up there! "Sure, count me in!" Walking together a bit further, they split paths toward their courtyards.

Bai Feng trailed behind Prince Yi toward Princess Jiahe's rear garden, keeping both hands tucked behind his back the whole walk.

Left alone at the retreat all day, Qiao Mengrong had tasted the sharpest longing—and dread—of her life. Out roaming town, had Brother slipped into Goulan Wasi? Word was those theaters teemed with dazzling songstresses, dancers, and musicians; what if he got cozy with them? Around the old Zhao Pu, she had felt rock-solid confidence; around this new healthy brother, insecurity gnawed nonstop. Darkness fallen and still no sign of him—had he wandered into pleasure quarters? Lin'an beauties were renowned across the land; what if tender arms made him forget his little sister?

Unable to bear another second, she stepped out to send Butler Li searching town, and froze right past her doorway seeing Brother standing in her courtyard! Overwhelmed by relief and longing, she threw herself straight into his arms. "Gone all day long, Brother—I worried myself sick! From now on, wherever you go, you have to bring me!"

Coming by just to check in so she wouldn't fret—after all her selfless devotion to Zhao Pu—he hadn't braced for another sudden embrace. Instead of joy, heavier guilt weighed his shoulders; Zhao Pu's love felt like an iron yoke he couldn't shake. Still keeping his arms down, he promised gentle, "Alright. Wherever I go next, you come along. Speaking of which, Wenyuan invited me to a poetry salon tomorrow—come with us."

Like sunshine breaking storm clouds, her face lit up! Letting go with a bright smile, she said, "I'll go with you tomorrow, Brother—won't embarrass you!"

At a total loss what to say next, Shen Jianchen stood stiff and awkward. Right then Bai Feng stepped up before Qiao Mengrong and whipped a paper parcel from behind his back. "Princess! His Highness bought you chestnut cakes while out in town—super tasty, tested 'em myself!"

Taking the parcel, Qiao Mengrong beamed head to toe. "Thank you for thinking of me, Brother! Chestnut cakes from you are guaranteed the sweetest in the whole world!"

Having spent all day chasing Qianli Xun without one spare thought for Mengrong, Shen Jianchen realized Bai Feng had bought them on his own—oh well, chalk it up as doing Zhao Pu's duty! "Getting late, Mengrong. Get some rest."

"Rest well too, Brother."

Watching his departing back, she passed the cakes to Xilan. "Plate some up; I want a few right now!" Taking the package, Xilan ducked indoors. Gazing at the empty spot where he had stood, a wistful shadow returned: hugged him tight again, still not a single arm wrapped back around her... when would he ever pull her into his chest on his own again?

Back in the main courtyard, Shen Jianchen snapped brisk: "Start drilling boxing! One hour before bed!"

Bai Feng groaned. "Aw, Your Highness, just got to Lin'an—can't we take a couple nights off?!"

"No!" Shen Jianchen put on a stern face.

"Not even one night off for buying Princess Jiahe chestnut cakes in your name?!"

"No!"

Stuck under a strict master, Bai Feng sighed and started throwing forms in the yard. Heading inside to rest, Shen Jianchen dozed off, waking almost two hours later to steady thuds and whooshes still echoing outside. Getting into the groove, the kid had blown straight past bedtime!

Stepping into the yard, he called, "Still going? Wrap it up and hit the sack!"

Halting mid-stance, Bai Feng blinked. "Hey, Your Highness, how come the longer I drill, the lighter I feel? Not even tired!"

"Means you've caught the inner rhythm of Shen Fist—officially crossed the threshold! You'll advance twice as fast from here."

Gratitude shone in Bai Feng's eyes. "You're awesome, Your Highness!"

"Where'd that come from?"

"Always felt it! Gave me a home, teach me real martial arts, feed me banquets, hand me silver—best prince on earth! So I want you happy, happy right alongside Princess Jiahe!"

"So that's why you bought her chestnut cakes?"

Bai Feng nodded earnest. "Your Highness stays busy and slips up; I look out for Princess Jiahe for you!"

Shen Jianchen couldn't bring himself to praise him: were he in love with Mengrong, brilliant wingman; feeling zero romance for her, Bai Feng's sweet moves only dug his guilt deeper! "Keep your head on your boxing and stop playing matchmaker—especially between Princess Jiahe and me! And don't forget tomorrow's job: West Third Alley!"

"Locked in, Your Highness!"

Tossing him a pouch of silver: "Five taels inside—catch!"

Catching it with a wide grin, Bai Feng cheered, "Best trait about Your Highness is deep pockets—food, roof, kung fu, and silver!"

"Enough lip—go sleep!"

...

Perched in a scenic spot outside Lin'an's walls, Zuiyun Tower took a full hour by carriage to reach. Keeping his word, Shen Jianchen brought Qiao Mengrong along. Stepping down to behold a broad river sweeping past rolling green hills across the far bank, Qiao Mengrong sighed in wonder at Lin'an's beauty.

Scanning around, Shen Jianchen saw nothing but river, hills, and open countryside; Zuiyun Tower rose lone by the riverbank looking totally out of place. Local peasants couldn't afford dining here—how on earth did it stay open? "How's business way out here, Wenyuan?"

"Look around—dead empty!"

"Then why build it here?!"

"No clue. Just know it belongs to the Chen family, richest house in Lin'an, couldn't care less if one tavern loses coin! Rumor says they built the entire tower just because little Chen Haoyu liked the view here as a kid."

"Same Chen Haoyu we ran into yesterday?"

"The one and only!"

No use asking further; billionaire whims baffled regular minds. If the Chens didn't care about profits, why sweat it? Following Wenyuan inside Zuiyun Tower revealed sheer opulence: a grand rosewood table centering the entry hall ringed with refined bonsai, fine mountain-and-bird scrolls covering the walls bringing nature indoors, and a rear banquet hall lined with crimson-silk-draped tables gleaming with fine porcelain.

A middle-aged man stepped up smooth. "Here for Young Master Dongfang's poetry salon, gentlemen?"

"Yes," Qiao Wenxian confirmed.

Noticing the man's silk robes rivaled Wenyuan's and his own in quality—a Chen relative?—Shen Jianchen studied him curious.

"I am chief steward of Zuiyun Tower. Young Master Dongfang's gathering is upstairs; follow me." Odd, Shen Jianchen thought: unlike busy inns teeming with waiters yesterday, not a single waiter stood in sight!

Upstairs opened onto a bright landing bordered by closed private rooms. Pushing open one door, the steward announced, "Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Qiao and guests have arrived!"

Once the steward withdrew, Qiao Wenxian entered first, followed by Shen Jianchen, Qiao Mengrong with Xilan, and Nan Shuang bringing up the rear—far quieter without Bai Feng to banter with.

Dongfang Chenze jumped up and wrapped Qiao Wenxian in a big hug. "Missed you to pieces, Brother Qiao! Stay home for good now—salons lost half their spark without you!"

Hugging him tight back, Qiao Wenxian grinned, "Missed you too, Brother Dongfang—all of you!" Cao Ban and An Mingxuan stepped right up for warm hugs too once Dongfang let go.

Just as Qiao Wenxian released Cao Ban, a soft, musical woman's voice spoke: "Delighted to discuss verse alongside Young Master Qiao once more." Turning, Qiao Wenxian met Miao Zhiyu executing an elegant curtsy.

Returning a courteous bow, he replied, "Long time no see, Lady Miao; Wenyuan pays respects." Hiding a racing pulse beneath ladylike poise, Miao Zhiyu glowed inside: away almost a year, back at last! Now salons meant hearing his voice and brilliant insights again!

Spotting the newcomers behind Wenyuan, Dongfang Chenze asked, "Introduce your friends, Brother Qiao!"

Keeping Prince Yi's rank under wraps as requested, Qiao Wenxian introduced them smooth: "Young Master Shen, a friend I made in Bianliang, and Miss Qiao, a distant cousin of mine."

Cupping hands polite, Shen Jianchen greeted them. "Hearing Wenyuan had a salon, Mengrong and I got curious and tagged along—hope we aren't intruding."

"Intruding? Honored to have you! Quit standing—everyone take a seat!"

Sitting beside Qiao Wenxian, Shen Jianchen scanned the three unfamiliar scholars, hunting any sign of Qianli Xun. Qiao Mengrong settled next to Miao Zhiyu; trading courteous smiles, one girl kept adoring eyes anchored on her brother while the other stole shy peeks at Wenyuan.

Qiao Wenxian asked curious, "Why pick way out here for today's salon, Brother Dongfang? Lovely view, sure, zero atmosphere!"

"Between friends, no shame admitting it: short on cash!" Dongfang Chenze laughed.

"Short on cash I get—how's that explain Zuiyun Tower?"

"Ghostwrote a poem for Chen Haoyu so he passed his dad's test! Thrilled, he lent me Zuiyun Tower free for the day—unlimited vintage wine, chef on standby cooking anything we order! Pinching pennies, how could I pass up free luxury?!"

Cao Ban chimed in, "Turns out this whole tower runs on just one steward and one chef! Steward says normally nobody sets foot here except Young Master Chen."

An Mingxuan grinned. "Got here early and ordered signature dishes—chef knew every single one! Steward says the Chens poached him straight from Bianliang's Fan Tower, a master cook who took the gig for easy hours and massive pay!"

Right then the steward entered bearing two silver wine pots on a tray. "Young Master Chen ordered top hospitality—two pots of fine vintage, light sweet, zero burn, hint of pomegranate, ideal for both ladies too!" Setting the tray down, he poured full cups all around.

Once filled, Dongfang Chenze waved: "Withdraw for now, Steward; we'll call when needed."

"Yes, sir. Soon as dishes are ready, the chef and I will bring everything up." Setting the pots down, he backed out with a low bow.
