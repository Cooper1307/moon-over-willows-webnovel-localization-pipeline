# 第45章 才子佳人醉云楼

夜幕降临，今晚夜色不错，满天繁星围绕着半轮明月。

Night fell. Tonight the sky was beautiful, with countless stars surrounding a half moon.

---

寻找千里寻已有眉目，申简辰感到欣慰。他向马车窗外望去，远远的，是神柳的影子。半轮明月挂在天上，皎洁的月光洒在四周，神柳的影子在月光下更显婀娜多姿。

They had made progress in finding Qianli Xun, and Shen Jianchen felt relieved. He looked out the carriage window. In the distance was the silhouette of the divine willow. The half moon hung in the sky, its bright light spilling over everything. The willow's silhouette appeared even more graceful under the moonlight.

---

此情此景，申简辰在心里默念着："月上柳梢头，人约黄昏后。"浪漫的情怀油然而生，只是让他感受到浪漫情怀的人也许穿越成为才子。欣慰之余，又倍感落寞。

Seeing this scene, Shen Jianchen silently recited in his heart: "When the moon ascends the willow's crest, we shall meet just past the twilight hour." A romantic sentiment arose naturally, but the person who made him feel this romantic mood might have transmigrated into a talented young man. Amid his relief, he felt even more lonely.

---

马车进入幽林，已无法欣赏神柳的身影，申简辰收回心神，面对文渊继续保持沉默。

The carriage entered the secluded forest, and the willow's silhouette could no longer be seen. Shen Jianchen gathered his thoughts and continued to remain silent toward Wenyuan.

---

文渊很知趣，闭目养神。

Wenyuan knew when to keep quiet and closed his eyes to rest.

---

回到幽林别居，刚走下马车，李管家迎上来："仪王与公子终于回来，佳和郡主一直在念叨。"

Returning to Youlin Villa, they had just stepped down from the carriage when Steward Li came to greet them: "The prince and the young sir have finally returned. Princess Jiahe has been worrying about you."

---

乔文仙故意说："李管家这话说得不准确，佳和郡主念叨的人只有仪王，我呢，孤家寡人一个，哪像仪王，走到哪里都有佳人陪伴左右。"

Qiao Wenxian deliberately said, "Steward Li, your words aren't quite accurate. The person Princess Jiahe worries about is only the prince. As for me, I'm a lone bachelor. Unlike the prince, who has a beauty accompanying him wherever he goes."

---

李管家笑道："公子也该考虑婚事，我看苗姑娘不错，听说公子从汴梁回来，今儿专程来拜访，公子不在，又折回去，说改日再来。"

Steward Li smiled: "The young sir should also consider marriage. I think Miss Miao is quite suitable. I heard that when the young sir returned from Bianliang, she came specially to visit today. When the young sir wasn't here, she went back and said she'd come another day."

---

申简辰问："苗姑娘是谁？你可要老实告诉我。"

Shen Jianchen asked, "Who is Miss Miao? You must tell me honestly."

---

乔文仙不愿提及："仪王还是赶紧去见佳和郡主，以解她相思之苦。"

Qiao Wenxian didn't want to mention it: "The prince should hurry to see Princess Jiahe to relieve her longing."

---

二人说着往宅子内走去。

The two walked into the residence as they spoke.

---

李管家又想起一件事："东方公子派人送来口信，明日邀请公子去醉云楼赏词，还说是幽栖居士的新作，请公子您一定要去。"

Steward Li remembered something else: "Young Master Dongfang sent someone to deliver a message. He invites the young sir to Zuiyun Pavilion tomorrow to appreciate ci poetry. He also said it's Youqi Jushi's new work and asked the young sir to definitely come."

---

乔文仙说："知道了，你去忙吧，剩下的路我与仪王熟悉，无需跟着。"

Qiao Wenxian said, "Understood. You can go attend to your duties. Wenyuan and the prince know the rest of the way. No need to follow."

---

李管家行完礼退去。

Steward Li bowed and withdrew.

---

乔文仙邀请仪王："明日你可愿与我一同参加诗会？"

Qiao Wenxian invited the prince: "Would you like to attend the poetry gathering with me tomorrow?"

---

申简辰心想，参加诗会的都应是像文渊一样的才子，千里寻穿越成为才子，也许能在诗会中遇到。

Shen Jianchen thought to himself that those attending the poetry gathering should all be talented young men like Wenyuan. If Qianli Xun had transmigrated into a talented young man, perhaps he could meet her at the poetry gathering.

---

"好的，我与你同去。"

"Alright, I'll go with you."

---

他们又同行一会儿，才各自分开。

They walked together for a while longer before parting ways.

---

白风跟随王爷去往郡主居住的小院，一路上白风的双手一直背在身后。

Bai Feng followed the prince to the small courtyard where the princess lived. Along the way, Bai Feng kept his hands behind his back.

---

乔梦容被丢在别居整整一日，第一次体会到爱人不在身边时强烈的思念之情，思念中又充满担忧。

Qiao Mengrong had been left alone at the villa for an entire day. For the first time, she experienced the intense longing when a loved one wasn't by her side, a longing filled with worry.

---

哥哥逛街，会不会去勾栏瓦肆？听说那里有很多漂亮姑娘，既会唱曲儿，又会跳舞，还会演奏各种乐器，哥哥会不会与她们有亲密的举动？

Her brother went shopping. Would he go to the entertainment precincts? She'd heard there were many beautiful girls there who could sing, dance, and play various instruments. Would her brother have intimate interactions with them?

---

在以前的哥哥面前，乔梦容充满自信，可面对现在的哥哥，她自信不起来，总是不由自主担忧。

In front of her former brother, Qiao Mengrong was full of confidence. But facing her current brother, she couldn't feel confident and couldn't help but worry.

---

天都变黑，哥哥还不回来，难道去了烟花柳巷之地？临安的女子个个生得貌美，只怕哥哥沉迷在温柔乡里，早把妹妹抛之脑后。

It was already dark and her brother still hadn't returned. Had he gone to a red-light district? The women in Lin'an were all beautiful. She was afraid her brother was indulging in the land of softness and had long forgotten his sister.

---

乔梦容不敢想下去，打算再去找李管家，让他派人到城里找找。

Qiao Mengrong didn't dare think further. She planned to find Steward Li again and have him send someone to look in the city.

---

刚走出闺房，看到哥哥站在院中，乔梦容抑制不住担忧和思念之情，扑进哥哥怀里。

Just as she stepped out of her boudoir and saw her brother standing in the courtyard, Qiao Mengrong couldn't contain her worry and longing. She threw herself into her brother's arms.

---

"哥哥出去一整日，梦容十分担忧，以后无论哥哥去哪里，都必须带上我。"

"Brother went out for an entire day. Mengrong was very worried. From now on, no matter where brother goes, you must take me with you."

---

申简辰之所以来看梦容，是想亲口告诉她，他已回来，让她不要担心。毕竟她对赵朴付出太多，没成想又被她抱住。他没有一丝欢喜，反而内心更加沉重，赵朴这段情给他带来不小的心里负担。

The reason Shen Jianchen came to see Mengrong was to tell her in person that he had returned and not to worry. After all, she had given too much for Zhao Pu. He never expected to be hugged by her again. He felt no joy, but instead his heart grew heavier. This relationship with Zhao Pu brought him considerable psychological burden.

---

他依然没有搂抱梦容："好的，以后无论我去哪里都带上你，正好明日要与文渊参加诗会，你跟着一起去。"

He still didn't embrace Mengrong: "Alright, from now on I'll take you with me wherever I go. It just so happens that tomorrow I'm attending a poetry gathering with Wenyuan. You can come along."

---

哥哥的话就像阴雨天突然出现的阳光，一扫阴霾。乔梦容放开仪王，脸上露出笑容："明日我随哥哥去，定不会给哥哥丢脸。"

Her brother's words were like sunshine suddenly appearing on a rainy day, sweeping away the gloom. Qiao Mengrong let go of the prince and a smile appeared on her face: "Tomorrow I'll go with brother. I definitely won't embarrass you."

---

申简辰不知接下来该与佳和郡主说些什么，只能尴尬地站着。

Shen Jianchen didn't know what to say to Princess Jiahe next and could only stand there awkwardly.

---

白风突然站到乔梦容面前，把手从背后抽出，原来他手里提着一包东西："郡主，这是王爷逛街时给你买的栗子糕，很好吃，我尝过的。"

Bai Feng suddenly stood in front of Qiao Mengrong, pulling his hands from behind his back. It turned out he was holding a package: "Princess, this is chestnut cake the prince bought for you while shopping. It's very delicious. I've tasted it."

---

乔梦容接过，喜上眉梢："谢谢哥哥想着梦容，哥哥买的栗子糕肯定是全天下最好吃的栗子糕。"

Qiao Mengrong took it, joy appearing on her brows: "Thank you, brother, for thinking of Mengrong. The chestnut cake brother bought must be the most delicious chestnut cake in the whole world."

---

申简辰一整天光想着如何能遇到千里寻，早已将乔梦容抛到九霄云外。白风挺细心，就当他是替赵朴做些事情。

Shen Jianchen had been thinking all day about how to meet Qianli Xun and had long forgotten Qiao Mengrong. Bai Feng was quite thoughtful. He might as well consider it as doing something for Zhao Pu.

---

"梦容，天不早，你早些休息。"

"Mengrong, it's not early. You should rest early."

---

"哥哥也早些休息。"

"Brother should also rest early."

---

乔梦容盯着哥哥离去的背影，将栗子糕递给喜兰："把糕点放进盘子里，我要尝几块。"

Qiao Mengrong stared at her brother's departing back and handed the chestnut cake to Xilan: "Put the cake on a plate. I want to taste a few pieces."

---

喜兰接过，转身回屋。

Xilan took it and turned back into the room.

---

乔梦容继续盯着哥哥刚刚站过的地方，她抱住他时，他依然没有回应，什么时候他能像以前一样主动将她抱进怀里？

Qiao Mengrong continued staring at the spot where her brother had just stood. When she hugged him, he still didn't respond. When would he take the initiative to hold her in his arms like before?

---

回到主屋的院子，申简辰命令道："开始练拳！半个时辰后再休息！"

Returning to the main courtyard, Shen Jianchen ordered: "Start practicing fists! Rest after half a shichen!"

---

白风很不乐意："王爷，能不能看在我们刚到临安，休息几日再练？"

Bai Feng was very reluctant: "Prince, could we rest for a few days since we just arrived in Lin'an before practicing?"

---

"不行！"申简辰装作严肃的样子。

"No!" Shen Jianchen pretended to be serious.

---

白风继续找借口："能不能看在我替王爷给郡主买栗子糕的份上，让我休息一日。"

Bai Feng continued making excuses: "Could you let me rest for a day considering I bought chestnut cake for the princess on behalf of the prince?"

---

"不行！"

"No!"

---

王爷很严厉，白风只能在院子里开始练习。

The prince was very strict. Bai Feng could only start practicing in the courtyard.

---

申简辰回屋去休息，大约睡了快一个时辰，猛然醒来，听到白风在院子里练习拳法的声音，心想这小子倒是越练越起劲，竟然忘记时间。

Shen Jianchen went back to his room to rest. He slept for almost a shichen and suddenly woke up. He heard Bai Feng practicing拳法 in the courtyard and thought to himself that this kid was practicing with more and more energy, actually forgetting the time.

---

他起身来到院内："怎么还在练？赶紧休息。"

He got up and came to the courtyard: "Why are you still practicing? Hurry up and rest."

---

白风停下来问："王爷，为何我越练越轻松，一点儿都不觉得累？"

Bai Feng stopped and asked: "Prince, why do I feel more relaxed the more I practice? I don't feel tired at all."

---

申简辰回道："申拳的妙处你算是体会到，说明你开始入门，以后学起来会掌握得更快。"

Shen Jianchen replied: "You've finally realized the wonders of Shen Fist. This shows you're beginning to get the hang of it. You'll master it faster from now on."

---

白风感激道："王爷真好！"

Bai Feng said gratefully: "The prince is really the best!"

---

"怎么突然说这个？"

"Why say this all of a sudden?"

---

"不是突然，是一直想说。王爷给我一个家，又教会我拳法，还带我吃好吃的，又给我钱花，您是天底下最好的王爷，所以我也希望您好，更希望您与郡主幸福美满。"

"It's not sudden. I've always wanted to say it. The prince gave me a home, taught me拳法, took me to eat delicious food, and gave me money to spend. You're the best prince in the world, so I also want you to be well, and even more so, I hope you and the princess are happy together."

---

"所以，你才替我给郡主买栗子糕？"

"So that's why you bought chestnut cake for the princess on my behalf?"

---

白风点点头："王爷事多，难免顾不上郡主，我时刻替王爷想着。"

Bai Feng nodded: "The prince has many things to attend to and inevitably can't always think of the princess. I'm always thinking on behalf of the prince."

---

申简辰对白风的做法夸赞不起来。如果他爱乔梦容，白风的做法则是锦上添花。可他对乔梦容根本无法生起喜爱之情，白风这么做只会令他更为难。

Shen Jianchen couldn't praise Bai Feng's actions. If he loved Qiao Mengrong, Bai Feng's actions would be adding flowers to brocade. But he simply couldn't develop feelings of love for Qiao Mengrong. Bai Feng doing this would only make things more difficult for him.

---

"以后心里多想想拳法，其他事情不用你操心，尤其是我与佳和郡主的事，你更不必放在心上。对了，记着明日给你安排的事，西三胡同。"

"From now on, think more about拳法 in your heart. Don't worry about other things, especially matters between me and Princess Jiahe. You don't need to keep that in mind. By the way, remember what I arranged for you tomorrow—West Third Alley."

---

"王爷放心，记着呢。"

"Rest assured, prince. I remember."

---

申简辰掏出一袋银子："里面有五两银子，拿着！"

Shen Jianchen took out a bag of silver: "There are five taels of silver inside. Take it!"

---

白风笑嘻嘻接过："我发现王爷最大的优点是大方，跟着王爷有吃有喝有拳练，还有银子花。"

Bai Feng took it with a grin: "I've discovered the prince's greatest virtue is generosity. Following the prince, there's food to eat, drinks to drink,拳法 to practice, and silver to spend."

---

"行了，赶紧回屋休息。"

"Alright, hurry back to your room and rest."

---

……

...

---

醉云楼建在临安城外风景绝佳之地，乘坐马车足足行半个时辰才到。

Zuiyun Pavilion was built in a place with excellent scenery outside Lin'an city. It took a full half shichen to get there by carriage.

---

申简辰履行承诺，带上乔梦容。当乔梦容从马车上下来时，看到一条宽阔的江水流过，对岸是连绵起伏的山峦，忍不住慨叹临安真是个好地方。

Shen Jianchen fulfilled his promise and brought Qiao Mengrong. When Qiao Mengrong stepped down from the carriage, she saw a wide river flowing by, with rolling mountains on the opposite bank. She couldn't help but sigh that Lin'an was truly a wonderful place.

---

申简辰观察四周，除了江水与山峦之外，均是一派乡野风光，醉云楼建在江边，显得十分突兀。从醉云楼的外形判断，大略能猜出周围的百姓绝不会在此消费，也不知醉云楼如何在偏僻之地盈利？

Shen Jianchen observed the surroundings. Besides the river and mountains, it was all countryside scenery. Zuiyun Pavilion was built by the river, looking very out of place. Judging from the pavilion's appearance, he could roughly guess that the surrounding common people would never spend money here. He wondered how Zuiyun Pavilion could make a profit in such a remote place?

---

"文渊，醉云楼建在此处，生意如何？"

"Wenyuan, how is the business at Zuiyun Pavilion being built here?"

---

"看看周围就知道，生意冷淡。"

"Just look at the surroundings and you'll know. Business is slow."

---

"为何还要建在这里？"

"Why build it here then?"

---

"这我就不得而知，只知是陈家的产业，陈家是临安首富，不在乎一座酒楼赚不赚钱。而且我听说，当初建这座楼时，仅仅是因为陈浩宇小时候喜欢此处的风景，陈家便建了此楼。"

"I don't know about that. I only know it's the Chen family's property. The Chen family is the richest in Lin'an and doesn't care whether a restaurant makes money or not. Moreover, I heard that when this building was first constructed, it was simply because Chen Haoyu liked the scenery here as a child, so the Chen family built this building."

---

"陈浩宇可是昨日碰到的那位陈公子？"

"Is Chen Haoyu the Young Master Chen we met yesterday?"

---

"正是。"

"Exactly."

---

申简辰不再继续问下去，有钱人豪掷千金，普通人难以想象。既然人家都不在意是否盈利，他申简辰又何必杞人忧天？

Shen Jianchen didn't continue asking. Rich people throw away thousands of gold coins, which ordinary people can hardly imagine. Since they don't care about whether they make a profit, why should he, Shen Jianchen, worry unnecessarily?

---

申简辰与乔梦容跟上文渊进入醉云楼。

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Mengrong followed Wenyuan into Zuiyun Pavilion.

---

里面布置可用"豪华"二字形容：一楼的门厅放置着一张大红木桌，周围点缀着各式各样的盆景，十分精致。墙上挂着一幅幅山水花鸟的画作，让人感觉仿佛置身于大自然之中。后厅是一排排桌子，上面铺着厚厚的红色绸缎，显得十分典雅，桌子上摆着精美的瓷器……

The interior decoration could be described with the word "luxurious": In the first-floor entrance hall was a large rosewood table, surrounded by various exquisite potted landscapes. On the walls hung paintings of mountains, water, flowers, birds, and other scenes, making people feel as if they were in nature. The back hall had rows of tables covered with thick red silk, looking very elegant, with exquisite porcelain on the tables...

---

一位中年男子走过来："几位可是前来参加东方公子的诗会？"

A middle-aged man walked over: "Are you here to attend Young Master Dongfang's poetry gathering?"

---

文渊回道："正是。"

Wenyuan replied: "Exactly."

---

申简辰瞧那男子，他身上的衣裳所用的布料并不比文渊和自己差，难道是陈家人？

Shen Jianchen looked at the man. The fabric of his clothes was no worse than Wenyuan's and his own. Could he be from the Chen family?

---

男子说："我是醉云楼的总管，东方公子的诗会在二楼，请跟我来。"

The man said: "I'm the manager of Zuiyun Pavilion. Young Master Dongfang's poetry gathering is on the second floor. Please follow me."

---

申简辰感到奇怪，他昨日所逛之处都有伙计招呼，而醉云楼连一位伙计都看不到。

Shen Jianchen found it strange. Yesterday, everywhere he went had attendants to greet customers, but at Zuiyun Pavilion, he couldn't even see a single attendant.

---

他们跟随总管上了二楼，又是一番景象，除了有厅堂外，主要是各类雅间，门都紧闭着，不知里面是何风景。

They followed the manager upstairs to the second floor, where there was another scene. Besides the halls, there were mainly various private rooms with doors tightly closed, unknown what scenery was inside.

---

总管推开一扇门，向里面说："东方公子，乔公子与朋友到了。"

The manager pushed open a door and said inside: "Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Qiao and his friends have arrived."

---

总管离开后，乔文仙第一个走进雅间，随后是申简辰，乔梦容带着喜兰也走进去。南双最后一个进入，没有白风陪着，他安静许多。

After the manager left, Qiao Wenxian was the first to enter the private room, followed by Shen Jianchen. Qiao Mengrong also went in with Xilan. Nanshuang entered last, much quieter without Bai Feng accompanying him.

---

东方尘泽一把抱住乔文仙："乔兄，想死我了，回来就不要再离开，诗会没有你少了许多乐趣。"

Dongfang Chenze hugged Qiao Wenxian: "Brother Qiao, I've missed you to death. Don't leave again after coming back. The poetry gathering has much less fun without you."

---

乔文仙也紧紧抱住东方尘泽："东方兄，我也想你，还有你们。"

Qiao Wenxian also hugged Dongfang Chenze tightly: "Brother Dongfang, I've missed you too, and all of you."

---

曹班与安明轩早已走过来，乔文仙放开东方尘泽后，又与二位相互拥抱，表达好友之情。

Cao Ban and An Mingxuan had already walked over. After Qiao Wenxian let go of Dongfang Chenze, he hugged the two of them mutually, expressing friendship.

---

当乔文仙放开曹班时，听到一个柔美的女声。

When Qiao Wenxian let go of Cao Ban, he heard a soft, beautiful female voice.

---

"又能与乔公子一起品鉴诗词，知瑜不胜荣幸。"

"It's my great honor to be able to appreciate poetry with Young Master Qiao again."

---

乔文仙转过身，苗知瑜优雅地行了朋友之礼。

Qiao Wenxian turned around. Miao Zhiyu elegantly performed a friend's greeting.

---

乔文仙回礼道："苗姑娘，好久不见，文渊这厢有礼。"

Qiao Wenxian returned the greeting: "Miss Miao, long time no see. Wenyuan returns your courtesy."

---

苗知瑜行完礼，努力掩饰兴奋之情，他离开临安快一年，终于归来，以后可在诗会中常常遇见，亲耳聆听他的声音，感受他解读诗词时独到的见解。

After Miao Zhiyu finished the greeting, she tried hard to hide her excitement. He had left Lin'an for almost a year and finally returned. From now on, she could often meet him at poetry gatherings, hear his voice in person, and feel his unique insights when interpreting poetry.

---

东方尘泽早已注意到文渊身后跟着的人："乔兄，介绍一下你带来的朋友。"

Dongfang Chenze had already noticed the people following Wenyuan: "Brother Qiao, introduce the friends you brought."

---

仪王还是不愿暴露身份，乔文仙需帮他暂时隐瞒："这位是申公子，我在汴梁认识的朋友，这位是我的远亲乔姑娘。"

The prince still didn't want to reveal his identity, so Qiao Wenxian had to help him temporarily conceal it: "This is Young Master Shen, a friend I met in Bianliang. This is my distant relative, Miss Qiao."

---

申简辰拱手道："文渊来此参加诗会，我与梦容很感兴趣，故而跟着他前来，希望诸位不要介意。"

Shen Jianchen cupped his hands and said: "Wenyuan came here to attend the poetry gathering. Mengrong and I are very interested, so we followed him. I hope you all don't mind."

---

东方尘泽说："怎会介意？我等求之不得，大家别站着，尽快落座。"

Dongfang Chenze said: "Why would we mind? We'd be delighted. Everyone, don't stand. Please take your seats quickly."

---

申简辰在乔文仙身旁的位置坐下，观察新认识的三位才子，希望能找到与千里寻相关之处。

Shen Jianchen sat in the seat next to Qiao Wenxian and observed the three newly met talented young men, hoping to find something related to Qianli Xun.

---

乔梦容坐在苗知瑜旁边的位置，她们以微笑见礼，然后一位将目光停留在哥哥身上，另一位则时不时观察文渊。

Qiao Mengrong sat in the seat next to Miao Zhiyu. They greeted each other with smiles. Then one kept her gaze on her brother, while the other occasionally observed Wenyuan.

---

乔文仙问："东方兄，此次诗会，为何找这么偏远的地方？醉云楼除周围景致好以外，一点儿人气都没有。"

Qiao Wenxian asked: "Brother Dongfang, why choose such a remote place for this poetry gathering? Besides the good scenery around, Zuiyun Pavilion has no human presence at all."

---

东方尘泽说："乔兄既问起，我也不怕告诉你，只因囊中羞涩，才选择此处。"

Dongfang Chenze said: "Since Brother Qiao asks, I'm not afraid to tell you. It's only because I'm short of funds that I chose this place."

---

乔文仙倍感疑惑："囊中羞涩我懂，选择此处，却不甚理解。"

Qiao Wenxian was very puzzled: "I understand being short of funds, but choosing this place, I don't quite understand."

---

东方尘泽继续解释："我帮陈浩宇写了一首诗，他才顺利通过他爹那一关，一高兴，答应把醉云楼借我一用，还说好酒随便喝，想吃什么让厨子做。我正好囊中羞涩，只能选择此处。"

Dongfang Chenze continued explaining: "I helped Chen Haoyu write a poem, and he smoothly passed his father's test. He was so happy that he agreed to lend me Zuiyun Pavilion and said we could drink fine wine freely and have the chef cook whatever we wanted. I happened to be short of funds, so I could only choose this place."

---

曹班说："没想到醉云楼仅有一位总管和一位厨子，总管说平常只有陈公子过来，从未来过这么多人。"

Cao Ban said: "I didn't expect Zuiyun Pavilion to have only a manager and a chef. The manager said usually only Young Master Chen comes here. They've never had so many people before."

---

安明轩说："我们先到，已点好几道硬菜，厨子都会做。总管说厨子是陈家从汴梁的矾楼请来的，厨艺一流，之所以来这里是因为既清闲，陈家又给的工钱多。"

An Mingxuan said: "We arrived first and have already ordered several hard dishes. The chef can cook them all. The manager said the chef was invited by the Chen family from Fan Restaurant in Bianliang. His culinary skills are first-rate. The reason he came here is because it's leisurely and the Chen family pays high wages."

---

正说着，总管用盘子端着两壶美酒进来："各位公子，陈公子交代过，要好生款待诸位，这两壶美酒是佳酿，口感微甜，一点儿都不辣，还有石榴的味道，也适合两位姑娘。"

Just as they were speaking, the manager came in with a tray holding two pots of fine wine: "Young masters, Young Master Chen has instructed us to treat you well. These two pots of fine wine are vintage. The taste is slightly sweet, not spicy at all, and has a pomegranate flavor. It's also suitable for the two young ladies."

---

总管把盘子放在一旁的桌上，端起壶，给大家满上。

The manager put the tray on a nearby table, picked up the pot, and filled everyone's cups.

---

待总管一一斟满，东方尘泽叮嘱道："总管，你先退下，需要时再唤你。"

After the manager filled everyone's cups, Dongfang Chenze instructed: "Manager, you may withdraw first. We'll call you when needed."

---

"是，一会儿菜做好，我再与厨师给诸位摆上。"总管放下酒壶，弯着腰小心退出。

"Yes. When the dishes are ready in a moment, I'll arrange them with the chef for you all." The manager put down the wine pot and carefully withdrew, bowing.
