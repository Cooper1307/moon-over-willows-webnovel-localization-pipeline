第348章 燕京城外忙救人
## Chapter 348: Busy Rescuing a Life Outside Yanjing

离开完颜池那日，千里寻孤身一人在燕京街头逛荡，她犹豫是离开燕京还是去别处？能肯定的是她不会回临安或者去汴梁。
The day she left Wanyan Chi, Qianli Xun wandered the streets of Yanjing alone, torn between leaving the city and going somewhere else. One thing was certain: she would not return to Lin'an or go to Bianliang.

尽管这两个地方随便选一个她都十分乐意，可想到自己失身完颜池，她没有勇气去找她想要找的人，最终决定一个人在燕京度过。
She would have been glad to pick either place, but the thought of having given herself to Wanyan Chi stripped her of the courage to seek the people she wanted to see. In the end she decided to stay in Yanjing, on her own.

她身无分文，走出王府时什么都没带，该如何在燕京讨生活？想到自己一身功夫，不如卖艺先赚些钱，再租个地方住下来。
She had no money. She had walked out of the prince's mansion with nothing. How was she to make a living in Yanjing? She thought of her martial arts. She would perform in the street, earn some cash, then rent a place to sleep.

她在街头选了一块空地，把斗篷脱掉，叠整齐放在墙根的石头上，向来来往往的人大声说：“南来的北往的！向这儿瞧了！……”
She picked a patch of open ground, took off her cloak, folded it neatly, and set it on a stone at the foot of the wall. Then she called out to everyone passing by, 'Travelers from south and north! Look over here!...'

一些逛街的人被吸引，靠拢而来。
A few people out shopping were drawn by her voice and drew closer.

“小女初来乍到，身无分文，好在会些拳脚功夫，在此给各位表演一套拳法，若觉得好，赏小女几文，若觉得不好分文不取！”
'This girl is new here and hasn't a coin. Luckily I know a few moves. I'll perform a set of fist forms for you. If you like it, toss me a few coppers. If not, I won't take a single one.'

围观的人越来越多，都想目睹美娇娘功夫如何。
The crowd grew. Everyone wanted to see what a pretty young woman could do with her fists.

千里寻摆好姿势，她打算表演配合朱淑真的《念奴娇·冬晴无雪》创制的拳法。她边吟诵诗词边使出拳法，诗词吟诵得饱含情感，拳法招招与诗词相配，阳刚与阴柔之美交织在一起，既赏心悦目，又令人惊叹拳技之高超。
Qianli Xun set her stance. She would perform the fist sequence she had created to go with Zhu Shuzhen's 'Nian Nu Jiao: Clear Winter, No Snow.' She chanted the lines as she struck, her voice thick with feeling, every move fitted to a line of the poem. Hard strength and soft grace wound together, lovely to watch and astonishing in their skill.

诗词念完时，拳法也跟着结束，周围的百姓使劲儿鼓掌。千里寻掬起双手上前讨钱，许多人一文两文地放进她的手中，一圈儿下来，挣了一把钱，临时找个地方住应该不成问题。
When the last line faded, the last fist fell. The onlookers clapped hard. Qianli Xun cupped her hands and went among them for coins. People dropped in one and two at a time. By the time she had made the round she held a fistful of cash—enough for a bed for the night.

正当她高兴时，陆远峰过来，将围着的百姓驱散：“都走吧，别看了，她不会再表演。”
She was still smiling over it when Lu Yuanfeng came up and waved the crowd away. 'Go on, all of you. Nothing more to see. She's done performing.'

百姓们纷纷离开。
The people drifted off.

千里寻掬着钱，生气地质问道：“陆远峰！你太过分！凭什么赶走我的客人？”
Qianli Xun cupped her coins and glared at him. 'Lu Yuanfeng! How dare you? What right have you to chase away my customers?'

陆远峰转过身：“师傅，你是何等身份？怎能在此卖艺？”
Lu Yuanfeng turned around. 'Master, who are you? How can you perform in the street like this?'

“我不过是普通百姓一个，为什么不能卖艺？我卖艺既不违背良心也不违背律法，你管得着吗？”
'I'm an ordinary commoner. Why shouldn't I earn a living? I break no law and wrong no conscience. Since when is it your business?'

陆远峰见师傅掬着钱十分辛苦，到旁边的摊位上买来一个荷包，把师傅手中的钱往荷包里装：“师傅何必如此？跟着小王爷多好？偏偏跑出来在街头卖艺，小王爷要是知道心里肯定不好受。”
Lu Yuanfeng saw how hard she had worked for those coins. He went to a nearby stall, bought a small purse, and began pouring her money into it. 'Why do this, Master? Life with the young prince would be good. Instead you run out here to perform. If he learns of it, his heart will ache, I promise you.'

“是完颜池派你来的？”
'Did Wanyan Chi send you?'

陆远峰将钱全部装进荷包：“跟小王爷没关系，我出来办事，偶然看到师傅卖艺，才过来劝说。”
Lu Yuanfeng tucked the last coin into the purse. 'The young prince has nothing to do with it. I was out on an errand, saw you performing, and came over to talk sense into you.'

千里寻从陆远峰手中拿过荷包：“跟他没关系就好，你也不必劝说，我的事不许告诉完颜池。”
Qianli Xun took the purse from his hand. 'If it has nothing to do with him, good. Save your breath. Don't you dare tell Wanyan Chi a word of this.'

陆远峰假装道：“师傅和小王爷之间的事我不想参与，不过我得说句公道话，小王爷对师傅没的说，我在他身边也有一段时日，他从未对哪个姑娘像对师傅一样上心过。”
Lu Yuanfeng put on an innocent face. 'I don't want to meddle in what's between you and the prince. But I will say this much in fairness: the young prince has never been anything but good to you. I've served at his side a long time. He's never cared for a girl the way he cares for you.'

“那又如何？对我好的人多了，难道我要个个喜欢？像完颜池那种喜欢霸王硬上钩的人老娘最讨厌，他打着喜欢别人的旗号，采取强硬手段，纯粹是为满足个人私欲，别以为老娘是瞎子，什么都看不明白。”
'So what? Plenty of people are good to me. Must I love them all? A man like Wanyan Chi, who forces himself on a woman and calls it love—he's the sort I despise most. He waves a banner of affection while using brute force, all to feed his own desire. Don't think I'm blind. I can see straight through it.'

陆远峰竖起大拇指：“师傅是女中豪杰，跟一般女子不一样，佩服！”
Lu Yuanfeng raised his thumb. 'You're a heroine among women, Master. Not like other girls. I admire you.'

“少奉承我！”千里寻把荷包装好，到墙根的石头跟前取了斗篷披好，准备离开。
'Stop flattering me.' She tucked the purse away, went to the stone at the wall, picked up her cloak, and put it on, ready to leave.

陆远峰上前拦住：“师傅打算离开燕京？”
Lu Yuanfeng stepped in front of her. 'Are you leaving Yanjing, Master?'

“你管得着吗？少拦我！”千里寻从一旁闪开。
'What business is it of yours? Get out of my way.' She slipped past him.

陆远峰在身后喊：“师傅！你要是不打算离开燕京，我帮你找地方住！”
He called after her. 'Master! If you're not leaving Yanjing, I'll find you a place to stay!'

千里寻正为住的地方发愁，不如听他如何说。她转过身：“真的不是完颜池派你来的？”
She had been worrying about exactly that. She turned. 'You swear Wanyan Chi didn't send you?'

陆远峰继续撒谎：“真的不是，我称你一声师傅，总不能看着你沦落街头，伸手帮一把也是应该的。”
Lu Yuanfeng kept up the lie. 'He truly didn't. I call you Master. I can't stand by and watch you sleep in the street. Lending a hand is only right.'

“住的地方在哪里？带我去吧。”千里寻的语气缓和下来，此时的她急需找到住的地方，有人送上门来，不如先住下。即使与完颜池有关，她也不怕，他若敢出现，不是他死便是她亡。
'Where is this place? Take me there.' Qianli Xun's tone softened. She needed a roof badly. Here was one handed to her, and she might as well take it. Even if it had something to do with Wanyan Chi, she wasn't afraid. If he dared show his face, one of them would not walk away.

陆远峰见师傅松口，很开心：“不远处的巷子里，有个闲置的屋子，正好给师傅住。”
Lu Yuanfeng was delighted she had relented. 'Not far. There's an empty room in the lane nearby. Perfect for you, Master.'

千里寻怀疑道：“你整日跟完颜池住在王府，怎么会在外面有屋子？”
Qianli Xun eyed him suspiciously. 'You live in the prince's mansion with Wanyan Chi day and night. How do you come to have a room out here?'

陆远峰“嘿嘿”笑道：“我是男人，也喜欢养个把女人，在王府不太合适，临时租了间屋子，人还没安置进去，先给师傅住。”
Lu Yuanfeng chuckled. 'I'm a man. A man likes to keep a woman on the side. Can't do that in the mansion, so I rented a room. Hadn't moved her in yet. You can have it first, Master.'

“我岂不是坏了你的好事？”
'Am I ruining your little arrangement?'

“哪里话，给师傅住是我的荣幸。”
'Don't say that. It's an honor to have you there.'

千里寻把荷包掏出来，走上前，递给陆远峰：“今晚的租金，有些少，别介意，等我安顿下来挣到更多银子再补上。”
Qianli Xun pulled out her purse, stepped forward, and held it out to him. 'Rent for tonight. It's not much, don't mind that. Once I'm settled and earn more silver, I'll make it up to you.'

陆远峰推回师傅的手：“我怎能跟师傅要租金？师傅放心住下。”
Lu Yuanfeng pushed her hand back. 'How could I take rent from you, Master? Stay with an easy mind.'

千里寻坚持道：“你若不收，我便不住。”
Qianli Xun insisted. 'If you won't take it, I won't stay.'

陆远峰说：“我收我收，师傅还需钱生活，你先把钱拿回去，等以后挣到钱再补给我不迟。”
'All right, all right, I'll take it. You need money to live on. Keep your coins now and pay me later, when you've earned more.'

千里寻心想，也成，便收回荷包：“谢了，带我去吧。”
Qianli Xun thought that made sense and drew the purse back. 'Thanks. Lead the way.'

陆远峰租的屋子尚可，对于急需容身之地的人来讲已是不错，她安心住下。
The room Lu Yuanfeng had rented was passable—more than passable for someone who just needed somewhere to be. She settled in with a quiet mind.

后来陆远峰以能尽快还房租为理由，给她介绍帮人写书信以及给茶馆提供新的诗词供茶客品鉴的活儿，每个月能挣一两银子。虽不多，也足以令她心安。
Later, saying she would need to pay the rent back quickly, Lu Yuanfeng found her work writing letters for people and supplying new poems to a teahouse for the guests to enjoy. It came to a tael or two of silver a month. Not much, but enough to put her mind at rest.

千里寻自然没有忘记继续写话本，可惜在燕京说书不被允许。几经辗转才找到一位说话艺人，免费将话本的书稿送给他，假装千里寻才子认识她留给她的。说话艺人听后高兴不已，答应她想办法将话本寄出燕京城。
Qianli Xun had not forgotten to keep writing story scripts, but storytelling was forbidden in Yanjing. After some searching she found a professional storyteller and gave him the manuscripts for free, claiming a scholar named Qianli Xun, an acquaintance, had left them with her. The storyteller was delighted and promised to find a way to send the stories out of the city.

为了以后写的故事能被百姓听到，千里寻告诉说话艺人，后续千里寻才子还会提供话本。
So that the stories might one day reach ordinary listeners, Qianli Xun told him the scholar would be providing more.

说话艺人急切地说：“燕京城只有我有门路能将话本送出，后面的统统给我，来者不拒。”
The storyteller spoke eagerly. 'I'm the only one in Yanjing with a way to get scripts out. Send me every one you have. I'll take as many as come.'

千里寻欣然答应。
Qianli Xun gladly agreed.

大宋已进入靖康元年，一个耻辱即将来临的年份。
The Song had entered the first year of Jingkang—a year of shame about to arrive.

过元日时，千里寻一个人在他乡度过，心中充满对亲人和爱人的思念。恐怕大家都以为她不在人世，她不想回去打扰每个人平静的生活。
New Year's Day came and Qianli Xun spent it in a strange land, her heart full of thoughts of family and of the man she loved. No doubt they all believed her dead. She had no wish to go back and disturb the quiet lives of everyone she knew.

正月初六这晚，天空晴朗，新月升起。千里寻披上斗篷，来到院中，抬头仰望新月的模样，心中怀念旧人，也怀念她常常去看望的神柳。
On the sixth night of the first month the sky was clear and a new moon rose. Qianli Xun wrapped her cloak around her, went into the courtyard, and looked up at the crescent. She missed old friends, and she missed the sacred willow she used to visit.

时代的悲哀令身在异乡的她更有一种悲戚之情，明日该给茶馆提供诗词，茶馆的老板说春节期间最好不要写太过悲伤的句子，可此时的她只回忆起悲伤的诗词。
The sorrow of the age weighed heavier on her in a foreign place. Tomorrow she had to deliver poems to the teahouse. Its owner had said to avoid lines too sad during the New Year holidays. But all she could recall were sorrowful verses.

正为难间，想到今日是正月初六，正好朱淑真有首《忆秦娥·正月初六日夜月》，与今晚的夜景相合，还符合茶馆的要求，随即进屋提笔写下：
She was stuck, then remembered today was the sixth of the first month. Zhu Shuzhen had a poem, 'Yi Qin E: Moon on the Night of the Sixth Day of the First Month,' that suited the night and would please the teahouse. She went inside, took up her brush, and wrote:

弯弯曲，新年新月钩寒玉。钩寒玉，凤鞋儿小，翠眉儿蹙。
Crooked, so crooked, the new moon of the new year hooks cold jade. Hooks cold jade—small phoenix shoes, brows of green knit tight.

闹蛾雪柳添妆束，烛龙火树争驰逐。争驰逐，元宵三五，不如初六。
Moth and willow ornaments add to her dress, candle-dragon and fire-tree race along. They race along—the Lantern Festival on the fifteenth is not as good as this sixth day.

落款时，千里寻犹豫起来，金人已下令必须用金的年号，若不用便是杀头之罪。
When she came to the signature, Qianli Xun hesitated. The Jin had ordered everyone to use their reign title. To refuse was punishable by death.

片刻犹豫后，千里寻果断写下“靖康元年正月初六”，她偏不用金的年号，大不了死在金人刀下，反正她心里念着的人都以为她已不在。
She hesitated only a moment, then wrote firmly, 'First year of Jingkang, sixth day of the first month.' She would not use the Jin calendar. Let them kill her with their swords, if it came to that. Everyone she cared about already believed her gone.

结果她一点儿事没有，诗词照样挂在茶馆里，她猜测应是陆远峰帮忙，毕竟他在完颜池身边混得不错。
Nothing happened. The poem hung in the teahouse as before. She guessed Lu Yuanfeng had smoothed things over. He stood high enough in Wanyan Chi's favor, after all.

进入春季千里寻的日子会好过些，令她庆幸的是，完颜池没有打扰过她，她更相信陆远峰说的话，他所做的一切的确与完颜池没有任何关系。
Spring would make her life easier, and to her relief Wanyan Chi left her alone. She believed more strongly what Lu Yuanfeng had said—that everything he did truly had nothing to do with the prince.

桃花刚刚开放时，陆远峰又给她带来新的差事，给金人的孩子教汉人的文字。
When the peach blossoms first opened, Lu Yuanfeng brought her a new job: teaching Jin children to read and write Chinese.

开始千里寻不愿接受，事后一想，学习汉文化是将金人汉化的绝佳途径。金人发动战争伤害了多少汉人，不如趁机把他们的孩子汉化，让他们的思想变得与汉人一致。
At first she refused. Then she thought it over. Learning Han culture was the perfect way to turn the Jin into Han. How many Han people had their wars harmed? Better to win over their children while there was still time, to make their thoughts the same as Han thoughts.

千里寻为自己的计谋感到开心，等陆远峰再来时，告诉他愿意接下此事。
Qianli Xun was pleased with her little scheme. When Lu Yuanfeng came again, she told him she would take the work.

屋子的外厅变成学堂，每日会有六名金人的孩子前来学习。为了改造他们，千里寻夜以继日翻阅买回的典籍。
The outer room became a schoolroom. Six Jin children came every day. To reshape them, Qianli Xun pored over the books she bought, day and night.

在她的努力下，教学的效果不错，六名孩子都认为金汉乃一家，不应发动战争互相残杀。
Her efforts paid off. All six came to believe that Jin and Han were one family and that war between them was senseless killing.

...

今日，正在教孩子们学习《论语》，陆远峰跑来，坐在一旁听了一会儿。
Today she was teaching the children the Analects when Lu Yuanfeng came in and sat to one side to listen for a while.

待孩子们离开后，千里寻取来一袋银子给陆远峰：“感谢你一直以来帮我，一年的房租已挣到，你拿着。”
When the children had gone, Qianli Xun fetched a bag of silver and handed it to him. 'Thank you for all your help. A year's rent is covered. Take this.'

陆远峰装模作样接过：“师傅今日有什么打算？”
Lu Yuanfeng accepted it with a show of reluctance. 'What are your plans for today, Master?'

“这话问得奇怪，我能有什么打算？每日这个时候我会逛街，笔用了好久，开始掉毛，墨条也快用完，正好出去买些回来。”
'A strange question. What plans could I have? I go shopping at this hour every day. This brush has shed half its hairs, and the ink stick is nearly gone. Time to buy more.'

陆远峰担心师傅跑到主街，若碰到仪王，或是听到百姓议论，恐怕坏了小王爷的大事。建议道：“我知道有一家笔墨甚好，我带师傅去。”
Lu Yuanfeng worried she might wander onto the main street and run into Prince Yi, or overhear people talking, and spoil the young prince's plan. He suggested, 'I know a shop with very good brushes and ink. Let me take you there.'

千里寻并未多想：“你推荐的应该不错，在外面等会儿，我换件衣裳就来。”
Qianli Xun gave it no thought. 'If you recommend it, it must be good. Wait outside a moment. I'll change and come out.'

师傅进去换衣裳的空档，陆远峰走到一旁的柜子跟前，拉开抽屉，把一袋银子放进去，自语道：“租金都是小王爷出的，我怎敢要你的银子？”
While she changed, Lu Yuanfeng went to a cabinet, pulled open a drawer, put the bag of silver inside, and muttered to himself, 'The prince paid the rent. How could I take your silver?'

千里寻换好衣裳后，跟着陆远峰一起逛街。
Qianli Xun finished changing and went shopping with him.

陆远峰专挑偏僻的街道走，不仅人少，卖东西的铺子也不多。千里寻十分纳闷：“你怎么把我往偏僻的街道带？半天没看到一家卖笔墨的铺子。”
Lu Yuanfeng chose quiet back streets, where there were few people and few shops. Qianli Xun wondered at it. 'Why are you leading me into the back lanes? We haven't passed a single shop selling brushes.'

“别着急，再往前走就到。”陆远峰解释完，趁机说，“师傅，最近我想一直陪你。”
'Patience. Just a little farther.' He explained, then seized the moment. 'Master, lately I'd like to stay by your side.'

千里寻边走边说：“不用，还是好好陪你偷偷养在外面的女人。”
She walked on. 'No need. Go keep company with that woman you've secretly set up outside.'

陆远峰说：“外面的女人哪有师傅重要？我还是陪师傅。”
'What woman out there could matter as much as you? I'll stay with you, Master.'

千里寻担心这家伙对自己有想法，停下脚步：“什么意思？”
Worried he had designs on her, Qianli Xun stopped short. 'What do you mean?'

陆远峰从师傅的表情中看出他的话说得有些重，感情表达得不合适，赶紧解释：“师傅别误会，我哪敢对小王爷喜欢的人动心思？我对师傅只有尊重。”
Lu Yuanfeng saw from her face that he had gone too far and expressed himself badly. He hurried to explain. 'Don't misunderstand me, Master. How would I dare want the woman the young prince loves? I only respect you.'

千里寻松口气，继续朝前走：“少在我面前提他。”
Qianli Xun let out a breath and walked on. 'Don't mention him to me again.'

“你还没答应我是否让我陪你？”
'You haven't said whether I may keep you company?'

“不必！老娘现在习惯一个人！”
'No! I'm used to being on my own now.'

陆远峰跟上师傅，两人拐了一个弯，终于看到一家售卖笔墨的铺子。还好笔墨不错，否则今日肯定要穿帮。
Lu Yuanfeng followed her. They turned a corner at last and saw a shop selling brushes and ink. Let the brushes be good, he thought, or he was caught out today.

话说完颜池带人来到燕京城门口，登上高高的城墙，向下俯瞰。金兵已将进出的百姓拦截并驱散，城门口里里外外围满金兵。
Now Wanyan Chi had brought his men to the gate of Yanjing and climbed the high wall to look down. Jin soldiers had stopped and scattered everyone entering or leaving. The gate was ringed with them, inside and out.

申简辰站在城墙下，盯着高高在上的完颜池：“你怎么一个人出现？我要的人呢？”
Shen Jianchen stood below the wall, glaring up at him. 'Why are you here alone? Where is the person I asked for?'

完颜池继续撒谎：“去年已告诉过你，她死了。”
Wanyan Chi kept up the lie. 'I told you last year. She's dead.'

申简辰不会再轻易被欺骗：“你的谎言对我毫无作用，既然你没把我想要的人带来，也别想见到你想见之人。”
Shen Jianchen would not be fooled again. 'Your lies mean nothing to me. Since you haven't brought the one I want, don't expect to see the one you want.'

完颜池注视着两辆马车：“本王想见的人可在马车里？”
Wanyan Chi eyed the two carriages. 'Is the person I wish to see in one of those?'

申简辰指着一辆马车：“你想见的人就在那辆马车里。”
Shen Jianchen pointed. 'The one you want is in that carriage.'

激动的泪水滚落下来，完颜池稍加平静后，问道：“我怎么能知道你是否在欺骗本王？”
Tears of joy ran down Wanyan Chi's face. When he had steadied himself, he asked, 'How do I know you're not deceiving me?'

马车里传出女人的声音：“仪王，你过来。”
A woman's voice came from the carriage. 'Prince Yi, come here.'

女人的声音慈祥而温柔，完颜池站在高处，多少听到一些，与他想象中娘亲的声音一模一样。
The voice was kind and gentle. Standing high on the wall, Wanyan Chi caught enough of it to know it was exactly the voice of the mother he remembered.

大宋的仪王走到马车旁，里面的人好像给他说了什么，只见仪王拱手之后抬头瞧过来。
The Prince Yi of Song walked to the carriage. Someone inside seemed to speak to him. He cupped his hands, then looked up.

“小王爷想见的人告诉我你的左耳朵后面有颗红痣，不知可否属实？”
'The man who wishes to see you tells me there is a red mole behind your left ear. Is that true?'

完颜池彻底沦陷，他匆忙跑下城墙，冲出城门，来到马车跟前，已泪流满面。
Wanyan Chi was undone. He scrambled down the wall, rushed through the gate, and reached the carriage with tears streaming down his face.

“您是我娘？……”
'Are you my mother?...'

马车里的邵月婷也泪如雨下：“我是你亲娘……”
Inside the carriage Shao Yueting was weeping too. 'I am your own mother...'

完颜池跪在地上：“娘让我找得好苦！为何躲着一直不见我？”
Wanyan Chi fell to his knees. 'Mother, I searched so hard for you! Why hide away and refuse to see me?'

“见又有何用？只不过徒增烦恼。”
'What good would seeing me do? Only more grief.'

“娘，您下来，让儿子好好看看您。”
'Mother, come down. Let me look at you properly.'

“我今日是为救人而来，你不把那姑娘放了，娘绝不走下马车。”
'I have come today to save a life. Until you free that girl, I will not step down from this carriage.'

“好，我放，只要能见到娘，做什么我都愿意。”
'All right. I'll free her. To see you, I'd do anything.'

完颜池答应放人，申简辰再高兴不过：“人在哪里？”
With his promise given, Shen Jianchen was overjoyed. 'Where is she?'

完颜池骗道：“她在王府，见到娘亲我自然会放她；不过，你还需答应本王一个条件。”
Wanyan Chi lied. 'She's at the mansion. Once I've seen my mother, of course I'll release her. But first you must grant me one condition.'

申简辰谨慎地问：“什么条件？”
Shen Jianchen asked carefully, 'What condition?'

“本王会履行承诺将她送回临安，而你需在燕京留一年，把拳法尽数教给本王和师傅。还有，她离开时，你不许见她。”
'I will keep my word and send her back to Lin'an. But you must stay in Yanjing for a year and teach the fist art to me and my master, every move of it. And when she leaves, you are not to see her off.'

申简辰担心完颜池使诈：“你竟然敢跟我提条件？就不怕我原路返回？”
Shen Jianchen suspected a trick. 'You dare make demands of me? Have you no fear I'll turn back the way I came?'

“睁大眼睛瞧瞧，周围全是本王的人，你根本逃不掉。”
'Open your eyes. My men surround you. You cannot escape.'

申简辰早已看到，本以为带邵月婷前来必然顺利换回千里寻，然后他与她一起回临安，因而并未将周围的金兵当回事。
Shen Jianchen had seen them already. He had assumed bringing Shao Yueting would secure Qianli Xun's release without trouble, and that he and she would go home to Lin'an together. So he had not taken the soldiers seriously.

现在看来完颜池诡计多端，想趁机留下他学习拳法。若真打起来，他一个人难以取胜，说不定会将事情闹得更僵，不仅救不出千里寻，还把自己搭进去。留一年便留一年，也正好趁此机会搞清楚申拳的来历。
Now it was clear: Wanyan Chi was plotting to keep him here and learn the fist art. If it came to a fight, one man could not win, and might make things worse—he would fail to free Qianli Xun and lose himself as well. A year, then. A year would also give him a chance to learn where Shen-style boxing came from.

“你必须答应我老老实实放她回临安，不许诓我。”
'You must promise to let her go back to Lin'an in good faith. No tricks.'

“本王已得到最珍贵的，其他不敢奢求，她既不喜欢本王，本王放她自由，让她去找她爱的人。”
'I have gained the one thing most precious to me. I dare ask for nothing more. She does not love me, so I will set her free, to go to the man she loves.'

马车里再次传出邵月婷的声音：“你若欺骗仪王，娘绝不下车见你。”
Shao Yueting's voice came from the carriage again. 'If you deceive Prince Yi, I will never step down to see you.'

“娘，我说的是真的，您跟儿子回王府，只要让儿子看娘一眼，我一定放人。”
'Mother, I mean it. Come home to the mansion with me. Let me set eyes on you, and I will free her at once.'

“好，我随你回王府。”
'Good. I will go with you to the mansion.'
