第173章 决定不再忙退婚
## Chapter 173: Giving Up on Breaking the Engagement

两边立刻打起来，没有人再理会陈浩宇。
他松松胳膊和腿，第一次觉得朱淑真厉害挺不错，故意嘲笑道：“蔣爷，你的人根本不是对手，还是见好就收吧。”
蔣爷忍着脸上的疼痛，躲开朱淑真，生怕对方再一拳打来。
他瞧着自己带来的人没打几下被望月轩的三女七男迅速制服，只怪今日出门没看黄历，遇到硬茬，好汉不吃眼前亏，还是尽快撤退。
“女侠，我与你无冤无仇，今日若有得罪还请海涵。”
不清楚对方来历，千里寻不想将事闹大：“你在望月轩门口闹事，又对我无礼，老娘才打你，看在你是初犯，不与你计较，下次再到老娘的地盘欺负人，定不饶恕！”
“不敢不敢，没有下次。”蔣爷赔着笑脸，瞧到陈浩宇一脸得意，气不过，不由多说两句，“真想不通，女侠乃女中豪杰，为何要嫁给陈公子？他可是天天往春香楼跑，那里有他相好的。”
千里寻气不打一处来：“你哪只耳朵听说老娘要嫁给他？”
蔣爷趁机问：“女侠既然不嫁给陈公子，为何要帮他？”
千里寻十分不屑：“帮他？笑话！难道你长耳朵没听明白？你在望月轩门口闹事，老娘就得管，你们两个有什么恩怨最好到别处去解决，老娘才懒得插手管你们的破事。”
蒋爷请求道：“还请女侠放了我的人，我这就带他们离开。”
千里寻将手一挥，红豆与其他九人松开手，歹徒们迅速跑到蒋爷身边。
蒋爷拱手道：“我能不能带走陈公子？”
千里寻说：“现在还不行，我有事要问他。”
“女侠尽管问，我去陈公子必经之路上等他。”
蒋爷瞟了一眼陈浩宇，带着众人离开。
The two sides clashed at once, leaving Chen Haoyu forgotten on the ground. Flexing his sore arms and legs, he felt for the very first time that having a fierce fiancée had its perks, and jeered aloud, "Your boys are outmatched, Master Jiang! Better quit while you are ahead!"

陈浩宇一直听朱淑真与蒋爷说话，听那意思并不是为帮他，心中十分不爽。蒋爷一离开，不满道：“朱淑真，我姐是你的嫂嫂，你怎么能看着她的亲弟弟不管？况且你我还有婚约，太不够意思。”
Nursing his throbbing cheek, Master Jiang edged clear of Zhu Shuzhen lest she swing again. Watching his thugs get flattened in moments by Wangyue Pavilion's ten servants, he cursed his luck for stepping out without checking the almanac and bumping into a brick wall. A wise man knew when to cut losses.

“你被人打，我高兴都来不及，凭什么帮你？刚才出手救你，不过是想着你应该给我带来退婚的好消息。赶紧告诉老娘，是不是你家人已经去找我爹商议退婚之事？”
"Heroine, you and I have no grudge," Master Jiang called out. "If I gave offense today, please be magnanimous and forgive me."

千里寻自信应该如此，她都把朱淑真大家闺秀的形象糟践成那样，陈家退婚之心肯定坚不可摧。
Unsure who the man was and reluctant to blow things up, Qianli Xun held her stance. "You started a riot outside Wangyue Pavilion and spoke filth to my face—that is why I hit you. Since this is your first offense, I will let it slide. Pull another stunt on my turf and I will not be so lenient!"

“哼！偏不让你如意，想要退婚遥遥无期，我奶奶说了，除非你改了性子，才娶你过门。”
"Never again, never again!" Master Jiang put on a placating grin, then caught Chen Haoyu's smug smirk and could not resist a parting jab. "Beats me why a heroine like you would marry Young Master Chen. He runs to Chunxiang Brothel every single day—has a sweetheart waiting there!"

陈浩宇故意装出高兴的样子，谁让朱淑真不愿帮他。本来还想请她去聚香楼吃顿饭，感谢她到陈家闹，现在被气得一点儿兴致都没有，只想看朱淑真失落生气的样子。
Temper flaring, Qianli Xun snapped, "Which ear told you I am marrying him?!"

还真凑效，朱淑真果然不高兴。
"If you are not marrying him," Master Jiang countered at once, "then why step in to help him?"

“陈浩宇，你是不是有病？拿以前说过的话来搪塞老娘？老娘到陈府闹成那样，难道你没听说？”
"Help him? What a joke! Are your ears just for show? You picked a fight outside Wangyue Pavilion's gate, so I stepped in. Take whatever beef you two have somewhere else—I could not care less about your sordid messes!"

朱淑真生气，陈浩宇更得意：“我一进家门，青瓜追着赶着把一切都给我说了。我爹娘和奶奶是什么人？你那点儿计量，他们一眼看穿，所以退婚之事遥遥无期。”
"Then please tell your people to release my men, Heroine, and I will clear out right now."

千里寻充满担忧：“你那么得意干吗？好像你不愿退婚一样！”
Qianli Xun waved a hand. Hongdou and the other nine released their captives, who scrambled back behind Master Jiang.

“我当然不愿娶你！”想到自己的意愿，陈浩宇恢复正常，“你到我家闹，也不是没有用，我奶奶说，以前耗着，只是她想想而已，你今儿一闹，就有理由光明正大告诉你爹，你什么时候改了性子，我再什么时候娶你过门。”
Cupping his hands, Master Jiang asked, "May I take Young Master Chen with me?"

“难道你爹娘去找我爹就是为说这个？”千里寻猜测她写给父亲的信应该是被陈浩宇的爹娘截胡。
"Not yet," Qianli Xun said. "I have questions for him."

陈浩宇没有必要隐瞒：“听我奶奶说是这样，你现在要做的就是别改你的性子，这样你我的婚期便遥遥无期。”
"Ask away, Heroine. I will wait up the road on his way home." Shooting Chen Haoyu a dark glance, Master Jiang marched his gang away.

千里寻倍感失落，该做的都已做，毫无进展：“你们家人脑子是不是有问题？老娘都演成那样儿，他们怎么还不愿退婚？”
Listening to her deny she had helped him had soured Chen Haoyu's mood. The instant the thugs cleared out, he complained, "My sister is your sister-in-law, Zhu Shuzhen! How could you leave her own brother hanging—especially when we are betrothed? Cold-blooded!"

千里寻想起夏老太太提到朱晞颜逼迫结亲的原因，不等陈浩宇接话，继续问：“你奶奶说我爹逼你娶我有原因，原因到底是什么？赶紧告诉老娘。”
"Watching you get thrashed makes my day—why on earth would I help you? I only stepped out thinking you brought good news about canceling the engagement. Spit it out: did your parents go see my father to break it off?" Certain of it after shredding Zhu Shuzhen's lady-like image to ribbons, she expected nothing less than total surrender from the Chens.

陈浩宇担心，若让朱淑真知道散播谣言之人是他，恐怕以后不得安生，赶紧掩饰：“还有原因？我怎么不知道？恐怕是我奶奶随口胡说，或者是长辈们之间的陈年往事……”
"Hmph! I will not give you the satisfaction! Breaking off the match is off the table till kingdom come—Grandmother says she will only welcome you through our doors once you mend your temper!" Having meant to treat her to Juxiang Tower in thanks for storming his house, her cold shoulder killed all his goodwill; now he just wanted to watch her stew.

千里寻总觉得陈浩宇表情怪怪的：“你果真不知道？可你为何说话眼神闪烁？难道是编瞎话骗我？”
It worked like a charm—her face darkened at once. "Are you out of your mind, Chen Haoyu? Feeding me that same stale line after the ruckus I raised at Chen Manor?! Did nobody tell you what I did?"

陈浩宇稳住眼神，继续掩饰：“别把自己太当回事，你有什么值得本公子骗？该说的我已说，你也知道以后该怎么做，本公子还有事，懒得跟你瞎耽误工夫。”
Her anger only puffed him up further. "Qinggua gave me the play-by-play the second I stepped through the door! Who do you think my parents and grandmother are? They saw right through your cheap tricks! Breaking off this engagement is nowhere in sight."

陈浩宇刚要走，想到蔣爷在路上堵他，搞不好还得弄出满身伤来。
Dread pricked Qianli Xun. "Why are you grinning so smugly? Almost sounds like you do not want to break it off!"

他瞧瞧望月轩的十位下人：“你的手下能不能借我用用？”
"Of course I do not want to marry you!" Snapping back to his real goal, Chen Haoyu dropped the smirk. "Your little rampage was not totally useless, though. Before, stalling was just Grandmother's private idea; after your show today, she got an airtight excuse to tell your father outright that I will only wed you when you fix your temper."

“你想干什么？”
"Wait—your parents went to Zhu Manor to tell my father THAT?" Qianli Xun realized at once that Chen Haoyu's parents must have intercepted her letter.

“刚才那些人在路上堵我，难道你想见死不救？小心我下次见到我姐，在她面前说你坏话。”
"That is what Grandmother told me," Chen Haoyu confirmed. "All you need to do now is refuse to mend your temper, and our wedding gets stalled forever."

千里寻本不愿借，想到嫂嫂对她的弟弟上心，若是陈浩宇被打出残疾，嫂嫂肯定会伤心；若再知道她故意不帮陈浩宇，肯定会更伤心。
Deflation washed over Qianli Xun; she had pulled out every stop and ended up right back at square one. "Is your whole family touched in the head? I put on a show like that and they still refuse to cancel?!" Remembering Old Madam Xia's slip earlier, she pressed before he could reply, "Your grandmother said my father forced you to marry me for a specific reason. What reason? Spill it right now!"

千里寻即使再讨厌陈浩宇，也不好不帮：“红豆，带大家护送陈公子，只要别让刚才那帮人伤他就行。”
Terrified Zhu Shuzhen might tear him limb from limb if she ever learned he had spread those rumors, Chen Haoyu scrambled to cover. "A reason? How would I know? Grandmother was probably rambling, or talking about some old grudge between the elders..."

红豆干脆地应道：“是，小姐。”
Eyeing his twitchy face, Qianli Xun narrowed her eyes. "Really do not know? Then why are your eyes darting all over the place? Lying through your teeth?"

陈浩宇说了声“多谢”，得意洋洋，大踏步上了马车。
Steadying his gaze, Chen Haoyu bluffed hard. "Do not flatter yourself—what makes you worth lying to? I told you all I know, and you know what to do. I have places to be; no time to waste jawing with you."

红豆带领九个下人跟在马车后面负责保护陈家公子。
Turning to leave, he remembered Master Jiang waiting in ambush down the road—step out alone and he would end up beaten to a pulp. Glancing at Wangyue Pavilion's ten servants, he asked, "Can I borrow your crew for a bit?"

千里寻没有心情纠结帮助陈浩宇之事，第三次闹事又没成功，令她倍感惆怅。
"What for?"

耗着？要耗到何时去？说不定耗到最后还得嫁。
"Those thugs are waiting to jump me down the street! You going to stand by and watch me get crippled? Watch out, next time I see my sister I will tell on you!"

千里寻不得不开始思考韦嬷嬷叮嘱的话。
Qianli Xun had zero desire to lend them, yet her sister-in-law doted on this brother. If Chen Haoyu got maimed—and Chen Wenxin learned she had refused to help—her sister-in-law would be heartbroken. Loathing him as she did, she gave in. "Hongdou, take everyone and escort Young Master Chen. Just keep that gang from laying a hand on him."

她边往回走，边思考，若必须嫁到陈家，该如何自处？
"Yes, Miss!" Hongdou answered crisply.

陈浩宇在茶肆时可怜她的话回响在耳边，忽然觉得这家伙做人不怎么样，说的话倒是给她出了个主意。若必须嫁到陈家，不如各过各的，互不侵犯。
Calling out a breezy "Much obliged!" Chen Haoyu swaggered back into his carriage while Hongdou and the nine servants fell in behind to guard him.

千里寻不由嗟叹，穿越而来的感情婚姻之路实在不顺。喜欢的人嫁不了，跟不喜欢的人退个婚竟然比当初与林觉和离还不容易，不容易到仪王也帮不了。
Qianli Xun had no energy to dwell on doing him a favor; her third attempt to blow up the betrothal had fizzled, leaving a hollow ache behind. Stall? Stall until when? Drag it out long enough and she might still end up shoved into a bridal sedan.

仪王真的帮不了吗？千里寻又立刻否认，关键在于她不愿接受仪王的感情，说得更准确些，是她不能接受。
At last, she turned her mind to Matron Wei's warning. Walking back inside, she asked herself: if she truly had to marry into the Chen family, how would she live? Chen Haoyu's words from the teahouse echoed back—useless as the guy was, he had tossed out one practical blueprint: live separate lives under one roof and stay out of each other's way.

想到仪王，自然想到申简辰，自捶丸场分别，再没见过，白风来找苦荞时，捎过一次话，说王爷和郡主邀请她随时去幽林别居。
She let out a long sigh. Her romantic road since transmigrating had been pure disaster. She could not marry the man she loved, and breaking an unwanted betrothal was proving harder than her divorce from Lin Jue—so hard even Prince Yi could not untangle it.

千里寻让白风回的话模棱两可，申简辰应该能听出些意味来。
Could Prince Yi really not help? She shot that thought down on the spot. The catch was that she would not accept his feelings—or rather, she could not. Thinking of Prince Yi brought back Shen Jianchen. Since parting at the chuiwan grounds they had not met; Bai Feng had passed along one message through Kuqiao saying the prince and princess welcomed her at Youlin Retreat anytime, and Qianli Xun had sent back an evasive reply Shen Jianchen could surely read between the lines.

回到闺房，她高兴不起来，还是继续写话本，沉浸在自己编织的故事中，也许会很快忘记所有烦恼。
Back in her room, unable to shake her gloom, she turned back to her huaben, hoping losing herself in a made-up world would sweep away real troubles.

苦荞已知小姐帮陈公子处理好麻烦，并不知退婚未成。见小姐不高兴，猜出七八分。小心问：“小姐，送给老爷的书信是不是不应该给陈家老爷和夫人？”
Kuqiao knew her mistress had bailed Young Master Chen out yet had not heard the engagement was still intact; one look at her mistress's downcast face told her most of it. "Miss," she asked with care, "should I not have handed Master's letter to Master and Madam Chen?"

千里寻在书桌前坐好：“给了或不给，都已不重要，退婚之事遥遥无期，谁知道结果是个什么样。算了，以后我不再想退婚之事，该干什么干什么。”
Sitting at her desk, Qianli Xun shook her head. "Handing it over or not makes no difference now. Breaking the engagement is pushed off with no end in sight—who knows how it will turn out. Forget it. I am done chasing an annulment. From now on, I live my life as usual."

小姐变得消极，苦荞很自责，总觉得与自己没把信亲自送到老爷手中有关：“小姐，都怪我，陈公子的爹娘实在狡猾……”
Guilt tugged at Kuqiao; she blamed herself for letting the Chen parents intercept the letter. "It is all my fault, Miss! Young Master Chen's parents were so sly..."

“行了，都说跟你没关系，伺候笔墨，我要继续写话本。”
"Enough, I told you it was not your fault. Grind the ink—I am writing my huaben."

苦荞开始研磨，千里寻提起笔的一刻，努力让自己忘记无法退婚的不快，沉浸在新的故事中……
As Kuqiao ground the ink, Qianli Xun took up her brush, shoving the stalled engagement out of mind to dive into her story.

大概写过七八页纸后，红豆回来，见小姐忙着写字，怕打扰，正准备出去，被千里寻叫住：“怎么才回来？”
Seven or eight pages later, Hongdou returned. Seeing her mistress absorbed in writing, she started to slip back out when Qianli Xun called her back. "Why so long?"

红豆站住回道：“陈公子要求把他护送到正街，看在他与小姐订婚，我就没拒绝。”
"Young Master Chen asked us to escort him all the way to the main avenue," Hongdou reported. "Seeing he is betrothed to Miss, I did not refuse."

千里寻并没批评，关切地问：“那伙人可对你们动手？”
Qianli Xun did not scold her. "Did that gang try anything?"

红豆骄傲道：“他们哪敢？见我们跟着陈公子，黑胖子只好带人离开，说再找机会与陈公子算账。”
"They would not dare!" Hongdou lifted her chin with pride. "One look at us shadowing Young Master Chen, and the swarthy fat man pulled his crew back, muttering he would settle the score another day."

“你去忙吧，这里有苦荞伺候。”千里继续提笔写话本。
"Go tend to your duties. Kuqiao can wait on me here."

红豆答应着离开。
Dipping her brush once more, Qianli Xun went back to her manuscript as Hongdou withdrew.
