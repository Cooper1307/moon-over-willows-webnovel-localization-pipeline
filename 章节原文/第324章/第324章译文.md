## Chapter 324: Bidding Farewell to Prince Yi

Catching up with Nan Shuang below the wooden platform where Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian shared farewell wine, Bai Feng whispered news of Mengrong's shattered mind—while up top, Shen Jianchen quietly confessed Qianli Xun was his whole reason for living, swearing he'd return to Lin'an's Divine Willow wherever she landed.

At dawn, a massive crowd gathered outside Youlin Retreat to send Prince Yi off: Zhu Xiyan nearly kowtowing in gratitude before coaching Haoyu inside his carriage on Bianliang contacts (cautioning grimly that greedy Liang Shicheng's half-million bribe was pure money down the drain); loyal Master Jiang slipping Prince Yi a crucial warning from his wife that **Princess Consort Yi** might be behind Shuzhen's arrest; Shao Zhengye and Shao Zhize sharing bold farewell bowls of wine; Han Gu and Wen Xin vowing to quit the rotten new Judicial Office and go back to farming; and Dongfang Chenze and An Mingxuan racing up for a warm sendoff.

Three carriages rolled north—until Qiao Shouren, Madam Qian, Concubine Mi, and Wenxian intercepted them down the road. Drawing Prince Yi aside privately, Qiao Shouren finally unloaded his guilty secret: right after returning from Bianliang, Matron Zou had come begging the Qiaos to hire assassins to kill Zhu Shuzhen!

Certain at last that Mengrong—aided by Matron Zou and Xilan—had orchestrated Liang Shicheng's trap, Shen Jianchen shut down inside. Every last tie of affection between him and Mengrong snapped clean in two.
