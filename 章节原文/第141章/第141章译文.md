## Chapter 141: The Chen Family Agrees to Double the Bond

Reaching Chen Manor's main gate, Zhu Xiyan stormed straight inside—whip in one hand, bound Chen Haoyu hauled by the other.

Assuming Second Young Master had pulled some colossal disaster to get tied up by the Prefect, gatekeepers skipped bowing and stepped forward: "Hold on, Lord Prefect—talk things out calm, why tie up our Young Master?"

Zhu Xiyan snapped, "Run tell Chen Jianglin I'm here to help discipline his son!"

Not daring another word, a servant sprinted off to report.

Unfamiliar with Chen Manor's maze of paths, Zhu Xiyan followed the direction the servant had bolted.

Halfway in, Qinggua ran up blocking his way: "Lord Prefect, how did my master offend you to get you this furious—bringing a whip and tying him up?!"

"Shut up and lead the way!" Perfect—a free guide!

Qinggua glanced at his bound master: "Should I lead the way, Young Master?"

Chen Haoyu roared, "The Prefect's standing right here—why ask me?! Lead!"

Qinggua answered: "Yes, Young Master, leading now—your orders, so don't blame me later!"

Bound above the waist yet nimble below, Chen Haoyu swung a kick at Qinggua: "One more peep and I'll kick you dead!"

Tugged along by Zhu Xiyan while Qinggua hopped clear, the kick missed clean.

Letting out a cheeky "Heheh," Qinggua trotted ahead: "This way, Lord Prefect!"

Dragging Chen Haoyu deeper inside, Zhu Xiyan met Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan hurrying out with maids.

Chatting indoors when the servant burst in saying the Prefect had hauled their son home trussed up with a whip in hand, both knew in a flash their boy had stirred up giant trouble.

Both realized at once why their wild son had played angel at home lately—running home for cover after a massive screw-up!

Heart aching yet furious at seeing her son bound, Yao Qingyan joined Chen Jianglin in bowing: "Lord Prefect."

Zhu Xiyan declared, "No Prefect of Lin'an today—only the Zhu house and the Chen house! Your son committed an outrageous wrong; I brought him here so we can discipline him together and settle how he'll make amends and restore the Zhu family's face."

Chen Jianglin caught the hint at once: his in-law wanted to settle this in private, giving the Chen house full face.

"In-law." Since Zhu Xiyan preferred setting aside his official title, Chen Jianglin switched to warmer terms: "What on earth did Haoyu do to make you tie him up yourself? Tell me, and I'll tan his hide proper!"

Zhu Xiyan said, "Not out here—indoors."

"This way, In-law!"

Chen Jianglin led everyone into Chen Manor's reception hall.

Still gripping Chen Haoyu tight without sitting, Zhu Xiyan requested, "Servants get in the way—clear the room."

Yao Qingyan waved them out: "All of you, step outside."

Filing out, Qinggua cast a worried glance back at his master.

Chen Haoyu flashed Qinggua a sharp wink; catching on at once, Qinggua slipped past the door and sprinted straight for Old Madam Xia's quarters.

Alone in the hall, Zhu Xiyan shoved Chen Haoyu straight down onto the floor: "Kneel!"

Scowling, Chen Haoyu dropped to his knees—no plan cooked up during the ride, forced to wing it, sworn on his life never to marry Zhu Shuzhen.

Looking at his hopeless son filled Chen Jianglin with fury and shame, swallowing his temper before the Prefect.

"Have a seat and tell us, In-law."

All three sat, Yao Qingyan glancing to her husband to speak.

Catching her look, Chen Jianglin pressed, "Tell me straight, In-law—what crime did Haoyu commit? I'll settle accounts for you right now!"

Zhu Xiyan slapped the whip onto the table: "What crime? A monstrous one! Let him confess himself!"

Yao Qingyan demanded of her son, "What did you do to push our in-law this far?!"

Cornered with zero escape, Chen Haoyu laid it out: "I paid thugs to spread those rumors about Zhu Shuzhen..."

Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan gasped in near unison: "What?!"

Without another word, Chen Jianglin leaped up and cracked his palm across his son's cheek: "You worthless bastard! Raising hell outside wasn't enough—you targeted Miss Zhu?! Don't you know a maiden's honor is her life?! Got the whole city cursing her and wrecked her fine match with the Qiao family—you... you...!"

Blind with rage, Chen Jianglin snatched the whip off the table and brought it slashing across Chen Haoyu's back.

Chen Haoyu howled bloody murder: "Ow! Killing me! Are you even my real dad?! Zhu Shuzhen bullied me—can't I even hit back?!"

Zhu Xiyan sat watching unmoved—a spoiled brat like this needed a real thrashing.

Two more lashes landed; as Chen Jianglin raised his arm for a fourth, Yao Qingyan broke in: "Husband, watch you don't cripple our son!"

Dropping his arm with a ragged sigh, Chen Jianglin set the whip back on the table, saw his wife's reddened eyes, sat down, and buried his face in his hands.

Though his father had spared real force, Chen Haoyu hammed it up loud: "Mother! Look—Father ripped my back to shreds! Burning like fire!"

With their in-law sitting right there, Yao Qingyan refused to coddle him: "Pulling a wicked stunt like that, your father went easy on you! Can a few bruises compare to the agony Miss Zhu suffered?!"

Seeing neither parent taking his side, Chen Haoyu sulked into silence.

At last Zhu Xiyan spoke: "Even beating him to death won't ease the rage in my chest—or give my Shuzhen back her good name."

Lowering his hands, Chen Jianglin looked across at Zhu Xiyan: "Tell me, In-law—what will ease your heart?"

Right on cue! Zhu Xiyan dropped his terms: "Your son wrecked Shuzhen's match with Young Master Qiao and tarnished her name, leaving her hard-pressed to find a worthy house. If the Chen family agrees to double our bond through marriage, I'll wipe the slate clean."

Chen Jianglin perked up at once, trading a glance with Yao Qingyan; after a quiet beat, she nodded.

Chen Jianglin jumped in: "Splendid idea, In-law! The Chen family welcomes doubling our bond!"

"Done! No going back on your word—come propose at Zhu Manor seven days from today!" Refusing to let a golden deal slip, Zhu Xiyan nailed down the date on the spot.

Chen Jianglin agreed with all his heart: the cleanest fix possible, and having his wild son marry Jiangnan's celebrated poetess was a stroke of luck beyond merit!

Plus, Wenxin praised Shuzhen to the skies every visit, leaving both parents delighted to welcome her into the family.

"Rest easy, In-law—seven days it is! Every matchmaker and every betrothal gift, down to the last detail!"

Dropping his injury act in sheer panic, Chen Haoyu yelled, "Father! Mother! Zhu Shuzhen and I clash like fire and water—we'd murder each other married! Plus she's divorced and three years older than me—I refuse!"

Chen Jianglin snapped back: "Once wed under one roof you'll get along! As for divorce, even Emperor Zhenzong didn't mind—who are you to turn up your nose?! And three years older? Ever hear 'marry a wife three years older, hug a brick of solid gold'?!"

Right then Old Madam Xia shuffled in supported by a maid—spotting her grandson bound on his knees with his outer robe slashed to ribbons! Luckily thick winter padding had saved his skin.

Even so, heartbreak swept the old lady as she rushed to his side: "Does it hurt, my boy?!"

Chen Haoyu laid it on thick: "Hurts to death, Grandmother!"

Furious, Old Madam Xia barked at Qinggua: "Standing around like a post?! Untie Young Master right now!"

Qinggua darted forward to loosen the knots.

Marching up to her son, Old Madam Xia pointed a trembling finger in Chen Jianglin's face: "Unfilial wretch! Call yourself a father?! Tying your own son up and whipping him—when did your father ever beat you like this?! Hurt one hair on my grandson and I'll never forgive you!"

Stepping up to steady her elbow, Chen Jianglin scrambled to appease: "Calm down, Mother—my fault, all right? I'll never discipline him like that again! Come sit down."

Shoving him off, Old Madam Xia snapped, "I have no son like you! Strike my grandson once more and I'll strike you ten times back!"

With Old Madam Xia standing in fury, neither Chen Jianglin nor Yao Qingyan dared sit, hovering with sheepish smiles to coax her down.

Freed from his ropes, Chen Haoyu stood off to one side enjoying the show.

Seeing firsthand how Chen Haoyu got molded into a hopeless rake, Zhu Xiyan stood and offered a courteous bow: "Don't blame Jianglin, Old Madam—I tied Haoyu up myself. He made a grave mistake and had to face the music. Everything's settled now, though: the Zhus and Chens are doubling our marriage bond, so sit back and enjoy the good news!"

Only then noticing the Prefect, Old Madam Xia offered a quick nod: "Forgive an old woman not greeting Lord Prefect first—this hopeless son of mine keeps bullying my grandson until the poor boy dreads coming home! Just started staying home and he whips him again—can't stand seeing my boy happy!"

Mid-rant, "doubling our marriage bond" registered; catching her breath, she asked, "What did Lord Prefect mean by doubling our marriage bond?"

Before Zhu Xiyan could answer, Chen Haoyu stepped in to hold her arm: "Stand up for me, Grandmother! The Prefect and my parents are forcing me to marry Miss Zhu! You don't know—Miss Zhu is a total tigress! Marry her and my life turns into daily torture—please save me!"

Melted to butter by her grandson clinging to her, Old Madam Xia beamed: "Quit talking nonsense! Even if Grandmother hasn't met Miss Zhu, I know her fame, read her poems, and hear your sister rave about her—gentle, lovely, refined, and brimming with learning! Landing her makes Grandmother thrilled for you!"

Zhu Xiyan grinned wider: "Seven days from now we seal the betrothal—does Old Madam approve?"

Old Madam Xia nodded like a pellet drum: "Approve! Approve! High time Haoyu settled down—seal it fast and wed fast! Only thing I don't get is why on earth you tied my grandson up and whipped him over happy news?!"

Stepping up to take Old Madam Xia's other arm, Yao Qingyan said, "Let me walk you back to your room, Mother, and explain the whole story."

Agreeing, Old Madam Xia paused at the door to order: "Qinggua, take Young Master to change clothes and check his skin—if there's a bruise, apply salve!"

Qinggua bowed: "Right away, Old Madam!"

Once they cleared out, Chen Jianglin cupped his hands to Zhu Xiyan: "Holding no grudge and still giving your daughter to our house leaves me speechless with gratitude, In-law. Rest assured, the day Shuzhen steps into Chen Manor, we'll cherish her like our own daughter."

Joy bubbled inside Zhu Xiyan—calamity flipped into triumph: Shuzhen secured a rich house, Zhu face stood intact, and he had doubled ties with Lin'an's richest clan! Months of gloom blew clean away.

"Family doesn't talk like strangers. Time for me to head back."

Snatching his whip off the table, Zhu Xiyan strode out, Chen Jianglin escorting him all the way to the front gate.
