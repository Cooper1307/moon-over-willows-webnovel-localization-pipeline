## Chapter 301: Two Visits, Two Motives

Last time Shen Jianchen came here, he’d traded punches and left—never noticing how plush the Qiantang Gang’s headquarters actually was. Clearly Master Jiang had stacked up a small fortune.

Refusing the chief’s seat before royalty, Master Jiang stood hunched even after Prince Yi sat—only taking a chair when the prince threatened to walk out, then sliding closer at his prodding.

“You had Youqi Jushi build the bridge,” Shen Jianchen teased. “I walked across it—now you go mute?”

Master Jiang perked right up. “Never dealt with royalty before, waited for Your Highness to speak first! Truth is, my boys and I have done plenty of dirt over the years—didn’t care when I was single. Now I’ve got a wife, hoping for a bouncing son, and want my brothers settled into honest lives under solid backing. Nobody in Lin’an stands taller or straighter than Prince Yi—point us onto a clean road, Your Highness!”

Shen Jianchen had already mapped out their landing spot on the ride over. “You run half—no, probably all—of Lin’an’s gambling houses?”

“One word from Your Highness and I shutter every last one!”

“Plus the black market...”

“Shut it down today! Nothing gets past Your Highness!”

“And murder-for-hire...”

“Sworn off for good!”

Seeing genuine grit behind the bluster, Shen Jianchen laid out terms. “Murder and arson stop dead—zero exceptions.”

“Already washing my hands clean!”

“Keep the black market running—if you close it, someone worse opens one. Run by you, the yamen keeps tabs and cracks cases faster.”

“Brilliant, Your Highness! Makes catching crooks a breeze.”

“Shut half the gambling dens, and donate half the profits from the rest to support the elderly and infirm in Lazybones Village.”

Delighted he got to keep half his income, Master Jiang agreed on the spot. “And what honest post do you have in mind for us, Your Highness?”

“Lin’an’s river ferries drive commercial taxes, yet the yamen can’t police them—most of all along the Qiantang River. Since you’re the Qiantang Gang, take charge of policing the Qiantang ferries—”

Before he even finished, Master Jiang hit his knees banging his forehead to the floor. “Living bodhisattva to the Qiantang Gang!”

“Counties appoint Ferry Superintendents locally; as Military Commissioner, I hold that authority. Starting today, you’re Ferry Superintendent along the Qiantang River.”

Nearly weeping with joy, Master Jiang kowtowed again. “An official post in my lifetime—changing our whole family’s destiny! Now I can hold my head high before my future son!”

Calling for brush and paper, Shen Jianchen drafted a recommendation letter straight to Prefect Zhu. Accepting it with trembling reverence, Master Jiang pledged lifelong service—and reassured the prince nobody linked him to the black market since a trusted deputy ran it single-channel, a channel he’d already used once to tip off Lin Jue on a child trafficker.

Escorting Prince Yi out until horse and rider vanished down the street, Master Jiang muttered in awe, “Thought he came just for Youqi Jushi—turns out he’s looking out for Lin’an’s people too. I underestimated him!” Turning back grinning ear to ear, he roared for his wife—only to be reminded she’d gone out with friends—and beamed as his men hailed him “Lord Ferry Superintendent!”

Little did he know Chun Jinmo hadn’t gone to meet friends—she had driven straight to Youlin Retreat to pitch an alliance to Princess Consort Yi.

Inside Youlin Retreat, Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo were paying a polite catch-up visit to Qiao Mengrong, everyone carefully steering clear of the pamphlet mess: Mengrong didn’t want to alienate Miao Zhiyu after ratting her out to Wenyuan, while both ladies had resolved to stay out of Mengrong’s vendetta and enjoy the drama safely from the sidelines.

When a servant announced Master Jiang’s wife at the gate, Qiao Mengrong snapped, “Tell her I’m busy!”—still fuming over Master Jiang’s botched ambush.

Bai Meiruo jumped in eager. “Know who Master Jiang’s wife is, Your Highness? Bet you’ll want to see her!” Explaining Chun Jinmo was Chunxiang Brothel’s former star who lost Young Master Chen to Youqi Jushi and ended up wed to homely Master Jiang, she drew Miao Zhiyu’s smirk: “Youqi Jushi even stepped on brothel turf!”

Intrigued at once, Qiao Mengrong sent Xilan to fetch Madam Jiang in—while Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo smoothly excused themselves, trading quiet smirks as they passed the striking beauty outside.

“Pretty thing—and Chen Haoyu still ditched her for Youqi Jushi,” Miao Zhiyu murmured.

“My husband still pines for her without ever touching her!” Bai Meiruo griped as usual, before both hurried home to watch the fireworks from afar.

Led into the inner hall, Chun Jinmo trembled before a royal wife for the first time, dropping to her knees stammering. Swallowing distaste for her courtesan past, Qiao Mengrong honored her surname “Madam Jiang” and bade her sit.

“Master Jiang took my silver and botched the job,” Qiao Mengrong began coolly. “Here to apologize for him?”

“Don’t trust Master Jiang—drop him,” Chun Jinmo said bold. “I came for myself. I only married him because I had no choice; my heart’s always belonged to Young Master Chen until Youqi Jushi tore him away. I hate her just as much as Your Highness.”

“Coming to my door takes a gift. What did you bring?”

Chun Jinmo dropped one name: “Black-Fiend Tiger.”

“Who?”

“Bandit king of Mount Tianmu—kills without blinking, makes Master Jiang look like a lapdog!”

“And how do we get him?”

“Silver—and someone willing to climb Mount Tianmu in person. He’s old buddies with Master Jiang, came down for my wedding, and couldn’t keep his lecherous eyes off me—only held back because of Master Jiang. I’ll offer myself to him on Mount Tianmu to get him down here and kill Youqi Jushi.”

Half impressed, half sneering at a courtesan’s casual trade of her body, Qiao Mengrong asked, “What’s your price?”

“Once she’s dead, treat me as a friend and bring me into Lin’an’s noble ladies’ circle.”

Repulsed at the thought of introducing an ex-courtesan to high society—yet confident her royal rank would silence whispers once Zhu Shuzhen lay in a coffin—Qiao Mengrong agreed. “Get it done, and you have my word.”

A relieved smile lit Chun Jinmo’s face.
