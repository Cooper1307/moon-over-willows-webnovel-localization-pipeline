## Chapter 301: Two Visitors with Opposite Goals

Over tea inside the surprisingly lush Qiantang Gang hall, Master Jiang laid his heart bare: married now and hoping for sons, he wanted to go legit under Prince Yi's banner.

Shen Jianchen laid down crisp terms: zero murder or arson forever; keep the hidden black market quietly open under single-line control to feed the yamen criminal leads; shut down half his gambling dens and donate half the remaining profits to Lazybones Village's elderly and frail—and in return, Shen Jianchen used his Military Commissioner authority to hand Master Jiang a letter appointing him **Ferry Superintendent** overseeing all Qiantang River ferries and trade wharves!

Weeping with gratitude as he kowtowed—"A real official post for my sons' sake!"—Master Jiang marched back indoors beaming as his men hailed him *Superintendent*, itching to tell his bride—only to hear she was out visiting friends.

In fact, Chun Jinmo had ridden straight to Youlin Retreat!

Inside, Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo had been paying a careful courtesy call—all three steering clear of the failed pamphlets—when a servant announced Master Jiang's wife. Mengrong waved her off at first until Bai Meiruo spilled that she was *Chun Jinmo*, the very courtesan Chen Haoyu dumped after Youqi Jushi dragged him home!

Suddenly intrigued, Mengrong ordered Xilan to bring her in, smoothly excusing Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo (who departed relieved to watch the coming fireworks from the bleachers).

Kneeling nervously at first, Chun Jinmo cut straight to business: ditch spineless Master Jiang—she brought a deadlier gift: **Black-Fiend Tiger** of Mount Tianmu! Having caught the bandit chief's lustful eye at her wedding feast, she was willing to hike up Mount Tianmu and trade her own body to send him down to butcher Youqi Jushi—asking only one prize in return: bring her into Lin'an's noble ladies' circle!

Despising her inwardly yet desperate for results, Qiao Mengrong sealed the pact: "Pull it off, and you have my word."
