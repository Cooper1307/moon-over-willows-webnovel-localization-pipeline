## Chapter 282: Shadowing the Same Target by Chance

Back at the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen sent Wen Xin to comb the country villa and Han Gu to grill Scarface's old crew—right before last night's gate constable stepped up and placed a cloth packet of four silver taels onto his desk!

"Your Highness reads minds! Eldest Young Master really *did* sneak in last night, bribed me, and slipped into Chun Jinlan's cell—sounds like he's the one who dug her up!"

Rewarding the honest constable with the whole four taels, Shen Jianchen knew Chen Haodong was the linchpin. With Bai Feng off building the bamboo cottage in Zhuli Village, he slipped out solo, bought a plain dark robe and matching black face-cloth scrap from a street tailor, checked the silk emporium, and tracked Chen Haodong to the Chen Tea Emporium.

Just as Chen Haodong boarded a carriage outside, a familiar slender figure dressed as a flashy young scholar stepped out of the attached teahouse.

Stepping right across her path, Shen Jianchen murmured, "Dressed that loud and you expect not to tip off Chen Haodong?"

Qianli Xun shot back, "Think a cheap tunic hides *you*, Prince Yi?"

No time to bicker—they piled into his hired carriage together to tail Haodong's wheels out past the city gates.

Riding knee-to-knee for the first time since transmigration—yet separated by vows and marriages—Qianli Xun shifted cleanly back to keep polite distance and explained Matron Wei's tip from Yongtai Teahouse.

"Doing all this for Chen Haoyu?" Shen Jianchen asked quietly.

"He's my husband—fighting for him is fighting for myself."

"I'll help you. Only when you're well can I be well."

"Don't," she answered gently, hearing Mengrong's venom in her head. "Put your heart into Mengrong instead."

"About those pamphlets—I'm sorry on Mengrong's behalf."

"I never blamed her—only mourned our friendship. From now on, you're Prince Yi Zhao Pu to me; I'll keep that other name tucked safely inside my heart."

"Then I'll keep *Qianli Xun* tucked inside mine too."

They shared a soft, bittersweet smile, swapping notes on why Haodong was sabotaging Haoyu over the inheritance—while Shen Jianchen privately vowed that even if he stopped actively hunting a tree-climbing pig, if fate ever handed him one, he still wouldn't quit.

At dusk, Haodong's carriage stopped outside a quiet, unstaffed country cottage. Hiding their carriage under a spreading tree, both masked their faces, vaulted the courtyard wall, and hopped lightly onto the roof just as a Chunxiang Brothel carriage rolled up and delivered Chun Jinchan straight into Haodong's eager arms!

Blushing furious as the pair vanished indoors bantering shamelessly, Qianli Xun tried to bail—only for Shen Jianchen to urge staying in case pillow talk spilled clues. Lifting a roof tile to peek down, he caught Chen Haodong and Chun Jinchan tangled half-dressed on the bed.
