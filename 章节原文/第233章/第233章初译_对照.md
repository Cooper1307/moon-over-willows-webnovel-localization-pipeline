第233章 想反抗根本没用
## Chapter 233: Resistance Gets Him Nowhere

陈府挺大，跑上三四圈都让人受不了，何况十圈？陈浩宇和青瓜跑得气喘吁吁，浑身酸痛，加之心中不乐意，自然腿脚愈加沉重，到最后两圈时基本上是慢跑。
他们两个的速度，阿升与三宝只需跟着快走。
青瓜边跑边说：“公子，少夫人……只让您跑，能……能不能……让我歇会儿？”
陈浩宇喘着气反对道：“不……不行！我一个人跑……多丢人，有你……陪着还好些。”
青瓜不敢再言语。
跑着跑着，抬头瞧见陈浩东站在路中央，一脸笑容。
陈浩东心中着实爽快：“看到你受罚的样子，我真是太开心！不妨再给你们挑拨挑拨，让你跟母老虎的矛盾继续激化，我则在一旁看好戏。”
见到堂兄，陈浩宇像见到救命稻草一般，顿时有了力气，快跑几步，上前抱住陈浩东：“东哥！我跑了半天，陈家长辈没一个理会我，只有你最好！”
陈浩东假意关心的样子：“我一听说你的事就去找奶奶，可奶奶院子的大门紧闭，大伯和大婶子的院门也紧闭着，根本见不到人。其他长辈又不敢违拗老太太的意思，我便在这里等你，希望能帮你出出主意。”
阿升与三宝不好阻拦，站在一旁等待。青瓜觉得甚好，正好趁此机会休息休息。
陈浩宇放开东哥：“快帮我出出主意，怎么样才能避免被母老虎惩罚？”
陈浩东先不着急出主意，反而对浩宇脸上的伤颇感兴趣：“你脸上的伤是怎么回事？”
陈浩宇回道：“还不是被母老虎打的？”
陈浩东心中更乐：“打吧！打得遍体鳞伤才好！”嘴上则说：“等着，我先过去训斥他们两句。”
Chen Manor sprawled wide enough that three or four laps wrecked an untrained runner, let alone ten. By the final two laps, Chen Haoyu and Qinggua were reduced to a wheezing shuffle, forcing Asheng and Sanbao to slow to a brisk walk just to stay alongside.

陈浩东走到阿升和三宝面前，训斥道：“你们两个有眼无珠的下人，竟然敢公开欺负陈家的二公子，谁给你们这么大的胆量？赶明儿我回禀老太太，将你们两个卖到乡下去，永远别回临安城！”
"Young Master," Qinggua gasped, "Young Madam only... made you run... let me catch my breath..."

三宝想强辩，被阿升阻止。只见阿升心平气和解释道：“大公子恐怕不知，我家小姐也是得到老太太、太太和大老爷的同意管教姑爷，才让小的前来负责监督。”
"No... way!" Chen Haoyu panted. "Too embarrassing... running solo... you suffer with me!"

陈浩东故意生气道：“你们两个下人给我靠边站，乖乖在这里等着，我们兄弟到旁边的亭子里坐会儿，弟妹若有意见，让她来找我。”
Qinggua shut up and shuffled on.

陈浩东拉起陈浩宇往旁边的亭子走去。
Rounding a bend, Chen Haoyu looked up to find Chen Haodong standing right in the middle of the lane wearing a broad smile.

陈浩宇竖起大拇指：“东哥威武！”
Inside, Chen Haodong was walking on air: Watching you suffer makes my day! Time to pour a little oil on the fire between you and the tigress and sit back for the show!

陈浩东边走边说：“你啊，丢尽咱们陈家男人的脸，到那边坐坐，我给你好好传授一下如何管教自家娘子。”
Spotting his cousin felt like grabbing a lifeline. Finding a fresh burst of wind, Chen Haoyu jogged up and threw an arm around him. "Brother Dong! Been running for hours and not one elder lifts a finger for me—you're the only one who cares!"

两人来到亭子坐下后，陈浩宇叹口气：“唉，并非我要给陈家男人丢脸，怪只怪朱淑真太厉害，她手底下的人也厉害，就那两个，我既打不过，也跑不过，只好认栽，装作老实的样子。”
Putting on a solemn show of sympathy, Chen Haodong sighed. "The moment I heard, I hurried to Grandmother's, yet her gate was bolted tight, and Eldest Uncle and Aunt's too! Nobody else dares cross Old Madam Xia, so I waited out here to help you brainstorm."

陈浩东故意煽风点火：“你要是男人，就不能认栽，要努力反抗。弟妹再厉害，她也不敢伤你，更不敢危及你的性命。动动你的脑子好好想想，奶奶和婶子敢放开手不理会，肯定是因为拿准了她不会伤你。既如此你怕什么？大胆反抗便是。”
Asheng and Sanbao halted respectfully to wait, while Qinggua collapsed gratefully onto a stone to catch his breath.

陈浩宇立即醒悟过来：“对啊！我怎么没有想到？还是东哥厉害，难怪大嫂知书达理又温柔。剩下的一圈我不跑了，现在就回去跟朱淑真对着干！”
Pulling back, Chen Haoyu pleaded, "Give me a plan! How do I dodge the tigress's punishments?"

陈浩东夸道：“这就对了！”
Instead of answering right away, Chen Haodong pointed at his cheek. "Where'd that bruise come from?"

看着堂弟自信满满离开，陈浩东心中乐道：“回去好好跟你娘子对着干，你大哥我才有好戏看。”
"The tigress punched me!"

青瓜见公子回来，迎上去：“公子，您也歇息够，还有一圈，我陪您继续跑。”
Serve you right—hope she beats you black and blue! Chen Haodong cheered inside, saying aloud, "Hang tight while I put these lackeys in their place."

“谁说本公子要跑？”陈浩宇发问道，指着阿升和三宝，“你们两个听着，剩下的一圈本公子不会跑，现在就回墨香苑，看朱淑真能把我怎样？”
Striding over to Asheng and Sanbao, he barked, "Blind, insolent servants—daring to humiliate Second Young Master in broad daylight! Who gave you the gall? Tomorrow I'll report to Old Madam Xia and have you sold off to the countryside for good!"

阿升和三宝毕竟是下人，不敢过分违抗姑爷，只好无奈地跟在后面。
Sanbao bristled to argue, yet Asheng laid a calm hand on his arm and bowed. "Forgive us, Eldest Young Master. Our mistress received full permission from Old Madam Xia, Eldest Master, and Eldest Madam to discipline Second Young Master, and ordered us to supervise."

阿升谨慎提醒道：“姑爷，您不按照要求完成，可是要倒马桶的。”
Feigning wrath, Chen Haodong snapped, "Step aside and stay put while my cousin and I sit in that pavilion! If Sister-in-law has a problem, send her to me!" Taking Haoyu's arm, he steered him toward the pavilion.

陈浩宇站住，对着阿升骂道：“倒你个头！本公子就是不倒！朱淑真又能将我如何？”
"Brother Dong, you're a legend!" Chen Haoyu flashed a thumbs-up.

阿升更无奈地继续跟着往回走。
"You're embarrassing every man in the Chen clan," Chen Haodong chided as they walked. "Sit down and let me teach you how a man keeps his wife in line."

青瓜凑到公子身边，边走边小声问：“大公子说了什么？您怎么突然变得大胆起来？”
Settling onto stone stools, Chen Haoyu sighed. "Not like I want to lose face, Brother Dong—Zhu Shuzhen is a beast, and her servants are beasts too! Can't outfight or outrun those two, so I had to play along."

陈浩宇笑着回道：“金玉良言。”
Chen Haodong leaned in to fan the flames. "A real man never rolls over—fight back! Tough as Sister-in-law is, she won't dare cripple you or endanger your life. Use your head: why did Grandmother and Aunt step back without lifting a finger? Because they know for a fact she won't actually hurt you! So what are you scared of? Push right back!"

回到墨香苑，院子里并排站着三个丫鬟。陈浩宇有印象，以前他调戏过一回，记得应该是三叔院中的，可为何站在自己院子？他不甚理解，也懒得理会。
Light flared across Chen Haoyu's face. "Brilliant! How didn't I see that?! No wonder Sister-in-law Anya is so gentle and obedient under you! Forget the last lap—I'm marching straight back to Moxiang Courtyard to go head-to-head with Zhu Shuzhen!"

正要往后院拐去，一个丫鬟堵在前面：“姑爷，小姐说了，您若跑完步，先向这三个丫鬟道歉，然后背诵《论语》和王右丞的诗，再然后负责打扫小姐的闺房。”
"That's the spirit!" Chen Haodong clapped his shoulder, grinning silently as his cousin swaggered off: Go stir up a war with your wife so I get front-row seats!

一连串要求，陈浩宇刚要发作，想到朱淑真不会伤他，也不会要他的命，立刻变得轻松：“你叫什么名字？我怎么从来没见过？”
Seeing his master strut back, Qinggua hurried up. "Rested up, Young Master? Just one lap left—I'll finish it with you."

丫鬟说：“我叫阿美，帮姑爷打过蒋爷的手下，也跟着小姐去过青楼带姑爷回来，姑爷您从来没注意过我。再说，自从小姐嫁过来，姑爷整日不着家，对我没印象倒也正常。”
"Who said I'm running?" Chen Haoyu jabbed a finger at Asheng and Sanbao. "Listen up! I'm skipping the final lap and heading straight back to Moxiang Courtyard! Let's see what Zhu Shuzhen does about it!"

“原来是阿美。”陈浩宇指着身后的阿升，“你小子的妹妹，你们兄妹两个想管本公子，没门儿。告诉朱淑真，我不怕她，她想让我干什么，我偏不干！”
Unwilling to rough up their young master, Asheng and Sanbao fell in helplessly behind him. "Young Master," Asheng cautioned, "skipping tasks means emptying the chamber pot."

陈浩宇说完，正准备回博雅斋，被千里寻的声音拦住：“陈浩宇！你该不会是想给老娘倒马桶？没想到你的爱好满独特。”
Chen Haoyu spun around barking, "Empty my foot! Not doing it! Let's see what she dares do!"

陈浩宇站住，面向朱淑真：“本公子再强调一遍，你让我做的事，我一概不做，倒马桶我更不会做。”
Resigned, Asheng trailed along while Qinggua whispered beside his master, "What did Eldest Young Master tell you to put all that fire in your belly?"

千里寻迅速上前将陈浩宇的胳膊拧到背后：“做不做？”
"Pure golden wisdom," Chen Haoyu boasted.

陈浩宇感受到一阵疼痛，“哎吆”叫唤着：“不做就是不做，反正你不会伤我，也不会要我的命，我什么都不怕！看你能把我怎样！”
Stepping into Moxiang Courtyard, Chen Haoyu saw three maids standing in a neat line—familiar faces from Third Uncle's courtyard whom he had flirted with once. Wondering why they stood here yet not caring, he turned toward the back courtyard—until a maid stepped right across his path.

对方识破她的底线，倒让千里寻措手不及，她一把推开陈浩宇：“你身上可是一文钱都没有，难道不想赚银子？”
"Young Master, Miss says once your laps are done, first apologize to these three maids, then memorize the *Analects* and Wang Wei's poems, and after that dust Miss's room."

陈浩宇站稳后，揉揉胳膊：“哼！反正出不去，吃住都在自己家里，没有需要使银子的地方，不赚也罢。”
About to explode, Chen Haoyu remembered she dared not hurt him and relaxed into a smirk. "What's your name? Never seen you around."

千里寻努力吓唬：“那我就永远把你关在墨香苑。”
"I'm Amei. Helped fight off Master Jiang's thugs for you and went with Miss to Chunxiang Brothel to haul you home—you just never noticed me. Hardly surprising since you've stayed out of the manor ever since the wedding."

陈浩宇为拿捏住对方而窃喜：“哼！大不了本公子当个宅男，永远不出去。反正该玩儿的都玩儿够，该吃的都吃过，该见的也都见过，就是没体会过当宅男的感觉，正好趁此机会体验一回。”
"Ah, Amei!" He jabbed a thumb at Asheng behind him. "Your brother's shadow! Think you two siblings can boss me around? Tell Zhu Shuzhen I'm not scared of her—whatever she tells me to do, I'm doing the exact opposite!"

对方死猪不怕开水烫，稳稳拿捏住她的底线，千里寻想了一会儿，终于想到致命的一招。
Turning toward Boya Study, he froze at Qianli Xun's voice floating from the porch. "Chen Haoyu! Eager to empty my chamber pot? Didn't peg you for that kind of hobby."

“你要是不遵照今日写下的条款，我只好差人做一个木牌，上面写着‘我是大混蛋’，然后挂在你的脖子上，再将你五花大绑，由我的下人带着你先在陈府游一遍，再到临安城日日游街。不知你是愿意按照条款来执行，还是愿意被人拉出去游街？”
He spun to face her. "I'll say it one last time: I'm not lifting a finger for any of your chores, chamber pot included!"

陈浩宇被气得炸毛，指着朱淑真：“你……你……，我被拉去游街，对你有什么好处？我可是你名义上的夫君！”
In a flash Qianli Xun stepped in and pinned his arm behind his back. "Doing them or not?"

对方越生气，千里寻越高兴：“都说名义上的，你游街丢的是你的人，跟我有什么关系？只要能报了散播谣言之仇，我就开心！再说自从我嫁过来，你日日待在春香楼，何曾考虑过我是你的娘子？我又何必考虑你是我的夫君？”
Wincing hard, he yelled, "Not doing them! You won't cripple me and you won't kill me—I'm not scared of anything you throw at me!"

陈浩宇又找到调侃的点，装出疑惑的表情：“你该不会喜欢上我了吧？本公子这么优秀，你喜欢上我倒也正常……”
Caught off guard that he had called her bluff, Qianli Xun shoved him clear. "Not a copper in your pocket—don't you want to earn silver?"

“你哪只耳朵听到老娘喜欢你？老娘只喜欢折磨你！”千里寻两句话将陈浩宇的调侃打回去，立刻命令道，“阿升，找个木牌来！”
Rubbing his shoulder, Chen Haoyu sneered. "Locked in here with free food and board, what do I need silver for? Keep it!"

陈浩宇大声喊道：“朱淑真！你来劲儿了是吧？”
"Then I lock you in Moxiang Courtyard forever!"

千里寻走到陈浩宇跟前：“老娘有使不完的劲儿，怎么？还想吃一拳？”
Gleeful at reading her hand, he crowed, "Great! I'll become a homebody! Seen everything, eaten everything, played everything—only thing I haven't tried is staying home all day! Perfect chance!"

没想到跟朱淑真打几个回合的口水仗，被她重重击打在要害。东哥出的主意，一点用也没有。再说这女人是说到做到的主儿，若真把他拉出去游街，以后哪还有脸面见人？不如认输。
Faced with shameless immunity, Qianli Xun paused a beat—and found his true Achilles' heel.

“得得得！不必挂牌子，本公子听你的就是，谁让本公子散播你的谣言犯下大错，就当是欠你的。”
"Break today's rules, and I'll have a wooden signboard carved reading 'I Am a Giant Scumbag,' hang it around your neck, truss you up in ropes, and have my servants parade you first through every courtyard in Chen Manor, then up and down every street in Lin'an daily! Your choice: follow the rules, or go on tour."

“早这么想不就结了？”千里寻面向阿美说，“阿美，带陈浩宇向三个丫鬟道歉。”
Chen Haoyu's hair nearly stood on end. "Y-you... how does parading me through town help you?! I'm your husband on paper!"

阿美来到姑爷面前：“姑爷，这边请。”
The madder he got, the wider Qianli Xun beamed. "Keyword: on paper! You lose face, not me—so long as I get payback for last year's slander, I'm thrilled! Hanging around Chunxiang Brothel every night since our wedding, did you spare one thought for me being your wife? Why would I spare one for you?"

陈浩宇满心不乐意，又无可奈何。心想，朱淑真一定是自己的克星，早知今日，当初就不该娶她。想起蒋爷在妓院里说的话，一心认为是朱晞颜设计害他，转过头说：“朱淑真，你爹就是一只老狐狸，害得我好惨。”
Grabbing at his favorite needle, he feigned surprise. "Wait—did you fall for me? Makes sense, stellar catch like me—"

千里寻不明白陈浩宇所言，并未当回事：“少废话！赶紧道歉！”
"Which ear heard me say I like you? I only like torturing you!" Cutting him off flat, she barked, "Asheng, go fetch a wooden board!"

陈浩宇来到三个丫鬟面前，把她们上上下下瞧一遍，问道：“我不就是以前摸过你们的手和脸蛋儿吗？这都得让本公子道歉？老老实实告诉本公子，怎么被母老虎揪出来的？”
"Zhu Shuzhen! You're pushing it!"

一个丫鬟说：“二少夫人身边的红豆对我们说，二公子您打算向您欺负过的下人道歉，我们听说后，都觉得好奇，就赶着过来试试。”
Stepping right into his space, she flexed her knuckles. "Got plenty of energy left—want another punch?"

陈浩宇质问道：“你们是好奇，本公子可遭罪，要给你们这些下人道歉，成何体统？”
Crushed across every round, Chen Haoyu cursed Brother Dong's useless advice. This woman never bluffed; let her drag him through Lin'an wearing a scumbag sign and his life was over. He threw up both hands. "Fine, fine, put the board away! I'll listen! Spreading those rumors was my fault—consider it paying my debt!"

一旁的阿美提醒道：“姑爷，快些道歉。”
"Saved us both time if you'd said that first." Qianli Xun nodded to Amei. "Take Chen Haoyu to apologize to the three maids."

“要你提醒！多嘴！”陈浩宇扭头瞅一眼朱淑真，对方很不屑地盯着他，只好硬着头皮说，“对不起。”
"Right this way, Young Master," Amei gestured.

三个丫鬟第一次听主人家给自己说“对不起”，都觉得好玩儿，不由笑起来。
Fuming yet cornered, convinced Zhu Shuzhen was his nemesis—and recalling Master Jiang's lie at the brothel—he shot back over his shoulder, "Your father's a sly old fox, Zhu Shuzhen! Screwed me over good!"

阿美继续提醒：“只说一声不行，共三个人，还差两声。”
Clueless what he meant, Qianli Xun barked, "Quit stalling and apologize!"

陈浩宇只好又加两声：“对不起，对不起。”
Stopping before the three maids, Chen Haoyu eyed them up and down. "All I did was pinch your hands and cheeks once—and I have to apologize for that? Be honest, how'd the tigress dig you three up?"

三个丫鬟继续笑着，其中一个说：“二公子，您以后可别再对我们这些丫鬟无礼，否则您以后有说不完的‘对不起’。”
"Hongdou told us Second Young Master wanted to apologize to servants he mistreated," one maid answered. "Curiosity got the better of us, so we came to see!"

三个丫鬟满意离开。
"Your curiosity is my misery! A master apologizing to maids—where's the decency?"

陈浩宇转过身：“朱淑真，你可满意？”
"Apologize already, Young Master," Amei nudged.

千里寻回道：“一切才刚刚开始，离我满意还尚早。”
"Shut up! Nobody asked you!" Glancing back at Zhu Shuzhen's icy glare, he bit the bullet and muttered, "Sorry."

青瓜很羡慕三个丫鬟有幸接受公子当面道歉，他来到少夫人面前：“少夫人，我能不能也加入被道歉的队伍？”
Never having heard a master apologize before, the three maids giggled behind their sleeves.

千里寻说：“当然，你经常被陈浩宇欺负，他欠你很多‘对不起’。”
"One won't cut it," Amei reminded him. "Three people—two more to go."

青瓜很开心。
"Sorry. Sorry!" he ground out.

自己身边的人毫不避讳要求加入被道歉的队伍，陈浩宇既感到面上无光，也气得不行，冲上去就是一脚。结果没踢着，因用力过猛，摔倒在地上。
Still giggling, one maid piped up, "Don't get handsy with maids anymore, Second Young Master, or you'll never run out of 'sorrys' to say!" Satisfied, the three trotted off.

青瓜顺利躲开公子的一脚，见公子倒地，赶紧上前搀扶：“公子怎么还踢我？”
Chen Haoyu spun back. "Happy now, Zhu Shuzhen?"

陈浩宇趁机抓住青瓜的衣领：“谁都可以要求我道歉！就你不行！”
"Just getting warmed up—nowhere near happy yet."

青瓜强辩道：“少夫人已经答应。”
Envious of the three maids, Qinggua stepped right up to Qianli Xun. "Young Madam, can I join the apology line too?"

陈浩宇把衣领抓得更紧，命令道：“你是我的人！只准听我的！”
"Of course!" Qianli Xun smiled. "He bullies you nonstop—he owes you dozens of 'sorrys'!"

青瓜勉强地点点头。
Qinggua beamed—and Chen Haoyu lost his cool completely. Lunging forward with a furious kick at his own bold servant, he swung so hard on empty air as Qinggua sidestepped that his legs flew out from under him and he hit the flagstones flat on his backside.

千里寻懒得理会主仆二人，转身进屋去了。
"Still trying to kick me, Young Master?" Qinggua hurried to help him up.

苦荞给阿升使使眼色，也进屋去了。
Chen Haoyu snatched Qinggua's collar. "Anyone else can ask for an apology—never you!"

陈浩宇一把推开青瓜，想要努力爬起来。青瓜见状，上前搀扶公子。
"Young Madam already said yes!"

阿升说：“姑爷，请回博雅斋完成背书任务。”
"You belong to me! You listen to me alone!" he hissed, tightening his grip.

陈浩宇起身站稳后，举起拳头，在阿升和三宝面前晃晃：“你们两个阴魂不散，总有一日，让你们倒在我的拳头之下。”
Qinggua nodded reluctantly.

说归说，斗不过朱淑真，还得乖乖背书。可一腔怨气需找人发泄，青瓜又成为出气筒。
Ignoring the comedy act, Qianli Xun turned and went indoors, Kuqiao signaling Asheng before following.

“青瓜，走我前面。”
Shoving Qinggua off, Chen Haoyu clambered up with Qinggua's help as Asheng reminded him, "Back to Boya Study for your memorization task, Young Master."

“为何？”
Brushing off his knees, Chen Haoyu shook a fist at Asheng and Sanbao. "Pair of ghosts haunting my shadow—one day I'll flatten you both!" Still, beaten for now, he had to study—and needed an outlet for his bile. "Qinggua, walk in front of me."

“让你走前面就走前面，那么多废话干什么？”
"Why?"

青瓜猜不透公子的想法，乖乖走到前面。
"Less mouth, more walking!"

陈浩宇瞅准机会，将力气集中在右脚，使劲向青瓜的屁股踢去：“去你的！朱淑真！”
Unsuspecting, Qinggua stepped ahead. Gathering every ounce of steam in his right leg, Chen Haoyu booted him square in the rear: "Take that, Zhu Shuzhen!"
