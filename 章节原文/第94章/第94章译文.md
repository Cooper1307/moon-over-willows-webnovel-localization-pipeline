## Chapter 94: Two Willow Leaves

Once Shuzhen's carriage had vanished from sight, Qiao Wenxian turned around and saw Bai Feng standing by the gate, gazing off into the distance. He thought nothing of it.

"What is Prince Yi doing?"

With Young Master Qiao asking, Bai Feng could not keep craning his neck. "His Highness is inside his room."

"Come with me to see Prince Yi."

Bai Feng stole one last glance down the road before following Qiao Wenxian back into the retreat. When they entered the chamber in the main courtyard, Prince Yi was sitting in a chair in a daze, breaking into a smile every now and then.

Qiao Wenxian stepped closer and waved a hand in front of Prince Yi's eyes, yet Prince Yi did not react at all.

Qiao Wenxian turned his head to ask Bai Feng, "What's wrong with Prince Yi?"

Bai Feng shook his head.

Shen Jianchen was lost in savoring the memory of Qianli Xun coming to ask him for a favor, above all the moment he steadied her and she bolted in a panic. Startled to notice someone standing right in front of him, he snapped back to his senses. "Oh, Wenyuan. What brings you here?"

Qiao Wenxian picked a chair and sat down. "What was Prince Yi thinking about so hard?"

"Nothing."

Facing Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen felt a rush of guilt rise in his chest. If Wenyuan ever found out they were in love with the same woman, what would he think?

Before long, Shen Jianchen came up with a neat excuse for himself: Wenyuan loved Zhu Shuzhen, while he loved Qianli Xun.

Yet thinking it through, the Zhu Shuzhen standing here today was Qianli Xun, and Qianli Xun was Zhu Shuzhen. There was no telling them apart, just as he could no longer tell whether Shen Jianchen was Zhao Pu, or Zhao Pu was Shen Jianchen.

Qiao Wenxian said, "I'd like to ask Your Highness to come back to Qiao Manor with me tomorrow."

"Back to Qiao Manor?" Shen Jianchen guessed at once that Wenyuan wanted him to persuade the Qiao family elders to send a marriage proposal to Zhu Manor.

In an earnest tone, Qiao Wenxian said, "I want to marry Shuzhen soon, and I'm hoping Prince Yi will help me convince my father."

Deep down, Shen Jianchen hated the idea, yet reality left him no choice but to agree. For one thing, he had already volunteered to help Wenyuan. For another, he and Qianli Xun had no chance anyway. Never mind His Majesty's marriage decree standing in the way, Qianli Xun's head-over-heels devotion to Wenyuan was enough to stop Shen Jianchen in his tracks.

Still, the thought of Qianli Xun becoming another man's wife was more than Shen Jianchen could bear. What was he supposed to do? He sat torn in two.

Qiao Wenxian looked worried. "Prince Yi isn't thinking of going back on his word, is he?"

Shen Jianchen longed to take it back, yet the thought of his engagement to Mengrong caught the words in his throat, and he forced them back down with an ache in his chest.

Bound by an engagement he could not break, how could he ever bring Qianli Xun happiness? Better to help her and Wenyuan be together. As long as she was happy, that would be enough.

"How could I go back on words I've already spoken? You have my word—I'll go back to Qiao Manor with you tomorrow."

Qiao Wenxian shot to his feet in excitement and threw his arms around Prince Yi. "I knew you were the best!"

Shen Jianchen could not muster a shred of cheer, heavy with sorrow that he had no right to take Qianli Xun away from Wenyuan.

"All right, all right, let go of me! Both you and Youqi Jushi come running to me for favors. Here I am, a dignified royal prince, reduced to your personal errand boy to be ordered around. It's beneath my station! I've made up my mind: from now on, no matter what favors you two ask of me, the answer is a flat no."

Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi with a laugh. "Don't worry. After this, I won't bother you again."

"You said it—don't you dare break your word!" Shen Jianchen forced a smile onto his face.

Qiao Wenxian studied Prince Yi's face. "You look out of sorts. What's going on?"

"Both you and Youqi Jushi keep pestering me for favors. It's driving me nuts. How could I be in a good mood?" Shen Jianchen stood up and pushed Wenyuan toward the door. "Quit asking questions, get back to your own room, and give me some peace and quiet."

Shoved out the door, Qiao Wenxian cupped his hands in salute. "My thanks, Prince Yi. On Qixi Festival, Shuzhen wants to treat you to a storytelling show. It ought to be one of Qianli Xun's huaben stories—you're sure to love it."

Another chance to see her filled Shen Jianchen with eager anticipation. "Tell her she's not allowed to back out."

"Shuzhen never breaks her word." A puzzled look crossed Qiao Wenxian's face. "There's one thing I don't understand, though. Why did you stop having Bai Feng search for Qianli Xun? Has Prince Yi found him already?"

Shen Jianchen had promised Qianli Xun to keep it a secret, so of course he would not tell Wenyuan. This secret was the only thing he and Qianli Xun shared. How could he share it with anyone else?

"I already told you to leave me alone! Why are you still jabbering?"

Prince Yi had already agreed to help, so there was no need to keep pestering him. If he wanted some peace and quiet, better to leave him be. Qiao Wenxian smiled, turned, and left the main courtyard.

After Wenyuan left, Shen Jianchen walked out into the courtyard and stood on the exact spot where Qianli Xun had stood when she asked for his help. As if her scent still lingered there, he closed his eyes and breathed it in for a quiet moment...

Once he had his fill, he opened his eyes and found Bai Feng standing beside him, looking as if he had something to say.

"You need something?"

"Your Highness, I... I like Kuqiao?"

"Youqi Jushi's personal maid?"

"Yes. Could Your Highness ask for her hand on my behalf?"

Shen Jianchen chided him: "Two people have already come begging favors of me today, and now you too?"

"I'm afraid Youqi Jushi might marry Kuqiao off to someone else." Bai Feng looked downright aggrieved.

Shen Jianchen reassured him. "It's still too soon to ask for her hand. Once the time is right, I'll handle it for you. And don't fret—Youqi Jushi is open-minded. She'd never marry off her own maid without asking what the girl wants. From what I've seen, that maid Kuqiao has feelings for you too, so she won't end up with anyone else."

Bai Feng pleaded, "You have to promise me, Your Highness. As soon as you and the Princess are married, you'll ask for her hand on my behalf."

At the mention of his marriage to Mengrong, gloom settled back over Shen Jianchen's face. He let out a sigh. "Ah, eight or nine things out of ten in life never go the way we want. Fine, you have my promise."

Bai Feng could not fathom what life's disappointments had to do with asking for Kuqiao's hand for him, though as long as His Highness agreed, he was thrilled. "Thank you, Your Highness!"

"Come with me to see the Divine Willow." Shen Jianchen wanted to sit in peace before the Divine Willow and think things through.

"Yes, Your Highness."

On the way to the Divine Willow, Bai Feng kept wondering why His Highness always loved sitting beneath it. He could not see anything special about that thick-trunked willow. Aside from a messy tangle of red silk ribbons hanging all over its branches, it looked plain as dirt. Even if it had stood for a thousand years, it was still just a tree.

Arriving beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen sat back down on the boulder and leaned against the trunk. The chill in the air felt much sharper than before; it was the Beginning of Autumn, after all.

Autumn had only just arrived, so the Divine Willow's leaves showed no sign of yellowing yet. Still, he could picture how, before long, the whole canopy of green would wither, turn yellow, and fall away.

If only his love for Qianli Xun could wither and fall away like those leaves! Then he would not hurt like this; he could accept everything this new destiny had arranged for him with a willing heart.

Quite the opposite: the more out of reach Qianli Xun remained, the fiercer his love for her burned, and the harder it became to let go. On top of that, he had to swallow the pain and help Wenyuan win her. How many people could ever fathom what he was going through!

In silence, Shen Jianchen spoke to the Divine Willow in his mind: "Divine Willow, you helped me find Qianli Xun, yet she fell in love with Wenyuan, and I'm tied down by an engagement. Even so, I still love her, more than I ever have. What should I do? Should I try every means I have to make her love me and be with me, or should I let go right here and wish each other well? If it's the first, please drop one leaf. If it's the second, please drop two."

Shen Jianchen held out his open palm, waiting in hope for a leaf to drift into his hand...

Standing not far off, Bai Feng found it bizarre to see Prince Yi holding out his palm and muttered to himself, "Has His Highness lost his mind? Why is he sticking his hand out? Does he expect the Divine Willow to drop gold ingots? The Divine Willow isn't a money tree, and His Highness isn't short on cash either. What on earth is going on?"

Bai Feng watched a while longer. Just as Prince Yi's arm grew sore from holding it out and he was about to pull it back, a single willow leaf drifted down and landed smack in the middle of his palm.

Prince Yi sprang to his feet in delight and shouted, "One! Just one! So you're on my side too, telling me to fight to be with her! Wonderful!"

Shen Jianchen pinched the willow leaf between his fingers and studied it from every angle, as if he had just found a priceless treasure.

Just then, Bai Feng saw another leaf drift down and land right on Prince Yi's shoulder. "Your Highness, another one fell—right on your shoulder."

Bai Feng's voice acted like a mighty broom, sweeping away the burst of joy that had just reached Shen Jianchen. Refusing to accept it, he snapped, "Nonsense! There was only one! What second leaf?!"

Bai Feng stepped over, plucked the second leaf off his master's shoulder, and held it out in front of him. "Here, look for yourself, Your Highness."

Staring at the second leaf he had prayed not to see, Shen Jianchen pinched it from Bai Feng's fingers in bitter reluctance, a crushing weight sinking over his chest.

"Let go... let go... So you're telling me to let go..." Shen Jianchen murmured to himself, raw pain etched across his face.

Bai Feng froze in alarm. It was just one extra willow leaf; why did Prince Yi look on the verge of tears, with both eyes rimmed red?

"Your Highness, did I do something wrong?"

Shen Jianchen said nothing. He stumbled back along the path as the two willow leaves slipped from his fingers and drifted down beneath the tree. Walking on, he murmured to himself, "Fine. Since you tell me to let go, I'll let go. May we each find our own peace."

Bai Feng trailed after him, not daring to utter a single word.
