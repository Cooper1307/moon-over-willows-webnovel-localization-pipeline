### 1. 术语一致性 (18/20)
**优点**:
- 核心术语翻译准确且一致："醒酒汤"→"hangover soup"、"女德"→"feminine virtue"、"断片"→"blacked out"

- 《清平乐·夏日游湖》译为"Qing Ping Yue: Summer Day on the Lake"符合术语表

**问题**:
- "郡主"译为"princess"不够准确。乔梦容是郡主身份，但"princess"通常指皇帝女儿或王妃，建议改为"young lady"或直接使用"her ladyship"

**修订建议**:
- "put the cloak on the princess" → "drape the cloak over her ladyship"
- "Madam wants you" → "Madam Zhu wants you"

### 2. 文化适应 (17/20)
**优点**:
- "猪狗不如"译为"worse than a pig or a dog"保留了原文的生动比喻
- "造化弄人"译为"fate played tricks on people"自然地道
- "两情相悦"译为"in love"简洁准确
- 网络小说元素("女主吐男主一身")翻译保留了现代感

**问题**:
- "大家闺秀"译为"a young lady from a good family"略显冗长，可优化为"a proper young lady"
- "有失女德"译为"lost your feminine virtue"可以更口语化为"scandalous behavior"

**修订建议**:
- "You're a young lady from a good family" → "You're a proper young lady"
- "people will say you've lost your feminine virtue" → "people will talk about your scandalous behavior"

### 3. 句式优化 (18/20)

- 对话简洁明快，口语化程度高

**问题**:
- "After barely managing to come back to life in ancient times in someone else's body, her way of thinking would naturally change"句子过长，可拆分
- "She had blacked out from drinking"可以简化为"She'd blacked out"

**修订建议**:

- "She'd blacked out"

**修订建议**:
