# 第74章 是否无奈地生活

---

已接近凌晨，申简辰一天之内第二次来到神柳下。周围寂静得可怕，他毫无感觉，内心纠结对千里寻的情感，根本无法感受其他情绪。

It was nearly dawn. Shen Jianchen came to the Divine Willow for the second time that day. The surroundings were eerily silent, but he felt nothing. His heart was tangled with emotions for Qianli Xun—he simply couldn't feel anything else.

---

月亮早已变换位置，正好在神柳的枝头，显得寂静而又孤独，如同申简辰此刻的心情一般。

The moon had long since shifted position, now hanging right above the branches of the Divine Willow. It looked quiet and lonely, just like Shen Jianchen's mood at that moment.

---

他为寻找千里寻来到临安，好不容易找到，她却已倒在他人的怀抱。

He had come to Lin'an to find Qianli Xun. After all that trouble, she had already fallen into someone else's arms.

---

穿越前的千里寻因不愿涉足爱情屡屡拒绝他的表白，可为何她穿越而来这么快接受文渊的感情？

Before the transmigration, Qianli Xun had repeatedly rejected his confessions because she didn't want to get involved in love. But why, after transmigrating, did she accept Wenyuan's feelings so quickly?

---

申简辰自问着，也许是问题的力量太过强大，脱口而出："她为何不等我？为何不寻找我？为何要轻易接受文渊的感情？"

Shen Jianchen asked himself. Perhaps the questions were too powerful, and they slipped out: "Why didn't she wait for me? Why didn't she look for me? Why did she so easily accept Wenyuan's feelings?"

---

神柳沉默依旧，没有给出任何答案。

The Divine Willow remained silent as ever, giving no answer.

---

申简辰望着神柳沉默半晌，为自己找到一个合理的解释。

Shen Jianchen gazed at the Divine Willow in silence for a long while, then found a reasonable explanation for himself.

---

"也许她像我一样内心太孤单，一场地震一切化为泡影，好不容易在古代借助他人的身体活过来，想法必然会有所改变。"

"Maybe she's as lonely inside as I am. An earthquake turned everything to bubbles. She barely came back to life in ancient times, in someone else's body. Her way of thinking would naturally change."

---

又怎能不变？他申简辰再次活过来对人生的态度与穿越前大不相同，曾经追求的功名利禄在他眼中已如同草芥，现在只想与爱的人生活在一起，千里寻一定也是如此想法。

How could it not change? He, Shen Jianchen, had a completely different attitude toward life after coming back to life—so different from before the transmigration. The fame and fortune he once pursued now meant as much as grass to him. Now he just wanted to live with the one he loved. Qianli Xun must feel the same way.

---

想到这里，申简辰不再埋怨千里寻，反而埋怨自己没有及时出现在她身边。出现了，为何不早些认出她？真是造化弄人。

Thinking of this, Shen Jianchen stopped blaming Qianli Xun. Instead, he blamed himself for not appearing by her side sooner. He had appeared—so why hadn't he recognized her earlier? Truly, fate played tricks on people.

---

"她已有文渊，他们看上去很爱对方，我该怎么办？"申简辰在心里自问着。

"She already has Wenyuan. They look like they love each other very much. What should I do?" Shen Jianchen asked himself in his heart.

---

"哥哥！这么晚，你为何还要来这儿？"

"Brother! Why are you still coming here so late?"

---

是乔梦容的声音。

It was Qiao Mengrong's voice.

---

申简辰转过身，月光下，乔梦容显得更加柔弱。他心中想道："难道我只能接受她的感情？可我并不爱梦容。"

Shen Jianchen turned around. Under the moonlight, Qiao Mengrong looked even more fragile. He thought to himself: "Can I only accept her feelings? But I don't love Mengrong."

---

"哥哥，你怎么了？为何不说话？"

"Brother, what's wrong? Why aren't you saying anything?"

---

"你怎么跑来？"

"Why did you come here?"

---

"我睡不着，很难受，打算去找哥哥，瞧见哥哥往外走，便带着喜兰悄悄跟在后面。"

"I couldn't sleep. I felt terrible. I was going to find you, but I saw you leaving, so I brought Xilan and followed quietly behind."

---

"明日我找个大夫给你瞧瞧……"

"Tomorrow I'll find a doctor to check on you..."

---

"不用！大夫解决不了我的问题。"乔梦容强调道，"今日哥哥没来看我，我心中不安，才无法入睡，只要哥哥每日看看我，对我没有任何隐瞒，我定会安然无恙。"

"No need! A doctor can't solve my problem," Qiao Mengrong emphasized. "When you didn't come see me today, I felt uneasy and couldn't sleep. As long as you check on me every day and keep no secrets from me, I'll be perfectly fine."

---

申简辰想过代替赵朴为乔梦容负责，可找到千里寻，想要负责的想法越来越淡，哪怕她已爱上别人。

Shen Jianchen had thought about taking responsibility for Qiao Mengrong in Zhao Pu's place. But after finding Qianli Xun, that desire to take responsibility grew weaker and weaker—even though she had already fallen in love with someone else.

---

"梦容，你不要再把我当成……"

"Mengrong, stop treating me as..."

---

"哥哥不要说下去！"乔梦容很快打断，她能猜到哥哥要说什么，她根本不愿听。她走到哥哥跟前，抱住他，"不要再说，我爱的是现在的哥哥，不管你变成什么样，你都是我的哥哥。"

"Don't say it, Brother!" Qiao Mengrong quickly interrupted. She could guess what he was about to say, and she simply didn't want to hear it. She walked up to her brother and hugged him. "Don't say anymore. I love the brother you are now. No matter what you become, you're still my brother."

---

申简辰很希望自己能爱上梦容，也希望能把她搂在怀里。可双手抬起后，又不得不放下，若真的抱住梦容，他会觉得自己猪狗不如。

Shen Jianchen really wished he could fall in love with Mengrong. He also wished he could hold her in his arms. But after raising his hands, he had to lower them again. If he really hugged Mengrong, he would feel worse than a pig or a dog.

---

"哥哥，我冷，你能抱抱我吗？"

"Brother, I'm cold. Can you hug me?"

---

梦容主动要求，并未改变申简辰的心意，他看到喜兰手里拿着披风："喜兰，把披风给郡主披上。"

Mengrong's request didn't change Shen Jianchen's mind. He saw Xilan holding a cloak: "Xilan, drape the cloak over her ladyship."

---

喜兰捧着披风过来，准备为郡主披上。

Xilan came over with the cloak, ready to drape it over her ladyship.

---

乔梦容拒绝道："我不要披风，我要哥哥抱着我，哥哥的怀抱最温暖。"

Qiao Mengrong refused: "I don't want the cloak. I want you to hug me. Your arms are the warmest."

---

喜兰不知如何是好。

Xilan didn't know what to do.

---

申简辰哄道："梦容，你听话，把披风披上。"

Shen Jianchen coaxed: "Mengrong, be good. Put on the cloak."

---

乔梦容没有说话，放开哥哥，迅速踮起脚尖，在哥哥的唇上吻了一下，害羞地转过身。

Qiao Mengrong said nothing. She let go of her brother, quickly stood on her tiptoes, kissed him on the lips, then shyly turned around.

---

喜兰立刻把披风给郡主披好，乔梦容红着脸带喜兰离开。今晚，她终于能睡个好觉。

Xilan immediately draped the cloak over her ladyship. Qiao Mengrong, her face red, led Xilan away. Tonight, she could finally get a good night's sleep.

---

突如其来的吻让申简辰有些懵，余温还留在他的唇上，并没有任何美好的感觉。

The sudden kiss left Shen Jianchen dazed. The lingering warmth remained on his lips, but it brought him no sense of beauty or joy.

---

申简辰的嘴角露出一丝无奈的笑容，难道他穿越而来的人生就要这么无奈地过下去？爱的人得不到，不爱的人又主动投怀送抱。

A helpless smile appeared at the corner of Shen Jianchen's mouth. Was his transmigrated life going to be lived like this—so helplessly? The one he loved, he couldn't have; the one he didn't love threw herself into his arms.

---

他该怎么办？申简辰找不到答案，还是将命运交给时间，他需要做的唯有遵循内心真实的感情。

What should he do? Shen Jianchen couldn't find an answer. He might as well leave his fate to time. All he needed to do was follow his true feelings.

---

……

...

---

昨日酒喝得太多，沉沉地睡了一晚。窗外的鸟鸣声将千里寻吵醒，她睁开眼，习惯性地唤了声："苦荞！"

She had drunk too much wine yesterday and slept soundly all night. The chirping of birds outside the window woke Qianli Xun. She opened her eyes and called out habitually: "Kuqiao!"

---

苦荞正在收拾屋子，听到小姐呼唤的声音，放下手中活计，来到床前，将床幔挂起。

Kuqiao was tidying the room. Hearing her miss's call, she put down her work, came to the bedside, and hung up the bed curtain.

---

"我让厨房给小姐做了醒酒汤，温度刚刚好，您现在喝吗？"

"I asked the kitchen to make hangover soup for you, Miss. It's just the right temperature. Would you like to drink it now?"

---

"睡了一晚上，醉意全消，不需要喝那玩意儿。"千里寻坐起来，还有些许眩晕，"把醒酒汤端来吧，头还有些沉，也不知我喝了多少酒。"

"After sleeping all night, I'm completely sober. No need for that stuff." Qianli Xun sat up, still feeling a bit dizzy. "Bring the hangover soup, though. My head still feels heavy. I don't even know how much I drank."

---

苦荞把醒酒汤端到小姐面前："昨晚您把东方公子他们带去的酒都喝光了。"

Kuqiao brought the hangover soup to her miss: "Last night, you drank all the wine that Young Master Dongfang and the others brought."

---

千里寻接过醒酒汤埋怨道："都喝光？你为什么不阻止我？任由你家小姐我当众丢人？"

Qianli Xun took the hangover soup and complained: "Drank it all? Why didn't you stop me? Just let your miss embarrass herself in public?"

---

苦荞委屈道："我阻止了三次，您不听，嫌我多事，还说酒酿太好喝，根本不会醉。我最后一次阻止时，乔公子说您高兴，多喝些没关系，大不了他把您送回来，我只好作罢。"

Kuqiao said aggrievedly: "I tried to stop you three times, but you wouldn't listen. You said I was being nosy and that the sweet rice wine tasted so good it couldn't possibly make you drunk. When I tried to stop you the last time, Young Master Qiao said you were happy and it was fine to drink more—that at worst he'd bring you back home. So I had to give up."

---

千里寻将醒酒汤喝光，还别说，醒酒汤刚下肚，顿时觉得神清气爽，她把碗递给苦荞，下地站起来，到窗前伸了伸懒腰："昨晚是文渊送我回来的？"

Qianli Xun drank all the hangover soup. She had to admit—as soon as it went down, she felt refreshed. She handed the bowl to Kuqiao, got out of bed, stood up, and stretched by the window: "Did Wenyuan bring me back last night?"

---

苦荞把碗放好："是的。"

Kuqiao put the bowl away: "Yes."

---

千里寻想起写过的网络小说，一般这种情况下，她都会让女主吐男主一身，心想："难道昨晚，女主喝醉吐男主一身污秽的桥段，也发生在我身上？"

Qianli Xun thought of the web novels she had written. Usually in such situations, she would have the female lead throw up all over the male lead. She wondered: "Did last night's 'drunk female lead throws up all over male lead' scene happen to me too?"

---

她转过身，充满期待地问："昨晚我喝多，什么都不记得，我该不会给文渊吐了一身？"

She turned around and asked expectantly: "I drank too much last night and don't remember anything. I didn't throw up all over Wenyuan, did I?"

---

苦荞摇摇头："那倒没有。"

Kuqiao shook her head: "No, that didn't happen."

---

千里寻感到失望，现实总是没有想象出来的美好，如若她吐文渊一身，他又不嫌弃，那才叫一个美。

Qianli Xun felt disappointed. Reality was never as nice as imagination. If she had thrown up all over Wenyuan and he didn't mind, that would have been truly romantic.

---

"这么平淡，难道没发生些什么？"

"So plain? Did nothing happen at all?"

---

苦荞说："当然发生了，还是大事。"

Kuqiao said: "Of course something happened. Something big."

---

"什么大事？说来听听。"千里寻兴致很浓，好像是在听别人的八卦一般。

"What big thing? Tell me." Qianli Xun was very interested, as if she were listening to gossip about someone else.

---

"小姐可还记得昨晚您作诗之事？"

"Does Miss remember composing a poem last night?"

---

"我竟然作诗了？"千里寻什么都不记得，她喝断片，只怕胡邹了些像诗又不像诗的白话句子，岂不是给朱淑真丢脸？担忧地问，"你可还记得我写的诗句？"

"I actually composed a poem?" Qianli Xun didn't remember anything. She'd blacked out. She was afraid she'd spouted some lines that were like poems but not really poems—wouldn't that embarrass Zhu Shuzhen? She asked worriedly, "Do you still remember the lines I wrote?"

---

"有一句印象深刻。"

"One line left a deep impression."

---

"念来听听。"

"Recite it for me."

---

"您真的要听？"

"Do you really want to hear it?"

---

千里寻紧张地点点头。

Qianli Xun nodded nervously.

---

只听苦荞念道："和衣睡倒人怀。"

Kuqiao recited: "Fully dressed, I fall asleep in his arms."

---

千里寻松口气，原来她醉酒之后吟诵的是朱淑真的《清平乐·夏日游湖》，还好没给原主丢脸。

Qianli Xun breathed a sigh of relief. So after getting drunk, she had recited Zhu Shuzhen's "Qing Ping Yue: Summer Day on the Lake." At least she hadn't embarrassed the original owner.

---

"你说我作诗，吓坏我，我是做了一首新词好不好！"

"You said I composed a poem—you scared me! I actually composed a new ci poem, okay!"

---

"小姐以前告诉我词也是诗。"

"Miss told me before that ci is also poetry."

---

"我不就作了一首新词，你怎么说发生了大事？"

"I just composed a new ci poem. Why do you say something big happened?"

---

"还不是大事？您昨晚在亭子里把乔公子一把推倒，当众坐在他的怀里，才吟出'和衣睡倒人怀'，东方公子他们眼睛瞪得老大。您是大家闺秀，以后可得注意些，否则别人会议论您有失女德。"

"Isn't that a big deal? Last night in the pavilion, you pushed Young Master Qiao down and sat in his lap in public, then recited 'fully dressed, I fall asleep in his arms.' Young Master Dongfang and the others had their eyes wide open. You're a proper young lady. You should be more careful in the future, or people will talk about your scandalous behavior."

---

千里寻感到满意，两人之间终于发生浪漫之事："我与文渊两情相悦，坐在他的怀里再正常不过。"

Qianli Xun felt satisfied. Something romantic had finally happened between them: "Wenyuan and I are in love. It's perfectly normal for me to sit in his lap."

---

苦荞强调道："可您还没嫁给乔公子。"

Kuqiao emphasized: "But you haven't married Young Master Qiao yet."

---

千里寻肯定道："我迟早会嫁给他。"

Qianli Xun said firmly: "I'll marry him sooner or later."

---

门外传来绿桃的声音："小姐，夫人让您用过早饭后去她屋里一趟。"

Outside the door came Lvtiao's voice: "Miss, Madam wants you to come to her room after breakfast."

---

千里寻对外回道："好的！知道了！"

Qianli Xun replied to the outside: "Alright! I understand!"

---

要去见长辈，需收拾一番，在苦荞的伺候下洗了脸，坐到铜镜前，仔细整理妆容。

To meet an elder, she needed to freshen up. With Kuqiao's help, she washed her face, sat in front of the bronze mirror, and carefully touched up her makeup.
