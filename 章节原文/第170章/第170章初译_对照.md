第170章 本想退婚又耗着
## Chapter 170: Calling Off the Match, Then Stalling Again

扶老太太回到屋中，安顿她坐下，姚轻琰屏退下人，把门关上，向陈江林使了使眼色。
陈江林明白夫人的意思，坐下主动向老太太解释：“母亲，您今日中计了。”
夏老太太一脸严肃，对儿子和媳妇甚是不满：“中什么计？”
姚轻琰这才坐到老太太身边，赔着笑脸：“母亲，只怪江林没来得及叮嘱您，知府大人上周专程去绸缎庄找江林，当面告诉他，这俩孩子故意瞎闹，就是想逼两家退婚，在朱府外闹过一回，年跟前儿全临安城的官员在聚香楼宴饮，他两个也跑去瞎闹，都把仪王给惊动了，还好知府大人没中计。”
陈江林接着解释：“知府大人叮嘱我，俩孩子在他跟前没闹成功，肯定还会跑到陈家来闹，让我们别当回事。”
夏老太太虽听明白，可依然不满：“哼！就算她是瞎胡闹，我可是亲眼瞧见她无缘无故一拳把椅子砸烂，以后浩宇娶了她，稍微做些她不满意的事，岂不是连命都没了？”
陈江林被老太太的话给问住，不知如何继续往下说。面对生意，他处理得头头是道，可面对家事，只觉得常言说得好：清官难断家务事，要不怎么将儿子管教成那样。
姚轻琰见夫君无言，想出应对之语：“母亲，文心常常在我面前夸朱家姑娘，她肯定不会骗自家人，我想朱家姑娘既然故意来闹，粗鲁的样子肯定是有意装出来……”
老太太哪里肯听，用拐杖敲着地：“你到底是不是浩宇的娘？只管替别人说话！她再装，拳头的劲儿能装得出来？只怕我孙子与她成亲那日，连洞房都入不了，你们两个还想指望当爷爷奶奶？”
Once Old Madam Xia was settled in a chair inside their room, Yao Qingyan dismissed the maids, shut the door, and glanced at Chen Jianglin.

轮到姚轻琰不敢言语，再说下去，她就成了临安城最不合格的母亲。
Reading her cue, Chen Jianglin sat down and spoke straight to his mother. "Mother, you walked straight into a trap today."

陈江林心想不如用知府大人的身份压压老太太：“母亲既然想退婚，儿子当然同意，只是陈家的生意能做这么大，离不开临安府衙关照，若因退婚得罪知府大人，只怕……”
Old Madam Xia's brow knotted in displeasure. "What trap?"

“哼，你别用知府大人吓唬我，且不说有文心这层关系维系，就是没有，凭陈家现今在临安商界的地位，府衙也不敢将我们怎样。我也相信知府大人是明事理的人，绝不会公私不分。”
Taking the seat beside her with a placating smile, Yao Qingyan explained, "Blame Jianglin for not briefing you in time, Mother. Prefect Zhu paid a special visit to our silk shop last week to warn him that those two brats are staging tantrums on purpose to force both families into breaking the betrothal. They staged a brawl outside Zhu Manor once, and before New Year's they wrecked the prefecture officials' banquet at Juxiang Tower—even startling Prince Yi. Luckily Prefect Zhu did not take the bait."

夏老太太并未受任何影响，自从有了“耗着”的念头，已将事情前后想明白，这一番话把儿子抛出的球顺利挡回去。
"Prefect Zhu warned me that after failing with him, they were sure to bring the circus to our gate, and told us to ignore it," Chen Jianglin added.

姚轻琰见夫君顺着老太太的方式不错，老人家面色缓和许多，不如也顺着她：“母亲这么一说我终于放心，就听母亲的，退了朱家的亲事，可我又担心……”
Even understanding the ploy, Old Madam Xia stayed grim. "Hmph! Put-on or not, I watched with my own two eyes as she smashed a solid chair to splinters with one bare fist! Once Haoyu marries her, one tiny misstep and she might snap his neck!"

儿媳顺着她，夏老太太也尽量将语气放平缓：“担心什么？”
Chen Jianglin had no answer for that. He ran an empire of trade with effortless precision, yet domestic tangles lived up to the saying: not even an upright judge could untangle family affairs—which explained how his son had turned out the way he had.

姚轻琰见老太太中计，装作为难的样子：“可我担心知府大人揪住谣言之事不放，他之所以愿意私了，就是打着想跟陈家结亲的想法，现在陈家主动退婚，只怕知府大人会把事闹大，说不定两家还得对簿公堂……”
Seeing her husband tongue-tied, Yao Qingyan stepped in. "Mother, Wenxin sings Miss Zhu's praises to me all the time, and she would never lie to her own family. Since Miss Zhu came specifically to make a scene, all that rough swagger must have been an act..."

陈江林悄悄向夫人竖起大拇指，以示表扬。姚轻琰偷乐一会儿，再瞧老太太，双眉紧锁，沉思着。
Old Madam Xia thumped her cane against the floor. "Are you Haoyu's mother or hers, sticking up for an outsider? Act all she wants—can you fake the force to split stone and wood? Come the wedding night, my grandson will not even make it into the bridal chamber! You two can kiss any hopes of grandchildren goodbye!"

过了一会儿，老太太开口说：“我已让朱家姑娘向知府大人传话，此事陈家愿意用钱解决。”
Now Yao Qingyan fell silent; one more word and she would be branded the worst mother in Lin'an.

陈江林趁机夸赞：“母亲的主意甚好，能用钱解决的事儿那都不叫事儿。”
Chen Jianglin tried leaning on the Prefect's authority. "If Mother insists on breaking off the match, I will obey. Only—Chen family trade grew this vast thanks in no small part to Lin'an Prefecture's goodwill. Offend Prefect Zhu over a jilted daughter, and I fear..."

夏老太太倒忧虑起来：“我听文心说过，她那公公极好声誉，只怕他不要钱，只要声誉，浩宇散播谣言毁人家姑娘清誉，我们再去退婚，知府大人肯定面子上挂不住，看来这事儿用钱不一定能解决，说不定到时真的闹到公堂上。”
"Hmph! Stop waving the Prefect to scare me! Even without Wenxin linking the two houses, with the Chen family's standing in Lin'an's merchant circles, the yamen would not dare touch us. Besides, Prefect Zhu is a fair man who will not mix private grudges with official duty." Having already mulled over her stalling plan, Old Madam Xia had worked through every angle and batted her son's argument right back.

姚轻琰故意装作不怕的样子：“上公堂就上公堂，官府要是把浩宇在狱中关起来，大不了我们拿出些钱财，上下打点，他在牢狱中过得也不会太差。再说只是散播谣言，应该关不了几日，也就放出来。”
Noticing her husband's yielding tone had softened the old woman's mood, Yao Qingyan followed suit. "Hearing Mother put it that way sets my mind at rest. We will obey Mother and cancel the Zhu match—though I do worry about one thing..."

“呸呸呸！说这么不吉利的话！”夏老太太批评完问道，“散播谣言官府也会治罪？”
Pleased by her daughter-in-law's deference, Old Madam Xia eased her tone. "Worry about what?"

陈江林半真半假添油加醋：“儿子听说三年前有两家闹到公堂，就是因为一家散播另一家的谣言，官府经过核实，还真有此事，便将散播谣言之人打了五十大板。后来听人说，本来要把人在狱中关五日，只因散播者家中有儿子即将去汴梁参加科举，怕牢狱之灾影响儿子，便上下疏通用钱买了五十大板，五十大板打下去，整个人都快不行……”
Putting on a troubled frown, Yao Qingyan baited the hook. "I worry Prefect Zhu will dig up the rumor incident and refuse to let it go. He agreed to settle it privately only because he wanted a marriage alliance with our family. If we break off the betrothal now, what if he blows the matter open and drags both houses into court?"

“别说了！”老太太被唬住，“江林，赶紧去朱家，告诉知府大人，这婚陈家不会退，不过因朱家姑娘这一闹，婚事得往后拖一拖，至于什么时候成亲，那得看朱家姑娘什么时候改了性子。”
Out of his mother's sight, Chen Jianglin shot his wife a silent thumbs-up. Yao Qingyan hid a triumphant smirk as Old Madam Xia knitted her brows in deep thought.

夫妻二人一番机智应对，保住两家的亲事，可还是回到老太太“耗着”的法子，不同的是，那时只有老太太一个人这么想，现在被朱家姑娘一闹，“耗着”的法子不得不公开。
After a pause, Old Madam Xia said, "I already told Miss Zhu to tell Prefect Zhu we will settle it with money."

只是不知会耗到什么时候，要是朱家姑娘永远不改性子，岂不是浩宇永远无法娶妻？夫妻二人暂时没有更好的办法，只能走一步看一步。
"Brilliant idea, Mother," Chen Jianglin praised smoothly. "Any trouble silver can fix is no trouble at all."

陈浩宇从外面浪荡完，本想随便找所外面的宅子住几日，听到有人提起乔文仙今日成亲，他不由想到自己，眼见婚事将近，便没心思住在外面。
Yet doubt crept over Old Madam Xia's face. "Wenxin told me her father-in-law prizes his reputation above everything. What if he refuses silver and demands face instead? Haoyu spread rumors slandering his daughter's honor, and now we jilt her—Prefect Zhu will lose face all over the city. Silver may not smooth this over, and it really could end up in court."

他几次跟奶奶聊天儿，打探她如何耗着。夏老太太悄悄说，到时只要她装病，会搞得陈府上上下下没有心思再准备婚礼，也就有正当理由拖延婚期。
Yao Qingyan put on a brave, unbothered front. "Let it go to court, then! Even if the yamen throws Haoyu in jail, we can spread silver around upstairs and down so he lives decently inside. Besides, it is only slander—they will hold him a few days at most and turn him loose."

主意虽好，却不是一个了断的法子。陈浩宇别无他法，只能应允，现在还是尽快赶回去与奶奶商议装病的日期。
"Bah! Bah! Bah! Watch your unlucky tongue!" Old Madam Xia scolded, then paused in alarm. "Does the yamen actually convict people for spreading rumors?"

青瓜一直站在陈府大门口向外张望，见公子的马车回来，急匆匆上前，伸手去扶公子：“公子，您可回来，家中发生了大事。”
Mixing half-truth with tall tale, Chen Jianglin laid it on thick. "Three years back, I heard two households went to court over one spreading rumors about the other. Once the yamen confirmed it, they sentenced the culprit to fifty strokes of the heavy bamboo! Word was he was first slated for five days in prison, yet because his son was heading to Bianliang for the imperial exams and a jail record would ruin him, the family bribed their way into trading prison for fifty blows. Fifty heavy strokes nearly beat the poor man to death..."

陈浩宇没理会青瓜伸过来的手，跳下马车，以为奶奶已经开始实施计划，面露喜色：“难道奶奶生病了？”
"Stop! Say no more!" Thoroughly spooked, Old Madam Xia waved him off. "Jianglin, go to Zhu Manor at once! Tell Prefect Zhu the Chen family will not cancel the betrothal—yet after Miss Zhu's tantrum today, the wedding must be postponed. As for when it takes place, that depends on when Miss Zhu mends her temper!"

“不是，老太太身体好着呢……”
Through nimble teamwork, husband and wife had saved the betrothal—only to land right back on Old Madam Xia's stalling tactic. The sole difference was that what had been the grandmother's private scheme was now forced out into the open. How long would the stall drag on? If Miss Zhu never mended her temper, would Haoyu stay single forever? Lacking a better move for now, they could only take it one step at a time.

陈浩宇向青瓜一脚踢去：“能不能说话干脆点儿，到底发生什么事？”
Out on a bender, Chen Haoyu had planned to crash at one of his outside residences for a few days—until gossip that Qiao Wenxian was marrying today reminded him his own wedding loomed right around the corner, killing his mood to stay out. He had quizzed his grandmother several times about how she meant to stall; Old Madam Xia had whispered she would fake an illness so the whole Chen household dropped wedding preparations, giving them an airtight excuse to push back the date. Not a permanent fix, yet with nothing better on hand, he hurried home to coordinate the day she would fall "sick."

青瓜向一旁闪开：“公子别急，我这就给您说，是朱家姑娘来府上闹事。”
Watching the street from Chen Manor's gate, Qinggua hurried up the instant his master's carriage pulled in and offered a hand. "Young Master, you are back! Something huge happened at home!"

“呵！这么好的事，没叫上本公子，真不够意思！”陈浩宇边说边向府内走。
Ignoring the hand, Chen Haoyu leaped down, his face lighting up at the thought his grandmother had launched the plan already. "Did Grandmother fall ill?"

青瓜跟上，描述朱家姑娘闹事的场景：“公子，您都不知道，朱家姑娘身边的丫鬟真是厉害，三拳两脚打伤几名下人，朱家姑娘更是厉害，那么厚的石头她一拳砸断，还有椅子也被她一拳砸烂，吓得老太太直喊退婚。”
"No, Old Madam is healthy as an ox—"

陈浩宇站住，喜出望外：“真的同意退婚？”
Chen Haoyu aimed a kick at Qinggua. "Spit it out straight! What happened?"

“那还能有假？不同意朱家姑娘也不会离开。”
Dodging sideways, Qinggua hurried on. "Do not get mad, Young Master, I am telling you! Miss Zhu came and raised hell at the gate!"

“我爹娘可同意？”
"Hah! Fun like that and she did not invite me? How rude!" Chen Haoyu chuckled, striding inside.

“老爷和夫人似乎不愿意，可他们听老太太的话，最终也会同意。”
Trotting alongside, Qinggua painted the scene. "You should have seen it, Young Master! Miss Zhu's maid laid out several gatekeepers in three blows! And Miss Zhu was scarier still—split a thick stone block with one punch and smashed a chair to splinters! Scared Old Madam into yelling for a broken engagement on the spot!"

“没想到朱淑真背着我做了件好事，不如今日请她吃饭，好好感谢。”
Chen Haoyu halted in his tracks, overjoyed. "She really agreed to cancel the wedding?!"

陈浩宇直奔夏老太太的院子。
"Would I lie? Miss Zhu would never have left otherwise!"

青瓜跟在公子身边，跟个话痨似的：“以前老听人说朱家姑娘是女词人，写的词软了吧唧，想着本人应该柔弱，那长相看着也柔弱，没承想劲儿比我还大。”
"Did my parents agree too?"

陈浩宇在青瓜面前抖起文化来：“瞧你那没文化的样子，还‘软了吧唧’，那叫婉约，懂不懂？”
"Master and Madam looked reluctant, yet they always listen to Old Madam, so they will come around."

青瓜笑道：“嘿嘿，公子就是比我懂得多。我现在终于明白公子为何不愿娶朱家姑娘，还说她是母夜叉。只怕公子一旦娶了她，以后既没有机会去青楼、赌坊，更没有机会欺负黄花大姑娘……”
"Well I will be damned—Zhu Shuzhen went behind my back and pulled off a miracle! Maybe I should treat her to dinner tonight as thanks!"

“滚！能不能说点儿好听的？”
Beaming, Chen Haoyu made a beeline for Old Madam Xia's courtyard.

“当然能，青瓜祝公子退婚成功，娶个又温柔又听话的少夫人回来。”
Qinggua chattered nonstop at his heels. "Folks always rave about Miss Zhu being a poetess writing all those mushy lyrics, and she looks dainty enough, so I figured she was soft as silk—never guessed she packs more punch than I do!"

青瓜说话顺耳许多，陈浩宇不由美滋滋的。
Chen Haoyu flaunted his learning. "Listen to your uncultured mouth—'mushy'? That is called the delicate and restrained style, got it?"

夏老太太居住的院子挺大，种着花花草草，春天到来，真是春色满园。最吸引人的则是一树的杏花，时不时有花瓣落下。
"Heh, Young Master always knows more than me," Qinggua grinned. "Now I finally see why you refused to marry Miss Zhu and called her a she-demon. Marry her, and you could kiss brothels, gambling dens, and chasing pretty young maidens goodbye..."

天气回暖的时间不长，屋子里还有些凉，不如外面暖和。夏老太太从儿子房中回来，不想在屋里待着，来到院中，半躺在藤椅上。丫鬟为她盖上毯子，她则闭上眼睛尽情享受春日的阳光。
"Scram! Can you say something pleasant for once?"

边享受边思忖孙子的婚事该拖到什么时候？今日也看明白，朱家姑娘根本不想嫁到陈家，她肯定不会改性子，难道就永远这么拖下去？
"Sure! Qinggua wishes Young Master a smooth broken engagement and a gentle, obedient young madam in her place!"

老太太还没想明白，听到孙子的声音：“奶奶！”
Much nicer to the ears. Chen Haoyu strutted on, grinning ear to ear.

夏老太太睁开眼，瞧见孙子出现在眼前，不由自主满脸堆笑：“怎么才回来？快到奶奶跟前坐着。”
Old Madam Xia's spacious courtyard bloomed with spring flowers, centered around a tall apricot tree shedding pale petals on the breeze. With the indoor rooms still holding winter's chill, she had returned from her son's quarters and settled onto a rattan chair outside under a blanket, eyes closed to soak up the sun while wondering how long she could drag out her grandson's match. Plainly Miss Zhu wanted no part of the Chens and would never soften her ways—could they really stall forever?

一旁的丫鬟搬张凳子放在老人身边，陈浩宇坐上去，在奶奶脸上亲一口。
"Grandmother!"

老人家被亲得心花怒放：“还是我孙子最好，知道哄奶奶开心。”
Her eyes fluttered open, and a radiant smile broke over her face at the sight of her grandson. "Why are you just getting back? Come sit right here by Grandmother."

陈浩宇继续哄道：“奶奶是我在这个世上最爱的人。”
A maid set a stool beside her chair; Chen Haoyu perched on it and planted a kiss on her cheek.

“你爹娘呢？”
Old Madam Xia beamed with delight. "My grandson is the sweetest, always knowing how to cheer Grandmother up."

“我爹娘要是不批评我，我就爱他们。”
"Grandmother is the person I love most in this whole world," Chen Haoyu cooed.

“我要是批评你呢？”
"What about your parents?"

“奶奶怎么会批评我？”
"I love them whenever they are not lecturing me."

“臭小子，奶奶舍不得批评你。”
"And if I lecture you?"

陈浩宇跟奶奶一番真情流露后转入正题：“奶奶，我听青瓜说我那未过门儿的媳妇闹到家里来，您同意退婚，是也不是？”
"When would Grandmother ever lecture me?"

“当然，我怎么会让你娶个厉害的媳妇回来找罪受？”
"Little rascal—Grandmother could never bear to."

“还是奶奶最好。”
After sufficient buttering up, Chen Haoyu pivoted to business. "Grandmother, Qinggua told me my unwed bride-to-be stormed the house and you agreed to cancel the match. Is that true?"

“不过跟你爹娘商量后，这婚还是不能退……”
"Of course! How could I let you marry a terror like that and suffer?"

陈浩宇急了：“为何？”
"Grandmother really is the best!"

夏老太太微笑的面庞上略微露出批评的神色：“要问你自己，都是你惹下的事。”
"Still, after talking it over with your parents, we cannot cancel the match after all..."

应该是因散播谣言一事，陈浩宇后悔已来不及：“奶奶，您今日也见识朱淑真的厉害，也说不愿意我娶她，赶紧想个办法退了这门亲事。”
Alarm shot through Chen Haoyu. "Why not?!"

“你以为奶奶不想？现在唯一的办法还是奶奶之前想的，耗着！”
A shadow of reproach touched Old Madam Xia's smile. "Ask yourself—this mess is all your own doing."

“怎么还是耗着？有没有更好的办法？”
The rumors! Too late for regrets now. "Grandmother, you saw firsthand how terrifying Zhu Shuzhen is, and you said yourself you do not want me marrying her! Think of a way to break it off!"

夏老太太向孙子耐心解释：“现在耗着跟之前不一样，之前只有你与奶奶这么想，还没法向朱家说明。今日朱家姑娘这么一闹，我们就有理由向知府大人讲明，理直气壮提要求。”
"You think I do not want to? Our only move is still my original plan: stall!"

“什么要求？”
"Stall again? Isn't there anything better?"

“要求朱家姑娘什么时候改了她的性子什么时候娶她过门。”
Old Madam Xia explained with patience. "Stalling now is different from before. Earlier it was only between the two of us, with no excuse to give the Zhus. After Miss Zhu's spectacle today, we have every right to go straight to Prefect Zhu and lay down terms."

“朱淑真不愿嫁给我，她肯定不会改性子，我与她的婚事就这么遥遥无期地耗着，之前没觉得这个主意好，现在仔细一想，真不错！”
"What terms?"

夏老太太又变得忧虑：“只是苦了你，以后想娶个可心的人过门儿都难。”
"Tell him we will welcome Miss Zhu through our doors the day she mends her temper."

“我是男子，奶奶不用为我发愁，大不了我先纳妾，照样给您生小重孙，等哪天朱家实在耗不下去，主动要求退婚，我再将妾室扶正，不就万事大吉。”
"Zhu Shuzhen hates my guts—she will never mend her temper! That pushes the wedding off till kingdom come! I was lukewarm on this plan before, yet thinking about it now, it is genius!"

夏老太太立刻面露笑容：“还是我孙子聪明，三言两语让奶奶豁然开朗。”
