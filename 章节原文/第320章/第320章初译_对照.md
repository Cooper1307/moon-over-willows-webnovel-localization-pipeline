第320章下决心勇敢面对
## Chapter 320: Choosing to Face It Bravely

夏老太太带着儿子和孙子下了马车后，敲开柳岸别馆的大门，告诉开门的下人，只需报上“梁师慧”的名字。
Old Madam Xia stepped down from the carriage with her son and grandson, knocked at the gate of Liu'an Villa, and told the servant who answered to announce only one name: "Liang Shihui."

梁师成正坐在亭子里听曲儿，听得极其入神，用手在大腿上拍着节奏，跟着唱曲的人轻哼着。
Liang Shicheng sat in the pavilion listening to a singer, utterly transported, tapping the rhythm on his thigh and humming along under his breath.

下人来到跟前，不敢大声打搅，靠近他，在耳边说出那个名字。
The servant came close, not daring to disturb him aloud, and breathed the name into his ear.

梁师成立刻睁开眼，示意唱曲儿的退下，问道：“你没听错？”
Liang Shicheng's eyes flew open. He waved the singer away and asked, "Did you hear me right?"

“小人听得真切，来人是位老太太，说的就是这个名字。”
"This slave heard clearly. The visitor is an old lady, and that is precisely the name she gave."

“老太太？”梁师成的眼睛不由变得湿润，他起身，急急忙忙向门口迎去。
"An old lady?" Liang Shicheng's eyes welled up before he could stop them. He rose and hurried toward the gate to receive her.

陈浩宇和陈江林见梁师成亲自迎出来，表情显得十分激动，仿佛是见到许久未见的亲人一般，两人甚是惊讶。
Seeing Liang Shicheng come out in person to greet them, Chen Haoyu and Chen Jianglin wore looks of such raw emotion, as though greeting kin they had not seen in years, that both men were quite taken aback.

眼前站着一位银发老太，拄着拐杖。梁师成希望从她的面容中看到熟悉的样貌，可惜什么都没看出，他还是亲切地唤出：“姐姐，是你吗？”
An elderly woman with silver hair stood before them, leaning on a cane. Liang Shicheng searched her face for something familiar and found nothing, yet he still called out gently, "Sister—is it you?"

夏老太太心想，梁师慧的名字还真管用，赶紧回道：“让内侍大人失望，我不是您的姐姐梁师慧，我是陈家人，不过我与师慧妹妹交往甚密，她在我这里留了些东西，让我亲手交给她的弟弟。”
Old Madam Xia thought to herself that the name Liang Shihui had indeed done the trick. She replied at once, "I'm afraid I've disappointed you, sir. I am not your sister Liang Shihui. I'm of the Chen family. But I was very close to Sister Shihui, and she left certain things with me to place in her brother's own hands."

父子两个才算明白老太太手中的王牌是什么。
Only then did father and son understand what card the old lady held.

梁师成感到失望的同时，清楚对方前来不过是为陈家和女词人的事。她手中有姐姐留下的东西，自然知道姐姐的下落，还需请入屋内。
Disappointment washed over Liang Shicheng, but he knew perfectly well the visitor had come on account of the Chen family and the woman ci poet. She had things his sister had left behind, so she must know where his sister had ended up. She had to be invited inside.

“原来如此，请到屋内说话。”
"I see. Please come in and speak."

千里寻让苦荞坐在马车里等她，她则隐蔽好身形，偷偷朝大门口瞧，发现陈浩宇和公爹陪着奶奶站在柳岸别馆的大门口。不一会儿出来一人，说了会儿话，三人才被请入别馆内。千里寻猜测，出来迎接的人应该就是梁师成。
Qianli Xun had Kuqiao wait in the carriage while she kept herself hidden and peered toward the gate. She saw Chen Haoyu and her father-in-law standing there with Grandma at the entrance of Liu'an Villa. Presently a man came out; they talked a while, and then all three were invited in. Qianli Xun guessed the man who had come to greet them must be Liang Shicheng.

“他们为何来此？难道陈家的生意与梁师成有关？”
"Why have they come here? Could the Chen family's business have something to do with Liang Shicheng?"

千里寻决定偷偷进去查探明白。
Qianli Xun decided to slip in and find out.

她抬头瞧了瞧柳岸别馆的院墙，不算太高，用轻功上去很容易。于是启动轻功，顺利落入院内。
She glanced up at the courtyard wall of Liu'an Villa. Not too high—easy enough with her lightness skill. She gathered her breath and leapt, landing cleanly inside the compound.

还好有一排树挡着，不容易被人发现。透过树与树之间的空隙，清楚地窥探家人跟着梁师成一直向里走。她迂回着悄悄尾随，时刻警惕地观察着四周。
A row of trees worked in her favor; she was not easily spotted. Through the gaps between trunks she watched her family follow Liang Shicheng deeper in. She took a winding path after them, silent, eyes and ears alert on every side.

众人拐过叠石假山后，她轻手轻脚靠近假山，正打算跟上，一队侍卫巡逻经过，她谨慎躲好。
After the party rounded a rockery of stacked stones, she edged close on light feet, about to follow, when a patrol of guards came past. She held perfectly still until they were gone.

侍卫的队伍中最后一位感觉到有人影闪过，停下来，抽出剑，向假山后靠近。
The last guard in the line caught a flicker of movement. He stopped, drew his sword, and moved toward the back of the rockery.

千里寻担心被发现，屏住呼吸身体紧贴假山。
Afraid of being discovered, Qianli Xun held her breath and pressed herself flat against the stone.

等拿着剑的手出现在面前，她迅速抓住那只手，将侍卫拽到假山后，没等人反应过来，将其打晕，然后用轻功飞身到屋顶。
The instant the sword-hand came into view, she seized the wrist, yanked the guard behind the rockery, knocked him out before he could react, and used her lightness skill to spring onto the roof.

身居高处，清楚地看到三人被带进一个屋内。院中有侍卫巡逻，不好跟踪，千里寻决定还是躲在屋顶偷听。
From up high she saw the three taken into a room. Guards patrolled the courtyard, so following on foot was out of the question. Qianli Xun decided to stay on the roof and listen.

她踩着屋顶的瓦片，像蜻蜓点水般穿越屋顶……
She stepped over the tiles, skimming across the rooftops like a dragonfly skimming water…

正好经过申简辰被关的屋顶，白风听到声音，提醒道：“王爷，屋顶有人。”
Her path ran right over the roof under which Shen Jianchen was held. Bai Feng heard the sound and warned, "Your Highness, someone's on the roof."

申简辰早已听到，瞧着周围结实的铁笼：“希望能有人帮我杀了梁师成。”
Shen Jianchen had heard it too. He looked at the stout iron cage around him. "I only wish someone up there would kill Liang Shicheng for me."

又一想，历史上记载梁师成死在宋钦宗之手，楼顶上穿过的那人不可能杀了他。
Then he remembered the histories: Liang Shicheng had died at the hands of Emperor Qinzong of Song. Whoever was crossing that roof could not possibly kill him.

申简辰安慰自己：“他还有三年的寿命，我会耐心等到那一日。”
Shen Jianchen consoled himself. "Three more years of life remain to him. I can wait that long."

千里寻来到众人所在的屋顶，轻轻趴下，挪开瓦片，将耳朵靠近，大略能够听到里面的声音。
Qianli Xun reached the roof above the room where they had gone, lay flat, eased a tile aside, and put her ear to the gap. She could make out most of what was said below.

众人在屋内落座后，梁师成急切地问：“老人家可知我姐姐人在哪里？”
Once they were seated, Liang Shicheng asked eagerly, "Can you tell me, madam, where my sister is?"

夏老太太叹口气：“唉，五十多年前，她已离开人世，走之前，拜托我将这个亲手交给她的弟弟，还告诉我她的弟弟进了宫，伺候宫里的贵人。”
Old Madam Xia sighed. "Ah. More than fifty years ago she left this world. Before she went, she asked me to place this in her brother's own hands—and told me her brother had entered the palace to serve one of the nobles there."

夏老太太边说边将紫色大荷包递上。
She handed over the large purple purse as she spoke.

下人接过，送至梁内侍手中。
A servant took it and carried it to the Imperial Attendant.

他颤抖着手打开荷包，取出信，展开用心念了一遍，已是泪眼婆娑，再取出刻有姐姐名字的玉，更确信都是姐姐的遗物。
His hands shaking, he opened the purse, drew out the letter, unfolded it and read it with his whole heart, his eyes already swimming with tears. Then he took out the jade engraved with his sister's name, and knew beyond doubt that these were her belongings.

“难怪我一直寻找姐姐没有任何结果，原来她早已离开人世。可你为何不将手中之物早些交给我？”
"No wonder every search for my sister came to nothing. She had been gone all those years. Why did you wait so long to give me these?"

夏老太太解释道：“不是没想着找您，去过好几次京城，宫门口的人说根本没有此人。后来内侍大人得到官家重用，才渐渐听到您的名号，又担心找您会被认为陈家想攀高枝，便一拖再拖。要不是为家中之事，恐怕我这辈子都不会将荷包拿出来。”
Old Madam Xia explained, "It isn't that I never thought of finding you. I went to the capital several times, and the men at the palace gate said no such person existed. Later, when you came into His Majesty's favor, I began to hear your name. But I feared that coming to you would make the Chens look like they were climbing toward a higher branch, so I kept putting it off. If not for this trouble in the family, I might never have brought the purse out at all."

“我姐姐葬在何处？”
"Where is my sister buried?"

“夏家陵园。”
"In the Xia family cemetery."

“你是怎么认识我姐姐的？”
"How did you come to know my sister?"

“我那会儿还未出嫁，来到街上，见一姑娘跪在街边卖自己，我甚是心疼，主动将她买回家，这位姑娘便是内侍大人的姐姐梁师慧。我与师慧甚觉投缘，结为姐妹，让她待在夏家。我成亲后，父母便认她做女儿，想着日后也给她寻一个好婆家，谁知她后来得了怪病，根本治不好，不久离开人世。离开前给了我那个荷包，嘱咐我她有一个亲弟弟，被迫入宫，让我帮她找到人，亲手把荷包给了，今日总算帮她完成遗愿。”
"I was still unmarried then. I was out on the street when I saw a girl kneeling by the roadside, selling herself. My heart went out to her, and I bought her home of my own accord. That girl was your sister, Liang Shihui. She and I took to each other at once and became sworn sisters, and she stayed with the Xia household. After I married, my parents adopted her as a daughter, meaning to find her a good husband one day. But then she caught a strange illness no one could cure, and she passed away not long after. Before she went, she gave me that purse and asked me to find her only brother, who had been forced into the palace, and put it into his hands myself. Today I have finally carried out her last wish."

梁师成更是心痛不已，眼泪“吧嗒，吧嗒”往下掉：“姐姐比我大八岁，我们从小没了父母，生活艰辛，她既当爹又当娘照顾我，我不想她辛苦，主动净身进了宫，就是希望她有好日子过，可等我有能力让她过上好日子时，却找不到她……”
Liang Shicheng's grief poured out of him; his tears fell pat, pat, pat. "She was eight years older than I. We lost our parents while we were small, and life was hard. She was father and mother both to me. I didn't want her to suffer, so I had myself castrated and entered the palace, hoping she might have an easier life. But by the time I could give her a good life, I couldn't find her…"

梁内侍开始抽泣，以情动人的效果已拉满。夏老太太顺势跪下，陈浩宇和陈江树也跟着跪下。
The Imperial Attendant began to sob; the effect of his grief was complete. Old Madam Xia seized the moment and knelt, with Chen Haoyu and Chen Jiangshu kneeling after her.

“请梁内侍看在我与师慧妹妹有过一段情谊，对陈家高抬贵手，放过淑真。”
"I beg you, sir, for the sake of the bond I shared with Sister Shihui, to show mercy to the Chen family and let Shuzhen be."

千里寻听到这里，总算明白公爹与陈浩宇愁眉不展之事原来是为自己。她与梁内侍从未有过交集，为何梁内侍要针对她？
Hearing this, Qianli Xun finally understood what had put her father-in-law and Chen Haoyu into such black moods. It was herself. She had never crossed paths with the Imperial Attendant—so why had he come after her?

她自然想到梦容，难道梦容还不愿放过她？才请到一位权高位重之人？
Mengrong, of course. Could Mengrong still not let her go? Had she found someone this powerful to do her bidding?

为了进一步作出判断，千里寻继续认真听。
To be sure, Qianli Xun kept listening.

梁内侍止住抽泣声，抬起眼，用手中的帕子擦着眼泪：“老人家快快请起，你有恩于我姐姐，我自然不会动陈家。看在你的面子上，我还会帮陈家拓展在汴梁的生意，往后有我在，谁也动不了陈家，”
The Imperial Attendant stifled his sobs, lifted his eyes, and dabbed at them with his handkerchief. "Rise, madam, rise. You were kind to my sister; of course I will not touch the Chen family. For your sake, I will even help them expand their trade in Bianliang. While I am here, no one will lay a finger on them."

三人听后自然开心，齐声称谢：“多谢内侍大人！”
The three were delighted, and said together, "Our thanks to you, sir!"

父子两个将夏老太太扶起来继续坐好，心里无不认为淑真这下算是安全。
Father and son helped Old Madam Xia back to her seat, both convinced that Shuzhen was safe now.

谁知梁师成继续说：“至于女词人，我还是想见她一面。你们放心只是见一面，没有别的意思。”
But Liang Shicheng went on. "As for the woman ci poet, I should still like to meet her once. Rest easy—just one meeting. Nothing more is meant by it."

三人脸上的笑容瞬间消失。
The smiles vanished from all three faces at once.

陈江林认为如果只是见一面又何必大动干戈？
Chen Jianglin thought: if it were only a meeting, why make such a fuss?

陈浩宇忍不住说道：“内侍大人若想见我家娘子，明日我陪她一起登门拜访。”
Chen Haoyu could not hold back. "If your honor wishes to meet my wife, I will accompany her to call on you tomorrow."

梁师成红红的眼圈衬着微笑，看上去很不协调：“不必陈公子亲自送她前来，既然我不会再拿陈家做文章，两日时间取消，今日未时，林统制会到陈家将女词人请到柳岸别馆做客。”
Liang Shicheng's reddened rims around a smile made an odd picture. "There's no need for you to bring her yourself, young master Chen. Since I shan't make trouble for the Chen family, we can dispense with the two days' grace. This very afternoon, at the hour of wei, Commandant Lin will come to the Chen house and escort the woman ci poet to Liu'an Villa as my guest."

听到此处，千里寻更加确定与梦容有关，恐怕她到柳岸别馆走一趟凶多吉少。
Hearing that, Qianli Xun was more certain than ever that Mengrong was behind it—and that a trip to Liu'an Villa boded her no good.

千里寻很想冲下去将梁师成打得稀巴烂，想到陈家长辈对她有情有义，若直接下去，只怕会牵连陈家。再说别馆内有许多侍卫，恐怕也占不到多少便宜，于是迅速用轻功飞檐走壁离开。
Qianli Xun itched to drop down and pound Liang Shicheng to pulp, but the Chen elders had been good and true to her; if she simply appeared, the family would likely pay for it. Besides, the villa was crawling with guards, and she would gain little advantage. She used her lightness skill to leap over the eaves and away.

陈江林本想说两句，被母亲阻止，只听夏老太太说：“既然梁内侍执意要见我孙媳，未时恭候大驾。”
Chen Jianglin was about to say something when his mother stopped him. Old Madam Xia said, "Since the Imperial Attendant insists on seeing my grandson's wife, we shall await him at the hour of wei."

离开柳岸别馆后，三人又乘坐马车原路返回。
After leaving Liu'an Villa, the three climbed back into the carriage and retraced their road.

马车上，陈浩宇担忧地问：“奶奶，您真的想让梁内侍到陈家带走我娘子？”
In the carriage, Chen Haoyu asked anxiously, "Grandma, do you truly mean to let the Imperial Attendant take my wife away from our house?"

老太太解释道：“你把奶奶看成什么人？梁内侍一心想见淑真，你们父子说什么都没用，还不如赶紧回家想办法。现在已无两难之选，只剩下保护淑真一桩难事，应付起来倒也轻松。离未时还有一个时辰，我想好了，回去后让淑真到乡下躲躲，林觉来陈家找不到人，只能空手而归。”
The old lady explained, "What do you take me for? The Imperial Attendant is set on seeing Shuzhen; nothing either of you can say will change that. Better go home at once and think of a way. There's no longer a choice between two difficulties—only the single hard task of protecting Shuzhen, and that is simpler to manage. We have one shichen until the hour of wei. I've made up my mind: when we get back, I'll send Shuzhen into the countryside to lie low. When Lin Jue comes to the Chen house and finds no one, he'll have to go away empty-handed."

陈江林担忧道：“只怕找不到淑真，梁内侍又会怪罪陈家。”
Chen Jianglin worried aloud, "What if he can't find Shuzhen and blames the Chen family?"

“我对她姐姐有恩，他绝不会为难陈家。还好我没有早早把荷包送给他，否则今日又如何能救陈家？”夏老太太多少对自己曾经的决定感到庆幸。
"I did his sister a kindness; he would never make trouble for us. It's just as well I didn't hand over the purse sooner—otherwise how could I save the Chen family today?" Old Madam Xia was more than a little pleased with her own past decision.

凭着对母亲的了解，陈江林做出判断：“我猜母亲之所以后来没找梁内侍送荷包，肯定有原因；或者说，母亲是想选择合适的时机拿出。”
Knowing his mother well, Chen Jianglin judged, "I'll wager there was a reason you never went to the Imperial Attendant with the purse—or that you meant to bring it out at the right moment."

夏老太太赞道：“你说对一半，的确另有原因。起初是没找到人，只能搁置；后来听说了他，可他已得官家重用，成为百姓口中诟病的奸臣，我不愿陈家与此人扯上任何关系，才把荷包藏起来，本以为永远不会送到他手中，可他为难陈家，不得已才拿出。”
Old Madam Xia praised him. "You're half right; there was indeed another reason. At first I couldn't find the man, so the matter had to wait. Later I heard of him, but by then he stood high in His Majesty's favor and was reviled by the common people as a corrupt minister. I didn't want the Chen family tied to such a man in any way, so I hid the purse. I thought it would never reach his hands—but when he made trouble for us, I had no choice but to bring it out."

陈浩宇担忧道：“现在陈家与梁师成扯上关系，慢慢大家会知道，只怕陈家将来被人诟病。”
Chen Haoyu said, worried, "Now that the Chen family is linked to Liang Shicheng, word will get around in time. I'm afraid people will condemn us later."

老太太叮嘱道：“你们父子二人记着，今日是为陈家和淑真才去攀这层关系，往后老老实实护着陈家现有的根基，不要想着往汴梁拓展生意，也不要求到梁内侍门下，只有如此才能保陈家平安。”
The old lady cautioned them. "You two remember this: today we reached out to this connection for the Chen family and for Shuzhen. From now on, guard the foundations the family already has, be honest and quiet, don't dream of expanding our trade into Bianliang, and don't seek the Imperial Attendant's patronage. Only that will keep the Chens safe."

两人齐声回答，一个说“儿子记下”，一个说“孙儿记下”。
They answered together—one saying "Your son will remember," and the other "Your grandson will remember."

三人回到府中时，千里寻到家有一段时间。她已做好打算，要只身面对即将发生的事，哪怕死，她也不怕。已经死过一回，多活一段时日已是幸运，她倒要瞧瞧梁师成想要将她如何？
By the time the three reached home, Qianli Xun had been back a while. She had made up her mind to face what was coming alone, and death did not frighten her. She had died once already; a few more years of life were a gift. She would like to see just what Liang Shicheng meant to do with her.

为不引起淑真怀疑，夏老太太和陈江林没有一起来墨香苑，他们把重任交给陈浩宇。
So as not to rouse Shuzhen's suspicion, Old Madam Xia and Chen Jianglin did not come to Ink Fragrance Court with him; they left the task to Chen Haoyu.

陈浩宇装作若无其事的样子，一进院子开始大喊：“娘子！我回来了！有件事必须跟你说！”
Chen Haoyu put on an air of nothing being wrong and began shouting the moment he entered the courtyard. "Wife! I'm back! There's something I must tell you!"

还没等他进屋，娘子出现在他面前：“什么事？”
Before he could reach the room, his wife appeared in front of him. "What is it?"

陈浩宇尽量表现得轻松自在：“因生意需要，要到乡下走一趟，娘子待家里无事，不如陪我一起去？”
Chen Haoyu tried to keep his tone light. "Business takes me to the countryside for a trip. You've nothing on at home—why not come with me?"

“去乡下的宅子住吗？”
"To the country estate?"

“不是那个乡下，有些远，路上得走两天两夜。”
"Not that one. It's farther away. Two days and two nights on the road."

原来陈浩宇是想带她逃走，恐怕是长辈想到的办法。
So Chen Haoyu meant to take her away—the elders must have thought of it.

“太远了，我不想去。”
"Too far. I don't want to go."

陈浩宇装不下去：“不去也得去，这是命令。”
Chen Haoyu could not keep up the act. "You're going whether you want to or not. That's an order."

“笑话，你能命令我？”
"A fine joke. Since when can you order me around?"

“这件事我说了算，容不得你做主。”为了娘子，陈浩宇已不管不顾，他大声喊道，“苦荞！”
"On this I have the final say; you don't get to choose." For his wife's sake, Chen Haoyu had stopped caring about anything else. He shouted, "Kuqiao!"

苦荞从屋里出来：“姑爷，何事？”
Kuqiao came out of the house. "Young Master Chen, what is it?"

陈浩宇的声音不由自主显得有些着急：“给娘子收拾些衣物！立刻！马上！我要带她去乡下！”
His voice betrayed his urgency despite himself. "Pack some clothes for my wife! Now! This instant! I'm taking her to the countryside!"

苦荞不明所以，还是回屋收拾。
Kuqiao had no idea why, but she went back inside to pack.

陈浩宇又开始喊：“红豆！”
Chen Haoyu called out again, "Hongdou!"

红豆听到喊声，从西边跑过来：“姑爷有何吩咐？”
Hongdou heard him and ran over from the west side. "What are your orders, Young Master Chen?"

“把其他下人都叫齐！护送我和娘子去乡下！”
"Round up all the other servants! Escort my wife and me to the countryside!"

红豆更是不明所以，见小姐站在一旁不言语，只好去做。
Hongdou was even more baffled. Seeing the young mistress standing by in silence, she went to do as told.

陈浩宇自语着：“对，差点儿忘了韦嬷嬷。”
Chen Haoyu muttered to himself, "Right—I nearly forgot Matron Wei."

他正要跑到后院找人，被娘子从背后抱住。
He was running toward the back courtyard to find her when his wife caught him from behind in a hug.

第一次被娘子如此拥抱，陈浩宇的内心产生莫名的幸福感。想到林觉快要带人上门，他无法再沉迷下去。
It was the first time she had ever held him like that, and a strange happiness welled up in Chen Haoyu. Remembering Lin Jue would soon be at the door with his men, he could not afford to linger in it.

“娘子，我要去找韦嬷嬷，你松开一会儿。”
"Wife, I'm going to look for Matron Wei. Let go for a moment."

千里寻并未松开：“韦嬷嬷不在后院，她一早带着两个下人出去了。”
Qianli Xun did not let go. "Matron Wei isn't in the back courtyard. She went out early this morning with two servants."

“只好不带她，我们先走。”
"Then we'll go without her. Let's leave now."

“为何这么着急？”
"Why such a hurry?"

“没办法，生意上的事有些急。”
"No choice—there's urgent business to settle."

“我知道，不是生意上的事。梁内侍要见我，你不放心，才想让我到乡下避一避。”
"I know it isn't business. The Imperial Attendant wants to see me, and you're worried, so you mean to hide me in the countryside."

陈浩宇想不通娘子怎么会知道真相？依旧掩饰道：“你从哪里听到这种说法？梁内侍是什么人？怎么可能提出见你？再说他即使提出要见你，见一见又何妨？你别瞎猜，单纯是生意上的事。”
Chen Haoyu could not fathom how she knew the truth, and still tried to cover. "Where did you hear that? Who is the Imperial Attendant, that he should ask to see you? And even if he had, what harm would one meeting do? Don't guess wildly. It's purely a business matter."

“你跟奶奶和公爹去柳岸别馆时，我一直跟在后面。你们在屋里说的话我在屋顶已听得明白。”千里寻放开陈浩宇，“你都说了，见一见又何妨？那我就去见他一见。”
"When you went to Liu'an Villa with Grandma and my father-in-law, I followed the whole way. I heard every word you said in that room from the roof." Qianli Xun let him go. "You said it yourself—what harm would one meeting do? Then I'll go and meet him."

已瞒不下去，陈浩宇转过身急切地说：“你既已知道实情，也不必瞒你。梁内侍提出见你，绝非那么简单。为了此事，他派人到茶庄找事，还把仪王和岳父大人关起来，你前去只怕有危险。我听爹讲，岳父大人说梁内侍好色，尤其喜欢成了亲的女子，我又怎能让你前去涉险？”
There was no hiding it now. Chen Haoyu turned to her urgently. "Since you know the truth, I'll not keep it from you. His request to see you is anything but simple. He's already sent men to pick fights at the tea house, and he has Prince Yi and your father locked up. There could be danger in your going. Father told me your father said the Imperial Attendant lusts after women—especially married ones. How could I let you walk into that?"

千里寻冷笑道：“就他？一个奸臣，想好老娘的色，没那么容易，老娘一拳打得他脑袋开花！”
Qianli Xun laughed coldly. "Him? A corrupt minister who thinks he can have me? He'll find it harder than he thinks. I'll punch his head clean open!"

娘子毫不畏惧的神情，令陈浩宇欣慰，他还是劝道：“与其硬碰硬，不如躲起来让他找不到你，也许慢慢就忘了。”
The fearlessness on her face gladdened Chen Haoyu, yet he still urged, "Better to hide where he can't find you than to meet him head-on. Perhaps in time he'll forget."

千里寻只觉陈浩宇想得太过简单，连仪王和父亲朱晞颜都被梁师成关起来，恐怕不见到她，梁师成不会罢休，躲起来又有何用？
Qianli Xun thought him too simple-minded. Even Prince Yi and her father Zhu Xiyan were locked up by Liang Shicheng—he would hardly stop until he had seen her. What use was hiding?

“躲起来根本不是办法，梁师成想找到我有的是办法。我已想好，大大方方去见他，看他能将我如何？”
"Hiding won't solve anything. Liang Shicheng has a hundred ways to find me. I've decided: I'll go see him openly and aboveboard, and we'll see what he can do to me."
