第144章 朱陈两家把亲定
## Chapter 144: The Zhu and Chen Families Seal the Betrothal

七日之内，千里寻除写话本依旧是训练下人练习拳法。
Over the next seven days, Qianli Xun split her hours between writing huaben scripts and drilling her household staff in Qianli Fist.

而陈浩宇遍体鳞伤，想要好，也得十天半个月。他一直躲在永宁居养伤，不敢出来。
Meanwhile, bruised from crown to heel and needing two full weeks to heal, Chen Haoyu stayed holed up inside Yongning Residence nursing his wounds.

关于订亲一事，陈浩宇自认为凭朱淑真一己之力，便可阻止。他身上有伤，无暇顾及，便将此事抛诸脑后。
Figuring Zhu Shuzhen's fiery temper alone would blow the engagement sky-high, he dismissed the whole matter from his mind while recuperating.

七日时间过得很快，陈家果然不食言，陈江林与姚轻琰以及媒人，带上丰厚的聘礼，热热闹闹来到朱府。
Seven days flew by—and true to his word, Chen Jianglin arrived at Zhu Manor with Yao Qingyan, a matchmaker, and a festive procession of lavish betrothal gifts.

一早起来，朱晞颜吩咐下人准备，向夫人以及儿子和儿媳才知道订亲之事属实，高兴之余，充满担忧。
Only when Zhu Xiyan roused the household at dawn to deck the halls did Madam Xiang, Zhu Jingyuan, and Chen Wenxin discover the betrothal was real—thrilled, yet nervous all the same.

朱晞颜安慰道：“婚姻之事向来父母做主，淑真不同意根本没用。”
Zhu Xiyan waved their worries away: "Parents rule marriages—Shuzhen objecting changes nothing."

于是一家人也热热闹闹迎接了陈家人。陈家人给的聘礼丰厚，朱家十分满意。
So the Zhu household rolled out a grand welcome for the Chens, beaming over the generous betrothal gifts.

六礼中，纳彩、问名、纳吉、纳征、请期“五礼”已成，只差亲迎一礼，待来年春暖花开之日再迎娶。
Out of the Six Rites of marriage, five—proposing, exchanging names, divining auspiciousness, presenting betrothal gifts, and setting the wedding date—were sealed in one sweep, leaving only the final rite of escorting the bride home once spring flowers bloomed next year.

申简辰心里念着千里寻，找了个借口，说是到朱府与知府大人商量政务。乔梦容很理解哥哥，自然不会多想。
Pining for Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen cooked up an excuse about discussing local affairs with the Prefect—Qiao Mengrong trusted him without a second thought.

进了城，申简辰吩咐白风去望月轩。
Once inside the city gates, however, Shen Jianchen ordered Bai Feng straight to Wangyue Pavilion.

白风心中嘀咕：“王爷不是要去朱府吗？为何去望月轩？”
Driving along, Bai Feng wondered silently: "Didn't Your Highness say we were going to Zhu Manor—why Wangyue Pavilion?"

不过，白风也开心，他每隔三四日去趟望月轩，在大门外与苦荞相见，次数根本不够。昨日刚见过，今日再见她，那才更好。
Not that Bai Feng complained! Visiting Kuqiao outside the gate every three or four days never felt enough—having just seen her yesterday, getting an extra visit today was pure gold!

可王爷到达望月轩门口，既不下马车，也不允许他找苦荞，大约待了一刻钟，又吩咐去朱府。
Yet arriving outside Wangyue Pavilion, His Highness neither stepped down nor let Bai Feng summon Kuqiao; after sitting mute for a quarter-hour, he ordered them on to Zhu Manor.

朱府正门外张灯结彩，看上去像是有喜事。
Red lanterns and silk festoons decked Zhu Manor's main gate like a wedding feast.

申简辰纳闷，下了马车，来到侧门。
Puzzled, Shen Jianchen stepped down and walked to the side gate.

门口的下人认识他，主动上前行礼：“仪王今日来得不巧，陈家与朱家正结亲，估计老爷脱不开身。”
Recognizing him, the gatekeeper bowed: "Your Highness picked an awkward day—the Chen and Zhu families are sealing a betrothal right now, so Master may be tied up."

申简辰并未强求，再说他也没有什么重要的事，只想从朱晞颜口中探得千里寻一二，既然他忙，还是算了。
Having no real business anyway—just hoping to glean a scrap of news about Qianli Xun from Zhu Xiyan—Shen Jianchen almost left.

可也因好奇问道：“不知两家为谁结亲？”
Still, curiosity nudged him: "Which two are getting betrothed?"

“为姑娘和陈家公子。”
"Our Young Lady and Young Master Chen."

申简辰担忧地问：“可是幽栖居士与陈浩宇？”
Alarm pierced Shen Jianchen: "Youqi Jushi and Chen Haoyu?!"

“正是。”
"Yes, Your Highness."

申简辰感到震惊，他好不容易等到千里寻与文渊分开，没想到这么快朱家又与陈家结亲。
Shock struck Shen Jianchen cold: no sooner had Qianli Xun broken clean from Wenyuan than the Zhus turned right around and engaged her to Chen Haoyu?!

“告诉知府大人，我在门外等他。”
"Tell the Prefect I'm waiting outside for him."

下人感到奇怪，仪王刚才还好好的，为何突然变得不高兴？既然仪王硬是要见老爷，只能去叫：“是，王爷稍等片刻。”
Startled at how fast Prince Yi's sunny mood had turned stormy, the gatekeeper dared not stall: "Right away, Your Highness—please wait a moment."

不多时，朱晞颜满面笑容而出，见到仪王赶紧行礼：“仪王，请里面坐，今日朱府有喜事，您能来真是喜上加喜。”
Moments later Zhu Xiyan hurried out beaming and bowed low: "Please step inside, Prince Yi! Happy occasion at Zhu Manor today—Your Highness's arrival doubles our joy!"

“朱家因喜事忙碌，我就不进去添乱，只是我不明白，为何知府大人要与陈家结亲？难道不忌讳……？”
"With Zhu Manor busy celebrating, I won't intrude. One thing puzzles me, though: why on earth is Lord Prefect marrying your daughter to the Chens? Don't you remember...?"

朱晞颜清楚仪王是说陈浩宇散播流言一事：“那件事下官不想张扬，只想私了，结亲是最好的私了办法，关键陈家长辈对淑真甚是满意，才有今日订亲之事。”
Knowing Prince Yi meant Chen Haoyu's rumor stunt, Zhu Xiyan explained, "I wanted to keep that scandal quiet and settle it privately—a marriage alliance is the cleanest fix, and the Chen elders think the world of Shuzhen, so we sealed the betrothal today."

“幽栖居士可愿意？”
"Does Youqi Jushi agree?"

堂堂王爷竟然关心自己女儿是否乐意一门亲事，朱晞颜觉得奇怪。很快又想明白，也许是因为乔文仙的缘故。
Surprised a royal prince cared whether his daughter liked the match, Zhu Xiyan figured it had to be over Qiao Wenxian.

他听人议论过，乔家自从与苗家定亲后，乔家公子整个人像打了霜的茄子一样。仪王这样问，估计是担心乔文仙知道会伤心。
He had heard street talk that young Qiao looked dead on his feet since getting betrothed to the Miaos—Prince Yi must be worrying this news would crush his buddy further.

“子女婚姻大事，历来由父母做主，淑真愿不愿意不重要。下官知道王爷与乔家公子关系要好，还请王爷多加规劝，既然乔家公子已订亲，以后还是不要再念着淑真。”
"Parents decide marriages, Your Highness—whether Shuzhen likes it doesn't matter. I know Your Highness is close with Young Master Qiao; please advise him to move on now that he's engaged himself."

被朱晞颜误会，申简辰没有必要过多解释，他忍不住表达道：“我本想到朱家提亲，看来已没有机会。”
Not bothering to correct Zhu Xiyan's guess, frustration pushed the truth right past Shen Jianchen's lips: "I had meant to come propose to the Zhu house myself. Looks like I missed my chance."

朱晞颜被惊住，才明白仪王看上淑真，难怪他会帮忙消除流言蜚语，可今日亲已定下，无法挽回。
Thunderstruck, Zhu Xiyan froze—so Prince Yi himself had his eye on Shuzhen?! No wonder he had moved mountains to kill the rumors! Yet the betrothal was already sealed—too late!

“下官惶恐，不知仪王心意，还望王爷恕罪。”
"This humble official is mortified! Had I known Your Highness's heart—please forgive me!"

申简辰装作轻松的样子：“知府大人不必惶恐，我开个玩笑而已，只是想让知府大人知道，幽栖居士不愁嫁，您又何必着急给她订亲？”
Forcing an easy shrug, Shen Jianchen said, "Don't panic, Lord Prefect—just teasing. Only pointing out Youqi Jushi isn't desperate for suitors; no need to rush her into a match."

朱晞颜不知该如何接话，只顾弯腰行礼，沉默着。
At a total loss for words, Zhu Xiyan only stayed bowed low in silence.

“我还有事，不打扰你，希望幽栖居士对这门亲事满意。”申简辰扔下朱晞颜，上马车离开。
"I have errands to run; won't keep you. Hope Youqi Jushi loves her new match." Leaving Zhu Xiyan standing there, Shen Jianchen climbed back into his carriage and rolled off.

朱晞颜站在原地，半晌才缓过神来，仪王说话时显然对他不满，看来他果真看上淑真。朱晞颜埋怨自己为何如此愚钝，不早些发现仪王的心思。
Long after the wheels faded, Zhu Xiyan stood stunned—Prince Yi's sharp edge left zero doubt he truly wanted Shuzhen! Zhu Xiyan kicked himself for being blind as a bat!

仪王即使已与佳和郡主有婚约，若淑真能被仪王纳为妾室，也比嫁给普通人家荣耀，他们朱家也算是皇亲国戚。唉，失去绝好机会。
Even with Princess Jiahe locked as primary wife, entering Prince Yi's Manor as a concubine beat marrying any merchant clan—making the Zhus imperial kin! What a golden chance blown!

朱晞颜叹口气，只怪朱家没有成为皇亲国戚的命。他很快恢复正常，陈家也不错，还是回去继续陪亲家。
Letting out a long sigh, Zhu Xiyan chalked it up to fate—the Chen clan was still a rich prize anyway—and walked back inside to host his in-laws.

白风一边赶马车，一边纳闷。王爷刚才说想到朱家提亲，真是将他吓坏，要是郡主听到，岂不伤心？
Driving away, Bai Feng's head spun: His Highness talking about proposing to the Zhu house had scared him half to death! What if Princess Jiahe ever heard?!

还好王爷说是开玩笑，白风才替郡主松口气。可他就是想不通，为何王爷偏偏开这种玩笑？而且一点儿都不好笑。
Thank goodness His Highness laughed it off as a joke—still, what a weird, unfunny joke!

“白风，去望月轩。”
"Bai Feng, back to Wangyue Pavilion."

听到王爷命令，白风只能扭转马头。又去望月轩，真是猜不透王爷的心思，难道王爷真的喜欢上幽栖居士？白风不敢想下去。
Swinging the reins around, Bai Feng wondered silently: back to Wangyue Pavilion again?! Could His Highness really be in love with Youqi Jushi? He dared not finish that thought.

到达望月轩，申简辰没有下马车，吩咐道：“去，告诉门口的小厮，就说我今日见不到幽栖居士绝不离开，让她出来，到马车上见我。”
Pulling up outside Wangyue Pavilion, Shen Jianchen stayed inside and ordered: "Go tell the gate boy I'm not leaving today until I see Youqi Jushi—tell her to come out to my carriage."

王爷语气严厉，白风不敢违拗，只好跳下马车，给门口的小厮说了。
Hearing iron in his master's voice, Bai Feng hopped down and delivered the ultimatum to the gate boy.

小厮小跑着，朝闺房而去，半路上撞到夏果。
Sprinting toward the bedchamber courtyard, the gate boy crashed straight into Xiaguo.

“你这小子，只顾跑，也不看人，撞得我生疼。”夏果揉着撞疼的手臂。
"Watch where you're going, idiot—nearly broke my arm!" Xiaguo rubbed her bruised elbow.

小厮解释道：“夏果姐姐担待些，仪王在外面等姑娘，我要赶去向姑娘报告。”
"Sorry, Sister Xiaguo! Prince Yi is outside demanding to see Young Lady—gotta report fast!"

“小姐不在闺房，她在后院，你去那里找她。”
"Miss isn't in her room; she's in the back courtyard. Run there."

小厮跑向后院，夏果则朝前院走去，仪王肯定知道乔公子的状况，不如向他打听打听。
As the boy sprinted toward the back courtyard, Xiaguo headed toward the front gate—Prince Yi was bound to know how Young Master Qiao was doing; why not ask?

白风看到夏果从大门出来，上前问：“苦荞呢？”
Spotting Xiaguo step out, Bai Feng hurried up: "Where's Kuqiao?"

夏果很不满：“就知道找苦荞，我要是没记错，你们两个昨日才见过。”
Xiaguo rolled her eyes: "All you care about is Kuqiao—if I remember right, you two just met yesterday!"

白风向夏果吐吐舌头，不再理他。
Bai Feng stuck out his tongue and ignored her.

夏果走到马车跟前行礼道：“仪王，我家小姐在后院训练下人练习拳法，一会儿过来。”
Stepping up to the carriage, Xiaguo bowed: "Prince Yi, my mistress is in the back courtyard training servants in martial arts; she'll be right out."

“知道了。”
"Understood."

夏果大胆问：“仪王，您与乔公子关系好，不知他近期可好？”
Working up her nerve, Xiaguo asked, "Since Prince Yi is close with Young Master Qiao, may I ask how he's been lately?"

“是幽栖居士让你问的？”
"Did Youqi Jushi tell you to ask?"

“不是，是我想问。”
"No—I wanted to ask myself."

“既是你问，我不妨告诉你，文渊近期不错，吃胖了一些。”
"Since you asked, Wenyuan's doing fine—even put a little weight back on."

“不知乔公子什么时候会走出乔府？”
"Any idea when Young Master Qiao will step out of Qiao Manor?"

“这个……说不准，他的确好久没出来，也许是因为天冷。”
"Hard to say—he hasn't come out in ages, maybe just the cold weather."

“谢仪王告知。”
"Thank you for telling me, Prince Yi."

夏果刚问完，千里寻带着苦荞走来……
Right as Xiaguo finished, Qianli Xun walked out with Kuqiao...

当小厮来到后院，千里寻正在训练下人练习拳法，经过多日训练，十人已基本掌握千里拳的要领。
Back in the rear yard, Qianli Xun had been drilling her ten students—every one now solid on Qianli Fist fundamentals.

其中有一名叫红豆的丫鬟，最为突出。千里寻决定，再训练些时日，出去也将红豆带在身边，若碰到欠揍的，就由她出面，省得自己亲自动手。
One maid named Hongdou stood out sharpest of all. Qianli Xun planned to train her a bit more, then bring Hongdou along as a personal bodyguard whenever she went out—no more dirtying her own hands on jerks!

正训练得开心，小厮前来报告：“姑娘，仪王在门外等您，他说不见到姑娘绝不离开。”
Mid-session, the gate boy ran up panting: "Young Lady, Prince Yi is outside the gate swearing he won't leave until he sees you!"

千里寻根本不想再与仪王碰面，可挡不住他前来骚扰，干脆先将这尊大佛哄走，于是带着苦荞来到门外。
Dreading another encounter with Prince Yi yet knowing he'd camp at her gate all day if ignored, she figured she'd better shoo this nuisance off fast and walked out with Kuqiao.

白风自然不管不顾，把苦荞拉到一边，苦荞也心甘情愿。千里寻并不理会，径自走到马车旁。夏果赶紧退到一边。
Bai Feng shamelessly tugged Kuqiao aside right away, Kuqiao tagging along gladly. Ignoring them, Qianli Xun walked straight to the carriage while Xiaguo stepped clear.

“仪王，淑真这厢有礼，不知您今日前来，有何指教？”
"Greetings, Prince Yi. What brings Your Highness to my door?"

千里寻语气平静，难道她对订亲一事甚是满意？申简辰越想越不耐烦：“上车，我有话要问你。”
Hearing her cool, casual tone—wait, was she actually HAPPY about the betrothal?! Impatience flared in Shen Jianchen: "Climb aboard. I have questions for you."

对方什么都不说，直接让她上车，千里寻自然不乐意：“仪王有什么话尽管说，淑真无不听从。”
Refusing to play along, Qianli Xun stood firm: "Whatever Prince Yi has to ask, ask right here—Shuzhen is listening."

“你要不上来，我会天天来望月轩烦你。”
"Climb in, or I'll camp outside Wangyue Pavilion pestering you every single day."

仪王竟然如此赖皮，看来今日不上马车是不行，为了以后的清静日子，不如上去，听听对方到底想说什么。
What a shameless pest! Seeing no easier way to buy future peace, she climbed inside to hear what on earth he wanted.

小姐登上马车，夏果在心里嘀咕：“原来仪王对小姐有意思，以前竟没瞧出来。”
Watching her mistress enter Prince Yi's carriage, Xiaguo muttered inside: "So Prince Yi really DOES have a thing for Miss—never noticed before!"

她瞧向苦荞，与白风聊得甚欢，马车内又有一对暧昧的。夏果甚觉无趣，想到自己孤身一人，便不想在此处待下去，不如回屋继续读书，在想象中与乔公子会面。
Glancing from Kuqiao laughing with Bai Feng to the cozy carriage, Xiaguo felt left out and bored—better head back indoors to her books and daydream about Young Master Qiao.

马车上，千里寻坐定后，顾不上礼节，不满道：“仪王有话快说，有屁快放，你我孤男寡女，单独坐一辆马车上甚是不合适，我本就名声不好，您再这样下去，只怕我的名声会更不好。”
Seated inside the carriage, Qianli Xun dropped all ceremony and snapped, "Spit it out fast, Prince Yi! A single man and woman shut inside a carriage together looks awful—my name's bruised enough without you making it worse!"

申简辰并不生气，只要千里寻在他面前，他都不知道气为何物？
Nothing Qianli Xun said could ever make Shen Jianchen mad—just having her sit across from him melted every trace of temper.

“你怎么能对一位王爷如此无礼？就不怕我治你的罪？”
"Talking so rude to a royal prince—aren't you afraid I'll punish you?"

“王爷都不顾礼俗，我只好以无礼回赠。”
"When a prince tosses propriety out the window, I return the favor."

“算了，我不跟你计较。我来是想问你，陈家与朱家订亲之事，你可知道？难道你就这么着急嫁人？”
"Fine, won't argue with you. Tell me—did you know the Chen and Zhu families just sealed a betrothal? Are you really that desperate to get married?!"

“王爷这话是什么意思？什么时候朱家与陈家订亲？”千里寻担忧不已。
"What are you talking about?! When did the Zhus and Chens seal a betrothal?!" Alarm jolted Qianli Xun upright.

原来她并不知道，申简辰既开心又担忧：“我刚去过朱府，跟办喜事一般，一问才知道陈家前来提亲，看那样子，你跟陈家公子成亲的日子已定……”
So she truly had no clue! Equal parts relieved and urgent, Shen Jianchen told her: "I just came from Zhu Manor—red lanterns everywhere like a wedding! Asked the gatekeeper and learned the Chens just came to propose, and your wedding date with Young Master Chen is practically set..."

千里寻已坐不住，就要下马车，被仪王一把抓住手臂：“你去做什么？”
Shooting up to jump out of the carriage, Qianli Xun felt Prince Yi catch her arm: "Where are you going?"

“去阻止两家订亲。”
"To stop that betrothal!"

“现在去一点儿用处都没有，不如坐下来商量商量对策。”
"Going right now won't change a thing—sit down and let's think of a real countermove."

千里寻见仪王一脸真诚，也许他真的有好办法，便继续坐下。
Seeing dead-serious concern on his face and hoping he might actually have a workable fix, she sat back down.

申简辰放开抓住千里寻的手：“你真的不愿嫁给陈浩宇？”
Releasing her arm, Shen Jianchen asked, "You truly refuse to marry Chen Haoyu?"

“那还用说？”
"Need you even ask?!"

“既如此，我帮你。”
"Then I'll help you."

“怎么帮？”
"How?"

申简辰犹豫片刻，大胆说道：“只要你答应跟我在一起，我便能帮你。”
Pausing a tense heartbeat, Shen Jianchen gambled everything: "Agree to be with me, and I'll stop it."

千里寻二话不说，又起身打算离开，又被仪王一把抓住：“你只要答应做我的妾室，凭我的身份，让两家取消婚约是很容易的事。”
Without a word, Qianli Xun rose again to leave, only for him to catch her wrist once more: "Just agree to enter my manor as a concubine—with my royal status, making both houses cancel this betrothal takes a snap of my fingers!"

千里寻转过头：“这就是仪王的办法？跟没说有什么两样？我不会给您当妾室，也希望仪王以后不要再管我的事。”
Qianli Xun whipped her head back cold: "That's your grand plan?! Might as well have said nothing! I will NEVER be your concubine—and stay out of my business from now on!"

她甩开仪王的手，走下马车，没有心情再回望月轩，喊了苦荞，坐上望月轩的马车直奔朱府。
Wrenching her wrist free, she hopped down from the carriage, skipped going back inside, called Kuqiao over, boarded her own Wangyue Pavilion carriage, and raced straight for Zhu Manor.

申简辰既开心又落寞，开心的是千里寻根本不愿嫁给陈浩宇，落寞的是她依然不愿意接受他的感情。
Left behind, Shen Jianchen felt equal parts relief and hollow ache: glad to his bones she despised marrying Chen Haoyu, crushed that she still shut every door against his heart.
