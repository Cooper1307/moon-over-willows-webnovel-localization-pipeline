## Chapter 243: Winning Over a Wife Takes Method

Twice in one day pushing concubines on him—how desperate was she to offload him? Swallowing the sting, Chen Haoyu refused flatly. "You alone are plenty—add another woman and I'd go nuts! Quit bringing up concubines."

Hoping to splash cold water over his newfound ardor, Qianli Xun spoke blunt. "Seen your type a hundred times: rich playboys sleeping around shameless, then one day acting 'reformed' chasing one true love so everyone applauds like the dirty past never happened! Makes my skin crawl. Save the cheesy lines—not buying it."

Nothing she flung could dent his mood anymore. "What rich playboy? I'm dead broke! Never met anyone I loved before, so I fooled around; now I found her, so I quit. Why hate me for that?"

She let out a cool huff.

Recalling Matron Wei's rule two—care for her—and remembering how she had wolfed down his lamb skewers at the chuiwan field, an idea popped up. "Stuck inside Chen Manor gets boring—I'm free today, let's head out to West Lake! I'll grill fresh skewers by the shore while we admire the water—my barbecue's unbeatable!"

Memories of his noisy lakeside picnic last year lit her fuse instant. "Have you zero civic pride, Chen Haoyu?!"

"Inviting you for grilled meat and scenery—what's civic pride got to do with it?!"

"Last year your gang trampled the grass by West Lake, choked the air with grease smoke, and left piles of trash ruining gorgeous scenery! Trash the lake yourself—don't drag me into it!"

Chastised by the woman he loved, shame actually pricked him. "My bad, my bad—swear I won't grill by West Lake again! Don't stay mad. Pick wherever you want to go and I'll tag along."

"Going nowhere! Vanish right now and leave me in peace!"

Struck out again—even trying to be sweet earned a lecture! Realizing cracking her shell took real strategy—and not starting at the silk house until tomorrow—he decided to go study under the family master: his father. Rising politely, he said, "Won't bother you further, Wife."

"Don't let the door hit you."

Stepping outside with a sigh, he muttered, "How'd I marry the hardest nut in Lin'an—and go crazy enough to fall for her?"

"Call it karma," Qinggua piped up.

"Watch that mouth—" Automatically checking his boot—having sworn off kicking him—Chen Haoyu left Qinggua feeling sheepish. "Just joking, Young Master!"

"No, explain why it's karma."

"Too many girls fell easy for you before, Young Master. Heaven sending an impossible boss like Young Madam balances the scales!"

"Fair point! Guess I'll own it—can't help falling for her anyway."

Reaching his parents' courtyard, Chen Haoyu waved Yingxue quiet so he could spy on how his father actually treated his mother. Peeking past the doorway, he saw Chen Jianglin standing at the dressing table delicately penciling Yao Qingyan's eyebrows while both smiled softly.

"Ever since marrying you, my own brow-drawing skills have gone completely rusty," Yao Qingyan teased.

"With me right here, you never need lift a brush."

"You spoiled me rotten."

"My wife—who else should I spoil?"

Chen Haoyu blinked in awe: Father puts every smooth line I ever tried to total shame! Clearing his throat—"Ahem!"—he stepped inside as Chen Jianglin laid down the brow pencil.

"Haoyu! Come sit!"

Yao Qingyan rose beaming to take his arm. "Not keeping Shuzhen company in Moxiang Courtyard?"

"Need to ask Father's advice on something."

Thrilled to see her son transformed, Yao Qingyan excused herself to go hear the storyteller's fresh huaben with Old Madam Xia, leaving father and son alone.

"Starting at the silk house tomorrow—ask anything you want about trade, I'll hold nothing back," Chen Jianglin invited.

Chen Haoyu gave a sheepish chuckle—"Hehe..."

"Quit giggling and talk."

"Not business, Father... came to ask your secret for winning over your wife! Hehe!"

Bursting into warm laughter, Chen Jianglin nodded. "Winning your wife's heart is just as vital as running the business—came to the right teacher!"

Emboldened, Chen Haoyu leaned in. "Spill all your master moves, Father, so you hold a grandson sooner!"

"No fancy tricks—pure sincerity."

"I'm sincere! Zhu Shuzhen still doesn't buy it!"

"Sincere, yet still calling your wife by her full name?"

"Dropping the surname from now on!"

Seeing his boy genuinely humble, Chen Jianglin happily laid out his three golden rules.

"Rule one: total devotion. Nothing poisons a home faster than a wandering eye leaving your wife weeping. Ever seen me take a concubine?"

"Check! Rule two?"

"Rule two: real companionship. No matter how busy trade gets, carve out time beside her—leave her lonely and suspicion creeps in, unsettling the home; when home shakes, nothing outside succeeds."

"Got it!"

"Rule three—the clincher: master one skill your wife truly needs better than anyone else. Every time she needs it, your face springs first to her mind—once she relies on you, her heart follows!"

Chen Haoyu flashed a huge thumbs-up. "Genius, Father! No wonder Mother never fights with you! Drawing her eyebrows is your secret skill?"

"What else?"

"How long did it take to master?"

"Practiced from our wedding day—she mocked my shaky lines at first, yet after a year I perfected it. Now she won't let herself or a maid touch her brows—only calls for me! Can't live without me!"

As a boy, Chen Haoyu had thought painting a woman's brows looked unmanly; today he saw pure genius. "Rule three's tough—need to scout what Shuzhen actually needs daily, then train like mad!"

"Seeing you pour heart into Shuzhen makes me proud," Chen Jianglin smiled. "Good girl underneath that fiery temper—the day she softens toward you, you've won."

Armed with a battle plan, Chen Haoyu bowed out—and spotted the shortcut walking right beside him. "Qinggua, sneak Kuqiao over to Boya Study—tell her I have urgent questions."

Returning to Moxiang Courtyard, Chen Haoyu waited in Boya Study while Qinggua crept up to Suxin Thatched Hall's doorway peering inside for Kuqiao—until Kuqiao tapped his shoulder from behind. "Creeping like a burglar, Qinggua?"

"Looking for you without interrupting Young Madam! Young Master needs you—just took wife-wooing lessons from Eldest Master and needs insider intel!"

Glad to back Young Master's efforts, Kuqiao smiled. "For his sincerity, I'll go!"

Stepping into Boya Study, Kuqiao blinked to find Chen Haoyu voluntarily flipping through a poetry volume—baffling even Qinggua. "Actually enjoying books now, Young Master?"

Setting down *Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng*, Chen Haoyu smirked. "Uncultured oaf! Never heard 'Within books lie houses of gold and jade-like beauties'?"

"Used to say 'Within books lies nothing but dog poop,' Young Master!"

"Asking for trouble dredging up ancient quotes?" Spotting Kuqiao, he beamed. "Pull up a chair for Kuqiao, Qinggua!"

"Never offered me a chair—she's a servant too!" Qinggua grumbled.

"Comparing yourself to Kuqiao? Sit anywhere you please, Kuqiao!"

"No need for ceremony, Young Master," Kuqiao curtsied. "Ask anything—if it helps Miss, I'll tell all."

"See how polite real servants talk?" Chen Haoyu jabbed at Qinggua before turning eager to Kuqiao. "You can see I'm dead serious about Shuzhen, yet she won't budge. Need your help: what does Shuzhen need most every single day?"

"Need most...?" Kuqiao racked her brain.

"Something she does every single day without fail."

"Oh! Either writing poetry, or..." Catching herself before slipping huaben, she pivoted quick: "...practicing Qianli Fist!"

Chen Haoyu wilted—poetry baffled him completely, and his puny grappling wouldn't survive one flick of her wrist! "Besides those two! Some small daily habit that throws her whole day off if she misses it?"

Kuqiao thought hard. "Drinking my whisked tea?"

Jackpot! Chen Haoyu sprang up beaming. "YES! Tea-whisking I can learn! How hooked is she on your tea?"

"Not obsessed, just deep habit—needs a bowl first thing every morning. Once I got swamped and missed making it, and she stayed fidgety and out of sorts until I finally whisked her a bowl."

"Brilliant! I'll master whisked tea till she can't live without me! Hehehe! Keep this secret from her for now, Kuqiao!"

"My lips are sealed, Young Master!"

To master tea-whisking fast, he needed a real pro—the female tea master at Yongtai Teahouse! Needing tuition silver with empty pockets, he marched straight to Old Madam Xia and told her the honest reason; thrilled he was investing heart into his wife, Old Madam Xia handed over silver on the spot and waved off repayment, calling it seed capital for her future great-grandson!
