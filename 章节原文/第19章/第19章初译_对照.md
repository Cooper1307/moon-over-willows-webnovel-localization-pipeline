第19章 茶楼品诗展才华
## Chapter 19: Chapter 19: Displaying Literary Wit at the Teahouse

曹班见朱淑真很不自在，看来他们三人如此瞧着她实在不妥，便用话语打破尴尬。
Noticing Zhu Shuzhen's discomfort and realizing their staring crossed a line, Cao Ban broke the awkward hush.

“幽栖居士带伤而来，看来练兵官又对你动手，实在不行到知府大人面前告他一状，看他还敢再有下次！”
"Arriving with an injury again, Youqi Jushi—looks like the Training Officer laid hands on you once more! If it comes to it, report him straight to the Prefect—see if he dares pull that again!"

听到这话，千里寻立即明白，朱淑真被家暴之事并对在坐诸位隐瞒，倒令她轻松许多：“诸位放心，不会再有下次，明日我会与林觉和离。”
Realizing her friends already knew about the domestic abuse took a load off Qianli Xun's mind. "Don't worry, everyone—there won't be a next time. Tomorrow I'm getting a mutual divorce from Lin Jue."

苗知瑜说：“早该如此，当日你嫁给练兵官时，我等都替你惋惜。”
Miao Zhiyu nodded. "High time! We all grieved for you the day you married that Training Officer."

安明轩说：“幽栖居士若能离开练兵官，我等都会替你高兴。”
An Mingxuan agreed, "If you break free of him, Youqi Jushi, we'll all celebrate for you!"

东方尘泽神秘地笑道：“有一人要是听说此事，肯定比在座诸位都高兴。”
A sly grin curved Dongfang Chenze's lips. "And one person out there will celebrate harder than all of us combined."

三位公子又互换眼神，露出意味深长的笑容。
The three men traded knowing smiles.

千里寻搞不明白笑容背后的深意，毕竟朱淑真的记忆她一丝一毫都没有，也懒得琢磨，提醒道：“易安居士的诗在此，大家还是尽快品评。”
Clueless about the inside joke—having zero of Shuzhen's memories—Qianli Xun couldn't care less and steered them back: "Yi'an Jushi's poem is right in front of us—let's get to discussing it."

易安居士的诗是乔文仙想方设法得来，苗知瑜一心在诗上，仿佛字里行间也有乔文仙的气息一般。至于三位公子的笑容，她并未关注。
Because Qiao Wenxian had gone out of his way to obtain this poem, Miao Zhiyu had eyes for the verse alone, half imagining his presence between the lines, oblivious to the gentlemen's knowing looks.

听到朱淑真的提醒，苗知瑜第一个发表意见：“从诗的序文能够看出易安居士人在莱州，与德甫先生夫妻团聚，本应高兴才对，可字里行间让我感受到孤独和落寞，难道他们夫妻之间的感情出现问题？如果真是这样，岂不更令我等女子伤感？”
Prompted by Zhu Shuzhen, Miao Zhiyu spoke first: "From the preface we see Yi'an Jushi is in Laizhou, reunited with Master Defu—which ought to bring joy. Yet every line radiates solitude and chill. Has strain crept into their marriage? If even they grow estranged, how heartbreaking for women like us!"

安明轩好奇地问：“为何会令女子伤感？”
Curious, An Mingxuan asked, "Why heartbreaking for women?"

苗知瑜回道：“身为女子最是羡慕易安居士，既嫁给喜欢之人，又与丈夫志趣相投，如果这样还会孤独落寞，像我等待字闺中的女子真不知道以后该嫁什么样的人。相比较这首诗，我还是喜欢‘莫道不销魂，帘卷西风，人比黄花瘦。’起码这几句还能让我感受到易安居士甜蜜的思念之情。”说完苗知瑜一脸怅然。
"Every woman envies Yi'an Jushi—marrying the man she loves and sharing his mind and soul," Miao Zhiyu replied. "If even she feels lonely, unmarried daughters like me have no idea what sort of husband to hope for! Compared to this, I still prefer: 'Don't say she's not heartbroken, / The west wind lifts the blinds, / She's more wasted than the yellow flowers.' [TN: Translated by Ronald Egan] At least those lines carry the sweetness of longing." A wistful shadow lingered on her face.

曹班接着说：“我最喜诗中用典，只有如此，方可展现我的才华。易安居士的诗，正中我意，请允许我在诸位面前献丑，解解其中典故。”
Cao Ban spoke next: "Nothing delights me more than historical allusions—where else can I show off my reading? Yi'an Jushi's poem suits me down to the ground! Allow me to embarrass myself by unpacking her allusions."

东方尘泽笑道：“你啊，就怕别人不知道你读的书多，好吧，我们大度，机会让给你。”
Dongfang Chenze laughed. "Always itching to prove how many volumes you've read! Fine—we'll be generous and yield the floor."

曹班开始侃侃而谈：“此诗有三处用典，第一处‘公路可怜合至此’，袁术字公路，此句来源于《三国志·袁术传》注引《吴书》：袁术兵败后，饥渴交至，叹曰：‘袁术至于此乎！’；第二处‘青州从事孔方兄’，‘青州从事’指美酒，《世说新语·术解》有云：‘桓公有主簿，善别酒，辄令先尝。是谓青州从事，恶者谓平原督邮。’‘孔方兄’指钱财，鲁褒《钱神论》中说道：‘亲之如兄，字曰孔方’；第三处‘乌有先生子虚子’，则是司马相如《子虚赋》中虚拟的人物。”
Cao Ban launched right in: "Three allusions anchor this poem. First, 'Pity Gonglu brought to such a pass'—Yuan Shu's courtesy name was Gonglu, drawn from the Book of Wu cited in the Records of the Three Kingdoms: after defeat, racked by hunger and thirst, Yuan Shu sighed, 'Has Yuan Shu truly come to this?' Second, 'Qingzhou Inspector and Brother Kong Fang'—'Qingzhou Inspector' means fine wine; A New Account of the Tales of the World records that Duke Huan had a clerk skilled at judging wine who called fine vintages 'Qingzhou Inspector' and poor ones 'Pingyuan Postmaster.' 'Brother Kong Fang' means money, from Lu Bao's Discourse on the God of Money: 'Cherished as a brother, styled Kong Fang.' Third, 'Sir No-such and Sir Fantasy' are fictional figures from Sima Xiangru's Rhapsody on Sir Fantasy."

曹班讲完，众人无不佩服。
Everyone listened in open admiration.

千里寻更佩服得五体投地，她觉得自己此刻像一个无知的小学生，慨叹这家伙怎么懂得这么多，更慨叹写这首诗的人学富五车。还是悠着点，听听他们说些什么，再做定论。
Qianli Xun felt blown away—like an ignorant schoolkid next to a walking encyclopedia, marveling both at Cao Ban's memory and Li Qingzhao's vast erudition. Better play it smart, hear everyone out first, then speak.

曹班道了句：“献丑了！”
"I've embarrassed myself!" Cao Ban bowed in mock modesty.

安明轩说：“曹兄果然厉害，引经据典的能力无人能及。”
An Mingxuan praised him: "Nobody matches Brother Cao at unpacking classical allusions!"

安明轩注意到苗知瑜虽被曹班的解析折服，却依然沉浸在感伤之中，安慰道：“苗姑娘不必伤感，德甫先生任莱州知州，公务繁忙，多多少少会冷落易安居士。我以为，如果仅把这首诗看作书写闺中女子的孤独和落寞，则是小觑了易安居士的才华。我读此诗，品味到她表面写孤独和落寞，以及对德甫先生的不满与失落，实则暗藏对朝廷官员的不满，集中在这两句‘青州从事孔方兄，终日纷纷喜生事’，不就是朝廷官员日常生活的写照吗？”
Seeing Miao Zhiyu still steeped in gloom despite Cao Ban's erudition, An Mingxuan offered comfort: "Don't be downcast, Lady Miao. Master Defu serves as Prefect of Laizhou—busy with official duties, he's bound to neglect Yi'an Jushi at times. In my view, reading this solely as a wife's lonely lament underestimates her scope. Beneath the surface loneliness and frustration with her husband lies a veiled jab at court officials—look at 'Qingzhou Inspector and Brother Kong Fang, bustling all day stirring trouble.' Isn't that the spitting image of officialdom's daily life?"

安明轩的解释令苗知瑜释然许多。
An Mingxuan's reading lifted much of the cloud from Miao Zhiyu's brow.

东方尘泽说：“安兄慎言，在座五位的父亲虽是地方官员，也都是朝廷所封，你如此解析，被父辈们知道，可是要挨板子的，呵呵，再说如此解析易安居士的诗，会将她置于众矢之的，慎言！慎言！”
Dongfang Chenze warned with a laugh, "Watch your tongue, Brother An! Every father of the five of us holds an imperial post—let them hear that reading and you'll catch a beating! Worse, spreading such an interpretation would turn Yi'an Jushi into a target. Careful, careful!"

安明轩谦虚道：“东方兄所言甚是，我的理解仅代表个人，也仅限于我们五人知道，恳请大家不要传扬出去。”
An Mingxuan bowed in quick humility. "Well spoken, Brother Dongfang. Just a personal fancy—strictly between the five of us. Please keep it inside these walls!"

东方尘泽批评道：“亏我们五人常在一起论诗作词，竟如此不信任大家，尤其不信任两位才女，该罚，罚你为幽栖居士做盏茶来。”
"We debate poetry together all the time, and you don't trust us—least of all our two talented ladies?" Dongfang Chenze teased. "Penalty! Whisk a bowl of tea for Youqi Jushi!"

安明轩站起来，向大家拱手致歉：“该罚，该罚，我这就为幽栖居士做茶。”说完走到做茶桌前，开始忙碌。
An Mingxuan stood and cupped his hands. "Guilty as charged! I'll whisk tea for Youqi Jushi at once." Stepping to the tea table, he set to work.

曹班赶紧把自己的茶喝完，要求道：“罚你也给我做一盏。”
Cao Ban downed his own tea fast and chimed in, "Penalty includes a bowl for me too!"

“放心，我给你们一人做一盏，我做的茶口感比不上茶楼的茶师，你们可别嫌弃。”安明轩开始认真做茶，边做边用心听其他人如何品鉴此诗。
"Relax, one bowl each! Don't complain if my whisking falls short of the teahouse master's." Focusing on his utensils, An Mingxuan kept listening closely to the discussion.

东方尘泽说：“易安居士这首诗的妙处在于她将两种生活对比着来写，一种是‘青州从事孔方兄，终日纷纷喜生事’，另一种则是‘作诗谢绝聊闭门，燕寝凝香有佳思’。前者带来的结果只能是‘寒窗败几无书史’，后者则是‘静中吾乃得至交，乌有先生子虚子’，两相对比，警示世人应该选择什么样的生活。不管别人如何，易安居士只会选择‘作诗谢绝聊闭门，燕寝凝香有佳思’，所以在这首诗中我们能感受到易安居士的生活志趣。”
Dongfang Chenze gave his view: "The genius of Yi'an Jushi's poem lies in contrasting two worlds: 'Qingzhou Inspector and Brother Kong Fang, bustling all day stirring trouble,' set against 'Declining guests with verse behind closed doors, fragrant quiet breeds fine thoughts.' The former leaves only 'cold window, broken desk, stripped of histories and books'; the latter yields 'in stillness I gain true friends—Sir No-such and Sir Fantasy.' Placed side by side, they challenge the world to choose what life is worth living. Whatever others chase, Yi'an Jushi chooses quiet study behind closed doors—revealing her true spirit."

三位公子与苗知瑜都已谈完，只剩下千里寻。别人已从诗中品出各种滋味，她听了无不赞叹，也在心中组织着语言，还不断问自己，该怎么说才能不辜负朱淑真的才华？
All three men and Miao Zhiyu had spoken; every gaze now turned toward Qianli Xun. Impressed by their insights, she scrambled to assemble her own take—how to speak without shaming Zhu Shuzhen's name?

这时听到东方尘泽的声音：“幽栖居士有何见解？你可是易安居士忠实的追随者。”
Dongfang Chenze prompted, "What are your thoughts, Youqi Jushi? You're Yi'an Jushi's most devoted admirer."

朱淑真竟然是李清照忠实的追随者？更不能给朱淑真丢脸。千里寻越如此想，心里越紧张。她努力稳住，使得自己看上去平淡自如：“各位见解独到，才华横溢，令淑真佩服。大家都品到诗中的精华和韵味所在，以至于我都不知该说些什么……”
A devoted Li Qingzhao fangirl, huh? Even more reason not to drop the ball! Heart pounding, she forced a calm, poised smile. "Every one of your insights is sharp and brilliant—Shuzhen stands in awe. You've already uncovered every subtle layer of the poem, leaving me hard-pressed to add a word..."

夸赞谦虚式的开场白效果不错，四位友人面露笑容，浑身轻松下来。那么接下来她千里寻无论说什么，哪怕稍有不到位之处，他们应该不会与她计较。
Flattery and modesty worked like magic; all four beamed and relaxed. Even if her commentary fell slightly short now, nobody would nitpick.

“既然受邀参加诗会，总得发表发表意见，我在大家的基础之上说说个人的浅薄之见。”
"Still, invited to a salon, I owe a few humble thoughts built upon yours."

千里寻站起来，缓步走到卷轴跟前，她这么做无非是想给自己更多思考的时间。她在卷轴跟前稍立片刻，转身面向大家。
Rising, Qianli Xun strolled over to the hanging scrolls—buying herself precious seconds to think—stood before them a beat, and turned back to face the room.

“别人写诗，诗眼均在诗中，易安居士这首诗诗眼却在序中。‘独坐一室，平生所见，皆不在目前。’一个‘独’字道尽所有，既有女子独处一室没有丈夫陪伴的孤寂，亦有摩诘居士‘独坐幽篁里’品性高洁的孤独。‘平生所见’到底是什么？可以是易安居士以前幸福的婚姻生活，亦可是美好的人物或事物，令人回味无穷，无比向往，只可惜‘皆不在目前’，让人失落惆怅……”
"Most poets hide the poetic core inside the verse itself; Yi'an Jushi placed hers in the preface: 'Sitting alone in one room, all I have known in life lies absent before my eyes.' That single word 'alone' holds everything—both a woman's quiet ache without her husband beside her, and the lofty solitude of Wang Wei's 'Alone I sit in dark bamboo.' [TN: Translated by Burton Watson] What is 'all I have known in life'? Past marital bliss, cherished people, finer days—all longed for, all 'absent before my eyes,' breeding sorrow and loss..."

千里寻还未说完，传来苗知瑜轻声叹息。
Before she finished, a soft sigh escaped Miao Zhiyu.

千里寻心中喜悦：“竟然讲到她心里，不愁不被她认可。”
Score! Qianli Xun celebrated inside. Hit her right in the heart—she's hooked!

千里寻放松许多，越放松，话越多。
Her nerves melted away, words flowing free.

“理解完诗眼，再来看诗句，便能够把握其中意味。‘寒窗败几无书史，公路可怜合至此’是写居住环境，一个‘寒’字既让人感受到秋风萧瑟，也写出环境并不符合诗人的志趣，‘可怜’二字，到底是说什么可怜？接着往下看，‘至此’引出下面两句，我才恍然大悟，原来‘可怜’说的并不是缺少物资，而是精神的贫乏。与‘平生所见，皆不在目前’遥相呼应，印证了诗眼中的‘独’字定然是品性高洁的孤独。易安居士的诗，让我想起了佛教中有言‘如露亦如电，应作如是观’，世间男女情爱，以及名利如同露水，再美好，太阳一出来会消失不见，只有精神的志趣爱好，以及高洁的人格品德才是永恒。”
"Grasp that core, and the lines unlock themselves. 'Cold window, broken desk, stripped of histories and books; pity Gonglu brought to such a pass' paints her surroundings—'cold' evokes autumn chill and a setting mismatched to her spirit. What does 'pity' mourn? Read the next lines introduced by 'such a pass' and it clicks: she isn't mourning material poverty, she's mourning spiritual emptiness! That echoes 'all I have known in life lies absent before my eyes,' proving the 'alone' in her preface is the solitude of a noble mind. Her poem brings to mind the Buddhist saying: 'like dew, or again like lightning—in this way should you view them.' [TN: Translated by Burton Watson] Worldly love, fame, and wealth vanish like morning dew the moment the sun rises; only spiritual pursuits and unblemished character endure."

千里寻终于讲完，深深吐口气，总算没有白白顶着朱淑真的名头，她从容地走回桌前坐下。
Finished at last, Qianli Xun exhaled in quiet relief—she hadn't disgraced Zhu Shuzhen's crown—and strolled back to her seat.

东方尘泽第一个赞道：“幽栖居士的品鉴令人佩服，自愧不如，自愧不如。”
Dongfang Chenze spoke first: "Brilliant critique, Youqi Jushi! I fall far short—far short!"

曹班也说：“自愧不如！”
Cao Ban echoed, "Far short indeed!"

苗知瑜只顾沉浸在个人的伤感中，一言不发。
Lost in her own wistful thoughts, Miao Zhiyu sat silent.

安明轩已将茶做好，先将第一盏端给朱淑真：“用这盏茶表示我的赞赏之情，幽栖居士，请用。”然后又把茶盏给其他人一一端了。
An Mingxuan finished whisking and set the first bowl before Zhu Shuzhen. "This bowl carries my admiration—please drink, Youqi Jushi." Then he served the others one by one.

大家都拿起茶盏刚品了一口新茶，还没来得及放下，听到从楼下传来的声音：“本公子今儿偏让她来陪我！看你们能把我怎样！”接着听到楼下乱成一片。
Everyone lifted their bowls for a single sip—when a rough shout erupted from downstairs: "Today I say she's keeping me company! Let's see what any of you can do about it!" Chaos crashed out below.
