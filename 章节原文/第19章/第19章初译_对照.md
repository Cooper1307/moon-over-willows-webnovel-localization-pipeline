# Chapter 19: Displaying Talent at the Poetry Gathering

Cao Ban saw that Zhu Shuzhen was very uncomfortable. The three of them staring at her like that was clearly inappropriate, so he broke the awkward silence with words.

"Youqi Jushi came wounded. It seems the Military Training Officer has laid hands on you again. If it comes to it, file a complaint with the Prefect. See if he dares do it again!"

Hearing this, Qianli Xun immediately understood. Zhu Shuzhen hadn't hidden the domestic violence from those present. That actually made her feel more at ease. "Rest assured, everyone. There won't be a next time. Tomorrow I'll divorce Lin Jue."

Miao Zhiyu said, "It's about time. When you married the Military Training Officer, we all felt sorry for you."

An Mingxuan said, "If Youqi Jushi can leave the Military Training Officer, we'll all be happy for you."

Dongfang Chenze smiled mysteriously. "There's one person who, upon hearing this news, will be even happier than all of us here."

The three gentlemen exchanged glances again, revealing meaningful smiles.

Qianli Xun couldn't figure out the deeper meaning behind those smiles. After all, she didn't have a single shred of Zhu Shuzhen's memories, and she couldn't be bothered to ponder it. She reminded them, "Yi'an Jushi's poem is right here. Let's get on with appreciating it."

Yi'an Jushi's poem was obtained through great effort by Qiao Wenxian. Miao Zhiyu's mind was fully on the poem, as if Qiao Wenxian's presence lingered between the lines. As for the gentlemen's smiles, she paid them no attention.

Hearing Zhu Shuzhen's reminder, Miao Zhiyu was the first to offer her opinion. "From the poem's preface, we can see Yi'an Jushi is in Laizhou, reunited with Mr. Defu. She should be happy. Yet between the lines, I sense loneliness and melancholy. Could there be trouble in their marriage? If that's truly the case, wouldn't it make us women even more sorrowful?"

An Mingxuan asked curiously, "Why would it make women sorrowful?"

Miao Zhiyu replied, "As women, we envy Yi'an Jushi most. She married the man she loved, and she and her husband share the same interests. If even she feels lonely and melancholy, what should women like me, still waiting in our boudoirs, know about what kind of man to marry? Compared to this poem, I still prefer 'Do not say it does not consume the soul— / The west wind curls the curtain, / And a person is thinner than the yellow chrysanthemums.' [TN: From Li Qingzhao's "Drunk in Flowers: Double Ninth," translated by Xu Yuanchong] At least those lines let me feel Yi'an Jushi's sweet longing." After speaking, Miao Zhiyu looked wistful.

Cao Ban continued, "I most enjoy historical allusions in poetry. Only thus can one's talent be displayed. Yi'an Jushi's poem suits my taste perfectly. Please allow me to show off my ignorance before you all and explain the allusions within."

Dongfang Chenze laughed. "You're just afraid others don't know how much you've read. Fine, we'll be magnanimous and give you the chance."

Cao Ban began to speak eloquently. "This poem has three allusions. The first, 'How pitiable Yuan Shu has come to this'—Yuan Shu's courtesy name was Gonglu. After his army was defeated, hunger and thirst both arrived. He sighed, 'Has Yuan Shu come to this!' The second, 'Qingzhou Attendant and Brother Kong Fang'—'Qingzhou Attendant' refers to fine wine, and 'Brother Kong Fang' refers to money. [TN: These are classical allusions to wine and coin, respectively.] The third, 'Mr. Nonexistent and Mr. Imaginary,' are fictional characters from Sima Xiangru's 'Rhapsody on Master Zixu.'"

After Cao Ban finished, everyone was impressed.

Qianli Xun was even more impressed. She felt like a clueless schoolgirl at that moment. She marveled at how much this guy knew, and marveled even more at how learned the person who wrote this poem must have been. Better take it slow, listen to what they say, then make a conclusion.

Cao Ban said, "I've shown off my ignorance."

An Mingxuan said, "Brother Cao is truly impressive. Your ability to cite classics and allusions is unmatched."

An Mingxuan noticed that although Miao Zhiyu was impressed by Cao Ban's analysis, she was still immersed in melancholy. He comforted her, "Lady Miao, don't be sad. Mr. Defu serves as Prefect of Laizhou. He's busy with official duties and will inevitably neglect Yi'an Jushi to some degree. I think if we only view this poem as expressing the loneliness and melancholy of a woman in the boudoir, we underestimate Yi'an Jushi's talent. When I read this poem, I taste that on the surface it writes of loneliness and melancholy, and dissatisfaction and disappointment with Mr. Defu, but hidden within is dissatisfaction with court officials. This is concentrated in these two lines: 'Qingzhou Attendant and Brother Kong Fang, all day long bustling and causing trouble.' Isn't this a portrayal of the daily life of court officials?"

An Mingxuan's explanation relieved Miao Zhiyu considerably.

Dongfang Chenze said, "Brother An, be careful with your words. The fathers of the five of us here, though local officials, were all appointed by the court. If you analyze it this way and our elders find out, you'll be caned. Hehe. Besides, analyzing Yi'an Jushi's poem this way will put her in the crosshairs. Be careful! Be careful!"

An Mingxuan said modestly, "Brother Dongfang is right. My understanding represents only myself, and should be known only to the five of us. I implore everyone not to spread it."

Dongfang Chenze criticized, "We five often gather to discuss poetry and write ci, yet you don't trust everyone, especially the two talented ladies. You deserve punishment. You're punished to make a bowl of tea for Youqi Jushi."

An Mingxuan stood up and cupped his hands to apologize to everyone. "I deserve punishment. I deserve punishment. I'll make tea for Youqi Jushi right now." With that, he walked to the tea table and began working.

Cao Ban quickly finished his own tea and requested, "As punishment, make one for me too."

"Don't worry, I'll make one for each of you. My tea-making skills can't compare to the teahouse's tea masters. Don't despise it." An Mingxuan began making tea seriously, listening carefully to how others appreciated the poem as he worked.

Dongfang Chenze said, "The brilliance of Yi'an Jushi's poem lies in how she contrasts two kinds of life. One is 'Qingzhou Attendant and Brother Kong Fang, all day long bustling and causing trouble.' The other is 'Writing poetry, I decline conversation and close the door. In the fragrant bedroom, fine thoughts arise.' The result of the former can only be 'Cold window, broken desk, no books or records.' The latter is 'In stillness I find my closest friends—Mr. Nonexistent and Mr. Imaginary.' The two contrasted warn the world what kind of life one should choose. No matter what others do, Yi'an Jushi would only choose 'Writing poetry, I decline conversation and close the door. In the fragrant bedroom, fine thoughts arise.' So in this poem we can feel Yi'an Jushi's life aspirations."

The three gentlemen and Miao Zhiyu had all finished speaking. Only Qianli Xun remained. Others had already tasted various flavors in the poem. She admired everything they said. She was organizing language in her mind, constantly asking herself: How should I speak to not disappoint Zhu Shuzhen's talent?

Then she heard Dongfang Chenze's voice: "Does Youqi Jushi have any insights? You are Yi'an Jushi's loyal follower."

Zhu Shuzhen was actually Yi'an Jushi's loyal follower? She couldn't embarrass Zhu Shuzhen even more. The more Qianli Xun thought this way, the more nervous she became. She steadied herself to appear calm and composed. "Everyone's insights are unique, your talents brilliant. Shuzhen is impressed. You've all captured the essence and charm of the poem, so much so that I don't even know what to say..."

The modest opening of praise worked well. The four friends smiled, their bodies relaxing. No matter what Qianli Xun said next, even if slightly off, they probably wouldn't hold it against her.

"Since I'm invited to the poetry gathering, I should at least express my opinion. I'll offer my humble thoughts based on what everyone has said."

Qianli Xun stood up and walked slowly to the scroll. She did this purely to give herself more time to think. She stood before the scroll for a moment, then turned to face everyone.

"When others write poetry, the poetic core is within the poem. But Yi'an Jushi's poem has its poetic core in the preface. 'Sitting alone in one room, all I've seen in my life is not before my eyes.' The single character 'alone' says it all. It contains both the loneliness of a woman alone in a room without her husband's company, and the noble solitude of Wang Wei's 'Sitting alone in the secluded bamboo grove.' What exactly is 'all I've seen in my life'? It could be Yi'an Jushi's previous happy married life, or it could be beautiful people or things. It leaves one with endless reflection, infinitely yearning. But alas, 'is not before my eyes,' making one feel lost and wistful..."

Before Qianli Xun finished speaking, Miao Zhiyu's soft sigh came through.

Qianli Xun was delighted inwardly. *I've actually touched her heart. No worry about not being recognized.*

Qianli Xun relaxed much more. The more relaxed she became, the more words flowed.

"Understanding the poetic core, then looking at the poem's lines, one can grasp the meaning within. 'Cold window, broken desk, no books or records. How pitiable Yuan Shu has come to this' describes the living environment. The single character 'cold' makes one feel the bleak autumn wind, and also shows the environment doesn't match the poet's aspirations. The two characters 'pitiable'—what exactly is pitiable? Continuing to read below, 'come to this' leads to the next two lines. Only then do I suddenly realize. It turns out 'pitiable' doesn't refer to lacking material goods, but spiritual poverty. It echoes 'all I've seen in my life is not before my eyes' from afar, confirming the 'alone' in the poetic core is definitely the solitude of noble character. Yi'an Jushi's poem reminds me of the Diamond Sutra: 'Like dew and like lightning, thus should one view it.' [TN: From the Diamond Sutra, a key Buddhist text.] Love between men and women, as well as fame and profit, are like dew. No matter how beautiful, once the sun rises, they vanish. Only spiritual pursuits and noble character endure."

Qianli Xun finally finished speaking. She took a deep breath. At least she hadn't wasted Zhu Shuzhen's name. She walked calmly back to the table and sat down.

Dongfang Chenze was the first to praise: "Youqi Jushi's appreciation is admirable. I'm ashamed. I'm ashamed."

Cao Ban also said, "I'm ashamed!"

Miao Zhiyu was immersed in her own melancholy and didn't speak a word.

An Mingxuan had finished making tea. He first served the first bowl to Zhu Shuzhen. "Use this bowl of tea to express my admiration. Youqi Jushi, please enjoy." Then he served tea bowls to everyone else one by one.

Everyone had just picked up their tea bowls and tasted a sip of the new tea. Before they could set them down, they heard a voice from downstairs: "This young master insists she accompany me today! See what you can do about it!" Then chaos erupted downstairs.
