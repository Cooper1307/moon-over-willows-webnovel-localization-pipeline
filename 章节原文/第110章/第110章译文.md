## Chapter 110: Visiting His Beloved to Speak His Heart

While the clerk fetched a small wooden box to pack the peeing-boy figurine, two young women strolled in, chatting as they browsed the clay dolls.

"This shop's clay figurines are wonderful! Which one should I buy for him?"

"Have you no shame? Careful folks don't start gossiping about you the way they do about Youqi Jushi!"

"How can you lump me in with her? My parents arranged this betrothal, with every matchmaker and betrothal gift in place!"

"Sigh. Who knew Youqi Jushi writes great poetry while her conduct leaves so much to be desired?"

"Tell me about it! Nothing matters more to a maiden of the inner chambers than her chastity, yet she's bold enough to put it in verse and act on it, putting every sheltered young lady to shame! Makes you wonder what the men she traded secret glances and had affairs with think..."

Shen Jianchen could not listen to another word. Hearing "traded secret glances"—wasn't that talking about him?

Qianli Xun had not spared his own yearning glances a second thought, so how could he let two strangers smear her?

He marched right up to them. "Two young ladies blessed with faces fair as flowers—pity your words don't match your looks. Keep running your mouths behind people's backs and you might never find a husband's house. Even if you do, once your in-laws learn their future daughter-in-law is a gossipmonger, they'll regret the match and might even call off the wedding."

Flattered at first by his praise of their looks, both girls bristled at the sting that followed.

One snapped, "What's the use lecturing us? Everyone on the street is talking about her! Are all the girls in Lin'an going to end up unwed?"

The other grabbed a lucky-doll figurine and handed it to the clerk. "Wrap this up. What rotten luck today, running into a meddler!"

Taking her doll, the clerk handed the wooden box holding the peeing boy to Bai Feng.

Bai Feng paid the silver, annoyed at how rude the girls had been to His Highness, and snapped back, "Keep badmouthing folks behind their backs and watch your mouths rot off tomorrow!"

Both girls flared up hotter, one barking, "What business is it of yours if we talk about Youqi Jushi? Dogs catching mice—mind your own business!"

Bai Feng yelled back, "Gossipmongers! A pair of gossipmongers!"

Shen Jianchen did not stop him; they had it coming for slandering the woman he loved.

The clerk jumped in as peacemaker: "Now, now, folks—harmony brings wealth! Sir, it isn't right for the young ladies to gossip, sure, yet the whole city's buzzing right now. Can you police every last person? Blame the Prefect's daughter for crossing lines no sheltered lady should cross and inviting talk."

Shen Jianchen asked, "You gossip about her too?"

The clerk chuckled. "Who doesn't? Everybody's talking. Heard the Prefect even threw his own daughter out to save the Zhu family's name."

Taking her wrapped figurine, the second girl snorted, "Even her own father doesn't want her—what's wrong with us saying two words? Hmph!"

Paying up, both girls flounced out in a huff.

Dread deepened in Shen Jianchen's chest; how hard must Qianli Xun be hurting right now? Something about these rumors smelled fishy, as if engineered on purpose.

"Clerk, how did you hear these rumors about Miss Zhu?"

"Running a shop, folk stream in and out all day; hear enough talk and it sticks. Honest truth, I'm busy minding the counter and never cared a fig about poets. Don't laugh, sir, I don't know a single poem by heart, yet now even I remember 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'! Haha—the only difference between refined scholars and common folk like us is dressing up dirty deeds in fancy words, haha..."

Unable to stomach another word, Shen Jianchen walked out without reply, boarded his carriage, and headed east.

He kicked himself for holing up in Youlin Retreat for over a month. Had he strolled the streets sooner, he might have caught the rumors early and snuffed them out before they swept the whole city.

Yet without those rumors, the proposal would have gone through, Qianli Xun would belong to Wenyuan, and he would not be riding east right now with a gift in hand.

Shen Jianchen gave his own cheek a light slap and muttered under his breath, "How can you think like that? You're an engaged man! Wenyuan is suffering, Qianli Xun must be hurting worse, and here you are feeling glad—shame on you!"

Yet he could not crush that stubborn spark of gladness—glad that one sliver of hope still survived between him and Qianli Xun.

Reaching the east quarter, it took no effort to find the wealthy neighborhood and locate Wangyue Pavilion.

Compared with Zhu Manor, Wangyue Pavilion was tiny, yet its layout held an understated grace.

Matron Wei had picked the house straight to her mistress's tastes: graceful orchids and slender bamboo filled the grounds, and chrysanthemums of every hue stood in full bloom across the yard, vying in beauty. It combined the best touches of Zhulan Residence and her old courtyard at Zhu Manor.

Qianli Xun loved it; above all, her bedroom was arranged identical to her old room at Zhu Manor, wrapping her in the comfort of home.

The maids busied themselves unpacking clothes while Matron Wei took charge of household affairs and laid down rules for the servants.

With nothing to fuss over, Qianli Xun collapsed onto bed to sleep. One day had dragged her through wild highs and lows; she needed to collect her thoughts, hoping sleep might wash every misfortune away.

Deep in slumber, she dreamed her huaben stories had taken Song readers by storm—books selling out the instant they left the press, a copy in every hand down every lane, her name headed straight into the history books.

"Miss! Miss, wake up! Prince Yi is here to visit!" Kuqiao's voice yanked her out of her dream.

Opening her eyes and sitting up, she glanced out the window at gathering dusk. "Who's here?"

Kuqiao repeated, "Prince Yi."

"Prince Yi? How did he know I moved to Wangyue Pavilion? Don't tell me you leaked it to Bai Feng!"

"You wrong me, Miss! However long you haven't seen Prince Yi, I haven't seen Bai Feng either."

Remembering her vow never to meet Prince Yi alone again, Qianli Xun said, "Go tell Prince Yi I'm in a foul mood and have no time to see him."

"Miss, he's Prince Yi—turning him away like that might not go over well."

Qianli Xun felt stuck: see him, and he would start tossing sparks at her again; refuse him, and she risked offending a royal prince.

Kuqiao handed her the ideal excuse: "Better see him, Miss—you can ask him how Young Master Qiao is doing."

Qianli Xun swung out of bed at once. "Help me freshen up! I want Prince Yi to see me looking my absolute best."

Dressed and polished by Kuqiao, she stepped into the main hall, where Prince Yi sat waiting with Matron Wei keeping him company.

Seeing her mistress enter, Matron Wei rose. "Since Prince Yi has words for our Young Lady, I'll take my leave."

Shen Jianchen nodded, his gaze lingering on Qianli Xun. She had taken great care with her dress and makeup—had she really dressed up just to see him? Could she have feelings for him after all?

A second later he saw through it: she was only using bright makeup to mask the ache inside.

On the ride over, Bai Feng had figured out the clay figurine was for Youqi Jushi. Baffled at first, he had rationalized it as His Highness visiting on Young Master Qiao's behalf and not wanting to arrive empty-handed—still, what on earth was the idea behind a peeing boy?

Spotting Kuqiao, he dropped the puzzle, set the wooden box on the table, tossed Kuqiao a wink, and slipped out in silence with her.

Alone at last, Shen Jianchen felt satisfied and stood up. "How is Youqi Jushi holding up?"

Skipping an answer, Qianli Xun bowed. "How did Prince Yi find Wangyue Pavilion?"

"Anyone I want to find can't stay hidden from me."

Right—he was a royal prince; what a pointless question.

"How is Wenyuan?"

Two sentences in and she was already asking about Wenyuan. Stung, Shen Jianchen answered anyway: "With the proposal off, Wenyuan is taking it hard. He begged me to go persuade Grandfather at Qiao Manor, yet Grandfather swore he'd give up his life before allowing your marriage. There was nothing I could do."

A wave of bitterness choked Qianli Xun silent.

Spotting the bruise on her hand, Shen Jianchen said, "Youqi Jushi needs to take better care of herself."

Swamped in heartache, she had no urge to talk, yet etiquette before a prince demanded a reply.

"Thank you for your concern, Prince Yi. Please pass word to Wenyuan to meet me beneath the Divine Willow tomorrow."

To Shen Jianchen, the Divine Willow belonged to him and Qianli Xun alone. "Why meet beneath the Divine Willow?"

"Why not beneath the Divine Willow?" Qianli Xun thought he was meddling way too much—now policing where she and Wenyuan met? What a busybody!

"Fine, suit yourself." Shen Jianchen picked up the wooden box. "Brought you a gift. Guaranteed to lift your spirits once you open it."

"Did Wenyuan send it?"

"Can't it be from me?"

Qianli Xun hesitated to accept. "Prince Yi shouldn't spend money on me."

Shen Jianchen pressed the box into her arms. "Hardly any money at all—buying you a gift makes me happy. Take it, or I won't pass your message to Wenyuan."

Shen Jianchen turned to leave.

Clutching the wooden box, tangled emotions churned inside Qianli Xun. "Prince Yi!"

Shen Jianchen halted without looking back. "Yes?"

"Wenyuan is the one I love."

"I know."

"Princess Jiahe is a wonderful girl. Don't you dare let her down."

"No need for you to remind me."

With that, Shen Jianchen strode out of the hall and across the courtyard. Seeing him leave, Bai Feng bade Kuqiao a hasty farewell and followed his master out of Wangyue Pavilion.
