## Chapter 241: He Really Changed

Eager to see her grandson—knowing he would visit his parents first—Old Madam Xia had marched straight to Eldest Son's courtyard at dawn, eaten breakfast there, and waited.

Hardly had Yingxue announced "Second Young Master is here" than Chen Haoyu breezed through the door with Qinggua, bowed briskly—"Father, Mother"—and dove straight into Old Madam Xia's arms. "Grandmother, I missed you to death!"

Stroking his hair fondly, Old Madam Xia asked, "Missed you too! Not mad at us for locking our gates on you?"

"Too grateful to ever be mad!"

Every face beamed until Chen Jianglin put on his stern father mask. "Warm morning—quit draping yourself on Grandmother and stand straight to answer questions."

Chen Haoyu hopped upright. "Before you quiz me, Father, I have something to ask you."

"Answer your elders first."

Yao Qingyan jumped in. "Did Shuzhen truly forgive those rumors?"

"Would I lie? I'm her husband—we're one family! Since when does family hold grudges against family? Right, Mother?"

Hearing him toss out "one family" cracked even Chen Jianglin's stern mask into a chuckle. Taking a sip of tea to steady his face, Chen Jianglin probed, "Shuzhen let you off way too easy—ought to have kept you locked up longer! What progress could you possibly make in two days?"

Chen Haoyu paused, then spoke straight. "Two days isn't long, yet something shifted in me. I'm done roaming outside—want to stay home and pitch in. Father, assign me some business work so I can earn my own silver, instead of coming up empty-handed when I want to buy my wife a gift and getting laughed at."

Surprise flashed across Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan's faces, blooming instantly into joy. Even Chen Jianglin dropped all pretense and grinned. "That's what you came to ask?"

"Exact right!"

"Seriously quitting the streets and joining family trade?"

"He just said yes—why keep grilling him?" Old Madam Xia chided her son. "Drop that habit of doubting your own boy!"

"My fault, Mother—I'll fix it," Chen Jianglin chuckled while Yao Qingyan dabbed happy tears: "Haoyu truly grew up! Happiest day of my life!"

Seeing how ecstatic one decent choice made his family suddenly struck Chen Haoyu with a sharp pang of sheepish guilt over years of disappointing them. Changing the pace fast, he asked, "When do I start, Father?"

"Whenever you're ready."

"Sooner the better!"

"With our southwest trade booming, the silk house is swamped. Report there—I'll send word to Steward Qiu to mentor you. Veteran hand; learn everything you can from him."

"Count on me, Father! Uh—what's the monthly pay?"

Chen Jianglin laughed. "Haven't lifted a finger and already asking wages? Nail the work first, and silver won't be short!"

"Not sending him today, are you?" Old Madam Xia cut in. "Once he gets busy I'll barely see him—let him start tomorrow!"

Nobody said no to Mother. Agreeing every bit of this miracle belonged to Shuzhen, all three decided to walk over and thank her together.

Meanwhile, right after Chen Haoyu left Moxiang Courtyard, Matron Wei had invited Qianli Xun for a morning stroll through Chen Manor's sprawling gardens—her first real look around since arriving.

Strolling amid birdsong and spring blossoms, Matron Wei marveled, "Didn't realize Chen Manor hid gardens this lush—flowers on every path!"

Remembering Chen Haoyu's bouquet, Qianli Xun remarked, "Even got roses here."

Glancing around, Matron Wei saw none. "Where?"

Not wanting to bring up his gift, Qianli Xun hedged, "Maids mentioned a small rose garden somewhere."

Dropping flowers, Matron Wei steered to her real reason for the walk. "Letting go of last year's slander in mere days—even at my age I couldn't match your generosity, Miss."

"Holding grudges only tortures myself. Letting him off lets me off too—now the whole garden looks twice as bright."

"Grasping that wisdom at your age is remarkable."

"Hardly my wisdom—that elderly nun at the hillside nunnery woke me up."

"Plenty listen to her sermons; few actually wake up. You have genuine insight, Miss."

"Takes insight to spot insight, Matron!" Both shared a warm laugh.

Reaching a wide pond dotted with broad lotus pads soon to push up buds, they rested inside a lakeside pavilion. Gazing over the water, Matron Wei asked out of the blue, "Miss, do you truly plan never to consummate your marriage with Young Master—living separate lives forever?"

Unfazed, Qianli Xun answered crisp, "Our prenuptial contract said no meddling."

"Heard you burned it."

"We'll draw up a new one."

"I usually indulge everything you wish, Miss, yet on this I must speak plain."

Knowing Matron Wei never wasted words, Qianli Xun turned attentive. "Go ahead, Matron."

"How do you find the Chen household?"

"Wonderful in every way—except Chen Haoyu."

"What if Young Master genuinely reforms?"

"Him? Reform?"

"From what I've watched, brief as your discipline was, he has changed deeply."

"Don't fall for it, Matron! Just a fresh act to disarm me."

"I disagree. He's truly changing."

Forced to entertain it for a second, Qianli Xun shrugged. "So what? Him changing doesn't mean I have to sleep with him!"

"I've lived long enough to know how hard marriage is for a woman, and how rare a decent family is. The Chens outshine the Lins and Qiaos ten times over—treasure it, Miss. If Young Master turns good, learn to open your heart slowly."

Hearing Matron Wei push this meant the inevitable loomed closer than she wanted. "I don't love Chen Haoyu."

Matron Wei sighed. "How many women in this world ever get to marry for love?"

Just then Kuqiao hurried up. "Miss! Old Madam Xia, Eldest Master, and Eldest Madam are waiting at Moxiang Courtyard!"

Matron Wei stood. "Come greet the elders—bet they came to thank you."

Rising with a tangled mind, Qianli Xun knew full well society expected her to share her husband's bed and bear children—yet another man still filled her heart. Where was Shen Jianchen now? Remembering his vow at Zuiyun Tower—"I'll stay chaste for you"—flooded her with equal parts ache and guilt toward Mengrong. Trying to shove his image out only made it dance sharper before her eyes.

Walking back into Moxiang Courtyard, she paused at the scene beneath the hehuan tree: Old Madam Xia lounging in the rattan chair, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan seated around the table sipping Hongdou's whisked tea, Chen Haoyu perched on a low stool leaning affectionately against his grandmother. Warmth and peace radiated from the cluster—the exact big, loving family she had always longed for. In her bones, Chen Manor already felt like home... if only Chen Haoyu weren't part of the deal.

Before she could finish the daydream, a warm hand caught hers. "Hey! Where'd you run off to? Kept us waiting!"

Snapping awake, she whipped her hand clean out of Chen Haoyu's grasp. "Keep your hands off me!"

"All right, all right, no touching!" Grinning sheepish, he trotted back to his stool beside Old Madam Xia.

Matron Wei read the whole picture at a glance: Miss loved this home, just needed time for the husband. Stepping forward, she greeted them warmly. "Had I known you three were visiting, I wouldn't have dragged Miss off for a walk!"

Hongdou and Amei fetched two more chairs while Qianli Xun lingered a beat taking in the cozy tableau. Yao Qingyan rose and walked over. "Why stand back there, Shuzhen? Still mad at us?"

"Not at all—just looked so peaceful I wanted to freeze the picture in my head."

Taking her hand, Yao Qingyan smiled. "If it's lovely, step inside and become part of the picture."

Those simple words struck a deep chord—why stay outside gazing in when she could step inside? Walking under the tree, she curtsied. "Had I known you elders loved this spot, I'd have set more chairs out."

"One rattan chair and table keep the yard uncluttered," Old Madam Xia said. "Sit down!"

As Qianli Xun took a seat beside Matron Wei, Chen Haoyu's gaze stayed glued to her face with shameless fondness—ducking only to steal more looks whenever she glared back. Watching her son, Yao Qingyan beamed silently: Love really did move mountains, turning a wild rake overnight—making Shuzhen the Chen family's lucky star!

"We came to thank you today, Shuzhen," Yao Qingyan began.

"First for letting last year's rumors go," Chen Jianglin added, "and second for turning Haoyu around!"

Refusing stolen credit, Qianli Xun spoke straight. "No thanks needed. Dropping the rumors was for my own peace of mind, and I can't claim credit for Chen Haoyu—I only punished him for payback, and nobody transforms in two days."

"Exactly!" Chen Haoyu jumped in. "I was always a gem underneath—needed zero discipline!"

"Whatever your motive," Old Madam Xia chuckled from her rattan chair, "the result is pure gold, so credit belongs to you! Name any reward you want for giving me back a good grandson—even if it's tough, Grandmother will deliver!"

About to wave any reward aside, Qianli Xun suddenly remembered Matron Wei's warning by the lotus pond. Sooner or later these elders would press her to consummate the marriage—better cash this blank check right now to buy herself breathing room!

"Since you offered, Grandmother, I won't be shy! Keep gold, jewels, estates, and trinkets—I only ask one promise from you elders."
