# 第78章 能拖一年拖一年

---

儿子走后，乔守仁吩咐下人："去！把夫人和米姨娘请来，就说我有要事商量。"

After his son left, Qiao Shouren ordered a servant: "Go! Bring Madam Qian and Concubine Mi. Tell them I have important matters to discuss."

---

下人去了不多时，钱氏与米氏一起前来，跟乔守仁打过招呼后，各自落坐。

The servant didn't take long before returning with Madam Qian and Concubine Mi. After greeting Qiao Shouren, they each took their seats.

---

钱氏是主母，自然先发话："老爷，不知唤我们二人前来有何要事？"

As the principal wife, Madam Qian naturally spoke first: "My lord, what important matters did you wish to discuss with us?"

---

乔守仁已命下人把茶具搬到屋内，继续品茶，品完一口说："刚才文渊回来了一趟……"

Qiao Shouren had already ordered the servants to bring in the tea set. He continued tasting his tea, then spoke after a sip: "Wenyuan just came back for a visit..."

---

话还未说完，米氏急忙问："儿子回来为何不来看我跟姐姐？"

Before he could finish, Concubine Mi asked anxiously: "Why didn't our son come to see Sister and me when he returned?"

---

"急什么？听我把话说完。"乔守仁批评完，继续心平气和地说，"是我不让他去找你们，你们两个妇道人家，容易感情用事，他若见到你们，只怕会坏了大事。"

"Why the rush? Let me finish." After reprimanding her, Qiao Shouren continued calmly, "I was the one who told him not to see you. You two are emotional women. If he had seen you, I'm afraid it would have ruined the bigger plan."

---

钱氏不明所以："不知文渊为何事回来？"

Madam Qian didn't understand: "What was Wenyuan's reason for returning?"

---

"是为婚姻之事，他有中意的姑娘，回来请我们去女方家里提亲。"乔守仁显得很轻松。

"It's about marriage. He has a girl he likes and came back to ask us to propose marriage to her family." Qiao Shouren appeared quite relaxed.

---

钱氏更是不解："这是好事，我与妹妹听了只会开心，老爷怎能说我们会坏事？"

Madam Qian was even more puzzled: "That's good news. Sister and I would only be happy to hear it. How could you say we'd ruin things?"

---

乔守仁说："本是一件桩好事，不过文渊喜欢的人是朱家姑娘。"

Qiao Shouren said: "It would have been good news, except the girl Wenyuan likes is the Zhu family's daughter."

---

米氏问："可是朱家刚刚和离的那个女词人？"

Concubine Mi asked: "The female poet who just divorced from the Zhu family?"

---

乔守仁点点头："正是。"

Qiao Shouren nodded: "Exactly."

---

钱氏说："文渊与朱家姑娘倒是志同道合，可惜那姑娘离过婚，怎么能配得上我家文渊？"

Madam Qian said: "Wenyuan and the Zhu family's daughter are indeed like-minded. It's a pity the girl has been divorced—how could she be worthy of our Wenyuan?"

---

米氏撇撇嘴："当然配不上！我上次到裁缝铺做衣裳，碰到通判家的娘子，量完尺寸后，跟她聊了一会儿，从她口中得知朱家姑娘看着柔弱，其实厉害着呢，连陈家公子都打不过，估计林练兵官嫌弃她是母老虎，才不得不同意和离。"

Concubine Mi curled her lips: "Of course she's not worthy! Last time I went to the tailor shop for clothes, I ran into the magistrate's wife. After taking measurements, I chatted with her for a while. From her, I learned that the Zhu family's daughter looks weak but is actually quite fierce—even Young Master Chen couldn't beat her. I estimate Military Training Officer Lin had to agree to the divorce because he found her a tigress."

---

钱氏问："秦夫人怎么知道这些？"

Madam Qian asked: "How does Madam Qin know these things?"

---

米氏回道："秦夫人的女儿苗知瑜经常跟朱家姑娘参加诗会，她自然晓得。"

Concubine Mi replied: "Madam Qin's daughter Miao Zhiyu often attends poetry gatherings with the Zhu family's daughter. Naturally, she knows."

---

钱氏叹道："原来如此。老爷，我与妹妹正在给文渊选亲，还没个结果，他就提出这档子事，该如何是好？"

Madam Qian sighed: "I see. My lord, Sister and I are still selecting a bride for Wenyuan with no results yet, and now he brings up this matter. What should we do?"

---

乔守仁笑道："看来你们两个与我想法一致，这事就好办。我们三人要统一口径，一旦文渊回来想要说服你们，坚决不能改变态度。"

Qiao Shouren smiled: "It seems you two share my thoughts. This makes things easier. The three of us must speak with one voice. Once Wenyuan returns to try persuading you, we must not change our stance."

---

米氏为难道："老爷，要是文渊死缠硬磨，我怕自己拗不过他。"

Concubine Mi said hesitantly: "My lord, if Wenyuan pesters relentlessly, I'm afraid I won't be able to resist him."

---

乔守仁说："拗不过也得坚持住，实在拗不过就拖着，告诉他婚事一年后再议。"

Qiao Shouren said: "Even if you can't resist, you must hold firm. If you truly can't resist, then delay—tell him the marriage will be discussed again in a year."

---

米氏更加为难："又拖一年？文渊也不小了。"

Concubine Mi was even more troubled: "Delay another year? Wenyuan isn't young anymore."

---

乔守仁说："不拖还能怎样？难道要把朱家姑娘娶回来？表面上拖着，你们私下利用一年的时间好好给他物色个好姑娘。再说文渊正与朱家姑娘情浓，现在强行拒绝他，只怕他做出什么出格的事来，拖上一年，感情也就淡了，到那时，我们的话他也能听进去。"

Qiao Shouren said: "If we don't delay, what else can we do? Are we supposed to marry the Zhu family's daughter? On the surface, we delay. In private, you use the year to find him a good girl. Besides, Wenyuan is deeply in love with the Zhu family's daughter right now. If we bluntly reject him, I'm afraid he might do something reckless. If we delay for a year, his feelings will fade. By then, he'll be able to listen to reason."

---

钱氏十分赞同："就按老爷说的办。"

Madam Qian agreed wholeheartedly: "We'll do as you say, my lord."

---

米氏也点头同意。

Concubine Mi also nodded in agreement.

---

申简辰离开朱府，吩咐白风将马车赶到聚香楼。白风以为王爷早饭没吃好，专程来此撮一顿。

Shen Jianchen left the Zhu residence and ordered Baifeng to drive the carriage to Juxiang Restaurant. Baifeng thought the prince hadn't eaten well at breakfast and had come specifically for a feast.

---

没想到，一进聚香楼，王爷选好包间，只让他点菜。白风才知道王爷是专程请他。

Unexpectedly, upon entering Juxiang Restaurant, the prince chose a private room and only asked him to order dishes. Only then did Baifeng realize the prince had invited him specifically.

---

"王爷，您为何要请我吃饭？"

"My prince, why are you treating me to a meal?"

---

"这段时间你天天往西三胡同跑，错过两次享用美食的机会，今日给你补回来，想吃什么随便点。"

"You've been running to Xisan Hutong every day lately, missing two chances to enjoy fine food. Today I'm making it up to you. Order whatever you want."

---

王爷如此慷慨，白风毫不客气，一下子点了七八道菜，还都是聚香楼的拿手好菜，当然少不了他爱吃的东坡肉。

With the prince being so generous, Baifeng didn't stand on ceremony. He ordered seven or eight dishes at once, all Juxiang Restaurant's specialties, including his favorite Dongpo Pork, of course.

---

点完菜后，伙计将茶水端上，又去忙活。

After ordering, the waiter brought tea and went back to work.

---

白风问："王爷是怎么知道幽栖居士就是千里寻？"

Baifeng asked: "How did Your Highness discover that Youqi Jushi is Qianlixun?"

---

"机缘巧合。"

"By chance."

---

"幽栖居士真奇怪，为何要用别的名字，让王爷找得好苦。"

"Youqi Jushi is so strange. Why use another name, making it so hard for Your Highness to find?"

---

申简辰叮嘱道："幽栖居士是千里寻这件事，不能对他人提起。"

Shen Jianchen instructed: "The matter of Youqi Jushi being Qianlixun must not be mentioned to others."

---

"为何？"

"Why?"

---

"不要问原因，记住就行。"

"Don't ask why. Just remember."

---

"对南双也不能提吗？"

"Not even to Nanshuang?"

---

"当然，你和我知道即可。"

"Of course. Only you and I need to know."

---

"这么神秘？"

"So mysterious?"

---

"不是神秘，是我答应幽栖居士要这么做。"

"It's not about being mysterious. I promised Youqi Jushi I would do this."

---

"好吧，王爷都答应她，我当然会守口如瓶。"

"Very well. Since Your Highness promised her, I'll keep my mouth shut."

---

今日见过千里寻，申简辰心情好了许多。哪怕她未向他流露出丝毫爱意，只与她说说话，他都会觉得人生充满灿烂的阳光。

After seeing Qianlixun today, Shen Jianchen's mood improved greatly. Even though she hadn't shown him the slightest affection, just talking with her made him feel life shone with brilliant light.

---

申简辰昨日些微想过，也许放弃对千里寻的情感，人生也不错，起码他还是王爷，身份尊贵，物质优渥，身边还有一位温柔漂亮的妹妹。可今日与千里寻见过一面，他更确定自己根本无法放下她。

Yesterday, Shen Jianchen had briefly considered that perhaps giving up his feelings for Qianlixun wouldn't be so bad. At least he was still a prince, with noble status, abundant wealth, and a gentle, beautiful sister by his side. But after seeing Qianlixun today, he was even more certain he could never let her go.

---

聚香楼的伙计把饭菜端上来后，不用王爷招呼，白风自觉地大嚼大咽起来。

After the Juxiang Restaurant waiter brought the dishes, without the prince even inviting him, Baifeng enthusiastically began devouring his food.

---

心情变好，食欲跟着变好，申简辰拿起筷子吃起来。他边吃边想，只要千里寻不嫁给文渊，他便一直等着。

In a better mood, his appetite improved too. Shen Jianchen picked up his chopsticks and began eating. As he ate, he thought: as long as Qianlixun didn't marry Wenyuan, he would keep waiting.

---

想起与乔梦容的婚约，申简辰的刚好一些的心情又变得沉重。他放下筷子："白风，问伙计要壶皇都春。"

Thinking of his engagement to Qiao Mengrong, Shen Jianchen's slightly improved mood grew heavy again. He put down his chopsticks: "Baifeng, ask the waiter for a pot of Capital Spring."

---

白风起身出了包间，过一会儿回来："王爷，皇都春马上到。"然后继续坐下大嚼大咽。

Baifeng got up and left the private room. After a while, he returned: "My prince, the Capital Spring is coming right away." Then he sat back down and continued eating heartily.

---

不一会儿，伙计将酒壶和酒盅端来。白风不让伙计倒酒，他主动为王爷满上。

Soon, the waiter brought the wine pot and cups. Baifeng didn't let the waiter pour. He took the initiative to fill the prince's cup.

---

申简辰端起酒盅，一饮而尽，接着又满上，又喝下，一连饮了四盅。

Shen Jianchen picked up his cup, drained it in one gulp, then filled it again and drank it down. He drank four cups in succession.

---

第五盅时，被白风拦住："王爷，您不能这样喝酒，会醉的。"

On the fifth cup, Baifeng stopped him: "My prince, you can't drink like this. You'll get drunk."

---

白风实在不解，王爷刚才还满脸笑容，为何这会儿又愁容满面，还想要借酒消愁。

Baifeng truly didn't understand. The prince had been smiling just moments ago. Why was his face now full of sorrow, wanting to drown his worries in alcohol?

---

"吃你的，不用管我，我酒量好着呢。"申简辰说着，继续喝下第五盅。

"Keep eating. Don't worry about me. I can hold my liquor well." Shen Jianchen said this as he continued to drink the fifth cup.

---

想要再倒酒时，白风把酒壶挪开："不行，您得听我的，吃会儿菜，过会儿再喝。"

When he tried to pour more wine, Baifeng moved the pot away: "No. You have to listen to me. Eat some food first, then drink later."

---

申简辰不再强求，放下酒盅："白风，我问你，如果你爹娘给你定下一门亲，可你不喜欢那姑娘，你该怎么办？"

Shen Jianchen stopped pressing. He put down his cup: "Baifeng, let me ask you. If your parents arranged a marriage for you, but you didn't like the girl, what would you do?"

---

"在我身上不可能发生这种事，我娘在的时候听我的，现在她不在了，我的人生我做主。"白风显得很得意。

"That could never happen to me. When my mother was alive, she listened to me. Now she's gone. I'm the master of my own life." Baifeng looked quite proud.

---

白风得意的样子令申简辰羡慕，重申道："我是说如果，假设你父母健在，发生这种事，你该如何做？"

Baifeng's proud expression made Shen Jianchen envious. He reiterated: "I'm saying if—suppose your parents were still alive and this happened, what would you do?"

---

从王爷渴求的眼神判断，今日必须给他个答复。看在王爷请客的份儿上，白风决定说说心里的想法。

Judging from the prince's eager gaze, he had to give an answer today. Considering the prince was treating him, Baifeng decided to share his thoughts.

---

"既然不乐意父母定下的亲事，我会离家出走，跑得越远越好。过几年，姑娘等不下去，自然会嫁给别人，到时我再回去，什么问题都解决。"

"If I didn't want the marriage my parents arranged, I'd run away from home, as far as possible. After a few years, the girl wouldn't be able to wait and would naturally marry someone else. By then, I'd return, and the problem would be solved."

---

白风说的方法，申简辰不是没想过。对他来讲，逃跑根本解决不了问题，除圣旨限制外，还有佳和郡主为赵朴的付出，都像沉重的枷锁，令他无法轻松迈开双腿。

Shen Jianchen had considered the method Baifeng suggested. For him, running away couldn't solve the problem at all. Besides the imperial decree's restrictions, there was also Princess Jiahe's sacrifice for Zhao Pu. Both were like heavy shackles, preventing him from easily taking a step forward.

---

"逃跑，哪有那么容易。"想到无缘无故多出的姻缘，申简辰又觉得自己没有资格再追求千里寻，"把酒给我，只有喝醉，我才不会如此难受。"

"Running away... it's never that simple." Thinking of the marriage that had been thrust upon him for no reason, Shen Jianchen felt he had no right to pursue Qianlixun. "Give me the wine. Only when I'm drunk won't I feel so miserable."

---

白风坚持不给，申简辰使出功夫。白风一只手拿着酒壶，另一只手拿着筷子应对。坚持了一会儿，实在抵挡不住，被王爷抓住手腕，一阵疼痛，筷子自然掉落在桌上，又从桌上弹落在地上。

Baifeng insisted on not giving it. Shen Jianchen used his martial arts. Baifeng held the wine pot in one hand and used chopsticks in the other to defend. After holding on for a while, he couldn't resist any longer. The prince grabbed his wrist, causing sharp pain. The chopsticks naturally fell onto the table, then bounced onto the floor.

---

王爷顺利从他另一只手中抢过酒壶，继续自斟自饮。

The prince successfully snatched the wine pot from his other hand and continued pouring and drinking by himself.

---

白风捡起筷子，用桌上的布子胡乱擦擦，仍旧吃起来。

Baifeng picked up the chopsticks, wiped them carelessly with the cloth on the table, and continued eating.

---

一壶酒不够，申简辰又要了两壶……

One pot of wine wasn't enough. Shen Jianchen ordered two more pots...

---

喝到第五壶时，醉意袭来，被白风扶上马车。

By the fifth pot, intoxication claimed him, and Baifeng helped him into the carriage.

---

白风自语道："喝成这个样子，怎能去府衙？还是回幽林别居。"

Baifeng muttered to himself: "In this state, how can he go to the yamen? Better return to Youlin Villa."
