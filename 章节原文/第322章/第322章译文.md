## Chapter 322: The Black-Draped Prison Cart

Refusing to sit idle, Haoyu grabbed a carriage and chased straight after Lin Jue, hammering uselessly at Liu'an Villa's bolted gates before slumping against the wood to wait.

Inside, a servant locked Qianli Xun into a misty bathhouse draped in pink silk curtains—where wrinkled, grinning Liang Shicheng splashed in a hot pool groping half a dozen nervous girls!

Dripping wet, Liang Shicheng stepped out leering—openly admitting someone paid for her head, yet offering her life and safe passage home if she'd "entertain" him once!

*"Over my dead body!"* Answering his slimy pitch with a flying kick that dumped him straight back into the pool, Qianli Xun vaulted out to plant a solid right cross on his jaw as palace swordsmen poured through the doors!

*"Attempted murder of an imperial official! Seize her for the Court of Judicial Review in Bianliang!"* Liang Shicheng shrieked from the water.

Fierce as she was, seven or eight relentless waves of elite imperial guards wore her down over half an hour until a blade sliced across her back and a ring of steel pinned her throat. Closing her eyes in exhausted defiance—and spitting straight onto Liang Shicheng's swollen bruise when he offered one last sleazy deal—she was bound, gagged, and locked inside a **black-draped prison cart** rolling quietly out the side gate straight toward Bianliang!

Outside, Haoyu tried blocking the mysterious cart, got kicked aside by guards, and never dreamed his wife sat gagged behind that black cloth—until dusk fell and a gate servant jeered that Second Young Madam was already halfway to Bianliang facing execution for attempted murder unless the Chens coughed up **five hundred thousand taels**!

Chasing blindly out the north gate until his driver talked sense into him—save her neck with silver first!—Haoyu turned bitterly back home, where Kuqiao sat weeping past her tenth burnt incense stick as Matron Wei stopped Hongdou and the ten loyal servants from launching a suicidal raid on Liu'an Villa.
