第234章 一滴眼泪明真情
## Chapter 234: A Single Tear Reveals the Heart

青瓜的屁股上没长眼睛，被重重踢到，向前趔趄，重心失衡，没站稳，眼看即将倾斜，若倒在地上时，必然来个狗吃屎。
说时迟，那时快，丑丫飞快冲来，将青瓜扶住：“青瓜，你没事吧？”
青瓜感到庆幸：“谢谢你，丑丫。”
“跟我不必客气。”丑丫放开青瓜，上前行礼道，“姑爷，以后能不能不要欺负我家青瓜？”
陈浩宇气没出成，心中正郁闷，又被一个丫鬟质问，长这么大，从来没如此委屈过。
瞅瞅院子，不是自己喜欢的样子，再瞅瞅眼前的下人，个个都是朱淑真训练出来的。连唯一可以撒气的青瓜也有人撑腰，而自己连青瓜都不如，眼眶不由变红，一句话没说往博雅斋走去。
阿升和三宝紧跟上。
丑丫纳闷道：“青瓜，你刚才看到没？姑爷好像哭了？”
青瓜并未注意：“什么？公子哭了？怎么可能？我家公子从小到大只有让别人哭的份儿，从没见他哭过。”
阿美走过来：“我刚才也看到，姑爷应该是感到委屈，眼睛很红，像是要哭的样子。唉，遇到我家小姐这么厉害的人，姑爷不哭才怪。”
青瓜在，丑丫顾不上阿美：“青瓜，有时间去西边的亭子跟我约会。”
阿美打趣道：“丑丫，真不害臊，我还在此，都不知道避开。”
“我看你还是回避一下。”丑丫推开阿美，“赶紧去忙吧，别打扰我们。”
阿美笑一下，去了西院。
青瓜担心公子，也想尽快甩开丑丫：“你忙，我去看看我家公子。”
青瓜说完急忙向后院跑去。
丑丫并未去追，她还要干活，瞅着青瓜的背影议论道：“姑爷总欺负你，还老往他跟前凑，我看你就是欠揍，下次再也不管你。”
With no eyes on his backside, Qinggua took the kick square on, pitching forward off balance straight toward a face-plant on the flagstones.

回博雅斋的路上，陈浩宇努力控制即将落下的泪水，还是有一滴划过脸颊掉落下来，他站住脚步，用衣袖将脸上的泪水抹掉，猛然想起以前跟狐朋狗友在一起饮酒玩乐时说过的话……
Out of nowhere, Chouya darted in and caught him steady. "Are you all right, Qinggua?"

有一次，他与一群公子哥在聚香楼饮酒作乐，什么低俗的话题都聊，偶尔也会聊起真情，那些朋友一一谈起曾动过真情的姑娘，唯独他陈浩宇没有。
"Whew—thanks, Chouya!"

有位公子哥问道：“陈兄采过的花可不少，难道没有一个动真情的？”
"Don't mention it." Letting him go, Chouya turned and curtsied to Chen Haoyu. "Young Master, could you please stop bullying my Qinggua?"

陈浩宇左边搂一个姑娘，右边也搂一个姑娘，一脸淫笑：“本公子就是喜欢跟她们玩儿，谈什么真情？”
Denied even his lone vent and scolded by a maid in his own yard—every tile altered against his wishes, every servant loyal to Zhu Shuzhen, even Qinggua backed up while he stood utterly alone—a sudden, raw sting of humiliation burned behind Chen Haoyu's eyes. Not once in his entire life had he felt this cornered. Without another word, eyes reddening, he turned and walked back toward Boya Study with Asheng and Sanbao trailing behind.

另一位公子问：“我一直很好奇，陈兄到底会对什么样的女子动真情？”
Chouya blinked after him. "Qinggua, did you see that? Did Young Master just cry?"

陈浩宇不愿谈及此话题：“谈这个多没意思，抓紧吃喝玩乐。”
"What? Cry? No way! My master only makes other people cry—never shed a tear in his life!"

没想到其他几位公子也对此话题感兴趣，都催促起来：“陈兄不妨说说，我们都说了，你不说，太不公平。”
Amei strolled over. "I saw it too. Looked crushed, eyes red as cherries. Well, married to a force of nature like Miss, anyone would cry."

陈浩宇松开两位姑娘：“你们两个去那边，我要说正事，一会儿再陪你们玩儿。”
Ignoring Amei, Chouya turned shyly to Qinggua. "Come meet me at the west pavilion when you're free."

两个姑娘离开后，陈浩宇认真地说：“你们既然这么感兴趣，谈谈也无妨。小时候我流过多少次眼泪，已经记不清楚，反正从我记事起，就没怎么流过泪，要是哪个姑娘能让我流泪，我便喜欢上她。”
"No shame at all, Chouya, flirting right in front of me!" Amei teased.

有人故意问：“如果那姑娘是个丑八怪怎么办？”
"Then make yourself scarce!" Chouya nudged her away. "Go do your chores!" Laughing, Amei strolled off to the West Courtyard.

陈浩宇说：“本公子对颜值要求很高，长得丑一律入不了本公子的法眼，又怎能让本公子因她流下眼泪？”
Worried about his master—and eager to ditch Chouya—Qinggua said quick, "Gotta go check on Young Master!" and jogged off to the back courtyard.

回想到这些，陈浩宇在心中自问：“难道我喜欢上母老虎？”
Letting him go since she had work anyway, Chouya muttered at his retreating back, "Young Master boots you nonstop and you still run after him like a pup! Next time I'm leaving you on the ground!"

他晃晃脑袋：“怎么可能？我跟她势不两立！可为何刚才会流下眼泪？不，一定是我想多了，当初说的话不能太当真。”
Along the walk to Boya Study, Chen Haoyu fought back the burn, yet one lone teardrop slipped down his cheek anyway. Halting, he wiped it away with his sleeve—and froze as an old drinking boast rushed back into his head.

陈浩宇带着疑惑跨入博雅斋，阿升和三宝则守在门外。
One night at Juxiang Tower, drinking and swapping ribald stories with his rich buddies, the talk had turned to real love. Every friend named some girl who had moved his heart—everybody except Chen Haoyu.

红豆正在书桌旁收拾，见姑爷进来，上前行礼：“姑爷，书桌已给您收拾好，那一摞书是小姐专门为您挑选的，先背诵《论语》和王右丞的诗，小姐说了，《论语》每日背诵五篇，王右丞的诗每日背五首，背完后，您需到素心草堂由小姐检验。”
"All the flowers you've plucked, Brother Chen, never once caught real feelings?" one friend had asked.

陈浩宇瞧见书桌上放着高高一摞书，还未读，脑袋已经十个大，又在心中骂起朱淑真：“你就是一只讨厌的母老虎！本公子根本不可能喜欢上你！”
With a girl draped over each arm, Chen Haoyu had smirked. "I just like playing around. Who bothers with real love?"

红豆叮嘱道：“姑爷还是抓紧时间背诵。”
"Always wondered what kind of woman could actually make you fall, Brother Chen," another pressed.

陈浩宇摸摸肚子：“腹中空空，跑了一上午，也该给些东西吃，吃完再背不迟。”
Waving the girls away when the whole table clamored for an answer, Chen Haoyu had turned strangely serious. "Can't recall how much I cried as a toddler, yet since I've been old enough to remember, I've never shed a tear. If any woman ever makes me cry, that's the woman I've fallen for."

红豆说：“姑爷先背着，我去给您端些饭食来。”
"What if she's hideous?" someone joked.

红豆离开后，青瓜从外面小心进来，见公子已在书桌前坐下，书桌上破天荒地放了好些书。他笑嘻嘻地上前问：“公子，刚才听丑丫和阿美说您流眼泪，是不是真的？”
"My standards for looks are sky-high—no plain face ever catches my eye, let alone makes me cry!"

陈浩宇训斥道：“一边去！少烦本公子！本公子流泪跟你有什么关系？”
Memory fading, Chen Haoyu stared blankly ahead: Wait... did I just fall for the tigress?!

青瓜说：“看来是真的。我知道公子委屈，可有什么办法？陈家长辈不出面干涉，少夫人又厉害，您只有吃苦的份儿，不如我给您出个主意？”
He shook his head hard. Impossible! We're sworn enemies! Then why did a tear just fall? No—overthinking it! Old drunken talk doesn't count!

陈浩宇盯着一摞书，说起话来都觉无力：“你能有什么好主意？”
Shaking off the eerie thought, he stepped into Boya Study while Asheng and Sanbao took posts outside.

青瓜凑到书桌前：“硬碰硬公子已试过，根本不奏效，不如来软的？”
Inside, Hongdou was tidying the desk. Turning to curtsy, she gestured to a tall stack of bound volumes. "Desk's all set, Young Master. Miss handpicked these for you—start with the *Analects* and Wang Wei's poetry. Five chapters of the *Analects* and five poems daily, tested by Miss in Suxin Thatched Hall once you finish."

陈浩宇立即认同，双眼放光，盯着青瓜：“没想到你还有些脑子，说来听听。”
One look at the tower of books gave Chen Haoyu an instant migraine, firing his curses back up: Rotten tigress! As if I could ever fall for you!

公子没有骂他，还很感兴趣，青瓜更是来了精神。
"Better start memorizing, Young Master," Hongdou urged.

“您跟少夫人本就是夫妻，不如拿出对待自家娘子的好来，少夫人再厉害，毕竟是女子，喜欢听些软言软语。关切的话说多了，她一心软，拳头怎么可能使得下去？您再把自己变成一个好夫君，少夫人发现您的好，会一门心思想着跟您好好过日子，肯定不会再欺负您。”
Patting his growling belly, he countered, "Starving after running all morning! Bring lunch first, then I'll memorize."

陈浩宇仔细思量：“软言软语好说，只是怎么做一个好夫君，我从没试过，不知道如何做？”
"Start reading while I fetch your meal."

青瓜继续出主意：“大老爷就是个好夫君，他怎么对待大夫人，您就怎么对待少夫人。”
After Hongdou left, Qinggua crept in, blinking at his master seated before a mountain of classics. Grinning, he sidled up. "Chouya and Amei said you cried, Young Master—true?"

“对啊！我爹就是活生生的范本，不如向他学？”陈浩宇将手掌在桌上拍一下，兴奋道。无意中目光接触到眼前的一摞书，兴致立刻全无，“母老虎还让我背书，背还是不背？”
"Beat it! Quit bugging me! None of your business whether I cried!"

“当然背。”青瓜把最上面的一本书翻开，摆在公子面前，“公子仔细想想，大老爷有没有违抗过夫人的意思？”
"Aha, so it's true! I know you're hurting, Young Master, yet with the elders staying out and Young Madam unstoppable, hard times are here—unless you hear out an idea of mine?"

陈浩宇仔细思索：“还真没有，不过，我娘从来没有欺负过我爹，母老虎明明是欺负我，我本来就不喜欢背书，想到被她欺负更来气。”
Staring gloomily at the stack of books, Chen Haoyu muttered, "What bright idea could you have?"

“公子消消气。”青瓜用手在公子背后轻轻地从上到下捋着，“得想想少夫人为何欺负您？肯定是您做过对不起她的事，我可是听说去年关于少夫人的谣言是公子散播的。”
Qinggua leaned over the desk. "Going head-to-head flopped completely. Why not try going soft?"

“没想到本公子散播谣言之事，你都知道。”
Chen Haoyu's eyes snapped up bright and alert. "Got a brain in that skull after all—keep talking!"

“陈府的下人都传开了，无人不知。少夫人一个女子被全城人臭骂，您想想她的心情，估计那会儿躲在闺房哭得跟个泪人似的，我还听说本来乔家打算到朱家提亲，因为谣言，亲事也黄了，真没想到这么损的事竟然是我家公子干的。”
Thrilled not to be cursed at, Qinggua warmed up. "You and Young Madam are husband and wife! Treat her sweet—tough as she is, she's still a woman, and women melt for soft words and warm care! Pour on concern every day until her heart softens; how can she swing fists at a devoted husband? Turn yourself into a model husband, let her see your good side, and she'll want a real marriage instead of torturing you!"

“不能全怪我，母老虎还写了句‘和衣睡倒人怀’，是她自己把自己好名声给毁了。”
Turning it over, Chen Haoyu mused, "Soft words I can do—never tried being a good husband though. How do you even do that?"

青瓜停下帮公子捋背的手，回想一会儿听到的谣言：“是有这句，可谣言是公子您散播的，您不散播那句子，老百姓也不可能知道。”
"Eldest Master is a model husband! Copy how he treats Eldest Madam!"

青瓜的话才令陈浩宇明白，普通老百姓根本不会关注酸臭文人写的诗词，朱淑真写的那句子本来只会在文人才子中传播，因他放在散播的谣言里，老百姓才得知。由此看来，他陈浩宇的罪过更大。
"Right! My own father's a living textbook!" Chen Haoyu slapped the desk in excitement—then sagged seeing the books staring back. "Still stuck memorizing these?"

“好吧好吧，错在我，我好好背书。”
"Of course!" Qinggua flipped the top volume open in front of him. "Think about it: has Eldest Master ever crossed Eldest Madam once?"

他拿起书看起来，只觉古人实在无趣，写这么多字有何意义？还给后人添麻烦。
"Never—though Mother never tortured Father either! The tigress is flat-out bullying me, and I hate reading!"

没过一会儿，红豆把饭送来，陈浩宇吃饱后，困意袭来，坐在书桌旁直打盹儿，实在困得不行，直接趴在桌上呼呼大睡。
"Cool down, Young Master." Qinggua gently rubbed his master's back. "Ask yourself why she's mad at you—heard every servant in the manor talking about you spreading those slanderous rumors about her last year! Imagine a young lady cursed by an entire city, crying her eyes out in her room, watching her match with Qiao Manor blow up! Hard to believe you pulled something that dirty."

青瓜见状，叹口气，并不理会，谁让他也开始打盹儿，人虽站着，头却时不时耷拉下去。
"Not all on me—she wrote 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed' herself!"

青瓜正在享受晕晕乎乎的状态时，被人戳了一下，不由变得清醒，定睛一瞧，原来是丑丫。
Qinggua paused. "Sure, yet ordinary folks don't read scholars' poetry! Nobody on the street would've heard that line if you hadn't woven it into your gossip!"

丑丫小声说：“走，跟我去西边的亭子。”
That struck home like a cold splash: commoners never paid attention to highbrow verses; he really had lit the match that burned her name down. "All right, all right—my bad. I'll memorize."

青瓜本不喜欢丑丫，老被她缠着也不是个事儿，还容易被红豆误会，干脆想个办法解决此事。
He opened the *Analects*, muttering at ancient sages for writing so many tedious words just to torment future generations.

他灵机一动，拉起丑丫离公子远一些，免得吵到他。
When Hongdou brought lunch, Chen Haoyu ate his fill, grew heavy-lidded, bobbed over his book, and soon slumped face-down on the desk snoring. Sighing, Qinggua stood beside him nodding off on his own feet—until a poke jolted him awake to find Chouya standing there.

“丑丫，你以后不要老来找我。”
"Come on, west pavilion," she whispered.

“为何？”
Not liking Chouya and worried Hongdou might get the wrong idea, Qinggua grabbed a quick scheme and drew her clear of his snoring master. "Chouya, stop coming to look for me."

“公子被少夫人欺负，没好日子过，我根本没有心情跟你在一起。”
"Why?!"

“他是他，你是你。”
"While Young Master's miserable under Young Madam, I have zero mood for romance."

“错，公子高兴我便高兴，公子不高兴，我也高兴不起来。”
"He's him and you're you!"

“这么说只有我家小姐跟姑爷好了，你才愿意跟我去西边的亭子？”
"Nope—when he's happy I'm happy; when he hurts, I hurt."

青瓜点点头。
"So you won't meet me at the pavilion until Miss and Young Master make up?"

丑丫说：“这得等到啥时候？只怕遥遥无期。”
Qinggua nodded.

青瓜想到一个好办法：“要不你就别来找我，要不你就帮帮我家公子，让她跟少夫人快些和好。”
"That could take forever!"

“和好根本不可能，看来我只能不来找你。”丑丫失落地离开。
"Your choice: stop coming around, or help Young Master and Young Madam patch things up!"

这么快把丑丫打发走，青瓜为自己的聪明才智感到骄傲。刚高兴一会儿，又觉不对，丑丫的表现岂不说明公子跟少夫人得永远斗下去？难道给公子出的服软的主意根本没用？
"Patching them up is impossible... guess I'll stay away then." Crestfallen, Chouya slipped out.

正想着，听到公子的声音：“朱淑真！你等着！我要打得你落花……流……水……”
Thrilled at shaking her off so clean, Qinggua's grin faded a second later: wait—if even Chouya thinks they'll never make up, is my soft-approach plan doomed?

原来是公子说梦话而已。
Just then a mumble rose from the desk: "Zhu Shuzhen... wait till I beat you... flowers falling, water flowing..." Talking in his sleep.

青瓜继续站在公子身边，刚站一会儿，又听到公子说出四个字：“我……喜欢……你……”
Moments later, another quiet murmur drifted out: "I... like... you..."

青瓜第一次听到公子说喜欢某人，不由兴致满满，凑近问：“公子喜欢谁？”
Qinggua's ears perked up. Leaning close, he whispered, "Who do you like, Young Master?"

陈浩宇梦中呓语，含含糊糊地说出一个名字：“朱……淑……真……”
Through sluggish sleep, Chen Haoyu slurred three syllables: "Zhu... Shu... zhen..."

尽管模糊，青瓜还是听明白，脸上露出笑容，自语道：“原来公子悄悄喜欢上少夫人， 他醒着时，自己都不知道，实在好玩儿。真希望少夫人和公子能好起来，我们做下人的生活也好回归正常。”
Blurry as it sounded, Qinggua caught every syllable and broke into a wide grin. "Young Master fell for Young Madam without even knowing it while awake! Hilarious! Hope they really make up so life gets normal again!"

熟睡的陈浩宇梦见自己被朱淑真一拳打得跌进坑里，一下子被吓醒，揉揉眼睛，坐直后问道：“什么时辰？”
Right then, dreaming of Zhu Shuzhen punching him into a ditch, Chen Haoyu jerked awake rubbing his eyes. "What hour is it?"

青瓜回道：“申时。”
"Hour of the Monkey—mid-afternoon."

“都申时了，我一点儿都没背过，怎么办？”
"Already?! I didn't memorize a single line! What do I do?"

“没办法，只能继续背。”
"Only thing to do—cram right now!"

青瓜话音刚落，红豆走进来：“姑爷，背书时间已结束，该到小姐跟前背上一背。”
Before he could grab the book, Hongdou stepped in. "Study time's up, Young Master. Miss is waiting to test you."

陈浩宇问：“能不能再让我背会儿？”
"Give me a few more minutes?"

“不行！小姐定的时间，不能改！”
"No! Miss's schedule doesn't bend!"

“不行就不行，声音那么大干什么？”
"Fine, no need to yell!"

陈浩宇悻悻地起身往出走，青瓜跟在身后担心不已，公子一句没背，去了只能被劈头盖脸一顿臭骂。
Dragging his feet, Chen Haoyu headed out with Qinggua trailing in dread, bracing for an eruption.
