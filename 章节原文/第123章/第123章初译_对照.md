第123章 望月轩内送美食
## Chapter 123: Bringing a Feast to Wangyue Pavilion

千里寻带领众人来到望月轩门口，仪王与白风站在那里，白风手里提着两个食盒。
Leading the household out to Wangyue Pavilion's gate, Qianli Xun found Prince Yi waiting with Bai Feng, who held two food carriers.

千里寻上前拜道：“感谢仪王亲自将苦荞送回。”
Stepping forward, Qianli Xun bowed: "Thank you for escorting Kuqiao home yourself, Prince Yi."

曾提醒过她见自己不必行礼，她也答应，可千里寻每次见他依旧依礼而行，只怪仪王的身份太过尊贵。
He had told her time and again to skip bowing, and she had agreed—yet every time they met she bowed all the same, unable to forget his lofty rank.

申简辰本想再次说出不让她行礼的话，见千里寻平静的面色中略显严肃，便知她想拒他于千里之外，只好把想说的话咽回去。
On the tip of his tongue to tell her again not to bow, Shen Jianchen saw the cool reserve on her face—clear notice she meant to hold him at arm's length—and swallowed his words.

“幽栖居士若想谢我，不如请我在望月轩用午饭，饭食我已带来。”
"If Youqi Jushi wants to thank me, invite me to share lunch at Wangyue Pavilion. I brought the food."

千里寻正犹豫要不要让仪王进入，韦嬷嬷怕得罪贵人，赶紧说：“仪王光临望月轩，实乃荣幸，我替姑娘做主，您请！”
Before Qianli Xun could waver, Matron Wei spoke fast lest they offend royalty: "Having Prince Yi honor Wangyue Pavilion is our privilege! Forgive me for speaking for my mistress—please come right in!"

韦嬷嬷既然应下，千里寻不好再说什么，她侧身站向一旁，为仪王让开道。
With Matron Wei extending the invite, Qianli Xun could not object and stepped aside to let Prince Yi pass.

申简辰自然开心，迈开步子走进去，白风手提食盒紧跟上。
Delighted, Shen Jianchen strode inside, Bai Feng trailing with the food carriers.

进入用饭厅，苦荞与白风将饭菜摆好，都是聚香楼的硬菜。
In the dining hall, Kuqiao and Bai Feng set out dish after dish of Juxiang Tower's finest specialties.

申简辰不请自坐：“这么多菜，我一人吃不完，幽栖居士不如陪我一起吃？”
Helping himself to a seat, Shen Jianchen said, "Too much food for one man—won't Youqi Jushi join me?"

千里寻拒绝道：“我没胃口，也有些累，想去休息，还是让苦荞和白风在这里陪仪王，或者嬷嬷也行。”
Qianli Xun turned him down flat: "No appetite, and I'm tired—going to rest. Let Kuqiao and Bai Feng keep Prince Yi company, or Matron Wei."

“可惜，我已答应让白风带苦荞去聚香楼吃饭，银子都给了。至于嬷嬷，她应该有更重要的事情做，是不是？嬷嬷？”申简辰为他找的理由而得意。
"Too bad—I already gave Bai Feng silver to take Kuqiao to Juxiang Tower for lunch. As for Matron Wei, I'm sure she has more pressing tasks, don't you, Matron?" Shen Jianchen felt pretty smug about his airtight setup.

仪王的面子还是要给，韦嬷嬷说：“仪王、姑娘，你们用饭，我还有事，先退下。”
Unable to cross a prince, Matron Wei bowed: "Enjoy your meal, Prince Yi, Young Lady. I have chores to attend to and take my leave."

韦嬷嬷因仪王的身份不得已退出，千里寻不好阻拦。还好，望月轩又不是只有苦荞一个丫鬟。
Forced to let Matron Wei withdraw, Qianli Xun still wasn't out of options—Kuqiao wasn't the only maid in Wangyue Pavilion!

“仪王若嫌一人用饭寂寞，我差人把夏果找来，再多找几个丫鬟伺候您……”
"If Prince Yi hates dining alone, I'll summon Xiaguo and a few more maids to wait on you..."

“不必，我与幽栖居士有话要说，不想其他人在此打扰。白风，还不赶紧带苦荞去聚香楼！”
"No need. I have words for Youqi Jushi and don't want onlookers. Bai Feng, take Kuqiao to Juxiang Tower right now!"

听到仪王命令，白风拉起苦荞迅速离开。
At the prince's order, Bai Feng grabbed Kuqiao's hand and bolted.

千里寻在心中埋怨苦荞：“这么快被人收买，等你回来，看我怎么批评你！”
Qianli Xun grumbled at Kuqiao in her mind: "Bought off that easy?! Wait till you get back—I'll give you an earful!"

周围已没其他人，申简辰很满意，他站起来，将桌边的一把椅子往外拉出一些：“幽栖居士，请坐。”
Alone at last, Shen Jianchen smiled, stood up, and pulled out a chair beside the table: "Have a seat, Youqi Jushi."

仪王的动作，千里寻觉得熟悉。在另一个时代，与申简辰一起出去吃饭，他每次都会帮她把座椅拉出一些，为何仪王也会如此？难道……
That gesture hit Qianli Xun with a flash of deja vu. Back in her own era, whenever she ate out with Shen Jianchen, he pulled her chair out like that every single time. Why would Prince Yi do the exact same thing? Could...

千里寻露出疑惑和期待的表情，申简辰猜到她定是想起穿越前的事。
Catching the flicker of wonder and hope in her eyes, Shen Jianchen knew she had just remembered their past.

本想找机会告诉她，他就是申简辰，几次以失败告终，便不想再说。说了有何用？又不能摆脱与梦容的婚约。
He had tried telling her he was Shen Jianchen more than once and failed every time; now he didn't even want to say it. What good would it do when his betrothal to Mengrong still chained him down?

只要婚约还在，他是赵朴还是申简辰有什么区别？不如借着赵朴的身份与她接近，她拒绝嫌弃的也是赵朴，而非申简辰。
As long as that betrothal stood, what difference did it make whether he was Zhao Pu or Shen Jianchen? Better to approach her as Zhao Pu—that way, every rejection fell on Zhao Pu, not Shen Jianchen.

“想什么呢？还不赶紧坐下。”
"Daydreaming? Sit down already."

挪出的椅子，千里寻有种想要坐上去的欲望，她遵循欲望，乖乖坐下：“仪王为何要帮我把椅子挪出来？您可是身份尊贵的王爷，无论走到哪里都有人伺候，什么时候学会做这种事？”
Drawn toward the pulled-out chair by an odd pull, Qianli Xun sat down: "Why did Prince Yi pull my chair out? A royal prince waited on everywhere he goes—where did you pick up a habit like that?"

“这么简单的动作还用学吗？我又不是傻子。”申简辰坐回位置，“我是身份最贵的王爷，可也是一位没有架子，又亲民的王爷，为你挪挪椅子没什么，我也经常帮梦容挪椅子，可能是习惯了。”
"Does a tiny gesture like that need lessons? I'm not an idiot." Sitting back down, Shen Jianchen shrugged: "High-ranking prince I may be, yet I'm down-to-earth with zero airs. Pulling out a chair is nothing—I do it for Mengrong all the time. Force of habit."

“哦，原来如此。”千里寻变得清醒，他怎么可能是申简辰？他就是个不折不扣的花心王爷。
"Oh, I see." Clarity snapped back into Qianli Xun's mind. How could he ever be Shen Jianchen? Just a textbook playboy prince.

本来因伤心没有胃口，坐到桌旁，靠美食近些，食物屡屡香气扑鼻而来，颜色更是诱人，竟然把本已消失的食欲又刺激回来。
She had thought heartache had killed her appetite, yet sitting up close to the fragrant, gleaming dishes woke her stomach right back up.

千里寻拿起筷子夹一口菜，品尝后甚是满意：“仪王很会点菜，色香味俱全。”千里寻大吃起来，暂时把痛苦搁置一旁。
Lifting her chopsticks, she tasted a bite and nodded: "Prince Yi sure knows how to order—looks, smells, and tastes great." Digging in with gusto, she pushed her grief aside for a spell.

申简辰认真欣赏，她的吃相一点儿没变，与大家闺秀的身份实在不搭，可申简辰喜欢。他最欣赏不来扭扭捏捏，故作柔弱，吃饭时还要注意形象的女子。
Shen Jianchen watched in quiet pleasure. Her hearty appetite hadn't changed one bit—hardly ladylike, yet he adored it. He couldn't stand women who nibbled like sparrows and fussed over appearances at the dinner table.

“仪王不吃吗？”
"Aren't you eating, Prince Yi?"

“当然吃，不能辜负美食。”申简辰也拿起筷子吃起来。
"Of course—can't waste fine food." Picking up his chopsticks, he joined in.

两人专注享用美食，并未聊天儿，申简辰很享受只有他与千里寻在一起的安静感。
Neither spoke as they ate, Shen Jianchen drinking in the rare peace of sharing a quiet table with Qianli Xun alone.

吃着吃着，千里寻用余光瞧到仪王一脸享受的表情，心里甚是不满，放下筷子：“仪王，我已吃饱，您慢慢享用。”
Mid-bite, Qianli Xun caught Prince Yi watching her with a look of sheer bliss. Annoyance flared, and she set her chopsticks down: "I'm full, Prince Yi. Enjoy the rest at your leisure."

“我有话对你说，说完你再离开。”
"Stay until I say my piece."

“什么话？”
"What piece?"

“以后我会帮你和文渊传话。”申简辰之所以愿意这么做，不过是想找一个借口多见见千里寻。
"From now on, I'll pass messages between you and Wenyuan." All Shen Jianchen really wanted was a steady excuse to keep seeing her.

千里寻感激的同时，也充满怀疑：“仪王为何要帮我？”
Torn between gratitude and suspicion, Qianli Xun asked, "Why is Prince Yi helping me?"

“以前你求我帮忙时，也没问过原因，为何这次要问？”申简辰放下筷子，心爱的女子不吃，他只顾一个人吃，显得太不将她当回事。
"You never asked why back when you begged favors from me—why ask now?" Shen Jianchen set his chopsticks down too; eating alone while she sat there felt rude.

“不一样，我求仪王时，满心只希望仪王答应，根本不管什么原因。而我现在又未求仪王，您却答应我，定是有所要求，只怕我无法满足仪王。”
"That was different. When I asked for help, I only cared if you'd say yes. Now I didn't ask, yet you're volunteering—which means you want something in return, and I'm afraid I can't give it."

“我是有所求，只想幽栖居士知道，我心中深爱着一个女子，从第一次见时就爱上她，我不奢求能与她在一起，只需她明白有一个人默默地爱着她，哪怕她偶尔会在心里想起我，我也心满意足。”
"I do want something: I just want Youqi Jushi to know I love one woman with all my heart—loved her the very first time I saw her. I don't ask to possess her; just knowing she knows someone loves her in silence—and maybe thinks of me once in a blue moon—is enough."

千里寻沉默半晌，冷笑道：“哼！仪王心里到底有多少女子？恐怕是见一个爱一个吧？”
After a cold pause, Qianli Xun let out a sharp laugh: "Hmph! Just how many women sit in Prince Yi's heart? Fall in love with every single one you meet?"

“随你怎么想，我心中从来只有一人，她就是千里寻……”
"Believe what you want. Only one woman has ever held my heart, and her name is Qianli Xun..."

仪王的表白在千里寻看来实在可笑：“哼！仪王不是答应要替我保密吗？为何动不动会提起这个名字？小心我把你看成不守承诺之人。”
His declaration sounded absurd to Qianli Xun: "Hmph! Didn't Prince Yi swear to guard my secret? Why keep throwing that name around at every turn? Watch out I don't brand you a liar!"

“我会替你保密，之所以提起，除了因这里只有你我二人外，还因‘千里寻’这个名字是我们之间的秘密，只属于你和我，而幽栖居士、朱淑真之类的称谓人人可称得……”
"I will guard your secret. I only say it because nobody else is here—and because 'Qianli Xun' is a secret belonging to just the two of us, whereas anyone on the street can call you Youqi Jushi or Zhu Shuzhen..."

千里寻继续笑道：“呵呵……，令你失望，苦荞和苦蕨早知道，我曾在林家长辈面前也提到我是千里寻，只不过林家长辈不相信而已……”
Qianli Xun smirked back: "Haha, hate to burst your bubble—Kuqiao and Kujue already know! I even told the Lin elders I was Qianli Xun; they just didn't believe me..."

“你又何必如此噎我？我一片真心，为何总换不来你一丝感动？”
"Why do you always shoot me down like that? Why can't all my sincerity move you even an inch?"

“我劝仪王不要白费心思，还是老老实实对待佳和郡主，我做人有底线，什么该做，什么不该做，心里清清楚楚。即使我与文渊分开，也不可能为你所谓的真心感动，你的真心在我看来一文不值。”
"Save your breath, Prince Yi, and treat Princess Jiahe right. I have lines I don't cross, and I know right from wrong clear as day. Even if Wenyuan and I part ways, your so-called sincerity will never move me—to me, your sincerity isn't worth a single copper coin."

千里寻的话又一次戳进申简辰的心里：“一文不值？哼，竟然一文不值？”
Her words drove a knife straight into Shen Jianchen's chest: "Not worth a copper coin? Hmph... not even one copper coin?"

千里寻站起来：“仪王以后不要再对我说同样的话，我不喜欢听，佳和郡主视我为闺蜜，她若听到您今日所说，会作何感想？您还是回去好好想想如何偿还佳和郡主在您病着时，悉心照料床前的恩情，不要病着时需要她，才说些甜言蜜语哄她，病一好，就变成花心大萝卜。”
Qianli Xun stood up: "Never say things like that to me again, Prince Yi—I hate hearing them. Princess Jiahe treats me like her closest friend; how would she feel if she heard you today? Go home and think about repaying her devotion when she nursed you at your sickbed! Don't whisper sweet nothings when you need her, then turn into a total playboy the second you recover!"

千里寻说完，要往出走，又被仪王叫住。
Spinning on her heel, she started out—only for Prince Yi to call after her.

“你等等！”
"Hold on!"

千里寻站住回过头：“仪王还有什么吩咐？”
Stopping to glance back: "What else does Prince Yi want?"

申简辰走到千里寻身边：“幽栖居士放心，我会偿还梦容的恩情，也会娶她……”
Shen Jianchen stepped right up to her: "Rest easy, Youqi Jushi—I will repay Mengrong's kindness, and I will marry her..."

千里寻很不屑：“那就结了，还来招惹我干吗？”
Qianli Xun scoffed: "Then that settles it! Why keep pestering me?!"

“幽栖居士难道没听过情不自已吗？”
"Has Youqi Jushi never heard of a heart that cannot help itself?"

“听过又怎样？我不想再听到仪王对我说这些话，希望您以后不要再来望月轩。”
"Heard it—so what? I don't want to hear another word like this from Prince Yi. Please don't come to Wangyue Pavilion again."

千里寻已顾不上仪王的身份，将他甩在屋内，自顾离开。
Throwing royal etiquette to the wind, Qianli Xun left him standing there and marched out.

申简辰对外大声说：“放心，以后我不会来望月轩，会让白风把文渊的消息传给你。”
Shen Jianchen called after her into the empty room: "Rest easy—I won't come to Wangyue Pavilion again! I'll send Bai Feng with Wenyuan's news!"

没有佳人在侧，一桌子美食申简辰提不起一点儿兴致，他叹口气走出屋外，很不情愿地朝望月轩的门口走去。
With her gone, the whole table of delicacies turned tasteless as sawdust. Letting out a heavy sigh, he walked out with dragging feet toward the front gate.

半路上碰到韦嬷嬷，嬷嬷上前拜见：“仪王可吃好？”
Halfway across the yard, he met Matron Wei, who stepped up with a bow: "Did Prince Yi enjoy his meal?"

“还行。”申简辰觉得需叮嘱几句，“你家姑娘的脾气得改改，多亏我是个随和又明事理的王爷，否则，就她对我那态度，被罚跪多久都不为过。”
"Passable." Feeling a nudge to speak, Shen Jianchen said, "Your mistress needs to work on that temper of hers. Lucky for her I'm an easygoing, reasonable prince—pull that attitude on anyone else and she'd be kneeling in punishment for hours."

韦嬷嬷奉承道：“临安人都知道，仪王是位心有百姓，最没架子的好王爷，我等都希望像您这样的王爷多一些。我家姑娘脾气有时是坏了些，还望仪王担待。怪只怪她最近心情不好，满城风言风语不说，乔家又出那档子事，搁谁都会有情绪。”
Matron Wei flattered smooth: "Everyone in Lin'an knows Prince Yi cares for the common folk and has zero airs—we all pray for more princes like you! Our Young Lady's temper does flare now and then; please bear with her. Between the whole city's gossip and the Qiao family's mess, anyone would be on edge."

“算了，我不与她计较。”
"Forget it—I won't hold it against her."

韦嬷嬷继续说：“我在这里是专程等仪王。”
Matron Wei paused, then added, "Actually, I waited here on purpose to catch Prince Yi."

“哦，嬷嬷有话对我讲？”
"Oh? You have words for me, Matron?"

韦嬷嬷又拜一下：“近期满城都是关于姑娘的风言风语，把她说得一无是处，还望仪王考虑到我家姑娘的声誉，今后不要再来望月轩……”
Matron Wei bowed low once more: "With ugly rumors about our Young Lady still fresh across Lin'an, for the sake of her reputation, I beg Prince Yi not to visit Wangyue Pavilion again..."

再次被叮嘱，还是韦嬷嬷充满礼貌地叮嘱，申简辰感到落寞：“好吧，今后我不会再来望月轩。”
Warned away a second time—polite as Matron Wei's tone was, cold loneliness washed over Shen Jianchen: "Very well. I won't come to Wangyue Pavilion again."

申简辰既坚定又不舍地向外走去……
Turning with firm yet reluctant steps, he walked out toward the gate...

韦嬷嬷面向仪王的背影，再次行礼：“感谢仪王！”
Matron Wei bowed once more to his retreating back: "My thanks to Prince Yi!"
