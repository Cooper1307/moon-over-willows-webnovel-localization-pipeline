## Chapter 298: Finding an Excuse to Slip Away

Right on cue, Lin Jue's uncle Lin Zixiao rose to defend the five repetitions of *"alone"*—only for Qiao Mengrong to chime in smoothly, asking Qiao Wenxian to deliver the critique instead!

Clueless that his own wife had written that exact slam, Qiao Wenxian delivered an impassioned defense elevating the lyric into a poignant portrait of every cloistered woman's loneliness that taught husbands deeper tenderness—leaving Qianli Xun marveling at his spin while Haoyu whispered cheeky promises about ending her "sleeping alone."

Moments later, Qiao Mengrong strolled right over to Wenyuan's table and dropped her bomb with a silken smile: the very idiot he had just shredded before all of Lin'an's elite was his own wife, *Miao Zhiyu*!

Leaving Wenyuan stunned pale, Mengrong swept back past Qianli Xun with a glacial smirk that sent a real chill down Qianli Xun's spine—especially when Wenyuan quietly slipped out the door seconds later.

Losing all heart for the rest of the salon, Qianli Xun penned her promised fresh lyric for Xiangrong to present during the brush-painting round and slipped quietly out to her carriage with Haoyu (teasing away his jealous suspicion that she left over Wenyuan) and rode home.

Storming straight into Miao Zhiyu's east wing back at Qiao Manor, Qiao Wenxian confronted her bluntly—and even after Miao Zhiyu knelt sobbing that she couldn't refuse Princess Consort Yi, Xiaguo walked right in exposing her deeper jealousy over Shuzhen!

Fed up with endless bickering, Qiao Wenxian laid down the iron law to both women: yes, Shuzhen had been his dream, yet fate gave him *them*, and if they didn't cut out the backstabbing and slander, he'd divorce his wife and boot his concubine both!

Terrified onto their knees, both women quickly forged a polite surface truce once he stalked out—each secretly guarding her own survival.
