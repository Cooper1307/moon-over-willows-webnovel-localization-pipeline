### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 修正中式英语，生动刻画配角情绪（对应第19、20段：大哥二哥的对话）
> **【原文】** 大哥气道："这叫什么事儿！三弟怎么又把商妓带到家里？有辱门风！" 二哥劝道："大哥，你要理解三弟，他那娘子不跟他圆房，心也没在他身上，他毕竟是个血气方刚的男子。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    The eldest brother said angrily, "What is this? How can Third Brother bring a merchant courtesan home again? It disgraces our family reputation!"
    The second brother advised, "Eldest brother, you need to understand Third Brother. His wife doesn't consummate the marriage with him... After all, he's a young man full of vigor."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "**What an absolute disgrace!**" the eldest brother fumed. "How could Third Brother bring a merchant courtesan into the house again? **It tarnishes our family's reputation!**"
    "Brother, you need to understand him," the second brother reasoned. "His wife refuses to share his bed, and her heart clearly isn't with him. After all, **he's a young man in the prime of his vigor.**"
*   **💡 修改理由:** "What is this?" 翻译“这叫什么事儿”过于平淡生硬。改用 "What an absolute disgrace!"（真是一桩丑闻/太丢人了），更符合保守的长兄愤怒的口吻。

#### 2. 女主“看热闹不嫌事大”的霸气插话（对应第22段）
> **【原文】** 千里寻在身后听到，十分赞同："大哥二哥早，你们的建议甚好，赶紧给林觉纳房妾室，我看刚出去的春金巧挺合适，不如就她了？"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Qianli Xun heard this from behind and strongly agreed. "Good morning, eldest brother and second brother. Your suggestion is excellent. Quickly get Lin Jue a concubine..."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Overhearing them, Qianli Xun couldn't have agreed more. "Good morning, **brothers-in-law**! That is an excellent suggestion. You really should get Lin Jue a concubine as soon as possible. In fact, I think the girl who just left—Chun Jinqiao—would be a perfect fit. Why not her?"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 英语中直接称呼对方为 "eldest brother and second brother" 极为累赘且不地道，直接用 "brothers-in-law" 代替。重构了句子节奏，凸显女主略带嘲讽的爽文气场。

#### 3. 购物送礼时的地道口语表达（对应第32、34段）
> **【原文】** 千里寻毫不吝啬地说："同样的，再拿一个包上。" 
> "不是送你，是送你娘。我去你家需住一个月，得麻烦你的家人，带些礼物以表心意。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Qianli Xun said generously, "Another one just like it, please wrap it up." 
    "It's not for you. It's for your mother. I'll be staying at your house for a month. **I'll be troubling your family**, so I'm bringing some gifts to show my appreciation."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "**I'll take another one exactly like it,**" Qianli Xun said without hesitation. "**Wrap it up, please.**"
    "It's not for you, it's for your mother. **I'll be intruding on your family for a whole month.** It's only right that I bring a gift to show my gratitude."
*   **💡 修改理由:** "Troubling your family" 带有中式翻译痕迹。英文中表达“登门叨扰/借宿添麻烦”最地道的词是 "intrude on someone"，使对白更自然流畅。

#### 4. 身份设定的精准用词与内心吐槽格式（对应第47、48、52段）
> **【原文】** 苦荞骄傲地介绍道："这位是朱府大小姐...她可是竹林的主人，你们可得恭敬些。"
> "竹林的主人？"千里寻感到惊讶，苦荞这小丫头有多少事瞒着自己。
> 村长朝那村民吼道："乱喊什么？滚一边去！"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Kuqiao proudly introduced, "This is the eldest miss of the Zhu residence... She's the **owner** of the bamboo forest. You all must be respectful."
    "The **owner** of the bamboo forest?" Qianli Xun was surprised. How many things was this little girl Kuqiao hiding from her?
    The village head shouted at that villager, "What are you shouting about? **Get lost!**"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Kuqiao puffed out her chest and proudly announced, "This is the Eldest Miss of the Zhu family... She is the **landlady** of this entire bamboo forest, so you all better show some respect!"
    ***The landlady of the bamboo forest?*** Qianli Xun was caught completely off guard. *Just how many secrets is this little maid keeping from me?*
    "What are you hollering about?!" the village head **barked** at the man. "**Get out of here!**"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. 封建社会的“东家/收租人”用 **Landlady** 比 Owner 更加贴切，因为牵扯到佃户和租金（rent）。
    2. 将千里寻内心的惊讶严格改为**斜体 (Italics)** 的第一人称视角，符合网文规范。
    3. 村长的动作词用 "barked" (厉声喝骂)，比单纯的 "shouted" 更有画面感。

#### 5. 成语的视觉化意译（对应第72、85段）
> **【原文】** 听到谈论自己的婚事，苦蕨坐在一旁的凳子上一直红着脸。
> "我这些衣裳在村里穿着显得跟大家格格不入，还是穿你的好。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Hearing his marriage being discussed, Kujue sat on a stool to the side **with a red face**.
    "These clothes of mine **look out of place** in the village. It's better to wear yours."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Hearing his impending marriage being discussed openly, Kujue sat on a stool in the corner, **his face burning bright red**.
    "My clothes are far too flashy and will just make me **stick out like a sore thumb** here. I'd rather wear yours."
*   **💡 修改理由:** "With a red face" 画面感太弱，改为 "burning bright red"；“格格不入”翻译为英文绝佳的习惯用语 "stick out like a sore thumb" (惹眼/显得突兀)，让小说语言变得极其地道。
