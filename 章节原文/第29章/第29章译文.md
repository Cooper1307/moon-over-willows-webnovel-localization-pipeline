# Chapter 29: The Bamboo Forest Owner Returns to the Bamboo Forest

Early in the morning after breakfast, Kubiao arranged the carriage, called three or four maids, and moved the bundles and a wooden box packed last night onto the carriage.

The bundles contained nothing more than the miss's clothes, rouge and powder, brush, ink, paper, and inkstone; the wooden box contained tea-making utensils. There were no such things in the countryside, and the miss needed to drink tea every day, so they had to bring everything.

After everything was ready, Kubiao returned to the Zhulan Residence. "Miss, we can depart now."

Qianli Xun asked, "Have you brought enough silver?"

"Don't worry, Miss. There's enough silver."

Qianli Xun had just left the Zhulan Residence and walked out of the west courtyard. Because she had tasted peach blossom rice last night and the fragrance still lingered in her mouth this morning, while walking on the road in the Lin residence, she couldn't help but look at the ground peach blossoms a few more times, naturally slowing her pace.

Just as she was admiring them, a voice came from behind. "Oh my! The young mistress really leaves as soon as she says she will. It's so early in the morning, you're leaving even earlier than me."

Qianli Xun stopped and turned around. It was Chun Jinqiao. "What? Going back to Chunxiang Pavilion?"

"Of course. How can a woman like me compare to you, young mistress? You can go wherever you want."

A mosquito was about to attack Chun Jinqiao. Qianli Xun saw it and raised her hand to shoo it away.

Chun Jinqiao quickly covered her right cheek with her handkerchief. "Young mistress, you want to hit me again?"

Qianli Xun put down her hand. "I was swatting a mosquito, but it's already flown away."

"You scared me to death. I thought you were going to hit me again." Chun Jinqiao no longer covered her face and swayed her waist as she walked in front of Qianli Xun.

Qianli Xun looked at Chun Jinqiao's swaying waist and thought, so this is what Lin Jue likes. Maybe she can help me. "Hey! Wait a moment."

Chun Jinqiao stopped and turned around. "What's the matter, young mistress?"

Qianli Xun moved closer to Chun Jinqiao. "You're the merchant courtesan Lin Jue brings home every time, right?"

Mentioning this, Chun Jinqiao was quite proud. "Of course. In the entire Chunxiang Pavilion, the Military Training Officer is only good to me."

"Since he's good to you, help him. Persuade him to agree to a mutual divorce with me."

Chun Jinqiao stared at the Lin family's young mistress with an analytical gaze. "The young mistress is truly a special woman. It's just a pity that she doesn't have the Military Training Officer in her heart. Don't worry, for the Military Training Officer, I'll persuade him. It's just that he can't let go of you yet."

Chun Jinqiao swayed her waist and walked away. At the main gate, she met Lin Jue's eldest brother and second brother. The two were neatly dressed and about to go out. Chun Jinqiao didn't greet them and directly got on the Chunxiang Pavilion's carriage.

The eldest brother said angrily, "What is this? How can Third Brother bring a merchant courtesan home again? It disgraces our family reputation!"

The second brother advised, "Eldest brother, you need to understand Third Brother. His wife doesn't consummate the marriage with him, and her heart isn't with him. After all, he's a young man full of vigor."

The eldest brother said, "I'll persuade Third Brother another day and have him quickly take a concubine."

Qianli Xun heard this from behind and strongly agreed. "Good morning, eldest brother and second brother. Your suggestion is excellent. Quickly get Lin Jue a concubine. I think Chun Jinqiao who just left is quite suitable. Why not her?"

The eldest brother and second brother looked at each other.

The eldest brother replied, "Taking a concubine also requires choosing a girl from a good family. How can we choose a merchant courtesan? No, no!"

The second brother didn't take the bait and directly asked, "Where is sister-in-law going so early in the morning?"

Qianli Xun didn't avoid the question. Why should she? They were the ones who blocked the mutual divorce. "I'm going to live in the countryside for a while, breathe some fresh air. Staying in the Lin residence all the time is about to suffocate me."

The eldest brother wanted to say critical words but was stopped by the second brother. He could only watch as Qianli Xun led Kubiao out of the main gate and got on the carriage.

The eldest brother could only sigh. "What is this? Whose wife would be like her?"

The second brother advised, "Even Third Brother can't do anything about her. What can we do? We might as well persuade Third Brother to agree to the mutual divorce and let her go."

The eldest brother nodded. "Tonight, we three brothers will get together for a drink and persuade him while we're at it."

...

The carriage reached the busy market. Through the window, Qianli Xun saw a jewelry shop and asked the coachman to stop. She wanted to go in and take a look.

The jewelry shop mainly sold gold and silver jewelry. As soon as she entered the shop, she could see various styles displayed on the counter.

Qianli Xun saw a silver bracelet, picked it up to admire it, and thought it was very nice. "Kubiao, put out your hand."

The miss planned to give her a bracelet. Kubiao didn't stand on ceremony and put out her wrist. "Thank you, Miss."

Qianli Xun grabbed Kubiao's wrist and put the silver bracelet on her. Kubiao pulled back her hand and admired it lovingly. It was even more beautiful than the original one.

The shopkeeper said, "One tael of silver."

Qianli Xun said generously, "Another one just like it, please wrap it up."

Kubiao tried to stop her. "Miss, one is enough. You don't need to give me so many."

"It's not for you. It's for your mother. I'll be staying at your house for a month. I'll be troubling your family, so I'm bringing some gifts to show my appreciation."

"Don't be so formal, Miss. My parents will be happy enough that you're coming to stay at our house. How can we ask for gifts?"

"Whether they want them or not is their business. Giving gifts is my sentiment."

The shopkeeper had already wrapped the gift. "Two taels of silver."

Kubiao had to take it and took out two taels of silver from her money pouch to pay.

Qianli Xun wanted to buy more gifts. Kubiao couldn't persuade her otherwise and suggested that the miss just buy some special pastries from Lin'an city to take along.

When the carriage left Lin'an city and got closer to the village, the bamboo around became more abundant. They grew more lush and thicker than the bamboo in the Zhulan Residence. Soon, a large bamboo forest appeared before them.

Passing through the dense bamboo forest, they continued deeper. Qianli Xun couldn't imagine that people would live deep in the bamboo forest, even hiding a village. It wasn't until the bamboo was no longer crowded together in clusters and individual bamboo houses appeared before her that she believed a village could really be hidden deep in the bamboo forest.

The bamboo houses were all simple but sturdy enough. Qianli Xun was amazed at how powerful human creativity was, connecting bamboo poles in various ways to build houses.

The carriage kept going until there was no more road, so it had to stop.

Qianli Xun looked outside and saw many villagers looking at the carriage.

It was rare for a carriage to come in all year round, so the villagers were very curious. Some poked their heads out from the bamboo houses; some stopped their work; some who were about to leave the village turned back to watch the fun; a group of children who were playing were also attracted to come over.

Kubiao said, "Miss, I'll go down and call some people to help carry the things."

After Kubiao jumped down from the carriage, someone said, "So it's Kubiao. Going to the city to work as a maid for a wealthy family, you're different when you come back. Riding such an impressive carriage. I thought it was the city's rent collector coming."

Some of the children knew Kubiao and gathered around. "Sister Kubiao, did you bring any good food from the city?"

Kubiao was prepared in advance. She took out five or six candies from her bosom and distributed them to the children, then said to the surrounding villagers, "Come a few people and help carry the things to my house."

Three or four young men came over at once. "I'll do it! I'll do it!"

Kubiao got back on the carriage and pushed out the bundles and wooden box one by one. The young men grabbed them.

Kubiao got off the carriage first, then Qianli Xun followed.

Kubiao said to the coachman, "Uncle, please return to the Lin residence first. Come pick up the miss and me on the same day next month."

The coachman agreed and drove the carriage out of the village.

After watching the carriage leave, Kubiao turned around and saw everyone staring at her miss. No wonder. The villagers had never seen a refined and noble young lady like the miss.

Kubiao proudly introduced, "This is the eldest miss of the Zhu residence. She'll be staying at our house for a while. She's the owner of the bamboo forest. You all must be respectful."

"The owner of the bamboo forest?" Qianli Xun was surprised. How many things was this little girl Kubiao hiding from her? She stared at Kubiao with an inquiring gaze.

Kubiao was getting more and more used to the current miss. She had inadvertently forgotten that the miss had lost her memory after rebirth. She quickly explained, "I didn't mean to hide it from you, Miss. I forgot that you don't remember this."

Qianli Xun couldn't ask Kubiao too much in front of everyone. She said to the crowd, "Dear elders and fellow villagers, I'll be troubling you all for a few days. I hope you won't mind."

While the two were talking, the village head had already appeared. Since she was the owner of the bamboo forest, he had to come forward to greet her. "Madam has come here. I failed to welcome you from afar. You should have sent someone ahead to let me know so I could prepare a place for you to stay."

"Who are you?" Qianli Xun didn't know the village head. She just felt that this middle-aged man who took the initiative to greet her must have an unusual identity.

"He's the village head," Kubiao quickly introduced. "Uncle Li, the miss is staying at my house. You don't need to worry."

Qianli Xun also said, "Village head, don't be so polite. I'll just stay at Kubiao's house."

The village head had to say, "In that case, please come, Madam."

The village head led the way in front. Qianli Xun had just taken two steps when a villager shouted, "Madam! Can you collect less rent?"

Qianli Xun hadn't met Nanny Wei yet and didn't understand the rent collection matter, so she didn't pay attention.

The village head shouted at that villager, "What are you shouting about? Get lost!"

The village roads were narrow and winding, all built around each family's bamboo house. Bamboo was the only plant along the road. After going around about ten bamboo houses, they finally reached Kubiao's house. The bamboo house was quite large, but it had been in disrepair for a long time and looked somewhat dilapidated. It was barely habitable.

The village head shouted outside the bamboo house, "Lao Ku! Your family has honored guests!"

Two men and a woman came out from the house. The two men were one old and one young. The old one's face was full of weathering, with white hair at his temples and forehead; the young one was about fourteen or fifteen years old, with dark skin and strong muscles on his arms from years of labor. The woman also had graying temples, a few wrinkles on her forehead, and a somewhat dirty complexion. It wasn't that she hadn't washed her face, but the marks of labor that couldn't be washed away.

The three saw Kubiao first. The woman said excitedly, "Qiao, you're finally back. I missed you to death." She quickly walked down the bamboo ladder and went forward to hold Kubiao.

Kubiao said, "Mother, let me introduce you. This is the miss. She'll be staying at our house for a while."

Only then did the woman look at Qianli Xun. "This girl who looks like she came out of a painting is the miss from the Zhu residence? She looks kind. You can rest assured following her, my dear." Then she pulled Qianli Xun. "Quick! Come sit in the house."

Qianli Xun was pulled into the bamboo house.

Several villagers who helped carry the things went into the house, put them down, then walked out. They didn't want to go far and stretched their necks to peek into the house.

The village head shouted, "What's so good to look at? Hurry up and get back to your own business!"

The villagers were most afraid of the village head and could only leave while laughing.

Lao Ku and his son went forward to invite the village head to sit in the house. The village head said, "Madam is here to stay. Just say what you need. Don't be polite with me."

Lao Ku smiled so much he couldn't close his mouth. "With the village head's words, how could I not come to you? If there's anything we need, I'll have Kujue go tell you."

The village head voiced his suspicion. "In previous years, Nanny Wei came to collect rent. Why did Madam come in person this year? There won't be any changes, will there?"

Lao Ku explained, "Village head, you're just too suspicious. Didn't our Kubiao say? Madam is here to stay at our house for a while. She probably isn't here to collect rent."

"Never mind. I'll go in and say a few words to Madam, then leave."

The village head walked toward the house, and Lao Ku and Kujue followed.

All the furniture in the bamboo house was made of bamboo. Although not elegant, it was simple and rustic. Qianli Xun sat on a low bamboo chair. On the table beside her was tea brewed with bamboo leaves, steaming hot.

The village head came in and said politely, "Madam will stay at Lao Ku's house first. If you need anything, have Lao Ku come find me."

Qianli Xun said politely, "Thank you for your care, Village Head. Please go about your business. If there's any need, I'll definitely come to you."

"Madam is busy. There are still matters in the village that need my attention. I won't disturb you here." After saying this, the village head took his leave and left.

After Kubiao introduced her family members to the miss one by one, Qianli Xun had her take out the silver bracelet and special pastries. The Ku family was all delighted.

Lao Ku asked, "I wonder how long Madam will be staying here?"

Qianli Xun replied, "One month."

Lao Ku said, "Madam is a noble person. You can't live in a dilapidated bamboo house like us. It just so happens that we built a new bamboo house for Kujue last year. No one has lived in it yet. The house is close to the eastern bamboo forest with a nice view. If Madam doesn't mind, you can stay there temporarily."

This house suited Qianli Xun's taste perfectly. She had no objections.

Kubiao asked, "Father, mother, is Kujue getting married? Why build him a bamboo house so early?"

Lao Ku replied, "It's about time. The marriage has been arranged. The new bride will come next year."

Hearing his marriage being discussed, Kujue sat on a stool to the side with a red face.

Kubiao's mother said, "Madam, Kubiao is by your side, causing you trouble. Her future marriage will need your attention."

Kubiao interrupted, "Mother, what are you saying? I'm not getting married. I'll accompany the miss forever."

Qianli Xun smiled and said, "Don't worry, Aunt. I'll definitely help her find a good family."

After talking for a while longer, Kubiao went to help her mother steam bamboo tube rice. She also took out the dried bamboo shoots from last year, soaked them in water, and stir-fried a bamboo shoot dish. Because Madam had come, Kujue went to the river behind the village and caught a fish, made fish soup. The whole family sat around a table to entertain Madam.

The Ku family's food was far inferior to that of the Lin residence, but it had a natural fragrance. Qianli Xun thought the taste was quite special.

After the meal, with the help of the Ku family, they moved the things to the new bamboo house. After the family left, Kubiao started to organize everything according to the miss's preferences, arranging each item.

The furniture in the new bamboo house wasn't complete yet, but fortunately there was a low table made of bamboo. Kubiao placed the table facing the door and arranged the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone neatly. When the miss sat here to write, she could just see the bamboo forest outside. It was really pleasant. Judging by the table's height, one could only sit on the ground. Kubiao brought a square cotton cushion from home and placed it beside the table. It was just right for the miss to sit on.

There was also a low bamboo cabinet that could be used as a tea table. Kubiao found a piece of printed coarse cloth and laid it out, then arranged the tea-making tools one by one.

In the bedroom was a newly made bamboo bed. Kubiao laid out the new bedding made for Kujue at home, then hung the miss's everyday clothes on the bamboo rack.

After everything was organized, Qianli Xun was very satisfied. She looked at the clothes hanging on the bamboo rack, then looked at what she was wearing. They were too exquisite and created a sense of distance from the villagers.

"Kubiao, find me a few sets of your clothes."

"Why do you want my clothes, miss?"

"These clothes of mine look out of place in the village. It's better to wear yours."

"In that case, I'm sorry to make you compromise, miss. I happen to have brought two extra sets. You can try them on first, miss."

Kubiao fetched a set and helped the miss change. Even in ordinary clothes, the miss couldn't hide the elegant temperament that emanated from her bones.

Qianli Xun also removed the jewelry from her head and had Kubiao put it away.

She came to the door of the bamboo house. Facing her was the bamboo forest. A cool breeze blew, and the bamboo fragrance was refreshing. Qianli Xun felt that staying in such a beautiful place for a month would definitely help her destiny.
