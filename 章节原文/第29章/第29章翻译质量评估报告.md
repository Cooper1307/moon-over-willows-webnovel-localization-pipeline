### 2. 过度意译检查（需修改）

#### 问题1：表达不够自然
- **位置**：第116行
- **原文**：春金巧盯着林家的少夫人，露出研究的眼神
- **初译**：Chun Jinqiao stared at the Lin family's young mistress with a researchful look
- **问题**："researchful"不是常用词
- **建议**：改为 **with a studying look** 或 **with an analytical gaze**

#### 问题2：表达不够自然
- **位置**：第488行
- **原文**：看着面善，你跟着她娘就放心了
- **初译**：She looks kind. You can rest assured following her, mother.
- **问题**：对话中称呼"mother"不自然，应是苦荞娘对苦荞说话
- **建议**：改为 **She looks kind. You can rest assured following her, my dear.**

#### 问题3：表达不够自然
- **位置**：第626行
- **原文**：苦荞娘说："东家，苦荞在您身边，给您添麻烦了，她以后的婚事，还得东家您多操心。"
- **初译**：Kubiao's mother said, "Owner, Kubiao is by your side, causing you trouble. Her future marriage will need your attention, owner."
- **问题**：重复使用"owner"不自然
- **建议**：改为 **Kubiao's mother said, "Owner, Kubiao is by your side, causing you trouble. Her future marriage will need your attention."**
