# 第29章 竹林东家归竹林
# Chapter 29: The Secret Landlady of the Bamboo Grove

---

一大早用过早饭，苦荞安排好马车，叫来三四个丫鬟，把昨晚收拾好的包袱和一个木箱搬到马车上。

Dawn had barely broken when Kuqiao finished breakfast and set to work. She roped in three or four maids to haul last night's bundles and a wooden chest onto the carriage.

---

包袱里装的无非是小姐的衣裳、胭脂水粉、笔墨纸砚之类；木箱里装着做茶的器具，乡下没这些玩意儿，小姐每日要喝茶，必须带齐全。

The bundles held the usual—clothes, rouge, brushes, ink, paper, and inkstone. The wooden chest carried tea-making tools. The countryside had none of these luxuries, and the miss drank tea daily, so they had to pack everything.

---

一切收拾好之后，苦荞回到竹兰居："小姐，可以出发。"

Once everything was ready, Kuqiao returned to the Zhulan Residence. "Miss, we're ready to go."

---

千里寻问："银子可带够？"

Qianli Xun asked, "Did you bring enough silver?"

---

"小姐放心，银子尽够。"

"Don't worry, Miss. We've got plenty."

---

千里寻才出了竹兰居，又走出西院。因为昨晚品尝过桃花饭，一早想起来依然口齿留香，走在林府的路上，不由自主多用目光照顾地桃花几眼，步子自然放慢。

Qianli Xun stepped out of the Zhulan Residence and crossed the west courtyard. The taste of last night's peach blossom rice still lingered on her tongue. As she walked through the Lin residence, her gaze kept drifting to the ground peach blossoms along the path, her steps slowing without her noticing.

---

正欣赏着，身后传来声音："哎吆！少夫人真是说走就走，这一大早的，走得比我还早。"

She was still admiring the flowers when a voice called out behind her. "Well, well! The young mistress says 'go' and goes. It's barely dawn, and you're already outpacing me."

---

千里寻停下脚步，回过头，原来是春金巧："怎么？要回春香楼？"

Qianli Xun stopped and turned. Chun Jinqiao. "What? Heading back to Chunxiang Pavilion?"

---

"可不是吗？我这样的女人哪比得上少夫人您，想去哪里就去哪里。"

"Of course. How can someone like me compare to you, young mistress? You can go wherever you please."

---

一只蚊子打算袭击春金巧，千里寻看到，抬手去赶。

A mosquito buzzed toward Chun Jinqiao. Qianli Xun spotted it and raised her hand to swat it away.

---

春金巧赶紧用巾帕捂住右脸："少夫人又想打我？"

Chun Jinqiao flinched and clapped her handkerchief over her cheek. "You're going to hit me again, young mistress?"

---

千里寻放下手："我是打蚊子，不过它已经飞走。"

Qianli Xun dropped her hand. "Just a mosquito. It's gone now."

---

"吓死我了，还以为你又要打我。"春金巧不再捂脸，扭着腰肢走到千里寻前面。

"You nearly gave me a heart attack! I thought you were going to hit me again." Chun Jinqiao lowered her handkerchief and swanned off, all hips and perfume.

---

千里寻瞅着春金巧扭动的腰肢，心想原来林觉喜欢这样的，也许她能帮到自己："喂！你等会儿。"

Qianli Xun watched the sway of Chun Jinqiao's hips and thought, *So this is what Lin Jue likes. Maybe she can help me.* "Hey! Wait a moment."

---

春金巧站住，转过身："少夫人有什么事？"

Chun Jinqiao stopped and turned. "What can I do for you, young mistress?"

---

千里寻靠近春金巧："林觉每次带到家里的商妓都是你吧？"

Qianli Xun stepped closer. "You're the merchant courtesan Lin Jue brings home every time, aren't you?"

---

提到这一点，春金巧倒是挺骄傲："那是自然，在整个春香楼，练兵官只对我好。"

Chun Jinqiao's pride showed at the mention. "Of course. In all of Chunxiang Pavilion, the Military Training Officer only has eyes for me."

---

"看在他对你好的份上，你帮帮他，劝他跟我和离。"

"Since he treats you well, do me a favor. Persuade him to agree to a mutual divorce with me."

---

春金巧盯着林家的少夫人，露出研究的眼神："少夫人真是个特别的女子，只可惜心里没有练兵官。放心，为了练兵官，我会劝他，只是他目前还放不下你。"

Chun Jinqiao studied the Lin family's young mistress with an analytical gaze. "The young mistress is truly a special woman. It's just a pity that she doesn't have the Military Training Officer in her heart. Don't worry—for his sake, I'll persuade him. It's just that he can't let go of you yet."

---

春金巧又扭着腰肢走开，在大门口碰到林觉的大哥和二哥。两位衣装整洁，正要出门。春金巧并未与他们打招呼，直接上了春香楼的马车。

Chun Jinqiao swanned off, all hips and perfume. At the main gate, she ran into Lin Jue's eldest and second brothers. The two were neatly dressed and about to head out. Chun Jinqiao didn't so much as glance at them. She climbed into the Chunxiang Pavilion's carriage and was gone.

---

大哥气道："这叫什么事儿！三弟怎么又把商妓带到家里？有辱门风！"

"What an absolute disgrace!" the eldest brother fumed. "How could Third Brother bring a merchant courtesan into the house again? It tarnishes our family's reputation!"

---

二哥劝道："大哥，你要理解三弟，他那娘子不跟他圆房，心也没在他身上，他毕竟是个血气方刚的男子。"

"Brother, you need to understand him," the second brother reasoned. "His wife refuses to share his bed, and her heart clearly isn't with him. After all, he's a young man in the prime of his vigor."

---

大哥说："改日我劝劝三弟，让他赶紧纳一房妾室。"

The eldest brother said, "I'll talk to Third Brother another day and have him take a concubine soon enough."

---

千里寻在身后听到，十分赞同："大哥二哥早，你们的建议甚好，赶紧给林觉纳房妾室，我看刚出去的春金巧挺合适，不如就她了？"

Overhearing them, Qianli Xun couldn't have agreed more. "Good morning, brothers-in-law! That is an excellent suggestion. You really should get Lin Jue a concubine as soon as possible. In fact, I think the girl who just left—Chun Jinqiao—would be a perfect fit. Why not her?"

---

大哥二哥面面相觑。

The eldest brother and second brother looked at each other.

---

大哥回道："纳妾也要选好人家的姑娘，怎么能选商妓？不行不行！"

The eldest brother replied, "Taking a concubine also requires choosing a girl from a good family. How can we choose a merchant courtesan? No, no!"

---

二哥不接这茬，直接问："一大早弟妹要去哪里？"

The second brother didn't take the bait. "Where is sister-in-law heading so early in the morning?"

---

千里寻毫不避讳，谁让他们阻止和离："去乡下住段时日，呼吸呼吸新鲜空气，一直待在林府快要把我憋坏。"

Qianli Xun saw no reason to dissemble. They'd blocked her divorce. She owed them nothing. "I'm going to live in the countryside for a while, breathe some fresh air. Staying in the Lin residence all the time is about to suffocate me."

---

大哥想要说出批评的话，被二哥拦住，只能眼睁睁看着千里寻带苦荞走出大门，登上马车。

The eldest brother wanted to scold but the second brother held him back. He could only watch as Qianli Xun led Kuqiao out the main gate and onto the carriage.

---

大哥只能慨叹道："这叫什么事儿！谁家的娘子会像她一样？"

The eldest brother could only sigh. "What a mess! Which wife in the world acts like her?"

---

二哥劝道："三弟都奈何不了她，我们又能如何？不如劝三弟同意和离，放她走吧。"

The second brother advised, "Even Third Brother can't do anything about her. What can we do? We might as well persuade him to agree to the divorce and let her go."

---

大哥点点头："今晚我们三兄弟约在一起喝点儿，顺便劝劝他。"

The eldest brother nodded. "Tonight, we three brothers will get together for a drink and talk some sense into him."

---

……

...

---

马车行走到闹市，透过窗户，千里寻看到一家首饰店，叫车夫把车停下，她要进店瞧瞧。

The carriage reached the busy market. Through the window, Qianli Xun spotted a jewelry shop and told the coachman to stop. She wanted to take a look.

---

首饰店以金银首饰为主，一进店门，能看到柜台上摆着各式各样的款式。

The shop dealt mostly in gold and silver jewelry. Inside the door, counters displayed all sorts of designs.

---

千里寻看到一个银镯子，拿起来欣赏一番，觉得很不错："苦荞，手伸出来。"

Qianli Xun spotted a silver bracelet, picked it up to admire it, and liked what she saw. "Kuqiao, hold out your hand."

---

小姐打算赔给她手镯，苦荞并不客气，伸出手腕："谢谢小姐。"

Her miss was buying her a bracelet. Kuqiao didn't stand on ceremony and held out her wrist. "Thank you, Miss."

---

千里寻抓住苦荞的手腕，给她把银镯子戴上。苦荞收回手，爱溺地欣赏着，比原来的还好看。

Qianli Xun took Kuqiao's wrist and slid the bracelet on. Kuqiao pulled back her hand and admired it lovingly. Even more beautiful than the original.

---

掌柜说："一两银子。"

The shopkeeper said, "One tael of silver."

---

千里寻毫不吝啬地说："同样的，再拿一个包上。"

"I'll take another one exactly like it," Qianli Xun said without hesitation. "Wrap it up, please."

---

苦荞阻止道："小姐，一个够了，不用送我那么多。"

Kuqiao tried to stop her. "Miss, one is enough. You don't need to give me so many."

---

"不是送你，是送你娘。我去你家需住一个月，得麻烦你的家人，带些礼物以表心意。"

"It's not for you—it's for your mother. I'll be staying at your place for a whole month. The least I can do is bring a gift."

---

"小姐不要见外，您能去我家住，我爹娘高兴还来不及，怎能要礼物？"

"Don't be so formal, Miss. My parents will be thrilled you're coming to stay. How could we ask for gifts?"

---

"他们要不要是他们的事，送礼物是我的心意。"

"Whether they want them or not is their business. Giving gifts is my gesture."

---

掌柜的已将礼物包好："二两银子。"

The shopkeeper had already wrapped the gift. "Two taels of silver."

---

苦荞只能接过，从钱袋里掏出二两银子给了。

Kuqiao had no choice but to take it. She fished two taels of silver from her money pouch and paid.

---

千里寻又要求再买些礼物，苦荞拗不过，建议小姐买些临安城的特色糕点带上即可。

Qianli Xun wanted to buy more gifts. Kuqiao couldn't talk her out of it, so she suggested the miss just buy some specialty pastries from Lin'an to take along.

---

当马车出了临安城，越来越靠近村子时，周围的竹子变得多起来，比竹兰居的竹子长得更茂盛，更粗壮，很快眼前出现一大片竹林。

As the carriage left Lin'an and drew closer to the village, bamboo began to appear everywhere—more lush and thick than the bamboo in the Zhulan Residence. Soon, a vast bamboo forest stretched before them.

---

穿过茂密的竹林，一直向深处行进。千里寻难以想象，在竹林深处还会有人居住，甚至藏着一个村子。直到竹子不再成片成片挤在一起，一个个竹屋出现在眼前时，她才相信竹林深处真的可以藏着一个村庄。

They pushed deeper through the dense bamboo forest. Qianli Xun couldn't imagine people living in such a place, let alone an entire village hidden away. Only when the bamboo stopped crowding together and individual bamboo houses came into view did she believe it—a village, hidden deep in the bamboo forest.

---

竹屋都很简陋，但够结实。千里寻惊讶人的创造力实在强大，把一根根竹子想方设法连接在一起可以造出一座座房屋来。

The bamboo houses were simple but sturdy. Qianli Xun marveled at human ingenuity—connecting bamboo pole by bamboo pole to build entire homes.

---

马车走着走着，已无路可走，只能停下来。

The carriage went as far as it could, then stopped. There was no more road.

---

千里寻往外看去，有许多村民都看向马车。

Qianli Xun looked out. Many villagers were staring at the carriage.

---

一年到头很难有马车进来，村民们很是好奇。有的从竹屋里探出脑袋；有的停下手中的活儿；有的本要出村，又拐回来看热闹；一群小孩子，本来正在玩耍，也被吸引而来。

A carriage. HERE. The village hadn't seen one in a year, maybe two. Heads popped from doorways. Hands stilled over weaving. A man halfway out the gate turned on his heel and came BACK. And the children—abandoning whatever game they'd been playing—swarmed.

---

苦荞说："小姐，我下去叫些人帮忙拿东西。"

Kuqiao said, "Miss, I'll go down and get some help to carry the things."

---

苦荞跳下马车后，有人说："原来是苦荞啊，到城里大户人家当丫鬟，回来就是不一样，坐这么气派的马车，我还以为是城里收租的来了。"

After Kuqiao jumped down, someone said, "So it's Kuqiao! Working as a maid for a wealthy family in the city has changed you. Riding such an impressive carriage—I thought it was the city's rent collector coming."

---

小孩子中有认识苦荞的，都围过来："苦荞姐姐，你从城里回来有没有带好吃的？"

Some of the children recognized Kuqiao and gathered around. "Sister Kuqiao, did you bring any treats from the city?"

---

苦荞提前有准备，从怀里掏出五六个糖果分给小孩子，然后对围观的村民说："来几个人，帮忙把东西拿到我家。"

Kuqiao was prepared. She pulled five or six candies from her bosom and handed them out to the children, then called to the gathered villagers, "Come a few of you, help carry the things to my house."

---

一下子过来三四个年轻男子："我来！我来！"

Three or four young men rushed over at once. "I'll do it! I'll do it!"

---

苦荞又走上马车，把包袱和木箱一个一个推出来，年轻男子们抢着拿了。

Kuqiao climbed back onto the carriage and pushed out the bundles and wooden chest one by one. The young men grabbed them eagerly.

---

苦荞先下车，千里寻才跟着下来。

Kuqiao got off first, then Qianli Xun followed.

---

苦荞向车夫说："大叔，您先回林府，下个月同一天来接我和小姐。"

Kuqiao told the coachman, "Uncle, please return to the Lin residence first. Come pick up the miss and me on the same day next month."

---

车夫答应着，赶马车出了村。

The coachman agreed and drove the carriage out of the village.

---

苦荞目送马车离开后，回过头，看到大家都直愣愣地盯着自家小姐。也难怪，村里人哪里见过像小姐这样气质典雅又高贵的大家闺秀。

Kuqiao watched the carriage leave, then turned around. Everyone was staring at her miss. No wonder—when had these villagers ever seen a woman like her? Silk sleeves, porcelain skin, the kind of lady who'd never touched a broom in her life.

---

苦荞骄傲地介绍道："这位是朱府大小姐，要在我家住一段时日，她可是竹林的主人，你们可得恭敬些。"

Kuqiao puffed out her chest and proudly announced, "This is the Eldest Miss of the Zhu family. She'll be staying at our house for a while. She's the landlady of this entire bamboo forest, so you all better show some respect!"

---

"竹林的主人？"千里寻感到惊讶，苦荞这小丫头有多少事瞒着自己，她用询问的目光盯着苦荞。

*The landlady of the bamboo forest?* Qianli Xun was caught completely off guard. *Just how many secrets is this little maid keeping from me?*

---

苦荞越来越适应现在的小姐，竟然无意中忘记小姐重生失忆之事，赶紧解释："我不是故意瞒着小姐，是忘了您不记得此事。"

Kuqiao was getting more used to her miss these days. She'd almost forgotten about the memory loss from the rebirth. She quickly explained, "I didn't mean to hide it from you, Miss. I forgot you don't remember this."

---

千里寻不便在众目睽睽之下向苦荞打听太多，对众人说："各位父老乡亲，我在此打扰几日，还望大家不要嫌弃。"

Qianli Xun couldn't ask Kuqiao too much in front of everyone. She said to the crowd, "Dear elders and fellow villagers, I'll be troubling you all for a few days. I hope you won't mind."

---

两人说话时，村长已出现，既是竹林的东家，他必须上前拜见："东家来此，有失远迎，您应该提前派人说一声，我好准备东家住的地儿。"

While the two were talking, the village head had already appeared. Since she was the landlady of the bamboo forest, he had to come forward to greet her. "Madam, you've honored us. I failed to welcome you from afar. You should have sent someone ahead to let me know so I could prepare a place for you to stay."

---

"您是？"千里寻不认识村长，只觉这位主动前来打招呼的中年汉子身份应该不一般。

"Who are you?" Qianli Xun didn't know the village head. She just felt that this middle-aged man who took the initiative to greet her must have some status.

---

"是村长。"苦荞赶紧介绍，"李叔，小姐住我家，您不用操心。"

"He's the village head," Kuqiao quickly introduced. "Uncle Li, the miss is staying at my house. You don't need to worry."

---

千里寻也说道："村长不必客气，我住苦荞家就行。"

Qianli Xun also said, "Village head, don't be so polite. I'll just stay at Kuqiao's house."

---

村长只好说："既如此，东家请。"

The village head had to say, "In that case, please come, Madam."

---

村长在前面带路，千里寻跟着刚走出两步，有村民喊道："东家！能不能少收些租子？"

The village head led the way. Qianli Xun had just taken two steps when a villager shouted, "Madam! Any chance of lower rent this year?"

---

千里寻还未见过韦嬷嬷，对收租一事不明了，并未理会。

Qianli Xun hadn't met Matron Wei yet and didn't understand the rent collection matter, so she didn't pay attention.

---

村长朝那村民吼道："乱喊什么？滚一边去！"

The village head shouted at that villager, "What are you shouting about? Get lost!"

---

村子的路狭窄弯曲，均是绕着各家的竹屋而设，竹子是路边唯一的植物。绕了大约十来个竹屋，终于到达苦荞家门口，竹屋倒是建得挺大，只是日久失修，看上去有些破烂，勉强还能住人。

The village roads were narrow and winding, weaving between bamboo houses. Bamboo was the only plant along the path. After passing about ten houses, they reached Kuqiao's home—quite large, but years of neglect had left it looking run-down. 	Still livable—just.

---

村长在竹屋外大喊："老苦！你家来贵客了！"

The village head bellowed outside the bamboo house, "Lao Ku! You've got honored guests!"

---

从屋里走出两男一女。两个男的，一老一少，老的满脸沧桑，鬓角和额头已有白发；年轻的大约十四五岁，皮肤黝黑，因常年劳作臂膀上露出结实的肌肉。女的也是两鬓斑白，额头上挂着几道皱纹，面色有些脏，并不是没洗脸，而是洗也洗不掉的劳动痕迹。

Two men and a woman stepped out. One old, one young. The elder's face was weathered, white hair at his temples and forehead; the younger, about fourteen or fifteen, had dark skin and arms corded with muscle from years of labor. The woman also had graying temples and a few lines etched across her forehead. Her skin was weathered—not dirty, never dirty, just... marked. Years of sun and soil had stained it in ways no water could reach.

---

三人先看到苦荞，女人激动地说："荞，你可回来，想死娘了。"她快步走下竹梯，上前拉住苦荞。

The three saw Kuqiao first. The woman's face lit up. "Qiao! You're BACK!" She rushed down the bamboo steps and seized her daughter's hands. "I've missed you something awful."

---

苦荞说："娘，我给您介绍，这位是小姐，她要在我们家住段时日。"

Kuqiao said, "Mother, let me introduce you. This is the miss. She'll be staying at our house for a while."

---

女人才看向千里寻："这位像从画里出来的姑娘就是朱府小姐？看着面善，你跟着她娘就放心了。"然后拉起千里寻，"快！屋里坐。"

Only then did the woman notice Qianli Xun. "She looks like she stepped out of a scroll painting! THIS is the Zhu family's young lady?" She took Qianli Xun's hand. "Quick! Come sit inside."

---

千里寻被拉着走进竹屋。

Qianli Xun was pulled into the bamboo house.

---

几位帮忙搬东西的村民进屋把东西放好后，走出门，不愿走远，伸长脖子朝屋内瞧去。

Several villagers who'd helped carry the things went inside, set them down, then stepped out. They didn't want to leave, craning their necks to peer back in.

---

村长吼道："有什么好看的？还不赶紧忙自家的事去！"

The village head barked, "Nothing to see! Get back to your own business!"

---

几位村民最怕村长，只能嘻哈着离开。

The villagers scattered, grinning and nudging each other. Nobody dared linger—not with the village head watching.

---

老苦和儿子上前招呼村长家里坐，村长说："东家来住，需要什么尽管说，不要跟我客气。"

Lao Ku and his son invited the village head inside. The village head said, "The landlady is staying here. Just say what you need. Don't be shy with me."

---

老苦笑得合不拢嘴："有村长这话，我还能不找你？有什么需要，我让苦蕨去说。"

Lao Ku's face split into a grin so wide it looked painful. "With your words behind me, how could I not come to you? If we need anything, I'll send Kujue to find you."

---

村长说出心中疑虑："往年都是韦嬷嬷前来收租，今年东家怎么亲自前来？该不会有什么变动？"

The village head voiced his suspicion. "In previous years, Matron Wei came to collect rent. Why did the landlady come in person this year? There won't be any changes, will there?"

---

老苦解释道："村长就是疑心重，我家苦荞不是说了吗？东家是来我家住段时日，应该不是为收租而来。"

Lao Ku explained, "Village head, you're just too suspicious. Didn't our Kuqiao say? The landlady is here to stay at our house for a while. She probably isn't here to collect rent."

---

"不管了，我进去跟东家说两句就走。"

"Never mind. I'll go in and say a few words to the landlady, then leave."

---

村长往屋里走去，老苦与苦蕨也都跟上。

The village head walked toward the house. Lao Ku and Kujue followed.

---

竹屋内一切家具均用竹子打制而成，虽不典雅倒也古朴。千里寻坐在低矮的竹椅上，身边的桌子上放有用竹叶泡好的茶水，正冒着热气。

Every piece of furniture inside the bamboo house was crafted from bamboo. Not elegant, but rustic and sturdy. Qianli Xun sat on a low bamboo chair. Beside her, on the table, sat a cup of bamboo-leaf tea, steam curling upward.

---

村长进来客套道："东家先住老苦家，有什么需要让老苦来我找。"

The village head came in and said politely, "The landlady will stay at Lao Ku's house for now. If you need anything, have Lao Ku send word."

---

千里寻客气道："感谢村长照顾，您去忙吧，一旦有任何需要，定会找您。"

Qianli Xun said politely, "Thank you for looking after me, village head. Please go about your business. If I need anything, I'll certainly send for you."

---

"东家忙，村里还有事需要我去处理，就不在此打扰。"村长说完辞别而去。

"The landlady must be busy. There are still matters in the village that need my attention. I won't disturb you further." With that, the village head took his leave.

---

苦荞向小姐一一介绍家人后，千里寻让她把银镯子和特色糕点拿出来，苦家人无不高兴。

After Kuqiao introduced her family one by one, Qianli Xun had her bring out the silver bracelet and the specialty pastries. The whole Ku family beamed with delight.

---

老苦问："不知东家要在这里住多久？"

Lao Ku asked, "How long will the landlady be staying with us?"

---

千里寻回道："一个月。"

Qianli Xun replied, "One month."

---

老苦又说："东家是贵人，总不能跟我们一样住在破竹屋里。正好，去年刚给苦蕨建了新竹屋，还没人住过，屋子靠近东面的竹林，景色也好，东家若不嫌弃，暂时住那里。"

Lao Ku said, "The landlady is a person of status. We can't have you living in a run-down hut like ours. As it happens, we built a new bamboo house for Kujue last year. Nobody's lived in it yet. It's near the eastern bamboo grove with a lovely view. If the landlady doesn't mind, you're welcome to stay there."

---

此屋正合千里寻心意，她无不同意。

The house suited Qianli Xun perfectly. She agreed without hesitation.

---

苦荞问："爹，娘，苦蕨该不会要成亲？怎么这么早给他建竹屋？"

Kuqiao asked, "Father, Mother, is Kujue getting married? Why build him a house so early?"

---

老苦回道："是该了，婚事已定下，新媳妇明年过门。"

Lao Ku replied, "It's about time. The marriage is set. The new bride arrives next year."

---

听到谈论自己的婚事，苦蕨坐在一旁的凳子上一直红着脸。

Hearing his impending marriage being discussed openly, Kujue sat on a stool in the corner, his face burning bright red.

---

苦荞娘说："东家，苦荞在您身边，给您添麻烦了，她以后的婚事，还得东家您多操心。"

Kuqiao's mother said, "Madam, Kuqiao has been at your side and caused you no small trouble. Her future marriage—I'll be counting on you to look out for her."

---

苦荞插嘴道："娘，您说什么呢？我不嫁人，永远陪着小姐。"

Kuqiao interrupted, "Mother, what are you saying? I'm not marrying anyone. I'm staying with the miss forever."

---

千里寻笑道："伯母放心，我定会帮她找户好人家。"

Qianli Xun smiled. "Don't worry, Auntie. I'll make sure she finds a good family."

---

大家又说一会儿话，苦荞才去帮娘蒸竹筒饭，又把去年晒的竹笋干拿出来用水泡开，炒了一道竹笋菜。因东家来，苦蕨到村后的河里捞来一条鱼，烧了鱼汤，一家人围坐一桌，款待东家。

Then the real work began. Kuqiao helped her mother steam rice in bamboo tubes. Last year's dried shoots came out, soaked, sliced, tossed in the wok. Because the landlady had come, Kujue disappeared to the river behind the village and came back with a wriggling fish. Fish soup bubbled over the fire. The whole family crowded around the table to feast the landlady.

---

苦家的饭菜比起林府差得很远，胜在有股自然清香，千里寻认为口感倒也特别。

The Ku family's food was a world away from the Lin residence's elaborate dishes, but it carried a natural fragrance all its own. Qianli Xun found the taste surprisingly delightful.

---

吃过饭后，在苦家人的帮助下，把东西搬到新的竹屋。家人走后，苦荞开始收拾，按照小姐的喜好，一件一件安排。

After the meal, the Ku family helped carry everything to the new bamboo house. Once they were gone, Kuqiao set to work arranging things one by one, just the way her miss liked them.

---

新的竹屋里家具还未补全，好在有张用竹子打制的矮桌，苦荞将桌子对着门口放好，把笔墨纸砚摆放整齐，小姐坐在这里写字时，刚好能看到外面的竹林，实在惬意。目测桌子的高矮，只能席地而坐，苦荞从家里拿来一个方形的棉花垫子放在桌旁，小姐坐上刚刚好。

	
The new hut's furniture wasn't complete yet, but fortunately there was a low bamboo table. Kuqiao set it facing the doorway and arranged the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone in a neat row. Sitting here to write, the miss could look straight out at the bamboo forest. Truly pleasant.

Judging by the table's height, one could only sit on the floor. Kuqiao fetched a square cotton cushion from home and placed it beside the table. The miss sat down—just right.
---

还有一个低矮的竹柜，可以当茶桌使用。苦荞找到一块印花的粗布铺上，把制茶的工具一一摆好。

There was also a low bamboo cabinet, perfect as a tea table. Kuqiao found a piece of printed coarse cloth, spread it out, and arranged the tea-making tools one by one.

---

卧室放着一张新打制的竹床，苦荞把家里为苦蕨新做的被褥铺好，再把小姐平日穿的衣裳挂在竹架上。

In the bedroom sat a newly built bamboo bed. Kuqiao laid out the fresh bedding made for Kujue, then hung the miss's everyday clothes on the bamboo rack.

---

一切收拾完毕后，千里寻十分满意，她瞧了瞧挂在竹架上的衣裳，再看看自己身上所穿，太过精美，显得与村民有距离感。

Everything settled, Qianli Xun was thoroughly pleased. She glanced at the clothes hanging on the bamboo rack, then down at what she wore—far too fine, far too grand. They'd set her apart from the villagers.

---

"苦荞，给我找几身你的衣裳。"

"Kuqiao, find me a few sets of your clothes."

---

"小姐为何要我的衣裳？"

"Why do you want my clothes, miss?"

---

"我这些衣裳在村里穿着显得跟大家格格不入，还是穿你的好。"

"These clothes of mine are far too flashy and will just make me stick out like a sore thumb here. I'd rather wear yours."

---

"既如此，委屈小姐，正好我多带了两身，小姐先试试。"

"I'm sorry, miss. I happen to have brought two extra sets. Try one on first."

---

苦荞取来一身，给小姐换上。小姐穿上普通衣裳，依然难掩骨子里透出的高雅气质。

Kuqiao fetched a set and helped the miss change. Even in ordinary clothes, she still looked like a lady who'd wandered into the wrong painting. Bone-deep elegance. Nothing to be done about it.

---

千里寻又把头上的首饰去掉，让苦荞收起来。

Qianli Xun also removed the jewelry from her head and had Kuqiao put it away.

---

她来到竹屋门口，迎面是竹林，一股清风袭来，竹香沁人心脾。千里寻觉得在这么美的地方待着，一个月后，定会对她的命运产生助益。

She came to the door of the bamboo house. Facing her was the bamboo forest. Wind moved through the grove. Ten thousand leaves whispered at once. The scent was green and sharp and clean—nothing like the Lin residence, nothing like incense and silk and suffocating politeness. She breathed in. Held it. Let go. *One month. That's all I need.*
