## 三、重复用词排查

### A. "the owner"（东家，出现 12 次）

| 修改前 | 修改后 |
|--------|--------|
| 村长每次都说 *the owner* | 保留 4–5 次（体现村长的恭敬/正式） |
| 叙述中 *the owner* | → *she / Qianli Xun / the young lady* |
| *"The owner has come here"* | *"You've come, Madam"*（村长面对面说话，用 you） |
| *"The owner is busy"* | *"You must be busy"*（同上） |
| *"The owner is a noble person"* | *"You're a person of status"*（同上） |

**原则：** 村长对千里寻说话时，英文应用 *you / Madam*，而非第三人称 *the owner*（英文中面对面称对方为 "the owner" 极不自然，像翻译腔）。仅在村长对**第三人**提及她时用 *the owner / the landlord*。

## 四、表达正确性与地道性

### 1. 春金巧的"想死娘了"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *The woman said excitedly, "Qiao, you're finally back. I missed you to death."* |
| **修改后** | *The woman's face lit up. "Qiao! You're BACK!" She rushed down the bamboo steps and grabbed her daughter's hands. "I've missed you something awful."* |
| **理由** | ① 原文"想死娘了"中"娘"是母亲自称（"想死你娘了"= 你娘想死你了），不是"我想你，妈妈"。现译"I missed you to death"语义正确但缺乏母亲的急切感；② 加入动作（rushed down, grabbed hands）展示而非告诉；③ "something awful" 比 "to death" 更有乡土母亲的口语感 |

---

### 2. "东家"的翻译策略

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | 统一译为 *the owner* |
| **修改后** | 分场景处理（见下表） |

| 场景 | 修改前 | 修改后 | 理由 |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| 村长对千里寻说话 | *"The owner has come here"* | *"Madam, you've honored us"* | 英文面对面不说 "the owner" |
| 村长对村民说话 | *"The owner is here to stay"* | *"The landlord's here for a while"* | 第三人称可用，但 "landlord" 比 "owner" 更贴合"收租的东家" |
| 苦荞介绍 | *"She's the owner of the bamboo forest"* | *"She owns this entire bamboo forest. Every stalk."* | 更有冲击力，强调"整片都是她的" |
| 村民喊 | *"Owner! Can you collect less rent?"* | *"Madam! Any chance of lower rent this year?"* | 村民不会喊"东家！"，英文中喊 "Owner!" 像喊宠物 |

### 3. "有辱门风"

| | 内容 |

## 五、句子过长

### 1. 村景描写（列表式）

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *It was rare for a carriage to come in all year round, so the villagers were very curious. Some poked their heads out from the bamboo houses; some stopped their work; some who were about to leave the village turned back to watch the fun; a group of children who were playing were also attracted to come over.* |
| **修改后** | *A carriage. HERE. The village hadn't seen one in a year, maybe two. <br><br> Heads popped from doorways. Hands stilled over weaving. A man halfway out the gate turned on his heel and came BACK. And the children—abandoning whatever game they'd been playing—swarmed.* |
| **理由** | 原文用"有的...有的...有的..."排比，英文用碎片句 + 短句模拟同样的"镜头切换"节奏。"A carriage. HERE." 单独成句制造震惊感 |

## 六、标点与网文格式

| 问题 | 修改前 | 修改后 |
|------|--------|--------|
| 千里寻内心用引号 | *she thought, so this is what Lin Jue likes* | *So THAT'S his type.*（斜体） |
| 感叹号不足（村民好奇段） | *so the villagers were very curious* | *A carriage. HERE.*（碎片句 + 句号制造震惊） |
| 春金巧的"吓死我了" | *"You scared me to death."* | *"You nearly gave me a HEART attack!"*（更口语、更夸张） |
| 大哥的"这叫什么事儿" | *"What is this?"* | *"What in the—"* 或 *"Unbelievable."*（"What is this" 过于平淡） |

## 七、其他细节

| 修改前 | 修改后 | 理由 |
|--------|--------|------|
| *Kubiao arranged the carriage, called three or four maids* | *Kuqiao had the carriage ready and roped in three or four maids* | "arranged" 偏正式；"roped in" 更有丫鬟指挥人干活的画面 |
| *There were no such things in the countryside* | *The countryside had no such luxuries* | "no such things" 偏口语/ childish；"luxuries" 更精准 |
| *she couldn't help but look at the ground peach blossoms a few more times* | *her eyes kept drifting to the ground peach blossoms lining the path* | "couldn't help but look...a few more times" 是中文"不由自主多看几眼"的直译 |
| *"Oh my! The young mistress really leaves as soon as she says she will."* | *"Well, well! The young mistress says 'go' and GOES. No warning, no farewell."* | 春金巧的语气应更酸、更带刺 |
| *Chun Jinqiao didn't greet them and directly got on the carriage* | *Chun Jinqiao didn't so much as glance at them. She climbed into her carriage and was gone.* | "directly got on" 偏平；"didn't so much as glance" 更有态度 |
| *Qianli Xun didn't avoid the question. Why should she?* | *Qianli Xun saw no reason to dissemble. They'd blocked her divorce. She owed them nothing.* | "didn't avoid the question" 是叙述者总结；修改后用千里寻的逻辑（他们挡了我和离→我不欠他们）更有角色感 |
| *the miss couldn't hide the elegant temperament that emanated from her bones* | *she still looked like a lady who'd wandered into the wrong painting. Bone-deep elegance. Nothing to be done about it.* | "emanated from her bones" 是"骨子里透出"的直译 |
| *the bamboo fragrance was refreshing* | *the scent hit her—green, clean, alive* | "refreshing" 太泛；用具体感官词 |

## 八、吸引力评估

**做得好的：**
- 春金巧清晨相遇的喜剧节奏好 ✓
- "东家"身份揭示的惊喜感保留 ✓
- 苦荞娘的乡土热情有温度 ✓
- 竹林村庄的世界观构建有新鲜感 ✓
- 千里寻"谁让他们阻止和离"的态度一致 ✓

**可加强：**
- **千里寻发现自己是"竹林东家"是本章最大爽点**，但翻译处理太平：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *"The owner of the bamboo forest?" Qianli Xun was surprised. How many things was this little girl Kubiao hiding from her?* |
| **修改后** | *"I... OWN this?" <br><br> She stared at Kuqiao. The girl had the decency to look sheepish. <br><br> *How many other secrets is she sitting on? Does she own the MOON too?*（斜体）* |
| **理由** | 加入千里寻的现代吐槽（"Does she own the MOON too?"）和情绪反应（stared），让"身份揭示"有网文的"爽感"节奏 |

---

- **结尾竹林感官体验**是本章情绪落点，现译偏平：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *A cool breeze blew, and the bamboo fragrance was refreshing. Qianli Xun felt that staying in such a beautiful place for a month would definitely help her destiny.* |
| **修改后** | *Wind moved through the grove. Ten thousand leaves whispered at once. The scent was green and sharp and clean—nothing like the Lin residence, nothing like incense and silk and suffocating politeness. <br><br> She breathed in. Held it. Let go. <br><br> *One month. That's all I need.*（斜体）* |
| **理由** | 用听觉（whispered）、嗅觉（green and sharp）、对比（nothing like the Lin residence）建立"自由感"。最后用碎片句 + 内心独白收束，比 "would definitely help her destiny" 更有余韵 |

## 九、总结

| 维度 | 评分 | 核心问题 |
|------|------|----------|
| 表达正确性 | ★★★☆☆ | "东家"翻译策略不当；多处中文直译残留 |
| 地道性 | ★★★☆☆ | 村长对话全用第三人称 "the owner" 极不自然 |
| 句长控制 | ★★★★☆ | 大部分段落合理；3 处需拆分 |
| 标点/网文节奏 | ★★★☆☆ | 内心独白仍用引号；感叹号/碎片句不足 |
| 用词重复 | ★★☆☆☆ | **Kubiao ×22（应为 Kuqiao）**；owner ×12；bamboo house ×9；Miss ×14 |
| 读者吸引力 | ★★★★☆ | 春金巧段和村庄段有趣；"东家"揭示和结尾可加强 |
| 术语一致性 | ★☆☆☆☆ | **Kubiao 错误第四次出现，系统性问题** |

**本章修改优先级：**
1. 🔴🔴 **Kubiao → Kuqiao 全书替换**（第四次指出，必须执行）
2. 🔴 "the owner" 对话中改为 *you / Madam*；叙述中改为 *the landlord / she*
3. 🟡 内心独白改斜体（3 处）
4. 🟡 村景描写 + 做饭段 + 新竹屋段拆分并加入感官细节
5. 🟢 "东家"身份揭示段加强爽感；结尾竹林段加强感官层次
