## Chapter 302: Jinmo Sells Herself to Black-Fiend Tiger

While Master Jiang busily moved into his new waterfront residence, Chun Jinmo asked to stay at her country aunt's house until moving wrapped up—slipping out a day later by hired cart straight to the gates of **Black Wind Stronghold** on Mount Tianmu.

Sending her terrified maid to hide behind a boulder, Jinmo sauntered into the bandit hall, wrapped burly, vicious Black-Fiend Tiger around her little finger with courtesan charm—talking him into letting her return home rather than sparking a war with Master Jiang—and traded an afternoon in his bed for Youqi Jushi's head.

Returning to Youlin Retreat the next day, Jinmo briefed Mengrong: two nights from now, Black-Fiend Tiger would raid wealthy Chen Manor for loot, knock everyone out with sleeping smoke so Shuzhen's fists wouldn't save her, and finish her cleanly—he only needed a floor plan of Chen Manor dropped off with the innkeeper at Wanyou Inn.

Dispatching Matron Zou straight back to Chen Haodong's private booth at Yongtai Teahouse—where Haodong confessed the Shao Escort Agency had turned him down—she obtained the full Chen Manor blueprint within half an hour (with Moxiang Courtyard neatly circled in ink, Haodong gullibly trusting nobody else would be harmed) and delivered it to Wanyou Inn.

Meanwhile, Shao Zhengye and Shao Zhize brought two jars of fine wine to the Judicial Office. Drinking heartily from big bowls as true friends, Shen Jianchen finally asked about Peng Jingxin's twenty-year-old rape case.

After confirming the prince asked as a friend rather than magistrate, Shao Zhengye poured out the hidden family tragedy: twenty years ago, his seventeen-year-old sister Shao Yueting had been seduced at West Lake by a visiting Jin nobleman traveling incognito—**Wanyan Wuqimai** (the very prince about to ascend the Jin throne this year after Wanyan Aguda's death, future architect of the Jingkang catastrophe!). Abandoned pregnant, cornered by furious clan elders assuming rape, Yueting had blindly pointed at loyal escort guard Peng Jingxin—who secretly loved her so deeply he confessed silently to shield her honor!
