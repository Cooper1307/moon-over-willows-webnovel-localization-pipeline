## Chapter 304: Abducted to Mount Tianmu in the Dead of Night

Black-Fiend Tiger’s masked bandits swarmed through Chen Manor ransacking chests of silver and jewels while the drugged household snored like logs.

Guided straight to Moxiang Courtyard, Black-Fiend Tiger and his crew flicked every door latch open with knives, bagged every valuable, and stepped into the inner bedchamber. Drawing his heavy saber, he stepped up to the bed where Qianli Xun lay fast asleep under the smoke.

Just as his blade rose to strike, she rolled face-up. His arm froze midair. “What a stunner! Criminal to kill her—better haul her up Mount Tianmu as my bandit queen!”

Sheathing his blade, he rolled her tight inside her quilt, slung her over his shoulder, and marched out to waiting carriages and horses outside the gate. “Move out before the night patrol shows!”

By sheer chance, Lin Jue—who had been taking breaks from Lazybones Village—had volunteered to cover a subordinate’s night street patrol for old times’ sake. Riding near Chen Manor, he caught the rumble of heavy hooves and wheels, spotted the manor’s south gate hanging wide open, left two guards at the gate, and galloped in pursuit—cutting the convoy off near the west city gate.

“Halt! Drop the masks and open those carriages!”

“Cut ’em down!” Black-Fiend Tiger roared.

Steel clashed in the dark streets. Clashing blades with Black-Fiend Tiger, Lin Jue felt his wrist go numb against brute power—realizing he was outmatched, he yelled while parrying, “Who are you?! Just hit Chen Manor?!”

“Mount Tianmu!” Black-Fiend Tiger knocked Lin Jue clean out of the saddle as his bandits routed the patrol and thundered out the west gate.

“Black-Fiend Tiger!” Scrapping up into his saddle, Lin Jue dashed back to Chen Manor—seeing looted rooms yet everyone still breathing, assuming Zhu Shuzhen lay safe inside too—and galloped straight for Youlin Retreat to wake Prince Yi.

Roused by Butler Li announcing Lin Jue outside—and Qiao Mengrong snapping wide awake at the words “Chen Manor,” eager to hear Zhu Shuzhen lay dead—Shen Jianchen leaped out of bed the instant Lin Jue shouted Black-Fiend Tiger had raided Chen Manor and knocked everyone out.

Dressing in seconds, Shen Jianchen promised a deceptively sweet Mengrong—“Tell me first thing how Shuzhen is!”—and rode hard to Chen Manor with Lin Jue.

Fetching an apothecary who recognized knockout smoke and supplied counter-incense, Shen Jianchen had servants woken first to rouse the masters, sitting tense in the main hall beside silent Lin Jue—two men worrying over the same woman.

Soon Chen Jianglin, his brothers, Chen Haodong, and Chen Haofei hurried in to bow in thanks, relieved the bandits seemed after loot alone. Chen Haodong squirmed with quiet guilt over handing out the map, praying his sister-in-law lay dead so his sacrifice paid off.

“Where’s Second Young Master?” Shen Jianchen asked sharp.

Right then Chen Haoyu burst through the doors wild-eyed. “Father! My wife’s gone! Combed all of Moxiang Courtyard—her daytime clothes are still there, and her quilt’s missing!”

Before Chen Jianglin could speak, Shen Jianchen was already on his feet. “Commander Lin, I’m riding for Mount Tianmu now—fetch Chief Constable Wen and reinforcements right behind me!”

“Wait for me, Your Highness—I’m coming too!” Chen Haoyu sprinted straight after him. By first light they reached the foot of Mount Tianmu, left their horses, and pushed up the trail.

Up at Black Wind Stronghold, flush with enough silver to party a year and thrilled with his gorgeous trophy, Black-Fiend Tiger laid the quilt-wrapped beauty on his couch and waved revival incense under her nose—dismissing his henchman to enjoy her wide awake.

Waking to a rough voice calling “Beauty, wake up,” Qianli Xun blinked open to a dark, burly stranger in an unfamiliar room and shoved his face away in shock. *Not again—did I transmigrate twice?!* “Who am I?!”

“Asking me who you are?” he laughed. “I’m Black-Fiend Tiger—you’re up on Mount Tianmu! Snatched you straight out of Chen Manor. Be my bandit queen and I’ll treat you right!”

*Thank goodness—just kidnapped, not transmigrated!* Checking her modest sleepwear and her own quilt, she realized he’d knocked her out with smoke. “Black-whatever Tiger, never met you—why snatch me?”

“Black-Fiend Tiger! Someone paid me to kill you, only you’re too gorgeous to waste, so I brought you up here instead!”

Mengrong again—a cold ache brushed her heart before Black-Fiend Tiger reached for her and ate a solid fist right to the cheekbone, tumbling clean off the couch!

“Touch me and lose your teeth!”

Spitting blood and grinning wickedly, he scrambled up. “Got fire too—never broken a wildcat yet!”

Fists flew as Qianli Xun vaulted off the couch, weaving around his wild swings and battering him back bloodied until a swarm of bandits piled in. Fighting barefoot through the night till dawn, she dropped bandit after bandit—until one sneaky thug tossed a jagged flint under her descending foot.

Piercing agony shot through her sole, blood gushing; in that split second of distraction, thugs piled on and lashed her to a wooden chair.

Gritting through the throbbing cut, she snapped, “Let me go before the authorities skin you alive!”

Wiping blood off his lip, Black-Fiend Tiger laughed. “Already whipped the city patrol on our way out—the yamen’s a joke!”

“Who paid you to kill me?”

Keeping Chun Jinmo’s name locked tight, he sneered, “Who cares? Paid to kill you, gave you extra hours for your pretty face, and since you’re begging to die—hand me my saber!”

Catching a heavy blade tossed by a henchman, Black-Fiend Tiger stepped straight toward her.
