## Chapter 304: Abducted to Mount Tianmu in the Dead of Night

Black-Fiend Tiger's raiders ransacked Chen Manor undisturbed while everyone slept like logs. Marching straight into Moxiang Courtyard using Haodong's blueprint, Black-Fiend Tiger cleared the side rooms of loot before stepping into Suxin Thatched Hall's bedchamber with broadsword drawn.

Just as he lifted the blade over the bed, sleeping Qianli Xun rolled face-up. Stunned by her beauty—"Way too gorgeous to kill!"—he sheathed his sword, rolled her neatly inside her silk quilt, hoisted her onto his shoulder, packed her into a waiting carriage alongside the loot, and raced toward the west gate.

By sheer luck, Lin Jue—taking a nostalgic night patrol for a subordinate—heard the midnight convoy, saw Chen Manor's south gate standing wide open, left two men to guard it, and rode hard to intercept the raiders near the west gate! Outmatched by Black-Fiend Tiger's brute blade, Lin Jue still forced three words out of him—*"Mount Tianmu!"*—before getting knocked off his horse as the bandits escaped out the west gate.

Checking Chen Manor's ransacked yet unharmed halls, Lin Jue galloped straight to Youlin Retreat to wake Prince Yi. Hearing Black-Fiend Tiger had hit the Chens, Shen Jianchen sprang out of bed instantly—leaving Mengrong masking jealous venom behind sweet concern (*"Tell me first thing how Shuzhen is!"*)—and raced to Chen Manor with Lin Jue.

Summoning a physician with antidote incense, they woke the servants and masters. Chen Jianglin and his brothers bowed in thanks—while Haodong quietly sweated bullets regretting handing over the blueprint!

Then Chen Haoyu burst in pale with panic: *"Father, my wife's gone! Her day clothes are still there, only her quilt's missing!"*

Not wasting half a breath, Shen Jianchen snapped orders for Lin Jue to fetch Wen Xin and troops as backup, bolted straight for Mount Tianmu through the night—with Haoyu charging doggedly right on his heels—reaching the mountain foot right at dawn!

Meanwhile, up in Black Wind Stronghold, Black-Fiend Tiger burned revival incense under Qianli Xun's nose to enjoy waking his prize. Blinking awake at a burly stranger calling her "beauty" in a strange room, Qianli Xun first wondered if she'd transmigrated *again*—before snapping wide awake at *"Mount Tianmu!"*

Realizing Mengrong had hired this thug to murder her—and that her sleeping robes were still intact—she met his grabby lunge with a crushing right hook that sent him flying off the couch! Brawling bare-handed against him and a stream of rushing bandits all the way till dawn, she dropped attacker after attacker until one dirty bandit tossed a jagged stone under her bare foot!

Pierced deep across her sole, blood gushing, she stumbled for one split second—and the mob piled on, binding her to a chair.

Spitting blood, Black-Fiend Tiger lost patience with wooing a tigress, called for a broadsword, and stepped close to finish his contract.
