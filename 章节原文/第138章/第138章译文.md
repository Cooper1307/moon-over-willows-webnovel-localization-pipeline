## Chapter 138: Comforting Two Broken Hearts by Night

After dinner, reluctant to sleep on a full stomach, the two sat around the glowing brazier sipping the tea Xiaguo had whisked, dismissing all maids for private talk.

When Xilan worried who would wait on Princess later, Qianli Xun waved her off: "Go rest—I'll wait on Mengrong myself."

"I've got two hands and two feet—who needs waiting on?" Qiao Mengrong laughed, turning to Xilan: "Go sleep, come back in the morning."

With Princess's order laid down, Xilan trailed Xiaguo and Kuqiao out and shut the door behind them.

Studying Shuzhen's face, Qiao Mengrong saw the redness around her eyes had faded over dinner, though her breezy tone still sounded forced.

"If it hurts, Shuzhen, cry it all out right in front of me—then stop thinking about Young Master Qiao and live your own life."

"Want me bawling a second time? Already cried my tank dry—not another single tear for this mess."

"Of course I don't want you crying again!" Lifting her tea bowl for a sip, Qiao Mengrong praised, "Great tea—your maid whisks rings around me."

Xiaguo had made that tea—bringing back the image of her kneeling in the snow begging to go comfort Wenyuan.

When had that girl fallen for Wenyuan right under her nose without Qianli Xun having a clue? Was Xiaguo that good at hiding, or was she herself that blind?

Either way, Qianli Xun felt an odd twinge: a maid could chase Wenyuan with zero chains, while she stood hemmed in by a hundred walls.

Loving someone needed zero reason; not being able to love someone brought ten thousand reasons—and any single one built an iron wall between them.

"Mengrong, would you ever agree to be Prince Yi's concubine?"

Caught off guard, Qiao Mengrong froze—she had never once imagined such a thing.

Seeing her blank stare, Qianli Xun gave a crooked smile: "Blessed by an imperial marriage decree, how could you fathom commoners' woes?"

"Did you ever think about becoming Young Master Qiao's concubine?"

Qianli Xun shook her head: "Never once. Wenyuan tested me today, and I turned him down flat."

"Good for you! After the way the Qiaos treated you, stooping to concubine would be letting them off way too easy!"

Warmed by her fierce loyalty, Qianli Xun teased, "Your surname is Qiao too—why aren't you siding with the Qiaos?"

"Different Qiao—I have zero blood relation to their Qiao family! I only side with what's right. Sigh... you and Young Master Qiao were a match made in heaven; tearing you apart is such a crime."

Lowering her eyes to the glowing embers in the brazier, Qianli Xun spoke the line she hated saying most.

"Wenyuan and I were fated to meet, never meant to stay. Reaching this end was bound to happen. Shame human eyes can't see the future—know ahead of time and you'd spare yourself walking down a dead end."

Qiao Mengrong saw that beneath the pain, Shuzhen had made peace with letting go—no need for empty lectures, just a loyal pair of ears.

"Do you regret ever loving Young Master Qiao?"

Qianli Xun shook her head: "Not a bit. Wenyuan was my first love—I'll keep that memory tucked warm in my heart forever."

Qiao Mengrong grinned: "First love? Heard you had a thing for some Young Master Xu before marrying Lin Jue!"

"Amnesia wiped him clean—to the post-amnesia me, Wenyuan was the first man I ever loved. First love shines bright because it's the first: brief as a night-blooming epiphyllum, dazzling as fireworks—gone in a flash, leaving the glow behind in memory."

"Funny how both you and Brother went through amnesia."

Mengrong's remark stirred that old suspicion in Qianli Xun all over again about some tie between Prince Yi and Shen Jianchen.

A second later, she chalked it up to missing Shen Jianchen so much her thoughts were running wild.

"Hardly the same! He came back from the dead; I got beaten into amnesia by Lin Jue."

Expecting Mengrong to chime right back, Qianli Xun met silence instead.

Shuzhen's talk of first love had plunged Qiao Mengrong into her own thoughts.

Prince Yi was her first love and the man she was going to wed—luckier than Shuzhen by far.

Yet Shuzhen had hit a raw nerve too: ever since coming back from death with his memory wiped, her own first love felt just like a vanishing night flower.

"What are you brooding over, Mengrong?" Guessing she was thinking of Prince Yi, Qianli Xun sighed inside: how would sweet Mengrong survive married to a playboy prince?

Still, she had to tip her hat to Prince Yi's game: endless patience, thoughtful gestures, silky lines, asking nothing back—she was tempted to hang a sign around his neck reading "Northern Song's Number-One Heartbreaker"!

"Oh, just wondering what Brother and Young Master Qiao are talking about right now."

Hating to watch her best friend get played, Qianli Xun poked a hole in the lie: "Actually, Mengrong, after Wenyuan and I broke up today, Prince Yi escorted me all the way home—he wasn't with Wenyuan..."

Qiao Mengrong did not even blink: "I know! Brother told me already—said he worried you might do something reckless, so for my sake he looked after you on Young Master Qiao's behalf. Then right after dropping you off, he bumped into Young Master Qiao at the city gate and comforted him too! Brother really knocks himself out for you two."

Qianli Xun's eyes bulged and her jaw dropped—Prince Yi had covered every track in advance, playing Mengrong like a fiddle!

"Prince Yi escorting another woman—doesn't that bother you?!"

"What 'other woman'? You're my best friend! Brother looking after you puts my mind at ease."

Torn between warmth and heartache for Mengrong, Qianli Xun's own grief slipped right into the background—already cried clean out behind closed doors earlier.

"Mengrong, Prince Yi's handsome, rich, and royal—stop letting him escort other girls alone, or some girl might fall for him!"

"Did you fall for Brother?"

"Are you kidding?! Not in a million years!"

"See? That's why I'm happy to let Brother help you!"

Exasperated, Qianli Xun blurted, "Are you naive?! Aren't you afraid Prince Yi might fall for me?!"

"Asking that straight out proves there's nothing shady between you two—besides, you're my best friend, I trust you."

Poor Mengrong—zero clue about the modern warning to guard against fire, thieves, and best friends!

"Of course you can trust me. Still, whatever excuse Prince Yi cooks up to look after me next time, say no—stay sharp! Besides, I don't need his help."

"You're so sweet looking out for me, Shuzhen—I'll listen! Sure you don't need Brother's help, though? You've asked him for favors plenty of times."

"For your sake, I won't bother him ever again."

Drowsiness rolled over them; Qianli Xun pulled Mengrong into bed, meaning to chat a bit more, yet the second their heads hit the pillows, both dropped fast asleep.

Meanwhile, after leaving the Divine Willow, Qiao Wenxian had skipped Youlin Retreat—with Shuzhen gone, who cared where he slept?

A hollow fog settled over him, making him wonder why he even walked this earth. Unable to choose his own bride, what kind of man was he? Death crossed his mind, only for duty to his family to chain him back down.

How was he supposed to drag through the years ahead—just breathing like an empty shell?

Back at Qiao Manor, he shut himself in his room, replaying every sweet hour with Shuzhen—then her iron stance in the snow ending it all, twisting the knife deeper every second.

He hated himself for even hinting at making her a concubine—an insult to her pride and to their love.

At dusk Prince Yi arrived bearing wine and dishes from Juxiang Tower. Setting out the feast, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang slipped off to another room to talk.

Taking a seat first, Shen Jianchen called out, "Brought Capital Spring wine—come have a few cups with me and drown your sorrows!"

Lying dull-eyed on his bed lost in misery, Qiao Wenxian dragged himself up at mention of wine, shuffled into the outer room, dropped into a chair, filled his own cup, and downed it in one gulp.

As he reached to pour a second, Prince Yi caught the pot: "Pace yourself! I came to help you let off steam—pass out cold and how do we talk?"

"What's talking fix?! Won't undo the betrothal or bring Shuzhen back!" Pouring another cup and tossing it back, he slammed the cup down and broke into sobs: "Waaah!"

"A real man doesn't shed easy tears—why start bawling?"

Choking through tears: "Can't even choose my own wife—what kind of man am I?! Why shouldn't I cry?! Waaah!" Pouring another cup, he drained it whole.

"You and Youqi Jushi are over—face it. Instead of wallowing, climb out and shoulder the duties waiting for you."

Checking his sobs, Qiao Wenxian spat bitter: "Duties?! Hmph—if not for family duty, I'd be dead already!"

"Forget Youqi Jushi. You two were never meant to be."

"Forget? How?! I've loved her forever—how do you erase that overnight?!"

"Only by letting go can you start a new life."

"New life? Hmph! Couldn't care less—without Shuzhen, every woman's the same!"

Shen Jianchen knew that ache down to his marrow—losing Qianli Xun felt no different. Filling both cups, he raised his: "Come on, drink with me—two poor fools in the same boat."

Lifting his cup, confusion pierced Qiao Wenxian's misery: "With a beauty right beside you and an imperial betrothal everyone envies, how are you a poor fool?"

Unable to tell Wenyuan the truth about Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen clinked his cup: "Here's to the women we love and can never have."

Draining his cup, Shen Jianchen looked up to find Wenyuan holding an empty cup and staring at him in puzzlement.

"Why stare at me like that?"

Setting down his cup as wine warmed his cheeks, Qiao Wenxian slurred, "Can't have? Princess Jiahe is right in your palm—who can't you have? Don't tell me Prince Yi has another woman in his heart?"

"Another woman? Wish I could swap—can't do it. My heart's stuck on her alone."

"Talking in riddles, Prince Yi—makes no sense."

Too drowned in his own pain to puzzle over Prince Yi, Qiao Wenxian kept pouring cup after cup without touching a morsel of food until drunkenness rolled in and his wishes spilled loose:

"Know what, Prince Yi?... Shuzhen turning me down... was all fake... She was just... too mad... talking out of anger..."

Even knowing it was drunken rambling, unease pricked Shen Jianchen: "How do you know Youqi Jushi didn't mean it?"

"She told me once... wanted to marry me... give me... lots and lots of kids... even sat right on my lap... haha, falling asleep in my arms, fully dressed..." Slumping over the table, Wenyuan passed out cold.

Shen Jianchen let out a quiet sigh: "Better asleep—no hurting tonight, and tomorrow brings a fresh day."

Calling Bai Feng and Nan Shuang in, they hauled Young Master Qiao onto his bed.

With night grown deep, Prince Yi stayed overnight at Qiao Manor—delighting Qiao Shouren, who had guest quarters made up at once.
