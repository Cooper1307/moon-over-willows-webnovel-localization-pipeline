## Chapter 331: Meeting a Childhood Sweetheart at Lady Wei's Banquet

Stepping into the yard to explain how Mao's verse had landed in Song Bianliang, Shen Jianchen left Qianli Xun briefly with Matron Zou—who dropped to her knees begging forgiveness for Mengrong, nearly slipping about the marriage edict before hurrying indoors.

Reluctant to visit Shen Jianchen's chamber alone until Bai Feng revealed an invitation from **Lady Wei (Wei Wan)**—Chancellor Zeng's famously unconventional poetess wife—Qianli Xun eagerly went along the next day.

Welcoming her warmly as a kindred spirit, Lady Wei toasted her release, confessed her husband had been too timid to fight Liang Shicheng, and unleashed graceful dancers—while Shen Jianchen bristled jealously at a handsome young guest across the hall staring and raising toasts at Qianli Xun.

Invited to leave fresh verses, Qianli Xun rose and brushed all five of Zhu Shuzhen's celebrated quatrains, ***At Lady Wei's Banquet***:

*I. When pipes and strings urge her onto the brocade rug, / her slender form floats lightly as if ready to take flight. / Had Emperor Minghuang beheld her in his day, / Aman would have stood helpless—let alone Consort Yang!*

*II. Fragrant rugs cradle the crescent curve of her slippers, / gliding over ripples till cloud-shadows fade away. / Tight strings drive the beat, chasing every swift turn, / twin sleeves whirling back like drifting winter snow.*

*III. Her willow waist refuses spring's narrow bounds, / spinning like a phoenix with rosy silk cuffs flying. / Dancing through the Yizhou tune till her strength gives out, / petals rustle down in showers before the banquet mats.*

*IV. Claiming first prize for all the capital's spring, / her clear songs and subtle steps outshine the crowd. / I only dread that by dawn the guests scatter like stars, / and all turns into a wisp of cloud on Mount Wu.*

*V. Soft powdered grace poised amid flickering candlelight, / one speck of sorrow clouds her twin mountain brows. / Careless of the perfumed censer beneath her hem, / silent steps follow the curve of her tiny arched shoes.* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Applause broke out—followed by the staring guest, **Young Master Xu**, speaking up to praise her *Circle Ci* and casually noting their childhood friendship in Lin'an! Recognizing Zhu Shuzhen's faithless first fiancé—the man whose superstitious relocation had ruined the original girl's youth—Qianli Xun coolly shut him down with her amnesia until he drank himself gloomy.

At the banquet's close, Lady Wei drew Prince Yi and Qianli Xun aside with a priceless lead: in **Yanjing**, an eccentric miracle doctor named **Liu Miaoshou** had cured an identical madness case—though he only treated patients in person if someone impressed him with superior poetry!
