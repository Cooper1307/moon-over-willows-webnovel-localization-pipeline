## Chapter 208: A Clever Trap for Two Lovers' Meetings

小间内没有外面宽敞，赢在清幽雅静。门一关，听不到想绒姑娘的歌喉和琴声，还好有扇窗户，能够目睹到钱塘江的盛景。
乔文仙走进后，听到门被关起来的声音，迅速转过身，瞧向他还难以放下的人：“淑真，谢谢你愿意跟我一叙。”
小间内放有几案，千里寻并不想坐下，她走到窗前，瞧着外面的美景。
“文渊，你答应过我，不再单独与我说话，为何还要让三位公子借诗会之名约我前来？你我都已婚，往后还是不要再单独见面……”
乔文仙虽已决定是最后一次会面，可此话从淑真的口中说出，他多少被伤到。
“你放心，是最后一次，有些话你不亲口告诉我，我便难以放下你我之间的感情。尽管在捶丸场时，我已决定要放下，可哪有想得那么容易。为了放下你，我才想方设法与你相见。”
千里寻面向文渊，文渊也已面向她。她发现再次接触到文渊投来的目光时，已没有以前心动的感觉：“你想听什么话？”
乔文仙靠近淑真：“你真的爱过我？”
千里寻并未后退，冷静地盯着文渊：“在捶丸场时，我已回答过。”
乔文仙紧追不舍：“我还想听，能再说一遍吗？”
“多说无益。”千里寻垂下眼帘。
乔文仙略停片刻，大胆问出：“难道你与我在一起相处时，心里已有别人？”
千里寻不愿自己的情感被人玷污，立刻否认：“没有！要有的话我岂能坐在文渊的怀里写出‘和衣睡倒人怀’？”
提到这句词，乔文仙露出满意的笑容：“对，我还有什么好怀疑的？你的《夏日游湖》是为我而写。能够与心爱的人有过一段美好的回忆，我已心满意足……”
很快，乔文仙又没有了笑容，表情郑重，充满担忧：“听说你成亲时仪王前去抢亲，我想听听你的解释。”
申简辰不管不顾跑去抢亲，梦容也因此受伤，事情闹得很大，文渊知道再正常不过，千里寻也没想过要瞒着文渊：“如此明显之事，又何必多问？”
“什么时候的事？”乔文仙忍不住继续追问。
千里寻从文渊的眼神读出，他想知道她何时喜欢上仪王。
当然是在喜欢上文渊之前，只是那时以为再也遇不到申简辰，才会对文渊一见钟情。若将仪王是申简辰的事告诉文渊，他不仅不能理解，还会因此多费口舌加以解释。不如往后说，既合情合理，亦可令文渊心安。
“与你分手后不知不觉对仪王动起心思。”
听到淑真确切的回答，乔文仙难过的同时，不得不承认她心里已经有别人，他继续爱着还有何意义？是到了真正放下的时候。
“你就不怕伤害佳和郡主？”
“怕，正因为怕，才举步维艰，才答应嫁给陈浩宇，让自己无路可退，还梦容一个好哥哥。”千里寻说着双眼不由湿润起来，“文渊，你说，为什么在感情里让步的人总是我？”
“因为你善良，总是不由自主为别人考虑。”
“我不想善良，我想跟喜欢的人在一起。”
“你根本做不到，善良已刻在你的骨子里。我想，这也正是我和仪王喜欢你的原因。”
听得出，文渊已没有刚才难过，变得平静和理性。
千里寻眼中闪着泪光，露出轻松的笑容：“文渊，能与你像朋友一样说话，真好。”
乔文仙走到窗前，站在淑真身边，望着窗外的江水：“朱弦断，明镜缺，朝露晞，芳时歇，白头吟，伤离别，努力加餐勿念君，锦水汤汤，与尔诀。”
Smaller than the main hall, the inner chamber won on hushed stillness; the instant the door clicked shut, zither and song faded away, leaving only a wide window framing the rolling Qiantang River.

听着很熟悉，仔细想来，正是汉代才女卓文君《诀别书》中的句子，文渊不过改了一两个字而已。千里寻也转身面向江水，扭头再看文渊的表情，平静中略带微笑。
Spinning to face the woman he still could not release, Qiao Wenxian whispered, "Thank you for granting me a word, Shuzhen."

“你看上去并未埋怨我，又何必改用卓文君《诀别书》中的句子？”
Skipping the low table, Qianli Xun walked straight to the window to look out over the water. "You promised never to speak to me alone again, Wenyuan—why put your three friends up to faking a poetry salon? Both of us are married now; no more private meetings."

“从我认识你，就爱着你，算起来已经六七年的光阴，这一刻要放下，总得让我发泄发泄。”
Even braced for a final parting, her brisk tone stung him. "My word—this is the very last time. Without hearing certain truths straight from your own lips, my heart refuses to let go. Back on the chuiwan meadow I swore to move on, yet it is harder than breathing. I had to see you one last time to let you go for good."

千里寻望着窗外逝去的江水，想着与文渊经历过的感情，一切如同江水，一去而不复返。
Turning to face him fully, Qianli Xun realized with quiet clarity that his gaze no longer sped up her pulse by a single beat. "What do you need to hear?"

乔文仙以更加平静的口吻说：“我又想起两句，最能表达我此刻的心境。”
Stepping closer, Qiao Wenxian searched her face. "Did you truly love me?"

“哪两句？”
Holding her ground calmly, she replied, "I already answered that on the chuiwan meadow."

乔文仙忍不住将目光从窗外的江水移向淑真：“此情若是长相守，你若无心我便休。”
"Say it one more time—please."

千里寻也不由自主看向文渊，从他的眼神中判断出，他的确愿意放下，她倍感轻松：“我也送文渊一句话：相濡以沫，不如相忘于江湖。”
"Repeating it changes nothing," she murmured, lowering her eyes.

两人相视一笑，又一起望向窗外的江水。
After a tense breath, he forced out the question burning inside: "When you were with me—was someone else already in your heart?"

“淑真，我不会与你相忘于江湖，你我还是朋友，我依然视你为知己。”
Refusing to let their honest past be dirtied, she answered sharply: "No! If someone else had been in my heart, would I have climbed into your lap and composed 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'?!"

“有你这样的朋友，是朱淑真一生的幸运。”
A soft smile of relief broke across his face. "Yes—what was I even doubting?! Your *Summer Excursion on the Lake* was written for me. Holding one bright memory with the woman I loved is enough..."

“说得好像你不是朱淑真一样？”
Then the smile faded into solemn ache. "I heard Prince Yi raided your wedding to snatch you. Explain that to me."

“我当然是。”一高兴，食欲大增，闻到一阵美食的香气，千里寻不免腹中饥饿，“晌午了，估计饭食已摆上，我们还是快些出去享用。”
No hiding a street spectacle that wounded Mengrong. "Plain as day—why even ask?"

见过淑真，算是给无法得到的感情画上圆满的句号，乔文仙也觉得没有必要与淑真单独聊下去，再说他也觉出饥饿感来。
"When did it start?" he pressed softly.

乔文仙主动上前打开门，当他与淑真走出时，所有人都看向他们。
Reading his fear—wondering if she had loved Prince Yi all along—she weighed the truth. Yes, Shen Jianchen came first, yet explaining transmigration would take hours and baffle him completely; placing it afterward was kind and spared needless wounds. "After we broke up, bit by bit, my heart turned toward Prince Yi."

瞧一眼摆在桌上的美食，乔文仙打趣道：“饭食都已摆上，你们三个也不知道叫我和淑真，难道是担心我们两个把所有美食吃光不成？”
Hearing it flat out hurt sharp as glass—yet broke the final chain holding him prisoner. She loved someone else now; clinging on was senseless. Time at last to let go.

三位公子见乔兄一脸轻松，幽栖居士也面带笑容，便知二人谈得不错。
"Aren't you scared of hurting Princess Jiahe?"

东方尘泽说：“乔兄冤枉我们，饭食刚摆上，我们三个和想绒姑娘一点儿都没动，就等着你与幽栖居士出来，既然二位已聊完正事，赶紧落座，不要辜负美食。”
"Terrified! That is why every step felt impossible—why I married Chen Haoyu to burn every bridge behind me and give Mengrong back her brother!" Tears suddenly brimmed along Qianli Xun's lashes. "Tell me, Wenyuan—why am I ALWAYS the one who has to step back in love?!"

两人落座后，大家在愉快的氛围中推杯换盏，享用美食，气氛相当和谐。
"Because you are kind—always putting everyone else before yourself."

三位公子算是看出，乔兄今日与幽栖居士聊完，已无往日阴霾，也都不由替他感到高兴。
"I do not WANT to be kind! I want to be with the man I love!"

难题接着出现，仪王快要到来，总不能将乔兄赶走，再说乔兄看上去也没有想要立刻离开的意思。
"You could never be ruthless—kindness is carved into your bones. Probably the very reason both Prince Yi and I fell for you."

还好三人有准备。
Hearing the calm, clear tone return to Wenyuan's voice brought a wet smile to her eyes. "Talking with you like real friends again feels so good, Wenyuan."

午饭过后，总管和厨师进来将桌子收拾干净，上好茶水，又退出。
Stepping beside her at the window to gaze over the rushing river, Qiao Wenxian recited quietly, adapting Zhuo Wenjun's famed *Letter of Farewell*:

东方尘泽品过一口茶：“乔兄，我们三人想去江边转转，能不能陪我们出去走走？正好饭后消消食。”
"Red lute-string snapped, bright mirror split;

乔文仙毫无戒备：“难道淑真与想绒姑娘不去？”
Morning dew dried, fragrant season spent.

曹班插嘴道：“想绒姑娘与幽栖居士一见如故，她们想聊些女子之间的话题，我们还是不要在此打扰。”
*Song of White Hair* mourns our parting—

曹班趁机向想绒使眼色。
Eat well, take care, think no more of this gentleman;

想绒放下茶盏：“没错，我的确想跟幽栖居士聊聊，就不跟几位去江边，等你们回来，我再给大家唱幽栖居士的新词。”
Where Brocade Waters surge wide, with you I part!" [TN: Translator's rendering]

乔文仙信以为真。
Turning toward the water before glancing at his serene half-smile, Qianli Xun teased softly, "You do not sound bitter—why tweak Zhuo Wenjun's *Letter of Farewell*?"

几人伪装得太好，千里寻根本未发现异常。
"Loved you from the day we met—six or seven years now. Letting go right this second calls for a little dramatic venting."

待三位公子与文渊去江边后，想绒倒一杯酒酿，举起酒杯：“幽栖居士，想绒敬你一杯。”
Watching endless water roll seaward—just like their love, never flowing back—Qiao Wenxian added even more peacefully, "Two more lines fit my heart best today."

苦荞为小姐满上，千里寻也端起酒杯。
"Which two?"

饮完酒后，千里寻问：“想绒姑娘想与我聊些什么？”
Turning his eyes gently from the river onto her face: "'Even were this love meant to last forever, once your heart is no longer in it, I will let go.'" [TN: Translator's rendering]

不过是按照三位公子的吩咐，拖延时间等待仪王到来。为拖延时间，想绒早已想好话题：“幽栖居士的事我听三位公子说起过，听后甚觉感慨。”
Seeing real release shine clear in his gaze lifted a boulder from Qianli Xun's chest. "Then take a line from me too: 'Rather than wet each other down with spit, it is better to forget each other in the rivers and lakes.'" [TN: Adapted from Burton Watson's translation of Zhuangzi]

想绒的话说得模棱两可，千里寻甚是不解：“什么事？”
Both smiled softly and looked back at the river.

“你与乔公子，还有嫁到陈家的事。”
"I will not forget you in the rivers and lakes, Shuzhen," Qiao Wenxian said warmly. "We stay friends—and I still count you my kindred spirit."

千里寻倒不忌讳与人聊起自己的情感经历：“我都不在意，想绒姑娘又何必为我介怀？”
"Having a friend like you is Zhu Shuzhen's lifelong blessing."

“不仅仅是为幽栖居士，你我都是女子，我为幽栖居士感慨的同时，也为女子感慨，更为像我这等风尘女子感慨。”想绒说着不免悲叹，“幽栖居士是大家闺秀，尚且不能与心爱之人在一起，何况我等无家可归飘零风尘之人。”
"Funny—you talk as if you weren't Zhu Shuzhen!"

“想绒姑娘可有心仪之人？”
"Of course I am!" Cheer rushing back woke a sudden rumble in her belly as rich aromas drifted through the wall. "Noon already—lunch must be on the table! Let us eat!"

“暂时还没有。我等风尘女子又能入得了哪家公子的贵眼？东方公子他们喜欢叫上我，不过是因为在花云楼里我唱幽栖居士的诗词唱得最好。”
Closing his chapter cleanly and feeling hungry himself, Qiao Wenxian swung the door wide. Every eye in the main hall snapped over as he joked, "Food laid out and you three did not call us—scared Shuzhen and I would clear every platter?!"

千里寻安慰道：“想容姑娘以一技之长容身于世，并不比临安的大家闺秀差，又岂能将自己等同于风尘女子？依我看，春香楼那些人才是真正的风尘女子，俗不可耐。你比她们不知要强多少倍。”
Seeing both emerge smiling and light, Dongfang Chenze cheered, "Untouched—we waited on you two! Dig in!"

想绒听后，更是感激不尽：“幽栖居士不仅才华出众，也是见识非凡之人，想绒再敬你一杯。”
Over crisp dishes and clinking cups, warmth filled the suite as the three friends marveled at Wenyuan's lifted shadow. Yet hurdle two ticked closer: Prince Yi would arrive any minute, and Wenyuan showed zero sign of leaving!

二人又喝下一杯酒酿。
Once lunch cleared and fresh tea arrived, Dongfang Chenze launched Phase Two: "Care to walk down by the riverbank with us three to walk off lunch, Brother Qiao?"

千里寻继续说：“大家闺秀看着光鲜艳丽，其实没你想得那么好。你看看我，婚姻不能自主，被我爹许来许去，最终还是嫁给自己不喜欢的人。你就不同，孑身一人，自由自在，哪一日真有了喜欢之人，没有父母限制，尽管跟着他天涯海角，多好！”
Unsuspecting, Qiao Wenxian blinked. "Aren't Shuzhen and Miss Xiangrong coming?"

“幽栖居士有所不知，我已卖身花云楼，要想赎身需千两黄金。纵观整个临安城，又有哪位公子愿意为我舍得千两黄金？即使有公子愿意，他的家人也未必同意。故而我虽无父母限制，却被卖身契和千两黄金牢牢套住，只有为花云楼的妈妈赚银子的份儿。”
"Those two hit it off like old sisters and want private girl-talk!" Cao Ban jumped in, throwing Xiangrong a sharp wink.

又换千里寻感慨：“只怪这个世道对女子太不公平，相信未来会有女子当家做主的一日。到那时，我们女子与男子一样，可以自己赚钱养活自己，不用再依靠男人，更可以自由恋爱，什么父母之命媒妁之言，什么花云楼、春香楼统统被破除！”
Setting her cup down smoothly, Xiangrong backed him up: "Indeed—I want a long chat with Youqi Jushi! When you gentlemen get back, I will sing her newest lyrics!"

幽栖居士的一番言论令想绒姑娘充满向往：“真希望这样的时代快些到来，也不知你我能否遇到？”
Both Wenyuan and Qianli Xun bought it hook, line, and sinker!

那可是千年以后的事，千里寻有幸经历过，自由到不想恋爱不想结婚，也无人干涉；自由到申简辰跟在屁股后面追求她，她从来没给过他好脸色。
The second the four men headed downstairs, Xiangrong poured sweet wine to stall cleanly until Prince Yi arrived—opening a real conversation: "Hearing the gentlemen talk about your story—Young Master Qiao, marrying into the Chen family—left me sighing deep."

想到申简辰，千里寻更加后悔，早知穿越而来想跟申简辰走到一起难上加难，还不如在那个自由的时代接受他的感情。可惜世上没有后悔药可吃，她带着惆怅的心情斟满酒酿：“来！为自由的时代快些到来干一杯！”
"Even I do not mind it anymore, Miss Xiangrong—why fret for me?"

想绒很是配合，也斟满酒酿，与幽栖居士同饮：“为自由干了！”
"Not merely for you—for all women, especially drifters in the pleasure quarters like me," Xiangrong sighed wistfully. "Even a celebrated noble daughter cannot marry the man she loves—what hope for homeless women like us?"

喝完酒酿，想绒朝窗外瞧瞧，估摸时间差不多：“幽栖居士，你稍等片刻，我出去方便一下。”
"Have someone in your heart, Miss Xiangrong?"

人有三急，千里寻能理解，点点头：“快去快回，今日与你聊得畅快，还要继续聊。”
"Not yet. What rich heir glances twice at a courtesan? Even Young Master Dongfang invites me only because I sing Youqi Jushi's lyrics best at Flower Cloud Pavilion."

想绒没让丫鬟跟上，起身径自走出房间。真是巧，迎面走来两人，从衣着和面相判断，前面那人应该是仪王。三位公子早已叮嘱过，不能称呼仪王，要唤申公子。
"You stand on real artistry—no lesser than any noble lady in Lin'an!" Qianli Xun consoled warmly. "Those tacky girls at Chunxiang Brothel are the real drifters—you tower miles above them!"

想绒上前行礼：“申公子可来了。”
Deeply moved, Xiangrong toasted her again.

申简辰点点头：“你是想绒姑娘？”
"Noble daughters look shiny outside yet live trapped inside cages—look at me, passed around by my father straight into a man I despise! At least you have no parents tying your hands—find a good man someday and ride off free!"

“是，请申公子跟我这边来。”想绒带申简辰来到旁边的一道门，将门推开，“申公子，里边请，您在此稍候片刻，过会儿幽栖居士自会进来。”
"Youqi Jushi does not know—my deed of sale sits locked at Flower Cloud Pavilion for a thousand taels of gold! Who in Lin'an drops a thousand gold to redeem me—and what family would let him? No parents, yet chains of gold tie me down to earn silver for the madam."

申简辰满意地点点头。
Sighing right back, Qianli Xun spoke from a millennium away: "Blame an era rigged against women! Mark my word, one day women will stand equal—earning their own coin without leaning on men, marrying purely for love, smashing arranged marriages and Flower Cloud Pavilions and Chunxiang Brothels alike!"

想绒从正门出去，又从外面把门关好，打算去找醉云楼的总管。刚走到楼梯口，发现他正缓慢向上走。
Wonder lit Xiangrong's eyes. "Pray that day arrives soon—wonder if we will live to see it?"

“总管来得正好，我刚好找您。”
*A thousand years too early!* A sudden ache hit Qianli Xun—she HAD lived in that free world, blowing off Shen Jianchen without a second look! If only she had taken his hand back then instead of bleeding through cages here! Filling her cup high, she called, "Here is to that free age arriving soon—bottoms up!"

总管走上楼梯后，先问道：“刚才好像有两个人上来，姑娘有没有看到？”
"To freedom—bottoms up!" Xiangrong matched her drop for drop.

“总管不用操心，都是东方公子请来的朋友。”
Glancing out the window to gauge the hour, Xiangrong rose smoothly. "Give me a quick moment, Youqi Jushi—nature calls."

总管客气地问：“要不要我再上些茶水和酒酿，或者糕点之类？”
"Go right ahead—want to talk more when you get back!"

“不必，陈家少夫人让我告诉总管，她要在里面专心听我唱曲儿，不想被人打扰，你不必再派人进去送任何吃食。”
Leaving her maid behind, Xiangrong stepped into the hall right as two men climbed the stairs—recognizing Prince Yi from description and remembering strict orders to address him as "Young Master Shen." Curtsying, she guided him straight through the outer door into the empty side chamber—"Wait just a moment, Young Master Shen; Youqi Jushi will be right in"—closed the door softly, and intercepted Zuiyun Tower's steward coming up the steps: "Young Master Dongfang's guests, Steward—and Young Madam wants zero interruptions while I sing, so skip sending more snacks!"

总管回道：“是，谨遵少夫人吩咐。”
"Right away!" the steward bowed down the stairs.
