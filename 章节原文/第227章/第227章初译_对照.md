第227章 借口管教报仇恨
## Chapter 227: Payback Under the Cover of Discipline

将陈浩宇从春香楼五花大绑带回来，千里寻根本不怕被陈家长辈批评。她还盼着他们前来寻事，正好趁此机会质问一番，为何明明知道陈浩宇散播谣言坏她名声，还要同意这门亲事？
天都黑了，陈家长辈连半个人影都没见着。千里寻记得老太太房中的丫鬟明明看到，她老人家溺爱孙子，应该第一个来。一切出乎意料，千里寻琢磨不出其中缘由，便不去琢磨。
已将讨厌之人关起来，愤恨之情没有白天时那么强烈，少有的悲情反而袭上心头。搞得她无心写话本，坐在书桌前回想流言蜚语对她的生活造成的伤害。那段时日是她人生中最黑暗的时期，不仅流下许多泪水，还觉得人生已毫无希望。
一切是陈浩宇一手造成，她却与他成亲。幸好没与他圆房，否则后悔都来不及。
想起那段黑暗的日子，千里寻自然想到家人，想到梦容，想到申简辰。
家人的亲情带给她温暖，才让她有力气走出黑暗的日子。虽说朱晞颜没有去过望月轩，可若没他允诺，娘和哥哥嫂嫂也不敢随便踏出朱府。父亲表面上严厉，还是在用他认为正确的方式爱护她，与陈家结亲便是他认为的正确方式之一。
本想找朱晞颜闹一闹，被韦嬷嬷阻止，现在千里寻的心中也没了去闹的想法。闹有何用？难道能让她回到成亲之前？显然已不可能。
那段黑暗的时日，梦容来过望月轩两三回。别看次数不多，却让她感受到她没有被这个世界抛弃，还是有人愿意靠近她，愿意与她一起坐在火盆旁说心里话。也是从那时起，在心里悄悄告诉自己，她会一辈子将梦容视作闺蜜。
后来知道仪王就是申简辰，千里寻也终于明白为何那时的仪王对她很不一般。又是送礼物，又是送美食，梦容陪她聊天时，他则在望月轩外的马车里等候，这份情谊只怕这一世无法偿还。
因为梦容，她果断拒绝仪王，也因为梦容，她无法接受申简辰。但她不得不承认，在她被全城人非议的日子里，那时的仪王流露出的情感，无形中让她充满自信。要知道人家可是位王爷，竟然瞧上被人骂得狗血喷头的落难女子，还真有些英雄救美的意思。
“苦荞，把去年在望月轩时仪王送的礼物找来。”
苦荞想了半晌实在想不出是什么礼物：“小姐，我不记得仪王送过什么礼物。”
“一个撒尿娃娃。”
苦荞猛然想起：“哦，想起来，还真送过一个，是个光屁股的娃娃，小姐要它做什么？”
“别问那么多，快去找来。”
苦荞回想着，到底将那娃娃放在哪里？仪王送时，小姐只看那么一回就让她收起来，从此再没拿出过。小姐嫁到陈家后，是搬过来一些东西，也不知撒尿娃娃还在不在？
Dragging Chen Haoyu back from Chunxiang Brothel in ropes, Qianli Xun had not feared criticism from the Chen elders—in fact, she had welcomed a confrontation so she could demand why they had pushed this marriage knowing full well he had smeared her name across Lin'an.

“我去杂货间试着找一找，一般小姐不大关注的东西我都会放那里。”
Yet night fell without a single elder darkening her doorway. She had seen a maid from Old Madam Xia's courtyard watch the whole procession; with how the old woman doted on her grandson, she should have charged over first. Puzzled yet unwilling to overthink it, Qianli Xun let it go.

苦荞走出素心草堂，去寻找仪王送的礼物。
With the culprit locked away, her hot fury ebbed, replaced by a rare wave of raw grief. Unable to focus on her huaben, she sat at her desk remembering how those rumors had torn her life apart—her darkest months, drowned in tears and bleak despair.

千里寻百无聊赖，一腔愁情，不由展纸提笔写下：
All of it Chen Haoyu's doing—and she had married him. Thank goodness they had never consummated the marriage, or she would have regretted it to her dying day.

春来春去几经过，不似今年恨最多。
Thinking back to that bleak winter brought up images of her family, Mengrong, and Shen Jianchen. Family warmth had kept her standing; even though Zhu Xiyan had never visited Wangyue Pavilion, her mother, brother, and sister-in-law could never have come without his nod. Stern as her father was, he protected her in his own rigid way—matching her with the Chens included. Matron Wei had been right to stop her from storming Zhu Manor; raising a fuss now could not rewind the clock.

寂寂海棠枝上月，照人清夜欲如何。
During those dark months, Mengrong had come to Wangyue Pavilion two or three times—few visits, yet enough to prove the world had not abandoned her, someone willing to sit beside the brazier and share heart-to-hearts. That was when Qianli Xun had vowed to treat Mengrong as her lifelong confidante.

《春日杂书十首·其一》
And once she learned Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen, everything else snapped into focus: the gifts, the treats, waiting outside Wangyue Pavilion in his carriage while Mengrong visited—kindness she could never repay in one lifetime. For Mengrong's sake she had turned him down cold, yet she could not deny that having an imperial prince see her worth while an entire city spat on her had restored her backbone. Real white-knight stuff.

刚写完，苦荞手中捧只盒子进来，她把盒子给小姐放在桌旁：“我翻了好一阵儿，才翻出来。”
"Kuqiao, bring out that gift Prince Yi sent to Wangyue Pavilion last year."

小姐放下笔，苦荞过去将写有诗句的纸张拿起来，用嘴吹吹还未干的墨迹：“我把它收起来，等南双或是化雨来时，正好送给他们。”
Kuqiao racked her brains. "Miss, I don't recall Prince Yi sending a gift."

墨迹还未完全干，诗句中的一个“恨”字引起苦荞注意：“小姐，您是不是恨姑爷？”
"The peeing clay boy."

千里寻把桌上的盒子挪到面前，打开，拿出光屁股的撒尿娃娃，脸上终于露出笑容，只因娃娃实在可爱，让人看后不由不笑：“恨，当然恨，他害我好惨。”
Light dawned. "Oh, right! That bare-bottomed clay figurine! What do you want it for, Miss?"

苦荞继续向未干的墨迹吹一口气，见小姐边笑边说，放心许多。她并不是担心姑爷，而是担心自家小姐带着恨度过第二次的婚姻生活。
"Stop asking and go fetch it."

“小姐笑了，我就高兴。”
Kuqiao frowned trying to remember where she had tucked it—her mistress had looked once and told her to put it away, never asking for it again. "I'll check the storage room; that's where I stash things you rarely look at." She hurried out.

墨迹已干，苦荞将写有诗的纸张收好。
Left alone with melancholy pressing on her chest, Qianli Xun spread a sheet of paper and wrote:

再看小姐时，她依然将目光留在泥人娃娃的身上。
"How many springs have come and gone, / Yet never a year with resentment so deep as this. / On quiet crabapple branches shines the moon— / How shall I endure this clear, lone night?" —"Ten Spring Reflections, No. 1" [TN: Translator's rendering]

苦荞想起白风送她的泥人：“也不知白风和仪王什么时候回临安？”
As she lifted her brush, Kuqiao returned holding a small wooden box and set it beside her. "Had to dig for ages to find it."

这话正好问到千里寻的心里，她也希望申简辰快些回来，可又不知他回来后她该如何面对梦容：“我倒希望他们留在汴梁，永远别回来。”
Setting her brush aside, Qianli Xun watched Kuqiao lift the poem sheet and blow across the wet ink. "I'll save this for the next time Nan Shuang or Huayu comes asking."

苦荞理解小姐心中所想，还是换个话题，省得想起抢亲之事，惹小姐心烦：“小姐，您真的打算以后一直把姑爷关着？”
Catching the word "resentment" in the damp strokes, Kuqiao paused. "Miss, do you hate Young Master?"

千里寻将撒尿娃娃放进盒子盖好：“先关着吧，至于关多久，还没想好。”
Qianli Xun slid the box closer, lifted the lid, and took out the bare-bottomed clay figurine. A genuine smile broke across her face—the mischievous little doll made it impossible not to laugh. "Hate him? Of course I hate him. He put me through hell."

苦荞竟然抽泣起来……
Blowing once more on the ink, Kuqiao relaxed at seeing her mistress smile. She did not worry for the groom; she worried for her mistress spending a second marriage poisoned by hatred. "So long as you smile, Miss, I'm happy."

千里寻扭过头，见苦荞面带泪痕：“好好的，怎么哭了？”
Once dry, Kuqiao tucked the poem away and noticed her mistress still gazing down at the clay doll. Remembering the figurine Bai Feng had bought her, Kuqiao murmured, "Wonder when Bai Feng and Prince Yi are coming back to Lin'an."

苦荞边哭边说：“我为小姐难过，之前的婚姻本就不幸，好不容易和离，又嫁给散播您谣言之人，小姐以后的日子该怎么过？呜呜……！”
That echoed Qianli Xun's own tangled wish—wanting Shen Jianchen back, yet dreading facing Mengrong. "Honestly, I hope they stay in Bianliang and never come back."

被苦荞这么一说，千里寻也难过起来，她没有像苦荞一般啼哭流泪，而是失落地说：“我要让陈浩宇付出代价。”
Understanding her heart, Kuqiao steered away from reminders of the wedding raid. "Miss, do you really plan to keep Young Master locked up forever?"

苦荞继续哭：“他现在是姑爷，是您的夫君，在外人的眼中跟您是一家人，一荣俱荣，一损俱损，姑爷若付出什么代价，只怕小姐也避免不了，我都替小姐感到为难，呜呜……！”
Setting the doll back inside its box and closing the lid, Qianli Xun said, "Locked up for now. Haven't decided for how long."

“别哭了，哭得我心烦。”千里寻把盒子递给苦荞，“把盒子放在架子上，方便我随时拿来瞧。”
Suddenly Kuqiao began to sniffle.

苦荞擦擦眼泪，不敢再继续哭，怕惹得小姐难过。她接过盒子，在一旁的架子上找个地方放好。
Qianli Xun turned. "Why on earth are you crying?"

“你去休息吧，不必伺候我，我想一个人静一静。”
"I feel so awful for you, Miss!" Kuqiao sobbed. "Your first marriage was misery, you fought so hard for a mutual divorce, and now you're wed to the man who spread slander about you! How are you supposed to live like this? Boo-hoo!"

小姐吩咐，苦荞还是要听的，便回悦宁居休息。
Her maid's tears tugged at Qianli Xun's own heart. Without weeping, she answered grimly, "I'm going to make Chen Haoyu pay."

屋子里太闷，千里寻想出去透透气。
"He's your husband now, Miss!" Kuqiao wept on. "In outsiders' eyes you're one household—rising together, sinking together! If he pays a steep price, you'll get dragged down with him! I hate seeing you trapped like this!"

她起身来到院中，走到池塘边，水面刚长出荷叶，不大，铺展开来。月光下，池塘里只有两种色彩，除了水面映出月光的皎洁外，其他都是黑色。
"Quit crying, you're giving me a headache." Handing her the wooden box, Qianli Xun said, "Put this on the shelf where I can reach it whenever I want."

刚刚在屋里苦荞说的“一荣俱荣，一损俱损”的道理，千里寻岂能不知？尽管她不喜欢陈浩宇，尽管姻缘是长辈牵下，嫁过来却是她自己的选择。
Wiping her tears lest she upset her mistress further, Kuqiao placed the box on the shelf.

无论什么原因嫁过来，她只想将这个家当成人生的最后一站，想在这里过下去。累了也罢，屈服命运也罢，还是为还梦容一个好哥哥，她从未想过第二次和离。
"Go get some rest. I want to sit alone for a while."

陈浩宇今日大喊要休了她，千里寻并非害怕被人所休，也并非害怕再和离一次，而是她从未想过，突然被人提及，心中难免不适。
Obeying, Kuqiao retreated to Yuening Room.

该如何做？既可泄心头之恨，又能全她人生最后一站的想法。
Finding the room stifling, Qianli Xun stepped outside to the edge of the lotus pond. Small green pads floated over dark water, bathed in moonlight—pure silver reflection against deep shadow.

千里寻想到奶奶、公爹和婆婆让她管教陈浩宇之事，轻松中带着一丝冷笑：“那我就借着管教的理由认真报仇，只盼你一辈子都别改，我好管教你一辈子。若你改好，我就成陈家的功臣，在这家中的地位必然屹立不倒。”
Kuqiao's warning about rising and sinking together rang true. Even though she did not love Chen Haoyu and the elders had forced the match, walking into that wedding had been her own choice. Whether out of exhaustion, bowing to fate, or giving Mengrong back her brother, she meant this manor to be her final stop and had never considered a second divorce. Hearing Chen Haoyu howl about repudiating her today had not scared her, yet it had stung.

见惯陈浩宇吊儿郎当的样子，很难想象他变好后会是个什么样？像文渊那样？还是像申简辰？
How could she vent her grudge while still building a stable life here?

千里寻被自己的想象逗乐，事情想通，心情好一些。不如明日主动找长辈说明管教的想法，给他们吃颗定心丸，也好避免他们无端插手，影响自己报仇。不，应该是“管教”。
A sharp idea flashed: Old Madam Xia and her in-laws had begged her to discipline Chen Haoyu. A dry smile curved her lips. "Fine—I'll take my revenge under the banner of 'disciplining' him! Pray you never change, Chen Haoyu, so I get to discipline you for life! And if you actually reform, I become the Chen family's hero with an unshakable seat in this house."

悦宁居内，苦荞通过窗户看到小姐的身影，叹口气，把窗户关上，对红豆说：“我真替小姐担心，怕她再和离一次。”
Picturing scatterbrained Chen Haoyu turning refined like Wenyuan or steady like Shen Jianchen made her laugh out loud. With her mind clear, her spirits rose. Tomorrow she would call on the elders upfront and lay out her plan to "discipline" him, reassuring them so nobody meddled in her payback—sorry, her education program.

悦宁居有两张丫鬟床，红豆已躺下，还未睡着：“苦荞姐姐放心，小姐在陈家跟长辈处得相当和谐，陈家人舍不得小姐。”
From the window of Yuening Room, Kuqiao watched her mistress's silhouette by the pond, sighed, shut the casement, and turned to Hongdou. "I'm terrified Miss will end up going through another divorce."

苦荞走到红豆床前，在床边坐下：“姑爷散播谣言，小姐绝不会饶他，肯定会折磨他。姑爷可是老太太的心头肉，大夫人也心疼自己的儿子，怎么可能由着小姐？他们再喜欢小姐，亲疏关系还是分得清的。”
Lying awake on one of the two maids' beds, Hongdou replied, "Stop stressing, Sister Kuqiao. Miss gets along great with the Chen elders—they'd never let her go."

红豆翻了个身：“别瞎操心，韦嬷嬷都不担心，你担心什么劲儿？”
Kuqiao sat on the edge of Hongdou's bed. "After finding out Young Master spread those rumors, Miss won't go easy on him. He's Old Madam Xia's precious darling and Eldest Madam's own son—how long will they sit back and watch? Fond as they are of Miss, blood runs thicker than water."

苦荞一想，也对，韦嬷嬷是何等人，从姑爷被带回来，只见她出去过一趟，再没见到人，好像此事与她无关似的。于是放松心情：“你给姑爷送晚饭时，他有没有继续骂小姐？”
Hongdou rolled over. "Quit borrowing trouble. Matron Wei isn't worried one bit, so why are you?"

红豆转过身：“怎么能少？我真怀疑姑爷跟小姐是前世的冤家，这一世被硬生生凑到一起，也不知这对儿冤家这一世谁能斗过谁？”
True enough—since Young Master had been marched home, Matron Wei had stepped out once and stayed totally unruffled. Kuqiao relaxed. "When you delivered dinner, was Young Master still cursing Miss?"

“还用问？肯定是小姐能斗得过姑爷，斗得他落花流水！霹雳啪嚓！”苦荞已忘记刚才的担忧，得意地说。
Hongdou turned back. "Nonstop! Swear they must've been bitter rivals in a past life forced under one roof in this one. Wonder who'll win?"

红豆倒担忧起来：“你说他们是夫妻，斗来斗去有意思吗？我希望小姐好，也希望有个好姑爷。你说大老爷和大夫人人那么好，姑爷又是他们生的，怎么就不像大老爷？如果姑爷对小姐也像大老爷对大夫人那样，想着都替小姐高兴。”
"Need you ask? Miss will wipe the floor with him!" Kuqiao crowed, all worry forgotten.

苦荞打了一个哈欠，困意袭来，走到桌前熄灭蜡烛，脱掉外衣，躺在床上才说道：“我们担心也没用，只能希望姑爷快些变好，小姐也能有好日子过。”
Hongdou frowned softly. "Still, they're husband and wife—what's the fun in fighting forever? I want Miss happy with a decent husband. Eldest Master and Eldest Madam are such wonderful people—how didn't Young Master take after his father? If he ever treated Miss the way Eldest Master treats Eldest Madam, I'd be over the moon for her."

晚上，陈江林从茶庄回来，姚轻琰将白天发生的事情一五一十说了。
Stifling a yawn, Kuqiao snuffed the candle, slipped out of her outer jacket, and climbed into bed. "Worrying won't fix him. All we can do is hope Young Master turns over a new leaf soon so Miss can have peace."

陈江林高兴道：“就应该把他关起来，我早想这么做，碍于你跟老太太，才没敢下狠手。”
Late that night, when Chen Jianglin returned from the tea house, Yao Qingyan recounted the whole day's drama.

姚轻琰一边给陈江林褪去外衣，一边说：“刚听说时，我跟老太太心疼不已，还好韦嬷嬷前来，宽慰我们一番，还说淑真已经知道浩宇散播谣言之事，也是因为此事才把他关起来。”
Chen Jianglin grinned. "Locking him up is just what he needed! I wanted to do it years ago—only held back because of you and Mother."

外衣脱去，陈江林坐在床边：“唉，纸包不住火，淑真迟早会知道，现在知道也好，那小子做下此等错事，把他关起来都是轻的，要我说就得拿鞭子好好抽打抽打。”
Helping him slip off his outer robe, Yao Qingyan said, "When I first heard, Mother and I both ached for him. Thank goodness Matron Wei came over to reassure us—turns out Shuzhen found out Haoyu spread those rumors last year, and that's why she locked him up."

姚轻琰准备帮陈江林脱掉鞋子。
Sitting on the edge of the bed, Chen Jianglin sighed. "Fire never stays wrapped in paper. Just as well she knows now. After pulling a stunt that foul, locking him up is mercy—he deserves a good horsewhipping."

陈江林说：“这种事情我来，夫人赶紧上床歇息。”
When Yao Qingyan bent to remove his boots, he waved her off. "I'll handle my own boots. Get into bed and rest."

姚轻琰便到妆台前去掉发饰，也将外衣脱掉，熄了灯，躺到床上：“比起鞭子来，我更怕她的拳头，你说淑真会不会把浩宇打出残疾来？”
Taking off her hairpins at the dressing table, Yao Qingyan shed her outer robe, snuffed the lamp, and slipped under the covers. "I'm less worried about whips than her fists! What if Shuzhen breaks his bones?"

“把心放在肚子里，淑真的拳头虽厉害，自嫁过来，何曾见过她用拳头？相反，知礼、懂事，还有心，她嫁进来没几日，府中的气象大变。”
"Put your mind at rest. Tough as her fists are, has she thrown one punch since marrying in? Quite the opposite—courteous, sharp, and warmhearted. Barely days under our roof and the whole atmosphere of the manor brightened."

“这么说来她还给陈家带来福气？”
"Sounds like she brought good luck into Chen Manor."

“还用说？我现在倒觉得她跟林觉和离，跟乔家的事没成，都是因为老天把她给我们陈家留着，呵呵……！”
"No question! Now I'm convinced her divorce from Lin Jue and the broken Qiao match were Heaven saving her for our Chen household! Hahaha!"

“你这么说我不认同，朱陈两家之所以亲上加亲，还不是因为浩宇散播流言一事。”
"Hardly Heaven—our two families only sealed this match because Haoyu spread those rumors."

“所以说，冥冥中注定浩宇要给自己挣回个好娘子，呵呵……！”
"Which proves fate forced Haoyu to earn himself a fine wife! Hahaha!"

姚轻琰不完全认同夫君所言，还是被对方说得高兴起来，她在心中祝福淑真与浩宇的关系能慢慢好起来。
Half amused despite disagreeing, Yao Qingyan smiled in the dark, silently wishing for her son and daughter-in-law to find their way to peace.
