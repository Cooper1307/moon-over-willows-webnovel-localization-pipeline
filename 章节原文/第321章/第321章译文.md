## Chapter 321: Stepping Voluntarily onto a Road of No Return

Haoyu grabbed her hand to drag her out anyway—backed urgently by Old Madam Xia, Chen Jianglin, and Yao Qingyan rushing into the courtyard—yet Qianli Xun shook free twice and vaulted back inside Suxin Thatched Hall, having Kuqiao bolt the door.

Slumping against the panels outside, Haoyu choked out, "You never really loved me—that's why you're willing to walk away from me!"

Biting back the truth—that she *had* started loving him, willing to bear his children—lest he throw his life and the Chen clan away chasing her, she forced out a cold answer: *"If you already know, why ask?"*

Just then Butler Wang rushed in shouting that Lin Jue's troops had surrounded the manor ahead of schedule!

Calming her tearful martial servants and sending Kuqiao indoors with a gentle white lie (*"Light eight sticks of incense praying—I'll be home by the eighth"*), Qianli Xun walked solo to the front gate—where Haoyu was already throwing furious punches at Lin Jue, while Chen Jianglin, Old Madam Xia, and Yao Qingyan stood blocking the threshold swearing Lin Jue would have to climb over their corpses first!

Knocking back the advancing soldiers cleanly, Qianli Xun stepped forward—"You're here for *me*, Lin Jue; leave my family alone!"—stinging Lin Jue with how warmly she called the Chens *her family*.

Holding back tears so she wouldn't trigger a bloody last stand from the elders, she stepped past the wall of drawn blades into Lin Jue's carriage while Haoyu roared after her, *"Come back to me no matter what!"*—silently vowing inside that if she walked back through that gate alive, she'd scrap the two-year wait and consummate their marriage on the spot.

Riding opposite her, Lin Jue crassly warned her about Liang Shicheng's twisted needle fetish and advised her to submit to save her neck—earning a blistering threat to rearrange his face if he opened his foul mouth again.
