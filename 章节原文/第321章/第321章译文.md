## Chapter 321: Walking Willingly Down a Road of No Return

“No! I’m not letting you go see him—pack up and come with me right now!” Chen Haoyu grabbed his wife’s arm and pulled her toward the door. “Any longer and it’ll be too late!”

Having packed bundles of clothes, Kuqiao emerged carrying two packs while Hongdou led the rest of the servants into the hall, assuming their mistress meant to flee at once.

Two paces out, Qianli Xun wrenched her arm free of Chen Haoyu’s grip. “Dodge him today, and I can’t dodge him tomorrow! Worse, if I run off into hiding, it’s the Chen family that gets dragged down…”

Too worried to stay inside, Chen Jianglin, Yao Qingyan, and Old Madam Xia hurried into the courtyard just in time to catch her words—realizing she already knew the whole truth.

Before she finished, Old Madam Xia cut in firmly, “It won’t hurt the Chen family right now! Just go into hiding—leave your elders to deal with everything else.”

Stepping up, Yao Qingyan pleaded, “Listen to your father-in-law and go with Haoyu! Once Commander Lin marches soldiers here, you won’t be able to slip out!”

Chen Jianglin added, “Imperial Attendant Liang already promised not to move against the Chens. Hide away safely until Liang leaves Lin’an, then come right back home.”

Their fierce loyalty warmed Qianli Xun’s heart—making her all the more determined never to endanger them. Stepping forward, she bowed deeply to her three elders. “Liang Shicheng isn’t touching the Chen family *yet*, which doesn’t mean he won’t later. I can’t save my own neck by abandoning my family. This mess started because of me—let me settle it.”

Old Madam Xia insisted, “The moment you married into this house, you became a Chen! It’s our duty to protect you—we don’t need you walking into a tiger’s den alone!”

“Because I am a Chen,” Qianli Xun answered steady and clear, “it’s my duty to protect this family too…”

“With me around, nobody needs *you* doing the protecting!” Chen Haoyu snapped, grabbing her wrist again. “Come with me—now!”

Shaking free a second time, Qianli Xun leaped gracefully onto Suxin Thatched Hall’s doorstep using light-foot technique.

“My mind is made up—stop trying to talk me out of it. I brought this trouble down; I’ll shoulder it.” Stepping inside, she barked, “Kuqiao! Get in here and shut the door!”

Though still foggy on all the details, Kuqiao could tell from the panic outside that grave danger had struck. Part of her wanted her mistress to flee too, yet she dared not disobey—scrambling inside, she slammed the doors shut.

A heartbeat behind her, Chen Haoyu reached the porch just as the bolt slid home.

He pounded hard against the wood. “Wife! Open up! Don’t go—you mustn’t! If anything happens to you, how am I supposed to go on living?!”

From behind the door came his wife’s calm voice. “Chen Haoyu, this world is full of wonderful girls. If something happens to me, go marry another one—she’ll treat you ten times better than I ever did.”

Heart torn apart, Chen Haoyu rested both palms flat against the door and slid straight down until he sat crumpled on the steps, shouting hoarsely at the panels, “You never truly loved me, did you?! That’s why you’re so cold—ready to throw me aside without blinking! Tell me I’m right!”

Inside, seated at her desk, Qianli Xun mulled over his raw cry. How desperately she wanted to tell him she *had* started caring—that she was willing to bear his children, willing to grow old beside him till their hair turned white.

Yet terrified Chen Haoyu would throw away his entire family and his birthright as heir just to gamble for her life, she forced out a cruel lie. “If you already know the answer, why bother asking?”

Refusing to believe it, Chen Haoyu hammered his fists against the door. “No! You’re lying to me! I know you’re lying!”

Watching from the yard, the three elders knew nothing on earth could bend Shuzhen once her mind locked. Walking over together, Old Madam Xia put a hand on her grandson’s shoulder. “Haoyu, since Shuzhen has decided, we have to let her handle this her way. Don’t break down—Shuzhen knows martial arts; Liang Shicheng won’t dare bully her easily. And if that eunuch lays a finger on her, I’ll risk this old life of mine to shield her.”

Chen Haoyu’s voice broke into a choked sob. “Even Prince Yi couldn’t beat Liang Shicheng—what chance do we stand?!”

Just then Butler Wang sprinted into the courtyard, gasping for breath. “Old Madam! Eldest Master! Disaster! Commander Lin has surrounded Chen Manor with troops—demanding we hand over Second Young Madam!”

Not even the Hour of Wei yet, and Lin Jue was already at the gates—clearly Liang Shicheng feared his prey might bolt. No escaping this now.

Scrambling off the steps, Chen Haoyu sprinted toward the main gate with the three elders hurrying right behind him.

Once the yard outside fell quiet, Kuqiao finally found her voice. “Miss… is Imperial Attendant Liang demanding to see you because of Princess Consort Yi?”

“Eight times out of ten, yes.”

“Then you really shouldn’t go see him!”

“All the more reason to go. This score between Mengrong and me has dragged on long enough—time to settle it.”

“How do you plan to settle it, Miss?”

“Haven’t figured that out yet. One step at a time.”

Rising, Qianli Xun walked toward the door.

Kuqiao dropped to her knees and clutched her hem. “You can’t go, Miss! Walk through that door and you might never come back alive!”

Turning around, Qianli Xun lifted Kuqiao gently to her feet. “With my fists, who on earth could put me down? Stay calm and wait here—I promise I’ll be back after dark.”

She pulled open the door and stepped out.

Hongdou, Chouya, Asheng, Sanbao, and the others stood waiting in a tight cluster right outside.

Stepping up first, Hongdou urged, “Miss, Commander Lin’s soldiers don’t know real martial arts! Take us along and we’ll fight our way straight out—there’s still time to escape!”

Looking at the loyal servants she had trained herself, Qianli Xun felt a lump in her throat. Back then, nursing a broken heart, she had taught them Qianli Fist just to sweat out her frustration—never dreaming the day would come when they’d volunteer to throw their lives down for her. There was zero chance she’d take Hongdou’s offer.

“Think I don’t know how much weight you lot carry?” she said lightly. “Hand-to-hand against those patrol soldiers? Sure, you’d hold your own. Yet Lin Jue has waves of troops—keep swinging long enough and anyone runs out of gas. Stay put right here in Moxiang Courtyard. It’s nowhere near as grim as Young Master and the elders make out. I’m just popping out and coming right back.”

Hearing how breezy their mistress sounded—and knowing next to nothing of the danger—the servants relaxed right away.

Asheng grinned. “If Miss says so, then we’re not worried!”

Glad her bluff worked, Qianli Xun kept up the casual front. “What are you all standing around gawking for? Scatter! Moxiang Courtyard’s got plenty of chores waiting—catch anyone slacking when I get back and I won’t let them off easy!”

Chuckling, the servants dispersed to their tasks.

Kuqiao trailed at her heels. “Let me at least walk you to the front gate, Miss.”

Qianli Xun stopped her. “Not today. Go back inside, light incense, and pray I come back quick. I give you my word—by the time eight sticks of incense burn down, I’ll walk back through that door.”

“Truly, Miss?”

“Believe it or don’t.”

“I believe you! I believe you! I’ll go light the first stick right now and pray hard over every single one!”

Watching Kuqiao hurry indoors cheerfully to fetch incense, Qianli Xun murmured to herself, “At least give you something to hold onto, so you don’t insist on tagging behind me.”

With the pesky servants safely sidetracked, Qianli Xun strode alone toward the main gate.

First to reach the gate, Chen Haoyu spotted Lin Jue standing outside in crisp military commander’s robes looking thoroughly pleased with himself—and without a second thought drove a fist straight into Lin Jue’s face!

“You bastard! Daring to march soldiers around the Chen estate—I’ll beat you to death!”

Blindsided by the punch, Lin Jue exploded in rage. “Chen Haoyu! Assaulting a court official—watch me haul you in for treason!”

“With that slimy rat face of yours, you dare call yourself an official?! *You’re* the exact bastard I’m punching!”

Chen Haoyu swung a second punch, which Lin Jue caught easily in his palm. “Where’s Zhu Shuzhen? Bring her out right now!”

“Over my dead body!” Chen Haoyu lunged again.

Trading two quick blows, Lin Jue shoved Chen Haoyu stumbling backward several paces. “With pathetic skills like yours, you still dare pick a fight with me?”

Arriving just behind, Yao Qingyan caught her son’s arm while Old Madam Xia stepped forward sharply. “What good does hitting *him* do? He isn’t even the one demanding Shuzhen!”

“Just looking at his face makes my blood boil!” Chen Haoyu snarled.

Stepping forward politely, Chen Jianglin said, “Don’t take Haoyu to heart, Commander Lin. It isn’t even the Hour of Wei yet—what brings you here so early?”

Straightening his ruffled uniform, Lin Jue replied coldly, “Imperial Attendant Liang worried something might go wrong and rushed word to bring her early. I dared not delay. Now where is Zhu Shuzhen? Have her come meet the Lord Attendant quietly. If she plays nice, who knows—it might turn out well for her *and* the Chen family.”

Chen Haoyu shouted back, “Go tell Liang Shicheng my wife isn’t setting one foot inside Liu’an Villa—not ever!”

Lin Jue didn’t flinch an inch. “The Lord Attendant says he still honors his debt to Old Madam Xia and won’t touch a hair on the Chen family—unless the Chens refuse to hand over the poetess. In that case, old favors go straight out the window, and he’ll crush your house however he pleases. Hand her over nicely, though, and the favor stays intact—from here on out, he’ll be the strongest patron backing the Chen clan!”

The commotion at the front gate drew the rest of the household running.

Chen Jiangshu and Chen Jiangmu were out tending the family’s shops; Chen Haofei was away grinding hard at his academy under his tutor ahead of next spring’s palace examination.

That left only Madam Bao and Madam Cui, who hurried up just in time to catch Lin Jue’s speech.

Though clueless about the background, Madam Cui absorbed every word about backing. Her son was studying himself blind for official rank—with a powerful court patron behind them, wouldn’t promotion come easy? Stepping right up beside Old Madam Xia, she whispered urgently, “Mother! Whatever we do, we must never offend Imperial Attendant Liang!”

Old Madam Xia shot Madam Cui a blistering glare. “What do *you* know?!”

Madam Bao asked plaintively, “What on earth is going on here?”

Nobody had the patience to explain things to Madam Bao; left ignored again, a fresh sting of bitterness pricked her chest—ever since her son landed in prison, her standing inside this manor had shrunk to nothing.

“The Chen family doesn’t need a patron like Imperial Attendant Liang!” Chen Haoyu roared at Lin Jue.

Lin Jue warned smoothly, “Seeing how decent the Chens always were to me, I’ll pretend I didn’t hear that. Hand over Zhu Shuzhen right now, or don’t blame me when I drop all pleasantries!”

Fired up by his son’s backbone, Chen Jianglin stepped forward like a lion. “If Commander Lin wants to take Shuzhen, step over my corpse and my son’s first!”

Old Madam Xia planted her cane firm. “And over these old bones too!”

Yao Qingyan stood shoulder to shoulder with them. “And me! Shuzhen is my daughter-in-law—I’m not abandoning her!”

Lin Jue froze dumbstruck. Look at how fiercely the entire Chen household stood behind Zhu Shuzhen—no wonder she had married Chen Haoyu so willingly and settled right into life with him. Comparing that to how wretchedly *he* had treated her made shame burn his throat; no wonder she had moved Heaven and Earth to escape his house.

Snapping back to duty, Lin Jue barked outward, “What are you waiting for?! Storm in and arrest the whole Chen family!”

Soldiers surged through the gateway—only to be sent flying backward by Qianli Xun’s fists and kicks before getting anywhere near the Chens. Planting her feet steady, she glared straight at Lin Jue. “Lin Jue! I’m the one you came for—why bully my family?!”

“My family.” Those two words grated like gravel down Lin Jue’s spine. During their marriage, Zhu Shuzhen had never once looked at him or his kin as *family*. Counting the months, she’d barely been at Chen Manor six or seven months—about the same stretch she’d stayed with the Lins! Same span of time: with him she’d fought tooth and nail for *heli* (mutual divorce), yet these Chens she called *my family* with fierce devotion. Bitter jealousy coiled inside his gut.

“Since you’re out here, come peacefully to see the Lord Attendant.”

Wasting no more breath, Qianli Xun walked straight forward.

“Wife!” Chen Haoyu called desperately from behind.

Qianli Xun halted, pasted an unbothered smile onto her face, and turned back. “I’ll be home soon. Wait quietly for me inside.”

“Don’t go!” Chen Haoyu pleaded again.

Smiling faintly without replying, she swept her gaze across her family—Grandmother, Father-in-law, Mother-in-law, every pair of eyes begging her to stay.

For a second she wanted to drop to her knees and kowtow to the three elders to thank them for every ounce of warmth they’d shown her these past months. Yet kowtowing would tip them off she didn’t expect to return, and they’d throw their lives in her path to stop her—ruining both her and the Chens for nothing.

Hardening her heart, Qianli Xun turned around and walked straight out the main gate.

Chen Haoyu and his family lunged after her.

With one wave of Lin Jue’s hand, soldiers snapped naked swords into a gleaming wall barring the gateway. Helpless, the Chen household could only watch Shuzhen cross the threshold and climb into the waiting carriage.

Chen Haoyu yelled with everything in his lungs, “Wife! I’m waiting right here! No matter what happens, you *have* to come back to me!”

Inside the carriage, hearing his voice carry clear across the courtyard, Qianli Xun promised silently: *Chen Haoyu—if I walk out of this alive and make it home safe, I’m canceling our two-year deal. We’ll consummate this marriage right away.*

As the carriage rolled forward, Lin Jue sat opposite her, eyes glued to Zhu Shuzhen. She still looked breathtaking—no wonder Imperial Attendant Liang had singled her out by name.

A twinge of reluctance—even regret—tugged at Lin Jue’s chest. “Walking in there on your own like this… aren’t you scared Liang Shicheng has filthy plans for you?”

Qianli Xun shot him a scorching glare. “Who walked in on her own?! You’re the one who surrounded Chen Manor with troops and forced me onto this carriage!”

“Think I wanted to? A man in officialdom has no choice—can I afford to cross Imperial Attendant Liang or the new Prefect Li?”

“Hmph! Spare me the sob story! You’re nothing but a spineless bootlicker chasing power—people like you make me sick!”

Hit dead on the mark, Lin Jue shrugged carelessly. “Paint me ugly all you want. How many folks in this world *don’t* chase power and clout? Count them on one hand.”

Qianli Xun let out another cold snort and looked away, done wasting words on him.

“Hey,” Lin Jue spoke up awkwardly. “Seeing as we were husband and wife once, take one friendly warning: Imperial Attendant Liang is sick in the bedroom—loves poking women with needles. Watch yourself.”

Qianli Xun blinked. “Sick where?”

“Married twice and you can’t even follow plain talk?”

Catching his drift, Qianli Xun curled her lip. “Pigs like that? Every one I meet, I flatten!”

“He’s Imperial Attendant Liang—His Majesty’s favorite! Touch him and aren’t you scared he’ll slap a capital charge on your head?”

“Only spineless bootlickers like you wet themselves over corrupt eunuchs. I don’t scare that easily.”

Lin Jue sneered back. “Think strong fists make you untouchable? Wake up—against real power, your fists aren’t worth a copper coin! Take my advice: if worst comes to worst, grit your teeth and let him stick a few needles in you. Keeps you alive and keeps the Chen family safe—why fight it?”

“Shut your filthy mouth!” Qianli Xun snapped. “Yabber one more word of trash and I’ll clobber you too right here in this carriage!”
