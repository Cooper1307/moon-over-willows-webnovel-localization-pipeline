## Chapter 179: A Dear Friend Calls for Help

忧愁难过一夜，乔梦容早早起来，收拾完毕，草草吃过早饭，第一次不想一早去找哥哥，带上喜兰打算出幽林别居。
经过正院外，听到哥哥教白风练习拳法的声音，不由叹口气，想想昨晚之事，仿佛是一场不愉快的梦。
“喜兰，信已寄出多久？”
喜兰明白，郡主是问写给梁内侍的信：“回郡主，已半月有余。”
“你觉得官家会同意将婚期提前吗？”
喜兰哪里知道，她关心郡主，不希望她伤心：“有梁内侍帮忙，官家必然会同意。”
“我担心梁内侍不会帮我。”
After a sleepless night of grief, Qiao Mengrong rose at dawn, dressed, picked at her breakfast, and—for the very first time—skipped going straight to her brother's courtyard, heading instead out of Youlin Retreat with Xilan.

“怎么会？您大小也是郡主，再说曾给过他不少好处。”
Passing the main courtyard, she caught the sound of her brother drilling Bai Feng in martial arts and let out a quiet sigh. Last night felt like a bitter dream.

“也只能这么想，我们走吧。”
"Xilan, how long since we sent that letter?"

乔梦容走出幽林别居，乘坐马车去望月轩找闺中密友。
Knowing she meant the letter to Imperial Attendant Liang, Xilan answered, "Over half a month now, Your Highness."

自捶丸场一别，她再没见过淑真，也不知与陈家退婚之事进展得如何。乔梦容已没有心情关心别人的事，她有更重要的事情问淑真。
"Do you think His Majesty will agree to move the wedding up?"

千里寻一早起来，去后院看完下人们训练，回到闺房继续写话本。近期她写的故事还是穿越前写过的网络小说，只需稍作改动而已。
Unable to know yet unwilling to see her mistress hurt, Xilan reassured her. "With Imperial Attendant Liang putting in a word, His Majesty is sure to agree."

写着写着，千里寻写不下去，需要加一段有关赌博的情节。她并不懂赌博，于是撂下笔。
"I worry Imperial Attendant Liang will not help me."

想起曾在街上瞎逛时，看到陈浩宇从赌坊出来的情景，决定去赌坊走走，实地考察一番，再亲身体验一下，写起来才会顺手。
"Why would he not? You are a princess—and you slipped him plenty of silver over the years."

“苦荞，去把红豆找来，再把男装拿出来，我要出去办点儿事。”
"I can only hope so. Let us go."

找红豆，苦荞能理解，小姐又要女扮男装，苦荞不甚理解：“小姐为何又要穿男装？街上已没人再议论您。”
Exiting Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong boarded her carriage for Wangyue Pavilion to see her closest friend. Since parting at the chuiwan grounds, they had not seen each other, and she had no idea how Shuzhen's efforts to cancel the Chen betrothal were faring—not that she had room to worry over anyone else's troubles today. She had far heavier questions to ask Shuzhen.

千里寻并不隐瞒：“话本里需要写有关赌博的场景，你家小姐我对那种地方不熟悉，需要实地考察一番。”
At Wangyue Pavilion, Qianli Xun had risen early, checked on the servants' morning drills in the back yard, and sat back down to her huaben. Lately she was adapting an online novel she had written before transmigrating, tweaking details to fit the Song setting. Halfway through a chapter, her brush stalled: the plot called for a gambling scene, and she knew next to nothing about Song gambling dens.

“没想到写话本比写诗词还难，还需要实地考察？难道小姐您还要赌博不成？”
Remembering spotting Chen Haoyu strolling out of a gambling house once, she decided to visit one in person for field research—maybe even try a hand or two so the scenes rang true.

“有必要的话，上上赌桌也无妨。”
"Kuqiao, go fetch Hongdou and pull out my men's clothes. I am going out on an errand."

苦荞还是难以理解小姐的举动，打算先去叫红杏。
Bringing Hongdou made sense, yet disguising herself again puzzled Kuqiao. "Why dress as a man again, Miss? Nobody in the streets gossips about you anymore."

刚要出闺房，佳和郡主匆忙进来。苦荞赶紧行礼，也明白小姐今日是去不了赌坊，她也没必要再去唤红豆。
Qianli Xun made no secret of it. "My huaben needs a gambling scene, and I have no clue how those places work. Time for field research."

梦容前来，千里寻自然不会再出去，立刻迎上去：“梦容，快到里面坐，苦荞，去把书桌收拾完，给郡主做盏茶来。”
"Who knew writing huaben was tougher than writing poetry—even needs field research?! Don't tell me Miss means to gamble too?"

苦荞明白小姐的意思，先到书桌前将手稿收好，再坐到茶桌旁做茶。
"If needed, sitting down at a table will not hurt."

等乔梦容坐好后，千里寻才仔细瞧她，双眼微红，气色看上去并不好，担忧地问：“你哭过，发生什么事？”
Still baffled by her mistress's ways, Kuqiao turned to go fetch Hongdou—only for Princess Jiahe to hurry right through the doorway. Dropping into a curtsy, Kuqiao realized the gambling trip was off and stayed put.

乔梦容的眼睛不由变得更红，落下几滴泪来。
Setting her outing aside at once, Qianli Xun hurried to welcome her friend. "Come sit inside, Mengrong! Kuqiao, clear the desk and whisk Her Highness a bowl of tea."

千里寻拿起丝帕为梦容擦掉眼泪：“别哭，快说到底发生了什么？”
Catching the hint, Kuqiao stowed the manuscript pages safely away before sitting at the tea table.

“哥哥……哥哥他……昨晚亲口告诉我，他心里有喜欢的人，只想把我当妹妹看待……”
Only once Qiao Mengrong sat down did Qianli Xun get a clear look at her: faint red rims around her eyes, pale cheeks. Alarm tugged at her. "You have been crying—what happened?"

越担心什么，什么越容易发生，申简辰还是向梦容透露真情。从梦容的表现来看，申简辰并未说出心中之人到底是谁。
Mengrong's eyes reddened darker, and fresh tears spilled over.

千里寻不知该对梦容说些什么，好像任何安慰的话从她口中说出都不合适。
Qianli Xun pulled out a silk handkerchief and gently dabbed her cheeks. "Do not cry—tell me what happened."

苦荞在茶桌旁做茶，郡主的话一个字不落进入她的耳朵。思忖道：“仪王心中喜欢之人肯定是我家小姐，要是被郡主知道，估计不会再与我家小姐做朋友，好替她们担心。”
"Brother... Brother told me straight to my face last night that he has someone else he loves, and only wants to treat me as a little sister..."

乔梦容见淑真不语，继续说：“淑真，你以前给我提醒过，让我多个心眼儿，我没当回事，现在想来，是我太过信任哥哥，快帮我出出主意，我该怎么办？”
What she had dreaded most had come to pass—Shen Jianchen had told Mengrong the truth. Judging from Mengrong's manner, though, he had not revealed who that person was.

千里寻不得不开口：“我都向你解释过，是我识人不准，仪王不是我说的那种人……”
Qianli Xun froze, every soothing word feeling hollow and false on her tongue.

“不，我反而觉得你看人十分准确，哥哥他与我有婚约，心里却喜欢上别人，他还说，即使以后成亲，依然会把我当妹妹看待，如果我有喜欢的人，便放我自由，你听听，他说的什么话？分明从来没喜欢过我，呜呜……！”乔梦容说着又哭起来。
Over at the tea table, Kuqiao caught every syllable and panicked inside: Prince Yi's beloved was clearly Miss! If Her Highness found out, their friendship was toast!

原来申简辰已将一切想清楚，他会为赵朴照顾这个妹妹，但不可能像赵朴一样爱上她。
Seeing Shuzhen silent, Qiao Mengrong went on. "You warned me once before to stay sharp, Shuzhen, and I brushed it off. Looking back, I trusted Brother too blindly. Help me think—what do I do now?"

千里寻既心疼梦容，又能理解申简辰。她唯一要做的就是不给申简辰留有任何希望，没有希望，说不定哪天他会全心全意对待梦容。
Forced to speak, Qianli Xun said, "I already told you back then I misread him—Prince Yi is not that kind of man..."

“别难过，依我看，仪王也是有责任心之人，即使以后身边有其他女子，也不会弃你不顾，你要对他有信心。”
"No! You read him dead right! Betrothed to me, yet his heart wandered to someone else! He even said after we marry he will still treat me like a sister, and if I ever love another man he will let me go! Listen to that—he never loved me at all! Wuuu!" She dissolved into sobs again.

乔梦容擦擦眼泪，忍住哭声：“以前你那样说哥哥，肯定知道他有喜欢的女子，能不能告诉我，哥哥心中的人到底是谁？”
So Shen Jianchen had mapped everything out: he would look after Zhao Pu's sister for him, yet he could never love her the way Zhao Pu had. Qianli Xun ached for Mengrong and understood Shen Jianchen both. Her only course was leaving Shen Jianchen zero scrap of hope; maybe once hope died, he would turn his full heart to Mengrong.

千里寻还未如何，苦荞却不小心把茶盏打翻。
"Do not despair," she said. "Prince Yi is a man of duty. Even if another woman enters his life, he will never cast you aside. Keep faith in him."

原来苦荞一边做茶，一边专注听二人对话，听到佳和郡主的问题，一紧张，力道用错，不小心用茶筅把茶盏打翻。她赶紧收拾好，重新做一盏。
Wiping her tears and choking back her sobs, Qiao Mengrong looked straight at her. "When you warned me about Brother before, you must have known he fancied someone. Tell me—who is the woman in Brother's heart?"

千里寻听到茶盏打翻的声音，向茶桌旁瞧去，见苦荞慌张的样子，知道她正为自己担忧，因而并未批评。
Before Qianli Xun could blink, a sharp clatter rang out from the tea table—Kuqiao had knocked the tea bowl clean over!

面对梦容的问题，千里寻既不能回避，也不能照实说，她选择善意的谎言：“我……我当然不知，说不定仪王是哄你，你别把他说的话太当回事，搞得自己难受。反正你们有婚约，你必然嫁给他。即使仪王真的有喜欢的人，也只会纳妾，你是官家赐婚，在仪王府的地位无人能撼动。把心放在肚子里，好好等着做仪王妃。”
Listening hard while whisking, Kuqiao had flinched at the princess's question and tipped the bowl with her bamboo whisk. Flushing, she scrambled to mop it up and start a fresh bowl.

梦容充满怀疑：“你真的不知？那为何以前会对我说那些话？”
Glancing over at Kuqiao's panicked face, Qianli Xun knew the maid was sweating on her behalf and said nothing to scold her. Unable to dodge Mengrong's gaze yet unable to tell the truth, she chose a white lie. "I... of course I have no idea! Maybe Prince Yi was just teasing you—do not take it to heart and make yourself miserable. With your betrothal locked in, you are bound to marry him. Even if he truly fancies someone, she could only ever be a concubine. You hold His Majesty's decree; nobody can shake your seat as Prince Yi's primary consort. Set your heart at ease and get ready to be Princess Consort Yi."

“都说了，我是误会仪王。”千里寻继续说着善意的谎言，“以前见仪王看到美女会多瞧几眼，才误会，现在我收回以前说过的话。”
Doubt flickered in Mengrong's eyes. "You truly do not know? Then why warn me like that before?"

千里寻装得真诚，乔梦容不得不信：“好吧，我信你。哥哥心中已有人，是改变不了的事实，我一定要搞清楚，他心中的人到底是谁。淑真，你可要帮我。”
"I told you, I misjudged Prince Yi," Qianli Xun pressed on with her lie. "Seeing him glance an extra second at pretty women gave me the wrong impression. I take back every word I said back then."

苦荞这次倒是将茶盏扶稳，并未因紧张而打翻。
She looked so earnest that Qiao Mengrong had no choice but to believe her. "Alright, I trust you. Still, Brother having someone else in his heart is a hard fact, and I have to find out who she is. You have to help me, Shuzhen."

千里寻勉强地点点头：“我会帮你，可你让我帮什么？”
This time Kuqiao kept a death grip on the fresh tea bowl so it would not tip.

“帮我找出哥哥心中喜欢的人。”
Qianli Xun forced a stiff nod. "I will help—what do you want me to do?"

“找到……你会怎样？”
"Help me track down the woman Brother loves."

“我……我不知道，也许……也许我会给她些钱财，让她离开哥哥。”
"And once you find her... what will you do?"

梦容就是善良，根本想不到龌龊的手段，搞得千里寻更是自责：“好，我帮你找，可我不能保证一定会找到。”
"I... I do not know. Maybe... maybe offer her silver to leave Brother."

“谢谢你淑真。”乔梦容抓住淑真的手，“在我最无助的时候，还有你能帮我。”
Mengrong was too decent to even imagine nasty schemes, piling fresh guilt onto Qianli Xun. "Alright, I will help you look—though I cannot promise I will find her."

千里寻也抓住梦容的手，似乎想要牢牢抓住她们的友情：“放心，我会帮你，让你完完全全拥有你的哥哥。”
"Thank you, Shuzhen." Gripping her friend's hands tight, Qiao Mengrong whispered, "When I feel most helpless, at least I still have you."

乔梦容很感动，认为有淑真这样的好友真是幸运。
Qianli Xun gripped back, as if clinging to their fragile friendship. "Rest easy. I will help you keep your brother all to yourself."

苦荞终于将茶做好，小心翼翼端上来，给郡主放在面前，然后站在小姐身边，仔细观察郡主，她看上去确实不知道仪王心里的人是自家小姐。
Deeply touched, Qiao Mengrong counted herself blessed to have a friend like Shuzhen.

千里寻松开梦容的手：“你用过茶后，在望月轩用过午饭再回去，如何？”
Kuqiao carried the fresh tea over with utmost care, set it before the princess, and watched from beside her mistress—sure enough, Her Highness had no inkling the rival was standing right in front of her.

尽管淑真并未解决多大问题，乔梦容的情绪还是比刚来时缓和许多，她端起茶盏，品了一口茶，回道：“我出来得匆忙，哥哥还不知，需赶快回去。”
Releasing Mengrong's hands, Qianli Xun asked, "Stay for lunch after tea, alright?"

梦容还是担心她的哥哥，既如此，千里寻也不好再挽留。她决定与申简辰见一面，劝说他不要对自己有任何期望。
Though no real solution had been found, Mengrong's mood had calmed noticeably since arriving. Taking a sip of tea, she shook her head. "I slipped out in a hurry without telling Brother. I ought to get back soon."

乔梦容品完茶后，千里寻将她送至门外，再送上马车。
Seeing how her friend still worried over her brother, Qianli Xun did not press her to stay. Instead, she made up her mind to meet Shen Jianchen face-to-face and urge him to give up all hope of her.

目送马车走远后，千里寻问苦荞：“白风今日可会来见你？”
Once Mengrong finished her tea, Qianli Xun walked her out to her carriage and watched it roll down the lane before turning to Kuqiao. "Is Bai Feng coming to see you today?"

苦荞小心回道：“本来是昨日来，乔家举行婚礼，他需跟随王爷参加，就没来。至于今日，我也不确定。”
"He meant to come yesterday, yet had to escort His Highness to the Qiao wedding," Kuqiao replied with caution. "Not sure about today."

“若见到白风，让他给仪王带句话，酉时西湖边的永泰茶楼见。”
"If he shows up, send word through him to Prince Yi: meet me at Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake at the Hour of the Rooster."

千里寻说完转身进了望月轩，她已没心情女扮男装去赌坊体验生活，还是好好想想与申简辰见面时该说些什么。
Turning back into Wangyue Pavilion, Qianli Xun had lost all appetite for disguising herself to research gambling dens; she needed the afternoon to decide what to say to Shen Jianchen.
