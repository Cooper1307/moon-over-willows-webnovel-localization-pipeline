## Chapter 210: Turning the Tables on the Interrogation

Delighted to hear Eldest Sister-in-Law's son suffer wedding-night humiliation, both aunts hid snickers behind silk handkerchiefs.

Spotting their glee, Qianli Xun pivoted sharp: "Both aunts look tickled pink—care to share the joke so Grandmother can laugh too?"

Old Madam Xia whipped a blistering glare onto both daughters-in-law. "What is so funny?! Think your precious sons outshine Haoyu?!"

Madam Bao scrambled to defend herself: "Haodong may not sweet-talk Old Madam like Haoyu, yet he shoulders Chen family commerce as Eldest Uncle's right hand!"

Madam Cui jumped right in: "My Haofei skips trade yet hits his books hard—might win imperial rank someday and bring honor to the Chens!"

Thrilled to derail their ambush—and spotting her golden opening to coax the matriarch!—Qianli Xun pivoted smoothly to praise: "When Grandmother held up this massive empire solo in your youth, it took grit few could imagine! Your name rings across Lin'an no quieter than mine as poetess—every merchant in town gives Grandmother thumbs-up!"

Bull's-eye! A rare, genuine smile broke across Old Madam Xia's wrinkled face, even as she tried staying stern. "Think sweet talk erases your blunders?"

Savoring her first taste of grandma-coaxing magic, Qianli Xun laid it on smoother still: "Had Grandmother stayed locked inside inner boudoirs like ordinary sheltered ladies, how could you ever have defended the Chen empire? You HAD to step outside and face the wider world—your vision and breadth of mind leave both aunts miles in the dust!"

Memories surged back over Old Madam Xia: left widowed with three young sons while clan brothers clawed to split the family fortune, defying her dying husband's final plea to keep the estate whole—only her iron nerve had held the Chens together!

Stung at being called narrow-minded by a two-day bride, Madam Bao nudged sharply: "Old Madam, surely two lines of flattery will not let her walk free?!"

Snapping back from memory, Old Madam Xia raised her chin. "Save the honeyed talk—own your faults, move Haoyu back into the main hall, and consummate properly!"

Unflustered, Qianli Xun pressed straight ahead: "The *Book of Changes* says: 'The family that accumulates goodness is sure to have superabundant happiness, and the family that accumulates evil is sure to have superabundant misery.' [TN: Translated by James Legge] I understand Grandmother doting on your grandson—yet since I met Chen Haoyu, has he done ONE decent thing? Harassing decent girls, haunting brothels and gambling dens, running with lowlife riffraff—aren't you terrified a grandson like that runs the whole Chen fortune into the ground?!"

Secretly cheering every jab against Haoyu, Madam Cui still put on a righteous frown. "Since when does a wife trash her own husband?! Look at your own faults first!"

Silent now, Old Madam Xia leaned forward waiting for her bride's play.

"I already said—I committed zero fault!" Qianli Xun declared boldly, grabbing the exact mandate her in-laws had handed her last night. "Banishing Chen Haoyu out back and skipping consummation is my way of disciplining your wayward heir! Redecorating the entire courtyard into refined scholarly calm—naming his room *Boya Study*, 'Erudite and Refined'—aims to soak better character into his bones! If Grandmother and Aunts prefer keeping Chen Haoyu rotten, say the word: I rip the courtyard back to gaudy gold, hand him the main hall, move out back myself—and leave giving the Chens grandchildren to somebody else!"

Raising a hand to hush both aunts about to erupt, Old Madam Xia nodded dryly. "Seeing you went to all that trouble for Haoyu's sake, I waive count two. Clever mouth—now let us hear your excuse for grabbing Haoyu's assets!"

"Simple!" Qianli Xun shot right back. "I refused to marry into this house—CHEN HAOYU dropped to both knees begging me, offering every asset in his name just to get me into that bridal sedan!"

Already knowing the rigged plot, Madam Bao clamped shut; clueless Madam Cui burst out: "Lies! Haoyu hated marrying you—why on earth would he kneel begging?!"

Time to drop the bomb! "Looks like Chen Haoyu kept you all in the dark! He confessed straight to me: he gambled with Master Jiang and LOST THE ENTIRE CHEN FAMILY FORTUNE! Master Jiang offered one way out: marry me, or kiss Chen wealth goodbye! Marrying into your house saved EVERY SINGLE COIN you own—his little private slice next to the whole Chen empire is one hair off nine oxen, yet here you are haggling over pennies! Hardly worthy of Lin'an's richest house!"

Shock flashed across Old Madam Xia's sharp eyes. "Miss Zhu's hand in marriage outweighed our entire Chen fortune?!"

Coaxing on cue, Qianli Xun beamed: "Trust someone who steered great enterprises to nail the heart of the puzzle in one second! Puzzled me too—famed poetess or not, how do I equal the Chen estate? Asked your grandson straight, and he swore he and Master Jiang were fighting over a brothel courtesan—Master Jiang figured marrying fierce ME would lock Chen Haoyu out of Chunxiang Brothel, handing the courtesan straight to him! Doesn't Master Jiang sound cracked in the head, Grandmother?"

Right on cue, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan hurried into the back courtyard—tipped off fast by Matron Wei after she exited!

"Stop giving Shuzhen grief, Mother!" Chen Jianglin stepped straight in. "I approved every single thing she did!"

"Come back to my rooms with both sisters-in-law, Mother—let us talk calmly," Yao Qingyan reached for her arm.

Seeing son and daughter-in-law together lit a fresh fire under Old Madam Xia! "All you two ever do is fawn over each other! Son and bride unconsummated—you don't care! Courtyard flipped upside down—you don't care! Haoyu vanished out on the streets—you don't care! Nearly gambled away the entire Chen fortune—you STILL don't care! And you dare stroll in lecturing ME?!"

"Calm down, Mother," Chen Jianglin chuckled uneasily. "Wild as Haoyu gets, he would never gamble away Chen holdings..."

"After your father died, I raised three sons solo without producing ONE rotten rake—and look at YOU!" Pushing shakily to her feet on her maids' arms, Old Madam Xia jabbed a trembling finger at Chen Jianglin. "Grown father running a whole empire, orbiting your wife nonstop while ignoring your own child! Spent half that devotion raising Haoyu and he would never have ended up like this!"

"Unfair, Mother—every time I tried disciplining Haoyu, YOU stepped in to shield him! Out of filial duty, Qingyan and I backed off!"

"Dare hide behind filial duty?! Real filial duty means quitting clinging to your wife's skirt and leaving Haoyu tossed aside!"

Watching from the side, Qianli Xun blinked—*Whoa, Old Madam actually RESENTS her son and daughter-in-law loving each other?! What backstory lives there?!* Grateful the firestorm had pivoted straight off her back, she leaned back enjoying the show.

Yao Qingyan dropped straight to her knees! "All my fault, Mother! Do not stay angry—starting tonight Jianglin and I split courtyards and take in several concubines for him so he stops orbiting me!"

Dropping back onto her chair furious, Old Madam Xia pointed hard at her: "Call THAT apologizing?! Paint me as some vicious mother-in-law tearing loving spouses apart?!"

Chen Jianglin dropped to his knees beside his wife: "Ease your anger, Mother!"

"Wild as Haoyu is, in some ways neither of you holds a candle to him!" Old Madam Xia snapped. "Love kneeling? Stay down there!"

Puzzled by that cryptic dig—unable to crack what she meant yet—Qianli Xun watched the matriarch's fire ebb down into quiet exhaustion as she turned back to her granddaughter-in-law with a markedly softer voice: "Since you defended every one of those three counts cleanly, I drop the matter. From this hour on, nobody in Chen Manor breathes another word about these three issues."

*Wait—did Old Madam stage this whole trial just to shut down household gossip FOR me?* No way—she threw my tea bowl yesterday! Definitely overthinking!

"No counting on Haoyu's useless parents," Old Madam Xia went on smoothly. "Since you claim to be disciplining Haoyu for the Chens, discipline him! Tomorrow marks day three—your return visit to Zhu Manor. Showing up solo shames both houses before Prefect Zhu. Go drag Haoyu home so he escorts you tomorrow—let me see how well you handle him!" Rising on her maids' arms, she snapped at her kneeling son: "Go ask Master Jiang face-to-face WHY he played that wager with Haoyu!" Throwing one cold "Hmph!" at her kneeling daughter-in-law, Old Madam Xia stalked out.

"Safe walk, Grandmother!" Qianli Xun called—silently groaning at walking straight into her own trap over disciplining Chen Haoyu!

Once Old Madam Xia cleared the gate, Qianli Xun hurried to help her mother-in-law up alongside the two aunts (while Qinggua alone hoisted Chen Jianglin). Smoothing their robes, Madam Cui tutted about disciplining Haoyu, and Madam Bao smugly offered parenting tips citing Haodong—until Yao Qingyan cut them both off crisp: "Dusk is falling; run along, Sisters—I need words with Shuzhen."

Inviting her in-laws into Suxin Thatched Hall, Qianli Xun smiled as both marveled at the refined ink-scented study replacing gaudy luxury. Sitting down, Yao Qingyan pressed immediately for the full story of Haoyu's gambling catastrophe.

Hearing every detail left both parents pale with anger—vowing to thrash the prodigal son the second he surfaced. Soothing them, Qianli Xun reasoned calmly that Master Jiang clearly cared nothing for Chen assets and had only toyed with Chen Haoyu, meaning he likely already held his contract back. (Wincing slightly at her formal use of "Chen Haoyu" instead of a wife's warmth, both parents kept polite silence.)

"Shuzhen makes solid sense," Chen Jianglin nodded. "Still, I am calling at the Qiantang Gang myself tomorrow to verify."

"Definitely go," Yao Qingyan agreed, rising to bid Shuzhen goodnight. "And track Haoyu down for tomorrow's home visit—it is late, we will leave you be."

Seeing her in-laws politely past Moxiang Courtyard's gate, Qianli Xun turned right back inside with zero intention of hunting down Chen Haoyu. Play tutor to the Chen heir? Hilarious! Agreeing politely to Old Madam was one thing; obeying her signed non-interference pact was another. Tossing the errand clean out of mind, she sat back down at her desk to write her huaben—who said she could not visit Zhu Manor solo tomorrow?
