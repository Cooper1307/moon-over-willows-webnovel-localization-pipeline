## Chapter 30: Drum Songs and a Run-In with the Dandy

乡下的第一个晚上，千里寻睡得相当踏实。
Her first night in the countryside, Qianli Xun slept like a rock.

当她睁开眼睛时，天刚刚放亮，太阳还未出山，干脆再睡一会儿。这一睡，反而做了一个梦，梦中她身着婚纱，正与申简辰举行婚礼，很多人前来为他们祝福。
Waking at first light before sunrise, she rolled over to catch a bit more sleep—and fell straight into a dream: wearing a wedding gown, standing beside Shen Jianchen at their wedding surrounded by cheering guests.

当申简辰将戒指戴在她的手上时，千里寻猛然醒来，她想让自己再次睡去，可怎么也睡不着，美好的梦境就这样没了。
Just as Shen Jianchen slipped the ring onto her finger, she jolted wide awake! Squeezing her eyes shut, she tried diving back in, yet sleep refused to return—the sweet dream gone like smoke.

她坐起来，怅然若失，自语道：“申简辰，你在哪里？我好想谈恋爱，为什么我不想谈恋爱时，你在我面前晃来晃去，我想时，却连个人影都没有？”
Sitting up hollow-hearted, she murmured aloud: "Shen Jianchen, where are you? I actually want to fall in love now—why'd you hover around nonstop back when I didn't care, and vanish without a trace the second I do?"

又转念一想，她已是朱淑真，即使见到申简辰，他也无法认出她是千里寻；如若申简辰也像她一样穿越而来，估计自己见了，也无法认出。原本相熟的两人，因一场地震于另一个时代丧生，穿越而来，又无法相见，人生的悲哀莫过于此。
Then sober reality crept in: wearing Zhu Shuzhen's face, even standing right in front of Shen Jianchen he'd never recognize her—and if he had transmigrated into another body too, she wouldn't recognize him either! Two souls who knew each other inside out, killed together in one quake, flung into the past unable to recognize each other—what crueler irony existed?

苦荞早早起来，先回家为小姐做好早饭，用竹篮提着走进竹屋。听到里面传出小姐的叹息声，便知小姐已醒，放下竹篮，进到里屋。
Up early, Kuqiao walked home to cook breakfast and returned carrying a bamboo basket. Hearing a sigh inside, she set the basket down and stepped into the bedroom.

“小姐醒了，吃过早饭，我带您去各处逛逛，保准您心情越来越好。”
"Awake, Miss? After breakfast I'll show you around the area—guaranteed to lift your spirits!"

千里寻穿好衣裳，只让苦荞简单为她梳了发髻，没有簪任何发饰，也没有涂任何胭脂水粉，只有如此，才有入乡随俗之感。
Dressing in plain clothes, Qianli Xun had Kuqiao tie a simple bun with zero hairpins and zero makeup—blending right into village life.

收拾完毕后，来到桌前。苦荞已将笔墨纸砚收起，摆好早饭，一碗散发着竹香的软粥，一盘竹笋干炒肉。
Out front, Kuqiao cleared the writing tools and laid breakfast on the low table: steaming rice porridge scented with bamboo and a plate of stir-fried dried bamboo shoots with pork.

千里寻坐在软垫上，瞅着那盘菜，心想，苦荞的家人估计平日不舍得吃肉，这道肉菜应是专门为她而做。
Eyeing the pork, Qianli Xun knew Kuqiao's family rarely ate meat—cooked special just for her.

“苦荞，记得把我的伙食费给伯父伯母，不能让你的家人为我破费。”
"Kuqiao, make sure you pay your parents board money—don't let them spend out of pocket for me."

“小姐放心，伙食费我给着呢，乡下没什么好吃的，只怕小姐不习惯。”
"Already paying them, Miss! Just worried simple country fare won't suit you."

“我口粗，吃什么都行。”
"I'm easy—eat anything."

千里寻抬头朝门外瞧去，眼前尽是一片绿色，一根根竹子直立挺拔，向上生长，无形中透出一股生命之力，梦境带来的失落感因这股力量去了大半。她嘴角露出笑容，拿起筷子开始享用早餐。
Looking out the doorway at towering emerald stalks thrusting skyward filled with raw vitality, she felt half the dream's ache dissolve. With a faint smile, she picked up her chopsticks and ate.

早餐刚享用到一半，传来唱曲儿的声音，时不时伴有打鼓的节奏。因传来的声音不是很清晰，千里寻听不清到底唱些什么内容。
Halfway through breakfast, singing drifted over on the breeze punctuated by rhythmic drumbeats—too far to catch the lyrics.

“外面怎么有唱曲儿的？”
"Somebody singing outside?"

“哦，是唱鼓词的，经常到各村表演，挣些糊口的小钱。”
"Oh, a drum-song performer! Travels village to village earning enough to get by."

千里寻听说过鼓子词，她来了兴致，迅速吃完剩下的饭菜，放下碗筷：“苦荞，先不忙着收拾，随我出去听曲儿。”
Having read about Song drum songs, Qianli Xun perked up, polished off her bowl fast, and set her chopsticks down. "Leave the dishes for now, Kuqiao—let's go listen!"

她们循着声音来到唱曲儿之地，在村口处，有一小片空地，已经有许多人围坐一旁认真听曲儿。苦荞找到一块石头，把丝帕铺上：“小姐，坐这儿。”
Following the rhythm to a small clearing at the village entrance, they found a crowd already gathered around. Kuqiao spread her silk handkerchief over a flat rock. "Sit right here, Miss."

千里寻坐下，瞧着唱曲儿的老人，是位盲人，坐在一个木凳上，左手扶着一个鼓，右手拿着打鼓的木棒，说唱结合。说词说得行云流水，唱词唱得悦耳动听，既不像千里寻听过的戏曲，更不像流行歌曲，要说介于二者之间，好像也不大合适，反正是一种她从未听过的唱腔。
Sitting down, Qianli Xun watched the performer—a blind old man perched on a wooden stool, steadying a small drum in his left hand and tapping beats with a wooden stick in his right, weaving spoken narrative and song together. His patter flowed smooth as water, his melodies rang clear and moving—neither opera nor pop, an ancient vocal cadence she had never heard before.

最令千里寻感到神奇的是，乐器只有鼓，就那么简单敲一下，尽显节奏之美。认真听那唱词，原来负鼓盲翁唱的是汉代蔡邕蔡伯喈的故事，讲他考中状元，不认发妻，娶了丞相之女。
Most mesmerizing was how a single drum could carry such gripping rhythm. Listening closely to the lyrics, she realized the blind drummer was performing the tale of Han scholar Cai Yong—passing first in the imperial exams, disowning his loyal wife, and marrying the chancellor's daughter!

千里寻心里暗笑：“这故事简直是毁了蔡邕的美名，完全把他虚构成一个宋代的陈世美。”
Qianli Xun chuckled inside: Poor Cai Yong's name dragged straight through the mud—turned into a Song-dynasty Chen Shimei!

本想等负鼓盲翁唱完后，给蔡邕正名。又转念一想，鼓子词也是文学，既是文学，虚构一下前人的事迹倒也正常，她千里寻写网络小说时，不知道虚构过多少人的故事，鼓子词与她写的小说比起来真是小巫见大巫，便放弃了为蔡邕正名的想法。也许蔡邕在天有灵，知道用他杜撰的故事娱乐大众，说不定还甚感欣慰。
For a beat she considered clearing Cai Yong's historical name once the song ended—then thought better of it. Drum songs were entertainment, and fiction bent history all the time; how many plots had she invented herself as a web novelist? Small potatoes next to modern web fiction! Maybe Cai Yong's spirit up above didn't even mind giving common folk a good show.

再观察周围的村民，大家听得很认真，时不时发出笑声和慨叹。千里寻认识到诗词是宋代高雅文人喜欢的愉情方式，而普通百姓则更喜欢通俗易懂的鼓子词表演。
Watching the villagers laugh, gasp, and sigh along, Qianli Xun had a lightbulb moment: classical ci poetry belonged to refined Song literati, whereas everyday people craved lively, down-to-earth storytelling!

千里寻借着朱淑真的身份时不时写诗词高雅一回，心里却清楚这些诗词不属于她。
Borrowing Zhu Shuzhen's verse for salons was fine, yet deep down those poems didn't belong to her.

她突然想在这个时代写出属于自己的作品，负鼓盲翁的表演给她带来灵感，心里想道：“对啊，普通百姓如此喜欢通俗文学，我为何不在这个时代写写故事，供大家娱乐，也好发挥我的特长。”
An itch flared in her fingertips—she wanted to write her own stories in this era! Inspired by the blind drummer, she thought: Ordinary folks eat up popular tales—why not write stories for them here and put my own real talent to work?!

正想着，听到一个女子喊叫的声音：“爷爷！救救我！坏人要抓我！”
Mid-epiphany, a terrified girl's scream sliced the air: "Grandpa! Save me! Bad men are after me!"

盲翁停下表演，所有人都朝声音传来的方向看去。
The blind drummer froze; every head in the clearing whipped round.

千里寻也站起来，她看到一个如花似玉的姑娘朝这边跑来，身上虽穿着打补丁的衣裳，却难掩靓丽的容貌。
Qianli Xun sprang up as a breathtaking young girl sprinted toward them—clad in patched rags that couldn't dim her striking beauty.

姑娘跑得气喘吁吁，身后有四五个壮汉在追。她使出全身力气，冲过人群，扑到盲翁的怀里：“爷爷！那些坏人要抓我！”
Gasping for air with four or five burly thugs pounding on her heels, she burst through the crowd and threw herself into the blind old man's arms: "Grandpa! They're trying to drag me away!"

大家还没反应过来，四个壮汉已冲进人群。
Four thugs shoved straight into the ring.

其中一个说：“喂，瞎老头，赶紧劝说你孙女，让他从了陈公子，只要跟着陈公子，吃香的喝辣的，也省得你这么大年纪还到处卖唱为生。”
One barked: "Hey, blind old man! Talk sense into your granddaughter and hand her over to Young Master Chen! Go with him and she eats like royalty—beats dragging your old bones around singing for scraps!"

盲翁扶住孙女，气道：“我卖唱凭本事吃饭，总比让我孙女跟着你们挣昧良心的钱好，我就不信，你们敢光天化日之下抢人！小心我去官府告你们！”
Shielding his granddaughter, the blind man shook with rage: "I earn honest bread by my craft—better than selling my granddaughter for dirty coin! Dare snatch her in broad daylight? I'll drag you straight to the yamen!"

周围的人也开始斥责壮汉：“你们这些人真是无法无天！”
Villagers broke into angry murmurs: "Lawless bullies!"

“爷孙两个那么可怜，你们就饶了他们吧。”
"Poor grandpa and girl—leave them alone!"

“你们这些地痞流氓，就知道欺侮老百姓！”
"Nothing but local thugs preying on honest folk!"

四个壮汉露出臂膀上的肌肉，在众人面前展示拳头，露出凶狠的表情。围观的百姓都不敢再言语，有的不愿多管闲事，转身回家；有的则远远躲开，怕被伤到。
Flexing thick arms and shaking fists with ugly snarls, the four thugs shut the crowd right up; half the villagers hurried home wanting no trouble, the rest backed far off.

千里寻并不怕，继续站在原地，她想先看看这群人到底想干什么，该出手时，定将他们打得服服帖帖。有小姐撑腰，苦荞也是面不改色。
Qianli Xun stood her ground without blinking, sizing up the thugs ready to flatten them the second they moved—and backed by her mistress, Kuqiao didn't flinch either.

壮汉调戏道：“喂，你们两个，怎么不后退？你这姑娘长得不错，难道想代替她跟了陈公子？陈公子正在马车里等着，我这就带你过去。”
One thug leered straight at them: "Hey, you two—why aren't you backing off? Pretty little face there—volunteering to go with Young Master Chen in her place? His carriage is right down the road, let me escort you!"

千里十分不屑：“老娘本来还想忍忍，没想到你偏要送上门来激老娘，别怪老娘不客气！”
Qianli Xun snorted cold: "I was trying to hold back, and you had to walk right up asking for it—don't blame me for what happens next!"

趁壮汉没注意，千里寻猛地一拳打在壮汉的脸上，不仅把脸打得乌青，嘴角流出血，也因拳头的力度，壮汉退出两米多。
Before the thug blinked, she drove a lightning punch square into his jaw—bruising his cheek black, splitting his lip, and sending him staggering over two paces back!

壮汉稳住，用手擦擦嘴角的血迹，对其他人喊道：“一起上！”
Wiping blood off his mouth, he roared: "Jump her together!"

四个壮汉环绕在千里寻周围，想着聚集四人之力定能将眼前的女子打倒，让她知道多管闲事的坏处，他们一起用拳头向千里寻袭击而来。
All four surrounded Qianli Xun, swinging fists from every side to teach this meddling girl a lesson.

千里寻轻轻一跃，拳脚连绵，犹如一阵狂风暴雨，又用变幻莫测的拳法组合快速而又准确地将四个壮汉分别击倒在地。
Leaping light off the ground, Qianli Xun unleashed a storm of crisp punches and kicks, weaving unpredictable Qianli Fist combos that dropped all four men flat onto the dirt in seconds!

有一壮汉咬紧牙关爬起来，全力出击，千里寻冷静面对他的攻势，以快速的拳脚将自不量力的壮汉逼退，接着那壮汉又一次倒在地上。
Gritting his teeth, one thug scrambled back up charging full force; cool and steady, Qianli Xun met him with rapid strikes that battered him backward straight onto the ground again.

周围的百姓欢呼起来：“东家打得好！打得得劲儿！打得呱呱叫！”
Villagers erupted in cheers: "Nice punch, Proprietor! Knock 'em flat! Brilliant!"

千里寻看着四个壮汉从地上爬起，一个个捂着伤口，一脸疼痛的样子，训斥道：“这是老娘的地盘，敢在老娘的地盘上欺负人，就是找打！再告诉你们，这一老一少以后老娘罩着，你们胆敢再欺负那姑娘，我的拳头可不是吃素的！”
Glaring down at the four thugs groaning and clutching bruises as they crawled up, Qianli Xun snapped: "This is MY turf! Bully people on my turf and you're begging for a beating! Listen close—this old man and his granddaughter are under my protection now! Touch one hair on her head again, and see what my fists do!"

四个壮汉见打不过，只能互相说道：“我们去找陈公子，让陈公子来收拾她。”
Outmatched, the four thugs muttered among themselves: "Let's tell Young Master Chen—let him handle her!"

“她那么厉害，陈公子肯定也打不过。”
"Strong as she is, Young Master Chen can't beat her either!"

“打不过也得说，否则陈公子会怪罪我们。”
"Still gotta report, or he'll skin us alive!"

“就是，让他来试试，知道那女的有多厉害，才不会责怪我们。”
"Right—let him see how fierce she is so he doesn't blame us!"

四人刚要退去，传来一个声音：“你们四个追个姑娘怎么这么久？让本公子好等！”
Just as they turned to flee, a familiar drawl floated over: "Took you four this long to grab one girl? Keeping me waiting!"

四人向身后看去，原来是陈公子亲自过来。
Glancing back, they saw Young Master Chen strolling up himself.

他们忍着痛，围上去：“陈公子，那女的半路杀出来，说这是她的地盘。她很厉害，您瞧，我们被她打成什么样？”
Limping over clutching aches, they whined: "Young Master Chen, that woman jumped out claiming this is her turf! She's a monster—look what she did to us!"

千里寻仔细瞧去，陈公子正是陈浩宇，真是冤家路窄。
One glance and Qianli Xun recognized Chen Haoyu—talk about bad pennies turning up!

陈浩宇开始没认出朱淑真，只因她穿着一身丫鬟衣裳，看到一旁的苦荞，才笑道：“怎么哪里都有你？一个大家闺秀跑到这种地方干什么？”
At first Chen Haoyu didn't recognize her in Kuqiao's plain cotton dress—until he spotted Kuqiao beside her and smirked: "How do you pop up everywhere? What's a noble lady doing slumming out here?"

千里寻很是不屑：“我还纳闷陈公子是谁，原来还是你这粒老鼠屎，到哪里都能搅坏一锅汤！”
Qianli Xun sneered right back: "Wondered which Young Master Chen—of course it's the one rat dropping ruining every pot of soup!"

被说成“老鼠屎”，陈浩宇很是气愤，他清楚打不过朱淑真，只能忍住怒气。
Burning at being called a rat dropping yet knowing full well he stood zero chance against her fists, Chen Haoyu swallowed his rage.

“小爷我今儿看在你的面子上，放了那姑娘。姑娘，别以为我放了你是好事，跟着本公子，你的日子才好过。撤！”
"Out of respect for you, I'll spare the girl today. Hey, girl—don't think you got lucky! Stick with me and you'd live in luxury. Move out!"

刚走出几步，陈浩宇回过头：“朱淑真，两次了，别让我第三次再碰见你，你的地盘是吧？明儿就不知道是谁的喽。”说完转身带着四个打手吹着口哨离去。
A few steps away, he looked back with a venomous smirk: "Twice now, Zhu Shuzhen—pray we don't make it three! Your turf, huh? Wait till tomorrow and see whose turf it really is!" Turning on his heel, he whistled his way off with his four bruised thugs.

千里寻真想不通，世上竟有如此猖狂之人。
Hard to believe anyone walked around this arrogant!

经历此事，盲翁无法再表演下去，孙女搀扶他感谢千里寻：“谢谢姑娘相助，老朽无以为报，若喜欢听曲儿，改日老朽带孙女登门专为恩人表演。”
Shaken, the blind drummer could perform no more; leaning on his granddaughter, he bowed low. "Thank you for saving us, young lady! Nothing to repay you with—if you enjoy drum songs, let me bring my granddaughter to your door someday for a private performance."

盲翁的孙女跪在地上磕头：“谢谢姐姐帮我。”
The girl dropped to her knees kowtowing: "Thank you for saving me, Elder Sister!"

千里寻扶起女孩儿：“你叫什么名字？”
Pulling her up, Qianli Xun asked, "What's your name?"

“我叫夏果。”
"Xiaguo."

“名字不错。”千里寻瞅着夏果，真是个美人胚子，不由心生怜爱，“不如以后跟着我？”
"Pretty name!" Studying Xiaguo—a true natural beauty—pity stirred in Qianli Xun's chest. "How about coming to work for me?"

夏果感激不尽，又要跪下，被千里寻扶着：“别跪来跪去的，我不喜欢。不知家里还有什么人？”
Overwhelmed, Xiaguo started kneeling again; Qianli Xun caught her arms. "Quit kneeling—I hate kneeling! Who else is in your family?"

“还有奶奶和爹娘。”
"Grandmother and my parents."

“那便好，我把你要走，不至于没人照顾老人家。”
"Good—taking you won't leave your grandfather without help."

千里寻说着，给苦荞使了一个眼色。苦荞拿出一两银子塞到盲翁的怀里：“老人家，这是我家小姐给您打赏的。”
She shot Kuqiao a nod; Kuqiao slipped one tael of silver into the blind old man's hands. "A reward from my Miss, Grandpa."

盲翁摸着银两，激动道：“多了！要不了这么多！”
Feeling the heavy silver, the old man stammered: "Too much! Way too much!"

千里寻说：“我把您孙女从您身边要走，这些钱都少了，您老就拿着吧。”
"I'm taking your granddaughter away from your side—one tael's barely enough! Keep it!"

老人感激道：“小姐让夏果待在您身边，算是给这孩子找个去处，老朽感激不尽，怎能收您的银子？”
"Giving Xiaguo a safe place beside you is already a blessing beyond words, Miss—how could I take your silver?"

苦荞在一旁搭腔：“老人家，您别客气，再客气我家小姐可就不收夏果当丫鬟。”
Kuqiao jumped in playful: "Don't be polite, Grandpa—turn it down and Miss won't take Xiaguo as her maid!"

老人赶紧说：“好，好，不客气。”
"Alright, alright—no more refusing!" the old man hurried to accept.

千里寻让夏果先将盲翁送回家，安顿好后再来竹屋报道。
Qianli Xun told Xiaguo to escort her grandfather home first, settle things with her family, and report to the bamboo house afterward.

不能继续听曲儿，村民们都散去，村口恢复一片寂静，只剩下千里寻和苦荞。
With the performance cut short, the villagers dispersed, leaving quiet behind at the village edge—just Qianli Xun and Kuqiao.

越是由热闹转而寂静，越容易让人的内心出现些许空虚。对未曾恋爱过的千里寻来讲，此刻她想用爱情填补空虚，爱她的人，她想爱的人又在哪里？既然找不到，不如做些其他事情消磨空虚的感觉。
Silence rushing in right after excitement left a hollow ache inside. Never having loved once in her life, Qianli Xun felt an empty space craving warmth—where was the man meant to love her, the man she wanted to love? If she couldn't find him yet, better keep busy.

“苦荞，给我找些话本来。”
"Kuqiao, get me some huaben storybooks."

“小姐要它们做什么？”
"What do you want those for, Miss?"

“在竹屋待着无聊，用来打发时间。”
"Bored sitting around the bamboo house—something to pass the time."

“小姐以前从来不看这些下里巴人的故事。”
"You never read lowbrow tales like that before, Miss!"

“你还知道下里巴人这个成语，不愧是我千……”本来要说千里寻，甚觉不合适，“不愧是我朱淑真的丫鬟，不过，苦荞你记着，不管是阳春白雪还是下里巴人，只要老百姓喜欢就是好东西。”
"Even you know 'lowbrow'! Truly a maid of Qian—uh, of Zhu Shuzhen! Remember this, Kuqiao: highbrow art or lowbrow tales, if ordinary folks love reading it, it's good stuff!"

“好吧，我让苦蕨去城里给小姐买几本来。”
"Alright, I'll have Kujue buy a few in town for you."

千里寻叹口气：“唉，我现在不能离开竹林，否则亲自上街，要么去茶馆，或者去勾栏瓦肆逛逛，亲自听听说话这门艺术。现在只能麻烦你们兄妹两个。”
Qianli Xun sighed: "Pity I can't leave this bamboo grove for a month, or I'd hit town myself—visit teahouses and Goulan Wasi to hear live storytelling firsthand! Guess I'll have to trouble you and your brother."

“小姐不用跟我客气。”
"No trouble at all, Miss!"

千里寻又到竹林周围转了一圈儿，才回到竹屋。
Taking another stroll around the bamboo grove, Qianli Xun returned to her cottage.

苦荞安顿好小姐，去找苦蕨安排买书之事。安排完后，半路上碰到夏果打听小姐的住处，顺道把她带回去，将平日如何照顾小姐之事说了一遍，夏果用心一一记下。
Once her mistress was settled, Kuqiao went to give Kujue the book errand. On her way back, she bumped into Xiaguo asking directions to Miss's cottage, brought her right over, and walked her through every daily routine—which Xiaguo soaked up word for word.
