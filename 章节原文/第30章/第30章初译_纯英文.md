# Chapter 30: Listening to Drum Verses and Encountering a Dandy

On the first night in the countryside, Qianli Xun slept quite soundly.

When she opened her eyes, it was just dawn and the sun hadn't risen yet, so she decided to sleep a little longer. This sleep, instead, brought her a dream. In the dream, she was wearing a wedding dress, holding a wedding ceremony with Shen Jianchen, and many people came to bless them.

Just as Shen Jianchen was putting the ring on her hand, Qianli Xun suddenly woke up. She tried to fall asleep again, but couldn't no matter what. The beautiful dream was just gone like that.

She sat up, feeling lost, and said to herself, "Shen Jianchen, where are you? I really want to fall in love. Why were you hovering in front of me when I didn't want to fall in love, but now when I want to, there's not even a shadow of you?"

Then she thought again. She was already Zhu Shuzhen. Even if she met Shen Jianchen, he wouldn't be able to recognize her as Qianli Xun. If Shen Jianchen had also transmigrated like her, she probably wouldn't be able to recognize him either. Two people who were originally familiar with each other died in another era due to an earthquake, transmigrated here, but couldn't meet. There's no greater sorrow in life than this.

Kubiao got up early, first went home to make breakfast for the miss, and carried it in a bamboo basket to the bamboo house. Hearing the miss's sigh from inside, she knew the miss was awake. She put down the bamboo basket and went into the inner room.

"The miss is awake. After breakfast, I'll take you around to see the place. I guarantee your mood will get better and better."

Qianli Xun got dressed and only had Kubiao simply comb her hair into a bun, without any hair ornaments, and without applying any rouge or powder. Only this way would she feel like she was following local customs.

After getting ready, she came to the table. Kubiao had already put away the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone, and set out breakfast—a bowl of soft porridge with a bamboo fragrance, and a plate of stir-fried meat with dried bamboo shoots.

Qianli Xun sat on the soft cushion, looking at that dish of meat, thinking that Kubiao's family probably couldn't bear to eat meat on ordinary days. This meat dish must have been made specially for her.

"Kubiao, remember to give my food expenses to your uncle and aunt. I can't let your family spend money on me."

"Don't worry, Miss. I'm giving the food expenses. There's nothing good to eat in the countryside. I'm just afraid the miss won't be used to it."

"I'm not picky. I can eat anything."

Qianli Xun looked up toward the door. Before her was a sea of green. Bamboo stalks stood straight and tall, growing upward, revealing a life force invisibly. The sense of loss brought by the dream was mostly gone because of this power. A smile appeared on her lips. She picked up her chopsticks and began to enjoy breakfast.

She had just halfway through breakfast when the sound of singing came, occasionally accompanied by the rhythm of drumming. Because the sound wasn't very clear, Qianli Xun couldn't hear clearly what was being sung.

"Why is there singing outside?"

"Oh, it's someone performing drum verses. They often perform in various villages to earn a little money to make a living."

Qianli Xun had heard of drum verses. She became interested, quickly finished the remaining food, put down her bowl and chopsticks. "Kubiao, don't rush to clean up. Come with me to listen to the singing."

Following the sound, they came to the place where the singing was happening. At the village entrance, there was a small open area where many people were already sitting around, listening carefully. Kubiao found a stone and spread her silk handkerchief on it. "Miss, sit here."

Qianli Xun sat down and looked at the old man singing. He was blind, sitting on a wooden bench, holding a drum in his left hand and a drumstick in his right hand, combining speaking and singing. His speaking flowed smoothly, and his singing was pleasant to the ear. It was neither like the opera Qianli Xun had heard, nor like pop songs. Saying it was between the two didn't seem quite right either. Anyway, it was a singing style she had never heard before.

What amazed Qianli Xun most was that the only instrument was the drum. With just simple beats, it fully displayed the beauty of rhythm. Listening carefully to the lyrics, it turned out the blind drummer was singing the story of Cai Yong (courtesy name Bojie) from the Han Dynasty. It told how he passed the imperial examination as the top scholar, didn't recognize his first wife, and married the prime minister's daughter.

Qianli Xun secretly laughed in her heart. "This story simply ruined Cai Yong's good name, completely fabricating him into a Chen Shimei of the Song Dynasty."

She originally wanted to set the record straight for Cai Yong after the blind drummer finished. Then she thought again. Drum verses were also literature. Since it was literature, it was normal to fabricate the deeds of predecessors. When she, Qianli Xun, wrote web novels, she didn't know how many people's stories she had fabricated. Compared to the novels she wrote, the drum verses were really small potatoes. So she gave up the idea of setting the record straight for Cai Yong. Perhaps if Cai Yong had a spirit in heaven, knowing that stories fabricated about him were used to entertain the masses, he might even feel quite gratified.

Observing the villagers around, everyone was listening very carefully, occasionally laughing and sighing. Qianli Xun realized that poetry was a way of enjoyment favored by elegant literati in the Song Dynasty, while ordinary people preferred the easily understandable drum verse performances.

Qianli Xun, using Zhu Shuzhen's identity, occasionally wrote poetry to be elegant, but she knew clearly in her heart that these poems didn't belong to her.

She suddenly wanted to write her own works in this era. The blind drummer's performance brought her inspiration. She thought to herself, "That's right. Ordinary people like popular literature so much. Why don't I write stories in this era for everyone's entertainment, and also make use of my strengths?"

Just as she was thinking, she heard a woman's voice shouting, "Grandpa! Save me! Bad people are trying to catch me!"

The blind drummer stopped his performance, and everyone looked in the direction of the sound.

Qianli Xun also stood up. She saw a beautiful girl running toward them. Although she was wearing patched clothes, it couldn't hide her beautiful appearance.

The girl was panting from running, with four or five strong men chasing behind her. She used all her strength, rushed through the crowd, and threw herself into the blind drummer's arms. "Grandpa! Those bad people are trying to catch me!"

Before everyone could react, the four strong men had already rushed into the crowd.

One of them said, "Hey, blind old man, hurry up and persuade your granddaughter to follow Young Master Chen. As long as she follows Young Master Chen, she'll eat well and drink well. It will also save you from having to sing everywhere at your age to make a living."

The blind drummer held his granddaughter and said angrily, "I make a living by singing with my skills. It's better than having my granddaughter follow you to earn money against her conscience. I don't believe you dare to snatch people in broad daylight! Be careful, I'll report you to the authorities!"

The people around also began to scold the strong men. "You people are really lawless!"

"The grandfather and granddaughter are so pitiful. Just let them go."

"You hooligans just know how to bully common people!"

The four strong men showed the muscles on their arms, displayed their fists in front of everyone, and made fierce expressions. The surrounding people didn't dare to speak anymore. Some didn't want to get involved and turned to go home; others hid far away, afraid of being hurt.

Qianli Xun wasn't afraid. She continued to stand in place. She wanted to see first what these people really wanted to do. When it was time to act, she would definitely beat them into submission. With the miss backing her up, Kubiao also didn't change her expression.

One of the strong men teased, "Hey, you two, why aren't you backing off? You, girl, you're quite pretty. Do you want to take her place and follow Young Master Chen? Young Master Chen is waiting in the carriage. I'll take you there now."

Qianli Xun was very disdainful. "This queen originally wanted to hold back, but I didn't expect you to come to my door to provoke me. Don't blame this queen for being impolite!"

Taking advantage of the strong man's inattention, Qianli Xun suddenly punched him in the face. Not only did it bruise his face and make blood flow from the corner of his mouth, but because of the force of the punch, the strong man retreated more than two meters.

The strong man steadied himself, wiped the blood from the corner of his mouth with his hand, and shouted to the others, "Attack together!"

The four strong men surrounded Qianli Xun, thinking that gathering the strength of four people would definitely be able to defeat the woman in front of them and teach her the consequences of meddling in other people's business. They all attacked Qianli Xun with their fists.

Qianli Xun lightly leaped, her punches and kicks continuous, like a violent storm. Using unpredictable fist combinations, she quickly and accurately knocked the four strong men to the ground one by one.

One strong man gritted his teeth and climbed up, attacking with all his strength. Qianli Xun calmly faced his attack, using fast punches and kicks to force back the strong man who overestimated his abilities. Then the strong man fell to the ground again.

The surrounding people cheered. "Madam fights well! What a satisfying fight! What an amazing fight!"

Qianli Xun watched the four strong men climb up from the ground, each holding their wounds, their faces showing pain. She scolded them, "This is this queen's territory. You dare to bully people on this queen's territory? You're asking for a beating! Let me tell you again, this old man and young girl are under this queen's protection from now on. If you dare to bully that girl again, my fists are no joke!"

Seeing they couldn't win, the four strong men could only say to each other, "Let's go find Young Master Chen and have him deal with her."

"She's so strong. Young Master Chen definitely can't beat her either."

"We have to tell him even if we can't beat her. Otherwise, Young Master Chen will blame us."

"Right. Let him try. Once he knows how strong that woman is, he won't blame us."

Just as the four were about to leave, a voice came. "Why did it take you four so long to chase a girl? You made this young master wait!"

The four looked behind them. It turned out Young Master Chen had come in person.

Enduring the pain, they gathered around him. "Young Master Chen, that woman suddenly appeared and said this was her territory. She's very strong. Look, see how she beat us up?"

Qianli Xun looked carefully. Young Master Chen was none other than Chen Haoyu. Enemies meeting on a narrow road—what fate!

Chen Haoyu didn't recognize Zhu Shuzhen at first because she was wearing a maid's clothes. Seeing Kubiao beside her, he smiled. "Why are you everywhere? What is a young lady from a good family doing in a place like this?"

Qianli Xun was very disdainful. "I was wondering who Young Master Chen was. It turns out it's you, this rat dropping that spoils the whole pot of soup wherever it goes!"

Being called a "rat dropping," Chen Haoyu was very angry. But he knew he couldn't beat Zhu Shuzhen, so he could only suppress his anger.

"This young master will let that girl go today out of respect for you. Girl, don't think it's a good thing that I'm letting you go. If you follow this young master, you'll have a good life. Retreat!"

Having just walked a few steps, Chen Haoyu turned back. "Zhu Shuzhen, this is twice. Don't let me meet you a third time. Your territory, right? Tomorrow we'll see whose it is." With that, he turned and left with his four henchmen, whistling.

Qianli Xun really couldn't understand how there could be such arrogant people in the world.

After this incident, the blind drummer couldn't continue performing. His granddaughter supported him as he thanked Qianli Xun. "Thank you for your help, miss. This old man has nothing to repay you with. If you like listening to songs, another day this old man will bring his granddaughter to your door to perform specially for our benefactor."

The blind drummer's granddaughter knelt on the ground and kowtowed. "Thank you, sister, for helping me."

Qianli Xun helped the girl up. "What's your name?"

"My name is Xia Gu."

"Nice name." Qianli Xun looked at Xia Gu. She was really a beauty in the making. She couldn't help but feel affection. "Why not follow me from now on?"

Xia Gu was extremely grateful and was about to kneel again, but Qianli Xun held her. "Don't keep kneeling. I don't like it. Do you have any other family at home?"

"There's also my grandmother and parents."

"That's good. If I take you away, it won't leave the old people without care."

As Qianli Xun spoke, she gave Kubiao a look. Kubiao took out one tael of silver and stuffed it into the blind drummer's arms. "Old man, this is from our miss as a reward for you."

The blind drummer felt the silver and said excitedly, "It's too much! We don't need this much!"

Qianli Xun said, "I'm taking your granddaughter away from you. This money is still too little. Just take it, old man."

The old man said gratefully, "The miss letting Xia Gu stay by your side is finding a place for this child. This old man is extremely grateful. How can I take your silver?"

Kubiao chimed in from the side. "Old man, don't be polite. If you're too polite, our miss won't take Xia Gu as a servant."

The old man quickly said, "Alright, alright. I won't be polite."

Qianli Xun had Xia Gu first take the blind drummer home and settle him down before coming to the bamboo house to report.

Unable to continue listening to the songs, the villagers dispersed. The village entrance returned to silence, leaving only Qianli Xun and Kubiao.

The more the lively atmosphere turned to silence, the more easily a sense of emptiness appeared in one's heart. For Qianli Xun, who had never been in love, at this moment she wanted to use love to fill the emptiness. Where was the person who loved her, the person she wanted to love? Since she couldn't find them, she might as well do something else to pass the feeling of emptiness.

"Kubiao, find me some storybooks."

"What does the miss want them for?"

"Staying in the bamboo house is boring. I'll use them to pass the time."

"The miss never used to read these popular stories."

"You even know the idiom 'popular art—low art.' You're truly worthy of being my Qian..." She was about to say Qianli Xun but felt it wasn't appropriate. "You're truly worthy of being my, Zhu Shuzhen's, servant. But, Kubiao, remember this: whether it's high art or popular art, as long as common people like it, it's good."

"Alright, I'll have Ku Jue go to the city to buy a few books for the miss."

Qianli Xun sighed. "Sigh, I can't leave the bamboo forest now. Otherwise, I'd go to the streets myself, either to a teahouse or to the theaters and entertainment quarters, to listen to the art of storytelling in person. Now I can only trouble you two siblings."

"The miss doesn't need to be polite with me."

Qianli Xun walked around the bamboo forest again before returning to the bamboo house.

After settling the miss, Kubiao went to find Ku Jue to arrange the book purchase. After arranging it, on the way back, she met Xia Gu asking about the miss's residence. She brought her back along the way and told her how to take care of the miss on ordinary days. Xia Gu carefully memorized everything.
