## Chapter 190: Seeing Off a Friendship with Tears

话说白风从神柳处回到幽林别居，被喜兰叫到郡主房中。一路上他已猜到郡主叫他的原因，一再叮嘱自己千万不能说漏嘴。
果不其然，郡主开口说：“白风，你经常跟哥哥在一起，应该知道他常去哪里，老实告诉我，我定不会亏待你。”
白风装作轻松的样子：“王爷除待在幽林别居外，会去乔家、府衙，偶尔为公事会去朱府，对了，还去过望月轩，郡主都知道。”
乔梦容以为自己没将话说明白，更加直白地问：“哥哥说他有喜欢的人，你可知道是谁？”
“喜兰曾问过我，郡主今日又问，可我还是不知道。郡主，您别瞎想，我跟王爷这么久，从来没见过他对哪个女孩子像对您这么好。”白风甚觉违心，可又不得不如此说。
“哪是我瞎想？那天你也亲耳听到，是哥哥亲口说的，我看你是不愿告诉我，既如此，你走吧，我迟早会知道哥哥心中的人到底是谁。”
问不出所以然，乔梦容不知该如何是好，此刻除了喜兰，她连个帮手都没有。
白风离开后，乔梦容悲戚一阵儿，无意中瞧到桌上的喜帖，想到淑真，明日她就要成亲，不如去看看她，再与她聊聊心事。
乔梦容乘坐马车先去趟望月轩，下人说淑真已回到朱府，她便来到朱府。
竟然发现哥哥的马车停在外面，担忧的情绪立刻减少许多，原来哥哥是来朱府见知府大人，不如见完淑真再与哥哥一同回去。
朱府的下人见佳和郡主前来，既不能照实说，也不敢阻拦，只说仪王与朱家人都在会客厅。
乔梦容要求下人带路，与喜兰往会客厅方向而去。她万万没想到到达后竟然目睹哥哥拉着淑真的手，还说要带她离开。
乔梦容只觉心口被堵住，全身跟着变得无力，眼泪控制不住地流下来，用颤抖的声音说出那些话，然后向地上倒去……
申简辰动作迅速，梦容并未倒在地上，被他稳稳扶住。他抱起梦容，回头对千里寻说：“不要嫁！等着我！”
然后抱着梦容向朱府外匆忙走去……
千里寻忍住泪水，她不想再为感情而流泪，因文渊已流得够多。她只顾一味埋怨自己，如果她不对申简辰产生感情，一切就不会发生。
朱晞颜松口气，佳和郡主来得实在及时，晕得也恰到好处，见仪王不得不离开，带头行礼，大声说：“恭送仪王！”
其他人也跟着大声说：“恭送仪王！”
等仪王走远，朱家人连同韦嬷嬷都从地上爬起来。大家跪的时间有些长，起来不免适应一番。
陈文心见淑真愣在那里，一言不发，走过去：“淑真，就当刚才发生的是一场梦，晚上好好休息，明日还要成亲。”
“成亲？是，明日还要成亲。”千里寻像是提醒自己，继而担忧道，“嫂嫂，恐怕以后梦容不会再当我是朋友。”
陈文心安慰道：“不要想那么多……”
陈文心还未说完，被朱晞颜的声音打断：“不必对她废话！都回去吧！”
朱晞颜走到女儿跟前，指着她：“你给我记着，嫁到陈家后本本分分过日子！少去招惹仪王！！
朱景元担心爹收拾妹妹，上前扶好朱晞颜：“爹，您也跪累了，我扶您回屋休息。”
朱晞颜向女儿“哼”了一声，带着不满的情绪，被儿子扶着离开。
韦嬷嬷扶着向夫人，她只顾抹眼泪。见老爷已走，才来到女儿跟前：“淑真，娘不图你嫁得有多好，只希望你日子过得平平安安，不要再有什么波折，可为什么总是不顺？呜呜……！”
陈文心又安慰婆婆：“快顺了，快顺了，只要嫁给浩宇就顺了，婆婆快别再哭，多不吉利。”
Returning to Youlin Retreat from the Divine Willow earlier that evening, Bai Feng had been summoned straight to the princess's chambers by Xilan. Guessing the reason on the walk over, he swore to keep his mouth clamped shut.

韦嬷嬷劝道：“少夫人说得对，朱家有喜事，您该乐，而不是哭。”
Sure enough, Qiao Mengrong opened straight away: "Bai Feng, you stay at Brother's side day in and day out and know everywhere he goes. Tell me the truth, and I will reward you handsomely."

向夫人这才停止哭泣，用巾帕擦擦脸上的泪水：“苦荞，快扶小姐回去。”
Putting on an easy air, Bai Feng answered, "Aside from Youlin Retreat, His Highness visits Qiao Manor, the prefecture yamen, Zhu Manor on official errands—oh, and Wangyue Pavilion, all places Your Highness already knows."

苦荞走到小姐身边：“小姐，我陪您回园子。”
Thinking she had been too subtle, Mengrong pressed straight out: "Brother told me he loves someone. Do you know who?"

千里寻没有说任何话，向母亲行过礼，默然离开。
"Xilan asked me before, and now Your Highness asks again, yet I truly do not know," Bai Feng lied against his conscience. "Stop tormenting yourself, Your Highness—in all my time serving His Highness, I have never seen him treat another girl half as warmly as he treats you."

向夫人担忧道：“希望明日顺顺利利，不要发生任何事。”
"Tormenting myself? You heard him say it with his own mouth that night! Fine—if you refuse to talk, get out. I will uncover who holds Brother's heart sooner or later."

韦嬷嬷说：“不会，夫人放宽心，姑娘肯定会老老实实嫁过去。”
Left with no answers and no ally outside Xilan, Qiao Mengrong sank into gloom until her eyes landed on the red wedding card on the table. Shuzhen married tomorrow—why not go see her and talk her heart out?

“有嬷嬷这话，我就放心了。”向夫人在儿媳和韦嬷嬷陪伴下回屋休息。
Riding first to Wangyue Pavilion, where servants told her Shuzhen had returned to Zhu Manor, she steered straight there—and breathed a sudden sigh of relief spotting her brother's carriage parked outside! So he had only come to see Prefect Zhu; she could visit Shuzhen and ride home together with Brother!

朱府终于回归平静，千里寻的内心却平静不下来。今日发生太多事，她的人生之路再次被定格，她已无路可退。既如此，明日便老老实实嫁了，省得再惹出更多事端来。
Caught in a vise, Zhu Manor's servants dared neither blurt the truth nor block Princess Jiahe, saying only that Prince Yi and the Zhu family were at the reception hall. Following a guide there with Xilan, she walked straight into the sight of her brother clutching Shuzhen's hand and promising to elope with her!

她躺在闺床上，翻来覆去，回想发生的一切，充满对梦容的担忧，不知她可好些？不知她以后再见到自己会如何？
Chest squeezed breathless and limbs turning to water, tears poured down Qiao Mengrong's cheeks as those broken words left her throat, and she crumpled toward the stones—only for Shen Jianchen's swift arms to catch her. Scooping her up, he glanced back at Qianli Xun with blazing eyes. "Do not marry him! Wait for me!" Then he strode rapidly out of Zhu Manor with Mengrong in his arms.

她也回想起申简辰走时说的话：“不要嫁！等着我！”
Qianli Xun fought back her tears; she had shed enough over Wenyuan and refused to weep over love again. She blamed only herself—if she had never let feelings for Shen Jianchen creep back in, none of this wreckage would exist.

千里寻自言自语道：“都怪你，为何要跑来朱府？老娘才不会等你……。申简辰，早知如此，我应该在地震前接受你，可谁会想到世事难料，你忘了我吧，好好对待梦容。”
Exhaling hard—Princess Jiahe's arrival and swoon could not have been timed better!—Zhu Xiyan led the kneeling line in a loud chorus: "Farewell, Your Highness!"

申简辰把乔梦容抱上马车，将她搂在怀里，向幽林别居赶去。他并不后悔今晚做下令梦容伤心之事，梦容一时接受不了十分正常，相信慢慢她会从痛苦中走出来。
Once Prince Yi vanished beyond the gate, the Zhu family and Matron Wei stiffly clambered to their feet after kneeling so long.

乔梦容之所以晕倒，一半是因为难以接受哥哥心中之人竟然是她的闺中密友；一半儿不过是她装的而已。她实在不愿面对眼前的一切，只有晕倒才可避免尴尬和羞辱，也只有晕倒才能立刻将哥哥抢回来。
Seeing Shuzhen standing frozen and mute, Chen Wenxin stepped beside her. "Treat what just happened like a bad dream, Shuzhen. Get some sleep tonight—you have a wedding tomorrow."

喜兰真是配合，她装作晕倒时，她离得最近，并未迅速去扶，应是了解她的心思。
"A wedding... yes, tomorrow is the wedding." Coming back to herself, Qianli Xun murmured in quiet dread, "Sister-in-Law, I doubt Mengrong will ever count me a friend again."

靠在哥哥怀里，乔梦容甚是享受，也倍感悲哀，哥哥心中之人是她的好友，她该如何是好？
"Stop dwelling on that—"

她回忆哥哥与淑真相处的场景，实在找不出任何破绽。只怪自己太单纯善良，没有发现他们两个不对劲儿，也许他们在她面前偷偷眉来眼去不知多少回，岂不是把她当成傻子？
"Save your breath on her! Everyone back to your rooms!" Zhu Xiyan cut across, marching up to point a finger in his daughter's face. "Hear me well: once you enter Chen Manor, keep your head down and live clean! Stay clear of Prince Yi!"

到底他们从何时开始？乔梦容闭着眼，用心搜索时间。淑真明明喜欢的是乔公子，什么时候对哥哥动了心思？
Afraid his father might strike her, Zhu Jingyuan stepped in to take his arm. "Father, your knees must ache—let me help you back to rest."

乔梦容仔细回想，认为应该是哥哥在幽林别居举办完诗会后。记得就是从那以后，淑真才不再对哥哥有看法。
With a dark snort at his daughter, Zhu Xiyan let his son lead him off.

本以为她是为帮她才会说哥哥的好，现在才知原来她是喜欢上哥哥，才会说哥哥的好。
Dabbing away tears on Matron Wei's arm, Madam Xiang approached once her husband left. "Shuzhen, Mother never asked for some lofty match—only a calm, safe life without storms. Why does nothing ever go smooth for you? Wuuu!"

淑真定是得不到乔公子，才移情别恋勾引哥哥，看来曾经被百姓传的谣言说得一点儿没错，她就是个行为不检点的女子。
"Smooth days are right around the corner, Mother-in-Law!" Chen Wenxin soothed her. "Once she marries Haoyu, it all settles down! Dry those tears—crying on wedding eve brings bad luck!"

乔梦容甚是后悔与淑真做朋友，后悔不该在知道哥哥心中有了他人后，跑去淑真面前诉苦，还求她帮忙，傻傻地给她提供了一次见哥哥的机会。
"Young Madam is right," Matron Wei agreed. "A wedding calls for smiles, not tears."

她也立刻想通，哥哥为何从永泰茶楼回来会改变想法，主动提出要回汴梁亲自要求官家取消婚约……
Wiping her cheeks with a handkerchief, Madam Xiang nodded. "Kuqiao, walk Miss back to her courtyard."

乔梦容越想越恨，不由自主将哥哥的衣裳紧紧拽在手中。
Without a word, Qianli Xun curtsied to her mother and withdrew alongside Kuqiao.

申简辰发觉，轻声唤道：“梦容，你醒了？”
"Pray tomorrow passes without another hitch," Madam Xiang fretted.

乔梦容不能再伪装下去，她睁开泪眼，紧紧抱住哥哥。
"Not a hitch, Madam—rest easy, Miss will marry cleanly," Matron Wei reassured her as they escorted Madam Xiang inside.

“哥哥，你别离开我，要是没有你，我死的心都有。呜呜……”
Silence settled back over Zhu Manor, yet Qianli Xun's mind churned nonstop. Every bridge behind her had burned to ash; going through with tomorrow's wedding cleanly was the sole way to prevent fresh disasters. Tossing on her bed, she worried over Mengrong—was she alright, and how would she look at her next time?—while Shen Jianchen's parting vow echoed loud: *Do not marry him! Wait for me!*

申简辰没有做出任何回应，梦容正处于悲伤之际，说什么都会听不进去，等她平复情绪后，再与她好好聊聊心里话。
"All your fault for storming Zhu Manor," she whispered to the dark ceiling. "I am NOT waiting for you... Shen Jianchen, if I had known things would turn out like this, I should have said yes before the earthquake. Who knew life twists like this? Forget me—treat Mengrong well."

抱着梦容离开朱府时，申简辰叮嘱千里寻：“不要嫁！等着我！”那一刻他已从千里寻的眼神中看出，她明日必然会嫁。
Inside the carriage racing back to Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen cradled Qiao Mengrong in his arms. He felt no regret for breaking the truth tonight—shock was inevitable, yet in time she would heal and move on.

他该怎么办？申简辰想到抢亲的办法，不如明日借仪王的身份，调动临安兵力，去陈家抢亲，不相信搅不乱两家的婚礼，到时他便带着千里寻离开。
Truth be told, Qiao Mengrong's faint had been half genuine shock at finding her best friend was her rival, and half sheer calculation—the quickest way to dodge humiliation and yank her brother straight back into her arms! Xilan had played along sharp as a tack, holding back instead of catching her so Brother would step in.

眼下的情形他只能顾一头，他已想好，将属于赵朴的家财大部分留给梦容，也算是替他照顾妹妹。
Nestling against his chest brought both comfort and stabbing agony. Replaying every scene between Brother and Shuzhen, she found zero slips—only cursed her own blind trust! How many secret glances had they traded right under her nose, laughing at her like a fool?! When had it started? Shuzhen had loved Young Master Qiao—when had she set her sights on Brother? Right after Brother's poetry salon at Youlin Retreat! That was the exact turning point when Shuzhen stopped badmouthing him and started singing his praises! Unable to win Young Master Qiao, Shuzhen had pivoted to seducing Brother—maybe those old street rumors calling her shameless had been dead right all along! How Mengrong loathed herself for befriending her, pouring out her heartbreak, begging her help—handing Shuzhen a golden excuse to meet Brother at Yongtai Teahouse and twist him into demanding an annulment from His Majesty!

申简辰在心中自语：“赵朴啊赵朴，我不能为了替你照顾妹妹放弃我爱的人，本想把梦容放在身边，当妹妹一样看待，计划赶不上变化，我想带着我爱的人远走高飞，不想再当什么王爷，也不想再理会你留给我的婚约。”
Hatred surged so hot her fingers clenched tight into Shen Jianchen's robe.

乔梦容哭了一会儿，见哥哥不言语，心中更是担忧，把哥哥抱得更紧：“呜呜！哥哥，你为什么不说话？你是不是想要抛弃我？呜呜……！”
Feeling the tug, he murmured, "Mengrong, you are awake?"

申简辰本以为乔梦容醒来会开口质问他为何会喜欢淑真，可乔梦容并未质问，只是一味地哭，说些可怜巴巴的话。
Dropping the swoon, she opened swimming eyes and clung to his neck. "Do not leave me, Brother! Without you, I would rather die! Wuuu!"

听着马车碾压道路的声音混着梦容可怜巴巴的哭声，申简辰觉得自己实在可恶。即便如此，他的心意也不会改变。
Staying quiet while her grief raged—reasoning now would bounce off deaf ears—Shen Jianchen made his own ruthless choice inside. Seeing the steel in Qianli Xun's eyes as he carried Mengrong out, he knew she meant to board that bridal sedan tomorrow. Only one card remained: use Prince Yi's rank to mobilize Lin'an troops tomorrow, raid the Chen bridal procession, wreck the wedding, and take Qianli Xun far away! He could only save one side; he would leave the bulk of Zhao Pu's wealth to Mengrong as his duty to the late prince. *Sorry, Zhao Pu,* he thought silently. *I cannot throw away the woman I love to play your sister's husband. I meant to keep her close as family, yet plans cannot outrun fate—I am taking my love far away, ditching this royal title and your betrothal for good.*

“梦容，你现在什么都清楚，知道我心里喜欢的人是谁，不如成全我……”
Feeling his silence, panic shook Qiao Mengrong harder as she clung tighter. "Why won't you answer, Brother?! Are you going to abandon me?! Wuuu!"

乔梦容的哭声停止，抱紧他的手不由自主松开，坐端后，擦干泪水，用冷冷的声音说：“明日她就要成亲，我成全哥哥又有何用？”
Hearing her pitiful sobs blend with the rumble of carriage wheels made Shen Jianchen feel like the lowest heel alive—yet his resolve stayed iron. "Now you know everything, Mengrong—know who holds my heart. Let me go and grant us our chance..."

“只要你愿意成全，其他事我自有办法。”
Her sobs cut off dead. Releasing him, she sat bolt upright, wiped her eyes dry, and spoke in an ice-cold voice. "She marries tomorrow—what good does me letting you go do?"

哥哥语气坚定，就像一根针刺进乔梦容的内心，她痛了一会儿，继续冷冷地说：“哥哥还是清醒些，现在不是我成全不成全的事，官家赐婚，想退根本不可能。”
"Just grant your blessing—leave the rest to me."

申简辰不再说什么，反正他准备豁出去，为了个人幸福，不想再顾及其他。
His unyielding tone pierced like a needle. Swallowing the stab, she kept her voice wintry. "Wake up, Brother. This is not up to me—His Majesty decreed our marriage, and canceling it is impossible."

回到幽林别居后，将乔梦容送回闺房，吩咐喜兰伺候郡主睡下，申简辰忧心忡忡地离开。
Saying no more, Shen Jianchen steeled himself to gamble everything for his own happiness.

哥哥刚离开，乔梦容迅速起身，冲到桌前，拿起喜帖，一边撕，一边用柔弱的声音狠狠地说：“朱淑真！枉我真心将当你朋友看待，你却如此对我，从今往后，我不再是你的朋友！”
Back at Youlin Retreat, he carried her to her chambers, told Xilan to tuck her in, and walked out with a furrowed brow.

与淑真的友情就像眼前撕碎的喜帖，纷纷落在地上。乔梦容还不解恨，用脚狠劲踩着：“朱淑真！你妄想得到哥哥，别说你即将成亲，即使你未嫁，我也绝不会让你轻易得到哥哥！”
The second the door shut behind him, Qiao Mengrong sprang off the bed, snatched the red wedding invitation off the table, and ripped it to shreds with soft, venomous fury. "Zhu Shuzhen! I treated you like a true sister, and this is how you stab me?! From this hour on, we are enemies!"

乔梦容发泄完，踉跄着走到闺床跟前，趴在床上哭起来：“呜呜……！”用哭声送别她失去的友情。
Like their dead friendship, red paper confetti snowed across the floor. Stomping the scraps under her slippers, she hissed, "Dream on about getting Brother, Zhu Shuzhen! Even if you were NOT marrying tomorrow, I would never let you have him!"

喜兰没有劝郡主，她太可怜，几日之内不仅知道王爷心中有他人，还失去与幽栖居士的友情，发泄发泄也是好的。
Spent, she stumbled back to bed and buried her face in the pillows weeping aloud—sending off her dead friendship in tears. Xilan let her cry without interruption: learning her betrothed loved another AND losing her closest friend in one blow—letting it out was mercy.
