## Chapter 127: Delivering a Memorial to Zhu Manor

近期，申简辰没有去望月轩，可心无不被望月轩牵绊，他派白风送过两次关于文渊的消息。
Lately Shen Jianchen had stayed away from Wangyue Pavilion, yet his heart never left it for a moment; twice he had sent Bai Feng over with news of Wenyuan.

﻿现在只能靠传送消息的方式与千里寻发生千丝万缕的联系。
Passing messages was the sole fragile thread tying him to Qianli Xun now.

他无法忍受他的生活不与千里寻发生关系。
He couldn't bear a life severed from Qianli Xun.

申简辰觉得自己很贱，人家不断拒绝，他就是难以放下，理智在情感面前显得极其脆弱。
Shen Jianchen despised his own weakness—rejected over and over, yet still unable to let go, reason crumbling to sand before his heart.

他也觉得自己很坏，搂抱过乔梦容，还两次说过要娶她，心里却无时无刻想着什么时候出现奇迹，牵绊他的婚姻从此消失，自由自在奔赴在追求千里寻的道路上。
He felt guilty too: holding Qiao Mengrong in his arms and promising twice to marry her, while every hour secretly praying for a miracle to dissolve that betrothal and set him free to chase Qianli Xun.

申简辰去过乔家两次，第一次是那日离开望月轩时，没见到文渊本人，只与乔守仁聊了一会儿，说些劝慰的话。
He had visited Qiao Manor twice. The first time, right after leaving Wangyue Pavilion, he hadn't seen Wenyuan at all, only chatting with Qiao Shouren to smooth feathers.

从乔守仁的话语中得知，乔家正忙着给文渊选亲，已经有几个中意的姑娘入选，需进一步斟酌。
From Qiao Shouren he learned the Qiaos were busy picking a bride for Wenyuan, with several promising young ladies already shortlisted for final review.

申简辰莫名开心，他又觉得自己不重朋友情谊，朋友痛苦，他却以此为乐，实在不该。他用理智压制开心，却毫无用处。
A wicked rush of gladness had warmed Shen Jianchen—followed by guilt for rejoicing over his friend's misery. Reason shouted it down; his heart ignored reason.

第二次去乔家，文渊已能自由活动，范围仅限于他居住的院子。申简辰要求去看文渊，乔守仁不好阻拦，亲自将仪王带到儿子居住的院落。
On his second visit, Wenyuan had been allowed out into his own courtyard. When Shen Jianchen asked to see him, Qiao Shouren couldn't refuse and escorted Prince Yi to his son's courtyard himself.

门上有一把大大的锁，门口左右两边分别守着两个下人。院墙一人多高，上面临时放置着长满利刺的荆棘，显然是为防止文渊翻墙逃跑。外公真是用心良苦。
A heavy padlock hung on the gate, flanked by two guards. Overhead, fresh brambles bristling with thorns lined the man-high wall to stop Wenyuan climbing out. Grandfather sure left no loopholes.

仪王进入院中，乔守仁不便从外面锁上门，亲自守在门口，不敢有一丝松懈。
With Prince Yi inside, Qiao Shouren couldn't lock the gate from outside, so he stood sentry in the doorway himself without blinking.

文渊坐在院子里，闭着眼睛，晒太阳，人瘦了一圈。南双站在一旁，也显得没精神。
Wenyuan sat in the yard soaking up the sun with eyes shut, whole frame thinner by a size, with a listless Nan Shuang beside him.

当南双看到白风进来，兴奋地冲上去：“白风，你跟仪王终于来看我和公子，我被关了整整十日，快闷死了！”
Spotting Bai Feng walk in, Nan Shuang bolted over in glee: "Bai Feng! You and Prince Yi came at last! Locked up ten whole days—I'm dying of boredom!"

白风说：“我这次来，就是帮你解闷儿。”两个人站在院子的一旁，聊起天儿来。
Bai Feng grinned: "Came just to cure your boredom!" The two drifted to one corner of the yard to catch up.

乔文仙听到仪王前来，睁开眼，起身抱住仪王：“你可来了，是不是给我捎来淑真的消息？”
Hearing Prince Yi arrive, Qiao Wenxian opened his eyes, stood, and hugged him: "You came at last! Did you bring word from Shuzhen?"

“幽栖居士不愿见我，我如何有她的消息？我是来看看你。”
"Youqi Jushi refuses to see me—where would I get word? I came to check on you."

乔文仙失望地放开仪王：“她该不会想要放弃我？”
Disappointment dropped Qiao Wenxian's arms: "She isn't thinking of giving up on me, is she?"

“你是如何想的？”申简辰不想将千里寻的状态如实告知文渊。
"What do you plan to do?" Shen Jianchen had no intention of sharing how Qianli Xun was doing.

“父亲在门口站着，说话不方便，我们进屋说。”乔文仙拉起仪王，走进屋内，把门关上。
"With Father standing right at the gate, we can't talk out here—come inside." Qiao Wenxian tugged Prince Yi indoors and shut the door.

“你想对我说什么？”
"What do you want to tell me?"

“我后悔没听你的建议，现在我想通，打算跟淑真私奔，仪王定要帮我……”
"I regret turning down your idea. I've made up my mind to elope with Shuzhen—you have to help me, Prince Yi..."

“不行！”没等文渊讲完，申简辰立刻阻止。上次提出后，他已十分后悔，怎么可能让私奔之事真实发生？
"No!" Before Wenyuan finished, Shen Jianchen cut him off sharp. He had regretted pitching elopement the second it left his mouth—like hell he'd let it happen for real!

乔文献十分不解：“为何？仪王不是支持我吗？主意还是你出的。”
Baffled, Qiao Wenxian asked, "Why not? Weren't you on my side? It was your idea!"

“此一时彼一时，外公都掏出匕首，我再帮你，岂不是舍外公性命不顾？要不……要不你放了幽栖居士……”
"Things have changed! Grandfather pulled a dagger on himself—help you now and I'd be risking Grandfather's life! Why don't you... why don't you let Youqi Jushi go..."

“不！淑真亲口对我说，她不会放弃，我相信她，我也不会放弃！”
"Never! Shuzhen promised me herself she won't give up—I trust her, and I won't give up either!"

“文渊，我今日来，想告诉你，以后你与幽栖居士的事，我不会再插手，你们好自为之。”
"I came today to tell you one thing, Wenyuan: from now on I'm staying out of your and Youqi Jushi's business. You two are on your own."

说完，申简辰推门而出，在院中喊了白风，迅速离开。
With that, Shen Jianchen pulled the door open, called Bai Feng, and strode straight out.

南双喊道：“才来一会儿就走，是不是太着急？能不能和我多聊会儿？”
Nan Shuang yelled after him, "Leaving already?! Can't you stay and chat five more minutes?!"

仪王出去后，院门又被从外面锁住。
The second Prince Yi stepped past the threshold, the padlock snapped shut behind him.

乔文仙从屋内走出，整个人毫无生气，自语着：“连仪王都不打算帮我，我该怎么办？”
Stepping out into the yard drained of life, Qiao Wenxian muttered, "Even Prince Yi won't help me anymore—what do I do now?"

南双迎上去：“公子，要不您就答应老爷，不要再喜欢幽栖居士……”
Nan Shuang hurried over: "Young Master, why not just give in to Master and stop pining for Youqi Jushi..."

“滚！滚开！滚得越远越好！”乔文仙把南双推开，“我绝不放弃！哪怕剩下最后一口气，也绝不放弃！除非淑真亲口对我说放弃的话，否则我会一直……一直坚持下去。”
"Get lost! Far away from me!" Qiao Wenxian shoved Nan Shuang back. "I'll never give up! Until my dying breath I won't give up—unless Shuzhen tells me to my face she's quitting, I'll hold on forever!"

南双小声埋怨道：“我能滚到哪里去？大门紧锁，还不得陪公子在里面待着？这日子什么时候是个头？”
Nan Shuang grumbled under his breath, "Get lost where? Gate's padlocked—I'm stuck right in here with you! When will this end?"

走出乔府，申简辰不由自主往望月轩而去，他不敢进去，让白风将今日在乔家看到的向苦荞说明。
Leaving Qiao Manor, Shen Jianchen's carriage rolled toward Wangyue Pavilion on autopilot. Unwilling to step inside, he sent Bai Feng to tell Kuqiao what he had seen at Qiao Manor.

白风问：“王爷，我该如何对苦荞说？”
Bai Feng asked, "What should I tell Kuqiao, Your Highness?"

“就说乔公子已可以在院子里活动。”
"Just tell her Young Master Qiao is allowed out into his courtyard now."

“好的。”
"Got it."

白风进入望月轩，按照王爷的吩咐向苦荞转达。王爷在外面候着，他不敢跟苦荞待的时间太久，只聊两句，便匆忙分开。
Going inside Wangyue Pavilion, Bai Feng delivered the message to Kuqiao. With His Highness waiting outside, he dared not linger—exchanging just two words before hurrying back.

自听到流言之日起，申简辰一直在想如何解决流言之事，除抓住散播流言的人外，还要想办法阻止流言继续传播下去。
Ever since the rumors started, Shen Jianchen had been plotting not only how to catch the culprit, but how to kill the gossip cold across the whole city.

昨晚终于想到好办法，连夜写好奏章。还需去趟朱府，找朱晞颜商量此事。
Last night he had struck gold and stayed up drafting a throne memorial—now he needed Zhu Xiyan's backing at Zhu Manor.

白风又将马车赶往朱府。
Bai Feng steered the carriage straight for Zhu Manor.

朱晞颜刚从府衙回来，回来的路上听到关于淑真的流言蜚语，心情大受影响，换了官服，坐在榻上生闷气。
Just back from the prefecture office, Zhu Xiyan had caught more whispers about Shuzhen on his ride home, putting him in a black temper. Changing out of his robes, he slumped onto his couch simmering.

嘱咐林觉查探传播谣言之人，已半个月过去，毫无线索。他派出去的朱府下人也被一一调回，那些下人一点儿用处都没有，又何必继续浪费人力。
Half a month had passed since he tasked Lin Jue with hunting the rumor-mongers, turning up zero leads. His own Zhu Manor servants had proven useless too, so he had called them all back rather than waste manpower.

想到可恶的谣言，朱晞颜痛恨不已，又想不出更好的解决办法，只能静待林觉查探结果。
Burning with hatred for the smears yet stuck without a fix, he could only wait on Lin Jue.

可查探出结果，又能如何？最多将散播流言之人痛打一顿，却堵不住悠悠众口。关键还得想个办法，让城里城外的百姓不要再关注此事。
Even if Lin Jue caught someone, beating one culprit wouldn't shut the whole city's mouths—he needed something huge to yank public attention away for good.

正想着，下人来报：“老爷，仪王来到府中，等您前去迎接。”
Just then a servant announced, "Master, Prince Yi is inside the manor waiting for you!"

朱晞颜立刻吩咐下人伺候他换上官服，出门迎接。结果发现仪王正站在正屋的门外，仪王主动进府，应该有要事与他商议。
Zhu Xiyan scrambled back into his official robes and rushed out—finding Prince Yi waiting right outside the main hall. Stepping inside himself meant serious business!

“拜见仪王，不知仪王驾到，有失远迎，还望恕罪。”
"Greetings, Prince Yi! Forgive me for failing to welcome Your Highness sooner!"

申简辰不想说客套话：“知府大人还不赶快请我里面坐？一路过来，甚是辛苦。”
Skipping pleasantries, Shen Jianchen said, "Aren't you going to invite me inside to sit, Lord Prefect? Long ride over."

“仪王，里面请。”
"Please come in, Prince Yi!"

进入屋内后，下人上好茶，朱晞颜不敢坐下，一直恭敬地站着。
Once indoors and served tea, Zhu Xiyan stayed standing in deference.

申简辰喝口茶后说道：“知府大人不落座，我如何与你商量事情？”
Taking a sip, Shen Jianchen said, "If Lord Prefect won't sit, how are we supposed to talk business?"

朱晞颜斗胆坐下：“仪王有什么想要差遣下官，尽管说，下官义不容辞。”
Venturing onto a chair, Zhu Xiyan replied, "Whatever Your Highness needs of this humble official, say the word!"

申简辰向白风使使眼色，白风掏出奏章，递到朱晞颜手中。
At a nod from Shen Jianchen, Bai Feng drew out the memorial and handed it to Zhu Xiyan.

朱晞颜接过，打开认真阅览一番，仪王的字迹真不怎么样，恐怕是长期病着没有时间练字的缘故。
Unfolding it to read with care, Zhu Xiyan winced inward at the sloppy calligraphy—no doubt years of illness had left no time for brush practice.

可看完内容，朱晞颜心中无不赞赏。本以为仪王不关心临安军政，是因才能匮乏，今日总算明白，他是深藏不露。
Yet by the final line, admiration filled his chest. He had thought Prince Yi stayed out of government out of incompetence; turns out the prince had real depth up his sleeve!

朱晞颜看完后，将奏章恭敬地装入袖中：“仪王的奏章，下官定派人及时送到汴梁。只是……”
Tucking the memorial into his sleeve with reverence, Zhu Xiyan said, "I'll dispatch a courier to Bianliang with Your Highness's memorial at once. Only..."

“知府大人有什么话请直说。”
"Speak your mind, Lord Prefect."

“仪王关心临安百姓，建议减免赋税，对临安士农工商无不有利，下官甚是赞同，相信官家若同意，全临安城人都会欢呼雀跃。只是官家重用李大人，听说他在京城周边圈地三万四千三百余顷，恐怕他会阻止官家同意减免赋税一事。”
"Your Highness's proposal to cut taxes in Lin'an benefits scholars, farmers, artisans, and merchants alike—if His Majesty approves, all Lin'an will cheer! Only His Majesty favors Lord Li, who's been enclosing over thirty-four thousand three hundred qing of land around the capital—I fear he may block any tax cut."

“李大人何许人也？”
"Who is this Lord Li?"

仪王的问题，令朱晞颜诧异，一位皇子竟然不知朝中重臣？他还是耐心回道：“李大人现任大内总管，名为李彦。”
Startled that an imperial prince didn't know a top court favorite, Zhu Xiyan answered patiently: "Lord Li is Grand Intendant of the Inner Palace—Li Yan."

申简辰在心中骂道：“原来是北宋六贼之一！”
Shen Jianchen cursed silently: "One of the Six Traitors of the Northern Song!"

“哦，是他啊，不过是些趋炎附势之辈而已，何须惧之，也希望知府大人莫要惧怕。我之所以提出减免赋税一事，不过是想帮知府大人。近期关于朱家姑娘的谣言越传越烈，恐怕普通的事情不会引起百姓兴趣。”
"Oh, him—just a sycophant toadying for favor, nothing to fear! Don't sweat him, Lord Prefect. I wrote this tax-cut proposal to help you out: with rumors about Miss Zhu raging hotter by the day, no ordinary headline will pull the crowd's eyes away."

原来仪王是为朱家考虑，令朱晞颜感动不已，起身行礼：“谢仪王殿下，没想到朱家之事竟引得仪王挂怀。”
Realizing Prince Yi had cooked this up for the Zhu family's sake overwhelmed Zhu Xiyan; he jumped up and bowed: "Thank you, Your Highness! Never dreamed the Zhu family's plight weighed on Prince Yi's mind!"

“知府大人不必多礼，再说是一箭双雕之事，不完全为朱家。之所以说是为朱家，不过是希望知府大人对此事上心些。”
"Skip the bows, Lord Prefect—two birds with one stone, not just for the Zhus. I mention it so you'll put your full weight behind pushing it through."

“下官定当尽心竭力促成此事。”为了朱家，朱晞颜打算好好动用在京城的人脉。
"I'll spare zero effort to make it happen!" For the Zhu house, Zhu Xiyan was ready to pull every string he had in Bianliang.

“以我的名头送上去，再加上知府大人在朝中的人脉，官家定会同意。再说官家向来重视救济贫苦百姓，我在奏章中也说明，减免赋税，一来减少贫苦百姓的数量，二来富人挣到钱后，临安官府会发动富人建居养院、安济坊等。官家一高兴，自然会同意。”
"Sent up under my name, backed by Lord Prefect's connections at court, His Majesty is sure to sign off. Besides, His Majesty prides himself on charity for the poor—I wrote right in the memorial that cutting taxes reduces destitution, and once wealthy merchants profit more, Lin'an's government will rally them to fund poorhouses and charity infirmaries. That'll put His Majesty in a great mood and seal approval."

“仪王高明！下官临时决定亲自去趟汴梁，将奏章亲手交到官家手中。只是居养院、安济坊、漏泽园三处临安已有，无需再建，不如说会让富人将钱财投入这三处。”
"Brilliant, Prince Yi! I've changed my mind—I'll travel to Bianliang myself and lay this memorial straight in His Majesty's hands! Only Lin'an already has poorhouses, charity infirmaries, and pauper cemeteries, so rather than building new ones, let's say wealthy donors will expand funding for all three."

“就按你说得办，知府大人亲自前去，此事定成，我等着你的好消息。”
"Do it your way! With Lord Prefect going in person, it's a lock—I'll wait for your good news."

朱晞颜更是佩服不已，仪王不仅有头脑，还懂得如何迎合官家，难怪从小被官家冷落，病一好，立即得到重用。
Zhu Xiyan marveled even more: Prince Yi had brains AND knew how to play His Majesty like a lute—no wonder a neglected sick prince shot into imperial favor the second he recovered!

同时朱晞颜的心里也捏了一把汗，多亏仪王不参与临安军政，否则不知会翻出多少见不得人的事情来。
A cold sweat prickled Zhu Xiyan's neck too: thank heaven Prince Yi stayed out of Lin'an's politics, or who knew what dirty laundry he might dig up!

朱晞颜也纳闷，仪王写奏章不早不晚偏偏在淑真被众人非议之时拿出来，他到底是关心百姓？还是关心朱家？朱晞颜想不明白，也懒得多费心思，只要对朱家有利，他定竭尽全力促成。
One puzzle still nagged Zhu Xiyan: why had Prince Yi unveiled this memorial right at the peak of Shuzhen's scandal—out of love for the people, or for the Zhu family? Too tangled to solve, and why care—so long as it saved the Zhu family, he'd move heaven and earth to get it passed!
