# Chapter 73: Falling Asleep in His Arms, Fully Dressed

---

Baifeng followed Yi Wang as they hurried toward the Wangjing Pavilion on the north side. The prince's anxious expression left him in no mood to enjoy the West Lake night scenery.

---

When they reached a spot not far from the Wangjing Pavilion, Baifeng first spotted Nanshuang. He was sitting on a bench in the pavilion, chatting with the maid they had met in the bamboo forest—the one called Kuqiao, if he remembered correctly. There was another person with them, likely Huayu, whom Nanshuang often mentioned.

---

Baifeng was about to call out to Nanshuang when he noticed the prince staring at the girl from the bamboo forest. His eyes were filled with complex emotions—seeming both happy and as if he wanted to cry. Baifeng dared not speak, but silently accompanied him. He guessed that the girl must be the Youqi Jushi whom Nanshuang had mentioned.

---

Several lanterns hung in the pavilion. Shen Jianchen could clearly see Qianli Xun. She no longer looked the same as before the transmigration, but she was just as beautiful. He blamed himself for not recognizing her sooner. If he had, perhaps she wouldn't have run off to date Wenyuan.

---

Inside the pavilion, the four young men were still chatting happily. Qianli Xun had already finished a pot of sweet rice wine. Combined with the osmanthus wine she'd drunk earlier, the intoxication gradually crept over her. She felt a floating, almost heavenly sensation, and poetic inspiration stirred within her.

---

Shen Jianchen watched for a while and finally summoned the courage to call out: "Qian..."

---

Before he could finish, he heard Qianli Xun say: "Stop chatting, you all. I... I've suddenly gotten inspiration. I... I'm going to compose a poem."

---

She stood up and staggered toward Wenyuan.

---

When the others heard that the Youqi Jushi was going to compose a poem, they were all in high spirits and listened attentively.

---

Shen Jianchen had to give up for now. He stood in the darkness not far away, gazing at Zhu Shuzhen—the woman with the fascinating soul—in the pavilion.

---

Qiao Wenxian stood up to support Shuzhen: "You're drunk. Should I take you home?"

---

Qianli Xun stared at the man who had given her love and smiled: "I'm not drunk. I'm going to compose a poem."

---

She pushed Wenyuan away, staggered forward, gazed at the lake under the moonlight, and recited: "The mist and dew tease and tempt, asking me to stay a moment longer."

---

Then she turned back to look at Wenyuan. His loving, doting gaze intoxicated her. She stepped forward and took his hand: "Hand in hand along the lotus-lined lake path, a sudden drizzle in the plum rain season."

---

Wenyuan smiled at her. She pushed him onto the bench in the pavilion, and he had no choice but to sit down.

---

"In my charming folly, I fear no gossip," Qianli Xun recited another line. Then, to everyone's shock, she actually sat in Wenyuan's lap, wrapped her arms around his neck, rested her face against his chest, and closed her eyes: "Fully dressed, I fall asleep in his arms."

---

At this sight, Shen Jianchen could no longer bring himself to call out "Qianli Xun." He still loved her, but she had fallen in love with Wenyuan. He pondered again the words Qianli Xun had once said: "Only when pigs fly will I ever marry you!" Only now did Shen Jianchen truly grasp the meaning of her clear rejection.

---

Perhaps she had never loved him. What would be the point of letting her know he was Shen Jianchen? Besides, she had already fallen in love with Wenyuan. Was he to make her reject him once more, without mercy? The more Shen Jianchen thought about it, the more heartbroken he became. He sighed and turned to leave.

---

Baifeng followed the prince. He really couldn't understand—the prince had clearly come to find Young Master Qiao, who was right there in the pavilion. Why had they rushed over, said nothing, done nothing, and now were leaving?

---

In the pavilion, An Mingxuan whispered: "We should go. The Youqi Jushi has already recited 'fully dressed, I fall asleep in his arms.' It's not appropriate for us to be here."

---

Dongfang Chenze said: "The poem isn't finished yet—just two more lines. Wait a bit longer. Cao Ban, you have a good memory. Pay attention and remember it."

---

Cao Ban said: "Don't worry. I've got it all memorized."

---

Kuqiao was so shocked she stood up, thinking: "How can Miss be so bold? She's actually sitting in Young Master Qiao's lap in front of everyone!"

---

Nanshuang and Huayu widened their eyes and looked at each other, revealing knowing smiles.

---

Qiao Wenxian hadn't expected Shuzhen to sit in his lap in public. She must have been drunk to become so unrestrained. But Qiao Wenxian was delighted. He wrapped his arms around her and said gently: "Shuzhen, you're drunk."

---

Qianli Xun, her eyes hazy with intoxication, moved her face from Wenyuan's chest and looked at him: "Wenyuan, I'm not drunk. I want to tell you—I love you."

---

Those around them felt another wave of awkwardness.

---

Qiao Wenxian whispered in her ear: "I know. But if you say it in front of them, I'll be embarrassed."

---

Qianli Xun turned her intoxicated gaze toward the three young men: "Them? It's fine. They're all familiar faces. I still have two more lines..."

---

The three young men immediately perked up their ears as the Youqi Jushi, still half-drunk, recited: "Most painful is the moment of parting—returning home, too listless to approach the vanity."

---

Dongfang Chenze hastily said: "Done! We should go now. Let's not disturb Wenyuan and the Youqi Jushi any longer."

---

The three young men swiftly departed.

---

Once outside the pavilion, An Mingxuan asked Cao Ban: "Did you memorize the whole poem?"

---

Cao Ban replied confidently: "Is that even a question?"

---

When Qiao Wenxian saw the three young men leave, he urged: "Everyone's gone. Should I take you home too?"

---

Qianli Xun still held onto Wenyuan's neck: "I love you. I want to marry you. I want to give you many, many children..."

---

Qiao Wenxian lifted Shuzhen up and whispered in her ear: "I love you too."

---

Hearing this, Qianli Xun was overjoyed and peacefully fell asleep in Wenyuan's arms.

---

Qiao Wenxian carried her all the way to the carriage. It was somewhat strenuous, but he persevered. When they reached it, he lifted Shuzhen inside and let her lean against him as she slept.

---

A smile hung on Qiao Wenxian's face all the way. He savored Shuzhen's line—"fully dressed, I fall asleep in his arms"—and murmured: "I never expected a girl like you could write such a line. Others would criticize you, but I adore it—for you were in my arms when you thought of it. It was written for me."

---

The two of them were alone in the carriage. He gazed at Shuzhen in his arms. Under the influence of the sweet rice wine, she slept soundly. Qiao Wenxian couldn't help but gently kiss her forehead: "Shuzhen, rest assured. I will grow old with you. I will stay by your side for a lifetime."

---

When they reached the side gate of the Zhu residence, Qiao Wenxian was about to carry Shuzhen back inside. Kuqiao stopped him: "Young Master Qiao, you've brought her far enough. The Zhu residence is vast. Carrying Miss would surely exhaust you. The Zhu family has sedan chairs. Just have Miss sit in one, and they'll carry her back."

---

Qiao Wenxian lifted Shuzhen again. When the servants brought out the sedan chair, he carefully settled her inside.

---

Kuqiao bid Young Master Qiao farewell: "It's late. Young Master Qiao should go home and rest early too."

---

Qiao Wenxian watched the sedan chair enter the Zhu residence before he felt at ease and returned to the carriage.

---

Nanshuang asked: "Should Young Master return to the Qiao residence or go to the Youlin Villa?"

---

"Is that even a question?" Qiao Wenxian's tone carried a hint of reproach.

---

Nanshuang suggested: "It's so late. Perhaps Young Master shouldn't return to the Youlin Villa tonight. The Zhu residence is closer to the Qiao residence."

---

Qiao Wenxian had been very happy today. He wanted to see Yi Wang immediately and share his joy: "Are we listening to you or to me?"

---

"Of course we listen to Young Master." Nanshuang had no choice but to drive the carriage toward the outskirts of the city.

---

When they returned to the Youlin Villa, Qiao Wenxian didn't go to his own room. He headed straight for Yi Wang's courtyard.

---

At the courtyard gate, he saw the light was still on inside, which made him even happier: "Yi Wang is so thoughtful. Knowing I hadn't returned, he stayed awake for me."

---

As soon as he entered the courtyard gate, he shouted: "Yi Wang! I'm back!"

---

Before he could enter the room, Baifeng blocked him at the door: "Young Master Qiao, the prince is tired. He just fell asleep. If you have something to discuss, please wait until tomorrow."

---

Qiao Wenxian looked toward the room again: "Isn't the light still on? Don't block me. I just want to say a few words."

---

The moment he finished speaking, the light inside the room was immediately extinguished.

---

"In that case, I'll find him tomorrow." Qiao Wenxian didn't think much of it. He assumed Yi Wang must be exhausted—otherwise, why would he put out the light upon hearing his voice?

---

After Wenyuan left, Shen Jianchen sat up from the bed. He couldn't fall asleep at all. The reason he didn't want to see Wenyuan was that he feared Wenyuan would talk about his date with the Youqi Jushi, which would only make him even more heartbroken.

---

Shen Jianchen walked into the courtyard in the dark and began practicing his fist forms. Baifeng had been about to go to sleep, but the prince was up again. He had to accompany him.

---

The prince's fist techniques were exquisite. Baifeng watched from the side, completely captivated...

---

After about an hour, the prince finally stopped: "Go to sleep. You don't need to accompany me. I want to go out for a walk alone."

---

Baifeng was just about to go to sleep when he was called back. He heard the prince say: "Starting tomorrow, there's no need to go to West Third Alley anymore."

---

This was the happiest news Baifeng had heard in a long time: "Understood!"

---

He watched the prince leave the courtyard, unable to understand why the prince, though clearly unable to sleep, didn't want to see Young Master Qiao. Since he couldn't figure it out, he didn't want to think about it any longer. He went back to his room to get some proper sleep.

---
