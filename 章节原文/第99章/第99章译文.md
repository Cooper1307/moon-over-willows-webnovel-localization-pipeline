## Chapter 99: Rumors Begin to Spread

Several couples passed by, each young woman holding two lotus buds that looked as though they grew from a single stem.

Curiosity sparked in Qianli Xun's eyes. "Wenyuan, what is that?"

Qiao Wenxian laughed. "After losing your memory, you really don't remember a thing! That's a twin-headed lotus. It's a symbol of good fortune."

"I want one too!" Qianli Xun was not short on silver, yet she wanted Wenyuan to buy it for her.

Wenyuan pulled her over to a street stall packed with Qixi Festival wares, twin-headed lotuses most of all. He bought a stem and handed it to her.

Holding it in her hand as a novel keepsake, Qianli Xun gave it a closer look and saw it had been fashioned by joining two lotus buds together, not a real twin-headed lotus at all.

Turning his head, Shen Jianchen spotted the twin-headed lotus in Qianli Xun's hand. Without knowing what it was called, he stepped up to a passing stall and bought one for Qiao Mengrong as well.

Taking it into her hands, Qiao Mengrong felt a rush of emotion. This was her first time spending Qixi Festival out in the city with Prince Yi, and his first time giving her a twin-headed lotus. Tears welled in her eyes. "Brother, do you know what a twin-headed lotus stands for?"

Shen Jianchen shook his head, a fresh weight of pressure settling on him at Qiao Mengrong's reaction. He had seen Qianli Xun holding one and knew he had no chance of buying one for her, so he could only give it to Mengrong instead.

"A twin-headed lotus means two hearts bound together forever."

"Oh. Two hearts bound together forever."

"I'll treasure the twin-headed lotus Brother gave me with all my care."

"It's bound to wilt sooner or later."

"Even after it wilts, I'll still keep it safe."

Shen Jianchen glanced back at Qianli Xun. She was smiling so bright; no doubt she had long since forgotten Shen Jianchen, so why keep clinging to her?

"No need. When it wilts, toss it out, and I'll buy you a fresh one."

Qiao Mengrong caught his meaning plain as day: he meant to grow old together with her. A radiant smile broke across her face as she continued strolling the streets beside him.

Walking along, Qianli Xun breathed in the scent of her twin-headed lotus while taking in the crowds and street stalls around her. As she looked about, the person she loathed most drifted into view. She turned her head away and pretended not to see him.

Chen Haoyu had already spotted her and strolled right over on purpose with a handful of lackeys at his heels. "What fate brings my dear in-law and me together! Even out strolling the streets, we bump into each other."

"Chen Haoyu, if you know what's good for you, stay back before I beat you up!" Qianli Xun thrust her twin-headed lotus into Xiaguo's hands and raised her fist in warning.

"I only came over to say hello. Why act like a ferocious she-demon?" Chen Haoyu clapped Qiao Wenxian on the shoulder. "Qiao Wenxian, I feel sorry for you. If you marry her someday, every day of your life is going to be misery."

Qianli Xun snapped in anger, "One more second and I'm swinging!"

Qiao Wenxian stepped in to soothe her. "Shuzhen, no need to lose your temper with him. He's just running his mouth."

Chen Haoyu said, "Qiao Wenxian knows me best! I've had my fun running my mouth, so I'm off. Won't disturb the two of you any longer." With that, he led his lackeys away without a fuss.

Qianli Xun unclenched her fist and held out her palm, waiting for Xiaguo to hand back the twin-headed lotus.

Xiaguo hesitated for a beat before placing it back in her mistress's hand. Young Master Qiao had bought that twin-headed lotus, and she hated letting go of it.

Taking back the twin-headed lotus, Qianli Xun asked, "Do you like twin-headed lotuses too?"

Xiaguo nodded.

"It wouldn't be right for me to buy you one of these. You'll just have to wait until you find someone you like and let him buy one for you."

Noticing a stem in Kuqiao's hand up ahead, Qianli Xun realized Kuqiao had fallen for Bai Feng; no wonder the two of them had been walking side by side laughing and chatting the whole time.

Nan Shuang piped up, "If Youqi Jushi approves, I can buy one for Sister Xiaguo."

Qianli Xun laughed. "Whether I approve doesn't matter. What matters is whether Xiaguo agrees."

Xiaguo cut in at once: "I don't want you buying me anything!"

Qianli Xun chided her with a laugh, "Even if you don't want it, don't be so blunt. You'll hurt Nan Shuang's feelings."

Sure enough, Nan Shuang stormed off in a huff and hurried ahead to join Kuqiao and Bai Feng.

Qiao Wenxian chuckled. "Look at my Nan Shuang, all grown up and tasting his first crush. Xiaguo, won't you consider him?"

Hearing the man she loved play matchmaker for her stung Xiaguo's heart; it proved Young Master Qiao did not have her in his heart at all.

"No need to persuade me, Young Master Qiao. There's already someone I like..."

Qianli Xun lit up with delight. "Who is it?"

"I like him, though he might not like me back. Better not say; it would only be embarrassing." Xiaguo lowered her head and refused to say another word.

Since Xiaguo did not want to tell, Qianli Xun could not press her further.

Remembering their plan to treat Prince Yi to a storytelling show, Qiao Wenxian said, "Shuzhen, Qiaotou Wa is just up ahead."

"We're here already? I still wanted to stroll around with you a bit longer."

"After the storytelling, I'll stroll with you some more."

"All right."

Quickening their pace, they caught up to Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe.

Their masters gave the servants leave to go off on their own. Kuqiao and Bai Feng were over the moon, Xilan wanted to wander the streets on her own too, and Nan Shuang jumped at the chance to act as Xilan's guide. Only Xiaguo stayed behind with her mistress; after all, the man she loved stayed right by her mistress's side.

Meanwhile, Chen Haoyu's lackeys were bored stiff trailing him through the streets. One of them spoke up: "Young Master Chen, this Qixi Festival is a total bore. We'd be better off heading over to Chunxiang Brothel."

Chen Haoyu let out a sigh. "I know it's boring, all right? I only came out to gauge how well my rumors are spreading. Today's got the biggest crowds on the streets. Have any of you heard people talking about Zhu Shuzhen?"

The lackeys shook their heads. "Not a word."

"Why is it taking so long? I'm losing my patience! You!" Chen Haoyu pointed at one of his lackeys. "Go tell the rumor-mongers to pick up the pace and spread it twice as hard. I'll pay them extra taels of silver."

"Yes, sir!" The lackey took his orders and scurried off.

Another lackey said, "Don't get too impatient, Young Master Chen. Rumors travel by word of mouth, so they're bound to take time. Besides, it's been less than ten days. Give it a month, then see what happens."

"Fair point, no use rushing. Still, how can I not be impatient? You all saw it just now: the way Zhu Shuzhen glared daggers at me made my blood boil. Until I knock her down a few pegs, I've got a knot sitting right here." Chen Haoyu rubbed his chest in mock agony.

One lackey said, "We've got knots in our chests too! The whole street is packed with couples, while you and the rest of us are all bachelors. We stick out like sore thumbs in this crowd. We really ought to hit Chunxiang Brothel."

Chen Haoyu pulled out a pouch of silver and tossed it into one man's arms. "Go on if you want. My vow of abstinence isn't up yet, and I won't step a single inch over that line."

"Thank you, Young Master Chen!" Clutching the silver, the lackeys grinned from ear to ear and headed off toward Chunxiang Brothel.

Left all alone, Chen Haoyu watched the couples drifting up and down the street and could not help wondering when he might find someone who suited his heart too.

Chen Haoyu had dallied with plenty of women, yet not a single one had found her way into his heart so far.

Perhaps he had played around with too many women to ever feel for one the way Qiao Wenxian did, or the way these men on the street did—men who knew nothing of what women were like.

At times he even envied those naive men, who could still look at women through misty, starry eyes and feel emotions beyond words, whereas to this day he still had no idea what love was.

He had thought before that if any woman ever stirred real feelings in him, he would marry her and mend his ways for good. Yet he figured that notion was nothing more than a pipe dream.

Before he knew it, he had wandered into an ordinary teahouse, the sort of place he never set foot in. Breaking his own habit today had only one purpose: to listen in and hear how the rumors were spreading.

Chen Haoyu picked a seat, ordered a pot of tea at random, filled his cup, and strained his ears. Customers came and went, chatting for the most part of household trivia and romance; after listening for ages, he caught not a single word of his rumors.

Two young couples walked in, and the waiter happened to seat them at the table right beside Chen Haoyu. He was just about to leave when one of the young women asked, "Have you heard the ci lyric Youqi Jushi wrote the other day?"

At last, someone was talking about Zhu Shuzhen! Not about to miss a word, Chen Haoyu pricked up his ears.

One of the men asked, "Which ci?"

The young woman answered, "'Qingping Yue: Summer Excursion on the Lake.'"

The other man said, "I've heard it. One line in it is bold beyond belief."

The second woman chimed in, "It really is! How could she be so brazen as to write 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'? Just reading it makes me blush."

A wave of glee washed over Chen Haoyu. "Hmph!" he thought. "Before my rumors even make the rounds, your own ci has already spread far and wide. You reap what you sow, all right! Heheh..."

The first woman went on, "I've also heard ugly rumors about Youqi Jushi these past days—calling her a tigress, saying she had affairs and traded secret glances with men, even saying she deserves to be pointed at by a thousand fingers and spat on by ten thousand. It's awful to hear."

One of the men looked skeptical. "Could someone be making all that up?"

The second woman said, "There's no smoke without fire. Someone who can write 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed' is capable of just about anything."

The other three nodded in agreement.

Chen Haoyu gloated all the more to himself: "Just you wait, Zhu Shuzhen! Before long, the whole city will be buzzing with scandals about you. No matter how tough your fists are, who're you going to punch when it's everyone talking? Hahaha...!"
