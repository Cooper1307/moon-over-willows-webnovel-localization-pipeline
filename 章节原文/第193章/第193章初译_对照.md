第193章 陈家喜宴好热闹
## Chapter 193: A Lively Chen Family Wedding Feast

夏果猛然提起此话，千里寻不知该如何回应。她无心关注他人之事，何况还是这等闺中私密。
苦荞小心提醒：“夏姨娘怎能将您闺中之事说与小姐？也不怕羞得慌。”
夏果批评道：“我与小姐说话，你少插嘴。”
苦荞撇撇嘴，不再说什么。
夏果继续说：“小姐别介意，我之所以这么说，无非是将您当家人看待，也想让您知道，文渊还未放下小姐……”
千里寻感觉出试探的意味，她向来讨厌别被人试探：“你能将我当家人，我很欣慰，至于我跟文渊之前的那段情，已过去。你今日也看到，我连婚都结了，还有什么好担心的？”
夏果放心许多：“说句心里话，我很羡慕小姐，能得到文渊的真情。我不敢奢求真情，只要能在文渊身边伺候，能与他说说话，便心满意足。”
夏果又露出笑容：“有件事，说起来我倒开心，文渊好几次到我等房中聊天儿，却从来没理过苗知瑜。”
千里寻庆幸自己没有答应做他人的妾室，不管是文渊，还是仪王。她才不愿像夏果一样，为争夺一个男人与其他女人互相攀比，到最后只会严重到勾心斗角。
夏果见小姐什么话都不说，还露出不想谈论此话题的表情，也觉多说无益，站起来告辞：“此处是婚房，说不定新郎一会儿回来，我不便在此久留，还得回到婚宴，以后有时间再来看望小姐。”
千里寻只说出两个字：“慢走。”
夏果行完礼，带着艾墨离开。
苦荞见人走远，把门关起好：“小姐，我不喜欢现在的夏果。”
“为何？”
“说不清楚，反正感觉不舒服。”
“管她呢，跟你我没什么关系。我累了，想睡一会儿，待会儿陈浩宇回来，让他到后院找个地方睡。”
“小姐不都跟姑爷说好，让他睡偏房？”
“我改主意了，偏房离我太近，我可不想一早起来就看到陈浩宇那张脸。”
她和衣躺在床上，毕竟第一次睡在别人家，说不定陈浩宇会喝醉酒而归，要是跑错地方，自己岂不被他看光？
苦荞猜出小姐的心思，伺候她躺好，放下床幔，出去将门轻轻关好，打算去后院安排一番，省得姑爷回来埋怨没给他准备好睡觉的地方。
在婚宴的男宾区内，乔文仙一直喝闷酒。
乔家接到喜帖时，他感到难过。本不想目睹淑真与别人的婚礼，可乔守仁一再要求他必须代表乔家参加。乔文仙拗不过父亲，遵从父命带着一妻一妾一起前来。
刚一到被东方尘泽等人拉到男宾区，他的妻妾则去了女宾区，没有她们在一旁，清静许多。
与东方尘泽等人聊一会儿，终于看到淑真身着嫁衣，与陈浩宇一起走来。他内心充满嫉妒，为何陈浩宇有这等福分将他喜欢的人娶走？他狠劲捏着手中的酒盅。
酒盅没有任何反应，他的手指却捏得疼。他不再说话，只顾喝闷酒。
仪式举行完，淑真离开婚宴，他的心也跟着离开。东方尘泽等人劝他少喝些，他并不听劝，依然一杯接着一杯喝。
南双担心自家公子喝坏身体，走过去劝道：“公子，少喝些。”
乔文仙根本不管，推开南双：“有你什么事？一边待着！”
青瓜端着酒壶陪陈浩宇前来敬酒。
陈浩宇见乔文仙喝闷酒，并未理会，端起酒盅，对其他人说：“诸位，感谢前来参加婚礼，我敬诸位一杯。”
东方尘泽、曹班和安明轩，以及其他人都起身端起酒盅回敬。大家再瞧不上这位纨绔子弟，再为幽栖居士感到惋惜，毕竟是婚礼，面子上还需过得去。
唯独乔文仙坐着，不予理会。
酒过一巡，陈浩宇打算去其他酒桌敬酒。
乔文仙才借着酒劲儿说：“你还没向我敬酒。”
Blindsided by the bedroom confession, Qianli Xun had zero idea how to answer—wanting no part of anyone's private laundry.

陈浩宇不爽：“我刚敬了，是你不接。”
Kuqiao spoke up sharply. "How can Concubine Xia spill bedchamber secrets in front of Miss?! Have you no shame?!"

乔文仙倒满一盅，站起来：“一桌人一起敬酒有什么意思，为何不一个一个敬？”
"Mind your tongue when I am speaking with Miss!" Xiaguo snapped back.

陈浩宇说：“没办法，我成亲，大家都给面子，来得人多，一个一个敬，只怕到明日都敬不完。”
Kuqiao pursed her lips and fell quiet.

乔文仙将手中的酒泼在陈浩宇的脸上：“你根本不配娶淑真！”
"Do not take offense, Miss," Xiaguo said softly. "I only speak freely because I think of you as family—and wanted you to know Wenyuan still has not let go of you..."

一旁的青瓜把酒壶放在桌上，赶紧用袖子替陈浩宇擦脸：“乔公子喝多了，公子不跟他计较。”
Catching the subtle probe—and hating being tested—Qianli Xun answered smoothly, "I am glad you think of me as family. As for Wenyuan and me, that chapter is closed. As you saw today, I just got married myself—what is left to fret over?"

陈浩宇推开青瓜，过去抓住乔文仙的衣领：“怎么？想打架？”
Relief washed over Xiaguo's face. "Truth be told, I envy you winning Wenyuan's real love. I dare not ask for love—just waiting on him and chatting with him makes me content." A small triumphant smile curved her lips. "One thing does cheer me up, though: Wenyuan has dropped by my room to chat several times, yet he never spares Miao Zhiyu a glance."

东方尘泽见情势不妙，起来劝道：“大喜的日子，陈公子别跟乔兄计较。”
Qianli Xun thanked her lucky stars she had never accepted concubinage—not for Wenyuan, not for Prince Yi! Measuring petty victories against rival wives until hearts rotted with schemes sounded like pure hell.

曹班和安明轩一起将陈浩宇拉走，此事才算结束。
Seeing her former mistress stay silent with obvious boredom, Xiaguo rose to excuse herself. "This is the bridal chamber, and the groom might return any minute—I should head back to the feast and visit again when you have time."

这一幕被另一桌的林觉瞧见，心中不屑：“一个我不要的人，你们却来抢，真可笑！
"Take care," Qianli Xun offered two cool words as Xiaguo curtsied out with Aimo.

很快林觉倍感失落，毕竟朱淑真是他一见钟情之人，当时的感觉永生难忘，他也端起一盅酒猛然饮下。
Latching the door once they cleared the yard, Kuqiao wrinkled her nose. "I do not like the new Xiaguo, Miss."

乔文仙继续坐下喝闷酒，喝着喝着想起仪王，心中纳闷，怎么不见仪王的身影？
"Why?"

乔文仙纳闷之际，主桌上，陈江林也纳闷，为何仪王到现在也未出现？小声向一旁的亲家朱晞颜打听：“亲家可知仪王为何没来？”
"Hard to put my finger on—just rubs me the wrong way."

朱晞颜想起昨晚发生的事，解释道：“估计有事给绊住，贵人未到，大不了明日你我一起上门亲自拜见。”
"Leave her be—nothing to do with us. I am exhausted and need a nap. When Chen Haoyu gets back, send him to find a bed in the back courtyard."

“说的也是。”陈江林继续露出笑脸与他人敬酒。
"Wait—didn't Miss tell Young Master he gets the side room?"

陈江木已知仪王半路抢亲之事，他的儿子陈浩飞跟着迎亲队伍，什么都看到，悄悄告诉过他，他准备等婚宴结束后再告诉大哥。
"Changed my mind. Side room is too close—last thing I want waking up is staring at Chen Haoyu's mug." Lying down fully clothed—first night in a strange house, what if Chen Haoyu staggered back drunk into the wrong room?—she let Kuqiao draw the bed curtains and slip out to ready a back-courtyard room before the groom griped.

陈江树还不知仪王抢亲一事，沉浸在儿子计谋得逞的喜悦中。他实在搞不明白，大哥高兴个什么劲儿！娶个离过婚，名声又不好的儿媳妇，有什么好开心的？还把婚礼举行得这么盛大。
Out in the men's banquet wing, Qiao Wenxian drank cup after bitter cup. Heartbroken at receiving the invitation, he had wanted to stay home, yet Qiao Shouren insisted he represent the Qiaos, so he arrived dutifully with wife and concubine—glad at least to split off with Dongfang Chenze's table while the women headed to their own wing. Watching Shuzhen walk past in bridal crimson beside Chen Haoyu stoked blazing jealousy; he gripped his wine cup so tight his knuckles ached. Once the bride withdrew, his soul walked out with her, and he ignored every friend's plea to slow down.

女宾区内，临安有头有脸的家眷实在不少。
Worried for his health, Nan Shuang stepped up. "Go easy, Young Master."

新媳妇不能出面敬酒，姚轻琰带着几名能喝酒的丫鬟挨桌敬酒，实在逃不过时，则以清水代酒，谁让她的酒量根本不怎么样。
"Stay out of it! Back off!" Qiao Wenxian shoved him away.

转完一圈才发现，佳和郡主未到，心中也十分纳闷，向身旁的向夫人问起。
Soon Chen Haoyu strolled over to make toasts with Qinggua carrying the wine jug. Ignoring Qiao Wenxian's black cloud, Chen Haoyu raised his cup to the table. "Thank you all for gracing my wedding—a toast to you, gentlemen!"

向夫人与朱晞颜一样想起昨晚之事，只因韦嬷嬷并未将迎亲路上发生的事告知她。昨晚之事不便与姚轻琰提起，向夫人也仅是轻描淡写：“贵人事多，恐怕有什么事耽搁住。”
Dongfang Chenze, Cao Ban, An Mingxuan, and the others rose politely to drink back—much as they scorned this rake and pitied Youqi Jushi, wedding courtesy came first. Only Qiao Wenxian stayed seated without lifting a finger.

再问女儿，陈文心不好将昨晚的事在这等场合说了，仅说不知。
As Chen Haoyu turned to move to the next table, wine-bold Qiao Wenxian called out, "You have not toasted me yet."

姚轻琰不再问下去，继续招呼众人。
"I just toasted the table—you refused to drink," Chen Haoyu shot back.

许多贵妇与大家闺秀也会借喜宴之际互相走动。
Filling a cup to the brim and rising, Qiao Wenxian sneered, "What kind of toast lumps a whole table together? Why not toast man to man?"

白梅若本与别人坐一桌，瞧到苗知瑜的母亲秦夫人去了别处，端起酒盅走到苗知瑜身边坐下。
"Cannot help being popular—half Lin'an showed up. Toast one by one and we would still be drinking tomorrow!"

“乔家少夫人怎么看上去不高兴？”
With one flick of his wrist, Qiao Wenxian flung his wine straight into Chen Haoyu's face! "You are not fit to marry Shuzhen!"

苗知瑜向白梅若看一眼：“还是唤我知瑜吧。”
Dropping the jug, Qinggua scrambled to dab his master's wet face with his sleeve. "Young Master Qiao had too much wine—do not mind him, Young Master!"

白梅若故意问：“怎么？不喜欢乔家少夫人的称呼？”
Shoving Qinggua clear, Chen Haoyu snatched Qiao Wenxian by the collar. "Looking for a brawl?!"

苗知瑜淡淡地说：“你我也算认识，虽未走动过，但见到你，我并不觉得讨厌，故而你没必要如此客气，还是像朋友一样唤我知瑜吧。”
Springing up, Dongfang Chenze stepped between them. "On your wedding day, Young Master Chen—do not trade blows with a drunk!" With Cao Ban and An Mingxuan hauling Chen Haoyu back, the flare-up cooled.

白梅若刚嫁到临安不久，认识的人不多，也希望能结交到好友。上次捶丸场大家第一次见面，不免客气一番，事后她也没敢去打扰苗知瑜，只是在苗知瑜的婚礼上又见过一次而已。
Watching from a nearby table, Lin Jue scoffed silently: *Look at them fighting over a woman I already ditched—hilarious!* Yet an instant later, a dull hollowness hit him; Zhu Shuzhen had been his love at first sight, a rush he could never erase, and he knocked back a full cup himself.

至于幽栖居士，白梅若更不好随意打扰，毕竟她以前也是林家人，与她做朋友总觉怪怪的。
Slumping back into drinking, Qiao Wenxian suddenly blinked—where was Prince Yi?

倒是在乔家的婚礼上与佳和郡主聊过几句，后来她想过要去幽林别居拜见郡主，若能与郡主成为朋友，不管对自己还是夫君都是好的。
At the head table, Chen Jianglin wondered the same thing, leaning to whisper to Zhu Xiyan, "Any idea why Prince Yi has not arrived?"

可惜她去过一次，扑了空，下人说郡主去了望月轩。一打听，才知望月轩是幽栖居士居住之所。如此看来，郡主与幽栖居士关系十分要好。
Recalling last night's storm, Zhu Xiyan answered smoothly, "Likely tied up with urgent affairs. If His Highness missed today, you and I will call on him together tomorrow to pay respects."

白梅若便放弃继续拜访郡主的想法，只怕郡主因幽栖居士不会真正将她当成朋友看待。不过今日之后她们之间的朋友关系肯定会走到尽头，白梅若的机会终于到来。
"Fair enough," Chen Jianglin nodded, smiling back into his toasts.

发生那么大的事，郡主没来，没办法利用婚宴的机会与郡主结交，她便将苗知瑜视为最佳人选。
At another table, Chen Jiangmu already knew about Prince Yi's raid on the bridal procession—his son Haofei had walked with the escort and whispered every detail—and planned to tell Eldest Brother after guests cleared out. Second Brother Chen Jiangshu knew nothing of the raid yet, still basking in his son's triumph while marveling at why Eldest Brother looked so thrilled throwing a fortune at a bride with a divorced past and messy name.

看上去，苗知瑜与幽栖居士走得并不近。况且有一次参加贵妇人的聚会，偶然听到有人谈论幽栖居士与乔公子的一段情，白梅若心领神会，更觉得她与苗知瑜可以成为朋友。
Over in the women's wing, packed with Lin'an's elite ladies, Yao Qingyan circulated table to table with stout-drinking maids covering for her weak tolerance—swapping in plain water whenever trapped. Completing her rounds, she noticed Princess Jiahe absent too and queried Madam Xiang. Uninformed by Matron Wei about the street ambush yet haunted by last night's scene at Zhu Manor, Madam Xiang deflected mildly: "Royals stay busy—probably delayed." Asking Chen Wenxin brought only a discreet "No idea," so Yao Qingyan dropped it and kept hosting.

从苗知瑜的话语判断，显然是愿意与她做朋友，白梅若亲切称呼道：“知瑜，你我同饮一杯。”
Meanwhile, guests mingled between tables. Spotting Madam Qin step away, Bai Meiruo carried her cup over and slid into the empty seat beside Miao Zhiyu. "Why does Young Madam Qiao look so downcast?"

苗知瑜端起酒盅，两人碰过后，慢慢饮下。
Miao Zhiyu glanced at her. "Just call me Zhiyu."

白梅若放下酒盅，无意中瞧见夏果走进女宾区，朝妾室的席位走去。
"Oh? Not fond of 'Young Madam Qiao'?" Bai Meiruo probed lightly.

“不知多少男子羡慕乔公子，既娶了知瑜你这等才女，还纳了一位貌美的丫鬟做妾室。可不知为什么，我瞧着夏姨娘身着绫罗，还是一脸丫鬟相。”
"We know each other well enough, and I actually find your company pleasant—no need for stiff titles. Call me Zhiyu like a friend."

有人奚落夏果，苗知瑜自是欢心：“来，你我再饮一杯。”
New to Lin'an with few acquaintances, Bai Meiruo had hoped to build her own circle. Approaching Youqi Jushi felt awkward given her past as Lin Jue's wife; after chatting briefly with Princess Jiahe at the Qiao wedding, she had even tried calling at Youlin Retreat once—only to learn the princess was out at Wangyue Pavilion, Youqi Jushi's home! Figuring the two were thick as thieves, Bai Meiruo had backed off—until today's bombshell tore that friendship to ribbons! With the princess absent today, Miao Zhiyu made the ideal ally—especially after Bai Meiruo overheard noblewomen whispering about Youqi Jushi's old romance with Young Master Qiao.

她为二人满上，两人继续对饮。
Warming at once to Miao Zhiyu's invitation, she raised her cup. "A toast between us, Zhiyu."

饮完，苗知瑜说：“我还有几位闺中密友，今日她们没来，估计是家人不愿他们抛头露面，改日大家相约去永泰茶楼畅聊，如何？”
Clinking cups, they sipped—just as Xiaguo strolled back into the hall toward the concubines' table.

“能得知瑜邀请，不胜荣幸。”白梅若悄悄靠近苗知瑜：“你可知佳和郡主今日为何没来？”
"How men envy Young Master Qiao," Bai Meiruo murmured slyly, "marrying a talent like you AND taking a pretty maid as concubine! Yet funny—deck Concubine Xia in fine silks, and she still reeks of servant ways."

苗知瑜摇摇头：“不知。”
Hearing Xiaguo jabbed delighted Miao Zhiyu. "Pour another—let us drink again!"

“听说郡主被剑刺伤……”
Finishing the second cup, Miao Zhiyu suggested, "A few of my close friends skipped today because their families keep them out of crowds. Another day, let us all meet for tea and talk at Yongtai Teahouse!"

“啊，何人如此大胆？敢刺伤贵人？”
"I would be honored!" Leaning closer, Bai Meiruo dropped her voice. "Do you know why Princess Jiahe missed today?"

“我身边的丫鬟喜欢看热闹，非要跟着迎亲队伍跑，我没拦着。等她回来时，告诉我仪王今日跑去抢亲，佳和郡主前来阻拦，以性命威胁……”
Miao Zhiyu shook her head.

苗知瑜惊得声音不由变大：“什么？”
"Word is Her Highness took a sword wound..."

同桌的人都不解地看向苗知瑜。
"What?! Who dared stab royalty?!"

“没什么事，你们继续。”苗知瑜意识到自己失态，尴尬地笑笑。她拉起白梅若，来到喜宴一旁无人之处，急切地问，“听你刚才所说，仪王抢的是陈家的亲？”
"My nosy maid chased after the bridal procession for fun. When she ran back, she told me Prince Yi tried snatching the bride mid-street, and Princess Jiahe stepped in threatening her own life..."

白梅若点点头：“没想到幽栖居士连仪王都勾引。”
"WHAT?!" Miao Zhiyu's gasp drew puzzled stares across the table.

苗知瑜仔细回想：“怎么可能？她喜欢的人明明是文渊？”
Flushing, she forced a polite smile. "Nothing—carry on!" Pulling Bai Meiruo aside to a quiet corner, she whispered urgently, "You mean Prince Yi tried snatching the CHEN bride?!"

白梅若虽跟幽栖居士没有往来，但她作为林觉的前任，让她想起来甚觉不爽，不免胡乱猜测一番。
Bai Meiruo nodded. "Who knew Youqi Jushi even hooked Prince Yi?!"

“你嫁给乔公子，她没什么机会，为恶心你，把她身边的丫鬟给乔公子当妾室，自己则移情别恋，跑去勾引仪王。”
"Impossible! She loved Wenyuan!"

苗知瑜听着别人污蔑幽栖居士，心中多少受用，可依然需保持大家闺秀的风范。
Bearing quiet resentment toward her husband's ex-wife, Bai Meiruo spun freely. "Once you married Young Master Qiao, she had no shot left—so to spite you she shoved her maid into his house, pivoted straight around, and set her hooks in Prince Yi!"

“幽栖居士应该不是你说的那种人。”
Pleasant as it felt hearing Youqi Jushi torn down, Miao Zhiyu kept her refined mask intact. "Youqi Jushi hardly seems that sort..."

“是不是，很难说，能确定的是她将佳和郡主得罪。郡主今日受伤，若出什么意外倒也罢了；若无碍，幽栖居士以后的日子可不好过。”
"Hard to say—what IS certain is she crossed Princess Jiahe! If the princess recovers, Youqi Jushi is in for stormy weather."

苗知瑜无法想象即将发生的事：“她已嫁到陈家，郡主又能将她怎样？”
"She is already married into the Chen family—what can the princess do to her?"

“我们就等着看热闹吧。现下情形，你我及时关注郡主的消息，好找机会拜见她。”
"Wait and watch the fireworks! Right now, let us both keep an ear out for Her Highness's condition so we can call on her when the moment is right."

苗知瑜点点头，心中不解：“你为何对幽栖居士如此不满？”
Miao Zhiyu nodded, then asked curiously, "Why do you dislike Youqi Jushi so strongly?"

白梅若面色平静，吐露心声：“她倒没做过得罪我的事，可他是我夫君的前妻，夫君嘴上不说，心里依然对她念念不忘，没事总抱着本《王右丞诗集》看。本以为他喜欢诗，好几次见他连书都拿反，脸上却挂着笑容，便知他心不在诗。后来向他身边的下人打听，才知他是因幽栖居士才会捧着诗集看。”
A flat calm settled over Bai Meiruo's face. "She never wronged me directly—yet she is my husband's former wife. He says nothing aloud, yet his heart still clings to her! Always sitting around holding *Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng*. At first I thought he loved poetry—until I caught him grinning at it while holding the book UPSIDE DOWN! Asking his servants gave me the answer: he only holds it because of Youqi Jushi."

苗知瑜叹口气：“唉，没想到你我同病相怜。”
Miao Zhiyu let out a quiet sigh. "Looks like we are two sisters sharing the same ache."

二人怕引起他人注意，不便再说下去，回到喜宴，各自归位，继续与其他人说些客套话。
Wary of drawing stares, both returned to their seats and resumed polite small talk.
