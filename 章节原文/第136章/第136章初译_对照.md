第136章 一路上默默陪伴
## Chapter 136: Silent Company on the Road Home

千里寻径直往回走，速度比来时快得多，她担心自己忍不住会回头。
Qianli Xun marched back far faster than she had come, terrified one breath slower would make her turn around.

直到听不见文渊的哭声时，她再也忍不住，手炉落在地上，周围的雪因热气开始融化……
Only once Wenyuan's weeping faded behind the hills did her strength give out; the hand warmer tumbled from her grip into the snow, melting a wet ring where it landed...

眼看小姐要倒下，两个丫鬟正要去扶，仪王不知从哪里出现，将小姐扶住。
Just as her knees buckled and both maids lunged to catch her, Prince Yi stepped out of nowhere and caught her steady in his arms.

千里寻稍微缓一会儿，一把推开仪王，继续往回走。
Catching her breath for a second, Qianli Xun shoved Prince Yi off and kept walking.

苦荞捡起手炉，向仪王行完礼，跟上自家小姐。
Scooping up the hand warmer, Kuqiao bowed quick to Prince Yi and hurried after her mistress.

夏果以更快的速度赶上，堵在小姐面前，跪在雪地里，哭道：“小姐，乔公子太可怜，我想替您安慰安慰他。”
Racing ahead even faster, Xiaguo blocked her mistress's path, dropped to her knees in the snow, and wept: "Miss, Young Master Qiao is so pitiful—let me go comfort him for you!"

千里寻这才明白夏果的心思，用无力而又冷静的声音说：“你想做什么就去做吧，与我无关。”
Everything clicked at last for Qianli Xun. In a drained, cool tone she answered, "Do whatever you want—nothing to do with me."

夏果磕头感谢：“谢小姐！”
Xiaguo kowtowed in thanks: "Thank you, Miss!"

千里寻绕过夏果继续往回走，申简辰在她身后默默跟着。
Stepping around Xiaguo, Qianli Xun trudged on while Shen Jianchen followed in silence behind her.

他很羡慕文渊，竟然能让他难以追求的女子如此痛苦。他在心里埋怨道：“你若喜欢我，跟我在一起，我绝不会让你这么痛苦。”
Jealousy burned inside him for Wenyuan—that the one woman he could never win would bleed like this for another man! "If you loved me and chose me," he grumbled silently, "I'd never let you hurt like this."

走到马车旁，不等苦荞搀扶，千里寻径自登上马车。
Reaching the carriages, Qianli Xun climbed straight into hers without waiting for Kuqiao's arm.

仪王的马车也停在那里，白风守在一旁。
Prince Yi's carriage stood parked right beside it, Bai Feng waiting at the reins.

苦荞好久没见到白风，很想上前与他说话，可小姐的状态搞得她心情全无，根本顾不上白风，跟着小姐就要上马车，却被仪王挡住。
Much as Kuqiao had missed Bai Feng, seeing her mistress wrecked left her zero mood for romance. Stepping up to follow Miss aboard, Prince Yi stepped in her way.

“你去跟白风坐一辆，我与幽栖居士有话要说。”
"Ride with Bai Feng. I have words for Youqi Jushi."

对方是仪王，身份尊贵，他的话不得不听。
Against a royal prince's command, Kuqiao couldn't argue.

苦荞递上手炉：“麻烦王爷将手炉给我家小姐。”
Holding out the hand warmer: "Please give this back to my mistress, Your Highness."

申简辰接过，登上千里寻的马车。
Taking it, Shen Jianchen climbed into Qianli Xun's carriage.

白风见状，满心欢喜，上前拉起苦荞坐上仪王的马车。
Overjoyed, Bai Feng took Kuqiao's hand and helped her up into Prince Yi's carriage.

申简辰并未立刻将手炉送给千里寻，他担心一旦给她，她会立刻将他赶下马车。于是握在自己手中，坐在她对面，一句话不说，默默陪伴。
Holding onto the hand warmer instead of handing it right over—afraid she'd boot him straight back out—Shen Jianchen sat across from her in total silence, keeping quiet company.

千里寻视若无睹，她没有心情批判仪王，没有力气将他赶下车，任由他坐在对面，用目光照顾她。她只顾垂下眼帘，体会内心的伤痛。
Qianli Xun acted as if he were thin air, too drained to scold him or shove him out, letting him watch her while she kept her eyes lowered over her raw ache.

小姐走后，夏果从雪地里爬起来，向乔公子跑去。
The moment her mistress departed, Xiaguo scrambled out of the snow and ran straight back toward Young Master Qiao.

到达神柳旁时，乔文仙依然跪在雪地里流泪。
Beneath the Divine Willow, Qiao Wenxian still knelt in the snow weeping.

“公子，起来吧，地上的雪太冰冷，小心膝盖受凉。”南双一边劝说，一边去扶公子，被乔文仙甩开，只好继续无奈地站着，抬眼瞧见夏果。
"Get up, Young Master—that snow's freezing, you'll ruin your knees!" Nan Shuang tried hauling him up, got shoved off again, and stood helpless until spotting Xiaguo running up.

“公子，夏果来了，肯定是幽栖居士有话捎来。”
"Young Master, it's Xiaguo! Youqi Jushi must have sent word!"

乔文仙抬起泪眼，模模糊糊中夏果向他走来。
Lifting blurred, tear-soaked eyes, Qiao Wenxian saw Xiaguo hastening toward him.

他努力爬起，冲到夏果跟前：“淑真是不是后悔刚才说的话？她是不是同意跟我私奔？”
Scrambling up, he stumbled straight to her and caught her arms: "Did Shuzhen take it back?! Did she agree to elope with me?!"

夏果的眼中充满怜爱：“小姐并未让我捎话，我见乔公子可怜，想过来看看您。”
Tenderness brimmed in Xiaguo's eyes: "Miss didn't send me. Seeing Young Master hurting broke my heart, so I came back to check on you."

乔文仙放开夏果，一脸失落，转过身，往回走。
Dropping her arms in hollow disappointment, Qiao Wenxian turned and began trudging away.

夏果追上去，挡在乔文仙面前：“乔公子，世上女子千千万，不止小姐一个，她放弃您，还有别人值得您喜欢……”
Xiaguo ran ahead to block him: "Young Master Qiao, there are ten thousand women in this world besides Miss! She gave up on you, yet someone else right here loves you..."

乔文仙绕开夏果，继续踉跄着脚步。
Stepping around her, Qiao Wenxian stumbled on.

夏果继续追上去，堵在面前：“乔公子看看我，我跟小姐长得有几分相似，小姐不愿意给您当妾室，我愿意！”
Xiaguo circled ahead again: "Look at me, Young Master Qiao! Everyone says I look a little like Miss! Miss refused to be your concubine—I'm willing!"

公子还未惊讶，一旁的南双倒是惊讶不已，原来夏果看不上他，是因为眼光高，瞧上自家公子。
Before his master even reacted, Nan Shuang's jaw hit the snow—so THAT was why Xiaguo turned up her nose at him! Aiming straight for his master!

乔文仙双眼通红，往日未仔细瞧，今日瞧来，夏果与淑真的确有几分相似，他苦笑道：“相似又如何？你又不是她。”
Eyes rimmed raw red, Qiao Wenxian glanced at her—never noticed before, yet yes, she did carry a faint shadow of Shuzhen. A hollow laugh escaped him: "So what if you look like her? You aren't her."

“我已识字，也学会做茶，还读过些书，《王右丞诗集》我也读过，以后我还会读很多书……”夏果强调自身的优点，希望乔公子在失望之际愿意接受她。
"I can read now, I know how to whisk tea, I've read books—even 'Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng'! And I'll read heaps more..." Desperate to win him while his heart lay shattered, Xiaguo rattled off every credential she had.

“你走吧，不要烦我。”乔文仙冷冷地回应道，再一次绕过夏果踉跄着向池塘对岸走去。
"Go away and leave me in peace." Speaking chill as ice, Qiao Wenxian brushed past her once more, stumbling toward the far bank.

夏果还想上前拦住，被南双挡在面前：“我家公子不稀罕你，你还是不要再拦他，赶紧走吧。”
As Xiaguo moved to block him again, Nan Shuang stepped straight in front of her: "My master doesn't want you! Stop hounding him and scram!"

夏果停下来，对着乔文仙的背影大喊道：“乔公子！我不会放弃！相信总有一天，你会答应我！”
Halting, Xiaguo shouted after Qiao Wenxian's retreating back: "I won't give up, Young Master Qiao! One day you'll say yes to me!"

南双不满道：“你能嫁给我这样儿的都算不错，竟然想找我家公子那样的，别痴心妄想！”
Nan Shuang scoffed: "You'd be lucky to marry someone like me—dreaming of my master? Quit hallucinating!"

夏果怼道：“是你别痴心妄想才对！改日我成乔公子身边的妾室，看你还敢这么对我说话！”
Xiaguo snapped back: "You're the one hallucinating! Watch when I become Young Master Qiao's concubine—see if you dare talk to me like that then!"

南双“哼”一声，转身去追自家公子。
With a loud "Hmph!", Nan Shuang ran off to catch his master.

马车已到大街上，申简辰确定千里寻不会将他赶下去，才把手炉塞进她怀里：“天气冷，你拿着，手会暖和些。”
Once the carriage rolled onto the city avenues and Shen Jianchen felt sure Qianli Xun wouldn't kick him out, he tucked the hand warmer into her lap: "It's freezing—hold this to warm your hands."

千里寻没有拒绝，乖乖接过，揣在手中。也许她太需要有人关怀，仪王出现得恰如其分。尽管如此，她心中还是清楚，仪王不过是想乘虚而入，她再难过，也会守好防线，不会让他有机可乘。
Without pushing it back, Qianli Xun took it and curled her fingers around the warm brass. Maybe she really did crave a scrap of warmth right now, and Prince Yi's timing hit dead on. Even so, she stayed sharp—knowing he was fishing for an opening while her guard sat low, refusing to let him slip past her defenses.

苦荞与白风在一起虽开心，可因担心小姐，一直无法轻松下来。
Happy as Kuqiao was sitting beside Bai Feng, worry for her mistress kept her tense.

白风安慰道：“不用为幽栖居士担心，我家王爷说，她难过一阵儿，很快会从痛苦中走出来。”
Bai Feng comforted her: "Don't worry over Youqi Jushi—His Highness says she'll grieve a spell and bounce right back."

苦荞诧异道：“仪王何时如此了解我家小姐？”
Kuqiao blinked: "Since when does Prince Yi understand my mistress that well?"

白风想想说：“幽栖居士跟郡主是好朋友，王爷应该是从郡主那里了解到的。”
Bai Feng thought a second: "Youqi Jushi is Princess's best friend—His Highness probably heard it from Princess."

苦荞更感到纳闷：“白风，你不觉得奇怪吗？”
Kuqiao frowned deeper: "Don't you think it's weird, Bai Feng?"

“什么？”
"What's weird?"

“我家小姐与郡主是好朋友，今日受伤，郡主没来，反而你家王爷来，你说说，到底怎么回事？”
"My mistress is Princess's best friend—yet when Miss gets heartbroken today, Princess didn't come, Prince Yi came instead! Tell me what's up with that!"

“能为什么？还不是因为我家王爷心疼郡主，这么冷的天儿，怕她出来受凉，便帮她前来安慰幽栖居士。”
"Simple! His Highness spoils Princess—freezing snow out here, didn't want her catching cold, so he came to look after Youqi Jushi in her place!"

“哪有这道理？仪王对我家小姐来讲可是外男，难道郡主不忌讳？”
"What kind of logic is that?! Prince Yi's an unmarried man to my mistress—doesn't Princess mind?"

“倒把我问住，别管那么多，我们两个好不容易见面，说点儿别的。”
"Stumped me there! Forget all that—rare enough we get time alone, talk about something nicer!"

苦荞还是没有心情说别的，叹道：“乔公子与小姐本是郎才女貌，天生一对儿，我就是想不通，为何两人走不到一起？最后只能伤心分开。”
Still unable to shake her gloom, Kuqiao sighed: "Young Master Qiao and Miss were a match made in heaven—why on earth couldn't they end up together, ending in tears like this?"

“王爷说是因为父母之命媒妁之言所致，你放心，我父母都不在，你跟我之间，只有你情我愿。”
"His Highness says it's all because of parents' orders and matchmakers' words. Don't worry about us—I've got no parents, so between you and me it's pure free will!"

苦荞忽然觉得自己比小姐幸运许多，故意说：“你虽无父无母，可还有仪王，如若他强迫你娶别的女子，你会怎么办？”
Realizing she was luckier than her own mistress, Kuqiao teased, "No parents, sure, yet you've got Prince Yi! What if he orders you to marry someone else?"

“王爷要是强迫我，我就不给他当侍卫，带你离开临安，去汴梁，那里我熟人多，跟着王爷也攒了些银子，到时做点小买卖，日子不会太差。”
"If His Highness ever forces me, I'll quit being his guard and run away with you to Bianliang! Got plenty of buddies there and savings stacked up from serving His Highness—we'll open a little shop and live cozy!"

苦荞害羞道：“我才不跟你去汴梁，我还要伺候小姐。”
Kuqiao blushed: "Who said I'm running to Bianliang with you? I have to wait on Miss!"

“没事，你不想去汴梁，我就陪你在临安，大不了我给幽栖居士当侍卫。”
"Easy—don't want Bianliang, I'll stay right here in Lin'an and sign on as Youqi Jushi's guard instead!"

苦荞笑着埋怨：“说得好像仪王已强迫你娶别人一样。”
Kuqiao laughed while chiding him: "Talking like Prince Yi already forced you into a wedding!"

“说说而已，我家王爷很开明，绝对不会强迫我。”
"Just saying! His Highness is open-minded—he'd never force me."

马车已到望月轩门口，车夫将马车停下。
The carriage pulled up outside Wangyue Pavilion and came to a halt.

千里寻没有立刻下来，呆坐了一会儿，才说道：“感谢仪王一路陪伴，我该回去，还望仪王不要再做同样的事，徒劳无益。”
Qianli Xun stayed seated a silent beat before speaking: "Thank you for riding along, Prince Yi. I'm heading inside—please don't do things like this again. It's a waste of effort."

申简辰并未接话，关切地问：“你可好些？”
Ignoring the warning, Shen Jianchen asked with gentle care: "Feeling any better?"

“我好不好，与仪王无关。”
"How I feel is none of Prince Yi's concern."

“怎么与我无关？你难受，我心里也不好受。”
"How is it none of my concern? When you hurt, I hurt too."

千里寻只觉仪王不愧是情场高手，一路陪伴，又说些甜言蜜语，对一个受情伤的女子来讲，若理智稍微松懈，便成为他囊中之物。
What a grandmaster player, Qianli Xun marveled: quiet escort, silky lines—any heartbroken girl whose armor slipped an inch would fall straight into his lap!

还好，千里寻并非一般女子，岂能被他的甜言蜜语轻易蛊惑？
Lucky she wasn't any ordinary girl to melt under sweet talk.

“仪王是官家之子，何等尊贵，却陪草民一路，还说些甜言蜜语，难道想娶梦容后，再纳草民为妾？我把今日拒绝文渊时说过话送给仪王，这辈子我即使不嫁，孤独终老，也绝不给人当妾室。”
"Prince Yi is the Emperor's son, high above all—yet here you are escorting a commoner and whispering sweet nothings. Planning to marry Mengrong and take me as your concubine on the side? Let me give you the exact answer I gave Wenyuan today: even if I grow old alone without ever marrying, I will never be anyone's concubine."

千里寻说完，推开马车的门，将仪王一个人甩在马车里。
Pushing open the carriage door, she hopped down, leaving Prince Yi sitting alone inside.

申简辰苦笑道：“我什么时候想让你给我当妾室？你竟如此看我？我只是忍不住想要关心你，因为……我想娶的人只有你。”
A bitter laugh escaped Shen Jianchen: "When did I ever want you as a concubine? Is that really how you see me? I just can't stop caring about you—because you're the only woman I want to marry."

苦荞早已站在马车外，见小姐下车后直奔望月轩，赶紧与白风分别，跟着往回走。
Already waiting outside, Kuqiao saw her mistress march straight into Wangyue Pavilion, bade Bai Feng a hurried goodbye, and chased inside.

王爷半天不下来，白风等得不耐烦，提醒道：“王爷，该回去了。”
When His Highness still didn't climb down, Bai Feng nudged, "Time to head back, Your Highness."

申简辰如同大梦初醒，从马车上下来，朝望月轩内瞧了瞧，瞧不出所以然，又不敢贸然闯进去，尽管他很想。
Jolting as if from a trance, Shen Jianchen stepped out, gazed into Wangyue Pavilion's gate seeing nothing, and fought down the urge to stride right through.

最终只能叹口气，登上自己的马车，回幽林別居。
Letting out a sigh, he climbed back into his own carriage for Youlin Retreat.

千里寻回到闺房，一进屋，将门关上。
Inside Wangyue Pavilion, Qianli Xun marched straight into her bedchamber and shut the door.

苦荞慢了一步，被关在外面，她不用亲眼目睹便知，小姐定是又躺在床上，舔舐内心的伤口。
One step behind, Kuqiao stood outside the closed door knowing without looking that Miss was curled back under the quilt nursing her broken heart.

韦嬷嬷走来：“姑娘回来了？”
Matron Wei walked up: "Back already?"

苦荞点点头：“小姐比昨日还难过。”
Kuqiao nodded: "Even sadder than yesterday."

“不难过才不正常，这个坎儿总得过，过了便好。不要打扰姑娘，过一阵到门口听听，她若唤你就进去，不唤，你正好乐得轻松，该干什么干什么，又不是发生什么大事，姑娘大不了睡个三天三夜，也就恢复如初。”韦嬷嬷朝周围瞅瞅，“夏果呢？”
"Wouldn't be human if she weren't! Every hurdle has to be crossed once, then it's behind her. Leave her in peace—check the door later; if she calls, go in, if not, enjoy a breather. Nothing catastrophic happened; three days of sleep and she'll bounce right back." Scanning the yard, Matron Wei frowned: "Where's Xiaguo?"

苦荞靠近嬷嬷，小声说：“我今日才知，夏果喜欢乔公子，小姐正难受着，她竟然跪下，请小姐同意让她去安慰乔公子。”
Stepping close, Kuqiao whispered, "Found out today Xiaguo's got a crush on Young Master Qiao! Right while Miss was falling apart, she dropped to her knees begging to go comfort Young Master Qiao!"

韦嬷嬷不满道：“我早知道她不安分，没想到心气儿如此高，竟瞧上乔公子，还不顾姑娘心痛，主动要求去安慰，真不知羞耻！等她回来，看我怎么批评她。”
Matron Wei bristled: "Knew that girl didn't know her place—didn't dream she'd aim all the way up at Young Master Qiao, ditching a heartbroken mistress to chase him! Shameless! Wait till she gets back—I'll give her a proper dressing-down!"

苦荞劝道：“嬷嬷还是算了，小姐都不跟她计较，您又何必管她？”
Kuqiao put in a mild word: "Forget it, Matron—even Miss didn't hold it against her."

“姑娘不与她计较，那是心胸宽广，我与她计较，是作为长辈教她怎么做人。等她回来，你让她来我屋里一趟。”韦嬷嬷说完离开。
"Young Lady lets it go out of generosity; I step in as an elder to teach her basic decency! Send her straight to my room the second she walks through the gate." Turning sharp, Matron Wei marched off.

小姐无需人伺候，苦荞进入丫鬟房，一边为小姐担心，一边拿出白风送的泥人，念叨着：“我家小姐人好、心好、又有才华，失忆前感情之路不顺，为何失忆后还是如此？”
With her mistress needing no tending, Kuqiao returned to the maids' quarters, worried for Miss as she took out Bai Feng's clay figurine and murmured to it: "Miss is kind, good, and brilliant—why was her love life cursed before amnesia and cursed all over again after?"

念叨完，又甚觉可笑，泥人又不是菩萨像，对着它说有何用？只怪最近难得见到白风，总喜欢把泥人拿出来睹物思人，才养成对着泥人说话的习惯。
Mid-murmur she caught herself and laughed—a clay doll wasn't a Bodhisattva, what use praying to it? Seeing Bai Feng so rarely lately had turned talking to his figurine into a silly habit.
