第231章 折磨计划开始了
## Chapter 231: The Torture Plan Begins

从老太太的院子回来，千里寻刚走进望月轩，碰见丑丫面带桃花急急匆匆而来。
她见到小姐小心拜见：“小姐。”
“怎么着急忙慌的？”
丑丫心虚，撒谎道：“没……没什么，我还有事，不打扰小姐。”
看着丑丫慌张离开，千里寻纳闷：“丑丫今日怎么了？”
苦荞说：“看上去像是担心您批评她。”
千里寻开玩笑道：“不对，我怎么觉得她好像是急着私会情郎。”
两人议论完，并未当回事。
刚走到素心草堂门口，想起陈浩宇，也该实施报复计划。
“你去把博雅斋的钥匙取来。”
苦荞去西院拿钥匙，千里寻则往后院拐去。
来到后院，她先看向静宜堂，门紧闭，韦嬷嬷应该一大早出去忙活。千里寻从心底感激这位嬷嬷，她悄悄为自己做了许多事，事事都做在她的心上。
然后才面向博雅斋，关着陈浩宇也不是长久之计，再说关着也不能泄心头之恨，今日不如与他好好聊聊，说一说折磨他的方法。
很快苦荞将钥匙取来，上前打开大锁……
青瓜出去后，陈浩宇一直坐在厅堂等着。
Returning from Old Madam Xia's courtyard into Moxiang Courtyard, Qianli Xun ran straight into Chouya rushing past with rosy, glowing cheeks.

听到有人开锁，一阵高兴，以为是奶奶前来做主，要求朱淑真放他出去。他快速起身凑到门口，期待看到老太太心疼他的目光。
"Miss," Chouya curtsied nervously.

结果门被打开，走进来的只有朱淑真和丫鬟苦荞。
"What's the rush?"

陈浩宇朝门外再瞧瞧，一个人影都没有。
Flustered, Chouya fibbed, "N-nothing! Got chores to do—won't keep you!" and scampered off.

千里寻不屑地问：“怎么？还希望陈家长辈来救你？告诉你，他们已经把管教你的权力交给我，老娘正好趁此机会好好折磨你。”
"What's gotten into Chouya today?" Qianli Xun wondered aloud.

“你胡说！奶奶那么疼我，我娘也心软，怎么可能让你管教我？”陈浩宇认为朱淑真不过是欺骗他而已，门并未被关上，他打算趁机跑出去。
"Looked scared you'd scold her," Kuqiao guessed.

结果被千里寻挡在面前：“想跑？没那么容易，今儿这门敞开着，只要我在，你甭想走出去。”
"Nah—looked more like she's rushing off to meet a secret boyfriend!" Qianli Xun joked, dismissing it.

陈浩宇气得用食指指着朱淑真，说不出话来。干脆转身坐到椅子上，平复心情后，问道：“你倒是说说，怎么样才能放我出去？”
Reaching Suxin Thatched Hall, she remembered Chen Haoyu—time to roll out her payback schedule. "Go fetch the key to Boya Study."

“我今儿来，就是打算放你出去。”
While Kuqiao ran to the West Courtyard, Qianli Xun strolled into the back courtyard. Glancing first at Jingyi Hall's shut door—Matron Wei out handling business as usual—gratitude warmed her chest for everything the matron quietly did behind the scenes. Then she faced Boya Study. Locking Chen Haoyu up forever solved nothing and didn't even scratch her itch for payback; better lay out her terms face to face.

尽管希望如此，陈浩宇还是一脸不相信：“你会这么好？”
Kuqiao returned with the key and clicked open the heavy padlock.

“我还真没有那么好。”千里寻向苦荞吩咐，“我边说，你边记。”
Inside, Chen Haoyu had sat waiting for Qinggua's rescue. At the click of the lock, he leaped up and hurried to the door expecting his grandmother's doting face—only to find Zhu Shuzhen and Kuqiao standing alone on the threshold, not another soul behind them.

苦荞走到书桌前坐下，展开纸认真听小姐说的重要信息。
Qianli Xun shot him a smirk. "Still waiting for the Chen elders to ride to your rescue? Hate to break it to you, they handed me full authority to discipline you—and I plan to put it to great use making your life miserable."

千里寻又面向陈浩宇：“打今儿起，你可以走出博雅斋，但不能走出陈府。”
"Liar! Grandmother dotes on me and Mother's softhearted—no way they handed me over to you!" Seeing the door wide open, he lunged to bolt past her.

陈浩宇怒道：“凭什么？”
Qianli Xun stepped squarely across the doorway. "Running? Dream on. That door can stay propped open all day, and while I stand here, you aren't taking one step out."

“凭你打不过我，凭陈家长辈把管教你的权力交给我。”
Shaking a furious finger at her speechless, Chen Haoyu spun back and slumped into a chair. Catching his breath, he snapped, "Fine—what does it take for you to let me out?"

“我才不信你的一面之词！”
"I came here right now to let you out."

“信不信由你，识相的乖乖配合，认真听老娘说话。”
Suspicion narrowed his eyes. "You're being that generous?"

陈浩宇捂住耳朵：“不听！不听！就不听！看你能把我怎么的！”
"Hardly generous." Qianli Xun nodded to Kuqiao. "I dictate, you write."

千里寻甚觉聒噪，迅速上前点了陈浩宇的穴位，他立刻不能说话也无法动弹，只能通过表情表达自己的不满。
Kuqiao sat at the desk and spread a sheet of paper.

“这下你总算安静，我可以好好说说如何管教你。首先，取消你所有月钱，要想有钱花，必须自己努力赚取……”
Facing Chen Haoyu, Qianli Xun declared, "Starting today, you may leave Boya Study—just not Chen Manor."

陈浩宇口不能说话，拳头攥紧，心想：“哼！连二十两的月钱都取消，实在过分！本公子长这么大，从来都是衣来伸手，饭来张口，什么时候自己赚过银子？”
"Says who?!"

“其次，给你列出几条赚取银子的方法。其一，每日绕陈府的主路跑十圈儿，可得十文；其二，每日完成背诵诸子百家的文章和诗词任务，可得三十文；其三，每日完成一件劳动任务，可得五十文；其四，向你欺负过的人说一声‘对不起’可得一两银子……”
"Says my fists, and says the Chen elders who put me in charge of whipping you into shape."

以上要求，都是千里寻临时起意想出，折磨中有教育，教育中有折磨，她甚是满意，因为这四条都是陈浩宇不喜欢做的事。还想继续添加，一时想不起来：“先说四条，后面想起什么来，再随时添加。苦荞，可记好？”
"I don't buy one word of your story!"

苦荞放下笔：“记得清清楚楚。”
"Believe it or not, be smart and listen close."

“好，让陈浩宇在上面按下指印。”
Chen Haoyu clapped both hands over his ears. "Not listening! Not listening! Do your worst!"

陈浩宇不能动弹，任由朱淑真掰开他的拳头，在苦荞拿来的印朱里摩擦两下，又在纸上狠狠按下。他心中那个气，无处宣泄，脸憋得通红。
Fed up with his racket, Qianli Xun stepped in and tapped two acupoints. Instantly he froze mute and rigid, reduced to glaring daggers.

完毕后，千里寻点开陈浩宇的穴位。
"Peace and quiet at last. Now listen to how I'm disciplining you. First, your entire monthly allowance is canceled. Want spending money? Earn it yourself."

陈浩宇二话不说，用拳头向朱淑真袭击而去：“朱淑真！你说的那些本公子一件都不会做！”
Unable to speak, Chen Haoyu's fists clenched white inside his sleeves: Canceling even twenty taels?! Outrageous! Grown up with robes handed to me and meals spoon-fed—when have I ever earned silver?!

千里寻迅速拿住陈浩宇，将他的手臂在背后拧住：“你倒提醒我，不做是吧？还有惩罚，一件不做替老娘倒一日马桶！二件不做替老娘倒两日马桶！全部不做替老娘连续倒十日马桶！若负隅顽抗，就替老娘倒一辈子马桶！”
"Second, here is your wage scale. Item one: run ten laps around Chen Manor's main road daily for ten wen. Item two: memorize assigned classics and poetry daily for thirty wen. Item three: complete one daily chore for fifty wen. Item four: say 'I'm sorry' to someone you've bullied for one tael of silver."

陈浩宇忍着痛说：“我们陈家茅厕多，又干净，上大号都去那里，根本没有马桶，只有夜壶，气死你！”
Improvised on the spot—half torture, half boot camp, every task guaranteed to make Chen Haoyu crawl out of his skin—she beamed in satisfaction. "Four items to start; I'll add more as they occur to me. Got all that, Kuqiao?"

千里寻笑道：“这还不简单，为惩罚你，大不了老娘往后用马桶专门解大号，臭死你！”
Kuqiao laid down her brush. "Every word."

陈浩宇占不上任何便宜，打又打不过，骂又骂不过，不如装得态度好些：“朱淑真，我向你道歉好不好？你就别跟我计较，散播流言是我不对，要打要骂随你，只是别把我打出残疾来。”
"Good. Get his thumbprint on it."

对方态度好转，千里寻松开手：“苦荞，去把红豆叫来。”
Paralyzed, Chen Haoyu could only turn purple with rage as Qianli Xun pried his thumb open, pressed it into the red ink paste Kuqiao held out, and stamped it firm onto the contract.

苦荞在按有姑爷指印的纸张上添加完小姐刚刚说的，放下笔，赶快出去找红豆。
Done, she tapped open his acupoints.

博雅斋的门不会再锁上，陈浩宇也终于放心，坐回椅子上：“朱淑真，本公子这辈子最后悔的事情就是娶你，你呢？是不是也后悔嫁给本公子？”
The instant feeling flooded back, Chen Haoyu swung a wild punch at her. "Zhu Shuzhen! I'm not doing a single one of those things!"

自从决定嫁给陈浩宇，千里寻告诫自己，以后无论发生什么，千万不能后悔。因此她从未想过后悔之事：“老娘后不后悔关你屁事！”
Catching his wrist effortlessly, Qianli Xun twisted his arm up behind his back. "Glad you reminded me—forgot the penalties! Skip one task, empty my chamber pot for one day! Skip two, two days! Skip all four, ten straight days! Resist altogether, and you empty my chamber pot for life!"

陈浩宇臆测道：“你肯定也后悔，不如我们和离，你也好自由自在。再说仪王对你有意思，我看他对你那个痴情的样子，绝对不会嫌弃你和离过两次，我们两个不如心平气和商量商量和离之事？”
"Ow! Chen Manor has spotless latrines everywhere—nobody uses chamber pots for number two, only urinals! Joke's on you!" he yelped through the pain.

听到“和离”两个字，千里寻心中莫名悲伤。本想着第一次和离之后能获得美满姻缘，谁知再次嫁的人又开口提出这两个字。莫说她没想过，就是想过，也没有了第一次和离时的冲劲儿，也许是因为第一次和离耗去她大部分心力。
Qianli Xun grinned wickedly. "Easy fix! Just to punish you, I'll start using a chamber pot exclusively for number two—stink you right out of your boots!"

陈浩宇见朱淑真表现出悲伤的神情，自然开心，故意调侃：“你该不会喜欢上本公子？不舍得离开？也是，本公子玉树临风，人见人爱，花见花开，你喜欢倒十分正常，既然喜欢我，就不要折磨我，好好待我，说不定我一高兴也喜欢上你，呵呵……！”
Outmatched in muscle and outmatched in wit, Chen Haoyu tried softening his tone. "Look, Zhu Shuzhen, what if I just apologize right now? Let it go! Spreading rumors was my fault—hit me, curse me, do whatever, just don't cripple me!"

千里寻从悲伤的情绪中清醒过来，对方调侃的表情和语气，令她厌恶：“闭上你的臭嘴！告诉你，你想和离，老娘偏不如你的愿，等老娘哪天把你折磨够，气消了再考虑要不要跟你和离的事儿。”
Seeing him fold, Qianli Xun let go. "Kuqiao, go call Hongdou."

陈浩宇本想发怒，又觉发怒无用，干脆继续调侃：“听说你跟林觉和离时使了老鼻子劲儿，现在本公子主动要求和离，你却不同意，不是喜欢本公子又是什么？本公子对美女来者不拒，要不主动投怀送抱一个？”
Jotting down the chamber-pot penalty on the thumbprinted sheet, Kuqiao hurried off to summon Hongdou.

千里寻向来最讨厌男人的色相，遇见必打无疑，伸出拳头一拳打在陈浩宇的脸上……
Relieved the door stayed unlocked, Chen Haoyu sank back into his chair. "Zhu Shuzhen, my biggest regret in life is marrying you. What about you? Regret marrying me?"

陈浩宇的左半边脸立刻一片乌青，他捂着脸：“你是不是有病？”
Ever since choosing to walk down the aisle, Qianli Xun had forbidden herself from wasting breath on regret. "Whether I regret it is none of your damn business!"

千里寻收起拳头：“你才有病！没事调侃老娘，老娘最讨厌你刚才的样子！”
"Bet you regret it too," Chen Haoyu probed. "Why don't we get a mutual divorce and set you free? Prince Yi's clearly crazy about you—infatuated as he is, he won't care if you've divorced twice! Why not sit down calm and hash out a mutual divorce?"

手中的汗接触到伤口，蜇得慌，陈浩宇不得放下手：“就你这样儿！也不知道乔文仙和仪王喜欢你什么？”
The words "mutual divorce" struck an unexpected hollow note in her chest. After her first divorce she had dreamed of a happy match, only for her second husband to bring up divorce right out of the gate. Not that she wanted one—she simply had no energy left for that war; her first divorce had drained every ounce of fight out of her.

红豆和苦荞进来，她们见姑爷的脸上一片淤青，甚是滑稽，心中不觉偷乐。
Spotting sadness cross her face, Chen Haoyu lit up and teased, "Wait—don't tell me you fell for me and can't bear leaving? Makes sense, tall and handsome as I am, beloved by every maiden in town! Since you like me, quit torturing me and treat me sweet—maybe I'll get in a good mood and like you back! Hahaha!"

苦荞到书桌旁去拿写有条款的纸张；红豆则问：“小姐，有何吩咐？”
Snapping out of her gloom, disgust flared hot at his leering smirk. "Shut your filthy mouth! Want a divorce? Too bad—I'm refusing just to spite you! Maybe once I've tortured you raw and my grudge cools, I'll think about it."

“你派两个人，从现在起跟着陈浩宇，负责监督他，每日让他按照纸上写好的一一完成。”
Too smart to blow up, he kept needle-poking. "Heard you fought tooth and nail to divorce Lin Jue, yet now I hand you a divorce on a silver platter and you say no? If that isn't secret love, what is? I never turn down a pretty woman—go ahead, throw yourself in my arms!"

“是，我这就去安排。”
Nothing set Qianli Xun's fist flying faster than a sleazy smirk. Crack! One clean punch landed square on Chen Haoyu's left cheek.

苦荞把纸张给了红豆：“都在上面写着，你让监督的下人看后记在心里，然后再拿给我。”
Clutching a bruised face swelling blue on the spot, he gasped, "Are you crazy?!"

红豆接过纸张，出去安排人。
Qianli Xun shook out her knuckles. "You're the crazy one! Keep flirting like a creep—I hate that leering mug more than anything!"

陈浩宇问：“我现在是不是可以出去？”
Sweat stinging the fresh welt forced his hand down. "Acting like a monster like this—heaven knows what Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi ever saw in you!"

千里寻说：“现在还不行，等负责监督你的下人来了再说。”
Right then Hongdou and Kuqiao stepped in, struggling to stifle grins at the comic purple shiner blooming across his face.

陈浩宇心想只要不被锁着便好，他一旦出去先找奶奶哭诉一番。
Kuqiao retrieved the rule sheet while Hongdou asked, "Your orders, Miss?"

想到找奶奶，不免心中疑惑，为何青瓜出去这么久还未回来？按说他不可能不去找老太太，既然去找了老太太为何到现在还没来？难道陈家的长辈真的被朱淑真收买？
"Assign two men to shadow Chen Haoyu starting now. Make sure he completes every task on this sheet daily."

“奶奶和我爹娘果真让你管教我？”
"Yes, Miss—right away."

千里寻骄傲道：“那是当然，长辈们还说，他们绝不插手此事。”
Kuqiao handed the sheet to Hongdou. "Have the watchers memorize every line, then bring it back to me." Hongdou hurried off.

“我不信！”
"Can I walk out now?" Chen Haoyu asked.

“信不信由你。我既然敢把你放出来，就不怕你到长辈面前哭诉。”
"Not until your shadows arrive."

红豆带着阿升和一名男仆前来：“小姐，我把阿升和三宝带来，以后由他们两个负责监督姑爷。”
So long as the padlock stayed off, Chen Haoyu figured he could run straight to Grandmother and weep his case. Wait—why hadn't Qinggua come back yet? Had Zhu Shuzhen truly bought off the entire Chen clan? "Did Grandmother and my parents really put you in charge of disciplining me?"

红豆把手中的纸张还给苦荞，小声说：“他们都已记下。”
"Obviously!" Qianli Xun lifted her chin. "Promised not to meddle one bit, too."

苦荞点点头。
"Don't believe you!"

千里寻对眼前的两个下人十分满意，嘱咐道：“你们两个记着，每日紧跟着姑爷，不准让他走出陈府，当然在陈府内他想去哪里不必限制。白天可以走出博雅斋，到了晚上，他睡下后依旧给门上好锁，以免他趁大家睡着跑出去。”
"Suit yourself. Think I'd unlock your door if I feared you running crying to the elders?"

两名下人回道：“谨遵小姐吩咐。”
Hongdou returned with Asheng and another male servant. "Miss, Asheng and Sanbao will watch Young Master from here on out." Returning the paper to Kuqiao, she added, "They memorized every word."

一切安排妥当，千里寻朝陈浩宇“哼”一声：“你可以出去了。”
Satisfied, Qianli Xun instructed both men. "Stick to him like glue all day. Do not let him step outside Chen Manor's gates—inside the manor, he can roam wherever he wants. Days out of Boya Study are fine, yet once he goes to bed at night, lock the door so he doesn't slip out in the dark."

陈浩宇二话不说，跑着跳出门外，慨叹道：“自由的空气真好！”
"Understood, Miss!" both chorused.

他转过头：“朱淑真，你等着，我现在就去找奶奶！”
Everything set, Qianli Xun gave Chen Haoyu a cool scoff. "You're free to go."

阿升和三宝紧跟姑爷向前院走去。
Chen Haoyu shot out the door with a whoop. "Sweet air of freedom!" Spinning back, he jabbed a finger at her. "Just wait, Zhu Shuzhen—I'm heading straight for Grandmother!"

报复计划已开始实施，千里寻却没有多么开心，只因陈浩宇提到和离，提到仪王，她又想起梦容。她之所以愿意嫁给陈浩宇，不愿走上和离之路，多半是因为她。
With Asheng and Sanbao hot on his heels, he marched toward the front yard.

抢亲那日梦容说出恨她的话依然萦绕在耳边，这恨，令她望而却步，不敢对心中的感情有任何奢望。
Her revenge campaign had launched, yet Qianli Xun felt strangely flat. Chen Haoyu mentioning divorce and Prince Yi had brought Mengrong right back into her mind—the real reason she had accepted this marriage and refused divorce. Mengrong's furious vow of hatred from the wedding day still echoed in her ears, killing any dare to hope for her own heart.

苦荞陪小姐回到素心草堂，小姐不想说话，也不写话本，坐在书桌前发呆。苦荞则将手中的纸张收起来，默默站在一旁陪伴。
Following her back into Suxin Thatched Hall, Kuqiao put away the rule sheet and stood quietly beside her mistress as Qianli Xun stared blankly at her untouched desk.
