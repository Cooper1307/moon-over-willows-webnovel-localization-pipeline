第7章 不被官家付重任
## Chapter 7: An Unfavored Prince

仪王府挺大，像申简辰在城市里逛过的公园那么大。
Prince Yi's Manor was huge—about the size of a city park Shen Jianchen used to stroll through.

房屋建得不多，更多的是景观，赏心悦目；还有一半的土地并未开发，长满了天然的野花、野草和野树。逛到这里，申简辰停住脚步。
Buildings took up only a small part; most of the grounds showcased pleasing landscapes, while half the land remained untouched, overrun with wildflowers, tall grass, and wild trees. Reaching this edge, Shen Jianchen stopped.

乔梦容一直跟在仪王身后，显然新生的哥哥对她没有以前那么热情。要搁以前，他绝对会拉着她的手，处处照顾，看她的眼神也是含情脉脉。尽管那时的他拖着病体，很少在王府走动，可仅有的几次，已让她感受到浓浓的温情。
Trailing behind him, Qiao Mengrong felt the chill in her reborn brother's manner. Before, he would hold her hand, fuss over her every step, and gaze at her with deep affection. Even frail and rarely able to walk the grounds, those rare moments had wrapped her in warmth.

奇迹般醒来的哥哥什么都不记得，看样子连他们之间的感情都忘了。乔梦容在心里埋怨孟婆汤，转念一想，要不是孟婆汤，哥哥怎能身体康健？以前虽情浓，可哥哥拖着病体，即使嫁了迟早也要守寡。
Now he remembered nothing—not even their love. Resentment toward Mengpo's Soup flickered in her heart, until another thought checked it: without that soup, how could he be healthy? Intense love meant little if his frail body left her a young widow.

想到这里，乔梦容认为自己不能要求太多，现在的哥哥身形虽然还像以前一样消瘦，但看着精神饱满，更有股阳刚之气，就是那背影也仿佛充满力量，是乔梦容多次在梦中梦到的哥哥的样子。
She told herself not to ask for too much. Lean as his frame still was, he radiated vigor and masculine strength; even his back looked sturdy, just like the brother she had seen countless times in her dreams.

乔梦容不由向背影靠近，刚走近两步，背影转过来，乔梦容立刻止步，害羞地站在原地。
Drawn forward, she took two steps closer—only for him to turn around. Qiao Mengrong froze in place, cheeks flushing shy.

“佳和郡主，为何王府有一半儿土地荒着？”
"Princess Jiahe, why is half the manor left wild?"

“哥哥觉得建造一半儿已够居住，另一半便保持原貌，关键是哥哥喜欢天然的景色。”
"You felt half was plenty to live in, Brother, so you left the rest untouched. Above all, you love natural scenery."

乔梦容说完，提出要求：“哥哥以前从来不称呼我佳和郡主，只会唤我梦容，能不能还像以前一样称呼我？”
Then she ventured a plea. "You never called me Princess Jiahe before—only Mengrong. Could you still call me that?"

眼前的郡主一脸娇羞，申简辰不忍伤害，毕竟她与赵朴有情，又有婚约：“好吧，我就称呼你梦容，不过，很多事情我都不记得，那婚约……”
Faced with her shy look, Shen Jianchen hated to hurt her; she and Zhao Pu had loved each other and shared a betrothal. "Alright, Mengrong it is. Still, I don't remember much, so about that betrothal..."

哥哥又像以前一样称呼她，乔梦容很开心：“至于婚约，哥哥不必着急，官家赐婚的诏书上写着明年中秋节时成亲。”
Delighted to hear her name on his lips again, Qiao Mengrong beamed. "No rush on the betrothal, Brother. His Majesty's marriage decree sets the wedding for next year's Mid-Autumn Festival."

申简辰再瞧瞧眼前的女孩儿，娇羞婉约，应是很多男子喜欢的类型：“我要是不醒来，岂不是婚约无效？”
Studying the gentle, blushing girl—surely the type most men fell for—he asked, "If I hadn't woken up, wouldn't the betrothal be void?"

“怎么会无效？可是官家金口玉言所赐，即使哥哥不醒来，我都想好，替哥哥守一辈子仪王府。”
"How could an imperial decree spoken by His Majesty himself be void? Even if you hadn't woken up, I had made up my mind to stay and guard Prince Yi's Manor for you all my life."

申简辰替仪王感动，还是问道：“你看着也才十五六岁，正是玩耍的好年纪，为何要早早成亲？”
Moved on Zhao Pu's behalf, Shen Jianchen still asked, "You look barely fifteen or sixteen—right at the age to enjoy life. Why marry so young?"

“我已经十六了，到了该嫁人的年纪，如果再拖下去，只怕嫁不出去。”
"I'm already sixteen—right at marrying age. Wait any longer and no one will want me."

申简辰才想起这是古代，女子和男子成婚的年纪都小，不由对仪王的年龄产生兴趣：“梦容，你告诉我，我到底多大？”
Remembering people wed young in this era, Shen Jianchen grew curious about Zhao Pu's age. "Mengrong, tell me—how old am I?"

“哥哥刚过十八岁。”
"You just turned eighteen, Brother."

穿越后竟然比以前年轻了十二岁,申简辰忍不住说了声:“Yes!”
Twelve years younger in one leap! Shen Jianchen couldn't help cheering, "Yes!"

看到佳和郡主与丫鬟不解的表情，赶紧解释：“我是太高兴，我还这么年轻，实现了人生重来一次的愿望。”
Catching the puzzled stares of the princess and her maids, he covered fast. "Just thrilled to be this young—feels like getting my wish to start life over!"

乔梦容不甚理解，刚要继续追问哥哥为何要说人生重来一次？话还未出口，下人来报：“王爷，郡主，宫中派内侍前来探望王爷。”
Baffled, Qiao Mengrong opened her mouth to ask what he meant by starting life over, when a servant hurried up to report: "Your Highness, Princess, an Imperial Attendant from the palace has come to call on His Highness."

乔梦容说道：“哥哥，内侍来了，我们得去见见。”
"Brother, an Imperial Attendant is here. We have to go greet him," Qiao Mengrong said.

“内侍？是干什么的？”申简辰不大明白古代的称谓。
"Imperial Attendant? What's that?" Shen Jianchen wasn't up on court titles.

“内侍，是宫中伺候官家的人，肯定是官家派来的，你我必须前去接待。”
"Someone who serves His Majesty inside the palace. His Majesty must have sent him—we have to receive him."

听了这解释，申简辰明白对方身份尊贵，便与乔梦容匆匆往回走。
Catching the rank involved, Shen Jianchen hurried back with Qiao Mengrong.

来到厅堂，已有三人在此等候，中间一位年长许多，两边站在两个小跟班，衣着都一样。他们的打扮令申简辰想起影视剧中太监的形象，心想：“原来内侍就是太监。”
Three men waited in the main hall: an older man in the center flanked by two young attendants in matching robes. Their outfits brought TV eunuchs straight to mind. So Imperial Attendants are eunuchs, Shen Jianchen realized.

只见内侍迎上来：“真是神奇，殿下果然跟以前不同。官家开始听说殿下不幸病逝，正伤心，结果又传来喜讯，说殿下活过来，而且疾病全无，便派我来瞧瞧。从走路的姿态看，殿下的确已脱离病体。”
The senior eunuch stepped forward. "Truly a marvel! Your Highness looks nothing like before. His Majesty was grieving upon hearing you had passed away, only for glad news to follow that you had revived with every ailment gone, so he sent me to see for myself. Just by your stride, Your Highness has cast off illness for good!"

申简辰对仪王的生活不熟悉，但他也是见过世面之人，轻松应对：“感谢父皇关怀，辛苦内侍亲自跑一趟，只是我虽有幸活过来，身体的疾病也消除，可以前的记忆全无，要不是郡主和邹嬷嬷帮我解答疑惑，我都不知道自己是谁。”
Unfamiliar with Zhao Pu's past yet well versed in handling people, Shen Jianchen responded with ease. "My thanks for Imperial Father's concern, and thank you for making the trip in person. Though fortunate enough to revive and shed my illness, I have lost all memory of the past. If not for the Princess and Matron Zou, I wouldn't even know who I am."

“竟有这等事？”内侍觉得新奇，目光瞟向一旁的佳和郡主。
"Such a thing happened?" Intrigued, the eunuch glanced over at Princess Jiahe.

乔梦容赶紧说：“哥哥所说非虚，他睁开眼睛时，我是谁都不记得，还请内侍禀报官家，如若哥哥以后哪里做得不对，定要海涵。”
Qiao Mengrong spoke up at once. "Brother speaks the truth—when he opened his eyes, he didn't even recognize me. Please report this to His Majesty, and ask him to be magnanimous and forgive Brother if he makes any slip in the future."

内侍笑道：“记不得就记不得，重要的是人还在，疾病全消，真是可喜可贺，佳和郡主以后可是有福了。”
The eunuch chuckled. "Lost memory or not, what matters is that he lives and his ailments are gone—a joyous blessing indeed! Princess Jiahe has great fortune ahead of her."

乔梦容红着脸，从丫鬟手中拿过钱袋，走到内侍跟前：“辛苦内侍跑一趟，一点心意，不成敬意。”
Blushing, Qiao Mengrong took a money pouch from a maid and stepped forward. "Thank you for taking the trouble, Attendant. A small token of appreciation—nothing worthy of your rank."

内侍很自然地接过：“还是佳和郡主懂礼数，我回去定向官家把仪王殿下的情况禀明，告辞。”
The eunuch pocketed it with practiced ease. "Princess Jiahe knows proper etiquette as always. I will report Prince Yi's recovery to His Majesty in full. Farewell."

内侍行过礼后，带人离开。
After bowing, he led his attendants out.

申简辰瞅着内侍离去的背影，问道：“你刚才为什么要给他钱？”
Watching them leave, Shen Jianchen asked, "Why did you give him money?"

乔梦容叹口气：“唉，内侍是官家身边的人，在官家跟前说话大有作用，给他些银子，他会在官家面前替哥哥美言几句，即使不夸哥哥，也不会说哥哥坏话。”
Qiao Mengrong sighed. "Imperial Attendants stay at His Majesty's side, so their words carry weight. Slipping him some silver ensures he puts in a good word for you—or at least doesn't speak ill of you."

申简辰很不满：“我不是官家的儿子吗？怎么还需要内侍替我美言？”
Shen Jianchen frowned. "Aren't I His Majesty's son? Why do I need a eunuch to put in a good word for me?"

乔梦容解释道：“看来哥哥确实什么都不记得。我来告诉哥哥，你虽是官家的儿子，可历来不被官家重视，你病着，官家从来都没看过你，也从不召见哥哥，每次只吩咐内侍来传话。所以哥哥的情况都是内侍说给官家听，他想怎么说都可以，使了银子起码保证他不说坏话。”
"You really don't remember a thing, Brother," Qiao Mengrong explained. "Though you are His Majesty's son, he has never favored you. Through all your years of illness, he never visited and never summoned you, only sending attendants to pass messages. Everything His Majesty hears about you comes through their mouths. A bit of silver at least buys peace."

申简辰这才知道，原来赵朴是位不受宠的王爷。他倒无所谓，不受宠有不受宠的好处，省去很多麻烦，自由自在。
So Zhao Pu was an unfavored prince. Fine by Shen Jianchen—less attention meant fewer headaches and more freedom.

“以后内侍来，不用再给他银子，他想在官家面前说什么，随他。”
"Next time an attendant comes, don't give him a single coin. Let him tell His Majesty whatever he pleases."

“绝对不行！”乔梦容立即否认，“银子还是要给，我不求哥哥被官家重视，只求哥哥平平安安。”
"Absolutely not!" Qiao Mengrong shook her head at once. "We have to give silver. I don't ask for His Majesty's favor—only for your safety, Brother."

申简辰被乔梦容多情的眼神触动：“你想怎样就怎样吧，我饿了，有没有吃的？”
Touched by the devotion in her eyes, Shen Jianchen relented. "Do as you like. I'm hungry—is there anything to eat?"

邹嬷嬷正好来到厅堂，听到仪王说饿了，急忙说：“饭食已为王爷摆好，就等王爷享用。”
Matron Zou walked into the hall right then and caught his words. "The meal is already set for you, Your Highness—just waiting for you to sit down."

来到用膳厅，申简辰看着一桌子菜，有的认识，有的根本没见过，慨叹一个不受宠的皇子也有这么好的伙食。
In the dining hall, Shen Jianchen eyed the spread—some dishes familiar, others total mysteries—amazed that even an unfavored prince ate this well.

邹嬷嬷和乔梦容与仪王同桌吃饭，好久了，她们没有这么开心过。
Matron Zou and Qiao Mengrong sat down to dine with him, happier than they had been in years.

以前仪王只在病榻上用饭，吃的饭菜都是厨房单独做成，要蒸煮得又软又烂，才不会对他的身体产生不良影响。
In the past, Prince Yi ate only in bed, his meals cooked separate and stewed soft to mush so they wouldn't upset his weak frame.

而现在，他正活生生坐在她们面前，津津有味享用着美食，与正常人没有任何不同。
Now he sat right across from them, full of life, enjoying every bite like any healthy man.

“王爷喜欢吃就多吃些，你病的时间太长，人太瘦，要胖些才好看。”邹嬷嬷夹了菜放入仪王碗中。
"Eat up if you like it, Your Highness," Matron Zou said, placing food in his bowl. "You've been sick so long you're skin and bones—putting on some weight will do you good."

乔梦容只顾欣赏哥哥吃饭的模样，就像从来没见过一般，她的确从来没见过。
Qiao Mengrong just watched him eat as if seeing him for the first time—and in truth, she was.

6岁那年她被亲生父母遗弃在路边，正好仪王的马车从旁边经过，听到她的哭声，停下车后，见她可怜，便把她带回仪王府当亲妹妹对待。
When she was six, her birth parents had abandoned her by the roadside. Prince Yi's carriage happened past; hearing her sobs, he halted, pitied her, and brought her back to Prince Yi's Manor to raise as his own sister.

从第一次见到仪王，他就病着，从不与她一处吃饭，哪怕到后来他们互相喜欢上对方，都未曾像现在这样在同一张桌子上吃过饭。她曾多次梦想过这样的场景，没想到在今天得以实现。
From the first day she met him, he had been ill and never ate at the same table—not even after their feelings grew into love. She had dreamed of this scene countless times, never expecting to live it today.

申简辰被两个女人盯着，甚是不习惯：“你们难道不吃吗？”他给两人分别夹了菜放进碗里。
Uncomfortable under two pairs of staring eyes, Shen Jianchen asked, "Aren't you two eating?" He picked up food with his chopsticks and served each of them.

邹嬷嬷说：“我有生之年能看到王爷恢复正常，就是即刻死了也是开心的。”
Matron Zou beamed. "Seeing Your Highness healthy in my lifetime, I could die this very instant and go happy."

乔梦容笑着批评道：“邹嬷嬷休说不吉利的话，您会长命百岁，以后还要享哥哥的福。”
Qiao Mengrong chided her with a smile. "Don't talk such bad luck, Matron Zou! You'll live to a hundred and enjoy Brother's good fortune for years to come."

她们说完也高兴地吃了起来。
With that, both women picked up their chopsticks and ate in high spirits.

申简辰能看得出，眼前的二位对赵朴真是没得说，觉得自己有义务将实情告知。他向旁边的丫鬟吩咐：“你们都下去吧，不用在这里伺候。”
Seeing how wholehearted their devotion to Zhao Pu was, Shen Jianchen felt he owed them the truth. He waved to the maids standing by. "All of you step outside. No need to wait on us."

丫鬟们都一一往外走。
The maids filed out one by one.

乔梦容感到奇怪：“哥哥，你怎么能让丫鬟们走？她们得留这儿伺候哥哥。”
Puzzled, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Why send the maids away, Brother? They're here to serve you."

申简辰说：“我有事跟你们说，外人在此很不方便。”
"I have something to tell you two, and I don't want outsiders listening."

邹嬷嬷瞅着最后一个丫鬟出去把门带好后，说道：“人都走了，王爷放心说。”
Watching the last maid pull the door shut, Matron Zou said, "Everyone's gone, Your Highness. Speak freely."

申简辰放下筷子，显得郑重其事：“其实我不是赵朴，我叫申简辰，来自另一个时代，遇到地震，死了，就来到这里，我亲眼看到赵朴被一道金光和仙乐带走，然后我就进入到他的身体里……”
Shen Jianchen set his chopsticks down with grave focus. "Truth is, I'm not Zhao Pu. My name is Shen Jianchen, and I come from another era. I died in an earthquake and ended up here. I watched a beam of golden light and celestial music carry Zhao Pu away, and then I fell into his body..."

乔梦容笑道：“哥哥，没想到你这么会编故事。”
Qiao Mengrong laughed. "Brother, I never knew you could spin such tall tales!"

“我没有编故事，说的全是实情。”
"I'm not making it up. Every word is true."

乔梦容从仪王坚定的眼神中感受到他说的有可能是真的，她看向邹嬷嬷。
Sensing from his steady gaze that he might actually mean it, Qiao Mengrong glanced at Matron Zou.

邹嬷嬷显得很镇定：“不管你是谁，既然你在仪王府借助仪王的身体醒来，你只能是仪王，不可能是其他人，请王爷以后不要再对任何人提起此事。”
Matron Zou stayed calm as stone. "Whoever you were, you woke up in Prince Yi's Manor inside Prince Yi's body. You can only be Prince Yi—no one else. Please never speak of this to another soul, Your Highness."

乔梦容根本不愿承认仪王是别人，否则她的感情该找谁依托？哥哥若今日永远醒不来倒也罢了，可他偏偏醒来，还恢复成她多次在梦中见到的状态，她又岂能让一切的美好被他一句“我不是赵朴”打破？
Qiao Mengrong refused to accept he was anyone else—if he weren't, where would all her love go? Had her brother never woken up, so be it. Yet he had awakened, transformed into the healthy man of her dreams—how could she let one sentence, "I'm not Zhao Pu," shatter everything?

“不，你就是我哥哥！只是失忆了，很多事情想不起来而已。”
"No, you are my brother! You just lost your memory and can't recall the past."

申简辰很无奈，仔细想来，这个时代根本没有申简辰，他已成为赵朴，别人只会认为他是赵朴。
Helpless, Shen Jianchen had to admit the truth of it: Shen Jianchen didn't exist in this world. He was Zhao Pu now, and nobody would ever see him otherwise.

“算了，说这么多有什么用，也没人相信，我就是赵朴，以后认真做个不被官家重视的仪王。”
"Forget it—no use talking when nobody believes me. Fine, I'm Zhao Pu, and from now on I'll be a proper unfavored Prince Yi."

邹嬷嬷与乔梦容相视而笑，拿起筷子又高兴地吃起来。
Matron Zou and Qiao Mengrong exchanged a smile, picked up their chopsticks, and went back to eating in high spirits.
