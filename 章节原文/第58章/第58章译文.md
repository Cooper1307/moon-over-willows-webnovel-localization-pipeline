# Chapter 58: Progress on the Mutual Divorce

---

In the female guest section, Madam Xiang was the first to notice Zhu Xiyan leaving. She carefully observed her husband's direction—he was heading back to his room. She had planned to bring Nanny Wei to meet him after the banquet, but plans never go as expected.

---

The banquet was still going on, and her husband had left again. Madam Xiang needed to stay and entertain the guests, so she couldn't follow. She waved toward the group of personal servants gathered under the big tree.

---

Yinxing saw Madam Xiang waving and immediately came over: "What are your orders, Madam?"

---

Madam Xiang whispered: "Go tell Nanny Wei that the master has returned to his room. This is the perfect timing. Tell her to go now."

---

Yinxing replied: "Yes, Madam."

---

Lin Jue also noticed his father-in-law leaving. He got up and followed, wanting to take this opportunity to express his attitude about the mutual divorce to Zhu Xiyan.

---

Shen Jianchen noticed and said to Wenyuan: "The opportunity has come. I'll go find the prefect and put in a good word for you. Wait for my good news."

---

Qiao Wenxian was naturally delighted: "I'm extremely grateful!"

---

After Prince Yi left his seat, Qiao Wenxian really wanted to immediately tell Shuzhen the good news, but he held back. Once there was a result, she would naturally know.

---

Qianli Xun received Wenyuan's gaze. Her heart felt sweet and beautiful, just like the candied fruit before her. She picked one up and smiled.

---

Qiao Wenxian understood and also picked up a candied fruit.

---

Qianli Xun thought: "Wenyuan and I are truly two hearts beating as one. I hope our feelings will be as sweet as this candied fruit, and he's responding with sweetness too. Let me eat it and see what he does."

---

Qianli Xun put the candied fruit in her mouth. Qiao Wenxian did the same. They chewed the candied fruit, looked at each other, and smiled, instantly understanding each other's feelings.

---

For the first time, Qianli Xun felt the wonder of being so in sync with someone of the opposite sex. She found a reasonable explanation for why she didn't want to get involved in love in her previous era: it must be because her lover was waiting for her in this era. She traveled through time precisely to meet and be with the one she loved a thousand years ago.

---

Miao Zhiyu saw this scene and became even more certain that the relationship between the two had reached the "second type" that Youqi Jushi had mentioned. She felt both envious and sad that her own love received no response.

---

Miao Zhiyu's mother, Madam Qin, saw her daughter was unhappy and asked: "What's wrong?"

---

"Nothing." After answering, Miao Zhiyu turned to ask Madam Qin: "Mother, how is the progress of the Qiao family's bride selection for Young Master Qiao?"

---

Madam Qin said: "According to the matchmaker, the Qiao family is being very careful. After all, Young Master Qiao is their only heir, so they can't produce results so quickly. I also heard from the matchmaker that your qualifications are among the best of all the young ladies."

---

Miao Zhiyu smiled bitterly: "I think the Qiao family should stop searching. Young Master Qiao already has someone he likes."

---

Madam Qin said: "What do you young people know? It doesn't matter what Young Master Qiao likes. What matters is that the elders of the Qiao family approve."

---

Miao Zhiyu yearned for free love, hoping to find someone who mutually admired her. She liked Young Master Qiao, but Young Master Qiao had someone else in his heart. At this moment, she suddenly felt that there was nothing wrong with parents' orders and matchmakers' words.

---

Her brother was leaving again. Qiao Mengrong got up and followed, walking behind her brother for a while until she saw he had caught up with Lin Jue.

---

Qiao Mengrong called out: "Brother!"

---

Shen Jianchen stopped, turned around, and walked to Qiao Mengrong: "Why did you follow me?"

---

"What are you going to do, Brother?"

---

There was no harm in telling Mengrong, so Shen Jianchen told the truth: "Wenyuan likes Youqi Jushi, but Youqi Jushi hasn't divorced yet. Look, that's her husband..."

---

Qiao Mengrong looked toward Lin Jue.

---

Lin Jue knew very well that a young lady who could call Prince Yi "brother" must have an extraordinary status. He had heard that there was a Princess Jiahe by Prince Yi's side, who took good care of him, and the two were already engaged. This young lady must be Princess Jiahe. Lin Jue didn't dare to be negligent. He opened his mouth, showed a smile, and returned the greeting.

---

"Under my persuasion, he has already agreed to the mutual divorce. Now I'm having him take me to meet the prefect. Once we convince the prefect, the mutual divorce will definitely happen, and Wenyuan can be with Youqi Jushi."

---

So it was for this matter. Qiao Mengrong finally felt relieved: "With you on the case, Brother, it will surely succeed."

---

"Of course. Go back and sit comfortably. Wait for me to return."

---

After giving his instructions, Shen Jianchen took a few quick steps to reach Lin Jue's side and went with him to find Zhu Xiyan.

---

Watching her brother's back, Qiao Mengrong breathed a sigh of relief, turned around, and returned to the female guest section to continue enjoying the performance.

---

Zhu Xiyan returned to his room and sat on the daybed. He opened the letter from the Old Ancestor and read it again. It contained two sentences: "I agree to the mutual divorce between the Zhu family's young lady and her husband. Please have the head of the Zhu family expedite the matter."

---

Zhu Xiyan put the letter on the table, his expression even more serious.

---

The Old Ancestor had spoken. As a junior, he must treat it with caution. According to the Zhu family's rules, when an elder of the Old Ancestor's level spoke, Zhu Xiyan could only obey. But his daughter's marriage was something he had carefully considered and chosen. How could he easily agree to a mutual divorce?

---

The Lin family was also a prominent household in Lin'an, and Lin Jue held a position of some sort. His only shortcoming was that he didn't understand poetry, but in Zhu Xiyan's view, this shortcoming was actually an advantage. His daughter had been clever and bright since childhood, well-read, especially fond of poetry. Influenced by poetry, she lived in unrealistic fantasies all day, liking the kind of person who only knew how to daydream, like Young Master Xu. Zhu Xiyan believed his daughter should find someone practical to live life with, and Lin Jue was exactly that kind of person.

---

At first, because the Zhu and Xu families were on good terms, Zhu Xiyan didn't think there was anything wrong with Young Master Xu, thinking that when he got older, he would naturally improve. It wasn't until the Xu family moved away and Young Master Xu married someone else that Zhu Xiyan saw the Xu family's intentions and also realized that Young Master Xu was not just a daydreamer but also a man without opinions. Not marrying into the Xu family was a blessing in disguise. From that time on, Zhu Xiyan put the matter of marrying off his daughter on the agenda.

---

The first few families they approached, his daughter was unwilling, just because she had Young Master Xu in her heart. Zhu Xiyan didn't force her. They delayed and delayed, and she got older and older. If they continued to delay, he was afraid that no matter how good the Zhu family's background was, no one would want her.

---

Coincidentally, the Lin family came to propose marriage. Zhu Xiyan felt that Lin Jue suited him very well, so he told his daughter about it. She still didn't agree. Later, after continuous persuasion, she finally nodded. He never expected their married life would reach this point.

---

Last time his daughter came back, she smashed the dining table with one punch. Zhu Xiyan realized that his daughter now was not the same as before.

---

He knew best what Shuzhen had done since childhood. She had never practiced any martial arts. How could she have such great strength! Something must have happened at the Lin family that completely changed her, but this was not something to mention to outsiders.

---

Although Zhu Xiyan appeared indifferent on the surface, he had already accepted this different daughter in his heart.

---

He thought that the reason his previous daughter couldn't get along with her husband was because their temperaments didn't match. Now that she had changed, had some martial arts skills like her husband, and spoke roughly when angry, the two should be able to find common topics.

---

He didn't expect that this different daughter was so capable—she could not only get the Old Ancestor to intervene but also make the Old Ancestor stick to her opinion.

---

As Zhu Xiyan was thinking, a servant came in: "Master, Nanny Wei requests an audience."

---

After receiving Madam Xiang's instructions, Yinxing ran all the way back to Madam Xiang's room.

---

Nanny Wei had long finished enjoying the delicacies and was resting on the couch. Yinxing woke her up, told her that the master had returned to his room, and said this was the best time to find him. Nanny Wei quickly got down from the couch and hurried to the master's courtyard.

---

Zhu Xiyan guessed that Nanny Wei must be here for the matter of mutual divorce. This nanny's greatest virtue was her loyalty to Shuzhen, thinking of everything for her and busy herself with her affairs. It was also because of this character that Zhu Xiyan felt at ease entrusting Shuzhen to her care.

---

"Please let her in."

---

After receiving permission, Nanny Wei entered the master's room: "Greetings, Master."

---

"Sit."

---

Nanny Wei chose a chair and sat down.

---

"Did Nanny Wei return with Shuzhen?"

---

"Yes. Besides visiting the master and madam on this trip, I also have something to discuss with the master."

---

"Speak."

---

Originally, Nanny Wei had thought of many ways to first make the master happy, but when she got here, she couldn't say a single word to please him. She just got straight to the point: "The master entrusted the young lady to me out of trust, but I didn't take good care of her, letting her often suffer harsh beatings from the young master..."

---

"Brutal beatings? That's an exaggeration. It's normal for couples to argue. The young master has told me about this. Besides, Shuzhen refused to consummate the marriage with the young master, which led to what happened later. It's all her own doing."

---

"How can the master downplay it so casually? You're only indulging the young master this way..."

---

"Indulging? That's not it. Now the young master might not even be able to beat Shuzhen. You can rest assured."

---

"It seems the master already knows about this. After I went out to collect rent, the young lady's temperament completely changed, and she even knows martial arts. The young master indeed can't beat her. I'm happy for the young lady—she won't be bullied in the future."

---

"So don't blame yourself for not taking good care of her, and don't worry about her anymore."

---

Nanny Wei didn't want to talk about other things and quickly got to the main topic: "Although the young master no longer dares to bully the young lady, they still can't live together. The young lady is set on a mutual divorce and hopes the master can intervene to make it happen."

---

"Nanny Wei only wants to follow Shuzhen's wishes. Have you thought about where Shuzhen will go after the divorce? What will she do with her future life?"

---

"I've already thought it through. After the divorce, if the Zhu family is willing to accept the young lady, we'll move into the Zhu residence. If not, I've already bought a courtyard house outside for the young lady. Moving there would be fine too."

---

"Living arrangements are easy to solve. What about her future marriage?"

---

"After all, the young lady is the master's daughter, talented and beautiful. Finding a good family shouldn't be a problem. Even if there's no one to marry, at worst I'll accompany the young lady for life. When I'm old, there's still Kuqiao. It's better than staying in the same courtyard with someone you can't stand."

---

Nanny Wei had already planned for the future. Zhu Xiyan felt both admiration and comfort, but he still couldn't easily agree to the divorce: "A daughter of the Zhu family must prioritize the Zhu family's reputation. Although a mutual divorce sounds better than being cast out by the husband's family, it still damages reputation. It will not only damage Shuzhen's reputation but also affect the Zhu family."

---

"The master is well-read and bears the heavy responsibility of Lin'an. By rights, your insight should be above mine, a mere woman. Yet why do I know that fame and profit are external things, while the master doesn't?"

---

Just as Zhu Xiyan was about to speak, a servant came in again: "Master, the young master requests an audience."

---

Nanny Wei said: "The young master has come at the perfect time. Why not settle the matter of mutual divorce with him face to face?"

---

Zhu Xiyan also thought Lin Jue had come at the perfect time. He had always been unwilling to divorce, and it would be good to have him help persuade Nanny Wei: "Let him in."

---

Lin Jue and Shen Jianchen had already arrived outside the door. The servant said the master was talking to someone inside. Shen Jianchen decided to wait in the courtyard for a while, and Lin Jue could only agree.

---

Lin Jue didn't dare to casually strike up a conversation with Prince Yi and stood respectfully. Shen Jianchen had nothing to say to Lin Jue and also remained silent.

---

The two waited for a while. When Lin Jue thought it was about time, he asked Prince Yi for permission before entering the room to greet his father-in-law.

---

After entering, Lin Jue first bowed to his father-in-law and Nanny Wei. After receiving permission, he sat in the chair opposite Nanny Wei.

---

Zhu Xiyan asked: "I wonder what brings my worthy son-in-law here?"

---

Before his bottom even warmed the seat, Lin Jue quickly got up again and said respectfully: "Father-in-law, I've come to discuss the matter of mutual divorce with my wife."

---

Zhu Xiyan said: "Don't be nervous. Sit down and take your time."

---

Lin Jue sat down again and boldly said: "I've thought it through recently. Instead of living so unhappily, Shuzhen and I might as well set each other free. That way, everyone will be happy, and I can remarry a wife who suits me."

---

Nanny Wei smiled: "It's truly rare for the young master to think this way."

---

Lin Jue changed his mind so quickly, which surprised Zhu Xiyan: "The young master's thoughts have changed so quickly. May I ask the reason?"

---

Lin Jue thought to himself that there was still an important figure waiting outside. He couldn't let him stand there forever—he needed to hurry and bring him in. But he also needed to first speak ill of Zhu Shuzhen to vent a bit.

---

"Father-in-law, there's something you don't know. I originally wanted to live well with my wife, no matter how she treated me, I wouldn't mind. Even if she refused to consummate the marriage, I wouldn't mind. At worst, I'd take a concubine. But my wife said that if I didn't agree to the divorce, she would wear me down. If I brought in a woman, she would chase her away. She wouldn't give me a chance to take a concubine. Considering the Lin family's ancestral line, I had no choice but to agree to the divorce."

---

Zhu Xiyan said angrily: "That's outrageous!"

---

Nanny Wei countered: "Does the young master usually badmouth our young lady like this in front of the master?"

---

Lin Jue didn't respond to Nanny Wei's words.

---

Nanny Wei continued: "The master doesn't need to be angry. You only need to believe half of what the young master says."

---

Lin Jue was thinking about Prince Yi and quickly said: "Father-in-law, during the banquet, I did think about telling you about the divorce, but I wasn't very firm. Someone came to persuade me, and I had to agree."

---

Lin Jue walked up to Zhu Xiyan and whispered in his ear: "Prince Yi is outside waiting to see you."

---

Zhu Xiyan was so startled that he immediately stood up: "Nanny Wei, leave through the back gate. An honored guest has come. We'll discuss the divorce later."

---

Nanny Wei was also someone who had seen the world. She guessed that an extraordinary person from the government had come and quickly got up and left through the back gate.

---

Zhu Xiyan adjusted his clothes and took Lin Jue to the courtyard.
