第266章 林觉怒斥白梅若
## Chapter 266: Lin Jue Lambastes Bai Meiruo

千离循夫妇离开后，申简辰无心回幽林别居，他一直坐在堂上等待白风和温信押人回来，他定要审问清楚，是何人想要诋毁幽栖居士，诋毁千里寻。
仪王面色沉重，一言不发，韩谷不敢打扰，坐在师爷的位置默默陪伴。又觉太过无趣，拿本书卷随便翻翻，消磨时光。
天色刚刚暗下，白风与温信一起回来，身后的差役押着两个人。一个年纪大些，穿戴整齐；一个是疤子。
差役将他们押到堂内，厉声说：“跪下！”
温信先汇报情况：“王爷，在林统制的协助下，我等已将正在分发的和书局里存放的书卷全部收回，都堆放在院外。这两人一个是书局的管事，一个是分发书卷的小混混，将他二人押回来，请王爷发落。”
申简辰命令道：“白风，你负责将院子里堆放的书卷一把火全部烧毁。”
“是！”
白风来到院外，那里堆放着有半人高的书卷。他掏出火折子，吹出火苗，弯下腰，将书卷点燃。只见火苗碰到纸张，越烧越旺。
堂内，申简辰将惊堂木狠狠拍了一下，跪的两人吓得直呼：“大人饶命！”
申简辰厉声问：“老实交代，是谁指使你们干这等毁人声誉的勾当？”
疤子第一个说话：“大人，不能怪我，我也是收银子办事，书卷上写的是什么，我大字不识，根本不知道。”
自从陈浩宇变得正经起来，疤子等人没得混，又需要银子花，正好碰上这种省力又能挣些银子的活儿，他们干得十分起劲儿。没想到正干着，官府来人，其他人腿快，迅速溜掉，他稍微慢了些，被抓住。此时想来，甚觉委屈。
“你收了谁的银子？”
“应该是苗夫人的银子。”
“苗夫人？”
申简辰甚感诧异，她与幽栖居士有诗会之谊，又十分欣赏她的诗词，怎么会做出此等事？又一想梦容后来与她走得近，难道此事与梦容有关？
疤子解释道：“就是乔家的少夫人，她身边的丫鬟找到我们，那丫鬟我见过，所以认得。”
After Qian Lixun and his wife left, Shen Jianchen stayed glued to his courtroom chair, too wound up to head home until Bai Feng and Wen Xin returned with suspects. He meant to drag out whoever was smearing Youqi Jushi—smearing Qianli Xun.

另一位跪在地上的显然是书局的管事，接着疤子说：“也不关书局的事，是苗夫人和白夫人找到书局，委托我们将书稿刊印出来，她们给的银子不少，看在银子的份上，我就接了。”
With the prince sitting in grim silence, Han Gu kept quiet at his clerk's desk, flipping through an old register to pass the hours.

两位夫人出面，一定与梦容有关。申简辰充满自责，都怪他一味执着自己的感情，没有顾及梦容的情绪，才致使她想出此等报复千里寻的招数。
Just after dusk, Bai Feng and Wen Xin returned with constables herding two prisoners inside: a neatly dressed older man and Scarface.

既与梦容有关，申简辰不想将事情闹大：“记住！不准再有下次！若再有，我定封了书局，把你们统统关起来！”
"Kneel!" a constable barked.

书局管事和疤子都吓得跪地求饶：“大人恕罪！”
Wen Xin stepped forward to report. "With Commander Lin's help, Your Highness, we confiscated every copy being handed out plus the entire stockpile at the printing house—all stacked in the yard outside. These two are the printing house manager and one of the street thugs passing them out."

“滚！”
"Bai Feng," Shen Jianchen ordered, "put a torch to the whole stack outside."

听到让他们滚，两人感谢完，爬起来迅速离开。经过院子时，看到正在燃烧的书卷，一句话不敢说，跑出司理院。
"Yes, Your Highness!" Stepping out to a waist-high mountain of pamphlets, Bai Feng struck his flint, blew a bright spark into flame, and lit the dry paper until roaring flames shot skyward.

申简辰从堂内走出，盯着熊熊烈火：“白风，随我回幽林别居。”
Inside, Shen Jianchen slammed his clapper down—*CRACK!*—making both kneeling men shriek, "Mercy, Your Honor!"

话说林觉带领士兵帮着把书卷回收完毕后，气冲冲地回到林府，径直走进竹兰居。
"Confess!" Shen Jianchen thundered. "Who hired you to run this smear campaign?"

自从和离后，他搬进竹兰居，将里面按照自己的喜好重新布置一番，只是竹兰居的名字并未更换，以此纪念第一段婚姻。
Scarface blurted first, "Don't blame me, Your Honor! I just took silver to pass 'em out—can't read a single word on the page!" Ever since Haoyu went straight, Scarface's crew had been hurting for cash; easy silver for handing out free flyers had looked like heaven until soldiers swarmed the block and his buddies outran him.

林觉顾不上坐，命令下人去唤白梅若：“去！把夫人叫来！”
"Whose silver did you take?"

不多会儿，白梅若前来，以为夫君今晚想让她作陪，满脸笑容走进竹兰居。
"Madam Miao's, I reckon."

进入屋内，见夫君背对着她，上前从身后抱住：“夫君，这么晚才回来，吃过饭吗？累不累？是不是想让我今晚陪你？”
"Madam Miao?" Shen Jianchen frowned—she had attended poetry salons with Youqi Jushi and praised her verse; why turn venomous now? Then he remembered Mengrong growing close to her. Was Mengrong behind this?

“松开！”林觉声音中强压着怒气。
"Qiao Manor's Young Madam," Scarface clarified. "Her personal maid came to hire us—I know her face."

白梅若并不理会，继续撒娇：“嗯……！我不嘛！就要这么抱着你。”她将脸在林觉的背上贴得更紧。
The printing house manager jumped right in. "Not our fault either, Your Honor! Madam Miao and Madam Bai came in together to commission the print run. Paid top silver, so I took the job!"

“再不松开！我可要动手！”
Both wives working together—Mengrong's fingerprints were all over this. Guilt hit Shen Jianchen square in the gut. His own stubborn clinging had pushed Mengrong into pulling ugly stunts like this.

夫君的声音充满怒气，白梅若才意识到林觉真的生气。她松开手，担心地说：“夫君，你可不能听信那贱人胡言乱语，她嫉妒我是主母，才会在你面前编排我。”
Unwilling to blow Mengrong's involvement wide open, he barked, "Hear me well: no next time! Pull anything like this again, and I shut your print shop down and throw you all in chains!"

林觉转过身，将书卷扔在白梅若面前：“瞧瞧你干的好事！你是我的夫人，不知道帮我，净干些影响我前程之事！我现在真的开始考虑要不要休了你！”
Both kowtowed wildly. "Forgive us, Your Honor!"

白梅若被吓坏，看向地上的书卷，封皮上的八个大字映入眼帘，立刻什么都明白，跪在地上哭：“呜呜……！夫君误会我，我与仪王妃走得近，就是为了攀上贵人，对夫君您的前程有利，可仪王妃恨幽栖居士，想着法儿要折磨她，让我和苗知瑜帮忙，她是贵人，我们不敢不听，只能帮她。呜呜……！”
"Get out!"

林觉继续训斥道：“你帮仪王妃，却帮我得罪仪王！他今日知道此事，不仅派司理院的人，还让白侍卫给我传来口谕，吩咐我带人满街收回书卷，我捉拿书局的管事时从他口中得知你与此事有关，你这不是害我吗？用你的脑袋好好想想，他一个王爷缺女人吗？朱淑真嫁到陈家那日，他跑去抢亲，说明什么？”
Scrambling up with stammered thanks, they bolted past the crackling bonfire in the yard and fled out the gate. Stepping outside, Shen Jianchen stared into the leaping flames. "Bai Feng, come with me back to Youlin Retreat."

白梅若继续哭着：“呜呜……！说明他不是什么好男人！呜呜……！”
Across town, having finished rounding up pamphlets, Lin Jue stormed straight back to Lin Manor's Zhulan Residence—where he had moved after his divorce, refurnishing it to his taste yet keeping its old name in memory of his first marriage. Not even sitting down, he barked at a servant, "Go summon Madam!"

林觉一记耳光打在白梅若的脸上，大声说道：“说明他喜欢她喜欢到骨子里！你竟然敢诋毁仪王喜欢到骨子里的人，这不是找死吗？虽说仪王妃身份尊贵，可我升迁靠的不是她，而是仪王！仪王！你听清楚没？”
Moments later Bai Meiruo drifted in beaming, assuming her husband wanted company tonight. Seeing his back turned, she wrapped her arms around his waist from behind. "Back so late, Husband—have you eaten? Tired? Want me to keep you company tonight?"

白梅若捂着被打疼的脸，边哭边说：“呜呜，夫君以前亲口说让我和仪王妃搞好关系，我照做了，你却来怪我，呜呜，我到底怎么做你才能满意？呜呜，你自己心里放不下她，才会对我大发雷霆，别以为我不知道！现在却以仪王为借口，呜呜，仪王要怪就去怪他的王妃，跟我有什么关系？呜呜……！”
"Let go," Lin Jue said through clenched teeth.

林觉气得大骂道：“你这不知好歹的妇人！滚！滚得越远越好！”
Ignoring the warning, she pressed her cheek tighter against his back with a coy purr. "Mmm, no! I just want to hold you..."

丫鬟将白梅若扶起来，她哭着回到自己的屋子，继续趴在桌上大哭起来：“呜呜……！”
"Let go before I lose my temper!"

院门口，春金巧手拿团扇，边扇边说：“咱们这位夫人跟以前的朱家姑娘比起来差远了，难怪主君不喜欢她。”
Sensing real fury, she stepped back in alarm. "Husband, don't listen to that tramp badmouthing me! She's just jealous I'm primary wife!"

丫鬟说：“主君喜欢春姨娘。”
Lin Jue spun around and hurled a pamphlet straight at her feet. "Look at the mess you made! Instead of helping your husband, you're torpedoing my career! I'm seriously starting to wonder if I should write you a divorce letter!"

“你说这话我爱听，走，随我进去安慰安慰夫人。”
Spotting the eight characters on the cover, Bai Meiruo turned white and dropped to her knees sobbing. "You're misjudging me, Husband! I only got close to Princess Consort Yi to build royal ties for your career! She hates Youqi Jushi and wanted to torture her, so she ordered Miao Zhiyu and me to print those—she's royalty, how could we refuse?! *Sob*..."

“您就不怕夫人认为您是去看她的笑话？”
"You kiss up to Princess Consort Yi and land me on Prince Yi's hit list!" Lin Jue roared. "He found out today, sent the Judicial Office *and* had Guard Bai deliver a personal order making me sweep the streets! The print manager spilled your name right in front of me! Are you trying to ruin me?! Use that skull of yours—does an imperial prince lack women? He crashed Chen Haoyu's wedding to snatch Zhu Shuzhen! What does that tell you?!"

“随她怎么认为，我内心坦荡，再说都生活在林府西院，抬头不见低头见的，需与她搞好关系。”
"Tells me he's a rotten man! *Sob*..."

春金巧走进院子，来到门外向里说：“姐姐，妹妹来看你。”
*SMACK!* Lin Jue's palm cracked across her cheek. "It tells you he loves her to the marrow of his bones! And you dared slander the woman Prince Yi loves to his bones—have you got a death wish?! Noble as Princess Consort Yi is, my promotion rides on Prince Yi, not her! *Prince Yi!* Got that through your head?!"

白梅若的哭声立刻停止，声音从里传出：“你来做什么？莫不是来看我的笑话？”
Clutching her stinging cheek, Bai Meiruo wailed back, "You're the one who told me to butter up Princess Consort Yi—now I do it and you beat me! What do I have to do to please you?! You're still hung up on Zhu Shuzhen yourself—don't think I don't know! Using Prince Yi as an excuse to blow up at me! If Prince Yi's mad, let him blame his own wife—why take it out on me?!"

“岂敢？我是帮姐姐出主意，省得姐姐往后再惹主君生气。”
"Unreasonable shrew! Get out! Out of my sight!"

春金巧话音刚落，丫鬟将门从里面打开：“春姨娘，夫人有请。”
A maid hurried in to haul Bai Meiruo back to her own room, where she collapsed weeping across her table.

春金巧走进去，白梅若已在桌旁坐得端正，只是双眼哭得红肿。她一脸怀疑：“你有这么好心？会帮我出主意？”
Outside the courtyard gate, Chun Jinqiao waved a round silk fan with a cool smirk. "Our Madam doesn't hold a candle to Miss Zhu—no wonder the Master can't stand her."

“姐姐帮我赎身，我感激不尽，自然心甘情愿帮你出主意，希望姐姐以后不要把我当外人。”
"The Master favors Concubine Chun," her maid flattered.

“别一口一个姐姐，我可比你小两岁。”
"Music to my ears. Come on, let's go console Madam."

“姐姐身份尊贵，也比我清白，尊称您为姐姐我不亏。”
"Aren't you afraid she'll think you came to gloat?"

对方态度甚好，白梅若不好计较：“随便坐。”
"Let her think what she likes. We share the same West Courtyard—keeping peace with the primary wife pays off."

春金巧这才在椅子上坐好：“姐姐以后记着，朱家姑娘已是过去，现在你我是主君身边的人，他心中再有她，已不可能。莫说人家已成亲，就是没成亲，仪王惦记着，也轮不到主君，您又何必因为她惹主君不高兴？这不是自己给自己添堵？”
Stepping up to the doorway, Chun Jinqiao called softly, "Elder Sister, your little sister came to visit."

“你说得不无道理，可你没在我的位置，体会不到我的苦，他常常拿我跟她比，你说气人不气人？”
The sobbing snapped off. "What do you want? Come to laugh at me?"

“离过婚的人都喜欢拿现任夫人与以前的比，人之常情，毕竟以前生活过一段时日，姐姐要理解。主君说这些话时，您听听得了，别往心里去，该怎样还怎样，我想他也只是说说。”
"Never! I came with a plan so you won't upset the Master again."

“你这么一说，我心里亮堂许多。可今日，他不分青红皂白，将我骂一顿，还扇我，我的脸到现在还疼。”
A maid pulled the door open. "Madam invites you in, Concubine Chun."

“这算什么，主君以前打朱家姑娘可比这厉害，拳打脚踢的。”
Inside, Bai Meiruo sat stiffly with swollen red eyes. "Since when are you kind enough to offer me advice?"

白梅若脸上露出笑容：“如此一比较，他对我还算不错。”
"You redeemed my contract, Elder Sister—I owe you a debt I'll never forget. Stop treating me like an outsider."

白梅若觉得今日可以与春金巧谈谈更深入的话题，吩咐丫鬟将门关好，又充满忧虑地说：“你不知道，仪王妃要整幽栖居士，让我和苗知瑜帮忙，刚整了一出，看样子要泡汤，还惹得夫君不高兴，你说以后还要不要帮仪王妃？”
"Quit calling me Elder Sister—I'm two years younger than you."

“既然主君不高兴，我劝姐姐还是不要再帮。”
"You're nobler and cleaner than me—calling you Elder Sister is only proper."

“我好不容易跟仪王妃拉上关系，不帮她又担心她不理我。”
Mollified by her deference, Bai Meiruo waved at a chair. "Sit."

“姐姐不如与苗夫人一起将实情告知仪王妃，她即使怪也怪不到你们头上，此事也就不了了之。”
"Listen to me, Elder Sister," Chun Jinqiao began smoothly. "Miss Zhu is ancient history. Even if she weren't married, with Prince Yi pining for her, our Master wouldn't stand a chance. Why drive yourself crazy and anger the Master over a ghost?"

“只怕往后她还会麻烦我。”
"Easy for you to say—you don't have to hear him compare me to her every other day!"

“若以后仪王妃有其他要求，您只管表面答应，应付应付，回来及时告诉主君，让他转达仪王，既给主君提供和仪王交好的机会，也会让仪王对主君更加信任，主君开心，姐姐也有好日子过。”
"Divorced men always compare their new wife to the old one—human nature. Let it go in one ear and out the other; he doesn't actually mean half of it."

白梅若起身，抓住春金巧的手：“你的主意甚好，真没想到你如此为我考虑。”
"Hearing you put it like that does ease my chest. Still, he screamed at me and slapped me without listening—my face still throbs!"

“此事姐姐不能与苗夫人说，她想如何那是她的事，姐姐还需记着，往后不要把家里的事往外说，让人笑话。前日妾室们聚会时，大家都拿姐姐说过的话向我求证，搞得我甚是为难。”
"That's mild! Back when he was married to Miss Zhu, he kicked and beat her way worse."

白梅若想起幽栖居士骂她的话，此时才意识到自己的问题：“好，我听你的，以后会多加注意。”
A relieved smile cracked Bai Meiruo's gloom. "Put like that, he treats me fairly well!" Warming up, she had her maid shut the door and confided her worry. "Princess Consort Yi enlisted Miao Zhiyu and me to mess with Youqi Jushi. Now this first stunt blew up and angered my husband—should I keep helping Her Highness?"

外面传来下人的声音：“春姨娘可在夫人屋里？”
"If it angers the Master, definitely stop."

白梅若身边的丫鬟回道：“在，有何事？”
"I worked so hard to get in with her—if I quit, she'll freeze me out!"

下人回道：“主君正在春姨娘屋里候着。”
"Go with Madam Miao and lay the whole truth before the Princess Consort. She can't blame you two for Prince Yi intervening, so this round drops clean. And if Her Highness asks for anything else later, smile and play along to her face—then come right home and tip off our Master so he can pass it to Prince Yi! Earns our Master huge trust with the prince, keeps the Master smiling, and keeps *you* sitting pretty!"

白梅若心中不爽，放开春金巧的手，因她刚刚说的那一番话，不好立刻给她脸色看，淡淡地说：“我今日跟夫君吵架，倒便宜你，还愣着干什么，他差人唤你，还不赶紧去陪？”
Bai Meiruo jumped up and clasped Chun Jinqiao's hands. "Brilliant! Never knew you looked out for me like this!"

春金巧行礼道：“我听姐姐的，去陪主君。”
"Keep this strictly from Madam Miao—let her fend for herself. Oh, and stop airing Lin family dirty laundry outdoors, Elder Sister. At the concubines' tea two days ago, everyone grilled me over gossip you spilled—put me in a tight spot."

说完，扭着腰肢离开。
Remembering Qianli Xun tearing into her for the exact same flaw, Bai Meiruo flushed. "Point taken. I'll zip my lip from now on."

白梅若一直盯着春金巧扭动的腰肢，心中慨叹，得软到什么程度才能将腰扭成她那样。等春金巧完全走出后，骂道：“青楼出身，浑身透着骚劲儿，你看那腰扭的，哪像个正经人家的妾室？真后悔为她赎身，放在家里给自己找气受！”
Just then a servant called outside, "Is Concubine Chun in Madam's room? Master is waiting for her in her quarters."

丫鬟说：“夫人把她赎回来是对的，要不主君老往春香楼跑，您也受不了。把她放在家里既免了主君老往外跑，您也可拿出主母的威严约束她。您瞧，她刚刚不还向您示好，给您出主意来者？”
Irritation flashed in Bai Meiruo's eyes, though Chun Jinqiao's timely advice kept her civil. Dropping her hands, she said coolly, "Looks like my fight worked out great for you. Go on—he's calling."

白梅若认可道：“说的也是！”
With a demure curtsy, Chun Jinqiao swayed gracefully out the door. Watching those sinuous hips vanish, Bai Meiruo spat bitterly, "Brothel trash through and through—look at that shameless sway! I regret ever buying her out just to watch her get under my skin!"

她懒得再去计较自己夫君的床上今晚躺着的人不是自己，还是想想明日见到仪王妃怎么向她说明今日之事。
"You made the right call, Madam," her maid soothed. "Stops the Master running to Chunxiang Brothel, keeps her under your thumb—and didn't she just hand you a winning plan?"

不如先告诉苗知瑜，想办法借她之口说给仪王妃，贵人想要责怪，苗知瑜也可分去一半的责任。
"True enough." Brushing off where her husband slept tonight, Bai Meiruo turned her thoughts to tomorrow—she'd rope Miao Zhiyu into breaking the bad news to Princess Consort Yi together so they could split any royal wrath down the middle.
