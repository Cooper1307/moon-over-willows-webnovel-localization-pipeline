## Chapter 142: Beaten Black and Blue After Visiting Wangyue Pavilion

陈浩宇悻悻地离开，回到自己屋中，继续思忖着如何拒绝这门亲事。怎么都没想到，整来整去，他竟然成朱淑真的接盘侠，真是越想越气。
Fuming back to his own quarters, Chen Haoyu racked his brains over how to kill this betrothal. After all his scheming, winding up as Zhu Shuzhen's fallback husband made his blood boil!

青瓜很快拿来衣裳，帮公子将打烂的衣裳脱下，在他背上仔细查看，没有发现任何损伤。
Bringing fresh clothes, Qinggua helped him shed his slashed robe and checked his back carefully—not a single welt!

“老爷还是心疼公子，手下留情，没伤到皮肉，只将衣裳打烂而已。”
"Master still loves you, Young Master—pulled his strikes so only the robe tore!"

“你想冻死本公子？还不赶紧给我把衣裳穿好！这么多废话！”
"Trying to freeze me to death?! Put my clothes on and shut up!"

青瓜边给公子穿衣裳边说：“总盼公子娶个少奶奶回来，好有人看着您，没想到公子要娶朱家姑娘，她在外名声可不怎么好，还离过婚，也不知老爷和夫人是怎么想的。”
Dressing him, Qinggua prattled on: "Always hoped Young Master would wed a Young Madam to keep you in line—never guessed it'd be Miss Zhu, with her bad name and a divorce behind her! Wonder what Master and Madam were thinking."

陈浩宇第一次夸青瓜：“你这话说到我心里，连你都觉得不妥，我爹娘却被知府大人蛊惑，让我娶朱家没人要的姑娘，你说我得有多惨。”
For the very first time, Chen Haoyu praised Qinggua: "Hit the nail on the head! Even you see it's crazy, yet my parents got hoodwinked by the Prefect into dumping his unwanted daughter on me—how miserable is my luck?!"

青瓜越想越不对，担忧道：“公子，您被知府大人绑着回来，该不会在外面非礼朱家姑娘，被知府大人抓住，硬要把女儿嫁给您？老爷和夫人不得已才应下此事？”
Thinking harder, alarm lit Qinggua's face: "Wait—the Prefect hauling you home in ropes... don't tell me you forced yourself on Miss Zhu outside, got caught red-handed, and got strong-armed into marrying her?! That why Master and Madam caved?!"

衣裳已穿好，陈浩宇转过身，在青瓜的身上踢了一脚：“胡说什么！你家公子我是喜欢漂亮姑娘，可也不至于轻薄一只母老虎！”
Fully dressed, Chen Haoyu spun and aimed a kick at Qinggua's shin: "Shut your trap! Sure I love pretty girls, yet molesting a tigress?! Not in a million years!"

青瓜向一旁躲开，还是被公子踢到，不是很疼，继续多嘴道：“公子就该娶个厉害的少夫人回来好好管管您，要不您老欺负我。”
Dodging half clear so the kick barely grazed him, Qinggua kept running his mouth: "You really do need a tough Young Madam to rein you in, Young Master—otherwise all you do is bully me!"

陈浩宇又去踢青瓜：“我欺负你怎么了？本公子欺负你是瞧得起你，别人想让我欺负，我还不一定瞧得上。”
Chen Haoyu swung another kick: "So what if I bully you? Bullying you means I like you—plenty of folks beg to get bullied by me and don't make the cut!"

青瓜继续向一旁躲去，陈浩宇没踢着，也懒得再踢。
Qinggua hopped clear again, leaving Chen Haoyu too lazy to chase.

青瓜觍着笑脸说：“公子还是不要瞧得上我。”
Flashing a cheeky grin, Qinggua said, "Better skip liking me then, Young Master."

陈浩宇果断说道：“好，以后不许跟着我。”
Chen Haoyu snapped cool: "Fine—stop following me from now on."

“那怎么行？公子还是踢我吧。”青瓜撅起屁股。
"No way! Go ahead and kick me, Young Master!" Qinggua stuck his rear out.

陈浩宇笑道：“你怎么这么贱！本公子才懒得踢你。走，跟本公子出去走一趟。”
Chen Haoyu cracked a laugh: "What a shameless little bootlicker—not even worth my foot! Come on, you're coming out with me."

青瓜喜出望外：“公子，我没听错吧？您要带我出去？”
Qinggua's eyes lit up like lanterns: "Did I hear right, Young Master?! Taking me outside?!"

“怎么？不想出去？那我走了。”陈浩宇说着往出走。
"Don't want to go? Fine, I'm off." Chen Haoyu started for the door.

青瓜赶紧跟上，嬉皮笑脸：“能跟公子出去，是我的荣幸，我还听说，跟公子混，吃香的喝辣的。”
Scrambling after with a huge grin: "My honor to tag along, Young Master! Heard rolling with you means non-stop gourmet feasts and high living!"

“知道就行，以后只要你一心向着我，保准让你想逛青楼逛青楼，想去赌坊去赌坊，想吃什么就吃什么，人生快快乐乐。”陈浩宇边往外走边说。
"You bet! Stay loyal to me and I'll treat you to brothels, gambling dens, fine food—pure paradise!" Chen Haoyu boasted as they walked out.

青瓜兴奋道：“公子，我还没去过青楼，啥时候带我去？”
Excited, Qinggua bounced: "Never even seen inside a brothel—when are you taking me, Young Master?"

“办完正事，就带你去。先出去套好马车，与本公子去趟望月轩，解决完大事，带你去春香楼快活快活。”
"Right after we handle urgent business! Go hitch a carriage—we're swinging by Wangyue Pavilion first, and once this big mess is sorted, straight to Chunxiang Brothel for fun!"

陈浩宇认为，因他散布谣言，父母理亏，不敢驳了知府大人的面子，才答应与朱家的亲事。而奶奶长期待在陈府，什么都不了解，自然认为这门亲事再好不过。说到底，陈家没人能帮到他。
Chen Haoyu had done the math: guilty over his rumor stunt, his parents wouldn't dare defy the Prefect; sheltered inside Chen Manor, Grandmother thought this match was heaven-sent—nobody inside Chen Manor could bail him out.

他打算去找朱淑真，凭她的脾气，听到两家要订婚肯定会被气得炸毛，不用费吹灰之力，婚事会被朱淑真搅黄。
His best weapon was Zhu Shuzhen herself! With her volcanic temper, tell her about the betrothal and she'd blow the roof off Zhu Manor—killing the engagement without him lifting a finger!

能跟着公子出去，青瓜当然开心：“公子，望月轩是什么地方？”
Thrilled just to step past the gate, Qinggua asked, "What's Wangyue Pavilion, Young Master?"

“废话真多，只管跟我走就是，少瞎问。”
"Quit yapping and follow orders!"

到达陈府门外，青瓜让车夫把马车套上，他亲自赶车，带公子去望月轩。
Outside Chen Manor, Qinggua had the stablehands hitch up a carriage and took the reins himself to drive his master over.

有公子指路，青瓜很容易找到望月轩，停好马车后，等公子下来，才去门口，向一位小厮说：“陈公子来访，麻烦通报。”
Guided by Chen Haoyu, Qinggua located Wangyue Pavilion fast, pulled up, waited for his master to step down, and went to tell a gate boy: "Young Master Chen is calling—go announce him."

小厮说：“稍等，我这就去通报。”
"Wait a moment; I'll report."

小厮来到主人的闺房门外，向里说：“姑娘，门外来了位陈公子，说要见您。”
Reaching the bedchamber courtyard, the boy called through: "Young Lady, a Young Master Chen is at the gate asking to see you."

千里寻指挥下人训练完千里拳，刚坐下写话本，思路也刚打开，听到“陈公子”的称谓，第一个猜到陈浩宇。心想：“他来做什么？哼！肯定没好事！”
Fresh from drilling her servants in Qianli Fist and just catching her flow on a new huaben chapter, Qianli Xun guessed "Young Master Chen" meant Chen Haoyu—nothing good ever followed him!

千里寻并不理会，继续写话本。
Refusing to budge, she kept right on writing.

苦荞明白小姐的意思，掀开帘子，走到屋外：“你去告诉陈公子，小姐今日没空。”
Reading her mistress's mood, Kuqiao lifted the curtain and told the boy: "Tell Young Master Chen Miss has no time today."

小厮答应后，跑到门口，回道：“我家姑娘今日忙，没空见陈公子。”
Trotting back to the gate, the boy relayed: "My mistress is busy today and can't see Young Master Chen."

陈浩宇一听，十分不满：“你以为本公子愿意见她？你去告诉朱淑真，她要是不想嫁给本公子，就老老实实出来见我。”
Chen Haoyu bristled: "Think I'm dying to see HER?! Go tell Zhu Shuzhen if she doesn't want to marry me, she'd better drag herself out here right now!"

小厮不明所以，又不敢多问，只能回去继续禀报。在闺房外，小心说道：“姑娘，陈公子说，您要不想嫁给他，就老老实实出去见他。”
Mystified yet scared to ask, the boy sprinted back to whisper outside her door: "Young Lady, Young Master Chen says if you don't want to marry him, come out and see him right now."

千里寻听了，甚觉蹊跷，又充满担忧，搁下笔，站起来：“苦荞，随我出去会会陈浩宇。”
Alarm flashing with suspicion, Qianli Xun set down her brush and stood: "Come on, Kuqiao—let's see what Chen Haoyu's up to."

苦荞取下披风，替小姐披好，跟随她来到望月轩门口。
Draping a cape over her shoulders, Kuqiao followed her out to the gate.

千里寻瞧到陈浩宇欠揍的样子，不满道：“找老娘干什么？有话快说！有屁快放！”
Taking one look at Chen Haoyu's punchable smirk, Qianli Xun snapped, "What do you want?! Spit it out and get moving!"

陈浩宇跨过门槛，站到里边，调侃道：“朱淑真，你爹老奸巨猾，自己女儿没人要，却来祸害我，逼着我爹娘到你家提亲，七日后亲事就要定下，本公子可不想娶你，你肯定也不想嫁给本公子，赶紧回朱府好好劝说你爹，不要把你嫁到我们陈家。”
Stepping across the threshold, Chen Haoyu sneered: "That sly old fox father of yours couldn't offload his unwanted daughter, so he strong-armed my parents into proposing! In seven days our betrothal gets sealed! I don't want to marry you, and you sure don't want to marry me—march straight back to Zhu Manor and talk your dad out of marrying you to the Chens!"

千里寻既震惊又不敢相信：“你该不会是骗我吧？怎么可能发生这种毫无道理的事情？”
Stunned in disbelief, Qianli Xun stared: "Are you pulling my leg?! How on earth could something that insane happen?!"

“本公子看着有这么无聊吗？你回朱府问问你爹便知，我可不想拿自己的幸福开玩笑，跟你成亲，咦……，想着浑身都起鸡皮疙瘩。你呢，不管用任何方法，负责劝说你爹，我呢，负责劝说我爹娘，还有奶奶。我保证，只要这件事情成功，我以后跟你井水不犯河水，以后见到你，二话不说，立刻滚蛋，你看如何？”
"Look like I've got time to joke about my own wedding?! Go ask your dad yourself! Marrying you—ugh, gives me goosebumps just picturing it! Here's the deal: you work on your dad by any means necessary, I work on my parents and grandmother. Pull this off, and I swear I'll stay out of your lane for life—spot you on the street and I'll bolt on sight! Deal?"

千里寻见陈浩宇说得认真，不像欺骗她的样子。再说他没有任何理由用个人的婚姻大事欺骗她，看来八成是真。
Watching Chen Haoyu's dead-serious panic, she knew he wasn't joking—nobody joked about his own forced wedding! Eighty percent chance it was real.

“此事我还需详细了解，若正如你所言，你觉得我会同意嫁给你？”千里寻说完，扭头离开，“不谢！再也不见！”
"I'll verify this myself. If you're telling the truth—do you honestly think I'd say yes to marrying YOU?!" Turning sharp, she tossed back: "No thanks, and never darken my door again!"

陈浩宇对着她的背影喊道：“再也不见说得有些早！”
Chen Haoyu yelled after her: "Bit early to say 'never again'!"

喊完后，带着得意的表情自语道：“本公子太聪明，有你出马，订婚之事必败无疑！”
Turning away with a smug grin, he patted himself on the back: "Pure genius! Unleash her on old Zhu, and this betrothal is toast!"

然后对身旁的青瓜说：“走！本公子带你去春香楼快活快活，现在终于不用绷着，可以开荤！”
Clapping Qinggua's shoulder: "Come on! Taking you to Chunxiang Brothel to celebrate—no more holding back, time to break my fast!"

青瓜高兴地答应着：“好嘞！跟公子混就是好！”
Qinggua cheered: "Woohoo! Rolling with Young Master rocks!"

话说林觉与朱晞颜分别后，来到幽林别居，向仪王说明情况。
Meanwhile, after parting from Zhu Xiyan, Lin Jue had ridden straight to Youlin Retreat to report the culprit to Prince Yi.

申简辰万万没想到，背后散播谣言之人竟然是陈浩宇。在他的印象中，陈浩宇跟千里寻基本没来往过。
Shen Jianchen never guessed Chen Haoyu was the mastermind behind the smears—as far as he knew, Chen Haoyu and Qianli Xun barely crossed paths!

他想起在朱府参加宴会时，无意中看到的一幕，才明白二人早已有过节。
Then that glimpse at Zhu Manor's banquet clicked back into memory: those two had bad blood from way back.

申简辰虽跟陈浩宇没打过交道，但在聚香楼一遇，已看出此人纨绔无比，能做出散播谣言之事，倒也符合他的做派。
Even without dealing with Chen Haoyu directly, one run-in at Juxiang Tower had shown him the kid was an incorrigible rake—a smear campaign fit his playbook to a tee.

申简辰不参与临安政务，可对临安的情况已有所了解。陈家虽无从政之人，但经济实力不可小觑，大到可以影响整个临安的经济命脉。
Hands-off though he stayed in local politics, Shen Jianchen knew Lin'an's balance of power: the Chen clan held no offices yet controlled half the city's lifeblood.

陈家与朱家又是亲家，朱晞颜才单独处理此事，看来他不想让此事宣扬开来，估计是想私了。
Plus the Chens and Zhus were in-laws—explaining why Zhu Xiyan handled it behind closed doors to keep things quiet.

考虑到陈家在临安的地位，申简辰也觉此事不宜公开，
Weighing the Chens' clout, Shen Jianchen agreed dragging it into court made no sense.

“林统领办事迅速，这么快抓到幕后之人，能力不可小觑。”
"Fast work, Commandant Lin—nabbing the mastermind this quick proves real skill."

被仪王夸赞，林觉自知升职有望：“王爷谬赞，能为王爷做事，下官不胜荣幸。”
Glowing with visions of another promotion, Lin Jue bowed: "Your Highness flatters me—serving Prince Yi is my greatest honor."

申简辰点点头，问道：“有多少人知道此事？”
Shen Jianchen nodded and asked, "How many people know?"

“王爷您，知府大人与下官，还有几个负责此事的手下，不过他们都是我的心腹，必然守口如瓶。”
"Only Your Highness, the Prefect, myself, and a handful of my most loyal men—every one sworn to silence."

“你先回去，让我仔细想想。”
"Head back for now; let me think this over."

林觉拜别后，申简辰认为没有什么可想的，欺负他心爱之人，只有一个字：打！
Once Lin Jue bowed out, Shen Jianchen decided there was nothing to think over—bully the woman he loved, and only one word applied: BEAT!

“白风！”
"Bai Feng!"

白风从外面进来：“王爷有何吩咐？”
Stepping in from outside: "Your orders, Your Highness?"

“你去把陈浩宇给我抓来，带到神柳下，我要好好教训他。”
"Go grab Chen Haoyu and haul him beneath the Divine Willow—I'm teaching him a lesson."

王爷要打人，白风无不高兴，他练拳法以来，还未打过人，不如用陈浩宇练练手。
A royal order to beat someone up thrilled Bai Feng—he'd been drilling punches for months without a live punching bag! Chen Haoyu made great practice!

“是！王爷！我这就去把他抓来！”
"Yes, Your Highness! Bringing him right back!"

白风一溜烟跑出去，赶了马车，到城里后，先去陈府外转一圈儿，没发现人。向陈府门外的下人一打听，才知道陈公子不在家。
Bolting out, Bai Feng drove into town, swung past Chen Manor, and learned from gatekeepers Second Young Master wasn't home.

白风心想，像陈浩宇这类人要么去青楼，要么去赌坊。他去过春香楼，不如去那里碰碰运气。
Where did a rake like Chen Haoyu go? Either a brothel or a gambling den! Having been to Chunxiang Brothel before, he checked there first.

白风刚将马车赶到春香楼外不远处，瞧见陈浩宇从马车里下来。他立刻启动功力，利用轻功迅猛之力，以迅雷不及掩耳的速度抓住陈浩宇……
Sure enough, pulling up near Chunxiang Brothel, he caught Chen Haoyu stepping straight out of his carriage! Launching his light-foot technique at blinding speed, Bai Feng swooped in like lightning and snatched Chen Haoyu clean off his feet...

近期，王爷开始给他教轻功，白风学得很快，加上拳法的功力，他比以前厉害许多。
Recently coached in light-foot work by Prince Yi on top of his boxing drills, Bai Feng had leveled up fast.

青瓜只觉一股强风袭来，迅速闭上眼睛，等睁开时，公子已不见人影，他四下瞅瞅，才发现公子被人抓上一辆马车，要去追时，马车已迅速启动。
Blinded by a sudden blast of wind, Qinggua squeezed his eyes shut—opening them to empty air where his master had stood! Whipping his head round, he saw his master tossed into a strange carriage that tore off down the lane before he could even yell!

青瓜吓得瘫坐在地上：“这可怎么办？我怎么给老爷和夫人交代！”
Qinggua collapsed onto his backside in panic: "What do I do?! How do I face Master and Madam?!"

陈浩宇坐在马车里，身上被点了穴位，既不能喊叫，也不能动弹。
Inside the carriage, struck on his acupoints, Chen Haoyu sat frozen stiff and mute.

他见过白风，知道是仪王身边的侍卫。心想抓他的人肯定是仪王，他与仪王毫无过节，他为何要抓他？
He recognized Bai Feng as Prince Yi's guard—meaning Prince Yi had snatched him! Having zero grudge with Prince Yi, why on earth was he kidnapped?!

能确定的是，仪王不会害他性命，因为大家都说仪王人好，心好。
At least Prince Yi wouldn't kill him—everyone swore Prince Yi was kindhearted.

一路上陈浩宇带着疑惑，被带到城外的山脚下。
Riddled with confusion, he was driven straight out past the city gates to the foot of the hills.

白风停好马车后，先用绳子把陈浩宇绑起来，再点开穴位。
Stopping the carriage, Bai Feng tied Chen Haoyu's wrists first, then tapped open his acupoints.

“陈公子，下车吧。”
"Out you get, Young Master Chen."

能说话后，陈浩宇大声问：“你凭什么抓我？”
Finding his voice, Chen Haoyu barked, "By what right do you kidnap me?!"

白风笑道：“凭王爷吩咐我这么做！赶紧下车，王爷还在神柳下等你。”
Bai Feng grinned: "By His Highness's order! Move it—His Highness is waiting beneath the Divine Willow."

陈浩宇很不情愿地走下马车，一日之内被绑两次，实在晦气！边走边说：“仪王这么有身份的人找我，我敢不去？何至于将我绑起来？”
Climbing down in foul disgust—kidnapped TWICE in one day, talk about cursed luck!—he grumbled as he walked: "If a royal prince summons me, wouldn't I come on my own? Why tie me up?!"

白风说：“少废话！一会儿见到王爷，有你好果子吃！”
Bai Feng sneered: "Quit yapping—you're about to get a taste of what's coming!"

白风押着陈浩宇顺着山脚下向神柳处走去。周围的雪早已融化，满眼是冬季的萧瑟。
Marching Chen Haoyu along the base of the hills toward the Divine Willow, melted snow left bare winter earth all around.

陈浩宇第一次来这里，他早听说此处有一棵神柳，经常有青年男女把红绸系在柳树上。他觉得甚是无聊，也不相信神柳真的像大家传言的那么神奇。
Chen Haoyu had never set foot out here before, having heard endless talk of young lovers tying red silk ribbons to some magic willow—total nonsense, he figured.

他只觉眼前神柳除粗壮些，年代久远些，与普通柳树毫无区别。
Up close, aside from a thick trunk and old age, it looked like any plain willow.

申简辰站在柳树下，瞧见白风将陈浩宇带来。没等陈浩宇行礼，直接命令道：“白风，把陈公子身上的绳子解开，你只管用拳头打他，避开要害处即可。”
Standing beneath the branches as Bai Feng marched Chen Haoyu up, Shen Jianchen skipped waiting for bows and barked: "Untie him, Bai Feng, and beat him with your bare fists—just avoid anything fatal."

白风二话不说，为陈浩宇解身上的绳子。
Without a second thought, Bai Feng slipped the ropes off Chen Haoyu.

陈浩宇急了：“仪王！仪王！您再身份尊贵，也不能随便打人……”
Panic seized Chen Haoyu: "Prince Yi! Prince Yi! Royal rank or not, you can't beat people for nothing—"

话还未说完，只见白风对着陈浩宇一阵拳打脚踢……
Before he finished the sentence, Bai Feng's fists and boots rained down in a relentless storm...
