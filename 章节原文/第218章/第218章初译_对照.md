第218章 可怜可叹叶安雅
## Chapter 218: Poor, Pitiful Ye Anya

叶安雅在墨香苑用过午饭，又与淑真聊会儿，才不舍地离开。
她之所以愿意与淑真交好，除年纪相仿外，这位妯娌的经历让她既好奇又倾佩。
和离一事已让很多女子汗颜，去年被全城百姓那样非议，她竟然还活得有滋有味，搁一般女子都不知跳过多少次钱塘江。
与浩宇定婚后，她竟然为退婚不顾大家闺秀的形象跑来胡闹，虽以失败告终，却勇气可嘉。
成亲那日，当叶安雅听说仪王前来抢亲，郡主因此而受伤时，她更加对淑真产生兴趣，认为她实在是女子中的楷模，哪怕要成亲，依然能有人那么疯狂地爱上她。
今日是叶安雅第一次与淑真畅谈，并未提到她感兴趣的话题。她只是边说话，边用心观察，想看出她身上有哪些独特之处，更想让自己受到淑真的影响，做出一直想做又不敢做的事情来。
叶安雅决定以后要跟淑真多多往来。
当叶安雅回到自己院中，竟然发现婆婆包氏站在面前，表情严肃。
叶安雅上前行礼：“婆婆，您怎么来了？”
包氏问：“大中午的，你不在自己院中用饭，过了饭点儿才回来，跑去哪里逛荡？”
婆婆用“逛荡”一词说她，叶安雅心中不满，又不敢在婆婆面前表现出来，努力微笑着：“婆婆用‘逛荡’一词太过严重，不过是找淑真聊聊，她热情邀请，我不好拒绝，顺便在她院中用饭而已。”
包氏不满道：“哼！你瞧瞧人家淑真，嫁过来没几日，就把老太太搞定。你呢？都嫁过来两年多，到老太太屋里去过几回？更别说帮浩东搞定老太太。还有你这肚子，怎么这么不争气？到现在也不见个动静？难怪浩东瞧不上你，总不喜欢回这院子。”
叶安雅不想继续被婆婆训斥：“婆婆，您要无事，我进屋了。”
没等包氏同意，叶安雅自顾走进屋去。
包氏很生气，对着身边的丫鬟说：“你瞧瞧，她哪像个脾气温顺的儿媳妇？亏得别人在外面夸她温柔孝顺，搞得我好像是个恶婆婆一样！”
包氏说完，没好气地走了。
屋内，叶安雅躺在闺床上，心中生着闷气。
丫鬟小翠走过来：“小姐，今日一早，我去老地方，曹公子派人送来一张纸条。”
After lunch in Moxiang Courtyard and another long chat with Shuzhen, Ye Anya took her leave with reluctance.

叶安雅立刻来了精神，起身，从小翠手里接过纸条，打开后看到上面写着：“上邪，我欲与君相知，长命无绝衰。山无陵，江水为竭。冬雷震震，夏雨雪。天地合，乃敢与君绝。”
Beyond being close in age, her new sister-in-law's life fascinated and inspired her. Going through a mutual divorce would have crushed most women's pride, and enduring an entire city's slander last year would have driven ordinary gentlewomen to throw themselves into the Qiantang River ten times over—yet Shuzhen had come through thriving. Betrothed to Haoyu, she had thrown ladylike decorum to the wind and stormed Chen Manor to break off the match; even in failure, her nerve commanded awe.

叶安雅叹道：“他对我如此用情，可我已嫁为人妇，让我如何全了这份情？”
Then on her wedding day, Prince Yi had raided the bridal procession and Princess Jiahe had taken a blade over it, making Shuzhen even more remarkable in Ye Anya's eyes—a woman who could inspire that kind of mad devotion even while wed to another.

“小姐与曹公子相爱在先，嫁给姑爷是逼不得已。姑爷对您又打又骂，这样的日子小姐愿意过下去，我都不愿看下去。”
During this first real talk, Ye Anya had not brought up the question burning inside her. She had watched and listened instead, studying what set Shuzhen apart, hoping some of that courage would rub off on her and push her to take the step she had dreamed of yet feared to attempt. She resolved to visit Moxiang Courtyard often.

“我也不愿跟他过下去，可婚已成，该如何是好？”
The moment Ye Anya stepped back into her own courtyard, however, her mother-in-law Madam Bao stood waiting with a thunderous face.

“小姐不是想跟姑爷和离吗？”
Ye Anya curtsied. "Mother-in-law, what brings you here?"

“是这么想，可要走上这条路哪有那么容易？”
"High noon, and instead of eating in your own courtyard, you come strolling back past mealtime! Where have you been gallivanting?"

“刚嫁过来的朱家姑娘和离过，小姐不如向她取取经？”
Stung by the word "gallivanting," Ye Anya swallowed her resentment and forced a smile. "That's a bit harsh, Mother-in-law. I only went to chat with Shuzhen. She pressed me to stay for lunch, and it felt rude to refuse."

“我今日找她正是为此事，可话到嘴边一直难以开口，等下次再找机会向她请教。”
"Hmph!" Madam Bao snapped. "Look at Shuzhen—barely married a few days and she already has Old Madam Xia eating out of her hand! And you? Over two years in this house, and how many times have you visited Old Madam Xia, let alone won her over for Haodong? Not to mention that useless belly of yours—still not a sign of an heir! No wonder Haodong has no use for you and hates setting foot in this courtyard."

二人正说着，陈浩东从外面进来，小翠赶紧拿过小姐手中的纸条，塞进自己袖子里，躲到一边。
Unwilling to stand there taking another tongue-lashing, Ye Anya said flatly, "If you have nothing else, Mother-in-law, I'm going inside." Without waiting for dismissal, she walked straight into her room.

陈浩东批评道：“你这丫头越来越没礼貌，也不知道问候我一声？”
Fuming, Madam Bao turned to her maid. "Look at that attitude! Does she act like a meek daughter-in-law? Everyone outside praises her as gentle and dutiful, making me look like some wicked mother-in-law!" With a huff, she stormed away.

小翠慌张地问候：“姑爷好。”
Inside, Ye Anya lay down on her bed, simmering in silent misery.

叶安雅已站起来：“夫君今日回来得早？”
Her maid Xiaocui hurried over. "Miss, I checked our usual spot first thing this morning. Young Master Cao sent a note."

“怎么？嫌我回来得早？这屋里该不会藏着不该藏的人？别以为我不知道成婚前你跟曹家公子有私情。”
Life sparked back into Ye Anya's eyes. Sitting up, she unfolded the slip and read:

叶安雅柔弱中带着倔强：“我是与他有过一段情，都是过去的事，成亲时你也知道，又何必现在拿出来说事？夫君说我，我倒也想说说你，别以为我不知道春金蝉，你跟她在外面干的勾当我也有所耳闻。”
"By Heaven above! I would know you as my own, / Our bond unbroken, undecaying through all our years. / Only when mountains have no peaks, when rivers run dry, / When thunder rumbles in winter, snow falls in summer, / And heaven and earth fuse as one— / Only then would I dare part from you!" 小翠料到接下来姑爷肯定会动手，她替自家小姐感到紧张
Ye Anya let out a long sigh. "He loves me with all his heart, yet I am another man's wife. How can I ever answer such devotion?"

陈浩东对小翠大喊：“滚出去！”
"You and Young Master Cao loved each other first, Miss," Xiaocui whispered. "You only married the young master because you had no choice. He beats you and curses you—even if you're willing to endure this life, I can't bear watching it anymore!"

小翠立刻跪下：“姑爷！您千万不要打我家小姐，您上次打在她身上的伤还未全好！”
"I don't want to live with him either, yet the marriage is done. What can I do?"

“来人！”陈浩东向门外大喊。
"Didn't you want a mutual divorce from him, Miss?"

进来两名下人。
"I do, yet walking that road isn't easy."

陈浩东命令道：“给我把小翠拉出去，把门关好。”
"The new Zhu bride went through a mutual divorce before! Why not ask her how she did it?"

两名下人照做。
"That's why I went to see her today, yet every time the words reached my lips, I couldn't get them out. I'll find another chance to ask her."

门被从外面关好后，陈浩东靠近叶安雅：“你越来越张狂，看来还是我打得太轻。”
Before Xiaocui could answer, Chen Haodong strode through the doorway. Xiaocui snatched the note from her mistress's fingers, jammed it up her sleeve, and backed to the wall.

叶安雅向一旁躲去：“别以为你打我，我就会屈服？你错了，你越打我我越对你失望！”
Chen Haodong scowled at her. "Getting bolder by the day, aren't you? Don't even bother greeting your master?"

陈浩东上前抓住叶安雅的衣领：“刚才听我娘说，你跑去跟浩宇的媳妇拉关系，我警告你，少跟她学，她在全城臭名昭著，你可得好好把你的名声给我保住，一年后，是定陈家产业继承人的日子，到时别坏了我的好事！”
"Greetings, Young Master," Xiaocui stammered.

叶安雅依然嘴上不饶人：“你在外人面前装得好，只有我知道你是什么货色，就你这样还想继承陈家产业……”
Ye Anya rose to her feet. "Back early today, Husband?"

话还没说完，陈浩东一巴掌打在她的脸上：“让你胡说！”
"What, annoyed I came back early? Hiding someone in here who doesn't belong? Don't think I don't know about your little affair with that Cao boy before our wedding."

接着陈浩东又是一阵猛打，打得叶安雅无力反抗时，把她扔到床上，扯去衣裳：“打完你，再让你尽妻子的义务，那才叫一个爽！”
Beneath her fragile frame flared quiet defiance. "I did have feelings for him—that was in the past, and you knew before we married. Why dredge it up now? And since you're lecturing me, let's talk about you. Don't think I haven't heard about Chun Jinchan and the filthy business you carry on with her outside."

叶安雅倍感屈辱，却无力反抗，只能默默忍受……
Certain a beating was coming, Xiaocui went rigid with terror for her mistress.

完事后，陈浩东整理好衣装，对着床上衣衫凌乱，满身是伤，满脸泪痕的妻子说：“你这身子冷冰冰的，实在无趣，难怪总也怀不上孩子，你应该多向春香楼的商妓学学怎么伺候我。”
"Get out!" Chen Haodong roared at Xiaocui.

叶安雅听到一阵摔门声后，才流下屈辱的泪水。
Xiaocui dropped to her knees. "Young Master, please don't hit Miss! The bruises from last time haven't even healed!"

小翠走进屋内，看到小姐的样子，眼泪不由流下来：“小姐，以后您还是顺着姑爷说话，不要跟他对着来，否则受伤的都是您。”
"Servants!" Chen Haodong bellowed toward the door. Two men hurried in. "Drag Xiaocui out and shut the door!"

“面对讨厌的人，我没办法顺着。顺着他只能委屈自己的心，我宁可身上受伤，也不想心里难受。我……我要离开他，哪怕是被他休了，也要结束这段婚姻。”
They obeyed at once. Once the door clicked shut behind them, Chen Haodong stalked toward Ye Anya. "Getting mouthier every day. Guess I went too easy on you last time."

小翠从小姐的脸上看出比往日任何时候都坚定的表情，她感到欣慰，小姐嫁到陈家没过一天好日子，还不如彻底离开，哪怕孤身一人，被人唾骂，也比过这样的日子强。
Ye Anya edged away. "Think beating me will make me submit? You're wrong. Every blow only makes me despise you more!"

……
Chen Haodong lunged and grabbed her collar. "Mother just told me you went cozying up to Haoyu's wife. I'm warning you: stay clear of her bad habits! Her name is mud across Lin'an, and you'd better keep yours spotless. One year from now the clan chooses the heir to the Chen fortune—don't you dare ruin my chances!"

接下来的每日，说话艺人都会来陈府给老太太说书，老太太的院子里每天都围满人，不再像以前一样冷冷清清。
Ye Anya spat back without flinching. "You put on a grand act for outsiders, yet I know what kind of rot you are underneath. Someone like you expecting to inherit the Chen estate—"

只是没怎么见过大嫂叶安雅的身影，千里寻刚来陈家，很多事情还不了解，不敢随意多打听，也不方便随意上门询问，还是等等再说。
A stinging slap cut her off mid-sentence. "Keep running that mouth!"

千里寻还发现姚轻琰与陈江林也一直未出现，她便趁老太太不注意，大家也都听得认真时，悄悄离开。
A barrage of blows followed until Ye Anya had no strength left to fight back. He hurled her onto the bed and tore open her robes. "Beating you first and then making you do your wifely duty—nothing feels better!"

结果没走出多远看到姚轻琰带着丫鬟映雪在一棵树下徘徊。
Swallowing her humiliation, powerless to resist, Ye Anya endured in silence.

千里寻迎上去：“婆婆，您都到门口，为何不进去？”
When he finished, Chen Haodong straightened his clothes and sneered down at his bruised, tear-streaked wife in her rumpled garments. "Lying there cold as a slab of stone—no fun at all. No wonder you can't get pregnant! You should take lessons from the courtesans at Chunxiang Brothel on how to please a man."

姚轻琰说：“我怕老太太见到我生气。”
Only after the door slammed behind him did Ye Anya let her tears spill over.

公爹和婆婆什么都好，就是不懂揣摩老太太的心思，搞得大家长期误会，不如帮着提点一下。
Xiaocui rushed back in and burst out crying at the sight of her mistress. "Miss, next time just humor him and don't talk back! You're the only one who gets hurt!"

“婆婆多虑，奶奶是个喜欢热闹之人，若婆婆以后每日让奶奶屋里热闹些，她指定开心，又怎么会生您的气？”
"I can't humor a man I loathe. Bending to him tortures my heart. Better to carry bruises on my skin than rot inside. I'm leaving him—even if he has to write a letter of repudiation to cast me out, I'm ending this marriage."

姚轻琰不大相信：“这办法好使吗？”
Seeing firmer resolve in her mistress's eyes than ever before, Xiaocui felt a swell of relief. Her mistress had not known one happy day in Chen Manor; walking out—even alone, even scorned—beat living in this cage.

“当然好使，儿媳正是用此办法把奶奶哄得笑得不拢嘴，现在对我的态度大变。”
Over the next few days, the storyteller came to Chen Manor every afternoon, turning Old Madam Xia's once-quiet courtyard into the liveliest spot in the estate.

“为何你刚来就能揣摩到老人家的心思？我可是给她当儿媳妇当了这么久，都没发现她喜欢热闹？”
Ye Anya, however, never showed up. Still new to the household, Qianli Xun refrained from prying or barging into her cousin-in-law's quarters, deciding to wait and see.

“我哪有那么厉害？不过是得到韦嬷嬷的提点。”
She noticed too that neither Yao Qingyan nor Chen Jianglin ever stepped inside. One afternoon, while Old Madam Xia and everyone else sat absorbed in the tale, Qianli Xun slipped out on her own—and found Yao Qingyan pacing beneath a tree just outside the gate with her maid Yingxue.

“所以我说这临安城里许多大家闺秀都羡慕朱家姑娘身边有个韦嬷嬷这号人物。”
Qianli Xun walked right up. "Mother-in-law, you're right at the gate. Why aren't you going in?"

千里寻趁机夸道：“临安城里最令人羡慕的则是公爹对婆婆的好。”
"I'm afraid seeing me will only upset Old Madam Xia," Yao Qingyan murmured.

“你这张嘴，难怪这么快能将老太太搞定。”姚轻琰笑完，脸上又出现愁容，“别人羡慕我跟你公爹之间的感情，可老太太却很嫌弃。”
Both her father-in-law and mother-in-law were wonderful people, Qianli Xun thought, just clueless about reading the old lady's heart, trapped in years of misunderstanding. Time to lend a hand.

千里寻提醒道：“婆婆有没有想过，奶奶见您和公爹总生气，不是因为你们感情好，而是因为别的？”
"You're overthinking it, Mother-in-law. Grandmother loves lively company. If you fill her courtyard with warmth and chatter every day, she'll be thrilled—why would she get mad at you?"

姚轻琰很是疑惑：“因为别的？我倒想不明白。”
Yao Qingyan looked doubtful. "Does that actually work?"

“我把奶奶哄高兴后才知道，她每次见我不高兴，并不是真正生我的气，而是想用不高兴引起我注意，然后让我在她身上多花些心思，她也就不那么孤单。”
"Of course it works! That's the exact trick I used to get Grandmother grinning ear to ear, and look how her attitude toward me flipped overnight."

姚轻琰立刻醒悟：“原来因为这个！怪不得我和你公爹怎么做都做不到老太太心里，反而被她骂，她越骂，我们越是避开她。老太太也真是，不明说，让我和你公爹一直误会她。”
"How did you figure out her mind right after arriving, when I've been her daughter-in-law for years and never noticed she craved company?"

千里寻解释道：“奶奶告诉我，说了多没意思，我估计她是想体会别人在她身上花心思的感觉，老人家就跟小孩子一样，喜欢被人关注被人重视，以后婆婆和公爹只要日日去陪伴老太太，让她的院子热闹些，保准她会开心。”
"I'm not that clever—Matron Wei pointed it out to me."

“我终于明白为何老太太喜欢浩宇，除了因为浩宇长得跟他死去的爷爷像，还因为浩宇喜欢去老太太屋里坐，会说些哄老太太开心的话。”姚轻琰抓住淑真的手，“淑真，陈家娶你真是娶对了，你一进门，我就明白老太太的心思，谢谢你。”
"No wonder every well-bred lady in Lin'an envies you for having Matron Wei by your side."

千里寻心想：“你们陈家是娶对人，我可是嫁错人！”嘴上却说：“我们别站这里只顾说话，您跟我进去，奶奶这会儿开心，绝不会生气。”
Qianli Xun grinned. "What everyone in Lin'an envies most is how Father-in-law dotes on you, Mother-in-law."

姚轻琰点点头，跟着淑真小心走进院中，说话艺人正讲得眉飞色舞：
"That silver tongue of yours—no mystery how you won Old Madam Xia over!" After a chuckle, sorrow crept back onto Yao Qingyan's face. "Outsiders envy our bond, yet Old Madam Xia can't stand seeing it."

玄知为玉面狐狸取了一个名字：落英，落英从此成为玄知殿中的婢女，负责每日伺候玄知饮食用度。
"Did you ever consider, Mother-in-law, that Grandmother isn't upset because you and Father-in-law are close, just for another reason?"

玄知是人人称赞的真神，拥有无上神力，在天界的地位也无人能比，连天君都要给他几分薄面。可落英却瞧不上他，只因他总是冷冰冰的，不苟言笑，甚是无趣。
"Another reason? I can't imagine what."

落英每日伺候完玄知，喜欢待在荷花池旁，对着一朵荷花自语。这一日她又对着荷花说：“荷花啊荷花，我整日伺候那位真神实在没趣，我想离开真神殿，到别处逛逛。”
"I only figured it out after cheering her up. Whenever she acts cross, she isn't really angry—she's just putting on a frown to get attention, hoping we'll fuss over her so she doesn't feel so lonely."

没想到传来一个声音：“我带你去逛。”
Light dawned across Yao Qingyan's face. "So that's it! No wonder everything your father-in-law and I did backfired and earned us a scolding—and the more she scolded, the more we steered clear! Honestly, why didn't she just say so instead of leaving us misunderstanding her all these years?"

落英吓坏：“哪里来的声音？”她瞧瞧周围，什么都没有。便从石头上坐起来，准备离开，一转身，看到一个帅气的男子出现在眼前。
"Grandmother says spelling it out ruins the feeling," Qianli Xun explained. "She wants to feel people making an effort on their own. Elders are just like children—they want to feel noticed and cherished. From now on, if you and Father-in-law visit her every day and keep her courtyard lively, she's bound to beam."

落英向后退了一步：“你是谁？”
"Now I understand why she spoils Haoyu—not just because he looks like his late grandfather, but because he visits her quarters and sweet-talks her!" Yao Qingyan squeezed Shuzhen's hand. "Marrying you into the Chen family was the best decision we ever made. Barely through the door, and you unlocked Old Madam Xia's heart for me. Thank you."

男子说：“我是每日听你说话的荷花，你就唤我花美男。”
Good decision for the Chens, maybe—I'm the one who got stuck with the wrong groom! Qianli Xun thought, yet said aloud, "Don't just stand out here talking. Come inside with me. Grandmother's in a great mood; she won't get mad."

落英“噗嗤”一笑：“好俗的名字！”……
Yao Qingyan nodded and followed Shuzhen into the yard, where the storyteller was in full swing:

夏老太太正听得专注，没有注意大儿媳妇进来。姚轻琰也没去打扰，与淑真走到老太太跟前。
"Xuanzhi gave the jade-faced fox a name: Luoying. From that day on, Luoying served as a maid in his celestial palace, tending to his meals and daily needs. Every immortal praised Xuanzhi, supreme in power, deferred to even by the Heavenly Sovereign—yet Luoying couldn't stand him because he stayed icy and stone-faced, dull as dishwater.
