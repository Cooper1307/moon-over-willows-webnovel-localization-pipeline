# Chapter 56: Youqi Jushi is Tested

---

Shen Jianchen put down his wine cup and looked at Zhu Shuzhen. She was casting ambiguous glances at Wenyuan again.

---

"Wenyuan, you two are exchanging flirtatious glances. I think I'd better step away for a while."

---

Shen Jianchen got up to leave, but Qiao Wenxian held him back: "Look, someone is looking at you too."

---

Shen Jianchen looked over. Qiao Mengrong was raising her wine cup toward him.

---

Shen Jianchen smiled, a mix of satisfaction and disappointment, picked up the wine pot to fill his cup, raised it, made a toasting gesture toward Qiao Mengrong from afar, then drank it down.

---

Getting her brother's response, Qiao Mengrong also finished her wine. When she put down her cup, she noticed her brother had already gotten up and left the banquet area. She guessed he had probably gone to relieve himself.

---

Zhu Jingyuan took his wine cup over to drink with Qiao Wenxian. With her brother gone, Qiao Mengrong didn't bother observing. Remembering Youqi Jushi, she poured a little wine, picked up her cup, and went to sit beside Youqi Jushi.

---

Qianli Xun's sister-in-law had been sitting beside her. When Chen Wenxin saw a friend, she went over to greet her and chat, leaving the seat empty. Qiao Mengrong took the opportunity to sit down: "Why does Youqi Jushi keep looking at Young Master Qiao?"

---

Being discovered, Qianli Xun felt embarrassed and defended herself: "Don't misunderstand. I was watching my brother toast guests. He happened to be drinking with Young Master Qiao."

---

"Youqi Jushi doesn't need to explain. I know everything about Young Master Qiao's feelings for you."

---

Qianli Xun turned to Qiao Mengrong: "Young Lady Qiao is Wenyuan's distant relative. Do you come to Lin'an often?"

---

"No, I live in Bianliang. This is my first time in Lin'an. When I was in Bianliang, I often heard people mention Youqi Jushi's name. I've also read your poems and ci, and I admire them greatly. To meet you today is truly an honor. I toast you."

---

Qianli Xun raised her cup: "It's also my honor to meet Young Lady Qiao."

---

The two drank their wine together.

---

Qianli Xun asked: "You and Young Master Shen are both from Bianliang. You wouldn't happen to know each other already, would you?"

---

"He's my brother."

---

"So you're brother and sister. Why does one have the surname Shen and the other Qiao?"

---

"We're not biological siblings, and I have a marriage engagement with him."

---

Having just met Young Lady Qiao, Qianli Xun didn't feel it appropriate to press for the complicated details. Instead, she remembered meeting Young Master Shen in the bamboo forest: "Why is your brother looking for a talented scholar named Qianli Xun?"

---

"I didn't expect Youqi Jushi to know about this?"

---

"Oh, I lived in a bamboo forest in the countryside for a while. Your brother led people to find the bamboo forest. I happened to meet him there. We had a brief encounter."

---

"I see. Actually, it's nothing much. I heard from Bai Feng that my brother attended a storytelling session and thought the stories written by this talented scholar named Qianli Xun were excellent. He just wants to make his acquaintance."

---

It seemed completely consistent with Kuqiao's guess. Why not tell Young Master Shen that she was Qianli Xun? She quickly rejected the idea. Even if she told him, no one would believe her, and it would only make things more complicated.

---

"What is Youqi Jushi thinking about?"

---

"Nothing."

---

"My brother and I just arrived in Lin'an. We were fortunate to attend Young Master Dongfang's poetry gathering. The piece we appreciated was exactly 'Heartbreak Riddle.' Everyone praised Youqi Jushi's clever mind and superb ci skills."

---

Faced with others' praise, Qianli Xun didn't know how to respond. Besides, the ci wasn't written by her, so she didn't know what to say even more.

---

Miao Zhiyu sat with her mother Madam Qin. Madam Qin ran off to chat with Madam Xiang and others, leaving her alone. She ate while observing Young Master Qiao. Ever since Young Master Qiao took his seat, his gaze hadn't left Youqi Jushi.

---

Miao Zhiyu had always known about Qiao Wenxian's feelings for Youqi Jushi. Since Youqi Jushi had Young Master Xu in her heart, Miao Zhiyu felt she and Qiao Wenxian might still have a chance. When Young Master Xu moved his entire family and reportedly married, Miao Zhiyu grew nervous. It wasn't until Youqi Jushi married Lin Jue that she felt relieved.

---

Although she often felt it was a pity for Youqi Jushi, thinking she shouldn't have married someone without literary talent, as long as she was married, Young Master Qiao wouldn't have any chance. Now that Youqi Jushi was planning a mutual divorce, Miao Zhiyu was happy for her while also worried for herself.

---

At the banquet, Miao Zhiyu noticed that the way Youqi Jushi looked at Young Master Qiao was very different from usual. Could it be that Youqi Jushi had also fallen for Young Master Qiao?

---

On the wooden stage, the female dancers had changed to a new dance. Miao Zhiyu had no mind to appreciate it. She noticed that many people had finished most of their food and gathered in groups of two or three. Some stood, some sat, and some even went together to tour the Zhu residence. After all, the scenery of the Zhu residence was quite beautiful.

---

Miao Zhiyu had eaten her fill. She got up and went to Zhu Shuzhen, interrupting her conversation with Qiao Mengrong: "Youqi Jushi, would you do me the honor of taking a walk with me?"

---

Qiao Mengrong clearly sensed Miao Zhiyu's intention to invite Youqi Jushi alone. She got up and said politely: "Let's chat another day. Excuse me."

---

Qiao Mengrong got up, smiled and nodded at Miao Zhiyu, then left.

---

Qianli Xun stood up: "Sitting for so long, I'm tired too. I might as well take a walk with you."

---

Miao Zhiyu glanced at Qiao Wenxian. He was still looking in this direction, and Youqi Jushi also glanced back at Young Master Qiao, a sweet smile appearing on her face.

---

"Youqi Jushi looks very happy. I wonder what happy event there is?"

---

Being asked this, Qianli Xun felt embarrassed: "Today's banquet has everyone in high spirits. Of course I'm happy. Young Lady Miao should also be happier."

---

The two walked side by side out of the female guest area, picked a random direction, and walked while enjoying the scenery.

---

Xia Guo saw her young lady leave the female guest area and said to Kuqiao: "The young lady has left."

---

Kuqiao replied: "The young lady probably ate a bit too much and is touring the Zhu residence to digest. No need to pay attention. You and I can just enjoy the food in peace."

---

Xia Guo felt relieved and focused her attention even more on Young Master Qiao. She turned her head to take a look at Young Master Qiao. Dongfang Chenze, Cao Ban, and An Mingxuan had already gathered around him, and they were chatting happily. Although the three young masters were well-dressed and good-looking, they were still inferior compared to Young Master Qiao. Xia Guo was even more entranced as she watched.

---

Nan Shuang waved her chopsticks in front of Xia Guo's eyes: "Hey, why do you keep looking at my young master?"

---

Xia Guo's face immediately turned red. She turned her head and picked up some vegetables to eat.

---

Huayu laughed: "Her reaction is too normal. Wherever Young Master Qiao goes, he attracts the girls' admiration."

---

Xia Guo saw that Huayu had an excellent position where she could see Young Master Qiao without turning around: "Hey! Switch places with me."

---

Huayu was unwilling: "I'm not switching with you."

---

"If you switch with me, I'll save you a good one from the discarded manuscripts my young lady wrote."

---

"That's more like it."

---

Huayu got up and switched places with Xia Guo. Xia Guo happened to sit opposite Nan Shuang.

---

Kuqiao was very puzzled: "The dishes over there have all been eaten by Huayu. If you sit there, won't you have nothing to eat? Let me bring this plate of food over to you."

---

"Young Lady Kuqiao, keep it for yourself. I don't need it." After refusing, Xia Guo glanced at Qiao Wenxian again and asked Nan Shuang: "Has your young master been betrothed?"

---

"Why are you asking?"

---

"Just asking casually."

---

"Since you're just asking casually, of course I can't tell you."

---

"If you won't tell me, forget it."

---

Xia Guo continued to look at Qiao Wenxian. The delicious food in front of her was less interesting than Young Master Qiao.

---

Miao Zhiyu and Youqi Jushi walked and chatted in the Zhu residence. Finally, she brought the topic to Qiao Wenxian: "The other day, I heard that the Qiao family is selecting a bride for Young Master Qiao. With Young Master Qiao's character and family background, the Qiao family should choose a good young lady for him."

---

Qianli Xun stopped: "Is the news reliable?"

---

Miao Zhiyu also stopped and observed Youqi Jushi's expression: "Absolutely true. The Qiao family has sent out several matchmakers to collect information on suitable young ladies in Lin'an. Without my knowledge, my parents also gave my picture to the matchmakers."

---

Qianli Xun smiled bitterly: "How ridiculous. You and Wenyuan know each other. You still need a portrait?"

---

Miao Zhiyu replied: "There's no choice. The elders have their ways. Surely Youqi Jushi must be happy for Young Master Qiao?"

---

Of course Qianli Xun wasn't happy. She finally had someone she liked, and before their romance had even officially started, the man's family was selecting a bride for him. Wasn't this just creating obstacles for herself? If she weren't married, she could also be a candidate for the Qiao family's bride selection. But now...

---

Qianli Xun's heart was heavy as she continued walking forward.

---

Miao Zhiyu hurried to keep up: "Youqi Jushi is unhappy? Could it be that you've already fallen for Young Master Qiao?"

---

Qianli Xun wanted to admit it, but remembering her marriage, she didn't dare. She didn't care about herself, but she was afraid of ruining Wenyuan's reputation. She still needed to cover up: "Young Lady Miao is joking. Wenyuan and I consider each other kindred spirits in poetry. The Qiao family is selecting a bride for him. Of course I'm happy for him. I'm just feeling sad for the young ladies who won't be chosen. I can imagine now how many young ladies will cry their eyes red on Wenyuan's wedding day. Hehe..."

---

Miao Zhiyu felt somewhat relieved, but remembering the way the two looked at each other, she was filled with worry again and continued to probe: "But Young Master Qiao likes Youqi Jushi."

---

Qianli Xun tried to stay calm: "Young Lady Miao really knows how to joke..."

---

"I'm definitely not joking. Everyone who regularly attends Young Master Dongfang's poetry gatherings can see Young Master Qiao's feelings. Besides, Young Master Qiao hasn't hidden this from Young Master Dongfang."

---

"I'm already married. What do you mean by telling me these things, Young Lady Qiao?"

---

"You're in the process of a mutual divorce with the Military Training Official. After the divorce, would Youqi Jushi be willing to accept Young Master Qiao?"

---

Qianli Xun didn't know how to answer Miao Zhiyu's question. She remained silent and continued walking forward, hoping something would interrupt this topic.

---

Seeing Youqi Jushi not speak, Miao Zhiyu said something that was like a sigh and also like a reminder: "I truly feel it's a pity for Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao. If there were no marriage, you and Young Master Qiao together would be a beautiful story. But you're married. After a divorce, if you want to marry Young Master Qiao, I'm afraid the Qiao family won't agree..."

---

Miao Zhiyu had said this much. If Qianli Xun remained silent, wouldn't she be letting Miao Zhiyu guess and ask freely?

---

Qianli Xun stopped: "Young Lady Miao doesn't need to probe. My relationship with Wenyuan can only be one of two kinds. Either we're like friends without romantic feelings, or Wenyuan and I are in love. If it's the first kind, Young Lady Miao can rest assured. If it's the second kind, as long as I want to be with him, no one can stop me, even if I've been divorced once. Currently, Wenyuan and I are still in the first kind of relationship. But if some people keep reminding me in my ear that Wenyuan likes me, I'm worried I won't be able to resist accepting Wenyuan. Whether the second kind of relationship will appear is hard to say."

---

Miao Zhiyu quickly apologized: "Youqi Jushi, please don't misunderstand. I'm friends with both you and Young Master Qiao. I was worried about you, so I said so much..."

---

Before Miao Zhiyu could finish, Chen Haoyu's voice came: "Zhu Shuzhen, looks like we've run into each other. Shouldn't we say something?"

---

At the banquet, Chen Haoyu had nothing in common with the officials and literary talents. No one bothered to pay attention to him. He was bored alone, ate a few bites casually, and left the male guest area early. He wandered around, planning to say goodbye to his sister Chen Wenxin when it was about time so he could leave the Zhu residence early. He didn't expect to see Zhu Shuzhen and Miao Zhiyu walking toward him. He looked around and saw no one else. He might as well give Zhu Shuzhen a hard time with words and get some revenge, as long as he didn't provoke her into using her fists.

---

For the first time, Qianli Xun was grateful for Chen Haoyu's appearance. She finally didn't have to listen to Miao Zhiyu continue: "When you see me, shouldn't you hurry up and greet me then get lost? You actually dare to talk to me. Are you looking for a beating?"

---

Chen Haoyu smiled: "I see there's no one else here. I want to tell you something. You'll definitely be happy to hear it."

---

Qianli Xun was puzzled. What was Chen Haoyu up to? She might as well listen to what he had to say. She figured he wouldn't dare act recklessly: "Go ahead."

---

Chen Haoyu said: "When you were in the bamboo forest, I went to Juxiang Lou to eat and ran into Lin Jue. Not only did I help you deal with him, I also persuaded him to divorce you as soon as possible..."

---

Qianli Xun didn't quite believe it: "Are you that good?"

---

Chen Haoyu continued: "I'm actually not a bad person. It's just that you misunderstand me too deeply. Do you know what else I said to Lin Jue?"

---

"What did you say?" Qianli Xun was still full of anticipation.

---

Chen Haoyu prepared to run and shouted: "I told Lin Jue that you're divorcing him because you want to marry me!"

---

After saying this, Chen Haoyu took off running in the opposite direction.

---

Qianli Xun was furious and shouted: "Chen Haoyu! You wait! I'll catch you and chop you into pieces!"

---

She was about to chase after him, but Miao Zhiyu stopped her: "He looks quite afraid of you. Today is a banquet. No need to argue with him."

---

Qianli Xun had no choice but to give up.

---

This scene happened to be noticed by Shen Jianchen. After finishing in the latrine, he didn't want to go back to the banquet area. He didn't want to witness Wenyuan and Youqi Jushi exchanging flirtatious glances, and he didn't want to accept Qiao Mengrong's occasional glances either. He decided to walk around.

---

As he walked, he saw Zhu Shuzhen and Miao Zhiyu, then saw Chen Haoyu block them. He had seen Chen Haoyu's behavior before and was worried the two young ladies would be bullied. After getting closer, he hid to the side to observe, ready to protect them if Chen Haoyu made any moves.

---

When he heard Zhu Shuzhen refer to herself as "this lady," the style was very similar to Qianli Xun. He couldn't help but secretly look Zhu Shuzhen over and said to himself: "Could she be Qianli Xun? I didn't expect this era to also have such a fascinating spirit."
