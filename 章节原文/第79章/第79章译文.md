# Chapter 79: Almost Revealing the Truth After Drinking

---

Back at Youlin Villa, Baifeng supported the prince all the way.

---

The prince didn't act wild from drunkenness. He just kept grinning foolishly and loved to talk: "Hehe... Baifeng, let me... tell you. I used to be... a senior executive... at a company. Managed lots of... employees. And... I was also... handsome. Many girls... all liked me. Hehe..."

---

What "senior executive," what "company"—Baifeng didn't understand any of it. As he supported the prince, he chimed in: "Your Highness is truly amazing."

---

"Hehe... Amaz... amazing, right? Hehe... Let me tell you... even though many people like me, uh... I only like one person. Do you know... what her name is?"

---

Just as he was about to ask who the prince liked, Princess Jiahe blocked his path: "Baifeng, why is my brother so drunk?"

---

Seeing Princess Jiahe, Baifeng thought: "Of course I know. The person the prince likes must be Princess Jiahe, right?" He quickly replied: "The prince took me to Juxiang Restaurant for a meal. The wine there is good, so he had a few extra cups."

---

Qiao Mengrong ordered: "Quickly help my brother back to his room. Xilan, hurry to the kitchen and make some hangover soup."

---

Xilan followed the order and left.

---

Through drunken eyes, Shen Jianchen saw Qiao Mengrong. His already drooping head sank even lower. Drunk as he was, the sight of his sister still weighed on him. The heaviness silenced him again. He stopped grinning foolishly. He didn't want to say another word.

---

Baifeng helped the prince into the room, then to the bedside, and attended to him lying down. Not long after, Xilan brought the hangover soup.

---

Qiao Mengrong had Baifeng prop her brother up. She personally fed him spoonful by spoonful.

---

Though drunk, Shen Jianchen's mind wasn't muddled. He saw everything. Not wanting Mengrong to continue feeding him, he suddenly took the bowl from her hands, drank it all in one gulp, handed the bowl to Baifeng, then dropped his head and slept.

---

Qiao Mengrong was deeply saddened by her brother's sudden action. Clearly, he wasn't worried about her getting tired—he didn't want her to continue caring for him. She stared at her brother's back, feeling a rejection she had never experienced before.

---

She got up, walked out of the room, and came to the courtyard. She remembered the question her brother had drunkenly asked Baifeng: "But I only like one person. Do you know... what her name is?"

---

Qiao Mengrong couldn't be sure if the "her" her brother mentioned referred to herself: "Xilan, who do you think is the person my brother likes?"

---

Xilan said: "Of course it's you, Princess."

---

Baifeng followed her out. Hearing the princess's question, he also gave a definite answer: "Rest assured, Princess. The person the prince just mentioned must be you."

---

Only then did Qiao Mengrong show a smile. Baifeng was her brother's personal guard. He knew her brother's whereabouts better than anyone. If he said so, then it must be true.

---

"Xilan, later bring some pastries from my room to Baifeng as my reward."

---

Baifeng never refused food: "Thank you, Princess!"

---

Early that morning, after Chen Haoyu visited his sister Chen Wenxin at the Zhu residence, he stirred up trouble between Lin Jue and Zhu Shuzhen at the gate. Feeling pleased, he gathered four or five lackeys. After eating and drinking, they went to the pleasure quarters.

---

People strolling along the way, seeing Chen Haoyu coming with his gang, didn't dare bump into him and stepped aside. Chen Haoyu specifically chose places with beauties—singing girls, dancing girls—he wanted to visit them all.

---

Inside the dancing quarter, Chen Haoyu saw the beauties on stage and was actually stunned.

---

One lackey said: "Young Master Chen, why not invite that girl out for a meeting?"

---

Chen Haoyu didn't speak. He continued immersed in the dancing girl's swaying waist.

---

Another lackey said: "Why just meet? She should at least keep Young Master Chen company for a night."

---

Only then did Chen Haoyu speak: "I have important business to handle lately. To ensure success, I've secretly sworn an oath—no women for half a year. Just looking is fine."

---

The lackey asked: "Young Master Chen, what important business do you have? So important that you don't even care about beauties?"

---

Chen Haoyu beckoned with his finger. When the lackeys drew close, he whispered: "My important business is dealing with Zhu Shuzhen. I've never suffered a loss from anyone in my life. If I don't bring her down, I'm not a man!"

---

One lackey asked softly: "Bringing her down doesn't require abstaining from women."

---

"You don't understand. She's so formidable. How can it be easy to bring her down? Besides using your brain, you also need luck. Using your brain is easy to talk about, but luck isn't so simple. Have you heard? Scholars going to the capital for the imperial exams abstain from women before and after the exams, just to build up good luck for their names to appear on the honor roll. I'm learning from them." Chen Haoyu spoke very solemnly.

---

Another lackey said: "But you're not going to the capital for exams. Why abstain from women?"

---

Chen Haoyu pointed at that lackey: "I can tell you're uneducated just by listening. Though I'm not going for exams, the principle is the same. When you want to succeed at something, you need luck. To get luck, you must abstain from women. After I succeed, then I'll end my abstinence!"

---

The lackeys cheered: "We wish Young Master Chen ends his abstinence soon!"

---

They entered a few more pleasure quarters and looked around. But with Young Master Chen abstaining, everyone lost interest. They came out of one quarter and prepared to go back.

---

Chen Haoyu saw Dongfang Chenze leading Cao Ban and An Mingxuan passing by. He stepped forward and called out: "Dongfang Chenze, where are you three going?"

---

Only then did the three notice Chen Haoyu.

---

Dongfang Chenze smiled: "So it's Young Master Chen. Last time you lent me Zuiyun Tower, I haven't had the chance to thank you in person. Let me thank you now."

---

"No need to be polite. You also helped me." Chen Haoyu appeared magnanimous. "The three talented gentlemen appearing here—are you holding another poetry gathering?"

---

Dongfang Chenze said: "It is a poetry gathering, but not organized by me. It was initiated by a senior in the ci poetry world. We're mainly here to present a new ci poem by Youqi Jushi."

---

Hearing it was written by Zhu Shuzhen, Chen Haoyu's interest grew even more. Perhaps he could find another opportunity to deal with Zhu Shuzhen: "Could Young Master Dongfang take me along?"

---

The three stared at Chen Haoyu in surprise, then glanced at each other. When had he ever attended such an event? That was a miracle.

---

Dongfang Chenze couldn't easily refuse: "Taking Young Master Chen is no problem. But the people you brought really don't fit the poetry gathering's atmosphere. How about another day when I hold a poetry gathering, I'll invite you?"

---

How could Chen Haoyu let such an opportunity slip? He ordered his lackeys: "All of you, get away from me immediately!"

---

As soon as the words left his mouth, the lackeys quickly vanished. Their speed was astonishing.

---

Chen Haoyu smiled: "Now it should be fine, right?"

---

Dongfang Chenze continued: "It's fine now, but Young Master Chen must not show your usual attitude when you go."

---

Chen Haoyu pretended to be sincere: "Don't worry. When I get there, I won't say a word. I'll sit to the side and listen to everyone. I guarantee I won't embarrass you."

---

The three looked at each other again and nodded.

---

Chen Haoyu followed the three young masters to a tea-tasting quarter. Inside sat only literati—the oldest around sixty, the youngest about ten.

---

The four chose a tea table and sat down. The waiter brought the teapot and tea cups, then served some tea snacks.

---

The place had been reserved. Only those invited could enter.

---

First was the classic ci appreciation segment. At the moment, everyone was appreciating Dongpo Jushi's "Water Melody."

---

The three young masters came late and couldn't interrupt. They could only sit, sipping tea and listening attentively, waiting for the new ci recommendation segment to present Youqi Jushi's poem.

---

As soon as Chen Haoyu entered this environment, his scalp tingled. Goosebumps covered his whole body. He felt truly uncomfortable. But he didn't want to leave, so he simply lay on the tea table to nap. The literati's voices appreciating poetry were like hypnotic incense. Unaware, he drifted into dreams...
