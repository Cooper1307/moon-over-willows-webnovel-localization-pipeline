第214章 陈家兄弟各忙各
## Chapter 214: The Chen Brothers Each Mind Their Own Affairs

儿子携儿媳回门，陈江林则乘坐马车来到钱塘帮。
陈江林来访，面子还是要给的，毕竟他是陈家的老大，掌管陈家家业，能亲自登门拜访，定是有要事。
才两三天功夫，蔣爷身上的伤自然未痊愈，硬撑着坐到椅子上接见陈江林。
陈江林进入后，瞧见蔣爷身上伤势严重，心中窃喜，不知谁给打的，也算是帮陈家出口恶气。
心里如此想，嘴上却不能这么说，他不请自坐。
While his son and daughter-in-law paid their visit to Zhu Manor, Chen Jianglin rode by carriage to the Qiantang Gang.

“蔣爷这是怎么了？谁吃了熊心豹子胆，把您打成这样？”
As the eldest Chen brother and head of the family fortune, Chen Jianglin warranted respect. For him to call in person meant urgent business.

蔣爷哪敢说是仪王：“不提也罢，人在河边走，哪有不湿鞋？我不能总得势，也得让别人发泄发泄。陈老爷可是稀客，平日都派你家大侄子跟我往来，今日怎么会亲自登门？”
Only two or three days had passed, so Master Jiang's wounds were nowhere near healed. Still, he propped himself up in a chair to receive his guest.

“蔣爷做下什么事，难道不知？为何还问起我来？”
Stepping inside and seeing Master Jiang battered and bruised, Chen Jianglin felt a surge of quiet glee. Whoever had worked him over had done the Chen family a favor. Keeping his thoughts to himself, he took a seat without waiting to be asked.

蔣爷立刻明白是为赌博之事，笑道：“你可得感谢我，要不是我，陈浩宇能愿意娶个母老虎回去？”
"What happened to you, Master Jiang? Who had the gall to beat you into this state?"

“浩宇再不愿意亲事，迟早得娶。我不明白蒋爷为何突然关心起陈家之事？还要用陈家的产业作为赌注？赢了又不要，让人匪夷所思。”
Master Jiang dared not breathe a word about Prince Yi. "Not worth mentioning. Walk by the river long enough and your shoes get wet. I can't hold the upper hand every day; got to let folks blow off steam now and then. You're a rare visitor, Master Chen. Usually your eldest nephew handles dealings with me. What brings you here in person today?"

蒋爷谨守江湖规矩，不会随意将真实原因告知陈江林。
"Do you need me to remind you what you pulled?"

“不过是跟陈浩宇开个玩笑，谁让他在春香楼霸占我喜欢的人。我混江湖也明白一个道理，不是自己的不能随便沾手。你们陈家的产业我当然眼红，可我消受不起。只不过采取迂回手段，让您儿子赶紧成亲，好被母老虎管着，春金沫就是我的人。”
Catching on at once that this was about the wager, Master Jiang chuckled. "You ought to thank me. Without my nudge, would Chen Haoyu ever have agreed to marry that tigress?"

陈江林还是不信，总觉得另有隐情：“真没想到蒋爷还是一个情种，在您眼中，一个商妓竟然比陈家的财产还重要？”
"Reluctant or not, Haoyu was going to marry sooner or later. What puzzles me is why Master Jiang took such a sudden interest in my family's affairs—wagering the entire Chen fortune, winning, and then walking away without touching a coin. Makes no sense."

蒋爷不想再继续聊下去，担心说多，被陈江林看出什么来：“陈老爷与其前来质问我，不如好好管管你的儿子，都成亲的人，别整日往春香楼跑，省得被知府大人知道挨鞭子。”
True to underworld code, Master Jiang refused to spill the real story. "Just playing a joke on your boy for hogging the woman I wanted at Chunxiang Brothel. In my line of work, you learn not to reach for what isn't yours. Sure, the Chen fortune makes my mouth water, yet I'd choke trying to swallow it. I just took the roundabout path to push your son into marriage so his tigress could rein him in and leave Chun Jinmo to me."

陈江林听出对方有赶客的意思，他也不想跟蒋爷这种人多说。就目前情形来看，问不出什么，最后叮嘱道：“陈家能发展到今日，首富的地位屹立不倒，也不是谁想拿捏就能拿捏得了，奉劝蒋爷，往后在外面跟浩宇玩时，把握好尺度，否则我陈江林动用临安兵力灭了钱塘帮也不在话下。”
Chen Jianglin did not buy a word of it. "Who knew Master Jiang was such a romantic—valuing one courtesan above the Chen family's entire estate?"

陈江林说完，起身离去。
Wary of letting anything slip if the talk dragged on, Master Jiang shifted to the offensive. "Instead of interrogating me, Master Chen, you'd better rein in your son. He's a married man now. Tell him to stay out of Chunxiang Brothel before Prefect Zhu finds out and takes a whip to his hide."

蒋爷被陈江林最后说的话给气到，怎奈身上有伤，不方便随意发作，只吐出两个充满情绪的字：“不送！”
Hearing the dismissal in his tone, Chen Jianglin saw no point pushing further; he would pry nothing out of the man today. Before rising, he delivered a parting warning. "The Chen family did not reach the top of Lin'an's wealth by letting anyone push us around. Take a word of advice, Master Jiang: when you play around with Haoyu outside, know your limits. Push too far, and calling in the Lin'an garrison to wipe out the Qiantang Gang wouldn't cost me a sweat."

等陈江林走后，蒋爷奚落道：“横什么横！你们陈家表面看着光鲜艳丽，其实里面也不怎么好！”
With that, Chen Jianglin stood and walked out.

陈府内，陈江树的屋中，包氏与儿子陈浩东也在，三人将下人屏退，关上门窗，坐在桌旁悄悄密谈。
That parting threat stung Master Jiang's pride, yet his battered ribs kept him from jumping up to roar back. All he managed was a tight, venomous "Don't let the door hit you!"

“我看浩宇娶的娘子不好惹，都敢跟老太太顶嘴，我和弟妹到老太太跟前一阵蛊惑，老太太终于发怒，前去闹事，找出她三个错处，结果被她说得一点儿错处都没有，白白闹一回。”
Once Chen Jianglin was gone, Master Jiang spat in disgust. "Act all high and mighty! Your Chen household looks shiny on the outside, yet it's rotten enough on the inside!"

“厉害就好，儿子盼着浩宇赶紧吃厉害娘子的亏，我也好从梦中笑醒。”
Back at Chen Manor, inside Chen Jiangshu's quarters, Madam Bao and Chen Haodong had dismissed every servant and shut the doors and windows for a private huddle around the table.

“老太太倒是让她负责管教浩宇，娘担心，有这么个厉害的媳妇管教，恐怕浩宇会好起来，影响到我儿的前程。”
"That bride Haoyu married is no pushover," Madam Bao said. "She even talks back to Old Madam Xia! Third Sister-in-law and I stirred the old madam up until she marched over to make trouble, armed with three solid grievances—and that girl talked her way out of every single one. Wasted the whole trip."

“娘多虑，浩宇那样儿真的变好，也比不上儿子，我可是从十岁起就学着做生意，他十岁时不过是上树掏鸟窝，下河捉螃蟹，没事欺负欺负姑娘家，哪能跟我比？”
"The tougher she is, the better," Chen Haodong said. "I can't wait to see Haoyu suffer under a fierce wife. I'll wake up laughing from my dreams."

陈江树终于忍不住：“他自然比不上浩东，做生意除了积累人脉，就是积累经验，浩宇即使变好，什么都没积累，又怎能与浩东比？把心放在肚子里。”
"Old Madam Xia put her in charge of disciplining Haoyu, though," Madam Bao worried. "What if a wife that tough actually whips him into shape and threatens your future?"

“说得有道理。”父子两个都如此说，包氏自然欢喜。转念一想，又觉不对，“既然我儿如此优秀，又何必担心浩宇继承家业？还费尽心思利用蒋爷唱那出戏？”
"You're worrying over nothing, Mother. Even if Haoyu turns into a saint, he can't hold a candle to me. I started learning business at ten. At ten, he was climbing trees for bird nests, catching crabs in streams, and teasing neighborhood girls. How could he compare?"

陈江树帮儿子解释：“妇道人家就是想得不够长远不够全面，听我好好给你解释。浩东的能力和经验自然比浩宇强，可要继承家业，老太太那一票至关重要。她眼中只有浩宇，浩东什么时候入过她的法眼？为保险一些，才想办法让浩宇快些娶朱家名声不好的姑娘……”
Chen Jiangshu chimed in. "Of course he can't match Haodong. Business takes years of building contacts and judgment. Even if Haoyu reforms, he starts from zero. Put your mind at ease."

陈浩东忍不住打断父亲：“娘，您不知道，陈家选择继承人时，其中有一条规定娶的正室必须家世清白，贤良淑德，品行端正。这一条朱家姑娘只符合家世清白，而贤良淑德，品行端正实在与她不匹配。族中除奶奶以外其他长辈对此十分看重，所以儿子才费尽心思促成浩宇的婚事。”
"True enough." Reassured by father and son, Madam Bao brightened—then paused. "Wait. If my son is that far ahead, why worry about Haoyu inheriting the estate at all? Why go through all that trouble setting up that play with Master Jiang?"

“原来如此！甚好！甚好！你们父子在外筹谋，我在这后宅见机行事，一切都是为我儿子。”包氏这才明白，接着又叮嘱道，“这些事可要瞒着安雅，她平常跟大嫂走得近，我看她也挺喜欢朱家姑娘，敬茶那日，你没见她对待朱家姑娘那个亲热劲儿。”
Chen Jiangshu answered with mild condescension. "Women never think far enough ahead. Listen: Haodong beats Haoyu in skill and experience, yet when it comes time to pick an heir, Old Madam Xia's vote carries huge weight. She only has eyes for Haoyu; when has she ever looked twice at Haodong? To be safe, we had to get Haoyu tied down fast to a Zhu daughter with a ruined name..."

“娘放心，我有分寸。”陈浩东又担忧道，“听说浩宇跟朱家姑娘签下婚前契约，其中有一条：婚后互不干涉，儿子担心朱家姑娘婚后不管浩宇，任由他四处浪荡，儿子精心设计，一半儿功夫会前功尽弃。”
Chen Haodong cut in. "Mother, you don't know—one of the Chen clan's rules for choosing the family heir is that his primary wife must come from an upright family, possess virtuous character, and hold an untarnished name. The Zhu girl only passes the first test. Virtuous and untarnished? Not a chance. Every clan elder aside from Grandmother takes that rule to heart. That's why I went to such lengths to push Haoyu's wedding through."

陈江树说：“不会，我们有杀手锏，实在不行拿出来，到时不怕浩宇不吃亏。”
"So that's why! Brilliant, brilliant! You two scheme outside, and I'll watch for openings inside the house—all for my son." Madam Bao nodded, then added a caution. "Keep every word of this from Anya. She's close with First Sister-in-law, and she seems quite fond of the Zhu girl too. Did you see how warm she was toward her at the tea ceremony?"

陈浩东反应了一会儿，立刻醒悟：“父亲说得对，儿子竟然把这么重要的事情忘记，实在不行时，抖搂出来，估计朱家姑娘气得杀了浩宇的心思都有。”
"Don't worry, Mother, I know my limits," Chen Haodong said, before a frown returned. "Word has it Haoyu signed a prenuptial contract with the Zhu girl saying they won't meddle in each other's lives. I'm worried she'll leave him alone to keep playing around, wasting half my hard work."

包氏不明所以：“你们父子在跟我打哑谜吗？到底是什么？”
"Not a chance," Chen Jiangshu said. "We still have a trump card. If push comes to shove, we play it, and Haoyu won't escape getting burned."

陈浩东在包氏耳边悄悄说了一回，包氏也笑道：“原来是浩宇干的呀！他可真行，不仅给自己招个名声不好的媳妇回来，到头来还要遭罪，真是满盘皆输。”
Chen Haodong paused a beat before lighting up. "You're right, Father! How did I forget something that huge? When the time comes, we spill it, and the Zhu girl will be mad enough to murder Haoyu on the spot."

陈浩东想起财产一事：“想到浩宇名下的财产被朱家姑娘霸占，我着实不爽，等我当家那天，定要把她名下的财产夺回来！”
Madam Bao blinked. "Talking in riddles right in front of me? What is it?"

陈江树并不在乎：“那些财产跟整个陈家的家业比起来不过是九牛一毛，不必当回事。”
Chen Haodong leaned over and whispered in her ear. Madam Bao burst into laughter. "So Haoyu was behind that! What a piece of work—not only did he saddle himself with a disgraced bride, he's going to pay for it too. Total disaster for him."

陈浩东回道：“父亲说得在理。”
A sour thought crossed Chen Haodong's mind. "Thinking of Haoyu's personal properties sitting in the Zhu girl's hands still irks me. The day I take over the family, I'm taking every last one back."

陈江林从钱塘帮回到陈府，将情况向姚轻琰说了一遍。
Chen Jiangshu waved it off. "Compared with the entire Chen fortune, those holdings are a drop in the bucket. Not worth sweating over."

姚轻琰说：“如此看来，字据应该已给浩宇，陈家的产业倒没事，可我难以相信蒋爷会为一个商妓不要陈家的产业？”
"You're right, Father."

陈江林说：“我也不信，其中定有隐情。蒋爷不愿说，也不能逼迫，只要不会威胁到浩宇和陈家，此事也就作罢。”
Returning from the Qiantang Gang, Chen Jianglin reported everything to Yao Qingyan.

姚轻琰点点头：“也对，陈家虽然不能得罪黑帮，但也没必要跟这帮人多往来，就此罢手最好不过。你赶紧去给老太太回一声。”
"So Haoyu got the promissory note back, and the Chen estates are safe," Yao Qingyan mused. "Still, I find it hard to believe Master Jiang gave up the Chen fortune over a courtesan."

“你不与我一同去？”
"I don't buy it either," Chen Jianglin agreed. "There's more to it. Since he won't talk and we can't force him, as long as Haoyu and the family aren't in danger, let's drop it."

“只怕老太太见我跟你在一起会生气，我还是不去了。”
Yao Qingyan nodded. "Fair enough. We shouldn't make enemies of underworld gangs, nor should we get tangled up with them. Better to leave it there. Go report to Old Madam Xia now."

陈江林只好一个人来到老太太的院子。
"Aren't you coming with me?"

天气越来越暖和，老人家仍旧是喜欢晒太阳，坐在院子里看几个丫鬟绣花。见儿子进来，脸上的笑容立刻消失，瞅瞅儿子身边没有带儿媳，故意问：“你不是跟她形影不离吗？怎么一个人过来？”
"I'm afraid seeing us walk in together will only set her off. Better I stay behind."

陈江林老实回道：“本来轻琰要来，怕您生气，就没跟着。”
Chen Jianglin went alone to his mother's courtyard.

老太太一听，更加生气：“你们两个平时嘴上说孝顺，心里不知道把我想成什么人？说什么怕我生气没跟着，我看她就是找借口不愿踏进我的院子！”
With the spring weather turning warm, the old lady still loved soaking up the sun, sitting in the yard watching maids embroider. The second her eldest son stepped in, her smile vanished. Noticing he was alone, she baited him. "Aren't you glued to her hip? Why did you come by yourself?"

老太太一生气，丫鬟们不敢再绣花，放下手中的活计，忙安慰老太太。
"Qingyan meant to come, Mother," Chen Jianglin answered straight. "She stayed back for fear of upsetting you."

“母亲，您别一见我就生气，搞得我都不敢来您的院子。”
That only fanned Old Madam Xia's temper. "Both of you talk a big game about filial piety, yet heaven knows what kind of monster you paint me as in your heads! Afraid of upsetting me? Sounds to me like she's making excuses to stay out of my courtyard!"

老太太叹口气：“唉，只怪我光教你做生意，没教你如何处理家事，如何管教孩子。”
At the old madam's outburst, the maids set aside their needles and rushed to soothe her.

“怎么能怪母亲？都是儿子不好。”
"Mother, please don't get angry the moment you lay eyes on me," Chen Jianglin pleaded. "You make me afraid to set foot in here."

老太太不想继续计较：“你过来有何事？”
Old Madam Xia let out a heavy sigh. "My own fault for teaching you nothing but business, and never teaching you how to run a household or raise a child."

陈江林这才说起正事：“一早儿子到钱塘帮找蒋爷，一番了解下来，确定浩宇和陈家家产都无事，警告过蒋爷之后，便赶回来向母亲汇报。”
"How could it be your fault, Mother? I'm the one at fault."

“你该不会是先向你媳妇汇报后才来向我汇报？”
She waved the subject away. "What brought you here?"

陈江林担心母亲生气，哄道：“儿子当然是先向母亲汇报。”
Only then did Chen Jianglin state his business. "I went to the Qiantang Gang this morning to see Master Jiang. After talking with him, I confirmed Haoyu and the family estates are safe. I gave him a stern warning and came straight back to report to you."

夏老太太听后，更生气，将拐杖在地上敲敲：“你的谎话张嘴就来！刚才还说你媳妇本来要来，怕惹我生气就不来，这会儿又说你先到我这里。你以为我老了，脑子不中用？怎么编瞎话我都会当真？哼！”
"You didn't stop by your wife's room to tell her first?"

陈江林这才意识到自己说的话前后矛盾，立刻跪下：“母亲，儿子不是想骗您，是怕您生气，结果越担心，反而越容易出错，惹得您更生气，您要打要骂都行，儿子绝无怨言。”
Anxious not to provoke her further, Chen Jianglin fibbed, "Of course I came to you first, Mother."

老太太又将拐杖在地上敲两下：“我最讨厌你这种态度，看似态度又好，又乖，又孝顺，其实是逼我！你什么时候才能懂我想要什么？”
Old Madam Xia thumped her cane against the paving stones. "Lies roll right off your tongue! One second ago you said your wife meant to come with you and stayed back to avoid upsetting me, and now you claim you came straight to me! Think I've gone senile and will swallow any fib you spin? Hmph!"

陈江林更加不知所以：“娘想要什么，尽管对儿子讲，儿子无不满足。”
Catching his own slip, Chen Jianglin dropped to his knees. "Mother, I didn't mean to lie. I was so scared of making you angry that I tripped over my own words and made it worse. Beat me or scold me all you want—I won't breathe a word of complaint."

老太太气道：“我让你以后不要老围着你的媳妇转，你能做到吗？”
Old Madam Xia rapped her cane twice more. "That's the exact attitude I can't stand—acting so polite, docile, and dutiful that you box me in! When will you ever understand what I actually want?"

陈江林跪着一言不发。
Chen Jianglin stared up in bewilderment. "Tell me whatever you want, Mother, and I will fulfill it without fail."

老太太又叹口气：“唉，你什么都做不到，还让我说什么？你要是真孝顺，不用我说，就知道我想要什么。行了，我也懒得继续跟你生气，赶紧说完正事离开，省得我看着你生气。”
"Stop orbiting around your wife all day!" she snapped. "Can you do that?"

陈江林跪着说：“儿子总觉得蒋爷跟浩宇赌博另有隐情，蒋爷不说，儿子也不好继续追问，想着既然浩宇和陈家无事，其他的我们也不必计较。”
Chen Jianglin knelt in silence.

老太太的情绪稍微平静些：“肯定有隐情，傻子都能听出来，至于隐情是什么，你多留个心眼儿，说不定哪一日就浮出水面。”
Old Madam Xia sighed again. "You can't do a single thing I ask, so why waste my breath? If you were half as filial as you claim, I wouldn't have to spell it out. Forget it, I'm tired of losing my temper with you. Finish your report and get out of my sight."

“是，儿子谨遵母亲吩咐。”陈江林继续跪着。
Still kneeling, Chen Jianglin spoke. "I suspect there is a hidden story behind Master Jiang's gamble with Haoyu. He refused to talk, and I had no way to press him. Since Haoyu and the estates are safe, I figured we should leave the rest alone."

老太太没好气地说：“起来吧，赶紧离开！”
Her temper cooled a notch. "Any fool can tell there's more behind it. Keep your eyes peeled; the truth will float to the surface sooner or later."

陈江林便起身行礼离开。他自认为没有做错任何事，只是不清楚母亲为何总是见到他和轻琰就生气。在别人看来母亲是嫌弃他对轻琰好，他觉得并非如此。
"Yes, Mother. I will keep watch." He stayed on his knees.

他们兄弟三人娶媳妇时，母亲都会叮嘱要对媳妇好，还说男人只有对自己女人好，才能守住家财。
"Get up and get out!" she barked.

陈家三兄弟再不济，在临安城对老婆好可是出了名的，尤其是他陈江林，简直是将老婆捧在手心里。按说也是照母亲的意思做，她没有必要为此生气。
Chen Jianglin rose, bowed, and took his leave. He could not see what he had done wrong, nor why the sight of him and Qingyan always grated on his mother. Outsiders assumed she resented him doting on his wife, yet he knew that could not be it. When each of the three Chen brothers married, their mother had lectured them to treat their wives well, insisting a man had to cherish his wife to hold onto family wealth. All three Chen brothers were famous across Lin'an for spoiling their wives, Chen Jianglin most of all. He had followed her own rule to the letter; why would she resent him for it?

到底因为什么？陈江林实在想不通，生意上的事她忙起来头头是道，家事总是理不清楚。他自认为自己比两个弟弟做得要好，就是不明白为何母亲总是生他的气，连带着也牵连到轻琰。
Try as he might, he could not puzzle it out. In business he kept every ledger crystal clear, yet family affairs left him lost in a fog. He did better than both his younger brothers, so why did his mother always vent her ire on him and drag Qingyan into it too?

话说新媳妇回门是不能在娘家过夜，千里寻对这个规矩十分不满，可一切拗不过习俗在古人心中的地位，只好跟着陈浩宇打道回府。
Because custom forbade a new bride from spending the night at her maiden home on the return visit—a rule that annoyed Qianli Xun to no end, though ancient tradition left no room to argue—she rode back to Chen Manor with Chen Haoyu before dark.

他们乘坐马车来到陈府大门外时，春香楼的妈妈、三个打手，还有疤子等小弟站在外面等候。
As their carriage halted outside the main gates of Chen Manor, a waiting party loomed ahead: the madam of Chunxiang Brothel, the three hired fighters, and Scarface with his pack of street cronies.

当陈浩宇从马车上下来时，他们全都围了过来……
The moment Chen Haoyu stepped down from the carriage, the whole lot swarmed around him.
