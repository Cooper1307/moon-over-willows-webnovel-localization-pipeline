## Chapter 196: Paying the Shao Escort Agency a Foul-Mooded Visit

Startled to see the Prefect of Lin'an kneeling outside the front gate, the gatekeepers dared not budge him and sprinted for Butler Li, who hurried straight to the main courtyard. "Your Highness, Prefect Zhu is kneeling outside our gate for some reason, and the guards dare not question him!"

Still sitting grimly with the yellow decree in hand, Shen Jianchen called out, "Invite Prefect Zhu inside."

Right as Zhu Xiyan had calculated, Butler Li emerged moments later to summon him. Rising and following him to the prince's quarters, Zhu Xiyan saw Prince Yi holding the imperial scroll with an unreadable face and dropped straight back to his knees. "This humble official Zhu Xiyan begs Your Highness's forgiveness!"

"What offense have you committed, Prefect Zhu?" Shen Jianchen asked evenly.

Bowing his head low, Zhu Xiyan answered, "My daughter caused a grave incident—all due to my poor parenting. I beg Your Highness's pardon!"

Realizing he meant today's raid, Shen Jianchen replied, "Today's commotion was my own doing and involves no one else. Do not blame yourself, Prefect."

A huge breath of relief eased Zhu Xiyan's chest—thank heaven for a fair-minded prince! "Your Highness's graciousness shames me even more. Aside from today's apology, I will return another day with the Chen family to offer formal amends."

Catching the hint that the Chens might try smoothing things over with lavish gifts, Shen Jianchen's face darkened. Could silver measure his heart for Qianli Xun?! "I already said no one else is to blame. If the Chens truly wish to make amends, hand Youqi Jushi back to me! If they cannot, keep out of my sight—looking at them only sets my teeth on edge."

Startled by the sudden chill, Zhu Xiyan pressed his brow down. "Forgive my presumption, Your Highness!"

"Go. I want peace and quiet—do not disturb me."

Bowing out backward, Zhu Xiyan straightened outside the gate, swiped sweat off his brow, and smiled in sheer relief. False alarm! Not only did Prince Yi take full blame without dragging others down, His Majesty's decree had arrived right on cue to calm the storm for both the princess and the Zhu and Chen families. If Zhu Xiyan had felt a brief pang over missing royal in-laws before, that regret died dead today: hearing from escort servants how fragile-looking Princess Jiahe had thrown herself onto a sword proved what a ruthless mistress she would make—marrying Shuzhen into the Chen household had been far wiser.

Hearing from Butler Li that Imperial Attendant Liang had arrived from Bianliang with a decree, Xilan guessed Her Highness's letter had worked and dashed straight into her courtyard shouting, "Good news, Your Highness! Imperial Attendant Liang came!"

Energy surging back through her, Qiao Mengrong sat straight up ignoring her bandage. "Quick, dress me so I can see Imperial Attendant Liang!"

Steadying her, Xilan shook her head. "Butler Li says he delivered the decree and rode right back to Bianliang."

"Gone already?" Disappointment flashed before eagerness took over. "What did the decree say?"

"Need you ask? Advancing the wedding date, of course—Your Highness gets your wish at last!"

Xilan's words worked better than any tonic; pain faded and appetite roared back. "I am starving—bring food!"

"Right away! Lie back a minute while I fetch dinner."

"No, I want to sit up." Yielding, Xilan hurried to the kitchen.

Meanwhile, after wandering Lin'an, Bai Feng had stopped by Chen Manor hoping to see Kuqiao—only for the gatekeeper to neither know Kuqiao nor let any servant out on wedding day, slamming the gate in his face. Looking into inns, fifty copper coins a night felt outrageous when he was pinching every copper to marry Kuqiao, so by dusk he crept back to Youlin Retreat torn between kneeling at the willow or gambling His Highness had forgotten the punishment.

Approaching the prince's yard and hearing fist drills inside, he eased open the gate—only for Prince Yi to spot him at once. "Where did you vanish to all day, Bai Feng?"

*Jackpot—he forgot!* "Your Highness asks me?! You ordered me to kneel beneath the Divine Willow!"

Remembering now, Shen Jianchen threw another crisp punch. "Then why crawl back here? March right back to the willow!"

Kicking himself for opening his big mouth, Bai Feng pleaded, "Spare me, Your Highness! Let me kneel right here in the yard all night—beats the willow!"

With bigger storms roaring in his chest, Shen Jianchen had zero appetite for punishing his guard. Bottled-up rage over the failed raid and the forced decree had nowhere to go; hitting air wasn't cutting it. Dropping his stance, he asked, "In the mood for a real brawl, Bai Feng?"

Grinning wide at being let off the hook, Bai Feng cheered, "Always! Point the target, Your Highness!"

Remembering Lin Jue's report about Jurchen raiders hitting the Shao Escort Agency—something he had sidelined over personal drama—Shen Jianchen decided tonight was perfect for paying them a visit. "Shao Escort Agency."

"I pass their gate all the time!" Bai Feng lit up. "Folks say everyone inside is a blade master—always wanted to test them! Do we slip into black stealth suits and masks and sneak over the wall?"

"What nonsense! Are we sneaking thieves? No masks, no black suits—we walk straight through the front door!"

Heading out, they ran right into Qiao Mengrong leaning on Xilan's arm outside the gate.

"Your wound is fresh—why are you out walking?" Shen Jianchen asked gently.

"It barely hurts—just worried about Brother," Qiao Mengrong murmured. "Where are you heading?"

"Errands," he deflected, keeping the escort agency to himself.

Knowing Zhu Shuzhen had married into the Chen family today, Mengrong breathed easier—at least he wasn't chasing her! "I heard Imperial Attendant Liang came from Bianliang today—what was the decree?"

"His Majesty commanded us back to Bianliang within half a month to complete our wedding," Shen Jianchen answered plainly.

Quiet triumph glowed across both princess and maid. "How eager His Majesty is—shows how deeply he holds Brother in his heart."

Unwilling to linger, Shen Jianchen instructed Xilan, "Help Her Highness back inside to rest and stop her from wandering."

Docile as a lamb now, Qiao Mengrong turned back inside while Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng drove their carriage out into the night toward Shao Escort Agency.

The biggest escort outfit in Lin'an boasted two to three hundred armed guards under Master Shao Zhengye, renowned for his broadsword skill, alongside his seventeen- or eighteen-year-old handsome son Shao Zhize, heir to the family blade. Proud of standing on pure martial grit without sucking up to officials or tycoons—even skipping today's Chen wedding—the Shaos stood aloof from Lin'an's elite circles, happy to be left alone to build their trade.

Pulling up outside their gate, Bai Feng pounded the wood and barked at the guard who opened up, "Go tell your boss my young master is here to challenge Shao Escort Agency!"

Unfazed, the guard hollered inward, "Brothers! Challengers at the door—come greet them!"

Within moments, a dozen broadsword-wielding escorts blocked the entrance. "Looking for a fight?" their leader sneered. "Get past us first!"

More than enough for Bai Feng—Shen Jianchen folded his arms to watch. Eager for action, Bai Feng flashed forward and planted a punch dead on one guard's jaw before anyone blinked, dropping man and blade to the stones. As the rest ringed him with gleaming steel, Bai Feng laughed, "Think bare fists lose to blades? Come all at once!"

As blades slashed inward, Bai Feng vaulted with light-foot skill straight onto the flat of their broadswords, pinning their arms down before unleashing a blur of sweeping kicks that sent men and steel clattering everywhere. "Just you bunch? Save your breath!"

Seeing Prince Yi stroll casually inside the main yard, Bai Feng followed straight into a wider ring of dozens more armed escorts. Outnumbered now, Bai Feng welcomed his prince stepping beside him. Bare fists against flashing steel, both dodged razor edges by a hair and struck crisp non-lethal knockout blows. With every guard dropped, the knot of fury in Shen Jianchen's chest loosened notch by notch until the last man fell and real clarity washed over him: Master Jingguang had been right—follow fate, never force it. Qianli Xun was married now, maybe already in Chen Haoyu's chamber; railing against destiny changed nothing. Accept the hand dealt him, wish her well from afar, and hold his true love quietly in his heart.

Heart settled, time for business.

Watching from the porch throughout the melee, Shao Zhengye and Shao Zhize had noticed two vital clues: first, neither intruder aimed to maim; second, their fist style looked uncannily like Yan Chi's! Were they his allies?

Stepping forward with cupped hands as the last guard groaned on the stones, Shao Zhengye spoke evenly. "We have never met nor crossed paths, Young Master. Why raid our Shao compound?"

"Not raiding—just testing Shao martial skill," Shen Jianchen answered coolly. "Turns out it is nothing special—beats me how you built such a big name in the martial world."

Shao Zhize bristled ready to charge, yet his father raised a hand to stay him. "The rivers and lakes teem with masters; beating us today means someone stronger awaits you tomorrow. We practice blades to guard caravans and keep fit, not chase hollow fame."

A poised answer that took the edge off further taunts. "Smoothly spoken, Master Shao—respect. Enough banter; I came to ask about something serious."

"Ask freely, Young Master."

"I hear a band of Jurchens raided Shao Escort Agency. Where are they now, and why did they come looking for trouble here?"

Shao Zhengye blinked inwardly—so this man wasn't allied with Yan Chi after all! Then how on earth did they share the exact same fist art?!
