# Chapter 24: No Divorce No Surrender

Everyone's reactions made Qianli Xun realize she had played her hand too aggressively. It was only because she wanted to leave Lin Jue so desperately. She'd never even had a boyfriend. Why should some cosmic accident dump her into a marriage with a man she couldn't stand?

She had been harming herself since last night. She'd come early this morning, laid her heart bare, even had the Old Ancestor deploy *The Heartbreak Riddle*. She'd begged Lin Jue to write a divorce letter. Nothing worked.

Time for a scorched-earth play. If she couldn't win cleanly, she'd burn it all down—starting with her own reputation.

Matchmaker Yan was the first to break the silence. "Young Madam, how can you say such a thing about yourself?! A woman's reputation is everything! Yes, the Military Training Officer said something—but he was guessing. Listening to gossip. No one caught you in the act. Not one person! And I guarantee every elder here knows better than to take his word as truth. If *this* is grounds for a divorce letter, then I, Yan, will be the first to object!"

Qianli Xun knew the woman meant well, only wanting to clear her name. But she felt zero gratitude—Matchmaker Yan's "kindness" had just slammed the final door to her freedom shut.

The Old Ancestor resumed rolling her beads. "My granddaughter has gone so far as to brand *herself* unchaste. She's thrown her reputation to the wind just to escape this marriage. Does that not tell you everything? Why must the Lin family—and you, young master—still drag this out?"

A Lin family elder advised, "Nephew, perhaps an amicable divorce *is* for the best. She is clearly determined to leave. What is the point of forcing her to stay?"

Lin Jue was not ready to concede. He stood up and bowed deeply to the room. "Old Ancestor, Uncles, Brothers—I will neither agree to a divorce nor will I cast Shuzhen out. My reasons are two-fold: First, I still harbor deep feelings for her and wish to make this marriage work. Second, while Shuzhen may not care for the Zhu family's face, I must. My father-in-law prizes the Zhu family's honor above all else; I cannot and will not fail him."

He then turned directly to the Old Ancestor. "You are a Zhu, Old Ancestor. You, of all people, should be protecting your family's honor. Please, help me persuade Shuzhen to stay."

The Old Ancestor let out a long, weary sigh. She said nothing, her fingers continuing their rhythmic dance over the beads.

Hearing that sigh, Qianli Xun felt a cold pit form in her stomach. The Old Ancestor was helpless. *Is this really not going to happen?*

Another elder barked, "Niece-in-law, since you've voluntarily claimed a violation of the 'Seven Grounds,' you've admitted to lacking wifely virtue! In that case, don't you dare complain about Lin Jue hitting you. If you ask me, he was right to do it! You were at fault first. Whether he divorces you or keeps you is entirely up to him now!"

"Seventh Brother is wrong," another elder cut in. "In our Great Song Dynasty, regardless of who is at fault, divorce has always been the husband's call. Since Lin Jue is generous enough not to hold her past against her and is looking out for the Zhu family, I say this matter is closed."

Qianli Xun felt a wave of bitter regret. She had been too rash in claiming "unchastity," underestimating the deep-seated misogyny of this era. She was now in a much worse position than before.

She finally saw through it. Lin Jue hadn't suggested the divorce out of goodwill. He'd set the stage for a public humiliation—let the elders lecture her, let the "facts" slap her back to reality. Was this revenge for the punch? For locking him up? The man was a petty, small-minded snake.

The more she thought, the hotter her blood boiled. She stood up, marched right up to Lin Jue, and stared him dead in the eyes. Not a word.

It was the first time Lin Jue had ever been stared down so closely by his wife. *She's different now*, he thought. *There's still a chance for us.* How could he let her go so easily? He offered her a smug, knowing smile and sat back down, triumphant.

Qianli Xun retracted her gaze. Divorce or not, she had to clear her name before this crowd.

"Old Ancestor, Elders—please disregard my earlier words. I only spoke such nonsense because I was desperate for freedom. Yesterday, when I returned from a poetry gathering, Lin Jue suspected me. I told him then that the next time I was invited, I would take him with me so he could see for himself how I interact with the scholars. You can ask my maid, Kuqiao. She is my shadow; she knows exactly what happens at those gatherings. The rumors you hear in the streets are nothing but malicious slander!"

Kuqiao stepped forward, her voice trembling but brave. "I can testify to that! Every time my Miss attends a gathering, she does nothing but recite poetry and appreciate the work of others. She has never once overstepped her bounds!"

Third Uncle chimed in again, his voice oily. "Niece-in-law, a woman's place is in the home, caring for her husband. I advise you to stay away from those 'scholars.' Spending too much time with those men will always lead to filthy rumors. You should be grateful Lin Jue is so magnanimous. If you were my wife, I'd have locked you up straight away to keep you from wandering!"

The Old Ancestor could no longer remain silent. "If a daughter of the Zhu family is to be treated like a prisoner and abused, I see no reason for this marriage to continue. Divorce them."

Qianli Xun seized the opening. "He may not have locked me in yet, but his actions are already a form of torture! Third Uncle, Elders, listen to the Old Ancestor! Let him divorce me!"

"Calling it torture is a gross exaggeration!" Lin Jue countered instantly. "If anything, *you* were the one torturing *me*. You refused to consummate our marriage on our wedding night—is that not mistreatment? Three days ago, you beat me and tied me up like a common criminal—is that not mistreatment?!"

Qianli Xun froze. She hadn't expected the brute to actually air his dirty laundry in public. The tide of the battle had turned completely against her.

Third Uncle looked outraged. "Niece-in-law! You are entirely in the wrong! It's one thing to deny him the marriage bed—he could have just taken a concubine—but to *strike* him? To lock him up? You have single-handedly tarnished your reputation as a refined lady! But Nephew... you are a Military Training Officer. You have plenty of strength. How on earth did you let a woman hit you?!"

Lin Jue didn't want to admit she was a different person, and he certainly didn't want to lose face. "I was merely indulging her," he lied smoothly. "I didn't want to stoop to her level."

Third Uncle turned to the Old Ancestor. "Old Ancestor, do you truly think a divorce is still appropriate given these facts?"

The Old Ancestor stopped her prayer beads and stood up. "I believe it is time for me to leave."

Qianli Xun cried out. "Old Ancestor, wait! Let me finish!"

The Old Ancestor paused. She didn't leave immediately, granting her granddaughter one last shred of face.

Qianli Xun decided to go for broke. She was going to reveal the impossible truth. "The truth is, I am not the real Zhu Shuzhen! The original Zhu Shuzhen was beaten to death by Lin Jue! I am a soul from a far more advanced era named Qianli Xun, and I am a master of the Qianli Fist! If you don't believe me, watch!"

She exploded into a flurry of movements, her fists blurring in a standard, high-level routine. With a final, explosive shout, she drove her fist into the heavy wooden chair next to Matchmaker Yan. 
*CRACK!* 
The chair split perfectly in two, crashing to the floor. The room erupted in gasps of terror.

Kuqiao was used to it. She only worried about her miss in her heart. The Old Ancestor, however, remained as still as a mountain, her fingers resuming their slow dance over the beads.

Qianli Xun's final strike whistled through the air, stopping a fraction of an inch from Lin Jue's face. The sheer force of the wind forced him to screw his eyes shut and recoil in fear.

"Well? Scared? If you're scared, tell everyone that Zhu Shuzhen was beaten to death by you!"

The displaced air washed over his face—and with it, a faint, warm scent. Her scent. He inhaled before he could stop himself. When he finally opened his eyes, he saw her small, white fist clenched tight before him. He stared. Forgot to blink. Forgot to breathe.

Qianli Xun felt a wave of disgust at the strange look in his eyes and yanked her hand back. "What are you staring at? Confess your crimes!"

Lin Jue snapped out of his daze and spun a lie—half truth, half fiction—without missing a beat. "My wife has been secretly practicing martial arts; I only discovered this recently. I always thought she was just a poet, but it turns out she's a kindred spirit! I'm sure we'll have much more to talk about from now on. Shuzhen, stop this nonsense. And stop saying you aren't yourself—that's enough."

The room breathed a collective sigh of relief.

"Sister-in-law," Lin Zixiao added, "you'll say anything to get a divorce, won't you? First you ruin your own reputation, now you try to frame my brother for murder? You've looked exactly like this since the day you entered our home. Who would believe such a ghost story? And since you're clearly a martial arts master, there's no way my brother could have beaten you. I don't believe a word of it!"

"Neither do we!" the other elders echoed.

Qianli Xun could only call out, "Kuqiao!"

Kuqiao stepped into the center of the room. "My Miss never knew a single move of martial arts before! She was always the one being beaten! Three days ago, the young master threw her against a stone pillar—the wound on her forehead is still there for all to see! She was unconscious for hours! When she finally woke up, she was like this. In the countryside, we call this a rebirth of the soul!"

Lin Jue threw his head back and laughed. "Kuqiao is *your* maid; she'll recite whatever script you give her. Enough, Wife. The Old Ancestor and the elders are busy. Let them go home."

Qianli Xun had never felt so utterly defeated. She wanted to use her fists to beat a "yes" out of him, but she knew that in this room, violence would only prove his point. She turned a desperate gaze to the Old Ancestor. "Old Ancestor..."

The Old Ancestor stood up fully now, her gaze fixed calmly on the tip of her own nose. "The world is vast, and many things are beyond human understanding. It is only natural that the ignorant refuse to believe in rebirth. I never met the previous girl, only the one before me now, so I cannot judge. Today... I am powerless to grant this divorce. Such is the world we live in, where a woman's destiny is never her own to decide."

With that, the Old Ancestor turned and walked out, followed by Matron Xiuzhen.

The Lin family and Matchmaker Yan all bowed low. "Farewell, Old Ancestor!"

Qianli Xun watched her go, feeling as though her very life was slipping away. *Am I really stuck with him? Is my story over before it even began?*

In the silence, she saw the silhouette of Shen Jianchen in her mind. If only she'd said yes to him back then. A real romance. A real choice. Instead of this.

Matchmaker Yan cleared her throat, breaking the silence. "Young Madam, I have to say... he's willing to stay with you even after you tied him up and struck him? You should be grateful! Since there's no divorce, I'm leaving. I have actual weddings to arrange. You two can settle the rest behind closed doors."

Once the Matchmaker and the elders had dispersed, only Qianli Xun, Lin Jue, and Kuqiao remained in the cavernous hall.

Qianli Xun turned to Lin Jue, her voice vibrating with cold fury. "Lin Jue! Don't you dare think for one second that you've won! I will never be a 'wife' to you!

"Hear me now: You are forbidden from ever stepping foot in the Zhulan Residence again! I will do exactly what I want, when I want, and you will not interfere. We live in the same courtyard, but we are strangers. Stay in your lane and stay out of mine, or I promise you will bear the full, agonizing consequences! I have my own dowry and my own money—I don't need a copper from you to survive. From now on, you'd best stay as far away from me as possible!"

Lin Jue snapped, his face turning a deep, angry purple. "Zhu Shuzhen! You've gone too—!"

Seeing him explode only made Qianli Xun feel better. "This is your karma for refusing the divorce! Did you think keeping me here would make me some submissive little wife you could toy with? Don't think I'll turn into some clinging, obedient woman just because you refused to let me go! Hmph! Mark my words, Lin Jue—I am Qianli Xun, and I am not the woman you used to bully!"

Without waiting for a response, Qianli Xun swept out of the hall. Kuqiao shot a final, sharp glare at the young master before scurrying after her mistress.

Lin Jue sat alone in the empty hall. He'd won. So why did it feel like losing?
