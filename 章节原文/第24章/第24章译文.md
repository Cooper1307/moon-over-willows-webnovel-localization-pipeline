# Chapter 24: No Divorce No Surrender

Judging by every horrified face in the room, Qianli Xun knew she had swung way too hard—she just wanted out of Lin Jue's clutches so bad! She hadn't even tasted romance once in thirty years—why spend her transmigration chained to a man she couldn't stand?

She had burned her own forehead, argued herself hoarse, brought out the Old Ancestor and "The Heartbreak Riddle," even begged for repudiation—nothing budged them. Fight to survive from the brink of ruin: if trashing her reputation bought freedom, so be it!

Matchmaker Yan jumped in first: "Young Madam, how can you slander yourself?! A woman's good name is everything! Even if the Training Officer made accusations, that's just idle guesswork and gossip—nobody caught you in bed with anyone! Not a single elder here takes his words as truth. Using that as grounds to cast you out—I, Matchmaker Yan, will be the first to block it!"

Qianli Xun knew the matchmaker meant well trying to protect her honor—yet she couldn't feel an ounce of thanks when the woman had just slammed her last exit shut.

Resuming her beads, the Old Ancestor said, "My Zhu granddaughter has gone so far as to throw away her own reputation and claim 'adultery' just to break free. Plain as day she cannot bear living with Son-in-law. Why do you Lin elders insist on forcing this marriage?"

One Lin uncle softened: "Nephew Lin Jue, grant the mutual divorce. Clearly her heart is set on leaving—what joy is there dragging it out?"

Refusing to fold, Lin Jue stood and bowed to the room. "Old Ancestor, uncles, brothers—I will neither agree to mutual divorce nor cast Shuzhen out! Two reasons: first, I still love her and want a life with her; second, if Shuzhen won't guard Zhu family honor, I will! Father-in-law prizes the Zhu name above all—I cannot let him down."

Turning directly to the Old Ancestor, he added, "Old Ancestor, you are of the Zhu clan—guard your family's face and persuade Shuzhen to stay."

The Old Ancestor let out a quiet sigh and rolled her beads in silence.

Hearing helplessness in that sigh, Qianli Xun's stomach tightened—was the divorce really slipping away?

Another Lin elder spoke sharp: "Nephew's wife, claiming one of the Seven Conditions admits you failed wifely virtue! No wonder Lin Jue hit you—sounds like you earned it! With you at fault, whether to divorce or keep you is Lin Jue's call alone!"

Another added, "Seventh Brother misses the point—fault or no fault, in our dynasty the husband always decides divorce! Since Lin Jue doesn't hold it against her and protects Zhu family face, this discussion is over!"

Regret stung Qianli Xun—claiming one of the Seven Conditions had backfired hard! She didn't understand this era's rules, and now the room had flipped completely against her.

It dawned on her at last: Lin Jue hadn't suggested mutual divorce out of mercy—he had set a trap to lecture her in public and crush her hopes! Still sore over that black eye and spending the night tied up? Petty, vengeful jerk!

Boiling over, Qianli Xun stood, marched right up to Lin Jue, and glared straight into his eyes without a word.

Never had his wife stood this close staring straight at him—this fiery new wife gave him fresh hope, making it even harder to let her go. He met her glare with a calm smile and sat back down.

Pulling her gaze off him, Qianli Xun figured divorce or not, better clear her name first.

"Old Ancestor, Lin elders—forget what I just said. I only blurted it out desperate for a divorce! When I got back from yesterday's poetry salon and Lin Jue accused me, I offered to bring him along next time so he could watch with his own eyes and clear my name! Ask my maid Kuqiao—she attends every single salon with me and knows every word spoken. Every rumor outside is pure slander!"

Kuqiao stepped forward at once: "I swear it! Every salon Miss attends, all they do is write verse and discuss poetry—never once an improper act!"

Third Uncle piped up again: "Nephew's wife, a woman's duty is tending her husband at home! Quit attending salons and mingling with scholars—keep that up and gossip's bound to follow and ruin your name! Lucky Lin Jue's patient—were it me, I'd lock you inside the house and see you try stepping out!"

At that, the Old Ancestor broke her silence: "If a daughter of the Zhu house faces mistreatment like that under a Lin roof, this marriage cannot stand. Grant the divorce!"

Seizing the opening, Qianli Xun cried, "Lin Jue didn't lock me in, yet weren't his beatings and courtesans mistreatment?! Third Uncle, Lin elders—heed the Old Ancestor and let him divorce me!"

Not about to let the tide turn back, Lin Jue shot back: "Calling that mistreatment stretches the truth! She mistreated me first! Refusing to consummate our marriage on our wedding night—isn't that mistreatment? Beating me three days ago and trussing me up in a locked room—isn't that mistreatment?!"

Qianli Xun froze—never dreamed he'd air that dirty laundry in front of the whole clan! Game over—the divorce battle was lost.

Third Uncle bristled: "Nephew's wife, plain talk—every bit of this is on you! Refuse his bed? Fine, leave you as primary wife and let him take a concubine. Yet raising fists to your husband and locking him up?! Disgracing the name of a talented lady! Wait—Lin Jue, you're a Training Officer, strong as a bull—how'd you let a woman beat you?"

Loath to bring up her strange transformation or lose face as a man, Lin Jue shrugged: "I held back—refused to stoop to fighting my wife."

Third Uncle turned right to the Old Ancestor: "Old Ancestor—can this marriage still be dissolved?"

The Old Ancestor stopped her beads. "I should take my leave."

"Wait, Old Ancestor—hear me out!" Qianli Xun cried.

Granting her granddaughter's plea, the Old Ancestor stayed seated.

Odds stacked against her, Qianli Xun risked one final Hail Mary—spilling everything: "Truth is, I'm not the real Zhu Shuzhen! Lin Jue beat the old Zhu Shuzhen to death! I'm Qianli Xun from a far more advanced era, master of Qianli Fist! Don't believe me? Watch!"

Stepping into the center, Qianli Xun launched into a crisp, seamless martial form—ending with a thunderous punch into the empty chair beside Matchmaker Yan! Wood splintered clean in two and crashed to the floor, shocking every soul in the room.

Unfazed by now, Kuqiao just worried sick for her mistress.

The Old Ancestor rolled her beads without flinching.

On the final beat of her form, Qianli Xun drove a punch straight at Lin Jue's face—stopping a hair's breadth from his nose!

The sheer gust slammed his eyes shut and snapped his head back.

"Well? Scared now? If you're scared, confess to everyone you beat Zhu Shuzhen to death!"

The breeze from her fist brushed his cheeks, carrying a subtle, clean fragrance of skin. Drawing an involuntary breath, Lin Jue felt his anger melt away; opening his eyes to behold her slender white fist poised inches from his face, he went completely starstruck.

Creeped out by his weird, dreamy stare, Qianli Xun yanked her fist back. "What are you gawking at? Confess your crime in front of everyone!"

Snapping back to earth, Lin Jue spun a half-truth on the spot: "I only learned recently my wife practiced martial arts in secret! Always thought she only wrote poems—turns out we share martial pursuits too! Surely we'll have more in common from now on. Quit acting wild and stop saying you aren't Shuzhen."

A collective sigh of relief swept the Lin elders.

Lin Jue's second brother shook his head: "Sister-in-law, you'll say anything for a divorce—trashing your own name and slandering Third Brother too! You look identical to the day you stepped through our doors—who'd swallow tales of another soul? And with fists like yours, Third Brother isn't even your match—no way he ever beat you!"

A chorus of elders chimed in: "Exactly! Nobody buys it!"

Desperate, Qianli Xun called out: "Kuqiao!"

Stepping forward, Kuqiao pleaded, "My Miss never knew a lick of martial arts before—only took Son-in-law's beatings! Three days ago he hit her again and shoved her into a pillar—look at the cut still on her forehead! She fainted dead away, wouldn't wake up for anything, and when she finally opened her eyes she turned into this and knew martial arts! Country folk call it rebirth!"

Lin Jue barked a loud laugh. "Haha! Kuqiao's your own maid—she'll recite whatever script you feed her! Give it a rest, Wife. The Old Ancestor and our elders are busy folks—let's not hold them up!"

For the first time in her life, Qianli Xun hit a brick wall. Part of her longed to beat Lin Jue until he signed—yet fists couldn't unlock this mess. Helpless, she turned to her sole ally: "Old Ancestor..."

Rising to her feet, gaze resting serene upon the floor ahead, the Old Ancestor murmured: "The world is vast and holds every marvel, yet most mortals stay blind—no surprise nobody accepts rebirth. Never having met the old Zhu daughter, knowing only you before me, I cannot tell one from the other for these elders. Today I am powerless—such is a world where women hold no say over their own fate."

With Matron Xiuzhen beside her, the Old Ancestor walked out of the reception hall.

Every Lin elder and Matchmaker Yan rose to bow: "Farewell, Old Ancestor!"

Watching her retreating silhouette, hollow defeat washed over Qianli Xun. Was she really trapped with Lin Jue forever? Was her life over before it began?

Shen Jianchen's image flashed in her mind. Had she known this awaited her, she should have said yes to him and lived out a whirlwind romance—maybe she wouldn't feel this bleak now.

Once the Old Ancestor was gone, Matchmaker Yan offered parting advice: "Young Madam, word to the wise—you beat the Training Officer, tied him up, locked him away, and he still wants to stay married! Count your blessings! Since there's no divorce, I'm off—got several matches waiting on me! Sort the rest behind closed doors."

Once the matchmaker left, Third Uncle waved a hand: "Everyone disperse!"

One by one the clan filed out, leaving only Qianli Xun, Lin Jue, and Kuqiao in the hall.

Rounding on Lin Jue with blazing eyes, Qianli Xun declared: "Don't think you won and I'm playing obedient wife, Lin Jue! Mark my words right here: never set half a foot inside Zhulan Residence! I go where I want, do what I want—don't dare meddle! We share the West Courtyard, so you stay in your lane and stay out of mine, or you'll regret it! My Zhu dowry keeps me rich—I don't need a coin from you! See me coming, walk the other way!"

Furious, Lin Jue sprang up. "Zhu Shuzhen, you go too far...!"

His rage lit a wicked grin on her face. "You refused the divorce—reap what you sow! Think holding me here turns me into a timid little bird obeying your every whim? Hmph! Remember this: I, Qianli Xun, am NOT the old Zhu Shuzhen you used to push around!"

Spinning on her heel, she strode out; Kuqiao shot Son-in-law a fierce glare and hurried after her mistress.

Left alone to savor his "victory," Lin Jue felt a strange emptiness—funny, he didn't feel victorious at all.
