# Chapter 24: Divorce Fails, Bold Words Emerge

Everyone's reactions made Qianli Xun realize she had spoken too harshly. It was only because she wanted to leave Lin Jue so desperately. She hadn't even tasted the flavor of romance. Why should she have to live a married life with someone she had no interest in just because she transmigrated here?

She had been harming herself since last night. She came early this morning and expressed everything she needed to. The Old Ancestor even brought out "The Heartbreak Riddle." She even asked Lin Jue to write a divorce letter. Nothing worked.

She might as well try a desperate move—fight for survival with her back to the wall. At this moment, she only wanted to gain freedom by destroying her reputation.

Matchmaker Yan spoke first. "Young Madam, how can you speak of yourself like this? A woman's reputation is most important. Even if the Military Training Officer said it, he was only guessing or listening to others' nonsense. No one caught you in the act. I believe every elder present would never take the Military Training Officer's words seriously. If this is the reason to let the Military Training Officer repudiate you, I, Yan the Matchmaker, would be the first to disagree!"

Qianli Xun knew Matchmaker Yan meant well, only wanting to clear her name. But she couldn't feel grateful at all, because Matchmaker Yan's words had once again blocked the door to her freedom.

The Old Ancestor started fingering her prayer beads again. As she fingered them, she said, "My Zhu family granddaughter has spoken to this extent. She doesn't even care about her personal reputation, insisting on taking the 'promiscuity' label. This shows enough that she doesn't want to live with the young master. Why do the Lin family elders and the young master still insist on maintaining this marriage?"

A Lin family elder advised, "Lin Jue, nephew, why not agree to the divorce? It seems your wife is determined to leave you. What's the point of continuing to live with her?"

Lin Jue still didn't want to give up easily. He stood up and bowed to the elders. "Old Ancestor, uncles, Big Brother, Second Brother, I neither agree to the divorce nor will I repudiate Shuzhen. There are two reasons: First, I still have feelings for her and want to continue living with her. Second, Shuzhen doesn't care about the Zhu family's face, but I do. Because Father-in-law values the Zhu family's reputation, I can't disappoint him."

Lin Jue faced the Old Ancestor again. "Old Ancestor, you're from the Zhu family. You should also care about the Zhu family's face. Help me persuade Shuzhen."

The Old Ancestor sighed and said nothing, continuing to finger her prayer beads.

Qianli Xun sensed the old lady's helplessness from her sigh. She became more nervous. Could it be that this divorce wouldn't work?

Another Lin family elder spoke. "Niece-in-law, since you've voluntarily claimed one of the 'Seven Grounds for Divorce,' you're obviously admitting you've lost your virtue as a wife. In that case, don't blame Lin Jue for beating you. I think he beat you quite right! You were at fault first. Whether it's divorce or repudiation, it should be up to Lin Jue."

Someone among the Lin family elders continued, "Seventh Brother is wrong. Whether the niece-in-law is at fault or not, in our dynasty, divorce has always been decided by the husband. Since Lin Jue doesn't mind his wife and can consider the Zhu family's perspective, I think the divorce matter should end here."

Qianli Xun began to regret her rash decision to claim one of the "Seven Grounds." She could only blame herself for not understanding this era. From the current situation, the overall position was very unfavorable to her.

She finally figured it out. Lin Jue's idea of divorce wasn't out of kindness. He wanted her to be lectured by everyone in person, using facts to give her a wake-up call. Was he holding a grudge for that punch? And for locking him up for half a day and a night? This person was truly narrow-minded, seeking revenge for the smallest grievance.

The more Qianli Xun thought about it, the angrier she became. She stood up, walked to Lin Jue's face, stared at him, and said nothing.

This was the first time Lin Jue had been stared at so closely by his wife. The different wife made him feel there was still hope between them. How could he easily let her go with divorce? He smiled at Zhu Shuzhen and sat back in his seat.

Qianli Xun withdrew her gaze. Regardless of whether today's divorce would happen, she should first prove her innocence.

"Old Ancestor, Lin family elders, please don't take what I just said seriously. I just wanted a divorce so badly that I spoke nonsense.

"Yesterday I was invited to a poetry gathering. When I came back, Lin Jue suspected me. I told him next time I attend a poetry gathering, I'll take him along so he can see with his own eyes how I interact with the talents and prove my innocence.

"You can also ask my maid Kubiao. She follows me every time. She knows best what I do at the poetry gatherings. As for the nonsense people outside are spreading, it's pure slander!"

Kubiao took the initiative to clear her miss's name. "I can testify. Every time my miss attends a poetry gathering, she either recites poetry or appreciates others' work. She has never overstepped."

Third Uncle spoke again. "Niece-in-law, what's most important for a woman is to take good care of her man at home. Third Uncle advises you not to attend any more poetry gatherings in the future. Gathering with those literary talents, over time, all kinds of rumors will spread, affecting your reputation. It's also that Lin Jue is magnanimous. If it were me, I'd lock you at home and see if you'd dare go out!"

Hearing this, the Old Ancestor didn't want to remain silent. "If the Zhu family girl is treated so badly in the Lin family, I don't think there's any need to continue living like this. Better divorce."

Qianli Xun seized the opportunity. "Although Lin Jue didn't lock me at home, aren't the things he did also mistreating me? Third Uncle, Lin family elders, listen to the Old Ancestor and let Lin Jue divorce me."

How could Lin Jue let the favorable situation be reversed again? He immediately said, "Saying I mistreated you is really an exaggeration. It should be you mistreating me first. On our wedding night, you refused to consummate the marriage. Isn't that mistreatment? Three days ago, not only did you beat me, but you also tied me up and locked me away. Isn't that mistreatment?"

Qianli Xun never expected Lin Jue to bring this up. Now that things had reached this point, the divorce battle was sure to be lost.

Third Uncle was very angry. "Niece-in-law, it's not that Third Uncle is criticizing you, but you're all wrong. Not consummating the marriage is fine. At most, he could keep you at home and take a concubine. But you? How could you beat him? And lock him up? You've truly tarnished the reputation of a talented woman! But, Lin Jue, nephew, you're the Military Training Officer. You have considerable strength. How could you be beaten by a woman?"

Lin Jue didn't want to mention that Zhu Shuzhen was different from before, nor did he want to lose a man's face. "I was letting her win. I didn't want to stoop to her level."

Third Uncle immediately asked the Old Ancestor for instructions. "Old Ancestor, do you think this marriage can still be divorced?"

The Old Ancestor stopped fingering her prayer beads. "I might as well leave."

Qianli Xun stopped her. "Old Ancestor, wait. Let me finish."

The Old Ancestor didn't stand up. She still had to give her granddaughter some face.

Although the favorable situation was no longer on Qianli Xun's side, she wanted to try once more. She decided to reveal everything. "Actually, I'm not the real Zhu Shuzhen. The previous Zhu Shuzhen was beaten to death by Lin Jue. I come from another, more advanced era. My name is Qianli Xun, and I know the Qianli Fist. If you don't believe me, I'll perform it for everyone here."

After speaking, Qianli Xun performed a set of fist techniques. Her movements were standard and fluid. Then she punched a chair next to Matchmaker Yan. The chair split in two and fell to the ground, once again shocking everyone present.

Kubiao was used to it. She only worried about her miss in her heart.

The Old Ancestor still fingered her prayer beads, unmoved.

When Qianli Xun threw the last punch, she attacked directly at Lin Jue. The fist didn't actually hit Lin Jue's face but stopped right in front of him.

The force of the attack made Lin Jue unable to keep his eyes open. He quickly closed them and ducked his head back.

"Well? Scared? If you're scared, tell everyone that Zhu Shuzhen was beaten to death by you!"

The force of the punch brought a gust of air, brushing past Lin Jue's cheeks and features. A mellow fragrance of skin hit his face. Lin Jue took a deep breath and was immersed for a moment. Only when the inner arrogance was extinguished did he open his eyes. He saw Zhu Shuzhen's jade fist clenched tight, her skin delicate and fair, emitting fragrance. He was actually utterly mesmerized.

Seeing Lin Jue's strange expression and gaze, Qianli Xun felt very uncomfortable. She quickly withdrew her fist. "What are you dazed for? Hurry up and confess your crimes in public!"

When Zhu Shuzhen withdrew her fist, Lin Jue instantly became sober. He half-truthfully, half-falsely made up a story. "Wife has been secretly practicing fist techniques. I only recently found out. I always thought you only knew how to write poetry. I didn't expect you to be a kindred spirit. I believe in the future we'll have more in common. Stop making trouble. And don't say you're not Shuzhen or anything like that again."

Lin Jue's words relieved everyone around.

Lin Jue's second brother said, "Sister-in-law, for the sake of divorce, you dare to say anything. Not only do you ruin your own reputation, but you also ruin my third brother's reputation. Since you entered the Lin family, you've looked like this. Not a bit has changed. If you say you're someone else, who would dare believe it? Since you've secretly practiced powerful fist techniques, third brother is no match for you. I really don't believe he could beat you."

Other Lin family elders also discussed, "Yes! We all don't believe it!"

Qianli Xun could only call out, "Kubiao!"

Kubiao stepped forward and said to everyone, "My miss didn't know any fist techniques originally. Only the young master could beat her. Three days ago, the young master beat miss again. He pushed miss to a pillar. Her forehead was injured. The wound is still there. Everyone can see it. Because of this injury, miss fainted and wouldn't wake up no matter how we called her. Later she finally woke up, and she became like this, and also knew fist techniques. Don't you think it's strange? In our countryside, we call this rebirth."

Lin Jue laughed loudly. "Haha...! Kubiao is your maid. You want her to say whatever you tell her. Wife, calm down. The Old Ancestor and Lin family elders are all very busy. Hurry up and let them go back!"

This was the first time Qianli Xun encountered such a frustrating situation. She really wanted to beat Lin Jue until he obediently agreed to divorce her. But judging from the current situation, fists couldn't solve the problem at all. What should she do? She could only appeal to the Old Ancestor. "Old Ancestor."

The Old Ancestor stood up, her gaze resting on the tip of her nose. "The world is vast, and there are many strange things. But most people are ignorant. It's normal that the theory of rebirth isn't recognized. I haven't seen the previous Zhu family girl, only the one before me now. I can't distinguish for you and everyone. Today I'm powerless to help with the divorce. Who lets women not be able to make their own decisions in this era."

The Old Ancestor walked out of the reception hall with Nanny Xiu Zhen.

The Lin family elders and Matchmaker Yan all stood up. "Respectfully see off the Old Ancestor!"

Qianli Xun watched the Old Ancestor's back, feeling a sense of loss. Could it be that she must continue living with Lin Jue? Could it be that her life would end like this?

At this moment, Shen Jianchen's figure appeared in her mind. If she had known these things would happen, she might as well have accepted him and had a passionate romance with him. Perhaps she wouldn't feel so lost and uncomfortable right now.

Seeing the Old Ancestor had gone far away, Matchmaker Yan advised, "Young Madam, I have to say a few words to you. You beat the Military Training Officer, tied him up, and locked him away. He's still willing to continue living with you. Be content! Since divorce isn't possible, I'd better leave quickly. There are several marriages waiting for me to handle. I'm busy! The rest of you can handle it behind closed doors as a family."

After Matchmaker Yan left, the Lin family elders had nothing more to say. Third Uncle said, "Everyone, disperse!"

The remaining people also left. The reception hall was left with Qianli Xun, Lin Jue, and Kubiao.

Qianli Xun faced Lin Jue and said sternly, "Lin Jue! Don't think you've won! I won't live my life well!

"Today I'm leaving these words here. In the future, you're not allowed to set foot in the Zhulan Residence! I'll do whatever I want, and you're not allowed to interfere.

"You and I live in the west courtyard of the Lin residence. Keep your well water from touching my river water. Otherwise, you'll bear the full consequences!

"Also, the dowry from the Zhu family is enough for me to use. I don't need you to support me. In the future, when you see me, you'd better stay far away!"

Lin Jue was furious. He stood up and shouted angrily, "Zhu Shuzhen! You've gone too far!..."

Seeing Lin Jue angry, Qianli Xun was even happier. "Who told you not to agree to divorce? This is your karma! Don't think that because you won't let me go, I'll be like a little bird resting on someone, at your mercy. Hmph! Remember, I, Qianli Xun, am not the Zhu Shuzhen who used to be bullied by you!"

After speaking, Qianli Xun left with long strides. Kubiao glared at the young master and quickly followed her miss.

Leaving Lin Jue alone to enjoy the joy of victory. That seemed wrong. He couldn't feel happy at all right now.
