这一章是女主阶段性的“挫败”，但同时也为后续的“分居/互不干涉”设定了爽点。你很好地处理了林家长辈那种令人血压升高的“受害者有罪论”，以及女主最后霸气外露的通牒。

针对你的审校需求，以下是具体的优化建议：

### 1. 致命的拼写错误（必须修正！）
*   **纠错**：本章中，丫鬟苦荞的名字依然被大量错误拼写成了 **Kubiao**（P2, P3, P18, P20, P40, P54, P56）。在英文网文界，角色名字的一致性是专业度的底线，请务必修正为 **Kuqiao**。

### 2. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 24: Divorce Fails, Bold Words Emerge*
*   **点评**：稍微有些平淡，像是在做总结陈词。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 24: A Failed Divorce and a Fierce Ultimatum**（失败的离婚与强硬的最后通牒）或者 **Chapter 24: If I Can’t Leave, I’ll Make Your Life Hell**（如果我走不了，我就让你生活在地域里）。后者更具网文的张力和“爽感”。

### 3. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P1 “说猛了”的口语化**：`realize she had spoken too harshly.`
    *   **优化**：“说猛了”在中文里带有一种“用力过猛、玩脱了”的感觉。
    *   **修改**：`realized she had played her hand too aggressively.` (玩脱了/操之过急)。
*   **P3 经典成语的翻译（置之死地而后生）**：`fight for survival with her back to the wall.`
    *   **点评**：**翻译得不错！** 如果想更具文学感，也可以用 `adopt a "burn-the-bridge" strategy.`
*   **P13 时代背景的称呼**：`in our dynasty`
    *   **优化**：虽然 dynasty 没错，但古人自称时通常会说“我朝”或特定的朝代名。
    *   **修改**：`"in our Great Song Dynasty..."` 或者 `"under the laws of our land..."`
*   **P15 俗语的翻译（睚眦必报）**：`seeking revenge for the smallest grievance.`
    *   **点评**：**翻译得极好！** 完美还原了意思。
*   **P19 渣男的诬陷（勾勾搭搭）**：`flirting with the talents`
    *   **优化**：林觉是带着愤怒和怨恨说的，词汇可以更脏、更具侮辱性一点。
    *   **修改**：`"shamefully carousing with those scholars"` 或者 `"carrying on illicitly..."`
*   **P43 现代武侠感的翻译（根骨）**：`you had good bone structure.`
    *   **纠错**：上一章提过，`bone structure` 指的是长相。形容练武的“根骨”，地道表达是 **martial frame** 或 **natural potential**。
*   **P54 “井水不犯河水”的翻译**：`Keep your well water from touching my river water.`
    *   **优化**：这是中式翻译的经典保留，老外能看懂比喻，但如果想更霸气一点：
    *   **修改**：`"Stay in your lane and stay out of mine!"` 或者保留中式风味：`"We shall mind our own business; keep your well water away from my river!"`

**Chapter 24: A Failed Divorce and a Fierce Ultimatum**

The crowd's reaction made Qianli Xun realize she had played her hand too aggressively. She was simply too desperate to escape Lin Jue. She hadn't even experienced the thrill of a first love yet—why should she be forced to endure a miserable marriage with a man who repulsed her just because of a freak transmigration?
She had been self-harming since last night, laid her heart bare this morning, and even had the Old Ancestor deploy *The Heartbreak Riddle*. She had even gone so far as to beg for a letter of repudiation, yet nothing had worked. In a final, desperate move to burn her bridges, she had tried to win her freedom by torching her own reputation.
Matchmaker Yan was the first to break the stunned silence. "Young Madam, how could you say such a thing about yourself?! A woman’s honor is everything! Even if the Military Training Officer said those things, he was only guessing or listening to baseless gossip. No one caught you in the act! I refuse to believe any elder here would take such talk seriously. If you think this is a valid reason for him to cast you out, I, Matchmaker Yan, will be the first to object!"
Qianli Xun knew the woman meant well, but she felt zero gratitude. Matchmaker Yan’s "kindness" had just slammed the final door to her freedom shut.
The Old Ancestor resumed fingering her prayer beads. "My granddaughter has gone to such lengths that she is willing to destroy her own name just to get away from him," she said softly. "This alone proves her misery. Why do you elders and the Young Master still insist on chaining her to this marriage?"
One of the Lin elders wavered, turning to Lin Jue. "Nephew, perhaps an amicable divorce *is* for the best. She is clearly determined to leave. What is the point of forcing her to stay?"
Lin Jue was not ready to concede. He stood up and bowed deeply to the room. "Old Ancestor, Uncles, Brothers—I will neither agree to a divorce nor will I cast Shuzhen out. My reasons are two-fold: First, I still harbor deep feelings for her and wish to make this marriage work. Second, while Shuzhen may not care for the Zhu family’s face, I must. My father-in-law prizes his reputation above all else; I cannot and will not fail him."
He then turned directly to the Old Ancestor. "You are a Zhu, venerable one. You, of all people, should be protecting your family’s honor. Please, help me persuade Shuzhen to stay."
The Old Ancestor let out a long, weary sigh. She said nothing, her fingers continuing their rhythmic dance over the beads.
Hearing that sigh, Qianli Xun felt a cold pit form in her stomach. The Matriarch was helpless. *Is this really not going to happen?*
"Niece-in-law," another elder barked, "since you’ve voluntarily claimed a violation of the 'Seven Grounds,' you’ve admitted to lacking wifely virtue! In that case, don't you dare complain about Lin Jue hitting you. If you ask me, he was right to do it! You were at fault first. Whether he divorces you or keeps you is entirely up to him now!"
"Seventh Brother is wrong," another elder cut in. "In our Great Song Dynasty, regardless of who is at fault, divorce has always been the husband's prerogative. Since Lin Jue is generous enough not to hold her past against her and is looking out for the Zhu family, I say this matter is closed."
Qianli Xun felt a wave of bitter regret. She had been too rash in claiming "unchastity," underestimating the deep-seated misogyny of this era. She was now in a much worse position than before.
Suddenly, the truth dawned on her. Lin Jue’s suggestion of an amicable divorce hadn't been an act of kindness—it was a trap. He wanted her to be publicly lectured and humiliated, using the weight of the clan to crush her spirit. Was this revenge for the punch? For locking him up? The man was a petty, small-minded snake.
The more she thought, the more her blood boiled. She stood up, marched right into Lin Jue’s personal space, and glared at him with lethal intensity.
It was the first time Lin Jue had ever been stared down so closely by his wife. This fierce, fiery version of her only made his heart beat faster. It gave him hope that their marriage could be revitalized. He offered her a smug, knowing smile and sat back down, triumphant.
Qianli Xun retracted her gaze. Divorce or not, she had to clear her name before this crowd.
"Old Ancestor, Elders—please disregard my earlier words," she said, her voice now cold and steady. "I only spoke such nonsense because I was desperate for freedom. Yesterday, when I returned from a poetry gathering, Lin Jue accused me of illicit behavior. I told him then that the next time I was invited, I would take him with me so he could see for himself that we do nothing but discuss literature. 
"You can ask my maid, **Kuqiao**. She is my shadow; she knows exactly what happens at those gatherings. The rumors you hear in the streets are nothing but malicious slander!"
Kuqiao stepped forward, her voice trembling but brave. "I can testify to that! Every time my Miss attends a gathering, she does nothing but recite poetry and appreciate the work of others. She has never once overstepped her bounds!"
Third Uncle chimed in again, his voice oily. "Niece-in-law, a woman's place is in the home, caring for her husband. I advise you to stay away from those 'scholars.' Spending too much time with those men will always lead to filthy rumors. You should be grateful Lin Jue is so magnanimous. If you were my wife, I'd have locked you in the house long ago to keep you from wandering!"
The Old Ancestor could no longer remain silent. "If a daughter of the Zhu family is to be treated like a prisoner and abused, I see no reason for this marriage to continue. Divorce them."
Qianli Xun seized the opening. "He may not have locked me in yet, but his actions are already a form of torture! Third Uncle, Elders, listen to the Old Ancestor! Let him divorce me!"
"Calling it torture is a gross exaggeration!" Lin Jue countered instantly. "If anything, *you* were the one torturing *me*. You refused to consummate our marriage on our wedding night—is that not mistreatment? Three days ago, you beat me and tied me up like a common criminal—is that not mistreatment?!"
Qianli Xun froze. She hadn't expected the brute to actually air his dirty laundry in public. The tide of the battle had turned completely against her.
Third Uncle looked outraged. "Niece-in-law! You are entirely in the wrong! It’s one thing to deny him the marriage bed—he could have just taken a concubine—but to *beat* him? To lock him up? You have single-handedly tarnished your reputation as a refined lady! But Nephew... you are a Military Training Officer. You have the strength of ten men. How on earth did you let a woman beat you?!"
Lin Jue didn't want to admit she was a different person, and he certainly didn't want to lose face. "I was merely indulging her," he lied smoothly. "I didn't want to stoop to her level."
Third Uncle turned to the Old Ancestor. "Venerable one, do you truly think a divorce is still appropriate given these facts?"
The Old Ancestor stopped her prayer beads and stood up. "I believe it is time for me to leave."
"Old Ancestor, wait!" Qianli Xun cried out. "Let me finish!"
The Old Ancestor paused. She didn't leave immediately, granting her granddaughter one last shred of face.
Qianli Xun decided to go for broke. She was going to reveal the impossible truth. "The truth is, I am not the real Zhu Shuzhen! The original Zhu Shuzhen was beaten to death by Lin Jue! I am a soul from a far more advanced era named Qianli Xun, and I am a master of the Qianli Fist! If you don't believe me, watch!"
She exploded into a flurry of movements, her fists blurring in a standard, high-level routine. With a final, explosive shout, she drove her fist into the heavy wooden chair next to Matchmaker Yan. 
*CRACK!* 
The chair split perfectly in two, crashing to the floor. The room erupted in gasps of terror.
Kuqiao buried her face in her hands, her heart aching for her miss. The Old Ancestor, however, remained as still as a mountain, her fingers resuming their slow dance over the beads.
Qianli Xun's final strike whistled through the air, stopping a fraction of an inch from Lin Jue’s face. The sheer force of the wind forced him to screw his eyes shut and recoil in fear.
"Well? Scared yet?!" she hissed. "If you value your life, tell them the truth—tell them you killed the real Zhu Shuzhen!"
The gust of wind from her fist carried with it the faint, intoxicating scent of her skin. Lin Jue took a subconscious breath, the "mellow fragrance" of her presence momentarily dousing his anger. When he finally opened his eyes, he saw her small, white "jade fist" clenched tight before him. He found himself utterly mesmerized by her fierce beauty.
Qianli Xun felt a wave of disgust at the strange look in his eyes and yanked her hand back. "What are you staring at? Confess your crimes!"
Lin Jue snapped out of his daze and began spinning a half-truth. "My wife has been secretly practicing martial arts; I only discovered this recently. I always thought she was just a poet, but it turns out she’s a kindred spirit! I'm sure we'll have much more to talk about from now on. Shuzhen, stop this nonsense. And stop saying you aren't yourself—it’s embarrassing."
The room breathed a collective sigh of relief.
"Sister-in-law," Lin Jue’s second brother added, "you’ll say anything to get a divorce, won't you? First you ruin your own name, now you try to frame my brother for murder? You’ve looked exactly like this since the day you entered our home. Who would believe such a ghost story? And since you’re clearly a martial arts master, there’s no way my brother could have beaten you. I don't believe a word of it!"
"Neither do we!" the other elders echoed.
"Kuqiao!" Qianli Xun shouted in frustration.
Kuqiao stepped into the center of the room. "My Miss never knew a single move of martial arts before! She was always the one being beaten! Three days ago, the young master threw her against a stone pillar—the wound on her forehead is still there for all to see! She was unconscious for hours! When she finally woke up, she was like this. In the countryside, we call this a rebirth of the soul!"
Lin Jue let out a booming laugh. "Haha! Kuqiao is *your* maid; she'll recite whatever script you give her. Enough, Wife. The Old Ancestor and the elders are busy men. Let them go home."
Qianli Xun had never felt so utterly defeated. She wanted to use her fists to beat a "yes" out of him, but she knew that in this room, violence would only prove his point. She turned a desperate gaze to the matriarch. "Old Ancestor..."
The Old Ancestor stood up fully now, her gaze fixed calmly on the tip of her own nose. "The world is vast, and many things are beyond human understanding. It is only natural that the ignorant refuse to believe in rebirth. I never met the previous girl, only the one before me now, so I cannot judge. Today... I am powerless to grant this divorce. Such is the world we live in, where a woman's destiny is never her own to decide."
With that, the Old Ancestor turned and walked out, followed by Nanny Xiuzhen.
The Lin family and Matchmaker Yan all bowed low. "Respectfully seeing off the Old Ancestor!"
Qianli Xun watched her go, feeling as though her very life was slipping away. *Am I really stuck with him? Is my story over before it even began?*
In the silence, she saw the silhouette of Shen Jianchen in her mind. If she had known this was her fate, maybe she should have just said "yes" to him back then. At least then, she would have known what love felt like.
Matchmaker Yan cleared her throat, breaking the silence. "Young Madam, I have to say... he’s willing to stay with you even after you tied him up and beat him? You should be grateful! Since there’s no divorce, I’m leaving. I have actual weddings to arrange. You two can settle the rest behind closed doors."
Once the Matchmaker and the elders had dispersed, only Qianli Xun, Lin Jue, and Kuqiao remained in the cavernous hall.
Qianli Xun turned to Lin Jue, her voice vibrating with cold fury. "Lin Jue! Don't you dare think for one second that you've won! I will never be a 'wife' to you!
"Hear me now: You are forbidden from ever stepping foot in the Zhulan Residence again! I will do exactly what I want, when I want, and you will not interfere. We live in the same courtyard, but we are strangers. Stay in your lane and stay out of mine, or I promise you will bear the full, agonizing consequences! I have my own dowry and my own money—I don't need a copper from you to survive. From now on, you'd best stay as far away from me as possible!"
Lin Jue snapped, his face turning a deep, angry purple. "Zhu Shuzhen! You have gone too far!"
Seeing him explode only made Qianli Xun feel better. "This is your karma for refusing the divorce! Did you think keeping me here would make me some submissive little bird you could toy with? Hmph! Mark my words, Lin Jue—I am Qianli Xun, and I am not the woman you used to bully!"
Without waiting for a response, Qianli Xun swept out of the hall. Kuqiao shot a final, sharp glare at the young master before scurrying after her mistress.
Lin Jue was left standing alone in the wreckage of the hall, supposedly the victor. Yet, looking at the broken chair and feeling the lingering sting of her words, he didn't feel like a winner at all.
