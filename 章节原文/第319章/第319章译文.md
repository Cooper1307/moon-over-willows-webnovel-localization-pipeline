## Chapter 319: Family and Prince Scrambling in Crisis

Armed only with *Shen Fist*, Shen Jianchen battered down guard after guard until Liang Shicheng triggered a hidden mechanism beside his daybed—dropping a half-ton **iron cage** straight from the ceiling as the guards leaped clear, trapping Prince Yi cold! Tossing Bai Feng a jar of salve to patch his arrow wound, Liang Shicheng bolted the doors, bricked the roof, and walked right back to finish tormenting Chun Jinqiao before sending her home to Lin Manor—where she casually tipped off Lin Jue that Prince Yi was caged at Liu'an Villa over Zhu Shuzhen!

First thing next morning, Zhu Xiyan marched straight into Liu'an Villa—and instantly got bound, gagged, and locked in a back room!

After a frantic day waiting with zero word from Zhu Manor or Youlin Retreat, Chen Jianglin and Haoyu knocked at Liu'an Villa's gate only to get a chilling warning: both Prince Yi and Zhu Xiyan were "guests" until Second Young Madam showed up—one day left!

Returning home defeated, father and son briefed Old Madam Xia behind closed doors. Rather than panic, the matriarch had a maid fetch a dusty **purple silk pouch** from the bottom of her pearwood trunk containing an old letter and an engraved jade pendant—and declared she'd confront Liang Shicheng herself!

Meanwhile, sensing Haoyu and Chen Jianglin's grim gloom—and catching them sneaking off with Grandmother on a phony "old client" excuse—Qianli Xun promptly bundled Kuqiao into a hired carriage and tailed them straight through north town to **Liu'an Villa**!
