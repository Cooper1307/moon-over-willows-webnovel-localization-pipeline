## Chapter 349: Reunited with His Mother, Freeing the Beauty

Inside the manor, touching the tall, weathered face of the son she'd mourned for twenty years, Shao Yueting refused to leave her vows yet agreed to stay fifteen days—making Wanyan Chi swear never to tell the Jin Emperor she lived.

Handing Shen Jianchen brush and paper to write Qianli Xun an urgent farewell letter—unsigned except for a single **"Shen"**—Wanyan Chi sent a maid straight to Lu Yuanfeng's house.

Unmasking Lu Yuanfeng's whole covert caretaking act, Qianli Xun tore open the letter and read with brimming eyes: how Shen Jianchen had learned she lived from her stories and poem, brought Wanyan Chi's mother to trade her free, vowed to stay one year sparring with Nahegu, asked her to bring Mengrong's and Xilan's bones home to Lin'an—and finally revealed the imperial marriage edict plus Haoyu's new family with pregnant Xiangrong, begging her simply to wait for him in their bamboo cottage!

Packing her manuscripts and the refunded rent silver, she hurried outside the east gate to Mengrong's grave—where Wanyan Chi waited with diggers and the bound Jin soldier who had driven Mengrong to suicide (already condemned for murdering his superior officer). When Qianli Xun hesitated to execute a bound man, Wanyan Chi snapped the killer's neck with one blow, oversaw exhuming both coffins cleanly, and bade her a wistful, empty-handed farewell as a fresh carriage waited down the road.

Returning to the heavily guarded chamber where Shen Jianchen sat calmly drinking, Wanyan Chi couldn't resist dropping one sharp barb: *"I was her first man!"*

Drenched in tossed wine and floored under furious fists—not out of regret, Shen Jianchen roared, only raw agony over failing to save her from his filth!—Wanyan Chi wiped his split lip and burst out laughing when Shen Jianchen snarled, *"I don't care a damn! Say it again and I'll kill you!"*

"Exactly what I needed to hear," Wanyan Chi chuckled from the floor. "Take good care of her."
