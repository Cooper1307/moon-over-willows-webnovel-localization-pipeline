第349章 见过娘亲放美人
## Chapter 349: A Meeting with Mother, Beauty Set Free

王府内，完颜池终于见到娘亲。她一身女尼装扮，端坐在椅子上，手持佛珠，双目湿润。
Inside the prince's mansion, Wanyan Chi finally saw his mother. She sat upright in a chair, dressed as a nun, prayer beads in hand, her eyes wet.

完颜池再次跪在娘亲面前：“娘，难怪我找不到您，原来您出家为尼，现在找到儿子，不如还俗？跟儿子住在燕京，或者回会宁？”
Wanyan Chi knelt before her again. "Mother, no wonder I could not find you. You had become a nun. Now that you have found your son, why not return to secular life? Come live with me in Yanjing, or go back to Huining?"

曾几何时邵月婷想象过儿子若还在世，会长什么样。每次在尼姑庵的香客中看到年纪相仿的公子，她都会畅享一番。今日终于看到亲生儿子的真实面目，比她想象中更加高大魁梧，五官分明，鼻梁高挺，只是皮肤黑了些。
Shao Yueting had often wondered what her son would look like, had he lived. Each time she saw a young gentleman among the pilgrims at the convent, she let her imagination run. Now, at last, she saw the real face of her own flesh and blood—taller and broader than she had pictured, with sharp features and a high bridge of his nose, only his skin a little dark.

“你上前些，让娘仔细看看。”
"Come closer. Let me look at you properly."

完颜池朝前跪了些，抬头仔细观察娘亲的容貌和表情，仿佛要将娘的样子刻在心里一般。
Wanyan Chi shuffled forward on his knees and lifted his head, studying his mother's face and every expression on it, as if to carve her image into his heart.

申简辰站在一旁，被眼前的情景感动，他再讨厌完颜池，不得不承认他长这么大头一次见娘亲，着实可怜。
Shen Jianch stood to one side, moved by the sight. Much as he disliked Wanyan Chi, he had to admit the man was pitiful: the first time in his life he had ever seen his mother.

邵月婷用手摸着儿子的脸：“原来我的孩子长大后是这样……”
Shao Yueting touched her son's cheek. "So this is what my child grew up to be..."

完颜池继续追问：“娘可否愿意留下？”
Wanyan Chi pressed on. "Will Mother stay?"

“娘是出家人，见过你之后，还需回到尼姑庵。”
"I am a woman of the cloth. Having seen you, I must return to the convent."

“娘是不是不想见我才出家？”
"Did you become a nun because you did not want to see me?"

“哪有娘不愿见儿子的？我以为你不在人世，还有你爹也已死，才看破红尘出家为尼。”
"What mother would refuse to see her son? I thought you were dead, and your father too. That is why I saw through the vanity of the world and took the veil."

“肯定是邵正邺欺骗了娘。”
"It must be Shao Zhengye who deceived you."

因心疼儿子，邵月婷说：“你起来，坐下与娘说话。”
Her heart going out to him, Shao Yueting said, "Get up. Sit and talk with me."

完颜池站起来，吩咐丫鬟端把椅子，他要坐在娘亲身边说话。丫鬟拿来椅子后，他坐下后一直抓着娘的手。
Wanyan Chi rose and told a maid to bring a chair—he wanted to sit beside his mother as they spoke. When the chair came, he sat down and kept hold of her hand.

“怨不得哥哥，要怨只怨娘年轻不懂事，喜欢上不该喜欢的人……”
"Do not blame your brother. Blame me. I was young and foolish, and I loved someone I should not have loved..."

“娘后悔遇见父皇？”
"Do you regret meeting His Majesty?"

“曾经后悔过，现在已云淡风轻。”
"I did, once. Now it is all water under the bridge."

“若父皇见到您，肯定开心。”
"If His Majesty saw you, he would be overjoyed."

“答应娘，不要告诉他你见过我，娘想安静度过此生。”
"Promise me you will not tell him you found me. I want to spend the rest of my days in peace."

完颜池点头答应：“好，但娘必须与儿子多待些时日。”
Wanyan Chi nodded. "All right. But you must stay with your son a while longer."

“好，我答应与你待上半个月再回临安。”
"Good. I promise to stay half a month, then return to Lin'an."

完颜池心情大好，向一旁站着的仪王说：“桌上有纸笔，给她写封信，劝她今日离燕京，不要再回来生事。”
Wanyan Chi was in high spirits. He turned to the Prince of Yi, standing nearby. "There is paper and ink on the table. Write her a letter. Urge her to leave Yanjing today and never come back to stir up trouble."

完颜池的意思显然是要放人，申简辰二话不说坐到桌前，提笔写书信。他无心再理会母子二人互诉衷肠，思忖着完颜池刚才说的话。
Wanyan Chi clearly meant to let her go. Shen Jianch sat down at once, brush in hand. He had no stomach for the mother and son pouring out their hearts to each other; his mind was on what Wanyan Chi had just said.

千里寻若听说完颜池将他留下，恐怕为救自己不愿离开，甚至会与完颜池大打出手。别说完颜池不愿看到，他也不愿看到。好不容易换回千里寻，不希望她再有事，一定要通过书信劝她乖乖回临安等他。
If Qianli Xun heard that Wanyan Chi had kept him here, she might refuse to leave for fear of endangering him—might even come to blows with the prince. Wanyan Chi did not want that, and neither did he. He had got Qianli Xun back at last; he could not bear for harm to come to her again. He would write and coax her into going quietly back to Lin'an to wait for him.

想好后，申简辰提笔书写书信。他没有写称谓，只因大家知道的称谓并非他心中之人，而他心中的称谓是他与她之间的秘密。
Once he had thought it through, Shen Jianch set brush to paper. He wrote no form of address, for the name everyone knew was not the person in his heart, and the name he called her by was a secret between the two of them.

写完时，只在落款处写下一个“申”字，也只有她能明白其中的意思。
At the end, he wrote only the character Shen. Only she would know what it meant.

丫鬟见信写完，主动上前接过。
Seeing the letter finished, the maid stepped forward to take it.

完颜池将丫鬟招呼到身边，对她耳语几句，丫鬟只顾点头。
Wanyan Chi beckoned the maid close and whispered a few words in her ear. She only nodded and nodded.

申简辰警惕地说：“完颜池，不许耍花招。”
Shen Jianch said warily, "Wanyan Chi, no tricks."

完颜池笑道：“娘在此，我怎敢耍花招？放心，我现在只想成全你们。”
Wanyan Chi laughed. "With my mother here, how could I play tricks? Rest easy. All I want now is to see you two together."

他的表情中透出诚意，申简辰心安许多。
There was sincerity in his face, and Shen Jianch felt easier in his mind.

……
...

千里寻买完笔墨刚回到住处，王府的丫鬟前来，将信给了陆远峰。
Qianli Xun had just bought ink and brushes and returned to her lodgings when a maid from the prince's mansion came and handed the letter to Lu Yuanfeng.

那丫鬟千里寻见过，质问道：“陆远峰，你是不是一直在骗我？”
Qianli Xun knew the maid by sight. "Lu Yuanfeng," she demanded, "have you been lying to me all this time?"

陆远峰老实交代：“的确是小王爷派我帮师傅，住的地方也是小王爷吩咐我租的，包括师傅赚钱的活儿都是小王爷帮忙找的，我只是个跑腿的。”
Lu Yuanfeng confessed plainly. "It is true. His Lordship sent me to help you, master. He told me to rent this place, and he found the work that earned you money too. I am only the errand boy."

千里寻伸出拳头向陆远峰打去：“让你骗我！”
Qianli Xun threw out a fist at him. "Take that for lying to me!"

陆远峰边躲避边说：“师傅别生气，丫鬟带来仪王的信，您看后准高兴。”
Lu Yuanfeng dodged. "Do not be angry, master. The maid brought a letter from the Prince of Yi. Read it and you will be glad."

千里寻打不下去，不由自主收起拳头：“什么？仪王的信？他不是以为我死了吗？为何还会给我写信？”
Her fist would not land. She lowered it against her will. "What? A letter from the Prince of Yi? Does he not think I am dead? Why would he write to me?"

“具体什么原因，我不清楚，师傅看完信后自然明白。”陆远峰将信递到师傅面前。
"I could not say. Read it, master, and you will understand." Lu Yuanfeng held the letter out to her.

千里寻接过，打开认真浏览……
She took it, opened it, and read with care...

完颜池与蔡靖骗我你已不在人世，我匆忙回临安来到神柳下，希望它能帮忙让你回到我身边。后得法师开示，我在临安苟延残喘些时日，老天护佑，既听说千里寻才子写了新话本，还有幽栖居士新写的诗词，才知你没死。于是我请来完颜池的娘亲邵月婷，带她来到燕京，希望把你换回。……
Wanyan Chi and Cai Jing told me you were gone. I hurried back to Lin'an and went to the Divine Willow, hoping it would bring you back to me. Then a monk showed me a way. I dragged out a few more days in Lin'an, and heaven took pity: I heard that the scholar Qianli Xun had written a new story, and that Youqi Jushi had new poems besides. Only then did I know you were alive. So I fetched Wanyan Chi's mother, Shao Yueting, and brought her to Yanjing, hoping to trade her for you...

千里寻自语道：“你怎么这么傻？我已不在王府，竟然还这么努力地救我。”
Qianli Xun murmured to herself, "How could you be so foolish? I am no longer in the prince's mansion, and still you work this hard to save me."

完颜池见到他亲娘后，答应放你回临安，我很开心。完颜池的拳法与申拳有渊源，我需在燕京待上一年，与他的师傅互相切磋。原谅我在你离开燕京时不能见你一面。……
Now that he has seen his own mother, Wanyan Chi has promised to let you go back to Lin'an, and I am glad. His boxing has roots in the Shen style, and I must stay a year in Yanjing to trade pointers with his master. Forgive me that I could not see you off when you left Yanjing...

千里寻感动之余气愤道：“肯定是完颜池的阴谋，我现在就去找他算账！”
Tears welled in Qianli Xun's eyes, and anger with them. "It is a plot. I am going to find him and settle this now!"

陆远峰提醒道：“师傅先别生气，看完信再决定要不要找小王爷算账。”
Lu Yuanfeng warned her, "Do not fly off the handle, master. Finish the letter before you decide whether to settle with His Lordship."

千里寻稳住情绪继续向下看：
Qianli Xun steadied herself and read on:

出了王府别再回头，不要找完颜池闹事，我好不容易把你救出来，不想你再有任何闪失。你若心里有我，乖乖回临安，在我们的竹屋里等我回家，我向你保证，一年后会完好无损回到你身边。
Once you leave the mansion, do not look back, and do not pick a fight with Wanyan Chi. I went to a great deal of trouble to get you out; I will not have you come to harm. If you have any feeling for me, go quietly back to Lin'an and wait for me in our bamboo hut. I promise I will return to you in one piece, a year from now.

我把梦容和喜兰葬在燕京城外，出了城一直向东边走，便可看到她和喜兰的坟头，墓碑上刻着她们的名字，很容易找到。你帮我把她们的尸骨迁回临安，安葬的地方早已预备好，白风都知道……
I buried Mengrong and Xilan outside Yanjing. Go out of the city and keep walking east, and you will see their graves. Their names are carved on the stones; you cannot miss them. Move their bones back to Lin'an for me. The burial place is already prepared—Bai Feng knows all about it...

千里寻已泪眼婆娑，本是为梦容医病来到燕京，燕京却成为她的葬身之地。
Qianli Xun's eyes brimmed with tears. She had come to Yanjing to cure Mengrong's illness, and Yanjing had become the place where she was laid to rest.

她瞪着陆远峰：“是不是金人杀了梦容和喜兰？”
She glared at Lu Yuanfeng. "Did the Jin kill Mengrong and Xilan?"

陆远峰根本不知此事：“她们是谁我都不认识，怎么知道她们是不是被金人所杀？”
Lu Yuanfeng knew nothing of it. "I did not even know them. How would I know if the Jin killed them?"

“要是承认你是大宋子民，要是还认我这个师傅，给我把杀害梦容和喜兰的人找出来，我要替她们报仇。”
"If you admit you are a subject of the Song, and if you still call me your master, then find me the ones who killed Mengrong and Xilan. I will avenge them."

“找个把人倒不难，需告诉我她们什么时候死的？在哪里？我好查寻。”
"That is not hard. Tell me when they died and where, so I can look into it."

千里寻仔细推断，梦容应该是在金兵进入燕京那日：“应该是去年我刚遇见你时，她们从一个巷子逃走，估计是在巷子里被害，你只要打听那日是哪群人进入巷子肯定能找到人。”
Qianli Xun thought it through. It must have been the day the Jin troops entered Yanjing. "It should be last year, just after I met you. They fled from an alley. They were likely killed in that alley. Ask who went into the alley that day, and you will find them."

陆远峰心想进入巷子的那队金人正好是小王爷的手下的人马，找起来应该容易，不过此事还需征求小王爷同意。
Lu Yuanfeng thought of the Jin squad that had gone into the alley—His Lordship's own men. They would be easy enough to find, but he would need the prince's leave first.

“刚才来的丫鬟说，小王爷让师傅尽快出城，若天黑前您还没出去，他便不会放您离开。师傅把握好时间，我这就去替师傅找人，找到后带到城外任由师傅处置。”
"The maid who came just now said His Lordship wants you out of the city as soon as possible. If you are still inside by nightfall, he will not let you go. Watch the time, master. I will find the men now and bring them outside the city for you to deal with as you see fit."

陆远峰离开后，千里寻继续阅览剩下的内容：
When Lu Yuanfeng had gone, Qianli Xun read the rest:

以后不必再纠结与陈浩宇的婚姻，在汴梁时官家下旨将你许给我。陈浩宇虽不情愿，可圣意难违，为了家人他只能取消婚姻。我心里高兴，却不想逼迫你成为我的人，向陈浩宇承诺绝不对你说出实情，等你出狱后，尊重你的选择。……
You need not agonize over the marriage to Chen Haoyu any longer. Back in Bianliang, the emperor decreed that you were to be given to me. Chen Haoyu was unwilling, but the imperial will could not be opposed; for his family's sake he had to call it off. I was glad, yet I would not force you to be mine. I promised Chen Haoyu I would never tell you the truth, and that after you came out of prison I would leave the choice to you...

千里寻终于明白为何他们二人都向她问过同样的问题。
At last Qianli Xun understood why both of them had asked her the same question.

告诉你一个好消息，陈家为想绒姑娘赎身，把她放在陈浩宇身边，我来燕京前听说陈浩宇快要当爹。他已有着落，你不必再挂心。我的努力终于感动上天，你我可以毫无阻碍地走到一起。记着，什么都别做，回临安等我。
Good news, too. The Chen family has bought Xiangrong's freedom and installed her at Chen Haoyu's side. Before I came to Yanjing, I heard he was to be a father soon. He is settled; you need not worry about him. My efforts have moved heaven at last, and nothing now stands between us. Remember: do nothing. Go back to Lin'an and wait for me.

千里寻盯着落款处“申”字：“好，我听你的，回临安在竹屋等你。”
Qianli Xun stared at the character Shen at the foot of the letter. "All right. I will do as you say. I will wait for you in the bamboo hut in Lin'an."

千里寻将信装好，开始收拾东西，不过是些衣物和她写的手稿，再就是所剩无几的银子。
She folded the letter away and began to pack—a few clothes, the manuscripts she had written, and the little silver she had left.

她拉开抽屉准备将不多的银子带走，发现交房租的一袋银子乖乖躺在里面，定是陆远峰所为。
She opened the drawer to take her money and found the bag of rent silver sitting meekly inside. Lu Yuanfeng must have put it back.

“不要也好，完颜池欠我太多，要了他的命都偿还不清，区区一袋银子算不了什么。”千里寻拿起银子，再次想到她失身完颜池之事，“我努力守住的贞洁被狗吃了，你会不会嫌弃我？……，你我好不容易能走到一起，我想这么多做什么？还是尽快出城。”
"Let him have his way. Wanyan Chi owes me more than his life could pay, so one bag of silver is nothing." She picked up the silver, and her thoughts turned again to the night she had been forced by Wanyan Chi. "The virtue I fought to keep went to the dogs. Will you still want me? ... We have finally found our way to each other. Why should I dwell on it? Better to leave the city before dark."

千里寻来到城外，找了好久才找到埋葬梦容的地方。
Qianli Xun reached the outskirts and searched a long while before she found where Mengrong was buried.

她眼中噙着泪水：“梦容，都怪我，若不是我极力要求带你来燕京找神医诊治，你也不会这么早离开，呜呜……”
Tears in her eyes, she said, "Mengrong, it is all my fault. If I had not insisted on bringing you to Yanjing to see the physician, you would not have left so soon. Oh, oh..."

千里寻哭了一会儿继续说：“今日我会带你一起回临安……”
She wept a while, then went on. "Today I am taking you back to Lin'an with me..."

“什么准备都没有，怎么挖出尸骨回临安？岂不是空口白牙说瞎话？”
"You have made no preparations. How will you dig up the bones and carry them to Lin'an? Empty words, all of them."

身后传来完颜池的声音，千里寻猛然转过头，发现完颜池押着一金人，身后还跟着些拿工具的人。
Wanyan Chi's voice came from behind her. She spun around and saw him with a Jin man in his grip, and several men with tools following.

“你来做什么？”
"What are you doing here?"

“来帮你。”完颜池将押着的金人推到坟头，“是他逼死了你的朋友，本王把人带来，任由处置。”
"Helping you." Wanyan Chi shoved the prisoner toward the grave. "He is the one who hounded your friend to death. His Lordship has brought him for you to deal with as you please."

千里寻站起来，露出难以置信的表情：“你会这么好心？”
Qianli Xun rose, unable to believe her ears. "You would be so kind?"

“本王当然没这么好心，若是本王信任之人，绝不会随便带到你面前，可惜此人杀了本王手下一名将领，他必须死，顺便卖你个人情，只可惜找不到杀死丫鬟的人。”
"Of course I am not kind. I would never bring a man I trusted before you so lightly. But this one killed one of my officers, so he must die. I might as well do you a favor. Pity I could not find the one who killed your maid."

“哼！就知道你没这么好心。”
"Hah! I knew you were not kind."

“少啰嗦，快动手吧。”
"Less talk. Get on with it."

千里寻攥紧拳头：“梦容，今日我要为你报仇。”她用拳向金人打去，却下不去手，拳头停在半空。她从没杀过没有反抗之力的人，还真有些不习惯。
Qianli Xun clenched her fists. "Mengrong, today I avenge you." She struck at the Jin man, but the blow would not come; her fist hung in the air. She had never killed a man who could not fight back, and the strangeness of it stayed her hand.

被绑的金人不断求饶：“姑娘饶命！小王爷饶命！”
The bound Jin man begged for mercy. "Spare me, miss! Spare me, Your Lordship!"

完颜池一拳过去，砸在那人的太阳穴，被绑的金人立刻毙命。完颜池收起拳头：“本王帮你，是不是该说些感谢的话？”
Wanyan Chi drove a fist into the man's temple, and the bound Jin man died on the spot. He drew back his hand. "I helped you. Should you not thank me?"

千里寻冷笑一声，并未理会。
Qianli Xun gave a cold laugh and said nothing.

完颜池招呼拿工具的人：“可以忙活了！”
Wanyan Chi called to the men with tools. "Get to work!"

拿工具的人开始刨坟挖尸骨，千里寻则走到一旁耐心等待。
They began to break the grave and dig out the bones. Qianli Xun walked aside and waited patiently.

完颜池有意靠近美人，美人转过头懒得理他，他并不在意：“你记着，放你走是因为本王爱你，希望你好。”
Wanyan Chi edged closer to the beauty. She turned her head and would not look at him, but he did not mind. "Remember this. I let you go because I love you and want the best for you."

千里寻冷笑道：“少来迷惑老娘，老娘不吃这一套。警告你，一年后把仪王乖乖送回临安，否则老娘这辈子跟你没完！”
Qianli Xun sneered. "Save your sweet talk for someone else. I do not buy it. And hear this: send the Prince of Yi quietly back to Lin'an in a year, or I will never leave you alone in this life."

“跟本王没完才好，这样你与本王时时有牵绊。”
"If you never leave me alone, so much the better. Then you and I will always be tied together."

“你！……”
"You! ..."

“行了，别生气，本王答应你一年后乖乖把仪王还给你。”
"Enough. Do not be angry. I promise to return the Prince of Yi to you in a year, quietly."

“你就是个卑鄙小人，我明明已不在王府，还设计骗他？”
"You are nothing but a low, contemptible man. I am no longer in your mansion, and still you trick him?"

“仪王看上去也是聪明人，可惜遇到你的事他会变笨，笨到他自己都意识不到，本王略施小计，他便信了。不过你放心，本王只想与他切磋拳法，没别的意思。”
"The Prince of Yi looks clever enough, but where you are concerned he turns stupid—too stupid to know it. I played a small trick and he believed me. Rest easy, though. All I want is to trade boxing moves with him. Nothing more."

“量你也不敢。”
"I doubt you would dare do more."

完颜池收起戏谑的表情，一脸认真：“你这一走，也不知往后你我还能否相见？”
Wanyan Chi dropped his teasing and turned serious. "You are going away. Who knows whether we shall meet again?"

“最好永远别见！”
"Best never to see you again!"

“本王的娘出家为尼，不愿留在本王身边，以后本王会抽时间去临安看望她，如果到临安，你会不会与本王像朋友一样一起喝茶？”
"My mother has taken the veil and will not stay by my side. From time to time I will go to Lin'an to see her. If I come to Lin'an, will you drink tea with me as a friend?"

千里寻沉默片刻，用平缓的语气说：“我不想回忆不愉快的往事。”
Qianli Xun was silent a moment, then said evenly, "I do not want to remember unhappy things."

完颜池明白美人的意思，失落地说：“好吧，以后本王不会再打扰你。”
Wanyan Chi read her meaning, dashed. "All right. I will not trouble you again."

他很想上前最后拥抱一下美人，看到美人警惕的眼神，只好作罢：“马车已给你预备好，在前面路口等着。”
He longed to take her in his arms one last time, but the wariness in her eyes stopped him. "The carriage is ready for you. It waits at the crossroads ahead."

完颜池既失落又深情地看了美人最后一眼，转身离开。
Wanyan Chi gave the beauty one last look, heavy with loss and longing, then turned and walked away.

千里寻对完颜池所做的一切一点儿也感激不起来，只盼望此人永远别出现在她面前。
Qianli Xun felt not the least gratitude for all Wanyan Chi had done. She only wished the man would never cross her path again.

完颜池回到王府来到一处屋子，外面安排有重兵把守。
Wanyan Chi returned to the mansion and went to a house guarded by armed men.

守卫门口的人见小王爷前来，主动打开门上的大锁。
The guards at the door saw the young prince coming and unlocked the heavy lock themselves.

完颜池推门而入，仪王正悠然自得坐在案前饮酒，他走上前在仪王对面坐好。
Wanyan Chi pushed the door open. The Prince of Yi was sitting at a table, drinking at his ease. He stepped forward and sat down opposite him.

“本王已按照你的要求给她安排好一切，你可以安心留下。”
"I have arranged everything for you as you asked. You may stay in peace."

“外面的人就是你的待客之道？”
"Armed men outside. Is that your idea of hospitality?"

“你的拳法在本王和师傅之上，怕你逃走。”
"Your boxing is better than mine and my master's. I am afraid you will run away."

“你我拳法甚有渊源，我是心甘情愿留下。”
"Our styles share the same roots. I am here of my own accord."

“人心难测，还是小心为妙。”
"People's hearts are hard to read. Better to be careful."

“什么时候开始切磋？”
"When do we start trading moves?"

“师傅说明日开始。”
"My master says tomorrow."

“好，那就明日。”
"Good. Tomorrow, then."

仪王悠然自得应对，还夺走美人的心，完颜多少不舒服，不如刺激刺激他：“有件事你可能还不知。”
The Prince of Yi answered with perfect composure—and had stolen the beauty's heart besides. Wanyan Chi could not help but feel a sting. He would needle him a little. "There is something you may not know."

申简辰并未当回事，继续饮酒：“什么事？”
Shen Jianch did not take the bait and went on drinking. "What?"

完颜池大言不惭说出十个字：“本王是她的第一个男人。”
Wanyan Chi said it shamelessly, ten words: "I was the first man she ever had."

申简辰将杯中未喝完的酒泼在完颜池的脸上。
Shen Jianch flung the rest of his wine into Wanyan Chi's face.

完颜池笑道：“呵呵……！怎么？是不是知道没办法成为她的第一个男人，后悔自己付出这么多？”
Wanyan Chi laughed. "Ha! What is it? Sorry you cannot be her first, after all you have given?"

申简辰没有回答，一拳向完颜池打去。完颜池躲避过后，迎拳而上。两人打了几个回合，完颜池逐渐败下阵来。
Shen Jianch did not answer. He threw a punch. Wanyan Chi dodged, then countered. They traded several rounds, and Wanyan Chi was slowly driven back.

打不过对方只好用言语攻击：“看来你真的后悔，往后少在本王面前表现出你有多爱她。”
Losing on his feet, he went for words. "You really do regret it. Stop showing me how much you love her."

申简辰一拳将完颜池打倒：“不是后悔，是心痛，心痛被你和蔡靖所骗没有及时救她，让她被你糟蹋，她当时肯定很难过，你就是个畜生！根本不配喜欢她！”
Shen Jianch knocked him down with one punch. "Not regret. Pain. Pain that you and Cai Jing fooled me, that I did not save her in time, that you defiled her. She must have been miserable. You are a beast. You do not deserve to love her at all!"

玩延迟的嘴角被打裂，鲜血流出，他用手抹去，强调道：“本王因为爱她才会动她，别的女人想让本王动，本王都不稀罕。”
Wanyan Chi's split lip bled. He wiped it away and insisted, "I touched her because I love her. Other women want me to touch them, and I could not care less."

申简辰又一拳打在完颜池的脸上：“她是我的人，你再敢动他我就杀了你！”
Shen Jianch hit him in the face again. "She is mine. Touch her again and I will kill you!"

玩延迟向后趔趄，忍着痛笑道：“本王不会再动她，能成为她的第一个男人本王很满足，可惜，你永远不可能，呵呵……！”
Wanyan Chi staggered back and laughed through the pain. "I will not touch her again. I am content to have been her first. Pity you never can be. Ha!"

申简辰一脚将完颜池踢倒在地，上前抓住他的衣领：“告诉你，我不在乎，以后少用此事激我，再提我就送你归西！”
Shen Jianch kicked him to the floor, grabbed his collar and hauled him close. "Listen. I do not care. Stop using this to provoke me. One more word and I send you to your grave."

完颜池终于说出心中所想：“本王想听的就是你这句话，本王把她还给你，你答应本王要好好待她。”
At last Wanyan Chi said what was on his mind. "That is what I wanted to hear. I am giving her back to you. Promise me you will treat her well."

申简辰一把将完颜池推开，站起来：“不必你来叮嘱。”
Shen Jianch shoved him aside and stood up. "I do not need you to tell me that."

完颜池躺在地上笑起来：“呵呵呵……！”
Wanyan Chi lay on the floor and laughed. "Ha, ha, ha!"
