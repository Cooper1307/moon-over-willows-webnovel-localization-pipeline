# Chapter 54: Hurrying Back to the Zhu Residence for Family Chat

---

Shen Jianchen walked up to the Sacred Willow, sat down on a stone, and leaned his back against its thick trunk. He closed his eyes and reached out to the Sacred Willow with his heart, hoping it could understand his feelings.

---

Qiao Mengrong also found a stone to sit on, imitating her brother by leaning against the Sacred Willow and closing her eyes to feel it with her heart.

---

Xilan watched the two of them, finding it quite strange. She had no idea what the Prince and the County Princess were doing.

---

After feeling it for a while, Qiao Mengrong only noticed that the willow trunk was cold and cool. Maybe Brother felt hot and wanted to cool off here.

---

A gust of cool air swept over, and she couldn't help but sneeze: "Achoo!"

---

She opened her eyes, shifted her body, and looked at her brother. He was still pressed against the Sacred Willow, eyes closed tight.

---

Qiao Mengrong remembered the couple tying the red silk ribbon and asked tentatively: "Brother, when you have time, could you go with me to the nunnery to get a red silk ribbon?"

---

"Sure." Shen Jianchen kept his eyes shut.

---

"I want to write both our names on it, and then Brother has to go with me to tie it on the Sacred Willow."

---

Shen Jianchen regretted answering so thoughtlessly. He opened his eyes: "Let's go back." He stood up, about to leave.

---

Qiao Mengrong also stood up and looked at her brother's back: "Does Brother not want to tie the red silk ribbon with me?"

---

Shen Jianchen turned around. Qiao Mengrong's eyes glistened with tears. He couldn't bear to hurt her. He thought, might as well fulfill her wish on Zhao Pu's behalf: "I'm willing."

---

Qiao Mengrong smiled again and walked to her brother's side: "Brother, thank you for saying yes."

---

"Let's go. The sun is about to set and the air is getting cool. Brother is worried you'll catch a chill."

---

Qiao Mengrong felt incredibly happy. Before, it was always her caring for Brother. Now it was him caring for her. The feeling was truly wonderful.

---

Brother had already turned and walked back the way they came. Qiao Mengrong followed closely, staring at Brother's large palm. Ever since Brother recovered, he had never held her hand in that palm of his.

---

Leaving the pond and stepping onto the forest path, Qiao Mengrong hurried a few steps forward and grabbed Brother's broad palm with her slender fingers.

---

Shen Jianchen stopped immediately: "Mengrong, what are you doing?"

---

"I want to hold Brother's hand and walk together."

---

"The forest path is too narrow. Holding hands would be really inconvenient."

---

Qiao Mengrong had to let go and asked tentatively: "If this path were wide enough for two, would Brother be willing to hold my hand and walk together?"

---

"If it were Zhao Pu, he'd certainly be willing."

---

Hearing this, Qiao Mengrong didn't know whether to be sad or happy. She insisted: "In my eyes, Brother is Zhao Pu, and Zhao Pu is Brother."

---

"But I don't remember anything..."

---

Qiao Mengrong could tell—Brother was trying to tell her that he didn't remember the feelings between them. She had always known this, but hearing it from his own lips still hurt. A tear fell.

---

"It's okay if Brother doesn't remember. As long as I remember that Brother loves me and I love Brother, that's enough. I'll wait for Brother to marry me next Mid-Autumn Festival."

---

Qiao Mengrong's confession and reminder made Shen Jianchen uneasy. He didn't want to let Qiao Mengrong down, nor did he want to betray his own feelings. He needed to find Qianli Xun as soon as possible before making any decision.

---

"Don't stand here talking. Let's go."

---

Shen Jianchen walked forward and Qiao Mengrong followed. Though sad, she was confident that Brother would only belong to her. After all, there was the Emperor's arranged marriage. Even if the reborn Brother had forgotten her, he couldn't go against the Emperor's own words. She smiled through her tears.

---

Xilan observed the two and worried deeply for the County Princess. Ever since Prince Yi was brought back to life, he'd been like a different person—no longer as close to the County Princess as before, treating her only like a brother treats a sister.

---

...

---

Zhu Xiyan hosted the banquet for three reasons: first, to follow the Emperor's example; second, to strengthen bonds among officials; third, to provide a stage for Jiangnan's talented scholars. To be honest, the third reason was also about following the Emperor. After all, His Majesty valued letters over arms and championed the way of the scholar. As court-appointed officials, they had to follow the Emperor's lead if they wanted rapid promotion.

---

The Emperor's banquets usually required a month of preparation. Zhu Xiyan didn't dare compare himself to the Emperor, but even so, he needed at least half a month to prepare.

---

Everyone in the Zhu household worked hard for the annual banquet. There were people in charge of coming up with ideas to make each year's banquet unique. Deploying the Four Departments and Six Bureaus went without saying.

---

The "Four Departments" were the Office of Setup, the Office of Cuisine, the Office of Refreshments, and the Office of Tableware. The "Six Bureaus" were the Bureau of Fruit, the Bureau of Confections, the Bureau of Greens, the Bureau of Lighting, the Bureau of Incense, and the Bureau of Logistics. The female singers and dancers also had to rehearse their performances in advance.

---

The banquet was held in the Zhu Family Garden, which had been built specifically for hosting banquets. It was divided into two large areas—one for male guests and one for female guests. Each area had a wooden plaque displaying a line from Li Bai's poetry. The men's area read: "Clear wine in golden cups, ten thousand coins per vat." The women's area read: "Rare delicacies on jade plates, worth a fortune."

---

Several square long tables were set up in both areas, with drum stools arranged according to the number of guests. Low flowers were planted around the tables—pleasing to the eye without blocking anyone's view. A wooden stage of moderate height was also set up in the garden, where the Zhu family's performers would entertain the guests.

---

Qianli Xun did not travel with Lin Jue back to the Zhu residence. She brought only her two personal maidservants and Aunt Wei.

---

Lin Jue was quite displeased but helpless. He thought that as long as Zhu Shuzhen was around, he wouldn't even be able to bring another woman home. He might as well take the opportunity today to tell his father-in-law about the divorce, and hope the old man wouldn't think any less of him. But thinking about letting Zhu Shuzhen go, he felt somewhat reluctant.

---

Arriving at the Zhu residence, Qianli Xun headed straight for her mother's quarters.

---

It was Xiaguo's first time seeing such a grand estate. She felt both envious and jealous, and more than ever convinced that beauty was ill-fated.

---

The banquet had dedicated staff managing it, so Madam Xiang didn't need to worry too much. Besides, everything was already set up—just waiting for the guests to arrive.

---

She sat in her room resting, also looking forward to her daughter's return. Last time, they'd even brought out the Elder to help with the divorce, and it still failed. When her daughter came back, she wanted to offer more comfort.

---

Just as she was thinking this, Yinxing's voice came from outside: "Madam, Young Lady is back."

---

Madam Xiang couldn't wait. She got up and went out to welcome her daughter. Seeing her, her eyes moistened: "Shuzhen, Mother is truly happy to see you."

---

Qianli Xun said lightly: "If Mother is so happy to see me, why do you always talk to me with red eyes?"

---

Madam Xiang insisted through tears: "Mother is happy."

---

Seeing Aunt Wei had also returned, Madam Xiang asked: "Has Auntie come back with something important to tell the Master?"

---

"Madam, let's talk inside." Aunt Wei ordered the maidservants following them: "Wait outside. Don't enter the room unless called. If you have anything to report, do it outside. Kuqiao, you come in."

---

Kuqiao replied: "Yes."

---

Xiaguo was quite annoyed. Of the maidservants following, only she and Kuqiao were here, and Aunt Wei was clearly targeting her. Humph! Relying on the Young Lady's favor, she threw her weight around in front of the servants every day. Just wait until she became the mistress—she'd take Aunt Wei down a peg.

---

Once inside and everyone was seated, Madam Xiang made small talk: "Since Shuzhen married into the Lin family, this is Auntie's first time coming back. It shouldn't be about the banquet, should it?"

---

Aunt Wei said: "First, I've come back to visit Madam and have a chat. Second, I need to tell you about Young Lady's situation in the Lin family. Madam and the Master entrusted Young Lady to me—I'm responsible for her and need to make sure you both know what she's really going through. Third, I want to see the Master and talk to him about Young Lady's divorce."

---

Madam Xiang said: "Thank you for all your trouble. The Master and I placed you by Shuzhen's side specifically so you could look after her on our behalf."

---

"Sigh! I failed to take good care of Young Lady. The Young Master behaves himself when I'm around, but the moment I go out on business, he beats Young Lady. He even brought a courtesan home. This time while I was out collecting rent, he actually beat her so badly she lost her memory. This marriage can't go on."

---

Madam Xiang started wiping tears again: "I already know what you've described. Shuzhen told me everything last time she came home. I was heartbroken and supported her divorce, but the Master wouldn't agree. It's gotten to the point where no one in the Zhu family dares to speak up for Shuzhen."

---

"That's why I plan to have a serious talk with the Master. Madam may be afraid of him, but I'm not. Worst case, he'll send me back to my hometown."

---

Madam Xiang said: "Hearing you say that puts my mind at ease. After the banquet, I'll take you to see the Master."

---

Qianli Xun had never attended such a banquet and asked: "Mother, are you going to the banquet?"

---

Madam Xiang felt a pang of sadness. Shuzhen was a regular at banquets, yet she was asking such an ignorant question. She explained patiently: "Mother must go. I need to help your father host the officials' households. You and Wenxin need to come along too."

---

Qianli Xun continued: "Is Auntie going?"

---

Aunt Wei replied: "I'm not qualified to attend such banquets. I'll take this chance to rest and think over what to say to the Master. But I'm sure the banquet food won't skip me, right, Madam? Hehe..."

---

"Yes, you won't be missed." Madam Xiang finally smiled and called out: "Yinxing!"

---

Yinxing came in, and Madam Xiang told her to notify the banquet staff to prepare a portion of food and wine and deliver it here.

---

Yinxing agreed and went out to arrange it.

---

After arranging everything, she hurried back and reported: "Madam, it's all set. On the way, I ran into one of the Master's attendants. He said it's almost time and asked Madam to come to the garden as soon as possible."

---

Madam Xiang stood up and took her daughter's hand: "Shuzhen, come with Mother."

---

Aunt Wei saw them to the door and told Kuqiao to attend closely to Young Lady. Seeing Xiaguo about to follow, she stopped her again: "Xiaguo, you don't need to follow Young Lady. Stay here with me."

---

Xiaguo replied reluctantly: "Yes." She watched helplessly as Kuqiao followed Young Lady to the banquet, growing even more resentful toward Aunt Wei.

---

On the way to the garden, Qianli Xun and Madam Xiang happened to run into Chen Wenxin, who had settled Laibao and hurried over to help her mother-in-law host the female guests.

---

After greeting her mother-in-law, she turned to Shuzhen: "Sister-in-law, hello."

---

Qianli Xun returned the greeting: "Sister-in-law, hello."

---

The three of them walked toward the garden together.

---

Madam Xiang asked as they walked: "Who came from the Chen family?"

---

Chen Wenxin replied: "My mother is ill, and my father is worried about her, so the two of them couldn't attend. They sent my brother Haoyu instead."

---

Madam Xiang said: "What I envy most about the Chen family isn't their wealth—it's how deeply devoted your parents are to each other. Men like your father are truly rare. Your mother's marriage to him has made countless women envious."

---

Chen Wenxin said: "Mother-in-law flatters them. My parents argue often too. It's not as perfect as outsiders make it seem."

---

Madam Xiang stopped and scolded: "How can you speak of your own parents like that?"

---

"I'm telling the truth." Chen Wenxin knew that her father-in-law was usually cold toward her mother-in-law. If she praised her own parents' relationship too much, Mother-in-law would feel even more envious—and even sadder because of it.

---

Qianli Xun couldn't figure it out. The Chen parents had a deep relationship, and Sister-in-law Chen Wenxin was educated and refined. How could the Chen family produce someone like that scumbag Chen Haoyu?

---

"Sister-in-law, why isn't Chen Haoyu anything like you?"

---

"How is he not like me?"

---

"His behavior—he's a total playboy. I've run into him twice, and both times he was bullying respectable women. His reputation out there isn't great at all."

---

Madam Xiang scolded: "Shuzhen, how can you speak of the Young Master of the Chen family like that!"

---

Chen Wenxin didn't mind: "It's fine. No one likes my brother the way he is. But he's not bad—he was just spoiled by his grandmother. Once you get to know him better, you'll naturally see his good side."

---

Qianli Xun was dumbfounded. She thought, here's this total playboy, and someone's telling me he's not bad! If someone like Chen Haoyu isn't bad, then who in this world is?

---

The three chatted as they walked and arrived at the banquet grounds. Flowers bloomed everywhere, and the air was filled with the scent of delicacies. The Zhu family's maidservants bustled about, weaving between the tables arranging fruit, fine wine, and exquisite dishes.
