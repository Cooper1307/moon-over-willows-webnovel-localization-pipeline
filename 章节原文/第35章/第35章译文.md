# Chapter 35: Satirizing the Emperor and Appreciating Genyue

---

The others stepped aside, while the princes gathered in the center and lined up.

---

Qiao Wenxian gave Prince Yi a nudge, and Shen Jianchen had no choice but to walk toward the line of princes.

---

His Majesty certainly had quite a few sons. Lined up from eldest to youngest, the Crown Prince stood at the front, looking to be in his twenties. The last row had boys around ten years old at most, and the youngest looked only three or four.

---

Shen Jianchen thought to himself that Zhao Pu was the thirteenth son, so he should stand in the middle row. He quickly squeezed in.

---

Led by the Crown Prince, all the princes entered the grand hall, followed by the civil and military officials, and finally the literary scholars.

---

Standing among the princes, Shen Jianchen peeked forward and could only see the backs of the heads of the first two rows. Glancing to either side, he saw that each prince wore an expressionless face. He found it rather boring, so he simply gazed up at the imperial throne.

---

The throne should have been carved from precious rosewood. Its base was wide and stable, the backrest covered with brocade, the armrests shaped like dragons, carved so lifelike they enhanced the emperor's noble status.

---

Liang Shicheng's voice rang out: "His Majesty has arrived!"

---

Shen Jianchen's gaze left the dragon throne just in time to see Zhao Ji walking toward it. He was tall, wearing a red dragon robe, a hat with long wings on his head, his face fair, two mustache strokes on his lips, with long eyebrows and eyes—he was quite a handsome man indeed.

---

After settling onto the dragon throne, His Majesty gazed at everyone in the hall and said slowly, "There's no need to be nervous. Relax. Today is not a court session. We are looking forward to taking you to appreciate the beautiful scenery of Genyue. Then we shall eat well, drink well, and enjoy ourselves. The literary scholars must also display your talents to the fullest."

---

After the emperor finished speaking, Liang Shicheng announced loudly: "Everyone, kneel and bow to His Majesty in order!"

---

First, the princes walked to the center of the hall and knelt, chanting in unison: "Long live the Emperor!" Shen Jianchen could only follow along, kneeling and shouting.

---

"Rise!" After the princes stepped aside, His Majesty suddenly asked, "Is Prince Yi here?"

---

Shen Jianchen had no choice but to step out from among the princes: "Your son is present."

---

Zhao Ji carefully examined this son he had never met: "You do look quite handsome. Since you were sick from birth, We never let you come to the palace. We were also too busy with state affairs to visit you. Time has flown—you've grown so big. We hear you've returned from the dead. Do tell Us the details, after all, such a thing is rare to witness."

---

Shen Jianchen felt quite dissatisfied. The emperor didn't care about state affairs, but was busy building Genyue and interested in tales of resurrection—truly hopeless! Might as well embellish and make things up, while subtly satirizing the corrupt court.

---

"Father Emperor, listen as your son explains in detail. That day, I felt my breathing grow rapid, and when my last breath wouldn't come, I closed my eyes. I felt my body growing lighter and lighter, floating into the air, rising higher and higher, until I arrived at a very strange place. There were also an emperor and ministers there, but the emperor and ministers there spent all day eating, drinking, and carousing, ignoring the common people. I couldn't stand it and planned to leave. Just then, a divine being appeared and taught me a wonderful set of boxing techniques. I quickly mastered them, beat up the emperor, killed the treacherous ministers, and having nowhere else to go, returned to my body. Then I came back to life, and my illnesses miraculously disappeared."

---

Zhao Ji listened for a long time without saying a word. The other ministers also dared not speak.

---

Qiao Wenxian, standing in the line of scholars, was quite worried. After hesitating for a moment, he stepped forward and said: "Your Majesty, what Prince Yi means is that after he died, he saw a place not as good as the Great Song, and being dissatisfied, he returned. This fully shows Prince Yi's deep attachment to the Great Song's realm. In this humble one's view, Prince Yi's resurrection proves that Your Majesty's governance has won the hearts of the people."

---

Qiao Wenxian's explanation easily dissolved the awkwardness, and the atmosphere relaxed.

---

His Majesty finally showed a smile: "Qiao Wenxian's words please Us. During the banquet, We shall surely reward you with a pot of fine wine."

---

"Thank Your Majesty!" Qiao Wenxian stepped back into the line of scholars.

---

His Majesty said to Prince Yi: "You may also step down."

---

Shen Jianchen had long since not wanted to stay under everyone's gaze. He quickly returned to the line of princes.

---

Then the civil and military officials and the scholars all knelt and bowed to the emperor. After the ceremony ended, everyone was free. Zhao Ji also changed into casual clothes and led everyone to board sedan chairs, heading toward Genyue.

---

The people of the imperial harem, as well as the families of officials, were also permitted and hurried to enjoy the beautiful scenery of Genyue.

---

Arriving at the destination, everyone disembarked from their sedan chairs. Except for the important ministers gathered around the emperor, the others found like-minded companions and traveled in groups of three to five. Shen Jianchen naturally stayed with Qiao Wenxian, and they walked at the back of the group.

---

Qiao Wenxian scolded: "You really scared me to death just now. You can't do that again."

---

Shen Jianchen was dismissive: "I only talked about the place I went to after dying. I didn't mention the emperor or those treacherous ministers. What's there to worry about?"

---

Qiao Wenxian cautioned: "Keep your voice down! Even a fool could understand your meaning. Besides, the emperor and the officials are no fools. You must be careful with your words from now on!"

---

Shen Jianchen had to say: "Alright, I'll be careful from now on."

---

As they spoke, they looked up and saw the words "Genyue" written on a large stone.

Passing through the stone, waves of beautiful scenery came into view: towering peaks with countless plum blossoms; waterfalls cascading down, the pool water clear to the bottom; wild geese floating on the water's surface, resting among the rocks...

There was no end to what could be said, no way to describe it all. Truly exotic flowers and rare grasses, strange beasts and precious creatures—the ultimate extravagance.

---

As Shen Jianchen admired the scenery, he sighed inwardly. This emperor was the most pleasure-seeking person he had ever seen—he wished he could move all the beautiful things in the world into Genyue.

---

Just as he was thinking, he heard a woman's voice: "Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao, may I join your company?"

---

Shen Jianchen stopped. Before him stood a woman in her forties, dignified and beautiful. In her youth, she must have been a graceful figure.

---

Qiao Wenxian replied: "To accompany Madam Wei would be an honor beyond measure."

---

Madam Wei asked again: "And Prince Yi? Are you willing to join us?"

---

Shen Jianchen said: "If Wenyuan is willing, I certainly don't mind."

---

Qiao Wenxian introduced: "This is Madam Wei, the wife of Chancellor Zeng. She is also a famous female poet of our dynasty, ranked in the north alongside Householder Yi'an, and comparable in the south to Householder Youqi. She is a master among women of the boudoir."

---

Shen Jianchen began to blame Shen Liu in his heart. Why didn't he transmigrate to an era that valued martial arts over literature, full of heroes? Then he would feel he had a place to show his abilities. But no, he had to come to the Song Dynasty, which valued literature over martial arts. Either he met treacherous ministers or people who could write poetry, making him afraid to even open his mouth in front of them.

---

Facing Wenyuan's introduction, he couldn't not respond, so he said politely: "I have long heard of Madam Wei's name. To meet you today is an honor beyond measure."

---

Madam Wei smiled: "Prince Yi has long resided in the manor. I only knew your name but had never seen you in person. Today, I finally see the true face of Mount Lu. Let me first congratulate Prince Yi on your new life. If you don't mind, please visit our manor more often in the future."

---

Shen Jianchen replied politely: "If the opportunity arises, I will certainly go."

---

The three walked side by side, admiring the scenery as they went. After a while, Madam Wei asked: "When is Young Master Qiao returning to Lin'an?"

---

"I should already be on the road back to Lin'an today, but I was delayed by the emperor's banquet."

---

"When you return to Lin'an, be sure to help me invite Householder Youqi. Ask her to find time to visit Bianliang, and tell her that there is a Madam Wei in Bianliang who wishes to befriend her."

---

"Rest assured, I will certainly convey Madam Wei's message."

---

Everyone spent a whole morning touring Genyue. When they finally finished, they left and took sedan chairs to Jiying Hall. Jiying Hall was the grand hall in the palace for banqueting ministers. In the most honorable position was a large square table, filled with delicacies, surrounded by drum stools. The emperor, the Crown Prince, and the important ministers all walked to the square table and sat down.

---

Not far from the square table were various small tables, arranged in order for the imperial concubines, princes, other ministers, scholars, and officials' families to sit. Shen Jianchen didn't want to sit with the princes, so he stayed with Qiao Wenxian. The two had just sat down when Madam Wei came over and sat beside them.

---

After the journey through Genyue, Shen Jianchen and Madam Wei had become familiar. He took the initiative to ask: "Why doesn't Madam Wei sit with the other officials' families? Why do you prefer to squeeze in with Wenyuan and me?"

---

Madam Wei smiled: "Why doesn't Prince Yi sit with the other princes? Why do you prefer to squeeze in with Young Master Qiao?"

---

This question caught Shen Jianchen off guard. He answered truthfully: "As you know, I was sick from childhood and rarely left the manor. Therefore, I am not familiar with my imperial brothers and have nothing in common with them."

---

Madam Wei said: "That is my answer as well. Those officials' families, when they sit together, only know how to discuss whose jewelry is expensive, whose clothes and brocades are precious, whose daughter married whom, and whose son took which wife. It simply doesn't suit me, so I can only come over to your side. But why are there only two of you at this table?"

---

Qiao Wenxian sighed: "Ai! It's all Prince Yi's fault. He said those things in front of everyone, making the emperor unhappy. Everyone is avoiding him, and because of that, I can't make friends either."

---

Madam Wei smiled: "Prince Yi must toast me a cup."

---

Shen Jianchen didn't quite understand: "Why?"

---

Madam Wei said: "Because I am not afraid of power and nobility. When everyone else was staying away from you, I still spoke with you."

---

Shen Jianchen immediately picked up the wine pot, filled Madam Wei's cup, and also poured one for himself. He raised his cup: "Madam Wei is a heroine among women—she must be toasted! I'll drain my cup first, drink as you please!" Shen Jianchen drank it all in one gulp.

---

Madam Wei also drank it down in one sip.

---

Qiao Wenxian complained: "You two just focus on drinking and leave me aside. That's really unfair. I have stayed by Prince Yi's side through thick and thin, and even spoke up for you at a critical moment. Shouldn't you toast me a few more cups?"

---

Shen Jianchen said nothing. He waved to the palace maid attending nearby. When she came over, he ordered her to bring three more cups. The maid quickly brought them, and Shen Jianchen filled all four cups.

---

"Wenyuan's deep loyalty and friendship to me—one cup is not enough. I'll drink four cups, and you drink as you please!"

---

With that, he drank all four cups one by one, his expression unchanged.

---

Qiao Wenxian praised: "What a drinking capacity!" He also picked up his cup and drank.

---

Just then, a eunuch came over with a wine pot: "Young Master Qiao, His Majesty rewards you with fine wine."

---

Qiao Wenxian stood and accepted it: "Thank Your Majesty for the gift. I am honored beyond measure."

---

After the eunuch left, Madam Wei said: "Can Prince Yi still drink this pot of fine wine?"

---

Shen Jianchen smiled: "Absolutely no problem!"

---

The three ate, drank, and chatted.

---

The entire banquet passed amidst abundant delicacies. From time to time, dancing girls performed, and women singers played the pipa and sang. It was truly a scene of song and dance, of peace and prosperity.

---

Then Liang Shicheng conveyed the emperor's wish, asking each literary scholar to write a poem praising Genyue. The palace maids quickly brought brush, ink, and paper, setting them up on a neat desk not far away. Each scholar rubbed their hands in anticipation and wrote with their brushes. The maids then presented the poems to the emperor for review. In the end, Madam Wei's poem won first place and received the emperor's praise.

---

The emperor also caught the mood and wanted to paint on the spot. The palace maids cleared the bowls and leftover items from the square table, brought a large piece of clean cloth to spread over it, and quickly arranged the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone. The old minister Cai Jing ground the ink, and Gao Qiu picked up the brush and presented it to the emperor with both hands. After drinking the tea offered by the eunuch, the emperor took the brush and swiftly began to paint on the paper.

---

Before long, a painting titled *Winter Plum and Mountain Birds* was complete. On it, he inscribed a poem:

> *The mountain bird boasts a leisurely grace,*
> *The plum blossoms display their gentle softness.*
> *An eternal pact in ink and color we embrace,*
> *For a thousand autumns, till our heads turn white.*

He added his signature. The palace maids brought several large seals, and he stamped them one by one.

---

The surrounding ministers all praised it extravagantly.

---

The emperor put down the brush: "You people are extremely familiar with Our poetry and painting. We are not interested in your excessive praise. Let Us choose two people to come and appraise it."

---

Zhao Ji looked toward the scholars: "Why not let Qiao Wenxian appraise Our poem? As for the painting, Prince Yi might as well give it a try."

---

Everyone looked toward Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi...

---

Everyone had experienced Qiao Wenxian's abilities—appraising poetry was certainly no problem for him. As for Prince Yi, whether he could appraise the emperor's painting was truly uncertain.

---

Shen Jianchen guessed that his veiled criticism in the grand hall must have displeased the emperor. This imperial father wanted to make him lose face in public. He couldn't refuse under everyone's gaze, so he could only grit his teeth and go through with it.

---
