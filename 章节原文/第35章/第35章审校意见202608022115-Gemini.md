### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 致命误译与网文独白格式修正（对应第46段：埋怨神柳）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰开始在心里埋怨神柳，为何不让他穿越到一个重武轻文全是英雄豪杰的时代？这样他还会觉得自己有用武之地。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen began to blame **Shen Liu** in his heart. Why didn't he transmigrate to an era that valued martial arts over literature, full of heroes? Then he would feel he had a place to show his abilities.
* **修改后 (After):**
    ***Why couldn't you have sent me to an era that valued martial arts over literature?*** Shen Jianchen grumbled inwardly at the **Divine Willow**. ***An era full of genuine heroes, where I’d actually have a place to put my fists to good use!***
* **💡 修改理由:**
    **重要修正！** 初译版将“神柳”直接拼音化成了 "Shen Liu"，这会导致英文读者以为这是一个叫“沈刘”的人。必须统一修改为前面的术语 **Divine Willow**。同时，将叙述性的心理活动改为第一人称的**斜体吐槽 (Italics)**，更符合网文读者的阅读习惯。

#### 2. 男主编故事映射昏君的“爽感”强化（对应第15段）
>
> **【原文】** 那里的官家和臣子整日就知道吃喝玩乐，置百姓于不顾，儿臣看不惯打算离开。这时出现一位神人，教会我一套精妙的拳法，我很快练会，打了官家，杀了奸臣……

* **修改前 (Before):**
    ...but the emperor and ministers there spent all day eating, drinking, and carousing, **ignoring the common people**. I couldn't stand it and planned to leave. Just then, a divine being appeared and taught me a wonderful set of boxing techniques. I quickly mastered them, **beat up the emperor, killed the treacherous ministers**...
* **修改后 (After):**
    ...but the emperor and ministers there did nothing but feast and carouse all day, **leaving the common people to rot**. I couldn't stand the sight of it and was about to leave. Just then, a divine being appeared and taught me a peerless set of fist techniques. I mastered them instantly, **beat the living daylights out of that emperor, slaughtered his treacherous lackeys**...
* **💡 修改理由:**
    男主在朝堂上是在指桑骂槐，语言风格极具颠覆性和爽感。将平淡的 "ignoring the common people" 升级为 "leaving the common people to rot"（任百姓自生自灭）；将 "beat up the emperor" 升级为英文极具画面感的 "beat the living daylights out of that emperor"（把皇帝揍得鼻青脸肿/魂飞魄散），把爽文的张力拉到极致。

#### 3. 容貌白描的自然化（对应第9段：宋徽宗赵佶出场）
>
> **【原文】** 他身材高大，身着红色龙袍，头顶长翅帽，面色白皙，唇上两撇胡须，须眉长目，倒是一位帅哥。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    He was tall, wearing a red dragon robe, a hat with long wings on his head, his face fair, **two mustache strokes on his lips**, with long eyebrows and eyes—he was **quite a handsome man indeed**.
* **修改后 (After):**
    He was tall, dressed in a crimson dragon robe and a winged official's hat. With his fair complexion, **a neatly trimmed mustache**, and elegantly long eyebrows framing striking eyes—he was, **surprisingly, an exceedingly handsome man**.
* **💡 修改理由:**
    直译 "two mustache strokes on his lips" (嘴唇上的两撇胡须) 听起来像用笔画上去的，英文中 "neatly trimmed mustache" 更符合古代帝王修剪得体的形象。"quite a handsome man indeed" 略显平淡，加入 "surprisingly" (竟然/出人意料地)，贴合男主内心“这昏君长得还挺帅”的心理反差。

#### 4. 社交酒桌文化的“豪爽感”翻译（对应第57段：我干了你随意）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰立刻端起酒壶，给魏夫人杯中斟满酒，也给自己倒满，举起酒杯："魏夫人乃女中豪杰，必须敬！我先干了，你随意！"申简辰一饮而尽。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    ...I'll drain my cup first, **drink as you please**!" Shen Jianchen **drank it all in one gulp**.
* **修改后 (After):**
    ...**Bottoms up for me—you may drink at your leisure!**" With that, Shen Jianchen **downed the cup in a single shot**.
* **💡 修改理由:**
    男主前世是CEO，在酒桌上游刃有余。"我干了你随意" 翻译成 "Bottoms up for me—you may drink at your leisure!" 既保留了江湖豪气，又不失对女性的绅士风度。"downed the cup in a single shot" 比 "drank it all in one gulp" 更能凸显他酒量惊人、动作干脆。

#### 5. 结尾压轴的心理危机感刻画（对应第73段）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰猜测定是自己在大殿之上映射之词引起官家不满，这位父皇是要以此让他当众丢脸，众目睽睽之下不能拒绝，只能硬着头皮上。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen guessed that his veiled criticism in the grand hall must have displeased the emperor. This imperial father wanted to make him lose face in public. He couldn't refuse under everyone's gaze, so he could only **grit his teeth and go through with it**.
* **修改后 (After):**
    ***That veiled criticism in the main hall definitely pissed him off,*** Shen Jianchen concluded instantly. ***This so-called 'imperial father' wants to publicly humiliate me!*** But with hundreds of eyes pinned on him, he couldn't exactly refuse. He had no choice but to **bite the bullet and step up**.
* **💡 修改理由:**
    将结尾的心理活动全部转换为**斜体独白**，使用 "pissed him off" 还原男主的现代人大白话思维。将“硬着头皮上”翻译为英文绝佳的习惯用语 "bite the bullet and step up"（咬紧牙关硬上），为下一章的“装逼打脸”情节完美铺垫出强烈的悬念与紧张感！
