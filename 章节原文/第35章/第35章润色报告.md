## 三、感官/画面增强（原文有描写处）

- **艮岳美景段**：原译平铺直叙，改 "wave upon wave of beauty unfolded before them / crowned with countless plum blossoms / pools of jade-green water clear to the bottom / No words could capture it all / a garden of rare blossoms and exotic beasts, luxurious beyond compare"。
- **魏夫人初见**："dignified and beautiful... a graceful figure" → "dignified and lovely... a vision of grace"。
- **集英殿布席**："a large square table, filled with delicacies, surrounded by drum stools" → "a large square table laden with delicacies, ringed by drum-shaped stools"。
- **宴乐段**："dancing girls performed... women singers played the pipa and sang... a scene of song and dance" → "dancers took the floor... singing girls plucked the pipa and lifted their voices... a tableau of music and dance, of an age at peace and in prosperity"。

## 四、官家称谓一致性（叙述层统一为 His Majesty）

将叙述层（非角色私密对话）中对"官家"的指代统一为 "His Majesty"（共 7 处：跪拜、换常服、围拢、集英殿、赏诗、当场泼墨×2、撂笔、宴请）。角色私密对话/内心独白中保留 "the emperor"（如申简辰与乔文仙私语、申简辰内心想法），符合英语自然语域。
