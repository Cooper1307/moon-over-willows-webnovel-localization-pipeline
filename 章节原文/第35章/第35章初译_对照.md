# 第35章 映射官家赏艮岳 / Satirizing the Emperor and Appreciating Genyue

---

其他人向两边退去，皇子们则聚集到中间排好队。

The others stepped aside, while the princes gathered in the center and lined up.

---

乔文仙推了仪王一把，申简辰只好向皇子的队伍走去。

Qiao Wenxian gave Prince Yi a nudge, and Shen Jianchen had no choice but to walk toward the line of princes.

---

官家的儿子的确不少，按照从大到小排列，太子站在最前面，他看上去大约二十多岁，最后一排大的也就十岁左右，最小的看上去只有三四岁。

His Majesty certainly had no shortage of sons. Lined up from eldest to youngest, the Crown Prince stood at the front, looking to be in his twenties; the last row held boys no older than ten, the smallest of them barely three or four.

---

申简辰心想，赵朴是官家十三子，应该站在中间一排，便迅速挤进去。

Shen Jianchen thought, *Zhao Pu is the thirteenth son—he should be standing in the middle row.* He quickly squeezed in.

---

所有皇子在太子带领下走进大殿内，接着是文武百官，最后是文人才子。

Led by the Crown Prince, all the princes entered the grand hall, followed by the civil and military officials, and finally the literary scholars.

---

申简辰站在皇子中，向前瞅瞅，只看到前两排皇子的后脑勺；再用余光向两边瞧瞧，皇子们一个个面无表情。他甚觉无趣，干脆瞻仰起帝王的宝座。

Standing among the princes, Shen Jianchen peeked ahead and could see only the napes of the two rows before him. A glance to either side revealed every face a blank mask. Bored, he contented himself with studying the imperial throne.

---

宝座应是用珍贵的红木雕刻而成，底座宽阔而稳固，靠背上覆盖锦绣，扶手为龙形，雕刻得栩栩如生，烘托出皇帝的高贵身份。

The throne looked to be carved from precious rosewood. Its base was broad and steady, its backrest draped in brocade, the armrests wrought into coiling dragons so lifelike they bespoke the emperor's exalted station.

---

传来梁师成的声音："官家驾到！"

Liang Shicheng's voice rang out: "His Majesty has arrived!"

---

申简辰的视线离开龙椅，刚好瞧见走向龙椅的赵佶。他身材高大，身着红色龙袍，头顶长翅帽，面色白皙，唇上两撇胡须，须眉长目，倒是一位帅哥。

Shen Jianchen's gaze left the dragon throne just in time to see Zhao Ji approaching it. He was tall, clad in a crimson dragon robe and the long-winged hat. Fair-skinned, with a neatly trimmed mustache, long arched brows, and clear, penetrating eyes, he was, surprisingly, quite the handsome man.

---

官家在龙椅上坐定后，双目环顾殿内所有人，慢条斯理地说："大家不必紧张，放轻松些，今日又不是上朝。寡人还等着带你们欣赏艮岳美景，然后定要吃好喝好玩好，文人才子们也要尽情展示你们的才华。"

After settling onto the dragon throne, His Majesty gazed at everyone in the hall and said slowly, "There's no need to be nervous. Relax. Today is not a court session. We are eager to show you the beauty of Genyue. Then we shall eat well, drink well, and make merry. The literary scholars must display your talents to the fullest."

[TN: "寡人" (guǎ rén), literally "the lonely one," was the humble self-reference used by pre-Qin and Song Dynasty emperors. It is translated as "We" to convey imperial authority while maintaining historical flavor.]

---

官家说完，梁师成大声宣道："请大家依次跪拜官家！"

After His Majesty finished speaking, Liang Shicheng announced loudly: "Everyone, kneel and bow to His Majesty in order!"

---

先是皇子们走到大殿中间跪拜，都齐声称道："父皇万岁！"申简辰只能跟着又跪又喊。

First, the princes walked to the center of the hall and knelt, chanting in unison: "Long live the Emperor!" He had no choice but to join in, dropping to his knees and lifting his own cry.

---

"平身吧！"当皇子们退到一边后，官家突然问，"仪王可来？"

"Rise!" After the princes stepped aside, His Majesty suddenly asked, "Is Prince Yi here?"

---

申简辰不得已从皇子中走出："儿臣在。"

He had no choice but to step out from among the princes: "Your son is here."

---

赵佶将这位从未谋面的儿子仔细瞧了瞧："倒是长得一表人才，因你从生下来就病着，寡人从未让你来过宫里，也因政务繁忙，没时间去看你，一晃都长这么大。听说你起死回生，不妨给寡人讲讲具体细节，毕竟这种事情难得一见。"

Zhao Ji carefully examined this son he had never met: "You do look quite handsome. Since you were sick from birth, We never let you come to the palace. We were also too busy with state affairs to visit you. Time has flown—you've grown so big. We hear you've returned from the dead. Do tell Us the details, after all, such a thing is rare to witness."

---

申简辰心里很不满，官家不关心国家政事，又是建造艮岳，又是对起死回生之说感兴趣，真是没得救！不如给他添油加醋瞎说一通，顺便映射一下腐败的朝廷。

Shen Jianchen felt a fresh surge of displeasure. *The emperor cares nothing for affairs of state—he busies himself building Genyue and fancies these tales of resurrection. Truly beyond hope!* he thought. *I might as well spin a wild tale and needle this corrupt court while I'm at it.*

---

"父皇在上，听儿臣细细道来。那日我只觉呼吸急促，最后一口气上不来，就闭了眼，感觉身体越来越轻，飘到空中，越飘越高，来到一个很奇怪的地方，也有官家和臣子，可那里的官家和臣子整日就知道吃喝玩乐，置百姓于不顾，儿臣看不惯打算离开。这时出现一位神人，教会我一套精妙的拳法，我很快练会，打了官家，杀了奸臣，无处可去又回到身体里，人就活过来，疾病也奇迹般消失。"

"Father Emperor, hear your son's tale. That day, my breath grew labored; when my last breath would not come, I closed my eyes. My body grew lighter and lighter, drifting into the air, rising higher and higher, until I reached a most peculiar realm. There were an emperor and his ministers there too—but that emperor and his ministers did nothing but feast and carouse all day, leaving the common people to their fate. I could not abide it and resolved to leave. Then a deity appeared and taught me a superb set of fist techniques. I mastered them at once, beat that emperor and cut down his treacherous ministers. With nowhere else to go, I returned to my body—and woke to find myself alive, my illness miraculously gone."

---

赵佶听了半天没言语，其他臣子也不敢说话。

Zhao Ji listened for a long time without saying a word. The other ministers also dared not speak.

---

站在才子队伍中的乔文仙甚是担心，犹豫片刻走出来说："官家，仪王的意思是他死后，看到一个不如大宋的地方，很不满，就回来了，足以说明仪王对大宋的天下十分眷恋，在草民看来仪王起死回生之事，正说明官家治理的天下深得人心。"

Qiao Wenxian, standing in the line of scholars, was quite worried. After hesitating for a moment, he stepped forward and said: "Your Majesty, what Prince Yi means is that after he died, he saw a place not as good as the Great Song, and being dissatisfied, he returned. This fully shows Prince Yi's deep attachment to the Great Song's realm. As your humble servant sees it, Prince Yi's resurrection proves that Your Majesty's governance has won the hearts of the people."

---

乔文仙的一番解释轻松化解尴尬，气氛变得缓和。

Qiao Wenxian's words dissolved the awkwardness, and the mood eased.

---

官家终于露出笑脸："乔文仙的话令寡人满意，宴饮时，寡人定赏你一壶美酒。"

His Majesty finally broke into a smile: "Qiao Wenxian's words please Us. During the banquet, We shall surely reward you with a pot of fine wine."

---

"谢官家！"乔文仙又退回到才子队伍中。

"Thank Your Majesty!" Qiao Wenxian stepped back into the line of scholars.

---

官家对仪王说："你也退下吧。"

His Majesty said to Prince Yi: "You may also step down."

---

申简辰早已不想在众目睽睽之下待着，很快回到皇子的队伍中。

He had no wish to linger under so many watching eyes, and quickly slipped back into the princes' ranks.

---

接着文武百官和才子们也都跪拜官家。礼拜结束后，众人获得自由，赵佶也换上常服，带领众人坐上轿子，往艮岳而去。

Then the civil and military officials and the scholars all knelt and bowed to His Majesty. After the ceremony ended, everyone was free. Zhao Ji also changed into everyday robes and led everyone aboard the sedan chairs, heading toward Genyue.

---

官家后宫之人，以及官宦家眷也被允许，纷纷赶来享受艮岳美景。

The ladies of the inner palace, along with the families of court officials, were likewise permitted to attend, and they flocked to take in Genyue's splendor.

---

到达目的地，众人下了轿子，除了围在官家身边的要臣外，其他人则找趣味相投之人，三五人结伴而行。申简辰自然与乔文仙在一起，他们走在队伍的后面。

Arriving at their destination, everyone alighted from their sedan chairs. Except for the important ministers clustered around His Majesty, the others sought out like-minded companions and wandered in groups of three or five. Shen Jianchen naturally stayed with Qiao Wenxian, and the two trailed at the rear of the party.

---

乔文仙批评道："刚才真是吓死我了，以后可不能再如此。"

Qiao Wenxian reproached him: "You nearly scared me to death just now. You must never do that again."

---

申简辰不以为然："我只说起死回生前去的地方，又没说官家和那些奸臣，你担心什么？"

Shen Jianchen was dismissive: "I only talked about the place I went to after dying. I didn't mention the emperor or those treacherous ministers. What's there to worry about?"

---

乔文仙谨慎道："小声些！傻子都能听出你的意思，再说官家与文武百官可都不是傻子，以后还需慎言！"

Qiao Wenxian cautioned: "Keep your voice down! Even a fool could understand your meaning. Besides, the emperor and the officials are no fools. You must be careful with your words from now on!"

---

申简辰只好说："好吧，我以后注意。"

Shen Jianchen relented: "Alright, I'll be careful from now on."

---

正说着，抬头见一个大石上写着"艮岳"两字。穿过大石，一波一波的美景映入眼帘：有高峰耸立，梅花万数；有瀑布飞流直下，池水碧青见底；亦有凫雁浮泳水面，栖息石间……，说也说不完，道也道不尽，真是奇花异草，奇珍异兽，极尽奢华。

As they spoke, they looked up and saw the words "Genyue" written on a large stone.

Passing through the stone, wave upon wave of beauty unfolded before them: towering peaks crowned with countless plum blossoms; waterfalls cascading down into pools of jade-green water clear to the bottom; ducks and geese drifting on the surface, resting among the rocks...

No words could capture it all. Truly a garden of rare blossoms and exotic beasts, luxurious beyond compare.

[TN: Genyue (艮岳) was an imperial garden built by Emperor Huizong of Song. It was famous for its elaborate landscaping and rare plants, but its construction drained the imperial treasury and burdened the people with heavy taxes.]

---

申简辰边欣赏边在心里慨叹，这位官家是他见过的最会享受之人，恨不得把天底下所有美的事物统统搬到艮岳之内。

As Shen Jianchen admired the scenery, he sighed inwardly. *This emperor is the most pleasure-seeking man I have ever seen,* he thought. *He would move every beautiful thing in the world into Genyue if he could.*

---

正想着，听到一个女声："仪王，乔公子，可否与你们结伴而行？"

Just as he was thinking, he heard a woman's voice: "Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao, may I join your company?"

---

申简辰站住，眼前出现一位妇人，看上去四十多岁，端庄秀丽，年轻时定是位亭亭玉立的女子。

Shen Jianchen stopped. Before him stood a woman in her forties, dignified and lovely. In her youth she must have been a vision of grace.

---

乔文仙回道："能与魏夫人同行，文渊不胜荣幸。"

Qiao Wenxian replied: "To accompany Lady Wei would be an honor beyond measure."

---

魏夫人又问："仪王呢？可愿与我结伴而行？"

Lady Wei asked again: "And Prince Yi? Are you willing to join us?"

---

申简辰说："文渊乐意，我当然不介意。"

Shen Jianchen said: "If Wenyuan is willing, I certainly don't mind."

---

乔文仙介绍道："这位是魏夫人，乃曾丞相的家眷，也是我朝有名的女词人，北可与易安居士齐名，南可与幽栖居士媲美，是闺阁中的大家。"

Qiao Wenxian introduced: "This is Lady Wei, the wife of Chancellor Zeng. She is also a celebrated poet of our dynasty—ranked in the north alongside Yi'an Jushi, and matched in the south by Youqi Jushi. She is a luminary of the women's quarters."

[TN: Yi'an Jushi (易安居士) was the pseudonym of Li Qingzhao (李清照), one of the most celebrated female poets in Chinese history. Youqi Jushi (幽栖居士) was the pseudonym of Zhu Shuzhen (朱淑真), another renowned female poet. Both terms are transliterated as they are literary pseudonyms, not religious titles.]

---

申简辰开始在心里埋怨神柳，为何不让他穿越到一个重武轻文全是英雄豪杰的时代？这样他还会觉得自己有用武之地。偏偏来到重文轻武的宋朝，不是遇到大奸臣，就是遇到会写诗词之人，搞得他在这些人面前都不敢轻易张嘴。

Shen Jianchen silently reproached the Divine Willow. *Why couldn't you have sent me to an age that prized martial prowess over letters—an age of heroes?* he thought. *Then I would have a place to prove myself. But no—I had to land in the Song Dynasty, which prizes letters over martial prowess. I keep meeting either treacherous ministers or poets, and it makes me afraid to even open my mouth before them.*

---

面对文渊的介绍，又不能不回应，客气地说："久闻魏夫人大名，今日得以相见，不胜荣幸。"

Pressed to reply to Wenyuan's introduction, he said politely: "I have long heard of Lady Wei's name. To meet you today is an honor beyond measure."

---

魏夫人笑道："仪王久居王府，我也只知你的大名，没见过人，今日终于见到庐山真面目，先祝贺仪王得以新生，仪王若不嫌弃，往后多到我府上走动走动。"

Lady Wei smiled: "Prince Yi has long resided in the manor. I only knew your name but had never seen you in person. Today, I finally see the true face of Mount Lu. Let me first congratulate Prince Yi on your new life. If you don't mind, please visit our manor more often in the future."

[TN: "见到庐山真面目" (seeing the true face of Mount Lu) is a Chinese idiom meaning to finally see someone's true appearance or the truth of a matter. It comes from a poem by Su Shi.]

---

申简辰客气道："如若有机会定去。"

He replied politely: "If the opportunity arises, I will certainly go."

---

三人并肩走着，边走边欣赏美景。走了一会儿，魏夫人问："乔公子什么时候回临安？"

The three walked side by side, admiring the scenery as they went. After a while, Lady Wei asked: "When is Young Master Qiao returning to Lin'an?"

---

"本应今日已在回临安的路上，怎奈官家宴请，耽搁下来。"

"I should already be on the road back to Lin'an today, but I was delayed by His Majesty's banquet."

---

"如若回到临安，定要帮我邀请幽栖居士，让她抽时间来趟汴梁，顺便告诉她汴梁有位魏夫人想与她结交。"

"When you return to Lin'an, be sure to help me invite Youqi Jushi. Ask her to find time to visit Bianliang, and tell her that there is a Lady Wei in Bianliang who wishes to befriend her."

---

"放心，我定帮魏夫人转达。"

"Rest assured, I will certainly convey Lady Wei's message."

---

大家在艮岳足足逛了一上午，终于逛完，出了艮岳又乘坐轿子来到集英殿。集英殿是宫中宴请群臣的大殿，最尊贵的位置摆着一张大大的方桌，上面放满了珍馐佳肴，周围则摆着鼓墩。只见官家、太子与重要的臣子都走到方桌旁坐下。

Everyone spent a whole morning touring Genyue. When they finally finished, they left and took sedan chairs to Jiying Hall, the palace's great hall for banqueting the court. At the place of highest honor stood a large square table laden with delicacies, ringed by drum-shaped stools. His Majesty, the Crown Prince, and the important ministers all walked to the square table and took their seats.

[TN: Jiying Hall (集英殿) was one of the main halls in the Song Dynasty palace, used for grand banquets and literary gatherings.]

---

离方桌不远处，则安置着各色小桌，依次供后宫妃嫔、皇子、其他大臣、才子，以及官眷就坐。申简辰不愿与皇子们一道，依然跟着乔文仙。他们两个刚坐下，魏夫人过来，坐在他们旁边。

Not far from the square table stood a scattering of smaller tables, arranged in order for the imperial concubines, princes, other ministers, scholars, and officials' families. He had no wish to sit with the princes, so he stayed with Qiao Wenxian. The two had barely sat down when Lady Wei came over and settled beside them.

---

经过艮岳一路，申简辰与魏夫人已熟，主动开口问："魏夫人为何不与各位官眷同桌？偏偏喜欢与我和文渊挤在一起？"

After the journey through Genyue, Shen Jianchen and Lady Wei had become familiar. He took the initiative to ask: "Why doesn't Lady Wei sit with the other officials' families? Why do you prefer to squeeze in with Wenyuan and me?"

---

魏夫人笑道："仪王为何不与皇子们同桌？偏偏与乔公子挤在一处？"

Lady Wei smiled: "Why doesn't Prince Yi sit with the other princes? Why do you prefer to squeeze in with Young Master Qiao?"

---

这话倒把申简辰问住，照实说道："你也知道，我从小病着，很少出王府，故而与皇兄皇弟们不熟，没有共同语言。"

This question caught him off guard. He answered truthfully: "As you know, I was sick from childhood and rarely left the manor. Therefore, I am not familiar with my imperial brothers and have nothing in common with them."

---

魏夫人说："这便是我的答案，那些官眷坐在一起，只懂得讨论谁的首饰贵重，谁的衣裳锦缎珍贵，还有谁家女儿嫁给谁，谁家儿子又娶了谁，跟我实在不合，只能跑到你们这里来。不过，为何这一桌只有你们两个？"

Lady Wei said: "That is my answer as well. Those officials' families, when they sit together, only know how to discuss whose jewelry is expensive, whose clothes and brocades are precious, whose daughter married whom, and whose son took which wife. It simply doesn't suit me, so I can only come over to your side. But why are there only two of you at this table?"

---

乔文仙叹道："唉！还不都怪仪王，当着众人的面说出那些话，让官家不高兴，大家都避之不及，害得我也交不到朋友。"

Qiao Wenxian sighed: "Ai! It's all Prince Yi's fault. He said those things in front of everyone, making the emperor unhappy. Everyone is avoiding him, and because of that, I can't make friends either."

---

魏夫人笑道："仪王得敬我一杯。"

Lady Wei smiled: "Prince Yi must toast me a cup."

---

申简辰不大明白："为何？"

He didn't quite understand: "Why?"

---

魏夫人说："只因我不畏权贵，在众人远离你时，还与你说话。"

Lady Wei said: "Because I am not afraid of power and nobility. When everyone else was staying away from you, I still spoke with you."

---

申简辰立刻端起酒壶，给魏夫人杯中斟满酒，也给自己倒满，举起酒杯："魏夫人乃女中豪杰，必须敬！我先干了，你随意！"申简辰一饮而尽。

He immediately picked up the wine pot, filled Lady Wei's cup, and also poured one for himself. He raised his cup: "Lady Wei is a heroine among women—she must be toasted! I'll drain my cup first, drink as you please!" He downed it in one gulp.

---

魏夫人也一口喝下。

Lady Wei also drank it down in one sip.

---

乔文仙不满道："你们两个只顾喝酒，把我晾到一边，实在不公平，我可是对仪王不离不弃，关键时候还替你说话，你是不是得多敬我几杯？"

Qiao Wenxian complained: "You two just focus on drinking and leave me out in the cold. That's really unfair. I never left Prince Yi's side, and even spoke up for him at a critical moment. Shouldn't you toast me a few more cups?"

---

申简辰二话不说，向旁边伺候的宫女挥挥手，等宫女过来，吩咐她再拿三个酒杯。宫女很快拿来，申简辰将四个酒杯倒满。

He said nothing. He waved to the palace maid standing nearby. When she came over, he ordered her to bring three more cups. The maid quickly brought them, and he filled all four cups.

---

"文渊对我情深义重，一杯怎么能够？我喝四杯，你随意！"

"Wenyuan's deep loyalty and friendship to me—one cup is not enough. I'll drink four cups, and you drink as you please!"

---

说着将四杯酒一一喝下，竟然面不改色。

With that, he drank all four cups one by one, without so much as a flicker of expression.

---

乔文仙赞道："好酒量！"也拿起自己的酒杯喝了。

Qiao Wenxian praised: "What a drinking capacity!" He also picked up his cup and drank.

---

这时一位内侍端着酒壶过来："乔公子，官家赏你的美酒。"

Just then, a eunuch came over with a wine pot: "Young Master Qiao, His Majesty rewards you with fine wine."

---

乔文仙起身接过："感谢官家恩赐，文渊不胜荣幸。"

Qiao Wenxian stood and accepted it: "Thank Your Majesty for the gift. I am honored beyond measure."

---

内侍离开后，魏夫人说："仪王可还喝得下这壶美酒？"

After the eunuch left, Lady Wei said: "Can Prince Yi still drink this pot of fine wine?"

---

申简辰笑道："绝不在话下！"

He smiled: "Absolutely no problem!"

---

三人吃着、喝着、聊着。

The three ate, drank, and chatted.

---

整个宴会在丰盛的珍馐美味中度过，不时有舞伎表演跳舞，也有唱曲儿的女子手握琵琶弹唱，真是一派歌舞升平。

The entire banquet unfolded amid a profusion of delicacies. From time to time, dancers took the floor, and singing girls plucked the pipa and lifted their voices. It was truly a tableau of music and dance, of an age at peace and in prosperity.

---

接着梁师成传达官家的意思，让文人才子各写一首赞美艮岳的诗词。很快宫女们拿来笔墨，在不远处整齐的书桌上摆好。文人才子个个摩拳擦掌提笔写下，再由宫女呈给官家阅览，最终魏夫人的诗词取得头筹，得到官家赞赏。

Then Liang Shicheng conveyed His Majesty's wish, asking each literary scholar to write a poem in praise of Genyue. The palace maids quickly brought brush, ink, and paper, setting them up on a neat desk not far away. Each scholar rubbed his hands in eager anticipation and wrote with his brush. The maids then presented the poems to His Majesty for review. In the end, Lady Wei's poem won first place and received His Majesty's praise.

---

官家也来了兴致，要当场泼墨，宫女们纷纷撤去方桌上的碗碟和吃剩的杂物，拿来一大块干净的布子铺上，迅速摆好笔墨纸砚，老臣蔡京研磨，高俅拿起笔双手捧到官家面前。官家喝完内侍捧的茶后，接过笔，迅速在纸上挥毫起来。

His Majesty, too, caught the mood and wanted to paint on the spot. The palace maids cleared the bowls and leftovers from the square table, brought a large piece of clean cloth to spread over it, and quickly arranged the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone. The old minister Cai Jing ground the ink, and Gao Qiu picked up the brush and presented it to His Majesty with both hands. After drinking the tea offered by the eunuch, His Majesty took the brush and swiftly began to paint.

[TN: Cai Jing (蔡京) was a powerful and controversial chancellor during Emperor Huizong's reign, known for his calligraphy but also for corruption. Gao Qiu (高俅) was a military official, later portrayed as a villain in *Water Margin*.]

---

没多时，一幅《腊梅山禽图》已成，并在上面题下一首诗："山禽矜逸态，梅粉弄轻柔。已有丹青约，千秋指白头。"写了落款。宫女拿来数方大印，一一盖了。

Before long, a painting titled *Winter Plum and Mountain Birds* was complete. On it, he inscribed a poem:

> *The mountain bird boasts a leisurely grace,*
> *The plum blossoms display their gentle softness.*
> *An eternal pact in ink and color we embrace,*
> *For a thousand autumns, till our heads turn white.*

He added his signature. The palace maids brought several large seals, and he stamped them one by one.

[TN: This poem and painting are based on a real historical work by Emperor Huizong of Song. The painting *Winter Plum and Mountain Birds* (《腊梅山禽图》) is one of his most famous surviving works, showcasing his artistic talent alongside his political failures.]

---

周围的臣子无不赞叹有加。

The surrounding ministers all praised it extravagantly.

---

官家撂下笔："你们这些人对寡人的诗画极其熟悉，溢美之词寡人不感兴趣，还是让寡人挑两人前来品鉴一番。"

His Majesty put down the brush: "You people are extremely familiar with Our poetry and painting. We are not interested in your excessive praise. Let Us choose two people to come and appraise it."

---

赵佶瞧向文人才子处："不如让乔文仙品品寡人的诗，至于画，仪王不妨品鉴一番。"

Zhao Ji looked toward the scholars: "Why not let Qiao Wenxian appraise Our poem? As for the painting, Prince Yi might as well give it a try."

---

众人都看向乔文仙和仪王……

Everyone looked toward Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi...

---

乔文仙的水平大家领教过，品诗绝不在话下；至于仪王，是否能品得了帝王之画，真不好说。

Everyone had experienced Qiao Wenxian's abilities—appraising poetry was certainly no problem for him. As for Prince Yi, whether he could appraise His Majesty's painting was truly uncertain.

---

申简辰猜测定是自己在大殿之上映射之词引起官家不满，这位父皇是要以此让他当众丢脸，众目睽睽之下不能拒绝，只能硬着头皮上。

Shen Jianchen guessed that his veiled criticism in the grand hall had displeased the emperor. *This so-called imperial father means to humiliate me in public,* he thought. *But with all eyes upon me, I cannot refuse. I will simply have to grit my teeth and see it through.*
