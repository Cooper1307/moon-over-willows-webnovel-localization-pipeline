第35章 映射官家赏艮岳
## Chapter 35: Veiled Barbs and Touring Genyue

其他人向两边退去，皇子们则聚集到中间排好队。
Officials and scholars parted to both sides while imperial princes formed neat ranks down the center.

乔文仙推了仪王一把，申简辰只好向皇子的队伍走去。
Nudged forward by Qiao Wenxian, Shen Jianchen walked over to join his brothers.

官家的儿子的确不少，按照从大到小排列，太子站在最前面，他看上去大约二十多岁，最后一排大的也就十岁左右，最小的看上去只有三四岁。
Huizong sure had plenty of sons! Lined up oldest to youngest, the Crown Prince led the front row looking mid-twenties, while the back rows held ten-year-olds down to toddlers barely three or four.

申简辰心想，赵朴是官家十三子，应该站在中间一排，便迅速挤进去。
Thirteenth son—somewhere right in the middle! Shen Jianchen slipped straight into the center row.

所有皇子在太子带领下走进大殿内，接着是文武百官，最后是文人才子。
Led by the Crown Prince, the princes filed into the hall, followed by civil and military officials, and scholars last.

申简辰站在皇子中，向前瞅瞅，只看到前两排皇子的后脑勺；再用余光向两边瞧瞧，皇子们一个个面无表情。他甚觉无趣，干脆瞻仰起帝王的宝座。
Standing mid-pack, Shen Jianchen saw only the backs of older brothers' heads ahead and blank, expressionless faces on either side. Bored already, he studied the imperial throne instead.

宝座应是用珍贵的红木雕刻而成，底座宽阔而稳固，靠背上覆盖锦绣，扶手为龙形，雕刻得栩栩如生，烘托出皇帝的高贵身份。
Carved from prized red sandalwood on a wide, massive dais, draped in gold brocade with lifelike dragon armrests, the seat radiated imperial majesty.

传来梁师成的声音：“官家驾到！”
Liang Shicheng's cry rang out: "His Majesty arrives!"

申简辰的视线离开龙椅，刚好瞧见走向龙椅的赵佶。他身材高大，身着红色龙袍，头顶长翅帽，面色白皙，唇上两撇胡须，须眉长目，倒是一位帅哥。
Shen Jianchen shifted his eyes as Zhao Ji strode up to the throne—tall build, crimson dragon robes, long-winged court cap, pale refined features, neat mustache, fine brows and eyes—actually quite a handsome guy!

官家在龙椅上坐定后，双目环顾殿内所有人，慢条斯理地说：“大家不必紧张，放轻松些，今日又不是上朝。寡人还等着带你们欣赏艮岳美景，然后定要吃好喝好玩好，文人才子们也要尽情展示你们的才华。”
Settling onto the dragon throne, His Majesty swept a leisurely gaze across the hall. "Relax, everyone—no formal court session today! I'm eager to show you the wonders of Genyue, followed by fine food, fine wine, and merrymaking! And you scholars—be ready to shine!"

官家说完，梁师成大声宣道：“请大家依次跪拜官家！”
At his cue, Liang Shicheng announced: "Kneel and pay homage to His Majesty in order!"

先是皇子们走到大殿中间跪拜，都齐声称道：“父皇万岁！”申简辰只能跟着又跪又喊。
Princes stepped into the center first, dropping to their knees in unison: "Long live Imperial Father!" Shen Jianchen knelt and chanted right along.

“平身吧！”当皇子们退到一边后，官家突然问，“仪王可来？”
"Rise!" As the princes stepped back into rank, His Majesty spoke up: "Did Prince Yi come?"

申简辰不得已从皇子中走出：“儿臣在。”
Stepping out from the middle row, Shen Jianchen bowed: "Your son is here."

赵佶将这位从未谋面的儿子仔细瞧了瞧：“倒是长得一表人才，因你从生下来就病着，寡人从未让你来过宫里，也因政务繁忙，没时间去看你，一晃都长这么大。听说你起死回生，不妨给寡人讲讲具体细节，毕竟这种事情难得一见。”
Zhao Ji looked over the son he had never seen face to face: "Fine bearing indeed! Sickly from birth, I never summoned you to court, and state affairs kept me from visiting—blink and you're grown! Heard you returned from the dead—tell me every detail! Rare marvel like that deserves hearing."

申简辰心里很不满，官家不关心国家政事，又是建造艮岳，又是对起死回生之说感兴趣，真是没得救！不如给他添油加醋瞎说一通，顺便映射一下腐败的朝廷。
Disgust stirred in Shen Jianchen—ignoring state crises to build pleasure parks and chase ghost stories! Hopeless ruler! Why not spin a spicy tall tale poking straight at his rotten court?

﻿“父皇在上，听儿臣细细道来。那日我只觉呼吸急促，最后一口气上不来，就闭了眼，感觉身体越来越轻，飘到空中，越飘越高，来到一个很奇怪的地方，也有官家和臣子，可那里的官家和臣子整日就知道吃喝玩乐，置百姓于不顾，儿臣看不惯打算离开。这时出现一位神人，教会我一套精妙的拳法，我很快练会，打了官家，杀了奸臣，无处可去又回到身体里，人就活过来，疾病也奇迹般消失。”
"Hear your son's account, Imperial Father. That day my breath grew short; gasping my last, I shut my eyes and felt light as air, floating higher and higher until I landed in a strange world! It had a sovereign and ministers too—only that ruler and his court did nothing all day but feast and play while ignoring the common people! Disgusted, I turned to leave—when a divine sage appeared and taught me a peerless boxing art! Mastering it in a flash, I beat that sovereign, slew his treacherous officials, and with nowhere left to go, dropped straight back into my body—waking alive with every illness gone!"

赵佶听了半天没言语，其他臣子也不敢说话。
Dead silence fell over the throne—not a minister dared breathe.

站在才子队伍中的乔文仙甚是担心，犹豫片刻走出来说：“官家，仪王的意思是他死后，看到一个不如大宋的地方，很不满，就回来了，足以说明仪王对大宋的天下十分眷恋，在草民看来仪王起死回生之事，正说明官家治理的天下深得人心。”
Breaking into cold sweat back in the scholars' rank, Qiao Wenxian hesitated one heartbeat before stepping out with cupped hands: "Your Majesty, Prince Yi means that after death he glimpsed a realm far inferior to our Great Song, felt repelled, and hurried back! Proof of his deep devotion to our Song empire—his miraculous revival confirms how deeply Your Majesty's reign wins all hearts!"

乔文仙的一番解释轻松化解尴尬，气氛变得缓和。
Smooth spin melted the freeze on the spot.

官家终于露出笑脸：“乔文仙的话令寡人满意，宴饮时，寡人定赏你一壶美酒。”
Zhao Ji broke into a pleased grin: "Well put, Qiao Wenxian! At the feast, I reward you a pot of imperial fine wine!"

“谢官家！”乔文仙又退回到才子队伍中。
"Thank you, Your Majesty!" Qiao Wenxian backed smoothly into the scholars' line.

官家对仪王说：“你也退下吧。”
His Majesty waved at Prince Yi: "Step back too."

申简辰早已不想在众目睽睽之下待着，很快回到皇子的队伍中。
Glad to dodge the spotlight, Shen Jianchen slipped back among the princes.

接着文武百官和才子们也都跪拜官家。礼拜结束后，众人获得自由，赵佶也换上常服，带领众人坐上轿子，往艮岳而去。
Next civil officials, military brass, and scholars kowtowed in turn. Ceremonies done, everyone relaxed; changing into informal robes, Zhao Ji led the crowd out to sedan chairs bound for Genyue.

官家后宫之人，以及官宦家眷也被允许，纷纷赶来享受艮岳美景。
Palace consorts and officials' wives joined too, streaming toward the imperial gardens.

到达目的地，众人下了轿子，除了围在官家身边的要臣外，其他人则找趣味相投之人，三五人结伴而行。申简辰自然与乔文仙在一起，他们走在队伍的后面。
Arriving and dismounting, senior favorites clustered tight around the sovereign while everyone else split into congenial small groups. Naturally Shen Jianchen fell in beside Qiao Wenxian toward the rear.

乔文仙批评道：“刚才真是吓死我了，以后可不能再如此。”
Qiao Wenxian hissed in low rebuke: "Nearly scared the soul out of me back there! Never pull that again!"

申简辰不以为然：“我只说起死回生前去的地方，又没说官家和那些奸臣，你担心什么？”
Unbothered, Shen Jianchen shrugged: "Only talked about some place I saw while dead—never mentioned His Majesty or his cronies! What's there to panic over?"

乔文仙谨慎道：“小声些！傻子都能听出你的意思，再说官家与文武百官可都不是傻子，以后还需慎言！”
"Keep your voice down! Any fool caught your drift—and neither His Majesty nor these court foxes are fools! Watch your tongue from now on!"

申简辰只好说：“好吧，我以后注意。”
"Fine, fine—I'll watch it."

正说着，抬头见一个大石上写着“艮岳”两字。穿过大石，一波一波的美景映入眼帘：有高峰耸立，梅花万数；有瀑布飞流直下，池水碧青见底；亦有凫雁浮泳水面，栖息石间……，说也说不完，道也道不尽，真是奇花异草，奇珍异兽，极尽奢华。
Ahead loomed a monumental boulder inscribed "Genyue." Stepping past it revealed marvel after marvel: craggy peaks bristling with ten thousand plum trees, waterfalls plunging into emerald pools clear to the bottom, wild ducks swimming and perching over sculpted stones—rare blooms, exotic beasts, luxury pushed beyond imagination.

申简辰边欣赏边在心里慨叹，这位官家是他见过的最会享受之人，恨不得把天底下所有美的事物统统搬到艮岳之内。
Touring along, Shen Jianchen marveled silently: Nobody on earth knew how to splurge like Huizong—practically hauled every wonder under Heaven inside one garden!

正想着，听到一个女声：“仪王，乔公子，可否与你们结伴而行？”
Just then a poised woman's voice hailed them: "Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao—mind if I walk with you?"

申简辰站住，眼前出现一位妇人，看上去四十多岁，端庄秀丽，年轻时定是位亭亭玉立的女子。
Stopping, Shen Jianchen met a woman in her forties—dignified, graceful, clearly a stunner in her younger days.

乔文仙回道：“能与魏夫人同行，文渊不胜荣幸。”
Qiao Wenxian bowed with warm pleasure: "Walking alongside Lady Wei honors Wenyuan!"

魏夫人又问：“仪王呢？可愿与我结伴而行？”
Turning to Shen Jianchen, Lady Wei asked, "And Prince Yi? Mind my company?"

申简辰说：“文渊乐意，我当然不介意。”
"If Wenyuan welcomes you, count me in."

乔文仙介绍道：“这位是魏夫人，乃曾丞相的家眷，也是我朝有名的女词人，北可与易安居士齐名，南可与幽栖居士媲美，是闺阁中的大家。”
Qiao Wenxian introduced her: "This is Lady Wei, wife of Chancellor Zeng—one of our dynasty's foremost female ci poets! Up north she rivals Yi'an Jushi, down south she matches Youqi Jushi—a towering master of the inner chambers!"

申简辰开始在心里埋怨神柳，为何不让他穿越到一个重武轻文全是英雄豪杰的时代？这样他还会觉得自己有用武之地。偏偏来到重文轻武的宋朝，不是遇到大奸臣，就是遇到会写诗词之人，搞得他在这些人面前都不敢轻易张嘴。
Silent curses flew toward the Divine Willow again—why drop him into this bookish Song dynasty crawling with treacherous court officials and world-class poets instead of a martial era full of warriors?! Barely dared open his mouth around people like this!

面对文渊的介绍，又不能不回应，客气地说：“久闻魏夫人大名，今日得以相见，不胜荣幸。”
Still, manners first—he bowed smooth: "Long admired Lady Wei's great name; meeting you today is an honor."

魏夫人笑道：“仪王久居王府，我也只知你的大名，没见过人，今日终于见到庐山真面目，先祝贺仪王得以新生，仪王若不嫌弃，往后多到我府上走动走动。”
Lady Wei laughed warm: "With Your Highness tucked away in Prince Yi's Manor, I only knew your name until today—glad to behold the real person! Congratulations on your new lease on life! Drop by our manor anytime you're free."

申简辰客气道：“如若有机会定去。”
"Whenever I have the chance, certainly."

三人并肩走着，边走边欣赏美景。走了一会儿，魏夫人问：“乔公子什么时候回临安？”
Strolling three abreast taking in the vistas, Lady Wei asked after a while: "When are you heading back to Lin'an, Young Master Qiao?"

“本应今日已在回临安的路上，怎奈官家宴请，耽搁下来。”
"Meant to be on the road today—delayed by His Majesty's banquet."

“如若回到临安，定要帮我邀请幽栖居士，让她抽时间来趟汴梁，顺便告诉她汴梁有位魏夫人想与她结交。”
"Once you return to Lin'an, invite Youqi Jushi on my behalf! Tell her to find time to visit Bianliang—Lady Wei longs to make her acquaintance."

“放心，我定帮魏夫人转达。”
"Count on it—I'll pass word straight to her."

大家在艮岳足足逛了一上午，终于逛完，出了艮岳又乘坐轿子来到集英殿。集英殿是宫中宴请群臣的大殿，最尊贵的位置摆着一张大大的方桌，上面放满了珍馐佳肴，周围则摆着鼓墩。只见官家、太子与重要的臣子都走到方桌旁坐下。
Touring Genyue took the entire morning; exiting by sedan chair, everyone reconvened at Jiying Hall, the grand palace banquet hall. At the seat of honor stood a huge square table laden with delicacies encircled by drum stools where His Majesty, the Crown Prince, and top ministers sat.

离方桌不远处，则安置着各色小桌，依次供后宫妃嫔、皇子、其他大臣、才子，以及官眷就坐。申简辰不愿与皇子们一道，依然跟着乔文仙。他们两个刚坐下，魏夫人过来，坐在他们旁边。
Grouped nearby sat smaller tables for imperial consorts, princes, lesser ministers, scholars, and officials' wives. Steering clear of his royal brothers, Shen Jianchen stuck with Qiao Wenxian—and moments later Lady Wei walked right over to share their table.

经过艮岳一路，申简辰与魏夫人已熟，主动开口问：“魏夫人为何不与各位官眷同桌？偏偏喜欢与我和文渊挤在一起？”
Warm after their walk through Genyue, Shen Jianchen teased, "Why sit squeezed in with Wenyuan and me instead of the other court ladies, Lady Wei?"

魏夫人笑道：“仪王为何不与皇子们同桌？偏偏与乔公子挤在一处？”
Lady Wei smiled back: "Why sit squeezed in with Young Master Qiao instead of your royal brothers, Prince Yi?"

这话倒把申简辰问住，照实说道：“你也知道，我从小病着，很少出王府，故而与皇兄皇弟们不熟，没有共同语言。”
Touché! Answering plain, he said, "Sick all my life, barely left the manor—I'm a stranger to my brothers with nothing in common."

魏夫人说：“这便是我的答案，那些官眷坐在一起，只懂得讨论谁的首饰贵重，谁的衣裳锦缎珍贵，还有谁家女儿嫁给谁，谁家儿子又娶了谁，跟我实在不合，只能跑到你们这里来。不过，为何这一桌只有你们两个？”
"Exact same answer! All those ladies gab about is whose jewels cost more, whose silk is rarer, whose daughter married whom and whose son took which bride—drives me crazy! So I fled to your table! Wait—why are there only you two sitting here?"

乔文仙叹道：“唉！还不都怪仪王，当着众人的面说出那些话，让官家不高兴，大家都避之不及，害得我也交不到朋友。”
Qiao Wenxian sighed wryly: "Blame Prince Yi! After his speech in the hall displeased His Majesty, everyone steers ten paces clear—costing me company too!"

魏夫人笑道：“仪王得敬我一杯。”
Lady Wei laughed: "Then Prince Yi owes me a toast!"

申简辰不大明白：“为何？”
"Why's that?" Shen Jianchen blinked.

魏夫人说：“只因我不畏权贵，在众人远离你时，还与你说话。”
"For defying power and sitting with you when everyone else ran clear!"

申简辰立刻端起酒壶，给魏夫人杯中斟满酒，也给自己倒满，举起酒杯：“魏夫人乃女中豪杰，必须敬！我先干了，你随意！”申简辰一饮而尽。
Snatching the wine pot at once, Shen Jianchen filled both her cup and his to the brim and raised his glass: "A true heroine deserves a toast! Bottoms up for me—drink at your pace!" He tossed his straight back.

魏夫人也一口喝下。
Lady Wei drained hers clean in one shot too!

乔文仙不满道：“你们两个只顾喝酒，把我晾到一边，实在不公平，我可是对仪王不离不弃，关键时候还替你说话，你是不是得多敬我几杯？”
Qiao Wenxian protested: "Drinking up a storm while leaving me high and dry? Unfair! I stuck by Prince Yi through thick and thin and saved his neck back there—don't you owe me several cups?!"

申简辰二话不说，向旁边伺候的宫女挥挥手，等宫女过来，吩咐她再拿三个酒杯。宫女很快拿来，申简辰将四个酒杯倒满。
Without a word, Shen Jianchen waved a palace maid over and called for three extra cups, filling all four brimful.

“文渊对我情深义重，一杯怎么能够？我喝四杯，你随意！”
"For loyalty deep as Wenyuan's, one cup won't cut it! Four for me—sip as you please!"

说着将四杯酒一一喝下，竟然面不改色。
Downing all four cups straight without flushing a shade, he set them dry.

乔文仙赞道：“好酒量！”也拿起自己的酒杯喝了。
"Iron liver!" Qiao Wenxian cheered, emptying his own cup.

这时一位内侍端着酒壶过来：“乔公子，官家赏你的美酒。”
Right then an eunuch approached carrying a wine pot: "Young Master Qiao, His Majesty's promised gift of fine wine."

乔文仙起身接过：“感谢官家恩赐，文渊不胜荣幸。”
Rising with cupped hands, Qiao Wenxian accepted: "Wenyuan bows in gratitude for His Majesty's favor!"

内侍离开后，魏夫人说：“仪王可还喝得下这壶美酒？”
Once the eunuch left, Lady Wei teased: "Got room left for this whole pot, Prince Yi?"

申简辰笑道：“绝不在话下！”
Shen Jianchen grinned: "Piece of cake!"

三人吃着、喝着、聊着。
Food, wine, and lively talk flowed on.

整个宴会在丰盛的珍馐美味中度过，不时有舞伎表演跳舞，也有唱曲儿的女子手握琵琶弹唱，真是一派歌舞升平。
Course after sumptuous course graced the tables while dancing girls twirled and singing girls plucked Chinese lutes—a dazzling vision of gilded peace.

接着梁师成传达官家的意思，让文人才子各写一首赞美艮岳的诗词。很快宫女们拿来笔墨，在不远处整齐的书桌上摆好。文人才子个个摩拳擦掌提笔写下，再由宫女呈给官家阅览，最终魏夫人的诗词取得头筹，得到官家赞赏。
Next Liang Shicheng relayed His Majesty's call for every scholar to compose a poem praising Genyue. Palace maids set writing gear along rows of desks; scholars dipped brushes eagerly and submitted their pieces—Lady Wei winning top prize and glowing praise from the throne!

官家也来了兴致，要当场泼墨，宫女们纷纷撤去方桌上的碗碟和吃剩的杂物，拿来一大块干净的布子铺上，迅速摆好笔墨纸砚，老臣蔡京研磨，高俅拿起笔双手捧到官家面前。官家喝完内侍捧的茶后，接过笔，迅速在纸上挥毫起来。
Caught up in the mood, His Majesty called for brush and ink himself. Maids swept the great square table clean, spread fresh cloth, and laid out the Four Treasures; senior minister Cai Jing ground ink while Gao Qiu presented the brush with both hands. Taking a sip of tea, Zhao Ji took the brush and swept bold strokes across the paper.

没多时，一幅《腊梅山禽图》已成，并在上面题下一首诗：“山禽矜逸态，梅粉弄轻柔。已有丹青约，千秋指白头。”写了落款。宫女拿来数方大印，一一盖了。
In minutes "Winter Plum and Mountain Birds" stood complete, crowned with an inscribed quatrain: "The mountain bird boasts a leisurely grace, / The plum blossoms display their gentle softness. / An eternal pact in ink and color we embrace, / For a thousand autumns, till our heads turn white." [TN: Translator's rendering] Signing his cipher, he had maids stamp several vermilion imperial seals.

周围的臣子无不赞叹有加。
Ministers erupted in ecstatic applause.

官家撂下笔：“你们这些人对寡人的诗画极其熟悉，溢美之词寡人不感兴趣，还是让寡人挑两人前来品鉴一番。”
Setting down his brush, His Majesty spoke: "You lot know my work too well—spare me empty flattery! Let me pick two fresh eyes to critique it."

赵佶瞧向文人才子处：“不如让乔文仙品品寡人的诗，至于画，仪王不妨品鉴一番。”
His gaze swept over to the scholars' side: "Qiao Wenxian—critique my poem! As for the painting—Prince Yi, step up and critique it!"

众人都看向乔文仙和仪王……
Every head in Jiying Hall swiveled toward Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi...

乔文仙的水平大家领教过，品诗绝不在话下；至于仪王，是否能品得了帝王之画，真不好说。
Everyone knew Qiao Wenxian's brilliance with verse—could Prince Yi actually critique an imperial masterpiece?
