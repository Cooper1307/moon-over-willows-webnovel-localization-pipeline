## 三、建议优化（3处）

### 优化1：增强口语化
**位置**：第87段（申简辰回应乔文仙）
**原文**：
> 申简辰不以为然："我只说起死回生前去的地方，又没说官家和那些奸臣，你担心什么？"

**当前译文**：
> Shen Jianchen was dismissive: "I only talked about the place I went to after dying. I didn't mention the emperor or those treacherous ministers. What are you worried about?"

**优化建议**：
```
Shen Jianchen was dismissive: "I only talked about the place I went to after dying. I didn't mention the emperor or those treacherous ministers. What's there to worry about?"
```
**理由**："What's there to worry about?" 比 "What are you worried about?" 更口语化，更符合男主轻松的态度。

### 优化2：提升流畅度
**位置**：第259段（魏夫人对话）
**原文**：
> 魏夫人笑道："仪王久居王府，我也只知你的大名，没见过人，今日终于见到庐山真面目，先祝贺仪王得以新生，仪王若不嫌弃，往后多到我府上走动走动。"

**当前译文**：
> Madam Wei smiled: "Prince Yi has long resided in the manor. I only knew your name but had never seen you in person. Today, I finally see the true face of Mount Lu. First, let me congratulate Prince Yi on your new life. If you don't find us unworthy, please visit our manor more often in the future."

**优化建议**：

**理由**：将 "If you don't find us unworthy" 简化为 "If you don't mind"，更符合现代英语口语习惯，同时保持谦逊的语气。
