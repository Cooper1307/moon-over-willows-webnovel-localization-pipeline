# 第52章 一见钟情观景亭

---

夏果往竹兰居跑去，路上将有些乱的发髻再弄乱些，衣裳的纽绊解开一些，让自己显得惊慌错乱。

Xiaguo ran toward Zhulan Residence. On the way, she messed up her already disheveled hair even more and loosened some of the buttons on her clothes to make herself look flustered and distraught.

---

一进竹兰居，小姐正好站在院内欣赏竹子。

As soon as she entered Zhulan Residence, she found Young Lady standing in the courtyard admiring the bamboo.

---

夏果扑上前，跪下大声哭："呜呜……小姐！您一定要替我做主，主君轻薄于我，我没脸见人，我……我……我现在就一头撞死算了！"夏果起身向院墙上撞去。

Xiaguo threw herself forward, knelt down, and wailed loudly: "Wuwu... Young Lady! You must... you must stand up for me! The Lord molested me! I have no face to see anyone anymore. I... I... I might as well smash my head against the wall and die right now!" Xiaguo got up and charged toward the courtyard wall.

---

千里寻一把拽住夏果，注意到她凌乱的头发和解开的纽绊，很是担心："到底怎么回事？说清楚！"

Qianli Xun grabbed Xiaguo and noticed her disheveled hair and loosened buttons. She was very worried: "What exactly happened? Explain clearly!"

---

夏果又跪下："呜呜……主君找我问话，没问几句，把我抱到床上，他劲儿大，我怎么反抗都没用，呜呜……！"

Xiaguo knelt down again: "Wuwu... The Lord summoned me for questioning. After just a few questions, he carried me to the bed. He's so strong, no matter how I resisted, it was useless. Wuwu...!"

---

"林觉可得手？"

"Did Lin Jue succeed?"

---

"呜呜……！那倒没有，我踹了主君一脚，趁机逃走，呜呜……！"

"Wuwu...! No, he didn't. I kicked the Lord and took the chance to escape. Wuwu...!"

---

千里寻松口气，气愤道："竟然敢欺负我的人，看我怎么削他！起来，跟我一起找林觉算账！"

Qianli Xun breathed a sigh of relief, then said angrily: "He actually dares to bully my people. Let me teach him a lesson! Get up, come with me to settle accounts with Lin Jue!"

---

苦荞拉住小姐："您去必然与姑爷打起来，只怕和离之事遥遥无期！"

Kuqiao held back Young Lady: "If you go, you'll definitely fight with the Young Master. I'm afraid the mutual divorce will be postponed indefinitely!"

---

"林觉欺人太甚，顾不了那么多。"

"Lin Jue has gone too far. I can't worry about that now."

---

千里寻执意找林觉算账，不管在哪个时代，她身边的人都不能随意被人欺负。

Qianli Xun was determined to settle accounts with Lin Jue. No matter which era she was in, the people around her couldn't be bullied at will.

---

她气冲冲来到主屋门外，大喊道："林觉！给我滚出来！"

She stormed to the door of the main hall and shouted: "Lin Jue! Get your ass out here!"

---

林觉在屋内坐着，听到朱淑真的声音，还是有些胆怯，犹豫要不要出去，毕竟是丑事，闹到大庭广众之下，脸上挂不住，决定还是不出去得好。

Lin Jue was sitting inside. Hearing Zhu Shuzhen's voice, he was still somewhat timid. He hesitated whether to go out. After all, it was a scandalous matter. If it became public, he'd lose face. He decided it was better not to go out.

---

没任何反应，千里寻干脆直接冲进屋内。

There was no response. Qianli Xun simply charged into the room.

---

林觉正坐在桌前拿本书，一副道貌岸然的样子。

Lin Jue was sitting at the table holding a book, looking hypocritically dignified.

---

她上前把林觉手中的书抢走，放到一边："你这种人还有脸看王右丞的诗集！起来！给夏果道歉！"

She stepped forward, snatched the book from Lin Jue's hands, and put it aside: "Someone like you still has the face to read Wang Wei's poetry collection! Get up! Apologize to Xiaguo!"

---

林觉质问道："凭什么给一个下人道歉？"

Lin Jue challenged: "Why should I apologize to a servant?"

---

"她是我的丫鬟，你欺负她就是欺负我。"

"She's my maid. Bullying her is bullying me."

---

"我是主君，欺负个把丫鬟你又能把我怎样？"

"I'm the Lord. Even if I bully a maid or two, what can you do to me?"

---

"给你两个选择，要么被我打一顿，要么乖乖道歉！"

"I'll give you two choices: either take a beating from me, or apologize like a good boy!"

---

林觉装作和颜悦色的样子："朱淑真，不要为一个丫鬟伤你我和气，小心我不同意和离，耗着你。"

Lin Jue pretended to be amiable: "Zhu Shuzhen, don't damage our relationship over a maid. Be careful, or I won't agree to the divorce and keep you waiting."

---

千里寻说："哼！我已经决定，不会把夏果送给你，至于不同意和离那是你的事，在我心里早已与你和离。耗着我是吧？我也耗着你！本来打算早些搬出林府，此时我改变主意，偏偏要住在林府西院，以后你别再想把任何商妓带回林府，带一个我打一个；更别想纳妾，我会闹得没人敢给你当妾室；你要是出去逛青楼，我会随时派人盯着，你去哪里我就打到哪里，看哪个青楼还敢接待你！"

Qianli Xun said: "Hmph! I've already decided I won't give Xiaguo to you. As for not agreeing to the divorce, that's your business. In my heart, I've already divorced you. You want to stall? I'll stall you too! I originally planned to move out of the Lin residence soon, but now I've changed my mind. I'll stay in the west courtyard on purpose. From now on, don't even think about bringing any merchant prostitutes back. Bring one, and I'll beat her. Don't even think about taking a concubine. I'll make such a scene that no one will dare. If you go out to visit brothels, I'll send someone to watch you. Wherever you go, I'll go and beat you up. Let's see which brothel will still dare to serve you!"

---

林觉被激怒："你……你……！陈浩宇说得没错，你就是个母老虎！小心我休了你！"

Lin Jue was provoked: "You... you...! Chen Haoyu was right. You're a shrew! Be careful, or I'll divorce you!"

---

"休我？求之不得！如果不想挨打，老老实实给夏果道歉！"

"Divorce me? I'd love that! If you don't want to get beaten, obediently apologize to Xiaguo!"

---

打又打不过，至于道歉，林觉一百个不乐意。可总不能这么僵着，得有人低头，目前的情形只有他低头的份儿，毕竟他非礼了夏果，有错在先。

He couldn't beat her anyway. As for apologizing, Lin Jue was extremely unwilling. But they couldn't stay in this deadlock forever. Someone had to bow down. In the current situation, only he could bow down. After all, he had molested Xiaguo and was in the wrong first.

---

林觉忍住怒气，好言相劝："我是主君，怎么能向一个丫鬟道歉？大不了我当着你的面发誓，以后不再动她，不再对她有非分之想，你觉得如何？"

Lin Jue suppressed his anger and tried to persuade her gently: "I'm the Lord. How can I apologize to a maid? At most, I can swear in front of you that I won't touch her again and won't have any improper thoughts about her. How does that sound?"

---

千里寻历来最讨厌好色之徒，见一个打一个，绝不会轻易饶了林觉。

Qianli Xun had always hated lecherous men the most. She'd beat every one she saw and would never let Lin Jue off easily.

---

"不行！必须道歉！"

"No! You must apologize!"

---

"朱淑真！来劲了是吧！"

"Zhu Shuzhen! You're really pushing it!"

---

"你说呢？"千里寻举起拳头在林觉眼前晃晃。

"What do you think?" Qianli Xun raised her fist and waved it in front of Lin Jue's eyes.

---

林觉逼不得已："好！你厉害！我道歉，行了吧。不过你得答应我，以后不能闹到青楼去。"

Lin Jue had no choice: "Fine! You win! I'll apologize, okay? But you have to promise me not to cause a scene at the brothels in the future."

---

千里寻在心里冷笑道："一句假话，还真把他吓住。"口中说道："我答应你。"

Qianli Xun sneered in her heart: "One lie and he's actually scared." She said out loud: "I promise you."

---

林觉走到夏果跟前，很不情愿地吐出三个字："对不住。"

Lin Jue walked up to Xiaguo and reluctantly spat out three words: "I'm sorry."

---

夏果没有理会。

Xiaguo ignored him.

---

林觉瞧着她的头发比刚才乱了些："把头发弄那么乱干什么？好像我得手一样，我也没落上什么好，被你踹得生疼。"

Lin Jue noticed her hair was more disheveled than before: "Why did you mess up your hair so much? It's like I succeeded. I didn't get anything good out of it either. You kicked me so hard it still hurts."

---

千里寻见好就收，不愿与林觉计较太多，走之前好心叮嘱："你我耗着对谁都不好，建议你认真考虑一下，尽快与我和离。"

Qianli Xun knew when to stop. She didn't want to argue with Lin Jue too much. Before leaving, she kindly reminded him: "Stalling isn't good for either of us. I suggest you think seriously about it and divorce me as soon as possible."

---

说完带着苦荞和夏果离开。

With that, she left with Kuqiao and Xiaguo.

---

林觉被逼道歉，心里很不爽，对着朱淑真的背影说："我就不同意和离，耗死你！"

Lin Jue was forced to apologize and was very unhappy. He said to Zhu Shuzhen's back: "I won't agree to the divorce. I'll stall you to death!"

---

夏果之事让期待的和离遥遥无期，千里寻的内心感到很无助，难道竹林之行带来的运气很快被消耗殆尽？

The matter with Xiaguo made the anticipated divorce seem indefinitely distant. Qianli Xun felt very helpless inside. Had the luck brought by the bamboo forest trip been quickly exhausted?

---

她想起净光法师说的改变观念和行为之事，她的观念和行为与朱淑真不同，只有身体是她的，为何还不能过上属于千里寻的生活？

She remembered what Master Jingguang had said about changing one's mindset and behavior. Her mindset and behavior were different from Zhu Shuzhen's. Only her body belonged to Zhu Shuzhen. Why couldn't she live a life that belonged to Qianli Xun?

---

又仔细一想，如若是朱淑真，只会被林觉欺负；而现在，林觉只有被千里寻欺负的份儿，不就证明已过上千里寻的生活。

Thinking carefully, if it were Zhu Shuzhen, she'd only be bullied by Lin Jue. But now, Lin Jue was the one being bullied by Qianli Xun. Didn't that prove she was already living Qianli Xun's life?

---

想到这些，又有一丝快意，剩下婚姻这堵墙需慢慢攻克。与净光法师聊天儿的过程中，她已猜到终将会结束这段婚姻，还是耐心等待时机到来。

Thinking of this, she felt a bit satisfied. The remaining wall of marriage needed to be slowly dismantled. During her chat with Master Jingguang, she had already guessed that this marriage would eventually end. She just needed to patiently wait for the right moment.

---

第二日午时，千里寻来到观景亭。西湖的观景亭位于水色天光之中，她再次被西湖美景吸引。

The next day at noon, Qianli Xun arrived at the Sightseeing Pavilion. The pavilion at West Lake was situated amidst the water's color and sky's light. She was once again captivated by the beautiful scenery of West Lake.

---

亭中有位男子的身影，远远看去，身姿挺拔，那背影优雅中不失力度，散发出的气息与天地融为一体，仿佛周围的空气都弥漫着他身上的魅力一般。

There was a man's figure in the pavilion. From afar, his posture was upright. His back view was elegant yet powerful. The aura he emitted seemed to merge with heaven and earth, as if the surrounding air was filled with his charm.

---

千里寻再仔细观察周围，亭中除那背影再无一人，心中纳闷："不是诗会吗？东方尘泽他们为何不见人影？"

Qianli Xun observed the surroundings more carefully. There was no one else in the pavilion except that back view. She was puzzled: "Isn't this a poetry gathering? Why aren't Dongfang Chenze and the others here?"

---

怀疑归怀疑，还是向苦荞询问："他可是乔文仙乔公子？"

Despite her doubts, she still asked Kuqiao: "Is that Young Master Qiao Wenxian?"

---

苦荞笑道："正是，没想到乔公子是借诗会之名约小姐出来。"

Kuqiao smiled: "Yes. I didn't expect Young Master Qiao to use the poetry gathering as an excuse to invite Young Lady out."

---

夏果心里想道："唉！一个逛青楼，一个出来私会外男，难怪闹到和离。"

Xiaguo thought to herself: "Sigh! One visits brothels, the other meets men in secret. No wonder it came to divorce."

---

千里寻向亭子靠近，边走边在心里想着如何与乔文仙开口。到达亭子跟前，谨慎地唤了声："乔公子。"

Qianli Xun approached the pavilion, thinking along the way how to start a conversation with Qiao Wenxian. Reaching the pavilion, she cautiously called out: "Young Master Qiao."

---

乔文仙转过身，面带笑容，快一年了，他的心里一直装着淑真，此刻真真切切地见到，她丝毫未变，还像以前一样美……

Qiao Wenxian turned around with a smile. For almost a year, his heart had been filled with Shuzhen. Now seeing her in person, she hadn't changed at all. She was still as beautiful as before...

---

当乔文仙转过身时，千里寻惊呆了，世上竟有如此好看的男子！不管是身姿还是五官，比例恰到好处，无论视觉触及到他身上哪一处都觉得无比舒畅。

When Qiao Wenxian turned around, Qianli Xun was stunned. How could there be such a handsome man in the world! Whether it was his posture or his features, the proportions were perfect. Wherever her gaze landed on him, it felt incredibly pleasant.

---

她实在想不通有这么一位尤物当知己，为何朱淑真会挂念徐公子？反正她千里寻已心跳加快，脸色微红，想要恋爱的想法愈加强烈。不行，得尽快和离，否则岂不是辜负了眼前的美男子？

She really couldn't understand. With such a stunning man as a confidant, why would Zhu Shuzhen still think of Young Master Xu? Anyway, Qianli Xun's heart was beating faster, her face slightly flushed, and her desire to be in a relationship grew stronger. No, she had to divorce as soon as possible. Otherwise, wouldn't she be letting down the handsome man before her?

---

夏果也被乔文仙吸引，她多次在梦中梦到的人不就是这个样子吗？眼前的公子满足了她对男子外形所有的美好想象。

Xiaguo was also attracted to Qiao Wenxian. Wasn't this exactly the person she had dreamed of many times? The young man before her satisfied all her beautiful imaginations of what a man should look like.

---

朱淑真称他"乔公子"，乔文仙想起失忆之事，看来她真的忘记自己，连平日对他的称呼都不记得。他强调道："你还是像以前一样称呼我文渊吧。"

Zhu Shuzhen called him "Young Master Qiao." Qiao Wenxian remembered her amnesia. It seemed she had really forgotten him, even forgetting how she usually addressed him. He emphasized: "You can still call me Wenyuan like before."

---

千里寻回过神来："哦，是该称呼文渊。"

Qianli Xun came to her senses: "Oh, I should call you Wenyuan."

---

她带着羞涩和期待走进亭内。

She walked into the pavilion with shyness and anticipation.

---

夏果想要跟进去，被苦荞拉住，拽到远处。

Xiaguo wanted to follow them in, but Kuqiao held her back and pulled her away.

---

夏果很不满："为何不能跟小姐进去？"

Xiaguo was very dissatisfied: "Why can't I go in with Young Lady?"

---

苦荞解释道："主人说话，我们当丫鬟的还是少听为妙。"

Kuqiao explained: "When the master is talking, it's better for us maids not to listen."

---

夏果立刻笑着说："苦荞姐姐，问你个事。"

Xiaguo immediately smiled and said: "Sister Kuqiao, let me ask you something."

---

"问呗。"

"Ask away."

---

"乔公子的家世如何？"

"What's Young Master Qiao's family background?"

---

"问这做什么？"

"Why ask that?"

---

"我刚给小姐当丫鬟，总得了解小姐身边的人，以后碰见就知道该如何应对。"

"I just became Young Lady's maid. I should know about the people around her. That way, when I meet them in the future, I'll know how to respond."

---

"给你说说也无妨，乔家是皇亲国戚，家世当然好。"

"It's fine to tell you. The Qiao family are imperial relatives. Their family background is naturally excellent."

---

"啊！皇亲国戚！"夏果更对乔文仙充满向往。

"Ah! Imperial relatives!" Xiaguo was even more filled with longing for Qiao Wenxian.

---

观景亭中，千里寻忍住花痴的表情问道："不是诗会吗？怎么只来你一人？"尽管苦荞已给出答案，可千里寻还想问，她想听乔文仙如何回答。

In the Sightseeing Pavilion, Qianli Xun suppressed her smitten expression and asked: "Isn't this a poetry gathering? Why are you the only one here?" Although Kuqiao had already given the answer, Qianli Xun still wanted to ask. She wanted to hear how Qiao Wenxian would answer.

---

"我很想光明正大单独约你出来，可你已成亲，总得顾及你的名声，只能以诗会为由。"

"I really wanted to invite you out openly and alone, but you're married. I have to consider your reputation, so I could only use the poetry gathering as an excuse."

---

千里寻故意装作严肃的样子："以诗会为由约我出来，难道就能保全我的名声？"

Qianli Xun deliberately pretended to be serious: "Using the poetry gathering as an excuse to invite me out—does that really protect my reputation?"

---

"我顾不了那么多，只想见到你。你知道吗？我离开临安是因为你成婚我太过伤心，以为离开后会渐渐把你忘记，可在汴梁没有一日不想你，听说你被夫君毒打，我更是痛心疾首，想回来将林觉打一顿。后来又听说你打算和离，我已无心在汴梁待下去，便匆忙赶回来。"

"I couldn't care about that anymore. I just wanted to see you. Do you know? I left Lin'an because I was too heartbroken over your marriage. I thought after leaving, I'd gradually forget you. But in Bianliang, there wasn't a single day I didn't think of you. Not a single day. When I heard your husband was beating you, I was even more heartbroken and wanted to come back and beat Lin Jue myself. Later, when I heard you were planning to divorce, I had no heart to stay in Bianliang anymore, so I hurried back."

---

千里寻第一次对异性的表白动心。男女之间哪有真正的友情？所谓的"知己"，不过是一方想要接近另一方的由头。

This was the first time Qianli Xun was moved by a man's confession. Where was there true friendship between men and women? The so-called "confidant" was just an excuse for one party to get closer to the other.

---

朱淑真心里没有乔文仙，她千里寻却在瞬间把乔文仙装进心里，也许是她太想谈恋爱的缘故，怪只怪乔文仙出现得恰到好处。

Zhu Shuzhen didn't have Qiao Wenxian in her heart, but Qianli Xun had instantly put Qiao Wenxian in hers. Perhaps it was because she wanted to be in a relationship so badly. She could only blame Qiao Wenxian for appearing at just the right moment.

---

"没想到你对我是这等心思。"

"I didn't expect you to have such feelings for me."

---

"你心里一直有徐公子，我不敢向你表露心迹，后来你又无缘无故嫁人，我更不敢向你说明。发生这么多事，我不想再错失机会，才忍不住把你请出来，向你表明一切。……听东方兄说你失忆，可否还记得……"

"You always had Young Master Xu in your heart. I didn't dare reveal my feelings to you. Later, you married for no reason, and I dared even less to explain. So much has happened. I didn't want to miss this opportunity again, so I couldn't help but invite you out and reveal everything to you... I heard from Brother Dongfang that you have amnesia. Do you still remember..."

---

千里寻猜出乔文仙的意思，红着脸说："我已不记得徐公子，连他长什么样都不知道，估计在大街上遇见只会把他当成陌生人。可我却记得文渊，你说奇怪不奇怪？"

Qianli Xun guessed Qiao Wenxian's meaning. Blushing, she said: "I don't remember Young Master Xu anymore. I don't even know what he looks like. If I met him on the street, I'd probably treat him like a stranger. But I remember Wenyuan. Isn't that strange?"

---

朱淑真隐晦的表白令乔文仙开心，终于听到期待已久的话，他想上前抓住她的手，或是抱住她，考虑到她的婚姻，还是控制住冲动："和离之事是不是很难解决？"

Zhu Shuzhen's subtle confession made Qiao Wenxian happy. Finally hearing the words he had longed for, he wanted to step forward and hold her hand, or even hug her. But considering her marriage, he controlled his impulse: "Is the divorce matter hard to resolve?"

---

千里寻点点头："是很麻烦，不过我会想办法。"

Qianli Xun nodded: "It's very troublesome, but I'll find a way."

---

"需要我做什么？"

"What do you need me to do?"

---

"你只需等着我的好消息。"

"Just wait for my good news."

---

"淑真，你能接受我的感情我真的很开心。"

"Shuzhen, I'm really happy you can accept my feelings."

---

"我也开心，在陌生的时代碰到自己喜欢的人不容易，我很珍惜。"

"I'm happy too. It's not easy to meet someone you like in a strange era. I cherish it very much."

---

乔文仙沉浸在表白得到肯定的喜悦中，没有心思追问"陌生的时代"背后的含义："我想找知府大人，劝说他同意和离之事……"

Qiao Wenxian was immersed in the joy of having his confession accepted. He had no mind to pursue the meaning behind "a strange era": "I want to find the prefect and persuade him to agree to the divorce..."

"千万不要！你去找他只能将事情搞得愈加复杂。你有这份心思，我已心满意足。我有一个小小的要求，希望你能答应。"

"Absolutely not! If you go to him, it'll only make things more complicated. Having this intention from you is enough for me. I have a small request, and I hope you'll agree."

"不管你有什么要求，我无不答应。"

"No matter what your request is, I'll agree to all of it."

"能不能在我和离之前，我们尽量不要见面？我怕林觉抓住什么把柄，再来刁难我。"

"Can we try not to meet before I get divorced? I'm afraid Lin Jue will catch some evidence and use it to trouble me again."

"虽然我很不情愿，还是会答应你，放心，我会控制好自己的感情，等着你。"

"Although I'm very reluctant, I'll still agree to you. Don't worry, I'll control my feelings and wait for you."

千里寻行礼道："文渊，就此别过，定要等着我。"

Qianli Xun bowed and said: "Wenyuan, let's part here for now. You must wait for me."

望着淑真离去的背影，乔文仙兴奋不已，终于得到心仪女子肯定的答复，他要赶回去把好消息告诉仪王，也让他替自己高兴高兴。

Watching Shuzhen's departing figure, Qiao Wenxian was extremely excited. Finally getting a positive response from the woman he loved, he wanted to hurry back and tell Prince Yi the good news, letting him be happy for him too.

---

临行前，仪王想要跟着一起来。乔文仙没有同意，只说出去与朋友见一面，仪王在一旁不太方便。

Before leaving, Prince Yi wanted to come along. Qiao Wenxian didn't agree, saying he was just going out to meet a friend. It wouldn't be convenient with Prince Yi there.

仪王一下子猜中，问他该不会去见朱淑真。乔文仙并未承认，推脱着迅速出了幽林别居。因怕被朱淑真拒绝，当着更多人面子上挂不住，他甚至连南双都未带在身边。

Prince Yi guessed immediately and asked if he was going to see Zhu Shuzhen. Qiao Wenxian didn't admit it and made excuses to quickly leave Youlin Villa. Afraid of being rejected by Zhu Shuzhen and losing face in front of more people, he didn't even bring Nan Shuang with him.

没成想一切顺利，乔文仙从心里感谢淑真失忆，要不是失忆，她又怎会接受他的感情？

He didn't expect everything to go so smoothly. Qiao Wenxian was truly grateful for Shuzhen's amnesia in his heart. If not for the amnesia, how could she have accepted his feelings?
