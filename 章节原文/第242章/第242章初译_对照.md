## Chapter 242: Children Walk Their Own Fate

姑娘还未说出，韦嬷嬷已心知肚明。
她今日主动向姑娘提起与姑爷圆房一事，有两个目的：一是提醒姑娘若想在这个家待下去恐怕与姑爷圆房在所难免，她需有个思想准备；二是希望姑娘提前想好应对之策，省得陈家长辈提出时，横冲直撞，搞得大家关系紧张。
果然起到良好的效果，姑娘抓住要奖赏的时机，必然提及此事。
只听陈江林问：“什么事？尽管说。”
千里寻深感此事重大，站起来，向三位长辈行礼：“请奶奶、公爹和婆婆答应，在我对陈浩宇没产生感情之前，别要求我与他圆房。”
韦嬷嬷观察三位主人，他们均露出失落的表情，因失落沉默不语。
陈浩宇也是一阵失落，说明母老虎还没喜欢上他，又担心她被长辈批评，赶紧打圆场：“行了行了，我答应你。你现在不喜欢我，是因为还没发现我的优点，以后本公子会时不时在你面前展示优点，你迟早会喜欢上我。”
三位长辈沉默带来的尴尬被陈浩宇的话打破，千里寻心里感到轻松许多，第一次觉得陈浩宇盲目自信的话没那么讨厌。
韦嬷嬷的嘴角也露出笑容，心想：“姑爷的确比以前可爱许多。”
孙子的话已说，夏老太太不再继续沉默：“浩宇既同意，我更没有必要反对，只是孙媳的想法的确与其他女子不同，我老了，需慢慢适应。”
毕竟是儿媳妇，陈江林面对此话题不好表态，向姚轻琰使眼色。
姚轻琰还沉浸在失落的情绪中，淑真什么都好，就是讲究太多。接触到夫君的目光，不得不说：“淑真啊，你跟浩宇婚前立下契约，长辈们只当你们太年轻玩过家家。既然婚也结了，浩宇也愿意变好，没必要再把契约当回事……”
提到契约，千里寻忍不住说：“契约已被我烧毁。”
姚轻琰终于露出笑容：“烧得好！既然已烧毁，好好跟浩宇过日子，圆房是早晚的事。”
千里寻说：“一时情绪所迫，才烧毁，打算再与陈浩宇重新立一契约。”
陈浩宇立刻说：“我不同意！男子汉大丈夫，一言九鼎，答应你的事记在心里就行，何必被一契约限制？放心，我不会随便逼你圆房。”
千里寻很不爽，心想：“哼！老娘也是你能逼的？”
姚轻琰劝道：“淑真，契约既然已烧毁，没有必要再重新立。女子嫁人，就是要为夫家生儿育女，你不愿圆房，我跟你公爹何时才能抱上孙子？”
陈浩宇认为自己最多一年功夫便能将朱淑真搞定，见母亲依然不松口，哄道：“娘放心，一年后保准让您抱上孙子。”
三位长辈都“呵呵”笑起来。
Before Qianli Xun even opened her mouth, Matron Wei knew exact what was coming. Bringing up consummation by the lotus pond had served two aims: preparing her mistress for reality, and giving her space to negotiate smoothly instead of clashing head-on with the elders. Right on target!

千里寻对陈浩宇所言不以为然：“婆婆着急抱孙子也不难，我愿意帮陈浩宇纳妾，孩子由妾室来生。”
"Name it," Chen Jianglin invited.

三位长辈又没了笑容。
Rising solemnly to curtsy before all three elders, Qianli Xun stated, "Please promise me, Grandmother, Father-in-law, and Mother-in-law: until I genuinely develop feelings for Chen Haoyu, do not pressure me to consummate our marriage."

陈浩宇急忙说：“我不同意！我只跟喜欢的人生孩子。”
Heavy disappointment dropped over all three elders' faces, locking the yard in awkward silence.

已到这个份上，韦嬷嬷再不开口，恐怕两边为难：“老太太、老爷、夫人，可喜可贺。”
Chen Haoyu felt a sharp sting too—proof she hadn't fallen for him yet—yet jumped right in to shield her from scolding. "Deal, deal! I promise! Only reason you don't like me yet is you haven't seen my good points—once I show them off, you'll fall for me soon enough!"

姚轻琰沉浸在淑真不愿圆房的忧愁中：“喜从何来？”
Breaking the stiff silence lifted a stone off Qianli Xun's chest; for the first time, his cocky bluff didn't grate on her ears. Matron Wei hid a small smile too—Young Master was growing downright likable.

韦嬷嬷娓娓道来：“要搁往常，姑爷早已迈出陈府大门，可他被姑娘管教几日后终于获得自由，不仅没着急往出跑，还陪长辈们拉家常，甚至还帮我家姑娘说话，明显是转了性情，陈家又多出一位做事的后辈，难道不该恭贺？”
"If Haoyu agrees, I won't object," Old Madam Xia broke the silence. "Your views sure differ from ordinary girls—takes an old woman time to adjust."

夏老太太立刻眉开眼笑：“是啊，今儿还主动要求他爹给安排生意上的事。”
Awkward discussing bedroom affairs with a daughter-in-law, Chen Jianglin nudged his wife with a glance.

姚轻琰也乐起来，暂时忘记刚才忧愁之事，见儿媳妇站着，说道：“淑真，别站着了，坐下说话。”
Yao Qingyan sighed. "Shuzhen, we elders treated that prenuptial contract as youthful games. Now that you're wed and Haoyu is reforming, why cling to paper?"

只有奶奶同意不要求她与陈浩宇圆房，公爹与婆婆还未松口，千里寻很不情愿坐下。又想着韦嬷嬷已开口，应该会向着她说话，不如坐下听听她说些什么，再见机行事。于是回到椅子旁乖乖坐下。
"I already burned the contract," Qianli Xun noted.

韦嬷嬷接着说：“所以说，一切无形中自然而然往好的方面发展，急不得。既然急不得，不如顺着；顺着，大家都舒心，心情舒畅，自然顺风顺水，还愁不会有好结果出现？再说我等都是俗人，眼界有限，谁也无法预知明日之事，姑爷就是现成的例子。两日前他还在青楼逛荡，而现在……”
Yao Qingyan brightened. "Good riddance! Then live as real husband and wife—consummation is only a matter of time."

陈浩宇倒懂事，再说他也需在母老虎面前多多表现，此时不说更待何时？
"Burned it in a fit of anger—meant to draw up a new one."

“往后我决不会再去青楼。”
"No way!" Chen Haoyu cut in. "A real man's word is gold! Promised you already—why hide behind paper? Relax, I won't force you!"

三位长辈再次笑成一朵花。
As if you ever could! Qianli Xun scoffed silently.

韦嬷嬷见主人家开心，继续说：“刚才姑娘提的要求的确唐突些，不过是她今日所想，明日会不会如此想，很难说。”
"Skip a new contract, Shuzhen," Yao Qingyan pleaded gently. "A wife enters her husband's house to carry on the family line. If you refuse to consummate, when will your father-in-law and I ever hold a grandson?"

陈江林终于忍不住开口：“唉，孩子们的事，由他们自己解决，大人们操再多心也没用，儿孙自有儿孙福。”
Confident he could crack her armor within twelve months, Chen Haoyu grinned. "Relax, Mother! One year from now I guarantee you're holding a grandson!"

夫君已松口，姚轻琰也没必要坚持，再者韦嬷嬷都说了，急不得，需顺着，说不定顺着顺着就顺出好结果，于是笑道：“淑真，圆不圆房是你跟浩宇的事，老太太都不理会，我还理会个什么劲儿？往后可别再提起。”
All three elders chuckled—until Qianli Xun tossed cold water over it: "Want a grandson fast, Mother-in-law? Easy—I'm happy to arrange concubines for Chen Haoyu to bear children right away!"

听完长辈所说，千里寻反而生出愧疚来。换位思考一下，若她是陈家长辈，儿媳妇不愿与自己的儿子圆房，心里肯定不好受。
Every smile vanished.

她起身向三位长辈行礼：“感谢奶奶、公爹和婆婆体恤，千言万语无从表达，以后淑真会从心底把陈家当成自己的家。”
"No way!" Chen Haoyu blurted instantly. "I only have children with the woman I love!"

夏老太太说：“一家人不说两家话，奖赏就算给你了，以后不许再问我讨要别的。”
Sensing deadlock looming, Matron Wei stepped gracefully into the breach. "Old Madam, Master, Madam—congratulations are in order!"

大家都笑起来。
"Congratulations for what?" Yao Qingyan sighed.

以往陈浩宇没有认真体会过亲情，今日竟然觉出跟家人在一起的温暖和舒适。陈浩宇看向朱淑真，都是因为对她动情，才会体验到从未有过的感觉。原来喜欢上一个人后，周围所有事物都不由自主变得美好起来。
"Think back," Matron Wei said smoothly. "Normally Young Master would have bolted out the gate the instant he got free. Instead he stayed home chatting with elders, stood up for Miss, and completely shifted his tune—giving the Chen family another capable heir! Isn't that worth celebrating?"

长辈们离开后，千里寻抱住韦嬷嬷感谢一番。
"True!" Old Madam Xia lit up. "Even asked his father for business work today!"

陈浩宇在一旁笑眼旁观，心中思忖，这位嬷嬷实在不一般，说不定，她有办法让母老虎喜欢上自己，不如找机会向她请教。
Yao Qingyan cheered right back up and waved Shuzhen down. "Stop standing—sit!"

当韦嬷嬷与朱淑真分开，一个回素心草堂，一个往后院拐。
With only Old Madam Xia agreeing so far, Qianli Xun sat reluctantly, waiting to see where Matron Wei steered this.

陈浩宇犹豫一会儿，决定还是先尾随韦嬷嬷，瞅准时机向她讨教一番。
"Everything is already moving naturally toward good," Matron Wei continued. "You cannot rush ripeness; push against the grain and everyone chafes, flow with it and good results bloom on their own. Who among us predicts tomorrow? Two days ago Young Master haunted brothels, and today—"

刚走出几步，丑丫过来：“姑爷，我能不能找青瓜说会儿话？”
"Swear I'll never step foot in a brothel again!" Chen Haoyu chimed in on cue, making all three elders beam wide.

“随便，我忙着呢，只要别打扰我就行。”陈浩宇继续跟上韦嬷嬷。
"Miss's request sounds blunt today," Matron Wei finished lightly, "yet who knows how her heart will feel tomorrow?"

丑丫迅速拉起青瓜往西院走，丑丫劲儿大，青瓜挣脱不开，很不情愿地被她拽着。
Chen Jianglin finally spoke up. "Leave young folks' business to young folks. Worrying won't help—children walk their own fate."

刚跟到后院，陈浩宇发现韦嬷嬷站着等他：“姑爷找我有事？”
With her husband yielding and Matron Wei's calm logic sinking in, Yao Qingyan smiled warm. "Fine, Shuzhen—consummation is between you and Haoyu. If Mother isn't fussing, neither will I! Drop the subject."

陈浩宇嬉皮笑脸竖起大拇指：“今日才发现，嬷嬷就是这个，厉害！太厉害！”
Unexpectedly, their grace left Qianli Xun feeling a twist of guilt—any parents would ache hearing a bride refuse their son's bed. Standing to bow deep, she spoke from the heart: "Thank you for your kindness, Grandmother, Father-in-law, and Mother-in-law. Words fall short—from today on, I truly treat Chen Manor as my home."

“姑爷想跟我说什么？”
"One family needs no fancy speeches," Old Madam Xia grinned. "Consider your reward paid—no asking for anything else!" Laughter rippled under the tree.

“嬷嬷常年跟母老虎在一起，对她应该很了解，给我教教，怎么才能让母老虎喜欢上我？”
For the first time in his life, Chen Haoyu truly tasted family warmth, gazing fondly at Shuzhen—funny how loving one person painted the whole world bright.

“这个简单，首先不能再唤我家姑娘母老虎。”
Once the elders left, Qianli Xun threw her arms around Matron Wei in thanks. Watching from the side, Chen Haoyu thought: That matron's a genius—maybe she can coach me on winning Zhu Shuzhen over!

“以后再也不唤。”
When Qianli Xun returned to Suxin Thatched Hall and Matron Wei headed back toward Jingyi Hall, Chen Haoyu trailed after the matron—waving Chouya off to drag a reluctant Qinggua toward the West Courtyard—only to find Matron Wei waiting calmly at the rear archway.

“其次，要懂得关心姑娘。”
"Something on your mind, Young Master?"

“怎么关心？”
Grinning wide, he flashed a thumbs-up. "Just realized you're the real MVP, Matron! Amazing!"

“多向大老爷学习。”
"What do you want to ask?"

“没问题，还有呢？”
"You know the tigress inside out—coach me! How do I make her fall for me?"

“姑爷的软招使得不错，继续努力。”
"Simple. Rule one: stop calling Miss 'tigress.'"

韦嬷嬷说完转身回静宜堂。
"Done! Never again!"

陈浩宇站在原地，甚是诧异：“我使软招时，韦嬷嬷也没在跟前，她怎么知道？真是神了，难道是哪个下人告诉她的？看来是这样。”
"Rule two: learn to care for her thoughtfully."

陈浩宇着急到朱淑真面前表现，顾不上想那么多，又往前院拐去。
"How?"

刚到前院，见青瓜耷拉个脑袋出现在面前。
"Watch Eldest Master."

“怎么了？看上去跟霜打的茄子一样？”
"Got it! Anything else?"

青瓜一脸委屈：“我刚到西院，无意中听到阿升向红豆表白，红豆答应了。我一气之下拉着丑丫到红豆面前生气地说，‘你竟然答应跟阿升在一起，告诉你，我也有喜欢的人，就是丑丫。’说完，我就后悔了。”
"Your soft approach works nicely—keep at it." With that, Matron Wei turned into Jingyi Hall.

“呵呵……，省得本公子再帮你和红豆撮合，你注定跟丑丫在一起。”陈浩宇笑完，手臂搭在青瓜的肩上，“其实丑丫挺不错，会功夫，你带她出去，都没人敢欺负你。”
Chen Haoyu blinked after her: She wasn't even around when I used the soft approach—how'd she know?! Must have spies everywhere!

“红豆也会功夫。”
Eager to score points with his wife, he headed back to the front yard—and bumped into Qinggua drooping like frostbitten eggplant.

“她是会功夫，可她心里有别人，即使跟你在一起迟早会红杏出墙。不像丑丫，对你一心一意，把她带出去，都不用担心她会红杏出墙，多省心。远了不说，就说西院那七个男仆，你把红豆放在他们中间，可放心？”
"What's with the long face?"

青瓜摇摇头：“不放心。”
"Walked into the West Courtyard and caught Asheng confessing to Hongdou—and she said yes! Mad out of my mind, I dragged Chouya right in front of Hongdou yelling, 'Fine, I've got Chouya!' Regretted it two seconds later!"

“把丑丫放在他们中间呢？”
Chen Haoyu burst out laughing and slung an arm over Qinggua's shoulders. "Saves me playing matchmaker—guess Chouya's your destiny! Honestly she's a catch: knows martial arts, nobody dares touch you when she's around!"

“自然放心。”
"Hongdou knows martial arts too!"

“你若跟红豆在一起，时刻担心她被别人抢走，她跟哪个男仆多说一句话你都担心，肯定没有心思伺候本公子，时间一长，本公子对你自然不满，到时换个跟班都是有可能的事。相反，你跟丑丫在一起，什么都不用担心，可以安心做自己的事，反过来丑丫还会处处照顾你，仔细想想哪个更好？”
"Yeah, and her heart's on another guy—even if you married her, she'd stray sooner or later. Chouya's dead loyal to you! Leave Hongdou surrounded by those seven male servants—would you sleep easy?"

“经公子这么一说，跟丑丫在一起更好。”
Qinggua shook his head. "Nope."

“这就对了。”陈浩宇放开青瓜，“安心跟丑丫在一起。现在随本公子去见少夫人。”
"Leave Chouya surrounded by them?"

青瓜拉住公子：“公子，您说少夫人是哪种人？”
"Sleep like a baby!"

“什么意思？”
"See? Marry Hongdou and you'll go gray guarding her instead of serving me, till I fire you! Marry Chouya and you live stress-free while she pampers you! Which is smarter?"

“您放心的人？还是不放心的人？”
"Put like that... Chouya wins!"

“本公子跟你不一样。”
"Attaboy! Settle down with Chouya and follow me to see Young Madam!"

“可我听说您成亲那日，仪王抢亲来着。”
Qinggua caught his sleeve. "Wait, Young Master—which kind is Young Madam? Sleep-easy or worry-gray?"

“去去去！少拿本公子的事跟你的事比，本公子要什么有什么？你哪一点能跟我比？母……”陈浩宇想说母老虎，想起韦嬷嬷叮嘱的话，赶紧改称呼，“朱淑真已嫁给我，成了你们的少夫人，我有什么好担心的？本公子没时间跟你废话，这就去讨好我娘子。”
"My league isn't your league!"

青瓜放开公子，跟在身后，心中想：“公子竟然用‘讨好’两个字，把自己摆得位置够廉价的。”
"Didn't Prince Yi raid your wedding though?"

陈浩宇悄悄走进素心草堂，按照他的猜测，朱淑真应该坐在书桌前。果不其然，她就坐在那里写写写。陈浩宇纳闷，诗词也不长，她怎么写那么久？也不知有什么好写的？
"Shoo! Quit comparing yourself to me! I've got looks, wealth, charm—she married me, she's your young madam, what's to worry about? Quit wasting breath—I'm off to woo my wife!"

苦荞注意到姑爷，小声提醒：“小姐，姑爷来了。”
Trailing behind, Qinggua smirked silently: Look at Young Master calling it "wooing"—dropped his ego straight through the floor!

千里寻抬起头：“不准向前走！就站那里说话！”
Slipping into Suxin Thatched Hall, Chen Haoyu found her bent over her desk writing—wondering how short poems took hours of scribbling.

“我怎么说也是你的夫君，墨香苑的一家之主，总像下人一样站着跟你说话，也太不像话？还是坐着好。”没等朱淑真答应，陈浩宇找椅子坐下，“苦荞，听说你很会做茶，给你家姑爷我做盏茶来。”
"Young Master is here," Kuqiao alerted softly.

苦荞正要去做，被小姐拦住。
Qianli Xun lifted her head. "Halt right there! Talk from where you stand!"

“他有青瓜伺候，什么时候轮你伺候他？”
"I'm your husband and master of Moxiang Courtyard—can't stand here like a lackey!" Plopping into a chair uninvited, he called, "Kuqiao, hear you make killer tea—whisk me a bowl!"

苦荞只得站住。
"He's got Qinggua—since when do you wait on him?" Qianli Xun cut her off.

陈浩宇并不计较：“青瓜，交给你个任务，好好跟苦荞学做茶，赶明儿我也在博雅斋安置一个茶桌，也感受感受附庸风雅的玩意儿。”
Unfazed, he told Qinggua, "New mission: learn tea-whisking from Kuqiao! I'll set up a tea table in Boya Study too and try out this classy hobby!"

千里寻搁下笔：“有什么事？赶紧说，老娘还忙着。”
Setting down her brush, Qianli Xun snapped, "Spit out what you want—I'm busy."

陈浩宇主动邀请道：“你整日没事趴在书桌上写来写去，多没意思，时间长了，腰上会长肉，影响身材，不如我带你出去逛逛？”
"Hunched over a desk all day will pad your waist—come out for a stroll with me!"

通过今日观察，千里寻已确定陈浩宇的确对她动起心思，只可惜她还无法接受，于是果断拒绝：“跟你出去逛，老娘毫无兴致。”
Clear now he had genuine feelings she couldn't return, she shut it down crisp: "Zero interest strolling with you."

“你说什么我都不生气，给你提个意见。”
"Say what you like, never makes me mad. Quick tip though—"

“有话快说，有屁快放。”
"Talk fast or leave."

“以后能不能别自称‘老娘’？这个称呼跟你的外表实在不搭。”
"Could you quit calling yourself 'yours truly like a tough old dame'? Doesn't match your pretty face."

“你管得着吗？老娘就喜欢这个称呼，不喜欢，以后最好少在我眼前晃。”
"None of your business! Love talking how I talk—hate it, stay out of my sight!"
