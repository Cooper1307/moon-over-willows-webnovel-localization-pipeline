第137章 闺蜜又来望月轩
## Chapter 137: Her Best Friend Returns to Wangyue Pavilion

夏果被乔公子拒绝，伤心不已。小姐乘坐马车离开，山下也无其他车可坐，她只好步行进入城内。又冷又累，还好身上带有碎银，便租辆马车回望月轩。
Crushed by Young Master Qiao's rejection, Xiaguo found herself stranded at the foot of the hill with Miss's carriage gone and no rides in sight, forced to trudge all the way back into town on foot. Freezing and exhausted, she thank goodness had loose silver on her and hired a carriage back to Wangyue Pavilion.

也许是因为人多的缘故，城里比山下暖和一些，周围的雪明显正在融化，路边的树上不时掉下一坨快要融化的雪来，时不时听到屋檐上落下雪水的声音: 滴答！滴答！
Warmer inside the bustling city than out by the hills, snow was already melting off eaves and bare boughs—plops of wet slush dropping from branches while steady drip, drip, drip echoed from rooftops.

到达望月轩，夏果将银子给了车夫，直奔丫鬟房，一进屋，趴在床上只顾哭泣：“呜呜呜……！”
Paying the driver outside Wangyue Pavilion, Xiaguo marched straight into the maids' quarters, flung herself onto bed, and burst into sobs: "Waaah!"

苦荞早已收起泥人，正在绣花，见夏果回来，一句话不说，大哭起来，便批评道：“小姐那么难过，你舍她而去，看样子没落上什么好，也不瞧瞧自己的身份，乔公子怎么可能看上你？”
Having put away her figurine to embroider, Kuqiao watched Xiaguo bawl without a word and snapped, "Ditching Miss right while she was breaking down—doesn't look like it got you anywhere! Look in a mirror at your station; how could Young Master Qiao ever look twice at you?!"

夏果哭着回道：“苦荞姐姐少对我说风凉话，呜呜！我喜欢乔公子有什么错？难道因为你我是丫鬟，就不能喜欢像乔公子那样的人？呜呜……”
Sobbing back, Xiaguo cried, "Quit rubbing salt in it, Sister Kuqiao! Waaah! What crime is loving Young Master Qiao?! Just because we're maids, aren't we allowed to love a man like him?! Waaah!"

夏果提到丫鬟的身份，苦荞感同身受，不好再批评她：“我是嫌你不顾小姐才说你，小姐刚对乔公子说过伤人的话，你就扑上去，她得多难受，你相当于给她伤口上撒把盐。”
Mention of a maid's lot struck a chord in Kuqiao, softening her edge: "I only scolded you for ditching Miss! She'd just forced out cruel words to break up with Young Master Qiao, and you jump right onto him—how do you think Miss felt? Pure salt in her wound!"

“呜呜……！我当时没想那么多，呜呜！我现在找小姐，跪在她面前请罪。”夏果哭着要去找小姐。
"Waaah! I didn't think straight! Waaah! I'll go kneel at Miss's feet and beg forgiveness right now!" Xiaguo scrambled up toward the door.

苦荞拦住她：“小姐一回来，把自己一个人关在屋里，你还是不要打扰她。韦嬷嬷让你回来去她屋里一趟，有话对你说。”
Kuqiao blocked her: "Miss locked herself indoors the second she got back—don't bother her. Matron Wei told you to report to her room the minute you returned."

夏果猜到，定是她没跟着小姐回来，韦嬷嬷问起，苦荞照实说了，才叫她前去训话。
Guessing Kuqiao had spilled everything when Matron Wei asked why she was not back, Xiaguo wiped her eyes on her sleeve: "Honest to a fault, aren't you, Sister Kuqiao—couldn't cover for me once!"

夏果努力控制好情绪，用衣袖擦擦眼泪：“苦荞姐姐真老实，也不知替我撒个谎。”
Kuqiao did not blink: "I'm still mad at you—why on earth would I lie for you? Go get a proper lecture from Matron Wei!"

苦荞毫不掩饰：“我心里怨着你呢？怎么可能替你撒谎？还是让你被韦嬷嬷好好说道说道！”
Sulking and aggrieved, Xiaguo dragged herself out toward Matron Wei's quarters.

夏果一边不满，一边委屈地走出丫鬟房，往韦嬷嬷的屋子而去。
Matron Wei was half the master of Wangyue Pavilion, inspiring equal parts envy and dread in Xiaguo.

韦嬷嬷算半个望月轩的主人，夏果既羡慕又害怕。
Maids at Zhu Manor whispered Matron Wei had started out as an ordinary maid in her youth—how had she climbed into half a mistress whom even Master Zhu treated with deference?

听朱府的丫鬟私下议论过，年轻时韦嬷嬷也是丫鬟。夏果想不通，她为何能一步步走到半个主子的地位？在朱府时，连老爷也会给她几分薄面。
Outside Matron Wei's door, Xiaguo called in a small voice: "Matron, Xiaguo is here."

来到韦嬷嬷的屋外，夏果怯怯地说：“嬷嬷，夏果前来拜见。”
From inside came Matron Wei's voice: "Come in."

里面传出韦嬷嬷的声音：“进来吧。”
Head hung low, Xiaguo stepped inside—more nervous than she had ever been before her mistress.

夏果低下头，小心走进去，她在小姐面前都没这么谨慎过。
Sitting on the couch, Matron Wei took one look at Xiaguo's swollen red eyes and knew she had cried up a storm.

韦嬷嬷坐在榻上，瞧到夏果进来，见她双眼红肿，便知哭的时间不短。
Once Xiaguo halted, Matron Wei asked, "Just got back, or came back with Young Lady?"

待夏果站定后，韦嬷嬷说：“你是刚回来？还是跟姑娘一道回来的？”
Xiaguo thought to herself: "She knows already and still asks?"

夏果心想：“都知道，还问？”
Swallowing her resentment, she answered in a meek voice: "Just got back."

想归想，还是小心回道：“刚回来。”
"Why didn't you come back with Young Lady? You're the personal maid she picked herself!"

“为何不跟姑娘一起回来？你可是她亲自收的贴身丫鬟。”
Xiaguo lied: "Miss told me to stay behind and comfort Young Master Qiao for her."

夏果编道：“小姐让我留下帮她安慰乔公子。”
Matron Wei's voice turned steel: "Bold-faced lie! Young Lady went today to make a clean break with Young Master Qiao—why would she ask you to comfort him?!"

韦嬷嬷语气严厉：“满嘴胡言！姑娘今日见乔公子本是与他做个了断，既了断又何须你去替她安慰？”
Xiaguo dropped to her knees: "Is loving Young Master Qiao a crime, Matron?!"

夏果跪下：“嬷嬷，我喜欢乔公子难道有错？”
Xiaguo dropped to her knees. "Matron, is it wrong for me to like Young Master Qiao?"

“还敢反过来问我？今日就让你明白到底错在哪里。其一，姑娘对你有恩，你不该将恩人喜欢的人装在心里；其二，你是姑娘的贴身丫鬟，不该在姑娘受伤时去顾别人的主子，这叫不守本分；其三，你出身卑贱不该有非分之想，别以为有几分姿色就能混进上流社会，要不是姑娘收你当丫鬟，你哪有机会认识这些公子哥？认识也就罢了，偏偏把不该想的人装进心里，还觍着脸要给人做妾室，结果如何？还不是被人拒绝？”
"Daring to talk back?! Let me count your sins for you! First, Young Lady saved you from ruin, yet you coveted the man your benefactor loved! Second, as her personal maid, you abandoned your wounded mistress to chase another household's master—forgetting your place! Third, born humble, you harbored wild delusions—thinking a pretty face buys your way into noble society! Without Young Lady taking you in, would you ever have met gentlemen like him? Meeting him wasn't enough—you offered yourself as his concubine! And look what it got you—thrown straight back in your face!"

夏果慨叹，韦嬷嬷难道是顺风耳，怎么知道她想给乔公子当妾室？
Xiaguo gaped—did Matron Wei have supernatural ears?! How did she know she had offered to be his concubine?!

“嬷嬷怎么知道得如此清楚？”
"How did Matron know all that?!"

“怎么知道？用脚趾头都能猜到你会对乔公子说些什么，你太给姑娘丢脸！我给些你银子，回乡下去吧。”
"A child could guess what nonsense you spouted to Young Master Qiao! You've shamed our Young Lady enough—I'll give you some silver and send you back to the countryside!"

夏果不满道：“嬷嬷凭什么赶我走？我是小姐身边的丫鬟，要走也得小姐赶我，只要是小姐赶我，我二话不说回乡下。”
Indignation flared in Xiaguo: "What right does Matron have to fire me?! I'm Miss's maid—only Miss gets to send me away! If Miss says leave, I'll pack up for the countryside without a peep!"

韦嬷嬷继续严厉地说：“姑娘把管家的权交给我，我当然有权处置任何丫鬟，相信姑娘不会说半个不字。”
Matron Wei's tone stayed iron: "Young Lady handed management of this house to me—I hold full authority over every maid, and Young Lady won't breathe one word against my decision."

夏果大哭道：“呜呜……！嬷嬷绕我一回，以后我再也不敢有非分之想，呜呜……！即使嬷嬷想赶我走，也要等小姐出来，我与她道别后再说，毕竟与小姐主仆一场，呜呜……！”
Xiaguo burst into frantic sobs: "Waaah! Spare me this once, Matron! I'll never harbor wild ideas again, waaah! Even if Matron sends me away, let me wait till Miss comes out so I can say goodbye—we've been mistress and maid together! Waaah!"

“算你有良心。”
"At least you have a shred of conscience left."

韦嬷嬷话音刚落，有丫鬟在外面报告：“嬷嬷，佳和郡主前来拜访。”
Right then a maid called from outside: "Matron, Princess Jiahe is here to visit!"

贵人到来，韦嬷嬷不便继续处理夏果：“起来吧，先回丫鬟房，贵人前来，我懒得跟你掰扯，等姑娘好了，再说你的事。”
With royalty at the gate, Matron Wei set Xiaguo aside: "Get up and go back to the maids' quarters. No time to argue with you while a noble guest waits—once Young Lady feels better, we'll settle your fate."

韦嬷嬷从榻上下来，带人前去迎接。
Stepping off the couch, Matron Wei hurried out to welcome the princess.

夏果松口气，从心里感激佳和郡主来的甚是时候。
Xiaguo let out a shaky breath, blessing Princess Jiahe for showing up right in the nick of time!

话说申简辰担心千里寻，回到幽林别居后左思右想，只能求梦容出面。
Worried sick over Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen had racked his brains back at Youlin Retreat and seen only one lifeline: ask Mengrong to step in.

乔梦容本不知今日淑真与乔公子相见之事，听哥哥说后，很是担心，主动提出要去望月轩安慰淑真。
Unaware of today's breakup beneath the willow, Qiao Mengrong grew worried the instant he told her and volunteered right away to go stay with Shuzhen and comfort her.

申简辰开心不已，将梦容送到望月轩门口，见到是韦嬷嬷带人前来迎接，便知千里寻受伤不轻，他更揪心不已，恨不得亲自冲进去安慰她。
Relieved, Shen Jianchen escorted Mengrong to Wangyue Pavilion's gate—and seeing Matron Wei come out to receive them told him Qianli Xun was hurting bad, making him itch to march straight inside himself.

韦嬷嬷行礼道：“有劳仪王与佳和郡主，我家姑娘今日不便见人，恐二位贵人白跑一趟。”
Bowing low, Matron Wei said, "Forgive the wasted trip, Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe—our Young Lady isn't up to seeing guests today."

乔梦容说：“怎能是白跑？今日我要在望月轩睡一晚，跟淑真好好聊聊。”
Qiao Mengrong smiled: "Not a wasted trip at all! I'm staying overnight at Wangyue Pavilion tonight to have a good heart-to-heart with Shuzhen."

韦嬷嬷感激不尽：“我家姑娘有郡主这等好友真是三生有幸。郡主，里面请。”
Gratitude beamed on Matron Wei's face: "Our Young Lady is blessed three lifetimes over to have a friend like Princess! Right this way, Princess!"

乔梦容担心哥哥，犹豫要不要也请哥哥一起进去坐坐。
Worrying about Prince Yi, Qiao Mengrong glanced back, wondering whether to invite him in for a cup of tea.

申简辰猜到梦容的心思：“今晚，我住在乔府，陪文渊。”
Reading her mind, Shen Jianchen said, "I'm staying at Qiao Manor tonight to keep Wenyuan company."

乔梦容这才放心，带着喜兰进入望月轩。
Reassured, Qiao Mengrong stepped inside Wangyue Pavilion with Xilan.

看不到梦容的身影后，申简辰才登上马车赶往乔府。
Only once Mengrong vanished inside did Shen Jianchen climb back into his carriage for Qiao Manor.

马车上，申简辰又开始自责，他利用梦容的善良达到目的，实在卑鄙。她要是知道实情，不知会如何想他？
All the way there, guilt chewed at him for exploiting Mengrong's sweet heart again—if she ever learned the truth, what would she think of him?

韦嬷嬷引领佳和郡主来到闺房门外，门依然紧闭。她向里面说道：“姑娘，佳和郡主来访，想要与你住一晚。”
Guiding Princess Jiahe to the still-closed bedchamber door, Matron Wei called through: "Young Lady, Princess Jiahe is here and wants to stay the night with you."

千里寻听到梦容前来，从床上爬起，瞧向窗外，天已黑，老天在冬季仿佛怕冷似的，总是早早隐去亮光。
Hearing Mengrong had come, Qianli Xun scrambled off bed, glancing at the window—already pitch dark! Winter skies snuffed daylight out so early.

她走下床，绕到前厅，打开门，掀开厚厚的帘子，顾不上没穿抵寒的外衣，抱住梦容。
Stepping out of bed into the outer room, she unlatched the door, flung back the thick curtain, and wrapped her arms around Mengrong without even a warm coat on.

“你可来了，再不来，我连夜去幽林别居找你的心都有。”
"You're here at last! One more hour and I would've trekked through the dark to Youlin Retreat myself!"

闺房院内，灯笼的光芒微弱，韦嬷嬷还是发现姑娘的眼睛又红又肿。
Even in the dim glow of courtyard lanterns, Matron Wei spotted her mistress's puffy, reddened eyes.

“赶紧进屋，外面冷，你穿这么少会着凉。”乔梦容说完，迅速拉起淑真进入屋内，喜兰也跟着进去。
"Get back inside—it's freezing and you're barely dressed!" Pulling Shuzhen straight indoors, Qiao Mengrong stepped in with Xilan right behind.

佳和郡主到来，千里寻不好再阻止丫鬟进入。
With Princess Jiahe here, Qianli Xun could no longer bar her maids.

韦嬷嬷在门外喊道：“苦荞！夏果！赶紧过来伺候郡主和姑娘！”
Out in the yard, Matron Wei called, "Kuqiao! Xiaguo! Come wait on Princess and Young Lady!"

夏果听到韦嬷嬷喊她，心里高兴，说明嬷嬷已无心再赶她走，便与苦荞走出丫鬟房，来到闺房内伺候。
Hearing Matron Wei call her lifted a boulder off Xiaguo—maybe Matron would not kick her out after all! Hurrying out with Kuqiao, she stepped inside to serve.

夏果查看火炉，再填些炭火；苦荞帮小姐穿上外衣。
Xiaguo checked the brazier and added fresh charcoal while Kuqiao draped a warm robe over her mistress.

韦嬷嬷请示道：“姑娘，吃些东西吧？不能让郡主饿着。”
Matron Wei suggested, "Eat some dinner, Young Lady—can't let Princess go hungry."

乔梦容一听便知淑真还未用晚饭：“我来得匆忙，在幽林别居没吃晚饭，不如在你这里蹭一顿？”
Catching on at once that Shuzhen had not eaten, Qiao Mengrong jumped in: "I rushed over without dinner at Youlin Retreat—mind if I mooch a meal here?"

喜兰心想，郡主明明用过晚饭，却要装作未用，明显是为幽栖居士考虑，她对闺中密友实在好。
Xilan smiled inside: Princess had eaten a full dinner already, pretending just to make Youqi Jushi eat—what a loyal friend!

千里寻没有胃口，考虑到郡主，点头答应：“厨房做了什么？”
No appetite, yet unable to starve her guest, Qianli Xun nodded: "What did the kitchen make?"

“做了姑娘爱吃的小鸡双色莲子羹，还有蔬菜包。我这就让厨房端来。”韦嬷嬷回完，赶去厨房安排。
"Your favorites—two-color lotus seed chicken soup and wild vegetable steamed buns! I'll have them brought right over." Matron Wei hurried off to the kitchen.

千里寻刚穿越而来时在林府吃的就是这两样，美味可口。以后每隔一段时间，韦嬷嬷都会吩咐厨房做，她仿佛永远也吃不腻。
Those were the exact two dishes she had eaten on her first day waking up in Lin Manor—delicious enough she never tired of them whenever Matron Wei had them cooked.

不一会儿，厨房的丫鬟将饭食端来，为二人摆好。
Moments later kitchen maids brought the dishes in and laid the table for the two of them.

夏果在外面做茶，一会儿郡主与小姐吃完饭肯定要喝茶，她需提前预备着。
Out in the outer room, Xiaguo whisked tea ahead of time, knowing Princess Jiahe and her mistress would want tea after supper.

苦荞与喜兰则在饭桌前伺候。
Kuqiao and Xilan stood beside the dining table waiting on them.

乔梦容并不饿，象征性地喝了半碗羹，蔬菜包一个未动。
Not hungry at all, Qiao Mengrong took only half a bowl of soup for show and left the buns untouched.

千里寻看出梦容已吃过晚饭，她感动道：“都吃过，还装。”
Seeing straight through her, warmth welled in Qianli Xun: "Already ate and still putting on a show!"

“看出来了？还不是因为你，要是好好吃饭，我也不至于装，希望是第一次，也是最后一次。”
"Busted! Only because of you—if you ate right, I wouldn't have to fake it! Make this the first and last time, okay?"

“放心，绝对是最后一次，我已决定再也不涉足爱情。”
"Deal—the very last! I've sworn off romance for good."

“什么话！总不能一朝被蛇咬十年怕井绳？还有一个词，对！因噎废食！”
"Nonsense! Once bitten by a snake, scared of rope for ten years? What's that other saying—right, refusing to eat for fear of choking!"

“你说什么都没用，我现在越来越觉得另一个我实在明智，不恋爱，不结婚，只顾忙事业。以后，我会恢复原本的状态。”
"Save your proverbs. Every day I realize the other me had it right—no dating, no marriage, pure focus on career! Back to my original self from now on."

佳和郡主十分不解：“另一个你？说法很奇怪。”
Princess Jiahe tilted her head: "The other you? Strange way to put it."

“一点儿不奇怪，难道郡主心里没有另一个你？”
"Not strange at all—doesn't Princess have another self inside her too?"

“另一个我？”
"Another me?"

梦容用心理解，她内心确实有两个声音，一个说哥哥还像以前一样爱她；另一个声音却说，他不是以前的哥哥。
Turning inward, Qiao Mengrong felt two distinct voices in her own chest: one insisting Brother still loved her just as before, the other whispering this man was not the Brother she used to know.

乔梦容似乎对淑真说的另一个我有所领悟：“好像真有另一个我，淑真，你真厉害，竟能说出这么深奥的话。”
A glimmer of awe crossed her eyes: "Maybe I really do have another self... You say the deepest things, Shuzhen!"

梦容的夸赞，并不能立刻消除千里寻内心的悲伤：“你怎知我今日心情不好？”
Her friend's praise could not melt the ache just yet: "How did you know I was having a rough day?"

“我本不知，哥哥告诉我你今日与乔公子彻底了断，还说乔公子很受伤，我想你肯定难过，所以赶过来陪陪你。”
"I didn't—Brother told me you made a clean break with Young Master Qiao today and said Young Master Qiao was devastated. Figured you had to be hurting too, so I rushed over."

仪王欺骗人的手段实在高，明明一直赖在马车里陪她，却对郡主说文渊很受伤，搞得他好像一直陪着文渊似的。
Prince Yi sure knew how to spin a yarn! Sitting right across from her in the carriage all the way home, yet telling Mengrong how devastated Wenyuan was as if he had been holding Wenyuan's hand the whole time!

千里寻想着就来气，顾不上个人悲伤：“梦容，听你的意思，仪王一直在安慰文渊？”
Annoyance pushed aside her grief: "Sounds like Prince Yi was comforting Wenyuan today?"

“哥哥是安慰过乔公子，要不怎么知道乔公子的状况？”
"Of course he comforted Young Master Qiao—how else would he know how broken up he was?"

“说不定仪王是骗你。”
"What if Prince Yi lied to you?"

“怎么会？哥哥从来不骗我，再说这种事情他没必要欺骗我。”
"No way! Brother never lies to me—besides, why would he lie about something like that?"

千里寻更觉仪王厉害，在心中警告自己往后更要时刻警惕，千万不能被仪王这等情场高手蛊惑，否则只怕做出伤害梦容之事。
More convinced than ever that Prince Yi was a top-tier smooth operator, Qianli Xun warned herself inside to keep her shields locked tight so he could never tangle her up and hurt Mengrong.

千里寻继续边聊边吃，为回馈佳和郡主的好意，将一碗羹喝完，又吃两个包子。
Talking on, she honored Mengrong's kindness by finishing her whole bowl of soup and eating two steamed buns.

﻿闺蜜到来，为她提供倾诉的机会，今晚她打算向梦容诉尽衷肠。
With her closest confidante right beside her, tonight she meant to pour out every ounce of her heart.
