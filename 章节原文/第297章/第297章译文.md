## Chapter 297: The New Segment—Setting the Verse Straight

Leaning in with a wicked grin, Haoyu busted Qiao Wenxian flat: a friend at the silk emporium had spilled that Xiaguo was secretly hunting up an old brothel matron to teach her bedroom tricks!

Unruffled, Qiao Wenxian quoted Mencius—*"Appetite for food and sex is human nature"*—then turned the tables, asking if Haoyu and Shuzhen were "harmonious" (making Haoyu sputter "Super harmonious!"). Raising his cup, Wenyuan swore quietly: "Take care of her for life, Chen Haoyu—mistreat her once and I'll steal her back!"

"Never giving you the chance!" Haoyu shot back, clinking cups before sending Wenyuan back to his friends.

Rejoining Haoyu with Xiangrong, Qianli Xun watched Dongfang Chenze take the stage to unveil a brand-new segment: **"Setting the Verse Straight"**—calling out the malicious street pamphlets attacking Youqi Jushi's *"Jianzi Mulan Hua: Spring Resentment"* and announcing that Cao Ban and An Mingxuan would transcribe today's praise into free rebuttal booklets!

Instantly, the fragile truce inside Qiao Mengrong's chest shattered to ash. *So THIS is why Brother funded the salon!* Even as Shen Jianchen gripped her hand tightly trying to reassure her, every ounce of warmth froze back into lethal venom: *I'll never fall for her smooth tongue again—she MUST die!*

Caught speechless when Dongfang Chenze invited her to address the hall while Mengrong turned stony across the room, Qianli Xun was rescued by Haoyu jumping straight up to deliver a hilariously blunt speech calling every guest, Prince Yi, Princess Consort Yi, and even His Majesty "super good people"—delighting Zhu Xiyan into plugging the emperor's hollow "recovery" of Yanjing (while Shen Jianchen rolled his eyes inwardly at celebrating an empty shell handed back by the Jin).

Then Xiangrong mounted the stage, sweeping her guqin into a haunting melody as she sang Zhu Shuzhen's entire *"Jianzi Mulan Hua: Spring Resentment"*—captivating even Haoyu into vowing he'd memorize every poem his wife ever wrote!

Dongfang Chenze opened the rebuttal by quoting Su Shi (*"Men have sorrow and joy, partings and reunions; the moon dims and brightens, waxes and wanes—nothing has ever been perfect since ancient times"* [TN: Translator's rendering]) to demolish Bai Meiruo's attack on writing sad verses. Slipping her hand coolly out of Brother's, Mengrong burned to vent her fury on someone—and her gaze locked slyly onto Qiao Wenxian.
