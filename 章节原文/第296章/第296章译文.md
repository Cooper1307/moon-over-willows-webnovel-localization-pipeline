## Chapter 296: Could They Really Reconcile?

Spotting Qiao Wenxian gazing straight toward their table—ignoring Haoyu's pointed toast—made Haoyu pout and down his wine alone.

Poets stood in turn proposing classic inspirations: *"Forget-Sorrow Grass"* from the *Book of Songs* (*"Bo Xi"*), *"Fresh Emerald"* from Xing Qun's couplet *"Wild greens stay fresh and emerald through winter's cold, while mountain plums bloom everywhere before the year's last month"* [TN: Translator's rendering], and Qiao Wenxian's *"River-Fresh Green Waves"* from Li Qunyu's *"Resting my oar by the sandy islet as evening falls, fresh river fare, wild greens, and peach-blossom rice. One long song fades deep into evening mist; homeward bound where the blue river's green waves stretch far."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Then Ye Mengde stepped forward, revealing himself with sparkling humor to Zhu Xiyan's playful scolding and offering four lines from his own famously marathon-titled poem (*"Again on the Following Day, Visiting Tianxi Monastery and Climbing Yuhua Terrace with Commissioner Dunli, Transport Intendant Wu Desu, and Commissioner Zhang Sitai, Using the Previous Rhymes Once More"*): *"Late spring sweeps away the lingering blooms; thick leaves hold no cicadas yet. From the high terrace sweeping all four horizons, green wilds float lush and boundless."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Admiring Ye Mengde's unpretentious integrity, Shen Jianchen crowned the dish **"Lush Floating Green Wilds"**, which Zhu Xiyan promptly urged every tavern and manor kitchen to serve daily.

As Dongfang Chenze unveiled Su Shi's *"Dingfengbo"* for the classic appraisal round, Qiao Mengrong—pleased Brother hadn't glanced once at Zhu Shuzhen—rose and swept straight over to Qianli Xun's table, tossing a snide jab at Haoyu before asking Haoyu and Xiangrong to step aside for a private chat.

Sitting down with an icy smirk, Mengrong bragged how Brother had brought her voluntarily and thought only of her since their wedding night.

Reading the hollow ache beneath her bravado, Qianli Xun told a quiet fable of a little girl who bragged endlessly about having a mother—because her stepmother barely loved her—before gently pleading: "Focus on your life with Prince Yi, Mengrong. Look at my husband sitting right over there—he loves me and changed for me. Give me your hand."

When Mengrong hesitantly laid her hand on the table and felt Qianli Xun's warm clasp, hot tears brimmed over. "Time changes everything," Qianli Xun promised earnestly. "I've made up my mind to grow old with Chen Haoyu—and I truly wish you and Prince Yi lifelong harmony."

Swallowing hard, Mengrong whispered, "Fine—I'll trust you once," and returned to Brother's side, warming both Shen Jianchen's and Zhu Xiyan's hearts from afar.

No sooner had she left than Qiao Wenxian wandered over to pour drinks—triggering Haoyu's hilarious possessive banter before Haoyu shooed his wife over to Xiangrong so he could trade "man-to-man" talk with his old rival.
