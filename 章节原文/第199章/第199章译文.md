## Chapter 199: Serving Morning Tea on the First Day of Marriage

On her first morning as a Chen bride, Qianli Xun rose at dawn, dressed cleanly, finished breakfast, and waited to go pay respects to the family elders—waiting and waiting with zero sign of Chen Haoyu.

"Kuqiao, check the back courtyard and tell Chen Haoyu to shake a leg."

Rounding into the back yard to knock on the right-hand door, Kuqiao called out, "Young Master, awake yet? Miss wants you to greet the elders together."

Qinggua cracked the door wide with a grimace. "Dead to the world—cannot drag him up!"

"Alright, I will tell Miss."

Moments later, Qianli Xun marched straight into the back yard and slammed the door wide open! "Chen Haoyu! Out of bed NOW—stay down and I punch you awake!"

Chen Haoyu shot bolt upright clutching the quilt to his chest. "Back off! I am naked under here—do not want a tigress eyeballing me!"

Beside the bed, Qinggua fought back laughter—he had yelled and even kicked the bed to no avail, yet one bark from Young Madam jolted him wide awake!

Spinning around to face the wall, Qianli Xun scoffed, "Like I care to look at your scrawny hide! I count to ten—be dressed and standing before me. One... two... three..."

With Qinggua shoving robes onto him at lightning speed, Chen Haoyu stood fully dressed at "nine."

"Good," Qianli Xun nodded. "I do not know Chen Manor's layout—lead the way to the elders."

"If I knew marriage meant losing my beauty sleep, I would never have bothered!" Chen Haoyu whined.

"Think I wanted to marry you?! You knelt begging me—only pitied you enough to agree!"

Catching Qinggua snickering on the side, Chen Haoyu snapped, "Quit airing my dirty laundry in front of servants!"

"No need to air what hangs out in plain sight—every servant here sees it, just too polite to say! Move it!"

"Wait, Young Madam—Young Master has not had breakfast!" Qinggua piped up.

"Skipping one meal will not kill him."

Shutting his mouth, Qinggua trailed behind them. Stepping past Matron Wei's shut door across the yard, Chen Haoyu jeered, "Look—your precious matron is still sleeping in! Want me to go bang her door down too?"

"Think everyone is a lazy hog like you? Matron Wei was up before dawn heading off to transfer all your assets into my name!"

Regret pricking him all over again over signing away his fortune, Chen Haoyu swallowed his grumble and kept walking.

Picking up Kuqiao at the main hall, they reached the courtyard gate just as Yao Qingyan's personal maid Yingxue curtsied up. "Young Master, Young Madam—Madam sent me to guide you."

"Mother really loves me—sparing me even guide duty!" Chen Haoyu cheered.

Ignoring him, Qianli Xun nodded politely. "Much obliged, Yingxue."

Under her red veil yesterday she had seen zero of Chen Manor; walking through now, she admired its bright, open elegance—best of all, main paths ran dead straight in neat squares, curving only inside gardens, pure mercy for someone with terrible sense of direction. Yet the quiet stroll pulled yesterday's nightmare back: Mengrong's stinging slap, lunging toward steel... One slap had declared their friendship dead. Bearing no grudge, Qianli Xun swore silently never to harm Mengrong again; live calmly inside Chen Manor, stay clear of Prince Yi, and maybe someday their sisterhood would heal. In six months or a year, let Chen Haoyu take a concubine to bear Chen heirs while she enjoyed peace and total freedom!

Arriving at her in-laws' courtyard, where a smiling door boy announced them inside, Yingxue gestured ahead—prompting Chen Haoyu to snap, "Walked into my parents' house a thousand times—say 'after you' to HER, not me!" Smiling smoothly, Yingxue pivoted to usher Young Madam across the threshold.

Inside sat more elders than expected: Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan in the seats of honor, two men to the left, three women to the right. Stepping forward, Qianli Xun sank into a crisp, graceful curtsy. "Your daughter-in-law pays respects to Father-in-Law, Mother-in-Law, and all elders."

Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan beamed in quiet delight—every inch a polished noble daughter today, worlds away from the rock-smashing terror at their gate! Catching his son standing slack beside her without bowing, Chen Jianglin's face went stone cold; Yao Qingyan shot her son a frantic glance until Chen Haoyu muttered a hasty bow: "Father, Mother, your son greets you."

Softening at once lest his bride feel unwelcome, Chen Jianglin smiled and introduced the room. "Meet the Chen elders: on your left sit your Second Uncle and Third Uncle."

Turning left, Qianli Xun greeted them smoothly. "Greetings, Second Uncle and Third Uncle." Both smiled back.

"And on the right," Yao Qingyan added, "your two aunts and Eldest Sister-in-Law."

"Shuzhen greets both aunts and Eldest Sister-in-Law," Qianli Xun curtsied cleanly.

Both aunts nodded while Eldest Sister-in-Law Ye Anya rose warmly to take her hands. "Long have I heard of Zhu Manor's celebrated poetess and hoped to meet you—now we are sisters under one roof! Whenever you pen fresh poems or lyrics, you must let me admire them!"

Giving the textbook modest answer, Qianli Xun smiled. "Merely empty fame outside, Sister-in-Law. If you do not mind my clumsy brush, I will send a poem over soon—hardly worthy of admiration, yet worth merit if it eases a dull afternoon."

Watching from the side, Chen Haoyu rolled his eyes inside: *What a phony! Acting sweet, refined, not one swaggering boast out of her mouth—total hypocrite!*

"Gorgeous, gifted, and silver-tongued—Haoyu struck gold!" Ye Anya laughed before returning to her seat for the tea rite.

Briefed by Matron Wei the night before, Qianli Xun accepted a lacquer tray holding two bowls of tea from a maid, knelt gracefully, and raised it. "Please take tea, Father-in-Law and Mother-in-Law." Maids conveyed the bowls to Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan, who took ceremonial sips. Following suit, she presented fresh bowls to both uncles, then both aunts—skipping peer-generation Ye Anya.

Rites finished, Yao Qingyan said, "Haoyu, take Shuzhen to pay respects to Old Madam."

Remembering Old Madam Xia's fury on her earlier raid—unlikely to go as smooth as her in-laws—Qianli Xun resolved to keep polite and low-key on day one.

Hardly had they cleared the courtyard gate before Chen Haoyu sneered, "Quit the fake act, Zhu Shuzhen! Got guts? Pull your tigress act in front of the elders so they see the real monster!"

Stopping dead, Qianli Xun tilted her head and glared sideways. "Nobody thinks you are mute if you keep your jaw shut! Hear me straight: BOTH faces are real! Treat me warm like your family does, and I return every ounce of grace; act like slime like YOU do, and out comes the tigress! Besides, your parents and grandmother already saw me wreck the gate—who am I fooling?!" She marched right ahead.

"Slime like me?!" Chen Haoyu yelled after her. "What kind of man do you think I am?!"

Spinning on her heel and stepping right into his nose, fists planted on hips and bright eyes blazing wide, Qianli Xun unloaded every single scathing four-character epithet in her vocabulary in one breathless volley: "A spoiled dandy! Libertine rake! Bully of decent women! Brothel hound! Chief of scoundrels! Tyrant of the streets! Compulsive gambler! Sanctimonious fraud! Habitual crook! Arrogant fool! Idle loafer! Gluttonous sluggard! Shameless creep! Shortsighted dimwit! Slick-tongued liar! Pompous peacock! Smug idiot! Ignorant dunce!..." Stopping only when out of breath, she gasped, "Done!"

Hit by a lexical avalanche, Chen Haoyu blinked half-furious, half-awed by a poetess's insult range—*Am I REALLY that awful?!*—while Qinggua and Kuqiao burst into muffled giggles behind them.

Stung by the servants laughing too, Chen Haoyu lashed back where it hurt: "Call me all that trash—and yet YOU STILL MARRIED ME! Didn't see Qiao Wenxian or Prince Yi marrying you, did I?!"

The cheap shot landed dead on the raw bruise. Dropping her hands without another syllable, Qianli Xun turned sharply and strode off ahead, Kuqiao hurrying right behind. Scoring one win at last, Chen Haoyu whistled triumphantly down the path—pausing only at a fork when she steered left: "Hey! Left goes to Second Uncle's—right for Grandmother!"

Turning right without a word, Qianli Xun paused outside Old Madam Xia's gate to enter together, only for Chen Haoyu to brush straight past her and dash inside putting on a syrupy baby voice: "Grandmother! Your grandson missed you SO much!"

Goosebumps prickling up her arms, Qianli Xun gritted her teeth and walked inside to see Chen Haoyu throwing himself into Old Madam Xia's lap on the couch—gross enough to turn her stomach! Dropping into a curtsy, she said evenly, "Shuzhen pays respects to Grandmother."

Beaming over her grandson while intentionally freezing out the bride to knock her down a peg, Old Madam Xia ignored her completely. "What a sweet boy, coming bright and early! Sit right down and share tea with Grandmother."

Delighted by the snub, Chen Haoyu perched opposite and laid it on thick. "Heard on the street about a miracle doctor for sore legs—I will bring him over for you soon, Grandmother!"

"My boy is always so thoughtful!"

"Only because Grandmother spoils me best!"

Leaving Qianli Xun standing like a lamp post in the middle of the floor, the two cooed back and forth—killing every flattering phrase Qianli Xun had rehearsed on the walk over. Steadying her breath, she chose patience while Kuqiao fretted silently beside her: lovely in-laws, terrifying grandmother!

"Lean closer—let Grandmother see if you lost weight," Old Madam Xia cooed.

Chen Haoyu leaned in puffing his cheeks out; she tapped them chuckling. "Quit puffing—Grandmother's eyes are sharp!" Dropping his cheeks, he said, "Not thinner—plumper!" Touching his face again, she fretted, "Lying to Grandmother—definitely thinner!"

Seeing a golden opening to sick Grandmother on Zhu Shuzhen, Chen Haoyu sighed dramatically. "No wonder I look gaunt, Grandmother—marched straight over here on an empty stomach without a bite of breakfast!"

Old Madam Xia snapped straight up. "No breakfast?! Qinggua! How are you looking after Young Master, letting him starve?!"

Qinggua stammered helplessly.

"Do not blame Qinggua," Chen Haoyu sighed like a martyr. "Blame me for marrying a terrifying wife—SHE refused to let me eat!"

At last, Old Madam Xia whipped a blistering glare onto Miss Zhu and barked: "KNEEL!"
