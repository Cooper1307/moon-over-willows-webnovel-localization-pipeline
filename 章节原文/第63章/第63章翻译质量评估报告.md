## 三、修订建议

### 关键修订点

1. **"地痞流氓"**
   - 初译: "local thugs"
   - 修订: "local hooligans" 或保持原译

2. **"未时"**
   - 初译: "the hour of wei"
   - 修订: "the hour of wei (between 1 and 3 pm)" 补充时间范围

3. **"好色之徒"**
   - 初译: "lechers"
   - 修订: "lustful men" 或保持原译

4. **"罩着你"**
   - 初译: "look after you"
   - 修订: "protect you" 或 "have your back" 更口语化

5. **"搞得她将神柳差点忘记"**
   - 初译: "She had almost forgotten about the Divine Willow"
   - 修订: "She'd nearly forgotten about the Divine Willow" 更口语化

6. **"她一个主子没享你几天福"**
   - 初译: "She's your mistress, yet she hasn't enjoyed your service for even a few days"
   - 修订: "She's your mistress, yet she's barely had a few days of your service" 更简洁

7. **神柳场景**
   - 初译: "It fluttered in the wind like a banner"
   - 修订: "It fluttered in the wind like a banner, a silent testament to her hopes" 更诗意
