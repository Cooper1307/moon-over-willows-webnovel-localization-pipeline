# Chapter 63: Settling Xiaguo and Visiting the Divine Willow

Back at the garden, Xiaguo said worriedly: "Young lady, the master doesn't want to see me at the Zhu residence. If I stay here, I'm afraid I'll anger him."

Qianli Xun said with difficulty: "Should I send you back to the countryside?"

Xiaguo panicked. She didn't want to go back at all. If she returned, she'd probably never see Young Master Qiao again.

She knelt down crying: "No! Young lady! I can't go back to the countryside. If those local thugs see me, they'll tell Young Master Chen. Without your protection, I'm as good as dead."

"Get up. Since you don't want to go back, stay with me. You can't stay at the Zhu residence anymore. My father has made his position clear, and I need to respect that. How about this—Nanny Wei bought me a residence outside. You go there first and tidy it up. Maybe one day I'll move in."

Just as Qianli Xun finished speaking, Nanny Wei walked into the garden. Seeing Xiaguo about to get up, she said displeased: "Stay on your knees!"

Xiaguo had no choice but to kneel again.

Nanny Wei scolded: "The young lady took you in to serve her, but how much trouble have you caused her? She's your mistress, yet she's barely had a few days of your service. She's been busy dealing with your problems. If you ask me, the young lady should just send you back to the countryside."

Xiaguo cried: "Wuwu... I don't want to cause the young lady trouble either. I don't go looking for trouble, but trouble finds me. It's all because I was born looking like this. I'll take scissors and slash my face right now. Then everything will be peaceful! Wuwu..."

Xiaguo got up crying and rushed toward the room, ready to find scissors to ruin her looks.

Qianli Xun grabbed her: "Enough! What's the point of making such a scene? Being pretty is a good thing. The only fault lies with lustful men like Lin Jue and Chen Haoyu. Since you're following me, I have a responsibility to have your back. Go back to your room and pack. Get ready to go to the new residence."

Being held back by her young lady, Xiaguo was naturally relieved. If she really ruined her looks, how could she ever face Young Master Qiao? She thanked her young lady and went back to pack her things.

Nanny Wei said: "The young lady is too kind-hearted. Since you've already made arrangements for Xiaguo, I won't say more. Letting her stay at the newly bought residence is a solution. It won't offend the master, and someone will be there to tidy up the place. However, since the master has already agreed, I'm afraid it won't be easy to fool him."

"I've said I don't want to force someone against their will. Since Xiaguo doesn't want to, how could I just hand her over to Lin Jue? Let her stay at the new residence. My father won't see her there, and maybe he'll gradually forget about this."

"I'm afraid it won't be that easy."

"We'll cross that bridge when we come to it. I don't believe Lin Jue would forcibly take a commoner's daughter."

Seeing the young lady's determination, Nanny Wei acquiesced: "Madam said the master allows you to continue living at the Zhu residence. If you don't want to, you can move out."

Qianli Xun was genuinely happy to stay at the Zhu residence because she had come to treat Zhu Shuzhen's family as her own. In the modern era, she had never experienced having multiple family members around her. Since she now had family in this unfamiliar era, she might as well fully experience the warmth of family.

"I want to stay at the Zhu residence for now. We'll see what happens later."

"Whatever the young lady wants, I have no objections."

Just then, Xiaguo came out with a bundle and bowed. Nanny Wei instructed: "Go to Madam's courtyard and find my page boy. He knows the way to the new residence. Have him take you there. Tell him I sent you. There are a few servants at the new residence. Once you're there, arrange for them to tidy up the place daily. The young lady might move in someday."

Xiaguo bowed again: "Thank you, young lady. Thank you, Nanny."

Nanny Wei continued to instruct: "Remember the young lady's kindness. Don't do anything to betray her in the future."

"Don't worry, Nanny. The young lady is the best person to me in this world besides my family. I would never do anything to betray her."

"Good that you know. Go now."

After Xiaguo left, Nanny Wei chatted for a while longer before leaving the garden.

Left alone, with nothing to disturb her, Qianli Xun's feelings for Wenyuan surged again. She wanted to tell him the good news about the divorce as soon as possible.

She looked around but didn't see Kuqiao. Where had that girl been all this time?

Since she couldn't find Kuqiao, she decided to rest for a while. Qianli Xun sat down on the rattan chair, closed her eyes, and thought about the first time she met Wenyuan.

Lvtao came in from outside, bringing many maids. Everyone held lanterns in their hands. As soon as they entered the garden, they started hanging lanterns everywhere.

Lvtao saw her young lady sitting on the rattan chair with eyes half-closed, looking relaxed. She stepped forward and bowed: "Young lady, last time you came back, you asked to hang more lanterns in the garden. I've been busy and haven't had the time. Since you'll be living at the Zhu residence this time, I brought maids today to hang the lanterns."

Qianli Xun kept her eyes half-closed: "Thank you for your thoughtfulness. Have you seen Kuqiao?"

"I ran into Kuqiao on my way here. She said Nanshuang, who serves Young Master Qiao, is waiting outside the residence. She went out to check."

Hearing Lvtao's words, Qianli Xun opened her eyes and smiled. Wenyuan must have sent a message.

The lanterns were soon hung. Lvtao took her leave and left with the servants.

Qianli Xun sat on the rattan chair a while longer, wondering what message Wenyuan had sent.

Just as she was guessing, Kuqiao returned. As soon as she stepped into the garden, Qianli Xun asked expectantly: "Did Wenyuan send a message?"

Kuqiao said in surprise: "Young lady, you're amazing! How did you know Young Master Qiao sent a message?"

Qianli Xun urged: "Don't ask so many questions. Just tell me!"

Kuqiao replied: "Young Master Qiao asked Nanshuang to deliver a message. He's inviting you to go boating on the lake tomorrow."

"What time? Where?"

Seeing her young lady's anxious expression, Kuqiao wanted to laugh: "The hour of wei—between one and three in the afternoon—at West Lake."

Qianli Xun bounced up from the rattan chair and hugged Kuqiao: "Kuqiao, you know what? I've fallen in love with Wenyuan!"

"I know, I know. I've known all along. The young lady should have done this sooner."

Qianli Xun let go of Kuqiao: "From today on, I'll have a new life. It's all thanks to the good luck the bamboo grove brought me."

Kuqiao reminded her: "And the red silk tied on the willow tree."

During her month in the countryside, Qianli Xun's heart and mind were filled only with the bamboo grove. After returning from the bamboo grove, there was finding love, the banquet, and the divorce—she'd nearly forgotten about the Divine Willow. Kuqiao's words reminded her and stirred strange emotions.

"Come with me to see the Divine Willow."

The mistress and maid left the Zhu residence, took a carriage, and went out of the city to the foot of the mountain.

After getting off the carriage, they walked along the path at the foot of the mountain toward the Divine Willow.

When the pond and the Divine Willow appeared before her, Qianli Xun thought of Shen Jianchen. Where exactly was he? If he had traveled through time, had he also found love like she did?

Qianli Xun looked at the red silk hung high on the Divine Willow. It fluttered in the wind like a banner, a silent testament to her hopes. If Shen Jianchen came to the Divine Willow, would he be as excited as she was? Would he look up and see the red silk she had tied high up?

The lover Qianli Xun had dreamed of was Shen Jianchen, but reality had her meet Wenyuan. If Shen Jianchen appeared before her right now and asked, "Qianli Xun, would you be willing to marry me?" how should she answer?

Soon, Qianli Xun already had her answer. She would definitely say: "Shen Jianchen, you're too late. If you had come a step earlier, I would have agreed."

A voice came from behind: "How is Youqi Jushi here?"
