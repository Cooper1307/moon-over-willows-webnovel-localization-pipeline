## Chapter 308: After Scarface’s Release

Moved by his brother’s loyalty—and unwilling to leave him blind as a dying man—Black-Fiend Tiger asked, “Happy with your wife, Brother Jiang?”

“Course! First woman to steal my heart—pried her off Chen Haoyu, planning on kids and building a respectable name for her.”

“Something I can’t keep from you: your wife climbed Mount Tianmu herself and begged me to kill the poetess. That’s the only reason I hit Chen Manor.”

Fury flashed in Master Jiang’s eyes. Grabbing Black-Fiend Tiger’s collar, he snarled, “Did you touch her?!”

Black-Fiend Tiger gave one quiet nod.

Crack! Master Jiang floored him with a thunderous punch, stopping at the cell door as jailers stepped up. “Treated you like a brother and you stab me like this—our brotherhood ends today!”

As the iron padlock slammed shut, Black-Fiend Tiger climbed back to the table muttering, “Only told you *because* you’re my brother.”

Over at the Ferry Superintendent’s new riverfront manor, Chun Jinmo sat daydreaming by the window about Youqi Jushi’s death—ignoring Master Jiang’s storm-cloud face when he strode inside.

Sitting across from her, voice shaking, he asked, “Done anything behind my back lately, Wife?”

“What’re you talking about?” she asked flatly without looking away from the window.

“Get out and shut the door!” he roared at the maid, then slammed the shutters tight and cracked his palm straight across Chun Jinmo’s face!

Clutching her stinging cheek, eyes red, she shrieked, “Why hit me?!”

“I gave you my whole heart and you sneak out to sleep with another man?!”

“Baseless slander—prove it!”

“Black-Fiend Tiger told me himself you climbed Mount Tianmu to hire him against Chen Haoyu’s wife!”

“Lies! Never set foot on Mount Tianmu!”

Snatching her collar, he roared, “He’s headed for the chopping block—dying men don’t lie!” Shoving her to the floor, he glared down.

“Chopping block?!” Confined indoors since Qiao Mengrong’s warning, she knew nothing of the raid’s collapse.

“Because of you! Botched the hit, his whole stronghold got wiped out, and he’s rotting on death row!”

“Botched?! Youqi Jushi’s still alive?!”

Her blurted shock proved every word. Heart shattered, Master Jiang dropped into a crouch clutching his head and wept aloud.

Climbing up rubbing her cheek, Chun Jinmo sneered cold, “My heart belongs to Young Master Chen—insisting on marrying me brought this curse on yourself!”

Rising with tear-streaked cheeks, Master Jiang pointed a trembling finger. “Don’t step half an inch outside this manor! Pull one more filthy stunt and I’ll kill you myself!”

Rattled yet defiant, she snapped, “Go yell at Princess Consort Yi—she’s the one who wanted her dead! Promised to bring me into noble society!”

Crack! A second slap spun her head sideways. “Breathe one more word like that and I butcher you right now! Know what happens if Prince Yi hears you sicced bandits on the woman in his heart?! Your neck goes, and my new post goes up in smoke!”

Sinking to the floorboards weeping—not over her furious husband, only over losing her last shot at Young Master Chen—Chun Jinmo heard Master Jiang lay down his ultimatum: “One month to think! Decide to be my wife and I forgive everything; refuse, and I set you loose—live or die, none of my business!”

Stepping out, he had men padlock her door and nail wooden boards across every window.

Meanwhile, Scarface’s thirty-day sentence ended. Blinking in bright sunlight after a month in the dark, he marched straight toward Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake to collect his hundred taels.

Upstairs in his private booth, Chen Haodong sipped tea riding high—heir selection hit tomorrow, and Princess Consort Yi still backed him since his map had worked fine!

When a waiter announced Scarface, Chen Haodong wasn’t surprised—high time to clear this debt before tomorrow’s triumph.

Shutting the booth door, Scarface sat right down. “Kept my mouth shut and did my time—about that silver?”

“Keep your voice down,” Chen Haodong hissed, sliding a crisp bank note across the table. “Added fifty extra—one hundred fifty taels total. Take it and clear out fast.”

Eyes shining at the one-hundred-fifty-tael note, Scarface tucked it inside his shirt—“Worth every lash and every day in jail!”—and hurried out.

Just clearing West Lake, Scarface ran dead into Chief Constable Wen.

“Whoa—served my full month clean, Chief Constable! Why stop me?”

“Curious how much silver you just collected.”

Bolting backward, Scarface bumped into two constables sealing his retreat. “No idea what you mean—penniless straight out of jail!”

“Search him!”

Seconds later the crisp one-hundred-fifty-tael bank note landed in Wen Xin’s palm. “Heh—one hundred fifty taels! Suspect you robbed somebody fresh out of lockup. Back to the Judicial Office!”

Realizing his jackpot was toast and refusing to eat a fresh prison stretch for nothing, Scarface folded on the walk over. Dropping to his knees before Prince Yi, he wailed out the entire truth from night one to the payoff.

“Willing to testify if I arrest Chen Haodong?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“Not built for big money and done taking falls—spare me and I’ll testify to every word!”

Ordering constables to lodge Scarface comfortably inside the compound under strict house arrest, Shen Jianchen fielded Wen Xin’s puzzled question: “Why wait instead of grabbing Chen Haodong right now at Yongtai Teahouse?”

“Tomorrow’s the Chen family heir selection—Prefect Zhu and officials are all attending,” Shen Jianchen explained. “Dragging Chen Haodong away today slaps the whole Chen clan across the face in front of Lin’an. Let their big ceremony finish, then scoop him up quietly.”

“What if they pick Chen Haodong tomorrow—won’t they waste the whole vote?”

“Who they pick is Chen business; arresting him two days later is ours.”
