## Chapter 308: The Day Scarface Walks Out

Moved by brotherhood and facing the chopping block, Black-Fiend Tiger decided he couldn't die leaving his brother blind. "Your wife hiked up Mount Tianmu herself and begged me to kill that poetess—that's the only reason I hit Chen Manor."

"Did you touch her?!" Master Jiang hissed, grabbing his collar.

One silent nod—and Master Jiang smashed a fist straight into his face, snapping their brotherhood cold and slamming out of the cell.

Marching back into his new waterfront residence, Master Jiang sent the maids out, shut every window, and slapped Chun Jinmo across the cheek—exposing her Mount Tianmu betrayal and Black-Fiend Tiger's imminent execution!

Unrepentant even on the floor, Jinmo mocked him for marrying a woman who loved Haoyu—then foolishly blurted Princess Consort Yi's name, earning a second thunderous slap as Master Jiang warned her that Prince Yi would wipe them both out if he ever found out! Giving her one month locked inside boarded windows to decide whether to stay faithful or walk out free forever, he snapped a heavy padlock across her door.

That very day, Scarface blinked out into sunlight after finishing his one-month sentence and marched straight to Chen Haodong's private booth at Yongtai Teahouse—where Haodong was celebrating tomorrow's heir election (still backed by Princess Consort Yi!) and gladly handed him a crisp **150-tael** banknote before rushing him out.

Half a block from West Lake, Wen Xin and two constables boxed Scarface in, patted down the 150-tael note, and hauled him straight to the Judicial Office on suspicion of grand larceny!

Not willing to eat another prison term for cash he'd already lost, Scarface spilled the entire dirty truth before Shen Jianchen and agreed to testify against Chen Haodong. Keeping Scarface comfortably confined in the yamen barracks rather than arresting Haodong immediately, Shen Jianchen explained gently to puzzled Wen Xin: "Tomorrow is the Chen clan's grand heir election in front of all Lin'an's notables. Grabbing him today slaps the whole Chen family across the face in public—let them wrap up their event first, then we'll pick him up quietly."
