## Chapter 153: Prince Yi Is Shen Jianchen

Facing the gathering, Qiao Mengrong began, "Listening to you all deepened my grasp of this 'Niannujiao.' Most of Shuzhen's lyrics I read before carried tender sorrow, yet this one breaks free with bold, sweeping vigor, above all 'Gullies all smoothed level, heaven and earth painted fair, crowned by the icy moon's pure light.' Had I not watched Shuzhen write it myself, I would have sworn it came from Dongpo Jushi's brush! Grand scope and soaring vision: deep snow leveling every ravine into a vast silver expanse, then night falling as snow stops and a full moon rises to mirror the white earth in dazzling purity. Vast and boundless, untouched by dainty parlor airs—proof Shuzhen holds a far wider world inside her heart."

Only Chen Haoyu and Qiao Wenxian remained. Everyone knew better than to expect poetry from Chen Haoyu, so every eye turned toward Qiao Wenxian.

Locked inside his own private chill, drinking osmanthus wine cup after cup, Qiao Wenxian heard snatches of their praise and tormented himself with guesses. Why write "Clear Winter Sky, No Snow"? Was she reminding him never to forget her rejection out in the snow? Disliking bright winter sunshine to dream up sweeping snowy moonlight—had their breakup left not a scratch on her? Had she never truly loved him?

"Any words to add, Wenyuan?" Shen Jianchen nudged.

Setting down his cup with misty eyes, Qiao Wenxian answered flat, "I only have taste for wine today, none for poetry. Forgive me, nothing to add."

Qianli Xun refused to look his way; once broken off, offering false hope only hurt worse. Itching to meet Qianli Xun beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen wrapped things up right there. "That closes today's poetry salon! Feel free to spend the rest of the day as you please."

The three young gentlemen rose together to bow. "We have errands waiting, Your Highness. Allow us to take our leave."

Shen Jianchen nodded warm. "Drop by Youlin Retreat often, you three, and bring your sisters along next time! Mengrong gets lonely out here; having other girls around to chat with would brighten her days."

"Whenever time allows, we'll visit and bring our sisters!" Dongfang Chenze promised.

Once the three left, Chen Haoyu stood to bow too. "Your Highness, I ought to head back as well."

Thinking of the Zhu-Chen betrothal darkened Shen Jianchen's brow. Bowing out and walking toward the gate, Chen Haoyu muttered to himself: Prince Yi looked mad at me—still sore on Qiao Wenxian's behalf? Then why invite me? Better steer clear of Prince Yi before catching another beating!

Past the outer gate, Qinggua whispered, "Young Master, didn't Prince Yi look displeased with you?"

Chen Haoyu kicked his shin. "Run your mouth again and stay home next time!" Qinggua clamped his lips shut.

Back inside, Qiao Mengrong caught Shuzhen's hand. "Don't rush off. Stay and chat a bit longer?"

Torn—yet burning to confirm whether Prince Yi really was Shen Jianchen—Qianli Xun excused herself smooth. "Love to, except before I left, Mother and Sister-in-law sent word they're coming by Wangyue Pavilion later and I promised to be there. I'll come back another day just to keep you company!"

Shen Jianchen smirked inside: Now you get a taste of how tough lying to Mengrong is!

"Fine, I'll let you go today, on condition you come back the day after tomorrow and stay two full days!" Qiao Mengrong bargained.

Agreeing at once, Qianli Xun rose with Kuqiao and Xiaguo to bid Prince Yi farewell. Back when she didn't suspect he was Shen Jianchen, bowing felt natural; now picturing Shen Jianchen's cocky grin made curtsying to him feel ridiculous, so she gave a quick, minimal bob. "Shuzhen takes her leave, Your Highness."

Shen Jianchen dropped a crisp hint. "Keeping your word to family is admirable, Youqi Jushi. See you don't break other promises either."

"Rest assured, Your Highness, I keep every promise," Qianli Xun shot back. Thinking Brother meant Shuzhen's vow to return in two days, Qiao Mengrong glowed—best brother under Heaven!

As Qianli Xun stepped out with her maids, Qiao Wenxian threw caution aside and trailed straight behind her, Nan Shuang hurrying after. With Young Master Qiao gone, Miao Zhiyu saw no reason to linger; she bowed farewell to Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe and hastened out with her maid.

Right beside the wooden tree platform, Wenyuan's voice caught Qianli Xun. "Shuzhen!"

Halting, she paused a beat before turning. "Yes, Wenyuan, what is it?"

"I... I still can't let you go."

His raw plea tugged hard at her heartstrings. Clamping reason down fast, she put on an icy mask. "What good does that do? I moved on fast! You're betrothed now; stop pulling me aside to talk before gossip starts flying over me again!"

Seeing the flicker of hope die straight out of Young Master Qiao's eyes broke Xiaguo's heart. Turning brisk, Qianli Xun strode on toward the gate leaving Wenyuan behind, relieved that the pang passed in one heartbeat. With a duel awaiting beneath the Divine Willow to test if Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen, she had zero room left for a closed chapter.

Stung raw by her chill, Qiao Wenxian stood rooted to the earth. Walking up behind him, Miao Zhiyu asked hesitant, "Has Youqi Jushi already left, Young Master Qiao?"

Locked in pain, Qiao Wenxian gave no answer; Nan Shuang filled in polite, "Just walked a few steps ahead, Lady Miao." Seeing no comfort of hers would reach him anyway, Miao Zhiyu picked up her pace to catch Youqi Jushi.

Just as Qianli Xun lifted her foot toward her carriage step, Miao Zhiyu hailed her. "Youqi Jushi, may I speak with you a moment?"

Turning back, Qianli Xun asked, "What is it, Lady Miao?"

Taking her hand, Miao Zhiyu drew her aside out of earshot while the maids stood obedient back by the carriage. Letting go, Miao Zhiyu asked low, "Can you ever forgive me, Youqi Jushi?"

"Forgive you for what?" Qianli Xun asked, knowing the answer full well.

"For the Miao and Qiao families sealing a betrothal."

"What's that got to do with you? Parents arranged it, and it's got nothing to do with me either."

"How could it not? Because of our betrothal, you and Young Master Qiao can't be together."

Irritated seeing the winner act apologetic, Qianli Xun kept crisp. "Relax, Lady Miao. Wenyuan and I shared feelings once, yet fate didn't grant us marriage. Since you're his bride-to-be, I let go clean with zero loose threads."

She turned to leave, only for Miao Zhiyu to tug her sleeve again. "You let go, yet Young Master Qiao stays stuck..."

Qianli Xun gave a cool smile. "How Wenyuan feels is no longer my concern. Instead of wasting breath on me, Lady Miao, figure out how to fill Wenyuan's eyes and heart with yourself alone."

Unable to hold her further, Miao Zhiyu let go and watched her carriage pull away, sighing soft. "Young Master Qiao never once held me in his eyes or heart. How on earth do I make him forget you?"

After chatting a bit with Mengrong, Shen Jianchen stepped outside and spotted Qiao Wenxian standing frozen like stone, struck down once more. Turning to Mengrong, he seized the golden excuse. "Head inside and rest, Mengrong. I'll escort Wenyuan home. I might get back late, so turn in early without waiting."

Trusting him with all her heart, Qiao Mengrong nodded. "Don't worry about me, Brother. Talk some comfort into Young Master Qiao along the way."

Once Mengrong withdrew, Shen Jianchen slung an arm around Wenyuan's shoulders. "Quit standing here freezing; I'll ride back with you." Leaning into that sturdy support, Wenyuan walked with him out to the gate just as Miao Zhiyu's carriage wheels faded down the road. Climbing into the Qiao carriage beside Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen signaled Bai Feng to trail behind in Youlin Retreat's own rig.

Wenyuan stayed mute the whole ride, and Shen Jianchen respected his hush. Words could not heal a broken heart; Wenyuan had to walk out on his own. Approaching Lin'an's city gate, needing an exit without entering town, Shen Jianchen spoke up. "Truth is, Wenyuan, I hate leaving Mengrong alone out at the retreat. Mind if I see you off right here?"

Qiao Wenxian gave a bitter smile. "I envy you having someone you love to fuss over. My mess with Shuzhen is past saving; don't let me cut into your time with her. Head back."

"Knew you'd understand!" Hopping down and watching Wenyuan's carriage roll safe through the city gate, Shen Jianchen boarded his own rig at once. "To the foot of the hill!"

Confusion wrinkled Bai Feng's brow. Leaving to escort Young Master Qiao, stopping short at the city gate, then detouring straight to the foot of the hill to see the Divine Willow from the far side instead of walking from the retreat? Strange beyond words, yet keeping his mouth shut, he drove where ordered.

Arriving at the base of the hill and spotting Wangyue Pavilion's carriage parked beside the clearing, everything clicked for Bai Feng: secret tryst with Youqi Jushi! Prince Yi really was sweet on her behind Princess Jiahe's back! Kuqiao had wondered why Miss detoured to the willow too, till Prince Yi stepped out and confirmed every hunch. And Xiaguo smirked inside: acting proper before crowds, sneaking off for secret dates the second backs turned!

Seeing Qianli Xun standing right beneath the Divine Willow waiting for him filled Shen Jianchen with pure elation. Walking close, he waved the servants back. "Step far off, all of you. I have important business with Youqi Jushi."

Bai Feng tugged Kuqiao down the trail while Xiaguo trailed aside; nobody disobeyed an imperial prince.

Without uttering one syllable, Qianli Xun fired a punch straight at Prince Yi. Fists would prove the truth once and for all! Answering crisp with a textbook block and counter, Shen Jianchen met her strike for strike, trading blows not to hurt, but speaking straight through boxing.

Watching from afar, Bai Feng beamed widest of all—first time seeing his master trade live moves! "Whoa, Youqi Jushi fights like a beast! Did they meet up just to spar?!"

"Course my Miss is tough!" Relief washed over Kuqiao; just martial sparring, not a secret romance! Even Xiaguo blinked doubting her own guess.

Every clash lit brighter fire in Qianli Xun's veins. Who else on earth moved with that exact Shen Fist rhythm?! Ten exchanges in, unable to hold back another second, she dropped her guard and threw both arms around Prince Yi. "You ARE Shen Jianchen! You really crossed over with me!"

Held in Qianli Xun's arms for the very first time in either life, Shen Jianchen locked his arms tight around her. "I came all the way to Lin'an hunting for you, and I found you! I recognized you ages ago and tried telling you over and over, only for something to block me every single time!"

Watching them embrace from afar, Kuqiao fretted. "Bai Feng, what on earth is going on between your prince and my Miss?"

Bai Feng scratched his head uneasy. "Hard to say. His Highness is betrothed to Princess Jiahe; hugging Youqi Jushi like that, doesn't that betray Princess Jiahe?"

Xiaguo chimed in breezy, "Betray how? My Miss can always become Prince Yi's concubine!"

"Except the Zhu and Chen families sealed a betrothal," Kuqiao protested. "Miss is slated to marry Young Master Chen!"

"Think of Prince Yi's rank! Canceling a Zhu-Chen betrothal is snapping his fingers! Now I get why Miss hated marrying into the Chens—she wants Prince Yi! Plus if Miss and Prince Yi end up together, won't it be twice as easy for you two to see each other?"

Xiaguo's spin almost tempted Kuqiao and Bai Feng, until guilt pricked Bai Feng fast: if Princess Jiahe ever learned Prince Yi held Youqi Jushi in his arms, it would shatter her!
