## Chapter 240: Second Young Master Wins Back His Freedom

By the time Qianli Xun returned to Chen Manor, dinner had passed; not hungry, she waved off Kuqiao's offer of food. Master Jingguang's counsel still rang clear in her mind—time to set herself free by calling off the war. Changing into fresh clothes, she walked out to inform Chen Haoyu in person.

Stepping from Suxin Thatched Hall under the glow of courtyard lanterns, she spotted a figure approaching and waited until Chen Haofei stepped into the light.

"Good evening, Second Sister-in-law," he bowed respectfully.

"What brings you here?"

"Came to check on Second Brother."

Catching on at once, she raised a brow. "The elders sent you, didn't they?"

Chen Haofei owned up straight. "Grandmother and Eldest Aunt swore not to meddle, yet their hearts ache—couldn't come themselves, so they sent me to peek in."

"Why put me in charge if they can't bear watching?" Qianli Xun sighed—the elders were bound to be let down that she was calling it off after two days anyway. "Since you're here, walk with me to Boya Study."

Trailing behind her toward the back courtyard, Chen Haofei silently prayed his own future bride would not be fierce like this, locking her husband in back rooms!

Boya Study stood unlocked yet flanked by Asheng and Sanbao like two temple door-gods. Chen Haofei stuck his tongue out inwardly: No wonder Second Brother calls her a tigress—even posting sentries!

"You two can knock off," Qianli Xun told the guards. "No need to watch him anymore."

Thrilled, both replied, "Yes, Miss!" and hurried off while Kuqiao pushed the door open.

Stepping inside behind her, Chen Haofei called cheerily, "Second Brother! Came to visit!"

Emerging from the bedroom with Qinggua, Chen Haoyu lit up—doubly thrilled at spotting Zhu Shuzhen herself! Throwing an arm around Haofei, he crowed, "Took you long enough!"

Haofei whispered quick in his ear, "Scared of Second Sister-in-law—Grandmother and Aunt sent me to check on you!"

Releasing him, Chen Haoyu grinned wide. "Look me over—fit as a fiddle! Your second sister-in-law treats me great, plenty to eat and wear—feel sharper than I have in years!"

Poor Second Brother—she must have broken him so bad he's terrified to complain! Haofei pitied him silently, while Qianli Xun stood amused at his theatrical act.

Suddenly Chen Haoyu swung toward her. "Hey! Disappeared all afternoon—where were you till dark?"

"Since when do I report my schedule to you?" she shot back, then spoke plain. "Chen Haoyu, starting right now, you're free. Lockup's over, and my grudge against you is wiped clean. I'll draft a fresh copy of our old contract tomorrow—just stamp your thumbprint."

Free—joy leaped up, then crashed at "fresh contract." "No way!"

"What, begging for more discipline?"

"I mean the contract! Burned it already—no need to rewrite it!"

Puzzled, she frowned. "Meaning what?"

Stepping closer, Chen Haoyu said, "My thinking flipped completely these past two days. Those old terms don't fit anymore—why waste ink copying them?"

Fair point. "Suit yourself. Think over what you want tonight, come to Suxin Thatched Hall tomorrow, and we'll hash out fresh terms. Chat with your cousin—I'm turning in."

Just as she reached the threshold, he called after her, "Hey! Seriously done holding those rumors against me?"

Without turning back, Qianli Xun delivered a crisp parting line. "If a dog bites a person, does the person drop down and bite the dog back? What's the difference between them then? I'm human—refuse to turn into a dog, so I'm done beefing with that hound."

Even insulted, Chen Haoyu grinned beaming at her back. "Definitely fell for me—why else set me free so fast and drop hints about loyalty?! Hehe!"

Completely lost, Chen Haofei blinked. "Hint about loyalty? Did I miss something, Second Brother?"

"Dead certain!" Chen Haoyu turned beaming. "Didn't you hear her bring up dogs?"

"Uh, yes?"

"What are dogs famous for?"

"What?"

"Loyalty to their owner! She's hinting I better stay faithful to her! Fair enough—handsome as I am, girls drop at my feet outside; natural she worries!"

Chen Haofei shot a helpless look at Qinggua. "Was that what she meant?"

Qinggua just waved both hands in mute surrender.

"Tell Grandmother and Mother it's late so I won't disturb them tonight," Chen Haoyu instructed. "I'll pay respects first thing tomorrow—and not one bad word about your second sister-in-law!"

Hurrying straight to Eldest Uncle's courtyard, Chen Haofei found Old Madam Xia waiting right beside his uncle and aunt. After two days of white-knuckling her locked gate, dusk had shattered the old woman's nerves—worried ten laps broke his legs and apologizing to maids crushed his pride—so she had marched to Eldest Son's quarters demanding news. Chen Jianglin picked Haofei as spy: Shuzhen liked him enough to gift him poems, and the boy never stirred gossip.

Bursting inside, Haofei announced, "Good news! Great news!"

Tension melted from both women's faces. "Spill it!" Old Madam Xia urged.

"Second Sister-in-law unlocked Second Brother, called off all punishments, and wiped the rumor grudge clean!"

All three faces instantly fell. "Quit after two days?!" Chen Jianglin frowned. "Nobody reforms in two days!"

"We hoped she'd whip him into shape AND spark romance," Old Madam Xia sighed. "Looks like wishful thinking—she never cared."

"Guess Haoyu—and we—just lack the luck," Yao Qingyan murmured.

Realizing what they actually hoped for, Haofei hurried to add, "Don't fret yet, Grandmother, Uncle, Aunt! Can't tell how Second Sister-in-law feels, yet Second Brother is a totally different man around her! Looks head-over-heels for her—even warned me not to say one bad word about her!"

Instant sunshine! All three burst into relieved laughter.

"Huge leap forward!" Old Madam Xia beamed. "Already protective of his wife—think he'll still roam wild?!"

"Proof Shuzhen is a miracle-worker!" Chen Jianglin grinned. "Two days beat fifteen years of my lectures!"

"Did Haoyu send any message?" Yao Qingyan asked eagerly.

"Said it's too late to disturb you tonight, so he'll come pay respects first thing tomorrow morning!"

Tears of joy brimmed in Yao Qingyan's eyes. "When has Haoyu ever been this considerate? Usually vanishes without a word—now checking in politely!"

"Speak for yourself—he always visits me!" Old Madam Xia teased.

Chen Jianglin grinned. "Every time he visits you, Mother, he's fishing for handouts!"

"Asking me means he keeps me in his heart—when did you ever ask me for anything?"

Knowing better than to argue, Chen Jianglin just smiled as the night wound down.

Walking his grandmother home—bearing zero jealousy toward his favored cousin—Chen Haofei bowed at her gate, only for her to pause him. "Wait, Haofei. Grandmother believes you're going to win an official post."

"May your words come true, Grandmother."

"Know why I say that?"

"Because I'm family and you root for me."

"Wrong. Past fifty, I've read plenty of hearts. Yours is pure and upright—Heaven never lets a soul like yours down. You're the hope that lifts the Chen clan's standing."

Warmth flooded his chest. "Thank you, Grandmother."

"Off to bed now."

Next morning after breakfast, stepping out of Boya Study with Qinggua, Chen Haoyu bumped straight into Matron Wei—who had vanished during his entire lockup only to pop up the second he went free.

"Congratulations on regaining your freedom, Young Master," she curtsied.

He jumped a fraction. "Didn't even see you around—how'd you know Zhu Shuzhen let me out?"

"Seen or unseen, nothing in this courtyard escapes me."

Feeling as if her sharp eyes x-rayed straight into his ribs, he backed off fast. "Carry on—got errands!"

Watching him bolt, Matron Wei smiled softly. "Miss stays steady, Young Master turns fast—progress at last."

Passing Suxin Thatched Hall, Chen Haoyu gazed fondly at the door plaque. "Say, Qinggua, how come 'Suxin Thatched Hall' suddenly looks classy and refined to me today?"

"Because Young Madam named it and lives inside," Qinggua nailed it dryly.

Chen Haoyu chuckled, sweeping a fond eye across the courtyard. "Whole yard looks gorgeous today! Wife's got real taste—lotus brightening the pond, and that hehuan silk tree right there! 'Harmonious union'—think she planted it hinting she wants lifelong harmony and wedded bliss with me? Hehe!"

From reckless playboy straight into lovesick daydreamer—Qinggua sighed silently, then answered when his master nudged him: "Hint or not, having a hehuan tree in Moxiang Courtyard definitely promises wedded bliss and devotion!"

"Sweet talk! First payday, you get first bonus! Come on—time to see Father and Mother!" Full of fresh fire to amount to something real, Chen Haoyu strode out of Moxiang Courtyard.
