第186章 终于决定成婚日
## Chapter 186: At Last Setting the Wedding Day

陈浩宇从赌坊回到陈府已很晚，爹娘早已睡去，今晚无法将赌坊发生的事向爹娘言明。
他直接回到自己居住的院子，青瓜与其他下人也都休息，陈浩宇只好一个人进屋。
屋里没有点灯，黑漆漆的，应该是下人以为他不会回来，将灯全部熄灭。
陈浩宇摸黑来到床边，懒得脱衣裳，倒头便睡。
约莫睡过两刻钟， 他的头脑变得清晰许多，睁开眼睛坐起来，自语道：“对啊，我难过个什么劲儿？只要我娶朱淑真，字据不就作废？根本没必要让家人知道在赌坊立下字据之事。再说爹娘本来就同意两家的亲事，只是奶奶不同意，可奶奶听我的。我明日告诉大家，想三日内完婚，不就皆大欢喜？”
陈浩宇轻松许多，又躺下，盖上被子，继续自语：“蔣爷啊蔣爷，我让你既谋不到陈家家产，也别想得到春金沫，明日我便去找朱淑真商议成亲之事。嘿嘿……！”
陈浩宇觉得自己实在聪明，一下子将事情想明白；也觉得蔣爷实在笨，搞了一整，没什么用。
……
天亮后，青瓜打开公子的房门，进屋这里摸摸那里摸摸，没有发现灰尘，不必叫丫鬟前来打扫，正要掩门而出，听到公子的声音。
“朱淑真！娶你可以，各过各……！”
青瓜纳闷：“怎么是公子的声音？昨晚明明没回来。”
纳闷归纳闷，还是进里屋瞧瞧。青瓜轻手轻脚走进里屋，发现床上的确躺着公子，和衣而睡，被子被蹬到一边，嘴里嘟嘟囔囔说着梦话
青瓜担心公子着凉，走过去将被子从公子身下往出拽。
拽被子的过程将陈浩宇从睡梦中拽醒，他睁开睡眼，一眼瞧见青瓜，伸腿踢去：“谁让你吵醒我！”
青瓜快速躲闪开来：“公子冤枉我，我怕您着凉，给您盖被子。”
陈浩宇坐起来：“被你打扰，好梦都没了，还睡个什么劲儿？给我拿身干净的衣裳来。”
青瓜一边取衣裳，一边说：“公子直唤朱家姑娘的名字，恐怕做的是噩梦。”
竟然在梦中唤朱淑真，陈浩宇认为肯定是昨晚睡前念叨过她的缘故。
“少废话，赶紧给本公子换衣裳。”
青瓜先给公子把身上的衣裳脱下，一个纸团掉落在地上，正要去捡，陈浩宇立刻阻止：“别动！”
青瓜只好住手。
陈浩宇弯腰捡起，将纸团展开，用心读一遍上面的内容：“去，把火折子拿来。”
青瓜放下手中的衣裳，找来火折子，打开，吹出火苗，递给公子。
陈浩宇接过，用火点燃纸张，扔在地上，只见字据很快化为灰烬。陈浩宇决定等成亲后，再从蔣爷手中要回另外一份。
陈浩宇把火折子熄灭，递给青瓜：“还愣着干什么？赶紧给本公子穿衣裳。”
青瓜将火折子放在一边，再把干净衣裳给公子换上，又出去吩咐丫鬟把洗脸水端进来，他手拿面巾，伺候公子洗脸。
陈浩宇洗完脸，青瓜递上面巾。
陈浩宇接过，擦完脸将面巾往青瓜怀里一扔：“老爷和夫人可起来？”
By the time Chen Haoyu stumbled back into Chen Manor, night had grown deep and his parents were long asleep, leaving no chance to confess tonight. Heading straight to his own courtyard, where Qinggua and the other servants had turned in too, he pushed into his pitch-dark room—his servants must have assumed he was staying out again—felt his way to the bed, and collapsed fully clothed.

青瓜接住面巾，整理好放在桌上：“这个时辰老爷和夫人早起了。”
Half an hour later, his racing head cleared. Sitting bolt upright in the dark, he muttered aloud, "Wait a minute! What am I panicking over?! Marry Zhu Shuzhen and that contract turns to scrap paper! Nobody in the family even needs to find out I signed it! Parents already want the wedding, only Grandmother stalled—and Grandmother listens to me! Tell everyone tomorrow I want to wed within three days, and problem solved!"

陈浩宇二话不说往外走。
Relief flooding his chest, he flopped back down and pulled up the quilt with a smug chuckle. "Master Jiang, oh Master Jiang—you get neither Chen wealth nor Chun Jinmo! Tomorrow I go straight to Zhu Shuzhen and fix the wedding! Heh!" Feeling downright brilliant—and convinced Master Jiang was an idiot—he drifted right off.

青瓜在后面喊：“公子要不要用早饭？”
At dawn, Qinggua cracked open his master's door, checked the outer room for dust—clean enough to skip maids—and started to pull it shut when a voice drifted from inside: "Zhu Shuzhen! Fine, I will marry you—each lives their own life...!"

陈浩宇根本没搭理，径直走出去，直奔爹娘的院子。
Blinking in surprise—when had Young Master come home?—Qinggua tiptoed into the bedchamber and found him sprawled in rumpled clothes, quilt kicked aside, muttering in his sleep. Worried he might catch cold, Qinggua reached down to tug the quilt free from underneath him.

到达时，爹娘正在屋中用早饭，他们边吃，边聊，边笑。从小到大，陈浩宇几乎没见爹娘红过脸，他既满足又羡慕，如若他成亲，也能与对方像爹娘一般相亲相爱那该多好。
The tug jerked Chen Haoyu awake. Blinking sleep away and spotting Qinggua, he lashed out a kick. "Who told you to wake me?!"

想到三日之内要娶朱淑真，陈浩宇兴致全无：“爹、娘，我有事跟你们讲。”
Dodging clear, Qinggua protested, "I was only covering you up so you would not catch a chill!"

陈江林与姚轻琰才发现儿子进来。
Sitting up, Chen Haoyu grumbled, "Ruined a good dream—no point sleeping now. Fetch fresh clothes!"

姚轻琰问：“用过饭吗？”
"Calling Miss Zhu's name in your sleep sounds more like a nightmare," Qinggua remarked while pulling out clean robes.

“还没。”
"Quit yapping and dress me!"

姚轻琰吩咐丫鬟给儿子添副碗筷。
As Qinggua helped peel off his outer robe, a crumpled paper ball hit the floor. Qinggua reached down—only for Chen Haoyu to bark, "Hands off!"

丫鬟把碗筷放好，陈浩宇乖乖坐过去。丫鬟又开始给他的碗中添豆蔻汤，添完后，站在一旁伺候。
Scooping it up himself, Chen Haoyu flattened it out, scanned the ink lines once more, and ordered, "Bring the fire-starter."

陈江林懒得抬眼瞧儿子，还是叮嘱道：“今日笋肉馒头不错，尝一个。”
Blowing a flame alive, Qinggua handed it over. Chen Haoyu touched the tip to the contract and dropped it to the tiles, watching it curl into ash—once the wedding wrapped up, he would go rip the second copy out of Master Jiang's hands too.

陈浩宇哪有心情吃早饭，直言道：“爹、娘，我想三日内娶朱淑真过门。”
Snuffing the flame, he tossed it back. "Standing around for what? Finish dressing me!"

儿子破天荒答应婚事，还这么着急，倒把陈江林和姚轻琰搞得既疑惑又高兴。
After getting dressed and washing his face, Chen Haoyu tossed the damp cloth straight into Qinggua's arms. "Are Father and Mother up?"

姚轻琰笑着批评道：“你这孩子，不愿意时，想方设法破坏，现在同意，又着急忙慌地要成亲，总得让我和你爹缓缓。”
"Up ages ago at this hour."

儿子终于说出符合心意之事，陈江林放下筷子，才抬头瞧儿子：“婚事早已差人筹备好，三日内举行，也不算匆忙。只是爹不明白，你为何这么快改变想法？”
Without another word, Chen Haoyu strode straight out, ignoring Qinggua's call about breakfast, and marched to his parents' courtyard.

陈浩宇瞎编道：“儿子在外面浪荡许久，也该成家立业，再说见爹娘如此恩爱，儿子羡慕不已，也想娶个娘子回来恩恩爱爱过日子。”
Inside, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan sat sharing breakfast—eating, chatting, laughing warm and easy. Growing up, Chen Haoyu had almost never seen them cross a harsh word, filling him with both comfort and quiet envy; how sweet it would be if he could share a bond like that with a wife someday. Then the thought of marrying Zhu Shuzhen inside three days wiped away the warmth. "Father, Mother, I have something to say."

夫妻两个头一次听儿子说些懂事的话，甚是高兴。
Noticing him walk in, Yao Qingyan asked, "Eaten yet?"

姚轻琰说：“三日内就三日内，我与你爹这就差人到朱家打声招呼。你呢，负责说服老太太，再去你两位叔叔处，向他们报个喜，就说今日准备一番，明日迎娶朱家姑娘过门儿。”
"Not yet."

陈浩宇无不答应，端起豆蔻汤，一口气喝下：“我这就去找奶奶。”
She signaled a maid to lay another bowl and chopsticks. Taking his seat as the maid ladled hot cardamom broth for him, Chen Haoyu heard his father remark without looking up, "The bamboo-shoot and pork buns are good today—try one."

夏老太太刚用过早饭，孙子就来，她甚是欢喜，叫孙子坐在榻上与她聊天儿。
With zero appetite, Chen Haoyu blurted straight out, "Father, Mother—I want to marry Zhu Shuzhen within three days."

陈浩宇拉住老太太的手，先是一通撒娇：“奶奶，孙儿在外面逛时，时时刻刻把奶奶挂在心上。”
Both parents froze in stunned delight at their son's sudden flip.

夏老太太笑着批评：“又哄我，你要是挂念奶奶，还会去青楼赌坊？”
Yao Qingyan laughed and chided him, "Look at you—first fighting tooth and nail to wreck the match, now rushing headlong into a wedding! Give your father and me a second to catch our breath!"

“没想到奶奶什么都清楚。”陈浩宇猜想肯定是青瓜告诉老太太的。
Setting down his chopsticks to look squarely at his son for the first time, Chen Jianglin asked, "Everything is already prepared, so three days is no rush at all. Still, explain to your father why the sudden change of heart."

“奶奶虽然老，但不糊涂，以后可不能再去那些地方，听到没？”
Thinking fast, Chen Haoyu spun a tidy tale. "I have fooled around outside long enough—time to settle down. Watching Father and Mother so devoted made me envious; I want a wife of my own to live happily with too."

“听到听到，奶奶的教诲孙儿肯定听。”还要找朱淑真，陈浩宇不想太耽误工夫，“奶奶，我打算娶朱家姑娘过门，跟爹娘商议好，明日成亲，希望奶奶不要再想耗着的事儿。”
Hearing sensible words from their son for the first time ever filled the couple with joy.

夏老太太立刻没了笑脸：“她来陈家都闹成那样，你还愿意娶？”
"Three days it is!" Yao Qingyan declared. "Your father and I will send word to Zhu Manor right away. Your job is convincing Grandmother and sharing the good news with your two uncles—tell them we set up today and welcome Miss Zhu home tomorrow!"

“愿意愿意，孙儿突然发现，就喜欢像朱家姑娘那样的。”
Agreeing to everything, Chen Haoyu downed his cardamom broth in one gulp. "Going to Grandmother right now!"

夏老太太怀疑道：“该不会有人逼你娶她？告诉奶奶是谁，奶奶帮你出面。”
Finishing breakfast herself, Old Madam Xia beamed at her grandson's early visit and patted the couch beside her. Taking her hand, Chen Haoyu laid on the charm thick. "Whenever I am out, Grandmother never leaves my thoughts for a second!"

老太太一语说中，陈浩宇赶紧掩饰：“哪会有人逼我娶她？奶奶别胡乱想，是我自己想通。朱家姑娘才貌双全，又厉害，以后有她在我身边，既能教我读书作诗，还能保护我，好处多多。”
"Sweet-talking me again!" Old Madam Xia chuckled. "If you missed Grandmother so much, would you still run off to brothels and gambling dens?"

老太太忧虑道：“可她名声不好，你要是娶她，陈家的长辈绝不会将陈家产业交到你手中。”
"Nothing gets past Grandmother!"—must have been Qinggua snitching!

原来奶奶担心此事，陈浩宇趁机表露心迹：“奶奶太高估我，就我这样儿，哪能继承陈家产业？有东哥在，他可是做生意的好手，奶奶别对我有任何期望。至于朱家姑娘名声不好，都是孙儿的错，不能怪她。”
"Old, not blind! Stay clear of those filthy places from now on, hear me?"

孙子一番通情达理的话，老太太心意逐渐改变：“你既没有继承家业之心，奶奶也不强求。之所以想耗着两家的婚事，也是因为你不乐意，既然你愿意娶，奶奶还有什么好阻止的？娶就是。只是明日成亲，会不会太匆忙了些？”
"Loud and clear—I obey every word Grandmother teaches!" Mindful he still had to track down Zhu Shuzhen, he cut straight to the chase. "Grandmother, I have decided to marry Miss Zhu. Father and Mother already agreed on tomorrow, so let us drop the stalling plan."

“不匆忙，我爹该准备的已准备好，今日再把府中布置一番，明日举办婚礼刚刚好。”
The smile vanished off Old Madam Xia's face. "After the riot she raised at our gate, you STILL want to marry her?!"

老太太虽担忧孙子，怎奈他已同意，老人家又不愿拗着孙子，笑容中带着忧虑点头答应。
"I do! Suddenly hit me—a fiery girl like Miss Zhu is my exact type!"

陈浩宇又去二叔和三叔房中报喜。
Suspicion narrowed Old Madam Xia's eyes. "Did someone force you into this? Tell Grandmother who, and I will deal with them!"

二叔陈江树知道是儿子陈浩东谋划的结果，只觉一切顺心如意，装作替侄子高兴的样子。
Bull's-eye! Covering fast, Chen Haoyu waved it off. "Who could force me? Stop worrying, Grandmother—I came around all on my own! Miss Zhu has beauty, brains, and fierce fists: with her by my side, she can coach my reading and poetry AND protect me! Pure win!"

三叔陈江木搞不明白，朱家姑娘闹成那样，大哥为何还要给自家儿子把她娶回来？他不好多说什么，挤出笑容点头道喜。
"Yet her reputation is bruised," Old Madam Xia fretted. "Marry her, and the Chen elders will never hand the family business to you."

陈浩宇叫上青瓜准备去望月轩时，整个陈府的下人已忙碌起来，到处布置。
So that was her hang-up! Seizing the chance to speak his true mind, Chen Haoyu laughed. "Grandmother gives me way too much credit! Look at me—could I ever run the Chen empire? Brother Haodong is a natural merchant; pin no hopes on me! As for Miss Zhu's reputation, that was all my blunder—cannot blame her."

陈浩宇心中提不起多少兴致，为了婚后能继续自由，还得去找朱淑真在婚前将一切商议妥当。
Softened by his candor, Old Madam Xia relented. "If you have no wish to inherit the business, Grandmother will not force you. I only stalled because you hated the match; if you want to wed her, why would I stand in your way? Marry her, then! Only—isn't tomorrow terribly rushed?"

青瓜跟公子上了马车坐好后，见公子双眉紧锁，关切地问：“公子，明日成亲是您提出来的，为何不高兴？”
"Not rushed at all! Father already had everything lined up; decorate the manor today and we are set for tomorrow!"

“你懂个屁！把嘴闭上！不准跟我说话！”陈浩宇骂完，将身体斜靠着，吩咐道，“告诉车夫去望月轩。”
Still uneasy yet unable to cross her beloved grandson, Old Madam Xia nodded with a worried smile.

青瓜不敢再多问，对车夫说：“去城东的望月轩。”
Next, Chen Haoyu stopped by his second and third uncles to spread the news. Knowing his own son Haodong had engineered the whole turn, Second Uncle Chen Jiangshu glowed inside while putting on a warm congratulatory smile. Third Uncle Chen Jiangmu was utterly baffled why Eldest Brother still welcomed such a fierce bride, yet offered polite blessings anyway.

马车启动后，青瓜保持沉默，一直观察公子，他的眉头始终未舒展，还不时叹气。在青瓜的印象中，从未见过公子这样，能看得出他有多么不想娶朱家姑娘。
By the time Chen Haoyu grabbed Qinggua to head for Wangyue Pavilion, servants were already scrambling across Chen Manor hanging lanterns and red silk. Still heavy-hearted despite the bustle, he needed to lock down ground rules with Zhu Shuzhen before tomorrow so he kept his freedom afterward.

沉默一路，来到望月轩，陈浩宇竟破天荒要求进去等。门口的小厮将他带进会客厅后，跑去通知姑娘。
Once inside the carriage, seeing his master's tight frown, Qinggua ventured, "Young Master, you were the one who asked to wed tomorrow—why the gloomy face?"

刚将椅子坐热，千里寻带着苦荞前来。
"What do you know?! Shut your trap and do not say another word!" Slumping back against the cushions, Chen Haoyu barked, "Tell the driver Wangyue Pavilion!"

陈浩宇竟然恭敬地站起来，拱手行礼。
"East quarter—Wangyue Pavilion!" Qinggua relayed, keeping silent the rest of the way as he watched his master sigh over and over. Never had he seen Young Master look this miserable—plain as day how little he wanted this wedding.

别说千里寻惊讶，连青瓜都惊得不行，公子什么时候这么礼貌过！
Reaching Wangyue Pavilion, Chen Haoyu amazed everyone by walking straight inside to wait in the reception hall. Hardly had he warmed a chair when Qianli Xun walked in with Kuqiao.

千里寻问：“陈浩宇，你这是做什么？怎么还给我行礼？少见，少见。”
Chen Haoyu shot to his feet and gave a deep, crisp cupped-hand bow.

陈浩宇行完礼，站直后说：“明日你就要嫁到陈家，我行礼是希望你嫁过去后对我高抬贵手，不要找我麻烦。”
Both Qianli Xun and Qinggua gaped—when had this pampered rake ever bowed with manners?!

明日出嫁之事千里寻并不知，既惊讶又气愤：“什么？明日成亲？昨日嫂嫂还告诉我要推迟一段时日，怎么又是明日？你们陈家人是不是想一出是一出？把我和朱家当猴耍！”
"What act is this, Chen Haoyu?" Qianli Xun asked. "Bowing to me? Now I have seen everything."

陈浩宇劝道：“反正两家退婚已不可能，你迟早得嫁给我，晚嫁不如早嫁，我来呢，是想和你商议……”
Straightening up, Chen Haoyu spoke earnestly. "Tomorrow you marry into the Chen family. I bow to beg you to go easy on me once you move in and stay off my back."

刚要说关键内容，见青瓜杵在一旁，怕他听去告诉爹娘，伸出一脚踢过去：“滚！别杵在这儿，影响我说话。”
Blindsided, Qianli Xun flared up at once. "What?! Tomorrow?! My sister-in-law JUST told me yesterday it was postponed! Now it is tomorrow?! Does your whole Chen clan just make things up on a whim, playing me and the Zhus for fools?!"

青瓜躲闪开：“这就滚。”
"Breaking off the match is dead anyway," Chen Haoyu urged. "Sooner or later you have to marry me—might as well get it over with. I came today to work out—" Catching sight of Qinggua standing nearby, afraid he might run straight to his parents, he swung a foot at him. "Scram! Quit standing around eavesdropping!"

等青瓜出去后，陈浩宇对苦荞说：“你也出去吧。”
"Scramming right now!" Qinggua hopped clear and darted outside.

千里寻正在气头上，拦道：“怎么？还想撵我的人？”
Once Qinggua exited, Chen Haoyu looked at Kuqiao. "Step outside too."

陈浩宇不敢再说什么，继续刚才的话题：“既然我俩成亲不可避免，不如商议一下婚后该如何相处。我的建议是你过你的，我过我的，两不相干，如何？”
Still fuming, Qianli Xun snapped, "Now you think you can boss my people around?!"

千里寻还未从明日要成亲的气愤中走出，根本顾不上陈浩宇的建议：“你说明日要成亲，我就得信你？你算老几！朱家还没给我传来消息，我凭什么跟你商议？”
Backing down fast, Chen Haoyu got to the point. "Since marrying is unavoidable, let us agree how to live afterward. My offer: you live your life, I live mine, zero interference. Deal?"

事出突然，朱淑真不接受也能理解，可总不能把昨晚在赌坊发生的事告诉她，只怕会被她嘲笑一番。
Too livid over the ambush wedding date to care about terms, Qianli Xun snapped, "Who put you in charge of wedding dates?! Zhu Manor has not sent a single word—why would I bargain with you?!"

正为难，外面传来下人的声音：“姑娘，夫人、少夫人和大公子来了。”
Unwilling to humiliate himself by confessing last night's gambling fiasco, Chen Haoyu hung stuck—when a servant's call rang from outside: "Miss, Madam, Young Madam, and Eldest Young Master are here!"
