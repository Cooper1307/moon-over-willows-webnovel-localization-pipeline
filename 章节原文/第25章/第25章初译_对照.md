第25章 多愁善感思人生
# Chapter 25: Sentimental Thoughts on Life

---

千里寻坚持涂抹吉祥药房买来的治伤之药，仅仅两日，额头的伤已好。她坐在铜镜前瞧着，担心留下疤痕，瞧来瞧去没发现一丝疤痕，以后不用再顶着伤到处招摇。

Qianli Xun religiously applied the medicine from Jixiang Pharmacy. In just two days, the wound on her forehead had completely healed. She sat before the bronze mirror, fretting over the possibility of a scar, but after checking and rechecking, she found her skin as flawless as before. Finally, she no longer had to parade around with a bandaged head.

---

额头的皮肤白净细腻，本应开心，千里寻却怎么也高兴不起来。和离失败后，她还真有了朱淑真的多愁善感。

Yet, looking at her fair and delicate skin, she couldn't bring herself to smile. Following her failed divorce, it seemed she had truly inherited Zhu Shuzhen's melancholic disposition.

---

昨晚，做了一个梦，梦见申简辰，他在梦中说：“千里寻，做我女朋友吧！”她破天荒地点头答应。申简辰把她紧紧搂在怀里，她则用心体会着爱情到来时的甜蜜。申简辰的怀抱很温暖，她醒来时，觉得身上还有被他拥抱的感觉和温度。

Last night, she had dreamed of Shen Jianchen. In the dream, he had asked, “Qianli Xun, be my girlfriend！” To her own surprise, she nodded and agreed—something she had never done before. Shen Jianchen held her tightly, and she savored the sweet arrival of romance. His embrace was so warm that when she woke up, she could still feel the lingering pressure of his arms and the heat of his body.

---

经历和离之战，败下阵来，越是有失败的感觉，对爱情的渴望越强烈，千里寻萌生想要谈一场恋爱的念头，这是她在另一个时代从来没有过的想法。婚姻却像一堵厚厚的围墙，把她紧紧包围着，让她的感情不得自由。

Defeat in the divorce battle only fueled her longing for love. The more frustrated she felt, the stronger the desire grew. For the first time, Qianli Xun actually *wanted* to fall in love—a thought she had never entertained in her own era. But this marriage was like a thick, impenetrable wall, caging her heart and suffocating her freedom.

---

千里寻不由叹一口气：“唉！”

Qianli Xun couldn't help but sigh. "Ah!"

---

叹息过后，想起朱淑真的一首词，与此时此刻她的心境十分吻合：“苦荞，准备纸笔，我要作词。”

The sigh reminded her of a ci by Zhu Shuzhen that matched her mood perfectly. "Kuqiao, prepare paper and ink. I want to write."

---

苦荞端着刚做好的茶过来：“已准备好，小姐先喝茶再写不迟。”

Kuqiao brought over freshly made tea. "It's ready, Miss. Drink your tea first."

---

千里寻坐在铜镜前将茶喝完，放下茶盏，起身走到书桌前坐下，提笔在砚台里蘸满墨汁，开始书写：

Qianli Xun finished her tea in front of the bronze mirror. She set down the cup, rose, walked to the desk, and sat. Dipping the brush in the inkstone, she began to write:

---

斜风细雨作春寒，对尊前，忆前欢。曾把梨花，寂寞泪阑干。芳草断烟南浦路，和别泪，看青山。
昨宵结得梦夤缘，水云间，悄无言。争奈醒来，愁恨又依然。展转衾裯空懊恼，天易见，见伊难。《江城子·赏春》

Slanting wind and fine rain make spring cold.
Before the winecup, I recall past joys.
I used to hold pear blossoms,
My lonely tears flowing down.
On the South Port Road where fragrant grass and mist meet,
With parting tears, I look at the green mountains.

Last night I had a dream-link,
In the mist and water, silent and wordless.
Alas, upon waking,
Sorrow and regret remain the same.
Tossing and turning under the covers, in vain regret,
The sky is easy to see, but it is hard to see you.
— “Jiangchengzi: Spring Cold” (tr. Xu Yuanchong, *The West Wind Blows: Selected Song Lyrics*, Dolphin Press / China Intercontinental Press) [TN: This ci poem was written by Zhu Shuzhen, a renowned female poet of the Song Dynasty. The poem expresses her longing for a lost love and the pain of separation.]

---

写完后，一心沉浸在词句中，笔依然捏在指间，用心品着：“展转衾裯空懊恼，天易见，见伊难。”

After finishing, she immersed herself in the lines. The brush still rested in her fingers as she savored the words, "Tossing and turning under the covers, in vain regret, the sky is easy to see, but it is hard to see you."

---

申简辰到底有没有跟她一样穿越而来？如果有，人又在哪里？正想着，笔尖的墨汁不断向下聚集，“吧嗒”，不偏不倚掉在“伊”字上，辛苦写下这些字，白白浪费功夫。

Did Shen Jianchen transmigrate here with her? If so, where was he? As she pondered, ink from the brush tip gathered and dripped down—plop—it landed right on the character “you，” wasting all her hard work.

---

千里寻搁下笔，把纸卷起：“扔了吧？”

Qianli Xun put down the brush and rolled up the paper. "Throw it away?"

---

苦荞接过，放入旁边的木桶中，端起木桶往外走。

Kuqiao took it and placed it in the wooden bucket nearby, then carried it outside.

---

木桶里放满卷起来的纸张，千里寻好奇，问道：“里面怎么放了那么多？”

The wooden bucket was full of rolled-up papers. Qianli Xun grew curious. "Why so many?"

---

苦荞回过头：“都是小姐平日写废的，让我积攒多了，拿到大门口放着。”

Kuqiao turned. "They're all the papers Miss threw away. I save them up, and when there's enough, I leave them outside the gate."

---

千里寻疑惑地问：“大门口放着？”

Qianli Xun asked, confused, "Outside the gate?"

---

苦荞解释道：“小姐把它们当垃圾，有人却把它们当成宝贝，每隔一段时日会有人守在林府门外等着捡垃圾呢！以前在朱府也是。”

Kuqiao explained, "Miss treats them as trash, but someone else treats them as treasure. Every so often, people wait outside the Lin residence to collect the discards. It was the same at the Zhu residence."

---

千里寻甚觉好笑，又懒得去理会，此时此刻她的内心充满莫名的忧伤。

Qianli Xun found it amusing but didn't dwell on it. Right now, her heart was filled with an inexplicable sadness.

---

苦荞端着木桶来到林府门外，把木桶放在地上，从里面抽出小姐刚刚写下的词，拿在手中，对周围喊道：“你们都出来吧！尽快拿走，我好去忙正事。”

Kuqiao carried the wooden bucket outside the Lin residence gate. She set it down, pulled out the ci her miss had just written, and called out, "Come out! Take them quickly so I can get back to work."

---

话音刚落，不知从哪里窜出五六个小厮和书童，一拥而上，将木桶里的字画抢个精光，临走前不忘说句：“感谢苦荞姐姐！”

Before she finished, five or six young servants and page boys rushed out from somewhere and swarmed the bucket, snatching all the calligraphy and paintings inside. As they left, they called back, "Thanks, Sister Kuqiao!"

---

苦荞叫住一位小厮：“南双！你等会儿！”

Kuqiao stopped one of the young servants. "Nanshuang! Wait a moment!"

---

南双回到苦荞身边：“苦荞姐姐有什么吩咐？”

Nanshuang returned to Kuqiao's side. "What does Sister Kuqiao need?"

---

苦荞问：“听说你家公子人在汴梁，你怎么没跟着去？”

Kuqiao asked, "I heard your young master is in Bianliang. Why didn't you follow him?"

---

“以前苦荞姐姐见我从不问我家公子的事，怎么今日突然关心起来？”

"In the past, Sister Kuqiao never asked about my young master. Why the sudden interest today?"

---

“以前没想起来，现在想起了呗！问那么多干嘛？”

"I didn't think of it before, but now I have. Why ask so many questions?"

---

“姐姐也不想想，我要是去了，谁帮我家公子搜集幽栖居士的诗词？”南双展开拿到的纸张，“只可惜今日手气不好，纸上只有两句。”

"Sister, think about it. If I went, who would help him collect Youqi Jushi's poems and ci?" Nanshuang unrolled the paper he had gotten. "It's just bad luck today. There are only two lines on this paper."

---

苦荞把手中的纸递给南双：“我家小姐刚写的，很完整，送给你家公子。”

Kuqiao handed him the paper in her hand. "My miss just wrote this. It's complete. Give it to your young master."

---

“苦荞姐姐为何对我家公子这么好？”

"Why is Sister Kuqiao so good to him?"

---

“还用问？乔公子最会解我家小姐的诗词，他们视对方为知己。”

"Do I need to ask? Young Master Qiao is the best at understanding my miss's poems and ci. They consider each other soulmates."

---

“既如此，不如让你家姑娘直接把写好诗词送给我家公子得了，也省得我整日在林府门外蹲守。”

"If that's the case, why doesn't your miss just give the poems directly to him? That would save me from waiting outside the Lin residence every day."

---

“那怎么行？本来外面就有我家小姐跟你家公子的风言风语，把没有的事都说出事来，所以更需谨慎，你以后还是在门口蹲守吧，有好的，我自然会给你。”

"How can that work? There are already rumors about my miss and him. People are making something out of nothing, so we need to be even more careful. You should keep waiting outside the gate. When there's something good, I'll naturally give it to you."

---

南双谢过后离开，苦荞端起木桶往回走，半路上碰到姑爷。

Nanshuang thanked her and left. Kuqiao carried the wooden bucket back. Halfway there, she ran into Lin Jue.

---

林觉没好气地质问：“又有人捡拾没用的垃圾？”

Lin Jue asked angrily, "Are people picking up useless trash again?"

---

苦荞没好气地说：“回姑爷，在您看来是垃圾，外面的人却当成宝。”

Kuqiao replied coldly, "Young Master, what you consider trash, others treat as treasure."

---

林觉气道：“我今日就派士兵守在府外，看谁敢来，来一个抓一个，来两个抓一双。”

Lin Jue said furiously, "Today I'll send soldiers to guard outside the residence. Let's see who dares to come. I'll catch them one by one, two by two!"

---

“我劝姑爷不要招惹与我家小姐相关的任何事，只怕她知道，再用拳头打你。”苦荞说完，继续往里走。

"I advise Young Master not to provoke anything related to my miss. I'm afraid she'll find out and beat you again with her fists." After Kuqiao finished, she continued walking inside.

---

林觉盯着苦荞的背影，甚是气愤：“这丫头以前不这样说话，越来越放肆！真是有其主必有其仆！”

Lin Jue glared at Kuqiao's retreating back, furious. "This girl never used to talk like that. She's getting more and more out of line! Like mistress, like maid!"

---

这两日林觉也思考，不放走朱淑真到底是对还是错？从理性角度讲是错，从感性讲，他舍不得放她离开，哪怕把她搁在林府，一根毫毛不碰，只要还挂着林觉娘子的名号，他都会觉得受用。尤其是临安才子们对朱淑真只能看在眼里，却吃不到嘴里，林觉想想都觉得面上有光。当然，最重要的是他对她依然有情。

These past two days, Lin Jue had also been thinking. Was keeping Zhu Shuzhen the right choice?
Rationally, no. Emotionally, he couldn't bear to let her go. Even if he just kept her in the Lin residence without touching a single hair—as long as she still carried the title of Lin Jue's wife, he'd feel satisfied.
Especially knowing Lin'an's scholars could only admire Zhu Shuzhen from afar but never have her—just thinking about it made Lin Jue feel proud. Of course, the real reason was simpler: he still had feelings for her.

---

苦荞回到竹兰居，见小姐坐在书桌前发呆，没敢过去打扰，放下木桶，开始收拾屋子。

Kuqiao returned to Zhulan Residence to find her miss sitting at the desk, lost in thought. Not daring to disturb her, she set down the bucket and began tidying the room.

---

千里寻穿越成朱淑真，竟然开始像她一样多愁善感。她猛然想起朱淑真四十五岁离开人世，她千里寻可不想英年早逝。既然不想，就不该学朱淑真，要记住自己是千里寻。

Having transmigrated into Zhu Shuzhen, Qianli Xun found herself growing as sentimental as her predecessor. Suddenly, she remembered—Zhu Shuzhen had died at forty-five. Qianli Xun had no intention of dying young. If she didn't want that fate, she couldn't keep imitating Zhu Shuzhen. She needed to remember who she really was.

---

她拍拍脑门，心里想：“对啊！我是千里寻，千里寻可从来不伤春悲秋，振作起来，仔细想想，接下来的人生该怎么过，我可不愿像朱淑真四十多岁郁郁而终。”

She patted her forehead. *That's right! I'm Qianli Xun. Qianli Xun doesn't waste away feeling sorry for herself. Pull yourself together! Think about how you want to live the rest of your life. You're not going to die of depression in your forties like Zhu Shuzhen.*

---

千里寻立即振作精神，她要付诸行动改变命运，到底该怎么做？暂时想不到更好的办法，不如迷信一回。

Qianli Xun shook off her gloom. She needed to take action, to change her destiny. But how? With no better plan in mind, she decided to try something—superstition included.

---

“苦荞，你认不认识看相算命特别准的人？”

"Kuqiao, do you know anyone who's very accurate at fortune-telling?"

---

苦荞停下手中的活儿：“小姐可是问对人，我虽不认识，却知道有这么一位。临安城外有座山，山上有个尼姑庵，里面的女住持人称净光法师，能知过去未来，老爷就曾找她看过。”

Kuqiao stopped her work. "Miss, you've asked the right person. Although I don't know her personally, I know there's such a person. Outside Lin'an city, there's a mountain with a nunnery on it. The abbess there is called Master Jingguang. She can know the past and future. The master once had his fortune told by her."

---

“我爹？”

"My father?"

---

苦荞点点头：“我听说，老爷那会儿年轻，与净光法师聊过后，一路青云直上，才成为今日的临安知府。”

Kuqiao nodded. "I heard that when the master was young, after talking with Master Jingguang, his career skyrocketed, and he became today's Prefect of Lin'an."

---

“这么厉害！”

"Really? That amazing?"

---

苦荞接着说：“不过，据说净光法师不随便给人看，她有条件，只有达到她的条件才能与她见上一面。”

Kuqiao continued, “But they say Dharma Master Jingguang doesn't read fortunes for just anyone. She has conditions—only those who meet them get to see her.”

---

“什么条件？”

"What conditions?"

---

苦荞为难道：“嗯，我不大清楚。”

Kuqiao hesitated. "Um, I'm not exactly sure what they are."

---

千里寻来了兴致，站起来：“我要去会会净光法师，多带些银子。”

Intrigued, Qianli Xun stood. "I want to meet this Dharma Master Jingguang. Bring plenty of silver."

---

“这就要去吗？”

"Are we going now?"

---

“现在就去！”

"Now!"

---

苦荞带好银子，跟在小姐身后，来到林府门外，吩咐车夫套了马车，往临安城外而去。

Kuqiao grabbed the silver and followed her miss outside the Lin residence. She told the coachman to ready the carriage, and they set off toward the city gates.

---

千里寻无心欣赏街头闹市的繁华，她只想为自己的人生找到一线曙光，更想知道还有没有机会摆脱与林觉的婚姻，能不能在属于朱淑真的时代有属于千里寻的人生。

Qianli Xun had no interest in the bustling streets. She only wanted to find a glimmer of hope—a chance to escape this marriage, a way to live her own life in Zhu Shuzhen's era.

---

不知马车走了多久，终于出了临安城；也不知在临安城外又走了多久，终于到达山脚下。

The carriage rattled on for who knows how long. Finally, they left Lin'an behind and reached the foot of the mountain.

---

车夫提醒道：“夫人，到了。”

"Madam, we're here," the coachman called.

---

千里寻第一个跳下马车，一座像刷满绿色彩釉的高山矗立在眼前。

Qianli Xun was the first to leap down. A mountain, lush and green as if glazed, towered before her.

---

苦荞从马车上下来后，指着大山说：“小姐，尼姑庵就在半山腰，剩下的路马车上不去，我们得步行。”

Kuqiao climbed down and pointed up the slope. "Miss, the nunnery's halfway up. The road's too narrow for the carriage from here. We'll have to walk."

---

千里寻没有说话，朝大山的入口处走去。她从小练习千里拳，爬山对她来讲并不费体力。尽管现在的身体不是千里寻的，但她的潜意识依然能够操纵朱淑真的身体，让它和千里寻的身体同样敏捷。能顺利使出千里拳，力度丝毫不减，就是最好的证明。

Qianli Xun didn't answer. She strode toward the mountain path. She'd trained in Qianli Fist since childhood—climbing a mountain was nothing. Though this body wasn't hers, her subconscious still controlled it, making it as agile as her original. The fact that she could still use Qianli Fist at full strength proved it.

---

山路并不宽敞，又窄又弯曲，从山路的泥土被踩得结实程度判断，尼姑庵的香火应该不错。

The mountain path was narrow and winding. Judging by how packed the dirt was, the nunnery must be well-attended.

---

千里寻看到有五六个香客顺着山路往下走。当第一个香客走近时，千里寻和苦荞侧身让路，顺便问道：“大娘，尼姑庵还有多远？”

Five or six pilgrims were coming down. When the first one drew near, Qianli Xun and Kuqiao stepped aside and asked, "Auntie, how much farther to the nunnery?"

---

“不远不远，再走一阵就到。”大娘继续往下走。

"Not far, not far. Just a little more." The woman kept walking.

---

千里寻和苦荞则继续向上爬，碰到其他香客也是侧身让路，只怪山路太窄，仅能容纳一人行走。

Qianli Xun and Kuqiao kept climbing, stepping aside whenever they met pilgrims coming down. The path was barely wide enough for one person.

---

山路周围的景致不错，千里寻边走边欣赏，不知不觉来到半山腰，才有平坦之地，视野变得开阔起来。一座尼姑庵出现在眼前，门口是进进出出的香客，男男女女均有，还有卖香火的摊位。

The scenery along the path was lovely. Qianli Xun admired it as she walked. Before she knew it, they'd reached a flat area halfway up. The view opened up, and a nunnery appeared. Pilgrims—men and women—came and went through the gate. Stalls selling incense lined the area.

---

千里寻抬头看到门楣上的匾额写着“承佛慈力”四字：“苦荞，买些香火，随我进去拜拜。”

Qianli Xun looked up at the plaque above the gate. Four characters read: "By the power of the Buddha's compassion." "Kuqiao, buy some incense. Follow me inside."

---

苦荞买来三柱高香，跟着小姐走进尼姑庵。

Kuqiao bought three joss sticks, and they entered the nunnery.

---

迎面是正殿，很多人在殿外先把香点燃，再一一插入大香炉中。

The main hall stood before them. Outside, people lit their incense and placed it in the large burner one stick at a time.

---

苦荞照做，完成后，又跟随小姐走进正殿内。

Kuqiao followed suit, then accompanied her miss inside.

---

刚把脚踏进去，两边传来念佛的声音：“南无阿弥陀佛，南无阿弥陀佛……”声音很好听，像是在吟唱一首歌曲，让人听后不由心境舒畅。

The moment they stepped inside, chanting filled the air from both sides: "Namo Amitabha, Namo Amitabha..." The melodic voices soothed the mind.

---

千里寻向两边瞧去，各盘腿坐着六名女尼，神态自然安详，口中念念有词，吟唱的声音正是从她们口中发出。

Qianli Xun glanced to either side. Six nuns sat cross-legged on each side, their expressions serene as they chanted in unison.

---

高大的神像前面放着三个蒲团，千里寻合掌跪拜；苦荞也学着小姐的样子拜起来。她们每拜一下，会响起一声敲击木鱼的声音“梆！”伴着佛号，甚是和谐。

Before the towering statue lay three prayer cushions. Qianli Xun pressed her palms together and knelt. Kuqiao followed her example. With each bow, the wooden fish sounded—*thwack*—keeping perfect time with the chanting.

---

三拜之后，两人起身，朝木鱼声传来的方向瞧去，佛像旁边坐着一位女尼，她面前的桌上放着木鱼和犍稚。

After three bows, they rose and looked toward the source of the wooden fish. Beside the statue sat a nun, the instrument and its striker on the table before her.

---

千里寻走上前有礼道：“小师傅，我想找住持净光法师，不知她今日可在？”

Qianli Xun approached respectfully. "Sister, I'm looking for Abbess Jingguang. Is she available today?"

---

女尼起身说：“跟我来。”

The nun stood up and said, "Follow me."

---

千里寻一阵庆幸，没想到如此容易，心想苦荞得到的信息应该有误，不由盯了一眼苦荞。
