## Chapter 25: Chapter 25: Melancholy Thoughts on Life

千里寻坚持涂抹吉祥药房买来的治伤之药，仅仅两日，额头的伤已好。她坐在铜镜前瞧着，担心留下疤痕，瞧来瞧去没发现一丝疤痕，以后不用再顶着伤到处招摇。
Applying Jixiang Pharmacy's healing ointment faithfully, Qianli Xun saw her forehead cut vanish clean in just two days. Examining herself in the bronze mirror, she found not a trace of a scar—no more walking around advertising a wound.

额头的皮肤白净细腻，本应开心，千里寻却怎么也高兴不起来。和离失败后，她还真有了朱淑真的多愁善感。
Smooth, clear skin should have cheered her up, yet gloom clung tight. After the failed divorce, she seemed to have caught Zhu Shuzhen's melancholy.

昨晚，做了一个梦，梦见申简辰，他在梦中说：“千里寻，做我女朋友吧！”她破天荒地点头答应。申简辰把她紧紧搂在怀里，她则用心体会着爱情到来时的甜蜜。申简辰的怀抱很温暖，她醒来时，觉得身上还有被他拥抱的感觉和温度。
Last night she had dreamed of Shen Jianchen. In the dream he asked, "Qianli Xun, be my girlfriend!"—and for once she nodded yes. He pulled her tight against his chest, and she tasted the sweet warmth of love for the very first time. His embrace felt so warm she woke convinced its heat still lingered across her shoulders.

经历和离之战，败下阵来，越是有失败的感觉，对爱情的渴望越强烈，千里寻萌生想要谈一场恋爱的念头，这是她在另一个时代从来没有过的想法。婚姻却像一堵厚厚的围墙，把她紧紧包围着，让她的感情不得自由。
Defeat in the divorce battle sharpened her hunger for real love—sparking a longing to fall in love that she had never felt back in her old life. Yet this unwanted marriage rose like a thick wall around her heart, barring every path to freedom.

千里寻不由叹一口气：“唉！”
A heavy sigh escaped her lips: "Ah..."

叹息过后，想起朱淑真的一首词，与此时此刻她的心境十分吻合：“苦荞，准备纸笔，我要作词。”
Right behind the sigh rose a lyric of Zhu Shuzhen's that fit her mood to the bone. "Kuqiao, lay out brush and paper—I want to write a ci."

苦荞端着刚做好的茶过来：“已准备好，小姐先喝茶再写不迟。”
Carrying fresh whisked tea over, Kuqiao smiled. "Already set up, Miss. Drink your tea first before writing."

千里寻坐在铜镜前将茶喝完，放下茶盏，起身走到书桌前坐下，提笔在砚台里蘸满墨汁，开始书写：
Drinking the tea by the mirror, Qianli Xun set the bowl aside, moved to the study desk, dipped her brush full in the inkstone, and wrote:

斜风细雨作春寒，对尊前，忆前欢。曾把梨花，寂寞泪阑干。芳草断烟南浦路，和别泪，看青山。
Slanting wind and fine rain chill the spring; before my wine cup, I recall old joys. Once before pear blossoms, lonely tears fell in streams. Fragrant grass fades in mist along the southern parting road; through farewell tears, I gaze at green hills.

昨宵结得梦夤缘，水云间，悄无言。争奈醒来，愁恨又依然。展转衾裯空懊恼，天易见，见伊难。《江城子·赏春》
Last night in dreams a tender tie was forged; amid water and clouds, we stood without a word. Yet waking finds sorrow and resentment waiting just as before. Tossing beneath the quilt in vain regret—Heaven is easy to behold, yet seeing you is hard. ("Jiangchengzi: Admiring the Spring") [TN: Translator's rendering]

写完后，一心沉浸在词句中，笔依然捏在指间，用心品着：“展转衾裯空懊恼，天易见，见伊难。”
Brush still poised in her fingers, she stayed lost in the closing line: "Tossing beneath the quilt in vain regret—Heaven is easy to behold, yet seeing you is hard."

申简辰到底有没有跟她一样穿越而来？如果有，人又在哪里？正想着，笔尖的墨汁不断向下聚集，“吧嗒”，不偏不倚掉在“伊”字上，辛苦写下这些字，白白浪费功夫。
Had Shen Jianchen transmigrated here too? And if he had, where in this vast world was he? As her thoughts wandered, excess ink gathered at the brush tip and—plop—dropped dead-center onto the character "you" (伊), ruining all her careful work.

千里寻搁下笔，把纸卷起：“扔了吧？”
Setting the brush down, she rolled up the sheet. "Throw it away."

苦荞接过，放入旁边的木桶中，端起木桶往外走。
Kuqiao took the scroll, tucked it into the wooden tub beside the desk, and hoisted the tub toward the door.

木桶里放满卷起来的纸张，千里寻好奇，问道：“里面怎么放了那么多？”
Noticing the tub brimmed with rolled sheets, Qianli Xun asked, "Why are there so many in there?"

苦荞回过头：“都是小姐平日写废的，让我积攒多了，拿到大门口放着。”
Kuqiao glanced back over her shoulder. "All your discarded practice drafts, Miss! You always have me save them up and leave the tub outside the front gate."

千里寻疑惑地问：“大门口放着？”
"Outside the front gate?"

苦荞解释道：“小姐把它们当垃圾，有人却把它们当成宝贝，每隔一段时日会有人守在林府门外等着捡垃圾呢！以前在朱府也是。”
"What you call trash, outsiders treat like gold!" Kuqiao explained. "Every few days folks camp outside Lin Manor waiting to snatch up your scraps—same back at Zhu Manor!"

千里寻甚觉好笑，又懒得去理会，此时此刻她的内心充满莫名的忧伤。
Amused yet too low-spirited to care, Qianli Xun let it go as quiet sadness filled her chest.

苦荞端着木桶来到林府门外，把木桶放在地上，从里面抽出小姐刚刚写下的词，拿在手中，对周围喊道：“你们都出来吧！尽快拿走，我好去忙正事。”
Carrying the tub outside Lin Manor's main gate, Kuqiao set it down, pulled out the fresh lyric her mistress had just written, kept it in her hand, and called out: "Come on out, all of you! Grab what you want fast so I can get back to chores!"

话音刚落，不知从哪里窜出五六个小厮和书童，一拥而上，将木桶里的字画抢个精光，临走前不忘说句：“感谢苦荞姐姐！”
On cue, five or six page boys and study attendants popped out from corners and swarmed the tub, clearing every scrap in seconds while calling out, "Thanks, Sister Kuqiao!"

苦荞叫住一位小厮：“南双！你等会儿！”
Kuqiao stopped one page boy: "Nan Shuang! Hold up!"

南双回到苦荞身边：“苦荞姐姐有什么吩咐？”
Nan Shuang trotted back. "What can I do for you, Sister Kuqiao?"

苦荞问：“听说你家公子人在汴梁，你怎么没跟着去？”
"Heard your young master's up in Bianliang—why didn't you go with him?"

“以前苦荞姐姐见我从不问我家公子的事，怎么今日突然关心起来？”
"You never asked about my master before, Sister Kuqiao—what's got you curious today?"

“以前没想起来，现在想起了呗！问那么多干嘛？”
"Didn't think of it before, thought of it today! Quit asking questions!"

“姐姐也不想想，我要是去了，谁帮我家公子搜集幽栖居士的诗词？”南双展开拿到的纸张，“只可惜今日手气不好，纸上只有两句。”
"Think about it, Sister—if I left, who'd collect Youqi Jushi's poems for my master?" Unrolling the scrap he had grabbed, Nan Shuang sighed: "Rotten luck today—only two lines on this sheet."

苦荞把手中的纸递给南双：“我家小姐刚写的，很完整，送给你家公子。”
Kuqiao handed him the intact scroll from her hand. "Miss just wrote this—complete from start to finish. Give it to your master."

“苦荞姐姐为何对我家公子这么好？”
"Why so generous to my master today, Sister Kuqiao?"

“还用问？乔公子最会解我家小姐的诗词，他们视对方为知己。”
"Need you ask? Young Master Qiao reads my Miss's poetry deeper than anyone—they're true confidants."

“既如此，不如让你家姑娘直接把写好诗词送给我家公子得了，也省得我整日在林府门外蹲守。”
"Then why doesn't your mistress just send her finished poems straight to my master instead of making me camp outside Lin Manor every day?"

“那怎么行？本来外面就有我家小姐跟你家公子的风言风语，把没有的事都说出事来，所以更需谨慎，你以后还是在门口蹲守吧，有好的，我自然会给你。”
"Not a chance! Enough rumors already swirl outside twisting nothing into gossip about Miss and your master—better stay cautious! Keep waiting by the gate; whenever something good turns up, I'll slip it to you."

南双谢过后离开，苦荞端起木桶往回走，半路上碰到姑爷。
Thanking her, Nan Shuang darted off. Lifting the empty tub, Kuqiao headed back inside—and ran straight into Lin Jue halfway down the lane.

林觉没好气地质问：“又有人捡拾没用的垃圾？”
"More people scavenging useless trash?" Lin Jue snapped.

苦荞没好气地说：“回姑爷，在您看来是垃圾，外面的人却当成宝。”
"Trash to you, Son-in-law—pure treasure to everyone outside," Kuqiao shot back.

林觉气道：“我今日就派士兵守在府外，看谁敢来，来一个抓一个，来两个抓一双。”
Lin Jue bristled. "Watch me station soldiers outside the gate today—arrest every scavenger who shows up, one by one or two by two!"

“我劝姑爷不要招惹与我家小姐相关的任何事，只怕她知道，再用拳头打你。”苦荞说完，继续往里走。
"Better leave anything touching my Miss alone, Son-in-law—she hears about it, she'll give you another taste of her fists!" With that, Kuqiao walked straight past him.

林觉盯着苦荞的背影，甚是气愤：“这丫头以前不这样说话，越来越放肆！真是有其主必有其仆！”
Glaring at her retreating back, Lin Jue fumed: That maid never used to mouth off like this—getting bolder by the day! Like mistress, like servant!

这两日林觉也思考，不放走朱淑真到底是对还是错？从理性角度讲是错，从感性讲，他舍不得放她离开，哪怕把她搁在林府，一根毫毛不碰，只要还挂着林觉娘子的名号，他都会觉得受用。尤其是临安才子们对朱淑真只能看在眼里，却吃不到嘴里，林觉想想都觉得面上有光。当然，最重要的是他对她依然有情。
Over the past two days, Lin Jue had wondered himself whether refusing the divorce was right or wrong. Reason said wrong; his heart said he couldn't let her go. Even keeping her untouched inside Zhulan Residence, as long as she bore the name of Lin Jue's wife, it satisfied his pride—especially knowing all those Lin'an scholars could only gaze from afar and never have her! Above all, he still loved her.

苦荞回到竹兰居，见小姐坐在书桌前发呆，没敢过去打扰，放下木桶，开始收拾屋子。
Back inside Zhulan Residence, seeing her mistress staring blankly at the desk, Kuqiao set the tub down and tidied the room without disturbing her.

千里寻穿越成朱淑真，竟然开始像她一样多愁善感。她猛然想起朱淑真四十五岁离开人世，她千里寻可不想英年早逝。既然不想，就不该学朱淑真，要记住自己是千里寻。
Transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen had actually infected Qianli Xun with her gloom! Wait—Zhu Shuzhen had died around forty-five! Qianli Xun had zero intention of dying young and heartbroken! Time to stop acting like Zhu Shuzhen and remember who she was.

她拍拍脑门，心里想：“对啊！我是千里寻，千里寻可从来不伤春悲秋，振作起来，仔细想想，接下来的人生该怎么过，我可不愿像朱淑真四十多岁郁郁而终。”
Slapping her own forehead, she pep-talked herself: Wake up! You're Qianli Xun—since when did Qianli Xun mope over spring winds and autumn rain?! Pull yourself together and plan your next move! No way am I fading away in my forties!

千里寻立即振作精神，她要付诸行动改变命运，到底该怎么做？暂时想不到更好的办法，不如迷信一回。
Energy surged right back. Time to take action and reshape her fate—and lacking a concrete plan yet, why not try a bit of superstition?

“苦荞，你认不认识看相算命特别准的人？”
"Kuqiao, know any fortune-teller around here who's crazy accurate?"

苦荞停下手中的活儿：“小姐可是问对人，我虽不认识，却知道有这么一位。临安城外有座山，山上有个尼姑庵，里面的女住持人称净光法师，能知过去未来，老爷就曾找她看过。”
Pausing her chores, Kuqiao brightened. "You asked the right person, Miss! Don't know her personally, yet everybody knows her name: on a hill outside Lin'an stands a nunnery whose abbess, Master Jingguang, sees past and future! Even Master visited her once."

“我爹？”
"My dad?"

苦荞点点头：“我听说，老爷那会儿年轻，与净光法师聊过后，一路青云直上，才成为今日的临安知府。”
Kuqiao nodded. "Word is back when Master was young, after one talk with Master Jingguang his career shot straight up till he became Prefect of Lin'an!"

“这么厉害！”
"That legit?!"

苦荞接着说：“不过，据说净光法师不随便给人看，她有条件，只有达到她的条件才能与她见上一面。”
"Only catch is Master Jingguang doesn't see just anyone—she sets conditions, and you only get an audience if you meet them."

“什么条件？”
"What conditions?"

苦荞为难道：“嗯，我不大清楚。”
Kuqiao hesitated. "Not quite sure..."

千里寻来了兴致，站起来：“我要去会会净光法师，多带些银子。”
Fired up, Qianli Xun sprang to her feet. "Let's go meet Master Jingguang! Pack plenty of silver!"

“这就要去吗？”
"Right now?"

“现在就去！”
"Right this second!"

苦荞带好银子，跟在小姐身后，来到林府门外，吩咐车夫套了马车，往临安城外而去。
Packing a pouch of silver, Kuqiao trailed her mistress out of Lin Manor, ordered the coachman to hitch the carriage, and rode out of Lin'an.

千里寻无心欣赏街头闹市的繁华，她只想为自己的人生找到一线曙光，更想知道还有没有机会摆脱与林觉的婚姻，能不能在属于朱淑真的时代有属于千里寻的人生。
Ignoring the lively market streets along the way, Qianli Xun focused on one wish: finding a ray of hope for her future, learning whether she'd ever break free from Lin Jue and carve out Qianli Xun's own destiny in Zhu Shuzhen's era.

不知马车走了多久，终于出了临安城；也不知在临安城外又走了多久，终于到达山脚下。
After a long ride out through the city gates and further along country roads, the carriage rolled to a stop at the foot of a mountain.

车夫提醒道：“夫人，到了。”
"We're here, Madam," the coachman called.

千里寻第一个跳下马车，一座像刷满绿色彩釉的高山矗立在眼前。
Hopping down first, Qianli Xun looked up at a towering hill glazed lush green from base to peak.

苦荞从马车上下来后，指着大山说：“小姐，尼姑庵就在半山腰，剩下的路马车上不去，我们得步行。”
Climbing down behind her, Kuqiao pointed upward. "The nunnery sits halfway up the mountain, Miss—carriages can't climb the trail, so we walk from here."

千里寻没有说话，朝大山的入口处走去。她从小练习千里拳，爬山对她来讲并不费体力。尽管现在的身体不是千里寻的，但她的潜意识依然能够操纵朱淑真的身体，让它和千里寻的身体同样敏捷。能顺利使出千里拳，力度丝毫不减，就是最好的证明。
Without a word, Qianli Xun strode straight onto the trailhead. Years of Qianli Fist made hill climbing effortless; though this body belonged to Zhu Shuzhen, Qianli Xun's instincts steered its reflexes just as nimbly—her full-power punches proved as much.

山路并不宽敞，又窄又弯曲，从山路的泥土被踩得结实程度判断，尼姑庵的香火应该不错。
The winding mountain track ran narrow, its dirt packed hard by countless feet—clearly the nunnery drew heavy traffic.

千里寻看到有五六个香客顺着山路往下走。当第一个香客走近时，千里寻和苦荞侧身让路，顺便问道：“大娘，尼姑庵还有多远？”
Five or six pilgrims descended toward them. Stepping aside to let the first pass, Qianli Xun asked, "How much further to the nunnery, Auntie?"

“不远不远，再走一阵就到。”大娘继续往下走。
"Not far at all—just a bit further up!" the woman called back as she continued down.

千里寻和苦荞则继续向上爬，碰到其他香客也是侧身让路，只怪山路太窄，仅能容纳一人行走。
Climbing on, they stepped aside several more times along the single-file path.

山路周围的景致不错，千里寻边走边欣赏，不知不觉来到半山腰，才有平坦之地，视野变得开阔起来。一座尼姑庵出现在眼前，门口是进进出出的香客，男男女女均有，还有卖香火的摊位。
Taking in the lush hill views as they went, Qianli Xun reached a broad mid-mountain clearing before she knew it. Ahead stood a nunnery bustling with men and women coming and going, incense stalls lining the entrance.

千里寻抬头看到门楣上的匾额写着“承佛慈力”四字：“苦荞，买些香火，随我进去拜拜。”
Glancing up at the plaque reading "By the Compassionate Power of the Buddha," Qianli Xun said, "Buy some incense, Kuqiao—let's go pay respects."

苦荞买来三柱高香，跟着小姐走进尼姑庵。
Buying three tall sticks of incense, Kuqiao followed her mistress through the gate.

迎面是正殿，很多人在殿外先把香点燃，再一一插入大香炉中。
Outside the main hall stood a huge bronze censer where pilgrims lit incense and planted their sticks.

苦荞照做，完成后，又跟随小姐走进正殿内。
Kuqiao did the same before following her mistress into the hall.

刚把脚踏进去，两边传来念佛的声音：“南无阿弥陀佛，南无阿弥陀佛……”声音很好听，像是在吟唱一首歌曲，让人听后不由心境舒畅。
The instant they stepped across the threshold, chanting rose from both sides: "Namo Amitabha, Namo Amitabha..." Flowing like calm song, the cadence soothed the heart on contact.

千里寻向两边瞧去，各盘腿坐着六名女尼，神态自然安详，口中念念有词，吟唱的声音正是从她们口中发出。
Glancing left and right, Qianli Xun saw six nuns seated cross-legged along each side, serene and composed, chanting in unison.

高大的神像前面放着三个蒲团，千里寻合掌跪拜；苦荞也学着小姐的样子拜起来。她们每拜一下，会响起一声敲击木鱼的声音“梆！”伴着佛号，甚是和谐。
Before the towering Buddha statue lay three prayer cushions. Qianli Xun pressed her palms together and knelt to bow; Kuqiao followed suit. Each bow drew a resonant clack from a wooden fish—"tok!"—blending seamless with the chant.

三拜之后，两人起身，朝木鱼声传来的方向瞧去，佛像旁边坐着一位女尼，她面前的桌上放着木鱼和犍稚。
Rising after three bows, they looked toward the sound and saw a young nun seated beside the statue with a wooden fish and mallet before her.

千里寻走上前有礼道：“小师傅，我想找住持净光法师，不知她今日可在？”
Qianli Xun stepped over politely. "Little Master, I'd like to see Abbess Master Jingguang—is she available today?"

女尼起身说：“跟我来。”
The young nun rose at once. "Follow me."

千里寻一阵庆幸，没想到如此容易，心想苦荞得到的信息应该有误，不由盯了一眼苦荞。
Thrilled at how easy that was—guess Kuqiao's rumors were overblown—Qianli Xun shot Kuqiao a teasing glance.

﻿苦荞接触到小姐的目光，有些不好意思，更为能顺利见到住持感到高兴。
Flushing slightly at being proved wrong, Kuqiao grinned anyway, glad her mistress got her wish so fast.

﻿她们跟上女尼向正殿后面走去。
Together they followed the young nun behind the main hall.
