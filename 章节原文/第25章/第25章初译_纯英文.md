# Chapter 25: Sentimental Thoughts on Life

---

Qianli Xun persisted in applying the medicine from Jixiang Pharmacy. In just two days, the wound on her forehead had healed. She sat in front of the bronze mirror, worried about leaving a scar. After looking and looking, she didn't find a single scar. From now on, she wouldn't have to show off her wound everywhere.

---

The skin on her forehead was fair and delicate. She should have been happy, but Qianli Xun just couldn't feel cheerful. After the divorce failed, she had truly developed Zhu Shuzhen's sentimental nature.

---

Last night, she had a dream. She dreamed of Shen Jianchen, who said in the dream, "Qianli Xun, be my girlfriend!" She nodded and agreed, something she had never done before. Shen Jianchen held her tightly in his arms, and she savored the sweetness of love. His embrace was warm, and when she woke up, she still felt the sensation of being held by him and his warmth.

---

After experiencing the defeat in the divorce battle, the stronger the sense of failure, the stronger her longing for love became. Qianli Xun developed the desire to have a romance—something she had never thought of in her own era. But marriage was like a thick wall surrounding her, trapping her feelings and denying her freedom.

---

Qianli Xun couldn't help but sigh, "Sigh!"

---

After sighing, she remembered one of Zhu Shuzhen's ci poems that matched her current mood perfectly. "Kubiao, prepare paper and ink. I want to write a ci."

---

Kubiao brought over freshly made tea. "It's ready, Miss. Drink your tea first before writing."

---

Qianli Xun sat in front of the bronze mirror and finished her tea. She put down the cup, stood up, walked to the desk, and sat down. She dipped the brush in the inkstone and began to write:

---

Slanting wind, fine rain, spring's chill,
Before the cup, I recall past joy.
Once held pear blossoms,
Lonely tears on the rail.
Fragrant grass, mist-cut Southern Ferry road,
With parting tears, I gaze at green mountains.

Last night, a dream of watery clouds,
Water and clouds, silently without words.
How helpless to wake,
Sorrow and resentment still remain.
Tossing on the quilt, vainly troubled,
Heaven is easy to see, but seeing you is hard. "Jiangchengzi: Admiring the Spring" [TN: This ci poem was written by Zhu Shuzhen, a renowned female poet of the Song Dynasty. The poem expresses her longing for a lost love and the pain of separation.]

---

After finishing, she immersed herself in the lines. The brush still rested in her fingers as she savored the words, "Tossing on the quilt, vainly troubled. Heaven is easy to see, but seeing you is hard."

---

Did Shen Jianchen transmigrate here with her? If so, where was he? As she pondered, ink from the brush tip gathered and dripped down—plop—it landed right on the character "you," wasting all her hard work.

---

Qianli Xun put down the brush and rolled up the paper. "Throw it away?"

---

Kubiao took it and placed it in the wooden bucket beside her, then carried the bucket outside.

---

The wooden bucket was filled with rolled-up papers. Qianli Xun was curious. "Why are there so many inside?"

---

Kubiao turned her head. "They're all the papers Miss has thrown away. I save them up, and when there are enough, I put them outside the gate."

---

Qianli Xun asked in confusion, "Put them outside the gate?"

---

Kubiao explained, "Miss treats them as trash, but someone treats them as treasure. Every so often, people wait outside the Lin residence to pick up the trash. It was the same at the Zhu residence before."

---

Qianli Xun found it amusing but didn't bother to think about it. At this moment, her heart was filled with an inexplicable sadness.

---

Kubiao carried the wooden bucket to outside the Lin residence gate. She placed it on the ground, pulled out the ci her miss had just written, held it in her hand, and called out to those around, "Come out! Take them quickly so I can get back to my work."

---

Before she finished speaking, five or six young servants and book boys rushed out from somewhere and swarmed the bucket, grabbing all the calligraphy and paintings inside. Before leaving, they didn't forget to say, "Thank you, Sister Kubiao!"

---

Kubiao stopped one of the young servants. "Nanshuang! Wait a moment!"

---

Nanshuang returned to Kubiao's side. "What does Sister Kubiao need?"

---

Kubiao asked, "I heard your young master is in Bianliang. Why didn't you follow him?"

---

"Before, Sister Kubiao never asked about my young master. Why are you suddenly interested today?"

---

"I didn't think of it before, but now I have. Why ask so many questions?"

---

"Sister, think about it. If I went, who would help my young master collect Youqi Jushi's poems and ci?" Nanshuang unrolled the paper he had gotten. "It's just bad luck today. There are only two lines on this paper."

---

Kubiao handed him the paper in her hand. "My miss just wrote this. It's complete. Give it to your young master."

---

"Why is Sister Kubiao so good to my young master?"

---

"Do I need to ask? Young Master Qiao is the best at understanding my miss's poems and ci. They consider each other soulmates."

---

"If that's the case, why doesn't your miss just give the poems directly to my young master? That would save me from waiting outside the Lin residence every day."

---

"How can that work? There are already rumors about my miss and your young master. People are making something out of nothing, so we need to be even more careful. You should keep waiting outside the gate. When there's something good, I'll naturally give it to you."

---

Nanshuang thanked her and left. Kubiao carried the wooden bucket back. Halfway there, she ran into her young master.

---

Lin Jue asked angrily, "Are people picking up useless trash again?"

---

Kubiao replied coldly, "Young Master, what you consider trash, others treat as treasure."

---

Lin Jue said furiously, "Today I'll send soldiers to guard outside the residence. Let's see who dares to come. I'll catch them one by one, two by two!"

---

"I advise Young Master not to provoke anything related to my miss. I'm afraid she'll find out and beat you again with her fists." After Kubiao finished, she continued walking inside.

---

Lin Jue stared at Kubiao's back, very angry. "This girl didn't used to talk like this. She's getting more and more out of line! Like master, like servant!"

---

These past two days, Lin Jue had also been thinking. Was it right or wrong not to let Zhu Shuzhen go?
From a rational perspective, it was wrong. From an emotional perspective, he couldn't bear to let her leave. Even if he just kept her in the Lin residence without touching a single hair, as long as she still carried the title of Lin Jue's wife, he would feel satisfied.
Especially when Lin'an's talents could only look at Zhu Shuzhen but couldn't have her—just thinking about it made Lin Jue feel proud. Of course, the most important thing was that he still had feelings for her.

---

Kubiao returned to the Zhulan Residence and saw her miss sitting at the desk, lost in thought. She didn't dare disturb her. She put down the wooden bucket and began cleaning the room.

---

Qianli Xun, having transmigrated into Zhu Shuzhen, had actually started becoming as sentimental as her. She suddenly remembered that Zhu Shuzhen had died at forty-five. Qianli Xun didn't want to die young. Since she didn't want to, she shouldn't imitate Zhu Shuzhen. She needed to remember she was Qianli Xun.

---

She patted her forehead and thought, "That's right! I'm Qianli Xun. Qianli Xun never laments spring and autumn. Cheer up! Think carefully about how to live the rest of my life. I don't want to die of depression in my forties like Zhu Shuzhen."

---

Qianli Xun immediately cheered up. She wanted to take action to change her destiny. But what should she do? Since she couldn't think of a better way, she might as well be superstitious for once.

---

"Kubiao, do you know anyone who's very accurate at fortune-telling?"

---

Kubiao stopped her work. "Miss, you've asked the right person. Although I don't know her personally, I know there's such a person. Outside Lin'an city, there's a mountain with a nunnery on it. The abbess there is called Master Jingguang. She can know the past and future. The master once had his fortune told by her."

---

"My father?"

---

Kubiao nodded. "I heard that when the master was young, after talking with Master Jingguang, his career skyrocketed, and he became today's Prefect of Lin'an."

---

"That amazing!"

---

Kubiao continued, "But it's said that Master Jingguang doesn't give fortunes to just anyone. She has conditions. Only those who meet her conditions can meet her."

---

"What conditions?"

---

Kubiao said with difficulty, "Um, I'm not very clear."

---

Qianli Xun became interested and stood up. "I want to meet Master Jingguang. Bring plenty of silver."

---

"Are we going now?"

---

"Now!"

---

Kubiao brought the silver and followed her miss outside the Lin residence gate. She instructed the coachman to harness the carriage, and they headed toward outside Lin'an city.

---

Qianli Xun had no mind to enjoy the bustling streets. She only wanted to find a glimmer of hope for her life. She wanted to know if there was still a chance to escape her marriage with Lin Jue, and if she could have a life of her own in Zhu Shuzhen's era.

---

The carriage traveled for an unknown time. Finally, they left Lin'an city and continued on the road outside. After another stretch of journey, they reached the foot of the mountain.

---

The coachman reminded her, "Madam, we've arrived."

---

Qianli Xun was the first to jump off the carriage. A mountain painted with green glaze stood before her eyes.

---

After Kubiao got off the carriage, she pointed at the mountain and said, "Miss, the nunnery is halfway up the mountain. The rest of the road is too narrow for the carriage. We have to walk."

---

Qianli Xun didn't speak. She walked toward the mountain entrance. She had practiced the Qianli Fist since childhood. Climbing the mountain wasn't physically demanding for her. Although her current body wasn't Qianli Xun's, her subconscious could still control Zhu Shuzhen's body, making it as agile as Qianli Xun's. Being able to use the Qianli Fist smoothly, with no loss of power, was the best proof.

---

The mountain path wasn't wide. It was narrow and winding. Judging from how compacted the soil was, the nunnery's incense offerings must be quite good.

---

Qianli Xun saw five or six pilgrims walking down the mountain path. When the first pilgrim approached, Qianli Xun and Kubiao stepped aside to let her pass and asked, "Auntie, how much farther is the nunnery?"

---

"Not far, not far. Just a bit more and you'll be there." The auntie continued walking down.

---

Qianli Xun and Kubiao continued climbing. When they met other pilgrims, they also stepped aside to let them pass. The mountain path was just too narrow, only wide enough for one person.

---

The scenery around the mountain path was nice. Qianli Xun admired it as she walked. Unconsciously, they reached halfway up the mountain. There was flat ground, and the view opened up. A nunnery appeared before them. Pilgrims were entering and exiting the gate—men and women alike. There were also stalls selling incense.

---

Qianli Xun looked up and saw the plaque above the door with four characters: "By the Compassionate Power of the Buddha." "Kubiao, buy some incense. Follow me inside to worship."

---

Kubiao bought three tall incense sticks and followed her miss into the nunnery.

---

Facing them was the main hall. Many people lit their incense outside the hall first, then inserted them one by one into the large incense burner.

---

Kubiao did the same. After finishing, she followed her miss into the main hall.

---

As soon as they stepped inside, they heard chanting from both sides: "Namo Amitabha, Namo Amitabha..." The voices were beautiful, like singing a song, making people feel at ease.

---

Qianli Xun looked to both sides. Six nuns sat cross-legged on each side, their expressions natural and serene, murmuring chants. The singing voices came from their mouths.

---

In front of the tall statue were three praying mats. Qianli Xun pressed her palms together and knelt to worship. Kubiao also imitated her miss and bowed. Each time they bowed, a wooden fish sound would ring out—"Clack!"—accompanying the Buddha's name in perfect harmony.

---

After three bows, the two stood up and looked toward where the wooden fish sound came from. Beside the Buddha statue sat a nun. On the table in front of her were a wooden fish and a striker.

---

Qianli Xun stepped forward politely. "Little sister, I want to find Abbess Master Jingguang. Is she here today?"

---

The nun stood up and said, "Follow me."

---

Qianli Xun felt relieved. She didn't expect it to be so easy. She thought the information Kubiao had gotten must be wrong, so she glanced at Kubiao.

---

Kubiao met her miss's gaze and felt a bit embarrassed, but was also happy they could see the abbess so smoothly.

---

They followed the nun toward the back of the main hall.
