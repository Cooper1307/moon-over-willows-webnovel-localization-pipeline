第205章 气别梅若回草堂
## Chapter 205: Parting in Anger with Meiruo and Returning to the Thatched Hall

林觉没有了笑容，一脸平静地问：“你怎么跑来？”
在夫君面前白梅若需保持形象，她努力控制好情绪。
“奴家出来买些糕点，经过此处，见夫君在此饮酒，进来打声招呼。没想到幽栖居士也在。哦，现在应该称呼您陈家少夫人。”
千里寻听出些不满来，站起来说：“我还有事，失陪，酒钱已付过，算我请客，你们夫妻好好对饮。”
白梅若哪能放过奚落朱淑真的机会，拦住她：“别一见我来，就要走，好像怕我一般，难道你正在与我夫君说些不愿我听到的话？”
林觉批评道：“别瞎胡说，要是没事，赶紧回去。”
白梅若担心夫君生气，立刻改变态度，装作热情的样子，上前拉住朱淑真的手：“幽栖居士，我刚才跟你说笑，你我好不容易碰上，别着急走，坐下来说说话。”
对方态度好转，千里寻不好驳她的面子，关键是白梅若刚才的话显然是怀疑她，就这么走了，岂不显得做贼心虚？
“怎么？不称呼我陈家少夫人？”
“称呼幽栖居士更亲切些。”白梅若继续装道，“来，我们坐下聊。”
千里寻坐回原位，白梅若则坐在她身旁。
林觉甚觉无趣：“女人家聊的话题我不感兴趣，府衙还有事，失陪。”
白梅若见她来，夫君却要走，倍感失落，对着林觉的背影说：“夫君，早些从府衙回家，厨房里炖了鸡汤，你最近辛苦，需要补补。”
林觉没有回头，简单“嗯”了一声，迅速走出酒肆。
千里寻看到这一幕，觉出白梅若的不易，刚才的不满烟消云散，不由生出怜悯之情：“林觉能娶你，是他上辈子修来的福气。”
白梅若叹口气：“唉，我嫁给他也不知是不是我的福气？”
千里寻不知该如何安慰，只能以沉默回应。
白梅若继续说：“幽栖居士在林家时可听说一位叫春金巧的商妓？”
千里寻怎能不知？因不清楚白梅若的想法，不敢轻易谈论，怕因哪句话说的不对影响林觉与白梅若的夫妻关系。故而没有言语，仅露出微笑，端起酒杯装作品酒的样子。
白梅若见幽栖居士不言语，猜出她的心思，直白地说：“你不必担心与我谈论此人，我早听林府的丫鬟说过，你还未与我夫君和离时，他时常将春金巧带回林府恶心你。”
千里寻松口气，放下酒杯：“你既已知，还问我做什么？”
白梅若不由露出稍显得意的神情：“不过，自从我嫁给夫君，他还从来没把春金巧带回过林府。”
千里寻忽觉自己怜悯白梅若甚是可笑：“林觉肯定没少往春香楼跑。”
白梅若不再得意，暂时将奚落朱淑真的想法搁在一旁，毕竟家中之事不好对他人提起，而眼前这位对自家夫君知根知底，说出来也不怕她笑话。
“好姐姐，你帮我出出主意，往后我该怎么办？”
突然被白梅若称呼“好姐姐”，倒令千里寻一时无法适应：“你们的事我不好掺和，再说跟我也没关系。”
白梅若拿过林觉用过的酒杯，给自己满上，端起来：“我敬姐姐，我干了，您随意。”
The smile vanished off Lin Jue's face at once. "What are you doing here?"

接着连喝两杯，喝得猛些，给呛住，白梅若用丝帕捂住口咳起来，咳得脸色通红。
Clamping tight control over her jealousy in front of her husband, Bai Meiruo dipped a smooth curtsy. "I came out for pastries, spotted Husband taking wine inside, and stepped in to greet you—never expected Youqi Jushi here too. Oh, excuse me—I should say Young Madam Chen now."

一旁的丫鬟上前帮忙轻拍背部：“夫人，您没事吧？”
Catching the frosty prickle, Qianli Xun stood up. "Excuse me, I have errands. The wine is already paid—my treat, enjoy your drink together."

对方如此状况，逼得千里寻不得不多说两句：“一切取决于你的态度。”
Unwilling to let her slip away unneedled, Bai Meiruo stepped in front of her. "Bolting the second I show up, like you are scared of me? Saying things to my husband you do not want my ears catching?"

白梅若的咳嗽缓解许多，坐正后问：“姐姐是何意？”
"Quit talking nonsense!" Lin Jue snapped. "Got nothing to do, head home!"

千里寻解释道：“我呢，眼里揉不得沙子，才选择和离。当然，在这个时代我的态度不一定是正确的，却符合我的心意。”
Spooked by his sharp bark, Bai Meiruo flipped instantly into sugary warmth, catching Shuzhen's hand. "Only teasing, Youqi Jushi! Rare running into each other—do not dash off, sit and chat!"

白梅若已领悟，思索一会儿，说道：“姐姐家世好，长得貌美又有才华，自然天不怕地不怕；而我不一样，嫁给夫君算是高攀。家里人认为我嫁了好人家，很多姊妹羡慕不已。我若走姐姐的路，恐被家人所不容。”
Refusing to look guilty by fleeing, Qianli Xun raised an eyebrow. "Not calling me Young Madam Chen anymore?"

话既已说开，千里寻那股子乐于助人的精神被激发，帮对方出起主意：“林觉这人唯一的优点是找商妓十分专一，从我知道他找商妓到现在还没换过人，说明他对春金巧有感情。你呢，不如主动给春金巧赎身，放在林觉身边当个妾室。既会让林觉感恩于你，还可将春金巧放在身边加以约束，也免了林觉整日往春香楼跑被他人在背后非议。”
"Youqi Jushi sounds warmer! Sit, sit!"

白梅若脸上露出笑容：“还是姐姐聪明，给我出这么好的主意，我怎么就没想到？今儿我回去定然好好筹谋筹谋。”
Once both sat down, Lin Jue pushed back bored. "No taste for women's chatter—yamen business calls. Excuse me."

千里寻本以为自己帮了白梅若，不会与对方成闺蜜，怎么说也是朋友，可接下来白梅若的话让她大跌眼镜。
Crushed watching him bolt the instant she arrived, Bai Meiruo called softly at his back, "Come home early from the yamen, Husband—chicken soup is simmering in the kitchen to build up your strength."

白梅若面色忽然变得平静：“幽栖居士帮我出这么好的主意，我很感激，但你我不可能成为朋友……”
Giving a clipped grunt without glancing back, Lin Jue strode clean out of the tavern.

刚才还亲热地称呼她“好姐姐”，这会儿称呼又变。千里寻适应片刻后，总结出经验教训，以后帮人前还是先将人看清楚再说。
Watching her swallow the cold shoulder melted Qianli Xun's irritation into quiet pity. "Lin Jue earned three lifetimes of luck marrying you."

面对白梅若这类人，千里寻是不愿轻易吃亏：“你想多了，我今日无聊，看在你陪我解闷儿的份上，随便帮你出出主意，我可从来没想过要跟你交朋友。”
Bai Meiruo let out a bitter sigh. "Hard to tell if marrying him was my luck or not..."

“你也别生气，你是我夫君的前妻，我若与你成为朋友，任谁见了都会觉得奇怪。”
Qianli Xun stayed tactfully quiet.

千里寻实在不想待下去，跟这种人多说无益：“我还有事，先行一步。”
"When Youqi Jushi lived at Lin Manor, did you know a courtesan named Chun Jinqiao?"

白梅若并不愿朱淑真就此离去，重要的话她还未说。
Unsure where she was steering—afraid of sparking household strife—Qianli Xun stayed mute behind a polite smile, lifting her cup.

“幽栖居士可真有本事，搞得郡主因你而受伤，明日我打算去幽林别居探望郡主，不知幽栖居士可否愿意与我同往？”
Reading her caution, Bai Meiruo laid it bare. "No need to tiptoe around her name—Lin Manor's maids already told me how Husband used to drag Chun Jinqiao home to spite you before your divorce."

千里寻很想探望梦容，可也明白现在最正确的做法便是远离梦容。白梅若如此说，无疑在她伤口上撒把盐。她努力忍住，往酒肆外走。
Relaxing, Qianli Xun set her cup down. "If you already know, why ask?"

身后传来白梅若的声音：“幽栖居士该不会是做了亏心事，怕见到郡主？”
A tiny gleam of smugness flickered across Bai Meiruo's face. "Since I married Husband, though, he has never once brought Chun Jinqiao through Lin Manor's door."

千里寻已忍不住，攥紧拳头，忽地回来，伸到白梅若面前：“白梅若，老娘的拳头从不打女人，你若再瞎掰掰，只怕我的拳头也开始打起女人来。”
Her sudden pity feeling downright silly now, Qianli Xun observed dryly, "Sure he still visits Chunxiang Brothel plenty, though."

白梅若被吓住，不敢再言语。
The smugness collapsed at once. Dropping her barb—unable to air dirty laundry to strangers, whereas this woman knew her husband inside out—Bai Meiruo leaned forward pleadingly. "Good Sister, give me advice—what do I do?"

千里寻这才收起拳头，“哼”了一声，走出酒肆。
Threw off stride by the sudden "Good Sister," Qianli Xun shook her head. "None of my business—better I stay out of your marriage."

白梅若缓了一会儿，骂道：“原来是只母老虎！难怪我家官人不要你！”
Grabbing Lin Jue's cup, Bai Meiruo poured herself full—"To you, Sister!"—and knocked two strong cups straight down, choking into a violent red-faced coughing fit while her maid pounded her back.

既然是步行出来，必然也步行回去。遇到白梅若这等人，窝一肚子火，也得疏散疏散。千里寻希望街市的繁华能让她很快忘记刚才遇到的人和事。
Softened by the sight, Qianli Xun spoke plainly. "Everything comes down to what you want."

苦荞替小姐鸣不平：“林统制的夫人脑子指定有问题，要不怎么翻脸比翻书还快！”
Catching her breath, Bai Meiruo asked, "What do you mean, Sister?"

苦荞主动搭话，千里寻也算找到一处发泄途径：“你说得对，她的脑子肯定有问题。那日在捶丸场第一次遇见，说话又懂事又知礼，一点儿都看不出是这等变化无常之人。”
"I could not stand a single speck of grit in my eye, so I chose mutual divorce. Maybe not this era's textbook choice, yet true to my own heart."

苦荞安慰道：“小姐不必跟她置气。”
Understanding flickered in Bai Meiruo's eyes. "Sister has powerful family, beauty, and genius—fearing neither Heaven nor earth. I married UP into Lin Manor; my relatives envy my good match. Walk your road and my own family would disown me."

千里寻也安慰自己：“跟她置气只会拉低我的档次。吃一堑长一智，你家小姐我以后帮人时，需把眼睛睁大些，看清楚再帮，否则后悔都来不及。”
Her helpful streak kicking in once the wall dropped, Qianli Xun offered practical counsel. "Lin Jue's sole virtue is sticking to one courtesan—never switched off Chun Jinqiao the whole time I knew him, proving he genuinely cares for her. Buy her freedom yourself and bring her into Lin Manor as a concubine! One move earns Lin Jue's gratitude, puts Chun Jinqiao right under your thumb where you can keep her in check, AND stops him haunting brothels and drawing street gossip!"

苦荞疑惑地问道：“小姐，您说她为何要那样对待您？”
Bai Meiruo's face lit right up. "Leave it to Sister's sharp mind! Why did I never think of that?! Going home tonight to put it in motion!"

千里寻想想说：“仔细想想，白梅若刚看到我时，说话的表情和语气才是她真实的情绪流露。林觉走后对我姐姐长姐姐短地称呼，无非是想让我帮她出主意。主意刚出完，她就是忍不住恢复原样。她对我的态度前后变化明显，也许是因为我与林觉和离过。”
Qianli Xun figured they would part on friendly terms—only for Bai Meiruo's face to snap ice-flat the very next breath!

苦荞不解：“我实在理解不了，您与林统制已和离，她有什么不高兴的？”
"Thank you for the brilliant advice, Youqi Jushi—yet you and I can never be friends."

千里寻也想不通，她跟白梅若仅仅第二回见面，她也没必要因为朱淑真是林觉的前妻如此待她。
Whiplash! "Good Sister" one second, frosty wall the next! Making a mental note to vet people before offering free advice, Qianli Xun refused to eat the snub. "Flatter yourself—I was bored, figured you kept me company, and tossed out free tips. Never once planned on befriending you."

“人心难测，谁知道她抽哪门子风？以后见她还是躲远些，尽量不跟这种人打交道。”
"Do not take offense—you are my husband's ex-wife. Becoming chummy would look bizarre to everyone."

二人边说边穿越闹市，渐渐来到人烟稀少之地，陈府的大门出现在眼前。此时天已蒙蒙黑。
Done wasting air, Qianli Xun stood up. "Business awaits—goodbye."

千里寻瞧着陈府的大门，倍感惆怅。
Unwilling to let her leave without landing her real sting, Bai Meiruo added sweetly: "Youqi Jushi certainly packs a punch—getting Princess Jiahe stabbed on your account! Tomorrow I am visiting Youlin Retreat to check on Her Highness—care to tag along?"

从昨日起，她住进陈府，以后会从这道门走出，无论走到哪里，都会回到这里。陈府成为她新的人生驿站，也许会永远停留在这里。
Longing to visit Mengrong yet knowing staying far away was the only kindness left, Qianli Xun felt salt grind straight into the raw wound and turned toward the door without replying.

千里寻累了，想把陈府看作她人生最后一道驿站，希望与陈府里每一个人和谐相处。
"Afraid to face Her Highness with a guilty conscience?" Bai Meiruo purred behind her.

想到和谐相处，自然想起夏老太太，一位毫无底线宠爱孙子，第一日没给她好脸色的人。千里寻决定，为了在陈府活得自在顺利，要想办法把老太太搞定。
Snapping straight back with a tight fist thrust an inch from Bai Meiruo's nose, Qianli Xun hissed, "My fist normally skips women—keep flapping that venomous jaw and I make YOU my first exception!"

她跨进陈府的大门，来到居住的院子外，借着月光，“墨香苑”三个字赫然入目。
Frozen white in terror, Bai Meiruo shut her trap on the spot. Dropping her fist with a cold snort, Qianli Xun marched out—only after she cleared the door did Bai Meiruo dare mutter, "Total tigress! No wonder my husband dumped you!"

千里寻十分满意，赞叹韦嬷嬷办事麻利，一日之内，匾额已挂好，就凭这名字，她以后踏进院子会觉得心情舒畅，不由对里面充满期待。
Walking home through the bustling streets to cool off, Kuqiao fumed beside her: "Division Commander Lin's wife is touched in the head—flips faces faster than turning a page!"

走进院子，穿过院中的花坛池水，来到正屋跟前，“素心草堂”也已挂上。苦荞与红豆住的侧屋则挂上“悦宁屋”的匾额。
"Dead right!" Qianli Xun vented. "Seemed sweet and polished at the chuiwan grounds—never guessed she was two-faced! Waste of breath getting mad at someone that petty. Live and learn—next time I check folks close before lending a hand."

苦荞高兴道：“我住的屋子从此后也有名字！”
"Why treat you so nasty, Miss?"

“走，看看别处。”
"Looking back, her frosty dig at the start was her real face; calling me Sister once Lin Jue left was purely fishing for advice—the second she got it, the mask dropped! Probably bitter just because I was Lin Jue's ex."

她们来到后院，韦嬷嬷的屋子挂着“静宜堂”，陈浩宇的屋子挂着“博雅斋”。
"You already divorced him—why hold a grudge?!"

苦荞议论道：“博雅斋三个字倒好，可姑爷跟这三个字一点儿不匹配。”
"People's hearts are murky—steer clear of her from now on."

千里寻批评道：“在我面前议论议论也就罢了，可不能形成习惯，一不小心在陈家人面前也如此议论。”
As noisy markets gave way to quiet lanes under gathering dusk, Chen Manor's towering gate loomed ahead, bringing a quiet ache to Qianli Xun's chest. Since yesterday, this threshold marked her new waystation—maybe her final stop. Tired of fighting every wind, she hoped to live at ease with everyone inside—including figuring out how to charm Old Madam Xia.

苦荞意识到失言：“小姐批评的是，以后我会管住这张嘴，不乱说话。”
Stepping inside and walking to her courtyard, moonlight illuminated fresh carved wood above the archway: Moxiang Courtyard (*Ink-Fragrance Courtyard*). Thrilled at Matron Wei's lightning efficiency—plaques hung in a single day!—she stepped past flowerbeds and pond to find Suxin Thatched Hall (*Pure-Heart Thatched Hall*) above the main door and Yuening Room above the side quarters ("My room has a real name now!" Kuqiao cheered).

苦荞如此懂事，千里寻不好再批评下去：“虽与人不匹配，可与这院子匹配，再说陈浩宇都不怎么回来，不用理会他。”
Rounding into the back yard, Jingyi Hall graced Matron Wei's door while Boya Study—meaning "Erudite and Refined"—hung over Chen Haoyu's quarters.

千里寻忽觉自己有些过分，霸占了陈浩宇的院子不说，还把他赶到后院。
"Fine name, Boya Study," Kuqiao giggled, "only it fits Young Master like silk on a pig!"

“苦荞，你有没有觉得你家小姐我做得很过分？”
"Save cracks like that for my ears alone—slip up in front of Chen elders and we catch grief," Qianli Xun cautioned gently.

苦荞小心回道：“在他人看来，应该会认为小姐做得过分，可我了解其中的弯弯绕绕，并不会这么认为。”
"Got it—zipping my lip from now on!"

千里寻没有说什么，走出后院，往西边拐，经过一道圆形石门，是下人居住的屋子，男仆住的房子挂着“忠勤舍”，丫鬟的房子挂着“勤安居”
"Does not match him, yet matches the courtyard—and he barely sleeps home anyway." Pausing, a brief twinge pricked Qianli Xun. "Honestly, Kuqiao—seizing his whole yard and shoving him out back—did I go too far?"

到处视察一遍，千里寻很满意。她出陈府时，韦嬷嬷正吩咐人布置屋子，匾额既然都挂上，屋子也肯定布置好，千里寻充满期待：“跟我回素心草堂。”
"Outsiders might say yes," Kuqiao answered carefully, "yet knowing the whole mess between you two, I say not one bit."

来到素心草堂，里面已褪去奢华之风，不仅素雅清淡，还充满笔墨书香之气。千里寻看着书架上一排一排码得整齐的书卷，赞道：“现在走进来才觉既熟悉又舒心。”
Rounding through the west moon-gate to admire Zhongqin Quarters for male servants and Qin'an Lodge for maids, Qianli Xun hurried back eagerly to Suxin Thatched Hall. Stepping inside took her breath away: gaudy bridal gold gone, replaced by serene simplicity, clean ink-scented stillness, and shelves stacked neat with her beloved scrolls. "Feels like home at last!"

苦荞径直走到茶桌前：“小姐，我给您做盏茶。”
"Making Miss a bowl of tea right now!" Kuqiao headed straight to the tea stand.

千里寻笑道：“我怎么有种回到成亲前的感觉。”
"Funny—feels like I never got married at all!"

苦荞已在茶桌前坐好，开始做茶：“那可不是？小姐都把洞房改成了草堂。”
"Of course it does—Miss turned a bridal suite into a scholar's thatched hall!"

也许是因环境影响，千里寻有了创作的冲动，她走到书桌旁坐下，开始提笔写话本。
Inspired by the calm ink fragrance, Qianli Xun sat straight down at her desk and picked up her brush to write her huaben.
