第257章 邹嬷嬷拜访乔府
## Chapter 257: Matron Zou Calls on the Qiao Manor

众人离开后，乔梦容坐在屋内，不想说一句话，刚才大家帮着诋毁朱淑真时的开心已荡然无存。
她抬眼瞧瞧哥哥的屋子，被她布置成洞房的样子，可新郎并不在身边。她擦掉眼泪，心中更生起恨意。
喜兰说：“王妃，今日诗会甚是成功，大家都替王妃宣泄了心中不满。”
“只宣泄还不够，我说过要让她消失。”乔梦容说完，问道，“你觉得我狠心吗？”
邹嬷嬷正好从外面进来，听到王妃的话，强调道：“王妃是何等身份？想让谁消失，谁还不得立刻消失。”
“邹嬷嬷快坐，我正好心中烦闷，陪我说说话。”
邹嬷嬷坐在诗会时苗知瑜坐过的位置：“众人刚刚替王妃骂过那个狐狸精，您应该高兴。”
“哥哥不在，我高兴不起来。”
“王妃要对仪王有耐心，男人就像风筝，随他去飞，只要线拽在王妃手里就行。”
“可哥哥不是风筝，是个大活人，再说我手中没有一根线。”
邹嬷嬷见仪王妃说得可怜，心中对朱淑真更是不满：“王妃刚才提到想让她消失，不知王妃有没有什么好办法？”
“让她消失的想法也是今日当着她的面临时起意，既已说出，不好退缩，可我想来想去不知该如何做。一来我不会功夫，即使会也不一定能打得过她；二来我一个妇道人家哪有权利调遣兵力？即使有，她爹是临安知府，也未必把我放在眼里，再说哥哥是节度使，事情闹大必然会惊动他。”
邹嬷嬷叹道：“王妃是良善之辈，自然想得都是正大光明的途径。虽说碾死她跟碾死一只蚂蚁一样简单，也需想些见不得光的方法。”
“嬷嬷说来听听。”
“此事王妃不能亲自动手，也不能被王爷知道，我们需假借他人之手。”
“假借何人？”
Once the guests cleared out, Qiao Mengrong sat alone in the hall without a word. Every drop of petty triumph she had felt while they tore Zhu Shuzhen to shreds had evaporated. Lifting her gaze around Brother's room—decked out like a bridal chamber, yet missing its groom—she brushed away a hot tear as fresh venom surged inside her.

“我回临安，还未去拜见过乔老爷和夫人，不如今日就去。”
"Your Highness," Xilan ventured, "today's poetry salon went splendidly. Everyone helped you vent your anger."

乔梦容立刻明白：“有劳邹嬷嬷。”
"Venting isn't enough. I told her I'd make her vanish." Qiao Mengrong turned her eyes on her maid. "Do you think I'm cruel?"

邹嬷嬷在丫鬟的陪同下，乘坐马车来到乔府。
Right then, Matron Zou stepped inside and caught her last words. "With Your Highness's rank, if you want someone to vanish, who on earth wouldn't vanish on the spot?"

当乔守仁和钱氏听到邹嬷嬷前来拜见，十分欣喜。她曾是乔府的丫鬟，后来去了汴梁，待在乔贵妃身边，还成为仪王的奶娘。女儿走后，是她把仪王抚养成人，对乔家贡献极大，他们决不能怠慢，必须亲自出去迎接。
"Come sit, Matron Zou. My mind is in knots—keep me company."

邹嬷嬷站在乔府主人的院子门口，见二位主人亲自前来相迎，便知自己在他们心中的地位，作为下人能获此等礼遇，实属难得。
Matron Zou took the seat Miao Zhiyu had occupied earlier. "Everyone just tore that little vixen to strips for you. You ought to be smiling."

邹嬷嬷赶紧行礼：“老爷，夫人，我随仪王回到临安，帮王爷和王妃处理完事情，就跑来看望你们。”
"With Brother gone, I can't bring myself to smile."

钱氏上前拉住邹嬷嬷的手：“不必多礼。”不觉想起女儿，鼻子一酸，眼睛也跟着变红。
"Be patient with Prince Yi, Your Highness. Men are like kites—let them soar all they like, so long as the string stays in your fist."

乔守仁说：“别站着，进屋说话。”
"Brother isn't a kite—he's flesh and blood, and I don't hold a single string."

进入屋内，三人落座后，先寒暄些往事。有乔贵妃当姑娘时在临安的趣事，还有乔贵妃进宫成了贵妃后的不易，再到生下仪王，很快撒手人寰之事。
Her mistress's misery stoked Matron Zou's hatred for Zhu Shuzhen even hotter. "You just said you want her to vanish, Your Highness. Do you have a plan?"

说着说着，三人都忍不住悲伤起来。
"The thought only popped out in the heat of facing her today. Now that I've said it aloud, I can't back down—yet I'm stumped on how to pull it off. First, I don't know martial arts, and even if I did, I couldn't beat her. Second, as a woman I have no authority to mobilize troops—and even if I tried, her father is the Prefect of Lin'an and wouldn't necessarily roll over for me. Worse, Brother is Military Commissioner; any big commotion is bound to reach his ears."

乔守仁第一个控制好情绪：“唉，都是过去的事，贵妃泉下有知，见仪王身体康健，又来到临安成为节度使，必然欣慰。”
Matron Zou gave a wry sigh. "Your Highness is too kind-hearted—you only think of open, aboveboard paths. Squashing her is as easy as crushing an ant, yet it calls for methods that stay out of the daylight."

邹嬷嬷擦完眼泪，趁机引出来意：“仪王现在得官家重用，来到临安担任要职，也与郡主成亲，人生可谓圆满，郡主却高兴不来。”
"Tell me."

钱氏也擦去眼泪：“为何？”
"Your Highness cannot dirty her own hands, nor can Prince Yi ever catch wind of it. We need to borrow someone else's blade."

邹嬷嬷说：“亲是成了，可未圆房，仪王还跑去司理院忙活，都好几日没回幽林别居。”
"Whose blade?"

乔守仁与钱氏对视一番，仪王抢亲之事他们听说过，只是不大明白朱家姑娘能有这么大魅力？
"Since returning to Lin'an, I still haven't called on Master Qiao and Madam Qian. Why not pay them a visit today?"

邹嬷嬷继续说：“仪王心里装着别人，王妃整日以泪洗面，我看着心疼。”
Understanding dawned on Qiao Mengrong at once. "I'll trouble you to handle it, Matron Zou."

乔守仁说：“邹嬷嬷多劝劝仪王妃，让她别难过，男人总有不开窍的时候，仪王还年轻，将个把女子放心上再正常不过，等醒悟后，自然会好。”
Accompanied by a maid, Matron Zou rode by carriage straight to the Qiao Manor.

邹嬷嬷瞧瞧屋子里站着乔府的下人：“乔老爷，让下人回避一下，我有话跟二位说。”
Hearing Matron Zou had come calling, Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian lit up with welcome. She had started out as a maid right here in the Qiao household before following Consort Qiao to Bianliang and becoming Prince Yi's wet nurse. After their daughter passed away, Matron Zou had raised the prince to manhood—her service to the Qiao clan was beyond measure. They couldn't dream of treating her lightly and hurried out to greet her in person.

乔守仁向下人吩咐道：“都下去吧，出去时把门关好。”
Standing outside the masters' courtyard, Matron Zou saw both host and hostess rushing out to receive her and felt a quiet swell of pride. For a servant to command such respect was rare indeed. She dropped into a crisp bow. "Master, Madam—now that I've returned to Lin'an with Prince Yi and settled things for His Highness and the Princess Consort, I came straight over to see you."

邹嬷嬷也对跟着自己的丫鬟示意，丫鬟明白，与乔家的下人们一起出去把门从外面关好。
Madam Qian hurried forward to clasp her hands. "No need for all that ceremony!" Thinking of her late daughter made her nose prickle and her eyes turn red.

邹嬷嬷这才说：“老爷和夫人应该清楚仪王心中之人是谁，只要她还在，王妃心中难安。若是在京城，得罪王妃，只有死路一条，仪王府的侍卫便能将她处理干净……”
"Don't stand out in the breeze—come inside and talk," Qiao Shouren urged.

听到这话，乔守仁和钱氏心中害怕。
Once the three sat down indoors, they drifted into memories: Consort Qiao's girlhood pranks in Lin'an, the harsh trials after entering the palace as an imperial consort, giving birth to Prince Yi, and slipping away far too young. Before long, all three were dabbing damp eyes.

钱氏小心说：“她也已嫁人，王妃没必要跟她计较。”
Qiao Shouren pulled himself together first. "All water under the bridge now. If the Consort knows from the netherworld that Prince Yi is healthy again and back in Lin'an as Military Commissioner, she'll rest in peace."

邹嬷嬷说：“仪王因她跑去抢亲，令王妃颜面何存？现在又因心里念着她，连洞房都未入，王妃更是难过，只有她消失，王妃才能心安。可王妃在临安没有可用之人，我便替她前来拜见老爷和夫人。”
Wiping her tears, Matron Zou seized her cue. "Prince Yi enjoys His Majesty's favor, holds a grand post here, and has wed Princess Jiahe—his life looks complete on paper, yet the Princess Consort can't find a shred of joy."

乔守仁头上冒出冷汗，毕竟是条人命：“王妃会不会想得太严重？都是过去的事儿，听说她在陈家过得不错，还把陈家二公子给管教好，看得出是想安心过日子。再说她爹是临安知府，若女儿出事，必然彻查到底。”
Madam Qian blotted her eyes. "Why is that?"

邹嬷嬷很是不屑：“哼！临安知府又如何？见到仪王妃还不得跪拜？谁让他们的女儿不开眼得罪贵人！”
"They held the wedding, yet they still haven't consummated the marriage. Now Prince Yi has moved over to the Judicial Office—hasn't set foot back in Youlin Retreat for days."

乔守仁试探地问道：“邹嬷嬷前来，想让乔家做什么？”
Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian traded an uneasy look. They had heard about Prince Yi crashing Zhu Shuzhen's wedding, though neither could fathom what spell that Zhu girl had cast on him.

邹嬷嬷说：“仪王妃现在只能靠乔家，乔家家大业大，为了看家护院收取租子，养着不少打手，希望乔老爷帮王妃解决心腹之患。”
"His Highness carries another woman in his heart," Matron Zou sighed. "The Princess Consort cries herself sick every day—breaks my heart to watch."

钱氏吓得不敢说话。
"Do try to soothe her, Matron," Qiao Shouren said. "Men are slow to come to their senses. Prince Yi is young—getting infatuated with a girl here or there happens. Once the fog clears, he'll come right back around."

乔守仁头上继续冒着冷汗，心下思忖：
Matron Zou cast a meaningful glance at the Qiao servants lining the walls. "Master Qiao, send the servants out. I have something private to say to you both."

乔家仰仗的是仪王而并不是仪王妃，此事仪王若知，肯定会对乔家不满。乔家再看不上朱家姑娘，也不能干伤天害理之事，何况是一条人命？
Qiao Shouren waved a hand. "All of you leave, and shut the doors behind you."

即便帮仪王妃，将此事做得天衣无缝，凭朱晞颜的本事最终也能查到他的头上，只怕到时仪王妃不认账，乔家岂不是成替死鬼？﻿可乔家也是万万不能得罪仪王妃。
Matron Zou nodded to her own maid too, who filed out with the Qiao servants and pulled the doors tight.

乔守仁犹豫片刻想到一个好办法，他继续擦擦额头的冷汗：“乔家帮仪王妃自然义不容辞，可让人消失的事儿乔家从未做过，下不去手。再说我养的那些个打手吓唬吓唬人还可以，让他们干要命的事儿，估计逃跑的速度比兔子还快。不过，我倒有个主意……”
Only then did Matron Zou lean forward. "Master and Madam both know who sits in Prince Yi's heart. So long as she lives, the Princess Consort will never know peace. Back in the capital, crossing a princess consort meant instant death—the prince's guards would have made her disappear without a trace..."

邹嬷嬷算是听出来，乔守仁不乐意，那就听听他会出什么主意：“乔老爷说来听听。”
A cold knot gripped Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian.

“嬷嬷说的事儿还需找黑道人做，临安城有个黑帮叫钱塘帮，听说也做收钱取人性命的买卖，不如找找他们？”乔守仁又担心邹嬷嬷帮仪王妃麻烦乔家帮忙，紧接着说，“乔家只跟白道人往来，黑道从未接触过。”
"She's already married to another man," Madam Qian ventured timidly. "Surely the Princess Consort needn't bother with her?"

邹嬷嬷心想：“看来乔家铁定心不愿帮忙，不过出的主意倒不错，可以试试。”然后叮嘱道：“乔老爷既然帮不了这个忙，就当我的话没说，你们还需将今日听到的烂在肚子里。”
"Prince Yi crashed her wedding over that woman, leaving the Princess Consort with not a scrap of face! Now he's still pining for her and won't even enter the bridal chamber! Only if that woman vanishes will the Princess Consort breathe easy. Since Her Highness has no trusted hands of her own in Lin'an, I came on her behalf to ask Master and Madam for help."

两人起身将邹嬷嬷送出后，钱氏差点倒下，还好被乔守仁扶住。
Cold sweat beaded on Qiao Shouren's brow—this was a human life she was talking about! "Isn't the Princess Consort taking this too far? It's all in the past. Word is the girl's settled down nicely at Chen Manor and even reformed Second Young Master Chen—clearly she intends to live a quiet married life. Besides, her father is the Prefect of Lin'an! If anything happens to his daughter, he'll tear this city apart hunting the culprit!"

“老爷，她看着柔弱，怎么会如此想？”
Matron Zou let out a contemptuous snort. "Humph! What's a Lin'an Prefect? He still has to kneel before Princess Consort Yi! Blame their blind daughter for daring to cross royalty!"

“人是看不出来的。”
Testing the waters, Qiao Shouren asked, "What exactly does Matron Zou want the Qiao family to do?"

“要不要告诉仪王？”
"The Princess Consort has nobody to lean on here except the Qiaos. Your family owns vast estates and keeps plenty of hired thugs to guard the property and collect rents. Her Highness hopes Master Qiao will clear this thorn out of her side."

“还是不要瞎掺和。”
Terror froze Madam Qian's tongue.

钱氏站稳后，乔守仁松开手。
Beads of sweat kept trickling down Qiao Shouren's temples as his mind raced. *The Qiao family's real patron is Prince Yi, not Princess Consort Yi! If the prince ever finds out, we're finished! No matter how little I like that Zhu girl, I won't touch murder! Even if we pulled it off cleanly, Zhu Xiyan's sharp enough to trace it straight back to my door—and if the Princess Consort plays innocent, the Qiaos take the fall!* Yet he couldn't afford to flat-out offend Princess Consort Yi either.

“老爷，毕竟是人命……”
After a tense pause, a way out struck him. Swiping the sweat off his brow, he said, "Serving Princess Consort Yi is our duty, of course—only the Qiao household has never made anyone disappear, and we don't have the stomach for it. Those thugs on my payroll are fine for roughing up rent-skippers, yet tell them to spill blood and they'll bolt faster than rabbits! Still... I do have a suggestion."

乔守仁用手捂住钱氏的嘴巴：“烂在肚子里，对谁都不要提起。我会让文渊多劝劝仪王，不要再想着朱家姑娘，况且她已成为陈家二少夫人。”
Matron Zou saw right through his dodge, yet she leaned in anyway. "Let's hear it, Master Qiao."

钱氏点点头。
"Work like that takes underworld hands. Lin'an has a syndicate called the Qiantang Gang—rumor says they take silver for wet work. Why not sound them out?" Afraid she might ask him to broker the deal, he added in a hurry, "The Qiaos only deal with respectable folks—we've never crossed paths with the underworld."

乔守仁拿开手，对外面的下人说：“去，把公子叫来。”
*Clearly the Qiaos refuse to lift a finger,* Matron Zou thought, *still, that lead is worth checking.* "Since Master Qiao can't help," she warned darkly, "forget I ever opened my mouth. Take every word you heard today to your grave."

没过多久，乔文仙前来，拜见父母：“不知父亲和母亲唤儿子前来有何事？”
The instant they saw Matron Zou out the gate, Madam Qian's knees buckled; only Qiao Shouren's arm kept her upright.

儿子与去年闹绝食时完全不同，钱氏用满意的笑容掩饰掉刚才的惊恐：“来，坐下说。”
"Husband—she looks so gentle and fragile! How could she plot something like that?"

乔文仙在旁边的椅子上坐下，等待父母发话。
"You can never judge a heart by its face."

乔守仁说：“文渊啊，你现在是好了，妻妾都有，还心甘情愿接纳她们。可仪王……，刚才邹嬷嬷来过，从她口中得知仪王还未跟王妃圆房，长此以往，哪个女人能受得了？何况还是仪王妃？你跟仪王无话不谈，抽个时间找他聊聊，劝说劝说。”
"Should we warn Prince Yi?"

“他回来那日，儿子亲自去幽林别居已劝说过，无济于事。”
"Stay out of it!" Once Madam Qian caught her footing, he released her arm.

钱氏说：“你再去劝说劝说，说多了，他的观念自然会被改变过。”
"Husband, it's a human life—"

“人的观念哪有那么容易改变？”
Qiao Shouren clapped a hand over her mouth. "Bury it in your belly! Not a peep to anyone! I'll have Wenyuan keep working on Prince Yi to give up that Zhu girl—she's Second Young Madam Chen now, for heaven's sake!"

乔守仁说：“你不是已经改变过来？爹相信仪王也会慢慢改变。不变又能如何？总不能利用手中的权势把人家抢过去当他的侧妃？”
Madam Qian gave a shaky nod. Letting go, Qiao Shouren barked at a servant outside, "Go fetch the Young Master!"

“好吧，我答应爹娘今日找仪王聊聊。”
Before long, Qiao Wenxian arrived and bowed. "Father, Mother—what did you summon me for?"

两人齐声说：“这就对了！”
Looking at her son—nothing like the lovesick wreck who had starved himself last year—Madam Qian hid her lingering panic behind an approving smile. "Come, sit down."

乔文仙拜别父母回到自己的院子。
Qiao Wenxian took a chair and waited.

自成亲后，乔守仁给他换了更大的院子，毕竟娶了一妻一妾，以前的小院子根本住不下。
"Wenyuan," Qiao Shouren began, "you've sorted yourself out nicely—wife and concubine both, and you've come to accept them willingly. Prince Yi, on the other hand... Matron Zou just stopped by and let slip that His Highness still hasn't consummated his marriage with the Princess Consort! How long do you expect any woman to stomach that, let alone Princess Consort Yi? You and the prince tell each other everything—find a chance to talk some sense into him."

院子的正屋属于男主人，东厢房住着苗知瑜，西厢房住着夏果，下人们则住在后院。
"I already tried the day he got back at Youlin Retreat," Qiao Wenxian sighed. "Didn't budge him an inch."

每次踏进院子，乔文仙心中不免变得沉重，总怕见到两个女人为他争风吃醋。这两个女人他并不喜欢，与她们圆房也只是屈从于生活而已，当然也有身体萌生的欲望。
"Try again!" Madam Qian urged. "Keep hammering at it and his mind will come around."

当他被身体的欲望支配时，觉得自己跟动物没有什么区别。自从放下淑真，他心甘情愿接受自己变成动物，获得身体快乐的同时，别人也快乐，一家人都高兴。
"People don't change their hearts that easily."

说是放下淑真，只是讲给别人听而已。其实他把淑真藏在心底最深处，他人都无法窥探到。
"Didn't *you* change yours?" Qiao Shouren countered. "I believe Prince Yi will come around too. What choice does he have? He can't exactly use his title to kidnap another man's wife and make her his secondary consort!"

每当回忆起以前的经历，想淑真想得难以入眠时，他都会去找夏果。她与淑真有几分相似，乔文仙只能用这几分相似慰藉思念之情，故而他去夏果的房中居多。
"All right," Qiao Wenxian relented. "I'll go talk to him today."

苗知瑜很不开心，为了院中安宁，他又不得不去苗知瑜房中。
"Now you're talking!" both parents beamed.

若说他跟夏果在一起主动居多，跟苗知瑜则相反，他懒得主动。倒是苗知瑜，跟他在一起时一点儿不像淑女，令乔文仙汗颜。第一晚时，的确将乔文仙给惊到。苗知瑜解释说，新婚前家人找来有经验的嬷嬷，将方法传授给她。
Taking leave of them, Qiao Wenxian walked back to his own courtyard. Since marrying, his father had moved him into a larger compound—one wife and one concubine couldn't squeeze into his old bachelor quarters. The main hall belonged to him, the east wing to Miao Zhiyu, the west wing to Xiaguo, and the rear quarters to the servants.

苗知瑜的主动渐渐会将乔文仙搞得躁动不安，这时他便会由被动变为主动，享受暂时成为动物的快乐。
Every time he stepped through his own gate, a dull weight pressed on his chest, dreading fresh jealousy between his two women. He loved neither of them; bedding them was simply surrendering to life—alongside plain physical appetite. Whenever bodily urge took over, he felt no better than an animal. Ever since letting Shuzhen go, he had resigned himself to being that animal—he got his release, the women stayed content, and his parents beamed.

今日苗知瑜回娘家，夏果也去参加妾室的聚会，乔文仙走进院子有种说不出的轻松。
Claiming he had "let Shuzhen go" was just a line for everyone else. In truth, he had locked her away in the deepest corner of his chest where nobody could peek. Whenever old memories kept him tossing sleepless at night, missing Shuzhen till his ribs ached, he drifted to Xiaguo's room. She bore a faint likeness to Shuzhen, and he borrowed that shadow to soothe his longing—which meant he spent most nights in the west wing.

幼凡跟南双正站在院中说话。
That left Miao Zhiyu sulking, so to keep the peace he had to visit the east wing too. If he usually took the lead with Xiaguo, with Miao Zhiyu he couldn't muster the spark—yet Miao Zhiyu shocked him breathless by throwing all ladylike reserve out the window. On their wedding night she had nearly bowled him over, explaining later that her family had hired a seasoned matron beforehand to drill her in bedroom arts. Her bold advances soon got his blood racing anyway, flipping him from passive to eager as he lost himself in brief, mindless pleasure.

乔文仙很纳闷：“幼凡，你不是跟少夫人回娘家了吗？怎么回来？”
Today Miao Zhiyu was visiting her parents and Xiaguo was out at a concubines' gathering, leaving the courtyard wonderfully quiet. Stepping inside, Qiao Wenxian spotted Youfan and Nan Shuang chatting by the veranda.

幼凡行礼道：“回姑爷，少夫人忘记带东西，让我回来取，取完还得赶回苗府。”
"Youfan?" he asked, puzzled. "Didn't you go with Young Madam to her parents' house? Why are you back?"

幼凡说完，对南双笑笑，向东厢房走去。
Youfan dropped a hasty curtsy. "Young Madam forgot something, Young Master, so she sent me back to fetch it before hurrying back to Miao Manor." Flashing Nan Shuang a quick smile, she hurried into the east wing.

南双上前拉住公子的手臂：“公子，能不能让我快些娶幼凡？我也想……”
Nan Shuang sidled up and tugged his master's sleeve. "Young Master, couldn't you let me marry Youfan sooner? I want to—"

乔文仙用手敲敲南双的脑门儿：“别以为我不知，都传到我耳朵里，你们两个注意点儿，别把我的院子搞得乌七八糟，小心我棒打鸳鸯。”
Qiao Wenxian rapped his knuckle on Nan Shuang's forehead. "Don't think I haven't heard the rumors! Behave yourselves and stop turning my courtyard into a scandal, or I'll break you two lovebirds up!"

南双埋怨道：“公子只顾自己快乐，都不管我。”
"Young Master only cares about his own fun and leaves me hanging!" Nan Shuang pouted.

乔文仙笑道：“行了，明年给你们成亲。”
Qiao Wenxian chuckled. "Fine, fine—I'll marry you two off next year."

南双高兴道：“谢公子！”
"Thank you, Young Master!" Nan Shuang whooped.

幼凡手里拿着卷好的纸张，从东厢房出来，能看得出上面写有字迹。
Just then Youfan emerged from the east wing clutching a roll of paper covered in ink.

乔文仙十分纳闷：“怎么回娘家还要带这些东西？”
"Why does she need written sheets at her parents' house?" Qiao Wenxian wondered aloud.

幼凡看上去很紧张，并未回答，行完礼匆匆离开。
Looking flustered, Youfan didn't answer—she dipped a quick curtsy and hurried straight out.

乔文仙并未多想，对南双说：“进屋换身衣裳，再陪我去趟司理院拜见仪王。”
Thinking little of it, Qiao Wenxian told Nan Shuang, "Come help me change robes, then walk with me to the Judicial Office to see Prince Yi."

两人进入屋内，南双帮公子换完衣裳后出来，夏果刚好从外面回来，身后跟着丫鬟艾墨。
They had barely changed and stepped back out when Xiaguo returned through the gate with her maid Aimo trailing behind.

“文渊这是要出门？”
"Heading out, Wenyuan?"

“你回来得倒快？”
"Back already?"

夏果搀住文渊，故意撒娇：“怎么？嫌我回来得早？你穿得这么整齐，该不会是出去会情人？”
Linking her arm through his, Xiaguo put on a syrupy pout. "What, disappointed I came home early? Dressed up so sharp—don't tell me you're sneaking out to meet a sweetheart?"

夏果撒娇的样子，乔文仙无法与她置气：“别胡说，我去见仪王。”
Her playful coaxing melted any edge off him. "Stop talking nonsense. I'm going to see Prince Yi."

夏果放开文渊，继续用撒娇的声音说：“既是见贵人，快去快回，我在家里等着你。”
Letting go of his sleeve, Xiaguo kept her honeyed purr going. "If it's His Highness, hurry back—I'll be waiting right here for you."
