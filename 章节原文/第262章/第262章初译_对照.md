## Chapter 262: Only When I Fall for You

跟着女茶师大概学个皮毛，陈浩宇已深切认识到做茶并不容易，尤其是茶汤中作画，更是难上加难。起码得跟着女茶师学个十天半月，再勤加练习才可做得像样。
学习结束后，天色已暗下，回陈府还是比往常早了许多。想起答应女茶师的事，一个弯儿没拐，直奔父母的院子。
陈江林和姚轻琰闲来无事，坐在榻上品茶聊天儿。
“江林，你真的打算让淑真管理施粥棚？”
“老太太都发话，总不能拗着她。”
“淑真也是，在家里写写诗，作作词，没事陪长辈聊聊天儿，多好，非要主动提出来管理施粥棚，出去抛头露面的被人笑话，再遇到些地痞流氓可怎么办？”
“我开始和你想得一样，现在倒认为淑真去也挺不错。”
姚轻琰不解：“为何？难道是因为她的拳头厉害？”
“只是一个方面。当初给浩宇定下淑真时，有人在背后议论浩宇吃了大亏，竟然娶个和离过一次的姑娘，还说陈家是被知府大人给逼的。现在淑真成为陈家的儿媳妇，指不定还有人说三道四。我想不如让她管管施粥棚，让那些爱嚼舌根的人瞧瞧我们陈家娶了一位能干的儿媳妇。也顺便羡慕羡慕陈家的女人，既有夫君疼爱，又不受礼俗约束。”
姚轻琰笑道：“说得也是，我听你的，不再嫌弃淑真管理施粥棚。”
陈浩宇进来正好听到：“什么？我娘子竟然要管理施粥棚？不行，我得去帮她。”
陈江林笑着批评道：“进来也不通报一声？越来越不懂礼数。”
“我这不是想悄悄进来看爹怎么对待娘，好学习学习，回头用在我娘子身上。”陈浩宇行完礼，在一旁的椅子上坐下。
姚轻琰夸道：“我儿子知道疼媳妇，礼数不会太差。”
“还是娘最懂我。”陈浩宇趁机提出要求，“爹，要不给我几天假，我陪淑真去施粥棚？”
陈江林说：“需征求淑真的意思，她若同意，我也没意见，给你放几天假都行。她若不同意，你还是老老实实当伙计。听秋管事说，你已将伙计的差事做得轻车熟路，再锻炼七日，可以跟着账房先生学习管账做账。”
陈浩宇一脸怀疑：“这么快？秋管事说得当两个月的伙计，难道他骗我？”
姚轻琰说：“秋管事没骗你，难道听不出来？你爹是夸你学得快。”
陈江林点头认可：“既然伙计的差事已学会，没必要继续浪费时间，早早进入下一步才是正事。”
陈浩宇高兴不已，以前只顾玩乐，竟不知自己如此能干，不由喜上眉梢。还是尽快说女茶师的事儿：“爹，我听说您要收购西湖边的永泰茶楼？”
陈江林回道：“是有此事，已谈妥，再过五日永泰茶楼就是陈家的，得重新把里面布置布置。那里可是许多文人雅士聚集之地，前一个商家经营不善，里面的字画许久未更换，去的人越来越少，等陈家接手后必然一个月更换一次，再让永泰茶楼恢复往日盛况。”
Barely scratching the surface with the female tea master taught Chen Haoyu that whisking tea was no walk in the park—and painting pictures in tea foam was harder still. It would take at least ten days or half a month of steady drills before he could produce anything decent.

陈浩宇并不关心字画的事：“爹，能不能求您件事。”
Darkness had fallen when he wrapped up, still far earlier than usual. Remembering his promise to the tea master, he headed straight to his parents' courtyard without a single detour.

“说。”
Inside, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan sat sharing tea on the daybed.

“可不可以把永泰茶楼的女茶师留下，最好把原来的人都留下。”
"Jianglin, are you really letting Shuzhen run the congee shed?"

“你怎么管起这等事来？”
"Mother already gave her word—hardly my place to overrule her."

“儿子以前欺负过永泰茶楼的人，现在想来甚觉愧疚，想做点事补偿。”
"Still, Shuzhen could stay home writing poems and keeping the elders company. Why volunteer to run a congee shed? Showing her face out in public invites gossip—and what if street thugs cause trouble?"

两人为儿子的变化感到欣慰。
"I felt the same at first, yet now I think it's a fine move."

姚轻琰说：“浩宇好不容易替别人求你一次，就答应吧。”
Yao Qingyan blinked. "Because of her fists?"

陈江林笑道：“夫人都发话，我只有同意的份儿。”
"Partly. Back when we engaged Haoyu to her, gossips whispered he got stuck with a divorced woman because Prefect Zhu twisted our arm. Now that she's our daughter-in-law, tongues are still wagging. Letting her whip that congee shed into shape shows every gossip in Lin'an what a capable daughter-in-law we married—and makes everyone envy Chen women, loved by their husbands and free from stuffy rules."

陈浩宇起身：“谢谢爹！谢谢娘！你们继续，儿子回墨香苑。”
Yao Qingyan laughed. "Fair point. I won't fuss about it anymore."

陈浩宇又带着青瓜快步离开。
Stepping right through the door, Haoyu blurted, "What?! My wife is managing the congee shed? No way—I'm going to help her!"

姚轻琰面带笑容埋怨道：“这孩子！一看就是想媳妇了，跑得这么快！”
"Walk right in without announcing yourself? Where are your manners?" Chen Jianglin teased.

刚走进墨香苑，丑丫一把将青瓜拽走，连招呼都不打。陈浩宇懒得计较，瞧向素心草堂，灯还亮着，说明娘子并未休息。
"Just sneaking in to watch how Father spoils Mother so I can copy your moves on my own wife!" Haoyu grinned, bowing before dropping into a chair.

他走到门前，贴着门向里说：“娘子，我今儿回来得早，有事跟你商量，能不能让我进去？”
"A son who knows how to dote on his wife can't be lacking in manners," Yao Qingyan beamed.

明日开始要管理施粥棚，写话本的时间必然缩短，千里寻想趁今日多写一些。正写着，外面传来陈浩宇的声音。既打算接受他，态度需好些。
"Mother understands me best!" Haoyu pressed his luck. "Father, give me a few days off so I can go with Shuzhen to the congee shed."

“今儿就写到这儿，你把手稿收了，我去开门。”
"Ask Shuzhen first," Chen Jianglin said. "If she agrees, take all the days you like. If not, stay at the counter. Steward Qiu tells me you've mastered shop-clerk duties already—seven more days of polish and you're moving to the counting house to learn bookkeeping."

苦荞边整理手稿边观察小姐。
Haoyu stared in disbelief. "That fast? Steward Qiu said two full months—was he bluffing?"

小姐起身走到门口，犹豫一会儿，将门打开。
"He wasn't bluffing, silly," Yao Qingyan chuckled. "Your father's praising how fast you learn."

姑爷先愣住，应该是没想到会是小姐给他开门，接着一脸兴奋。
Chen Jianglin nodded. "Once you know the counter inside out, why waste another six weeks? Better move on to the next stage."

“娘子竟然亲自为我开门，真是喜从天降。”
Haoyu grinned ear to ear—he'd wasted years playing around without realizing he actually had a head for trade. Remembering his errand, he shifted gears. "Father, I heard you're buying Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake?"

苦荞忍不住笑了一下，姑爷自从变好后，嘴巴一日比一日甜，也不知什么时候能把小姐给甜透。
"Deal's sealed. Five days from now it's ours, and we'll give it a full overhaul. Literati used to flock there until the old owner got sloppy and stopped rotating the wall paintings. Once we take over, we'll swap in fresh art every month and restore its former glory."

千里寻本想怼两句，还是忍住，脸上尽量带些笑容：“你今儿回来得倒早，有什么事吗？”
"Could I ask a favor, Father? Keep the female tea master on—in fact, keep the whole original staff."

“能不能进去说？门口也不是说话的地儿。”没等娘子同意，陈浩宇大大方方走进去，转过身，“我今日虽然一直在忙，可心里无时无刻不牵挂娘子。听说娘子要去管理施粥棚，能不能带上我？爹说了，只要你同意，他就同意。”
"Since when do you meddle in staffing?"

千里寻不断提醒自己不要说怼人的话：“你不是在绸缎庄忙吗？哪有时间跟我去施粥棚？”
"I used to bully the folks there. Looking back, I feel awful about it and want to make amends."

“只要你乐意，我可以向秋管事请几日假，专程陪你。”
Warm pride shone in both parents' eyes. "First time Haoyu's ever pleaded on someone else's behalf—say yes," Yao Qingyan urged.

“我不需要人陪，你还是好好在绸缎庄忙活。”
"When my wife speaks, I obey," Chen Jianglin laughed.

“可我想陪你，能不能给个机会？”陈浩宇故意靠近娘子，盯着她那双漂亮的眼睛，越看心里越痒，真想上前亲上一口。
Haoyu sprang up. "Thanks, Father! Thanks, Mother! Carry on—I'm off to Moxiang Courtyard!" Whisking Qinggua out, he bolted down the path, leaving Yao Qingyan smiling fondly: "Look at him dash—obviously missing his wife!"

要搁往常，千里寻必然一拳打上去，可她已决定要学会接纳，攥紧的拳头渐渐松开：“陈浩宇，别这样盯着老娘看，小心我没忍住，用拳头打你。”
The second they entered Moxiang Courtyard, Chouya pounced and hauled Qinggua off without a word. Paying them no mind, Haoyu saw lamps glowing inside Suxin Thatched Hall, leaned near the wooden door, and called in, "Wife, I'm home early! Got something to run by you—may I come in?"

陈浩宇收回眼神：“你不是没打吗？说明你已经喜欢上我，对不对？喜欢就直说，我是你夫君，在我面前没必要矜持，要不今晚我留下来陪你？”
Knowing tomorrow's congee-shed duty would cut into her writing time, Qianli Xun had been pushing out extra huaben pages. Hearing Haoyu outside, she reminded herself to soften her tone now that she'd decided to give him a fair shot. "That's enough for tonight," she told Kuqiao. "Pack up the pages—I'll get the door."

千里寻本想发作，想起苦荞说过的话，将大骂的话吞了回去，走到书桌前坐下：“你且坐下，我有话问你。”
Kuqiao gathered the manuscript while watching her mistress walk over, pause a heartbeat, and open the door.

陈浩宇知道娘子不会那么轻易答应与他圆房，今日她没发火，已是进步，便乖乖挑椅子坐下：“娘子请说，我洗耳恭听。”
Haoyu froze in stunned delight—his wife answering the door herself! "Wife opening the door for me in person? Did luck just drop out of the sky?"

“你有多久没去青楼？”
Kuqiao hid a giggle; ever since turning over a new leaf, the young master's tongue had grown sweeter by the day.

“自从你把我绑回来就没再去过。”
Swallowing a snappy comeback, Qianli Xun offered a faint smile. "You're back early. What's on your mind?"

“以前你多久去一次？”
"Let me come in—can't talk in the doorway." Without waiting for permission, he breezed inside and spun around. "Busy as I was today, my mind never left you. I heard you're taking charge of the congee shed—take me along! Father said if you say yes, he says yes."

“没仔细算过，没事就去。”
Holding back her usual barbs, she replied, "Aren't you busy at the silk emporium? Where would you find the time?"

“难怪。”千里寻停顿片刻，继续说，“晚上时，是不是很想身边有个姑娘陪你？”
"Say the word and I'll ask Steward Qiu for a few days off to keep you company."

“我想让娘子你陪我。”
"I don't need company. Stick to your work at the emporium."

“如果你娶别人当你的娘子会不会也想让她陪你？”
"What if I *want* to keep you company? Give me a chance?" Stepping closer, he gazed into her bright eyes until an itch to steal a kiss made his fingers twitch.

陈浩宇想想，回道：“应该会吧。”
Any other day her fist would have flown; tonight her clenched fingers eased open. "Chen Haoyu, quit staring at me like that before my fist forgets my manners."

“那就对了，说明你只是想……，才跑来烦我，对我说那些话。不如我帮你纳房妾室，晚上有人陪你，你也开心，我也落得清静。”
He blinked back with a cheeky grin. "You didn't hit me! Means you've already fallen for me, right? I'm your husband—no need to be shy! How about I stay the night right here?"

陈浩宇瞪大眼睛，娘子问来问去，原来是想给他纳妾。他腾地站起来：“我不纳妾！”
Her temper flared, then cooled at the memory of Kuqiao's warning. Swallowing her curses, she walked to her desk and sat. "Sit down. I have a few questions for you."

“纳妾多好，有人陪你。”
Knowing she wouldn't cave that fast—and counting no shouting as a huge win—Haoyu obediently pulled up a chair. "I'm all ears, Wife."

陈浩宇气呼呼地靠近书桌，双手撑在桌上，瞪着铜铃般的眼睛：“最后强调一遍！我不纳妾！我要跟你圆房！晚上只想让你陪着我！”
"How long since you last went to a brothel?"

苦荞心想：“没想到姑爷对小姐如此痴情。”
"Not once since you tied me up and dragged me home."

千里寻被陈浩宇突如其来的举动搞得懵住，一时不知该如何是好。
"Before that, how often did you go?"

陈浩宇生完气已忘记来此的目的，收回撑着书桌的手，准备离开。
"Never counted. Whenever I was bored."

他刚走出几步，被千里寻叫住：“陈浩宇！”
"Figures." Qianli Xun paused. "At night, do you itch to have a girl warm your bed?"

陈浩宇站住：“娘子还有何事？”
"I want *you* to keep me company."

“想跟我圆房，必须让我爱上你。”
"If you'd married some other woman, wouldn't you want her keeping you company too?"

“我会努力，总有一日你会爱上我。”
Haoyu thought a second. "I suppose so."

“我等着。”
"There you go. You're only pestering me and spouting sweet nothings because of plain physical itch. Tell you what—let me take in a concubine for you. You get company at night, and I get peace and quiet."

娘子愿意等，陈浩宇一高兴转过身：“明日我陪你去施粥棚？”
Haoyu's eyes popped wide. All those questions had been leading up to foisting a concubine on him! He shot to his feet. "I am *not* taking a concubine!"

“不必，你还是好好学习做生意，我希望我的夫君是一个能为我撑起一片天的人。”
"Why not? Someone to keep you happy—"

陈浩宇脸上露出欣慰的笑容，第一次被人拒绝后，还如此开心。
Stalking up to her desk, he planted both palms on the wood and glared down at her with blazing eyes. "One last time! I don't want any concubine! I want to consummate our marriage with *you*! You're the only woman I want beside me at night!"

姑爷走后，苦荞夸赞道：“小姐今日表现得很好，没有骂姑爷，还能跟姑爷认真聊天儿，你们的关系向前迈进一大步。”
*Who knew Young Master was this devoted?* Kuqiao marveled silently.

千里寻并没有开心的感觉，多少还是有些委屈：“苦荞，你说我会爱上陈浩宇吗？”
Startled speechless by his raw outburst, Qianli Xun stared up at him. Steaming so hard he forgot why he had come, Haoyu pushed off the desk and headed for the door—only for her voice to halt him after three steps.

“只要小姐愿意，肯定会。”
"Chen Haoyu!"

“如果说爱就能爱上一个人，那该多好。”
He stopped. "What else, Wife?"

千里寻在委屈自己的道路上越走越远，已无回头路可走，只能勇往直前，也许有一日“委屈”没了，只剩下心甘情愿。
"If you want to consummate this marriage, you have to make me fall in love with you first."

……
"I'll work for it. One day you *will* love me."

昨晚，心甘情愿宠幸了梦容，将她变成他的女人，申简辰决定要好好待她。
"I'll be waiting."

他坐起来，瞅瞅身旁熟睡中的梦容，她的面庞变得更加娇艳，他不由垂下头吻了一下。
At *I'll be waiting*, pure sunlight broke across his face. Spinning back, he grinned, "So—can I come with you to the congee shed tomorrow?"

“我会为你守身如玉。”曾在千里寻面前说过的话回荡在耳边，他的嘴角露出嘲笑自己的表情，心中骂道：“ 你根本做不到，又何必在她面前信誓旦旦？”
"No. Stick to learning the business. I want a husband who can hold up the sky on his own."

申简辰准备下床，乔梦容醒来，从背后抱住他：“哥哥住回别居吧？或者不要再做司理参军的事，日日陪我，好吗？”
Grinning like a fool, Haoyu left—the first time in his life being turned down had ever felt this sweet.

申简辰没有回过身，背对着梦容：“我是男人，需做些正经事，节度使的事儿我毫无兴致，这几日倒是发现很喜欢做司理参军，你放心，不忙时会天天回来，忙时也会隔几日回来。”
Once the door shut, Kuqiao beamed. "You handled that so well, Miss! Didn't scold him once, actually talked things through—you two took a giant leap forward!"

“我想哥哥时，能不能去司理院找你？”
Yet a quiet ache lingered in Qianli Xun's chest. "Kuqiao... do you think I'll ever actually fall in love with Chen Haoyu?"

“你是仪王妃，是我的娘子，什么时候想去都可以。”
"If you open your heart to it, you will."

听到哥哥称她为“娘子”，乔梦容将哥哥抱得更紧：“昨晚哥哥十分有力，我从来没这么快乐过，一早醒来，仍不舍得放哥哥走，能否再陪我一会儿，我还想……”
"If only loving someone were as simple as flipping a switch." She had already walked too far down the road of bending to reality to turn back now; all she could do was march forward and hope compromise eventually turned into genuine willingness.

申简辰不愿梦容失望，转过身：“只要你开心，哥哥再陪陪你。”
Across town at Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen sat up at dawn, gazing down at Mengrong asleep beside him. Having taken her willingly last night, he resolved to treat her with wholehearted kindness. Her sleeping face looked softer, more radiant than ever; leaning down, he brushed a gentle kiss against her cheek.

乔梦容抱住哥哥疯狂地吻起来……
*I will stay chaste for you.* His own bold vow to Qianli Xun echoed back, twisting a wry, self-mocking smile onto his lips. *Couldn't even keep it—why on earth swear oaths you can't honor?*

梦容折腾够后，太阳已老高，申简辰终于可以下床。丫鬟进来为他将衣裳穿好，梳洗整理完毕后，来到前厅。
As he moved to rise, Mengrong stirred and wrapped her arms around his back. "Move back to Youlin Retreat, Brother? Or drop that Judicial Investigator job and stay with me every day—please?"

邹嬷嬷已在那里候着，见王爷出来，问道：“怎么不见王妃？”
Without turning, he answered gently, "A man needs proper work. Military Commissioner bores me, whereas I actually enjoy running the Judicial Office. Don't worry—when work is light I'll come home every night, and even when busy I'll return every few days."

“梦容累了，让她多睡会儿。”
"May I visit you at the Judicial Office when I miss you?"

邹嬷嬷从丫鬟手中端过盘子：“昨晚王爷辛苦，这是专程为您熬的滋补粥。”
"You're Princess Consort Yi—my wife. Come whenever you wish."

邹嬷嬷将盘子放在桌上。
Hearing him call her *my wife* made her hug him tighter. "You were so strong last night, Brother... I've never known bliss like that. Waking up, I still can't bear to let you go. Stay just a little longer—I still want..."

申简辰走过去坐下，拿起盘中的勺子开始享用。
Unwilling to disappoint her, he turned back into her arms. "If it makes you happy, I'll stay a bit longer." Mengrong pulled him straight into a feverish kiss.

邹嬷嬷坐在一旁抹起眼泪来：“王爷终于开窍，我真替王妃高兴，她多年的付出都是值得的。”
The sun stood high before Mengrong finally let him rise. Maids helped him wash and dress before he stepped out to the front hall, where Matron Zou waited.

申简辰不知作何回应，继续一勺一勺地吃粥。他的头脑里还是闪现出千里寻的身影，一直以来每日想她是必然之事，习惯成自然，想立刻改变习惯还需时日。
"Where is the Princess Consort?" she asked.

吃完粥，他带着白风回司理院，暂时没有新的案子，不如翻翻以前的卷宗，忙起来应该容易忘记心中所想之人。
"Mengrong's tired. Let her sleep in."

哥哥走后，乔梦容又睡了一会儿，才心满意足地从床上爬起来。回想昨晚哥哥对待她的情景，陶醉不已。
Taking a covered bowl from a maid, Matron Zou set it on the table. "After Your Highness's exertions last night, I had this nourishing tonic porridge simmered specially for you."

喜兰伺候她穿好衣装，梳洗完毕后，用过邹嬷嬷为她准备好的饭食，坐在桌前，手把书卷，双眼并不向书上瞧，继续回想哥哥将她由妹妹变成娘子的过程……
Shen Jianchen sat and spooned up the porridge while Matron Zou dabbed happy tears beside him. "At last Your Highness has come to his senses! I'm so glad for the Princess Consort—every year she poured into caring for you paid off!"

哥哥的身体实在有力，令她贪恋不已，越贪恋越担心，司理院会不会有其他女子？若有的话，哥哥在那里过夜会不会……？
Unsure what to say, he ate in silence. Qianli Xun's silhouette still flickered behind his eyes; daily habits forged over months wouldn't vanish overnight. Finishing his bowl, he took Bai Feng back to the Judicial Office—no fresh cases today, so diving into old dossiers would help keep his mind occupied.

乔梦容不敢继续想下去：“喜兰，你亲自去趟司理院，告诉哥哥，今晚一定要回幽林别居，就说他一走，我便开始想他，再到处瞧瞧司理院有没有其他女子。”
Left behind, Mengrong slept another hour before waking in a warm haze, replaying every second of last night. Even after Xilan dressed her and she ate Matron Zou's meal, she sat holding an unread scroll, daydreaming of how Brother had turned her from little sister into wife. His strength intoxicated her—and the deeper she craved him, the sharper a fresh anxiety stabbed her: what if there were other women hovering around the Judicial Office when he slept there?

喜兰答应道：“是，奴婢这就去。”
"Xilan," she ordered, unable to bear the thought, "go straight to the Judicial Office and tell Brother he *must* come home tonight—tell him I've missed him the second he walked out. And look around every corner to see if there are any women working there."

乔梦容本以为哥哥宠幸过她，便完全属于她，不用再整日担心他会喜欢上别人。
"Right away, Your Highness."

现在才发现她错了，哥哥昨晚让她很开心，她反而更担心，只怪哥哥太优秀。
Mengrong had assumed once Brother bedded her, he'd be wholly hers and her worries would vanish. Instead, tasting how extraordinary he was only made her terrified of losing him.
