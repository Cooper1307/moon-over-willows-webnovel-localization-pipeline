### 3.2 风格与流畅度问题（Style & Fluency Issues）

#### 1. 重复用词（Repetitive Vocabulary）
- **"young lady"** 出现8次以上
  - 建议：部分替换为 `miss`、`girl` 或直接使用代词 `you`，避免单调
- **"bamboo forest"** 出现12次以上
  - 建议：部分替换为 `the forest`、`the grove` 或使用代词 `it`，增强阅读流畅度
- **"bamboo house"** 出现6次
  - 建议：部分替换为 `the house`、`the cottage` 或直接使用 `home`

#### 2. 表达不够地道（Unnatural Expressions）
- **位置**：第43行
- **原文**：`He has clear eyebrows and bright eyes`
- **问题**：直译"眉清目秀"显得生硬
- **优化**：`He has handsome features` 或 `He's good-looking with sharp features`

- **位置**：第121行
- **原文**：`Qianli Xun believed that dating without the intent of marriage was playing the rogue`
- **问题**："playing the rogue" 直译不够自然
- **优化**：`Qianli Xun believed that dating without the intent of marriage was basically stringing someone along` 或 `was playing with someone's heart`

- **位置**：第547行
- **原文**：`Kujue said foolishly`
- **问题**："foolishly" 带有贬义，原文"憨憨地"更多是可爱、朴实
- **优化**：`Kujue said innocently` 或 `Kujue said with a simple smile`

#### 3. 长句需拆分（Long Sentences Need Splitting）
- **位置**：第127行
- **原文**：`Having died once, the ideals and career she once pursued were shattered at the moment of the earthquake. Borrowing Zhu Shuzhen's body to live again in another era, she gradually realized that worldly fame and fortune were like passing clouds. For an ordinary person like her, the greatest happiness was to grow old with another person. Added to that, having transmigrated, a marriage that didn't belong to her was placed before her, giving her, who had never been involved in love, a strong sense of loss.`
- **问题**：虽然已拆分为多句，但整体仍显冗长，可进一步精简
- **优化**：`Having died once, her former ideals and career were shattered in the earthquake. Now living in another era through Zhu Shuzhen's body, she realized that worldly fame and fortune were like passing clouds. For someone like her, the greatest happiness was simply growing old with another person. And now, faced with a marriage that wasn't hers—a marriage arranged for someone else—she, who had never experienced love, felt a profound sense of loss.`

#### 4. 口语化程度不足（Insufficient Colloquial Tone）
- **位置**：第511行
- **原文**：`"Young master, why do you have so many questions? Qianli Xun came suddenly and left suddenly. We're not close to him. My brother delivered manuscripts for him for money. That's all we know. We don't know anything else."`
- **问题**：语气略显正式，不够口语化
- **优化**：`"Young master, why so many questions? Qianli Xun came out of nowhere and left just as suddenly. We barely know him. My brother just delivered manuscripts for pay—that's it. We don't know anything else."`

### 3.3 文化适配问题（Cultural Adaptation Issues）

- **问题**："scraps" 可能让西方读者困惑，不够优雅
- **建议**：改为 `silver pieces` 或 `loose silver`，更符合日常用语

#### 2. "不以结婚为目的恋爱是耍流氓"的翻译（Translation of Modern Chinese Internet Meme）
- **位置**：第121行
- **原文**：`dating without the intent of marriage was playing the rogue`
- **问题**：这是中国互联网的经典梗，直译不够生动
- **建议**：改为 `dating without the intent of marriage was basically stringing someone along` 或 `was playing with someone's heart`，更符合现代英语表达

#### 3. "竹筒饭"的翻译（Translation of "Bamboo Tube Rice"）

- **原文**：`bamboo tube rice`
- **评价**：翻译准确，可接受。可考虑添加简短说明：`bamboo tube rice (a traditional dish cooked inside bamboo stalks)`
