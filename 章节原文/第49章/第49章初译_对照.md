# 第49章 相逢对面不相识

---

回到竹林，苦蕨跑去向老东家汇报，他现在见到老东家不再紧张。

Back in the bamboo forest, Kujue ran to report to the old employer. He no longer felt nervous around her.

---

千里寻刚从竹林散步回来，在竹屋门口碰到苦蕨。

Qianli Xun had just returned from a walk in the bamboo forest and ran into Kujue at the door of the bamboo house.

---

“今日回来得挺快？”

"You're back early today?"

---

苦蕨往日送手稿，来回都是步行，时间全花在路上。今日发生意外，故而回来早些。

In the past, when Kujue delivered manuscripts, he walked both ways, spending all his time on the road. Today, something unexpected happened, so he came back earlier.

---

“老东家，书稿已送到，奇怪的是有人问我是不是送书稿的，我承认后，竟然想把我抓走。”

"Old employer, the manuscript has been delivered. But strangely, someone asked me if I was the one delivering manuscripts. After I admitted it, he actually tried to take me away."

---

“哦？竟有这等事？”千里寻很疑惑，难道她写的书稿内容有问题？如果有问题说话艺人不接即可，为何会有人因此抓苦蕨？继续问，“那人是干什么的？长什么样？”

"Oh? Something like that?" Qianli Xun was very confused. Was there something wrong with the content she wrote? If there was a problem, the storyteller could just refuse to accept it. Why would someone try to arrest Kujue because of it? She continued to ask, "What does that person do? What does he look like?"

---

苦蕨回道：“干什么的我不清楚，长得眉清目秀，穿的衣裳不错，劲儿挺大。”

Kujue replied, "I don't know what he does. He has handsome features, wears nice clothes, and is quite strong."

---

“他有没有问起我的名姓？”

"Did he ask about my name?"

---

“那倒没有，即使问起，我也不会说，老东家您叮嘱过，只说是位叫千里寻的才子所写，其他一概不能说。”

"No, he didn't. Even if he did, I wouldn't tell him. You instructed me, old employer, to only say it was written by a talented scholar named Qianli Xun, and not to say anything else."

---

千里寻很满意：“你做得对。苦荞！”

Qianli Xun was very satisfied: "You did the right thing. Kuqiao!"

---

苦荞知道小姐的意思，掏出碎银，走到弟弟身边：“小姐赏你的。”

Kuqiao knew what her young lady meant. She took out some silver pieces and walked to her brother's side: "This is a reward from the young lady."

---

苦蕨推辞道：“替老东家跑腿，我乐意，不能收钱。”

Kujue declined: "I'm happy to run errands for the old employer. I can't take money."

---

苦荞硬塞到苦蕨手中：“拿着，要不小姐会不高兴。”

Kuqiao forced it into Kujue's hands: "Take it, or the young lady will be unhappy."

---

苦蕨只能接到手中：“老东家，再需要送书稿，您尽管说。”

Kujue had to take it: "Old employer, if you need manuscripts delivered again, just say the word."

---

千里寻说：“以后你不用再送手稿，还有三日，就整整一个月，我也该回去。”

Qianli Xun said, "You don't need to deliver manuscripts anymore. In three days, it will be a full month. I should go back too."

---

苦蕨小心揣着碎银，高兴离去。

Kujue carefully pocketed the silver pieces and left happily.

---

一个月实在太快，千里寻在竹林还未待够，本想处理完和离之事，再来竹林住些时日。可一想竹林已是陈浩宇的田产，只能放弃再来暂住的想法。

A month passed too quickly. Qianli Xun hadn't stayed long enough in the bamboo forest. She had planned to come back for a while after handling the divorce. But thinking that the bamboo forest was now Chen Haoyu's property, she had to give up the idea of staying here temporarily again.

---

在竹林这段时日，千里寻没有感觉到竹林为她加持了什么神奇的力量，只觉远离林府，内心越来越平静，思考问题也越发理智。她打算回去后不再用武力逼迫林觉，与他像朋友一样好好聊聊，心平气和地解决和离之事。

During her time in the bamboo forest, Qianli Xun didn't feel that the bamboo forest had bestowed any magical power on her. She only felt that being away from the Lin residence, her heart was becoming more and more peaceful, and her thinking was becoming more rational. She planned that after going back, she would no longer use force to pressure Lin Jue. Instead, she would have a good talk with him like friends and resolve the divorce calmly.

---

千里寻也理性地思考过她想要谈场恋爱的心理。

Qianli Xun had also rationally thought about her psychology of wanting to have a romantic relationship.

---

穿越前，之所以不涉足爱情，除了有理想和事业要追求外，还因她不想涉足婚姻。尽管很多人说只谈恋爱不结婚，可千里寻认为不以结婚为目的恋爱是耍流氓，她不想成为女流氓。因而，对于摆在面前的恋爱机会只有一种态度：拒绝。

Before transmigrating, the reason she didn't get involved in love, besides having ideals and a career to pursue, was that she didn't want to get involved in marriage. Although many people said they only wanted to date without marrying, Qianli Xun believed that dating without the intent of marriage was basically stringing someone along, and she didn't want to be that kind of person. Therefore, she had only one attitude towards the romantic opportunities placed before her: rejection.

---

死过一回，曾追求的理想和事业在地震那一刻被震得粉碎。借助朱淑真的身体于另是一个时代活过来，逐渐意识到人生的功名利禄如过眼烟云，对于像她这样的普通人来讲，最大的幸福莫过于与另一个人相伴到老。加之穿越而来，一场不属于她婚姻摆在面前，让从未涉足恋爱的她有种强烈的失落感。

Having died once, her former ideals and career were shattered in the earthquake. Now living in another era through Zhu Shuzhen's body, she realized that worldly fame and fortune were like passing clouds. For someone like her, the greatest happiness was simply growing old with another person. And now, faced with a marriage that wasn't hers—a marriage arranged for someone else—she, who had never experienced love, felt a profound sense of loss.

---

所以，千里寻想要谈一场恋爱，想要找一位能与她在陌生的时代共度余生之人。

So, Qianli Xun wanted to have a romantic relationship, to find someone who could spend the rest of their life with her in this unfamiliar era.

---

千里寻想过，要与谁来一场甜蜜的恋爱之旅？每一次申简辰的身影会浮现在她的脑海，她会问自己：“不知他有没有穿越而来？如果也穿越而来，该到哪里找他？”

Qianli Xun had thought about who she should have a sweet romantic journey with. Every time, Shen Jianchen's figure would appear in her mind. She would ask herself, "I wonder if he has also transmigrated? If he has, where should I look for him?"

---

她无法给出答案，也许再也遇不到申简辰。她决定将自己的爱情交给命运，若遇到心仪之人，哪怕不是申简辰，也要与那人开始一段刻骨铭心的恋情。

She couldn't give an answer. Maybe she would never meet Shen Jianchen again. She decided to leave her love to fate. If she met someone she liked, even if it wasn't Shen Jianchen, she would start an unforgettable romance with that person.

---

她到底喜欢什么样的男子？也许是像申简辰那样，毕竟曾经追求她的异性中，申简辰是最优秀的一位。也许不是，只有遇到后才知道。

What kind of man did she actually like? Maybe someone like Shen Jianchen. After all, among the opposite sex who had pursued her, Shen Jianchen was the most outstanding. Maybe not. She would only know after meeting him.

---

她幻想过申简辰穿越而来成为他喜欢的类型，神奇般地出现在她面前：“千里寻，嫁给我吧。”她一定会答应，像梦中梦到的那样。

She had fantasized that Shen Jianchen had transmigrated and become her type, appearing in front of her miraculously: "Qianli Xun, marry me." She would definitely agree, just like she had dreamed.

---

千里寻回到竹屋，吩咐苦荞和夏果把能收拾的东西先整理好，甚至让她们把书桌也收了，剩下的两三日，她不想再动笔墨，打算除吃饭睡觉外，其他时间去竹林转转。

Qianli Xun returned to the bamboo house and instructed Kuqiao and Xiaguo to pack up what they could first, even having them put away the desk. For the remaining two or three days, she didn't want to write anymore. She planned to spend her time walking around the bamboo forest, except for eating and sleeping.

---

要离开，真有些舍不得，她甚至想过再从陈浩宇手中买回竹林，又怕对方趁机抬高价格，故意讹人，便暂时放弃买回竹林的想法。

Leaving, she really felt reluctant. She had even thought about buying back the bamboo forest from Chen Haoyu, but was afraid the other party would take the opportunity to raise the price and deliberately extort her, so she temporarily gave up the idea of buying it back.

---

第二天，千里寻用过早饭，不想任何人陪伴，一个人顺着竹林的小路缓缓前行。脚下铺着干枯的竹叶，被她踩得沙沙作响。

The next day, after breakfast, Qianli Xun didn't want anyone's company. She walked slowly along the path in the bamboo forest alone. Under her feet, dry bamboo leaves rustled as she stepped on them.

---

穿过小路，来到竹林深处的溪水旁，溪水清澈见底，从一块一块的顽石上流过，神态悠然自得。千里寻在旁边找块石头坐下，用心欣赏山泉美景。

Passing through the path, she came to the stream deep in the bamboo forest. The stream was clear to the bottom, flowing over stubborn rocks with a leisurely and carefree demeanor. Qianli Xun found a stone nearby and sat down, carefully appreciating the beautiful mountain spring.

---

正当她欣赏得入神，发现两名男子踩踏着溪水中的石头，从对岸走来，看那衣着并不像居住在竹林里的村民。前面的是位公子，后面跟着的应是随身小厮。

Just as she was absorbed in her appreciation, she noticed two men stepping on the stones in the stream, walking from the opposite bank. Judging by their clothes, they didn't look like villagers living in the bamboo forest. The one in front was a young master, and the one following should be his personal servant.

---

他们正是申简辰与白风，二人穿越溪流，抬头看到千里寻。

They were none other than Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng. The two crossed the stream and looked up to see Qianli Xun.

---

白风上前问道：“姑娘，请问竹林里的村庄怎么走？”

Bai Feng stepped forward and asked, "Young lady, may I ask how to get to the village in the bamboo forest?"

---

千里寻觉得纳闷，这二人真奇怪，放着大道不走，偏偏走小路，说不定居心不良。

Qianli Xun felt puzzled. These two were really strange. Instead of taking the main road, they insisted on taking the small path. They might have bad intentions.

---

“你们从哪里来？为何不走大道直接从村口进？”

"Where are you from? Why didn't you take the main road directly into the village entrance?"

---

白风回道：“哦，我们坐马车来的，碰到一大片竹林，我家公子没见识过，很好奇，又见竹林里有小路，想走走试试，可走着走着迷了路，幸好遇见姑娘。”

Bai Feng replied, "Oh, we came by carriage. We encountered a large bamboo forest. My young master had never seen one before and was very curious. Seeing there was a small path in the bamboo forest, we wanted to try walking it. But we got lost along the way. Fortunately, we met you, young lady."

---

千里寻瞅瞅站在远处的公子，长得倒是高大挺拔，五官俊朗，面前的小厮也是一脸真诚，看上去不像骗人：“你们到村子里做什么？”

Qianli Xun glanced at the young master standing in the distance. He was tall and upright, with handsome features. The servant in front of her also had a sincere expression. They didn't look like liars: "What are you doing in the village?"

---

白风回道：“找人。”

Bai Feng replied, "Looking for someone."

---

千里寻继续问：“找什么人？”

Qianli Xun continued to ask, "Looking for who?"

---

白风不知如何回答：“姑娘怎么像审讯犯人一样？”

Bai Feng didn't know how to answer: "Why is the young lady interrogating me like a criminal?"

---

千里寻不屑道：“谁知道你们是好人还是坏人。”

Qianli Xun said disdainfully: "Who knows if you're good people or bad people."

---

申简辰喊道：“白风！不用向她打听，既然她能出现在这里，说明离村子不远，前面有条路，顺着那条路走，肯定能找到村子！”

Shen Jianchen shouted: "Bai Feng! No need to ask her. Since she can appear here, it means the village isn't far. There's a road ahead. Follow that road, and you'll definitely find the village!"

---

白风回到王爷身边，小声说：“王爷，竹林里难道也住着有钱人家？那姑娘看着像有钱人家的丫鬟。”

Bai Feng returned to the prince's side and whispered: "Your Highness, do rich people also live in the bamboo forest? That young lady looks like a maid from a wealthy family."

---

申简辰瞧向那姑娘：“也许是城里的丫鬟回乡下探亲。”

Shen Jianchen looked at the young lady: "Maybe she's a maid from the city visiting relatives in the countryside."

---

“也对。”二人顺着千里寻走来的路向村子而去。

"That's true too." The two followed the path Qianli Xun had come from towards the village.

---

千里寻自语道：“倒挺聪明。”

Qianli Xun murmured to herself: "Quite smart."

---

她继续欣赏山泉，努力向山泉流来的方向望去，远处有座山，泉水应该是那座山上流下来的。

She continued to appreciate the mountain spring, trying to look in the direction the spring was flowing from. In the distance, there was a mountain. The spring water should be flowing down from that mountain.

---

申简辰与白风顺着竹林的小路一直往前走，终于看到各色竹屋。

Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng walked along the path in the bamboo forest and finally saw various colored bamboo houses.

---

白风喜道：“王爷，应该是这里。”

Bai Feng said happily: "Your Highness, this should be the place."

---

申简辰批评道：“都给你叮嘱过不要称呼王爷，又忘记。”

Shen Jianchen criticized: "I've told you not to call me 'Your Highness.' You forgot again."

---

白风不好意思：“一高兴给忘了。”

Bai Feng was embarrassed: "I forgot in my excitement."

---

“你找人打听一下送书稿的下人住哪里？”

"Ask someone where the servant who delivers manuscripts lives?"

---

“是，公子。”

"Yes, young master."

---

白风看到一位竹民扛着几根竹子经过，上前行礼：“大叔，向你打听个人。”

Bai Feng saw a bamboo villager carrying several bamboo poles passing by. He stepped forward and greeted: "Uncle, I'd like to ask about someone."

---

竹民站住：“你想打听谁？”

The bamboo villager stopped: "Who do you want to ask about?"

---

白风编道：“我曾向一人借过钱，还未还，却找不到人，只知他住在这村子，跟我年纪差不多，个头没我高，长得又黑又壮，人看上去挺老实。”

Bai Feng made up a story: "I once borrowed money from someone and haven't paid it back. But I can't find him. I only know he lives in this village. He's about my age, not as tall as me, dark and stocky, and looks quite honest."

---

竹民问道：“你来还钱？”

The bamboo villager asked: "You're here to return the money?"

---

白风笑道：“是。”

Bai Feng smiled: "Yes."

---

“现在的世道，像你这样的人不多，看在你懂得还钱的份上，我帮你想想。”竹民想了一会儿说，“你说的人很像苦蕨。”

"In today's world, not many people are like you. Considering you know to return money, I'll help you think." The bamboo villager thought for a while and said, "The person you described sounds a lot like Kujue."

---

白风兴奋道：“不知他住在哪个竹屋？”

Bai Feng said excitedly: "I wonder which bamboo house he lives in?"

---

竹民指着村里的小路：“顺着这条路，一直向里走，看到第一个最破烂的竹屋就是苦家，苦蕨住那里。”

The bamboo villager pointed to the path in the village: "Follow this road, keep going in. The first most dilapidated bamboo house you see is the Ku family. Kujue lives there."

---

白风谢道：“多谢大叔！”

Bai Feng thanked: "Thank you, uncle!"

---

申简辰与白风顺着村里曲里拐弯的路向里走，边走边瞧哪个竹屋比较破烂。

Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng walked along the winding path in the village, looking to see which bamboo house was more dilapidated as they walked.

---

苦蕨在屋里忙完，刚走到竹屋门口，一眼瞧见白风，立刻缩了回去，拉住苦荞：“姐，昨日向我打听写手稿的人竟然找来，就在外面，我躲一下，他们要是问起，就说没我这个人。”

Kujue had just finished busy work in the house. As he walked to the door of the bamboo house, he saw Bai Feng at first glance. He immediately retreated back inside, pulling Kuqiao: "Sister, the person who was asking about the manuscript writer yesterday actually found us. He's right outside. I'll hide. If they ask, just say I don't exist."

---

苦荞还没反应过来，苦蕨已溜出后门。

Before Kuqiao could react, Kujue had already slipped out the back door.

---

申简辰与白风终于瞧到第一个最破烂的竹屋，白风在外面大声问道：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”

Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng finally saw the first most dilapidated bamboo house. Bai Feng asked loudly outside: "Excuse me, does Kujue live here?"

---

没有任何回应，白风又喊一遍：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”

There was no response. Bai Feng shouted again: "Excuse me, does Kujue live here?"

---

苦荞走出来，看向两人：“你们找谁？”

Kuqiao walked out and looked at the two: "Who are you looking for?"

---

白风又重复一遍：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”

Bai Feng repeated: "Excuse me, does Kujue live here?"

---

苦荞说：“没听说过此人，你找错地方。”

Kuqiao said: "I've never heard of this person. You've got the wrong place."

---

正好有竹民从苦荞家经过，打招呼：“苦荞，你家来客人了？”

Just then, a bamboo villager passed by Kuqiao's house and greeted: "Kuqiao, do you have guests?"

---

直接将苦荞出卖，苦荞气得没理会那人，竹民不知所以，疑惑着离开。

This directly exposed Kuqiao. Kuqiao was so angry she ignored the person. The bamboo villager didn't know what was going on and left in confusion.

---

白风笑道：“苦荞？你姓苦，还说没听过苦蕨？快说，苦蕨到底跟你什么关系？”

Bai Feng smiled: "Kuqiao? Your surname is Ku, and you say you've never heard of Kujue? Tell me quickly, what's Kujue's relationship to you?"

---

苦荞问：“你们是什么人？为什么找我弟弟？”

Kuqiao asked: "Who are you? Why are you looking for my brother?"

---

申简辰解释道：“姑娘放心，我不会伤害你弟弟，只想问问他给说话艺人送的书稿是谁所写？”

Shen Jianchen explained: "Don't worry, young lady. I won't harm your brother. I just want to ask who wrote the manuscript he delivered to the storyteller."

---

小姐警告过，她写话本之事不能向外人提起，苦荞虽不懂小姐的用意，但只要是小姐说的话，她必然遵从。再说也不知道眼前这位公子为何打听此事，说不定对小姐不利。

The young lady had warned her that her writing of story scripts must not be mentioned to outsiders. Although Kuqiao didn't understand the young lady's intentions, she would definitely follow whatever the young lady said. Besides, she didn't know why this young master in front of her was asking about this. It might be disadvantageous to the young lady.

---

“你问这干什么？”

"Why are you asking this?"

---

申简辰问道：“写书稿的人可叫千里寻？”

Shen Jianchen asked: "Is the person who wrote the manuscript called Qianli Xun?"

---

眼前的公子不回答问题反而向她发问，苦荞又怎能直接回答？骗道：“这位公子，我弟弟送书稿没错，也告诉说话艺人是位叫千里寻的才子所写，只可惜你们晚来一步，他已离开，不知所踪。”

The young master in front of her didn't answer the question but instead asked her one. How could Kuqiao answer directly? She lied: "Young master, my brother did deliver the manuscript, and he did tell the storyteller it was written by a talented scholar named Qianli Xun. But unfortunately, you're one step too late. He has already left. No one knows where he went."

---

申简辰失望道：“什么？他走了？去了哪里？”

Shen Jianchen said in disappointment: "What? He left? Where did he go?"

---

苦荞强调道：“都说了，不知所踪。”

Kuqiao emphasized: "I already said, no one knows where he went."

---

“什么时候走的？”

"When did he leave?"

---

“三天前离开。”

"He left three days ago."

---

“朝哪个方向走的？”

"Which direction did he go?"

---

“你这公子，怎么这么多问题？千里寻来得突然，走得也突然，我们跟他不熟，我弟弟帮他送书稿也是收钱办事，其他一概不知。”

“Young master, why so many questions? Qianli Xun came out of nowhere and left just as suddenly. We barely know him. My brother just delivered manuscripts for pay—that's it. We don't know anything else.”

---

苦荞装得坦然，瞎话编得也算圆满，申简辰不得不信：“打扰姑娘。”然后垂头丧气地离开。

Kuqiao acted calm and made up the lies quite perfectly. Shen Jianchen had to believe her: "Sorry to disturb you, young lady." Then he left in defeat.

---

王爷要走，白风再留着没有任何意义，紧跟上王爷，边走边扭过头对苦荞“哼”了一声。

Since the prince was leaving, there was no point in Bai Feng staying. He followed closely behind the prince, and as he walked, he turned his head and snorted at Kuqiao.

---

苦荞以白眼回应，庆幸自己够聪明，很快将两个陌生人打发走。

Kuqiao responded with a roll of her eyes, feeling relieved that she was smart enough to quickly get rid of the two strangers.

---

苦蕨从后门进屋，来到前门：“姐，人走了？”

Kujue entered the house from the back door and came to the front door: "Sister, are they gone?"

---

“有你姐姐我出马，问题立刻解决。”苦荞很骄傲，叮嘱道，“爹娘近几日走亲戚不在家，我过两日也要离开，你要照顾好自己。”

"With your sister me taking action, the problem was solved immediately." Kuqiao was very proud and instructed: "Mom and Dad are visiting relatives these past few days and aren't home. I'll also be leaving in a couple of days. You need to take care of yourself."

---

苦蕨憨憨地说：“不用替我担心，我都快成亲，已是大人，会照顾好自己。”

Kujue said with a simple smile: "Don't worry about me. I'm about to get married. I'm an adult. I can take care of myself."

---

“有你这话，姐就放心。”苦荞说完，回屋去忙活，小姐在竹林吃不了几顿饭，临走前更要给她做几顿好吃的，今日她打算做最拿手的竹筒饭。

"With those words, sister can rest assured." After Kuqiao finished speaking, she went back into the house to busy herself. The young lady wouldn't be able to eat many meals in the bamboo forest. Before leaving, she needed to make her a few good meals. Today, she planned to make her specialty: bamboo tube rice.

---

申简辰带着白风向村口走去，一路上沉默不语，唯一的希望就此破灭，他又该到何处去找千里寻？

Shen Jianchen led Bai Feng towards the village entrance. Along the way, he was silent. His only hope was shattered. Where should he go to look for Qianli Xun now?

---

二人走到村口附近，正好遇见千里寻从竹林回来。

The two walked near the village entrance and happened to meet Qianli Xun returning from the bamboo forest.

---

毕竟刚刚有过一面之缘，申简辰向对方微笑点头，千里寻则礼貌回应。两人就这么完美地错过第一次相认的机会。

After all, they had just met briefly. Shen Jianchen smiled and nodded at the other person. Qianli Xun responded politely. The two perfectly missed the opportunity to recognize each other for the first time.
