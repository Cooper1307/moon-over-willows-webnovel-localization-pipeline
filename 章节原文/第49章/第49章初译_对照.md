## Chapter 49: Chapter 49: Face to Face, Yet Unrecognized

回到竹林，苦蕨跑去向老东家汇报，他现在见到老东家不再紧张。
Back in the bamboo grove, Kujue hurried straight to report to the former Proprietor—by now his old nerves around her had vanished.

千里寻刚从竹林散步回来，在竹屋门口碰到苦蕨。
Returning from a morning stroll through the bamboo, Qianli Xun met him right outside her cottage steps.

“今日回来得挺快？”
"Back early today?"

苦蕨往日送手稿，来回都是步行，时间全花在路上。今日发生意外，故而回来早些。
Usually Kujue walked both ways, eating up half the day; today's wild ride cut hours off!

“老东家，书稿已送到，奇怪的是有人问我是不是送书稿的，我承认后，竟然想把我抓走。”
"Delivered the script, former Proprietor—weird thing happened though! Some stranger asked if I delivered the pages, and when I said yes, he tried dragging me off!"

“哦？竟有这等事？”千里寻很疑惑，难道她写的书稿内容有问题？如果有问题说话艺人不接即可，为何会有人因此抓苦蕨？继续问，“那人是干什么的？长什么样？”
"Huh? Really?!" Qianli Xun frowned—issues with her script? If the storyteller hated it he'd just refuse it; why grab the courier?! "Who was he? What'd he look like?"

苦蕨回道：“干什么的我不清楚，长得眉清目秀，穿的衣裳不错，劲儿挺大。”
"No idea who—sharp, pretty face, fancy clothes, crazy strong grip!"

“他有没有问起我的名姓？”
"Did he ask my name?"

“那倒没有，即使问起，我也不会说，老东家您叮嘱过，只说是位叫千里寻的才子所写，其他一概不能说。”
"Didn't ask—and even if he had, my lips stayed sealed! Just told the storyteller a scholar named Qianli Xun wrote it, like you instructed, not a word more."

千里寻很满意：“你做得对。苦荞！”
Pleased, Qianli Xun nodded: "Good lad! Kuqiao!"

苦荞知道小姐的意思，掏出碎银，走到弟弟身边：“小姐赏你的。”
Catching her cue, Kuqiao stepped over pressing loose silver into her brother's palm: "Reward from Miss."

苦蕨推辞道：“替老东家跑腿，我乐意，不能收钱。”
"Happy to run errands free—can't take coin!" Kujue tried dodging.

苦荞硬塞到苦蕨手中：“拿着，要不小姐会不高兴。”
Shoving it firm into his fist: "Take it, or Miss gets mad!"

苦蕨只能接到手中：“老东家，再需要送书稿，您尽管说。”
Tucking the silver away grinning: "Any more scripts to run, former Proprietor, just holler!"

千里寻说：“以后你不用再送手稿，还有三日，就整整一个月，我也该回去。”
"No more runs needed—three days left till my full month is up, then I head back to town."

苦蕨小心揣着碎银，高兴离去。
Patting his pocket happy, Kujue trotted home.

一个月实在太快，千里寻在竹林还未待够，本想处理完和离之事，再来竹林住些时日。可一想竹林已是陈浩宇的田产，只能放弃再来暂住的想法。
One month flew by in a blink! Qianli Xun hated leaving already—almost planned to come back once her divorce settled, until remembering Chen Haoyu owned the grove now! Oh well, scratch that plan.

在竹林这段时日，千里寻没有感觉到竹林为她加持了什么神奇的力量，只觉远离林府，内心越来越平静，思考问题也越发理智。她打算回去后不再用武力逼迫林觉，与他像朋友一样好好聊聊，心平气和地解决和离之事。
No magical thunderbolt had struck her destiny in this bamboo grove—yet stepping away from Lin Manor had cooled her head and sharpened her thinking. Once back, she planned to ditch fists and sit down with Lin Jue like calm adults to talk through mutual divorce.

千里寻也理性地思考过她想要谈场恋爱的心理。
She had also looked squarely at her sudden longing for love.

穿越前，之所以不涉足爱情，除了有理想和事业要追求外，还因她不想涉足婚姻。尽管很多人说只谈恋爱不结婚，可千里寻认为不以结婚为目的恋爱是耍流氓，她不想成为女流氓。因而，对于摆在面前的恋爱机会只有一种态度：拒绝。
Back in her modern life, she had steered clear of romance—chasing career goals and loathing casual flings, convinced dating without marriage in mind was pure player nonsense she refused to touch, so she turned down every suitor cold.

死过一回，曾追求的理想和事业在地震那一刻被震得粉碎。借助朱淑真的身体于另是一个时代活过来，逐渐意识到人生的功名利禄如过眼烟云，对于像她这样的普通人来讲，最大的幸福莫过于与另一个人相伴到老。加之穿越而来，一场不属于她婚姻摆在面前，让从未涉足恋爱的她有种强烈的失落感。
Dying once shattered every ambition and business dream into rubble beneath that quake. Waking up in another era inside Zhu Shuzhen's body drove home how hollow fame and fortune really were—real happiness meant walking through life beside one cherished partner! Worse, waking trapped inside an unwanted marriage sharpened the ache of never having tasted real love once.

所以，千里寻想要谈一场恋爱，想要找一位能与她在陌生的时代共度余生之人。
So yes—she wanted to fall in love, to find someone worth sharing the rest of this strange new life with.

千里寻想过，要与谁来一场甜蜜的恋爱之旅？每一次申简辰的身影会浮现在她的脑海，她会问自己：“不知他有没有穿越而来？如果也穿越而来，该到哪里找他？”
Who? Every time she pictured romance, Shen Jianchen's face surfaced unbidden: Did he cross over too? Where on earth would I even find him?

她无法给出答案，也许再也遇不到申简辰。她决定将自己的爱情交给命运，若遇到心仪之人，哪怕不是申简辰，也要与那人开始一段刻骨铭心的恋情。
No answers—maybe their paths would never cross again. Leaving love to fate, she decided: if the right man appeared—Shen Jianchen or not—she'd dive in with an open heart!

她到底喜欢什么样的男子？也许是像申简辰那样，毕竟曾经追求她的异性中，申简辰是最优秀的一位。也许不是， 只有遇到后才知道。
What sort of man would win her? Probably someone like Shen Jianchen—head and shoulders above every suitor she'd ever met back home—or maybe someone totally unexpected; she'd know when she met him.

她幻想过申简辰穿越而来成为他喜欢的类型，神奇般地出现在她面前：“千里寻，嫁给我吧。”她一定会答应，像梦中梦到的那样。
Sometimes she daydreamed Shen Jianchen crossing into a dashing Song gentleman, popping out of nowhere proposing: "Qianli Xun, marry me!"—and just like her dream, she'd say yes in a heartbeat!

千里寻回到竹屋，吩咐苦荞和夏果把能收拾的东西先整理好，甚至让她们把书桌也收了，剩下的两三日，她不想再动笔墨，打算除吃饭睡觉外，其他时间去竹林转转。
Stepping inside, she told Kuqiao and Xiaguo to start packing up—even clearing the study desk; done writing for the final two or three days, she planned to soak in pure bamboo serenity till departure.

要离开，真有些舍不得，她甚至想过再从陈浩宇手中买回竹林，又怕对方趁机抬高价格，故意讹人，便暂时放弃买回竹林的想法。
Part of her hated parting with this grove so much she almost considered buying it back from Chen Haoyu—only to drop the thought fast, knowing that petty jerk would jack the price to the sky!

第二天，千里寻用过早饭，不想任何人陪伴，一个人顺着竹林的小路缓缓前行。脚下铺着干枯的竹叶，被她踩得沙沙作响。
Next morning after breakfast, craving solo quiet, Qianli Xun wandered down a narrow bamboo trail alone, crisp dry bamboo leaves rustling underfoot.

穿过小路，来到竹林深处的溪水旁，溪水清澈见底，从一块一块的顽石上流过，神态悠然自得。千里寻在旁边找块石头坐下，用心欣赏山泉美景。
Deep inside the grove she emerged beside a crystal mountain stream rippling carefree over smooth boulders. Settling onto a flat rock, she drank in the tranquil scene.

正当她欣赏得入神，发现两名男子踩踏着溪水中的石头，从对岸走来，看那衣着并不像居住在竹林里的村民。前面的是位公子，后面跟着的应是随身小厮。
Mid-reverie, two men stepped across stepping-stones from the opposite bank—finely dressed, clearly outsiders, not villagers: a tall gentleman in front, a sharp-looking attendant behind.

﻿他们正是申简辰与白风，二人穿越溪流，抬头看到千里寻。
Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng—crossing the stream and looking up straight at Qianli Xun!

白风上前问道：“姑娘，请问竹林里的村庄怎么走？”
Stepping forward polite, Bai Feng asked: "Miss, which way to the bamboo village?"

千里寻觉得纳闷，这二人真奇怪，放着大道不走，偏偏走小路，说不定居心不良。
Suspicion flickered in Qianli Xun—ignoring the main road to sneak through back paths? Up to no good?

“你们从哪里来？为何不走大道直接从村口进？”
"Where'd you two come from? Why skip the main road into the village?"

白风回道：“哦，我们坐马车来的，碰到一大片竹林，我家公子没见识过，很好奇，又见竹林里有小路，想走走试试，可走着走着迷了路，幸好遇见姑娘。”
"Rode out by carriage," Bai Feng explained smooth. "Spotted this huge bamboo forest—my young master had never seen one, got curious walking a side trail, and got turned around! Lucky we bumped into you!"

千里寻瞅瞅站在远处的公子，长得倒是高大挺拔，五官俊朗，面前的小厮也是一脸真诚，看上去不像骗人：“你们到村子里做什么？”
Sizing up the tall, upright young gentleman standing back—handsome features, earnest attendant, zero shady vibe—Qianli Xun asked: "What brings you to the village?"

白风回道：“找人。”
"Looking for someone," Bai Feng replied.

千里寻继续问：“找什么人？”
"Who?"

白风不知如何回答：“姑娘怎么像审讯犯人一样？”
Stuck—Bai Feng frowned: "Talking like a magistrate interrogating suspects, miss!"

千里寻不屑道：“谁知道你们是好人还是坏人。”
Qianli Xun sniffed: "How do I know if you're honest folks or crooks?"

申简辰喊道：“白风！不用向她打听，既然她能出现在这里，说明离村子不远，前面有条路，顺着那条路走，肯定能找到村子！”
Calling from behind, Shen Jianchen cut in: "Drop it, Bai Feng! If she's sitting here, the village is right nearby—there's a clear path right ahead, follow it and we hit the village!"

白风回到王爷身边，小声说：“王爷，竹林里难道也住着有钱人家？那姑娘看着像有钱人家的丫鬟。”
Jogging back beside his master, Bai Feng whispered: "Think rich folks live in this forest too, Your Highness? Looks like a fancy household's maid!"

申简辰瞧向那姑娘：“也许是城里的丫鬟回乡下探亲。”
Glancing back at her plain cotton clothes, Shen Jianchen guessed: "Probably a city maid home visiting family."

“也对。”二人顺着千里寻走来的路向村子而去。
"Makes sense!" Side by side, they turned straight onto the trail Qianli Xun had just walked.

千里寻自语道：“倒挺聪明。”
"Pretty sharp," Qianli Xun murmured after them.

她继续欣赏山泉，努力向山泉流来的方向望去，远处有座山，泉水应该是那座山上流下来的。
Returning her gaze to the clear stream, she traced its sparkling path back toward the distant green hills feeding it.

申简辰与白风顺着竹林的小路一直往前走，终于看到各色竹屋。
Following the woodland track straight ahead, Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng soon emerged among clustered bamboo houses.

白风喜道：“王爷，应该是这里。”
"Found it, Your Highness!" Bai Feng cheered.

申简辰批评道：“都给你叮嘱过不要称呼王爷，又忘记。”
"Told you to stop calling me Your Highness—forgot again!" Shen Jianchen chided.

白风不好意思：“一高兴给忘了。”
"Oops—got excited!" Bai Feng grinned sheepish.

“你找人打听一下送书稿的下人住哪里？”
"Ask around where that script courier lives."

“是，公子。”
"On it, Young Master!"

白风看到一位竹民扛着几根竹子经过，上前行礼：“大叔，向你打听个人。”
Spotting a villager hauling cut bamboo poles, Bai Feng stepped up bowing: "Uncle, hoping to ask after someone."

竹民站住：“你想打听谁？”
Halting, the villager asked: "Who?"

白风编道：“我曾向一人借过钱，还未还，却找不到人，只知他住在这村子，跟我年纪差不多，个头没我高，长得又黑又壮，人看上去挺老实。”
Spinning smooth on the spot, Bai Feng said: "Borrowed money from a guy and came to pay him back—lost his address! Know he lives here: about my age, shorter, tanned, stocky, honest look."

竹民问道：“你来还钱？”
"Came all this way to repay a debt?"

白风笑道：“是。”
"Yep!" Bai Feng grinned.

“现在的世道，像你这样的人不多，看在你懂得还钱的份上，我帮你想想。”竹民想了一会儿说，“你说的人很像苦蕨。”
"Rare to see honest folks these days—for that, let me think!" Pausing a beat: "Sounds dead-on like Kujue!"

白风兴奋道：“不知他住在哪个竹屋？”
"Which bamboo house is his?!" Bai Feng lit up.

竹民指着村里的小路：“顺着这条路，一直向里走，看到第一个最破烂的竹屋就是苦家，苦蕨住那里。”
Pointing down the lane: "Straight down this path—very first super rundown bamboo shack is the Ku house! Kujue lives there!"

白风谢道：“多谢大叔！”
"Thanks a million, Uncle!"

申简辰与白风顺着村里曲里拐弯的路向里走，边走边瞧哪个竹屋比较破烂。
Winding down the narrow lanes, Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng scanned left and right for the shabbiest bamboo shack.

苦蕨在屋里忙完，刚走到竹屋门口，一眼瞧见白风，立刻缩了回去，拉住苦荞：“姐，昨日向我打听写手稿的人竟然找来，就在外面，我躲一下，他们要是问起，就说没我这个人。”
Inside, finishing chores and stepping to his doorway, Kujue spotted Bai Feng instantly—and jerked straight back inside grabbing Kuqiao: "Sis! That kidnapper hunting the writer tracked me right here! I'm hiding—if they ask, swear you never heard of me!"

苦荞还没反应过来，苦蕨已溜出后门。
Before Kuqiao even blinked, Kujue bolted straight out the back door!

申简辰与白风终于瞧到第一个最破烂的竹屋，白风在外面大声问道：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”
Reaching the first weathered bamboo shack, Bai Feng hollered outside: "Excuse me—does Kujue live here?!"

没有任何回应，白风又喊一遍：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”
Dead silence. He called again: "Does Kujue live here?!"

苦荞走出来，看向两人：“你们找谁？”
Kuqiao stepped out onto the stoop eyeing both strangers: "Looking for who?"

白风又重复一遍：“请问苦蕨是不是住这里？”
"Does Kujue live here?" Bai Feng repeated.

苦荞说：“没听说过此人，你找错地方。”
"Never heard of him—wrong house!" Kuqiao lied flat.

正好有竹民从苦荞家经过，打招呼：“苦荞，你家来客人了？”
Right on cue, a neighbor strolled past waving cheerful: "Hey Kuqiao—got visitors?!"

直接将苦荞出卖，苦荞气得没理会那人，竹民不知所以，疑惑着离开。
Sold out on the spot! Shooting the clueless neighbor a death glare as he wandered off baffled, Kuqiao braced herself.

白风笑道：“苦荞？你姓苦，还说没听过苦蕨？快说，苦蕨到底跟你什么关系？”
Bai Feng smirked wide: "Kuqiao?! Same surname 'Ku' and never heard of Kujue?! Spill it—what's Kujue to you?!"

苦荞问：“你们是什么人？为什么找我弟弟？”
"Who ARE you two, hunting my little brother?!" Kuqiao bristled.

申简辰解释道：“姑娘放心，我不会伤害你弟弟，只想问问他给说话艺人送的书稿是谁所写？”
Stepping forward gentle, Shen Jianchen reassured her: "Don't panic, miss—mean your brother zero harm! Just want to know who wrote the huaben scripts he drops off for the storyteller."

小姐警告过，她写话本之事不能向外人提起，苦荞虽不懂小姐的用意，但只要是小姐说的话，她必然遵从。再说也不知道眼前这位公子为何打听此事，说不定对小姐不利。
Remembering Miss's strict warning never to expose her pen name—and unsure if these strangers meant trouble—Kuqiao played stone wall:

“你问这干什么？”
"Why do you care?"

申简辰问道：“写书稿的人可叫千里寻？”
"Is the author's name Qianli Xun?" Shen Jianchen pressed eager.

眼前的公子不回答问题反而向她发问，苦荞又怎能直接回答？骗道：“这位公子，我弟弟送书稿没错，也告诉说话艺人是位叫千里寻的才子所写，只可惜你们晚来一步，他已离开，不知所踪。”
Dodging straight answers, Kuqiao improvised smooth: "Sure, my brother ran scripts and gave the name Scholar Qianli Xun—too bad you're a step late! He already packed up and left, no idea where!"

申简辰失望道：“什么？他走了？去了哪里？”
Every spark crashed out of Shen Jianchen's eyes: "What?! Left?! Where did he go?!"

苦荞强调道：“都说了，不知所踪。”
"Told you—no idea where!"

“什么时候走的？”
"When did he leave?!"

“三天前离开。”
"Three days ago!"

“朝哪个方向走的？”
"Which direction?!"

“你这公子，怎么这么多问题？千里寻来得突然，走得也突然，我们跟他不熟，我弟弟帮他送书稿也是收钱办事，其他一概不知。”
"So many questions, young master! Qianli Xun drifted in out of nowhere and drifted out just as fast! Barely knew him—my brother just took coin to run errands, know nothing else!"

苦荞装得坦然，瞎话编得也算圆满，申简辰不得不信：“打扰姑娘。”然后垂头丧气地离开。
Calm face, airtight story—deflated to the core, Shen Jianchen bowed heavy: "Forgive the intrusion, miss." Turning, he walked off shoulders slumped.

王爷要走，白风再留着没有任何意义，紧跟上王爷，边走边扭过头对苦荞“哼”了一声。
No point lingering; shooting Kuqiao a parting "Hmph!" over his shoulder, Bai Feng hurried after his master.

苦荞以白眼回应，庆幸自己够聪明，很快将两个陌生人打发走。
Rolling her eyes right back, Kuqiao beamed proud of herself for brushing off two strangers clean!

苦蕨从后门进屋，来到前门：“姐，人走了？”
Slipping back in through the back door, Kujue peeked out front: "Gone, Sis?"

“有你姐姐我出马，问题立刻解决。”苦荞很骄傲，叮嘱道，“爹娘近几日走亲戚不在家，我过两日也要离开，你要照顾好自己。”
"Your big sister handled it in two seconds!" puffing up proud before reminding him: "Parents are away visiting kin, and I leave in two days too—take good care of yourself!"

苦蕨憨憨地说：“不用替我担心，我都快成亲，已是大人，会照顾好自己。”
Grinning sheepish: "Almost a married man—I can handle myself!"

“有你这话，姐就放心。”苦荞说完，回屋去忙活，小姐在竹林吃不了几顿饭，临走前更要给她做几顿好吃的，今日她打算做最拿手的竹筒饭。
"Good to hear!" Heading back to the kitchen, Kuqiao set to cooking her signature bamboo tube rice—only a few country meals left for Miss!

申简辰带着白风向村口走去，一路上沉默不语，唯一的希望就此破灭，他又该到何处去找千里寻？
Walking glum toward the village exit beside silent Bai Feng, Shen Jianchen felt his sole lead crumble to ash—where on earth to hunt Qianli Xun now?

二人走到村口附近，正好遇见千里寻从竹林回来。
Right near the village edge, they crossed paths once more with Qianli Xun strolling back from the bamboo stream.

毕竟刚刚有过一面之缘，申简辰向对方微笑点头，千里寻则礼貌回应。两人就这么完美地错过第一次相认的机会。
Recognizing each other from minutes earlier, Shen Jianchen offered a polite nod and faint smile; Qianli Xun smiled politely back—walking clean past each other, missing their very first chance at reunion by inches!
