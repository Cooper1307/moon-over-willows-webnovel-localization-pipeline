第41章 幽林别居遇神柳
## Chapter 41: Youlin Retreat and the Divine Willow

第二天，申简辰离开乔府，往乔家城外的别居而去，他与乔文仙同乘一辆马车。白风负责赶王爷乘坐的马车，南双则为佳和郡主服务。
从临安城到别居还有一段路，闲来无聊，乔文仙与申简辰聊起天儿来。
“仪王，我发现你对佳和郡主没有以前热情，到底是怎么回事？不会真的因为失忆不记得与佳和郡主的感情？”
申简辰点点头。
“佳和郡主真可怜，你病着时，她把你照顾得无微不至，我在一旁看了都歆羡不已，仪王可不能因不记得而辜负郡主。”
申简辰不知该说什么，他心里念着千里寻已替赵朴辜负了乔梦容。能有什么办法？谁让他喜欢千里寻。申简辰已打定主意，如若找不到千里寻，便替赵朴娶了他的妹妹，没有千里寻，娶谁都一样；如若找到，肯定要跟千里寻在一起，如何解决乔梦容，他还没有头绪。
仪王沉默不语，乔文仙不便再继续这个话题。
申简辰突然问：“别光说我，幽栖居士已成婚，你还喜欢人家，还想约人家，有没有觉得自己很不道德？”
乔文仙急了：“我喜欢她在先，她成婚在后，我约她出来，只是想帮她，我与她从来没有逾越之举，你别想用道德二字绑架我。”
申简辰又问：“你既然喜欢人家，为何不早些提亲？你若娶了她，岂不是没后来的破事？”
乔文仙叹口气：“唉！我喜欢她时，她心里装着别人，我哪敢鼓动家人提亲？结果被可恶的林练兵官抢先，知府大人竟然还同意这门亲事，当时我肠子都快悔青，眼睁睁看着她嫁给别人，伤心之余去了汴梁。”
“知府大人？她爹难道是临安知府朱晞颜？”
“正是。”
“她爹是知府，怎么都不管管女儿被打之事？”
“也许她并未告诉知府大人。”
“你人在汴梁，又怎么知道她被丈夫毒打之事？”
“我虽身在汴梁，依然关心她，给东方尘泽写信，让他把幽栖居士婚后的状况通过书信告诉我。”
Next morning Shen Jianchen left Qiao Manor for the Qiao family's suburban retreat, sharing a carriage with Qiao Wenxian; Bai Feng drove Prince Yi's carriage while Nan Shuang drove Princess Jiahe's. With a stretch of country road ahead, Qiao Wenxian struck up conversation: "Notice you aren't near as warm toward Princess Jiahe as before, Prince Yi. What gives? Amnesia really wiped out all your love for her?"

“原来如此，不过，你也挺可怜，一直是单相思。”
Shen Jianchen nodded.

“是啊，她虽知道我的心思，因心里装着别人，无法接受我。”
"Poor Princess Jiahe! While you lay sick, she waited on you hand and foot—even watching from the side made me envious! Don't let amnesia make you break her heart."

“你有没有想过，她若真和离，也不一定跟你，毕竟人家心里装着别人。”
Words caught in Shen Jianchen's throat; pining for Qianli Xun had already betrayed Zhao Pu's love for Mengrong! What choice did he have when his heart belonged to Qianli Xun? Quiet inside himself, he had drawn a line: if he never found Qianli Xun, fine, he'd honor Zhao Pu's wish and marry Mengrong—without Qianli Xun, marrying anyone was the same; yet if he did find Qianli Xun, he was choosing her, though how to untangle things with Mengrong left him stumped.

乔文仙轻松道：“她心里那人已搬家，娶了别人，连孩子都有，他们不可能在一起。我已想好，如若她与丈夫和离，我立刻向父母表明，让他们到朱家提亲。”
Seeing Prince Yi fall silent, Qiao Wenxian dropped the subject. Flipping the table, Shen Jianchen asked, "Quit lecturing me! Youqi Jushi is a married woman and you're still chasing her and sending secret invites. Ever think that's kinda sketchy?"

“她已嫁过人，难道你不嫌弃？”
Qiao Wenxian fired up at once. "I loved her first, she married after! Inviting her out is strictly to help her; never once crossed a single proper line! Don't throw morality lectures at me!"

“真正喜欢一个人怎么会嫌弃？”
"If you loved her first, why didn't you propose earlier? Marry her yourself and none of this mess happens!"

申简辰被文渊的真情感动，他暗暗决定，文渊走向爱情的道路上遇到任何困难，他都会尽全力帮他。
Qiao Wenxian let out a bitter sigh. "Back when I loved her, her heart belonged to someone else—how could I push my parents to propose?! Then that bastard Training Officer Lin jumped ahead, and Prefect Zhu actually agreed! Nearly tore my guts out watching her wed another man; fled straight to Bianliang heartbroken!"

马车已来到密林之外，仔细推算时间，确实离临安城不远，公开节度使的身份后，从别居去府衙也花不了多长时间。
"Prefect Zhu? Her father is Prefect of Lin'an Zhu Xiyan?!"

别居建在密林中，远远望去，只见密林丛生，很难发现有座别居建在里面。近看，密林中有一条修葺的土路，刚好容一辆马车经过。听乔文仙说，是乔家专门为别居而修。
"The very one!"

到达别居门口，大家走下马车，抬头看到匾额上写着“幽林别居”四个字，倒是应景。
"Her dad's the Prefect—why didn't he stop her husband from beating her?!"

门被打开，出来一名管家急急忙忙忙迎上来：“仪王、郡主、乔公子，昨儿老爷派人过来嘱咐，说从今日起仪王与郡主要住在这里，我已派下人连夜里里外外收拾一番，仪王和郡主若有什么不满意的尽管说，我再吩咐下人完善。”
"Maybe she never told him."

乔文仙说：“辛苦李管家，先带仪王和郡主去住处安顿。”
"Wait—up in Bianliang, how did you know her husband beat her?"

“仪王、郡主、公子，这边请！”李管家在前面带路。
"Far away didn't mean I stopped caring. I wrote Dongfang Chenze asking him to keep me posted on how she fared after marriage."

乔文仙第一次来自家别居，也充满好奇。别居占地面积不是很大，只有乔府的一半儿，妙在将密林的一部分圈在围墙之内。一进门，看到三排林木形成的照壁，照壁两边是用石砖修葺的小路，路两旁则保留着丛林中的树木。
"Got it. Rough deal though—one-sided love all along."

绕过“照壁”，依然是林木，时不时传来各类鸟鸣之声。穿过林木，眼前才出现房舍，明显是砍伐了树木之后，用开辟出来的空间建造而成，房屋与房屋之间也由树木间隔起来。
"Yep. She knew my feelings, yet with another man in her heart she couldn't accept me."

李管家说：“这是主屋，仪王住这里，主屋后面有闺房，郡主可居住，都已打扫干净。我这就带郡主过去。”
"Ever think even if she divorces Lin Jue, she still might not pick you if she loves that other guy?"

乔文仙被自家别居的景色吸引，临时决定也留在这里：“不行，我也要住在别居，李管家给我安排安排。”
Qiao Wenxian brightened. "That guy moved away years ago, married, already has kids—zero chance for them! My mind's set: the day she divorces, I tell my parents straight and send them proposing to Zhu Manor!"

李管家笑道：“屋子尽有，除了主屋和后面的闺房，公子随便挑，每个屋子下人都收拾了一遍。”
"Already been married once—doesn't bother you?"

乔文仙立刻吩咐：“南双，回趟乔府，把我平日用的东西统统搬来，此处虽不及仙境，也颇具意趣之美，你家公子我决定陪仪王住在这里。”
"Truly love someone, why would that ever matter?"

“是，公子。”南双拉上白风，“你陪我一起去吧？”
Moved by Wenyuan's fierce devotion, Shen Jianchen swore in silence: whatever roadblocks stood between Wenyuan and love, he'd clear every last one!

白风很不乐意：“不行，我还要照顾我家王爷。”
Soon the carriages reached the edge of dense woods—close enough to Lin'an that once he took office, commuting to the prefecture yamen would be a breeze. Tucked deep inside thick forest, the retreat stayed invisible from afar; only up close did a tidy dirt lane cut through the trees wide enough for a single carriage, graded special by the Qiao family. Pulling up before the entrance, everyone stepped down beneath a plaque reading "Youlin Retreat"—fitting to a T.

申简辰说：“你去给南双帮把手，我这里无妨。”
The gate swung open as a steward hurried out bowing: "Prince Yi, Princess, Young Master! Master sent word yesterday you'd be moving in today, so I had servants scrub every hall inside and out overnight! Anything lacking, just say the word and I'll fix it at once!"

白风这才答应：“既然王爷都发话，我就陪你再回乔府一趟。”
"Good work, Butler Li. Show Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe to their quarters first," Qiao Wenxian directed.

南双与白风走后，李管家领着乔梦容向主屋后面绕去，也是绿树丛生，踩着铺好的石板路，在绿树环绕中出现一处闺房小院，真是情趣盎然，乔梦容很喜欢。
"Right this way, Your Highness, Princess, Young Master!" Butler Li led them in.

李管家说：“郡主住这里，丫鬟都给您备齐，需要什么尽管让您的贴身丫鬟告诉我，我就不在此打扰，还要到前边去看看我家公子选中哪个院子，好给他安排一番。”
First visit here himself, Qiao Wenxian looked around delighted. Half the size of Qiao Manor, its genius lay in folding wild woods straight inside the estate walls: right past the gate stood three rows of live trees forming a living screen wall, stone paths curving around both sides amid untouched woodland. Beyond the green screen stood more forest echoing with birdsong before graceful pavilions appeared in small clearings, each courtyard cushioned from the next by living trees.

乔梦容也想在没有外人时，让喜兰把屋子按照自己的喜好再收拾收拾：“李管家去忙吧，不用管我。”
"Main hall right here for Prince Yi," Butler Li announced. "Behind it sits a private ladies' courtyard for Princess Jiahe, both spotless! Let me show the Princess back."

乔文仙在主屋的东面挑一处院子，屋子虽比主屋小，却十分惬意，关键是院子外搭有一座木质高台。
Enchanted by the woodland haven, Qiao Wenxian changed plans on the spot. "Wait—I'm staying at the retreat too! Set up a courtyard for me, Butler Li!"

仔细观察，是用五棵粗壮高大的树木建造而成。周围四棵，中间一棵，树木的顶端被修葺后，搭了木架，周围还修有栏杆，五棵树木的其余枝叶则从一旁伸展开来。
Butler Li chuckled. "Empty courtyards galore! Aside from the main hall and rear ladies' wing, pick any you like; every room's cleaned!"

乔文仙不由赞道：“没想到我爹平日思想古板，新建的宅子倒是意趣横生。仪王，我们一起爬上高台欣赏风景，如何？”
"Nan Shuang!" Qiao Wenxian called at once. "Run back to Qiao Manor and haul my daily gear over. May not be fairyland, yet this place oozes charm! I'm keeping Prince Yi company right here!"

申简辰自然乐意，他们顺着木质台阶一前一后爬上去。
"Yes, Master!" Nan Shuang tugged Bai Feng's sleeve. "Come help me haul!"

顶端的木架十分结实，踩上去没有嘎吱嘎的声音。上面摆着一个圆形的木桌，两把椅子，没事时上来品品茶，再极目远眺，看看密林的风景，绝对十分惬意。
Bai Feng balked. "No way—I have to wait on His Highness!"

乔文仙惊叹道：“真是太美了！我们竟然站在密林高处，将整个密林尽收眼底！”
"Go lend Nan Shuang a hand. I'm fine here," Shen Jianchen waved him off.

李管家走来，朝高处喊道：“公子！您可把院子挑好？”
"Since His Highness says so, fine—let's go back to Qiao Manor!"

乔文仙向下大声说：“已挑好！就是这里！”
Once the two lads left, Butler Li guided Qiao Mengrong along stone flagstones curving behind the main hall into a secluded courtyard ringed with emerald foliage; she fell in love with it on sight. "All maids assigned and ready, Princess. Need anything, send your personal maid for me. Excuse me while I see which courtyard Young Master picks."

李管家又大声说：“公子与仪王可满意？”
Eager to arrange the rooms to her taste with Xilan, Qiao Mengrong smiled. "Go tend your work, Butler Li. I'm all set."

申简辰未搭话，乔文仙大声回道：“满意满意！你去忙吧！不用管我们！”
East of the main hall, Qiao Wenxian picked a cozy smaller courtyard, won over by a raised wooden platform built right outside its wall. Engineered across five towering, stout tree trunks—four at the corners, one central—its pruned crowns supported sturdy timber decking ringed with railings while lush outer boughs spread leafy shade all around.

主人家不再有需要，李管家笑着离开，边走边自语：“公子第一次来，新鲜劲儿十足，我啊，天天在别居，都不觉得有多美，冷冷清清的，没城里热闹。”
"Never knew my stiff old man had such flair!" Qiao Wenxian grinned. "Come climb the tree platform with me for the view, Prince Yi!"

申简辰靠近栏杆，视线穿越密林，朝不远处望去，看到一座山：“那里有座山，叫什么名字？”
Game at once, Shen Jianchen climbed the timber stairs right behind him. Rock-solid decking—not even a creak underfoot—held a round wooden table and two chairs, ideal for sipping tea over endless green canopy.

乔文仙朝大山瞧去：“没名字，不过山上的尼姑庵倒是挺出名，只因里面有位住持，能知过去未来。”
"Breathtaking!" Qiao Wenxian breathed. "Floating right on top of the forest looking out over every tree!"

申简辰不大相信：“真有这样的人？”
Strolling up below, Butler Li called upward: "Picked your courtyard, Young Master?!"

乔文仙随口说道：“大家都这么传，我也是人云亦云。”
"Right here!" Qiao Wenxian shouted back down.

申简辰继续面向大山，不知不觉被山下的一片红色吸引：“那又是什么？”
"Do you and Prince Yi like it?!"

乔文仙向仪王指的方向望去：“哦，是一棵千年神柳，临安城许多青年男女到尼姑庵求姻缘后，喜欢把红绸系在神柳的枝条上，以求护佑，据传言这棵神柳是月老的一根胡须幻化而成。”
Shen Jianchen stayed quiet while Qiao Wenxian boomed, "Love it! Go on about your work, don't mind us!" Chuckling as he walked off, Butler Li muttered to himself, "First visit, all starry-eyed—living out here every day, I'm sick of how dead quiet it is compared to town!"

“千年神柳！”申简辰重复着四个字，他显得有些激动，“文渊，你能带我过去看看吗？”
Leaning against the railing, Shen Jianchen scanned past the treetops toward a green mountain rising nearby. "What mountain is that?"

“有何不可？离得又不远，你我步行即可到达。”
Glancing over, Qiao Wenxian shrugged. "Nameless hill, though the nunnery halfway up is famous because its abbess supposedly sees past and future."

二人下了木质高台，出了幽林别居。乔文仙在前面带路，沿着密林中的天然小径，穿梭在林间。
"People actually buy that?"

当穿越过密林，视野变得开阔时，一片池塘出现在眼前。
"Word on the street—just repeating what everyone says."

申简辰的双眼变得湿润起来，眼前的池塘与他穿越之前见到的竟然十分相似，不由产生一种回到穿越之前的感觉，要不是余光瞧见文渊，他绝对会这么认为。
Still gazing toward the hill, a blazing splash of crimson at its base snagged Shen Jianchen's eye. "What's that red down there?"

沿着池塘的岸边，一步一步走向对岸的神柳，越是走近，申简辰内心越激动，就是这棵神柳在地震中倾倒，压在他与千里寻的身上，然后带着他穿越而来，没想到这个时代也有它的身影。
Following his finger, Qiao Wenxian smiled. "Oh, a Millennial Divine Willow! Young couples in Lin'an hike down from the nunnery after praying for love and tie red silk ribbons onto its branches. Legend says the tree grew from a single whisker of the Old Man Under the Moon!"

乔文仙对仪王看到神柳时的表情感到疑惑，又不便多问，默默跟着他朝对岸走去。
"Millennial Divine Willow!" Heart hammering, Shen Jianchen gripped the rail. "Wenyuan, take me over there right now!"

到达神柳跟前，竟然发现一滴泪水从仪王的眼中滚落，掉在脚下的草丛中，正好被一片叶子接住，晶莹剔透，像极了清晨的一滴露珠。他不由在心里自问：“仪王第一次见神柳，为何会如此激动？”
"Sure—just a short walk through the woods!"

仪王走过去，抱住神柳粗壮的树干，更令乔文仙不解。
Descending the platform and stepping out of Youlin Retreat, they followed a natural forest trail winding beneath thick canopy. Stepping out of the trees into bright open sky, a tranquil pond spread right before them. Heat stung Shen Jianchen's eyes: this pond mirrored the exact water from his old life so close he half thought he had jumped home, until Wenyuan's silhouette beside him anchored reality.

申简辰抱着神柳默默在心中念叨：“你就是把我送到这个时代的神柳，告诉我，千里寻到底在哪里？”
Walking step by slow step around the pond toward the ancient willow on the far shore, his pulse thundered harder every pace. This very tree had crashed over Qianli Xun and him during the quake and hurled him across a millennium! Puzzled by Prince Yi's raw emotion yet too tactful to pry, Qiao Wenxian trailed silent behind.

乔文仙忍不住问：“仪王，你怎么了？”
Reaching the trunk, a single glistening tear spilled off Prince Yi's cheek onto a grass blade below like morning dew, making Qiao Wenxian wonder: First time seeing this willow, why is he moved to tears? Then Prince Yi stepped right up and wrapped both arms around the thick bark, bewildering him further.

听到文渊发问，申简辰意识到自己见到神柳时太过激动，很是失态。他放开手，忍住即将掉下来的泪水，转过身：“没什么，我在梦中多次见到跟它一模一样的柳树，倍感亲切。”
Pressing his forehead against the trunk, Shen Jianchen pleaded in silence: You brought me to this century, Divine Willow—tell me where Qianli Xun is!

“原来如此。”乔文仙望着一树的红绸随风飘动，接着瞧见高处的枝条上系着一根红绸，鲜艳而又孤独，“谁这么厉害，竟然把红绸系那么高？这得搬多高的梯子才能上去？”
Unable to hold back, Qiao Wenxian asked, "Prince Yi, are you alright?"

申简远离神柳几步，也向上望去：“系红绸之人应该会轻功，飞身而上，再以轻功踩着柳树的枝叶挂上去的。”
Startled back to himself, embarrassed at breaking down, Shen Jianchen let go, blinked back stinging moisture, and turned with a quick cover. "Fine. Just saw a willow identical to this in dreams over and over; felt uncanny up close."

乔文仙叹道：“竟然有这么厉害的人！”
"Aha!" Gazing up at hundreds of red silk ribbons fluttering in the breeze, Qiao Wenxian spotted one lone ribbon tied blazing high in the top boughs. "Whoa—who on earth tied a ribbon way up there?! How tall a ladder did they haul?!"

申简辰很不屑：“不算什么，我也会。”
Stepping back to squint upward, Shen Jianchen replied, "No ladder. Whoever tied it knows light-foot skill—vaulted straight up and balanced on the branches."

乔文仙要求道：“要不仪王表演一下？让我见识见识。”
"Someone that skilled?!" Qiao Wenxian marveled.

申简辰启动轻功，“蹭”地跳上神柳，踩在柳枝上，如履平地。
Shen Jianchen scoffed playful. "No big deal—I can do that too."

“怎么样？厉害吧？”
"Prove it! Show me!"

乔文仙既佩服又疑惑：“也是神人教你的？”
Kicking off with light-foot skill, Shen Jianchen shot up into the high canopy, standing light as a feather on a slender swaying limb. "Well? Impressed?"

“当然。”申简辰露出得意的表情。
Staring up in awe, Qiao Wenxian called, "Divine sage teach you that too?!"

乔文仙再次要求道：“既然仪王已上去，不如看看红绸上写些什么。”
"Course!" Shen Jianchen grinned smug.

申简辰瞧一眼红绸：“还是算了，别人的隐私，跟你我又不相干。”他一跃而下，站在乔文仙面前。
"Since you're right next to it, peek at what's written on that red ribbon!"

“真没趣，白白跳上去一回。”乔文仙抬头瞅着挂在高处的红绸，很是失望。
Glancing sideways at the fluttering silk, Shen Jianchen shook his head. "Pass—somebody else's private wish, none of our business!" Dropping smooth back to earth, he landed neat before Qiao Wenxian.

申简辰拍拍他的肩膀：“行了，走吧，明日带我逛遍整个临安城。”
"Boring! Jumped all the way up for nothing!" Qiao Wenxian groaned, craning his neck at the high silk in letdown.

两人又沿原路返回，申简辰边走边想，往后定要时常来神柳处坐坐。
Clapping his shoulder, Shen Jianchen laughed. "Come on—tomorrow you're showing me every corner of Lin'an!"

靠近密林处，他回头望一眼神柳，再次想道：“神柳，你既然在这个时代，千里寻肯定也在，我相信一定能找到她。”
Heading back into the woods, Shen Jianchen vowed to visit this willow often. At the forest edge he threw one last glance back across the pond: If you're here in this era, Divine Willow, Qianli Xun has to be here too—and I will find her!
