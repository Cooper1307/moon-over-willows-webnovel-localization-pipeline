# 第41章 幽林别居遇神柳

第二天，申简辰离开乔府，往乔家城外的别居而去，他与乔文仙同乘一辆马车。白风负责赶王爷乘坐的马车，南双则为佳和郡主服务。

The next day, Shen Jianchen left the Qiao Manor and headed for the Qiao family's country residence. He shared a carriage with Qiao Wenxian. Baifeng was in charge of driving the prince's carriage, while Nanshuang attended to Princess Jiahé.

---

从临安城到别居还有一段路，闲来无聊，乔文仙与申简辰聊起天儿来。

It was still a ways from Lin'an to the country residence. With nothing to do to pass the time, Qiao Wenxian struck up a conversation with Shen Jianchen.

---

"仪王，我发现你对佳和郡主没有以前热情，到底是怎么回事？不会真的因为失忆不记得与佳和郡主的感情？"

"Prince Yi, I've noticed you're not as warm toward Princess Jiahé as you used to be. What's really going on? Don't tell me you've truly forgotten your feelings for her because of the amnesia?"

---

申简辰点点头。

Shen Jianchen nodded.

---

"佳和郡主真可怜，你病着时，她把你照顾得无微不至，我在一旁看了都歆羡不已，仪王可不能因不记得而辜负郡主。"

"Poor Princess Jiahé. When you were ill, she took care of you with such meticulous devotion that even I, watching from the side, was filled with envy. You mustn't let her down just because you don't remember, Prince Yi."

---

申简辰不知该说什么，他心里念着千里寻已替赵朴辜负了乔梦容。能有什么办法？谁让他喜欢千里寻。申简辰已打定主意，如若找不到千里寻，便替赵朴娶了他的妹妹，没有千里寻，娶谁都一样；如若找到，肯定要跟千里寻在一起，如何解决乔梦容，他还没有头绪。

Shen Jianchen didn't know what to say. His heart was already on Qianli Xun—he'd already failed Qiao Mengrong on Zhao Pu's behalf. What could he do? He loved Qianli Xun. Shen Jianchen had made up his mind: if he couldn't find Qianli Xun, he'd marry Zhao Pu's sister for him. Without Qianli Xun, marrying anyone would be the same. But if he did find her, he'd definitely be with her. As for how to handle Qiao Mengrong—he had no idea yet.

---

仪王沉默不语，乔文仙不便再继续这个话题。

The prince remained silent. Qiao Wenxian didn't press the matter further.

---

申简辰突然问："别光说我，幽栖居士已成婚，你还喜欢人家，还想约人家，有没有觉得自己很不道德？"

Shen Jianchen suddenly asked, "Don't just talk about me. Youqi Jushi is already married, yet you still have feelings for her and want to meet her. Don't you feel that's a bit... immoral?"

---

乔文仙急了："我喜欢她在先，她成婚在后，我约她出来，只是想帮她，我与她从来没有逾越之举，你别想用道德二字绑架我。"

Qiao Wenxian grew agitated. "I liked her first—she got married after. I only want to meet her to help her. I've never crossed any line with her. Don't try to bind me with morality."

---

申简辰又问："你既然喜欢人家，为何不早些提亲？你若娶了她，岂不是没后来的破事？"

Shen Jianchen asked again, "If you liked her so much, why didn't you propose earlier? If you'd married her, none of this mess would've happened, would it?"

---

乔文仙叹口气："唉！我喜欢她时，她心里装着别人，我哪敢鼓动家人提亲？结果被可恶的林练兵官抢先，知府大人竟然还同意这门亲事，当时我肠子都快悔青，眼睁睁看着她嫁给别人，伤心之余去了汴梁。"

Qiao Wenxian sighed. "When I liked her, her heart was already with someone else. How could I dare urge my family to propose? In the end, that wretched Military Training Officer Lin got there first. And the Prefect actually agreed to the match. I kicked myself so hard back then, watching her marry someone else. Heartbroken, I left for Bianliang."

---

"知府大人？她爹难道是临安知府朱晞颜？"

"The Prefect? Is her father Zhu Xiyan, the Prefect of Lin'an?"

---

"正是。"

"Exactly."

---

"她爹是知府，怎么都不管管女儿被打之事？"

"Her father is the Prefect. Why doesn't he do something about his daughter being beaten?"

---

"也许她并未告诉知府大人。"

"Perhaps she never told the Prefect."

---

"你人在汴梁，又怎么知道她被丈夫毒打之事？"

"You were in Bianliang. How did you know she was being beaten by her husband?"

---

"我虽身在汴梁，依然关心她，给东方尘泽写信，让他把幽栖居士婚后的状况通过书信告诉我。"

"Though I was in Bianliang, I still cared about her. I wrote to Dongfang Chenze, asking him to keep me informed of Youqi Jushi's situation after her marriage."

---

"原来如此，不过，你也挺可怜，一直是单相思。"

"I see. But you're pretty pitiful too—loving her all this time from afar."

---

"是啊，她虽知道我的心思，因心里装着别人，无法接受我。"

"Yes. Though she knows my feelings, she can't accept me because her heart belongs to someone else."

---

"你有没有想过，她若真和离，也不一定跟你，毕竟人家心里装着别人。"

"Have you ever considered that even if she does get a mutual divorce, she might not choose you? After all, her heart is with someone else."

---

乔文仙轻松道："她心里那人已搬家，娶了别人，连孩子都有，他们不可能在一起。我已想好，如若她与丈夫和离，我立刻向父母表明，让他们到朱家提亲。"

Qiao Wenxian said lightly, "The person in her heart has moved on—married someone else, even has children. They could never be together. I've already decided: if she gets a mutual divorce from her husband, I'll tell my parents immediately and have them go to the Zhu family to propose."

---

"她已嫁过人，难道你不嫌弃？"

"She's already been married. Aren't you bothered by that?"

---

"真正喜欢一个人怎么会嫌弃？"

"How could I be bothered if I truly love someone?"

---

申简辰被文渊的真情感动，他暗暗决定，文渊走向爱情的道路上遇到任何困难，他都会尽全力帮他。

Shen Jianchen was moved by Wenyuan's sincere devotion. He silently resolved that no matter what difficulties Wenyuan faced on his path to love, he'd do everything he could to help.

---

马车已来到密林之外，仔细推算时间，确实离临安城不远，公开节度使的身份后，从别居去府衙也花不了多长时间。

The carriage had reached the edge of the dense forest. Counting carefully, it really wasn't far from Lin'an. After revealing his identity as Military Commissioner, it wouldn't take long to get from the country residence to the prefectural office.

---

别居建在密林中，远远望去，只见密林丛生，很难发现有座别居建在里面。近看，密林中有一条修葺的土路，刚好容一辆马车经过。听乔文仙说，是乔家专门为别居而修。

The residence was built deep within the forest. From a distance, you could only see thick trees—hard to notice a residence hidden inside. Up close, a well-maintained dirt road wound through the forest, just wide enough for a carriage. According to Qiao Wenxian, the Qiao family had built it specifically for the residence.

---

到达别居门口，大家走下马车，抬头看到匾额上写着"幽林别居"四个字，倒是应景。

When they reached the gate, everyone got off the carriage. Looking up, they saw a plaque with the four characters "Youlin Bieju"—Secluded Forest Villa. Quite fitting.

---

门被打开，出来一名管家急急忙忙忙迎上来："仪王、郡主、乔公子，昨儿老爷派人过来嘱咐，说从今日起仪王与郡主要住在这里，我已派下人连夜里里外外收拾一番，仪王和郡主若有什么不满意的尽管说，我再吩咐下人完善。"

The gate opened, and a steward hurried out to greet them. "Prince Yi, Princess, Young Master Qiao—yesterday the master sent word that from today, Prince Yi and the Princess would be staying here. I've had the servants clean everything inside and out through the night. If there's anything unsatisfactory, please tell me, and I'll have the servants fix it right away."

---

乔文仙说："辛苦李管家，先带仪王和郡主去住处安顿。"

Qiao Wenxian said, "Thank you for your hard work, Steward Li. Please show Prince Yi and the Princess to their quarters first."

---

"仪王、郡主、公子，这边请！"李管家在前面带路。

"This way, Prince Yi, Princess, Young Master." Steward Li led the way.

---

乔文仙第一次来自家别居，也充满好奇。别居占地面积不是很大，只有乔府的一半儿，妙在将密林的一部分圈在围墙之内。一进门，看到三排林木形成的照壁，照壁两边是用石砖修葺的小路，路两旁则保留着丛林中的树木。

It was Qiao Wenxian's first time visiting the family's country residence, and he was full of curiosity. The residence wasn't very large—only half the size of the Qiao Manor—but its brilliance lay in enclosing part of the dense forest within its walls. Upon entering, you'd see three rows of trees forming a natural screen wall. On both sides of the screen were stone-paved paths, with the forest trees preserved along either side.

---

绕过"照壁"，依然是林木，时不时传来各类鸟鸣之声。穿过林木，眼前才出现房舍，明显是砍伐了树木之后，用开辟出来的空间建造而成，房屋与房屋之间也由树木间隔起来。

Past the "screen wall," more forest stretched out, with birdsong of various kinds echoing from time to time. Only after passing through the trees did the buildings come into view—clearly constructed in the spaces cleared by cutting down trees, with the remaining trees serving as natural dividers between the houses.

---

李管家说："这是主屋，仪王住这里，主屋后面有闺房，郡主可居住，都已打扫干净。我这就带郡主过去。"

Steward Li said, "This is the main house—Prince Yi will stay here. Behind the main house is a boudoir for the Princess. Everything has been cleaned. I'll take the Princess there now."

---

乔文仙被自家别居的景色吸引，临时决定也留在这里："不行，我也要住在别居，李管家给我安排安排。"

Qiao Wenxian was captivated by the scenery of the family residence and made a sudden decision to stay. "No, I want to stay at the villa too. Steward Li, arrange something for me."

---

李管家笑道："屋子尽有，除了主屋和后面的闺房，公子随便挑，每个屋子下人都收拾了一遍。"

Steward Li smiled. "We have plenty of rooms. Besides the main house and the boudoir in the back, Young Master can pick any room. The servants have cleaned every one of them."

---

乔文仙立刻吩咐："南双，回趟乔府，把我平日用的东西统统搬来，此处虽不及仙境，也颇具意趣之美，你家公子我决定陪仪王住在这里。"

Qiao Wenxian immediately ordered, "Nanshuang, go back to the Qiao Manor and bring all my daily belongings here. This place may not be a fairy realm, but it has a charm all its own. I've decided to stay here with Prince Yi."

---

"是，公子。"南双拉上白风，"你陪我一起去吧？"

"Yes, Young Master." Nanshuang pulled Baifeng along. "Come with me, will you?"

---

白风很不乐意："不行，我还要照顾我家王爷。"

Baifeng was reluctant. "No, I still need to attend to my prince."

---

申简辰说："你去给南双帮把手，我这里无妨。"

Shen Jianchen said, "Go give Nanshuang a hand. I'll be fine here."

---

白风这才答应："既然王爷都发话，我就陪你再回乔府一趟。"

Only then did Baifeng agree. "Since the prince has spoken, I'll go back to the Qiao Manor with you."

---

南双与白风走后，李管家领着乔梦容向主屋后面绕去，也是绿树丛生，踩着铺好的石板路，在绿树环绕中出现一处闺房小院，真是情趣盎然，乔梦容很喜欢。

After Nanshuang and Baifeng left, Steward Li led Qiao Mengrong around to the back of the main house. More lush greenery surrounded the area. Stepping along the paved stone path, a small boudoir courtyard appeared, encircled by green trees. It was truly charming, and Qiao Mengrong loved it.

---

李管家说："郡主住这里，丫鬟都给您备齐，需要什么尽管让您的贴身丫鬟告诉我，我就不在此打扰，还要到前边去看看我家公子选中哪个院子，好给他安排一番。"

Steward Li said, "The Princess will stay here. All the maids have been arranged for you. If you need anything, just let your personal maid tell me. I won't disturb you any further—I need to go see which courtyard Young Master has chosen so I can prepare it for him."

---

乔梦容也想在没有外人时，让喜兰把屋子按照自己的喜好再收拾收拾："李管家去忙吧，不用管我。"

Qiao Mengrong also wanted to have the room rearranged to her liking by Xilan when no outsiders were around. "Go ahead, Steward Li. Don't worry about me."

---

乔文仙在主屋的东面挑一处院子，屋子虽比主屋小，却十分惬意，关键是院子外搭有一座木质高台。

Qiao Wenxian chose a courtyard to the east of the main house. Though the rooms were smaller than the main house, they were quite cozy. The key feature was a wooden terrace built just outside the courtyard.

---

仔细观察，是用五棵粗壮高大的树木建造而成。周围四棵，中间一棵，树木的顶端被修葺后，搭了木架，周围还修有栏杆，五棵树木的其余枝叶则从一旁伸展开来。

Looking closely, it was built using five thick, tall trees—four around the edges and one in the center. The tops of the trees had been trimmed, and a wooden platform was built on top, with railings around the edges. The remaining branches and leaves of the five trees spread out from the sides.

---

乔文仙不由赞道："没想到我爹平日思想古板，新建的宅子倒是意趣横生。仪王，我们一起爬上高台欣赏风景，如何？"

Qiao Wenxian couldn't help but exclaim, "I never expected my father, with his old-fashioned ways, to build such an imaginative residence. Prince Yi, how about we climb up the terrace and enjoy the view together?"

---

申简辰自然乐意，他们顺着木质台阶一前一后爬上去。

Shen Jianchen was naturally happy to. They climbed up one after the other via the wooden steps.

---

顶端的木架十分结实，踩上去没有嘎吱嘎的声音。上面摆着一个圆形的木桌，两把椅子，没事时上来品品茶，再极目远眺，看看密林的风景，绝对十分惬意。

The wooden platform at the top was very sturdy—no creaking when you stepped on it. A round wooden table and two chairs were set up there. On idle days, you could come up here to sip tea and gaze into the distance at the forest scenery. Absolutely delightful.

---

乔文仙惊叹道："真是太美了！我们竟然站在密林高处，将整个密林尽收眼底！"

Qiao Wenxian exclaimed, "It's truly beautiful! We're actually standing high above the forest, with the entire woodland spread out before us!"

---

李管家走来，朝高处喊道："公子！您可把院子挑好？"

Steward Li walked over and called up, "Young Master! Have you chosen your courtyard?"

---

乔文仙向下大声说："已挑好！就是这里！"

Qiao Wenxian called down loudly, "Already chosen! Right here!"

---

李管家又大声说："公子与仪王可满意？"

Steward Li called back loudly, "Are you and Prince Yi satisfied?"

---

申简辰未搭话，乔文仙大声回道："满意满意！你去忙吧！不用管我们！"

Shen Jianchen didn't respond, but Qiao Wenxian called back loudly, "Satisfied, satisfied! Go about your business! Don't worry about us!"

---

主人家不再有需要，李管家笑着离开，边走边自语："公子第一次来，新鲜劲儿十足，我啊，天天在别居，都不觉得有多美，冷冷清清的，没城里热闹。"

With the young master needing nothing more, Steward Li left with a smile, muttering to himself as he walked away: "It's the Young Master's first time here—he's full of fresh excitement. As for me, I'm at the villa every day and don't find it all that beautiful. It's quiet and lonely, not as lively as the city."

---

申简辰靠近栏杆，视线穿越密林，朝不远处望去，看到一座山："那里有座山，叫什么名字？"

Shen Jianchen leaned against the railing, his gaze piercing through the forest toward the distance. He spotted a mountain. "There's a mountain over there. What's it called?"

---

乔文仙朝大山瞧去："没名字，不过山上的尼姑庵倒是挺出名，只因里面有位住持，能知过去未来。"

Qiao Wenxian looked toward the mountain. "It doesn't have a name. But the nunnery on the mountain is quite famous, because the abbess there is said to know the past and the future."

---

申简辰不大相信："真有这样的人？"

Shen Jianchen was skeptical. "There really is such a person?"

---

乔文仙随口说道："大家都这么传，我也是人云亦云。"

Qiao Wenxian said casually, "That's what everyone says. I'm just repeating what I've heard."

---

申简辰继续面向大山，不知不觉被山下的一片红色吸引："那又是什么？"

Shen Jianchen continued facing the mountain, gradually drawn to a patch of red at its base. "What's that over there?"

---

乔文仙向仪王指的方向望去："哦，是一棵千年神柳，临安城许多青年男女到尼姑庵求姻缘后，喜欢把红绸系在神柳的枝条上，以求护佑，据传言这棵神柳是月老的一根胡须幻化而成。"

Qiao Wenxian looked in the direction the prince was pointing. "Oh, that's a Millennial Divine Willow. Many young men and women from Lin'an, after praying for a good match at the nunnery, like to tie red silk ribbons to the willow's branches for blessings. Legend has it that this divine willow was transformed from one of Yue Lao's whiskers."

---

"千年神柳！"申简辰重复着四个字，他显得有些激动，"文渊，你能带我过去看看吗？"

"Millennial Divine Willow!" Shen Jianchen repeated the words, visibly agitated. "Wenyuan, can you take me over to see it?"

---

"有何不可？离得又不远，你我步行即可到达。"

"Why not? It's not far—we can walk there."

---

二人下了木质高台，出了幽林别居。乔文仙在前面带路，沿着密林中的天然小径，穿梭在林间。

The two climbed down from the wooden terrace and left Youlin Villa. Qiao Wenxian led the way, following a natural path through the dense forest, weaving among the trees.

---

当穿越过密林，视野变得开阔时，一片池塘出现在眼前。

When they passed through the forest and the view opened up, a pond appeared before them.

---

申简辰的双眼变得湿润起来，眼前的池塘与他穿越之前见到的竟然十分相似，不由产生一种回到穿越之前的感觉，要不是余光瞧见文渊，他绝对会这么认为。

Shen Jianchen's eyes grew moist. The pond before him was surprisingly similar to the one he'd seen before transmigrating. He couldn't help but feel like he'd been transported back to his old world. If it weren't for Wenyuan in the corner of his eye, he'd have believed it completely.

---

沿着池塘的岸边，一步一步走向对岸的神柳，越是走近，申简辰内心越激动，就是这棵神柳在地震中倾倒，压在他与千里寻的身上，然后带着他穿越而来，没想到这个时代也有它的身影。

Walking along the pond's edge, step by step toward the divine willow on the opposite bank, Shen Jianchen grew more and more excited with each step. This was the very divine willow that had toppled in the earthquake, crushing him and Qianli Xun beneath it, then carrying him through time. He never imagined this era had its own version.

---

乔文仙对仪王看到神柳时的表情感到疑惑，又不便多问，默默跟着他朝对岸走去。

Qiao Wenxian was puzzled by the prince's expression upon seeing the divine willow, but it wasn't appropriate to ask too many questions. He silently followed him toward the opposite bank.

---

到达神柳跟前，竟然发现一滴泪水从仪王的眼中滚落，掉在脚下的草丛中，正好被一片叶子接住，晶莹剔透，像极了清晨的一滴露珠。他不由在心里自问："仪王第一次见神柳，为何会如此激动？"

Reaching the divine willow, he was shocked to see a tear roll down from the prince's eye. It fell into the grass at his feet, caught perfectly by a leaf—crystal clear, just like a morning dewdrop. He couldn't help but ask himself: "Why is Prince Yi so emotional upon seeing the divine willow for the first time?"

---

仪王走过去，抱住神柳粗壮的树干，更令乔文仙不解。

The prince walked forward and embraced the thick trunk of the divine willow, leaving Qiao Wenxian even more bewildered.

---

申简辰抱着神柳默默在心中念叨："你就是把我送到这个时代的神柳，告诉我，千里寻到底在哪里？"

Holding the divine willow, Shen Jianchen silently murmured in his heart: "You're the divine willow that sent me to this era. Tell me—where exactly is Qianli Xun?"

---

乔文仙忍不住问："仪王，你怎么了？"

Qiao Wenxian couldn't help but ask, "Prince Yi, what's wrong?"

---

听到文渊发问，申简辰意识到自己见到神柳时太过激动，很是失态。他放开手，忍住即将掉下来的泪水，转过身："没什么，我在梦中多次见到跟它一模一样的柳树，倍感亲切。"

Hearing Wenyuan's question, Shen Jianchen realized he'd been too emotional upon seeing the divine willow—he'd completely lost composure. He let go, held back the tears threatening to fall, and turned around. "It's nothing. I've seen a willow just like this one many times in my dreams. It felt... deeply familiar."

---

"原来如此。"乔文仙望着一树的红绸随风飘动，接着瞧见高处的枝条上系着一根红绸，鲜艳而又孤独，"谁这么厉害，竟然把红绸系那么高？这得搬多高的梯子才能上去？"

"I see." Qiao Wenxian gazed at the tree covered in red silk ribbons fluttering in the wind. Then he noticed a single ribbon tied high up on the branches—vivid and solitary. "Who's skilled enough to tie a ribbon that high? How tall a ladder would you need to get up there?"

---

申简远离神柳几步，也向上望去："系红绸之人应该会轻功，飞身而上，再以轻功踩着柳树的枝叶挂上去的。"

Shen Jianchen stepped back a few paces from the divine willow and also looked up. "The person who tied the ribbon must know Qinggong—lightness skill. They leaped up and stepped on the willow's branches to hang it."

---

乔文仙叹道："竟然有这么厉害的人！"

Qiao Wenxian sighed. "To think there's someone that skilled!"

---

申简辰很不屑："不算什么，我也会。"

Shen Jianchen was dismissive. "It's nothing special. I can do it too."

---

乔文仙要求道："要不仪王表演一下？让我见识见识。"

Qiao Wenxian requested, "How about you demonstrate, Prince Yi? Let me see it."

---

申简辰启动轻功，"蹭"地跳上神柳，踩在柳枝上，如履平地。

Shen Jianchen activated his Qinggong and leaped onto the divine willow with a swoosh, stepping on the willow branches as if walking on flat ground.

---

"怎么样？厉害吧？"

"How about that? Impressive, right?"

---

乔文仙既佩服又疑惑："也是神人教你的？"

Qiao Wenxian was both impressed and puzzled. "Did the divine man teach you this too?"

---

"当然。"申简辰露出得意的表情。

"Of course." Shen Jianchen wore a proud expression.

---

乔文仙再次要求道："既然仪王已上去，不如看看红绸上写些什么。"

Qiao Wenxian requested again, "Since you're already up there, Prince Yi, why not see what's written on the ribbon?"

---

申简辰瞧一眼红绸："还是算了，别人的隐私，跟你我又不相干。"他一跃而下，站在乔文仙面前。

Shen Jianchen glanced at the ribbon. "Forget it. It's someone else's privacy—none of our business." He leaped down and stood before Qiao Wenxian.

---

"真没趣，白白跳上去一回。"乔文仙抬头瞅着挂在高处的红绸，很是失望。

"How boring. You went up there for nothing." Qiao Wenxian looked up at the ribbon hanging high, quite disappointed.

---

申简辰拍拍他的肩膀："行了，走吧，明日带我逛遍整个临安城。"

Shen Jianchen patted his shoulder. "Alright, let's go. Tomorrow, take me all around Lin'an."

---

两人又沿原路返回，申简辰边走边想，往后定要时常来神柳处坐坐。

The two returned the way they came. As they walked, Shen Jianchen thought to himself that he'd definitely come sit by the divine willow often from now on.

---

靠近密林处，他回头望一眼神柳，再次想道："神柳，你既然在这个时代，千里寻肯定也在，我相信一定能找到她。"

Near the edge of the forest, he turned back to glance at the divine willow one more time, thinking again: "Divine willow, since you're in this era, Qianli Xun must be here too. I believe I'll definitely find her."
