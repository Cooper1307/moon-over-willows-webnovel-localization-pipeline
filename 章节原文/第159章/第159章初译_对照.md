第159章 捶丸场求纳夏果
## Chapter 159: Chapter 159: Requesting Xiaguo on the Chuiwan Lawn

临安捶丸场地要数陈家的最大，乔家今年花大价钱租了陈家的，无非是因为儿子乔文仙快要成亲，成亲前举行一场捶丸赛先热热场，给儿子的婚事添些人气，也讨个吉利。
乔守仁对钱氏的建议无不赞同，一方面满足了钱氏的喜好；另一方面也好与临安有头有脸的人家继续保持良好关系。
要不要往朱家送请帖，乔守仁没犹豫多久，决定还得送，不送便是将矛盾摆在明面上；送了若朱家拒绝，乔家没面子，礼数尽在，朱家不来，就不能怪乔家什么事。
捶丸这日，春光明媚，千里寻带着韦嬷嬷、苦荞、夏果，还有新收在身边的红豆，乘坐马车，向陈家捶丸场而去。
陈家的捶丸场地位于城东最偏僻处，千里寻到达时，从马车上刚走下，瞧见哥哥扶着嫂嫂也从马车上下来，她迎上去打招呼。
“哥哥嫂嫂，没想到我们竟然一起到。”
“要不怎么是一家人？”朱景元见妹妹前来，没有悲戚之情，心中甚觉欣慰。
陈文心拉起淑真：“走，我们一起进去。”
姑嫂两个走在前面，朱景元和韦嬷嬷跟在一旁，身后则跟着众位丫鬟，一起向捶丸场地内走去。
捶丸场外筑有高墙，一时瞧不到里面的场景。大门敞开，走进后，千里寻以为自己进入到古代园林中。
“嫂嫂，你确定这是捶丸之地？怎么还有亭台楼阁，假山池水？该不会是陈家的别居？”
陈文心笑道：“你还真是失忆，什么都不记得。现在看到的不过是供大家休闲娱乐赏景之处，再往里走，才是捶丸的场地。”
“哦，原来如此。”
众人顺着园林的小径向里走，穿过池水上的长亭，再穿越一道园门，眼前变得开阔起来，一片草地映入眼帘，零星冒出的新芽在枯草中显得鲜艳夺目。
千里寻总算看清捶丸场地是怎么回事，确实与高尔夫球的场地有异曲同工之妙，也有平坦、凹凸的地势，球窝处插有小小的旗子。
已有人正在捶丸，千里寻瞧那人手中的球杆，心中“嘿嘿”一笑：“的确与高尔夫球的球杆很像。”
乔家的丫鬟迎上来：“这边请。”
千里寻发现，捶丸场地的两边有凉亭，专供人歇息观看，凉亭里有桌椅，茶水和果品，已有好些人坐在凉亭下，边说笑，边观看众人捶丸。
在丫鬟的带领下，他们来到一处凉亭刚坐下，钱氏与米氏前来打招呼。
“朱家公子、少夫人，还有朱家姑娘，你们能到来，乔家的捶丸赛举办得更有意义。”
说话的正是雪天跪在她面前的钱氏，当时痛哭流涕，此时却春光满面。
千里寻觉得人真的是一种善变的动物。出于礼节，她与哥哥嫂嫂，以及韦嬷嬷都站起来。有嫂嫂在，千里寻不用说任何话。
“钱夫人严重，此处是陈家的场地，我好久未来，正好借此机会过来瞧瞧，景元疼爱我，自然陪着；至于淑真，她在我的指导下，捶丸技术大大提升，收到乔家的请帖，甚是开心，想来一展身手。”
陈文心的话显然带着情绪，钱氏与米氏心中不快，怎奈是东道主，又是为儿子的婚事热场，不好与对方计较。
“不管因为什么，既然来了，就是乔家的座上宾。”钱氏说完，对身边管事的丫鬟吩咐，“告诉下人，好生伺候朱家人。”
丫鬟回道：“是。”
钱氏瞧见苗家人来，立即说：“你们先坐，我去招呼客人。”
千里寻又与嫂嫂等人坐下，向钱氏走的方向瞧去，原来她们迎接的是苗知瑜与秦夫人，两家已是亲家，见面分外热情。
钱氏携着秦夫人的手，米氏也拉起苗知瑜，向另外一处凉亭而去。
陈文心很不屑：“有什么了不起！苗家怎能和朱家相比？苗知瑜也比淑真差远了。”
千里寻内心仍有酸楚之感，却挤出笑容：“嫂嫂不用替我说话，我已经对此事没什么感觉。”
朱景元说：“淑真已有好人家，没必要再跟乔家置气。”
想到弟弟要娶淑真，陈文心开心道：“是啊，我还跟乔家置什么气？也不知浩宇这家伙有没有来？”
朱景元说：“你与淑真在这里坐着，我去跟乔老爷打声招呼，顺便找找浩宇。”
“去吧。”
得到允许，朱景元起身离开。
哥嫂说话之际，千里寻开始认真观察捶丸之人，有男有女，手执球杆，在固定的位置向球窝内击球，那动作与打高尔夫球如出一辙。
Boasting the largest chuiwan grounds in Lin'an, the Chen estate rented its venue at top silver to the Qiaos this spring so Madam Qian could host a lively warm-up tournament ahead of Qiao Wenxian's wedding, drumming up festive cheer and good luck. Qiao Shouren backed his wife on both counts, indulging her hobby while keeping ties polished with every notable house in Lin'an. Debating whether to send an invitation to Zhu Manor took him only a minute: skipping them publicized bad blood, whereas sending one preserved courtesy; if the Zhus declined, nobody could fault the Qiaos.

千里寻在心中笑道：“捶丸定是高尔夫球的老祖宗。”
Tournament day dawned crisp and sun-drenched. Boarding her carriage with Matron Wei, Kuqiao, Xiaguo, and newly promoted Hongdou, Qianli Xun rode out to the Chen chuiwan estate tucked on Lin'an's quiet eastern outskirts. Stepping down right beside another carriage, she spotted her brother helping Sister-in-law out and hurried over. "Look at that timing, Brother, Sister-in-law!"

夏果站在小姐身后，一直寻找乔公子的身影，终于看到乔公子在球场一边，向这边瞧来，他应该是看小姐，看来他还未真正放下小姐。
"What else makes us family?" Zhu Jingyuan smiled, relieved to see zero gloom on his sister's face.

小姐只顾欣赏别人打球，根本没注意乔公子。夏果为乔公子感到心酸，悄悄溜出凉亭，从亭子后面迂回着来到乔公子身边。
Linking arms with Shuzhen, Chen Wenxin led the way—"Let's head in!"—with Zhu Jingyuan, Matron Wei, and the cluster of maids trailing behind.

“乔公子，能否与我到那边说话？”
High outer walls hid everything from the lane; stepping past open gates made Qianli Xun blink in surprise at pavilions, tiered halls, rockeries, and clear ponds. "Sure this is a chuiwan field, Sister-in-law, and not a private Chen villa?"

乔文仙本不愿理夏果，可又没有其他办法接近淑真，也许夏果能帮他，犹豫片刻后跟上夏果。
Chen Wenxin chuckled. "Amnesia really wiped everything! This front garden is just for strolling and tea; chuiwan lawns lie straight past!"

南双拉住自家公子：“公子，我喜欢夏果。”
Winding along garden walks, crossing a long bridge pavilion over water, and passing an inner moon gate opened onto sweeping emerald turf where bright green spring shoots broke through winter grass, complete with rolling slopes, flats, and tiny flags marking each target hole. Seeing players already swinging curved wooden clubs made Qianli Xun grin inside: Dead ringer for golf!

乔文仙并未理会，甩开南双，继续跟上夏果。南双只好跟在公子身后，一脸委屈。
Qiao family maids stepped forward guiding them to shaded wooden pavilions ringing both flanks of the turf, stocked with tables, tea, and seasonal fruit where clusters of guests already chatted while watching matches. Right after they sat in their pavilion, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi approached beaming. "Young Master Zhu, Young Madam, and Miss Zhu, your presence makes our chuiwan match shine twice as bright!"

他们来到一棵树的后面，树枝上已冒出新芽，还未长成大片的树叶，无法更好地挡住身影。
The very Madam Qian who had knelt sobbing in the snow weeks ago now radiated effortless springtime polish. How fast human faces flipped! Rising polite beside brother, sister-in-law, and Matron Wei, Qianli Xun left talking to Chen Wenxin.

“你叫我来这里做什么？”乔文仙虽跟来，却也不满。
"Too generous, Madam Qian! Being Chen family grounds I haven't visited in ages, I dropped by to look around—with Jingyuan doting along—and under my coaching Shuzhen's chuiwan form shot straight up, so she jumped at your invitation to show off!"

夏果跪在地上：“乔公子对我家小姐依然情根深种，可您也看到，她根本未瞧见您，说明小姐心中已无乔公子。您醒醒吧，瞧瞧我，我对乔公子的感情就像乔公子对小姐的感情一样深。”
Sharp barbs hid under every smile. Displeasure flickered across Madam Qian and Concubine Mi, yet playing host before their son's wedding kept them polite. "Whatever the reason, every guest under our roof is honored!" Turning to her head maid, she ordered, "Make sure the Zhu party wants for nothing!" Spotting the Miao clan arriving just then, Madam Qian excused herself fast. "Sit tight; more guests to greet!"

“那又如何？只要我喜欢她即可，哪怕远远看她一眼，我也心满意足。”
Resuming their seats, they watched Madam Qian clasp Madam Qin's hands while Concubine Mi linked arms warm with Miao Zhiyu, escorting their new in-laws toward another pavilion.

南双更委屈，夏果又跪下求公子，显然没把他当回事，他此刻决定，这么无情的人，还是不要再惦记。
Chen Wenxin snorted in scorn. "Big deal! The Miao house can't hold a candle to the Zhus, and Miao Zhiyu isn't fit to tie Shuzhen's shoes!"

夏果见不奏效，使出最后一招，反正小姐已不可能与乔公子在一起，她如何说，小姐应该不会介意。
A faint pinch still tugged Qianli Xun's chest, masked behind an easy smile. "Save your breath, Sister-in-law; doesn't faze me one bit anymore."

“小姐不允许我以后再跪下求乔公子纳我为妾，她说……她说……她说既然要与乔公子了断，就要断得干干净净，不留一丝痕迹，不给乔公子任何希望……”
"Shuzhen has a fine match ahead; no sense nursing grudges with the Qiaos," Zhu Jingyuan added smooth.

乔文仙内心被深深击痛：“她真这么说？”
Remembering her brother marrying Shuzhen cheered Chen Wenxin right up. "Right! Why waste breath on Qiaos? Wonder if Haoyu even showed up yet!"

“小姐亲口……亲口对我说，还说……”夏果一边说，一边想着怎么说，才能将乔公子激怒，“还说即使把我许给临安城最丑的乞丐做妾，也不会许给乔公子……”
"Stay here with Shuzhen; I'll go greet Master Qiao and track down Haoyu," Zhu Jingyuan said, rising as his wife waved him off.

乔文仙彻底被激怒，因为怒气，不管不顾，转身就走。
Watching players grip wooden clubs on the tee and drive balls across rolling turf toward flagged holes, Qianli Xun chuckled to herself: Chuiwan has to be golf's great-grandfather!

刚走出没几步，碰见韦嬷嬷站在路边，显然他与夏果说的话已被听到。乔文仙才不管这些，也没有心情打招呼，直奔凉亭而去。
Standing behind her mistress, Xiaguo kept scanning the sidelines until she spotted Young Master Qiao standing off at the edge of the field staring straight toward their pavilion, eyes glued on Miss, still pining! Yet Miss never looked his way once, absorbed in the game. Aching for him, Xiaguo slipped unseen out the back of the pavilion and circled behind the turf to reach his side. "Young Master Qiao, could we speak over there a moment?"

原来韦嬷嬷瞧见夏果悄悄离开，尾随而来。夏果与乔文仙在树后说的话她尽数听在耳中。
Reluctant to talk to Xiaguo yet desperate for any bridge to Shuzhen, Qiao Wenxian hesitated a beat before following. Tugged back by Nan Shuang—"Master, I like Xiaguo!"—Qiao Wenxian shook him off without a word, leaving a crestfallen Nan Shuang trailing behind.

当夏果为自己的计谋感到高兴，起身要走时，被韦嬷嬷一巴掌打在脸上。
Halting behind a budding tree whose sparse spring shoots offered thin cover, Qiao Wenxian frowned. "Why pull me over here?"

夏果捂着被打疼的脸：“嬷嬷凭什么打我？”
Dropping straight onto both knees, Xiaguo pleaded, "You're still devoted to my Miss, Young Master Qiao, yet look—she didn't even spare you a glance! Her heart holds zero room for you! Wake up and look at me; my devotion to you runs every bit as deep as yours for her!"

“哼！凭什么？凭你跪在地上求着当人家的妾室，给姑娘丢脸；凭你信口雌黄，给姑娘编排的那些瞎话；凭你不顾丫鬟本分，喜欢不该喜欢的人。”
"So what? Loving her is enough for me. Even gazing from afar fills my heart."

夏果放下捂着脸的手，理直气壮：“嬷嬷说那么多有什么用？很快我就不是小姐的丫鬟，嬷嬷也管不着我。”
Behind him, Nan Shuang's chest sank cold. Kneeling before Master again like he didn't even exist! Time to cut this heartless girl out for good.

“我倒盼着你早些离开望月轩。”
Seeing gentle pleas fail, Xiaguo played her trump card, figuring since Miss and Young Master Qiao were done forever, bending truth would not hurt Miss anyway. "Miss forbade me from ever begging you to take me as concubine again! She said... she said since she broke clean with Young Master Qiao, she wants every last trace wiped out, zero hope left..."

“既然嬷嬷和我的想法一致，就别再管我。”
Pain jabbed straight through Qiao Wenxian's ribs. "She said that?!"

“管你？你也配！我是担心你伤害姑娘。”
"Word for word! Even swore..."—improvising sharp to push him past boiling—"...swore she'd hand me to Lin'an's ugliest beggar as concubine before ever giving me to Young Master Qiao!"

“嬷嬷放心，小姐是我的恩人，我绝不会做伤害她的事，我编的那些瞎话，对小姐来讲无伤大雅，却可达到我的目的。”
Blind fury snapped Qiao Wenxian's last thread of restraint. Pivoting on his heel without another glance, he stormed straight away, brushing right past Matron Wei standing beside the path, too furious even to greet her as he marched straight toward the Zhu pavilion.

“只盼你别再给姑娘惹什么大麻烦！”
Having spotted Xiaguo sneak off and shadowed her step for step, Matron Wei had caught every poisonous word! Just as smug Xiaguo stood to leave, crack! A stinging slap across the face stopped her cold.

乔家公子被夏果激怒，从走去的方向判断，应该是朝朱家人歇息的凉亭而去。韦嬷嬷担心姑娘，不再理会夏果，转身沿原路返回。
Clutching her burning cheek, Xiaguo flared, "What right do you have hitting me, Matron?!"

乔文仙满载怒气，来到淑真待的凉亭下，她依旧在观看别人捶丸，不时与陈文心说笑。淑真越高兴，乔文仙越受伤，他更不愿让她如愿以偿。
"What right?! Kneeling in the dirt begging to be a concubine, shaming our young lady! Spinning filthy lies slandering your own mistress! Forgetting a maid's place chasing a man above you!"

“淑真……”
Dropping her hand with brazen defiance, Xiaguo shot back, "Lecture all you want, Matron! I won't be Miss's maid much longer; soon you won't have any say over me!"

听到熟悉的声音，千里寻刚刚露出的笑脸变得平静，文渊什么时候来到身边，她竟然没有注意到。
"Can't wait for the day you pack out of Wangyue Pavilion!"

“文渊……不……乔公子找我何事？”
"Then since we both want the same thing, stay out of my lane!"

淑真竟然开始称呼他为“乔公子”，如此生分，看来她连相爱前朋友的情谊都不愿再继续，又深深刺痛乔文仙的自尊，他失去理智，只想挽回自尊，只想留下他与她千丝万缕的联系。
"Care about you? Don't flatter yourself—I only guard my young lady from your venom!"

“幽……幽栖居士……”乔文仙尽管不想这样称呼，却不由自主脱口而出，“我想问你要个人，望幽栖居士答应并成全。”
"Relax, Matron. Miss saved my life; I'd never harm her! A couple harmless fibs won't scratch Miss and win me what I want!"

“什么人？”千里寻感到害怕，担心乔文仙说出朱淑真或是幽栖居士。
"Pray you don't bring real disaster down on her head!" Seeing furious Young Master Qiao bearing down on the Zhu pavilion, Matron Wei left Xiaguo behind and hurried straight back.

乔文仙脱口而出：“你身边的丫鬟夏果。”
Boiling with hurt pride, Qiao Wenxian strode up to Shuzhen's pavilion, where she still sat laughing and chatting with Chen Wenxin over the game. Her bright smiles twisted the knife deeper; no way was he letting her erase every tie clean! "Shuzhen..."

千里寻松口气，同时也感到一丝失落，好好的感情真真实实就这么没了，两人以后只能客气地说话。
Hearing that familiar voice wiped the grin off Qianli Xun's face. When had he walked right up? Catching herself fast, she said, "Wenyuan... no, Young Master Qiao, what brings you here?"

淑真不说话，乔文仙以为她不想答应，毕竟她不想再与他有任何联系：“幽栖居士难道想让我跪在你面前，才肯答应吗？”
"Young Master Qiao"—so frosty she would not even keep old friendship! Stung raw in his pride and stripped of cool sense, clinging to one last twisted thread linking them, he blurted, "You... Youqi Jushi! I come to ask one person from you—grant my wish!"

千里寻刚想说答应的话，乔守仁走来：“文渊，有很多公子找你，你怎么在这里？”
Heart lurching lest he blurt out "Zhu Shuzhen" or "Youqi Jushi," Qianli Xun asked tense, "Who?"

乔文仙还在与父亲赌气，他来得正好，不如将气发泄在他的身上：“父亲来得正好，儿子想纳幽栖居士身边的丫鬟夏果为妾，还望父亲成全。”
"Your personal maid, Xiaguo."

听到是纳妾，不是与朱家姑娘旧情复燃，乔守仁“呵呵”笑道：“原来是想纳妾，为父当然同意，不知幽栖居士意下如何？”
Relief washed over Qianli Xun, trailing a whisper of hollow ache: their whole love story truly dead and buried, reduced to stiff formality.

千里寻自觉没有福气嫁到乔家，文渊既然愿意纳夏果为妾，也算是给她谋个好去处。
Mistaking her silence for refusal—determined to sever every bond—Qiao Wenxian pressed bitter, "Do I have to drop to my knees before Youqi Jushi before you say yes?!"

“乔老爷，乔公子，夏果是我的贴身丫鬟，希望她到乔家后，乔家人能善待她。”
Just as Qianli Xun opened her mouth to agree, Qiao Shouren strolled up. "Crowds of young gentlemen looking for you, Wenyuan. What are you doing over here?"

淑真爽快答应，乔文仙又感到难过，他一句话未说，转身离开。南双行礼后，去追自家公子。
Still raw and spiteful toward his father too, Qiao Wenxian aimed his anger straight at him. "Right on time, Father! Your son wishes to take Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo as concubine. Grant your consent!"

乔文仙突然离开，搞得千里寻不知所措。
Relieved it was just taking a maid as concubine rather than reigniting things with Miss Zhu, Qiao Shouren chuckled breezy. "Just taking a concubine? Father agrees, of course! What says Youqi Jushi?"

陈文心赶紧说：“乔老爷，乔公子今日看着奇怪，不知他怎么了？”
Since fate barred her from the Qiao door, letting Xiaguo enter as Wenyuan's concubine gave the girl her wish anyway. "Master Qiao, Young Master Qiao, Xiaguo is my personal maid; I only ask your house to treat her well."

“呵呵……，文渊即将成亲，又要纳妾，一时适应不过来。”乔守仁解释完，又转向朱家姑娘，“文渊成婚那日，乔家会派人将轿子抬到望月轩，还望朱家姑娘做好准备。”
Her crisp consent sliced Qiao Wenxian fresh. Without another syllable, he spun around and stalked off, Nan Shuang bowing fast before chasing after him.

文渊的婚事将近，一丝难过之后，千里寻很快恢复正常：“乔老爷放心，我在望月轩静候佳音。”
Stunned by his abrupt exit, Qianli Xun blinked speechless while Chen Wenxin smoothed it over. "Young Master Qiao looks off today, Master Qiao—everything alright?"

乔守仁离开后，韦嬷嬷才回到座位坐下，刚才大家说的话，她在一旁已听到。
"Haha, wedding right around the corner plus taking a concubine—just adjusting!" Turning courteous to Shuzhen, Qiao Shouren added, "On Wenyuan's wedding day, Qiao Manor will send a sedan chair straight to Wangyue Pavilion. Please have her ready, Miss Zhu."

“夏果终于得偿所愿，姑娘就当做了件善事。”
Hearing his wedding loomed so close pinched one brief second before calm returned. "Rest assured, Master Qiao. We'll await your glad news at Wangyue Pavilion."

陈文心开玩笑道：“嬷嬷刚刚去了哪里？又从哪里突然冒出来？”
Once Qiao Shouren walked away, Matron Wei stepped back into the pavilion—having overheard the closing lines from outside—and took her seat. "Xiaguo got her wish at last; chalk it up as doing a good deed, young lady."

“我到别处转转，回来时见乔老爷在此说话，就在一旁待了会儿。”
"Where'd you vanish to, Matron, popping out of thin air?" Chen Wenxin teased.
