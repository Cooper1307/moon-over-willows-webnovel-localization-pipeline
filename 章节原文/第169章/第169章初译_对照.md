第169章 大闹陈府逼退婚
## Chapter 169: Wrecking Chen Manor to Force a Broken Engagement

千里寻坐在椅子上，耐心等待陈浩宇的父母前来。
她听嫂嫂说起过，他们不仅人好，二人之间的感情也甚好，唯一失败的便是培养了一个不成器的儿子。
千里寻思忖，等二位长辈到来，怎么能将其威慑一番？让他们觉得这个未来的儿媳妇绝对不能娶。
﻿她环顾四周，发现花坛旁边有块被打磨成方形的石头，比砚台大些。
“红豆，去把那块石头给我取来。”
红豆跑到花坛旁，将石头双手搬起来，倒不沉，一只手托好，另一手掏出帕子，把石头上的尘土擦干净，回到小姐面前，双手呈上。
千里寻接过石头，一只手掂了掂，重量一般，待会儿靠它吓唬陈浩宇的爹娘应该不成问题。
正掂着石头，青瓜闻风而来：“朱家姑娘，您这是做什么？我家公子今日不在，您要有气，等他回来再撒。”
千里寻故作严肃的样子：“谁说是来找你家公子？老娘要想揍他，在哪里都能解决，何必来陈府？老娘今日是来见见未来的公爹和婆婆，让他们见识一下未来的儿媳妇是什么样的人，省得日后嫁到陈家他们不习惯。”
青瓜很无语，退到一边。心想，未来的少夫人一口一个“老娘”，与那日在捶丸场遇到时实在不同，真怀疑是不是同一人。
千里寻继续掂着石头，有意跷起二郎腿，余光瞟到有人走来。
朝那方向瞧去，一男一女被丫鬟簇拥着向这边走来，看上去年纪与朱淑真的父母不相上下，应该是陈浩宇的爹娘。
本应站起来行礼迎接，可今日是来闹事，礼节还是免了，说不定见到未来的儿媳妇是无礼之人，他们更会生出退婚的想法。
对方越走越近，千里寻故意露出不屑的表情，斜眼瞥向两位长辈。
陈江林与姚轻琰走近后，将未来的儿媳妇上下瞧一遍。以前他们与朱家姑娘有过几面之缘，留下的印象均是知书达理，温婉动人。
眼前这位，如果手中不拿着石头，不斜眼瞧人，别跷二郎腿，再站起来唤一声“伯父伯母”，便妥妥是位大家闺秀。
Seated in her chair, Qianli Xun waited for Chen Haoyu's parents to appear. From her sister-in-law, she had heard they were decent, deeply devoted people whose sole failure in life was raising a useless son. How could she shake them up enough to swear off marrying her into their family?

要不是陈江林提前叮嘱过，姚轻琰真想拿出婆婆的姿态，好好教训一下晚辈。
Scanning the courtyard, she spotted a square dressed stone beside a flowerbed, a bit bigger than an inkstone. "Hongdou, fetch me that stone."

千里寻见二位不说话，只顾打量她，将二郎腿翘得更高：“你们就是陈浩宇的爹娘？也就是我未来的公爹和婆婆？”
Hongdou dashed over, scooped the block up with both hands—not too heavy—balanced it on one palm, wiped the dust off with her handkerchief, and presented it to her mistress.

陈江林没有回答而是问道：“你是朱家姑娘？”
Qianli Xun tossed the stone once in her palm. Decent heft—plenty to put a scare into Chen Haoyu's parents.

“老娘行不改名，坐不改姓，朱淑真是也！”千里寻故意装出女侠客的口吻。
Just then Qinggua came hurrying out at word of the ruckus. "Miss Zhu, what is going on? My young master is not home today. If you are mad at him, wait until he gets back to vent!"

陈江林夫妇对视一番，这“老娘”的称呼实在与大家闺秀的形象不符。
Qianli Xun put on a stern scowl. "Who said I came for your young master? If I want to thrash him, I can do it anywhere—why bother coming to Chen Manor? I came today to meet my future in-laws so they can get a good look at the bride they are getting, lest they get a shock later!"

姚轻琰问：“你今日来所谓何事？”
Speechless, Qinggua shrank to the side. This future young madam swaggering around like a street boss felt worlds apart from the girl he had met at the chuiwan grounds—was she even the same person?

千里寻放下二郎腿，她实在不习惯这个动作，继续掂着石头：“老娘快跟陈浩宇成亲，成亲前，让二位见识见识未来儿媳妇的真实面目，省得日后后悔，如果看不上老娘，现在退婚也不迟。”
Tossing the stone up and down, Qianli Xun crossed one leg over the other on purpose as movement caught the corner of her eye. A man and woman approached surrounded by maids, roughly the same age as Zhu Shuzhen's parents—Chen Haoyu's father and mother. Proper etiquette called for rising to bow, yet she had come to pick a fight; letting them see an insolent brat might speed the breakup along. As they closed in, she put on a sneer and eyed the elders sideways.

提到“退婚”二字，陈江林与姚轻琰再次看一眼对方，互相会心一笑。
Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan sized up their future daughter-in-law from head to toe. Having met Miss Zhu a handful of times before, they remembered an educated, gentle, graceful young lady. Strip away the stone in her hand, the sidelong glare, and the cocky crossed leg—have her stand and greet them as Aunt and Uncle—and she would still look every inch a noble daughter. Had Chen Jianglin not briefed her beforehand, Yao Qingyan would have stepped right into her mother-in-law role and dressed the girl down.

陈江林说：“今日我与你未来的婆婆算是见识过，你可以回去了。”
Seeing the pair study her in silence, Qianli Xun hiked her crossed leg even higher. "You two are Chen Haoyu's parents? My future father-in-law and mother-in-law?"

如此轻松回应，倒将千里寻整懵，目的还未达到，岂能离开？
Without answering, Chen Jianglin asked, "You are Miss Zhu?"

“二位还没说能不能接受老娘这个样子。”
"Same name walking, same surname sitting—Zhu Shuzhen at your service!" Qianli Xun barked in a mock wandering-heroine swagger.

姚轻琰笑道：“能不能接受由两家父母说了算，你只管回去等消息。”
Husband and wife traded a glance. That brash tone fit zero part of a sheltered daughter.

难道今日表演又失败？不能就这么结束。
"What brings you here today?" Yao Qingyan asked.

千里寻瞧向手中的石头，也不能白白掂这么久：“二位先别赶人，既然老娘来都来了，也得让二位见识见识我的厉害。”
Dropping her crossed leg—the posture cramped her anyway—Qianli Xun kept hefting the stone. "Since I am about to marry Chen Haoyu, I came to show you my true colors ahead of time so neither of you cries regret later. If you do not like what you see, it is not too late to call off the wedding right now."

她站起来，左手托着石头，右手攥紧拳头，一拳朝那石头打去，只见石头被打成两半，掉落在地上……
At the words "call off the wedding," Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan exchanged a knowing smile.

陈浩东本打算去陈家铺子走走，刚走到离大门不远处，瞧见眼前的一幕，躲起来观看，不由慨叹：“如此厉害，难怪浩宇不愿娶。可颜兄却看上她，实在令人费解。”
"Well, your future mother-in-law and I have seen plenty today," Chen Jianglin said breezily. "You may head home now."

陈浩东继续偷瞧着，希望能看出些名堂来。
That unbothered brush-off threw Qianli Xun off stride. Leave before getting what she came for? Not a chance! "Neither of you said whether you can stomach me like this!"

当朱家姑娘用拳头向石头砸去时，姚轻琰吓得闭上眼睛，再次睁开时，看到地上掉落的两半石头，甚是惊讶，一个姑娘家怎么这么大力气？
"Whether we accept you is between the two sets of parents," Yao Qingyan smiled. "Go home and wait for word."

千里寻拍拍手掌的灰尘：“二位也看到，老娘的拳头力气不小，专打瞧不上的人。不瞒二位，在这临安城，老娘最瞧不上的人便是陈浩宇，只怕嫁到陈家，您家儿子没好日子过。二位也别犹豫，现在就给我个答复，是愿意接受老娘嫁到陈家日日暴揍陈浩宇，还是找我爹好好合计合计退婚之事？”
Was today's whole performance about to flop too? She was not letting the curtain drop yet. Eyeing the block in her palm—she had not been hefting it for nothing—she rose to her feet. "Hold off on chasing me out! Since I came all this way, let me show you what I can really do."

陈江林犹豫一会儿说道：“亲事是两家大人商议定下，岂能说退就退？浩宇向来纨绔，我这当父亲的管不了他，只盼你嫁过来替我好好管教。”
Cradling the stone on her left palm, she cocked her right fist and drove it straight down. The square stone cracked clean in two and clattered to the flagstones.

千里寻着实佩服陈浩宇的父母四两拨千斤的功力，几句话搞得她白白努力，此计不成再施一计：“既然你不爱护自己的儿子，老娘又何必多劝，不如取来纸笔，白纸黑字写下朱淑真若打死陈浩宇不必偿命，二位还要签字画押，我便即刻离开！”
Setting out to inspect the Chen family shops, Chen Haodong had reached the gate just in time to catch the spectacle and ducked out of sight to watch. "Fierce as a beast," he marveled under his breath. "No wonder Haoyu wants no part of her. Yet Brother Yan fell for her? Baffling." He stayed tucked in the shadows, eager to see how the showdown played out.

陈江林与姚轻琰还未来得及为难要不要写，传来夏老太太的声音：“不许写！”
Yao Qingyan had squeezed her eyes shut the instant the fist came down; opening them to see two jagged halves of stone on the ground left her stunned. How did a slip of a girl pack strength like that?

众人都向夏老太太瞧去，她满面怒色，拄着拐杖，被丫鬟搀扶，急急忙忙颤颤巍巍走来。
Qianli Xun dusted off her hands. "You saw it yourselves. My fists hit hard, and they specialize in people I despise. I will level with you: in all of Lin'an, the man I despise most is Chen Haoyu. Marry me into this house and your son will not see a single peaceful day. Save the hesitation and give me an answer right now: do you want me marrying in to beat Chen Haoyu black and blue every day, or will you go talk to my father about calling off this betrothal?"

千里寻为自己将老人家激怒感到不安，可事情已发展到这种地步，她不得不坚持下去，也许老太太到来能进一步促成退婚一事。
Chen Jianglin paused a beat before answering. "This match was sealed by the elders of both houses; it is not something to cancel on a whim. Haoyu has always been an unruly rake, beyond a father's control. I am counting on you to marry in and whip him into shape for me."

姚轻琰迎上去：“母亲，您怎么来了？”
Qianli Xun almost had to applaud the pair's slick deflection—turning a thousand pounds of force aside with four ounces of effort and leaving her swinging at air. Plan A dead? Time for Plan B! "If you care so little for your own son, I will not waste my breath warning you! Bring brush and paper right now! Put it down in black and white that if Zhu Shuzhen beats Chen Haoyu to death, she owes no life in return, sign it and press your thumbprints, and I will walk straight out!"

“我怎么来了？你还有脸问？发生这么大的事，你们二人也不知道派人知会我一声，多亏我听下人议论才知道有人闹到陈府。”老太太边走边批评。
Before Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan could weigh how to respond, a sharp voice rang out: "Write no such thing!"

已有下人搬来椅子，身边的丫鬟将拐杖接过，扶老太太坐下。
Every head turned toward Old Madam Xia, marching up in a fury on her walking stick, supported by maids as she hurried forward with trembling steps.

夏老太太坐好后，严厉地问：“你就是朱家姑娘？江南有名的女词人？人称幽栖居士？”
A flicker of unease pricked Qianli Xun at riling up an elderly woman, yet she had gone too far to retreat—and maybe the grandmother's arrival would seal the breakup at last.

千里寻回道：“老人家在上，正是在下。”
"Mother, why did you come out?" Yao Qingyan rushed up to meet her.

“哼！今日闹到陈家所为何事？”
"Why did I come out? You have the nerve to ask?" Old Madam Xia snapped as she walked. "Something this huge happens and neither of you sends word! Good thing I overheard the servants gossiping, or I would never have known someone stormed Chen Manor!"

陈江林打算向母亲说明，被老太太阻止：“不用你回答，我要听她说。”
Servants scrambled to set down a chair; a maid took her cane and eased her onto the seat.

“老娘刚才已解释过，不妨向老人家再解释一遍。”千里寻见老太太听到她自称“老娘”，面露不满，她则甚是满意，要的就是这个效果，于是继续说，“一来，让陈家见识见识我的真实面目，省得嫁过来，陈家人后悔；二来，想告诉陈家，若愿退婚，现在还来得及。”
Settling in, Old Madam Xia glared daggers. "So you are Miss Zhu? Jiangnan's famed poetess? The one they call Youqi Jushi?"

“你做人倒坦率，不欺不瞒，比你爹强。你爹仗着自己是临安知府，把嫁不出去的女儿往陈家塞，却从来没告诉我们众人称赞的幽栖居士竟然是个悍妇，难怪浩宇死活不愿娶你。今日我当着众人的面，给你个答复，这婚陈家必然要退，麻烦你回去知会一声知府大人。至于你爹当时逼迫浩宇娶你的原因，我们陈家愿意用金钱偿还，让你爹出个价。”
"Respects to you, Elder Madam. That would be me," Qianli Xun answered.

夏老太太一口气说那么多话，清清楚楚告知已同意退婚，千里寻兴奋不已。
"Hmph! What business brought you raising a storm at Chen Manor?"

可也疑惑，听老太太所言，父亲逼迫陈浩宇娶她是有原因，陈家竟然愿意用钱偿还，到底是什么原因值得陈家如此做？
Chen Jianglin stepped forward to explain, only for his mother to cut him off. "Not a word from you. I want to hear it from her."

一时想不明白，千里寻也不愿多想，同意退婚才是关键：“老太太就是明事理，比你儿子和儿媳强多了，淑真在此谢过，我这就回去告诉父亲。”
"I already explained once, yet I do not mind telling Elder Madam again," Qianli Xun said, well pleased to see the old woman bristle at her brash swagger. "First, so the Chens can see my true face before the wedding and not cry foul afterward. Second, to tell you straight: if you want to cancel this match, there is still time."

千里寻说完，准备转身离开。
"At least you are blunt and hide nothing—better than your father," Old Madam Xia scoffed. "Using his rank as Prefect of Lin'an to dump a daughter nobody else wants onto our doorstep, never once breathing a word that the celebrated Youqi Jushi is a raging shrew! No wonder Haoyu fought tooth and nail against marrying you. Right here in front of everyone, I give you my answer: the Chen family is calling off this match! Go back and notify Prefect Zhu yourself. Whatever score your father held over Haoyu to force this betrothal, the Chen family will settle it in coin—tell him to name his price!"

“慢着！”陈江林阻止道，“朱家姑娘先别着急走，退婚之事有待商议。”
Hearing Old Madam Xia spell out the cancellation in plain, ringing words sent Qianli Xun's spirits soaring—even as one line snagged her ear. Her father had held something over Chen Haoyu to force the betrothal, something the Chens would pay silver to settle? What on earth had happened?

千里寻站住：“怎么？又想反悔？”
No time to unravel riddles right now; ending the betrothal came first. "Elder Madam talks real sense—leagues ahead of your son and daughter-in-law! Shuzhen thanks you, and I will go tell my father right away." She spun to leave.

陈江林面向老太太 ：“婚姻之事是人生大事，岂可说退就退？还望母亲三思。”
"Hold on!" Chen Jianglin stepped in. "Do not rush off, Miss Zhu. Calling off a betrothal takes deliberation."

老太太生气道：“有什么好三思的？你们两个就知道自己恩恩爱爱，不把浩宇的幸福当回事，再说浩宇未来要承担大任，怎能娶个名声不好的进门？”
Qianli Xun halted. "What now? Going back on it already?"

陈浩东听到老太太的话，心中不由紧张起来，难道奶奶有心将陈家家业交到浩宇手中，才同意退了这门亲事？
Chen Jianglin turned to his mother. "Marriage is a lifelong matter, not to be tossed aside lightly. I beg Mother to reconsider."

陈江林继续劝：“母亲，浩宇根本无法承担大任，即使母亲有这心，我也不会同意，我只求他娶妻生子，安稳度过一生即可。”
"Reconsider what?!" Old Madam Xia snapped. "All you two care about is billing and cooing with each other, never sparing a thought for Haoyu's happiness! Besides, Haoyu has heavy responsibilities ahead of him—how could he take a wife with a tarnished name?"

听到大伯的话，陈浩东紧张的心情稍微轻松一些。
Overhearing from the shadows, Chen Haodong's heart skipped a beat. Did Grandmother intend to hand the Chen family business to Haoyu, and was that why she wanted this match broken?

老太太依然坚持：“即使不能承担大任，也不能娶朱家姑娘……”
"Mother, Haoyu is nowhere near fit for heavy responsibilities," Chen Jianglin argued. "Even if you wish it, I will never agree. All I ask is that he marry, have children, and live out a peaceful life."

千里寻趁机搭话：“是，绝不能娶，我名声不好，再说我跟陈浩宇合不来，只怕嫁到您家三天两头揍他，老太太如此心疼孙子，肯定不愿见他被我揍，对吧？”
His uncle's words loosened the knot in Chen Haodong's chest a notch.

老太太更是担心不已：“你们两个听听，她都说些什么话！这样的人怎能娶到陈家？”
"Even without heavy responsibilities, he is not marrying Miss Zhu!" Old Madam Xia stood firm.

千里寻更是开心，有老太太在，嫁到陈家之事八成成不了：“陈伯伯，还是听老人家的，与我父亲好好商议退婚之事，我在望月轩静候佳音。”
Qianli Xun piled right on. "Exactly—do not marry me! My name is mud, I cannot stand Chen Haoyu, and once I move in I will probably thrash him every other day. Elder Madam dotes on her grandson so much—you surely cannot bear watching me pound him, right?"

千里寻使出拳头，将椅子一拳砸烂，带红豆扬长而去。
Alarm deepened on Old Madam Xia's face. "Listen to the words coming out of her mouth! How could we ever welcome someone like that into Chen Manor?"

大家再次被她拳头的力度镇住，一个个眼睛瞪得老大，嘴巴微微张开。
Thrilled—with the matriarch firmly on her side, the wedding was nine-tenths dead—Qianli Xun grinned. "Listen to Elder Madam, Uncle Chen, and go settle the breakup with my father. I will wait for the good news at Wangyue Pavilion."

老太太大声说：“瞧瞧！哪有个大家闺秀的样子！乔家不愿娶，想来祸害陈家，没门儿！”
Cocking her fist, she brought it smashing down on the chair she had sat in, splintering it to pieces, then swaggered out the gate with Hongdou.

陈江林与姚轻琰很无奈，没想到朱家姑娘为退婚，一点儿不顾及大家闺秀的形象。
Every onlooker froze, eyes bugging wide and mouths hanging open at the sheer power in her punch.

姚轻琰向陈江林使使眼色，陈江林赶紧向老太太说：“母亲，您进屋，我们好好商议此事。”
"Look at that!" Old Madam Xia cried. "Not a shred of breeding! The Qiaos refused to take her, so they try offloading the menace onto us? Over my dead body!"

“有什么好商议的？退婚就是！”
Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan exchanged helpless sighs—who knew Miss Zhu would torch every scrap of a lady's dignity just to wriggle out of a match?

丫鬟把拐杖送到老太太手边，她扶着拐杖努力站起来。
Catching his wife's glance, Chen Jianglin stepped to his mother's side. "Come inside, Mother. Let us talk this over properly."

姚轻琰上前去扶婆婆：“母亲，就是商量退婚之事，总不能因退婚伤了两家和气，所以得想一个万全的法子。”
"What is there to talk over? Cancel the match and be done with it!"

“既如此，就去你们屋里坐会儿。”
A maid handed over her cane, and the old woman struggled to her feet. Yao Qingyan hurried to take her arm. "Mother, we mean discussing how to cancel it cleanly. We cannot ruin goodwill between the two households, so we need a thorough plan."

得到老太太允许，陈江林也过去扶住母亲，往他居住的院子而去。
"Very well. I will sit in your rooms a while."

陈浩东心想，大伯并没有让浩宇继承家业的想法，可老太太却如此想，而长辈中老太太的意见又至关重要。
Supported by both son and daughter-in-law, Old Madam Xia headed toward their courtyard.

父亲携母亲代表陈家去参加乔家的婚礼还未归，无人商议，还是先去铺子再说。
Back in the shadows, Chen Haodong frowned. Eldest Uncle had no mind to give Haoyu the business, yet Grandmother clearly did—and among the elders, her vote carried enormous weight. With his parents away representing the Chens at the Qiao wedding, there was nobody to consult yet; he would head to the shops first.

目送众人离开后，陈浩东才走到大门口，下人正在收拾被劈成两半的石头和砸烂的椅子。陈浩东心中很是不屑：“真是个活脱脱的母夜叉，浩宇要是娶了她，有得受，哼！”
Once the courtyard cleared, he strolled past the gate, where servants were sweeping up split stone and splintered wood. "A real she-demon," Chen Haodong sneered under his breath. "Haoyu is in for misery if he marries her. Hmph!"

下人看到大公子，都有礼貌地打招呼，陈浩东并未理会，吹着口哨走出大门。
Ignoring the servants' polite greetings, he whistled his way out the gate.
