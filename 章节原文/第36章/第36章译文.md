# Chapter 36

Under hundreds of eyes, Qiao Wenxian stayed still and murmured low, "Don't panic. The poem sits right on the painting and mirrors its scenes; once I unpack the verse, you'll have plenty to say about the painting. Hurry up and thank His Majesty with me."

Walking together up to the great table beneath every stare, Qiao Wenxian bowed: "Thank you for your grace, Your Majesty."

Shen Jianchen echoed, "Thank you for your grace, Imperial Father."

"Good! Which of you goes first?" Zhao Ji asked.

"This humble subject always loves going first lest someone steal my words. Don't compete with me, Prince Yi!" Qiao Wenxian volunteered smooth.

Staring blank at the scroll with zero ideas yet—and grateful for Wenyuan covering him—Shen Jianchen kept a calm face. "Wouldn't dream of stealing your turn, Wenyuan. Go ahead."

As His Majesty and senior ministers stepped back to clear the view, Qiao Wenxian bowed, took in "Winter Plum and Mountain Birds," studied the inscribed verse a beat, and began: "Your Majesty's calligraphy grows ever more vigorous and crisp without losing supple beauty; every stroke balances iron bone with graceful flow, radiating imperial grandeur that leaves this subject in awe..."

Pleased, Zhao Ji nudged him, "Enough praising my brushwork; unpack the poem."

"Yes, Your Majesty!" Bowing again, Qiao Wenxian went on, "Your Majesty's verse pairs mountain birds with winter plum—birds poised in ease, blossoms unfolding in soft grace, both rich in charm. Purest genius lies in the two words 'boasts' and 'displays': 'boasts' captures stillness inside motion, dignity within leisure; 'displays' captures vibrant life stirring within stillness! Motion and stillness weave together into boundless poetic mood. Moving to the third line reveals a pact sealed in ink and color, bursting with wit. And the closing line, 'For a thousand autumns, till our heads turn white,' lifts the whole poem on three layered meanings: first, faithful love between partners growing old together; second, Your Majesty's lifelong devotion poured into calligraphy and painting; third, Your Majesty's steadfast pursuit of a thousand-year imperial legacy—sheer sovereign majesty that leaves Wenyuan humbled!"

Delighted, Zhao Ji praised him aloud: "Nobody understands me like Wenyuan! Matchless gift for verse—reward him three pure gold bowls!"

Liang Shicheng stepped up presenting a brocade box: "Guard His Majesty's golden bowls well, Young Master Qiao." Bowing thanks, Qiao Wenxian accepted the box.

Listening beside him, Shen Jianchen marveled that Wenyuan squeezed an essay out of twenty characters, while scoffing in silence at that closing flattery: Keep blowing smoke! Northern Song is about to fall on this fool's watch—some "thousand-year legacy" while propping up crooks! Hearing gold bowls handed out for kissing up annoyed him even more: Three pure gold bowls just for praising a poem?! Gold ought to reward soldiers bleeding for the empire!

Right then His Majesty turned to him: "Now, Prince Yi—what do you make of my painting?"

Still drawing a blank yet calm as water—not worth stressing over an incompetent emperor—Shen Jianchen answered light: "Imperial Father, illness kept me from books; I know nothing of poetry or painting and fear clumsy words would stain your masterpiece. Better invite someone learned so your painstaking art gets its due."

Snickers rippled among the surrounding ministers.

Zhao Ji's face darkened. "Refusing my command before the whole court?"

"Your son wouldn't dare."

"Then critique it right now! Impress me and rewards await; disappoint me and never set foot in this palace again!"

Couldn't care less about palace invites—might as well wing a few lines and wrap up! "Yes, Imperial Father."

Stepping closer to "Winter Plum and Mountain Birds," he stared, still blank; even winging it needed an opening hook.

Just then a memory surfaced: once Qianli Xun went to an art gallery, and he trailed behind faking a chance encounter to spend the afternoon with her. Strolling through the rooms, he only watched Qianli Xun and ignored every canvas—until she asked his take on one painting and he quipped, "Whatever you think, I think!" She had scolded him for pretending to care about art, then lectured him like a teacher: "To read a painting, latch onto the visual subjects; every small detail reflects the painter's mind. And never parrot others—say what you see and think yourself; that's what makes it interesting!"

Light burst across Shen Jianchen's mind! Glancing around the circle of officials pressing near, aside from Wenyuan's genuine worry, every single face—His Majesty included—smirked waiting to watch him make a fool of himself!

Those smug grins killed any plan to half-ass it! Pulling every ounce of wit from memory and locking his gaze onto the painting, a faint grin touched his mouth as he began: "First, your son sees orchids blooming pure in deep valleys, untainted by worldly dust. Painting orchids expresses Imperial Father's love for noble souls pure as orchids. A man is what he loves; a sovereign pure as orchids will never keep treacherous flatterers close—how could our Great Song fail to prosper?!"

"Well said!" Zhao Ji blurted out. "Keep going!"

Typical incompetent emperor, eats flattery right up! Shen Jianchen smirked to himself. Fine, I'll lay it on thick! Think I made CEO young on talent alone? Polishing egos is a core skill!

"Rising past the orchids stands a winter plum, bare of leaves yet, half buds tight, half petals open bright and proud. Winter plum blooms in the twelfth lunar month, deepest bitter frost..." Suddenly a poem memorized in school popped straight into his head: "It brings to mind a lyric: 'Wind and rain escorted Spring's departure, / Flying snow welcomes Spring's return. / On the ice-clad rock rising high and sheer / A flower blooms sweet and fair. / Sweet and fair, she craves not Spring for herself alone, / To be the harbinger of Spring she is content. / When the mountain flowers are in full bloom / She will smile mingling in their midst.' [TN: Official Foreign Languages Press translation] That lyric reminds us to hold unbroken courage and bright hope amid harsh winter—just like the winter plum under Imperial Father's brush!"

Overwhelmed, Zhao Ji stepped forward and gripped Prince Yi's hand. "Never dreamed you'd see such depth—truly my own son! And what a lyric you composed! 'On the ice-clad rock rising high and sheer, a flower blooms sweet and fair' captures the very heart of my winter plum! What else do you see? Tell me!"

Sorry, Grandpa Mao, borrowing your masterpiece! This clueless emperor thinks I wrote it—please don't blame me from above! Beside them, Qiao Wenxian murmured under his breath, "Prince Yi sure hides his talents deep!"

Letting go of his hand, Zhao Ji listened rapt.

Studying the scroll closer, Shen Jianchen went on, "Perched atop the plum bough sit two white-headed bulbuls facing opposite ways, calm and composed. To your son, the front bird represents Imperial Father, while the rear bird represents the empire and its common people. A sovereign rests his back upon land and subjects so his rule stands rock-solid; Imperial Father is just such a ruler, backed by Song hills and rivers, holding millions of people in his heart..."

"Magnificent!" Zhao Ji exclaimed again!

"Knowing nothing of painting or layout, your son still senses complete harmony across the entire scroll—every branch staggered in balance, upper and lower echoing each other, verse and painting intertwined so removing either would mar perfection. Even the square vermilion seals belong: crimson stamps poised high above bring the composition alive, showing a sovereign exalted on high whose heart stays forever bound to his subjects!"

Zhao Ji beamed wide. "Unschooled in painting yet gifted with keen eyes—your brilliance rivals Wenyuan's! Reward Prince Yi ten pure gold bowls!"

Pure hot air hailed as brilliance matching Wenyuan?! Hopeless sovereign! Ignoring Huizong's glow, Shen Jianchen shot Wenyuan a cheeky wink, earning a discreet thumbs-up in return.

Liang Shicheng hurried over bearing an even larger brocade box: "Ten gold bowls for Prince Yi—keep them safe!"

Accepting the box, bowing thanks to the fool on the throne, and backing up beside Qiao Wenxian, Shen Jianchen whispered, "More gold bowls than you—do I have to lug this thing around all day?"

"Watch a pro," Qiao Wenxian grinned, waving a palace maid over. "Pass both my box and Prince Yi's to our guards and have them placed inside our carriage at Xihua Gate."

"Yes, Young Master." Taking both boxes, the maid withdrew.

"Won't somebody swipe them out of the carriage?"

"Look at you turning miser—ten gold bowls bought you off fast! Relax, guards stand watch beside the carriage; nobody dares touch them."

Just then Liang Shicheng called aloud across the hall: "To celebrate Genyue's completion, the palace has arranged a cuju match! Please proceed to the cuju field!"

Set just a short walk from the banquet hall, the cuju field drew everyone on foot except His Majesty in his sedan. A shaded pavilion held the sovereign's seat before a folding screen painted with auspicious clouds and cranes, a plain rosewood chair centered in front; rows of guest chairs paired with small tea-and-snack tables ringed the field.

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian chose seats as His Majesty settled in with Liang Shicheng standing beside him. The Crown Prince, Grand Commandant Gao, Cai Jing, and Chancellor Zeng sat close by beaming, while imperial consorts, princes, and court ladies filled the surrounding tiers.

"Is Grand Commandant Gao actually good at cuju?" Shen Jianchen asked.

"Top-tier—master level!" Qiao Wenxian replied.

"Getting on in years though. Hasn't he lost a step?"

"Sharp as ever—not that old yet!"

Mid-chat, Lady Wei walked over and took the seat beside Prince Yi. "Prince Yi is the craftiest dark horse I've ever met!"

"How so?" Qiao Wenxian asked.

"Acting clueless about poetry and painting, claiming he'd barely read a book, then winning praise after praise from His Majesty and walking off with ten gold bowls—outdoing even Young Master Qiao! If that isn't hiding your strength, what is?"

Lowering his voice with a smirk, Shen Jianchen said, "Truth be told, Lady Wei, master the art of flattery, and both your brain and your tongue run like greased lightning!"

"Don't buy for one second you only know flattery!"

"Believe what you want, facts are facts—plus I had pointers from a friend."

"Pointers on flattery?"

"Nope. Pointers on how to look at paintings."

Qiao Wenxian frowned. "Sick in bed till days ago—aside from me, what other friend do you even have? Don't recall teaching you art critique!"

"Wrong!" Lady Wei chimed in. "Who says you're Prince Yi's only friend? There's his new friend right here—though I didn't coach him either, Young Master Qiao can vouch!"

A shadow crossed Shen Jianchen's eyes. "A friend from another world—someone I can't find now."

"Another world?" Trading amused glances, Lady Wei and Qiao Wenxian wrote it off as princely nonsense and let the subject drop.

Drums boomed—the cuju exhibition began, drawing every eye onto the pitch. Dead center stood the tall netted goal ring called the Fengliu Yan. Two teams of five marched out, one squad in blue uniforms, one in red, taking positions on either side of the goal. Bowing to each other, at the next drumbeat they plunged into fierce action.

Watching cuju live for the first time, Shen Jianchen saw instant kinship with soccer—except the ball could never touch the ground, juggled teammate to teammate until passed to the Lead Striker to volley clean through the elevated ring! Grinning inside, he thought: I'm a soccer beast—keepie-uppies were child's play, and add Shen Fist power on top? Easy!

Glancing toward Gao Qiu watching rapt, his Water Margin hero complex flared straight up: Might have let you slide if we'd never met—now that we have, time to teach you a lesson for the hundred and eight heroes!

Spotting a player poised to loft a pass to his Lead Striker, Shen Jianchen kicked off with light-foot skill, vaulted straight onto the field, intercepted midair before the striker blinked, and blasted the ball dead-center through the goal ring!

Gasps froze the crowd before cheers rippled out. "Prince Yi's skills keep getting wilder!" Qiao Wenxian applauded.

"Heard court whispers a divine sage taught Prince Yi martial arts after death—looks true!" Lady Wei marveled.

His Majesty's voice boomed down: "Prince Yi! What is the meaning of this?!"

Every player froze at the interruption. Shen Jianchen bowed: "Imperial Father, your son itches to play cuju too!"

Zhao Ji laughed. "Haha, wanted to play? Should've said so—plenty of room! Why pull a stunt like that?"

"Your son heard Grand Commandant Gao's cuju skills are unmatched and longs to test himself against him—begging Imperial Father's permission!"

Zhao Ji looked to Gao Qiu. "Grand Commandant Gao, Prince Yi just came back from death—indulge him with a friendly match!"

With His Majesty speaking, Gao Qiu could not refuse; rising with a bow, he replied, "Your minister obeys."

Shen Jianchen slipped on a red jersey, Gao Qiu donned blue, each taking Lead Striker for his squad. Drums sounded—game on! Red launched a "Meteor Chasing the Moon" combo; resting one hand on a teammate's shoulder, Shen Jianchen vaulted and drilled home the opening goal! Blue countered with "Fallen Flowers and Flowing Water," Gao Qiu answering right back with a crisp score. Seven or eight rounds later, both teams stood dead even.

Old or not, Gao Qiu's footwork was wicked; beating him on pure finesse looked tough. Speed and raw force would crack him! Shen Jianchen called a brief halftime breather, His Majesty nodding assent.

Huddling his squad during the break, Shen Jianchen issued crisp orders: "No matter how wild the pass, I'll catch it. Quit wasting time juggling; ping the ball straight to me fast as you can!" Never having played under a striker like this, the teammates gaped yet complied—Prince Yi called the shots, plus it saved them tons of work!

Drums rolled again and the second half exploded! Every rapid pass from red jerseys met Shen Jianchen's foot on the volley, rockets drilling clean through the goal ring until red jumped two goals ahead! Flustered, Gao Qiu fired a "Meteor Chasing the Moon" strike straight at the ring—only for Shen Jianchen's counter-kick to slam the ball dead in midair right outside the net, knocking it down to the turf! Three straight shots blocked cold!

Out of breath, Gao Qiu cupped his hands in defeat. "Prince Yi strikes too fast and fierce! This old minister's strength fails him—I concede, I concede!"

Stepping close, Shen Jianchen asked cool, "Grand Commandant Gao, ever heard of the hundred and eight heroes of Mount Liang?"

Blinking baffled, Gao Qiu replied, "Your Highness means the Marshes of Mount Liang? Bandits swarm there year round—never heard of any 'heroes.'"

"Guess we mean different folks. Either way, those heroes asked me to test your mettle for them."

Totally perplexed—this revived prince spoke in riddles nonstop!—Gao Qiu bowed: "Your minister takes his leave, Prince Yi."

Turning toward the throne pavilion, Shen Jianchen bowed. "Did your son's footwork please Imperial Father?"

Beaming wide, Zhao Ji laughed. "Never knew your cuju ran this sharp! Proof that divine sage truly trained you! Since your martial prowess shines so bright, I hereby appoint you Military Commissioner of Lin'an! Stay a few days in Bianliang, then head south to take office!"

Floor-dropped—one game of cuju just landed him a regional military post! No wonder Gao Qiu rose through the ranks kicking balls! With His Majesty's decree spoken, he bowed low: "Your son thanks Imperial Father for this boundless grace!"

Both Qiao Wenxian and Lady Wei beamed with joy for him; what a jackpot trip to the palace!
