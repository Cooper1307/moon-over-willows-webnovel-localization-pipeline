# Chapter 36

Qiao Wenxian didn't dare move with everyone staring at them. He dropped his voice to a whisper. "Don't worry. The poetry prompt is on the painting, and it's about the painting. Once I finish interpreting the poem, you'll come to savor the painting yourself. Hurry up and come with me to thank the Emperor for selecting us."

The two walked over to the square table under everyone's gaze. Qiao Wenxian offered, "Thank you, Your Majesty, for your recognition."

Shen Jianchen echoed, "Thank you, Father, for your recognition."

Zhao Ji beamed. "Excellent! Who will go first?"

Qiao Wenxian replied, "I'd prefer to go first, simply because I'm afraid someone else will say what I was going to say. I hope Prince Yi won't compete with me."

Shen Jianchen stared at the painting on the table, utterly at a loss. Why would he rush to go first? Besides, Wenxian was only covering for him. He schooled his features into calm. "Compete with Wenxian? Wouldn't dream of it. After you."

The Emperor and senior officials made way. Qiao Wenxian bowed and walked up to *Winter Plum and Mountain Birds*. He studied the painting, his gaze landing on the poem. After contemplating for a moment, he said, "Your Majesty's calligraphy has grown even more vigorous and upright, yet without losing its grace. Every stroke reveals elegance within strength, and each character exudes imperial dignity. Your servant is truly impressed..."

Zhao Ji chuckled, pleased. "Don't just focus on admiring Our calligraphy. Interpret the poem."

"Only upon reading the next line does one realize it's a promise through painting, instantly brimming with charm.

Zhao Ji couldn't help praising, "Wenxian understands Us best. Your talent for savoring poetry is unmatched. We award you three solid gold bowls!"

Liang Shicheng brought over a brocade box and handed it to Qiao Wenxian. "Young Master Qiao, do take good care of the gold bowls bestowed by the Emperor."

Qiao Wenxian thanked the Emperor and put away the box.

Shen Jianchen listened and watched from the side, genuinely impressed that Wenxian could extract so much meaning from just four lines of poetry. But when Wenxian finished speaking, he thought to himself, *Go ahead and flatter the Emperor. The Northern Song will soon fall into the hands of an incompetent ruler. What lasting legacy, what faithful pursuit? If he truly cared, he wouldn't be promoting treacherous officials.*

Hearing about the reward, Shen Jianchen bristled. *Wenxian flatters the Emperor's poem and gets gold bowls? This ruler is truly hopeless! Such awards should go to those who risk their lives for the country and build real merit!*

Just as he was thinking, the Emperor's voice came, "What are Prince Yi's thoughts on Our painting?"

Shen Jianchen still had no idea, but he was no longer nervous. He saw no reason to get tense over a witless sovereign. He remarked, "Father, your son has been ill and hasn't read much, let alone understand poetry, calligraphy, or painting. I'm afraid my words would defile Father's artwork. Perhaps someone more knowledgeable should savor it, so as not to waste Father's efforts."

The surrounding officials exchanged knowing smirks.

Zhao Ji's face darkened. "You dare refuse Us in public?"

"Your son wouldn't dare."

"Since you don't dare, then study it in earnest. If you speak well, We will naturally reward you. If not, don't bother coming to the palace again."

Shen Jianchen couldn't care less about being at court. He'd just say a few words to get by. He replied, "Yes."

He approached *Winter Plum and Mountain Birds*, still unable to make sense of it. Even if he meant to talk nonsense, he needed to know how to start.

Suddenly, he remembered a time when Qianli Xun went to an art exhibition. He found out and secretly followed, pretending it was a coincidence to get the chance to be with her. In the gallery, he only focused on admiring Qianli Xun, not paying any attention to the paintings at all. When Qianli Xun asked his opinion on a painting, he answered, "Your view is my view."

Qianli Xun criticized him for not understanding painting at all, yet pretending to be refined and coming to admire it. Then she lectured him like a teacher, "To understand what a painting is saying, you must grasp the artistic imagery. Every subtle detail of the artistic imagery reflects the artist's mind. Also, when studying a painting, never parrot what others say. You must speak of what you see and think—that's what makes it interesting."

At this memory, Shen Jianchen suddenly had an idea. He glanced at everyone who had the right or opportunity to approach the table. Apart from Wenxian, who was genuinely worried about him, everyone else was waiting to see him fail—including the Emperor.

Their expectant faces made Shen Jianchen abandon the idea of bluffing his way through. He searched his memory, drew on everything he'd learned, and looked at the painting again. At last something came to him. A smile touched his lips as he spoke, "Your son first sees the orchid. The orchid dwells in an empty valley, unstained by worldly dust. Father painting orchids must express your admiration for those with noble character like the orchid. What a person likes reflects what they are. An emperor of such noble character as my Father would certainly never promote treacherous officials. How could our Great Song not prosper!"

Zhao Ji burst out, "Well said! What else?"

Shen Jianchen thought, *Truly an incompetent ruler—he loves flattery. In that case, I'll praise you properly. I, Shen Jianchen, am the best at flattery. Otherwise, how could I become a company CEO at such a young age? Hmph! Besides personal ability, flattery skills are also essential.*

"Following the orchid upward, there's a wintersweet plum. The leaves haven't grown yet. Some buds are about to bloom, while others have already opened, incredibly beautiful. The wintersweet blooms in the twelfth lunar month, which is very cold..." As he spoke, a poem by Chairman Mao—one he had memorized in school—rose to mind. "I can't help but think of a poem: *'Wind and rain escort Spring's return, / Flying snow welcomes Spring's arrival. / Though a hundred-foot cliff is clad in ice, / There remains a sprig of blossoms, elegant and nice. / Elegant, yet she vies not for Spring's grace, / But only heralds Spring's embrace. / When mountain flowers bloom in full array, / She will smile amongst them, hidden away.'* This poem tells us that in harsh conditions, one should be like the plum blossom, maintaining an unyielding spirit and optimistic attitude. The plum blossom under Father's brush is the same."

Zhao Ji couldn't help stepping forward and grasping Prince Yi's hand. "I didn't expect you to see such depth. Truly worthy of being Our son. Your poem is also well written. 'Though a hundred-foot cliff is clad in ice, there remains a sprig of blossoms, elegant and nice' captures the true meaning of the wintersweet under Our brush. What else do you see? Continue telling Us."

Shen Jianchen thought, *Grandpa Mao, my deepest apologies for borrowing your poem! This foolish ruler actually thinks I wrote it. If your spirit is watching from heaven, please don't hold it against me.*

Hearing Prince Yi's take on the painting, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "Prince Yi truly hides his talents well!"

Zhao Ji let go of Prince Yi's hand and went on listening, intent.

Shen Jianchen examined the painting with care. "On the wintersweet branch stand two white-headed birds. They face away from each other, looking in different directions, calm and composed. In your son's view, the front one symbolizes Father, while the back one symbolizes the empire and its people. A ruler should have the empire and people as his support, so the foundation can be stable. Father is a ruler who leans on the Great Song empire and keeps ten thousand people in his heart..."

Zhao Ji exclaimed, "Well said!"

Shen Jianchen continued, "Your son doesn't understand painting, let alone composition, but I sense an overall serenity in Father's painting. Not only is it composed with such pleasing variety—top and bottom answering each other—but the relationship between the calligraphy and painting is also close. Missing any part would make it feel incomplete.

Zhao Ji finally praised, "Although Prince Yi doesn't understand painting, he knows how to read a painting. Your talent rivals Wenxian's. We award you ten gold bowls!"

All that flattery was actually taken for real talent, even comparable to Wenxian. *Unbelievable,* Shen Jianchen thought. *A pile of barefaced flattery, spun into literary genius to rival Wenxian. This hopeless ruler is truly beyond saving.* Too lazy to deal with him, he winked at Wenxian.

Qiao Wenxian gave him a thumbs up.

Liang Shicheng brought over a larger brocade box. "Prince Yi, do take good care of your ten bowls."

Shen Jianchen took it, thanked the emperor, and retreated to Qiao Wenxian's side. He whispered, "I got more bowls than you. Are we going to carry these things around the whole time?"

"No need. Watch me." Qiao Wenxian beckoned a palace maid and instructed, "Take the gold bowls from Prince Yi and me to the guards. Have them taken to Xihua Gate and placed in our carriages."

"Yes." The palace maid took the two brocade boxes, one large and one small, and left.

Shen Jianchen asked, "Won't they get stolen in the carriage?"

"Look at you, so greedy! Ten gold bowls and you're bought off. Don't worry, guards are watching the carriage. No one dares to steal."

Just as Qiao Wenxian finished speaking, Liang Shicheng called out to everyone, "To celebrate the completion of Gen Yue, the palace has organized a cuju performance. Please proceed to the cuju field!"

The palace's cuju field wasn't far from the banquet hall. Apart from the Emperor, who took a sedan chair, everyone else walked. The main seat was set up for the Emperor, with a shaded pavilion and a backrest screen painted with auspicious clouds and cranes. In front of the screen was a rosewood chair, plain in appearance. Many seats were arranged around the field, with small wooden tables between them, filled with tea and snacks.

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian arrived and chose seats. They saw the Emperor seated, with Liang Shicheng standing by to attend. The Crown Prince, Grand Commandant Gao, Cai Jing, Chancellor Zeng, and other important officials sat close to the Emperor, all beaming with satisfaction. Palace concubines, other princes, and officials' families also found places to sit.

Shen Jianchen asked, "Is Grand Commandant Gao really good at cuju?"

Qiao Wenxian replied, "Of course. His cuju skills are first-rate."

"He's quite old now. Could he be past his prime?"

"The older, the stronger. Besides, he's not that old."

As the two were talking, Lady Wei came over and sat beside him. "Prince Yi, you are the best at hiding your abilities that I've ever seen."

Qiao Wenxian asked, "What do you mean?"

Lady Wei remarked, "Prince Yi gives the impression that he doesn't understand poetry, calligraphy, painting, and such. He even pretends to be modest, saying he hasn't read much and doesn't understand anything. Yet when studying the painting, he was continuously praised by the Emperor and awarded ten gold bowls, even surpassing Young Master Qiao. Isn't that hiding one's abilities?"

Shen Jianchen murmured, "Lady Wei, you don't know. Once you master the art of flattery, not only does your mind become sharp, but your tongue becomes incredibly smooth."

"I don't believe you only know how to flatter."

"If Lady Wei doesn't believe me, there's nothing I can do. It's the truth. Of course, I also benefited from a friend's guidance."

"Guidance on how to flatter?"

"No, guidance on how to read paintings."

Qiao Wenxian asked, "You've been ill and just recovered. Apart from me, where do you have other friends? Did you get my guidance? But I don't remember such a thing."

"Wrong!" Lady Wei cut in. "Who says Prince Yi only has you as a friend? There's also me, his new friend. But I haven't guided you either. Young Master Qiao can testify."

Shen Jianchen admitted, dejected, "This friend is someone I met in another world. I can't find her now."

"Another world?" Lady Wei and Qiao Wenxian exchanged glances. They didn't believe it at all, thinking Prince Yi was talking nonsense. Neither wanted to continue the topic.

The drum sounded, and the cuju performance began.

In the center of the field stood a goal called the Flowing Wind Eye. The two performing teams entered—one in blue uniforms, the other in red. Each team had five players, positioned on either side of the goal. After exchanging greetings, when the drumbeat sounded again, both teams began playing cuju. The scene was exceptionally intense.

Shen Jianchen was watching cuju for the first time. After watching for a while, he thought it was a lot like soccer, which he knew how to play. The difference was that in cuju, the ball couldn't touch the ground. It had to stay aloft, passed from player to player, eventually reaching the team captain, who would then kick it into the goal for a successful score.

Delighted, Shen Jianchen thought, *I'm a soccer expert. I've practiced keeping the ball in the air. With my Shen Fist skills, playing this kind of ball is a piece of cake.*

Shen Jianchen looked at Gao Qiu, who was immersed in the match. Remembering the description of Gao Qiu in *Water Margin*, his heroic complex flared up. *If I'd never crossed paths with you, so be it. But since I have, I'm going to teach you a lesson on behalf of all one hundred and eight heroes of Liangshan!*

A teammate wound up to pass to their captain. Shen Jianchen surged to his feet and channeled his qinggong to vault into the air—and before the captain could react, he had already driven the ball into the Flowing Wind Eye.

The crowd fell silent at Prince Yi's move, then erupted a beat later.

Qiao Wenxian praised, "Prince Yi, your skills have truly improved!"

Lady Wei noted, "I've heard people discussing that Prince Yi learned martial arts from a divine being after his death. It seems to be true."

The Emperor's voice came, "Prince Yi! What are you doing?"

With the unexpected incident, both teams stopped.

Shen Jianchen bowed and said, "Father, your son also wants to play cuju."

The Emperor laughed, "Ha ha, if you wanted to play cuju, you should have said so earlier. We would have let you play. Why make such a scene?"

Shen Jianchen continued, "Your son has heard that Grand Commandant Gao's cuju skills are superb. I'd like to match him. Please grant your permission, Father."

The Emperor looked at Gao Qiu. "Grand Commandant Gao, Prince Yi has just come back from the dead. Why not satisfy him and have a match?"

The Emperor had spoken. How could Gao Qiu not obey? He stood up and bowed. "This subject obeys."

Shen Jianchen changed into a red uniform, while Gao Qiu wore blue. The two served as team captains for their respective teams. At the sound of a drum, both teams began the match.

The red team used the Meteor Chasing the Moon formation. Shen Jianchen placed his hand on a teammate's shoulder and kicked it straight into the goal, winning a point. Then the blue team used the Fallen Flowers and Flowing Water formation, and Grand Commandant Gao also scored a goal.

After seven or eight consecutive matches, the two teams were tied.

Shen Jianchen thought, *Although Gao Qiu is older, his ball skills are truly impressive. It's not easy to defeat him. Given the current situation, I can only win with speed and strength.* He suggested a halftime break, and the Emperor agreed.

During the break, Shen Jianchen told his teammates, "No matter how you pass the ball, I can catch it. You don't need to juggle too much. Just pass it to me at once."

The players had never encountered such a team captain. Surprised, they could only agree. After all, he was Prince Yi, and it saved them a lot of trouble.

The drumbeat sounded again, and a new round began. Both teams engaged in intense scoring. No matter how the red team passed the ball, Shen Jianchen caught every pass with speed and precision, driving it into the Flowing Wind Eye without the slightest deviation. In an instant, he was two goals ahead.

Gao Qiu grew anxious. He used Meteor Chasing the Moon, driving his teammate's pass toward the goal. Just as it reached the Flowing Wind Eye, it was knocked back by a ball kicked by Shen Jianchen and fell to the ground.

Again and again, every ball met the same fate. Grand Commandant Gao had no choice but to concede. He cupped his hands and admitted defeat. "Prince Yi kicks with both speed and force. This subject is old and can't keep up. I concede, I concede."

Shen Jianchen asked, "Grand Commandant Gao, have you heard of the 108 heroes of Liangshan?"

Gao Qiu was confused. "Is Prince Yi referring to the Marshes of Mount Liang? Bandits have been roaming there for years. I've never heard of any heroes."

Shen Jianchen said, "Maybe we're talking about different things. Anyway, the heroes asked me to meet Grand Commandant Gao on their behalf."

Gao Qiu didn't understand. The resurrected Prince Yi always said strange things. "Prince Yi, this old subject takes his leave."

Shen Jianchen faced the Emperor. "Is Father satisfied with your son's ball skills?"

Zhao Ji laughed, "I didn't expect you to be so skilled at cuju. We can see now that it's true—a divine being taught you martial arts. Since your skills are impressive, We appoint you as the Military Commissioner of Lin'an. Stay in Bianliang for a few days, then go to Lin'an to take up your post."

Shen Jianchen was stunned. A cuju match won him an official position. No wonder Gao Qiu rose through the ranks because of cuju. The Emperor had spoken. He could only accept. "Thank you, Father, for your great grace."

Qiao Wenxian and Lady Wei were both happy for Prince Yi. At least this grand palace banquet wasn't a wasted trip for him.
