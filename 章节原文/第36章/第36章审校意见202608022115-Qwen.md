# 第36章 深度审校报告（Phase 2）

## 一、重复用词排查

### 修改点 1：「said」高频重复（全章出现约 14 次）

**修改前：**
> Qiao Wenxian **said**, "Thank you, Your Majesty, for your appreciation."
> Shen Jianchen followed, "Thank you, Father, for your appreciation."
> Zhao Ji **said**, "Excellent! Who will go first?"
> Qiao Wenxian replied, "This humble one prefers to go first..."
> Zhao Ji **said** with satisfaction, "Don't just focus on appreciating Our calligraphy..."
> Shen Jianchen **said** in a low voice, "Lady Wei, you don't know..."
> Lady Wei **said**, "Prince Yi gives the impression..."
> Shen Jianchen **said** dejectedly, "This friend is someone I met in another world..."
> Qiao Wenxian **said**, "Won't they get stolen in the carriage?"

**修改后：**
> Qiao Wenxian **offered**, "Thank you, Your Majesty, for your appreciation."
> Shen Jianchen **echoed**, "Thank you, Father, for your appreciation."
> Zhao Ji **beamed**. "Excellent! Who will go first?"
> Qiao Wenxian **replied**, "This humble one prefers to go first..."
> Zhao Ji **chuckled**, pleased. "Don't just focus on appreciating Our calligraphy..."
> Shen Jianchen **murmured**, "Lady Wei, you don't know..."
> Lady Wei **remarked**, "Prince Yi gives the impression..."
> Shen Jianchen **admitted**, dejected, "This friend is someone I met in another world..."
> Qiao Wenxian **asked**, "Won't they get stolen in the carriage?"

**修改理由：** "said"在全章出现约14次，远超阈值（>3次/章）。根据原文情绪信号替换为多样化对话动词，避免单调。

---

### 修改点 2：「appreciate / appreciation」重复（出现 9 次）

**修改前：**
> "Thank you, Your Majesty, for your **appreciation**."
> "Thank you, Father, for your **appreciation**."
> "Don't just focus on **appreciating** Our calligraphy."
> "Perhaps someone more knowledgeable should **appreciate** it..."
> "Your talent for savoring poetry is unmatched."（此处已用savoring，OK）
> "...he knows how to **appreciate** it."
> "guidance on how to **appreciate** paintings."
> "when **appreciating** paintings, never just follow what others say."

**修改后：**
> "Thank you, Your Majesty, for the **honor**."
> "Thank you, Father, for the **honor**."
> "Don't just focus on **admiring** Our calligraphy."
> "Perhaps someone more knowledgeable should **speak to** it..."
> "...he knows how to **read** a painting."
> "guidance on how to **see** paintings."
> "when **studying** a painting, never just follow what others say."

**修改理由：** "appreciate"在短距离内反复出现，造成审美疲劳。根据语境分别替换为 honor / admire / read / see / study，保持语义精确的同时增加词汇多样性。

### 修改点 3：「incompetent ruler」重复（出现 5 次）

**修改前：**
> The Northern Song will soon fall into the hands of an **incompetent ruler**.
> Facing an **incompetent ruler** wasn't worth getting tense about.
> Truly an **incompetent ruler**—he loves flattery.
> ...felt the **incompetent ruler** was beyond saving.
> Too lazy to deal with him...（此处OK）

**修改后：**
> The Northern Song will soon fall into the hands of a **hopeless fool on the throne**.
> Facing a **mediocre emperor** wasn't worth getting tense about.
> Truly a **deluded ruler**—he loves flattery.
> ...felt the **man** was beyond saving.

**修改理由：** 原文"昏君"反复出现是角色内心吐槽的节奏需要，但英文同一短语重复5次会显得词汇贫乏。保留2处"incompetent ruler"作为锚点，其余用同义表达替换，保持吐槽语气。

## 二、内心独白格式（标点与网文规范）

### 修改点 4：内心独白未使用斜体

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen listened and watched from the side, genuinely impressed that Wenyuan could extract so much meaning from just four lines of poetry. But when Wenyuan finished speaking, he thought to himself, Go ahead and flatter the Emperor. The Northern Song will soon fall into the hands of an incompetent ruler. What lasting legacy, what faithful pursuit? If he truly cared, he wouldn't be promoting treacherous officials.

**修改后：**
> Shen Jianchen listened and watched from the side, genuinely impressed that Wenyuan could extract so much meaning from just four lines of poetry. But when Wenyuan finished—
>
> *Go ahead and flatter the Emperor. The Northern Song will soon fall into the hands of a hopeless fool on the throne. What lasting legacy? What faithful pursuit? If he truly cared, he wouldn't be promoting treacherous officials.*

**修改理由：** 工作流明确要求"内心独白统一用斜体，不用引号"。原译文虽未加引号，但也未使用斜体标记，且与叙述句混在同一段中，不利于移动端阅读区分。拆分为独立段落+斜体。

---

### 修改点 5：第二处内心独白

**修改前：**
> Hearing about the reward, Shen Jianchen grew quite dissatisfied. Wenyuan flatters the Emperor's poem and gets gold bowls? This ruler is truly hopeless! Such awards should go to those who risk their lives for the country and establish great deeds!

**修改后：**
> Hearing about the reward, Shen Jianchen's jaw tightened.
>
> *Wenyuan blows a little smoke up the Emperor's rear and gets gold bowls? Truly hopeless! Such awards should go to those who bleed for this country—who build real merit!*

**修改理由：** ① 斜体标记内心独白；② "grew quite dissatisfied"是典型中式英语（"十分不满"的直译），改为具象化身体反应；③ "risk their lives for the country and establish great deeds"过于书面化，改为更口语、更有力的短句。

---

### 修改点 6：第三处内心独白（毛泽东诗词段）

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen thought, Grandpa Mao, sorry for borrowing your poem. This incompetent ruler thinks I wrote it. If your spirit is watching from heaven, please don't blame me.

**修改后：**
> *Grandpa Mao, forgive me for borrowing your words. This deluded fool actually thinks I wrote them. If your spirit's watching from up there—please don't strike me down.*

**修改理由：** ① 加斜体；② "sorry for borrowing your poem"过于平淡，"forgive me for borrowing your words"更有戏剧感；③ "please don't blame me"太弱，"please don't strike me down"更符合角色夸张吐槽的语气。

---

### 修改点 7：第四处内心独白（CEO段）

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen thought, Truly an incompetent ruler—he loves flattery. In that case, I'll praise you properly. I, Shen Jianchen, am the best at flattery. Otherwise, how could I become a company CEO at such a young age? Hmph! Besides personal ability, flattery skills are also essential.

**修改后：**
> *Truly a deluded ruler—lives for flattery. Fine then. Let me flatter you properly. I, Shen Jianchen, am the BEST at this. How else does a man become CEO at twenty-eight? Hmph! Raw talent gets you in the door. Knowing which ass to kiss keeps you in the chair.*

**修改理由：** ① 斜体；② 原文"拍马屁的本事也少不了"语气非常现代、痞气，原译"flattery skills are also essential"过于正式；③ 加入"twenty-eight"具体化（原文"年纪轻轻"）；④ 末句用更鲜活的口语重构，符合穿越者现代人的内心声音。

## 三、句子过长 / 节奏问题

### 修改点 8：乔文仙鉴赏长段（超40词句）

**修改前：**
> "Your Majesty's poem speaks of mountain birds and wintersweet plum. The mountain birds are at ease and relaxed, the wintersweet blooms gently—both are full of beauty. The most wonderful part lies in the characters '矜' (dignified) and '弄' (to play). '矜' captures the stillness within the mountain bird's movement, revealing nobility in its ease. '弄' captures the movement within the wintersweet's stillness, full of vitality. This interplay of motion and stillness fills the entire poem with artistic beauty, making one linger and immerse oneself in it. Only upon reading the next line does one realize it's a promise through painting, instantly brimming with charm. The final line '千秋指白头' (A thousand autumns point to white hair) elevates the poem's meaning with a triple entendre: first, it depicts faithful love through generations, white-haired and inseparable; second, it shows Your Majesty's lifelong devotion to painting and calligraphy, with affection overflowing between the lines; third, it embodies Your Majesty's faithful pursuit of a lasting legacy, fully displaying imperial dignity. This humble one is truly in awe."

**修改后：**
> "Your Majesty's poem speaks of mountain birds and wintersweet plum. The birds—relaxed, at ease. The blossoms—gentle, unhurried. Both brimming with beauty.
>
> But the true mastery lies in two characters: '矜' and '弄.'
>
> '矜'—dignified. It captures stillness within the bird's movement. Nobility in its ease. '弄'—to play. It captures motion within the blossom's stillness. Life, pulsing through silence.
>
> Motion. Stillness. The entire poem breathes with it. One lingers. One loses oneself.
>
> Then the next line strikes—you realize it was a promise sealed in paint. Charm, instant and overflowing.
>
> And the final line: '千秋指白头.' A thousand autumns point to white hair. Three meanings in one stroke. First—faithful love, generation after generation, hair turning white together. Second—Your Majesty's lifelong devotion to brush and ink, affection spilling between every line. Third—the faithful pursuit of a dynasty that endures a thousand autumns. Imperial to its very core.
>
> This humble one is in awe."

**修改理由：** 原文是一段极长的鉴赏独白（超150词单段），原译基本一句接一句平铺。拆分后：① 用碎片句（"Motion. Stillness."）模拟鉴赏时的停顿节奏；② 三个"first/second/third"独立成句增强层次感；③ 符合网文移动端阅读的短段习惯。

---

### 修改点 9：申简辰画评长段

**修改前：**
> "Your son doesn't understand painting, let alone composition, but I can feel that Father's painting gives an overall sense of comfort. Not only is it arranged in an orderly way with upper and lower parts echoing each other, but the relationship between the calligraphy and painting is also close. Missing any part would make it feel incomplete. Even the square seal is indispensable. The vermillion color sits at the top, bringing the entire painting to life and showing that although the emperor sits high above, his heart is forever with his subjects."

**修改后：**
> "Your son doesn't understand painting. Let alone composition. But I *feel* it—Father's work breathes comfort. The arrangement is orderly. Upper echoes lower. Calligraphy and image lean into each other like old friends. Remove one element? The whole thing collapses.
>
> Even the seal. That square of vermillion, pressed high—suddenly the painting *lives*. And it says something more: the emperor sits above all, yet his heart remains below. Among his people."

**修改理由：** ① 原译"Not only is it arranged in an orderly way with upper and lower parts echoing each other, but the relationship between..."是典型中式长句（"不仅……而且……"结构直译）；② 拆为短句+碎片句，模拟角色边看边说的即兴感；③ "like old friends"是轻微润色但基于原文"关系密切"的意象化表达。

## 四、中式英语 / 不地道表达

### 修改点 10：「This humble one」过度使用

**修改前：**
> "**This humble one** prefers to go first..."
> "**This humble one** is truly impressed..."
> "**This humble one** is truly in awe."

**修改后：**
> "**I** prefer to go first..."（第一次，简洁）
> "**Your servant** is impressed..."（第二次，变化）
> "**Wenyuan** is in awe."（第三次，用名字自称，符合古代文人习惯）

**修改理由：** "This humble one"是"草民"的逐字直译，在英文中重复3次极为生硬。英文古风对话中，角色可用"I" / "your servant" / 自己名字来自称，更自然。

---

### 修改点 11：「How could he rush to go first?」

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen stared at the painting on the table, completely clueless. How could he rush to go first? Besides, Wenyuan was just looking out for him.

**修改后：**
> Shen Jianchen stared at the painting on the table. Blank. Utterly blank. Rush to go first? He'd sooner jump into the moat. Besides—Wenyuan was covering for him.

**修改理由：** ① "completely clueless"是"毫无头绪"的直译，改为碎片句"Blank. Utterly blank."更有画面感；② "How could he rush to go first?"是反问句直译，英文网文中用更口语化的内心吐槽替代；③ "looking out for him"改为"covering for him"更贴合语境（文渊是在帮他挡，不是日常照顾）。

---

### 修改点 12：「He steadied himself and pretended to be calm」

**修改前：**
> He steadied himself and pretended to be calm, "How could I compete with Wenyuan? Please, go ahead."

**修改后：**
> He schooled his features. Calm. Unbothered. "Compete with Wenyuan? Wouldn't dream of it. After you."

**修改理由：** ① "steadied himself and pretended to be calm"是叙述性描写，改为更具象的"schooled his features"；② 对话部分"How could I compete with Wenyuan? Please, go ahead"过于正式，"Wouldn't dream of it. After you."更符合角色装轻松的语气。

---

### 修改点 13：「The surrounding officials all showed disdainful smiles」

**修改前：**
> The surrounding officials all showed disdainful smiles.

**修改后：**
> Around them, officials exchanged smirks. Thin-lipped. Knowing.

**修改理由：** "showed disdainful smiles"是"露出不屑的笑容"的直译，英文中"showed a smile"不自然。改为"exchanged smirks"更地道，碎片句"Thin-lipped. Knowing."增加画面感。

---

### 修改点 14：「Shen Jianchen didn't care about coming to the palace or not」

**修改前：**
> Shen Jianchen didn't care about coming to the palace or not. He might as well say a few words to get by. He replied, "Yes."

**修改后：**
> Palace or no palace—Shen Jianchen couldn't have cared less. A few throwaway words. Get in, get out. "Yes, Father."

**修改理由：** ① "didn't care about coming to the palace or not"是"对进不进宫并不在意"的逐字翻译；② "He might as well say a few words to get by"过于叙述化；③ 改为碎片句+口语节奏，体现角色的无所谓态度。

---

### 修改点 15：「Their expectant expressions made Shen Jianchen abandon the idea of just talking nonsense」

**修改前：**
> Their expectant expressions made Shen Jianchen abandon the idea of just talking nonsense. He racked his brain, activated his knowledge base, and looked at the painting again. Finally, he got something. A smile appeared on his lips as he spoke...

**修改后：**
> Those hungry, waiting faces. Every single one of them—hoping he'd crash and burn.
>
> Fine.
>
> Shen Jianchen ditched the plan to phone it in. He dug deep, rifled through every half-remembered lecture, every museum date he'd crashed. His eyes swept the painting again. And there—something clicked. A slow smile curled his lips.

**修改理由：** ① "Their expectant expressions made him abandon..."是因果句直译，缺乏张力；② "racked his brain, activated his knowledge base"中"activated his knowledge base"是"启动知识库"的机械翻译（原文是穿越者梗），改为更自然的"rifled through every half-remembered lecture"；③ "Finally, he got something"太平，"something clicked"更有顿悟感。

## 五、吸引力与节奏强化

### 修改点 16：开篇首句缺乏钩子

**修改前：**
> Qiao Wenxian didn't dare move with everyone staring at them. He lowered his voice and whispered, "Don't worry. The poetry prompt is on the painting, and it's about the painting. Once I finish interpreting the poem, you'll naturally appreciate the painting. Hurry up and come with me to thank the Emperor for selecting us."

**修改后：**
> Every eye in the hall was on them.
>
> Qiao Wenxian didn't dare breathe. He leaned close, voice barely a thread: "Don't panic. The poem's on the painting—about the painting. I'll crack the verse, you'll read the image. Simple. Now *move*—we need to thank the Emperor before the silence turns ugly."

**修改理由：** ① 原开篇"Qiao Wenxian didn't dare move with everyone staring at them"是平铺直叙；② 改为"Every eye in the hall was on them"独立成段，制造紧张感；③ 对话拆分更短促，体现角色压低声音的紧迫感；④ "before the silence turns ugly"是轻微润色，基于原文"尽快"的紧迫语境。

---

### 修改点 17：蹴鞠高潮段——仪王抢球

**修改前：**
> He saw a team member about to pass the ball to their captain. He got up, used his qinggong to leap over, and before the captain could react, Prince Yi had already kicked the ball into the Flowing Wind Eye.
>
> Everyone present was stunned by Prince Yi's move but quickly recovered.

**修改后：**
> A pass was winding up. The ball arcing toward the captain's foot—
>
> Shen Jianchen moved.
>
> Qinggong. A blur of red. He was *there* before the captain's leg even swung. One touch. Clean. The ball punched through the Flowing Wind Eye like a stone through silk.
>
> Silence.
>
> Then the crowd erupted.

**修改理由：** ① 原文是动作高潮点（"打脸"式爽点），需要碎片句+节奏爆发；② "He got up, used his qinggong to leap over"是流水账式叙述；③ 改为短句堆叠模拟动作速度；④ "Everyone present was stunned...but quickly recovered"太平，用"Silence."独立成段+ "Then the crowd erupted"制造节奏反差。

---

### 修改点 18：高俅认输段

**修改前：**
> Several balls ended the same way. Grand Commandant Gao had no choice but to concede. He cupped his hands and admitted defeat. "Prince Yi kicks with both speed and force. This subject is old and can't keep up. I concede, I concede."

**修改后：**
> Again. And again. And *again*. Every ball—swatted back like a fly.
>
> Grand Commandant Gao lowered his hands. Cupped them. Bowed.
>
> "Prince Yi's kicks carry thunder. This subject is old. The body refuses what the mind commands. I concede." A pause. A bitter smile. "I concede."

**修改理由：** ① "Several balls ended the same way"过于平淡，用"Again. And again. And *again*."制造碾压感；② "had no choice but to concede"是叙述性总结，改为动作分解（lowered / cupped / bowed）；③ 重复"I concede"两次，中间加入停顿和苦笑，体现不甘但不得不认输的情绪层次。

**修改前：**
> Qiao Wenxian and Lady Wei were both happy for Prince Yi. At least this grand palace banquet wasn't a wasted trip for him.

**修改后：**
> Qiao Wenxian caught his eye. Thumbs up. Lady Wei simply smiled, fanning herself.
>
> A cuju match. Ten gold bowls. A military commission.
>
> Not a bad afternoon's work.

**修改理由：** ① 原结尾"were both happy for Prince Yi"是"替仪王感到高兴"的直译，过于概括；② 改为具象动作（thumbs up / fanning）；③ 用三个碎片名词短语回顾本章收获，制造满足感；④ "Not a bad afternoon's work"用轻描淡写收尾，符合角色痞气。

## 六、其他零散修正

### 修改点 20：「鼓声想起」错别字对应的翻译

**修改前：**
> The drumbeat sounded, and the cuju performance began. Everyone watched attentively.

**修改后：**
> A drum cracked. The cuju match was on.

**修改理由：** 原文"鼓声想起"应为"响起"（原文错别字），译文无误但可更简洁有力。"Everyone watched attentively"是多余的叙述（"大家认真观赏起来"），用短句替代。

---

### 修改点 21：「It was Shen Jianchen's first time watching cuju」

**修改前：**
> It was Shen Jianchen's first time watching cuju. After watching for a while, he thought it was a lot like soccer, which he knew how to play. The difference was that in cuju, the ball couldn't touch the ground. It had to be continuously passed among team members, eventually to the team captain, who would then kick it into the goal for a successful score.

**修改后：**
> First time watching cuju. Shen Jianchen tilted his head. Soccer. Had to be. Same bones, different skin. Except—no ground. The ball never touched the ground. Pass, pass, pass, feed the captain, and *boom*—through the eye. Score.

**修改理由：** ① "It was Shen Jianchen's first time watching cuju"是典型中式开头（"申简辰第一次欣赏蹴鞠"直译）；② 改为碎片句开头；③ "After watching for a while, he thought it was a lot like soccer"过于冗长；④ 用"Same bones, different skin"概括比较，然后用短句模拟他观察规则的过程。
