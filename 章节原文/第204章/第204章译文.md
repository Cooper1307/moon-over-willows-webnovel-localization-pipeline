## Chapter 204: Drinking in Town and Running into Her Ex-Husband

Dongfang Chenze handled notifying Prince Yi and Qiao Wenxian—carefully framing it as a men-only banquet rather than a poetry salon so wives and fiancées stayed home—plus inviting Xiangrong from Flower Cloud Pavilion, both to sing Youqi Jushi's lyrics so the salon looked authentic and to keep Youqi Jushi company between meetings.

Cao Ban and An Mingxuan took on inviting Youqi Jushi and borrowing Zuiyun Tower from Chen Haoyu, heading together to Chen Manor's closed south gate (skipping the busy west business gate). Knocking, An Mingxuan learned Young Master Chen had stepped out for anywhere from four days to half a month. Before the gatekeeper shut the door, Cao Ban jumped in: "Is Youqi Jushi home? We came for her too!"

"No Youqi Jushi here."

"Miss Zhu!" Cao Ban clarified.

"Oh, Young Madam! Still inside—let me announce you."

By sheer coincidence, with Matron Wei's crew turning her hall upside down with moving furniture, Qianli Xun had stepped out for a stroll through town and walked straight up to the gatekeeper—one of the bruised guards from her raid, who bowed eagerly to report two young gentlemen waiting outside.

Stepping out to greet Cao Ban and An Mingxuan, she heard Cao Ban's smooth pitch inviting her to Dongfang Chenze's poetry gathering tomorrow—and asked cautiously who else was invited.

"Brother Dongfang aims to make his name in Lin'an's literary world, so he invited ONLY top local poets," Cao Ban fibbed smoothly. "Brother Qiao is coming; Lady Miao falls short on renown so she was skipped; Prince Yi and Her Highness outrank us yet hold no literary fame, so neither was invited."

Hearing Prince Yi and Mengrong weren't coming eased Qianli Xun's nerves—she could face Wenyuan calmly now. "Why send you two in person instead of a formal invitation card?"

"We have cards—Brother Dongfang insisted luminaries like Youqi Jushi deserve a personal call!"

"Fair enough. Time and place?"

"Hour of the Snake tomorrow at Zuiyun Tower by the Qiantang River—southwest out the city gate, half an hour by carriage. Happens to be a Chen property too."

Wondering briefly why hold a big salon so far out yet shrugging it off, Qianli Xun promised to attend.

"Could Youqi Jushi tell us where Young Master Chen went?" An Mingxuan asked next. "Since Zuiyun Tower sits under his name, we need his nod to borrow it."

Amused they invited guests before locking down the venue, Qianli Xun waved it off. "Skip hunting Chen Haoyu—all his assets just transferred to me, so Zuiyun Tower is mine now. Everyone come freely tomorrow."

Thrilled to cross both errands off in one shot, the two scholars hurried off to report to Dongfang Chenze.

Skipping a carriage to walk into the bustling market, Qianli Xun let her thoughts drift first to Mengrong—hoping her wound healed clean—and straight from there to Shen Jianchen: running through the earthquake, sparring in the bamboo grove, hinting under the willow, playing chuiwan side by side, storming Zhu Manor, blocking her bridal sedan... Frame after frame played behind her eyes, pulling smiles and sharp stabs of grief by turns.

*Thought you swore off romance?* she scolded herself. Back as Qianli Xun she had kept her heart locked tight; inside Zhu Shuzhen's skin, her walls melted every time! Remembering Bai Feng hounding that poor piglet to death brought a bitter pang: *Why be that stubborn when fate wrote us off?* Surely time would dull Shen Jianchen just as it had dulled Wenyuan—yet why was SHE always the one forced to step back, never Miao Zhiyu or Mengrong?

Spotting a wine shop ahead, four words flashed in her mind—*drown your sorrows*—and she ducked straight inside to a window table, ordering a pot of Capital Spring. Normally untouched by strong liquor, one cup burned fiery trails down her throat and flushed her cheeks hot.

"Switch to sweet osmanthus wine, Miss?" Kuqiao suggested.

"Osmanthus wine is juice—today I want real liquor!" pouring a second cup.

"Who says osmanthus wine isn't liquor? Knocked you out cold last summer!"

*Last summer...* The exact night Shen Jianchen realized Zhu Shuzhen was Qianli Xun—watching her sit drunk in Wenyuan's lap! Why did they keep missing by inches?! All because of her loud mouth taunting *"When pigs climb trees!"* Swirling her cup, she muttered, "Wonder if taking those words back is too late..." Of course it was—she was wed, he held an imperial marriage decree.

Lifting the cup in gloom, a familiar voice broke in: "Drinking solo is dull—let me join you."

Looking up, she saw Lin Jue standing beside her table. "What do you want?"

"Relax—not stalking you! Just stepped out of Goulan Wasi listening to Master Qianli Xun's huaben, passed the window, spotted you drinking alone, and dropped in to say hi." Taking a seat uninvited, he waved for an extra cup, poured himself full, and knocked it straight down.

Holding zero grudge since their divorce, Qianli Xun let him sit. "Out solo without your bride?"

"Men have men's errands—who drags a wife everywhere?"

Letting out a tiny hiccup, Qianli Xun scoffed, "Pure chauvinism! Still sneaking Chun Jinqiao into Lin Manor?"

"Nope—my new wife is gentle and obedient, so no need. Still hit Chunxiang Brothel plenty, though," Lin Jue admitted freely.

Pouring herself another cup, Qianli Xun shook her head. "Leopard never changes his spots! Got a decent wife now—cherish her instead of living at the brothel! If you truly care for Chun Jinqiao, talk to your wife about buying her freedom as a concubine—keep this up and you are heading for divorce number two!"

Lin Jue laughed aloud. "Think every woman has your nerve to demand mutual divorce? My new wife lacks that kind of spine—save your worry."

"Like I care about your mess—just chatting."

A sudden glint crossed Lin Jue's eyes. "Hey—level with me: is that huaben writer Qianli Xun actually YOU?"

Sobering a quick notch, Qianli Xun blinked. "They call him a male master—how could it be me? Since when do I write huaben?"

"Really not?" Lin Jue squinted shrewdly. "Back in Lin Manor's hall during our divorce, you swore in front of my elders your name was Qianli Xun—forgot?! Back then I thought it was gibberish; once Master Qianli Xun blew up across Lin'an, I figured you picked a male pen name to hide writing fiction!"

Scrambling to cover that old slip, she waved it off. "Made a random name up on the spot to shake loose from you—pure fluke it matched the famous writer!"

"Shame—I really hoped it was you," Lin Jue sighed with genuine regret. "His huaben stories wipe the floor with your poetry! Never could stomach those fancy literary verses, yet I am hooked on Master Qianli Xun's tales!" Leaning closer, he dropped his voice. "You really are nothing like the old Zhu Shuzhen..."

"All thanks to you," Qianli Xun parried dryly.

Before Lin Jue could reply, a shadow fell across the table—looking up, both froze as Bai Meiruo stood right beside them with her maid! Out shopping for pastries and strolling the lively market, her maid had spotted Master drinking and laughing warmly across from his ex-wife—a bright, relaxed grin Bai Meiruo never saw at home—lighting instant jealousy inside her chest.
