第192章 婚礼后夏果来访
## Chapter 192: Xiaguo Visits After the Ceremony

迎亲队伍终于到达陈家大门外，乐官、茶酒司等人堵住门口不让新娘进入，他们都以陈浩东为首，个个满面喜色。尤其是陈浩东，计谋得逞，更是春风得意。
他代表乐官念起《拦门诗》：“拦门礼物多为贵，岂比寻常市道交。十万缠腰应满足，三千五索莫轻抛。”
陈浩宇向迎亲队伍中摆摆手，他的堂弟陈浩飞跑过来，看上去大约十四五岁的模样：“二哥，叫我何事？”
陈浩宇说：“东哥拦门，你知道该怎么做。”
陈浩飞双手叉腰，大声念出《回拦门诗》：“从来君子不怀金，此意追寻意转深。欲望诸亲聊阔略，毋烦介绍久劳心。”
众人听后，都笑笑，让开路。占卜的阴阳人手拿斗，里面装着谷类、豆类、铜钱及彩果等，一边念咒文一边往门上撒。
差不多时，苦荞向轿子里提醒：“小姐，该下轿。”
千里寻还沉浸在梦容冲向利刃的一刻，听到苦荞的声音，平复心情，将凤冠戴好，红盖头顶上。感觉到轿帘被掀开，起身走出轿子，扶住苦荞伸过来的手。脚下是提前铺好的软青锦，踩上去软绵绵的。
被苦荞引到陈浩宇身边，两人并肩而行走进陈府，一路上脚下尽是软青锦。
顶着盖头，只能看到脚下，她发现只给新娘脚下放有软青锦，新郎脚下什么都没有，心中甚是疑惑。没疑惑多久，已被引入陈家的礼堂内。
千里寻跨过礼堂门槛的一刻，依靠第六感，觉出里面很大。她的脚下已换成红毯，借着红毯，她胡乱猜想，会不会里面有前来参加婚礼之人？到底会有多少人？千里寻靠感觉难以猜测。
礼堂的正前方挂着一张画像，画像前放着供桌，上有供品香炉，香炉内燃着三炷香。供桌左边坐有夏老太太，右边坐着陈江林和姚轻琰。其他陈家人则围坐在一边，除陈江树和陈江木外，不过是陈浩宇的两位婶婶，还有堂嫂，也就是陈浩东的发妻。
主持婚礼之人是陈家专门请的临安官员，当然也是看在朱晞颜的面子上才答应为陈家主持婚礼。那官员身着便服，站立一旁，见新人走得差不多时，笑道：“新人请止步。”
千里寻停住脚步，心想：“应是拜天地。唉，也不知梦容如何？”
只听官员说：“一拜先灵！”
千里寻鞠躬行礼，心中疑惑：“不都是先拜天地吗？怎么宋代是先拜祖先？”
接着拜公婆和陈家老太太，又是夫妻对拜。尤其听到“夫妻对拜”，千里寻内心无比难过，搞来搞去，与她夫妻对拜之人竟是她最瞧不上的人。
拜完后，跟陈浩宇进入洞房，坐在大大的婚床边，走进几个丫鬟和婆子，向床上撒同心果和钱币。
撒完后，有婆子说：“陈公子，该揭新娘的盖头。”
千里寻不愿陈浩宇来揭，自行将盖头取下。
婆子说：“新娘怎么能自行娶盖头？得新郎取，否则不吉利。”婆子想给新娘盖上，结果手被千里寻挡住，怎么都盖不上。
At last the bridal procession reached Chen Manor's main gate, where musicians and banquet staff barred the doorway under Chen Haodong's lead, everyone beaming—none brighter than Chen Haodong, basking in his plot's success.

陈浩宇劝道：“算了，她揭开跟我揭开没什么区别。”
Speaking for the musicians, he chanted the *Gate-Blocking Poem*:

婆子立刻换副嘴脸，将盖头放到一边：“新郎懂得尊重新娘，预示你们以后的日子红红火火。”
"Rich gifts to clear the gate count most of all, Far above ordinary marketplace trade; A hundred thousand cash strapped to the waist should satisfy— Three thousand and five hundred strings—do not toss them lightly!" [TN: Translator's rendering]

有丫鬟过来，分别在两人头上取出一撮头发，用剪刀剪下，再将两撮头发用红色发带绑在一起，放入锦盒；又有丫鬟端来两瓣系着红绸的瓢瓜。
Chen Haoyu waved into the procession, and his fourteen- or fifteen-year-old cousin Chen Haofei trotted up. "Need me, Second Brother?"

千里寻见瓢瓜中有酒，心想该是该喝交杯酒的环节。用余光瞅瞅身旁的陈浩宇，他竟然面带笑容，伸手去拿瓢瓜。
"Haodong is blocking the gate—you know the drill."

婆子见新娘坐着不动，提醒道：“新娘子快行合卺礼，没见新郎都等不及。”
Planting fists on his hips, Chen Haofei belted out the *Reply to the Gate-Blocking Poem*:

陈浩宇说：“没事没事，我一个人饮也可。”
"True gentlemen never keep gold in mind; True feeling sought grows deeper still with time. We beg our worthy kin to ease the way, And spare the go-betweens prolonged delay!" [TN: Translator's rendering]

“不可。”婆子将瓢瓜拿起来，递到千里寻手中，“新娘子快些。”
Laughing, the crowd parted. A geomancer bearing a wooden measure filled with grains, beans, copper coins, and festive fruits chanted blessings while scattering handfuls over the doorway.

千里寻端起瓢瓜一口喝下，将瓢瓜放进盘中。
When the moment came, Kuqiao called softly beside the curtain, "Time to step out, Miss."

婆子急了：“怎能这么饮？应该先喝一半，再与新郎交换着喝。”
Still haunted by Mengrong plunging onto the sword, Qianli Xun forced herself steady, settled the phoenix coronet back on her head, draped the red veil, and stepped out into Kuqiao's waiting hands. Soft blue-green brocade cushioned her slippers all the way forward. Guided beside Chen Haoyu, she walked into Chen Manor shoulder to shoulder with him, peering down under her veil to wonder why brocade lined only her path and not the groom's—until they crossed the threshold into a vast ceremony hall where red carpet replaced brocade.

陈浩宇劝婆子：“我不在意这些，都是形式。”也将瓢瓜中的酒喝完。
At the front of the hall hung an ancestral portrait above an offering table bearing fruit and three burning incense sticks. To its left sat Old Madam Xia; to the right sat Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan, flanked by Second Uncle Chen Jiangshu, Third Uncle Chen Jiangmu, both aunts, and Chen Haodong's primary wife. A Lin'an official invited out of courtesy to Prefect Zhu stood in plain robes as master of ceremonies.

婆子与丫鬟们无奈，只能收了，离开洞房。
"Halt, bride and groom!" he announced with a warm smile.

待众人走后，陈浩宇站起来：“委屈你在此坐着，我得出去应酬。”
Bracing to bow to heaven and earth—while still fretting over Mengrong—Qianli Xun heard instead: "First bow: to the ancestral spirits!"

千里寻并不理会，早都不希望陈浩宇坐在身边，任由其离开。她取下凤冠，递给苦荞：“放起来。”
*Wait—don't weddings bow to heaven and earth first? The Song bows to ancestors first?* Dutifully she bowed, then bowed to her in-laws and Old Madam Xia, then faced Chen Haoyu for the mutual spousal bow—a heavy lump rising in her throat at bowing as wife to the very man she despised most.

苦荞接过，放到桌上，回头见小姐坐在床边一脸忧愁，猜想一定是因佳和郡主受伤之事。
Escorted next to the bridal chamber, she sat on the wide wedding bed while maids and matrons scattered paired wedding fruits and coins across the quilt.

她不愿小姐继续难过下去，想到一个话题：“小姐，没想到陈家吃酒席的地方好大，我从来没见过这么大的地方，您说官家吃酒席的地方会不会比陈家的还要大？”
"Young Master Chen, time to lift the bride's veil!" an elderly woman announced.

千里寻毫无兴致，一言不发。
Unwilling to let Chen Haoyu touch it, Qianli Xun reached up and pulled the red veil off herself.

苦荞又想到一个话题：“小姐，今日来了好多人，大部分我都不认识。对了，我看到夏果，她真是，嫁了人也不知道回望月轩看看您。”
"Oh, a bride cannot remove her own veil—unlucky! The groom must lift it!" The matron tried draping it back on, only for Qianli Xun's hand to block her solidly.

提到夏果，千里寻自然想到文渊。仔细算算日子，她与文渊从相恋到分手也就七个来月，可对她来讲恍若隔世。
"Leave it," Chen Haoyu shrugged. "Her taking it off or me taking it off—same difference."

记得捶丸场时，情还在，后来不知不觉慢慢消失，申简辰再次住进她的心里。尤其是近日，她从未想起过文渊。
Flipping smooth as oil, the matron set the veil aside. "Look how the groom respects his bride—a sure sign of glowing days ahead!"

苦荞一直说话，她不好不理，随口问道：“文渊可来？”问完又觉得这个问题实在多余，夏果都来了，文渊岂能不来？
Next a maid clipped a tiny lock of hair from each of their heads, bound the two locks together with a red cord, and laid them in a brocade box; another brought forward two split gourd halves linked by red silk and filled with wine. Guessing this was the nuptial gourd-cup rite, Qianli Xun glanced sideways to see Chen Haoyu already smiling and reaching for his half.

苦荞还是回道：“我朝男宾区瞧过一眼，人太多，没看到乔公子，可我看到南双，他站着，容易被发现，他既在，乔公子肯定在。”
"Hurry with the nuptial gourd rite, bride—even the groom cannot wait!" the matron nudged.

关于文渊，千里寻没有什么可说的，继续担心起梦容。
"No fuss—I can drink mine solo," Chen Haoyu offered.

苦荞再找不出任何话题，站在小姐身旁陪她一起沉默。
"Impossible!" Pressing the gourd into Qianli Xun's hand, the matron urged, "Quick now, bride!"

千里寻再次想起白风的话，开口问：“苦荞，你会不会觉得我是坏人？”
Qianli Xun tossed the whole gourd down in one swallow and set it back on the tray.

“小姐为何这么问？”
"Not like that!" the matron yelped. "Drink half and swap gourds with the groom!"

“今日之事你也看到。”
"Quit fussing over formalities—I do not mind," Chen Haoyu said, draining his own half clean.

苦荞才品出小姐的意思：“怎么能怪您？您可是一直拒绝仪王。……，其实……其实我一直想不通，小姐为何不答应做仪王的妾室？您跟佳和郡主是朋友，再共同服侍仪王，岂不完美？”
Defeated, the matrons and maids cleared the tray and withdrew.

“哪有你想得那么简单。梦容为了仪王连命都不要，若有人与她一起分享仪王，她定然不会好过。正因为我把梦容当好友看待，也清楚她对仪王的付出，才更不会答应待在仪王身边。”
Once the room emptied, Chen Haoyu stood up. "Sorry to leave you sitting here, yet I have guests to greet outside."

小姐心善，总是替他人考虑，如此善良之人，为何还会碰到许多不顺心之事？苦荞在心中慨叹老天实在不公。
Glad to see the back of him, Qianli Xun said nothing as he left, lifting off the heavy coronet and handing it to Kuqiao. "Put this away."

千里寻继续说：“再说，我也不愿与人共事一夫。”
Setting it on the table, Kuqiao saw her mistress's glum face and guessed she was brooding over Princess Jiahe's injury. Casting about for a distraction, she chatted, "Miss, I never saw a banquet hall as massive as the Chens'! Wonder if His Majesty's own hall is even bigger?"

若有人与苦荞分享白风，她也会不乐意，小姐无法答应仪王的难处她完全理解。
No response.

千里寻站起来，伸伸懒腰：“不想了，想这么多有什么用？新生活开始，我要好好安排一番。”
Kuqiao tried again. "So many guests today—half of them strangers! Oh, I spotted Xiaguo too! Can you believe she never once dropped by Wangyue Pavilion to visit you after getting married?"

她放下伸完懒腰的手臂，将婚房四周仔细瞧一遍，皱起眉头：“陈家将婚房布置得奢华，可惜没有书香之气，去，把韦嬷嬷叫来。”
Mention of Xiaguo brought Wenyuan briefly to mind. Counting back, her whole romance and breakup with Wenyuan had spanned barely seven months, yet it felt lifetimes ago. Back at the chuiwan grounds the ache had still lingered; then bit by bit it vanished as Shen Jianchen moved back into her heart—these past days she had hardly spared Wenyuan a single thought.

苦荞答应后，来到宴请宾客之处寻找韦嬷嬷。
Not wanting to leave Kuqiao hanging, she asked idly, "Did Wenyuan come?"—then realized how pointless the question was if Xiaguo was here.

要说这陈家的确为婚事费尽心思，竟将对陈家来讲十分重要的兴隆阁腾出来使用。
"I peeked into the men's section," Kuqiao reported. "Too crowded to spot Young Master Qiao, yet I saw Nan Shuang standing tall—if he is here, Young Master Qiao definitely is."

兴隆阁内设议事厅，每隔几日陈家重要人物，以及临安的掌柜会来此商议生意中的大事，许多重要决策都是在此处定夺下来。每年年底也要在兴隆阁内宴请各地掌柜，安排来年的生意。
With nothing more to ask about Wenyuan, Qianli Xun drifted back to worrying about Mengrong, while Kuqiao ran out of topics and stood quietly beside her.

刚刚陪小姐进入兴隆阁时，苦荞并未细看。此时一跨进大门，先看到两边放置着两个形状像大鼓一样的玩意儿，上面刻镂着大大的喜字。
Bai Feng's sharp condemnation surfaced again. "Kuqiao, do you think I am a bad person?"

中间红毯的两边放置各色花卉，正值春季，一盆一盆的花开得甚艳。墙壁和柱子上也都贴有喜字。屋顶上挂着各色灯笼，轻轻垂下，抬头望去，像漫天繁星。
"Why on earth ask that, Miss?"

苦荞觉得兴隆阁仿佛一个用红色涂成的世界，周围尽是一片红光。她顾不上欣赏，向右边的女宾区拐去。
"You saw what happened today."

韦嬷嬷本与朱家女眷一起坐在桌旁享用珍馐美味，同时替向夫人应酬各家女眷。
Catching on, Kuqiao protested at once, "How could anyone blame YOU?! You turned Prince Yi down nonstop! Actually... one thing still puzzles me, Miss: why not just agree to be Prince Yi's concubine? You and Her Highness are best friends—serving Prince Yi side by side would be lovely!"

她刚刚离开席位与秦夫人饮过一杯酒，见苦荞站在女宾区的入口处向她瞧来，猜到姑娘找她有事，与秦夫人笑着说了句话，回到向夫人跟前，耳语几句，得到允许，离开宴席朝苦荞走去。
"If only life were that tidy. Mengrong was ready to die for Prince Yi—sharing him would tear her apart. Precisely because I count Mengrong a real friend and saw how deeply she loves him, I could never stay by his side."

“可是姑娘吩咐你来找我？”
Kuqiao sighed silently at Heaven's injustice toward someone so kind.

“是。”
"Besides, I refuse to share a husband with anyone," Qianli Xun added. Thinking how furious she would be sharing Bai Feng, Kuqiao got it completely.

韦嬷嬷迅速走出女宾区，再走出兴隆阁，在苦荞的引领下，来到婚房。推开门的一刻，姑娘已将婚服脱去，换上平常的衣裳。
Standing up with a big stretch, Qianli Xun shook off the gloom. "Enough moping—what does brooding fix? New chapter starts today, time to organize my turf!" Scanning the ornate bridal suite, she wrinkled her nose. "Flashy luxury without a shred of literary taste! Go call Matron Wei."

“婚礼还未结束，姑娘就把婚服换了，陈家人看到会批评姑娘不懂事。”
Kuqiao hurried out to the banquet hall to find her.

韦嬷嬷的责备，千里寻并未当回事，上前扶住嬷嬷：“婚服穿着实在不舒服，没有日常的衣裳受用。嬷嬷快坐下，我有事跟您商议。”
The Chens had spared zero expense, clearing out their prized Xinglong Pavilion for the feast—normally the nerve center where Chen elders and Lin'an shop managers met every few days to decide major trade moves and gather each year-end to map out the next year's commerce. Too busy guiding Miss earlier to look close, Kuqiao stepped through the doors now onto a dazzling scarlet world: twin drum-shaped lanterns carved with giant double-happiness characters flanking the doorway, blooming spring potted flowers lining the crimson carpet, joy stickers on every pillar, and colored lanterns dangling from the rafters like a sky full of stars. Turning right into the women's wing, she spotted Matron Wei returning from a toast with Madam Qin, caught her eye, and waited while Matron Wei whispered an excuse to Madam Xiang before hurrying over.

韦嬷嬷在桌旁坐下：“姑娘说吧，我洗耳恭听。”
"Miss sent you for me?"

千里寻也坐下，暂时忘记梦容受伤之事：“第一件，烦请嬷嬷明日将陈浩宇名下的财产过继到我名下。”
"Yes!"

韦嬷嬷并不惊讶：“此事我已听苦荞说过，明日定帮姑娘办妥。”
Slipping back to the bridal chamber, Matron Wei pushed open the door to find her mistress already stripped out of wedding crimson into everyday clothes. "The feast is not even over and you changed out of your bridal gown! The Chens will call you ill-mannered!"

“这第二件……”千里寻朝屋子周围瞧了瞧，“屋子的布置实在不合我心意，陈浩宇说了，他的院子以后就是我的，这屋子也归我，请嬷嬷安排人帮我重新布置一番。”
Taking her arm lightly, Qianli Xun brushed it off. "That gown felt like armor—give me everyday clothes any day. Sit down, Matron; I have errands to go over."

“姑娘放心，不出两日，定布置得跟望月轩的闺房一模一样。”韦嬷嬷爽快答应。
Sitting at the table, Matron Wei nodded. "I am all ears."

“还有我读过的书……”
Pushing Mengrong's injury aside for now, Qianli Xun ticked off her list. "First, tomorrow please transfer all of Chen Haoyu's assets into my name."

“姑娘更不用担心，天黑前红豆会带人用马车将姑娘的书统统搬来。”
"Kuqiao already told me," Matron Wei nodded without blinking. "I will handle it first thing tomorrow."

千里寻很满意，无论什么事，韦嬷嬷总能替她安排妥当。
"Second..." Qianli Xun gestured around the gaudy room. "This decor drives me crazy. Chen Haoyu promised me this whole courtyard and main hall—please have someone redecorate to my taste."

提起红豆，韦嬷嬷想起件事，向姑娘请示：“我刚经过院子，仔细观察，姑爷身边的下人不少，不缺伺候的人。望月轩的下人姑娘不必尽数带来，只留训练过拳法的十人，其他人给些银子都遣散回去，再将望月轩转手卖了，姑娘可同意？”
"Two days tops, and it will match your chamber at Wangyue Pavilion down to the brush-rest."

既已开启另一段人生，以前的也没必要留着，千里寻点点头：“嬷嬷说的我都同意。”
"And my books..."

韦嬷嬷还得去婚宴应酬：“姑娘若没其他事，我先去应酬，只怕夫人应付不过来。”
"Before nightfall, Hongdou will bring every volume over by carriage."

“有劳嬷嬷。”
Everything ran like clockwork with Matron Wei. Mention of Hongdou reminded the matron of one more item. "Walking through this courtyard, I noticed Young Master already has plenty of attendants. No sense hauling all of Wangyue Pavilion's staff here—keep just the ten trained in fist arts, send the rest off with generous severance silver, and sell Wangyue Pavilion. Agree?"

苦荞将门打开，韦嬷嬷的脚还未走出去，夏果出现在门口。
With a new chapter open, no need to cling to the old shell. "Agreed on every count."

韦嬷嬷笑道：“原来是乔家的夏姨娘，今日终于想起姑娘。”
"If nothing else, I must head back to the feast before Madam gets overwhelmed."

“嬷嬷别拿话噎我，是迟了些，可我到乔家也没几日。”夏果侧身从韦嬷嬷身边经过，进入屋内。
"Much obliged, Matron."

她身边的丫鬟艾墨出自望月轩，对韦嬷嬷十分恭敬，退到一边行礼，等韦嬷嬷离开后，才跟在夏果后面进入。
Just as Kuqiao opened the door for Matron Wei to exit, Xiaguo stepped into view.

夏果见到小姐自然高兴，行礼道：“小姐，我没及时回门看您，您可别怪我。”
"Well now—Concubine Xia of the Qiao house finally remembers her old mistress!" Matron Wei teased.

千里寻没有心情露出笑脸，淡淡地地说：“望月轩又不是你的娘家，谈不上回门。再说你我都在临安，见面的机会多的是。”
"Save the barbs, Matron—only been at Qiao Manor a few days!" Sliding past Matron Wei into the room—while her maid Aimo stepped aside to bow respectfully before following—Xiaguo curtsied warmly. "Please do not blame me for not coming back to visit sooner, Miss."

夏果毫不客气坐在小姐的桌旁，以前她只有站在小姐身旁的份儿，现在哪怕是给人当妾室，也可与小姐平起平坐。她瞧一眼苦荞，还是老样子，真是个当丫鬟的命。
With zero mood for fake warmth, Qianli Xun replied evenly, "Wangyue Pavilion is hardly your maiden home, so skip talk of post-wedding visits. Both of us live in Lin'an—plenty of chances to cross paths."

“今日陈家的婚礼果真气派，完全将乔家比下去。小姐，您可是没看到，苗知瑜和秦夫人那脸色，呵呵……！”
Without waiting for an invite, Xiaguo perched straight down beside her former mistress—no longer a standing maid, even as a concubine she sat level now! Throwing a smug glance at Kuqiao still standing in servant attire, she chuckled, "The Chen wedding really is grand—blew the Qiaos' clean out of the water! You should have seen the sour faces on Miao Zhiyu and Madam Qin! Heh!"

苦荞一脸不满：“夏姨娘何必笑乔家少夫人，她成亲那日，你也被接到乔家，她的婚礼也是你的婚礼。”
Kuqiao frowned sharply. "Why mock Young Madam Qiao, Concubine Xia? You entered Qiao Manor the exact same day—her wedding was your wedding too."

夏果忧伤道：“我只是个妾室，哪有资格谈婚礼。”
Xiaguo's face fell. "I am only a concubine—what right do I have to call it a wedding?"

苦荞说：“算你还有自知之明。”
"At least you know your place," Kuqiao muttered.

见此情景，千里寻打算岔开话题：“你可将家人接到城里住？”
Stepping in to shift topics, Qianli Xun asked, "Did you bring your family into town?"

提起家人，夏果想起小姐的恩情：“我是有此想法，凭小姐送给我的嫁妆，把家人接到城里，日子也能过得不错，可家人不同意，他们已习惯乡下的生活，我打算每隔一段时日给家里送些银子，也可让家人日子过得好些，省得我爷爷到处卖唱。”
Gratitude softened Xiaguo's eyes. "I wanted to—with the dowry Miss gave me, we could live well in Lin'an—yet my folks refused, too used to country life. I plan to send silver home regularly so Grandfather never has to wander singing for coin."

“老人家可好？”千里寻想起负鼓盲翁，“有机会还想再听他唱蔡伯喈的故事。”
"How is the old gentleman?" Remembering the blind drummer, Qianli Xun smiled. "I would love to hear him sing Cai Bojie's ballad again sometime."

“爷爷身体尚可，等过段时日，我差人把他接到城里，专门唱给小姐听。”
"Healthy enough! In a while I will bring him into town to sing privately for Miss."

“那可真是太好了！”
"That would be lovely!"

夏果脸上的笑容很快消失：“小姐，您不知道，我到乔家这些日子，还未与文渊圆房，苗知瑜也是。”
Then the smile slid right off Xiaguo's face. "Miss, you have no idea—all these days at Qiao Manor, Wenyuan has not consummated our marriage yet. Nor with Miao Zhiyu either."
