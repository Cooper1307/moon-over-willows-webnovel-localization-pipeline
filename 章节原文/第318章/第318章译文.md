## Chapter 318: Spying on Liang Shicheng from the Rooftops

Back at Chen Manor, Chen Haoyu handed the chest of silver notes to Steward Fang at Xinglong Pavilion, then hurried straight out to Secluded Forest Villa.

Night had fallen by the time he arrived. A gatekeeper informed him Prince Yi was taking dinner with Princess Consort Yi and told him to wait outside.

Hoping to lift Mengrong’s spirits, Shen Jianchen ate in her room.

Xilan helped prop Princess Consort Yi upright against the pillows, then fed her patiently one spoonful at a time.

Qiao Mengrong knew her brother sat beside her, yet between her weakened body and yesterday’s horrors, she couldn’t muster the energy to speak a single word to him.

Remembering the physician’s warning against any agitation, Shen Jianchen kept silent too, terrified one careless remark might worsen her state.

After dinner, once the maids cleared the dishes, Shen Jianchen sat on the bed’s edge and kept his voice feather-soft. “Would you like me to stay with you tonight?”

Qiao Mengrong’s blank gaze flickered. At the question, she shook her head violently. “No! Don’t stay with me, Brother—go back to your own room!”

Seeing her behave so unlike herself pained Shen Jianchen. Leaning forward, he pressed a light kiss onto her cheek.

At one time, a kiss from her brother would have sent Mengrong soaring. Instead, memories of Imperial Attendant Liang kissing her face and body hit like lightning; clutching her head, she curled into a tight ball, trembling uncontrollably.

Alarmed, Shen Jianchen asked, “What’s wrong, Mengrong? Are you frightened?”

She chanted over and over, “I want to go home, I want to go home…”

“You are already home.”

At his gentle reminder, Qiao Mengrong quieted, lowered her arms, and uncurled slowly.

Blaming himself for kissing her without thinking, Shen Jianchen waited until she looked calmer, helped her lie back down, tucked the quilt snugly around her, and stepped out.

No sooner had he reached his own courtyard than Bai Feng reported, “Your Highness, Chen Haoyu came looking for you—he’s been waiting outside the gate for a while.”

“Let him in.” Shen Jianchen was already in a foul mood; losing an official post meant nothing, yet Mengrong’s sudden collapse caught him completely off guard.

Too restless to go indoors, he stood out in the yard letting the crisp autumn wind blow through him, hoping it might clear the gloom sitting on his chest.

Striding in, Chen Haoyu dropped straight to his knees on the dirt without greeting. “Prince Yi! I beg you—save my wife, save the Chen family!”

All personal gloom vanished instantly. “What happened to her?”

“Imperial Attendant Liang started harassing our businesses. Father thought we could buy peace, so we carried silver notes to call on him—only he turned down five hundred thousand taels flat and demanded to see my wife instead! He’s clearly up to no good! Worse, he gave us two days: if my wife doesn’t go see him in two days, every Chen shop is finished! Out of options, I came begging Your Highness for help.”

“Does she know?”

“Not yet—and we can’t breathe a word to her! Knowing her, if she finds out, she’ll march straight into Liu’an Villa and beat Liang Shicheng black and blue, making everything ten times worse!”

“I’ll go pay Liang Shicheng a visit myself.” Leaving Chen Haoyu kneeling in the dirt, Shen Jianchen swept straight out of Secluded Forest Villa with Bai Feng at his heels.

By rights, seeing Prince Yi jump up without hesitation should have cheered Chen Haoyu—yet his heart only sank heavier. How desperately Prince Yi reacted the second anything touched his wife proved just how deeply the man still held her in his heart.

Chen Haoyu sat stunned on the flagstones for a long moment before climbing to his feet and heading home.

Meanwhile, Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng galloped two swift steeds straight toward the city.

Outside Liu’an Villa, Bai Feng tethered the horses out of sight, and the two used light-foot technique to vault over the perimeter wall.

Spotting a squad of patrol guards approaching, they ducked behind a cluster of trees. One glance at the guards’ uniforms told Shen Jianchen they had come straight from the Bianliang palace—bringing imperial guards all the way to Lin’an proved just how deep His Majesty’s trust in this crooked eunuch ran.

Shen Jianchen could have marched up to the front door openly, yet doing so would put Liang Shicheng on guard, hiding his true colors and his real motive for forcing the Chen family to hand over Youqi Jushi.

Evading the patrols, the two slipped onto the rooftops the first clear chance they got. Moving silently across the tiles, they caught the drift of music floating up from below and guessed Liang Shicheng must be inside that hall.

Reaching the roof above the music, they lifted two clay tiles and peered down through the gap. Sure enough, Liang Shicheng lounged indoors, sipping fine wine while watching dancers perform.

The women dancing wore sheer, skimpy garments far bolder than even the courtesans at Chunxiang Brothel.

Bai Feng muttered under his breath, “It’s already autumn—aren’t they freezing wearing next to nothing like that?”

Shen Jianchen didn’t answer, keeping his eyes locked below.

When the number ended, Liang Shicheng waved the musicians out, leaving only the scantily clad women behind.

“Bring the newcomer in.”

At his order, the door swung wide and a woman stepped inside.

Startled, Bai Feng whispered, “I recognize her! She’s Commander Lin’s concubine—the former courtesan! What was her name again?”

“Chun Jinqiao,” Shen Jianchen murmured back.

“Right, Chun Jinqiao! What on earth is she doing here?”

Bai Feng’s question echoed Shen Jianchen’s own bewilderment as both kept watching below.

Downstairs, Liang Shicheng chuckled, “Commander Lin really knows how to read the room—bright future ahead for that boy.”

Chun Jinqiao hadn’t been thrilled about servicing Imperial Attendant Liang either, yet Lin Jue’s smooth talking had won her over.

As Lin Jue put it, emptying the entire Lin treasury wouldn’t even make a dent in Imperial Attendant Liang’s eyes; sending a woman was the smartest ticket forward.

When Chun Jinqiao asked why he didn’t send Bai Meiruo instead of her, Lin Jue flattered her flat out: Bai Meiruo couldn’t hold a candle to her face, let alone her skill in bed. Once she kept Imperial Attendant Liang thoroughly pleased, not only would his own career shoot sky-high, she’d reap endless rewards too—so why pass up a golden deal?

Asking what she’d get out of it, she heard Lin Jue promise she’d become the most respected woman in all of Lin’an—even the new Prefect would step aside politely at the sight of her, and those snooty noblewomen who looked down on her courtesan past wouldn’t dare sneer again.

Thinking it over, Chun Jinqiao figured she’d entertained plenty of men in her brothel days anyway; one more wouldn’t kill her, especially with juicy rewards waiting and her own master pushing her straight through the door. So Lin Jue had dropped her outside Liu’an Villa, leaving her to walk in alone and offer herself up.

The moment Liang Shicheng finished speaking, Chun Jinqiao switched into full courtesan mode: sliding off her outer robe to bare smooth shoulders, she cast a coy, sultry glance his way. “Tell your servant how you’d like to be entertained tonight, Lord Attendant.”

Liang Shicheng grinned lewdly. “Hehehe… first time I’ve met one so eager. If every woman who waited on me were half as cooperative as you, it’d save me a world of bother.”

Up on the roof, Bai Feng hissed quietly, “Shameless hussy! Aren’t you scared Commander Lin will find out?”

“Didn’t you hear?” Shen Jianchen murmured. “Lin Jue sent her here himself.”

Bai Feng’s jaw dropped open and stayed stuck for three seconds. Tonight he truly learned how low men could sink for promotion—some would happily gift-wrap their own women and hand them over.

“Since you’ve taken off your outer robe,” Liang Shicheng said, “strip the rest off too and dance a tune for me like those girls.”

“What’s hard about that? Back at Chunxiang Brothel, I danced more rounds than I can count—I’ll outdance all of them. Only I’ve never tried dancing stark naked before; tonight seems a fine time to try.” Squeezing her voice syrupy-sweet, Chun Jinqiao peeled off one garment after another.

Eyes flashing red with excitement, Liang Shicheng laughed aloud. “Good! Good! What a rare little treasure!”

Bai Feng squirmed uncomfortably. “I’m not even married yet—better not watch this.”

Ignoring Bai Feng, Shen Jianchen kept staring closely, fearing Liang Shicheng’s demand to see Youqi Jushi hid some equally depraved scheme.

After looking away for a heartbeat, Bai Feng couldn’t fight his curiosity and peeked back down anyway.

Chun Jinqiao was now dancing completely naked. Watching made Bai Feng blush furiously and grow flustered, his mind suddenly flashing to Kuqiao’s face.

Seeing her far outshine them, the other dancers stepped back one by one.

Liang Shicheng rose and walked straight up to Chun Jinqiao, greedy eyes devouring every inch of her bare skin.

Not one bit shy, Chun Jinqiao swayed her hips circling around him, tossing provocative glances until she saw his eyes glow scarlet. Guessing the iron was hot, she melted limp into his chest. “Lord Attendant, let me wait on you properly in bed.”

To her shock, Liang Shicheng’s face darkened and he shoved her straight off. “Mocking me for not being a whole man?!”

Chun Jinqiao snapped alert instantly. Right—this man wasn’t intact; she’d never served a eunuch before and had slipped up badly. She had to smooth it over fast.

As the eunuch sat back on the couch, Chun Jinqiao glided over, sank onto his lap, and hooked her arms around his neck. “Nobody in my eyes comes close to you, Lord Attendant. Tell me whatever you want—I’ll gladly do it.”

Reaching beside the couch, Liang Shicheng grabbed a small case, snapped it open, and lifted out a needle. “Look—know what this is?”

Chun Jinqiao blinked lightly. “A needle. Why show me that, Lord Attendant?”

Holding the needle between two fingers as though admiring a priceless jewel, he murmured, “This brings me pleasure—means the world to me.”

Still clueless, she pouted playfully. “It’s just an ordinary sewing needle—how could it compare to me? Only I can bring you true bliss.”

“Ordinary, yes—until I sink it into your skin. The louder you scream, the happier I get.”

The smile froze dead on Chun Jinqiao’s face. So the Lord Attendant had *that* kind of twisted hobby—one glance at the glint of steel already sent phantom pricks tingling across her skin.

“What? Unwilling?”

Forcing a smile back, Chun Jinqiao cooed, “How could I be? I came here to please you! Only… isn’t that a bit cruel? Why not try another way? I didn’t spend all those years at Chunxiang Brothel for nothing—I picked up plenty of tricks for pleasing men. Care to test a few and see which you like best?”

“None of your tricks hold a candle to mine.” Leaning down, Liang Shicheng kissed Chun Jinqiao’s shoulder—and before she could blink, drove the needle straight in.

Stung out of nowhere, Chun Jinqiao gasped, “Ah!” Cursing him black and blue inside, she purred aloud, “Since you’ve already pricked me, Lord Attendant, I’ll play along! Only promise me one thing: after tonight, make me the highest-ranking woman in Lin’an—make those noble ladies watch *my* mood instead of me watching theirs! And make me Lin Jue’s primary wife, knocking Bai Meiruo down to concubine!”

“Easy enough. I have just one condition: hold me tight, and every time I jab you, scream as loud as you can. Louder you scream, happier I am.”

Wrapping both arms tight around Liang Shicheng, Chun Jinqiao buried her face against his chest and braced herself.

Pleased, Liang Shicheng kissed another patch of her pale skin and jabbed in again.

Pain forced a cry out of her—yet wanting to wrap this up fast, she worked her brothel polish into the scream, letting out a loud, sultry yowl like an alley cat in heat. That single shriek sent Liang Shicheng into half an eternity of bliss, sparing her several extra stabs.

Unable to stomach the sickening spectacle a second longer, Shen Jianchen boomed straight down from the roof, “My, Imperial Attendant Liang sure knows how to have a good time!”

Caught mid-raise for a third jab, Liang Shicheng heard Prince Yi’s voice from overhead—meaning everything in this room had just been spied on.

Dropping the needle, Liang Shicheng snapped at the half-naked women, “Throw her clothes on and haul her into the inner room!”

The women scrambled to drape garments over Chun Jinqiao.

Seizing one girl by the wrist, Liang Shicheng hissed in her ear, “Slip out the back door and tell the guards to wait outside for my signal.”

Nodding, the woman hurried Chun Jinqiao into the inner chamber.

Chun Jinqiao silently fumed: *What rotten timing! One more poke and I’d have been done! Now we’ll have to start all over again later—no dodging extra holes now!*

Adjusting his loose robe, Liang Shicheng stood and called up toward the rafters. “Since Prince Yi dropped by, why hide on the roof peeking at my private affairs? What’s the meaning of this?”

With a sharp bang, the hall door flew kicked inward, and Shen Jianchen strode in with Bai Feng beside him.

“If I’d walked through your front door like a gentleman, how would I ever catch such a grand show—or learn about Imperial Attendant Liang’s special little hobbies?”

“I’m not a whole man, Your Highness—I can’t taste the pleasures a prince enjoys, so I find my own path. Why look down your nose at me like some self-righteous saint?”

Shen Jianchen couldn’t care less about the man’s sick fetishes. His voice dropped to ice. “Touch whoever else you like and I won’t blink—lay one finger on Youqi Jushi, and my fists aren’t made of bean curd.”

Liang Shicheng gave a soft laugh. “Does Your Highness mean Princess Consort Yi is fair game then?”

In a flash, Shen Jianchen clamped one hand around Liang Shicheng’s throat. “Say one more filthy word and I’ll snap your neck clean off!”

“Just a joke—why take it so seriously, Your Highness?”

“One last warning: keep your hands off Youqi Jushi, or I take your life right here and now!”

Scarcely had the threat left his mouth when an arrow whistled straight through the window—dead on course for Shen Jianchen. Sharp-eyed Bai Feng spotted the shaft in time and lunged in front, taking the arrow square in his own body. Not a lethal spot, yet blood poured fast as Bai Feng crumpled to the floor with a groan.

Dropping Liang Shicheng, Shen Jianchen shoved him aside and dropped to catch Bai Feng. “Why did you do something so foolish?!”

Bai Feng forced a crooked grin. “I’m fine… watch out, Your Highness.”

Guards wielding naked blades flooded through the doorway from outside.

Shoved back onto the couch, Liang Shicheng settled himself casually and drawled, “Youqi Jushi is a married Chen daughter-in-law, Your Highness. No matter how badly you want her, you can’t have her—why make an enemy of me over her?”

Helping Bai Feng onto a chair nearby, Shen Jianchen faced the ring of guards. “You men belong to His Majesty’s personal guard—how dare you point blades at an imperial prince? I command you right now: arrest Liang Shicheng!”

One guard answered stiffly, “His Majesty ordered us to protect Imperial Attendant Liang—nowhere did he command us to take orders from Prince Yi in Lin’an!”

Liang Shicheng burst into loud laughter. “Hahaha! Hear that, Your Highness? His Majesty knows right from wrong—knows exactly who deserves protecting! Since His Majesty told you to guard my safety, what are you standing around for? Seize Prince Yi!”
