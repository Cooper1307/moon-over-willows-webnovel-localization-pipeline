## Chapter 318: Spying on Liang Shicheng from the Roof

Arriving at Youlin Retreat at dusk, Haoyu waited while Shen Jianchen quietly sat beside Mengrong during dinner—only to watch her flinch violently and chant *"Take me home!"* when he brushed a gentle kiss across her cheek, sending him out into the chilly yard heavy with guilt.

Dropping straight to his knees, Haoyu spilled Liang Shicheng's two-day ultimatum. Without wasting a syllable, Shen Jianchen swept Bai Feng onto horseback and galloped straight to Liu'an Villa.

Vaulting the garden walls past patrolling palace guards to catch Liang Shicheng's true colors off guard, master and guard crept across the tiles to a hall lit with music, lifted a roof tile, and froze in disgust: dancing bare-skinned before the leering eunuch was none other than Lin Jue's concubine **Chun Jinqiao**—gleefully delivered by her own ambitious husband to climb the official ladder!

Watching Liang Shicheng whip out his needle and jab Chun Jinqiao's shoulder—making her wail shamelessly as she bargained for Lin'an supremacy and primary-wife status over Bai Meiruo—pushed Shen Jianchen past his limit!

"Having a grand time down there, Imperial Attendant Liang?!" he roared from the eaves, kicking the front doors clean in alongside Bai Feng before Liang Shicheng could stab a third time.

As Liang Shicheng quietly signaled a girl to summon guards, Shen Jianchen clamped an iron grip around his throat: "Touch Youqi Jushi and I snap your neck!"

*TWANG!* An arrow zipped through the window straight for the prince's back—and Bai Feng threw himself across the shaft, dropping in a splash of blood!

Releasing Liang Shicheng to catch his bleeding guard, Shen Jianchen watched palace swordsmen pour through the doors, blatantly ignoring a royal prince's command because His Majesty's orders put Liang Shicheng above him!
