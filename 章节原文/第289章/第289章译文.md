## Chapter 289: Wait Two Years Before Consummating

Back at Chen Manor, eager to keep family dirt hidden, Chen Jianglin shut the parlor doors with Haodong alone, gently urging him to stop plotting against a cousin who posed zero threat to his inheritance—and swore him to silence even from his parents. Overjoyed that his uncle still clearly favored him as heir, Haodong hurried straight home to brief Chen Jiangshu and Madam Bao, who exhaled in relief and told him to cease fire—while Haodong privately figured it was finally time for Master Jiang to strike!

Meanwhile, Chen Jianglin broke the news to Yao Qingyan and floated a bold move: advancing the heir election by a full year to this very year to steady the clan, winning her full support before briefing Old Madam Xia.

Over at Moxiang Courtyard, Haoyu walked straight to Suxin Thatched Hall's closed door and dropped onto his knees in the blazing afternoon sun without a word—Qinggua dropping loyally beside him until Haoyu chased him off to tell Chouya he was safe.

Inside writing fresh huaben pages, Qianli Xun left him kneeling for half an hour—long enough for Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan to peek in, approve silently, and walk back out—before worrying sweat would infect his stabbed arm and opening the door.

Limping inside on numb legs with Kuqiao's arm, Haoyu swallowed Kuqiao's tea in one gulp, answered quietly about Jinlan's suicide, and begged forgiveness—brightening instantly when Qianli Xun barked at him never to breathe the word *divorce* again because she'd never trade away Grandmother and her in-laws.

After a short silence, Qianli Xun laid down her terms. "I was ready to fall for you and consummate soon—till all this blew up. So here's the deal: I'm giving you two years. Stay completely away from other women and keep yourself clean for me for two full years—and I'll consummate our marriage."

Light exploded across Haoyu's face. "Deal! Two years to scrub every stain off my past and make myself worthy of you!" Rising with an earnest bow, he vowed to treat her with pure respect until that day came—leaving Qianli Xun blinking awkwardly at his sudden solemnity.
