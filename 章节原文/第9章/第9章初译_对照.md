第9章 点茶聊天探朱府
## Chapter 9: Whisked Tea, Talk, and the Road to Zhu Manor

千里寻回到竹兰居时，苦荞正坐在茶桌前做茶。她来了兴致，坐在一旁仔细欣赏，暂时把正事抛诸脑后。
“怎么又做起茶来？”
苦荞用茶筅边在茶盏中击拂边说：“小姐每日吃完早饭，都要喝上一盏。”
“你手里那玩意儿叫什么名字？”
“茶筅。”
苦荞提起一旁的紫砂壶，沿着茶盏倒入些许热水，继续用茶筅击拂着，细腻的泡沫逐渐生起。
千里寻叹道：“好神奇！”
苦荞继续添些热水，用茶筅击拂，如此共添了七回水，直到茶汤被击拂得乳雾汹涌，溢盏而起，才作罢。她将茶筅放好，端起茶盏，递到小姐跟前：“小姐，您可以品尝了。”
千里寻接过，不再像第一次一样一饮而尽，她先品一小口，慢慢回味着茶的香味，再品第二口。顿时觉得高雅二字跟科技发达真没什么关系，在那个先进的时代她从未这么高雅过。
苦荞为小姐做完茶后，又到书桌前收拾，放好纸笔，开始研磨。
千里寻把剩下的最后一口茶品完，将茶盏放在桌上问道：“苦荞，你这是做什么？”
“小姐每日喝完茶，要坐在这里写诗作词或读书，我先替小姐预备好。”苦荞继续研磨。
这些都是苦荞每日不断重复的工作程序，她不愿丢掉，更不愿征求千里寻的意见，去努力适应重生的小姐，只因她不想从心底承认以前的小姐已不在。
千里寻在书桌前坐下，她从未真正有过自己的书桌。
上学时，书桌是父母和学校准备的，她并不喜欢。
后来,她凭借自己的能力买了房,装修时,设计师问她要不要设计一个书桌.她想了想,觉得不需要,只要个电脑桌就行.再后来电脑桌她也很少用,写小说时总是坐在床上,背靠靠垫,怀里抱着一只大型的HelloKitty,拿着手机开始码字.
穿越后成为朱淑真，才真正拥并且需要一个书桌。
千里寻看看桌上放着的笔墨纸砚，她的记忆停留在用手指敲击电脑和手机键盘的阶段，而朱淑真这双手只会拿毛笔写字。
﻿还好千里寻上中学时学过软笔书法，一直练到大学，大约也有七八年的功底。
千里寻用余光发现地上有一个方型木桶，里面放着许多卷起来的字画。她从中拿起一副字，将纸展开，上面用娟秀的字迹写着一首词：
玉体金钗一样娇，背灯初解绣裙腰，衾寒枕冷夜香消。
When Qianli Xun returned to Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao sat at the tea table making tea. Her curiosity sparked, so she pulled up a chair to watch, pushing serious errands to the back of her mind for a moment.

深院重关春寂寂，落花和雨夜迢迢，恨情和梦更无聊。（《浣溪沙·春夜》）
"Making tea again?" she asked.

这首词千里寻读过，此时翻开，以朱淑真的身份再次体会，更能感受到身处一段并不理想的婚姻中朱淑真的幽怨、不满和孤寂，以及她对理想爱情的渴望。
Whisking brisk waves inside the tea bowl with a bamboo tool, Kuqiao answered, "You drink a bowl after breakfast every morning, Miss."

千里寻提起笔，当笔尖吸饱墨汁后，照着纸上的笔迹将那词又抄了一遍。
"What do you call that gadget in your hand?"

苦荞见小姐撂下笔，凑过来欣赏了一会儿：“小姐的字和以前写得有七八分相似，再多练练，肯定会十分相似。”
"A tea whisk." Kuqiao lifted the purple clay teapot beside her, poured a splash of hot water along the rim of the bowl, and whisked again until fine froth began to rise.

千里寻想起和离之事：“苦荞，我得回趟朱府，你给我说说朱府的情况，我好提前有个准备。”
"That's magic!" Qianli Xun marveled.

苦荞摸着脑袋：“朱府的情况？小姐突然问，我一时不知从何说起。小姐能不能问具体点儿？”
Kuqiao added hot water and whisked again, seven infusions in all, until thick creamy foam surged to the very lip of the bowl. Setting the whisk down, she lifted the tea bowl and presented it. "Ready to taste, Miss."

千里寻又觉口渴：“我口渴，再给我做盏茶，你边做我们边聊。”
This time Qianli Xun did not gulp it down in one swallow. She took a small sip, lingered over the fresh fragrance, then took a second. Refinement, she thought, had nothing to do with modern gadgets; back in her high-tech era, she had never lived half this refined.

“好嘞。”苦荞又坐回茶桌前，打开盛茶粉的小瓷坛：“没有茶粉了，我现做些，小姐尽管问，我知无不言。”
With the tea served, Kuqiao moved to the study desk to straighten up, laying out brush and paper before grinding ink. Finishing her last mouthful, Qianli Xun set the bowl down and asked, "What are you up to now, Kuqiao?"

千里寻看着苦荞取了烤干的茶叶放入石臼中，用石杵轻轻捣着：“你先告诉我朱家都有哪些人？”
"Every morning after tea, you sit here to read or write poetry and ci, Miss. I'm getting everything ready," Kuqiao replied without pausing the inkstick. These daily rituals were habits she refused to drop, never once asking her reborn mistress what she preferred, because deep down she still could not bear admitting her old mistress was gone.

苦荞继续捣着茶叶：“老爷，夫人，大公子，以及少夫人，也就是小姐的嫂嫂，小姐嫁到林家时，少夫人已有八个月身孕，估计孩子已出生。”
Qianli Xun walked over and took a seat at the desk. She had never owned a real study desk before. During her school years, desks belonged to her parents or classrooms, and she had disliked them all. Later, when she bought her own apartment and the designer asked whether to include a study desk, she waved it off and asked for a simple computer desk instead. Even that gathered dust; whenever she wrote web novels, she propped herself against bed pillows, hugged a giant Hello Kitty plush, and tapped out chapters on her phone. Only after transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen did she both own and need a study desk.

“难道我嫁过来半年多从未回过一次朱家？”
She eyed the Four Treasures of the Study spread before her. Her fingers remembered keyboards and touchscreens, whereas Zhu Shuzhen's hands knew only the brush. Good thing Qianli Xun had practiced brush calligraphy from middle school through college, putting in seven or eight solid years.

苦荞将茶臼中捣碎的茶叶全部放入茶碾中，来回碾着：“当初是老爷答应下这门亲事，小姐开始根本不同意，可婚都定了，只能点头，被迫无奈才嫁过来，到第三日该回门时，小姐生气就没回。”
Out of the corner of her eye, she spotted a square wooden tub on the floor stuffed with rolled sheets of calligraphy. Pulling one out and smoothing it flat, she found a ci lyric penned in graceful strokes:

“我和父母的关系闹得很僵吗？”千里寻担心朱淑真与父母关系闹得太僵，会给合离增加难度。
"Spring night, my jade body is soft as a gold hairpin as, back to the lamp, I unfasten my silk skirt. But the quilt and pillow are cold. The night's fragrance is gone.

苦荞继续碾着茶叶：“当时是挺僵，不过父母与子女之间哪有什么隔夜仇？再说夫人好几次在街上碰见我，都向我问起小姐的情况。”
Spring is a deep courtyard of many locked doors. Petals falling in falling rain make the night seem forever. Regret comes to me in dream. There is no escape." ("Huanxisha: Spring Night") [TN: Translated by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping]

“你说得对，父母跟子女之间确实没有隔夜仇……”
Qianli Xun had read the lyric before, yet opening it now in Zhu Shuzhen's own shoes, she felt the poet's ache far deeper: the quiet resentment, loneliness, and frustration of a loveless marriage, and the fierce hunger for true love. She lifted the brush, soaked the tip in ink, and copied the entire poem stroke for stroke beside the original hand.

千里寻想起自己的父母。小时候，她像男孩子一样调皮，经常被父母批评，有一次甚至离家出走，结果被父母找回来，她搂着母亲哭了起来，所有怨气瞬间消散。
Once she laid the brush down, Kuqiao leaned over to admire the sheet. "Your writing is already seven or eight parts like before, Miss! Keep practicing and nobody could tell the difference."

后来母亲在她十二岁时出车祸离世，父亲把千里拳尽数传给她后，嘱咐她要将千里拳发扬光大，然后剃度出家，云游四海。父亲出家时，她刚好从大学毕业，从此独自撑起千里拳庄。
That reminded Qianli Xun of her real business. "Kuqiao, I have to make a trip back to Zhu Manor. Give me the rundown on the household so I know what I'm walking into."

也不知地震后，千里拳庄还是否幸存？
Kuqiao scratched her head. "Zhu Manor? Asking out of the blue, I don't know where to start. Could you ask something specific, Miss?"

想到这里，千里寻十分感伤，不由叹了口气。
Thirst crept back into Qianli Xun's throat. "My mouth's dry. Make me another bowl of tea, and we'll talk while you work."

苦荞已将茶叶碾成细小的茶末，用小刷子把茶末刷进茶筛中，听到小姐叹气，开解道：“小姐如若想念老爷太太，我就随小姐回去看看他们，他们看到您指定高兴。”
"Right away!" Returning to the tea table, Kuqiao opened the small porcelain jar for tea powder. "Out of tea powder, so I'll grind some fresh. Ask whatever you like, Miss; I'll tell you all I know."

千里寻说：“我想不通，女儿不回门，当爹的官职不小，难道就不能向他挑的女婿问问情况？我不相信林觉敢驳了临安知府的面子。”
Watching Kuqiao drop roasted tea leaves into a stone mortar and pound them with a stone pestle, Qianli Xun asked, "First, who lives in Zhu Manor?"

苦荞边筛茶边说：“老爷和姑爷之间的事，我一个丫鬟不是很了解，但我知道老爷十分看重清誉。”
"Master, Madam, Eldest Young Master, and Young Madam, your sister-in-law," Kuqiao replied as she worked the pestle. "When you married into the Lin family, Young Madam was already eight months pregnant, so the baby must be born by now."

千里寻心想未谋面的亲爹看重清誉，不知面对和离之事，他能否替女儿出面？
"I've been married over half a year and never went back home once?"

千里寻认为没有必要再向苦荞打听下去，等见到朱淑真的爹娘，亲自了解一番，再做打算。
Kuqiao transferred the crushed leaves into a tea roller and ground them back and forth. "Master agreed to the match on his own. You hated the idea, yet the betrothal was sealed, so you had no choice but to nod and go through with the wedding. On the third day, when it was time for the return visit to your maiden home, you were too furious to go."

“苦荞，做完这盏茶，就陪我回朱府。”
"Did I leave things real tense with my parents?" Qianli Xun worried a bitter fallout would ruin her chances at mutual divorce.

“好的，小姐。”
"Tense at the time, sure," Kuqiao said, keeping the roller moving. "Still, what parent holds an overnight grudge against their own child? Besides, Madam ran into me on the street several times and asked after you every time."

苦荞已筛完茶，把筛好的绿色茶末在茶盏中放些，剩下的全部用刷子刷进小瓷坛中放着备用。接着，在茶盏中加入些许热水后，拿起茶筅开始击拂，如此七回后，一盏茶做好，起身端到小姐跟前。
"True enough. Parents don't stay mad at their kids forever..."

“小姐，请用。”
Her own parents flashed across Qianli Xun's mind. Growing up wild as a boy, she had earned endless scoldings and once even ran away from home; the moment they found her, she threw her arms around her mother in tears and every bit of anger vanished on the spot. Then a car crash took her mother when Qianli Xun was twelve. Her father passed every technique of Qianli Fist on to her, charged her with keeping the school alive, and shaved his head to become a wandering monk right around her college graduation, leaving her to run Qianli Fist Manor alone. Had Qianli Fist Manor even survived the earthquake? Grief tightened her chest, and a heavy sigh escaped her.

千里寻接过，慨叹道：“看你做茶的过程，一步一步甚是用心，我都不忍心喝下它。”
Having ground the leaves into fine dust and brushed them into a tea sieve, Kuqiao heard the sigh and tried to lift her spirits. "If you miss Master and Madam, I'll go back with you to visit them, Miss. They'll be thrilled to see you."

“小姐只有喝下这盏茶，才不枉费我这番功夫。”
"What I don't get is this," Qianli Xun said. "If a daughter skips her return visit, her father holds a high post. Couldn't he question the son-in-law he handpicked? Don't tell me Lin Jue dares snub the Prefect of Lin'an."

“没想到你茶做得好，嘴也挺会说，从你这位丫鬟的身上就能看出你以前主子的风格，她一定是位事事都十分考究的人，哪像我大大咧咧……”
Sifting the tea powder, Kuqiao answered, "I'm only a maid, so I don't know what goes on between Master and Son-in-law. All I know is Master guards family honor above everything."

苦荞急了：“小姐说的什么话！我需要强调多少遍您才明白，我的主子只有您一位！”
A father obsessed with reputation. Would he step forward to back a mutual divorce? No sense pumping Kuqiao for guesses; better to meet Zhu Shuzhen's parents face to face and size them up herself.

千里寻没有理会苦荞，她急着回朱府，更确切地说，她想尽快结束这段对她来讲十分没有必要的婚姻，已没心情品茶，一口气喝下，放下茶盏后，站起来：“苦荞，回朱府！”
"Kuqiao, finish that bowl of tea, then come with me to Zhu Manor."

苦荞跟着小姐走出林府，吩咐林府的车夫套好马车，二人先后登上马车坐好。
"Yes, Miss." Done sifting, Kuqiao spooned green powder into the tea bowl and swept the surplus into the porcelain jar for later. She added hot water and whisked through seven infusions, then carried the finished bowl to her mistress. "Please drink, Miss."

这是千里寻第一次乘坐马车，充满新奇，比她第一次乘坐申简辰的劳斯莱斯还要新奇。在她眼中劳斯莱斯只不过是申简辰炫富的筹码，她最讨厌像申简辰那种有几个臭钱就在女孩子面前炫来炫去的男人，显得极其无知。
Taking the bowl, Qianli Xun sighed in admiration. "Watching you pour so much care into every single step makes me almost hate to drink it."

可当马车启动后在颠簸中前进，千里寻才从心底承认还是劳斯莱斯坐着舒服，她努力让自己坐稳。
"Only if you drink it is all that work worthwhile, Miss."

马车行了一会儿，听到热闹喧嚣之声，掀开布帘，向外瞧去，临安城的街道上人来人往，甚是热闹，街道两旁依也是店铺林立。马车从人群中穿梭而过，能听到各种交谈和讨价还价的声音；还有茶馆酒肆，也是人满为患，时不时从茶馆里传出说书的声音。
"Good with tea and smooth with words too. Just looking at you tells me what your old mistress was like: meticulous about every detail, nothing like sloppy me..."

千里寻放下帘子：“没想到临安的街市如此热闹。”
Kuqiao flared up at once. "What talk is that, Miss?! How many times do I have to say it? You are my one and only mistress!"

“这不算什么，东京汴梁才热闹。”
Ignoring the scolding, eager to reach Zhu Manor and cut loose from a marriage she had zero use for, Qianli Xun drained the tea in one gulp, set down the bowl, and stood up. "Kuqiao, let's go to Zhu Manor!"

“你去过？”
They walked out of Lin Manor, had the Lin coachman hitch a carriage, and climbed aboard one after the other. Her very first ride in a horse-drawn carriage thrilled Qianli Xun even more than her first ride in Shen Jianchen's Rolls-Royce. To her, that Rolls-Royce had been nothing more than a prop for flashing wealth, and she loathed men who waved cash in front of girls like clueless fools. Yet the instant the carriage lurched forward over uneven cobblestones, she had to admit the Rolls-Royce won hands down for comfort, bracing both hands to stay steady.

“没有，听小姐以前说的，东京汴梁有位魏夫人来信，邀请小姐去汴梁以诗会友，小姐还说什么时候带我去汴梁逛逛。”
Soon loud street clamor swelled outside. Lifting the cloth curtain, she looked out over Lin'an's crowded avenues, lined shoulder to shoulder with storefronts. As the carriage threaded through throngs of pedestrians, snippets of banter and haggling drifted in; teahouses and taverns overflowed with guests, snatches of storytelling carrying out onto the street.

“魏夫人？可叫魏玩？”
Dropping the curtain, Qianli Xun remarked, "Lin'an's markets are livelier than I expected."

“我不清楚，小姐从未对我说过魏夫人的名字。”
"This is nothing. Bianliang, the Eastern Capital, is way livelier!"

千里寻心想，应该就是魏玩，后世有资料记载，朱淑真与那魏玩有过来往，而且有诗为证。她此时没有心情搜寻那些诗词，还是想想怎么拿下朱淑真的爹娘。
"You've been there?"

一路思忖着，当周围安静下来时，马车缓缓停下。
"No, you told me before, Miss. A Lady Wei from Bianliang wrote inviting you up there to make friends through poetry, and you promised to take me sightseeing in the capital someday."

苦荞说：“小姐，到了。”
"Lady Wei? Is her name Wei Wan?"

千里寻多少有些紧张：“就这么到了？我还没想清楚见了爹娘说些什么，或者应该有什么样的表情。”
"No idea. You never told me her given name."

“小姐别紧张，这可是您从小生活的地方。”
Must be Wei Wan, Qianli Xun thought. Later historical notes recorded Zhu Shuzhen corresponding with Wei Wan, with surviving poems to prove it. Right now she had no patience to dig through old verse; figuring out how to win over Zhu Shuzhen's parents came first.

“有那么明显吗？都被你瞧出来。”
Lost in thought, she felt the clamor hush as the carriage rolled to a stop. "We're here, Miss," Kuqiao announced.

苦荞笑着没说话，先下了马车，站在车旁，等待伺候小姐。
Nerves fluttered in Qianli Xun's stomach. "Here already? I haven't even figured out what to say or what face to put on when I meet my parents!"

千里寻在车内犹豫了一会儿，还是鼓起勇气走下马车。
"Don't panic, Miss. You grew up right here."

苦荞见小姐出来，伸手去扶。
"Does it show that much? Even you noticed."

千里寻抬眼，看到朱红色的大门紧紧关闭，匾额上写着“朱府”二字，门前左右两边的石狮将朱府的大门衬得更加气派。
Kuqiao smiled without answering, hopped down first, and stood ready beside the step. After a brief hesitation inside, Qianli Xun braced herself and climbed down, taking Kuqiao's steadying hand. Looking up, she saw massive vermilion gates shut tight beneath a plaque reading "Zhu Manor," flanked by imposing stone lions that gave the entrance grand weight.

“苦荞，你去敲门。”
"Kuqiao, go knock."

“小姐，我们不从正门进入。”
"We don't enter through the main gate, Miss."

“不从正门进，难道要走后门？”
"Not the main gate? The back gate, then?"

“是侧门，小姐跟我来。”
"The side gate. Follow me, Miss."

“真是奇怪，不让人走，建得那么气派干嘛？岂不浪费？”
"Weird. Why build such a grand gate if nobody walks through it? What a waste!"

“小姐有所不知，正门平常不开，一旦府中来了贵重之人，才会打开正门迎接，小姐是自家人，适合走侧门，就连老爷平常也是走侧门。”
"You wouldn't know, Miss. The main gate stays shut unless honored guests visit. Family members use the side gate; even Master uses the side gate every day."

千里寻在苦荞的引领下向大门的左侧拐去，走了不多时，果然看到一个与正门比起来逊色许多的侧门，两边修竹成林，风景倒也雅致。
Following Kuqiao around the left wall, Qianli Xun soon reached a far more modest side gate framed by slender bamboo groves, quiet and tasteful.
