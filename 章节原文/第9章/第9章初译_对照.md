# 第9章 点茶聊天探朱府 / Chapter 9: Whisking Tea and Chatting on the Way to the Zhu Residence

---

## 段落 1

**原文**：千里寻回到竹兰居时，苦荞正坐在茶桌前做茶。她来了兴致，坐在一旁仔细欣赏，暂时把正事抛诸脑后。

**译文**：Upon returning to the Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun found Kuqiao sitting at the tea table, preparing tea. Piqued by curiosity, she sat down to watch, temporarily tossing her important errands to the back of her mind.

---

## 段落 2

**原文**：“怎么又做起茶来？”

**译文**：“Why are you making tea again？”

---

## 段落 3

**原文**：苦荞用茶筅边在茶盏中击拂边说：“小姐每日吃完早饭，都要喝上一盏。”

**译文**：Kuqiao whisked the tea in the bowl and said, “Miss drinks a bowl every day after breakfast.”

---

## 段落 4

**原文**：“你手里那玩意儿叫什么名字？”

**译文**：“What's that thing in your hand called？”

---

## 段落 5

**原文**：“茶筅。”

**译文**：“A tea whisk.”

---

## 段落 6

**原文**：苦荞提起一旁的紫砂壶，沿着茶盏倒入些许热水，继续用茶筅击拂着，细腻的泡沫逐渐生起。

**译文**：Kuqiao picked up the purple clay teapot nearby, poured some hot water along the edge of the tea bowl, and continued whisking. Fine foam gradually formed.

---

## 段落 7

**原文**：千里寻叹道：“好神奇！”

**译文**：“How magical！” Qianli Xun marveled.

---

## 段落 8

**原文**：苦荞继续添些热水，用茶筅击拂，如此共添了七回水，直到茶汤被击拂得乳雾汹涌，溢盏而起，才作罢。她将茶筅放好，端起茶盏，递到小姐跟前：“小姐，您可以品尝了。”

**译文**：Kuqiao added more hot water and whisked. She repeated this seven times until the tea liquor transformed into a surging, creamy froth that rose right to the brim. Only then did she stop. Setting the tea whisk aside, she picked up the bowl and presented it to her miss. “Miss, please have a taste.”

---

## 段落 9

**原文**：千里寻接过，不再像第一次一样一饮而尽，她先品一小口，慢慢回味着茶的香味，再品第二口。顿时觉得高雅二字跟科技发达真没什么关系，在那个先进的时代她从未这么高雅过。

**译文**：Qianli Xun took it. This time, instead of downing it in one gulp like last time, she took a small sip, slowly savoring the fragrance, before taking a second. It suddenly dawned on her that “elegance” had absolutely nothing to do with advanced technology. Back in her highly advanced era, she had never been this elegant.

---

## 段落 10

**原文**：苦荞为小姐做完茶后，又到书桌前收拾，放好纸笔，开始研磨。

**译文**：After finishing the tea for her miss, Kuqiao went to the desk to tidy up. She arranged the paper and brush, then began grinding ink.

---

## 段落 11

**原文**：千里寻把剩下的最后一口茶品完，将茶盏放在桌上问道：“苦荞，你这是做什么？”

**译文**：Qianli Xun finished the last sip of tea, placed the bowl on the table, and asked, “Kuqiao, what are you doing？”

---

## 段落 12

**原文**：“小姐每日喝完茶，要坐在这里写诗作词或读书，我先替小姐预备好。”苦荞继续研磨。

**译文**：“Every day after drinking tea, Miss sits here to write poems, compose ci, or read. I'm preparing everything for her in advance.” Kuqiao continued grinding ink.

---

## 段落 13

**原文**：这些都是苦荞每日不断重复的工作程序，她不愿丢掉，更不愿征求千里寻的意见，去努力适应重生的小姐，只因她不想从心底承认以前的小姐已不在。

**译文**：This was Kuqiao's unvarying daily routine. She refused to abandon it, nor did she bother asking Qianli Xun's opinion or trying to adapt to this “reborn” miss. Deep down, she simply couldn't accept that her original miss was truly gone.

---

## 段落 14

**原文**：千里寻在书桌前坐下，她从未真正有过自己的书桌。

**译文**：Qianli Xun sat down at the desk. She had never truly had a desk of her own.

---

## 段落 15

**原文**：上学时，书桌是父母和学校准备的，她并不喜欢。

**译文**：When she was in school, her parents and the school had provided desks, but she never liked them.

---

## 段落 16

**原文**：后来，她凭借自己的能力买了房，装修时，设计师问她要不要设计一个书桌。她想了想，觉得不需要，只要个电脑桌就行。再后来电脑桌她也很少用，写小说时总是坐在床上，背靠靠垫，怀里抱着一只大型的HelloKitty，拿着手机开始码字。

**译文**：Later, she bought a house on her own. During renovation, the designer asked if she wanted a desk. She thought about it and decided she didn't need one—a computer desk would do. Later still, she rarely used the computer desk either. When writing novels, she always sat on the bed, back against cushions, hugging a large Hello Kitty, typing on her phone.

---

## 段落 17

**原文**：穿越后成为朱淑真，才真正拥并且需要一个书桌。

**译文**：Only after transmigrating and becoming Zhu Shuzhen did she truly own and need a desk.

---

## 段落 18

**原文**：千里寻看看桌上放着的笔墨纸砚，她的记忆停留在用手指敲击电脑和手机键盘的阶段，而朱淑真这双手只会拿毛笔写字。

**译文**：Qianli Xun looked at the brush, ink, paper, and inkstone on the desk. Her muscle memory ended with typing on computer and phone keyboards, while Zhu Shuzhen's hands only knew how to hold a brush.

---

## 段落 19

**原文**：还好千里寻上中学时学过软笔书法，一直练到大学，大约也有七八年的功底。

**译文**：Fortunately, Qianli Xun had learned calligraphy with a soft brush in middle school and practiced through university, giving her seven or eight years of training.

---

## 段落 20

**原文**：千里寻用余光发现地上有一个方型木桶，里面放着许多卷起来的字画。她从中拿起一副字，将纸展开，上面用娟秀的字迹写着一首词：

**译文**：Out of the corner of her eye, Qianli Xun noticed a square wooden bucket on the floor filled with rolled-up calligraphy and paintings. She picked up one piece and unrolled it. A ci poem appeared in elegant handwriting:

---

## 段落 21

**原文**：玉体金钗一样娇，背灯初解绣裙腰，衾寒枕冷夜香消。
深院重关春寂寂，落花和雨夜迢迢，恨情和梦更无聊。（《浣溪沙·春夜》）

**译文**：Spring night, my jade body is soft as a gold hairpin,
as, back to the lamp, I unfasten my silk skirt.
But the quilt and pillow are cold. The night's fragrance is gone.
Spring is a deep courtyard of many locked doors.
Petals falling in falling rain make the night seem forever.
Regret comes to me in dream. There is no escape.

*翻译注解：本词采用 Tony Barnstone 与周平（Ping Zhou）合译的权威英译本，收录于《The Anchor Book of Chinese Poetry》。该译本在保持诗意意境的同时，更符合英语诗歌的表达习惯，适合网文小说的流畅阅读体验。*

---

## 段落 22

**原文**：这首词千里寻读过，此时翻开，以朱淑真的身份再次体会，更能感受到身处一段并不理想的婚姻中朱淑真的幽怨、不满和孤寂，以及她对理想爱情的渴望。

**译文**：Qianli Xun had read this ci before. But now, reading it through Zhu Shuzhen's identity, she could deeply feel the resentment, dissatisfaction, and loneliness of a woman trapped in an unfulfilling marriage, as well as her desperate longing for ideal love.

---

## 段落 23

**原文**：千里寻提起笔，当笔尖吸饱墨汁后，照着纸上的笔迹将那词又抄了一遍。

**译文**：Qianli Xun picked up the brush. After the tip absorbed enough ink, she copied the ci again, following the handwriting on the paper.

---

## 段落 24

**原文**：苦荞见小姐撂下笔，凑过来欣赏了一会儿：“小姐的字和以前写得有七八分相似，再多练练，肯定会十分相似。”

**译文**：Seeing her miss put down the brush, Kuqiao came over to admire it for a while. “Miss's handwriting is about seventy or eighty percent similar to before. With more practice, it will definitely become a perfect match.”

---

## 段落 25

**原文**：千里寻想起和离之事：“苦荞，我得回趟朱府，你给我说说朱府的情况，我好提前有个准备。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun thought about the divorce. “Kuqiao, I need to return to the Zhu residence. Tell me about the situation there so I can be prepared.”

---

## 段落 26

**原文**：苦荞摸着脑袋：“朱府的情况？小姐突然问，我一时不知从何说起。小姐能不能问具体点儿？”

**译文**：Kuqiao scratched her head. “The situation at the Zhu residence? You're asking so suddenly, Miss, I don't even know where to start. Could you be more specific？”

---

## 段落 27

**原文**：千里寻又觉口渴：“我口渴，再给我做盏茶，你边做我们边聊。”

**译文**：Feeling thirsty again, Qianli Xun said, “I'm thirsty. Make me another bowl of tea, and we can chat while you do.”

---

## 段落 28

**原文**：“好嘞。”苦荞又坐回茶桌前，打开盛茶粉的小瓷坛：“没有茶粉了，我现做些，小姐尽管问，我知无不言。”

**译文**：“Alright.” Kuqiao sat back down at the tea table and opened a small porcelain jar. “There's no tea powder left. I'll grind some now. Miss can ask whatever you want, and I'll tell you everything I know.”

---

## 段落 29

**原文**：千里寻看着苦荞取了烤干的茶叶放入石臼中，用石杵轻轻捣着：“你先告诉我朱家都有哪些人？”

**译文**：Qianli Xun watched Kuqiao place roasted tea leaves into a stone mortar, gently pounding them with a pestle. “First, tell me who is in the Zhu family.”

---

## 段落 30

**原文**：苦荞继续捣着茶叶：“老爷，夫人，大公子，以及少夫人，也就是小姐的嫂嫂，小姐嫁到林家时，少夫人已有八个月身孕，估计孩子已出生。”

**译文**：Kuqiao continued pounding the tea leaves. “The master, the madam, the eldest son, and the young mistress—that's your sister-in-law. When you married into the Lin family, Miss, the young mistress was already eight months pregnant. The child has probably been born by now.”

---

## 段落 31

**原文**：“难道我嫁过来半年多从未回过一次朱家？”

**译文**：“Could it be that I haven't returned to the Zhu family once in over half a year since I married here？”

---

## 段落 32

**原文**：苦荞将茶臼中捣碎的茶叶全部放入茶碾中，来回碾着：“当初是老爷答应下这门亲事，小姐开始根本不同意，可婚都定了，只能点头，被迫无奈才嫁过来，到第三日该回门时，小姐生气就没回。”

**译文**：Kuqiao transferred the crushed leaves to a tea roller, grinding them back and forth. “It was the master who agreed to this marriage. Miss strongly opposed it at first, but once it was set in stone, you could only nod and marry here against your will. On the third day, when it was time for the customary visit back to your maiden home, you were so angry you refused to go.”

---

## 段落 33

**原文**：“我和父母的关系闹得很僵吗？”千里寻担心朱淑真与父母关系闹得太僵，会给合离增加难度。

**译文**：“Is my relationship with my parents very tense？” Qianli Xun worried that if Zhu Shuzhen's relationship with her parents was too strained, it would make the divorce more difficult.

---

## 段落 34

**原文**：苦荞继续碾着茶叶：“当时是挺僵，不过父母与子女之间哪有什么隔夜仇？再说夫人好几次在街上碰见我，都向我问起小姐的情况。”

**译文**：Kuqiao continued rolling the tea leaves. “It was pretty tense at the time, but parents and children don't hold grudges overnight. Besides, Madam has run into me on the street several times and always asks how you are doing.”

---

## 段落 35

**原文**：“你说得对，父母跟子女之间确实没有隔夜仇……”

**译文**：“You're right. There really is no grudge between parents and children that lasts overnight...”

---

## 段落 36

**原文**：千里寻想起自己的父母。小时候，她像男孩子一样调皮，经常被父母批评，有一次甚至离家出走，结果被父母找回来，她搂着母亲哭了起来，所有怨气瞬间消散。

**译文**：Qianli Xun thought of her own parents. When she was little, she was a real tomboy and often got scolded by her parents. Once, she even ran away from home, but her parents found her. She hugged her mother and cried, and all her resentment instantly vanished.

---

## 段落 37

**原文**：后来母亲在她十二岁时出车祸离世，父亲把千里拳尽数传给她后，嘱咐她要将千里拳发扬光大，然后剃度出家，云游四海。父亲出家时，她刚好从大学毕业，从此独自撑起千里拳庄。

**译文**：Later, her mother died in a car accident when she was twelve. After her father taught her all the Qianli Fist techniques and instructed her to carry on the legacy, he took the tonsure and became a wandering monk. When he left, she had just graduated from university and had single-handedly kept the Qianli Fist Manor afloat ever since.

---

## 段落 38

**原文**：也不知地震后，千里拳庄还是否幸存？

**译文**：She wondered if the Qianli Fist Manor had survived the earthquake.

---

## 段落 39

**原文**：想到这里，千里寻十分感伤，不由叹了口气。

**译文**：Thinking of this, Qianli Xun felt very sad and couldn't help sighing.

---

## 段落 40

**原文**：苦荞已将茶叶碾成细小的茶末，用小刷子把茶末刷进茶筛中，听到小姐叹气，开解道：“小姐如若想念老爷太太，我就随小姐回去看看他们，他们看到您指定高兴。”

**译文**：Kuqiao had already ground the tea leaves into fine powder and swept it into a sieve with a small brush. Hearing the sigh, she softly comforted her, “If Miss misses the master and madam, I'll accompany you back to visit them. They'll definitely be overjoyed to see you.”

---

## 段落 41

**原文**：千里寻说：“我想不通，女儿不回门，当爹的官职不小，难道就不能向他挑的女婿问问情况？我不相信林觉敢驳了临安知府的面子。”

**译文**：“I just don't get it，” Qianli Xun muttered. “A daughter refuses to return home, and her father holds a high official position. Couldn't he at least question the son-in-law he handpicked? I doubt Lin Jue would dare slight the Prefect of Lin'an.”

---

## 段落 42

**原文**：苦荞边筛茶边说：“老爷和姑爷之间的事，我一个丫鬟不是很了解，但我知道老爷十分看重清誉。”

**译文**：Kuqiao sifted the tea as she spoke. “The matters between the master and the son-in-law—I'm just a maid and don't know much about them. But I know the master values his reputation highly.”

---

## 段落 43

**原文**：千里寻心想未谋面的亲爹看重清誉，不知面对和离之事，他能否替女儿出面？

**译文**：Qianli Xun thought to herself that her unseen father valued reputation. She wondered if, when faced with the matter of divorce, he would actually stand up for his daughter.

---

## 段落 44

**原文**：千里寻认为没有必要再向苦荞打听下去，等见到朱淑真的爹娘，亲自了解一番，再做打算。

**译文**：Qianli Xun decided there was no need to ask Kuqiao any more. She would wait until she met Zhu Shuzhen's parents and find out in person before making plans.

---

## 段落 45

**原文**：“苦荞，做完这盏茶，就陪我回朱府。”

**译文**：“Kuqiao, after you finish this bowl of tea, accompany me back to the Zhu residence.”

---

## 段落 46

**原文**：“好的，小姐。”

**译文**：“Yes, Miss.”

---

## 段落 47

**原文**：苦荞已筛完茶，把筛好的绿色茶末在茶盏中放些，剩下的全部用刷子刷进小瓷坛中放着备用。接着，在茶盏中加入些许热水后，拿起茶筅开始击拂，如此七回后，一盏茶做好，起身端到小姐跟前。

**译文**：Kuqiao had finished sifting the tea. She put some of the green powder into the tea bowl and swept the rest into the small porcelain jar for later use. Then she added some hot water to the bowl, picked up the tea whisk, and began whisking. After seven rounds, a bowl of tea was ready. She got up and brought it to her miss.

---

## 段落 48

**原文**：“小姐，请用。”

**译文**：“Miss, please enjoy.”

---

## 段落 49

**原文**：千里寻接过，慨叹道：“看你做茶的过程，一步一步甚是用心，我都不忍心喝下它。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun took it and sighed, “Watching you make tea, step by step with such care, I can't bear to drink it.”

---

## 段落 50

**原文**：“小姐只有喝下这盏茶，才不枉费我这番功夫。”

**译文**：“Only by drinking this bowl of tea will Miss not waste my effort.”

---

## 段落 51

**原文**：“没想到你茶做得好，嘴也挺会说，从你这位丫鬟的身上就能看出你以前主子的风格，她一定是位事事都十分考究的人，哪像我大大咧咧……”

**译文**：“I didn't expect you to be so skilled at making tea and so sharp-tongued. From you, a maid, I can see the style of your previous miss. She must have been someone who was very particular about everything. Not like me, so careless...”

---

## 段落 52

**原文**：苦荞急了：“小姐说的什么话！我需要强调多少遍您才明白，我的主子只有您一位！”

**译文**：Kuqiao panicked. “What are you saying, Miss? How many times must I emphasize it before you believe me? I only have one miss—and that is you！”

---

## 段落 53

**原文**：千里寻没有理会苦荞，她急着回朱府，更确切地说，她想尽快结束这段对她来讲十分没有必要的婚姻，已没心情品茶，一口气喝下，放下茶盏后，站起来：“苦荞，回朱府！”

**译文**：Qianli Xun didn't respond. Eager to return to the Zhu residence and put an end to this completely unnecessary marriage as soon as possible, she was in no mood to savor the tea. She downed it in one gulp, slammed the bowl down, and stood up. “Kuqiao, let's go！”

---

## 段落 54

**原文**：苦荞跟着小姐走出林府，吩咐林府的车夫套好马车，二人先后登上马车坐好。

**译文**：Kuqiao followed her miss out of the Lin Manor and instructed the Lin family's coachman to harness the horse carriage. The two of them boarded the carriage one after the other and sat down.

---

## 段落 55

**原文**：这是千里寻第一次乘坐马车，充满新奇，比她第一次乘坐申简辰的劳斯莱斯还要新奇。在她眼中劳斯莱斯只不过是申简辰炫富的筹码，她最讨厌像申简辰那种有几个臭钱就在女孩子面前炫来炫去的男人，显得极其无知。

**译文**：This was Qianli Xun's first time riding in a horse carriage. The novelty of it all far surpassed her first ride in Shen Jianchen's Rolls-Royce. In her eyes, that car was nothing more than a prop for him to flaunt his wealth. She absolutely despised men like him—those who waved their dirty money in front of women, appearing nothing but shallow.

---

## 段落 56

**原文**：可当马车启动后在颠簸中前进，千里寻才从心底承认还是劳斯莱斯坐着舒服，她努力让自己坐稳。

**译文**：The carriage started and moved forward with bumps and jolts. Qianli Xun had to admit deep down that the Rolls-Royce was more comfortable to sit in. She tried hard to steady herself.

---

## 段落 57

**原文**：马车行了一会儿，听到热闹喧嚣之声，掀开布帘，向外瞧去，临安城的街道上人来人往，甚是热闹，街道两旁依也是店铺林立。马车从人群中穿梭而过，能听到各种交谈和讨价还价的声音；还有茶馆酒肆，也是人满为患，时不时从茶馆里传出说书的声音。

**译文**：After the carriage traveled for a while, she heard bustling sounds. She lifted the cloth curtain and looked out. The streets of Lin'an City were crowded with people coming and going, very lively. Shops lined both sides of the streets. The carriage wove through the crowd, and she could hear all kinds of conversations and bargaining. There were also tea houses and taverns, packed with customers. From time to time, the sound of storytellers drifted out from inside.

---

## 段落 58

**原文**：千里寻放下帘子：“没想到临安的街市如此热闹。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun put down the curtain. “I didn't expect Lin'an's markets to be this lively.”

---

## 段落 59

**原文**：“这不算什么，东京汴梁才热闹。”

**译文**：“This is nothing. Dongjing Bianliang is where it's really lively.”

---

## 段落 60

**原文**：“你去过？”

**译文**：“Have you been there？”

---

## 段落 61

**原文**：“没有，听小姐以前说的，东京汴梁有位魏夫人来信，邀请小姐去汴梁以诗会友，小姐还说什么时候带我去汴梁逛逛。”

**译文**：“No, I heard Miss say before that Lady Wei from Dongjing Bianliang sent a letter inviting her to make friends through poetry. She also said she'd take me to visit the capital sometime.”

---

## 段落 62

**原文**：“魏夫人？可叫魏玩？”

**译文**：“Lady Wei? Is her name Wei Wan？”

---

## 段落 63

**原文**：“我不清楚，小姐从未对我说过魏夫人的名字。”

**译文**：“I'm not sure. Miss never told me Lady Wei's name.”

---

## 段落 64

**原文**：千里寻心想，应该就是魏玩，后世有资料记载，朱淑真与那魏玩有过来往，而且有诗为证。她此时没有心情搜寻那些诗词，还是想想怎么拿下朱淑真的爹娘。

**译文**：Qianli Xun thought it must be Wei Wan. Later historical records showed that Zhu Shuzhen had corresponded with her, with poems as evidence. She had no mood to search for those verses now. Better to think about how to win over Zhu Shuzhen's parents.

---

## 段落 65

**原文**：一路思忖着，当周围安静下来时，马车缓缓停下。

**译文**：She pondered all the way. When the surroundings grew quiet, the carriage slowly stopped.

---

## 段落 66

**原文**：苦荞说：“小姐，到了。”

**译文**：Kuqiao said, “Miss, we've arrived.”

---

## 段落 67

**原文**：千里寻多少有些紧张：“就这么到了？我还没想清楚见了爹娘说些什么，或者应该有什么样的表情。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun was somewhat nervous. “We're here already? I haven't even figured out what to say or what expression to wear when I see them.”

---

## 段落 68

**原文**：“小姐别紧张，这可是您从小生活的地方。”

**译文**：“Don't be nervous, Miss. This is the place where you grew up.”

---

## 段落 69

**原文**：“有那么明显吗？都被你瞧出来。”

**译文**：“Is it that obvious? Even you noticed.”

---

## 段落 70

**原文**：苦荞笑着没说话，先下了马车，站在车旁，等待伺候小姐。

**译文**：Kuqiao smiled without speaking. She got off the carriage first, stood beside it, and waited to help her miss.

---

## 段落 71

**原文**：千里寻在车内犹豫了一会儿，还是鼓起勇气走下马车。

**译文**：Qianli Xun hesitated in the carriage for a moment, then steeled herself and got off.

---

## 段落 72

**原文**：苦荞见小姐出来，伸手去扶。

**译文**：Seeing her miss come out, Kuqiao reached out to help her.

---

## 段落 73

**原文**：千里寻抬眼，看到朱红色的大门紧紧关闭，匾额上写着“朱府”二字，门前左右两边的石狮将朱府的大门衬得更加气派。

**译文**：Qianli Xun looked up and saw the vermilion main gate tightly closed. The plaque above read “Zhu Residence.” The stone lions on both sides of the gate made the entrance look even more grand.

---

## 段落 74

**原文**：“苦荞，你去敲门。”

**译文**：“Kuqiao, go knock on the door.”

---

## 段落 75

**原文**：“小姐，我们不从正门进入。”

**译文**：“Miss, we don't enter through the main gate.”

---

## 段落 76

**原文**：“不从正门进，难道要走后门？”

**译文**：“If not through the main gate, are we going through the back door？”

---

## 段落 77

**原文**：“是侧门，小姐跟我来。”

**译文**：“It's the side gate. Come with me, Miss.”

---

## 段落 78

**原文**：“真是奇怪，不让人走，建得那么气派干嘛？岂不浪费？”

**译文**：“How strange. If they don't let people use it, why build it so grand? Isn't that a waste？”

---

## 段落 79

**原文**：“小姐有所不知，正门平常不开，一旦府中来了贵重之人，才会打开正门迎接，小姐是自家人，适合走侧门，就连老爷平常也是走侧门。”

**译文**：“Miss, you may not know, the main gate is usually kept closed. Only when important guests come to the residence is it opened to welcome them. As a family member, Miss should use the side gate. Even the master uses it most of the time.”

---

## 段落 80

**原文**：千里寻在苦荞的引领下向大门的左侧拐去，走了不多时，果然看到一个与正门比起来逊色许多的侧门，两边修竹成林，风景倒也雅致。

**译文**：Led by Kuqiao, Qianli Xun turned to the left of the main gate. After walking a short distance, she indeed saw a side gate that was much less grand compared to the main gate. Tall bamboo grew on both sides, creating a refined scene.
