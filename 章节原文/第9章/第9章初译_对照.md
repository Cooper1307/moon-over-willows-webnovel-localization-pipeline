第9章 点茶聊天探朱府
## Chapter 9: Chapter 9: Whisked Tea, Talk, and the Road to Zhu Manor

千里寻回到竹兰居时，苦荞正坐在茶桌前做茶。她来了兴致，坐在一旁仔细欣赏，暂时把正事抛诸脑后。
“怎么又做起茶来？”
When Qianli Xun returned to Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao sat at the tea table making tea. Intrigued, Qianli Xun pulled up a seat to watch, putting serious business on hold for a moment.

苦荞用茶筅边在茶盏中击拂边说：“小姐每日吃完早饭，都要喝上一盏。”
"Making tea again?"

“你手里那玩意儿叫什么名字？”
Whisking away inside the tea bowl with her bamboo whisk, Kuqiao replied, "You drink a bowl after breakfast every morning, Miss."

“茶筅。”
"What's that gadget in your hand called?"

苦荞提起一旁的紫砂壶，沿着茶盏倒入些许热水，继续用茶筅击拂着，细腻的泡沫逐渐生起。
"A tea whisk."

千里寻叹道：“好神奇！”
Kuqiao lifted the purple clay teapot beside her, poured a splash of hot water along the rim of the bowl, and whisked again until fine foam began to rise.

苦荞继续添些热水，用茶筅击拂，如此共添了七回水，直到茶汤被击拂得乳雾汹涌，溢盏而起，才作罢。她将茶筅放好，端起茶盏，递到小姐跟前：“小姐，您可以品尝了。”
Qianli Xun marveled, "That's magic!"

千里寻接过，不再像第一次一样一饮而尽，她先品一小口，慢慢回味着茶的香味，再品第二口。顿时觉得高雅二字跟科技发达真没什么关系，在那个先进的时代她从未这么高雅过。
Kuqiao added hot water and whisked again—seven infusions in all—until thick, creamy foam surged to the very brim of the bowl. Setting the whisk down, she lifted the bowl and offered it. "Ready to taste, Miss."

苦荞为小姐做完茶后，又到书桌前收拾，放好纸笔，开始研磨。
This time Qianli Xun didn't gulp it down. She took a small sip, savored the lingering aroma, then took another. Refinement, she realized, had nothing to do with modern technology—back in her high-tech era, she had never felt half this refined.

千里寻把剩下的最后一口茶品完，将茶盏放在桌上问道：“苦荞，你这是做什么？”
Tea done, Kuqiao moved to the writing desk to tidy up, laid out paper and brushes, and began grinding ink.

“小姐每日喝完茶，要坐在这里写诗作词或读书，我先替小姐预备好。”苦荞继续研磨。
Finishing her last sip, Qianli Xun set the bowl down. "What are you doing now, Kuqiao?"

这些都是苦荞每日不断重复的工作程序，她不愿丢掉，更不愿征求千里寻的意见，去努力适应重生的小姐，只因她不想从心底承认以前的小姐已不在。
"Every morning after tea, you sit here to read or write poetry and ci, Miss. I'm getting everything ready." Kuqiao kept grinding ink.

千里寻在书桌前坐下，她从未真正有过自己的书桌。
These were the daily rituals Kuqiao repeated without fail; she refused to drop them or ask Qianli Xun's preference, unable in her heart to admit her former mistress was truly gone.

上学时，书桌是父母和学校准备的，她并不喜欢。
Qianli Xun took a seat at the desk. She had never owned a real study desk before.

后来，她凭借自己的能力买了房，装修时，设计师问她要不要设计一个书桌。她想了想，觉得不需要，只要个电脑桌就行。再后来电脑桌她也很少用，写小说时总是坐在床上，背靠靠垫，怀里抱着一只大型的HelloKitty，拿着手机开始码字。
During school years, desks belonged to her parents or classrooms, and she had never cared for them.

穿越后成为朱淑真，才真正拥并且需要一个书桌。
Later, when she bought her own apartment, the designer asked if she wanted a study desk. She said no—a computer desk would do. Even that sat idle most days; she wrote her web novels propped up in bed against cushions, hugging a giant Hello Kitty plush while tapping away on her phone.

千里寻看看桌上放着的笔墨纸砚，她的记忆停留在用手指敲击电脑和手机键盘的阶段，而朱淑真这双手只会拿毛笔写字。
Only after transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen did she both own and need a proper desk.

﻿还好千里寻上中学时学过软笔书法，一直练到大学，大约也有七八年的功底。
She eyed the Four Treasures of the Study laid out before her. Her muscles remembered keyboards and phone screens, whereas Zhu Shuzhen's hands knew only the brush.

千里寻用余光发现地上有一个方型木桶，里面放着许多卷起来的字画。她从中拿起一副字，将纸展开，上面用娟秀的字迹写着一首词：
Good thing Qianli Xun had studied brush calligraphy from middle school through college—seven or eight solid years of practice.

玉体金钗一样娇，背灯初解绣裙腰，衾寒枕冷夜香消。
Out of the corner of her eye, she spotted a square wooden tub on the floor stuffed with rolled sheets of calligraphy. Pulling one out and unrolling it, she saw graceful brushstrokes spelling out a ci:

深院重关春寂寂，落花和雨夜迢迢，恨情和梦更无聊。（《浣溪沙·春夜》）
Spring night, my jade body is soft as a gold hairpin as, back to the lamp, I unfasten my silk skirt. But the quilt and pillow are cold. The night's fragrance is gone.

这首词千里寻读过，此时翻开，以朱淑真的身份再次体会，更能感受到身处一段并不理想的婚姻中朱淑真的幽怨、不满和孤寂，以及她对理想爱情的渴望。
Spring is a deep courtyard of many locked doors. Petals falling in falling rain make the night seem forever. Regret comes to me in dream. There is no escape. ("Huanxisha: Spring Night") [TN: Translated by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping]

千里寻提起笔，当笔尖吸饱墨汁后，照着纸上的笔迹将那词又抄了一遍。
Qianli Xun had read this lyric before. Opening it now in Zhu Shuzhen's own shoes, she felt far deeper the ache, frustration, and loneliness of a loveless marriage—and the poet's yearning for true love.

苦荞见小姐撂下笔，凑过来欣赏了一会儿：“小姐的字和以前写得有七八分相似，再多练练，肯定会十分相似。”
Picking up the brush, she dipped the tip full of ink and copied the lyric line for line, matching the original hand.

千里寻想起和离之事：“苦荞，我得回趟朱府，你给我说说朱府的情况，我好提前有个准备。”
Once she set the brush down, Kuqiao leaned over to admire the page. "Your writing is already seven or eight parts like before, Miss! A bit more practice and nobody could tell them apart."

苦荞摸着脑袋：“朱府的情况？小姐突然问，我一时不知从何说起。小姐能不能问具体点儿？”
Remembering the mutual divorce, Qianli Xun said, "Kuqiao, I need to make a trip back to Zhu Manor. Tell me about the family so I know what I'm walking into."

千里寻又觉口渴：“我口渴，再给我做盏茶，你边做我们边聊。”
Kuqiao scratched her head. "Zhu Manor? Asking all at once, I don't know where to begin. Could you ask something specific, Miss?"

“好嘞。”苦荞又坐回茶桌前，打开盛茶粉的小瓷坛：“没有茶粉了，我现做些，小姐尽管问，我知无不言。”
Thirst crept back. "I'm thirsty—make me another bowl of tea, and we'll talk while you work."

千里寻看着苦荞取了烤干的茶叶放入石臼中，用石杵轻轻捣着：“你先告诉我朱家都有哪些人？”
"Right away!" Returning to the tea table, Kuqiao opened the small porcelain jar for tea powder. "Out of powder—I'll grind some fresh. Ask anything you want, Miss, I'll tell you all I know."

苦荞继续捣着茶叶：“老爷，夫人，大公子，以及少夫人，也就是小姐的嫂嫂，小姐嫁到林家时，少夫人已有八个月身孕，估计孩子已出生。”
Watching Kuqiao put dried tea leaves into a stone mortar and pound them with a stone pestle, Qianli Xun asked, "First, who lives in Zhu Manor?"

“难道我嫁过来半年多从未回过一次朱家？”
"Master, Madam, Eldest Young Master, and Young Madam—your sister-in-law," Kuqiao said as she pounded. "When you married into the Lin house, Young Madam was eight months pregnant, so the baby must be born by now."

苦荞将茶臼中捣碎的茶叶全部放入茶碾中，来回碾着：“当初是老爷答应下这门亲事，小姐开始根本不同意，可婚都定了，只能点头，被迫无奈才嫁过来，到第三日该回门时，小姐生气就没回。”
"I've been married over half a year and never went back once?"

“我和父母的关系闹得很僵吗？”千里寻担心朱淑真与父母关系闹得太僵，会给合离增加难度。
Kuqiao transferred the crushed leaves into a tea roller and ground them back and forth. "Master agreed to the match without asking you. You hated the idea, yet the betrothal was sealed, so you had no choice but to go through with the wedding. On the third day, when it was time for the return visit to your maiden home, you were too furious to go."

苦荞继续碾着茶叶：“当时是挺僵，不过父母与子女之间哪有什么隔夜仇？再说夫人好几次在街上碰见我，都向我问起小姐的情况。”
"Did I leave things real tense with my parents?" Qianli Xun worried a bitter rift would complicate the divorce.

“你说得对，父母跟子女之间确实没有隔夜仇……”
"Tense at the time, sure," Kuqiao said, keeping the roller moving. "Still, what parent holds an overnight grudge against their own child? Besides, Madam bumped into me on the street several times and asked after you every time."

千里寻想起自己的父母。小时候，她像男孩子一样调皮，经常被父母批评，有一次甚至离家出走，结果被父母找回来，她搂着母亲哭了起来，所有怨气瞬间消散。
"True enough—parents don't stay mad at their kids forever..."

后来母亲在她十二岁时出车祸离世，父亲把千里拳尽数传给她后，嘱咐她要将千里拳发扬光大，然后剃度出家，云游四海。父亲出家时，她刚好从大学毕业，从此独自撑起千里拳庄。
Thoughts of her own parents surfaced. Growing up as wild as any boy, she had gotten scolded nonstop, once even running away from home—only to hug her mother in tears the moment they found her, every grievance melting away on the spot.

也不知地震后，千里拳庄还是否幸存？
Later, when she was twelve, a car crash took her mother. Her father taught her every move of Qianli Fist, charged her with keeping the style alive, then shaved his head to become a wandering monk right around her college graduation—leaving her to run Qianli Fist Manor on her own.

想到这里，千里寻十分感伤，不由叹了口气。
Had Qianli Fist Manor even survived the earthquake?

苦荞已将茶叶碾成细小的茶末，用小刷子把茶末刷进茶筛中，听到小姐叹气，开解道：“小姐如若想念老爷太太，我就随小姐回去看看他们，他们看到您指定高兴。”
Grief tightened Qianli Xun's chest, and a sigh slipped out.

千里寻说：“我想不通，女儿不回门，当爹的官职不小，难道就不能向他挑的女婿问问情况？我不相信林觉敢驳了临安知府的面子。”
Having ground the leaves into fine dust and brushed them into the tea sieve, Kuqiao heard the sigh and tried to cheer her up. "If you miss Master and Madam, I'll go back with you to visit them, Miss. They'll be thrilled to see you!"

苦荞边筛茶边说：“老爷和姑爷之间的事，我一个丫鬟不是很了解，但我知道老爷十分看重清誉。”
Qianli Xun wondered aloud, "What I don't get is, if a daughter skips her return visit, her father holds high office—couldn't he check in with the son-in-law he handpicked? Don't tell me Lin Jue has the guts to snub the Prefect of Lin'an."

千里寻心想未谋面的亲爹看重清誉，不知面对和离之事，他能否替女儿出面？
Sifting the powder, Kuqiao answered, "I'm only a maid, so I don't know what goes on between Master and Son-in-law. All I know is Master cares deeply about family honor."

千里寻认为没有必要再向苦荞打听下去，等见到朱淑真的爹娘，亲自了解一番，再做打算。
A father obsessed with reputation—would he step forward to back a mutual divorce?

“苦荞，做完这盏茶，就陪我回朱府。”
No point pumping Kuqiao for more guesses; better to meet Zhu Shuzhen's parents face to face and size them up herself.

“好的，小姐。”
"Kuqiao, finish that bowl of tea, then come with me to Zhu Manor."

苦荞已筛完茶，把筛好的绿色茶末在茶盏中放些，剩下的全部用刷子刷进小瓷坛中放着备用。接着，在茶盏中加入些许热水后，拿起茶筅开始击拂，如此七回后，一盏茶做好，起身端到小姐跟前。
"Yes, Miss."

“小姐，请用。”
Done sifting, Kuqiao spooned green powder into the tea bowl, brushed the rest into the porcelain jar for later, added hot water, and whisked through seven infusions before bringing the finished bowl over.

千里寻接过，慨叹道：“看你做茶的过程，一步一步甚是用心，我都不忍心喝下它。”
"Please drink, Miss."

“小姐只有喝下这盏茶，才不枉费我这番功夫。”
Taking the bowl, Qianli Xun marveled, "Watching you pour so much care into every single step makes me almost hate to drink it."

“没想到你茶做得好，嘴也挺会说，从你这位丫鬟的身上就能看出你以前主子的风格，她一定是位事事都十分考究的人，哪像我大大咧咧……”
"Only if you drink it is my work worthwhile, Miss."

苦荞急了：“小姐说的什么话！我需要强调多少遍您才明白，我的主子只有您一位！”
"Good with tea and smooth with words too. Just looking at you tells me what your old mistress was like—meticulous about every detail, not sloppy like me..."

千里寻没有理会苦荞，她急着回朱府，更确切地说，她想尽快结束这段对她来讲十分没有必要的婚姻，已没心情品茶，一口气喝下，放下茶盏后，站起来：“苦荞，回朱府！”
Kuqiao protested at once, "What talk is that, Miss? How many times do I have to say it—you are my one and only mistress!"

苦荞跟着小姐走出林府，吩咐林府的车夫套好马车，二人先后登上马车坐好。
Ignoring the lecture, eager to reach Zhu Manor and cut loose from a marriage she had zero use for, Qianli Xun downed the tea in one gulp, set the bowl down, and stood up. "Kuqiao, let's go to Zhu Manor!"

这是千里寻第一次乘坐马车，充满新奇，比她第一次乘坐申简辰的劳斯莱斯还要新奇。在她眼中劳斯莱斯只不过是申简辰炫富的筹码，她最讨厌像申简辰那种有几个臭钱就在女孩子面前炫来炫去的男人，显得极其无知。
They walked out of Lin Manor, had the Lin coachman hitch a carriage, and climbed aboard one after the other.

可当马车启动后在颠簸中前进，千里寻才从心底承认还是劳斯莱斯坐着舒服，她努力让自己坐稳。
Her first ride in a horse-drawn carriage thrilled Qianli Xun—even more than her first ride in Shen Jianchen's Rolls-Royce. To her, that Rolls-Royce had just been a prop for showing off wealth, and she despised men who flaunted cash in front of girls like clueless fools.

马车行了一会儿，听到热闹喧嚣之声，掀开布帘，向外瞧去，临安城的街道上人来人往，甚是热闹，街道两旁依也是店铺林立。马车从人群中穿梭而过，能听到各种交谈和讨价还价的声音；还有茶馆酒肆，也是人满为患，时不时从茶馆里传出说书的声音。
Yet the moment the carriage lurched forward over bumpy stone streets, she had to admit the Rolls-Royce beat this for comfort, bracing herself to stay steady.

千里寻放下帘子：“没想到临安的街市如此热闹。”
Soon lively street noise swelled around them. Lifting the curtain to peek out, she saw Lin'an's avenues packed with pedestrians and lined shoulder-to-shoulder with shops. Voices haggled and chatted as the carriage threaded through the crowd; teahouses and taverns overflowed with patrons, snatches of storytelling drifting from inside.

“这不算什么，东京汴梁才热闹。”
Letting the curtain drop, Qianli Xun remarked, "Lin'an's streets are livelier than I expected."

“你去过？”
"This is nothing—Bianliang, the Eastern Capital, is way livelier!"

“没有，听小姐以前说的，东京汴梁有位魏夫人来信，邀请小姐去汴梁以诗会友，小姐还说什么时候带我去汴梁逛逛。”
"You've been there?"

“魏夫人？可叫魏玩？”
"No, you told me before, Miss. A Lady Wei from Bianliang sent a letter inviting you up there to make friends through poetry, and you promised to take me sightseeing in the capital someday."

“我不清楚，小姐从未对我说过魏夫人的名字。”
"Lady Wei? Is her name Wei Wan?"

千里寻心想，应该就是魏玩，后世有资料记载，朱淑真与那魏玩有过来往，而且有诗为证。她此时没有心情搜寻那些诗词，还是想想怎么拿下朱淑真的爹娘。
"No idea—you never told me her given name."

一路思忖着，当周围安静下来时，马车缓缓停下。
Must be Wei Wan, Qianli Xun thought. Later historical records noted Zhu Shuzhen corresponded with Wei Wan, backed up by surviving poems. Right now she had no head for tracing verse—winning over Zhu Shuzhen's parents came first.

苦荞说：“小姐，到了。”
Lost in thought, she felt the clamor fade as the carriage rolled to a stop.

千里寻多少有些紧张：“就这么到了？我还没想清楚见了爹娘说些什么，或者应该有什么样的表情。”
"We're here, Miss," Kuqiao said.

“小姐别紧张，这可是您从小生活的地方。”
Nerves fluttered in Qianli Xun's stomach. "Here already? I haven't even figured out what to say or what face to put on when I meet my parents!"

“有那么明显吗？都被你瞧出来。”
"Don't be nervous, Miss—you grew up here."

苦荞笑着没说话，先下了马车，站在车旁，等待伺候小姐。
"Does it show that much? Even you noticed."

千里寻在车内犹豫了一会儿，还是鼓起勇气走下马车。
Kuqiao smiled without answering, hopped down first, and stood ready to assist her.

苦荞见小姐出来，伸手去扶。
After a brief hesitation inside, Qianli Xun braced her nerve and stepped down, taking Kuqiao's steadying hand.

千里寻抬眼，看到朱红色的大门紧紧关闭，匾额上写着“朱府”二字，门前左右两边的石狮将朱府的大门衬得更加气派。
Looking up, she saw massive vermilion gates shut tight beneath a plaque reading "Zhu Manor," flanked by imposing stone lions.

“苦荞，你去敲门。”
"Kuqiao, go knock."

“小姐，我们不从正门进入。”
"We don't go in through the main gate, Miss."

“不从正门进，难道要走后门？”
"Not the main gate? Then the back gate?"

“是侧门，小姐跟我来。”
"The side gate. Follow me, Miss."

“真是奇怪，不让人走，建得那么气派干嘛？岂不浪费？”
"Weird—why build such a grand gate if nobody walks through it? What a waste!"

“小姐有所不知，正门平常不开，一旦府中来了贵重之人，才会打开正门迎接，小姐是自家人，适合走侧门，就连老爷平常也是走侧门。”
"You wouldn't know, Miss—the main gate stays shut unless honored guests visit. Family members use the side gate. Even Master uses the side gate every day."

千里寻在苦荞的引领下向大门的左侧拐去，走了不多时，果然看到一个与正门比起来逊色许多的侧门，两边修竹成林，风景倒也雅致。
Following Kuqiao around the left wall, Qianli Xun soon came to a far humbler side gate framed by slender bamboo groves, quiet and tasteful.
