## Chapter 151: Chapter 151: Fleeing After a Confession

With Wenyuan calm again and Chen Haoyu dropping the grudge, Shen Jianchen had no further brawl to worry over. His heart stayed fixed on Qianli Xun, yet to avoid raising eyebrows, he ordered Bai Feng to summon the storyteller.

Servants had already set up a small table. Moments later the storyteller entered carrying his trademark purple clay teapot, took a sip, set it on the table, and greeted the room. "Honored guests, first time in my life I've been invited into a grand estate to tell tales—feels a bit strange, so forgive any slips!"

"Quit stalling and start the story—we're all waiting!" Cao Ban called out.

"Patience, young masters, right away!" Clapping his gavel block crisp on the wood, the storyteller launched in: "Last time we left off with heroic Wu Xuanying taking command of the army in his father's place to crush the rebellion! Mounted astride his crimson Red Hare steed and gripping the Xuanyuan Sword, Wu Xuanying led fifty thousand troops south in a thunderous march..."

Having no head for the tale, Shen Jianchen beckoned Xilan over. She stepped up at once and nodded along as Prince Yi murmured instructions in her ear, then hurried back to whisper beside Princess Jiahe: "His Highness needs to step out to the latrine and asks Princess to look after the guests—above all, don't let Young Master Qiao and Young Master Chen clash again."

Qiao Mengrong glanced over and gave her brother a quiet nod. Answering with a reassuring smile, Shen Jianchen rose and stepped out. When Bai Feng moved to follow, Prince Yi stopped him: "Just heading to the latrine, stay put and tend the guests for me." Bai Feng had no choice but to remain behind.

Latrine, right—Shen Jianchen was hunting Qianli Xun! Stepping out the front doors, luck smiled on him: right past the courtyard stood Kuqiao beneath the wooden tree platform, and gazing upward, he spotted Qianli Xun standing atop the platform looking out over the forest.

Spotting Prince Yi, Kuqiao stepped forward and curtsied. "Your Highness."

"Bai Feng is waiting for you in the back courtyard; why are you idling out here?" Shen Jianchen fibbed without flinching—getting Kuqiao out of the way cleared his path up the stairs.

Hearing Bai Feng waited for her, Kuqiao hesitated. "I'm supposed to attend Miss; I can't run off to see Bai Feng."

As if that could stop Shen Jianchen! "Go right ahead. If Youqi Jushi asks later, I'm right here—she won't blame you."

With royal backing, Kuqiao lit up at once. "Thank you, Your Highness!" Off she skipped toward the back courtyard.

Climbing the wooden stairs two steps at a time, Shen Jianchen hurried upward, eager to catch Qianli Xun alone at last—just the two of them high above the woods, facing the very Divine Willow that had carried them across a thousand years.

Up on the platform, Qianli Xun gazed toward the distant willow, turning over her tangled fate in silence: Divine Willow, oh Divine Willow, I swore off romance and just wanted a quiet solo life, yet fate plays a cruel joke tying me to a ridiculous betrothal! Is Chen Haoyu really my ending? No, never! Tell me, Divine Willow—where on earth do I belong?

Too far off to answer, the willow gave no sign—yet right behind her sounded a clear voice: "Qianli Xun!"

Spinning around to find Prince Yi standing right there, Qianli Xun blinked in dazed confusion, a wild thought flashing across her mind: Is HE where I belong? Instantly she shook herself awake. No, impossible! He's the man Mengrong loves—I could never betray Mengrong!

Skipping formal greetings, she scolded him sharp, "I warned Your Highness already not to blurt out that name! Why say it again?"

"Because I love calling you by it. Nobody's around—what harm?"

Warmth and deep devotion shone in Prince Yi's eyes. Qianli Xun cursed under her breath—Trying to seduce me again!—and glanced down over the railing only to find Kuqiao gone. "Since Your Highness came up for the view, I'll head down and leave you the platform." She turned toward the steps.

Shen Jianchen taunted her on purpose: "Has Youqi Jushi fallen for me already, fleeing in panic?"

Halting her steps, she snapped back, "Who's fleeing?! I've been out long enough and ought to get back inside for the dances! Besides, I have zero feelings for Your Highness—quit saying stuff like that before Mengrong hears and gets her heart broken!"

Turning his gaze toward the distant willow, Shen Jianchen murmured, "I love one girl with all my heart and long for her to love me back. See over there? That Divine Willow stands witness to our love."

Remembering how Prince Yi and Mengrong had hung red ribbons beneath that willow together, Qianli Xun frowned—Wait, meant Mengrong? Didn't sound like it! Ready to dress down a playboy prince on Mengrong's behalf, she shot back, "Your Highness's greatest gift is flirting with other women behind Mengrong's back! Which other girl did you sneak under the Divine Willow to tie ribbons with? Poor Mengrong still thinks her brother is loyal to the bone!"

Stepping closer, eyes brimming with earnest feeling, Shen Jianchen said straight, "The girl I love is Youqi Jushi—Qianli Xun! Tell me, do you love me?"

Even having heard his confession before, Qianli Xun froze and stepped back a pace. "I never tied ribbons under the Divine Willow with you..."

Taking a step forward to close the gap, Shen Jianchen spoke with a tone bordering on command: "Stop loving Wenyuan, and do not marry Chen Haoyu! You belong to me alone!"

Her clenched fist stayed pinned at her side. Strange—today the warmth in Prince Yi's eyes felt like winter sunshine melting away her chill, and his bossy decree didn't even grate on her nerves! Wait—why?! Alarm jolted her chest. Thinking of Mengrong, she dared not stay another second; Prince Yi knew how to shake her heart too well, and lingering risked losing her head!

Spotting the stairway opening, she shoved Prince Yi aside and bolted down the steps in full flight.

Before he could utter the crucial words, she was gone! Leaning over the railing watching her hurry down the stairs, Shen Jianchen called aloud: "After the salon—meet me under the Divine Willow! I have something vital to tell you!"

"Don't hold your breath, Your Highness—I'm not going!" she tossed back without breaking stride.

"Skip it, and I tell Mengrong straight to her face that I love you!"

Dead-center hit! Qianli Xun skidded to a halt and glared straight up at his smug grin. "Low blow! Fine, I'll show up! Don't dream of me falling for you—and don't whine if you catch a beating!"

"Just looking for a good sparring partner—sparring with the woman I love couldn't be sweeter!" Since he meant to reveal that Zhao Pu was Shen Jianchen, what better proof than Shen Fist itself?

Hurrying down the remaining stairs and straight back into the courtyard, Qianli Xun refused to let Prince Yi catch up and risk gossip walking in together.

Inside the hall, lunch platters had been cleared for fruit and tea snacks; the dancers were gone, and the storyteller was mid-set spinning Hongxiang and Xuanying's tale—none other than the storyteller from West Third Alley! Scanning for Kuqiao and finding her absent brought huge relief; if Kuqiao walked in here, her secret pen name would blow wide open!

Slipping back to her seat, she met Qiao Mengrong leaning close to whisper, "What kept you out so long?"

"Youlin Retreat's views looked too pretty; wandered a few extra steps."

Smiling without pushing, Qiao Mengrong turned back to the gripping tale. Qianli Xun beckoned to Xiaguo standing behind her, yet Xiaguo stayed glued on Young Master Qiao without noticing until Huayu poked her arm. Jolted awake, Xiaguo leaned down. "Your orders, Miss?"

"Where's Kuqiao?"

"Went out with you and never came back inside."

Qianli Xun nodded; seeing Bai Feng standing right at his post, she assumed Kuqiao had spotted the storyteller and hidden away to keep her cover safe. Settling back, she gave herself over to the story—hearing her own writing come alive before an enthralled audience felt amazing.

Standing behind his table, the storyteller delivered every twist with gripping drama: Returning victorious from battle, Wu Xuanying searched high and low for Hua Hongxiang without a trace. Torn apart—having struggled so hard to be together, could they lose each other now?—Wu Xuanying marched straight to his father the City Lord demanding, "Where did Hongxiang go?!"

Right then Shen Jianchen returned from outside, took his seat, and cast a warm glance across at Qianli Xun, still chuckling inside at her hasty flight off the platform.

The storyteller pressed on: "The City Lord said, 'Forget Hongxiang, my son.' 'Never!' Wu Xuanying dropped to his knees. 'Tell me where you hid her, Father!' The City Lord answered, 'Why would I hide her? She has vanished from this world and will never return.' 'Vanished?' Crushed beyond bearing, Wu Xuanying broke into piercing sobs and fainted cold onto the floor!" Clack! Down came the gavel block. "To learn what follows next, stay tuned for our next session!"

Having written that chapter during a dark mood, Qianli Xun had laid the tragedy on thick, leaving nearly the whole room sighing in sympathy—especially Qiao Wenxian, who felt every word cut to the bone, Shuzhen lost to him just as Hongxiang had vanished. Having walked in late and still glowing from his moment on the platform, Prince Yi felt none of the gloom, while Chen Haoyu scoffed at melodramatic romance—what real man faints crying over a girl?!

As the storyteller bowed to leave, Dongfang Chenze halted him: "Wait a moment, sir! What happens next?"

"My lips are sealed, young master! Want the next chapter, come to Qiaotou Wa tomorrow!" Bowing to all, the storyteller withdrew.

Qiao Wenxian's eyes drifted back to Shuzhen—seated mere paces away, yet speaking one word to her felt impossible—then shifted onto Chen Haoyu's cocky slouch. How could a dandy like that deserve Shuzhen?! Ache crushing his chest, he murmured to Nan Shuang: "Fetch me a pot of wine." Nan Shuang obeyed at once.

Miao Zhiyu sighed in admiration. "Young Master Qianli Xun writes with such piercing feeling—tomorrow I'll bring my close friends to hear the next part!"

"Funny thing," Cao Ban mused aloud. "Always get the hunch Qianli Xun is a young lady, even though street rumors insist he's a young gentleman. What do you all think?"

At that remark, Shen Jianchen shot a sly sidelong glance at Qianli Xun, hiding a grin.

"Same hunch," An Mingxuan agreed. "Word is Young Master Qianli Xun was spotted once in the market dressed as a man, yet graceful and pretty as a girl."

Chen Haoyu snorted in scorn. "Bet Qianli Xun's just some soft sissy—how else write a spineless tale like that? Who on earth faints crying like Wu Xuanying?! Qiao Wenxian, you've cried before—ever pass out?"

Being called a sissy didn't faze Qianli Xun one bit—she WAS a woman disguised as a guy! Yet Qiao Wenxian's blood boiled—on what merit did Chen Haoyu get to marry Shuzhen?! "Quit gloating after stealing a prize, Chen Haoyu! Whether I cried or fainted is none of your business!"

Chen Haoyu smirked back: "Hear that? Even Qiao Wenxian doesn't pass out crying—proves Qianli Xun is a sissy!"

Worried another clash would embarrass Shuzhen, Qiao Mengrong cut in smooth: "Sounds more like Qianli Xun is a breathtaking beauty—step out in public and ladies would toss fruit at his carriage like Pan An, so he stays out of sight!"

Reading Mengrong's cue, Shen Jianchen backed her right up: "Well said, Mengrong! We've feasted, watched dances, and heard a tale—now for poetry! Youqi Jushi promised to compose a fresh lyric on the spot for us to appreciate. Set out brush and paper for Youqi Jushi!"

Maids hurried in laying out the Four Treasures of the Study. Having already chosen her piece while strolling outside, Qianli Xun lifted the brush at once and penned:

"Clear winter sky without snow—Heaven's heart withholds it, not that Creation lacks craft. It will not let jade blossoms flutter down to earth, holding them back inside the Palace of Vast Cold. Just as clouds gather and sleet starts to form, the red sun climbs three poles high. Six-petaled flakes cut ready—where on earth will they be scattered?

Pity the winter plum upon the ridge, blooming alone without a mate, grieving deep before this view. Waiting for the hand that stirs the golden cauldron to scatter down the jade salt. Gullies all smoothed level, heaven and earth painted fair, crowned by the icy moon's pure light. Feasting guests in Liang Garden fear not the lamps going out in the bright night." ("Niannujiao: Clear Winter Sky, No Snow") [TN: Translator's rendering]

While Qianli Xun wrote with full focus, Kuqiao slipped back indoors—having gone to the rear courtyard only to find no trace of Bai Feng and realize Prince Yi had tricked her! Why trick her away unless he wanted Miss alone? Wait—did Prince Yi have feelings for Miss?! Panic chilled her veins. Too late to return to the platform, she had headed to the rear door of the hall, caught the storyteller's voice, peeked around the screen to spot the man from West Third Alley, and stayed hidden out back until he left lest she expose Miss as Qianli Xun!
