## Chapter 191: The Princess Risks Her Life to Stop a Bride-Snatching

Returning late the night before, Bai Feng had missed reporting the piglet's death. At dawn he stepped outside his prince's door to call, "Your Highness, awake?"

Before he finished, the door swung open—His Highness already dressed, dark rings shadowing sleepless eyes.

"Your Highness, the piglet refused to climb and dropped dead from exhaustion."

A flicker of bleakness crossed Shen Jianchen's face. Was he truly barred from ever being with Qianli Xun? *Screw fate!* Drawing his Military Commissioner's command token, he slapped it into Bai Feng's palm. "Take my token to the Military Training Office, muster one thousand troops, and meet me at the city gate."

Bai Feng's eyes lit up. "Are we riding to war, Your Highness?!"

"No," Shen Jianchen said flatly. "I am snatching a bride."

The grin died off Bai Feng's face—today was Youqi Jushi's wedding to Young Master Chen! Dropping straight to both knees, he pleaded, "Think thrice, Your Highness!"

"You are my guard—obey orders, do not lecture me! Move!"

Forced to rise, Bai Feng clutched the token and dashed out of the main courtyard—paused three strides out in fierce conflict, then sprinted straight to Princess Jiahe's courtyard and pounded the door. "Xilan! Xilan! Open up! Quick!"

Yanking the door wide, Xilan snapped, "What are you yelling for at crack of dawn?!"

"Where is Her Highness?! I need to see her!" Brushing past Xilan into the yard, Bai Feng spotted Qiao Mengrong stepping to her doorway with swollen red eyes.

"What do you want?"

Skipping ceremony, Bai Feng blurted out, "His Highness has lost his mind! He sent me to muster a thousand soldiers to raid the wedding and snatch the bride! As his guard I have to obey—only Your Highness can stop him!" Spinning around, he raced off to fulfill his orders.

The shock nearly knocked Qiao Mengrong off her feet. Brother really had lost his mind—mobilizing Lin'an troops for Zhu Shuzhen?! Envy and jealousy erupted into scorching hatred. Catching her breath, she hissed, "Xilan, bring me a dagger."

Terrified, Xilan gasped, "Do not do anything rash, Your Highness!"

"Fetch it—NOW!"

Dashing inside, Xilan brought out a sheathed dagger with both hands. Drawing the blade just enough to flash bright steel in her eyes, Qiao Mengrong slid it back into its sheath and tucked it up her sleeve. "Come with me to find Brother."

Arriving at Prince Yi's courtyard clutching the hidden hilt—ready to put her own life on the blade if he refused to yield—she waited as Xilan called twice into the empty room, checked inside, and hurried back out. "Your Highness, His Highness is gone!"

Heart racing, Mengrong hurried straight to the front gate, bumping right into Butler Li.

"Heading to the Zhu-Chen wedding, Your Highness?" Butler Li bowed cheerfully, clueless to the storm. "Why did you not ride with His Highness? He just left—oddly enough on horseback instead of carriage."

Keeping her tone rock steady, Mengrong replied, "I told Brother to go ahead. Please tell the driver to bring my carriage—I am heading to the feast too."

"Already hitched and waiting outside!"

Thanking him, Mengrong boarded and had Xilan order the driver straight onto the bridal procession's main route—wherever Brother meant to strike, it had to be on that road.

Over at Zhu Manor, Kuqiao woke Qianli Xun early for a quick breakfast and bridal dressing. Sleepless half the night, Qianli Xun sat fighting off yawns as Kuqiao chattered nonstop to keep gloom at bay.

"At least the Chens have sense, Miss—sending crimson bridal robes!"

"Aren't wedding robes always red?" Qianli Xun murmured sleepily. "What else would they be?"

Tracing her brows gently with indigo eyebrow pigment, Kuqiao explained, "Never heard 'red for the groom, green for the bride'? Brides can wear green robes too!"

"Green wedding robes? Neat. Then why is mine red?"

"Miss is the Prefect's daughter, while the Chens—rich as they are—hold zero official rank! You are marrying down, so red honors you bringing luster to their house! Remember when Young Madam married into Zhu Manor, she wore green because she was marrying up." Stepping back, Kuqiao beamed. "My brow-painting keeps getting better—Miss looks lovelier than ever today!"

"This wedding was not my idea—just slap something basic on and skip the fuss."

Dusting powder across her cheeks, Kuqiao insisted, "Wanted or not, a wedding is a wedding! Starting today I live at Chen Manor with Miss—start day one cheerful so every day after stays bright!"

"Didn't know you were this superstitious."

"Not superstition—good wishes!"

Kuqiao's bright spirit nudged Qianli Xun loose from her funk. Since she had agreed to walk through Chen Manor's door, why drag a sour face along? Set last night aside and step in light.

Dressed in crimson robes and crowned with a heavy phoenix coronet, she walked to her parents' hall where her brother, sister-in-law, and Matron Wei waited. Hardly had she knelt to bow to her parents when festive pipes and drums struck up outside.

"Rise," Zhu Xiyan said. "The groom is at the gate. Walk out with your parents."

As Qianli Xun stood, Yinxing approached with a lacquer tray holding a square red veil, which Kuqiao draped over her head. Guided through a red haze by Kuqiao behind her parents, with her brother, sister-in-law, and Matron Wei following, she passed through Zhu Manor's wide-open main gate for the very first time.

Peeking up under the red hem, she spotted Chen Haoyu astride a tall steed in crimson groom's robes, grinning brightly as though yesterday's panic had never happened. Dismounting to bow to his in-laws, he reached out a hand to lead his bride—only for Qianli Xun, having already sighted the bridal sedan, to stride straight past him with Kuqiao and hop right inside!

The second the curtain dropped, she yanked the veil off and tossed it aside. "Suffocating thing!" Next came the phoenix coronet. "Weighs a ton! Why on earth do brides wear torture gear on their heads? Ancients love making themselves miserable!"

Unbothered by the snub, Chen Haoyu chuckled wryly and swung back onto his horse. When the sedan bearers lingered without lifting, Zhu Xiyan nodded to Zhu Jingyuan, who grabbed a tray of red gift packets from Lutao and stuffed them into the bearers' hands. Pipes blared back up, the sedan rose, and the procession marched off down Lin'an's widest avenues—picked by a geomancer for maximum fanfare.

Riding a palanquin for the first time made Qianli Xun's stomach pitch until her breakfast threatened to come back up—then without warning, the sedan lurched to a dead stop, the music snapped off mid-note, and hush fell over the street.

Cracking the curtain, Qianli Xun blinked at a wall of armed soldiers ringing the procession tight. "What is going on, Kuqiao?"

Leaning close, Kuqiao whispered in panic, "Miss, Prince Yi really lost his marbles—he brought an army to block the wedding!"

Dropping the curtain, Qianli Xun held her breath and listened inward: bolt with Shen Jianchen right now, or stay seated and ride on?

Outside, astride a tall warhorse barring the street, Shen Jianchen stared down. Dismounting to bow in confusion, Chen Haoyu called, "Your Highness Prince Yi—today is my wedding! What is the meaning of this?"

Shen Jianchen's voice rang calm and iron-hard: "Hold your wedding if you like—leave the woman inside that sedan to me."

Dim as he could be, the truth finally clicked for Chen Haoyu—Prince Yi wanted Zhu Shuzhen! No wonder His Highness had beaten him half to death over those rumors! Wait—marrying Zhu Shuzhen brought trouble THIS big?! Edging beside Prince Yi's stirrup, he whispered fast, "Your Highness does not know—I never even wanted to marry Zhu Shuzhen! Master Jiang forced me into it, holding a contract for the whole Chen fortune! If Your Highness gets that paper back from Master Jiang for me, I cancel this wedding on the spot and you can carry Zhu Shuzhen wherever you please—not a peep out of me!"

*Pure genius!* Chen Haoyu congratulated himself—get Prince Yi to crush Master Jiang AND offload Zhu Shuzhen in one shot!

"Your business with Master Jiang means nothing to me," Shen Jianchen answered coldly. "I want only the woman in that sedan."

Swinging off his horse without sparing Chen Haoyu a glance, he strode straight toward the bridal chair—only for Matron Wei to step into his path. "Does Your Highness mean to shred my mistress's reputation to bits all over again?!"

"Stand aside, Matron. I am listening to no one today."

Reaching past her, his fingers grazed the crimson curtain—when Qiao Mengrong's sharp cry pierced the street: "Pull back that curtain, Brother, and I die right before your eyes!"

Hand freezing midair, Shen Jianchen whipped around. There stood Mengrong, bared dagger pressed straight against her chest!

With a jolt, he realized Mengrong would never yield. Heart torn between the sedan behind him and the blade at her breast, he flicked a sharp look at Bai Feng—*Wait for an opening and disarm her!*—and tried distracting her with words. "Why push me like this, Mengrong?"

"YOU pushed me, Brother!"

"Be good—put the dagger down and let us talk."

Having heard every word inside the chair, Qianli Xun felt her heart wrench at Mengrong putting her life on the line. Unable to hide behind red silk another second, she kicked off with light-foot skill and burst straight out of the sedan!

Spectators gasped as a streak of scarlet flew through the air—*clang!*—and Qiao Mengrong's dagger spun clattering across the cobblestones! Floating down like a celestial maiden in crimson bridal silk, Youqi Jushi stood dazzling before the crowd.

Blind to everything save rage, Qiao Mengrong stepped straight up and cracked a ferocious slap across Shuzhen's cheek! "I hate you, Zhu Shuzhen!"

Before anyone moved, Mengrong's eyes locked onto a soldier's drawn sword beside her—and she threw herself straight toward the blade!

Stunned by the stinging slap, Qianli Xun hesitated half a heartbeat; Bai Feng stood frozen between awe at her flying leap and shock at the slap; Shen Jianchen flinched in worry over Qianli Xun—then snapped back with a roar: "Mengrong!"

Launching forward with light-foot speed, he arrived half a breath too late. Qiao Mengrong slammed into the blade's point; scarlet bloomed across her robe as she crumpled to the ground!

Dropping his sword in sheer terror, the soldier crashed to his knees. "Not my fault! Not my fault!"

Scooping Mengrong into his arms, Shen Jianchen sprinted down the street toward a clinic, Xilan racing weeping at his heels.

Left cleaning up the chaos, Bai Feng barked at the unit captain, "March the men back to barracks!" Once the troops pulled out, he cupped his hands to Chen Haoyu. "Apologies for the disturbance, Young Master Chen." Shooting one quick look at Kuqiao, he leaped onto a horse and galloped after his prince.

Still gaping at the whirlwind, Chen Haoyu walked over to where Zhu Shuzhen stood motionless. "Hey—that circus was all your mess, nothing to do with me. Still marrying or what?"

Her cheek still burned from Mengrong's slap—for someone so fragile, the blow packed pure venom, proving hatred had replaced every drop of friendship. Looking at what had just unfolded, how could she NOT marry?

"If you have the guts to marry me, I have the guts to marry you!"

Thinking of Master Jiang's contract, Chen Haoyu squared his shoulders. "If you have the guts to marry, I have the guts to take you!"

"Then what are we standing around for?!"

Turning sharply, Qianli Xun climbed straight back into the bridal sedan. Festive drums and pipes picked back up, yet not a shred of cheer lived in her chest—only heavy dread over Mengrong's wound. Even knowing she had seen the point miss vital organs, worry refused to let her go.
