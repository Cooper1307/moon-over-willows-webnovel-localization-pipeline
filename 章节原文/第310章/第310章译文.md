## Chapter 310: One Storm Barely Passes Before Another Strikes

Quietly weighing Anya's accusations against Haoyu's courtroom ordeal, Chen Jianglin kept silent while Old Madam Xia asked Anya what she truly wanted.

"Two things, Grandmother: freedom from Chen Haodong, and stripping him of the inheritance!"

Scolding Haodong for beating his wife until she snapped, Old Madam Xia didn't stop him when he called for brush and paper, scrawled a divorce letter (*xiushu*) on the spot, and flung it onto the floor—"Want it? Pick it up!"

Without a flinch, Anya calmly picked up her freedom, checked every character, tucked it safely into her waist pouch—and before leaving, fired one parting broadside: *"Chen Haodong is a hypocrite sleeping with brothel women—and I'll bet he was behind that courtesan raiding Chen Manor too!"* Eating one final vicious slap from Haodong, she spat back that her pregnancy proved *she* wasn't the barren one, spat at his boots, and strode out free beside Xiaocui.

An uncomfortable hush fell. Coughing softly, Granduncle Lu announced the elders felt fertility mattered to clan continuity—forcing cornered Haodong to confess his doctor had promised a cure within a year, while insisting fertility wasn't in the written rules!

Ruling they'd add fertility for future elections yet honor today's ballot since Anya was already gone, Chen Jianglin drafted the official appointment scroll, pressed his red thumbprint first, and invited the elders forward one by one—while Qianli Xun silently marveled at Anya's fierce gamble and laughed softly as Haoyu teased her about green hats.

Seventeen thumbprints down, only *three* elders left—when Han Gu's voice echoed across the hall:

*"The Judicial Investigator arrives!"*

Leaving Chen Manor earlier, Qiao Mengrong had dropped by the Judicial Office and gleefully dished the whole Chen scandal to Brother—prompting Shen Jianchen to realize there was zero Chen face left to spare, and march straight over before the clan crowned a rapist! (Riding home afterward, Mengrong sighed over losing another pawn, eyed Imperial Attendant Liang as a dream trump card—and suddenly wondered why Bai Feng had vanished for weeks, sparking fresh paranoia about Shuzhen!)

Striding into Xinglong Pavilion with Han Gu, Wen Xin, and armed constables, Shen Jianchen smiled coolly at stunned Chen Jianglin: "Looks like I arrived just in time."

Stopping straight before sweating, white-faced Chen Haodong, Shen Jianchen proclaimed loud enough for every elder in the room to hear:

"Remember Chun Jinlan? On the third night of the third month last year, maid Aye saw a man shrouded head-to-toe in a black cloak walk out of that room. That man was *you*—Chen Haodong, you are the rapist who violated Chun Jinlan!"
