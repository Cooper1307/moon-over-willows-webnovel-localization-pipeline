## Chapter 310: One Wave Down, Another Rises

Ye Anya’s bombshell—most of all claiming Haodong plotted against Haoyu—made Chen Jianglin remember Haoyu’s jail stay and wonder if moving the selection up had been a blunder. Yao Qingyan stayed silent beside him, shocked that gentle Anya had been beaten past her breaking point.

Old Madam Xia spoke with stern pity. “Speak straight, Anya—what are you after today?”

“Two things, Grandmother: first, the clan’s blessing to leave Chen Haodong; second, keeping him out of the inheritance.”

Old Madam Xia rounded on her eldest grandson. “How many times did I warn you to treat your wife right?! Swinging fists day in and day out—see what it bought you?!”

Unrepentant, Chen Haodong snapped, “She cheated on me and treated me like ice—beating her was mercy! Now she wrecks our clan ceremony! Wants out? Fine—I’ll write a divorce letter right here so she’s no longer my wife and can’t touch today’s vote!”

Seeing him dump his wife to protect his crown without an ounce of remorse, Old Madam Xia sighed heavy. “Publicly admitting infidelity—she’s forcing your hand. Go ahead, let her go; staying here only makes her miserable, hard as life is for a divorced woman. I’m washing my hands of you two.”

Chen Jiangshu hurried brush and paper to the center table. Striding over, Chen Haodong dashed off a divorce letter, scanned it, and flung it onto the floor at her feet. “Your divorce letter—want it, pick it up!”

Heart soaring at freedom despite his sneer, Ye Anya walked calmly up, scooped it off the tiles, checked every line, folded it neat, and tucked it into her waist pouch.

“No longer a Chen—get out,” Chen Haodong snapped.

Ignoring him, she faced Old Madam Xia. “Goal two isn’t done yet—”

“She’s divorced—no right to speak! Throw this shrew out!” Madam Bao and Chen Jiangshu shouted together until Old Madam Xia roared them silent.

“Go, Anya,” Old Madam Xia said firm. “Chen business was never your place, even less now.”

Unwilling to leave quiet, Ye Anya cried out one parting blast: “Fine, I’ll go—yet hear my warning! Chen Haodong is a hypocrite unfit to lead! Not only does he sleep with Chunxiang Brothel courtesans, I bet he was behind that courtesan storming Chen Manor too!”

Crack! Chen Haodong lashed across her face. “Whore carrying another man’s bastard—dare smear me?! Move before I kill you!”

Clutching her cheek, Ye Anya laughed bitter. “See that, everyone?! Dressed like a gentleman, pure trash inside—only good for beating his wife! You couldn’t get me pregnant while Mother-in-law blamed me—now my belly proves I’m fine!”

Stomping frantic, Chen Jiangshu shouted her out; spitting straight at Chen Haodong, Ye Anya walked free with Xiaocui.

A chill ran down Old Madam Xia at her parting words—putting together the courtesan trial and Eldest Son suddenly rushing the heir vote the very day Haoyu got freed! Yet if not Haodong, who else? Neither Haoyu nor Haofei fit—better wrap this up fast before more chaos hit.

Just as she opened her mouth, Chen Lu coughed sharp. “The clan elders just conferred—whether an heir can continue the bloodline affects the whole Chen clan’s future! We must pause and verify.”

“Don’t swallow that woman’s lies!” Chen Haodong protested.

“I know medicine,” another elder volunteered. “Hand over the prescription and let me take Haodong’s pulse right here.”

Cornered, Chen Haodong chose partial confession over humiliation. “Fine—my health has a minor issue, and a physician did write that prescription, only he promised one more year of medicine cures it completely! Besides, fertility isn’t even one of the six written rules!”

Airtight—stumping the elder. “True,” Chen Lu conceded, “yet rules evolve—let’s add continuing the bloodline starting this year!”

Breaking silence at last, Chen Jianglin stepped in. “I’ll add that clause afterward; today’s vote stands under existing rules. Anya is divorced and out of the picture, and I’ll arrange a proper new bride for Haodong myself. With the result clear, I’ll draft the appointment scroll right now for all twenty voting elders to stamp their thumbprints.”

Relief flooded Chen Haodong and his parents as Chen Jianglin spread a special scroll and began writing.

Watching quietly, Qianli Xun admired Ye Anya’s fierce nerve—even if she couldn’t topple the vote, walking away free regardless of scandal took guts she herself lacked. *Which way is right—compromising like me, or burning every bridge like her?*

Chen Haoyu leaned in. “You’re the best, Wife.”

“Where’d that come from?”

“For not putting a green hat on my head.”

She burst out laughing. “Funny—feels like I’m the one wearing a green hat!”

Pantomiming lifting a hat off her head, he grinned. “Took it off for good—never letting you wear one again.”

Smiling silent, she realized neither path was right or wrong—so long as you found peace. She felt happy right now, and Ye Anya would be too.

Finishing the scroll, Chen Jianglin pressed the first red thumbprint, followed eagerly by Chen Jiangshu, Chen Jiangmu, Old Madam Xia, and a line of elders. Seventeen prints stamped—only three elders left! Chen Haodong’s heart nearly burst with triumph—

When Han Gu’s voice boomed from the doorway: “The Judicial Investigator arrives!”

The final three elders froze mid-reach. *What’s Prince Yi doing here?!*

After leaving Chen Manor, Qiao Mengrong had stopped by the Judicial Office to chat with her brother and gleefully dished the whole explosive Chen scandal—prompting Shen Jianchen to realize zero Chen face remained to protect and march straight over before they handed their clan to a rapist! Relieved his arrest only touched Chun Jinlan and not Black-Fiend Tiger, Qiao Mengrong had ridden back to Youlin Retreat sighing over losing her errand boy, eyeing high-powered Imperial Attendant Liang Shicheng in Bianliang as a dream weapon—and suddenly realizing Bai Feng had vanished for weeks, sparking fresh dark paranoia about Zhu Shuzhen.

Now Shen Jianchen strode into Xinglong Pavilion flanked by Han Gu, Wen Xin, and armed constables.

Scrambling up to greet him, Chen Jianglin bowed. “Welcome, Your Highness!”

“Heir chosen yet?” Shen Jianchen asked coolly.

“Just three thumbprints away from finalized.”

“Looks like I made it just in time.” Walking straight up to Chen Haodong, Shen Jianchen said clear, “Eldest Young Master Chen, please accompany me to the Judicial Office.”

Stunned murmurs swept the hall as cold sweat drenched Chen Haodong—*Scarface ratted me out!* “Why summon me, Your Highness?”

“Since you ask, I’ll announce it before everyone.” Sweeping the room—catching Qianli Xun’s puzzled look and shifting his eyes right away—Shen Jianchen proclaimed loud and clear: “Remember Chun Jinlan? On the night of the third day of the third month last year, maid Aye saw a man shrouded head to face in a black cloak walk out of that room. That man was *you*—Chen Haodong, you are the rapist who violated Chun Jinlan!”
