# 第77章 送还红绸赞自称

---

乔守仁听完，品了一会儿，皱起眉头："神人真会故弄玄虚，到底说的是什么，为父一时品不出。"

After listening, Qiao Shouren pondered for a while, then frowned: "The divine being truly loves being cryptic. What exactly is being said? Your father can't figure it out at the moment."

---

乔文仙心想，也难怪，德甫先生的父亲是宰相，满腹才学，自然很快品出。自己的父亲虽也有学问，可才学疏浅，又如何能品出其中意味？不如给他提醒提醒："父亲，我觉得这三句话里包含着字谜。"

Qiao Wenxian thought to himself: No wonder. Master Defu's father was a prime minister, full of learning—naturally he could figure it out quickly. His own father, though educated, had shallower literary skills. How could he decipher the meaning? Better give him a hint: "Father, I think these three sentences contain word riddles."

---

"字谜？让我再品品。"乔守仁继续在心里念着三句话，还是毫无头绪，"是字谜吗？"

"Word riddles? Let me think again." Qiao Shouren continued reciting the three sentences in his heart, still with no clue. "Are they word riddles?"

---

乔文仙很无语，为何这么有趣的事放在自己身上就是不灵，干脆直接说："据儿子猜'言与司合'是个'词'字，'安上已脱'是个'女'字，'芝芙草拔'是'之'和'夫'字，连起来就是'词女之夫'，儿子觉得神人是说我未来要娶之人是位女词人。"

Qiao Wenxian was speechless. Why was something so interesting so hard for him to figure out? He might as well just say it directly: "According to my guess, 'speech combined with office' forms the character 'ci' (word/poetry); 'peace with its roof removed' forms the character 'nv' (woman); 'herbs stripped of their green heads' gives 'zhi' and 'fu.' Put together, it reads 'Husband of the Poetess.' I believe the divine being is saying that the person I'm destined to marry is a female poet."

---

"女词人。"乔守仁口中默默念着。

"A female poet." Qiao Shouren murmured silently.

---

钱氏已把选来的闺秀一一给乔守仁详细说过，其中没有女词人。在临安最有名的女词人除了朱家姑娘，还能有谁？

Lady Qian had already described in detail all the young ladies selected for marriage. Not one of them was a female poet. In Lin'an, who was the most famous female poet besides the Zhu family's daughter?

---

乔守仁早听到有关儿子与朱家姑娘的风言风语，本也想去朱家提亲，可后来朱家把女儿匆匆嫁给林家，乔守仁也就断了念想。

Qiao Shouren had long heard rumors about his son and the Zhu family's daughter. He had originally considered proposing marriage to the Zhu family, but when they hastily married their daughter to the Lin family, Qiao Shouren gave up the idea.

---

近几日朱家姑娘和离一事在临安富贵人家传开，乔守仁又怎能不知？儿子以梦为由，暗示想娶朱家姑娘，看来二人确实有情。可乔家就这么一个儿子，娶谁不好，偏偏要娶一个离过婚的女子，乔守仁是万万不能同意。

In recent days, the news of the Zhu family's daughter's divorce had spread among Lin'an's wealthy families. How could Qiao Shouren not know? His son used a dream as an excuse to hint at wanting to marry the Zhu family's daughter. It seemed the two truly had feelings for each other. But the Qiao family had only this one son. Why did he have to marry a divorced woman of all people? Qiao Shouren absolutely could not agree.

---

"哦，女词人，你的婚姻大事要父母说了算，只是一个梦，别太当回事。"

"Oh, a female poet. Your marriage is a matter for your parents to decide. It's just a dream—don't take it too seriously."

---

父亲显然是拒绝，既然话已说开，他心中也应知道自己想娶谁，不如更加直白地表明态度："请父亲到朱家为儿子提亲，儿子这辈子非淑真不娶。"

His father was clearly refusing. Since the topic was already out in the open, and his father should know whom he wanted to marry, he might as well be more straightforward: "Please go to the Zhu family to propose marriage for me, Father. I will marry no one but Shuzhen in this lifetime."

---

乔守仁觉得儿子还是太年轻，不懂什么是婚姻："千万不要说非谁不娶之类的话，小心到时食言。"

Qiao Shouren felt his son was still too young to understand what marriage was: "Never say things like 'I'll marry no one but her.' Be careful not to break your word later."

---

"父亲难道不同意去朱家提亲？"

"Does Father disagree with going to the Zhu family to propose marriage?"

---

乔守仁叹口气："唉，要是朱家姑娘没婚姻那回事，我当然会去，他们家姑娘也肯定是乔家的第一首选。可惜啊，嫁人不淑，还闹到和离，好好的姑娘身上有了污点，你是乔家的独苗，娶妻更应慎重，娶一个别人家不要的姑娘回来，岂不是给乔家门楣上抹黑？"

Qiao Shouren sighed: "Sigh, if the Zhu family's daughter hadn't been married before, of course I would go. Their daughter would definitely be the Qiao family's first choice. But alas, she married the wrong man and ended in divorce. A good girl now has a stain on her. You're the only son of the Qiao family. Choosing a wife requires even more caution. Marrying a girl that another family has cast aside—wouldn't that tarnish the Qiao family's honor?"

---

乔文仙急道："淑真虽嫁过人，可她一身清白，若真没了清白，我也不嫌弃，还请父亲同意！"

Qiao Wenxian said anxiously: "Though Shuzhen has been married, she is completely pure. Even if she weren't, I still wouldn't mind. Please consent, Father!"

---

"胡闹！"乔守仁批评完，又和颜悦色，"文渊啊，你看，朱家姑娘刚刚和离，就去提亲，也不合适，此事放放再说。"

"Nonsense!" After scolding, Qiao Shouren softened his tone: "Wenyuan, you see, the Zhu family's daughter just recently divorced. It's not appropriate to propose marriage right away. Let's put this matter aside for now."

---

"父亲可是愿意去朱家提亲？"

"Is Father willing to go to the Zhu family to propose marriage?"

---

儿子再次逼问，乔守仁不想与儿子闹得太僵："要不要去朱家提亲，我得和你母亲，还有你娘好好合计合计再说，毕竟是乔家的大事，总不能仓促决定。你跟我说了，就不用再亲自找她们，我跟她们说就行，你只管等消息。好好在别居陪仪王，不要到处乱跑，有事可以让南双回来传话。"

His son pressed again. Qiao Shouren didn't want to make things too tense: "Whether to go to the Zhu family to propose marriage—I need to discuss it carefully with your mother and your concubine. After all, it's a major matter for the Qiao family. We can't decide in haste. You've told me, so there's no need to seek them out yourself. I'll tell them. Just wait for news. Stay at the villa and accompany Prince Yi. Don't run around everywhere. If there's something, you can have Nanshuang come back to deliver a message."

---

父亲的态度模棱两可，乔文仙充满担忧，他又不能逼迫父亲立即答应，不如让家人好好商量，过上几日再回来催问。便起身拜别父亲，继续回幽林别居。

His father's attitude was ambiguous, leaving Qiao Wenxian full of worry. He couldn't force his father to agree immediately. Better let the family discuss it properly and come back in a few days to press the matter. He rose, bid farewell to his father, and continued back to Youlin Bieju.

---

话说申简辰出了幽林别居，进了城，吩咐白风去朱府。

Meanwhile, after leaving Youlin Bieju, Shen Jianchen entered the city and ordered Baifeng to go to the Zhu residence.

---

白风不解，明明出门时说要去府衙，为何又要去朱府？王爷看上去不高兴，他不敢多问，乖乖扭转马头。

Baifeng was puzzled. When they left, the prince clearly said they were going to the yamen. Why were they going to the Zhu residence now? The prince looked unhappy, so he didn't dare ask more and obediently turned the horse around.

---

白风在临安城的几日，没事到处逛，已对路况了然于胸。尤其是大户人家住在哪里，他已基本摸清。主要是因为以前太穷，老寻摸着去大户人家当个下人，养成关注大户人家的习惯。成了王爷的贴身侍卫，还是改不了养成的臭毛病。

During his days in Lin'an, Baifeng had wandered around in his spare time and become thoroughly familiar with the roads. He especially knew where the wealthy families lived. Mainly because he used to be so poor, always looking for opportunities to become a servant in a rich household, he'd developed the habit of paying attention to where the big families lived. Even after becoming the prince's personal guard, he couldn't break this old habit.

---

马车很快来到朱府的侧门外，申简辰走下马车，对守在门口的下人说："请告诉幽栖居士，有位申公子来访。"

The carriage soon arrived at the side gate of the Zhu residence. Shen Jianchen stepped down and told the servant guarding the gate: "Please inform Youqi Jushi that a Young Master Shen has come to visit."

---

"请公子随我进去等。"

"Please come in and wait, Young Master."

---

"不必，我就在府外，与你家姑娘说两句话就走。"

"No need. I'll just wait outside the residence. I only need to speak a few words with your young lady and then leave."

---

下人进入府内，寻找小姐的身影。

The servant entered the residence to look for the young lady.

---

千里寻与向夫人和韦嬷嬷刚刚逛完园子，正往回走。

Qianli Xun had just finished strolling through the garden with Lady Xiang and Nanny Wei and was heading back.

---

下人迎面走来，拜道："小姐，门外有位申公子求见。"

The servant approached and bowed: "Young lady, a Young Master Shen is requesting an audience at the gate."

---

"申公子？"千里寻想起宴会那日假借申公子之名的仪王，"娘，估计是仪王，我出去会会他。"

"Young Master Shen?" Qianli Xun remembered Prince Yi using that name at the banquet. "Mother, it's probably Prince Yi. I'll go out and meet him."

---

"既然是仪王，不如把他请进家里来。"

"Since it's Prince Yi, why not invite him in?"

---

"他既不愿借着仪王的名头来朱府，应该是不想进来，您与嬷嬷先回，我去去就来。"

"Since he doesn't want to come to the Zhu residence under Prince Yi's name, he probably doesn't want to come in. You and Nanny Wei go back first. I'll be right back."

---

申简辰一直盯着朱府的侧门，这是他知道朱淑真就是千里寻后，第一次正式见她，难免有些紧张。

Shen Jianchen had been staring at the side gate of the Zhu residence. This was the first time he would formally see her after learning that Zhu Shuzhen was Qianli Xun. He couldn't help but feel somewhat nervous.

---

白风不大明白，王爷为何不见朱晞颜，而是要见他的女儿？

Baifeng didn't quite understand why the prince wanted to see Zhu Xiyan's daughter instead of Zhu Xiyan himself.

---

当千里寻从朱府走出时，申简辰迎上去："幽……幽栖居士。"

When Qianli Xun walked out of the Zhu residence, Shen Jianchen stepped forward: "You...Youqi Jushi."

---

千里寻赶紧行礼："仪王殿下，不知找我何事？"

Qianli Xun quickly bowed: "Your Highness Prince Yi, may I ask what brings you here?"

---

申简辰想要把他是申简辰之事说明，可想到她与文渊情意浓浓，还有她曾拒绝他的话，又变得犹豫不决。

Shen Jianchen wanted to reveal that he was truly Shen Jianchen, but thinking of her deep affection for Wenyuan and her previous rejection of him, he became hesitant again.

---

他从怀里掏出红绸："昨晚，我去神柳下看风景，一条红绸飘落下来，我见上面写着幽栖居士的名字，还有'千里寻'三个字，今日过来，一是送红绸，二是想当面问问，幽栖居士可是写话本的千里寻？"

He took out the red silk from his robe: "Last night, I went to admire the scenery under the Sacred Willow. A red silk floated down. I saw Youqi Jushi's name written on it, along with the three characters 'Qianli Xun.' I came today for two reasons: first, to return the red silk; second, to ask in person—is Youqi Jushi the Qianli Xun who writes storytelling scripts?"

---

白风听到，才明白为何王爷不让他再去西三胡同守着，原来千里寻才子正是幽栖居士。

Hearing this, Baifeng finally understood why the prince no longer needed him to guard Xisan Hutong. It turned out that the talented Qianli Xun was actually Youqi Jushi.

---

真是奇怪，一个姑娘家写个话本偏偏要装作才子，也不知是怎么想的，害自己一到临安整日待在西三胡同，错过好几次享用美食的机会。

How strange—a young lady writing storytelling scripts yet pretending to be a male scholar. Who knew what she was thinking? It had caused him to spend all his time at Xisan Hutong ever since arriving in Lin'an, missing several opportunities to enjoy fine food.

---

千里寻接过红绸，拽住仪王手臂，把他拉到一边。

Qianli Xun took the red silk, grabbed Prince Yi's arm, and pulled him to the side.

---

被心爱之人抓着手臂，申简辰的内心不由泛起美好的感觉。

Held by the arm by the one he loved, Shen Jianchen felt a warm flutter rise within him.

---

白风正要跟着过去，苦荞瞪着眼睛挡在他面前："主人家说话，你还是好生在此候着。"

Baifeng was about to follow when Kuqiao blocked his path, glaring: "The young lady is speaking with our guest. You'd better stay here and wait."

---

白风虽有力气，却不会对女人动手，加之苦荞瞪他的那一刻，竟然发现这丫鬟的眼睛挺美，不由多看了几眼。

Though Baifeng was strong, he would never lay hands on a woman. Besides, the moment Kuqiao glared at him, he was surprised to find the maid's eyes quite beautiful and couldn't help but steal a few more glances.

---

苦荞气道："谁让你这么盯着我看？"

Kuqiao said angrily: "Who told you to stare at me like that?"

---

白风说："你啊，你瞪我不就是让我看你？难道想让我视而不见？"

Baifeng said: "You there—you glared at me, didn't you? That's basically asking me to look at you. Did you want me to turn a blind eye?"

---

苦荞被怼得无语，不再说什么。

Kuqiao was rendered speechless by the retort and said nothing more.

---

千里寻把仪王拽到一边，放手后，先行礼："仪王不要介意，此事我不想让人知道，才把你拉到一边。"

Qianli Xun pulled Prince Yi to the side. After letting go, she bowed first: "Please don't take offense, Your Highness. I didn't want others to know about this matter, which is why I pulled you aside."

---

申简辰故意装作生气的样子："你都把我拉过来，我介意又有何用？"

Shen Jianchen deliberately pretended to be angry: "You've already pulled me over. What use is it if I take offense?"

---

千里寻见仪王生气，想着怎么能把他哄高兴，他既喜欢听说书，不如从此处着手。

Seeing Prince Yi angry, Qianli Xun thought about how to coax him into a good mood. Since he enjoyed listening to storytelling, she might as well start from there.

---

"仪王喜欢我写的故事，大不了我写好话本，先让苦荞送给仪王阅览，您看完后，我再让人送到说话艺人那里，您觉得如何？或者等我再写好新的故事，请仪王去听说书？"

"Since Your Highness enjoys the stories I write, at most I can have Kuqiao deliver the completed storytelling scripts to you for review first. After you've read them, I'll have someone send them to the professional storytellers. How does that sound? Or, once I've written new stories, I could invite Your Highness to come listen to the storytelling?"

---

千里寻哪曾哄过申简辰？这一世成为王爷，身份尊贵，竟然被美人所哄，申简辰甚是享受，再也装不下去，笑道："幽栖居士可要说话算话，不仅手稿要给我看，还要请我听说书。"

When had Qianli Xun ever coaxed Shen Jianchen before? In this life, as a prince with noble status, he was actually being coaxed by a beauty. Shen Jianchen thoroughly enjoyed it and could no longer keep up the act. He smiled: "Youqi Jushi must keep your word. Not only will you show me the manuscripts, but you'll also invite me to listen to the storytelling."

---

"没问题！也请仪王替我保密，不要把我写话本的事说出去。"

"No problem! But I also ask Your Highness to keep this secret—don't tell anyone that I write storytelling scripts."

---

"连文渊都不能说吗？"

"Not even Wenyuan?"

---

"当然。"

"Of course."

---

申简辰很满意，还是问道："你们互相喜欢对方，还需有所保留？"

Shen Jianchen was quite satisfied but still asked: "You two like each other, yet you still need to keep things from each other?"

---

"文渊喜欢诗词，我不想让他知道我偷偷写下里巴人之作，更不想破坏幽栖居士在他心中的形象。"

"Wenyuan loves poetry. I don't want him to know I secretly write such lowbrow works. I certainly don't want to ruin Youqi Jushi's image in his mind."

---

"该不会因为文渊你才在话本上留下千里寻的名字？"

"Could it be that you left the name 'Qianli Xun' on the storytelling scripts because of Wenyuan?"

---

"不是。"千里寻果断否认，她在竹林写话本时，文渊还未出现在她的人生中，"在我看来，这些故事只属于千里寻。"

"No." Qianli Xun denied it decisively. When she was writing storytelling scripts in the bamboo forest, Wenyuan hadn't yet appeared in her life. "In my view, these stories belong only to Qianli Xun."

---

申简辰明白过来，继续问："红绸已给你，打算怎么处理？难道还要挂到神柳上？"

Shen Jianchen understood and continued: "The red silk has been returned to you. What do you plan to do with it? Are you going to hang it back on the Sacred Willow?"

---

"既然掉落，说明红绸的任务已完成，再挂上去没有任何意义，我会把它收起来，保存好。"

"Since it fell, it means the red silk has completed its mission. Hanging it back up would be meaningless. I'll keep it and preserve it well."

---

申简辰瞅一眼千里寻手中的红绸，心想，它的任务难道是帮助他找到千里寻？如果是这样，足以证明他与千里寻之间依然有缘分："红绸已送到，我还有事，就不在此打扰。"

Shen Jianchen glanced at the red silk in Qianli Xun's hand and thought: Could its mission have been to help him find Qianli Xun? If so, that was enough proof that the bond between him and Qianli Xun still existed. "The red silk has been delivered. I still have matters to attend to, so I won't disturb you further."

---

千里寻行礼道："仪王慢走。"

Qianli Xun bowed: "Take care, Your Highness."

---

申简辰在马车上坐好后掀开帘子，探出脑袋："幽栖居士，你自称'老娘'很有趣，以后不用绷着。"

After settling into the carriage, Shen Jianchen lifted the curtain and poked his head out: "Youqi Jushi, your habit of calling yourself 'lao niang' is quite amusing. You don't need to hold back from now on."

---

千里寻感到疑惑，她从未在仪王面前自称过"老娘"，他如何得知？再说她穿越成朱淑真，向来注意，只有碰到像林觉和陈浩宇那类人，才会忍不住爆粗口。

Qianli Xun felt puzzled. She'd never called herself "lao niang" in front of Prince Yi. How did he know? Besides, ever since transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen, she'd always been careful. Only when encountering people like Lin Jue and Chen Haoyu would she blurt out coarse language.

---

千里寻把红绸递给苦荞："帮我收好，不要让人看到。"

Qianli Xun handed the red silk to Kuqiao: "Help me keep this safe. Don't let anyone see it."

---

苦荞接过："是，小姐。"

Kuqiao took it: "Yes, Young Lady."

---

边往府内走，千里寻边思索，不由问出口："我在仪王面前自称过'老娘'？"

Walking back into the residence, Qianli Xun pondered and couldn't help asking aloud: "Did I ever call myself 'lao niang' in front of Prince Yi?"

---

苦荞想想说："小姐见仪王恭恭敬敬，怎么会自称'老娘'？不过您在乔公子面前这样称呼过自己。"

Kuqiao thought for a moment and said: "Young Lady is always respectful when seeing Prince Yi. How could you call yourself 'lao niang'? But you did refer to yourself that way in front of Young Master Qiao."

---

千里寻想起昨日乘船游湖碰到陈浩宇时，的确如此自称过："也对，应该是文渊告诉他的。"

Qianli Xun remembered yesterday, when boating on the lake and encountering Chen Haoyu, she had indeed referred to herself that way. "That's right. Wenyuan must have told him."

---

她对这个答案很满意，不再多想。

She was quite satisfied with this answer and didn't think about it further.

---
