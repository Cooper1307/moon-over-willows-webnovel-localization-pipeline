第184章 离开赌坊遇颜池
## Chapter 184: Running into Yan Chi Outside the Gambling House

千里寻来到赌坊，吩咐车夫不用在外等待，将马车赶回去，然后带红豆一起走进赌坊。
里面挺大，有许多赌桌，每张桌子跟前围满人，大家玩得十分投入。
赌坊的伙计迎上来：“二位小爷看着面生，应该是第一次来吧？”
千里寻故意压粗嗓子：“问那么多干什么？赶紧给小爷介绍一下都有哪些玩法，小爷好选一个喜欢的玩玩儿。”
伙计说：“玩法多了去，有赌球、赌棋、掷骰子、牌九、斗鸡、斗蛐蛐，还有关扑，小爷想玩什么？”
千里寻甚是疑惑：“赌球？赌什么球？”
伙计解释道：“蹴鞠、马球、捶丸均可赌。”
千里寻向四周瞧瞧：“这里也没法蹴鞠，打马球之类，怎么赌？”
伙计继续解释：“这里当然没法赌球，您要是想玩儿，会有人专门带您前去。”
“什么是关扑？”千里寻前来学习，对于不懂的必然要虚心请教。
伙计见对方连关扑都不知道，推测眼前的小爷涉世未深，必然是待宰的羔羊，劝道：“这位小爷，您连关扑都不知，还是回去吧，小心到时输得光屁股出去。”
千里寻带目的而来，还没了解清楚，怎能轻易回去？
“哪有做买卖的把客人往出赶？赶紧的，给小爷我说说关扑怎么个玩法。”
不等伙计开口，红豆说：“公子，我知道关扑怎么玩儿。”
千里寻露出笑容，对伙计说：“你还是别说了，听我身边的人怎么说。”
伙计很不屑：“哼，不用听，我就知道他会说什么。”
红豆仔细介绍：“逛街的时候常常会看到，街边卖小玩意儿的，既可用银子买，还可与卖主商量好价钱，再商量好条件，然后拿几枚铜钱放进碗里摇，若卖家赢，银子都归他；若买家赢，免费将小玩意儿拿走。”
“哦，原来这就叫关扑。”千里寻看向伙计，“难道你们赌坊也卖小玩意儿？”
伙计见此时无新客到来，耐心解释：“那位小爷只在街边玩过关扑，却不知我们赌坊玩关扑都是玩大的，赌资既可是人，也可是房屋、田产、铺子，甚至所有家财，有些人一晚上从这里出去就成富翁，有些人则一晚上输尽所有。”
千里寻心想关扑还真不能玩儿，她瞅向旁边，见有人掷骰子，简单好学，不如玩玩儿。便不再理会伙计，拉起红豆挤到桌子跟前。
只见一人拿着木盒，里面放着骰子，在大家面前晃来晃去，玩的人仅需买大买小而已，若与坐庄之人猜中便赢，猜不中便输。
千里寻让红豆拿出银子买几回大小，她买大，人家是小，她买小，人家又是大，看来她确实没有赌博的命。
接着又到推牌九处看一回，推牌九稍微复杂些，她看半天没学明白，觉得无趣，招手叫来伙计。
“两位小爷有什么吩咐？”伙计倒客气。
千里寻问：“斗蛐蛐的在哪里？”
“两位小爷跟我来。”
伙计在前面带路，千里寻和红豆跟上。
Arriving at the gambling house, Qianli Xun sent the carriage home rather than making the driver wait, then stepped inside with Hongdou. The cavernous hall buzzed with packed gaming tables and shouting bettors.

伙计推开赌坊的后门，来到一处院落，全是人，有斗蛐蛐的，也有斗鸡的。虽是晚上，却灯火通明，并不影响赌博人的兴致。
An attendant hurried over. "Two new faces—first time here, young masters?"

谢过伙计后，两人先凑到斗蛐蛐的人群中，看到两只蛐蛐在蟋蟀碗中互相撕咬，斗得不可开交，千里寻押一回，结果还是输了。
Pitching her voice low, Qianli Xun barked, "Less nosiness, more rundown! Tell this young master what games you run so I can pick one."

她与红豆挤出人群，打算去玩斗鸡。无意中瞧见陈浩宇带着小弟在斗鸡处，玩得不亦乐乎。顿时失去兴致，更不愿被陈浩宇认出。再说该了解的已了解清楚，写话本尽够，也是该回去的时候。
"Plenty of choices: ball wagers, chess wagers, dice, pai gow, cockfighting, cricket fighting, and guanpu! What strikes your fancy?"

“我们回去吧。”
"Ball wagers? What balls?" Qianli Xun asked.

千里寻带红豆走出赌坊，天色比来时更晚些。没有留马车，二人步行回家。她本就这么打算，想逛逛夜晚的街市，大宋没有宵禁制度，夜晚的街市也是极其繁华。
"Cuju, polo, chuiwan—bet on any of them."

她无心留意街头的小吃和好看的玩意儿，看到陈浩宇，不由自主想到婚约。想到婚约，又不由自主想到申简辰。
Glancing around the crowded hall, she asked, "You can hardly play cuju or polo in here—how do you bet?"

他简直是疯了，竟然想到训练猪爬树。若真的训练成功，难道要答应嫁给他？
"Not right here, of course—want to wager, and someone escorts you straight to the pitch."

千里寻发现她竟然渐渐地不再去想文渊，而文渊给她留下的情伤也正在一点一点消退。
"And what is guanpu?" Here to learn, she asked freely.

她从心底感谢申简辰，尽管他们不可能在一起，他却用穿越前的奋不顾身和穿越后的执着慢慢住进她的内心，如同一剂良药，逐渐平复她内心的伤口。
Spotting a greenhorn who had never even heard of guanpu—a lamb begging to be sheared—the attendant actually warned her: "Young master, if you do not even know guanpu, better head home before you lose your trousers."

理智告诉她想仪王是不对的，可偷偷摸摸想一下应该不会伤害梦容。她敞开心扉，回忆她与申简辰的过往，一边走，一边露出让人捉摸不透的笑容。
"Since when does a shop chase paying customers out? Quit stalling and explain guanpu!"

红豆跟在小姐身边，见小姐名义上是逛街，却从不看街上的人和物件，仿佛沉浸在另外一个世界，脸上还时不时浮现笑容。
Before the attendant could speak, Hongdou chimed in. "Young Master, I know how guanpu works."

正当千里寻沉浸在个人的世界时，被一阵喊声拉回现实。
Qianli Xun grinned at the attendant. "Zip it—let my boy here explain."

“人往那里跑！快追！”
The attendant sniffed. "Hmph, I already know what he is going to say."

千里寻不再沉迷于个人世界，借着夜晚街市的灯光，视线穿过人群，发现有个人影朝一旁的巷子跑去，人影看上去已受伤，行动起来有些艰难。
Hongdou explained clearly: "You see it all the time on market streets—peddlers selling trinkets let you pay cash OR agree on a price and terms, then shake copper coins in a bowl. If the vendor wins, he keeps your silver; if the buyer wins, you walk off with the goods for free."

快到巷口时，那人捂住伤口，紧张地向后瞧。
"Aha, so that is guanpu!" Qianli Xun glanced back at the attendant. "Don't tell me your gambling house hawks trinkets too?"

这一瞧不要紧，千里寻觉得他很是面熟，仔细一想，原来是曾在街上遇到的“延迟男”。心想：“当初那个张狂劲儿，现在怂了吧！”正想着，颜池已进入巷子，不见人影。
With no new patrons walking in, the attendant explained with patience. "Your lad only knows street-side guanpu. Inside our hall, we play guanpu for real stakes—people, houses, fields, shops, even whole family fortunes! Some walk out overnight tycoons, and others lose the shirt off their backs in one throw."

接着看到七八个人手执利刃，向巷口冲将而来。为首的公子长得浓眉大眼，相貌周正。刚才的声音应该是这伙人喊出，显然他们正在追“延迟男”。
Note to self: steer clear of guanpu! Spotting a lively dice table nearby—easy rules—she ditched the attendant and squeezed up to the rail with Hongdou. A dealer rattled dice inside a wooden box while players bet high or low. Having Hongdou drop silver a few rounds, Qianli Xun bet high when the dice came up low and low when they came up high—zero gambler's luck in her bones.

因好奇心使然，千里寻对红豆说：“跟上去瞧瞧。”
Next they watched pai gow for a spell, yet the tiles looked too fussy to crack on the fly, so she waved the attendant over again. "Where are the cricket fights?"

待七八个人追进巷子，千里寻带红豆放轻脚步尾随而去。
"Follow me, young masters."

别看街市人多，巷子里却乌漆墨黑，而且拐来拐去，很容易将人绕晕，那伙人绕来绕去，找不到人影，只能放弃。
Pushing open a rear door, the attendant led them into a brightly lit courtyard packed with bettors clustered around cricket bowls and cockfighting pits. Thanking him, they squeezed into the cricket ring, watched two crickets lock jaws in an earthenware bowl, dropped another bet—and lost again.

为首的说：“今日放他一马，再来邵家镖局，让他有去无回。撤！”
Working free of the crowd to check out the cockfights, Qianli Xun froze: Chen Haoyu stood right across the pit cheering with his lackeys! Her interest died on the spot, along with any desire to be recognized. She already had more than enough material for her huaben anyway. "Time to head home."

邵家镖局的人要原路返回，千里寻与红豆不想被对方看到，小心躲起来。待人走远后，没有什么热闹可瞧，她准备带红豆原路返回。
Stepping out into the deeper night, they set off on foot; without curfew in the Song capital and prefectures, Lin'an's night markets glittered with life. Yet Qianli Xun paid zero mind to snacks or trinkets. Seeing Chen Haoyu had dragged back thoughts of the betrothal—which bounced straight to Shen Jianchen. The guy really was nuts, training a pig to climb a tree! What if it actually climbed one—would she have to marry him?

说是原路返回，可在黑漆漆的巷子里瞎撞，怎么都走不出去。
With a jolt, she noticed Wenyuan barely crossed her mind anymore, the old heartbreak fading bit by bit. In her heart she thanked Shen Jianchen: impossible as their future was, his fearless dash through the earthquake and stubborn devotion here had slipped quietly into her chest like medicine soothing an old wound. Reason told her thinking of Prince Yi was wrong, yet sneaking a quiet memory hurt Mengrong none. Letting her guard drop, she drifted through memories of him, a faint, unreadable smile playing across her lips.

“红豆，你家小姐我方向感不好，难道你也是？”
Walking beside her, Hongdou watched her mistress ignore every lantern and stall as if wandering another world, smiling at thin air.

“我对这里不熟悉，跟着小姐进来时，一心都在那伙人身上，忘了记路。”
A harsh shout ripped Qianli Xun back to the street: "He ran that way! After him!"

千里寻很无奈，指着一个方向：“试试那边。”
Through the lamplit crowd, she saw a limping figure clutch a wound and duck into a side alley, shooting a panicked glance over his shoulder right at the mouth of the lane. Wait a second—that face! The "Delayed" guy from the market! So much for all his cocky swagger—running scared now! Moments after Yan Chi vanished into the dark, seven or eight men brandishing sharp blades charged past, led by a square-jawed young man with thick brows.

两人向另一个巷子拐去。
Curiosity winning out, Qianli Xun nudged Hongdou. "Let us tail them for a look."

走着走着，千里寻感觉有一只手拽住她的脚。要搁别的女子早已吓得魂飞魄散，她从小胆大，加之拳法高超，什么都吓不到她。
Silently shadowing the blades into the pitch-black alley, they watched the pursuers wind through twist after twist until the quarry vanished clean away.

只见她迅速转身，通过感觉判断那人的方向，一拳打去……
"Let him off easy tonight!" the leader barked. "Come near Shao Escort Agency again and he will never walk out alive! Pull back!"

拽住她的手很快松开，只听：“哎哟！原来……是你呀，叫……叫什么来着，对，朱……淑真。”
Ducking into shadow while the Shao men marched back out, Qianli Xun waited for quiet before turning to retrace their steps—only to hit dead end after twisting fork in the dark.

黑暗中竟然有人知道她的名字，看来对方了解她的拳法，千里寻更是好奇：“红豆，把火折子拿出来，让我看清他是谁。”
"Hongdou, your mistress has zero sense of direction—don't tell me you do too?"

红豆将火折子打开，吹出火苗，递给小姐。
"I do not know this maze either, Miss. Kept my eyes glued on those men and forgot the turns."

千里寻拿着火折子，蹲下，借着火光看清对方正是“延迟男”：“原来是你，你倒机灵躲过那帮人。”
Qianli Xun sighed and pointed at random. "Try down there."

颜池说：“我受……重伤，能不能……看在我们认识，帮……帮我？”
Halfway down the next pitch-black turn, a hand shot out and grabbed her ankle! Any other lady would have screamed bloody murder; bold by nature and sharp with her fists, Qianli Xun whipped around, judged the target by feel, and fired a punch straight down.

千里寻“哼”一声：“你是好是坏，我还不清楚，为何要帮你？”
The grip dropped instantly, followed by a groan: "Ow! So... it is you... what was it again? Right—Zhu... Shuzhen!"

颜池从怀里掏出珠簪，递到千里寻面前：“你……你看，这个……这个……我还留着。”
Someone in the pitch dark knew her name—and recognized her fist?! "Hongdou, strike your fire-starter so I can see who this is!"

千里寻瞧一眼珠簪，很是疑惑：“一枚男子用的普通珠簪而已，跟我有什么关系？”
Blowing a flame alive on the tinder tube, Hongdou handed it over. Crouching down, Qianli Xun held the glow to a pale face—the "Delayed" guy himself! "Well, well—pretty slick dodging that mob."

颜池忍着伤痛说：“第一次……遇见……你时，你掉下的，我……我一直保留着。”
"I took... a bad cut..." Yan Chi panted. "For old acquaintance's sake... give me... a hand?"

千里寻想起在街上相遇对打的场景，那时她女扮男装，头发散落，掉下这枚珠簪。她并未当回事，对方却偷偷珍藏起来。千里寻甚是气愤，一把夺过：“既是我的东西，没必要让你留着。”
"Hmph! For all I know you are a crook—why should I help you?"

转而递给红豆：“送给你。”
Reaching inside his robe, Yan Chi held up a beaded hair-pin. "Look... I still... kept this..."

红豆看出小姐根本不想陌生的男子对她有念想，爽快接过：“谢小姐。”
Qianli Xun blinked. "Just a plain man's hair-pin—what is that to me?"

“你啊，自求多福。”千里寻吹灭火折子，起身准备离开。
"The first time... we met... you dropped it... I kept it safe..."

“没想到……你竟……见死不救，看来……那日在街头……姑娘帮人……不过是……是假装善良而已……”
Flashback to their street brawl when her disguised hair had come undone and lost that pin—she had forgotten it on the spot, yet this stranger had hoarded it?! Annoyed, she snatched it right back. "Since it is mine, no reason for you to hold onto it!" Tossing it to Hongdou, she added, "Yours now."

颜池这话，还真的击中千里寻的要害：“你既拿话噎我，我就帮你一回，把你送到医馆。”
Seeing her mistress wanted zero strange men harboring notions, Hongdou pocketed it crisply. "Thank you, Miss!"

千里寻正要吩咐红杏去扶颜池，旁边一户人家的门被打开。
"Fend for yourself!" Blowing out the flame, Qianli Xun rose to leave.

借着院内的灯光，千里寻一眼认出开门的人正是与她在竹林比试的高手。不如将大麻烦送给他，自己落得轻松。
"Never thought... you would leave a man to bleed out..." Yan Chi wheezed. "Guess helping folks on the street that day... was all just fake kindness..."

“徒弟，师傅在此，为何不来跪拜！”
The barb hit square on Qianli Xun's pride. "Since you are playing the guilt card, fine—I will drop you at a clinic!"

小姐突然说这话，红豆甚是诧异。
Just as she turned to have Hongdou hoist Yan Chi up, a wooden courtyard door creaked open right beside them. Spilling lamplight lit up the man stepping out—the martial master who had dueled her in the bamboo grove! Jackpot! Dump the headache straight onto him!

颜池也不明所以：“你……到底帮不帮……我？”
"Disciple! Your master stands right here—why aren't you kneeling?!"

陆远峰边关门，边朝传来声音的方向看去，黑暗中有两个人影。他关好门后，大声问：“你是在和我说话吗？”
Hongdou stared in bewilderment at her mistress's sudden line, while Yan Chi groaned, "Are you... helping me... or not?"

千里寻打趣道：“在竹林时，你跪在我面前，强行认我当师傅，难道忘了？”
Shutting his gate and squinting into the dark, Lu Yuanfeng called out, "Talking to me?"

陆远峰明白过来，走上前：“师傅，真的是你？”
"Back in the bamboo grove, you dropped to your knees begging me to be your master—forgot already?" Qianli Xun teased.

“如假包换，只不过我今日为出行方面，穿上男装而已。”千里寻又问，“那是你家？”
Recognition lit up Lu Yuanfeng's face as he hurried forward. "Master?! Really you?!"

陆远峰答道：“是，师傅进去坐坐？”
"In the flesh—just wearing men's clothes for getting around. That your house?"

“不了。”千里寻哪有心情到别人家里坐，眼前的大麻烦得赶紧找人解决，“你当初认我做师傅，我还没答应，今日有缘遇见，不妨收下你这位徒弟……”
"Yes! Care to come inside for a seat, Master?"

陆远峰立刻跪在地上，激动地说：“师傅在上，请受徒弟一拜。”
"Pass!" No time for tea with a bleeding man at her feet. "Back then I had not accepted you yet. Since fate threw us together tonight, I will officially take you as my disciple..."

千里寻上前扶起陆远峰：“免礼免礼，既然我已将你收下，得送给你个见面礼，不过这见面礼有些麻烦，不知道你愿不愿意收？”
Lu Yuanfeng dropped straight to his knees in excitement. "Master above, accept your disciple's bow!"

“师傅都发话，再麻烦，我也得收。”陆远峰倒豪爽。
Pulling him up, Qianli Xun grinned. "At ease, at ease. Since you are officially my disciple, you get a welcome gift—a bit of a hassle, though. Willing to accept?"

新收的徒弟态度不错，千里寻甚是满意：“有个受伤的人，你把他带到你家里，找个大夫帮他治伤。”
"When Master gives the word, hassle or not, I take it!" Lu Yuanfeng declared heartily.

陆远峰毫不犹豫，立即答应，又向师傅请示：“师傅，我能不能先把人扶进屋里，等办完事再给他请大夫。”
"There is an injured man right here. Carry him inside your house and fetch a doctor to patch him up."

千里寻随口问道：“你有事？”
Agreeing without hesitation, Lu Yuanfeng paused to ask, "Master, may I lay him inside first, run a quick errand, and fetch the doctor right after?"

陆远峰照实说：“陈公子雇我保护他，今晚他去赌坊，也要求我跟着，正要去，没想到一出来遇见师傅。”
"Got an errand?"

“陈浩宇？”
"Young Master Chen hired me as his bodyguard. He went to the gambling house tonight and expects me there. I was just heading out when I bumped into Master."

“是。”
"Chen Haoyu?"

“不用去了，我刚从赌坊出来，见过他，好得不行。”
"Yes."

“我收过他的银子，不去不太好。”
"Skip it. I just walked out of that gambling house and saw him—healthy as a horse."

“你是听师傅的，还是听陈浩宇的？”
"I took his silver, though—bad form not to show."

“当然听师傅的，可也得赚银子。”
"Do you listen to your master, or to Chen Haoyu?"

“既然听师傅的，就别去保护陈浩宇，他要怪你白嫖银子，让他来找我。”
"Master, of course—still got to earn my pay."

“有师傅这话，我白拿银子也心安理得。”
"Listen to Master and ditch Chen Haoyu. If he gripes you pocketed silver for nothing, tell him to take it up with me!"

安排妥当后，千里寻为自己能顺利甩掉大麻烦感到满意：“喂，听到没？我徒弟愿意帮你。”
"With Master's backing, I will pocket his silver with a clear conscience!"

颜池说：“我不叫‘喂’，我叫……颜池。”
Thrilled to offload her problem so cleanly, Qianli Xun nudged the wounded man. "Hey, hear that? My disciple will patch you up."

“延迟嘛，谁不知道？就是不守时呗！”
"My name is not 'Hey'..." Yan Chi muttered. "It is... Yan Chi."

“是……颜色的颜，池水的……池，姑娘……可记清楚了？”
"'Delayed,' yeah, everybody knows—never on time!"

千里寻并未理会颜池，对陆远峰说：“徒弟，把他扶进你屋里，再把师傅带出这曲里拐弯的L巷子。”
"It is 'Yan' (颜, as in color) and 'Chi' (池, as in pond)... got it straight, Miss?"

“是。”
Ignoring him, Qianli Xun told Lu Yuanfeng, "Haul him inside, Disciple, then lead your master out of this twisting maze of an alley."

陆远峰将颜池扶起来，再扶进屋里。出来后，将千里寻与红豆带出黑漆漆的巷子，来到大街上，与师傅分别后去找大夫。
"Right away!"

陆远峰走后，天色更晚，千里寻不想在街上逗留，租辆马车回望月轩。
Carrying Yan Chi indoors, Lu Yuanfeng came right back out, guided Qianli Xun and Hongdou back onto the main thoroughfare, and bade farewell to fetch a physician. With the hour late, Qianli Xun hailed a hired carriage straight back to Wangyue Pavilion.
