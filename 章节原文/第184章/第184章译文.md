## Chapter 184: Leaving the Gambling House, Running into Yan Chi

At the gambling house, Qianli Xun told the driver he needn't wait outside and sent the carriage home, then went in with Hongdou.

Inside it was quite large, with many gaming tables, every one ringed with people, all of them utterly absorbed in play.

An attendant at the gambling house stepped forward to greet them. "The two young masters look unfamiliar. First time here, is it?"

Qianli Xun deliberately thickened her voice. "Why all the questions? Tell me what games you've got, quick, so this young master can pick one to play."

The attendant said, "Plenty of games—ball betting, chess betting, dice, pai gow, cockfighting, cricket fighting, and guanpu too. What does the young master fancy?"

Qianli Xun was puzzled. "Ball betting? What kind of ball?"

The attendant explained, "Cuju, polo, and chuiwan can all be bet on."

Qianli Xun glanced around. "You can't play cuju or polo in here. How do you bet on them?"

The attendant went on, "Of course you can't bet on ball games here. If you want to play, someone will take you there specially."

"What's guanpu?" Qianli Xun had come to learn, and she was sure to ask humbly about anything she didn't understand.

Seeing that the young master didn't even know guanpu, the attendant reckoned he was green and must be a lamb waiting to be slaughtered, so he advised, "Young master, you don't even know guanpu. You'd best go back. Mind you don't lose your trousers and walk out bare-arsed."

Qianli Xun had come with a purpose, and she hadn't learned what she needed to know. How could she turn back so easily?

"What sort of shop turns customers away? Hurry up and tell this young master how guanpu works."

Before the attendant could open his mouth, Hongdou said, "Young master, I know how guanpu works."

Qianli Xun broke into a smile. "Never mind, then," she told the attendant. "Let's hear what my companion has to say."

The attendant sneered. "Hah, no need. I can guess what he'll say."

Hongdou explained carefully, "When you stroll the streets you often see vendors selling little trinkets by the roadside. You can buy them with silver, or agree a price with the seller and agree on terms, then drop a few copper coins in a bowl and shake it. If the seller wins, the silver is his; if the buyer wins, he walks off with the trinket for free."

"Oh, so that's guanpu." Qianli Xun looked at the attendant. "Does your gambling house sell trinkets too?"

With no new customers coming in, the attendant patiently explained, "That young master has only played guanpu by the roadside. He doesn't know that in a gambling house we play for big stakes. The stake can be a person, a house, farmland, a shop, even your entire fortune. Some people walk out of here one evening rich; others lose everything in a single night."

Qianli Xun thought to herself that guanpu was best left alone. She glanced to one side and saw people throwing dice—simple and easy to learn. She might as well try that. She paid the attendant no more mind and pulled Hongdou through the crowd to the table.

A man was holding a wooden box with dice inside, shaking it back and forth before the onlookers. Players only had to bet big or small; guess right against the banker and you won, guess wrong and you lost.

Qianli Xun had Hongdou take out silver and bet on big or small a few times. When she bet big, it was small; when she bet small, it was big. She clearly had no luck for gambling.

Next they went to watch a round of pai gow. Pai gow was a bit more complicated; she watched for ages without grasping it and found it dull, so she beckoned the attendant over.

"What can I do for the two young masters?" The attendant was polite enough.

Qianli Xun asked, "Where are the cricket fights?"

"Follow me, young masters."

The attendant led the way, with Qianli Xun and Hongdou behind him.

He pushed open the back door of the gambling house onto a courtyard packed with people, some fighting crickets, others fighting cocks. Though it was night, the place was lit as bright as day, and it did nothing to dampen the gamblers' spirits.

After thanking the attendant, the two pushed into the cricket-fighting crowd. Two crickets were biting each other in a cricket bowl, locked in a fierce fight. Qianli Xun placed one bet and lost again.

She and Hongdou squeezed out of the crowd, meaning to try the cockfighting. By chance she spotted Chen Haoyu at the cockfighting pit, enjoying himself with his cronies. Her interest evaporated at once, and she was even less keen on being recognized by him. Besides, she had learned all she needed to write her story. It was time to go.

"Let's head back."

Qianli Xun led Hongdou out of the gambling house. It was later than when they had arrived. They hadn't kept the carriage, so the two walked home. That had been her plan all along—to stroll the night market. The Great Song had no curfew, and the night markets were thriving.

She wasn't in the mood for the street snacks or the pretty trinkets. Seeing Chen Haoyu put her in mind of the marriage contract, and the marriage contract put her in mind of Shen Jianchen.

He was out of his mind, thinking of training pigs to climb trees. If he actually succeeded, did that mean she had to agree to marry him?

Qianli Xun found that she had gradually stopped thinking about Wenxian, and the hurt he had left in her heart was fading bit by bit.

She thanked Shen Jianchen from the bottom of her heart. Though they could never be together, he had slowly made his way into her heart—with his reckless devotion before the transmigration and his persistence after it—like a good medicine, soothing her inner wounds little by little.

Reason told her it was wrong to think of the Prince of Yi, but a secret little thought wouldn't hurt Mengrong, surely. She opened her heart and recalled her times with Shen Jianchen, walking along with a smile no one could fathom on her lips.

Hongdou followed her mistress. Her mistress was supposedly out strolling the market, yet never once looked at the people or goods on the street, as though lost in another world, and now and then a smile crossed her face.

Just as Qianli Xun was lost in her own world, a shout pulled her back to reality.

"There he goes! After him!"

Qianli Xun shook off her daydream. By the light of the night market, she looked through the crowd and saw a figure slipping into an alley to one side. The figure seemed wounded and moved with difficulty.

As he neared the mouth of the alley, the man pressed a hand to his wound and glanced back nervously.

That glance was all it took: Qianli Xun thought he looked familiar, and after a moment's thought realized he was the "Mr. Tardy" she had run into on the street. She thought, All that swagger before, and now you've turned chicken! Even as the thought crossed her mind, Yan Chi slipped into the alley and vanished from sight.

Then seven or eight men came rushing toward the alley mouth, blades in hand. The one in front was a well-set young man with thick brows and large eyes and a handsome, upright face. The shout must have come from this lot, and they were clearly chasing "Mr. Tardy."

Curiosity got the better of her. Qianli Xun said to Hongdou, "Let's follow and see."

Once the seven or eight had chased into the alley, Qianli Xun took Hongdou in after them, treading softly.

Busy as the market was, the alley was pitch-black, and it twisted and turned so much that it was easy to get dizzy. The gang wound this way and that, lost sight of their quarry, and had to give up.

The man in charge said, "We'll let him off today. Next time he shows at the Shao family escort agency, he'll go in and never come out. Withdraw!"

The Shao agency men started back the way they had come. Unwilling to be seen, Qianli Xun and Hongdou hid carefully. After the men had gone and there was no more excitement to watch, she prepared to take Hongdou back the way they had come.

Back the way they had come, indeed—but stumbling around a pitch-black alley, they couldn't find their way out at all.

"Hongdou, your mistress has no sense of direction. Don't tell me you do too?"

"I'm not familiar with this place. When I followed you in, miss, I was watching those men the whole time and forgot to note the way."

Qianli Xun was at a loss. She pointed one way. "Try over there."

The two turned down another alley.

As they walked, Qianli Xun felt a hand grab her foot. Any other girl would have been scared out of her wits, but she had been bold since childhood, and with her skill in boxing there was nothing that could frighten her.

She spun around at once, judged where the person was by feel, and threw a punch...

The hand let go at once, and a voice said, "Ouch! It's... it's you! What was your name again? Right—Zhu... Shuzhen."

Someone in the dark knew her name. He must know her style of boxing. Qianli Xun grew more curious. "Hongdou, get out the fire strike and let me see who this is."

Hongdou opened the fire striker, blew up a flame, and handed it to her mistress.

Qianli Xun took the fire striker, crouched down, and by its light saw that the man was none other than "Mr. Tardy." "So it's you. You were quick enough to dodge that lot."

Yan Chi said, "I'm badly... hurt. Could you... since we know each other, help... help me?"

Qianli Xun snorted. "I don't yet know whether you're good or bad. Why should I help you?"

Yan Chi drew a pearl hairpin from his robe and held it out to her. "You... look. This... this I still kept."

Qianli Xun glanced at the hairpin, baffled. "It's just an ordinary pearl hairpin a man would wear. What's it got to do with me?"

Yan Chi said through his pain, "The first time... I met you, you dropped it. I... I've kept it all this while."

Qianli Xun recalled their clash on the street, when she had been dressed as a man, her hair had come loose, and this hairpin had fallen out. She had thought nothing of it, yet he had treasured it in secret. Furious, she snatched it back. "If it's mine, there's no reason for you to keep it."

She handed it to Hongdou instead. "Yours."

Hongdou could tell her mistress didn't want some strange man thinking of her, and took it without hesitation. "Thank you, miss."

"You're on your own, you know." Qianli Xun blew out the fire striker and got up to leave.

"To think... you'd leave a person to die. So that day on the street, when you helped someone... it was all... all pretence of kindness..."

That jab of Yan Chi's hit home. "Since you'd twist my words, I'll help you once and take you to a doctor."

Qianli Xun was about to tell Hongdou to help Yan Chi up when a door opened at a house beside them.

By the light from the courtyard, Qianli Xun saw at once that the person opening the door was the expert she had sparred with in the bamboo grove. Better hand him this big trouble and be rid of it herself.

"Disciple, your master is here. Why aren't you on your knees kowtowing?"

Hongdou was startled by this sudden remark from her mistress.

Yan Chi was no less at a loss. "Will you... help me... or not?"

Lu Yuanfeng closed the door and looked toward the voice; in the dark he made out two figures. When the door was shut, he called out, "Are you talking to me?"

Qianli Xun teased him. "In the bamboo grove you knelt before me and insisted I be your master. Have you forgotten?"

Lu Yuanfeng caught on and came forward. "Master, is it really you?"

"As real as can be. Only today I'm dressed as a man for ease of travel." She added, "Is this your house?"

Lu Yuanfeng answered, "It is. Will master come in and sit?"

"No." Qianli Xun was in no mood to sit in someone else's house; this big trouble had to be disposed of. "When you insisted I be your master I never agreed, but now that fate has brought us together, I may as well take you as my disciple..."

Lu Yuanfeng dropped to his knees at once, saying fervently, "Master, please accept your disciple's bow."

Qianli Xun stepped forward and helped him up. "No need, no need. Since I've taken you on, I ought to give you a welcome gift—though this gift is a bit of trouble. I don't know if you'll take it?"

"If master says so, I'll take it however much trouble it is." Lu Yuanfeng was nothing if not forthright.

His attitude pleased his new mistress. "There's a wounded man. Take him into your house and find a doctor to treat him."

Lu Yuanfeng agreed without a moment's hesitation, then asked his master, "Master, may I help him inside first and call a doctor after I've finished my business?"

Qianli Xun asked casually, "You have business?"

Lu Yuanfeng told her plainly, "Young Master Chen hired me to protect him. He's gone to the gambling house tonight and asked me to follow. I was on my way when I ran into master."

"Chen Haoyu?"

"Yes."

"No need to go. I've just come from the gambling house and seen him. He's doing just fine."

"I've taken his silver. It wouldn't be right not to go."

"Do you obey your master, or Chen Haoyu?"

"My master, of course—but I still need to earn silver."

"If you obey your master, don't go protect Chen Haoyu. If he complains you took his silver for nothing, let him come find me."

"With master's word, I can keep the silver with an easy conscience."

With that settled, Qianli Xun was pleased with herself for dumping the trouble so neatly. "Hey, did you hear? My disciple here will help you."

Yan Chi said, "My name isn't 'Hey.' I'm... Yan Chi."

"Mr. Tardy—who doesn't know that? It means you can't keep an appointment!"

"It's... Yan as in colour, Chi as in pond, miss... have you got that clear?"

Qianli Xun paid him no attention and said to Lu Yuanfeng, "Disciple, help him into your house, then show your master out of this twisty L-shaped alley."

"Yes."

Lu Yuanfeng helped Yan Chi to his feet and into the house. Coming back out, he led Qianli Xun and Hongdou out of the dark alley to the main street, took leave of his master, and went to find a doctor.

After Lu Yuanfeng left, the hour grew later still. Qianli Xun didn't want to linger on the street, so she hired a carriage back to Wangyue Pavilion.
