# Chapter 72: Finally Found Qianli Xun

---

By the time they reached the Divine Willow, the setting sun had gradually faded, and a new moon was slowly rising on the horizon. It wasn't fully dark yet, so the moon's silhouette wasn't as clear as it would be on a pitch-black night.

---

Shen Jianchen still sat on the rock, leaning against the thick trunk of the Divine Willow, silently communicating with it in his heart.

---

"If you've already brought Qianli Xun back, please take me back too; if she's still in Lin'an, please let me find her as soon as possible."

---

The Divine Willow gave no response. Shen Jianchen closed his eyes and continued to reach out to it with all his heart.

---

A voice came: "Amitabha."

---

"Could it be the voice of the Divine Willow?" Shen Jianchen opened his eyes and saw an elderly nun standing beneath the Divine Willow, her face kind and her smile beaming.

---

So it wasn't the Divine Willow after all. Disappointed, Shen Jianchen stood up and greeted her: "Amitabha. Master, do you come from the nunnery on the mountain?"

---

The elderly nun was precisely Master Jingguang: "Indeed. I often see you sitting alone beneath the Divine Willow. Today I couldn't resist coming over to say hello."

---

Shen Jianchen felt puzzled. He had never encountered the elderly nun by the Divine Willow before. Why would she say she often saw him? Could she see what was happening at the foot of the mountain from up above?

---

Shen Jianchen looked up toward the mountain. It wasn't particularly high, but it would still be difficult to see the Divine Willow from the summit. Aside from the tree cover, the vertical distance made it hard to look down and spot anything.

---

Master Jingguang seemed to guess what Shen Jianchen was thinking. She smiled and explained: "I've come down from the back of the mountain several times. You were so focused on the Divine Willow that you never noticed me."

---

"I see. May I ask, Master, does the Divine Willow belong to the nunnery?" Shen Jianchen wanted to learn more about the Divine Willow, after all, it was exactly the same as the one he had seen before he transmigrated.

---

Master Jingguang said: "The Divine Willow doesn't belong to the nunnery. Before my master passed into nirvana, she told me it had been here for over a thousand years. She also said it still has nearly another thousand years of life ahead. Imagine—by then, you and I will be long gone, yet it will still be alive. Who knows what era it will witness."

---

Shen Jianchen calculated the timeline in his mind. The more he calculated, the more excited he became. This Divine Willow was exactly the one he had seen in modern society. He had thought so before, but only by instinct. Only after hearing the elderly nun speak did he truly confirm that this was the Divine Willow that had brought him to this era.

---

What puzzled him, though, was why this thousand-year-old Divine Willow existed in such different environments a thousand years ago and a thousand years later. Perhaps all his experiences were nothing but a dream.

---

"Over the next thousand-plus years, eras will continue to change. I'm not clear on the details, but what's certain is that it will witness a new era of equality for all and advanced technology."

---

Master Jingguang feigned surprise: "Oh? Has the benefactor experienced such an era?"

---

For some reason, Shen Jianchen felt inexplicably close to the elderly nun. He didn't want to hide anything: "To tell you the truth, I come from a more advanced era. This Divine Willow brought me to the Song Dynasty and made me someone else. Another friend was also brought here, but I can't find her. That's why I come here so often—to find out where it brought her."

---

Master Jingguang glanced up at the red silk ribbon high above: "When the affinity is destined, you will meet. I suspect you have already encountered her—you simply do not realize she is the one you seek."

---

"What? I've already met her? Who is she? How does the Master know this?" Shen Jianchen stepped closer to Master Jingguang and asked excitedly.

---

Master Jingguang revealed a kindly smile: "You'll know soon enough. Amitabha. Farewell, benefactor."

---

Master Jingguang left the Divine Willow and walked toward the path leading to the back of the mountain. Though elderly, her steps were swift and sure.

---

Shen Jianchen watched the elderly nun's figure until it was hidden by the trees and grass at the foot of the mountain.

---

He turned back to the Divine Willow and murmured to himself: "The old nun just said I've already met Qianli Xun. Divine Willow, tell me—who is Qianli Xun? Wenyuan? Dongfang Chenze? Cao Ban? Or An Mingxuan? Sigh, why am I only guessing men? It's because the storyteller told me the story was written by a talented scholar named Qianli Xun, which made me think she transmigrated and became a man. Could she still be a woman? If she is, then who? Mengrong? Impossible. Miao Zhiyu? Doesn't seem like it. Could it be... the Youqi Jushi?"

---

Shen Jianchen remembered the scene where she called herself "old lady": "Could it really be her?"

---

The sky had darkened. The crescent moon had climbed high, casting its limited moonlight on the branches and leaves of the Divine Willow. The red silk ribbon high above swayed in the wind under the moonlight, unknowingly loosened by the breeze, and began to drift downward...

---

Shen Jianchen watched the red silk ribbon drifting down and sighed: "What's the point of hanging it so high? It's hard for others to reach, yet it's easily blown away by the wind. Luckily I'm here and I know qinggong. I might as well catch it and hang it back up."

---

Shen Jianchen reached out to catch the drifting red silk ribbon. As it neared his hand, in the moonlight, he seemed to see the three characters "Qianli Xun."

---

He quickly caught the red silk ribbon in his hand and swiftly unfolded it. In the moonlight, the three characters "Qianli Xun" were plainly visible.

---

Shen Jianchen was overwhelmed with excitement. Looking more carefully, he also saw Zhu Shuzhen's name, with a rectangular box drawn around it. Tears of excitement and joy rolled down: "So you transmigrated and became the Youqi Jushi."

---

Shen Jianchen tucked the red silk ribbon into his robe and ran excitedly, leaping over the pond and dashing through the Youlin forest. Just as he reached the gate of the villa, he happened to see Baifeng returning from West Third Alley. Before Baifeng could greet him, he quickly said: "Drive the carriage and come with me to West Lake."

---

Baifeng asked: "Your Highness, it's already dark. Why go to West Lake now?"

---

"Less talk!" Shen Jianchen was about to board the carriage when he suddenly remembered something and beckoned to the servant at the gate: "Come here!"

---

After the servant approached, Shen Jianchen asked: "Has Wenyuan returned?"

---

The servant replied: "Young Master Qiao hasn't returned yet."

---

Shen Jianchen urged: "Baifeng! Drive the carriage to West Lake! Hurry!"

---

Baifeng didn't know what was going on, but since the prince had ordered it, he dutifully complied.

---

The prince had already boarded the carriage. Baifeng quickly jumped up too. A cloud of dust rose as the carriage galloped along the road to West Lake.

---

The Youlin Villa wasn't far from West Lake. After about half an hour, they finally arrived.

---

Shen Jianchen quickly jumped down from the carriage. Baifeng settled the horses and followed the prince. At this hour, the area around West Lake was lit up bright. There were still quite a few people around, mostly those finishing their stroll and heading home.

---

West Lake was so vast—where on earth should he look? Just as Shen Jianchen was at a loss, he spotted Miao Zhiyu and her close friends about to board their carriage and leave. He stepped forward and asked: "Miss Miao, do you know where Wenyuan is?"

---

Miao Zhiyu already knew the other was Yi Wang. She curtsied: "Yi Wang."

---

"No need for formalities. Quick, tell me where Wenyuan is!" Shen Jianchen looked very anxious.

---

Miao Zhiyu said: "The last time I saw them was on the north side of the lake. Your Highness can try looking there."

---

A voice came: "Your Highness Yi Wang, I know where Qiao Wenxian is."

---

Shen Jianchen turned around. It was Chen Haoyu, with a group of playboys at his side. They all stepped forward to greet him: "Your Highness Yi Wang."

---

Miao Zhiyu disliked Chen Haoyu greatly. She quickly curtsied: "Your Highness Yi Wang, Zhiyu will take her leave now."

---

Shen Jianchen nodded to Miao Zhiyu and asked Chen Haoyu: "Has Young Master Chen seen Wenyuan?"

---

"Of course I have. He's at the Wangjing Pavilion on the north side with a group of talented young men. Zhu Shuzhen is sitting there looking bored, drinking his sorrows away. It's not far from here, just that way." Chen Haoyu pointed in a direction. "Your Highness, just follow this way and you'll see it soon enough."

---

"Many thanks!" After thanking him, Shen Jianchen swiftly headed in the direction Chen Haoyu had pointed.

---

Someone asked: "Young Master Chen, could Yi Wang be the Military Commissioner sent by the Emperor?"

---

Chen Haoyu replied: "That's what I hear from my sister."

---

Another person said: "Why is Young Master Chen so well-behaved when meeting Yi Wang? You even gave him directions?"

---

"He's a prince, alright? No matter how wild I am, I don't dare mess with a prince. You guys better show some respect from now on. Alright, let's head back." Chen Haoyu and his group boarded their carriage and left West Lake.

---

Miao Zhiyu shared a carriage with a young lady. The young lady asked in surprise: "That person was actually Yi Wang? When I heard you address him just now, I was so scared I didn't even dare get off the carriage to greet him."

---

"I only learned from my father that he was Yi Wang. The first time I met him, I had no idea of his identity. Luckily, I didn't offend a noble."

---

"I used to hear that Yi Wang was always bedridden. When you were talking to him just now, I took a few secret glances from the carriage. He doesn't look like someone who's ever been sick at all."

---

"Illnesses can always be cured. He must have recovered."

---

"That's true. Now that Yi Wang has recovered and is favored by the Emperor, with such a powerful tree to lean on, everyone in Lin'an will surely take the Qiao family even more seriously from now on."

---

Miao Zhiyu acknowledged with a smile.

---

The Qiao family originally had ancestors who served as officials in the court. Later, not a single one rose to high office. The younger generations relied on the blessings of their forebears, and their lives could still be described as "wealthy and noble." It wasn't until their eldest daughter caught the Emperor's eye and became a Noble Consort, and later gave birth to Yi Wang, that the Qiao family began to hold their heads high again.

---

Later, when Noble Consort Qiao passed away and rumors spread that Yi Wang was sickly and wouldn't live long, some people stopped taking the Qiao family seriously again. Now Yi Wang appeared before everyone in good health and had been appointed Military Commissioner of Lin'an by the Emperor. All the officials in Lin'an, including the prefect himself, would give face to the Qiao family.

---

Thinking of this, Miao Zhiyu worried that the Qiao family, out of regard for the Lin'an prefect, might overlook the divorce and accept the Youqi Jushi. If that happened, she wouldn't have the slightest hope left.

---
