# Chapter 8: Three Rules to Set My Husband Free

After dawn broke, the morning air drifted through the window, pure and sweet, without the smell of concrete or car exhaust. Qianli Xun kicked off the blanket, breathing in the fresh air as she slept, calling out: "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!..."

Kuqiao heard the voice, lifted the bed curtain, and asked softly, "Miss, what did you say?"

"Shen Jianchen!" Qianli Xun didn't open her eyes, still asleep.

Kuqiao muttered, "Who is Shen Jianchen? How come I've never heard Miss mention him?"

She tucked the blanket back over her miss, drew the bed curtain closed, sat down by the bed, and began to embroider, waiting patiently for her to wake.

Kuqiao had just finished embroidering a leaf when Qianli Xun opened her eyes and sat up. Looking at the bed and curtains, she felt like she was in a different world: "Right, I've transmigrated. I thought I was still in my own time."

Hearing the sound, Kuqiao stopped embroidering, put everything into the bamboo basket and set it aside, got up, and lifted the bed curtain: "Miss, you're awake?" She drew the curtain aside as she spoke.

Seeing Kuqiao, Qianli Xun felt even more real that she had become Zhu Shuzhen: "What time is it?"

Kuqiao thought for a moment and asked, "Is Miss asking what hour it is?"

Qianli Xun smiled bitterly. Different times, different ways to say the same thing. At least they could still communicate: "Yes."

"It's not yet the Si Hour." Kuqiao brought over clothes for her miss.

Qianli Xun had written web novels about ancient times and studied the ancient time system, which meant it wasn't yet nine o'clock. She got out of bed and let Kuqiao dress her.

"Miss, the master was quite well-behaved last night. I didn't hear a single sound."

"I gagged him, so of course he was well-behaved."

"Ah! How could Miss gag the master? Aren't you afraid he'll cast you out?"

"Hmph! I'm not afraid at all. Yesterday I even asked him to divorce me, but he refused and even gave me a good idea." Qianli Xun looked quite pleased.

Kuqiao had finished dressing her miss and immediately clasped her hands in prayer: "Amitabha! Amitabha! Thank goodness the master didn't agree, otherwise Miss would have been ruined!"

Qianli Xun looked at Kuqiao's worried, silly expression and laughed, "Look how scared you are. It's just being cast out, what's the big deal?"

Kuqiao said worriedly, "Looking at it this way, you really aren't the previous Miss. She would never make such a request to the master."

"I was never her in the first place."

"But I only have you as my Miss. Please, let me beg you, don't let the master cast you out."

Seeing Kuqiao's pitiful, nervous expression, Qianli Xun asked, "Tell me, what's so bad about a woman being cast out? Why are you so worried?"

Kuqiao said immediately, "A woman cast out by her husband will be considered to be of bad character. It will be hard for her to remarry, and it will shame her paternal family. If there are other unmarried sisters in the family, it will affect their reputation too. I'm afraid no one would dare come to propose marriage. So to protect the purity of the other girls, the paternal family would never let a daughter cast out by her husband back into the home."

It really was serious. Qianli Xun couldn't help asking, "Do I have any other sisters?"

"No, there aren't. The Zhu residence only has you as the young lady. You have an older brother."

"Then I'm relieved. Even if Lin Jue casts me out, I won't worry about affecting anyone else."

"No way. If Miss is cast out by the master, you probably won't even be able to enter the gates of the Zhu residence. Your father is a man who values reputation above all else. He would never allow his children to do anything that tarnishes the family reputation."

Kuqiao led her miss to sit in front of the bronze mirror and began to tidy her appearance.

Qianli Xun carefully watched Kuqiao help her style her hair through the bronze mirror: "Don't worry. Lin Jue won't cast me out. He gave me the idea of a mutual divorce."

"Then the master isn't so bad. A mutual divorce is the best choice. In our village, Aunt Yao agreed to a mutual divorce with her former husband. Her paternal family not only let her back in, but later found someone for her to marry. I heard her family is living happily now."

"But I feel Lin Jue isn't that kind-hearted. There might be some dirty tricks in store for me."

Kuqiao continued to tidy her miss's hair and put in hair ornaments: "The master really wasn't good to Miss before. Miss and the master were originally meant for different paths, but were forced onto the same one. Not only did you suffer, but others watching also suffered."

"So I must leave him. It's good for him, and good for me too."

Qianli Xun decided she must leave Lin Jue.

From Qianli Xun's perspective, she had never even had a proper boyfriend. After transmigrating to the Great Song and becoming someone else, she entered a marriage for the first time ever, only to find her husband was a scumbag who beat his wife and even openly brought courtesans home. If she didn't divorce such a person, even heaven wouldn't stand for it!

From Zhu Shuzhen's perspective, she and Lin Jue had different goals and incompatible lifestyles, with no common hobbies or interests. It was like a beautiful flower planted in cow dung—so discordant, and this flower would sooner or later be killed by the stench. Since Qianli Xun was fortunate enough to become Zhu Shuzhen, she had an obligation to help her escape this marriage. Besides, helping her was helping herself.

Just as she was thinking, Kuqiao asked, "Miss, you called out a name in your sleep. It seemed to be Shen Jianchen. Who is he?"

Qianli Xun had no need to hide it from Kuqiao: "He's a friend who died with me in the earthquake. I probably dreamed of the earthquake scene in my sleep and couldn't help calling out his name."

"Is he someone Miss liked in another era?"

"Hehe! If I liked him, pigs would fly. Every time he sees me, he has to insult me a few times or he's not satisfied, and he calls it 'pursuing me.' It's really funny!"

After speaking, Qianli Xun felt inexplicably sad: "Now I don't even have a chance to let him insult me to my face. I've become Zhu Shuzhen. I don't know how he'll react. Maybe I'll never see him again."

As the two chatted, Kuqiao had finished dressing her miss: "Miss, look. How is it? Are you happy with it?"

Qianli Xun glanced at the mirror again: "You're a clever and skilled maid. You even know how to style your miss most beautifully. Not bad, I'm very satisfied."

After Qianli Xun finished getting ready and eating breakfast, she decided to go see Lin Jue. He probably didn't have an easy night. It was better to let him go quickly.

She pushed open Lin Jue's room and walked in.

Hearing the sound, Lin Jue quickly sat up. His mouth was stuffed with cloth and he couldn't speak. His eyes clearly demanded that Zhu Shuzhen release him as soon as possible.

"Don't glare at me. I came here to untie you." As she spoke, Qianli Xun went over and pulled the cloth from his mouth.

Lin Jue blurted out, "You vicious woman!"

Qianli Xun untied him as she spoke: "If I were truly vicious, you wouldn't survive past today. Be smarter. You can't beat me, so from now on, listen to me and agree to a peaceful mutual divorce. From then on, we'll each mind our own business. You live your life, I'll live mine. When we meet in the future, we can still greet each other. Isn't that good? My father is an important figure in Lin'an, with a higher official rank than yours. If you treat me well, he definitely won't treat you badly."

After Qianli Xun finished her chatter, she had just finished untying the ropes. Thinking that once the ropes were loose, Lin Jue might attack, she had prepared herself. But she found Lin Jue made no move to attack. Instead, he stared at her, his gaze much gentler than before.

"What are you looking at me for?"

"Since you married me, all the words you've said to me combined don't come close to what you said from yesterday to today. If you had been like this before, I wouldn't have beaten or scolded you."

Qianli Xun suddenly felt Lin Jue was also quite pitiable, having married a wife who gave him the silent treatment: "So we're not suitable. Together, you suffer, and I'm not happy either."

Lin Jue got off the bed and stretched his arms and legs: "I'll give you three days. As long as you can invite your parents and the Zhu family elders in three days, I will definitely let you leave the Lin family."

Qianli Xun said excitedly, "It's a deal! But you have to tell me, how many elders do you plan to invite? My side must have at least as many as yours. Both sides must be evenly matched, so when negotiating, neither side will overwhelm the other. Only then will it be fair."

"Don't worry. However many you invite, I'll invite the same number. If you can't invite even one, then my dear wife, don't think about mutual divorce anymore."

Hearing this, Qianli Xun said warily, "Do you have some kind of scheme?"

Lin Jue stared at Zhu Shuzhen. Though she wasn't the same person as before, her appearance remained the same. Just one look and he couldn't bear to send her into another man's arms: "I was the one who suggested mutual divorce. What scheme could there be?"

"That's true too!" Qianli Xun thought she still had to stay at the Lin residence for some time and needed to set three rules with him. Looking at Lin Jue's back as he walked out, she called loudly, "Hey!"

Lin Jue stopped and turned around: "What else?"

"I need to stay at the Lin residence for some time. I have to make some requests."

"I'm all ears."

Lin Jue actually liked hearing Zhu Shuzhen speak. Even if it was all nonsense, he was happy to listen. He could only blame himself—before, it was hard to even hear her say a word.

"First, you're not allowed to enter the Zhulan Residence. You've already felt my fists."

Lin Jue didn't have good impressions of the Zhulan Residence. All it left him with was coldness and neglect. Even though he'd entered a few times, besides beating Zhu Shuzhen, he couldn't remember what he'd done there. Not entering was fine too. He nodded.

"Second, you're not allowed to bring merchant courtesans back to the Lin residence. If you want to have fun, go to the Qinglou."

"Fine."

"Third, before the mutual divorce, you have to give me silver to spend."

"You're still the mistress of the house. Of course you have the right to spend silver."

"I didn't expect you to be so straightforward. My good impression of you has grown even better."

After Lin Jue left, Qianli Xun prepared to work on the mutual divorce matter. It seemed she had to make a trip to Zhu Shuzhen's paternal home. She knew nothing about that family and planned to first ask Kuqiao about the situation before making plans.

Three days was too short. To have a new life in this unfamiliar era, she decided to give it her best shot.
