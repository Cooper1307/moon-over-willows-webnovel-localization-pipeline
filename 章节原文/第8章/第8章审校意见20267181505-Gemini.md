整体来看，第八章的翻译质量依然保持了很高的水准！你对现代口语和古代设定的转换把握得非常到位，尤其是像**“猪都能上树”（pigs would fly）**这种俚语的本土化处理，非常地道，能让海外读者立刻Get到笑点。

不过，针对你提出的几个审校维度，这章在**“名词指代歧义”、“长句精简”以及“休妻 vs 和离的文化差异表达”**上，还有进一步优化的空间。

以下是具体的审校意见与优化建议：

### 1. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P14 词汇不够生动**：`I gagged him. Of course he was obedient.`
    *   **问题**：obedient 略显书面。中文里的“老实”，在这里是说他被制服了，不敢乱动。
    *   **修改**：I shoved a gag in his mouth. Of course he was **well-behaved**. 
*   **P23 / P29 核心文化概念混淆**：“休了（被休）”与“和离”
    *   **问题**：原文中，苦荞极度害怕小姐被“休”（单方面扫地出门/休妻），而女主觉得“和离”（协议离婚）挺好。目前的翻译都用了 **divorce**，这会让外国读者一头雾水：“既然都是 divorce，为什么苦荞前面吓得要死，后面又觉得可以接受？”
    *   **修改**：必须在词汇上做出区分。
        *   “被休”译为：**cast out / repudiated / unilaterally divorced**（带有强烈的贬义和弃妇意味）。
        *   “和离”译为：**amicable divorce / mutual separation**。
*   **P27 致命的称呼歧义**：`the master rejects you... Your father values reputation...`
    *   **问题**：在这段对话中，Kuqiao 嘴里的 `the master` 前面指林觉（姑爷），到了 P27 突然又指朱淑真的父亲（老爷）。外国读者绝对会看懵。
    *   **修改**：需要把身份翻译明白。指代林觉用 `the young master` 或直接说 `your husband`；指代朱父时，需要明确加上 `Your father, the Old Master, values...`
*   **P49 “嘚啵嘚啵”的生动翻译**：`As Qianli Xun finished her chatter...`
    *   **优化**："chatter" 一般指闲聊。这里女主是一通连珠炮式的输出/谈判，用 **spiel** 或 **rant** 更契合网文女主飒爽的语境：`Just as Qianli Xun finished her spiel...`
*   **P70 过于直译**：`I like you even more now.`
    *   **问题**：原文是“我对你的好印象又增加一分”，翻译成 I like you 容易引起感情线上的误会（林觉可能会误会女主喜欢他）。
    *   **修改**：**"My opinion of you just went up a point."** （更带有一点现代女主的高高在上和调侃味道）。

### 2. 句子是否过长？表达是否过于重复？

*   **P35 和 P36 的吐槽太长**：
    *   这两段是女主内心戏的爆发，原文稍微有点长，翻译时的定语从句也偏多。为了体现网文女主“快准狠”的吐槽风格，可以将长句切断。
    *   比如 P36 中文是“一朵鲜花插在牛粪上”，你的翻译 `a beautiful flower planted in cow dung` 已经很棒了，可以稍微精简得更有画面感：`like sticking a fresh flower into cow dung`。
*   **P52 林觉的台词太啰嗦**：`Since you married me, all the words you've said to me combined don't come close to what you said from yesterday to today.`
    *   **优化**：符合网文节奏的精简版：**"You've spoken more words to me since yesterday than you have in our entire marriage."** （简练且扎心）。

### 3. 标点使用是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **P16 和 P20 标点错误**：
    `"Hmph! I'm not afraid at all..." Qianli Xun looked quite pleased.`
    在英文中，如果引号后面跟着的是**说话人的动作/神态**（如 looked pleased），不是 said/asked，那么引号里面的最后一定要用**句号/问号/感叹号**，且后面的字母大写。（这里你用对了感叹号，但某些地方的逗号需要留意）。

### 🌟 综合精修版英文参考 (Polished Version)

以下是经过润色、修正了称呼歧义，并强化了女主“现代大女主”语气的精修版，极具网文爽感：

**(P1-12: Waking Up)**
After dawn broke, morning air drifted through the window—pure and sweet, untainted by the smell of concrete or car exhaust. Qianli Xun kicked off the blanket. Breathing in the fresh air in her sleep, she mumbled, "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!..."
Hearing her stir, Kuqiao parted the bed curtains and asked softly, "Miss, what did you say?"
"Shen Jianchen!" Qianli Xun’s eyes remained shut, still fast asleep.
Kuqiao muttered to herself, "Who is Shen Jianchen? How come I've never heard Miss mention him before?"
She tucked the blanket back over her miss and drew the curtains closed. Then, sitting down by the bed, she began to embroider, waiting patiently for her to wake.
Kuqiao had just finished stitching a leaf when Qianli Xun opened her eyes and sat up. Staring at the antique bed and its canopy, she felt a surreal disconnect. "Right, I've transmigrated. For a second, I thought I was still Qianli Xun."
Hearing her voice, Kuqiao stopped her needlework. She packed everything into a bamboo basket, set it aside, and stood up to pull back the bed curtains. "Miss, you're awake?" 
Seeing Kuqiao snapped her fully back to reality—she truly had become Zhu Shuzhen. "What time is it?"
Kuqiao thought for a moment. "Is Miss asking what hour it is?"
Qianli Xun smiled bitterly. Different times, different ways of saying the same thing. At least they could still communicate. "Yes."
"It is not yet the Hour of Si." Kuqiao brought over a fresh set of clothes.
Qianli Xun had written ancient-setting web novels and studied their timekeeping. That meant it wasn't even nine o'clock in the morning yet. She got out of bed, letting Kuqiao dress her.

**(P13-36: Discussing Divorce)**
"Miss, the young master was quite well-behaved last night. I didn't hear a peep."
"I shoved a gag in his mouth. Of course he was well-behaved."
"Ah! How could you gag your husband? Aren't you afraid he'll cast you out and write a letter of divorce?"
"Hmph! I'm not afraid at all. Yesterday, I practically begged him to divorce me, but he refused. He even gave me a brilliant idea instead." Qianli Xun looked quite pleased with herself.
Having finished dressing her miss, Kuqiao immediately clasped her hands in prayer. "Amitabha! Amitabha! Thank goodness he didn't agree, otherwise you would be doomed!"
Looking at Kuqiao's ridiculously worried expression, Qianli Xun laughed. "Look how scared you are. So what if I get divorced? What's the big deal?"
Kuqiao said fretfully, "You really don't sound like the old Miss. She would never have dared to make such a request to her husband."
"I was never her to begin with," Qianli Xun murmured.
"But you are the only Miss I have! I beg you, please don't let the young master repudiate you."
Seeing Kuqiao's pitiful state, Qianli Xun asked, "Tell me, what's so terrible about a woman being cast out? Why are you this terrified?"
"A woman thrown out by her husband is seen as lacking in morals!" Kuqiao explained urgently. "Remarrying would be near impossible, and it would bring shame to her maiden home. If she had unmarried sisters, their reputations would be ruined, and no one would dare propose to them. To protect the other girls' purity, families absolutely never let a cast-out daughter step foot back in the house."
That was indeed serious. Qianli Xun couldn't help asking, "Do I have any other sisters?"
"No, the Zhu residence only has you as the sole daughter. You just have an older brother."
"Then I'm relieved. Even if Lin Jue kicks me out, I won't have to worry about dragging anyone else down."
"No, Miss! If you are cast out, you probably won't even be allowed through the gates of the Zhu estate. Your father, the Old Master, values his reputation above all else. He would never allow his children to tarnish the family name."
Kuqiao guided her miss to the bronze mirror, helped her sit down, and began styling her hair.
Watching Kuqiao carefully pin up her tresses in the mirror, Qianli Xun reassured her, "Don't worry. Lin Jue isn't going to cast me out. He gave me the idea of an amicable mutual divorce."
"Oh! Then the young master isn't so bad," Kuqiao breathed a sigh of relief. "A mutual divorce is the best option. Back in my village, Auntie Yao had an amicable separation from her husband. Her family not only welcomed her back but later found her a new match. I heard their new family is living very happily now."
"Still, I feel Lin Jue isn't that kind-hearted. He might have some dirty trick up his sleeve."
Kuqiao continued adorning her miss's hair with ornaments. "The young master really treated you poorly before. You two were meant for completely different paths but were forced onto the same one. Not only did you suffer, but it was painful for others to watch."
"Exactly. So I must leave him. It's good for him, and good for me."
Qianli Xun solidified her resolve to leave Lin Jue.
From her modern perspective, she had never even had a proper boyfriend. Transmigrating to the Great Song Dynasty, her very first experience with marriage was to a wife-beating scumbag who openly brought brothel girls home. Not divorcing a trashy man like that would be a crime against heaven!
From Zhu Shuzhen's perspective, she and Lin Jue shared zero common ground—no shared ideals, no shared hobbies. It was like sticking a fresh flower into cow dung. Not only was it visually offensive, but the flower would eventually die from the stench. Since Qianli Xun had the fortune of taking over this body, she had a moral obligation to help Zhu Shuzhen escape this nightmare. Besides, helping Zhu Shuzhen was helping herself.

**(P37-53: The Friend and Releasing Lin Jue)**
Just then, Kuqiao asked, "Miss, you called out a name in your sleep. It sounded like Shen Jianchen. Who is he?"
There was no need to hide it. "He's a friend who died with me in the earthquake. I was probably dreaming about the disaster and called out his name subconsciously."
"Is he someone you liked in... your other era?"
"Hehe! If I liked him, pigs would fly. Every time that guy saw me, he couldn't resist throwing a few insults my way. He actually called that 'pursuing me.' Utterly ridiculous!"
Yet, after saying this, a wave of inexplicable sadness washed over her. "Now, even if I wanted him to insult me to my face, I don't have the chance. I've become Zhu Shuzhen. I don't know what happened to him... Maybe I'll never see him again."
As they chatted, Kuqiao finished the final touches. "Miss, look. How is it? Are you pleased?"
Qianli Xun glanced in the mirror. "You've got skilled hands, and you know exactly how to bring out my best features. Not bad at all. I'm very satisfied."
After washing up and having breakfast, Qianli Xun decided to check on Lin Jue. He probably hadn't had an easy night. It was time to let him go.
She pushed open the door to his room and walked in.
Hearing her enter, Lin Jue immediately sat up. With the cloth still jammed in his mouth, he couldn't speak, but his glaring eyes clearly demanded his immediate release.
"Stop glaring at me. I'm here to untie you." Qianli Xun walked over and yanked the gag from his mouth.
Lin Jue gasped for air and blurted out, "You vicious woman!"
Qianli Xun began loosening his ropes. "If I were truly vicious, you wouldn't have lived to see today. Be smart. You can't beat me in a fight, so from now on, you will do as I say and agree to a peaceful, mutual divorce. After that, we'll cross the bridge and go our separate ways. You live your life, I live mine. If we bump into each other on the street, we can just nod. Isn't that nice? My father is a prominent figure in Lin'an with a higher rank than you. If you cooperate, he definitely won't make things hard for you."
Just as Qianli Xun finished her spiel, the last rope fell away. She braced herself, anticipating that Lin Jue might lunge at her the moment he was free. 
However, he did no such thing. Instead, he just sat there staring at her, the hostility in his eyes significantly softer than before.
"What are you looking at me for?"
"You've spoken more words to me since yesterday than you have in our entire marriage. If you had been like this from the start, I never would have hit or scolded you."
Qianli Xun suddenly felt a tinge of pity for him. He had married a wife who constantly gave him the cold shoulder. "Which proves we aren't suitable. Being together makes you miserable, and it makes me miserable too."

**(P54-72: The Three Rules)**
Lin Jue got off the bed and stretched his stiff limbs. "I will give you three days. As long as you can invite your parents and the Zhu family elders here within three days, I will gladly let you leave the Lin household."
"It's a deal!" Qianli Xun said excitedly. "But you have to tell me, how many elders do you plan to invite? My side needs to match yours in numbers. Both sides must be evenly matched so that neither party can overpower the other during negotiations. Only then is it fair."
"Don't worry. However many you bring, I will match. But if you fail to bring even one... then my dear wife, do not speak of mutual divorce again."
Hearing this, Qianli Xun’s eyes narrowed warily. "Are you plotting something?"
Lin Jue stared at her. Though the soul inside seemed different, the beautiful face remained the same. Just looking at her, he couldn't bear the thought of sending her into another man's arms. "I'm the one who suggested the mutual divorce. What scheme could there possibly be?"
"Fair point!" Qianli Xun thought to herself. Still, she had to stay in the Lin residence for a while longer and needed to establish some ground rules. Watching his retreating back, she shouted, "Hey!"
Lin Jue stopped and turned around. "What else?"
"Since I have to stay here for a bit, I have a few demands."
"I'm all ears."
Strangely, Lin Jue realized he actually enjoyed hearing her speak. Even if she spouted nonsense, he was willing to listen. Before this, getting a single word out of her had been a struggle.
"Rule number one: You are not allowed to step foot in the Zhulan Residence. You've already tasted my fists."
Lin Jue had no fond memories of the Zhulan Residence anyway. It was a place of coldness and neglect. He couldn't even remember what he usually did there, aside from lashing out at her. Keeping out was perfectly fine with him. He nodded.
"Rule number two: No bringing courtesans back to the estate. If you want to mess around, go to the brothel."
"Acceptable."
"Rule number three: Until the divorce is finalized, you must provide me with silver to spend."
"You are still the mistress of this house. Naturally, you have the right to manage the funds."
"I didn't expect you to be so straightforward," Qianli Xun raised an eyebrow. "My opinion of you just went up a point."
After Lin Jue left, Qianli Xun began preparing for the divorce. It seemed a trip to the Zhu family estate was inevitable. She knew absolutely nothing about her maiden home and planned to pump Kuqiao for information before making her next move.
Three days was a tight deadline. But for the sake of starting a brand new life in this unfamiliar era, she was ready to give it everything she had.
