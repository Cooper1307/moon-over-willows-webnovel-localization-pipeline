## Chapter 8: Chapter 8: Three Rules to Free Her Husband

天放亮后，清晨的气息从窗户传入房中，纯净而甘甜，没有掺杂混凝土和汽车尾气的味道。千里寻把被子踢开，在梦中嗅着清新的空气，口中唤着：“申简辰！申简辰！……”
Day broke, and morning air drifted through the window—crisp and sweet, untainted by concrete dust or car exhaust. Kicking off her quilt, Qianli Xun breathed it in through her sleep and murmured, "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!..."

苦荞听到声音，掀开床幔，轻声问道：“小姐，您说什么？”
Hearing her, Kuqiao parted the bed curtains and asked in a hushed voice, "Miss, what did you say?"

“申简辰！”千里寻并未睁眼，依然睡着。
"Shen Jianchen!" Eyes still closed, Qianli Xun slept on.

苦荞嘀咕：“申简辰是谁？我怎么从来没听小姐提起过？”
Kuqiao muttered under her breath, "Who's Shen Jianchen? Never heard Miss mention that name before."

她替小姐把被子盖好，继续放好床幔，坐在床边开始绣花，耐心等待小姐醒来。
She tucked the quilt back over her mistress, dropped the curtains, and sat by the bed working on embroidery while waiting for her to wake.

苦荞刚绣完一片叶子，千里寻睁开眼，坐起来，瞧着床铺和床幔，恍若隔世：“对，我穿越了，还以为自己依然是千里寻。”
Kuqiao had just finished stitching a single leaf when Qianli Xun opened her eyes, sat up, and stared at the bed frame and curtains in dazed disorientation. Right—I transmigrated. For a second I thought I was still Qianli Xun.

苦荞听到声音，停下绣花，把所有东西放入收纳的竹篮中，搁到一边，起身掀开床幔：“小姐，您醒了？”说着边把床幔挂好。
Hearing movement, Kuqiao put down her needlework, stowed everything in a woven basket, set it aside, and pulled back the curtains. "Awake, Miss?" She hooked the drapes up neatly.

千里寻看到苦荞，更清楚自己已成为朱淑真：“现在几点了？”
Seeing Kuqiao drove home the fact that she was Zhu Shuzhen now. "What time is it?"

苦荞想了一会儿，问道：“小姐是问现在是什么时辰吗？”
Kuqiao paused to think. "Are you asking what hour it is, Miss?"

千里寻苦笑了一下，时代不同，人们对同样事物的表达不同，还好，依然能交流：“是的。”
Qianli Xun gave a wry smile—different eras, different terms, at least they still understood each other. "Yes."

“还未到巳时。”苦荞边说边给小姐拿来衣裳。
"Not yet the Hour of the Snake," Kuqiao replied, fetching her clothes.

千里寻写过关于古代的网络小说，研究过时辰，说明现在还不到九点。她走下床，任由苦荞为她穿好衣裳。
Having researched ancient timekeeping for her web novels, Qianli Xun knew that meant not yet nine in the morning. She climbed out of bed and let Kuqiao dress her.

“小姐，昨晚上姑爷挺老实，没有听到任何声音。”
"Miss, Son-in-law stayed real quiet last night—didn't hear a peep out of him."

“我把他的嘴堵住，当然老实。”
"I stuffed a gag in his mouth. Of course he stayed quiet."

“啊！小姐怎么能把姑爷的嘴堵住？您就不怕姑爷休了您？”
"Ah! Miss, how could you gag Son-in-law? Aren't you afraid he'll cast you out?"

“哼！我才不怕，昨天我还亲自让他把我休了，结果他不愿意，还给我出了一个好主意。”千里寻显得很得意。
"Hmph! Afraid of what? Yesterday I told him to his face to write me a bill of divorce! He wouldn't do it—and gave me a better idea instead," Qianli Xun bragged.

苦荞已为小姐穿好衣裳，立刻合掌：“阿弥陀佛！阿弥陀佛！多亏姑爷没答应，否则小姐可就惨了！”
Finished dressing her, Kuqiao pressed her palms together at once. "Amitabha! Amitabha! Thank goodness Son-in-law refused, or you'd be in terrible trouble, Miss!"

千里寻瞅着苦荞担忧的傻样，笑道：“看把你吓的，不就是被休吗？有什么了不起的？”
Amused by Kuqiao's panicked face, Qianli Xun laughed. "Look how scared you are. What's the big deal about being cast out?"

苦荞愁道：“这么看来，您确实不是以前的小姐，她绝对不会向姑爷提出这个要求。”
Kuqiao sighed in worry. "Hearing that, you really aren't the old Miss. She would never ask Son-in-law for that."

“我本来就不是她。”
"I told you I'm not her."

“可我只有您这一位小姐，就当苦荞求您，千万不要让姑爷休了您。”
"Still, you're the only Miss I have. Consider this Kuqiao begging you—never ask Son-in-law to cast you out!"

瞧着苦荞可怜紧张的样子，千里寻问道：“你倒是说说女子被休有什么不好？为何担心成这样儿？”
Seeing her genuine distress, Qianli Xun asked, "Tell me then—what's so terrible about a woman getting repudiated? Why are you this worried?"

苦荞立刻说：“被夫君休了的女子会被大家认为品行不端，以后再要嫁人都难，而且会让娘家蒙羞，如果娘家还有其他未出阁的姐妹，也会影响到她们的名声，只怕没有人敢再上门提亲，所以娘家为保住其他姑娘的清白，绝对不会让被夫家休了的女儿进门。”
Kuqiao rattled off at once, "Everyone assumes a cast-out wife committed some disgraceful misconduct. Remarrying becomes next to impossible, and it shames her maiden family! If she has unmarried sisters at home, their reputations get ruined too and nobody dares propose. To protect the other daughters' honor, maiden families won't even let a cast-out daughter back through the door!"

确实挺严重，千里寻不由问道：“我可否还有其他姐妹？”
Pretty harsh indeed. Qianli Xun asked, "Do I have any sisters?"

“那倒没有，朱府只小姐一位千金，您上面有位哥哥。”
"No, you're the only daughter of Zhu Manor. You have an older brother."

“那我就放心，即使被林觉休了，也不会担心连累他人。”
"Then I'm in the clear—even if Lin Jue casts me out, I won't drag anyone else down."

“不行，小姐要是被姑爷休了，估计连朱府的门都进不了，老爷是位很看重清誉之人，他绝对不会允许子女做出有辱门楣之事。”
"No, Miss! If Son-in-law casts you out, you probably won't even be allowed inside Zhu Manor! Master values family honor above everything—he'd never tolerate his children bringing disgrace to the household!"

苦荞把小姐拉到铜镜前坐下，开始帮她整理妆容。
Kuqiao guided her to sit before the bronze mirror and began fixing her hair.

千里寻透过铜镜仔细观察苦荞帮她整理发髻：“你放心，林觉不会休我，他给我出了和离的主意。”
Watching Kuqiao coil her bun in the mirror, Qianli Xun said, "Relax. Lin Jue isn't casting me out—he suggested mutual divorce instead."

“这么说来，姑爷还不错，和离是最好的选择，我们村有位姚婶婶当年跟以前的夫君和离，娘家不仅允许她进门，后来还找人嫁了，听说现在一家人日子过得和和美美。”
"Then Son-in-law isn't half bad! Mutual divorce is the best path. Back in our village, Aunt Yao got a mutual divorce from her first husband. Her maiden family welcomed her right back, she remarried later, and folks say her whole family lives happy as can be!"

“可我总觉得林觉没那么好心，说不定有什么幺蛾子等着我。”
"Still, I doubt Lin Jue is acting out of kindness. He's probably cooking up some dirty trick."

苦荞继续给小姐整理头发，装点发饰：“姑爷以前对小姐确实不好，小姐和姑爷本是两条道上的人，偏偏给拉到一条道上，不仅自己难受，别人看着也难受。”
Pinning ornaments into her hair, Kuqiao sighed. "Son-in-law truly treated you poorly before. You and he belong on two different roads—forcing you onto the same path makes you miserable and hurts to watch."

“所以，我一定要离开他，既对他好，也对我好。”
"Which is why I have to leave him. Better for him, better for me."

千里寻决定必须离开林觉。
Qianli Xun's mind was made up: she was divorcing Lin Jue.

从千里寻的角度来讲，她连正经男朋友都没有过，穿越到大宋，成为别人，先破天荒地进入到一段婚姻中，丈夫竟然还是打老婆的渣男，甚至公然将青楼女子带到家里，这种人不跟他离婚，天理难容！
From Qianli Xun's side, she had never even had a proper boyfriend—transmigrating into the Song dynasty only to wake up married to a wife-beating scumbag who hauled courtesans into the house? Not divorcing him would defy Heaven itself!

从朱淑真角度来讲，她跟林觉志不同道不合，没有共同爱好和情趣，就像一朵鲜花插在牛粪上，如此不协调，而且这朵鲜花迟早会被牛粪熏死。既然千里寻有幸成为朱淑真，有义务帮助她摆脱这段婚姻，再说帮她就是帮自己。
From Zhu Shuzhen's side, she and Lin Jue shared zero ideals or tastes—like a fresh flower stuck in cow dung, mismatched to the bone, bound to wither from the stench. Since Qianli Xun had inherited her life, she owed it to Zhu Shuzhen to break free—and helping her meant helping herself.

正想着，苦荞问道：“小姐，您说梦话时叫出一个人名，好像是申简辰，他是谁？”
Mid-thought, Kuqiao spoke up. "Miss, you called out a name in your sleep—sounded like Shen Jianchen. Who is he?"

千里寻没有必要瞒着苦荞：“他是跟我一起在地震中死亡的朋友，估计是睡着时梦到地震的场景，不由自主喊出他的名字。”
Seeing no reason to hide it, Qianli Xun said, "A friend who died in the earthquake with me. I must have dreamed of the quake and called his name."

“他是不是小姐在另一个时代喜欢的人？”
"Was he someone you loved back in your era?"

“呵呵！我要是喜欢他，估计猪都能上树，这个人每次见到我，不损我几句，就不舒服，还美其名曰追求我，真是搞笑！”
"Ha! Me love him? Only when pigs climb trees! Every time that guy saw me, he couldn't rest until he got a few digs in, and he had the nerve to call that pursuing me—hilarious!"

千里寻说完，又莫名悲伤：“现在想让他当面损我，都没有机会，我成了朱淑真，他不知道会怎样，也许永远也见不到他。”
A sudden ache tightened her chest as she added, "Now I don't even have the chance to hear him tease me to my face. I ended up as Zhu Shuzhen—no telling what happened to him. I might never see him again."

两人聊着，苦荞已为小姐装扮完毕：“小姐，您看，怎么样？满意吗？”
While they talked, Kuqiao finished her hair. "Look, Miss—how is it? Do you like it?"

千里寻再向镜中瞅一眼：“你这丫鬟心灵手巧，还知道你家小姐怎么装扮最美，不错，我很满意。”
Qianli Xun glanced in the mirror. "Clever hands—you know just how to make your Miss shine. Very nice, I love it."

千里寻梳洗完毕，用过早饭，决定去看看林觉，估计这一晚上他不怎么好过，还是赶紧把人给放了。
After washing up and eating breakfast, Qianli Xun went to check on Lin Jue. He must have had a rough night; time to turn him loose.

她推开关押林觉的房门，走进去。
She pushed open the door of his room and stepped inside.

林觉听到有声音，迅速坐起来，嘴里塞着布子，不能说话，那眼神，明显是命令朱淑真尽快放了他。
At the sound, Lin Jue sat straight up. Gagged and unable to speak, his glare practically ordered her to untie him on the spot.

“你别拿眼睛瞪我，我过来就是给你松绑。”说着，千里寻过去，拔掉林觉嘴里的布子。
"Stop glaring—I came to let you go." Stepping up, Qianli Xun yanked the rag from his mouth.

林觉脱口而出：“你这恶毒的女人！”
"You wicked woman!" Lin Jue burst out.

千里寻边松绑边说：“我要是真恶毒，你都活不过今天，还是聪明点儿。你呢，打不过我，以后好好听我差遣，跟我把婚和和美美地离了，咱们以后井水不犯河水，你过你的日子，我过我的日子，以后见了面还能打个招呼，不也挺好？我爹在临安也是有头有脸的人物，官职也比你大，你对我好，他肯定不会亏待你。”
Untying the knots, Qianli Xun shot back, "If I were wicked, you wouldn't have lived to see sunrise. Smarten up. You can't beat me in a fight, so behave yourself, go through with a clean mutual divorce, and after that we stay out of each other's hair. You live your life, I live mine, and if we bump into each other later we can even say hello—isn't that nice? My father's a man of standing in Lin'an and outranks you. Treat me right and he won't shortchange you."

千里寻嘚啵嘚啵完，绳子也正好解完。想着绳子松开，说不定林觉会袭击而来，她已做好防备，却发现林觉并未如此做，而是盯着她看，眼神比刚才缓和许多。
Just as her lecture wrapped up, the last knot came free. Half expecting Lin Jue to swing at her the second he got loose, she braced herself—only to find him staring at her with a far softer look.

“你看我干嘛？”
"What are you staring at?"

“自从你嫁给我，跟我说过的话加起来，都没有昨天到今天说的多，如果你以前像这样，我也不会打你骂你。”
"Since the day you married me, every word you spoke to me combined doesn't match what you've said since yesterday. If you'd talked to me like this before, I never would've hit or cursed you."

千里寻突然觉得林觉也挺可怜，娶一位对她实施冷暴力的妻子：“所以我们不合适，在一起你难受，我也不好受。”
A pang of pity flickered in Qianli Xun—stuck with a wife who gave him nothing but cold violence. "All the more reason we're wrong for each other. Staying together makes both of us miserable."

林觉从床上下来，活动活动四肢：“给你三日，只要三日后能把你的父母和朱家长辈请来，我定会放你离开林家。”
Lin Jue climbed off the bed and stretched his stiff limbs. "I'll give you three days. Bring your parents and the Zhu elders here in three days, and I'll let you leave the Lin family."

千里寻兴奋道：“一言为定！不过，你得告诉我，你打算请几位长辈？我这边在数量上不能比你少，两边得势均力敌，互相谈判起来才不会你方压倒我方，或者我方压倒你方，这样才公平。”
Qianli Xun beamed. "It's a deal! Tell me one thing, though—how many elders are you bringing? My side can't be outnumbered. We need an even match so neither family bullies the other at the table. Only fair."

“放心，你请几位，我就请几位，如若一位都请不来，还请娘子不要再想什么和离之事。”
"Don't worry—however many you bring, I'll match. And if you can't bring a single one, forget about mutual divorce."

听到这话，千里寻警惕道：“你是不是有什么阴谋？”
Suspicion flared. "You planning some scheme?"

林觉盯着朱淑真，她虽不是以前那个她，但容貌还是。他只要看一眼，就不忍心把她送到其他男子的怀抱：“和离的主意是我出的，能有什么阴谋？”
Lin Jue gazed at her. Different soul or not, her face hadn't changed—one look and he couldn't bear handing her over to another man. "Mutual divorce was my idea. What scheme could I have?"

“也对！”千里寻想着自己还得在林府待些时日，需跟眼前这位约法三章，瞅着林觉往外走的背影，大声道：“喂！”
"Fair point!" Remembering she still had to stay under this roof for a few days, she needed ground rules. Calling after his retreating back, she yelled, "Hey!"

林觉停下来，转过身：“还有什么事？”
Lin Jue paused and turned. "Something else?"

“我需在林府住些时日，总得跟你提出些要求。”
"While I'm still staying in Lin Manor, I have three rules."

“洗耳恭听。”
"I'm all ears."

林觉竟然喜欢听朱淑真说话，哪怕都是废话他也愿意，只怪以前想听她说句话都难。
Strange as it was, Lin Jue found himself enjoying the sound of her voice—even idle chatter—after starving for a single word from her for so long.

“第一，不准进竹兰居，我的拳头你可是领教过。”
"First, stay out of Zhulan Residence. You've already tasted my fists."

林觉对竹兰居没有什么好印象，那里给他留下的只有冷漠和忽视，即使进过几次，除了打朱淑真，都想不起进去做过什么。不进也罢，他点点头。
Lin Jue held no fond memories of Zhulan Residence—only cold shoulders and humiliation; aside from hitting her, he couldn't recall why he ever went in. Fine by him. He nodded.

“第二，不准把商妓带回林府，你要快活去青楼。”
"Second, no bringing courtesans into Lin Manor. Want fun, go to a brothel."

“可以。”
"Agreed."

“第三，没有和离前，你得给我银子使。”
"Third, until the divorce is final, you give me silver to spend."

“你还是女主人，当然有使银子的权利。”
"You're still mistress of the house—spending silver is your right."

“没想到你这人还挺痛快，我对你的好印象又增加一分。”
"Look at you, agreeing so fast. My opinion of you just went up a notch."

林觉离开后，千里寻准备着手和离之事，看来得到朱淑真的娘家走一趟。她对那个娘家一无所知，计划先向苦荞了解些情况，再做打算。
Once Lin Jue left, Qianli Xun set her mind to the mutual divorce. She needed to visit Zhu Shuzhen's maiden home—a place she knew nothing about. First, get the rundown from Kuqiao, then plan her move.

三日的时间太短，为了自己能在这个陌生的时代有新的生活，她决定拼一拼。
Three days was tight, yet for the sake of a fresh start in this unfamiliar era, she would give it everything she had.
