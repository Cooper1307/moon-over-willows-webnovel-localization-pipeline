# 第8章 约法三章放夫君 / Chapter 8: Three Rules to Set My Husband Free

---

## 段落 1

**原文**：天放亮后，清晨的气息从窗户传入房中，纯净而甘甜，没有掺杂混凝土和汽车尾气的味道。千里寻把被子踢开，在梦中嗅着清新的空气，口中唤着："申简辰！申简辰！……"

**译文**：After dawn broke, the morning air drifted through the window, pure and sweet, without the smell of concrete or car exhaust. Qianli Xun kicked off the blanket, breathing in the fresh air as she slept, calling out: "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!..."

---

## 段落 2

**原文**：苦荞听到声音，掀开床幔，轻声问道："小姐，您说什么？"

**译文**：Kuqiao heard the voice, lifted the bed curtain, and asked softly, "Miss, what did you say?"

---

## 段落 3

**原文**："申简辰！"千里寻并未睁眼，依然睡着。

**译文**："Shen Jianchen!" Qianli Xun didn't open her eyes, still asleep.

---

## 段落 4

**原文**：苦荞嘀咕："申简辰是谁？我怎么从来没听小姐提起过？"

**译文**：Kuqiao muttered, "Who is Shen Jianchen? How come I've never heard Miss mention him?"

---

## 段落 5

**原文**：她替小姐把被子盖好，继续放好床幔，坐在床边开始绣花，耐心等待小姐醒来。

**译文**：She tucked the blanket back over her miss, drew the bed curtain closed, sat down by the bed, and began to embroider, waiting patiently for her to wake.

---

## 段落 6

**原文**：苦荞刚绣完一片叶子，千里寻睁开眼，坐起来，瞧着床铺和床幔，恍若隔世："对，我穿越了，还以为自己依然是千里寻。"

**译文**：Kuqiao had just finished embroidering a leaf when Qianli Xun opened her eyes and sat up. Looking at the bed and curtains, she felt like she was in a different world: "Right, I've transmigrated. I thought I was still in my own time."

---

## 段落 7

**原文**：苦荞听到声音，停下绣花，把所有东西放入收纳的竹篮中，搁到一边，起身掀开床幔："小姐，您醒了？"说着边把床幔挂好。

**译文**：Hearing the sound, Kuqiao stopped embroidering, put everything into the bamboo basket and set it aside, got up, and lifted the bed curtain: "Miss, you're awake?" She drew the curtain aside as she spoke.

---

## 段落 8

**原文**：千里寻看到苦荞，更清楚自己已成为朱淑真："现在几点了？"

**译文**：Seeing Kuqiao, Qianli Xun felt even more real that she had become Zhu Shuzhen: "What time is it?"

---

## 段落 9

**原文**：苦荞想了一会儿，问道："小姐是问现在是什么时辰吗？"

**译文**：Kuqiao thought for a moment and asked, "Is Miss asking what hour it is?"

---

## 段落 10

**原文**：千里寻苦笑了一下，时代不同，人们对同样事物的表达不同，还好，依然能交流："是的。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun smiled bitterly. Different times, different ways to say the same thing. At least they could still communicate: "Yes."

---

## 段落 11

**原文**："还未到巳时。"苦荞边说边给小姐拿来衣裳。

**译文**："It's not yet the Si Hour." Kuqiao brought over clothes for her miss.

---

## 段落 12

**原文**：千里寻写过关于古代的网络小说，研究过时辰，说明现在还不到九点。她走下床，任由苦荞为她穿好衣裳。

**译文**：Qianli Xun had written web novels about ancient times and studied the ancient time system, which meant it wasn't yet nine o'clock. She got out of bed and let Kuqiao dress her.

---

## 段落 13

**原文**："小姐，昨晚上姑爷挺老实，没有听到任何声音。"

**译文**："Miss, the master was quite well-behaved last night. I didn't hear a single sound."

---

## 段落 14

**原文**："我把他的嘴堵住，当然老实。"

**译文**："I gagged him, so of course he was well-behaved."

---

## 段落 15

**原文**："啊！小姐怎么能把姑爷的嘴堵住？您就不怕姑爷休了您？"

**译文**："Ah! How could Miss gag the master? Aren't you afraid he'll cast you out?"

---

## 段落 16

**原文**："哼！我才不怕，昨天我还亲自让他把我休了，结果他不愿意，还给我出了一个好主意。"千里寻显得很得意。

**译文**:"Hmph! I'm not afraid at all. Yesterday I even asked him to divorce me, but he refused and even gave me a good idea." Qianli Xun looked quite pleased.

---

## 段落 17

**原文**：苦荞已为小姐穿好衣裳，立刻合掌："阿弥陀佛！阿弥陀佛！多亏姑爷没答应，否则小姐可就惨了！"

**译文**：Kuqiao had finished dressing her miss and immediately clasped her hands in prayer: "Amitabha! Amitabha! Thank goodness the master didn't agree, otherwise Miss would have been ruined!"

---

## 段落 18

**原文**：千里寻瞅着苦荞担忧的傻样，笑道："看把你吓的，不就是被休吗？有什么了不起的？"

**译文**：Qianli Xun looked at Kuqiao's worried, silly expression and laughed, "Look how scared you are. It's just being cast out, what's the big deal?"

---

## 段落 19

**原文**：苦荞愁道："这么看来，您确实不是以前的小姐，她绝对不会向姑爷提出这个要求。"

**译文**：Kuqiao said worriedly, "Looking at it this way, you really aren't the previous Miss. She would never make such a request to the master."

---

## 段落 20

**原文**："我本来就不是她。"

**译文**:"I was never her in the first place."

---

## 段落 21

**原文**："可我只有您这一位小姐，就当苦荞求您，千万不要让姑爷休了您。"

**译文**:"But I only have you as my Miss. Please, let me beg you, don't let the master cast you out."

---

## 段落 22

**原文**：瞧着苦荞可怜紧张的样子，千里寻问道："你倒是说说女子被休有什么不好？为何担心成这样儿？"

**译文**：Seeing Kuqiao's pitiful, nervous expression, Qianli Xun asked, "Tell me, what's so bad about a woman being cast out? Why are you so worried?"

---

## 段落 23

**原文**：苦荞立刻说："被夫君休了的女子会被大家认为品行不端，以后再要嫁人都难，而且会让娘家蒙羞，如果娘家还有其他未出阁的姐妹，也会影响到她们的名声，只怕没有人敢再上门提亲，所以娘家为保住其他姑娘的清白，绝对不会让被夫家休了的女儿进门。"

**译文**：Kuqiao said immediately, "A woman cast out by her husband will be considered to be of bad character. It will be hard for her to remarry, and it will shame her paternal family. If there are other unmarried sisters in the family, it will affect their reputation too. I'm afraid no one would dare come to propose marriage. So to protect the purity of the other girls, the paternal family would never let a daughter cast out by her husband back into the home."

---

## 段落 24

**原文**：确实挺严重，千里寻不由问道："我可否还有其他姐妹？"

**译文**：It really was serious. Qianli Xun couldn't help asking, "Do I have any other sisters?"

---

## 段落 25

**原文**："那倒没有，朱府只小姐一位千金，您上面有位哥哥。"

**译文**:"No, there aren't. The Zhu residence only has you as the young lady. You have an older brother."

---

## 段落 26

**原文**："那我就放心，即使被林觉休了，也不会担心连累他人。"

**译文**:"Then I'm relieved. Even if Lin Jue casts me out, I won't worry about affecting anyone else."

---

## 段落 27

**原文**："不行，小姐要是被姑爷休了，估计连朱府的门都进不了，老爷是位很看重清誉之人，他绝对不会允许子女做出有辱门楣之事。"

**译文**:"No way. If Miss is cast out by the master, you probably won't even be able to enter the gates of the Zhu residence. Your father is a man who values reputation above all else. He would never allow his children to do anything that tarnishes the family reputation."

---

## 段落 28

**原文**：苦荞把小姐拉到铜镜前坐下，开始帮她整理妆容。

**译文**：Kuqiao led her miss to sit in front of the bronze mirror and began to tidy her appearance.

---

## 段落 29

**原文**：千里寻透过铜镜仔细观察苦荞帮她整理发髻："你放心，林觉不会休我，他给我出了和离的主意。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun carefully watched Kuqiao help her style her hair through the bronze mirror: "Don't worry. Lin Jue won't cast me out. He gave me the idea of a mutual divorce."

---

## 段落 30

**原文**："这么说来，姑爷还不错，和离是最好的选择，我们村有位姚婶婶当年跟以前的夫君和离，娘家不仅允许她进门，后来还找人嫁了，听说现在一家人日子过得和和美美。"

**译文**:"Then the master isn't so bad. A mutual divorce is the best choice. In our village, Aunt Yao agreed to a mutual divorce with her former husband. Her paternal family not only let her back in, but later found someone for her to marry. I heard her family is living happily now."

---

## 段落 31

**原文**："可我总觉得林觉没那么好心，说不定有什么幺蛾子等着我。"

**译文**:"But I feel Lin Jue isn't that kind-hearted. There might be some dirty tricks in store for me."

---

## 段落 32

**原文**：苦荞继续给小姐整理头发，装点发饰："姑爷以前对小姐确实不好，小姐和姑爷本是两条道上的人，偏偏给拉到一条道上，不仅自己难受，别人看着也难受。"

**译文**：Kuqiao continued to tidy her miss's hair and put in hair ornaments: "The master really wasn't good to Miss before. Miss and the master were originally meant for different paths, but were forced onto the same one. Not only did you suffer, but others watching also suffered."

---

## 段落 33

**原文**："所以，我一定要离开他，既对他好，也对我好。"

**译文**:"So I must leave him. It's good for him, and good for me too."

---

## 段落 34

**原文**：千里寻决定必须离开林觉。

**译文**：Qianli Xun decided she must leave Lin Jue.

---

## 段落 35

**原文**：从千里寻的角度来讲，她连正经男朋友都没有过，穿越到大宋，成为别人，先破天荒地进入到一段婚姻中，丈夫竟然还是打老婆的渣男，甚至公然将青楼女子带到家里，这种人不跟他离婚，天理难容！

**译文**：From Qianli Xun's perspective, she had never even had a proper boyfriend. After transmigrating to the Great Song and becoming someone else, she entered a marriage for the first time ever, only to find her husband was a scumbag who beat his wife and even openly brought courtesans home. If she didn't divorce such a person, even heaven wouldn't stand for it!

---

## 段落 36

**原文**：从朱淑真角度来讲，她跟林觉志不同道不合，没有共同爱好和情趣，就像一朵鲜花插在牛粪上，如此不协调，而且这朵鲜花迟早会被牛粪熏死。既然千里寻有幸成为朱淑真，有义务帮助她摆脱这段婚姻，再说帮她就是帮自己。

**译文**：From Zhu Shuzhen's perspective, she and Lin Jue had different goals and incompatible lifestyles, with no common hobbies or interests. It was like a beautiful flower planted in cow dung—so discordant, and this flower would sooner or later be killed by the stench. Since Qianli Xun was fortunate enough to become Zhu Shuzhen, she had an obligation to help her escape this marriage. Besides, helping her was helping herself.

---

## 段落 37

**原文**：正想着，苦荞问道："小姐，您说梦话时叫出一个人名，好像是申简辰，他是谁？"

**译文**：Just as she was thinking, Kuqiao asked, "Miss, you called out a name in your sleep. It seemed to be Shen Jianchen. Who is he?"

---

## 段落 38

**原文**：千里寻没有必要瞒着苦荞："他是跟我一起在地震中死亡的朋友，估计是睡着时梦到地震的场景，不由自主喊出他的名字。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun had no need to hide it from Kuqiao: "He's a friend who died with me in the earthquake. I probably dreamed of the earthquake scene in my sleep and couldn't help calling out his name."

---

## 段落 39

**原文**："他是不是小姐在另一个时代喜欢的人？"

**译文**:"Is he someone Miss liked in another era?"

---

## 段落 40

**原文**："呵呵！我要是喜欢他，估计猪都能上树，这个人每次见到我，不损我几句，就不舒服，还美其名曰追求我，真是搞笑！"

**译文**:"Hehe! If I liked him, pigs would fly. Every time he sees me, he has to insult me a few times or he's not satisfied, and he calls it 'pursuing me.' It's really funny!"

---

## 段落 41

**原文**：千里寻说完，又莫名悲伤："现在想让他当面损我，都没有机会，我成了朱淑真，他不知道会怎样，也许永远也见不到他。"

**译文**：After speaking, Qianli Xun felt inexplicably sad: "Now I don't even have a chance to let him insult me to my face. I've become Zhu Shuzhen. I don't know how he'll react. Maybe I'll never see him again."

---

## 段落 42

**原文**：两人聊着，苦荞已为小姐装扮完毕："小姐，您看，怎么样？满意吗？"

**译文**：As the two chatted, Kuqiao had finished dressing her miss: "Miss, look. How is it? Are you happy with it?"

---

## 段落 43

**原文**：千里寻再向镜中瞅一眼："你这丫鬟心灵手巧，还知道你家小姐怎么装扮最美，不错，我很满意。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun glanced at the mirror again: "You're a clever and skilled maid. You even know how to style your miss most beautifully. Not bad, I'm very satisfied."

---

## 段落 44

**原文**：千里寻梳洗完毕，用过早饭，决定去看看林觉，估计这一晚上他不怎么好过，还是赶紧把人给放了。

**译文**：After Qianli Xun finished getting ready and eating breakfast, she decided to go see Lin Jue. He probably didn't have an easy night. It was better to let him go quickly.

---

## 段落 45

**原文**：她推开关押林觉的房门，走进去。

**译文**：She pushed open Lin Jue's room and walked in.

---

## 段落 46

**原文**：林觉听到有声音，迅速坐起来，嘴里塞着布子，不能说话，那眼神，明显是命令朱淑真尽快放了他。

**译文**：Hearing the sound, Lin Jue quickly sat up. His mouth was stuffed with cloth and he couldn't speak. His eyes clearly demanded that Zhu Shuzhen release him as soon as possible.

---

## 段落 47

**原文**："你别拿眼睛瞪我，我过来就是给你松绑。"说着，千里寻过去，拔掉林觉嘴里的布子。

**译文**:"Don't glare at me. I came here to untie you." As she spoke, Qianli Xun went over and pulled the cloth from his mouth.

---

## 段落 48

**原文**：林觉脱口而出："你这恶毒的女人！"

**译文**：Lin Jue blurted out, "You vicious woman!"

---

## 段落 49

**原文**：千里寻边松绑边说："我要是真恶毒，你都活不过今天，还是聪明点儿。你呢，打不过我，以后好好听我差遣，跟我把婚和和美美地离了，咱们以后井水不犯河水，你过你的日子，我过我的日子，以后见了面还能打个招呼，不也挺好？我爹在临安也是有头有脸的人物，官职也比你大，你对我好，他肯定不会亏待你。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun untied him as she spoke: "If I were truly vicious, you wouldn't survive past today. Be smarter. You can't beat me, so from now on, listen to me and agree to a peaceful mutual divorce. From then on, we'll each mind our own business. You live your life, I'll live mine. When we meet in the future, we can still greet each other. Isn't that good? My father is an important figure in Lin'an, with a higher official rank than yours. If you treat me well, he definitely won't treat you badly."

---

## 段落 50

**原文**：千里寻嘚啵嘚啵完，绳子也正好解完。想着绳子松开，说不定林觉会袭击而来，她已做好防备，却发现林觉并未如此做，而是盯着她看，眼神比刚才缓和许多。

**译文**：After Qianli Xun finished her chatter, she had just finished untying the ropes. Thinking that once the ropes were loose, Lin Jue might attack, she had prepared herself. But she found Lin Jue made no move to attack. Instead, he stared at her, his gaze much gentler than before.

---

## 段落 51

**原文**："你看我干嘛？"

**译文**:"What are you looking at me for?"

---

## 段落 52

**原文**："自从你嫁给我，跟我说过的话加起来，都没有昨天到今天说的多，如果你以前像这样，我也不会打你骂你。"

**译文**:"Since you married me, all the words you've said to me combined don't come close to what you said from yesterday to today. If you had been like this before, I wouldn't have beaten or scolded you."

---

## 段落 53

**原文**：千里寻突然觉得林觉也挺可怜，娶一位对她实施冷暴力的妻子："所以我们不合适，在一起你难受，我也不好受。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun suddenly felt Lin Jue was also quite pitiable, having married a wife who gave him the silent treatment: "So we're not suitable. Together, you suffer, and I'm not happy either."

---

## 段落 54

**原文**：林觉从床上下来，活动活动四肢："给你三日，只要三日后能把你的父母和朱家长辈请来，我定会放你离开林家。"

**译文**：Lin Jue got off the bed and stretched his arms and legs: "I'll give you three days. As long as you can invite your parents and the Zhu family elders in three days, I will definitely let you leave the Lin family."

---

## 段落 55

**原文**：千里寻兴奋道："一言为定！不过，你得告诉我，你打算请几位长辈？我这边在数量上不能比你少，两边得势均力敌，互相谈判起来才不会你方压倒我方，或者我方压倒你方，这样才公平。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun said excitedly, "It's a deal! But you have to tell me, how many elders do you plan to invite? My side must have at least as many as yours. Both sides must be evenly matched, so when negotiating, neither side will overwhelm the other. Only then will it be fair."

---

## 段落 56

**原文**："放心，你请几位，我就请几位，如若一位都请不来，还请娘子不要再想什么和离之事。"

**译文**:"Don't worry. However many you invite, I'll invite the same number. If you can't invite even one, then my dear wife, don't think about mutual divorce anymore."

---

## 段落 57

**原文**：听到这话，千里寻警惕道："你是不是有什么阴谋？"

**译文**：Hearing this, Qianli Xun said warily, "Do you have some kind of scheme?"

---

## 段落 58

**原文**：林觉盯着朱淑真，她虽不是以前那个她，但容貌还是。他只要看一眼，就不忍心把她送到其他男子的怀抱："和离的主意是我出的，能有什么阴谋？"

**译文**：Lin Jue stared at Zhu Shuzhen. Though she wasn't the same person as before, her appearance remained the same. Just one look and he couldn't bear to send her into another man's arms: "I was the one who suggested mutual divorce. What scheme could there be?"

---

## 段落 59

**原文**："也对！"千里寻想着自己还得在林府待些时日，需跟眼前这位约法三章，瞅着林觉往外走的背影，大声道："喂！"

**译文**:"That's true too!" Qianli Xun thought she still had to stay at the Lin residence for some time and needed to set three rules with him. Looking at Lin Jue's back as he walked out, she called loudly, "Hey!"

---

## 段落 60

**原文**：林觉停下来，转过身："还有什么事？"

**译文**：Lin Jue stopped and turned around: "What else?"

---

## 段落 61

**译文**:"I need to stay at the Lin residence for some time. I have to make some requests."

---

## 段落 62

**原文**："洗耳恭听。"

**译文**:"I'm all ears."

---

## 段落 63

**译文**：Lin Jue actually liked hearing Zhu Shuzhen speak. Even if it was all nonsense, he was happy to listen. He could only blame himself—before, it was hard to even hear her say a word.

---

## 段落 64

**原文**："第一，不准进竹兰居，我的拳头你可是领教过。"

**译文**:"First, you're not allowed to enter the Zhulan Residence. You've already felt my fists."

---
