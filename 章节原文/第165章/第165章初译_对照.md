第165章 来到草地放风筝
## Chapter 165: Flying Kites on the Meadow

陈文心对韦嬷嬷的判断深信不疑，也帮忙劝说两个丫鬟：“你们两个听韦嬷嬷的话准没错，整日伺候你家小姐，还不趁此机会好好歇着。”
陈文心边说边朝捶丸场上瞧去，钱氏已找人组好队，她的对手是林觉与其夫人。
两个丫鬟不再多言，韦嬷嬷也不再说什么，她虽对夏果不满，也觉夏果刚刚问的有些道理。姑娘心好，总喜欢给身边收丫鬟，却不知身边丫鬟多了，也不见得是好事。
收夏果时，韦嬷嬷不在，无法阻拦；收红豆时，韦嬷嬷在，姑娘却深受情伤，又被定下不满意的婚约，心里苦，韦嬷嬷便纵着她。
重要的是夏果心不在姑娘身边，苦荞迟早也会出嫁，总得给姑娘身边再培养一个贴心的丫鬟，红豆既勤快又老实，无疑是最佳人选。
……
在捶丸场时，眼界受限，千里寻根本看不出捶丸场之外还有一处放风筝之地。
“梦容，你怎知道后面能放风筝？”
“哥哥告诉我的，本来没想着去，他见我不喜捶丸，太无聊，主动要求带我去放风筝。”
“仪王前来捶丸，还随身带了风筝？”
“那倒没有，哥哥说陈家在后面的草地上准备了风筝。”
千里寻半信半疑，跟着梦容继续走着，越走视野越开阔。渐渐，眼前竟然出现一片比捶丸场还大的草地。极目远眺，看不到边。
Trusting Matron Wei's judgment without question, Chen Wenxin backed her up to soothe the two maids. "Listen to Matron Wei—she is never wrong. You wait on your mistress all day long, so take the chance to catch your breath." As she spoke, she looked out over the field, where Madam Qian had formed a fresh team and stood squared off against Lin Jue and his bride.

千里寻不由慨叹：“真是踏青的好去处！”
Neither maid said another word, and Matron Wei held her peace as well. Irritated as she was with Xiaguo, she admitted the girl had a point. Her mistress had a soft heart and loved taking in maids, not realizing a swollen retinue brought trouble of its own. Matron Wei had been away when Xiaguo was taken in; when Hongdou joined, her mistress had been reeling from heartbreak and saddled with an unwanted betrothal, so Matron Wei had indulged her. More to the point, Xiaguo's heart was no longer with her mistress and Kuqiao would marry sooner or later. Someone loyal and dependable had to be trained up to take their place, and hardworking, honest Hongdou was the clear choice.

“难道比西湖还美？”
Back on the chuiwan grounds, the surrounding walls and pavilions had blocked the view, so Qianli Xun had never guessed a kite meadow lay beyond. "How did you know we could fly kites back here, Mengrong?"

“西湖当然美，只不过经常去，已没新鲜感，这片草地我可是第一次来，该不会也是陈家的？”
"Brother told me. I had no plans to come, yet he saw how bored I was with chuiwan and offered to bring me out."

申简辰有意走过来，听到千里寻发问，主动回答：“当然是陈家的。”
"Did Prince Yi pack kites along for a chuiwan match?"

当着梦容的面，千里寻还是得行礼：“仪王殿下。”
"No, Brother said the Chen family set up kites on the back meadow."

“感谢幽栖居士与我组队，彩头我可不能一个人独占，我已吩咐白风将彩头给苦荞。”
Half skeptical, Qianli Xun walked on beside Mengrong as the path opened wide. Soon a meadow even larger than the chuiwan field unfolded before them, stretching green as far as the eye could see.

“那我岂不是独占了仪王的彩头？”
"What a splendid spot for a spring outing!" Qianli Xun exclaimed.

“无妨，我是王爷，身边什么东西没有，一个金如意就别跟我客气。”
"Even lovelier than West Lake?"

千里寻还想说“我也不在乎”时，乔梦容劝道：“淑真，你就拿着吧，都是哥哥的一片心意，也是我的一片心意。”
"West Lake is gorgeous, of course, yet I go there so often the novelty has worn off. This meadow is brand new to me. Do not tell me the Chens own this too?"

千里寻爽快答应：“既是梦容的一片心意，我就不客气。”
Shen Jianchen walked up just in time to catch her question. "Of course it belongs to the Chen family."

申简辰指着不远处坐在草地上扎风筝的人：“梦容，我带你过去买风筝。”
With Mengrong right beside them, Qianli Xun dipped into a curtsy. "Your Highness Prince Yi."

乔梦容拉起淑真：“好，哥哥也要给淑真买一个。”
"Thank you for teaming up with me, Youqi Jushi," Shen Jianchen said. "I cannot hog the prize all to myself, so I had Bai Feng hand it over to Kuqiao."

“好，你们两个一人一个。”申简辰答应的同时寻思，待会儿怎么找机会与千里寻一起放风筝。
"Does that not mean I am hogging Your Highness's prize instead?"

来到扎风筝处，待两位姑娘挑好后，申简辰问：“风筝怎么卖？”
"No matter. As a prince, I have all the treasures I could ask for. Do not stand on ceremony over one gold ruyi scepter."

扎风筝的老人回道：“不要银子，你们喜欢哪个，随便挑。”
Qianli Xun opened her mouth to say she did not care for it either, yet Qiao Mengrong cut in. "Take it, Shuzhen. It is a token of goodwill from Brother—and from me too."

千里寻关切地问：“这么好看的风筝，都是你辛苦扎的，不要钱，你怎么生活？”
"If it is a token from Mengrong, then I will accept without fuss," Qianli Xun agreed.

陈浩宇正好走来，青瓜跟在身后，听到朱淑真的问题，觉得十分可笑。
Shen Jianchen pointed toward an old man sitting on the grass not far away, framing bamboo kites. "Come, Mengrong, let me buy you a kite."

“这片草地是陈家的，扎风筝的人也是陈家的，陈家给他工钱，比他在外面风餐露宿卖风筝强多了。”
"Yes, and buy one for Shuzhen too, Brother," Qiao Mengrong said, pulling her friend along.

老人放下风筝，站起来问好：“陈公子好！”
"Alright, one for each of you," Shen Jianchen agreed, already scheming how to steal a turn flying kites with Qianli Xun.

“忙你的吧。”陈浩宇见贵人，还得行礼，“仪王，佳和郡主，二位贵人放风筝时若需有人指导，尽管告诉我，捶丸场的下人都会，我为二位找两个。”
Once the two girls chose their kites, Shen Jianchen asked, "How much for these?"

陈浩宇出现，申简辰本就不爽，又凑到跟前，实在令人不悦，他用更冷淡的语气说：“不必，本王放风筝的技术难道还比不上一个下人？退下吧，不必前来打扰。”
"No silver needed," the old kite-maker answered. "Pick whichever ones you fancy."

陈浩宇听出仪王对他依然有成见，还是赶紧撤，于是恭敬行礼：“二位贵人忙。”
Concern furrowed Qianli Xun's brow. "You put all this labor into making such fine kites—if you take no money, how do you make a living?"

千里寻对陈浩宇只顾巴结仪王十分不满，陈浩宇正好瞧向她，便趁机狠狠瞪他一眼。
Just then Chen Haoyu sauntered up with Qinggua trailing behind and burst out laughing at the question. "This meadow belongs to the Chens, and the kite-maker works for the Chens. We pay him regular wages—miles better than sleeping rough on the streets hawking kites."

陈浩宇则露出不屑的表情。
Setting his work down, the old man stood and greeted him. "Good day, Young Master Chen!"

这一幕被乔梦容看到，待陈浩宇离开后，关切地说：“淑真，你们两个看上去很不合，你嫁给他，以后该怎么办？”
"Carry on with your work," Chen Haoyu told him, then turned to bow to the royal pair. "Your Highness Prince Yi, Princess Jiahe—if either of Your Highnesses wants pointers on flying kites, just say the word. The servants here know how, and I can fetch a couple for you."

“什么看上去不合，事实上就是不合，放心，我不会嫁给他。”
Already annoyed by Chen Haoyu's appearance, Shen Jianchen turned colder still at the man's intrusion. "Unnecessary. Do you think this prince flies a kite worse than a servant? Dismissed. Do not disturb us."

“可以吗？婚姻向来是长辈做主，只怕……”
Sensing Prince Yi's lingering hostility, Chen Haoyu bowed low to beat a hasty retreat. "I leave Your Highnesses to your pleasure."

申简辰趁机再次表露心迹：“恐怕婚约挡着，你不得不嫁，除非找一个能压过朱陈两家之人，答应嫁给他，退婚之事尚可成功。”
Disgusted by his bootlicking, Qianli Xun caught Chen Haoyu's eye and shot him a fierce glare. Chen Haoyu answered with a disdainful sneer.

千里寻明白申简辰的意思，上次没答应他，这次更不会答应。令她不安的是，梦容还在身边，他竟然不管不顾，说出如此隐晦又引人怀疑的话，
Qiao Mengrong caught the exchange. Once Chen Haoyu walked off, she asked with concern, "Shuzhen, the two of you do not seem to get along at all. What will you do after you marry him?"

手里拿着风筝，行礼十分不便，千里寻还是郑重屈身谢道：“淑真感谢仪王关怀，自己的事还得自己解决，依靠他人也无非是用一段婚姻代替另一段婚姻而已，淑真已决定这辈子谁也不嫁。”
"Not just 'seem'—we flat-out cannot stand each other. Relax, I am not marrying him."

乔梦容正在思忖哥哥的建议：“能压得过朱陈两家之人，在整个临安城恐怕只有哥哥。哥哥说这话是什么意思？难道他看在淑真与我关系好，想要帮她？”
"Is that even possible? Parents decide marriages. I fear..."

乔梦容越想越担忧，开口说：“哥哥的建议别说淑真不同意，我听了也觉不妥，目前临安城能压过朱陈两家之人非哥哥莫属，可哥哥与我有婚约……”
Seizing the opening, Shen Jianchen let his true intent slip through. "With the betrothal in place, you will have no choice but to wed—unless you find someone who outranks both the Zhu and Chen families and agree to marry him instead. Only then could the engagement be broken."

千里寻担心梦容看出申简辰的心思，赶紧解释：“梦容，千万别误会，仪王肯定不是那个意思，他是看在我是你闺蜜的份上，才会这么说。”
Qianli Xun knew full well what he meant. She had refused him before, and she would refuse him again. What rattled her was his recklessness—dropping hints that transparent right in front of Mengrong! Holding her kite made curtsying awkward, yet she still sank into a solemn bow. "Shuzhen thanks Your Highness for your concern, yet I must settle my own affairs. Leaning on someone else only trades one marriage for another. Shuzhen has already decided never to marry anyone in this life."

申简辰很想告诉乔梦容，他喜欢的人是谁，又见不得千里寻为难，很不情愿地解释道：“你们两个都别多想，我随口说说而已，只是想警告幽栖居士，想要退婚，难上加难。”
Qiao Mengrong mulled over her brother's words. Who in all of Lin'an could outrank both the Zhu and Chen families? Only Brother himself. What had he meant by that? Was he offering to step in because Shuzhen was her friend? Unease gripped her. "Never mind Shuzhen refusing—even I think Brother's idea is improper. Right now, nobody in Lin'an outranks both families except Brother, yet Brother is betrothed to me..."

听了哥哥的解释，乔梦容才放松心情：“确实难，与林觉和离之事，哥哥还能帮你，可退婚之事哥哥也无能为力。”
Terrified Mengrong might see straight through him, Qianli Xun jumped in. "Do not get the wrong idea, Mengrong! Prince Yi never meant that. He only spoke out of concern because I am your closest friend."

“你与仪王已帮我很多，我打心里感激不尽。仪王以后不必再因乔家而帮我，我……我早已不怪乔家……”
Shen Jianchen burned to tell Qiao Mengrong outright whom he loved, yet seeing Qianli Xun cornered held him back. He forced out a stiff explanation. "Do not overthink it, either of you. I spoke off the cuff—just warning Youqi Jushi how impossible breaking off a betrothal is."

申简辰猜到千里寻提乔家，不过是寻一个合理的解释，以免梦容多想。
His words eased the knot in Qiao Mengrong's chest. "Impossible indeed. Brother could help with your divorce from Lin Jue, yet even he is powerless against a betrothal."

乔梦容比刚才更显轻松，原来哥哥为淑真出主意，不过是因乔家对朱家食言，硬生生拆散两个相爱之人。
"You and Prince Yi have already helped me more than I can repay, and I am grateful from the bottom of my heart," Qianli Xun said. "Your Highness need not go out of your way for me over the Qiao family anymore. I... I stopped holding a grudge against the Qiaos long ago."

申简辰很不情愿地帮千里寻圆谎：“我确实因乔家想要帮你，既然幽栖居士已想好，以后你的事，我不再理会。梦容，哥哥带你去放风筝。”
Shen Jianchen guessed she brought up the Qiao family just to hand Mengrong a clean explanation. Sure enough, Mengrong relaxed further—so Brother had been trying to make amends because the Qiaos broke their word to the Zhus and tore two lovers apart.

好意再次被拒绝，又听到心爱的人对他说出“谁也不嫁”这种决绝的话，申简辰再次被伤到，可又不愿放弃一直以来努力追求的人，不！是那个有趣的灵魂。
Reluctant to the bone, Shen Jianchen backed up her cover story. "I did wish to help because of the Qiao family. Since Youqi Jushi has made up her mind, I will meddle no further. Come, Mengrong, Brother will take you kite-flying."

他觉得自己很贱,无论身为大公司的CEO,还是身为官家之子,面对心爱的人,不由自主会贱起来,也许在爱情面前,身份并不那么重要.
Rejected once again—and told "never to marry anyone" with such finality—stung Shen Jianchen raw. Yet he still could not give up the woman he had chased so long, or rather, that vivid soul inside her. How pathetic he felt. Whether CEO of a major corporation or son of the Song Emperor, standing before the woman he loved stripped away every shred of pride. Maybe rank meant nothing in the face of love.

乔梦容主动上前拉起哥哥的手：“淑真，我不太会放风筝，得哥哥教我，你可会放风筝？”
千里寻点点头：“曾经放过，有些经验。”
三人向草地中央走去，看到草地上有坐着晒太阳的，有情侣一起在周围踏青的，最多的当然是放风筝的人。
千里寻看到南双正在与化雨一起放风筝，便向草地四周瞧了一遍，果然看到乔文仙与三位公子坐在草地上饮酒聊天儿，看那样子，还未发现自己。
千里寻想要放下风筝离开，传来梦容的声音：“淑真！你快些！我的风筝都快飞起来！”
在申简辰的帮助下，梦容的风筝渐渐飘到空中。她满面笑容，一会儿瞧风筝，一会儿瞧他的哥哥，幸福的样子令千里寻羡慕，也令千里寻不忍伤害，她在内心祝福梦容永远这么幸福下去。
申辰帮梦容放风筝的样子很认真，他们看上去也很般配，希望他不要再执着穿越前的感情，能安心对待梦容，代替已故的赵朴成为梦容真正的哥哥。
刚才差一点引起梦容怀疑，千里寻决定以后还是尽量不要与申简辰见面。
既然不好离开，又得一个人放风筝，不如不要逃避，凭她一个人也能将风筝放到高空。
她把风筝放在地上，打算先将风筝线放得长一些，然后向后跑去，等风筝飞起来，再一点点将线放长。
红豆凑过来：“小姐，我帮您。”
这个声音，对不得不孤单的千里寻来讲，无疑是一种安慰：“你把风筝拿起来，向那边跑。”
Qiao Mengrong took her brother's hand. "Shuzhen, I am hopeless with kites, so Brother has to teach me. Do you know how?"

红豆按照小姐吩咐，将风筝从地上拿起，向小姐指的方向跑去。千里寻一点一点放着线，很快风筝从红豆的手中飞到空中。
"I have flown them before," Qianli Xun nodded. "I know the basics."

“小姐！风筝飞起来了！太好了！”红豆拍手喊起来。
The three strolled toward the center of the meadow, passing people sunbathing on the grass, couples strolling in the spring breeze, and crowds launching kites. Spotting Nan Shuang and Huayu running with a kite, Qianli Xun scanned the grounds and saw Qiao Wenxian drinking with the three scholars on the grass; none of them seemed to have noticed her yet.

“红豆！你过来！跟我一起放！”
She thought about putting her kite down and slipping away when Mengrong called out, "Hurry up, Shuzhen! My kite is taking off!"

“好！”
With Shen Jianchen guiding her hands, Mengrong's kite lifted into the air. Glowing with joy, she glanced back and forth between the soaring kite and her brother. Her radiant happiness roused a pang of envy in Qianli Xun—and deepened her resolve never to hurt her. Silently, she wished Mengrong happiness like this forever. Shen Jianchen looked attentive helping her, and the two made a handsome pair. If only he could let go of his pre-transmigration feelings, care for Mengrong wholeheartedly, and step into the late Zhao Pu's shoes as her true brother. Having come so close to raising Mengrong's suspicions just now, Qianli Xun resolved to avoid meeting Shen Jianchen from this day on.

红豆跑到小姐身边，两人一起放风筝，主仆二人的笑声回荡在周围……
Unable to leave and left flying a kite on her own, she decided to face it head-on—she could get a kite aloft by herself. Setting it on the grass, she unspooled a stretch of line, preparing to run backward and let the wind catch it.

听到笑声，申简辰向千里寻看去，她手中的风筝已飞得老高。
"Miss, let me help," Hongdou spoke up beside her.

申简辰有莫名失落感，本想故意冷落她，让她体会到被他冷落后的孤寂，从心里后悔拒绝他。可她即使没有他依然那么开心，反而令他开心不起来。
To someone bracing to stand alone, that quiet offer brought real warmth. "Hold the kite up and run that way."

“梦容，我不想再放风筝，文渊在那边，我过去与他聊聊。”
Following her lead, Hongdou lifted the kite and sprinted across the grass while Qianli Xun fed out the line bit by bit. In seconds, the kite leaped from Hongdou's hands and climbed high into the sky.

没等梦容答应，申简辰已放手，向乔文仙等人的方向走去。
"Miss! It is flying! Wonderful!" Hongdou clapped and cheered.

哥哥的表现与往日不同，到底怎么不同，乔梦容又说不出所以然。因忧心哥哥，手上的劲儿没跟上，已放到空中的风筝渐渐落下。
"Come over here, Hongdou! Fly it with me!"

“哥哥！风筝落下了！”
"Yes!" Hongdou dashed back to her side, and mistress and maid laughed together as the kite danced above them.

申简辰听到梦容的呼唤，假装没有听到，继续向前走。他有种不想掩饰下去的冲动，甚至想要找适合的时机向梦容坦白真情。
Hearing her laughter, Shen Jianchen glanced over and saw her kite soaring high. An odd hollowness settled in his chest. He had meant to give her the cold shoulder, make her feel the sting of being left out and regret turning him away. Instead, she looked happier than ever without him—which only soured his own mood.

哥哥第一次不理她，乔梦容不知如何是好。
"Mengrong, I am tired of flying kites. Wenyuan is over there; I will go chat with him."

看到郡主站在原地，不知所措，喜兰猜到原因，上前帮王爷解释：“郡主不用担忧，王爷肯定是没听到，不如等王爷与各位公子聊完，您再找王爷问问。”
Without waiting for an answer, Shen Jianchen let go of the spool and strode toward Qiao Wenxian and his friends.

“也许是这样。”乔梦容的声音显然失去一半的力气。
Something about her brother felt different today, though Qiao Mengrong could not pin down what. Distracted by worry, she slackened her grip on the string, and the kite sank back toward the grass.

一切被千里寻看在眼中：“红豆，你继续玩儿，我去看看梦容。”
"Brother! The kite is falling!"

千里寻放开风筝线，来到梦容身边：“怎么？仪王不在，你连风筝都不会放？我来帮你。”
Shen Jianchen heard her call and pretended not to, walking right on. An urge to drop the mask swelled inside him—even to find the right moment to tell Mengrong the whole truth.

“我不想再放风筝，淑真，你陪我在草地上走走。”
Ignored by her brother for the very first time, Qiao Mengrong froze, at a loss.

“好吧，不如我们去那边。”千里寻指指离几位公子较远的方向。
Seeing her mistress stricken, Xilan hurried to smooth it over. "Do not fret, Your Highness. His Highness must not have heard you. Wait until he finishes talking with the young gentlemen and ask him then."

得到乔梦容同意后，二人往远处走去。
"Perhaps so," Qiao Mengrong murmured, half the strength drained from her voice.

喜兰从地上捡起风筝，送到扎风筝处，才小跑着跟上郡主。
Having watched it all unfold, Qianli Xun handed her line off. "Keep playing, Hongdou. I will go check on Mengrong." Walking over, she teased gently, "What is this? Prince Yi steps away and you cannot keep a kite up? Let me help."

乔梦容回头向哥哥去的方向眺望一眼，哥哥已与几位公子坐在一处，应该聊得不错。她回过头，叹口气：“唉。”
"I do not feel like flying kites anymore, Shuzhen. Walk with me across the meadow."

“为何叹气？”
"Alright. Let us head that way." Qianli Xun pointed away from where the gentlemen sat, and once Mengrong agreed, they strolled off together. Xilan gathered the fallen kite, returned it to the kite-maker, and trotted to catch up.

“我刚才喊哥哥，他没理我，不知因何缘故？”
Glancing back once, Qiao Mengrong saw her brother seated comfortably with the young scholars, deep in conversation. Turning forward, she let out a heavy sigh.

千里寻充满自责，只怪今日捶丸与申简辰走得近了些。
"Why the sigh?"

“我当什么事，肯定是仪王没听到你唤他。即使听到，没理你，也没什么，仪王毕竟是男子，男子有男子的想法，总不能时时刻刻围着女子转，再喜欢也不能这样。”
"I called out to Brother just now and he ignored me. I do not know why."

“也许……你说得对，我以后还是注意些，不要老缠着哥哥，时间长了，他肯定会烦我，毕竟现在的哥哥跟以前不一样。”
Guilt stabbed Qianli Xun—she blamed herself for getting too close to Shen Jianchen during the chuiwan match. "Here I thought it was something serious! He must not have heard you. Even if he did, it is nothing to fuss over. Prince Yi is a man, with a man's own thoughts. He cannot orbit a woman every single second, no matter how fond of her he is."

千里寻不知如何搭话，真正的仪王已死，梦容实在可怜。可站在申简辰的角度，硬让他去爱并不爱的人，对他来讲无疑也是一种折磨。
"Maybe... you are right. I should be more careful from now on and stop clinging to Brother. Keep it up too long and he is bound to grow sick of me. After all, the brother now is not the same as before."

为何人无论怎么活着都要面对无奈的生活？哪怕申简辰穿越成为仪王也是如此。
Qianli Xun had no words to answer. The real Prince Yi was dead, leaving Mengrong in a heartbreaking spot—yet forcing Shen Jianchen to love someone he did not love was its own cruelty. Why did life force everyone into impossible corners, even after transmigrating into a prince?

“淑真，你为何不说话？”
"Why so quiet, Shuzhen?"

“哦，我是在想你以前怎样便怎样，没有必要故意改变，仪王也会不适应。”
"Oh, I was just thinking you should stay just as you have always been. No need to force yourself to change, or Prince Yi will find it strange."

“好吧，待会儿哥哥来找我，我再当面问他为何不理我。”
"Alright. When Brother comes looking for me later, I will ask him face-to-face why he ignored me."

“这就对嘛，问清楚，也就没有误会。”
"That is the spirit. Ask straight out and clear up any misunderstanding."

传来一阵烧烤的味道，乔梦容四下瞅瞅：“哪里传来的味道？真好闻。”
A whiff of sizzling roast meat drifted over on the breeze. Qiao Mengrong looked around. "Where is that smell coming from? It smells delicious."

千里寻仔细闻闻，想起在西湖时碰到陈浩宇吃烤肉的情景：“如果我没猜错，肯定是陈浩宇带人在周围吃烤肉，我们还是离他远些。”
Sniffing the air, Qianli Xun recalled catching Chen Haoyu roasting meat by West Lake. "If I am not mistaken, Chen Haoyu has dragged his buddies out here for barbecue. Let us steer clear of him."
