# 第42章 逛街寻人聚香楼

一大早，在幽林别居用过早饭，申简辰与乔文仙乘坐马车来到临安闹市。

Early in the morning, after breakfast at Youlin Villa, Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian took a carriage to Lin'an's bustling market district.

---

南双在街市入口处找好地方，把马车栓了，那里有人专门负责看管，收取些许小费，养家糊口。

Nanshuang found a spot at the entrance of the market and tied up the carriage. There were people there specifically in charge of watching carriages, collecting small tips to make a living.

---

能出来逛街，最高兴的莫过于白风。他与南双跟在王爷和乔公子身后，享受着街市的繁华。

The happiest about being able to go out shopping was none other than Baifeng. He and Nanshuang followed behind the prince and Young Master Qiao, enjoying the prosperity of the market.

---

"临安城虽繁华，可比不上汴梁，跟汴梁街市卖的东西也不大一样。"

"Though Lin'an is bustling, it can't compare to Bianliang. The things sold in Lin'an's markets aren't quite the same as in Bianliang either."

---

南双强调道："你懂不懂？这叫地方特色！如果临安跟汴梁一样，还有什么意思？"

Nanshuang emphasized, "Do you understand or not? This is called local character! If Lin'an were the same as Bianliang, what would be the point?"

---

王爷与乔公子走进一家茶馆，他们也尾随而入。

The prince and Young Master Qiao walked into a teahouse, and they followed in behind.

---

伙计见有人来，立即迎上去："二位公子，里边请！"

Seeing customers arrive, the attendant immediately came forward: "Two young gentlemen, please come in!"

---

大家挑张桌子坐下，乔文仙要了壶煮好的茶。

Everyone chose a table and sat down. Qiao Wenxian ordered a pot of brewed tea.

---

白风一直观察王爷，他边饮茶边巡视四周，像是找人一般。白风觉得今日的王爷跟以往不同，要不怎么出门前一再嘱咐他不能称呼王爷，而要叫"申公子"。

Baifeng had been observing the prince. As he drank tea, he scanned the surroundings, as if looking for someone. Baifeng felt today's prince was different from usual—otherwise, why would he repeatedly instruct him before leaving not to call him "Your Highness," but instead "Young Master Shen"?

---

白风问："公子，您是不是想找人？"

Baifeng asked, "Young Master, are you looking for someone?"

---

白风都能看得出，何况文渊？申简辰掩饰道："喝你的茶，怎么那么多废话？"

If even Baifeng could see it, what about Wenyuan? Shen Jianchen covered it up: "Drink your tea. Why so much nonsense?"

---

茶喝到一大半儿，申简辰不想在茶馆待下去，要求文渊带他去酒肆。

When they'd drunk more than half the tea, Shen Jianchen didn't want to stay at the teahouse any longer and asked Wenyuan to take him to a tavern.

---

来到一家酒肆，文渊要了一小壶皇都春："皇都春是临安名酒，申公子品尝后，绝对会喜欢上。"

Arriving at a tavern, Wenyuan ordered a small pot of Huangdu Chun: "Huangdu Chun is Lin'an's famous wine. After Young Master Shen tastes it, you'll absolutely love it."

---

申简辰并未搭话，专注地观察四周。

Shen Jianchen didn't respond, focusing on observing the surroundings.

---

乔文仙不满道："申公子如此表现，我也会认为你是寻人？到底找谁？能不能告诉我？"

Qiao Wenxian said dissatisfied, "Acting like this, Young Master Shen, I'd also think you're looking for someone. Who exactly are you looking for? Can you tell me?"

---

申简辰不知如何将千里寻之事告诉文渊，说了他也不信，还会劝他不要辜负佳和郡主。

Shen Jianchen didn't know how to tell Wenyuan about Qianli Xun. Even if he told him, he wouldn't believe it, and he'd just advise him not to let Princess Jiahé down.

---

从幽林别居出发时，乔梦容要求跟他一起逛街。考虑到要逛整整一日，她一个弱女子在身边，必然让人顾虑多多。于是拒绝道："今日事多，又累，带上你极其不便，改日我还会逛街，到时定会带上你。"

When they were about to leave Youlin Villa, Qiao Mengrong had asked to go shopping with him. Considering they'd be out for a whole day, having a delicate woman like her around would surely cause many concerns. So he refused: "Today there's a lot to do, and it'll be tiring. It's very inconvenient to bring you along. Another day I'll go shopping again, and I'll definitely take you then."

---

他见乔梦容垂下眼帘，似有失望之色："哥哥早去早回。"

He saw Qiao Mengrong lower her eyelids, seemingly disappointed: "Brother, go early and come back early."

---

申简辰答应着，不知不觉中他面对乔梦容总会生出愧疚之感来。

Shen Jianchen agreed. Unconsciously, whenever he faced Qiao Mengrong, he'd always feel a sense of guilt.

---

乔文仙继续问："想什么呢？"

Qiao Wenxian continued asking, "What are you thinking about?"

---

申简辰不再回忆，应对道："寻什么人？我第一次来临安，只认识你。之所以四处瞧，不过是刚到一个地方，好奇而已。"

Shen Jianchen stopped recalling and responded, "Looking for someone? It's my first time in Lin'an—I only know you. The reason I'm looking around is just curiosity about a new place, that's all."

---

乔文仙凑近："看你刚才那样子，像是在寻多年未见的心上人，实在古怪。"

Qiao Wenxian leaned closer: "The way you were just now, it was like you were looking for a lover you haven't seen in years. It's really strange."

---

申简辰掩饰道："别胡乱猜测，喝酒。"

Shen Jianchen covered it up: "Don't make wild guesses. Drink."

---

他端起酒杯，品了一口皇都春，丰满醇厚，回味悠长："酒不错，白风，再给我斟满。"

He picked up his wine cup, tasted a sip of Huangdu Chun—full-bodied and mellow, with a long aftertaste: "The wine is good. Baifeng, fill it up again."

---

白风拿起酒壶替王爷斟满酒。

Baifeng picked up the wine pot and filled the prince's cup.

---

也许是美酒的作用，申简辰忽然之间想明白许多，世上之事大多是有心栽花花不发，无心插柳柳成荫。他极尽双目盯着每个人看，不仅令身边人感到奇怪，还不一定能盯出千里寻来。不如放松心情，只要两人有缘，总能遇上。

Perhaps it was the effect of the fine wine, but Shen Jianchen suddenly figured many things out. Most things in this world are like "flowers planted with care may never bloom, while a willow twig planted by chance grows into a shade-giving tree." Staring at every person he saw not only made those around him feel strange but also wouldn't necessarily help him find Qianli Xun. Better to relax. As long as the two of them had fate, they'd meet eventually.

---

一小壶酒对申简辰来讲不算什么，再说还有文渊与白风、南双分享，很快喝完，没有丝毫感觉。

A small pot of wine was nothing for Shen Jianchen. Besides, with Wenyuan, Baifeng, and Nanshuang sharing it, it was quickly finished without any effect on him.

---

乔文仙本想再要一壶，被申简辰阻止："酒肆一游到此为止，去街上逛逛，午饭找家饭庄解决。"

Qiao Wenxian wanted to order another pot, but Shen Jianchen stopped him: "The tavern visit ends here. Let's go stroll the streets and find a restaurant for lunch."

---

"好主意，我们出酒肆一直向北走，边走边逛，等又累又饿时，正好到达聚香楼。"

"Good idea. We'll exit the tavern and walk straight north, strolling as we go. By the time we're tired and hungry, we'll arrive right at Juxiang Restaurant."

---

乔文仙的建议正合申简辰之意。

Qiao Wenxian's suggestion suited Shen Jianchen perfectly.

---

大家走出酒肆，安心逛街，由南到北，一路走去。

Everyone left the tavern and leisurely strolled the streets, going from south to north.

---

白风与南双逛得不亦乐乎，见什么都开心。两位公子则边逛边聊，申简辰依然不由自主注视街上来来往往的人群。

Baifeng and Nanshuang enjoyed their shopping immensely, happy at everything they saw. The two young gentlemen strolled and chatted, while Shen Jianchen still couldn't help but watch the crowds coming and going on the street.

---

很快他意识到自己很傻，他穿越而来容貌已变，千里寻如若也穿越而来，容貌定与以前不同，他只顾通过外表来寻，实在可笑。想到这些申简辰不由叹了口气："唉！"

Soon he realized he was being foolish. He'd transmigrated and his appearance had changed. If Qianli Xun had also transmigrated, her appearance would definitely be different from before. Just looking for her by appearance was truly ridiculous. Thinking of this, Shen Jianchen couldn't help but sigh: "Sigh!"

---

"申公子为何叹气？"

"Why is Young Master Shen sighing?"

---

"慨叹人生无常。"

"Sighing at life's impermanence."

---

"如此繁华的街市，竟能让你产生人生无常之感？"

"In such a bustling market, you actually feel life's impermanence?"

---

"越是繁华，越容易让人想到繁华退尽的落寞感。"

"The more prosperous it is, the easier it makes one think of the loneliness when prosperity fades."

---

乔文仙并不理解仪王为何会有此种感慨，他更关心聚香楼是否已到。一路逛来，肚子开始叫唤，必须添些食物："再往前走一段，就到聚香楼。"

Qiao Wenxian didn't understand why Prince Yi would have such feelings. He was more concerned with whether they'd reached Juxiang Restaurant yet. After strolling all this way, his stomach was starting to grumble—he needed some food: "A bit further ahead, and we'll be at Juxiang Restaurant."

---

白风问："乔公子，聚香楼的饭菜可香？"

Baifeng asked, "Young Master Qiao, is the food at Juxiang Restaurant delicious?"

---

没等乔文仙回答，南双说："聚香楼，聚香楼，饭菜能不香吗？它可是临安最有名的饭庄，我家公子是那里的常客。"

Before Qiao Wenxian could answer, Nanshuang said, "Juxiang Restaurant, Juxiang Restaurant—how could the food not be delicious? It's Lin'an's most famous restaurant. Our young master is a regular there."

---

白风反问道："难道比汴梁矾楼的美味佳肴还好吃？"

Baifeng countered, "Is it even better than the delicious food at Bianliang's Fan Restaurant?"

---

南双没去过汴梁，无言以对。

Nanshuang had never been to Bianliang and had no reply.

---

乔文仙回道："各具特色，难道你去矾楼吃过？"

Qiao Wenxian answered, "Each has its own specialties. Have you been to Fan Restaurant?"

---

白风说："那倒没有，在汴梁老听人说矾楼的饭菜有多好吃，就记下了。"

Baifeng said, "Not exactly. In Bianliang, I always heard people say how good the food at Fan Restaurant was, so I remembered it."

---

南双批评道："没去过还问？想炫耀自己是汴梁来的？告诉你，我虽没去过汴梁，可我在聚香楼吃过好几回，都是沾了公子的光，绝对好吃。"

Nanshuang criticized, "You haven't been there and you're asking? Trying to show off that you're from Bianliang? Let me tell you, though I haven't been to Bianliang, I've eaten at Juxiang Restaurant several times, all thanks to our young master. It's absolutely delicious."

---

白风的哈喇子已在口中打转，靠近王爷："公子，一会儿能不能也让我尝尝聚香楼的饭菜？"

Baifeng's saliva was already pooling in his mouth. He leaned close to the prince: "Young Master, can you let me taste the food at Juxiang Restaurant later?"

---

申简辰笑道："放心，让你吃个够。"

Shen Jianchen smiled: "Don't worry. I'll let you eat your fill."

---

四人说着笑着，已来到聚香楼门外。门口站着的伙计上前招呼："乔公子来了，好久不见。"转头朝里面喊："乔公子到！"

The four of them chatting and laughing, they'd already arrived outside Juxiang Restaurant. The attendant standing at the door came forward to greet them: "Young Master Qiao is here! Long time no see." He turned and called inside: "Young Master Qiao has arrived!"

---

立刻从里面走来一个伙计："乔公子，里面请，二楼的雅间随时给您预备着。"

Immediately an attendant came from inside: "Young Master Qiao, please come in. The private room on the second floor is always ready for you."

---

申简辰跟着乔文仙走进去，饭庄内很是宽敞。一楼是散客用饭之地，大约摆放了二十来张桌子，客流量不小，甚是热闹；二楼是雅间，专为常客准备，每个雅间都有其固定客户。

Shen Jianchen followed Qiao Wenxian inside. The restaurant was quite spacious. The first floor was for regular customers, with about twenty tables arranged. The customer flow was considerable, quite lively. The second floor had private rooms, specially prepared for regular customers, each room with its fixed clientele.

---

申简辰不想去雅间，只因雅间之内无法看到形形色色的人。他认为，如果千里寻出现，定会有征兆。躲在雅间之内，不跟外人接触，又如何能觉察到征兆？

Shen Jianchen didn't want to go to the private room, precisely because he couldn't see all kinds of people from inside a private room. He believed that if Qianli Xun appeared, there would definitely be signs. Hiding in a private room, not contacting outsiders, how could he detect those signs?

---

"文渊，我不想去雅间。"

"Wenyuan, I don't want to go to the private room."

---

"雅间环境甚好，安静，不受人打扰，你我可自由畅聊。"

"The private room has a great environment—quiet, undisturbed. You and I can chat freely."

---

"刚来临安，想多接触些人，感受一下风土人情。"

"I just arrived in Lin'an. I want to meet more people and experience the local customs."

---

"这是饭庄，哪里有什么风土人情？"

"This is a restaurant. Where are the local customs?"

---

"怎么没有？吃饭的人一颦一笑，一举一动都与汴梁人不同，这便是风土人情。"

"How is there not? The diners' every frown and smile, every movement is different from people in Bianliang. This is local customs."

---

乔文仙只能同意："好吧，谁让你是客，顺着你的意思。"

Qiao Wenxian could only agree: "Fine. You're the guest, so I'll go along with your wishes."

---

伙计在一楼找了一张较偏僻的桌子，安排他们坐下。

The attendant found a relatively secluded table on the first floor and arranged for them to sit.

---

乔文仙点了：宋嫂鱼羹，东坡肉、龙井虾仁、杭菊鸡丝、干炸响铃五道硬菜，又点了几道清爽的素菜，要了一壶西湖龙井。

Qiao Wenxian ordered: Sister Song's Fish Broth, Dongpo Pork, Longjing Shrimp, Hangzhou Chrysanthemum Shredded Chicken, and Deep-fried Tofu Skin Rolls—five main dishes. He also ordered a few refreshing vegetarian dishes and a pot of West Lake Longjing Tea.

---

白风与南双听着菜名口水已忍不住流了出来，只等菜上来后大饱口福。

Listening to the dish names, Baifeng and Nanshuang couldn't help but drool, just waiting for the food to arrive so they could feast to their heart's content.

---

申简辰环顾四周，离他们较远的桌子旁坐着一人，是位比他稍年长的男子，身强体壮，边享用美食，边朝这边瞧来。因离得远，无法通过目光读出对方心中所想。

Shen Jianchen looked around. At a table relatively far from them sat a man, slightly older than him, strong and sturdy. As he enjoyed his meal, he kept glancing over. Because of the distance, he couldn't read what the other person was thinking through his gaze.

---

"文渊，那人一直看向我们，是不是你认识的人？"

"Wenyuan, that person keeps looking at us. Is he someone you know?"

---

乔文仙抬头看去，冷笑道："哼！此人正是幽栖居士的丈夫，名叫林觉，现任临安练兵官，以后你可是他的上司。"

Qiao Wenxian looked up and sneered: "Hmph! This person is precisely Youqi Jushi's husband, named Lin Jue, currently Lin'an's Military Training Officer. In the future, you'll be his superior."

---

申简辰调侃道："你刚回临安，还没见朱淑真本人，倒先偶遇她的夫君，有何感慨？"

Shen Jianchen teased, "You just returned to Lin'an and haven't even seen Zhu Shuzhen herself, yet you've already run into her husband. What are your feelings?"

---

"什么感慨都没有，只想跟他打一架。"

"No feelings at all. I just want to fight him."

---

"他老老实实吃饭，你去跟人家打一架，有失身份，再说你们两个的身板一比较，明显你不是他的对手，估计会被他三拳两脚打趴下。"

"He's eating peacefully. You going to fight him would be beneath your dignity. Besides, comparing your builds, you're obviously no match for him. You'd probably be knocked down by his three punches and two kicks."

---

"喂！你到底站哪一边的？说话都不向着我！"

"Hey! Whose side are you on anyway? You're not even on my side when talking!"

---

"我当然是你这边的，怕你一时冲动干下傻事。"

"Of course I'm on your side. I'm afraid you'll do something stupid in a moment of impulse."

---

饭菜已做好，几个伙计过来将饭菜摆了，也把泡好的西湖龙井放在桌上。

The food was ready. Several attendants came and arranged the dishes, also placing the brewed West Lake Longjing Tea on the table.

---

白风站起来，主动给王爷和乔公子把茶盏满上，也给南双与自己满上，坐下直盯着菜肴。

Baifeng stood up and proactively filled the tea bowls for the prince and Young Master Qiao, also filling Nanshuang's and his own. He sat down and stared straight at the dishes.

---

申简辰看到他那谗样儿，说道："都动筷子吧。"

Seeing his eager expression, Shen Jianchen said, "Everyone, start eating."

---

大家吃了起来，南双与白风吃得尤其快。

Everyone started eating. Nanshuang and Baifeng ate especially fast.

---

申简辰看了直想笑："你们两个慢些，没人跟你们抢。"

Shen Jianchen wanted to laugh watching them: "You two slow down. No one's competing with you."

---

林觉到朱家提亲之前，已知乔文仙对朱淑真心怀不轨。成亲后，又听说乔文仙因伤心离开临安，没想到这么快回来。难道是听说他与朱淑真和离之事？林觉越想越不爽。本来今日心情挺好，出来享用美食，却碰到讨厌之人。

Before Lin Jue went to the Zhu family to propose, he already knew Qiao Wenxian had improper feelings for Zhu Shuzhen. After marrying her, he heard Qiao Wenxian had left Lin'an in sadness. He didn't expect him to return so quickly. Could it be that he heard about his divorce from Zhu Shuzhen? The more Lin Jue thought about it, the more upset he became. He'd been in a good mood today, coming out to enjoy fine food, yet he ran into someone he hated.

---

朱淑真跑到乡下，让林觉丢尽脸面，如若她借口去乡下，偷偷与乔文仙行苟且之事，他岂不是更没面子？乔文仙早不出现，晚不出现，偏偏在朱淑真去乡下时出现，不是为了见她又是什么？

Zhu Shuzhen running off to the countryside had made Lin Jue lose all face. If she used the excuse of going to the countryside to secretly have an illicit affair with Qiao Wenxian, wouldn't he lose even more face? Qiao Wenxian didn't appear early or late, but precisely when Zhu Shuzhen went to the countryside. If not to see her, then what?

---

林觉想到这里，将酒杯重重放下，招呼伙计再拿一个酒杯。伙计将酒杯放好后，林觉把两个酒杯斟满，端起来，起身走向乔文仙……

Thinking of this, Lin Jue slammed his wine cup down and called for the attendant to bring another cup. After the attendant placed it, Lin Jue filled both cups, picked them up, stood, and walked toward Qiao Wenxian...

---

申简辰夹一块东坡肉刚放进嘴里，瞧见林觉走过来，用胳膊肘顶了顶文渊："他过来了。"

Shen Jianchen had just picked up a piece of Dongpo Pork and put it in his mouth when he saw Lin Jue coming over. He nudged Wenyuan with his elbow: "He's coming."

---

乔文仙正用筷子夹虾仁，抬头看去，林觉已站在面前。他放下筷子，与林觉四目相对。情敌相见，虽谈不上分外眼红，气氛却不由紧张起来。

Qiao Wenxian was using chopsticks to pick up shrimp when he looked up and saw Lin Jue already standing in front of him. He put down his chopsticks and met Lin Jue's gaze. When rivals in love meet, though not exactly seeing red, the atmosphere inevitably tensed.

---

申简辰对白风说："你们两个没看到吗？有人找文渊，各端两盘菜，去那边吃。"

Shen Jianchen said to Baifeng, "Don't you two see? Someone's looking for Wenyuan. Each take two plates of food and go eat over there."

---

白风与南双每人挑了两盘肉菜，坐到旁边的桌子跟前大嚼大咽起来。

Baifeng and Nanshuang each picked two plates of meat dishes and sat at a nearby table, devouring their food.

---

林觉坐下，把一杯酒放在乔文仙面前："乔公子何时回到临安？"

Lin Jue sat down and placed a cup of wine in front of Qiao Wenxian: "When did Young Master Qiao return to Lin'an?"

---

"我何时回到临安，应该不必向练兵官汇报吧？"乔文仙看出林觉来者不善，不想在气势上输给对方。

"When I returned to Lin'an shouldn't need to be reported to the Military Training Officer, should it?" Qiao Wenxian could see Lin Jue meant ill and didn't want to lose the upper hand in presence.

---

林觉端起手中的酒杯："你若是男人，跟我喝几杯。"

Lin Jue picked up the wine cup in his hand: "If you're a man, drink a few cups with me."

---

乔文仙说："六一居士曾写过两句诗：酒逢知己千杯少，话不投机半句多。最能表达我此时的心境，练兵官对乔某来讲正是'话不投机半句多'。"

Qiao Wenxian said, "Liu Yi Ju Shi once wrote two lines of poetry: 'A thousand cups of wine are too few when drinking with a bosom friend; half a sentence is too much when talking to someone at cross purposes.' This best expresses my state of mind right now. For me, the Military Training Officer is precisely 'half a sentence is too much.'"

---

欧阳修的诗句着实气到林觉，只听"啪"的一声，他将手中的酒杯重重放下，大半杯酒洒在桌上。

Ouyang Xiu's poem lines truly angered Lin Jue. With a "snap," he slammed the wine cup in his hand down, spilling most of the wine on the table.

---

"别以为你会几句文绉绉的诗词，就能骗过所有人，只有我知道，你道貌岸然，内心龌龊，觊觎别人的娘子，等有一日，我定会把你的面具撕下来，让所有人看清江南才子的真实面目。"

"Don't think just because you know a few literary poems you can fool everyone. Only I know that you're hypocritical, inwardly filthy, coveting another man's wife. One day, I'll definitely tear off your mask and let everyone see the true face of the 'Talented Scholar of Jiangnan.'"

---

乔文仙被气坏，站起来："你血口喷人！"

Qiao Wenxian was furious and stood up: "You're slandering me!"

---

林觉也站起来，一把拽住乔文仙的衣领："老实告诉我，你现在跑回来是不是到乡下跟她厮混，行苟且之事？"

Lin Jue also stood up and grabbed Qiao Wenxian by the collar: "Tell me honestly. Did you run back now to go to the countryside and have an illicit affair with her?"

---

乔文仙不想解释："凭什么告诉你？"

Qiao Wenxian didn't want to explain: "Why should I tell you?"

---

林觉更气愤，举起一只拳头打向乔文仙……

Lin Jue was even more furious and raised a fist to strike Qiao Wenxian...

---

白风与南双已没有心情享用美食，尤其是南双，替自家公子担心不已。

Baifeng and Nanshuang no longer had the mood to enjoy their food, especially Nanshuang, who was extremely worried about his young master.

---

当林觉举拳头要打时，申简辰向白风使了使眼色。白风迅速上前抓住林觉的拳头，他刚练申拳，手的力度还不够大，挡一下林觉倒不在话下。

When Lin Jue raised his fist to strike, Shen Jianchen gave Baifeng a look. Baifeng quickly stepped forward and caught Lin Jue's fist. He'd just started practicing Shen Fist, so his hand strength wasn't great yet, but blocking Lin Jue was no problem.

---

林觉被白风挡住，拳头无法砸下去，意识到有高手在此，于是松开乔文仙，瞅了一眼申简辰。刚才那小子定是他调教出来，若真动起手来，自己势单力薄，定打不过他们。

Blocked by Baifeng, Lin Jue couldn't bring his fist down. Realizing there was an expert present, he let go of Qiao Wenxian and glanced at Shen Jianchen. That kid must have been trained by him. If they really fought, he'd be outnumbered and definitely couldn't beat them.

---

白风见林觉松开乔公子，也撤回手，拉着南双继续坐回桌旁享用美食。

Seeing Lin Jue let go of Young Master Qiao, Baifeng also withdrew his hand and pulled Nanshuang back to continue enjoying their food at the table.

---

林觉说："乔文仙，告诉你一个好消息和一个坏消息，你想听哪个？"

Lin Jue said, "Qiao Wenxian, let me tell you a good piece of news and a bad piece of news. Which do you want to hear?"

---

乔文仙坐下，并未理会。

Qiao Wenxian sat down and didn't respond.

---

"好消息就是，我大哥和二哥劝我与朱淑真和离，我本也想通，放她离开林家；坏消息就是，看到你之后，我又不想放她离开，和离之事遥遥无期，你就好好苦等吧，哈哈……！"林觉说完，抓起一只酒杯，笑着回到自己的桌前，坐下将酒斟满，举起来大声说，"乔文仙！跟我干了这杯！"林觉一饮而尽，继续吃起来。

"The good news is, my eldest brother and second brother advised me to divorce Zhu Shuzhen. I'd also thought it through and was going to let her leave the Lin family. The bad news is, after seeing you, I don't want to let her go anymore. The divorce is indefinitely postponed. You can just wait bitterly, hahaha!" After finishing, Lin Jue grabbed a wine cup, smiling as he returned to his table. He sat down, filled his cup, raised it, and said loudly, "Qiao Wenxian! Drink this cup with me!" Lin Jue drank it all in one gulp and continued eating.

---

一楼其他客人本以为二人会打起来，担心自己被伤到，已做好随时离开的准备。见又平静下来，也都恢复正常。

The other guests on the first floor had thought the two would fight and, worried about getting hurt, had prepared to leave at any moment. Seeing things calm down again, they all returned to normal.
