# Chapter 42: Shopping and Searching for Someone at Juxiang Restaurant

Right after breakfast at Youlin Retreat next morning, Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian rode into Lin'an's market district. At the entrance to the avenues, Nan Shuang hitched their carriage at a guarded livery yard where attendants watched rigs for small change. Thrilled to hit town, Bai Feng trailed behind Prince Yi and Young Master Qiao alongside Nan Shuang, soaking up every sight.

"Lively enough, sure, still can't touch Bianliang! Different stuff on the stalls too," Bai Feng remarked.

Nan Shuang scoffed. "Ever heard of local flavor?! What's the fun if Lin'an copied Bianliang?!"

Following their masters into a busy teahouse, they stepped inside as a waiter hurried up beaming: "Right inside, young masters!" Taking a table, Qiao Wenxian ordered a pot of brewed tea.

Eyeing his master sip tea while sweeping sharp eyes over every single face in the room, Bai Feng thought today's outing felt odd—especially His Highness warning him before leaving never to call him Prince Yi, only "Young Master Shen."

"Looking for somebody, Young Master?" Bai Feng blurted out.

If even Bai Feng noticed, Wenyuan couldn't miss it! Shen Jianchen covered brusque: "Drink your tea and cut the chatter."

Half a pot down, restless already, Shen Jianchen steered Wenyuan out to a wine tavern. Inside, Qiao Wenxian ordered a small flagon of Capital Spring: "Lin'an's signature vintage, Young Master Shen—one sip and you'll love it!"

Barely listening, Shen Jianchen scanned every booth and diner. Irritated now, Qiao Wenxian called him out: "Acting like that, even I know you're hunting someone! Who is it—can't you tell me?"

How could he explain Qianli Xun? Wenyuan wouldn't buy transmigration and would only lecture him about breaking Princess Jiahe's heart! Back at Youlin Retreat that morning, Qiao Mengrong had begged to come along; dreading dragging a delicate lady through a full day's foot search, he had brushed her off: "Long, exhausting errands today—way too tiring for you! Next stroll I'll bring you." Her eyes had dropped with quiet hurt—"Come home early, Brother"—and guilt had pricked him once more, just as it did every time he faced Mengrong.

"Earth to Shen—what are you spacing out on?" Qiao Wenxian poked.

Snapping back, Shen Jianchen shrugged. "Searching for who? First time in Lin'an, you're the only soul I know! Just curious gawking at a new city."

Leaning close, Qiao Wenxian squinted. "Scanning every corner like hunting a long-lost sweetheart—mighty suspicious!"

"Quit imagining things—drink!" Lifting his cup, he tasted Capital Spring: rich, mellow, lingering sweet. "Great wine! Fill me up, Bai Feng!" Bai Feng poured straight to the brim.

Maybe the fine wine cleared his head: chasing fate too hard only made it slip through your fingers, whereas unexpected wonders bloomed when you relaxed! Hawkeyeing every stranger just spooked his companions and wouldn't spot Qianli Xun anyway; better chill out and trust destiny!

Split four ways among him, Wenyuan, Bai Feng, and Nan Shuang, the small flagon vanished fast without leaving a buzz. Before Qiao Wenxian could order a second pot, Shen Jianchen waved him off: "Enough tavern. Let's walk the streets and hit a restaurant for lunch."

"Perfect! Stroll straight north out of here, browse shops along the way, and right when hunger hits we land smack at Juxiang Tower!"

Music to Shen Jianchen's ears. Leaving the tavern, they strolled north through lively avenues. Bai Feng and Nan Shuang had a blast pointing at every novelty while the two gentlemen chatted, though Shen Jianchen's eyes still drifted across passing faces by habit. Then he almost smacked his own forehead: idiot! Crossing over had changed his face—if Qianli Xun crossed over too, she'd wear someone else's face! Hunting by looks was hopeless! A quiet sigh slipped out.

"Why the sigh, Young Master Shen?"

"Just thinking how unpredictable life is."

"Lively market streets make you brood over fleeting life?"

"Brighter the boom, deeper the hush once the party ends."

Baffled by the prince's sudden gloom—and more focused on his own growling stomach—Qiao Wenxian pointed ahead. "Just a bit further to Juxiang Tower!"

"Food actually good at Juxiang Tower, Young Master Qiao?" Bai Feng piped up.

Before Wenyuan spoke, Nan Shuang bragged, "Is Juxiang Tower good?! Top restaurant in all of Lin'an—my master eats there nonstop!"

"Better than Fan Tower back in Bianliang?" Bai Feng challenged. Never having been to Bianliang, Nan Shuang went mute.

"Each has its own glory," Qiao Wenxian mediated with a smile. "Ever actually eaten at Fan Tower, Bai Feng?"

"Well, no... just heard folks in Bianliang raving about Fan Tower all day, so it stuck!"

Nan Shuang scoffed. "Never even ate there and still bragging?! Showing off you're from Bianliang? Well, never been to Bianliang myself, yet I've feasted at Juxiang Tower plenty thanks to my master—guaranteed heavenly!"

Mouth already watering, Bai Feng sidled up to Prince Yi. "Young Master, do I get to try Juxiang Tower too?"

Shen Jianchen chuckled. "Relax—eat till you burst!"

Bantering along, they reached Juxiang Tower. A doorman darted out beaming: "Young Master Qiao! Long time no see!" Turning indoors he hollered, "Young Master Qiao has arrived!" At once a waiter hurried forward: "Welcome inside, Young Master Qiao! Your upstairs private room is always ready!"

Stepping in behind Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen took in the wide ground floor: twenty-odd lively tables packed with walk-in diners, while upstairs housed exclusive private rooms reserved for regulars. Ducking into a closed room upstairs meant seeing nobody—and if fate really brought Qianli Xun near, hiding behind walls might miss every clue!

"Skip the private room, Wenyuan."

"Upstairs is quiet, private, zero interruptions—much nicer for talking!"

"New in Lin'an—want to rub elbows with locals and soak up local flavor!"

"Inside a restaurant? What local flavor?"

"Every gesture and laugh of Lin'an diners differs from Bianliang—that IS local flavor!"

Outvoted, Qiao Wenxian yielded. "Fine, guest's choice—downstairs it is."

The waiter seated them at a tucked-away ground-floor table. Qiao Wenxian ordered five signature feasts—Sister Song's Fish Soup, Dongpo pork, Longjing Shrimp, Hangzhou Chrysanthemum Shredded Chicken, and Crispy Fried Tofu Rolls—plus crisp greens and a pot of West Lake Longjing tea. Just hearing the menu had Bai Feng and Nan Shuang salivating!

Scanning the dining hall, Shen Jianchen noticed a burly man slightly older than himself seated at a far table, eating away while glaring straight toward them, too far to read his thoughts. "Wenyuan, guy over there keeps staring at us. Know him?"

Glancing over, Qiao Wenxian let out a cold snort. "Hmph! That's Youqi Jushi's husband—Lin Jue, Lin'an's Training Officer! Your future subordinate!"

Shen Jianchen smirked playful. "Barely back in Lin'an, haven't even seen Zhu Shuzhen yet, and you run straight into her husband! How's that feel?"

"Feels like punching his lights out!"

"Guy's just sitting there eating; pick a fight and you look petty! Plus look at your frames side by side—three moves and he'd mop the floor with you!"

"Hey, whose side are you even on?!"

"Yours—just keeping you from doing something dumb!"

Hot platters arrived in a wave along with steaming West Lake Longjing tea. Jumping up, Bai Feng poured tea for Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao, Nan Shuang, and himself before locking hungry eyes on the dishes. Laughing at his starving look, Shen Jianchen waved: "Dig in!" Chopsticks flew, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang shoveling like racers. "Slow down, you two—nobody's stealing your plates!" Shen Jianchen chuckled.

Over at his table, Lin Jue's blood boiled. Even before proposing to Zhu Manor he knew Qiao Wenxian coveted Zhu Shuzhen; after the wedding he heard Qiao fled heartbroken, and now here he popped right back in Lin'an! Heard about the divorce mess?! Worse, Zhu Shuzhen had moved out to the countryside humbling Lin Jue's pride—what if "countryside" was a cover to sneak around with Qiao Wenxian?! Showing up right while she was gone—what else could it mean?!

Slamming down his wine cup, Lin Jue hailed a waiter for a second cup, filled both to the brim, rose, and stalked straight toward Qiao Wenxian.

Mid-bite of Dongpo pork, Shen Jianchen nudged Wenyuan's elbow. "Incoming."

Chopsticks hovering over shrimp, Qiao Wenxian looked up straight into Lin Jue's eyes; setting down his chopsticks, he met the glare cold as tension crackled between rivals. Shen Jianchen glanced at Bai Feng: "Take the hint—Wenyuan's got company! Grab two plates each and move to the next table!" Scooping two meat platters apiece, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang hopped one table over and kept chowing down without missing a beat.

Dropping into a chair, Lin Jue slid one cup before Qiao Wenxian. "When did Young Master Qiao stroll back into Lin'an?"

"Didn't realize my travel dates needed reporting to the Training Officer," Qiao Wenxian fired back, refusing to give ground.

Raising his cup, Lin Jue taunted, "Call yourself a man, drink a few with me!"

Cool and cutting, Qiao Wenxian quoted, "Liu Yi Jushi wrote: 'A thousand cups are too few with a bosom friend; half a word is too much when views clash.' [TN: Translator's rendering] Sums up my mood right now—for me, Training Officer, half a word is already too much!"

Ouyang Xiu's barb lit Lin Jue's fuse—slam! His cup crashed onto the table, splashing wine across half the wood. "Think tossing fancy verses fools everybody?! I see straight through your sanctimonious mask—sleazy hypocrite coveting another man's wife! Mark my word, one day I'll rip that mask off and show everyone what a famed Jiangnan scholar really looks like!"

Furious, Qiao Wenxian shot to his feet. "Slanderous garbage!"

Shooting up too, Lin Jue yanked Qiao Wenxian by the collar. "Spill it—did you sneak back here to shack up with her out in the countryside?!"

"Why should I tell you a thing?!"

Rage boiling over, Lin Jue cocked a heavy fist straight at Qiao Wenxian's jaw. Bai Feng and Nan Shuang froze mid-bite, Nan Shuang going pale in terror for his master. At one subtle flick of Shen Jianchen's eyes, Bai Feng shot forward and clamped onto Lin Jue's swinging fist! Fresh to Shen Fist though he was, blocking one raw punch from Lin Jue took zero strain.

Stopped dead mid-swing, Lin Jue realized real fighters sat at this table. Releasing Qiao Wenxian's collar, he shot a sharp glance at Shen Jianchen; this calm stranger had trained that kid, and outnumbered against pros, brawling meant getting flattened. Seeing Lin Jue back off, Bai Feng released his wrist and strolled right back to his plates beside Nan Shuang.

Lin Jue sneered down at Qiao Wenxian. "Got good news and bad news for you, Qiao Wenxian—which first?"

Sitting back down, Qiao Wenxian ignored him cold.

"Good news: my two brothers talked me round to granting Zhu Shuzhen her mutual divorce—I was actually ready to cut her loose! Bad news: seeing your smug face just killed that! Divorce is off forever—enjoy waiting till you rot! Haha...!" Snatching a cup, he strutted grinning back to his own table, poured full, raised it high, and hollered, "Cheers, Qiao Wenxian!" Down went the wine, and back he went to eating.

Around the ground floor, nervous patrons poised to bolt relaxed back into their meals as calm returned.
