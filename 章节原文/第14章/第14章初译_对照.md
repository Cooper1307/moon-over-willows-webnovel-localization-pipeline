## Chapter 14: Chapter 14: Turns Out I'm Rich

千里寻走出后，苦荞已在外面等候，她手里提着灯笼。见小姐出来，迎上去：“我寻思着还需等一会儿，没想到小姐这么快出来。”
Outside waited Kuqiao, lantern in hand. Seeing her mistress emerge, she hurried over. "I figured it'd be a while longer—didn't expect you out so soon, Miss."

千里寻没有搭话，径直往外走。走出正院后，不明去向，只好站住。
Silent, Qianli Xun marched straight out of the Main Courtyard—only to stop dead the moment she cleared the gate, clueless which way led back.

苦荞看出小姐不高兴，她不敢多问，只顾跟着。见小姐停下，知道她找不到回去的路，赶紧走到前面：“小姐只管跟我走。”
Sensing her dark mood, Kuqiao held back questions and trailed quiet behind. Seeing her halt, she stepped ahead. "Just follow me, Miss."

又是一阵穿梭。
Another maze of winding paths.

千里寻没有心情关注朱府夜间幽密的小径，一心盘算着接下来该怎么办？父亲不愿出面，说明朱府没有人能替她出面，和离之事岂不是要以失败告终？难道自己穿越成朱淑真必须要跟林觉过日子不成？
Paying no mind to Zhu Manor's shadowed walkways, Qianli Xun racked her brain for a plan. With her father refusing to show, nobody in Zhu Manor would back her—did that mean the mutual divorce was dead on arrival? Was she really doomed to spend her transmigrated life stuck with Lin Jue?

朱淑真留给后世的信息太少，依据仅存的信息，朱淑真与林觉并未离婚，而是常年分居；后来遇到相爱之人，婚内出轨，因战乱又与相爱之人分开；最后郁郁而终，离开人世时不过四十来岁。
Sparse as historical records on Zhu Shuzhen were, Qianli Xun knew the outline: Zhu Shuzhen never divorced Lin Jue, lived estranged from him for years, later fell in love with another man outside marriage, lost him to war, and died brokenhearted in her forties.

难道她千里寻也要如此度过此生？坚决不！她一定要改变！要从结束这段婚姻开始。
Live out that script? Never! She was rewriting this destiny—starting by ending this marriage!

想着想着，已到朱淑真居住的园子。朱府的路真是奇怪，用心关注时，只觉怎么都走不到目的地，不去关注，反而快了许多。
Before she knew it, they stood back at Zhu Shuzhen's garden. Strange place, Zhu Manor—watch every turn and it felt endless; tune out and you arrived in half the time.

苦荞推开园门，提着灯笼在前面照亮。院子里没有挂一个灯笼，黑漆漆的。想到朱晞颜居住的正院灯火通明，千里寻说：“院子太黑，应该多挂些灯笼。”
Kuqiao pushed the garden gate open, lighting the dark yard with her lantern. Not a single lantern hung outside—pitch black compared to Zhu Xiyan's blazing Main Courtyard. "Too dark in here," Qianli Xun said. "We need more lanterns."

绿桃在屋内听到声音，出来迎接：“小姐回来了，奴婢已把床铺好，您随时可以休息。”
Hearing them, Lutao stepped out to greet her. "Welcome back, Miss. Your bed is all made whenever you're ready to rest."

千里寻问：“绿桃，院子一直是你管理？”
"Lutao, you're in charge of this courtyard?"

“是的，小姐。”
"Yes, Miss."

“明儿找几个丫鬟照着我爹的正院给院子多挂些灯笼，到了晚上点着，也好亮堂些。”
"Tomorrow grab some maids and hang lanterns all around this yard just like Father's Main Courtyard—light them up at night so it isn't so gloomy."

绿桃感到纳闷：“以前院子里挂过灯笼，小姐不喜欢，才让我吩咐下人把灯笼都撤了，您……确定要把灯笼再挂上去？”
Lutao blinked in confusion. "We used to hang lanterns here, Miss, until you said you disliked them and ordered them all taken down. Are you... sure you want them back up?"

“确定！而且十分肯定！”千里寻说着，走进屋子。
"Sure—one hundred percent sure!" Qianli Xun stepped inside.

苦荞把灯笼递给绿桃：“姐姐按照小姐的吩咐去安排吧。”
Kuqiao passed the lantern to Lutao. "Just do as Miss says, Sister."

绿桃接过灯笼，边往出走边小声嘀咕：“听丫鬟们议论，小姐跟以前很不一样，还真是。不管了，主子说怎样就怎样，改天吩咐人把灯笼挂上就是。”
Taking the lantern, Lutao walked off muttering under her breath, "Heard the other maids whispering Miss isn't the same—guess they weren't kidding. Oh well, mistress's orders are orders. I'll have them hung soon."

千里寻在苦荞的伺候下，躺到床上，继续盘算着。就目前情形来看，朱家人十有八九靠不住，只怕哥哥嫂嫂也得听爹的。这一天充满希望而来，瞬间所有希望被那个面无表情的爹给击得粉碎。
With Kuqiao's help, Qianli Xun climbed into bed, still turning options over. The Zhu household looked like a bust—even her brother and sister-in-law probably dared not defy her father. A whole day of high hopes shattered flat by one stone-faced dad.

尽管心里搁着事儿，可千里寻从小是那种头一挨枕头呼呼大睡的人，因此一晚上睡眠质量挺高。
Still, worries or not, Qianli Xun had always been the type to knock out the second her head hit the pillow, and she slept solid through the night.

……
...

当清晨第一声鸡叫响起时，她睁开眼睛，这是她平生第一次被鸡叫醒，甚是新奇。
At the first rooster crow of dawn, her eyes blinked open—first time in her life an actual rooster had been her alarm clock. Kind of novel.

千里寻起身掀开床幔，溜下床，简单披上外衣，打开窗户，一阵清新的空气扑面而来，她深深吸口气：“真好闻，有植物的香味儿。”
Pulling back the bed curtains, she slid out of bed, threw on an outer robe, and pushed open the window. Fresh morning air rushed in; she drew a deep breath. "Smells so clean—like fresh greens!"

她看到一只公鸡站在院墙上，红冠鲜艳夺目，脖子粗壮有力，全身的羽毛黑中略带红色，它伸长脖子，又发出一串清脆的啼叫：“喔喔喔……”
Perched atop the courtyard wall stood a rooster with a bright red comb, sturdy neck, and glossy black feathers glinting red. Stretching its neck, it let out another crisp crow: "Cock-a-doodle-doo!"

苦荞也被吵醒，走出丫鬟房里，从地上捡起一个石子，朝公鸡打去：“让你在这里瞎吵！”公鸡被吓坏，扑棱棱地飞到院墙之外。
Woken too, Kuqiao stepped out of the maids' room, snatched a pebble from the ground, and tossed it at the bird. "Quit making a racket here!" Startled, the rooster flapped over the wall and vanished.

“小姐，我把它赶走了，您再睡会儿。”
"Chased it off, Miss. Go back to sleep for a bit."

“我睡不着，不如起来。”
"Wide awake already. Might as well get up."

苦荞进到屋里，开始为小姐穿衣打扮。
Kuqiao came inside to help her dress and fix her hair.

千里寻问：“朱府怎么会有公鸡？”
"Why does Zhu Manor have a rooster?" Qianli Xun asked.

“有可能是哪个下人养的，也可能是佛堂养的。”
"Could belong to one of the servants—or maybe the Buddhist Shrine."

“佛堂？”千里寻不解。
"Buddhist Shrine?"

苦荞给小姐边整理衣裳边说：“朱府有个佛堂，听说里面住着一位老祖宗，都一百多岁，可我跟小姐从来没见过，主要是那位老祖宗待在佛堂里，根本不出来。”
Straightening her mistress's collar, Kuqiao explained, "Zhu Manor has a Buddhist Shrine where an Old Ancestor lives—over a hundred years old! Neither you nor I have ever seen her, though, because she never steps out of that shrine."

“我爹也没见过？”
"Not even my father?"

“不知道，反正我从没见老爷去过佛堂。”
"No idea—never once saw Master go near the shrine."

“朱府真是一个深不可测的府邸。不过，你凭什么判断公鸡可能是佛堂养的？”
"This manor is full of mysteries. Wait—why guess the rooster belongs to the Buddhist Shrine?"

“在朱府大家形成一个习惯，对于少见的东西，总会说一句可能是佛堂的，我习惯了，顺口说的。”
"Habit around here! Whenever something odd turns up in Zhu Manor, folks shrug and say it's probably from the Buddhist Shrine. Slipped right out."

“原来如此。”
"Got it."

千里寻没有心情纠结佛堂之事，在苦荞为她整理妆容后，吩咐道：“苦荞，一会儿不必为我做茶，随便弄点儿喝的，我要早些去见哥哥和嫂嫂，问问他们还能不能为我出面。”
Too busy to dwell on shrines, Qianli Xun waited until Kuqiao finished her hair and ordered, "Skip the fancy whisked tea this morning—grab me something simple to drink. I want to go see my brother and sister-in-law early and check if they'll still back me."

苦荞担心道：“难道老爷昨晚拒绝在您和姑爷和离时出面？”
Worry flickered across Kuqiao's face. "Did Master refuse to appear at your divorce meeting last night?"

千里寻点点头。
Qianli Xun nodded.

“小姐还是不要去问，大公子和少奶奶肯定不会出面。”
"Don't bother asking, Miss—Eldest Young Master and Young Madam won't dare step forward."

“跟我爹有关？”尽管千里寻知道是这个原因，还是不由问道。
"Because of Father?" She already knew the answer, yet asked anyway.

苦荞点点头：“朱府历来如此，只要老爷不答应，其他人都不敢违拗，只有干着急的份儿。”
Kuqiao nodded. "Always been that way in Zhu Manor. Once Master says no, nobody dares defy him—all they can do is fret."

“为什么？”千里寻很想知道原因。
"Why?"

“朱家家法甚严，可以说老爷就是家法。”
"Zhu family discipline is ruthless—Master's word is the law."

“哼！什么家法！我看他就是法西斯！”
"Hmph! Family discipline my foot—he's a straight-up Fascist!"

苦荞听不懂什么是法西斯，不一样的小姐总会说些她听不懂的名词儿。
Kuqiao had no idea what a "Fascist" was; her changed mistress tossed out strange words all the time.

千里寻又问：“不知道朱家有没有几个像林家那样的族人？”
Qianli Xun thought of another angle. "Does the Zhu clan have outside relatives in town like the Lin family?"

“有，临安城里住着五六个，都是小姐的叔叔和伯伯。”
"Yes! Five or six of your uncles live right here in Lin'an."

千里寻立刻高兴起来：“真是柳暗花明又一村，今日我前去拜见这些叔叔和伯伯，不相信说不动一位。”
Hope flared bright again. "Just when the road looks blocked, another path opens! Today I'll visit every single uncle—no way I can't talk at least one into coming!"

千里寻觉得空手去显得不诚心，要说动别人替自己出面，需带些贵重的礼物，最好直接塞银子。她摸摸身上，空空如也，刚穿越而来，还没遇到需花钱之事，出林府时，也忘记要些银子带上。
Going empty-handed felt weak; if she wanted elders on her side, lavish gifts—or cold silver—would grease the wheels. Patting her sleeves, she came up empty; fresh off transmigrating, she hadn't needed cash yet and forgot to grab any before leaving Lin Manor.

“苦荞，要不你回林府一趟，问林觉要些银子，他答应给我银子使。”
"Kuqiao, run back to Lin Manor and get some silver from Lin Jue—he promised me spending money."

“小姐要用银子，我可以回林府取，可也没必要问姑爷要。”
"If you need silver, Miss, I can fetch it from Lin Manor, no need to ask Son-in-law."

“什么意思？不问林觉要，问谁要？”
"Wait—if not from Lin Jue, then who?"

“小姐嫁给姑爷时，带过去很多嫁妆，其中不乏田产和铺子，每年收的租子小姐都花不完，姑爷可没小姐有钱。”
"You brought a huge dowry when you married, Miss—landed estates and shops galore! You can't even spend half the annual rent they bring in. Son-in-law isn't nearly as rich as you!"

“什么！”千里寻站起来，“我这么有钱！那我还怕什么！俗话讲嫁汉穿衣吃饭，我穿衣吃饭又不靠他林觉，凭什么还要跟他过日子？这婚必须得离，离的时候还要把我所有嫁妆带走！我真傻，在林府跟他约法三章时，让他给我银子使，他竟然笑着答应，真是个阴险小人！苦荞，用完早饭，你就去林府取银子，多多益善。”
"What?!" Qianli Xun shot to her feet. "I'm loaded?! Then what am I scared of?! People say women marry for clothes and a roof—I don't need Lin Jue for either! Why on earth would I stay married to him? This divorce is happening, and I'm taking every last bit of my dowry with me! What a fool I was making him promise me spending money—no wonder the sly bastard smiled and agreed! Kuqiao, right after breakfast, go fetch silver from Lin Manor—the more the better!"

“好勒。”
"Yes, Miss!"

二人正说着，绿桃带人来将早饭摆上。苦荞陪小姐吃完早饭，整理好书桌上的笔墨纸砚，正要回林府，听到外面传来银杏的声音：“夫人来看小姐。”
Right then Lutao led servants in with breakfast. After eating and watching Kuqiao tidy the study desk, just as Kuqiao prepared to leave for Lin Manor, Yinxing's voice rang from outside: "Madam is here to see Miss."

千里寻对苦荞说：“你先别着急回林府，看我娘前来说些什么，再做定夺。”
"Hold off on Lin Manor," Qianli Xun told Kuqiao. "Let's hear what Mother has to say first."

向夫人走进来，见女儿站在屋里迎接她，上前抓住女儿的手：“淑真，娘一晚上没睡好，你虽跟以前不一样，可还是娘的亲女儿，娘希望你不要怪你爹。”
Madam Xiang walked in, saw her daughter waiting, and clasped her hands. "Shuzhen, Mother barely slept a wink last night. Even changed as you are, you're still my flesh and blood. Please don't hold a grudge against your father."

千里寻说：“如果和离时，娘和哥哥嫂嫂能为我出面，我就不怪爹。”
"If you, Brother, and Sister-in-law back me at the divorce meeting, I won't hold a grudge."

向夫人放开女儿的手，显得十分为难：“你爹只要不同意，一家人没人敢替你出面。”
Letting go of her hands, Madam Xiang sighed in distress. "Once your father says no, nobody in this house dares step forward."

“既如此，娘别嫌我怪他。我爹就是个冷血动物，一点儿不关心女儿的人生幸福，只看重他所谓的清誉，真不明白，我与林觉和离，能影响他多少清誉？”
"Then don't ask me not to resent him! My father is cold-blooded—doesn't care a bit about his daughter's happiness, only his precious reputation! How much reputation can one mutual divorce even cost him?"

向夫人在椅子上坐下：“你坐下，听娘慢慢给你讲。”
Madam Xiang sank into a chair. "Sit down and hear Mother out."

千里寻乖乖坐下：“娘要给我讲什么？”
Qianli Xun sat. "What do you want to tell me?"

向夫人先吩咐苦荞：“去，给我们娘俩做两盏茶来。”
Madam Xiang turned to Kuqiao first. "Go whisk two bowls of tea for my daughter and me."

苦荞答应着，去茶桌前做茶。银杏对做茶很感兴趣，也跟着去茶桌旁凑热闹。
Kuqiao went to the tea table; fascinated by whisked tea, Yinxing wandered over to watch.

向夫人叹口气：“唉，你从小十分聪慧，又喜读书，而且无师自通，十岁便能写诗作词，你爹说是遗传他的才华，便让你频频在诗会上抛头露面，女词人的名头渐渐传遍整个江南。当年我们家与徐家交好，你与徐家公子青梅竹马，两小无猜，也暗生情愫，大人看在眼里，喜在心里，本想结秦晋之好……”
Madam Xiang let out a long sigh. "You were bright from the cradle, loved books, and taught yourself to write poetry and ci by age ten. Proud that you inherited his gifts, your father paraded you at banquet after banquet until your name as a female ci poet spread across all of Jiangnan. Back then we were close to the Xu family. You and Young Master Xu grew up childhood sweethearts, fond of each other, and both families hoped to seal a marital alliance..."

千里寻对朱淑真曾经的感情经历不感兴趣，不想听下去：“娘给我讲这些做什么？跟和离又没有什么关系。”
Uninterested in Zhu Shuzhen's old crush, Qianli Xun cut in, "Why tell me all this, Mother? What does it have to do with my divorce?"

“你要是还承认自己是淑真，就听娘讲下去。”
"If you still call yourself Shuzhen, listen to the end."

千里寻只好耐着性子听下去。
Qianli Xun bit her tongue and listened.

向夫人继续说：“突然有一天，徐家要搬到北边，理由是南边生意不好做，家族所有生意都要迁往北边，至于你与徐公子的姻缘，徐家没有提一个字。徐家搬走后，我与你爹骗你说徐家败落，不得已举家搬迁。再后来，娘参加临安官眷的聚会，偶然听到有人议论，说徐家之所以搬迁，是因为不想让儿子娶你为妻，据说有风水先生给徐家看过风水，告诉徐家若让儿子娶才女，必然家道中落，什么生意都做不成……”
"Out of nowhere, the Xu family announced they were moving north—claiming southern trade had soured and relocating every shop, without breathing a word about your betrothal. After they left, your father and I told you they had fallen on hard times so you wouldn't break down. Later, at a gathering of Lin'an officials' wives, I overheard gossip: the Xus moved because they didn't want their son marrying you! Some geomancer told them marrying a talented woman would ruin their family fortune and sink every venture..."

“一派胡言！”千里寻忍不住为朱淑真鸣不平。
"Complete garbage!" Qianli Xun snapped on Zhu Shuzhen's behalf.

苦荞已将茶做好，与银杏一起端着茶盏给夫人和小姐放在桌上。
Finished with the tea, Kuqiao and Yinxing brought the two bowls over and set them down.

千里寻没有心情品茶，端起茶盏一口气喝完，然后放下茶盏：“娘，您与爹要感到庆幸，多亏没把女儿嫁给徐家。依我看，徐家人见识浅薄，根本不值得结亲家。”
Too fired up to savor it, Qianli Xun drained her bowl in one gulp and slammed it down. "Mother, you and Father should thank your lucky stars you didn't marry me into that house! Narrow-minded fools like the Xus aren't worth being in-laws with!"

向夫人也端起茶盏，品了一小口：“你爹是临安知府，官职不小，徐家得罪不起，才找了搬迁的理由，其实徐家在临安依然有生意，只是全家搬到北边居住而已，后来徐家公子也娶了妻。可怜我女儿一等就是五六年。”向夫人又开始抹眼泪。
Madam Xiang took a small sip. "Your father is Prefect of Lin'an—the Xus dared not offend him openly, so they made up an excuse to leave. They still run businesses here, just live up north now, and their son married someone else. My poor girl waited five or six years for nothing!" Madam Xiang dabbed at fresh tears.

千里寻问：“娘与爹为什么不将实情告诉女儿？”
"Why didn't you and Father tell me the truth?"

向夫人稳定情绪后继续说：“还不是怕你听到受伤？你爹看似对你冷淡，其实也关心你。他后悔让你接触诗词，让你参加诗会，可也阻挡不了，你的名声已传遍江南，身边有志同道合的朋友，其中不乏青年才俊。你爹时常会听到各种风言风语，甚至有污秽之语，他才体会到什么是女子无才便是德。正好林家上门提亲，你爹经过考量，认为林觉与你很不相同，嫁给他才能让你真正明白身为女子应该相夫教子才不负满腹才华。再说，你年纪也不小，不嫁人只怕耽搁了你。”
Composing herself, Madam Xiang went on, "We didn't want to crush your heart! Your father acts cold, yet he cares in his own way. He regretted pushing you into poetry and banquets, yet by then your fame stretched across Jiangnan and you kept company with scholars and young gentlemen. Ugly rumors—even crude slander—reached his ears, making him believe 'a woman's virtue is having no talent.' Right then the Lin family proposed. Your father weighed it and figured someone different like Lin Jue would bring you down to earth to raise a family instead of chasing dreams. Besides, you weren't getting any younger—he feared waiting longer would ruin your chances."

千里寻忍不住说：“谬论！谬论！简直就是谬论！娘现在也看到，朱淑真嫁给林觉只有痛苦，就是我也想跟他离婚。”
"Ridiculous! Utter nonsense!" Qianli Xun burst out. "You can see for yourself, Mother—marrying Lin Jue brought Zhu Shuzhen nothing but misery! Even I want out of this marriage!"

千里寻的话又引得向夫人伤心：“你……你不认为自己是淑真？”
Her phrasing stung Madam Xiang again. "You... still don't consider yourself Shuzhen?"

千里寻立刻意识到自己太过激动，忘记她就是朱淑真：“娘，我当然是您的女儿。”
Catching her slip at once, Qianli Xun backpedaled. "Of course I'm your daughter, Mother!"

向夫人起身走到女儿面前：“让娘好好看看，对，你就是淑真，我家淑真还活着。”
Rising, Madam Xiang stepped close and cupped her face. "Let Mother look at you... yes, you are Shuzhen. My Shuzhen is still alive."

千里寻被感动，也站起来：“不管我变成什么样，您就是我娘。”
Touched, Qianli Xun stood too. "No matter how much I change, you're still my mother."

“娘知道。”向夫人抬手捋了捋女儿鬓角的头发，“娘说这么多，想让你明白，你爹也是用心良苦，不要怪他。娘给你出个主意，你爹既然不愿出面，可以找朱家的族人，族中有六位你的叔叔伯伯，都住在临安城，我派人给你哥哥说了，让他今日陪你上门拜访，你哥哥已在府外等候。”
"I know." Tucking a stray lock behind her daughter's ear, Madam Xiang said, "I told you all this so you understand your father meant well—don't hate him. Now here's an idea: since your father won't appear, go to the Zhu clan elders. Six of your uncles live right here in Lin'an. I already sent word to your brother to take you visiting today—he's waiting outside the gate right now."

千里寻高兴不已，抱住向夫人：“娘，您是天底下最好的娘！”
Overjoyed, Qianli Xun hugged Madam Xiang tight. "Mother, you're the best mother in the whole world!"

向夫人在女儿背上拍了拍：“都要和离，还这么高兴。”
Patting her daughter's back, Madam Xiang laughed through her tears. "Getting divorced and grinning ear to ear!"

千里寻放开向夫人：“娘，要不要给叔叔伯伯送钱或者礼物？”
Pulling back, Qianli Xun asked, "Should I bring silver or gifts for the uncles?"

向夫人笑着摇摇头：“都是自家人，送钱和礼物太见外。”
Madam Xiang shook her head with a smile. "We're all one family—gifts or money would feel stiff."

千里寻已等不及：“娘，我现在就去找哥哥。苦荞，愣着干什么？还不赶紧跟你家小姐我出发！”
Eager to move, Qianli Xun grabbed her skirt. "I'm going right now, Mother! Kuqiao, what are you staring at? Let's roll!"

向夫人瞧着女儿的背影，既熟悉又陌生，叹道：“唉，希望你的叔叔伯伯们不要把你爹的想法当回事。”
Watching her daughter dash out—both familiar and foreign—Madam Xiang murmured a sigh. "Heaven grant those uncles don't care what your father thinks..."
