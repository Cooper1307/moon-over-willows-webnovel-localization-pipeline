## Chapter 209: Trouble Waiting After Zuiyun Tower

Everything sealed tight, Xiangrong slipped back into the main hall, poured another cup opposite Youqi Jushi, and dropped the reveal after one sip: "Youqi Jushi, someone waits for you inside that side chamber—and if he misses you today, he is liable to storm Chen Manor."

Qianli Xun froze mid-sip—knowing instantly who sat behind that wall! So the three scholars had staged a double bill—Wenyuan first, Prince Yi second! Longing to see him AND desperate to ask after Mengrong, she set her half-full cup down and walked straight toward the connecting door without a word.

Reading her silence, Xiangrong returned to her zither, slender hands gliding over strings as *"Jiangchengzi: Admiring the Spring"* floated across the suite once more—while Kuqiao silently prayed to every Bodhisattva above that neither secret meeting leaked to ruin Miss's reputation!

Before Qianli Xun touched the latch, the door swung open—Bai Feng bowing smoothly: "Please, Youqi Jushi." As she stepped inside, Bai Feng shut the door behind her and sidled straight over to Kuqiao.

"Now that you moved into Chen Manor, catching sight of you is brutal!" he whispered.

"Seeing me right now, aren't you?"

"Tomorrow I ride back to Bianliang with His Highness."

Instant dismay crossed Kuqiao's face. "Why?! Coming back?!"

Thrilled by her longing, Bai Feng grinned. "Of course! His Majesty's decree ordered His Highness back to Bianliang to marry Princess Jiahe—we return right after the wedding."

Kuqiao's cheer collapsed in silent ache for her mistress.

"Once we get back, I will wait outside Chen Manor every three days—scouted it already, waiting by the west gate where Chen family folks never pass!"

Sighing inwardly for Miss, Kuqiao nodded softly. "Got it—shush and listen to Miss Xiangrong singing Miss's lyrics."

"Care nothing for lyrics—only care about you."

A bright blush bloomed across Kuqiao's cheeks.

Inside the side chamber, Shen Jianchen stood at once when Qianli Xun entered, smiling warmly: "'A day without seeing you is like three seasons!'" [TN: Adapted from James Legge's translation of the Book of Songs]

A genuine laugh burst out of Qianli Xun, melting half the weight off her chest. "Why does gorgeous poetry sound ridiculous coming out of YOUR mouth? Talk normal—drop the scholar act!"

Chuckling easy, he waved to a seat. "Just trying to make you laugh."

Sitting across from him, Qianli Xun asked straight: "Why go to all this trouble to see me?"

"Tomorrow... I leave for Bianliang," Shen Jianchen said quietly. "His Majesty decreed I take Mengrong back to wed. Needed to see you before leaving."

Fresh heaviness sank right back over her—fate really had locked every door. "How is... Mengrong?"

"Shallow wound—heals fine in a few days," he answered lightly to spare her guilt.

Remembering that blazing slap on the street, she whispered anxiously, "Does she... hate me?"

"Every bit of it is my fault—nothing to do with you."

"How could she not hate me?! I stole her brother's heart!"

His eyes lit right up. "Calling it 'stole' proves you hold me in your heart too—right?!"

Caught red-handed, Qianli Xun scrambled back behind steel. "Right my foot! I am married, you are riding back to Bianliang to wed—no more meetings from here on, save us both gossip!"

"Chen Haoyu spilled everything—your prenuptial pact of non-interference, never even entering the bridal chamber..."

"So what?!"

"Every day you do not consummate with Chen Haoyu, I wait for you."

"Quit dreaming—I am NOT staying chaste for you!"

"I will stay chaste for you."

Stunned and shaken to her core, Qianli Xun gasped, "Want Mengrong hating me for life?!"

"Cannot worry about anyone else—you are the sole woman in my heart."

Terrified one more minute would shatter her willpower, Qianli Xun shot to her feet. "Pretend today never happened! Wishing Your Highness and Princess Jiahe a century of harmony and a hall full of children!"

Throwing the door wide, she stepped straight back into Xiangrong's haunting line: *"Tossing and turning beneath the quilt in vain regret— / Heaven is easy to see, / Yet seeing that one is hard."* Hearing *"seeing that one is hard"* pierced clean through her; today might truly be their final private hour—and crazy as he was swearing chastity, she vowed to dodge him even if Heaven tossed them together again.

Stepping back inside after seeing her leave, Bai Feng found his master visibly lighter—having laid his vow at her feet, Shen Jianchen could now ride down the road of following fate with a clear heart, waiting patiently through the clouds. Slipping out the back corridor door, they rolled off in their carriage—just as Qiao Wenxian and the three scholars strolled back from the river and spotted Prince Yi's departing wheels!

Caught red-handed, the three scholars traded sheepish glances.

"Helped Prince Yi too, eh—why hide it from me?" Qiao Wenxian asked with a wry smile.

"Scared you two might swing fists!" An Mingxuan admitted.

"Swinging fists at Prince Yi—not that crazy! Even if he were a commoner, I am done fighting. From today on, I accept the marriage my parents arranged and keep the past as a warm memory."

"Hearing you say that lifts a load off all three of us!" Dongfang Chenze cheered as they returned upstairs to finish enjoying Xiangrong's songs before parting ways.

Returning to Chen Manor near sunset, Qianli Xun stepped into Moxiang Courtyard to find gardeners planting rows of chrysanthemums and clearing black silt from the pond ("Fresh rich soil for lotus roots so you get blooms come summer!") while a lush, leafy hehuan silk tree already stood planted on the east edge ("Blooms June and July!"). Pleased, she had Kuqiao hand out silver tips—making every gardener beam and dig twice as hard—and stood enjoying its cool shade. "Tell Matron Wei to build a rattan chair for right here—reading and sipping tea under these boughs come summer!"

"Right away, Miss!"

Before Kuqiao finished, a sharp, furious bark cut across from the back courtyard: "I am sitting right here waiting for her! Refuses to stay indoors, gallivanting outside! Not a shred of breeding!"

Old Madam Xia! Stepping into the back yard with a taut stomach, Qianli Xun froze: every single servant of her courtyard knelt on the flagstones—Matron Wei included! Seated rigid in a chair flanked by grim-faced Second Aunt Madam Bao and Third Aunt Madam Cui, Old Madam Xia glared daggers.

Refusing to flinch, Qianli Xun stepped forward and curtsied crisply. "What offense did Matron Wei and the servants commit to anger Grandmother into making them kneel?"

"Home at last—and got the nerve to ask?!" Old Madam Xia snapped. "They are kneeling for YOUR blunders!"

Holding steady, Qianli Xun answered evenly, "Granddaughter-in-Law knows zero blunder—please enlighten me, Grandmother."

"Then hear all three counts! One: flouting womanly virtues—freshly wed and running outside to poetry salons mingling with strange men! Two: refusing consummation on your wedding night, banishing Haoyu out back, seizing the main hall, and tearing this whole courtyard apart! Three: transferring Haoyu's assets straight into YOUR name!"

Second Aunt Madam Bao piled right on: "Really, Shuzhen—your name was already rocky, quit running outside! Haoyu barely stays home as is, and turning his yard upside down drives him clear out! Worst of all, grabbing his assets—aside from Eldest Brother, ONLY Haoyu held personal property in this house; neither his uncles nor his cousins got such favor!"

Third Aunt Madam Cui chimed right in: "Too true, Shuzhen! Every Chen holding sits in Eldest Brother's name—only because Old Madam begged Prefect Zhu in person did Haoyu get assets under his name! Ask your own father if you doubt us!"

*Aha—all about the assets!* Rather than answering first, Qianli Xun walked straight to Matron Wei and pulled her to her feet. "Stand up, Matron—no need to kneel." Rising at her mistress's call, Matron Wei watched Qianli Xun wave to the whole yard: "All of you, on your feet!"

Old Madam Xia pounded her cane hard against the flagstones! "Outrageous!! Telling them to rise right under my nose—do I count for nothing?!"

Turning calmly, Qianli Xun met her gaze. "Of course Grandmother counts—only since I committed zero wrong, why keep innocent people on their knees?"

"Did we not just list your three wrongs?!" Madam Bao bristled.

"Grandmother, Aunts—not one of those three counts is a wrong."

Old Madam Xia sneered. "Let us hear how you spin your way out!"

Still unhurried, Qianli Xun told Matron Wei, "The yard still needs work—take everyone back to chores instead of wasting time here." Seeing her mistress shielding the staff from wrath, Matron Wei curtsied to Old Madam Xia and led everyone out—until Old Madam Xia barked, "Qinggua, stay!" Halting Qinggua in his tracks.

Now Qianli Xun opened cleanly: "Those charges are laughable, Grandmother and Aunts. As Jiangnan's famed poetess, attending salons and trading verse with scholars was my daily life long before entering Chen gates—since the Chens chose to marry me in, clearly you had zero issue with it! Better yet, before leaving for today's salon, I asked Father-in-Law and Mother-in-Law first, and both gladly approved!"

A flicker of hidden amusement tugged Old Madam Xia's lips before she clamped her stern mask back on. "Slick tongue! Grant you count one—what excuse for banishing Haoyu out back and refusing consummation?! Speak up, Qinggua—tell how she bullied Second Young Master on his wedding night!"

Compelled to answer truthfully, Qinggua blurted, "Young Madam did banish Young Master out back and clear out his maids—only mad as Young Master was, I could tell clear as day he had zero wish to consummate with Young Madam either! So you cannot pin it all on her!"
