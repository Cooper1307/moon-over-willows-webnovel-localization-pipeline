## 1. 修改点清单（10 处，均含前后对比）

### 修改点 1：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "quickly presented"
**修改前：** The attending maids quickly presented his clothes.
**修改后：** The attending maids hurried to present his clothes.
**理由：** 对应"赶紧将衣裳捧上"的"赶紧"；quickly 为弱填充 -ly 直接修饰 presented，违反项目英文风格红线（`-ly` 副词修饰动词）。改用强动词 hurried 重构，忠实且更利落。

### 修改点 2：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "Surprisingly, he did so with ease"
**修改前：** Surprisingly, he did so with ease.
**修改后：** To his surprise, he performed them with effortless ease.
**理由：** 对应"竟然无比自如"；原句 surprisingly 为句首 -ly 标记且 did so with ease 偏弱。改为 to his surprise（"竟然"）+ performed them with effortless ease（"打出…一招一式…无比自如"），既消除 -ly，又还原原文"无比"的语感与画面，提升吸引力。

### 修改点 3：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "quickly got up"
**修改前：** Qiao Mengrong quickly got up, straightened her clothes, and walked toward where her brother was practicing.
**修改后：** Qiao Mengrong hurried to her feet, straightened her clothes, and walked toward where her brother was practicing.
**理由：** 对应"迅速起床"的"迅速"；quickly 弱填充，hurried to her feet 强动词重构，忠实。

### 修改点 4：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "scolded sharply"
**修改前：** She stepped forward and scolded sharply, "What are you all gathered here for?…"
**修改后：** She stepped forward and snapped, "What are you all gathered here for?…"
**理由：** 对应"厉声训斥"的"厉声"（原文有明确情绪信号）；sharply 为 -ly 修饰动词。snapped 为强动词且承载"厉声"情绪，符合工作流 2.3（原文有情绪信号方可补标签）。

### 修改点 5：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "immediately scattered"
**修改前：** The maids and servants immediately scattered in all directions.
**修改后：** The maids and servants scattered in all directions at once.
**理由：** 对应"立刻四散而去"的"立刻"；immediately 属项目替换表明文列示的 -ly（>2 次/章即换），转为 at once 重构，消除 -ly+动词。

### 修改点 6：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "casually said"
**修改前：** Shen Jianchen casually said, "Here."
**修改后：** Shen Jianchen said with casual ease, "Here."
**理由：** 对应"随口说"的"随口"；casually 为 -ly 修饰动词 said。改为 with casual ease 保留"随口"语感且消除 -ly，校准申简辰（现代穿越男主）随性口语声线。

### 修改点 7：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "explained quickly"
**修改前：** Not wanting to disappoint them, he explained quickly, "When a man's health comes back…"
**修改后：** Not wanting to disappoint them, he hurried to explain, "When a man's health comes back…"
**理由：** 对应"赶紧解释"的"赶紧"；explained quickly 为弱填充 -ly；hurried to explain 强动词重构，忠实。

### 修改点 8：`-ly` 副词修饰动词 — "finally felt relieved"
**修改前：** After hearing this, the two finally felt relieved.
**修改后：** After hearing this, the two felt relieved at last.
**理由：** 对应"终于放心"的"终于"；finally 属项目明文列示需转写的 -ly（例：finally understood→understood at last）。转为 at last 重构，消除 -ly+动词。

### 修改点 9：感官/意象还原（忠实补回原文"逐渐"）
**修改前：** Shen Jianchen gazed at the sky brightening in the east.
**修改后：** Shen Jianchen gazed at the sky brightening little by little in the east.
**理由：** 原文"东方逐渐亮起来的一片天空"含"逐渐"，原译漏译该渐进感；little by little 还原"逐渐"，属 Phase 3 感官层次增强（视觉渐进），未添加原文无内容。

### 修改点 10：意象强化（忠实"大树"）
**修改前：** enjoying the feeling of her brother standing like a big tree, sheltering her from wind and rain.
**修改后：** enjoying the feeling of her brother standing like a great tree, sheltering her from the wind and rain.
**理由：** 原文"像一棵大树一样"；big tree→great tree 更具文学质感与"遮风挡雨"的稳靠庇护意象，忠实不添戏。

## 2. 刻意保留项（非疏漏）

- **角色声线**：申简辰现代口语（"I'm starving" / "coarse palate" / "What are all the personal attendants… maids?"）与乔梦容古雅正式声线均已就位，本次未改对话，声线保持。
- **情感高潮碎片句**：`*Take it, or leave it?*`、`To put it bluntly`、喜剧化"我饿了"化解，原已具备，保留。
- **强隐喻**："as light as a dragonfly skimming water"、"melted on his tongue, sweet without being cloying" 原译已佳，保留。
- **允许的 `-ly`**：gradually/slowly（对应慢慢/逐渐，内容副词）、truly/exactly/fully/mainly/completely（程度修饰形容词/介词）、miraculously（对应"奇迹般"，明文例外）、carefully（对应"精挑细选"）、inwardly（对应"心中"）、honestly（对应"照实"）、really（对应"真的"）、simply/naturally/bluntly（话语标记/全句修饰）、early（时间短语）、only/daily/family（名词修饰）——均非"弱填充副词直接修饰动词"，保留。
