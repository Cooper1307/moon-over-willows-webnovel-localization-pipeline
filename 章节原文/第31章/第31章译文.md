# Chapter 31: Not the Sister, but the Estates

Shen Jianchen's life in Prince Yi's Manor was comfortable and unhurried. Princess Jiahe and Matron Zou had arranged everything down to the last detail—whether he went out or stayed in, servants saw to his every need, and little was left to trouble him. The only flaw was the strangeness he kept hidden inside.

He could gradually get used to the unfamiliar environment and people. Even if he wasn't familiar with Song Dynasty culture, he could slowly learn. But when facing Qiao Mengrong's feelings, he always felt an inexplicable distance. That sense of distance made him uneasy all over.

Qiao Mengrong was, by any measure, a great beauty. But no matter how amorous her gaze, Shen Jianchen simply felt no spark. To put it bluntly, Qiao Mengrong was Zhao Pu's type, not his. He liked women like Qianli Xun—the one who had filled his heart when the earthquake struck, who filled it still after he died, and who filled it even now that he had woken in another era.

Since the power of the Divine Willow could bring him here, Qianli Xun should have transmigrated with him. Where was she? Could he still meet her in this unfamiliar era?

Thinking of Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen couldn't sleep anymore. He sat up from the bed. Having transmigrated for several days, he was gradually adapting to the life of Prince Yi. The request came naturally. "I need to change clothes."

The attending maids hurried to present his clothes.

Shen Jianchen sat on the edge of the bed. One maid helped him put on his shoes, while another brought a basin of warm water for him to wash his face. After he washed, she handed him a soft towel. Shen Jianchen took it and wiped his face dry. The maid took back the towel.

After he stood up, the two maids came forward to straighten his clothes.

After that, Shen Jianchen said, "I'm going for a walk. You don't need to follow."

The maids had to stop.

Shen Jianchen walked out of the room alone. The sky hadn't fully brightened yet. As he walked, he recalled the past with Qianli Xun.

They had met at a folk martial arts exchange event. At that time, he represented Shen Fist to spar with Qianli Fist, and the one representing Qianli Fist was none other than Qianli Xun.

After that sparring session, Qianli Xun entered his heart. To pursue her, he often used sparring as an excuse to visit the Qianli Fist Manor. But Qianli Xun was always lukewarm toward him.

At first, he thought she had someone she liked. Later, he discovered she was single. Shen Jianchen was so happy he couldn't sleep all night. Convinced that enough persistence could wear down any resistance, he settled into a campaign of shameless pursuit, certain he would win her over before long. Who knew an earthquake would happen? If not for the earthquake, perhaps that very night, he would have already embraced the beauty in his arms.

Shen Jianchen gazed at the sky brightening little by little in the east. In the past, at this hour, he would already be up practicing his forms. Since transmigrating he had been busy adapting to a new world, and training had been set aside. Now, on a sudden whim, he noticed an open space ahead and walked over to it, trying to perform the moves of Shen Fist with Zhao Pu's body. To his surprise, he performed them with effortless ease.

Xilan, Qiao Mengrong's personal maid, heard from the servants that Prince Yi was already up and practicing martial arts in the yard. It startled her. She hurried to the princess's bedchamber and told her everything.

Qiao Mengrong hurried to her feet, straightened her clothes, and walked toward where her brother was practicing.

As she approached, she noticed many maids and servants hiding at a distance, watching and whispering to each other from time to time.

Qiao Mengrong signaled to Xilan.

Xilan understood the princess's meaning. She stepped forward and snapped, "What are you all gathered here for? Hurry up and get back to work! There's a lot to do in the residence early in the morning!"

The maids and servants scattered in all directions at once.

Qiao Mengrong drew closer and watched Prince Yi run through an exquisite set of fist forms, and a sense of strangeness washed over her. *When did Brother ever know a single martial art? This is not my brother.*

Tears welled in Qiao Mengrong's eyes. *Has Brother truly left me?*

Almost at once she denied the thought. He looked exactly like her brother. If not him, who else could it be? Besides, she had watched her brother wake with her own eyes, and nothing strange had happened. The fist forms must be heaven's mercy—a new gift bestowed upon him.

After comforting herself, Qiao Mengrong was no longer sad. She dabbed at the tears welling in the corners of her eyes with her silk handkerchief.

"Brother!"

Hearing Qiao Mengrong's voice, Shen Jianchen stopped his martial arts practice and turned around. "Mengrong, why are you up so early?"

"Aren't you up early too, Brother?"

"I couldn't sleep."

Qiao Mengrong moved closer to her brother. "This set of fist techniques is truly exquisite. Although I don't understand martial arts, I can see that since you miraculously came back to life, you've been very different from before. It's all heaven's grace, giving you such ability. From now on, with you protecting me, I won't be afraid of being bullied."

As she spoke, Qiao Mengrong leaned against Prince Yi's chest, wrapping her arms around his waist. She closed her eyes, enjoying the feeling of her brother standing like a great tree, sheltering her from the wind and rain.

She and Zhao Pu had held each other before, but always with him propped up in bed, frail and ailing. Never once had she pressed her whole body against him like this. Back then, afraid of taxing his health, every embrace had been as light as a dragonfly skimming water—she had never truly listened to his heartbeat.

But now, truly feeling her brother's heartbeat quicken, she held him even tighter.

The maid Xilan, watching from the side, felt both shy and happy.

Then Qiao Mengrong threw her arms around him. Shen Jianchen froze for a beat, caught off guard. His heartbeat kicked up before he could stop it.

Then his head cleared. He couldn't bring himself to wound the woman Zhao Pu had loved, but neither would he hold this sister the way Zhao Pu once had. He needed something to say—something that would make it awkward for her to hold on. "Mengrong, I haven't eaten yet. I'm starving."

Qiao Mengrong didn't receive the embrace she hoped for. She was somewhat disappointed, but this was her first intimate contact with her brother after he had come back to life.

Amnesia had erased this part of their history; of course it felt unfamiliar to him. What mattered was that he had not pushed her away. Her brother might no longer remember loving her, but he did not dislike her either—and that was a beginning.

Thinking of this, Qiao Mengrong let go of her brother and smiled. "At this time, the kitchen is preparing breakfast. If you're hungry, let's go back to the room and have some pastries to tide you over."

The two walked side by side, not speaking along the way. For Qiao Mengrong, walking with her brother, even without talking, was beautiful.

But Shen Jianchen's heart wasn't on his sister. While thinking of Qianli Xun, he was also struggling with how to handle his feelings for Qiao Mengrong.

He felt this world was truly strange. Before he could win Qianli Xun's heart, he came to a strange new era and encountered an unexpected romance.

*Take it, or leave it?* Refuse, and he would have to meet that soft, pleading gaze of hers again and again—he could not bear to hurt her. Accept, and he would be lying to himself and cheating her both. To hold a woman he did not love, to marry her even—heaven itself would not forgive it. And the heart of the matter was this: Qianli Xun still lived in his heart. He had a fierce premonition that even in this strange era, he would find her again.

The two entered the room. Qiao Mengrong ordered the maids to bring pastries for Prince Yi.

The pastries looked tempting. Shen Jianchen sat down at the table, picked one up, and bit into it—it melted on his tongue, sweet without being cloying. Before he knew it, he had finished the plate. Then he glanced at the maids standing by. A grown man with a cluster of young women waiting on him at every hour of the day: he could not get used to it.

As heir to Shen Fist, he had laid down one rule for taking disciples: men only. He simply had no patience for soft, delicate girls—to his mind they had no business on the road of martial arts. Outside the office, he went everywhere trailed by a pack of male students. Now he was ringed by women, and the wrongness of it gnawed at him. So he turned to Qiao Mengrong, seated beside him. "Why are all the personal attendants in Prince Yi's Manor maids? Aren't there any guards?"

Qiao Mengrong replied, "Prince Yi's Manor does have some guards. They are mainly responsible for the residence's safety. They are not suitable for attending to you. Besides, in the past, you were sickly and needed careful, gentle people to attend to you. Maids are most suitable, and they were all carefully selected by me."

Shen Jianchen was moved by Qiao Mengrong's care and attention to Zhao Pu. "You've put in a lot of effort."

Qiao Mengrong emphasized, "You don't need to be polite with me. We are family."

Shen Jianchen still didn't know much about the residence and this era. He continued to ask, "Now that I'm completely free of illness, I don't need maids attending to me all day. I want to choose a personal guard. I wonder if any of the residence's guards know martial arts? Or are there any martial arts heroes outside the residence?"

Qiao Mengrong sighed inwardly. *Brother really has changed.* Then she answered him honestly, "I don't usually pay much attention to these things. You are the master of the residence and have the right to summon the guards. Just call them all and ask, and you'll know. As for martial arts heroes, when Young Master Qiao returns, you can ask him."

"Who is Young Master Qiao?"

"Young Master Qiao is the younger brother of Consort Qiao's family. When Consort Qiao gave birth to you, he was only two years old, about your age. More than half a year ago, he came from Lin'an to Bianliang and has been living in Prince Yi's Manor. A month ago, a friend invited him to Laizhou for fun, so he went along, missing your passing and miraculous recovery. Calculating the time, he should be back soon. Although Young Master Qiao is a scholar, he has been traveling all year round and has a wide network of friends. He must know where the martial arts heroes are."

Having an uncle almost the same age as him, Shen Jianchen gave a bitter smile. "Alright, when he returns, I'll ask. But I have a question. I don't know if I should ask it."

"Please speak, Brother."

"Why do you also have the surname Qiao?"

"Qiao was not my name to begin with. I was Mu—Mu Mengrong. After Brother took me in and made me his sister, he wanted to honor Consort Qiao. He was also fond of the lines from the Book of Songs: 'Chop, chop go the axes, / Tweet, tweet cry the birds. / From the dark valleys they come, / To settle on the tall trees.' The tall tree in that verse is qiao mu. So he had me take the surname Qiao."

"I see."

As the two were talking, a maid came in. "Your Highness, Princess, Matron Zou asked me to inquire, will breakfast be served in the dining hall or here?"

Qiao Mengrong said, "Brother, you decide."

Shen Jianchen said with casual ease, "Here."

The maid replied, "I'll arrange it right away." Then several maids moved two small round tables that had been set aside to appropriate positions and arranged the chairs.

Soon, Matron Zou came, followed by three maids, each holding a tray. One of them placed the breakfast from the tray on Shen Jianchen's table, while the other two placed theirs on the two small round tables. Matron Zou chose a round table and sat down. Qiao Mengrong got up and sat at the other round table. The maids stood to the side to attend.

Matron Zou said, "Please eat, Your Highness. If anything is not to your taste, only say the word. In the old days the Princess and I knew every one of your habits at table. Now—heaven be thanked—you are well, and the two of us hardly know how to look after you anymore."

Shen Jianchen said, "From now on, whatever you and Mengrong like, I'll eat. No need to fuss over me. I was born with a coarse palate."

At the words *a coarse palate*, Matron Zou and Qiao Mengrong exchanged a bewildered glance. Their eyes spoke the same silent thought: *This is not the Prince Yi we knew.*

Shen Jianchen caught it. Not wanting to disappoint them, he hurried to explain, "When a man's health comes back, he can eat anything and enjoy all of it. If that isn't a coarse palate, what is?"

After hearing this, the two felt relieved at last. The three continued to enjoy their breakfast in peace.

As he ate, Shen Jianchen turned a thought over in his mind. *So many mouths in this residence, three solid meals a day—the upkeep must be enormous. And I haven't done a stroke of honest work since I got here. Where is the money coming from?*

He looked at Matron Zou and asked, "Matron, I don't remember many things. I want to ask, where does the daily expenditure of such a large Prince Yi's Manor come from?"

Matron Zou was eating porridge. At Prince Yi's question she set down her spoon. "Your Highness has always been in poor health. The Emperor never assigned you an official post, but out of compassion he has paid your stipend all the same. You are His Majesty's son—naturally there are fertile fields and shops in your name. Each has its own steward, and each brings in income every year. In addition, the dowry Consort Qiao brought from the Qiao family during her lifetime, as well as what the Emperor bestowed, are also fertile fields and shops. Some are in Bianliang, some in Lin'an, and some scattered in other places. In short, although the income doesn't compare to the Emperor's other sons, it's more than enough to support the entire Prince Yi's Manor."

Shen Jianchen was delighted. He had never imagined that transmigrating would turn him, overnight, into a man who owned estates. Now he could rest easy—no more lying awake wondering how to feed an entire residence.
