## 三、重复用词排查

### A. "Brother"（哥哥，出现 22 次）

乔梦容称申简辰为"哥哥"是角色特征，但英文中连续 22 次 "Brother" 极度冗余。

| 原则 | 修改方式 |
|------|----------|
| 对话中每 2–3 句保留一次 *Brother* | 其余省略或用 *you* |
| 叙述中 | → *he / Shen Jianchen / Prince Yi* |
| 乔梦容撒娇/正式场合 | 保留 *Brother*（体现亲密） |
| 日常对话 | → 省略称谓，直接说内容 |

**示例：**

| 修改前 | 修改后 |
|--------|--------|
| *"Brother doesn't need to be polite with me. We are family."* | *"Don't be polite with me. We're family."* |
| *"If Brother is hungry, let's go back..."* | *"If you're hungry, let's go back..."* |
| *"Brother is the master of the residence"* | *"You're the master of the residence"* |
| *"with Brother protecting me"* | *"with you protecting me"* |

**保留 *Brother* 的场景：** 首次出场呼唤（"Brother!"）、情感高潮（靠在胸前时）、向第三人介绍时。

## 四、表达正确性与地道性

### 1. "花容月貌"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Qiao Mengrong was indeed beautiful with a flower-like face and moon-like appearance.* |
| **修改后** | *Qiao Mengrong was beautiful—undeniably, objectively beautiful. The kind of face that stopped conversations.* |
| **理由** | "flower-like face and moon-like appearance" 是"花容月貌"的逐字直译，英文中完全不自然。用现代叙述语气替代成语翻译 |

---

### 2. "不来电"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Shen Jianchen just couldn't feel attracted.* |
| **修改后** | *Shen Jianchen felt... nothing. No spark. No pull. Just polite blankness.* |
| **理由** | "couldn't feel attracted" 偏平。"不来电"是口语化表达（没有电流感），英文对应 "no spark / no chemistry"。碎片句传达"努力感受但感受不到"的无奈 |

---

### 3. "赵朴的菜"

| **理由** | 现译可以，但 "To put it bluntly" 偏书面。碎片句 + "Never his" 的重复更有申简辰"直男"的内心语气 |

## 八、吸引力评估

**做得好的：**
- 视角切换到申简辰，读者终于看到"男主"的内心 ✓
- 乔梦容拥抱 → 申简辰"我饿了"的喜剧反差 ✓
- "为什么你也姓乔？"的悬念设置好 ✓
- 结尾"我有田了！"的喜剧落点精准 ✓
- 邹嬷嬷和乔梦容"对眼神"的细节（"他不是以前的仪王"）✓

**可加强：**
- **申简辰对千里寻的思念是本章情感核心**，但现译处理偏平：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *He liked someone like Qianli Xun—someone who was in his heart during the earthquake, in his heart after death, and still in his heart after transmigrating here.* |
| **修改后** | *He liked Qianli Xun. Liked her the way a bruise likes being pressed—constantly, painfully, unable to stop. She'd been in his thoughts during the earthquake. In his last conscious moment. And now, a thousand years from everything he knew, she was STILL there. The only familiar thing in an alien world.* |
| **理由** | 原文用三个"心里装着她"排比，现译用三个 "in his heart" 对应但缺乏情感递进。修改后用"bruise"比喻传达"痛但停不下来"的执念，最后 "the only familiar thing in an alien world" 点明穿越者的孤独 |

---

- **结尾"变成有田产之人"的喜剧感**可加强：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *After hearing this, Shen Jianchen was very happy. He didn't expect that after transmigrating, he would suddenly become a person with estates. Now he could rest assured and not worry about how to support the entire residence.* |
| **修改后** | *Shen Jianchen set down his chopsticks. Stared at the wall. <br><br> *I transmigrated. I got a new body. A title. A household of servants. And now—ESTATES. Fields. SHOPS.* <br><br> A grin spread across his face. Uncontainable. Undignified. <br><br> *In my old life, I was a martial arts instructor with a studio lease and a motorcycle. Here, I'm a LANDLORD.* <br><br> He picked up his chopsticks again. The porridge tasted better now.* |
| **理由** | 现译是叙述者总结（"was very happy"）。修改后用内心独白 + 前世今生对比（studio lease vs ESTATES）制造喜剧反差。最后"porridge tasted better"用感官细节收束，比"rest assured"更有余韵 |
