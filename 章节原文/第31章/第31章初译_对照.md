# Chapter 31: Not the Sister, but the Estates

---

申简辰在仪王府的生活过得舒心自在,佳和郡主与邹嬷嬷把一切安排得妥妥当当,出行坐卧均有下人伺候,没有多少需要他费心之事。唯一美中不足的只有藏在他内心的陌生感。

Shen Jianchen lived comfortably in the Yi Wang Residence. Princess Jiahe and Nanny Zou arranged everything properly. He had servants attending to his every need when traveling, sitting, or resting. There was little that required his attention. The only flaw was the sense of unfamiliarity hidden in his heart.

---

对环境和人不熟悉,可以慢慢了解,哪怕是对宋代的文化不熟悉也可以慢慢学习。唯独面对乔梦容的感情,他总是有说不出距离感,因距离感搞得浑身不自在。

He could gradually get used to the unfamiliar environment and people. Even if he wasn't familiar with Song Dynasty culture, he could slowly learn. But when facing Qiao Mengrong's feelings, he always felt an inexplicable distance. That sense of distance made him uncomfortable all over.

---

要说乔梦容长得也是花容月貌,可申简辰面对她多情的眼神就是不来电。说白了,乔梦容是赵朴的菜,不是他申简辰的菜。他喜欢千里寻那样的,地震时心里装着她,死后心里还装着她,穿越而来心里依然装着她。

Qiao Mengrong was indeed beautiful with a flower-like face and moon-like appearance. But when facing her amorous gaze, Shen Jianchen just couldn't feel attracted. To put it bluntly, Qiao Mengrong was Zhao Pu's type, not his type. He liked someone like Qianli Xun—someone who was in his heart during the earthquake, in his heart after death, and still in his heart after transmigrating here.

---

神柳的力量既然能让他穿越而来,千里寻应该也与他一起穿越而来。她到底在哪里?在这个陌生的时代是否还能与她相见?

Since the power of the Divine Willow could bring him here, Qianli Xun should have transmigrated with him. Where was she? Could he still meet her in this unfamiliar era?

---

想起千里寻,申简辰再也睡不着,从床上坐起来,已穿越来几日,逐渐适应仪王的生活,很自然地说:"我要更衣。"

Thinking of Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen couldn't sleep anymore. He sat up from the bed. Having transmigrated for several days, he was gradually adapting to the life of Prince Yi. He said naturally, "I need to change clothes."

---

伺候的丫鬟赶紧将衣裳捧上。

The attending maids quickly presented his clothes.

---

申简辰坐在床边,一名丫鬟伺候他穿好鞋子,另一名丫鬟给她捧上水盆,让他清洁面部,待他清洗过,又递上柔软的面巾。申简辰接过,将面部的水擦干净,丫鬟又接过面巾。

Shen Jianchen sat on the edge of the bed. One maid helped him put on his shoes, while another presented a basin of water for him to wash his face. After he washed, she handed him a soft towel. Shen Jianchen took it and wiped his face dry. The maid took back the towel.

---

他站起来后,两名丫鬟上前为他整理好衣装。

After he stood up, the two maids came forward to straighten his clothes.

---

完毕后,申简辰说:"我出去走会儿,你们不必跟着。"

After that, Shen Jianchen said, "I'm going for a walk. You don't need to follow."

---

丫鬟们只好止步。

The maids had to stop.

---

申简辰独自一人走出屋,天还未完全放亮,他边走边回忆与千里寻的过往。

Shen Jianchen walked out of the room alone. The sky hadn't fully brightened yet. As he walked, he recalled the past with Qianli Xun.

---

他们是在一次民间拳法切磋交流活动中认识,当时他代表申拳与千里拳切磋,而代表千里拳出战的正是千里寻。

They had met at a folk martial arts exchange event. At that time, he represented Shen Fist to spar with Qianli Fist, and the one representing Qianli Fist was none other than Qianli Xun.

---

一场切磋下来,千里寻便走进他的心里,为追求她,他经常以切磋拳技为借口去千里拳庄,可千里寻总是对他不冷不热。

After that sparring session, Qianli Xun entered his heart. To pursue her, he often used sparring as an excuse to visit the Qianli Fist Manor. But Qianli Xun was always lukewarm toward him.

---

开始以为她有喜欢的人,后来发现她单身一枚,申简辰高兴得一晚上没睡着。本着精诚所至金石为开的原则,选择死缠烂打的方法,想着不久肯定会将她拿下,谁知发生了地震。要不是因为地震,说不定那晚,他已将美人拥入怀中。

At first, he thought she had someone she liked. Later, he discovered she was single. Shen Jianchen was so happy he couldn't sleep all night. Following the principle of "absolute sincerity can crack even metal and stone," he chose the method of persistent pursuit, thinking he would surely win her over soon. Who knew an earthquake would happen? If not for the earthquake, perhaps that very night, he would have already embraced the beauty in his arms.

---

申简辰望着东方逐渐亮起来的一片天空,以前这个时候,他总会起来练功,穿越而来,忙着适应新环境,练功之事暂时搁置,此时心血来潮,正好眼前有片空地,他走过去,试着用赵朴的身体打出申拳的一招一式,竟然无比自如。

Shen Jianchen gazed at the gradually brightening sky in the east. In the past, at this time, he would always get up to practice. After transmigrating, he was busy adapting to the new environment, so practice was temporarily put on hold. Now, feeling inspired, and seeing an open space ahead, he walked over and tried to perform the moves of Shen Fist with Zhao Pu's body. Surprisingly, he did so with ease.

---

乔梦容的贴身丫鬟喜兰听下人说仪王已起床,正在外面练功,甚是惊讶。她便匆忙来到郡主的卧房,将一切说了。

Qiao Mengrong's personal maid, Xilan, heard from the servants that Prince Yi had gotten up and was practicing martial arts outside. She was quite surprised. She hurriedly went to the princess's bedroom and told her everything.

---

乔梦容迅速起床,整理好衣装,往哥哥练功之处走去。

Qiao Mengrong quickly got up, straightened her clothes, and walked toward where her brother was practicing.

---

快到跟前时,发现许多丫鬟和下人躲在远处观看,还时不时互相交头接耳。

As she approached, she noticed many maids and servants hiding at a distance, watching and whispering to each other from time to time.

---

乔梦容向喜兰示意。

Qiao Mengrong signaled to Xilan.

---

喜兰明白郡主的意思,走上前厉声训斥:"都围在这里干什么?还不赶紧去忙!一大早的王府很多活等着人干!"

Xilan understood the princess's meaning. She stepped forward and scolded sharply, "What are you all gathered here for? Hurry up and get back to work! There's a lot to do in the residence early in the morning!"

---

丫鬟和下人立刻四散而去。

The maids and servants immediately scattered in all directions.

---

乔梦容走近看着正在打出一套精妙拳法的仪王,她感到很陌生。哥哥哪里会什么拳法?他真的不是哥哥。

Qiao Mengrong approached and watched Prince Yi performing an exquisite set of fist techniques. She felt very unfamiliar. How could her brother know martial arts? He really wasn't her brother.

---

泪水在乔梦容眼中打转,难道哥哥真的已离开她?

Tears swirled in Qiao Mengrong's eyes. Had her brother really left her?

---

很快,她又否认了自己的想法,他跟哥哥长得一模一样,不是哥哥又能是谁?况且她亲眼目睹哥哥醒来,当时并未发生异样,拳法定是上天垂怜,赐予哥哥的新本事。

Soon, she denied her own thoughts. He looked exactly like her brother. If not him, who else could it be? Besides, she had witnessed her brother wake up with her own eyes. Nothing unusual happened at that time. The fist techniques must be heaven's blessing, a new ability granted to her brother.

---

自我安慰后,乔梦容不再难过,她用丝帕擦掉已流到眼角的泪水。

After comforting herself, Qiao Mengrong was no longer sad. She used her silk handkerchief to wipe away the tears that had flowed to the corners of her eyes.

---

"哥哥!"

"Brother!"

---

听到乔梦容的声音,申简辰收起练拳的动作,回过身:"梦容,你怎么起这么早?"

Hearing Qiao Mengrong's voice, Shen Jianchen stopped his martial arts practice and turned around. "Mengrong, why are you up so early?"

---

"哥哥不是也早早起来?"

"Aren't you up early too, Brother?"

---

"我睡不着。"

"I couldn't sleep."

---

乔梦容靠近哥哥身边:"哥哥这套拳法实在精妙,梦容虽不懂功夫,也能看出哥哥自从奇迹般活过来,跟以前很不同,都是上天垂怜,让哥哥有了这等本事,以后梦容有哥哥保护,就不怕被人欺负。"

Qiao Mengrong moved closer to her brother. "This set of fist techniques is truly exquisite. Although I don't understand martial arts, I can see that since Brother miraculously came back to life, you've been very different from before. It's all heaven's blessing, giving Brother such ability. From now on, with Brother protecting me, I won't be afraid of being bullied."

---

乔梦容说着,靠在仪王的胸前,双手环在他的腰间。她闭上眼睛,享受着哥哥像一棵大树一样站立着,给她遮风挡雨的感觉。

As she spoke, Qiao Mengrong leaned against Prince Yi's chest, wrapping her arms around his waist. She closed her eyes, enjoying the feeling of her brother standing like a big tree, sheltering her from wind and rain.

---

以前她与赵朴也搂抱在一起过,可都是对方拖着病体坐在床上,从未有过像现在这般将整个身体贴进他怀里的感觉。以前怕影响哥哥身体,每次拥抱只是蜻蜓点水,也从来没有认真感受过他的心跳。

In the past, she and Zhao Pu had embraced before, but it was always when he was sitting on the bed with his sickly body. She had never experienced the feeling of pressing her whole body against him like now. In the past, afraid of affecting her brother's health, each embrace was just a fleeting touch. She had never seriously felt his heartbeat.

---

而此时,真真切切地感受到哥哥的心跳加快,她把他抱得更紧。

But now, truly feeling her brother's heartbeat quicken, she held him even tighter.

---

一旁的丫鬟喜兰瞧见,又害羞又高兴。

The maid Xilan, watching from the side, felt both shy and happy.

---

猛然被乔梦容抱住,申简辰感觉整个人有些懵,心跳也不由加速。

Suddenly hugged by Qiao Mengrong, Shen Jianchen felt a bit dazed. His heartbeat also quickened involuntarily.

---

很快他清醒许多,不忍心伤害赵朴所爱之人,又不愿像赵朴一样把这位妹妹拥入怀中。必须说些话,让她不好再抱下去:"梦容,我还没吃早饭,肚子很饿。"

Soon, he became much clearer. He couldn't bear to hurt the person Zhao Pu loved, but he also didn't want to embrace this sister like Zhao Pu did. He had to say something to make it awkward for her to continue hugging. "Mengrong, I haven't had breakfast yet. I'm very hungry."

---

乔梦容没有迎来哥哥拥抱她的双臂,有些失望,但这是哥哥起死回生之后,她第一次与他亲密接触。

Qiao Mengrong didn't receive the embrace she hoped for. She was somewhat disappointed, but this was her first intimate contact with her brother after he came back from the dead.

---

他因失忆忘记这段感情,不习惯倒也正常,重要的是他没有一把将她推开,说明失忆的哥哥并不讨厌她,不讨厌就是良好的开端。

He had forgotten this feeling due to amnesia. It was normal that he wasn't used to it. What mattered was that he didn't push her away. This showed that her amnesiac brother didn't dislike her. Not disliking was a good start.

---

想到这里,乔梦容放开哥哥,面带笑容:"这个时间,厨房正在做早膳,哥哥要是饿了,先回屋吃些糕点垫垫。"

Thinking of this, Qiao Mengrong let go of her brother and smiled. "At this time, the kitchen is preparing breakfast. If Brother is hungry, let's go back to the room and have some pastries to tide you over."

---

两人并肩走着,一路上未说话。对乔梦容来讲,与哥哥走在一起,即使不说话,也很美好。

The two walked side by side, not speaking along the way. For Qiao Mengrong, walking with her brother, even without talking, was beautiful.

---

而申简辰的心并不在妹妹身上,他想着千里寻的同时,纠结如何处理与乔梦容的感情。

But Shen Jianchen's heart wasn't on his sister. While thinking of Qianli Xun, he was also struggling with how to handle his feelings for Qiao Mengrong.

---

他觉得这个世界真的很奇怪,还未将千里寻追到手,就来到一个陌生的时代,还遇到一段陌生的恋情。

He felt this world was truly strange. Before he could win Qianli Xun's heart, he came to an unfamiliar era and encountered an unfamiliar romance.

---

接还是不接?不接吧,乔梦容每次面对他都含情脉脉,楚楚可怜,令他不忍伤害;接吧,又实在违心,也会对不起乔梦容,不爱她还要将她搂在怀里,甚至娶她,天理难容!关键是他心里装着千里寻。他有种强烈的预感,在这个陌生的时代,他依然能够遇见她。

Accept it or not? If he didn't accept it, Qiao Mengrong looked at him with affection and pitiful eyes every time, making him unable to bear to hurt her. If he did accept it, it would go against his heart and also be unfair to Qiao Mengrong. Embracing her in his arms and even marrying her when he didn't love her—this would be against heaven's principles! The key was that his heart was filled with Qianli Xun. He had a strong premonition that in this unfamiliar era, he would still be able to meet her.

---

两人走进屋,乔梦容吩咐丫鬟给仪王端上糕点。

The two entered the room. Qiao Mengrong ordered the maids to bring pastries for Prince Yi.

---

看着诱人的糕点,申简辰在桌旁坐下,拿起一块,咬了一口,入口即化,甜而不腻,忍不住全部吃完。瞅瞅一旁的丫鬟,自己一个大男人身边老是些妙龄女子伺候实在不习惯。

Looking at the tempting pastries, Shen Jianchen sat down at the table, picked up one, and took a bite. It melted in his mouth, sweet but not greasy. He couldn't help but finish them all. Glancing at the maids beside him, he really wasn't used to having young women attending to him all the time as a grown man.

---

他作为申拳的传人,收徒弟时定下一个规矩,收男不收女。只因他不喜欢柔柔弱弱的女子,认为她们根本不适合走上练武这条道路。除了在公司,他无论走到哪里都是一群男弟子跟着,现在被一群女子环绕,实在感到不自在。便向坐在一旁的乔梦容问道:"仪王府近身伺候的怎么全是些丫鬟?难道没有侍卫什么的?"

As the successor of Shen Fist, he had set a rule when accepting disciples: accept men, not women. It was simply because he didn't like weak and delicate women, believing they were not suitable for the path of martial arts at all. Except when at the company, wherever he went, he was followed by a group of male disciples. Now, surrounded by a group of women, he felt truly uncomfortable. So he asked Qiao Mengrong, who was sitting beside him, "Why are all the personal attendants at Prince Yi's Residence maids? Aren't there any guards or something?"

---

乔梦容回道:"仪王府倒是有些侍卫,主要负责王府安全,伺候哥哥之事,他们并不适合。再说以前哥哥身体病弱,需要细心、手脚轻软之人伺候,丫鬟最合适,而且都是我精挑细选出来的。"

Qiao Mengrong replied, "Prince Yi's Residence does have some guards. They are mainly responsible for the residence's safety. They are not suitable for attending to Brother. Besides, in the past, Brother was sickly and needed careful, gentle people to attend to him. Maids are most suitable, and they were all carefully selected by me."

---

申简辰感动于乔梦容对赵朴的关怀和照料:"让你费心了。"

Shen Jianchen was moved by Qiao Mengrong's care and attention to Zhao Pu. "You've put in a lot of effort."

---

乔梦容强调道:"哥哥不必跟我客气,我们是一家人。"

Qiao Mengrong emphasized, "Brother doesn't need to be polite with me. We are family."

---

申简辰对王府和这个时代很多事情还不大清楚,继续问:"我现在疾病全无,不需要丫鬟整日围在身边伺候,想选名近身侍卫,不知王府侍卫有没有会功夫的?或者府外有没有武林好汉之类的人物?"

Shen Jianchen still didn't know much about the residence and this era. He continued to ask, "Now that I'm completely free of illness, I don't need maids attending to me all day. I want to choose a personal guard. I wonder if any of the residence's guards know martial arts? Or are there any martial arts heroes outside the residence?"

---

乔梦容心中叹道:"哥哥真的变了。"而后照实向哥哥说:"梦容平日不大关心这些,哥哥是王府的主人,有权召集侍卫,把他们都叫来,问问便知;至于武林好汉,等乔公子回来,哥哥可以向他打听。"

Qiao Mengrong sighed in her heart, "Brother has really changed." Then she told her brother honestly, "I don't usually pay much attention to these things. Brother is the master of the residence and has the right to summon the guards. Just call them all and ask, and you'll know. As for martial arts heroes, when Young Master Qiao returns, Brother can ask him."

---

"乔公子是谁?"

"Who is Young Master Qiao?"

---

"乔公子是乔贵妃娘家的弟弟,乔贵妃生下哥哥时,他才两岁,与哥哥年纪相仿。大半年前,他从临安来到汴梁,一直住在仪王府。一个月前有朋友约他去莱州玩儿,便跟着去了,错过哥哥离世又奇迹生还之事。算算时间,也该回来。乔公子虽是文人,却常年在外,结交甚广,肯定知道哪里有武林好汉。"

"Young Master Qiao is the younger brother of Consort Qiao's family. When Consort Qiao gave birth to Brother, he was only two years old, about the same age as Brother. More than half a year ago, he came from Lin'an to Bianliang and has been living in Prince Yi's Residence. A month ago, a friend invited him to Laizhou for fun, so he went along, missing Brother's death and miraculous recovery. Calculating the time, he should be back soon. Although Young Master Qiao is a scholar, he has been traveling all year round and has a wide network of friends. He must know where the martial arts heroes are."

---

多了一位差不多同龄的舅舅,申简辰苦笑一下:"好吧,等他回来,我问问。不过我有个疑问,不知当问不当问?"

Having an uncle almost the same age as him, Shen Jianchen gave a bitter smile. "Alright, when he returns, I'll ask. But I have a question. I don't know if I should ask it."

---

"哥哥请说。"

"Please speak, Brother."

---

"你为何也姓乔?"

"Why do you also have the surname Qiao?"

---

"我本不姓乔,而是姓木,叫木梦容,哥哥把我捡回来后,认我做妹妹,为纪念乔贵妃,加之哥哥喜欢《诗经》中的诗句:'伐木丁丁,鸟鸣嘤嘤。出自幽谷,迁于乔木',便让我改了乔姓。"

"I didn't originally have the surname Qiao. My surname was Mu, and my name was Mu Mengrong. After Brother picked me up and acknowledged me as his sister, to commemorate Consort Qiao, and because Brother liked the lines from the Book of Songs: 'Chop, chop go the axes, / Tweet, tweet cry the birds. / From the dark valleys they come, / To settle on the tall trees,' he had me change my surname to Qiao."

---

"原来如此。"

"I see."

---

两人正说着,一个丫鬟进来:"王爷,郡主,邹嬷嬷让奴婢来问,早膳是在用膳厅用还是在这里?"

As the two were talking, a maid came in. "Your Highness, Princess, Nanny Zou asked me to inquire, will breakfast be served in the dining hall or here?"

---

乔梦容说:"哥哥,你来定。"

Qiao Mengrong said, "Brother, you decide."

---

申简辰随口说:"在这里吧。"

Shen Jianchen casually said, "Here."

---

丫鬟回道:"奴婢这就去安排。"接着几名丫鬟把放在一旁的两个小圆桌搬到合适的位置,也把椅子放好。

The maid replied, "I'll arrange it right away." Then several maids moved two small round tables that had been set aside to appropriate positions and arranged the chairs.

---

不一会儿邹嬷嬷过来,身后跟着三名丫鬟,手中各端着盘子。其中一名把盘子中的早膳放在申简辰的桌子上,另外两名分别放在两个小圆桌上。邹嬷嬷挑了一个圆桌坐下,乔梦容起身在另一个圆桌旁坐下。丫鬟们则站在一旁伺候。

Soon, Nanny Zou came, followed by three maids, each holding a tray. One of them placed the breakfast from the tray on Shen Jianchen's table, while the other two placed theirs on the two small round tables. Nanny Zou chose a round table and sat down. Qiao Mengrong got up and sat at the other round table. The maids stood to the side to attend.

---

邹嬷嬷说:"王爷请用,有什么不合胃口的,尽管说。以前王爷的用膳习惯我与郡主尽知,现在老天保佑,你身体健康,我们两个反而不知道该如何照顾你。"

Nanny Zou said, "Please enjoy your meal, Your Highness. If anything doesn't suit your taste, just say so. Nanny and I knew Your Highness's dining habits well in the past. Now, thank heaven, you are healthy. The two of us actually don't know how to take care of you anymore."

---

申简辰说:"以后嬷嬷和梦容爱吃什么,我就是吃什么,不用特别照顾,我天生口粗。"

Shen Jianchen said, "From now on, whatever Nanny and Mengrong like to eat, I'll eat too. No need for special care. I'm naturally not picky about food."

---

听到"天生口粗",邹嬷嬷与乔梦容面面相觑,她们似乎用目光向对方说:"他不是以前的仪王。"

Hearing "naturally not picky about food," Nanny Zou and Qiao Mengrong looked at each other. They seemed to say to each other with their eyes, "He's not the previous Prince Yi."

---

申简辰看到,不希望她们失望,赶紧解释:"身体好了,自然什么都能吃,都爱吃,不是口粗又是什么?"

Shen Jianchen saw this. Not wanting them to be disappointed, he quickly explained, "When you're healthy, you can eat everything and love to eat everything. If that's not being not picky about food, what is?"

---

两人听后,终于放心,三人继续安心享用早膳。

After hearing this, the two finally felt relieved. The three continued to enjoy their breakfast in peace.

---

申简辰边吃边想:"王府这么多人,一日三餐吃的也不错,开销应该挺大。自从我穿越而来,也没干什么正事,到底钱从哪里来?"

As Shen Jianchen ate, he thought, "With so many people in the residence, and three good meals a day, the expenses should be quite large. Since I transmigrated here, I haven't done anything proper. Where does the money come from?"

---

他看向邹嬷嬷,问道:"嬷嬷,好多事情我不记得,想问问,这么大的仪王府每日的花销都从哪里来?"

He looked at Nanny Zou and asked, "Nanny, I don't remember many things. I want to ask, where does the daily expenditure of such a large Prince Yi's Residence come from?"

---

邹嬷嬷正在吃粥,听到仪王发问,放下勺子:"王爷一直身体病弱,官家虽没安排任何职务,但体恤仪王,俸禄照给。您是官家的儿子,名下自然有许多良田铺子,都有专人负责管理,每年也有收益。加之乔贵妃生前从乔家带的嫁妆,以及陛下赐给的,也均是良田和铺子,有的在汴梁,有的在临安,还有分散在其他地方的。总之,收益虽比不上官家别的儿子,养活整个仪王府绰绰有余。"

Nanny Zou was eating porridge. Hearing Prince Yi's question, she put down her spoon. "Your Highness has always been sickly. Although the Emperor didn't assign you any position, out of consideration for Prince Yi, your salary is still given. You are the Emperor's son. Naturally, you have many fertile fields and shops under your name. They are all managed by dedicated personnel and generate income every year. In addition, the dowry Consort Qiao brought from the Qiao family during her lifetime, as well as what the Emperor bestowed, are also fertile fields and shops. Some are in Bianliang, some in Lin'an, and some scattered in other places. In short, although the income doesn't compare to the Emperor's other sons, it's more than enough to support the entire Prince Yi's Residence."

---

申简辰听后很开心,没想到穿越而来,竟然一下子变成一位有田产之人,这下他可放心,不用担忧怎么养活整个王府的人。

After hearing this, Shen Jianchen was very happy. He didn't expect that after transmigrating, he would suddenly become a person with estates. Now he could rest assured and not worry about how to support the entire residence.
