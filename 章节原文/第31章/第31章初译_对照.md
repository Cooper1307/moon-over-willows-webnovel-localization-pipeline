第31章 不爱妹妹爱田产
## Chapter 31: Chapter 31: No Love for Sister, Plenty for Estates

申简辰在仪王府的生活过得舒心自在，佳和郡主与邹嬷嬷把一切安排得妥妥当当，出行坐卧均有下人伺候，没有多少需要他费心之事。唯一美中不足的只有藏在他内心的陌生感。
Life in Prince Yi's Manor ran smooth and pampered for Shen Jianchen: Princess Jiahe and Matron Zou handled every detail, servants waited on his every breath, leaving nothing to fret over—nothing except the quiet alienation sitting in his chest.

对环境和人不熟悉，可以慢慢了解，哪怕是对宋代的文化不熟悉也可以慢慢学习。唯独面对乔梦容的感情，他总是有说不出距离感，因距离感搞得浑身不自在。
Unfamiliar halls and faces could grow familiar in time; Song customs could be learned. Only Qiao Mengrong's devotion left an awkward wall between them that made him squirm.

要说乔梦容长得也是花容月貌，可申简辰面对她多情的眼神就是不来电。说白了，乔梦容是赵朴的菜，不是他申简辰的菜。他喜欢千里寻那样的，地震时心里装着她，死后心里还装着她，穿越而来心里依然装着她。
Beautiful as Qiao Mengrong was, her adoring eyes sparked zero chemistry in him. Plain truth: Qiao Mengrong was Zhao Pu's type, not Shen Jianchen's. He loved Qianli Xun—loved her during the quake, after death, and right here in this new life.

神柳的力量既然能让他穿越而来，千里寻应该也与他一起穿越而来。她到底在哪里？在这个陌生的时代是否还能与她相见？
If the Divine Willow's force had carried him across time, surely Qianli Xun had crossed too. Where was she? Would he ever find her in this foreign era?

想起千里寻，申简辰再也睡不着，从床上坐起来，已穿越来几日，逐渐适应仪王的生活，很自然地说：“我要更衣。”
Thinking of her chased sleep away. Sitting up in bed—already sliding into princely habits after a few days—he called out natural as breathing: "Time to dress."

伺候的丫鬟赶紧将衣裳捧上。
Attendant maids hurried forward bearing robes.

申简辰坐在床边，一名丫鬟伺候他穿好鞋子，另一名丫鬟给她捧上水盆，让他清洁面部，待他清洗过，又递上柔软的面巾。申简辰接过，将面部的水擦干净，丫鬟又接过面巾。他站起来后，两名丫鬟上前为他整理好衣装。
Seated on the bedside, he let one maid slip on his shoes while another held out a washbasin, then handed him a soft towel to dry his face. Standing up, he let two more maids straighten his sash and robes.

完毕后，申简辰说：“我出去走会儿，你们不必跟着。”
Finished, he said, "Going out for a walk. Stay behind."

丫鬟们只好止步。
The maids halted at the doorway.

申简辰独自一人走出屋，天还未完全放亮，他边走边回忆与千里寻的过往。
Stepping out solo into pre-dawn twilight, Shen Jianchen drifted back over memories of Qianli Xun.

他们是在一次民间拳法切磋交流活动中认识，当时他代表申拳与千里拳切磋，而代表千里拳出战的正是千里寻。
They had met at a folk martial-arts exhibition where he represented Shen Fist against Qianli Fist—and Qianli Xun stepped into the ring for Qianli Fist.

一场切磋下来，千里寻便走进他的心里，为追求她，他经常以切磋拳技为借口去千里拳庄，可千里寻总是对他不冷不热。
One sparring match stole his heart. To chase her, he kept showing up at Qianli Fist Manor using "sparring" as an excuse, even when she met him with nonstop cold shoulders.

开始以为她有喜欢的人，后来发现她单身一枚，申简辰高兴得一晚上没睡着。本着精诚所至金石为开的原则，选择死缠烂打的方法，想着不久肯定会将她拿下，谁知发生了地震。要不是因为地震，说不定那晚，他已将美人拥入怀中。
At first he figured she had a boyfriend; discovering she was single kept him grinning awake all night! Banking on sheer persistence wearing down stone, he stuck to her like glue, convinced she'd crack soon—right up until the earthquake struck. Without that quake, maybe that very night he'd have held her in his arms.

申简辰望着东方逐渐亮起来的一片天空，以前这个时候，他总会起来练功，穿越而来，忙着适应新环境，练功之事暂时搁置，此时心血来潮，正好眼前有片空地，他走过去，试着用赵朴的身体打出申拳的一招一式，竟然无比自如。
Gazing at the pale eastern sky brightening overhead, he felt his muscles itch. Back home he trained every dawn; busy adjusting since crossing over, he hadn't thrown a single punch. Spotting an open yard ahead, he stepped out and ran through a sequence of Shen Fist using Zhao Pu's body—fluid, crisp, and effortless.

乔梦容的贴身丫鬟喜兰听下人说仪王已起床，正在外面练功，甚是惊讶。她便匆忙来到郡主的卧房，将一切说了。
Hearing from servants that Prince Yi was up at dawn practicing martial arts, Qiao Mengrong's personal maid Xilan hurried to wake her mistress with the astonishing news.

乔梦容迅速起床，整理好衣装，往哥哥练功之处走去。
Dressing in haste, Qiao Mengrong rushed straight toward the courtyard.

快到跟前时，发现许多丫鬟和下人躲在远处观看，还时不时互相交头接耳。
Nearing the yard, she spotted clusters of maids and servants peeking from afar and whispering.

乔梦容向喜兰示意。
She gave Xilan a sharp glance.

喜兰明白郡主的意思，走上前厉声训斥：“都围在这里干什么？还不赶紧去忙！一大早的王府很多活等着人干！”
Catching the signal, Xilan marched over and snapped: "What are you all loitering around for? Get back to work! Plenty of chores waiting across the manor this morning!"

丫鬟和下人立刻四散而去。
The servants scattered at once.

乔梦容走近看着正在打出一套精妙拳法的仪王，她感到很陌生。哥哥哪里会什么拳法？他真的不是哥哥。
Stepping closer to watch Prince Yi flow through crisp, powerful strikes, Qiao Mengrong felt a chill of strangeness. Since when did her brother know martial arts? Maybe he really wasn't her brother...

泪水在乔梦容眼中打转，难道哥哥真的已离开她？
Tears welled in her eyes—had her real brother truly left her forever?

很快，她又否认了自己的想法，他跟哥哥长得一模一样，不是哥哥又能是谁？况且她亲眼目睹哥哥醒来，当时并未发生异样，拳法定是上天垂怜，赐予哥哥的新本事。
Seconds later she pushed the dread down: identical face, identical frame—who else could he be? She had watched him wake with her own eyes; nothing uncanny had happened. This martial prowess had to be Heaven's blessing bestowed on his recovery!

自我安慰后，乔梦容不再难过，她用丝帕擦掉已流到眼角的泪水。
Comforted by her own logic, she blotted away the tear slipping down her cheek with a silk handkerchief.

“哥哥！”
"Brother!"

听到乔梦容的声音，申简辰收起练拳的动作，回过身：“梦容，你怎么起这么早？”
At her voice, Shen Jianchen halted his form and turned. "Mengrong? Up this early?"

“哥哥不是也早早起来？”
"Weren't you up early too, Brother?"

“我睡不着。”
"Couldn't sleep."

乔梦容靠近哥哥身边：“哥哥这套拳法实在精妙，梦容虽不懂功夫，也能看出哥哥自从奇迹般活过来，跟以前很不同，都是上天垂怜，让哥哥有了这等本事，以后梦容有哥哥保护，就不怕被人欺负。”
Stepping right up beside him, Qiao Mengrong beamed: "What marvelous boxing, Brother! Even knowing nothing of martial arts, I see how much you've changed since waking up—Heaven took pity and gifted you such strength! Now with Brother protecting me, nobody will ever dare bully me!"

乔梦容说着，靠在仪王的胸前，双手环在他的腰间。她闭上眼睛，享受着哥哥像一棵大树一样站立着，给她遮风挡雨的感觉。
Saying so, she leaned into his chest and wrapped both arms around his waist, eyes shutting in bliss as she drank in the feeling of him standing tall like a sturdy tree shielding her from every storm.

以前她与赵朴也搂抱在一起过，可都是对方拖着病体坐在床上，从未有过像现在这般将整个身体贴进他怀里的感觉。以前怕影响哥哥身体，每次拥抱只是蜻蜓点水，也从来没有认真感受过他的心跳。
She and Zhao Pu had embraced before, always with him propped frail in bed—never pressing her whole body against a solid, upright chest like this, never daring hug tight lest she strain his weak lungs, never really hearing his pulse.

而此时，真真切切地感受到哥哥的心跳加快，她把他抱得更紧。
Now, feeling his heartbeat quicken beneath her ear, she tightened her arms.

一旁的丫鬟喜兰瞧见，又害羞又高兴。
Watching from nearby, Xilan blushed and smiled in delight.

猛然被乔梦容抱住，申简辰感觉整个人有些懵，心跳也不由加速。
Ambush-hugged out of nowhere, Shen Jianchen froze, his heart kicking faster by pure reflex.

很快他清醒许多，不忍心伤害赵朴所爱之人，又不愿像赵朴一样把这位妹妹拥入怀中。必须说些话，让她不好再抱下去：“梦容，我还没吃早饭，肚子很饿。”
Catching himself fast—hateful to wound the girl Zhao Pu loved, yet refusing to wrap arms around her like a lover—he searched for an exit line: "Mengrong, haven't had breakfast yet—I'm starving."

乔梦容没有迎来哥哥拥抱她的双臂，有些失望，但这是哥哥起死回生之后，她第一次与他亲密接触。
Though disappointed he hadn't closed his arms around her, this was their first real embrace since he came back from death.

他因失忆忘记这段感情，不习惯倒也正常，重要的是他没有一把将她推开，说明失忆的哥哥并不讨厌她，不讨厌就是良好的开端。
Forgetting their love through amnesia made shyness natural—what counted was he hadn't shoved her away! Not pushing her away meant he didn't dislike her, and that was a hopeful start.

想到这里，乔梦容放开哥哥，面带笑容：“这个时间，厨房正在做早膳，哥哥要是饿了，先回屋吃些糕点垫垫。”
Releasing him with a warm smile, Qiao Mengrong said, "The kitchen is still making breakfast, Brother. If you're hungry, come back inside for some pastries to tide you over."

两人并肩走着，一路上未说话。对乔梦容来讲，与哥哥走在一起，即使不说话，也很美好。
They walked back side by side in silence. For Qiao Mengrong, simply walking beside him without a single word felt like paradise.

而申简辰的心并不在妹妹身上，他想着千里寻的同时，纠结如何处理与乔梦容的感情。
Shen Jianchen's mind stayed miles away—missing Qianli Xun while agonizing over how to handle Qiao Mengrong's devotion.

他觉得这个世界真的很奇怪，还未将千里寻追到手，就来到一个陌生的时代，还遇到一段陌生的恋情。
What a crazy twist: hadn't even won Qianli Xun yet, got flung into another century, and landed straight inside someone else's romance!

接还是不接？不接吧，乔梦容每次面对他都含情脉脉，楚楚可怜，令他不忍伤害；接吧，又实在违心，也会对不起乔梦容，不爱她还要将她搂在怀里，甚至娶她，天理难容！关键是他心里装着千里寻。他有种强烈的预感，在这个陌生的时代，他依然能够遇见她。
Accept or reject? Reject her, and her melting, devoted gaze tore at his conscience; accept her, and living a lie—holding or marrying a girl he didn't love—betrayed them both! Worst of all, his heart still belonged to Qianli Xun, and a deep gut hunch told him they would cross paths again in this era.

两人走进屋，乔梦容吩咐丫鬟给仪王端上糕点。
Inside the hall, Qiao Mengrong had maids bring pastries.

看着诱人的糕点，申简辰在桌旁坐下，拿起一块，咬了一口，入口即化，甜而不腻，忍不住全部吃完。瞅瞅一旁的丫鬟，自己一个大男人身边老是些妙龄女子伺候实在不习惯。
Sitting at the table, Shen Jianchen bit into one—melting sweet on his tongue without being cloying—and polished off the whole piece. Glancing around at the ring of young maids hovering nearby, he frowned; being fussed over all day by delicate girls felt deeply awkward.

他作为申拳的传人，收徒弟时定下一个规矩，收男不收女。只因他不喜欢柔柔弱弱的女子，认为她们根本不适合走上练武这条道路。除了在公司，他无论走到哪里都是一群男弟子跟着，现在被一群女子环绕，实在感到不自在。便向坐在一旁的乔梦容问道：“仪王府近身伺候的怎么全是些丫鬟？难道没有侍卫什么的？”
As heir to Shen Fist, he had enforced one strict rule: male apprentices only—convinced delicate girls weren't cut out for hard boxing. Outside corporate meetings, a pack of male disciples followed him everywhere; surrounded by maids here made his skin itch. Turning to Qiao Mengrong, he asked, "Why are all my personal attendants maids? Don't we have guards or anything?"

乔梦容回道：“仪王府倒是有些侍卫，主要负责王府安全，伺候哥哥之事，他们并不适合。再说以前哥哥身体病弱，需要细心、手脚轻软之人伺候，丫鬟最合适，而且都是我精挑细选出来的。”
"Prince Yi's Manor keeps guards for security, Brother, yet they're clumsy for daily care," Qiao Mengrong replied. "When you were frail, you needed gentle, careful hands—maids suited best, and I handpicked every single one."

申简辰感动于乔梦容对赵朴的关怀和照料：“让你费心了。”
Touched by her endless care for Zhao Pu, Shen Jianchen murmured, "You went to a lot of trouble."

乔梦容强调道：“哥哥不必跟我客气，我们是一家人。”
"Never speak like an outsider with me, Brother—we're family."

申简辰对王府和这个时代很多事情还不大清楚，继续问：“我现在疾病全无，不需要丫鬟整日围在身边伺候，想选名近身侍卫，不知王府侍卫有没有会功夫的？或者府外有没有武林好汉之类的人物？”
Still piecing together both the manor and this era, he pressed on: "Now that I'm healthy, I don't need maids crowding around me all day. I want a personal guard—do any of the manor's guards know real martial arts? Or are there skilled martial artists out in the city?"

乔梦容心中叹道：“哥哥真的变了。”而后照实向哥哥说：“梦容平日不大关心这些，哥哥是王府的主人，有权召集侍卫，把他们都叫来，问问便知；至于武林好汉，等乔公子回来，哥哥可以向他打听。”
Brother really has changed, Qiao Mengrong thought with a quiet sigh, answering plain: "I don't pay much attention to such matters. You're master of this manor—summon the guards and see for yourself. As for outside martial heroes, ask Young Master Qiao once he gets back."

“乔公子是谁？”
"Who's Young Master Qiao?"

“乔公子是乔贵妃娘家的弟弟，乔贵妃生下哥哥时，他才两岁，与哥哥年纪相仿。大半年前，他从临安来到汴梁，一直住在仪王府。一个月前有朋友约他去莱州玩儿，便跟着去了，错过哥哥离世又奇迹生还之事。算算时间，也该回来。乔公子虽是文人，却常年在外，结交甚广，肯定知道哪里有武林好汉。”
"Consort Qiao's younger brother—only two when she gave birth to you, nearly your own age! Over half a year ago he came up from Lin'an to Bianliang and stayed here at Prince Yi's Manor. A month back a friend invited him to visit Laizhou, so he missed your death and recovery—he should be back any day now. Scholar though he is, he travels wide and knows all sorts of people; he'll surely know where martial heroes gather."

多了一位差不多同龄的舅舅，申简辰苦笑一下：“好吧，等他回来，我问问。不过我有个疑问，不知当问不当问？”
An uncle practically his own age—Shen Jianchen smiled wryly. "Alright, I'll ask him when he returns. Wait—one question I've been meaning to ask..."

“哥哥请说。”
"Go ahead, Brother."

“你为何也姓乔？”
"Why is your surname Qiao too?"

“我本不姓乔，而是姓木，叫木梦容，哥哥把我捡回来后，认我做妹妹，为纪念乔贵妃，加之哥哥喜欢《诗经》中的诗句：‘伐木丁丁，鸟鸣嘤嘤。出自幽谷，迁于乔木’，便让我改了乔姓。”
"My birth surname was Mu—Mu Mengrong. After you picked me up and took me in as your sister, to honor Consort Qiao—and because you loved the lines from the Book of Songs: 'Chop, chop go the axes on the trees; chirping, chirping sing the birds; leaving the dark valley, they rise onto the lofty Qiao tree' [TN: Translator's rendering]—you gave me the surname Qiao."

“原来如此。”
"Got it."

两人正说着，一个丫鬟进来：“王爷，郡主，邹嬷嬷让奴婢来问，早膳是在用膳厅用还是在这里？”
Just then a maid entered: "Your Highness, Princess—Matron Zou asks whether you wish to take breakfast in the dining hall or right here?"

乔梦容说：“哥哥，你来定。”
"Your choice, Brother," Qiao Mengrong deferred.

申简辰随口说：“在这里吧。”
"Right here," Shen Jianchen tossed out.

丫鬟回道：“奴婢这就去安排。”接着几名丫鬟把放在一旁的两个小圆桌搬到合适的位置，也把椅子放好。
"Right away!" Stepping out, the maid sent others moving two small round tables and chairs into position.

不一会儿邹嬷嬷过来，身后跟着三名丫鬟，手中各端着盘子。其中一名把盘子中的早膳放在申简辰的桌子上，另外两名分别放在两个小圆桌上。邹嬷嬷挑了一个圆桌坐下，乔梦容起身在另一个圆桌旁坐下。丫鬟们则站在一旁伺候。
Moments later Matron Zou walked in followed by three maids bearing trays—one placed breakfast before Shen Jianchen, the other two set the small round tables where Matron Zou and Qiao Mengrong took their seats while maids stood ready.

邹嬷嬷说：“王爷请用，有什么不合胃口的，尽管说。以前王爷的用膳习惯我与郡主尽知，现在老天保佑，你身体健康，我们两个反而不知道该如何照顾你。”
"Please eat, Your Highness—tell us if anything doesn't suit your palate," Matron Zou beamed. "We knew your old sickbed diet by heart; now that Heaven restored your health, we almost don't know how to spoil you!"

申简辰说：“以后嬷嬷和梦容爱吃什么，我就是吃什么，不用特别照顾，我天生口粗。”
"Whatever you and Mengrong eat works for me—don't make anything special. I'm not picky."

听到“天生口粗”，邹嬷嬷与乔梦容面面相觑，她们似乎用目光向对方说：“他不是以前的仪王。”
"Not picky" made Matron Zou and Qiao Mengrong trade startled glances—as if silently confirming: He isn't the old Prince Yi.

申简辰看到，不希望她们失望，赶紧解释：“身体好了，自然什么都能吃，都爱吃，不是口粗又是什么？”
Catching their dismay, Shen Jianchen smoothed it over fast: "Healthy now means everything tastes good—of course I'm not picky!"

两人听后，终于放心，三人继续安心享用早膳。
Reassured, both smiled and tucked into breakfast.

申简辰边吃边想：“王府这么多人，一日三餐吃的也不错，开销应该挺大。自从我穿越而来，也没干什么正事，到底钱从哪里来？”
Chewing away, a practical question hit Shen Jianchen: Feeding this massive household three lavish meals a day must cost a fortune—and since arriving he hadn't worked a day! Where did the money come from?

他看向邹嬷嬷，问道：“嬷嬷，好多事情我不记得，想问问，这么大的仪王府每日的花销都从哪里来？”
Looking over at Matron Zou, he asked, "Matron, since I forgot everything—where does the money to run Prince Yi's Manor come from?"

邹嬷嬷正在吃粥，听到仪王发问，放下勺子：“王爷一直身体病弱，官家虽没安排任何职务，但体恤仪王，俸禄照给。您是官家的儿子，名下自然有许多良田铺子，都有专人负责管理，每年也有收益。加之乔贵妃生前从乔家带的嫁妆，以及陛下赐给的，也均是良田和铺子，有的在汴梁，有的在临安，还有分散在其他地方的。总之，收益虽比不上官家别的儿子，养活整个仪王府绰绰有余。”
Setting down her spoon, Matron Zou explained: "Though illness kept Your Highness from holding official posts, His Majesty pitied you and kept your princely stipend flowing in full. As an imperial son, you also own vast estates and storefronts managed by stewards bringing in yearly profits—plus Consort Qiao's dowry from the Qiao family and imperial gifts from His Majesty, all landed estates and shops spread across Bianliang, Lin'an, and beyond. Less than your imperial brothers hold, perhaps, yet more than enough to keep Prince Yi's Manor thriving!"

申简辰听后很开心，没想到穿越而来，竟然一下子变成一位有田产之人，这下他可放心，不用担忧怎么养活整个王府的人。
Delight lit Shen Jianchen's face—landed wealth handed straight to him! Zero stress over payrolls now!
