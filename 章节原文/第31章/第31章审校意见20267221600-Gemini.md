### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 现代流行语与古风成语的地道转化（对应第3段）
> **【原文】** 要说乔梦容长得也是花容月貌,可申简辰面对她多情的眼神就是不来电。说白了,乔梦容是赵朴的菜,不是他申简辰的菜。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Qiao Mengrong was indeed beautiful with a **flower-like face and moon-like appearance**. But when facing her amorous gaze, Shen Jianchen just **couldn't feel attracted**. To put it bluntly, Qiao Mengrong was **Zhao Pu's type, not his type**.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Qiao Mengrong was undeniably a **breathtaking beauty**, but no matter how amorous her gazes were, Shen Jianchen simply felt **no spark**. To put it bluntly, she was **Zhao Pu's cup of tea, not his**.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. “花容月貌”直译为 flower-like and moon-like 会让英语读者一头雾水，直接意译为 "breathtaking beauty"（惊人的美貌）更自然。
    2. “不来电”翻译为 "no spark" 完美契合现代人的恋爱口语。
    3. “谁的菜”用英文经典俚语 "someone's cup of tea" 替换平淡的 "type"，既幽默又极其地道。

#### 2. 男主“死缠烂打”的生动刻画（对应第13段）
> **【原文】** 本着精诚所至金石为开的原则,选择死缠烂打的方法,想着不久肯定会将她拿下,谁知发生了地震。要不是因为地震,说不定那晚,他已将美人拥入怀中。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Following the principle of "**absolute sincerity can crack even metal and stone**," he chose the method of **persistent pursuit**, thinking he would surely win her over soon. Who knew an earthquake would happen? If not for the earthquake, perhaps that very night, he would have already **embraced the beauty in his arms**.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Believing in the principle that "**relentless perseverance can melt even the hardest stone**," he had resorted to **shameless badgering**, entirely confident he would soon win her heart. Who could have foreseen the earthquake? If not for that disaster, he might have already **swept the beauty into his arms** that very night.
*   **💡 修改理由:** "死缠烂打" 译为 persistent pursuit 太过正式，"shameless badgering" (厚颜无耻的纠缠) 更符合男主回想自己当“舔狗”时的自嘲。动词 "swept... into his arms" 比 "embraced" 更有动作张力和浪漫色彩。

#### 3. 内心纠结与吐槽的“网文感”排版（对应第34、35段）
> **【原文】** 他觉得这个世界真的很奇怪... 接还是不接？...天理难容！关键是他心里装着千里寻。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    **He felt this world was truly strange.** Before he could win Qianli Xun's heart, he came to an unfamiliar era... **Accept it or not?** If he didn't accept it... Embracing her in his arms and even marrying her when he didn't love her—this would be **against heaven's principles!** 
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ***This world is seriously messed up,*** he thought. Before he could even win Qianli Xun's heart, he had been tossed into an unfamiliar era... 
    ***Do I accept her feelings or not?*** If he didn't, her pitiful, lovestruck expressions made him feel guilty. But if he did, it would be a total betrayal of his own conscience. Embracing—or worse, marrying—a woman he didn't love? ***That would be a crime against heaven!***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 在英文网文中，**所有心理活动和吐槽必须使用斜体 (Italics)**。不仅修正了排版，还将 "truly strange" 升级为更口语化的 "seriously messed up" (简直糟透了/太奇葩了)，增强了现代人在古代的违和感与幽默感。

#### 4. “眼神交流”的张力与俚语（对应第52、53段）
> **【原文】** 申简辰说:"以后嬷嬷和梦容爱吃什么,我就是吃什么,不用特别照顾,我天生口粗。"
> 听到"天生口粗",邹嬷嬷与乔梦容面面相觑,她们似乎用目光向对方说:"他不是以前的仪王。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    ...I'm **naturally not picky about food**."
    Hearing "**naturally not picky about food**," Nanny Zou and Qiao Mengrong looked at each other. They seemed to say to each other with their eyes, "**He's not the previous Prince Yi.**"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ...I've always had **a peasant's palate** anyway."
    Hearing the phrase "**a peasant's palate**," Nanny Zou and Qiao Mengrong exchanged a bewildered glance. Their eyes seemed to silently scream the same realization: ***He isn't the Prince Yi we knew.***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. “天生口粗”直译为 not picky 太过普通，"have a peasant's palate" (长了个穷人/乡下人的胃口) 更符合古代贵族听到这种话时的震惊感。
    2. “用目光向对方说”改为 "silently scream the same realization" (眼神里无声地尖叫着同一个结论)，戏剧张力直接拉满。

#### 5. 发现自己是“土豪”的窃喜（对应第58段）
> **【原文】** 申简辰听后很开心,没想到穿越而来,竟然一下子变成一位有田产之人,这下他可放心,不用担忧怎么养活整个王府的人。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    After hearing this, Shen Jianchen was very happy. He didn't expect that after transmigrating, he would suddenly become **a person with estates**. Now he could rest assured and not worry about how to support the entire residence.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Shen Jianchen was overjoyed. He never expected that by transmigrating, he would instantly become **a wealthy landowner**. He could finally breathe a sigh of relief—at least he wouldn't have to stress over how to feed a mansion full of people!
*   **💡 修改理由:** "a person with estates" 听起来像法律文书。改为 "a wealthy landowner" (有钱的地主) 更加干脆。"breathe a sigh of relief" 和 "stress over how to feed..." 更生动地刻画了男主从“担忧破产”到“发现自己是富二代”的如释重负感。
