## Chapter 121: Wenyuan Locked Away Without Freedom

Having trussed his son up tight, Qiao Shouren sat opposite him in silence all the way back to Qiao Manor.

Fury simmered in Qiao Wenxian. Father had gone way too far—he was a respected scholar of Lin'an, yet Father hadn't spared him a shred of dignity, tying him up like a thief and even threatening suicide in front of Prince Yi! Never in all his years had he seen Father like this.

Worse, facing iron stubborness like this made winning his parents' blessing to wed Shuzhen look hopeless.

Remembering Prince Yi's suggestion to elope, Qiao Wenxian kicked himself for turning it down yesterday! Had he agreed and carried Shuzhen off yesterday, none of this would have happened.

Watching his son squirm in ropes hurt Qiao Shouren's heart too, yet he had no choice.

Last night, when his trusted servant at the retreat rushed over reporting Young Master had kept Miss Zhu overnight, panic seized Qiao Shouren lest his son do something reckless.

If the two crossed a line together, there would be no undoing it—even cast out by Zhu Manor, the Zhus would raise hell and force the Qiaos into marriage!

Worse still, what if they eloped? He knew his own innocent son, yet who knew what Miss Zhu was capable of? With a reputation that dark, what wouldn't she try—sleeping overnight at another family's house with two unmarried men under the roof!

How on earth had the Prefect raised such a shameless daughter?! Having been married once already, she knew the ways of the world and had nothing left to lose, whereas pure-hearted Wenyuan stood no chance against her charms.

Lying awake all night in dread, Qiao Shouren had rallied his men at dawn prepared for anything—determined to drag his son home in ropes and lock him up for ten days or half a month until he came to his senses and cut ties with Miss Zhu.

"Quit glaring at me like that. Hate me today if you want; one day you'll thank me."

Qiao Wenxian refused to give his father a single word.

"Forget Miss Zhu. Lin'an is overflowing with fine girls—why cling to her? If you ask me, those street rumors didn't lie one bit! She's no decent girl!"

Hearing Father smear Shuzhen broke Qiao Wenxian's silence: "Scold me all you want, Father, just don't slander Shuzhen! In my eyes, she's the finest woman alive."

"Finest? Does a decent girl stay overnight at a man's house on a whim?"

"Princess Jiahe invited her! She shared a bed with Princess Jiahe and even sent her maid back to tell her matron!"

"So what? What respectable daughter sleeps under someone else's roof overnight?"

"Save your breath, Father—to me, Shuzhen is the best woman in this world."

"Believe that if you like—then sit tight at Qiao Manor! The day you forget Miss Zhu is the day I let you out."

Qiao Wenxian clamped his jaw shut.

Back at Qiao Manor, Qiao Shouren ordered servants to haul his son into his usual bedchamber, nail every window shut, and leave only one small square hatch just wide enough to slide meal trays through.

Inspecting the room to confirm no escape route existed, Qiao Shouren untied his son himself, then had Nan Shuang shoved inside too.

"You stay locked in here with Young Master—wait on him and talk some sense into him. When he stops pining for Miss Zhu, you both come out."

Stepping outside, Qiao Shouren pulled the door shut, and Nan Shuang heard the heavy padlock click home.

"Young Master, Master really locked us in!"

Eerily calm, Qiao Wenxian flopped back onto his bed: "Let him lock us in. Eat when food comes, sleep when tired—Father can lock up my body, never my heart! I'll never give up Shuzhen—see what he can do about it! Think he'll lock me up till I die?"

Nan Shuang grinned: "Great attitude, Young Master! With all this free time, why not teach me chess?"

"Deal!" Qiao Wenxian sounded light, yet agony twisted his chest.

Hearing Master had hauled their son home in ropes, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi hurried to his courtyard, equal parts relieved and worried, calling through the hatch.

Peering through the square opening, Concubine Mi called, "Son, Sister and I came to see you."

Hearing their voices, Nan Shuang stepped out from the inner chamber and spoke through the hatch: "Madam, Concubine Mi, Young Master is lying down inside—please persuade Master to let him out!"

Madam Qian scolded him: "What do you know, boy? Go fetch Young Master—I have words for him."

Nan Shuang ducked back inside: "Young Master, Madam and Concubine Mi want to see you."

Rising from bed, Qiao Wenxian walked to the window, hoping to recruit them to soften his father.

Thoughtful as ever, Nan Shuang pulled over a chair: "Sit while you talk, Young Master."

Sitting down, Qiao Wenxian spotted Concubine Mi's face framed in the hatch: "Mother, Mama, what's the point of visiting? Father locked me up like a prisoner."

Madam Qian pleaded, "Be good, Wenyuan—forget Miss Zhu. She isn't right for you."

"Save your words, Mother. Lock me up for life and I still won't give up Shuzhen."

Concubine Mi fretted: "Why are you so stubborn?! You're giving her your whole heart, yet who knows if she feels the same! Her name outside is mud—yesterday I even heard folk claiming she and Young Master Dongfang carry on behind closed doors..."

"Rubbish! Nobody knows Shuzhen and Brother Dongfang better than me!"

"How could you know? Were you glued to them day and night?" Madam Qian chimed in. "Yesterday Intendant An's wife invited me for tea—her son goes to your salons too. Word is Young Master Dongfang runs those salons just to praise Miss Zhu's verse and collects her poems to spread everywhere! Tell me, if there's nothing going on, why would he do all that?"

Qiao Wenxian barked a bitter laugh: "Give a dog a bad name and hang him! Nothing could be cleaner than Shuzhen and Brother Dongfang—just because he admires her poetry, folk twist it into filth! This world can't tell black from white!"

"Son," Concubine Mi added, "I heard Young Master Dongfang got locked up at home too—Master Dongfang forbade him from holding salons or seeing Miss Zhu ever again."

Qiao Wenxian let out a hollow sigh: "Looks like our six-person poetry circle is done for."

Madam Qian asked, "What did you mutter, Wenyuan? Speak up—Mother can't hear."

Raising his voice, Qiao Wenxian called, "I said instead of wasting breath on me, go tell Father to ignore rumors and go propose to the Zhu family!"

With that, he stood, walked back into the inner room, and collapsed onto bed.

Calling over and over to dead silence, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi gave up and went off to confer with Qiao Shouren.

Out in the courtyard, Qiao Shouren stood watering his flowers at a leisurely pace, calm as could be.

Madam Qian marched up, plucked the ladle from his hand, and passed it to a servant: "With servants all around, why water flowers yourself? Figure out what to do about Wenyuan!"

Taking the ladle right back and scooping more water from the wooden bucket, Qiao Shouren said, "Tending flowers calms the mind—let servants do it and where's the calm?"

Madam Qian protested, "Calm your mind all you like, yet keeping Wenyuan locked up solves nothing."

Watering on, Qiao Shouren chided them: "You two women have zero patience. Locked under our roof, at least we know he can't sneak off to see Miss Zhu. Out of sight, out of mind—give it time and his infatuation will cool."

Concubine Mi worried: "Makes sense, Master, yet Wenyuan won't let go overnight."

Qiao Shouren's jaw set hard: "If a month isn't enough, lock him up a month! Half a year isn't enough, lock him up half a year! A year isn't enough, lock him up a whole year!"

Tears sprang to Madam Qian's eyes: "What if locking him up drives him out of his mind?! He's the Qiao family's only son!"

Concubine Mi nodded fast: "I've really heard of people going mad after being locked up too long! No lie, Master—it's true!"

With both women nagging his ears off, Qiao Shouren lost all mood for gardening, tossed the ladle to a servant, and headed indoors.

Both women trailed right after him: "Master, we can't just leave Wenyuan locked up!"

Halting at the doorway, Qiao Shouren turned around: "Relax—I won't keep him locked up long, a month at most. Use this time to pick a fine girl and nail down Wenyuan's betrothal fast! Once a marriage is set, what does it matter who sits in his heart?"

Madam Qian brightened: "Brilliant as ever, Master! Sister and I will get right on it!"

The two women hurried off one after the other, praying to settle a bride fast so Wenyuan could walk free again.
