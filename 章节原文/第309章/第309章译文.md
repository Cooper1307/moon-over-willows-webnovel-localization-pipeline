## Chapter 309: Selecting the Family Heir

The big day arrived—nobody beaming brighter than Chen Haodong and his parents.

Xinglong Pavilion’s grand reception hall stood freshly arrayed: two seats of honor up front—Prefect Zhu Xiyan on the left, Old Madam Xia on the right. Below them on the left sat invited officials in the front row with Granduncle Chen Lu and a dozen clan elders behind; on the right sat the three Chen brothers and three candidates in front, wives and womenfolk behind. On a center table sat three wooden ballot boxes labeled Chen Haodong, Chen Haoyu, and Chen Haofei.

Seated among the wives, Qianli Xun felt an uneasy flutter—not over whether Haoyu won, only wondering what wild bomb Ye Anya planned to drop in this solemn hall.

Chen Jianglin rose to greet the Prefect, officials, Old Madam Xia, and elders, introducing Second Brother Chen Jiangshu to read the clan’s strict qualifications—when Matron Zou’s voice echoed from outside: “Princess Consort Yi arrives!”

Delight flashed across Chen Haodong’s face—she kept her word! Everyone stood baffled why royalty graced a merchant vote, Qianli Xun’s stomach tightening as Qiao Mengrong swept in flanked by Matron Zou and Xilan, shooting a fleeting sneer straight at Zhu Shuzhen.

“At ease,” Qiao Mengrong declared. “Came uninvited simply to back one standout Chen junior whose virtue and skill won wide praise—making sure he doesn’t get overlooked.”

Only Zhu Xiyan dared ask, “Which junior does Your Highness honor?”

“Eldest Young Master Chen Haodong!”

Satisfied murmurs rippled through the elders—locking in even the few fence-sitters—while Chen Haodong’s parents beamed triumphant. *What backroom deal ties Chen Haodong to Mengrong?* Qianli Xun wondered.

Old Madam Xia yielded her head chair to Her Highness, Chen Haoyu smoothly gave up his front-row seat to sit beside his wife—drawing an approving glance from Chen Jianglin as Old Madam Xia took the vacated front chair.

“Sharp move giving up your seat for Grandmother,” Qianli Xun whispered.

“Outcome’s already locked—why warm a front chair?” he whispered back, earning her smile.

Unrolling a scroll, Chen Jiangshu read the six pillars aloud: filial virtue; diligence and courage; mastery of trade; a wife of spotless family; a virtuous wife of fair repute; and clan unity.

Two whole rules about wives! Rule five alone torpedoed Chen Haoyu—karma for his own old gossip! Qianli Xun shot him a playful glare, softening at once seeing his carefree grin.

Next came the three candidates’ speeches. Chen Haodong boasted of training since age eight, running deals by twelve, and using royal backing to conquer Bianliang’s market—drawing warm applause. Chen Haoyu kept it breezy: “Still a rookie—if I had a vote I’d cast it for Brother Dong! Wherever he points, I’ll follow.” (Making Chen Haodong sneer inside while Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan nodded in quiet pride.) Chen Haofei kept it shorter still, focused purely on his books yet pledging full brotherhood.

Voting opened. Led by Chen Lu, every single clan elder dropped their ballots straight into Chen Haodong’s box. After brief thought, Old Madam Xia, Chen Jianglin, and Chen Jiangmu followed suit; certain of victory, Chen Jiangshu played modest and dropped his lone token ballot into Chen Haoyu’s box.

Two officials tallied the count: “Twenty ballots cast—Chen Haodong nineteen, Chen Haoyu one, Chen Haofei zero!”

Glancing at Zhu Shuzhen and seeing zero disappointment soured Qiao Mengrong’s glee.

Zhu Xiyan rose to seal it. “Clearly Eldest Young Master Chen Haodong is chosen—any objections?”

Heart pounding in the back row, Ye Anya seized her one opening and stood straight up. “I object!”

Shock froze the hall. Madam Bao hissed, “Sit down at once!” while Chen Haodong boiled inside swearing to skin her alive afterward. Qianli Xun held her breath—*here comes the twist!*

Steeling her voice, Ye Anya declared, “I object to Chen Haodong inheriting because I—his wife—am of corrupt conduct, unfit for ‘virtuous repute,’ and about to be notorious across Lin’an!”

Horrified, Old Madam Xia cried, “Anya, stop slandering yourself!”

“Not slander, Grandmother—truth! I’m pregnant! Call a physician right now to check my pulse!”

Madam Bao brightened. “Pregnant?! Wonderful—Haodong has an heir, double joy today!”

Sweat broke across Chen Haodong’s brow.

Pulling a folded sheet from her sleeve, Ye Anya held it high. “Don’t celebrate too fast, Mother-in-law! This child isn’t Chen Haodong’s—he’s sterile and can’t father children, and this medical prescription proves it!”

Chen Haodong sprang up roaring, “Waving some fake paper raving like a lunatic! Get back to your room!”

“Drag her out!” Chen Jiangshu barked.

Two male servants seized her arms, yet she fought and shouted across the hall: “Chen Haodong sleeps with brothel courtesans, can’t sire an heir, and plots against Haoyu nonstop out of jealousy—pick him and Chen Manor will live to regret it!”

Qianli Xun shot a desperate look at Old Madam Xia, who thundered right on cue: “Halt!”

Breaking free, Ye Anya knelt before Old Madam Xia and handed up the prescription. “His name is right on it, Grandmother—call the physician who wrote it and verify every word!”

Seeing her eldest grandson’s name plain on the paper, Old Madam Xia tucked it away and bowed with poised grace to the guests. “An unforeseen household crisis—forgive us for troubling Your Highness and Lord Prefect. Once sorted, Jianglin will thank you in person.”

Disappointed she hadn’t even rattled Zhu Shuzhen, Qiao Mengrong swept straight out without a word; Zhu Xiyan—secretly tickled his son-in-law might get another shot—withdrew politely with his officials.

Clan elders stayed seated as Old Madam Xia reclaimed the chair. “Sit, Anya. Everyone sit.”

Chen Haoyu leaned close to whisper, “Did Eldest Sister-in-law lose her mind?”

“People only snap when backed off a cliff,” Qianli Xun murmured back.

“Don’t believe her lies, Grandmother!” Chen Haodong pleaded.

“Sit down, Haodong,” Old Madam Xia commanded. “Clean this up in the open, or whispers will dog you forever.”

Gritting his teeth, Chen Haodong made up his mind: write a divorce letter right here on the spot and cut her loose before she wrecked his crown.
