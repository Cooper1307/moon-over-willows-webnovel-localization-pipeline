## Chapter 309: Electing the Chen Clan Heir

Election morning arrived at Xinglong Pavilion's grand hall: Prefect Zhu Xiyan and Old Madam Xia seated side by side at the head, officials and ten clan elders led by Granduncle Lu on the left, Chen's three brothers, three candidates, and all the wives on the right, and three wooden ballot boxes on a central table. Qianli Xun sat tense—not over Haoyu, yet wondering what bomb Ye Anya planned to drop.

Just as Chen Jiangshu rose to read the rules, Matron Zou's ringing call cut through the courtyard: *"Princess Consort Yi arrives!"*

Sweeping in to surprised bows, Qiao Mengrong declared openly she had come specifically to back Eldest Young Master Chen Haodong—sending Haodong's parents soaring and locking in every clan elder's vote! Claiming Old Madam Xia's top seat, she watched Haoyu smoothly surrender his front chair for Grandmother and slip back beside his wife.

Chen Jiangshu read out the six strict heir requirements—virtue and filial piety, diligence, business mastery, wife of clean birth, wife of spotless renown, and clan unity. All three candidates gave brief speeches—Haodong bragging of Bianliang expansion backed by royalty, Haoyu happily endorsing Brother Dong, and Haofei bowing out for his books—before the twenty voters cast ballots: **nineteen votes for Haodong, one for Haoyu** (a face-saving courtesy vote from his uncle Chen Jiangshu), **zero for Haofei**.

Seeing zero gloom on Zhu Shuzhen's face deflated Mengrong's spite—just as Zhu Xiyan asked routinely, "Does anyone present object?"

"I object!" Ye Anya shot to her feet!

Shock rippled across the hall. Ignoring Madam Bao's hiss and Haodong's boiling glare, Anya spoke clear as a bell: "Chen Haodong is unfit—because *I*, his wife, am unchaste and disgraced! I'm pregnant—and the child isn't his! Because Chen Haodong is *impotent*—here is his doctor's prescription to prove it!"

As Chen Jiangshu yelled for male servants to drag her out while she shouted about Haodong's brothel trysts and jealousy toward Haoyu, Old Madam Xia thundered *"Stop!"*—accepting the prescription bearing Haodong's own name, then gracefully excusing Princess Consort Yi and Prefect Zhu from messy family business.

With outsiders gone—Mengrong storming off empty-handed, Zhu Xiyan secretly thrilled for his son-in-law—Old Madam Xia resumed the chair, determined to dig out the truth while Haodong seethed, resolved to repudiate Anya on the spot to save his crown.
