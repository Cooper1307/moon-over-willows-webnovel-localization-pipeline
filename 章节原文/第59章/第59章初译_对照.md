# 第59章 成功说服朱晞颜
# Chapter 59: Successfully Persuading Prefect Zhu

---

朱晞颜看到院中站着一人，背影尽显贵气，肯定是位王爷。只是想不通仪王长期病着，官家并不重视，怎么突然好了？还被任命为临安节度使？

Zhu Xiyan saw a person standing in the courtyard. His bearing exuded nobility—definitely a prince. He just couldn't figure out: Prince Yi had been ill for a long time, and the emperor didn't pay much attention to him. How did he suddenly recover? And why was he appointed Military Commissioner of Lin'an?

---

朱晞颜没有时间纠结心中的疑惑，与林觉上前拜道："不知仪王驾到，有失远迎，还望恕罪。"

Zhu Xiyan had no time to dwell on his doubts. He and Lin Jue stepped forward and bowed: "We didn't know Prince Yi was visiting. We didn't welcome you properly. Please forgive us."

---

申简辰转过身，被年长之人以礼相拜，甚觉荣耀："临安风景美，我想多玩几日，故而没有公开身份，知府大人何罪之有？"

Shen Jianchen turned around. Being respectfully greeted by an elder filled him with honor: "The scenery in Lin'an is beautiful. I wanted to stay a few more days, so I didn't reveal my identity. What offense could the prefect possibly have?"

---

"宴会中没认出仪王就是罪过。"

"Not recognizing Prince Yi at the banquet is an offense."

---

"知府大人没认出我，十分正常，在汴梁时，很多朝中大臣也没怎么见过我。"

"It's perfectly normal that the prefect didn't recognize me. When I was in Bianliang, many court officials hadn't seen me much either."

---

朱晞颜不知该如何搭话，只能说："仪王，里边请。"

Zhu Xiyan didn't know how to respond and could only say: "Prince Yi, please come inside."

---

申简辰走进屋内随便挑张椅子坐下。朱晞颜吩咐下人上茶，他与林觉依然弯腰站立。

Shen Jianchen walked into the room and casually picked a chair to sit. Zhu Xiyan ordered a servant to serve tea. He and Lin Jue remained standing respectfully.

---

下人上好茶后，申简辰说："知府大人就坐吧，站着怪累的。"

After the servant served the tea, Shen Jianchen said: "Please sit down, Prefect. Standing must be tiring."

---

朱晞颜不敢坐下："下官还是站着聆听仪王教诲。"

Zhu Xiyan didn't dare sit: "Your subordinate will stand and listen to Prince Yi's instructions."

---

岳父大人不坐，林觉也不敢随便就坐，恭恭敬敬弯腰站立着。

Since the father-in-law didn't sit, Lin Jue didn't dare sit either. He stood respectfully with his waist bent.

---

申简辰不好再劝："自从我疾病痊愈后，与高太尉比试蹴鞠，不小心赢了他，官家一高兴，给了我临安节度使的职位，不得已来此任职。我对临安不熟，以后还要多多仰仗知府大人。"

Shen Jianchen didn't press further: "Ever since I recovered from my illness, I competed in cuju with Grand Marshal Gao and unexpectedly won. The emperor was so pleased that he gave me the position of Military Commissioner of Lin'an. I had no choice but to take up the post here. I'm not familiar with Lin'an, so I'll have to rely on the prefect from now on."

---

"仪王能来临安任职是临安之福，下官定会听从仪王差遣。"

"Prince Yi's appointment to Lin'an is a blessing for the city. Your subordinate will certainly follow your commands."

---

"差遣谈不上，我初来乍到，需先熟悉环境，至于临安事务，知府大人以前如何现在依然如何。"

"Commands are too strong a word. I'm new here and need to get familiar with the environment first. As for Lin'an's affairs, the prefect should continue managing them just as before."

---

听仪王这话，显然不大愿意多插手临安事务，朱晞颜心里轻松许多："下官记下，明日下官定携临安全体官员为仪王接风洗尘。"

Hearing Prince Yi's words, it was clear he didn't want to interfere much in Lin'an's affairs. Zhu Xiyan felt much more at ease: "Your subordinate takes note. Tomorrow, I'll definitely bring all of Lin'an's officials to host a welcome banquet for Prince Yi."

---

申简辰拒绝道："不必，今日参加宴会，算是接风洗尘。"

Shen Jianchen declined: "No need. Attending today's banquet counts as the welcome."

---

朱晞颜依然客气道："那怎么成？仪王来临安任职，官员们需隆重迎接才对。"

Zhu Xiyan still insisted politely: "How can that be? With Prince Yi coming to Lin'an, the officials should give you a grand welcome."

---

想到北宋即将成为南宋，之后又是各种朝代更替，申简辰一点儿从政的欲望都没有。他来临安主要是找千里寻，至于其他，并不看重。

Thinking about how the Northern Song would soon become the Southern Song, followed by various dynastic changes, Shen Jianchen had no desire to engage in politics at all. He came to Lin'an mainly to find Qianli Xun. As for everything else, he didn't care much.

---

"我向来喜欢低调，隆重迎接也免了，知府大人只需吩咐人在府衙给我留个办理公务的地儿即可，有时间我会过去坐坐。"

"I've always preferred to keep a low profile, so spare me the grand welcome. The prefect just needs to arrange an office for me at the prefecture office. I'll drop by when I have time."

---

朱晞颜又一阵开心，庆幸来了位闲散王爷，只要他不过分参与临安府衙之事，大权就不会旁落他人："临安的军政要务以后还需仪王多多费心。"

Zhu Xiyan was pleased again, relieved that a leisurely prince had come. As long as he didn't interfere too much in Lin'an's affairs, the power wouldn't fall into others' hands: "Lin'an's military and political affairs will need Prince Yi's attention in the future."

---

"那是自然。"寒暄这么多，申简辰不想再寒暄下去，还是尽快表明来意，"我前来见知府大人，是有私事要与您谈，还是坐下说吧。"

"Of course." After so much small talk, Shen Jianchen didn't want to continue. He'd better get to the point: "I came to see the prefect about a personal matter. Let's sit down to discuss it."

---

仪王已是第二次请他坐下，朱晞颜不好再站着，赶紧坐下。再说仪王要谈私事，坐下才有谈私事的氛围。

Prince Yi had asked him to sit for the second time. Zhu Xiyan couldn't keep standing and quickly sat down. Besides, if Prince Yi wanted to discuss a personal matter, sitting down created the right atmosphere.

---

林觉见岳父大人坐下，也跟着坐下。

Seeing his father-in-law sit down, Lin Jue followed and sat as well.

---

朱晞颜问："不知仪王要与下官谈什么私事？"

Zhu Xiyan asked: "I wonder what personal matter Prince Yi wants to discuss with your subordinate?"

---

申简辰说："也不是什么大事，林练兵官已知，不如让他告诉知府大人。"

Shen Jianchen said: "It's not a big matter. Military Training Officer Lin already knows. Why not let him tell the prefect?"

---

朱晞颜扭头用疑惑的目光看向林觉："贤婿请讲。"

Zhu Xiyan turned and looked at Lin Jue with a puzzled gaze: "Please speak, son-in-law."

---

林觉说："是我与淑真和离之事，仪王劝说我放了淑真，只因她与乔文仙两情相悦。"

Lin Jue said: "It's about my mutual divorce with Shuzhen. Prince Yi persuaded me to let Shuzhen go because she and Qiao Wenxian are mutually in love."

---

申简辰趁机说："我已说服林练兵官，现在还想说服知府大人，希望你以幽栖居士的幸福为重，同意和离。"

Shen Jianchen seized the opportunity: "I've already persuaded Military Training Officer Lin. Now I want to persuade the prefect. I hope you'll prioritize Youqi Jushi's happiness and agree to the mutual divorce."

---

朱晞颜是聪明人，迅速意识到仪王是帮乔文仙。不过，乔文仙与淑真两情相悦他倒是第一次听说。

Zhu Xiyan was smart and quickly realized that Prince Yi was helping Qiao Wenxian. However, this was the first time he heard that Qiao Wenxian and Shuzhen were mutually in love.

---

和离最大的障碍是女儿未来的婚姻，如果和离后婚姻无法解决，不仅影响女儿，也影响朱家的声誉。

The biggest obstacle to the mutual divorce was his daughter's future marriage. If the marriage couldn't be resolved after the divorce, it would not only affect his daughter but also the Zhu family's reputation.

---

既然她与乔文仙有情，又有仪王出面，乔家娶淑真应该没有多大问题。女儿的未来和朱家的声誉都能保住，他还有什么可说的。

Since she had feelings for Qiao Wenxian, and Prince Yi was intervening, the Qiao family marrying Shuzhen shouldn't be a problem. His daughter's future and the Zhu family's reputation could both be preserved. What more could he say?

---

朱晞颜瞬间释然许多，仪王的面子还是要给的。

Zhu Xiyan instantly felt much more at ease. He still had to give Prince Yi face.

---

"下官本不同意贤婿与淑真和离，主要考虑一段婚姻来之不易。可他们的日子过得并不顺心，此事还惊动仪王，让您费心，仪王都发话，我没什么可反对的，同意便是。"

"I originally didn't agree to the mutual divorce between my son-in-law and Shuzhen, mainly because a marriage is not easily built. But their life together hasn't been happy. This matter has even disturbed Prince Yi and caused you to take the trouble. Since Prince Yi has spoken, I have nothing to object to. I agree."

---

朱晞颜说出"同意"二字，林觉一阵失落，这段婚姻来得快，去得也快。

When Zhu Xiyan said the word "agree," Lin Jue felt a surge of disappointment. This marriage came quickly and left just as fast.

---

申简辰笑道："婚姻就像一扇挂着锁的大门，要成功打开锁，必须用适合的钥匙。若把男子比为锁，女子便是钥匙，一把锁配一把钥匙，锁和钥匙不相配，门怎么都打不开。幽栖居士这把钥匙不适合林练兵官这把锁，分开才是正确的选择，练兵官不必伤感。"

Shen Jianchen smiled: "Marriage is like a big gate with a lock. To successfully open the lock, you need the right key. If a man is the lock, then a woman is the key. One lock matches one key. If the lock and key don't match, the door can never be opened. Youqi Jushi is a key that doesn't fit Military Training Officer Lin's lock. Separating is the right choice. There's no need to be sad."

---

两人齐声说："受教了。"

The two said in unison: "We've learned something."

---

宴会结束后，参加宴会之人纷纷在朱府外乘坐马车回府。

After the banquet ended, the guests one by one took their carriages outside the Zhu residence to return home.

---

申简辰将朱晞颜同意和离之事告诉文渊，乔文仙高兴地说："不行！我太兴奋！必须马上跳下去，直接跑回幽林别居！"

Shen Jianchen told Wenyuan that Zhu Xiyan had agreed to the mutual divorce. Qiao Wenxian happily said: "No way! I'm too excited! I have to jump down right now and run straight back to Youlin Biejü!"

---

他让南双停下马车，直接跳下，在大街上开心地跑着。

He had Nanshuang stop the carriage, jumped right down, and ran happily along the main street.

---

申简辰吩咐南双："马车不要赶得太快，紧跟在文渊身后即可，等他跑累了，好上来休息。"

Shen Jianchen instructed Nanshuang: "Don't drive the carriage too fast. Just follow closely behind Wenyuan. When he's tired from running, he can come up and rest."

---

南双回道："是。"

Nanshuang replied: "Yes, sir."

---

乔梦容从后面的马车里探出头，见乔公子在大街上兴奋地奔跑，笑道："没想到乔公子这么大个人，也像个孩子似的，一高兴就跑起来。"

Qiao Mengrong poked her head out from the carriage behind. Seeing Young Master Qiao running excitedly along the main street, she smiled: "I didn't expect Young Master Qiao, a grown man, to be like a child, running off the moment he's happy."

---

乔梦容将身体坐正，很快又变得伤感。

Qiao Mengrong sat up straight and soon became melancholy again.

---

乔公子高兴定是因为知道幽栖居士和离必然成功，他能因即将得到所爱之人兴奋成那样子。可哥哥呢？自身体恢复健康后，面对他们的感情从未兴奋过。

Young Master Qiao must be happy because he knew Youqi Jushi's mutual divorce would surely succeed. He could be that excited about soon getting the person he loved. But what about her brother? Ever since he recovered his health, he had never been excited about their relationship.

---

喜兰问道："郡主，您怎么了？"

Xilan asked: "Princess, what's wrong?"

---

乔梦容沉默一会儿，想听听喜兰的看法："喜兰，你说现在的哥哥到底喜不喜欢我？"

Qiao Mengrong was silent for a moment, wanting to hear Xilan's opinion: "Xilan, do you think Brother really likes me now?"

---

喜兰心里虽怀疑，还是肯定道："王爷当然喜欢郡主。"

Although Xilan had doubts in her heart, she affirmed: "The prince certainly likes the princess."

---

"可我怎么越来越感觉不到哥哥对我的感情？"

"But why do I feel less and less of Brother's feelings for me?"

---

"郡主不要瞎想，王爷病好后是有些变化，可王爷对郡主依然关怀照顾。"

"Princess, don't overthink it. The prince has changed somewhat after recovering, but he still cares for and looks after the princess."

---

关怀照顾不假，可哥哥对她更像是对待家人，而非爱人。

It was true that he cared and looked after her, but Brother treated her more like a family member than a lover.

---

她悄悄羡慕起幽栖居士，还未和离，就有人为她爱得疯狂，她与乔公子在宴会上眉目传情，乔梦容偶尔看到，也是歆羡不已。

She secretly envied Youqi Jushi. Even before the divorce was finalized, someone loved her madly. She and Young Master Qiao exchanged loving glances at the banquet. Qiao Mengrong occasionally saw it and was filled with envy.

---

为何哥哥不主动看她一眼？哥哥是不懂？还是根本不愿意？乔梦容宁可相信哥哥根本不懂。

Why didn't Brother take the initiative to look at her? Did Brother not understand? Or did he simply not want to? Qiao Mengrong preferred to believe that Brother simply didn't understand.
