第256章 陈府办案偶相遇
## Chapter 256: Crossing Paths During an Investigation at Chen Manor

回到陈府南门口，千里寻刚走下马车，冲上来一群人，队列两边，从衣着判断应是府衙之人。
正纳闷，瞧见申简辰向这边走来，他的身边跟着白风和两个人，那两人看着面生。他现在是司理参军，应该是与他一起办差之人。
一再叮嘱过自己不要再见眼前之人，还是不期而遇。
申简辰想过好几种遇见千里寻的方式，唯独没有预设是在办理公务之时。见到日思夜想之人，他不由停住脚步，注视一会儿，才走上前。
“你……可好？”
他眼中依然饱含感情，与以前相比丝毫不差，难怪梦容今日会如此对待她。
千里寻从心底根本无法对眼前之人生起埋怨之情，谁让她的内心依然还有无法向人道出的情感。已决定要放下他，自然不能再有任何流露。
千里寻恭敬行礼：“仪王殿下，民妇自从嫁到陈家一切安好，劳烦贵人挂念。”
如此充满距离的话语，申简辰早已预料到，还是忍不住说：“我会继续寻找会爬树的猪。”
千里寻心中为之一动，他怎么还如此执着？必须打击两句，让他清醒清醒。
The moment Qianli Xun stepped out of her carriage at Chen Manor's south gate, a column of men rushed up and flanked both sides of the entrance—yamen runners, judging by their uniforms.

“殿下的爱好实在独特，民妇难以理解。别说我没见过会爬树的猪，即使真有，殿下找到又能如何？不如珍惜当下，不要把时间浪费在毫无意义的事情上。”
Blinking in puzzlement, she saw Shen Jianchen striding straight toward her, flanked by Bai Feng and two unfamiliar men—colleagues from his new Judicial Investigator post, no doubt. She had just sworn up and down never to see this man again, and here they bumped right into each other.

“无论你说什么，都无法动摇我的想法，否则我都不知道来到这个时代还有什么意义？”
Shen Jianchen had imagined a dozen ways he might run into Qianli Xun again, never once picturing an official case call. Seeing the woman who filled his waking thoughts stopped him dead in his tracks; he gazed at her for a quiet beat before stepping forward.

“殿下身为仪王又是临安节度使，现在又是司理参军，只要用好其中任何一个身份，都可做出有意义之事，不愁在这个时代实现不了自身的价值。”
"Are you... well?"

“我当然会做有意义的事，但我依然不会放弃。”
The devotion burning in his eyes hadn't dimmed by a spark—no wonder Mengrong had lashed out like a cornered beast today. Deep down, Qianli Xun could never bring herself to resent him, since her own heart still harbored feelings she couldn't speak aloud. Yet she had vowed to let go, so not a crack could show.

正说着，陈江林从陈府出来，行礼道：“不知仪王驾到，有失远迎。”
She dropped into a formal, respectful curtsy. "Your Highness Prince Yi. Since marrying into the Chen family, this humble wife has fared very well. Thank you for your gracious concern."

仪王显然有公务，千里寻不便多言。已说得够多，再说下去恐怕被人误会。今日诗会被人辱骂之语还萦绕在耳边，往后需老实窝在陈府写话本，少出去招惹是非。
He had braced himself for that polite wall of ice, yet the words still slipped out of him. "I'm going to keep looking for a pig that can climb trees."

千里寻又屈膝行礼后走进陈府。
Her heart gave a sudden lurch. How could he still cling to that? She had to dash cold water on him and snap him awake. "Your Highness has quite an eccentric hobby—beyond my simple understanding. Forget whether I've ever seen a tree-climbing pig; even if one did exist and you found it, what good would it do? Better cherish the life right in front of you instead of wasting time on pointless pursuits."

苦荞趁机瞧向白风，白风对她笑笑，张嘴摆着口型，说些她猜不明白的话。苦荞皱皱眉头，不予理会，快步跟上小姐。
"Nothing you say can change my mind. Otherwise, I don't even know what meaning my coming to this era has left."

申简辰目送千里寻进入陈府，才看向陈江林：“陈老爷，我今儿是以司理参军的身份来你府上寻一人。”
"You are Prince Yi, Military Commissioner of Lin'an, and now Judicial Investigator too. Put any one of those titles to real use, and you'll accomplish plenty of meaningful work—no shortage of ways to prove your worth in this era."

陈江林仍旧弯腰而立：“不知仪王要寻何人？”
"Oh, I'll do meaningful work," he answered. "Still, I'm not giving up."

“听说陈府的兴隆阁有位管账的方管事名叫方计远？”
Right then, Chen Jianglin hurried out of the manor and bowed low. "I was unaware Your Highness had arrived—forgive me for failing to greet you at the gate!"

“方管事是叫方计远，不知仪王找他何事？”
Prince Yi was clearly here on official business, and she had already said more than enough; lingering risked wagging tongues. With today's vicious salon insults still ringing in her ears, she meant to hole up inside Chen Manor writing huaben and stay clear of trouble. Dipping another curtsy, Qianli Xun turned and walked through the gate.

“此处不便多说，陈老爷先带我进去，把方计远唤来，有些事需要当面问他。”
Kuqiao stole a glance at Bai Feng, who grinned and mouthed silent words she couldn't make head or tail of. Frowning, she ignored him and hurried after her mistress.

“仪王，里边请。”
Shen Jianchen watched Qianli Xun disappear inside before turning to Chen Jianglin. "Master Chen, I'm here today in my capacity as Judicial Investigator looking for someone in your household."

陈江林不敢怠慢，将仪王请进兴隆阁的会客室，吩咐伙计摆上茶点和水果，然后派人去叫方管事。
Still bent in deference, Chen Jianglin asked, "Whom does Your Highness seek?"

方管事正在打算盘核对账目，有伙计前来传话：“方管事，大老爷请你去会客室。”
"I hear Xinglong Pavilion has an accountant named Steward Fang—full name Fang Jiyuan?"

方管事停下打算盘的动作：“可知找我何事？”
"Steward Fang's name is indeed Fang Jiyuan. May I ask what Your Highness wants with him?"

伙计说：“是司理院的人找你。”
"Not a conversation for the street. Lead me inside and summon Fang Jiyuan—I have questions to ask him face to face."

方管事纳闷：“司理院的人找我何事？我又没做什么伤天害理的事。”
"Right this way, Your Highness!"

纳闷归纳闷，放下账目，乖乖来到会客室。
Wasting no time, Chen Jianglin ushered the prince into the reception room of Xinglong Pavilion, ordered clerks to bring tea, pastries, and fruit, and sent someone to fetch Steward Fang.

只见会客室的门口站着差役，一个差役问：“你是方计远？”
Steward Fang was clicking away at his abacus checking ledgers when a clerk hurried in. "Steward Fang, Eldest Master wants you in the reception room."

方管事回道：“正是。”
His fingers paused over the beads. "Know what it's about?"

差役进去禀报完，出来说：“大人让你进去。”
"Folks from the Judicial Office are asking for you."

方计远小心进入。
"The Judicial Office? What do they want with me? I haven't done anything crooked!" Baffled as he was, he laid down his ledger and headed straight over.

正面坐着一位年轻英俊的公子，从那穿着看不出是官府之人，既坐在正位应该是参军大人。两边分别站着两人，右边那位他见过，是司理院的都头。下位的左边坐着大老爷陈江林，右边坐着的应该是师爷，手中执笔准备记录案情。
Constables flanked the doorway; one stopped him. "Are you Fang Jiyuan?"

方计远心中不由紧张，难道自己不小心犯了案子？仔细回想，没做过什么错事。赶紧上前拜见：“大人，老爷，不知唤我何事？”
"Yes, that's me."

申简辰问：“你是方计远？”
The constable stepped in to announce him, then returned. "His Honor says go on in."

“小的是方计远。”
Fang Jiyuan stepped inside with trepidation. Seated in the main chair was a handsome young nobleman whose robes didn't look like standard yamen attire—yet sitting center stage meant he had to be the Investigator. Flanking him stood two men, the one on the right familiar as the Judicial Office's chief constable. On the lower left sat Eldest Master Chen Jianglin, and on the right sat a clerk holding a brush poised over paper.

“家中可有四十岁左右的女人失踪？”
Nerves churned in Fang Jiyuan's stomach. Had he somehow gotten tangled in a lawsuit? Racking his brain, he couldn't think of a single offense. He hurried forward to bow. "Your Honor, Master—may I ask why you summoned me?"

方计远想想，自家夫人正是这个年纪，确实不在家，只是回了娘家，于是回道：“没有。”
"You are Fang Jiyuan?" Shen Jianchen asked.

申简辰向旁边的温信看一眼，温信会意，拿出一个玉镯，举在手中，展示给大家。
"Yes, Your Honor, I'm Fang Jiyuan."

申简辰继续问：“可认得此物？”
"Is a woman around forty years old missing from your household?"

方计远瞅去，那玉镯翠绿，看着眼熟，便走近瞧一眼，心中更加紧张，将那玉镯接在手中，翻看一遍，当看到镯子上刻的一个“玉”字，急切地问：“此玉镯正是我家娘子的，怎么会在大人手中？”
Fang Jiyuan blinked—his wife was right around forty, and she wasn't home, though she had only gone back to visit her parents. "No, Your Honor."

申简辰说：“确定是你家娘子之物？”
Shen Jianchen glanced at Wen Xin beside him. Taking the cue, Wen Xin held up a jade bangle for everyone to see.

“小人确定，我家娘子过四十岁生辰时，我买来此物送她，她的名字中有一‘玉’字，我便麻烦工匠刻在玉镯上。”
"Do you recognize this?" Shen Jianchen asked.

“既然是你娘子之物，为何要说没有女人丢失？”
Spotting the vivid emerald jade, Fang Jiyuan leaned in with rising dread. Taking the bangle into his hands, he turned it over—and froze at the character *yu* (玉, jade) carved inside. "This bangle belongs to my wife! How did it end up in Your Honor's hands?"

“我娘子只是回娘家而已。”
"You're certain it's your wife's?"

“你娘子已被人杀害，抛尸在竹林边的溪水旁，现在尸体正放在司理院的停尸房，仵作在停尸房继续查验尸体时，从她身上搜得。奇怪，你家娘子为何不将手镯戴在手上，偏偏装进怀里？”
"Positive! I bought it for her fortieth birthday. Her given name has the character *yu* in it, so I paid the jeweler to engrave it right on the jade."

方计远听到娘子被人杀害，震惊之余，伤心不已，不由跪在地上，摸着玉镯哭起来：“呜呜……，我娘子平日舍不得戴，只有回娘家时才会揣进怀里，呜呜……”
"If it's hers, why did you claim no woman was missing from your home?"

陈江林安慰道：“方管事，事已至此，哭也没用，还是配合大人查找凶手。”
"My wife only went to visit her parents' home!"

申简辰问：“为何你会说你娘子回了娘家？”
"Your wife has been murdered," Shen Jianchen said flatly. "Her body was dumped beside the stream near the bamboo grove and is now lying in the Judicial Office morgue. When the coroner inspected her body again there, he found this tucked inside her robe. Strange—why didn't she wear the bangle on her wrist instead of hiding it inside her clothes?"

方管事止住哭声：“前日我从兴隆阁忙完事回到家中，不见我家娘子，便到处找，没找到人。正生气时，我家下人孙二告诉我她回娘家几日。我埋怨两句，便没说什么。”
Shock gave way to crushing grief. Fang Jiyuan collapsed to his knees, clutching the bangle as tears poured down his face. "My wife hated wearing it for daily chores—she only ever tucked it inside her blouse when going back to her parents' house... *sob*..."

申简辰敏锐地感觉到孙二不对：“孙二？他说的话你为何如此相信？”
Chen Jianglin tried to steady him. "What's done is done, Steward Fang. Crying won't bring her back—help His Honor catch the killer."

“孙二是我远房亲戚的孩子，家住在山里，来临安城混口饭吃，我家正好缺下人，便把他留下，帮夫人干些粗活。这孩子嘴巴甜，整日叔叔婶婶地叫着，我就没把他当外人，他的话自然信。”
"Why did you believe she had gone to her parents' house?" Shen Jianchen pressed.

“此人可还在你家中？”
Swallowing his sobs, Fang Jiyuan answered, "Two days ago, I came home from Xinglong Pavilion and couldn't find her anywhere. Just as I was getting irritated, my house servant Sun Er told me she'd gone home to her parents for a few days. I grumbled a bit and thought nothing more of it."

“自然在，他也没别的去处。”
Shen Jianchen's instincts zeroed straight in on that name. "Sun Er? Why did you take his word so readily?"

申简辰迅速起身：“陈老爷，我需到方计远家中走一趟，将兴隆阁的管事带走，你应该不会反对吧？”
"Sun Er is a distant relative's boy from the hills. Came into Lin'an looking to earn his keep, and since my household was short-handed, I took him in to help my wife with heavy chores. He's got a sweet tongue—always calling us Uncle and Auntie—so I treated him like family and never doubted a word he said."

陈江林赶紧站起来行礼：“仪王尽管带走，不必知会我。”
"Is he still at your house?"

陈江林侧身站立，恭送贵人。等贵人走出，才又跟上，准备将贵人送至大门口。
"He must be—he's got nowhere else to go."

刚走出兴隆阁，申简辰突然停住脚步对白风说：“不必继续跟着，去见你想见的人。”
Shen Jianchen rose at once. "Master Chen, I need to visit Fang Jiyuan's house. You don't object if I take your Xinglong Pavilion steward along?"

白风高兴道：“谢王爷！”
Chen Jianglin sprang up and bowed. "Take him as you please, Your Highness—no need to ask my leave!" Stepping aside to let the prince exit first, he followed along to see him to the main gate.

白风目送着王爷等人往陈府外而去，他则站在原地不知该如何去找苦荞，第一次进陈府，道路一点儿都不熟悉。
Just outside Xinglong Pavilion, Shen Jianchen paused and looked at Bai Feng. "No need to tag along. Go see who you want to see."

正犹豫着，走来一个下人：“白侍卫是想找二少夫人身边的丫鬟苦荞吗？”
Bai Feng's face lit up. "Thank you, Your Highness!"

白风不解：“是的，你怎么知道？”
Watching his master and the constables head out the gate, Bai Feng stood in place scratching his head—his first time inside Chen Manor, and he had no clue where to find Kuqiao.

下人回道：“我家大老爷说的，吩咐我帮您将苦荞叫出来。”
Just then a servant walked up. "Is Guard Bai looking for Kuqiao, Second Young Madam's maid?"

白风觉得这位陈老爷真不简单，连他跟苦荞的事情都知道：“你去告诉苦荞，我在那个亭子里等她。”
"Yes—how did you know?"

下人去后不多会儿，苦荞来到亭子里，见白风正站在亭子里赏花，没好气地说：“你不跟着王爷去忙，找我干什么？”
"Eldest Master guessed and ordered me to fetch her for you."

白风转过身：“你怎么看上去不高兴？”
*This Master Chen doesn't miss a trick—even knows about me and Kuqiao,* Bai Feng marveled. "Tell Kuqiao I'll wait for her in that pavilion over there."

“有什么好高兴的？你跟着王爷跑去什么司理院，以后还不得有很多事情忙？想见你都难。”
A few minutes later, Kuqiao marched into the pavilion, where Bai Feng stood admiring the flowers. "Shouldn't you be off working with Prince Yi?" she asked tartly. "What do you want with me?"

“原来因为这个，放心，刚到司理院一切还没理顺，等过了这一阵，我还会和以前一样三天两头过来见你。你瞧，今日王爷主动允许我留下，他对我多好。”
Bai Feng spun around. "Why the long face?"

苦荞走到亭子中间，叹口气：“唉，仪王的小心思以为我不知？他不过是想让你从我口中打听些有关我家小姐的事。说得好听些，叫痴情。说得难听些，叫风流。都已成亲还惦记我家小姐，他是男子又是王爷，别人不敢将他怎样，可我家小姐是女子，名声对女子多重要？你回去告诉仪王，我家小姐已打算跟姑爷好好过日子，让他别再惦记。”
"What's there to smile about? You followed Prince Yi off to that Judicial Office—you'll be buried in work from now on, and I'll barely ever get to see you!"

白风警惕地瞧瞧周围：“小声些，小心隔墙有耳，被人听去，对幽栖居士不利。”
"So *that's* what's eating you! Relax—we just moved into the Judicial Office, so everything's still chaotic. Once things settle down, I'll be sneaking over every few days just like before. Look, His Highness even told me to stay behind today—isn't he good to me?"

苦荞才知自己因为不满有所失言，也向亭子周围瞧一遍，还好没有别人。
Stepping to the center of the pavilion, Kuqiao let out a long sigh. "Oh, please—think I don't see right through Prince Yi's little scheme? He only let you stay so you could pump me for news about Miss! Call it *devotion* if you want to be polite; call it *philandering* if you're honest! He's already married, yet he keeps hanging onto my mistress. He's a man and a prince—nobody dares lay a hand on him—whereas Miss is a woman, and a woman's reputation is her life! Go back and tell Prince Yi that Miss has decided to settle down and live right with her husband, so tell him to leave her alone."

白风说：“放心，我会把你的话转达给王爷。”
Bai Feng cast a wary eye around the garden. "Keep your voice down! Walls have ears—if someone overhears that, it'll hurt Youqi Jushi."

苦荞轻声说：“是我家小姐的话。”
Realizing frustration had made her careless, Kuqiao glanced around too; thankfully nobody was nearby.

“幽栖居士能这么想，说明她人品不错。”
"Don't worry," Bai Feng said. "I'll pass your words along to His Highness."

“说什么呢？我家小姐向来人品不错，只是别人总是误解她，把她说得一无是处，就今日……”
"They're *Miss's* words," Kuqiao murmured.

苦荞话说一半，想起小姐的叮嘱，不敢继续往下说。
"If Youqi Jushi truly feels that way, it speaks well of her character."

白风好奇地问：“今日怎么了？”
"What kind of nonsense is that? My mistress has *always* had sterling character! It's everyone else who keeps twisting things and slandering her—just like today..." Catching herself mid-sentence, she remembered Qianli Xun's strict orders and bit her tongue.

“没什么。”苦荞掩饰道，迅速转移话题，“不要老说小姐的事，说说我们吧。”
Bai Feng caught the slip. "What happened today?"

白风拉起苦荞的手：“王爷已成亲，我也想向你提亲，娶你过门。”
"Nothing!" Kuqiao covered up, swerving straight off the subject. "Enough about Miss—let's talk about us."

苦荞羞涩后，为难道：“我要是嫁给你，岂不是要跟你住在幽林别居？仪王妃是那里的主人，她肯定不会喜欢我，到时我恐怕没好日子过？”
Bai Feng reached out and took her hand. "His Highness is wed now, so I want to ask for your hand and marry you too."

“仪王妃人很好。”
A blush warmed Kuqiao's cheeks, then faded into a troubled frown. "If I marry you, won't I have to move into Youlin Retreat with you? Princess Consort Yi runs that house—she's bound to despise me. Won't my life there be pure misery?"

“那是以前，现在不见得，人总是会变。”
"Princess Consort Yi is a kind soul."

“难道你不想嫁给我？”
"Maybe she used to be. Not anymore—people change."

“我当然想！只是现在……，要不我们再等等？等仪王妃跟小姐的关系变好。”
"Don't you want to marry me?"

“那得等到什么时候？如果好不了呢？”
"Of course I do! Just... not right now. Can't we wait a little longer? Wait until things thaw between Princess Consort Yi and Miss."

“我也不知道，起码要变得不像现在这样。”
"How long would *that* take? What if they never make up?"

“她们现在不过是互相不往来，其实也没什么，是你想得太多。”
"I don't know—at least until things aren't as bad as they are right now."

小姐叮嘱过，不能将今日诗会之事告诉白风，苦荞欲言又止。白风哪里清楚，仪王妃连逼死小姐的心都有，她们的关系已经十分糟糕。
"They're only avoiding each other—hardly the end of the world. You're overthinking this."

“你怎么了？”
Bound by her promise not to breathe a word about today's salon, Kuqiao swallowed back the truth. How could Bai Feng know Princess Consort Yi literally wanted her mistress dead?

“白风，我们再等等，小姐正遇到难处，我不能撒手，等她过了这个坎儿，我再嫁给你。”
"What's wrong?" he asked, noticing her silence.

白风不解：“幽栖居士的拳头多厉害，什么坎儿过不去！多你一个不多，少你一个不少。再说她能有什么坎儿？”
"Please wait a little longer, Bai Feng. Miss is facing a rough patch right now—I can't walk out on her. Once she gets over this hurdle, I'll marry you."

“你不懂，有些事不是用拳头就能解决。总之，现在不是我嫁给你的最佳时机，再给我些时日，好不好？”
Bai Feng frowned in confusion. "With Youqi Jushi's fists, what hurdle could stop her? One maid more or less won't matter—and what trouble could she even be in?"

白风看着苦荞诚恳的样子，很是心疼：“好吧，我答应你，但不要让我等得太久。”
"You don't understand. Some problems can't be punched away. All I know is now isn't the right time for me to marry you. Give me a little more time, please?"

苦荞点点头，想起仪王妃让小姐选择死亡的方式，她脸上不由挂满愁容。
Seeing the honest plea in her eyes softened his heart. "All right, I promise. Just don't make me wait too long."

话说申简辰与众人来到方管事家中，前前后后找那位叫孙二的下人，却已不见踪影，到他的住处一瞧，个人之物已收拾干净。
Kuqiao nodded, though remembering Princess Consort Yi telling Miss to pick her own way to die left a heavy cloud over her face.

方管事说：“今早我离开时，他还在，该不会临时回山里了？”
Meanwhile, Shen Jianchen and his men arrived at Steward Fang's home and searched high and low for Sun Er—not a hair of him remained, and his bedroom had been stripped clean of every personal belonging.

申简辰说：“方管事到现在还替孙二说话，难道就不怀疑他是杀死你娘子的凶手？”
"He was still here when I left for work this morning," Steward Fang stammered. "Could he have gone back to the hills on a sudden whim?"

方管事难以置信：“怎么可能？这孩子一直很老实，只知道干活，我和娘子对他都不错，他没有理由做出这等凶狠之事。”
"You're still making excuses for him, Steward Fang?" Shen Jianchen asked. "Doesn't it cross your mind that he might be the one who killed your wife?"

申简辰没有说话，仔细观察周围。
"How could he?" Steward Fang shook his head in disbelief. "That boy's always been honest and hard-working. My wife and I treated him well—he had no reason on earth to commit a crime like that!"

温信已吩咐差役到处搜寻凶器。孙二住的屋子被翻了个遍，并未找到凶器。
Shen Jianchen said nothing, scanning the premises with sharp eyes while Wen Xin had constables tear apart Sun Er's room hunting for the murder weapon. Nothing turned up inside.

申简辰走出屋子，瞧见院子里堆着码得整整齐齐的柴火：“温都头，派人翻翻那堆柴火。”
Stepping out into the yard, Shen Jianchen spotted a neat stack of firewood piled against the wall. "Chief Constable Wen—have your men pull that woodpile apart."

温信一摆手，差役上前立刻搜寻起来，将那堆柴火搞得乱七八糟，终于看到一把匕首，一名差役捡起，送到温信面前。
At Wen Xin's gesture, constables pitched into the logs, scattering them across the dirt until a gleam of steel flashed free. One constable scooped up a dagger and brought it over to Wen Xin.

他接过后，仔细瞧一遍：“王爷，这把匕首的大小与妇人的伤口很吻合，不过还需仵作进一步验证。”
Studying the blade, Wen Xin nodded. "Your Highness, the blade's width matches the woman's fatal wound—though the coroner will still need to confirm it."

方管事已受不了，瘫坐在地上：“真是知人知面不知心啊！呜呜……”
The last of Steward Fang's strength gave out; he slumped onto the ground weeping. "You know a man's face, never his heart! *Sob*..."

申简辰说：“现在还不是哭的时候，你可知孙二会去哪里？”
"Save your tears for later," Shen Jianchen said. "Where would Sun Er run to?"

方管事抹抹眼泪：“回大人，他无处可去，肯定是回山里。”
Wiping his eyes, Fang Jiyuan choked out, "Nowhere else to go, Your Honor—he must've fled back to the hills!"

问清山里的具体位置后，申简辰带着众人离开方管事家中，派温信带上几名差役迅速赶往山里捉拿孙二。
Pinning down the exact village in the hills, Shen Jianchen led his group out of Fang's house and dispatched Wen Xin with a squad of constables to ride straight into the mountains and capture Sun Er.
