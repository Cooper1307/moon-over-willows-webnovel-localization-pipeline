# 第71章 好友相邀论国事

When Qianli Xun and Qiao Wenxian reached the pavilion, the female singer had just finished her song.

Dongfang Chenze picked up a cup of wine and held it out to the singer: "Miss Xiangrong sang wonderfully. This cup is my reward to you."

"Thank you for your praise, Young Master Dongfang." Xiangrong took it, drank it in one gulp, and showed the empty cup to everyone.

Dongfang Chenze praised: "Miss Xiangrong holds her liquor well. How about another cup?"

Qiao Wenxian's voice came over: "Don't put Miss Xiangrong in a difficult position. If she gets drunk, how will she sing for everyone?"

Everyone looked outside the pavilion and saw Qiao Wenxian holding the hand of the Youqi Jushi. They immediately understood everything.

Dongfang Chenze stepped forward and said: "No wonder you declined my invitation yesterday, Brother Qiao. It turns out you had a date with a beauty."

In front of the group of young men, Qianli Xun felt embarrassed to continue being held by Wenyuan. She withdrew her hand: "Wenyuan and I were drawn here by the sound of singing. We didn't expect to run into you all."

Cao Ban said: "A chance meeting is better than a planned invitation. Stay and listen to songs with us, chat about life, and discuss national affairs."

Qiao Wenxian looked at Shuzhen for her permission.

Qianli Xun understood Wenyuan's thoughts. If she didn't agree now, wouldn't she be embarrassing him in front of everyone?

"In that case, Wenyuan and I will stay and chat with you all."

An Mingxuan said: "With a refined and accomplished beauty like the Youqi Jushi by his side, Brother Qiao is truly the envy of us all."

Qiao Wenxian just smiled happily and didn't respond.

When the others heard that the woman before them was the Youqi Jushi, they came forward one by one to greet her: "Youqi Jushi, we've long heard of your fame. It's truly an honor to meet you today!"

Xiangrong also stepped forward: "The lyrics I just sang were written by the Youqi Jushi. It must have been these words that drew you here. Xiangrong pays her respects."

Zhu Shuzhen was beloved by her contemporaries, and Qianli Xun felt deeply honored. She helped Xiangrong up: "You and I look about the same age. From now on, we're friends. How can friends bow to friends?"

Xiangrong said excitedly: "The Youqi Jushi is willing to treat me as a friend?"

Qianli Xun nodded: "Of course."

Xiangrong's eyes turned slightly red, as if she were about to cry.

Qianli Xun was puzzled: "Why do you look sad when I'm treating you as a friend?"

Xiangrong explained: "Not sad—happy."

Cao Ban said: "Youqi Jushi may not know this, but Xiangrong is a girl from the Huayun Tower. No refined young lady has ever been willing to be her friend."

"What kind of place is the Huayun Tower?" Qianli Xun worried that the woman before her might be a prostitute like Chun Jinqiao.

Xiangrong quickly explained: "Please rest assured, Youqi Jushi. The girls at the Huayun Tower perform arts but not their bodies. We earn our rice by our skills."

Qianli Xun was finally relieved: "No profession is noble or base—we all earn our living by our skills."

Qiao Wenxian was left on the sidelines. He deliberately put on a sad expression: "You're all gathered around the Youqi Jushi. It seems I'm superfluous. I might as well leave."

"Stop pretending, Brother Qiao. With the Youqi Jushi here, you'd actually leave? Sit down properly!" Dongfang Chenze grabbed Qiao Wenxian and pushed him down at the stone table.

Xiangrong said: "I'm so happy to have met the Youqi Jushi today. I'll sing the song again. Don't worry, Young Master Dongfang—I won't charge a single coin for any of the songs I sing today."

Dongfang Chenze was naturally pleased: "That's wonderful. We're all riding on the Youqi Jushi's coattails."

For the rest of the time, everyone listened to songs, drank wine, enjoyed the scenery in the pavilion, and chatted freely about life...

Qianli Xun looked at this group of young men and thought: "How wonderful it is to be young and carefree. They enjoy life so happily every day, unaware of what this era will soon face."

Thinking of the era's upheaval that would soon come, Qianli Xun felt even more grateful that Wenyuan had appeared in her life after she transmigrated. No matter what happened in the future, as long as she could be with Wenyuan, nothing else mattered.

As time passed, the people at the gathering dispersed one by one. Because the Huayun Tower had curfew rules, Miss Xiangrong also left with her maid. Only Qianli Xun and the four young men remained in the pavilion.

The four young men gathered together to discuss national affairs. Qianli Xun wasn't interested.

She looked around for Kuqiao and found her sitting on a rock by the lake, chatting with Nanshuang and Huayu. They had their own world, and Qianli Xun didn't want to disturb them.

She picked up the sweet rice wine on the stone table and poured herself a cup, sipping it while listening to the four young men chatting nearby.

An Mingxuan said: "Do you know that after the Great Song allied with the Jin Dynasty, we attacked Yanjing of the Liao Dynasty twice, and both times we failed? I've heard that the Jin Dynasty plans to send troops to attack the Liao Dynasty again."

Cao Ban said: "Our Great Song, such a great and mighty nation, can't even defeat the Liao Dynasty? Are the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun going to fall into others' hands?"

Dongfang Chenze said: "The Jin Dynasty may seem allied with the Great Song, but only because both the Liao and the Song are its potential rivals. It must ally with one to attack the other. Once it grows strong enough, it will absolutely turn on us. I suspect that if the Jin defeats the Liao, it will never hand over the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun to us willingly."

Qiao Wenxian said: "When the alliance was formed, it was agreed that the Great Song would have to take the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun by itself. But our army has been suffering defeat after defeat. It's probably hopeless to recover the Sixteen Prefectures. Alas, the Great Song appears prosperous on the surface, but in reality, we face constant internal troubles and external threats that are hard to defend against."

Cao Ban said: "Speaking of internal troubles, the people keep rising up in rebellion. After the Fang La Rebellion was suppressed last year, Song Jiang and his men were executed this year. I've heard that someone named Jia Jin has also gathered a group of people and is causing chaos in Xiangzhou."

Qiao Wenxian said: "Your news is outdated. I've heard that there's a hero in Xiangzhou named Yue Fei who volunteered to lead the troops and has already suppressed this force. So the Great Song's greatest enemy is not our own people, but the Jin."

Listening to their discussion, Qianli Xun found it amusing. What was there to discuss? The Jin army's southern invasion of the Great Song was written in history books. Having transmigrated into Zhu Shuzhen, she would inevitably witness the fall of the Northern Song firsthand. Thinking of this, she felt sad—not for the era's changes, but for how unpredictable life is, how it can change in an instant.

Feeling sad, she drank a few more cups. Looking at the lake under the setting sun, she realized they had been out for a long time.

Wenyuan and the three young men were still chatting enthusiastically. Qianli Xun didn't want to disturb them, so she continued drinking and waiting. She had never liked silently waiting for someone as much as she did today...

Early that morning, Shen Jianchen had sent Baifeng to keep watch at West Third Alley. With nothing else to do, he visited the government office and sat in the room where official business was conducted. Aside from a few officials who came to curry favor and make small talk, Lin Jue also came by and asked him to give a speech to the Lin'an soldiers when he had time, saying that the Yi Wang's personal appearance would surely boost morale.

Shen Jianchen nodded but didn't give a clear answer.

After Lin Jue left, no one else disturbed him. Shen Jianchen felt quite bored. He left the government office, wandered the streets for a while, and then took a carriage back to the Youlin Villa.

On the way, he couldn't help looking out the carriage window at the Divine Willow. The Divine Willow under the setting sun had a unique charm that piqued his interest, and he wanted to go sit under it again.

When the carriage arrived at the gate of the Youlin Villa, he didn't go inside. Instead, he walked alone through the Youlin forest toward the Divine Willow by the pond.
