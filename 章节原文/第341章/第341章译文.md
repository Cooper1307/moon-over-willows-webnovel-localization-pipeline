## Chapter 341: Coaxing Her with Drugged Osmanthus Wine

Back inside Yanjing's Under-Manor, Wanyan Chi lied that Prince Yi had never shown up with the ransom, trapped furious Qianli Xun under a ceiling net triggered by floor switches, and bragged of shaman drugs arriving from Huining—leaving her fuming and puzzled why the traps obeyed only his footsteps.

Pumping her chatterbox Jurchen maid for intel over five quiet days, Qianli Xun learned Wanyan Chi's surprisingly tragic backstory: son of the Jin Emperor and a Han mother from Lin'an (Shao Zhengye's sister!), mocked as a half-breed, forced into loveless political marriages after his father murdered his first love—who looked hauntingly like Qianli Xun!

On day six, Wanyan Chi arrived with a pot of osmanthus wine, playing his deepest card with genuine red-eyed grief—saying today was the anniversary he celebrated for the mother he'd never met, begging her to share just two cups before he released her from the Under-Manor.

Touched by his motherless ache and watching him drink first without flinching, Qianli Xun dropped her guard and sipped—only for her vision to spin dark mid-sentence as she pitched forward unconscious into his grinning arms.
