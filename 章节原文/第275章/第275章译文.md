## Chapter 275: Enter Chun Jinlan

"Miss—watch out!" Kuqiao shrieked.

Qianli Xun pivoted a hair sideways, slipping the blade cleanly—holding back from disarming her just to gauge her moves. The woman thrust again straight for her heart. *Amateur!* Leaning past the lunge, Qianli Xun cracked a fist onto her wrist, sending the dagger clattering, and kicked her flat on her back before she could dive for it.

Scooping up the gleaming blade—"Nice steel, wasn't cheap"—Qianli Xun tossed it to Kuqiao, who caught it proudly: "Anyone stupid enough to cross my Miss gets what's coming!"

"Who the hell are you?" Qianli Xun demanded as Chun Jinmo helped the groaning attacker up. "Never laid eyes on you—what's your beef with me?"

"You didn't cross me—*Chen Haoyu* did!" the girl hissed. "You're his wife, the reason he threw away every girl he ruined! Kill you and he'll go back to his old ways—then I get my shot!"

Too baffled by such twisted logic to argue, Qianli Xun was about to shut her down when Old Madam Xia stepped out the front gate flanked by Yao Qingyan, Madam Bao, and Madam Cui—having paused the storyteller the instant a servant whispered of an armed scene outside.

"Causing a brawl at Chen Manor's gate and attacking my granddaughter-in-law—disgraceful!" Old Madam Xia declared, unwilling to air dirty laundry on the street. "Take it inside!"

Moving into the front-most garden courtyard—no brothel women entered the formal main hall—the Chen ladies took seats while the two courtesans stood on the flagstones.

Cutting off their curtsies, Old Madam Xia demanded names and motives.

"Jiang Liluo, wife of Master Jiang of the Qiantang Gang," Chun Jinmo announced smoothly, spinning her polished sob story—how Haoyu dumped her, how she married Master Jiang, and how the night before her wedding this girl ambushed her with a dagger, revealing that Haoyu had snatched her from a country village, violated her, and sold her to a brutal Yangzhou brothel until she escaped back to Lin'an and was sheltered at Chunxiang Brothel under the name Chun Jinlan.

"Unless Chen Haoyu takes me into his household," Chun Jinlan spat with blazing eyes, "this grudge ends in blood!"

Madam Bao beamed silently—her son's second bomb was a masterpiece! Madam Cui thanked heaven her studious Haofei never pulled stunts like this, while Yao Qingyan burned with shame before her sisters-in-law.

Qianli Xun kept her head cool despite the knot in her chest. "One-sided stories prove nothing—we need Chen Haoyu here to answer."

Old Madam Xia agreed at once, dispatching a servant to fetch Haoyu from the silk emporium and inviting the two women to stroll the gardens during the half-hour wait.

Heading back toward Old Madam Xia's courtyard to wait, the family crossed paths with Ye Anya and her maid Xiaocui stepping out. Madam Bao snapped at her for leaving without permission; Old Madam Xia defended Anya's freedom, yet Anya meekly stayed to keep them company—catching Qianli Xun's sharp eye as she signaled Xiaocui to slip out the gate alone to cancel her tryst with Cao Ban. Watching Anya's reckless courage made Qianli Xun wonder if Master Jingguang's *Right Thought and Right Mindfulness* had trapped her into endless compromise—and whether Anya's gamble would bring ruin or freedom.

Meanwhile, touring the vast Chen estate in awe—even Xinglong Pavilion's sprawling office complex and clerks' quarters—Chun Jinmo leaned in to whisper secrets to Chun Jinlan: *Eldest Young Master Chen Haodong* had tracked Jinlan down, fed her silver, and pointed her straight at Jinmo! Better still, Jinmo confided smugly, Youqi Jushi's days were numbered anyway—after Chen Haodong visited Master Jiang yesterday, Jinmo had talked her hesitant husband into taking the hit! Once Youqi Jushi dropped dead, Jinlan might even rise from concubine to primary wife!

Just as Jinlan daydreamed of ruling this palace, a servant arrived to summon them to the east pavilion: Second Young Master had returned.
