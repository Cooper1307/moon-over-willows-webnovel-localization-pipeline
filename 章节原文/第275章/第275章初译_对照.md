第275章 又杀出个春金兰
## Chapter 275: Enter Chun Jinlan

明晃晃的匕首吓得苦荞大喊道：“小姐！小心！”
匕首即将刺来的瞬间，千里寻微微侧身，巧妙躲开致命的一击。本来可顺利夺过匕首，因想试试对方身手，她仍旧采取躲避的姿态。
女子见没刺中，立刻调整招式，再次发起攻击，这次她瞄准心脏……
千里寻心想：“就这两下子！还敢攻击我！”
她从容不迫，身体向一边倾斜，伸出一拳，击中对方的手腕，女子吃痛，匕首掉落在地上。
女子依然不放弃，准备去捡匕首，再次袭击对方。
千里寻不再给她机会？伸出一脚，将女子踢倒在地，一时爬不起来。
千里寻上前捡起匕首：“匕首不错，应该不便宜吧？”
女子忍着痛并未回答，眼神中依然充满憎恨。
春金沫走过去将女子扶起来，劝道：“我给你说过她很厉害，你偏不听，一心要找她斗一斗，怎么样？自己受伤了吧？”
The glinting blade made Kuqiao shriek. “Miss! Watch out!”

女子说：“我就是死，也要出这口恶气。”
The split second before the dagger reached her, Qianli Xun shifted sideways, slipping clear of the lethal thrust. She could have disarmed her on the spot, yet wanting to test the woman’s skill, she held back and dodged.

千里寻将匕首递给苦荞：“别人送上门来，不要白不要。”
Missing her mark, the woman switched angles and thrust again, aiming straight for the heart.

苦荞接过：“小姐放心，我一定收好，要让那些没长眼的人都知道，敢欺负我家小姐，必然有去无回！”
*That’s all you’ve got, and you dare come at me?*

千里寻对苦荞说话的架势感到满意，不过她甚是疑惑，为何眼前的女子会刺杀她？
Unruffled, Qianli Xun leaned clear and snapped a punch into the woman’s wrist. With a gasp of pain, the woman dropped the dagger onto the flagstones. Refusing to quit, she dove to snatch it back.

“喂！你谁啊？老娘第一次见你，应该没得罪过你吧？”
Qianli Xun gave her no second chance. A crisp kick sent the woman sprawling flat on the ground, unable to get up.

女子用愤恨的语气说：“你是没得罪我！可陈浩宇得罪过我！”
Qianli Xun bent and picked up the blade. “Nice dagger. Couldn’t have been cheap.”

千里寻仍旧在心中骂道：“陈浩宇！等你回来，非让你跪搓衣板不可！”
Biting back pain, the woman glared up in silent fury.

生气归生气，眼前的麻烦还需处理：“既然是他得罪你，找他算账便是，为何找我麻烦？”
Chun Jinmo hurried over to help her up. “I told you she’s tough, and you wouldn’t listen! Insisted on taking her on—and look, you got yourself hurt!”

女子说：“怪只怪你是他娘子，他又因为你撇下所有被他糟蹋过的姑娘。因此，你是罪魁祸首，只有杀了你，他才会像以前一样，我才有机会与他在一起！”
“Even if it kills me,” the woman spat, “I’m getting my revenge!”

千里寻认为能说出这种话的女人定是傻帽一个，她懒得与一个傻子计较，准备说些安慰对方的话，却见众人扶着夏老太太从大门走出来。
Qianli Xun tossed the dagger to Kuqiao. “Delivered right to our door—waste not, want not.”

陈府门口发生大事，又没人叮嘱下人，早已有人报告给老太太。大家正陪她听说书，下人前来将门口发生的事一五一十说给丫鬟，丫鬟又悄悄在老太太耳边说了。
Kuqiao caught it. “Count on me, Miss, I’ll put it away safe. Let every blind fool know that crossing my miss ends one way—you don’t walk back out!”

夏老太太让说话艺人停下，由丫鬟伺候说话艺人品会儿茶，吃些果子，她要出去看个究竟，不能什么事只让淑真一人冲在前面。
Pleased by Kuqiao’s grit, Qianli Xun frowned in puzzlement. “Hey! Who even are you? First time I’ve laid eyes on you—I never wronged you, did I?”

夏老太太是见过世面之人，一眼瞧出此事不小，在门口处理，恐被人瞧去笑话陈家。
“You didn’t!” the woman hissed. “Chen Haoyu did!”

“你们在陈府门口闹事，欺负我孙媳，成何体统？有什么话进府说！”
*Chen Haoyu, wait till you get home—I’m making you kneel on a washboard!*

有长辈出面撑腰，千里寻甚觉惬意，上前行礼：“此事惊动奶奶，是孙媳的不是。”
Fuming though she was, the mess in front of her came first. “If he wronged you, take it up with him! Why come after me?”

姚轻琰扶住淑真，代替老太太说：“怎能怪你？都是浩宇惹下的事。”
“Blame yourself for being his wife! Because of you, he tossed aside every girl he ruined! You’re the root of it all—kill you, and he’ll go back to his old ways so I can have him!”

婆婆的话令她舒心，一家人不必再客气，她主动搀扶老太太走进府内。
What a brainless idiot, Qianli Xun thought. Not worth arguing with a fool—she was about to toss out a soothing word when a group of servants came through the main gate supporting Old Madam Xia.

姚轻琰则对门外的两名女子说：“你们也跟着进来吧。”
With nobody gagging the gatekeepers this time, word of the commotion had flown straight to the Old Madam while the family sat listening to a storyteller. Having the maid serve the storyteller tea and fruit while he paused, Old Madam Xia had marched out to see for herself—she wasn’t about to leave Shuzhen facing every storm alone.

春金沫在女子耳边说：“陈家长辈都出面，你很有希望，我陪你进去。”
Seasoned as she was, Old Madam Xia saw at one glance that handling a scandal like this out on the street would make the Chens a laughingstock.

女子的脸上终于露出一抹笑容：“希望如姐姐所说。”
“Causing a scene at Chen Manor’s gate and attacking my granddaughter-in-law—what kind of behavior is this? Whatever you have to say, say it inside!”

接待两名青楼女子，又岂能在正堂？陈家的下人早已在离大门最近的院子摆好椅子。陈家人，包括千里寻在内都坐下，瞧着两名女子从外面走进来。
Having elders back her up felt wonderful. Qianli Xun curtsied. “Forgive me for alarming you, Grandmother.”

女子边走边小声说：“姐姐，她们连个坐的地方都不给，显然瞧不起我们。”
Yao Qingyan caught her arm and spoke for the Old Madam. “How is this your fault? It’s all Haoyu’s doing.”

春金沫低声安慰：“先忍忍，毕竟我们出自青楼，好人家瞧不上也正常。”
Warmed by her mother-in-law’s words, Qianli Xun stepped in to help support Old Madam Xia through the gate. Yao Qingyan called over her shoulder to the two women, “Come along inside, both of you.”

两人正要向各位长辈行礼，被老太太阻止：“罢了，你们的礼数我可承受不起，先报上姓名，再说明来意。”
Chun Jinmo whispered in her companion’s ear, “The Chen elders are stepping in—you’ve got a real shot now. I’ll go in with you.”

春金沫主动开口说：“我本姓江，名唤离洛，已跟了钱塘帮的蒋爷，你们可以唤我江夫人……”
A faint smile touched the woman’s lips at last. “I hope you’re right, Sister.”

包氏幸灾乐祸地问：“这倒奇了？你已是江夫人，为何还来闹事？”
Receiving two brothel women in the main hall was out of the question; servants had already arranged chairs in the courtyard closest to the gate. The Chens, Qianli Xun included, sat facing the entrance as the pair walked in.

春金沫继续说：“夫人有所不知，我本出自春香楼，一直陪伴的人都是陈公子，本希望他能帮我赎身，在他身边做个妾室好伺候他，可他自从被少夫人从春香楼带走后，已把我抛之脑后。我整日以泪洗面，后来也想通，蒋爷对我好，愿意为我赎身，还乐意娶我，我便嫁了他。”
“They didn’t even set out seats for us, Sister,” the girl muttered. “Clearly they look down on us.”

崔氏问道：“你也算有好去处。不过你说了半天我们还是听不出你为何还来寻事？难道不怕惹蒋爷生气？”
“Swallow it for now,” Chun Jinmo murmured back. “We come from a brothel—respectable families are bound to look down their noses.”

春金沫说：“诸位别急，听我详细道来。在我出嫁的头一日，这位妹妹拿着一把匕首要杀我，问明原因后才知她和我一样是个可怜人。她在乡下时，陈公子见她美貌，强行霸占，可并没有娶她的意思，还把她卖到扬州的青楼。她整日以泪洗面，后来被逼无奈开始接客，扬州青楼里的妈妈手段残忍，妹妹实在受不了，才逃回临安，她听说陈公子喜欢到春香楼找我，便对我怀恨在心，才拿着匕首前来找我算账……”
Before they could bow, Old Madam Xia cut them off. “Save it—I can’t stomach your courtesies. Give your names and state your business.”

女子将春金沫打断：“姐姐，我来说。我知道姐姐也被陈公子甩掉，跟我一样是个可怜人，便不与她计较。她说动春香楼的妈妈，把我留下，给我取名春金兰。我对陈浩宇怀恨在心，一心想杀他，除非……除非他乐意将我放在他身边，否则此恨难消。听姐姐说他娶了娘子，一心扑在他娘子的身上，以前惹下的事，犯下的错当没发生过一般，我便更加恨他！”
Chun Jinmo spoke first. “My family name is Jiang, given name Liluo. I am married to Master Jiang of the Qiantang Gang, so you may call me Madam Jiang.”

众人已将事情的前因后果听明白。
Madam Bao spoke up with ill-concealed glee. “How odd! If you’re already Madam Jiang, what are you doing here making a scene?”

包氏心中赞叹儿子真有本事，找出的第二个麻烦定会让浩宇吃不了兜着走。他的娘子再厉害，估计也想不出解决的办法，到最后只能让浩宇纳个青楼女子为妾，他更别想成为陈家产业的继承人。
“Madam may not know,” Chun Jinmo went on, “I came from Chunxiang Brothel, where Young Master Chen was my sole patron. I hoped he would buy my freedom and take me as a concubine, yet after Young Madam hauled him out of the brothel, he forgot I existed. I cried my eyes out before coming to my senses. Master Jiang treated me right, bought my freedom, and married me as his primary wife, so I went with him.”

崔氏庆幸她有个乖儿子，只喜欢读书，绝不会在外惹出这等麻烦事。
Madam Cui chimed in. “Sounds like you landed well enough. Still doesn’t explain why you’re here causing trouble. Aren’t you afraid of angering Master Jiang?”

姚轻琰虽知儿子荒唐，却没想到在外面荒唐到这等地步，不仅霸占人家姑娘，还把人卖到青楼，让她这当娘的在妯娌面前毫无颜面，羞愧得无法言语。
“Don’t rush, Madams—hear me out. The day before my wedding, this younger sister came at me with a dagger. When I asked why, I learned she’s just as pitiable as me. Back in the countryside, Young Master Chen saw her beauty, forced himself on her, and instead of marrying her, sold her to a Yangzhou brothel! She wept day and night until they forced her to take customers. The Yangzhou madam was brutal, so she fled back to Lin’an. Hearing Young Master Chen spent all his time with me at Chunxiang Brothel, she blamed me and came after me with a knife...”

千里寻很难过，嫁给一位欠揍的丈夫，跪搓衣板都是轻饶。她想起陈浩宇在聚香楼给她说的那些情话，她安慰自己也许他没有那么坏。
The girl cut her off. “Let me tell it, Sister! Once I found out Young Master Chen had dumped Sister too, I stopped blaming her. She talked the Chunxiang Brothel madam into taking me in and gave me the name Chun Jinlan. I hate Chen Haoyu to the bone and want him dead—unless... unless he takes me by his side! Hearing from Sister that he married a wife, dotes on her alone, and acts as if all his past sins never happened only made me hate him ten times more!”

“只听你一面之词，我与长辈们无法相信，还得听听陈浩宇如何说。”
Now everyone had the whole story.

夏老太太为孙儿所做之事头疼的同时，也正有此意：“淑真说的对。来人！”
Madam Bao marveled inside at her son’s resourcefulness—this second bomb would wreck Haoyu for sure! No matter how sharp his wife was, she’d never talk her way out of this; Haoyu would end up saddled with a brothel concubine and kiss the inheritance goodbye.

过来一名下人：“老太太有何吩咐？”
Madam Cui thanked her lucky stars for her quiet, bookish son who never dragged home messes like this.

“你去趟绸缎庄，将二公子接回来。”
Yao Qingyan knew her son had been wild, yet abducting a girl and selling her into a brothel? Mortification robbed her of speech in front of her sisters-in-law.

“是！”
Heart heavy, Qianli Xun thought a washboard was far too kind for a husband like this. Remembering his tender words at Juxiang Tower, she tried telling herself maybe he wasn’t that rotten.

下人走后，老太太说：“浩宇回来还需半个时辰，二位可在园子里逛逛，待他回来，我会差人去唤二位。”
“We can’t believe one side of the story alone,” she said. “We need to hear what Chen Haoyu has to say.”

有机会逛陈府，两人甚是高兴，一起行礼：“谢过老太太。”
Nursing a splitting headache over her grandson, Old Madam Xia agreed at once. “Shuzhen is right. Someone!”

千里寻扶着老太太，与婆婆和两位婶婶打算回屋歇着，耐心等待陈浩宇回来再兴师问罪。
A servant stepped up. “Your orders, Old Madam?”

她们走着走着，瞧见叶安雅带着丫鬟小翠迎面走来。
“Go to the silk shop and fetch Second Young Master back.”

包氏一脸不满：“你去哪里？”
“Yes, Old Madam!”

叶安雅上前小心行礼：“在屋里待得心烦，出去走走。”
Once the servant left, Old Madam Xia told the pair, “It’ll take half a shichen for Haoyu to get back. Feel free to stroll around the garden; I’ll send someone for you once he arrives.”

包氏上前指着叶安雅批评道：“一点儿礼数都不懂，出去是不是该向我这当婆婆的告个假？要不是我碰见你，都不知道你人在哪里？”
Thrilled to tour Chen Manor, the two curtsied. “Thank you, Old Madam.”

叶安雅说：“儿媳想出去走走，望婆婆应允。”
Helping Old Madam Xia up, Qianli Xun, her mother-in-law, and the two aunts headed back toward the inner quarters to wait for Chen Haoyu’s interrogation.

包氏没好气地说：“我不同意，今儿你就好好待在家里，陪着长辈们。”
Along the path they ran into Ye Anya walking out with her maid Xiaocui.

夏老太太说：“安雅想出去就让她出去，我们陈家的女人不受礼数约束。”
Madam Bao’s face darkened. “Where do you think you’re going?”

老太太的话令叶安雅羞愧：“奶奶，我不出去了，在家陪您。”
Ye Anya curtsied with caution. “Feeling stifled in my room, so I stepped out for air.”

千里寻对叶安雅的事心知肚明，上前拉起她的手：“大嫂，同我一起去奶奶的院子，陪她老人家说说话。”
Madam Bao jabbed a finger at her. “No manners at all! Shouldn’t you ask your mother-in-law’s leave before stepping out? If I hadn’t run into you, I wouldn’t even know where you’d wandered off to!”

叶安雅点点头：“好。”
“Your daughter-in-law would like to go out for a walk. Please grant permission, Mother.”

众人一起向老太太的院子走去。
“Denied!” Madam Bao snapped. “Stay home today and keep the elders company.”

千里寻注意到叶安雅向小翠使眼色，小翠点点头，故意放慢脚步，直到众人都走在她前面，才转身向大门外走去。
Old Madam Xia stepped in. “Let Anya go if she wants. Women in the Chen family aren’t bound by petty rules.”

千里寻看破不说破，心中替叶安雅担忧，她如此做，会不会带来不好的结果？偶尔也佩服大嫂不管不顾的勇气，哪像她瞻前顾后，一步一步把自己逼到替夫君擦屁股的地步。
Shamed by the Old Madam’s kindness, Ye Anya bowed. “I won’t go out, Grandmother. I’ll stay and keep you company.”

如若当初跟着文渊私奔，或者在仪王抢亲时跟他走，她的生活肯定是另一种景象。都怪净光法师说的“正思正念”四个字害了她，每次不知该如何选择人生之路时，想起这四个字，都会走上成全他人委屈自己的道路。
Knowing full well what Ye Anya was up to, Qianli Xun stepped over and took her hand. “Come with me to Grandmother’s courtyard and chat with her, Eldest Sister-in-law.”

她在心里问自己：“我能否像大嫂一样只顾追求个人的快乐？”
“All right,” Ye Anya murmured.

她给出的答案依然是否定，她已在委屈自己的道路上走得太远，无法回头。也才真正体会到每个人的命运在选择人生道路的那一刻已注定。
As the group walked on, Qianli Xun caught Ye Anya flashing a signal to Xiaocui. Xiaocui nodded, lagged behind until everyone passed, then slipped back toward the front gate.

净光法师提到的“福祸无门，惟人自召”八个字第一次清晰地回荡在耳边，千里寻瞧向身边的叶安雅，不知大嫂的行为招来的是祸还是福？也不知她委屈自己的行为又会招来什么？
Qianli Xun said nothing, yet worry tugged at her. Where would Ye Anya’s gamble lead? Part of her even envied her sister-in-law’s reckless nerve, while she herself fretted over every step until she wound up cleaning up a husband’s messes.

有人上门来闹事，众人已无心听说书，让下人将说话艺人送出。大家陪老太太坐着，沉默一会儿又闲聊一会儿，一边打发时间，一边各自在心里思忖解决事情的方法。
Had she eloped with Wenyuan, or gone with Prince Yi when he raided the wedding, life would look unrecognizable now. Blame Master Jingguang’s “Right Thought and Right Mindfulness”—every time she hit a crossroads, those words steered her into sacrificing herself to spare everyone else.

叶安雅则想着与曹公子的约会算是泡汤；她的婆婆包氏则希望事情越闹越大，大到浩宇只能纳了青楼女子为妾的地步。
*Could I chase my own happiness without a backward glance like Eldest Sister-in-law?*

话说春金沫与春金兰两人在陈府里欣赏景色，越欣赏春金沫越惆怅。
The answer was still no. She had walked too far down the road of self-restraint to turn back now. Every person’s fate really was sealed the instant they chose their road. Master Jingguang’s other warning echoed clear in her ears: *Fortune and calamity have no gate; people summon them themselves.* Glancing at Ye Anya, she wondered what fate waited at the end of her sister-in-law’s road—and her own.

“我与他已不可能，只能把希望寄托在妹妹身上。”
With trouble at the gate, nobody had the heart for storytelling, so they sent the storyteller home. Sitting with Old Madam Xia, the women drifted between silence and small talk, each turning the crisis over in her head. Ye Anya lamented her ruined rendezvous with Young Master Cao, while Madam Bao hoped the scandal blew up big enough to force Haoyu into taking a brothel girl as concubine.

春金兰发着狠心：“他若不要我，我便杀了他，若杀不了，我就死在他面前。”
Meanwhile, Chun Jinmo and Chun Jinlan strolled the manor grounds, and the more Chun Jinmo took in, the heavier her gloom grew.

“妹妹是有血性的人，着实令人佩服。只是我有一事不明，你怎么会与陈家大公子认识？”
“He’s out of my reach now,” she sighed. “All my hopes ride on you, Sister.”

“我本不认识他，他不知从哪里知道我这号人，又给我银子，还给我出了这主意，也是他告诉我陈公子与姐姐的事，我才找上你。”
Hardness glinted in Chun Jinlan’s eyes. “If he won’t take me, I’ll kill him—and if I can’t kill him, I’ll die right in front of him.”

“原来如此。”春金沫神秘地在春金兰耳边小声嘀咕了一会儿。
“What fire—I admire that,” Chun Jinmo said. “One thing puzzles me, though. How did you get hooked up with Chen Manor’s Eldest Young Master?”

春金兰惊讶道：“竟有这种事？”
“I didn’t know him at all. Somehow he tracked me down, gave me silver, and fed me this whole plan. He’s the one who told me about Young Master Chen and you so I’d come find you.”

“妹妹小声些，一定要保密。她遇上大麻烦，小命不保，若今日陈家同意把你放在陈公子身边，以后你日日在这么美的府中住着，真是羡煞姐姐。她若死了，说不定你还会成为正室，到时可别忘记姐姐。”
“So that’s it.” Chun Jinmo leaned in and whispered something in Chun Jinlan’s ear.

“只要能成，我自然不会忘了姐姐。”
Chun Jinlan gasped. “No way! Really?”

春金沫叹道：“唉，说句心里话，我心里还有陈公子，谁让他是我第一个男人。都怪女词人，要不是她，我也不会被逼无奈嫁给蒋爷。”
“Keep your voice down—swear you won’t breathe a word! She’s in deep trouble, and her life hangs by a thread. If the Chens take you in today, you’ll live every day in a palace like this—enough to turn me green with envy. And once she’s dead, who knows, you might even be promoted to primary wife! Don’t forget me when that day comes.”

“姐姐恨她？”
“If it works out, I’ll never forget you, Sister.”

“那是自然。”
Chun Jinmo let out a wistful sigh. “Truth be told, I still carry a torch for Young Master Chen—he was my first man. All that poetess’s fault! If not for her, I’d never have been forced to marry Master Jiang.”

“既如此，姐姐好好劝说蒋爷接下此事。”
“You hate her too, Sister?”

“昨日陈家大公子到钱塘帮找我夫君说过此事后，他竟然犹豫不决。我才发现钱塘帮看着吓人，其实我夫君也是个孬货，只说女词人不好对付。还好他听我的，三言两语被我说服。等着瞧吧，很快有好戏看。”
“Of course I do.”

春金兰小心向四周瞧瞧：“姐姐小声些，小心被人听去。”
“Then talk Master Jiang into taking the job.”

“放心，我都瞧过，没人。”
“When Eldest Young Master Chen came to the Qiantang Gang yesterday, my husband actually hemmed and hawed! Turns out the Qiantang Gang looks scary on the outside, yet my husband’s a coward who kept saying the poetess is too tough to mess with. Lucky for us he listens to me—took me two minutes to talk him round. Just watch: a good show is coming soon.”

两人继续逛着，陈府真大，大到两人难以想象，春金兰觉得比乡下的村子还要大。
Chun Jinlan glanced around in nerves. “Lower your voice, Sister! What if someone overhears?”

逛到西边时，瞧见兴隆阁，两人慨叹不已，尤其是进进出出忙碌的伙计，更让她们难以想象陈家的产业到底有多大。
“Relax, I checked. Nobody’s around.”

“姐姐，这些都在陈府？”
They wandered on, floored by the manor’s sheer scale—Chun Jinlan thought it bigger than her whole home village. Reaching the west wing, they gaped at Xinglong Pavilion and the steady stream of clerks bustling in and out.

“你不就是在陈府吗？”
“Wait—all this is inside Chen Manor, Sister?”

“陈家怎么把铺子搬进府内来？”
“Where else do you think we are?”

春金沫笑道：“不是铺子，没见写着‘兴隆阁’吗？陈家所有生意都由兴隆阁管，真正的铺子都在外面。我们刚才经过的地方如果我没猜错，应该是兴隆阁的伙计住的地儿。”
“Why did the Chens stick a shop inside their house?”

“啊，伙计们也能住在陈府？”
Chun Jinmo laughed. “That’s not a shop—see the plaque? ‘Xinglong Pavilion.’ All of the Chen family’s business runs out of there; the actual shops are out on the streets. And those buildings we just passed? If I’m right, that’s housing for the clerks.”

“我刚才仔细观察过，那边走动的人都是伙计，应该是陈家为伙计到兴隆阁方便给提供的住处。”
“Even the clerks live inside Chen Manor?”

春金兰叹道：“对伙计都这么好，难怪陈家生意能做大。”
“Everyone walking around over there was a clerk. The Chens put them up right next to Xinglong Pavilion for convenience.”

“记着，今日之后你很有可能会成为这里的主人之一。”
“Treating even their clerks this well—no wonder the Chens got so rich.”

春金兰笑着畅想未来的生活。
“Just remember: after today, you might become one of the mistresses of this place.”

正畅想着，陈府的下人前来：“二位，二公子已到府中，老太太吩咐我带你们去东边的亭子。”
Chun Jinlan smiled, picturing that glittering future—right as a Chen servant approached. “Ladies, Second Young Master has returned. Old Madam sent me to escort you to the eastern pavilion.”
