## Chapter 336: Face-to-Face Against Wanyan Chi

Unwilling to let go, both dropped back to the ground beside him, where the Song general lay motionless without breath.

No time to mourn. Hundreds of Jin troops closed in, and whoever loosed that arrow shot fast, lethal, and true—one more leap skyward would pick either of them off mid-air.

“Looks like no running today,” Qianli Xun said. “Head-on it is.”

“Scared?” Shen Jianchen asked.

“Never knew the word growing up.”

“If you’re not scared, neither am I.”

Standing shoulder to shoulder, they braced as Jin ranks parted—and a familiar voice rang out in disbelief.

“Master?! Is that you?!”

Out stepped Lu Yuanfeng in Jin armor, grinning sheepishly as Qianli Xun recognized the stubborn fighter she had beaten years ago. Right beside him rode “Yan Chi” in full Jin battle mail, holding the bow—the very man she had rescued two years earlier, clearly no ordinary traveler, rather a highborn Jin royal!

Relaxing at the sight of the beauty he had never forgotten, Wanyan Chi handed his bow to an officer and looked down coolly to see how she would handle this trap.

“Lu Yuanfeng!” Qianli Xun snapped. “If you still call me Master, obey me! Leave these Jin dogs and step over here!”

That name triggered Shen Jianchen’s memory at once—Confusing Soul Alley in Lin’an, and Shao Zhengye’s secret about Wanyan Chi, son of the Jin Emperor Wanyan Wuqimai and Shao Yueting.

Torn between loyalty and pride, Lu Yuanfeng begged her to join the young prince instead. Disgusted, Qianli Xun disowned him on the spot, snapping she had never taught a traitor a single move and wanted nothing more to do with him.

Unruffled, Wanyan Chi smiled down. “Meeting again in Yanjing—wouldn’t you call that fate?”

“You know him?” Shen Jianchen asked low.

“Met him once on a Lin’an street—beat him flat too. Had no idea he was Jin, or I’d never have let him go.”

“Nothing else?”

“What else? Scared I’d fall for a Jin?”

“I’m worried about *him*.”

Annoyed watching the beauty whisper with a tall, striking stranger, Wanyan Chi demanded who he was—and grew sharper guessing he might be her husband. “No matter if you married,” Wanyan Chi declared. “Today ends your marriage. Nobody move! I’ll deal with this fellow myself!”

Like a tiger bounding downhill, Wanyan Chi lunged at Shen Jianchen’s face with Shen Fist’s signature blow, *Shifting Winds and Clouds*. Sliding clear like a darting fish, Shen Jianchen countered with *Dragon Soaring Across the Four Seas* straight at his ribs. Wanyan Chi flipped away with *Earth Dragon Turning Over*, stunned that this stranger wielded his master’s secret fist art smoother and sharper than he did himself!

Soft-within-hard clashed against hard-within-soft, fist shadows blurring like wind and thunder while Qianli Xun murmured in surprise that Shen Fist actually held moves even Qianli Fist had to respect.

At the height of the duel, Shen Jianchen sank low as if rooted into stone, coiled every ounce of force, and exploded upward in a devastating arc toward Wanyan Chi’s temple—freezing his knuckles a hair’s breadth from bone.

Spared at death’s door, Wanyan Chi gasped. “How do you know *Shared Span of Heaven and Earth*? I trained for months and never mastered that last move! Who are you?!”

Wanting answers about Shen Fist’s origin, Shen Jianchen smirked coldly. “My art is called Shen Fist. Since I beat you flat, that makes it the grand ancestor of whatever you practice. An elder shouldn’t bully a junior—consider yourself spared today.”

Walking back to Qianli Xun with a victorious grin, he left Wanyan Chi trembling with humiliation in front of his officers. Nobody could call him a junior and walk away!

“All troops, listen up!” Wanyan Chi roared. “Leave the woman to me! Surround that man and wear him down till he drops!”

As soldiers swarmed Shen Jianchen, another ring sealed off Qianli Xun, Lu Yuanfeng standing inside. Stepping into her circle half-joking, half-earnest, Wanyan Chi boasted his uncle was marching straight through Hebei for Bianliang while leaving him to govern Yanjing. The Song stood on the brink of ruin—stay with him, and he would pluck the stars from the sky for her, confessing one glance in Lin’an had haunted him ever since.

Playing along with an innocent blink, she asked if he would give her *anything* she asked—then snapped, “Good! I want your life, and I want every Jin soldier off Song soil! Hand those over!”

Choking on his own boast, Wanyan Chi snarled in fury and ordered the troops to attack while stepping clear himself.

Late autumn dusk bled into darkness. Jin squads rotated fresh men in wave after wave, denying Shen Jianchen and Qianli Xun a single breath. Heavy limbs turned to lead; sweat soaked their robes. Gasping as stars swam before his eyes, Shen Jianchen held upright on sheer grit, while Qianli Xun still spat defiance between blows that even if she died today, she had flattened plenty of Jin dogs first.

Stepping through the circle, Wanyan Chi caught her swaying form as she spat at him, tapped the fainting acupoint at her neck, and smiled as she collapsed limp into his arms. Handing her over to Lu Yuanfeng to carry back to the manor, he turned and kicked Shen Jianchen flat into the dirt, ordering his soldiers to hack the Song man to pieces under a storm of blades.

Too spent to rise, Shen Jianchen watched through blurring eyes as Lu Yuanfeng lifted Qianli Xun into a waiting carriage, shutting his eyes silently as steel closed in, hoping only that once he died, she might keep him in her heart.

Hours earlier, after weeping himself dry over Mengrong and Xilan, Bai Feng had gathered every scrap of strength to carry both women off the freezing stones onto the bed inside the abandoned home—collapsing beside them dizzy from blood loss.

As the western sun faded, his eyes fell on a dead Jin officer slumped against the wall. Crawling over on a prayer, he searched the corpse’s robe and pulled out two medicine bottles—swallowing a pill from one, pouring powder blind across the stinging cuts on his back.

Within half an hour, the pain dulled and his head cleared. Lighting the oil lamp with his fire-striker, he knelt beside the bed, promising Princess Consort Yi and Xilan to wait while he found their prince, and limped down the darkening alleys toward the sounds of battle—reaching the rear street just as raised blades hovered above Shen Jianchen’s chest.

Forgetting his own wounds, Bai Feng leaped square into the ring with *Thunderbolt Chain Kicks*, knocking the nearest killers flying, and threw himself flat over Shen Jianchen. “Your Highness! I’m here to save you!”
