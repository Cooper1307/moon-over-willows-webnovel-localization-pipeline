## Chapter 336: Clashing Head-On with Wanyan Chi

Landing beside the dead Song commander under a forest of drawn Jurchen bows, Qianli Xun froze at a familiar cry: *"Master?!"*

Standing beside bow-wielding **Wanyan Chi** in full Jurchen armor was **Lu Yuanfeng**! Cutting off all master-disciple ties on the spot with the turncoat, Qianli Xun refused Wanyan Chi's arrogant offer to stay in Yanjing—prompting Wanyan Chi to challenge Shen Jianchen one-on-one.

*Fist met fist!* To Wanyan Chi's stunned horror, Shen Jianchen met his secret *Clouds Shift* and *Earth-Dragon Roll* with cleaner, sharper *Shen Fist*—stopping a hair from his temple with the ultimate *Heaven-and-Earth Strike* Wanyan Chi hadn't even mastered!

Humiliated, Wanyan Chi ordered wave after endless wave of Jin troops to grind both fighters down until dusk fell and their limbs turned to lead. Tapping Qianli Xun's fainting acupoint the instant she stumbled, Wanyan Chi handed her to Lu Yuanfeng's carriage—and ordered his troops to skewer helpless, fallen Shen Jianchen where he lay!

Just as blades dove toward his back, bandaged Bai Feng (patched up with medicine looted off a dead Jin officer after laying Mengrong and Xilan together on the cot!) dropped from above in a whirlwind of *Thunder Chain Kicks* and threw his body across his master: *"Your Highness, I'm here!"*
