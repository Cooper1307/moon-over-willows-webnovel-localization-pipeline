## Chapter 317: Demanding to See the Poetess

Climbing the wooden viewing platform where Prince Yi stood gazing toward the Divine Willow, Matron Zou spun a smooth cover story: Mengrong had stumbled across a bloody street brawl with blades after the ladies' tea and been scared half to death. Accepting it guiltily—wishing he'd gone to fetch her—Shen Jianchen vowed he'd never abandon her and planned to return to Bianliang once she recovered.

Meanwhile, Lin Jue surrounded the Chen Tea Emporium with troops on Prefect Li Ke's orders, accusing them of selling fake tea—until Chen Jiangmu slipped him a pouch of silver and got the real tip: go pay tribute to greedy Liang Shicheng, or tomorrow the silk emporium got raided next!

Consulting Zhu Xiyan on the eunuch's appetite, Chen Jianglin heard a jaw-dropping figure—**five hundred thousand taels**—which Zhu Xiyan figured might at least buy Bianliang market protection. Summoning Haoyu (now deftly overseeing Chen outlets while counting down eighteen months on his two-year vow), Chen Jianglin rode with his son and a heavy chest of half a million taels straight to Liu'an Villa.

Receiving them smoothly and praising Haoyu's famous poetess bride, Liang Shicheng startled both father and son by pushing the half-million-tael chest right back across the floor!

"Keep the silver—I want to meet one person: your wife, the Poetess of Jiangnan. Two days! Deliver her here, or the Chen empire shuts down across the whole realm!"

Riding away stunned, Haoyu instantly smelled lechery—which Zhu Xiyan furiously confirmed when Chen Jianglin stopped by Zhu Manor: Liang Shicheng was infamous for preying on married women! While Zhu Xiyan vowed to confront Liang Shicheng himself tomorrow morning, Haoyu dropped the silver chest back at Xinglong Pavilion and bolted straight to Youlin Retreat to beg Prince Yi's help.
