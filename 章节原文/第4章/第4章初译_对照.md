第4章 老实当好朱淑真
## Chapter 4: Chapter 4: Playing Zhu Shuzhen for Real

一切处理完毕，千里寻感到额头一阵刺痛：“苦荞，你家小姐住哪个屋子？赶紧带我进去，让我好好休息休息。”
With the mess settled, a sharp sting shot across Qianli Xun's forehead. "Kuqiao, which room is your Miss's? Take me there right now—I need proper rest."

苦荞上前扶住小姐：“小姐跟我来，您住的屋子就在后面。”
Kuqiao stepped forward to steady her. "Come with me, Miss. Your quarters are right in the back."

千里寻没有拒绝苦荞的搀扶，她太累。地震可怕的场景还历历在目，那股神力带来的恐惧感也未消失，穿越成朱淑真，又需要时间适应。帮朱淑真处理完那两个家伙，一轻松，疲惫感自然袭来。
Qianli Xun did not refuse the support; exhaustion weighed her down. The horrors of the earthquake still burned behind her eyes, dread from that uncanny force lingered, and waking up as Zhu Shuzhen took energy to process. Once those two nuisances were dealt with and her tension dropped, fatigue hit like a wave.

苦荞扶着小姐拐到后面。千里寻抬头看到一个园门，上写竹兰居。走进院中，发现到处种着竹子和兰花，倒也符合竹兰居的雅称。
Kuqiao guided her around to the rear. Qianli Xun looked up at a garden gate inscribed "Zhulan Residence." Inside the courtyard, bamboo and orchids filled every corner, living up to the refined name.

进入屋子，给人一种古朴而典雅的感觉，家具都是用红木打制而成，桌上放着精致的花瓶和香炉，增添了一丝清新。窗帘是用织物制作而成，使得房间充满通透感，上面的花鸟图案为房间添加了生气。墙壁上挂着字画，加上案上的书卷，只觉得书香之气扑面而来。
Stepping inside the rooms brought an understated, classic grace. Every piece of furniture was carved from rosewood; a delicate vase and incense burner sat on the table, lending a fresh charm. Sheer woven curtains let light breathe through the room, their bird-and-flower patterns adding life. Calligraphy scrolls hung along the walls, and volumes stacked on the desk filled the air with a scholar's quiet fragrance.

“朱淑真住的屋子真不错！”千里寻边说边坐到床榻上。
"Zhu Shuzhen has quite a nice place!" Qianli Xun said, sinking onto the bed.

“您说的哪里话？好像您不是我家小姐一样？”苦荞虽怀疑，却不愿承认，她找来一个小木盒，打开，用干净的巾帕从里面沾些药膏，涂在小姐额头的伤口上，“这药很管用，不出两日，小姐额头的伤就好了。”
"What are you saying, Miss, talking as if you aren't yourself?" Doubt lingered in Kuqiao's mind, yet she refused to accept it. She fetched a small wooden box, opened it, dabbed ointment onto a clean cloth, and applied it to her mistress's forehead. "This salve works wonders. In two days your cut will heal."

涂完药，苦荞把小木盒放好，乖巧地站在一边。
Once done, Kuqiao put the box away and stood obediently to the side.

千里寻瞅着苦荞：“你靠近些，我给你说件事。”
Qianli Xun eyed her. "Come closer. I have something to tell you."

苦荞小心靠近。
Kuqiao stepped in with caution.

千里寻压低声音：“其实我不是你家小姐，我本叫千里寻，在我们那时代遇到大地震，估计得有7.5级以上，我在地震中丧生，魂魄被一股可怕的力量卷走，醒来时，就跑到朱淑真身上，到现在我都不相信这会是真的。据我估计，你家小姐已被林觉家暴而亡。”
Lowering her voice, Qianli Xun said, "Truth is, I'm not your Miss. My real name is Qianli Xun. In my era, a massive earthquake struck—at least magnitude 7.5. I died in it, a terrifying force swept my soul away, and when I woke up, I was inside Zhu Shuzhen's body. I still can't believe it's real. My guess is Lin Jue beat your real Miss to death."

苦荞不愿承认，跪在地上：“您就是我家小姐，只是被姑爷打晕，失忆了而已。”
Refusing to accept it, Kuqiao dropped to her knees. "You are my Miss! Son-in-law just knocked you cold and made you lose your memory!"

“你也看到，我会功夫，你家小姐可会？”
"You saw me fight. Did your Miss know martial arts?"

“我家小姐只会写诗作词，一点儿功夫都不会，可这样貌、身段就是我家小姐，别人只会认为您是朱府千金。”
"My Miss only knew poetry and lyrics—not a scrap of martial arts. Still, that face and figure are hers. Everyone will take you for the daughter of Zhu Manor."

苦荞的话说得没错，千里寻在地震中已死亡，老天又给了一次新生的机会让她成为朱淑真，在这个时代别人只会认为她是朱淑真。
Kuqiao had a point. Qianli Xun had died in the quake, and Heaven had handed her a second life as Zhu Shuzhen. Nobody in this era would ever see her as anyone else.

“起来吧，跪着干什么？唉，我既成为你家小姐，那就老老实实做朱淑真，只是她的记忆我一点儿都没有。我还会功夫，重要的是我说话的风格跟你家小姐肯定不一样，别人很容易认出来，你没听林觉都说我不是朱淑真吗？”
"Get up—why are you kneeling? Fine. Since I've stepped into her shoes, I'll live as Zhu Shuzhen. Problem is, I don't have a shred of her memories. Plus I know martial arts, and my way of talking is nothing like hers. People will spot it right away—didn't you hear Lin Jue already saying I'm not Zhu Shuzhen?"

苦荞想了想说：“小姐放心，我们乡下有时也会发生这样的怪事，一个人突然生一场病，病好后就像变了个人，乡下管这叫重生。到时别人问起，就说姑爷把您打晕，醒来后重生了，当然跟以前不一样。”
Kuqiao thought for a moment. "Don't worry, Miss. Back in our village, strange things like this happen sometimes—someone falls ill, recovers, and acts like a whole new person. Country folk call it 'rebirth.' If anyone asks, we'll say Son-in-law knocked you unconscious, and when you woke up you were reborn—of course you're different now!"

“好！就这么说！再说事实如此。对了，我问你，为什么林觉打你家小姐？”
"Done! We'll go with that—it's pretty much the truth anyway. By the way, why did Lin Jue beat your Miss in the first place?"

“怎么还你家小姐，就是小姐您！”
"There you go saying 'your Miss' again! You're my Miss!"

“对，是我，我就是朱淑真。”说这话时，千里寻很不习惯，可她已经决定要老老老实实做朱淑真，以后还是把千里寻这个名字藏在心底。
"Right, right—me. I'm Zhu Shuzhen." The words still felt awkward on her tongue, yet having chosen to live as Zhu Shuzhen, she resolved to tuck the name Qianli Xun deep inside her heart.

苦荞说：“当初老爷把小姐您嫁给姑爷时，您老大不愿意，因为姑爷根本不是您喜欢的类型，他只会练兵，人又粗鲁，常常醉酒而归，而您喜欢的是能陪您花前月下吟诗作赋之人。所以，嫁给姑爷后您一直未与他圆房……”
Kuqiao explained, "When Master married you off to Son-in-law, you didn't want it one bit. He's nothing like the man you dreamed of—all he knows is drilling troops, he's rough, and he comes home drunk all the time. You wanted someone to recite poetry with beneath flowers and moonlight. So after the wedding, you never consummated the marriage..."

听到未圆房，千里寻忍不住高兴：“太好了！我的清白总算保住！”
Hearing that, Qianli Xun brightened at once. "Yes! My virtue is still intact!"

“正因为您不愿与姑爷圆房，又以死相逼，不准姑爷靠近，他才把商妓领到家里，当着您的面做出那些见不得人的事，姑爷见您毫不介意，更加生气，才大打出手。”
"Because you refused to consummate the marriage and even threatened to kill yourself if he came near, he started dragging courtesans home and doing shameless things right in front of you. When you didn't even blink, he got madder and started hitting you."

“原来如此，为何朱老爷，不，我爹要把我嫁给一个我不喜欢的人？”千里寻开始慢慢承认自己就是朱淑真。
"So that's why. Then why did Master Zhu—no, my father—marry me to someone I didn't love?" Qianli Xun began accepting her role as Zhu Shuzhen bit by bit.

苦荞对小姐的转变感到满意：“小姐本与徐家公子青梅竹马，两小无猜，怎奈后来徐家败落，不知迁往何处，徐公子临行前允诺小姐一旦考上功名，必然迎娶，可左等右等，等了整整五年，也不见徐公子前来。可您都二十岁，已是老姑娘，再不嫁人只怕没人要，正好姑爷上门提亲，老爷和夫人认为林家不错，便应下这门亲事。”
Pleased by the shift, Kuqiao went on, "You and Young Master Xu were innocently devoted childhood sweethearts. Sadly, the Xu family fell on hard times and moved away, nobody knows where. Before leaving, Young Master Xu promised he would come marry you once he passed the civil exams. You waited five whole years without a word from him. By then you were twenty—already an old maid—and Master and Madam feared nobody would want you if you waited longer. Right then Son-in-law came to propose. Master and Madam thought the Lin family was a decent match, so they agreed."

千里寻慨叹，无论在哪个时代她都是老姑娘。心里如此想，嘴上却不愿承认：“二十岁怎么能算老姑娘？在我来的那个时代，我都三十岁，还没结婚，周围还有好几个人追求我。”
Qianli Xun sighed inwardly—an old maid in any era! Out loud, she refused to admit it. "How is twenty an old maid? Where I come from, I was thirty, still single, and still had plenty of guys chasing me!"

说到这里，她想起申简辰，他也被神秘的力量卷走，不知道是否也穿越到这个时代，希望他安然无恙。
At that, Shen Jianchen flashed across her mind. That mysterious force had swept him away too. Had he landed in this era as well? She hoped he was safe.

苦荞瞪大眼睛：“什么？三十岁还没嫁人？要知道在这个时代女子三十岁都是当奶奶的岁数。”
Kuqiao's eyes popped wide. "What? Still unmarried at thirty? In this day and age, women are grandmothers by thirty!"

千里寻笑道：“三十岁就当奶奶？你骗我吧！”
Qianli Xun laughed. "Grandmothers at thirty? You're pulling my leg!"

“我怎么可能骗小姐您？再说我喜欢现在的小姐。”
"Why would I lie to you, Miss? Besides, I like you better now."

“为什么？”
"Why?"

“以前的小姐虽好，可话不多，整日写些我读不懂的诗词，还柔柔弱弱，被姑爷欺负。现在不一样，不仅跟我聊天儿，重要的是会功夫，以后跟小姐混，肯定不会被人欺负。”
"The old Miss was kind, sure, yet she kept quiet, spent all day writing poems I couldn't understand, and let Son-in-law push her around because she was frail. You're different—you chat with me, and best of all, you know martial arts! Sticking with you, nobody's going to bully us ever again!"

千里寻笑道：“你家小姐我又渴又饿，赶紧弄点吃的喝的来，我吃饱喝足好美美睡一觉。”
Qianli Xun chuckled. "Your Miss is starving and parched. Go grab me some food and drink so I can fill up and get a good sleep."

“好嘞，我这就给小姐去准备。”
"Right away! I'll go fetch it for you."

苦荞出去后，千里寻走到铜镜跟前，镜子模模糊糊，根本无法与玻璃镜子相比。她还是看到镜子中的脸与千里寻根本不是一人，她只是朱淑真。
After Kuqiao left, Qianli Xun walked over to the bronze mirror. The hazy surface couldn't compare to modern glass, yet it still showed clearly that the face staring back wasn't Qianli Xun—it belonged to Zhu Shuzhen.

她在镜前坐下，认真欣赏这张脸，既古典又精致：“没想到朱淑真也是个美人儿。”
Sitting down, she admired the refined, classic features. "Turns out Zhu Shuzhen's a real beauty."

她对镜中说：“朱淑真，你听着，以后你就是我，我就是你，我要借着你的名字和身份在属于你的时代生活下去。”说完，一一去掉头饰。
She spoke to her reflection. "Listen up, Zhu Shuzhen. From now on, you're me and I'm you. I'll borrow your name and your life to live on in your era." With that, she removed her hairpins one by one.

朱淑真的头发真是又多又长，漆黑如墨，这发质，放在千里寻那个时代绝对可以接洗发水广告。
Zhu Shuzhen's hair was thick, long, and black as ink—good enough to land a shampoo commercial back in Qianli Xun's time.

正想着，苦荞用盘子端着饭食进来：“小姐，厨房正好做了您爱吃的小鸡双色莲子羹和野菜包子，我给您端些来，您先垫垫。”
Just then, Kuqiao returned carrying a tray. "Miss, the kitchen happened to make your favorites—two-color lotus seed chicken soup and wild vegetable steamed buns. I brought some to tide you over."

千里寻已忍不住，赶紧坐到桌前：“大宋的伙食真不错，虽然只有两样，却看着精致，尤其是这小鸡……”
Unable to wait, Qianli Xun hurried to the table. "Song food looks great! Only two dishes, yet so delicate—especially this chicken..."

“小鸡双色莲子羹。”苦荞见小姐一时没记住，又说了一遍。
"Two-color lotus seed chicken soup," Kuqiao prompted when her mistress stumbled over the name.

“对，小鸡双色莲子羹，听这名字就直到不好做，估计得好几道工序，而且熬制时间还不短。”
"Right, two-color lotus seed chicken soup. You can tell just from the name it takes a ton of steps and hours of simmering."

“小姐说得没错，确实麻烦，您赶紧享用吧。”
"You're right, Miss, it's real work. Go ahead and eat."

千里寻已忍不住，大口吃了起来，确实美味。
Qianli Xun dug in with big bites. Delicious.

苦荞在一旁看着她的吃相，比以前的小姐豪放许多。想起被关的姑爷，为难道：“小姐，姑爷在屋子里大喊大叫，说您殴打夫君，要到衙门告您，依我看，不如把他放了，省得他胡乱喊叫。”
Watching her eat with far more gusto than her former mistress ever had, Kuqiao remembered the prisoner and frowned. "Miss, Son-in-law is hollering his head off in that room, saying you assaulted your husband and threatening to haul you before the yamen. Maybe we should let him out before he raises the roof?"

千里寻喝口羹，三下五除二吃完一个包子，边吃边说：“没关系，等我吃饱，把他的嘴堵住，就没人能听到他瞎掰掰。”
Qianli Xun took a sip of soup and polished off a bun in three bites. "No problem. Once I finish eating, I'll gag him so nobody hears his nonsense."

“可姑爷毕竟是练兵官，您关押临安城的练兵官，恐怕官府会治罪。”
"He's still the Training Officer, though! Locking up Lin'an's Training Officer—won't the local yamen charge you?"

“治什么罪？这是家事，官府管不着，再说官府真要来管，应该先管管他怎么家暴你家小姐。”
"Charge me with what? It's a domestic matter—none of their business! And if the yamen wants to poke its nose in, they can start by asking why he beats his wife!"

千里寻把羹和包子吃了个精光，起身说：“羹虽好喝，解渴差些，你再给我弄些茶水来，我先去看看林觉，回来好喝。”
Having cleaned the bowl and plate, Qianli Xun stood up. "Great soup, didn't quench my thirst though. Make me some tea—I'll go check on Lin Jue and drink it when I get back."

“是。”苦荞说完，看着小姐出了屋门，走到放着做茶工具的茶桌前开始做茶。
"Yes, Miss." Watching her step out, Kuqiao walked to the tea table stocked with utensils and set to work.

千里寻来到关林觉的屋外，一名下人端着盘子和空碗出来，显然是刚刚伺候林觉用过饭，见到千里寻前来，赶紧行礼：“夫人。”
Outside the room holding Lin Jue, a servant emerged with a tray and an empty bowl—he had just fed the master. Seeing Qianli Xun, he bowed in haste. "Madam."

千里寻对这个称呼很不喜欢，她已是朱淑真，只能接受，没好气地问：“他在里面可老实？”
She hated the title, yet as Zhu Shuzhen she had to swallow it. "Is he behaving in there?" she asked in a flat voice.

下人怎能随便说主人家老实或不老实，随口回道：“主君被夫人绑着很不舒服。”
Hardly daring to judge his master's behavior, the servant hedged, "Master is uncomfortable tied up like that, Madam."

“你下去吧。”
"Leave us."

下人离开后，千里寻推开门，穿过折叠的木质屏风，看到林觉被五花大绑躺在床上，这会儿倒老实，没有胡乱喊叫。
Once the servant left, Qianli Xun pushed the door open, stepped past the folding wooden screen, and spotted Lin Jue trussed up tight on the bed—quiet now, no longer yelling.

林觉听到有脚步声，立刻坐起来，到底是有些武功根基的人，不用手撑着，都能迅速起身。
At her footsteps, he sat straight up—proof of real martial training, rising without even using his hands.

见朱淑真向他走来，头上没有发饰，依然十分貌美，可给他的感觉既熟悉又陌生。这个女人以前只会写些他看不懂的诗词，还整日愁眉不展，幽怨多情，显得既酸臭又清高，而且从心里瞧不上他这位武夫。为何被他家暴打得晕死后醒来竟像变了个人？不仅眉头舒展许多，说话的声音也变大，关键是会功夫，拳头实在厉害。
Watching Zhu Shuzhen walk toward him, hair bare of ornaments yet still striking, Lin Jue felt an eerie mix of familiar and strange. Before, this woman only scribbled poems he could not fathom, wore a constant frown, acted aloof and priggish, and looked down her nose at a military man like him. How had one beating knocked her out and turned her into someone else—brow relaxed, voice loud, and packing punches like iron?

林觉决定先服软：“娘子，我们毕竟是夫妻，你把夫君捆起来传出去被人笑话，还是把我放开。”
Lin Jue chose to soften first. "Wife, we're still husband and wife. Word gets out you tied up your husband, people will laugh at us. Untie me."

千里寻手叉腰：“谁是你娘子？谁跟你是夫妻？告诉你，以前的朱淑真已被你打死，老娘是重生后的朱淑真，不仅没以前好欺负，还要让你老老实实休了我！”
Qianli Xun planted her hands on her hips. "Who's your wife? Who's married to you? Let me make this clear: the old Zhu Shuzhen died under your fists! I'm the reborn Zhu Shuzhen—not only am I done being your punching bag, you're going to sit right there and write me a bill of divorce!"
