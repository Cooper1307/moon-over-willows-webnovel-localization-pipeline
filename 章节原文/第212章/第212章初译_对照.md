第212章 拜访郡主探虚实
## Chapter 212: Visiting the Princess to Sound Out the Truth

青瓜一直守在陈府的大门口，瞧见两驾马车回来，兴冲冲迎上去：“公子，您回来了。”
结果从马车里下来的全是像他一样的下人，青瓜立刻收起笑容：“怎么就你们回来？公子呢？”
一个男仆指着后面的马车：“在后面跟着。”
Qinggua had been waiting by the main gate of Chen Manor the whole time. The moment two carriages rolled up, he bounced forward. "Young Master, you're back!"

青瓜又露出笑脸，迎上去：“公子在外面玩得可开心？”
Out climbed a crowd of servants just like himself. His grin vanished. "Why is it just you lot? Where's Young Master?"

陈浩宇从马车上下来，瞅到青瓜就来气：“你瞧瞧人家，一个个武功高强，你就知道嬉皮笑脸，我一进家门，像狗皮膏药一样黏着我。”
One servant jabbed a thumb behind him. "In the carriage behind."

被公子嫌弃，青瓜不满，依然笑着脸：“公子不要光怪我，他们都是少夫人的人，少夫人多厉害，您又不是不知道。”
Qinggua plastered his smile back on and trotted over. "Did you have a good time out, Young Master?"

陈浩宇更来气，一脚踢过去：“怎么着！你还敢内涵本公子不厉害？小心你的狗命！”
The sight of Qinggua set Chen Haoyu's temper flaring as he stepped down. "Look at them, every one a trained fighter. And look at you, grinning like an idiot and sticking to me like a dog-skin plaster the second I step through the door."

还好青瓜有经验，早做好防范准备，迅速躲开。
Stung by the scorn, Qinggua kept smiling all the same. "Don't put it all on me, Young Master. They're Young Madam's people, and you know how formidable she is."

红豆等下人看到青瓜被姑爷欺负的样子，觉得好笑，在一旁直乐。
That stung worse. Chen Haoyu aimed a kick at him. "What's that? You're sneaking in a jab saying I'm weak? Watch your miserable hide!"

陈浩宇继续批评：“以后你跟着他们好好学功夫，学好了我保准会对你另眼相待。”
Seasoned by long habit, Qinggua had braced for it and hopped clear.

青瓜面露难色：“公子，您这是为难我。”
Hongdou and the others chuckled at the sight of the young master taking his frustration out on Qinggua.

陈浩宇说：“要是不好好学，我就把你从后院赶到西院去，别再想跟我睡一个屋子。”
"From now on," Chen Haoyu barked, "train with them and learn real martial arts. Get good at it and I'll treat you with fresh respect."

青瓜刚体验两天睡在主人房中的感觉，甚是舒适，根本不想回去跟下人挤在一起：“好吧，公子既发话，我……好好学！”
Qinggua pulled a sour face. "Young Master, you're asking the impossible."

进入陈府，来到自己的院子外，陈浩宇以为走错方向，回头瞧瞧，没错啊。天虽黑，可这方向他走过十几年，怎么可能错？
"If you don't train hard, I'll boot you out of the back courtyard and back into the West Courtyard. Forget about sleeping in the same room as me."

可他记得自己住的院子外没有栽种竹子，怎么院门两边多了茂密的翠竹？
Qinggua had tasted two nights of sleeping in the master's quarters and found it far too comfortable to give up for a crowded servants' dorm. "Fine! Since you say so, Young Master, I'll... I'll train hard!"

正疑惑间，青瓜解释道：“公子，就是您的院子，竹子是少夫人让栽种的，今日花匠才种好。您再抬头瞧，您的院子连名字都有了。”
Inside Chen Manor, Chen Haoyu reached the entrance of his own courtyard and stopped short, convinced he had taken a wrong turn. He glanced back. No, the path was right. Dark as it was, he had walked this route for over a decade; how could he miss it?

陈浩宇抬头看到“墨香苑”三个字，怒火中烧：“俗！俗不可耐！朱淑真！你太过分！”
Yet he remembered no bamboo outside his gate. Where had these thick clumps of green stalks flanking both sides come from?

陈浩宇冲进院内，向正屋走去。借着月色，瞅到东边种着一棵茂盛的树木，更是来气，无处发泄，回身一脚踢在青瓜的大腿上：“本公子不喜欢在院子里栽树，难道你不知？”
Before he could ask, Qinggua spoke up. "This is your courtyard, Young Master. Young Madam had the gardeners plant the bamboo today. Look up, too—your courtyard even has a name now."

青瓜一时疏忽，被踢到，捂住大腿：“公子怎能怪我？少夫人让移过来的，我一个下人哪能管得了主人？”
Chen Haoyu tilted his head up at the plaque reading Moxiang Courtyard and boiled over. "Vulgar! Beyond vulgar! Zhu Shuzhen, you've gone too far!"

陈浩宇又抬头看到“素心草堂”四个字：“起这么个穷酸名，还号称女词人，哼！”
He stormed through the gate toward the main hall. By the moonlight, he spotted a lush tree planted on the eastern side. With nowhere else to vent his rage, he spun around and kicked Qinggua square in the thigh. "Didn't you know I hate trees in my courtyard?"

千里寻正在看书，一抬头，见陈浩宇站在面前，一脸怒气。
Caught off guard this time, Qinggua clutched his thigh. "How is that my fault, Young Master? Young Madam ordered it transplanted here. What can a servant do to stop his mistress?"

她将书放下：“你回来的速度比我想象得要快，表现不错，以后跟我好好配合，咱两个都会相安无事。”
Chen Haoyu looked up again and read the plaque over the main room: Suxin Thatched Hall. "Calling it something that shabby, and she calls herself a poetess. Hmph!"

陈浩宇训斥道：“朱淑真，你太过分！不仅给院门口种竹子，给院子里栽树，还给每个屋子取名字，更过分的是你把我的屋子变成这么个穷酸样，还是人住的地儿吗？”
Inside, Qianli Xun was reading a book. She glanced up to find Chen Haoyu standing before her, fuming.

千里寻并未将陈浩宇的怒气当回事，站起来，走到他身边：“睁开你的眼睛好好瞧瞧，哪里穷酸了？我的家具全用香樟木打造而成，选用的颜色素淡些，不像你以前的家具尽显奢华，俗不可耐。”
She set the book down. "You got back faster than I expected. Not bad. Cooperate with me from here on out, and we'll stay out of each other's hair."

陈浩宇再生气，也拿朱淑真没办法，他尽量控制住怒气：“你我不是说好互不干涉吗？为何我在外面玩得好好的，你偏偏为回门这种小事差人把我叫回来？”
"Zhu Shuzhen, you've gone too far!" Chen Haoyu snapped. "Planting bamboo by my gate, sticking a tree in my courtyard, renaming every single room—and worst of all, turning my hall into this bare, penniless dump! Is this even fit for a human being to live in?"

千里寻解释道：“不怪我，老太太前来寻事，给布置的任务，我要不把你找回来，只怕会更麻烦，所以派红豆他们把你叫回来。放心，我还是有契约精神的，绝不限制你的自由。”
Unbothered by his tantrum, Qianli Xun rose and walked over. "Open your eyes and take a proper look. What's shabby about this? Every piece of my furniture is carved from fragrant camphorwood in clean, understated tones—nothing like that gaudy, tacky heap you used to keep in here."

听到奶奶前来寻事，朱淑真也怕惹麻烦事，陈浩宇转怒为喜：“呵呵，你不是天不怕地不怕吗？怎么害怕起我奶奶来？”
Furious as he was, Chen Haoyu had no leverage against her. He swallowed his temper. "Didn't we agree not to interfere with each other? I was having a fine time out there. Why did you send people to drag me home over something trivial like a return visit?"

“你哪只耳朵听到我怕老太太？我只是怕麻烦，多一事不如少一事，把你找回来可以省去很多麻烦。你呢，明日好好配合我回个门，你好我好大家好，你我也就相安无事。”
"Don't blame me," Qianli Xun explained. "Old Madam Xia came looking for trouble and handed me that task. If I hadn't fetched you back, it would have meant bigger headaches later, so I sent Hongdou and the men. Relax—I honor my contracts. I won't restrict your freedom."

陈浩宇总算抓住朱淑真的一根软肋，她怕麻烦，以后不如多给她制造些麻烦，让她不舒服，也好报了霸占他的院子和屋子以及遣散丫鬟的仇恨。
Hearing that Zhu Shuzhen had bent because Old Madam Xia came making trouble turned Chen Haoyu's anger into glee. "Heh! Didn't you act fearless of heaven and earth? Why are you scared of my grandmother?"

“本公子不跟你计较，回去睡觉，明儿还要跟你回朱府。”
"Which ear heard me say I'm scared of Old Madam Xia? I just hate hassle. Less drama beats more drama, and dragging you home saves me a mountain of trouble. As for you, behave tomorrow for the return visit to Zhu Manor. You stay happy, I stay happy, everyone stays happy, and we leave each other in peace."

陈浩宇出了素心草堂，来到后院，看到“博雅堂”三个字，没有刚才那么生气。他已找到对付朱淑真的方法，他决定等回完门，去见奶奶，奶奶这柄利刃他得好好用用。
At last Chen Haoyu had spotted a chink in her armor: she hated trouble. From now on, he would stir up plenty of it to make her miserable and pay her back for stealing his courtyard, gutting his rooms, and dismissing his maids.

又想到昨日敬茶时，奶奶把朱淑真气走后，还说她明事理，甚是喜欢她，看来奶奶是表面上装作讨厌朱淑真的样子。
"I won't stoop to arguing with you. I'm going to bed so I can make it to Zhu Manor tomorrow."

陈浩宇管不了那么多，不管是表面讨厌还是实际讨厌，只要讨厌朱淑真，他陈浩宇就得利用。
Stepping out of Suxin Thatched Hall into the back courtyard, Chen Haoyu glanced at the plaque reading Boya Study without flaring up as before. He had a weapon against Zhu Shuzhen now. After the return visit tomorrow, he would pay his grandmother a call and put that sharp blade to good use.

白天申简辰离开醉云楼回到幽林别居时，正好碰见苗知瑜和白梅若来访。
Then a snag occurred to him. Yesterday, after Old Madam Xia drove Zhu Shuzhen off during the tea ceremony, she had praised the girl as sensible and admitted she liked her. Maybe Grandmother was only pretending to dislike her.

二人在门口遇到仪王，上前行礼。
No matter, Chen Haoyu decided. Pretend or real, so long as she gave Zhu Shuzhen grief, he would make use of it.

申简辰吩咐下人将二人带去见梦容，他不愿与二人一同进入别居，大家无话可说，一路走着怪别扭的，趁此机会，不如去神柳下坐坐，便带着白风转身向密林深处走去。
Earlier that afternoon, when Shen Jianchen returned to Youlin Retreat from Zuiyun Tower, he ran right into Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo arriving at the gate.

白梅若瞅着仪王的背影，悄悄问：“仪王该不会不喜欢我们前来探望郡主？”
Both women bowed to Prince Yi at once.

苗知瑜否认道：“不会，仪王向来生性冷淡，对郡主以外的女子都比较冷漠。”
Shen Jianchen instructed a servant to escort them to Qiao Mengrong. Walking in with them meant painful small talk with nothing to say, so he seized the excuse to visit the Divine Willow instead, turning back into the deep woods with Bai Feng in tow.

白梅若小声提醒：“我看未必，难道你忘了？”
Watching Prince Yi's retreating figure, Bai Meiruo murmured, "You don't think Prince Yi dislikes us coming to visit the princess?"

苗知瑜装作醒悟的样子：“哦，她是个例外。”
"Not at all," Miao Zhiyu replied. "Prince Yi has always been aloof, distant toward every woman except the princess."

二人在别居下人的带领下来到郡主的闺房小院，再被下人带进院中。
Bai Meiruo dropped her voice. "I wouldn't be so sure. Have you forgotten?"

下人向里禀报：“郡主，乔家和林家的少夫人前来拜见。”
Miao Zhiyu put on a show of sudden realization. "Ah, right. She is the exception."

失去与淑真的友情，乔梦容更觉孤单，正想结交新朋友。仔细想来，门外的二位都与朱淑真存在产生芥蒂的可能，不如请进来聊聊，探探虚实。
A retreat servant guided them to the princess's private courtyard and announced at the door, "Your Highness, the young madams of the Qiao and Lin households request an audience."

喜兰明白郡主的心意，走到门口：“郡主有请二位夫人。”
Having lost Shuzhen's friendship, Qiao Mengrong felt hollow and eager for new companions. Both women outside had every reason to harbor grudges against Zhu Shuzhen; inviting them in for a chat would let her test the waters.

二人才缓缓进入，见郡主面色发白，坐在书桌前，身上披着一件外衣，显然身上的伤还未大好。她们上前拜见：“佳和郡主。”
Reading her mistress's mind, Xilan stepped to the doorway. "Her Highness invites both madams inside."

乔梦容放下手中的书卷：“喜兰，看坐。”
They entered at an unhurried pace. Qiao Mengrong sat at her desk, pale and draped in an outer cloak, her wound far from healed. Both guests curtsied. "Greetings, Princess Jiahe."

喜兰上前说：“二位夫人请坐下与郡主说话。”
Qiao Mengrong laid down her scroll. "Xilan, bring seats."

两人挑位置坐下，外面有丫鬟进来为二人上好茶。
Xilan arranged the chairs, and another maid brought in fine tea.

苗知瑜先开口说话：“许久未见郡主，我二人正好今日无事，便约着前来拜见，还望郡主不要嫌弃。”
Miao Zhiyu spoke first. "It has been ages since we last saw Your Highness. With nothing pressing today, we agreed to pay our respects. We hope we are not intruding."

“说的哪里话？你们能来探望，陪我聊天儿，帮我解闷儿，我感谢还来不及，怎能嫌弃？”
"Nonsense," Qiao Mengrong said. "Coming to visit, keep me company, and lift my spirits—I can't thank you enough. How could it be an intrusion?"

郡主一席话令二人轻松许多。
Her warmth put both visitors at ease.

白梅若问：“郡主身上可大好？”
"Has Your Highness recovered from your injury?" Bai Meiruo asked.

乔梦容意识到二人已知哥哥抢亲之事，闹得那么大，又岂能不知？说不定整个临安城的人都在议论。
Qiao Mengrong realized they knew all about her brother raiding the wedding procession. A scandal that loud had no doubt traveled across all of Lin'an.

“看来你们已经知道，我长这么大，还是头一次成为大家议论之人，都是拜朱淑真所赐。”
"So you already heard. In all my years, this is the first time I've become the talk of the town—all thanks to Zhu Shuzhen."

二人从郡主的话语中听出明显的不满来，她们相视一笑。
Hearing the raw bitterness in her voice, the two guests traded a subtle smile.

苗知瑜说：“郡主不必忧虑，大家怎敢随便议论仪王和郡主，只是为郡主鸣不平而已。”
"Your Highness need not fret," Miao Zhiyu said. "Who would dare speak out of turn about Prince Yi or Your Highness? Folks are only indignant on your behalf."

“你们不必哄我，议论指定少不了，要是把我当朋友，实话实说，告诉我大家如何议论？”
"Don't try to flatter me. There's bound to be gossip. If you count me as a friend, give me the truth. What are people saying?"

郡主此话显然愿意与二人做朋友，看来不枉此行。
Those words signaled that Princess Jiahe wanted them in her circle; their trip had already paid off.

白梅若说：“郡主既愿意跟我二人做朋友，我说了，郡主可别治我的罪。”
"Since Your Highness is willing to take us as friends," Bai Meiruo said, "promise you won't hold my bluntness against me."

“既是朋友，应当无话不谈，何来治罪一说？”
"Friends speak without holding back. Why would I hold it against you?"

白梅若并非无脑之人，仔细挑些郡主爱听的：“大家都议论幽栖居士有才无德，自己遇不到好姻缘就去勾引仪王，想要攀高枝。还说她最会勾男子的魂儿，不经意间会让男子为她神魂颠倒，做下傻事。还有人说去年全城人议论她喜欢红杏出墙、暗送秋波实在不假。”
No fool, Bai Meiruo picked the exact tidbits the princess craved. "Everyone says Youqi Jushi has talent without virtue—that failing to land a good marriage herself, she set out to seduce Prince Yi and climb above her station. They say she has a knack for bewitching men, making them lose their heads and do foolish things without even realizing how she hooked them. People are also saying last year's rumors about her wandering eyes and flirting behind closed doors were true all along."

乔梦容发现人的变化实在快，去年大家议论时，她还为淑真鸣不平，今日听到这些话，却认为骂得甚是解气，还认为朱淑真就是这样的人。
Qiao Mengrong marveled at how fast feelings could turn. Last year, she had bristled in defense of Shuzhen; today, those same jabs felt vindicating, even accurate.

“不经意间”和“暗送秋波”的说法，她甚是认同，在她看来朱淑真就是借着陪她，找她聊天儿的空当偷偷勾引哥哥，才使得哥哥心智被迷惑，做下令所有人难以理解的抢亲之事。
"Without realizing" and "flirting behind closed doors" hit home hardest. In her eyes, Zhu Shuzhen had used every visit and chat at Youlin Retreat as cover to seduce her brother, clouding his mind until he committed a wedding raid nobody could fathom.

“没想到大家如此了解她，我真后悔以前没看清她的真面目，捧出一颗真心将她视作闺中密友，结果因她身心俱伤。”
"I didn't know people saw through her so clear. How I regret failing to recognize her true face sooner. I handed her my whole heart as a confidante, and she left me wounded body and soul."

苗知瑜说：“郡主与她接触的时日并不长，看不出她的真面目实属正常。我可是从小认识她，真正看清她的真面目也是与文渊成亲后，只能说明幽栖居士是个很会掩饰的人。”
"You only knew her a short while, Your Highness," Miao Zhiyu said. "It's natural you missed her true nature. I grew up knowing her, and even I didn't see through her until after marrying Wenyuan. It only proves how skilled Youqi Jushi is at wearing a mask."

乔梦容冷笑道：“她掩饰得实在太好。不过苗夫人还是比我强，你与她从小相识，都爱好诗词，经常一起参加诗会，也没见你视她为闺中密友，足以看出你与她不是一类人。”
Qiao Mengrong gave a cold laugh. "She wears it well indeed. Still, Madam Miao, you did better than me. You knew her from childhood, shared a love of poetry, and attended salons together, yet you never made her your close friend. That shows you and she were never cut from the same cloth."

苗知瑜解释道：“知瑜曾经也想过这个问题，开始怎么都想不明白，后来还是我的一位闺中密友一语点醒梦中人。她说我虽也参加诗会，却从未被他人非议，说明我时刻谨守闺中女子的操守。而幽栖居士乃性情中人，常常忘记女子的操守，才惹得他人非议。我们二人骨子里甚不相同，故而很难成为真正的朋友。”
"I used to wonder about that myself," Miao Zhiyu explained. "At first I couldn't figure out why, until a close friend opened my eyes with a single remark. She pointed out that though I attend poetry banquets too, no scandal has ever touched me because I always guard a gentlewoman's propriety. Youqi Jushi follows her impulses and forgets feminine decorum, which invites gossip. At our core we are opposites, so we could never be true friends."

乔梦容叹道：“是啊，她哪有女子的操守？无论品德还是行为都不符合大家闺秀的风范。亏我去年还为她写的那句‘和衣睡倒人怀’正名，如今想来也只有她才能写出厚颜无耻的句子。枉我这么多年默默喜欢她的诗词，从今往后，我再也不会这么傻。”
Qiao Mengrong sighed. "Indeed. What propriety does she have? Neither her morals nor her conduct fit a well-bred lady. And to think last year I defended that line she wrote, 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'! Looking back, only someone as shameless as her could pen such words. All those years I admired her poetry in secret—never again will I be such a fool."

白梅若趁机说：“要是大家知道她品行不端，估计江南女词人的美称很快就没了。”
Bai Meiruo seized the opening. "Once word gets out about her lack of virtue, her title as the Poetess of Jiangnan won't last long."

这话算是提醒了乔梦容：“她既不仁，也别怪我不义，等我从汴梁回来，需做些事情，让她丢了江南女词人的美称。”
That sparked an idea in Qiao Mengrong. "Since she showed no kindness, she can't blame me for showing no mercy. Once I return from Bianliang, I'll take steps to strip her of that title."

二人听了更加开心。
Both visitors brightened at the news.

苗知瑜问：“郡主为何要回汴梁？”
"Why is Your Highness returning to Bianliang?" Miao Zhiyu asked.

乔梦容脸上才露出笑容：“官家下旨，让我跟哥哥回汴梁成亲。”
Only then did a genuine smile touch Qiao Mengrong's lips. "His Majesty issued an edict summoning Brother and me back to Bianliang to wed."

二人赶紧恭贺：“恭喜郡主如愿以偿！”
Both women offered their congratulations at once. "Congratulations on seeing your wish fulfilled, Your Highness!"

乔梦容满意地点点头。
Qiao Mengrong nodded in satisfaction.

白梅若说：“可惜郡主大婚我二人无法前去，等郡主回到临安，我们再前来贺喜。”
"What a pity we cannot attend your grand wedding," Bai Meiruo said. "Once you return to Lin'an, we will come offer our congratulations in person."

苗知瑜也说：“恐怕到时不光是你我二人，乔家、林家还有临安所有权贵都会前来贺喜。”
"When that day comes, it won't just be the two of us," Miao Zhiyu added. "The Qiaos, the Lins, and every noble household in Lin'an will come to celebrate."

乔梦容更开心：“以后你们二人要多与我走动，苗夫人也要将你的闺中密友多给我引荐引荐，希望我再次回到临安时，可以和临安城所有的大家闺秀成为朋友。”
Qiao Mengrong's spirits lifted further. "Come visit me often from now on, both of you. Madam Miao, introduce your close friends to me as well. By the time I return to Lin'an, I hope to count every well-bred lady in the city among my friends."

二人听得出，佳和郡主是想将临安所有大家闺秀联合起来孤立幽栖居士，二人甚是欣慰，更慨叹不枉此行。
Both guests caught the subtext: Princess Jiahe meant to unite Lin'an's entire gentlewoman circle to freeze Youqi Jushi out. Delighted, they felt their visit could not have gone better.

说完重要的话后，又陪佳和郡主闲聊一会儿，太阳快落山时两人才离开。
With the real business settled, they chatted a while longer and took their leave as the sun dipped toward the horizon.
