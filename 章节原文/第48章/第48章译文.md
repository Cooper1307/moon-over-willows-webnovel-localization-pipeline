# Chapter 48: Mistaken Identity in Excitement

Returning to Youlin Villa, it was already dark.

Bai Feng returned from West Third Alley and came to report to the prince. The prince was practicing martial arts in the courtyard. Bai Feng didn't dare disturb him, so he stood aside and watched. While watching, he sighed in admiration in his heart. The prince's martial arts were exquisite. He wondered when he could reach such a level.

Shen Jianchen caught sight of Bai Feng from the corner of his eye and quickly finished his martial arts: "How was today?"

Bai Feng replied, "The person delivering the manuscript didn't come today. I've already become familiar with the storyteller. He even invited me to his home for a meal and told me to wait at his place."

Shen Jianchen smiled, "You're quite good at making friends. That's good, saves you from waiting outside in the cold. It's normal not to catch him today. Continue tomorrow."

Bai Feng replied, "Yes, Your Highness. Shall I help you rest?"

Shen Jianchen wasn't sleepy: "Come with me to the high platform and take a look."

The master and servant left the main room and came to the high platform. The lamp in Wenyuan's room was still lit. He should be reading by candlelight at night. This guy wasn't taking the imperial examinations, so why was he working so hard?

Soon Shen Jianchen found the answer. The Song Dynasty wasn't like the era he had experienced. There weren't so many modern entertainment facilities. For Song people at home, reading should be the best way to pass the time.

Shen Jianchen looked toward the divine willow by moonlight. The moon was much rounder than when he first arrived at Youlin Villa, but it still wasn't truly full. The divine willow in the distance stood peacefully. Against the backdrop of mountain shadows, it appeared extremely small, yet in Shen Jianchen's heart, it was incomparably tall.

He came to this unfamiliar era with his love for Qianli Xun. The divine willow was the witness to his love for Qianli Xun. Although before transmigrating, they only had duels under the divine willow, yet for Shen Jianchen, every move and every stance of those duels was filled with love.

Qianli Xun might have transmigrated and become a talented scholar. Shen Jianchen was full of resentment toward the divine willow. Why did it have to destroy his feelings so ruthlessly?

Seeing the prince gazing silently into the distance, Bai Feng felt he needed to say something: "Your Highness, the storyteller told me today that the person delivering the manuscript doesn't look like a city person, but a country person. Could the person you're looking for live in the countryside?"

"The countryside?" Shen Jianchen seemed to be asking himself. He pondered in his heart. Perhaps Qianli Xun really did transmigrate and become an impoverished scholar. How could he have well-dressed servants around him? He decided to go to Dongren Street tomorrow. "Let's go back."

...

Dongren Street was easy to find. The Qiao family's coachman knew the place. After daybreak, he drove Shen Jianchen toward the city.

Qiao Wenxian didn't want to go out. He stayed in his room, licking the wounds of Zhu Shuzhen's pain. Shen Jianchen could understand Wenyuan very well. Just like himself, when he learned that Qianli Xun might have transmigrated and become a destitute literatus, he felt both worried and sad.

Qiao Mengrong actually volunteered to go along, but Shen Jianchen didn't agree. Dongren Street was a gathering place for down-and-out scholars. There were definitely more men there. It would be inconvenient for Mengrong to follow.

Faced with her brother's refusal, Qiao Mengrong didn't say much. She already knew that the friend her brother was looking for was a man. There was no need to worry too much.

As soon as they entered the city, Bai Feng jumped off the carriage and headed straight for West Third Alley. He was about to start his daily task again.

The carriage carried Shen Jianchen through the busy market and turned east. The further east they went, the worse the living environment of the common people became.

The carriage stopped at an intersection. The coachman said, "Young Master Shen, this is Dongren Street. The street is too narrow for the carriage to enter. We need to walk."

Shen Jianchen got off the carriage and instructed, "Wait here."

Dongren Street was indeed narrow. On both sides of the street were dilapidated houses. On the ground were various kinds of garbage and sewage. From time to time, a sour stench wafted over.

Shen Jianchen discovered that there were actually children and women too.

The children were playing in the garbage. Each one had dirty hands and faces.

The women sat at their doorways—nothing like Qiao Mengrong or Miao Zhiyu. Dull eyes, coarse cloth, hair in messy buns without ornaments. Rough skin, no trace of powder.

From time to time, down-and-out scholars passed by, heads bowed, brows lowered, clutching manuscripts in their arms, their clothes worn and tattered.

Shen Jianchen felt a wave of heartache. He stopped a passing scholar: "Brother, is there a scholar named Qianli Xun here?"

That person shook his head: "Never heard of him."

Shen Jianchen went to ask a woman sitting by the street doing needlework: "Sister, is there someone named Qianli Xun here?"

The woman looked up for a moment, didn't say anything, and continued her needlework.

A group of children ran over. One child said, "Brother, you dress so well. You must be very rich. Could you buy us some candy?"

Shen Jianchen thought of a plan and said to the children, "If you help brother find the person named Qianli Xun, I'll buy you lots of good food."

That child said, "It's a deal!"

"It's a deal! Brother will wait for you at the intersection. After you find him, come to the intersection and find me."

After Shen Jianchen finished speaking, the children scattered in all directions.

He returned to the intersection, gave the coachman a chunk of silver, and told him to buy some candies and snacks.

The coachman quickly bought them. There were two large paper bags in total, which he placed in the carriage.

The children hadn't come yet. Shen Jianchen got into the carriage first and sat inside, pretending to sleep for a while to rest his mind.

Finally, he heard the children's voices: "Where is brother?"

Shen Jianchen immediately came out of the carriage: "Did you find out?"

The child said, "We found out. There's someone named Qian Lixun."

Shen Jianchen was both happy and disappointed. He told the coachman to distribute the candies and snacks to the children.

The children were very happy, as if it were New Year. The older children packed up the candy and pulled Shen Jianchen: "Brother, I'll take you to find him."

Surrounded by the children, Shen Jianchen walked deeper into Dongren Street.

After walking not far, they stopped in front of a house. The child said, "Brother, the person lives here." The child went forward and knocked on the door twice with his fist.

The person who opened the door was a woman holding a baby. Seeing it was a group of children, she said angrily, "Who told you to knock on our door like that?"

The children ran away making a racket.

Shen Jianchen stepped forward and asked, "Excuse me, does Qianli Xun live here?"

The woman looked Shen Jianchen up and down, then shouted inside, "Old Qian! Someone's looking for you!"

A man came out from the house. He had a scholarly air, probably close to thirty years old, holding a book in his hand: "Who is it?"

His wife scolded, "All you do is nose in that book all day! What good's that doing? Go out and peddle something instead—feed me and your son!"

The man said, "With a stranger here, say less."

Shen Jianchen walked into the courtyard and stared at the man: "Are you Qianli Xun?"

The man said, "I am indeed Qian Lixun. I wonder what business the young master has with me?"

[TN: In Chinese, "千离循" (Qian Lixun) and "千里寻" (Qianli Xun) are homophones—they sound identical but are written with completely different characters. This phonetic coincidence is the reason for Shen Jianchen's mistaken identity.]

Hearing the affirmative answer, Shen Jianchen couldn't hold back any longer. He let his emotions burst forth and went forward to hug the man: "I finally found you!"

The man was very surprised: "But I don't know you, young master."

Shen Jianchen still tightly hugged the man: "I'm Shen Jianchen. The divine willow brought you and me here. I didn't expect you to be like this now, with a wife and child."

The man was even more confused and pushed Shen Jianchen away: "Don't hug me. It's awkward. You say you're Shen Jianchen, but I don't know you. What divine willow? I don't understand what you're saying."

Being pushed by the other person, Shen Jianchen suddenly came to his senses. He transmigrated and became Zhao Pu. How could Qianli Xun still be called Qianli Xun after transmigrating? How could there be such a coincidence? But the storyteller clearly also knew Qianli Xun?

"Are you the Qianli Xun who goes to West Third Alley to provide manuscripts to the storyteller?"

"I've never written a story script, but I do have that plan. I just haven't carried it out yet."

"How is your name written?"

"The 'qian' from 'thousand armies and ten thousand horses,' the 'li' from 'leaving one's hometown,' and the 'xun' from 'following the rules.'"

So it wasn't Qianli Xun. It was just that the pronunciation of his name was similar to hers.

Shen Jianchen breathed a sigh of relief: "It seems I mistook you for someone else. Sorry to bother you."

He took out a tael of silver from his body: "For bothering you for half a day, please accept this small token."

The woman quickly came forward and took it: "Thank you, young master. You can tell at a glance that you're both rich and kind-hearted."

Along with relief, Shen Jianchen also felt somewhat disappointed. He decided not to search blindly in the future, or else he would make a fool of himself again.

He should focus on watching West Third Alley. After all, the content the storyteller spoke came from Qianli Xun's novel. People's names could coincidentally be similar, but the content of the story scripts absolutely couldn't be that coincidental.

Bai Feng was bored waiting at the storyteller's house and his stomach was hungry. He went out and found a noodle stall, ordered a bowl of hot soup noodles, ate them, paid, rubbed his stomach contentedly, and headed back toward West Third Alley.

Just as he reached the entrance of the alley, he passed by Kujue. Bai Feng didn't think much of it. He walked to the storyteller's door and knocked.

The storyteller himself opened the door: "The person delivering the manuscript already came and just left."

"What did he look like?"

"The same person as before. Dark-skinned, stocky, not very tall."

"What's his name?"

"Haven't I told you already? He doesn't reveal his name. Don't waste time. Hurry up and chase after him."

Bai Feng ran off like a puff of smoke. He quickly judged it should be the person who passed by him, so he chased in the direction of leaving the city.

There were too many people in the market, hard to identify. Bai Feng walked quickly while carefully observing. Finally, he spotted a stocky back. He rushed forward and grabbed the person's arm: "Hey, are you the person who just delivered the manuscript to the storyteller at West Third Alley?"

The person Bai Feng grabbed was indeed Kujue. Although he was strong, the young man grabbing him had even more strength than him. He couldn't shake him off for a moment: "Why are you grabbing me?"

Bai Feng dragged Kujue to a less crowded place to the side, still not letting go: "You haven't answered my question yet. After you answer, I'll let you go."

Kujue never lied. But the old employer had instructed him not to reveal her identity. As for the reason, he didn't quite understand. He only knew to follow orders. After hesitating for a moment, he said, "It was me who delivered it."

Bai Feng still didn't let go: "Since it's you, please come with me."

Kujue was dragged to a place where carriages were rented. Bai Feng called a carriage and dragged him onto it: "Don't worry. I'm a good person. I won't harm you. I'm taking you to see someone. After asking some questions, I'll let you go."

Kujue was very scared. Although he was slow, he still had a sense of self-protection. He tried hard to think of a way to escape.

He discovered the carriage had left the city and became even more scared, worrying he had been captured by bandits. He couldn't just sit and wait for death. He had to quickly think of a way to escape.

He looked at Bai Feng: "Little master, I need to take a leak. I can't hold it anymore."

"You're not trying to escape, are you? Don't worry. I'm a good person. I won't harm you."

How could Kujue believe that? He continued, "I really can't hold it. Let me get off the carriage and relieve myself."

Bai Feng told the coachman to stop the carriage. The two got off and walked to a remote place.

Kujue pretended to relieve himself. Seeing Bai Feng staring at him the whole time, he felt very uncomfortable: "Little master, with you staring, I can't pee. Could you not look at me?"

Bai Feng thought that standing beside him, the other person couldn't escape his grasp, so he looked to the side.

Who knew that as soon as he turned his head, Kujue immediately bolted, running extremely fast.

Bai Feng immediately chased after him. Kujue sped up. The two ran onto the main road. Just as they were about to catch up to Kujue, a carriage came and stopped, picking Kujue up. That carriage sped forward. Bai Feng had to turn back, jump onto the carriage, and tell the coachman to speed up and follow.

It turned out the carriage Kujue got on happened to be the one the village chief had taken to the city for business. Seeing Kujue being chased, he thought the boy had encountered bad people and gave him a hand.

After getting on the carriage, Kujue was endlessly grateful. He had finally saved his life.

The village chief warned him, "I heard there's a gang of bandits outside the city recently. You still need to be careful when going out."

Kujue nodded in agreement.

Bai Feng kept closely following the carriage ahead until it entered the bamboo forest.

Bai Feng asked the coachman, "Are there people living in the bamboo forest?"

The coachman replied, "There's a village inside. The villagers make their living from the bamboo forest."

"Just one village?"

"Just one. The carriage from just now should be from the village."

Bai Feng thought to himself, since there's only one village, it should be easy to find. Today there was finally progress. In the future, he wouldn't need to guard West Third Alley anymore. He should hurry back and report to the prince.
