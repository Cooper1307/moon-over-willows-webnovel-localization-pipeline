## 三、主要问题分类

### 3.1 严重错误（Critical Errors）

#### 1. 中英夹杂错误（Code-switching Error）
- **位置**：第365行
- **原文**：`Being pushed by the other person, Shen Jianchen immediately became much more清醒.`
- **问题**：英文句子中夹杂中文"清醒"
- **修正**：`Being pushed by the other person, Shen Jianchen immediately became much more sober.` 或 `...suddenly came to his senses.`

#### 2. 同音异字未加译者注（Missing Translator's Note for Homophones）
- **位置**：第168行、第335行
- **原文**：`千离循` 与 `千里寻` 发音相同但汉字不同
- **问题**：西方读者无法理解为何男主要抱住一个陌生人，需在关键处添加 `[TN]` 解释拼音相同但汉字意义完全不同
- **修正建议**：在男子说"鄙人正是千离循"后添加译者注：
  ```
  [TN: In Chinese, "千离循" (Qian Lixun) and "千里寻" (Qianli Xun) are homophones—
  they sound identical but are written with completely different characters. 
  This phonetic coincidence is the reason for Shen Jianchen's mistaken identity.]
  ```

---

### 3.2 风格与流畅度问题（Style & Fluency Issues）

#### 1. 重复用词（Repetitive Vocabulary）
- **"down-and-out scholar"** 出现3次（第83行、第101行、第107行）
  - 建议替换：第101行改为 `impoverished scholar`，第107行改为 `destitute literati`
- **"your highness"** 在对话中出现5次以上
  - 建议：部分替换为 `my lord` 或直接使用代词 `you`，避免单调

#### 2. 长句需拆分（Long Sentences Need Splitting）
- **位置**：第161行
- **原文**：`The women sat at the doorways of the houses. Their clothing and appearance were vastly different from Qiao Mengrong and Miao Zhiyu. Their eyes were dull and lacked vitality. They wore coarse cloth garments. Their hair was casually tied into buns without any hair ornaments. The skin on their faces was rough, with no trace of powder or makeup.`
- **问题**：虽然已拆分为多句，但整体仍显冗长，可进一步精简
- **优化**：`The women sat at their doorways—nothing like Qiao Mengrong or Miao Zhiyu. Dull eyes, coarse cloth, hair in messy buns without ornaments. Rough skin, no trace of powder.`

#### 3. 口语化程度不足（Insufficient Colloquial Tone）
- **位置**：第317行
- **原文**：`The woman scolded, "All you know is holding a book and reading all day! What can you get from that? You might as well go out and do some small business to support us mother and son!"`
- **问题**：语气略显正式，不够泼辣
- **优化**：`His wife scolded, "All you do is nose in that book all day! What good's that doing? Go out and peddle something instead—feed me and your son!"`

### 3.3 文化适配问题（Cultural Adaptation Issues）

#### 1. "碎银"的翻译（Translation of "Broken Silver"）
- **位置**：第227行
- **原文**：`gave the coachman a piece of broken silver`
- **问题**："broken silver" 直译可能让西方读者困惑
- **建议**：改为 `a chunk of silver` 或 `silver pieces`，更符合日常用语

#### 2. "一言为定"的翻译（Translation of Idiom）
- **位置**：第209行、第215行
- **原文**：`"It's a deal!"`
- **评价**：翻译准确且口语化，无需修改

#### 3. "尿遁"的喜剧效果（Comedic Effect of "Taking a Leak"）
- **位置**：第527行、第539行、第551行
- **原文**：`I need to take a leak` / `relieve myself`
- **评价**：用词得当，"take a leak" 足够口语化，"relieve oneself" 稍显正式但可接受
