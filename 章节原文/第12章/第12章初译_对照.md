第12章 拜见哥嫂请出面
## Chapter 12: Chapter 12: Winning Over Brother and Sister-in-Law

走了一阵，依然是曲径通幽，不同的是朱淑真住的园子像是幽中之幽，她哥嫂住的地方则是开阔之地。到达时，千里寻只觉眼前一亮，视野也随之变得开阔起来。
They walked on through winding paths—only where Zhu Shuzhen's courtyard was tucked in the deepest seclusion, her brother and sister-in-law's quarters opened into wide, airy grounds. Arriving there, Qianli Xun felt her eyes brighten as the view expanded.

朱淑真的哥哥叫朱景元，文才不及妹妹，倒也能写些文章。毕竟是家中男丁，父亲朱晞颜通过关系给他在临安谋了个文职，也娶了临安首富陈家的女儿陈文心为妻，夫妻挺和睦。朱淑真成婚时，陈文心已有八个月身孕，后诞下一儿子，有六个月有余。
Zhu Shuzhen's older brother, Zhu Jingyuan, lacked his sister's brilliance yet could turn out decent essays. Being the sole son, his father Zhu Xiyan had pulled strings to land him a civil post in Lin'an and married him to Chen Wenxin, daughter of the Chen family, the wealthiest clan in Lin'an. The couple got along well. When Zhu Shuzhen married, Chen Wenxin had been eight months pregnant; their son was now over six months old.

千里寻到达朱景元住的园子外，停住脚步：“苦荞，哥哥和嫂嫂人可好？”
Outside Zhu Jingyuan's courtyard gate, Qianli Xun halted. "Kuqiao, what are my brother and sister-in-law like?"

苦荞回道：“大公子才华虽比不上小姐您，人却老实，对小姐也好。至于少夫人，我听说嘴是刻薄些，人吧，还行。”
"Eldest Young Master may not match your talent, Miss, yet he's honest and treats you well," Kuqiao replied. "As for Young Madam—I've heard she has a sharp tongue, though she's decent at heart."

“听说？你没见过她？”
"Heard? You haven't met her?"

“跟着小姐见过几次，小姐不甚理她，她也没机会跟小姐说话，所以我没领教过。”
"Only a few times when following you, Miss. You barely spoke to her, so she never got the chance to say much—haven't seen her sharp tongue myself."

“我以前不理人家，这次可是要求到人家门上来，你去敲门吧。”
"Used to ignore her, huh? Well, today I'm the one asking a favor. Go knock."

苦荞上前敲了敲园门。
Kuqiao stepped up and rapped on the garden gate.

门从里面打开，探出一个丫鬟的脑袋：“是苦荞啊，听说你跟小姐回来了，本来不信，现在果真见到。”
The gate cracked open and a maid poked her head out. "Oh, Kuqiao! Heard you came back with Miss—didn't believe it till I saw you!"

苦荞说：“姐姐好，小姐好不容易回来一趟，过来拜见公子和少夫人。”
"Good day, Sister," Kuqiao said. "Miss finally made it home and came to visit Young Master and Young Madam."

丫鬟给里面的小厮说：“快去禀报大公子，小姐来访。”
The maid called over her shoulder to a page boy inside, "Run tell Eldest Young Master that Miss is here!"

丫鬟把门开大，行礼道：“小姐来得正好，大公子刚忙完公务回来，您跟苦荞进来，我带您过去。”
Swinging the gate wide, she curtsied. "Perfect timing, Miss—Eldest Young Master just got back from office duties. Come on in, I'll take you right to him."

千里寻用眼神向苦荞询问，苦荞领会，在她耳边悄悄说：“她叫月桂，是少夫人从陈家带来的丫鬟。”
Catching her mistress's questioning glance, Kuqiao leaned in and whispered, "Her name is Yuegui, the maid Young Madam brought from the Chen house."

千里寻点点头，二人跟着月桂朝里走。院子甚大，里面的房屋不少，看上去像一座府宅。千里寻感慨，同一个朱府，每个园子的风格甚不相同。
Qianli Xun nodded, and they followed Yuegui inside. The yard was vast and lined with pavilions, almost a manor of its own—every courtyard in Zhu Manor had its own distinct style.

走着走着，迎面来了一位公子，个头不低，长相周正不失帅气，笑起来的样子跟向夫人有五分相似，千里寻猜出他定是朱淑真的哥哥。
Walking along, they met a tall young gentleman approaching—clean-cut, handsome, his smile carrying half of Madam Xiang's warmth. Qianli Xun knew at once this was Zhu Shuzhen's brother.

朱景元见到妹妹，甚是喜悦：“我回府已见过娘，她告诉我你回到家中，正想换完衣裳，逗逗莱宝，再去看妹妹，没成想妹妹竟亲自登门，也省得我跑一趟。”
Zhu Jingyuan beamed at the sight of his sister. "Just saw Mother when I got back and heard you were home. I was about to change clothes, play with Laibao a bit, and come visit you—never expected you to walk right over and save me the trip!"

千里寻没有兄弟姐妹，穿越而来，平白无故多一位哥哥，她从心底感到满意，学着下人行礼的样子：“我半年多没回来，好不容易回来一趟，当然要看看哥哥，还得看我大侄子，也得和嫂嫂聊两句。”
Having grown up an only child, Qianli Xun loved gaining an older brother out of the blue. Copying the servants' bow, she said, "Been away over half a year—now that I'm home, of course I had to visit my brother, see my nephew, and chat with my sister-in-law."

朱景元已从母亲口中得知淑真重生失忆之事，亲眼见到，才知妹妹有多严重。她以前见他从不行礼，最多称呼声“哥哥”，再不会多说一个字。朱景元从小已习惯，他深知这位妹妹少言寡语，喜欢安静，心思全用在诗词上，从不愿跟人说些没用的废话，更别说与人聊天儿。今日，她一见面竟说要和嫂嫂聊两句，实在不一样得厉害。
Already briefed by Madam Xiang on Shuzhen's "rebirth" and amnesia, Zhu Jingyuan still gaped at seeing how drastic the change was. Before, she never bowed to him, barely uttered "Brother," and never wasted a syllable on small talk—quiet, buried in poetry, immune to chitchat. Today she walked up wanting to chat with her sister-in-law! Night and day.

朱景元还是上前扶住妹妹：“文心正在屋里逗莱宝，我带你去见她。”
Still, he reached out to steady her. "Wenxin is inside playing with Laibao. Come meet her."

千里寻对哥哥有说不出的喜欢，也许是因为她曾渴望有位哥哥，终于实现，一高兴，忘乎所以，直接用手搀住朱景元的手臂：“有个哥哥真好，以后我要在外面被人欺负，就有哥哥保护了。”
Warmth swelled in Qianli Xun—maybe she had always wished for an older brother. Carried away with delight, she linked her arm right through Zhu Jingyuan's. "Having a brother feels awesome! Now if anybody bullies me outside, my brother's got my back!"

向来只有千里寻欺负别人的份儿，但她毕竟是女孩子，从心里渴望被人保护，尤其是有亲哥哥的保护。
Usually Qianli Xun was the one knocking people flat, yet deep down she was still a woman who liked the idea of protection—especially from a big brother.

朱景元被很不一样的妹妹整懵，瞧见她额头的伤，应该是这伤搞得她重生后失忆：“没想到妹妹嫁人后变得跟以前大不相同。头上的伤还疼吗？”
Thrown off by this unrecognizable sister, Zhu Jingyuan spotted the wound on her forehead—that injury must have caused the rebirth and amnesia. "Marriage sure changed you, sister. Does your forehead still hurt?"

千里寻掺着朱景元，一边往里走一边说：“苦荞给我上过药，伤口不是很疼，可疼在心里，林觉家暴成性，我跟他实在过不下去，哥哥要替我做主。”
Clinging to his arm as they walked inside, Qianli Xun said, "Kuqiao put salve on it, so the cut barely hurts—what hurts is my heart! Lin Jue beats me nonstop. I can't live with him another day—Brother, you have to stand up for me!"

“娘已把你的情况告诉我，哥也很气愤，我这么个如花似玉的妹妹嫁给他，他还有什么不满意的？竟然出手打人！天理难容，哥肯定会为你出面，我们进去说。”
"Mother told me everything, and I'm furious too! Marrying a gem like my sister, what more could he want? Raising a fist to her defies Heaven! Count on me—I'll stand up for you. Let's talk inside."

二人说着走进屋子。
They stepped into the room together.

莱宝坐在用檀木做成的小床上，咿咿呀呀笑着，陈文心拿着拨浪鼓逗孩子，旁边站着伺候的丫鬟。
Little Laibao sat babbling and laughing on a small sandalwood crib while Chen Wenxin shook a pellet drum to amuse him, maids standing at attention beside her.

陈文心看到淑真进来，把拨浪鼓递给丫鬟，站起来迎上去：“妹妹真是稀客，怎么想着来我们这里？”
Seeing Shuzhen enter, Chen Wenxin handed the drum to a maid and rose to greet her. "Look what rare guest blew in—what brings you over to our side, Sister?"

千里寻放开朱景元，伸出手，苦荞赶紧把锦盒放到小姐手中。千里寻接过锦盒递给陈文心：“嫂嫂，这是给大侄子的见面礼，满月时跟爹赌气，没回来，今日既然回来就补上。”
Letting go of Zhu Jingyuan, Qianli Xun held out her hand; Kuqiao slipped the brocade box into it. Offering it to Chen Wenxin, Qianli Xun said, "Sister-in-law, a first-meeting gift for my nephew. I was sulking with Father during his full-month feast and missed it, so let me make it up today."

朱淑真第一次跟陈文心说这么多话，说话时还笑脸相迎，陈文心感到意外。她接过锦盒，打开一瞧，是枚金灿灿的锁子：“没想到妹妹成亲后，倒像变了个人，礼物不错，这就让你大侄子戴上。”
Never before had Zhu Shuzhen spoken this many words to her—let alone with a smile. Surprised, Chen Wenxin accepted the box and lifted the lid to see the gleaming gold lock. "Marriage really turned you into a new person! Lovely gift—let's put it right on your nephew."

月桂从陈文心手中接过锦盒，把金锁拿出来，给莱宝戴在脖子上。
Yuegui took the box from Chen Wenxin, lifted out the gold lock, and slipped it around Laibao's neck.

陈文心说：“妹妹坐吧。”
"Take a seat, Sister," Chen Wenxin said.

接着对旁边的丫鬟大声说：“杵着干什么？来客人了，还不赶紧上茶！”
Then she barked at a nearby maid: "What are you standing there like a post for? We have guests—go fetch tea!"

被训斥的丫鬟不敢说一句，忙去准备茶水。
Too scared to speak, the maid scurried off to prepare tea.

朱景元上前拉起陈文心：“别跟下人置气，来，坐下，妹妹好不容易来一趟，我们跟她好好聊聊。”
Zhu Jingyuan took Chen Wenxin's arm. "Don't snap at the servants. Come sit down—Shuzhen rarely visits, let's have a nice talk."

千里寻坐下，观察眼前这二位，看样子哥哥对嫂嫂不错，的确是个好男人。
Taking a seat, Qianli Xun observed the couple. Her brother treated his wife with genuine affection—a solid man.

陈文心与朱景元坐好后，瞅了一眼淑真：“妹妹平常都不大理我，今日突然过来，让我好生意外，我想除看你大侄子外，应该还有其他事吧？”
Once seated beside her husband, Chen Wenxin eyed Shuzhen. "You hardly ever look my way, Sister—dropping by out of the blue really surprises me. Aside from seeing Laibao, you must have something else on your mind?"

丫鬟进来把茶摆好，手拿盘子站在一旁伺候。
The maid returned, set the tea down, and stayed standing beside them with her tray.

陈文心气又不打一处来：“主人家说话，你个下人杵在那里干什么，还不赶紧出去！”
Chen Wenxin flared up again. "Masters are talking—why are you still loitering here? Get out!"

丫鬟心里着实委屈，屋里明明还有苦荞和月桂，以及另一名负责逗莱宝的丫鬟，为何她就不能在？再委屈也不能与主人顶嘴，只能灰溜溜地出去。
Stung—Kuqiao, Yuegui, and the maid tending Laibao were all still in the room, so why single her out?—the maid dared not talk back and slunk out.

千里寻这才说：“难道我就不能来看望哥哥和嫂嫂？以前是淑真不懂事，怠慢了嫂嫂。”
Qianli Xun spoke up smoothly. "Can't I just come visit my brother and sister-in-law? Shuzhen was immature before and neglected you."

为迅速切入正题，千里寻接着故意装道：“苦荞，伤口有些疼，再帮我上些药。”
To steer straight to the point, she put on an act: "Kuqiao, my cut is stinging—dab on some more ointment."

苦荞从怀里掏出小木盒，打开，当着众人的面用丝帕沾些，给小姐涂到伤口处。
Kuqiao pulled out the wooden box, opened it, and dabbed ointment onto her mistress's forehead in plain view.

陈文心自然注意到伤口：“妹妹是碰到哪里？伤得严重不严重？”
Chen Wenxin noticed at once. "Where did you bang your head, Sister? Is it bad?"

千里寻没有回答，她知道哥哥定会帮她说明。
Qianli Xun kept silent, letting her brother carry the ball.

果不其然，朱景元说：“我还没来得及告诉你，淑真自从嫁给林觉，经常被家暴，那伤正是林觉打的，也因为那伤，她失去以前的记忆，整个人变得很不一样，娘说是重生。”
Right on cue, Zhu Jingyuan spoke up. "Haven't had a chance to tell you—Lin Jue has been beating Shuzhen ever since she married him. He gave her that cut, and the blow wiped her memory and changed her whole personality. Mother says she was reborn."

“什么？重生？”陈文心感到诧异，很快镇定下来，“我倒觉得重生失忆后的妹妹比以前更接地气儿，看着让人舒服。重生就重生呗，失忆也没什么大不了，重要的是人还活着。不过，林觉竟然打朱府的小姐，实在过分，应该让公公好好教训那小子。”
"What? Reborn?" Surprise flickered across Chen Wenxin's face before she recovered fast. "If you ask me, reborn and memory-less Shuzhen is way more down-to-earth and pleasant to be around! Reborn is reborn, amnesia is no big deal—what matters is she's alive. Still, Lin Jue laying a hand on a daughter of Zhu Manor? Outrageous! Father-in-law ought to teach that punk a real lesson!"

千里寻听后，喜上心头，看来哥哥嫂嫂都站在她这边，趁机说：“嫂嫂，你我都是女子，知道女子嫁个好男人有多重要。我没嫂嫂幸运，能嫁给像哥哥这样的好男人，却嫁给林觉那样打女人的渣男，我与他实在过不下去，打算和离，和离之时，还请哥哥嫂嫂前去为我撑腰。”
Delighted that both brother and sister-in-law backed her, Qianli Xun seized the moment. "Sister-in-law, woman to woman, you know how much marrying a good man matters. I didn't have your luck landing a gem like my brother—I got stuck with a wife-beating scumbag like Lin Jue. I can't bear another day with him, so I'm pushing for mutual divorce. When the day comes, please stand behind me!"

千里寻说话间，陈文心看向丈夫，他虽没有多少本事，却胜在对她好，听她话，把她看得很重，比起很多男子不知强多少倍。她的脸上不由露出幸福的表情。
As she spoke, Chen Wenxin glanced at her husband. No towering genius, maybe, yet he cherished her, listened to her, and put her first—better than ten times the men out there. A warm glow lit her face.

“像林觉这种男人，的确不能再跟他过下去，和离倒是不错的选择，离开他妹妹依然能找到好婆家。”
"You really can't stay with a man like Lin Jue. Mutual divorce is the smart move—leave him, and you can still find a fine husband."

千里寻继续说：“嫂嫂和哥哥是否同意在我与林觉和离时帮我出面？”
Qianli Xun pressed on, "So you and Brother agree to appear on my behalf at the divorce meeting?"

陈文心回道：“那还用说？我同意。”
"Need you even ask? I agree," Chen Wenxin answered.

朱景元跟着说：“你嫂嫂同意，哥哥肯定同意。”
Zhu Jingyuan chimed right in: "If your sister-in-law agrees, I agree!"

千里寻站起来，上前抱住陈文心：“嫂嫂对我真好，有你这样的嫂嫂真是幸运。”
Jumping up, Qianli Xun threw her arms around Chen Wenxin. "You're the best, Sister-in-law! I'm so lucky to have you!"

陈文心说：“我还得感谢林觉。”
"Actually, I ought to thank Lin Jue," Chen Wenxin remarked.

千里寻放开手：“什么意思？”
Qianli Xun let go. "What do you mean?"

陈文心笑道：“他家暴妹妹是可恶，可要没有他打妹妹，妹妹也不会变成我喜欢的样子。以前的你清高冷漠，话不多，让人看着很有距离感，反而现在像个正常人。”
Chen Wenxin laughed. "Beating you is despicable, sure, yet without his beating, you never would've turned into someone I actually like! Before, you were frosty and aloof, barely spoke two words, kept everyone miles away—now you act like a real person!"

千里寻怨道：“嫂嫂这话说的，好像林觉打我还有功劳？”
Qianli Xun pouted. "Sister-in-law, you make it sound like Lin Jue deserves credit for hitting me!"

陈文心笑道：“好了，我不开玩笑，林觉就是你说的那种……”
"Alright, alright, just teasing! Lin Jue really is that word you used..."

“渣男。”
"Scumbag."

“对！渣男！”
"Right! A scumbag!"

两人正说着，门外传来银杏的声音：“公子，少夫人，老爷回来了，听说小姐在这里，让我过来请她过去，晚饭与老爷、夫人一起用。”
Mid-laugh, Yinxing's voice floated from outside the door: "Young Master, Young Madam—Master is home! Hearing Miss was here, he sent me to summon her to dine with Master and Madam."

朱景元说：“爹回来了，让你过去，你把林觉打你之事再好好给爹说说，哥相信，爹肯定会替你做主。”
Zhu Jingyuan encouraged her, "Father's back and calling for you. Tell him everything about Lin Jue beating you—I'm sure Father will back you up."

朱景元的话令千里寻感到踏实：“哥哥嫂嫂难道不一起过去用饭？”
Reassured by his words, Qianli Xun asked, "Aren't you and Sister-in-law coming to dinner too?"

朱景元看着失忆的妹妹，很是心疼，解释道：“爹只让你过去，没有叫我跟文心，我们不能随意过去打扰。”
Pitying his memory-less sister, Zhu Jingyuan explained, "Father only summoned you, not Wenxin and me. We can't intrude uninvited."

千里寻只能拜别哥嫂。
Qianli Xun had no choice but to take her leave.

走出屋外时，天已蒙蒙黑。一日即将过去，还有两日，只要再搞定朱淑真的父亲，一切万事大吉。千里寻带着希望，在银杏的带领下，借着她手中灯笼散发出的微弱光芒，在朱府继续穿梭。
Dusk had fallen by the time she stepped outside. One day gone, two left. Win over Zhu Shuzhen's father, and she was home free. Buoyed by hope, she followed Yinxing's dim lantern as they wound once more through the paths of Zhu Manor.
