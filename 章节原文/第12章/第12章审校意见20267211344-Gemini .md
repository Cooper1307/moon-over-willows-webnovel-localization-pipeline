以下是具体的审校意见与优化建议：

### 1. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Meeting the Brother and Sister-in-Law to Ask for Their Support*
*   **点评**：准确，但略显平淡（像是会议记录）。网文标题需要更有张力。
*   **建议**：**Chapter 12: Winning Over the Brother and Sister-in-law** （Winning over 有“拉拢、争取到对方支持”的意思，更符合女主有策略地拜访哥嫂的网文爽感）。

### 2. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P14 中式比例直译**：`struck a five-part resemblance to Madam Xiang.`
    *   **问题**：“五分相似”是典型的中文表达（满分十分）。英文里不说 five-part resemblance。
    *   **修改**：可以说 `bore a striking resemblance` 或者直接用 `looked remarkably like Madam Xiang when he smiled.`
*   **P16 称呼视角的转换**：`"I haven't been back... of course I must see my brother, see my eldest nephew, and chat with my sister-in-law."`
    *   **优化**：在直接引语中，一直用 my brother / my sister-in-law 显得有些生分，直接称呼 `you` 或者 `you two` 会更亲切。
    *   **修改**：`"...of course I had to come see you, Brother, check on my eldest nephew, and chat with my sister-in-law."`
*   **P20 致命的中式英语（高频错点）**：`Brother, you must make decisions for me.`
    *   **问题**：“替我做主”在古装剧中极常出现，绝不能直译为 make decisions for me（这听起来像是女主没有独立思考能力，让哥哥替她安排人生）。“做主”在这里的意思是“替我出头、为我撑腰”。
    *   **修改**：**"Brother, you have to stand up for me!"** 或者 **"You have to back me up!"**
*   **P21 语气的地道转换**：`I gave such a beautiful sister in marriage to him—what more could he want?`
    *   **优化**：原译有点像“我交易了一个妹妹给他”。
    *   **修改**：`"He got my beautiful, flower-like sister as his wife—what more could he possibly want?"` （体现哥哥的愤慨与护短）。
*   **P26 节日/习俗的统一**：`full moon celebration`
    *   **修改**：上一章我们定下了用 **one-month milestone**，这里需保持一致，避免老外想到“月圆之夜”。
*   **P43 女主的自称**：`"It's just that Shuzhen was thoughtless before and neglected you..."`
    *   **问题**：中国古人喜欢在对话中用自己的名字（或贱称）自称，但英文中第三人称自称非常违和。
    *   **修改**：翻译意图即可。`"It's just that I was thoughtless before and neglected you, Sister-in-law."`
*   **P48 态度的表达**：`She's more pleasant to look at.`
    *   **问题**：“看着让人舒服”这里不是指外貌好看（look at），而是指相处起来很舒服。
    *   **修改**：`She's much more pleasant to be around.`

### 3. 句子是否过长？标点与节奏是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **P27 情绪的顿挫**：`What are you standing there for? We have guests. Hurry up and serve tea!`
    *   **优化**：陈文心是个泼辣的大小姐，训斥下人时可以更狠一点。
    *   **修改**：`"What are you standing there like a log for? We have guests! Hurry up and serve the tea!"`
*   **P54 反问句的张力**：`"No need to ask? I agree."`
    *   **优化**：“那还用说？”是一句强烈的肯定反问。
    *   **修改**：`"Is that even a question? Of course I agree."`

**Chapter 12: Winning Over the Brother and Sister-in-Law**

After walking for a while, the path continued to wind through quiet scenery. But unlike the garden where Zhu Shuzhen lived—which felt like seclusion within seclusion—her brother and sister-in-law’s residence was delightfully open and spacious. Upon arriving, Qianli Xun's eyes brightened as the expansive view unfolded before her.
Zhu Shuzhen's brother was named Zhu Jingyuan. His literary talent couldn't compare to his sister's, but he could pen a decent essay. As the sole male heir of the family, their father, Zhu Xiyan, had used his connections to secure him a civil service position in Lin'an. He was married to Chen Wenxin, the daughter of Lin'an's wealthiest family. The couple had a very loving relationship. When Zhu Shuzhen was married, Chen Wenxin was already eight months pregnant, and she had since given birth to a son who was now just over six months old.
Reaching the outer gate of Zhu Jingyuan's courtyard, Qianli Xun stopped. "Kuqiao, are my brother and sister-in-law easy to get along with?"
"Though the eldest young master's talent doesn't match yours, Miss, he is an honest man and treats you well," Kuqiao replied. "As for the young mistress... I've heard her tongue can be a bit sharp, but she has a decent heart."
"Heard? You haven't actually interacted with her?"
"I've accompanied you to see her a few times, Miss, but you never paid her much attention. She never had a chance to really speak with you, so I haven't seen her temper firsthand."
"I might have ignored her before, but this time I'm the one who has to come begging at her door," Qianli Xun sighed. "Go knock."
Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked on the garden gate.
The door opened from the inside, and a maid peeked her head out. "Oh, it's Kuqiao! I heard you came back with the Miss, but I didn't believe it until now."
"Hello, Sister," Kuqiao greeted. "The Miss has finally come back for a visit and is here to pay her respects to the young master and young mistress."
The maid turned to a pageboy inside. "Quick, go tell the eldest young master that the Miss is here to visit!"
Opening the gate wider, the maid bowed. "You've come at the perfect time, Miss. The eldest young master just returned from his official duties. Please come in. I'll show you the way."
Qianli Xun shot Kuqiao a questioning look. Kuqiao understood immediately and whispered in her ear, "Her name is Yuegui. She's the dowry maid the young mistress brought with her from the Chen family."
Qianli Xun nodded, and the two followed Yuegui inside. The courtyard was massive, with numerous rooms built like a self-contained manor. Qianli Xun marveled that within the very same Zhu estate, each garden had such a drastically different architectural style.
As they walked, a young man approached them. He was quite tall, with handsome and proper features. When he smiled, he bore a striking resemblance to Madam Xiang. Qianli Xun guessed this must be Zhu Shuzhen's brother.
Zhu Jingyuan was overjoyed to see his sister. "I already saw Mother when I got back, and she told me you were home! I was just about to change my clothes and play with Laibao for a bit before going to see you. I didn't expect you to come in person—it saves me a trip."
In her previous life, Qianli Xun had no siblings. Having transmigrated and suddenly gained a brother out of nowhere, she felt genuinely delighted. Mimicking the way servants curtsied, she smiled, "I haven't been back in over half a year! Now that I'm finally here, of course I had to come see you, Brother, check on my eldest nephew, and chat with my sister-in-law."
Zhu Jingyuan had already learned from their mother about Shuzhen's "rebirth" and memory loss. But seeing it with his own eyes, he realized just how drastically she had changed. Before, she would never bow to him. At most, she would stiffly call out "Brother" and not utter a single word more. Zhu Jingyuan was used to it; he knew his sister loved solitude, poured all her soul into her poetry, and despised wasting breath on trivial talk—let alone casually "chatting" with people. Yet today, she was actively asking to chat with Wenxin. It was a staggering difference.
Still, Zhu Jingyuan stepped forward to support his sister. "Wenxin is inside playing with Laibao. Come, I'll take you to her."
Qianli Xun felt an inexplicable fondness for her new brother. Perhaps because she had always longed for an older brother, and now her wish had come true. In her excitement, she forgot ancient protocols and immediately latched onto Zhu Jingyuan's arm. "It's so good to have an older brother. If anyone bullies me out there from now on, I have a brother to protect me!"
In her past, Qianli Xun was always the one doing the bullying. But deep down, she was still a girl who longed to be protected, especially by her own family.
Zhu Jingyuan was completely bewildered by this affectionately clingy sister. Noticing the bruise on her forehead, he assumed the injury was the sole cause of her memory loss and personality shift. "I didn't expect you to change this much after marriage. Does your head still hurt?"
Holding onto Zhu Jingyuan as they walked inside, Qianli Xun said, "Kuqiao has already applied medicine, so the wound doesn't hurt much. But it hurts in my heart. Lin Jue is completely prone to domestic violence. I really can't continue living with him. Brother, you have to stand up for me!"
"Mother already told me everything. I am absolutely furious," Zhu Jingyuan fumed. "He got my beautiful, flower-like sister as his wife—what more could he possibly want? To actually lay hands on you! It's an outrage against heaven and earth. Don't worry, Brother will definitely back you up. Let's go inside and talk."

Entering the room, they found Laibao sitting in a small sandalwood crib, babbling and giggling. Chen Wenxin was holding a pellet drum, entertaining the baby, while a maid stood quietly by to attend.
Seeing Shuzhen enter, Chen Wenxin handed the drum to the maid and stood up to welcome her. "Sister-in-law! What a rare guest. What brings you to our courtyard?"
Qianli Xun let go of Zhu Jingyuan and extended her hand. Kuqiao quickly placed the brocade box into it. Qianli Xun offered the box to Chen Wenxin. "Sister-in-law, this is a greeting gift for my eldest nephew. I was throwing a tantrum at Father during the baby's one-month milestone and didn't come back. Now that I'm here, I'm making up for it."
This was the very first time Zhu Shuzhen had spoken so many words to Chen Wenxin, and she was doing it with a warm smile. Chen Wenxin was taken aback. She took the box, opened it, and saw a gleaming gold lock. "I didn't expect you to change so much after marriage. It's a beautiful gift. I'll have your nephew wear it right away."
Yuegui took the box, lifted the gold lock, and gently fastened it around little Laibao's neck.
"Please, sit down, Sister-in-law," Chen Wenxin said. She then shot a sharp glare at the maid beside her, her voice rising sharply. "What are you standing there like a log for? We have guests! Hurry up and serve the tea!"
The scolded maid didn't dare utter a peep and scurried off to prepare the tea.
Zhu Jingyuan gently pulled Chen Wenxin's arm. "Don't take your temper out on the servants. Come, sit down. Sister-in-law has finally come for a visit, let's have a good chat with her."
Taking a seat, Qianli Xun observed the couple. It seemed her brother truly treated his wife well—he was indeed a good man.
Once seated, Chen Wenxin eyed Shuzhen thoughtfully. "You usually don't pay me any mind. Dropping by so suddenly today is quite the surprise. I'm guessing that besides seeing your nephew, you have other business?"
Just then, the maid returned, arranged the teacups, and stepped to the side, holding the tray to attend them.
Chen Wenxin’s temper flared again. "We masters are talking. Why are you still standing there like a statue? Get out!"
The maid felt incredibly wronged. Kuqiao, Yuegui, and the maid watching Laibao were all allowed to stay in the room—why couldn't she? But no matter how unfair it felt, a servant couldn't talk back to her master. She tucked her head down and slunk out of the room.
Only then did Qianli Xun speak. "Can't I just come visit my brother and sister-in-law? It's just that I was thoughtless before and neglected you, Sister-in-law."
Wanting to cut straight to the chase, Qianli Xun deliberately put on an act. "Kuqiao, my wound stings a bit. Help me apply some more medicine."
Kuqiao pulled a small wooden box from her sleeve, opened it, and right in front of everyone, used a silk cloth to dab medicine onto her miss's forehead.
Chen Wenxin's eyes immediately locked onto the injury. "Where did you hit your head, Sister-in-law? Is it serious?"
Qianli Xun remained silent. She knew her brother would do the explaining for her.
Sure enough, Zhu Jingyuan sighed. "I haven't had the chance to tell you yet. Ever since Shuzhen married Lin Jue, she has been a frequent victim of his violence. That wound was caused by Lin Jue. Because of that hit, she lost all her previous memories and her entire personality shifted. Mother says it's a rebirth."
"What? Rebirth?" Chen Wenxin was shocked but quickly regained her composure. "Well, I actually think this amnesiac, reborn version of you is much more down-to-earth. You're much more pleasant to be around. Rebirth is rebirth, and losing some memories is no big deal—what matters is that you're alive. However... for Lin Jue to actually strike a daughter of the Zhu family is going way too far! Father-in-law needs to teach that brat a severe lesson."
Hearing this, joy bloomed in Qianli Xun's heart. It seemed both her brother and sister-in-law were firmly on her side. Seizing the moment, she said, "Sister-in-law, we are both women. We know how crucial it is to marry a good man. I wasn't as lucky as you to marry a wonderful man like my brother. Instead, I married a *scumbag* like Lin Jue who beats women. I truly cannot live with him anymore. I plan to seek an amicable divorce. When the time comes to negotiate, I need you and Brother to stand behind me and back me up."
*(TN: "Scumbag" [渣男] is modern Chinese slang for a terrible, abusive, or unfaithful partner. As a modern transmigrator, Qianli Xun casually uses this anachronistic term, and her ancient sister-in-law seamlessly picks it up.)*
As Qianli Xun spoke, Chen Wenxin looked fondly at her husband. Though Zhu Jingyuan wasn't a literary genius or a high-ranking official, his true strength lay in how well he treated her. He listened to her and valued her highly—making him a thousand times better than most men of their era. A look of genuine happiness graced her face.
"A man like Lin Jue... you really can't stay with him," Chen Wenxin agreed firmly. "A mutual divorce is a good choice. Once you leave him, you can still find a decent family to marry into."
"So, do you and Brother agree to step forward and support me when I confront the Lin family?" Qianli Xun pressed.
"Is that even a question? Of course I agree," Chen Wenxin declared.
"If your sister-in-law agrees, Brother definitely agrees," Zhu Jingyuan echoed loyally.
Overjoyed, Qianli Xun stood up and threw her arms around Chen Wenxin. "You are so good to me! I'm so incredibly lucky to have a sister-in-law like you."
"Actually," Chen Wenxin smirked, "I should be thanking Lin Jue."
Qianli Xun pulled back, confused. "What do you mean?"
Chen Wenxin laughed. "Him beating you is despicable, yes. But if he hadn't hit you, you wouldn't have turned into a version of yourself that I actually like! Before, you were aloof, arrogant, and icy. You barely spoke and always kept everyone at a distance. Now, you actually act like a normal human being."
"Sister-in-law," Qianli Xun pouted playfully, "the way you say that makes it sound like Lin Jue deserves credit for hitting me!"
"Alright, alright, I'm not joking anymore," Chen Wenxin chuckled. "Lin Jue is exactly the kind of... what did you call it?"
"*Scumbag*."
"Right! A *scumbag*!"

Just as the two women were bonding, Yinxing's voice called out from beyond the door. "Young Master, Young Mistress! The Master has returned. He heard the Miss was here and sent me to invite her over to have dinner with him and the Madam."
Zhu Jingyuan nodded seriously. "Father is back and wants to see you. Make sure you explain exactly how Lin Jue hit you. I know Father will definitely stand up for you."
Her brother's confidence made Qianli Xun feel grounded. "Aren't you two coming over for dinner as well?"
Looking at his amnesiac sister with a mix of pity and affection, Zhu Jingyuan gently explained, "Father only asked for you, not me and Wenxin. We can't just invite ourselves over and disturb him."
Having no other choice, Qianli Xun bid her brother and sister-in-law farewell.
When she stepped out of the courtyard, the sky was already growing dark. One day was nearly gone. She only had two days left. As long as she could win over Zhu Shuzhen's formidable father, everything would fall perfectly into place. 
Filled with newfound hope, Qianli Xun followed Yinxing. Guided only by the faint, flickering light of the maid's lantern, she continued to navigate the depths of the sprawling Zhu estate.
