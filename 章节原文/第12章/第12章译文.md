# Chapter 12: Meeting the Brother and Sister-in-Law to Ask for Their Support

After walking for a while, the path still wound through secluded spots, but the garden where Zhu Shuzhen lived felt like seclusion within seclusion, while her brother and sister-in-law's residence was open and spacious. When they arrived, Qianli Xun's eyes brightened as the view opened up before her.

Zhu Shuzhen's brother was named Zhu Jingyuan. His literary talent didn't match his sister's, but he could write essays. As the son of the family, Zhu Xiyan used his connections to secure him a civil position in Lin'an. He had also married Chen Wenxin, daughter of the Chen family, the richest family in Lin'an. The couple was quite harmonious. When Zhu Shuzhen married, Chen Wenxin was already eight months pregnant. She later gave birth to a son, who was now over six months old.

When Qianli Xun reached the outer gate of Zhu Jingyuan's garden, she stopped: "Kuqiao, are my brother and sister-in-law good people?"

Kuqiao replied, "Though the eldest young master's talent doesn't match yours, Miss, he's honest and kind to you. As for the young mistress, I've heard her tongue is a bit sharp, but as a person, she's alright."

"Heard? You haven't met her?"

"I've met her a few times when accompanying you, Miss, but you didn't pay her much attention, and she never had a chance to talk to you, so I haven't seen it firsthand."

"I ignored her before, but this time I'm the one who has to come begging at her door. Go knock."

Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked on the garden gate.

The door opened from inside, and a maid's head peeked out: "It's Kuqiao. I heard you came back with the miss, but I didn't believe it. Now I see it's true."

Kuqiao said, "Hello, sister. The miss has finally come back for a visit and is here to pay respects to the young master and young mistress."

The maid told the page boy inside, "Quick, go inform the eldest young master that the miss has come to visit."

The maid opened the gate wider and bowed: "You've come at the perfect time, Miss. The eldest young master just returned from handling official business. Please come in with Kuqiao, and I'll show you the way."

Qianli Xun glanced at Kuqiao with a questioning look. Kuqiao understood and whispered in her ear, "Her name is Yuegui. She's the maid the young mistress brought from the Chen family."

Qianli Xun nodded, and the two followed Yuegui inside. The courtyard was quite large, with many rooms resembling a manor. Qianli Xun marveled that within the same Zhu residence, each garden had a very different style.

As they walked, a young master approached. He was quite tall, with handsome and proper features. His smile struck a five-part resemblance to Madam Xiang. Qianli Xun guessed he must be Zhu Shuzhen's brother.

Zhu Jingyuan was overjoyed to see his sister: "I've already returned to the manor and met with Mother. She told me you're back home. I was just thinking of changing clothes, playing with Laibao for a bit, then going to see you. I didn't expect you to come in person, which saves me a trip."

Qianli Xun had no siblings. After transmigrating, she suddenly gained a brother. She felt genuinely pleased and mimicked the way servants bowed: "I haven't been back for over half a year. Now that I'm finally here, of course I must see my brother, see my eldest nephew, and chat with my sister-in-law."

Zhu Jingyuan had already learned from his mother about Shuzhen's rebirth and memory loss, but seeing it in person, he realized just how serious it was. Before, she never bowed when seeing him—at most, she'd call out "brother" and not say another word. Zhu Jingyuan had been used to this since childhood. He knew this sister was quiet, loved solitude, and focused all her energy on poetry. She never wasted words on trivial talk, let alone chatted with people. Today, she was saying she wanted to chat with Wenxin—it was completely different.

Zhu Jingyuan stepped forward anyway to support his sister: "Wenxin is inside playing with Laibao. I'll take you to see her."

Qianli Xun felt an inexplicable fondness for her brother. Perhaps because she had always longed for a brother, and now her wish had come true. In her excitement, she forgot herself and immediately took Zhu Jingyuan's arm: "It's so good to have a brother. If I get bullied outside from now on, I'll have a brother to protect me."

In the past, Qianli Xun was always the one bullying others. But she was, after all, a girl who deep down longed to be protected, especially by her own brother.

Zhu Jingyuan was bewildered by his very different sister. Seeing the injury on her forehead, he figured it must have caused her memory loss after rebirth: "I didn't expect you to change so much after marriage. Does your head still hurt?"

Holding onto Zhu Jingyuan, Qianli Xun walked inside while saying, "Kuqiao has already applied medicine for me. The wound doesn't hurt much, but it hurts in my heart. Lin Jue is prone to domestic violence. I really can't continue living with him. Brother, you must make decisions for me."

"Mother has already told me about your situation. Brother is also very angry. I gave such a beautiful sister in marriage to him—what more could he want? To actually lay hands on you! It's against all reason. Brother will definitely stand up for you. Let's go inside and talk."

As they spoke, the two walked into the house.

Laibao sat in a small sandalwood crib, babbling and laughing. Chen Wenxin held a pellet drum, entertaining the child, while a maid stood by to attend.

Seeing Shuzhen enter, Chen Wenxin handed the pellet drum to the maid and stood up to welcome her: "Sister-in-law, what a rare guest! What brings you to our place?"

Qianli Xun let go of Zhu Jingyuan and extended her hand. Kuqiao quickly placed the brocade box in her miss's hand. Qianli Xun took the box and handed it to Chen Wenxin: "Sister-in-law, this is a greeting gift for my eldest nephew. I was angry with Father during his full moon celebration and didn't come back. Now that I'm here, I'm making up for it."

This was the first time Zhu Shuzhen had said so much to Chen Wenxin, and she was smiling while speaking. Chen Wenxin was surprised. She took the brocade box, opened it, and saw a golden lock: "I didn't expect you to change so much after marriage. Nice gift. I'll have your eldest nephew wear it right away."

Yuegui took the brocade box from Chen Wenxin, took out the gold lock, and put it around Laibao's neck.

Chen Wenxin said, "Please sit, sister-in-law."

Then she loudly snapped at the maid beside her: "What are you standing there for? We have guests. Hurry up and serve tea!"

The scolded maid didn't dare say a word and hurried to prepare the tea.

Zhu Jingyuan stepped forward and pulled Chen Wenxin: "Don't get angry with the servants. Come, sit down. Sister-in-law has finally come for a visit. Let's have a good chat with her."

Qianli Xun sat down and observed the two before her. It seemed her brother treated his wife well—he was indeed a good man.

After Chen Wenxin and Zhu Jingyuan sat down, she glanced at Shuzhen: "You usually don't pay much attention to me. Suddenly coming today really surprises me. I think besides seeing your eldest nephew, there must be something else?"

The maid came in, arranged the tea, and stood to the side holding the tray to attend.

Chen Wenxin was angry again: "The masters are talking. What are you doing standing there, servant? Hurry up and get out!"

The maid felt truly wronged. There were clearly Kuqiao, Yuegui, and another maid responsible for entertaining Laibao in the room. Why couldn't she stay? No matter how wronged she felt, she couldn't talk back to her masters and could only slink out.

Only then did Qianli Xun say, "Can't I just come visit my brother and sister-in-law? It's just that Shuzhen was thoughtless before and neglected you, sister-in-law."

To quickly get to the main point, Qianli Xun deliberately pretended: "Kuqiao, my wound hurts a bit. Help me apply some more medicine."

Kuqiao took out a small wooden box from her sleeve, opened it, and in front of everyone, used a silk cloth to dab some medicine and apply it to her miss's wound.

Chen Wenxin naturally noticed the wound: "Where did you get hurt, sister-in-law? Is it serious?"

Qianli Xun didn't answer. She knew her brother would explain for her.

Sure enough, Zhu Jingyuan said, "I haven't had the chance to tell you. Ever since Shuzhen married Lin Jue, she's been frequently beaten by him. That wound was caused by Lin Jue. Because of that injury, she lost her previous memories and became completely different. Mother says it's rebirth."

"What? Rebirth?" Chen Wenxin was surprised but quickly calmed down. "I actually think the sister-in-law after rebirth and memory loss is more down-to-earth than before. She's more pleasant to look at. Rebirth is rebirth, and memory loss isn't a big deal. What matters is that you're still alive. But for Lin Jue to actually hit the miss from the Zhu residence—that's really going too far. Father-in-law should give that boy a good lesson."

Hearing this, Qianli Xun was delighted. It seemed both her brother and sister-in-law were on her side. She took the opportunity to say, "Sister-in-law, we're both women. We know how important it is for a woman to marry a good man. I'm not as lucky as you, who married a good man like my brother. Instead, I married a *scumbag* like Lin Jue who beats women. I really can't continue living with him. I plan to get a mutual divorce. When the time comes, I hope brother and sister-in-law will come forward to support me."

[TN: "Scumbag" (渣男) is a modern Chinese slang term for a man who is unfaithful, abusive, or generally terrible to his partner. Qianli Xun, as a modern person transmigrated to ancient times, uses this anachronistic term, which her sister-in-law quickly picks up.]

As Qianli Xun spoke, Chen Wenxin looked at her husband. Though he didn't have much talent, he excelled at treating her well, listening to her, and valuing her highly—far better than many men. Her face showed a happy expression.

"A man like Lin Jue—you really can't continue living with him. A mutual divorce is a good choice. After leaving him, sister-in-law can still find a good family to marry into."

Qianli Xun continued, "Do sister-in-law and brother agree to come forward and help me when I get a mutual divorce from Lin Jue?"

Chen Wenxin replied, "No need to ask? I agree."

Zhu Jingyuan followed, "If your sister-in-law agrees, brother definitely agrees."

Qianli Xun stood up, went forward, and hugged Chen Wenxin: "Sister-in-law is so good to me. I'm so lucky to have a sister-in-law like you."

Chen Wenxin said, "I actually have to thank Lin Jue."

Qianli Xun let go: "What do you mean?"

Chen Wenxin smiled: "Him beating you is despicable, but if he hadn't hit you, you wouldn't have become the version I like. Before, you were aloof and cold, didn't talk much, and gave people a sense of distance. Now you're more like a normal person."

Qianli Xun complained, "Sister-in-law, the way you say it makes it sound like Lin Jue hitting me was a good thing?"

Chen Wenxin smiled: "Alright, I'm not joking. Lin Jue is the kind of... you said it..."

"*Scumbag*."

"Yes! *Scumbag*!"

As the two were talking, Yinxing's voice came from outside the door: "Young master, young mistress, the master has returned. He heard the miss is here and sent me to invite her over for dinner with the master and madam."

Zhu Jingyuan said, "Father has returned and wants you to go over. Tell Father about Lin Jue hitting you. Brother believes Father will definitely stand up for you."

Zhu Jingyuan's words made Qianli Xun feel reassured: "Aren't brother and sister-in-law coming over for dinner together?"

Zhu Jingyuan looked at his amnesiac sister with heartache and explained, "Father only asked you to come over, not me and Wenxin. We can't just go over and disturb him."

Qianli Xun could only bid farewell to her brother and sister-in-law.

When she walked out of the house, the sky was already dim. The day was about to end. There were still two days left. As long as she could win over Zhu Shuzhen's father, everything would be fine. Filled with hope, Qianli Xun followed Yinxing, guided by the faint light from Yinxing's lantern, continuing to navigate through the Zhu residence.
