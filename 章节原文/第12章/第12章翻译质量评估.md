## 段落 1

**原文**: 走了一阵，依然是曲径通幽，不同的是朱淑真住的园子像是幽中之幽，她哥嫂住的地方则是开阔之地。到达时，千里寻只觉眼前一亮，视野也随之变得开阔起来。

**译文**: After walking for a while, the path still wound through secluded spots, but the garden where Zhu Shuzhen lived felt like seclusion within seclusion, while her brother and sister-in-law's residence was open and spacious. When they arrived, Qianli Xun felt her eyes brighten, her view expanding accordingly.

### 1) 翻译合理性评估
- ✅ **"曲径通幽"**: "wound through secluded spots" 准确传达意境
- ✅ **"幽中之幽"**: "seclusion within seclusion" 准确传达层次感
- ✅ **"开阔之地"**: "open and spacious" 准确
- ✅ **"眼前一亮"**: "eyes brighten" 准确传达视觉冲击

### 2) 翻译缺陷识别
- "felt her eyes brighten" 略显生硬，"her eyes brightened" 更自然
- "her view expanding accordingly" 可考虑 "the view opening up before her" 更生动

### 3) 优化方向建议
```
原译: Qianli Xun felt her eyes brighten, her view expanding accordingly.
建议: Qianli Xun's eyes brightened as the view opened up before her.
理由: 更自然流畅，避免 "felt" 的生硬感
```
