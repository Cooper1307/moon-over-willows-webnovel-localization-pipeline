第238章 夸来夸去夸姑爷
## Chapter 238: Everyone Sings the Groom's Praises

陈浩宇先背《论语·学而》，本以为他只是背背前十篇而已，没想到把整个学而篇全部背下来。
学而篇内容并不多，可对于不熟悉《论语》的千里寻来讲，仍需翻开书对照陈浩宇背诵的内容。对方不打磕绊，流利背出，一个字不差，令千里寻佩服。
等陈浩宇背完，骄傲地看着她：“怎么样？我厉害吧，超额完成任务。”
千里寻放下手中的《论语》：“有什么好骄傲的？说不定你小时候上私塾，早都背得滚瓜烂熟。”
“本公子小时候是上过私塾，可那时心思根本没用在书上，《论语》的书名我是知道，里面的内容一点儿没记在心里，不信你可以问东哥，他什么都清楚。”
陈浩宇说完，准备找椅子坐下。今日表现这么好，坐下说话应该没什么关系。屁股刚要挨到椅子，被朱淑真的声音给阻止。
“书还没背完不准坐！老老实实站着！”
陈浩宇继续站好着：“能不能对自己的夫君别这么凶？温柔些会更好。”
千里寻起身，走到陈浩宇跟前，绕着他走了两圈，仔细研究，心想：“这家伙还是有优点，如果能把心思用在正经地方，说不定也是个不错的人。”
朱淑真离得近，她身上的香气扑鼻而来，陈浩宇甚是陶醉。头一次被她用研究的目光注视，既意外，又欣喜，难道她已喜欢上自己？
“怎么样？我是不是很不错？你嫁给我算是捡到宝了。”
这家伙毫无道理的自信也不知道是遗传谁？陈家的长辈也没有这样儿的。
“少废话！赶紧背诗！”
提到诗，陈浩宇先解释一番：“本公子向来不喜欢诗词，只完成要求的数量。”
千里寻并未理会，站在一旁听他背诵。
陈浩宇依然流利地背出《山居秋暝》《渭川田家》等十首诗。
千里寻认为除了对方记忆力尚可外，肯定是背的书太过简单，不如给他增加难度。
等陈浩宇背完后，她很不屑地说：“看来我列出的书太简单，不如你以后就背《四库全书》。”
陈浩宇纳闷道：“《四库全书》？从没听说过？”
这一问，千里寻立刻醒悟过来，她从未来穿越而来，有时会把历史搞混，只想一门心思找本难背的书，脑子里蹦出《四库全书》四个字，便脱口而出，要知道那可是清代的书。
不小心说错，坚决不能承认：“你整日吊儿郎当不干正事又能知道几本书？没听过很正常。算了，老娘也不为难你，那就背《史记》。”
《史记》应该是不错的选择，汉代就有，宋朝人肯定知道。重要的是里面的文章很长，够陈浩宇背的，不难死他才怪。
果不其然，陈浩宇面露难色：“你已吩咐人给我书桌上放了一摞书，够背的，为何重新选书？再说《史记》篇幅太长，这不是为难我吗？”
陈浩宇越为难，千里寻越高兴：“既然老娘有管教你的权利，背什么书，我说了算，临时改变主意难道不可以？”
“好吧，你说什么就是什么，但你必须答应我一个条件。”
陈浩宇临时提条件，千里寻气不打一处来，怒目圆睁，盯着陈浩宇：“你有什么资格提条件？去年散播谣言，害得我被全城人臭骂，叫天天不应，叫地地不灵，老娘找谁提条件？现在让你背几本书，你就有意见，告诉你，不管提什么条件，老娘都不会答应！”
想起散播流言一事，陈浩宇也渐渐生起悔恨之意：“好吧好吧，我不提任何条件，你想怎么折磨我都可以，我忍着，只要能让你消气，就是不知道你这气什么时候能消？”
千里寻继续怒目圆睁：“告诉你！我的气一辈子都不会消！”
陈浩宇竟然露出笑容：“这么久啊？岂不是你要跟我一辈子？嘿嘿，我觉得自己比乔文仙和仪王幸福许多，嘿嘿……”
千里寻的怒气就像一粒小石子被扔进湖水中，既没有改变湖面，一腔怒气还深陷在湖水里找不到出路。她不再盯着陈浩宇，担心自己盯的时间过长，会放弃报复的想法。
她转过身，背对着陈浩宇：“少来这一套，别以为我识不破你的真实面目？你只不过是想换一种方式对付我，老娘偏不会让你如意。”
“随你怎么理解，我该去打扫院子，否则怎么给下人们赚银子花？再说，我现在更不愿惹你不高兴，嘿嘿……”
陈浩宇笑着刚要出素心草堂，见红豆和阿美端着饭食进来，才知已是午饭时间，要求道：“朱淑真，看在我今日表现好的份上，能不能让我留下来跟你一起用饭？”
千里寻迅速转过身：“想得美！老娘哪怕跟街上的乞丐一起用饭，也不会跟你在一张桌子上吃饭。”
陈浩宇并未被伤到，继续笑着说：“没关系，以后有的是机会，反正你打算跟我一辈子，我相信总有一日，我们会在同一张桌上吃饭。”
千里寻本想继续怼回去，怎奈对方已出去。
红豆与阿美将饭食在桌上放好，互相对视后，红豆先说话：“小姐，饭食已摆好，请您慢用，您边用饭我边说说姑爷今日的表现。”
Chen Haoyu started reciting the opening "Xue Er" chapter of the *Analects*. Qianli Xun expected ten short passages at best—instead he rattled off the entire chapter from start to finish. Unfamiliar with the text herself, she had to flip the pages line by line to check him: smooth, crisp, not a single character missed. Genuine respect flickered inside her.

千里寻并未当回事，坐到饭桌前准备用饭。
Finishing, he puffed out his chest. "Well? Impressive, huh? Exceeded quota!"

红豆继续说：“今日姑爷与下人讲话又知分寸又懂礼，他背书时也认真，没有打瞌睡。”
Qianli Xun set the *Analects* down coolly. "Nothing to brag about. Probably hammered it into your skull at private school as a kid."

阿美接着说：“我今日也没见姑爷踢青瓜，他好像真的变好。”
"Went to school, sure, never opened a page! Knew the title *Analects*, didn't know a single line inside—ask Brother Dong if you don't believe me!" Reaching for a chair—figuring stellar work earned him a seat—he froze an inch above the cushion at her bark:

千里寻还未体会出两个丫鬟话中的滋味，叮嘱道：“刚分到他挣的几个钱，你们两个就被收买？千万不要被表象迷惑，他今日表现好，不等于以后日日表现好，路遥知马力，日久见人心，且耐心等待。下去吧，这里有苦荞伺候。”
"Recitation isn't over—stand straight!"

两个丫鬟不再说什么，给苦荞使使眼色，退了出去。
He snapped upright. "Have to bark so fierce at your own husband? A softer tone wouldn't kill you."

苦荞将筷子给小姐放好，又给碗里盛好汤。一边观察小姐用饭，一边思考怎么夸姑爷不显得有意为之。
Rising, Qianli Xun paced two slow circles around him, sizing him up: Guy actually has real brains—put them to decent use and he might amount to something.

终于想到一个话题，小心说：“小姐，这两日我都没见着韦嬷嬷。”
Standing so close, her faint fragrance drifted over him, making him lightheaded; being studied like this sent hope surging. "So? Decent catch, right? Hit the jackpot marrying me."

千里寻一边夹菜一边说：“她忙的都是外面的事，早出晚归的，别说你没见到，我也没见着。”
Where did this bottomless ego come from? Nobody else in the Chen family had it! "Less bragging, more poetry!"

苦荞假装叹道：“唉，自古以来都是男主外，女主内，小姐的身边若有一个男主人，也不必韦嬷嬷一介女流之辈总是往外面跑。”
"Warning upfront—never liked poetry, so I only memorized the exact quota."

千里寻放下筷子：“苦荞，亏你跟我这么久，什么时候思想如此狭隘？女流之辈怎么了？照样撑起半边天，不比男子差。韦嬷嬷的智慧有几人能比？你家小姐我也是江南女词人。再瞧瞧陈浩宇，长了一副男儿的模样，草包一个，不是欺负姑娘家，就是往赌坊青楼跑，身边净跟些什么样的人？世上若都是他这样的男子，还不如多些女流之辈。”
Saying nothing, she listened as he reeled off ten Wang Wei poems—"Autumn Dusk in the Mountains," "Countryside at Weichuan," and eight more—flawlessly.

小姐的话语中流露出对姑爷的不满，好在主动提到姑爷，倒让苦荞接下来说得话顺理成章。
Figuring the material was just too easy, Qianli Xun scoffed, "Clearly too simple. Starting tomorrow, memorize the *Complete Library of the Four Treasuries*!"

“其实姑爷也没那么差，他今日的表现挺好，还说以后不再去青楼，甚至打算参与陈家生意。我觉得肯定是小姐管教得好，姑爷才有这么大的变化。”
Chen Haoyu blinked. "*Complete Library of the Four Treasuries*? Never heard of it."

千里寻拿起勺子刚喝两口汤，听了苦荞所说，放下勺子：“你用脑子好好想想，陈浩宇从小到大就是那副德行，我才管教两日，他就能变得跟以前不一样？说出去谁信？事出反常必有妖，肯定是他的阴谋。”
Oops! Transmigrator brain slip—that mega-encyclopedia belonged to the Qing dynasty centuries ahead! Never admitting a slip, she bluffed smooth: "A slacker like you barely knows three book titles—no shock you've never heard of it! Fine, I'll take pity on you: memorize the *Records of the Grand Historian* instead!"

苦荞想想似乎也对，又想起青瓜说的话，认为姑爷是喜欢上自家小姐才会发生变化。
Han dynasty—safe for Song times, and massive enough to bury him!

“我听青瓜说了件事，不知当讲不当讲？”
Sure enough, Chen Haoyu winced. "You already stacked a tower of books on my desk—why switch to something that monstrous? Out to kill me?"

千里寻边吃边说：“既然都提起，又何必问当讲不当讲？”
His pain brought back her grin. "My authority, my rules—changing my mind is my right!"

苦荞大胆说：“我去西院时，遇见青瓜，他告诉我姑爷昨日梦中说喜欢小姐您……”
"Fine, whatever you say—on one condition!"

千里寻刚舀起一勺汤，不得不放下：“青瓜的话你也信？”
Conditions?! Qianli Xun's eyes widened in fury. "What nerve gives you the right to set conditions?! When your lies had the entire city cursing me with nobody to turn to, who let me set conditions?! Hand you a couple books and you bargain? Zero conditions accepted!"

苦荞继续大胆说：“青瓜还说，姑爷之所以变化这么大，是因为喜欢上小姐……”
Guilt flickered across his face at the reminder. "All right, all right—no conditions! Torture me however you please; I'll take it all until your anger cools. Just... how long till it cools?"

千里寻没有说话，继续喝汤，动作比之前慢了许多。
"Never!" she glared. "Not for my entire life!"

苦荞不再言语，该说的已说完，一切留给小姐自己体会。
Instead of flinching, a goofy grin split his face. "My entire life? Means you're sticking with me for life! Hehe—luckier than Qiao Wenxian and Prince Yi combined! Hehe!"

千里寻用完饭后，一句话未说，进了里屋。
Her fury felt like chucking a pebble into a pond—swallowed without leaving a ripple. Looking away before his goofy smile wrecked her resolve, she snapped, "Drop the act! Think I can't see through your new tactic? Won't work!"

苦荞将碗筷收拾好，拿到西院，在西院跟下人们用过饭后，又回素心草堂。
"Think what you want—time to sweep the courtyard so the servants earn more silver! Besides, hate seeing you upset anyway. Hehe!" Stepping toward the door just as Hongdou and Amei entered carrying lunch trays, he brightened. "Hey, Zhu Shuzhen—after acing my books, let me stay and eat lunch with you?"

走到前院时，姑爷已开始扫地。青瓜想要帮忙，姑爷不允许，他只好站在一边看。
She whipped back. "Dream on! I'd share a bowl with a street beggar before eating at the same table with you!"

阿升和三宝则在一旁监督。
Undented, he grinned wide. "No rush—plenty of time ahead since you're keeping me for life! One day we'll share a table." And out he bounced before she could snap back.

苦荞进屋后，见书桌旁没人，往里屋拐去，才看到小姐和衣躺在床上。
Setting out the dishes, Hongdou and Amei traded a quick look before Hongdou began: "Lunch is served, Miss. While you eat, let me report on Young Master—courteous and measured with every servant today, studied hard without dozing off once!"

“小姐，我给您宽了衣裳再午休。”
Amei chimed right in: "Didn't kick Qinggua once either! Really seems changed!"

千里寻坐起来：“我躺了一会儿，根本睡不着，你陪我出去一趟。”
Not catching their plot yet, Qianli Xun rolled her eyes. "Couple coins split your way and you're bought off already? Don't trust surface shows—distance tests a horse, time tests a heart. Wait and see! Leave us—Kuqiao will attend me."

“去哪里？”
Exchanging a glance with Kuqiao, both maids withdrew.

“去看看神柳。”
Laying out chopsticks and ladling soup, Kuqiao hunted for a natural opening. "Miss, haven't seen Matron Wei in two days."

小姐心中有事才会想起神柳，苦荞无须多问，只管陪着便是。
"Out running business dawn to dusk—haven't seen her myself."

当苦荞跟着小姐走出素心草堂，陈浩宇依旧在清扫院落，手中的扫帚一起一落，发出“沙沙”的声音。
Kuqiao put on a wistful sigh. "Traditionally men handle outside and women handle inside. If you had a capable man by your side, Matron Wei wouldn't have to wear herself out running around town."

千里寻一边走一边观察，他那样子看上去的确不像是假装，难道陈浩宇真的因为喜欢上她而改变？如果是这样，她心中的仇恨又能找谁发泄？
Qianli Xun clacked her chopsticks down. "Kuqiao, how long have you been with me and you're spouting narrow nonsense? What's wrong with women? Women hold up half the sky! Who matches Matron Wei's brains, or my poetry? Look at Chen Haoyu—male body, empty head, bullying girls and haunting brothels! If men are like him, give me women any day!"

正想着，阿升和三宝来到她面前行礼。
Perfect segue! Kuqiao jumped right in: "Young Master isn't that hopeless—did great today, swore off brothels, even wants to join family business! Must be your discipline transforming him!"

三宝说：“小姐，姑爷今日干得挺卖力，您瞧，凡是他扫过的地方都很干净。”
Qianli Xun set down her soup spoon. "Use your head! Rotten his whole life, reformed in forty-eight hours? Tell that to a toddler! Sudden weird behavior means hidden tricks."

阿升接着说：“小姐，我发现姑爷长得挺不错，个头又高，身材也不错，脸长得也好。”
Half agreeing yet remembering Qinggua's scoop, Kuqiao pressed on. "Heard something from Qinggua—not sure I should mention it..."

千里寻这才听出，下人们是有意在她面前夸陈浩宇，她装作生气的样子：“你们是我的人还是陈浩宇的人？”
"Already teased it—spit it out."

两人齐声说：“当然是小姐的人。”
"Ran into Qinggua in the West Courtyard. He said Young Master confessed in his sleep yesterday that he likes you, Miss..."

千里寻批评道：“既如此，为何要替他说话？”
Her soup spoon halted midair and sank back down. "You believe Qinggua's tall tales?"

两人不敢再言语，目送小姐走出墨香苑。
"He said Young Master changed overnight solely because he fell for you..."

阿升叹口气：“唉，急不得，得慢慢来。”
Qianli Xun stayed silent, lifting her spoon far slower than before. Having planted the seed, Kuqiao said no more.

三宝点头认可。
After lunch, Qianli Xun went straight into the inner room without a word. Kuqiao cleared the dishes, ate with the staff in the West Courtyard, and returned to find Chen Haoyu diligently sweeping the front yard—waving off Qinggua's help while Asheng and Sanbao watched.

两个下人的表现，陈浩宇看在眼中，听进耳朵里，对两个下人笑道：“谢了，以后要多在少夫人面前说本公子的好话，本公子亏待不了你们。”
Indoors, she found her mistress lying fully dressed on the bed staring at the canopy. "Let me help you change before your nap, Miss."

阿升说：“光靠我们作用不大，姑爷自己要好好表现。”
Qianli Xun sat up. "Couldn't sleep a wink. Come out with me."

陈浩宇低下头继续扫地：“努力表现。”
"Where to?"

千里寻往陈府大门口走的过程中碰见丑丫。
"The Divine Willow."

丑丫去陈府的后厨报完墨香苑吃食上需要的采买之物外，在陈府逛了一会儿，才往回走，不巧半道上碰见自家小姐，主动上前拜见：“小姐这是要出去？”
Knowing her mistress sought that tree whenever turmoil brewed inside, Kuqiao asked nothing and followed.

“出去走走。”千里寻并未多停留，绕过丑丫准备离开。
Crossing the courtyard past Chen Haoyu—broom swishing crisp and steady across the paving stones—Qianli Xun watched him sidelong. Hard to believe he was faking that focus. If he genuinely fell for her and turned good, where was she supposed to aim her grudge?!

丑丫想到夸姑爷之事，快步挡在小姐面前：“小姐，有件事，我必须跟您说说。”
Right then Asheng and Sanbao stepped up bowing. "Young Master's working hard today, Miss—spotless sweep!" Sanbao began.

千里寻不得不站住：“什么事？”
Asheng added earnestly, "Noticed Young Master's actually quite a looker too—tall, solid build, handsome face!"

“我发现姑爷变了，变得比以前好许多。”
Catching onto the coordinated campaign at last, Qianli Xun put on a sharp frown. "Whose payroll are you two on—mine or Chen Haoyu's?"

又是夸陈浩宇！难道自己身边的人一个个被陈浩宇收买不成？千里寻没有说一句话，绕过丑丫继续往大门外走。
"Yours, of course, Miss!"

丑丫搞不明白，对着小姐的背影继续大声说：“小姐！我没骗您！我说的是真的！”
"Then why are you campaigning for him?"

苦荞边走边扭头向丑丫摆摆手，示意她不要再说，赶紧回去。
Silenced, both bowed as she swept out the gate. Asheng sighed, "No rushing this—slow and steady." Sanbao nodded.

丑丫只好放弃，小跑着往墨香苑而去，她要赶着回去找青瓜。
Overhearing everything, Chen Haoyu grinned at them. "Much obliged, boys! Keep putting in good words—I won't forget you!"

车夫赶着马车行走在去神柳的道路上，千里寻与苦荞坐在马车里，主仆两人一路沉默。
"Words only go so far, Young Master—keep up the action," Asheng advised as Chen Haoyu returned to sweeping with gusto.

自从嫁到陈家，她似乎好久没出来过，这一出来，外面的空气和街市的嘈杂声仿佛变得陌生许多。
Walking toward the front gate, Qianli Xun ran right into Chouya returning from kitchen errands. Blocking her path eagerly, Chouya blurted, "Miss, gotta tell you something—Young Master really changed! Way better than before!"

知道陈浩宇是散播谣言之人后，她想过很多折磨和报复的方法，最终选择了并不残忍，有些管教意味的方式。本想着对方越是反抗，越不开心，她越快乐。可事实正好相反，她并不快乐。
Another cheerleader?! Had Chen Haoyu hypnotized her entire staff?! Saying not one word, Qianli Xun brushed right past her while Chouya called after her, "I'm not lying, Miss—it's true!" until Kuqiao frantically waved her back.

更令她没想到的是，陈浩宇变得逆来顺受，竟然说喜欢她，还时不时露出以前在他脸上从来没见过的笑容，千里寻更加不开心。
Inside the carriage rattling toward the hills, mistress and maid sat in heavy silence. First time stepping outside Chen Manor since her wedding, the bustling street noise felt almost foreign.

她想要将去年因谣言忍受的痛苦在陈浩宇身上好好报复一下，老天竟然都不给她提供机会。
After learning Chen Haoyu spread those rumors, she had dreamed up a dozen punishments before settling on relatively mild discipline—expecting his misery to feed her joy. Instead she felt miserable while he smiled through every chore and declared his love! Heaven wouldn't even grant her the clean satisfaction of hating him.
