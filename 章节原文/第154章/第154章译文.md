## Chapter 154: Chapter 154: Reunion Without Acceptance

Swept up in pure joy, Qianli Xun hugged Shen Jianchen a long moment before Mengrong flashed across her mind, nudging him back: "Alright, alright, let go!"

Loath to release his very first embrace of the woman he loved—no telling when another chance might come—Shen Jianchen still obeyed and opened his arms.

Twilight deepened as a bright moon climbed overhead, uncommonly round and luminous; bare winter willow twigs couldn't block its silver flood, lighting the clearing bright as day. Standing beneath the tree, neither seemed to notice the biting winter chill settling in.

"When did you figure out who I was?" Qianli Xun asked.

"Your very first date with Wenyuan."

Remembering the silk ribbon: "Because of the red ribbon I tied up there?"

"Yes. If that ribbon hadn't drifted down from the upper boughs, how would I ever have known Youqi Jushi was the very girl I'd been searching for?"

A pinch of sheepish letdown crossed Qianli Xun—all along she had assumed Prince Yi was her die-hard literary fan, turns out she had flattered herself! Dropping every noble-lady air, she snapped in her old sassy tone, "If you recognized me way back then, why keep your mouth shut?!"

Shen Jianchen laughed warm. "Now THAT'S the voice I missed! As for waiting—already told you, timing never lined up until today."

"Makes total sense now why an imperial prince singled me out. Sorry for pegging you as a shameless playboy."

Glancing up at the massive Divine Willow—bare winter boughs still curving with quiet poise—Shen Jianchen turned earnest: "Don't marry Chen Haoyu."

"Relax, zero chance I'm marrying him," Qianli Xun answered crisp.

"Betrothal is sealed—might not be up to you! This era isn't our old world."

Silence fell over Qianli Xun. Why did everyone in this century keep scripting her life? First the Qiaos crushed her romance with Wenyuan; barely back on her feet, the Zhus and Chens slapped a betrothal right over her head! "Even if the deck's stacked against me, I'm fighting to flip it!"

Words about Chen Haoyu spreading those filthy rumors rose to Shen Jianchen's tongue—then he swallowed them back; why add fresh hurt when all he wanted was her smiling? Instead he ventured soft, "Why not... say yes to being with me? That gives me ironclad reason to step in and break it off."

Had Shen Jianchen appeared back when she hungered for romance—with zero strings attached—she would have jumped straight in! Yet now fresh heartbreak had sworn her off love for good. Worse stood Mengrong: loving her brother with her whole soul while never knowing her real brother had died, pouring devotion into a stranger wearing his face! Qianli Xun refused to crush Mengrong's world.

Masking gravity behind a teasing smirk, she shot back: "Already told you—only when pigs climb trees will I marry you! Still stands!"

Pain flickered across Shen Jianchen's eyes before he steadied himself and asked grave: "Did you never once love me?"

Memories of her first lonely days after crossing over surfaced—every dream filled with Shen Jianchen! Answering plain: "I did love you... pity you weren't beside me back then."

Those quiet words lit Shen Jianchen up like buried treasure: "You actually loved me?! I... I don't even know what to say!"

"Figured I'd never see you again," Qianli Xun went on level. "Then Wenyuan came along and stole my heart—and you know how the rest played out, no need to rehash."

Self-blame hit Shen Jianchen hard: had he reached Lin'an sooner, met Youqi Jushi sooner, recognized Qianli Xun sooner, she never would have fallen for Wenyuan, never shattered her heart, never landed engaged to Chen Haoyu! "All my fault for showing up too late."

"Not your fault. Master Jingguang warned me Zhu Shuzhen's destiny carries a rocky road in love; crossing into her body means passing through romantic trials before life smooths out."

"Romantic trials?" Shen Jianchen repeated grim, refusing to watch her bleed again. "Then let me cut those trials short right now!"

"How?"

"Be with me—I swear I'll never let you get hurt!"

"And Mengrong? Prince Yi has an imperial betrothal to her!"

Mention of Qiao Mengrong and the imperial decree silenced him; even a prince had no magic wand over an emperor's decree.

Seeing him muted, Qianli Xun spoke gentle: "Fate played a giant prank on both of us. Surviving that quake through the Divine Willow handed us brand-new lives—everything changes, love included. Let me go, Shen Jianchen, and love Mengrong well."

Thinking revealing his identity would unlock her heart, getting turned down this crisp stung deep—yet stubborn hope made him try one last long shot: "Mengrong is kind-hearted! If I talk it through gentle with her, she'll agree to let me take you as concubine—then I can dissolve the Zhu-Chen betrothal on the spot!"

"Swore I'd never be anyone's concubine—forgot already? And if you know Mengrong is that kind, don't hurt her bringing another woman home! Grow old together with her alone."

Shut down with zero wiggle room, Shen Jianchen still refused to walk away empty-handed: "Tell me how to help you break Chen Haoyu's betrothal—and dodge whatever love trials wait ahead!"

"Everyone walks their own road—not even a prince can walk mine for me! Plan's already set: Chen Haoyu doesn't want me, I don't want him, so we'll team up to wreck the match from both sides! As for love trials—Wenyuan was round one, no clue how many wait next, yet I'm armored up: swear off love, never hand my heart to another soul, and every love trial snaps dead on contact! Clear skies ahead!"

She had mapped out every inch of her future leaving zero space for him to wedge in. Standing silent a long beat, Shen Jianchen spoke low: "I'll heed your words and care for Mengrong—and I'll wait for you! The day you ever open your heart to love again, don't think I'll let you slip away!"

Calling "Bai Feng!" he strode off down the moonlit mountain path with his guard trailing close behind.

Watching his silhouette fade, Qianli Xun faced the Divine Willow once more—wondering whether the tree slept beneath winter moonlight, praying silent it stayed awake to hear her wish: Keep me out of the Chen house, spare me more heartbreak, let me live quiet on my own—and bless Mengrong and her reborn brother to wed in joy and love each other well.

A sharp winter gust swept through; pulling her cloak tighter, Qianli Xun heard Kuqiao remind her: "Time to head back, Miss."

Leading both maids down toward the trailhead, she slowed her stride on purpose so Shen Jianchen's rig would clear out first. Sure enough, Youlin Retreat's carriage was gone when they arrived. Climbing aboard Wangyue Pavilion's carriage, all three rode silent all the way into bustling evening avenues, bright street clamor underscoring the hush inside.

Truth be told, joy still warmed Qianli Xun: finding Shen Jianchen alive, safe, and an imperial prince! Wild how fate spun—that cocky CEO waking up royalty! Never in ten lifetimes would she have guessed it!

Spotting her quiet smile, Kuqiao broke the silence: "Thinking of something happy, Miss?"

"Course."

"What happy news?"

Unable to spill Prince Yi's real identity: "None of your business—stop nosing around!"

Xiaguo ventured bold: "Did Miss fall for Prince Yi—is that why you're grinning?"

"Xiaguo! Watch your tongue!" Kuqiao snapped.

Can't blame Xiaguo for guessing wrong—secret moonlight meeting ending in a tight hug looked mighty romantic to outsiders! Setting her tone grave, Qianli Xun warned both girls: "Listen close, both of you—not ONE word about tonight leaves this carriage!"

Both maids nodded fast: "Never a word, Miss!"

"Prince Yi and I are spotless—nothing like what you're imagining," Qianli Xun added. "Can't explain why we met alone or hugged, just know romance has zero to do with it."

Both maids blinked baffled, still in a fog. Xiaguo risked one more bold nudge: "Even if Miss doesn't like Prince Yi, plain as day Prince Yi fancies you! Say yes to him and you dodge marrying Young Master Chen!"

"Clever idea—and zero chance I touch it!" Qianli Xun declared iron-firm. "I live by my own line; cross it, and no prize on earth tempts me!"

Silenced by her resolve, neither maid breathed another word the rest of the ride home.
