第132章 郡主探望望月轩
## Chapter 132: Princess Jiahe Calls on Wangyue Pavilion

到达望月轩门口，乔梦容见哥哥没有要下马车的意思，不解地问：“难道哥哥不打算跟我一起看望淑真？”
Pulling up outside Wangyue Pavilion, Qiao Mengrong saw no sign of Prince Yi stepping down and asked in puzzlement, "Isn't Brother coming in with me to see Shuzhen?"

申简辰当然想，可他已答应千里寻不再来望月轩，又岂能食言？
Shen Jianchen burned to go in, yet he had promised Qianli Xun never to enter Wangyue Pavilion again; how could he break his word?

“你们之间有女孩子的话题要聊，我去不大合适；再说我是外男，探望幽栖居士对她影响不好。我在车上等你，不用担心我等得久，今日无事，专程陪你，你与幽栖居士想聊多久都可以。”
"You girls have girl talk—me tagging along would be awkward. Besides, I'm an unmarried man; visiting Youqi Jushi wouldn't look right for her reputation. I'll wait right here in the carriage. Take all the time you want; my whole day is yours."

哥哥如此体贴，乔梦容甚是满意，尤其是哥哥对别的女子懂得男女有别，更令她欣慰，以后嫁给哥哥可以放一百个心。
Such thoughtfulness—above all, keeping proper distance from another woman—melted Qiao Mengrong's heart, leaving her at complete ease about marrying him.

乔梦容没有让望月轩的下人报告主人，她想给淑真一个惊喜，在望月轩下人的带领下，朝淑真的闺房走去。
Asking Wangyue Pavilion's servants not to announce her so she could surprise Shuzhen, Qiao Mengrong followed a servant straight to the bedchamber courtyard.

到达闺房门外，夏果正好在院中，见到郡主，刚要拜见，郡主将手指放在嘴边，“嘘”了一声，她便明白郡主想给小姐一个惊喜，便默声参拜完，帮郡主撩开帘子。
Spotting Xiaguo in the yard about to bow and announce her, Qiao Mengrong pressed a finger to her lips with a quiet "Shhh!" Catching on, Xiaguo bowed in silence and lifted the door curtain for her.

快要进入冬季，望月轩所有屋门上挂起布帘，既可装饰，亦可抵御冷风。
With winter nearing, heavy cloth curtains hung over every doorway in Wangyue Pavilion, both neat and snug against drafts.

千里寻正在桌前写话本，苦荞站在一旁伺候笔墨。
Inside, Qianli Xun sat writing her huaben script while Kuqiao tended ink beside her.

逛了一趟街，没人再议论她，又收拾了一个欠揍的，郁结之情得到疏散后，千里寻开始大胆提笔写男女主人公甜蜜的恋情。
After that market stroll—hearing rumors had died and beating up an arrogant bully—her gloom had lifted enough to start writing sweet romance for her hero and heroine again.

正写得专注，传来乔梦容的声音。
Lost in her script, she heard Qiao Mengrong's bright voice break the hush.

“在忙什么？好久没去看我，我只好过来探望你。”
"What are you busy with? You never came to visit me, so I had to come hunt you down!"

乔梦容到来，无疑让望月轩多出些许生气。
Qiao Mengrong's arrival injected instant warmth into Wangyue Pavilion.

千里寻放下笔，起身迎上去：“你既来，也不说一声，我好出去迎接。”
Laying down her brush, Qianli Xun jumped up to greet her: "You didn't even send word so I could come welcome you at the gate!"

“迎接多没意思，不如我进来给你个意外之喜。”
"Where's the fun in formal welcomes? Much better popping in as a surprise!"

二人说话之际，苦荞将小姐没写完的手稿收起来，走到书架跟前，打开一个木盒子，小心放入。
While they greeted each other, Kuqiao gathered her mistress's unfinished manuscript, walked over to the bookshelf, opened a wooden box, and tucked it safe inside.

千里寻拉起乔梦容在椅子上坐下，吩咐夏果做两盏茶。
Pulling Qiao Mengrong into a chair, Qianli Xun told Xiaguo to whisk two bowls of tea.

夏果做茶的技术已拿得出手，她高兴地应答着，走到茶桌前，忙碌起来。
Confident in her tea-whisking skills now, Xiaguo answered with a bright smile and moved to the tea table.

喜兰站在郡主身旁，扫视幽栖居士的闺房，书香四溢，心中慨叹，难怪她能成为江南女词人。
Standing beside her princess, Xilan took in the scholar's charm filling Youqi Jushi's chamber—no wonder she was known as the Poetess of Jiangnan!

“淑真，有机会再去幽林别居陪我住几日，别居又大又冷清，以前还有乔公子在，他一走，更冷清。”
"Come stay a few days at Youlin Retreat again sometime, Shuzhen! That big house feels so empty—and twice as lonely since Young Master Qiao left."

提到乔文仙，千里寻心中一痛，怕郡主瞧出来，掩饰道：“我已答应韦嬷嬷，以后不会在外留宿，再说别居有仪王在，他对我来讲，是外男，甚是不便。”
A sharp twinge pierced Qianli Xun at mention of Qiao Wenxian; masking it fast, she replied, "I promised Matron Wei no more staying out overnight. Besides, Prince Yi lives at the retreat—an unmarried man, so staying there isn't proper."

“怪我，只想着跟你多待些时日，没考虑周全，倒是你跟哥哥考虑全面，他跟我一起来，说不便进来，待在外面的马车里候着。”
"My fault! Just wanted to spend more time with you and didn't think it through. You and Brother both think ahead—he came with me today, said stepping inside wouldn't look right, and stayed waiting in the carriage outside."

“什么？仪王跟你一起来？让他待在外面岂不是慢待他？”千里寻犹豫要不要出去迎接。
"What?! Prince Yi came with you?! Leaving him waiting outside is awful manners!" Qianli Xun wavered over whether to go welcome him.

“哥哥说不用管他，还说我们想聊多久都可以。”
"Brother said leave him be—stay and chat as long as we like!"

千里寻本也不想见仪王，他既然懂事，不随便进来，再好不过。
Truth be told, Qianli Xun had zero urge to see Prince Yi; if he had the sense to stay outside, all the better!

苦荞听到仪王在外面等着，已呆不住，走到小姐跟前，大胆说：“小姐，我能不能出去跟白风说两句话。”
Hearing Prince Yi was outside, Kuqiao could not sit still and ventured, "Miss, may I step outside for two words with Bai Feng?"

“去吧，早知道你想见他。”千里寻答应得干脆。
"Go on—knew you were itching to see him!" Qianli Xun waved her off at once.

“谢谢小姐！”苦荞兴奋地跑出去。
"Thank you, Miss!" Kuqiao darted out beaming.

乔梦容想起乔公子订亲之事，犹豫如何开口告诉淑真。
Remembering Qiao Wenxian's betrothal, Qiao Mengrong hesitated how to break it to Shuzhen.

“淑真，有件事，我想对你说，只怕说出来，你会伤心……”
"Shuzhen, there's something I need to tell you—afraid it'll hurt you..."

千里寻猜到梦容想说什么：“你是想说文渊订亲一事？”
Qianli Xun guessed right away: "You mean Wenyuan getting engaged?"

“原来你已知，害我担心半天。”
"You already know?! Here I was sweating bullets over how to tell you!"

“我哥哥来时已告诉我。”
"My brother told me when he visited."

乔梦容观察淑真的表情，她看上去并没有想象中那么难过。
Studying Shuzhen's face, Qiao Mengrong saw far less devastation than she had braced for.

“淑真，我相信乔公子心中还有你，只是造化弄人，别想那么多，一切向前看。”
"I'm sure Young Master Qiao still holds you in his heart, Shuzhen—just cruel fate. Don't brood; keep looking forward."

“你放心，不管乔家订多少门亲事，只要开春前，文渊不亲口对我说放弃的话，我绝不放弃。”
"Don't worry. No matter how many matches the Qiao family arranges, until spring comes, unless Wenyuan tells me to my face he's giving up, I won't give up."

“开春后呢？”
"And after spring?"

“开春后……开春后，他若依然不说，我……我便放弃。”
"After spring... if he still stays silent, I'll... let go."

“看来，你已想清楚。我听哥哥说，乔家给乔公子订下亲后，不再关着他……”
"Sounds like you've thought it clear. Brother told me once the Qiaos sealed the betrothal, they stopped locking Young Master Qiao up..."

千里寻听到文渊不再被关着，忍不住流下眼泪：“他既已自由，为何不来找我？”
Hearing Wenyuan walked free now brought sudden tears spilling from Qianli Xun's eyes: "If he's free, why hasn't he come to see me?!"

“哥哥没告诉我，我猜想，应是乔公子有什么难处。”
"Brother didn't say. Maybe Young Master Qiao has his hands tied."

“梦容，你帮我拜托仪王，让他给文渊捎句话，就说我在望月轩等他。”
"Mengrong, ask Prince Yi to pass a message to Wenyuan for me—tell him I'm waiting for him at Wangyue Pavilion."

乔梦容点点头：“放心，我会转告哥哥。”
Qiao Mengrong nodded: "Don't worry, I'll tell Brother."

夏果已做好两盏茶，给郡主与小姐端上。
Having finished two bowls of whisked tea, Xiaguo served them to Princess Jiahe and her mistress.

她站在一旁听二人聊天儿，得知乔公子已获自由，为他高兴的同时，想着什么时候找机会见见他。小姐与乔公子已毫无希望，她要为自己的人生搏上一把。
Standing beside them listening to their chat, Xiaguo felt her pulse quicken at news that Young Master Qiao was free; with Miss's hopes gone, Xiaguo meant to gamble on her own future!

申简辰坐在马车里等得悠然自得，等得心甘情愿。表面上等的是梦容，事实上等的是千里寻，等关于她的消息，与等她这个人又有什么区别。
Out in the carriage, Shen Jianchen waited in complete content—ostensibly waiting for Mengrong, in truth waiting on Qianli Xun; waiting for news of her felt almost as good as waiting for her in person.

马车外，白风与苦荞在聊天儿。苦荞埋怨白风为何这么久才来望月轩，白风说王爷不让他随便来，他也没办法。
Outside the carriage, Bai Feng and Kuqiao chatted away, Kuqiao scolding him for taking so long to visit while Bai Feng pleaded His Highness would not let him come over whenever he liked.

“难道王爷不愿再给我家小姐送消息？”
"Doesn't Prince Yi want to send my mistress updates anymore?"

“怎么不愿？王爷对这事儿热心着呢？”
"Of course he does—he cares tons!"

“既热心，你为何才送过两次消息，后面再不见来？”
"Then why only two updates and then radio silence?"

“都不是什么好消息，王爷担心幽栖居士伤心，所以不让我送。”
"Nothing but bad news—His Highness didn't want to make Youqi Jushi cry, so he held me back."

“原来是这样，害得我好久没见到你。”
"Oh... still made me miss you forever!"

“我已想好，以后每隔三日，来看你，你在申时到门外等我就是。”
"Got it all planned: every three days, I'll come see you—meet me outside the gate at the hour of shen!"

“说好了，不能变卦。”
"Promise—no backing out!"

“放心，绝不变卦，变卦我是小狗。”
"Never! Back out and I'm a puppy!"

两人说得无比开心。
Both grinned ear to ear.

品完一盏茶后，千里寻的情绪平静许多，文渊不来找她，也许是有他的难处，她还是继续耐心等待。
Finishing her tea, Qianli Xun regained her calm: maybe Wenyuan really did have some bind keeping him away; she would keep waiting.

乔梦容放下茶盏说道：“听得出，关于你跟乔公子之事，你的心中已有主意，我便放心。就目前情形来看，他已订亲，你想与他在一起，从礼俗上讲，已……已不可能，除非……”
Setting down her bowl, Qiao Mengrong said, "Glad you've got a clear head about Young Master Qiao. Realistically, now that he's betrothed, proper etiquette leaves no path for you two—unless..."

千里寻果断说：“我不可能给文渊做妾，我等的不是他娶我或不娶我，而是等他一句话。”
Qianli Xun cut in firm: "I'm never becoming Wenyuan's concubine. I'm not waiting to see if he'll marry me—I'm just waiting for one straight answer from him."

“你也要为自己重新打算，不要为等乔公子一句话浪费大好光阴，我们女子耗费不起。”
"Still, plan your own future too! Don't burn away your best years waiting on one sentence from Young Master Qiao—women can't afford to waste time like that."

千里寻盯着梦容，一个遇见仪王便失去理智的郡主，安慰起别人来，却头头是道。
Qianli Xun could not help marveling at Mengrong: loses all logic the second Prince Yi enters the room, yet hands out rock-solid advice to everyone else!

“别光顾说我的事，说说你，跟你那位王爷怎么样？他最近有没有出去勾搭别的姑娘？”
"Enough about me—how about you and that prince of yours? Been sneaking off to flirt with other girls lately?"

乔梦容被逗乐：“‘勾搭’这个词用在哥哥身上，实在不恰当。”
Qianli Xun's slang cracked Qiao Mengrong up: "Using 'flirt' on Brother sounds so wrong!"

“恰当不恰当，现在还难说。”
"Wrong or right remains to be seen!"

“也不知你为何对哥哥有成见？他可是帮过你不少忙，为帮你和乔公子送消息，两头忙，今日愿意跟我来望月轩，估计又是想给乔公子送消息。”
"Why do you hold such a grudge against Brother? He's helped you so much—running back and forth with news for you and Young Master Qiao, and likely brought me here today just to get news for Young Master Qiao too!"

“你这位哥哥真奇怪，放着临安的大事不忙，倒对我和文渊的事热心，也不知他是真热心还是假热心。”千里寻想起仪王对她表白的话，想起减免赋税之事，更觉仪王心机深沉。
"Funny prince you've got—ignoring Lin'an's affairs to meddle in Wenyuan's and my love life! Hard to tell if he's truly helpful or up to something." Remembering his confessions and the tax cuts, Qianli Xun thought Prince Yi ran deep as a well.

乔梦容故作严肃道：“淑真，看在你跟我关系好，我不计较，若是别人说同样的话，我可不高兴。”
Putting on mock sternness, Qiao Mengrong scolded, "Only because you're my best friend do I let that slide—anyone else talks about Brother like that and I'd be furious!"

“好好好，我道歉，你的哥哥是天底下最好的男子，谁都比不上。”
"All right, all right, I apologize! Your Brother is the greatest man under heaven—nobody holds a candle to him!"

“你也别用故意夸他的话噎我，反正我哥哥就是好，他近期除忙你跟文渊的事，一直在幽林别居陪我，怕我在别居待得心烦，才想着带我出来逛逛，还心甘情愿在外面等我，哪怕我跟你聊到天黑，他都愿意等下去。你说，有几个男子能做到？”
"Quit teasing me with fake praise! Brother really is wonderful. Outside helping you and Wenyuan, he stays right beside me at Youlin Retreat all day, brought me out strolling today so I wouldn't get bored, and waits out here in the carriage without a single complaint even if we talk till dark! How many men would do that?"

千里寻心想：“我要是告诉你仪王对我表白过，看你还能说出几句夸赞他的话。”
Qianli Xun thought, "Wait till I tell you he confessed his love to me—let's see how sweet your praise sounds then!"

千里寻更觉仪王不仅心机深沉，还是情场老手。一边把单纯的郡主玩得滴溜转，一边对其他女子放电；更无耻的是，还能把自己的不道德说成是满腔痴情。
In her eyes, Prince Yi was not just scheming—he was a grandmaster player, spinning an innocent princess around his little finger with one hand while tossing sparks at other women with the other, and shameless enough to paint his cheating as tragic devotion!

如果她千里寻是个有貌无脑之人，估计早已因仪王的身份、地位，还有他看似痴情表白的话而倾倒。
Any pretty airhead would have melted straight into his lap over his royal title, wealth, and silky confessions.

“你说得对，你哥哥的作派确实没几个男子能做到。”
"True enough—not many men operate quite like your Brother."

淑真敷衍的认可，乔梦容并未当回事，只以为淑真太过关心她，怕她在感情上受到伤害，有句话说得甚好，关心则乱。所故而，她误会哥哥，在所难免。
Missing the half-hearted edge of Shuzhen's agreement, Qiao Mengrong thought nothing of it, assuming her friend cared so much she feared seeing her hurt—just like the saying that deep concern clouds the eye, making misunderstanding inevitable.

别说淑真会误会哥哥，就连她乔梦容也将哥哥误会许久。
Never mind Shuzhen misunderstanding Prince Yi—even Qiao Mengrong herself had misunderstood him for ages.

淑真既已清楚该如何去做，乔梦容没必要多加劝说。陪她又东拉西扯，胡乱聊了半个时辰，发现淑真的心情又好很多，觉得没必要再待下去，关键她担心哥哥在外面等得太久，便起身告辞。
Seeing Shuzhen had her head screwed on straight, Qiao Mengrong dropped the lectures and chatted about random trifles for another hour until Shuzhen's spirits brightened, then rose to leave lest Prince Yi wait too long outside.

千里寻将佳和郡主送至望月轩门外的马车上，便向马车里的仪王行礼问好，心里再对他不恭敬，表面功夫也要做到位。
Walking Princess Jiahe out to the carriage at the gate, Qianli Xun bowed with full courtesy toward the curtained coach—whatever she felt inside, surface etiquette stayed spotless.

“感谢仪王送梦容前来陪我说话，我的心情好了很多。”
"Thank you for bringing Mengrong to keep me company, Prince Yi. My spirits are much lighter."

申简辰并未掀开帘子，尽管他很想掀开，还是忍住，担心亲眼看到她，会不愿离开。
Refusing to lift the curtain lest one look at her make leaving impossible, Shen Jianchen answered from inside: "No need to thank me—Mengrong wanted to visit, I just tagged along."

“幽栖居士不必谢我，梦容想来，我陪她而已。”
Deciding to test the waters on the tax cuts, Qianli Xun added, "One more thing Shuzhen owes Prince Yi thanks for."

关于减免赋税一事，千里寻想要趁机探探虚实：“还有一事，淑真要谢过仪王。”
"If it's about checking on Wenyuan, forget it."

“若是关于帮你打探文渊消息一事，我看就免了。”
"About the tax cuts. Because of them, everyone's attention shifted and the street gossip died clean out—Shuzhen is grateful beyond words."

“是关于减免赋税一事，由于此事，百姓关注的焦点发生转移，街上无人再议论我，流言蜚语已过去，淑真对仪王感激不尽。”
"How do you plan to thank me?"

“你打算怎么谢我？”
"How do you plan to thank me?"

千里寻被仪王的问题给问住，怎么谢？她还没想好，只是探探虚实而已，了解仪王帮她是否有所求。还想趁佳和郡主在时，仪王不敢乱提要求，用良好的态度谢过他，此事也可了结。却不曾想仪王会反问她。
His question tripped her up—she had not planned anything! She only wanted to probe whether he wanted payback, figuring with Princess Jiahe right beside him he would not dare demand anything weird, letting her clear the debt with polite words. Never dreamed he would bounce the ball right back!

马车外一阵沉默，申简辰猜道千里寻感到为难，他最见不得她为难，便说道：“减免赋税是为临安百姓考虑，跟街上的流言毫无关系，在我眼中，幽栖居士岂可与临安百姓相提并论？”
Hearing silence outside and guessing she felt cornered—the last thing he ever wanted—Shen Jianchen spoke cool and clean: "I proposed tax cuts for the people of Lin'an, nothing to do with street gossip. In my eyes, how could Youqi Jushi compare with all the commoners of Lin'an?"

马车开始启动，千里寻松口气，不用再担心欠仪王一个大大的人情，也不用担心因为这个人情会被仪王纳为妾室。
As the carriage pulled away, Qianli Xun breathed a huge sigh of relief—no giant debt hanging over her head, and no risk of being strong-armed into becoming a royal concubine!

离望月轩远些后，申简辰忍不住问：“梦容，文渊订亲，幽栖居士可难过？”
Once down the lane, Shen Jianchen could not hold back: "Mengrong, how hard is Youqi Jushi taking Wenyuan's betrothal?"

乔梦容叹道：“怎能不难过？不过淑真想得明白，她说只要乔公子不说放弃的话，她便等着。”
Qiao Mengrong sighed: "Of course she's hurt. Still, she has a clear head—said as long as Young Master Qiao doesn't tell her he's giving up, she'll wait."

申简辰担忧不已：“文渊成亲了，她还要等吗？”
Alarm flared in Shen Jianchen: "Even after Wenyuan marries?!"

乔梦容解释道：“淑真说开春后，若乔公子不来找她，她便放弃。”
Qiao Mengrong explained: "Shuzhen said after spring comes, if Young Master Qiao still hasn't come for her, she'll let go."

申简辰不由轻松许多：“还好，她不会傻到永远等下去。”
A quiet breath escaped Shen Jianchen: "Thank goodness she isn't foolish enough to wait forever."

乔梦容想起淑真的嘱托：“对了哥哥，淑真想麻烦你给乔公子捎句话，她在望月轩等着乔公子。”
Then Qiao Mengrong remembered her errand: "Oh, Brother—Shuzhen asked if you'd pass word to Young Master Qiao that she's waiting for him at Wangyue Pavilion."

申简辰没有作出回应，他第一次不想帮心爱的姑娘，既然对方已订亲，见了又有何用？
Shen Jianchen gave no reply. For the very first time, he refused to help the woman he loved—with Wenyuan already engaged, what good would meeting do?
