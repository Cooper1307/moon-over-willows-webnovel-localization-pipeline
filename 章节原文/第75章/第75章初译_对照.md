# 第75章 携手家人逛朱府

---

千里寻收拾完，简单用过早饭，来到向夫人房中，韦嬷嬷也在。

After tidying up, Qianli Xun had a simple breakfast and went to Lady Xiang's room. Nanny Wei was also there.

---

韦嬷嬷与向夫人虽是主仆，却胜似姐妹，回朱府这段时日，天天住在向夫人的院子。

Though Nanny Wei and Lady Xiang were mistress and servant, they were closer than sisters. During their time back at the Zhu residence, Nanny Wei stayed in Lady Xiang's courtyard every day.

---

千里寻进屋先拜见两位长辈，然后坐在椅子上不敢言语。她已猜到，长辈找她肯定是因游湖约会一事。

Qianli Xun entered the room and first paid her respects to the two elders, then sat on a chair, not daring to speak. She'd already guessed why the elders had summoned her—the lake outing.

---

果然，向夫人开口问：“你昨日连午饭都没吃，就跑出去，很晚才回来，有没有饿着？”

Sure enough, Lady Xiang asked: "You didn't even eat lunch yesterday, ran out, and came back very late. Were you hungry?"

---

千里寻想起文渊为她带的小吃，幸福感难以掩饰：“我在外吃了，根本没饿着。”

Qianli Xun thought of the snacks Wenyuan had brought her, her happiness hard to hide: "I ate outside, I wasn't hungry at all."

---

向夫人说：“那便好，听绿桃说，你到西湖去见乔家公子，韦嬷嬷也告诉我，你对乔公子十分中意，可否给娘仔细说说？”

Lady Xiang said: "That's good then. Lvtiao told me you went to West Lake to see Young Master Qiao. Nanny Wei also told me you're very fond of Young Master Qiao. Can you tell Mother all about it?"

---

千里寻本就不想瞒着朱淑真的家人：“我失忆后第一次见到文渊，他向我表明想法，才知道他一直以来的心意。而我又将徐家公子忘得一干二净，自然对文渊一见……一见钟情，故而昨日与他相约一起游西湖。”

Qianli Xun never intended to hide anything from Zhu Shuzhen's family: "The first time I met Wenyuan after losing my memory, he expressed his feelings to me, and I learned of his long-held intentions. Since I had completely forgotten Young Master Xu, I naturally fell in love with Wenyuan at first...at first sight, which is why I agreed to go to West Lake with him yesterday."

---

向夫人点点头：“乔家公子在临安年轻一辈中算是出挑的，你爹也常夸赞他，只是……”

Lady Xiang nodded: "Young Master Qiao is indeed outstanding among the young generation in Lin'an. Your father often praises him. It's just..."

---

千里寻见向夫人停住，问道：“娘，只是什么？”

Seeing Lady Xiang pause, Qianli Xun asked: "Mother, just what?"

---

向夫人面有难色：“只是……我听说乔家已开始为乔公子选亲，好几个媒婆搜罗了不少大家闺秀的生辰八字，你当时有婚姻，乔家自然没有要你的，娘担心……”

Lady Xiang looked troubled: "It's just...I've heard that the Qiao family has already started selecting a bride for Young Master Qiao. Several matchmakers have collected the birth dates of many young ladies from good families. Since you were married at the time, the Qiao family naturally didn't consider you. Mother is worried..."

---

韦嬷嬷说：“夫人不必过分担忧，乔公子中意我们家姑娘，定会向他的父母提起。”

Nanny Wei said: "Madam needn't worry too much. Since Young Master Qiao is fond of our young lady, he will surely mention it to his parents."

---

千里寻插嘴道：“文渊告诉我，今日就向家人提起此事，让他父母来朱家提亲。”

Qianli Xun interrupted: "Wenyuan told me he would mention this to his family today and have his parents come to the Zhu family to propose marriage."

---

向夫人点点头，继续忧虑道：“你毕竟有过婚姻，只怕乔家嫌弃。”

Lady Xiang nodded, continuing anxiously: "After all, you've been married before. I'm afraid the Qiao family might look down on you."

---

韦嬷嬷接着说：“我们姑娘是临安知府的女儿，又是江南女词人，也是真正的大家闺秀，虽有过一次婚姻，却并未与练兵官圆房，依然清白，乔家没有嫌弃的道理。”

Nanny Wei continued: "Our young lady is the daughter of the Lin'an prefect, a famous female poet of Jiangnan, and a true young lady from a good family. Though she's been married once, she never consummated the marriage with the military training officer. She's still pure. The Qiao family has no reason to look down on her."

---

向夫人仍旧担忧：“话虽如此，乔家是清白人家，又是皇亲国戚，只有这么一个儿子，必然要求高，我就是怕淑真与乔公子的感情会是竹篮打水一场空。”

Lady Xiang remained worried: "Even so, the Qiao family is a respectable family and imperial relatives. They only have this one son, so their requirements must be high. I'm just afraid that Shuzhen's affection for Young Master Qiao will prove as futile as fetching water with a wicker basket."

---

千里寻起身，拉起向夫人的手：“娘，您别瞎想，趁女儿现在恢复自由之身，陪您好好逛逛，要不我们去西湖？”

Qianli Xun stood up and took Lady Xiang's hand: "Mother, don't overthink it. While your daughter is free now, let me take you out for a good stroll. How about we go to West Lake?"

---

向夫人笑道：“你昨日去了，今日又去，也不嫌烦？”

Lady Xiang laughed: "You went yesterday and want to go again today? Aren't you tired of it?"

---

“不烦！不烦！”千里寻像撒娇一样，有娘的感觉真不错。

"Not tired at all! Not tired at all!" Qianli Xun acted playfully. It felt so nice to have a mother.

---

向夫人说：“我才不去西湖，又远人又多，我们家的花园也不错，你陪娘和韦嬷嬷在花园里逛逛得了。”

Lady Xiang said: "I'm not going to West Lake. It's far and crowded. Our family garden is nice enough. You can accompany Mother and Nanny Wei for a stroll in the garden."

---

千里寻满口答应，她搀着向夫人，韦嬷嬷伴随在一旁，三人在园子里边聊边逛。

Qianli Xun readily agreed. She supported Lady Xiang, with Nanny Wei accompanying them. The three strolled through the garden, chatting as they walked.

---

银杏与苦荞跟在后面伺候。

Yinxing and Kuqiao followed behind to attend them.

---

千里寻认为她的生活终于恢复正常，不管现在的生活是千里寻的还是朱淑真的，能够确定的是，无论生活发生什么，她都会用积极的态度面对，只要坚持净光法师说的四个字“正思正念”，她相信即使现在是朱淑真的命运，终有一天会走上属于千里寻的命运。

Qianli Xun felt her life had finally returned to normal. No matter whether this life belonged to Qianli Xun or Zhu Shuzhen, what was certain was that no matter what happened, she would face it with a positive attitude. As long as she held fast to Master Jingguang's four words—“Right Thought and Right Mindfulness”—she believed that even if she were bound by Zhu Shuzhen's fate today, she would one day reclaim the destiny that belonged to Qianli Xun.

---

正逛着，一个熟悉的身影从眼前闪过，千里寻在心里冷笑道：“真是阴魂不散！”

As they were strolling, a familiar figure flashed before her eyes. Qianli Xun sneered in her heart: "What a persistent nuisance!"

---

“娘，陈浩宇为何来朱府？”

"Mother, why is Chen Haoyu at the Zhu residence?"

---

向夫人朝不远处瞧去，的确是陈家公子，他只身一人，看那样子是要离开。

Lady Xiang looked toward the distance. It was indeed Young Master Chen, alone and appearing to leave.

---

“他跟你嫂嫂姐弟情深，经常来看她，应该是看完亲姐姐准备离开。”

"He's very close to your sister-in-law. He often comes to visit her. He's probably finished visiting his older sister and is preparing to leave."

---

千里寻盯着陈浩宇往出走的身影，不满道：“娘，您都不知道陈浩宇有多坏！昨日我与文渊乘船，遇到他，他故意用船撞我们，我与文渊都掉进水里，全身上下湿透，这仇我迟早会报！”

Qianli Xun stared at Chen Haoyu's departing figure and said discontentedly: "Mother, you don't know how bad Chen Haoyu is! Yesterday, Wenyuan and I were on a boat when we ran into him. He deliberately rammed our boat. Wenyuan and I both fell into the water and got soaked from head to toe. I'll get revenge for this sooner or later!"

---

“唉！这孩子是淘气了些，你既然跟他处不来，以后离他远点儿。”向夫人说得很轻松。

"Sigh! That boy is a bit mischievous. Since you don't get along with him, just stay away from him in the future." Lady Xiang said it lightly.

---

千里寻诧异道：“娘，您怎么能认为他是淘气？他就是彻彻底底的坏人！”

Qianli Xun said in surprise: "Mother, how can you think he's just mischievous? He's thoroughly bad!"

---

“坏人？不至于。”向夫人依然不信。

"Bad? That's going too far." Lady Xiang still didn't believe it.

---

千里寻说：“不信，您问嬷嬷，在竹林时，他是怎么欺负我的。”

Qianli Xun said: "If you don't believe me, ask Nanny Wei. Ask her how he bullied me at the bamboo forest."

---

韦嬷嬷笑道：“夫人，陈家公子的确纨绔，姑娘从他手中救了夏果，他心中不服，买了竹林的田根，又找来打手，不仅把竹林拿走，还让打手跟姑娘打了一架，还好，现在的姑娘有些身手，倒把那打手打得要认姑娘当师傅，呵呵！我想起来都觉得好笑。”

Nanny Wei smiled: "Madam, Young Master Chen is indeed a dandy. After the young lady saved Xiaguo from him, he was unwilling to accept it. He bought the land rights to the bamboo forest and hired thugs. Not only did he take the bamboo forest, but he also had the thugs fight the young lady. Fortunately, the current young lady has some martial skills. She actually beat the thug so badly that he wanted to recognize her as his master. Hehe! It makes me laugh just thinking about it."

---

向夫人琢磨着“现在的姑娘”，她笑不出来：“竟然有这等事？看来陈家公子的确纨绔。不过，韦嬷嬷，你不觉得淑真现在会功夫，实在离奇？”

Lady Xiang pondered the words "the current young lady." She couldn't laugh: "There was such a thing? It seems Young Master Chen is indeed a dandy. But, Nanny Wei, don't you find it strange that Shuzhen now knows martial arts?"

---

韦嬷嬷看一眼姑娘，回道：“当然离奇，但我并不惊讶，不管姑娘变成什么样儿，只要她还在，其他并不重要。”

Nanny Wei glanced at the young lady and replied: "Of course it's strange, but I'm not surprised. No matter what the young lady has become, as long as she's still here, nothing else matters."

---

向夫人点点头：“你说的有道理。淑真，你与以前大不相同，但在娘的眼里，你还是以前的淑真。”

Lady Xiang nodded: "What you say makes sense. Shuzhen, you're very different from before, but in Mother's eyes, you're still the same Shuzhen."

---

千里寻很想对二位长辈说出她是谁，又来自哪里，又担心和盘托出，她们会难过，她不想看到亲人伤心的样子，故而将想要说出的话咽回肚内。

Qianli Xun very much wanted to tell the two elders who she was and where she came from, but worried that if she told everything, they would be sad. She didn't want to see her loved ones hurt, so she swallowed the words she wanted to say.

---

“娘，不管我变成什么样，我都是您的女儿。”

"Mother, no matter what I've become, I'm still your daughter."

---

女儿充满情感的表达，向夫人感到无比幸福，不再计较现在的女儿和以前女儿的不同。

Hearing her daughter's emotional expression, Lady Xiang felt overwhelmingly happy and stopped caring about the differences between her current daughter and her former daughter.

---

千里寻发现她们说话间，陈浩宇已不见身影，她的心情瞬间变好：“我们继续逛吧。”

Qianli Xun noticed that while they were talking, Chen Haoyu had disappeared. Her mood instantly improved: "Let's continue our stroll."

---

正走着，有丫鬟急急匆匆走来，挡住她们的去路：“夫人，姑爷……不对，林练兵官派人来问今日能不能把小姐身边的丫鬟夏果带回林府，林家的轿子已在外面候着。”

As they were walking, a maid came hurriedly and blocked their path: "Madam, the son-in-law...no, Military Training Officer Lin sent someone to ask if Xiaguo, the young lady's maid, can be brought back to the Lin residence today. The Lin family's sedan chair is waiting outside."

---

向夫人问女儿：“淑真，你说该怎么办？”

Lady Xiang asked her daughter: "Shuzhen, what should we do?"

---

千里寻向丫鬟吩咐：“你去给林家来的人说，夏果已不是我的丫鬟，人不在朱府，回乡下去了，林觉想要夏果，直接到乡下去找。”

Qianli Xun instructed the maid: "Go tell the people from the Lin family that Xiaguo is no longer my maid. She's not at the Zhu residence. She's gone back to the countryside. If Lin Jue wants Xiaguo, he can go look for her in the countryside directly."

---

“是。”丫鬟离开，前去回话。

"Yes." The maid left to deliver the message.

---

向夫人担心道：“这样可以吗？”

Lady Xiang worried: "Is this alright?"

---

韦嬷嬷说：“不管可不可以，也只能先这么回练兵官，实在不行时，再见机行事。”

Nanny Wei said: "Whether it's alright or not, we can only reply to the military training officer this way for now. If it really doesn't work, we'll deal with it as the situation demands."

---

千里寻赞道：“还是嬷嬷最了解我。”

Qianli Xun praised: "Nanny Wei knows me best."

---

丫鬟来到朱府侧门，对林家来的下人说：“我家姑娘说了，夏果已不是朱家丫鬟，她回乡下了，林家若还想要夏果，尽可去乡下找。”

The maid came to the side gate of the Zhu residence and told the Lin family's servant: "Our young lady says that Xiaguo is no longer a Zhu family maid. She's gone back to the countryside. If the Lin family still wants Xiaguo, you can go look for her in the countryside."

---

朱府丫鬟说话的气势与别家不同，林府的下人也不敢多问。瞧着那丫鬟进了门，朱府的下人又把门重重关上。林府来的人叹口气，吩咐抬轿子的人原路返回。

The Zhu family maid's tone was different from other families. The Lin family's servant didn't dare ask more. Watching the maid enter the gate, the Zhu family's servant heavily closed the gate again. The person from the Lin family sighed and ordered the sedan chair bearers to return the way they came.

---

正要走，被陈浩宇拦住：“等等！”

Just as they were about to leave, Chen Haoyu stopped them: "Wait!"

---

陈浩宇今日探望姐姐陈文心，顺便从姐姐口中探些有关朱淑真之事，好寻找机会再伺机报复。

Today, Chen Haoyu visited his older sister Chen Wenxin and took the opportunity to probe her for information about Zhu Shuzhen, looking for a chance to retaliate.

---

他从侧门出来，林家的下人刚好抬着轿子过来。陈浩宇站在一旁听了一会儿，才知原委，心想：“我看上的美人儿，没能得手，竟然便宜了林觉这小子，都怪朱淑真，不如在此观察一会儿，看能不能找到报复朱淑真的机会。”

He came out from the side gate just as the Lin family's servants were bringing the sedan chair. Chen Haoyu stood to the side and listened for a while before learning the whole story. He thought: "The beauty I set my sights on, I couldn't get, and it turns out she's gone to that brat Lin Jue. It's all Zhu Shuzhen's fault. Might as well observe here for a while and see if I can find a chance to get revenge on Zhu Shuzhen."

---

林府的下人认识陈浩宇，知道他是纨绔子弟，不敢得罪：“陈公子有何指教？”

The Lin family's servants recognized Chen Haoyu. Knowing he was a dandy, they didn't dare offend him: "Does Young Master Chen have any advice?"

---

陈浩宇笑道：“回去告诉林觉，朱淑真是骗他，夏果那丫头已被她藏起来，具体在哪里我不清楚，但人肯定还在城里。”

Chen Haoyu smiled: "Go back and tell Lin Jue that Zhu Shuzhen is lying to him. That maid Xiaguo has been hidden away by her. I don't know exactly where, but the person is definitely still in the city."

---

林府的下人不敢轻易相信陈浩宇，毕竟他是有名的纨绔子弟：“陈公子怎么如此清楚？”

The Lin family's servants didn't dare believe Chen Haoyu easily. After all, he was a famous dandy: "How does Young Master Chen know so clearly?"

---

陈浩宇说：“我怎么可能不清楚？我刚从朱府出来，是我姐姐告诉我的，她说的能有假？”

Chen Haoyu said: "How could I not know? I just came out from the Zhu residence. My older sister told me. Could what she says be false?"

---

林府下人谢道：“感谢陈公子相告。”

The Lin family's servant thanked: "Thank you for telling us, Young Master Chen."

---

陈浩宇为自己的一次挑拨感到满意，他没有机会收拾朱淑真，给她制造些麻烦也不错。

Chen Haoyu was satisfied with his act of stirring up trouble. Since he had no chance to deal with Zhu Shuzhen himself, causing her some trouble was not bad either.

---

林家下人抬着空轿子回到林府，将情况给林觉说了，又将陈浩宇的原话告知。

The Lin family's servants carried the empty sedan chair back to the Lin residence, told Lin Jue the situation, and relayed Chen Haoyu's exact words.

---

林觉气道：“朱淑真，你为了一个丫鬟，不顾曾经的夫妻情分，别怪我不客气，你不想把夏果给我，我偏要把她弄到手，走着瞧！”

Lin Jue said angrily: "Zhu Shuzhen, for the sake of a mere servant, you've cast aside what little remained of our past kinship. Don't blame me for showing no mercy. You don't want to give me Xiaguo? I'm determined to get her. Just you wait and see!"
