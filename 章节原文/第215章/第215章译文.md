## Chapter 215: Returning from Zhu Manor to Settle Debts

Confronted by the crowd, Chen Haoyu stopped in his tracks. "What are you all doing here?"

Quickest with her tongue, the madam of Chunxiang Brothel stepped up first. "Young Master Chen, word got around that your fierce new wife has locked up your money and cut off your spending. We worried you wouldn't pay up, so we came to collect in person."

One of the three hired fighters spoke next. "You always paid upfront before, Young Master Chen. Yesterday we fought a brawl for you without seeing a coin—look, our faces are still bruised. Isn't it time to settle?"

Chen Haoyu patted his empty sleeves and tried to bluff. "I've been swamped these past few days and haven't had time to draw silver from the shops. Once I finish my errands today, I'll fetch it myself. Go home and stop worrying."

Already tipped off by Chen Haodong, Scarface sidled closer. "Young Master Chen, rumor says every property under your name now belongs to Young Madam. Seems you can't draw silver anymore..."

Spotting Scarface and his crew joining the circus set Chen Haoyu's blood boiling. "What are you lot doing here? Didn't we call it even on your errand money?"

Scarface grinned. "We're not here for silver, Young Master. Just wondering if you're taking us out for fun today."

Being cornered at his own front gate over unpaid bills pushed Chen Haoyu past his limit. "Scram as far as your legs can carry you! I'm in no mood to hang out today!"

Already down from the carriage, Qianli Xun watched the spectacle from the side, growing disgusted at the lowlifes Chen Haoyu kept for company. Still, none of this concerned her—they had a non-interference pact. Turning on her heel, she started through the gates with Kuqiao.

Seeing Chen Haoyu had no silver on him—and hearing Scarface mention that the new wife held his assets—the brothel madam pivoted fast. "Hold on, Young Madam!"

Qianli Xun halted and turned back. "You calling me?"

"Anyone can see you're not the type to leave your husband hanging, Young Madam," the madam wheedled. "Besides, your servants started that brawl with these three men yesterday and smashed up half of Chunxiang Brothel. The damage wasn't cheap. You ought to compensate me."

Qianli Xun eyed the three bruised fighters. "My servants fought you three yesterday?"

"Right here," one fighter confirmed. "Look at the marks still on us."

Qianli Xun turned back to the madam. "If they joined the brawl and broke your furniture, shouldn't you collect from them too? Why come only to me?"

"Young Master Chen promised out loud he'd cover every copper himself," the madam explained.

"If he promised it, go collect from him. Why waste breath on me?" That shut the madam up. Satisfied, Qianli Xun added, "Since my people took part in the fight, I'll pay half the damages. Tally it up. How much silver?"

The madam broke into a bright smile, glad to salvage what she could. "How generous of you, Young Madam! I tallied it up—twenty-five taels of silver in total. What do you say?"

Qianli Xun did not blink. "I'll give you fifteen taels. Collect the other ten from these three."

"Many thanks, Young Madam!" The madam pushed her luck. "One more thing: Young Master Chen spent a full day and night in Chun Jinmo's room. Our girls can't entertain young lords for free, so that comes to another ten taels of silver..."

Qianli Xun bristled, reminded once again what a dud she had married. "Was I the one hiring courtesans? Why are you telling me?"

Done with them, she turned to walk inside.

Chen Haoyu stepped into her path, dropping his voice. "Do me a favor and help me send them packing."

"Pay your own debts," she murmured back, sidestepping him.

He blocked her again, swallowing his pride to whisper, "What if I take an advance on my monthly allowance?"

That sounded workable. "How much did you promise those three?"

"Ten taels each."

"Ten taels? Think Chen silver blows in on the wind?"

"Ten taels is cheap! I used to pay Lu Yuanfeng fifty!"

Qianli Xun raised a fist, then checked herself midair. With half the street watching, he was still her husband on paper; no sense humiliating him further or leaving creditors camped outside the Chen gates. "Thirty taels plus the fifteen makes forty-five..."

"Plus ten for the courtesan," Chen Haoyu muttered.

Her fist twitched again before she forced it down. "Fifty-five taels, every single copper docked from your allowance."

"Hey," he haggled under his breath, "fifteen of those taels were your servants' doing. You can't pin that on me."

"Keep haggling and I walk inside right now."

"Fine, fine, you win! Have it your way."

Chen Haoyu cursed himself all over again for signing away everything just to get that gambling slip back. Overnight his life had plunged from heaven to mud, boxed in on every side. He needed a way out, fast.

Qianli Xun sent Kuqiao inside for fifty-five taels of silver and paid off the three fighters and the madam on the spot.

"Listen well, all of you," she announced before leaving. "From today on, any debt Chen Haoyu runs up is none of my concern." Leaving him standing there, she marched inside with Kuqiao.

The madam wasted no time stepping up to the three fighters with an open palm. "Now that you've been paid, gentlemen, cough up the ten taels for Chunxiang Brothel's damages."

Knowing the brothel was not to be crossed, the three begrudgingly dug out silver until ten taels sat in her hand, sending her off satisfied.

Chen Haoyu called after the three fighters. "You took my silver, so you're still guarding me whenever I go out."

"We lost over three taels apiece paying for a mess you should've covered," one fighter countered. "Your ten taels bought a month before; now you get half a month."

"Fine, half a month," Chen Haoyu waved it off. "Just show up on call whenever I head out."

"Need us today?"

Empty-pocketed and needing to sort things out indoors, Chen Haoyu shook his head. "Not today. That visit with my wife wore me out. I'll summon you once I've rested."

With a chorus of "See you tomorrow," the three bodyguards left.

Scarface and his crew had lurked nearby sizing up the situation, wondering if Chen Haoyu was still worth leeching off. His funds were tight, sure, yet a starved camel still outweighed a horse. They hurried over before he could slip inside. "Going out for fun, Young Master Chen?"

"Didn't you hear me? I'm tired today. Not going out."

"First time I've ever heard Young Master Chen say he's too tired to party," Scarface baited him. "Don't tell us you're broke and scared to treat the boys?"

Stung where it hurt, Chen Haoyu puffed out his chest. "Do I look like a man short of silver? Wait right outside the gate. I'll be out in a minute to take you all out!"

"That's what we like to hear!" the hoodlums cheered.

Leaving Scarface's gang lounging outside the gates, Chen Haoyu hurried back inside to scare up some cash. His first stop would be Steward Fang at Xinglong Pavilion, where he had always drawn silver from his shops.

Trailing behind him was Qinggua, whom he had barely noticed during the commotion at the gate. Now the boy looked like an eyesore. Chen Haoyu swung a foot at him. "Still tailing me? Get lost!"

Qinggua hopped aside, grinning. "I'm your personal servant. Where else would I go?"

"Get back to Moxiang Courtyard and practice your martial arts! Follow me another step and I'll kick you into next week!"

Qinggua stopped in his tracks, watching his master march toward Xinglong Pavilion, and muttered, "No money makes Young Master's temper even nastier. Better keep my distance."

Inside Xinglong Pavilion, clerks bustled about as usual. Chen Haoyu ignored them and headed straight for Steward Fang, who sat bent over a ledger. "Busy?"

Looking up, Steward Fang rose at once. "Second Young Master! Please, have a seat."

Chen Haoyu plopped into a chair without ceremony. "No fancy errands—you know the drill. Draw me two hundred taels like usual."

Steward Fang stayed on his feet, bowing slightly with a polite smile. "Drawing silver is no trouble at all, Second Young Master. I can handle it right away—provided you bring a written order signed by Young Madam's own hand and stamped with her private seal."

"All that red tape?" Chen Haoyu groaned. "Should never have signed my properties over to her."

Steward Fang kept smiling. "Because you dote on Young Madam, of course you hand her the best of everything. I'm sure she won't make things difficult. Bring her authorization and I'll hand over the silver right away."

Dote on her my foot! I had no choice! Chen Haoyu fumed inside, saying aloud, "Why bother with paper? Just hand me the silver."

"Forgive me, Second Young Master. Eldest Master laid down that rule, and I dare not break it."

Pulling his father's name out left Chen Haoyu stuck. He got up. "Fine, wait here. I'll go get her note."

He stalked out knowing full well he dared not ask Zhu Shuzhen for a copper. Instead, he headed straight for his grandmother—both to stir up trouble for Zhu Shuzhen and to coax out some spending money.

Old Madam Xia had just woken from her nap. Seeing the afternoon sun slanting west, she figured her grandson should be back from Zhu Manor, and right on cue his voice rang out.

"Grandmother! Grandmother! I came to see you!"

Even before he crossed the threshold, the old lady beamed. The moment he stepped inside, her face broke into wrinkles of delight. "You're back! Come sit right beside me!"

Chen Haoyu slid onto the couch and threw an arm around her. "Grandmother, I wish I could hug you like this every single day."

"Sweet-talking me again." She did not believe a word, yet she glowed all the same. "If your father had half your knack for cheering me up, I wouldn't stay mad at him all the time."

Chen Haoyu poured it on thicker. "Father puts all his heart into Chen business and my mother. Only I save all my heart for you, Grandmother."

Glowing from ear to ear, she patted his arm. "Stop hugging me and sit across so we can talk."

Chen Haoyu let go and settled across from her.

"How was the return visit at Zhu Manor?" she asked with concern. "Did they give you a hard time?"

"Landing a son-in-law like me is a blessing the Zhus earned in a past life! They were overjoyed—why on earth would they give me a hard time?"

Old Madam Xia tapped his wrist with a laugh. "More nonsense! Did you forget why your father-in-law forced you to marry the Zhu girl?"

"A real hero doesn't rehash ancient battles. Please drop that subject, Grandmother. If the tigress overhears, I'll never hear the end of it."

Wanting to coax out more silver, Chen Haoyu kept her laughing and switched tracks. "Grandmother, I pray every day for your health and long life. At Zhu Manor today, I even uncovered the longevity secret of their Old Ancestor. Want to hear it?"

Certain her grandson was spinning another yarn, Old Madam Xia played along. "Of course I want to hear it."

Chen Haoyu leaned in, lowering his voice to a conspiratorial whisper. "I wandered around the Zhu Buddhist shrine for ages, and all I heard inside was the clack of a wooden fish drum and chanting. I figured their Old Ancestor must have lived so long from tapping a wooden fish and reciting sutras every day. How about I buy you a wooden fish and a string of prayer beads tomorrow so you can try it too?"

"Enough of that. I'm not cut out for chanting sutras. Just come visit me every day and keep me company. When I'm happy, health and longevity follow on their own."

"Deal! I'll visit you every day." Looking for a way to sic his grandmother on Zhu Shuzhen, Chen Haoyu put on a pitiful face. "Grandmother, you have to help me."

"Help you with what?"

"Zhu Shuzhen bullies me."

"How does she bully you?"

"She dismissed all my maids, took over my courtyard, seized my properties, and put me on a twenty-tael monthly allowance. I'm practically a pauper now! Nobody outside even believes I belong to the Chen family."

Old Madam Xia chuckled. "Your bride has a razor-sharp tongue. Even I can't outtalk her. Besides, who told you to drop to your knees and beg her to marry you, or sign some prenuptial contract? Blame yourself for gambling with Master Jiang and nearly throwing away the entire Chen fortune."

Chen Haoyu blinked. "How did you find out about that?"

"Your new bride told me. Your father and mother know too."

Zhu Shuzhen! Chen Haoyu cursed under his breath. Not a shred of face left for me in front of my own family! Just you wait!

"Grandmother, the gambling was my fault, and I swear I'll never touch it again. Still, you have to put Zhu Shuzhen in her place. She's walking all over me."

"How am I supposed to do that?"

"Find excuses to give her trouble whenever you're free."

"She's your wife now. Giving her trouble means giving you trouble."

"Grandmother, we're husband and wife in name only."

A crease of sorrow crossed Old Madam Xia's brow. "My grandson finally takes a wife, only to live like this. It breaks my heart just thinking about it."

Chen Haoyu hurried to soothe her. "Don't be sad, Grandmother. Sooner or later, I'll marry someone you actually approve of."

"I'm quite satisfied with the bride you have right now," Old Madam Xia said from the heart.

Seeing his plot to stir up his grandmother flop, Chen Haoyu cut straight to the silver. "Grandmother, Zhu Shuzhen won't give me a single coin, and I don't dare ask Father or Mother. Could you spare me a little spending money?"

When it came to her favorite grandson, Old Madam Xia never held back. She had a maid bring a wooden coffer and drew out a five-hundred-tael banknote. "Spend it with care. Not a copper on courtesans or gamblers, understand?"

Overjoyed, Chen Haoyu snatched the note and planted kisses all over his grandmother's cheeks until she beamed from ear to ear. After a few more minutes of banter, he tucked the banknote into his robe, hurried out the front gates, and swept Scarface and his crew off for another round of revelry.
