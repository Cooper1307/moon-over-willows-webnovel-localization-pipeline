# 第51章 夏果不愿当妾室

---

一桌子菜，林觉一口没动。他最后的提议搞得千里寻也毫无胃口，对门外喊道：“你们两个进来吧！”

A table full of dishes, Lin Jue hadn't touched a single bite. His final proposal had completely ruined Qianli Xun's appetite. She shouted toward the door: "You two, come in!"

---

苦荞与夏果进屋后，千里寻让她们坐下跟自己一起吃。两人高兴地坐下，拿起筷子吃起来。

After Kuqiao and Xiaguo entered the room, Qianli Xun asked them to sit down and eat with her. The two happily sat down, picked up their chopsticks, and started eating.

---

千里寻瞧着夏果，不知如何开口，总觉得用一个丫鬟交换自己的幸福太不仁义。

Qianli Xun looked at Xiaguo, not knowing how to bring it up. She felt it was too unjust to trade a maid for her own happiness.

---

又一想，林觉大小也是练兵官，林家在临安也算有些地位，像夏果这等乡下丫头能给林觉做妾室应该不错。

Then she thought again—Lin Jue was, after all, a drill commander, and the Lin family held some status in Lin'an. A country girl like Xiaguo becoming Lin Jue's concubine should be a good match.

---

于是大胆说：“夏果，有件事我需征求你的意思……”

So she boldly said: "Xiaguo, there's something I need to ask your opinion about..."

---

“小姐请说。”夏果放下筷子。

"Please speak, Young Lady." Xiaguo put down her chopsticks.

---

“嗯……我刚与林觉聊得不错，他答应与我和离，但提出一个要求，让我把你送给他……”

“Well... I just had a good chat with Lin Jue. He agreed to the mutual divorce, but he made one request—to have you given to him...”

---

夏果垂下眼帘：“可是给主君做妾室？”

Xiaguo lowered her eyes: "To become the Lord's concubine?"

---

千里寻点点头：“我跟他和离后，他身边就你一人，说不定将来会把你扶正。”

Qianli Xun nodded: "After I divorce him, you'll be the only one by his side. Who knows? He might promote you to primary wife in the future."

---

夏果依然垂着眼帘：“小姐不要哄我，我是乡下丫头，除长得好看些，其他都上不了台面，给主君当妾岂有被扶正的机会？”

Xiaguo still kept her eyes lowered: "Don't try to fool me, Young Lady. I'm just a country girl. Apart from being good-looking, I have nothing else going for me. How could a concubine like me ever have a chance to be promoted?"

---

夏果跪在地上：“小姐，我爷爷说过，宁可嫁给穷人做妻，也不嫁给富人当妾，还望小姐不要把我送给主君。”

Xiaguo knelt on the ground: "Young Lady, my grandfather used to say, 'Rather be a poor man's wife than a rich man's concubine.' I beg you, please don't give me to the Lord."

---

夏果这么一跪，千里寻更为难：“你起来吧，坐下边吃边说。”

With Xiaguo kneeling like this, Qianli Xun felt even more troubled: "Get up. Sit down and eat while we talk."

---

夏果很坚决：“小姐若不答应，我便长跪不起。”

Xiaguo was resolute: "If you don't agree, Young Lady, I'll stay kneeling and won't get up."

---

苦荞劝道：“你都说自己是乡下丫头，能嫁给姑爷当妾已不错，就别再为难小姐。”

Kuqiao tried to persuade her: "You've already said you're just a country girl. Being able to marry the Young Master as a concubine is already good enough. Don't make things difficult for Young Lady anymore."

---

夏果反问道：“苦荞姐姐既觉得当妾室不错，你为何不去？”

Xiaguo countered: "If you think being a concubine is so great, Sister Kuqiao, why don't you go?"

---

苦荞说：“可惜姑爷没瞧上我，如若姑爷瞧上我，我义不容辞，不为别的，只为小姐能顺利与姑爷和离。”

Kuqiao said: “It's a pity the Young Master didn't take a liking to me. If he had, I wouldn't hesitate—not for any other reason, but just so Young Lady could smoothly divorce him.”

---

夏果冷笑道：“苦荞姐姐没摊上事儿，只管说大话，如若主君选中你，我不相信你会拿自己的婚姻去赌？”

Xiaguo sneered: "Sister Kuqiao, you're not the one in trouble, so you can talk big. But if the Lord had chosen you, I don't believe you'd gamble your own marriage on it."

---

夏果态度坚决，千里寻不忍心将她强行送给林觉，怕她跟了林觉会成为第二个朱淑真，到时再被林觉家暴，她千里寻会一辈子内疚。

Xiaguo's attitude was firm. Qianli Xun couldn't bear to force her to Lin Jue, afraid she'd become a second Zhu Shuzhen. If Lin Jue abused her then, Qianli Xun would feel guilty for the rest of her life.

---

不会把夏果送给林觉的话还未出口，听到韦嬷嬷的声音：“如若苦荞遇到同样的事，为了姑娘，她绝对会答应！”

Before Qianli Xun could say she wouldn't give Xiaguo to Lin Jue, she heard Aunt Wei's voice: "If Kuqiao were in the same situation, for the sake of Young Lady, she would absolutely agree!"

---

千里寻招呼韦嬷嬷：“嬷嬷坐下一起吃。”

Qianli Xun greeted Aunt Wei: "Auntie, sit down and eat with us."

---

韦嬷嬷挑位置坐下：“我已在屋里用过饭，出来时碰到姑爷，与他聊了两句，得知他的想法，便过来瞧瞧。”

Aunt Wei chose a seat and sat down: "I've already eaten in my room. On the way out, I ran into the Young Master and chatted with him for a bit. I learned his thoughts, so I came over to check."

---

韦嬷嬷对夏果说：“姑娘对你有恩，你报答的机会就在眼前，给姑爷当妾，你一点儿都不亏，大不了我替姑娘做主，多给你些嫁妆，让你当个有钱的妾室。”

Aunt Wei said to Xiaguo: “Young Lady has been kind to you. Your chance to repay her is right here. Becoming the Young Master's concubine—you won't lose out at all. At most, I'll make the decision for Young Lady and give you a generous dowry, so you can be a wealthy concubine.”

---

夏果心想，小姐想要甩开的人却硬塞给自己，说不定主君有什么无法对外人提起的隐疾，她决不能将个人幸福随意葬送，凭她的容貌能嫁个更好的。再说她刚进城里，什么都没见识过，也没体验过，人生就被定格，她心有不甘。

Xiaguo thought to herself: The person Young Lady wants to get rid of is being forced on her. Who knows? Maybe the Lord has some hidden illness he can't mention to outsiders. She absolutely couldn't bury her own happiness like this. With her looks, she could marry someone better. Besides, she'd just entered the city—hadn't seen or experienced anything yet. Her life would be set in stone, and she wasn't about to accept that.

---

“正因感恩小姐救我，才下定决心一辈子不嫁，陪在小姐身边伺候她。韦嬷嬷说的嫁妆我不稀罕，跟着小姐不愁吃喝，当丫鬟我心满意足。”

"Precisely because I'm grateful Young Lady saved me, I've made up my mind never to marry. I'll stay by Young Lady's side and serve her for life. I don't care about the dowry Aunt Wei mentioned. Following Young Lady, I won't worry about food or clothing. Being a maid is more than enough for me."

---

韦嬷嬷还打算继续批评夏果，被千里寻拦住：“既然夏果不愿意，不必为难她。你起来吧，跟苦荞把酒菜端到丫鬟房慢慢吃，我想静一静，想些事情。”

Aunt Wei was about to continue criticizing Xiaguo, but Qianli Xun stopped her: "Since Xiaguo is unwilling, don't force her. Get up. Take the food and wine with Kuqiao to the maid's quarters and eat slowly. I want some quiet time to think things over."

---

夏果感谢完小姐，起身与苦荞一起将酒菜收拾到两个食盒中，提去丫鬟房。

After thanking Young Lady, Xiaguo got up and helped Kuqiao pack the food and wine into two tiered food carriers, then carried them to the maid's quarters.

---

韦嬷嬷劝道：“姑娘就是心软，看不得他人委屈。可姑娘也不应被夏果蒙蔽，她嘴上说想陪姑娘一辈子，心里可不一定这么想，说不定还想嫁给更好的人家当正室。”

Aunt Wei advised: "Young Lady is just too soft-hearted. You can't bear to see others wronged. But you shouldn't be deceived by Xiaguo either. She says she wants to stay with you for life, but she might not really think that way. Who knows? She might still want to marry into a better family as the primary wife."

---

“她有这想法倒也正常，如若心不甘情不愿嫁给林觉，像我一样遭遇家暴，岂不是令我一辈子不安心？”

"It's normal for her to have that thought. If she married Lin Jue unwillingly and suffered domestic violence like I did, wouldn't that make me feel guilty for the rest of my life?"

---

“按说丫鬟的人生大事掌握在主子手中，主子想把她嫁给谁，她不能说任何反对的话，主要是姑娘心善，才会处处替丫鬟们考虑。唉，姑娘无论做什么决定，我都支持，我这就去找姑爷说说此事。”

"Technically, a maid's life decisions are in the master's hands. Whoever the master wants to marry her off to, she can't object. It's mainly because Young Lady is kind-hearted that you consider things from the maids' perspective at every turn. Sigh. Whatever decision you make, Young Lady, I'll support it. I'll go talk to the Young Master about this right now."

---

“有劳嬷嬷。”

"Thank you, Auntie."

---

韦嬷嬷走后，千里寻陷入沉思，和离之事刚有眉目，又出了夏果这等事。如果没有收夏果当丫鬟，林觉就没有机会以此为条件再次为难她，真应了韦嬷嬷在竹林说过的话。

After Aunt Wei left, Qianli Xun fell into deep thought. The divorce matter had just shown some progress, and now this issue with Xiaguo had come up. If she hadn't taken Xiaguo in as a maid, Lin Jue wouldn't have had the chance to use this as a condition to trouble her again. Aunt Wei's words in the bamboo forest had truly come true.

---

韦嬷嬷来到主屋拜访林觉，林觉倒是对韦嬷嬷敬重，只因这位嬷嬷说话做事令他佩服，看人的眼光也独到，比很多男子都厉害。他家暴朱淑真时，每次都选韦嬷嬷不在才敢动手，韦嬷嬷那双眼睛太过犀利，只要被她看一眼，他的拳头都打不下去。

Aunt Wei came to the main hall to visit Lin Jue. Lin Jue actually respected the old nanny. Her words and actions impressed him. Her way of reading people was unique, sharper than many men. When he abused Zhu Shuzhen, he always waited until Aunt Wei wasn't around before daring to act. Those eyes of hers were too sharp. Just one look from her, and his fists couldn't go through.

---

林觉请韦嬷嬷坐下，不敢接触她的眼睛，怕那双眼睛看穿他心中所想：“不知嬷嬷前来有何指教？”

Lin Jue asked Aunt Wei to sit down, not daring to meet her eyes, afraid those eyes would see through his thoughts: "I wonder what instructions Auntie has brought?"

---

“指教谈不上，我是来替姑娘回个话，夏果那丫头想跟着姑娘一辈子，不愿嫁人，还请姑爷另觅妾室。”

"No instructions. I'm just here to reply on behalf of Young Lady. That girl Xiaguo wants to follow Young Lady for life and doesn't want to marry. Please find another concubine, Young Master."

---

韦嬷嬷的话如同当头一棒，他林觉的感情之路为何如此不顺？看上朱淑真，娶了她，日子过得糟心不说，还闹到和离。现在好不容易有个心仪的丫鬟，结果又被拒绝，让他情何以堪？

Aunt Wei's words were like a bolt from the blue. Why was Lin Jue's romantic path so unlucky? He had taken a liking to Zhu Shuzhen and married her. Not only was life miserable, but it had even come to divorce. Now, when he finally had a maid he liked, she rejected him too. How could he bear it?

---

一生气，也不顾及韦嬷嬷那双眼睛，大胆说：“既如此，和离之事免谈。”

In anger, he didn't care about Aunt Wei's sharp eyes anymore and boldly said: "In that case, forget about the divorce."

---

韦嬷嬷猜到林觉会如此说，早已准备好应对之词：“我来也是向姑爷招呼一声，和离不和离无所谓，我在外看好宅子，也付了定金，不管姑爷同不同意和离，我家姑娘很快会搬出去。”

Aunt Wei had guessed Lin Jue would say this and was already prepared: “I'm also here to let Young Master know—it doesn't matter whether there's a divorce or not. I've already found a good residence outside and paid the deposit. Whether Young Master agrees to the divorce or not, Young Lady will move out soon.”

---

林觉更像是被泼了一盆冷水，从外到里冰得透心凉：“这么快宅子都买好了？岳父大人可知此事？你们这么做，朱府的颜面何存？”

Lin Jue felt like he'd been doused with a bucket of cold water, chilled to the bone: "You've already bought a house so quickly? Does my father-in-law know about this? With you doing this, where would the Zhu family's face be?"

---

“既然朱府已将女儿嫁给林家，朱家老爷知不知道此事不重要。姑娘已是林家人，搬出去住与朱家颜面无关，丢得只会是林家的人。”

"Since the Zhu family has already married their daughter into the Lin family, whether Young Master Zhu knows about this doesn't matter. Young Lady is already a Lin family member. Moving out has nothing to do with the Zhu family's face. The only one losing face would be the Lin family."

---

韦嬷嬷句句戳中林觉的心窝，可林觉不敢大发雷霆：“我只是想要个丫鬟，朱淑真为何不能满足我？只要把夏果给我，大家都高兴，嬷嬷您说是也不是？您再帮我劝劝她，就让她把夏果给我得了。”

Every word from Aunt Wei struck Lin Jue's heart, but he didn't dare fly into a rage: "I just want a maid. Why can't Zhu Shuzhen satisfy me? As long as she gives me Xiaguo, everyone will be happy. Isn't that right, Auntie? Please help me persuade her again. Just let her give Xiaguo to me."

---

韦嬷嬷叹口气：“唉！姑娘当然愿意，夏果不乐意，姑娘心又善，不愿强人所难。我给姑爷出个主意，不如私下多与夏果接触接触，说不定能培养出感情来，有了感情，她自然乐意。”

Aunt Wei sighed: “Of course Young Lady is willing, but Xiaguo isn't. Young Lady is kind-hearted and doesn't want to force anyone. Let me give Young Master a piece of advice—why not privately spend more time with Xiaguo? You might be able to develop feelings. Once feelings develop, she'll be willing on her own.”

---

林觉立刻乐道：“嬷嬷的主意甚好，那我就试试，不过可不能这么快搬出去，否则我与那丫头怎么培养感情？”

Lin Jue immediately cheered up: "Auntie's idea is excellent. I'll give it a try. But you can't move out so quickly. Otherwise, how can I develop feelings with that girl?"

---

韦嬷嬷笑道：“那是自然。”

Aunt Wei smiled: "Of course."

---

千里寻独自想了一会儿，品着净光法师说的四个字“正思正念”，她决定不能将个人的幸福凌驾于他人不幸之上，尊重夏果的想法，才是正确的选择。想通之后，她到书桌前坐下，开始提笔写话本。

Qianli Xun thought alone for a while, pondering the four characters Master Jingguang had said—“Right Thought and Right Mindfulness.” She decided she couldn't place her personal happiness above another's misfortune. Respecting Xiaguo's wishes was the right choice. After figuring this out, she sat down at her desk and picked up her brush to start writing her story script.

---

苦荞拿着请帖进来：“小姐，乔公子已从汴梁回来，听说您从乡下回到林府，差南双送来请帖，邀请您参加诗会。”

Kuqiao came in holding an invitation card: "Young Lady, Young Master Qiao has returned from Bianliang. He heard you've come back from the countryside to the Lin residence. He sent Nan Shuang to deliver this invitation, inviting you to a poetry gathering."

---

千里寻放下笔，接过请帖打开，看到地点是在西湖边的观景亭，落款写着乔文仙。既是朱淑真的知己邀请，她必然要替朱淑真前去赴约，看看时间，是明日午时。

Qianli Xun put down her brush, took the invitation, and opened it. She saw the location was the Sightseeing Pavilion by West Lake, and the signature read Qiao Wenxian. Since it was an invitation from Zhu Shuzhen's close friend, she naturally had to go on Zhu Shuzhen's behalf. She checked the time—it was tomorrow at noon.

---

“你去告诉送请帖之人，明日我会按时参加。”

"Go tell the person who delivered the invitation that I'll attend on time tomorrow."

---

“我已替小姐应下。”

"I've already accepted on your behalf, Young Lady."

---

千里寻瞅一眼苦荞：“什么时候轮到你替我做主？”

Qianli Xun glanced at Kuqiao: "When did it become your turn to make decisions for me?"

---

“乔公子相邀，小姐从来不会拒绝，必然参加，有时我在想，如果小姐能和乔公子在一起那该多好。”

"Young Master Qiao invited you. Young Lady never refuses and always attends. Sometimes I think, it would be so great if Young Lady could be with Young Master Qiao."

---

“你不是说我以前心里有徐公子，怎么又希望我跟乔公子在一起？”

"Didn't you say I had Young Master Xu in my heart before? Why do you now hope I'll be with Young Master Qiao?"

---

“以前虽希望您跟徐公子，可他娶了别人，所以就想到乔公子。”

"Before, I did hope you'd be with Young Master Xu, but he married someone else. So I thought of Young Master Qiao."

---

“别瞎想，你家小姐我有婚姻挡在面前，再想其他男子会被人说成有失妇德，你竟然还敢说希望我与乔公子在一起的话？该打！”

"Don't think nonsense. Your Young Lady still has a marriage standing in the way. Thinking of other men would be scandalous for a married woman. You actually dare to say you hope I'll be with Young Master Qiao? You deserve a beating!"

---

苦荞乐道：“我宁可被打，也要说出希望小姐好的话，反正小姐跟姑爷和离是迟早的事，在我心里失忆后的小姐已是自由身，所以替您畅想一下未来。”

Kuqiao laughed: "I'd rather be beaten than keep quiet. Anyway, Young Lady divorcing the Young Master is just a matter of time. In my heart, you're already a free woman after losing your memory. So I'm just daydreaming about your future a little."

---

别说苦荞这么认为，千里寻穿越成为朱淑真，从来没把这段婚姻当回事，在她心里又何尝不认为自己是自由之身？

Not to mention Kuqiao thought this way. Ever since Qianli Xun transmigrated and became Zhu Shuzhen, she had never taken this marriage seriously. In her heart, didn't she also consider herself free?

---

韦嬷嬷走后，林觉思忖着如何能与夏果培养感情，让她心甘情愿给他做妾。思来想去，只觉良家女子实在麻烦，不像商妓，无需费心费力。可想起夏果的身段与容貌不比朱淑真差，费些心思倒也值得。

After Aunt Wei left, Lin Jue pondered how he could develop feelings with Xiaguo and make her willingly become his concubine. After thinking it over and over, he felt that decent women were really troublesome, unlike merchant prostitutes who didn't require effort. But thinking of Xiaguo's figure and looks, which were no worse than Zhu Shuzhen's, it was worth putting in some effort.

---

于是叫进一名下人：“去，把夫人新收的丫鬟夏果给我找来，就说我找她问些事情。”

So he called in a servant: "Go, find the maid Xiaguo that the young lady recently took in. Tell her I want to ask her about something."

---

不一会儿夏果前来，已猜到主君之意，不如趁此机会表明想法，让他死了这份心思。

Before long, Xiaguo came. She had already guessed the Lord's intentions. She might as well take this opportunity to make her thoughts clear and make him give up this idea.

---

夏果走到主屋门口，向里问：“主君找我？”

Xiaguo walked to the door of the main hall and asked inside: "The Lord summoned me?"

---

传来林觉的声音：“进来吧。”

Lin Jue's voice came: "Come in."

---

夏果走进去，不敢抬头，一直盯着自己的脚尖。

Xiaguo walked in, not daring to look up, always staring at her toes.

---

林觉越看越喜欢，站起来，绕着夏果将她上下打量好几遍。

The more Lin Jue looked, the more he liked her. He stood up and walked around Xiaguo, looking her up and down several times.

---

夏果心里发毛，担心主君非礼她：“主君找我什么事？”

Xiaguo felt uneasy inside, worried the Lord might molest her: "What did the Lord want to see me about?"

---

林觉在夏果面前站定，直接问：“你为何不愿跟我？”

Lin Jue stopped in front of Xiaguo and asked directly: "Why don't you want to be with me?"

---

果然是为此事，夏果回道：“我身份卑微，配不上主君……”

As expected, it was about this matter. Xiaguo replied: "My status is low. I'm not worthy of the Lord..."

---

“我不嫌弃。”

"I don't mind."

---

“我不想给人当妾室。”

"I don't want to be anyone's concubine."

---

林觉想了一会儿，说道：“我很快与朱淑真和离，身边没有正室，你若当妾室把我伺候得好，我一高兴，没准儿会将你扶正。”

Lin Jue thought for a while and said: "I'll soon divorce Zhu Shuzhen. I won't have a primary wife by my side. If you become a concubine and serve me well, when I'm happy, I might promote you to primary wife."

---

夏果说：“我要是嫁人，必须是明媒正娶的当家主母，才不愿意先当妾室，慢慢等着被扶正，再说主君的话能有几分可信？”

Xiaguo said: "If I marry, it must be as the legally wed head wife. I'm not willing to be a concubine first and slowly wait to be promoted. Besides, how much of the Lord's words can I trust?"

---

Xiaguo's eyes were truly beautiful, her features extremely pleasing to look at. Staring at them made Lin Jue's heart itch. He couldn't help but grab Xiaguo in his arms: "Just be with me. I'll never treat you badly. Let's go to the inner room now, and I'll let you experience how good I am."

林觉毕竟是练兵官，劲大，把夏果抱住，她不得挣脱，还被对方在脸上亲了两口。

林觉什么都顾不上，使劲儿把夏果抱起来，向里屋走去。

Lin Jue didn't care about anything anymore. He forcefully picked Xiaguo up and walked toward the inner room.

夏果在林觉身上又捶又打：“你放开我！快放开我！……”

林觉哪里肯放？一心只想从夏果的身上体验从未在朱淑真身上体验过的感觉。

How could Lin Jue let go? All he wanted was to experience on Xiaguo the feelings he had never experienced with Zhu Shuzhen.

When Zhu Shuzhen married him, she was beautiful but cold, and even threatened suicide. Plus, her father was the Lin'an prefect. Lin Jue didn't dare act rashly. After he beat her unconscious and she woke up, Zhu Shuzhen actually knew martial arts. Lin Jue couldn't beat her at all, and he certainly didn't dare take liberties with her.

可夏果身后没靠山，人也不冷漠，还柔柔弱弱，重点是长得跟朱淑真一样貌美，激起林觉霸占的欲望。
