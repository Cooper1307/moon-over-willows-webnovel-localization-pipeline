第51章 夏果不愿当妾室
## Chapter 51: Chapter 51: Xiaguo Refuses to Be a Concubine

一桌子菜，林觉一口没动。他最后的提议搞得千里寻也毫无胃口，对门外喊道：“你们两个进来吧！”
Not a single dish touched by Lin Jue—and his parting demand killed Qianli Xun's appetite cold too! Calling toward the doorway: "You two come inside!"

苦荞与夏果进屋后，千里寻让她们坐下跟自己一起吃。两人高兴地坐下，拿起筷子吃起来。
Stepping in, Kuqiao and Xiaguo gladly obeyed her gesture to sit and eat alongside her, picking up chopsticks right away.

千里寻瞧着夏果，不知如何开口，总觉得用一个丫鬟交换自己的幸福太不仁义。
Eyeing Xiaguo, Qianli Xun wrestled with how to broach it—trading a maid for her own freedom felt awfully shady!

又一想，林觉大小也是练兵官，林家在临安也算有些地位，像夏果这等乡下丫头能给林觉做妾室应该不错。
Still, Lin Jue was Lin'an's Training Officer from a respectable house; for a poor country girl like Xiaguo, becoming his concubine might mean security for life.

于是大胆说：“夏果，有件事我需征求你的意思……”
Biting the bullet, she spoke gentle: "Xiaguo, need to ask your honest opinion on something..."

“小姐请说。”夏果放下筷子。
Setting down her chopsticks, Xiaguo answered attentive: "Speak, Miss."

“嗯……我刚与林觉聊得不错，他答应与我和离，但提出一个要求，让我把你送给他……”
"Well... just talked things over with Lin Jue—he agreed to grant me a mutual divorce, on one condition: that I give you to him..."

夏果垂下眼帘：“可是给主君做妾室？”
Lowering her lashes, Xiaguo asked quiet: "As Lord's concubine?"

千里寻点点头：“我跟他和离后，他身边就你一人，说不定将来会把你扶正。”
Qianli Xun nodded: "Once I leave, you'd be the only woman beside him—who knows, he might even promote you to primary wife someday!"

夏果依然垂着眼帘：“小姐不要哄我，我是乡下丫头，除长得好看些，其他都上不了台面，给主君当妾岂有被扶正的机会？”
Eyes still downcast, Xiaguo murmured: "Don't sugarcoat it, Miss—I'm a country girl with nothing going for me except my face. How could a concubine ever get promoted to primary wife?"

夏果跪在地上：“小姐，我爷爷说过，宁可嫁给穷人做妻，也不嫁给富人当妾，还望小姐不要把我送给主君。”
Dropping straight to her knees on the floorboards, she pleaded: "My grandfather always taught me: rather be a poor man's wife than a rich man's concubine! Please don't hand me over to Lord, Miss!"

夏果这么一跪，千里寻更为难：“你起来吧，坐下边吃边说。”
Seeing her kneel tied Qianli Xun in tighter knots: "Get up—sit back down and talk while we eat."

夏果很坚决：“小姐若不答应，我便长跪不起。”
Iron-firm: "Until Miss promises, I'm staying on my knees!"

苦荞劝道：“你都说自己是乡下丫头，能嫁给姑爷当妾已不错，就别再为难小姐。”
Kuqiao jumped in nudging her: "You called yourself a country girl—becoming Son-in-law's concubine is a huge step up! Stop putting Miss in a bind!"

夏果反问道：“苦荞姐姐既觉得当妾室不错，你为何不去？”
Xiaguo fired right back sharp: "If Sister Kuqiao thinks being a concubine is so grand, why don't YOU go?!"

苦荞说：“可惜姑爷没瞧上我，如若姑爷瞧上我，我义不容辞，不为别的，只为小姐能顺利与姑爷和离。”
"Too bad Son-in-law didn't pick me! Had he asked for me, I'd say yes in a heartbeat—just to buy Miss her mutual divorce!" Kuqiao shot back.

夏果冷笑道：“苦荞姐姐没摊上事儿，只管说大话，如若主君选中你，我不相信你会拿自己的婚姻去赌？”
Xiaguo let out a bitter laugh: "Easy talking big when it isn't your neck on the block! If Lord really picked you, bet you wouldn't gamble your whole life away!"

夏果态度坚决，千里寻不忍心将她强行送给林觉，怕她跟了林觉会成为第二个朱淑真，到时再被林觉家暴，她千里寻会一辈子内疚。
Seeing Xiaguo dead set against it, Qianli Xun couldn't bear forcing her—what if Xiaguo ended up Zhu Shuzhen number two, beaten behind closed doors? Qianli Xun's conscience would eat her alive forever!

不会把夏果送给林觉的话还未出口，听到韦嬷嬷的声音：“如若苦荞遇到同样的事，为了姑娘，她绝对会答应！”
Just as she opened her mouth to promise she'd never hand her over, Matron Wei's voice cut in from the doorway: "Were Kuqiao in your shoes, she WOULD say yes for our young lady—without blinking!"

千里寻招呼韦嬷嬷：“嬷嬷坐下一起吃。”
"Sit and eat with us, Matron," Qianli Xun invited.

韦嬷嬷挑位置坐下：“我已在屋里用过饭，出来时碰到姑爷，与他聊了两句，得知他的想法，便过来瞧瞧。”
Taking a chair, Matron Wei said: "Already ate in my room—bumped into Son-in-law walking out, chatted two words, heard his demand, and came straight over."

韦嬷嬷对夏果说：“姑娘对你有恩，你报答的机会就在眼前，给姑爷当妾，你一点儿都不亏，大不了我替姑娘做主，多给你些嫁妆，让你当个有钱的妾室。”
Turning stern eyes onto Xiaguo: "Miss saved you from ruin—here's your chance to repay her! Being Son-in-law's concubine won't shortchange you one bit; on Miss's behalf, I'll even pack you a generous dowry so you walk in a rich concubine!"

夏果心想，小姐想要甩开的人却硬塞给自己，说不定主君有什么无法对外人提起的隐疾，她决不能将个人幸福随意葬送，凭她的容貌能嫁个更好的。再说她刚进城里，什么都没见识过，也没体验过，人生就被定格，她心有不甘。
Xiaguo thought suspicious: Miss can't run fast enough from this husband and wants to dump him on ME? Bet he's got some nasty hidden illness! No way am I tossing my whole future away—with my looks I can aim way higher! Plus I just hit the big city—haven't even tasted life yet!

“正因感恩小姐救我，才下定决心一辈子不嫁，陪在小姐身边伺候她。韦嬷嬷说的嫁妆我不稀罕，跟着小姐不愁吃喝，当丫鬟我心满意足。”
"Precisely out of gratitude for Miss saving me, I swore never to marry and stay serving Miss for life! Don't want any dowry, Matron Wei—well fed and clothed by Miss's side as a maid is all I ever want!"

韦嬷嬷还打算继续批评夏果，被千里寻拦住：“既然夏果不愿意，不必为难她。你起来吧，跟苦荞把酒菜端到丫鬟房慢慢吃，我想静一静，想些事情。”
Before Matron Wei could scold further, Qianli Xun stepped in: "If Xiaguo says no, we don't force her! Get up now—you and Kuqiao pack up this food and eat at your leisure in the maids' quarters. Need quiet to think."

夏果感谢完小姐，起身与苦荞一起将酒菜收拾到两个食盒中，提去丫鬟房。
Thanking Miss profusely, Xiaguo rose and helped Kuqiao pack every dish into two food carriers for the maids' quarters.

韦嬷嬷劝道：“姑娘就是心软，看不得他人委屈。可姑娘也不应被夏果蒙蔽，她嘴上说想陪姑娘一辈子，心里可不一定这么想，说不定还想嫁给更好的人家当正室。”
Alone again, Matron Wei sighed: "Too softhearted, young lady—can't stand seeing anyone unhappy! Still, don't let Xiaguo fool you: claims she wants to serve you forever, yet deep down I bet she's angling to marry primary wife into an even richer house!"

“她有这想法倒也正常，如若心不甘情不愿嫁给林觉，像我一样遭遇家暴，岂不是令我一辈子不安心？”
"Can't blame her for wanting better! Force her into Lin Jue's bed unwilling and watch him beat her like he beat me? I'd never sleep at peace!"

“按说丫鬟的人生大事掌握在主子手中，主子想把她嫁给谁，她不能说任何反对的话，主要是姑娘心善，才会处处替丫鬟们考虑。唉，姑娘无论做什么决定，我都支持，我这就去找姑爷说说此事。”
"By rights a maid's marriage rests wholly in her mistress's hands—not her place to protest! Only because you're so kind do you weigh her wishes. Fine—whatever you choose, I back you! I'll go give Son-in-law our answer right now."

“有劳嬷嬷。”
"Thank you, Matron."

韦嬷嬷走后，千里寻陷入沉思，和离之事刚有眉目，又出了夏果这等事。如果没有收夏果当丫鬟，林觉就没有机会以此为条件再次为难她，真应了韦嬷嬷在竹林说过的话。
Once Matron Wei left, Qianli Xun sat brooding—mutual divorce inches away, only for Xiaguo to snag the whole deal! Had she never hired Xiaguo, Lin Jue wouldn't have this card to block her—just like Matron Wei warned back in the bamboo grove!

韦嬷嬷来到主屋拜访林觉，林觉倒是对韦嬷嬷敬重，只因这位嬷嬷说话做事令他佩服，看人的眼光也独到，比很多男子都厉害。他家暴朱淑真时，每次都选韦嬷嬷不在才敢动手，韦嬷嬷那双眼睛太过犀利，只要被她看一眼，他的拳头都打不下去。
Walking over to the main hall to call on Lin Jue, Matron Wei commanded quiet respect even from him—sharp-tongued, razor-sighted, sharper than most men; back when he beat Zhu Shuzhen, he always checked Matron Wei was out first, because one piercing glare from her shriveled his nerve!

林觉请韦嬷嬷坐下，不敢接触她的眼睛，怕那双眼睛看穿他心中所想：“不知嬷嬷前来有何指教？”
Inviting her to sit while dodging direct eye contact lest she read straight through him, Lin Jue asked: "What brings you over, Matron?"

“指教谈不上，我是来替姑娘回个话，夏果那丫头想跟着姑娘一辈子，不愿嫁人，还请姑爷另觅妾室。”
"Just bringing our young lady's reply—Xiaguo wants to serve Miss lifelong and refuses to marry, so please look elsewhere for a concubine."

韦嬷嬷的话如同当头一棒，他林觉的感情之路为何如此不顺？看上朱淑真，娶了她，日子过得糟心不说，还闹到和离。现在好不容易有个心仪的丫鬟，结果又被拒绝，让他情何以堪？
The reply hit Lin Jue like a brick—why was his love life cursed?! Fell for Zhu Shuzhen, wed her, miserable every day till she demanded divorce; finally spotted a gorgeous maid who lit the same spark, and rejected flat again! How humiliating!

一生气，也不顾及韦嬷嬷那双眼睛，大胆说：“既如此，和离之事免谈。”
Tempers flaring past his usual fear of Matron Wei's eyes, he snapped: "Then mutual divorce is off the table!"

韦嬷嬷猜到林觉会如此说，早已准备好应对之词：“我来也是向姑爷招呼一声，和离不和离无所谓，我在外看好宅子，也付了定金，不管姑爷同不同意和离，我家姑娘很快会搬出去。”
Having seen this coming miles away, Matron Wei rolled out her counter smooth: "Also came to give you fair warning, Son-in-law—mutual divorce or not makes zero difference! I've already picked out a fine courtyard house outside and put down deposit. Agree or refuse, our young lady moves out soon!"

林觉更像是被泼了一盆冷水，从外到里冰得透心凉：“这么快宅子都买好了？岳父大人可知此事？你们这么做，朱府的颜面何存？”
Ice water poured straight down Lin Jue's spine: "Bought a house already?! Does Father-in-law know?! Pull that stunt and what happens to Zhu Manor's honor?!"

“既然朱府已将女儿嫁给林家，朱家老爷知不知道此事不重要。姑娘已是林家人，搬出去住与朱家颜面无关，丢得只会是林家的人。”
"Once Zhu Manor wed their daughter into Lin Manor, Master Zhu's opinion stopped mattering! She's a Lin wife now—moving out doesn't shame the Zhu house, it shames the LIN house!"

韦嬷嬷句句戳中林觉的心窝，可林觉不敢大发雷霆：“我只是想要个丫鬟，朱淑真为何不能满足我？只要把夏果给我，大家都高兴，嬷嬷您说是也不是？您再帮我劝劝她，就让她把夏果给我得了。”
Every syllable hit dead-center in Lin Jue's chest—yet daring not explode at Matron Wei, he pleaded tight: "All I ask is one maid—why won't Zhu Shuzhen humor me?! Hand over Xiaguo and everyone wins—isn't that right, Matron? Talk her round—just hand Xiaguo over!"

韦嬷嬷叹口气：“唉！姑娘当然愿意，夏果不乐意，姑娘心又善，不愿强人所难。我给姑爷出个主意，不如私下多与夏果接触接触，说不定能培养出感情来，有了感情，她自然乐意。”
Matron Wei sighed: "Our young lady would gladly agree—Xiaguo's the one balking, and Miss's kind heart refuses to force anyone! Tell you what, Son-in-law—why not spend some time getting close to Xiaguo on your own? Spark some affection between you two, and she'll say yes on her own!"

林觉立刻乐道：“嬷嬷的主意甚好，那我就试试，不过可不能这么快搬出去，否则我与那丫头怎么培养感情？”
Lin Jue's face lit up at once: "Brilliant idea, Matron! I'll try that! Only—don't move out so fast, or how do I get time to warm that girl up?"

韦嬷嬷笑道：“那是自然。”
"Naturally!" Matron Wei smiled smooth.

千里寻独自想了一会儿，品着净光法师说的四个字“正思正念”，她决定不能将个人的幸福凌驾于他人不幸之上，尊重夏果的想法，才是正确的选择。想通之后，她到书桌前坐下，开始提笔写话本。
Back in Zhulan Residence, mulling over Master Jingguang's four words—Right Thought and Right Mindfulness—Qianli Xun felt peace settle back: building her own freedom on another girl's misery was wrong; honoring Xiaguo's choice felt clean. Mind clear, she sat at her desk and resumed writing huaben scripts.

苦荞拿着请帖进来：“小姐，乔公子已从汴梁回来，听说您从乡下回到林府，差南双送来请帖，邀请您参加诗会。”
Kuqiao walked in holding an invitation card: "Miss, Young Master Qiao is back from Bianliang! Heard you returned from the countryside and sent Nan Shuang over with an invitation to a poetry salon!"

千里寻放下笔，接过请帖打开，看到地点是在西湖边的观景亭，落款写着乔文仙。既是朱淑真的知己邀请，她必然要替朱淑真前去赴约，看看时间，是明日午时。
Setting down her brush and opening the card, Qianli Xun read: Sightseeing Pavilion by West Lake, signed Qiao Wenxian. Zhu Shuzhen's prized confidant—of course she had to attend! Date: tomorrow at noon.

“你去告诉送请帖之人，明日我会按时参加。”
"Tell the messenger I'll be there on time tomorrow."

“我已替小姐应下。”
"Already told him yes for you, Miss!"

千里寻瞅一眼苦荞：“什么时候轮到你替我做主？”
Qianli Xun arched a brow: "Since when do you make my schedule?"

“乔公子相邀，小姐从来不会拒绝，必然参加，有时我在想，如果小姐能和乔公子在一起那该多好。”
"Whenever Young Master Qiao invites, you never say no—always go! Sometimes I catch myself wishing you and Young Master Qiao ended up together..."

“你不是说我以前心里有徐公子，怎么又希望我跟乔公子在一起？”
"Didn't you say my heart belonged to Young Master Xu? Now shipping me with Young Master Qiao?!"

“以前虽希望您跟徐公子，可他娶了别人，所以就想到乔公子。”
"Rooted for Young Master Xu before—he married someone else, so Young Master Qiao's next up!"

“别瞎想，你家小姐我有婚姻挡在面前，再想其他男子会被人说成有失妇德，你竟然还敢说希望我与乔公子在一起的话？该打！”
"Quit daydreaming! Still got a husband blocking the way—fantasizing over other men gets you labeled lacking wifely virtue, and you dare say that aloud? Asking for a swat!"

苦荞乐道：“我宁可被打，也要说出希望小姐好的话，反正小姐跟姑爷和离是迟早的事，在我心里失忆后的小姐已是自由身，所以替您畅想一下未来。”
Kuqiao giggled fearless: "Swat me all you like, still wishing you the best! Divorce is only a matter of time—in my book, reborn Miss is already a free woman, so I'm dreaming ahead for you!"

别说苦荞这么认为，千里寻穿越成为朱淑真，从来没把这段婚姻当回事，在她心里又何尝不认为自己是自由之身？
Not just Kuqiao—ever since transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen, Qianli Xun had never treated this bogus marriage as real either; in her own heart she felt free as air!

韦嬷嬷走后，林觉思忖着如何能与夏果培养感情，让她心甘情愿给他做妾。思来想去，只觉良家女子实在麻烦，不像商妓，无需费心费力。可想起夏果的身段与容貌不比朱淑真差，费些心思倒也值得。
Meanwhile, once Matron Wei left, Lin Jue plotted how to warm Xiaguo up until she willingly became his concubine. Respectable girls took such tedious effort—nothing like easy courtesans! Still, remembering Xiaguo's figure and face matching Zhu Shuzhen's beauty, putting in work felt worth every ounce!

于是叫进一名下人：“去，把夫人新收的丫鬟夏果给我找来，就说我找她问些事情。”
Calling a servant in: "Fetch Madam's new maid Xiaguo—say I have questions for her."

不一会儿夏果前来，已猜到主君之意，不如趁此机会表明想法，让他死了这份心思。
Moments later Xiaguo arrived, already guessing his angle and ready to shut him down cold for good.

夏果走到主屋门口，向里问：“主君找我？”
Stopping outside the main hall doorway, she called in: "You sent for me, Lord?"

传来林觉的声音：“进来吧。”
"Come inside," Lin Jue answered.

夏果走进去，不敢抬头，一直盯着自己的脚尖。
Stepping across the threshold, Xiaguo kept her eyes pinned to her toes.

林觉越看越喜欢，站起来，绕着夏果将她上下打量好几遍。
Liking her more by the second, Lin Jue stood and circled around her eyeing every inch top to bottom.

夏果心里发毛，担心主君非礼她：“主君找我什么事？”
Skin crawling with dread that he might get handsy, Xiaguo asked stiff: "What did Lord wish to ask?"

林觉在夏果面前站定，直接问：“你为何不愿跟我？”
Halting right in front of her, Lin Jue cut straight to the chase: "Why won't you come to me?"

果然是为此事，夏果回道：“我身份卑微，配不上主君……”
Just as she expected! Xiaguo answered flat: "My humble birth isn't worthy of Lord..."

“我不嫌弃。”
"Doesn't bother me."

“我不想给人当妾室。”
"I refuse to be anyone's concubine."

林觉想了一会儿，说道：“我很快与朱淑真和离，身边没有正室，你若当妾室把我伺候得好，我一高兴，没准儿会将你扶正。”
Pausing a beat, Lin Jue pitched: "I'm divorcing Zhu Shuzhen soon—no primary wife left! Serve me well as concubine, keep me happy, and maybe I promote you to primary wife!"

夏果说：“我要是嫁人，必须是明媒正娶的当家主母，才不愿意先当妾室，慢慢等着被扶正，再说主君的话能有几分可信？”
"If I marry, I enter legally wed as head matriarch—never starting as concubine waiting on scraps! Besides, how much weight does Lord's word even carry?"

这丫头的果断倒令林觉佩服，但林觉不可能直接让她成为正室。夏果家世不好，身份卑贱，要不是模样长得好，他又怎会注意她？若跟朱淑真一和离，就娶她做正室，不仅族人会反对，他林觉都会觉得面上无光。耐心劝道：“不如这样，我答应你，三年后将你扶正。”
Impressed by her grit yet refusing to wed a peasant girl as primary wife—zero background, sheer pretty face; marrying her straight after divorcing Zhu Shuzhen would ignite clan uproar and cost him face—he tried bargaining patient: "Tell you what—I promise to promote you to primary wife in three years!"

夏果被三年惊住，抬起头，盯着林觉：“三年！主君是哄我吧？三年的时间主君身边不知会有多少女人，到时还能记得承诺？我虽年轻，可我也不是三岁小孩儿，让主君这般哄骗。”
Startled by "three years," Xiaguo snapped her head up glaring straight at him: "Three years?! Playing me for a fool, Lord?! Who knows how many women pass through your bed in three years—think you'll remember one promise? Young as I am, I'm not a three-year-old buying fairy tales!"

夏果的眼睛实在太美，五官极其耐看，盯得林觉心里直痒，忍不住一把抱住夏果：“你就跟了我吧，我决不会亏待你，现在就去里屋，让你体验一下我的好。”
Her flashing eyes and striking features lit a fire straight in Lin Jue's gut; losing every ounce of restraint, he swept her tight into his arms: "Just be mine—I'll never mistreat you! Step into the inner room right now and feel how good I am to you!"

林觉毕竟是练兵官，劲大，把夏果抱住，她不得挣脱，还被对方在脸上亲了两口。
Strong as a Training Officer was, his grip locked Xiaguo helpless as he planted two sloppy kisses across her cheek!

林觉什么都顾不上，使劲儿把夏果抱起来，向里屋走去。
Throwing all caution aside, he hoisted her off her feet and carried her toward the inner chamber!

夏果在林觉身上又捶又打：“你放开我！快放开我！……”
Pounding and clawing at his chest, Xiaguo screamed: "Let me go! Let me go right now!..."

林觉哪里肯放？一心只想从夏果的身上体验从未在朱淑真身上体验过的感觉。
