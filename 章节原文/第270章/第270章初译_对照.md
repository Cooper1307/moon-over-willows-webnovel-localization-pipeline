## Chapter 270: A Surprise Date at the Silk Emporium

懒人村的事有人出面解决，陈家的施粥棚只剩些老弱病残的流浪之人前来讨要粥吃，人数一下子减少一大半，管理起来甚是容易。千里寻决定明日让红豆带几个下人前来即可，她来不来已经不重要。
想到施粥棚的问题能够解决与仪王息息相关，千里寻的内心依然会萌生特殊的情愫。可梦容愤恨的眼神，还有昨日看到的书卷，她不敢沉迷下去，还是想想陈浩宇。
昨晚被他亲到，那种感觉她从未体验过，真是妙不可言。既然已经不是那么讨厌他，不如与他圆房。
想到圆房两个字，她依然感到别扭，还是等等吧。陈浩宇都说了，他愿意等，不能辜负他。
正想着，红豆走来：“小姐，施粥棚比昨日好多了，不如回府忙您的事，这里交给我。若您不愿回府，到街上逛逛也行。”
千里寻正有此意，她站起来：“此处交给你，我去逛街。”
红豆继续请示：“要不要随身带两个人？没了懒人村的人，施粥棚现在用不了这么多人。”
千里寻想独自逛街散散心：“不必，都留在这里，明日你少带几人来即可。”
显然小姐想一个人逛，红豆不再强求：“一切听小姐的。”
千里寻离开施粥棚，没有乘坐马车，既是逛，还需依赖双脚。她决定等逛累了再租辆马车回府。
街上虽不是人挤人，也足以显示出街市的繁华。
街市越热闹，越能衬托出一个人逛街的落寞。即使千里寻并未带着落寞的情绪而来，身旁经过的一对对情侣足以令她感到落寞。
经历过与文渊的初恋，还悄悄喜欢上仪王，现在又嫁给陈浩宇，昨晚还被他亲，逛个街竟然还是孤身一人。
去年时，身边还有文渊、仪王和梦容陪伴。真是时过境迁，每个人都有了自己的归宿，不管这归宿你乐不乐意，冥冥中已替每个人安排得妥妥当当。
她突然想去找陈浩宇，现在也只能找他陪伴，否则她便是不守妇道。
经历过流言蜚语，又在诗会上被众人辱骂，千里寻觉出人言可畏，她不想再给他人留下辱骂她的机会。
记得今早陈浩宇走时，在门外说他还去绸缎庄忙活，不如去那里找他，顺便瞧瞧他如何当伙计。
千里寻租辆马车，车夫专门干拉人的活儿，对临安城各地儿无不熟悉，尤其是陈家的产业，那是出了名的，自然清楚走哪条道最方便。
到达陈家绸缎庄，千里寻走下马车，向车夫付了钱，望向匾额上写的“陈家绸缎庄”五个大字，心生一计，不如装作买料子的顾客，检验一下陈浩宇当伙计的成效。
她掏出丝帕，绑在脸上，只露出两只大眼睛，这样进去，只要不开口说话，陈浩宇一时半会儿应该认不出。
装扮好后，大胆走进去。里面很大，顾客不少，显然生意十分红火。再仔细瞧，各种花色和质地的绸缎应有尽有。
千里寻搜寻着陈浩宇的身影，只见各位伙计忙着招呼客人，大家穿一样的衣装，一时半会儿还真瞧不出哪位是陈浩宇。
倒是先瞧见青瓜，他的身形稍胖些，算是特点比较鲜明。青瓜也在招呼客人，满脸堆笑，给客人介绍这个又介绍那个，嘴皮子也顺溜。从跟班的身上就能瞧出主人的变化。
陈浩宇的个头不低，不如专挑个头高的伙计瞧，果然很快陈浩宇映入眼帘，他正将包装好的绸缎递给一位女顾客。
“姑娘，您要的丝绸已装好，请您拿好，若有需要，再来陈家绸缎庄。”
那位姑娘将纸盒接在手中：“你这伙计懂得不少，下次我来还找你接待。”
“荣幸之至，姑娘请。”
陈浩宇将那位姑娘送至门口，瞧见一位用丝帕蒙着面的姑娘盯着自己瞧，身形像极了家中的娘子，难道是她？又一想怎么可能？她在施粥棚忙活，不可能来这里。
青瓜也将顾客送至门口，见公子盯着美女瞧，没有接待的意思，干脆由他来。
“姑娘想买什么？我给您一一介绍，定能让您选到满意的。”
“去去去！她是我的客人，少跟我抢！”陈浩宇将青瓜推到一边。
青瓜很不满，凑近公子耳边：“小心我回去告诉少夫人，公子在外面盯着美女看。”
青瓜说完，担心公子揍他，迅速撤离，去迎接别的客人。
千里寻心中不满：“哼！好色之徒！见着美女就走不动！一双眼睛色眯眯地盯着我，还想跟我圆房，门儿都没有！”
只听陈浩宇说：“姑娘的身形酷似我家娘子，我不由多看几眼，还请姑娘见谅。”
“心里还有我，那我就暂时原谅你。”千里寻想着，一句话不说，往里走。
With Lazybones Village off the scene, only genuinely frail, elderly, and crippled paupers remained at the Chen congee shed—cutting the crowd in half and making order effortless. Qianli Xun figured Hongdou and a few servants could handle it solo starting tomorrow.

陈浩宇赶紧跟上：“姑娘，您气质高雅，定是识货之人。陈家绸缎庄有各式各样的丝绸，不知您想挑选哪种质地的？”
Knowing Prince Yi had cleared that mess still tugged a subtle ache in her chest, yet Mengrong's venomous glare and yesterday's smear pamphlet snapped her straight back to Chen Haoyu. That surprise kiss last night had caught her utterly off guard—and felt weirdly good. Maybe she really could consummate the marriage soon... no, the mere word still felt awkward. He'd promised to wait; let him wait a little longer.

千里寻心想：“我偏不说话，看他如何应对。”
Hongdou walked over. "Things are smooth today, Miss. Why not head back—or stroll the streets for a bit? I've got this covered."

蒙面姑娘不言语，陈浩宇只能继续介绍：“我们店的丝绸种类繁多，质地优良，无论是贡品，还是民间用品，都能满足您的需求。请问您想了解哪种？”
Qianli Xun stood up right away. "I'll go take a walk. Keep everyone here—and bring fewer hands tomorrow."

蒙面姑娘依然不言语，陈浩宇仔细观察，发现她的目光在各色丝绸上流转，似有几分犹豫。
Skipping a carriage to stretch her legs, she drifted into Lin'an's bustling markets—only for the lively crowd and strolling couples to highlight how solitary she felt. First love with Wenyuan, hidden longing for Prince Yi, wed to Haoyu, kissed last night—yet still walking the street all alone. Last year Wenyuan, Prince Yi, and Mengrong had filled her days; now everyone had landed in whatever slot fate picked for them.

陈浩宇心领神会，开口说：“姑娘想必是为挑选哪种丝绸而烦恼？这绫罗绸缎，绫之纹理细密，光滑柔软，若制成衣裳，穿在身上，行动之间，流光溢彩；罗则轻薄透气，触感细腻，最是适合夏日穿戴，清凉又舒适；绸之质地厚实，光泽温润，做裙装端庄大气；缎则光滑如镜，色泽明艳，做披风定能让姑娘在人群中脱颖而出。”
Suddenly she wanted Haoyu's company—and he was the only man she *could* walk with in public without fueling fresh venom after all the gossip and Mengrong's salon. Remembering he was at the silk emporium, she hired a carriage straight there.

千里寻心想：“这家伙懂得倒挺多，伙计的活儿没白干。”
Paying the driver outside the grand *Chen Silk Emporium* signboard, a playful scheme struck her: test-drive Haoyu's shop-clerk skills incognito! Tying a silk handkerchief across her lower face so only her bright eyes showed, she marched inside.

姑娘依然不言语，继续往里走，陈浩宇仍旧跟上，如数家珍：“我们这里有蜀锦、宋锦、缎子、罗、绫、纱等。蜀锦以色彩艳丽、图案精美著称；宋锦则质地细腻，光泽柔和；缎子光滑柔软，适合做高档衣装；罗则轻盈透气，适合夏天穿着；绫和纱则各有特色，既可做衣料，也可做装饰。”
Business buzzed across the sprawling sales floor packed with lustrous bolts. Scanning identical clerk tunics, she spotted Qinggua's rounder frame first—beaming and rattling off pitches like a pro! Next she scanned for tall clerks and caught Haoyu handing a wrapped box to a beaming female shopper.

姑娘还没有要买的意思，陈浩宇继续耐心介绍：“丝绸的质地主要取决于原料和工艺。比如，缎子是用桑蚕丝织成的，质地柔软，光泽好；而罗则是用麻、蚕丝织成的，轻盈透气。此外，我们还根据顾客的需求，采用不同的工艺，如缂丝、刺绣等，使丝绸更具特色。”
"Here's your silk, Miss. Please come again!"

对方依旧没有要购买的意思，难道今日要失败？陈浩宇打算使出最后一招：“姑娘高贵典雅，浑身上下透着书卷之气，若是配上这淡青色的绫，再绣上几株兰花，那定是清新脱俗，宛如仙子下凡。或者这月白色的罗，做一件里衣，贴身穿戴，肌肤的触感就如同春风拂面，轻柔至极。”
"You really know your fabrics—I'll ask for you next time!"

千里寻觉得自己被说动，转向陈浩宇：“那便依你所言，选淡青色的绫。”
"My honor, Miss!"

这声音不正是娘子的声音吗？陈浩宇激动的同时，又不敢相信，直到千里寻将丝帕取下，娘子的容颜出现在他面前，陈浩宇竟然忍不住抱住她。
Seeing her to the door, Haoyu spotted a veiled young woman watching him—her silhouette screaming his wife! Wait, couldn't be; she was running the congee shed.

“娘子！真的是你！没想到你跑来看我！我……我……我高兴得都不知道该说什么！”
Qinggua stepped up too, eager to grab the shopper. "Looking for silk, Miss? Let me show you—"

众目睽睽之下被他抱住，千里寻虽不习惯，又不好拒绝，怎么说也需给他留些面子。小声在他耳边说：“大众场合，你这样抱住我不太好，不如今日请假陪我去逛街。”
"Shoo! My customer!" Haoyu shouldered Qinggua aside.

“娘子有任何需求我无不满足。”陈浩宇放开娘子，“我这就找秋管事请假，娘子在此稍候片刻。”
"Careful or I'll tell Young Madam you're ogling pretty strangers!" Qinggua hissed, ducking off to another buyer before Haoyu could kick him.

他转过身，见所有伙计和客人瞧向这边，于是笑嘻嘻地向大家介绍：“我娘子！她来绸缎庄看我，嘿嘿嘿，大家继续，继续，嘿嘿嘿……”
*Filthy flirt!* Qianli Xun fumed behind her veil. *One pretty silhouette and his eyes bug out!*

青瓜心想：“公子一脸幸福的样子真令人羡慕，什么时候丑丫也能来绸缎庄瞧瞧我。”想归想，还得上前问候少夫人。
Then Haoyu spoke with warm earnestness. "Forgive me for staring, Miss—your figure reminds me so much of my wife."

其他伙计也都一一上前问候完少夫人，继续招待客人。
*Fine—reprieve granted!* Saying nothing, she wandered deeper into the racks to see how he handled a silent customer.

秋管事再严厉，也不能阻止人家小两口约会，要不大老爷会批评他。再说二公子的业务进展飞速，不在乎少学一两日。因此满口答应，还嘱咐二公子用心陪少夫人，其他不用放在心上。
Haoyu fell smoothly into stride. "You carry yourself like a true connoisseur, Miss. What weave are you looking for? From imperial-grade silks to everyday cloth, we stock the finest—which catches your eye?"

陈浩宇换过衣裳，拉起娘子的手往外走。青瓜见状跟上去。
Still silence. Catching her gaze drifting indecisively across the bolts, Haoyu launched effortlessly into his pitch.

走出绸缎庄，陈浩宇感觉身后跟着人，转过头，一脸不满：“别跟着，回去继续当伙计。”
"Trying to pick the right weave? Look at these four staples: *ling* twill has a fine, liquid sheen that ripples with light when you walk; *luo* gauze is sheer, breathable, and silky—unbeatable for summer; *chou* silk is thick and softly lustrous, ideal for stately skirts; and *duan* satin gleams like a mirror in bold hues, guaranteed to turn heads as a cloak!"

青瓜很委屈，眼睁睁瞧着公子牵着少夫人的手越走越远，自语道：“公子有了少夫人，都不让我跟着，下次我也约丑丫逛街，把公子撇在一边。”
*Not bad—he actually studied!*

埋怨归埋怨，他毕竟是下人，还得听主人吩咐，继续回去当伙计。想到当伙计有银子赚，青瓜立刻又高兴起来，打算拿到银子后给丑丫买件礼物。
Keeping mum, she walked further while he rattled on without missing a beat. "We carry Sichuan brocade, Song brocade, satin, gauze, twill, and sheer silk. Sichuan brocade dazzles with bold patterns; Song brocade glows with refined subtlety... Satin uses pure mulberry silk for softness and gloss; gauze blends hemp and silk for airy weightlessness—plus we offer *kesi* tapestry weave and custom embroidery."

手被陈浩宇牵着，千里寻并未拒绝。一来在外面要给夫君面子，当众拒绝，他脸上会挂不住；二来，既打算与他进一步发展，牵手是必然之事，何况昨晚他已亲过她，牵手对她来讲已不算什么。
Still no bite! Pulling out his closer, Haoyu smiled warmly. "With your refined, scholarly air, Miss, this pale celadon *ling* twill embroidered with a few sprays of orchids would look downright celestial—or this moon-white gauze for an inner robe, soft as a spring breeze against the skin."

只是感到惋惜，为何牵着他的手没有怦然心动的感觉？
Actually sold, Qianli Xun turned to him. "Then I'll take the pale celadon twill."

常言道十指连心，难道是因为她一直委屈自己的感情迎合不得不接受的婚姻？看来是这样，她还未从心底真正爱上陈浩宇。
That voice! Haoyu froze in stunned disbelief until she pulled down her silk veil—and without thinking twice, he swept her straight into a bear hug!

可为何昨晚他亲她时，她会有感觉？人们常说口腹之欲，难道是女性荷尔蒙作祟？千里寻越想脸越红，还未真正爱上陈浩宇，却对他产生感觉，实在不雅。
"Wife! It really *is* you! You came all this way to see me—I don't even know what to say!"

陈浩宇并未察觉娘子的变化，沉浸在牵着她的手逛街的满足感中，边走边说：“我还是第一次陪女子逛街，娘子累了告诉我，你要是不想坐马车，我背你。”
Flustered in front of a crowded shop yet unwilling to embarrass him, she whispered in his ear, "Public place, buddy—let go. Ask for the rest of the day off and walk around town with me."

千里寻安慰自己，他是她的夫君，对他产生情感以外的感觉应该是正常的，她的脸色逐渐恢复正常。
"Your wish is my command!" Letting go, he grinned sheepishly at the staring clerks and shoppers. "My wife! Dropped by to check on me—hehe, carry on, everybody!"

“我的确不想坐马车，可也轮不上你背我，试想想大街上你背着我多难看。”
Qinggua hurried up to bow while secretly wishing Chouya would come visit *him* at work too, and the other clerks chimed in greetings. Strict as Steward Qiu was, no steward dared block a young master's date with his wife—especially when Haoyu was flying through training—and happily waved them off.

“我不觉得，背自己的娘子幸福着呢，别人羡慕还来不及，怎么会难看？”
Changing robes, Haoyu clasped her hand and strode out—spinning around to scowl at Qinggua tagging behind. "Go back inside and work!"

“你背不动我。”
Left pouting as the couple walked away hand in hand, Qinggua muttered, "Gets his wife and ditches me! Next time I'm taking Chouya shopping and ditching *him*!" Still, earning real clerk's wages cheered him right back up—he'd buy Chouya a nice gift.

陈浩宇停下，转向娘子：“要不现在试试？你的拳头力气虽大，身体的重量肯定比不上我，背你小菜一碟。”
Qianli Xun let him hold her hand—partly to save his face outdoors, partly because after last night's kiss, holding hands was mild. Still, a quiet regret nagged her: why didn't his hand make her heart race? Was it because she was still forcing herself to settle? Wait—then why had his kiss stirred her last night?! *Must be plain hormones!* Heat crept up her cheeks at how improper that sounded.

“才刚逛，我还不累。”千里寻只觉脸上又有温热的感觉，她竟然在陈浩宇面前害羞起来。
Oblivious, Haoyu beamed down at her. "First time I've ever gone shopping with a girl! Tell me the second your feet get tired—if you hate carriages, I'll carry you on my back."

娘子不再粗鲁，脸还变得粉红粉红，像春天盛开的桃花，难道她真的爱上自己？
Steadying her blush, she replied, "Don't want a carriage, and definitely not riding piggyback down Main Street—how ridiculous would that look!"

“你对我说话越来越温柔，我现在感觉自己是世上最幸福的人。有了娘子后我突然觉得以前的日子都是白过，以后我会堂堂正正做人，谁让我娶了一位好娘子。”
"Who cares? Carrying my own wife—folks will just turn green with envy!"

千里寻不知该如何回应，曾经的她哪会想到有一日陈浩宇会站在街上对她说情话。街上人多，还是不要让他继续盯着自己说些让人面红耳赤的话。
"You couldn't even lift me."

“我们还是继续逛吧？”
Stopping dead, he turned. "Try me! Wicked fists don't make you heavy—carrying you's a breeze!"

“逛街必须买东西，娘子想买什么尽管告诉我。”
"We just started walking—I'm not tired yet." Another warm flush crept up her cheeks. *Wait—am I actually blushing in front of Chen Haoyu?!*

“谁说逛街必须买东西？我什么都不想买，只想体验用双脚走路的感觉。”
Watching his fierce wife turn soft and rosy as spring peach blossoms sent Haoyu soaring into the clouds. "You're talking so gently to me now—I feel like the luckiest man alive. Ever since marrying you, my whole past feels wasted. From now on I'll walk straight and proud—all because I married a wife like you."

“娘子想走多久，我陪你多久。”
Never in a million years had she pictured Chen Haoyu confessing sweet nothings in broad daylight. Too many eyes on the street—better keep moving before her ears caught fire. "Let's keep walking."

陈浩宇舍不得放开娘子的手，继续拉着她逛街。
"Shopping means buying—name whatever you want!"

千里寻从开始不习惯，逐渐变得用心享受起被牵着逛街的感觉。
"Says who? I don't want to buy a thing—just feel my own two feet on the street."

陈浩宇一直牵着她，没有丝毫放松的想法，仿佛担心她会从身边溜走似的。
"Walk as far as you like—I'm right beside you."

千里寻的脸上露出笑容，为她不再讨厌陈浩宇，心甘情愿跟着他逛街而高兴。只有这样她才会好，所有人都会好。
Refusing to loosen his grip for a second, as if she might slip away if he blinked, Haoyu walked on—and bit by bit, Qianli Xun's stiffness melted into genuine quiet pleasure. A genuine smile curved her lips. Walking willingly beside him like this felt right—good for her, and good for everyone.
