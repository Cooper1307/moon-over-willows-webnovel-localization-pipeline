第342章 洞房花烛烛泪滴
## Chapter 342: Tears Beside the Wedding Candles

千里寻醒来时，眼前是一片自然的亮光，终于从地府出来，刚要开心，想起自己是喝了完颜池的桂花酿才晕过去，桂花酿中一定放了什么药物，否则怎么会一杯就倒？
她从床上爬起来， 警惕地观察屋子，到处是红色，她躺的床榻铺着红色的褥子，连枕头和被子也是红色。显然她正躺在婚房里，难道完颜池想逼她成亲？
千里寻很气愤，使劲儿掀被子，浑身的肌肉像是在醋中泡过一般，又酸又软，只能慢慢地从被子里溜下床。
刚在地上站稳，听到门被推开，传来完颜池的声音：“人可醒？”
丫鬟回道：“应该还睡着，奴婢刚要进去瞧，小王爷就来了。”
“出去吧，把门关好，免得冷气进来冻到美人。”
“是。”
千里寻又听到丫鬟出去关门的声音，声音刚结束，完颜池出现在她面前，脸上挂着笑容。
“醒了，别站在地上，小心冻着。”
千里寻并不领情，将酸软的拳头伸到完颜池面前。他伸出手轻松推回：“别瞪着本王，本王不会害你，只想让你变得温顺些。”
“你给桂花酿里放了什么？”
“巫医特制的药。”
“卑鄙无耻！”
“别这么说你未来的夫君。”
“呸！我有夫君，他正在临安等我回去。”
“不管你以前有多少夫君，从今往后只会有本王一个。”
千里寻忍着怒气劝道：“我不爱你，又何必为难我？”
“你爱不爱本王不重要，重要的是本王喜欢你。”完颜池霸道的同时，露出回忆往事的表情，“临安街头，一眼万年，你是第二个令本王心动的女子。怪只怪你长成本王喜欢的样子，只是性情差些，拳头也比本王厉害。还好有巫医的药，只要今晚洞房花烛夜之后，你成为本王的女人，会慢慢喜欢上本王。”
“你敢动老娘一根毫毛，我就死在你面前。”
完颜池大胆靠近美人，在他眼中，此刻的她如同一块刚出炉的嫩豆腐，既软嫩又美味。
千里寻被他眼中的欲望之火吓到，向后退去。她的身后只有床榻，不小心向下倒去……
完颜池趁机扶住她：“你现在无力反抗的样子实在令本王心动。”
千里寻用手去推完颜池，他纹丝不动，她不得已罢手：“你若敢碰我，我就咬舌自尽。”
话音刚落，完颜池的嘴巴已堵在她的唇上。
千里寻厌恶至极，攥紧拳头，只能无力地在他背上拍打，一点儿用处没有。对方吻得越投入她越厌恶，拍打不成，使出嘴上的功夫，一口咬破完颜池的嘴唇，一股血腥味儿传到她的舌尖。
完颜池冷不丁被美人咬一口才停下，放开手，美人倒在床榻上。
“别以为我浑身酸软就拿你没办法，只要我能说话，咬人的力气还是有的。”
完颜池站好后用舌头舔去嘴唇上的血迹，笑道：“越难驯服的野兽，本王越感兴趣，今晚你会尝到本王的厉害。”
“滚！你给我滚！”
“好好休息，晚上本王再来看你。”
完颜池出去后，从外面进来五六个丫鬟，进入屋内到处查看。
千里寻坐起来不解地问：“你们要做什么？”
一个丫鬟回道：“小王爷让我们进来把尖锐之物统统拿出去。”
“为何？”
“小王爷担心姑娘寻短见。”
千里寻冷笑道：“我要是想寻短见有的是办法。”
丫鬟们查找半天，找出匕首和剪刀带了出去，还把门从外面锁上。
想到今晚有可能失身于完颜池，千里寻又急又气，她不想坐以待毙。屋内没有丫鬟，只好亲自把衣裳从衣桁上取下穿好，披上厚厚的斗篷，向外喊：“来人！”
有开锁的声音，门被打开后，进来一个丫鬟。
“姑娘有何吩咐？”
“我想出去走走。”
“小王爷说姑娘今日不能出去，明日小王爷会亲自带姑娘出去。”
一计不成，再施一计：“我饿了，给我弄些吃的来。”
“姑娘稍候，吃的一会儿就到。”
门又被锁上。
千里寻起身走到门口等待，她发现身上的肌肉不再酸软，试着使出一拳，力度跟没有练过拳法的人没有任何区别。自语道：“巫医的药到底是什么，搞得我功力全无，也不知什么时候能恢复？”
外面有脚步声传来，应该是送饭的丫鬟过来。千里寻警惕起来，她要趁门打开的一刻冲出去，需要瞅准时机。
门锁被打开，送饭的正是在地府时伺候过她的丫鬟，走进的过程中，千里寻趁机从她身边溜了出去。
两柄剑挡在面前，一队侍卫如铜墙铁壁般堵住她的去路。
要搁以前她根本不会把他们放在眼里，拳法使不出，只能认怂：“我不过想出去走走，透透气，你们堵我干吗？”
侍卫中有一人说：“小王爷吩咐，姑娘今日不能走出屋子！”
千里寻骂句：“可恶！”转身回到屋内，眼睁睁看着门被继续锁上。
丫鬟把饭菜从食盒里拿出在桌上摆好：“姑娘已从地府出来，不急这一日。”
丫鬟又上前帮千里寻脱掉厚厚的斗篷：“屋里有火盆，暖和着，姑娘披着它会热，我给您脱下，您快坐下吃饭。”
看着桌上的饭菜，千里寻根本没胃口，还是装模作样坐下。
“认识你这么久，还不知道你叫什么？”
丫鬟挂好斗篷回到姑娘身旁伺候：“奴婢叫会兰娜。”
“会兰娜，名字不错。看到你想起我的丫鬟苦荞，两年多没见，我好想她。”
“只要姑娘乖乖跟小王爷在一起，提任何要求小王爷都会答应，到时您让小王爷把苦荞接来，你们不就见着了？”
“我才不会让苦荞来金国，她有喜欢的人，我只希望她跟白风尽快成亲，不要再耽搁下去。”
会兰娜见姑娘不吃东西，只顾说话，提醒道：“姑娘快些吃，要不一会儿凉了。”
千里寻拿起筷子象征性吃了一口，问道：“会兰娜，你可知巫医是干什么的？”
“巫医是金国的神医，主要负责给皇室医病。”
“制作毒药害人，根本当不起‘神医’的美名。会兰娜，你能不能给我帮个忙？”
Natural sunlight greeted Qianli Xun’s eyes as she woke—free of the underground vault at last! Delight lasted one heartbeat before memory slammed back: one cup of osmanthus wine, a spinning room, and blackness.

“姑娘请说。”
Bolting upright, she froze. Red bed-curtains draped overhead, red silk padded the mattress, and red pillows and quilts wrapped around her—a bridal chamber! Every muscle in her body felt soaked in sour vinegar, weak and rubbery as she slid slowly off the edge onto the floorboards.

“完颜池心肠歹毒，在桂花酿里下了巫医制作的毒药，害得我使不出拳法，你能不能帮我找来解药？”
Barely steadying her feet, she heard footsteps outside the door.

会兰娜吓得跪在地上：“姑娘饶命！奴婢要是帮了您会立刻没命，况且奴婢也不知道解药放在哪里。”
“Is she awake?” Wanyan Chi’s voice asked.

千里寻失落道：“我只是随口一说，看把你吓的，起来吧，我不会为难你。”
“Still asleep, likely—I was just about to peek in,” a maid answered.

会兰娜起身宽慰道：“姑娘别急，等您与小王爷洞房花烛之后小王爷自然会给您解药。”
“Dismiss yourselves and shut the door tight so cold drafts don’t chill her.”

“那我岂不是羊入虎口？”
As the door clicked shut behind the maids, Wanyan Chi walked into view smiling broadly. “Awake? Don’t stand barefoot on the floor—you’ll catch cold.”

会兰娜在一旁偷笑起来。
Swinging a limp, aching fist toward his jaw, she watched him swat it aside with one finger. “Stop glaring at me. I won’t hurt you—just wanted you gentle for once.”

“你笑什么？”
“What did you put in that wine?!”

会兰娜忍住笑：“姑娘怎能将洞房之夜说成羊入虎口？您不知道，小王爷今天心情特别好，大家都说小王爷嘴角一直挂着笑容。”
“A special medicine mixed by our shaman.”

“哼！将个人的幸福凌驾于他人痛苦之上，有什么好高兴的？”
“Despicable bastard!”

“只要姑娘接受小王爷，您就不会痛苦。”
“Careful how you speak to your future husband.”

“我最讨厌像他这等霸道之人，想让老娘接受他根本不可能。”
“Bah! I have a husband waiting for me in Lin’an!”

会兰娜见劝说不下，便不再说话。
“Forget how many husbands you had before—from tonight on, I’m the only one.”

千里寻没有胃口吃饭，起身回里屋躺下。
Swallowing fury, she pleaded through clenched teeth, “I don’t love you! Why torture me like this?”

会兰娜把没怎么动的饭菜收回食盒，提着食盒在门口敲了敲门，门被打开后，又继续被锁上。
“Whether you love me doesn’t matter—what matters is I like you.” Memories softened his tone for a moment. “One glance on a Lin’an street, worth a thousand years. You’re only the second woman ever to stir my heart. Too bad your fists hit harder than mine—thank goodness for shaman medicine. Once tonight’s wedding candles burn down and you become my woman, you’ll grow to love me bit by bit.”

千里寻听着锁门的声音，情绪很不好，她长这么大头一次感受到人生无奈，也头一次认识到自己实在弱小，被人轻易拿捏住。
“Touch one hair on my head, and I swear I’ll die right in front of you!”

她的人生仿佛进入死局，难以突破，该如何是好？还没找到答案，却迷迷糊糊进入梦乡。
Stepping closer, eyes burning with hunger at how soft and defenseless she looked, he backed her step by step straight against the bedframe until her knees buckled. Catching her waist, he murmured how intoxicating her helpless look made him. Pushing against his chest moved him not an inch.

梦中她来到神柳下，神柳枝叶繁茂，上面挂着一些红绸，微风下柳枝和红绸随风摆动。
“Touch me,” she hissed, “and I’ll bite off my own tongue!”

“好久不见，你可想我？”
His mouth covered hers before the threat finished. Revolted down to her bones, she beat weakly at his back with useless fists—then sank her teeth straight through his lip until warm iron blood flooded her tongue!

神柳竟然开口说话：“对你来讲是好久，在我看来不过是瞬息之间。”
Jerking back with a sharp gasp, he let her drop onto the mattress.

“带我离开好吗？”
“Think turning me soft makes me harmless?” she panted. “As long as my mouth works, I can still draw blood!”

“我无法带走你。”
Licking the cut on his lip with a grin, Wanyan Chi chuckled. “The fiercer the beast, the sweeter the taming! Tonight you’ll taste what kind of man I am. Rest up—I’ll come back tonight.”

“明明是你把我带来，为何带不走我？”
Once he left, five or six maids swept into the room rummaging every chest and drawer for daggers and scissors—to keep her from harming herself—before locking the door heavy from outside.

“你之所以能来，不过是个人业力借助了我的神力而已，要想回去，也需靠个人的业力。”
Dreading nightfall, Qianli Xun pulled on her robes from the rack, draped a thick cloak over her shoulders, and hammered the door. A maid unlocked it only to say Wanyan Chi forbade her stepping out until tomorrow.

“业力？什么是业力？”
Switching tactics, she claimed she was starving and waited right behind the door. Testing a punch at empty air confirmed the worst: not a shred of martial power remained—her punches packed no more sting than an ordinary girl’s.

“累生累世所造之业凝聚在一起形成无穷无尽之力，这股力量将你带到各处，只有业力消除，方可获得自由。”
When footsteps returned carrying a food box, she waited till the door swung inward and darted past the maid—straight into twin crossed blades and an iron wall of royal guards.

梦中的千里寻听不明白，大声说：“我坚持正思正念，做事问心无愧，何来业力？”
“His Highness forbade you leaving this room today,” a guard barked.

神柳沉默不语。
Cursing under her breath, she retreated while the lock clicked shut once more. Setting dishes onto the table and helping slip off her heavy cloak in the brazier-warmed room, the maid from underground coaxed her to eat.

身后传来申简辰的声音：“千里寻！”
Too sick with dread to swallow, Qianli Xun sat down anyway and asked the maid’s name.

千里寻转过身，兴奋地跑到申简辰身边，抱住他：“我什么都不想管，只想和你在一起，永远在一起。”
“Huilanna, Miss.”

申简辰在她怀中消失，陈浩宇出现。
“Nice name. Seeing you makes me think of my maid Kuqiao—over two years since I saw her. I miss her terribly.”

“娘子，我等着你回来。”
“Obey the young prince, and he’ll grant anything—even fetching Kuqiao here to see you!”

接着梦容出现，手中拿着一把匕首对准胸口：“你若敢抢走我哥哥，我就死在你面前！”
“I’d never drag Kuqiao here. She has someone waiting for her—I only hope she and Bai Feng marry soon.”

千里寻被吓醒，发现屋内已点上红烛。
Asking about the Jin “shamans,” she learned they were royal miracle doctors—and pleaded desperately for the antidote. Huilanna dropped straight to her knees begging for her life, whispering afterward with a shy giggle that Wanyan Chi had been smiling ear to ear all day and would give her the antidote right after their wedding night.

会兰娜上前将她扶起：“姑娘，我伺候您换上新娘装。”
“Walking straight into a tiger’s jaws,” Qianli Xun muttered darkly, refusing food and lying back in the inner chamber while Huilanna cleared the untouched tray.

千里寻坐起，揉揉眼睛，才看清多了两名丫鬟，一个人手里捧着一身金国的新娘装，另一个捧着装饰精美的发冠。
Listening to the iron lock turn again, despair closed round her like stone walls—helpless for the first time in her life, trapped in a dead end until exhaustion dragged her into sleep.

“统统拿走！老娘才不会穿那玩意儿！”
In her dream, she stood beneath the Divine Willow—thick with fresh green leaves and fluttering red silk ribbons.

会兰娜好言相劝：“姑娘别再任性，您现在身上无力，根本打不过小王爷。奴婢还听说大宋的仪王不会来救您，说明他是薄情之人，您又何必为他长情？即使您在汉人中还有亲人，他们只要听说您被金人抓走，肯定都不敢前来，说不定只当您已死，人家该怎么过日子还怎么过。小王爷现在是您唯一的依靠，还是面对现实穿上嫁衣吧？”
“Long time no see,” she whispered. “Did you miss me?”

会兰娜一番劝说，倒也戳中千里寻的内心。尽管她不希望仪王筹钱来救她，可听到别人说他不会来救她，心中多少有些凄凉，也许所有感情面对现实总是不堪一击。
The willow spoke inside her mind. “Long to you; to me, a single blink.”

还有陈浩宇，曾一口一个娘子唤着她，听着十分亲热，若知道她被金人抓走，会不会愿意赶来救她？也许不会，他来了只有死路一条，说不定伤心过后又娶新欢。
“Take me home, please?”

多年之后，大家都会忘记还有她这么一个人。
“I cannot take you away.”

尽管伤感，千里寻还是不愿穿上嫁衣：“告诉完颜池，我绝不会穿上嫁衣与他成亲，死也不会。”
“You brought me here! Why can’t you take me back?”

会兰娜无法，走出洞房向小王爷禀报。
“Your own karma borrowed my power to bring you here; to return, you must rely on your own karma too.”

不一会儿完颜池身着新郎装而来，向捧着新娘妆的丫鬟挥挥手：“退下吧。”
“Karma? What karma?”

丫鬟退出后，完颜池开始脱身上的新郎装。
“Deeds woven across lifetimes gather into boundless force that carries you from place to place. Only when karma is cleared does freedom come.”

千里寻胆怯地问：“你要做什么？”
“I held to Right Thought and Right Mindfulness!” she cried out in the dream. “My conscience is clear—what karma do I owe?!”

完颜池边脱边说：“你既然不愿穿新娘装，本王穿着还有什么意义？衣裳不过是形式，关键在于洞房花烛之夜。”
The willow gave no reply. Behind her, Shen Jianchen called her name! Turning joyfully, she flung her arms around him swearing she only wanted to stay with him forever—only for Shen Jianchen to fade into Chen Haoyu waiting quietly, and then Mengrong pointing a gleaming dagger at her heart: “Steal my brother, and I die right before your eyes!”

千里寻从来没有像现在一样害怕过：“你别过来！你要是过来我就咬舌自尽！”
She gasped awake in cold sweat—and saw thick red wedding candles blazing across the room.

完颜池并不理会，将脱下的新郎装扔在地上，走到床边坐下：“你都是成过亲之人，有什么好害怕的？”
Huilanna stepped up with two maids cradling Jin bridal robes and an ornate headdress.

千里寻向床榻里边靠去：“别过来！你要是今天动我！等我功力恢复定要杀了你！”
“Get those things out of my sight! I’m never putting them on!”

“呵呵……！”完颜池笑起来，笑声停止后，继续说，“看来你不会咬舌自尽，本王就放心了。”
“Stop being stubborn, Miss!” Huilanna urged gently. “Your strength is gone; you can’t fight him. Word outside says the Song Prince Yi never came for you—he’s abandoned you. Even your family in Lin’an assumes you dead and moves on with their lives. His Highness is your only shelter left. Face reality and put on the wedding gown!”

千里寻拿起枕头扔向完颜池：“你混蛋！”
Cold reality cut deeper than any blade. Even hoping Shen Jianchen hadn’t bankrupted himself, hearing he hadn’t come chilled her heart—and Haoyu too might mourn briefly and marry someone new, until everyone forgot she ever lived. Still, pride refused to bend: “Tell Wanyan Chi I’ll die before putting on his bridal gown!”

枕头正好打在完颜池的脸上，软软的，对他毫无影响。他把掉落在身边的枕头扔在地上，袭击而去。
Minutes later, Wanyan Chi walked in wearing crimson groom’s silks, dismissed the maids, and calmly stripped off his own wedding coat.

千里寻用脚去踢，却被他抓住脚踝，她厌恶地看着对方用贪婪的目光盯着她的脚。
“What are you doing?!” she stammered, backing onto the bed.

“就不怕今晚我再咬伤你。”
“Since you hate bridal robes, why wear them? Clothes are empty form—tonight’s wedding bed is what counts.”

完颜池放下美人的脚，用手指摸摸唇上的伤：“这点儿伤对本王来讲不算什么，今晚你即使咬得本王全身是伤，本王也不怕。”
Cold terror gripped her throat. “Stay back! One step closer and I bite off my tongue!”

完颜池再次袭击而来，千里寻已做好准备，瞅准他的软肋，向下身踢去。
Tossing his coat onto the floor, he sat on the edge of the bed. “You’ve been married twice—what is there to fear?”

这招挺管用，完颜池停下袭击的动作，捂起裤裆：“你的心是石头做的吗？怎么往本王这里踢？”
“Keep back! Touch me tonight, and the moment my strength returns I’ll kill you!”

“活该！这就是欺负老娘的下场！只可惜我现在力气不大，伤不到你，否则非让你断子绝孙不可！”
“Haha! Good—if you’re waiting for strength to return, you won’t bite off your tongue after all.”

完颜池已无碍，仍旧袭击而来：“你既然想伤本王的命根子，今晚本王就用命根子征服你……”
Grabbing her pillow, she swung it straight into his face—useless against him. As he lunged forward, she kicked hard at his ankle, watched him catch it, waited till his grip eased, then lashed her foot straight up between his legs!

千里寻还想踢时，完颜池已点了她的穴位，她无法动弹，连话也说不出。
