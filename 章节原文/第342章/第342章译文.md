## Chapter 342: Tears Beneath the Bridal Candles

Waking to daylight inside a crimson wedding chamber, Qianli Xun tried jumping off the bed only to find every muscle limp as boiled noodles—her martial strength completely drained by a Jin shaman's drug!

Swearing she'd bite off her own tongue if he touched her, she bit Wanyan Chi's lip bloody when he forced a kiss—causing him to laugh darkly, send maids to clear out every blade and pair of scissors, and post armed guards outside the locked doors.

Getting nowhere trying to wheedle an antidote out of terrified maid **Huilanna**, Qianli Xun drifted into a fevered dream beneath the Divine Willow—where the spirit tree spoke of karma binding her travels before visions of Shen Jianchen, Haoyu, and knife-wielding Mengrong jolted her awake to glowing red wedding candles.

Refusing the Jurchen bridal gown outright, she watched Wanyan Chi shrug off his own groom's robe and climb onto the bed. Firing one desperate kick straight to his groin—only for him to tap her immobilizing acupoint before she could strike again—she lay paralyzed as silent tears of humiliation slipped down her cheeks.
