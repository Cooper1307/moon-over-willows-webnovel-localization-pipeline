第1章 网络作家遇网暴
## Chapter 1: A Web Novelist Meets the Online Mob

清晨的阳光努力透过窗帘的缝隙，一丝光芒洒落在一座高楼第二十二层屋内的卧室。
Morning light fought through the gap in the curtains, and a single ray fell across the bedroom on the twenty-second floor of a high-rise.

窗帘是紫色的，窗外的阳光衬得紫色更加鲜艳，使得卧室内的空气也像被染上淡淡的紫色。
The curtains were purple. The sunlight outside deepened the color until even the air in the room seemed tinted a faint violet.

卧室内装饰得简单素雅，床上躺着千里寻，她睡得正鼾。凌乱的头发散落在枕头上，被子盖到脖子处，发出轻轻的鼾声。
The room was plainly furnished. Qianli Xun lay asleep on the bed, her tangled hair spread over the pillow, the quilt drawn to her chin, soft snores escaping her.

放在床头柜上的手机铃声响起，显然是时下流行的一首歌曲。
Her phone rang on the bedside table, playing a song everyone seemed to know that season.

千里寻并未睁开双眼，伸手摸向床头柜，摸了一会儿，终于抓住手机，依然没睁眼，用睡意浓浓的声音问道：“喂，什么事？”
Without opening her eyes, Qianli Xun reached for the nightstand and felt around. At last she closed her hand on the phone. Still blind to the morning, she mumbled, "Hello? What is it?"

手机那头传来徒弟二杠焦急的声音：“师父！你怎么还睡着？赶紧打开手机，看我给你发的视频，网上都在骂你！”
From the other end came the frantic voice of her disciple Er'gang. "Master! You're still asleep? Open your phone now and watch the video I sent you. The whole internet is tearing into you!"

听到这话，千里寻完全清醒，她坐起来：“什么？网上都在骂我？为什么？”
That woke her. She sat up. "What? Everyone is after me? Why?"

“你自己看吧！早都劝你别写网络小说，你偏不听，最近躲在家里别出来，挂了！”
"See for yourself! I told you ages ago to stop writing web novels, but you wouldn't listen. Stay indoors for now. I'm hanging up!"

千里寻坐起来，被子很自然地从上身溜下去，露出淡紫色的睡衣。她急忙打开手机上的聊天工具，找到二杠的头像，是财神爷，这家伙整日梦想着发财。点开后，果然看到有视频链接，她赶紧打开，视频里传出一个女人的声音：
She sat up, and the quilt slid from her shoulders to reveal a pale lavender nightdress. She opened her messaging app and found Er'gang's avatar: the God of Wealth, fitting for a man who dreamed of riches all day. Inside was a video link. She tapped it, and a woman's voice came out of the phone:

“网络女作家千里寻被众多网友爆料，其新作品有抄袭他人，自我抄袭，水文字的现象，已失去早期作品的灵气，而且一味模式化，根本无法引起读者的兴趣……”
"Web novelist Qianli Xun has been called out by countless readers. Her new work copies other people and copies herself. It pads out chapters too. The spark of her early books is gone. She keeps grinding out the same formula, and readers have lost interest altogether..."

千里寻皱起眉头，关了视频，不想再听下去，大骂道：“老娘在网文界纵横十年，怎么写网文，要你在这里瞎掰掰！”
Qianli Xun frowned and shut the video off. She had heard enough. "I've ruled web fiction for ten years," she snapped. "Who are you to tell me how to write it?"

她把手机扔到一边，下床穿好拖鞋，走进厨房，从冰箱里拿出一瓶牛奶，给玻璃杯中倒入半杯。
She tossed the phone aside and slipped into her slippers. Then she went to the kitchen. From the fridge she took a bottle of milk and filled a glass halfway.

牛奶刚从冰箱里拿出，很凉，喝下肚对身体不好，千里寻还是知道的。她懒得到厨房热牛奶，干脆端起一旁的热水瓶，添些热水到玻璃杯中，温度刚刚好，端起来，“咕咚，咕咚”喝下，因为生气，将玻璃杯重重放在桌上。
She knew cold milk straight from the fridge was hard on the stomach, but she had no patience to heat it. She lifted the thermos beside her and added hot water until the glass felt right. Then she drank it down in two long gulps. Anger made her set the glass on the table with a thud.

她穿过客厅，走到阳台，向楼下看去，许多男男女女的记者堵在楼下，他们不断抬头向上张望。还好她住的是高档小区，每栋楼都有保安，那些记者根本上不来。
She crossed the living room to the balcony and looked down. A crowd of reporters, men and women alike, blocked the entrance below, tilting their heads up again and again. Luckily she lived in a gated compound with a guard in every building, so none of them could get in.

千里寻的嘴角露出笑容：“没想到我还挺出名，来了这么多记者。也对，能够被网暴的人大小也是个名人儿。”
A smile tugged at her mouth. "So I'm famous after all. Look at all these reporters. Then again, anyone worth dragging online must be somebody."

她离开阳台，又走进卧室，伸手从床上拿起手机，坐在床边，深吸一口气：“以放松的心态见证一下你有多出名。”
She left the balcony and went back to the bedroom. She picked up the phone from the bed and sat on the edge. She drew a breath. "Let's see just how famous you are, with a calm mind."

她点开一个短视频APP，第一个刷到的视频就在吐槽她。是位女主播，长得很一般，声音慢条斯理：
She opened a short-video app. The first clip to appear was about her. The host was a plain-looking woman with a slow, measured voice:

“家人们，大家好，千里寻这个名字不陌生吧？对！她就是著名的网络小说女作家，写过好几部网络小说，估计家人们都看过，实不相瞒，我也看过，以前还是她的粉丝。注意，我说的是以前，为什么现在不是？因为我作为读者已成长，她作为网文写手竟然还原地踏步！依然写一些十八九岁青春少女爱看的，能不被粉丝吐槽吗？回头再翻翻曾经看过的那些小说，我最终对自己说一句：曾经的你真是又傻又纯又幼稚，要不怎么会喜欢那样的小说？……”
"Hi everyone. You know the name Qianli Xun, don't you? She's the famous web novelist with several books to her name, and I'm sure most of you have read them. I have too. I used to be a fan — used to. Why not now? Because I grew as a reader while she stood still as a writer. She still churns out the sort of thing eighteen-year-old girls like. Of course her fans complain. I went back to those old books and had to tell myself: you were so young and so silly back then, or you'd never have loved that stuff..."

千里寻感慨自己内心够强大，竟然听了这么多，还是忍不住面对手机屏幕怼道：“你懂什么！老娘写小说定位的读者就是那个年龄段，你十几岁时爱看，不就证明老娘写得好？怪就怪你不再年轻！”
Qianli Xun marveled at her own patience for sitting through all that, then shot back at the screen. "What do you know? I write for readers that age. You loved my books at sixteen — doesn't that prove I did my job? Blame your own lost youth, not me!"

正埋怨着，手机里蹦出一条信息。千里寻收起视频，将信息点开，竟然是提醒她有一大半儿粉丝已退群。
A message popped up as she grumbled. She closed the video and opened it: more than half her fan group had left.

“有什么了不起！退就退！老娘是作家，又不是明星，当初网站让建粉丝群，老娘就十分不乐意，退了倒也干净，干脆全退群！”
"Big deal. Let them go! I'm a writer, not a celebrity. I never wanted a fan group when the site made me set one up. This is cleaner. Let the lot of them quit!"

话音刚落，又收到一条信息，提示她，粉丝群人数已成个位。千里寻懒得理会，继续点开视频，将那女主播刷走，映入眼帘的大标题是：“文学死了”，这个标题引起她强烈的兴趣，心想：“这条肯定跟我没关系，不妨听听。”
The words were still on her lips when another alert arrived: the group was down to single digits. She ignored it and swiped the host away. The next headline filled the screen: LITERATURE IS DEAD. That caught her. "This one can't be about me," she thought. "Might as well listen."

视频中出现一位老者，看上去很有学问，声音既洪亮又激进：
An elderly man appeared, scholarly in looks, his voice loud and combative:

“文学死了！可悲！可叹！传统作家的文学作品没有人阅读！年轻人却喜欢用手机看那些没有营养的网络小说！文学要为现实服务！要揭露现实，批判现实！要文以载道！网络小说为了经济利益，一味迎合市场，迎合读者，娱乐至死！爽文，小白文满天飞！没有一部符合文学的要求！就拿千里寻的小说来讲……”
"Literature is dead! A tragedy! A shame! Nobody reads the work of serious writers anymore, while the young scroll through brainless web novels on their phones! Literature must serve reality, expose it, indict it, carry the Way! Web fiction chases profit and panders to the market and to readers. It amuses us to death. Wish-fulfillment trash and empty prose are everywhere, and not one of them meets the standard of literature. Take the novels of Qianli Xun, for instance..."

铺垫这么多，还是为了批判千里寻。看来她真的一夜之间在网络上臭名昭著，那就听听这位激进的老者如何批判她。
All that buildup, and it came back to her. So she really had become notorious overnight. She might as well hear how the old firebrand would take her apart.

“全网都在批判，我便去翻了千里寻的小说，看了一段，实在看不下去，全是口水文，一点儿营养都没有！而传统文学语言文字有底蕴，内容丰富，值得人品味。网络小说根本无法与传统文学相比！我呼吁！趁全网批判千里寻的作品，掀起批判网络小说的浪潮，复兴传统文学！……”
"The whole internet is condemning her, so I went and read some of Qianli Xun's fiction. I couldn't get through a page. It is all filler, not an ounce of substance. Traditional literature has depth in its language and richness in its content; it rewards a second reading. Web novels cannot compare. I call on you: while the net is judging Qianli Xun, let us raise a wave of judgment against web fiction itself and restore real literature!..."

千里寻用手指在屏幕上点了一下，老者的声音立刻停止，她指着手机屏幕：
Qianli Xun tapped the screen. The old man's voice cut off. She pointed at the phone:

“你讲得那么慷慨激昂，有本事写部网络小说试试，你肯定不会写！老娘告诉你，文学没死！在网络时代，文学换了一个样貌与读者见面。你懂不懂文学史？要是不懂就去看看，每个时代的文学都有其独一无二的特点，传统文学已不符合这个时代，必然会被淘汰，你想复兴根本就没用！说不定几百年之后，我千里寻的作品也会成为经典。老娘还想问你，我的小说让读者开心快乐有什么不对？曹雪芹曾经还说《红楼梦》不过是游戏笔墨，陶情适性而已，连四大名著之首都娱乐大众，网络小说不过是继承了文学的优良传统而已！……”
"So stirring. Try writing a web novel yourself. You couldn't. Let me tell you something: literature isn't dead. In the internet age it meets readers in a new shape. Do you know the history of literature? Go read it. Every age has its own form, and the old form no longer fits this one, so it will pass away whether you revive it or not. A few centuries from now, my books may be classics too. And tell me this: what's wrong with my novels making readers happy? Cao Xueqin said Dream of the Red Chamber was mere play with a brush, written to amuse and soothe the spirit. Even the greatest of the Four Classics set out to entertain. Web fiction has inherited that fine tradition..."

千里寻还没发泄完，手机屏幕闪现出“悠悠编辑”四个字，是网站编辑打来的电话。
Before she had finished, four characters flashed on the screen: Editor Youyou. The site's editor was calling.

千里寻点了绿色的通话键，传来编辑的声音，是个温柔的女声：“千里寻，网站上层决定，鉴于网上喷你的人太多，为了维护网站形象，你的所有作品一律下架，包括你正在写的作品，因作品下架给你造成的损失，网站会酌情补偿。”
She pressed the green key. A gentle woman's voice came through. "Qianli Xun, the site's management has decided that, with so many people attacking you online, we have to protect our image. All your works are being taken down, including the one you're writing. The site will compensate you for the loss at its discretion."

“怎么能这样？再说能被人喷，说明我有名气，作品更不应该被下架……”
"How can you do that? Besides, if people are attacking me, that means I'm known. That's all the more reason to keep my books up..."

编辑根本不想继续听下去，迅速挂断电话，千里寻既无奈又生气，她想把电话扔到地上，发泄她的不满，还是忍住，得好好想想接下来该怎么办？难道创作生涯就此中断？这可是她最喜欢做的事。
The editor had no wish to hear more and hung up at once. Qianli Xun felt both helpless and furious. She wanted to hurl the phone at the floor to let it out, but held herself back. She had to think about what came next. Was her writing life over just like that? It was the one thing she loved doing.
