第1章 网络作家遇网暴
## Chapter 1: Chapter 1: Web Novelist Hit by Online Hate

清晨的阳光努力透过窗帘的缝隙，一丝光芒洒落在一座高楼第二十二层屋内的卧室。
Morning sunlight pushed through the gap in the curtains, spilling a thin shaft of light across a twenty-second-floor bedroom in a high-rise apartment.

窗帘是紫色的，窗外的阳光衬得紫色更加鲜艳，使得卧室内的空气也像被染上淡淡的紫色。
The curtains were purple. Sunlight from outside sharpened the hue, tinting the bedroom air with a pale violet glow.

卧室内装饰得简单素雅，床上躺着千里寻，她睡得正鼾。凌乱的头发散落在枕头上，被子盖到脖子处，发出轻轻的鼾声。
The room was plain and neat. Qianli Xun lay fast asleep on the bed, messy hair splayed across the pillow, quilt pulled up to her neck, letting out soft snores.

放在床头柜上的手机铃声响起，显然是时下流行的一首歌曲。
A phone rang on the nightstand, playing a current pop hit.

千里寻并未睁开双眼，伸手摸向床头柜，摸了一会儿，终于抓住手机，依然没睁眼，用睡意浓浓的声音问道：“喂，什么事？”
Without opening her eyes, Qianli Xun reached toward the nightstand, groped around for a moment, and grabbed the phone. Still eyes shut, she asked in a thick, drowsy voice, "Hello? What is it?"

手机那头传来徒弟二杠焦急的声音：“师父！你怎么还睡着？赶紧打开手机，看我给你发的视频，网上都在骂你！”
Her apprentice Er'gang's frantic voice burst through the speaker: "Master! How are you still asleep? Open your phone right now and watch the video I sent you—everyone online is tearing you apart!"

听到这话，千里寻完全清醒，她坐起来：“什么？网上都在骂我？为什么？”
Qianli Xun snapped wide awake and sat up. "What? Everyone online is tearing me apart? Why?"

“你自己看吧！早都劝你别写网络小说，你偏不听，最近躲在家里别出来，挂了！”
"See for yourself! I told you ages ago to stop writing web novels, and you wouldn't listen. Stay indoors for a while and don't go out. Hanging up!"

千里寻坐起来，被子很自然地从上身溜下去，露出淡紫色的睡衣。她急忙打开手机上的聊天工具，找到二杠的头像，是财神爷，这家伙整日梦想着发财。点开后，果然看到有视频链接，她赶紧打开，视频里传出一个女人的声音：
As she sat up, the quilt slid off her shoulders, revealing pale purple pajamas. She opened her chat app and tapped Er'gang's profile picture—the God of Wealth, since the guy dreamed of getting rich day and night. Sure enough, a video link sat there. She tapped it, and a woman's voice rang out:

“网络女作家千里寻被众多网友爆料，其新作品有抄袭他人，自我抄袭，水文字的现象，已失去早期作品的灵气，而且一味模式化，根本无法引起读者的兴趣……”
"Numerous netizens have exposed female web novelist Qianli Xun for plagiarizing others, recycling her own plots, and padding her chapters with fluff. She has lost the spark of her early work and churns out formulaic stories that fail to hold readers' interest..."

千里寻皱起眉头，关了视频，不想再听下去，大骂道：“老娘在网文界纵横十年，怎么写网文，要你在这里瞎掰掰！”
Qianli Xun frowned and shut off the video, refusing to listen to another word. "I've ruled the web-novel world for ten years! Who are you to spout nonsense about how I write?"

她把手机扔到一边，下床穿好拖鞋，走进厨房，从冰箱里拿出一瓶牛奶，给玻璃杯中倒入半杯。
She tossed the phone aside, climbed out of bed, slipped into her slippers, and walked to the kitchen. She pulled a bottle of milk from the fridge and poured half a glass.

牛奶刚从冰箱里拿出，很凉，喝下肚对身体不好，千里寻还是知道的。她懒得到厨房热牛奶，干脆端起一旁的热水瓶，添些热水到玻璃杯中，温度刚刚好，端起来，“咕咚，咕咚”喝下，因为生气，将玻璃杯重重放在桌上。
Cold milk straight from the fridge was bad for the stomach—she knew that much. Too lazy to heat it on the stove, she picked up the thermos next to her and topped the glass off with hot water until it felt just right. She raised the glass, gulped it down, and slammed it onto the table in anger.

她穿过客厅，走到阳台，向楼下看去，许多男男女女的记者堵在楼下，他们不断抬头向上张望。还好她住的是高档小区，每栋楼都有保安，那些记者根本上不来。
She crossed the living room to the balcony and looked down. Crowds of reporters, men and women alike, jammed the area below, craning their necks upward. Good thing she lived in an upscale complex with security guards stationed at every building; none of them could get up here.

千里寻的嘴角露出笑容：“没想到我还挺出名，来了这么多记者。也对，能够被网暴的人大小也是个名人儿。”
A smirk tugged at Qianli Xun's lips. "Didn't realize I was this famous—look at all those reporters. Well, makes sense. You've got to be somebody to get canceled online."

她离开阳台，又走进卧室，伸手从床上拿起手机，坐在床边，深吸一口气：“以放松的心态见证一下你有多出名。”
Leaving the balcony, she went back to the bedroom, grabbed her phone off the bed, sat on the edge, and took a deep breath. "Time to kick back and see just how famous you really are."

她点开一个短视频APP，第一个刷到的视频就在吐槽她。是位女主播，长得很一般，声音慢条斯理：
She opened a short-video app. The very first clip roasting her featured a plain-looking female streamer speaking in an unhurried drawl:

“家人们，大家好，千里寻这个名字不陌生吧？对！她就是著名的网络小说女作家，写过好几部网络小说，估计家人们都看过，实不相瞒，我也看过，以前还是她的粉丝。注意，我说的是以前，为什么现在不是？因为我作为读者已成长，她作为网文写手竟然还原地踏步！依然写一些十八九岁青春少女爱看的，能不被粉丝吐槽吗？回头再翻翻曾经看过的那些小说，我最终对自己说一句：曾经的你真是又傻又纯又幼稚，要不怎么会喜欢那样的小说？……”
"Hey, chat! The name Qianli Xun rings a bell, right? Yep! She's that big-name web novelist who wrote a bunch of hit stories—bet most of you have read them. Truth be told, I read them too, and I used to be a fan. Notice I said used to be. Why not anymore? Because I grew up as a reader, while she's still stuck in place as a writer! She keeps churning out stories aimed at eighteen- and nineteen-year-old girls. How could fans not roast her? Looking back at the novels I once loved, all I can tell my younger self is: you were so naive, gullible, and immature—how else could you have liked stuff like that?..."

千里寻感慨自己内心够强大，竟然听了这么多，还是忍不住面对手机屏幕怼道：“你懂什么！老娘写小说定位的读者就是那个年龄段，你十几岁时爱看，不就证明老娘写得好？怪就怪你不再年轻！”
Qianli Xun marveled at her own thick skin for listening that long, yet she could not help snapping back at the screen: "What do you know? That age group is my target audience! If you loved my books as a teenager, doesn't that prove I did my job right? Blame yourself for getting old!"

正埋怨着，手机里蹦出一条信息。千里寻收起视频，将信息点开，竟然是提醒她有一大半儿粉丝已退群。
Mid-rant, a notification popped up. She minimized the video and tapped it—more than half the members of her fan group chat had quit.

“有什么了不起！退就退！老娘是作家，又不是明星，当初网站让建粉丝群，老娘就十分不乐意，退了倒也干净，干脆全退群！”
"Big deal! Leave if you want! I'm a writer, not some pop idol. I hated setting up a fan group when the platform asked me to anyway. Good riddance—go ahead and clear out the whole chat!"

话音刚落，又收到一条信息，提示她，粉丝群人数已成个位。千里寻懒得理会，继续点开视频，将那女主播刷走，映入眼帘的大标题是：“文学死了”，这个标题引起她强烈的兴趣，心想：“这条肯定跟我没关系，不妨听听。”
No sooner had she spoken than another alert flashed: her fan group was down to single digits. Too annoyed to care, she went back to the video app and swiped past the streamer. A bold headline caught her eye: "Literature Is Dead." Intrigued, she thought, This one definitely has nothing to do with me. Might as well listen.

视频中出现一位老者，看上去很有学问，声音既洪亮又激进：
An elderly man appeared on screen, looking every inch the scholar, his voice booming and fiery:

“文学死了！可悲！可叹！传统作家的文学作品没有人阅读！年轻人却喜欢用手机看那些没有营养的网络小说！文学要为现实服务！要揭露现实，批判现实！要文以载道！网络小说为了经济利益，一味迎合市场，迎合读者，娱乐至死！爽文，小白文满天飞！没有一部符合文学的要求！就拿千里寻的小说来讲……”
"Literature is dead! How tragic! How lamentable! Nobody reads the works of traditional authors anymore! Young people would rather scroll through mindless web fiction on their phones! Literature must serve reality—expose it, critique it, and convey the Way! Driven by profit, web novels pander to the market and amuse readers to death! Power fantasies and brainless reads flood every corner, and not a single one meets the standard of real literature! Take Qianli Xun's novels, for instance..."

铺垫这么多，还是为了批判千里寻。看来她真的一夜之间在网络上臭名昭著，那就听听这位激进的老者如何批判她。
All that buildup just to tear into her. She really had become notorious overnight. Fine—she would hear what this firebrand old man had to say.

“全网都在批判，我便去翻了千里寻的小说，看了一段，实在看不下去，全是口水文，一点儿营养都没有！而传统文学语言文字有底蕴，内容丰富，值得人品味。网络小说根本无法与传统文学相比！我呼吁！趁全网批判千里寻的作品，掀起批判网络小说的浪潮，复兴传统文学！……”
"With the whole internet criticizing her, I went and flipped through Qianli Xun's novels. After one passage, I couldn't stomach another word—pure drivel with zero substance! Traditional literature has depth of language, richness of content, and layers worth savoring. Web fiction cannot hold a candle to it! I call on everyone: seize this wave of outrage against Qianli Xun's work, launch a campaign against web fiction, and revive traditional literature!..."

千里寻用手指在屏幕上点了一下，老者的声音立刻停止，她指着手机屏幕：
Qianli Xun tapped the screen, cutting the old man off mid-sentence, and pointed straight at her phone.

“你讲得那么慷慨激昂，有本事写部网络小说试试，你肯定不会写！老娘告诉你，文学没死！在网络时代，文学换了一个样貌与读者见面。你懂不懂文学史？要是不懂就去看看，每个时代的文学都有其独一无二的特点，传统文学已不符合这个时代，必然会被淘汰，你想复兴根本就没用！说不定几百年之后，我千里寻的作品也会成为经典。老娘还想问你，我的小说让读者开心快乐有什么不对？曹雪芹曾经还说《红楼梦》不过是游戏笔墨，陶情适性而已，连四大名著之首都娱乐大众，网络小说不过是继承了文学的优良传统而已！……”
"Such a fiery speech—if you're so capable, try writing a web novel yourself! You wouldn't even know where to start! Let me tell you something: literature isn't dead! In the digital age, it just put on a new face to meet its readers. Do you even know literary history? Go read up on it if you don't! Every era has its own unique form of literature. Traditional writing doesn't fit this age, so it's bound to fade—reviving it won't work! Who knows, a few hundred years from now, my books might be classics too! And let me ask you this: what's wrong with my stories making readers happy? Even Cao Xueqin said Dream of the Red Chamber was just playful ink meant to lift the spirits. If the greatest of the Four Great Classical Novels existed to entertain people, web fiction is just carrying on literature's finest tradition!..."

千里寻还没发泄完，手机屏幕闪现出“悠悠编辑”四个字，是网站编辑打来的电话。
Before she could finish venting, "Editor Youyou" flashed across her screen—a call from her platform editor.

千里寻点了绿色的通话键，传来编辑的声音，是个温柔的女声：“千里寻，网站上层决定，鉴于网上喷你的人太多，为了维护网站形象，你的所有作品一律下架，包括你正在写的作品，因作品下架给你造成的损失，网站会酌情补偿。”
Qianli Xun tapped the green call button. A gentle female voice came through. "Qianli Xun, upper management has made a decision. Given the massive backlash against you online, to protect the site's image, all of your works are being taken down, including your ongoing serialization. The platform will offer fair compensation for your losses."

“怎么能这样？再说能被人喷，说明我有名气，作品更不应该被下架……”
"How can you do that? Besides, getting roasted just proves I have clout—that's all the more reason not to pull my books..."

编辑根本不想继续听下去，迅速挂断电话，千里寻既无奈又生气，她想把电话扔到地上，发泄她的不满，还是忍住，得好好想想接下来该怎么办？难道创作生涯就此中断？这可是她最喜欢做的事。
The editor had no patience to listen and hung up at once. Helpless and fuming, Qianli Xun wanted to hurl the phone to the floor to blow off steam, yet she held back. She needed to think about her next move. Was her creative career really over just like that? Writing was the thing she loved most in the world.
