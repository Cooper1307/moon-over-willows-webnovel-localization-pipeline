# 第69章 乘船游湖遇纨绔

---

船只行到湖中心时，剩下的桂花酿已被二人喝完。

When the boat reached the center of the lake, the two had finished the remaining osmanthus wine.

---

千里寻拿起瓶子，继续往杯子里倒，只从瓶口掉下一滴。她放下瓶子：“桂花酿实在好喝，像我喝过的饮料。”

Qianli Xun picked up the bottle and tried to pour more into her cup, but only a single drop fell from the opening. She put down the bottle: “This osmanthus wine is so delicious—it reminds me of a soft drink I once had.”

---

“饮料？是酒吗？我怎么没听说过？”乔文仙仔细搜索记忆，临安的美酒他再熟悉不过，在汴梁时，也品过不少汴梁的美酒，从未听过“饮料”这个酒名。

"A soft drink? Is it a kind of wine? I've never heard of it." Qiao Wenxian searched his memory carefully. He was intimately familiar with the fine wines of Lin'an, and had also tasted many wines from Bianliang, but he had never heard of a wine called "soft drink."

---

千里寻认真解释：“饮料不是酒，喝起来甜甜的，有水果的味道。”

Qianli Xun explained seriously: "A soft drink isn't wine. It tastes sweet, with a fruity flavor."

---

文渊对新鲜事物总是充满好奇：“不知哪里有？我可否品尝一下？”

Wenyuan was always curious about new things: "I wonder where I can find it? Could I taste it?"

---

千里寻瞎编道：“有一天我睡得很沉，做了一个神奇的梦，在梦中见到饮料，喝过一口，味道实在好喝，就记住了。”

Qianli Xun made something up: "One day I was sleeping deeply and had a magical dream. In the dream, I saw a soft drink and took a sip. It tasted so good that I remembered it."

---

文渊瞧着淑真像桃花一样微红的脸颊：“原来是黄粱一梦，饮料并非酒，可桂花酿是酒，喝多了会上头。”

Wenyuan looked at Shuzhen's peach-blossom-pink cheeks: “So it was just a golden millet dream—a fleeting illusion. A soft drink isn't wine, but osmanthus wine is. Drink too much, and it'll go to your head.”

---

“难怪我觉得脸颊发热，原来桂花酿是酒，不过我脑子还很清晰，也不觉得晕，应该没有喝醉吧？”

“No wonder my face feels warm—so osmanthus wine is wine after all. But my mind is still clear, and I don't feel dizzy, so I shouldn't be drunk, right？”

---

“你只是微醉，不会有事，即使真醉了，也不用担心，有我在你身边。”乔文仙靠近淑真，“你微醉的样子实在好看。”

“You're just slightly tipsy. You'll be fine. And even if you were really drunk, there's no need to worry—I'm right here with you.” Qiao Wenxian moved closer to Shuzhen. “You look really beautiful when you're tipsy.”

---

被文渊夸奖，千里寻既开心又害羞，在酒精的作用下站起来，望着周围茫茫的湖水：“湖水的味道真好闻！我感觉自己像是水中的仙子！”

Flattered by Wenyuan's compliment, Qianli Xun felt both happy and shy. Under the influence of alcohol, she stood up and gazed at the vast lake around them: "The lake smells so wonderful! I feel like a nymph dancing on the water!"

---

千里寻闭上眼睛，感受着湖面的微风拂过面颊，希望风将面颊上的红晕带走，不要让文渊看出她的娇羞。

Qianli Xun closed her eyes, feeling the gentle breeze from the lake brush against her cheeks. She hoped the wind would carry away the blush on her face so Wenyuan wouldn't notice her shyness.

---

她感受到一只手臂搂在腰间，她没有睁开眼，很自然地将头靠在对方的肩膀处，用心享受着文渊的臂膀带来的温暖。

She felt an arm wrap around her waist. Without opening her eyes, she naturally rested her head against his shoulder, savoring the warmth from Wenyuan's embrace.

---

苦荞用手把南双的眼睛捂住：“你还小，不能看。”

Kuqiao covered Nanshuang's eyes with her hand: "You're too young to watch this."

---

南双把苦荞的手挪开：“我已经十四了，一点都不小，苦荞姐姐比我大不了几岁，你看得我为何看不得？”

Nanshuang moved Kuqiao's hand away: “I'm already fourteen—not young at all. Sister Kuqiao, you're only a few years older than me. If you can watch, why can't I？”

---

苦荞不再管南双，他们一起瞧着这对情侣亲密的背影。

Kuqiao stopped bothering with Nanshuang. Together, they watched the intimate silhouette of the couple.

---

南双悄悄说：“苦荞姐姐，我家公子跟你家小姐真是郎才女貌。”

Nanshuang whispered: "Sister Kuqiao, your young lady and my young master are truly a talented man and a beautiful woman."

---

苦荞反对道：“不对，应该是女才郎貌，好像也不对，反正他们两个都是既有才又有貌，实在般配，我家小姐总算找对人。”

Kuqiao corrected: “No, it should be a talented woman and a handsome man. Hmm, that doesn't sound right either. Anyway, they're both talented and good-looking—a perfect match. My young lady has finally found the right person.”

---

陈浩宇等纨绔子弟乘坐一艘较大的船只行驶到湖中心，几人继续在船上烧烤，边说笑饮酒，边吃着炙烤的肉块。

Chen Haoyu and the other playboys were on a larger boat that had sailed to the center of the lake. They continued their barbecue on board, laughing, drinking, and eating grilled meat.

---

有人说：“你们瞧，那不是乔文仙吗？什么时候身边多了一位美娇娘？还那么亲密？真是少见！”

Someone said: “Look over there! Isn't that Qiao Wenxian? When did a beautiful lady appear by his side? And they're so intimate—how unusual！”

---

陈浩宇向不远处的船只瞧去：“哼！我当是谁！不就是我们陈家的亲家姑娘，没想到，他们两个搞到一起，也忒快了些。听说她昨日才与林觉和离，今日就跑到别的男人怀里，实在有失女德。”

Chen Haoyu glanced at the boat not far away: “Hmph! I thought it was someone! Isn't that the Zhu girl—our affinal kin? I didn't expect them to get together so fast. I heard she just divorced Lin Jue yesterday, and today she's already in another man's arms—shameless.”

---

有人问：“那女的难道是江南女词人朱淑真？”

Someone asked: "Could that woman be Zhu Shuzhen, the famous female poet from Jiangnan?"

---

陈浩宇很不屑：“除了她还能有谁？”

Chen Haoyu was dismissive: "Who else could it be?"

---

有人边吃炙烤的肉，边说：“陈公子看上去对这位亲家姑娘很不满。”

Someone said while eating grilled meat: "Young Master Chen seems quite dissatisfied with this affinal relative."

---

“当然不满，她可是我的死对头。”陈浩宇心生一计，用手招来一位小弟，对着小弟的耳边悄悄说了几句。

“Of course I'm dissatisfied—she's my sworn enemy.” Chen Haoyu came up with a plan and beckoned one of his lackeys, whispering something in his ear.

---

那小弟走到四位划船的船工跟前，向他们传达了陈公子的意思。

The lackey went to the four boatmen and conveyed Young Master Chen's instructions.

---

只见船工开始用力划船，扭转大船的船头，向对方的船只撞去……

The boatmen began rowing hard, turning the bow of the large boat toward the other vessel and ramming into it...

---

千里寻睁开眼，仍旧沉浸在湖面美景和爱情之中，无意中用余光瞥见冲撞而来的大船，一把抱住文渊：“不好！”

Qianli Xun opened her eyes, still immersed in the lake's beauty and her romance. Out of the corner of her eye, she caught sight of the large boat charging toward them. She grabbed Wenyuan: "Oh no!"

---

乔文仙还未反应过来，大船已撞了上来，船身立刻颠簸起来，他与淑真一起掉进水里，还好二人识水性。

Before Qiao Wenxian could react, the large boat had crashed into them. The boat immediately lurched, and he and Shuzhen fell into the water. Fortunately, both could swim.

---

苦荞和南双坐着，问题不大，都担心自家小姐和公子。他们趴在船上，伸出手，去抓各自的主人，因船颠簸得厉害，怎么都无法抓住对方的手。

Kuqiao and Nanshuang, who were sitting, were fine, but they worried about their young lady and young master. They leaned over the boat, reaching out to grab their masters, but because the boat was rocking so violently, they couldn't grasp their hands.

---

船夫继续操纵船桨，用熟练的技巧努力控制颠簸的船只……

The boatman continued to work the oars, using skilled techniques to try to control the rocking boat...

---

船身未稳，二人只能先游到一起，准备待船稳后再爬上去。

With the boat still unsteady, the two could only swim together first, planning to climb back aboard once it stabilized.

---

传来一阵笑声：“哈哈哈……！”

A burst of laughter came over: "Hahaha..."

---

千里寻顺着笑声看去，不是陈浩宇又是哪个！

Qianli Xun followed the sound of laughter. Who else could it be but Chen Haoyu!

---

她大骂道：“陈浩宇！老娘今日没找你的事，你竟然主动欺负到我头上，待会儿老娘上去，看我怎么收拾你！”

She cursed loudly: “Chen Haoyu! I didn't come looking for trouble today, you hear me? Yet you dared to bully me! Just wait till I get over there—I'll teach you a lesson！”

---

乔文仙第一次见这样的淑真，既新奇又诧异。

Qiao Wenxian had never seen Shuzhen like this before. He felt both intrigued and surprised.

---

“哈哈……！都成落汤鸡，还嘴硬。”陈浩宇继续笑着，又向乔文仙说，“乔文仙，你放着临安那么多好姑娘不要，偏偏喜欢一个既离过婚，又是母老虎的女人，爱好实在独特，佩服佩服！”

“Hahaha...! You're both drowned rats, yet you still talk tough.” Chen Haoyu continued laughing, then turned to Qiao Wenxian: “Qiao Wenxian, with so many fine girls in Lin'an to choose from, you had to pick a woman who's both divorced and a tigress. Your taste is truly unique—I admire it！”

---

乔文仙虽觉得刚才的淑真与她所认识的不同，但不管淑真变成什么样，他都不介意。

Although Qiao Wenxian felt that Shuzhen just now was different from the one he knew, he didn't mind no matter what she became.

---

“陈公子，请你不要污蔑淑真，她是有过一段婚姻，那又怎样？我喜欢的是她这个人，至于你说她是'母老虎'，我不以为然，淑真跟我在一起时，既温柔又可爱，根本不是你说的那样。我看她只有遇到像你这样的人，才会变成母老虎。”

“Young Master Chen, please don't slander Shuzhen. Yes, she's had a marriage before—so what? I like her for who she is. As for calling her a 'tigress,' I disagree. When Shuzhen is with me, she's both gentle and adorable—nothing like what you describe. I think she only turns into a tigress when she encounters someone like you.”

---

在水中，乔文仙转向淑真：“淑真，以后有我在你身边，不用再将自己伪装得那么凶，我会保护你。”

In the water, Qiao Wenxian turned to Shuzhen: "Shuzhen, from now on, with me by your side, you don't need to pretend to be so fierce. I'll protect you."

---

事实上千里寻并不需要文渊保护，可听到喜欢的人说关切她的话，感动在所难免：“文渊，有你真好。”

In fact, Qianli Xun didn't need Wenyuan's protection. But hearing words of concern from the one she loved, she couldn't help but be moved: "Wenyuan, it's so good to have you."

---

陈浩宇实在看不下去：“喂！你们两个要不要脸？一个是临安才子，一个是女词人，当着这么多人的面说那些肉麻的话，真是丢人！”

Chen Haoyu couldn't stand it anymore: “Hey! Do you two have any shame? One is Lin'an's talented scholar, the other a famous female poet, yet you're saying such lovey-dovey nonsense in front of so many people—how embarrassing！”

---

船已稳，在苦荞、南双和船工的帮助下，两人先后被拉上船只。

The boat had stabilized. With the help of Kuqiao, Nanshuang, and the boatmen, the two were pulled back aboard one after the other.

---

浑身上下已湿透，一大早的装扮算是全毁了，一切拜陈浩宇所赐。

They were soaked from head to toe. The careful dressing from early morning was completely ruined—all thanks to Chen Haoyu.

---

对面的大船上，陈浩宇正在为他的杰作沾沾自喜，一群纨绔子弟都露出嘲笑的表情。

On the large boat opposite, Chen Haoyu was smugly pleased with his handiwork, while the group of playboys all wore mocking expressions.

---

千里寻气不打一处来：“陈浩宇！我两次碰到你光天化日之下欺负良家妇女，不要脸的是你，丢人的也是你！你等着，看我怎么收拾你！”

Qianli Xun was furious: “Chen Haoyu! Twice I've caught you bullying respectable women in broad daylight. The shameless one is you, the embarrassing one is you! Just you wait—I'll deal with you！”

---

她想要跳到对面的大船上。

She wanted to jump onto the large boat opposite.

---

乔文仙将她拦住：“离那船还有一段距离，恐怕跳不过去，你又掉到水里。不必跟他置气，我们把船划到那边，离他们远些。”

Qiao Wenxian stopped her: "There's still some distance to that boat. I'm afraid you won't make it and will fall into the water again. No need to get angry with him. Let's row our boat over there, away from them."

---

陈浩宇担心朱淑真跳过来打他一顿。按照他目测，两船之间的距离对朱淑真来讲没有任何阻碍，要不是乔文仙拦着，恐怕这会儿已被她三拳两脚打趴下，还是见好就收，不管怎么说今日算是把她搞得挺惨。于是大声命令：“赶紧把船划走！”

Chen Haoyu was worried that Zhu Shuzhen might jump over and beat him up. By his estimation, the distance between the two boats was no obstacle for Zhu Shuzhen. If Qiao Wenxian hadn't stopped her, he'd probably already be beaten down with three punches and two kicks by now. Better to take the win and run—after all, he'd made her pretty miserable today. So he shouted loudly: “Row the boat away quickly！”

---

船工卖力地划着，确定距离安全后，陈浩宇扯着嗓子喊道：“朱淑真！本公子今日总算赢你一回！哈哈哈……！”

The boatmen rowed hard. Once they confirmed a safe distance, Chen Haoyu shouted at the top of his lungs: "Zhu Shuzhen! This young master finally won one against you today! Hahaha..."

---

千里寻在心里骂道：“要不是顾及文渊，老娘今日肯定会跳到大船上，打得你跪地求饶！”

Qianli Xun cursed in her heart: "If it weren't for Wenyuan, I'd have jumped onto that big boat right now and beaten you until you begged for mercy on your knees!"

---

好好的一次乘船游湖，被陈浩宇搞得兴致全无。

What should have been a wonderful boat ride on the lake was completely ruined by Chen Haoyu.

---

因为水凉，文渊打了个喷嚏：“阿嚏！”

Because the water was cold, Wenyuan sneezed: "Achoo!"

---

“淑真，我们还是尽快靠岸，你我身上都湿漉漉的，需找个地方换件衣裳，北面有家裁缝铺，里面有成衣，我们过去现买一身换上。”

"Shuzhen, let's get to shore as soon as possible. We're both soaked and need to find a place to change clothes. There's a tailor's shop to the north that sells ready-made clothes. Let's go there and buy a set to change into."

---

千里寻无不同意，对船工说：“请两位师傅直接把船划到北岸，我们要在那里下船。”

Qianli Xun agreed without hesitation and said to the boatmen: "Please row the boat directly to the north shore. We'll disembark there."

---

船工调转船头，向西湖北岸滑去。

The boatmen turned the bow and glided toward the north shore of West Lake.
