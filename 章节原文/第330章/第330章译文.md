## Chapter 330: Mid-Autumn of the Seventh Year of Xuanhe

Back in Lin'an, Haoyu showed both clans Prince Yi's signed pledge, formally filed dissolution papers on paper while leaving Moxiang Courtyard untouched, turned away every matchmaker, threw himself into Chen trade, and traveled north every six months to visit his "wife"—always checking carefully that Shen Jianchen had kept his word about the edict.

Nearly two years flew past until **Mid-Autumn of the seventh year of Xuanhe** arrived. Riding north early with Kuqiao and Qinggua to bring his wife home, Haoyu ran straight into armed highwaymen outside Jiangning Prefecture! Thinking fast, Haoyu whispered to his driver to jab the horse's rump with a sewing needle the second Qinggua stepped down with the banknote chest—sending the carriage bolting clear with Kuqiao while the bandits hauled Haoyu and Qinggua off to cook in their mountain camp! Racing safely back toward Lin'an by back roads, Kuqiao and the driver went for rescue.

Meanwhile, on Mid-Autumn morning outside Bianliang's prison gates, Shen Jianchen waited with a hammering heart as Qianli Xun blinked out into bright sunlight—searching briefly for Haoyu before boarding the prince's carriage.

Bathed, dressed fresh, and devouring roast chicken, Hundred-Flavor Soup, and braised cabbage across from Shen Jianchen at Prince Yi's Manor, Qianli Xun froze when he set his chopsticks down and asked quietly: *"If you were completely free—go back to Lin'an with Haoyu, or stay in Bianliang with me?"*

Unable to betray Haoyu's two-year loyalty—and terrified her own heart would cave—she murmured she'd repay his debt in the next life and fled straight to Mengrong's courtyard.

Inside, clutching a silk-crafted plum blossom, Mengrong lit up at *"Shuzhen!"*—tugging her straight onto the bed to sleep in late, demanding an unlit brazier to chat beside, and weeping *"Promise you won't steal my Brother!"* before assuring her happily that her real Brother had gone to Heaven! When Xilan announced Prince Yi at the doorway, Mengrong hugged him briefly—then shoved him back crying *"You're not my Brother!"* and hugged her artificial plum blossom, reciting verbatim the poem Shen Jianchen had borrowed from Mao Zedong upon first transmigrating:

*"Wind and rain escorted Spring's departure, / Flying snow welcomes Spring's return. / On the ice-clad rock rising high and sheer, / A flower blooms sweet and fair. / Sweet and fair, she craves not Spring for herself alone, / To be the harbinger of Spring she is content. / When the mountain flowers are in full bloom, / She will smile mingling in their midst."* [TN: Translated by Foreign Languages Press]
