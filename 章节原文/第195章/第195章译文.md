## Chapter 195: Follow Fate, Never Force It

Reaching the back courtyard and seeing lamplight glowing across from him in Matron Wei's room, Chen Haoyu grumbled, "Shoving me right across from an old matron—she really has nerve!" Still powerless against Zhu Shuzhen, he pushed into his assigned room, where Qinggua was patting down the bedding.

"Young Madam had this room scrubbed spotless and I made your bed fresh, Young Master," Qinggua greeted him.

"Look how pathetic your master has been brought—banished to a plain shack in the back yard!"

"On your wedding night too—barred from your own bridal chamber!" Qinggua sympathized. "Why not tattle to Master and Madam tomorrow and let them put Young Madam in her place?"

Chen Haoyu kicked out at him. "What do you know?! Like I even WANT to share a bridal chamber with her!"

Dodging aside, Qinggua scratched his head. "Beats me why you even got married if you refuse the bridal chamber."

Ignoring him, Chen Haoyu spotted an extra cot against the wall. "Why is there a second bed in MY room?!"

Backing out of kicking range with a cheeky grin, Qinggua chuckled, "Heh, Young Madam ordered me to bunk in here so I can wait on you handy!"

"Zhu Shuzhen! You pushed me too far! Wait until I wrap up my business—I will slap you with a charge of abusing your husband and divorce your hide!" Slamming his fist onto the tabletop, Chen Haoyu winced—not a scratch on the wood, while his knuckles throbbed red. "Ow! Damn, that hurts!"

Rubbing his master's hand, Qinggua murmured, "You keep injuring yourself today—take it easy and stop feuding with Young Madam."

"Hands off—help me undress for bed!"

Peeling off his master's crimson groom robes and climbing into his own cot once Chen Haoyu lay down, Qinggua grinned into the dark—sharing a room with his master felt downright novel, and watching Young Madam tame him flat on day one finally explained why Master and Madam had fought so hard to bring a divorced Miss Zhu home!

Meanwhile, back when Shen Jianchen had carried Qiao Mengrong to a clinic, the physician bandaged her wound and reassured him it was shallow and posed no danger. Exhaling hard, Shen Jianchen carried her back to Youlin Retreat, cursing how hollow his princely title had proved—in every costume drama back home, princes took wives and concubines at will, yet one Qiao Mengrong had tied his hands and cost him the woman he loved!

Laying her onto her bed at Youlin Retreat and watching tears gleam on her lashes, he murmured softly, "Never pull a stunt like that again."

"Only if Brother stops pulling stunts first."

When he moved to rise, she caught his hand. "Stay with me a little while, Brother."

Weighed down with guilt, he sat on the bedside. "Alright. Brother will sit with you."

Closing her eyes, Qiao Mengrong let silent tears roll down her temples. Taking a silk handkerchief from Xilan, Shen Jianchen gently blotted them away. Before he drew back, her fingers closed around his wrist. "Tell me, Brother—when did it start?"

Dreading fresh grief, he hedged. "Wait until your wound heals, then I will answer."

Opening swimming eyes, she shook her head. "No. I want to know right now."

Glancing over, Shen Jianchen told Xilan, "Step outside with Bai Feng." Both attendants exited and closed the door behind them.

Freeing his wrist to wipe another tear, Shen Jianchen spoke the raw truth. "I am not Zhao Pu, and she is not Youqi Jushi. We knew each other in another age altogether. I loved her long ago, and after finding her again in this world against all odds, I could not just walk away..."

Flashes returned to Qiao Mengrong—Shuzhen claiming amnesia, Brother remembering nothing of his past—could it really be true? Was THAT why Brother had cast lots on the street and stopped hunting the writer Qianli Xun? Still, she refused to believe it. "If Brother's heart wandered, why invent some tall tale nobody could swallow?! Shuzhen is gorgeous and brilliant, turning every man's head—I just never thought even my own brother would fall for her spells! Shameless seductress..."

Fury triggered a fit of coughing that pulled sharp pain at her bandaged side—still nothing next to the agony tearing her heart.

Helpless, Shen Jianchen patted her shoulder. "Stop dwelling on it, Mengrong. You wrecked my last push—there is no road left between her and me..."

Hearing "no road left" calmed her ragged breathing. "Does Brother blame me?"

Sighing under life's crushing ironies, Shen Jianchen shook his head. "No—I blame myself, unable to bring joy to you or to myself."

Spent, Qiao Mengrong closed her eyes. "I want to sleep a bit, Brother. Stay beside me—do not leave, alright?"

Saying nothing aloud, he stayed put, dabbing the last tears from her lashes until, half an hour later, exhaustion pulled her into sleep. Setting the handkerchief by her pillow, he slipped quietly out and shut the door.

Gloom pressed heavy on his chest. Mengrong's desperate gamble had walled off every escape—yet his heart still tugged toward Qianli Xun. Glancing at the afternoon sun still high in the sky, he knew she must be bowing beside Chen Haoyu right now. Actually envying that worthless playboy Chen Haoyu! How unfair Heaven could be.

Feet carrying him on autopilot, Shen Jianchen wandered through the blooming woods out toward the Divine Willow. Reaching the pond's rim, he saw the willow draped in fresh green shoots glittering like jade in the sunlight. Lines from He Zhizhang's *The Willow*—memorized back in grade school—slipped off his tongue unbidden:

"The slender beauty's dressed in emerald all about; A thousand branches droop like fringes made of jade. But do you know by whom these slim leaves are cut out? The wind of early spring is sharp as scissor blade." [TN: Translated by Xu Yuanchong] Strange how such bright poetry surfaced while his chest felt hollowed out—maybe the Divine Willow was nudging brighter lines into his head to soften the blow. As red prayer ribbons fluttered on the breeze like scarlet bridal silk, Qianli Xun's dazzling leap from the wedding sedan flashed back before his eyes, fanning longing he dared not feed lest it cost Mengrong's life. "Tell me, Divine Willow," he whispered upward, "what am I supposed to do? Marry Zhao Pu's sister in his place, or use Prince Yi's power to pry her back from the Chens?"

Only wind answered, blowing one red ribbon loose onto a nearby briar bush where thorns snagged it fast. Too drained to care, Shen Jianchen stood still—until a weathered hand plucked the ribbon clear of the thorns and tied it neatly back onto a willow bough.

Turning, he recognized the kind-faced elderly nun with a cloth bundle slung over her shoulder—Master Jingguang!

"Amitabha," she greeted him.

"Peace to you, Master. What fate meeting you again."

"Hearing Lin'an's wealthiest Chen family held a wedding today, I came down the mountain for alms—and generous they were, gifting fine teas and herbs! Coming round the back hill, I bump into Patron too. Since I came down for alms, surely Patron owes me a donation?"

Having rushed out empty-handed, Shen Jianchen patted his sash, untied a fine jade pendant from his belt, and held it out. "This trinket is the only thing of value on me—if Master does not mind it, take it."

Accepting it, Master Jingguang smiled. "Not worried I am a swindler?"

"Wealth is external—you bring none at birth and carry none in death. On my waist it is just ornament; sold for coin to mend your nunnery or cover daily needs, it earns merit. Why fear being swindled?"

"Patron sees clean through worldly vanity," Master Jingguang praised. "In return for your jade pendant, I gift you seven words: *Wait through the clouds, and you will see the bright moon.*"

Puzzled, Shen Jianchen asked, "Could Master put it plainer?"

Smiling gently, she offered another phrase: "Follow fate; never force it."

"Please enlighten me further, Master."

"Following fate moves with wind and current, carrying life smooth; forcing fate rows upstream against headwinds, hitting snag after snag. Better to follow fate with the longing in your heart—only then will the clouds part to reveal the bright moon."

A quiet click settled in his mind. "Thank you for your guidance, Master."

"Amitabha." Joining her palms, Master Jingguang turned back up the mountain trail.

Watching her vanish among the trees, Shen Jianchen mulled it over: chasing Qianli Xun felt like clawing up a sheer cliff against every wind—was following fate telling him to accept his tie to Mengrong? Deep down, he still hated bowing to it.

"Your Highness! Imperial Attendant Liang arrived from Bianliang bearing an imperial decree!"

Spinning around at Bai Feng's voice, Shen Jianchen ignored the decree and snapped, "Was it you who tipped Her Highness off about the raid?"

Dropping to both knees, Bai Feng owned up straight. "It was me! For Your Highness's and Her Highness's own good—I could not watch you betray the princess! Besides, Youqi Jushi was marrying today—why keep clinging?"

Stepping past him, Shen Jianchen snapped, "Stay out of my private affairs! For meddling, kneel right here under the Divine Willow until dawn tomorrow!"

Indignant once his prince walked out of earshot, Bai Feng muttered, "HE messes up and punishes ME?! Totally unfair!" Kneeling five minutes without a soul watching felt idiotic—why not take a nap, hike to the nunnery, or stroll into town to see Kuqiao until morning? Choosing town, he hopped up, dusted off his knees, and headed around the foothills toward Lin'an.

Inside Youlin Retreat's reception hall, Shen Jianchen received Liang Shicheng, escorted by Zhu Xiyan now that the Chen banquet had wrapped up by mid-afternoon.

"When did Imperial Attendant Liang reach Lin'an?" Shen Jianchen asked.

"Just today, Your Highness! Shame I missed Prefect Zhu's daughter's wedding—came straight from the yamen without resting to deliver the decree."

"Much obliged, Imperial Attendant."

Unrolling the yellow silk scroll, Liang Shicheng announced, "Prince Yi, kneel to receive the imperial decree!"

Shen Jianchen and Zhu Xiyan knelt together.

Liang Shicheng read aloud in sonorous tones: "By the Mandate of Heaven, inheriting ancestral virtue, We ponder the realm's welfare and decree a joyous matter. Our son Zhao Pu, gifted in talent and upright in character, stands as a future pillar of Our Song and ought to wed early and establish his household. Though his wedding date was set, We yearn to witness the blissful union of fine son and fair bride sooner, and hereby command the date advanced: return to the capital within half a month of receiving this decree, complete the wedding, and thereafter resume your post in Lin'an. Respect this!"

Pinned between Mengrong's threat on her own life and His Majesty's decree, every exit slammed shut—he had no choice left but to follow fate and marry Zhao Pu's sister.

Forcing out the heaviest words on his tongue, he bowed. "Your son receives the decree."

Placing the scroll into his hands, Liang Shicheng bade both men rise. The silk weighed like iron in Shen Jianchen's palms. "Since Imperial Attendant Liang traveled all this way, stay a few days to enjoy Lin'an and travel back to Bianliang with me."

"Grateful as I am for Your Highness's hospitality, court business presses—especially ongoing talks with the Jurchens over returning Yanjing. I must set out on the return road today; please excuse me."

Words criticizing the court's appeasement rose to Shen Jianchen's lips, only to die unspoken—history would not bend, and neither emperor nor sycophant wanted hard truth. "In that case, safe journey, Imperial Attendant."

Escorting the emperor's favorite eunuch out to his carriage and watching it roll away, Zhu Xiyan did not leave; lifting the hem of his official robe, he dropped straight to his knees outside Youlin Retreat's front gate.
