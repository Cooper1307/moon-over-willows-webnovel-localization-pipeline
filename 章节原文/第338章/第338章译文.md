## Chapter 338: The Under-Manor Hidden Beneath the Prince's Residence

Hearing Wanyan Chi had taken Qianli Xun, Cai Jing dropped a vital clue: that Liao-built manor hid a secret subterranean palace—the **Under-Manor**—which even Tong Guan had failed to locate, and invited Prince Yi to lie low at his house while he probed Wanyan Chi.

Next morning, submitting his Yanjing handover ledger to Wanyan Chi, Cai Jing floated bait about the Under-Manor—instantly getting an arrow pointed straight at his nose as sharp Wanyan Chi exposed him for sheltering Prince Yi!

Falling on his knees, Cai Jing cleverly tugged Wanyan Chi's hidden heartstring—describing how motherless, neglected Prince Yi only had one woman who ever cared for him! Touched despite himself yet unwilling to yield, Wanyan Chi laughed and threw down an impossible gauntlet: *"Bring me **ten million taels of silver** within two months, and she's his!"*

Stomping a secret rhythm inside his bedroom to lift the daybed trapdoor, Wanyan Chi descended into the lamp-lit Under-Manor, traded playful blows with fiery Qianli Xun (taking a solid black eye when he stole a kiss!), bragged of his ten-million-tael test of Prince Yi's heart, and sealed the exit wall behind him with a bamboo flute tune before Qianli Xun could sprint through.

Back at Cai Jing's residence, ignoring the prefect's plea that no woman on earth was worth ten million taels, Shen Jianchen didn't blink: *"Tell Wanyan Chi I'll have ten million taels here in two months."* Turning to stunned Bai Feng outside, he set his jaw: *"Back to Bianliang—we sell Prince Yi's Manor and every acre and shop under my name!"*
