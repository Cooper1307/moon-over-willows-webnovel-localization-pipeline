## Chapter 149: Heading to Youlin Retreat for the Poetry Salon

Receiving the invitation card Xilan delivered left Qianli Xun equal parts pleased and puzzled.

Pleased to get an excuse to step out of Wangyue Pavilion, see friendly faces, and breathe fresh air.

Puzzled why on earth Prince Yi was throwing a poetry salon when he showed so little interest in verse!

Having Kuqiao usher Xilan inside, Qianli Xun asked once the maid stood still, "Who else did Prince Yi invite?"

Xilan listed them: "Young Master Qiao, Lady Miao, Young Master Dongfang, Young Master Cao, Young Master An, and Young Master Chen."

"Why invite Chen Haoyu?! Guy doesn't know one poem from a radish!" She could not fathom Prince Yi's angle.

Xilan replied, "Servants don't dare guess His Highness's mind—we just carry out orders."

Hesitation crept into Qianli Xun: Wenyuan and Miao Zhiyu would both be there, and she was not sure her heart was armored enough to face them yet.

Still, turning down an invitation from royalty was rude—and she truly missed her close friend Princess Jiahe.

"Thank Princess for me—tell her I'll be there on time."

Xilan bowed out.

Reading her mistress's face, Kuqiao murmured gentle: "If Miss doesn't feel up to going, just decline—Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe are reasonable and won't hold it against you."

"Sooner or later I have to face the people I dread seeing. Since Prince Yi set the stage, might as well go."

Dropping the subject, Kuqiao went back to grinding ink: "Is Miss's story hitting its finale today?"

Dipping her brush, Qianli Xun smiled: "Yep—wrapping it up today."

"The storyteller asked me to beg for a new story—crowds are hooked and waiting!"

"When you drop off this script, tell him your mistress has plenty more stories up her sleeve—once this wraps, the next one's right behind it."

"Your Young Master," Kuqiao corrected with a grin.

"Right, right—Young Master! Look at my slip-up—gotta stay careful not to blow cover!"

Just then Xiaguo stepped in: "Miss, Huayu's outside passing word that Young Master Dongfang is going to Youlin Retreat's poetry salon tomorrow and asks if you're going."

Just asked him to include her in salons and he checked in right away—true friend!

Pausing her brush, Qianli Xun said, "Tell Huayu to let Young Master Dongfang know Prince Yi and Princess invited me too, so I'll be there for sure."

Xiaguo answered "Yes," yet stayed planted by the door.

Kuqiao frowned: "Why aren't you going out to answer him?"

Hesitating a beat, Xiaguo worked up her nerve: "Miss... is Young Master Qiao going?"

Kuqiao snapped, "Xiaguo! How dare you ask Miss that?!"

Pausing her brush without looking up, Qianli Xun answered cool: "Wenyuan was invited too. Tag along tomorrow if you want."

"Thank you, Miss!" Beaming, Xiaguo hurried out to answer Huayu.

Qianli Xun resumed writing as if nothing had ruffled her.

Kuqiao muttered in irritation: "Why let Xiaguo come along, Miss?! She'll just drop to her knees begging Young Master Qiao to make her a concubine again and embarrass you!"

"Everyone has the right to chase happiness. If it makes Xiaguo happy, why fuss over it?"

"Miss is way too good-hearted—just hope Xiaguo snaps out of her delusions."

Finished grinding ink and seeing her mistress deep in focus, Kuqiao moved to the tea table in silence to whisk tea.

Next morning dawned bright and clear—a rare crisp blue sky in dead winter.

Weak though winter sunlight felt, just seeing golden rays spill across the earth warmed every soul who stepped outside.

Time had been stitching her heart back together bit by bit, and today's bright sun poured in fresh courage—Qianli Xun felt ready to face Wenyuan and Miao Zhiyu head-on.

Rolling out toward Youlin Retreat, she lifted the carriage curtain to take in the bustling avenue after weeks indoors.

New Year spirit already spiced the air: red spring couplets adorned shops and gates, while bursts of children's firecrackers popped down side lanes.

Another New Year coming—zero excitement for Qianli Xun. Turning close to twenty-one made her a certified old maid in the Song dynasty!

Funny—no matter which century she lived in, she could not shake the "old maid" tag! A wry spark of cheer hit her: maybe from day one of crossing over, she had been walking Qianli Xun's own destiny without realizing it.

Or maybe meeting Wenyuan was the real turning point—why else had a career woman sworn off romance fallen head over heels, only to rebound right back to her old independent self once love crashed?

Convinced that made total sense, she promised inside: "Fine—then I'll live Qianli Xun's best life right here in the Song!"

Then the Chen betrothal poked back into mind: "Wait—is that part of my fate too?" With zero clue how to untangle it, she would take it one step at a time.

And Prince Yi—throwing a random poetry salon stuffing every awkward romantic rival under one roof! What game was that slick, scheming heartbreaker playing? Locking her shields tight, she swore not to fall into whatever trap he had dug.

Arriving at Youlin Retreat, Qianli Xun discovered she was dead last—thinking she had left plenty early, yet everyone else had beaten her!

The salon was set in Qiao Wenxian's old courtyard, complete with its outdoor wooden viewing platform for gazing over the winter woods between verses.

Up at dawn, Shen Jianchen had ordered servants to scrub the courtyard and hall spotless.

Tables and chairs stood arranged neat around three roaring braziers pre-heating the room so no guest felt a chill.

For his very first salon at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong pitched in full tilt—writing out the kitchen menu for Xilan to deliver, and having maids lay fruit, candied preserves, osmanthus wine, and prime tea at every place setting.

Qiao Wenxian had arrived first of all—driven by one sole wish, to lay eyes on Shuzhen a minute sooner. He had put a bit of flesh back on and looked steadier, yet the wound still bled inside.

That hasty breakup in the snow beneath the willow left too much unsaid; he needed one more word with Shuzhen before his heart could truly die.

Hearing she was coming today, he had woken at dawn and tried on half his wardrobe before choosing the exact robe from their very first West Lake date—hoping a shred of that old magic lingered in its silk.

Because it was a thin summer gown, he had layered heavy winter clothes underneath and worn the summer robe right on top.

Shen Jianchen sat with Wenyuan first, reading in his restless fidgeting that he still had not let Youqi Jushi go.

Next came Miao Zhiyu—her first time seeing Young Master Qiao since their betrothal, hoping he might treat her warmer now.

As Miao Zhiyu walked in with her maid to bow to Prince Yi, awkwardness froze Qiao Wenxian; only when she greeted him with polite grace—"Young Master Qiao"—did he offer a silent smile without speaking.

Miao Zhiyu chose the seat right beside him on purpose.

Shen Jianchen hid a smirk—today's salon was going to be pure fireworks!

Not that he was here for cheap drama: he just wanted another hour near Qianli Xun, and a chance to size up Chen Haoyu's and Wenyuan's real feelings for her.

More important still, he meant to catch Qianli Xun alone and reveal he was not Prince Yi Zhao Pu—he was Shen Jianchen!

Once she knew he was Shen Jianchen, maybe their shared journey across time would move her heart toward him.

Third through the door came Chen Haoyu—baffled why Prince Yi invited a poetry illiterate! Since Bai Feng—the exact guy who pounded him flat—delivered the card, he dared not ask and said yes fast.

Terrified of crossing Prince Yi again, he showed up super early with Qinggua, bowed low to Prince Yi, and tucked himself into the lowest seat right across from Qiao Wenxian.

Knowing his own limits, keeping low beat making a fool of himself over poetry. Spotting Qiao Wenxian and Miao Zhiyu opposite him, he tossed out a cheeky greeting: "Send me an invite when you two tie the knot—I'll bring a massive gift!"

Miao Zhiyu flushed crimson, too shy to reply.

Mind stuck on Shuzhen, Qiao Wenxian ignored him cold.

Holding back a laugh, Shen Jianchen decided to deal Wenyuan a reality check right now: "Quit teasing Wenyuan, Young Master Chen—heard you're getting married soon too! When's the big day?"

Unable to dodge Prince Yi's question, Chen Haoyu answered honest: "Hate this match—whether it ever happens is anyone's guess."

Pleased—the last thing Shen Jianchen wanted was another man pining over Qianli Xun—he remarked, "Sounds like Young Master Chen really dislikes this betrothal."

"Sigh—Qiao Wenxian and I are two poor saps! The one he wants, I wouldn't touch with a pole—tell me, Prince Yi, why does neither of us get what we want?"

Before Prince Yi could reply, alarm snapped Qiao Wenxian upright: "What did you just say, Chen Haoyu?! What 'one I want' won't you touch?!"

"Whoa, didn't you hear? Let me fill you in: the Zhus and Chens sealed a betrothal—my bride-to-be is your precious Zhu Shuzhen! And I want zero part of marrying her! Honestly, how spineless are you—if you'd married her, I wouldn't be stuck with this betrothal!"

Fury surged in Qiao Wenxian to jump across the table and smash Chen Haoyu's face—why on earth was his beloved Shuzhen promised to this clown?! Then helplessness dragged him back down; blame cruel fate, not him. Clamping his jaw, he sank into dead silence.

Quiet joy warmed Miao Zhiyu—now that Youqi Jushi had a new match, marrying Young Master Qiao carried less guilt! Speaking up smooth: "Linking the Chen and Zhu houses doubles a fine bond—marrying Youqi Jushi is a rare blessing for Young Master Chen."

Chen Haoyu snorted: "Easy for Miss Miao to say, marrying your dream man! How could you feel my pain? Not only am I Lin Jue's fallback guy, I'm signing up for daily wife-beatings from Zhu Shuzhen—isn't my life tragic?!"

With nothing polite left to say, Miao Zhiyu fell quiet, casting sidelong glances at silent Qiao Wenxian, her heart fluttering toward their spring wedding.

Irritated at Chen Haoyu's disrespect toward Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen dropped a pointed barb: "Blame those rumors breaking out right on cue to ruin everyone's happiness—don't you agree, Young Master Chen?"

Feeling the sting of Prince Yi's warning, Chen Haoyu froze with a sheepish grin, tasting sweet karma straight in his teeth.

Saved by the bell—the three young masters walked in right on time, pulling eyes off his red face!

Bumping into each other outside town, all three had ridden in together—arriving extra early to show utmost respect for Prince Yi's invitation.

Seeing three guests already seated alongside Prince Yi in the host's chair, they hurried forward bowing: "Greetings, Prince Yi!"

Shen Jianchen nodded warm: "Sit wherever you like—no strict seating rank today."

Dongfang Chenze slid into the seat beside Chen Haoyu, while Cao Ban and An Mingxuan took the two seats next to Miao Zhiyu.

Standing behind his master, Huayu winked at Nan Shuang behind Young Master Qiao, who gave a subtle wave back.

Noticing Chen Haoyu, Huayu puzzled why a rake was invited to a poetry salon—then sized up Qinggua behind him, spotting zero scholarly polish and pegging him a total blockhead with quiet disdain.

Once seated, Dongfang Chenze spoke for the trio: "We tried arriving early, Prince Yi—didn't expect half the room to beat us! Forgive us if we looked slack."

"Nonsense, Young Master Dongfang—right on time, neither early nor late! Others haven't even arrived yet, so let's wait in comfort."

Meaning Princess Jiahe and Youqi Jushi—Princess lived right here and could pop over in a breath, so everyone hoped Youqi Jushi would not show up too late.

After trading nods around the hall—all three privately baffled at Chen Haoyu's presence yet mute before royalty—everyone sat waiting in hushed anticipation.
