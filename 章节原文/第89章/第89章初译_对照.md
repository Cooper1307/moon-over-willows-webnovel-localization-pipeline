# 第89章 丫鬟苦荞被表白

---

苦荞拿着新手稿，来到西三胡同。她没来过此处，幸好苦蕨早已将地址说明。她很容易找到说话艺人的家，一只手提着个布袋，另一只手敲了敲门。

Kuqiao, holding the new manuscript, arrived at West Three Alley. She had never been here before, but fortunately Kujue had already explained the address. She easily found the storyteller's house. Carrying a cloth bag in one hand, she knocked on the door with the other.

---

不一会儿，门从里打开，说话艺人探出脑袋："姑娘找谁？"

After a moment, the door opened from inside. The storyteller poked his head out: "Who are you looking for, miss?"

---

"我来送千里寻公子的书稿。"

"I'm here to deliver Young Master Qianlixun's manuscript."

---

说话艺人放心将门开大些："我正想着新书稿怎么还不送来，您就来了，真是及时，千里寻公子写的话本大家都喜欢听，您回去告诉他没事多写点儿。"

The storyteller felt relieved and opened the door wider: "I was just wondering why the new manuscript hadn't arrived yet, and here you are. Perfect timing. Everyone loves the stories Young Master Qianlixun writes. Tell him to write more when he's free."

---

苦荞从袋子里取出一沓纸："先给你送这些，后续还有，我家小……我家公子很会编故事，放心，少不了。"

Kuqiao took out a stack of papers from the bag: "Here's the first batch. There's more to come. My young... my young master is great at crafting stories. Don't worry, there'll be plenty."

---

说话艺人接过手稿："你家公子真的不打算从我这里分走银子？"

The storyteller took the manuscript: "Your young master really doesn't want to take any silver from me?"

---

苦荞强调道："我家公子不缺你那几个钱，她写话本纯粹是个人喜好。"

Kuqiao emphasized: "My young master doesn't need your money. She writes stories purely for personal enjoyment."

---

说话艺人笑道："这等好事真希望多遇些。"

The storyteller laughed: "I wish I could meet more such good things."

---

"我家公子说了，若大家喜欢听，辛苦你多多推荐给其他说话艺人，让全临安城的人都听她写的话本，我家公子的名字才会被更多人知道。"

"My young master said, if everyone enjoys listening, please recommend it to other storytellers, so that everyone in Lin'an can hear the stories she writes. Only then will my young master's name be known by more people."

---

"好说好说，那是自然。"说话艺人无不同意，若将书稿转让出去，岂不是还可赚一笔？欢喜之余邀请道，"请姑娘进屋喝盏茶再走。"

"Of course, of course. Naturally." The storyteller agreed to everything. If he passed the manuscript to others, wouldn't he make even more money? In his joy, he invited: "Please come in for a cup of tea before you go, miss."

---

"不了，我还等着回去向公子交差。"

"No thank you. I still need to go back and report to my young master."

---

说话艺人恭敬地说："姑娘慢走。"

The storyteller said respectfully: "Take care on your way, miss."

---

苦荞往胡同外走去，马车正停在胡同口。她上了马车，准备吩咐车夫回朱府，白风"蹭"地钻了进来。

Kuqiao walked out of the alley. The carriage was parked at the alley entrance. She got on the carriage and was about to tell the coachman to return to the Zhu residence when Baifeng suddenly jumped in.

---

苦荞急了："你怎么跑进来？"

Kuqiao was anxious: "Why did you jump in?"

---

"咱两个也算认识，捎我一段路。"

"We know each other, right? Give me a ride."

---

"我跟你不顺路，我要回朱府，你是出城，赶紧下去！"

"We're not going the same way. I'm going back to the Zhu residence. You're leaving the city. Hurry up and get out!"

---

"我又不是坏人，你何必赶我？我会功夫，坏人来了，还能保护你。再说我是仪王的贴身侍卫，你个丫鬟岂敢得罪我？"

"I'm not a bad guy. Why chase me away? I know martial arts. If bad guys come, I can protect you. Besides, I'm the Prince of Yi's personal guard. How dare you, a maid, offend me?"

---

苦荞不敢再赶白风下车，态度也缓和许多："你怎么知道我在马车上？"

Kuqiao didn't dare chase Baifeng out anymore. Her attitude softened: "How did you know I was on the carriage?"

---

白风掏出泥人："你把泥人还给我，我不高兴，又不好意思到朱府找你，就在街上瞎逛，不由自主来到西三胡同，也可能是我以前老来这里，走习惯了，结果瞧见你，我便躲在一旁等你。"

Baifeng took out the clay figurine: "You returned the clay figurine to me. I was unhappy, but too embarrassed to look for you at the Zhu residence. So I wandered the streets and ended up at West Three Alley. Maybe I used to come here so often I walked here out of habit. Then I saw you, so I hid to the side and waited."

---

苦荞盯着泥人，想起南双的话，脸不由红起来："听说泥人是你娘给的，你一直很珍惜，为何要送给我？"

Kuqiao stared at the clay figurine. Remembering Nanshuang's words, her face blushed: "I heard the clay figurine was given by your mother, and you've always treasured it. Why give it to me?"

---

白风大胆表白："正因为珍惜，才想把它送给喜欢的人。"

Baifeng boldly confessed: "Precisely because I treasure it, I want to give it to the person I like."

---

苦荞更害羞，低下头，不敢看白风。第一次有人对她表白，她有些不知所措。

Kuqiao was even more embarrassed. She lowered her head, not daring to look at Baifeng. It was the first time someone had confessed to her. She didn't know what to do.

---

"今日，你不收下泥人，我就不走，赖在朱家马车里。"

"Today, if you don't take the clay figurine, I won't leave. I'll stay in the Zhu family's carriage."

---

"你怎么这么无赖！"

"How can you be so shameless!"

---

"必须得无赖，否则你怎么会答应我？"

"I have to be shameless. Otherwise, how would you agree to me?"

---

"你看着比我小，我喜欢比我大些的。"

"You look younger than me. I like someone older."

---

"我都不嫌你大，你却嫌我小？什么道理！再说我才比你小两岁，年龄不是问题，重要的是你喜不喜欢我。反正你必须答应我，我都想好了，你要是不答应，我就天天缠着你。"

"I don't mind you being older. Why do you mind me being younger? What kind of logic is that! Besides, I'm only two years younger than you. Age isn't the problem. What matters is whether you like me. Anyway, you have to agree to me. I've thought it through. If you don't agree, I'll pester you every day."

---

苦荞继续低着头："你是仪王的贴身侍卫，要伺候王爷，哪有时间天天缠我？"

Kuqiao kept her head down: "You're the Prince of Yi's personal guard. You have to serve the prince. When would you have time to pester me every day?"

---

"我家王爷人可好了，从来不限制我，我想去哪里他都允许。我要是告诉他我喜欢你，他肯定会找幽栖居士为我提亲。"

"My prince is very kind. He never restricts me. He allows me to go wherever I want. If I tell him I like you, he'll definitely ask Youqi Jushi to propose marriage for me."

---

苦荞一把抓过泥人："你真麻烦，我……我还是收下，你不准让别人知道我收了你的泥人。"

Kuqiao snatched the clay figurine: "You're so troublesome. I... I'll take it. But you can't let anyone know I took your clay figurine."

---

"为什么？"白风很是不解。

"Why?" Baifeng was very confused.

---

苦荞红着脸催道："没有为什么，你赶紧下去吧。"

Kuqiao blushed and urged: "No reason. Just hurry up and get out."

---

"我马上下车，反正你收下我的泥人，证明你也喜欢我，既然你害羞不想让别人知道，我先听你的，等我觉得差不多时，会亲自请王爷帮我提亲。"

"I'll get off the carriage now. Anyway, you took my clay figurine, which proves you like me too. Since you're shy and don't want others to know, I'll listen to you for now. When I think it's about right, I'll personally ask the prince to propose marriage for me."

---

"别胡说！仪王还未成亲，什么时候轮到你。"

"Don't talk nonsense! The Prince of Yi hasn't married yet. When is it your turn?"

---

"那就等我家王爷成亲后我再提，回见！对了，明日我们还能相见。"白风迅速跳下马车，又从马车的窗户向里提醒，"回去后，想我时，多看看泥人。"

"Then I'll ask after my prince gets married. See you later! Oh right, we can meet again tomorrow." Baifeng quickly jumped off the carriage and reminded her through the window: "After you go back, when you miss me, look at the clay figurine more."

---

苦荞更加害羞，对车夫喊道："还不快赶车！"

Kuqiao was even more embarrassed. She shouted to the coachman: "Hurry up and drive!"

---

马车迅速启动，很快把白风甩在后面。

The carriage started quickly, soon leaving Baifeng behind.

---

苦荞坐在车里自语道："白风怎么说话这么直白？都不担心我骂他。我……我刚才为何不骂他？竟然爱听他说那些话，难道我也喜欢他？"她看着手中的泥人，小心抚摸着。

Kuqiao sat in the carriage and muttered to herself: "Why is Baifeng so straightforward? He's not afraid I'll scold him. Why... why didn't I scold him just now? I actually liked hearing him say those things. Could it be that I like him too?" She looked at the clay figurine in her hands and carefully stroked it.

---

回到朱府小院，小姐正坐在院子的藤椅上发呆。苦荞怕小姐发现手中的泥人，赶紧塞进布袋里。

Returning to the Zhu residence's small courtyard, she found her young lady sitting in a rattan chair in the yard, lost in thought. Afraid her young lady would discover the clay figurine, Kuqiao quickly stuffed it into the cloth bag.

---

"小姐，书稿已送到。"

"Young lady, the manuscript has been delivered."

---

"哦，你回来了，送到便好。"千里寻头靠在椅背上，望着夕阳西下后的天空，并未发现苦荞的动作。

"Oh, you're back. As long as it's delivered, that's fine." Qianlixun leaned her head against the chair back, gazing at the sky after sunset. She didn't notice Kuqiao's actions.

---

"小姐要没事，我先去忙。"

"If there's nothing else, young lady, I'll go tend to my duties."

---

"你去忙吧。"

"Go ahead."

---

苦荞庆幸没被小姐发现，她回到丫鬟房中，找来一个木盒，把泥人从布袋里取出，小心放在木盒里，回想着白风在马车上说的话，不由露出笑容。

Kuqiao was relieved she hadn't been discovered. She returned to the maids' room, found a wooden box, took the clay figurine out of the cloth bag, carefully placed it in the wooden box, and recalled what Baifeng had said in the carriage. She couldn't help but smile.

---

"苦荞！你出来一下，与我聊会儿！"

"Kuqiao! Come out for a moment and chat with me!"

---

外面传来小姐的喊声，苦荞收好木盒，来到小姐面前："小姐想和我聊什么？"

Her young lady's voice came from outside. Kuqiao put away the wooden box and came to her young lady: "What does young lady want to chat about?"

---

"我今日去哥哥院中，你猜我碰到谁？"

"I went to my brother's courtyard today. Guess who I ran into?"

---

"谁？"

"Who?"

---

"阴魂不散的人——陈浩宇。"

"The person who won't leave me alone — Chen Haoyu."

---

"啊，又碰到陈公子？"

"Ah, you ran into Young Master Chen again?"

---

"你说说，他该不会是我命中克星？只要碰见他准没好事。"

"Tell me, could he be my nemesis? Whenever I see him, nothing good happens."

---

"我也不好说。"

"I can't say for sure."

---

"那你说说，以前的我跟陈浩宇是怎么相处的？"尽管哥嫂已说过一些，千里寻还想听苦荞如何说。

"Then tell me, how did I get along with Chen Haoyu before?" Even though her brother and sister-in-law had already said something, Qianlixun still wanted to hear what Kuqiao would say.

---

"嗯……，相处谈不上，小姐每次见到陈公子都是有外人在的时候，您从来没理过他，他也从来没招惹过您。"苦荞又想了想，"好像不对，你们说过一次话……"

"Well... I wouldn't say you got along. Every time you saw Young Master Chen, there were other people around. You never acknowledged him, and he never bothered you either." Kuqiao thought for a moment. "Wait, that's not right. You two did talk once..."

---

千里寻急切地问："我跟他说了什么？或者他跟我说了什么？"

Qianlixun asked eagerly: "What did I say to him? Or what did he say to me?"

---

苦荞回想了一会儿："时间太久，我记得不大清楚，只记得是大公子成亲时，他来参加喜宴，多喝点酒，在朱府瞎逛，来到小院，见门开着，借着酒劲闯进来。您当时刚从酒宴回到小院，因酒席间有人不小心把汤洒在您身上，我陪您回来换衣裳，正换着陈公子闯进来，那种情况下，您最多骂他流氓，应该不会说别的。"

Kuqiao recalled for a moment: "It was so long ago, I don't remember clearly. I only remember it was when the eldest young master got married. He came to the wedding banquet, drank a bit too much, wandered around the Zhu residence, and came to the small courtyard. Seeing the door open, he barged in, emboldened by alcohol. You had just returned from the banquet. Someone accidentally spilled soup on you, so I accompanied you back to change clothes. Just as you were changing, Young Master Chen barged in. In that situation, you probably just called him a hooligan. You wouldn't have said anything else."

---

"什么？我换衣裳时被他看见？"千里寻从藤椅上坐起来，很是担心。

"What? He saw me changing clothes?" Qianlixun sat up from the rattan chair, very worried.

---

"小姐不用担心，您只是换外衣。当时陈公子态度还可以，很快离开，看那样子他应是不知道小院儿是您住的地儿，好奇才闯进来。"

"Young lady, don't worry. You were only changing your outer clothes. Young Master Chen's attitude was okay at the time. He left quickly. It seemed he didn't know the small courtyard was where you lived. He probably barged in out of curiosity."

---

千里寻松口气，多亏没被陈浩宇看光，否则她跳湖的心都有。

Qianlixun breathed a sigh of relief. It was fortunate Chen Haoyu didn't see everything. Otherwise, she would have wanted to jump into a lake.

---

听苦荞这么说，朱淑真以前跟陈浩确实没什么往来，由此看来，陈浩宇只跟千里寻有关。想到这些，千里寻又不由感到高兴，说明她正在一步一步摆脱朱淑真的命运。可如何能让千里寻的命运摆脱陈浩宇这等人？她感到为难。

Hearing Kuqiao say this, it seemed Zhu Shuzhen really had nothing to do with Chen Haoyu before. It seemed Chen Haoyu was only connected to Qianlixun. Thinking of this, Qianlixun felt happy. It meant she was gradually escaping Zhu Shuzhen's fate. But how could she escape someone like Chen Haoyu in Qianlixun's fate? She felt troubled.

---

"苦荞，你会不会认为我在茶楼帮助女茶师是错误的？解救夏果也是错误的？"

"Kuqiao, do you think I was wrong to help the tea master at the tea house? Was it also wrong to rescue Xiaguo?"

---

"我一点儿都不这么认为，我如果像小姐一样有本事，也会这么做，坏人就该受到惩罚。"

"I don't think so at all. If I were as capable as you, young lady, I would do the same. Bad people deserve punishment."

---

"你也认为陈浩宇是坏人？"

"You also think Chen Haoyu is a bad person?"

---

"他做的那些事，只有坏人才做得出。"

"The things he does — only a bad person would do them."

---

"也只有你理解我，可我哥哥嫂嫂却只说他顽劣。"

"Only you understand me. But my brother and sister-in-law just say he's wild."

---

苦荞不好评判主人，只能说："小姐怎么想，我就怎么想。"

Kuqiao didn't feel comfortable judging her masters. She could only say: "Whatever you think, young lady, I think the same."

---

千里寻笑笑，继续靠在藤椅上。明日立秋，她感到一丝凉意袭来，过不了多久，栅栏里的菊花会竞相开放。

Qianlixun smiled and leaned back on the rattan chair. Tomorrow was the Beginning of Autumn. She felt a hint of coolness. Soon, the chrysanthemums in the fence would bloom in competition.

---

千里寻决定，以后少跟陈浩宇碰面，能躲他多远便躲多远。

Qianlixun decided to avoid Chen Haoyu as much as possible in the future, staying as far away from him as she could.

---

"苦荞，你给绿桃和其他丫鬟交代一下，以后多在朱府注意些，要是碰到陈浩宇来，给我报告一声，我好避开，省得与他再碰面。"

"Kuqiao, tell Lvtao and the other maids to pay more attention around the Zhu residence from now on. If Chen Haoyu comes, report to me so I can avoid him and not run into him again."

---

"我记下了，这就去给绿桃姐姐和其他丫鬟嘱咐一声。"

"I'll remember. I'll go tell Sister Lvtao and the other maids right now."

---

看着苦荞走出小院的背影，千里寻叹道："希望从今往后陈浩宇不要出现在我穿越而来的命运中。"

Watching Kuqiao's back as she walked out of the small courtyard, Qianlixun sighed: "I hope Chen Haoyu won't appear in the fate I've transmigrated into from now on."
