这一章的看点在于女主到处“摇人（找帮手）”却屡屡碰壁，最后引出一位能“血脉压制”反派亲爹的120岁隐世老祖宗。你的翻译在节奏感上把握得很好。

针对你的审校需求，以下是具体的优化建议，主要集中在**“古言辈分称呼”、“情绪表达的地道化”以及“特定修辞（死马当活马医）”**上：

### 1. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 15: Brother and Sister Visit the Clansmen*
*   **点评**：稍显平淡。在网文里，找家族长辈出面撑腰通常有一种“搬救兵”的感觉。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 15: Seeking the Clan's Support** 或 **Chapter 15: A Hundred-and-Twenty-Year-Old Savior**（直接把老祖宗这个悬念抛给读者）。

### 2. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P8 古代家法惩罚的翻译**：`women get their palms beaten`
    *   **优化**：打手心/打手板，英文中有个非常固定且有时代感的表达 **caned on the palms** 或者是 **having their palms slapped with a ruler**。
    *   **修改**：`"The men in the family receive twenty strokes of the cane, and the women are caned on the palms."`
*   **P13 现代词汇与古代语境的碰撞**：`You're also a famous female ci poet in Lin'an. I wonder if you've forgotten your talent for writing poems and ci?`
    *   **优化**：这句翻译有点像现代面试。哥哥的意思是“你的名声还在，千万别因为失忆把写诗的本领给忘了”。
    *   **修改**：`"You are a renowned poetess here in Lin'an. I just hope your memory loss hasn't stolen your talent for poetry."`
*   **P23 “缩头乌龟”的翻译**：`they all chickened out.`
    *   **优化**：`chickened out` 是很现代的美式俚语，哥哥作为古代文人，用词可以稍微典雅、生动一点。
    *   **修改**：`they all shrank their heads back into their shells like turtles.`（这既保留了中文原梗，西方读者也完全能看懂乌龟缩头的比喻）。
*   **P28 哭得梨花带雨**：`She was crying so prettily`
    *   **问题**：“哭得漂亮”听起来有些奇怪。
    *   **修改**：可以用 **crying so delicately** 或 **her tear-stained face looking so pitiful**。
*   **P43 终极中式英语警告（死马当活马医）**：`They could only try their luck with a dead horse as if it were alive.`
    *   **问题**：这绝对是外国人看不懂的 Chinglish（中式英语）！老外看到这里会一头雾水：“哪里来的死马？为什么要医治死马？”
    *   **修改**：英语中表达“死马当活马医/尽管希望渺茫也要试一试”，最地道的俗语是 **grasping at straws**（抓住救命稻草），或者直接翻译为 **a desperate Hail Mary** 或 **nothing left to lose, might as well try.**
    *   **修改版**：`With no better options available, she had nothing left to lose. It was a desperate shot in the dark, but she had to try.`

### 3. 句子是否过长？标点与节奏是否合乎网文习惯？
*   **P34 极其复杂的辈分套娃**：`She's the mother of our grandfather's grandfather. Grandmother and grandfather, great-grandmother and great-grandfather, and grandfather's grandmother and grandfather have all passed away.`
    *   **优化**：原文这段辈分像绕口令一样，你的翻译很准确。但在英文中，读起来稍微有点拖沓。可以用高阶称呼如 **great-great-grandfather**。
    *   **修改**：`"She is the mother of our great-great-grandfather. Our grandparents, our great-grandparents, and even our great-great-grandparents have all passed away. Yet this old ancestor is still alive and kicking."`

**Chapter 15: A Hundred-and-Twenty-Year-Old Savior**

When they arrived outside the side gate of the Zhu residence, Zhu Jingyuan was already waiting. The horse carriage was fully prepared and ready to go.
The moment Qianli Xun stepped outside, she threw her arms around Zhu Jingyuan in sheer excitement. "It's so great to have an older brother!"
Zhu Jingyuan still wasn't entirely used to this drastically different sister, but he genuinely adored her new personality. "I took a leave of absence from my official duties today just to accompany you to visit the clan elders. Come, we can talk in the carriage."
They boarded the carriage with Kuqiao sitting opposite them. Watching how affectionately the Miss clung to the eldest young master, Kuqiao couldn't help but marvel to herself. *The old Miss would never have treated the eldest young master like this. The new Miss truly shares a deep sibling bond with him.*
The carriage rolled through the quiet residential streets until it reached the bustling markets of Lin'an City. The cacophony of vendors and crowds immediately drifted through the windows.
Zhu Jingyuan sat comfortably, quite enjoying having his sister hold his arm. "Mother told me everything about last night first thing this morning. As long as Father refuses to agree, absolutely no one in the family will dare stick their neck out for you. I hope you don't blame Mother, or me and your sister-in-law."
"How could I blame you guys? If anyone is to blame, it's Father for being so cold-blooded." Qianli Xun rested her head on her brother's shoulder. "I heard that if anyone defies Father's wishes, he enforces the 'family law.' I'm curious—what exactly is this family law? Mind telling me, Brother?"
"The men in the family receive twenty strokes of the cane, and the women are caned on the palms," Zhu Jingyuan explained gravely. "Then, the offender must kneel in the ancestral hall for two full days with no food, only water. Finally, they are forbidden from stepping a single foot outside the Zhu residence for an entire year."
Qianli Xun let go of her brother, her eyes flashing with anger. "He's a complete fascist!"
"Fascist? What does that mean?" Zhu Jingyuan blinked in confusion.
"It means he's a cruel, heartless tyrant!" Qianli Xun explained.
Zhu Jingyuan recalled his mother mentioning how his sister had literally smashed a solid wooden dining table in half with her bare fist last night. The old Shuzhen could never have done such a thing, nor would she have possessed the physical strength. It was undeniable—the sister sitting beside him was truly no longer the old Shuzhen.
"Shuzhen, after your 'rebirth,' your physical strength has suddenly become terrifying. I know you are no longer the same person you were... but in my heart, you will always be my little sister."
Deeply moved, Qianli Xun leaned her head back against his shoulder. "And you will always be my older brother."
Zhu Jingyuan smiled. "With how fierce you've become, I won't ever have to worry about anyone bullying you again. But you might not know this—our current Emperor highly values scholars, which means the entire empire places literati on a massive pedestal. You are a renowned poetess here in Lin'an. I just hope your memory loss hasn't stolen your talent for poetry."
Qianli Xun didn't want her brother to worry. She had memorized all of Zhu Shuzhen's poems anyway; that was more than enough to fake her way through. Sitting up straight, she looked him in the eye and said seriously, "Don't worry, Brother. Your sister's poetic talent is fully intact. I haven't lost a single drop of it."
"Don't worry, Eldest Young Master, I can testify to that!" Kuqiao chimed in, recalling the scene in the garden. She still remembered vividly the day her miss had originally composed the *Chrysanthemum* poem. Her miss was a lightweight, but that day she had drunk a few extra cups of wine. Driven by a wave of tipsiness, she had penned those exact words. Since the current Miss could flawlessly recite it, it proved her literary talent was still there. It was truly a silver lining in this whole disaster.
"That's a relief," Zhu Jingyuan sighed. "As long as your talent remains, the illustrious title of the 'Poetess of Jiangnan' will not be lost."

The carriage eventually rolled to a stop outside the grand gates of one of their clan uncles. Zhu Jingyuan told his sister and Kuqiao to wait inside the carriage while he handled the negotiations.
Qianli Xun and Kuqiao waited anxiously. Soon enough, her brother returned. The moment he stepped back into the carriage, he shook his head. One look at his face told them he had failed.
They visited five more uncles in a row. The result was exactly the same every single time.
"Brother, why is everyone refusing to speak for me?" Qianli Xun asked in frustration.
Zhu Jingyuan sighed heavily. "It's all because our father is the Prefect of Lin'an. None of them can afford to offend him. The moment I walked in, their first question was, 'Does the Prefect agree?' As soon as I told them Father opposed the divorce, every single one of them shrank their heads back into their shells like frightened turtles."
"Brother, you are way too honest!" Qianli Xun complained. "Why didn't you just lie to them? Tell them Father agreed and that he specifically sent you to invite them to mediate!"
"I can't lie about something this serious. An amicable divorce is a massive life event. If I trick them into coming to the Lin residence, and they realize Father isn't there, they'll turn right around and leave. Not only will you lose an immense amount of face, but it will permanently ruin your reputation."
"You're right... you thought it through better than I did. There's one uncle left, right? I'll go in with you this time to show our absolute sincerity."
The final relative was a clan elder, significantly older than Zhu Xiyan. He received the siblings politely. However, as soon as he heard his niece wanted a divorce, he predictably asked for the Prefect's stance. Upon hearing the truth, he immediately refused. He then launched into a long lecture, urging his niece to just settle down and live a proper life. A woman's reputation was paramount, he argued. While a mutual divorce sounded slightly better than being cast out, it would still permanently taint her name, making it incredibly difficult to find another husband in the future.
Squeezing her eyes shut, Qianli Xun forced out some tears. "Uncle!" she cried, her tear-stained face looking incredibly pitiful. "If your own daughter married a man who beat her black and blue and brought brothel girls into your home, would you honestly tell her to stay in that living hell?! *Sob...*"
Seeing her cry so delicately and realizing her words made complete sense, the uncle softened. "Child, if you were my own daughter, I would march over there myself. But your father is the Prefect of Lin'an. Who in this clan dares to defy him? However... let me give you a piece of advice. In all of Lin'an City, there is exactly one person who can step forward for you. If she agrees, your father wouldn't dare utter a single word of protest."
Qianli Xun instantly perked up. Wiping her half-fake tears, she asked eagerly, "Who is this person?"
Before the uncle could answer, Zhu Jingyuan's eyes lit up with sudden realization. He stood up and cupped his hands respectfully. "Thank you for the profound guidance, Uncle! I will take my sister to seek out the Old Ancestor immediately."
The uncle chuckled softly. "Go on. If you and Shuzhen can move her heart, her word alone carries more weight than the entire Zhu clan combined."
Although Qianli Xun had no idea who this "Old Ancestor" was, she was thrilled to finally have a viable solution. Bidding the uncle farewell, she quickly followed her brother back to the carriage.
The second the carriage doors closed, she couldn't hold back anymore. "Brother, who is this Old Ancestor?! What kind of divine being is she that even the Prefect of Lin'an is terrified of her?"
Zhu Jingyuan ordered the coachman to head straight back to the Zhu residence before turning to his sister. "The Old Ancestor lives deep inside the Buddhist shrine of our estate. She is one hundred and twenty years old. She is the mother of our great-great-grandfather. Our grandparents, our great-grandparents, and even our great-great-grandparents have all passed away long ago. Yet this Old Ancestor is still alive and kicking. Isn't that miraculous? Given her staggering age and absolute seniority, she naturally doesn't give a damn about Father's authority."
The dizzying string of "great-greats" completely scrambled Qianli Xun's brain. She couldn't untangle the family tree to save her life. But it didn't matter. As long as this Old Ancestor could socially bulldoze Zhu Shuzhen's father, that was all she needed to know.
"Brother! If you and Mother had just remembered this Old Ancestor in the first place, we wouldn't have spent the entire day running around Lin'an City for nothing!"
"You can't blame us for forgetting about her," Zhu Jingyuan defended. "She stays secluded in the shrine year-round and never steps outside. She refuses to be disturbed by anyone—even Father rarely gets to see her. After so many years, the family simply fell into the habit of forgetting she was there."
"But if she's a hundred and twenty years old... can she even walk?"
"I honestly don't know. I've only heard legends about her since I was a boy. I've never actually laid eyes on her."
A new wave of anxiety washed over Qianli Xun. A hundred-and-twenty-year-old woman probably had trouble even breathing, let alone walking. If she hadn't stepped out of the shrine in decades, how on earth were they going to convince her to leave the Zhu residence and travel to the Lin Manor to mediate a divorce? 
But with no better options left, they were out of choices. It was a desperate shot in the dark, but they had to try.
