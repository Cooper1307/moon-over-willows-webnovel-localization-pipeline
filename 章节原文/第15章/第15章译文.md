# Chapter 15: Brother and Sister Visit the Clansmen

---

When they reached the side gate of the Zhu residence, Zhu Jingyuan was already waiting there. The carriage was also ready.

---

As soon as Qianli Xun stepped out, she excitedly hugged Zhu Jingyuan. "It's so great to have a brother!"

---

Zhu Jingyuan wasn't yet used to his different sister, but he genuinely liked her. "I took the day off work to accompany you to visit our elders. We can talk in the carriage."

---

They boarded the carriage. Kuqiao sat opposite, watching how close Miss was to the eldest young master. She thought to herself, *The old miss never treated the eldest young master like this. The new miss truly shares a deep sibling bond with her brother.*

---

The carriage passed through quiet streets and reached the busy market of Lin'an City. Outside, the noisy sounds of people kept coming in.

---

Zhu Jingyuan sat with his sister holding his arm, quite enjoying it. "Mother told me everything about last night early this morning. As long as father doesn't agree, no one in the family dares to speak for you. I hope you don't blame mother, or your brother and sister-in-law."

---

"How could I blame you? If anyone is to blame, it's father for being cold-blooded." Qianli Xun rested her head on her brother's shoulder. "I heard that if you go against father's wishes, he'll enforce the family law. I wonder what the family law is. Brother, why don't you tell me?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan said, "The men in the family receive twenty strokes of the cane, and the women get their palms beaten. Then they all have to kneel in the ancestral hall for two days without food, only water. They're also forbidden from leaving the Zhu residence for a year."

---

Qianli Xun let go of her brother and said angrily, "He's truly a fascist!"

---

"Fascist? What does that mean?" Zhu Jingyuan was confused.

---

Qianli Xun explained, "It means father is cruel and heartless."

---

Zhu Jingyuan remembered what mother said about his sister smashing the dining table with her fist last night. The old Shuzhen would never have done that, nor would she have had the strength. It seemed the sister beside him really wasn't the old Shuzhen anymore.

---

"Shuzhen, after you were reborn, your strength suddenly increased. I know you're no longer the old you, but in your brother's heart, you'll always be my sister."

---

Qianli Xun was very moved and leaned on her brother's shoulder again. "You'll always be my brother too."

---

Zhu Jingyuan continued, "You've become so powerful. Your brother doesn't have to worry about you being bullied in the future. You probably don't know this yet, but His Majesty values scholars these days, so the whole empire holds scholars in high regard. You're also a famous female ci poet in Lin'an. I wonder if you've forgotten your talent for writing poems and ci?"

---

Qianli Xun didn't want to sadden her brother. She had memorized all of Zhu Shuzhen's poems and ci anyway, which was enough to handle it. She sat up straight, faced Zhu Jingyuan, and said seriously, "Don't worry, brother. Your sister's talent for writing poems and ci is still here. Not a bit of it is lost."

---

Kuqiao said, "Don't worry, eldest young master. I can testify." Kuqiao remembered the scene of Miss reciting the "Chrysanthemum" poem. She still had a deep impression of Miss writing that poem. Usually, Miss wasn't good at drinking, but that day she had a few more cups. When the drunkenness hit her, she wrote that poem. Since the current Miss could recite it, her talent for writing poems and ci must still be there. It was truly a blessing in disguise.

---

Zhu Jingyuan said, "That's good then. As long as your talent is still there, the good name of the Poetess of Jiangnan won't be lost."

---

The carriage arrived at the gate of a clan uncle's house. Zhu Jingyuan asked his sister and Kuqiao to stay in the carriage. He would handle it himself.

---

Qianli Xun and Kuqiao waited in the carriage for a while. Her brother soon came back. As soon as he entered the carriage, he shook his head. Looking at his expression, they knew he hadn't succeeded.

---

They visited five in a row, and it was the same for all of them.

---

Qianli Xun asked, "Brother, why are the uncles unwilling to speak for me?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan sighed. "It's all because our father is the prefect of Lin'an. They can't afford to offend him. Everyone asked as soon as they met us, 'Does the prefect agree?' As soon as they heard father didn't agree, they all chickened out."

---

Qianli Xun complained, "Brother, you're too honest. Why didn't you lie to them? Just say father agreed and you were specially sent to invite them to speak for you together."

---

"I can't lie about this. An amicable divorce is a major life event for you. If I trick them into coming and they find out father isn't there when they arrive, they'll leave. You'll lose face, and it will affect your reputation."

---

"Brother really thought it through. There's one more left. I'll go with you to invite him, to show our sincerity."

---

The last one was a clan uncle, older than Zhu Xiyan. He received the brother and sister. As soon as he heard his niece wanted an amicable divorce, he also asked about the prefect's opinion first, then refused. He kept advising his niece to live her life properly. A woman's reputation was most important. Although an amicable divorce was better for reputation than being cast out by her husband's family, it would still damage a woman's reputation. It wouldn't be easy to find another husband's family in the future.

---

Qianli Xun tried hard to squeeze out tears and cried, "Uncle, if your daughter married a man who beat her and brought merchant courtesans home, would you still let your daughter stay in a living hell? Boo hoo..."

---

The uncle saw that his niece's words made sense. She was crying so prettily, looking quite pitiful. He comforted her, "If you were my own daughter, I would definitely step forward. But your father is the prefect of Lin'an. Who dares to offend him? Let me give you a suggestion. In Lin'an City, there's only one person who can step forward and your father wouldn't dare say no."

---

Qianli Xun immediately perked up. With half-real, half-fake tears, she asked, "Who is this person?"

---

Before the uncle could answer, Zhu Jingyuan immediately understood. He stood up and cupped his hands. "Thank you for the guidance, uncle. I'll take my sister to find the old ancestor right away."

---

The uncle smiled and said, "Go ahead. As long as you and Shuzhen can persuade her, she alone can replace all the Zhu family clansmen."

---

Although Qianli Xun didn't quite understand who this old ancestor was, since there was another solution, she was naturally happy. She bowed and bid farewell to the uncle, then followed her brother onto the carriage.

---

Once in the carriage, she couldn't hold back anymore and asked eagerly, "Brother, who is this old ancestor? She's not even afraid of the prefect of Lin'an?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan instructed the coachman to "head back to the residence," then said to his sister, "The old ancestor lives in the Buddhist shrine of the Zhu residence. She's already one hundred and twenty years old. She's the mother of our grandfather's grandfather. Grandmother and grandfather, great-grandmother and great-grandfather, and grandfather's grandmother and grandfather have all passed away. But this old ancestor is still alive. Isn't that amazing? Given her age and seniority, she naturally wouldn't put father in her eyes."

---

The long string of tongue-twister-like titles completely confused Qianli Xun. She couldn't figure out the relationships at all. As long as she knew this old ancestor could override even Zhu Shuzhen's father, that was enough.

---

"Brother, if you and mother had thought of the old ancestor earlier, we wouldn't have run around Lin'an City for nothing."

---

"You can't blame mother and me for not remembering her. The main reason is that she stays in the Buddhist shrine all year round and never comes out. She doesn't allow anyone to disturb her either. Even father rarely sees her. Gradually, everyone got into the habit of forgetting about her."

---

"The old ancestor is so old. Can she even walk?"

---

"I'm not clear either. I've only heard about her since I was young. I've never seen her."

---

Qianli Xun started worrying again. A one hundred and twenty-year-old person probably had trouble moving around. Moreover, she had never left the Buddhist shrine of the Zhu residence. How would she be willing to leave the Zhu residence for her? There was no other better option now. They could only try their luck with a dead horse as if it were alive.
