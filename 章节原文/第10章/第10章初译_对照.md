# 第10章 回到朱府见生母 / Chapter 10: Returning to the Zhu Residence to Meet Her Birth Mother

---

## 段落 1

**原文**：苦荞上前敲了一会儿门。没过多久，当值的下人打开门，瞧见是苦荞，知道小姐回府，立即将门开大些："小姐可回来了，夫人一直念叨您。"

**译文**：Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked. Before long, a servant on duty opened the door. Seeing Kuqiao, he knew the young mistress had returned and immediately pulled the door wider. "The young mistress is back! The madam has been talking about you nonstop."

---

## 段落 2

**原文**：苦荞问："老爷和夫人在哪里？小姐要去拜见。"

**译文**：Kuqiao asked, "Where are the master and madam? The young mistress wishes to pay her respects."

---

## 段落 3

**原文**："老爷去了府衙，还未归来，夫人在园子里喂鱼。"下人说完，闪到一边，让出道来。

**译文**："The master went to the Prefectural Office and hasn't returned yet. The madam is feeding fish in the garden." After speaking, the servant stepped aside to let them through.

---

## 段落 4

**原文**：千里寻跨过门槛，苦荞紧随其后。

**译文**：Qianli Xun stepped over the threshold, with Kuqiao close behind.

---

## 段落 5

**原文**：待她们走进后，下人说道："小姐进屋等着，我去禀报夫人。"说完一路小跑朝园子方向而去。

**译文**：Once they were inside, the servant said, "Please wait in the room, young mistress. I'll go inform the madam." With that, he jogged off toward the garden.

---

## 段落 6

**原文**：千里寻问："现在我们去哪里？"

**译文**：Qianli Xun asked, "Where are we going now?"

---

## 段落 7

**原文**："去夫人房中，小姐只管跟着我。"

**译文**："To the madam's quarters. Just follow me, young mistress."

---

## 段落 8

**原文**：苦荞在前面带路，千里寻乖乖跟在后面。朱淑真从小生活过的地方对她来讲像进入迷宫，不过"迷宫"的景致实在不错，令人赏心悦目。

**译文**：Kuqiao led the way, and Qianli Xun followed obediently. The place where Zhu Shuzhen grew up felt like a maze to her—but the "maze" was quite scenic, pleasing to look at.

---

## 段落 9

**原文**：千里寻向来方向感极差，这么个拐法，如果没有人指引，她绝对会迷路。

**译文**：Qianli Xun had always been terrible with directions. With all these twists and turns, without a guide, she'd be lost for sure.

---

## 段落 10

**原文**："苦荞，在朱府你必须时刻待在我身边，府邸太大，七拐八拐的，我怕自己找不着方向。"

**译文**："Kuqiao, you must stay by my side at all times in the Zhu residence. This place is huge, with turns everywhere. I'm afraid I'll lose my way."

---

## 段落 11

**原文**："小姐放心，我一刻也不离开您。"

**译文**："Don't worry, young mistress. I won't leave your side for a moment."

---

## 段落 12

**原文**：也不知道拐了多少个弯，终于看到一个院落。

**译文**：After who knows how many turns, they finally reached a courtyard.

---

## 段落 13

**原文**："小姐，夫人就住这里，我带您进去。"

**译文**："Young mistress, the madam lives here. Let me take you in."

---

## 段落 14

**原文**：她们走进院子，迎面过来一名丫鬟。

**译文**：They entered the courtyard. A maid came toward them.

---

## 段落 15

**原文**："小姐可回来了，快！里面坐，我这就差人去叫夫人。"

**译文**："The young mistress is back! Quick, come in and sit down. I'll send someone to fetch the madam right away."

---

## 段落 16

**原文**："姐姐不必差人，门口的下人已去叫夫人。"

**译文**："No need, sister. The servant at the gate has already gone to fetch the madam."

---

## 段落 17

**原文**：苦荞说完介绍道："这位是夫人房中的丫鬟银杏。"

**译文**：After speaking, Kuqiao introduced, "This is Yinxing, the madam's personal maid."

---

## 段落 18

**原文**：没等千里寻说话，银杏批评道："小姐哪里不认得我？要你介绍？"说着拉起小姐向屋里走。

**译文**：Before Qianli Xun could say a word, Yinxing chided, "The young mistress knows me perfectly well. What do you need to introduce me for?" With that, she took the young mistress's hand and led her inside.

---

## 段落 19

**原文**：边走边说："小姐自从嫁到林家，就没回来过，要不是老爷挡着，夫人绝对会跑到林家找小姐。"

**译文**：As they walked, she said, "Ever since you married into the Lin family, you haven't been back once. If the master hadn't stopped her, the madam would have run straight to the Lin residence to find you."

---

## 段落 20

**原文**：千里寻很少像此时一样沉默，她怕一开口，在丫鬟面前漏了馅儿。进入屋内，挑椅子坐下。银杏忙着吩咐别的丫鬟上茶。

**译文**：Qianli Xun was rarely this silent. She was afraid that if she opened her mouth, she'd give herself away in front of the maids. Entering the room, she picked a chair and sat down. Yinxing was busy ordering other maids to serve tea.

---

## 段落 21

**原文**：茶摆好后，银杏把苦荞拉到一边，悄悄问："小姐怎么看着怪怪的？总觉得表情跟以前不太一样？"

**译文**：After the tea was set out, Yinxing pulled Kuqiao aside and whispered, "Does the young mistress seem strange to you? Her expression feels different somehow."

---

## 段落 22

**原文**："姐姐别瞎问，这里有我伺候，你尽管去忙。"苦荞说完，回小姐身边站好。

**译文**："Don't ask silly questions, sister. I'm here to attend to her. Go about your business." After speaking, Kuqiao returned to stand by her mistress's side.

---

## 段落 23

**原文**：银杏说："小姐，您坐着，我到门口瞅瞅夫人有没有回来。"

**译文**：Yinxing said, "Young mistress, please sit. I'll go check at the gate to see if the madam is back."

---

## 段落 24

**原文**：千里寻点点头："你去忙吧，不必在跟前伺候。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun nodded. "Go about your business. No need to attend me here."

---

## 段落 25

**原文**：银杏出去后，千里寻轻松许多。马上要见到朱淑真的母亲，她认为应该流出些眼泪才显得情真意切。可转念一想，怎么装也装不出朱淑真本人的状态，不如该怎样就怎样，然后再见机行事。

**译文**：After Yinxing left, Qianli Xun relaxed considerably. She was about to meet Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She figured she should shed a few tears to seem more sincere. But on second thought, no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't fake being the real Zhu Shuzhen. Better to just be herself and play it by ear.

---

## 段落 26

**原文**：刚打算好，一位四十多岁的妇人双眼微红，被四五个丫鬟簇拥着出现在面前。

**译文**：No sooner had she made up her mind than a woman in her forties appeared before her, eyes slightly red, surrounded by four or five maids.

---

## 段落 27

**原文**：千里寻猜这位应该是朱淑真的母亲，她不由站起来。

**译文**：Qianli Xun guessed this must be Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She couldn't help but stand up.

---

## 段落 28

**原文**：朱淑真的母亲向夫人正在园中喂鱼，听到小厮来报女儿回来了，已顾不上其他，带上丫鬟往回赶。

**译文**：Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Madame Xiang, had been feeding fish in the garden. When a page boy reported that her daughter was back, she had no mind for anything else and hurried back with her maids.

---

## 段落 29

**原文**：一进门，看到半年多没见的女儿，向夫人鼻子一酸，眼睛也跟着变红，见女儿站起来，急忙上前抓住她的手："淑真，娘以为你以后再也不会回来看我……"

**译文**：Stepping inside, she saw the daughter she hadn't seen in over half a year. Madame Xiang's eyes burned, and her nose stung. Seeing her daughter standing, she rushed forward and grabbed her hands. "Shuzhen, your mother thought you'd never come back to see me again..."

---

## 段落 30

**原文**：千里寻说："您就是朱淑真的亲娘？我醒来后，苦荞告诉我，我是朱淑真，是朱府的千金，所以今日就让她带我回来认亲。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun said, "You must be Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother? After I woke up, Kuqiao told me I am Zhu Shuzhen, the young lady of the Zhu residence. So today I had her bring me back to meet my family."

---

## 段落 31

**原文**：千里寻一番话直接将向夫人与周围丫鬟给整蒙。

**译文**：Qianli Xun's words left Madame Xiang and the surrounding maids completely stunned.

---

## 段落 32

**原文**：向夫人用禽满泪水的双眼再将女儿瞧一遍："淑真，你说这话是什么意思？"

**译文**：Madame Xiang looked her daughter over again with tear-filled eyes. "Shuzhen, what do you mean by such words?"

---

## 段落 33

**原文**：只听"扑通"一声，苦荞跪在地上，还未说话，先哭起来。

**译文**：With a thud, Kuqiao dropped to her knees. Before she could speak, she burst into tears.

---

## 段落 34

**原文**：千里寻心中赞叹："苦荞这丫头真是配合！"

**译文**：*Kuqiao is really playing along!* Qianli Xun admired inwardly.

---

## 段落 35

**原文**：向夫人松开女儿的手，向后退一步，猜到有事情发生，她用丝帕沾了沾眼角的泪水，向其他丫鬟吩咐道："你们都出去，把门关好。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang released her daughter's hands and stepped back. Sensing something had happened, she dabbed the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief and ordered the other maids, "All of you, leave and close the door behind you."

---

## 段落 36

**原文**：除了苦荞，其他丫鬟都乖乖退出。向夫人见门已关好，在椅子上坐下："你们两个这是唱得哪一出？"

**译文**：All the maids obediently filed out, except Kuqiao. Seeing the door closed, Madame Xiang sat down in a chair. "What's going on between you two?"

---

## 段落 37

**原文**：苦荞会将一切告诉朱淑真的娘，千里寻不必操心，她继续坐好，仔细观察向夫人接下来会如何。

**译文**：Kuqiao would tell Zhu Shuzhen's mother everything—no need for Qianli Xun to worry. She sat back and watched carefully, curious to see what Madame Xiang would do next.

---

## 段落 38

**原文**：苦荞哭着说："夫人，小姐自从嫁给姑爷，没过一天好日子，姑爷动不动打小姐，还把商妓带到家里。就昨天，又把商妓带到家里，还大打出手，结果把小姐猛一推，人撞到柱子上晕死过去，醒来后什么都不记得，像换了个人，呜呜……"

**译文**：Kuqiao said through tears, "Madam, ever since the young mistress married the young master, she hasn't had a single good day. The young master beats her for no good reason and has even brought a merchant courtesan into the home. Just yesterday, he brought her home again and beat her severely. He pushed the young mistress hard, and she hit her head on a pillar and passed out. When she woke up, she didn't remember anything. She's like a completely different person. Wuuu..."

---

## 段落 39

**原文**：向夫人越听越气："竟有这等事？为何不早回来告诉我和老爷？"

**译文**：The more Madame Xiang heard, the angrier she grew. "Such a thing? Why didn't you come back and tell your father and me sooner?"

---

## 段落 40

**原文**：苦荞抹着眼泪："小姐不让，说告诉了也没用，不如让姑爷打死算了。呜呜……"

**译文**：Kuqiao wiped her tears. "The young mistress wouldn't let us. She said telling you would be useless—that she might as well let the young master beat her to death. Wuuu..."

---

## 段落 41

**原文**：向夫人看向女儿，她额头上的确有伤，怪自己见到女儿太过激动，竟没留意到她有伤。向夫人起身走到女儿跟前，用丝帕在伤口上轻轻挨了一下："疼吗？"

**译文**：Madame Xiang looked at her daughter. There was indeed a wound on her forehead. She'd been so overcome with emotion upon seeing her daughter. She hadn't noticed the injury. Madame Xiang rose, walked over, and gently touched the wound with her silk handkerchief. "Does it hurt?"

---

## 段落 42

**原文**：自从母亲车祸去世后，千里寻对母爱更加渴望，向夫人的举动让她心里感到很温暖："不疼，您真的是我娘？"

**译文**：Ever since her mother died in a car accident, Qianli Xun had yearned even more for maternal love. Madame Xiang's gesture warmed her deeply. "It doesn't hurt. Are you really my mother?"

---

## 段落 43

**原文**：向夫人点点头："你就是我女儿淑真。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang nodded. "You are my daughter Shuzhen."

---

## 段落 44

**原文**："娘，我醒来后什么都不记得，如果我与您以前的女儿不一样，您千万不要不认我。"

**译文**："Mother, I don't remember anything after waking up. If I'm different from your daughter before, please don't disown me."

---

## 段落 45

**原文**：向夫人又流下眼泪："早知道你到林家是去受罪，娘绝对不会同意把你嫁给林觉。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang shed tears again. "Had I known going to the Lin family meant suffering, your mother would never have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue."

---

## 段落 46

**原文**：千里寻认为这个开场很不错，一下子把朱淑真的母亲拉到统一战线上来。

**译文**：*This opening is going quite well,* Qianli Xun thought. She'd won Zhu Shuzhen's mother over to her side in one stroke.

---

## 段落 47

**原文**：向夫人又说："苦荞，起来回话。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang spoke again. "Kuqiao, get up and answer me."

---

## 段落 48

**原文**：苦荞不再哭泣，乖乖站起来。

**译文**：Kuqiao stopped crying and obediently stood up.

---

## 段落 49

**原文**：向夫人又坐回原位："苦荞，你倒是给我说说，淑真跟以前有何不同？"

**译文**：Madame Xiang sat back in her chair. "Kuqiao, tell me—how is Shuzhen different from before?"

---

## 段落 50

**原文**：苦荞用目光征求小姐的意见，千里寻向她点点头，苦荞才大胆说："除五官和身段是以前的小姐，其他都不一样，现在的小姐说话声音又大，话还多，连茶都不会做，关键是会功夫，姑爷都打不过她，还吵着让姑爷休了她。夫人，我觉得小姐一定是被姑爷打晕后重生了。"

**译文**：Kuqiao looked at her mistress for permission. Qianli Xun nodded, and only then did Kuqiao speak freely. "Everything is different except her face and figure. The young mistress now speaks loudly and talks too much. She can't even make tea properly. Most importantly, she knows martial arts—the young master can't beat her. She's been demanding the young master divorce her. Madam, I think the young mistress must have been beaten unconscious and reborn."

---

## 段落 51

**原文**："什么？重生？"向夫人惊得站起来，接着又坐下，拍着桌子大哭，"我那可怜的女儿啊！难道已被林觉打死了不成？呜呜……"

**译文**："What? Reborn?" Madame Xiang stood up in shock, then sat back down, pounding the table and crying. "My poor daughter! Could she have been beaten to death by Lin Jue? Wuuu..."

---

## 段落 52

**原文**：千里寻见向夫人哭得伤心，心有不忍："娘，我活着，没死，只是跟以前有些不同。"

**译文**：Seeing Madame Xiang cry so bitterly, Qianli Xun couldn't bear it. "Mother, I'm alive. I'm not dead. Just a little different from before."

---

## 段落 53

**原文**：是啊，这声音还是女儿的声音，样貌也是女儿的样貌，她还活着。向夫人停止大哭："对，我家淑真还活着，过来，让娘好好看看。"

**译文**：Yes, this was still her daughter's voice, her daughter's face. She was alive. Madame Xiang stopped crying. "Yes, my Shuzhen is still alive. Come here, let your mother get a good look at you."

---

## 段落 54

**原文**：千里寻起身走到向夫人面前，蹲下，头靠在母亲的膝头："娘！"

**译文**：Qianli Xun rose, walked over to Madame Xiang, squatted down, and rested her head on her mother's knee. "Mother!"

---

## 段落 55

**原文**：向夫人摸着女儿的头发："娘差点儿见不到你，不行，我绝对不能再让你回林家。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang stroked her daughter's hair. "Your mother nearly lost you forever. No, I absolutely cannot let you return to the Lin family."

---

## 段落 56

**原文**：千里寻抬起头："娘，我虽然什么都不记得，可我醒来后，林觉还打我，我亲眼看到商妓在家中，一气之下，让林觉休了我，后来才知女子被夫家所休，名声不好，就打算和离，林觉也同意，让我请朱家长辈出面，三日后商议和离之事。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun looked up. "Mother, though I don't remember anything, after I woke up, Lin Jue still beat me. I saw the merchant courtesan in our home with my own eyes. In anger, I demanded Lin Jue divorce me. Only later did I learn that being cast out by the husband's family brings a bad reputation. So we agreed to a mutual divorce. Lin Jue consented and asked me to have the Zhu family elders step forward. We'll discuss the divorce terms in three days."

---

## 段落 57

**原文**：向夫人扶起女儿："放心，娘绝不会让你继续待在火坑里，算林觉识相，愿意和离，主要是我闺女没有错处，他找不到任何休你的理由。"

**译文**：Madame Xiang helped her daughter up. "Don't worry. Your mother will never let you stay in that pit of fire. Lin Jue has the sense to agree to a mutual divorce. Mainly because my daughter has done nothing wrong—he couldn't find any reason to cast you out."

---

## 段落 58

**原文**："娘，我想在家住两日，不想回林府。"

**译文**："Mother, I want to stay home for a few days. I don't want to go back to the Lin residence."

---

## 段落 59

**原文**："傻女儿，这是你的家，想住多久都可以。"

**译文**："Silly girl, this is your home. You can stay as long as you like."

---

## 段落 60

**原文**：向夫人朝门外喊道："银杏！"

**译文**：Madame Xiang called toward the door, "Yinxing!"

---

## 段落 61

**原文**：银杏推门而入："夫人，有何吩咐？"

**译文**：Yinxing pushed open the door. "Madam, what are your orders?"

---

## 段落 62

**原文**："你亲自到林府一趟，告诉林家，小姐在朱府住下，至于什么时候回去，暂时还不知。"

**译文**："Go to the Lin residence in person. Tell the Lin family that the young mistress is staying at the Zhu residence. As for when she'll return, we don't know yet."

---

## 段落 63

**原文**："是。"银杏领命而去。

**译文**："Yes." Yinxing accepted the order and left.

---

## 段落 64

**原文**：千里寻此刻感到很幸福，有家庭做后盾，让她在陌生的时代一下子充满踏实感。

**译文**：Qianli Xun felt truly happy at this moment. Having a family as her backbone gave her a sense of security in this unfamiliar era.

---

## 段落 65

**原文**："等你爹回来，我会将此事与他说明，你再去拜见他，娘相信你爹也会同意和离之事。"

**译文**："When your father returns, I'll explain everything to him. Then you can go pay your respects. Your mother believes your father will also agree to the divorce."

---

## 段落 66

**原文**："我爹会不会很凶？"

**译文**："Will my father be very fierce?"

---

## 段落 67

**原文**：千里寻的问题令向夫人伤心，看来女儿真的什么都不记得："你爹不凶，但他很严肃，有时候也很严厉，你不必担忧。以前的院子还给你留着，让苦荞带你过去，既然什么都记不得，就让苦荞再带你各处走走，熟悉熟悉，也许慢慢会想起来。"

**译文**：Qianli Xun's question saddened Madame Xiang. It seemed her daughter truly remembered nothing. "Your father isn't fierce, but he's serious, sometimes strict. Don't worry. Your old courtyard has been kept for you. Let Kuqiao take you there. Since you can't remember anything, have Kuqiao show you around the estate. Maybe you'll gradually recall things."

---

## 段落 68

**原文**：千里寻拜别向夫人，在苦荞的带领下继续拐着弯儿，向朱府深处走去。很难想象朱府到底有多大，反正此刻她觉得怎么走都走不到尽头。

**译文**：Qianli Xun bid farewell to Madame Xiang. Led by Kuqiao, she continued winding deeper into the Zhu residence. It was hard to imagine just how large the estate was. At this moment, it felt like no matter how far she walked, there was no end in sight.

---

## 段落 69

**原文**：走着走着，听到唱曲儿的声音。千里寻停下脚步，向声音传来的方向望去，果然有个院落，院墙挡着，根本看不到里面的房屋。

**译文**：As they walked, the sound of someone singing a tune drifted over. Qianli Xun stopped and looked toward the source. Sure enough, there was a courtyard, its walls blocking any view of the buildings inside.

---

## 段落 70

**原文**："苦荞，那里住着什么人，怎么有人在唱曲儿？"

**译文**："Kuqiao, who lives there? Why is someone singing?"

---

## 段落 71

**原文**："那里住着老爷养的歌伎和舞伎。"

**译文**："That's where the master's songstresses and dancers live."

---

## 段落 72

**原文**："什么？歌伎和舞伎？我爹养她们做什么？"

**译文**："What? Songstresses and dancers? What does my father keep them for?"

---

## 段落 73

**原文**："达官贵人家里基本都会养歌伎和舞伎，主要用来招待有身份的客人。老爷每年会举办诗会，邀请全临安有头有脸的文人才子参加，诗会也需歌伎和舞伎烘场面。"

**译文**:"Most noble families keep songstresses and dancers, mainly to entertain distinguished guests. The master hosts a poetry salon every year, inviting all the renowned scholars and talented poets in Lin'an. The salon also needs songstresses and dancers to liven things up."

---

## 段落 74

**原文**："我可参加过诗会？"

**译文**："Have I ever attended the poetry salon?"

---

## 段落 75

**原文**："小姐从十岁开始年年参加，您的好多诗词正是在诗会上做出，被文人才子争相传诵，才有了江南女词人的美称。"

**译文**:"You've attended every year since you were ten. Many of your ci poems were composed at the salon and passed around by the scholars and poets. That's how you earned the title 'Poetess of Jiangnan.'"

---

## 段落 76

**原文**："江南女词人。"千里寻低首沉吟，小说她倒会写，诗词她可是门外汉，顶着朱淑真的身份，肯定要维护她在文坛的地位，千里寻倍感压力。想到已将朱淑真的诗词记在脑中，才松口气，"苦荞，朱府难道没有一条直路吗？曲里拐弯的？"

**译文**:"The Poetess of Jiangnan." Qianli Xun lowered her head and murmured. Novels she could write, but poetry? She knew nothing about it. Wearing Zhu Shuzhen's identity, she'd have to uphold that literary reputation. The pressure was real. Thinking that she'd already memorized Zhu Shuzhen's poems, she finally relaxed. "Kuqiao, is there no straight path in the Zhu residence? Everything winds and twists?"

---

## 段落 77

**原文**："还真没有，我听说老爷建这座宅子时，取得就是曲径通幽，是不是很有诗意？"

**译文**:"There really isn't. I heard when the master built this estate, he chose the style of 'winding paths to secluded spots.' Isn't that poetic?"

---

## 段落 78

**原文**："是有诗意，可把我搞得晕头转向。我住的地方到底在哪里？"

**译文**:"It's poetic alright, but it's left me dizzy. Where exactly do I live?"

---

## 段落 79

**原文**："小姐别急，再拐两个弯就到了。"

**译文**:"Don't worry, young mistress. Just two more turns."

---

## 段落 80

**原文**：千里寻跟着苦荞拐了两个弯，眼前出现一片湖水，并不大，荷叶将湖水占去一大半儿，零零散散竖着几个花骨朵。湖上横着一座木质小桥，穿过这座桥，才真正到达朱淑真住过的园子。

**译文**：Qianli Xun followed Kuqiao around two more turns. A small lake appeared before them. Lotus leaves covered most of the water, with a few scattered flower buds standing here and there. A wooden bridge spanned the lake. Only after crossing it did they truly reach the garden where Zhu Shuzhen had lived.
