# 第10章 回到朱府见生母 / Chapter 10: Returning to the Zhu Residence to Meet Her Birth Mother

---

## 段落 1

**原文**：苦荞上前敲了一会儿门。没过多久，当值的下人打开门，瞧见是苦荞，知道小姐回府，立即将门开大些：“小姐可回来了，夫人一直念叨您。”

**译文**：Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked. Before long, a servant on duty opened the door. Seeing Kuqiao, the servant knew the young miss had returned and immediately pushed the door wider. “The miss is back! The madam has been talking about you nonstop.”

---

## 段落 2

**原文**：苦荞问：“老爷和夫人在哪里？小姐要去拜见。”

**译文**：Kuqiao asked, “Where are the master and madam? The miss wants to pay her respects.”

---

## 段落 3

**原文**：“老爷去了府衙，还未归来，夫人在园子里喂鱼。”下人说完，闪到一边，让出道来。

**译文**：“The master went to the prefectural office and hasn't returned yet. The madam is feeding fish in the garden.” With that, the servant stepped aside to let them through.

---

## 段落 4

**原文**：千里寻跨过门槛，苦荞紧随其后。

**译文**：Qianli Xun stepped over the threshold, with Kuqiao close behind.

---

## 段落 5

**原文**：待她们走进后，下人说道：“小姐进屋等着，我去禀报夫人。”说完一路小跑朝园子方向而去。

**译文**：Once they entered, the servant said, “Please wait inside, Miss. I'll go inform the madam.” With that, she trotted off toward the garden.

---

## 段落 6

**原文**：千里寻问：“现在我们去哪里？”

**译文**：“Where do we go now？” Qianli Xun asked.

---

## 段落 7

**原文**：“去夫人房中，小姐只管跟着我。”

**译文**：“To the madam's quarters. Just follow me, Miss.”

---

## 段落 8

**原文**：苦荞在前面带路，千里寻乖乖跟在后面。朱淑真从小生活过的地方对她来讲像进入迷宫，不过“迷宫”的景致实在不错，令人赏心悦目。

**译文**：Kuqiao led the way, and Qianli Xun followed quietly. The sprawling estate where Zhu Shuzhen grew up felt like a giant maze to her, though a truly picturesque one.

---

## 段落 9

**原文**：千里寻向来方向感极差，这么个拐法，如果没有人指引，她绝对会迷路。

**译文**：Qianli Xun had always been terrible with directions. With all these winding paths, without a guide, she would definitely get lost.

---

## 段落 10

**原文**：“苦荞，在朱府你必须时刻待在我身边，府邸太大，七拐八拐的，我怕自己找不着方向。”

**译文**：“Kuqiao, you must stay by my side at all times in this estate. It's too big, with so many twists and turns. I'm afraid I'll lose my way.”

---

## 段落 11

**原文**：“小姐放心，我一刻也不离开您。”

**译文**：“Don't worry, Miss. I won't leave your side for a moment.”

---

## 段落 12

**原文**：也不知道拐了多少个弯，终于看到一个院落。

**译文**：After who knows how many turns, they finally reached a courtyard.

---

## 段落 13

**原文**：“小姐，夫人就住这里，我带您进去。”

**译文**：“Miss, the madam lives here. I'll take you in.”

---

## 段落 14

**原文**：她们走进院子，迎面过来一名丫鬟。

**译文**：As they entered the courtyard, a maid came to greet them.

---

## 段落 15

**原文**：“小姐可回来了，快！里面坐，我这就差人去叫夫人。”

**译文**：“The miss is back! Quick, come inside and sit. I'll send someone to fetch the madam right away.”

---

## 段落 16

**原文**：“姐姐不必差人，门口的下人已去叫夫人。”

**译文**：“No need, Sister. The servant at the gate has already gone to call the madam.”

---

## 段落 17

**原文**：苦荞说完介绍道：“这位是夫人房中的丫鬟银杏。”

**译文**：Kuqiao then introduced, “This is Yinxing, the madam's personal maid.”

---

## 段落 18

**原文**：没等千里寻说话，银杏批评道：“小姐哪里不认得我？要你介绍？”说着拉起小姐向屋里走。

**译文**：Before Qianli Xun could speak, Yinxing chided, “How could the miss not recognize me? Why do you need to introduce me？” She then pulled her toward the room.

---

## 段落 19

**原文**：边走边说：“小姐自从嫁到林家，就没回来过，要不是老爷挡着，夫人绝对会跑到林家找小姐。”

**译文**：As they walked, she said, “Ever since you married into the Lin family, you haven't been back once. If it weren't for the master holding her back, the madam would have definitely run to the Lin residence to find you.”

---

## 段落 20

**原文**：千里寻很少像此时一样沉默，她怕一开口，在丫鬟面前漏了馅儿。进入屋内，挑椅子坐下。银杏忙着吩咐别的丫鬟上茶。

**译文**：Qianli Xun was rarely as silent as she was now. She was afraid that if she opened her mouth, she'd give herself away in front of the maids. Entering the room, she picked a chair and sat down. Yinxing busied herself ordering other maids to serve tea.

---

## 段落 21

**原文**：茶摆好后，银杏把苦荞拉到一边，悄悄问：“小姐怎么看着怪怪的？总觉得表情跟以前不太一样？”

**译文**：After the tea was set, Yinxing pulled Kuqiao aside and whispered, “Why does the miss seem strange? Her expression feels different from before.”

---

## 段落 22

**原文**：“姐姐别瞎问，这里有我伺候，你尽管去忙。”苦荞说完，回小姐身边站好。

**译文**：“Don't ask so many questions, Sister. I'll attend to things here. You can go on with your work.” Kuqiao then returned to her miss's side.

---

## 段落 23

**原文**：银杏说：“小姐，您坐着，我到门口瞅瞅夫人有没有回来。”

**译文**：Yinxing said, “Miss, please sit. I'll go to the gate and check if the madam is back.”

---

## 段落 24

**原文**：千里寻点点头：“你去忙吧，不必在跟前伺候。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun nodded. “You may go. No need to wait on me.”

---

## 段落 25

**原文**：银杏出去后，千里寻轻松许多。马上要见到朱淑真的母亲，她认为应该流出些眼泪才显得情真意切。可转念一想，怎么装也装不出朱淑真本人的状态，不如该怎样就怎样，然后再见机行事。

**译文**：After Yinxing left, Qianli Xun relaxed considerably. She was about to meet Zhu Shuzhen's mother and thought she should shed some tears to show genuine emotion. But then she reconsidered—no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't fake being Zhu Shuzhen. Better to just be herself and play it by ear.

---

## 段落 26

**原文**：刚打算好，一位四十多岁的妇人双眼微红，被四五个丫鬟簇拥着出现在面前。

**译文**：No sooner had she made up her mind than a woman in her forties appeared, eyes slightly red, flanked by four or five maids.

---

## 段落 27

**原文**：千里寻猜这位应该是朱淑真的母亲，她不由站起来。

**译文**：Qianli Xun guessed this must be Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She couldn't help but stand up.

---

## 段落 28

**原文**：朱淑真的母亲向夫人正在园中喂鱼，听到小厮来报女儿回来了，已顾不上其他，带上丫鬟往回赶。

**译文**：Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Madam Xiang, had been feeding fish in the garden. When a young servant reported that her daughter had returned, she dropped everything and rushed back with her maids.

---

## 段落 29

**原文**：一进门，看到半年多没见的女儿，向夫人鼻子一酸，眼睛也跟着变红，见女儿站起来，急忙上前抓住她的手：“淑真，娘以为你以后再也不会回来看我……”

**译文**：As soon as she entered and saw her daughter, whom she hadn't seen in over half a year, Madam Xiang felt a lump rise in her throat as tears welled up. Seeing her daughter stand up, she hurried forward and took her hands. “Shuzhen... I thought you'd never come back to see me again...”

---

## 段落 30

**原文**：千里寻说：“您就是朱淑真的亲娘？我醒来后，苦荞告诉我，我是朱淑真，是朱府的千金，所以今日就让她带我回来认亲。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun said, “You're Zhu Shuzhen's real mother? After I woke up, Kuqiao told me I am Zhu Shuzhen, the young miss of the Zhu residence. So today I had her bring me back to meet my family.”

---

## 段落 31

**原文**：千里寻一番话直接将向夫人与周围丫鬟给整蒙。

**译文**：Qianli Xun's words completely stunned Madam Xiang and the surrounding maids.

---

## 段落 32

**原文**：向夫人用禽满泪水的双眼再将女儿瞧一遍：“淑真，你说这话是什么意思？”

**译文**：With tear-filled eyes, Madam Xiang looked at her daughter again. “Shuzhen, what do you mean by that？”

---

## 段落 33

**原文**：只听“扑通”一声，苦荞跪在地上，还未说话，先哭起来。

**译文**：With a thump, Kuqiao dropped to her knees. Before she could speak, she started crying.

---

## 段落 34

**原文**：千里寻心中赞叹：“苦荞这丫头真是配合！”

**译文**：Qianli Xun marveled inwardly, *Kuqiao is really playing along perfectly!*

---

## 段落 35

**原文**：向夫人松开女儿的手，向后退一步，猜到有事情发生，她用丝帕沾了沾眼角的泪水，向其他丫鬟吩咐道：“你们都出去，把门关好。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang released her daughter's hands and stepped back. Something was clearly wrong. She dabbed at her tears with a silk handkerchief, then turned to the other maids. “All of you, out. Close the door behind you.”

---

## 段落 36

**原文**：除了苦荞，其他丫鬟都乖乖退出。向夫人见门已关好，在椅子上坐下：“你们两个这是唱得哪一出？”

**译文**：Except for Kuqiao, the other maids obediently withdrew. Seeing the door closed, Madam Xiang sat down in a chair. “What's this act you two are putting on？”

---

## 段落 37

**原文**：苦荞会将一切告诉朱淑真的娘，千里寻不必操心，她继续坐好，仔细观察向夫人接下来会如何。

**译文**：Kuqiao would tell Zhu Shuzhen's mother everything. Qianli Xun didn't need to worry. She continued sitting and carefully observed what Madam Xiang would do next.

---

## 段落 38

**原文**：苦荞哭着说：“夫人，小姐自从嫁给姑爷，没过一天好日子，姑爷动不动打小姐，还把商妓带到家里。就昨天，又把商妓带到家里，还大打出手，结果把小姐猛一推，人撞到柱子上晕死过去，醒来后什么都不记得，像换了个人，呜呜……”

**译文**：Kuqiao cried, “Madam, ever since the miss married the young master, she hasn't had a single good day. He beats her for the smallest thing and even brings that merchant courtesan into the house! Just yesterday, he dragged her in again and went wild. He shoved the miss so hard she slammed into a pillar and blacked out. When she woke up, she didn't remember anything. She's like a completely different person. *Sob...*”

---

## 段落 39

**原文**：向夫人越听越气：“竟有这等事？为何不早回来告诉我和老爷？”

**译文**：The more Madam Xiang heard, the angrier she became. “How could such a thing happen? Why didn't you come back earlier to tell your father and me？”

---

## 段落 40

**原文**：苦荞抹着眼泪：“小姐不让，说告诉了也没用，不如让姑爷打死算了。呜呜……”

**译文**：Kuqiao wiped her tears. “The miss wouldn't let me. She said telling you wouldn't help anyway. Better to just let the young master beat her to death. *Sob...*”

---

## 段落 41

**原文**：向夫人看向女儿，她额头上的确有伤，怪自己见到女儿太过激动，竟没留意到她有伤。向夫人起身走到女儿跟前，用丝帕在伤口上轻轻挨了一下：“疼吗？”

**译文**：Madam Xiang looked at her daughter more carefully. There—a wound on her forehead. How could she have been so overjoyed that she missed it? She rose, crossed to her daughter, and gently pressed her silk handkerchief against the wound. “Does it hurt？”

---

## 段落 42

**原文**：自从母亲车祸去世后，千里寻对母爱更加渴望，向夫人的举动让她心里感到很温暖：“不疼，您真的是我娘？”

**译文**：Ever since her mother died in a car accident, Qianli Xun had longed even more for maternal love. Madam Xiang's gesture made her feel warm inside. “It doesn't hurt. You're really my mother？”

---

## 段落 43

**原文**：向夫人点点头：“你就是我女儿淑真。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang nodded. “You are my daughter Shuzhen.”

---

## 段落 44

**原文**：“娘，我醒来后什么都不记得，如果我与您以前的女儿不一样，您千万不要不认我。”

**译文**：“Mother, I don't remember anything after waking up. If I'm different from your daughter before, please don't turn your back on me.”

---

## 段落 45

**原文**：向夫人又流下眼泪：“早知道你到林家是去受罪，娘绝对不会同意把你嫁给林觉。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang shed tears again. “If I had known going to the Lin family meant suffering, I would never have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue.”

---

## 段落 46

**原文**：千里寻认为这个开场很不错，一下子把朱淑真的母亲拉到统一战线上来。

**译文**：Qianli Xun thought this opening was excellent. She had won Zhu Shuzhen's mother over to her side in one stroke.

---

## 段落 47

**原文**：向夫人又说：“苦荞，起来回话。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang said again, “Kuqiao, get up and answer.”

---

## 段落 48

**原文**：苦荞不再哭泣，乖乖站起来。

**译文**：Kuqiao stopped crying and stood up.

---

## 段落 49

**原文**：向夫人又坐回原位：“苦荞，你倒是给我说说，淑真跟以前有何不同？”

**译文**：Madam Xiang sat back in her seat. “Kuqiao, tell me, how is Shuzhen different from before？”

---

## 段落 50

**原文**：苦荞用目光征求小姐的意见，千里寻向她点点头，苦荞才大胆说：“除五官和身段是以前的小姐，其他都不一样，现在的小姐说话声音又大，话还多，连茶都不会做，关键是会功夫，姑爷都打不过她，还吵着让姑爷休了她。夫人，我觉得小姐一定是被姑爷打晕后重生了。”

**译文**：Kuqiao looked at her miss for approval. Qianli Xun nodded, and only then did Kuqiao speak boldly. “Everything except her face and figure is different. Now the miss speaks loudly, talks too much, and can't even make tea. The key thing is she knows martial arts—the young master can't beat her. She's even demanding he cast her out. Madam, I think the miss must have been beaten unconscious by the young master and reborn.”

---

## 段落 51

**原文**：“什么？重生？”向夫人惊得站起来，接着又坐下，拍着桌子大哭，“我那可怜的女儿啊！难道已被林觉打死了不成？呜呜……”

**译文**：“What? Reborn？” Madam Xiang sprang up in shock, then slumped back down, pounding the table as she wailed. “My poor daughter! Could it be that Lin Jue beat you to death? *Sob...*”

---

## 段落 52

**原文**：千里寻见向夫人哭得伤心，心有不忍：“娘，我活着，没死，只是跟以前有些不同。”

**译文**：Seeing Madam Xiang cry so heartbrokenly, Qianli Xun couldn't bear it. “Mother, I'm alive, not dead. Just a bit different from before.”

---

## 段落 53

**原文**：是啊，这声音还是女儿的声音，样貌也是女儿的样貌，她还活着。向夫人停止大哭：“对，我家淑真还活着，过来，让娘好好看看。”

**译文**：Yes, this voice was still her daughter's, and this face was still her daughter's. She was alive. Madam Xiang stopped crying loudly. “Right, my Shuzhen is still alive. Come here, let mother get a good look at you.”

---

## 段落 54

**原文**：千里寻起身走到向夫人面前，蹲下，头靠在母亲的膝头：“娘！”

**译文**：Qianli Xun got up, walked over to Madam Xiang, squatted down, and rested her head on her mother's knee. “Mother！”

---

## 段落 55

**原文**：向夫人摸着女儿的头发：“娘差点儿见不到你，不行，我绝对不能再让你回林家。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang stroked her daughter's hair. “I nearly lost you. No, I absolutely cannot let you go back to the Lin family.”

---

## 段落 56

**原文**：千里寻抬起头：“娘，我虽然什么都不记得，可我醒来后，林觉还打我，我亲眼看到商妓在家中，一气之下，让林觉休了我，后来才知女子被夫家所休，名声不好，就打算和离，林觉也同意，让我请朱家长辈出面，三日后商议和离之事。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun raised her head. “Mother, I don't remember anything. But after I woke up, Lin Jue was still beating me. I saw that courtesan right there in the house. I was so furious I told him to cast me out.”

She paused. “Later I found out—a woman cast out by her husband's family is ruined in reputation. So I pushed for a mutual separation instead. Lin Jue agreed, but he wants the Zhu family elders to step forward. We settle it in three days.”

---

## 段落 57

**原文**：向夫人扶起女儿：“放心，娘绝不会让你继续待在火坑里，算林觉识相，愿意和离，主要是我闺女没有错处，他找不到任何休你的理由。”

**译文**：Madam Xiang helped her daughter up. “Don't worry. I will never let you stay in that living hell. At least Lin Jue had the sense to agree to a mutual divorce. After all, my daughter has done nothing wrong—he has no grounds to cast you out anyway！”

---

## 段落 58

**原文**：“娘，我想在家住两日，不想回林府。”

**译文**：“Mother, I want to stay at home for a few days. I don't want to go back to the Lin residence.”

---

## 段落 59

**原文**：“傻女儿，这是你的家，想住多久都可以。”

**译文**：“Silly girl, this is your home. You can stay as long as you want.”

---

## 段落 60

**原文**：向夫人朝门外喊道：“银杏！”

**译文**：Madam Xiang called toward the door, “Yinxing！”

---

## 段落 61

**原文**：银杏推门而入：“夫人，有何吩咐？”

**译文**：Yinxing pushed open the door and entered. “Madam, what are your orders？”

---

## 段落 62

**原文**：“你亲自到林府一趟，告诉林家，小姐在朱府住下，至于什么时候回去，暂时还不知。”

**译文**：“Go to the Lin residence in person. Tell the Lin family that the miss is staying at the Zhu residence. As for when she'll return, we don't know yet.”

---

## 段落 63

**原文**：“是。”银杏领命而去。

**译文**：“Yes.” Yinxing accepted the order and left.

---

## 段落 64

**原文**：千里寻此刻感到很幸福，有家庭做后盾，让她在陌生的时代一下子充满踏实感。

**译文**：At this moment, Qianli Xun felt very happy. Having a family as her support gave her a sense of security in this unfamiliar era.

---

## 段落 65

**原文**：“等你爹回来，我会将此事与他说明，你再去拜见他，娘相信你爹也会同意和离之事。”

**译文**：“When your father returns, I'll explain the matter to him. Then you can go pay your respects. I believe he'll also agree to the divorce.”

---

## 段落 66

**原文**：“我爹会不会很凶？”

**译文**：“Will he be very strict？”

---

## 段落 67

**原文**：千里寻的问题令向夫人伤心，看来女儿真的什么都不记得：“你爹不凶，但他很严肃，有时候也很严厉，你不必担忧。以前的院子还给你留着，让苦荞带你过去，既然什么都记不得，就让苦荞再带你各处走走，熟悉熟悉，也许慢慢会想起来。”

**译文**：Qianli Xun's question saddened Madam Xiang. It seemed her daughter really didn't remember anything. “He isn't fierce, just very serious—sometimes strict. Don't worry. Your old courtyard is still kept for you. Let Kuqiao take you there and show you around. Since you don't remember anything, she can help you get familiar with the place. Maybe you'll gradually remember.”

---

## 段落 68

**原文**：千里寻拜别向夫人，在苦荞的带领下继续拐着弯儿，向朱府深处走去。很难想象朱府到底有多大，反正此刻她觉得怎么走都走不到尽头。

**译文**：Qianli Xun said goodbye to Madam Xiang. Led by Kuqiao, she continued turning corners, heading deeper into the Zhu residence. It was hard to imagine just how big the estate was. It felt like no matter how far she walked, she'd never reach the end.

---

## 段落 69
