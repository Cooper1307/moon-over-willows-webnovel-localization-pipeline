# Chapter 10: Home to Zhu Manor, Meeting Her Mother

Kuqiao knocked on the side gate for a moment. Soon the servant on duty opened up, recognized Kuqiao, realized the young mistress was home, and swung the door wide. "Miss is back! Madam has been talking about you nonstop!"

"Where are Master and Madam?" Kuqiao asked. "Miss came to pay her respects."

"Master's still at the prefecture office. Madam is in the garden feeding the fish," the servant replied, stepping aside to clear the way. Qianli Xun crossed the threshold with Kuqiao right behind her. Once they were inside, the servant added, "Wait indoors, Miss; I'll run report to Madam." He jogged off toward the garden.

"Where do we go now?" Qianli Xun asked.

"Madam's quarters. Just stay right behind me, Miss."

Kuqiao led the way while Qianli Xun trailed after her. Zhu Shuzhen's childhood home felt like stepping into a labyrinth, though a scenic one that pleased the eye at every turn. With her hopeless sense of direction, Qianli Xun knew she would lose her bearings in seconds without a guide.

"Kuqiao, stick beside me the whole time in Zhu Manor. This estate is massive and twists every which way; I'll get lost for sure."

"Don't worry, Miss. I won't leave your side for a second."

After more twists than she could count, a courtyard gate appeared ahead. "Madam lives here, Miss. Come inside," Kuqiao said.

Stepping into the yard, they met a maid hurrying toward them. "Miss is home! Quick, come sit inside; I'll send someone to fetch Madam!"

"No need, Sister. The gatekeeper already went," Kuqiao replied, turning to introduce her. "Miss, this is Yinxing, a maid from Madam's rooms."

Before Qianli Xun could speak, Yinxing chided her, "Since when does Miss need you introducing me?" Taking Qianli Xun by the arm, she guided her indoors, chattering as they walked. "Miss, you haven't set foot home since marrying into the Lin house. If Master hadn't held her back, Madam would have marched right over to Lin Manor for you!"

Rarely this quiet, Qianli Xun clamped her lips shut lest one slip give her away. Inside, she picked a chair and sat while Yinxing sent other maids to brew tea. Once tea was laid out, Yinxing drew Kuqiao aside and asked in a hushed voice, "What's gotten into Miss? Her expression looks odd, nothing like before."

"Quit asking silly questions, Sister. I'm waiting on her here; go tend to your chores," Kuqiao brushed her off and resumed her post beside her mistress.

Yinxing turned back to Qianli Xun. "Sit tight, Miss. I'll step out to the doorway and watch for Madam."

Qianli Xun nodded. "Go ahead, no need to hover."

Once Yinxing left, Qianli Xun let out a quiet breath. About to face Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother, she wondered if she ought to squeeze out a few tears to look sincere, then thought better of it. Faking the original Zhu Shuzhen would look phony; better to stay natural and read the room as it came.

No sooner had she decided than a woman in her forties hurried in with reddened eyes, ringed by four or five maids. Guessing this was Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Qianli Xun rose from her seat.

Madam Xiang had been feeding the garden fish when a page boy ran up with word of her daughter's return; dropping everything, she rushed straight back with her maids. Stepping through the doorway and laying eyes on the daughter she had missed for over half a year, she felt her nose sting and tears well up. Seeing Shuzhen stand, she hurried over and seized both her hands. "Shuzhen, Mother thought you'd never come back to see me..."

"You're Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother?" Qianli Xun asked straight out. "When I woke up, Kuqiao told me I'm Zhu Shuzhen, daughter of Zhu Manor, so I had her bring me home today to meet my family."

That single speech froze Madam Xiang and every maid in the room. Staring through eyes brimming with tears, Madam Xiang looked her daughter over from head to toe. "Shuzhen, what on earth are you saying?"

Thump! Kuqiao dropped straight to her knees and burst into sobs before getting a word out. Good teammate, Qianli Xun applauded in silence!

Letting go of her daughter and stepping back, Madam Xiang sensed grave trouble. Dabbing the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief, she ordered the other maids, "All of you out, and shut the door." Once every maid except Kuqiao filed out and the door clicked shut, Madam Xiang sank into a chair. "What kind of scene are you two putting on?"

Knowing Kuqiao would lay out the whole tale, Qianli Xun sat back down and watched how Madam Xiang would take it.

Weeping hard, Kuqiao poured it all out. "Madam, Miss hasn't had one peaceful day since marrying Son-in-law! He beats her over nothing and drags brothel courtesans right into the house! Just yesterday he brought one home again, beat Miss, and shoved her straight into a pillar! She fainted dead away, and when she woke up, she couldn't remember a thing, like a whole different person! Sob..."

Fury surged across Madam Xiang's face. "How dare he! Why didn't you come home and tell Master and me sooner?"

Wiping her cheeks, Kuqiao sobbed, "Miss forbade me! Said telling you wouldn't help, might as well let Son-in-law beat her to death! Sob..."

Madam Xiang looked close at her daughter and spotted the wound across her forehead; overwhelmed at seeing her, she hadn't even noticed. Rising, she stepped over and touched the cut light as a feather with her handkerchief. "Does it hurt?"

Starved for motherly warmth since losing her own mother in that crash, Qianli Xun felt heat bloom inside her chest. "Doesn't hurt. Are you really my mother?"

Madam Xiang nodded. "You are my daughter Shuzhen."

"Mother, I woke up remembering nothing. If I'm not like the daughter you knew, please don't disown me."

Fresh tears rolled down Madam Xiang's cheeks. "Had I known you'd suffer like this in the Lin house, I never would have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue!"

What a clean start, Qianli Xun thought—already pulled Zhu Shuzhen's mother onto her side!

"Get up and speak, Kuqiao," Madam Xiang ordered. Drying her eyes, Kuqiao climbed to her feet. Sitting back down, Madam Xiang pressed on, "Tell me, Kuqiao: how is Shuzhen different from before?"

Glancing at her mistress for permission and catching a nod, Kuqiao spoke up. "Aside from her face and figure, everything's different! Now she speaks loud, talks nonstop, can't even whisk tea, and best of all, she knows martial arts! Even Son-in-law couldn't beat her, and she demanded he cast her out! Madam, I think Son-in-law knocked her cold and she was reborn!"

"What? Reborn?" Madam Xiang shot up in shock, then dropped back down, slapping the table as she wailed, "My poor daughter! Did Lin Jue beat you to death?! Sob..."

Unable to bear seeing her weep like that, Qianli Xun spoke up. "Mother, I'm alive, not dead, just a bit different from before."

Yes, same voice, same face, living and breathing before her eyes. Madam Xiang choked back her wails. "Yes, my Shuzhen is alive! Come here, let Mother get a good look at you."

Qianli Xun walked over, crouched down, and rested her head against her mother's knees. "Mother!"

Stroking her hair, Madam Xiang swore, "Mother almost lost you! No, I will never let you go back to that Lin house!"

Lifting her head, Qianli Xun said, "Mother, I may not remember the past, yet even after I woke up Lin Jue tried hitting me, and I saw that courtesan right there in the house. Out of anger I told him to write a bill of divorce, till I learned a cast-out wife loses her good name. So I pushed for mutual divorce. Lin Jue agreed and told me to bring Zhu family elders in three days to settle it."

Madam Xiang helped her stand. "Rest easy; Mother won't leave you trapped in that fiery pit! Good thing Lin Jue has the sense to agree to mutual divorce. My daughter did nothing wrong; he has zero grounds to cast you out anyway!"

"Mother, I want to stay home for a couple of days. I don't want to go back to Lin Manor."

"Silly girl, this is your home. Stay as long as you wish!" Madam Xiang called toward the door, "Yinxing!"

Yinxing pushed the door open. "Your orders, Madam?"

"Go to Lin Manor in person and inform the Lin family that Miss is staying at Zhu Manor. When she returns, we don't know yet."

"Yes, Madam." Yinxing bowed and went off on her errand.

A rush of joy warmed Qianli Xun. Having real family backing her anchored her in this strange century.

"Once your father gets home, I'll explain everything before you pay your respects," Madam Xiang told her. "Mother believes he'll consent to mutual divorce too."

"Is Father fierce?"

The question pierced Madam Xiang's heart, proving her daughter truly recalled nothing. "Not fierce, just stern, and strict at times. Nothing to fear. Your old courtyard is still kept for you. Have Kuqiao take you over and walk you around the estate so you can get your bearings; maybe your memories will come back bit by bit."

Bidding Madam Xiang farewell, Qianli Xun followed Kuqiao through winding lane after winding lane deeper into Zhu Manor. Impossible to guess how sprawling the grounds were; it felt as if they could walk forever without hitting a wall.

Along the way, singing drifted across the air. Qianli Xun halted and looked toward the sound, spotting another walled courtyard whose high walls hid the halls within. "Kuqiao, who lives in there? Why is someone singing?"

"Those are the singing girls and dancing girls Master keeps."

"What? Singing and dancing girls? Why does my father keep entertainers?"

"Most high officials' estates keep musicians and dancers to entertain honored guests. Every year Master hosts a poetry banquet for Lin'an's noted scholars, and entertainers help liven up the occasion."

"Did I attend those banquets?"

"Every year since you were ten, Miss! Many of your finest poems and lyrics were composed right at those banquets and passed from hand to hand by scholars. That's how you earned the title Poetess of Jiangnan."

"The Poetess of Jiangnan," Qianli Xun murmured. Web novels she could spin in her sleep, yet classical verse was not her craft. Wearing Zhu Shuzhen's name meant defending her place in the literary world, and pressure pricked at her chest until she remembered she knew Zhu Shuzhen's poems by heart. Exhaling, she asked, "Kuqiao, isn't there a single straight path in Zhu Manor? Everything twists like a maze!"

"Not one! Heard Master built this manor around the poetic ideal of winding paths leading to secluded spots. Isn't it poetic?"

"Poetic, sure, and it's making me dizzy! Where on earth is my courtyard?"

"Patience, Miss. Two more bends and we're there."

Two turns later, a small lake opened before them, over half its surface blanketed with lotus leaves dotted with upright lotus buds. A little wooden bridge spanned the water, leading at last into Zhu Shuzhen's own secluded garden.
