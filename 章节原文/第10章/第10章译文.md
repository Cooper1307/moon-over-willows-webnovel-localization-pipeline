# Chapter 10: Returning to the Zhu Residence to Meet Her Birth Mother

Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked. Before long, a servant on duty opened the door. Seeing Kuqiao, he knew the young mistress had returned and immediately pulled the door wider. "The young mistress is back! The madam has been talking about you nonstop."

Kuqiao asked, "Where are the master and madam? The young mistress wishes to pay her respects."

"The master went to the Prefectural Office and hasn't returned yet. The madam is feeding fish in the garden." After speaking, the servant stepped aside to let them through.

Qianli Xun stepped over the threshold, with Kuqiao close behind.

Once they were inside, the servant said, "Please wait in the room, young mistress. I'll go inform the madam." With that, he jogged off toward the garden.

Qianli Xun asked, "Where are we going now?"

"To the madam's quarters. Just follow me, young mistress."

Kuqiao led the way, and Qianli Xun followed obediently. The place where Zhu Shuzhen grew up felt like a maze to her—but the "maze" was quite scenic, pleasing to look at.

Qianli Xun had always been terrible with directions. With all these twists and turns, without a guide, she'd be lost for sure.

"Kuqiao, you must stay by my side at all times in the Zhu residence. This place is huge, with turns everywhere. I'm afraid I'll lose my way."

"Don't worry, young mistress. I won't leave your side for a moment."

After who knows how many turns, they finally reached a courtyard.

"Young mistress, the madam lives here. Let me take you in."

They entered the courtyard. A maid came toward them.

"The young mistress is back! Quick, come in and sit down. I'll send someone to fetch the madam right away."

"No need, sister. The servant at the gate has already gone to fetch the madam."

After speaking, Kuqiao introduced, "This is Yinxing, the madam's personal maid."

Before Qianli Xun could say a word, Yinxing chided, "The young mistress knows me perfectly well. What do you need to introduce me for?" With that, she took the young mistress's hand and led her inside.

As they walked, she said, "Ever since you married into the Lin family, you haven't been back once. If the master hadn't stopped her, the madam would have run straight to the Lin residence to find you."

Qianli Xun was rarely this silent. She was afraid that if she opened her mouth, she'd give herself away in front of the maids. Entering the room, she picked a chair and sat down. Yinxing was busy ordering other maids to serve tea.

After the tea was set out, Yinxing pulled Kuqiao aside and whispered, "Does the young mistress seem strange to you? Her expression feels different somehow."

"Don't ask silly questions, sister. I'm here to attend to her. Go about your business." After speaking, Kuqiao returned to stand by her mistress's side.

Yinxing said, "Young mistress, please sit. I'll go check at the gate to see if the madam is back."

Qianli Xun nodded. "Go about your business. No need to attend me here."

After Yinxing left, Qianli Xun relaxed considerably. She was about to meet Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She figured she should shed a few tears to seem more sincere. But on second thought, no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't fake being the real Zhu Shuzhen. Better to just be herself and play it by ear.

No sooner had she made up her mind than a woman in her forties appeared before her, eyes slightly red, surrounded by four or five maids.

Qianli Xun guessed this must be Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She couldn't help but stand up.

Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Madame Xiang, had been feeding fish in the garden. When a page boy reported that her daughter was back, she had no mind for anything else and hurried back with her maids.

Stepping inside, she saw the daughter she hadn't seen in over half a year. Madame Xiang's nose stung, and her eyes reddened. Seeing her daughter standing, she rushed forward and grabbed her hands. "Shuzhen, your mother thought you'd never come back to see me again..."

Qianli Xun said, "You must be Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother? After I woke up, Kuqiao told me I am Zhu Shuzhen, the young lady of the Zhu residence. So today I had her bring me back to meet my family."

Qianli Xun's words left Madame Xiang and the surrounding maids completely stunned.

Madame Xiang looked her daughter over again with tear-filled eyes. "Shuzhen, what do you mean by such words?"

With a thud, Kuqiao dropped to her knees. Before she could speak, she burst into tears.

*Kuqiao is really playing along!* Qianli Xun admired inwardly.

Madame Xiang released her daughter's hands and stepped back. Sensing something had happened, she dabbed the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief and ordered the other maids, "All of you, leave and close the door behind you."

All the maids obediently filed out, except Kuqiao. Seeing the door closed, Madame Xiang sat down in a chair. "What's going on between you two?"

Kuqiao would tell Zhu Shuzhen's mother everything—no need for Qianli Xun to worry. She sat back and watched carefully, curious to see what Madame Xiang would do next.

Kuqiao said through tears, "Madam, ever since the young mistress married the young master, she hasn't had a single good day. The young master beats her for no good reason and has even brought a merchant courtesan into the home. Just yesterday, he brought her home again and beat her severely. He pushed the young mistress hard, and she hit her head on a pillar and passed out. When she woke up, she didn't remember anything. She's like a completely different person. Wuuu..."

The more Madame Xiang heard, the angrier she grew. "Such a thing? Why didn't you come back and tell your father and me sooner?"

Kuqiao wiped her tears. "The young mistress wouldn't let us. She said telling you would be useless—that she might as well let the young master beat her to death. Wuuu..."

Madame Xiang looked at her daughter. There was indeed a wound on her forehead. She'd been so overcome with emotion upon seeing her daughter that she hadn't noticed the injury. Madame Xiang rose, walked over, and gently touched the wound with her silk handkerchief. "Does it hurt?"

Ever since her mother died in a car accident, Qianli Xun had yearned even more for maternal love. Madame Xiang's gesture warmed her deeply. "It doesn't hurt. Are you really my mother?"

Madame Xiang nodded. "You are my daughter Shuzhen."

"Mother, I don't remember anything after waking up. If I'm different from your daughter before, please don't disown me."

Madame Xiang shed tears again. "Had I known going to the Lin family meant suffering, your mother would never have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue."

*This opening is going quite well,* Qianli Xun thought. She'd won Zhu Shuzhen's mother over to her side in one stroke.

Madame Xiang spoke again. "Kuqiao, get up and answer me."

Kuqiao stopped crying and obediently stood up.

Madame Xiang sat back in her chair. "Kuqiao, tell me—how is Shuzhen different from before?"

Kuqiao looked at her mistress for permission. Qianli Xun nodded, and only then did Kuqiao speak boldly. "Everything is different except her face and figure. The young mistress now speaks loudly and talks too much. She can't even make tea properly. Most importantly, she knows martial arts—the young master can't beat her. She's been demanding the young master divorce her. Madam, I think the young mistress must have been beaten unconscious and reborn."

"What? Reborn?" Madame Xiang stood up in shock, then sat back down, pounding the table and crying. "My poor daughter! Could she have been beaten to death by Lin Jue? Wuuu..."

Seeing Madame Xiang cry so bitterly, Qianli Xun couldn't bear it. "Mother, I'm alive. I'm not dead. Just a little different from before."

Yes, this was still her daughter's voice, her daughter's face. She was alive. Madame Xiang stopped crying. "Yes, my Shuzhen is still alive. Come here, let your mother get a good look at you."

Qianli Xun rose, walked over to Madame Xiang, squatted down, and rested her head on her mother's knee. "Mother!"

Madame Xiang stroked her daughter's hair. "Your mother nearly lost you forever. No, I absolutely cannot let you return to the Lin family."

Qianli Xun looked up. "Mother, though I don't remember anything, after I woke up, Lin Jue still beat me. I saw the merchant courtesan in our home with my own eyes. In a fit of anger, I demanded Lin Jue divorce me. Only later did I learn that being cast out by the husband's family brings a bad reputation. So we agreed to a mutual divorce. Lin Jue consented and asked me to have the Zhu family elders step forward. We'll discuss the divorce terms in three days."

Madame Xiang helped her daughter up. "Don't worry. Your mother will never let you stay in that pit of fire. Lin Jue has the sense to agree to a mutual divorce. Mainly because my daughter has done nothing wrong—he couldn't find any reason to cast you out."

"Mother, I want to stay home for a few days. I don't want to go back to the Lin residence."

"Silly girl, this is your home. You can stay as long as you like."

Madame Xiang called toward the door, "Yinxing!"

Yinxing pushed open the door. "Madam, what are your orders?"

"Go to the Lin residence in person. Tell the Lin family that the young mistress is staying at the Zhu residence. As for when she'll return, we don't know yet."

"Yes." Yinxing accepted the order and left.

Qianli Xun felt truly happy at this moment. Having a family as her backbone gave her a sense of security in this unfamiliar era.

"When your father returns, I'll explain everything to him. Then you can go pay your respects. Your mother believes your father will also agree to the divorce."

"Will my father be very fierce?"

Qianli Xun's question saddened Madame Xiang. It seemed her daughter truly remembered nothing. "Your father isn't fierce, but he's serious, sometimes strict. Don't worry. Your old courtyard has been kept for you. Let Kuqiao take you there. Since you can't remember anything, have Kuqiao show you around the estate. Maybe you'll gradually recall things."

Qianli Xun bid farewell to Madame Xiang. Led by Kuqiao, she continued winding deeper into the Zhu residence. It was hard to imagine just how large the estate was. At this moment, it felt like no matter how far she walked, there was no end in sight.

As they walked, the sound of someone singing a tune drifted over. Qianli Xun stopped and looked toward the source. Sure enough, there was a courtyard, its walls blocking any view of the buildings inside.

"Kuqiao, who lives there? Why is someone singing?"

"That's where the master's songstresses and dancers live."

"What? Songstresses and dancers? What does my father keep them for?"

"Most noble families keep songstresses and dancers, mainly to entertain distinguished guests. The master hosts a poetry salon every year, inviting all the renowned scholars and talented poets in Lin'an. The salon also needs songstresses and dancers to liven things up."

"Have I ever attended the poetry salon?"

"You've attended every year since you were ten. Many of your ci poems were composed at the salon and passed around by the scholars and poets. That's how you earned the title 'Poetess of Jiangnan.'"

"The Poetess of Jiangnan." Qianli Xun lowered her head and murmured. Novels she could write, but poetry? She was a complete novice. Wearing Zhu Shuzhen's identity, she'd have to uphold that literary reputation. The pressure was real. Thinking that she'd already memorized Zhu Shuzhen's poems, she finally relaxed. "Kuqiao, is there no straight path in the Zhu residence? Everything winds and twists?"

"There really isn't. I heard when the master built this estate, he chose the style of 'winding paths to secluded spots.' Isn't that poetic?"

"It's poetic alright, but it's left me dizzy. Where exactly do I live?"

"Don't worry, young mistress. Just two more turns."

Qianli Xun followed Kuqiao around two more turns. A small lake appeared before them. Lotus leaves covered most of the water, with a few scattered flower buds standing here and there. A wooden bridge spanned the lake. Only after crossing it did they truly reach the garden where Zhu Shuzhen had lived.
