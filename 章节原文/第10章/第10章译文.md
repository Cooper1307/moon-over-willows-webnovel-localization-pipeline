# Chapter 10: Returning to the Zhu Residence to Meet Her Birth Mother

---

Kuqiao stepped forward and knocked. Before long, a servant on duty opened the door. Seeing Kuqiao, the servant knew the young miss had returned and immediately opened the door wider. "The miss is back! The madam has been talking about you nonstop."

Kuqiao asked, "Where are the master and madam? The miss wants to pay her respects."

"The master went to the prefectural office and hasn't returned yet. The madam is feeding fish in the garden." After speaking, the servant stepped aside to let them through.

Qianli Xun stepped over the threshold, with Kuqiao close behind.

Once they entered, the servant said, "Please wait inside, Miss. I'll go inform the madam." With that, she trotted off toward the garden.

"Where do we go now?" Qianli Xun asked.

"To the madam's quarters. Just follow me, Miss."

Kuqiao led the way, and Qianli Xun followed obediently. The place where Zhu Shuzhen grew up felt like entering a maze to her. But the scenery in this "maze" was truly delightful.

Qianli Xun had always been terrible with directions. With all these twists and turns, without a guide, she would definitely get lost.

"Kuqiao, you must stay by my side at all times in the Zhu residence. The manor is too big, with so many twists and turns. I'm afraid I'll lose my way."

"Don't worry, Miss. I won't leave your side for a moment."

After who knows how many turns, they finally reached a courtyard.

"Miss, the madam lives here. I'll take you in."

As they entered the courtyard, a maid came to greet them.

"The miss is back! Quick, come inside and sit. I'll send someone to fetch the madam right away."

"No need to send anyone, sister. The servant at the gate has already gone to call the madam."

After speaking, Kuqiao introduced, "This is Yinxing, the madam's personal maid."

Before Qianli Xun could speak, Yinxing scolded, "How could the miss not recognize me? Why do you need to introduce me?" With that, she pulled the miss toward the room.

As they walked, she said, "Ever since you married into the Lin family, you haven't been back once. If it weren't for the master holding her back, the madam would have definitely run to the Lin residence to find you."

Qianli Xun was rarely as silent as she was now. She was afraid that if she opened her mouth, she'd give herself away in front of the maids. Entering the room, she picked a chair and sat down. Yinxing busied herself ordering other maids to serve tea.

After the tea was set, Yinxing pulled Kuqiao aside and whispered, "Why does the miss seem strange? Her expression feels different from before."

"Don't ask nonsense, sister. I'll take care of things here. You can go about your business." After speaking, Kuqiao returned to stand by her miss.

Yinxing said, "Miss, please sit. I'll go to the gate and check if the madam is back."

Qianli Xun nodded. "Go about your business. No need to attend to me."

After Yinxing left, Qianli Xun relaxed considerably. She was about to meet Zhu Shuzhen's mother and thought she should shed some tears to show genuine emotion. But then she reconsidered—no matter how hard she tried, she couldn't fake being Zhu Shuzhen. Better to just be herself and play it by ear.

No sooner had she made up her mind than a woman in her forties appeared before her, eyes slightly red, surrounded by four or five maids.

Qianli Xun guessed this must be Zhu Shuzhen's mother. She couldn't help but stand up.

Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Madam Xiang, had been feeding fish in the garden. When a page boy reported that her daughter had returned, she cared for nothing else and rushed back with her maids.

As soon as she entered and saw her daughter, whom she hadn't seen in over half a year, Madam Xiang felt her nose sting and her eyes redden. Seeing her daughter stand up, she hurried forward and grabbed her hands. "Shuzhen, I thought you'd never come back to see me again..."

Qianli Xun said, "You are Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother? After I woke up, Kuqiao told me I am Zhu Shuzhen, the young miss of the Zhu residence. So today I had her bring me back to meet my family."

Qianli Xun's words completely stunned Madam Xiang and the surrounding maids.

With tear-filled eyes, Madam Xiang looked at her daughter again. "Shuzhen, what do you mean by that?"

With a thud, Kuqiao dropped to her knees. Before she could speak, she started crying.

Qianli Xun marveled inwardly, *Kuqiao is really cooperating!*

Madam Xiang released her daughter's hands and stepped back, guessing something had happened. She dabbed the tears from the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief and ordered the other maids, "All of you, go out and close the door."

Except for Kuqiao, the other maids obediently withdrew. Seeing the door closed, Madam Xiang sat down in a chair. "What act are you two performing?"

Kuqiao would tell Zhu Shuzhen's mother everything. Qianli Xun didn't need to worry. She continued sitting and carefully observed what Madam Xiang would do next.

Kuqiao cried, "Madam, ever since the miss married the young master, she hasn't had a single good day. The young master beats her at the slightest provocation and even brings the merchant courtesan home. Just yesterday, he brought her home again and got violent. He pushed the miss hard, and she hit her head on a pillar and passed out. When she woke up, she didn't remember anything, like a completely different person. Wuuu..."

The more Madam Xiang heard, the angrier she became. "How could such a thing happen? Why didn't you come back earlier to tell your father and me?"

Kuqiao wiped her tears. "The miss wouldn't let me. She said telling you wouldn't help anyway. Better to just let the young master beat her to death. Wuuu..."

Madam Xiang looked at her daughter. Indeed, there was a wound on her forehead. She blamed herself for being too excited to see her daughter and not noticing the injury. Madam Xiang got up, walked over to her daughter, and gently touched the wound with her silk handkerchief. "Does it hurt?"

Ever since her mother died in a car accident, Qianli Xun had longed even more for maternal love. Madam Xiang's gesture made her feel warm inside. "It doesn't hurt. You're really my mother?"

Madam Xiang nodded. "You are my daughter Shuzhen."

"Mother, I don't remember anything after waking up. If I'm different from your daughter before, please don't disown me."

Madam Xiang shed tears again. "If I had known going to the Lin family meant suffering, I would never have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue."

Qianli Xun thought this opening was excellent. She had won Zhu Shuzhen's mother over to her side in one stroke.

Madam Xiang said again, "Kuqiao, get up and answer."

Kuqiao stopped crying and obediently stood up.

Madam Xiang sat back in her seat. "Kuqiao, tell me, how is Shuzhen different from before?"

Kuqiao looked at her miss for approval. Qianli Xun nodded, and only then did Kuqiao speak boldly. "Everything except her face and figure is different. Now the miss speaks loudly, talks too much, and can't even make tea. The key thing is she knows martial arts—the young master can't beat her. She's even demanding he divorce her. Madam, I think the miss must have been beaten unconscious by the young master and reborn."

"What? Reborn?" Madam Xiang stood up in shock, then sat back down, slapping the table and crying loudly. "My poor daughter! Could it be that Lin Jue beat you to death? Wuuu..."

Seeing Madam Xiang cry so heartbrokenly, Qianli Xun couldn't bear it. "Mother, I'm alive, not dead. Just a bit different from before."

Yes, this voice was still her daughter's voice, and this face was still her daughter's face. She was alive. Madam Xiang stopped crying loudly. "Right, my Shuzhen is still alive. Come here, let mother get a good look at you."

Qianli Xun got up, walked over to Madam Xiang, squatted down, and rested her head on her mother's knee. "Mother!"

Madam Xiang stroked her daughter's hair. "I almost didn't get to see you again. No, I absolutely cannot let you go back to the Lin family."

Qianli Xun raised her head. "Mother, although I don't remember anything, after I woke up, Lin Jue still beat me. I saw the merchant courtesan in the house with my own eyes. In a fit of anger, I demanded Lin Jue divorce me. Later I learned that a woman divorced by her husband's family has a bad reputation, so I planned a mutual divorce instead. Lin Jue agreed and asked me to have the Zhu family elders mediate. We'll discuss the divorce in three days."

Madam Xiang helped her daughter up. "Don't worry, I absolutely won't let you stay in that pit of fire. It's good that Lin Jue has the sense to agree to a mutual divorce. Mainly because my daughter has done nothing wrong—he can't find any reason to divorce you."

"Mother, I want to stay at home for a few days. I don't want to go back to the Lin residence."

"Silly daughter, this is your home. You can stay as long as you want."

Madam Xiang called toward the door, "Yinxing!"

Yinxing pushed open the door and entered. "Madam, what are your orders?"

"Go to the Lin residence in person. Tell the Lin family that the miss is staying at the Zhu residence. As for when she'll return, we don't know yet."

"Yes." Yinxing accepted the order and left.

At this moment, Qianli Xun felt very happy. Having a family as her support gave her a sense of security in this unfamiliar era.

"When your father returns, I'll explain the matter to him. Then you can go pay your respects. I believe your father will also agree to the divorce."

"Will my father be very fierce?"

Qianli Xun's question saddened Madam Xiang. It seemed her daughter really didn't remember anything. "Your father isn't fierce, but he's very serious, sometimes strict. Don't worry. Your old courtyard is still kept for you. Let Kuqiao take you there. Since you don't remember anything, let Kuqiao show you around. Maybe you'll gradually remember."

Qianli Xun took her leave of Madam Xiang. Led by Kuqiao, she continued turning corners, heading deeper into the Zhu residence. It was hard to imagine just how big the Zhu residence was. At this moment, it felt like no matter how far she walked, she'd never reach the end.

As they walked, she heard the sound of singing. Qianli Xun stopped and looked toward the source. Sure enough, there was a courtyard, but the walls blocked the view of the buildings inside.

"Kuqiao, who lives there? Why is someone singing?"

"That's where the master's songstresses and dancers live."

"What? Songstresses and dancers? What does my father keep them for?"

"Most high-ranking officials keep songstresses and dancers, mainly to entertain distinguished guests. Every year, the master hosts a poetry salon, inviting all the notable literary talents in Lin'an. The poetry salon also needs songstresses and dancers to liven up the occasion."

"Have I ever attended the poetry salon?"

"You've attended every year since you were ten. Many of your ci poems were composed at the poetry salon and were eagerly recited by literary talents. That's how you earned the reputation as the Poetess of Jiangnan."

"The Poetess of Jiangnan." Qianli Xun lowered her head and pondered. She could write novels, but poetry was a completely foreign territory to her. With Zhu Shuzhen's identity, she would definitely need to uphold her status in the literary world. Qianli Xun felt tremendous pressure. Thinking that she had already memorized Zhu Shuzhen's poems in her mind, she finally relaxed a bit. "Kuqiao, isn't there a straight path in the Zhu residence? Why all these twists and turns?"

"Actually, no. I heard when the master built this residence, he chose the style of 'winding paths leading to secluded spots.' Isn't that very poetic?"

"It is poetic, but it's made me dizzy. Where exactly do I live?"

"Don't worry, Miss. Just two more turns and we'll be there."

Qianli Xun followed Kuqiao around two more turns. A lake appeared before them—not very large, with lotus leaves covering most of the water. A few flower buds stood scattered here and there. A wooden bridge spanned the lake. Only after crossing this bridge did they truly arrive at the garden where Zhu Shuzhen had lived.
