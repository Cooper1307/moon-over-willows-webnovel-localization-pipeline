## Chapter 305: Carrying His Wife Down the Mountain

Cold steel hovered inches from Qianli Xun's neck; she closed her eyes, bracing for the end—

Black-Fiend Tiger paused half a heartbeat regretting destroying such beauty.

*CRACK!* A whistling pebble slammed straight into his knuckles, knocking the sword clattering—and Shen Jianchen flew across the room in a blur, flooring Black-Fiend Tiger with a thunderous punch and planting himself squarely in front of Qianli Xun.

"Don't be afraid. I'm here."

No words of thanks needed between them—she simply watched him dismantle the rushing mob with crisp, effortless strikes until he kicked Black-Fiend Tiger clean out the door.

"Quit showing off and untie me!" she hollered.

Cutting her bonds, he saw blood pooling beneath her sole—"Hurt?"—"Scratch!" Slipping on a spare pair of women's shoes nearby and draping Shen Jianchen's outer robe over her sleeping clothes, she marched out into the courtyard with him: "Tell your men outside to hold off—haven't stretched my fists in ages!"

"Hold outside until Youqi Jushi and I finish our warm-up!" Shen Jianchen shouted.

Standing back to back, the pair swept through six straight waves of charging bandits like scythes through wheat until Haoyu—unable to stomach watching their seamless chemistry from outside—kicked the wooden stronghold gate wide yelling, "Wife, I'm here to save you!"—and walked straight into Black-Fiend Tiger's chokehold!

"Charge in!" Shen Jianchen barked.

Lin Jue, Wen Xin, and garrison troops flooded the yard! Distracted, Black-Fiend Tiger ate Qianli Xun's lightning punch—then rose slashing at her as Haoyu threw himself straight in front of her blade before Shen Jianchen dropped the bandit chief cold and trussed him tight.

Three hundred fifty bandits captured, ten abducted women freed, every Chen treasure tallied—even Lin Jue couldn't resist snarking at Shuzhen before Haoyu shoved him right back down the path!

The moment Qianli Xun stepped forward, fresh blood soaked through her shoe. Without a word, Haoyu crouched and hoisted her securely onto his back. Watching her wrap her arms gently around Haoyu's neck and drift off to sleep against his shoulder stabbed Shen Jianchen quietly, yet he walked ahead keeping silent watch—even catching Haoyu's elbow when his boot slipped on loose gravel, before yielding the lone carriage at the mountain foot to the couple and riding home on horseback.

Inside the carriage, Haoyu gently bound her sole with a clean handkerchief, promising earnestly he'd grow old right beside her—making Qianli Xun realize, with a quiet flutter, that she was genuinely starting to fear losing his heart.
