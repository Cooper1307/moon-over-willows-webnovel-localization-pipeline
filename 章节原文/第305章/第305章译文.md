## Chapter 305: Carrying His Wife Down the Mountain

Cold steel hovered above her; Qianli Xun shut her eyes, bracing for the end.

Even at the last split second, Black-Fiend Tiger hesitated a heartbeat—shame to waste such beauty.

Zip! A flying pebble cracked across his knuckles like lightning. His fingers spasmed, the saber clattering to the floor.

Qianli Xun’s eyes snapped open as Shen Jianchen blurred past everyone, decked Black-Fiend Tiger flat with a single punch, and planted himself straight in front of her. “Don’t be afraid. I’m here.”

No thanks needed between them—she let herself breathe behind his broad back.

Furious, Black-Fiend Tiger scrambled up yelling, “Kill him!” Bandits swarmed in with sabers, only for Shen Jianchen to weave through like wind, knocking blades spinning and driving men reeling back through the doorway before booting Black-Fiend Tiger clean out into the yard after ten bouts.

“Quit showing off and untie me first!” Qianli Xun called out.

Hurrying back to cut her ropes, Shen Jianchen spotted blood pooling under her sole. “Hurt bad?”

“Nothing.” Spotting a spare pair of women’s shoes in the room, she gritted past the sting and slipped them on, easing the cut off the grit. Shen Jianchen shrugged off his outer robe and handed it over; draping it round her shoulders, she grinned fierce. “Time to paint Mount Tianmu red!”

“Nearly got chopped up and still talking big?”

“Less talk, more fists!”

Stepping out into the yard, bandits ringed them tight. Bloodied yet stubborn, Black-Fiend Tiger bluffed, “Outnumbered—surrender and live!”

“My soldiers already surrounded your whole stronghold!” Shen Jianchen shot back cold. “Drop your blades or we raze this place flat!”

“Let your guys wait outside,” Qianli Xun murmured at his shoulder. “My fists are itching—let me fight my fill!”

“With you all the way.” Raising his voice, Shen Jianchen called out, “Boys outside—hold the perimeter till Youqi Jushi and I finish warming up!”

Half rattled, Black-Fiend Tiger rallied his mob. “Bluffing—hear how quiet it is? Swarm them in waves till they drop, then hack ’em to pieces!”

Standing back to back, Qianli Xun and Shen Jianchen tore through wave after wave bare-handed, dodging bright steel and sending bandits crashing groaning to the dirt—six full waves dropped before Shen Jianchen asked, “Tired?”

“Just getting warmed up!”

Peeking from outside the gate, Chen Haoyu couldn’t stomach watching Prince Yi and his wife fight in effortless sync another second. Kicking the timber gate open, he charged in yelling, “Wife! I’m here to save you!”

“What are you doing in here?! Get back!” Qianli Xun yelled—too late, as Black-Fiend Tiger snatched Chen Haoyu in a chokehold right beside him.

“Die right now, beauty, or I snap his neck!”

“Neither of us is dying today!”

“Charge in—now!” Shen Jianchen barked.

On cue, Lin Jue and Wen Xin burst through the stockade with Lin’an’s soldiers. Distracted for half a second, Black-Fiend Tiger ate Qianli Xun’s flying fist, stumbled back, snatched a blade to slash at her—Chen Haoyu leaping straight in front shouting “Watch out, Wife!”—before Shen Jianchen tackled the bandit chief down and knocked him cold.

Exhausted from six rounds with the pair, the remaining bandits folded in minutes. Soldiers bound all three hundred fifty brigands, freed ten captive women, and tallied Chen Manor’s stolen loot as Wen Xin hauled Black-Fiend Tiger down to death row.

Passing Zhu Shuzhen on his way out, Lin Jue couldn’t resist a snide dig. “Quit stirring up trouble outside—nearly got killed and dragged Chen Manor down with you!”

Chen Haoyu jumped right in his face. “My wife—not your place to lecture! Without her, you’d never have climbed this hill!”

“Look at you groveling—where’d your old swagger go?”

Shoving Lin Jue toward the gate, Chen Haoyu barked, “Scram down the mountain!” Catching Qianli Xun’s sharp glare, Lin Jue scoffed and walked out.

Shen Jianchen asked softly, “Can you walk down?”

“Scratch like this? Easy.”

Chen Haoyu bowed polite yet firm. “Thank you for saving my wife, Your Highness. We’ll escort you down.”

Reminded he had no place left at her side, Shen Jianchen stepped out to the gate. “Lead on, then.”

Yet one step forward split the deep gash in her sole wide open, blood soaking through the shoe as she winced sideways. Chen Haoyu caught her at once. “Foot’s torn up—climb on my back, I’ll carry you down!”

Glancing back in worry, Shen Jianchen saw Qianli Xun already resting atop Chen Haoyu’s back—the intimate sight piercing him with quiet envy. Looking away as he led the trail down, he asked, “Won’t carrying her down a mountain wear you out, Young Master Chen?”

“Carrying my own wife? Light as a feather!”

“Wait up here—I’ll send soldiers up with a stretcher.”

“No need! No need!”

Walking ahead without looking back, Shen Jianchen still caught every warm murmur behind him—“Put me down if you’re tired.” “Never—need to get that bleeding foot doctored fast!”—until Qianli Xun looped her arms snug around Chen Haoyu’s neck and drifted straight off to sleep in pure relief.

Halfway down, Chen Haoyu’s boot slipped on loose gravel; Shen Jianchen whipped back and caught him steady before either fell. “Watch your footing—trail’s slick.” Catching sight of Qianli Xun’s peaceful sleeping face, Shen Jianchen lingered a fraction too long until Chen Haoyu coughed pointedly—“Folks below are waiting, Your Highness”—and Shen Jianchen turned red-faced back down the trail, ears tuned to every step behind him.

At the trailhead under a high sun, Lin Jue waited with horses and a carriage. “I’ll ride,” Shen Jianchen said, mounting up. “Give them the carriage.”

Walking over, Lin Jue jeered as Chen Haoyu murmured gently trying not to wake his wife, “Call yourself a man? Watch a pro!” Leaning right by her ear, Lin Jue roared, “Zhu Shuzhen! Wake up or I start swinging!”

Half asleep, reflex fired—Qianli Xun swung a groggy fist muttering, “Dare hit me? I’ll beat you flat!” Lin Jue blocked her half-strength swing grinning, and she blinked awake, sliding down on her good foot before Chen Haoyu scooped her clean into his arms and carried her aboard while Lin Jue sneered “Pathetic!” and Shen Jianchen told himself silently, *Long as she’s happy.*

Inside the rolling carriage, Chen Haoyu rested her injured foot on his knee, slipped the shoe off, bound the cut tenderly with a clean handkerchief, and eased the shoe back on.

Pulling her foot back shyly, she asked, “Everyone home safe?”

“Prince Yi got there in time—nobody hurt, only looted. Black-Fiend Tiger came for silver and snatched you for your looks.”

Spared her family knowing Mengrong’s hand in it, Qianli Xun sighed. “Guess being pretty isn’t always a blessing.”

“Best blessing there is!”

“Typical lecher—only sweet on me while I’m young and pretty! Wait till I’m old and wrinkled, you’ll ditch me.”

“When you’re old and wrinkled, I’ll be old and wrinkled right beside you—perfect match!”

“I’m older—I’ll wrinkle first.”

“Three tiny years!”

Deep down, Qianli Xun realized a brand-new truth: she was actually starting to worry about Chen Haoyu ever looking at someone else.
