## Chapter 306: Ye Anya's Cryptic Farewell

Back at Chen Manor, Haoyu dressed her wound before Old Madam Xia, Yao Qingyan, and all the family women crowded into Moxiang Courtyard. Giving Haoyu leave from the emporium to nurse his wife, Old Madam Xia declared Chen Jianglin was donating every stolen treasure recovered from the bandits to charity—lives mattered far more than silver! When Madam Cui noticed the men's robe over Shuzhen's shoulders, Haoyu jumped right in claiming it as his own spare.

After the crowd left, Ye Anya lingered alone with Shuzhen, revealing the heir election was less than a month away—and speaking with eerie, radiant serenity: "I've found a way to ruin Chen Haodong's shot at the inheritance *and* force him to set me free—good for me, good for you, good for Haoyu and the whole Chen family." Ignoring Qianli Xun's urgent plea not to do anything reckless, Anya smiled brightly and glided out.

Later, after Haoyu brought freshly painted mountain-scene tea for Shuzhen and Matron Wei—bickering cheerfully with Kuqiao before heading to Boya Study to crash—Qianli Xun finally broke her silence and confided the whole horrifying truth to Matron Wei: Princess Consort Yi was hiring killers after her, and she feared dragging the innocent Chen household into ruin!

Horrified yet rock-steady, Matron Wei flatly forbade her from ever leaving her home—"I'm riding to Youlin Retreat right now to talk sense into her!"

Over at Youlin Retreat, having stayed awake since midnight waiting to celebrate Shuzhen's corpse only to learn at noon that Black Wind Stronghold was wiped out and Shuzhen lived, Qiao Mengrong fumed bitterly—and when Matron Wei was announced, sent Matron Zou out to field her while she listened behind the screen.
