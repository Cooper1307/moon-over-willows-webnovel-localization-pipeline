## Chapter 306: Ye Anya’s Cryptic Words

Back at Chen Manor, right after Chen Haoyu doctored his wife’s sole, Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan led the women of the house over to Moxiang Courtyard.

Insisting on rising despite her cut foot, Qianli Xun was gently guided back onto the bed by Yao Qingyan while Chen Haoyu lifted her legs up and Kuqiao arranged chairs.

“Foot still hurting?” Old Madam Xia asked with concern.

“Much better with salve on.”

Turning to her grandson, Old Madam Xia ordered, “Skip the silk shop a few days and stay right here tending Shuzhen—I’ll square it with your father.”

“Gladly, Grandmother.”

Old Madam Xia dabbed away tears with a silk handkerchief. “Thank goodness not a single soul in our house was lost—and Prince Yi wiping out Mount Tianmu counts as good karma for the Chens! Father-in-law already decided to donate every copper of the recovered loot for blessings and peace. Silver comes and goes; people are everything.”

Gloom hung over the room, sharper in Qianli Xun’s own chest—knowing she had drawn Mengrong’s blade straight onto Chen Manor.

Breaking the heaviness, Chen Haoyu piped up, “Coming through untouched means good fortune’s waiting—why mope?”

Madam Bao groused, “Untouched?! They swiped my favorite jade bangle and half my jewels!”

Yao Qingyan smoothed it over—“Second Brother will buy you better ones”—before Old Madam Xia clapped her hands briskly. “We came to cheer Shuzhen, not pull long faces! Smiles, everybody!”

Then Madam Cui noticed the garment draped over Shuzhen’s shoulders. “Why are you wearing a man’s outer robe, Shuzhen?”

Still wearing Prince Yi’s robe from the scramble home! Qianli Xun didn’t miss a beat. “Got snatched without a coat, so Chen Haoyu gave me his—taking it off now.”

As Kuqiao slipped it off and eyebrows rose seeing Haoyu already wearing an outer coat, Chen Haoyu backed her smooth: “Swapped into a fresh one right when we got back!”

Satisfied, everyone urged her to rest and filed out—all except Ye Anya.

“Mind giving Shuzhen and me a private minute, Second Brother?”

“Sure—I’ll go whisk tea outside.”

Once Chen Haoyu stepped out, Ye Anya sat on the bed’s edge and clasped Shuzhen’s hand. “I envy you so much.”

“Lying here bandaged and you envy me?”

“Haoyu’s heart belongs to you alone—who wouldn’t envy that?”

Remembering Chen Haodong’s sleazy tryst, Qianli Xun felt an ache for her without knowing what to say.

“Know about the big Chen family event coming up?” Ye Anya asked.

Shaking her head—“What event?”

“The business heir selection—moved up from next year to less than a month away.”

“Big deal for the family, nothing to me.”

“Don’t you want Haoyu chosen?”

“Never crossed my mind—besides, he’s not cut out for it. Everyone expects Eldest Cousin.”

“If you ask me, Chen Haodong doesn’t deserve it.”

Qianli Xun blinked. “You don’t want your own husband to inherit?”

After a beat of silence, Ye Anya’s eyes turned steel. “All I want is out—only getting free of him is nearly impossible. Now I’ve got a plan that keeps him out of the inheritance and forces him to set me free.”

A chill prickled Qianli Xun’s spine; she gripped Ye Anya’s hand tight. “Don’t do anything reckless, Eldest Sister-in-law!”

“Relax—nothing reckless. Something good for me, good for you, good for Haoyu, and good for the whole Chen family.” Rising with a calm, radiant smile that wiped away her usual shadow, Ye Anya glided out with Xiaocui.

Having overheard every word, Kuqiao whispered worriedly while fluffing her miss’s pillows, “What did Eldest Young Madam mean?”

“Forget you ever heard it—breathe not a word to a soul.”

Just as Qianli Xun shut her eyes, Matron Wei stepped in, sitting by the bed to fret over how frantic Young Master and the whole courtyard had been at dawn—right as Chen Haoyu strolled in with two cups of tea painted with distant mountain peaks!

Savoring her cup, Matron Wei marveled at his skill while Kuqiao pouted begging him to teach her foam-painting and Chen Haoyu teased she lacked the finesse—until both empty cups were collected and Haoyu headed back to Boya Study for a nap.

“Look what a gem Young Master is,” Matron Wei praised. “Even maids dare sass him—proves he has zero arrogance!”

“Think he’s good enough to inherit the Chen businesses too?” Qianli Xun tested.

Matron Wei caught the slip in a second. “‘Too’? Who else floated that?”

Brushing it off awkward, Qianli Xun listened as Matron Wei weighed it realistic: “If he’d straightened out years ago and mastered trade, sure—now? Odds are slim unless some earth-shattering twist flips the board, and don’t hold your breath for miracles.”

*An earth-shattering twist—could fragile Eldest Sister-in-law pull that off?*

As Matron Wei rose to leave, Qianli Xun caught her hand. “Stay a minute, Matron—something’s choking me and I need advice.” Taking a deep breath, she spilled the truth: “Princess Consort Yi wants me dead. She hired Black-Fiend Tiger—I got lucky today, yet she’ll come again. I don’t fear dying, only dragging Chen Manor down with me! Should I leave Chen Manor?”

Shocked yet steady, Matron Wei frowned. “Chen Manor is your home now—no leaving! You did nothing wrong; Princess Consort Yi’s narrow heart is to blame. Trust me—I’ll go to Youlin Retreat today and talk to her.”

“Doubt it’ll do any good, Matron...”

“Worth a shot—and drop any thought of leaving! Tell me what message to carry.”

“Tell her to aim straight at me and leave the Chen family out of it.”

Over at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong had lain awake since midnight expecting news of Zhu Shuzhen’s corpse—only to hear at noon via Prince Yi’s messenger that Mount Tianmu had been wiped out, Black-Fiend Tiger sat in irons, and Zhu Shuzhen walked away alive!

Seething in disbelief, she heard a servant announce Matron Wei at the gate—and ordered Xilan to fetch Matron Zou to handle her instead.
