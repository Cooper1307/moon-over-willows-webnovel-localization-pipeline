# 第62章 和离之后提要求
# Chapter 62: Making Demands After the Divorce

---

闫媒婆前脚刚走，林觉立刻跪在地上："岳父大人……不，知府大人，我现在跟淑真离了，身边也没个喜欢的人，能不能向您讨要个人？"

Matchmaker Yan had just left when Lin Jue immediately dropped to his knees: "Father-in-law... no, Prefect. Now that Shuzhen and I have divorced, I don't have anyone I like by my side. Could I ask you for someone?"

---

林子孝批评道："林觉侄儿，你这是做什么？想要媳妇儿，等回去后，林家长辈会帮你寻，怎么能在这里向知府大人讨要？"

Lin Zixiao scolded: "Nephew Lin Jue, what are you doing? If you want a wife, wait until we get back. The Lin family elders will help you find one. How can you ask the Prefect for someone right here?"

---

千里寻本来还有些怜悯林觉，他这么一跪，反而让她觉得讨厌，男儿膝下有黄金，这人说跪就跪，实在不像个男人。

Qianli Xun had felt some pity for Lin Jue, but seeing him kneel like this only turned her pity to disgust. A man's knees are precious as gold—yet this fellow dropped to the ground without a second thought. He really wasn't acting like a man.

---

朱晞颜倒没有生气，毕竟林觉对他一直毕恭毕敬，女儿跟他没处好，这位曾经的女婿与他倒是相处良好。

Zhu Xiyan wasn't angry. After all, Lin Jue had always been respectful to him. His daughter hadn't gotten along with him, but this former son-in-law had actually maintained a good relationship with him.

---

"起来说，不必跪着。"

"Get up and speak. No need to kneel."

---

林觉坚持道："知府大人不答应，我就不起来。"

Lin Jue insisted: "If the Prefect doesn't agree, I won't get up."

---

朱晞颜问："那得看你要什么人，说出来，我衡量衡量才知要不要答应。"

Zhu Xiyan asked: "That depends on who you're asking for. Tell me, and I'll weigh it before deciding whether to agree."

---

林觉说："请知府大人劝说淑真，让她把身边的丫鬟夏果送给我当妾室。"

Lin Jue said: "Please persuade Shuzhen, Prefect. Let her give me her maid Xiaguo as a concubine."

---

朱晞颜笑道："呵呵，不就是讨要个丫鬟，还需跪着？快起来，我同意就是。"

Zhu Xiyan laughed: "Heh, it's just asking for a maid. Why do you need to kneel? Get up. I agree."

---

林觉起身谢道："感谢知府大人，我想今日就把夏果带回去。"

Lin Jue got up and thanked him: "Thank you, Prefect. I'd like to take Xiaguo back with me today."

---

朱晞颜转向女儿："淑真，你大方些，今日就让他把你身边的丫鬟带走。"

Zhu Xiyan turned to his daughter: "Shuzhen, be generous. Let him take your maid away today."

---

千里寻为难道："爹，在林家时，林觉提过同样的要求，可夏果不乐意，我又不能逼她，不如让林觉重找一个，临安的女子多的是，又何必只要夏果？对不对？练兵官？"

Qianli Xun said with difficulty: "Father, when we were at the Lin residence, Lin Jue made the same request. But Xiaguo didn't want to, and I couldn't force her. Why not let Lin Jue find someone else? There are plenty of women in Lin'an. Why insist on only Xiaguo? Right, Military Training Officer?"

---

林觉清楚，在座的所有人绝对不会把夏果当回事，他更加坚定地说："我只要夏果。"

Lin Jue knew that no one present would take Xiaguo seriously. He said more firmly: "I only want Xiaguo."

---

朱晞颜说："来人！"

Zhu Xiyan said: "Someone!"

---

进来一名下人："老爷，有何吩咐？"

A servant came in: "Master, what are your orders?"

---

"去把丫鬟夏果找来，就说我有话要问。"

"Go find the maid Xiaguo. Tell her I have questions for her."

---

下人出去后，韦嬷嬷悄悄说："姑娘还是同意吧？也算给夏果找个好人家。"

After the servant left, Nanny Wei whispered: "Young lady, perhaps you should agree? It would be finding a good home for Xiaguo."

---

千里寻小声回道："需尊重夏果的意思，看她来了，当着这么多人的面会是什么态度。"

Qianli Xun whispered back: "We need to respect Xiaguo's wishes. Let's see what her attitude is when she comes, in front of all these people."

---

夏果腿上的伤昨晚已无碍，正在园子跟苦荞学习认字，听到老爷唤她，急急匆匆跟着传话的下人向朱府厅堂而去。

Xiaguo's leg injury had healed last night. She was in the garden learning characters with Kuqiao when she heard the master was calling for her. She hurriedly followed the messenger servant toward the Zhu residence's main hall.

---

一路上，猜测不出是何缘故。她只知朱家老爷正在厅堂处理小姐与姑爷和离之事，唤她前去，难道是小姐需要人伺候？若是小姐唤她，为何传话的人非要说老爷唤她问话？

Along the way, she couldn't guess the reason. She only knew the Zhu family master was handling the young lady and young master's mutual divorce in the hall. If they were calling her, was it because the young lady needed someone to attend to her? If the young lady was calling, why did the messenger insist it was the master who wanted to question her?

---

夏果长这么大，从来没见过什么大官，昨日在宴会中，看过几眼那些官员，也看过老爷，远远观察，已觉十分威严，竟然要当面向她问话，夏果越想越感到紧张。

Xiaguo had never seen any high officials in her life. At yesterday's banquet, she had caught a few glimpses of those officials, and also seen the master from afar. Even from a distance, he seemed very imposing. Now he was going to question her in person. The more Xiaguo thought about it, the more nervous she became.

---

她带着疑惑和紧张感来到厅堂，一屋子人都看向她，她更加紧张，不由跪下，不敢说一句话。

She arrived at the hall filled with confusion and nervousness. Everyone in the room looked at her, making her even more nervous. She couldn't help but kneel, not daring to say a word.

---

朱晞颜仔细瞧夏果，立刻明白林觉为何非夏果不要，这丫鬟的身段与淑真有几分相似，模样也生得好。

Zhu Xiyan studied Xiaguo carefully and immediately understood why Lin Jue insisted on having Xiaguo. The maid's figure was somewhat similar to Shuzhen's, and she was also quite pretty.

---

"你就是夏果？"

"You are Xiaguo?"

---

"是。"

"Yes."

---

"家住哪里？"

"Where is your home?"

---

"乡下。"

"The countryside."

---

"你既然给朱家姑娘当丫鬟，以后的亲事由朱家说了算，林练兵官看上你，是你的福气，我已答应将你许给他，今日你跟着他去林家吧，当个妾室，也不亏。"

"Since you serve as a maid for the Zhu family's young lady, your future marriage will be decided by the Zhu family. Military Training Officer Lin has taken a liking to you. It's your good fortune. I've already agreed to give you to him. Follow him to the Lin residence today. Being a concubine isn't a bad deal."

---

听到这话，夏果如同五雷轰顶，她心里刚有喜欢的人，就要被无缘无故许配给他人，她根本不乐意，何况还是去当妾室？

Hearing this, Xiaguo felt as if five thunderbolts had struck her down. She had just found someone she liked in her heart, and now she was being married off to someone else for no reason. She wanted no part of it—let alone becoming some man's concubine.

---

刚进厅堂时的紧张感完全消失，向小姐求救："小姐，您是知道的，我不想跟着姑爷，想陪您一辈子。"

The nervousness she felt when first entering the hall completely disappeared. She pleaded with her young lady: "Young lady, you know I don't want to follow the young master. I want to stay with you for life."

---

千里寻对朱晞颜说："爹，我看还是算了，夏果根本不乐意，我不想强人所难。"

Qianli Xun said to Zhu Xiyan: "Father, I think we should forget it. Xiaguo clearly doesn't want to. I don't want to force someone against their will."

---

"就这么定了，让练兵官把她带走，以后不要让我在朱府再见到她。"

"It's settled. Let the Military Training Officer take her away. I don't want to see her at the Zhu residence ever again."

---

朱晞颜厉声说完，起身离开厅堂，朱家族人也都跟着离开。

Zhu Xiyan spoke sternly, then got up and left the hall. The Zhu family clan members all followed him out.

---

林子孝起身对林觉说："侄儿，我们先走了，剩下的事你自己处理吧。"

Lin Zixiao got up and said to Lin Jue: "Nephew, we're leaving first. Handle the rest yourself."

---

林家的族人也都一一离去。

The Lin family clan members also left one by one.

---

向夫人劝道："夏果，老爷都发话，你跟着练兵官走吧，林家也是好人家，亏不了你。"

Madam Xiang persuaded: "Xiaguo, the master has spoken. Follow the Military Training Officer. The Lin family is a good family. You won't be treated badly."

---

夏果哭道："呜呜……！夫人，我不喜欢练兵官，我不想跟着他，更不想当他的妾室。呜呜……！"

Xiaguo cried: "Wuwu... Madam, I don't like the Military Training Officer. I don't want to follow him, and I definitely don't want to be his concubine. Wuwu..."

---

韦嬷嬷说："错过练兵官，你以后不一定能找到比他更好的，还是乖乖跟着他去吧。"

Nanny Wei said: "If you miss the Military Training Officer, you might not find someone better later. Just obediently follow him."

---

夏果哭着对小姐说："小姐，呜呜……！您不是说不会把我送给姑爷吗？为何又变卦？难道您为了自己能和离，不顾及我的幸福！呜呜……！"

Xiaguo cried to her young lady: "Young lady, wuwu... Didn't you say you wouldn't give me to the young master? Why have you changed your mind? Are you so eager to get your own divorce that you don't care about my happiness? Wuwu..."

---

韦嬷嬷厉声批评："夏果！闭上你的嘴！你是丫鬟，哪有资格说姑娘的不是！"

Nanny Wei scolded sharply: "Xiaguo! Shut your mouth! You're just a maid. What right do you have to criticize the young lady?"

---

夏果不敢再言语，只顾伤心地哭着。

Xiaguo didn't dare speak again. She just continued crying sadly.

---

千里寻思忖该如何处理此事，思来想去，关键还在林觉。

Qianli Xun pondered how to handle this matter. After thinking it through, the key was still Lin Jue.

---

林觉安然坐着，已没有刚才的失落，反而一副得意的样子，千里寻很是不爽。

Lin Jue sat there at ease, all trace of dejection gone. Instead, he wore a thoroughly smug expression. Qianli Xun was seething inside.

---

向夫人在此，她不好展示泼妇的一面。

With Madam Xiang present, she couldn't show her shrewish side.

---

"娘，您跟嬷嬷去休息吧，剩下的事，我来处理，放心，定会让大家满意。"

"Mother, you and Nanny go rest. I'll handle the rest. Don't worry, I'll make sure everyone is satisfied."

---

向夫人担心女儿，犹豫要不要离开。韦嬷嬷拉起她："夫人，我们走吧，姑娘既说能处理好，您只管信她。"

Madam Xiang worried about her daughter, hesitating whether to leave. Nanny Wei pulled her up: "Madam, let's go. Since the young lady said she can handle it, just trust her."

---

向夫人才放心与韦嬷嬷离开。

Only then did Madam Xiang feel reassured enough to leave with Nanny Wei.

---

夏果跪在地上哭得更伤心："呜呜……！姑爷为何老盯着我不放？"

Xiaguo knelt on the ground, crying even more sadly: "Wuwu... Why does the young master keep targeting me?"

---

千里寻强调道："以后不要再称呼他姑爷，我已跟他和离。"

Qianli Xun emphasized: "Don't call him 'young master' anymore. I've already divorced him."

---

林觉站起来，绕着夏果转了一圈："谁让你长得好看，又跟你家小姐身段有几分相似，我得不到你家小姐，把你放在身边也不错。朱淑真，知府大人都同意，你干脆些，让我把她带走。"

Lin Jue stood up and walked around Xiaguo in a circle: "Who told you to be pretty? Your figure is somewhat similar to your young lady's. Since I can't have your young lady, having you by my side isn't bad either. Zhu Shuzhen, even the Prefect has agreed. Just be straightforward and let me take her away."

---

林觉的话，千里寻听着极其不舒服，她更不情愿将夏果拱手相让。

Lin Jue's words made Qianli Xun extremely uncomfortable. She was even less willing to hand Xiaguo over.

---

"林觉，今日你我和离本是一桩美事，你偏偏当着众人的面提出这等无理要求，你也看到，夏果不乐意。告诉你，我也不乐意，你要是知趣的话，赶紧离开朱府，否则别怪老娘不客气！"

"Lin Jue, our divorce today was supposed to be a clean break. Yet you insist on making such an unreasonable demand in front of everyone. You've seen that Xiaguo doesn't want to. Let me tell you, I don't want it either. If you know what's good for you, get out of the Zhu residence now. Otherwise, don't blame this lady for losing her temper!"

---

林觉强调道："你爹，知府大人，都已同意，还说以后不想在朱府见到她，难道你没听见？"

Lin Jue emphasized: "Your father, the Prefect, has already agreed. He even said he doesn't want to see her at the Zhu residence anymore. Didn't you hear?"

---

千里寻也强调道："我爹是我爹，我是我，夏果是我的丫鬟，又不是我爹身边的下人，她以后嫁给谁，老娘说了算！夏果，你起来，不必再跪着，跟我走！"

Qianli Xun emphasized right back: "My father is my father, and I am me. Xiaguo is my maid, not my father's servant. Who she marries from now on—I call the shots! Xiaguo, get up. No more kneeling. Come with me!"

---

夏果起身，擦干眼泪，跟着小姐离开朱府厅堂。

Xiaguo got up, wiped her tears, and followed her young lady out of the Zhu residence's main hall.

---

林觉对着朱淑真的背影骂道："走着瞧！"

Lin Jue cursed at Zhu Shuzhen's retreating back: "We'll see about this!"

---

骂完将周围瞧一遍，没有一个人，才甩了甩袖子，放心离开。

After cursing, he looked around. No one was there. Only then did he flick his sleeves and leave with peace of mind.
