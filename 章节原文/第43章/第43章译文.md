# Chapter 43: Helping the Attendant and Watching the Spectacle

Ignoring Lin Jue, Qiao Wenxian fumed inwardly—what on earth had possessed Prefect Zhu to marry his brilliant daughter to such a crude, brutal bully?! His heart ached for Zhu Shuzhen.

Worse, Lin Jue's "bad news" stabbed him with guilt—had his return accidentally wrecked her divorce?!

Rising once more, he walked straight to Lin Jue's table, expression grave and steady: "Lin Jue—what will it take for you to set Youqi Jushi free?"

Startled a split second, Lin Jue barked a cold laugh: "Thought half a word with me was too much—why crawl over here talking?"

Qiao Wenxian stood firm: "What will it take to set Shuzhen free?"

Slamming down his chopsticks, Lin Jue snarled: "Who gave you leave to call her Shuzhen?! Hmph—since you begged for an answer, listen close! Drop on your knees right here before every diner, kowtow three loud times, call me 'Grandpa Lin' three times, and confess aloud you're a two-faced hypocrite lusting after another man's wife—do that, and I'll grant Zhu Shuzhen her mutual divorce!"

Every head across the dining hall snapped round at Lin Jue's roar.

Smug yet secretly sweating—what if the fool actually knelt and forced him to free her?!—Lin Jue stared hard.

Frozen in place, Qiao Wenxian wrestled fiercely inside: kneel, and both pride and his spotless literary name shattered forever! Yet refuse, and how would Shuzhen win freedom? If Shuzhen learned he sacrificed his dignity for her liberty, surely she'd run straight into his arms! Pride and fame were hollow smoke beside Shuzhen!

Mind made up, Qiao Wenxian began bending his knees...

Lin Jue's eyes popped wide in panic.

Inches before his knees hit floorboards, strong hands caught Qiao Wenxian's arms—Shen Jianchen whispering sharp in his ear: "Kneel now, and you prove every dirty accusation he hurled—you'll shred Youqi Jushi's reputation right alongside yours!"

Jolted wide awake, Qiao Wenxian realized his desperate love had blinded him—thank goodness Prince Yi caught him before wrecking Shuzhen's honor!

Exhaling silent relief that he hadn't knelt, Lin Jue sneered: "Then forget me ever letting her go!"

Sure Wenyuan wouldn't do anything foolish now, Shen Jianchen let go and faced Lin Jue cool: "Training Officer—Young Master Qiao here is imperial kin of noble lineage, an honored guest of His Majesty in Bianliang! Skipping proper courtesy is bad enough, yet you bully him in public with degrading demands and drag both his name and Youqi Jushi's through the mud! Youqi Jushi is your own wife—what do you gain smearing her? Truly a tragedy she married you!"

"Imperial kin" triggered an instant alarm in Lin Jue's head! After Zhu Shuzhen left for the countryside, anxious Prefect Zhu might blame him, Lin Jue had called on his father-in-law to say Shuzhen just went out to cool off for a month after the failed divorce.

Not only hadn't Zhu Xiyan blamed him—telling him patience would bring her round—he had dropped big news: the court was dispatching a Military Commissioner to Lin'an, quite possibly an imperial prince!

Studying Shen Jianchen now—commanding bearing, effortless authority, never seen in Lin'an before, arriving straight from Bianliang alongside Qiao Wenxian! Could this guy be that imperial prince?!

Lin Jue's glare melted straight into a smooth smile: "Might Young Master hail from Bianliang?"

Startled by the sudden flip, Shen Jianchen answered flat: "Suppose I do?"

Nine out of ten an imperial prince—play safe! Lin Jue laughed hearty: "Well spoken, Young Master! How could I ever offend imperial kin? Just pulling Young Master Qiao's leg—no hard feelings, Young Master Qiao! To apologize, your entire table is on my tab!"

Spotting the bully footing the bill, Bai Feng jumped right in: "Generous as you are, Nan Shuang and I won't be shy—another bowl of Dongpo pork and a plate of Longjing Shrimp!"

Nan Shuang piled right on: "Add a plate of Hangzhou Chrysanthemum Shredded Chicken!"

"Quiet, you two—depends what the two gentlemen say!" Lin Jue hedged.

"Their word is our word—put it on your tab!" Glowing with sweet satisfaction, Qiao Wenxian returned to his seat beside Prince Yi and resumed eating.

Gritting his teeth yet terrified of offending a possible prince, Lin Jue waved at a waiter: "Bring everything they ordered!" Then he cupped his hands politely toward Shen Jianchen.

Ignoring him, Shen Jianchen murmured low to Wenyuan: "Bet he guessed my identity—why else flip overnight?"

"Good if he did—saved me more public grief."

"Wait—your family IS imperial kin, he's just a local Training Officer! Why'd he treat you like dirt?"

"Imperial kin on paper! My sister Consort Qiao passed ages ago, and you stayed sickly and unfavored—who in Lin'an feared the Qiao house?"

"Makes sense."

Fresh platters landed before Bai Feng and Nan Shuang, who attacked them anew—Juxiang Tower's cooking was divine, just skimpy portions! Round two hit the spot!

Calm settled back over the dining room as satisfied diners left and new guests filtered in, folks drifting up and down from the second-floor rooms.

Glancing over, Shen Jianchen saw Lin Jue eating quietly now, done picking fights.

Beside him, Qiao Wenxian ate distracted, still brooding over Zhu Shuzhen's divorce.

Suddenly a waiter stumbled down the stairs pale and frantic; spotting Qiao Wenxian, he dropped to his knees weeping: "Young Master Qiao! Your word carries weight—please save me! Beg the boss not to fire me! Got a wife, kids, and old mother depending on me—lose this job and we starve! Sob...!"

"Why's the boss firing you?" Qiao Wenxian asked at once.

"One of Young Master Chen's thugs harassed a respectable girl outside and I spoke up two words! Today he came upstairs with Young Master Chen, bumped my tray on purpose dumping dishes on the floor—Young Master Chen chewed out the boss for clumsy service and ordered me fired! Please speak up for me, Young Master Qiao—I can't lose this work! Sob...!"

Hurrying down behind him, the restaurant manager sighed: "Just go quietly, lad—keep you on and Young Master Chen will smash Juxiang Tower flat! Tell you what, I'll pay you three extra months' wages!"

Standing up, Qiao Wenxian offered: "Hold on, Manager—let me go talk to Young Master Chen! He should give me face."

Puzzled and wary, Shen Jianchen murmured: "Sounds like this Chen guy is nastier than Lin Jue—how do you know he'll give you face?"

With a cryptic smile and no explanation, Qiao Wenxian headed upstairs.

"Playing mysterious on me!" Shen Jianchen muttered.

Over at his table, Lin Jue sneered: "Overestimating himself!" Going right back to food and wine, whenever he caught Shen Jianchen glancing his way he raised his cup in a polite toast.

Shen Jianchen lifted his tea bowl in return and drained it clean.

Down on the floor the waiter stayed kneeling while the manager pulled up a chair waiting.

Less than a quarter hour later, Qiao Wenxian strolled back down the stairs: "All sorted, Manager—Young Master Chen dropped it; the waiter keeps his job."

Jumping up, the manager beamed: "Nobody carries pull like Young Master Qiao! Much obliged!"

Spinning toward Qiao Wenxian, the waiter banged his forehead hard on the floor: "Thank you, Young Master Qiao! Thank you!"

"Quit kowtowing—get back to work!" Qiao Wenxian smiled.

Scrambling up amid endless blessings, the waiter hustled back to serving tables.

Genuine gratitude warmed the manager's face: "Hardworking kid, hated letting him go—thanks to you, Young Master Qiao! Let me send one more dish to your table on the house!"

Full already, Qiao Wenxian looked to Prince Yi: "Want anything else, Young Master Shen?"

Shen Jianchen shook his head.

Bai Feng piped right up: "Don't waste the manager's kindness, Young Master—let's get one more!"

Shen Jianchen nodded toward the manager: "Give the extra dish to him."

"What would you like, young brother?" the manager chuckled.

Without blinking: "Another bowl of Dongpo pork!"

Calling toward the kitchen: "One Dongpo pork for the lad!"

Once Qiao Wenxian sat back down, Shen Jianchen pressed: "Spill the secret now!"

"Nothing dramatic—when Young Master Chen was little, he fell in a river. Passing by, I hauled him out with everything I had! He's remembered that debt ever since, stays polite to me, and gives me face."

"How old were you pulling him out?"

"Eight—Chen Haoyu was five."

"Little hero!" Shen Jianchen grinned.

Watching Qiao Wenxian effortlessly rescue the waiter only soured Lin Jue more: Big deal! Cozy with scum like Chen Haoyu just proves birds of a feather!

Right on cue, Chen Haoyu sauntered down from the second floor flanked by two wealthy cronies and three thugs.

Flushed from feasting, Chen Haoyu spotted Qiao Wenxian and strolled over: "We're officially even now, Qiao Wenxian! Debt paid in full—never bring up that old favor again, because next time you get zero face!"

Cool and composed, Qiao Wenxian answered: "Rest easy—I'll never mention childhood favors again. Slate wiped clean."

Turning to leave, Chen Haoyu locked eyes straight onto Lin Jue—and a vicious idea sparked: Hmph! Humiliated twice by your tigress of a wife with nowhere to vent—walk right into my lap, might as well rub salt in YOUR pride to save mine!

Sauntering over to Lin Jue's table, he drawled: "Gotta admire your big heart, Training Officer Lin! Wife living out in the countryside alone and you're chilling over fine lunch at Juxiang Tower?"

Burning inside—everybody in town knows she ran off to the country, wait till she gets back!—Lin Jue kept cool and rose to leave: "Enjoy yourself, Young Master Chen—got duties calling, farewell."

Instantly Chen Haoyu's three thugs stepped up barring Lin Jue's exit. Halting, Lin Jue snapped: "Why block my way, Young Master Chen? I've never crossed you."

"Haven't finished talking—walking off mid-sentence is mighty rude!"

Swallowing irritation, Lin Jue stood stiff: "Speak, Young Master Chen."

Chen Haoyu grinned wicked: "Know WHERE Zhu Shuzhen is staying out in the country? Haha—right inside MY bamboo grove! Know what that means? Means she'd rather camp in my grove than step back into Lin Manor—clearly she's sweet on me! Dated endless young virgins before, never tried a married woman—might just break my fast on Zhu Shuzhen..."

Before Lin Jue even moved, Qiao Wenxian shot half out of his chair ready to swing—only for Shen Jianchen to hold him down: "He's baiting Lin Jue—wait and watch."

"He's trashing Shuzhen's honor in public! How can I sit watching?!"

"Her own husband hasn't swung yet! You jump in now and you don't clear her name—you feed gossip! Sit tight and watch the show!"

Seeing sense in Prince Yi's warning, Qiao Wenxian forced his temper down.

Just then the waiter dropped the fresh bowl of Dongpo pork on the side table and bolted fast sensing trouble.

Long as their masters stayed safe, nothing beat hot Dongpo pork—Bai Feng and Nan Shuang dove straight back in like bottomless pits!

Stung raw by Chen Haoyu's filth—first worrying she met Qiao Wenxian out there, now Chen Haoyu?! Shameless woman!—yet outnumbered three to one, Lin Jue tried pushing past only for the thugs to close tighter.

"Don't run yet—best part's coming!" Chen Haoyu cackled. "Heard Zhu Shuzhen's demanding divorce—know why? Hehe... because she wants to marry ME! Hurry up and cut her loose so I can bring her home!"

Zhus and Chens were in-laws, visiting back and forth constantly—what if they really HAD carried on behind his back?! Blind rage snapped Lin Jue's leash; clenching his fist, he swung straight at Chen Haoyu's face!
