# 第43章 帮助伙计看热闹

乔文仙不愿理会林觉，更想不通知府大人为何会将女儿嫁给一个没有文才，又无情趣，还十分粗暴之人，他更替朱淑真感到不值。

Qiao Wenxian didn't want to pay attention to Lin Jue. He couldn't understand why the Prefect would marry his daughter to a man with no literary talent, no refinement, and such brutish behavior. He felt even more sorry for Zhu Shuzhen.

---

林觉说的坏消息，令乔文仙自责，他以为自己无意中破坏了和离之事。

The bad news Lin Jue mentioned made Qiao Wenxian blame himself. He thought he had inadvertently ruined the mutual divorce.

---

于是又起身，走到林觉的桌前，表情严肃而又郑重地问："林觉，我要怎么做，你才能放了幽栖居士？"

So he stood up again, walked to Lin Jue's table, and asked with a serious and solemn expression: "Lin Jue, what do I need to do for you to let Youqi Jushi go?"

---

林觉先是怔一下，冷笑道："你既然认为与我话不投机半句多，为何又主动找我说话？"

Lin Jue was stunned for a moment, then sneered: "Since you think 'half a sentence is too much when talking to someone at cross purposes,' why are you taking the initiative to talk to me?"

---

乔文仙强调道："我要怎么做，你才能放了淑真？"

Qiao Wenxian emphasized: "What do I need to do for you to let Shuzhen go?"

---

林觉将筷子摔在桌上："淑真也是你叫的？哼！既然你主动找过来，我不妨告诉你，你若当着众人跪在地上，向我磕三个响头，唤我三声林大爷，再当众承认你觊觎别人的娘子，是道貌岸然的伪君子，我就同意与朱淑真和离。"

Lin Jue slammed his chopsticks on the table: "Shuzhen is not for you to call! Hmph! Since you took the initiative to come over, I might as well tell you. If you kneel on the ground in front of everyone, kowtow three times to me, call me 'Grandpa Lin' three times, and then publicly admit that you covet another man's wife and are a sanctimonious hypocrite, I'll agree to a mutual divorce with Zhu Shuzhen."

---

听到林觉声音的食客，都纷纷朝他们看来。

The diners who heard Lin Jue's voice all turned to look at them.

---

林觉说完得意而又充满担忧地盯着乔文仙，一旦跪下，他岂不是要真的放了朱淑真？

After finishing, Lin Jue stared at Qiao Wenxian with both triumph and worry. If he knelt, wouldn't he really have to let Zhu Shuzhen go?

---

乔文仙很为难，站在原地愣了半晌，内心进行着激烈的思想斗争：如若下跪，不仅丢面子，还会丢掉美名；可如果不这么做，淑真又怎能获得自由？如若淑真知道他为她的自由做出牺牲，定会感动地扑进他的怀里，那时他便可以抱得美人归。个人面子和美名乃身外之物，与淑真比起来根本不值一提。

Qiao Wenxian was in a difficult position. He stood there stunned for a long time, engaged in intense mental struggle: If he knelt, he would not only lose face but also his good reputation. But if he didn't do this, how could Shuzhen gain her freedom? If Shuzhen knew he had sacrificed for her freedom, she would definitely be moved and throw herself into his arms. Then he could embrace the beauty. Personal face and reputation were external things, not worth mentioning compared to Shuzhen.

---

乔文仙想明白后，打算屈膝跪下……

After figuring it out, Qiao Wenxian prepared to bend his knee and kneel...

---

他的动作让林觉大吃一惊。

His movement greatly surprised Lin Jue.

---

当文渊的膝盖即将落地时，被申简辰一把扶住，在他耳边小声说："你一旦跪下，岂不是坐实林觉的猜测？幽栖居士的清白恐怕也被你毁掉。"

Just as Wenyuan's knee was about to touch the ground, Shen Jianchen grabbed him and whispered in his ear: "Once you kneel, wouldn't you be confirming Lin Jue's suspicions? Youqi Jushi's reputation would probably be ruined by you."

---

乔文仙醒悟过来，怪他太想朱淑真离开林觉，一时考虑不周，多亏被仪王扶住，没有铸下大错。

Qiao Wenxian came to his senses. He blamed himself for wanting Zhu Shuzhen to leave Lin Jue so much that he hadn't thought things through. Thanks to the prince catching him, he hadn't made a big mistake.

---

乔文仙没跪成，林觉也不由松口气："既如此，别想我会放了她。"

Since Qiao Wenxian didn't kneel, Lin Jue couldn't help but breathe a sigh of relief: "In that case, don't think I'll let her go."

---

文渊不可能再做傻事，申简辰放开他，对林觉说："这位练兵官，你面前的乔公子乃皇亲国戚，家世显赫，在汴梁又是官家的座上宾，你见他不下跪拜见也就罢了，却一再咄咄逼人，甚至提出苛刻要求，还诋毁乔公子与幽栖居士的名声。幽栖居士可是你的夫人，诋毁她，你又能捞到多少好处？唉！幽栖居士嫁给你实在令人惋惜！"

Wenyuan wouldn't do anything stupid again. Shen Jianchen let go of him and said to Lin Jue: "Drill Commander, the Young Master Qiao before you is an imperial relative, from a prominent family, and a guest of the emperor in Bianliang. You didn't even kneel to greet him, but instead kept being aggressive, even making harsh demands and slandering Young Master Qiao and Youqi Jushi's reputations. Youqi Jushi is your wife. How much benefit can you get from slandering her? Sigh! It's truly regrettable that Youqi Jushi married you!"

---

"皇亲国戚"四个字让林觉想起一件事。朱淑真去乡下后，他担心朱晞颜知道此事，对他有看法，便去拜见岳丈大人，告诉他朱淑真是因和离失败去乡下散心，一个月后才回来。

The four characters "imperial relatives" made Lin Jue remember something. After Zhu Shuzhen went to the countryside, he worried that Zhu Xiyan would know about it and have a bad opinion of him, so he went to visit his father-in-law and told him that Zhu Shuzhen had gone to the countryside to relax after the failed mutual divorce and would return in a month.

---

朱晞颜并未责备林觉，只是让他对淑真有些耐心，时间一长，她自然会安心与他过日子。接着告诉他一件大事，朝廷打算派位节度使来临安，搞不好是位皇子。

Zhu Xiyan didn't blame Lin Jue. He just told him to be patient with Shuzhen. Given enough time, she would naturally settle down and live with him. Then he told him a big piece of news: the court planned to send a military commissioner to Lin'an, and it might be an imperial prince.

---

林觉瞧着申简辰，他说话的语气、表情不像普通人，更重要的是他从未见过此人。而乔文仙是从汴梁回来，难道此人也是来自汴梁？如若来自汴梁，搞不好是位皇子。

Lin Jue looked at Shen Jianchen. His tone and expression when speaking weren't like an ordinary person's. More importantly, he had never seen this man before. And Qiao Wenxian had returned from Bianliang. Could this man also be from Bianliang? If he was from Bianliang, he might be an imperial prince.

---

林觉和颜悦色道："公子可来自汴梁？"

Lin Jue said with a pleasant expression: "Is the young gentleman from Bianliang?"

---

林觉瞬间改变，申简辰感到意外："是又如何？"

Lin Jue's sudden change surprised Shen Jianchen: "So what if I am?"

---

林觉心想："十有八九是位皇子，还是谨慎些好。"继续笑道："公子说的是，我怎敢为难皇亲国戚？只是与乔公子开个玩笑，乔公子别介意，为表示歉意，你们那一桌就当我请客。"

Lin Jue thought: "Nine times out of ten, he's an imperial prince. Better be cautious." He continued smiling: "The young gentleman is right. How would I dare to trouble imperial relatives? I was just joking with Young Master Qiao. I hope Young Master Qiao doesn't mind. To show my apology, consider that table on me."

---

白风见欺负乔公子的人大包大揽，岂能放过机会："既然你这么大方，我和南双就不客气，再来一碗东坡肉和一盘龙井虾仁。"

Seeing the person who had bullied Young Master Qiao offering to pay for everything, how could Baifeng miss the opportunity: "Since you're so generous, Nanshuang and I won't be polite. Another bowl of Dongpo Pork and a plate of Longjing Shrimp."

---

南双接着说："再加一盘杭菊鸡丝！"

Nanshuang added: "And another plate of Hangzhou Chrysanthemum Shredded Chicken!"

---

林觉说："你们两个别胡闹，得两位公子说了算。"

Lin Jue said: "You two don't make trouble. It's up to the two young gentlemen."

---

"他们两个的意思就是我们的意思，你按他们说的办吧。"乔文仙说完感到十分解气，与仪王坐回位置，继续享用美食。

"What they two want is what we want. Just do as they say." After Qiao Wenxian finished speaking, he felt very satisfied. He sat back down with the prince and continued enjoying the food.

---

林觉心里虽气，因担心乔文仙身边的公子是位皇子，先压住不满，对一旁的伙计大声说："照他们说的上菜！"然后向申简辰拱手致意。

Although Lin Jue was angry inside, because he was worried that the young gentleman beside Qiao Wenxian was an imperial prince, he suppressed his dissatisfaction first and loudly said to the attendant nearby: "Serve the dishes as they requested!" Then he cupped his hands in salute to Shen Jianchen.

---

申简辰并未理会林觉，小声说："估计他猜出我的身份，要不然态度怎么转变得如此快？"

Shen Jianchen didn't pay attention to Lin Jue and whispered: "I guess he's figured out my identity. Otherwise, why would his attitude change so quickly?"

---

"猜出来也好，省得他当众欺负我。"

"It's good that he figured it out. That saves me from being bullied in public."

---

"乔家怎么说也是皇亲国戚，他就是个练兵官，为何不把你放在眼里？"

"The Qiao family is, after all, imperial relatives. He's just a drill commander. Why doesn't he respect you?"

---

"唉！什么皇亲国戚！乔贵妃，我的亲姐姐早已不在，仪王您又一直卧病在床，不被官家重视，在临安谁还把乔家当回事？"

"Sigh! What imperial relatives! Consort Qiao, my own sister, has long passed away. And you, Prince Yi, have been bedridden all this time, not valued by the emperor. In Lin'an, who still takes the Qiao family seriously?"

---

"原来如此。"

"I see."

---

伙计将新做好的菜摆在白风和南双面前，他们继续开吃。聚香楼的菜什么都好，对二人来讲只是量太少，再吃些才刚刚好。

The attendant placed the newly prepared dishes in front of Baifeng and Nanshuang, and they continued eating. Everything about Juxiang Restaurant's food was good. For the two of them, the portions were just too small. Eating a bit more was just right.

---

一楼的食客恢复平静，有人享用完美食，心满意足离开，时不时有新的食客前来。偶尔会有人从二楼雅间出来，又有人进去。

The diners on the first floor returned to calm. Some enjoyed their meal to their heart's content and left satisfied. New diners came from time to time. Occasionally, someone would come out from the private rooms on the second floor, and others would go in.

---

申简辰瞧向林觉，他此时变得安静，不再寻事。

Shen Jianchen looked at Lin Jue. He had become quiet at this point and was no longer looking for trouble.

---

乔文仙则边吃边担忧朱淑真和离之事。

Qiao Wenxian, meanwhile, ate while worrying about Zhu Shuzhen's mutual divorce.

---

有一位伙计跌跌撞撞从二楼下来，一脸无助，无意中瞧见乔文仙，上前跪下："乔公子，您面子大，帮帮我，给掌柜说一声，别让他辞退我，我家里有妻儿老母要养活，不能丢掉这份差事，呜呜……！"

An attendant stumbled down from the second floor, looking helpless. He happened to see Qiao Wenxian and knelt before him: "Young Master Qiao, you have great influence. Please help me. Tell the manager not to fire me. I have a wife, children, and elderly mother to support at home. I can't lose this job. Wuwu..."

---

乔文仙问："掌柜为何要辞退你？"

Qiao Wenxian asked: "Why does the manager want to fire you?"

---

"陈公子身边有位打手在外欺负良家妇女，只因我多说他两句，今日他随陈公子而来，故意找茬，把我端的菜撞在地上，陈公子认为我服务不周到，把掌柜叫去，将我数落一番，要求掌柜辞退我。求乔公子，帮我说说话，我不能没有这份差事。呜呜……！"

"Young Master Chen has a thug beside him who bullies decent women outside. Just because I said a few words about him, today he came with Young Master Chen and deliberately picked a fight. He knocked the dishes I was carrying to the ground. Young Master Chen thought my service wasn't good enough, called the manager over, scolded me thoroughly, and demanded the manager fire me. Please, Young Master Qiao, put in a good word for me. I can't lose this job. Wuwu..."

---

掌柜从楼上匆忙下来，劝道："还是赶紧走吧，你若不走，陈公子会砸了聚香楼，大不了我多给你三个月工钱。"

The manager hurried down from upstairs and persuaded: "Better leave quickly. If you don't leave, Young Master Chen will smash Juxiang Restaurant. At most, I'll give you three more months' wages."

---

乔文仙主动说："掌柜莫急，我去会会陈公子，他应该会给我面子。"

Qiao Wenxian voluntarily said: "Manager, don't worry. I'll go meet Young Master Chen. He should give me face."

---

申简辰既担心又不解，小声说："这位陈公子听着比林觉还难对付，你怎知他会给你面子？"

Shen Jianchen was both worried and puzzled. He whispered: "This Young Master Chen sounds even harder to deal with than Lin Jue. How do you know he'll give you face?"

---

乔文仙笑笑，没有回答，起身向二楼走去。

Qiao Wenxian smiled, didn't answer, and stood up to walk toward the second floor.

---

申简辰自语道："竟然跟我打哑迷！"

Shen Jianchen muttered to himself: "He's actually keeping me in the dark!"

---

林觉很不屑："自不量力！"然后继续悠闲地吃着菜，喝着酒，不时瞧向申简辰，见他向这边看来，端起酒杯做出碰杯的姿势。

Lin Jue was very disdainful: "Overestimating himself!" Then he continued leisurely eating and drinking, occasionally glancing at Shen Jianchen. When he saw him looking this way, he raised his wine cup in a toasting gesture.

---

申简辰只好端起茶盏，以茶代酒，向对方回礼，然后把茶水一饮而尽。

Shen Jianchen had no choice but to pick up his tea bowl, using tea instead of wine, and returned the salute to the other party, then drank the tea in one gulp.

---

伙计还跪在地上，不愿起来，掌柜找椅子坐下耐心等待。

The attendant was still kneeling on the ground, unwilling to get up. The manager found a chair and sat down to wait patiently.

---

不到一刻钟的时间，乔文仙终于从二楼走下来："掌柜的，陈公子已同意，不再计较此事，伙计可以留下来。"

In less than a quarter of an hour, Qiao Wenxian finally came down from the second floor: "Manager, Young Master Chen has agreed. He won't pursue this matter anymore. The attendant can stay."

---

掌柜的起身赞道："还是乔公子面子大，有劳！"

The manager stood up and praised: "Young Master Qiao really has great influence. Thank you for your trouble!"

---

伙计转向乔文仙，使劲儿磕头："谢谢乔公子！谢谢乔公子！"

The attendant turned to Qiao Wenxian and kowtowed hard: "Thank you, Young Master Qiao! Thank you, Young Master Qiao!"

---

乔文仙说："别再给我磕头，快去忙吧。"

Qiao Wenxian said: "Don't kowtow to me anymore. Hurry up and get back to work."

---

伙计爬起来，一番千恩万谢后去忙传菜之事。

The attendant got up and, after countless thanks, went back to busy work serving dishes.

---

掌柜发自内心表示感谢："这伙计在饭庄干得不错，勤勤恳恳，我也舍不得放他走，多亏乔公子帮忙，我给您再添道菜，以表谢意。"

The manager expressed his heartfelt thanks: "This attendant has been doing well at the restaurant, working diligently. I couldn't bear to let him go either. Thanks to Young Master Qiao's help, I'll add another dish for you to show my gratitude."

---

乔文仙已吃饱，转向仪王："申公子可需要再添道菜？"

Qiao Wenxian was already full. He turned to the prince: "Does Young Master Shen need another dish?"

---

申简辰摇摇头。

Shen Jianchen shook his head.

---

白风急了："公子，掌柜这么大方，不能辜负他，再添道菜吧？"

Baifeng was anxious: "Young Master, the manager is so generous. We can't let him down. How about another dish?"

---

申简辰对掌柜说："把菜添给他。"

Shen Jianchen said to the manager: "Add the dish for him."

---

掌柜问："不知这位小哥想添什么菜？"

The manager asked: "I wonder what dish this young man would like to add?"

---

白风毫不犹豫："再来碗东坡肉！"

Baifeng didn't hesitate: "Another bowl of Dongpo Pork!"

---

掌柜立刻向伙计喊道："给这位小哥来碗东坡肉！"

The manager immediately shouted to the attendant: "Bring this young man a bowl of Dongpo Pork!"

---

乔文仙坐回位置，申简辰问道："现在该告诉我谜底了吧？"

Qiao Wenxian sat back down. Shen Jianchen asked: "Now you should tell me the answer, right?"

---

"其实也没什么，陈公子小时候落水，我经过时，使出九牛二虎之力，把他拉上来，他一直记着这份人情，对我还算客气，也愿意给我面子。"

"Actually, it's nothing much. When Young Master Chen was little, he fell in the water. I happened to pass by and used all my strength to pull him out. He's always remembered this favor. He's been quite polite to me and willing to give me face."

---

"救人时你多大？"

"How old were you when you saved him?"

---

"八岁，陈公子五岁。"

"Eight years old. Young Master Chen was five."

---

申简辰不由赞道："真是英雄出少年！"

Shen Jianchen couldn't help but praise: "Truly, heroes come from the young!"

---

林觉见乔文仙顺利帮了伙计，更是不屑，心里想道："有什么了不起的，跟陈浩宇那种人熟络，只能证明你们是一丘之貉！"

Seeing Qiao Wenxian successfully help the attendant, Lin Jue was even more disdainful. He thought to himself: "What's so great about that? Being familiar with someone like Chen Haoyu only proves you're birds of a feather!"

---

刚想完，余光瞥见陈浩宇从二楼下来，身边跟着两位富家公子，身后则跟着三个打手。

Just as he finished thinking, he caught sight of Chen Haoyu coming down from the second floor out of the corner of his eye, accompanied by two young masters from wealthy families, with three thugs following behind.

---

陈浩宇吃得满面红光，刚到一楼，瞅见乔文仙，走过去说："乔文仙，今儿你的恩情算是还完，以后不准再拿恩情说事，即使你拿出来说，我也不会给你面子。"

Chen Haoyu had eaten until his face was glowing. Just as he reached the first floor, he spotted Qiao Wenxian and walked over to say: "Qiao Wenxian, today your favor is considered repaid. From now on, you're not allowed to bring up the favor anymore. Even if you do, I won't give you face."

---

乔文仙回道："放心，以后乔某不会再拿小时候的恩情说事，此事一笔勾销。"

Qiao Wenxian replied: "Don't worry. From now on, I won't bring up the childhood favor anymore. This matter is settled."

---

陈浩宇转身准备离开，一眼看到林觉，心想："哼！本公子被你那娘子一再羞辱，气没处撒，既然你送上门来，何不拿你撒撒气，也好挽回些面子。"

Chen Haoyu turned to leave when he suddenly saw Lin Jue. He thought: "Hmph! I've been repeatedly humiliated by your wife. I have nowhere to vent my anger. Since you've come to me, why not take it out on you to regain some face?"

---

于是悠闲地迈着步子来到林觉桌前，调侃道："林练兵官这心可真够大的，你那娘子一个人住在乡下也不担心？还有心情到聚香楼用饭？"

So he leisurely walked to Lin Jue's table and teased: "Drill Commander Lin, you really have a big heart. Aren't you worried about your wife living alone in the countryside? You still have the mood to eat at Juxiang Restaurant?"

---

林觉心想，朱淑真到乡下之事，搞得人尽皆知，等她回来，定要与她好好算算这笔账。林觉不想理会陈家的纨绔子弟，吃得差不多，起身告辞："陈公子忙，我还有事，先告辞。"

Lin Jue thought: The matter of Zhu Shuzhen going to the countryside has become common knowledge. When she comes back, I'll definitely settle accounts with her properly. Lin Jue didn't want to deal with the dandy from the Chen family. Having eaten enough, he stood up to take his leave: "Young Master Chen is busy. I still have matters to attend to. I'll take my leave first."

---

陈浩宇身后的三个打手立刻挡在林觉面前，他不得离开，只好站住："陈公子为何让人堵住我？林某从未得罪过你。"

The three thugs behind Chen Haoyu immediately blocked Lin Jue's path. He couldn't leave and had to stop: "Why has Young Master Chen had people block me? I, Lin, have never offended you."

---

陈浩宇说："本公子话还没说完，你就这么离开，岂不是很没礼貌？"

Chen Haoyu said: "I haven't finished speaking yet. You're leaving just like that. Isn't that very rude?"

---

林觉懒得与陈浩宇计较："陈公子请讲。"

Lin Jue was too lazy to argue with Chen Haoyu: "Please continue, Young Master Chen."

---

陈浩宇继续调侃："你知道朱淑真在乡下住在哪里吗？呵呵，她住在本公子的竹林！意味着什么？你知道吗？让本公子告诉你，她宁愿住在我的竹林，也不愿回林府，说明她喜欢本公子。本公子阅女无数，可都是些黄花大姑娘，还没跟有夫之妇相处过，不如拿朱淑真开个荤……"

Chen Haoyu continued teasing: "Do you know where Zhu Shuzhen is staying in the countryside? Hehe, she's staying in my bamboo forest! Do you know what that means? Let me tell you. She'd rather stay in my bamboo forest than return to the Lin residence. That shows she likes me. I've been with countless women, but they were all unmarried girls. I've never been with a married woman before. How about I take a taste with Zhu Shuzhen..."

---

林觉还没怎样，乔文仙已被气坏，想要冲上前与陈浩宇打一架，被申简辰拦住："他是气林觉，听听再说。"

Lin Jue hadn't reacted yet, but Qiao Wenxian was already furious. He wanted to rush forward and fight Chen Haoyu, but was stopped by Shen Jianchen: "He's trying to anger Lin Jue. Let's hear more first."

---

"他的话明显当着众人诋毁淑真，我怎能只顾看热闹？"

"His words are clearly slandering Shuzhen in public. How can I just watch the spectacle?"

---

"幽栖居士的丈夫都没说什么，你冲上去不但不能维护她的名声，更会被人非议，还是老实看热闹吧。"

"Youqi Jushi's husband hasn't said anything. If you rush up there, not only will you fail to protect her reputation, you'll also be criticized. Better just watch the spectacle."

---

乔文仙觉得仪王说得有些道理，火气消去不少。

Qiao Wenxian felt the prince had some point. His anger subsided considerably.

---

新的一碗东坡肉做好，伙计端过来放在桌上，见情势不妙，迅速撤离。

A new bowl of Dongpo Pork was ready. The attendant brought it over and placed it on the table. Seeing the situation was unfavorable, he quickly retreated.

---

只要自家主人无事，别人的事哪有东坡肉吸引人？白风与南双又开启大嚼大咽的模式，他们两个仿佛永远吃不饱似的。

As long as their young masters were safe, how could other people's business be as attractive as Dongpo Pork? Baifeng and Nanshuang started their loud munching and swallowing mode again. The two of them seemed like they could never get full.

---

林觉被陈浩宇的话伤到，本以为朱淑真到乡下是跟乔文仙私会，没想到又出现陈浩宇，真是个不要脸的女人！对方人多势众，他不敢轻易动手，想要离开，三个打手堵得更紧。

Lin Jue was hurt by Chen Haoyu's words. He had thought Zhu Shuzhen going to the countryside was to meet Qiao Wenxian in secret. He didn't expect Chen Haoyu to appear too. What a shameless woman! The other side had more people and power. He didn't dare to act rashly. He wanted to leave, but the three thugs blocked him even more tightly.

---

"别急着走，本公子还有话要说，听说朱淑真跟你闹和离，知道为什么吗？嘿嘿……，是因为她想嫁给本公子，赶紧的，把她放了，好让本公子娶她过门。"

"Don't be in such a hurry to leave. I still have something to say. I heard Zhu Shuzhen is demanding a mutual divorce from you. Do you know why? Hehe... it's because she wants to marry me. Hurry up and let her go so I can marry her."

---

陈家跟朱家是亲家，两家时常会有往来，说不定朱淑真与陈浩宇早有私情，自己竟然不得而知。林觉越想越气，握紧拳头打向陈浩宇……

The Chen family and the Zhu family were in-laws. The two families often had dealings with each other. Perhaps Zhu Shuzhen and Chen Haoyu had an affair long ago, and he hadn't known about it at all. The more Lin Jue thought about it, the angrier he became. He clenched his fist and punched at Chen Haoyu...
