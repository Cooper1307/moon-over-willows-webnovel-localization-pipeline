# 第66章 约会之前的等待
# Chapter 66: The Wait Before the Date

---

乔文仙为仪王做好茶，端着茶盏来到里屋。

Qiao Wenxian made tea for Prince Yi and carried the teacup into the inner room.

---

仪王正躺在床上发呆，他上前做出恭敬有礼的样子，夹着嗓子说：“仪王殿下，奴婢已为您把茶做好，请享用。”

Prince Yi was lying on the bed, lost in thought. Wenxian put on an act of respectful courtesy, pinching his voice to say: "Your Highness Prince Yi, your servant has prepared the tea for you. Please enjoy."

---

文渊学丫鬟的声音，把申简辰逗乐，他“噗嗤”笑出声，坐起来：“小心我把你现在的样子告诉幽栖居士。”

Wenyuan imitating a maid's voice made Shen Jianchen laugh. He burst out laughing and sat up: "Careful, or I'll tell Youqi Jushi what you look like right now."

---

乔文仙把茶盏递到跟前：“还是别，我只是看你不高兴，逗个乐子，又何必告诉她？”

Qiao Wenxian handed him the teacup: "Better not. I was just trying to cheer you up because you looked unhappy. Why tell her?"

---

申简辰接过茶盏，一口气喝下，又把茶盏递到文渊手中。

Shen Jianchen took the teacup, drank it in one gulp, and handed it back to Wenyuan.

---

乔文仙接过茶盏，批评道：“怎么能这么喝茶！要品，品才有滋味。”

Qiao Wenxian took the teacup and chided: “How can you drink tea like that! You have to savor it—only then can you appreciate the flavor.”

---

“不就是一盏茶吗？讲究那么多干嘛？”说着又要躺下。

"It's just a cup of tea. Why so particular?" He was about to lie down again.

---

乔文仙把茶盏随手放在一旁的桌上，迅速过去扶住仪王：“别躺了，跟你说个事。”

Qiao Wenxian casually set the teacup on the nearby table and quickly went over to support Prince Yi: "Stop lying down. Let me tell you something."

---

“什么事？”

"What is it?"

---

“明日我与淑真相约一起游西湖，你来临安还没去西湖逛过，要不带上佳和郡主一起去？”

"Tomorrow Shuzhen and I have a date to tour West Lake. You haven't visited West Lake since coming to Lin'an. Why not bring Princess Jiahe along?"

---

“你们两个约会，我和梦容去，难道要给你们当电灯泡？”

"You two are going on a date. If Mengrong and I go, wouldn't we be third wheels?"

---

“电灯泡？什么东西？”

"A light bulb? What on earth is that?"

---

申简辰才想起这个时代哪有什么“电灯泡”，曾经说顺口的词语，借用到古代，就如同他看到古代文章一样不知所云。

Shen Jianchen then remembered—how could there be “light bulbs” in this era? Words he used casually became just as incomprehensible in ancient times as ancient texts were to him.

---

“意思就是你与幽栖居士约会，我与梦容去了会影响你们。”

“What I mean is—you and Youqi Jushi are going on a date. If Mengrong and I tag along, we'd be intruding on you two.”

---

“怎么会？你和佳和郡主是一对儿，我跟淑真是一对儿，互不干扰。”

"How so? You and Princess Jiahe are a couple, Shuzhen and I are a couple. We wouldn't interfere with each other."

---

申简辰依旧拒绝：“还是算了，我明日有事。”

Shen Jianchen still refused: "Forget it. I have something to do tomorrow."

---

“你能有什么事？作为临安节度使连府衙都懒得去，我真担心哪天官家治你的罪。”

"What could you possibly have to do? As the Military Commissioner of Lin'an, you can't even be bothered to go to the yamen. I'm worried the emperor will punish you one day."

---

“放心吧，我在这里的情况只有临安知府上报官家才会知道，我不理临安政事，朱晞颜高兴着呢，他只会给官家说我的好话，我何罪之有？”

"Don't worry. The only way the emperor learns about me here is if the prefect of Lin'an reports to him. I don't involve myself in Lin'an's affairs, and Zhu Xiyan is happy about it. He'll only say good things about me to the emperor. What crime could I possibly have?"

---

“感情你是走一步想好几步，厉害！既然没什么事，跟我去吧？”

"So you've thought several steps ahead. Impressive! Since you have nothing to do, come with me then?"

---

“刚都说了，我有事，别缠着我，搞得好像你要跟我约会似的，小心幽栖居士嫉妒。”

"I just said I have something to do. Stop pestering me. It's like you want to go on a date with me. Careful, or Youqi Jushi will get jealous."

---

乔文仙放开手：“好吧，既然这次你不去，改日我再带你去游湖。”

Qiao Wenxian let go: "Fine. Since you won't go this time, I'll take you boating another day."

---

乔文仙走后，申简辰继续躺下，因文渊在一旁叽叽喳喳，失落的情绪疏散不少，关注点由神柳转向其他，猛然想起幽栖居士说过的话：“你没事多去勾栏瓦肆听听说书，如果再听到千里寻写的新故事，说明他并未离开。”

After Wenyuan left, Shen Jianchen lay back down. Wenyuan's chatter had dispelled much of his gloom, shifting his focus away from the Divine Willow. He suddenly remembered what Youqi Jushi had said: "When you have time, go to the entertainment quarters and listen to storytelling. If you hear new stories written by Qianli Xun, it means he hasn't left."

---

申简辰自语道：“对，千里寻只是给幽栖居士说神柳要带他离开，并不代表他真的离开，要是神柳能带千里寻离开，为何每次我到神柳跟前，甚至还抱过它，它都没有任何反应，说不定千里寻并未回去，还是让白风继续在西三胡同守着。”

Shen Jianchen said to himself: “Right. Qianli Xun only told Youqi Jushi that the Divine Willow was going to take him away—that doesn't mean he actually left. If the Divine Willow could take Qianli Xun away, then why does it show no reaction every time I go to it, even when I've embraced it? Maybe Qianli Xun hasn't gone back after all. I'll have Bai Feng keep watch at West Third Alley.”

---

申简辰再次充满希望。

Shen Jianchen was filled with hope once again.

---

一大早起来，千里寻用过早饭，开始为第一次约会做准备。

Early in the morning, after breakfast, Qianli Xun began preparing for her first date.

---

苦荞先为小姐做头发，绾了小姐喜欢的发髻。

Kuqiao first did her young lady's hair, styling it in the bun she liked.

---

千里寻亲自挑发饰，既不能太素雅，也不能太浓艳，她挑了一朵海棠花，让苦荞替她装饰在发髻上。

Qianli Xun personally chose her hair ornament—neither too plain nor too gaudy. She picked a begonia flower and had Kuqiao place it in her hair.

---

苦荞又为小姐涂脂抹粉，描眉添妆。完毕后，千里寻照照镜子，很是满意，接着开始挑衣裳。

Then Kuqiao applied powder and rouge to her young lady's face, drew her eyebrows and finished her makeup. When done, Qianli Xun looked in the mirror and was very satisfied. Then she began choosing her outfit.

---

朱府的园子里本没几件衣裳，昨日和离结束后，朱淑真成婚时带到林家的丫鬟婆子全部回来，也把朱淑真所有的家当和衣物带回，搞得园子里的衣柜衣裳充足，够千里寻挑的。

The garden residence at the Zhu household didn't originally have many clothes. But after the mutual divorce was finalized yesterday, all the maids and servant women who had been brought to the Lin family when Zhu Shuzhen married returned, bringing back all of Zhu Shuzhen's belongings and clothes. Now the wardrobe was well-stocked—plenty for Qianli Xun to choose from.

---

她让苦荞将衣裳一件一件展示出来，苦荞便把小姐穿过的和没穿过的一件一件挂出来，几乎挂了个遍，小姐终于挑到一件满意的。

She had Kuqiao display the clothes one by one. Kuqiao hung up every piece her young lady had worn and hadn't worn—nearly all of them—until her young lady finally found one she was satisfied with.

---

是一身襦裙装，上襦为天蓝色，下裙为乳白色，再搭一条乳白的披帛，既显示出姣好的身材，又将皮肤衬托得更加白皙。

It was a ruqun outfit—the upper jacket in sky blue, the lower skirt in creamy white, paired with a creamy white silk drape. It showed off her figure beautifully and made her skin look even fairer.

---

穿好衣裳后，苦荞将铜镜拿在手中，让小姐瞧瞧哪里不好，她再重新为小姐装扮。

After getting dressed, Kuqiao held the bronze mirror so her young lady could check if anything needed fixing, and she would redo the styling.

---

千里寻瞧着镜中配好衣裳和饰品后的容颜，不由在心里夸赞，朱淑真的这副皮囊实在好看，她很满意地点点头。

Looking at her reflection—clothes and accessories all matched—Qianli Xun couldn't help but admire Zhu Shuzhen's beautiful appearance in her heart. She nodded with satisfaction.

---

离约会时间还早，千里寻走到院中，坐在藤椅上。今日的太阳仿佛照顾她的情绪，并不是很强烈，温和中多了一丝惬意，搞得千里寻都想把藤椅从树下搬到阳光下。想归想，她并未实施，感受着有一丝凉意的清晨。

It was still early before the date. Qianli Xun walked into the courtyard and sat in a rattan chair. Today's sun seemed to accommodate her mood—not too intense, gentle and pleasant. It made Qianli Xun want to move the rattan chair from under the tree into the sunlight. But she only thought about it and didn't act on it, enjoying the slightly cool morning air.

---

“苦荞，是不是快立秋了？”

"Kuqiao, is the Beginning of Autumn coming soon?"

---

苦荞在心里推算着时间，脸上不由挂起笑容：“还有七八日才立秋，今年的七夕节在立秋之后。”

Kuqiao calculated the time in her head, a smile appearing on her face: "The Beginning of Autumn is still seven or eight days away. This year's Qixi Festival falls after the Beginning of Autumn."

---

千里寻想起宴会上朱晞颜因七夕快到，才让大家以七夕为题写诗作词：“怎么提到七夕你这么开心？”

Qianli Xun remembered that at the banquet, Zhu Xiyan had everyone write poems on the theme of Qixi because the festival was approaching: "Why are you so happy just mentioning Qixi?"

---

“七夕是非常有趣的节日，到了这一天，可好玩了。”

"Qixi is a very fun festival. When the day comes, it's so much fun."

---

“好玩？”千里寻难以想象，在她所处的现代，从未觉得七夕节有多好玩儿，还不如西方的情人节，“你倒是说说有什么好玩的？”

“Fun？” Qianli Xun found it hard to imagine. In her modern era, she had never found the Qixi Festival particularly fun—it wasn't even as exciting as Western Valentine's Day. “Tell me what's so fun about it？”

---

“到了七夕，大家都会穿上漂亮衣裳，朱府允许下人出去逛街，街上好玩的可多了，回到朱府还要焚香跪拜，望月穿针。我今年都已想好，要在七夕节那日捉一只蜘蛛，把它放在盒子里，第二天看它结出的网是否又圆又正。”苦荞说得兴高采烈。

“On Qixi, everyone puts on beautiful clothes. The Zhu household lets the servants go out to enjoy the streets. There's so much fun to be had on the streets. When we return to the Zhu household, we burn incense, kneel and bow, then thread a needle under the moonlight. I've already planned for this year—I'll catch a spider on Qixi, put it in a box, and the next day check if the web it spins is round and even.” Kuqiao spoke with great excitement.

---

千里寻听着也开心：“果然有趣。”

Qianli Xun was also happy listening: "It does sound interesting."

---

心里牵挂约会一事，她闭上眼睛，耐心等待时间消逝，不知为何，总觉得今日时间过得很慢。

With her mind on the date, she closed her eyes and patiently waited for time to pass. For some reason, today felt like it was dragging on.

---

苦荞问：“小姐，要不要在朱府用了午饭再去？”

Kuqiao asked: "Young lady, would you like to have lunch at the Zhu household before going?"

---

千里寻懒得睁开眼睛：“你去给绿桃说一下，今日午饭我不想吃，不用给我端来。”

Qianli Xun was too lazy to open her eyes: "Go tell Lvtao I don't feel like eating lunch today. No need to bring me any."

---

苦荞很担心：“那怎么行？小姐的身体重要。”

Kuqiao was worried: "How can you do that? Your health is what matters, young lady."

---

“好不容易装扮好，唇脂都涂了，一吃饭就没了，还是不吃的好。”

"I went through all that trouble getting dressed up. I've already applied my lip rouge. If I eat, it'll be ruined. Better to skip lunch."

---

“没关系，唇脂没了，我再给小姐涂上。”

"It's fine. If the lip rouge wears off, I'll reapply it for you, young lady."

---

“不行，现在涂得挺好看，若再涂估计涂不出这效果。”

"No way. It looks great right now. If I reapply, I probably won't get the same effect."

---

苦荞很无奈，这位小姐好像从来没约会过，竟然如此紧张，连午饭都不敢吃：“既如此，我去给绿桃姐姐说一声。”

Kuqiao was helpless. It was as if her young lady had never been on a date before—so nervous she didn't even dare eat lunch: “In that case, I'll go tell Sister Lvtao.”

---

苦荞出了园子，千里寻睁开眼睛，望望天上的太阳，希望它走得快些。可太阳根本体会不到千里寻焦急的心情。她很无奈，继续闭起眼睛想象着第一次约会的情景，边想边笑。

Kuqiao left the garden. Qianli Xun opened her eyes and looked up at the sun, wishing it would move faster. But the sun was indifferent to Qianli Xun's anxious heart. Helpless, she closed her eyes again and imagined her first date, smiling as she daydreamed.

---

苦荞回来，手里拿着个布袋：“小姐，我已给绿桃姐姐说了，她说要向夫人和韦嬷嬷报告后，才能确定要不要同意您不用午饭。”

Kuqiao came back with a cloth bag in her hand: "Young lady, I've told Sister Lvtao. She said she needs to report to Madam and Nanny Wei before confirming whether you can skip lunch."

---

千里寻睁开眼睛：“啊，还要报告长辈？”

Qianli Xun opened her eyes: "What? They need to report to the elders?"

---

苦荞点点头。

Kuqiao nodded.

---

千里寻立刻站起来：“现在赶紧走，否则一会儿我娘或者韦嬷嬷来了，岂不是被拦住？”

Qianli Xun immediately stood up: "Let's leave right now. Otherwise, if my mother or Nanny Wei comes, won't they stop us?"

---

小姐匆忙走出园子，苦荞只好跟上。

Her young lady hurried out of the garden, and Kuqiao had no choice but to follow.

---

两人来到朱府门外，上了马车，千里寻才注意到苦荞手中的布袋：“你拿个袋子做什么？”

The two arrived outside the Zhu household gate and got into the carriage. Only then did Qianli Xun notice the cloth bag in Kuqiao's hand: "Why are you carrying a bag?"

---

苦荞回道：“小姐不吃午饭，我怕您约会时会饿，装些糕点带上。”

Kuqiao replied: "You're skipping lunch, young lady. I was afraid you'd get hungry during your date, so I packed some pastries to bring along."

---

千里寻很是赞赏：“还是你会替我着想。”

Qianli Xun was very appreciative: "You really know how to think of me."

---

马车载着主仆二人前往西湖的方向。

The carriage carried the mistress and maid toward West Lake.
