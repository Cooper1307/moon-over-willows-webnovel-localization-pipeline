## Chapter 279: Haodong Hunts for Scarface by Night

Right after dusk, Chen Haodong had servants pack bedding, salve, and snacks, dismissed both servants and driver for the night, and drove the carriage toward the Judicial Office himself.

Once well clear of Chen Manor, he swung the horse toward Chunxiang Brothel instead. Parking down the street, he strolled past the front doors so he’d be spotted, then climbed back inside to wait.

Before long, Chun Jinchan popped open the carriage door. “Eldest Young Master hasn’t looked for me in ages—don’t tell me we’re fooling around in the carriage tonight?”

“Quit joking, I’m in no mood. Go fetch Scarface for me.”

“How funny—Scarface doesn’t work at Chunxiang Brothel. Why look for him here?”

“He hangs around here all the time—where else would I look?”

“Haven’t you heard? Ever since that tigress reined in your cousin, Scarface showed up once and vanished.”

“He hasn’t been by Yongtai Teahouse to see me either. Any idea where he went?”

“How should I know? Without Young Master Chen bankrolling them, rats like Scarface just drift around the streets.”

“Know where he lives?”

Chun Jinchan shook her head. “We never ask patrons where they live—only heard it’s somewhere on the east side.”

Panic pricked Chen Haodong: if the yamen got to Scarface first, he was sunk.

Chun Jinchan sidled closer. “What’s got you down, Eldest Young Master? Want me to keep you company tonight?”

“Not tonight—tomorrow, same time, same spot.”

She leaned against his chest. “Everything about you is great except you’re too sneaky. When can you stroll right into Chunxiang Brothel openly like your cousin used to?”

“Give me one more year.” He wrapped an arm around her. “If only my wife were half like you.”

“Don’t mention her when we’re together.”

After holding her a moment, he let go. “Head back inside. I still have to hit the Judicial Office.”

Chun Jinchan clearly knew what was up. “Jinlan’s little stunt?”

“Who else?”

“She really packs a punch.”

“Got you to thank too—without you egging her on, she wouldn’t have raised half that storm at Chen Manor.”

“Not just me,” Chun Jinchan said with a smirk. “Madam Jiang did the heavy lifting.”

“Madam Jiang?”

“Chun Jinmo.”

“Oh, her surname’s Jiang. Why’d she help me?”

“She wasn’t helping you—she was helping herself. Married Master Jiang, sure, yet she still pines for Second Young Master. That tigress marched in and hauled him off right under her nose, and he never came back. Bitter as gall, she jumped at using Chun Jinlan to stir up Chen Manor.”

“Makes sense. Slip back in before the madam comes looking and spots my carriage.”

Chun Jinchan threw him a sultry wink and hopped down. For a moment his pulse stirred, until the thought of Scarface doused it cold.

With Scarface drifting around the east quarter—not far from Chunxiang Brothel—he had to comb the streets tonight no matter what. Driving slow through the dark, scanning pedestrians, stalls, and storefronts, he finally struck gold: one of Haoyu’s old sidekicks stood scarfing soup dumplings by a street stand.

Pulling up, Chen Haodong hopped down and hurried over. “Where’s Scarface?”

Swallowing a dumpling whole, the lackey recognized him. “Oh, Eldest Young Master! Long time no see. Scarface is over at the vegetable market.”

Ditching him on the spot, Chen Haodong ducked into the side alley. Lanterns hung on bamboo poles over night vegetable stalls, crowds still shopping.

Scarface swaggered past with two lackeys lugging bamboo baskets, stuffing produce in free of charge while Scarface waved a club in a vendor’s face. “I like taking stuff for free—what’re you gonna do about it? Next time I walk up, load the baskets yourselves, got it?”

The vendors seethed in silence.

“Scarface!” Chen Haodong called. “Why rob them? Whatever vegetables you want, I’ll buy!”

Seeing his meal ticket, Scarface’s eyes crinkled into slits. Lowering his club, he jogged over. “Out after dark looking for me, Eldest Young Master?”

“Sure am. My carriage is right outside—let’s talk there.”

Dropping the shakedown, Scarface followed him out while his two sidekicks wandered off for dumplings.

Inside the carriage, Scarface grinned right away. “Don’t blame me for not stopping by Yongtai Teahouse, Eldest Young Master! I’d love to earn your silver, except you need fresh dirt for that, and since your cousin dropped the boys, I’ve got no news on him.”

“He’s stuck at the silk shop all day—I know his schedule better than you. No need for news.”

Scarface’s grin turned puzzled. “Then what’s the late-night rush?”

“Remember the girl you sold to that Yangzhou brothel?”

Scarface’s smile vanished. “Why bring her up?”

“She’s at Chunxiang Brothel now, calling herself Chun Jinlan.”

Scarface hadn’t set foot in Chunxiang Brothel much since Chen Haoyu reformed—lucky break, or he’d have bumped straight into a hornet’s nest. “Why tell me about her?”

“She stormed Chen Manor demanding Haoyu take her as a concubine or she’d make someone die. My cousin denied everything, so the Judicial Office stepped in and locked them both up. See these bundles? I’m on my way to deliver bedding to Haoyu.”

Scarface tensed. “How’d the Judicial Office get dragged in?”

“Chun Jinlan already named you. Tomorrow they’ll haul you in for questioning.”

Chen Haodong sounded breezy, yet Scarface’s stomach dropped—wait a minute, all he did was sell her! Putting on a casual air, Scarface countered, “How come Eldest Young Master isn’t nervous? You’re the one who defiled her and ordered me to sell her! If they start swinging bamboo paddles at the Judicial Office, I might not hold out and spill the truth!”

Chen Haodong had figured the old affair might resurface, yet he had scrubbed every trace a year ago—never expecting the Judicial Office to wade in and risk exposing him. Everything hinged on locking Scarface down.

“You pocketed every copper from selling her, and you swore not a third soul would ever hear.”

“I kept my word! I’m just scared of taking a beating at the Judicial Office!”

“Do as I say, and not one paddle will touch you.”

“I’m all ears, Eldest Young Master.”

Leaning close, Chen Haodong lowered his voice. “Take the whole rap yourself and confess upfront to the Judicial Investigator. Why would he beat a man who confesses?”

Realizing he was being cast as the fall guy, Scarface balked. “Not a chance! You stay clean while I rot in jail? Rape gets you executed! Asking me to take that rap is asking for my neck! No amount of silver buys my head!”

Expecting that pushback, Chen Haodong had his angle ready. “Who said anything about rape? Claim it was consensual! Hardest thing on earth to disprove—stick to that story and Chun Jinlan can’t touch you.”

Scarface chewed on it. “How much silver are we talking?”

Asking a price meant he was hooked. “Name it.”

“Twenty taels,” Scarface ventured.

*Cheap pauper—never seen real money.* If the man was taking the fall, pay him right. “I’ll give you a hundred taels.”

Scarface slid halfway off the bench. “No kidding?! A hundred taels?!”

“Have I ever gone back on my word?”

Scrambling back up, Scarface asked, “And if I lie in court to pin it on Second Young Master, I still get the hundred?”

“Of course—that’s Plan B, which you try first! Tomorrow in court, swear up and down Haoyu defiled Chun Jinlan and told you to sell her to Yangzhou. Only fall back on Plan A if Plan B fails. I guarantee you walk out whole.”

“Wish you’d led with Plan B instead of scaring me half to death with Plan A!”

“I prefer laying out the worst case first.”

“Leave tomorrow to me, Eldest Young Master—you’re clean as snow.”

Chen Haodong tossed him a pouch of silver. “Bonus spending money upfront.”

Grinning ear to ear, Scarface tucked it away. “Haven’t seen this much silver in ages—thanks on behalf of the boys!”

Chen Haodong turned sharp. “Do your buddies know anything about Chun Jinlan?”

“Remember? I sent them all away that night—they don’t know squat. I’d almost forgotten myself till you brought it up.”

“Good. Not a word to anyone. Off you go.”

Watching Scarface vanish into the dark, Chen Haodong exhaled and drove on to the Judicial Office to play the doting elder cousin.

At the Judicial Office gate, he spotted Qinggua pacing outside, torn over whether to knock. Needing hands to haul the bundles anyway, he reined in. “Qinggua? What are you doing here?”

Qinggua brightened. “Came to keep Young Master company, only I’m scared to go in.”

Hopping down, Chen Haodong said, “Grab the bedding, snacks, and salve from the carriage while I knock.”

“Eldest Young Master thinks of everything!” Grateful his master had such a decent cousin, Qinggua hoisted the bundles as Chen Haodong rapped three times.

A constable poked his head out. “What’s your business here this late?”

Chen Haodong pressed a silver ingot into his palm. “I’m Chen Haoyu’s elder cousin. The family’s worried about him and sent bedding.”

The constable broke into a beam. “A Chen! Prince Yi left orders to let any Chen family member right in. Step inside!”

Following him in with Qinggua loaded behind, Chen Haodong fished for details. “Is Prince Yi here?”

“Princess Consort Yi sent for him—His Highness went back to Youlin Retreat.”

“Chief Constable and Clerk Han?”

“Both asleep.”

“Can you authorize my drop-off?”

“Sure can. Our holding cells are just two converted back rooms—serious felons aren’t kept here, so any constable on duty has the keys.”

Prince Yi wasn’t here—better and better.

Guided to the rear courtyard, they found two padlocked rooms with tiny air vents cut high up on one wall. Unhooking a key from his belt, the constable unlocked one door. “I’m on watch tonight. Young Master Chen’s in here. Drop the stuff off—fifteen minutes tops.”

“Much obliged.”

Chen Haodong stepped in with Qinggua right behind, and the constable shut the door from outside.

Inside stood just a brick kang bed, a table with a flickering oil lamp, and not even a chair. Lying on the kang was Chen Haoyu.

“Young Master!” Qinggua blurted.

Sitting right up, Chen Haoyu blinked. “Brother Dong? What brings you here?”

“Uncle and Aunt were worried sick and sent me with bedding.” Chen Haodong glanced around. “Managing all right in here?”

“I’m fine anywhere—take life as it comes.”

Staring in misery, Qinggua forgot to put the bundles down. Chen Haodong chided him, “Put that stuff down already! Want your master sleeping on bare brick?”

Setting the bedding on the kang, Qinggua peered at his arm. “Does it hurt, Young Master?”

“Not a bit. How’s Young Madam?”

“No idea about Young Madam, I just see you looking rough! Eldest Young Master, could you talk to the guard outside and let me stay here with Young Master?”

“I don’t need a babysitter—go home,” Chen Haoyu refused.

“Let me stay! At least you’ll have someone to talk to!”
