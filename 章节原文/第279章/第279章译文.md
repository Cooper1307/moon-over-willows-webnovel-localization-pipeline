## Chapter 279: Haodong Hunts Down Scarface by Night

At dusk, Chen Haodong packed bedding, food, and ointment into his carriage, dismissed his driver, and steered straight toward Chunxiang Brothel instead of the Judicial Office.

Waiting outside until Chun Jinchan slipped into his carriage, he skipped her flirtation—demanding Scarface's whereabouts. Learning Scarface hadn't shown up since Haoyu went straight and only vaguely lived in the east quarter, Haodong promised Jinchan a rendezvous tomorrow ("Wait one more year till I win the inheritance"), thanked her for helping Jinmo wind up Jinlan, and drove off cruising the east quarter's night markets.

Spotting one of Haoyu's old cronies scarfing soup dumplings on a street corner, he got a hot lead straight to the night vegetable market, where Scarface was shaking down vendors with a club. Pulling him into the carriage, Haodong dropped the bomb: Jinlan was at the Judicial Office and had named Scarface!

"Why aren't *you* sweating, Eldest Young Master?!" Scarface shot back. "*You* raped her and paid *me* to haul her to Yangzhou! One slap of the yamen boards and I'll sing!"

Calm as ice, Haodong laid out his master deal: first, testify tomorrow that *Haoyu* raped Jinlan and ordered her sold; if the court didn't buy it, take the fall himself—claiming it was *consensual* (no death penalty, zero proof!)—all for a whopping **one hundred taels of silver** plus a pouch of pocket cash upfront! Checking that Scarface's lackeys had been sent away that night and knew nothing, Haodong sent him grinning into the dark.

Pulling up at the Judicial Office next, Haodong found Qinggua hesitating outside the gate, handed him the bedding bundle, slipped a silver ingot to the night constable—learning Prince Yi was home at Youlin Retreat and both Han Gu and Wen Xin were asleep—and stepped into the dim temporary lockup where Haoyu lay on a bare brick kang.

Haoyu tried waving Qinggua off, yet Qinggua insisted on staying to keep him company.
