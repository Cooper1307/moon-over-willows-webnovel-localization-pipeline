# 第53章 答应帮忙促和离

---

千里寻像个初恋少女，脸色微红，想起文渊说的话，时不时露出羞涩的笑容。

Qianli Xun was like a young girl in her first love, a faint blush on her cheeks. Thinking of what Wenyuan had said, she couldn't help but smile shyly from time to time.

---

苦荞猜小姐定是喜欢上乔公子，她从心里替小姐感到高兴，也慨叹，如若早些如此那该多好。

Kuqiao guessed that Young Lady must have fallen for Young Master Qiao. She was genuinely happy for her and sighed, wishing things had worked out this way sooner.

---

夏果则十分羡慕，都怪苦荞，硬是把她拉走，如若她也走进观景亭，说不定会被乔公子注意到。忍不住直白地问：“小姐，乔公子该不会是喜欢您？”

Xiaguo, on the other hand, was quite envious. She blamed Kuqiao for dragging her away. If she had also gone into the Sightseeing Pavilion, Young Master Qiao might have noticed her. She couldn't help but ask bluntly: "Young Lady, could it be that Young Master Qiao likes you?"

---

千里寻掩饰道：“别瞎说，乔公子是江南才子，向来爱好品鉴诗词，之所以请我来，不过是对我的一首词不甚理解，专门向我请教而已。”

Qianli Xun covered up: "Don't talk nonsense. Young Master Qiao is a renowned talent from Jiangnan who has always enjoyed appreciating poetry. The reason he invited me was simply because he didn't quite understand one of my ci poems and wanted to ask me about it."

---

夏果回道：“是，我以后不会再瞎说。”心里却想，难怪乔公子会喜欢小姐，只因她会写诗作词。自己的容貌虽不比小姐差，可连字都还没认全，更别说写诗作词。

Xiaguo replied: “Yes, I won't talk nonsense anymore.” But in her heart she thought, no wonder Young Master Qiao likes Young Lady—it's because she can write poetry. Though her looks were no worse than Young Lady's, she hadn't even learned all her characters yet, let alone how to write poetry.

---

“小姐，能不能等我把字认全，您也教我写诗作词？”

"Young Lady, after I've learned all my characters, could you teach me to write poetry too?"

---

千里寻感到为难，写小说她没问题，写宋代的诗词她并不会。得益于脑容量够大，将朱淑真的诗词记得多，有事没事来那么一首，可要教人，实在为难：“你先把字认全，以后再说。”

Qianli Xun found it difficult. Writing novels was no problem for her, but writing Song Dynasty poetry—she couldn't do that. Thanks to her excellent memory, she had memorized most of Zhu Shuzhen's poems and could recite one now and then, but teaching someone else was truly beyond her: “First learn all your characters. We'll talk about it later.”

---

夏果回道：“是，小姐。”

Xiaguo replied: "Yes, Young Lady."

---

苦荞十分不解：“夏果，你我是丫鬟，能学会认字已经不错，写诗作词不是一般人能学会，就别想了。”

Kuqiao was quite puzzled: "Xiaguo, you and I are maidservants. Being able to learn to read is already good enough. Writing poetry isn't something just anyone can learn. Don't even think about it."

---

夏果辩解道：“小姐是女词人，我们是她身边的丫鬟，如果也学会一些，别人见丫鬟都如此厉害，会认为小姐更厉害。”

Xiaguo argued: "Young Lady is a famous woman ci poet. We're her maidservants. If we also learned some, people would think, 'If even the maidservants are this talented, Young Lady must be even more impressive.'"

---

苦荞反对道：“你说得不对，我一直跟着小姐，不会写诗作词，大家照样认为小姐厉害。所以小姐厉害跟丫鬟一点儿关系都没有。”

Kuqiao disagreed: "You've got it wrong. I've been with Young Lady all along and can't write poetry, yet everyone still thinks Young Lady is amazing. So Young Lady's talent has absolutely nothing to do with her maidservants."

---

夏果被苦荞怼得一时无语。

Xiaguo was left speechless by Kuqiao's rebuttal.

---

突入其来的爱情让千里寻感到幸福，她内心的感情终于被填满，也找回久违的踏实与归属感。

This love that came out of nowhere filled Qianli Xun with happiness. Her heart was finally full, and she had found a long-lost sense of security and belonging.

---

从观景亭回来，穿越而来的陌生感荡然无存，这便是爱情力量。千里寻想要在文渊身上体验爱情的美好，想要与文渊步入琴瑟和鸣的婚姻生活。

After returning from the Sightseeing Pavilion, the sense of strangeness from traveling through time had completely vanished—such was the power of love. Qianli Xun wanted to experience the beauty of love with Wenyuan, to enter into a marriage of perfect harmony with him.

---

马车到达林府门口，她急急匆匆进了林府，来到西院，直奔韦嬷嬷居住的院子，吩咐两个丫鬟在外等候，她要与韦嬷嬷说些心里话。一进屋，迅速把门关上，立刻跪在韦嬷嬷面前。

The carriage arrived at the Lin residence. She hurried inside and went straight to the west courtyard. Then she rushed to Aunt Wei's quarters. She ordered the two maidservants to wait outside—she needed to have a heart-to-heart talk with Aunt Wei. As soon as she entered the room, she quickly closed the door and immediately knelt before Aunt Wei.

---

韦嬷嬷感到惊讶，上前去扶：“姑娘怎能跪我？快起来！”

Aunt Wei was surprised and stepped forward to help her up: "How can Young Lady kneel before me? Please, get up!"

---

千里寻执意不起：“求嬷嬷帮我尽快促成和离之事，我一刻也不想再等下去。”

Qianli Xun insisted on staying kneeling: "I beg you, Auntie, to help me expedite the mutual divorce. I don't want to wait another moment."

---

“我也希望姑娘尽快与姑爷和离，可只要姑爷不同意，我们是一点儿办法都没有，你先起来，坐下仔细说。”韦嬷嬷扶起姑娘，拉她坐在榻上。

"I also hope Young Lady can divorce the Young Master as soon as possible. But as long as he doesn't agree, there's nothing we can do. Get up first, sit down, and tell me everything." Aunt Wei helped her up and pulled her to sit on the platform bed.

---

榻上放一张小方桌，千里寻脱掉鞋子，学着韦嬷嬷盘腿而坐。

A small square table sat on the platform bed. Qianli Xun took off her shoes and, imitating Aunt Wei, sat cross-legged.

---

韦嬷嬷吩咐丫鬟为姑娘上茶，待茶放好后，问道：“我听说姑娘昨日还对姑爷说要耗着他，为何今日又如此着急？”

Aunt Wei ordered the maidservant to serve tea. Once the tea was set down, she asked: "I heard that just yesterday Young Lady told the Young Master you'd outlast him. Why are you in such a rush today?"

---

千里寻不愿瞒着韦嬷嬷：“我已有喜欢之人，想尽快结束不如意的婚姻。”

Qianli Xun didn't want to hide anything from Aunt Wei: "I have someone I like. I want to end this unhappy marriage as soon as possible."

---

“可是徐公子？”

"Is it Young Master Xu?"

---

“不是，我已不记得他。”

"No, I don't remember him anymore."

---

“不记得甚好，在我看来徐公子根本配不上姑娘。姑娘出去一趟就有喜欢之人，不知是哪家公子？”

"Forgetting is for the best. In my opinion, Young Master Xu was never worthy of Young Lady anyway. You went out once and already found someone you like. I wonder which young master it is?"

---

“乔家公子乔文仙。”

“Young Master Qiao from the Qiao family—Qiao Wenxian.”

---

“确实不错，我虽没见过乔公子本人，但总听苦荞提起，跟姑娘倒是志同道合，既如此，我定会帮姑娘。要想和离，除了说服姑爷，还有一位关键人物……”

"That's indeed a good match. Though I've never met Young Master Qiao in person, I've often heard Kuqiao mention him. He and Young Lady share the same interests. Since that's the case, I'll definitely help you. But for the divorce to happen, besides persuading the Young Master, there's another key person..."

---

“我爹？”

"My father?"

---

“对！只要朱家老爷同意，姑爷不敢说半个不字。他之所以能耗着，就是仗着他的岳丈大人撑腰。”

"Exactly! As long as Master Zhu agrees, the Young Master won't dare say no. The only reason he can drag his feet is because he has his father-in-law backing him up."

---

千里寻为难道：“可我爹根本不可能同意。”

Qianli Xun said with difficulty: "But my father would never agree to it."

---

“所以得想办法让他同意。”

"That's why we need to find a way to make him agree."

---

“什么办法？”

"What way?"

---

“唉，现在我还想不出来，只能走一步看一步，正好有个机会姑娘必须回朱府一趟。”韦嬷嬷拿出请帖，“这是今日朱府派人送来的，老爷要举行宴会，邀请临安有头有脸的人参加，也邀请你跟姑爷同去，我正好跟着姑娘回去看看夫人，顺便找机会当面与老爷说说和离之事，希望能说服他。”

“Sigh, I can't think of anything right now. We'll have to take it one step at a time. Fortunately, there's an opportunity—Young Lady must return to the Zhu residence.” Aunt Wei took out an invitation. “This was delivered by the Zhu family today. The Master is hosting a banquet and has invited all the big shots in Lin'an. He's also invited Young Lady and the Young Master to attend together. I'll take this chance to go back with Young Lady to see Madam, and find an opportunity to speak with the Master about the divorce face-to-face. I hope to persuade him.”

---

“有劳嬷嬷。”

"Thank you, Auntie."

---

“老爷十分固执，只怕难以说服，大不了我拼了这条老命也要让老爷同意你与姑爷和离。”

"The Master is very stubborn. I'm afraid it won't be easy to persuade him. But if it comes to it, I'll risk my old life to make him agree to the divorce between you and the Young Master."

---

“嬷嬷，千万不要。”

"Auntie, please don't."

---

“姑娘放心，我不会做傻事。”韦嬷嬷暗自决定，如若朱晞颜不同意，她便以性命胁迫。

"Don't worry, Young Lady. I won't do anything foolish." But secretly, Aunt Wei made up her mind: if Zhu Xiyan didn't agree, she would threaten him with her own life.

---

乔文仙回到幽林别居，直奔主屋。

Qiao Wenxian returned to Youlin Villa and headed straight for the main hall.

---

申简辰正在院中教白风练习拳法，见文渊前来，脸上有掩饰不住的笑容，猜到他此次出去密约佳人，定是满载而归。笑道：“文渊有什么喜事？如此开心！”

Shen Jianchen was in the courtyard teaching Bai Feng boxing techniques. Seeing Wenyuan arrive with an unmistakable smile on his face, he guessed that this secret rendezvous with his beloved must have been a complete success. He smiled: "What happy news, Wenyuan? Why so cheerful?"

---

乔文仙拉起仪王走进屋内，白风并不理会他们，继续练习拳法。

Qiao Wenxian pulled Prince Yi into the room. Bai Feng paid them no mind and continued practicing his boxing.

---

进屋后，二人还未坐下，乔文仙已忍不住，兴奋地说：“你肯定想不到，淑真竟然接受我的表白，我一路上都不敢相信这是真的，可的的确确真实发生。”

Once inside, before either of them had sat down, Qiao Wenxian couldn't contain himself and said excitedly: “You'll never guess—Shuzhen actually accepted my confession! I couldn't believe it was true the whole way back, but it really, truly happened.”

---

他一把抱住仪王：“我太激动！怎么办？她竟然答应我！其实我想这样抱着她，可我不敢，只能抱抱你，把你当成她……”

He threw his arms around Prince Yi: "I'm so excited! What do I do? She actually said yes! I wanted to hold her like this, but I didn't dare. I can only hold you instead and pretend you're her..."

---

申简辰觉得十分可笑，扶开文渊：“冷静，冷静，不就是表白得到回应，何须如此激动？”

Shen Jianchen found it quite ridiculous and pushed Wenyuan away: “Calm down, calm down. So your confession got a response—why be this excited？”

---

“你不知道，我等这一天等了多久！”

"You don't know how long I've waited for this day!"

---

“不管你等了多久，都不应该如此激动，要知道你现在喜欢的可是有夫之妇。”

"No matter how long you've waited, you shouldn't be this excited. Remember, the woman you like now is married."

---

乔文仙立刻清醒：“是啊，有婚姻挡在前面，我与她该怎么办？她说让我耐心等待和离的好消息，可我不想等，你说我该怎么办？总不能干着急，得帮她做些什么。”

Qiao Wenxian immediately sobered up: “Right. There's a marriage standing in the way. What should she and I do? She told me to wait patiently for good news about the divorce, but I don't want to wait. What should I do? I can't just sit here worrying—I need to do something to help her.”

---

申简辰郑重地问：“你真的很爱她？”

Shen Jianchen asked solemnly: "Do you really love her that much?"

---

“那还用问！”

"Is that even a question!"

---

“非她不娶？”

"Would you marry no one else?"

---

“当然！”

"Of course!"

---

“既如此，我帮你。”

"In that case, I'll help you."

---

“你帮我？”乔文仙愣了一下，很快醒悟过来，又抱住仪王，“太好了！你是仪王，又是临安节度使，林觉还不得听你的？知府大人也不敢驳你的面子，由你出面，和离之事一定能成。”

"You'll help me?" Qiao Wenxian was stunned for a moment, then quickly realized what this meant and hugged Prince Yi again. "That's wonderful! You're Prince Yi and the Regional Governor of Lin'an. Lin Jue would have to listen to you, wouldn't he? Even Prefect Zhu wouldn't dare refuse your face. With you stepping in, the divorce is as good as done."

---

“别再抱着我，好热。”

"Stop hugging me. It's hot."

---

乔文仙放开仪王：“我现在终于体会到作为皇亲国戚的好处，遇到难处有王爷撑腰，真好！”

Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi: “I finally understand the benefits of being imperial relatives. Having a prince backing you up when you're in trouble—it's truly wonderful！”

---

申简辰叹道：“唉，我是仪王，又是临安节度使，来到临安一件大事没办，倒先帮人离婚，传出去会不会被人笑话？”

Shen Jianchen sighed: "Sigh. I'm Prince Yi and the Regional Governor of Lin'an, yet since arriving in Lin'an I haven't accomplished a single important matter. Instead, I'm helping someone get a divorce first. Won't people laugh if this gets out?"

---

乔文仙说：“你是官家的儿子，要替官家关心黎民百姓。我与淑真均为草民，是你的百姓，百姓之事无小事，你帮我们只会被人竖大拇指夸赞，怎会笑话？”

Qiao Wenxian said: “You are the Emperor's son. You should care for the common people on His Majesty's behalf. Shuzhen and I are both commoners—your people. There are no small matters when it comes to the people. Helping us would only earn you thumbs-up praise, not ridicule.”

---

申简辰说：“有些道理！”

Shen Jianchen said: "That makes sense!"

---

李管家手里拿着请帖来到院中，见白风练习拳法十分起劲，夸道：“真厉害，不愧是仪王的贴身侍卫。我家公子和仪王可在？”

Butler Li arrived in the courtyard holding an invitation. Seeing Bai Feng practicing boxing with great vigor, he praised: “Truly impressive—worthy of being Prince Yi's personal guard. Are our young master and Prince Yi here？”

---

白风边练边回：“在里面说话。”

Bai Feng replied while continuing his practice: "They're inside talking."

---

李管家走到门口，向里说：“仪王，公子，乔府派人送来请帖，知府大人明日举办宴会邀请公子前去。”

Butler Li walked to the doorway and said inside: "Prince Yi, Young Master, the Qiao family has sent an invitation. Prefect Zhu is hosting a banquet tomorrow and invites Young Master to attend."

---

乔文仙到门口接过请帖：“我爹可还说别的？”

Qiao Wenxian went to the door and took the invitation: "Did my father say anything else?"

---

李管家回道：“来人只说请帖之事，其他一概未提。”

Butler Li replied: "The messenger only mentioned the invitation. Nothing else was said."

---

“好的，我知道了，你去忙吧。”乔文仙拿着帖子对仪王说，“你刚说帮我说服知府大人，他的帖子就到，真是天助我也，我预感到此事必成。”

"Very well, I understand. You may go attend to your duties." Qiao Wenxian held the invitation and said to Prince Yi: "You just said you'd help me persuade Prefect Zhu, and his invitation arrives right away. Heaven is truly helping me. I have a feeling this matter will surely succeed."

---

申简辰从文渊手中拿过请帖，打开浏览一遍：“临安的官员也喜欢搞宴会。”

Shen Jianchen took the invitation from Wenyuan's hands, opened it, and glanced through it: "The officials in Lin'an also love hosting banquets."

---

“官家喜欢，大家必然争先效仿，知府大人每年举办一次，对临安有头有脸的人来讲，也算是大事。”

"Since the Emperor enjoys them, everyone naturally competes to follow suit. Prefect Zhu holds one every year. For the prominent people of Lin'an, it's quite a significant event."

---

申简辰心想：“真是上梁不正下梁歪！”

Shen Jianchen thought to himself: "The fish really does stink from the head down!"

---

“林觉与朱淑真可去？”

"Will Lin Jue and Zhu Shuzhen be attending?"

---

“淑真是幽栖居士，名闻江南，知府大人是她的父亲，宴会怎么能少得了她？至于林觉，既然还是知府大人的女婿，应该也会给个面子让他参加。”

“Shuzhen is Youqi Jushi, famous throughout Jiangnan. The Prefect is her father—how could the banquet be complete without her? As for Lin Jue, since he's still the Prefect's son-in-law, they'll likely give him face and let him attend.”

---

“既如此，我不妨趁此宴会帮你解决难题，只是从此仪王的身份就得公开，一想到会有很多公务缠身，我浑身不自在。”

"In that case, I might as well use this banquet to help solve your problem. But from then on, my identity as Prince Yi would have to be revealed. Just thinking about all the official duties that would follow makes me uncomfortable all over."

---

“公开是迟早的事，早些公开也好。先谢过仪王，我会永远记着你这份情。”乔文仙拱手致谢。

"Going public is only a matter of time. It might as well be sooner rather than later. Thank you, Prince Yi. I'll remember this favor forever." Qiao Wenxian cupped his hands in salute.

---

两人又聊一会儿，乔文仙才离开。

They chatted a while longer before Qiao Wenxian took his leave.

---

剩下申简辰独自一人，听着白风在外面练拳的声音，他更加觉得内心孤单，走到门口说：“不必练了，去休息吧。”

Left alone, Shen Jianchen listened to the sound of Bai Feng practicing boxing outside. He felt even more lonely inside. He walked to the doorway and said: "No need to practice anymore. Go rest."

---

白风问：“我能不能去城里逛逛？”

Bai Feng asked: "Can I go into town and look around?"

---

申简辰依然没有放弃寻找千里寻，人既已离开竹林，不知所踪，目前唯一能抓住的机会还是说话艺人那里：“可以，继续到西三胡同守着。”

Shen Jianchen still hadn't given up searching for Qianli Xun. Since she had left the bamboo forest and vanished without a trace, the only lead he could still hold onto was the storytelling artists: "Sure. Continue keeping watch at West Third Alley."

---

“啊！还守着？那个叫千里寻的才子已经不知所踪，王爷为何还要找他？”

"Huh? Still watching? That talented scholar named Qianli Xun has already disappeared without a trace. Why does Your Highness still want to find him?"

---

“不必多问，守着便是。”

"Don't ask so many questions. Just keep watch."

---

“是。”白风摸摸腰包，“王爷，没银子了，能不能再给我些？”

"Yes." Bai Feng patted his money pouch. "Your Highness, I'm out of silver. Could you give me some more?"

---

申简辰掏出二两银子扔给白风，他迅速接住：“谢谢王爷！”一溜烟往出跑。

Shen Jianchen took out two taels of silver and tossed it to Bai Feng, who caught it quickly: "Thank you, Your Highness!" Then he dashed off like a streak.

---

在院门外遇到佳和郡主，打声招呼：“郡主来了，王爷在里面，我先失陪，忙着去逛街。”没等佳和郡主问话，“呲溜”跑掉。

Outside the courtyard gate, he ran into County Princess Jiahe and greeted her: “County Princess, you're here. Prince Yi is inside. Please excuse me—I'm in a hurry to go shopping.” Before County Princess Jiahe could say anything, he whooshed away.

---

喜兰怨道：“这白风，太不懂礼数，也不知王爷为何喜欢他？”

Xilan complained: "That Bai Feng is so rude. I don't know why Prince Yi likes him."

---

乔梦容批评道：“哥哥喜欢自有他的道理，什么时候轮到你瞎议论？”

Qiao Mengrong scolded: "Brother has his reasons for liking him. When did it become your turn to gossip?"

---

喜兰很快承认错误：“郡主批评得是，奴婢以后不会再瞎议论。”

Xilan quickly admitted her mistake: "County Princess is right to scold me. This servant won't gossip anymore."

---

乔梦容进入院内，见哥哥站在门口，似乎想着什么，上前说道：“我刚过来，路上碰见乔公子，他看上去很开心，说你们明日要参加临安知府举办的宴会，不知哥哥可愿意带上我？”

Qiao Mengrong entered the courtyard and saw her brother standing at the doorway, seemingly lost in thought. She stepped forward and said: "I just came over. On the way, I ran into Young Master Qiao. He looked very happy and said you're attending the banquet hosted by the Lin'an Prefect tomorrow. I wonder if Brother would be willing to take me along?"

---

文渊获得爱情的喜悦，搞得申简辰越发思念千里寻。哪怕她穿越成为男子也要找到他，找到后亲眼目睹男儿之身，他才会死心，否则，她在他心里永远是那个阳光靓丽的女子，他又怎能轻易放下，接受眼前温柔的妹妹？

Wenyuan's joy of finding love only made Shen Jianchen miss Qianli Xun even more. Even if she had traveled through time and become a man, he would still find him. Only after seeing his male form with his own eyes would he give up. Otherwise, she would forever remain that radiant, beautiful woman in his heart. How could he easily let go and accept the gentle sister before him?

“既然想去，哥哥带上你，宴会中会见到你喜欢的女词人幽栖居士。”

“Since you want to go, Brother will take you. At the banquet, you'll meet the woman ci poet you admire—Youqi Jushi.”

---

“太好了！哥哥参加宴会难道还要隐瞒身份？”

"That's wonderful! Will Brother still hide his identity at the banquet?"

---

“先瞒着吧，该公开的时候自会公开。”

"Let's keep it hidden for now. When it's time to go public, it will happen naturally."

---

“哥哥想怎样便怎样，我听哥哥的。”

"Whatever Brother wants is fine. I'll listen to Brother."

---

申简辰向院外走去，没有要带佳和郡主的意思，她急切地问：“哥哥要去哪里？”

Shen Jianchen walked toward the courtyard gate with no intention of bringing County Princess Jiahe along. She asked anxiously: "Where is Brother going?"

---

申简辰没有回头：“我想出去走走。”

Shen Jianchen didn't look back: "I want to go for a walk."

---

“我陪哥哥一起。”

"I'll accompany Brother."

---

“那就跟上吧。”

"Then keep up."

---

得到允许，乔梦容跟在哥哥身后。他们走出幽林别居，在林间的小径中穿梭。

Having received permission, Qiao Mengrong followed behind her brother. They left Youlin Villa and wound through the forest paths.

---

乔梦容不知道哥哥要去哪里，又不敢多问，哥哥看上去心情沉重，怕问了他会不高兴。

Qiao Mengrong didn't know where her brother was going and didn't dare ask too many questions. He looked heavy-hearted, and she was afraid asking would upset him.

---

终于穿过密林，来到池塘边，竟然也有荷花绽放，景致还不错。

Finally passing through the dense forest, they arrived at a pond. Lotus flowers were blooming there too—the scenery was quite pleasant.

---

继续尾随哥哥绕着池塘边行走，一直走到对岸的柳树旁。

She continued following her brother around the pond until they reached the willow trees on the opposite bank.

---

乔梦容第一次见如此粗壮的柳树，一树的红绸迎风飘动，有对情侣正在树下抓住一根柳枝一起系着红绸。

It was Qiao Mengrong's first time seeing such a thick, sturdy willow tree. Red silk ribbons fluttered in the wind throughout its branches. A couple was under the tree, holding a willow branch together and tying a red silk ribbon onto it.

---

乔梦容上前问：“你们在做什么？”

Qiao Mengrong stepped forward and asked: "What are you doing?"

---

女子以问作答：“你们也是来向神柳祈求二人的感情？”

The woman answered with a question: "Are you also here to pray to the Sacred Willow for your relationship?"

---

申简辰并未理会，一味盯着神柳发呆，在内心向神柳祈求，让他尽快见到千里寻。

Shen Jianchen paid no attention. He simply stared at the Sacred Willow in a daze, silently praying in his heart to the willow to let him see Qianli Xun as soon as possible.

---

乔梦容没有直接回答，继续问：“这样祈求有用吗？”

Qiao Mengrong didn't answer directly and continued asking: "Does praying like this actually work?"

---

女子回道：“当然有用！不过须到山上的尼姑庵请一条红绸，写下两人的名字，再一起挂在神柳上，爱情才会长长久久，两个相爱的人也才能走到一起。”

The woman replied: "Of course it works! But you need to go to the nunnery on the mountain to get a red silk ribbon, write both your names on it, and then hang it on the Sacred Willow together. Only then will your love last forever, and only then will two people in love be able to be together."

---

乔梦容望望高山：“尼姑庵就在这座山上吗？”

Qiao Mengrong looked up at the tall mountain: "Is the nunnery on this mountain?"

---

女子回道：“是，顺着那条路上去是尼姑庵的后门，只有拿到红绸后才允许从后门下山，你们可以绕着山下走，到山那边，会看到上山的路，顺着路往上走就能看到尼姑庵。”

The woman replied: "Yes. Follow that road up and you'll reach the nunnery's back gate. You're only allowed to come down from the back gate after getting the red silk ribbon. You can walk around the base of the mountain to the other side, where you'll see the path going up. Follow that path up and you'll see the nunnery."

---

“多谢姑娘告知。”

"Thank you for telling us, miss."

---

乔梦容目睹这对情人将红绸挂好，互相牵着手幸福地离开。她很羡慕，什么时候哥哥也能牵起她的手？

Qiao Mengrong watched the couple finish tying the red silk ribbon and leave happily, holding hands. She felt envious. When would Brother take her hand like that?
