## Chapter 295: Meeting Another Historical Poet at the Salon

Juxiang Tower stood closed to ordinary diners beneath a bold *"Dongfang Poetry Salon"* banner, its ground-floor stage flanked by Bai Juyi's couplet: *"At leisure we call refined drinking games exhausting the classics and histories; in wine we hear clear chanting finer than pipes and strings."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

Spotting zero other women yet, Qianli Xun silently blessed bringing Haoyu. As Haofei dashed off to greet friends, Dongfang Chenze, Cao Ban, and An Mingxuan welcomed her warmly, praising Haoyu's devotion and seating them alongside her musician friend Xiangrong—noting Princess Consort Yi was the sole other woman attending, seated beside Prince Yi.

Noticing a crisp green vegetable dish on every table, Haoyu smartly guessed it was wild greens from Lazybones Village promoted by Prefect Zhu.

Just then several senior literati stepped up to greet her—including a distinguished gentleman in his forties introduced as Ye Shaoyun, who quoted a classic line: *"Fallen blossoms have already danced before the wind, and again comes the twilight rain; by dawn half the courtyard lies strewn with fading red..."* [TN: Translator's rendering]

"You're Ye Mengde!" Qianli Xun blurted, thrilled to meet the famous Prefect of Yingchang dropping in incognito after building his Stone Forest Valley villa in Huzhou. Bowing warmly alongside Haoyu, she felt a funny flutter when Haoyu asked if history would remember *him* as her husband—"Maybe!"

Moments later lovely Xiangrong arrived, charmingly teasing Haoyu after he surprisingly quoted Li Bai's *"Her face is seen in flower and her dress in cloud"* [TN: Translated by Xu Yuanchong] to praise the velvet-flower pun (*rong*, 绒) in her name—laughing that rumors of a terrifying tigress couldn't be further from the affectionate couple before her.

Then Zhu Xiyan escorted Prince Yi and radiant Princess Consort Yi inside to thunderous greetings. Opening the floor, Prefect Zhu invited everyone to taste the refreshing Lazybones Village greens—and Dongfang Chenze launched a special opening round: proposing an elegant dish name drawn from classical verse, with Prince Yi casting final judgment.

Spotting Mengrong's cool, half-boastful, half-warning glance across the room, Qianli Xun offered a polite nod—while Haoyu scanned the crowd hunting for Qiao Wenxian.
