## Chapter 313: An Oral Edict in the Judicial Office

Warning Qianli Xun to tell Chen Jianglin to keep the Chen clan ultra-low-profile, Zhu Xiyan sighed that Prince Yi's very success in cleaning up Lin'an had offended His Majesty—his Military Commissioner title wouldn't last long.

Concerned for Shen Jianchen yet bound by marriage and respect for Mengrong, Qianli Xun kept her prayers silent.

Back at the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen sat drinking alone in his yard while Bai Feng tried cheering him up—right as Han Gu announced a richly dressed visitor straight from Bianliang: **Liang Shicheng**, wearing private gentleman's robes with zero escort.

Sending Bai Feng off (to visit Kuqiao, naturally!) to receive an imperial oral edict privately, Shen Jianchen felt acid rise in his throat when the smug eunuch stayed comfortably seated while forcing an imperial prince onto his knees on the stone floor.

"His Majesty bids me remind you," Liang Shicheng droned smoothly, "that making you Military Commissioner of Lin'an was merely a whim over your fine cuju game—don't flatter yourself! Effective today, your Military Commissioner post is revoked. Go back to being an idle prince with food on your plate."

Swallowing rage, Shen Jianchen murmured, "Your son accepts the edict."

Making a show of helping him up, Liang Shicheng lectured further: sidelining His Majesty's beloved Juyang Yuan, Anji Fang, and Louze Yuan to build dirt cottages slapped His Majesty straight in the face! Worse, an imperial prince squatting in a shabby Judicial Investigator's yamen shamed the throne! A new Investigator started tomorrow; Shen Jianchen was ordered back to doing nothing—just like when he was bedridden. Dropping his address at **Liu'an Villa** in north Lin'an, Liang Shicheng swept out.

Walking numbly out into Lin'an's bustling streets without even bidding Han Gu or Wen Xin goodbye, Shen Jianchen felt hollow failure: couldn't hold the woman he loved, couldn't even help ordinary folk under a rotten emperor.

Spotting him from a passing carriage, Qiao Wenxian pulled him into a nearby wineshop over three pots of *Capital Spring*. Sharing warm news of his own two expectant mothers, Wenxian gently urged him to embrace the life handed to him—mournful over how Cao Ban and Ye Anya's bold elopement had cost Anya's younger sisters their marriage prospects, proving every choice carried a price.

Parting ways once Wenxian drank himself under the table, Shen Jianchen wandered out past the city gates to the Divine Willow. Bare of red ribbons under crisp autumn wind and a pale half-moon, the great tree drew every buried ache back to the surface; unwilling to carry gloom home to Mengrong, he leaned against the trunk to clear the wine—and drifted off to sleep on the cold stone.
