第313章 司理院内传口谕
## Chapter 313: An Imperial Mouth-Decree at the Judicial Office

朱晞颜叹道：“我不再是临安知府，往后无法帮衬到陈家。如果没猜错，梁内侍之所以安排李克顶替我的位置，不过是想整顿临安。恐怕以前与我和仪王走得较近的官员都得受到影响，多少会波及陈家。你回去向你公爹转达我的意思，往后能多低调就多低调。”
Zhu Xiyan let out a long sigh. “I’m no longer Prefect of Lin’an, so I won’t be able to shield the Chen family from here on out. If I’m not mistaken, Imperial Attendant Liang installed Li Ke in my place in order to whip Lin’an back into line. Officials who used to stay close to me and Prince Yi are bound to feel the fallout, and some of it will spill onto the Chen household. Go back and pass my warning along to your father-in-law: keep as low a profile as possible.”

陈文心担忧地问：“难道临安的天要变了不成？”
Chen Wenxin asked anxiously, “Is the sky over Lin’an about to change?”

朱晞颜说：“不是临安的天要变，是仪王让临安改头换面，引起官家不满，临安节度使的位置难保啊！”
“It isn’t Lin’an’s sky changing,” Zhu Xiyan said. “Prince Yi turned Lin’an inside out, and it rubbed His Majesty the wrong way. Even his post as Military Commissioner of Lin’an won’t hold much longer.”

千里寻甚是担忧申简辰，他利用仪王的身份在即将没落的时代想为百姓做些事情，还没做多少已被忌惮，早知如此不如像刚来时什么都不做得好。
Qianli Xun felt a pang of worry for Shen Jianchen. Using his title as Prince Yi, he had tried to do some good for ordinary folks in a crumbling dynasty, only to draw suspicion before he had barely begun. Had he known it would come to this, doing nothing like when he first arrived would have saved him all this trouble.

她想去见见申简辰，哪怕陪他聊聊天儿也好。可想起梦容，想到自己的婚姻，千里寻只能选择退缩，在心里默默为他祈祷。
She longed to go see Shen Jianchen, even just to keep him company and talk for a while. Yet thinking of Mengrong and of her own marriage, Qianli Xun shrank back, able only to offer silent prayers for him in her heart.

司理院内，申简辰坐在院子的桌旁独自饮酒，本想大干一番，才有些成效，梁内侍并未向他这位临安节度使打招呼，便找人替代了朱晞颜，显然没将他放在眼里。
Inside the Judicial Office, Shen Jianchen sat alone at a courtyard table, pouring himself drink after drink. He had set out to achieve something meaningful and was just starting to see results—yet Imperial Attendant Liang hadn’t even bothered notifying him, the Military Commissioner of Lin’an, before replacing Zhu Xiyan. Clearly, Liang didn’t consider him worth acknowledging.

若没猜错，很快会有人来找他，恐怕节度使的职位也会就此失去。申简辰慨叹，在帝王昏庸的时代，一位不受宠的皇子连官家身边的一条狗都不如。
Before long, someone would come knocking for him too, and his post as Military Commissioner would vanish as well. Shen Jianchen gave a bitter inward sigh: under an incompetent emperor, an unfavored prince ranked lower than a lapdog at His Majesty’s heels.

王爷将一杯酒喝完，白风上前继续给满上。见王爷心事重重的样子，安慰道：“梁内侍不过是伺候官家的人，官再大能大得过王爷？王爷又何必因他烦恼？”
When he emptied his cup, Bai Feng stepped forward to refill it. Seeing his master heavy with worry, he tried to cheer him up. “Imperial Attendant Liang is just a servant waiting on His Majesty. No matter how high his rank, can a servant outrank an imperial prince? Why lose sleep over the likes of him, Your Highness?”

白风的话提醒了申简辰，对啊，又何必因奸臣还是一名内侍而烦恼？再说他也蹦跶不了几年。
Bai Feng’s words jolted Shen Jianchen awake. Right—why stress himself out over a treacherous official who was nothing more than an attendant? Besides, the man wouldn’t last many more years anyway.

“你说得对，这种人不值得我烦恼。”申简辰端起酒杯一口饮下，动作干脆利落，“我不想喝了，把酒收起来。”
“You’re right. Someone like that isn’t worth my frustration.” Shen Jianchen raised his cup and downed it in one clean gulp. “I’m done drinking. Put the wine away.”

白风正要收起酒壶，韩谷进来报告：“王爷，外面来了一人，看那穿着，非富即贵，自称是从汴梁来的，要面见王爷。”
Bai Feng reached for the wine pot just as Han Gu hurried in to report. “Your Highness, there’s a visitor outside—dressed like someone of wealth and status. He claims he’s come from Bianliang and demands an audience with you.”

申简辰纳闷，他在汴梁认识的人不多，会是谁？
Shen Jianchen frowned. He knew hardly anyone in Bianliang. Who could it be?

“带他来见我。”
“Bring him in.”

“是。”
“Yes, Your Highness.”

韩谷去后没多久，那人独自走进院中。正是梁内侍梁师成，没有穿内侍官服，一身富家老爷的装扮，也没带随从，只身而来。
Before long, a lone figure strolled into the courtyard. It was Imperial Attendant Liang—Liang Shicheng himself—not in eunuch robes, but dressed like a wealthy gentleman, and traveling completely without attendants.

白风心想：“还真邪门，刚与王爷提到他，他便出现。”
Bai Feng thought, *Speak of the devil—we just mentioned him and here he pops up.*

梁师成见到仪王倒也恭敬有礼：“殿下，我大老远从汴梁而来，别的官员没见，先来拜见您。”
Seeing Prince Yi, Liang Shicheng put on a show of polite deference. “Your Highness, I traveled all the way from Bianliang and haven’t called on a single official yet—I came to pay my respects to you first.”

申简辰深知此人来者不善：“梁内侍一路辛苦，坐下说话。”
Knowing full well the man meant trouble, Shen Jianchen said coolly, “It must have been a long journey, Imperial Attendant Liang. Have a seat and talk.”

梁师成在桌边坐下，瞧一眼一旁的白风：“仪王殿下，可否让您身旁的侍卫离开一会儿，官家让我带来口谕，需单独向殿下传达。”
Liang Shicheng sat at the table, then cast a sidelong glance at Bai Feng. “Your Highness, might your guard step outside for a moment? His Majesty entrusted me with a verbal decree to deliver to you in private.”

申简辰对白风说：“你出去玩会儿。”
Shen Jianchen told Bai Feng, “Go amuse yourself for a while.”

白风趁机要求：“我能不能去找苦荞？”
Bai Feng seized the chance. “Could I go see Kuqiao?”

“想去就去吧。”
“Go if you want.”

“谢谢王爷！”
“Thank you, Your Highness!”

白风离开后，申简辰转向梁师成：“官家有什么口谕？尽管传达。”
Once Bai Feng left, Shen Jianchen turned back to Liang Shicheng. “What verbal decree did His Majesty send? Speak.”

“殿下打算坐着听我传达吗？”
“Does Your Highness intend to hear it sitting down?”

梁师成的问题让申简辰意识到这是一个极其讲究尊卑的时代，听官家的口谕必须跪着。
That pointed question reminded Shen Jianchen just how fiercely this era clung to hierarchy: one had to kneel to receive a sovereign’s spoken decree.

他起身，在地上跪下。面前的奸臣却坐着，申简辰心里很不是滋味。常言道男儿膝下有黄金，他连天地都未跪过，却要跪在一位奸臣的面前。
Rising, he dropped to his knees on the ground while the treacherous official remained seated. A sour taste rose in Shen Jianchen’s throat. As the saying went, a man’s knees were made of gold; he had never knelt to Heaven or Earth, yet here he was kneeling before a corrupt eunuch.

申简辰安慰自己，既来到这个时代，需遵循时代法则。他强忍不满，装作平静的样子，等待梁师成开口。
He told himself that living in this age meant playing by its rules. Swallowing his disgust, he kept his face calm and waited for Liang Shicheng to speak.

梁师成见仪王已跪下，心中自是满意
Seeing Prince Yi on his knees, Liang Shicheng smiled with quiet satisfaction.

“官家派我前来告诉仪王，封殿下为临安节度使，不过是因殿下蹴鞠技术高超博得龙颜大悦而已，千万不能太把自己当回事。天下之土莫非王土，率土之滨莫非王臣，即使是小小的临安也容不得他人自作主张。从今日起，收回仪王临安节度使的身份，安心当个不愁吃喝的王爷。”
“His Majesty sent me to tell Prince Yi this: appointing Your Highness as Military Commissioner of Lin’an was merely a whim after your fine cuju play pleased the dragon countenance. Do not take yourself too seriously. Every inch under Heaven belongs to the Sovereign, every person within his borders is his subject—even small Lin’an will not tolerate anyone acting on his own authority. From today onward, your post as Military Commissioner of Lin’an is revoked. Be content as a prince with nothing to worry about beyond food and drink.”

此刻申简辰切身体会到在权势滔天的人面前，哪怕是皇子，命运也会任由拿捏。官家随口一开，职位加身，再一张口，职位立刻收回，他的人生自此回到穿越而来的原点。
In that moment, Shen Jianchen felt firsthand how completely an absolute favorite could crush even an emperor’s son. One casual word from His Majesty granted a post; one casual word stripped it away, flinging his life right back to where it stood when he first transmigrated.

无奈的是，他不能有任何怨言，还要说：“儿臣领旨。”
Worse yet, he couldn’t voice a single complaint and still had to say, “Your son obeys the decree.”

梁师成装模作样站起来，上前扶起仪王：“殿下快起来，我有几句体己话需给殿下说说。”
Putting on a false show of concern, Liang Shicheng stood and reached to help Prince Yi up. “Rise quickly, Your Highness. I also have a few candid words to share with you.”

申简辰心想，什么体己话？不过是警告而已，我倒要听听你会说些什么。
*Candid words?* Shen Jianchen thought. *Just more warnings. Let’s hear what rubbish you’ve got.*

待仪王落座，梁师成也坐下。
Once Prince Yi sat, Liang Shicheng took his seat again.

“仪王千不该万不该到临安后，晾着居养院、安济坊和漏泽园，自行建造什么土屋窑洞容纳流浪之人，还帮着乡下人谋生路，不就是告诉世人官家下令建的那三处一点儿用都没有？无异于当着众人硬生生在官家脸上打了一巴掌，他心里岂能好受？”
“What possessed Your Highness, after arriving in Lin’an, to ignore the Juyang Yuan poorhouse, the Anji Fang charity infirmary, and the Louze Yuan burial ground, and instead build mud huts and cave dwellings for vagrants and help country folk set up trades? Wasn’t that announcing to the whole world that the three institutions His Majesty ordered built are utterly useless? You might as well have slapped His Majesty straight across the face in front of everyone. How could he swallow that?”

申简辰心中不屑：“哼！原来官家的脸面比百姓更重要。”
Shen Jianchen sneered inwardly. *So His Majesty’s face matters more than ordinary people’s lives.*

“先前朱晞颜也不理会那三处，可当汴梁安排官员前来检查时，他会做做样子，脸面上还都过得去，官家也就没跟他计较。可仪王您来以后，大动干戈，甚至要改变临安官员的风气，官家忍无可忍，才让我带来口谕，还吩咐我在临安待一段时日，将府衙的风气扭转回去。”
“Zhu Xiyan ignored those three places too, yet whenever Bianliang sent inspectors, he put on a tidy show so everyone saved face, and His Majesty left him alone. Once Your Highness arrived, though, you kicked up a storm and tried to overhaul the whole official culture of Lin’an. Left with no patience, His Majesty sent me down with this verbal decree—and ordered me to stay in Lin’an for a spell to set the prefecture office back to the way it was.”

申简辰不想回应任何话，昏君的思维异于常人，他作为正常人说任何话都显得多余。
Shen Jianchen didn’t bother answering. An incompetent ruler’s mind ran on warped logic; a sane person wasting breath on it achieved nothing.

“官家还说，临安的官员怎能任由仪王您想干什么就干什么？不好好当节度使，偏偏当什么司理参军？”梁师成将参军住的小院瞧一遍，“我刚进来，已觉参军的府衙够简陋，殿下住的院子同样简陋。您可是官家的儿子，怎能住这种地方？不是给官家脸上抹黑，又是什么？”
“His Majesty also asked how Lin’an’s officials could just let Your Highness do whatever you please. Instead of acting like a proper Military Commissioner, you went off playing Judicial Investigator!” Liang Shicheng swept his eyes over the modest courtyard where the Judicial Investigator lived. “When I stepped in, I already found the Judicial Investigator’s quarters shabby enough, yet Your Highness’s own courtyard is just as bare. You are His Majesty’s own son—how can you live in a dump like this? If that isn’t smearing soot across His Majesty’s face, what is?”

申简辰依旧不说一句话，平静的面色下压着一腔怒气，要不是看在历史迟早会收拾昏君和奸臣，他绝对会将梁师成一拳打成肉饼。
Still Shen Jianchen kept silent, locking a surge of fury behind an unruffled mask. Were it not for knowing history would settle scores with this foolish emperor and his crooked lackey sooner or later, he would have pounded Liang Shicheng into mincemeat right there.

“我按照官家的意思，已选好新的司理参军，明日前来上任，还请仪王让出位置，回去只当个王爷，就当您还病着，什么都别理会。”
“Following His Majesty’s wishes, I’ve already chosen a new Judicial Investigator to take office tomorrow. Please vacate the post, return home, and just be a prince—pretend you’re still sick and stay out of everything.”

梁师成终于站起来，行礼道：“言尽于此，怎么做，殿下应该心知肚明。我一路颠簸而来，甚是辛苦，回别馆休息。仪王若有事，可差人到城北的柳岸别馆找我，告辞。”
Liang Shicheng rose at last and offered a bow. “I’ve said all that needs saying; Your Highness knows very well what to do. The road down here wore me out, so I’ll head back to my lodge to rest. Should Your Highness need anything, send word to Liu’an Villa in the north of the city. Farewell.”

申简辰并未理会。
Shen Jianchen offered no reply.

梁师成走后，他又默默坐了一会儿，才起身走出司理院，连向韩谷和温信道别的心情都没有，步行离开。
After Liang Shicheng left, he sat alone a little longer before standing and walking out of the Judicial Office. Too drained inside even to bid goodbye to Han Gu or Wen Xin, he left on foot.

他在街上一个人走了许久，想着喧嚣的街市会让自己心情好一些，可街市的热闹更令他觉出内心的落寞。
He wandered the streets alone for a long time, hoping the bustle of the market would lift his spirits, yet the noisy crowd only deepened the hollow ache inside him.

本是为寻找千里寻来到临安，人找着，却得不到，便想着不能白来一趟，不如做些有意义的事，既可打发时间，亦可不辜负穿越而来的身份。怎奈在一个颠倒是非的时代，想做些事难之又难。
He had come to Lin’an looking for Qianli Xun. Finding her yet being unable to have her, he had figured he might as well do some real good—both to pass the time and to make his transmigration count for something. Yet in an upside-down era where right meant wrong, trying to get anything done felt like pushing uphill through mud.

他忽然觉得自己很失败，没有得到想要的人，还被官家的口谕逼得什么事都做不成，难道今后只能当一个闲散的王爷？再跟梦容生一堆孩子？
All at once he felt like an utter failure. He hadn’t won the woman he wanted, and now His Majesty’s decree blocked him from doing anything useful. Was he really stuck playing a leisurely prince from here on out—and raising a houseful of children with Mengrong?

若身边的人是心中所爱，申简辰会觉得闲散的日子肯定是幸福的。可身边的人并不是他爱的人，什么都不做就这样闲散下去，日子会过得毫无乐趣。
If the person beside him were the woman he loved, a quiet, carefree life would have been bliss. With someone he didn’t love, drifting through empty days with nothing to do sounded lifeless beyond words.

不知走了多久，听到文渊的声音：“怎么一个人在街上闲逛？”
He had no idea how far he’d walked when Wenyuan’s voice called out, “Why are you strolling down the street all by yourself?”

申简辰站住，顺着声音瞧去，文渊坐在马车里，探出个脑袋。
Shen Jianchen halted and looked toward the voice. Wenyuan was leaning his head out of a carriage window.

“你一个人？”
“Are you alone?”

“一个人。”
“Alone.”

“能不能陪我喝两杯？”
“Care to join me for a couple of drinks?”

“有什么不可以的？”乔文仙走下马车，“不远处有家酒肆，我们去那里坐坐。”
“Why not?” Qiao Wenxian climbed down from the carriage. “There’s a wine shop just ahead. Let’s sit inside.”

两人来到酒肆，选择了一处靠窗户的位置，要了壶皇都春，各自满上。
They picked a table by the window, ordered a pot of Capital Spring, and poured each other full cups.

乔文仙问：“听说知府大人换人了，可是真的？”
Qiao Wenxian asked, “I heard the Prefect was replaced. Is it true?”

“没错，都是因为我。”申简辰落寞地端起酒杯一饮而尽。
“It is—and all because of me.” Shen Jianchen picked up his cup gloomily and drained it.

“怎么能怪你？我倒觉得自从你来后，临安的官府才变得无比正常。”
“How could anyone blame you? If you ask me, Lin’an’s yamen only started functioning properly after you stepped in.”

申简辰苦笑一下：“一个不正常的时代想做些正常的事太难，从今往后，我什么都不做，只做一个闲散的王爷，回到刚来临安时的状态。只是我的心态已不同，那时还有希望，现在连希望都没有。”
Shen Jianchen gave a wry smile. “Trying to do normal, decent work in an abnormal age is near impossible. From now on, I’m done trying. I’ll just be a leisurely prince, right back where I started when I first reached Lin’an—except my state of mind is different now. Back then, I still had hope. Now there’s none left.”

乔文仙安慰道：“怎么没有？有仪王妃陪着，你们再有个孩子，孩子就是希望。”
Qiao Wenxian tried to comfort him. “How can you say you have none? You have Princess Consort Yi at your side. Once you have a child together, that child becomes your hope.”

提到孩子，申简辰想起乔文仙快要当爹的事：“听说你的一妻一妾已有身孕，不久要当爹，心情如何？”
At the mention of children, Shen Jianchen remembered Qiao Wenxian was about to become a father himself. “I heard both your wife and your concubine are expecting. You’ll be a father soon—how does that feel?”

“心情自然与以往不同，充满期待。”
“Different from before, of course. Full of anticipation.”

申简辰瞧着文渊脸上的笑容，想起他当初爱得死去活来的样子，恍如隔世：“真羡慕你，娶了不爱的人，还能如此享受。”
Looking at the easy smile on Wenyuan’s face, Shen Jianchen thought back to how lovesick the man had once been—it felt like another lifetime. “I envy you. You married someone you didn’t love and still manage to enjoy life like this.”

乔文仙饮完一杯酒，给仪王满上：“人生哪有圆满？想通了自然会接受老天送到身边的人和物，若想不通，只能是自己折磨自己。你要像我一样，既然无法拒绝，接受了，抱着享受生活的姿态，人不要总是自己为难自己。”
Qiao Wenxian drained a cup and refilled the prince’s. “Who ever gets everything they want in life? Once you make peace with it, you accept whatever Heaven puts in your hands. Refuse to make peace, and you only torture yourself. Be like me: if you can’t refuse what’s dealt to you, take it and choose to enjoy what you have. Never make yourself your own worst enemy.”

“说得有道理。”申简辰端起酒杯，“往后我既不是节度使，也不再去司理院，空闲时间很多，一定要多来幽林别居坐坐，陪我说说话，最好把三位公子叫上。”
“Fair enough.” Shen Jianchen lifted his cup. “Now that I’m neither Military Commissioner nor going back to the Judicial Office, I’ll have time to burn. Make sure you drop by Youlin Retreat often to keep me company—and bring the other three young masters along.”

两人干了一杯，乔文仙说：“你既变得闲散，我自然会时常去打扰，只是三位公子已变成两位，往日景况已不复。”
They clinked and emptied their cups. Qiao Wenxian sighed, “Since you’re at leisure again, of course I’ll come bother you. Only those three young masters are down to two now—things aren’t what they used to be.”

申简辰想起曹班与心爱之人离开临安：“我竟然忘了曹公子的事。还别说，多少羡慕他，敢于跨出你我不敢跨出的一步，带着心爱的人远走他乡。”
Shen Jianchen recalled Cao Ban eloping with the woman he loved. “I’d almost forgotten about Young Master Cao. You know, I envy him a little—having the guts to take the step neither you nor I dared take, running off to start over with the woman he loves.”

“仪王不知，他之所以那么大胆，无非是家中兄弟多，不用担心无人照料父母。我如果也像他一样有好几个兄弟，当初肯定比他勇敢。”
“You don’t know the half of it, Your Highness. He only dared pull something that bold because he has plenty of brothers at home to look after his parents. If I had brothers like him, I’d have been braver than he was back then.”

申简辰想到自己，那时的他是想带着千里寻离开，她却不同意，坚决步入婚姻：“我更佩服叶家姑娘，不仅逼得陈浩东休了她，还放下所有，毅然决然跟着曹公子离开。”
Shen Jianchen thought of his own past attempt—he had tried to sweep Qianli Xun away too, yet she had turned him down flat and chosen marriage. “I admire Miss Ye even more. Not only did she push Chen Haodong into divorcing her, she walked away from everything and followed Young Master Cao without looking back.”

“的确值得人佩服，可也付出代价。为了几个妹妹的名声，她与叶家断绝关系。即便如此，还是影响到妹妹们，听说本来上门提亲的人不少，自从发生这件事，几乎没有人上门提亲。所以做任何选择有好有坏，就看选择的人是怎么想的，是想顾全大局还是只顾及个人幸福。”
“Admirable, yes—though she paid a price for it. To shield her younger sisters’ reputations, she severed all ties with the Ye family. Even so, it hurt her sisters’ prospects; plenty of matchmakers used to call at the Ye gate, and since that happened, almost nobody comes proposing. Every choice cuts both ways—it all depends on whether someone thinks about the bigger picture or only personal happiness.”

乔文仙说着，脸上露出落寞的神色：“她选择顾全大局，逼得我不得不放弃个人幸福。她现在跟陈浩宇在一起看上去蛮好，我在外人眼里也生活圆满，证明她当初的选择没有错。”
A shadow of sadness crossed Qiao Wenxian’s face as he spoke. “She chose the bigger picture, which forced me to let go of my own happiness. She seems content enough with Chen Haoyu now, and to outsiders my own household looks complete. I suppose that proves her choice wasn’t wrong.”

申简辰想到自己，人生的选择往往也存在着许多无奈，有时不必选，老天已替人选好，就像架在他头上的圣旨。
Shen Jianchen thought of himself. Often enough, a person didn’t even get to make the choice—Heaven shoved it onto their lap, just like an imperial edict hanging over his neck.

乔文仙举起重新添满的酒杯：“什么都别想，既已踏上选择的道路，勇敢走下去，哪怕是一条错误的道路，也得走下去，因为对你我而言没有回头路。”
Qiao Wenxian raised his freshly poured cup. “Stop overthinking it. Once you’ve stepped onto a road, walk it through with your chin up—even if it turns out to be the wrong road, you still have to keep walking, because for men like you and me, there’s no turning back.”

两人聊了许久，喝了整整三壶皇都春，文渊已醉得不省人事，申简辰才稍微有些酒意。
They talked on and on, polishing off three whole pots of Capital Spring. By the time they parted, Wenyuan was dead drunk while Shen Jianchen felt only a mild buzz.

二人分别后，申简辰步行出了城，来到神柳下。因一直在司理院忙，他好久没有来看望神柳。
After splitting up, Shen Jianchen strolled out through the city gate toward the Divine Willow. Tied up at the Judicial Office, he hadn’t come to visit the tree in ages.

神柳上的红绸已被全部取下，它看上去轻松自在。已入秋，神柳的叶子正在枯萎。一阵风吹来，散落下许多枯萎的柳叶。
Every red silk ribbon had been stripped from its branches, leaving the willow looking light and unburdened. Autumn had arrived, and its leaves were withering; a gust swept past, shaking loose a shower of dry foliage.

秋风拂面，几片神柳的叶子从申简辰的面颊摩擦而过，落在地上即将干枯的草丛中。
The crisp breeze brushed Shen Jianchen’s cheeks with drifting willow leaves that settled into the yellowing grass below.

已过中秋，月亮没有那么圆满，一轮半月慢慢爬上高空，缓缓地想要穿过神柳的枝头。
Mid-Autumn had come and gone, so the moon hung half-full, climbing high into the night sky as if inching slowly through the willow’s boughs.

来到神柳下，申简辰更加确定对千里寻的爱从未减退过，因得不到反而更加浓烈。
Standing beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen knew with absolute certainty that his love for Qianli Xun had never faded—being denied her only made it burn fiercer.

带着这样的心情不好回幽林别居见梦容，若流露太多，会让她的心情变差，再做出不理智的行为来，受伤的只能是他心爱之人。
Carrying a heart like this, he had no business facing Mengrong back at Youlin Retreat. If too much showed on his face, her mood would sour, she might do something reckless again, and the one who’d end up bleeding would be the woman he loved.

申简辰决定在神柳下多坐一会儿，既可散去酒气，亦可整理心情。
Shen Jianchen decided to linger under the willow a little longer—both to let the wine wear off and to pull himself together.

他走到石头上坐下，背靠在神柳粗壮的树干上，想着个人的心事，醉意渐渐袭来，他竟然不知不觉睡去……
Walking over to a boulder, he sat down and leaned his back against the willow’s thick trunk. Lost in his own thoughts, drowsiness crept over him, and without meaning to, he drifted off to sleep.
