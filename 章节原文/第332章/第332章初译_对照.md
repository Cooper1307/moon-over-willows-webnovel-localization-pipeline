第332章 汴梁接人一场空
## Chapter 332: An Empty Trip to Bianliang

出于各种原因，申简辰本已放弃为梦容医治，又有人给出希望，他一时不知如何回应。
千里寻急切地问：“不知神医怎么称呼？住在燕京何处？”
魏玩回道：“他叫刘妙手，医馆开在燕京繁华的街市，去燕京大街上打听，必然有人知道。不过，此人医病有个要求。”
千里寻问：“什么要求？”
“据说刘妙手虽是医者，却也喜好诗词，就医之人若能写出好诗好词，不管疾病大小，他都会亲自诊治；可若写不出好诗好词，不拘疾病大小，均由他的弟子医治。”
“真是个怪人。夫人提供的信息太重要，仪王定会带王妃前去诊治，我先代仪王妃谢过夫人。”
与魏玩分别后，两人出了曾府，刚要上马车，身后传来徐公子的声音。
“淑真！”
千里寻很不情愿地转过身：“徐公子唤我何事？”
徐公子的眼里充满期待：“可否借一步说话？”
千里寻犹豫片刻，走上前：“徐公子想与我说什么？”
徐公子瞧了仪王一眼，问道：“他是仪王，你跟他什么关系？”
“徐公子有没有觉得你的问题很多余？”
徐公子不再关注仪王，继续说：“我知道因我曾伤害过你，你才装作不认识我。”
“别太自作多情，我是真的失忆。苦荞倒是给我说起过你，可我一点儿印象都没有。今日见到你也想不起从前的事。”千里寻故意盯着徐公子瞧了一会儿，笑道，“喂，如果我以前真的喜欢过你，那我的眼光也太差。仪王比你长得气派许多，我连他都瞧不上，何况是你？”
千里寻说完，回到仪王身边，与他一起上了马车。
徐公子依然站在原地，似有不舍之情。
申简辰瞧向马车外：“你刚才跟他说些什么？”
“不过是帮朱淑真教训一下当年的负心人而已。”
“他看上去很失落。”
“管他呢，跟我又没关系，还是说正事。既然燕京有神医能治好梦容的病，不如带她前去医治？”
申简辰的眼中更是充满爱意：“她害得你差点儿被斩首，还忍受了将近两年的牢狱之苦，你竟然一点儿不恨她？还劝我带她去医治，让我怎么说你？”
“我从未真正恨过她。不是因为我心胸有多宽广，是因为……”千里寻不敢再说下去，她怕说得越多，流露出越多感情，会给申简辰带来希望，希望越大失望也会越大。
申简辰追问道：“因为什么？”
千里寻掩饰道：“没什么。趁我顶着女词人的名头，陪你和梦容去趟燕京，到时随便写首诗词，不怕神医不给梦容诊治。”
“你真的打算去燕京？”
“我不去谁帮你搞定神医？”
“既然你去，我同意带梦容前去诊治。”
两人沉默了一会儿，申简辰像想起了什么，忽然说道：“现在已是宣和七年，等我们到燕京恐怕正好遇到金军南下。”
千里寻仔细想想，的确如此，可她并不在意：“天下快要大乱，更要赶在大乱之前赶去燕京，否则因金兵南下，神医搬走更不好找。如若真的遇到金兵，凭你我的功夫，保护梦容不在话下。依我看，不如明日出发。”
申简辰只想顺着千里寻，何况这一去，他会有更多时日与她待在一起，何乐而不为？他面带笑容点点头：“都听你的。”
回到仪王府后，收拾好行李，想到陈浩宇肯定会迟些日子来到汴梁，千里寻向邹嬷嬷叮嘱些话，让她帮忙转告。
天一亮，仪王府门口启动两驾马车，仪王独自乘坐一辆，千里寻陪梦容乘坐一辆。白风负责赶王爷的马车，车夫则负责赶后面的。
喜兰要照顾仪王妃，与千里寻同乘一辆。
仪王妃并不胡闹，手里拿着像生花傻看，倒也显得乖巧。
喜兰鼻子一酸，拿起巾帕擦眼泪。
千里寻见喜兰默默流泪，安慰道：“这次去燕京找到神医，梦容的病肯定能医好。”
喜兰继续擦眼泪：“我不是因仪王妃的病难过，已两年，我早都习惯，即使王妃好不了，也没什么。”
“那你为何流泪？”
“既因感动，也因悔恨。”
“什么意思？”
“幽栖居士不怨恨仪王妃，亲自陪伴前往燕京治病，令我感动不已；更加后悔曾经没有规劝仪王妃，反而支持她害您，最终害人害己。不仅仪王妃精神失常，也害幽栖居士差点儿丢掉性命。”
喜兰说着又流下眼泪。
忏悔的话语最容易令人动情，千里寻感觉自己的泪水也快流出，她将头转向窗外，装作看外面的风景。
邹嬷嬷和喜兰都已认识到错误，相信梦容的病若好起来，也会忏悔她过往错误的想法。
千里寻忽然觉得她主动见梁师成，然后被押往汴梁，经受将近两年的牢狱之灾甚是值得。她更加期待燕京之行，希望梦容快些好，她们的关系也可恢复如初。
……
话说陈浩宇遇难后七八日，苦荞与车夫回到临安，将路上的遭遇说了，请陈家老爷找些人赶紧去救公子。
陈江林和姚轻琰听到儿子遇难，心中甚是焦急，吩咐人不准将苦荞回来的事告诉老太太。
自从救下淑真的性命，她又要经受牢狱之灾，夏老太太因忧心，加上年纪大身体本就潜藏着隐患，人一下子病倒，念叨着要等淑真回来，见她最后一面再咽气。故而家里有任何不好的事，大家都会瞒着老太太。
For many reasons, Shen Jianchen had given up on curing Mengrong, and this sudden spark of hope left him speechless. Qianli Xun spoke up eagerly. “What is this miracle physician called, Lady Wei, and where in Yanjing does he live?”

陈江林想到邵家镖局，带上银票拜访邵正邺，将陈浩宇在路上的遭遇说了，请邵当家帮忙派些人前去营救，还说钱不是问题。
“His name is Liu Miaoshou,” Lady Wei said. “His clinic sits on Yanjing’s busiest avenue—ask anyone there and they can point the way. Mind you, he has one odd rule: if a patient brings fine poetry or lyrics, he treats any illness himself, grave or slight. If not, his disciples handle it.”

说来也巧，邵家正好要派人押镖去汴梁。本来是派镖师前去，既然陈公子遇难，邵正邺决定亲自带人押镖，路上再多带些身手好的弟兄。他也正好趁此机会到汴梁拜访仪王殿下，与他叙叙旧。
“What an eccentric,” Qianli Xun remarked. “Still, your tip is priceless. Prince Yi will bring the Princess Consort to see him. Let me thank you first on her behalf.”

陈江林感激不已，将带来的银票全部给了，还说等邵当家回到临安后再重谢。
After parting from Lady Wei, they stepped out of Zeng Manor toward their carriage—only for Young Master Xu’s voice to ring behind them.

陈家的车夫拉上苦荞跟随邵家镖局的人再次踏上前往汴梁的路。
“Shuzhen!”

大概七八日后来到陈浩宇遇难的地儿，没有费力寻找，那伙盗贼主动挡在前面。
Qianli Xun turned round with visible irritation. “What do you want, Young Master Xu?”

苦荞大声说：“邵当家，就是他们虏走姑爷和青瓜。”
Hope glimmered in his eyes. “Could I speak with you privately?”

邵正邺瞧向那帮人，不过是些有三脚猫功夫的山贼而已，吓唬吓唬寻常百姓劫些钱财尚可，想要跟邵家镖局的人打斗，根本不是对手。邵正邺二话不说，命令手下人一起上。
After a beat’s hesitation, she stepped a few paces closer. “Speak, then.”

很快两边打起来，邵家镖局的人数虽没有山贼多，但个个经过专门训练，身手标准，速度和力度跟得上，不到两刻钟便将山贼拿下，一个个用绳子捆了。
Glancing toward Prince Yi, Xu asked, “He is Prince Yi. What is he to you?”

邵正邺对为首的说：“这年头混日子也不易，我不杀你们，只要乖乖放了陈公子，往后我邵家镖局经过此处不准为难，我便放你们一条生路。”
“Don’t you think that question is none of your business?”

为首的满口应下：“英雄，好说好说，往后只要听到邵家镖局，我绝对会带着弟兄们能闪多远就闪多远。您找的人就在山上，给我们当火夫，他做的饭不好吃，我早都不想要了，这就带您上山找人。”
Ignoring the prince, Xu pressed on. “I know you pretend not to know me because I hurt you back then.”

邵正邺让手下给为首的山贼松了绑，让他负责前面带路。
“Don’t flatter yourself. I really did lose my memory. Kuqiao mentioned you once, yet I have zero recollection of you—not a flicker even looking right at you today.” Staring him up and down, she let out a sharp laugh. “Hey, if I really did fancy you once, my taste must have been terrible. Prince Yi has ten times your bearing, and I don’t even want him—why on earth would I look twice at you?”

找到陈浩宇和青瓜时，两人正在厨房里做饭，他们身上值钱的衣裳早已被山贼抢走，都是一身火夫装扮。
Turning on her heel, she boarded the carriage beside Shen Jianchen, leaving Young Master Xu rooted in the street.

见到邵当家前来营救，两人激动不已。
“What did you say to him?” Shen Jianchen asked, peering out.

陈浩宇被救下后，与青瓜换上苦荞为他们准备的干净衣裳，跟随邵家镖局的人一路向汴梁行进。
“Just taught an old heartbreaker a lesson on Zhu Shuzhen’s behalf.”

他在心中推算时日，等到达汴梁时，娘子已从狱中出来半月有余，不知道她是否等得焦急？
“He looks crushed.”

陈浩宇摸摸怀里的字据，还稳妥地躺在他的怀中。
“Who cares? Nothing to do with me. Let’s talk business: since Yanjing has a miracle physician who can cure Mengrong, take her there.”

给山贼当火夫的这段日子，什么值钱的都给了，唯独仪王留下的字据他藏得最好。
Warmth flooded Shen Jianchen’s eyes. “She nearly got you beheaded and cost you two years in prison, yet you don’t hate her at all—and even urge me to heal her? What am I supposed to say about you?”

陈浩宇还是担心仪王将圣旨的事告诉娘子，若仪王真的说了，以娘子的品性，为保全他人，哪怕不爱仪王都会遵从圣旨，何况她心里有仪王。
“I never truly hated her. Not because I’m some saint, just because…” Catching herself before giving him false hope that would only lead to worse disappointment, she swallowed the rest.

娘子心里装着他人，陈浩宇多少能感觉出来。有时他也想不如趁此机会成全他们，可他做不到。谁让娘子是他爱的人，他为她改变心性，重新做人，还为她已两年多未近女色，就等着与她圆房。
“Because what?” he pressed.

当一个人一直以来期待一件美好之事，突然被人横插一脚，让曾经认为必然的结果变得扑朔迷离，充满希望的人会害怕失望到来。
“Nothing,” she deflected smoothly. “While I still carry the female poet’s name, I’ll ride to Yanjing with you both, dash off some verse, and make sure that doctor treats Mengrong.”

陈浩宇此时的心情便是如此，想到已解除的婚姻，想到圣旨，他很不自信。
“You’re coming to Yanjing?”

他再次摸摸怀里仪王写下的字据，想起在狱中娘子亲吻他的情景，他的脸上终于露出笑容，再次充满自信。
“If I don’t come, who’s going to satisfy his poetry rule?”

……
“If you come, I’ll take her.” After a quiet moment, Shen Jianchen added, “Wait—it’s the seventh year of Xuanhe. By the time we reach Yanjing, the Jin army may be marching south.”

十日后终于到达汴梁，一行人发现东京城内人心惶惶，街上是许多富人的马车，像逃难似的。
She thought a second and shrugged. “All the more reason to go before chaos breaks out, or the doctor might pack up and flee. Even if we run into Jin troops, with your skills and mine, keeping Mengrong safe won’t be a problem. Let’s leave tomorrow.”

邵正邺找街上人一打听，才知金军已分两路南下，直逼汴梁，城中的百姓能逃出城的都往外逃，无处可逃的人才不得已留下。
Eager to please her—and to spend more days beside her—Shen Jianchen smiled and agreed. Back at the manor, while packing, Qianli Xun left detailed word with Matron Zou for Chen Haoyu whenever he arrived.

邵正邺对陈浩宇说：“你先去仪王府接幽栖居士，我前去送镖，将镖安置好后，到仪王府与你会合。此等景况，不能在汴梁长待，还是尽快回临安。”
At daybreak, two carriages rolled out from Prince Yi’s Manor: Shen Jianchen alone in the front one with Bai Feng driving, and Qianli Xun, Mengrong, and Xilan in the rear. Mengrong sat docilely gazing at her silk flower without making trouble.

陈浩宇也这么认为，与邵正邺分别后他直奔仪王府。
Watching her, Xilan dabbed her eyes with a handkerchief. Thinking she wept over Mengrong’s illness, Qianli Xun comforted her that the miracle physician in Yanjing would heal her.

本以为能顺利接到娘子，去了才知娘子跟随仪王去了燕京。
“I’m not crying over her sickness,” Xilan murmured, wiping tears away. “It’s been two years; I’ve made peace with that even if she never recovers. I’m crying out of shame and gratitude.”

陈浩宇着急地质问道：“给仪王妃医病，仪王去便可，为何要带上我娘子？”
“Why?”

邹嬷嬷解释道：“据说神医有个怪癖，必须写诗作词，写得好他才亲自给医，幽栖居士关心王妃，主动要求跟随王爷一起去燕京。她对仪王妃这份情，我会牢记在心。”
“You hold no grudge against Princess Consort Yi and even ride to Yanjing to heal her—that moves me to tears. And it makes me hate myself all the more for helping her plot against you instead of stopping her, ruining her wits and nearly costing you your life.”

陈浩宇失落之余，继续问：“我娘子走时有没有嘱咐什么？”
Seeing Xilan weep in remorse brought tears pricking Qianli Xun’s own eyes; she turned toward the window, pretending to watch the scenery. Since both Matron Zou and Xilan repented so sincerely, she trusted Mengrong would mend her ways once healed too—making every bitter hour in prison worth it.

邹嬷嬷说：“嘱咐了，幽栖居士说等到燕京确定仪王妃的病能医好，她会从燕京直接回临安，让陈公子您不必在汴梁等她。”
Seven or eight days after Haoyu’s capture, Kuqiao and the driver reached Lin’an and begged Master Chen for help. Terrified for their son, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan kept the ambush secret from Old Madam Xia, who had fallen bedridden with worry during Shuzhen’s imprisonment, clinging to life only to see her daughter-in-law one last time.

陈浩宇对娘子的嘱咐很满意，说明仪王并未将圣旨的事告知。
Chen Jianglin carried a stack of silver notes to Shao Zhengye at the Shao Family Escort Agency, sparing no expense for rescue. By stroke of luck, the agency already had a convoy bound for Bianliang; Shao Zhengye took command himself with extra skilled guards, glad for the chance to visit Prince Yi as well. Grateful, Jianglin handed over every silver note and promised rich reward upon their return, sending Kuqiao and the driver back north with the convoy.

邹嬷嬷宽慰道：“陈公子放心，王爷叮嘱王府上下人等不能将圣旨的事向幽栖居士透露半句，她到现在都不知圣旨之事。她跟王爷之间相处也是光明磊落，行为从未逾越规矩，你作为她的夫君应该相信她。”
Seven or eight days later, near the ambush site, the bandits swaggered straight out into their path. Kuqiao pointed them out. Sizing up the rabble’s clumsy martial arts, Shao Zhengye ordered his guards to charge. Trained escorts tore through the bandits in less than half an hour, binding every man with rope.

陈浩宇点头认可：“我自然相信她，只不过来到汴梁扑空一场，心中难免失落。”
“Making a living these days isn’t easy, so I’ll spare your necks,” Shao Zhengye told the bandit chief. “Release Young Master Chen and never trouble my agency on this road again, and you live.”

邹嬷嬷说：“主要是陈公子您来得太晚。”
“Easy, hero, easy!” the chief yelped. “From today on, we hear ‘Shao Family Escort Agency’ and we scatter a mile wide! Your man’s up the hill cooking for us—awful cook too, glad to be rid of him!”

陈浩宇也认可，都怪路上遇到山贼，耽误了行程，才没能按时见到娘子。
Untying the chief to lead them up, they found Haoyu and Qinggua in rough kitchen clothes over the stoves, robbed of their fine silks. After changing into clean clothes Kuqiao brought, they rode north toward Bianliang with the convoy.

邹嬷嬷继续说：“幽栖居士对待仪王妃的态度令人感动，看在她的面子上，我跟陈公子说说心里话。”
Counting the days, Haoyu knew his wife had been out of prison over half a month, anxiously clutching the written pledge inside his robe—the one treasure he had kept hidden even while cooking for bandits. He dreaded Prince Yi breaking silence over the decree, knowing his wife’s selflessness might make her comply to protect others, especially when part of her heart already leaned toward the prince. Still, touching the paper and remembering her prison kiss lifted a smile back onto his face.

“嬷嬷请讲。”
Ten days later, reaching Bianliang, they found the Eastern Capital in turmoil, carriages of wealthy families clogging the roads fleeing south. Asking on the street, Shao Zhengye learned Jin armies had marched south along two routes toward the capital; anyone with means was fleeing, leaving only those with nowhere to run.

“陈公子不必担心圣旨的事，我家王爷是个说到做到的人，既然与你立下字据，必会遵守。你也看到，汴梁的百姓人心惶惶，恐怕天下要变，只要天下变了，之前的圣旨自然作废，你又何必担心？还是快些回临安等待幽栖居士。我相信，以她的品性，不会舍下陈公子；我家王爷会尊重幽栖居士的选择，她最终会回到你的身边。”
While Shao Zhengye delivered his cargo, Haoyu dashed straight to Prince Yi’s Manor—only to hear his wife had left for Yanjing with Prince Yi!

邹嬷嬷一番说辞实在管用，陈浩宇轻松许多。尤其是天下要变，圣旨作废之说，更是令陈浩宇开心。他甚至希望天下早些乱，朝堂上那位写下圣旨破坏他婚姻的人立刻嗝屁，那么他什么都不用再怕。
“If the Princess Consort needed a doctor, why bring my wife?!” Haoyu demanded.

“嬷嬷一番言论，惊醒梦中人，我这就回临安耐心等待娘子归来。”
Matron Zou explained the physician’s poetry rule and delivered her message: once sure Princess Consort Yi could be cured, she would head straight back to Lin’an from Yanjing without making Haoyu wait in Bianliang.

仪王府的下人来报：“邹嬷嬷，门外有位自称是临安邵家镖局的人前来拜见仪王。”
Seeing his crestfallen face, Matron Zou lowered her voice. “Rest assured, Young Master Chen. His Highness forbade everyone in the manor from breathing a word of the Imperial Decree—she still knows nothing. They behave cleanly and properly together. As her husband, trust her.”

陈浩宇说：“邵当家与我一同来到汴梁，应该是送完镖前来拜见。既然仪王不在，由我向他转达，我们也好一同回临安。”
“I do trust her,” Haoyu sighed. “It just hurts coming all this way for nothing.”

陈浩宇拜别邹嬷嬷后，到仪王府门外向邵正邺说明情况，带上苦荞和青瓜跟随邵家镖局的人原路返回临安。
“Only because bandits delayed you. And since she treats our Princess Consort so kindly, let me speak from the heart: stop worrying about that decree. His Highness keeps his word; what he put in writing stands. Besides, look at Bianliang in panic—the realm is about to overturn. Once the world changes, old decrees turn into waste paper. Go back to Lin’an and wait for her; a woman like her will come home to you.”

Her words lifted a mountain off Haoyu’s chest—especially the thought of that decree turning to waste paper and its sender meeting disaster. Just then a servant reported Shao Zhengye waiting outside. Taking leave of Matron Zou, Haoyu took Kuqiao and Qinggua back south with the escort convoy along roads packed with fleeing refugees.

这次来汴梁既没接到小姐，也没见着白风，又目睹汴梁百姓的状况，苦荞担心不已，害怕以后没有机会再见白风。
Haoyu prayed for his wife’s safety among advancing Jin forces, taking comfort in her martial skill and Prince Yi’s. Beside him, Kuqiao silently wept inside—missing both her mistress and Bai Feng, wondering if she would ever see him again.
