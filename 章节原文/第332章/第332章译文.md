## Chapter 332: Coming Up Empty in Bianliang

Outside Chancellor Zeng's gate, Young Master Xu chased after Qianli Xun hoping for a nostalgic reunion—getting roasted flat: *"Prince Yi outclasses you by miles and I still turned him down—dream on!"*

In the carriage, moved beyond words that Qianli Xun forgave Mengrong completely and volunteered to ride all the way to Yanjing to write poetry for the eccentric doctor, Shen Jianchen brushed aside worries of the coming Jurchen invasion and agreed to set out at dawn—leaving careful word with Matron Zou for Haoyu. Riding alongside blank-eyed Mengrong and tearfully remorseful Xilan, Qianli Xun hoped this journey would finally heal their broken sisterhood.

Meanwhile, back in Lin'an, Kuqiao's desperate return sent Chen Jianglin straight to **Shao Zhengye**—who conveniently led a Bianliang escort caravan personally, easily trashed the amateur highwaymen in fifteen minutes, and rescued Haoyu and Qinggua straight out of the bandits' kitchen!

Reaching panicked, war-jittery Bianliang ten days later—where rich households were already fleeing the two-pronged Jurchen invasion—Haoyu rushed to Prince Yi's Manor only to find his wife gone to Yanjing! Learning from Matron Zou that Prince Yi had honored his pledge never to mention the edict—and reassured by her sharp hint that war would soon shred the old emperor's edicts anyway—Haoyu rejoined Shao Zhengye's caravan and rode back south to Lin'an with Kuqiao and Qinggua to await his wife's return.
