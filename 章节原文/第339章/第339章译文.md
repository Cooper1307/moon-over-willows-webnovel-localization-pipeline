## Chapter 339: Trading a Fortune Only to Hear She Is Dead

Racing south through streams of war refugees, Shen Jianchen broke Mengrong's death to weeping Matron Zou and put his entire estate on the block. For a full month wartime panic froze buyers—until first snow fell and generous **Lady Wei** stepped up, paying full price without haggling for Prince Yi's Manor and rallying friends to buy every remaining asset! Sending Bai Feng to escort Matron Zou safely to Lin'an's bamboo cottage, Shen Jianchen rode north through winter storms alone with **ten million taels** in banknotes.

Stunned when Cai Jing announced Prince Yi had actually raised ten million taels, jealous Wanyan Chi hatched a vicious double-cross: inviting Shen Jianchen into his hall the next day, inspecting the bursting chest of banknotes—then claiming coldly that Qianli Xun had stabbed herself to death last night and been tossed in the pauper's pit!

Crushing Wanyan Chi's ambush squad and smashing Wanyan Chi's jaw bloody, Shen Jianchen faltered for one fatal heartbeat when Wanyan Chi taunted that her dying thoughts had been of him—taking a treacherous Jin sword straight through the back and blacking out as Wanyan Chi stole his fortune and dumped him outside the city wall!

Waking bandaged in a farmhouse cared for by Cai Jing—who spun a merciful lie and even staged a fresh dummy grave outside town so the ruined prince wouldn't throw his life away storming Wanyan Chi's garrison!—Shen Jianchen collapsed weeping across the fresh earth. Warned by Cai Jing not to exhume her during the forty-nine-day *bardo* spirit transition, Shen Jianchen hired a carriage straight back to Lin'an's Divine Willow.

Standing before bare winter branches, he pressed a lethal fist against his own heart to follow her—only for **Master Jingguang** to halt him cold: *"Suicide breeds dark karma that rebounds straight onto the dead! Live on and build merit in her name!"* Awakened from despair, Shen Jianchen bowed deep, resolving to spend his remaining years doing charity under *Qianli Xun*'s name.
