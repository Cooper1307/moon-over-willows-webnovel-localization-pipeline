**Chapter 13: Confronting the Patriarch and Smashing the Table**

By the time they reached the main courtyard of the Zhu residence, the sky had grown even darker.
Lanterns hung at regular intervals throughout the courtyard, their bright glow easily piercing the night.
Yinxing lifted her lantern, blew out the candle inside, and led Qianli Xun and Kuqiao through a long corridor to the courtyard of the main hall. The space was expansive, paved with smooth stone tiles. Bathed in the lantern light, three magnificent magnolia trees planted on either side were clearly visible, their flowers blooming vibrantly in white, purple, and yellow.
Captivated by the flowering trees, Qianli Xun paused to admire them.
"If you like them, Miss, you can come see them first thing in the morning," Yinxing urged gently. "They are even more beautiful under the sunlight. For now, we must hurry to dinner. If we're late, the Master will be upset."
Qianli Xun had no choice but to keep moving. As she walked, she pondered her strategy: when she met Zhu Shuzhen's father, should she act like the original Zhu Shuzhen, or just be herself? She ultimately decided to observe the situation in Zhu Shuzhen's "default mode" first and play it by ear. She reminded herself: Zhu Shuzhen's mode was to bow respectfully, and then remain absolutely silent.
Just as she formulated her plan, they arrived at the dining hall. Several maids were already carrying trays of food inside.
Yinxing stopped at the threshold and announced, "Master, Madam, the Miss has arrived."
A man's voice drifted out from inside—calm, yet chillingly aloof. "Let her in."
"Miss, the Master has permitted you to enter," Yinxing said.
Qianli Xun took two steps forward before noticing Kuqiao hadn't followed. She turned back. "Why aren't you coming?"
"I don't have the standing to enter, Miss," Kuqiao whispered. "I'll wait outside for you."
"Don't worry, Miss," Yinxing added. "I'll take Kuqiao to the maids' quarters for dinner."
"Alright," Qianli Xun sighed. "Looks like I'll have to go into battle with my father alone."

Walking into the main hall, a large privacy screen immediately blocked her view of the interior. She paused, taking a deep breath to remind herself: *Stay calm. Be polite. Keep silent.*
A maid stepped forward and whispered, "This way, Miss."
Qianli Xun followed the maid around the screen, and the scene inside finally came into full view.
Facing them was a low sitting couch holding a square wooden table. Madam Xiang sat on one side. Seeing her daughter enter, she didn't speak, offering only a warm, encouraging smile. On the other side sat Zhu Shuzhen's father. He held a book in his hand, completely absorbed in his reading, not even glancing up at his daughter's arrival.
Qianli Xun studied him carefully. He looked to be in his forties, sporting a well-groomed beard, thick eyebrows, and large eyes. His facial structure was sharp, and his skin was well-maintained. He must have been quite a heartthrob in his youth.
*Ah, so Zhu Shuzhen got her looks from her father,* Qianli Xun thought.
Stepping forward, she bowed properly. "Father. Mother."
Madam Xiang nodded, remaining silent.
Without even lifting his eyes from his book, Zhu Xiyan commanded, "Take a seat."
Qianli Xun found her spot and sat down, chanting internally: *Stay silent. Stay silent.*
She managed to hold it in for a while, but soon grew incredibly bored. Watching the maids constantly filing in and out to set the table, she couldn't help but critique: *It's just three people eating, yet there are so many dishes. How extravagant!*
Madam Xiang hadn't yet found the right moment to inform the Master about her daughter's condition, simply because she didn't dare speak out of turn in front of him. When her daughter walked in, she observed her closely. Shuzhen walked with graceful modesty and sat down quietly, exactly as she had in the past. A relieved smile blossomed on Madam Xiang's face.
But then, she heard her daughter's voice pipe up: "Father, Mother, dinner is ready. Let's hurry up and eat. Eating too late makes it hard to digest, and it's super easy to gain weight..."
A single tear rolled down Madam Xiang's cheek. The girl sitting there was definitely no longer her Shuzhen. She quickly turned her head and dabbed the tear away with her silk handkerchief.
Zhu Xiyan was equally startled. He finally lifted his eyes and shot his daughter a sharp glance.
Meeting her father's gaze, Qianli Xun shivered inwardly. His eyes were terrifyingly stern. Not daring to utter another word, she quickly lowered her head and resumed her vow of silence.

"Master, Madam, Miss, dinner is fully served," a maid announced, bowing.
*Finally!* Qianli Xun thought. She was the first to spring to her feet, but before she could take a single step, she caught Madam Xiang frantically shaking her head and gesturing for her to sit back down. Deflated, Qianli Xun dropped back into her seat and stole a glance at Zhu Xiyan. She watched as he deliberately placed a bookmark, set down his book, stepped off the couch, and walked past her.
Only then did Madam Xiang step down. She walked over, gently pulled her daughter up, and together they trailed behind Zhu Xiyan to the dining table, where she guided Qianli Xun to her proper seat.
Sitting down, Qianli Xun felt suffocated. In all her life, she had never experienced such a stifling dining atmosphere. It was unbearable, and she decided she was done being silent.
"You may begin," Zhu Xiyan finally declared.
Unable to hold back a second longer, Qianli Xun picked up a piece of duck with her chopsticks and dropped it into Madam Xiang's bowl. "Mother, eat this."
Madam Xiang shook her head at her again, her eyes wide with subtle panic.
Ignoring the warning, Qianli Xun picked up another piece of duck and reached out toward Zhu Xiyan. She had just managed to say "Father—" when she saw his expression. His face was thunderous, practically radiating displeasure.
She smoothly retracted her hand. "If you won't eat it, I will." She popped the duck into her mouth and began chewing loudly.
A maid immediately rushed over and whispered urgently in her ear, "Miss, we must eat in silence! Please mind your manners!"
Qianli Xun was thoroughly annoyed. *Eating in my own home, yet acting like I'm walking on eggshells. This isn't comfortable at all. Whatever. If I didn't need a favor from this guy, I wouldn't put up with this.* She eyed her father critically. *Look at him—a grown man eating like a bird without making a single sound. How unmanly.*
She shifted her gaze to Madam Xiang. Her mother’s movements were the picture of elegance. She parted her lips only slightly to take a bite and chewed meticulously slowly. Qianli Xun tried to imitate her. After one attempt, she almost laughed out loud—it was so completely contrary to her nature. Having practiced the Qianli Fist since childhood and hung around burly martial artists, her eating style had always been uninhibited and ravenous. When had she ever been "delicate"? 
Deciding not to torture herself any longer, she reverted to her true self. She completely let go, wolfing down her food with gusto.
Seeing this, Madam Xiang froze, her chopsticks hovering in the air. She stared in sheer shock. How could this wild village woman be her refined Shuzhen?! Fresh tears welled up, and she reached for her handkerchief again. The attending maids exchanged sideways glances, struggling to hide their bewildered smiles.
*Smack!*
Everyone jumped. Zhu Xiyan had slammed his chopsticks hard onto the table. 
Qianli Xun, who was just reaching for a chicken leg, yanked her hand back in fright. Immediately seizing the opportunity, she grabbed her forehead and started acting. "Ouch! Ah, my wound! The wound on my forehead hurts so much!"
"Shuzhen, are you alright?!" Madam Xiang cried out in panic. "Someone, hurry! Bring the medicinal ointment and apply it to the Miss's head!"
A maid scrambled over and quickly applied the medicine.
"Does it still hurt?" Madam Xiang fretted.
"Much better," Qianli Xun said slyly. She hoped this little act would either spark some paternal concern or at least forcefully pivot the conversation to the topic she needed.
Surprisingly, the trick worked. Zhu Xiyan finally spoke, his face still etched with stern authority. "I hear you want an amicable divorce from Lin Jue. Is this true?"
Now that the floodgates were open, how could Qianli Xun let the opportunity slip?
"Father, you can see it for yourself. The wound on my forehead was caused by Lin Jue. And you must have noticed how different I am from the daughter you knew. That's because Lin Jue beat your daughter unconscious. When she woke up, she became like this, with all her previous memories wiped clean! Furthermore, he openly brings brothel girls into the house. Isn't that going too far? Letting a beast like that continue being your son-in-law is pushing your daughter into a living hell! I beg you, Father, step forward and support my divorce."
"Do you still have Young Master Xu in your heart?" Zhu Xiyan asked coldly, his face utterly blank. He didn't even look at her. "I might as well tell you today. The Xu family has moved north. Young Master Xu is already married and now has a son and a daughter."
Qianli Xun blinked. She remembered Kuqiao mentioning a "Young Master Xu," Zhu Shuzhen's childhood sweetheart. But to her, he was literally a stranger.
"What Young Master Xu? I was beaten so badly by Lin Jue that I have amnesia! I don't remember anything!"
"Not remembering is for the best," Zhu Xiyan declared flatly. "Then settle down, live a proper life, and put this nonsense about a divorce out of your head."
Qianli Xun was staggered. His daughter had been beaten half to death, yet her own flesh-and-blood father didn't feel the slightest bit of heartache? What kind of twisted parenting was this?!
"Father, he physically beats me and sleeps with courtesans! Do you honestly expect me to keep living with him?"
"Hmph! Do you take me for a fool?" Zhu Xiyan scoffed. "Lin Jue came to the prefectural office to see me today. He explained the entire situation and even knelt before me to admit his faults. He injured you, yes, but you injured him just as badly! He brought a courtesan home, but are you entirely without fault? If you had just done your duty and consummated the marriage earlier, none of this would have happened."
Lin Jue had actually beaten her to the punch to play the victim! Qianli Xun’s blood boiled. She shot up from her chair. "Father, you can't just listen to his one-sided lies!"
"And I should only listen to yours?"
Furious at her father’s bizarre priorities, Qianli Xun pressed harder. "Father, Lin Jue literally beat your daughter to death! I am a reborn soul inhabiting Zhu Shuzhen's body. I am not your real daughter, and I possess none of her memories! Haven't you noticed?! Based on that alone, you should have Lin Jue arrested for murder and locked away!"
Zhu Xiyan finally looked up, his sharp eyes scrutinizing her. She was indeed drastically different in demeanor, but the face was unmistakably his daughter's. He lowered his gaze dismissively. "This nonsense about 'rebirth' is nothing but superstitious drivel invented by country folk to swindle the gullible. How can you expect a learned man to take it seriously? If you aren't Shuzhen, who else could you be?"
Qianli Xun raised a clenched fist. "I know martial arts! Did the old Zhu Shuzhen know how to fight? I beat Lin Jue black and blue, whereas the old Zhu Shuzhen could only cower and take his beatings! Do you still genuinely believe your original daughter survived his abuse?!"
Hearing this brutal declaration, Madam Xiang broke down into loud sobs. "Oh, Shuzhen! My poor Shuzhen! You died so tragically!"
The attending maids were utterly shell-shocked by the conversation, but with the Master present, they didn't dare breathe a word.
"What are you crying for?!" Zhu Xiyan snapped, his voice calm but brimming with authority. "The truth isn't even clear yet, and you're just adding to the mess!"
Madam Xiang instantly stifled her sobs, though she continued to silently wipe her tears.
Then, Zhu Xiyan delivered a line so cold it left Qianli Xun speechless. "Since you claim you are not my daughter Shuzhen, then I have even less reason to step forward and support this divorce. Leave. With you here, I cannot even finish my meal in peace."
Qianli Xun's eyes widened in disbelief. She had never imagined Zhu Shuzhen's father could be this heartless.
"And what if I *were* your daughter?" she challenged.
"Then you should divorce even less. Obey your parents' arrangements and live your days quietly with Lin Jue. I married you to him specifically to teach you that life isn't all moonlight and poetry."
The sheer rage inside Qianli Xun reached its boiling point. She desperately wanted to use her fists to beat Zhu Xiyan until he agreed to support the divorce. But in the eyes of this era, he was her elder and her father. Her fury gathered intensely in her fists, demanding a release.
Zhu Xiyan calmly picked up his chopsticks to resume his meal, acting as if nothing had happened.
Unable to contain herself a second longer, Qianli Xun brought her fist down.
*CRACK!*
With a deafening splinter of wood, the solid dining table split cleanly in two. Bowls, plates, and exquisite dishes crashed to the floor in a messy, chaotic ruin. Madam Xiang and Zhu Xiyan scrambled backward to dodge the flying debris, while the maids shrieked, terrified by this monstrous "reborn" young miss.
Her anger momentarily vented, Qianli Xun lowered her fist and glared at Zhu Xiyan. "Regardless of who I am, I still call you Father. But since you refuse to stand up for me, I'll handle it myself. I will get this divorce with or without your help!"
With that, Qianli Xun spun on her heel and strode out into the night.
Left behind in the ruined hall were a weeping Madam Xiang and Zhu Xiyan, whose face remained an unreadable mask. Yet, staring at the shattered table, he was finally forced to admit the truth in his heart: the girl who had just stormed out was definitely no longer his Shuzhen.
