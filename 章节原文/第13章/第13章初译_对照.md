第13章 面见父亲怒砸桌
## Chapter 13: Facing Her Father, Smashing the Table

到达朱府正院，天变得更黑。
正院里每隔一段距离挂有一个灯笼，黑夜根本无法遮住灯笼里散发出的亮光。
银杏将手中提着的灯笼抬起，吹灭里面的火光，带千里寻和苦荞穿过一个长廊，来到正屋的庭院。院子很大，铺着用石头打制的地砖。因灯光的原因能够清晰地看到两边分别栽种着三棵玉兰树，花开得正艳，有白色，紫色和黄色三种。
千里寻被眼前的花树吸引，驻足欣赏一会儿。
银杏催道：“小姐若喜欢，明儿天亮时再来瞧，太阳底下，花更美。现在还是尽快去跟老爷太太用饭，晚了老爷会怪罪。”
千里寻只好启步，她边走边寻思，一会儿见到朱淑真的父亲，是该学朱淑真一样对待他，还是按照千里寻的模式？最终决定，先按照朱淑真的模式观察一会儿，再看情况。她在心里叮嘱自己，朱淑真的模式是先行礼，然后保持沉默。
正想着，已来到朱府正院用饭之地，好几个丫鬟端着盘子往屋里走。
银杏站住，向屋内禀报：“老爷，夫人，小姐已到。”
传出一个男人的声音，冷静中带着冷漠：“让她进来。”
银杏说：“小姐，老爷允许您进去。”
千里寻走出两步，见苦荞未跟上，回头问：“怎么不跟着？”
苦荞说：“奴婢没资格进，我在外面等小姐。”
银杏说：“小姐放心，我会带苦荞到丫鬟房用饭。”
千里寻叹道：“好吧，看来我只能单枪匹马会会我爹。”
走进正屋，迎面被一个大大的屏风挡住，里面什么情况，根本无法看到。她停住脚步，在心里提醒自己：“先稳住，要有礼貌，保持沉默。”
一个丫鬟过来，小声提醒：“小姐，这边。”
千里寻跟着丫鬟拐过屏风，屋内的情景才尽收眼底。
迎面是坐榻，上面放着方形的木桌，一边坐着向夫人，看到女儿进来，她并不说话，仅以微笑示意。另一边坐着的应该是朱淑真的爹，他手中拿着一本书，全神贯注，根本没有抬眼看女儿。
千里寻仔细瞧这位爹，他大约四十多岁，留着胡须，浓眉大眼，脸型甚好，皮肤也不错，年轻时应该是个美男子。
千里寻心想：“原来朱淑真长得像她爹。”
千里寻上前行礼：“爹，娘。”
向夫人点点头，依然不说话。
朱晞颜未抬眼，吩咐道：“坐下吧。”
千里寻找位置坐下，在心里不断叮嘱自己：“保持沉默，保持沉默。”
坚持了一会儿，甚觉无聊，看向进进出出摆饭的丫鬟，心想：“三人吃饭，摆这么多菜，太奢侈了吧！”
向夫人还未找到机会把女儿的情况告知老爷，只因她在老爷面前不敢随意说话。女儿走进来时，她用心观察，淑真走路温婉可人，安安静静坐在那里，跟从前并无两样。向夫人的脸上不由露出笑容。
紧接着听到女儿说：“爹，娘，饭已摆好，赶紧过去吃吧，吃得太晚，不容易消化，还容易发胖……”
By the time they reached Zhu Manor's Main Courtyard, night had fallen thick. Lanterns hung at even intervals all around the yard, their bright pools of glow pushing back the dark. Yinxing raised her handheld lantern, blew out the flame, and led Qianli Xun and Kuqiao down a long corridor into the main hall's paved stone courtyard. By the lantern light, three magnolia trees stood clear on each side in full bloom, white, purple, and yellow.

向夫人流下一滴泪，眼前的女儿已经不是从前的淑真。她扭过头，悄悄用丝帕擦掉泪水。
Drawn by the blossoms, Qianli Xun paused to take them in. Yinxing nudged her along: "If you like them, Miss, come look in daylight tomorrow; they shine even brighter under the sun. Better hurry to dine with Master and Madam before Master grows displeased."

朱晞颜甚觉诧异，抬起眼，瞥向一旁的女儿。
Resuming her pace, Qianli Xun weighed her plan: act like the old Zhu Shuzhen before her father, or stay Qianli Xun? Better start in Zhu Shuzhen's mode, watch the room first, and adapt. She coached herself in silence: bow first, then stay quiet.

千里寻接触到这位父亲的目光，心里不由打了个寒颤，如此严厉，不敢再说下去，赶紧低下头，继续保持沉默。
Soon they reached the main dining hall, where maids bustled indoors bearing platters. Halting at the threshold, Yinxing announced, "Master, Madam, Miss has arrived."

丫鬟过来，行礼道：“老爷，夫人，小姐，饭已摆好。”
A man's voice answered from inside, cool and remote: "Let her in."

千里寻心想：“终于好了！”第一个起身，步子还没迈出，看到向夫人摇头，用手示意她坐下。千里寻只能继续坐下，偷偷瞧朱晞颜。只见他放下书卷，从榻上下来，经过她面前。
"Master says you may enter, Miss," Yinxing murmured.

向夫人也从榻上下来，走到女儿身边，拉起她，跟在朱晞颜身后，来到饭桌旁，把女儿安排在合适的位置。
Two steps in, Qianli Xun saw Kuqiao staying back and glanced over her shoulder. "Aren't you coming?"

千里寻坐下，长这么大第一次吃饭遇到如此不自在的氛围，她很不习惯，决定不再沉默。
"I'm not permitted inside, Miss. I'll wait right out here."

听到朱晞颜说了句：“可以开动。”终于忍不住，夹一块鸭肉给向夫人放进碗里：“娘，吃这个。”
"Don't worry, Miss," Yinxing added. "I'll take Kuqiao to eat in the maids' quarters."

向夫人又向她摇摇头。
Qianli Xun sighed. "Fine. Looks like I'm walking in solo to meet my dad."

千里寻不想再理会，继续夹一块鸭肉，递到朱晞颜跟前，刚叫了声“爹”，见爹一脸阴沉，看上去很不高兴，便抽回手：“爹不吃，我吃。”很快塞进自己嘴里，大嚼起来。
Stepping past the doorway, she met a massive standing screen blocking the interior. Pausing a beat, she reminded herself: Keep calm, mind manners, hold your tongue. A maid stepped out and murmured, "This way, Miss." Rounding the screen behind her, Qianli Xun took in the hall at a glance.

旁边的丫鬟过来，在耳边叮嘱道：“小姐，食不言，还要注意姿态。”
Straight ahead stood a wide couch with a low square table set across it. On one side sat Madam Xiang, greeting her daughter with a quiet smile and no words; across from her sat Zhu Shuzhen's father, absorbed in a volume without lifting his eyes once. Qianli Xun studied him: forties, neatly kept beard, bold brows, clear eyes, clean jawline, good complexion. He must have been quite handsome in his youth. So Zhu Shuzhen got her looks from her father, she mused.

千里寻很不满，心想：“在自己家里吃个饭，注意这注意那，一点儿都不自在，算了，谁让我有事求这位爹。看他那个样子，一个大男人吃个饭细嚼慢咽，连个吧唧声都没有，实在不像男人。”
Stepping up, she bowed. "Father. Mother."

再观察向夫人，她夹菜的动作十分优雅，吃菜时微启朱唇，也是细嚼慢咽。千里寻只能学着照做。学了一遍，自己都觉得好笑，与她的风格相去甚远。她从小练习千里拳，跟练武之人混在一起，吃饭从来是豪放派，何时这么婉约过？她决定不能让自己难受，还是恢复正常，于是完全放开，大嚼大咽起来。
Madam Xiang nodded without speaking. Without raising his gaze from the book, Zhu Xiyan ordered, "Sit."

向夫人看到，停下筷子，一脸惊讶。这哪像自己的女儿淑真！那个吃相，如同山野村妇。她又流下眼泪，拿丝帕去擦。旁边伺候的丫鬟互相交换眼色，脸上露出讶异的笑容。
Qianli Xun took a seat, chanting inside her head: Stay quiet, stay quiet. Minutes dragged in stiff silence while maids shuttled in and out laying dishes. All this spread for three people? Talk about wasteful!

只听“啪”的一声，原来是朱晞颜将筷子摔在桌上，周围的人都被吓坏。
Madam Xiang had not yet found an opening to brief her husband, never daring to speak out of turn before him. Watching Shuzhen walk in with poised grace and sit still without a peep, a relieved smile lit her face.

千里寻刚要去拽鸡腿，被这一声吓得抽回手，摸着额头，开始装疼：“哎呦！我额头上的伤口好疼呀！”
Then her daughter opened her mouth: "Father, Mother, food's all set! Let's go eat. Eating too late messes up your digestion and puts on weight..."

向夫人担心道：“淑真，你没事吧？来人，快拿创伤药来，给小姐涂上。”
A single tear slid down Madam Xiang's cheek: this was no longer her old Shuzhen. She turned her face aside to blot it with her handkerchief. Startled, Zhu Xiyan raised his eyes and shot a sharp look at his daughter. A chill ran down Qianli Xun's spine under that stern glare; biting off the rest, she ducked her head and went mute again.

丫鬟迅速拿来创伤药，给小姐的额头涂了些。
A maid stepped forward with a bow. "Master, Madam, Miss, dinner is served."

向夫人又急切地问：“还疼吗？”
At last! Qianli Xun started up first, only to catch Madam Xiang shaking her head and waving her back down. Sitting again, she sneaked a look as Zhu Xiyan laid down his book, stepped off the couch, and walked past her. Madam Xiang rose too, took her daughter's hand, trailed behind Zhu Xiyan to the dining table, and guided Shuzhen into her proper seat.

千里寻说：“好多了。”她希望这么一装，能引起父亲关怀，或是尽快引出她需要的话题。
Never in her life had Qianli Xun endured such a suffocating meal. Fed up with the chill, she ditched silence. The instant Zhu Xiyan said, "You may begin," she picked up a slice of duck and dropped it into Madam Xiang's bowl. "Mother, try this."

还别说，这招真灵，朱晞颜终于说话，表情依然严肃：“听说你想跟林觉和离，可有此事？”
Madam Xiang shook her head again. Ignoring the hint, Qianli Xun lifted a second piece of duck toward Zhu Xiyan's bowl, starting "Father..." before pulling back at his thunderous scowl. "Don't want it? I'll eat it!" She popped it straight into her own mouth and chewed away.

既然话匣子打开，千里寻岂能放过这么好的机会？
A maid leaned down and whispered in her ear, "Miss, no talking at the table, and watch your posture."

“爹，您也看到，我额头的伤就是林觉打的，您肯定也发现，我跟您以前的女儿很不一样，就是因为林觉把您的女儿打得晕死过去，醒来后，就成我这样，而且以前的事情都不记得。他还把商妓往家里带，您说过分不过分？让这样的人继续当您的女婿，岂不是把女儿往火坑里推？女儿求爹出面促成和离之事。”
Irritation simmered in Qianli Xun: Can't even eat in peace in my own home, rules for this and rules for that! Fine, I need a favor from him. Look at him though, a grown man nibbling without a sound, zero manly appetite! Glancing at Madam Xiang, she saw dainty chopsticks, tiny morsels, quiet chewing. Qianli Xun tried copying her, then almost laughed at herself; this wasn't her at all. Raised practicing Qianli Fist among martial artists, she had always eaten bold and hearty. Refusing to torture herself, she dropped the act and dug in with big bites.

“难道你心里还装着徐家公子？今日也不怕告诉你，徐家搬到北边，徐家公子早已娶妻，现在已儿女双全。”朱晞颜面无表情，连女儿看都不看一眼。
Madam Xiang froze mid-bite in shock. This was not her refined Shuzhen; she ate like a country bumpkin! Tears welled again as she reached for her handkerchief, while serving maids traded stunned smirks.

千里寻想起，苦荞提到过与朱淑真青梅竹马的徐家公子，徐家公子对她来讲不过是个陌生人而已。
Smack! Zhu Xiyan slammed his chopsticks down on the table, making everyone jump.

“什么徐家公子？我被林觉打得失忆，什么都不记得。”
Mid-reach for a drumstick, Qianli Xun jerked her hand back, clapped a palm to her forehead, and put on a show. "Ow! My forehead wound hurts so bad!"

“不记得最好，那就老老实实过日子，不要想什么和理之事。”
Alarmed, Madam Xiang cried, "Shuzhen, are you alright? Somebody fetch wound salve at once!" A maid rushed over and dabbed ointment onto her forehead. "Does it still hurt?" Madam Xiang asked in anxiety.

朱晞颜的话令千里寻感到意外，女儿被打成这样，亲爹竟然一点儿都不心疼，这算什么事！
"Much better," Qianli Xun said, hoping the stunt might stir a father's pity or at least crack open the subject she needed.

“爹，他打我，还找商妓，您觉得我还能跟他过下去吗？”
To her surprise, it worked. Zhu Xiyan spoke at last, face still carved in stone. "I heard you want a mutual divorce from Lin Jue. Is that true?"

“哼！你以为我什么都不清楚？今日林觉来府衙找过我，将事情的缘由说清楚，也给我跪下承认错误。他把你打伤，你也把他打得不轻，他带商妓到家里，难道你就没错？要早些与他圆房，也没后面的事。”
With the door cracked open, Qianli Xun pushed straight through. "Father, look at the cut on my forehead; Lin Jue gave me that! And you can tell I'm nothing like your old daughter because he beat her unconscious, and when she woke up she became me, with zero memory of the past! On top of that, he brings brothel courtesans right into the house! Isn't that outrageous? Keeping a man like that as your son-in-law shoves your daughter into a fiery pit! Father, please step forward and arrange a mutual divorce!"

林觉竟然恶人先告状，千里寻气不打一处来，她站起来：“爹，您不能听他一面之词！”
"Are you still clinging to Young Master Xu?" Without even glancing at her, Zhu Xiyan spoke flat and cold. "I may as well tell you today: the Xu family moved north, and Young Master Xu married long ago. He already has a son and a daughter."

“难道我要听你一面之词不成？”
Recalling Kuqiao's story about that childhood sweetheart, a total stranger to Qianli Xun, she brushed it off. "Young Master who? Lin Jue beat the memory out of me; I don't remember anyone!"

千里寻更来气，认为这位父亲的关注点实在奇葩，继续强调道：“爹，林觉已将您以前的女儿打死，我是借助您女儿身体重生后的朱淑真，不是您真正的女儿，她的记忆我一点儿都没有，难道您没有发觉吗？仅凭这一点，您也该治林觉一个杀人之罪，把他关起来。”
"Better that you don't. Then settle down and live your married life, and forget this nonsense about mutual divorce."

朱晞颜抬眼仔细看这个女儿，确实与以往不同，可样貌还是，他又垂下眼帘：“重生之说，只不过是乡野之人胡乱编造，哄骗人而已，岂可当真？你不是淑真，又能是谁？”
Qianli Xun gaped. His own daughter came home battered, and he didn't even blink? What kind of father was this? "Father, he beats me and fools around with courtesans! How can I keep living with him?"

千里寻举起握紧的拳头：“我会拳法，以前的朱淑真可会？我打了林觉，以前的朱淑真只有被林觉打的份儿，您难道还认为您以前的女儿没被林觉打死？”
"Hmph! Think I'm in the dark? Lin Jue came to the prefecture office today, laid out the whole affair, and knelt to admit his fault. He injured you, yet you beat him just as hard! As for bringing a courtesan home, are you blameless? Had you consummated the marriage when you should have, none of this would have happened."

向夫人听了在一旁哭出声来：“呜呜……，淑真！你死得好惨啊！”
So Lin Jue had beaten her to the punch with his own twisted story! Fury boiled over as Qianli Xun shot to her feet. "Father, you can't listen only to his side of the story!"

丫鬟们听了这番对话，甚是诧异，因主人在此，都不敢交头接耳。
"And I'm supposed to listen only to yours?"

朱晞颜用平静而有力的声音说：“哭什么？事情没搞清楚，跟着瞎起哄！”
Stung by his warped logic, Qianli Xun pressed harder. "Father, Lin Jue beat your original daughter to death! I'm a reborn Zhu Shuzhen borrowing her body, not your real daughter! I don't hold a single shred of her memories; haven't you noticed? On that alone, you should charge Lin Jue with murder and lock him up!"

向夫人立刻停止哭声，继续抹眼泪。
Zhu Xiyan raised his eyes to study her: different in manner, yes, yet identical in face and frame. Dropping his gaze again, he scoffed, "Tales of rebirth are rustic superstition made up to fool simpletons. How could anyone take that as truth? If you aren't Shuzhen, who else could you be?"

接着朱晞颜说出了千里寻怎么都想不到的话：“既然你不是我女儿淑真，我更没有必要为你跟林觉和离而出面。你走吧，有你在，连顿饭都吃不好。”
Qianli Xun raised a clenched fist. "I know martial arts; did the old Zhu Shuzhen? I beat Lin Jue flat; the old Zhu Shuzhen could only take his fists! Do you still refuse to believe he killed her?"

千里寻瞪大眼睛，没想到朱淑真的父亲如此无情。
Beside them, Madam Xiang burst into loud sobs. "Sob... Shuzhen! What a tragic death!" Stunned by the clash, the maids kept their heads down, not daring to whisper.

“如果我是您的女儿呢？”
Zhu Xiyan spoke with calm, iron weight: "What are you crying for? Joining the uproar without knowing the facts!" Madam Xiang choked back her wails at once and dabbed her tears in silence.

“那就更不应该和离，好好听从父母安排，安心与林觉过日子。当初把你嫁给他，就是想让你明白生活没有那么多花前月下。”
Then Zhu Xiyan delivered the last words Qianli Xun ever expected: "Since you claim you are not my daughter Shuzhen, I have even less reason to step forward for your divorce. Leave; your presence spoils my meal."

千里寻被着实气到，她很想用拳头逼朱晞颜替自己出面主持和离之事，可谁让他在别人眼里是她的长辈。气已将力量集聚到拳头上，必须找个途径发泄出去。
Eyes wide, Qianli Xun could not fathom how heartless Zhu Shuzhen's father was. "And if I am your daughter?"

朱晞颜若无其事继续享用美食，千里寻气不打一处来，忍不住一拳砸在饭桌上，桌子顺间变成两半，桌上的碗碟和饭菜纷纷掉在地上。向夫人和朱晞颜朝一旁躲去，丫鬟们也被重生的小姐吓坏。
"Then even more reason not to divorce! Obey your parents' arrangement and live peaceably with Lin Jue. I married you to him so you would learn life isn't all flowers and moonlight."

发泄完，千里寻收起拳头，对朱晞颜说：“不管怎样，我还叫你一声爹，你既不愿替我出面，我自己想办法，就不信，还离不了这个婚！”说完，千里寻大踏步走了出去。
Rage flooded straight into Qianli Xun's fists. Part of her longed to make him agree at fistpoint, yet in everyone's eyes he stood as her elder. All that banked fury demanded an outlet. Watching Zhu Xiyan pick up his chopsticks and keep eating without a flicker of feeling, Qianli Xun snapped and drove her fist straight down onto the dining table. The solid wood cracked clean in two, sending bowls, platters, and food crashing to the floor. Zhu Xiyan and Madam Xiang scrambled back while the maids gasped in terror at the reborn young mistress.

屋里留下抹眼泪的向夫人和面不改色的朱晞颜，此时的他在心里不得不承认刚才的女儿已不是以前的淑真。
Pulling back her fist, Qianli Xun glared at Zhu Xiyan. "Like it or not, I still call you Father. If you won't stand up for me, I'll find my own way. I refuse to believe I can't get out of this marriage!" Turning on her heel, she strode out of the hall, leaving behind a weeping Madam Xiang and an outwardly composed Zhu Xiyan, who deep inside could no longer deny that this daughter was no longer the Shuzhen he had known.
