# 第13章 面见父亲怒砸桌 / Chapter 13: Meeting Father and Slamming the Table in Anger

---

到达朱府正院，天变得更黑。

When they reached the main courtyard of the Zhu residence, the sky had grown even darker.

---

正院里每隔一段距离挂有一个灯笼，黑夜根本无法遮住灯笼里散发出的亮光。

Lanterns hung at intervals throughout the main courtyard. The darkness couldn't hide the light spilling from them.

---

银杏将手中提着的灯笼抬起，吹灭里面的火光，带千里寻和苦荞穿过一个长廊，来到正屋的庭院。院子很大，铺着用石头打制的地砖。因灯光的原因能够清晰地看到两边分别栽种着三棵玉兰树，花开得正艳，有白色，紫色和黄色三种。

Yinxing lifted the lantern in her hand, blew out the flame, and led Qianli Xun and Kuqiao through a long corridor to the courtyard of the main hall. The courtyard was spacious, paved with stone tiles. Thanks to the lantern light, they could clearly see three magnolia trees planted on each side. The flowers were in full bloom—white, purple, and yellow.

---

千里寻被眼前的花树吸引，驻足欣赏一会儿。

Qianli Xun was drawn to the flowering trees and paused to admire them for a moment.

---

银杏催道："小姐若喜欢，明儿天亮时再来瞧，太阳底下，花更美。现在还是尽快去跟老爷太太用饭，晚了老爷会怪罪。"

Yinxing urged, "If you like them, Miss, you can come see them tomorrow at dawn. The flowers are even more beautiful in sunlight. For now, we should hurry to dinner with the master and madam. If we're late, the master will be upset."

---

千里寻只好启步，她边走边寻思，一会儿见到朱淑真的父亲，是该学朱淑真一样对待他，还是按照千里寻的模式？最终决定，先按照朱淑真的模式观察一会儿，再看情况。她在心里叮嘱自己，朱淑真的模式是先行礼，然后保持沉默。

Qianli Xun had no choice but to move on. As she walked, she pondered: when she met Zhu Shuzhen's father, should she treat him the way Zhu Shuzhen would, or follow her own style? She finally decided to observe the situation following Zhu Shuzhen's approach first. She reminded herself: Zhu Shuzhen's mode was to bow first, then remain silent.

---

正想着，已来到朱府正院用饭之地，好几个丫鬟端着盘子往屋里走。

Just as she was thinking, they arrived at the dining area of the main courtyard. Several maids were carrying trays into the room.

---

银杏站住，向屋内禀报："老爷，夫人，小姐已到。"

Yinxing stopped and announced, "Master, Madam, the miss has arrived."

---

传出一个男人的声音，冷静中带着冷漠："让她进来。"

A man's voice came from inside, calm yet cold. "Let her in."

---

银杏说："小姐，老爷允许您进去。"

Yinxing said, "Miss, the master has permitted you to enter."

---

千里寻走出两步，见苦荞未跟上，回头问："怎么不跟着？"

Qianli Xun took two steps forward, then noticed Kuqiao hadn't followed. She turned and asked, "Why aren't you coming?"

---

苦荞说："奴婢没资格进，我在外面等小姐。"

Kuqiao said, "I'm not qualified to enter, Miss. I'll wait outside for you."

---

银杏说："小姐放心，我会带苦荞到丫鬟房用饭。"

Yinxing said, "Don't worry, Miss. I'll take Kuqiao to the maids' quarters for dinner."

---

千里寻叹道："好吧，看来我只能单枪匹马会会我爹。"

Qianli Xun sighed, "Alright, looks like I'll have to face my father alone."

---

走进正屋，迎面被一个大大的屏风挡住，里面什么情况，根本无法看到。她停住脚步，在心里提醒自己："先稳住，要有礼貌，保持沉默。"

Walking into the main hall, a large screen blocked her view. She couldn't see what was inside. She stopped and reminded herself: *Stay calm. Be polite. Keep silent.*

---

一个丫鬟过来，小声提醒："小姐，这边。"

A maid came over and whispered, "This way, Miss."

---

千里寻跟着丫鬟拐过屏风，屋内的情景才尽收眼底。

Qianli Xun followed the maid around the screen, and the scene inside the room came into full view.

---

迎面是坐榻，上面放着方形的木桌，一边坐着向夫人，看到女儿进来，她并不说话，仅以微笑示意。另一边坐着的应该是朱淑真的爹，他手中拿着一本书，全神贯注，根本没有抬眼看女儿。

Facing them was a sitting couch with a square wooden table on top. On one side sat Madam Xiang. Seeing her daughter enter, she didn't speak, only smiled to acknowledge her. On the other side sat Zhu Shuzhen's father. He held a book, completely absorbed, not even glancing up at his daughter.

---

千里寻仔细瞧这位爹，他大约四十多岁，留着胡须，浓眉大眼，脸型甚好，皮肤也不错，年轻时应该是个美男子。

Qianli Xun studied her father carefully. He was in his forties, with a beard, thick eyebrows, and large eyes. His facial features were well-defined, and his skin was good. In his youth, he must have been a handsome man.

---

千里寻心想："原来朱淑真长得像她爹。"

Qianli Xun thought, *So Zhu Shuzhen looks like her father.*

---

千里寻上前行礼："爹，娘。"

Qianli Xun stepped forward and bowed. "Father, Mother."

---

向夫人点点头，依然不说话。

Madam Xiang nodded, still silent.

---

朱晞颜未抬眼，吩咐道："坐下吧。"

Zhu Xiyan didn't look up, but instructed, "Sit down."

---

千里寻找位置坐下，在心里不断叮嘱自己："保持沉默，保持沉默。"

Qianli Xun found a seat and kept reminding herself: *Stay silent. Stay silent.*

---

坚持了一会儿，甚觉无聊，看向进进出出摆饭的丫鬟，心想："三人吃饭，摆这么多菜，太奢侈了吧！"

After holding on for a while, she grew bored. Watching the maids coming and going to set the table, she thought, *Three people eating, yet so many dishes. That's too extravagant!*

---

向夫人还未找到机会把女儿的情况告知老爷，只因她在老爷面前不敢随意说话。女儿走进来时，她用心观察，淑真走路温婉可人，安安静静坐在那里，跟从前并无两样。向夫人的脸上不由露出笑容。

Madam Xiang hadn't yet found a chance to inform the master about her daughter's situation, because she didn't dare speak freely in front of him. When her daughter walked in, she observed carefully. Shuzhen walked gracefully and sat quietly, no different from before. A smile naturally appeared on Madam Xiang's face.

---

紧接着听到女儿说："爹，娘，饭已摆好，赶紧过去吃吧，吃得太晚，不容易消化，还容易发胖……"

Then she heard her daughter say, "Father, Mother, dinner is ready. Let's hurry over and eat. Eating too late makes it hard to digest, and it's easy to gain weight..."

---

向夫人流下一滴泪，眼前的女儿已经不是从前的淑真。她扭过头，悄悄用丝帕擦掉泪水。

A tear rolled down Madam Xiang's cheek. The daughter before her was no longer the Shuzhen she knew. She turned her head and quietly wiped away the tear with a silk handkerchief.

---

朱晞颜甚觉诧异，抬起眼，瞥向一旁的女儿。

Zhu Xiyan was quite surprised. He looked up and glanced at his daughter.

---

千里寻接触到这位父亲的目光，心里不由打了个寒颤，如此严厉，不敢再说下去，赶紧低下头，继续保持沉默。

Meeting her father's gaze, Qianli Xun couldn't help but shiver inwardly. His eyes were so stern. She didn't dare continue and quickly lowered her head, resuming her silence.

---

丫鬟过来，行礼道："老爷，夫人，小姐，饭已摆好。"

A maid came over and bowed. "Master, Madam, Miss, dinner is ready."

---

千里寻心想："终于好了！"第一个起身，步子还没迈出，看到向夫人摇头，用手示意她坐下。千里寻只能继续坐下，偷偷瞧朱晞颜。只见他放下书卷，从榻上下来，经过她面前。

Qianli Xun thought, *Finally!* She was the first to stand up, but before she could take a step, she saw Madam Xiang shaking her head, gesturing for her to sit back down. Qianli Xun had to sit back down and steal glances at Zhu Xiyan. She watched him put down his book, step off the couch, and pass in front of her.

---

向夫人也从榻上下来，走到女儿身边，拉起她，跟在朱晞颜身后，来到饭桌旁，把女儿安排在合适的位置。

Madam Xiang also stepped off the couch, walked to her daughter's side, helped her up, and followed Zhu Xiyan to the dining table, seating her daughter in the proper place.

---

千里寻坐下，长这么大第一次吃饭遇到如此不自在的氛围，她很不习惯，决定不再沉默。

Qianli Xun sat down. In all her life, she had never experienced such an uncomfortable dining atmosphere. She felt very uneasy and decided to break the silence.

---

听到朱晞颜说了句："可以开动。"终于忍不住，夹一块鸭肉给向夫人放进碗里："娘，吃这个。"

Hearing Zhu Xiyan say, "You may begin," she finally couldn't hold back. She picked up a piece of duck with her chopsticks and placed it in Madam Xiang's bowl. "Mother, eat this."

---

向夫人又向她摇摇头。

Madam Xiang shook her head at her again.

---

千里寻不想再理会，继续夹一块鸭肉，递到朱晞颜跟前，刚叫了声"爹"，见爹一脸阴沉，看上去很不高兴，便抽回手："爹不吃，我吃。"很快塞进自己嘴里，大嚼起来。

Qianli Xun didn't want to pay it any more attention. She picked up another piece of duck and held it out to Zhu Xiyan. She had just called out "Father" when she saw his gloomy expression. He looked very displeased. She withdrew her hand. "If you won't eat it, I will." She quickly popped it into her mouth and began chewing heartily.

---

旁边的丫鬟过来，在耳边叮嘱道："小姐，食不言，还要注意姿态。"

A maid beside her came over and whispered, "Miss, eat in silence, and mind your manners."

---

千里寻很不满，心想："在自己家里吃个饭，注意这注意那，一点儿都不自在，算了，谁让我有事求这位爹。看他那个样子，一个大男人吃个饭细嚼慢咽，连个吧唧声都没有，实在不像男人。"

Qianli Xun was quite annoyed. She thought, *Eating in my own home, and I have to mind this and that. Not comfortable at all. Forget it. I need something from this father. Look at him—a grown man eating so daintily, not even a smacking sound. Not manly at all.*

---

再观察向夫人，她夹菜的动作十分优雅，吃菜时微启朱唇，也是细嚼慢咽。千里寻只能学着照做。学了一遍，自己都觉得好笑，与她的风格相去甚远。她从小练习千里拳，跟练武之人混在一起，吃饭从来是豪放派，何时这么婉约过？她决定不能让自己难受，还是恢复正常，于是完全放开，大嚼大咽起来。

She observed Madam Xiang again. Her movements as she picked up food were graceful. When eating, she parted her lips slightly, also chewing slowly. Qianli Xun tried to imitate her. After one attempt, she found it funny herself—it was so different from her style. She had practiced Qianli Fist since childhood, hanging around martial artists. When eating, she had always been the uninhibited type. When had she ever been so delicate? She decided not to make herself uncomfortable and returned to her normal self. She completely let go and began wolfing down her food.

---

向夫人看到，停下筷子，一脸惊讶。这哪像自己的女儿淑真！那个吃相，如同山野村妇。她又流下眼泪，拿丝帕去擦。旁边伺候的丫鬟互相交换眼色，脸上露出讶异的笑容。

When Madam Xiang saw this, she stopped her chopsticks, her face full of surprise. How could this be her daughter Shuzhen! That way of eating—like a village woman from the mountains. Tears welled up again, and she reached for her handkerchief. The attending maids exchanged glances, surprised smiles on their faces.

---

只听"啪"的一声，原来是朱晞颜将筷子摔在桌上，周围的人都被吓坏。

Then came a loud *crack*. It turned out Zhu Xiyan had slammed his chopsticks onto the table. Everyone around was startled.

---

千里寻刚要去拽鸡腿，被这一声吓得抽回手，摸着额头，开始装疼："哎呦！我额头上的伤口好疼呀！"

Qianli Xun was just about to grab a chicken leg when the sound made her yank her hand back. She touched her forehead and started pretending to be in pain. "Ouch! My wound on my forehead hurts so much!"

---

向夫人担心道："淑真，你没事吧？来人，快拿创伤药来，给小姐涂上。"

Madam Xiang asked worriedly, "Shuzhen, are you alright? Someone, hurry and get the medicine for the miss's wound."

---

丫鬟迅速拿来创伤药，给小姐的额头涂了些。

The maid quickly brought the medicine and applied some to the miss's forehead.

---

向夫人又急切地问："还疼吗？"

Madam Xiang asked anxiously, "Does it still hurt?"

---

千里寻说："好多了。"她希望这么一装，能引起父亲关怀，或是尽快引出她需要的话题。

Qianli Xun said, "Much better." She hoped this act would arouse her father's concern or quickly lead to the topic she needed to bring up.

---

还别说，这招真灵，朱晞颜终于说话，表情依然严肃："听说你想跟林觉和离，可有此事？"

Surprisingly, the trick worked. Zhu Xiyan finally spoke, his expression still stern. "I hear you want an amicable divorce from Lin Jue. Is this true?"

---

既然话匣子打开，千里寻岂能放过这么好的机会？

Now that the conversation had opened, how could Qianli Xun let such a good opportunity slip away?

---

"爹，您也看到，我额头的伤就是林觉打的，您肯定也发现，我跟您以前的女儿很不一样，就是因为林觉把您的女儿打得晕死过去，醒来后，就成我这样，而且以前的事情都不记得。他还把商妓往家里带，您说过分不过分？让这样的人继续当您的女婿，岂不是把女儿往火坑里推？女儿求爹出面促成和离之事。"

"Father, you can see for yourself. The wound on my forehead was caused by Lin Jue. You must have noticed that I'm very different from your daughter before. That's because Lin Jue beat your daughter unconscious. After she woke up, she became like this, and she doesn't remember anything from before. He even brings merchant courtesans into the house. Isn't that going too far? Letting such a person continue to be your son-in-law—wouldn't that be pushing your daughter into a living hell? I beg you to step forward and help with the divorce."

---

"难道你心里还装着徐家公子？今日也不怕告诉你，徐家搬到北边，徐家公子早已娶妻，现在已儿女双全。"朱晞颜面无表情，连女儿看都不看一眼。

"Do you still have Young Master Xu in your heart? I might as well tell you today. The Xu family has moved north. Young Master Xu is already married and now has both a son and a daughter." Zhu Xiyan's expression was blank. He didn't even glance at his daughter.

---

千里寻想起，苦荞提到过与朱淑真青梅竹马的徐家公子，徐家公子对她来讲不过是个陌生人而已。

Qianli Xun remembered. Kuqiao had mentioned Young Master Xu, Zhu Shuzhen's childhood sweetheart. To her, Young Master Xu was nothing more than a stranger.

---

"什么徐家公子？我被林觉打得失忆，什么都不记得。"

"What Young Master Xu? I was beaten by Lin Jue until I lost my memory. I don't remember anything."

---

"不记得最好，那就老老实实过日子，不要想什么和理之事。"

"Not remembering is best. Then just live your life honestly and don't think about divorce."

---

朱晞颜的话令千里寻感到意外，女儿被打成这样，亲爹竟然一点儿都不心疼，这算什么事！

Zhu Xiyan's words surprised Qianli Xun. Her daughter had been beaten like this, yet her own father didn't feel the slightest bit of heartache. What kind of father was this!

---

"爹，他打我，还找商妓，您觉得我还能跟他过下去吗？"

"Father, he beats me and sees merchant courtesans. Do you think I can continue living with him?"

---

"哼！你以为我什么都不清楚？今日林觉来府衙找过我，将事情的缘由说清楚，也给我跪下承认错误。他把你打伤，你也把他打得不轻，他带商妓到家里，难道你就没错？要早些与他圆房，也没后面的事。"

"Hmph! You think I don't know anything? Lin Jue came to the prefectural office to see me today. He explained the situation clearly and knelt before me to admit his mistakes. He injured you, but you also injured him badly. He brought a merchant courtesan home, but are you without fault? If you had consummated the marriage earlier, none of this would have happened."

---

林觉竟然恶人先告状，千里寻气不打一处来，她站起来："爹，您不能听他一面之词！"

Lin Jue had actually accused her first. Qianli Xun was furious. She stood up. "Father, you can't just listen to his side of the story!"

---

"难道我要听你一面之词不成？"

"And should I only listen to your side?"

---

千里寻更来气，认为这位父亲的关注点实在奇葩，继续强调道："爹，林觉已将您以前的女儿打死，我是借助您女儿身体重生后的朱淑真，不是您真正的女儿，她的记忆我一点儿都没有，难道您没有发觉吗？仅凭这一点，您也该治林觉一个杀人之罪，把他关起来。"

Qianli Xun grew even angrier. She thought this father's focus was truly bizarre. She continued to emphasize, "Father, Lin Jue has already beaten your previous daughter to death. I am Zhu Shuzhen reborn in your daughter's body. I'm not your real daughter. I don't have any of her memories. Haven't you noticed? Based on this alone, you should punish Lin Jue for murder and lock him up."

---

朱晞颜抬眼仔细看这个女儿，确实与以往不同，可样貌还是，他又垂下眼帘："重生之说，只不过是乡野之人胡乱编造，哄骗人而已，岂可当真？你不是淑真，又能是谁？"

Zhu Xiyan looked up and studied his daughter carefully. She was indeed different from before, though her appearance was the same. He lowered his eyes again. "The idea of rebirth is just nonsense made up by country folk to fool people. How can it be taken seriously? If you're not Shuzhen, then who are you?"

---

千里寻举起握紧的拳头："我会拳法，以前的朱淑真可会？我打了林觉，以前的朱淑真只有被林觉打的份儿，您难道还认为您以前的女儿没被林觉打死？"

Qianli Xun raised her clenched fist. "I know martial arts. Did the previous Zhu Shuzhen know martial arts? I beat Lin Jue. The previous Zhu Shuzhen could only be beaten by Lin Jue. Do you still think your previous daughter wasn't beaten to death by Lin Jue?"

---

向夫人听了在一旁哭出声来："呜呜……，淑真！你死得好惨啊！"

Hearing this, Madam Xiang burst into tears. "Wuuu... Shuzhen! You died so miserably!"

---

丫鬟们听了这番对话，甚是诧异，因主人在此，都不敢交头接耳。

The maids were quite astonished by this conversation, but with the masters present, they didn't dare whisper among themselves.

---

朱晞颜用平静而有力的声音说："哭什么？事情没搞清楚，跟着瞎起哄！"

Zhu Xiyan said in a calm but forceful voice. "What are you crying about? The matter isn't clear yet, and you're joining in the chaos!"

---

向夫人立刻停止哭声，继续抹眼泪。

Madam Xiang immediately stopped crying and continued wiping her tears.

---

接着朱晞颜说出了千里寻怎么都想不到的话："既然你不是我女儿淑真，我更没有必要为你跟林觉和离而出面。你走吧，有你在，连顿饭都吃不好。"

Then Zhu Xiyan said something Qianli Xun never expected. "Since you're not my daughter Shuzhen, I have even less reason to step forward for your divorce from Lin Jue. Leave. With you here, I can't even finish a meal in peace."

---

千里寻瞪大眼睛，没想到朱淑真的父亲如此无情。

Qianli Xun's eyes widened. She never expected Zhu Shuzhen's father to be so heartless.

---

"如果我是您的女儿呢？"

"What if I were your daughter?"

---

"那就更不应该和离，好好听从父母安排，安心与林觉过日子。当初把你嫁给他，就是想让你明白生活没有那么多花前月下。"

"Then you should divorce even less. Obey your parents' arrangements and live peacefully with Lin Jue. When I married you to him, it was to make you understand that life isn't all moonlight and roses."

---

千里寻被着实气到，她很想用拳头逼朱晞颜替自己出面主持和离之事，可谁让他在别人眼里是她的长辈。气已将力量集聚到拳头上，必须找个途径发泄出去。

Qianli Xun was truly enraged. She really wanted to use her fists to force Zhu Xiyan to step forward and support her divorce. But who made him her elder in others' eyes? Her anger had gathered strength in her fists. She needed to find a way to release it.

---

朱晞颜若无其事继续享用美食，千里寻气不打一处来，忍不住一拳砸在饭桌上，桌子顺间变成两半，桌上的碗碟和饭菜纷纷掉在地上。向夫人和朱晞颜朝一旁躲去，丫鬟们也被重生的小姐吓坏。

Zhu Xiyan continued enjoying his meal as if nothing had happened. Qianli Xun was so angry she couldn't contain herself. She slammed her fist onto the dining table. The table split in two instantly. The bowls, plates, and food on it all fell to the ground. Madam Xiang and Zhu Xiyan dodged to the side. The maids were also terrified by the reborn miss.

---

发泄完，千里寻收起拳头，对朱晞颜说："不管怎样，我还叫你一声爹，你既不愿替我出面，我自己想办法，就不信，还离不了这个婚！"说完，千里寻大踏步走了出去。
