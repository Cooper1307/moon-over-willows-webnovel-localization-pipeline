## Chapter 201: Thrashing Master Jiang and Hunting the Jurchen

Bigger crowds only fired Bai Feng up. "Do not steal my fun, Young Master!"

Shen Jianchen folded his arms in silence alongside Chen Haoyu to watch. Early exchanges went smooth for Bai Feng, yet sheer numbers slowly pressed him tighter; nimble footwork kept him dodging blades cleanly without letting them pin him down. Watching nearby, Chen Haoyu wondered why on earth Prince Yi did not just drop his royal title to flatten Master Jiang instead of playing "Young Master Shen."

Mid-brawl, Master Jiang strode into the yard. Sizing up one guard holding off dozens to a standstill—and spotting the tall, aristocratic stranger watching calmly while normally cocky Chen Haoyu stood tucked behind him—Master Jiang smelled real trouble at once.

"Enough! Hold back!"

At his bark, gangsters sheathed steel and parted. Floating lightly back to ground, Bai Feng scoffed, "Just warming up—talk about spoilsports!"

Ignoring the guard, Master Jiang addressed the poised nobleman. "Never met before, Young Master—why bring trouble to Qiantang Gang?"

Before Shen Jianchen opened his mouth, Chen Haoyu crowed, "Young Master Shen is my backup, Master Jiang! Be smart and cough up my contract!"

Hardly buying that Chen Haoyu commanded a man like this, Master Jiang still kept his swagger. "In the underworld, my word is iron—since you wed Miss Zhu, your contract is yours anyway. Why fight through my gate?"

Hearing it confirmed straight from Master Jiang's lips—using Qianli Xun's marriage as a pawn!—lit the fuse under Shen Jianchen's temper. In one blur of light-foot speed, he crossed the yard and seized Master Jiang by the throat of his robe. "Hand over the contract!"

Gangsters surged forward, only for Bai Feng to flash right beside his prince, freezing them dead.

"Hold off—do not scratch the hero!" Master Jiang grinned easily despite the fist at his collar. "Fierce skill, Hero—beats even Chen Haoyu's Lu Yuanfeng! Contract coming right up!" Signaling a lieutenant to fetch it, he held the paper up. "Inspect away, Hero."

Taking it with one hand and releasing Master Jiang with the other, Shen Jianchen scanned every line—no mention of Miss Zhu anywhere on the page, cooling half his wrath. Handing it toward Bai Feng—"Take it"—he watched Chen Haoyu snatch it midair: "Mine anyway—I will hold it!"

Checking every word clean, Chen Haoyu bowed hastily. "Much obliged, Young Master Shen—got errands, will repay you another day!" And bolted straight out the gate—running smack into Scarface arriving late with three middling fighters.

"Late as usual!" Chen Haoyu scoffed, waving the slip. "Already got it! Waiting on you lot, dinner would freeze over!"

"Late, sure, still earned sweat bringing them!" Scarface whined.

Eyeing the three passable hires—nowhere near Lu Yuanfeng's league—Chen Haoyu frowned. "Where is Lu Yuanfeng?"

"Hit his house first—big padlock on his gate! Probably took your silver and skipped town!"

Careless with silver as ever, Chen Haoyu shrugged. "You three shadow me everywhere from now on—good pay!" Accepting at once, the three fell in behind as Chen Haoyu glared at Scarface's crew. "Quit leeching off me—useless when blades flash!" After frantic begging and oaths of loyalty, Chen Haoyu let them off with zero errand pay—"Pass me a fire-starter!"

Touching flame to the contract, Chen Haoyu watched it burn to ash on the dirt—a mountain lifted off his chest! "To Chunxiang Brothel!" Starved from a wasted wedding night and safe while Prince Yi tied Master Jiang up, he strutted off toward Chun Jinmo.

Back inside the yard, paying zero mind to Chen Haoyu's exit, Shen Jianchen turned icy eyes back onto Master Jiang. "Two errands brought me here: first, that contract; second, tell me WHY you rigged a trap for Chen Haoyu using Miss Zhu's wedding as the condition!"

Outmatched physically yet bound by underworld code, Master Jiang tried talking his way clear. "Admire top fighters like you, Hero—why not trade blows for drinks and become friends? If folks say one good thing about me, I treasure true talent..."

"Cut the crap and answer my question."

"A man of iron like you understands my spot," Master Jiang cupped his hands. "Took pay to do a job—cannot sell out a client. Stay and drink with my brothers instead?"

"Tell your boys to clear out—far as they can!" Shen Jianchen snapped.

Seeing no talking his way past a beating, Master Jiang barked, "Jump them!" and backed clear as armed gangsters swarmed in.

Paper tigers all—with Prince Yi stepping in beside Bai Feng, the yard emptied in minutes, groaning thugs littering the dirt while survivors scrambled back in terror. Spinning to bolt, Master Jiang felt Bai Feng's iron grip lock onto his collar. "Not going anywhere!"

"Beat him!" Shen Jianchen ordered.

A crisp storm of punches and kicks echoed across the yard—Bai Feng striking lightning-fast only on flesh that hurt like hell without killing, leaving Master Jiang howling until Bai Feng paused panting. "Enough, Young Master?"

Eyeing Master Jiang swollen purple head to toe—worse than Chen Haoyu had looked—Shen Jianchen nodded, crouched down beside him, and murmured, "Ready to tell me why you dragged Miss Zhu into your rigged bet?"

Grasping at last who this terror fought for, Master Jiang wheezed through split lips, "Walk... the underworld... word is everything... cannot tell you... who... Yet rest easy, Hero... aimed only at... Chen Haoyu... nothing against Miss Zhu..."

Admiring the boss's stubborn grit despite himself, Shen Jianchen stood up coldly. "Keep quiet—I will dig it out anyway. Aimed at Chen Haoyu or not, using Miss Zhu as bait to trap her with trash like him earned every bruise you got. Pull her into another stunt and I take your life!"

Striding out with Bai Feng through a silent lane of terrified thugs, they left Master Jiang groaning as his men rushed to carry him inside and smear salve across his welts.

"Go... dig up... who that man is!" Master Jiang hissed.

"Recognized him, Boss!" a lieutenant spoke up. "That is the exact guy who tried snatching Young Master Chen's bride yesterday!"

"Bride-snatcher?!" Everything clicked—no wonder he came tearing in for Miss Zhu! "Who IS he?!"

"No clue, Boss!"

"Find out—NOW!" Familiar with every underworld face and half the yamen too, Master Jiang sensed cold sweat—he had stepped squarely on a hidden dragon's tail!

Venting his fury done, Shen Jianchen skipped Youlin Retreat and ordered Bai Feng straight to Mihun Alley to track the Jurchen.

Driving along, Bai Feng called back, "Why not hand this to Division Commander Lin? One ring of troops around the alley and that Jurchen has zero escape!"

"Court talks with the Jurchens over returning the lost territories are delicate—keeping surface peace," Shen Jianchen explained. "Mobilize troops over one Jurchen and give them a pretext to tear up talks? Better just you and me scout quietly." *Not that one transmigrator could shift history's avalanche anyway.*

"Sharp thinking as always, Your Highness!"

Hitching the carriage outside Mihun Alley, they stepped inside trying to memorize turns—and within ten minutes wound thoroughly turned around inside a living labyrinth. Spotting a passerby, Shen Jianchen nodded to Bai Feng, who stepped forward politely asking directions out.

"Easy—follow me!" the local agreed cheerfully.

Walking alongside, Shen Jianchen dropped a casual probe. "Live in Mihun Alley long?"

"Born and bred—only locals survive these twists."

"Seen anyone around lately who looks different from ordinary Lin'an locals?"

Pausing mid-stride, the man snapped his fingers. "Now that you mention it—one guy! Dropped by a friend's house yesterday and saw a relative staying there whose features did NOT look Han at all!"

Talk about striking gold! At Shen Jianchen's nod, Bai Feng pressed a tael of silver into the man's hand. "Guide fee—take us to your friend's door."

Eyeing the silver warily, the local hesitated. "Who are you two, chasing my friend's kin?"

"Relax—that non-Han man may be an old friend of ours who took an injury and ducked in here, so we came looking," Shen Jianchen reassured him smoothly.

Weighing their honest faces against real silver, the local pocketed it. "He really WAS hurt! Come on—and try any tricks, my friend packs mean fists that will flatten you both!"

Winding through four more tight twists, they stopped before Lu Yuanfeng's gate—sealed shut with a hefty iron padlock.

"Bad luck—my friend left town," the local said.

"How do you know?"

"Habit of his: stays in Lin'an, never locks up; slaps the big padlock on only when traveling far."

"Nobody else home?"

"Lives solo—one mouth to feed."

Disappointment clouded Shen Jianchen's face—missed the Jurchen AND the mystery behind his Shen Fist! "What is your friend's name, in case we cross paths someday?"

"Lu Yuanfeng! Killer fists—only ever heard of him losing once, to his own master."

Name noted, Shen Jianchen asked no more. Guided back out to the avenue, he had zero appetite to return to Youlin Retreat—with his wedding trip to Bianliang looming in days, he needed one last sight of Qianli Xun! Impossible to barge into Chen Manor—wait: one man could pull it off! "Bai Feng—to the Dongfang Manor!"
