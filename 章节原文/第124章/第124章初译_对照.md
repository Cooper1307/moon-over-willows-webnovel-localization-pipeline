第124章 中秋佳节有亲情
## Chapter 124: Family Warmth on Mid-Autumn Night

中秋之日，富贵人家都在装饰家中亭台楼阁，临安的各大酒楼早已被预订完，只待中秋晚上饮酒玩月。
On Mid-Autumn Festival, wealthy households decked out their pavilions and towers, and every major tavern in Lin'an had been booked full long ago for evening drinking and moon-viewing.

中秋之夜，走在大街上，时时能听到丝簧之声从各大酒楼传出；夜间的街市人头攒动，花灯绚烂；每家每户也充满笙竽之音；儿童在小巷内嬉戏，你追我赶，好不热闹。
Once night fell, music of strings and reed pipes drifted from every tavern onto the street; crowds thronged beneath dazzling lanterns, flutes and pipes echoed from home after home, and children chased each other laughing down the lanes.

望月轩显得冷清而又落寞。
Wangyue Pavilion, by contrast, felt quiet and forlorn.

尽管厨房里也做了月饼；庭院中也摆上祭拜月神的桌案，到处也被布置；丫鬟和小斯们心情也不错，聚在一起玩乐，欣赏着天上的一轮圆月。
The kitchen had baked mooncakes, an altar to the Moon Deity stood set in the courtyard with decorations all around, and the maids and page boys were in good spirits, gathering to play and admire the full moon overhead.

可千里寻懒懒地躺在竹椅上，怎么都高兴不起来，她的心情让望月轩中的一切失去色彩。
Yet Qianli Xun lay sprawled across a bamboo chair, unable to summon a shred of cheer; her gloom drained the color from every corner of Wangyue Pavilion.

中秋节是团圆的日子，她却无家可归。望月轩不是她的家，她的家到底在哪里？是穿越前住过的二十二层？还是千里拳庄？亦或是穿越而来的朱府？它们是过，可又不是。
Mid-Autumn meant family reunion, yet she had no home to return to. Wangyue Pavilion did not feel like home—where was home, anyway? That twenty-second-floor apartment before crossing over? Qianli Fist Manor? Or Zhu Manor? Every one had been home, yet none was home now.

千里寻找不到答案，乔家不同意她与文渊在一起，搞得她近期伤感不已。尤其是热闹的节日，更衬托出落寞的心情。
With no answer in sight and the Qiao family blocking her and Wenyuan, sorrow weighed on her all the heavier amid the festival cheer outside.

一阵桂花的香气飘进闺房，千里寻才想起望月轩栽种着几棵桂树。桂花浓郁的香气，令她的心情稍微好些。
A drift of osmanthus scent floated into her chamber, reminding her several osmanthus trees grew in Wangyue Pavilion's garden. That rich fragrance lifted her spirits a fraction.

“韦嬷嬷吩咐厨房做的，刚刚出炉，还热着呢。”苦荞端一盘月饼从外面进来，“您吃一块。”
"Fresh out of the oven, baked by the kitchen on Matron Wei's orders—still piping hot!" Carrying in a plate of mooncakes, Kuqiao urged, "Have one, Miss."

“放下吧，我想去桂花树旁走走。”千里寻瞅着那盘月饼，毫无胃口，她从竹椅上起来，向闺房外走去。
"Set it down. I want to walk by the osmanthus trees." With zero appetite for mooncakes, Qianli Xun rose from the bamboo chair and stepped outside.

苦荞放好月饼，拿上披风，跟随小姐出去。
Setting the plate down, Kuqiao grabbed a cape and followed her mistress out.

院子里，夏果正与其他丫鬟、小厮跪拜月神，她在心里默默祈求月神保佑她有机会与乔公子在一起。
Out in the yard, Xiaguo knelt with the other maids and page boys bowing to the Moon Deity, whispering a silent prayer for a chance to be with Young Master Qiao.

刚拜完，抬起头，发现小姐与苦荞从身边经过，赶紧爬起来跟上：“小姐，您要去哪里？”
Rising from her bow to see Miss and Kuqiao walk past, she scrambled up to follow: "Where is Miss headed?"

千里寻没有说话。
Qianli Xun stayed silent.

苦荞帮忙回答：“去桂花树旁走走。”
Kuqiao answered for her: "For a walk by the osmanthus trees."

小姐不说话，显然心情不好，夏果不敢再多问，默默跟着。
Seeing her mistress mute in gloom, Xiaguo dared ask no more and trailed behind in silence.

望月轩的园子里共栽种三棵桂树，满树的桂花密密地开着，散发出诱人的香气，越靠近，香气越浓。
Three osmanthus trees stood in Wangyue Pavilion's garden, branches crowded tight with golden blossoms pouring out rich perfume—sweeter with every step closer.

千里寻绕着三棵桂树走着，希望浓浓的香气能带走她莫落的心情。
Qianli Xun circled the three trees, hoping the thick scent might wash away her low spirits.

曾经的她并非多愁善感之人，为何穿越而来会变得不一样？她无法解释。
She had never been the sentimental type before; why had crossing over turned her into such a softy? No answer came.

绕着桂树走完一圈，看到旁边有座亭子，千里寻又走到亭子里。她发现站在亭子里欣赏桂树反而能将它们尽收眼底，还能清晰地嗅到飘拂而来的阵阵香气。
Completing a lap around the trees, she spotted a pavilion beside them and stepped inside. From the pavilion she could take in all three trees at a glance while waves of osmanthus fragrance drifted straight across her face.

她刚搬进望月轩不久，与其说是没来得及到处走动，不如说是没心情。要不是桂花香，她才懒得走出闺房。
Since moving in, it was not that she had lacked time to explore the garden—she just had not had the mood. Without that osmanthus scent, she would not have left her room tonight.

站在亭子处，千里寻不由自主抬头仰望夜空，今晚的月亮好大好圆，她才发现这个亭子是赏月的绝佳位置，也才真正理解为何宅子名为望月轩。
Standing in the pavilion, she lifted her eyes to the night sky. What a huge, round moon! Only now did she realize this pavilion was the prime moon-viewing perch—and why the house bore the name Wangyue Pavilion, the Pavilion for Gazing at the Moon.

“把灯熄了。”
"Put out the lanterns."

两个丫鬟互相看看，不甚理解，又不敢多问，乖乖将亭子中的灯火熄灭。
Exchanging a puzzled look, the two maids asked no questions and snuffed the pavilion's lamps.

周围没有灯光影响，天上的月亮显得更加明亮。今晚，天公作美，不辜负人间佳节。不知朱府的人在做什么？也不知文渊是否还被关着？
With the glare gone, moonlight spilled twice as bright across the garden. Heaven had blessed this festival night with clear skies. What were the folks at Zhu Manor doing right now? And was Wenyuan still locked up?

正想着，韦嬷嬷走来，边走边说：“我到处找姑娘，听丫鬟说你朝这个方向来，果真在这里，快跟我到前院，有人来看姑娘。”
Just then Matron Wei hurried up calling, "Been hunting everywhere for you, Young Lady! A maid said you headed this way, and here you are—come to the front courtyard! Guests are here to see you!"

有人来？会是谁？千里寻在心里猜测着，应该不是仪王，他已答应不会再来望月轩，会不会是家人？
Guests? Who? Not Prince Yi—he had promised never to come back. Could it be her family?

千里寻急忙走下亭子：“谁来看我？”
Qianli Xun hurried down the pavilion steps: "Who came?"

韦嬷嬷卖着关子：“姑娘一会儿便知。”
Matron Wei kept the surprise: "You'll see in a minute, Young Lady!"

千里寻快步往园子外走去，心里猜想着来看她的人……
Quickening her steps out of the garden, Qianli Xun tried guessing who waited ahead...

到达前院，进入会客厅，千里寻看到向夫人和陈文心坐在那里，她竟然开心地流下眼泪，上前拜道：“娘，嫂嫂，谢谢你们来看我……”
Entering the reception hall in the front courtyard, she saw Madam Xiang and Chen Wenxin sitting there, and joyful tears sprang straight to her eyes as she bowed: "Mother, Sister-in-law, thank you for coming to see me..."

陈文心最怕见感动的场面，调侃道：“怎么这么见外？又是拜，又是谢，搞得我跟婆婆好像跟你不是一家人似的，要不是一家人，我们两个何必大晚上来看你？”
Allergic to tearjerkers, Chen Wenxin teased, "Why act like strangers—bowing and thanking us as if Mother and I aren't family! If we weren't family, why on earth would we trek over here at night?!"

“我何时跟你们见外？”千里寻破涕为笑，上前拉住向夫人，“娘，我好想您，虽然搬出来时间不长，可我感觉好像很久都没见到您。”
Laughing through her tears, Qianli Xun rushed up to hold Madam Xiang's hands: "I missed you so much, Mother! Haven't been out long, yet it feels like ages since I saw you."

向夫人含笑的眼中闪着泪光：“淑真，娘才来看你，你不会怪我吧？”
Tears shimmered in Madam Xiang's smiling eyes: "You don't blame Mother for taking this long to visit, Shuzhen?"

“怎么会？本应是女儿回家看望娘，女儿不孝，却让娘亲自跑来看我。”
"How could I? A daughter ought to go home to see her mother—I'm the unfilial one making Mother trek all the way out here."

向夫人站起来：“你瘦了，要多吃饭，娘不在你身边，要懂得爱惜自己。”
Madam Xiang stood and looked her over: "You've gotten thinner! Eat more—with Mother not beside you, take good care of yourself."

“娘不用操心我，我吃得好，睡得好。倒是您，鬓角的白发又多几根，定要减少思虑。”
"Don't fret over me, Mother—I eat great and sleep great. Look at you, though, more silver hairs at your temples! Quit worrying so much."

陈文心笑道：“婆婆，您与淑真都不要站着，我们坐下聊，坐下才好说话。”
Chen Wenxin chuckled: "Mother, Shuzhen, quit standing around—sit down so we can have a proper chat!"

大家都坐下。
Everyone took seats.

韦嬷嬷吩咐丫鬟把刚刚做好的月饼端来摆上。
Matron Wei signaled a maid to bring out the fresh mooncakes.

向夫人招呼道：“嬷嬷也坐下，我们一家人聊会儿，今儿是中秋，大家一处才热闹。”
Madam Xiang beckoned: "Sit with us too, Matron—it's Mid-Autumn, merrier with the whole family together."

韦嬷嬷也坐下：“夫人与少夫人尝尝月饼，也让姑娘讨个吉利。姑娘独自住在外面，正好逢中秋节，她心里不好受，我尽知，可又做不了别的，只会做月饼。做月饼时，心想，若有人今晚来看姑娘，定要让他吃块月饼，只要来人吃下月饼，以后姑娘的日子肯定会圆圆满满。”
Taking a seat, Matron Wei said, "Try these mooncakes, Madam and Young Madam, and bring our Young Lady good fortune! Living out here alone on Mid-Autumn, I knew she felt lonely, yet baking mooncakes was all I could do. While baking, I wished that whoever visited tonight would eat a mooncake so our Young Lady's life ahead turns round and complete!"

向夫人与陈文心二话没说，各拿起一块，用心吃着，每吃一口，都在心中祝福淑真的未来顺利圆满。
Without a second thought, Madam Xiang and Chen Wenxin each picked up a mooncake and ate every bite with heart, silently blessing Shuzhen's future with fullness and peace.

千里寻被两位亲人吃月饼的动作感动，忽觉自己伤感实在没有必要。家在哪里？她已找到答案，不管她走到哪里，只要有家人的地方就有家。
Watching her two loved ones eat those mooncakes thawed Qianli Xun's chest, making all her earlier gloom look silly. Where was home? She had her answer now: wherever family stood, there was home.

陈文心第一个吃完，用丝帕擦了擦嘴角：“淑真，你可知我刚才吃月饼时想什么？”
Finishing first, Chen Wenxin dabbed her lips with a silk handkerchief: "Shuzhen, guess what I was thinking while eating that mooncake?"

“嫂嫂的心事，我怎能猜到？”
"How could I guess Sister-in-law's thoughts?"

“我在想，说不定跟乔家的婚事未成是件好事，肯定有更好的姻缘等着你，临安又不是只有乔家好，我们陈家也不错。”
"I was thinking maybe the Qiao match falling through is a blessing in disguise—a better match is waiting for you! The Qiaos aren't the only fine family in Lin'an; our Chen house isn't shabby either!"

“嫂嫂难道想让我嫁到陈家？”
"Sister-in-law wants me to marry into the Chen family?"

“未尝不可！”
"Why not?!"

“嫂嫂说笑，陈浩宇那样的，我可瞧不上。”
"Quit joking, Sister-in-law—a guy like Chen Haoyu couldn't pass my inspection in a million years!"

“陈家又不是只有浩宇一个男儿，我三叔的儿子浩飞不错，不爱做生意，只喜欢读书，不敢说学富五车，也是饱读诗书，你可以考虑一下。”
"Haoyu isn't the only Chen boy! My third uncle's son Haofei is a gem—hates business, loves books, well-read as can be. Worth considering!"

“我看嫂嫂是闲得慌，竟然当起媒人来。”
"Sounds like Sister-in-law is bored stiff, playing matchmaker!"

陈文心对向夫人说：“婆婆，您说呢？”
Chen Wenxin appealed to Madam Xiang: "What do you think, Mother?"

向夫人也已吃完月饼，笑着批评道：“我全当听个笑话，你三叔的儿子可比淑真整整小五岁，呵呵……！”
Finishing her mooncake, Madam Xiang laughed while chiding her: "I'm taking it as a joke—your third uncle's boy is five whole years younger than Shuzhen! Haha!"

陈文心不再撮合，本就是开个玩笑：“我也是想逗淑真乐乐。”
Dropping the match, Chen Wenxin grinned: "Just trying to make Shuzhen laugh!"

“亏嫂嫂想得出来，用这种事情逗我，就不怕我当真？”
"Leave it to Sister-in-law to joke about something like that—aren't you afraid I'll take you up on it?"

“当真才好呢！”
"Even better if you did!"

嫂嫂提议不佳，用心却好，千里寻心情又好许多：“娘，您与嫂嫂过来，爹会不会不高兴？”
Bad match aside, her sister-in-law's warm intent cheered Qianli Xun up more: "Coming over here tonight, Mother and Sister-in-law—won't Father be mad?"

向夫人说：“傻孩子，你爹要是不高兴，我跟你嫂嫂能出来？他对你严厉，把你赶出来，都是表演给外人看，心里时刻不挂念你。”
Madam Xiang smiled: "Silly child, if your father objected, could we have walked out the gate? Acting stern and sending you out was all a show for outsiders—he worries about you every single hour."

陈文心也说：“是啊，你要理解公爹，他是临安知府，要面子，又是朱家的掌舵人，出这么大的事，总得有个态度，方可对他人有个交代。”
Chen Wenxin chimed in: "True! Cut Father-in-law some slack—he's the Prefect of Lin'an and head of the Zhu house. With a scandal this loud, he had to put on a stern stance to answer to outsiders."

其实千里寻早已不埋怨朱晞颜，她故意装作生气的样子：“爹就知道对别人有个交代，他难道不知，对自己的女儿也需有个交代？把我赶出家门，只会让大家更加嘲笑我。”
Qianli Xun had long stopped blaming Zhu Xiyan, though she put on a mock pout: "Father only cares about answering to outsiders—doesn't he owe his own daughter an answer too? Throwing me out just gave folks more to laugh at."

韦嬷嬷听出自家姑娘已不再埋怨老爷，甚是欣慰：“古人云，福祸相依，姑娘遭此一劫，说不定是件好事，可以看作是老天给姑娘出的考题。我家姑娘面对全城人议论，面不改色，说明心态好，人又坚强；遇乔家那档子事，没有哭哭啼啼，要死要活，反而理智面对。我要是考官，绝对给姑娘打个优等，呵呵……！”
Hearing no real grudge left in her voice, Matron Wei beamed: "As the ancients say, fortune and misfortune lean on each other. Maybe this storm is Heaven's exam for you, Young Lady! Facing a whole city's gossip without flinching shows true grit; handling the Qiao mess with a cool head instead of weeping and threatening suicide shows real wisdom. If I were the examiner, I'd give you top honors! Haha!"

韦嬷嬷半开玩笑半认真的话，千里寻竟然听进去，暗暗决定，要谨慎对待人生的每一道难题，用最适合的方法解决。她与文渊之事，目前最好的解题方法只能靠等待。
Half joke, half wisdom—Matron Wei's words struck home. Qianli Xun vowed inside to tackle every test life threw at her with clear judgment. Right now, the best answer for Wenyuan was patience.

提到乔家，向夫人小心问：“淑真，与乔家公子之事，你是怎么想的？”
Bringing up the Qiao family with care, Madam Xiang asked, "What are your thoughts on Young Master Qiao, Shuzhen?"

“我给自己半年时间，若半年没有进展，我便……便放弃。”
"I'm giving myself half a year. If nothing moves in half a year, I'll... let go."

向夫人点点头：“想得对，只是苦了你。”
Madam Xiang nodded: "Sensible—just hard on you."

“你能用理智面对此事，嫂嫂很欣慰，像你这么好的姑娘，乔家不上门提亲，是他们的损失，以后也不知会便宜哪家小子。”陈文心想，若是自家弟弟稍微长进些，还能张得了口，谁让浩宇是那副德行，还跟淑真合不来，想将淑真说给弟弟的想法怎么都开不了口。
"I'm glad you're keeping a cool head, Shuzhen. A gem like you—the Qiaos are the losers for backing out! Lucky whichever boy wins you next." Chen Wenxin silently sighed: if only her own brother had half a spine she would pitch him right now, yet with Haoyu's wild streak and the way those two clashed, she could not even open her mouth.

“嫂嫂是自家人，当然会夸我。”
"Sister-in-law's family—of course you praise me."

﻿千里寻觉得望月轩不再落寞，已充满亲人之间浓浓的情意。
Wangyue Pavilion no longer felt cold or empty; family warmth filled every corner.

苦荞也觉开心，小姐并不孤单，朱家的人依然关心她。
Kuqiao felt happy too—Miss was not alone, and the Zhu family still cared for her.

﻿夏果却高兴不起来，小姐只等半年时间，若等不来，该怎么办？难道真的要放弃乔公子？若小姐放弃乔公子，她该如何是好？
Only Xiaguo could not raise a smile. Half a year—what if nothing happened in half a year and Miss gave up Young Master Qiao? What on earth would Xiaguo do then?
