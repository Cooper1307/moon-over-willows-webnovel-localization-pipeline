## Chapter 161: Two Awkward Encounters

Madam Qian went on, "Your father is the head of this household. Since he agreed, your mother and I have no reason to object. No matter how many concubines you take, remember this: once Zhiyu marries into our family, she will be your primary wife, and you must hold her in your heart. Go on now, take Zhiyu for a stroll."

Qiao Wenxian had meant to use taking a concubine to vent his bitterness over the marriage, yet the gesture fell flat. Left with no choice but to obey, he gave a courteous nod. "After you, Lady Miao."

Miao Zhiyu rose to her feet. This was her first time spending time alone with Young Master Qiao, and she treasured the chance enough to set aside her displeasure over the concubine for the moment. She stepped beside him and said, "After you, Young Master Qiao."

Saying no more, Qiao Wenxian descended from the pavilion and headed toward the gardens. Miao Zhiyu hurried after him, striving to keep pace shoulder to shoulder. Watching their son lead his bride-to-be off for a walk, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi beamed with delight, and Madam Qin looked just as pleased.

Madam Qian turned her gaze back to the playing field just as Chen Wenxin swung her club and knocked a ball straight into a hole. "Young Madam Zhu has a fine hand at chuiwan," she remarked. "She may not match what I could do in my youth, yet among women of her generation, skill like hers is rare."

"Young Master Chen plays well too," Madam Qin added. "It looks like the brother and sister will take the first match."

Chatting and laughing, the three women went on watching the game.

Trailing behind his master and Lady Miao, Nan Shuang could not stop thinking about Qiao Wenxian taking Xiaguo as a concubine. He needed to forget Xiaguo fast, or how could he face serving his master in the days ahead? The quickest way to forget one person, he decided, was to put someone new in his heart.

Who should take that place? His eyes fell on the maid walking beside him. He had paid her little mind before, yet looking closer today, she was not bad-looking at all. Leaning in, he murmured, "Hey, what is your name?"

The maid took no offense. Her mistress was about to marry Young Master Qiao, so getting on good terms with his attendants was only sensible. "My name is Youfan," she whispered back.

"I am Nan Shuang. Let us be friends from now on, alright?" he asked with care.

"Sure," Youfan agreed without hesitation.

That single crisp word lifted a weight off Nan Shuang's chest, and a satisfied smile spread across his face.

Meanwhile, Shen Jianchen left the field and entered the gardens. After searching a short while, he spotted the two he was looking for walking toward him. Quickening his stride to meet them, he flicked a glance at Qianli Xun before addressing Mengrong. "You made Brother search quite a while."

Happy that he had come looking for her on his own, Qiao Mengrong smiled. "What did Brother need me for?"

"The chuiwan matches have started, and I want to join. It takes a team of two, and I do not care to partner with anyone else. Will you pair up with me?"

The request put Qiao Mengrong in a bind. "I would love to team up with Brother, truly, yet I do not know how to play chuiwan, and I do not like it either."

"No matter. I can teach you," Shen Jianchen said in an encouraging tone. "Chuiwan is simple enough. You will pick it up in two swings."

Qiao Mengrong stayed hesitant, afraid clumsy playing would embarrass him.

Qianli Xun saw straight through Shen Jianchen's little scheme. "Since Mengrong feels uncomfortable, Your Highness would do better to skip the match and sit in the pavilion watching the others play with her."

Disappointment washed over Shen Jianchen. "Youqi Jushi makes a fair point. Do not force yourself, Mengrong. Brother will sit the match out."

Unwilling to let him down, Qiao Mengrong hit on an idea. "Brother, why not team up with Shuzhen instead?"

Hearing the exact suggestion he had angled for, Shen Jianchen put on a reluctant front. "Very well. Seeing that Youqi Jushi is your closest confidante, I suppose I can make do."

Qianli Xun understood what drove him, yet watching him play on Mengrong's innocence and kindness to steer her like that set her temper flaring. She shot Shen Jianchen a glare, then turned a warm smile on Mengrong. "I am not in the mood for chuiwan today, Mengrong. I just want to stay and chat with you."

Guilt pricked at Shen Jianchen. He had no business using Mengrong for his own private feelings. "Forget it. I will not play after all. Go on chatting with Youqi Jushi."

He turned to head back to the pavilion, only for Qiao Mengrong to catch his sleeve. "Do not go, Brother. Watch me handle this." She turned to plead with Shuzhen. "Shuzhen, please say yes? Brother offering to partner with you does you great honor. He is a prince, so you ought to show him some face."

Qianli Xun hesitated, then froze as Qiao Wenxian and Miao Zhiyu came into view, strolling side by side as they admired the garden scenery. She had thought seeing them together would leave her untouched, yet a sharp ache pierced her chest. Letting go of old feelings was never as simple as saying the words.

As the pair drew near, she raised her voice on purpose. "Alright! I agree to team up with Prince Yi!"

Shen Jianchen blinked in surprise. Much as he had wanted her agreement, her sudden turnaround struck him as odd. One look over his shoulder showed him Wenyuan and Lady Miao walking up, and he understood at once why she had said yes. His spirits sank lower still.

Qiao Wenxian saw Shuzhen and knew she had seen him too. Remembering her icy manner earlier and how she had handed Xiaguo over to be his concubine, anger flared inside him. A branch of blooming peach blossoms hung right beside the path, so he reached up and plucked a flower. "Zhiyu, this blossom is lovely. Let me place it in your hair."

Startled and thrilled to hear him use her given name and offer to pin a flower in her hair, Miao Zhiyu glanced past him, spotted Youqi Jushi a short distance away, and grasped the truth at once. She might as well play along. They were betrothed, after all, with no reason to shy from onlookers. "Young Master Qiao... no, Wenyuan, the flower you picked is lovely indeed. I adore it."

Qiao Wenxian tucked the peach blossom beside Miao Zhiyu's chignon. Against the soft petals, her delicate face looked more radiant than ever.

Seeing the man she had once loved place a flower in another woman's hair left Qianli Xun fighting a wave of awkwardness.

Worried for her friend, Qiao Mengrong tugged on her brother's sleeve and called out in a low voice, "Brother."

Shen Jianchen had seen the whole display. Beneath his own pang of gloom, a spark of satisfaction flickered. Wenyuan's clumsy attempt to provoke jealousy would backfire and only push Qianli Xun to give up on him for good. Reading Mengrong's silent plea from her tug on his robe, he stepped forward to greet them. "Wenyuan, I looked everywhere for you earlier, and here you are keeping a fair lady company."

Miao Zhiyu dropped into a swift curtsy. "Your Highness Prince Yi, Zhiyu pays her respects."

"No need for ceremony, Lady Miao," Shen Jianchen said. "In ten days you will marry into the Qiao family. Once you are one of them, there is no call to stand on formality with me." For a brief moment, his own betrothal slipped his mind. If Qianli Xun and Wenyuan had loved each other so much and still ended up torn apart, it could only mean she was meant for him. A smile crept onto his face.

Prince Yi's warm words lifted Miao Zhiyu's spirits higher still. She had first fallen for Young Master Qiao over his literary talent, yet now she saw how much family standing mattered too. Once she became a Qiao bride, aside from Princess Jiahe, who in all of Lin'an could rival her?

Qiao Wenxian had no heart to banter with Prince Yi. He flicked a glance at Shuzhen, who strolled up arm in arm with Princess Jiahe without a flicker of distress on her face.

Once they drew close, Miao Zhiyu curtsied to the princess as well. "Had I known Youqi Jushi and Your Highness were strolling in the gardens, I would have joined you. I do not care for chuiwan in the slightest."

"Nor do I," Qiao Mengrong replied. "Still, with Young Master Qiao by your side, I would hardly dare steal you away."

Unwilling to stand in silence, Qianli Xun forced out a remark so Wenyuan would think she cared nothing at all. "I happen to enjoy chuiwan. I just agreed to team up with Prince Yi. I have not had a proper workout in ages, so this is a good chance to stretch my limbs."

Shen Jianchen bit back a laugh. Her cover for her awkwardness was clumsy at best. Who on earth would chime in when you bragged about loving chuiwan to two women who hated it?

Sure enough, nobody picked up the thread.

Unwilling to leave the woman he loved hanging in silence, Shen Jianchen stepped in. "Since Youqi Jushi has agreed to partner with me, shall we head to the field now?"

Qianli Xun had been itching to leave anyway. Wenyuan stood mute, staring holes into her, and lingering longer might upset Miao Zhiyu. "When Prince Yi commands, Shuzhen dares not refuse."

Shen Jianchen glanced at Qiao Wenxian. "Wenyuan, care to come watch?"

Qiao Wenxian tore his gaze from Shuzhen's face. "If Your Highness is taking the field, I shall of course go cheer you on."

The five of them retraced their steps together.

Hardly had they stepped out of the gardens onto the chuiwan grounds when Lin Jue approached with his bride, seeking out Prince Yi. Having arrived late, Lin Jue had gone to Prince Yi's pavilion first, only to find the personal guard Bai Feng standing alone inside. Word from bystanders sent him out toward the gardens, and now he stumbled right into the prince himself. Stepping forward, he bowed low. "Your Highness, this humble official arrived late and failed to pay respects in time. Please forgive the oversight."

"This is a chuiwan field, not Lin'an Prefecture's yamen," Shen Jianchen said. "Rank does not count here. Relax, Division Commander Lin; no need to be so stiff."

"Your Highness speaks true. I shall obey your instructions."

Shen Jianchen glanced at the woman beside Lin Jue. She was no peerless beauty, yet she was more than a match for Lin Jue. "Is this your new wife?"

Before Lin Jue could answer, the bride dipped into another deep curtsy. "On our wedding day, my husband told me Your Highness graced us with your presence. I had hoped to pay my respects then, yet Your Highness departed early, so only today can I fulfill the rite."

Shen Jianchen recalled that day: bored to tears after watching the couple bow to heaven and earth, he had slipped out on the excuse of visiting the latrine. "Mengrong had an urgent matter that day, so I had to leave ahead of time. I hope you will excuse it. Besides, we will have plenty of chances to meet."

"Your Highness is most gracious," the bride replied. "My husband brought me here today for the sole purpose of paying respects together. Only after greeting Your Highness is our wedding truly complete."

Qianli Xun already knew Lin Jue had remarried, and she sighed inwardly. No matter the era, divorced men hated being lonely and rushed straight into another match. Plain as day, this bride felt quite content with Lin Jue, chiming in to assert her place as lady of the house.

Seeing her brother had no wish to drag out small talk with Lin Jue's bride, Qiao Mengrong stepped in. "I hear you are not from Lin'an. Where is your maiden home?"

Guessing this must be Princess Jiahe, whom her husband had made a point of mentioning, the bride curtsied again. "Indeed, I am not from Lin'an. My family hails from Weizhou."

"That is quite a distance. Marrying so far from home cannot be easy. How should we address you?"

"My maiden surname is Bai, and my given name is Meiruo."

"We are all around the same age, so from now on we will call you Meiruo."

"To be called so by Your Highness is Meiruo's great fortune."

Pleased to see his new bride winning the princess's favor, Lin Jue threw a smug glance at Zhu Shuzhen, thinking, You turned up your nose at me, yet plenty of others know my worth. Look at us now: I have a fresh bride, while you are tangled in a pile of messes. Heh!

Since Mengrong had said "we," Qianli Xun could not stay silent. She stepped forward. "If Her Highness calls you Meiruo, I shall call you Meiruo too. We are all friends now. Whenever you feel like strolling through Lin'an's markets, just invite us along."

Delighted to gain another acquaintance, Bai Meiruo asked, "May I ask how to address you, miss?"

"She is the famed poetess of Jiangnan," Qiao Mengrong introduced her, "known far and wide as Youqi Jushi."

Lin Jue had already told Bai Meiruo about his divorced former wife, though she had never expected to run into her here. Seeing no hint of awkwardness on the other woman's face, Bai Meiruo relaxed at once. "Back in Weizhou, a dear friend of mine loved Youqi Jushi's ci lyrics and spoke of them nonstop, so your name stayed fresh in my mind. Meeting you today is the blessing of three lifetimes."

Miao Zhiyu stepped up as well. "My surname is Miao, given name Zhiyu. Call me whatever you like."

"Pleased to meet you, Lady Miao," Bai Meiruo said, pouring on the flattery. "What struck me most upon arriving in Lin'an was how fresh and lovely the young women here are. Yet meeting you two and Her Highness today shows me everyone I saw before was plain as mud."

"I am not from Lin'an, though," Qiao Mengrong clarified.

"Even so, Your Highness was born with peerless beauty, and nourished by Lin'an's water and air, you look lovelier still."

Shen Jianchen had heard enough syrupy chatter. Just then, the referee's call rang out across the field: "Young Master Chen takes the Middle Tally first! Victory!"

Everyone looked toward the green, where Chen Haoyu hoisted his club and jumped up and down in glee.

Qianli Xun scoffed inside: How childish!

Chen Wenxin stepped up to the prize table, lifted a jadeite bangle from the tray, and held it high for all to see with a triumphant grin. Seeing her sister-in-law happy warmed Qianli Xun's heart too.

Chen Haoyu's strutting rubbed Shen Jianchen the wrong way. One moment ago he had let himself believe Qianli Xun would end up his; one look at Chen Haoyu snapped him back to reality, the betrothal blocking his path like a wall.

"Youqi Jushi, come play chuiwan with me at once. Let us take on Young Master Chen for a round."

"Give me a moment to get ready at my pavilion," Qianli Xun agreed on the spot, more than eager to escape this circle of awkward company.
