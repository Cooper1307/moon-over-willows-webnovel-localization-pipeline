# Chapter 27: Discussing Fate Change at the Nunnery

---

从外面看，两个山洞没有任何差异，唯一不同的是，净光法师的山洞窗户半开着。千里寻试图通过窗户瞧见里面的情景，可里面光线很暗，什么也瞧不到。

From the outside, the two caves looked no different. The only difference was that the window of Master Jingguang's cave was half-open. Qianli Xun tried to see inside through the window, but it was too dark inside to see anything.

---

千里寻纳闷，刚才的山洞没有开窗户，里面挺亮堂，为何住持的山洞光线如此暗？仔细一想，明白过来，刚才的山洞内点着几盏油灯，怪她忙着应付三件事，没有在其他事情上用心思。

Qianli Xun was puzzled. The previous cave didn't have an open window, yet it was quite bright inside. Why was the abbess's cave so dark? After thinking carefully, she figured it out. The previous cave had several oil lamps lit. She blamed herself for being too busy dealing with the three tasks to pay attention to other things.

---

正想着，一只蝴蝶从窗户里飞出来。千里寻从未见过这么大这么漂亮的蝴蝶，它的翅膀是蓝色的，翅膀的边缘呈黑色，扑闪着来到她面前，绕着她飞了一圈儿。她伸出手想要去抓，结果蝴蝶落在她的手背上，搞得她不忍心再去捉它，只用爱怜的目光欣赏着蝴蝶之美。

Just as she was thinking, a butterfly flew out from the window. Qianli Xun had never seen such a big and beautiful butterfly. Its wings were blue with black edges. It fluttered to her face and circled around her. She reached out to catch it, but the butterfly landed on the back of her hand. She couldn't bear to catch it anymore. She could only admire its beauty with loving eyes.

---

慈玄法师向洞内说："住持，女施主已在洞外等候。"

Master Cixuan spoke toward the cave. "Abbess, the female donor is waiting outside the cave."

---

慈玄话音刚落，窗户被人从里面紧紧关上。洞门被打开，对慈玄说悄悄话的女尼出现在面前："让她进来吧。"

As soon as Cixuan finished speaking, the window was tightly closed from inside. The cave door was opened. The nun who had whispered to Cixuan appeared before her. "Let her in."

---

蝴蝶仿佛能听懂人话，立刻飞走。

The butterfly seemed to understand human speech. It immediately flew away.

---

千里寻向蝴蝶飞去的方向望了一眼，才向山洞内走去。

Qianli Xun glanced in the direction the butterfly flew, then walked into the cave.

---

苦荞也跟着往里走，被慈玄法师拦住："你不必进去，在外面候着。"

Kubiao followed her inside, but Master Cixuan stopped her. "You don't need to go in. Wait outside."

---

苦荞只好站住，等慈玄离开后，她才有心情欣赏山洞外的院落。

Kubiao had to stop. After Cixuan left, she finally had the mood to appreciate the courtyard outside the cave.

---

院子打扫得很干净，没有过多布置，仅有一棵她叫不上名字的树，开着玫红色的花朵，实在美观。树下有石桌和石凳，上面落满花瓣。

The courtyard was swept very clean with no elaborate decorations. There was only a tree she couldn't name, blooming with rose-red flowers. It was truly beautiful. Under the tree were a stone table and stone stools, covered with fallen petals.

---

苦荞过去将石凳上的花瓣吹落，坐下耐心等待。

Kubiao went over and blew the petals off the stone stool, then sat down and waited patiently.

---

千里寻走进山洞后，女尼将洞门关上。

After Qianli Xun walked into the cave, the nun closed the cave door.

---

眼前立刻漆黑一片，无法往前走，她站在原地，适应一会儿，隐约看到女尼站在身边："师傅，住持在哪里？"

It was immediately pitch black before her eyes. She couldn't move forward. She stood in place and adapted for a while. She vaguely saw the nun standing beside her. "Master, where is the abbess?"

---

女尼没说话，走到一旁，用火折子将油灯点着，洞内立刻亮堂起来。

The nun didn't speak. She walked to the side, used a fire starter to light the oil lamp, and the cave immediately brightened.

---

千里寻发现这个山洞比另一个深邃许多，她站的地方只是个外洞，除桌子、两盏油灯、一个木盒外，基本没什么布置。心想，住持该不会是位不喜见光之人？

Qianli Xun discovered this cave was much deeper than the other one. Where she stood was just an outer cave. Besides a table, two oil lamps, and a wooden box, there was basically no decoration. She thought, "Could the abbess be someone who doesn't like light?"

---

女尼说："女施主，住持正在苦修，本不接见外客，只因有只蝴蝶不畏黑暗飞入洞中，落在住持的身上，让她老人家领悟到有人来此，甚觉有缘，才愿与你见上一面。"

The nun said, "Donor, the abbess is in ascetic practice. She doesn't receive outside visitors. It's just that a butterfly wasn't afraid of the darkness and flew into the cave. It landed on the abbess, making her realize someone had come here. She felt it was a strong destiny and was willing to meet you."

---

千里寻想起刚才的蝴蝶，内心生起感激之情。常言道：飞蛾扑火，它能来到光线暗淡之处，实属难得，住持如此想，倒也正常。无论如何，蝴蝶帮了她，以后有机会遇见……

Qianli Xun thought of the butterfly just now and felt grateful. As the saying goes, a moth flying into a flame is rare. It could come to a place with dim light, which was truly unusual. The abbess thinking so was normal. Anyway, the butterfly had helped her. If she had the chance to meet it again in the future...

---

千里寻想不出如果再次遇见那只蝴蝶，她需要怎么做，才能表达她的心意。

Qianli Xun couldn't figure out what she should do if she met that butterfly again to express her feelings.

---

千里寻向里面的山洞瞧瞧，黑漆漆的，什么也看不到，如果没有女尼在一旁，她绝对会感到害怕。

Qianli Xun looked toward the inner cave. It was pitch black. She couldn't see anything. If the nun wasn't beside her, she would definitely be scared.

---

女尼伸出手说："女施主，住持在里面，牵着我的手，跟我来。"

The nun reached out her hand. "Donor, the abbess is inside. Hold my hand and follow me."

---

千里寻伸手拉住女尼，跟着朝洞内深处走去，边走边说："住持法号净光，我以为她老人家喜欢亮光，没想到她喜欢在这么暗的地方待着。"

Qianli Xun reached out and held the nun's hand, following her deeper into the cave. As she walked, she said, "The abbess's dharma name is Jingguang (Pure Light). I thought she liked bright light. I didn't expect she likes staying in such a dark place."

---

女尼解释道："女施主是世俗之人，当然不明白苦修之人也要修炼无光之苦。"

The nun explained, "Donor, you're a worldly person. Of course, you don't understand that those who practice asceticism must also cultivate the suffering of no light."

---

千里寻不明白苦修为何意，也不愿在此事上多费脑筋，毕竟与自己关心之事没有多大关系，她只觉在黑暗中走一会儿，女尼站住，她也跟着站住。

Qianli Xun didn't understand what ascetic practice meant. She didn't want to spend too much brainpower on this matter. After all, it wasn't very related to what she cared about. She just felt that after walking in the darkness for a while, the nun stopped, and she followed.

---

女尼说："住持，女施主到了。"

The nun said, "Abbess, the female donor has arrived."

---

传来住持的声音："慈空，让她坐下吧。"显然是位老妇人的声音。

The abbess's voice came. "Cikong, let her sit down." It was clearly an old woman's voice.

---

慈空拉着千里寻："女施主再往前一步。"千里寻按照要求向前迈进一步。

Cikong pulled Qianli Xun. "Donor, one more step forward." Qianli Xun took one step forward as requested.

---

慈空又说："可以坐下。"千里寻摸着黑坐下，一分一毫不差坐在一个软软的蒲团之上。

Cikong said again, "You can sit down." Qianli Xun sat down in the dark. She sat perfectly on a soft prayer cushion without any deviation.

---

坐好后，她听到慈空走向一旁，不由从心里慨叹，这位师傅在黑暗中行走的本事实在厉害。

After sitting properly, she heard Cikong walk to the side. She couldn't help but sigh in her heart. This master's ability to walk in the darkness was truly amazing.

---

好不容易见到净光法师，千里寻不愿错过机会。尽管看不到住持的尊容，却能感觉到她就坐在对面。

Having finally met Master Jingguang, Qianli Xun didn't want to miss the opportunity. Although she couldn't see the abbess's face, she could feel her sitting opposite.

---

"住持，我遇到人生难题，无路可走，不得已才上山向您老人家请教，希望您为我指一条明路。"

"Abbess, I've encountered a life difficulty. There's no way out. I had no choice but to come up the mountain to seek your guidance. I hope you can point out a clear path for me."

---

"你可是对婚姻不满，想摆脱现在的夫君？"

"Are you dissatisfied with your marriage and want to get rid of your current husband?"

---

千里寻在心里叹道："住持果然厉害，一下子猜中我心中所想。"赶紧回道："是的。"

Qianli Xun sighed in her heart. "The abbess is truly amazing. She guessed what I was thinking right away." She quickly replied, "Yes."

---

"历来女子嫁鸡随鸡，嫁狗随狗，难有机会摆脱不如意的婚姻，即使摆脱，名声多少会受到影响，你有没有想过一旦离开现在的夫君，以后该如何是好？"

"Throughout history, women have always been expected to follow their husbands no matter what. They rarely have the chance to get rid of an unsatisfactory marriage. Even if they do, their reputation will be affected to some extent. Have you thought about what to do once you leave your current husband?"

---

"我没想那么多，只想离开这段婚姻，在我看来，只有离开，我的人生才能重新开始。"

"I haven't thought that much. I just want to leave this marriage. In my view, only by leaving can my life restart."

---

住持沉默着，黑暗中的空气仿佛凝固一般。

The abbess was silent. The air in the darkness seemed to freeze.

---

千里寻慢慢适应洞内的黑暗，朦胧中见到一个黑影坐在对面，旁边站着的应该是带她进来的慈空。住持不说话，千里寻却不想这么沉默下去。

Qianli Xun slowly adapted to the darkness in the cave. In the dimness, she saw a dark shadow sitting opposite. Beside it should be Cikong, who had brought her in. The abbess didn't speak, but Qianli Xun didn't want to remain silent like this.

---

"您刚才猜中我来此的目的，说明您的确有知过去和未来的能力，您能不能告诉我，我到底还有没有机会摆脱这段婚姻？还有，我未来的命运如何？"

"You just guessed my purpose for coming here. It shows you truly have the ability to know the past and future. Can you tell me if I still have a chance to get rid of this marriage? Also, what is my future destiny?"

---

住持终于又开口说话，她并未直接回答千里寻的问题："从你刚才进来，我已感受到你体内的神识不属于这个时代，先给我说说是怎么回事？"

The abbess finally spoke again. She didn't directly answer Qianli Xun's question. "Since you came in just now, I've felt that the divine consciousness in your body doesn't belong to this era. First, tell me what happened?"

---

千里寻更觉得这位她看不清容貌的住持实在厉害，便将自己遇到地震，成为朱淑真的过程毫无保留详细说明。最后问道："我还想知道，我到底是朱淑真还是千里寻？我在这个时代会像朱淑真一样？还是会有千里寻的命运？"

Qianli Xun felt even more that this abbess whose face she couldn't see was truly amazing. She detailed without reservation how she encountered an earthquake and became Zhu Shuzhen. Finally, she asked, "I also want to know, am I Zhu Shuzhen or Qianli Xun? In this era, will I be like Zhu Shuzhen? Or will I have Qianli Xun's destiny?"

---

住持反问道："你认为自己是谁？"

The abbess asked in return, "Who do you think you are?"

---

千里寻犹豫一会儿，坚定地回道："在别人眼中我是朱淑真，可我认为我依然是千里寻？"

Qianli Xun hesitated for a while, then replied firmly, "In others' eyes, I'm Zhu Shuzhen. But I think I'm still Qianli Xun."

---

"我朝有位能人写下一篇文章，名为《太上感应篇》，首句便是'福祸无门，惟人自召'，意思是福与祸不会随意找任何人，都是人自己的行为招感而来。佛门也讲五戒十善，均在告诫世人，想要改变命运最好的办法是改变自己的行为，而不是拜佛求神，以及星象占卜之类……"

"In our dynasty, a capable person wrote an article called 'Taishang Ganying Pian' (Treatise on the Response of the Tao). The first sentence is 'Fortune and misfortune have no door; they are summoned by people themselves.' It means fortune and misfortune don't randomly find anyone. They are attracted by people's own actions. The Buddhist school also talks about the Five Precepts and Ten Virtues. They all warn the world that the best way to change destiny is to change your own behavior, not to worship Buddha and seek gods, or astrology and divination..."

---

千里寻对《太上感应篇》，以及什么五戒十善根本不感兴趣，认为住持只是对她进行说教而已，直到住持说出"改变命运"四个字，她才提起兴趣，认真往下听。

Qianli Xun wasn't interested at all in the "Taishang Ganying Pian" or the Five Precepts and Ten Virtues. She thought the abbess was just lecturing her. It wasn't until the abbess said the four words "change destiny" that she became interested and listened carefully.

---

"你成为朱淑真还是千里寻，是由你的行为决定，行为又由观念操纵，你要从改变观念做起，在这个时代才能过上千里寻的生活。"

"Whether you become Zhu Shuzhen or Qianli Xun is determined by your behavior. Behavior is controlled by concepts. You need to start by changing your concepts. Only then can you live Qianli Xun's life in this era."

---

千里寻感到庆幸，她虽有着朱淑真的外表，观念倒与她截然不同，看来她正走在正确的道路上。

Qianli Xun felt fortunate. Although she had Zhu Shuzhen's appearance, her concepts were completely different from hers. It seemed she was on the right path.

---

"不过，这身体并不是千里寻的，千里寻的命运多多少少会受到朱淑真命运的牵制，你更需努力才对，婚姻便是牵制之一。据我观察，朱淑真的命格中情路颇为坎坷，你借着她重生，自然情路不会一帆风顺，可又与她不同，只要你经历过几段情路，人生自然顺畅。"

"However, this body isn't Qianli Xun's. Qianli Xun's destiny will be affected by Zhu Shuzhen's destiny to some extent. You need to work even harder. Marriage is one of the constraints. According to my observation, Zhu Shuzhen's path of love in her fate chart is quite bumpy. Since you borrowed her to be reborn, naturally your path of love won't be smooth. But it's different from hers. As long as you experience several paths of love, your life will naturally go smoothly."

---

千里寻在心里问道："这么暗的山洞，也不知净光法师是如何观察的？该不会她能看到我，而我看不到她？难道修佛之人都会有这种能力？"

Qianli Xun asked in her heart, "In such a dark cave, I wonder how Master Jingguang observes? Could it be that she can see me, but I can't see her? Do all Buddhist practitioners have this ability?"

---

又听住持说她需经历几段情路，高兴道："住持的意思是我会摆脱现在的婚姻？"

She heard the abbess say she needed to experience several paths of love. She happily said, "Does the abbess mean I will get rid of my current marriage?"

---

"我虽帮你看命，但天机不可泄露，有些事情不能明说。你只需记着，无论遇到任何事，要坚持正思正念，便可逢凶化吉。"

"Although I help you read your destiny, heavenly secrets cannot be revealed. Some things cannot be said explicitly. You just need to remember, no matter what you encounter, stick to righteous thoughts and intentions, and you can turn misfortune into fortune."

---

"住持的话真是醍醐灌顶，我定会按照您所说坚持正思正念。"

"The abbess's words are truly enlightening. I will definitely follow your advice and stick to righteous thoughts and intentions."

---

"既然你我有缘，我再帮帮你。"住持停了一会儿，继续说道，"你选了王右丞的《竹里馆》，解诗的内容我详细阅览，发现你的解析很独到，你说这首诗不是赞美隐居之人品性高洁，而是书写诗人的无奈以及对时局的批判。我认为有一定道理，像王右丞这等有才华之人，应该被重用，为百姓谋福，却独自隐居山林，实在是人才浪费，也的确与当时的时局密不可分。你能有如此见识，说明你是位有主见的女子，既有主见，便有了掌控自己命运的第一步。我还发现你的字里行间并未提到诗中非常关键的一个字：'竹'字，于是敏锐地感觉出竹能给你带来好运，回去后找一处有竹林的地方住上一个月，自然能体会到妙处。"

"Since you and I are destined, I'll help you more." The abbess paused for a while, then continued, "You chose Wang Youcheng's 'Bamboo Lodge.' I carefully read your poetry interpretation and found your analysis very unique. You said this poem isn't praising the noble character of a recluse, but expressing the poet's helplessness and criticism of the current situation. I think there's some truth to it. Someone as talented as Wang Youcheng should be reused to seek fortune for the people. Instead, he lives in seclusion in the mountains and forests alone. It's truly a waste of talent. It's also indeed closely related to the current situation at that time. You having such insight shows you're a woman with your own opinions. Having opinions is the first step to controlling your own destiny. I also noticed that between your lines, you didn't mention a very key character in the poem: 'bamboo.' So I keenly felt that bamboo can bring you good luck. After going back, find a place with a bamboo forest and live there for a month. You'll naturally experience the wonder."

---

千里寻终于明白，住持让人解诗并非只为解诗，而是要从解诗的内容中了解解诗之人。

Qianli Xun finally understood. The abbess asking people to interpret poetry wasn't just for the interpretation itself. It was to understand the person interpreting from the content.

---

"好，我定照做。"

"Okay, I will definitely do it."

---

"你的茶做得实在粗糙，我一口都没喝，知道我为何还愿见你吗？"

"Your tea was really rough. I didn't drink a single sip. Do you know why I was still willing to see you?"

---

"晚辈不知，还请住持明示。"

"This junior doesn't know. Please enlighten me, Abbess."

---

"只因你做的茶和我年轻时第一次做得一模一样。慈玄又将你做茶时的动作向我详细描绘，尤其是你把温盏的水倒在地上，呵呵，我第一次也是如此，让我甚觉亲切。"

"It's just that the tea you made was exactly the same as the first time I made it when I was young. Cixuan also described in detail your movements when making tea to me. Especially when you poured the water for warming the bowl on the ground. Hehe, the first time I did the same. It made me feel quite at home."

---

千里心想，也是她运气好，瞎猫撞上死耗子，否则今日哪有机缘与净光法师聊天儿。

Qianli Xun thought, "It's also her good luck. Just dumb luck. Otherwise, how could she have the opportunity to chat with Master Jingguang today?"

---

"你离开时，慈空会给你一条红绸，记得将自己的名字写在上面，至于写哪个名字由你决定。后山有棵千年神柳，你把红绸绑在神柳西边的树枝上，必然会对你产生助益。"

"When you leave, Cikong will give you a red silk. Remember to write your name on it. As for which name to write, it's up to you. There's a Millennial Divine Willow on the back mountain. Tie the red silk on the branches on the west side of the divine willow. It will definitely help you."

---

听到千年神柳，千里寻感到命运实在神奇，她来的那个时代有棵千年神柳，来到的这个时代又碰到一棵，看来她的命运与神柳有着密切的关系。

Hearing about the Millennial Divine Willow, Qianli Xun felt destiny was truly magical. In the era she came from, there was a Millennial Divine Willow. In the era she came to, she encountered another one. It seemed her destiny was closely related to the divine willow.

---

住持不再说话，传来慈空的声音："女施主，可以走了。"

The abbess didn't speak anymore. Cikong's voice came. "Donor, you can go now."

---

慈空走近，伸出手："牵着我的手。"

Cikong came closer and reached out her hand. "Hold my hand."

---

千里寻起身，摸到慈空的手，跟着往出走。

Qianli Xun stood up, felt Cikong's hand, and followed her out.

---

从黑暗中来到有灯光之处，光线刺眼，千里寻不由闭上双眼。奇怪的是，慈空竟然一点儿感觉都没有。

Coming from darkness to a place with lamplight, the light was dazzling. Qianli Xun couldn't help but close her eyes. Strangely, Cikong didn't seem to feel it at all.

---

千里寻闭一会儿眼睛慢慢缓过来，问道："师傅，从里面出来，您怎么一点儿都不怕光？"

Qianli Xun closed her eyes for a while and slowly recovered. She asked, "Master, coming out from inside, why aren't you afraid of light at all?"

---

"习惯就好。"慈空简单回道，然后从桌上的木盒里取出一条红绸递给千里寻，"拿着，到正殿找敲木鱼的小师傅，她会帮你指明神柳所在之处。"

"You'll get used to it," Cikong replied simply. Then she took out a red silk from the wooden box on the table and handed it to Qianli Xun. "Take this. Go to the main hall and find the young sister who strikes the wooden fish. She'll help point out where the divine willow is."

---

千里寻接过红绸，谢过后，推门离开山洞。

Qianli Xun took the red silk. After thanking her, she pushed the door and left the cave.

---

等千里寻离开后，慈空把门关上，将另一盏油灯点着，端着进入内洞，里面一下子亮堂起来。

After Qianli Xun left, Cikong closed the door, lit another oil lamp, and carried it into the inner cave. It immediately brightened.

---

洞内有一个石炕，石炕上铺着柔软的褥子，上面盘腿坐着的正是住持净光法师，她微闭双目，双手放置膝头。

Inside the cave was a stone bed. On the stone bed was a soft cushion. Sitting cross-legged on it was exactly the abbess Master Jingguang. Her eyes were slightly closed, and her hands were placed on her knees.

---

慈空把油灯放好后说道："师父每次都不点灯，搞得信众都不知道您长什么样。"

After putting down the oil lamp, Cikong said, "Master, you don't light the lamp every time. It makes the believers not know what you look like."

---

净光法师说："这样才好，省得我下山一趟都把我围起来，不得自由。"

Master Jingguang said, "This is better. It saves me from being surrounded every time I go down the mountain, with no freedom."

---

"师父，您给那位姑娘说的方法真管用吗？"

"Master, does the method you told that girl really work?"

---

"管不管用，不在于我，关键在她。世人愚昧，才会相信占卜之类，如果明心见性，才知道这命啊都是自己造作而成。"

"Whether it works or not doesn't depend on me. The key is her. People in the world are ignorant, so they believe in divination and such. If they see their true nature and enlighten their minds, they'll know that destiny is created by themselves."

---

"师父就是明心见性之人，所以才能看得透，说得透。"

"Master, you are someone who has seen your true nature and enlightened your mind. That's why you can see through and speak through."
