# 第27章 尼姑庵里谈改命
# Chapter 27: Pure Light in Darkness

---

从外面看，两个山洞没有任何差异，唯一不同的是，净光法师的山洞窗户半开着。千里寻试图通过窗户瞧见里面的情景，可里面光线很暗，什么也瞧不到。

From the outside, the two mountain caves looked identical. The only difference was that the window of Master Jingguang's chamber was half-open. Qianli Xun tried to peer inside, but the interior was shrouded in pitch blackness.

---

千里寻纳闷，刚才的山洞没有开窗户，里面挺亮堂，为何住持的山洞光线如此暗？仔细一想，明白过来，刚才的山洞内点着几盏油灯，怪她忙着应付三件事，没有在其他事情上用心思。

She found it odd. The previous cave had no open windows, yet it was quite bright. Why was the abbess's quarters so dark? After a moment's thought, the realization struck her—the first cave had been lit with several oil lamps. She scolded herself internally for being so preoccupied with passing the three trials that she had failed to pay attention to her surroundings.

---

正想着，一只蝴蝶从窗户里飞出来。千里寻从未见过这么大这么漂亮的蝴蝶，它的翅膀是蓝色的，翅膀的边缘呈黑色，扑闪着来到她面前，绕着她飞了一圈儿。她伸出手想要去抓，结果蝴蝶落在她的手背上，搞得她不忍心再去捉它，只用爱怜的目光欣赏着蝴蝶之美。

Just then, a butterfly—enormous, impossibly blue, edged in velvet black—drifted out of the window. It circled her once. Twice. She reached for it, but it landed softly on the back of her hand, wings opening and closing like a slow breath. She froze. Couldn't bring herself to grab it. Could only gaze at it with fond admiration.

---

慈玄法师向洞内说："住持，女施主已在洞外等候。"

Master Cixuan called toward the cave entrance, "Abbess, the woman is waiting outside."

---

慈玄话音刚落，窗户被人从里面紧紧关上。洞门被打开，对慈玄说悄悄话的女尼出现在面前："让她进来吧。"

The moment Cixuan finished speaking, the window was shut tight from within. The door creaked open, and the nun who had whispered to Cixuan earlier stepped out. "Let her in."

---

蝴蝶仿佛能听懂人话，立刻飞走。

As if understanding human speech, the blue butterfly immediately took flight.

---

千里寻向蝴蝶飞去的方向望了一眼，才向山洞内走去。

Qianli Xun cast a final glance in the direction it had flown before stepping across the threshold.

---

苦荞也跟着往里走，被慈玄法师拦住："你不必进去，在外面候着。"

Kuqiao attempted to follow her inside, but Master Cixuan blocked her path. "You need not enter. Wait out here."

---

苦荞只好站住，等慈玄离开后，她才有心情欣赏山洞外的院落。

Kuqiao stopped in her tracks. Only after Cixuan departed did she finally have the leisure to admire the quiet courtyard before the cave.

---

院子打扫得很干净，没有过多布置，仅有一棵她叫不上名字的树，开着玫红色的花朵，实在美观。树下有石桌和石凳，上面落满花瓣。

The yard was immaculately swept, devoid of lavish decorations save for a single nameless tree laden with vibrant magenta blossoms. It was a breathtaking sight. Beneath its branches sat a stone table and matching stools, softly dusted with fallen petals.

---

苦荞过去将石凳上的花瓣吹落，坐下耐心等待。

Kuqiao walked over, blew the petals off a stone stool, and sat down to wait patiently.

---

千里寻走进山洞后，女尼将洞门关上。

Inside, the nun closed the heavy door the moment Qianli Xun stepped in.

---

眼前立刻漆黑一片，无法往前走，她站在原地，适应一会儿，隐约看到女尼站在身边："师傅，住持在哪里？"

Black. Total, absolute black. Unable to move forward, she froze in place, waiting for her eyes to adjust. Vaguely, she sensed the nun standing beside her. "Master, where is the Abbess?"

---

女尼没说话，走到一旁，用火折子将油灯点着，洞内立刻亮堂起来。

Without a word, the nun walked to the side and struck a fire-reed to light an oil lamp. Soft yellow light instantly filled the space.

---

千里寻发现这个山洞比另一个深邃许多，她站的地方只是个外洞，除桌子、两盏油灯、一个木盒外，基本没什么布置。心想，住持该不会是位不喜见光之人？

Qianli Xun realized this cave was far deeper than the first one. Where she stood was merely an outer chamber, sparse and meagerly furnished with only a table, two oil lamps, and a wooden box. *Could the Abbess be someone who loathes the light?* she wondered.

---

女尼说："女施主，住持正在苦修，本不接见外客，只因有只蝴蝶不畏黑暗飞入洞中，落在住持的身上，让她老人家领悟到有人来此，甚觉有缘，才愿与你见上一面。"

The nun spoke softly: "Donor, the Abbess is currently engaged in deep ascetic cultivation and normally refuses all visitors. It was only because a butterfly unfearful of the dark flew into the cave and landed upon her that Her Eminence realized a fateful guest had arrived. Recognizing this karmic tie, she agreed to grant you an audience."

---

千里寻想起刚才的蝴蝶，内心生起感激之情。常言道：飞蛾扑火，它能来到光线暗淡之处，实属难得，住持如此想，倒也正常。无论如何，蝴蝶帮了她，以后有机会遇见……

Remembering the brilliant blue butterfly outside, Qianli Xun felt a surge of profound gratitude. Moths flew toward fire, not away from it. For a butterfly to seek out darkness? That was something else entirely. It was only natural for the Abbess to view it as an omen. Regardless, the butterfly had helped her, and if she ever encountered it again...

---

千里寻想不出如果再次遇见那只蝴蝶，她需要怎么做，才能表达她的心意。

Qianli Xun chuckled inwardly, unable to imagine how she could possibly repay a butterfly.

---

千里寻向里面的山洞瞧瞧，黑漆漆的，什么也看不到，如果没有女尼在一旁，她绝对会感到害怕。

Looking toward the inner cavern, she saw only an endless, pitch-black abyss. Had the nun not been beside her, she would have felt a creeping sense of dread.

---

女尼伸出手说："女施主，住持在里面，牵着我的手，跟我来。"

The nun extended her hand. "Come. She's inside. Hold my hand and follow me."

---

千里寻伸手拉住女尼，跟着朝洞内深处走去，边走边说："住持法号净光，我以为她老人家喜欢亮光，没想到她喜欢在这么暗的地方待着。"

Qianli Xun reached out, took the nun's hand, and let herself be guided deeper into the subterranean gloom. As they walked, she couldn't help but remark, "The Abbess's dharma name is Jingguang—Pure Light. I assumed she would favor brightness. I never expected her to dwell in such darkness."

---

女尼解释道："女施主是世俗之人，当然不明白苦修之人也要修炼无光之苦。"

The nun explained gently, "As a worldly person, it is natural you do not understand. Those who walk the path of asceticism must also master the hardship of utter darkness."

---

千里寻不明白苦修为何意，也不愿在此事上多费脑筋，毕竟与自己关心之事没有多大关系，她只觉在黑暗中走一会儿，女尼站住，她也跟着站住。

Qianli Xun didn't fully grasp the concept of asceticism, nor did she care to waste her mental energy pondering it—it had little to do with her purpose here anyway. After walking blindly through the dark for a short while, the nun came to a halt, and Qianli Xun stopped alongside her.

---

女尼说："住持，女施主到了。"

"Abbess," the nun announced softly, "the lady donor has arrived."

---

传来住持的声音："慈空，让她坐下吧。"显然是位老妇人的声音。

An aged, raspy voice echoed from the darkness ahead: "Cikong, bid her sit."

---

慈空拉着千里寻："女施主再往前一步。"千里寻按照要求向前迈进一步。

Cikong guided Qianli Xun's arm. "One more step." Qianli Xun complied.

---

慈空又说："可以坐下。"千里寻摸着黑坐下，一分一毫不差坐在一个软软的蒲团之上。

"You may sit now," Cikong instructed. Qianli Xun reached down in the dark and sat—landing squarely upon a soft prayer cushion with effortless precision.

---

坐好后，她听到慈空走向一旁，不由从心里慨叹，这位师傅在黑暗中行走的本事实在厉害。

As she settled, she heard Cikong's footsteps fading to the side. The woman moved through blackness like it was daylight. Unsettling.

---

好不容易见到净光法师，千里寻不愿错过机会。尽管看不到住持的尊容，却能感觉到她就坐在对面。

Having finally gained an audience with Master Jingguang, Qianli Xun was determined not to waste the opportunity. Though she couldn't see the Abbess's face, she could distinctly feel a powerful presence seated directly across from her.

---

"住持，我遇到人生难题，无路可走，不得已才上山向您老人家请教，希望您为我指一条明路。"

"Abbess," Qianli Xun began earnestly, "I have reached a dead end in my life. Having nowhere else to turn, I climbed this mountain to seek your wisdom. I beg you to point out a clear path for me."

---

"你可是对婚姻不满，想摆脱现在的夫君？"

"Are you dissatisfied with your marriage?" the raspy voice asked calmly from the dark. "Do you seek to break free from your husband?"

---

千里寻在心里叹道："住持果然厉害，一下子猜中我心中所想。"赶紧回道："是的。"

*The Abbess really is divine! She read my mind instantly!*

She hurriedly replied, "Yes! Exactly!"

---

"历来女子嫁鸡随鸡，嫁狗随狗，难有机会摆脱不如意的婚姻，即使摆脱，名声多少会受到影响，你有没有想过一旦离开现在的夫君，以后该如何是好？"

"Since ancient times, a woman is expected to abide by her marriage for better or worse, with little hope of escaping an unhappy union," the Abbess said slowly. "Even if she manages to break free, her reputation will inevitably be tarnished. Have you given any thought to how you will live once you leave your husband?"

---

"我没想那么多，只想离开这段婚姻，在我看来，只有离开，我的人生才能重新开始。"

"I haven't thought that far," Qianli Xun admitted frankly. "I only know I want out of this marriage. To me, only by leaving can my life truly begin anew."

---

住持沉默着，黑暗中的空气仿佛凝固一般。

Silence.

The air thickened. Froze.

---

千里寻慢慢适应洞内的黑暗，朦胧中见到一个黑影坐在对面，旁边站着的应该是带她进来的慈空。住持不说话，千里寻却不想这么沉默下去。

As her eyes gradually adapted to the gloom, Qianli Xun could vaguely discern a dark silhouette sitting cross-legged before her, with Cikong standing quietly beside it. Seeing that the Abbess remained silent, Qianli Xun refused to let the moment slip away.

---

"您刚才猜中我来此的目的，说明您的确有知过去和未来的能力，您能不能告诉我，我到底还有没有机会摆脱这段婚姻？还有，我未来的命运如何？"

"Since you guessed my purpose the moment I stepped inside, it proves you truly possess the power to divine the past and future. Tell me—do I still have a chance to escape this marriage? And what does the future hold for me?"

---

住持终于又开口说话，她并未直接回答千里寻的问题："从你刚才进来，我已感受到你体内的神识不属于这个时代，先给我说说是怎么回事？"

Then, from the dark: "From the moment you entered this chamber, I sensed that the soul residing within your body does not belong to this era. Tell me—how did this come to be?"

---

千里寻更觉得这位她看不清容貌的住持实在厉害，便将自己遇到地震，成为朱淑真的过程毫无保留详细说明。最后问道："我还想知道，我到底是朱淑真还是千里寻？我在这个时代会像朱淑真一样？还是会有千里寻的命运？"

Qianli Xun was thoroughly awestruck. Holding nothing back, she recounted everything—the earthquake, the darkness, waking up trapped in a body that wasn't hers.

Finally, she voiced the question that haunted her: "I must know... am I Zhu Shuzhen, or am I Qianli Xun? In this era, am I fated to suffer Zhu Shuzhen's tragic end, or can I live out my own destiny?"

---

住持反问道："你认为自己是谁？"

The Abbess countered with a question of her own: "Who do *you* believe you are?"

---

千里寻犹豫一会儿，坚定地回道："在别人眼中我是朱淑真，可我认为我依然是千里寻？"

Qianli Xun hesitated briefly before answering with absolute conviction: "In the eyes of the world, I am Zhu Shuzhen. But in my heart, I know I am—and always will be—Qianli Xun!"

---

"我朝有位能人写下一篇文章，名为《太上感应篇》，首句便是'福祸无门，惟人自召'，意思是福与祸不会随意找任何人，都是人自己的行为招感而来。佛门也讲五戒十善，均在告诫世人，想要改变命运最好的办法是改变自己的行为，而不是拜佛求神，以及星象占卜之类……"

"In our dynasty, a scholar of great wisdom penned a text titled *Taishang Ganying Pian*," the Abbess spoke softly. "Its opening line reads: *'Blessings and calamities have no door; they are summoned solely by one's own deeds.'* It means that fortune and misfortune do not strike at random—they are summoned by a person's own actions. Buddhism similarly teaches the Five Precepts and Ten Virtues to remind us that the best way to alter one's fate is to change one's own conduct, not to blindly worship gods, pray to Buddhas, or consult astrology."

---

千里寻对《太上感应篇》，以及什么五戒十善根本不感兴趣，认为住持只是对她进行说教而已，直到住持说出"改变命运"四个字，她才提起兴趣，认真往下听。

Initially, Qianli Xun had little interest in Taoist scriptures or Buddhist precepts, suspecting the Abbess was merely lecturing her. But the moment the words "alter one's fate" left the old nun's lips, her attention snapped back, and she listened intently.

---

"你成为朱淑真还是千里寻，是由你的行为决定，行为又由观念操纵，你要从改变观念做起，在这个时代才能过上千里寻的生活。"

"Whether you become Zhu Shuzhen or Qianli Xun is decided entirely by your actions, and your actions are governed by your mindset," the Abbess explained. "You must begin by transforming your mindset. Only then can you live as Qianli Xun in this era."

---

千里寻感到庆幸，她虽有着朱淑真的外表，观念倒与她截然不同，看来她正走在正确的道路上。

A wave of relief washed over Qianli Xun. Though she occupied Zhu Shuzhen's physical shell, her modern mindset was radically different from the tragic poetess's. It seemed she was already on the right path.

---

"不过，这身体并不是千里寻的，千里寻的命运多多少少会受到朱淑真命运的牵制，你更需努力才对，婚姻便是牵制之一。据我观察，朱淑真的命格中情路颇为坎坷，你借着她重生，自然情路不会一帆风顺，可又与她不同，只要你经历过几段情路，人生自然顺畅。"

"However," the Abbess cautioned, "this body is not originally yours. Your destiny as Qianli Xun will inevitably be weighed down by the shackles of Zhu Shuzhen's life, requiring you to fight twice as hard. This marriage is one such shackle. By my observation, Zhu Shuzhen's love line was written in thorns. Having been reborn through her, your path to love will naturally be filled with storms of the heart. Yet your fate differs from hers—so long as you navigate through a few romantic entanglements, your life will eventually smooth out."

---

千里寻在心里问道："这么暗的山洞，也不知净光法师是如何观察的？该不会她能看到我，而我看不到她？难道修佛之人都会有这种能力？"

*Wait. In this blackness—how is she observing me? Can she see me even if I can't see her? Do all Buddhist masters possess this kind of power?*

---

又听住持说她需经历几段情路，高兴道："住持的意思是我会摆脱现在的婚姻？"

Hearing the Abbess mention "a few romantic entanglements," Qianli Xun's eyes lit up with joy. "You mean—I'll get out? I'll actually get out?"

---

"我虽帮你看命，但天机不可泄露，有些事情不能明说。你只需记着，无论遇到任何事，要坚持正思正念，便可逢凶化吉。"

"Though I can read your path, heavenly secrets must never be spoken aloud," the Abbess replied cryptically. "Some things cannot be explicitly revealed. You need only remember this: whatever trial you face, hold fast to righteous thoughts and pure intentions, and you will turn every misfortune into a blessing."

---

"住持的话真是醍醐灌顶，我定会按照您所说坚持正思正念。"

"Your words just... cracked something open in my head," Qianli Xun said reverently. "I will treasure your advice and hold fast to righteous thoughts."

---

"既然你我有缘，我再帮帮你。"住持停了一会儿，继续说道，"你选了王右丞的《竹里馆》，解诗的内容我详细阅览，发现你的解析很独到，你说这首诗不是赞美隐居之人品性高洁，而是书写诗人的无奈以及对时局的批判。我认为有一定道理，像王右丞这等有才华之人，应该被重用，为百姓谋福，却独自隐居山林，实在是人才浪费，也的确与当时的时局密不可分。你能有如此见识，说明你是位有主见的女子，既有主见，便有了掌控自己命运的第一步。我还发现你的字里行间并未提到诗中非常关键的一个字：'竹'字，于是敏锐地感觉出竹能给你带来好运，回去后找一处有竹林的地方住上一个月，自然能体会到妙处。"

"You chose Wang Youcheng's *Bamboo Lodge* on the wall. I read your interpretation carefully."

A pause. Then, softer:

"You said the poem isn't about a recluse's noble character. You said it's about helplessness. About a man who *should* have been serving the people, rotting in the mountains instead. About a dynasty that wasted him." Another pause. "I agree. A talent like his, locked away in bamboo and silence—it was a crime. And you saw that. You *wrote* that. A woman with such insight already holds the first thread of her own fate."

"But." The single word hung in the dark. "You never once mentioned the most important character in the poem. *Bamboo.* You wrote around it, through it, past it—but never *to* it. I felt that. Bamboo is your luck, child. Find a grove. Live beside it for one month. You'll understand why."

---

千里寻终于明白，住持让人解诗并非只为解诗，而是要从解诗的内容中了解解诗之人。

Qianli Xun finally understood—the Abbess's poetry test wasn't about testing literary skill at all, but about reading the soul of the person behind the words.

---

"好，我定照做。"

"I understand! I will do exactly as you say!"

---

"你的茶做得实在粗糙，我一口都没喝，知道我为何还愿见你吗？"

"The tea you whisked earlier was abysmal—I didn't drink a single drop," the Abbess suddenly remarked with a light chuckle. "Do you know why I still agreed to see you?"

---

"晚辈不知，还请住持明示。"

"This junior is clueless. Please enlighten me, Abbess."

---

"只因你做的茶和我年轻时第一次做得一模一样。慈玄又将你做茶时的动作向我详细描绘，尤其是你把温盏的水倒在地上，呵呵，我第一次也是如此，让我甚觉亲切。"

She chuckled—a dry, papery sound. "Because the disaster of a tea bowl you made was identical to the very first bowl I whisked in my youth! Cixuan described your clumsy movements to me in detail—especially how you splashed the hot bowl-warming water straight onto the floor. I did the exact same thing when I was young. It filled my heart with a sudden, warm nostalgia."

---

千里心想，也是她运气好，瞎猫撞上死耗子，否则今日哪有机缘与净光法师聊天儿。

*Pure dumb luck. A blind cat catching a dead rat. If that tea had been even slightly competent, she'd never have gotten this meeting.*

---

"你离开时，慈空会给你一条红绸，记得将自己的名字写在上面，至于写哪个名字由你决定。后山有棵千年神柳，你把红绸绑在神柳西边的树枝上，必然会对你产生助益。"

"When you depart, Cikong will give you a strip of red silk," the Abbess instructed. "Write your name upon it—which name you choose to write is up to you. On the rear mountain stands a Thousand-Year Willow. Tie the red silk to the western branches of that ancient tree, and it will surely aid your destiny."

---

听到千年神柳，千里寻感到命运实在神奇，她来的那个时代有棵千年神柳，来到的这个时代又碰到一棵，看来她的命运与神柳有着密切的关系。

At the mention of a "Thousand-Year Willow," Qianli Xun's heart skipped a beat. *Fate truly has a sense of humor!* In her modern world, a Thousand-Year Willow had been the anchor of her life; now, in this ancient era, another one existed. It seemed her fate was inextricably bound to that sacred willow.

---

住持不再说话，传来慈空的声音："女施主，可以走了。"

The Abbess fell silent, signalling the end of the audience. Cikong's voice cut through the dark: "You may go now."

---

慈空走近，伸出手："牵着我的手。"

Cikong stepped forward and held out her hand. "Take my hand."

---

千里寻起身，摸到慈空的手，跟着往出走。

Qianli Xun stood up, grasped Cikong's hand in the dark, and let herself be led toward the exit.

---

从黑暗中来到有灯光之处，光线刺眼，千里寻不由闭上双眼。奇怪的是，慈空竟然一点儿感觉都没有。

Stepping out from the abyss into the lamp-lit outer chamber, the sudden glare stung Qianli Xun's eyes, forcing her to squeeze them shut. Strangely, Cikong didn't seem bothered by the light in the slightest.

---

千里寻闭一会儿眼睛慢慢缓过来，问道："师傅，从里面出来，您怎么一点儿都不怕光？"

Rubbing her eyes as she slowly adjusted, Qianli Xun asked curiously, "Master, coming out from utter darkness like that, how are your eyes completely unaffected by the light?"

---

"习惯就好。"慈空简单回道，然后从桌上的木盒里取出一条红绸递给千里寻，"拿着，到正殿找敲木鱼的小师傅，她会帮你指明神柳所在之处。"

"One grows accustomed to it," Cikong replied plainly.

She reached into the wooden box on the table, pulled out a strip of crimson silk, and handed it to Qianli Xun. "Take this. Head to the main hall and find the young sister striking the wooden fish. She will guide you to the Divine Willow."

---

千里寻接过红绸，谢过后，推门离开山洞。

Qianli Xun accepted the red silk, bowed in gratitude, and pushed open the cave door.

---

等千里寻离开后，慈空把门关上，将另一盏油灯点着，端着进入内洞，里面一下子亮堂起来。

Once Qianli Xun had departed, Cikong closed the heavy door, lit a second oil lamp, and carried it into the inner cavern. Instantly, the pitch-black chamber was bathed in warm, flickering light.

---

洞内有一个石炕，石炕上铺着柔软的褥子，上面盘腿坐着的正是住持净光法师，她微闭双目，双手放置膝头。

In the center of the cave sat a stone bed cushioned with soft padding. Seated cross-legged upon it was Abbess Jingguang herself, her eyes softly closed and her hands resting peacefully on her knees.

---

慈空把油灯放好后说道："师父每次都不点灯，搞得信众都不知道您长什么样。"

Setting the oil lamp down, Cikong sighed softly. "Master, you do this every time. Sit in the dark. No lamp. No face. They walk out with no idea what you even look like."

---

净光法师说："这样才好，省得我下山一趟都把我围起来，不得自由。"

Abbess Jingguang said, "This is better. It saves me from being mobbed every time I go down the mountain, with no freedom to move."

---

"师父，您给那位姑娘说的方法真管用吗？"

"Master... does the advice you gave that young lady really work?"

---

"管不管用，不在于我，关键在她。世人愚昧，才会相信占卜之类，如果明心见性，才知道这命啊都是自己造作而成。"

"Whether it works or not doesn't depend on me. The key lies with her. People in the world are ignorant, which is why they believe in divination and such. If one could enlighten the mind and see one's true nature, one would know that destiny is shaped by one's own actions."

---

"师父就是明心见性之人，所以才能看得透，说得透。"

Cikong bowed reverently. "Master, you are one who has awakened to your true nature. That is why you can see through all things—and speak the ultimate truth."
