## 四、表达正确性与地道性

### 1. 蝴蝶段

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Qianli Xun had never seen such a big and beautiful butterfly. Its wings were blue with black edges. It fluttered to her face and circled around her. She reached out to catch it, but the butterfly landed on the back of her hand. She couldn't bear to catch it anymore. She could only admire its beauty with loving eyes.* |
| **修改后** | *A butterfly—enormous, impossibly blue, edged in velvet black—drifted out of the window. It circled her once. Twice. She reached for it, but it landed softly on the back of her hand, wings opening and closing like slow breaths. She froze. Couldn't bring herself to grab it. Could only stare.* |
| **理由** | 现译 6 个短句结构完全相同（主语+动词），节奏单调。修改后用破折号插入描写、用碎片句（"Twice." "She froze."）制造惊喜感 |

### 5. 住持的长段独白（150+ 词）

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | 一整段 150+ 词，从"你选了王右丞"到"自然能体会到妙处" |
| **修改后** | 拆为 4 段（见下方） |

**修改后：**

> "You chose Wang Wei's *Bamboo Lodge*. I read your interpretation carefully."
>
> A pause. Then, softer:
>
> "You said the poem isn't about a recluse's noble character. You said it's about helplessness. About a man who *should* have been serving the people, rotting in the mountains instead. About a dynasty that wasted him." Another pause. "I agree. A talent like his, locked away in bamboo and silence—it was a crime. And you saw that. You *wrote* that. A woman with such insight already holds the first thread of her own fate."
>
> "But." The single word hung in the dark. "You never once mentioned the most important character in the poem. *Bamboo.* You wrote around it, through it, past it—but never *to* it. I felt that. Bamboo is your luck, child. Find a grove. Live beside it for one month. You'll understand why."

## 八、吸引力评估

**做得好的：**
- 蝴蝶意象（引路→缘分→飞走）的叙事线保留完整 ✓
- 黑暗中对话的氛围感基本建立 ✓
- 净光"茶和我年轻时一模一样"的反转有温度 ✓
- 慈空最后点灯揭示净光真面目的结构好 ✓

**可加强：**
- **黑暗感是本章最大的沉浸工具**，但翻译中 "dark" 出现 12 次却缺乏感官层次。应加入：听觉（呼吸声、衣料摩擦）、触觉（石壁冰凉、蒲团柔软）、嗅觉（檀香、陈年石头味）。
- **净光的角色魅力**在现译中被削弱。她应是一个"看透一切但不说破"的老妇人，说话有禅机、有幽默、有锋利。现译让她的话像说明书。
- **千里寻的现代感**在对话中消失。她是穿越女，面对"命由己造"应有现代人的反应（*"So... self-help? You're giving me ancient self-help?"*），而非乖乖说"醍醐灌顶"。
