## 5. 本次修改点（Phase 4 实际落地）

### 修改点 1：宗教色彩中性化（合规 + 用词吸引力）
**修改前：**
> …with the words "Divine Divination," "Destiny Reading," and "Doubt Resolution" written on them…

**修改后：**
> …with the words "Spirit Reading," "Destiny Reading," and "Doubt Resolution" written on them…

**理由**：`Divine` 带西方宗教"神性"联想，且与 `Divination` 语义重复；"Spirit Reading" 既去宗教色、又贴合"神课"（spirit + 课/session）原意，阅读更利落。

### 修改点 2：副词节制（用词吸引力 / 重复消重）
**修改前：**
> That person sat to the side, very unhappy.

**修改后：**
> That person sat to the side, visibly unhappy.

**理由**：`very` 在章内已多次出现；`visibly` 增加可视画面（"看得见的不悦"），更贴合网文"展示而非告知"原则。

### 修改点 3：重复用词消重（用词吸引力）
**修改前：**
> Qiao Mengrong beside him was very puzzled. Who exactly was her brother looking for?…

**修改后：**
> Qiao Mengrong beside him was puzzled. Who exactly was her brother looking for?…

**理由**：`very puzzled` 在 L284 已用过一次，短距离内重复；此处去 `very` 消重，faithfulness 不变（疑惑→puzzled 仍准确）。
