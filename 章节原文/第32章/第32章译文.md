# Chapter 32: Shopping, Divination, and Encountering a Thief

---

The guards at Prince Yi's Manor numbered about fifty. Upon receiving the prince's summons, they all gathered in the main courtyard, forming neat rows and waiting for the prince to speak. Since they had come to the manor, this was the first time the prince had gathered them all together. Each one was filled with curiosity and wanted to see with their own eyes the healthy prince who had returned from the dead.

---

Shen Jianchen walked in front of the guards. Only this group of masculine men made him feel a bit like he was back before his transmigration, and his mood naturally became much more cheerful.

---

"Who is usually responsible for managing you?"

---

One person stepped out from the guards: "Reporting to Your Highness, it is I. I am Duan Hui, the head of the guards, usually responsible for arranging the guards' shifts to protect the manor's safety."

---

"Are you familiar with the guards?"

---

"Reporting to Your Highness, I know clearly even how many moles each of them has on their body."

---

"Good. Since that's the case, tell me—are there any among them who know martial arts?"

---

Duan Hui thought for a moment and said: "Rest assured, Your Highness. They all know how to wield a saber a bit, and they all have some skill in hand-to-hand combat."

---

Hearing this, Shen Jianchen was very pleased: "Very good. Return to your position."

---

After Duan Hui returned to the formation, Shen Jianchen said loudly: "Everyone! Spread out one arm's length apart in all directions!"

---

The guards quickly positioned themselves as required.

---

Shen Jianchen commanded loudly again: "Assume the horse stance!"

---

The guards assumed the horse stance.

---

Shen Jianchen continued: "Clench your fists tightly! Punch left and right! Punch forward! Left! Right!..."

---

As Shen Jianchen shouted, he walked around, carefully observing each person. He wanted to select a personal guard, preferably someone with some martial arts foundation, and more importantly, someone with good bone structure who could learn Shen Fist from him in the future.

---

Not far away, Qiao Mengrong looked in this direction. Her brother was training the guards, his voice booming like a bell, as if his body had endless energy.

---

She recalled the past...

---

One day, her brother, who was bedridden, wanted to go out and sunbathe. Qiao Mengrong ordered the guards to bring a long couch, and then had the maids lay thick soft cushions on it. She and a maid supported her brother into the sunlight. After he sat down on the soft couch, he was already breathing lightly, leaning on her, and said weakly: "Mengrong, if I'm no longer here, you must marry into a good family."

---

"No! I want to guard the manor for you, Brother."

---

"Silly girl, what's the use of guarding the manor if I'm not here? I've already written my will. Half of the manor's property will go to you, and half to Nanny Zou..."

---

Having spoken too much, Zhao Pu had no strength to continue. At that time, Qiao Mengrong thought that if she could give some of her strength to her brother, he could chat with her a bit longer. All she could do was extend her arms and gently embrace her brother, her heart continuing to worry that he would leave at any moment.

---

As Qiao Mengrong recalled the past, she looked at her brother before her. He had finally become what she had hoped for. She truly wished the Mid-Autumn Festival next year would come quickly. She didn't want to be his sister anymore—she wanted to become his true woman.

---

Shen Jianchen had already looked over all fifty-plus guards. Each one was strong and robust, and with their skills in hand-to-hand combat and saber wielding, they were more than enough to protect the manor. But not one had good bone structure.

---

"That's all for today. Go back to your duties."

---

Hearing the prince's command, the guards gathered again, their formation still neat.

---

Duan Hui stepped out from the formation: "Everyone! As arranged this morning, each to your post. Guard the manor well. Dismissed!"

---

The guards dispersed to their respective posts.

---

Duan Hui bowed to the prince: "Your Highness, if you have any orders in the future, just find me directly. Your subordinate will go through fire and water without hesitation!"

---

Shen Jianchen was satisfied with his loyalty: "Good! If I need anything, I'll definitely find you. Go attend to your duties."

---

After Duan Hui left, Shen Jianchen planned to leave the manor. He had been cooped up in the manor all day and didn't know what the outside was like. It was better to go out for a stroll. Before he even reached the main gate, Qiao Mengrong called out to him: "Brother! Where are you going?"

---

Shen Jianchen turned around: "It's you, Mengrong. I'm planning to go out for a stroll."

---

"I'll accompany you, Brother."

---

"I don't plan to take a carriage. I'll be walking, which is tiring. You'd better not go."

---

"I'm not afraid of being tired. From childhood to now, Brother has never gone shopping with me. I've always dreamed of having such a day."

---

Qiao Mengrong's eyes, full of love and longing, made Shen Jianchen unable to refuse: "Alright, let's go shopping together."

---

Receiving her brother's affirmative response, Qiao Mengrong's face showed a relaxed smile. She stepped forward and took her brother's hand: "Let's go."

---

Xilan quickly arranged the carriage. Although the two masters planned to walk, they still needed a carriage to follow, in case they couldn't walk anymore and needed a ride at any time.

---

Being pulled by Qiao Mengrong, Shen Jianchen was very unaccustomed, but he couldn't bear to refuse. He was pulled out of the manor like this.

---

As they got farther and farther from the manor, they arrived at the bustling market. Shen Jianchen couldn't help but say: "Mengrong, it's very inconvenient for you to pull me while shopping. Can you let go?"

---

Qiao Mengrong let go of his hand: "Since Brother is not used to it, I won't pull anymore. As long as Brother is happy, that's all that matters."

---

Shen Jianchen felt very self-reproachful. Why couldn't he like such a good girl? If he could like her, it should be a pretty perfect thing.

---

The prosperity of the market made Shen Jianchen unable to continue his self-reproach. He was attracted by the scene before him.

---

He saw people flowing back and forth, vendors clamoring, a scene full of vitality and busyness. The liveliness was comparable to the modern metropolis Shen Jianchen had experienced. The stalls on both sides of the street displayed a dazzling array of goods: various brightly colored silks, delicate ceramic utensils, fragrant tea leaves, fresh fruits and vegetables of all kinds, and so on.

---

Beside them, common people passed by in an endless stream. Scholars wore blue robes and white belts, carrying small baskets, strolling on the street. Some tasted street snacks, some watched the performances of street artists. Vendors shouted about their goods, competing to attract customers' attention. Elderly women took their children shopping, bargaining while chatting with neighbors.

---

The sounds in the market rose and fell one after another—hawking sounds, vendors' shouting, the neighing of carriage horses, people's laughter—all merging together to form a magnificent symphony of prosperity. The busyness and hustle of the market made Shen Jianchen feel the vigor and vitality of urban life in Bianliang during the Song Dynasty.

---

He saw a shop with a sign that read "Jiao Dian" (Foot Shop) hanging at the entrance, and couldn't help but say: "I didn't expect the Song Dynasty to also have shops that provide foot baths."

---

"Foot bath?" Qiao Mengrong was very puzzled.

---

Shen Jianchen pointed at the "Jiao Dian" sign: "Look, isn't it written right there?"

---

After looking, Qiao Mengrong smiled and said: "That kind of small shop doesn't provide foot baths. It's just a place for people to rest their feet briefly. Bianliang is such a big city that you can't finish exploring it in a day. When you're tired from walking, you can rest at a foot shop."

---

Shen Jianchen felt ashamed of his ignorance: "I didn't expect the Song Dynasty to also be an era full of human consideration."

---

Qiao Mengrong didn't quite understand and was thinking about her brother's words when she heard him ask: "What do the shops with 'Heaven's' and 'Fine Gift' written on them do?"

---

Qiao Mengrong explained: "They are wine shops."

---

"Why not just call them wine shops? They insist on using such words that people can't understand." Shen Jianchen thought the ancients were really pretentious. He couldn't figure out what "Heaven's" and "Fine Gift" had to do with wine.

---

Qiao Mengrong explained: "Brother once told me that the *Book of Han* says: 'Wine is Heaven's fine gift.' This means praising wine as a beautiful gift bestowed by Heaven."

[TN: "天之美禄" (Heaven's Fine Gift) is a classical allusion from the *Book of Han* (《汉书》), used as an elegant euphemism for wine in ancient China.]

---

Only after transmigrating to ancient times did he truly realize that he was culturally ignorant. Shen Jianchen said awkwardly: "I told you that? Sigh! I can't remember anything because of my amnesia. It seems I'll have to take out the books I read before and read them again."

---

"You should read them again. His Majesty likes people with talent, especially literary scholars who are proficient in poetry, fu rhapsodies, and the arts of calligraphy and painting. When Brother was not well before, His Majesty didn't pay attention, and that was fine. Now that you're healthy, you'll surely have the opportunity to meet His Majesty. Reading more books will only benefit you."

---

Shen Jianchen could tell that this sister still very much hoped her brother would be valued by the Emperor. For Shen Jianchen, whether he met the Emperor or not didn't matter, and whether he was valued or not mattered even less. Anyway, he had plenty of estates, and living a wealthy life in this unfamiliar era shouldn't be a problem. He only cared about whether he could meet Qianli Xun again.

---

As Shen Jianchen thought and admired the market, he unknowingly walked to the base of a big tree.

---

Under the tree was a shed, and inside the shed was a divination stall. Above the table was a rope tied, and hanging from the rope were three long pieces of paper with the words "Divine Divination," "Destiny Reading," and "Doubt Resolution" written on them. The services were quite comprehensive.

---

Shen Jianchen wanted to know if he could still find Qianli Xun. Perhaps the fortune-teller could help resolve his doubts. But there were many common people waiting for divination, forming a long queue. He didn't know when it would be his turn, so he had to give up.

---

Qiao Mengrong saw through her brother's thoughts and signaled to Xilan beside her with a glance.

---

Xilan stepped forward and took out two taels of silver, placing them in front of the fortune-teller.

---

The old fortune-teller, upon seeing the silver, immediately took it in his hand and said with a smile to the person in front of him who was having his face read: "Excuse me, please wait over there for a while and let our honored guest go first."

---

That person sat to the side, very unhappy.

---

"Brother, since you have doubts in your heart, why not go over and ask?" Qiao Mengrong pulled Prince Yi to sit in front of the divination stall.

---

The old fortune-teller said: "What does the noble one wish to ask?"

---

Shen Jianchen replied: "Doubt resolution."

---

"Please write a character, and I'll help you interpret it."

---

Shen Jianchen thought for a while, picked up the brush, and wrote the character "Xun" (寻, meaning "to seek") on the paper. Because he hadn't practiced calligraphy, the character was really ugly.

---

Qiao Mengrong felt a pang of sadness as she looked. Although her brother rarely wrote characters before, and had only written a few times occasionally, his handwriting was much better than this. How could such handwriting catch the Emperor's eye?

---

The old man turned the paper around, looked at it for a long time, and asked: "Is the character written by the noble one 'Xun' (seek)?"

---

Only then did Shen Jianchen realize he had written the simplified character. He nodded awkwardly: "Yes."

---

The old man picked up his brush and rewrote it: "It should be written like this."

---

Shen Jianchen looked carefully. He was indeed unfamiliar with the traditional character "尋".

---

[TN: In Chinese character divination, the fortune-teller analyzes the structure of the character written by the client. The client wrote the simplified "寻", but the fortune-teller rewrote it as the traditional "尋" to perform the divination. The traditional character "尋" is composed of several parts: the top portion resembles part of "歸" (to return), the middle contains "工" (work) and "口" (mouth), and the bottom is "寸" (inch). Each part carries symbolic meaning in the divination.]

---

"It seems the noble one is asking about seeking a person?" The old man put down his brush and hit the nail on the head.

---

Shen Jianchen eagerly replied: "Yes, I'm seeking a person. I wonder if I can find them?"

---

The old man thought for a while and said: "The topmost strokes of the character '尋' are part of the character '歸' (to return). The other strokes of '歸' are completely lost, indicating that the place from which the noble one has returned can no longer be returned to..."

---

These words struck Shen Jianchen's heart. Wasn't that right? Having transmigrated here, it was impossible to go back.

---

"As for seeking a person, the 'Gong' (work) character in the middle indicates that it will require some effort. The 'Kou' (mouth) character indicates that the noble one will meet the person sought in a place related to the mouth, such as a place for eating, a place for drinking and merrymaking, or a place for composing poetry and fu rhapsodies..."

---

Shen Jianchen was overjoyed. From these words, it seemed Qianli Xun should have also transmigrated here, and they could still meet again.

---

"The 'Cun' (inch) character at the bottom indicates that even if you meet the person you seek, within your heart (方寸) you still won't be able to recognize each other..."

---

"That's alright. As long as I can find them, it's good. Can you tell where I can find her?"

---

Qiao Mengrong beside him was very puzzled. Who exactly was her brother looking for? Why had he never mentioned it to her?

---

"This character written by the noble one, from the orientation of the divination stall, faces south overall. If I'm not mistaken, the person you're looking for should be in the south."

---

After the old fortune-teller's words, Shen Jianchen suddenly saw the light. He stood up and thanked him: "Thank you, old sir, for resolving my doubts. Xilan, take out another tael of silver for the old sir, as my special reward."

---

Xilan took out another tael. The old man was even more pleasantly surprised. He stood up, took the silver, and was extremely grateful: "Thank you, noble one. Thank you, noble one. I wish you find the one you're thinking of soon." The old fortune-teller continued to sit down and divine for other guests.

---

Qiao Mengrong followed her brother away from the divination stall with doubts. She carefully observed her brother. After the divination, he had been smiling.

---

Qiao Mengrong couldn't help but ask: "Who is the person Brother is looking for?"

---

Shen Jianchen was caught off guard. How should he tell Qiao Mengrong? Even if he told her, she wouldn't believe it. Besides, if he told her, she would know that someone else was in his heart, and wouldn't she be sad? So he casually made up: "A friend I knew before. I haven't seen them for a long time. I thought I'd never find them again, so I had the old sir divine whether I could meet them again."

---

Her brother had always been sick. Who would be willing to be friends with him? Only Young Master Qiao often came to visit. His answer made Qiao Mengrong sad, because her brother had started to deceive her.

---

"Doesn't Brother remember nothing? Why do you still remember friends from before?"

---

Shen Jianchen continued to make up: "Oh, I just saw the divination stall and suddenly thought of someone. It was very vague. I got interested and wanted to go over and divine, and also test the old fortune-teller's skill."

---

"What was the result of the test? Was his divination accurate?"

---

"Good enough. Otherwise, why would I reward him with another tael of silver?"

---

Just as they were talking, a shout came from the crowd: "Catch that thief! He stole my money pouch!"

---

Shen Jianchen looked in the direction of the sound. A middle-aged man was weaving through the crowd, running and shouting: "Catch the thief! My money pouch!"

---

He saw a young figure flash past before his eyes, moving at incredible speed.

---

Shen Jianchen praised in his heart: "Good bone structure!" Then he said to Xilan: "Take the Commandery Princess to the foot shop to rest. I'll go catch the thief."

---

After speaking, Shen Jianchen disappeared from Qiao Mengrong's sight in a flash.

---

Qiao Mengrong looked disappointed: "Let's go to the foot shop and wait for Brother."
