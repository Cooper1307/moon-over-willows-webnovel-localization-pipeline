第32章 逛街占卜遇小贼
## Chapter 32: Strolling, Fortune-Telling, and a Young Thief

仪王府的侍卫大约有五十来人，接到仪王的召唤，都聚集在正院，排着整齐的队伍，等待王爷发话。自从他们来到仪王府，还是头一遭被王爷聚集在一起，一个个充满好奇，也想亲眼目睹起死回生后健健康康的王爷。
Roughly fifty guards served Prince Yi's Manor. Summoned by the prince, they lined up crisp and orderly in the main courtyard awaiting orders—first time in memory His Highness had ever called roll, leaving every man eager to see their healthy, revived prince up close.

申简辰走到侍卫面前，这群充满阳刚之气的男子才让他找回点儿穿越之前的感觉，心情不由舒畅许多。
Stepping before the ranks, Shen Jianchen felt his spirits lift—surrounded by sturdy, masculine energy at last, bringing back a taste of his old world!

“平时是谁负责管理你们？”
"Who commands you day to day?"

从侍卫中站出一位：“报告王爷，是我，我是侍卫总管，名叫段晖，平时负责安排侍卫轮流保卫王府的安全。”
One guard stepped forward crisp: "Reporting to Your Highness—I am Captain of the Guard Duan Hui, in charge of scheduling patrols to protect the manor."

“你对侍卫们可否了解？”
"Know your men well?"

“报告王爷，他们身上有多少颗痣我都一清二楚。”
"Reporting to Your Highness—I know every mole on every man's back!"

“好，既如此，你给我说说他们中有没有人会功夫？”
"Good. Then tell me: do any of them know real martial arts?"

段晖想想说：“王爷放心，都会耍两下大刀，格斗的本领多少会些。”
Duan Hui weighed it a second. "Rest easy, Your Highness—every man can swing a saber and handle rough brawling."

听到这话，申简辰甚是开心：“很好，你先入列。”
Pleased, Shen Jianchen nodded: "Good. Step back in line."

﻿待段晖回到队伍中，申简辰大声说：“所有人！前后左右按照一臂距离散开！”
Once Duan Hui rejoined the ranks, Shen Jianchen barked: "Everyone—spread out one arm's length front, back, left, and right!"

侍卫们按照要求很快站好。
The guards shifted smartly into spacing.

申简辰又大声命令：“蹲马步！”
"Horse stance!"

侍卫们将马步扎好。
Fifty men sank into horse stances at once.

申简辰继续说：“握紧双拳！左右开弓！向前出拳！左！右！……”
"Clench fists! Alternate punches straight ahead! Left! Right!..."

申简辰一边喊，一边走动，仔细观察每个人，他要选一名贴身侍卫，最好有些武功根基，更重要的是根骨要好，以后还可以跟着他学习申拳。
Calling cadence as he paced down the rows, Shen Jianchen scrutinized every guard—hunting for a personal bodyguard with martial roots and, above all, prime natural aptitude he could train in Shen Fist.

不远处，乔梦容朝这边望来，哥哥训练侍卫，声如洪钟，仿佛他的身体里有使不完的劲儿。
Watching from afar, Qiao Mengrong listened to his booming voice drill the men, energy bursting from every stride.

她回忆起往事……
Memories surfaced unbidden...

有一天，卧在病榻上的哥哥想出去晒太阳。乔梦容吩咐侍卫搬来一个长长的坐榻，再让丫鬟铺上厚厚的软垫。她与一名丫鬟扶着哥哥来到太阳底下，在软榻上坐好后，哥哥已经开始轻微喘气，靠在她的身上，有气无力地说：“梦容，我如果不在，你一定要找个好人家嫁了。”
One sunny afternoon, bedridden Zhao Pu had wished to sit in the sun. Qiao Mengrong had guards haul out a long couch piled with thick cushions; assisted by a maid, she helped him sit outdoors. Barely seated, breathless already, he leaned weak against her shoulder and whispered: "Mengrong... once I'm gone, promise you'll marry a good man."

“不！我要替哥哥守着王府。”
"No! I'll stay and guard Prince Yi's Manor for you!"

“傻姑娘，我不在，守着王府有什么用？我已写下遗嘱，王府的财产一半儿归你，一半儿给邹嬷嬷……”
"Silly girl—what use is guarding an empty manor once I'm gone? I've already written my will: half the estate goes to you, half to Matron Zou..."

话说的有些多，赵朴已没力气继续说下去。那时乔梦容心想，如果可以把自己的力气分给哥哥一些，他就可以跟她多聊会儿。而她能做的只是伸出手臂，轻轻搂住哥哥，内心继续担忧他会随时离开。
Those few sentences drained his breath. Back then she had wished with all her soul she could pour her own strength into him just so he could talk longer—yet all she could do was slip her arms gentle around his frail frame, dreading the moment he stopped breathing.

乔梦容边回忆边瞧着眼前的哥哥，终于成为她期待的样子，真希望明年的中秋节来得快一些，她不想再当他的妹妹，她要成为他真正的女人。
Gazing now at the strapping man before her, transformed into everything she had prayed for, she longed for next Mid-Autumn Festival to race closer—done being his sister, eager to become his wife!

申简辰已将五十多名侍卫看了个遍，每个人身强力壮，加上格斗与耍大刀的本领，守护王府绰绰有余，却没一个根骨好的。
Having inspected all fifty-plus guards, Shen Jianchen found plenty of brawny men good with sabers and brawls—fine for guarding gates, yet not a single gem with top-tier martial bones.

“今天就到这里，你们去忙吧。”
"Dismissed for today. Back to your posts."

听到王爷的命令，侍卫们又聚拢起来，队伍依然整齐。
Snapping back into tight formation at his command, the men stood ready.

段晖从队伍中出来：“所有人！按照今早安排的，各司其职，守卫好王府，散了！”
Duan Hui stepped out: "Carry on with morning patrols—guard the manor tight! Dismissed!"

侍卫们各自散去。
The guards dispersed.

段晖向王爷行礼：“王爷，以后有什么吩咐，您直接找我，属下定赴汤蹈火，在所不辞！”
Duan Hui bowed low: "Anything Your Highness needs, just give the word—through fire and boiling water, I won't blink!"

申简辰对他的忠心感到满意：“好！有事我定然找你，先去忙吧。”
Pleased by his loyalty, Shen Jianchen nodded. "I'll call when I need you. Go tend your duties."

段晖离开后，申简辰打算出府，整日憋在王府里，都不知道外面是什么样儿，不如出去逛逛。还没走到大门口，被乔梦容叫住：“哥哥！要去哪里？”
Once Duan Hui left, Shen Jianchen felt cabin fever creeping in—cooped inside the manor without seeing Bianliang outside? Time for a stroll! Barely halfway to the front gate, Qiao Mengrong called out: "Brother! Where are you headed?"

申简辰转过身：“是梦容啊，我打算出去逛逛。”
Turning, he smiled: "Ah, Mengrong—just heading out for a walk."

“我陪哥哥一起去。”
"I'll come with you, Brother!"

“我不打算坐马车，徒步行走，很累的，你还是别去了。”
"No carriage—walking on foot, real tiring. Better stay home."

“我不怕累，从小到大，哥哥从未与我一起逛过街，我一直梦想着能有这么一天。”
"I don't mind getting tired! Growing up, you never once strolled the streets with me—I've dreamed of this day forever!"

乔梦容充满爱而又渴望的眼神令申简辰不忍拒绝：“好吧，一起逛街。”
Melting under her eager, adoring eyes, he couldn't say no. "Alright, let's go together."

得到哥哥肯定的答复，乔梦容脸上露出轻松的笑容，上前拉起哥哥：“我们走吧。”
Lighting up with a breezy smile, she stepped over and linked her hand in his. "Let's go!"

喜兰很快安排好马车，两位主子虽要步行，也得有马车跟着，以防走不动时随时有车可乘坐。
Xilan ordered a carriage right behind them—walking or not, royal habit kept a carriage trailing in case either tired.

被乔梦容拉着，申简辰很不习惯，又不忍拒绝，就这样被拉着走出王府。
Uncomfortable holding hands yet loath to hurt her feelings, Shen Jianchen let her pull him out past the manor gates.

离王府越来越远时，他们来到热闹的街市，申简辰才忍不住说：“梦容，你拉着我逛街很不方便，能不能放开？”
Once they left the quiet lanes and hit bustling market avenues, he spoke up: "Mengrong, hard to walk through crowds holding hands—mind letting go?"

乔梦容松开手：“哥哥既不习惯，我不拉了，只要哥哥开心就好。”
She slipped her hand free at once. "If it bothers you, Brother, I won't hold on—whatever makes you happy."

申简辰很自责，为何这么好的女孩子他就是喜欢不起来？如果喜欢上她，应该是件挺完美的事。
Guilt pricked Shen Jianchen—why couldn't he fall for a girl this sweet? Life would be so simple if he could!

街市的繁华令申简辰无法再自责下去，他被眼前的景象吸引。
Bianliang's dazzling bustle soon swept guilt aside, grabbing every ounce of his attention.

只见人流穿梭，商贩喧哗，一派生机和忙碌，热闹程度直逼申简辰经历过的现代大都市。街道两旁的摊位上摆着琳琅满目的商品：各种颜色鲜艳的丝绸、细腻的陶瓷器皿、香气扑鼻的茶叶、各色新鲜的水果蔬菜等等。
Crowds surged past clamoring vendors, pulsing with raw energy rivaling any modern metropolis he had known! Roadside stalls overflowed with vivid silks, delicate porcelain, fragrant teas, fresh fruits and vegetables.

他们的身旁，百姓络绎不绝地走过。士人们穿着青衫白带，挑着小篮子，在街上闲逛；有的品味着街边的小吃，有的观赏着街头艺人的表演；商贩们吆喝着自己的商品，争相吸引顾客的目光；婆婆们携着孩子逛街，一边讨价还价，一边与邻里交流。
Every walk of life streamed by: scholars in blue robes and white sashes strolling with small baskets; folks sampling street snacks or watching jugglers and buskers; peddlers shouting wares to catch eyes; grandmothers towing kids while haggling and gossiping with neighbors.

街市上的声音此起彼伏，有叫卖声、商贩的吆喝声、马车的嘶鸣声、人们的笑语声，汇聚在一起，形成了一曲繁华的乐章。街市上的繁忙和喧嚣，让申简辰感受到宋代汴梁城市生活的兴旺和活力。
Hawkers' cries, vendors' calls, whinnying carriage horses, and cheerful chatter blended into a lively chorus of city life, showing Shen Jianchen the vibrant heartbeat of Song-dynasty Bianliang.

他看到一家店铺门口挂着“脚店”的招牌，不由说道：“没想到宋代也有提供足浴的店铺。”
Spotting a shop sign reading "Foot Shop" (Jiaodian), he chuckled: "Whoa—even the Song dynasty had foot-massage parlors!"

“足浴？”乔梦容很是不解。
"Foot massage?" Qiao Mengrong blinked blankly.

申简辰指着“脚店”的招牌：“你看，那里不是写着吗？”
Pointing at the signboard, he grinned: "Look right there—Foot Shop!"

乔梦容看后，笑道：“那种小店不提供足浴，只是供人短暂歇脚的地方，这么大的汴梁城一天也逛不完，逛累了可以到脚店歇歇。”
Glancing over, Qiao Mengrong burst into laughter. "That isn't a foot-massage parlor! It's a little tavern where travelers pause to rest their feet—Bianliang is too big to walk in a day, so tired shoppers drop into a Foot Shop for a breather!"

申简辰为自己的无知感到惭愧：“没想到宋代也是一个充满人性化的时代。”
Red-faced at his own blunder, Shen Jianchen muttered, "Turns out Song cities were pretty thoughtful!"

乔梦容听得不是很明白，正在思索哥哥的话语，又听他问：“写着‘天之’‘美禄’字样的店铺又是干什么的？”
Still puzzling over his phrasing, she heard him ask next: "And what do shops marked 'Heaven's' and 'Beautiful Boon' sell?"

乔梦容解释道：“是酒馆。”
"Wine taverns," Qiao Mengrong explained.

“叫酒馆不就行了，偏偏用这样的词语，让人看不懂。”申简辰认为古人真是矫揉造作，他想不通“天之”“美禄”与酒有什么关系。
"Why not just write 'Tavern' instead of fancy riddles nobody gets?" Pretentious ancients—what did "Heaven's Beautiful Boon" have to do with booze?

乔梦容解释道：“哥哥以前告诉我，《汉书》有云：‘酒者，天之美禄’，此意乃赞美酒为上天所赐之美物。”
"You told me yourself before, Brother!" Qiao Mengrong smiled. "The Book of Han says, 'Wine is Heaven's beautiful boon'—praising fine liquor as a gift from Heaven."

穿越到古代后才真正意识到自己是文化浅薄之人，申简辰尴尬地说：“是我告诉你的？唉！失忆了什么都记不得，看来以后要把以前读过的书再拿出来读读。”
Hit again by how thin his classical education was, Shen Jianchen laughed awkwardly: "I told you that? Amnesia wiped everything clean—guess I ought to dust off my old books!"

“是该拿出来读读，官家喜欢有才学之人，尤其是精通诗词歌赋以及书画技艺的文人才子，哥哥以前身体不好，官家不理会也就罢了，现在身体健康，必然有机会面见官家，多读些书，只有好处。”
"You really should, Brother! His Majesty prizes men of learning—especially masters of poetry, calligraphy, and painting. While you were sick, he left you be; now that you're healthy, you're bound to face him sooner or later. Reading more can only help."

申简辰听得出，在这位妹妹心里还是很希望他的哥哥受到官家重视。对申简辰来讲，见不见官家无所谓，有没有重视更无所谓，反正他有的是田产，在陌生的时代富足一生应该没有问题，他只关心还能不能再遇见千里寻。
Clearly his little sister still hoped he'd win imperial favor. Shen Jianchen couldn't care less about favor or court audiences—with estates paying his bills, he was set for life! Only one question mattered: could he find Qianli Xun?

申简辰边想边欣赏街市，不觉走到一棵大树底下。
Strolling along lost in thought, he wandered beneath a massive shade tree.

树下搭着一个棚子，棚子里面设有卦摊，桌子上方拴着绳子，绳子上挂着三张长形的纸张，上面写着“神课”“看命”“决疑”，服务倒挺周全。
Underneath stood a canvas awning sheltering a fortune-teller's stall, three paper banners hanging overhead: "Divine Divination," "Destiny Reading," and "Doubt Resolution"—full service!

申简辰想要知道还能不能找到千里寻，也许算卦先生能帮他解决心中疑惑。可等待占卜的百姓甚多，排着长长的队伍，也不知会等到什么时候，只好放弃。
Eager to ask whether he'd ever find Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen eyed the long line of townsfolk waiting—could take hours! He started turning away.

乔梦容看出哥哥的心思，给身边的喜兰使了个眼色。
Catching his look, Qiao Mengrong signaled Xilan with her eyes.

喜兰上前拿出二两银子放在算卦先生面前。
Stepping right up, Xilan laid two taels of silver smack in front of the old fortune-teller.

算卦的老先生见到银子，立刻拿在手中，笑眯眯地对面前正在看相的人说：“麻烦，过去等一会儿，让贵客先看。”
Scooping up the silver with a wide grin, the old man waved aside the customer mid-reading: "Step over there a moment, friend—honored guests first!"

那人很不高兴坐到一边。
Grumbling, the displaced man shuffled aside.

“哥哥，你心中既有疑惑，不妨过去问问。”乔梦容把仪王拉到卦摊前坐下。
"Since something's weighing on your mind, Brother, go ask!" Qiao Mengrong drew him into the seat before the table.

算卦老先生说：“贵人想问什么？”
"What does the young lord wish to ask?" the fortune-teller beamed.

申简辰回道：“决疑。”
"Doubt Resolution."

“请写一个字，我帮您看看。”
"Write down any single character for me to read."

申简辰想了一会儿，提起毛笔，在纸上写下一个“寻”字，因没练过书法，字写得实在难看。
Pausing a beat, Shen Jianchen picked up the brush and wrote "Xun" (寻)—having never practiced calligraphy, the strokes looked atrocious.

乔梦容看了一阵心酸，哥哥以前虽很少写字，偶尔写过几次，字迹也比这好看许多，如此字迹如何能入得了官家的法眼。
A pang twisted Qiao Mengrong's heart—seldom as her brother wrote before, his hand had been far cleaner than this! How could brushwork this rough ever impress His Majesty?

老先生将纸张转过来，看了半天，问道：“贵人写得可是一个寻字？”
Spinning the sheet around and squinting hard, the old man asked, "Did the young lord write the character 'Xun' (寻, meaning 'to seek')?"

申简辰才意识到他写的是简体字，尴尬地点点头：“是。”
Realizing he had written the modern simplified form, Shen Jianchen nodded sheepishly: "Yes."

老先生提笔重新写一遍：“应该这么写。”
Lifting the brush, the old man rewrote it in full traditional form (尋): "Ought to be written like this."

申简辰仔细瞧去，繁体的“尋”字他确实不熟悉。
Eyeing the dense traditional character, Shen Jianchen admitted he barely recognized it.

“看来贵人是问寻人之事？”老先生撂下笔，一语中的。
"Asking about finding someone?" The old man set down his brush, hitting the bull's-eye first shot.

申简辰急切地回道：“是寻人，不知能否寻到？”
"Yes—finding someone! Can I find her?" Shen Jianchen leaned in eager.

老先生思索了一会儿说：“‘尋’字最上面的笔画为‘歸’字的一部分，‘歸’字其他笔画尽失，说明贵人您归来之地已无法再回去。……”
Studying the strokes a beat, the old man spoke: "The top radical of 'Xun' (尋) comes from 'Gui' (歸, 'return'), stripped of all its remaining strokes—meaning the place you came from is closed to you forever; you can never return..."

此话说到申简辰的心里，可不是吗？穿越而来，想要回去，已不可能。
Bull's-eye again! Having crossed centuries, going back was impossible.

“至于寻人，中间的‘工’字说明需费一番工夫，‘口’字则说明贵人会在一个与口有关之地遇到所寻之人，比如吃饭的地方，饮酒作乐之地，抑或是吟诗作赋之处。……”
"As for your search: 'Gong' (工, 'effort') in the middle means it will take real work; 'Kou' (口, 'mouth') shows you will encounter the one you seek at a place linked to the mouth—a restaurant, a wine tavern, or a hall where verse is recited..."

申简辰一阵欣喜，听这话千里寻也应穿越而来，他们还能再次相见。
Joy surged in Shen Jianchen's chest—Qianli Xun really had crossed over too, and their paths would meet!

“底下一个‘寸’字，说明您与所寻之人即使见到，方寸之间又无法相认……”
"At the bottom sits 'Cun' (寸, 'inch')—meaning even standing inches apart, you two won't recognize each other at first..."

“没关系，能找到便好。不知可否看出我在哪里能找到她？”
"No problem—long as I find her! Can you tell which direction to look?"

一旁的乔梦容甚是疑惑，哥哥到底要寻找什么人？为何从来没在她面前提起过？
Listening beside him, Qiao Mengrong frowned in puzzlement—who on earth was Brother hunting for? Why had he never breathed a word of this person?

“贵人写下的这个字，从卦摊所处方位来看，整体面朝南，如果我没猜错，您所找之人应该在南边。”
"Judging by the orientation of my stall, the character you wrote faces straight south. Unless I miss my mark, the one you seek lies to the south!"

算卦老先生一席话，申简辰豁然开朗，他起身谢过：“感谢老先生解惑，喜兰，再拿出一两银子给老先生，算我格外打赏。”
Light flooded Shen Jianchen's mind! Rising with cupped hands, he thanked him: "Much obliged for clearing my doubts, Old Sir! Xilan, hand him one more tael as an extra tip!"

喜兰又拿出一两，老先生更是喜出望外，站起来接过银子，感激不尽：“谢过贵人，谢过贵人，祝您早日找到所思之人。”算卦老先生继续坐下给其他客人占卜。
Xilan produced another tael; beaming wide, the old man rose to accept it with profuse bows: "Thank you, young lord! Thank you! May you soon reunite with the one you long for!" Sitting back down, he called the next patron.

乔梦容带着疑惑跟随哥哥离开卦摊，她仔细观察哥哥，占卜完后他一直面带笑容。
Walking away beside her smiling brother, curiosity gnawed at Qiao Mengrong.

乔梦容忍不住问：“哥哥要找的人是谁？”
Unable to hold back, she asked: "Brother, who are you looking for?"

申简辰被问住，该怎么告诉乔梦容？说了她也不信，况且说了，她便知道他心里装着别人，岂不是会难过？于是随口编道：“以前认识的一位朋友，好久没见，以为再也找不到，故而让老先生算算还能否遇见。”
Caught on the spot—tell her the truth and she'd dismiss it or ache realizing his heart belonged to another! Improvising fast, he shrugged: "An old friend I lost touch with ages ago—figured I'd never find them, so I asked the old man if our paths would cross."

哥哥一直病着，哪有人愿意与他做朋友？只有乔公子时常来做客。他的回答令乔梦容伤心，因为哥哥开始欺骗她。
Bedridden all his life, who did Zhao Pu ever call friend aside from Qiao Wenxian? Pain flickered in Qiao Mengrong's chest—her brother had started lying to her.

“哥哥不是什么都不记得？为何还记得以前的朋友？”
"Didn't you say you forgot everything, Brother? How do you remember an old friend?"

申简辰继续编着：“哦，刚才看见卦摊，猛然起一人，很模糊，来了兴致，想过去算算，顺便检验一下算卦老先生的水平。”
Spinning faster, he said, "Just spotting that stall flashed a blurry face in my head—got curious and figured I'd test if the old fortune-teller was any good!"

“检验的结果如何？他算得可灵验？”
"And? Was he accurate?"

“还行还行，要不怎么又打赏了一两银子。”
"Pretty solid—why else tip an extra tael?"

正说着，人群中传来一阵喊叫声：“抓住那小贼！他偷了我的钱袋！”
Right then frantic shouts broke through the crowd: "Stop that little thief! He stole my money pouch!"

申简辰循声看去，一位中年男子在人群中穿梭着，边跑边喊：“抓小贼！我的钱袋！”
Turning toward the clamor, Shen Jianchen saw a middle-aged man pushing through the throng yelling, "Catch the thief! My money pouch!"

只见一个年轻的身影从眼前穿过，速度飞快。
A lithe young figure darted straight past them like a streak of wind.

申简辰心中赞道：“根骨不错！”然后对喜兰说：“你带郡主到脚店歇息，我去抓贼。”
Great martial bones! Shen Jianchen thought in a flash, barking to Xilan: "Take Princess Jiahe into a Foot Shop to rest—I'm catching that thief!"

说完申简辰“蹭”地消失在乔梦容面前。
Whoosh—he shot forward and vanished into the crowd before Qiao Mengrong's eyes.

乔梦容一脸失落：“我们去脚店等哥哥吧。”
Left standing with a hollow sigh, Qiao Mengrong murmured, "Let's wait for Brother at the Foot Shop."
