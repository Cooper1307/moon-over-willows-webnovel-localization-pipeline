### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 武侠网文核心术语的精准转化（对应第12、16段：根骨）
> **【原文】** 他要选一名贴身侍卫，最好有些武功根基，更重要的是根骨要好……
> 每个人身强力壮……却没一个根骨好的。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    ...preferably someone with some martial arts foundation, and more importantly, someone with **good bone structure**...
    Each one was strong and robust... But not one had **good bone structure**.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ...preferably someone with a martial arts foundation, and more importantly, someone with **exceptional innate aptitude**.
    Each one was strong and robust... But not a single one possessed the **innate martial potential** he was looking for.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    在武侠玄幻网文中，“根骨”指修练武功的先天资质。直译为 "good bone structure" 在英语中通常指“骨相好/长得好看（如颧骨高、下颌线清晰）”，会引发严重歧义。改译为 "innate aptitude" 或 "martial potential" 完美契合武学天赋的语境。

#### 2. 女配心理活动的网文格式与情绪强化（对应第19段）
> **【原文】** 乔梦容边回忆边瞧着眼前的哥哥,终于成为她期待的样子,真希望明年的中秋节来得快一些,她不想再当他的妹妹,她要成为他真正的女人。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    As Qiao Mengrong recalled the past, she looked at her brother before her. He had finally become what she had hoped for. She truly wished the Mid-Autumn Festival next year would come quickly. She didn't want to be his sister anymore—she wanted to become his true woman.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Snapping out of her reverie, Qiao Mengrong gazed at the brother standing before her. He had finally become the man she had always hoped he would be. ***If only next year's Mid-Autumn Festival would come sooner,*** she thought longingly. ***I don't want to be his sister anymore. I want to be his true woman.***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 英文网文中，**角色的心理活动必须使用斜体 (Italics)**，以区分客观叙事和主观思绪。同时将长句拆分并重构，增强了乔梦容心中“骨科”暗恋的拉扯感。

#### 3. 宋代生活细节的自然对话（对应第35-37段：脚店）
> **【原文】** 他看到一家店铺门口挂着"脚店"的招牌,不由说道:"没想到宋代也有提供足浴的店铺。" 
> 乔梦容看后,笑道:"那种小店不提供足浴,只是供人短暂歇脚的地方..."

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    He saw a shop with a sign that read "Jiao Dian" (Foot Shop) hanging at the entrance, and couldn't help but say: "I didn't expect the Song Dynasty to also have shops that provide **foot baths**."
    After looking, Qiao Mengrong smiled and said: "That kind of small shop doesn't provide **foot baths**. It's just a place for people to rest their feet briefly."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Spotting a shop with a signboard reading *"Jiao Dian"* (Foot Inn), he couldn't help but marvel, "I had no idea the Song Dynasty also had parlors offering **foot massages**!"
    Qiao Mengrong followed his gaze and giggled. "Those small inns don't offer **foot baths**; they're merely places for travelers to rest their feet for a bit."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 增加了 "followed his gaze and giggled" 等动作细节，让兄妹逛街的日常对话更具画面感，而不是像在念百度百科。把现代人脑补的足浴店翻译为 "parlors offering foot massages"，更能体现男主的现代穿越者身份。

#### 4. 算命拆字的流畅表达（对应第61段：寻与归）
> **【原文】** 老先生思索了一会儿说:"'尋'字最上面的笔画为'歸'字的一部分,'歸'字其他笔画尽失,说明贵人您归来之地已无法再回去。……"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    The old man thought for a while and said: "The topmost strokes of the character '尋' are part of the character '歸' (to return). The other strokes of '歸' are completely lost, indicating that the place from which the noble one has returned can no longer be returned to..."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    The old man stroked his beard and pondered for a moment. "The topmost strokes of your traditional *Xun* (尋) character form the exact top of the character *Gui* (歸 - to return). However, the rest of the *Gui* character is completely missing. This indicates that the place from which you came can no longer be returned to..."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 中文拆字算命对外国读者很难懂。精修版增加了老头“抚须沉思”的视觉动作 (stroked his beard)，并用 "form the exact top of..." 和 "completely missing" 等连词优化了逻辑解释，使英文读者能毫无障碍地顺畅阅读。

#### 5. 结尾动作场面的爆发力（对应第79、80段：男主抓贼）
> **【原文】** 申简辰心中赞道:"根骨不错!"然后对喜兰说:"你带郡主到脚店歇息,我去抓贼。"
> 说完申简辰"蹭"地消失在乔梦容面前。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen praised in his heart: "Good bone structure!" Then he said to Xilan: "Take the Commandery Princess to the foot shop to rest. I'll go catch the thief."
    After speaking, Shen Jianchen disappeared from Qiao Mengrong's sight **in a flash**.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ***What exceptional martial aptitude!*** Shen Jianchen praised inwardly. Turning to the maid, he ordered, "Xilan, take the Princess to the inn to rest. I'm going to catch that thief!"
    Before Qiao Mengrong could even blink, Shen Jianchen vanished with a ***swoosh***, darting into the crowd.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. 修正了内心的赞叹为斜体（且同样替换了 bone structure 误译）。
    2. “蹭地消失”翻译为 "disappeared... in a flash" 过于平淡，改用 "vanished with a *swoosh*, darting into the crowd" 极大地凸显了男主作为一个顶级武术家的身法速度，爽文味十足。
