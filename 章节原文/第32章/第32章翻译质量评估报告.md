## 二、必须修改（3处）

### 问题1：文化误译——"方寸之间"
**位置**：第489段（算卦先生解读"寸"字）
**原文**：
> "底下一个'寸'字,说明您与所寻之人即使见到,方寸之间又无法相认……"
**当前译文**：
> "The 'Cun' (inch) character at the bottom indicates that even if you meet the person you seek, you won't be able to recognize each other within an inch..."
**问题**："方寸"在中文里指"心"（方寸之心），不是字面意义的"一寸之内"。此处是说即使相见，内心也无法相认。
**修改建议**：
```
"The 'Cun' (inch) character at the bottom indicates that even if you meet the person you seek, within your heart (方寸) you still won't be able to recognize each other..."
```

### 问题2：称谓不统一——"郡主"译为"princess"
**位置**：第593段（申简辰对喜兰说话）
**原文**：
> 申简辰心中赞道:"根骨不错!"然后对喜兰说:"你带郡主到脚店歇息,我去抓贼。"
**当前译文**：
> Shen Jianchen praised in his heart: "Good bone structure!" Then he said to Xilan: "Take the princess to the foot shop to rest. I'll go catch the thief."
**问题**："郡主"应统一译为"Commandery Princess"，而非笼统的"princess"（公主）。
**修改建议**：
```
...Then he said to Xilan: "Take the Commandery Princess to the foot shop to rest..."
```

### 问题3：动作描写不够准确——"使了个眼色"
**位置**：第365段
**原文**：
> 乔梦容看出哥哥的心思,给身边的喜兰使了个眼色。
**当前译文**：
> Qiao Mengrong saw through her brother's thoughts and gave a glance to Xilan beside her.
**问题**："使了个眼色"是"示意/使眼色"，不是简单的"看了一眼"，应译为"signaled"或"gave a meaningful look"。
**修改建议**：
```
Qiao Mengrong saw through her brother's thoughts and signaled to Xilan beside her with a glance.
```

## 三、建议优化（4处）

### 优化1：重复用词——"foot shop"
**位置**：第275、293、593、603段
**问题**："脚店"在章节中多次出现，均译为"foot shop"，可适当简化。
**建议**：首次出现用"Jiao Dian (Foot Shop)"，后续可简化为"the foot shop"或"the rest tavern"交替使用。

### 优化2：口语化表达——"not bad, not bad"
**位置**：第567段
**原文**：
> "还行还行,要不怎么又打赏了一两银子。"
**当前译文**：
> "Not bad, not bad. Otherwise, why would I reward him with another tael of silver?"
**建议**：可改为更自然的口语表达："Good enough. Otherwise, why would I..."

### 优化3：文化注释——"天之美禄"
**位置**：第323段
**当前译文**：
> Qiao Mengrong explained: "Brother once told me that the *Book of Han* says: 'Wine is Heaven's fine gift.' This means praising wine as a beautiful gift bestowed by Heaven."
**建议**：可增加简短译者注说明典故来源：
```
[TN: "天之美禄" (Heaven's Fine Gift) is a classical allusion from the *Book of Han* (《汉书》), used as an elegant euphemism for wine in ancient China.]
```

### 优化4：测字占卜的译者注可更详细
**位置**：第449段
**当前译者注**：
> [TN: In Chinese character divination, the fortune-teller analyzes the structure of the character written by the client. The client wrote the simplified "寻", but the fortune-teller rewrote it as the traditional "尋" to perform the divination.]
**建议**：可补充说明拆字逻辑，帮助读者理解后续占卜解读。
