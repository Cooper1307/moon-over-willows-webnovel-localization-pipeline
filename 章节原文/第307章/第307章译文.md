## Chapter 307: Matron Wei's Futile Plea

Matron Wei didn't mince words before Matron Zou—calling straight through the screen to point out that Heaven clearly refused to take her mistress, that Shuzhen had never crossed lines with Prince Yi, and sharpest of all, that every murderous scheme Mengrong cooked up only threw Prince Yi right back into saving Shuzhen!

Stung behind the screen, Mengrong stepped out in glacial majesty: "Smart points—I'll keep them in mind, yet don't dream I'll spare her. Tell her I'll *try* not to drag the Chens down with her."

Heart heavy with dread, Matron Wei returned to Moxiang Courtyard—seeing Haoyu chatting happily by his wife's bed—and told a merciful white lie: claiming Princess Consort Yi had agreed to let bygones be bygones so long as Shuzhen steered clear of royal gatherings, sparing Qianli Xun from fleeing her hard-won home.

Following Chen Wenxin's relieved visit from Zhu Manor, Zhu Xiyan called on the Judicial Office and kowtowed three crisp times to Prince Yi in deep gratitude for rescuing his daughter—pledging total obedience from himself and every official in Lin'an, even if it killed their side-skimming!

Twenty days later, as Moxiang Courtyard's lotus blossoms opened and silk-tree buds swelled, the Judicial Office finished documenting Black-Fiend Tiger's five capital crimes—arson, murder, robbery, pillage, and rape—sentencing him to execution after the autumn assizes while drafting lighter bandits into the northern army or Master Jiang's river patrols.

Honoring ten years of sworn brotherhood, Master Jiang brought wine and four dishes to Black-Fiend Tiger's death-row cell. Reminiscing over failing Bianliang's army recruiting together, turning outlaw, and walking divergent paths, Master Jiang shook his head sadly: "Why on earth grab Chen Haoyu's wife?! Prince Yi moves mountains for her—she's the one who got me Ferry Superintendent!"
