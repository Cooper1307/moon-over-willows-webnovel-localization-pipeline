# 第11章 朱府小院菊花诗 / Chapter 11: The Chrysanthemum Poem at Zhu Manor

---

从园子里迎面走出一位丫鬟，见千里寻先行礼：“小姐，我与几个负责洒扫的丫鬟已把院子和屋子收拾干净，您回来，正好住。”

A maid walked out from the garden and greeted Qianli Xun first, “Miss, the cleaning maids and I have already tidied up the courtyard and the rooms. You're back—perfect timing to move in.”

---

苦荞小声对小姐说：“她是绿桃。”

Kuqiao whispered to her miss, "That's Lutao."

---

千里寻笑道：“原来是绿桃啊，没想到我刚回来，你们就把屋子收拾干净，真是神速！”

Qianli Xun smiled, "So you're Lutao. I didn't expect you to have the place cleaned up so quickly after I got back. That was fast!"

---

绿桃觉得小姐跟以前很不一样，作为下人，不能当面问：“小姐忘了，您出嫁时吩咐，不管您回不回朱府，都让我每日差人把院子和屋子收拾干净。”

Lutao felt the miss was very different from before, but as a servant, she couldn't ask directly, "Has Miss forgotten? When you were married, you ordered that whether you returned to the Zhu residence or not, I should send someone every day to clean the courtyard and rooms."

---

“哦，原来是我吩咐的，可我已出嫁，嫁出去的女儿泼出去的水，我爹为何还把这园子给我留着？难道他舍不得我，专门给我留的？”千里寻想通过这个问题，从绿桃口中了解还未谋面的父亲对出嫁后的女儿到底是什么态度。

“Oh, so it was my order. But I'm already married—a married daughter is like spilled water. Why did my father keep this garden for me? Could it be he couldn't bear to part with me and reserved it specially？” Qianli Xun threw out the question intentionally, hoping to gauge her unseen father's true attitude toward his married daughter through Lutao's reaction.

---

绿桃本想摸摸小姐的额头，看她是不是发烧，要不怎么会问出这样的问题。见小姐额头有伤，没敢去摸，直接说：“小姐，您该不会因为额头有伤，影响到记忆力？这园子又远又偏，没人能瞧得上，老爷更不会把它当回事，只有小姐这样喜欢安静的人才把这里当成宝。”

Lutao had half a mind to feel her miss's forehead to see if she was running a fever. Otherwise, why would she ask such an absurd question? But seeing the wound on her forehead, she didn't dare. She said bluntly, “Miss, could the injury to your forehead have affected your memory? This garden is remote and secluded—no one else in the manor thinks much of it. The Master certainly wouldn't care about it. Only someone like you, who loves absolute quiet, would treasure this place.”

---

千里寻感到尴尬，努力掩饰：“我当然知道是这样，只是希望我爹能关心一下我，才故意问问。苦荞，随我进园子！”

A flash of awkwardness hit Qianli Xun, but she quickly covered it up. "Of course I know that. I just wanted to see if my father actually cared about me, so I asked on purpose. Kuqiao, come with me inside!"

---

苦荞对绿桃说：“快中午，姐姐去安排饭吧，有我陪小姐，你不用操心。”

Kuqiao turned to Lutao. “It's almost noon. Sister, go arrange the meal. I'll keep the Miss company—you don't need to worry.”

---

绿桃拜别而去。

Lutao bowed and took her leave.

---

千里寻踏入园子，她觉得像是进入乡间小院儿，又比乡间小院儿更加别致。有三处用低矮的栅栏围起：一处种着紫苏，紫色的叶子一片挨着一片，长得很茂盛；一处种着向日葵，高高的花盘迎着阳光；一处种着菊花，不只一个品种，有的正开放，有的还未到开放的季节。

Stepping into the garden, Qianli Xun felt like she was entering a quaint countryside courtyard, yet far more elegant. Three sections were neatly enclosed by low fences. One was planted with perilla, its purple leaves growing thick and lush, overlapping one another. Another held sunflowers, their tall heads turned proudly toward the sun. The third was filled with chrysanthemums—not just one variety. Some were already in full bloom, while others were still waiting for their season.

---

围着菊花的栅栏旁，放置一张精致的圆形木桌，旁边有把藤椅。

Beside the fence around the chrysanthemums stood an exquisite round wooden table, with a rattan chair next to it.

---

苦荞说：“在朱府时，小姐总喜欢烹上一壶茶，坐在藤椅上赏菊花。”

Kuqiao noted, "When you lived at the Zhu residence, Miss, you always liked to brew a pot of tea, sit in that rattan chair, and admire the chrysanthemums."

---

千里寻想象着朱淑真坐在这里手捧书卷，品茶赏菊的情景。她也想体验一番，走到藤椅跟前坐下，欣赏着已开放的菊花，不由诗兴大发，吟诵起朱淑真写过的一首诗：

Qianli Xun imagined the real Zhu Shuzhen sitting here, a book in hand, sipping tea and gazing at the flowers. She wanted to experience it too. Walking over, she sank into the rattan chair and looked at the blooming chrysanthemums. Suddenly inspired, she recited a poem Zhu Shuzhen had famously written:

---

土花能白又能红，晚节犹能爱此工。宁可抱香枝上老，不随黄叶舞秋风。(《菊花》)

> *This flower can be white or red,*
> *In later years, it still cherishes this craft.*
> *It would rather age clinging to the fragrant branch,*
> *Than dance with yellow leaves in the autumn wind.*
> *("Chrysanthemum" by Zhu Shuzhen)*

[TN: This translation of poem is sourced from Giggle Academy, a bilingual Chinese poetry platform. In Chinese culture, the chrysanthemum symbolizes resilience and unyielding integrity. The heroine uses it to express her firm resolve to maintain her dignity and seek a divorce, rather than compromise in an abusive marriage.]

---

苦荞激动地问：“小姐，您恢复记忆了？”

Kuqiao gasped excitedly, "Miss! Have you recovered your memory?"

---

原来这丫头虽找了重生的理由，可从心里还是愿意接受小姐只是失忆，听到千里寻吟诵朱淑真写过的诗，以为从前的小姐又回来。

It turned out that the loyal maid had accepted the "rebirth" explanation. But deep down, she still desperately hoped her miss had merely suffered amnesia. Hearing Qianli Xun recite her old poetry, she thought the former Zhu Shuzhen had returned.

---

千里寻没有回答她，而是问道：“刚才你让绿桃去安排饭食，难道我要在这里用饭？”

Qianli Xun didn't answer her. Instead, she asked, "Just now you told Lutao to arrange the meal. Am I supposed to eat here?"

---

苦荞的希望落空，眼前的小姐对朱府极其陌生，什么都不懂，她失落地回道：“是。”

Kuqiao's hopes were instantly dashed. The woman before her was still completely clueless about the Zhu residence. "Yes," she replied, her tone dropping in disappointment.

---

“我刚回朱府，为何不与我娘一起用饭？”

"I just returned to the Zhu residence. Why aren't I eating with my mother?"

---

“朱府很大，平常各园子各自独立，只有特殊日子或是老爷提出要求，大家才在一起用饭。今日小姐刚回来，老爷还未归，夫人不能与小姐一起用饭，要等老爷回来，征得老爷同意方可。”

"The Zhu residence is massive. Usually, each courtyard dines on its own," Kuqiao explained. "Only on special occasions, or when the Master specifically requests it, does everyone eat together. Since you just returned today and the Master hasn't come back yet, the Madam cannot dine with you. She must wait until he returns and gives his permission."

---

“什么？”千里寻站起来，“我跟我娘一起吃个饭，还得我爹同意？”

"What?!" Qianli Xun shot up from her chair. "I need my father's permission just to eat a meal with my own mother?"

---

苦荞解释道：“这是朱府历来的规矩。”

Kuqiao explained, "This has always been the rule at the Zhu residence."

---

千里寻很担心：“我娘刚才跟我说的那些同意我和离的话，难道也需征求我爹同意？”

A new worry suddenly gripped Qianli Xun. "Those things my mother just said... about supporting my divorce. Do those require my father's approval too?"

---

苦荞点点头：“这个家历来都是老爷做主，夫人说的那些话出自真情，也是心里话，但都需老爷点头方可。”

Kuqiao nodded grimly. "In this household, the Master makes all the decisions. What the Madam said came from the bottom of her heart, but ultimately, everything requires the Master's nod."

---

千里寻本以为请长辈出面之事，已搞定一半儿，现在才知分毫未搞定。

Qianli Xun had thought that having the elders intervene meant half the battle was won. Now she realized nothing had been settled at all.

---

“你为什么不早告诉我家里是我爹做主？”

"Why didn't you tell me earlier that my father is the one who makes all the decisions at home?"

---

苦荞很委屈：“小姐没问。”

"You didn't ask, Miss..." Kuqiao muttered, feeling wronged.

---

“算了，不能怪你。我记得你说过我爹很重视清誉，只要和离不影响朱家清誉，我估计他应该不会阻拦。”

"Forget it, I can't blame you. I remember you saying my father values his reputation highly. As long as an amicable divorce doesn't drag the Zhu family's name through the mud, I think he probably won't stand in the way."

---

“小姐，和离虽比被夫家所休传出去好听，可也不是什么好事，只怕老爷不会同意。”

"Miss, a mutual divorce sounds better than being cast out, but it's still considered a scandal. I'm afraid the Master won't agree so easily."

---

苦荞的话给了千里寻当头一棒，她镇定了一会儿，安慰自己：“我相信，但凡是当爹的，肯定会把子女的幸福看得更重要。”

Kuqiao's words felt like a bucket of cold water to the face. Qianli Xun took a moment to steady herself. "I refuse to believe it. Any decent father would put his child's happiness first."

---

接着又问出在向夫人面前提到的问题：“我爹凶不凶？”

Then she asked the same question she had raised before Madam Xiang: "Is my father fierce?"

---

“老爷毕竟是知府，有学问，有修养，他很少发怒，不过很严肃，让人望而却步，下人见到老爷大气都不敢出。”

“The Master is the Prefect of Lin'an, after all. He is highly educated and refined，” Kuqiao said. “He rarely loses his temper, but he is incredibly stern—so much so that people naturally shrink back from him. The servants don't even dare breathe too loudly when he's around.”

---

“有这么可怕吗？”

"Is he really that scary?"

---

“有，小姐您从小到大跟老爷都不亲近，就是因为他太过严肃，您跟老爷除了礼节性的交流，从来没在一起谈过心，聊过天儿。”

"Yes. From childhood until now, you've never been close to him, precisely because he's so intimidating. Beyond formal, polite greetings, you two have never had a heart-to-heart or casually chatted."

---

穿越前的时代，千里寻从小与父亲的关系最好，父亲总是宠着她，没事给她讲故事，跟她聊天儿，天南地北地瞎扯。她一直认为所有的父亲对待女儿都是如此。后来父亲出家，云游四海，依然会在每年中秋节时回来陪她一起度过。

In her past life, Qianli Xun had been closest to her father. He had always doted on her, told her stories, and chatted with her about everything under the sun. She had always naively assumed all fathers treated their daughters that way. Even after her father shaved his head to become a wandering monk, he still made sure to return every Mid-Autumn Festival to spend it with her.

---

她很难想象，朱淑真与她的父亲将父女关系处理成上下级关系是怎样一种感觉。

She could hardly imagine what it felt like for a father and daughter to treat their relationship like that of a boss and a subordinate.

---

“我爹这个样子怎么搞诗会？还不得把那些文人给吓跑了！”

"How on earth does a man like that host poetry banquets? Doesn't he scare all the scholars away?"

---

“老爷只在家里如此，他对待文人和同僚还是有笑脸的。”

"He is only like this at home. He still smiles when dealing with literati and his colleagues."

---

“真是个奇怪的人。”

"What a weirdo," Qianli Xun muttered.

---

两人正聊着，绿桃带领五个丫鬟和一个婆子进来。

As they were chatting, Lutao returned, leading five maids and an older servant woman.

---

“小姐，该用饭了，夫人吩咐多给您加两道菜。”

"Miss, it's time for your meal. The Madam specifically ordered the kitchen to add two extra dishes for you."

---

只见每个丫鬟手里端着一盘菜，婆子提着食盒。她们走进后，先向小姐行礼，然后进屋把饭菜摆好。

Each maid held a steaming plate of food, while the older servant woman carried a large food box. Walking in, they first curtsied to Qianli Xun, then proceeded inside to set the table.

---

绿桃又带众人行礼离开：“小姐慢用，有苦荞伺候，奴婢就不在此打扰。”

Lutao led the group in another bow. "Please enjoy your meal, Miss. With Kuqiao here to serve you, we shall take our leave so as not to disturb you."

---

千里寻点点头，心想绿桃还真懂事，猜到她只想让苦荞留下。

Qianli Xun nodded, thinking Lutao was quite perceptive—she had guessed Qianli Xun only wanted Kuqiao around.

---

她走进屋内，里面的布置与林家的竹兰居并无两样，看来朱淑真就喜欢这种雅而不俗的风格。

Walking into the room, she noticed the layout and decor were nearly identical in style to the Zhulan Residence at the Lin Manor. It seemed Zhu Shuzhen truly preferred this elegant, unostentatious aesthetic.

---

瞧到一桌子精美的食物，食欲大增，她快速坐在桌前享用起来。吃了两口，见苦荞在一旁只管伺候她，命令道：“你也坐下一起吃。”

Seeing the table laden with exquisite delicacies, her appetite flared. She sat down and dug in eagerly. After a few bites, she noticed Kuqiao just standing beside her, waiting to serve. "Sit down and eat with me," Qianli Xun ordered.

---

“这怎么行？哪有下人与主子同桌用饭？朱家没这个规矩。”

"How could I?!" Kuqiao gasped. "A servant eating at the same table as the Miss? The Zhu family has no such rule!"

---

“什么规矩不规矩的，这么多菜，我一个人吃不完，剩下多浪费，赶紧的，别废话。”

"To hell with the rules. There's way too much food here for me to finish alone, and it'd be a crime to waste it. Hurry up and sit down. Cut the nonsense."

---

苦荞见小姐态度坚决，不再推辞，也坐在桌旁：“没想到我有生之年竟然能和小姐一桌吃饭。”

Seeing her miss's unyielding attitude, Kuqiao stopped protesting and timidly sat at the table. "I never thought I'd live to see the day I could share a table with you, Miss."

---

“快吃吧，允许你以后天天跟我一桌吃饭。”

"Just eat. I'm giving you permission to eat at the same table with me every day from now on."

---

苦荞很感动：“小姐，我喜欢您现在的样子，虽然还很怀念以前的小姐，甚至希望小姐回到以前的样子，但我从心里更喜欢现在的小姐。”

Kuqiao's eyes misted over. "Miss... I really like the way you are now. Even though I miss the old you and sometimes wish you'd go back to normal, deep down in my heart, I prefer the 'you' sitting right here."

---

“什么以前的现在的？我就是你家小姐。”

"What do you mean 'the old me' and 'the new me'? I am your Miss."

---

“您说得对！”

"You're right!"

---

主仆二人动起筷子，有了苦荞加入，饭菜很快被吃个精光。用过饭后，千里寻在书桌前翻阅朱淑真曾经读过的书，感到有些困倦时，才宽衣躺到床上。

The master and servant picked up their chopsticks, and with Kuqiao pitching in, the plates were soon wiped clean. After lunch, Qianli Xun sat at the desk flipping through some books Zhu Shuzhen had left behind. Only when she began to feel drowsy did she undress and climb into bed.

---

大约半个时辰后她醒来，刚爬起穿好衣裳时，银杏前来拜见。

She woke up about an hour (half a shichen) later. Just as she finished dressing, Yinxing arrived to pay her respects.

---

苦荞把银杏领进屋时，千里寻看到她手里拿着一个锦盒。

When Kuqiao led Yinxing into the room, Qianli Xun noticed a small brocade box in the maid's hands.

---

“小姐，夫人让我把这个送来，说是替您预备的，本来是要在您的大侄子满月时送上，可小姐那时并未回朱府，既然现在回来，就由您亲自或派人送给您的大侄子。”

"Miss, the Madam asked me to deliver this. She said it was prepared especially for you. Originally, it was meant to be your gift for your eldest nephew's one-month milestone, but since you hadn't returned to the manor then, it was kept here. Now that you're back, you can present it to your nephew yourself."

---

苦荞接过锦盒，送到小姐手中。

Kuqiao took the brocade box and handed it to her miss.

---

千里寻打开一看，原来是只长命百岁的金锁，纯金打造，价值不菲。她感慨这位母亲实在是贴心，什么都替女儿操心到。合上盖子递给苦荞：“收好，一会儿随我去看看我的大侄子。”

Opening it, Qianli Xun found a Longevity Gold Lock nestled inside. Crafted from solid gold, it was clearly highly valuable. She couldn't help but marvel at how thoughtful Zhu Shuzhen's mother was. She had meticulously planned every little detail for her daughter. Closing the lid, she handed it back to Kuqiao. "Keep this safe. You're coming with me to see my eldest nephew shortly."

---

银杏完成任务，行完礼就要离开，被千里寻拦住：“留步，我还有事要问你。”

Her task complete, Yinxing bowed and was about to leave when Qianli Xun stopped her. "Wait. I have something to ask you."

---

银杏只好站住：“小姐有什么事，尽管吩咐。”

Yinxing paused. "Whatever you need, Miss, just ask."

---

“你去过林家？”

"Have you been to the Lin Manor?"

---

“奴婢去了，林家大爷说小姐想在朱府住多久都行，等姑爷回来，他定向姑爷转达。”

"I did. The Eldest Master Lin said you may stay at the Zhu residence for as long as you wish. He also promised to pass the message along to the young master once he returns."

---

“林家大爷？”

"The Eldest Master Lin?"

---

银杏疑惑小姐怎么连林家大爷都不知道，又不好过问，于是回道：“就是姑爷的大哥？”

Yinxing found it incredibly bizarre that the Miss didn't even know who Eldest Master Lin was, but she didn't dare question her. "Yes... the young master's eldest brother?"

---

千里寻怕再问下去，引起银杏怀疑，把已到喉咙的问题咽进肚内，只说道：“辛苦了，你去忙吧。”

Terrified that asking more would arouse Yinxing's suspicion, Qianli Xun swallowed the barrage of questions on the tip of her tongue. "You've worked hard. Go attend to your duties."

---

银杏刚一走，千里寻急切地问：“苦荞，我在林家也待了半天一晚，再加一早上，也没发现林觉有个大哥，怎么突然蹦出个大哥来？”

The second Yinxing stepped out, Qianli Xun turned urgently to her maid. "Kuqiao! I was at the Lin Manor for a day and a half, and I never saw a trace of an eldest brother! Where did this guy suddenly pop out from?!"

---

苦荞解释道：“林府虽比不上朱府大，可也不小，林家大爷住北边，林家二爷住东边，姑爷排行老三，住西面，离得都不近，平常大家很少照面。”

"Although the Lin estate isn't as massive as the Zhu residence, it's still quite large," Kuqiao explained. "The Eldest Master lives in the north courtyard, and the Second Master lives in the east. Our young master is the third son and lives in the west. The courtyards are far apart, so they rarely run into each other on normal days."

---

千里寻听到还有个二爷，更是担忧：“林觉家里的人倒挺多，两个哥哥再加上他父母，到时和离岂不是人多势众？”

Hearing there was a *Second* Master as well, Qianli Xun's heart sank. "Lin Jue has that many people in his family? Two older brothers plus his parents? When we sit down to negotiate the divorce, aren't they going to completely outnumber me?"

---

“小姐有所不知，姑爷的父母早已离世。”

"You might not remember this, Miss, but the young master's parents passed away a long time ago."

---

顿时减少两人，千里寻又感到庆幸：“那我就放心了。”

That instantly knocked two people off the enemy roster. Qianli Xun breathed a sigh of relief. "Well, that puts me at ease."

---

苦荞又说：“可林家是大家族，姑爷的族叔我知道的就有七位，而且还有我不知道的。”

"However," Kuqiao added, "the Lin family is a sprawling clan. I personally know of at least seven of the young master's paternal uncles, and there are likely more I don't even know about."

---

“什么？这么多？”千里寻顿时站起来，在屋子里走来走去，“我一个都没搞定，他那里就有那么多长辈，如此对比下去，到时我岂不是处于弱势？只怕想和离都难。苦荞，你说到时林家的长辈会不会一个都不会来？”

"What?! That many?!" Qianli Xun shot up and began pacing the room frantically. "I haven't managed to win over a single elder on my side yet, and he has an entire army of uncles waiting in the wings?! If we compare numbers, I'd be completely outnumbered. They'll make getting this divorce impossible!" She stopped and looked at Kuqiao. "Tell me honestly... is it possible that none of the Lin family elders will even bother showing up?"

---

“我看难。”苦荞也替小姐担忧。

"I think it's highly likely," Kuqiao admitted, looking just as worried as her miss.

---

千里寻瞅一眼苦荞手中的锦盒：“不如去拜见一下哥哥，先把他搞定再说。”

Qianli Xun's eyes darted to the brocade box in Kuqiao's hands. "Change of plans. Let's go visit my older brother right now and get him on our side first."

---

千里寻打定主意，就往出走，苦荞只能跟上。走到园子门外，千里寻停住脚步，她根本不知道朝哪个方向走：“苦荞，还是你前面带路。”

Mind made up, Qianli Xun marched out. Kuqiao scrambled to keep up. Reaching the garden gate, Qianli Xun stopped—she had no idea which direction to go. “Kuqiao, you'd better lead the way.”

---

苦荞又当起引路人，她们朝朱府的东边走去。

Kuqiao became the guide again. They headed toward the east side of the Zhu residence.
