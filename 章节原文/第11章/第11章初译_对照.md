第11章 朱府小院菊花诗
## Chapter 11: Chrysanthemum Verse in the Quiet Courtyard

从园子里迎面走出一位丫鬟，见千里寻先行礼：“小姐，我与几个负责洒扫的丫鬟已把院子和屋子收拾干净，您回来，正好住。”
A maid stepped out of the garden and bowed the instant she saw Qianli Xun. "Miss, the sweeping maids and I have already cleaned the courtyard and rooms—all ready for you to move right in."

苦荞小声对小姐说：“她是绿桃。”
Kuqiao whispered in her ear, "That's Lutao."

千里寻笑道：“原来是绿桃啊，没想到我刚回来，你们就把屋子收拾干净，真是神速！”
Qianli Xun smiled. "Ah, Lutao! I just walked in the gate and you already got the place spotless—fast work!"

绿桃觉得小姐跟以前很不一样，作为下人，不能当面问：“小姐忘了，您出嫁时吩咐，不管您回不回朱府，都让我每日差人把院子和屋子收拾干净。”
Sensing her mistress sounded off yet knowing her place as a servant, Lutao kept her surprise to herself. "Did you forget, Miss? When you married, you instructed me to have the courtyard and rooms cleaned every single day, whether you came back or not."

“哦，原来是我吩咐的，可我已出嫁，嫁出去的女儿泼出去的水，我爹为何还把这园子给我留着？难道他舍不得我，专门给我留的？”千里寻想通过这个问题，从绿桃口中了解还未谋面的父亲对出嫁后的女儿到底是什么态度。
"Oh, right—my orders! Still, I'm married now, and a married daughter is like water poured out. Why did Father keep this garden for me? Did he miss me and save it on purpose?" Qianli Xun fished for clues about her unseen father's feelings toward his married daughter.

绿桃本想摸摸小姐的额头，看她是不是发烧，要不怎么会问出这样的问题。见小姐额头有伤，没敢去摸，直接说：“小姐，您该不会因为额头有伤，影响到记忆力？这园子又远又偏，没人能瞧得上，老爷更不会把它当回事，只有小姐这样喜欢安静的人才把这里当成宝。”
Lutao almost reached up to check her mistress's forehead for fever—why ask such a question? Spotting the wound there, she held back and answered plain, "Miss, did that cut on your forehead rattle your memory? This garden is so tucked away nobody wants it, and Master pays it no mind. Only someone who loves peace and quiet like you treats it like treasure."

千里寻感到尴尬，努力掩饰：“我当然知道是这样，只是希望我爹能关心一下我，才故意问问。苦荞，随我进园子！”
Embarrassed, Qianli Xun covered fast. "Of course I know that! I just hoped Father cared about me a little, so I asked. Kuqiao, let's go in!"

苦荞对绿桃说：“快中午，姐姐去安排饭吧，有我陪小姐，你不用操心。”
Kuqiao told Lutao, "Almost noon, Sister—go see to lunch. I'm here with Miss, don't worry about us."

绿桃拜别而去。
Lutao bowed and took her leave.

千里寻踏入园子，她觉得像是进入乡间小院儿，又比乡间小院儿更加别致。有三处用低矮的栅栏围起：一处种着紫苏，紫色的叶子一片挨着一片，长得很茂盛；一处种着向日葵，高高的花盘迎着阳光；一处种着菊花，不只一个品种，有的正开放，有的还未到开放的季节。
Stepping inside, Qianli Xun felt as if she had entered a rustic cottage yard, only far more refined. Three plots sat behind low fences: one brimming with lush purple perilla leaves; one holding tall sunflowers angled toward the sun; and one filled with several varieties of chrysanthemums, some already in bloom, others still waiting for their season.

围着菊花的栅栏旁，放置一张精致的圆形木桌，旁边有把藤椅。
Beside the chrysanthemum fence stood a fine round wooden table paired with a rattan chair.

苦荞说：“在朱府时，小姐总喜欢烹上一壶茶，坐在藤椅上赏菊花。”
"Back when you lived here, Miss, you loved brewing a pot of tea and sitting in that rattan chair to admire the chrysanthemums," Kuqiao said.

千里寻想象着朱淑真坐在这里手捧书卷，品茶赏菊的情景。她也想体验一番，走到藤椅跟前坐下，欣赏着已开放的菊花，不由诗兴大发，吟诵起朱淑真写过的一首诗:
Picturing Zhu Shuzhen seated there with a book, sipping tea among the blossoms, Qianli Xun wanted to try it herself. She settled into the rattan chair, gazed at the open chrysanthemums, and felt a poetic spark stir—reciting a poem by Zhu Shuzhen:

土花能白又能红，晚节犹能爱此工。宁可抱香枝上老，不随黄叶舞秋风。（《菊花》）
"Wild blooms can show both white and red; in late-season virtue I prize such craft. Sooner grow old on the branch hugging my scent than dance with yellow leaves on the autumn wind." ("Chrysanthemum") [TN: Translator's rendering]

苦荞激动地问：“小姐，您恢复记忆了？”
Kuqiao gasped in excitement. "Miss, your memory came back!"

原来这丫头虽找了重生的理由，可从心里还是愿意接受小姐只是失忆，听到千里寻吟诵朱淑真写过的诗，以为从前的小姐又回来。
Even after inventing the "rebirth" excuse, the girl still clung to the hope her mistress only had amnesia; hearing her recite her own verse made Kuqiao think the old Zhu Shuzhen had returned.

千里寻没有回答她，而是问道：“刚才你让绿桃去安排饭食，难道我要在这里用饭？”
Instead of answering, Qianli Xun asked, "You just told Lutao to arrange lunch—am I eating here?"

苦荞的希望落空，眼前的小姐对朱府极其陌生，什么都不懂，她失落地回道：“是。”
Hope draining from her face—this mistress still didn't know basic house rules—Kuqiao replied in a flat tone, "Yes."

“我刚回朱府，为何不与我娘一起用饭？”
"I just got back to Zhu Manor. Why aren't I eating with Mother?"

“朱府很大，平常各园子各自独立，只有特殊日子或是老爷提出要求，大家才在一起用饭。今日小姐刚回来，老爷还未归，夫人不能与小姐一起用饭，要等老爷回来，征得老爷同意方可。”
"Zhu Manor is huge, Miss. Each courtyard dines on its own unless it's a special occasion or Master gives the word. You just arrived and Master isn't home yet, so Madam can't dine with you until Master returns and approves."

“什么？”千里寻站起来，“我跟我娘一起吃个饭，还得我爹同意？”
"What?" Qianli Xun jumped up. "I need my father's permission just to eat lunch with my own mother?"

苦荞解释道：“这是朱府历来的规矩。”
"That's always been the rule in Zhu Manor."

千里寻很担心：“我娘刚才跟我说的那些同意我和离的话，难道也需征求我爹同意？”
Dread crept in. "Wait—everything Mother just said about backing my mutual divorce... does that need Father's nod too?"

苦荞点点头：“这个家历来都是老爷做主，夫人说的那些话出自真情，也是心里话，但都需老爷点头方可。”
Kuqiao nodded. "Master makes every decision in this house. Madam meant every word from the heart, yet none of it counts unless Master agrees."

千里寻本以为请长辈出面之事，已搞定一半儿，现在才知分毫未搞定。
Just when she thought half the battle was won, she hadn't even left the starting line.

“你为什么不早告诉我家里是我爹做主？”
"Why didn't you tell me earlier Father runs the whole show?"

苦荞很委屈：“小姐没问。”
"You didn't ask, Miss," Kuqiao mumbled, feeling wronged.

“算了，不能怪你。我记得你说过我爹很重视清誉，只要和离不影响朱家清誉，我估计他应该不会阻拦。”
"Forget it, not your fault. You said Father guards family honor—long as mutual divorce doesn't stain the Zhu name, he shouldn't block it."

“小姐，和离虽比被夫家所休传出去好听，可也不是什么好事，只怕老爷不会同意。”
"Miss, mutual divorce sounds better than being cast out, sure, yet it's still no honor. I'm afraid Master won't agree."

苦荞的话给了千里寻当头一棒，她镇定了一会儿，安慰自己：“我相信，但凡是当爹的，肯定会把子女的幸福看得更重要。”
Kuqiao's words hit like a cold slap. Steadying herself, Qianli Xun tried optimism. "Any real father puts his daughter's happiness first."

接着又问出在向夫人面前提到的问题：“我爹凶不凶？”
Then she asked the same question she had asked Madam Xiang: "Is Father fierce?"

“老爷毕竟是知府，有学问，有修养，他很少发怒，不过很严肃，让人望而却步，下人见到老爷大气都不敢出。”
"Master is a Prefect—learned and cultured. He rarely loses his temper, yet he's so stern he keeps everyone at arm's length. Servants barely dare breathe around him."

“有这么可怕吗？”
"That scary?"

“有，小姐您从小到大跟老爷都不亲近，就是因为他太过严肃，您跟老爷除了礼节性的交流，从来没在一起谈过心，聊过天儿。”
"Yes! You were never close to Master growing up because he's so stiff. Aside from formal greetings, you two never once sat down for a heart-to-heart or a casual chat."

穿越前的时代，千里寻从小与父亲的关系最好，父亲总是宠着她，没事给她讲故事，跟她聊天儿，天南地北地瞎扯。她一直认为所有的父亲对待女儿都是如此。后来父亲出家，云游四海，依然会在每年中秋节时回来陪她一起度过。
Back in her old life, Qianli Xun had been closest to her dad—he spoiled her, spun stories, and bantered about everything under the sun. She had assumed all fathers loved their daughters like that. Even after shaving his head to wander the land, he still came home every Mid-Autumn Festival to spend the holiday with her.

她很难想象，朱淑真与她的父亲将父女关系处理成上下级关系是怎样一种感觉。
Treating a father-daughter bond like boss and subordinate sounded surreal.

“我爹这个样子怎么搞诗会？还不得把那些文人给吓跑了！”
"How does he even host poetry banquets acting like that? Doesn't he scare all the scholars off?"

“老爷只在家里如此，他对待文人和同僚还是有笑脸的。”
"Master's only like that at home. Around scholars and colleagues, he smiles plenty."

“真是个奇怪的人。”
"What a weird guy."

两人正聊着，绿桃带领五个丫鬟和一个婆子进来。
As they talked, Lutao arrived leading five maids and an older servant woman.

“小姐，该用饭了，夫人吩咐多给您加两道菜。”
"Time for lunch, Miss. Madam ordered two extra dishes added for you."

只见每个丫鬟手里端着一盘菜，婆子提着食盒。她们走进后，先向小姐行礼，然后进屋把饭菜摆好。
Each maid carried a dish while the older woman carried a food carrier. They bowed, stepped inside, and laid the table.

绿桃又带众人行礼离开：“小姐慢用，有苦荞伺候，奴婢就不在此打扰。”
Lutao led them in a farewell bow. "Enjoy your meal, Miss. With Kuqiao attending you, we won't disturb you."

千里寻点点头，心想绿桃还真懂事，猜到她只想让苦荞留下。
Qianli Xun nodded—tactful girl, sensing she only wanted Kuqiao around.

她走进屋内，里面的布置与林家的竹兰居并无两样，看来朱淑真就喜欢这种雅而不俗的风格。
Stepping inside, she found the interior matched Lin Manor's Zhulan Residence—clearly Zhu Shuzhen favored this refined, uncluttered style.

瞧到一桌子精美的食物，食欲大增，她快速坐在桌前享用起来。吃了两口，见苦荞在一旁只管伺候她，命令道：“你也坐下一起吃。”
Appetite roused by the spread of delicacies, she sat down and dug right in. Two bites in, spotting Kuqiao standing by ready to serve, she ordered, "Sit down and eat with me."

“这怎么行？哪有下人与主子同桌用饭？朱家没这个规矩。”
"I couldn't! Servants never eat at the same table as masters—Zhu Manor has no such custom!"

“什么规矩不规矩的，这么多菜，我一个人吃不完，剩下多浪费，赶紧的，别废话。”
"Forget custom! I can't finish all this food alone, and wasting it is a sin. Sit down, quit arguing."

苦荞见小姐态度坚决，不再推辞，也坐在桌旁：“没想到我有生之年竟然能和小姐一桌吃饭。”
Seeing her mistress wouldn't budge, Kuqiao gave in and took a seat. "Never dreamed I'd live to share a table with Miss."

“快吃吧，允许你以后天天跟我一桌吃饭。”
"Eat up. From now on, you eat at my table every day."

苦荞很感动：“小姐，我喜欢您现在的样子，虽然还很怀念以前的小姐，甚至希望小姐回到以前的样子，但我从心里更喜欢现在的小姐。”
Moved, Kuqiao said, "Miss, I really like you now. Even though I still miss the old Miss and sometimes wish she'd come back, deep down I like you even better."

“什么以前的现在的？我就是你家小姐。”
"Old me, new me—I'm your Miss either way."

“您说得对！”
"You're right!"

主仆二人动起筷子，有了苦荞加入，饭菜很快被吃个精光。用过饭后，千里寻在书桌前翻阅朱淑真曾经读过的书，感到有些困倦时，才宽衣躺到床上。
With Kuqiao pitching in, the two of them polished off every plate. Afterward, Qianli Xun browsed through Zhu Shuzhen's books at the desk until drowsiness crept in, then shed her outer robes for a nap.

大约半个时辰后她醒来，刚爬起穿好衣裳时，银杏前来拜见。
She woke about an hour later and had just pulled on her clothes when Yinxing arrived to call.

苦荞把银杏领进屋时，千里寻看到她手里拿着一个锦盒。
Kuqiao showed Yinxing in; Qianli Xun spotted a brocade box in her hands.

“小姐，夫人让我把这个送来，说是替您预备的，本来是要在您的大侄子满月时送上，可小姐那时并未回朱府，既然现在回来，就由您亲自或派人送给您的大侄子。”
"Miss, Madam sent this over—she prepared it on your behalf for your nephew's full-month celebration. Since you weren't home then, now that you're back, you can deliver it yourself or send someone with it."

苦荞接过锦盒，送到小姐手中。
Kuqiao took the box and handed it to her mistress.

千里寻打开一看，原来是只长命百岁的金锁，纯金打造，价值不菲。她感慨这位母亲实在是贴心，什么都替女儿操心到。合上盖子递给苦荞：“收好，一会儿随我去看看我的大侄子。”
Opening the lid, Qianli Xun saw a solid-gold Longevity Gold Lock—worth a small fortune. What a thoughtful mother, looking out for every detail! Closing the box, she handed it back to Kuqiao. "Keep it safe. Come with me to see my little nephew in a bit."

银杏完成任务，行完礼就要离开，被千里寻拦住：“留步，我还有事要问你。”
Task done, Yinxing bowed to leave, only for Qianli Xun to stop her. "Hold on—I have a question."

银杏只好站住：“小姐有什么事，尽管吩咐。”
Yinxing paused. "Yes, Miss?"

“你去过林家？”
"Did you go to Lin Manor?"

“奴婢去了，林家大爷说小姐想在朱府住多久都行，等姑爷回来，他定向姑爷转达。”
"I did. Eldest Master Lin said you may stay at Zhu Manor as long as you wish, and he'll pass word to Son-in-law once he returns."

“林家大爷？”
"Eldest Master Lin?"

银杏疑惑小姐怎么连林家大爷都不知道，又不好过问，于是回道：“就是姑爷的大哥？”
Puzzled that her mistress didn't know who that was yet too polite to pry, Yinxing answered, "Son-in-law's eldest brother."

千里寻怕再问下去，引起银杏怀疑，把已到喉咙的问题咽进肚内，只说道：“辛苦了，你去忙吧。”
Afraid more questions would arouse suspicion, Qianli Xun swallowed the rest and said, "Good work. Go tend to your duties."

银杏刚一走，千里寻急切地问：“苦荞，我在林家也待了半天一晚，再加一早上，也没发现林觉有个大哥，怎么突然蹦出个大哥来？”
The second Yinxing stepped out, Qianli Xun pounced on Kuqiao. "I spent half a day, a night, and a morning at Lin Manor and never saw any big brother—where did an eldest brother pop out from?"

苦荞解释道：“林府虽比不上朱府大，可也不小，林家大爷住北边，林家二爷住东边，姑爷排行老三，住西面，离得都不近，平常大家很少照面。”
"Lin Manor isn't as vast as Zhu Manor, yet it's still big," Kuqiao explained. "Eldest Master Lin lives in the north wing, Second Master Lin in the east, and Son-in-law is the third son over in the west. They live far apart and rarely cross paths."

千里寻听到还有个二爷，更是担忧：“林觉家里的人倒挺多，两个哥哥再加上他父母，到时和离岂不是人多势众？”
Hearing there was a second brother too made Qianli Xun's stomach sink. "He's got a whole crowd—two brothers plus his parents! Won't they outnumber me at the divorce meeting?"

“小姐有所不知，姑爷的父母早已离世。”
"You don't know, Miss—Son-in-law's parents passed away years ago."

顿时减少两人，千里寻又感到庆幸：“那我就放心了。”
Two down already—Qianli Xun exhaled in relief. "Well, that's a break."

苦荞又说：“可林家是大家族，姑爷的族叔我知道的就有七位，而且还有我不知道的。”
"Except the Lins are a huge clan," Kuqiao added. "Just the clan uncles I know of number seven—and there are more I don't know."

“什么？这么多？”千里寻顿时站起来，在屋子里走来走去，“我一个都没搞定，他那里就有那么多长辈，如此对比下去，到时我岂不是处于弱势？只怕想和离都难。苦荞，你说到时林家的长辈会不会一个都不会来？”
"What? That many?" Qianli Xun sprang up and paced the floor. "I haven't nailed down a single elder yet, and he has an army! With those odds, they'll steamroll me—I'll never get divorced! Kuqiao, any chance none of the Lin elders show up?"

“我看难。”苦荞也替小姐担忧。
"Not likely," Kuqiao said, sharing her worry.

千里寻瞅一眼苦荞手中的锦盒：“不如去拜见一下哥哥，先把他搞定再说。”
Eyeing the brocade box in Kuqiao's hands, Qianli Xun decided, "Let's go see my brother first and get him locked in."

千里寻打定主意，就往出走，苦荞只能跟上。走到园子门外，千里寻停住脚步，她根本不知道朝哪个方向走：“苦荞，还是你前面带路。”
Mind made up, she headed straight out, Kuqiao hurrying after her. Right past the garden gate, Qianli Xun froze—she had zero clue which way to go. "Kuqiao, take the lead again."

苦荞又当起引路人，她们朝朱府的东边走去。
Resuming her role as guide, Kuqiao led her toward the eastern side of Zhu Manor.
