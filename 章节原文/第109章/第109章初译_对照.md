第109章 离开朱府买礼物
## Chapter 109: Leaving Zhu Manor to Buy a Gift

乔守仁态度坚决，申简辰没必要多加劝说，再说劝说无益。
With Qiao Shouren digging in his heels, further persuasion was pointless.

更重要的是申简辰不想继续劝说，既然老天让提亲之事出现阻碍，说明文渊不是千里寻的最终归宿，他申简辰还有希望。
More important, Shen Jianchen did not want to keep persuading him. If Heaven itself threw roadblocks in front of this proposal, maybe Wenyuan was not Qianli Xun's final destination—maybe Shen Jianchen still had a shot.

带着希望，申简辰吩咐白风赶马车去朱府。
With fresh hope stirring, Shen Jianchen told Bai Feng to drive to Zhu Manor.

把女儿赶出家门，朱晞颜去了趟府衙。独自坐在府衙内，思索该找谁查传播谣言之人？
After driving his daughter out, Zhu Xiyan had gone to the prefecture office. Sitting alone in his study, he mulled over whom to send after the rumor-mongers.

此事乃朱家私事，不宜动用官府之人。可朱家的下人伺候人尚可，查找传播谣言之人，能力尚欠缺。
This was private Zhu family business—not something to mobilize official yamen runners for. Yet Zhu Manor's servants were good enough for waiting tables, useless for tracking down schemers.

朱晞颜想到林觉，既是曾经的女婿，又听话，关键是手下有士兵，办事极其方便。于是他又出府衙，往练兵署而去。
Then Zhu Xiyan thought of Lin Jue: a former son-in-law, eager to please, and best of all, with soldiers under his command who could track leads with ease. Leaving the prefecture office, he headed straight for the Military Training Office.

到达练兵署，在士兵的引领下，来到统领办公之地，正好碰到副都统制从里面出来。
Guided by a soldier to the Commandant's quarters, he ran right into the Vice Commander-in-Chief stepping out.

副都统制见知府前来，甚至恭敬：“知府大人。”
Seeing the Prefect arrive, the Vice Commander-in-Chief bowed with utmost deference. "Lord Prefect."

朱晞颜寒暄道：“好久不见，副都统制大人近来可好？”
Zhu Xiyan exchanged pleasantries: "Long time no see. How has the Vice Commander-in-Chief been keeping?"

“还是老样子，公务繁忙，知府大人最清楚不过。”
"Same as ever—buried in official duties, as Lord Prefect knows all too well."

“是啊，我等虽为地方官员，也是为朝廷效力，难得有清闲时间。”
"Indeed. Local officials like us serve the court; idle moments are scarce."

“不知知府大人前来有何指教？”
"May I ask what brings Lord Prefect here?"

“哦，我找林统领有些事。”
"Oh, I have a word for Commandant Lin."

“林统领正在里面，知府大人请便，若有任何吩咐只管派人告知下官。”
"Commandant Lin is right inside. Please go ahead, Lord Prefect—if you need anything at all, just send word to me."

朱晞颜满意地点点头，管理军政的官员虽不直接受他管辖，见到他倒也恭敬。
Zhu Xiyan nodded in satisfaction. Even military officers outside his direct chain of command treated him with full respect.

他走进统领办公之地，林觉正在处理公务，抬头见曾经的岳丈前来，起身迎上去：“知府大人突然光临，实乃意外之喜，快上座。”
Stepping into the Commandant's office, he found Lin Jue working at his desk. Looking up to see his former father-in-law, Lin Jue jumped up to greet him: "Lord Prefect's sudden visit is a pleasant surprise! Please take the seat of honor!"

朱晞颜对林觉的表现甚感欣慰，这位曾经的女婿最大的优点就是对他够恭敬。
Zhu Xiyan felt pleased; his former son-in-law's biggest virtue was always his deference.

坐下后，林觉吩咐士兵上茶，然后恭顺地站立一旁等待发话。
Once Zhu Xiyan sat, Lin Jue ordered a soldier to serve tea, then stood waiting at attention.

待士兵上完茶退出后，朱晞颜看一眼旁边的士兵。
After the soldier served tea and left, Zhu Xiyan glanced at the guards still by the door.

林觉猜到朱晞颜前来所说之事不想被他人知晓，于是吩咐道：“你们都退下。”
Catching the hint that this was confidential, Lin Jue waved them off: "All of you, step outside."

士兵退出后，林觉将门关好，谨慎道：“已安全，知府大人请讲。”
Once they withdrew, Lin Jue shut the door and said with care, "All clear now. Please speak, Lord Prefect."

朱晞颜先恭喜道：“一段时日未见，你已从练兵官一跃成为统领，可喜可贺。”
Zhu Xiyan offered congratulations first: "Only a short while since we met, and you've leaped from Training Officer to Commandant. Well done."

林觉谦虚道：“主要是节度使与诸位大人信任，才选择下官。”
Lin Jue replied with modesty, "All thanks to the trust of the Military Commissioner and my superiors."

“节度使大人也知此事？”朱晞颜感到疑惑，仪王从不参与政事，林觉被提拔之事又怎与他有关？
"The Military Commissioner was involved?" Zhu Xiyan blinked in surprise. Prince Yi never touched civil or military affairs—how had he backed Lin Jue's promotion?

林觉笑笑说：“知府大人有所不知，有一日，节度使大人前来给士兵训话，下官正好在，接待了贵人，其他几位大人随后也都纷纷拜见，正好统领的位置空缺，大家都推举我，节度使大人也无不认可。”
Lin Jue smiled. "Lord Prefect wouldn't know. One day the Military Commissioner came by to address the troops, and I happened to be on hand to host him. The senior commanders hurried over to pay respects too; with the Commandant's post vacant, they all recommended me, and the Military Commissioner approved."

“哦，原来是这么回事。”朱晞颜不想再问下去，还是尽快说正事，“本官来找你，是想麻烦你件事。”
"Ah, so that's how it happened." Unwilling to linger on small talk, Zhu Xiyan moved to business. "I came to ask a favor of you."

“知府大人尽管说，只要下官能力所及，定当尽力而为。”林觉虽已放下朱淑真，可这位曾经的岳丈他可不想放弃，在官场混，不能没有靠山。
"Say the word, Lord Prefect—anything within my power, I'll give my all." Even though Lin Jue had let Zhu Shuzhen go, he was not about to let go of his former father-in-law; nobody climbed in officialdom without backing.

毕竟是麻烦林觉，朱晞颜不好让他一直恭顺地站着：“来，坐下说。”
Since he was asking a favor, Zhu Xiyan could not keep Lin Jue standing. "Come, sit down and talk."

林觉乖乖坐下。
Lin Jue sat down at once.

待林觉坐好后，朱晞颜犹豫片刻说道：“我找你是为私事，你肯定已听到关于淑真的谣言，我想麻烦你派些人手，帮我查查谣言是谁人传出。”
Once he was seated, Zhu Xiyan paused a beat before saying, "I came on a private matter. You must have heard the rumors about Shuzhen. I want you to send men to find out who started them."

提到谣言，林觉心情复杂。既觉传谣之人实在可恶，连林家都牵扯其中。又觉朱淑真被大家非议是罪有应得，谁让她嫁到林家不好好跟他过日子，一天尽想些风流才子，刚和离就扑到乔文仙怀里，实在有失大家闺秀的身份。
Mention of the rumors stirred mixed feelings in Lin Jue. On one hand, the rumor-monger was despicable for dragging the Lin family into the mud too; on the other, Zhu Shuzhen had it coming—refusing to live a quiet wife's life in the Lin house, pining for dashing scholars all day, and throwing herself into Qiao Wenxian's arms the second they divorced. Hardly noble-lady conduct.

知府大人为此事而来，林觉心有不满，为了前程，还是一口应下：“知府大人放心，我定派人彻查此事，您就等我的好消息。”
Annoyed as he felt, his career came first, so he agreed on the spot: "Rest easy, Lord Prefect. I'll send men to investigate from top to bottom—just wait for my good news."

朱晞颜说：“你虽与淑真和离，可在我眼里依然把你当自家孩子看待，以后要是看上哪家姑娘，尽管告诉我，我出面替你说合。”
Zhu Xiyan said, "Even though you and Shuzhen divorced, I still think of you as my own boy. Whenever a young lady catches your eye, tell me, and I'll speak on your behalf."

林觉心想，他想要个丫鬟，朱晞颜都解决不了，还请仪王出面劝说。现在用上他，又说这种客套话，要不是看在他是临安知府，官场上多少会帮到自己，他才不会如此恭敬。
Lin Jue scoffed to himself: when he wanted a single maid, Zhu Xiyan couldn't handle it and sicced Prince Yi on him! Now that he needed a favor, out came the sweet talk. If not for the Prefect's clout in officialdom, he wouldn't bother bowing and scraping.

“感谢知府大人关怀，若有中意的，定然告知。”
"Thank you for your kindness, Lord Prefect. If someone catches my eye, I'll be sure to let you know."

朱晞颜点头微笑，很快又笑容全无，用平静的语气说：“我没把淑真教育好，出了这等事，我已将她赶出家门，以后她与朱府再无关系。但谣言还在，我绝不允许有人故意诋毁朱家清誉，此事林统领若帮朱家办成，不出一年，统制的位置定然花落林家。”
Zhu Xiyan smiled, then let his smile vanish into a cool, steady tone: "I failed to raise Shuzhen right. After this scandal, I cast her out of Zhu Manor; she has no ties to us anymore. Yet the rumors remain, and I will not tolerate anyone targeting the Zhu family's good name. Pull this off for us, Commandant Lin, and within a year the Division Commander's post will be yours."

又有升职机会，林觉自然高兴，立刻起身，更加恭顺道：“知府大人放心，朱家的事，也就是我的事。”
Another promotion dangling right in front of him! Thrilled, Lin Jue sprang up and bowed even lower: "Rest easy, Lord Prefect! The Zhu family's business is my business!"

“很好，一旦查到任何线索，直接到朱府告诉我。”
"Good. The moment you find a lead, come straight to Zhu Manor and report to me."

“是。”
"Yes, sir!"

办完事，朱晞颜回到朱府，还未换官服，又将儿子朱景元叫到跟前，让他找些下人装扮成喝茶的，没事待在茶馆里查探谣言来源。下人虽作用不大，但朱晞颜觉还得派些人出去，能探多少探多少。
Back at Zhu Manor, before even changing out of his official robes, Zhu Xiyan summoned Zhu Jingyuan and told him to dress several servants as ordinary tea-drinkers to hang around teahouses fishing for leads. Servants might not turn up much, yet every extra pair of ears helped.

刚安排好，有人来报：“老爷，仪王前来拜访，正在门外候着。”
No sooner had he arranged it than a servant ran in to report: "Master, Prince Yi is at the gate asking to see you!"

朱晞颜整理好官服，带上儿子匆忙向府外走去。来到侧门时，见仪王站在门外，上前拜道：“下官拜见仪王殿下，不知殿下驾到，若有不到之处，还请见谅。”
Straightening his robes, Zhu Xiyan hurried out with his son. Reaching the side gate, he saw Prince Yi waiting outside and stepped forward to bow. "This humble official greets Your Highness Prince Yi. Forgive me for failing to welcome you sooner!"

申简辰说：“我来得匆忙，没有时间提前通知知府大人，还请知府大人不要介意。”
Shen Jianchen said, "I came in a rush with no time to send word ahead—don't mind it, Lord Prefect."

“仪王能来拜访，朱府蓬荜生辉，又怎敢介意？仪王若不嫌弃，请到府内坐坐。”
"Prince Yi's visit brings honor to Zhu Manor! If Your Highness doesn't disdain our humble home, please step inside for a seat."

“今日我还有事，改日再到府上叨扰。”
"I have other business today; I'll impose another time."

朱晞颜纳闷，仪王来了，又不愿进府，不知是为何事？于是恭敬地问：“不知仪王前来有何吩咐？”
Puzzled why Prince Yi had come without stepping inside, Zhu Xiyan asked with deference, "May I ask what instructions Your Highness has for me?"

申简辰说：“我从乔府而来，两家之事已清楚，希望知府大人不要因此对乔家不满。”
Shen Jianchen said, "I just came from Qiao Manor and heard what happened between the two houses. I hope Lord Prefect won't hold it against the Qiao family."

原来是为此事，朱晞颜怎能对乔家没有意见？可当着仪王的面，他万万不会承认：“怪不得他人，只怪我没把自家闺女教育好。此事惊动仪王，下官惶恐。”
So that was it! How could Zhu Xiyan not resent the Qiaos? Yet before Prince Yi, he would never admit it. "No one to blame but myself for raising my daughter poorly. Having this trouble Prince Yi fills me with shame."

“知府大人不必自责，依我看您把女儿教育得很好，至于外面的谣言，知府大人不必太当回事。”
"Don't blame yourself, Lord Prefect. In my eyes, you raised a fine daughter. As for the street gossip, don't pay it too much mind."

在全城百姓诋毁朱家姑娘的情况下，仪王如此替朱家说话，朱晞颜感激不尽，跪在地上：“仪王圣明！”
With the whole city tearing his daughter apart, hearing Prince Yi speak up for the Zhu family overwhelmed Zhu Xiyan. He dropped to his knees. "Your Highness is wise and gracious!"

申简辰扶起朱晞颜：“知府大人快起来，我是替文渊而来，他告诉我您把幽栖居士赶出去，不知她搬去哪里？”
Shen Jianchen helped him up. "Get up, Lord Prefect. I came on Wenyuan's behalf. He told me you sent Youqi Jushi away—where did she move?"

朱晞颜竟然感动得流下一滴泪，仪王不仅为朱家说话，还帮乔家那小子打听淑真，说明乔家那小子对淑真的确有真情，他的眼泪也是为淑真高兴而流。
A tear of genuine emotion slipped down Zhu Xiyan's cheek. Not only did Prince Yi defend the Zhu house, he was tracking down Shuzhen for young Qiao—proving that boy really did love Shuzhen! His tear was pure relief for his daughter.

朱景元见父亲激动，半天不言语，主动说：“仪王殿下，淑真新买的宅子在城东，叫望月轩。”
Seeing his father too moved to speak, Zhu Jingyuan stepped in: "Your Highness, the new house Shuzhen bought is in the east of the city—it's called Wangyue Pavilion."

“知府大人，我还有事，改日再聊。”已大听到重要信息，申简辰不想耽搁下去，匆忙登上马车，命令白风去城东。
"I have errands to run, Lord Prefect. We'll talk another day." Having got the vital piece of news, Shen Jianchen wasted no time, climbing back into his carriage and ordering Bai Feng to head east.

朱晞颜只觉这位仪王来得匆忙也走得匆忙。
Zhu Xiyan watched him go, marveling at how fast this prince came and went.

不过仪王说的一席话，令朱晞颜的心情好了许多，世间总有像仪王这等明理之人。
Still, Prince Yi's words lifted a boulder from his chest. At least there were still reasonable men like Prince Yi in this world.

马车行到闹市，申简辰让白风把车停下，他打算买件礼物送给千里寻。
Passing through a busy market, Shen Jianchen told Bai Feng to pull over—he wanted to buy a gift for Qianli Xun.

她被百姓非议，又被赶出朱府，与文渊的婚事也泡汤，心情肯定不好。送个礼物给她，心情应该会好些。
Mocked by the whole city, thrown out of Zhu Manor, her marriage to Wenyuan blown to pieces—she had to be miserable. A little gift might cheer her up.

申简辰从未给女孩子买过礼物，他感到为难。一下马车，问道：“白风，你说女孩子都喜欢什么礼物？”
Never having bought a gift for a girl before, Shen Jianchen felt stumped. Stepping down, he asked, "Bai Feng, what kind of gifts do girls like?"

“王爷要买给佳和郡主吗？”
"Is Your Highness buying for Princess Jiahe?"

“别瞎问，你只需告诉我送女孩子什么礼物她会高兴？”
"Quit guessing—just tell me what gift makes a girl happy."

白风想想说：“我给苦荞送的是泥人，她挺开心的。”
Bai Feng thought a second. "I gave Kuqiao a clay figurine, and she loved it."

“那就送给泥人吧。”
"A clay figurine it is, then."

正好瞧见有家店铺售卖泥人，两人走进去，里面摆放的泥人活灵活现，形态各异。
Spotting a clay figurine shop right nearby, the two stepped inside, surrounded by shelves of lifelike painted figures in every pose imaginable.

伙计见有人前来，迎上去，露出殷勤的表情：“客官里边请，我家店各色泥人均有，您尽管挑。”
A clerk hurried over with an eager grin. "Right this way, gentlemen! We carry every kind of clay figurine—take your pick!"

申简辰四下瞅瞅，将目光停留在撒尿娃娃的身上，小娃娃圆眼圆嘴圆脑袋，做出撒尿姿势，实在可爱，只看一眼，便觉开心，绝对能将千里寻逗乐。
Scanning the shelves, Shen Jianchen's gaze locked onto a peeing-boy figurine: round eyes, round mouth, round head, mid-pee—so cheeky and adorable that one glance made him grin. It was guaranteed to crack Qianli Xun up.

“伙计，把那个泥人装起来。”
"Clerk, wrap that figurine up."

白风一瞅，实在想不通，王爷为何要送佳和郡主一个撒尿娃娃？要送也应该送一对喜娃娃，既温馨，寓意又好。
One look at it left Bai Feng baffled. Why on earth would His Highness give Princess Jiahe a peeing boy? Shouldn't he get a pair of lucky wedding dolls—sweet and auspicious?
