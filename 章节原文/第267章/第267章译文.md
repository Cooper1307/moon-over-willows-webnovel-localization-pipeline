## Chapter 267: Maids Blocking Attendants in Two Households

When Xilan finally returned from the Judicial Office, Qiao Mengrong scolded her for taking so long until Xilan explained she had missed Prince Yi on his first errand and waited until he came back—using the wait to inspect every inch of the yamen and confirm not a single woman worked there.

Pleased, Mengrong asked, "Did Brother say when he's coming home?"

"He was about to leave right away when another case popped up, so he promised to come back once it wraps up, no matter how late."

Satisfied, Mengrong held off ordering another bath, mindful of Matron Zou's advice not to drain Brother's stamina—just having him beside her was bliss enough.

Yet dusk dragged into deep night with no sign of him, stirring fresh resentment over that Judicial Investigator job that kept him out from dawn till dark. Just as gloom threatened to swallow her, Brother stepped through the doorway. Every cloud vanished as she threw herself into his arms. "Brother, I missed you so much!"

On the ride home, Shen Jianchen had decided not to breathe a word about the pamphlets—he'd already burned them, and dragging it up would only sour things. Once he loved her right and healed her wound, she'd stop pulling foolish stunts. Wrapping his arms around her, he murmured, "Work ran late today, yet my thoughts stayed on you the whole time."

He hated how easily polite lies rolled off his tongue now, yet tonight's warmth and his vow to be good to her pushed the words out.

Thrilled by the firm grip of his arms—*he's mine, nobody touches him!*—Mengrong reached up to kiss him. After a split second's hesitation, he cupped her face and kissed her back.

Pulling the doors shut from outside, Xilan turned on Bai Feng. "What are you loitering here for? The Princess Consort's taking care of His Highness—he doesn't need you."

Wearing a mix of amusement and relief, Bai Feng turned toward the guard quarters before Xilan called him back.

"Hold up!"

"What?"

Stepping close, Xilan asked bluntly, "Still carrying on with Kuqiao?"

"Of course."

"Take my advice and drop her."

"Why?"

"Princess Consort Yi will never approve of you marrying her. Youqi Jushi crossed Her Highness, so Her Highness can't stand her maid either."

Frowning, Bai Feng argued, "His Highness consummated the marriage and treats the Princess Consort right—she's bound to let bygones be bygones with Youqi Jushi. Why block me and Kuqiao?"

"Said my piece—believe it or not, up to you." Turning on her heel, Xilan marched off to the maids' quarters, leaving Bai Feng muttering defensively, "His Highness makes the calls on my marriage!"

Inside, Mengrong rested her cheek against Shen Jianchen's chest after their kiss. "Why did you hesitate for a second just now, Brother?"

Because Qianli Xun's shadow had blinked across his mind—though he covered smoothly against her sharp radar. "Covered in dust from working the yamen all day. Didn't want to dirty you."

"I'll never find you dirty." Forgetting her vow to go easy on him tonight, she whispered, "Let me bathe with you."

"Too late for a bath—let's turn in." Scooping her into his arms, he carried her straight into the bedchamber, leaving Mengrong glowing as though she held the entire world.

Over at Chen Manor, Qianli Xun returned after dark from the congee shed too tired to touch a brush and flopped straight onto her bed.

Kuqiao came in to report. "Right after you took the ten servants out, Eldest Madam worried Moxiang Courtyard would sit uncleaned and sent ten house servants over to sweep and tidy—super polite, too! Said Eldest Madam ordered them to come every single day you run the congee shed."

"Talk about winning the mother-in-law lottery," Qianli Xun sighed. "Nobody's got a better one than mine." Remembering her resolve to test the waters with Haoyu tonight, she sat up. "Is Chen Haoyu back yet?"

"Not yet—ought to be any minute."

Right on cue, Haoyu's voice floated through the door. "Wife, I'm back! How did the congee shed go today?"

"Open up and let him in, Kuqiao." Sitting before her bronze mirror, Qianli Xun checked her reflection—looked decent enough.

Sensing something special in the air tonight, Kuqiao opened the door. "Miss invites you in, Young Master."

Haoyu strode in grinning, while Qinggua tried to follow—only for Kuqiao to block him and ease the door shut behind Haoyu. "Chouya worked herself ragged at the congee shed today—stop hanging around here and go check on her!"

"I have to wait on Young Master!"

Pushing him down the steps, Kuqiao hushed him. "My mistress is inside—he doesn't need you."

Qinggua's eyes lit up. "Wait—is Young Madam finally consummating the marriage with Young Master tonight?!"

"Consummating or not, tonight's different—don't barge in!"

Right then Chouya stormed over. "What're you two pawing at each other for?! Qinggua, don't tell me you've got eyes for Sister Kuqiao—she's got Guard Bai, she wouldn't look twice at you!"

Relieved to hand Qinggua off, Kuqiao rolled her eyes. "Don't drag me into your drama—I'm going to bed." Slipping back into Yuening Room, she left Qinggua grabbing Chouya's hand.

"Quit guessing wild stuff—come talk in the West Courtyard."

"You swear you don't like Sister Kuqiao?"

"Listen straight: I used to crush on Hongdou, only she had terrible taste and picked Asheng, so I dropped her flat. Since *you* have sharp eyes and picked me, you're the only girl I care about!"

Blushing crimson, Chouya pecked his cheek and tugged him off to the West Courtyard.

Inside Suxin Thatched Hall, Haoyu blinked at the empty front parlor. Kuqiao had shut the door and vanished—was his wife waiting on the bed inside?! Heart hammering against his ribs, he practically sprinted toward the inner chamber—and walked slap into Qianli Xun blocking the archway.

"Chen Haoyu, what are you barging into my bedroom for?"

"N-nothing! Didn't see you out here, thought you were waiting inside!"

Brushing past him into the front hall with him trailing close behind, Qianli Xun stopped dead and pivoted—making Haoyu slam on the brakes inches from her nose. Tall as he was, her gaze hit his chin, then rose a hair to his lips. *Should I test kissing him?*

While she wavered, Haoyu leaned down to kiss her—and she spun clear at the last instant. "I haven't fallen in love with you yet."

Stepping right back in front of her, he grinned. "Try it—maybe you'll fall in love on the spot!"

"Quit flattering yourself!" Dodging aside, she kicked herself for chicken-ing out when *she'd* planned to test it. Holding out her hand, she said, "Here—hold my hand."

Needing no second invitation, Haoyu snatched her hand right up. When balled into a fist, this hand bruised him black and blue; lying open in his palm, it felt like warm, spun silk, firing up every nerve in his chest.

Qianli Xun waited. His hand felt broad and warm—then sweaty and clammy after ten seconds, sparking zero butterflies. "Chen Haoyu... why don't I feel a thing?"

"Well *I* feel plenty! Let me spend the night in here—one night and you'll feel sparks flying!" Sliding his other arm around her waist, he puckered up and swooped in.

*Maybe he's right,* she told herself, squeezing her eyes shut and tilting up—only for his rough palm on her shoulder and hot breath across her face to trip every martial reflex in her body. *POW!* Her fist smashed straight into his cheek.

Kiss aborted, Haoyu staggered back clutching his face and howling, "Ow! Ow! Ow!"

Guilt flooded her. "I didn't mean to—just reflex! You okay?"

"Does this look okay?! Look—already swelling!" Dropping his hand, he thrust his cheek under her nose; sure enough, a purple welt was already blooming.

Ducking into the inner chamber for ointment, she turned around with her Jixiang Pharmacy jar to find him right behind her. "Why'd you follow me in here?"

"I'm your husband—hardly weird."

"Fine, sit down so I can put medicine on it."

Eyeing the bed with a sly glint, he asked, "Mind if I sit on the edge of the bed?"

Reading his cheeky scheme, she paused a beat before nodding. "Whatever."

Bruised or not, Haoyu beamed—his wife was acting miles softer tonight; strike while the iron was hot! Sitting beside him on the mattress, Qianli Xun uncapped the jar, dabbed cool brown salve onto a silk handkerchief, and smoothed it over his cheek.

"This salve works wonders—one coat and it'll be gone by tomorrow."

Capping the jar to rise, she dropped her guard for half a second—and Haoyu swooped straight onto her lips.

Shock nailed her in place; the ointment jar tumbled out of her fingers onto the floor.

Lost in the dizzy rush of kissing the woman he adored, Haoyu wrapped both arms around her and leaned her smoothly back onto the mattress—not a single claw of the "tigress" in sight! *Haven't even brewed her a cup of tea yet and Heaven's already handing me victory!* Pressing above her, he deepened the kiss while she lay still and bewildered beneath him like a stunned kitten, his hands wandering down to loosen her collar—

Back in the modern world nobody had ever kissed Qianli Xun like this, and for a dizzy moment the heat nearly swept her away—until his roaming hands snapped her wide awake! Shoving him sideways across the mattress, she flipped upright in a single crisp bound and yanked her robes shut. "Chen Haoyu, keep your hands to yourself!"

Rolling upright onto the bed's edge, Haoyu protested, "You let me kiss you—if my hands didn't move, I wouldn't be a red-blooded man!"

"Not tonight."

"When, then? I'll wait."

"I told you—not until I fall for you."

"You let me kiss you—doesn't that prove you like me?"

"I don't know! All I know is I'm not ready to consummate our marriage yet. Give me more time."

"Deal. I'll wait as long as it takes." Rising, he pulled her into a warm embrace. "You did great tonight, Wife—made me happier than words can say. Tell you a secret: I'm learning to whisk tea, and once I master it, I'll only ever whisk it for you, because you're the greatest love of my whole life."

Releasing her, he strode out of Suxin Thatched Hall.

Frozen where she stood, Qianli Xun replayed *the greatest love of my whole life* and felt a strange warmth bloom in her chest. Touching her tingling lips, she blinked—oddly enough, she hadn't felt disgusted at all.

Walking alone toward the back courtyard, Haoyu passed Boya Study when Matron Wei's voice called out, "Young Master."

Stopping, he turned. "Still awake this late, Matron?"

Catching the bruise on his cheek by lantern light, Matron Wei guessed her mistress's handiwork at once. "I waited up specifically for you. I hear you're doing splendidly at the silk emporium and moving to the counting house soon—before long, you'll be carrying major weight for the Chen clan."

"Me? No way—Brother Dong's got that covered."

"I pride myself on reading people. I misjudged you once, yet I won't misjudge you now. Have some faith in yourself."

Startled by the rare pep talk, Haoyu blinked. "Did you wait up just to tell me that?"

"Hardly. I wanted to urge you to stay patient with my mistress. She set aside her own heart to marry into this house; winning her over completely takes time. Good things take patience—don't give up halfway."

"If you don't misjudge me anymore, why worry?" Haoyu grinned. "I've never had this much patience in my entire life."

Matron Wei nodded with a warm smile. "Good. Get some rest—I won't keep you."
