## Chapter 168: Seeing Xiaguo Off and Storming the Chen Manor

A few days later, the Qiao family sent out wedding invitations across Lin'an for their son's marriage. An invitation went to Zhu Manor as well, yet none came to Wangyue Pavilion—both because the Qiaos were taking a maid from there as concubine and out of tact regarding Miss Zhu's past bond with their son. Qianli Xun felt the same and appreciated their restraint.

Xiaguo's dowry had been packed five days ahead. On the wedding morning, dressed in the concubine's bridal robes sent by the Qiaos and draped under a red veil, she sat waiting in the maids' quarters.

Sitting beside her, Kuqiao kept her company. "You got your wish at last, yet I still cannot warm up to you. Feels like you stole the man Miss loved."

Xiaguo held no grudge. "Wrong! The way I see it, I am marrying Young Master Qiao in Miss's stead..."

"If Miss married him, she would be primary wife. You are going in as a concubine—how does that count as taking her place?"

"Fair point. I just want to guard Young Master Qiao for Miss's sake, so Lady Miao does not steal his heart."

Approaching the door with Hongdou to see Xiaguo off, Qianli Xun overheard the exchange. After a beat of hesitation, she stepped inside and spoke with quiet gravity. "Remember this, Xiaguo: I have no need for you to guard anyone for me. Marrying into the Qiao family is your own choice and has nothing to do with me."

Lifting her veil, Xiaguo dropped to her knees before her mistress. "Miss, I can no longer serve you, and knowing how deep Young Master Qiao's feelings for you run, I could not help thinking that way."

Qianli Xun reached down and pulled her up. "Do not kneel to me anymore, and stop fretting over me. Go to Qiao Manor and build a good life for yourself."

Xiaguo nodded. "I will remember Miss's words and live well."

Qianli Xun called Hongdou forward, and the maid opened a wooden box in her hands. From inside, Qianli Xun drew out a sheet of paper covered in ink and red seals. "Here is your deed of sale. Today I tear it up right before your eyes. From this moment on, you belong to no one as a maid."

Watching the torn scraps flutter from her mistress's hands to the floor, Xiaguo wept with emotion. "I will remember Miss's kindness forever."

Lowering the red veil back over Xiaguo's head, Qianli Xun said, "Stop keeping the word 'kindness' on the tip of your tongue."

Matron Wei's voice called from outside. "Miss, the Qiao bridal sedan has arrived and waits at the gate."

Without answering aloud, Qianli Xun instructed Kuqiao, "Walk Xiaguo out with Hongdou."

Supporting the veiled bride on either side, the two maids guided her with care so she would not trip.

Matron Wei waited in the courtyard. "Your dowry is already outside, ready to follow the sedan into Qiao Manor," she told Xiaguo. "Worried you would have no one of your own there, Miss had me pick a maid from Wangyue Pavilion to accompany you."

"Thank you, Miss! Thank you, Matron!" Xiaguo said from beneath her veil.

"Aimo!" Matron Wei called.

"Here, Matron."

"From now on, you follow Xiaguo. Serve her well at Qiao Manor."

"Yes, Matron." Aimo stepped behind Xiaguo and trailed them to the gate.

Outside Wangyue Pavilion, Kuqiao and Hongdou helped Xiaguo into the waiting sedan while Aimo took her place beside the poles. As the bearers lifted the chair, Xiaguo parted the curtain, peeked under her veil, and called out, "Now that I am gone, you two take proper care of Miss!"

"Save your breath," Kuqiao retorted. "Even when you were here, I did all the work anyway!"

Kuqiao spoke truth; Xiaguo's heart had never been in serving her mistress. Yet leaving today, remembering how generously Miss had treated her, a genuine pang of parting tugged at her. Once the curtain dropped and she settled her veil back into place, though, regret gave way to pure bliss. Not only had she met a mistress this kind, she was marrying the man she loved—even as a concubine, she counted herself luckier than most women alive.

The sedan moved off, and the two maids watched the bearers and dowry porters vanish down the street before latching Wangyue Pavilion's gate and heading toward their mistress's chambers.

Back in her own room, Qianli Xun sat at her desk staring into space. Today was Wenyuan's wedding, and a strange hollowness settled over her—too empty to invent plots, too empty to pick up a brush, drifting back through sweet memories of their time together. Those bright days had not ended so long ago, yet they already felt half a lifetime away.

Entering the room, the two maids sensed her mood at once; Kuqiao tugged Hongdou's sleeve and slipped back out without a sound.

Qianli Xun never even noticed them come and go, sinking deeper into memory. The more she remembered, the sharper the ache grew. Picturing Wenyuan sharing a wedding chamber with someone else tonight sliced at her chest like a blade; an absurd urge surged up to storm Qiao Manor, grab Wenyuan's hand before the whole crowd, and run to the ends of the earth.

She clamped down on the fantasy and gave herself a mental slap. You played the cool, rational woman in front of Wenyuan and everyone else—what are you getting dramatic over now? Wake up! Your own wedding date is looming; figure out how to kill this engagement to Chen Haoyu!

One thought of the betrothal got her out of her chair, wiping Wenyuan's wedding from her mind. Chen Haoyu had only said his grandmother would stall, without explaining how. She could not sit around waiting for a miracle. Staging scenes before her father had failed twice; why not take the show straight to Chen Manor? Let the Chen elders see Miss Zhu's ferocious side firsthand—maybe they would recoil in horror and demand the engagement be broken themselves.

Her mind set, she strode out of her room and shouted toward the maids' quarters. "Hongdou! Come out with me!"

Both Kuqiao and Hongdou emerged, and Kuqiao pouted. "Ever since Hongdou arrived, Miss plays favorites and never takes me out anymore."

Qianli Xun laughed. "I am going out to raise hell and do not want you getting hurt. Stay home and wait for good news."

Fearing her mistress might do something rash over Young Master Qiao's wedding, Kuqiao gasped. "Miss, please do not go make a scene at Qiao Manor—"

"What are you imagining? What is Qiao Manor to me? I am heading to the Chens'."

"The Chens' is no better! Your reputation just recovered—make another scene and it will sink worse than before!"

"Stop fussing. What is reputation worth next to happiness?"

Walking up just then, Matron Wei caught the exchange and knew at once what her mistress planned. "Do not stop her, Kuqiao—you could not stop her if you tried. Let her go fight one round for her own life. Who knows, she might get her wish."

Qianli Xun wrapped her arms around Matron Wei. "Matron is the best!"

Smoothing a stray lock behind her mistress's ear, Matron Wei smiled. "You truly are not the girl you used to be—bold enough to fight for what you want. Matron is proud of you. Do whatever you judge right, only remember not to injure anyone. And if today fails, think hard on what I urged you before."

Stepping back, Qianli Xun racked her brain, drawing a blank. "Could Matron remind me what you urged?"

"What you will do if you truly must marry into the Chen family," Matron Wei said in earnest.

Only then did it come back to her; she had never sat down to face that question head-on. "I promise, Matron. If today fails, I will think it through."

"Go on, and come back early."

Matron Wei watched her mistress stride off with Hongdou.

"Matron," Kuqiao fretted, "aren't you scared Master will find out you indulged Miss and punish you?"

"Of course I am scared, yet I hate seeing Miss unhappy even more. Enough worrying—go about your chores and wait for good news."

"What if it is bad news?"

"There is no such thing as bad news."

Kuqiao blinked. "What do you mean, Matron? Do you really think Miss will scare the Chens into breaking the betrothal?"

Matron Wei gave a cryptic chuckle. "You are young yet and do not grasp that nothing in life is wholly bad. Enough chatter—I have errands to run."

Blocking her path, Kuqiao pouted. "Matron always loves talking in riddles! I am not letting you leave until you explain!"

Matron Wei chuckled. "Seeing how loyal you have been to Miss since childhood, I will humor you. Tell me: do you think it is better for Miss to marry into the Chen family, or not to marry?"

"Not to marry, of course!" Kuqiao answered in a heartbeat. "That is what Miss has wanted all along."

"To my eyes, marrying is fine, and not marrying is fine too."

"What does that mean?"

"Marrying brings its own rewards, and staying unwed brings its own. Look for the bright side in everything."

"I do not buy that!" Kuqiao protested. "By that logic, why did you back Miss's divorce from Lin Jue?"

"That was different." Declining to explain further, Matron Wei patted her arm. "Mule over it yourself. I have work to do."

After Matron Wei walked away, Kuqiao puzzled over it half the morning without cracking it, and gave up with a convenient excuse: Matron Wei must be getting old and rambling—if it were really true, why was Miss knocking herself out trying to escape?

Outside Chen Manor, the towering front gates stood shut beneath an imposing plaque reading "Chen Manor," radiating an air of wealth and authority that made visitors shrink back.

Hongdou leaned close. "Miss, maybe we should turn back?"

"Why back out after coming all this way? You know kung fu—what is there to fear? Since we are here, leaving without a proper ruckus would be a crime."

"Alright! If Miss is not scared, neither am I. I will charge right in with you."

"Remember—do not hurt anyone too bad."

"Got it. I will knock."

Hongdou marched up and pounded three times: *Bang! Bang! Bang!*

A gatekeeper cracked the door and poked his head out. "Who are you? What business do you have banging on Chen Manor's gate?"

Hongdou looked to her mistress; Qianli Xun gave a crisp nod.

With one kick, Hongdou blew the door wide open, sending the gatekeeper staggering backward, and snatched him by the collar. "The Chen family's future young madam has come to scout the territory and see if her future in-laws are easy to get along with! If you know what is good for you, call out every master in the house to form a welcoming line for Miss Zhu!"

Seeing trouble at the door, the gatekeeper was no pushover himself and swung a punch at Hongdou. Hongdou sidestepped with ease and punched him flying several paces back. Within seconds, a swarm of servants rushed up, several clutching heavy staves.

Wiping blood from his swollen lip, the downed gatekeeper bellowed, "Jump her together!"

The servants surged at Hongdou, who fought them off with brisk strikes.

Stepping unhurried across the threshold, Qianli Xun called out to the brawling servants, "With your half-baked kung fu, you cannot even lay a hand on my maid! Do yourselves a favor and fetch my future father-in-law and mother-in-law—I want a proper chat with them!"

Outmatched and already nursing bruises, the servants backed off, and one sprinted inside to alert the masters.

Hongdou barked at the gatekeeper who had opened the door, "You! Fetch a chair for my mistress at once!"

Having tasted her fist, the servant dared not argue—and besides, Miss Zhu was about to become his young madam anyway. He scrambled into the gatehouse, hauled out a chair, and set it beside her. "Please sit, Miss. Master and Madam will be right out."

Qianli Xun lowered herself into the chair, well pleased with the opening act. Once Chen Haoyu's parents laid eyes on a daughter-in-law like this, they were bound to regret ever sealing the betrothal.

The Qiaos had sent the Chen family a wedding invitation too, of course. Considering the Qiaos had jilted the Zhus while his own son was engaged to Miss Zhu, Chen Jianglin had found it awkward to attend in person and sent his second brother, Chen Jiangshu, and his wife to represent the Chens instead.

Back in the inner hall, Chen Jianglin was playing chess with Yao Qingyan and talking over plans for their son's wedding when a servant burst past the maids into the room. "Master! Madam! Disaster! The future young madam—Miss Zhu—just fought her way through the gates demanding to see her future father-in-law and mother-in-law!"

Yao Qingyan set down her chess piece in shock. "What? Fought her way in?"

"Yes, fought right in! Look at my face, Madam—her maid beat me black and blue!"

Sure enough, his face was blotched with fresh bruises.

"What do we do, Master?" Yao Qingyan asked.

"Go back and tell Miss Zhu to wait a moment," Chen Jianglin instructed the servant. "Madam and I will be right there."

"Yes, Master." The servant hurried out.

Yao Qingyan frowned in concern. "Miss Zhu really is a handful. No wonder Haoyu refused to marry her."

"Formidable indeed," Chen Jianglin nodded. "Still, Prefect Zhu warned me ahead of time: those two staged scenes in front of him twice without getting anywhere. Seeing her father will not budge, Miss Zhu must have brought the act here to force a broken engagement."

"So do we break it off or not?"

"Of course not! Why would grown adults walk straight into a pair of brats' trap?"

"Master is right. I will follow your lead."

"Come along, Madam. Let us go meet our future daughter-in-law and see firsthand just how fierce she is."

Setting his piece aside, Chen Jianglin stepped down from the couch with his wife and strode toward the main gate with their maids trailing behind.
