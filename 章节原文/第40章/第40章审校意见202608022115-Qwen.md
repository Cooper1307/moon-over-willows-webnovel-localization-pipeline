## 〇、硬伤级问题（最高优先级）

### 修改点 1：中文原文混入英文译文（格式事故）🔴🔴

**修改前：**
> Qianli Xun didn't pay him any mind. Before she could even think about how to throw a punch, the expert attacked with powerful force and fierce aggression. His fists and feet came like a storm, each punch and kick containing destructive power. **千里寻灵活地躲避着，运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚，努力化解对方的攻势。**
> Qianli Xun dodged nimbly, using her exquisite martial arts skills to overcome hardness with softness, trying to deflect his attacks.

**修改后：**
> Qianli Xun didn't pay him any mind. Before she could even think about throwing a punch, the expert was on her. Fists like hammer blows. Kicks like splitting wood. A storm of violence, every strike carrying enough force to crack bone.
>
> She dodged. Weaved. Let his power sail past her like wind through bamboo. Soft against hard. Yield, redirect, survive.

**修改理由：** ① 译文中混入了未翻译的中文原文，属格式事故级硬伤，必须删除；② 原英文译文"using her exquisite martial arts skills to overcome hardness with softness"是"运用精湛的拳法技巧，以柔克刚"的逐字直译；③ 打斗高潮段需要碎片句节奏，改为短句堆叠模拟格斗速度。

## 一、重复用词排查

### 修改点 4：「said」重复（出现约 6 次，超过阈值）

**修改前：**
> Chen Haoyu stared at Xiaguo and **said**, "It's your good fortune..."
> Xiaguo **said**, "If I followed Young Master Chen..."
> Chen Haoyu **said**, "Do we need to invite your father and my father?"
> Nanny Wei listened patiently...then couldn't help but **say**, "Young Master Chen..."
> Qianli Xun **said**, "Village head, bring paper and brush."

**修改后：**
> Chen Haoyu stared at Xia Guo, lips curling. "It's your good fortune..."
> "If I followed Young Master Chen—" Xia Guo **started**.
> Chen Haoyu **waved a hand**. "Do we need to invite your father and my father?"
> Nanny Wei listened patiently, then **leaned forward**. "Young Master Chen..."
> "Village head." Qianli Xun **raised her voice**. "Paper and brush. Now."
> "Village head, don't just thank us," Qianli Xun **cut in**.

**修改理由：** "said"出现6次，超过阈值（>3次/章）。根据原文情绪信号和动作描写替换为多样化表达，部分直接用动作替代对话标签。

## 二、内心独白格式（网文规范）

> She glanced a few more times at the fighter.

## 四、句子过长 / 节奏问题

### 修改点 16：陈浩宇解释田根权属（超长复合句）

**修改前：**
> Chen Haoyu said, "Do we need to invite your father and my father? That's completely unnecessary. The land root is in my hands, so I own the bamboo forest. Your father gave the bamboo forest as dowry to you, so you have the right to use it and collect rent. As long as you and I are here, everyone is present."

**修改后：**
> Chen Haoyu spread his hands. "Your father? My father? Please. The land root is in *my* hands. I own the forest. Your father gave you the use rights, the rent rights—fine. A dowry. But the root? Mine. You and I are the only two people in this equation. Everyone else is decoration."

**修改理由：** ① 原译是超60词的单段引语，信息密度过高；② 陈浩宇的语气应更傲慢、更不耐烦；③ 用碎片句（"Please."、"Mine."）体现他的盛气凌人；④ "Everyone else is decoration"比"everyone is present"更有角色个性。

**修改理由：** ① 原译是超80词的单段引语，严重违反"长独白需拆分"规则；② 加入对话节奏（Nanny Wei说→千里寻反应→Nanny Wei继续）；③ "You don't need to bleed for a maid"比"put yourself in danger for her"更有情感重量；④ 注意：此处"bleed"是比喻用法（流血/牺牲），非暴力描写。

## 五、吸引力与节奏强化

### 修改点 19：开篇首句缺乏钩子

**修改前：**
> The village head's bamboo house was probably the best in the entire village—large, sturdy, with a spacious courtyard.
>
> A bamboo canopy had been set up in the courtyard, with tables and chairs underneath. Chen Haoyu had arrived early with his men, sitting under the canopy waiting. The village head had already brewed tea and stood nearby with an obsequious smile.

**修改后：**
> Chen Haoyu was already there. Of course he was.
>
> He'd claimed the best seat under the bamboo canopy, legs spread, tea untouched, eyes on the road. Waiting. The village head hovered at his elbow, refilling a cup that didn't need refilling.
>
> The village head's house was the finest in the settlement—sturdy beams, wide courtyard, a gate that actually closed. Today it felt like an arena.

**修改理由：** ① 原开篇是环境描写的平铺直叙；② 改为以陈浩宇的"已在场"开头，制造对峙前的紧张感；③ "Of course he was."碎片句体现千里寻视角的不屑；④ "Today it felt like an arena"为即将到来的打斗埋下氛围。

### 修改点 20：高手震碎上衣——缺乏冲击力

**修改前：**
> The expert walked out of the bamboo canopy and came to a spacious area. Using his inner qi, he shattered his upper garment, and the pieces fell to the ground. His muscles rippled, intimidating everyone who saw them.

**修改后：**
> The fighter stepped into the open. Planted his feet. Breathed in—
>
> And his shirt *exploded*. Shreds of cloth scattering like startled birds. Beneath: a landscape of muscle, corded and twitching, each one a separate threat.
>
> Xia Guo flinched. The village head took a step back. Even Chen Haoyu's smile widened.

**修改理由：** ① 原译"Using his inner qi, he shattered his upper garment"是流水账式叙述；② 改为先蓄力（"Planted his feet. Breathed in—"）再爆发（"exploded"），制造节奏反差；③ 加入围观者反应（flinched / step back / smile widened），用他人反应衬托高手的压迫感。

---

### 修改点 21：陆远峰拜师——反转缺乏冲击

**修改前：**
> The expert struggled to get up and knelt on the ground. "Master, please accept a bow from your disciple!"
>
> Qianli Xun had many male disciples in another era, but none looked as rough as him. She had no interest in this person at all. "Get up. I don't want to take you as a disciple."

**修改后：**
> The fighter hit the dirt. Stayed down. Then—slowly—he pushed himself up. Not to his feet.
>
> To his knees.
>
> He pressed his forehead to the ground. "Master." His voice was raw. Reverent. "Accept this disciple's bow."
>
> Silence.
>
> Chen Haoyu's mouth fell open. The village head's tea cup slipped from his fingers.
>
> Qianli Xun stared down at the man. *In another life, I had disciples. Dozens. None of them looked like a butcher's apprentice.* "Get up," she said flatly. "I'm not taking you."

**修改理由：** ① 原文是本章最大的"打脸"爽点——陈浩宇请来的高手反过来拜千里寻为师；② 原译缺乏节奏铺垫，直接"knelt on the ground"一笔带过；③ 用"To his knees."独立成段制造停顿；④ 加入围观者震惊反应（Chen Haoyu's mouth / tea cup slipping），放大反转的戏剧效果；⑤ 千里寻的拒绝用"flatly"体现她的淡定，与全场震惊形成反差。

### 修改点 22：陈浩宇「傻眼」——情绪描写过于概括

**修改前：**
> Chen Haoyu was completely dumbfounded. Not only had the expert he brought lost, but he'd also recognized Zhu Shuzhen as his master. In all his life, Chen Haoyu had never been so humiliated.

**修改后：**
> Chen Haoyu stood frozen. His hired fighter—*his* weapon, *his* trump card—kneeling at Zhu Shuzhen's feet. Calling her *Master*.
>
> The silence stretched. Someone coughed. No one looked at him.
>
> In twenty-three years of life, Chen Haoyu had never wanted the ground to open and swallow him whole.

**修改理由：** ① "was completely dumbfounded"是"直接傻眼"的直译，过于概括；② 改为具象化画面（frozen / kneeling / calling her Master）；③ "Someone coughed. No one looked at him."用环境细节衬托尴尬；④ "wanted the ground to open and swallow him whole"是英文中表达极度羞耻的惯用语，比"had never been so humiliated"更生动。

### 修改点 23：结尾缺乏余韵

**修改前：**
> After seeing Nanny Wei off, Qianli Xun returned to the bamboo house. During her time in the bamboo forest, she hadn't yet felt any impact on her fate, but her heart had become increasingly peaceful. The more peaceful she was, the more it stimulated her literary thoughts. She planned to devote herself to writing vernacular stories in the days to come.

**修改后：**
> Qianli Xun watched the carriage shrink to a dot on the road, then turn, then vanish.
>
> She walked back to the bamboo house. Sat down. Picked up her brush.
>
> Fate hadn't shifted. Not yet. No thunderclap, no cosmic realignment. Just... quiet. The kind of quiet that made words come easy.
>
> She dipped the brush. Wrote the first line.
>
> Outside, the bamboo creaked in the wind. Like applause.

**修改理由：** ① 原结尾是叙述性总结（"She planned to devote herself to writing"），缺乏画面感和余韵；② 改为动作序列（watched → walked → sat → picked up → dipped → wrote），用节奏感收束全章；③ "Like applause"是轻微润色，基于原文"激发文思"的积极语境，用竹林声响呼应创作灵感。

**修改后：**

**修改理由：** 术语表中「小弟（陈浩宇的）」的译法为"lackeys / henchmen"。"hooligans"更接近"地痞流氓"（无组织的小混混），而陈浩宇的随从是有主从关系的跟班，用"lackeys"更准确。同时"snatched"比"took"更符合陈浩宇不甘心的情绪。

### 修改点 25：「Are the people behind me not human?」

**修改前：**
> Chen Haoyu shifted his gaze away from Xiaguo. "**Are the people behind me not human?**"

**修改后：**
> Chen Haoyu peeled his eyes off Xia Guo—reluctantly. "What, these men don't count?"

**修改理由：** "Are the people behind me not human?"是"难道我身后这几位不是人吗？"的逐字直译，英文中不会这样反问。改为更口语化的"What, these men don't count?"，同时"peeled his eyes off"比"shifted his gaze away"更有画面感（暗示不情愿）。

### 修改点 26：「Qianli Xun suppressed her anger」

**修改前：**
> **Qianli Xun suppressed her anger.** "Dream on! You can have the bamboo forest, but don't even think about touching a single hair on my maid's head!"

**修改后：**
> Qianli Xun's fingers curled into fists. She uncurled them. Slowly.
>
> "*Dream on.* The forest—fine. Take it. But Xia Guo? You touch one hair on her head and I'll break every finger you used to point at her."

**修改理由：** ① "suppressed her anger"是"忍住怒火"的直译，改为具象化动作（fists curling/uncurling）；② 千里寻的威胁应更具体、更有画面感；③ "I'll break every finger you used to point at her"基于原文"别想碰一根毫毛"的语义强化，符合角色"直率、爱吐槽"的语调。
