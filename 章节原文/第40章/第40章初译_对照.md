第40章 来到临安访乔府
## Chapter 40: Chapter 40: Arriving in Lin'an, Visiting Qiao Manor

汴梁的仪王府留给邹嬷嬷照顾，申简辰收拾行囊，带上乔梦容踏上去往临安之路。
白风换上侍卫的衣装，头发也梳得整齐，洗干净的脸更显清秀，跟以往判若两人。
自从跟着王爷，王爷每日抽时间教他申拳，均是在客栈住下后，趁着夜色，把他带到无人之处，教他一招一式。
白风的领悟能力很强，招式掌握得也快。但王爷说申拳的威力是靠每日练习得来，必须持之以恒。
除练习拳法，白风还要负责王爷每日的衣食住行。
佳和郡主建议给王爷身边多带几个丫鬟伺候，都被王爷拒绝。
白风仔细瞧过那些丫鬟，一个个长得挺水灵，王爷竟然不愿意要，他甚觉可惜，忍不住建议王爷带上一个，遥远的旅途中他好有个说话的。可王爷说，身边有他一人足矣。白风由此判断王爷是不近女色之人。
一路上，乔文仙带下人乘坐一辆马车；白风陪王爷乘坐一辆；佳和郡主带喜兰乘坐一辆。
好几次佳和郡主想要跟哥哥挤在一辆马车内，她想多靠靠哥哥的肩膀，说不定哥哥还会把她搂在怀里。哥哥却像是看出她的心思似的，拽着白风不放，让她没有任何机会靠近。
有一次，乔梦容说出想法，哥哥直接拒绝，理由是路途不平坦，车马颠簸，他太粗心，怕照顾不好妹妹，由喜兰照顾他才放心。
这种仔细一想能找出很多破绽的理由令乔梦容心酸，她只好老老实实带喜兰继续乘坐另一辆马车。
白风看到佳和郡主失落的表情时，他的心里挺不好受，在马车里劝王爷让他跟郡主换换。王爷并未答应，批评他尽好侍卫的职责，少管闲事。
白风觉得这哪是闲事，这是王爷的终身大事！他听喜兰说过，明年中秋节王爷要与佳和郡主成亲，跟未婚妻同乘一辆马车，多好的事！王爷却不愿意，白风想不通。
大约在路上行了半个多月，终于到达临安。
一路顺利，没有遇到梁师成口中的流寇，申简辰反而甚感失望。如若能碰到些像梁山好汉那样的义士，他定会与他们成为朋友。
申简辰不愿先去临安府任职，他想多自由一段时日，到处逛逛，碰碰运气，看能不能找到千里寻。
算卦先生说在与“口”有关的地方能见到她，申简辰决定到临安城的饭庄，酒肆等地先看看。
临安城没有汴梁繁华，但也不差，而且与汴梁相比别具一番韵味，到处充满婉约的风格。无论是建筑，街市的布置，商铺以及售卖之物，亦或是阑珊小桥，均是一副水乡之态。
乔文仙征求仪王的意思后，先带众人回乔府。
乔家是临安的大户，算不上数一数二，也排得上前五。
除了因祖上福荫外，还因大女儿有幸进宫，生了仪王。尽管女儿走得早，仪王一直病着，总归成为皇亲国戚，提起来面儿上既有光，别人瞧见乔家人也多少会礼让三分。
乔家当家的是乔守仁，已过花甲之年，除去大女儿外，膝下还有四个女儿，二女儿和三女儿均是正室钱氏所出，已嫁为人妇。
乔守仁四十多岁时，考虑到乔家香火无人继承，钱氏说服乔守仁纳一房妾室，姓米，年纪与大女儿相仿，进门没两年便生下乔文仙，一家人相当高兴。
因将来乔文仙要继承香火，一直养在钱氏身边。米氏倒不介意，她还年轻，可以继续生儿子，可谁知一连生了两位千金，现均待字闺中。米氏心里虽产生过些许不满，因钱氏脾气秉性都好，对她也不错，没有让亲生儿子疏远她，慢慢的也就不怎么介意，反而与钱氏相处融洽。
三辆马车经过临安闹市，往城南而行，到达乔府大门口，都下了马车。门口的下人见了迎上来：“公子可回来了！”
乔文仙吩咐道：“快去告诉老爷，仪王来访。”
下人瞧一眼公子身后之人，小跑着进入府院。
Leaving Prince Yi's Manor in Bianliang under Matron Zou's care, Shen Jianchen packed his bags and set off south for Lin'an with Qiao Mengrong. Dressed in neat guard attire, hair combed crisp, face scrubbed spotless revealing sharp, handsome features, Bai Feng looked like a whole different kid.

乔文仙带着仪王等人也走进府内，迎面是假山巨石和水流，像一道屏障，使得府内的景致不易被人一览无遗。拐过巨石，房屋出现在眼前，但需穿过架在水上的长廊，还好水面不宽，所谓的长廊并不算太长，大家很快穿越而过。
Ever since taking him on, Prince Yi made time every evening after checking into roadside inns to lead Bai Feng to quiet corners and drill Shen Fist move by move. Quick-witted and athletic, Bai Feng picked up forms fast, though Prince Yi reminded him true Shen Fist power grew only from daily sweat and persistence. Beyond martial drills, Bai Feng tended His Highness's daily needs along the road.

迎面走来一群人，领头的便是乔守仁。身材匀称，面容庄重，头发已经斑白，依然梳理得整齐。身旁跟着钱氏和米氏，身后是两个还未出阁的女儿。
Princess Jiahe had urged bringing several personal maids to see to Brother's comfort, only for him to turn every one down. Looking over those fresh, lively maids, Bai Feng felt it was a crying shame and even nudged Prince Yi to bring one along so he'd have company on the long journey—only for Prince Yi to insist Bai Feng alone was plenty, convincing Bai Feng his master had zero interest in women!

一行人见到仪王，先是跪拜。
The convoy rolled south in three carriages: Qiao Wenxian with his attendants in one, Prince Yi and Bai Feng in the second, Princess Jiahe and Xilan in the third. Time and again Qiao Mengrong longed to ride in her brother's carriage, hoping to lean against his shoulder or feel his arm slip around her, yet he clung tight to Bai Feng and gave her no opening. Once she asked outright, only for him to refuse flat: rough roads, heavy jouncing, clumsy guy like him wouldn't take good care of her, better let Xilan tend her! An excuse full of holes that stung Qiao Mengrong's heart, leaving her riding silent with Xilan.

申简辰穿越成王爷经常被下人跪拜，倒也渐渐习惯，可被亲人跪拜，还是头一遭，赶紧上前扶起乔守仁。
Seeing Princess Jiahe's downcast eyes, Bai Feng felt sorry for her and offered inside the carriage to swap places with the princess, earning a sharp reminder from Prince Yi to mind his guard duties and stay out of private matters. Private matters?! This was His Highness's future bride! Xilan had already told Bai Feng they were wed next Mid-Autumn Festival—who turns down riding with his own fiancée?! Total mystery to Bai Feng!

“外祖父、两位外祖母、还有两位……”
After just over half a month on the road, Lin'an rose ahead. Smooth travel without a single bandit sighting almost disappointed Shen Jianchen, who had half hoped to cross paths with gallant outlaws like Mount Liang heroes and befriend them. In no hurry to report for duty at the prefecture office yet, he planned to steal a few free days roaming town hunting for Qianli Xun. The fortune-teller pointed to places tied to the "mouth," so restaurants and taverns topped his list.

申简辰不知该如何称呼，她们看着比他年纪还小些，就是辈分大，称呼到嘴边，却怎么也说不出口，干脆不再称呼：“你们都是我的亲人，岂有亲人跪拜亲人之礼？再说我是小辈，大家更无需跪拜。”
Less vast than Bianliang yet every bit as thriving, Lin'an radiated southern grace: delicate timber halls, lively canal-town avenues, specialty shops, graceful stone arches curving over water. At Prince Yi's nod, Qiao Wenxian led the convoy straight to Qiao Manor first.

所有人都笑着起身。
Though not top one or two, the Qiao house ranked squarely in Lin'an's top five great clans. Beyond ancestral wealth, their eldest daughter had entered the palace and borne Prince Yi; though she passed young and her son stayed sickly, imperial kinship lent prestige that made everyone in Lin'an defer three steps.

乔守仁将仪王上下打量一番：“我刚听下人说仪王来访，甚觉惊讶，你病着，怎么可能来临安？亲眼见到才相信是真的，你能恢复健康，是老天保佑，也是乔家之福，更是佳和郡主的福气。”
Family patriarch Qiao Shouren stood past sixty. Besides his late eldest daughter, he had four more daughters: the second and third borne by primary wife Madam Qian, both already wed. In his forties, anxious for a male heir to carry ancestral incense, Qiao Shouren listened to Madam Qian and took a concubine surnamed Mi, near the eldest daughter's age, who delighted the whole house by bearing Qiao Wenxian within two years. Destined to inherit the estate, Qiao Wenxian was raised at Madam Qian's side. Still young and hoping for more sons, Concubine Mi hadn't minded—only to bear two more daughters, both still awaiting marriage. Brief resentment faded fast because Madam Qian stayed warm, fair, and never drove a wedge between mother and son, letting both women get along in genuine harmony.

乔梦容见提起自己，上前拜道：“我也跟着哥哥称呼各位，外祖父，两位外祖母，还有……，两位姑娘看着与我年纪相仿，我总不能称呼她们姨母？”
Rolling south through crowded streets, the three carriages halted outside Qiao Manor's main gate. Doormen hurried out beaming: "Welcome home, Young Master!"

两位姑娘害羞地笑着，一位说道：“佳和郡主可别称呼我姨母，羞死了！”
"Run tell Father that Prince Yi has arrived!" Qiao Wenxian ordered. Casting an awed glance behind him, a servant dashed inside.

钱氏说：“虽然按照辈分如此，但仪王与佳和郡主身份尊贵，只管唤名字就行。”
Leading Prince Yi and the others through the gates, they faced sculpted rockery and flowing water screening the inner halls from street view. Rounding the boulders revealed graceful courtyards across a short covered walkway spanning clear water. A whole welcoming party hurried out led by Qiao Shouren—trim, dignified, silver-streaked hair combed neat—flanked by Madam Qian and Concubine Mi, with two unmarried daughters trailing behind.

乔文仙主动介绍：“个高的叫文慧，矮一些的是文秀。”
Seeing Prince Yi, the entire family dropped to their knees in formal kowtow. Used to servants kneeling by now yet jolted seeing family elders kneel to him, Shen Jianchen rushed forward lifting Qiao Shouren by both arms. "Grandfather, Grandmothers, and my two..." Stuck mid-sentence—those two girls looked even younger than him despite outranking him as maternal aunts!—he skipped titles smooth: "We're all family! Since when does kin kneel to kin? Especially elders kneeling to a junior—everyone get up!"

文慧与文秀齐声拜道：“仪王，佳和郡主安好”
Laughing warm, the household rose. Looking Prince Yi up and down in wonder, Qiao Shouren beamed, "When servants announced Prince Yi, I thought I misheard—how could you travel all this way sick? Seeing you hale with my own eyes proves Heaven's blessing, joy for the Qiao house, and boundless luck for Princess Jiahe!"

乔文仙接着说：“爹，仪王与佳和郡主路途劳顿，有什么话进屋说。”
Hearing her name, Qiao Mengrong stepped forward with a graceful curtsy. "Following Brother's lead: Grandfather, Grandmothers, and... these two ladies look my own age; surely I can't call them aunts?"

“对对，文渊说得对，仪王、佳和郡主，快快进屋。”
Giggling shy, one girl cried, "Please don't call me Aunt, Princess Jiahe—I'd die of embarrassment!"

乔守仁领着众人进屋，不过说些叙旧之情，聊聊汴梁的趣事，再说说已故的大女儿，说着说着又怕仪王伤心，很快转移话题。
Madam Qian smiled warm. "Generations say one thing, yet with Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe's exalted rank, call them by name!"

仪王第一次来临安，对乔家不熟悉，乔守仁把乔家的情况一一说明后，请求仪王不要在临安另买府邸，乔家在城外有座宅子，周围景致甚好，又离京城不远，仪王与佳和郡主住在那里再好不过。
Qiao Wenxian introduced them: "Tall one's Wenhui, shorter one's Wenxiu."

申简辰当然乐意，向外祖父说了他来临安任职之事。乔守仁与乔家众人无不高兴，仪王受官家重用，只会给乔家带来好处。
In unison Wenhui and Wenxiu curtsied: "Greetings to Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe!"

大家聊了许久，才散去。乔家的两个女儿带着佳和郡主去安排住处，乔文仙则带着仪王去他的住处安顿。
"Father, they're worn out from half a month on the road. Let's talk indoors," Qiao Wenxian urged.

到了住处，白风本想帮王爷收拾屋子，一进门见屋内一切齐备，无需他再做什么，向王爷央求去街上玩玩儿。
"Right, right—Wenyuan's right! Come inside, Prince Yi, Princess Jiahe!"

申简辰没拦着：“你去吧，早些回来。”刚说完，白风一溜烟跑掉。
Inside the reception hall flowed warm family talk: Bianliang anecdotes, tender memories of late Consort Qiao cut short lest they sadden the prince. Seeing it was Prince Yi's first trip to Lin'an, Qiao Shouren briefed him on family affairs and urged him not to buy a separate residence: the Qiao family owned a scenic retreat just outside the city walls, close enough for daily errands, ideal for Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe! Gladly accepting, Shen Jianchen also shared his appointment as Military Commissioner of Lin'an, thrilling Qiao Shouren and the whole clan, knowing imperial favor lifted the Qiao house too.

乔文仙说：“也不知你为何要让白风伺候？这小子太不懂规矩，就知道自己玩儿。”
After long conversation they broke up: Wenhui and Wenxiu escorted Princess Jiahe to guest rooms, while Qiao Wenxian guided Prince Yi to his quarters.

“屋子什么都不缺，根本不需要白风收拾，既用不上他，让他出去逛逛也无妨。”
Inside, Bai Feng offered to unpack, only to find every necessity already spotless and ready, so he begged leave to roam Lin'an's streets. "Go on, be back early," Shen Jianchen waved him off; in a flash Bai Feng bolted out.

“白风真幸运，碰上你这么个好主子。”
Qiao Wenxian shook his head. "No idea why you keep Bai Feng around—zero discipline, runs off playing nonstop!"

两人正说着，南双进来一把抱住乔文仙：“公子，我一进府，听说您回来，赶紧跑来看个究竟，果然回来。快一年没见，想死我了。”
"Room has everything already. No chores left, why trap him indoors?"

申简辰在一旁笑道：“你还说我，你的书童也没大没小，一进门就把你抱住。”
"Lucky rascal landing a boss like you!"

“我家南双可比白风强多了。”乔文仙无奈地笑笑，扶住南双，“怪热的，别一见面就搂抱我，快放开。”
Mid-laugh, Nan Shuang burst in and threw both arms around Qiao Wenxian. "Young Master! Heard you were home the second I walked in and ran straight here! Nearly a whole year—missed you to death!"

南双只能放开：“人家想公子嘛，公子不在这段时日，都没机会出去见世面，整日守在林府门外，太没意思。”
Shen Jianchen burst out laughing. "Look who's talking about discipline—your own study attendant tackles you through the door!"

乔文仙说：“还不赶紧拜见仪王！”
"My Nan Shuang beats Bai Feng ten times over!" Chuckling helpless, Qiao Wenxian nudged him off. "Too warm for bear hugs—let go!"

南双拜道：“仪王安好。”
Releasing him, Nan Shuang whined, "Missed you, Master! Whole time you were gone I never got to see the world—stuck camping outside Lin Manor gate every day, boring as dirt!"

申简辰问：“你家公子为何让你守在林府门外？”
"Quit babbling and bow to Prince Yi!"

南双回道：“仪王有所不知，我家公子喜欢幽栖居士的诗词，幽栖居士嫁到林府后，公子去了汴梁，便让我守在林府门外搜集幽栖居士写废的手稿。”
Nan Shuang dropped into a quick bow: "Greetings, Prince Yi!"

申简辰心想，幽栖居士应该就是那位名叫朱淑真的女子，没想到文渊如此痴情。
"Why'd your master make you camp outside Lin Manor?" Shen Jianchen asked.

乔文仙并不介意南双将实情说出，直接问道：“你在林府门外守着，有没有机会见到幽栖居士？”
"Your Highness doesn't know—my master adores Youqi Jushi's poetry! When she married into Lin Manor and Master left for Bianliang, he posted me outside their gate collecting her discarded manuscript scraps!"

南双回道：“从来没见过，只见过她的丫鬟苦荞。”
Youqi Jushi—that had to be Zhu Shuzhen! Wenyuan really had it bad!

乔文仙走到桌旁坐下，提笔在花笺上写了些字，折好后，把南双叫到跟前：“你把这个交给苦荞，就说我想见幽栖居士一面。”
Unbothered by Nan Shuang spilling everything, Qiao Wenxian cut straight to business: "Camping out there all this time, did you ever glimpse Youqi Jushi?"

南双并未接：“公子还是省省吧，近期幽栖居士不在林府，我听林府的下人说，她现在可厉害了，跟林练兵官打了一架跑到乡下去，据说要住一个月，算算时间，已经快住大半个月，等她回来公子再约不迟。”
"Never once. Only her maid Kuqiao."

乔文仙收回花笺纸，揉成一团，扔到木桶里，略显失落：“你说她变得厉害，是怎么回事？”
Stepping to the desk, Qiao Wenxian penned a quick note on ornamental paper, folded it neat, and held it out. "Deliver this to Kuqiao—tell her I beg a brief meeting with Youqi Jushi."

南双解释道：“我是听林府下人说的，刚开始那会儿只有练兵官打幽栖居士的份儿，每次打得身上都是伤，可最近幽栖居士好像变了个人，一下子厉害起来，连练兵官都害怕。”
Nan Shuang didn't reach for it. "Save your ink, Master! Youqi Jushi isn't even at Lin Manor right now! Heard from Lin servants she's turned super fierce, fought Training Officer Lin, and moved out to the countryside for a whole month! Counting days, she's been gone almost three weeks—wait till she's back to invite her."

乔文仙叹道：“她本是文弱女子，嫁给那样的丈夫，不改变还怎么活下去？我已故的祖母说得没错，女子想改变男子不容易，男子却可以轻易改变一个女子，要想知道女子嫁的丈夫如何，只要看看那女子便知。”
Crumpling the ornamental sheet and tossing it into the waste tub, Qiao Wenxian looked crestfallen. "Turned fierce—what happened?"

申简辰在一旁听了，并未多想。他心里挂念着千里寻，幽栖居士与其丈夫的关系，以及她的变化，对他来讲毫无吸引力。但见文渊担忧伤感的样子，安慰道：“你不必伤心，她改变也是好事。她目前在乡下，起码不用担心练兵官打她，等她从乡下回来，你再约她出来见见。”
"Just what Lin servants whispered: early on the Training Officer beat her black and blue all the time, then out of nowhere she changed like a whole new person, got tough enough even the Training Officer fears her!"

“说的也是，不想了，想也没用，还是说说你的事，什么时候打算在临安公开节度使的身份？”
Qiao Wenxian let out a bitter sigh. "Gentle scholar forced into survival by a brutal husband... my late grandmother spoke truth: a wife can seldom change a husband, yet a husband changes a wife overnight! Want to know a man's character, just look at his wife!"

“明日先搬到乔家城外的宅子，你再带我到临安城的饭庄、茶馆、酒肆，还有勾栏瓦肆逛逛，等什么时候逛够了，再表明身份。”
Listening beside him, Shen Jianchen paid little mind; his thoughts stayed glued to Qianli Xun, leaving zero headspace for Youqi Jushi's marriage drama. Still, seeing Wenyuan's glum face, he offered comfort: "Cheer up—toughening up is good! Out in the countryside at least Lin Jue can't hit her. Invite her out once she gets back."

“就不怕官家怪罪？”
"True enough—no sense fretting. Tell me your plan: when do you reveal your title as Military Commissioner?"

“官家远在汴梁，他怎么知道我什么时候到达临安？即使知道，我也不怕，理由我都想好，就说我常年病着，没出来过，好不容易出来，到处逛逛，耽搁了时日，想必官家也能理解。”
"Tomorrow we move into your family's suburban retreat. Then walk me through every restaurant, teahouse, wine tavern, and Goulan Wasi in Lin'an. Once I've seen enough, I'll show my seal."

“看来你是有备而来，你打算在临安城逛多久？”
"Not scared His Majesty gets mad?"

“还没想好。不过你放心，时间不会太长，毕竟有任务在身。”
"His Majesty sits way up in Bianliang—how's he know when I reached Lin'an? Even if he finds out, excuse ready: sick indoors all my life, well at last, took a few days sightseeing! He'll understand."

“知道就好。”
"Got all the angles covered! How long do you plan roaming town?"

乔梦容在两位乔家姑娘的陪伴下也在乔府安顿下来。
"Haven't decided—not too long, got official work waiting."

赵朴认识的人乔梦容尽知，可醒来的哥哥就像猜不透的谜，虽离得很近，却怎么也猜不到谜底。
Meanwhile, settled into her guest courtyard by Wenhui and Wenxiu, Qiao Mengrong sat by the window staring at a phoenix tree outside. Past bloom, its canopy hung thick with broad green leaves big as her brother's palms, yet beauty escaped her. Aside from taking office, Brother had come to Lin'an searching for someone... who? Could he really not be Zhao Pu? She had known every soul in Zhao Pu's entire world, yet this awakened brother sat right beside her like an unsolvable riddle.
