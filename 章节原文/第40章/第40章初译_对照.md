# 第40章 来到临安访乔府

汴梁的仪王府留给邹嬷嬷照顾，申简辰收拾行囊，带上乔梦容踏上去往临安之路。

Zou Matron stayed behind to look after the Prince Yi's residence in Bianliang. Shen Jianchen packed his bags and set off for Lin'an with Qiao Mengrong.

---

白风换上侍卫的衣装，头发也梳得整齐，洗干净的脸更显清秀，跟以往判若两人。

Baifeng changed into his guard's uniform, his hair neatly combed. With his face washed clean, he looked even more refined—completely different from his former self.

---

自从跟着王爷，王爷每日抽时间教他申拳，均是在客栈住下后，趁着夜色，把他带到无人之处，教他一招一式。

Ever since joining the prince, the prince had been teaching him Shen Fist every day. They always trained after checking into an inn, under cover of night, in some secluded spot, going through each move step by step.

---

白风的领悟能力很强，招式掌握得也快。但王爷说申拳的威力是靠每日练习得来，必须持之以恒。

Baifeng picked things up quickly and mastered the moves fast. But the prince said the power of Shen Fist came from daily practice—consistent effort was the only way.

---

除练习拳法，白风还要负责王爷每日的衣食住行。

Besides practicing martial arts, Baifeng was also in charge of the prince's daily meals, clothing, lodging, and travel.

---

佳和郡主建议给王爷身边多带几个丫鬟伺候，都被王爷拒绝。

Princess Jiahé suggested bringing more maids to attend the prince, but he refused every time.

---

白风仔细瞧过那些丫鬟，一个个长得挺水灵，王爷竟然不愿意要，他甚觉可惜，忍不住建议王爷带上一个，遥远的旅途中他好有个说话的。可王爷说，身边有他一人足矣。白风由此判断王爷是不近女色之人。

Baifeng had taken a good look at those maids—each one pretty and fresh-looking. He couldn't believe the prince didn't want any of them. He found it such a waste that he couldn't help suggesting the prince take at least one along, so he'd have someone to talk to on the long journey. But the prince said having him alone was more than enough. From this, Baifeng concluded the prince had no interest in women.

---

一路上，乔文仙带下人乘坐一辆马车；白风陪王爷乘坐一辆；佳和郡主带喜兰乘坐一辆。

Along the way, Qiao Wenxian rode in one carriage with his attendants; Baifeng accompanied the prince in another; Princess Jiahé rode with Xilan in a third.

---

好几次佳和郡主想要跟哥哥挤在一辆马车内，她想多靠靠哥哥的肩膀，说不定哥哥还会把她搂在怀里。哥哥却像是看出她的心思似的，拽着白风不放，让她没有任何机会靠近。

Several times Princess Jiahé tried to squeeze into her brother's carriage. She wanted to lean against his shoulder, maybe even have him wrap his arm around her. But her brother seemed to see right through her intentions—he kept Baifeng close by his side the whole time, leaving her no chance to get near.

---

有一次，乔梦容说出想法，哥哥直接拒绝，理由是路途不平坦，车马颠簸，他太粗心，怕照顾不好妹妹，由喜兰照顾他才放心。

Once, Qiao Mengrong voiced the idea, and her brother shut it down immediately. His excuse was that the road was rough, the carriage bumpy, and he was too careless—he was afraid he wouldn't take good care of his sister. Only with Xilan looking after her could he feel at ease.

---

这种仔细一想能找出很多破绽的理由令乔梦容心酸，她只好老老实实带喜兰继续乘坐另一辆马车。

It was the kind of excuse that fell apart if you thought about it too carefully, and it made Qiao Mengrong's heart ache. She had no choice but to obediently stay in the other carriage with Xilan.

---

白风看到佳和郡主失落的表情时，他的心里挺不好受，在马车里劝王爷让他跟郡主换换。王爷并未答应，批评他尽好侍卫的职责，少管闲事。

When Baifeng saw Princess Jiahé's disappointed expression, he felt pretty bad about it. He tried persuading the prince in the carriage to let him switch places with the princess. The prince didn't agree, instead telling him to focus on his duties as a guard and stop meddling in other people's business.

---

白风觉得这哪是闲事，这是王爷的终身大事！他听喜兰说过，明年中秋节王爷要与佳和郡主成亲，跟未婚妻同乘一辆马车，多好的事！王爷却不愿意，白风想不通。

Baifeng didn't think this was “other people's business” at all—this was the prince's lifelong happiness! He'd heard from Xilan that the prince was supposed to marry Princess Jiahé next Mid-Autumn Festival. Riding in the same carriage as his fiancée—what could be better than that? Yet the prince didn't want to. Baifeng just couldn't figure it out.

---

大约在路上行了半个多月，终于到达临安。

After roughly half a month on the road, they finally reached Lin'an.

---

一路顺利，没有遇到梁师成口中的流寇，申简辰反而甚感失望。如若能碰到些像梁山好汉那样的义士，他定会与他们成为朋友。

The journey went smoothly. They didn't run into any of the roving bandits Liang Shicheng had warned about, which actually disappointed Shen Jianchen. If he'd encountered righteous heroes like those from Mount Liang, he'd have made friends with them for sure.

---

申简辰不愿先去临安府任职，他想多自由一段时日，到处逛逛，碰碰运气，看能不能找到千里寻。

Shen Jianchen didn't want to report to the Lin'an prefectural office right away. He wanted a bit more freedom first—to roam around, try his luck, and see if he could find Qianli Xun.

---

算卦先生说在与“口”有关的地方能见到她，申简辰决定到临安城的饭庄，酒肆等地先看看。

The fortune teller had said he'd find her in a place related to "mouth." Shen Jianchen decided to start by checking the restaurants, wine shops, and similar places around Lin'an.

---

临安城没有汴梁繁华，但也不差，而且与汴梁相比别具一番韵味，到处充满婉约的风格。无论是建筑，街市的布置，商铺以及售卖之物，亦或是阑珊小桥，均是一副水乡之态。

Lin'an wasn't as bustling as Bianliang, but it held its own. Compared to the capital, it had a charm all its own—elegant and graceful everywhere you looked. The architecture, the street markets, the shops and their wares, even the small arched bridges—all of it breathed the spirit of a water town.

---

乔文仙征求仪王的意思后，先带众人回乔府。

After checking with Prince Yi, Qiao Wenxian led the group back to the Qiao Manor first.

---

乔家是临安的大户，算不上数一数二，也排得上前五。

The Qiao family was a prominent household in Lin'an—not quite the top, but solidly in the top five.

---

除了因祖上福荫外，还因大女儿有幸进宫，生了仪王。尽管女儿走得早，仪王一直病着，总归成为皇亲国戚，提起来面儿上既有光，别人瞧见乔家人也多少会礼让三分。

Besides the blessings of their ancestors, it was also because their eldest daughter had been fortunate enough to enter the palace and bear Prince Yi. Though she'd passed away early and the prince had been ill for years, they'd still become imperial in-laws. It brought the family face, and people treated the Qiaos with extra courtesy.

---

乔家当家的是乔守仁，已过花甲之年，除去大女儿外，膝下还有四个女儿，二女儿和三女儿均是正室钱氏所出，已嫁为人妇。

The head of the Qiao family was Qiao Shouren, past sixty years of age. Besides the eldest daughter, he had four other daughters. The second and third were born to the main wife, Madam Qian, and were already married.

---

乔守仁四十多岁时，考虑到乔家香火无人继承，钱氏说服乔守仁纳一房妾室，姓米，年纪与大女儿相仿，进门没两年便生下乔文仙，一家人相当高兴。

When Qiao Shouren was in his forties, worried that no one would carry on the Qiao family line, Madam Qian persuaded him to take a concubine. Her surname was Mi, about the same age as the eldest daughter. Within two years of entering the household, she gave birth to Qiao Wenxian, to the whole family's delight.

---

因将来乔文仙要继承香火，一直养在钱氏身边。米氏倒不介意，她还年轻，可以继续生儿子，可谁知一连生了两位千金，现均待字闺中。米氏心里虽产生过些许不满，因钱氏脾气秉性都好，对她也不错，没有让亲生儿子疏远她，慢慢的也就不怎么介意，反而与钱氏相处融洽。

Since Qiao Wenxian was meant to carry on the family line, she was raised by Madam Qian. Madam Mi didn't mind—she was still young and could keep trying for a son. But who knew? She ended up having two more daughters, both still unmarried at home. Madam Mi had felt some resentment at first, but Madam Qian had a good temper and treated her well, never letting her own son grow distant from her. Gradually, the resentment faded, and the two women got along quite harmoniously.

---

三辆马车经过临安闹市，往城南而行，到达乔府大门口，都下了马车。门口的下人见了迎上来：“公子可回来了！”

The three carriages passed through Lin'an's busy market district and headed toward the south of the city. When they reached the front gate of the Qiao Manor, everyone disembarked. A servant at the gate rushed forward: "Young Master, you're back!"

---

乔文仙吩咐道：“快去告诉老爷，仪王来访。”

Qiao Wenxian instructed, "Quick, go tell the master that Prince Yi has come to visit."

---

下人瞧一眼公子身后之人，小跑着进入府院。

The servant glanced at the people behind the young master and jogged into the manor.

---

乔文仙带着仪王等人也走进府内，迎面是假山巨石和水流，像一道屏障，使得府内的景致不易被人一览无遗。拐过巨石，房屋出现在眼前，但需穿过架在水上的长廊，还好水面不宽，所谓的长廊并不算太长，大家很快穿越而过。

Qiao Wenxian led Prince Yi and the others into the manor. Right at the entrance stood a rockery with flowing water, like a screen that kept the interior from being seen all at once. Rounding the rocks, the buildings came into view, but they had to cross a long corridor built over the water. Fortunately, the water wasn't wide, so the corridor wasn't too long, and they crossed it quickly.

---

迎面走来一群人，领头的便是乔守仁。身材匀称，面容庄重，头发已经斑白，依然梳理得整齐。身旁跟着钱氏和米氏，身后是两个还未出阁的女儿。

A group of people approached, led by Qiao Shouren himself. He had a well-proportioned figure and a solemn face. His hair had turned gray, but was still neatly combed. Beside him stood Madam Qian and Madam Mi, and behind them were his two unmarried daughters.

---

一行人见到仪王，先是跪拜。

When the group saw Prince Yi, they first knelt and bowed.

---

申简辰穿越成王爷经常被下人跪拜，倒也渐渐习惯，可被亲人跪拜，还是头一遭，赶紧上前扶起乔守仁。

Shen Jianchen had gotten used to servants kneeling before him ever since he'd transmigrated into the role of prince. But having family members kneel—that was a first. He hurried forward to help Qiao Shouren up.

---

“外祖父、两位外祖母、还有两位……”

"Maternal Grandfather, two Maternal Grandmothers, and the two..."

---

申简辰不知该如何称呼，她们看着比他年纪还小些，就是辈分大，称呼到嘴边，却怎么也说不出口，干脆不再称呼：“你们都是我的亲人，岂有亲人跪拜亲人之礼？再说我是小辈，大家更无需跪拜。”

Shen Jianchen didn't know how to address them. They looked even younger than him, yet they were a generation above him. The titles were on the tip of his tongue, but he just couldn't bring himself to say them. He gave up: “You're all my family. How can family kneel to family? Besides, I'm the younger generation—you really don't need to bow.”

---

所有人都笑着起身。

Everyone rose with smiles.

---

乔守仁将仪王上下打量一番：“我刚听下人说仪王来访，甚觉惊讶，你病着，怎么可能来临安？亲眼见到才相信是真的，你能恢复健康，是老天保佑，也是乔家之福，更是佳和郡主的福气。”

Qiao Shouren looked Prince Yi up and down. “When the servants just told me Prince Yi was visiting, I was quite shocked. You've been ill—how could you possibly come to Lin'an? Only now that I see you with my own eyes do I believe it. Your recovery is heaven's blessing, a blessing for the Qiao family, and especially a blessing for Princess Jiahé.”

---

乔梦容见提起自己，上前拜道：“我也跟着哥哥称呼各位，外祖父，两位外祖母，还有……，两位姑娘看着与我年纪相仿，我总不能称呼她们姨母？”

Seeing that they'd mentioned her, Qiao Mengrong stepped forward and bowed. "I'll follow my brother in addressing everyone. Maternal Grandfather, two Maternal Grandmothers, and... these two young ladies look about my age. I can't possibly call them Aunts, can I?"

---

两位姑娘害羞地笑着，一位说道：“佳和郡主可别称呼我姨母，羞死了！”

The two young ladies smiled shyly. One of them said, "Please don't call me Aunt, Princess Jiahé. That would be too embarrassing!"

---

钱氏说：“虽然按照辈分如此，但仪王与佳和郡主身份尊贵，只管唤名字就行。”

Madam Qian said, “Though that's the proper seniority, Prince Yi and Princess Jiahé are of noble rank—just call them by their names.”

---

乔文仙主动介绍：“个高的叫文慧，矮一些的是文秀。”

Qiao Wenxian offered the introduction: "The taller one is Wenhui, the shorter one is Wenxiu."

---

文慧与文秀齐声拜道：“仪王，佳和郡主安好”

Wenhui and Wenxiu bowed in unison: "Your Highness Prince Yi, Your Highness Princess Jiahé."

---

乔文仙接着说：“爹，仪王与佳和郡主路途劳顿，有什么话进屋说。”

Qiao Wenxian continued, "Father, Prince Yi and Princess Jiahé have had a tiring journey. Let's talk inside."

---

“对对，文渊说得对，仪王、佳和郡主，快快进屋。”

"Yes, yes, Wenyuan is right. Prince Yi, Princess Jiahé, please come inside."

---

乔守仁领着众人进屋，不过说些叙旧之情，聊聊汴梁的趣事，再说说已故的大女儿，说着说着又怕仪王伤心，很快转移话题。

Qiao Shouren led everyone inside. They exchanged pleasantries, chatted about interesting things in Bianliang, and mentioned the late eldest daughter—but before long, worried the prince might feel sad, they quickly changed the subject.

---

仪王第一次来临安，对乔家不熟悉，乔守仁把乔家的情况一一说明后，请求仪王不要在临安另买府邸，乔家在城外有座宅子，周围景致甚好，又离京城不远，仪王与佳和郡主住在那里再好不过。

It was Prince Yi's first time in Lin'an, and he wasn't familiar with the Qiao family. After explaining the family situation in detail, Qiao Shouren asked the prince not to buy another residence in Lin'an. The Qiao family had a country house outside the city with beautiful surroundings, not far from the capital. It would be perfect for Prince Yi and Princess Jiahé to stay there.

---

申简辰当然乐意，向外祖父说了他来临安任职之事。乔守仁与乔家众人无不高兴，仪王受官家重用，只会给乔家带来好处。

Shen Jianchen was happy to agree. He told his maternal grandfather about his appointment in Lin'an. Qiao Shouren and the entire Qiao family were delighted—Prince Yi being trusted by the emperor could only bring benefits to the family.

---

大家聊了许久，才散去。乔家的两个女儿带着佳和郡主去安排住处，乔文仙则带着仪王去他的住处安顿。

They chatted for a long while before dispersing. The two Qiao daughters took Princess Jiahé to arrange her quarters, while Qiao Wenxian led the prince to his.

---

到了住处，白风本想帮王爷收拾屋子，一进门见屋内一切齐备，无需他再做什么，向王爷央求去街上玩玩儿。

When they reached the quarters, Baifeng had planned to help the prince tidy up. But the moment he stepped inside, he saw everything was already in order—nothing for him to do. He begged the prince to let him go play on the streets.

---

申简辰没拦着：“你去吧，早些回来。”刚说完，白风一溜烟跑掉。

Shen Jianchen didn't stop him. "Go ahead, but come back early." No sooner had he spoken than Baifeng dashed off like a puff of smoke.

---

乔文仙说：“也不知你为何要让白风伺候？这小子太不懂规矩，就知道自己玩儿。”

Qiao Wenxian said, “I don't know why you insisted on having Baifeng attend you. That kid has no manners at all—only knows how to play.”

---

“屋子什么都不缺，根本不需要白风收拾，既用不上他，让他出去逛逛也无妨。”

“The room has everything it needs—Baifeng isn't needed to tidy up at all. Since there's nothing for him to do here, letting him go for a stroll is fine.”

---

“白风真幸运，碰上你这么个好主子。”

"Baifeng is truly lucky to have such a good master."

---

两人正说着，南双进来一把抱住乔文仙：“公子，我一进府，听说您回来，赶紧跑来看个究竟，果然回来。快一年没见，想死我了。”

Just as the two were talking, Nanshuang burst in and threw his arms around Qiao Wenxian. “Young Master! The moment I got to the manor, I heard you were back and ran straight over to see for myself. Sure enough, you're here! It's been almost a year—I missed you so much！”

---

申简辰在一旁笑道：“你还说我，你的书童也没大没小，一进门就把你抱住。”

Shen Jianchen laughed from the side. “You're one to talk. Your page boy has no respect either—hugging you the moment he walks in.”

---

“我家南双可比白风强多了。”乔文仙无奈地笑笑，扶住南双，“怪热的，别一见面就搂抱我，快放开。”

“My Nanshuang is way better behaved than Baifeng.” Qiao Wenxian smiled helplessly and steadied Nanshuang. “It's hot enough—don't hug me the moment you see me. Let go.”

---

南双只能放开：“人家想公子嘛，公子不在这段时日，都没机会出去见世面，整日守在林府门外，太没意思。”

Nanshuang let go reluctantly. “I missed you, Young Master! While you were gone, I never got a chance to go out and see the world. I was stuck guarding the Lin Manor gate all day—so boring.”

---

乔文仙说：“还不赶紧拜见仪王！”

Qiao Wenxian said, "Hurry and greet Prince Yi!"

---

南双拜道：“仪王安好。”

Nanshuang bowed. "Your Highness Prince Yi."

---

申简辰问：“你家公子为何让你守在林府门外？”

Shen Jianchen asked, "Why did your young master have you guard the Lin Manor gate?"

---

南双回道：“仪王有所不知，我家公子喜欢幽栖居士的诗词，幽栖居士嫁到林府后，公子去了汴梁，便让我守在林府门外搜集幽栖居士写废的手稿。”

Nanshuang replied, "Your Highness may not know this, but my young master is a great admirer of Youqi Jushi's poetry. After Youqi Jushi married into the Lin Manor and the young master went to Bianliang, he had me stationed outside the Lin gate to collect Youqi Jushi's discarded drafts."

---

申简辰心想，幽栖居士应该就是那位名叫朱淑真的女子，没想到文渊如此痴情。

Shen Jianchen thought to himself—Youqi Jushi must be that woman named Zhu Shuzhen. He hadn't expected Wenyuan to be so devoted.

---

乔文仙并不介意南双将实情说出，直接问道：“你在林府门外守着，有没有机会见到幽栖居士？”

Qiao Wenxian didn't mind Nanshuang telling the truth. He asked directly, "While you were guarding the Lin gate, did you ever get a chance to see Youqi Jushi?"

---

南双回道：“从来没见过，只见过她的丫鬟苦荞。”

Nanshuang replied, "Never saw her. I only ever saw her maid, Kuqiao."

---

乔文仙走到桌旁坐下，提笔在花笺上写了些字，折好后，把南双叫到跟前：“你把这个交给苦荞，就说我想见幽栖居士一面。”

Qiao Wenxian walked over to the table, sat down, picked up a brush, and wrote something on a floral letter sheet. After folding it, he called Nanshuang over. "Give this to Kuqiao. Tell her I'd like to meet Youqi Jushi in person."

---

南双并未接：“公子还是省省吧，近期幽栖居士不在林府，我听林府的下人说，她现在可厉害了，跟林练兵官打了一架跑到乡下去，据说要住一个月，算算时间，已经快住大半个月，等她回来公子再约不迟。”

Nanshuang didn't take it. “Young Master, save your breath. Youqi Jushi hasn't been at the Lin Manor lately. I heard from the Lin servants that she's gotten really fierce now—she had a fight with Military Training Officer Lin and ran off to the countryside. Supposedly she's staying there a month. Counting the time, she's already been there most of a month. Wait till she's back before you ask to see her.”

---

乔文仙收回花笺纸，揉成一团，扔到木桶里，略显失落：“你说她变得厉害，是怎么回事？”

Qiao Wenxian took back the floral letter, crumpled it into a ball, and tossed it into a wooden bucket. He looked slightly dejected. “You say she's gotten fierce—what's that about？”

---

南双解释道：“我是听林府下人说的，刚开始那会儿只有练兵官打幽栖居士的份儿，每次打得身上都是伤，可最近幽栖居士好像变了个人，一下子厉害起来，连练兵官都害怕。”

Nanshuang explained, “I heard it from the Lin servants. At first, it was only the Military Training Officer beating Youqi Jushi—every time, she'd come back covered in bruises. But recently, she seems to have turned into a different person. She suddenly got fierce, even the Military Training Officer is afraid of her now.”

---

乔文仙叹道：“她本是文弱女子，嫁给那样的丈夫，不改变还怎么活下去？我已故的祖母说得没错，女子想改变男子不容易，男子却可以轻易改变一个女子，要想知道女子嫁的丈夫如何，只要看看那女子便知。”

Qiao Wenxian sighed. “She was a gentle, frail woman. Married to such a husband—if she didn't change, how could she survive? My late grandmother was right—a woman can't easily change a man, but a man can easily reshape a woman. To know what kind of husband a woman has married, one need only look at the woman herself.”

---

申简辰在一旁听了，并未多想。他心里挂念着千里寻，幽栖居士与其丈夫的关系，以及她的变化，对他来讲毫无吸引力。但见文渊担忧伤感的样子，安慰道：“你不必伤心，她改变也是好事。她目前在乡下，起码不用担心练兵官打她，等她从乡下回来，你再约她出来见见。”

Shen Jianchen listened from the side but didn't think much of it. His mind was on Qianli Xun. Youqi Jushi's relationship with her husband and her transformation held no interest for him at all. But seeing Wenyuan's worried, sorrowful expression, he offered comfort: “Don't be sad. Her changing is a good thing. She's in the countryside now—at least she doesn't have to worry about the Military Training Officer beating her. When she comes back, you can ask to see her then.”

---

“说的也是，不想了，想也没用，还是说说你的事，什么时候打算在临安公开节度使的身份？”

“You're right. No use thinking about it. Let's talk about you instead—when do you plan to reveal your identity as Military Commissioner in Lin'an？”

---

“明日先搬到乔家城外的宅子，你再带我到临安城的饭庄、茶馆、酒肆，还有勾栏瓦肆逛逛，等什么时候逛够了，再表明身份。”

"Tomorrow we'll move to the Qiao family's country house first. Then you can take me around Lin'an's restaurants, teahouses, wine shops, and entertainment precincts. Once we've seen enough, I'll reveal my identity."

---

“就不怕官家怪罪？”

"Aren't you afraid the emperor will be angry?"

---

“官家远在汴梁，他怎么知道我什么时候到达临安？即使知道，我也不怕，理由我都想好，就说我常年病着，没出来过，好不容易出来，到处逛逛，耽搁了时日，想必官家也能理解。”

“The emperor is far away in Bianliang. How would he know when I arrived in Lin'an? Even if he did, I'm not afraid. I've already thought of an excuse—I'll say I've been ill for years and never traveled. Now that I finally had the chance, I roamed around a bit and lost track of time. I'm sure the emperor will understand.”

---

“看来你是有备而来，你打算在临安城逛多久？”

"Looks like you came prepared. How long do you plan to roam around Lin'an?"

---

“还没想好。不过你放心，时间不会太长，毕竟有任务在身。”

“Haven't decided yet. But don't worry—it won't be too long. I do have a mission, after all.”

---

“知道就好。”

"Just make sure you remember that."

乔梦容在两位乔家姑娘的陪伴下也在乔府安顿下来。

Accompanied by the two Qiao girls, Qiao Mengrong also settled into the Qiao Manor.

---

她坐在窗前，瞧着窗外的梧桐树。梧桐已过花期，剩下一树跟哥哥的巴掌差不多大的叶片，翠绿翠绿的，可她无心欣赏。哥哥来临安除了任职，还要找人，他到底找的是谁？难道真如哥哥所说，他不是赵朴？

She sat by the window, gazing at the parasol tree outside. The tree had long passed its flowering season, leaving nothing but leaves as big as her brother's palm, lush and green. But she had no heart for the view. Her brother had come to Lin'an not just to take up his post, but also to find someone. Who exactly was he looking for? Could it really be true, as her brother had said, that he wasn't Zhao Pu?

---

赵朴认识的人乔梦容尽知，可醒来的哥哥就像猜不透的谜，虽离得很近，却怎么也猜不到谜底。

Everyone Zhao Pu knew, Qiao Mengrong knew too. But her brother since waking was like an unsolvable riddle—so close, yet she couldn't fathom the answer no matter how hard she tried.
