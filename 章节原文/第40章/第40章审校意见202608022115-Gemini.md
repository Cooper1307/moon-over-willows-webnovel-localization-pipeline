### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 小侍卫白风内心的幽默八卦（对应第7、8段：误以为王爷“不近女色”）
> **【原文】** 白风由此判断王爷是不近女色之人。白风觉得这哪是闲事，这是王爷的终身大事！...跟未婚妻同乘一辆马车，多好的事！王爷却不愿意，白风想不通。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    From this, Baifeng concluded the prince **had no interest in women**. Baifeng didn't think this was "other people's business" at all—this was **the prince's lifelong happiness!** ...Riding in the same carriage as his fiancée—what could be better than that?
*   **修改后 (After):**
    ***His Highness must be completely immune to female charms,*** Baifeng concluded in amazement. 
    How could the prince call this "other people's business"? This was his own **lifelong marriage on the line!** Baifeng had overheard from Xilan that the prince was set to marry Princess Jiahé next Mid-Autumn Festival. Sharing a carriage with his gorgeous fiancée—what could possibly be better than that? Yet the prince treated her like a hot potato. Baifeng simply couldn't wrap his head around it.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    1. **斜体格式**：将白风内心的吐槽严格写为**斜体 (Italics)**。
    2. 俚语化：用 "treated her like a hot potato"（像烫手山芋一样避之不及）替换平淡的 "didn't want to"，生动展现了申简辰躲避未婚妻时的尴尬。

#### 2. 现代穿越者面对古代辈分的爆笑尴尬（对应第24-26段：叫年轻姑娘“姨母”）
> **【原文】** "外祖父、两位外祖母、还有两位……" 申简辰不知该如何称呼，她们看着比他年纪还小些，就是辈分大... "佳和郡主可别称呼我姨母，羞死了！"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    "Maternal Grandfather, two Maternal Grandmothers, and the two..." Shen Jianchen didn't know how to address them. **They looked even younger than him...** "I can't possibly call them Aunts, can I?" The two young ladies smiled shyly. One of them said, "Please don't call me Aunt, Princess Jiahé. That would be too embarrassing!"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "Maternal Grandfather, both Maternal Grandmothers, and... these two..." Shen Jianchen choked on the rest of his words. **The two girls looked noticeably younger than him, yet in the family hierarchy, they were technically his aunts!** The titles hovered on the tip of his tongue, but he just couldn't force them out. 
    Qiao Mengrong piped up playfully, "Maternal Grandfather, both Grandmothers... but as for these two young ladies, they look about my age. I can't possibly call them *Auntie*, can I?"
    The two girls blushed furiously. "Please don't call us *Auntie*, Princess Jiahé! That's way too embarrassing!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    突出申简辰作为一个现代成年男性要叫十几岁小姑娘“小姨母”时的窒息感（choked on the rest of his words）。斜体强调 *Auntie*，增强了古代家族大辈分压人的幽默效果。

#### 3. 仆人传八卦时的口语化张力（对应第44-46段：朱淑真变厉害了）
> **【原文】** "刚开始那会儿只有练兵官打幽栖居士的份儿，每次打得身上都是伤，可最近幽栖居士好像变了个人，一下子厉害起来，连练兵官都害怕。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Nanshuang explained, "I heard it from the Lin servants. At first, it was only the Military Training Officer beating Youqi Jushi—every time, she'd come back covered in bruises. But recently, she seems to have turned into a different person. She suddenly got fierce, even the Military Training Officer is afraid of her now."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    "I heard it straight from the Lin household servants!" Nanshuang gossiped. "Back then, that Military Training Officer used to beat Youqi Jushi as he pleased—**she'd be left black and blue every time**. But recently? **It’s like Youqi Jushi became an entirely different person overnight!** She got so ridiculously fierce and tough that even that brute of a husband is terrified of her now!"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    书童南双是在眉飞色舞地讲八卦。将 "covered in bruises" 升级为口语表达 "left black and blue"（被打得鼻青脸肿/青一块紫一块）；将 "turned into a different person" 升级为 "became an entirely different person overnight"，极具戏剧感染力。

#### 4. 极致的戏剧讽刺（Dramatic Irony）（对应第49、50段：申简辰对老婆八卦冷眼旁观）
> **【原文】** 申简辰在一旁听了，并未多想。他心里挂念着千里寻，幽栖居士与其丈夫的关系，以及她的变化，对自己来讲毫无吸引力。

*   **修改前 (Before):**

*   **修改后 (After):**
    Shen Jianchen listened off to the side, completely unimpressed. ***Some stranger's martial spat and personality change has nothing to do with me,*** he thought, his mind solely preoccupied with finding Qianli Xun. This 'Youqi Jushi' and her domestic drama didn't interest him in the slightest. 
    Still, seeing Qiao Wenyuan looking so dejected, he offered a friendly pat on the shoulder. "Don't beat yourself up..."
*   **💡 修改理由:** 
    **本章最大的看点！** 申简辰根本不知道大家口中这个“把老公打得服服帖帖、跑去乡下避难的幽栖居士”正是他朝思暮想的千里寻！精修版强化了他的内心吐槽 ***Some stranger's martial spat... has nothing to do with me***，这种“近在咫尺却浑然不知”的戏剧讽刺效果在网文里会让读者疯狂叩桌、欲罢不能！
