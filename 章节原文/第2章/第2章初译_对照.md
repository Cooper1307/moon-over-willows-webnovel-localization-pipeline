第2章 对决地震后穿越
## Chapter 2: Chapter 2: A Duel, an Earthquake, and Transmigration

还没想清楚该怎么办，肚子开始“咕咕”叫，一杯掺了水的牛奶根本不够。千里寻又走到餐厅，打开冰箱，除了牛奶，什么都没有。
Before she could even map out her next move, her stomach let out a loud rumble. Half a glass of watered-down milk was nowhere near enough. Qianli Xun walked back into the dining area and pulled the fridge door open. Nothing inside except milk.

她拨通徒弟的电话：“喂，二杠，你师父我被网暴，出不去，楼下还有好多记者围着，你给我买一盒肠粉，一杯豆浆，一碗豆腐脑送过来，记着豆腐脑多加辣子。”
She dialed her apprentice. "Hey, Er'gang, your master is getting canceled online and trapped indoors with reporters swarming downstairs. Buy me a box of steamed rice rolls, a cup of soy milk, and a bowl of tofu pudding, and tell them to load the pudding with extra chili oil."

“这么多，能吃得下吗？”
"All that? Can you even finish it?"

“全网都在骂我，还不得吃饱些对付他们？对了，来时给我带身古代女装，把帷帽也带上。”
"The whole internet is tearing into me. Don't I need a full belly to fight back? Oh, and bring a set of ancient women's robes and a veiled hat when you come."

“要这些干嘛？”
"What do you need those for?"

“我想回千里拳庄，楼下那么多记者，总得装扮一下，才不会被他们发现。”
"I'm heading back to Qianli Fist Manor. With all those reporters parked outside, I need a disguise so nobody spots me."

“还好大家只知道你是写网络小说的，不知道你跟千里拳庄的关系，那就回拳庄躲躲，你等着，我很快就来。”
"Good thing people only know you as a web novelist and have no clue about Qianli Fist Manor. Lying low at the manor makes sense. Hang tight, I'll be right there."

大约半个小时后，二杠骑摩托车来到小区楼下，拎着早饭和一个大纸袋，看了记者们一眼，走进大楼，给保安悄悄说句话，拐到一旁的电梯，等了一会儿，电梯门自动打开，进入后按亮有数字“22”的按钮。
Roughly half an hour later, Er'gang pulled up outside her apartment complex on his motorcycle, carrying breakfast and a large paper bag. Casting one glance at the reporters, he strode into the lobby, murmured a quick word to the security guard, and turned toward the elevators. When the doors slid open, he stepped inside and hit the button marked "22." The reporters paid him no mind. Wearing a black martial arts uniform with bare, muscled arms, he looked like the last person connected to Qianli Xun.

记者们并未将二杠当回事，因为他身着黑色拳服，露出臂膀上的肌肉，怎么看都与千里寻扯不上关系。
二杠上了二十二层，敲开师父的门，进入后，把大纸袋扔到沙发上，走进厨房将早餐放到餐桌上。
边走边埋怨：“师父不该写小说，千里拳第五百零八代传人的身份足以让你炫耀，这倒好，被全网骂。也好，从此以后老老实实待在拳庄，没事接接商演，挣的钱够你生活，然后再把自己嫁出去，人生也就圆满。”
“我是师父，什么时候轮到徒弟教训师父？”千里寻跟在二杠身后，在餐桌旁坐下，打开塑料袋，拿出早餐，吃了起来。
二杠说：“师父慢慢吃，我在小区外等你。”
千里寻没说话，夹起一块肠粉吃起来，听着二杠出去的关门声，自语道：“我的这些徒弟越来越不把我当回事，等我回到拳庄，定要好好修理。”
千里寻的胃口还真不小，把所有早餐吃完，用纸巾擦擦嘴巴，将废纸巾扔到垃圾桶中，伸了一个大大的懒腰，然后走到卫生间，又是刷牙，又是洗脸，又是整理飘逸的长发。
Up on the twenty-second floor, Er'gang knocked, stepped inside when she opened, tossed the big paper bag onto the sofa, and carried breakfast to the kitchen table, grumbling as he walked. "You never should have written web novels, Master. Being the 508th-generation heir to Qianli Fist gives you plenty to brag about, and instead you end up roasted by the whole internet. Good thing, though. Stay put at the manor from now on, take commercial gigs when you feel like it, make a comfortable living, find yourself a husband, and call it a happy life."

收拾完后，来到客厅，打开纸袋，取出衣服和帷帽，脱了睡衣，将衣服换上。还不错，像个古装女侠。她把手机等重要物件放进包里，斜挎在身上。再拿起帷帽戴在头上，轻纱拂下，确实不容易被认出。
"I'm the master here. Since when does an apprentice lecture his master?" Trailing behind him, Qianli Xun sat down at the table, unpacked the plastic bags, and dug straight in.

到门口换上一双休闲鞋，打开门，上了电梯，来到一楼，心里还是有莫名的紧张感。经过保安身边时，他正在刷短视频，从传出的声音判断又是在喷千里寻。对于下楼外出的人，保安并不在意，继续津津有味地欣赏视频。
"Take your time eating, Master. I'll wait outside the complex gate."

千里寻走出楼门，透过轻纱看到记者们都瞧过来，她更加紧张。
Without answering, Qianli Xun picked up a piece of steamed rice roll. Listening to the front door click shut behind him, she muttered to herself, "These apprentices respect me less every day. Wait till I get back to the manor and whip them into shape."

一位女记者主动打招呼：“你好，请问你住这栋楼第几层？”
Her appetite lived up to the order. She polished off every last bite, wiped her mouth with a tissue, tossed it in the trash, and stretched her arms wide before heading into the bathroom to brush her teeth, wash her face, and comb out her long hair. Back in the living room, she pulled the robes and veiled hat from the paper bag, shed her pajamas, and slipped into the outfit. Not bad; she looked like a classic swordswoman straight out of a drama. Packing her phone and essentials into a crossbody bag, she settled the veiled hat onto her head. Light gauze drifted down around her face, hiding her features well.

“二十二层。”
Slipping into casual sneakers by the door, she took the elevator down to the lobby, an odd flicker of nerves tightening her chest. As she walked past the security guard, he was scrolling through short videos; from the sound blasting out, another clip was trashing Qianli Xun. Too hooked on his screen to notice anyone walking out, he kept right on watching.

听到二十二层，记者们一下子围过来：“你和千里寻住一层？可知道她的情况？”
Stepping through the main doors, Qianli Xun peered through the gauze and saw every reporter's eyes swing her way, making her pulse jump higher. A female reporter stepped forward first. "Excuse me, miss, which floor do you live on?"

千里寻后悔为何不说其他楼层，只怪自己太紧张，智商极度下降。
"Twenty-second."

“我对她不熟，只知道她住在我家对面，一早听到对面的屋子里传出凄惨的哭声，估计是被网暴吓的。你们忙，我还有事。”
At the words "twenty-second," the entire pack surged around her. "You live on the same floor as Qianli Xun? Know how she's holding up?"

千里寻慌忙从记者中逃离，来到小区门口，自语道：“还好没被认出。”
Qianli Xun kicked herself for not naming any other floor; nerves had fried her brain clean. "Don't know her well. All I know is she lives across the hall, and this morning awful crying came out of her place, probably scared half to death by all the online hate. Excuse me, I have places to be."

“师父！这儿！”传来二杠的声音。
Breaking free from the crowd, she hurried out to the complex entrance, breathing under her breath, "Thank goodness nobody recognized me."

千里寻赶紧走过去，跨上摩托的后座，抱住二杠。一阵摩托车启动的呜咽声后，他们已飞驰在马路上，朝千里拳庄而去。
"Master! Over here!" Er'gang called.

千里拳庄建在城市郊区，有一百多名徒弟，男女弟子均有。
Hurrying over, Qianli Xun swung onto the back seat of his motorcycle and wrapped her arms around his waist. With a sharp rev of the engine, they shot down the avenue toward Qianli Fist Manor.

当摩托车在千里拳庄外停下时，申简辰西装革履，带着申拳弟子又来千里拳庄大门外对战，看样子两边还未真正打起来。
Situated out in the city suburbs, Qianli Fist Manor trained over a hundred apprentices, men and women alike. The moment the motorcycle rolled to a stop outside the gates, Qianli Xun spotted Shen Jianchen dressed in a crisp suit, leading his Shen Fist disciples to challenge the manor again. Judging by the tense standoff, no blows had been thrown yet.

千里寻卸下帷帽，扔到二杠的怀里，大声说：“申简辰！不是每月二十五号才来切磋吗？怎么提前来了？”
Pulling off her veiled hat and tossing it into Er'gang's chest, Qianli Xun called out loud, "Shen Jianchen! Isn't our sparring match on the twenty-fifth of each month? Why show up early?"

申简辰转过身，见千里寻一身女侠装扮，甚是美艳，他露出深邃的目光，轻轻一笑：“今天一大早起来，看到你成为全网焦点，赶过来瞧瞧。”
Turning around to see Qianli Xun clad like a striking swordswoman, Shen Jianchen let a faint, knowing smile cross his face as his deep gaze lingered on her. "Woke up early to find you trending across the whole internet, so I came over to check on you."

二杠把帷帽挂在摩托车上，走到申简辰身边，调侃道：“你可是大公司的CEO，放着大公司不管，每个月带你们申拳的弟子找我们比试，该不会是看上我师父？”
Hanging the veiled hat off his handlebars, Er'gang strolled beside Shen Jianchen and teased, "You're CEO of a huge corporation. Leaving your company behind every month to haul Shen Fist students here for a match... sure you aren't just after my master?"

申简辰盯着千里寻：“看上你师父不假，比试也是真，你们这些当徒弟的人，没事劝劝你师父，都三十岁的老姑娘，再不嫁，可就没人要喽。”
Eyes anchored on Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen answered straight, "After your master, yes; here to spar, also yes. You apprentices ought to talk sense into her—an old maid of thirty already, wait any longer and nobody's going to take her."

一早被网暴，气没处撒，又听到说自己年龄大，千里寻更是气不打一出来：“老娘明天才过三十岁生日！……”
Already simmering from the morning's cyberbullying with nowhere to vent, hearing him poke at her age lit Qianli Xun's fuse on the spot. "I don't turn thirty until tomorrow!"

申简辰笑道：“别整天老娘老娘地称呼自己，本来年纪都不小，会把自己叫得更老。”
Shen Jianchen chuckled. "Quit referring to yourself like some tough old boss every other word. You're not getting any younger; talking like that ages you faster."

千里寻更加生气：“申简辰，你听着，老娘今日要跟你决一死战，你要是输了，以后不准来千里拳庄！”
Fury spiked even higher. "Listen up, Shen Jianchen! Today I'm challenging you to a real duel. Lose, and you never set foot near Qianli Fist Manor again!"

“你要是输了呢？”
"And if you lose?"

“输了就输了，你能把我怎样？再说老娘根本不会输！”
"Lose is lose; what could you do about it? Besides, no way I'm losing!"

“你要是输了就嫁给我。”
"If you lose, marry me."

听了申简辰的话，千里拳弟子议论道：“这申CEO什么都好，就是不会追女孩子，这把师父给气的，能嫁给他吗？”
Hearing that, Qianli Fist apprentices murmured among themselves: "CEO Shen has everything going for him except zero game with girls. Winding Master up like this—how on earth would she ever marry him?"

千里寻说道：“想得美！除非猪能上树，老娘再考虑要不要嫁给你！今天晚上七点千年神柳处见，你一人前来，不准带其他人！”
"Keep dreaming!" Qianli Xun shot back. "Only when pigs climb trees will I even think about marrying you! Tonight at seven under the Millennial Divine Willow. Come alone; bring nobody else!"

“收到！”申简辰看上去很开心，对弟子们说，“撤！”
"Copy that!" Looking thrilled beyond words, Shen Jianchen waved to his disciples. "Move out!"

申拳的弟子们跟着师父向一辆劳斯莱斯走去，看到师父脸上一直挂着笑容，一位弟子好奇地问：“师父，你都要被打，怎么还这么开心？”
As the Shen Fist disciples followed their master toward a parked Rolls-Royce, spotting the huge grin plastered across his face, one apprentice asked in puzzlement, "Master, you're about to get beaten up. Why are you grinning like that?"

“什么被打？明明是约会！晚上，明月升起，千年神柳旁，让我想起两句诗：月上柳梢头，人约黄昏后。”申简辰一脸畅想，丝毫都不担忧。
"Beaten up? That's a date! Nighttime, a bright moon climbing overhead beside the Millennial Divine Willow... brings to mind two classic lines: 'The moon rises above the willow tips; our tryst is set past dusk.'" Lost in a daydream, Shen Jianchen looked utterly carefree.

弟子们看着他上了车，议论道：“师父这脑子也不知道是怎么当上CEO？又是怎么管理那么大的公司？还开上这么豪华的车？”
Watching him climb into the luxury car, the disciples traded baffled whispers: "How did Master ever become a CEO with a brain like that? How does he even run a giant company and drive a car this fancy?"

傍晚时分，郊外的千年神柳下，千里寻扎着高马尾，身着白色体恤，一条休闲牛仔裤，脚蹬一双跑鞋，看上去青春靓丽，根本不像一个明日就过三十岁生日的女人。
申简辰换一身休闲装，看着既随意又不失帅气，他盯着千里寻，眼中充满爱意。
旁边的池塘里传来一阵阵青蛙的鸣叫声，打破二人之间的寂静。
等到聒噪的青蛙声停下来，申简辰抬头望了望月亮，快要十五，天上那轮月亮即将圆满，再看看池塘岸边一棵粗壮的神柳，笑道：“喂！千里寻，想和我约会直说，何必借口与我对决？”
“你哪只耳朵听到我想跟你约会？老娘就是来跟你对决！”
说着，千里寻伸出拳头，一个箭步冲上朝申简辰打去。
At dusk beneath the suburban Millennial Divine Willow, Qianli Xun stood with her hair tied in a high ponytail, wearing a crisp white T-shirt, casual jeans, and running shoes—fresh and bright, looking nothing like a woman turning thirty tomorrow. Shen Jianchen had changed into casual wear, relaxed yet sharp, watching her with open affection in his eyes. Frogs croaked in waves from the pond nearby, breaking the stillness between them.

申简辰向另一边躲开。
When the chorus paused, Shen Jianchen looked up at the moon—near the fifteenth of the lunar month, almost full—then glanced over at the thick trunk of the Divine Willow along the bank and laughed. "Hey, Qianli Xun, if you wanted a date with me, just say so. Why dress it up as a duel?"

千里寻不甘示弱，加大攻势力度，以迅猛的拳法组合，发起猛烈攻击，试图压制对方。
"Which ear of yours heard me say date? I came here to fight you!"

申简辰时而躲避，时而运用闪避和拆拳术，时而运用柔和的拳法化解攻势。他见千里寻使出快拳打来，立刻增加拳头的力度，迎上去，当他们的拳头撞到一起时，二人都被对方的力量击得向后退出两米多。
With that, Qianli Xun raised her fists and lunged straight at him. Shen Jianchen sidestepped clean. Unwilling to let up, she stepped up the tempo, unleashing a fierce barrage of rapid boxing combinations to pin him down. Shen Jianchen wove through her strikes, blending dodges, counter-grapple techniques, and supple deflections to neutralize her momentum. The instant she launched Quick Fist, he poured full power into his own punch and met hers head-on. The impact hurled both of them more than two meters back.

“千里寻，你一个女孩子家这么拼干嘛？不如嫁给我，我这么优秀，既是申拳第三百六十代传人，又是大公司CEO，你打着灯笼都难找，多少女孩子都想嫁给我，可我就对你感兴趣，你嫁给我后，不用再写什么网络小说，我养你。”
"Why push yourself this hard, Qianli Xun? You're a girl—why not just marry me? Look at me: 360th-generation heir to Shen Fist, CEO of a major corporation, a catch hard to find even with a lantern! Crowds of women would line up to marry me, yet you're the only one I want. Marry me, quit writing web novels, and I'll support you."

也许是申简辰称她“女孩子”，千里寻心情好了许多，竟然还有些感动，嘴上依然不依不饶：“别妄想，我说过，除非猪能上树，老娘才会嫁给你，老娘说到做到。”
话音刚落，脚下的地突然颠簸起来，幅度甚大，两人受颠簸力度影响，根本站不稳，倒在地上，亲眼目睹旁边的建筑顷刻间化为乌有，他们才反应过来是发生了地震，也真正体会到人在大自然面前太过脆弱。
千年神柳因地震晃动得厉害，拔地而起，即将倾倒，千里寻正好在千年神柳下。
﻿申简辰注意到，努力从倾斜的地面爬起，向千里寻冲去，他刚扑到千里寻身上，神柳倒下，重重地压在二人身上，鲜血渗入颠簸的泥土中。
Maybe because he called her a "girl," her sour mood lifted a shade, stirring an unexpected flicker of warmth, though her tongue refused to yield. "Keep dreaming! I told you: only when pigs climb trees will I marry you, and I keep my word!"

大约十分钟后，一切安静下来，周围一片狼藉，千里寻感觉到自己从身体剥离出来，飘到空中，看到血肉模糊的尸身压在神柳之下，她痛苦极了。
The words had no sooner left her lips than the ground beneath their feet heaved violently. Knocked off balance by the brutal jolts, both tumbled onto the dirt and watched nearby buildings crumble to rubble in seconds. Only then did realization hit: an earthquake! Humans stood fragile as dust before raw nature.

接着看到申简辰也从尸身中剥离出来，她大声喊着：“申简辰！申简辰！”可对方根本听不到她的声音。
Shaking hard under the tremors, the Millennial Divine Willow ripped loose from the earth and began to topple—straight toward Qianli Xun standing beneath its boughs. Spotting the falling trunk, Shen Jianchen forced himself up off the tilting earth and sprinted toward her. The very second he threw his body over hers, the ancient willow crashed down atop them both, crushing them into the heaving soil as bright blood soaked the dirt.

等到申简辰飘得足够高时，他们四目相对。千里寻能感受到，申简辰大声呼唤着她的名字，她努力回应着，却互相听不到对方的声音。
Ten minutes later, stillness fell over the wreckage. Qianli Xun felt herself peel free from her body and float upward into the air; looking down at her mangled corpse pinned beneath the willow broke her heart with searing agony. Then she saw Shen Jianchen drift free of his own body too. "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!" she screamed, yet he could not hear a single sound.

突然，从千年神柳粗壮的树干上涌出一股神力，将飘在空中的两人席卷而去。千里寻只觉眼前一片黑暗，她害怕极了……
Once Shen Jianchen rose high enough, their eyes locked. She could tell he was shouting her name back with everything he had, and she strained to answer, yet silence walled them off from each other. Then an immense, unearthly force surged out of the Divine Willow's massive trunk and swept both drifting spirits away. Pitch darkness swallowed Qianli Xun's sight, wrapping her in sheer terror...
