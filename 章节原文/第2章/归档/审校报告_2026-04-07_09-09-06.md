**Chapter 2: The Duel, the Earthquake, and the Transmigration**

Before she could even figure out her next move, her stomach rumbled. That single glass of milk did absolutely nothing. Qianli Xun walked back to the dining room and opened the fridge. Aside from the milk, it was completely empty.

She dialed her disciple's number. "Hey, Er'gang. Your master is officially getting canceled online. I'm trapped—there's a whole mob of reporters blocking the entrance downstairs. Go fetch me a box of steamed rice rolls, a cup of soy milk, and a bowl of spicy tofu pudding. And make sure to add extra chili to the tofu pudding!"

"That's a lot," Er'gang said. "Can you even finish all that?"

"The whole internet is cursing me out. Shouldn't I eat my fill to have the energy to fight back?" Qianli Xun replied. "Oh, right. When you come over, bring me a traditional ancient-style dress. And don't forget the veiled hat."

"What do you need those for?"

"I'm heading back to Qianli Fist Manor. With so many reporters gathered downstairs, I need a disguise so they won't recognize me."

"It's a good thing everyone only knows you as a web novel author. No one has a clue about your connection to Qianli Fist Manor," Er'gang noted. "Alright then, just head back to the manor and hide out. Wait up, I'll be there soon."

About half an hour later, Er'gang arrived downstairs on his motorcycle. Carrying the breakfast and a large paper bag, he glanced at the reporters, walked into the building, and quietly said something to the security guard. He then turned to a side elevator and waited for a while. The elevator doors automatically opened, and after stepping inside, he pressed the button for the 22nd floor.

The reporters paid Er'gang no attention. He was clad in a black martial arts uniform, his athletic arms in plain sight—nothing about him suggested any connection to Qianli Xun.

Er'gang went up to the 22nd floor, knocked on his master's door, and went inside. He tossed the large paper bag onto the sofa, walked into the kitchen, and put the breakfast on the dining table.

He complained as he walked. "Master, you shouldn't have written novels. Your identity as the 508th generation successor of Qianli Fist is enough for you to show off, but now look—you're being scolded by the whole internet. It's just as well. From now on, stay quietly at the manor and take on some commercial gigs occasionally. You'll earn enough to live on, then marry yourself off, and your life will be complete."

"Yours truly is the master here! Since when does a disciple get to lecture the master?" Following closely behind him, Qianli Xun sat down at the table, opened the plastic bag, and started eating.

"Take your time eating, Master," Er'gang said. "I'll wait for you outside the complex."

Qianli Xun ignored him, picking up a piece of steamed rice roll with her chopsticks. Hearing the front door click shut behind him, she muttered, "These disciples of mine take me less and less seriously. Just wait until I get back to the manor—I am *definitely* going to discipline them."

Qianli Xun truly had a ravenous appetite. She polished off every last bite of the breakfast, wiped her mouth, and tossed the tissue into the trash. Stretching lazily, she headed to the bathroom to brush her teeth, wash her face, and smooth out her long flowing hair.

Refreshed, she returned to the living room and opened the paper bag. Shedding her pajamas, she changed into the new outfit. *Not bad,* she thought. She looked exactly like an ancient swordswoman. Stashing her phone and valuables into her bag, she slung it across her body. She donned the veiled hat and let the light gauze drape over her face—it would be nearly impossible to recognize her now.

She slipped into a pair of casual shoes at the entryway, opened the door, and rode the elevator down. When the doors opened on the first floor, an inexplicable wave of anxiety gripped her. As she walked past the security guard, he was scrolling through short videos on his phone. Judging by the audio, the videos were about Qianli Xun again. Utterly absorbed, the guard didn't even glance at the woman walking out.

Qianli Xun stepped through the lobby doors. Through the translucent veil, she noticed the reporters looking over. Her anxiety spiked.

A female reporter came forward and greeted her. "Hello there! Excuse me, which floor of this building do you live on?"

"The 22nd floor," she blurted out.

Upon hearing '22nd floor,' the reporters instantly crowded around her. "You live on the same floor as Qianli Xun? Do you know anything about her situation?"

Qianli Xun instantly regretted her honesty. She could only blame the sheer panic for making her IQ plummet.

"I don't really know her. All I know is she lives across from my apartment," she said. "Early this morning, I heard miserable crying coming from her place. Probably frightened by all the cyberbullying. Anyway, keep up the good work. I have places to be."

Qianli Xun hurried through the crowd and made it to the complex gates. She muttered to herself, "Good thing they didn't recognize me."

"Master! Over here!" Er'gang's voice rang out.

Qianli Xun rushed over, swung her leg over the back of the motorcycle, and wrapped her arms around Er'gang's waist. With the muffled rumble of the engine, they sped down the road toward Qianli Fist Manor.

Qianli Fist Manor was nestled in the city's suburbs. It had over a hundred disciples, both male and female.

When the motorcycle pulled up outside the manor, Shen Jianchen—dressed in a suit—was already there with his Shen Fist disciples, facing off at the manor's main gates. Tensions were running high, but it looked like neither side had actually traded blows yet.

Qianli Xun yanked off her veiled hat, tossed it into Er'gang's arms, and shouted, "Shen Jianchen! Aren't we supposed to duel on the 25th of every month? Why are you here early?"

Shen Jianchen turned around. Seeing Qianli Xun dressed as a swordswoman, looking strikingly lovely, he gave a faint smile, his eyes calm and deep. "I woke up early this morning and saw you become the internet's top story. I came to check on you."

Er'gang casually hung the veiled hat on the motorcycle, walked up to Shen Jianchen, and smirked. "You're the CEO of a massive corporation. Leaving your empire unattended just to bring your disciples to challenge us every month... could it be that you've got a thing for my master?"

Shen Jianchen kept his gaze fixed on Qianli Xun. "It's true that I have a thing for your master, but the duel is also real. You disciples should spend some time talking some sense into her. She's already an old maid hitting thirty. If she doesn't get married soon, no one is going to want her."

After a morning of relentless cyberbullying with nowhere to vent, and now being called "old"—Qianli Xun had had enough.

"Yours truly is only turning thirty tomorrow!" she burst out.

Shen Jianchen chuckled softly. "Stop calling yourself 'yours truly' all day long. You're already getting up there in years; constantly saying that only makes you sound even older."

Qianli Xun's fury hit the limit. "Shen Jianchen, you listen to me! Yours truly is going to duel you to the death today! If you lose, you are permanently banned from Qianli Fist Manor!"

"And what if you lose?" Shen Jianchen challenged, raising an eyebrow.

"If I lose, I lose! What are you going to do about it?" Qianli Xun sneered. "Besides, yours truly will *never* lose!"

"If you lose, you marry me," Shen Jianchen declared smoothly.

Hearing this, the Qianli Fist disciples murmured among themselves. "This CEO Shen is great in every way, except he has no idea how to court a girl. He's infuriating Master to no end—how is she ever going to agree to marry him?"

"In your dreams!" Qianli Xun snapped back. "When pigs climb trees, yours truly *might* reconsider marrying you! Tonight at seven. By the Millennial Divine Willow. Come alone—you bring anyone else, and it's off!"

"Understood!" Shen Jianchen looked pleased with himself. He turned to his disciples and ordered, "Retreat!"

The Shen Fist disciples followed their master toward a Rolls-Royce. Noticing the smile that had never left his face, one curious disciple couldn't help but ask: "Master, you're about to get beaten. Why are you so happy?"

"What do you mean 'beaten'?" Shen Jianchen said lightly. "This is clearly a date! Tonight, when the bright moon rises by the Millennial Divine Willow... it reminds me of a poem."

He beamed, not looking worried at all:

> *When the moon ascends the willow's crest,*  
> *Lovers rendezvous at twilight's rest.*
