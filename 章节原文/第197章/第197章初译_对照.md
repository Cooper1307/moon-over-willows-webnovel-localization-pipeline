第197章 婚宴之后乔文仙
## Chapter 197: Qiao Wenxian After the Wedding Banquet

邵正邺将眼前的公子仔细瞧一遍，从对方的衣着和容貌来看，应该不是出自普通人家，说不定是哪位临安权贵。
邵正邺仔细回想，那日金人带人来寻事，只有林统制知道。他定然会禀报上司，难道眼前这位官职比林统制还高？没想到临安也有这等年轻有为的官员。
邵正邺拱手行礼道：“不知公子怎么称呼？”
申简辰本就不想隐瞒，向白风示意。
白风骄傲地说：“你眼前这位是官家的十三子仪王。”
邵正邺与儿子邵志泽怎么都没想到节度使仪王会来光临邵家镖局，武功还如此高强，二人既欣喜又敬重，上前跪下。
“不知公子正是仪王，刚才多有得罪，还请恕罪。”
“刚才跟你的人对打，算是帮本王疏散筋骨，你何罪之有？起来吧，给我说说那伙金人之事。”
邵正邺起身，先将仪王请入屋内，好茶伺候。他与儿子不敢入座，恭敬站立着。
待仪王品过茶后，与儿子互相交换眼色，才说道：“那伙金人，邵家也不知是何来头，看那样子，并不是普通客商。他们之所以来邵家镖局闹事，恐怕是听说我邵家有抗金之心，而且杀过几个金人，所以前来寻仇。”
听到“抗金”二字，申简辰兴致勃勃：“邵家爱国，甚合本王心意，你这个朋友本王交定了！”
一位王爷要与自己交朋友，邵正邺当然开心，同时也觉得自己对待朋友不够真诚，刚才的说辞真假参半，有些事邵家不能随意张扬。他与儿子行礼道：“仪王能瞧得上小人，是邵家之幸，还有一事需向仪王禀明。”
“什么禀明不禀明的，既是朋友，坐下说话。”申简辰早已想结交些好汉，没有遇到梁山好汉，认识像邵家这等抗金之人也是好的。
邵正邺不再客气，挑椅子坐下；邵志泽是晚辈，不敢落座，老实站在父亲身旁。
“今日见仪王拳法精妙，甚是佩服。令我感到奇怪的是仪王的拳法竟然与来邵家寻事的金人如出一辙。”
“竟有此等事？”申简辰是申拳第三百六十代传人，仔细推算，北宋到他穿越前的时代大约一千年，怎么会在这个时代也有申拳？他继续问，“不知那伙金人现在何处？”
Studying the tall young man before him—fine robes, noble bearing—Shao Zhengye guessed he was no common citizen, perhaps a high Lin'an official sent after Division Commander Lin reported the Jurchen raid. Cupping his hands, he asked politely, "May I ask Young Master's name?"

“那金人带七八个人先后两次前来闹事，第二次来时，对打过程中七八人全死在我儿手中，至于那领头之人被我一刀刺伤，但其轻功了得，逃了出去，我儿带人前去追赶，追到一条巷子不见踪影。”
Seeing no need to hide, Shen Jianchen nodded to Bai Feng, who announced with pride: "Standing before you is His Majesty's thirteenth son, Prince Yi!"

“巷子在何处？”申简辰想亲自前去查探一番，金人受伤，应该不会跑太远，估计正躲起来疗伤。
Neither father nor son had ever dreamed Military Commissioner Prince Yi himself would step through their gate—let alone pack martial skill like that! Thrilled and awed, both dropped to their knees. "Ignorant of Your Highness's identity, we gave grave offense—please pardon us!"

邵正邺说：“在离蔣爷的赌坊不远处的迷 魂巷。听名字，仪王便知此巷进得去不容易出来，尤其是天黑时，躲个把人还真不好找，那金人误打误撞逃进巷内，又是晚上，我儿寻不得，只能放弃。”
"Sparring with your men stretched my stiff limbs—no offense taken. Rise and tell me about those Jurchens."

“迷 魂巷可住人？”
Rising at once, Shao Zhengye ushered Prince Yi into the main hall, served fine tea, and stood respectfully alongside his son.

“三教九流，什么人都有。只有长期居住在迷 魂巷的人才能摸得清楚方向。”
Once Prince Yi sipped, father and son traded a quick look before Shao Zhengye answered, "We do not know those Jurchens' true backing either—only that they were no ordinary traders. Likely they got wind of our Shao family's anti-Jurchen sentiment and a few Jurchens we killed in the past, and came for revenge."

邵志泽插嘴道：“那天晚上，我带人追赶金人，进入迷 魂巷，寻找一会儿没有结果，想原路返回，结果也迷了路，还是敲开一户人家的门，塞些银子，才被带出来。”
At the words "anti-Jurchen," Shen Jianchen's eyes lit up. "Patriots after my own heart—consider yourself my friend!"

申简辰对迷魂巷产生兴趣，打算亲自走一趟。刚才邵正邺提到蔣爷，他想起白天抢亲时，陈浩宇说他娶朱淑真是被蔣爷所逼。申简辰想不通陈家的婚礼与蔣爷有何关系？
A royal prince calling him friend thrilled Shao Zhengye—even as guilt pricked him over telling only half the truth about a family secret best kept quiet. Bowing alongside his son, he said, "Your Highness's favor honors our entire house. One more detail I must mention..."

一日之内两次听到此人的名字，又是他逼得两家婚礼突然举行，搞得申简辰措手不及，失去所爱，必须让此人尝尝他的拳头。
"Skip the formal reporting—friends sit and talk!" Having longed to befriend bold heroes—failing to meet Mount Liang's outlaws, anti-Jurchen fighters were next best—Shen Jianchen waved him to a chair while young Shao Zhize remained standing respectfully behind his father.

“你刚提到的蔣爷是何许人？”
"Seeing Your Highness's extraordinary fist technique tonight amazed me," Shao Zhengye said carefully. "What struck me strangest is that your fist style matches the Jurchen leader who raided us almost stroke for stroke."

邵正邺说：“蔣爷是黑道中人，是钱塘帮的帮主，住在城东最偏僻之处，此人亦正亦邪。说他好吧，临安城的赌场一大半都是他开的，谁需要烧杀抢夺，只要给银子，蔣爷都敢接。说他不好吧，有时还会救济百姓。”
"Wait—really?!" Shen Jianchen froze. He was the 360th-generation heir of Shen Fist—nearly a thousand years separated the Northern Song from his own era, so how could a Jurchen here know Shen Fist?! "Where are those Jurchens now?"

申简辰才不管蔣爷是正是邪，既然影响到他的事，必然得挨打。
"He led seven or eight men raiding us twice," Shao Zhengye explained. "The second time, my son slew all seven or eight in the fight. I sliced the leader myself, yet his light-foot skill was astonishing—he escaped, and my son chased him into a maze of lanes where he vanished."

该了解的已了解，天色不早，不好继续打扰，申简辰起身：“感谢邵当家详细告知，深夜造访，甚是叨扰，改日到幽林别居与我痛饮几杯。”
"Which alley?" Shen Jianchen meant to track him down himself—wounded, the man could not have run far.

邵正邺起身相送：“能得仪王邀请，不胜荣幸，改日定当带上重礼登门造访。以后若有用得上邵家，仪王尽管言语。”
"Mihun Alley—literally 'Bewildering Alley'—not far from Master Jiang's gambling house. By the name alone, Your Highness can tell it is easy to enter and brutal to escape, especially after dark. That Jurchen stumbled inside at night and my son had to call off the hunt."

申简辰对这位新认识的朋友甚觉满意，按说与对方并不熟识，如此冒冒失失认领朋友似乎不妥，可谁让申简辰一穿越而来要么接触的是像文渊那样的才子，要么是昏君和奸臣，好不容易碰到像邵正邺这类人物，自然不管不顾认作朋友。
"People live in Mihun Alley?"

邵正邺父子将仪王送出门外，送上马车。
"Every walk of life—only longtime locals know their way through its twists."

目送对方离开后，邵志泽说：“爹，刚才没有向仪王说明实情，他以后知道肯定会怪罪我们。”
Shao Zhize chimed in, "That night I chased him inside, came up empty, and even got lost trying to back out—had to knock on a random door and bribe a resident with silver to lead us out."

邵正邺叹口气：“唉，能瞒就先瞒着，完颜池那小子暂时应该不会再来，看在你姑姑的面子上放他一马，下次若再来，我就用手中的大刀结果了他的小命！”
Making a mental note to check Mihun Alley—and catching the mention of Master Jiang! Twice in one day that underworld boss's name had crossed his path, the exact bastard who forced Chen Haoyu into rushing today's wedding and ripping Qianli Xun away! That man was definitely catching his fists. "Who is this Master Jiang you mentioned?"

话说陈家的婚宴结束后，乔文仙与一妻一妾同乘一辆马车回乔府。在外人看来，他甚是幸福，同一时间身边多出两个女人。
"Boss of the Qiantang Gang over on the far east side," Shao Zhengye explained. "Half villain, half rogue—owns over half Lin'an's gambling dens and takes dirty jobs for silver, yet sometimes hands out relief to the poor."

只有熟悉他的人清楚，不能跟喜欢的人在一起，他有多难过。新婚那晚，他难过得谁的房中都没去，搞得眼前的两个女人也难过一晚。
Rogue or saint, anyone who messed up Shen Jianchen's life was getting tuned up. With night deepening, he rose to take his leave. "Much obliged for the briefing, Master Shao. Forgive the late intrusion—drop by Youlin Retreat another day for a real drinking session!"

乔文仙打了一个酒嗝：“呃。”他喝得太多，脸色微红，双眼迷离。
"Honored beyond words, Your Highness! I will call with proper gifts soon—and if Your Highness ever needs the Shao Escort Agency, just send word."

一股浊酒之气立刻充满马车，苗知瑜掩住鼻孔；夏果则撩起马车窗帘，换换空气。
Walking out to his carriage, Shen Jianchen felt rare warmth over his blunt new friend—after months surrounded by dreamy poets, spineless emperors, and corrupt ministers, a straight-shooting martial man felt like fresh mountain air.

乔文仙借着酒劲儿打趣：“你们两个现在知道了吧，我……我没那么好，后悔还……还来得及……呃……”
Watching the carriage roll away, Shao Zhize murmured uneasily, "Father, hiding the full truth from Prince Yi—won't he be furious if he finds out later?"

又一阵浊酒之气，苗知瑜不好再掩着鼻孔，放下玉手：“文渊说的哪里话？我已嫁给你，无论你是什么样，我都不嫌弃。”
Shao Zhengye let out a heavy sigh. "Hide it while we can. That brat Wanyan Chi should stay clear for a spell—out of regard for your aunt, I let him off light. Dare show his face here again, and my blade ends his life!"

夏果撇撇嘴，脸上闪过不满之色，很快露出笑容：“不要瞎想，我跟苗姐姐怎么会嫌弃？”
Meanwhile, after the Chen wedding banquet ended, Qiao Wenxian rode back to Qiao Manor sharing a carriage with both wife and concubine—looking enviable from outside, hollow to the bone inside. On his own wedding night, grief had kept him out of both women's rooms, leaving them both sleepless till dawn.

夏果靠近乔文仙，故意将鼻子凑近他的嘴巴：“你看，我一点儿不嫌弃，要不文渊再打一个嗝儿，让我闻闻你的酒气。”
Flushed red with cloudy eyes, Qiao Wenxian let out a loud hiccup, filling the cab with stale wine fumes. Miao Zhiyu pinched her nose while Xiaguo lifted the window curtain for fresh air.

乔文仙半迷糊半清醒，虽知是夏果靠近他，面前的这张脸朦朦胧胧中像极了淑真。他的脸上露出笑容，准备伸手去触摸，结果那张脸消失在面前。
Slurring bitterly, Qiao Wenxian chuckled, "Now you two see... I am no prize... still time to regret... *hic*..."

原来苗知瑜嫌弃夏果装出娇媚的样子，没羞没臊当着她面勾引文渊，顾不上大家闺秀的风范，伸手将夏果推开。
Dropping her hand politely at his second hiccup, Miao Zhiyu replied gently, "What talk is that, Wenyuan? I am already your wife—no matter how you are, I would never look down on you."

夏果的头碰到一边，马车也因此晃了两下。碰得虽不严重，可也有些疼痛，夏果用手捂住右边的额头：“苗姐姐这是怎么了？想害我吗？”
Hiding a brief smirk, Xiaguo beamed right back. "Stop talking nonsense—Sister Miao and I could never turn up our noses at you!" Sliding straight against him, she tilted her nose right near his mouth. "See? Not disgusted one bit—hiccup again so I can smell it!"

苗知瑜坐端后，批评道：“夏果，你作为妾室，在外一举一动要懂得分寸。”
Through wine-blurred vision, Xiaguo's face pressing close shifted into Shuzhen's features! A soft smile broke over Qiao Wenxian's face as he raised a hand to touch her cheek—only for her face to vanish with a jolt!

夏果揉了揉额头，疼痛感已减弱，她放下手，不甘示弱：“我现在在乔家的马车里，不是在外面，也没外人，就我们三个，难道我还不能跟自己的夫君亲密些？”
Disgusted by Xiaguo flaunting herself shamelessly right before her eyes, Miao Zhiyu had forgotten lady-like restraint and shoved Xiaguo sideways! Xiaguo's forehead bumped the cab wall hard enough to rock the carriage. Rubbing her bruised temple, she snapped, "What is wrong with you, Sister Miao—trying to cripple me?!"

“夫君也是你叫的？”苗知瑜更是不满。
Sitting stiffly upright, Miao Zhiyu rebuked her. "As a concubine, know proper conduct outside the house!"

乔文仙醉眼紧闭，听着二人吵闹，甚觉聒噪：“别吵了！”
Rubbing away the sting, Xiaguo shot right back, "We are inside a closed Qiao carriage with zero outsiders—just the three of us! Cannot I show a little affection to my own husband?!"

夏果立刻换副腔调：“我听文渊的，不跟苗姐姐吵，苗姐姐也不要主动找我吵。”
"Since when do you have the right to call him 'husband'?!" Miao Zhiyu snapped.

苗知瑜不再言语，心中更加讨厌夏果，她那股子骚情的劲儿自己实在学不来。回想夏果在幽栖居士身边当丫鬟时，怎么都看不出她还有这等本事，只能说明人是最善变的动物。
Eyes squeezed shut against the noise, Qiao Wenxian barked, "Shut up, both of you!"

想到幽栖居士，苗知瑜想起仪王抢亲之事，不如告诉文渊，既能让他彻底对幽栖居士死心，也可恶心夏果，毕竟幽栖居士曾是她的主子。
Switching tones on a dime, Xiaguo cooed, "I listen to Wenyuan—I will not argue with Sister Miao, long as she stops picking fights with me."

苗知瑜顿了顿，用平稳的语气说：“文渊，我今日听到一件与幽栖居士有关的事，甚是惊讶，到现在都不敢相信是真的。”
Clamping her lips shut, Miao Zhiyu seethed inwardly—she could never stoop to that cheap flirtiness! Back when Xiaguo served Youqi Jushi she had looked quiet enough—how fast people showed their claws! Wait: thinking of Youqi Jushi brought back Prince Yi's raid on the wedding today! Telling Wenyuan would kill his lingering devotion to Youqi Jushi AND rub salt in Xiaguo's pride over her former mistress!

听到“幽栖居士”的称谓，乔文仙自然睁开双眼，酒意少去一些：“什么事？”
Steadying her voice, Miao Zhiyu spoke smoothly. "Wenyuan, I heard astonishing news today about Youqi Jushi—still can hardly believe it."

夏果没当回事，近期也没听说小姐身边发生什么大事，苗知瑜又能编排出什么事来。
At the name "Youqi Jushi," Qiao Wenxian's eyes snapped open, sobering half a shade. "What news?"

只听苗知瑜说：“今日陈家迎亲途中，有人前去抢亲……”
Curious yet unbothered, Xiaguo listened too.

乔文仙酒醒了一半儿：“谁？”
"During the Chen bridal procession today, someone raided the street to snatch the bride..."

夏果心中第一个想到仪王，又不敢相信，也问道：“谁？”
Sobering fast, Qiao Wenxian demanded, "Who?!"

苗知瑜从文渊的表情中看出，他根本不愿相信还有人像他一样喜欢幽栖居士。于是大胆吐出两个字：“仪王。”
A guess flashed in Xiaguo's mind before she voiced the same word: "Who?!"

乔文仙果然不信：“你胡说，仪王怎么可能去抢亲？他与佳和郡主有婚约。”
Reading his utter disbelief that any other man could love Shuzhen like he did, Miao Zhiyu dropped two crisp words: "Prince Yi."

心中的答案得到确证，夏果不再言语，仔细观察文渊。她也希望文渊知道仪王偷偷喜欢小姐后，彻底将小姐从心中清除，她才有机会得到文渊的心。
"Rubbish!" Qiao Wenxian barked at once. "How could Prince Yi snatch a bride when he is betrothed to Princess Jiahe?!"

苗知瑜继续说：“此事闹得甚大，很多人都看到，听说佳和郡主以性命相逼，受了重伤，仪王才不得不罢手，否则今日恐怕陈家的婚礼只有新郎，而没有新娘，文渊若不信，可以去幽林别居亲自问仪王，或者看看佳和郡主有没有受伤便知。”
Suspicions confirmed, Xiaguo held her tongue and watched Wenyuan close—hoping this bomb would blast her old mistress clean out of his heart so she stood a real shot!

此事如同一碗效果绝佳的醒酒汤，乔文仙完全从醉意中清醒，他半晌没有言语。苗知瑜说得有鼻子有眼，不得不令人相信。他搞不明白何时仪王对淑真动情？到底是在他与淑真分手之前还是之后，他难以确定。
"Half the street saw it!" Miao Zhiyu pressed smoothly. "Word is Princess Jiahe put her own life on the line and took a serious blade wound before Prince Yi backed down—otherwise the Chens would have had a groom with no bride! If you doubt me, ride to Youlin Retreat and ask Prince Yi yourself, or check Princess Jiahe's wound."

用余光瞅到夏果，她以前是淑真的贴身丫鬟，应该知道些什么：“夏果，你一直跟着淑真，应该最清楚不过，告诉我，什么时候仪王对淑真动了心思？”
The shock hit like a bucket of ice water, wiping every drop of wine from Qiao Wenxian's brain. Speechless, he remembered how detailed Miao Zhiyu's tale sounded—far too specific to invent! When had Prince Yi fallen for Shuzhen?! Before he and Shuzhen broke up, or after?!

事已至此，夏果没必要再瞒：“具体什么时候我不大清楚，只知那时满城都是关于小姐的流言蜚语，乔家因此不再上门提亲，小姐十分痛苦，后来与文渊你分手，前前后后仪王来过几次望月轩，有时陪佳和郡主，有时一个人来，我才发现他对小姐动了心思……”
Snapping his eyes onto Xiaguo—her former personal maid—he demanded, "You stayed by Shuzhen's side all day! Tell me straight: when did Prince Yi start chasing Shuzhen?!"

没有恶心到夏果，苗知瑜失望的同时，第一次对夏果十分满意。这人啊，只要有共同的目标，不愁说不到一起。夏果如此配合，说明她也希望文渊快些忘记幽栖居士。
Seeing no point hiding now, Xiaguo laid it out. "I do not know the exact day—only back when rumors flooded the city and the Qiaos stopped calling, leaving Miss devastated right before breaking up with you, Prince Yi visited Wangyue Pavilion several times—sometimes with Princess Jiahe, sometimes alone—and I caught on he had fallen for her..."

乔文仙本希望夏果果断否认，她却一句否认的话都没有。还说仪王是在他与家人抗争，闹着绝食的日子里喜欢上淑真，那时他还拜托仪王帮他与淑真传递消息，没想到仪王明着是传送消息，实则暗藏私心。
Disappointed she hadn't rattled Xiaguo, Miao Zhiyu nonetheless felt unexpected warmth toward the girl—shared enemies made smooth allies, and Xiaguo clearly wanted Youqi Jushi wiped out of Wenyuan's mind just as badly!

乔文仙已坐不住，对外大声喊道：“去幽林别居！”
Having prayed Xiaguo would deny it, Qiao Wenxian froze as she pinned the exact timeline to his own hunger strike—the very days he had begged Prince Yi to carry messages to Shuzhen! Playing messenger on the surface while harboring private schemes underneath?!

苗知瑜看出文渊想要找仪王问个究竟，劝道：“你找仪王问话，也不急于一时。佳和郡主刚受伤，仪王这会儿正处于忙乱中，再说眼看着太阳就要偏西，不如明日再去。”
Unable to sit still another breath, Qiao Wenxian yelled out the window, "To Youlin Retreat!"

乔文仙心里虽急，怎奈时机不对，仍需耐心等待。他打算明日再去幽林别居，找仪王当面问些事情。
Stepping in smoothly, Miao Zhiyu urged, "No need to rush tonight—Princess Jiahe just got wounded so Youlin Retreat is in chaos, and dusk is already falling. Go tomorrow morning instead."

苗知瑜见文渊已默认她的建议，对外说：“回乔府。”
Burning inside yet seeing her logic, Qiao Wenxian stayed silent. Catching his mute consent, Miao Zhiyu called out, "Back to Qiao Manor."

乔文仙靠在马车里，听着街市嘈杂的喧闹声，回想仪王帮助他与淑真的过程，心中不断追问：“到底什么时候仪王对淑真动了心思？”他着实找不出任何端倪。
