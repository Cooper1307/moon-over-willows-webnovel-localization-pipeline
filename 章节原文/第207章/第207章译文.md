## Chapter 207: A Poetry Gathering in Name at Zuiyun Tower

Hunting all day across Lin'an for clues on "Young Master Shen," Master Jiang's lieutenant came up dry until dusk led him past Chunxiang Brothel—where spotting Scarface strolling out tipped him off Chen Haoyu was inside! Stepping in past a welcoming courtesan, he found Chen Haoyu lounging below stage watching Chun Jinmo dance, and walked straight up with polite mockery.

"Pretty bold, Young Master Chen—snagged your contract and right back watching Chun Jinmo! Wait till Master Jiang hears!"

Scanning the room for other gangsters and seeing none, Chen Haoyu grinned smugly. "See my three new bodyguards? Let Master Jiang come!"

"While you party here, Master Jiang lies beaten black and blue."

Delighted that Prince Yi's guard had delivered a real thrashing, Chen Haoyu sneered, "What fools get for crossing me! Tell Master Jiang hands off Chun Jinmo!"

"Master Jiang sent me to ask one question: who IS that Young Master Shen?"

Eager to scare Master Jiang stiff and lock down Chun Jinmo for good, Chen Haoyu crooked a finger and whispered into his ear: "Prepare to drop dead—go scare the skin off Master Jiang! Young Master Shen is His Majesty's thirteenth son, Prince Yi!"

Staggered by the bombshell, the lieutenant bolted straight back to Qiantang Gang. Hearing whom he had crossed made every bruise on Master Jiang's body throb twice as hard—rule one of his gang was NEVER cross imperial power! Resolving to beg pardon the second he healed, his rage snapped straight onto Chen Haoyu: "Still lounging at Chunxiang Brothel?! Where is his fierce new bride?! Chen Haodong conned me—tricked me into running a trap for zero reward AND crossed Prince Yi! Wait till I walk again—I am tearing Chen Haodong apart!"

The next morning, Qianli Xun rode Chen Manor's carriage—driven by a coachman who knew Chen Haoyu's haunt well—out past the walls to Zuiyun Tower beside the wide Qiantang River. Stepping down to gaze across the sweeping current, she breathed in awe. "So this is the famed Qiantang River?"

"Yes, Miss—you brought me twice before during tidal bore season," Kuqiao reminded her.

Watching endless waves roll past, Confucius's classic sigh slipped from her lips: "'It passes on just like this, not ceasing day or night!'" [TN: Translated by James Legge] Shaking off melancholy to greet the shining spring water brighter, she shifted straight to Zhang Ruoxu's grand lines:

"'In spring the river rises as high as the sea,

And with the river's rise the moon uprises bright.

She follows the rolling waves for ten thousand li,

And where the river flows, there overflows her light.

The river winds around the fragrant islet...'" [TN: Translated by Xu Yuanchong]

"What grand mood, Youqi Jushi—shame it is morning with no bright moon rising to match Zhang Ruoxu!" Dongfang Chenze called out, strolling over with An Mingxuan and Cao Ban—and zero other guests in sight.

"Thought this was a poetry gathering—why only you three?" Qianli Xun blinked.

"Miss Xiangrong waits inside, and others arrive shortly," Dongfang Chenze explained smoothly. "One favor first, Youqi Jushi..."

"The steward here refuses to let us upstairs without Young Master Chen's word," Cao Ban chimed in. "Since you own Zuiyun Tower now, could you tell him?"

Shaking her head at how slapdash Dongfang Chenze's hosting was—inviting half Lin'an before unlocking the door!—she stepped inside to greet Xiangrong waiting beside her zither-bearing maid.

"Youqi Jushi! Missed you since West Lake—thrilled to join your salon today!" Xiangrong took her hands warmly.

Warming instantly to the graceful singer, Qianli Xun smiled back—just as Zuiyun Tower's steward marched up scolding: "Back again, Young Master Dongfang? Told you, no Young Master Chen's nod, no entry!"

Stepping up, An Mingxuan gestured toward Qianli Xun. "Look who stands right here!"

The steward blinked blankly. "Who?"

"The celebrated Youqi Jushi!"

Blank stare again. "Never heard the name."

"Miss Zhu!" An Mingxuan sighed.

Instantly the steward burst into bowing grins: "Young Madam! Why did you not send word ahead so we could roll out the welcome?!"

Irked he hadn't even heard "Youqi Jushi," Qianli Xun pressed coolly, "Just said Young Master Dongfang needs Chen Haoyu's word to enter?"

"Slip of the tongue from old habit! Master already sent word yesterday that Young Madam owns Zuiyun Tower now—of course Young Master Dongfang is welcome!"

Softened by his swift pivot, Qianli Xun still laid down the law: "Remember—without my say-so, Chen Haoyu gets zero entry here; catch you fawning over him and I find a new steward. Meanwhile my friends here are welcome anytime—clear?"

"Crystal clear, Young Madam! Right upstairs, everyone!"

Upstairs, Dongfang Chenze claimed the exact river-view suite from last time—not only framing sweeping river views, yet boasting an adjoining quiet side chamber with its own separate outer corridor door, tailor-made for staging two secret reunions cleanly!

Settling onto floor mats, Qianli Xun counted only eight low tables. "Sure this room fits a big crowd?"

"Truth be told," Dongfang Chenze fibbed smoothly, "my salon lacks prestige, so most guests flaked out at the last minute—leaving just us, plus Brother Qiao on his way."

"Fair enough. Whose verses are we enjoying today?"

"Yours, of course!" Cao Ban jumped in. "With all the lyrics you gifted him, Brother Dongfang has been busy composing melodies—already half done—and invited Miss Xiangrong to sing them live!"

Fresh and inventive—Qianli Xun smiled unsuspectingly as the steward served house-brewed pomegranate sweet wine and a master chef rolled in piping-hot pastries straight from the oven, introducing himself as a veteran poached from Bianliang's famed Fanlou at double wages ("Plenty of pay, light hours, and I still catered your whole wedding banquet!"). Leaving lunch orders to Dongfang Chenze's usual favorites, staff bowed out.

After everyone raised a first cup, Xiangrong took her seat at the zither, fingers gliding across silk strings as her clear voice rose in *"Jiangchengzi: Admiring the Spring"*: *"Slanting winds and fine rain bring a spring chill. / Before the wine cup, / I recall old joys. / Once beside the pear blossoms, / Lonely tears streamed down..."*

Right on that line, the door pushed open—Qiao Wenxian arriving right on his assigned cue. Smiling without interrupting the song, the three scholars cupped hands as Qiao Wenxian brightened seeing Shuzhen and sat right beside her. Feeling a faint flutter of awkwardness despite faded feelings, Qianli Xun raised her cup with a polite smile; pouring his own, Qiao Wenxian tapped rims lightly and drained it clean, Qianli Xun savoring the sweet pomegranate wine before turning back to Xiangrong's voice.

Leaning close, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "Shuzhen, may I borrow a private word?"

Meeting his eyes—still brimming with the same deep ache as before—a pang shook Qianli Xun. How could he ever build a new life if he refused to let go?

"Just in there," he nodded toward the side chamber, where An Mingxuan already held the connecting door open.

A flash of suspicion hit her—was this whole gathering staged to get her alone with Wenyuan?! Right as she wavered, Xiangrong sang the haunting closing lines: *"Tossing and turning beneath the quilt in vain regret— / Heaven is easy to see, / Yet seeing that one is hard."* When Qianli Xun had first written out Zhu Shuzhen's lyric, that word "yi" (伊, meaning "that one") had meant Shen Jianchen—and even today, it meant Shen Jianchen still. With a clear conscience and a chance to help Wenyuan close the door cleanly, she stood boldly and stepped into the side chamber with Qiao Wenxian behind her—leaving Kuqiao and Nan Shuang locked outside by An Mingxuan, fretting silently for their masters.
