第278章 陈家人各自散去
## Chapter 278: The Chens Disperse

仪王刚一走，姚轻琰再也忍不住，上前拉住夫君的手臂：“江林，这可怎么办？浩宇被带到司理院还不得被关在临时的牢房里？我听说里面环境艰苦，他可怎么受得了？”
陈江林安慰道：“不必担心，刚才仪王审案的过程，我仔细分析，浩宇多半没做傻事，放心，很快会回来。”
姚轻琰对丈夫十分信任，点点头表示认可。
陈浩东装作关心的样子走过来：“大伯，大婶子，过会儿我去趟司理院给浩宇送些吃的用的，再给看管牢狱的人塞些银子，请他们别为难浩宇。”
姚轻琰感激道：“还是浩东想得周到，一切拜托你，记得给浩宇带上疗伤药。”
“一家人不说两家话，大婶子放心，我屋里有好药，都给他带上。闲话不多说，这就去准备。”陈浩东拜别各位长辈，装作去忙碌的样子。
今日发生的事夏老太太自责不已，怪她平日太溺爱浩宇，才惹来麻烦事，她狠狠心，不想说一句担忧的话：“他长这么大没吃过几日苦，趁此机会也尝尝人生的苦头。都散了吧，各回各屋。”
姚轻琰去扶老太太，与两位妯娌一起送老太太回屋。
厅内剩下三位老爷，千里寻上前行礼：“公爹，二叔，三叔，淑真先行一步。”
千里寻行完礼刚要走，被陈江林叫住：“淑真。”
千里寻站住：“公爹有何吩咐？”
陈江林想替自己的儿子说几句，话到嘴边又不知该如何张口，毕竟浩宇做下的事没几个大家闺秀能容忍，何况还是心不甘情不愿嫁过来的江南女词人。
The second Prince Yi stepped out, Yao Qingyan grabbed her husband’s arm. “What do we do, Jianglin? Won’t Haoyu get locked in a holding cell at the Judicial Office? I hear those cells are awful—how will he stand it?”

“没……没什么，你去吧。”
“Don’t fret,” Chen Jianglin consoled her. “Listening to the questioning just now, I’m almost certain Haoyu didn’t do it. He’ll be back soon.”

千里寻这才真正转身离开。
Trusting her husband’s judgment, Yao Qingyan nodded.

陈江林对两位兄弟说：“我们也散了吧。”
Chen Haodong stepped up wearing a worried face. “Uncle, Aunt, I’ll head over to the Judicial Office in a bit with food and bedding for Haoyu, and slip the jailers some silver so they treat him right.”

陈江木并不走，一脸着急地说：“大哥，容我说两句，此事交给官府出面解决甚为不妥，此乃家事，官府一出面会搞得人尽皆知。”
“How thoughtful of you, Haodong,” Yao Qingyan said in gratitude. “Take some good wound medicine for his arm too.”

陈江林叹道：“唉，母亲如此安排，我也无法，只求浩宇根本没做那些事。”
“We’re family, Aunt—no need for thanks. I’ve got fine salve in my room and I’ll pack the lot. Let me go get it ready right now.” Bowing to the elders, he hurried off playing the dutiful cousin.

陈江木继续说：“恐怕悬，我与二哥早在外听人议论，浩宇喜欢抢些黄花大姑娘，有些放在陈府外面的宅子里当丫鬟，有些则卖到青楼。”
Blaming herself for spoiling Haoyu rotten, Old Madam Xia hardened her heart and held back every word of worry. “He’s never tasted real hardship in his life—high time he gets a bite of it! Disperse, everyone, back to your own rooms.”

陈江林质问道：“你们为何不早与我说？”
Yao Qingyan and her two sisters-in-law escorted the Old Madam back to her quarters.

陈江树这才说道：“老太太与大嫂护着，浩宇又会哄人，说了只会让大哥为难。”
Left in the hall with the three Masters, Qianli Xun curtsied. “Father-in-law, Second Uncle, Third Uncle, I’ll excuse myself.”

陈江林快要站立不住，陈江木赶紧扶他坐到椅子上。
As she turned, Chen Jianglin called out, “Shuzhen.”

陈江林又叹口气：“唉！如此看来，那姑娘多半儿是被浩宇糟蹋后卖到青楼，这该如何是好？”
She stopped. “Yes, Father-in-law?”

陈江木说：“大哥需按照最坏的结果想个解决的办法。”
He wanted to plead on his son’s behalf, yet the words stuck in his throat. What respectable lady could stomach Haoyu’s past—let alone the famous Poetess of Jiangnan who had been reluctant to marry him in the first place? “Never mind. Go rest.”

“最好的办法就是让浩宇纳那女子为妾，一切不就解决了？”陈江树为他的建议顺利提出感到高兴，浩宇娶的娘子名声不好，再纳个青楼女子为妾，更会拉低他的档次，想要竞争陈家产业继承人，便一点儿希望都没有。
Qianli Xun turned and left.

陈江林一口回绝：“万万不可！陈家的男子从未有纳妾一说，何况她还是个青楼女子。”
Chen Jianglin told his brothers, “Let’s call it a day too.”

陈江木愁道：“不如此解决，只怕司理院查出来真的是浩宇所为，恐怕得一个强奸的罪名，在我朝可是死罪。”
Chen Jiangmu stayed put, brow creased. “Hear me out, Eldest Brother. Handing a family scandal to the yamen was a mistake—now the whole city will know.”

陈江林从没像现在这样为难过，他狠狠心：“浩宇是我的儿子，他再不济，我也要相信他。”
“Mother made the call,” Chen Jianglin sighed. “All I can hope is Haoyu really is innocent.”

“哼！只怕到时浩宇真的没命，哭都来不及。浩宇若判了死罪，更是为我儿扫清障碍。呵呵，我才不相信这浪荡子什么都没做。”陈江树心中如此想，口中却说：“既然大哥相信浩宇，我也和大哥一样信他一回，浩宇毕竟是我陈家的儿郎，怎么可能做出此等龌龊之事？”
“I wouldn’t bet on it,” Chen Jiangmu said. “Second Brother and I heard talk outside ages ago—Haoyu used to snatch unwed girls, stashing some in outside residences as maids and selling others to brothels.”

二哥都顺着大哥，陈江木也不再坚持：“既如此，我也相信浩宇。”
Chen Jianglin whipped around. “Why didn’t you tell me sooner?”

千里寻拜别三位长辈后，向墨香苑走去，天色已渐渐暗下，却没有她的心情暗淡，暗淡的心情使得脚步比往日缓慢许多。
Chen Jiangshu chimed in, “With Mother and Sister-in-law shielding him, and Haoyu so slick with his tongue, telling you would only have put you in a bind.”

老天总是喜欢跟她开玩笑。当她以为必然会嫁给文渊时，跟她开了一个大大的玩笑；现在她屈服现实想要跟陈浩宇培养感情，刚刚有些感觉，连续两日发生的事，又像是新的玩笑。
Chen Jianglin’s knees buckled; Chen Jiangmu caught him and eased him into a chair.

她真的不知该如何做才符合老天的心意，也许老天根本不愿她的情感生活太过顺遂，总是制造些事端，给她来个措手不及。
Chen Jianglin let out a heavy sigh. “Then he likely really did ruin that girl and sell her off. What on earth do we do?”

“少夫人，我刚到大门口时，见公子被司理院的人带走，到底发生了什么？”
“Plan for the worst, Eldest Brother,” Chen Jiangmu urged.

耳边传来青瓜的声音，千里寻抬起头，发现自己已到墨香苑门口：“哦，青瓜啊，你什么时候回来的？”
“Best fix is simple—let Haoyu take her as a concubine and end the whole thing!” Chen Jiangshu was thrilled to slip that in: between a wife with a battered reputation and a brothel concubine, Haoyu’s standing would crater, knocking him out of the inheritance race for good.

少夫人答非所问，一脸失落，看样子家中发生了大事：“少夫人，您放心，我这就去司理院，让他们把我和公子关在一处，也好有人伺候他。”
Chen Jianglin rejected it flat-out. “Never! No Chen man has ever taken a concubine—much less a brothel woman!”

千里寻一边向里走，一边有气无力地说：“随你。”
“If we don’t settle it,” Chen Jiangmu fretted, “and the Judicial Office proves Haoyu did it, rape is a capital offense under Song law!”

青瓜顾不上许多，小跑着出了陈府，又一路跑向司理院。
Torn as never before, Chen Jianglin steeled himself. “Flawed as he is, Haoyu is my son. I choose to believe him.”

千里寻走进院子，一阵风送来小池中的气味，有池水的，还有荷叶的，清新中掺杂着淤泥的味道。
*Wait till he loses his head—then you’ll cry too late!* Chen Jiangshu thought with glee, keeping a pious face. “If Eldest Brother trusts Haoyu, I’ll trust him too. He’s a Chen lad—how could he do anything so vile?”

池塘里的荷叶已长得老大，许多花骨朵从水底正缓缓向上生长，每一朵都饱含着旺盛的生命力。千里寻根本没有力气去瞧，她懒懒地走向素心草堂，走进屋，直奔闺房，趴在床上。
Seeing Second Brother fall in line, Chen Jiangmu dropped it. “Then I’ll trust him too.”

她没有哭，她连痛苦都没有，又如何会哭？她只觉得累，想好好躺一躺，养精蓄锐。养足精神后，再与生活搏上一回。
Heading back to Moxiang Courtyard, Qianli Xun walked through gathering twilight that felt brighter than her mood, her steps dragging slow.

趴了没多久，苦荞走进来。
Heaven loved jerking her around. Just when she thought she’d marry Wenyuan, the rug got pulled out. Now she had bowed to reality and started warming up to Chen Haoyu—only for two days of chaos to hit like a fresh cosmic joke. Maybe Heaven just couldn’t stand seeing her love life run smooth.

“小姐想睡觉，我帮您宽了衣裳，舒舒服服躺下睡。”
“Young Madam! I saw the Judicial Office hauling Young Master away at the gate—what happened?”

小姐没有回应，趴在床上，纹丝未动。
Qinggua’s voice made her look up; she had already reached Moxiang Courtyard’s gate. “Oh, Qinggua. When did you get back?”

“我去给小姐做盏茶。”
Seeing her hollow stare, he knew disaster had struck. “Don’t worry, Young Madam—I’m running straight to the Judicial Office to ask them to lock me in with Young Master so he has someone to wait on him!”

苦荞拐到外厅在茶桌前开始做茶……
“Suit yourself,” she murmured, dragging herself inside. Qinggua sprinted off out of the manor toward the Judicial Office.

一盏茶做好，小姐依然趴在床上，从姿态上判断，她根本未动。
A breeze drifted off the courtyard pond, carrying crisp water, broad lotus leaves, and a whiff of silt. Fat lotus buds pushed upward from the dark water, brimming with life. Too drained to look, Qianli Xun shuffled into Suxin Thatched Hall, crossed to her bedchamber, and flopped face-down onto the bed.

“我已做好茶，小姐要不要品品？”
No tears came—she didn’t even hurt enough to cry. She was just bone-tired and needed to lie still until she gathered strength for the next round with life.

小姐依然未动。
Moments later Kuqiao stepped in. “If you’re sleepy, Miss, let me help you out of your outer robe so you can rest comfortable.”

苦荞端着茶盏有的没的聊起来：“您猜猜今日白风叫我出去见了谁？”
Not a twitch from the bed.

没有任何回应，苦荞自问自答：“是苦蕨，他现在帮仪王建竹屋，来城里向仪王汇报情况，顺便看看我。白风从今日起也跟着苦蕨去竹林忙活，能见到我爹娘他很开心。不过，白风很过分，现在就让苦蕨唤他姐夫。”
“I’ll go whisk you a bowl of tea.”

小姐依然没有回应，也对，老讲自己的事，小姐肯定不感兴趣，该说些什么她才会很快回应？苦荞搜刮肚肠，终于找到一个话题。
Kuqiao went to the outer room and whisked tea. Bringing it in, she saw her miss hadn’t moved an inch. “Tea’s ready, Miss. Care for a sip?”

“小姐，有件事不知道该不该告诉您？是白风今日告诉我的。”苦荞知道小姐不会理她，直接说道，“白风说仪王与仪王妃在汴梁成亲时并未圆房，回到临安后，乔公子拜访过一次仪王，悉心劝说，他才与仪王妃圆了房。”
Still dead silent.

小姐的身体果然动了一下，苦荞为自己找的话题触动小姐而高兴，否则她就这么趴着，令她担心不已。
Holding the bowl, Kuqiao rattled on at random. “Guess who Bai Feng brought for me to see today?” No answer, so she answered herself. “Kujue! He came into town to update Prince Yi on the bamboo cottage and dropped by to see me. Starting today, Bai Feng’s heading out to the bamboo grove with Kujue to help build it—he’s thrilled to meet my parents. The nerve of him, though, making Kujue call him brother-in-law already!”

只见小姐缓缓爬起来，坐在床边，整个人看上去无精打采。
Still nothing. Of course—babbling about herself wouldn’t budge her miss. Racking her brains, Kuqiao landed on one surefire topic.

苦荞将茶递到跟前：“温度刚刚合适。”
“Miss, I don’t know if I should tell you this—Bai Feng told me today.” Knowing she wouldn’t answer, Kuqiao forged straight ahead. “Bai Feng said Prince Yi and Princess Consort Yi never consummated their marriage in Bianliang. Only after they came back to Lin’an and Young Master Qiao paid Prince Yi a visit and talked him round did he consummate it with her.”

千里寻接过，懒懒地饮下，将盏杯递给苦荞：“刚才的话给我说说也就罢了，不要到处乱说，毕竟是贵人的私事。”
A slight shift rippled through her miss’s shoulders. Glad she had broken the freeze, Kuqiao watched her sit up on the edge of the bed, looking washed-out.

“小姐放心，我有分寸，刚才是担心您不得已说出。”
Kuqiao offered the bowl. “Just the right temperature.”

“我没事，只是心累，想多趴会儿。”
Qianli Xun drank it down in silence and handed the bowl back. “Keep that between us. Don’t go repeating royals’ private business.”

从外厅传来韦嬷嬷的声音：“姑娘可在里边？”
“I know my place, Miss—I only spoke up because I was worried about you.”

苦荞帮着向外说：“在！嬷嬷里边说话。”
“I’m fine. Just weary.”

韦嬷嬷只身一人走进里屋，见姑娘有气无力的样子，猜到是为今日之事。
Matron Wei’s voice carried from the outer hall. “Miss in there?”

苦荞端把椅子放在小姐对面，韦嬷嬷坐下后，笑道：“又没发生什么大事，姑娘怎么跟霜打的茄子似的？”
“Right here, Matron—come on in!” Kuqiao called back.

再难的事从韦嬷嬷口中说出总是让人感到轻松，千里寻已习惯，敏感的神经也变得麻木，还是努力回应：“确实不是什么大事，可我就是觉得累。”
Matron Wei entered alone, took one look at her wilted miss, and guessed why. Taking the chair Kuqiao set out, she chuckled. “Nothing major even happened—why are you drooping like a frostbitten eggplant?”

韦嬷嬷先向苦荞吩咐：“你去外面瞧着，别让他人进来，我与姑娘有话说。”
Even disasters sounded light out of Matron Wei’s mouth. Qianli Xun managed a weak reply. “Nothing major, sure—I’m just tired.”

苦荞明白，韦嬷嬷要说的话不想让自己听去，便知趣地离开，守在外面。
Matron Wei turned to Kuqiao. “Keep watch outside and don’t let anyone in. I need a private word with Miss.”

“嬷嬷要与我说什么？怎么还把苦荞支走？”
Taking the hint, Kuqiao slipped outside to stand guard.

韦嬷嬷小声说：“我前几日约朋友去永泰茶楼品茶，姑娘猜我在那里见到谁？”
“What’s so secret you sent Kuqiao out, Matron?”

“难道是陈浩宇？他去永泰茶楼学做茶，嬷嬷碰见他再正常不过。”
Matron Wei dropped her voice. “A few days ago I met a friend for tea at Yongtai Teahouse. Guess who I spotted?”

“是陈家大公子，还有今日来闹事的那位。”
“Chen Haoyu? He’s learning tea-making there, so no surprise.”

韦嬷嬷的话果真管用，千里寻因不解变得精神一些：“什么意思？”
“Eldest Young Master Chen—together with the woman who raised hell here today.”

“若各自去永泰茶楼用茶倒也罢了，他们在同一个雅间用茶。”
Qianli Xun perked up at once. “Wait—what?”

“嬷嬷怎么知道？”
“Going to Yongtai Teahouse separately would be nothing, yet they were sharing the same private booth.”

“我的朋友去二楼的雅间见她的一位朋友，亲眼瞧见一位姑娘进了大公子的雅间，还说那穿戴一看就是春香楼的姑娘。后来那姑娘从楼上下来，我有意多瞧几眼，果真姿态与春金巧不相上下。今日回府时，无意中瞧见司理院的人押的正是她。”
“How do you know?”

“她既和堂兄认识，为何要来陈家闹？”
“My friend went upstairs to meet an acquaintance and saw a woman slip into Eldest Young Master’s booth—dressed head to toe like a Chunxiang Brothel girl. When the woman came downstairs later, I took a good look—same airs as Chun Jinqiao. Just now coming back to the manor, I saw the Judicial Office constables escorting that exact woman out.”

“姑娘心善，不会往坏处想，依我看这女子来陈家闹与大公子息息相关，今日之事应该另有隐情。”
“If she knows our elder cousin, why come here raising hell?”

韦嬷嬷一番说辞令千里寻精神许多，她想了一会儿，仍旧不满：“即使与堂兄有关，也是陈浩宇有错在先，他若不干坏事，别人怎么可能找他的错处？我不会因此轻易原谅他。”
“You’ve got too good a heart to think ugly thoughts, Miss. If you ask me, her showing up today has Eldest Young Master’s fingerprints all over it—there’s more to this than meets the eye.”

“姑爷以前的确荒唐，贵在知错能改，还是要给他机会。以前他干过的坏事报应现前，也是好事，就当是帮着姑爷弥补错误，等一桩桩一件件错事弥补后，姑娘定有好日子过。”
The news revived Qianli Xun’s spirits, though her irritation lingered. “Even if Cousin is behind it, Chen Haoyu still started it! If he hadn’t pulled sleazy stunts, nobody could dig up dirt on him. I’m not letting him off easy.”

“都是他犯的错，凭什么我要帮他弥补？以前在林家嬷嬷从来站在我这边，为何到陈家竟站在陈浩宇那边替他讲话？”
“Young Master was foolish before, yes, yet owning up and changing counts for plenty. Having his old misdeeds come home to roost now is actually a blessing—think of it as helping him square his accounts. Once every old debt is settled, good days lie ahead.”

“我一直站在姑娘这边，只会选择对姑娘好的。姑娘陪同姑爷一起弥补过错，才更像一家人，才能与姑爷白头偕老。”
“His mistakes—why should I help him clean them up? Back at Lin Manor you always sided with me! Why are you sticking up for Chen Haoyu now?”

千里寻第一次不认同韦嬷嬷所说：“世上那么多夫妻，我从未见过一方要帮另一方弥补过错才能白头偕老。就拿我婆婆来说，她可曾帮公爹弥补过错？他们还不是恩恩爱爱，自然会白头偕老？”
“I’m always on your side, Miss—I only back what’s good for you. Helping Young Master clear his past makes the two of you real partners who can grow old together.”

韦嬷嬷变得一脸郑重：“每个人走的路不同，姑娘走上的路决定你必须陪同姑爷一起弥补过错。”
For the first time, Qianli Xun pushed back. “Out of all the couples in the world, when did one spouse have to mop up the other’s sins just to grow old together? Look at my mother-in-law—did she ever have to clean up Father-in-law’s messes?”

千里寻被说得哑口无言。
Matron Wei’s face turned grave. “Every person walks a different road, Miss. The road you chose demands you walk beside Young Master while he makes amends.”

这条路是她委屈自己勇敢踏上的，一路上遇到的沟沟坎坎也是她自己的选择。每个人都要为自己的选择付出代价，她千里寻也无法避免。既如此，还需勇敢面对令她讨厌的人和事。
Qianli Xun had no comeback. She had chosen this road with open eyes, bumps and all. Everyone paid the toll for their own choices—so she might as well face the ugly messes head-on.

“好吧，我听嬷嬷的，陪同他一起弥补过错。”
“Fine, Matron. I’ll help him clear his debts.”

韦嬷嬷露出笑容：“其实姑娘正在帮姑爷弥补过错。听说昨日你帮着解决了六名丫鬟，此事处理得甚好。”
Matron Wei smiled. “You’ve already started, Miss. Heard you sorted out those six maids yesterday—clean work, too.”

“奶奶和我公婆都不知，嬷嬷怎么知道？”
“Wait—Grandmother and my in-laws didn’t know! How did you hear?”

“姑娘一向聪明，怎么变得糊涂起来？那么多人在陈家门口闹事，长辈们会不知？只因姑娘解决得好，无需长辈出面，他们也乐得自在，既解决了何须再提此事？”
“Smart as you are, how’d you turn dense all of a sudden? A mob making a scene at the front gate, and you think the elders wouldn’t hear? You handled it so well they didn’t have to lift a finger, so why bring it up?”

千里寻纳闷：“嫂嫂都叮嘱王管家和下人不要乱说，他们怎么会知道？”
“Sister-in-law warned Butler Wang and the servants not to breathe a word!”

“陆爷可是当事人，被你怼得毫无还口之力，还不得在长辈面前编排你？顺带也把门口的事说了。”
“Granduncle Lu was right there, and you shut him down flat—think he wouldn’t go gripe to the elders and spill the whole gate scene?”

千里寻更是疑惑：“那时嬷嬷不在陈府，怎么知道得这么清楚？”
“You weren’t even in the manor then! How do you know every detail?”

韦嬷嬷不愿瞒着姑娘，老实交代：“我跟着姑娘来陈府也有些时日，在陈府认识不少下人，跟王管家也混熟，连兴隆阁的方管事也跟我熟识。”
Matron Wei owned up. “I’ve been at Chen Manor long enough to know plenty of the help. I’m friendly with Butler Wang—and Steward Fang over at Xinglong Pavilion too.”

千里寻故意不怀好意地说：“王管家有娘子，不合适，方管事的娘子没了，单身一枚，嬷嬷要不要考虑一下？”
A mischievous glint lit Qianli Xun’s eye. “Butler Wang has a wife, so he’s out. Steward Fang’s a widower—single and available! Thinking it over, Matron?”

韦嬷嬷先是一愣，接着笑道：“姑娘能跟我开玩笑，说明你已无事，我也就放心了，该回去睡觉。”
Matron Wei froze a split second, then laughed. “If you’re back to cracking jokes with me, you’re fine. I’m off to bed.”

千里寻对着韦嬷嬷的背影说：“说到重点您就跑，看来心里有鬼，等着吧，明日我还要当您的面提，呵呵……！”
Qianli Xun called after her back, “Running off the second I hit a nerve? Guilty conscience! Just wait—I’m bringing it up again tomorrow! Heh!”
