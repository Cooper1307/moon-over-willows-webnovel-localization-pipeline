## Chapter 278: The Chens Disperse in Gloom

The second Prince Yi left, Yao Qingyan grabbed Chen Jianglin's arm fretting over Haoyu sleeping in a yamen cell, while Chen Haodong smoothly volunteered to deliver bedding, food, ointment, and bribe silver to the jailers. Old Madam Xia hardened her heart—"Let him taste hardship for once"—and dismissed everyone.

Left alone with his brothers, Chen Jianglin heard Chen Jiangmu warn against airing family scandal in court—and learned both younger brothers had heard rumors for ages of Haoyu snatching village girls and selling some off! Staggered into a chair, Chen Jianglin still refused Chen Jiangshu's eager pitch to force Haoyu to take a brothel concubine—Chen men never took concubines, and he chose to trust his own son.

Walking slowly back to Moxiang Courtyard at dusk, Qianli Xun felt drained to the bone. Just as she opened her heart to Haoyu, Fate dropped two bombs back-to-back. Passing Qinggua at the gate—who bolted straight for the Judicial Office to sit in jail alongside his master—she dragged herself straight onto her bed in Suxin Thatched Hall without a tear, only pure exhaustion.

Kuqiao whisked tea and chattered gently to rouse her—mentioning Kujue and Bai Feng heading to build the bamboo cottage—until one quiet news nugget finally made her mistress stir: Bai Feng had let slip that Prince Yi hadn't consummated his marriage in Bianliang until Qiao Wenxian talked him into it back in Lin'an.

Sitting up slowly to drink her tea, Qianli Xun warned Kuqiao never to repeat royal gossip—just as Matron Wei stepped in and sent Kuqiao to watch the door.

"Guess who I spotted sharing a private booth at Yongtai Teahouse a few days back?" Matron Wei murmured. "Eldest Young Master Chen Haodong—and that exact courtesan the yamen just hauled off! There's more behind today's circus than meets the eye."

Her spirit perking up, Qianli Xun still bristled: "Even if Cousin Haodong pulled strings, Haoyu started it—why should I keep mopping up his sins?"

"Because every road has its price," Matron Wei said gravely. "Standing beside your husband while he pays off his old debts is how you build a real marriage." Seeing Qianli Xun nod quietly, Matron Wei grinned and added that the elders *already* knew how deftly she'd matched off the six maids and roasted Granduncle Lu—thanks to her cozy chats with Butler Wang and Xinglong Pavilion's Steward Fang!

"Butler Wang's married," Qianli Xun teased slyly, "whereas Steward Fang is newly single—fancy a match, Matron?"

Laughing that her mistress was back to her old self, Matron Wei beat a hasty retreat to bed.
