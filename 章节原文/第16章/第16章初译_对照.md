第16章 佛堂有请老祖宗
## Chapter 16: Appealing to the Old Ancestor

以朱府的布局来看，佛堂所在之处，比朱淑真住的园子还要偏僻。
By the layout of Zhu Manor, the Buddhist Shrine sat tucked even deeper than Zhu Shuzhen's garden.

在朱景元的带领下，千里寻跟着往朱府深处走去，直到眼前出现一座小山丘，千里寻才看到，在那山丘底下有座园子，外形像是一座寺庙，园墙不高，能清楚地看到里面的房屋。
Following Zhu Jingyuan into the farthest reaches of the estate, Qianli Xun spotted a small hillock ahead—and nestled at its base, a walled compound shaped like a temple, low enough that the rooftops showed clear above.

“哥哥，朱府怎么把山都给搬了进来？”
"Brother, did Zhu Manor haul an actual hill inside the walls?"

“那不是山，只是个大土堆而已，当年建造朱府时，为了搞这么一个像小山一样的大土堆，可是从临安城外拉了不少土进来。”
"Not a real hill—just a massive earth mound. When they built the manor, they hauled tons of soil in from outside Lin'an just to raise that mound."

千里寻又看到一条清澈见底的小河流，河面很窄，水并不深，水里放着几块石头，需要穿过这条小河才能到达佛堂。
Next came a clear, shallow stream spanned by stepping-stones leading straight toward the shrine.

三人踩着石头穿过小河，是一段石子甬路，走上去脚底硌硌的，像是在做足底按摩。当石子甬路走尽后，穿过野草地，才算真正到达佛堂跟前。门紧闭着，上面有一个小小的匾额，写着“佛堂”二字，里面连续传出敲击木鱼的声音。
Hopping across the stones, the three stepped onto a pebble path that poked through their soles like a foot massage, then crossed a patch of wild grass before reaching the shrine gate. Closed tight beneath a modest plaque reading "Buddhist Shrine," it vibrated with the steady clack of a wooden fish inside.

朱景元说：“淑真，你在这里等着，我前去敲门。”
"Wait here, Shuzhen—I'll knock," Zhu Jingyuan said.

千里寻点点头，看着哥哥走到跟前，将那木门敲了三下，等了一会儿，并没有人来开门。又敲了三下，又等了一会儿，还是没有人开门。继续敲了三下，终于听到里面有脚步声传来，三人一阵高兴。
Qianli Xun nodded as her brother stepped up and rapped three times. Silence. Three more knocks—still nothing. On the third round of knocks, footsteps sounded inside, lifting all three spirits.

里面有拔门栓的声音，接着“吱扭”一声门被打开，一位头发花白的老妇人出现在眼前，问道：“你们是？”
A bolt slid back and the wooden door creaked open to reveal a silver-haired elderly woman. "Who are you?"

苦荞赶紧上前有礼道：“嬷嬷好，这位是朱府大公子，这位是小姐，前来拜见老祖宗。”
Kuqiao stepped forward with a polite bow. "Good day, Matron. This is Eldest Young Master and Miss from the manor, come to pay respects to the Old Ancestor."

嬷嬷说：“原来是府中的公子和小姐，你们这些小辈可能还不知道，老祖宗一心向佛，从不见俗世之人，还是走吧。”说完就要关门。
"Ah, the young master and miss," the matron said. "Being young, you may not know—the Old Ancestor devotes herself solely to the Buddha and never sees worldly guests. Please go back." She started shutting the door.

朱景元阻拦道：“嬷嬷，这些晚辈都知道，本不该来打扰老祖宗清净，只因舍妹遇到人生难题，需要面见老祖宗，还请行个方便。”
Zhu Jingyuan wedged a hand up to stop her. "We know well, Matron, and wouldn't dare disturb her peace lightly—yet my sister faces a crisis of a lifetime and begs an audience. Please grant us this favor."

嬷嬷说：“老祖宗脱离世俗，六根清净，岂可涉足红尘之事，你们听话，还是离去吧。”
"The Old Ancestor has severed all worldly ties and purified her senses—how could she meddle in mortal affairs? Be good and leave."

这是唯一的机会，如果请不到一位长辈出面，和离只能失败。哪怕请一位，成功的几率也会多一些，只要有一线希望，千里寻都会牢牢抓住。于是大声说：“老祖宗修习佛法，难道不明白佛菩萨普度众生的夙愿吗？现在众生之一，您的不知道第几代的孙女有难，需要您这位菩萨出来救赎，可您却躲在佛堂里只顾一个人清修，这算哪门子修习佛法？”
This was her last shot—no elder, no divorce. Even one ally improved her odds, and Qianli Xun refused to let go of a single thread of hope. Raising her voice, she called straight over the matron's shoulder: "The Old Ancestor cultivates the Buddha's Dharma—doesn't she know the bodhisattvas vow to deliver all sentient beings from suffering? Right now one of those beings—her own descendant however many generations down—is in dire trouble and needs a bodhisattva to step out and save her! Hiding inside a shrine chasing private peace—what kind of Buddhist practice is that?!"

嬷嬷批评道：“你这小辈，太不知礼，怎能随意批评老祖宗？”
The matron bristled. "Impertinent child! How dare you lecture the Old Ancestor!"

里面的木鱼声戛然而止，传出一个苍老而有力的声音：“秀珍，让她进来吧。”
Inside, the wooden fish stopped dead. An aged yet resonant voice rang out: "Xiuzhen, let her in."

三人一阵欣喜，正要一起进入，被秀珍嬷嬷挡住：“没听老祖宗说只让她一人进去？你们两个在外面侯着。”
Delighted, all three moved forward, only for Matron Xiuzhen to bar the doorway. "Didn't you hear the Old Ancestor say her alone? You two wait outside."

朱景元和苦荞只好收住脚步，在外面等候。
Zhu Jingyuan and Kuqiao halted outside the gate.

千里寻兴奋之余跟着秀珍嬷嬷进入。
Heart racing, Qianli Xun followed Matron Xiuzhen in.

佛堂的小院不大，简单而整洁，最吸引人的是三株腊梅，早已过花季，枝叶倒是繁茂。
The tiny courtyard lay spare and spotless, its centerpiece three wintersweet trees—long past bloom, yet thick with lush green leaves.

走进佛堂的正屋，迎面最显眼的地方摆着一尊高大的佛像，看了不由让人肃然起敬。佛像前面的供桌上有个香炉，里面插着三炷香，正在燃烧，供桌前放着一个蒲团。
Inside the main shrine hall towered a grand statue of the Buddha that inspired instant awe. Three incense sticks smoldered in a censer on the altar table before it, with a woven prayer cushion laid on the floor.

秀珍嬷嬷说：“姑娘既然进来，需先拜拜。”
"Since you've entered, young lady, pay respects first," Matron Xiuzhen instructed.

千里寻乖乖照做，谁让她有求而来。她走上前，双手合十，跪在蒲团上，拜了三拜，心想：“老祖宗还没见到，倒先拜起佛像来。”
With a favor to beg, Qianli Xun obeyed without fuss—palms pressed together, kneeling on the cushion for three bows, thinking: Haven't even met the Old Ancestor yet, already bowing to Buddha!

拜完后起身，还没来得及瞧四周，听到秀珍嬷嬷的声音：“老祖宗，朱家的小辈进来了，就由您老教训教训她。”
Rising, before she could take in the rest of the hall, she heard Matron Xiuzhen announce: "Old Ancestor, the young Zhu descendant is here—please give her a good scolding."

千里寻扭头看去，在佛像的左边放着一张几案，上面放着木鱼和犍稚，几案旁坐着一位头发全白的老人，眼窝深陷，鼻梁高挺，嘴巴周围充满褶皱，可以说整张脸都充满褶皱。褶皱以及零散的老年斑也无法遮住皮肤散发出的白光，一看便知年轻时绝对是位皮肤白皙的美人。千里寻觉得这张脸虽苍老，却很慈祥，只要看那么一眼，心里便会产生奇妙的温暖的感觉。
Qianli Xun turned. Left of the altar sat a low table holding a wooden fish and mallet, and behind it sat a completely white-haired elder—deep-set eyes, high nose bridge, face etched top to bottom with wrinkles. Yet neither wrinkles nor scattered age spots dimmed the luminous pallor of her skin; she must have been a radiant beauty in her youth. Aged as that face was, kindness radiated from it, wrapping Qianli Xun in an unexpected glow of warmth at first sight.

老祖宗微闭双目，双腿盘坐，手里拿着佛珠用心盘着，嘴里念念有词。听到秀珍的话后，张嘴说：“让她过来。”
Eyes half-closed, legs crossed, the Old Ancestor rolled prayer beads through her fingers while murmuring sutras. Hearing Xiuzhen, she spoke: "Have her come over."

千里寻知道叫自己，走上前。
Knowing she was summoned, Qianli Xun stepped forward.

秀珍嬷嬷向她示意坐在蒲团上，她虽不习惯，还是听话地坐下。
Matron Xiuzhen gestured toward a prayer cushion; awkward as sitting cross-legged felt, Qianli Xun lowered herself obediently.

老祖宗依然盘着佛珠，双眼微闭：“听你刚才说话有些见识，所以请你进来，不知你找我所为何事？”
Still rolling her beads with eyes nearly shut, the Old Ancestor said, "Your speech outside showed some spirit, which is why I invited you in. Tell me why you came."

千里寻迅速在心里组织开场白，终于找到合适的：“老祖宗是女人，应该最能体会女人的不易，孙儿因父母之命媒妁之言嫁给才德无法与孙儿相匹配的丈夫，这些倒没什么，如果他人好，日子还可以过下去，可他打孙儿，还把商妓往家里带，孙儿实在无法跟他过下去，便想到和离。可父亲不同意替我出面，只要父亲不同意，一家的长辈，包括族中的长辈都不会替我出面，孙儿无路可走，只能来求老祖宗。求老祖宗可怜我，替我出面主持和离之事。”
Qianli Xun assembled her pitch fast and plunged in: "As a woman yourself, Old Ancestor, you know best how hard women's lives are. Obeying parents' orders and matchmakers' words, I married a husband unmatched in mind or character. I could have endured even that if he had a decent heart—instead, he beats me and brings brothel courtesans into the house! Unable to bear another day, I sought mutual divorce. Yet Father refuses to step in, and without his nod, not a single elder in the family or clan dares back me. With nowhere left to turn, I came to beg your mercy—please step forward and preside over my mutual divorce."

老祖宗终于抬眼，那双眼睛周围的皮肤虽有很多褶皱，但目光炯炯有神。千里寻被深深吸引。
At last the Old Ancestor opened her eyes. Surrounded by folds of age, her gaze shone clear and sharp—holding Qianli Xun transfixed.

“女人活着确实不易，但我为何要凭你一己之言出面为你主持和离？你父亲不同意，说明只是你这孩子瞎胡闹而已。”
"A woman's life is indeed hard. Yet why should I take your word alone and preside over a divorce? If your father opposes it, perhaps you're simply acting rash."

“老祖宗此言差矣，我身为女子，深知婚姻对女子的重要，岂能拿自己的婚姻大事胡闹？在一段不符合心意的婚姻中，就像深陷泥潭，无力自拔，即便如此，孙儿也要努力挣扎，哪怕死在泥潭之中，也比活着忍受婚姻的不幸要舒坦许多。”
"With respect, Old Ancestor, I know full well how grave marriage is for a woman—how could I act rash with my own future? Living in a loveless marriage feels like sinking in a swamp with no footing. Even so, I will struggle with all I have; dying in the fight beats living trapped in misery."

“看来，你和离的决心倒是很坚定。那你说说你的父亲为何不同意替你出面？”
"Your resolve runs deep, I see. Tell me then—why does your father refuse to back you?"

“他认为女子无才便是德，后悔将我培养成女词人，认为我嫁给现在的丈夫，才能明白身为女子重在相夫教子，而不是写诗作词，整日沉迷在不切实际的幻想中。他还十分看重朱家的脸面，认为即使我和离也会损害到家族清誉。”
"He believes a woman's virtue lies in having no talent. He regrets raising me as a ci poet and thinks marrying my current husband will force me to serve my spouse and raise children instead of writing verse and chasing airy dreams. Above all, he guards the Zhu family's face and fears even mutual divorce will tarnish its honor."

“你父亲没有什么不对，只是与你的想法不同而已。你刚说丈夫打你，还把商妓领到家里，我听着也认为女子不应该与这样的丈夫过下去，难道你父亲不知？还要因此反对和离？”
"Your father isn't wrong—he simply sees things differently from you. Still, if your husband beats you and brings courtesans home, even I agree no woman should endure that. Doesn't your father know? Why would he still oppose the divorce?"

千里寻不想隐瞒老祖宗：“我爹当然知道，他之所以还反对和离，只因他认为错在孙儿。”
Unwilling to hold anything back, Qianli Xun spoke plain: "Father knows everything. He still opposes it because he thinks I'm at fault."

“哦，说来听听。”
"Oh? Tell me how."

“孙儿在新婚之夜，不愿与丈夫圆房，还以死相逼，平日对丈夫也是不闻不问，冷漠至极。所以，父亲认为错在孙儿，才不同意和离，劝我回去好好过日子。”
"On our wedding night, I refused to consummate the marriage and threatened suicide. Day after day I ignored my husband and treated him like ice. So Father blames me, refuses divorce, and orders me back to behave."

老祖宗又微闭双目，沉默着。千里寻甚是担心，她会不会也像其他人一样拒绝自己？周围的空气就像凝固了一般，只能听到老祖宗盘佛珠的声音。
The Old Ancestor let her eyelids droop once more and fell silent. Anxiety gripped Qianli Xun—would she turn her down too? The air seemed frozen solid, broken only by the dry click of prayer beads.

过了一会儿，老祖宗终于又开口：“你既是女词人，当着我的面写下一首词，若打动我，我必然帮你出面。秀珍，纸笔伺候。”
At length the Old Ancestor spoke again: "Since you are a ci poet, compose a lyric before me right now. Move my heart, and I will stand up for you. Xiuzhen—brush and paper."

秀珍嬷嬷在佛像右侧的书桌上铺好纸张，将墨研好：“姑娘，纸笔已准备好，过来写吧。”
At a desk to the right of the Buddha, Matron Xiuzhen laid out paper and ground fresh ink. "Everything is ready, young lady. Come write."

从老祖宗让她写下一首词时，千里寻就开始搜刮脑细胞，回顾朱淑真写过的诗词，到底哪一首更加合适？
The instant the Old Ancestor asked for a lyric, Qianli Xun began ransacking her memory of Zhu Shuzhen's poems—which one would strike home?

当秀珍嬷嬷请她过去时，还毫无头绪，只能硬着头皮站起来，放慢脚步走向书桌。朱淑真的诗词虽然每一首都写得不错，可要选出一首能打动老祖宗，让她出面主持和离，还是有一定难度。
Even as Matron Xiuzhen beckoned, she drew a blank and stalled, walking slow toward the desk. Every poem of Zhu Shuzhen's carried power, yet picking the exact one to move a century-old Buddhist elder into backing a divorce wasn't easy.

千里寻已坐在书桌前，她提起笔，轻蘸墨汁，无意中抬头看到老祖宗身后的墙壁上挂着一个“谜”字，不由问道：“嬷嬷，一般修佛之人给墙上要么挂一‘禅’字，要么挂‘清净’二字，为何老祖宗要挂一个‘谜’字？”
Seated at the desk, dipping her brush into ink, she happened to glance up—and saw a single massive calligraphy character hanging behind the Old Ancestor: "Riddle" (谜). Curiosity struck. "Matron, most Buddhists hang 'Zen' or 'Serenity' on their wall—why does the Old Ancestor hang 'Riddle'?"

秀珍嬷嬷回道：“老祖宗认为人之所以无知，是因为身在谜中，她要用这个字时刻提醒自己突破谜境，才可明了人生真相，只有明了人生真相，方可放下贪嗔痴慢，达到内心清净。”
"The Old Ancestor holds that mortals stay ignorant because they dwell inside a riddle," Matron Xiuzhen replied. "She keeps that character there as a constant reminder to break past illusion and grasp life's truth—only then can one cast off greed, anger, delusion, and pride to reach inner peace."

佛理太过深奥，千里寻听了也是一知半解，但这个“谜”字却让一首词出现她的头脑中，迅速提笔写下：
Half the Buddhist philosophy sailed right over Qianli Xun's head—yet that single word "riddle" unlocked the perfect lyric in her mind! Her brush flew across the paper:

下楼来，金钱卜落；问苍天，人在何方？恨王孙，一直去了；詈冤家，言去难留。
Coming downstairs, golden coins drop for divination; I ask High Heaven, where has he gone? Resenting the noble lord who walked straight away; cursing the heartless one—speaking of parting, hard to hold back.

﻿悔当初，吾错失口，有上交无下交。皂白何须问？分开不用刀，从今莫把仇人靠，千种相思一撇销。（《断肠谜》）
Regretting how I first misspoke; there is upper union, no lower tie. Why ask right from wrong? Parting needs no knife; from this day on lean no more on a foe—a thousand kinds of longing wiped away with one stroke. ("The Heartbreak Riddle") [TN: Translator's rendering]

写完后，千里寻放下笔，拿起纸，用嘴巴吹了吹未干的墨迹，送到秀珍嬷嬷手中。秀珍嬷嬷接过，走过去，给老祖宗在桌上铺开。
Setting down the brush, Qianli Xun lifted the sheet, blew across the wet ink, and handed it to Matron Xiuzhen, who spread it on the table before the Old Ancestor.

﻿只见老祖宗停下盘佛珠的手，抬眼向纸上瞧去，用心仔细鉴赏了一会儿，说道：“你回去吧，我答应为你出面。”
The Old Ancestor halted her beads, raised her eyes to the page, and read every character with deep attention before declaring: "Go back. I agree to step forward for you."

千里寻兴奋不已：“晚辈谢过老祖宗！”
Joy surged through Qianli Xun. "Your descendant thanks you, Old Ancestor!"

秀珍嬷嬷将千里寻送出屋外，嘱咐道：“老祖宗既然同意出面，请姑娘明日把和离的时间和地点差人写在纸条上，从门缝塞进来即可。”
Walking her to the door, Matron Xiuzhen instructed, "Since the Old Ancestor has consented, send someone tomorrow with a note stating the time and place of the divorce meeting and slip it under the gate."

千里寻说：“嬷嬷放心，我定会办妥。不过，我怕老祖宗不认得路，要不我派人来接她老人家？”
"Count on it, Matron! Only—I'm worried the Old Ancestor might not know the way. Should I send a carriage to fetch her?"

“不必，老祖宗活了一百二十岁，什么没经历过？你只管等着就是，我自会与她过去。姑娘，请！”
"No need. At a hundred and twenty, what hasn't the Old Ancestor seen? Wait where you are—I will bring her myself. Farewell, young lady."

秀珍嬷嬷做出送客的手势，千里寻拜别离开。出了佛堂小院，门又被紧紧关上。
Gesturing her out, Matron Xiuzhen shut the shrine gate firm behind her.

朱景元与苦荞迎上来。
Zhu Jingyuan and Kuqiao hurried up.

“妹妹，老祖宗可同意？”
"Well, Sister? Did the Old Ancestor agree?"

千里寻轻松地点点头：“同意了！”
Qianli Xun nodded with a breezy smile. "She agreed!"

苦荞也替小姐感到高兴：“小姐，接下来咱们该怎么做？”
Thrilled for her mistress, Kuqiao asked, "What's our next move, Miss?"

千里寻干脆地说：“回林府！”
Qianli Xun answered crisp and clear: "Back to Lin Manor!"
