# 第16章 佛堂有请老祖宗 / Chapter 16: The Bodhisattva Behind the Door

---

以朱府的布局来看，佛堂所在之处，比朱淑真住的园子还要偏僻。

Judging from the layout of the Zhu residence, the Buddhist shrine was located in an even more remote place than the garden where Zhu Shuzhen lived.

---

在朱景元的带领下，千里寻跟着往朱府深处走去，直到眼前出现一座小山丘，千里寻才看到，在那山丘底下有座园子，外形像是一座寺庙，园墙不高，能清楚地看到里面的房屋。

Led by Zhu Jingyuan, Qianli Xun followed deeper into the Zhu residence. Only when a small hill appeared before her did she see a garden at the foot of the hill. It was shaped like a temple, with low garden walls that allowed a clear view of the buildings inside.

---

"哥哥，朱府怎么把山都给搬了进来？"

"Brother, how did the Zhu residence move a whole mountain inside?"

---

"那不是山，只是个大土堆而已，当年建造朱府时，为了搞这么一个像小山一样的大土堆，可是从临安城外拉了不少土进来。"

"That's not a mountain, just a big mound of earth. When the Zhu residence was built, to create such a hill-like mound, a lot of soil had to be brought in from outside Lin'an City."

---

千里寻又看到一条清澈见底的小河流，河面很窄，水并不深，水里放着几块石头，需要穿过这条小河才能到达佛堂。

Qianli Xun then saw a narrow, clear stream. The water wasn't deep, and a few stones were placed in it. They needed to cross this stream to reach the Buddhist shrine.

---

三人踩着石头穿过小河，是一段石子甬路，走上去脚底硌硌的，像是在做足底按摩。当石子甬路走尽后，穿过野草地，才算真正到达佛堂跟前。门紧闭着，上面有一个小小的匾额，写着"佛堂"二字，里面连续传出敲击木鱼的声音。

The three of them stepped on the stones to cross the stream. Then came a stone-paved path. Walking on it felt like a foot massage. After the stone path ended, they crossed a wild grass area before truly reaching the Buddhist shrine. The door was tightly closed, with a small plaque above it bearing the words "Buddhist Shrine." From inside came the continuous sound of a wooden fish being struck.

---

朱景元说："淑真，你在这里等着，我前去敲门。"

Zhu Jingyuan said, "Shuzhen, you wait here. I'll go knock on the door."

---

千里寻点点头，看着哥哥走到跟前，将那木门敲了三下，等了一会儿，并没有人来开门。又敲了三下，又等了一会儿，还是没有人开门。继续敲了三下，终于听到里面有脚步声传来，三人一阵高兴。

Qianli Xun nodded and watched her brother approach the wooden door. He knocked three times, waited for a while, but no one came to open it. He knocked three more times, waited again, still no one. He knocked three more times, and finally heard footsteps from inside. The three of them were delighted.

---

里面有拔门栓的声音，接着"吱扭"一声门被打开，一位头发花白的老妇人出现在眼前，问道："你们是？"

There was the sound of a bolt being drawn, then with a creak, the door opened. A gray-haired old woman appeared before them and asked, "Who are you?"

---

苦荞赶紧上前有礼道："嬷嬷好，这位是朱府大公子，这位是小姐，前来拜见老祖宗。"

Kuqiao quickly came forward and greeted her politely, "Greetings, Nanny. This is the eldest young master of the Zhu residence, and this is the young miss. We've come to pay respects to the Old Ancestor."

---

嬷嬷说："原来是府中的公子和小姐，你们这些小辈可能还不知道，老祖宗一心向佛，从不见俗世之人，还是走吧。"说完就要关门。

The nanny said, "So you're the young master and young miss of the residence. You younger generation may not know this, but the Old Ancestor is devoted to Buddhism and has never received visitors from the mortal world. You should leave." With that, she was about to close the door.

---

朱景元阻拦道："嬷嬷，这些晚辈都知道，本不该来打扰老祖宗清净，只因舍妹遇到人生难题，需要面见老祖宗，还请行个方便。"

Zhu Jingyuan stopped her, "Nanny, we juniors know this. We shouldn't disturb the Old Ancestor's peace. It's just that my sister has encountered a life crisis and needs to meet the Old Ancestor. Please do us a favor."

---

嬷嬷说："老祖宗脱离世俗，六根清净，岂可涉足红尘之事，你们听话，还是离去吧。"

The nanny said, "The Old Ancestor has detached herself from worldly affairs and purified her six senses. How can she involve herself in mortal matters? Listen, you should leave."

---

这是唯一的机会，如果请不到一位长辈出面，和离只能失败。哪怕请一位，成功的几率也会多一些，只要有一线希望，千里寻都会牢牢抓住。于是大声说："老祖宗修习佛法，难道不明白佛菩萨普度众生的夙愿吗？现在众生之一，您的不知道第几代的孙女有难，需要您这位菩萨出来救赎，可您却躲在佛堂里只顾一个人清修，这算哪门子修习佛法？"

This was the only chance. If they couldn't persuade an elder to step forward, the mutual divorce would fail. Even persuading one would increase the chances of success. As long as there was a glimmer of hope, Qianli Xun would grasp it firmly. So she said loudly, "The Old Ancestor practices Buddhism. Doesn't she understand the Buddha's vow to save all sentient beings? Now one of those beings—your descendant of who-knows-how-many generations—is in trouble and needs you, a bodhisattva, to come out and save her. Yet you hide in the Buddhist shrine, only cultivating yourself. What kind of Buddhist practice is this?"

---

嬷嬷批评道："你这小辈，太不知礼，怎能随意批评老祖宗？"

The nanny scolded, "You young one, you're too impolite. How dare you criticize the Old Ancestor at will?"

---

里面的木鱼声戛然而止，传出一个苍老而有力的声音："秀珍，让她进来吧。"

Inside, the sound of the wooden fish suddenly stopped. An aged yet powerful voice came out, "Xiuzhen, let her in."

---

三人一阵欣喜，正要一起进入，被秀珍嬷嬷挡住："没听老祖宗说只让她一人进去？你们两个在外面侯着。"

The three of them were delighted and about to enter together, but Nanny Xiuzhen blocked them. "Didn't you hear the Old Ancestor say only she can enter? You two wait outside."

---

朱景元和苦荞只好收住脚步，在外面等候。

Zhu Jingyuan and Kuqiao had to stop and wait outside.

---

千里寻兴奋之余跟着秀珍嬷嬷进入。

Qianli Xun excitedly followed Nanny Xiuzhen inside.

---

佛堂的小院不大，简单而整洁，最吸引人的是三株腊梅，早已过花季，枝叶倒是繁茂。

The small courtyard of the Buddhist shrine was simple and tidy. The most attractive feature was three wintersweet plum trees. Though the flowering season had long passed, their branches and leaves were lush.

---

走进佛堂的正屋，迎面最显眼的地方摆着一尊高大的佛像，看了不由让人肃然起敬。佛像前面的供桌上有个香炉，里面插着三炷香，正在燃烧，供桌前放着一个蒲团。

As they entered the main hall of the Buddhist shrine, a tall Buddha statue dominated the space before them, naturally inspiring reverence. On the offering table in front of the Buddha statue was an incense burner with three incense sticks burning. A prayer cushion was placed before the offering table.

---

秀珍嬷嬷说："姑娘既然进来，需先拜拜。"

Nanny Xiuzhen said, "Since you've entered, you must first pay respects."

---

千里寻乖乖照做，谁让她有求而来。她走上前，双手合十，跪在蒲团上，拜了三拜，心想："老祖宗还没见到，倒先拜起佛像来。"

Qianli Xun obediently complied. After all, she had come with a request. She moved forward, pressed her palms together, knelt on the prayer cushion, and bowed three times, thinking, *I haven't even met the Old Ancestor yet, and I'm already bowing to the Buddha statue.*

---

拜完后起身，还没来得及瞧四周，听到秀珍嬷嬷的声音："老祖宗，朱家的小辈进来了，就由您老教训教训她。"

After bowing, she stood up. Before she could survey the surroundings, she heard Nanny Xiuzhen's voice, "Old Ancestor, the young one from the Zhu family has entered. Please teach her a lesson."

---

千里寻扭头看去，在佛像的左边放着一张几案，上面放着木鱼和犍稚，几案旁坐着一位头发全白的老人，眼窝深陷，鼻梁高挺，嘴巴周围充满褶皱，可以说整张脸都充满褶皱。褶皱以及零散的老年斑也无法遮住皮肤散发出的白光，一看便知年轻时绝对是位皮肤白皙的美人。千里寻觉得这张脸虽苍老，却很慈祥，只要看那么一眼，心里便会产生奇妙的温暖的感觉。

Qianli Xun turned to observe. On the left side of the Buddha statue was a small table with a wooden fish and mallet on it. Beside the table sat an old woman with completely white hair. Her eye sockets were deep, her nose bridge high. Wrinkles covered her entire face, yet neither they nor the scattered age spots could hide the pale glow of her skin. One could tell at a glance that in her youth, she must have been a beauty with fair skin. Qianli Xun felt that although this face was aged, it was very kind. Just one look filled you with an inexplicable warmth.

---

老祖宗微闭双目，双腿盘坐，手里拿着佛珠用心盘着，嘴里念念有词。听到秀珍的话后，张嘴说："让她过来。"

The Old Ancestor had her eyes half-closed, legs crossed, fingering her prayer beads, chanting sutras softly. When she heard Xiuzhen's words, she said, "Let her come over."

---

千里寻知道叫自己，走上前。

Qianli Xun knew she was being called and approached.

---

秀珍嬷嬷向她示意坐在蒲团上，她虽不习惯，还是听话地坐下。

Nanny Xiuzhen gestured for her to sit on a prayer cushion. Though not used to it, she obediently sat down.

---

老祖宗依然盘着佛珠，双眼微闭："听你刚才说话有些见识，所以请你进来，不知你找我所为何事？"

The Old Ancestor kept counting her beads, eyes half-closed, "I heard your words just now showed some insight, so I invited you in. May I ask what brings you to me?"

---

千里寻迅速在心里组织开场白，终于找到合适的："老祖宗是女人，应该最能体会女人的不易，孙儿因父母之命媒妁之言嫁给才德无法与孙儿相匹配的丈夫，这些倒没什么，如果他人好，日子还可以过下去，可他打孙儿，还把商妓往家里带，孙儿实在无法跟他过下去，便想到和离。可父亲不同意替我出面，只要父亲不同意，一家的长辈，包括族中的长辈都不会替我出面，孙儿无路可走，只能来求老祖宗。求老祖宗可怜我，替我出面主持和离之事。"

Qianli Xun quickly organized her opening in her mind and finally found the right words, "Old Ancestor, you are a woman and should best understand women's hardships. I was arranged by my parents through a matchmaker to marry a husband whose talent and virtue couldn't match mine. That wouldn't matter much. If he were a good person, life could go on. But he beats me and even brings merchant courtesans home. I really can't continue living with him, so I thought of a mutual divorce. But father doesn't agree to speak for me. As long as father disagrees, no elder in the family, including clan elders, will speak for me. I have no other way out and can only come to beg the Old Ancestor. Please have pity on me and speak for me to oversee the mutual divorce."

---

老祖宗终于抬眼，那双眼睛周围的皮肤虽有很多褶皱，但目光炯炯有神。千里寻被深深吸引。

The Old Ancestor finally raised her eyes. Although the skin around her eyes was heavily lined with wrinkles, her gaze was bright and spirited. Qianli Xun was captivated.

---

"女人活着确实不易，但我为何要凭你一己之言出面为你主持和离？你父亲不同意，说明只是你这孩子瞎胡闹而已。"

"It's true that women have it hard in life. But why should I intervene to oversee your mutual divorce based solely on your words? Your father doesn't agree, which tells me you're just a child throwing a tantrum."

---

"老祖宗此言差矣，我身为女子，深知婚姻对女子的重要，岂能拿自己的婚姻大事胡闹？在一段不符合心意的婚姻中，就像深陷泥潭，无力自拔，即便如此，孙儿也要努力挣扎，哪怕死在泥潭之中，也比活着忍受婚姻的不幸要舒坦许多。"

"Old Ancestor, you're wrong. As a woman, I deeply understand the importance of marriage to women. How could I play around with such a major life event? Being in a marriage that doesn't suit one's heart is like being stuck in a mud pit, unable to pull oneself out. Even so, I must struggle. Even if I die in the mud pit, it would be better than living and enduring the unhappiness of marriage."

---

"看来，你和离的决心倒是很坚定。那你说说你的父亲为何不同意替你出面？"

"It seems your determination to seek a mutual divorce is quite firm. Then tell me, why doesn't your father agree to speak for you?"

---

"他认为女子无才便是德，后悔将我培养成女词人，认为我嫁给现在的丈夫，才能明白身为女子重在相夫教子，而不是写诗作词，整日沉迷在不切实际的幻想中。他还十分看重朱家的脸面，认为即使我和离也会损害到家族清誉。"

"He believes that a woman's virtue is having no talent and regrets raising me to be a female ci poet. He thinks that by marrying my current husband, I can understand that a woman's priority is to help her husband and educate her children, not write poems and ci, indulging in unrealistic fantasies all day. He also values the Zhu family's face very much, believing that even my mutual divorce would damage the family's reputation."

---

"你父亲没有什么不对，只是与你的想法不同而已。你刚说丈夫打你，还把商妓领到家里，我听着也认为女子不应该与这样的丈夫过下去，难道你父亲不知？还要因此反对和离？"

"Your father isn't wrong, he just has a different perspective. You just said your husband beats you and even brings merchant courtesans home. Listening to this, I also think a woman shouldn't continue living with such a husband. Doesn't your father know this? Yet he still opposes the mutual divorce?"

---

千里寻不想隐瞒老祖宗："我爹当然知道，他之所以还反对和离，只因他认为错在孙儿。"

Qianli Xun didn't want to hide anything from the Old Ancestor, "Of course my father knows. The reason he still opposes the mutual divorce is only because he thinks the fault lies with me."

---

"哦，说来听听。"

"Oh, tell me."

---

"孙儿在新婚之夜，不愿与丈夫圆房，还以死相逼，平日对丈夫也是不闻不问，冷漠至极。所以，父亲认为错在孙儿，才不同意和离，劝我回去好好过日子。"

"On my wedding night, I refused to consummate the marriage with my husband and even threatened suicide. In daily life, I also ignored my husband completely, being extremely cold. So, father thinks the fault lies with me and doesn't agree to the mutual divorce, advising me to go back and live properly."

---

老祖宗又微闭双目，沉默着。千里寻甚是担心，她会不会也像其他人一样拒绝自己？周围的空气就像凝固了一般，只能听到老祖宗盘佛珠的声音。

The Old Ancestor closed her eyes again and fell silent. Qianli Xun was very worried—would she also refuse her like the others? The air around seemed to solidify, with only the click of the Old Ancestor's prayer beads breaking the silence.

---

过了一会儿，老祖宗终于又开口："你既是女词人，当着我的面写下一首词，若打动我，我必然帮你出面。秀珍，纸笔伺候。"

After a while, the Old Ancestor finally spoke again, "Since you are a female ci poet, write a ci in front of me. If it moves me, I will definitely help you. Xiuzhen, prepare paper and brush."

---

秀珍嬷嬷在佛像右侧的书桌上铺好纸张，将墨研好："姑娘，纸笔已准备好，过来写吧。"

Nanny Xiuzhen spread paper on the desk to the right of the Buddha statue and ground the ink, "Young miss, the paper and brush are ready. Come and write."

---

从老祖宗让她写下一首词时，千里寻就开始搜刮脑细胞，回顾朱淑真写过的诗词，到底哪一首更加合适？

From the moment the Old Ancestor asked her to write a ci, Qianli Xun started racking her brains, reviewing the poems and ci that Zhu Shuzhen had written. Which one would be more suitable?

---

当秀珍嬷嬷请她过去时，还毫无头绪，只能硬着头皮站起来，放慢脚步走向书桌。朱淑真的诗词虽然每一首都写得不错，可要选出一首能打动老祖宗，让她出面主持和离，还是有一定难度。

When Nanny Xiuzhen invited her over, she still had no clue. She could only steel herself to stand up and move slowly toward the desk. Although every poem and ci by Zhu Shuzhen was well-written, choosing one that could move the Old Ancestor to intervene and oversee the mutual divorce was still quite difficult.

---

千里寻已坐在书桌前，她提起笔，轻蘸墨汁，无意中抬头看到老祖宗身后的墙壁上挂着一个"谜"字，不由问道："嬷嬷，一般修佛之人给墙上要么挂一'禅'字，要么挂'清净'二字，为何老祖宗要挂一个'谜'字？"

Qianli Xun was now sitting at the desk. She took up the brush, lightly dipped it in ink, and happened to glance up to see the character "mystery" hanging on the wall behind the Old Ancestor. She couldn't help but ask, "Nanny, usually those who cultivate Buddhism hang either the character 'zen' or the two characters 'pure and clean' on the wall. Why does the Old Ancestor hang the character 'mystery'?"

---

秀珍嬷嬷回道："老祖宗认为人之所以无知，是因为身在谜中，她要用这个字时刻提醒自己突破谜境，才可明了人生真相，只有明了人生真相，方可放下贪嗔痴慢，达到内心清净。"

Nanny Xiuzhen replied, "The Old Ancestor believes that the reason people are ignorant is that they are within the mystery. She uses this character to constantly remind herself to break through the mystery, so she can understand the truth of life. Only by understanding the truth of life can one let go of greed, anger, ignorance, and arrogance, and attain inner peace."

---

佛理太过深奥，千里寻听了也是一知半解，但这个"谜"字却让一首词出现她的头脑中，迅速提笔写下：

Buddhist principles were too profound. Qianli Xun only half-understood what she heard. But the character "mystery" triggered a ci in her mind. She seized her brush and wrote:

---

下楼来，金钱卜落；问苍天，人在何方？恨王孙，一直去了；詈冤家，言去难留。
悔当初，吾错失口，有上交无下交。皂白何须问？分开不用刀，从今莫把仇人靠，千种相思一撇销。（《断肠谜》）

Coming downstairs, I cast coins to divine;
Asking the azure sky, where could he be?
Hating my lord, he has gone straight away;
Cursing the faithless one, so hard to make him stay.
Regretting the past, I let careless words slip;
Ties severed above, with nothing below.
Why even ask who is right and who is wrong?
Separated without a knife, from now on rely on no foe,
A thousand kinds of longing, erased with one single stroke.
(“The Heartbreak Riddle”) 
[TN: This is a famous character riddle poem (字谜诗) by Zhu Shuzhen, where each line cleverly conceals a Chinese numeral from one to ten through visual wordplay on the characters. For example, "coming downstairs" (下楼来) suggests "one" (一) because removing the component "卜" from "下" leaves "一". Operating brilliantly on two levels, the poem is both a bitter lament to sever ties with a toxic lover, and an intricate numerical puzzle—a dazzling display of the poet's extraordinary wit.]

---

写完后，千里寻放下笔，拿起纸，用嘴巴吹了吹未干的墨迹，送到秀珍嬷嬷手中。秀珍嬷嬷接过，走过去，给老祖宗在桌上铺开。

After finishing, Qianli Xun set down the brush, lifted the paper, blew gently on the wet ink, and handed it to Nanny Xiuzhen. Nanny Xiuzhen took it, stepped over, and spread it on the table for the Old Ancestor.

---

只见老祖宗停下盘佛珠的手，抬眼向纸上瞧去，用心仔细鉴赏了一会儿，说道："你回去吧，我答应为你出面。"

The Old Ancestor ceased her bead-counting, raised her eyes to examine the paper, carefully appreciated it for a while, and said, "You may go back. I promise to speak for you."

---

千里寻兴奋不已："晚辈谢过老祖宗！"

Qianli Xun was overjoyed. "I, your junior, thank the Old Ancestor!"

---

秀珍嬷嬷将千里寻送出屋外，嘱咐道："老祖宗既然同意出面，请姑娘明日把和离的时间和地点差人写在纸条上，从门缝塞进来即可。"

Nanny Xiuzhen escorted Qianli Xun out of the room and instructed, "Since the Old Ancestor has agreed to speak for you, please have someone write the time and place of the mutual divorce on a piece of paper tomorrow and slip it through the door crack."

---

千里寻说："嬷嬷放心，我定会办妥。不过，我怕老祖宗不认得路，要不我派人来接她老人家？"

Qianli Xun said, "Don't worry, Nanny. I'll definitely handle it properly. However, I'm afraid the Old Ancestor might not know the way. Should I send someone to pick her up?"

---

"不必，老祖宗活了一百二十岁，什么没经历过？你只管等着就是，我自会与她过去。姑娘，请！"

"No need. The Old Ancestor has lived one hundred and twenty years. What hasn't she experienced? Just wait. I'll go with her myself. Young miss, please go!"

---

秀珍嬷嬷做出送客的手势，千里寻拜别离开。出了佛堂小院，门又被紧紧关上。

Nanny Xiuzhen made a gesture to see her off. Qianli Xun bowed and left. Once out of the Buddhist shrine courtyard, the door was tightly closed again.

---

朱景元与苦荞迎上来。

Zhu Jingyuan and Kuqiao came up to her.

---

"妹妹，老祖宗可同意？"
