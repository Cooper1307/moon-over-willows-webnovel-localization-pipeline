#### 《断肠谜》翻译详细分析

**原文特点**：
- 这是一首字谜诗，每句隐喻数字1-10
- "下"字少一点为"一"，"天"字没有"人"为"二"，以此类推
- 表面是抒情诗，实际是数字谜

**当前译文问题**：
1. 仅翻译了表面意思，完全未传达字谜结构
2. "Hating the prince"王孙翻译不准确，此处指代负心人
3. "having upper connections but no lower ones"有上交无下交翻译生硬
4. 缺乏对字谜特征的说明

**优化建议**：
```
Coming downstairs, I cast coins to divine;  
Asking the blue sky, where is he?  
Hating the faithless one, he's gone straight away;  
Cursing my foe, saying it's hard to keep him.  
Regretting the past, I let words slip;  
Having upper connections but no lower ones.  
Why ask about black and white?  
Separated without a knife,  
From now don't rely on enemies,  
A thousand kinds of longing erased with one stroke.  

("The Heartbreak Riddle" - TN: This is a character riddle poem where each line conceals a number from one to ten through wordplay. For example, "coming downstairs" (下楼) suggests "one" as the character for "down" (下) minus one stroke becomes "one" (一). The poem operates on two levels: as a lament of lost love and as an intricate numerical puzzle.)
```
