第98章 七夕到来忙逛街
## Chapter 98: Qixi Arrives and the Streets Come Alive

七夕节这日，朱府的下人一早起来，开始忙碌，只等着忙完去逛街。逛完街快天黑时，再赶回来安排乞巧宴。
On Qixi Festival, the servants of Zhu Manor rose at dawn and got straight to work, eager to finish their chores and head out to roam the streets. Once dusk closed in, they would hurry back to set up the Qiqiao Banquet, the Qixi feast for praying for skill.

有的拿来小木板，敷上泥土，将粟米的种子埋进泥土里；有的用各种瓜雕刻出不同花样，花纹精细巧妙；有的用油面蜜糖制作果食，花样百出，形态各异；有的把已长得长长的绿豆芽，用红蓝丝线捆绑起来，放进盘子里。
Some fetched small wooden boards, spread soil over them, and planted millet seeds; some carved fine, clever patterns into melons of every kind; some shaped all manner of pastries from flour, oil, and honey into endless designs and forms; and others bundled tall mung bean sprouts with red and blue silk threads and arranged them on plates.

还有一部分下人在朱家最大的庭院内张灯结彩，安排焚香桌案，排列摩睺罗、花瓜、美酒、笔墨纸砚、针线等。
Still other servants hung lanterns and silk festoons in Zhu Manor's largest courtyard, set up incense tables, and laid out Mohouluo clay dolls, carved flower-melons, fine wine, the Four Treasures of the Study, needles and thread, and more.

一切安排完毕后，下人们无论男女都换上新衣裳，三三两两相邀去逛街。
Once everything stood ready, the servants—men and women alike—changed into new clothes and headed out in twos and threes to stroll the streets.

绿桃换上新衣裳，约苦荞一起去街上玩儿，被苦荞拒绝，说她要陪小姐。苦荞本想跟丫鬟们去，可要是去了，就见不到白风，七夕节还是要跟喜欢的人在一起。
Dressed in her new outfit, Lutao invited Kuqiao to go out and have fun on the streets, only for Kuqiao to turn her down, saying she had to attend her mistress. Kuqiao had half wanted to go with the other maids, yet going with them meant missing Bai Feng, and Qixi Festival was meant to be spent with the one you liked.

因仪王帮忙，林觉不再纠缠夏果。夏果也大胆从望月轩回来，她更不愿离开小姐，只因跟着小姐才能见到乔公子。
Thanks to Prince Yi's help, Lin Jue no longer pestered Xiaguo. Xiaguo had ventured back from Wangyue Pavilion, and she was even less willing to leave her mistress's side, because staying with her mistress was the only way she could see Young Master Qiao.

两个丫鬟各有心思，千里寻也不好多说什么，想跟着就跟着吧。
Each of the two maids had her own private motive, and Qianli Xun saw no point saying much. If they wanted to tag along, let them.

三人换上新做的衣裳，来到朱府门外，上了马车。昨日南双来过，说明相约的时间和地点，此时出发到达临安城内的七星桥，不早也不晚。
Changing into their fresh, custom-made outfits, the three walked out of Zhu Manor and boarded the carriage. Nan Shuang had stopped by yesterday with the meeting time and place; setting off now would bring them to Seven Star Bridge in Lin'an right on time, neither early nor late.

千里寻叮嘱道：“夏果，今日仪王也来，你见到他，一定要好好磕几个头。”
Qianli Xun reminded her, "Xiaguo, Prince Yi will be there today too. The moment you see him, make sure you give him a few proper kowtows."

“小姐不用担心，见到仪王，我二话不说，跪下磕头。”
"Don't worry, Miss. As soon as I see Prince Yi, I'll drop to my knees and kowtow without a word."

“这就对了。”
"Good."

苦荞心里想着白风，一直不言语。
With Bai Feng on her mind, Kuqiao stayed quiet the whole way.

千里寻问：“你今儿怎么这么安静？”
Qianli Xun asked, "Why are you so quiet today?"

苦荞掩饰道：“我一直都很安静，难道小姐才发现？”
Kuqiao tried to cover it up. "I'm always quiet. Is Miss just noticing now?"

千里寻研究苦荞的表情：“不对，你今儿安静的样子跟往常不同，老实招来，是不是有喜欢的人？该不会是南双吧？我见你跟他走得很近，呵呵……！”
Qianli Xun studied Kuqiao's face. "No way. Your quiet today isn't like your usual quiet. Fess up: is there someone you like? Don't tell me it's Nan Shuang! I've seen you hanging around him a lot, haha...!"

苦荞红着脸：“小姐就喜欢拿我开玩笑，南双还是个孩子，我怎么可能喜欢他？跟他走得近，也是因为小姐总见乔公子，我不得已才跟他熟络起来。”
Kuqiao flushed crimson. "Miss just loves teasing me! Nan Shuang is still a kid. How could I like him? The only reason I got close to him is because Miss sees Young Master Qiao all the time, so I couldn't help getting friendly with him."

千里寻仔细一想，苦荞说得有道理，便不再逗她。
Thinking it over, Qianli Xun figured Kuqiao had a point and stopped teasing her.

苦荞提到乔公子，夏果心里产生异样的感觉。
Kuqiao's mention of Young Master Qiao stirred a strange ache inside Xiaguo.

她在望月轩这段时日，小姐与乔公子进展迅速，她不敢奢求乔公子会喜欢她，只要跟在小姐身边，能见到乔公子，夏果便心满意足。如若以后小姐嫁给乔公子，日日见到他，说不定还有近身伺候的机会。
During her stay at Wangyue Pavilion, her mistress and Young Master Qiao had grown close in leaps and bounds. Xiaguo dared not dream of Young Master Qiao liking her back; as long as she stayed by her mistress and got to see him, she would be content. And if her mistress married Young Master Qiao someday, seeing him every day might even bring her the chance to wait on him up close.

她听说过，临安富贵人家有丫鬟被纳为妾室的先例。夏果不愿当林觉的妾室，可若乔公子愿意，她会心甘情愿当他的妾室。
She had heard of plenty of wealthy households in Lin'an taking a mistress's maid as a concubine. Xiaguo wanted no part of being Lin Jue's concubine, yet if Young Master Qiao ever wanted her, she would become his concubine with all her heart.

通往七星桥的路不适合乘坐马车，她们早早下来，步行往七星桥走去。街上人来人往，都身着新衣，满面喜色，跟过年似的。
Since the lane leading to Seven Star Bridge was too narrow for a carriage, they stepped out early and continued on foot. Crowds streamed past in both directions, everyone dressed in new clothes and beaming with cheer, as festive as New Year's Day.

千里寻感慨，普通的七夕节被古人过得喜气洋洋，热闹非凡。
Qianli Xun marveled at how these ancient folk turned an ordinary Qixi Festival into such a jubilant, bustling spectacle.

主仆三人穿过人流，来到七星桥旁。千里寻向周围张望一番，不见文渊的人影。
Weaving through the crowd, the mistress and her two maids reached Seven Star Bridge. Qianli Xun scanned the area, yet saw no sign of Wenyuan.

正要埋怨，苦荞指向一旁：“小姐，乔公子在那里，正向我们招手。”
Just as she was about to grumble, Kuqiao pointed off to the side. "Miss, Young Master Qiao is over there! He's waving at us!"

千里寻瞧去，果然是文渊，他坐在茶肆里，挥动手臂。千里寻露出笑容，也挥挥手，带着丫鬟向茶肆走去。
Qianli Xun looked over, and sure enough, there sat Wenyuan inside a teahouse, waving his arm. Breaking into a smile, she waved back and led her maids toward the teahouse.

这家茶肆与别处不同，呈半露天状，搭着精美的顶棚，坐在棚下喝茶，四周一览无余。
This teahouse differed from most, built half open to the air beneath an exquisite awning; sitting under its shade over tea gave an unobstructed view all around.

千里寻清楚地看到仪王与佳和郡主也坐在桌旁，她上前先拜见两位贵人，特意未说出称呼之语，主要担心他们逛街不愿被人知道身份。
Qianli Xun spotted Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe sitting at the table too. Stepping forward, she bowed in greeting to the two noble guests, careful not to utter their titles aloud in case they preferred to keep their identities hidden while strolling the streets.

夏果二话不说，立刻跪下磕头：“多谢王爷！多谢王爷！”
Without a second thought, Xiaguo dropped to her knees and kowtowed at once. "Thank you, Your Highness! Thank you, Your Highness!"

申简辰皱起眉头：“哪来的王爷？今日只有申公子。”
Shen Jianchen knitted his brows. "What 'Highness'? Today there is only Young Master Shen."

“多谢申公子！多谢申公子！”
"Thank you, Young Master Shen! Thank you, Young Master Shen!"

“你只管谢你家小姐，至于我，得你家小姐来谢。”申简辰故意如此说，顺便向千里寻看了一眼，她今日打扮得甚是美艳，他忍不住不心动。
"Just thank your mistress. As for me, it's your mistress who owes me thanks." Shen Jianchen spoke those words on purpose, shooting Qianli Xun a glance. Dressed up today, she looked stunning, and his heart could not help racing.

仪王明显话中有话，眼神异常。千里寻心想：“又调戏老娘！”
Prince Yi's double meaning was plain as day, and that look in his eyes was anything but innocent. "Flirting with me again!" Qianli Xun thought.

再瞅瞅佳和郡主，她竟然还在笑，难道听不出仪王的话语中略带调戏？还有那眼神！郡主傻乎乎的，也不知品味一下。
Glancing over at Princess Jiahe, she saw the princess still smiling away. Couldn't she hear the flirtation laced into Prince Yi's tone? And that gaze! The princess was way too naive. Couldn't she read the room at all?

再观察文渊，也是一副高兴的样子，竟然对仪王毫无戒备。
Looking at Wenyuan, he looked just as cheerful, without a shred of guard up against Prince Yi.

“夏果，申公子既然说了，你起来吧，不必跪着。”千里寻在文渊身边坐下，“申公子放心，今日我就感谢您。”
"Xiaguo, since Young Master Shen said so, get up. No need to stay kneeling." Qianli Xun took a seat beside Wenyuan. "Rest assured, Young Master Shen, I will thank you today."

“你打算怎么谢我？”尽管已知，申简辰还想听千里寻亲口说出；尽管打算放下对她的感情，可见到她，还是不由自主想要与她多说话。
"And how do you plan to thank me?" Even though he already knew, Shen Jianchen wanted to hear Qianli Xun say it herself. Even though he meant to let go of his feelings for her, the moment he saw her, he could not stop himself from wanting to draw out every word with her.

千里寻问：“难道文渊没告诉您？”
Qianli Xun asked, "Didn't Wenyuan tell you?"

“他说归他说，我想听幽栖居士亲口说。”
"What he says is his business. I want to hear it from Youqi Jushi's own lips."

千里寻心里仍旧不爽：“竟然敢光明正大继续调戏？老娘现在就让你死了这份心思。”
Irritation flared in Qianli Xun's chest. "Still got the nerve to flirt right out in the open? Watch me kill that wishful thinking right now!"

千里寻将头靠在文渊的肩头：“文渊完全可以代表我，他说跟我说没有任何区别。”
Qianli Xun rested her head against Wenyuan's shoulder. "Wenyuan can speak for me anytime. Him saying it is no different from me saying it."

乔梦容露出羡慕的表情，她也想把头搭在哥哥的肩膀。哥哥表情严肃，垂下眼帘，搞得她不敢这么做，不由叹道：“幽栖居士与乔公子真令人羡慕。”
Envy flickered across Qiao Mengrong's face; she wished she could lean her head on Prince Yi's shoulder too. Yet his expression turned stern and his eyes dropped, leaving her too timid to try. A sigh escaped her lips: "Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao really make people envious."

乔文仙小声说：“佳和郡主与仪王也令人羡慕。”
Qiao Wenxian murmured, "Princess Jiahe and Prince Yi make people just as envious."

申简辰不愿面对二人亲密的样子，拉起乔梦容的手：“梦容，我们去逛街。”
Unable to stomach watching the two of them act so cozy, Shen Jianchen took Qiao Mengrong by the hand. "Mengrong, let's go stroll the streets."

哥哥主动拉她的手，乔梦容无比幸福，像只听话的羔羊，跟着哥哥离开茶肆。
With Prince Yi reaching for her hand on his own, bliss washed over Qiao Mengrong. Like a docile lamb, she followed him out of the teahouse.

千里寻放开文渊，喜道：“就该这样，有那么好的妹妹，要珍惜，不要吃着碗里的，还瞧着锅里。”
Qianli Xun sat up from Wenyuan's shoulder in delight. "That's how it should be! When you've got such a sweet girl, cherish her—don't eat from your own bowl while eyeing the pot."

乔文仙不解：“什么意思？”
Qiao Wenxian blinked in confusion. "What do you mean?"

“没什么，我们也去逛吧！”千里寻不便告诉文渊，她牵起乔文仙的手，也离开茶肆。
"Nothing. Let's go stroll too!" Unable to explain to Wenyuan, Qianli Xun took Qiao Wenxian's hand and led him out of the teahouse as well.

喜兰早已跟上郡主。
Xilan had already hurried after the princess.

南双一直观察苦荞与白风，更确定他们两个关系不一般。
Watching Kuqiao and Bai Feng the whole time, Nan Shuang grew all the more certain there was something special between those two.

白风舍不得苦荞，仪王要离开，他必须跟着，只得舍苦荞而去。
Bai Feng hated tearing himself away from Kuqiao, yet with Prince Yi leaving, he had to follow and could only leave her behind.

夏果顾不上别人，一双眼睛围着乔公子转。
Xiaguo paid no attention to anyone else; her eyes revolved around Young Master Qiao alone.

主人们起身要走，苦荞、夏果和南双都跟上。
When their masters rose to leave, Kuqiao, Xiaguo, and Nan Shuang all followed behind.

苦荞走得最快，赶上白风。
Kuqiao walked fastest of all and soon caught up to Bai Feng.

千里寻懒得管她，今日本就是放假，她想如何便如何。
Qianli Xun did not bother reining her in; today was a holiday anyway, so let her do as she pleased.

夏果则一直贴身跟着自家小姐，如同贴身跟着乔公子一般。
Xiaguo, meanwhile, stuck close to her mistress's heels, which felt just like staying right beside Young Master Qiao.

跟前是成双成对的人，周围走过的也是成双成对之人，尤其是白风跟苦荞暗生情愫，搞得南双心里直痒，思忖着是喜兰好还是夏果好。
Ahead of him walked couples in pairs, and all around him passersby strolled in pairs too; above all, watching Bai Feng and Kuqiao blossom with quiet affection made Nan Shuang's heart itch. He weighed his options: was Xilan better, or Xiaguo?

很快，他想明白，喜兰是郡主的丫鬟，他根本够不着，还是夏果吧，再说夏果比喜兰生得好看。
Before long he sorted it out: Xilan was the princess's maid, way out of his league. Better to go with Xiaguo. Besides, Xiaguo was prettier than Xilan anyway.

南双拉住夏果：“夏果姐姐，你走慢些，我有话对你讲。”
Nan Shuang tugged Xiaguo's sleeve. "Sister Xiaguo, slow down. I have something to tell you."

夏果盯着乔公子的背影，不耐烦道：“有什么话？赶紧说。”
Eyes glued to Young Master Qiao's back, Xiaguo snapped with impatience, "What is it? Spit it out."

南双害羞道：“夏果姐姐陪我过七夕节吧？”
A shy blush crept up Nan Shuang's cheeks. "Sister Xiaguo, spend Qixi Festival with me, won't you?"

夏果反问道：“这不正陪着你吗？”
Xiaguo shot back, "Aren't I walking with you right now?"

南双解释道：“我的意思是，你跟我能不能像白风跟苦荞姐姐一样互相喜欢？”
Nan Shuang explained, "What I mean is, could you and I like each other the way Bai Feng and Sister Kuqiao do?"

“你个小孩子，瞎想什么呢？还是想着怎么好好伺候你家公子吧。”夏果说完，甩下南双，追上二位主人，继续站在小姐身旁，观察乔公子。
"You little kid, what nonsense is running through your head? Worry about waiting on your master instead!" With that, Xiaguo shook Nan Shuang off, caught up to their two masters, and resumed walking beside her mistress while watching Young Master Qiao.

南双也赶上去，一脸不高兴，望着前面白风与苦荞高兴的样子，十分羡慕。
Nan Shuang hurried after her with a sulky scowl, gazing with deep envy at how happy Bai Feng and Kuqiao looked up ahead.

千里寻牵着文渊的手，跟在仪王与佳和郡主身后。注意到仪王放开佳和郡主的手时，她心中的失落不比乔梦容少。
Holding Wenyuan's hand, Qianli Xun walked behind Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe. When she noticed Prince Yi let go of Princess Jiahe's hand, the pang of disappointment in her chest was no smaller than Qiao Mengrong's.

千里寻从心底希望仪王能全心全意对待佳和郡主，希望他们幸福地生活在一起。她认为只有充满美好希望的祝福，才是感谢仪王的最佳方式。
From the bottom of her heart, Qianli Xun hoped Prince Yi would devote himself to Princess Jiahe with all his heart and that the two of them would share a happy life together. To her mind, offering a blessing filled with sincere goodwill was the finest way to thank Prince Yi.
