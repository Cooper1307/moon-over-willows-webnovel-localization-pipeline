## Chapter 337: Burying Mengrong and Probing the Prince's Manor

Stunned by Bai Feng's *Thunder Chain Kicks*—a move his own master hadn't even taught him yet!—and learning the beaten young man was Emperor Zhao Ji's thirteenth son **Prince Yi**, Wanyan Chi laughed wildly and called off the kill: *"Live to tell your emperor to roll out the surrender scroll!"*—ignoring Shen Jianchen's hoarse roar to release Qianli Xun.

The moment Wanyan Chi marched off, Bai Feng broke down sobbing the devastating blow: Mengrong and Xilan were dead!

Limping arm in arm back to the quiet house, Shen Jianchen wept silently over Mengrong's peaceful, faintly smiling face—forgiving every past sin and swearing to bring her bones home to Lin'an once the storm settled. Next day, buying two coffins and hiring bearers with generous silver, they marched past jeering Jin sentries (waved out under Wanyan Chi's taunting order) to bury both women outside the east wall.

After a simple meal at a peasant hut, Shen Jianchen and Bai Feng masked up at nightfall, scaled Yanjing's walls, interrogated and killed a lone patrolman to locate Wanyan Chi's manor, and searched every single courtyard room—even spotting Wanyan Chi asleep hugging another woman—without finding a single trace of Qianli Xun!

Intercepting a midnight official carriage leaving the yamen, Shen Jianchen flashed his royal token to **Prefect Cai Jing of Yanjing**—who wept bitterly over being forced to surrender by Guo Yaoshi and ushered Prince Yi inside.
