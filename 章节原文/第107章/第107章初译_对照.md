第107章 提亲之事被搁置
## Chapter 107: The Marriage Proposal Called Off

乔家本已做好提亲准备，一大早起来忙活，聘礼在院内摆得整整齐齐，族中许多年轻人也赶来，准备代表乔家一起到朱家提亲。
The Qiao household had prepared everything for the proposal, rising at crack of dawn to get busy. Betrothal gifts stood arrayed in neat rows across the courtyard, and young clansmen had gathered to accompany the family to Zhu Manor.

乔文仙也一大早赶回乔府，今日是他与淑真的好日子，必须跟家人前去。
Qiao Wenxian had hurried back to Qiao Manor at dawn too—today was the big day for him and Shuzhen, and he wouldn't miss going with his family for the world.

正当一切准备就绪，刚要出发时，米氏从外面回来，行色匆匆，说有要事相告，便将乔守仁与钱氏请到屋内，让丫鬟把门关上。
Just as everything stood ready for departure, Concubine Mi rushed back from outside in a fluster, claiming she had urgent news. She ushered Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian indoors and had a maid shut the door.

乔文仙很纳闷，到底发生何事？会不会影响他与淑真的好事？
Qiao Wenxian frowned in puzzlement. What on earth had happened? Would it disrupt his happy day with Shuzhen?

屋内，乔守仁批评道：“你唱的是哪一出？难道嫌我不让你跟着一起去朱府？都给你解释过，你去不合适。”
Inside, Qiao Shouren snapped, "What kind of stunt are you pulling now? Sulking because I wouldn't let you come to Zhu Manor? I already explained it isn't proper for you to go."

米氏说：“老爷也太小瞧我，哪是因为此事？一大早，我身边的丫鬟说，朱家姑娘在外面名声大臭，全城人都在议论她，只因乔家到朱家提亲的消息很多都听说，不敢将听到的告诉我们。我本不相信，出去转了一圈儿，一转才知道，大街小巷茶馆酒肆都在谈论她。”
Concubine Mi retorted, "Master thinks too little of me! It's nothing like that! First thing this morning, my maid told me Miss Zhu's name is mud out on the streets—the whole city is gossiping about her! Because everyone heard the Qiao family was proposing to the Zhus, nobody dared tell us. I didn't believe it, so I went out for a turn myself, and sure enough, every lane, teahouse, and tavern is buzzing about her!"

钱氏担忧道：“大家如何谈论朱家姑娘？”
Madam Qian gasped in alarm, "What are they saying about Miss Zhu?"

“可难听了！”米氏便将听到的细说一遍。
"Pure filth!" Concubine Mi laid out every word she had heard.

乔守仁听后，脸色变得难看，一言不发。
Qiao Shouren's face darkened to ash, and he said not a word.

钱氏说：“来了那么多族人，也没听大家说什么。”
Madam Qian said, "So many clansmen showed up today, yet none of them breathed a word."

“姐姐与老爷等着。”米氏起身，打开门，对丫鬟说，“去，叫位族中的晚辈，再把公子唤来。”
"Wait right here, Sister and Master." Concubine Mi rose, opened the door, and instructed a maid, "Go fetch one of the younger clansmen, and summon Young Master too."

没过多久，乔文仙与一位族中的晚辈一起前来，一进门，门又被丫鬟从外面关上。
Moments later, Qiao Wenxian walked in with a young kinsman, and the maid shut the door behind them.

乔文仙不明所以，问道：“父亲、母亲、娘，你们为何躲在屋里？不赶快去朱家提亲？”
Baffled, Qiao Wenxian asked, "Father, Mother, Mama—why are you hiding indoors instead of setting off for Zhu Manor to propose?"

长辈们没有回话，任由乔文仙站在那里。
None of the elders replied, leaving Qiao Wenxian standing there.

族中晚辈大约十来岁，见此情景，问道：“大伯可是听说了关于朱家姑娘的传言？”
The young kinsman, barely in his teens, sized up the room and asked, "Has Uncle heard the rumors about Miss Zhu?"

乔文仙感觉出不对：“传言，什么传言？”
A cold chill struck Qiao Wenxian. "Rumors? What rumors?"

晚辈说：“文渊哥哥难道不知？朱家姑娘写了一句‘和衣睡倒人怀’传遍临安，大家都在议论。”
The boy said, "Brother Wenyuan doesn't know? Miss Zhu wrote a line—'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'—and it's spread all over Lin'an. Everyone's talking about it."

乔文仙笑道：“我当是什么，这句词我知道，淑真写时我就在身边，她是写给我的。”
Qiao Wenxian laughed. "I thought it was something serious! I know that line—I was right beside Shuzhen when she wrote it. She wrote it for me!"

“啪”的一声，桌子被乔守仁拍响：“如此放荡的女子！岂能嫁到乔家？”
With a sharp crack, Qiao Shouren slammed the table. "Such a wanton woman! How could she ever marry into the Qiao family?!"

乔文仙急道：“父亲，淑真不就是写了一句词？您何须以此评判她的人品？再说她是跟我在一起时写下的，前前后后儿子最清楚不过……”
Qiao Wenxian panicked: "Father, it's just one line of ci! How can you judge her character from that? Besides, she wrote it while with me—I know every detail of what happened..."

米氏说：“儿子，还不止这些，你是没出去听听，大家都怎么议论她？难听死了，我都开不了口。”
Concubine Mi broke in: "Son, that's not the half of it! You haven't gone out to hear how folk are tearing her apart—it's so vile I can't even repeat it!"

乔守仁问族中晚辈：“外面对朱家姑娘的议论，族中人可知？”
Qiao Shouren turned to the young kinsman: "Do the rest of the clan know what folk are saying about Miss Zhu?"

晚辈点点头：“尽知。”
The boy nodded. "Every single one."

乔守仁质问道：“为何不告诉我？害我差点儿铸成大错！”
Qiao Shouren barked, "Why didn't anyone tell me?! You almost let me make a disastrous mistake!"

晚辈小心解释：“大伯家是皇亲国戚，朱家老爷又是临安知府，又听说，仪王也从中牵线搭桥，谁敢在大伯面前说这些？”
The boy explained in a small voice, "Uncle's house is imperial kin, Master Zhu is the Prefect of Lin'an, and everyone heard Prince Yi acted as go-between. Who would dare bring that up to Uncle?"

乔守仁用凝重的语气说：“你去吧，告诉大家，乔家向朱家提亲之事取消，让大家回去吧。”
In a grim voice, Qiao Shouren ordered, "Go out and tell everyone the proposal to the Zhu family is canceled. Send them all home."

晚辈还未退出，乔文仙着急道：“爹！都说好的事，岂能反悔？”
Before the boy could leave, Qiao Wenxian cried out, "Father! You already gave your word—how can you go back on it?!"

乔守仁大声吼道：“有什么不能反悔的？难道你想把一个品行不端的女子娶回家？”
Qiao Shouren roared, "Why can't I go back on it?! Do you want to bring a woman of loose morals into this house?!"

晚辈吓得赶紧开门退出，又小心把门从外面带上。
Terrified, the boy slipped out the door and shut it behind him.

“爹难道连仪王的面子都不给？”乔文仙想用仪王再次说服乔守仁。
"Father wouldn't even give Prince Yi face?" Qiao Wenxian tried playing Prince Yi's card again.

“仪王的面子我当然要给，可仪王劝说我时，流言未起，若当时听到流言，我舍了这条老命，也绝不同意你娶朱家姑娘！”
"Of course I give Prince Yi face—yet when Prince Yi persuaded me, these rumors hadn't broken out! Had I heard them back then, I would have staked my old life before letting you marry Miss Zhu!"

昨晚乔文仙高兴得睡不着，一大早又兴奋地起来赶回乔府，期待着提亲之事。本以为万无一失，谁知会发生这等事。
Last night Qiao Wenxian had lain awake from pure joy, leaping up at dawn to hurry home for the proposal. He had thought it was a done deal—never dreaming it would crash down like this.

乔文仙跪下：“父亲，您别相信那些流言，儿子与淑真交好，她是什么样的人我最清楚，一定是有人诋毁她……”
Qiao Wenxian dropped to his knees. "Father, don't believe those rumors! I know Shuzhen better than anyone—someone must be slandering her..."

乔守仁哪里肯听？厉声说：“诋毁？苍蝇不叮无缝的蛋！她没问题，别人能平白无故议论她？我看还是她有问题！你回别居去吧，不要再跟朱家姑娘来往，若不听，我就把你锁在乔府，直到帮你挑好姑娘，成了亲再放你出来。”
Qiao Shouren would hear none of it, snapping back: "Slandering her? Flies don't buzz around a seamless egg! If she's spotless, why would folk talk about her out of nowhere? Clearly the fault lies with her! Go back to the retreat and cut off all contact with Miss Zhu. Disobey me, and I'll lock you inside Qiao Manor until we pick another bride and get you wed!"

乔文仙求道：“母亲！娘！你们帮我说说话，劝劝爹，淑真她是好姑娘！”
Qiao Wenxian begged, "Mother! Mama! Say a word for me—persuade Father! Shuzhen is a good girl!"

米氏决定将流言的内容说给儿子听，希望他死心：“儿子，你可知道外头怎么议论朱家姑娘？”
Determined to make her son give up, Concubine Mi asked, "Son, do you even know what people outside are saying about Miss Zhu?"

乔文仙继续表明态度：“我不管别人怎么议论，只要我知道她好便可。”
Qiao Wenxian stood firm: "I don't care what outsiders say—all that matters is that I know she's good."

米氏果断说道：“你既是如此态度，我不妨说出来让你听听，外面人都在议论她跟林家和离前就与野男人暗送秋波，红杏出墙，当众睡到男人怀里，才写下‘和衣睡倒人怀’，还说她是母老虎，才逼得林家答应和离，啧啧啧，你听听，乔家怎能把这样的女子娶回来？”
Concubine Mi hardened her heart: "If that's your attitude, then hear it for yourself! Everyone out there says she traded secret glances with other men and had affairs before divorcing Lin Jue, sprawled in a man's arms in public to write 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed,' and acted like a tigress to force the Lin family into divorce! Tsk, listen to that—how could the Qiao family ever marry a woman like that?"

“全都是胡说！娘千万别信！”
"Pure lies! Don't believe a word of it, Mama!"

米氏劝道：“儿子，朱家姑娘成亲后，你就离开临安，你回来后，她很快和离，你当然什么都不清楚。乖，别闹了，听你爹的话，不要再跟朱家姑娘来往。”
Concubine Mi urged, "Son, you left Lin'an right after Miss Zhu wed, and she divorced right after you returned—how could you know what happened? Be good, stop making a scene, listen to your father, and stay away from Miss Zhu."

“母亲，您替儿子说说话，劝劝爹。”
"Mother, please put in a word for me with Father!"

钱氏叹口气：“唉，文渊，本来因朱家姑娘和离过一次，我们三人都不同意你跟她的事，仪王帮你出面，他的面子总是要给的。你也看到，本也要上门提亲，可谁知朱家姑娘名声如此差，怪只怪你与她不是一家人，进不了一家门，还是放弃吧。”
Madam Qian let out a sigh. "Wenyuan, none of us three wanted this match in the first place since she had been divorced. We only went along to give Prince Yi face. You saw we were ready to go propose, yet who knew her reputation was this foul? Blame fate—you two aren't meant to be under one roof. Let her go."

三位长辈口径一致，乔文仙清楚自己再怎么努力也无济于事。
With all three elders united against him, Qiao Wenxian saw no amount of pleading would move them.

他站起来，拜道：“父亲、母亲、娘，我这就回幽林别居，可你们别想让我与其他姑娘成亲，儿子这辈子非淑真不娶！”
Rising to his feet, he bowed. "Father, Mother, Mama—I'll return to Youlin Retreat right now. Don't dream of making me marry anyone else, though. In this life, I'll marry Shuzhen or no one at all!"

乔文仙说完，推门而出。
With that, he flung open the door and strode out.

聘礼还摆在院内，人已散去，喜庆的场面就像根本没发生一般。乔文仙觉得他一定是在做梦，如若不是，为何感觉如此不真实。
The betrothal gifts still stood lined up in the courtyard, yet the guests had dispersed, leaving the festive display looking like a mirage. Qiao Wenxian felt trapped in a nightmare—how else could it feel so unreal?

直到媒婆出现在面前：“乔公子，提亲之事真的取消了？”
Only when the matchmaker appeared before him—"Young Master Qiao, is the proposal really called off?"—did reality crash home.

乔文仙才清醒过来，抓住媒婆的手臂：“你是媒人，最会说合，快进去帮我说说，让父亲到朱家提亲。”
He gripped the matchmaker's arm. "You're a matchmaker, skilled at smoothing things over—go inside and help convince my father to propose to the Zhu family!"

媒婆说：“我前几日还在想，要不要把朱家姑娘被人议论之事告诉乔老爷，一直没敢开口，还好他知道得及时，否则这媒我做着都不安心。”
The matchmaker replied, "For days I debated whether to tell Master Qiao about the gossip surrounding Miss Zhu, never daring to speak up. Thank goodness he found out in time, or my conscience wouldn't have let me sleep arranging this match."

“没想到连你也这么认为，我这就去大街上听听，看谁在造谣！”乔文仙的内心就被浇了一盆凉水，他放开媒婆的手臂，冲出乔府。
"Even you believe it? I'll go out on the streets right now and see who's spreading these lies!" Cold water doused Qiao Wenxian's heart. Releasing her arm, he bolted out of Qiao Manor.

南双担心公子出事，跟着跑出去。
Worried for his master, Nan Shuang chased after him.

媒婆正要进屋问个究竟，乔守仁正好出来。
Just as the matchmaker turned to head inside, Qiao Shouren stepped out.

“麻烦你去趟朱府，告诉知府大人，朱家姑娘名声不好，乔家高攀不上。”
"Trouble yourself to go to Zhu Manor and tell the Prefect that with Miss Zhu's reputation in tatters, the Qiao family cannot presume to aim so high."

媒婆为难道：“只怕知府大人会打我板子……”
The matchmaker hesitated: "What if the Prefect has me beaten with boards?"

“你只传个话，知府大人再生气，也得讲究一个‘理’字，尽管去，银子少不了你的，文渊往后的婚事还需你多费心。”乔守仁掏出一锭银子，“你的辛苦费。”
"You're only delivering a message—furious as the Prefect may be, he still listens to reason. Go on, you'll be paid well, and I'll still need your help with Wenyuan's future match." Qiao Shouren tossed her an ingot of silver. "For your trouble."

媒婆接过银子，满口应下：“我这就去朱府，定然把话传到。”
Catching the silver, the matchmaker agreed at once. "I'll head straight to Zhu Manor and deliver your message."

离开乔府，南双见自家公子连马车都不坐，只顾往人多的地方走，边走边说：“都是胡说！哪有人议论淑真？肯定是你们不想上门提亲，才胡乱找出理由！”
Outside Qiao Manor, Nan Shuang watched his master shun the carriage and stride toward the busiest streets, muttering as he walked: "All lies! Who's gossiping about Shuzhen? They just didn't want to propose and cooked up an excuse!"

南双上前拽住公子：“公子，我们回去吧。”
Nan Shuang hurried ahead to tug his sleeve. "Young Master, let's go back."

乔文仙双手抓住南双，晃着他的肩膀：“南双，你告诉我你从来没有听到别人议论淑真……”
Qiao Wenxian seized Nan Shuang by both shoulders and shook him. "Nan Shuang, tell me you've never heard anyone gossiping about Shuzhen!"

“我整日跟公子在一起，公子没听到过，我也没听到过，我要是听到，肯定会告诉公子。”
"I'm with you all day, Young Master—if you haven't heard it, neither have I! If I had, I'd have told you."

“你瞧，街上这么多人，大家连自己的生活都忙不过来，哪有人有闲工夫议论他人？……”
"Look at all these people on the street! Everyone's busy enough making a living—who has spare time to gossip about others..."

话还未说完，一边有个买菜的妇人买完菜，边付钱边说：“最近听说没？知府家千金的事儿？”
Before he could finish, a woman buying vegetables nearby handed over her coins and said, "Heard the latest about the Prefect's daughter?"

卖菜的妇人回道：“怎么没听说，这条街无人不知。”
The vegetable vendor replied, "Who hasn't? Everyone on this street knows!"

买菜的妇人说：“我一直以为大家闺秀既有修养，又有品德，没想到知府家的千金跟瓦子里那些女子没什么区别。”
The buyer said, "I always thought highborn ladies had refinement and virtue—turns out the Prefect's daughter is no better than those women in the entertainment quarters."

“是啊，她叫什么来着？对！朱淑真！”卖菜的妇人摇摇头，“名字倒起得好，只可惜一点儿都不贤良淑德，谁娶她谁倒霉。”
"Right? What's her name again? Ah, Zhu Shuzhen!" The vendor shook her head. "Nice name, shame she hasn't an ounce of wifely virtue. Whoever marries her is in for misery."

一个老头儿过来批评道：“你们这些妇人，凑到一起就知道瞎胡议论，小心知府大人听到，把你们拉去打板子。”
An old man walked over and scolded them: "You women gather together and wag your tongues nonstop—careful His Lordship the Prefect hears and hauls you off for a beating!"

买菜的妇人说：“知府大人哪会来这种卖菜的地儿？我等小民说些闲话，他才不会知道。”
The buyer scoffed, "When does the Prefect ever set foot in a vegetable market? He'll never hear a word of our chatter."

“老光棍，你还有脸说我们，昨个，你还说把朱家千金说得越臭越好，没人要，你就上门提亲，到时知府大人会把他的女儿当废弃的菜叶子一样扔给你，正好便宜你，呵呵……！”卖菜的妇人拿起一片腐烂的菜叶，在老光棍面前晃晃。
"You old bachelor, what nerve lecturing us! Just yesterday you said the fouler her name gets, the better—when nobody wants her, you'll go propose, and the Prefect will toss his daughter to you like wilted greens! Cheap bargain for you, haha!" The vendor waved a rotting cabbage leaf under the old bachelor's nose.
