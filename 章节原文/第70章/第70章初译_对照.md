第70章 裁缝铺内表真情
## Chapter 70: Chapter 70: A Confession in the Tailor Shop

来到西湖北岸，下了船，南双请示道：“公子乘坐的马车离这里不远，我先过去把食盒放好，再到裁缝铺找您。”
乔文仙点头同意后，南双小跑着离开。
离裁缝铺还有一段路，乔文仙是男子，全身湿透，衣服贴在身上倒无伤大雅，可淑真是女子，令他看着心疼。
他四下瞅瞅，正好发现一位姑娘身上披着薄如羽翼的披风。
“淑真，你在这里等会儿，我去去就来。”
千里寻还未点头，文渊已从她身边离开，朝一位姑娘走去。
千里寻感到伤心，小声嘀咕：“文渊看到美女舍我而去，难道他认识那位姑娘？”
苦荞也搞不懂乔公子要做什么。
只见文渊走到那姑娘跟前，先行礼，然后对那姑娘说着什么。又见那姑娘开始摇头，直到文渊指了指淑真，又指了指另一个方向，说了些话，那女子才点头，将披风取下，送到文渊手中。
看到这一幕，千里寻才恍然大悟，原来文渊是为她借披风。
文渊回来后，将披风为她披上，一股暖流涌入心头，她身上的冷意荡然无存。
文渊牵起她的手，他们穿过弯弯曲曲的小路，来到景区之外，一家裁缝铺出现在面前。
正要过去时，苗知瑜与闺中密友从裁缝铺刚好出来。
苗知瑜见乔公子拉着幽栖居士的手，二人甚是亲密，本想装作没看到，从一旁拐走，一位闺秀偏偏说：“你们瞧，那是乔公子与幽栖居士，手都拉到一起，看来二人在一起真实不虚。”
苗知瑜只好上前打招呼：“乔公子，幽栖居士，你们二人身上的衣裳怎么湿了？”
苗知瑜主动前来搭话，千里寻担心对方伤心，想要抽回手。
文渊却将她的手抓得更紧，向苗知瑜解释道：“我与淑真乘船赏湖景，不慎与其他船只相撞，我们两个就成了落汤鸡。”
千里寻接着解释：“听说这里有家裁缝铺，我们过来看看，买身成衣换上。”
苗知瑜强颜欢笑：“这家裁缝铺的布料质地不错，成衣的款式也好看，二位忙，知瑜还要与闺中密友继续逛。”
“苗姑娘请便。”乔文仙继续牵着淑真向铺子内走去。
Stepping ashore along West Lake's northern bank, Nan Shuang bowed for instructions. "Your carriage is parked not far from here, Young Master. I'll go stow the food carrier first, then meet you at the tailor shop."

苗知瑜一阵心酸，面向乔文仙的背影，不由喊了一声：“乔公子！”
At Qiao Wenxian's nod, Nan Shuang jogged off.

乔文仙站住，回过头：“苗姑娘还有事？”
The tailor shop still lay a walk away. As a man, having soaked clothes cling to his frame didn't bother Qiao Wenxian, yet seeing Shuzhen drenched through made his heart ache. Scanning around, he spotted a young woman wearing a gossamer-thin cloak. "Wait right here a moment, Shuzhen. I'll be right back."

千里寻也跟着站住，并未回头，她不想看到苗知瑜失望的表情，仅用耳朵仔细听着。
Before Qianli Xun could even nod, Wenyuan walked away straight toward the stranger. A pinch of hurt pricked her chest as she muttered under her breath, "Sees a pretty girl and walks right off on me—does he know her?" Kuqiao looked just as puzzled over what Young Master Qiao was doing.

苗知瑜说：“东方公子他们在不远处的亭子里聚会，乔公子一会儿可过去？”
Watching from afar, they saw Wenyuan bow polite to the woman and speak a few words. At first the woman shook her head, until Wenyuan gestured back toward Shuzhen, pointed down another lane, and spoke again. Only then did the woman nod, unfasten her cloak, and hand it over. Light dawned on Qianli Xun at last: Wenyuan had gone to borrow a cloak for her!

乔文仙回道：“东方兄昨日已约过我，因我与淑真有约在先，已婉言拒绝，今日我只陪淑真，什么聚会都不参加。”
Returning, Wenyuan draped the cloak across her shoulders. Instant warmth rushed straight to her heart, wiping away every last chill. Taking her hand in his, he guided her along winding garden paths out past the scenic grounds until a tailor shop came into view.

苗知瑜本就无事，不过是想多与乔文仙搭话而已，对方表明态度，反而令她无话可搭，行礼离开，边走边回头瞧着他们二人牵手进入裁缝铺内。她叹口气，不想说一句话，继续跟随闺中密友闲逛。
Just as they stepped toward the entrance, Miao Zhiyu walked out of the tailor shop with a group of close female friends. Spotting Young Master Qiao holding Youqi Jushi's hand so close, Miao Zhiyu meant to pretend she saw nothing and slip past—until one noble lady piped up, "Look! Young Master Qiao and Youqi Jushi, holding hands right in public! Looks like those rumors of them being together are true after all!"

一位闺秀问：“知瑜，你该不会喜欢上乔公子？”
Cornered, Miao Zhiyu had no choice but to step up and greet them. "Young Master Qiao, Youqi Jushi—how did your clothes get drenched?"

苗知瑜没有回答，只当是默认。
Worried the sight might hurt Miao Zhiyu, Qianli Xun tried to slide her hand free. Instead, Wenyuan clasped her fingers even tighter and explained smooth, "Shuzhen and I went boating on the lake and bumped into another craft by mishap, turning us both into drowned rats."

另一位闺秀说：“像乔公子那样的家世、人品和容貌，谁见了不喜欢？你们敢说自己不喜欢？”
Qianli Xun followed up, "Heard there's a tailor shop nearby, so we came over to pick out ready-made clothes to change into."

其他闺秀都承认道：“确实喜欢！”
Forcing a bright smile, Miao Zhiyu replied, "Their fabrics are fine and the cuts look lovely. Go right ahead; Zhiyu still has shopping to do with her friends."

苗知瑜叹口气：“唉，只可惜，我们没有幽栖居士的福气，乔公子把幽栖居士的手拉得那么紧，仿佛怕她跑掉似的，让人看了羡慕。”
"Enjoy your outing, Lady Miao." Still holding Shuzhen's hand, Qiao Wenxian led her toward the door.

一位闺秀也叹道：“唉，我们几个只能寄希望于乔家选亲，要是有幸被选中，哪怕乔公子对我没那么好，只要在他身边，我也心满意足。”
A sharp pang pierced Miao Zhiyu's chest; gazing at his retreating back, she could not hold back from calling out, "Young Master Qiao!"

这话说到苗知瑜的心里，就目前情形来看，她只能寄希望于此。
Halting, Qiao Wenxian glanced over his shoulder. "Something else, Lady Miao?"

裁缝铺内，两人挑好成衣后，乔文仙向伙计买了两条干净的大帕子，给了苦荞一个。
Qianli Xun stopped beside him without turning around, unwilling to look at Miao Zhiyu's downcast face, listening sharp instead.

苦荞拿着帕子带小姐进入铺子里间专供客人试衣裳的地方，把小姐身上的湿衣裳脱下，用帕子帮小姐把身上的水擦干，再换上新的衣裳，又把头发重新整理一番。
"Young Master Dongfang and the others are gathering at a pavilion not far off. Will you join them later?" Miao Zhiyu asked.

当千里寻收拾完毕，从里间出来时，南双刚好回来，他负责伺候公子进里间换衣裳。
"Brother Dongfang invited me yesterday, and I already declined since Shuzhen and I had plans first," Qiao Wenxian answered clear. "Today I'm staying with Shuzhen alone; no gatherings for me."

走进一位姑娘，上前对千里寻说：“姑娘，可以把披风还给我吗？”
Miao Zhiyu had only wanted to keep him talking a moment longer; his blunt answer left nothing left to say. Curtsying to depart, she walked off glancing back over her shoulder as the pair stepped hand in hand inside the shop. Letting out a quiet sigh, she fell mute as she trailed beside her friends.

千里寻笑道：“多谢姑娘。”
"Zhiyu, don't tell me you fell for Young Master Qiao?" one girl asked.

苦荞把披风送到姑娘手中，姑娘接过，夸道：“你的夫君对你可真好。”说完那姑娘拿着披风离开。
Miao Zhiyu stayed silent, which answered plain enough.

千里寻品味着“夫君”一词，跟林觉有婚姻时，这个词对她来讲是个累赘，时刻想卸掉，现在品味起来却别有一番滋味。
"With Young Master Qiao's lineage, character, and looks, who wouldn't like him?" another girl chimed in. "Dare any of you swear you don't?"

裁缝铺的伙计看一眼放在桌上的湿衣裳，主动建议：“姑娘，你们前来逛西湖，拿着湿衣裳实在不方便，不如放在我们铺子，我找人给您洗干净，再熨烫平整了，您派人来取即可。”
The others all confessed in chorus, "We do!"

千里寻乐道：“没想到你们裁缝铺也承接洗衣店的业务。”
Miao Zhiyu sighed again. "Pity none of us have Youqi Jushi's luck. Saw how tight Young Master Qiao gripped her hand, as if terrified she'd slip away? Makes anyone green with envy."

伙计说：“这年头只要客人需要，又能赚银子，何乐而不为？”
Another young lady echoed with a sigh, "All we can pin our hopes on is the Qiao family's bride selection. If one of us gets picked, even if Young Master Qiao doesn't dote on me quite that much, just being by his side would be enough." That struck straight to Miao Zhiyu's own heart; as things stood, that bride selection was her sole remaining hope.

千里寻示意苦荞将银子给了。
Inside the tailor shop, once both had selected fresh outfits, Qiao Wenxian bought two large clean cloths from the clerk and handed one to Kuqiao. Taking the cloth, Kuqiao led her mistress into the shop's inner fitting room, helped her peel off the wet robes, dried her skin, dressed her in crisp new garments, and smoothed her hair back in place.

苦荞拿出银子，递给伙计。
The moment Qianli Xun stepped back out into the front room, Nan Shuang arrived right on cue and escorted his master inside to change. Just then a young woman walked in and approached Qianli Xun. "Excuse me, miss—may I have my cloak back?"

伙计接过问道：“那位公子的也一起算吗？”
Qianli Xun smiled warm. "Thank you so much, miss."

苦荞说：“当然。”
Kuqiao handed the cloak back. Accepting it, the girl beamed, "Your husband treats you so well!" With that, she left carrying her cloak.

伙计随口说道：“看来你们是一家人。”
Qianli Xun rolled the word "husband" around in her mind. Back when she was bound to Lin Jue, that word had felt like an iron shackle she couldn't wait to shake off; savoring it now brought an altogether different sweetness.

“一家人”又让千里寻十分享受，她希望与文渊成为一家人，忍不住想象他们婚后的情景，相亲相爱，儿女双全……
Eyeing the damp pile of garments on the table, the shop clerk offered, "Miss, touring West Lake lugging wet clothes is a hassle. Why not leave them here? I'll have them washed clean and pressed smooth, and you can just send someone to pick them up."

“淑真，你在想什么？”
Qianli Xun chuckled. "Didn't know tailor shops ran laundry service too."

文渊的声音将千里寻从想象中拉回现实。
"These days, if a customer needs it and there's silver in it, why not?"

文渊已换完衣裳，在新衣裳的衬托下，五官显得更加立体，皮肤也愈加白皙。
At Qianli Xun's glance, Kuqiao counted out silver and handed it over. Accepting the coin, the clerk asked, "Include that young master's wet clothes too?"

千里寻羡慕不已，不由伸手向文渊脸上摸去：“你的皮肤真好，得让多少女孩子嫉妒？”
"Of course," Kuqiao replied.

乔文仙抓住淑真的手：“你不必嫉妒，因为我是你的人。”
"Looks like you're all one family," the clerk remarked offhand.

直接的表白，令周围听到的人都不好意思起来。
"One family" warmed Qianli Xun straight through. Longing to build a real home with Wenyuan, she slipped into daydreams of married bliss, loving each other deep, a son and daughter running around their feet...

千里寻觉得文渊的话比美酒更令人陶醉，她在微醺的状态下，又被文渊牵着手，拉出裁缝铺。
"Shuzhen, what are you thinking about?"

一路上她只顾陶醉在“我是你的人”这句话中，时不时露出傻笑。
Wenyuan's voice pulled her back to earth. Freshly changed into new robes, his features looked even crisper, his complexion glowing pale and smooth. Envying skin like that, Qianli Xun reached right up to touch his cheek. "How is your skin this flawless? Enough to make half the girls in town jealous!"

文渊倒知趣，也不打扰她，一味地抓住她的手。
Catching her hand against his palm, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "No need to be jealous—because I belong to you."

他们又来到湖边，默默地走着，享受着二人之间此时无声胜有声的默契感。
Such an open confession made every bystander blush and look away. To Qianli Xun, his words went straight to her head stronger than fine wine; floating in a happy daze, she let him clasp her hand and lead her back out of the tailor shop. All down the lane she stayed wrapped inside "I belong to you," breaking into silly grins every few steps, while Wenyuan read her mood well enough not to interrupt, holding her hand tight.

走着走着，传来女子唱曲儿的声音，真是歌喉婉转，悦耳动听。
Reaching the lakeshore again, they strolled in comfortable silence, soaking in a harmony where words felt needless. Along the walk, a woman's clear singing floated over the breeze, sweet and lyrical.

乔文仙终于说话：“这唱词正是你写的一首《菩萨蛮》。”
At last Qiao Wenxian spoke: "Those lyrics are your 'Pusaman.'"

千里寻仔细听那唱词，果然听到：“山亭水榭秋方半，凤帏寂寞无人伴。愁闷一番新，双蛾只旧颦。……”
Listening close, Qianli Xun caught the opening lines: "Hill pavilions and waterside halls stand at mid-autumn; lone behind phoenix curtains, no one keeps me company. Fresh sorrow wells anew, while my moth brows knit in the same old frown..."

二人不由循着声音走去。他们穿过杨柳小径，面前出现一大片荷叶，有些荷花已凋谢，有些正在开放，有些还是花骨朵。离荷叶不远处是座亭子，亭子里围着些人，中间坐着一位歌伎，一边抚琴一边唱：“起来临绣户，时有疏萤度。我谢月相怜，今宵不忍圆。……”
Drawn by the music, the two followed a willow-lined path until a vast spread of lotus leaves opened ahead—some blossoms withered, some in full bloom, others tight buds. Just beyond stood a lakeside pavilion ringed with listeners, a singing girl seated at its center strumming a zither as she sang: "Rising to stand by the embroidered door, sparse fireflies drift past now and then. I thank the moon for pitying me—tonight it cannot bear to shine full." [TN: Translator's rendering]

千里寻叹道：“不知是谁谱的曲子，竟然如此好听。”
"Who set that to music?" Qianli Xun breathed in wonder. "What a gorgeous tune!"

乔文仙说：“还能是谁？当然是东方兄，他除了爱好诗词，也是谱曲高手，最喜欢为你的诗词谱曲，估计今儿谱了新曲子，才邀请大家来听。我们既然来了，不如过去打声招呼。”
"Who else? Brother Dongfang, of course," Qiao Wenxian smiled. "Beyond loving poetry, he's a master at composing melodies, and setting your lyrics to music is his favorite pastime. Must have written a new score today and invited everyone to hear it. Since we're right here, let's go say hello."

千里寻朝亭子望去，果然是东方尘泽他们，还有几位她没见过的公子。既然文渊已发话，她心甘情愿地点点头。
Glancing toward the pavilion, Qianli Xun recognized Dongfang Chenze's circle alongside several unfamiliar young gentlemen. Since Wenyuan suggested it, she nodded without hesitation.
