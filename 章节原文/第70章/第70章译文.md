## Chapter 70: A Confession in the Tailor Shop

Stepping ashore on West Lake's northern bank, Nan Shuang bowed for instructions. "Your carriage is parked not far from here, Young Master. I'll go stow the food carrier first, then meet you at the tailor shop."

At Qiao Wenxian's nod, Nan Shuang jogged off.

The tailor shop still lay a short walk away. As a man, having soaked clothes cling to his frame did not bother Qiao Wenxian, yet seeing Shuzhen drenched through made his heart ache. Scanning the bank, he spotted a young woman wearing a gossamer-thin cloak. "Wait here a moment, Shuzhen. I'll be right back."

Before Qianli Xun could nod, Wenyuan walked away straight toward the stranger. A pinch of hurt pricked her chest as she muttered under her breath, "Sees a pretty girl and walks right off on me. Does he know her?" Kuqiao looked just as puzzled over what Young Master Qiao had in mind.

Watching from afar, they saw Wenyuan bow to the woman and speak a few words. At first she shook her head, until Wenyuan gestured back toward Shuzhen, pointed down another path, and spoke again. Only then did the woman nod, unfasten her cloak, and hand it over. Understanding hit Qianli Xun at last: Wenyuan had gone to borrow a cloak for her.

Returning, Wenyuan draped the cloak over her shoulders. Instant warmth flooded her chest, chasing away every trace of chill. Taking her hand, he led her along winding paths out past the lakeside grounds until a tailor shop came into view.

Just as they neared the door, Miao Zhiyu walked out of the tailor shop with a group of close female friends. Spotting Young Master Qiao holding Youqi Jushi's hand in plain sight, Miao Zhiyu meant to pretend she saw nothing and slip past, until one young lady spoke up. "Look! That's Young Master Qiao and Youqi Jushi, hand in hand! Guess those rumors about them being together are true after all!"

Cornered, Miao Zhiyu had no choice but to step up and greet them. "Young Master Qiao, Youqi Jushi, how did your clothes get drenched?"

Worried the sight might hurt Miao Zhiyu, Qianli Xun tried to slip her hand free. Instead, Wenyuan gripped her fingers tighter and explained smooth, "Shuzhen and I went boating on the lake and bumped into another craft by mishap, turning us both into drowned rats."

Qianli Xun added, "Heard there's a tailor shop nearby, so we came over to pick out ready-made clothes to change into."

Forcing a smile, Miao Zhiyu said, "Their fabrics are fine and the cuts look lovely. Go right ahead; Zhiyu still has shopping to do with her friends."

"Enjoy your outing, Lady Miao." Still holding Shuzhen's hand, Qiao Wenxian led her toward the shop door.

A sharp pang gripped Miao Zhiyu. Gazing at his retreating back, she could not keep from calling out, "Young Master Qiao!"

Halting, Qiao Wenxian glanced back over his shoulder. "Something else, Lady Miao?"

Qianli Xun stopped beside him without turning around, unwilling to face Miao Zhiyu's downcast look, listening sharp instead.

"Young Master Dongfang and the others are gathering at a pavilion not far off," Miao Zhiyu said. "Will you go over in a bit?"

"Brother Dongfang invited me yesterday, and I already declined since Shuzhen and I had plans first," Qiao Wenxian answered clear. "Today I'm staying with Shuzhen alone. No gatherings for me."

Miao Zhiyu had only wanted to keep him talking a moment longer; his blunt answer left nothing more to say. She curtsied and walked off, glancing back over her shoulder as the two stepped hand in hand inside the shop. Letting out a sigh, she fell mute as she wandered along beside her friends.

"Zhiyu, don't tell me you fell for Young Master Qiao?" one girl asked.

Miao Zhiyu stayed quiet, which said yes plain enough.

"With Young Master Qiao's lineage, character, and looks, who wouldn't like him?" another girl chimed in. "Dare any of you swear you don't?"

The others all confessed together, "We do!"

Miao Zhiyu sighed again. "Pity none of us have Youqi Jushi's luck. Saw how tight Young Master Qiao held her hand, as if afraid she might vanish? Makes anyone envious."

Another young lady echoed with a sigh, "All we can pin our hopes on is the Qiao family's bride selection. If one of us gets chosen, even if Young Master Qiao doesn't dote on me quite that much, just staying by his side would be enough." Those words struck straight to Miao Zhiyu's heart; as things stood, that bride selection was her sole remaining hope.

Inside the tailor shop, once both chose ready-made outfits, Qiao Wenxian bought two large clean cloths from the clerk and handed one to Kuqiao. Taking the cloth, Kuqiao escorted her mistress into the shop's inner fitting room, helped her peel off the wet robes, dried her skin, dressed her in crisp new garments, and smoothed her hair back in place.

The moment Qianli Xun stepped back out into the front room, Nan Shuang arrived right on cue and escorted his master inside to change. Just then a young woman walked in and approached Qianli Xun. "Excuse me, miss. May I have my cloak back?"

Qianli Xun smiled warm. "Thank you so much, miss."

Kuqiao handed the cloak back. Accepting it, the girl beamed, "Your husband treats you so well!" With that, she left carrying her cloak.

Qianli Xun turned the word "husband" over in her mind. Back when she was tied to Lin Jue, that word had felt like a dead weight she could not wait to shed; savoring it now brought an altogether different sweetness.

Eyeing the damp garments on the table, the shop clerk offered, "Miss, touring West Lake lugging wet clothes is a hassle. Why not leave them at our shop? I'll have someone wash them clean and press them flat, and you can send a servant to pick them up."

Qianli Xun chuckled. "Didn't know tailor shops ran laundry service too."

"These days, if a customer needs it and there's silver to earn, why not?"

At Qianli Xun's glance, Kuqiao counted out silver and handed it to the clerk. Taking the coin, the clerk asked, "Include that young master's wet clothes too?"

"Of course," Kuqiao replied.

"Looks like you're all one family," the clerk remarked offhand.

"One family" warmed Qianli Xun straight through. Longing to build a real home with Wenyuan, she drifted into daydreams of married life: loving each other deep, a son and daughter running around their feet...

"Shuzhen, what are you thinking about?"

Wenyuan's voice pulled her back to earth. Freshly changed into new robes, his features looked even crisper, his complexion glowing pale and smooth. Envying skin like that, Qianli Xun reached right up to touch his cheek. "Your skin is so smooth. How many girls must envy you?"

Catching her hand in his palm, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "No need to envy me, because I belong to you."

Such a blunt confession made everyone within earshot blush and look aside. To Qianli Xun, his words went straight to her head stronger than fine wine; floating in a happy daze, she let him clasp her hand and lead her back out of the tailor shop. All down the lane she stayed wrapped inside "I belong to you," breaking into silly grins every few steps, while Wenyuan read her mood well enough not to interrupt, keeping her hand locked in his.

Reaching the lakeshore again, they strolled in comfortable hush, soaking in a bond where words felt needless. Along the walk, a woman's clear singing floated over the breeze, sweet and lyrical.

At last Qiao Wenxian spoke. "Those lyrics are your 'Pusaman.'"

Listening close, Qianli Xun caught the opening lines: "Hill pavilions and waterside halls stand at mid-autumn; lone behind phoenix curtains, no one keeps me company. Fresh sorrow wells anew, while my moth brows knit in the same old frown..."

Drawn by the music, the two followed a willow-lined path until a vast spread of lotus leaves opened ahead. Some blossoms had faded, some stood in full bloom, and others remained tight buds. Just beyond rose a lakeside pavilion ringed with listeners, a singing girl seated at its center strumming a zither as she sang: "Rising to stand by the embroidered door, sparse fireflies drift past now and then. I thank the moon for pitying me—tonight it cannot bear to shine full." [TN: Translator's rendering]

"Who set that to music?" Qianli Xun breathed in wonder. "What a lovely tune!"

"Who else? Brother Dongfang, of course," Qiao Wenxian smiled. "Beyond loving poetry, he's a master at composing melodies, and setting your lyrics to music is his favorite pastime. He must have written a new score today and invited everyone to hear it. Since we're right here, let's go say hello."

Glancing toward the pavilion, Qianli Xun spotted Dongfang Chenze's circle alongside several unfamiliar young gentlemen. Since Wenyuan suggested it, she nodded without hesitation.
