## 三、质量问题分类

### 3.1 准确性问题（Accuracy Issues）

#### 1. 称谓不一致（Inconsistent Honorifics）
- **"young master"** 用于指代林觉，但在第139行和第175行中，"姑爷" 也被译为 "young master"
  - 问题：韦嬷嬷和苦荞称林觉为"姑爷"（son-in-law/master），而夏果称其为"主君"（Lord），需区分
  - 建议：统一"姑爷"为 "the young master" 或 "master"，"主君"为 "Lord"
  - 影响范围：第139、175、205、217、223、229、235、255、355、367、373、385行

#### 2. 时间表达不地道（Unnatural Time Expression）
- **第115行**："It took half an hour by carriage" 
  - 问题："半个时辰" 应译为 "half a shichen (traditional Chinese time unit)" 或意译为 "about an hour"（古代一个时辰=2小时）
  - 建议：改为 "It took about an hour by carriage" 或 "half a shichen by carriage"
  - 影响：文化准确性

#### 3. 成语翻译过于直译（Over-literal Idiom Translation）
- **第445行**："If a person doesn't act for himself, heaven and earth will destroy him."
  - 问题："人不为己天诛地灭" 的翻译过于直译，缺乏语境解释
  - 建议：改为 "As the saying goes, 'If you don't look out for yourself, no one else will.' It's every man for himself." 或添加译者注
  - 影响：读者理解

### 3.2 风格与流畅度问题（Style & Fluency Issues）

#### 1. 重复用词（Repetitive Vocabulary）
- **"young lady"** 出现12次以上（第13、25、37、63、127、151、163、175、205、223、247、265行等）
  - 建议：部分替换为 "miss"、"my lady" 或直接使用代词 "you"，避免单调
  - 示例：第25行 "You're so good to Xiaguo" → "You treat Xiaguo so well"

- **"Aunt Wei"** 出现8次（第121、133、151、223、235、247、259、265行等）
  - 建议：在对话中使用 "Auntie" 或 "Nanny Wei"，叙述中可简化为 "the old nanny" 或 "she"
  - 示例：第241行 "Hello, Auntie" 更自然

#### 2. 长句需拆分（Long Sentences Need Splitting）
- **第175行**："At the drinking table, they earnestly persuaded the young master to agree to the divorce. The young master's attitude wasn't as firm as before. He started to be moved and told his two brothers that he planned to consider it for some time."
  - 问题：句子过长，不符合网文短句节奏
  - 建议：拆分为 "At the drinking table, they earnestly persuaded him to agree to the divorce. His attitude wasn't as firm as before. He began to waver. He told his brothers he needed time to think it over."

- **第265行**："The more she thought about it, the more dissatisfied she became. Worried that the young lady and Kuqiao would notice her dissatisfaction, she decided to walk around to relax her mood and go back to serve the young lady after her emotions subsided."
  - 建议：拆分为 "The more she thought, the more upset she became. She worried the young lady and Kuqiao would notice. So she decided to take a walk and calm down before returning to serve."

#### 3. 对话标签单调（Monotonous Dialogue Tags）
- **"said"** 出现过多（第49、61、79、139、151、163、175、205、223、229、235、247、253、259、265、283、289、301、307、313、325、337、343、355、367、373、385、391、397、409、415、421、427、433、439、445、451、457、463、469、475、481、487、493、499、505、511、517、523、529行）
  - 建议：使用多样化表达，如 "replied"、"whispered"、"exclaimed"、"added"、"murmured"、"retorted"、"insisted"
  - 示例：第49行 "Kuqiao shook her head: 'They didn't say...'" → "Kuqiao shook her head. 'They didn't say...'"

### 3.3 文化适应问题（Cultural Adaptation Issues）

#### 1. "半个时辰" 的时间单位（Time Unit）
- **第115行**：需明确是古代时间单位还是现代小时
  - 建议：改为 "about an hour" 以符合现代读者习惯，或保留 "half a shichen" 并加注

#### 2. "未时" 的时辰表达（Traditional Time System）
- **第373行**："at the hour of Wei"
  - 问题：普通读者不理解"未时"
  - 建议：改为 "around 2 p.m." 或 "in the early afternoon"

#### 3. "驷马难追" 的成语处理（Idiom Handling）
- **第481行**："A gentleman's word is final!"
  - 问题：虽然简洁，但失去了"驷马难追"的画面感
  - 建议：改为 "A gentleman's word is final! Not even four horses could drag it back!" 或简化为 "A promise is a promise!"

### 3.4 角色语调问题（Character Voice Issues）

#### 1. 韦嬷嬷的威严感不足（Insufficient Authority for Aunt Wei）
- **第127行**："You must be tired from the journey. Rest your feet first before busy work with brushes and ink."
  - 问题：语气过于温和，缺乏老练奶娘的威严
  - 建议：改为 "You must be exhausted from the journey. Rest your feet first. The brushes and ink can wait."

- **第247行**："Don't say a single word you shouldn't say, and don't do a single thing you shouldn't do."
  - 问题：重复结构显得生硬
  - 建议：改为 "Not a word out of turn, not a step out of line."

#### 2. 夏果的野心与不甘不够突出（Xiaguo's Ambition Not Prominent Enough）
- **第265行**："The more she thought about it, the more dissatisfied she became."
  - 问题：未能充分展现夏果的虚荣和不甘
  - 建议：改为 "The more she thought, the more bitter she felt. Why should she be treated this way just for being pretty?"

#### 3. 林觉的好色与傲慢（Lin Jue's Lechery and Arrogance）
- **第529行**："You're quite good-looking! Hehehe!"
  - 问题："Hehehe" 显得幼稚，不符合林觉的身份
  - 建议：改为 "You're quite good-looking! Hmph, heh heh." 或直接删除笑声，用动作描写替代
