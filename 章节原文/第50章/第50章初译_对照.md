## Chapter 50: Chapter 50: A Calm Talk of Mutual Divorce

苦荞做好竹筒饭，放进竹篮，提着回到竹屋，把饭摆好。桌上的笔墨纸砚已收拾，连做茶的工具也收了，临时布置的书桌彻底沦为饭桌。
Steaming hot bamboo tube rice packed in her basket, Kuqiao carried lunch back to the cottage and set it out—writing gear and tea utensils all packed away now, leaving the low study table pure dining table.

当小姐坐下享用美味的竹筒饭时，苦荞向周围瞅瞅，没见夏果的身影：“小姐，夏果怎么没在？”
Settling down to enjoy her meal, Kuqiao noticed Xiaguo missing: "Where's Xiaguo, Miss?"

“要回城里，我让夏果回去跟家人道别。”
"Heading back to town soon—sent her home to say goodbye to her family."

“小姐对夏果真好。”
"You spoil Xiaguo, Miss."

“我对你一样好。”
"Spoil you just as much!"

“我和夏果能跟着小姐，是几辈子修来的福气。”苦荞想起陌生公子打听千里寻之事，“对了，今日来了两个人，找苦蕨打听千里寻。”
"Serving you is lifetimes of luck for both of us!" Remembering the morning's surprise visitors, Kuqiao added: "Oh—two strangers came hunting Kujue today asking after Qianli Xun!"

千里寻停下手中的竹勺，怎么会有人知道她在另一个时代的名字？难道是申简辰？她激动地问：“问的人有没有说他是谁？”
Qianli Xun froze mid-scoop with her bamboo spoon! Who in this dynasty knew her real name?! Could it be Shen Jianchen?! Heart leaping, she blurted: "Did they say who they were?!"

苦荞摇摇头：“他们没说，我一心想着尽快打发他们离开，忘记问。”
Kuqiao shook her head: "Never said—too busy shooing them off, forgot to ask."

“他们为什么要找千里寻？”
"Why were they hunting Qianli Xun?"

“小姐给说话艺人提供书稿留的名字是千里寻，我猜那公子喜欢听说书，认为话本写得好，想要结识，才从说话艺人那里打听而来。我当然什么都没说，编个瞎话，告诉他们千里寻已离开竹林不知所踪，那位公子看上去很失落，然后就离开了。”
"You left the pen name Qianli Xun with the storyteller—bet that gentleman loved hearing the story and tracked the courier hoping to meet the author! Kept my mouth locked tight, told them Qianli Xun left the grove days ago to parts unknown! Poor guy looked crushed and walked right off."

千里寻想起在溪水旁遇到的两人：“那位公子是不是长得高高大大？身边的小厮也长得五官俊俏？”
Wait—connecting the dots to the two strangers by the mountain stream: "Tall, handsome gentleman with a sharp-looking attendant?"

“是，小姐难道见过他们？”
"Yep! Ran into them, Miss?"

千里寻瞬间接受苦荞的解释，怎么可能是申简辰？哪有这么巧的事？她又开始吃起竹筒饭：“见过，还向我问过路。”
Instantly Qianli Xun bought Kuqiao's theory—no way could that be Shen Jianchen, coincidence wasn't THAT wild! Resuming her bamboo tube rice: "Yeah—even asked me directions."

苦荞赞道：“我家小姐就是有才华，有人守在门口捡拾您写废的手稿也就罢了，才写起话本就有人亲自找上门来结识，您要是男子，肯定结交甚广，绝不比乔公子差。”
"My Miss is unstoppable!" Kuqiao beamed. "Folks camp at the gate stealing your poetry scraps, and one week writing huaben brings fans chasing you to the woods! Were you a man, you'd have friends across the whole empire—beat Young Master Qiao hands down!"

千里寻没有搭话，用心享受乡间美食，也不知结束竹林的生活回到城里等待她的又是什么。
Smiling silent, Qianli Xun savored her country meal, wondering what awaited her back in Lin'an once this peaceful month closed.

两天很快结束，林府的车夫按照约定准时在村口等候。
Two days flew by; right on schedule, Lin Manor's coachman waited at the village entrance.

苦蕨、村长，还有一些竹民前来相送。
Kujue, the village head, and a warm knot of villagers gathered to see her off.

千里寻向大家告别，走上马车，踏上回城之路。
Waving goodbye to all, Qianli Xun boarded the carriage for the road back to town.

竹林离城里不算太远，乘坐马车半个时辰即到，苦蕨步行也就一个多时辰。可对千里寻来讲，林府与竹林之间在她心里产生的距离根本无法用时间衡量。
Only an hour back to Lin'an by carriage—barely two hours on foot for Kujue—yet in Qianli Xun's heart, Lin Manor sat light-years apart from her bamboo haven.

马车到达林府，韦嬷嬷带着几个小厮已经在门口候着。等千里寻与两个丫鬟从马车上下来后，小厮们忙着上前搬东西。
Pulling up outside Lin Manor, Matron Wei stood waiting at the gate with several page boys who hustled forward to unload luggage the moment Qianli Xun and her maids stepped down.

“我已吩咐人把竹兰居打扫干净，姑娘刚回来，路途劳顿，先歇歇脚，再忙笔墨之事。”
"Had Zhulan Residence aired and scrubbed spotless—rest from the ride before touching brush or ink," Matron Wei welcomed warm.

韦嬷嬷的话令千里寻感到温馨，身边有位替她操心之人，也算在陌生的时代有所依靠。她点点头：“林觉可在？”
Having someone genuinely look out for her wrapped Qianli Xun in quiet comfort. Nodding thanks, she asked: "Is Lin Jue home?"

“姑爷还未回来，姑娘可是头一次一进门问姑爷在不在。”韦嬷嬷开着玩笑。
"Not back yet—first time in history you walked through the gate asking after Son-in-law!" Matron Wei teased.

千里寻凑近韦嬷嬷耳边，小声说：“我想快些与他谈和离之事。”
Leaning near Matron Wei's ear, Qianli Xun whispered: "Eager to bring up mutual divorce right away."

“姑娘别急，据我观察和离之事已有眉目，回屋我详细说给姑娘听。”韦嬷嬷拉起姑娘向府内走去。
"No rush—from what I hear, mutual divorce is already warming up! Come inside and I'll tell you everything." Linking arms, Matron Wei walked her in.

进入竹兰居，千里寻换件居家的衣裳，苦荞很自觉地坐在茶桌旁做茶，夏果待在旁边学习。
Inside Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun slipped into comfortable robes while Kuqiao naturally set to whisking tea with Xiaguo watching beside her to learn.

韦嬷嬷说：“夏果，你出去一下，我有事与姑娘讲。”
"Step outside a moment, Xiaguo—need a word with our young lady," Matron Wei directed.

夏果心中不满，她也是小姐的贴身丫鬟，凭什么让她出去？苦荞就可以心安理得在里面做茶？她不敢反抗，乖乖回声：“是。”
Resentment pricked Xiaguo—personal maid too, why always boot HER out while Kuqiao stayed pouring tea?! Swallowing her pride, she murmured: "Yes, Matron."

待夏果出去后，韦嬷嬷在桌旁坐下：“我回到林府，听下人说林家大爷和二爷跟姑爷在一起喝酒，酒桌上苦口婆心劝姑爷同意和离，姑爷的态度没有以前坚决，开始动心，告诉两位哥哥，打算考虑一段时日。”
Once Xiaguo stepped out, Matron Wei sat down: "Getting back to Lin Manor, I heard from servants that Eldest Master Lin and Second Master Lin sat Son-in-law down over wine and talked hard sense into him about mutual divorce! He isn't digging his heels in like before—actually swayed, told his brothers he'd think it over for a few days!"

听到这话，千里寻认为定是竹林给她带来好运，更在心里感激净光法师。
Bamboo luck REALLY worked! Qianli Xun silently sent a thousand thanks up the hill to Master Jingguang!

“希望林觉考虑的时间不要太久。”
"Hope he doesn't 'think it over' forever!"

“我还听说姑爷打算纳妾。”
"Even better—word is Son-in-law plans on taking a concubine!"

“他纳他的妾，跟我没有任何关系。”
"He can take ten concubines for all I care!"

“怎能没关系？姑爷纳妾说明他已打算放下姑娘。”
"You SHOULD care—taking a concubine proves he's finally letting you go!"

“此话怎讲？”
"How so?"

“我虽没瞧上姑爷，可也看得出他真心喜欢姑娘，宁可把商妓带到家里伤害你，也不愿意纳一房妾室。可惜你们两个不相称，在一起日子不会好过。他现在想要纳妾室，足以说明他打算放下姑娘。”
"Never cared for Son-in-law myself, yet I saw plain he truly loved you—even hauling courtesans home to spite you, he refused to take a proper concubine! Pity you two didn't fit. Now choosing to take a concubine proves he's ready to move on from you."

韦嬷嬷的解释更令千里寻高兴：“嬷嬷一会儿吩咐后厨做些好菜，再备壶好酒，我要在竹兰居宴请林觉，与他平心静气谈谈和离之事。”
Thrilled by Matron Wei's insight, Qianli Xun jumped on the opening: "Matron, order the kitchen to whip up fine dishes and good wine—I'm hosting Lin Jue right here in Zhulan Residence tonight to talk mutual divorce calm and friendly!"

“姑娘既然这么着急，我听你的，这就去安排。”
"Eager as you are, say no more—I'll arrange it at once!"

韦嬷嬷起身去忙活，走到院内，见夏果在院子里无所事事的样子，心里很不爽。
Rising to head out, Matron Wei spotted Xiaguo idling aimless in the courtyard and frowned hard.

夏果见到韦嬷嬷赶紧拜见：“嬷嬷好。”
Xiaguo bobbed a quick curtsy: "Good day, Matron."

韦嬷嬷平静而又严厉地叮嘱：“姑娘好心收你当丫鬟，你要记住这份恩情，用心伺候姑娘。现在来到城里不比乡下，说话做事要讲究分寸，不该说的一个字都不要说，不该做的一件都不要做。”
Calm yet razor-sharp, Matron Wei warned: "Remember Miss's kindness taking you in and serve with your whole heart. City life isn't a country village—watch every word and every step; never speak out of turn, never cross a line."

夏果回道：“是。”
"Yes, Matron."

韦嬷嬷又将她瞧一番，才离开竹兰居。
Giving her one last piercing look, Matron Wei strode out of Zhulan Residence.

夏果瞧着韦嬷嬷的背影，着实想不通，韦嬷嬷为何见自己总是如此严厉？她又没做错任何事，难道只因她长相出众些，就该遭受如此待遇？越想心中越不满，担心小姐与苦荞看出不满，决定到处走走散散心，等情绪疏散后再回屋伺候小姐。
Staring after Matron Wei's back, bitter resentment festered inside Xiaguo—why did that old woman treat her like dirt every single day?! Done zero wrong—just punished for having a prettier face?! Worried Miss or Kuqiao might read resentment on her face, she stepped out for a walk to cool off.

夏果出了竹兰居，走出西院，顺着林府的小径瞎逛。林府的路旁除种些地桃花外，也没种什么，景致很一般，还不如乡下。
Slipping past Zhulan Residence and out of the West Courtyard, Xiaguo drifted down Lin Manor's plain lanes—nothing but Caesarweed along the dirt paths, duller than the countryside!

夏果想着自己的容貌并不比小姐差，为何命运如此不同？她出生的家庭已不好，还被陈公子调戏，好不容易成为小姐的贴身丫鬟，又被韦嬷嬷嫌弃，真是越想越伤心，不由立住脚步，盯着地桃花悲伤起来。
Comparing herself to Miss—every bit as pretty, why was fate so wildly lopsided?! Born poor, chased by Chen Haoyu, rescued as a maid only to get bullied by Matron Wei! Eyes misting with self-pity, she paused staring down at pink Caesarweed blooms.

林觉从练兵署回来，无意中瞧见夏果的背影，以为是朱淑真，心想她终于从乡下回来。仔细一瞧，那背影身着丫鬟服，并不是朱淑真，没想到林府还有这等身姿的下人，他怎么从来没见过。上前问道：“你是新来的？”
Returning from the Military Training Office, Lin Jue caught sight of a slender silhouette from behind—thinking Zhu Shuzhen had come home at last! One step closer he saw maid's garb—who knew Lin Manor had a maid with a figure this graceful?! Stepping up, he asked: "New around here?"

夏果听到男子的声音，赶紧转过身，拜道：“我是小姐在乡下新收的丫鬟，名叫夏果。”
Spinning round at a man's voice, Xiaguo curtsied low: "Miss's new maid hired in the countryside—my name is Xiaguo."

夏果转过身的一刻，林觉看呆，这丫鬟的容貌绝不比朱淑真差，有过之而无不及，他竟然忘记说话，想起第一次见朱淑真的情景，心中也生起同样的情感。
The instant she lifted her face, Lin Jue went breathless—every ounce as breathtaking as Zhu Shuzhen, maybe even softer! Tongue-tied, that exact bolt of lightning from his very first glimpse of Zhu Shuzhen hit his chest all over again!

林觉不说话，夏果低着头不知该如何是好，看他身上的穿着，应该是林府的主人之一。沉默一会儿，夏果壮起胆子叫道：“老爷……”
Silence stretched; head bowed low, spotting fine robes telling her he was a master of the house, Xiaguo risked a timid murmur: "Master..."

“不要称呼我老爷，叫我主君。”林觉终于说话，说完转身向西院而去，
"Don't call me Master—call me Lord," Lin Jue spoke at last, pivoting brisk toward the West Courtyard.

夏果才敢抬头瞧林觉的背影，自语道：“主君？他难道是小姐的夫君？”
Daring a peek at his receding back, Xiaguo whispered: "Lord? That's Miss's husband?!"

等林觉走进西院后，夏果才回到西院的竹兰居。
Waiting till he entered the West Courtyard, Xiaguo slipped back into Zhulan Residence.

苦荞从屋里出来，见夏果走进院中，大声说：“我到处找你找不到，你去了哪里？”
Stepping out of the room, Kuqiao spotted her and called sharp: "Hunted everywhere for you—where'd you vanish to?!"

“我刚给一位洒扫的丫鬟帮忙，耽搁了一会儿。”
"Helped a sweeping maid a moment, got held up."

“你是小姐的贴身丫鬟，只需伺候小姐，洒扫的事以后不用理会。”
"You're Miss's personal maid—tend Miss alone, leave sweeping to sweeping maids!"

“是。”
"Yes."

又被苦荞嘱咐，夏果心里更不满。当着苦荞的面不敢流露太多，还是快些进屋伺候小姐。
Scolded by Kuqiao too—stinging resentment dug deeper inside Xiaguo, hidden fast behind an obedient mask as she stepped indoors.

苦荞则来到林觉屋外，向内说：“姑爷，小姐请您一会儿到竹兰居用饭。”
Meanwhile Kuqiao marched over to Lin Jue's quarters and called in: "Son-in-law, Miss invites you to Zhulan Residence for dinner shortly."

出来一位小厮：“主君让你进去回话。”
A page boy stepped out: "Lord says come inside."

林觉正坐在桌边喝茶，见苦荞进来，问道：“你们今日几时回来的？”
Sipping tea at his table, Lin Jue asked as Kuqiao entered: "What time did you get back today?"

“回姑爷，未时。”
"The Hour of the Sheep, Son-in-law."

“你家小姐身边什么时候多出一名丫鬟？”
"When did your Miss pick up a new maid?"

“回姑爷，是小姐在乡下新收的。”
"Hired her out in the countryside, Son-in-law."

“好吧，告诉朱淑真，待会儿我自会过去。”
"Fine. Tell Zhu Shuzhen I'll be over in a bit."

苦荞走后，林觉估摸着时间差不多，换过衣裳，来到竹兰居，苦荞与夏果正站在屋外伺候。
Once Kuqiao left, checking the hour, Lin Jue changed into fresh robes and walked over to Zhulan Residence, where Kuqiao and Xiaguo stood waiting outside the door.

﻿林觉瞧一眼夏果，甚觉满意，跨进屋内，一桌子菜出现在眼前，还有美酒。朱淑真坐在桌旁，面带微笑。林觉感到恍惚，难道她出去一趟想通了，想与他和好？
Casting a pleased glance at Xiaguo before stepping inside, he found a lavish table laden with dishes and fine wine—and Zhu Shuzhen seated smiling! A dizzy flash hit him: had a month away changed her mind about staying together?!

“你既不允许我进竹兰居，为何又请我前来用饭？”
"Barred me from setting foot in Zhulan Residence—why invite me to dine now?"

“此一时彼一时，很多事情会随着时间而变化，我今日请你前来，是想与你像朋友一样聊聊天儿。”
"Times change—people think clearer with space. Invited you tonight to talk things over calm, like friends."

林觉坐下，主动拿起酒壶将两人面前的酒盅斟满：“既如此，你我先饮酒，再聊。”
Taking a seat, Lin Jue lifted the wine pot and poured both cups brimful: "Friends it is—drink first, talk after!"

千里寻举起酒盅：“干！”
Raising her cup crisp: "Cheers!"

喝完酒，千里寻放下酒盅：“我在乡下一个月，认真思考你我之间的关系，你没错，我也没错，错只错在两个不合适的人硬被凑到一起……”
Setting down her empty cup, Qianli Xun spoke level and sincere: "Spent a whole month in the country thinking through us. Neither of us is evil—just two totally mismatched people shoved under one roof..."

原来她并不想与他和好，林觉在心里叹口气，继续认真听朱淑真说些什么。
Not making up after all! Suppressing a silent sigh, Lin Jue kept listening.

“如果你能娶到一位喜欢你，认可你的娘子，相信你也不会采取家庭暴力；如果我嫁给心仪之人，也应该会过得圆满。不如你放过我，不为别的，只为你自己。常言道，人不为己天诛地灭，你耗着我，对你没有任何好处。”
"Marry a wife who adores and respects you, and I bet you'd never raise a fist; marry a man who fits my heart, and I'd find peace too. Let me go—not for me, for YOUR own sake! Folks say look out for yourself first—trapping me here brings you zero joy!"

林觉“哼”了一声：“我耗着你，大不了再纳一房妾室，什么都不影响，而你到死只能待在林府。”
Lin Jue snorted: "Holding you here? Easy—I just take a concubine and live fine, while you stay stuck in Lin Manor till you die!"

“错！如果你耗着我，我会在外重新置办一处宅子，搬出林府，过自己想过的日子。到时在外面做出什么出格的事来，丢了林府的面子，可别怪我。”
"Wrong! Keep dragging your feet, and I buy my own house outside and move straight out of Lin Manor to live how I please—and whatever wild headlines I make outside shaming the Lin name will be on YOUR head!"

千里寻发现自己竟然一点儿都不生气，而对方看上去虽冷淡，却已没有以前那种令她讨厌的表情。
Funny enough, not a drop of anger heated her blood—and though Lin Jue sounded cool, that nasty bully glare had vanished too.

林觉已有和离的想法，朱淑真的话并未激起他的怒火，可总不能白白放朱淑真离开。
Already warming toward mutual divorce anyway, her blunt words didn't spark rage—yet no way was he letting her walk off empty-handed!

他将自己的酒盅斟满，又喝下一盅，爽快地说：“我放你走……”
Refilling his cup and tossing it back clean, he spoke smooth: "Fine—I'll let you go..."

千里寻开心道：“君子一言驷马难追！”
Qianli Xun lit up: "A gentleman's word stands solid!"

“听我把话说完，放你走可以，必须答应我一件事。”
"Hold on—not finished! I grant mutual divorce on ONE condition."

“尽管说。”
"Name it!"

“你新得的丫鬟不错，把她送给我，我就答应和离。”
"That new maid of yours caught my eye—hand her over to me, and I sign the mutual divorce!"

千里寻为难道：“此事需问问夏果，如若她同意，当然好说，如若她不同意，我也没办法。”
Qianli Xun hesitated: "Have to ask Xiaguo first—if she agrees, great; if she says no, my hands are tied."

“她是你的丫鬟，只要你想送给我，她不敢说一个不字。”林觉看着一桌子菜，“这么好的菜，可惜我没胃口，就不在此陪你享用。”
"She's YOUR maid—if you decide to give her to me, she can't say no!" Eyeing the untouched feast, he rose: "Fine dishes—shame I lost my appetite. Enjoy without me."

林觉起身走到门口，又将夏果瞧了一眼：“你叫夏果？”
Pausing at the threshold, he eyed Xiaguo again: "Your name is Xiaguo?"

“是，主君。”
"Yes, Lord."

“长得不错！呵呵呵！”林觉笑了一声离开竹兰居。
"Real looker! Hehehe!" Chuckling low, Lin Jue strolled out of Zhulan Residence.
