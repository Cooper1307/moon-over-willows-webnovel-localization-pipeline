# 第50章 平心静气谈和离

---

苦荞做好竹筒饭，放进竹篮，提着回到竹屋，把饭摆好。桌上的笔墨纸砚已收拾，连做茶的工具也收了，临时布置的书桌彻底沦为饭桌。

Kuqiao finished making bamboo tube rice, put it in a bamboo basket, carried it back to the bamboo house, and set out the food. The brushes, ink, paper, and inkstone on the table had been put away, and even the tea-making tools were collected. The temporarily arranged desk had completely become a dining table.

---

当小姐坐下享用美味的竹筒饭时，苦荞向周围瞅瞅，没见夏果的身影："小姐，夏果怎么没在？"

When the young lady sat down to enjoy the delicious bamboo tube rice, Kuqiao looked around and didn't see Xiaguo: "Young lady, where is Xiaguo?"

---

"要回城里，我让夏果回去跟家人道别。"

"We're going back to the city. I let Xiaguo go back to say goodbye to her family."

---

"小姐对夏果真好。"

"You're so good to Xiaguo."

---

"我对你一样好。"

"I'm just as good to you."

---

"我和夏果能跟着小姐，是几辈子修来的福气。"苦荞想起陌生公子打听千里寻之事，"对了，今日来了两个人，找苦蕨打听千里寻。"

"For Xiaguo and me to follow you is a blessing cultivated over several lifetimes." Kuqiao remembered the stranger asking about Qianli Xun. "Oh right, two people came today, looking for Kujue to ask about Qianli Xun."

---

千里寻停下手中的竹勺，怎么会有人知道她在另一个时代的名字？难道是申简辰？她激动地问："问的人有没有说他是谁？"

Qianli Xun stopped the bamboo spoon in her hand. How could anyone know her name from another era? Could it be Shen Jianchen? She asked excitedly: "Did the person who asked say who he was?"

---

苦荞摇摇头："他们没说，我一心想着尽快打发他们离开，忘记问。"

Kuqiao shook her head: "They didn't say. I was focused on getting rid of them as quickly as possible and forgot to ask."

---

"他们为什么要找千里寻？"

"Why were they looking for Qianli Xun?"

---

"小姐给说话艺人提供书稿留的名字是千里寻，我猜那公子喜欢听说书，认为话本写得好，想要结识，才从说话艺人那里打听而来。我当然什么都没说，编个瞎话，告诉他们千里寻已离开竹林不知所踪，那位公子看上去很失落，然后就离开了。"

"The name you left with the storyteller when providing manuscripts was Qianli Xun. I guess that young master likes listening to storytelling, thought the story script was well-written, and wanted to meet the author, so he asked the storyteller and came here. Of course, I didn't say anything. I made up a story and told them that Qianli Xun had already left the bamboo forest and no one knew where he went. That young master looked very disappointed and then left."

---

千里寻想起在溪水旁遇到的两人："那位公子是不是长得高高大大？身边的小厮也长得五官俊俏？"

Qianli Xun remembered the two people she met by the stream: "Was that young master tall and big? And the servant beside him also had handsome features?"

---

"是，小姐难道见过他们？"

"Yes. Have you met them, young lady?"

---

千里寻瞬间接受苦荞的解释，怎么可能是申简辰？哪有这么巧的事？她又开始吃起竹筒饭："见过，还向我问过路。"

Qianli Xun instantly accepted Kuqiao's explanation. How could it be Shen Jianchen? How could there be such a coincidence? She started eating bamboo tube rice again: "I've met them. They even asked me for directions."

---

苦荞赞道："我家小姐就是有才华，有人守在门口捡拾您写废的手稿也就罢了，才写起话本就有人亲自找上门来结识，您要是男子，肯定结交甚广，绝不比乔公子差。"

Kuqiao praised: "Our young lady is so talented. It's one thing that people wait at the door to pick up your discarded manuscripts, but now that you've just started writing story scripts, someone comes to your door in person to meet you. If you were a man, you would definitely have a wide circle of acquaintances, no less than Young Master Qiao."

---

千里寻没有搭话，用心享受乡间美食，也不知结束竹林的生活回到城里等待她的又是什么。

Qianli Xun didn't respond. She focused on enjoying the country delicacy, not knowing what awaited her in the city after ending her life in the bamboo forest.

---

两天很快结束，林府的车夫按照约定准时在村口等候。

Two days passed quickly. The Lin family's coachman waited at the village entrance on time as agreed.

---

苦蕨、村长，还有一些竹民前来相送。

Kujue, the village chief, and some bamboo villagers came to see her off.

---

千里寻向大家告别，走上马车，踏上回城之路。

Qianli Xun said goodbye to everyone, got on the carriage, and embarked on the journey back to the city.

---

竹林离城里不算太远，乘坐马车半个时辰即到，苦蕨步行也就一个多时辰。可对千里寻来讲，林府与竹林之间在她心里产生的距离根本无法用时间衡量。

The bamboo forest wasn't too far from the city. It took about an hour by carriage, and Kujue could walk it in just over two hours. But for Qianli Xun, the distance between the Lin residence and the bamboo forest in her heart couldn't be measured by time at all.

---

马车到达林府，韦嬷嬷带着几个小厮已经在门口候着。等千里寻与两个丫鬟从马车上下来后，小厮们忙着上前搬东西。

The carriage arrived at the Lin residence. Aunt Wei was already waiting at the door with several servants. After Qianli Xun and the two maids got off the carriage, the servants hurried forward to carry the things.

---

"我已吩咐人把竹兰居打扫干净，姑娘刚回来，路途劳顿，先歇歇脚，再忙笔墨之事。"

"I've instructed someone to clean Zhulan Residence. You've just come back, young lady. You must be exhausted from the journey. Rest your feet first. The brushes and ink can wait."

---

韦嬷嬷的话令千里寻感到温馨，身边有位替她操心之人，也算在陌生的时代有所依靠。她点点头："林觉可在？"

Aunt Wei's words made Qianli Xun feel warm. Having someone around who cared for her was like having a support in this unfamiliar era. She nodded: "Is Lin Jue here?"

---

"姑爷还未回来，姑娘可是头一次一进门问姑爷在不在。"韦嬷嬷开着玩笑。

"The young master hasn't returned yet. This is the first time you've asked if the young master is here as soon as you enter." Aunt Wei was joking.

---

千里寻凑近韦嬷嬷耳边，小声说："我想快些与他谈和离之事。"

Qianli Xun leaned close to Aunt Wei's ear and whispered: "I want to talk to him about the divorce as soon as possible."

---

"姑娘别急，据我观察和离之事已有眉目，回屋我详细说给姑娘听。"韦嬷嬷拉起姑娘向府内走去。

"Don't be anxious, young lady. From what I've observed, the divorce matter has some progress. I'll tell you the details when we go back to the room." Aunt Wei pulled the young lady and walked towards the residence.

---

进入竹兰居，千里寻换件居家的衣裳，苦荞很自觉地坐在茶桌旁做茶，夏果待在旁边学习。

Entering Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun changed into casual home clothes. Kuqiao sat at the tea table very consciously and made tea. Xiaguo stayed nearby to learn.

---

韦嬷嬷说："夏果，你出去一下，我有事与姑娘讲。"

Aunt Wei said: "Xiaguo, go out for a moment. I have something to discuss with the young lady."

---

夏果心中不满，她也是小姐的贴身丫鬟，凭什么让她出去？苦荞就可以心安理得在里面做茶？她不敢反抗，乖乖回声："是。"

Xiaguo was dissatisfied in her heart. She was also the young lady's personal maid. Why should she go out? Why could Kuqiao comfortably make tea inside? She didn't dare to resist and obediently replied: "Yes."

---

待夏果出去后，韦嬷嬷在桌旁坐下："我回到林府，听下人说林家大爷和二爷跟姑爷在一起喝酒，酒桌上苦口婆心劝姑爷同意和离，姑爷的态度没有以前坚决，开始动心，告诉两位哥哥，打算考虑一段时日。"

After Xiaguo went out, Aunt Wei sat down at the table: "When I returned to the Lin residence, I heard the servants say that the eldest and second young masters of the Lin family were drinking with the young master. At the drinking table, they earnestly persuaded him to agree to the divorce. His attitude wasn't as firm as before. He began to waver. He told his brothers he needed time to think it over."

---

听到这话，千里寻认为定是竹林给她带来好运，更在心里感激净光法师。

Hearing this, Qianli Xun believed that the bamboo forest must have brought her good luck, and she was even more grateful to Master Jingguang in her heart.

---

"希望林觉考虑的时间不要太久。"

"I hope Lin Jue doesn't take too long to consider."

---

"我还听说姑爷打算纳妾。"

"I also heard that the young master plans to take a concubine."

---

"他纳他的妾，跟我没有任何关系。"

"He can take his concubine. It has nothing to do with me."

---

"怎能没关系？姑爷纳妾说明他已打算放下姑娘。"

"How can it have nothing to do with you? The young master taking a concubine shows that he has already planned to let go of you."

---

"此话怎讲？"

"What do you mean?"

---

"我虽没瞧上姑爷，可也看得出他真心喜欢姑娘，宁可把商妓带到家里伤害你，也不愿意纳一房妾室。可惜你们两个不相称，在一起日子不会好过。他现在想要纳妾室，足以说明他打算放下姑娘。"

"Although I didn't think much of the young master, I could also see that he truly liked you. He would rather bring a merchant prostitute home to hurt you than take a concubine. It's a pity that you two are not a good match. Living together won't be easy. Now that he wants to take a concubine, it's enough to show that he plans to let go of you."

---

韦嬷嬷的解释更令千里寻高兴："嬷嬷一会儿吩咐后厨做些好菜，再备壶好酒，我要在竹兰居宴请林觉，与他平心静气谈谈和离之事。"

Aunt Wei's explanation made Qianli Xun even happier: "Aunt Wei, instruct the kitchen to make some good dishes in a while, and prepare a pot of good wine. I want to invite Lin Jue to dine at Zhulan Residence and talk about the divorce calmly with him."

---

"姑娘既然这么着急，我听你的，这就去安排。"

"Since you're so anxious, young lady, I'll listen to you and go arrange it right away."

---

韦嬷嬷起身去忙活，走到院内，见夏果在院子里无所事事的样子，心里很不爽。

Aunt Wei got up to busy herself. Walking into the courtyard, she saw Xiaguo doing nothing in the yard and felt very unhappy.

---

夏果见到韦嬷嬷赶紧拜见："嬷嬷好。"

When Xiaguo saw Aunt Wei, she hurriedly greeted: "Hello, Auntie."

---

韦嬷嬷平静而又严厉地叮嘱："姑娘好心收你当丫鬟，你要记住这份恩情，用心伺候姑娘。现在来到城里不比乡下，说话做事要讲究分寸，不该说的一个字都不要说，不该做的一件都不要做。"

Aunt Wei instructed calmly yet strictly: "The young lady kindly took you in as a maid. You must remember this kindness and serve the young lady with all your heart. Now that you've come to the city, it's not like the countryside. You must be measured in your words and actions. Not a word out of turn, not a step out of line."

---

夏果回道："是。"

Xiaguo replied: "Yes."

---

韦嬷嬷又将她瞧一番，才离开竹兰居。

Aunt Wei looked at her again before leaving Zhulan Residence.

---

夏果瞧着韦嬷嬷的背影，着实想不通，韦嬷嬷为何见自己总是如此严厉？她又没做错任何事，难道只因她长相出众些，就该遭受如此待遇？越想心中越不满，担心小姐与苦荞看出不满，决定到处走走散散心，等情绪疏散后再回屋伺候小姐。

Xiaguo watched Aunt Wei's back, really unable to understand why Aunt Wei was always so strict with her. She hadn't done anything wrong. Was it just because she was somewhat better-looking that she should suffer such treatment? The more she thought, the more bitter she felt. Why should she be treated this way just for being pretty? Worried that the young lady and Kuqiao would notice her dissatisfaction, she decided to take a walk and calm down before returning to serve.

---

夏果出了竹兰居，走出西院，顺着林府的小径瞎逛。林府的路旁除种些地桃花外，也没种什么，景致很一般，还不如乡下。

Xiaguo left Zhulan Residence, walked out of the west courtyard, and wandered along the paths of the Lin residence. Besides some ground peaches planted along the roads, there wasn't much else planted. The scenery was very ordinary, not even as good as the countryside.

---

夏果想着自己的容貌并不比小姐差，为何命运如此不同？她出生的家庭已不好，还被陈公子调戏，好不容易成为小姐的贴身丫鬟，又被韦嬷嬷嫌弃，真是越想越伤心，不由立住脚步，盯着地桃花悲伤起来。

Xiaguo thought that her appearance was no worse than the young lady's. Why was their fate so different? The family she was born into was already not good, and she was even teased by Young Master Chen. She had barely become the young lady's personal maid, and now she was disliked by Aunt Wei. The more she thought about it, the sadder she became. She couldn't help but stop and stare at the ground peaches in sorrow.

---

林觉从练兵署回来，无意中瞧见夏果的背影，以为是朱淑真，心想她终于从乡下回来。仔细一瞧，那背影身着丫鬟服，并不是朱淑真，没想到林府还有这等身姿的下人，他怎么从来没见过。上前问道："你是新来的？"

Lin Jue returned from the military training office and accidentally saw Xiaguo's back. He thought it was Zhu Shuzhen and thought she had finally returned from the countryside. Looking carefully, the back was wearing maid's clothes and wasn't Zhu Shuzhen. He didn't expect the Lin residence to have such a graceful servant. How come he had never seen her before? He stepped forward and asked: "Are you new here?"

---

夏果听到男子的声音，赶紧转过身，拜道："我是小姐在乡下新收的丫鬟，名叫夏果。"

Hearing a man's voice, Xiaguo quickly turned around and bowed: "I'm the new maid the young lady took in in the countryside. My name is Xiaguo."

---

夏果转过身的一刻，林觉看呆，这丫鬟的容貌绝不比朱淑真差，有过之而无不及，他竟然忘记说话，想起第一次见朱淑真的情景，心中也生起同样的情感。

The moment Xiaguo turned around, Lin Jue was stunned. This maid's appearance was no worse than Zhu Shuzhen's, and even better. He actually forgot to speak. Remembering the scene of meeting Zhu Shuzhen for the first time, he felt the same emotion arise in his heart.

---

林觉不说话，夏果低着头不知该如何是好，看他身上的穿着，应该是林府的主人之一。沉默一会儿，夏果壮起胆子叫道："老爷……"

Lin Jue didn't speak. Xiaguo lowered her head, not knowing what to do. Judging by his clothes, he should be one of the masters of the Lin residence. After a moment of silence, Xiaguo plucked up her courage and called out: "Master..."

---

"不要称呼我老爷，叫我主君。"林觉终于说话，说完转身向西院而去，

"Don't call me Master. Call me Lord." Lin Jue finally spoke. After finishing, he turned and walked towards the west courtyard.

---

夏果才敢抬头瞧林觉的背影，自语道："主君？他难道是小姐的夫君？"

Only then did Xiaguo dare to look up at Lin Jue's back and said to herself: "Lord? Could he be the young lady's husband?"

---

等林觉走进西院后，夏果才回到西院的竹兰居。

After Lin Jue walked into the west courtyard, Xiaguo returned to Zhulan Residence in the west courtyard.

---

苦荞从屋里出来，见夏果走进院中，大声说："我到处找你找不到，你去了哪里？"

Kuqiao came out of the room and saw Xiaguo walking into the courtyard. She said loudly: "I've been looking for you everywhere and couldn't find you. Where did you go?"

---

"我刚给一位洒扫的丫鬟帮忙，耽搁了一会儿。"

"I just helped a maid with sweeping and was delayed for a while."

---

"你是小姐的贴身丫鬟，只需伺候小姐，洒扫的事以后不用理会。"

"You're the young lady's personal maid. You only need to serve the young lady. You don't need to worry about sweeping and cleaning from now on."

---

"是。"

"Yes."

---

又被苦荞嘱咐，夏果心里更不满。当着苦荞的面不敢流露太多，还是快些进屋伺候小姐。

Being instructed by Kuqiao again, Xiaguo was even more dissatisfied. She didn't dare to show too much in front of Kuqiao and hurried into the room to serve the young lady.

---

苦荞则来到林觉屋外，向内说："姑爷，小姐请您一会儿到竹兰居用饭。"

Kuqiao came outside Lin Jue's room and said inside: "Young master, the young lady invites you to dine at Zhulan Residence in a while."

---

出来一位小厮："主君让你进去回话。"

A servant came out: "Lord lets you go in and reply."

---

林觉正坐在桌边喝茶，见苦荞进来，问道："你们今日几时回来的？"

Lin Jue was sitting at the table drinking tea. Seeing Kuqiao come in, he asked: "What time did you come back today?"

---

"回姑爷，未时。"

"Replying to the young master, at the hour of Wei."

---

"你家小姐身边什么时候多出一名丫鬟？"

"When did an extra maid appear beside your young lady?"

---

"回姑爷，是小姐在乡下新收的。"

"Replying to the young master, it's a new maid the young lady took in in the countryside."

---

"好吧，告诉朱淑真，待会儿我自会过去。"

"Alright. Tell Zhu Shuzhen that I'll go over there in a while."

---

苦荞走后，林觉估摸着时间差不多，换过衣裳，来到竹兰居，苦荞与夏果正站在屋外伺候。

After Kuqiao left, Lin Jue estimated that the time was about right, changed his clothes, and came to Zhulan Residence. Kuqiao and Xiaguo were standing outside the room waiting.

---

林觉瞧一眼夏果，甚觉满意，跨进屋内，一桌子菜出现在眼前，还有美酒。朱淑真坐在桌旁，面带微笑。林觉感到恍惚，难道她出去一趟想通了，想与他和好？

Lin Jue glanced at Xiaguo and was very satisfied. Stepping into the room, a table full of dishes appeared before him, along with fine wine. Zhu Shuzhen sat at the table with a smile on her face. Lin Jue felt dazed. Had she figured things out after going out and wanted to reconcile with him?

---

"你既不允许我进竹兰居，为何又请我前来用饭？"

"Since you don't allow me into Zhulan Residence, why are you inviting me to dine here?"

---

"此一时彼一时，很多事情会随着时间而变化，我今日请你前来，是想与你像朋友一样聊聊天儿。"

"Times change. Many things will change with time. I'm inviting you here today because I want to chat with you like friends."

---

林觉坐下，主动拿起酒壶将两人面前的酒盅斟满："既如此，你我先饮酒，再聊。"

Lin Jue sat down and took the initiative to pick up the wine pot and fill the wine cups in front of both of them: "Since that's the case, let's drink first and then chat."

---

千里寻举起酒盅："干！"

Qianli Xun raised her wine cup: "Cheers!"

---

喝完酒，千里寻放下酒盅："我在乡下一个月，认真思考你我之间的关系，你没错，我也没错，错只错在两个不合适的人硬被凑到一起……"

After drinking, Qianli Xun put down her wine cup: "During my month in the countryside, I seriously thought about the relationship between you and me. You're not wrong, and I'm not wrong either. The only mistake is that two unsuitable people were forced together..."

---

原来她并不想与他和好，林觉在心里叹口气，继续认真听朱淑真说些什么。

So she didn't want to reconcile with him after all. Lin Jue sighed in his heart and continued to listen carefully to what Zhu Shuzhen had to say.

---

"如果你能娶到一位喜欢你，认可你的娘子，相信你也不会采取家庭暴力；如果我嫁给心仪之人，也应该会过得圆满。不如你放过我，不为别的，只为你自己。常言道，人不为己天诛地灭，你耗着我，对你没有任何好处。"

"If you could marry a wife who likes you and approves of you, I believe you wouldn't resort to domestic violence either. If I marry someone I like, I should also live a fulfilling life. You might as well let me go. Not for any other reason, but just for yourself. As the saying goes, 'If a person doesn't act for himself, heaven and earth will destroy him.' Keeping me around has no benefit for you at all."

---

林觉"哼"了一声："我耗着你，大不了再纳一房妾室，什么都不影响，而你到死只能待在林府。"

Lin Jue snorted: "If I keep you around, at most I'll take another concubine. Nothing will be affected, while you can only stay in the Lin residence until you die."

---

"错！如果你耗着我，我会在外重新置办一处宅子，搬出林府，过自己想过的日子。到时在外面做出什么出格的事来，丢了林府的面子，可别怪我。"

"Wrong! If you keep me around, I'll buy a new residence outside and move out of the Lin residence to live the life I want. If I do something outrageous outside at that time and lose the Lin residence's face, don't blame me."

---

千里寻发现自己竟然一点儿都不生气，而对方看上去虽冷淡，却已没有以前那种令她讨厌的表情。

Qianli Xun discovered that she wasn't angry at all, and although the other person looked cold, he no longer had the expression that she used to find disgusting.

---

林觉已有和离的想法，朱淑真的话并未激起他的怒火，可总不能白白放朱淑真离开。

Lin Jue already had the idea of divorce. Zhu Shuzhen's words didn't arouse his anger, but he couldn't just let Zhu Shuzhen go for nothing.

---

他将自己的酒盅斟满，又喝下一盅，爽快地说："我放你走……"

He filled his wine cup again, drank another cup, and said straightforwardly: "I'll let you go..."

---

千里寻开心道："君子一言驷马难追！"

Qianli Xun said happily: "A gentleman's word is final!"

---

"听我把话说完，放你走可以，必须答应我一件事。"

"Let me finish my sentence. I can let you go, but you must agree to one thing."

---

"尽管说。"

"Just say it."

---

"你新得的丫鬟不错，把她送给我，我就答应和离。"

"Your newly acquired maid is quite good. Give her to me, and I'll agree to the divorce."

---

千里寻为难道："此事需问问夏果，如若她同意，当然好说，如若她不同意，我也没办法。"

Qianli Xun said with difficulty: "This matter needs to be asked to Xiaguo. If she agrees, of course it's easy to say. If she doesn't agree, there's nothing I can do."

---

"她是你的丫鬟，只要你想送给我，她不敢说一个不字。"林觉看着一桌子菜，"这么好的菜，可惜我没胃口，就不在此陪你享用。"

"She's your maid. As long as you want to give her to me, she won't dare say no." Lin Jue looked at the table full of dishes. "Such good dishes. It's a pity I don't have an appetite, so I won't stay here and enjoy them with you."

---

林觉起身走到门口，又将夏果瞧了一眼："你叫夏果？"

Lin Jue got up and walked to the door, then glanced at Xiaguo again: "Your name is Xiaguo?"

---

"是，主君。"

"Yes, Lord."

---

"长得不错！呵呵呵！"林觉笑了一声离开竹兰居。

"You're quite good-looking! Hehehe!" Lin Jue laughed and left Zhulan Residence.
