## Chapter 135: Parting Ways Beneath the Divine Willow

雪下到半夜才停，望月轩内积了厚厚一层，一早起来，下人们忙着清扫道路上的积雪。
Snow fell past midnight before ceasing, laying a thick white blanket across Wangyue Pavilion. At dawn, servants set to sweeping the garden paths clear.

韦嬷嬷知道姑娘内心苦，吩咐苦荞和夏果不要催促她起床，想躺多久就躺多久，只需将饭食按时端进去，以防姑娘饿了想吃。
Knowing her mistress's heartache, Matron Wei told Kuqiao and Xiaguo not to wake her—let her lie abed as long as she wanted, just keep warm meals coming in case hunger struck.

千里寻早已醒来，只是不想起床，不想让大家看到她红肿的眼泡。昨日一躺在床上不断告诫自己：“不要哭，不要哭，千万不要哭。”还是不由自主哭起来。
Qianli Xun had been wide awake for hours, refusing to get up so nobody would see her puffy, swollen eyes. All night under the quilt she had ordered herself—"Don't cry, don't cry, whatever you do don't cry"—and sobbed her eyes out anyway.

第一次投入感情，如此结局，到底哪里做错？
Her very first real dive into love, ending like this—what on earth had she done wrong?

她自认为没有错，只怪散播流言之人，若抓住此人，定要将他暴揍十日，不！整整一年，以泄心头之恨！
Not a single thing! All blame belonged to the rat behind those rumors—catch him and she would pummel him ten days straight, no, a whole year straight!

千里寻开始怀疑净光法师所说，她坚持正思正念，自认行为无误，为何还会招来如此非议？搞得她与文渊无法在一起。
Doubt crept in over Master Jingguang's teaching: she had clung to Right Thought and Right Mindfulness, walking clean and straight—why still get smeared until she and Wenyuan were ripped apart?

是不是人的命运背后有一双无形的手在操纵？如果有，千里寻想要抓住它，将那双手变为自己的，然后将命运掌握在自己手中。
Was some invisible hand pulling the strings of destiny? If so, Qianli Xun wanted to grab that hand, make it hers, and seize control of her own fate.

今日虽不下雪，天气却阴沉，积雪依然悄悄融化，给人的感觉比昨日还冷。
Though snow had stopped, leaden clouds hung low and melting snow made the air feel even colder than yesterday.

千里寻躺在被窝里也觉出一丝冷意，睡得肌肉酸痛，她爬起来，掀开床幔，向火盆望了望，火烧得很旺，难道这丝冷意来自内心？
A chill crept right under her quilt; aching from lying too long, she sat up, parted the bed curtains, and saw the brazier glowing bright red—so the chill came from inside her own chest.

得到确证后，千里寻不想再睡下去，被窝的暖意根本无法驱散她内心的寒冷，继续躺着又有何用？
Realizing no warm quilt could thaw the frost inside her, lying there felt pointless.

她唤了声：“苦荞！”
She called out: "Kuqiao!"

苦荞听到呼唤，端着刚做好的茶，进到里屋，见小姐双眼红肿，立刻明白她昨晚定是哭了许久。
Hearing her, Kuqiao hurried into the inner chamber carrying fresh hot tea—and one look at those puffy red eyes told her Miss had cried half the night.

“小姐醒了，先喝盏茶，刚刚做好，还热着。”
"You're awake, Miss. Drink a bowl of hot tea first—just whisked."

千里寻接过茶盏，苦荞则将床幔向两边挂好。
As Qianli Xun took the tea bowl, Kuqiao hooked back the bed curtains on both sides.

一盏热茶下肚，体内暖暖的，刚才的寒意消失一些。未时还要与文渊相见，需鼓足精神。
Hot tea slid down, warming her core and pushing back the chill. She had to face Wenyuan at the hour of wei—time to pull herself together.

苦荞接过茶盏，放在一边，取来衣裳给小姐穿上，边穿边说：“刚才端过来的饭已凉，一会儿厨房会端来热的饭食，我伺候小姐梳洗完，您吃过饭再出去不晚。”
Taking the empty bowl, Kuqiao brought fresh robes and helped her dress: "The morning meal went cold, so the kitchen will send hot dishes right over. Wash up, eat something, and we'll head out with plenty of time."

千里寻才知她赖在床上的时间太长，只怪天阴，搞得她忘记时间。
Only then did Qianli Xun realize how late she had lain in bed, the overcast sky hiding the hour.

梳洗完后，要求苦荞为她施粉黛，想以此遮盖红肿的双眼。
After washing up, she had Kuqiao apply powder to mask her swollen eyes.

苦荞细心将粉黛施好，却一点儿用处也没有。
Careful as Kuqiao brushed it on, the puffiness refused to hide.

“你去厨房找个煮熟的鸡蛋，要热的。”
"Run to the kitchen and bring back a hot hard-boiled egg."

“是。”苦荞明白小姐的意思，跑去厨房拿鸡蛋。
"Right away!" Catching on, Kuqiao darted off.

没多久怀里揣着热鸡蛋回来，拿在手中，温度合适后，分别在小姐的两只眼睛上放置片刻。
Returning with a piping-hot egg tucked inside her sleeve, Kuqiao tested the heat until just right, then held it in gentle turns over each of her mistress's closed eyelids.

千里寻闭上眼，享受鸡蛋带给她的温度，温暖而舒适。她能感觉到双眼因痛哭淤积的血液一下子活跃开来，向周围扩展。
Eyes shut, Qianli Xun drank in the comforting warmth, feeling the congested puffiness loosen and fade.

“小姐，这方法真管用，您的眼睛周围一下子好多了。”苦荞说着收了鸡蛋。
"Works like magic, Miss—the swelling around your eyes went right down!" Kuqiao put the egg aside.

千里寻向铜镜里瞧瞧，确实恢复如初。
Checking the bronze mirror, Qianli Xun saw her eyes looked normal again.

“挑个颜色鲜艳的唇脂。”
"Pick out a bright lip rouge."

千里寻担心面对文渊时，她会流露出痛苦的表情，也许鲜艳的色彩会掩饰部分情绪。
Afraid pain might bleed onto her face before Wenyuan, maybe bold color would mask the cracks.

苦荞挑选好鲜艳的红色给小姐涂在唇上。
Kuqiao chose a vivid crimson and painted her mistress's lips.

厨房的丫鬟将饭食端来，在桌上摆好，再将放凉的饭食搁入盘中，行完礼离开。
Kitchen maids brought fresh hot dishes, laid the table, cleared the cold platters, and bowed out.

尽管昨晚没吃晚饭，今日一早也没吃早饭，却一点儿饥饿感都没有。千里寻看向冒着热气的饭食，毫无食欲。
Despite skipping both dinner last night and breakfast today, not a hint of hunger stirred; the steaming dishes held zero appeal.

她决定多少吃些，只怕到时没有力气说出伤害文渊的话。
Still, she had to force some down—she would need strength to speak the cruel words ahead.

她走到桌前，拿起筷子吃起来，每一口如同嚼蜡，还是努力吃着，第一次感受到吃饭竟然也有艰难的时候。
Sitting down, she lifted her chopsticks and forced down bite after bite, every mouthful tasting like dry wax—learning for the first time how hard swallowing food could be.

将饭菜吃过一半，实在吃不下去，也该到出发的时间。穿上暖和的披风，握好手炉，带上苦荞往出走。
Halfway through, she could not stomach another bite, and the hour had come. Putting on her warm winter cape and clutching her hand warmer, she stepped out with Kuqiao.

刚到院中，夏果从丫鬟房出来：“小姐！也带我去吧？”
Out in the yard, Xiaguo popped out of the maids' quarters: "Miss! Take me along too!"

千里寻没有说话，只顾向外走。夏果自然跟上，她想乔公子想得发疯，今日有机会见到，岂能放过。
Saying nothing, Qianli Xun walked straight on, and Xiaguo hurried right after—wild with longing for Young Master Qiao, refusing to miss a single chance to lay eyes on him.

街市的积雪已被清扫到道路两旁，做小买卖的依然冒着寒冷叫卖，闹市的繁华并未受到天气影响。
Snow stood piled along both edges of the city streets as hawkers braved the freezing air, market bustle undimmed by winter.

与往日唯一不同的是街道两边的房屋和树上铺上一层厚厚的雪，整个城市银装素裹。
Only the thick white blankets capping every roof and bare bough turned Lin'an into a silver-and-jade landscape.

千里寻没有心情欣赏城市的雪景，她只觉天气再寒冷也冷不过她的内心。
Qianli Xun had zero eye for snow scenery; no winter frost could match the cold inside her chest.

……
...

到达山脚下，走下马车，脚下是厚厚的积雪。这种天气，上山的人少，积雪上没有留下多少脚印。
Stepping down from the carriage at the foot of the hill, deep snow met her boots. Few folk climbed the hill in weather like this, leaving the white drift near spotless.

千里寻手握暖炉，脚踩在积雪上，发出咯吱咯吱的声音。她一路听着声音向神柳处走去，山脚下的枯草被雪覆盖，不见踪影，高一些的植物也因冬季天气寒冷收敛起来。她只觉视线也变得开阔，远远的，已看到神柳。
Clutching her hand warmer, snow crunched beneath every step as she walked toward the Divine Willow. Dead grass lay buried deep, bare shrubs huddled tight, opening a clear vista—and far ahead stood the Divine Willow.

眼前白茫茫的一片，连神柳也穿上白色的冬装，从远处望去，分外妖娆。
Amid unbroken white, the willow wore its own cloak of frost, stark and breathtaking against the winter sky.

一片白色中，有一个红色的身影显得鲜艳夺目。那身影，千里寻再熟悉不过，自幽林别居分开，它不断出现在她的脑海。
And against that sea of white stood one vivid crimson figure—a silhouette Qianli Xun knew down to her bones, haunting her mind ever since Youlin Retreat.

她缓慢向身影走去，既想见又怕见。
Slowing her steps as she drew near, she both ached to reach him and dreaded reaching him.

乔文仙专门穿上红色的厚披风，他想让自己显得气色好些，不管淑真今日对他说什么，他都希望在她面前表现出最好的一面。
Qiao Wenxian had chosen his thick crimson cape on purpose to lend color to his pale face—whatever Shuzhen had to say today, he meant to show her his best self.

南双看到走来的三个人影，在雪地里十分显眼。
Nan Shuang spotted three silhouettes moving toward them, standing out stark against the white snow.

“公子，幽栖居士来了。”
"Young Master, Youqi Jushi is here."

乔文仙看似观察神柳上的积雪，心无时无刻不在身后，听到淑真到来，他还是兴奋地转过身，爱还在，见到爱人，无法不兴奋。
Though seeming to study snow-laden willow branches, Qiao Wenxian's whole soul had been tuned behind him. Hearing she had arrived, he spun round with an irrepressible rush of joy—love still burned, impossible to hide!

他忍不住迎上去，想要像上次一样抱住她，可淑真却向后退去。
Stepping forward to pull her into his arms like last time, he stopped dead as Shuzhen stepped back.

他失望地放下手臂：“淑真，我……我很想你……”
His arms dropped empty: "Shuzhen, I... I missed you so much..."

千言万语，乔文仙不知该如何表达。
A thousand words surged in his throat, none able to cross his lips.

文渊消瘦许多，尽管一脸笑容，衣着鲜艳，也难掩一脸憔悴。千里寻感到心疼，一段感情若无法给两个人带来幸福感，放弃也许是最好的选择。
Wenyuan had wasted so thin; bright robes and a forced smile could not hide the hollow weariness in his face. Pain twisted inside Qianli Xun—when love brought neither soul peace, letting go was the kindest cut.

“文渊，你真傻，不值得为我拿自己的性命开玩笑。以后要吃好，喝好，把自己照顾好。你看我，今日要来见你，反而吃得更多。”
"How foolish you are, Wenyuan—gambling your own life over me isn't worth it! Eat well, drink well, and look after yourself from now on. Look at me: knowing I'd see you today, I ate extra!"

苦荞在心里叹道：“小姐怎么说瞎话？她明明吃得不多。”
Kuqiao sighed inside: "Why lie, Miss? You ate half a bowl at best!"

夏果满心满眼都在乔公子身上，他比上次见到时瘦了整整两圈，看得夏果心酸不已。
Eyes glued solely to Young Master Qiao, Xiaguo felt her heart twist seeing him two sizes thinner.

南双站在公子身后，盯着夏果看，她的美貌跟幽栖居士不相上下，如果能喜欢上自己那该多好。
Standing behind his master, Nan Shuang stared at Xiaguo—pretty enough to rival Youqi Jushi! If only she would look his way...

淑真故作轻松的语气和神态，让乔文仙感到不安。
Shuzhen's breezy tone sent dread rippling through Qiao Wenxian.

“淑真，我知道你怨我，与苗家的亲事是父母背着我定下，我不得已才绝食逼他们退婚……”
"Shuzhen, I know you're angry—my parents arranged the Miao betrothal behind my back! I only starved myself to force them to cancel it..."

“文渊！苗知瑜是好姑娘，又有才华，人品也好，在我被人唾骂时，她还能替我说话，你娶她，我……我也放心……”
"Wenyuan! Miao Zhiyu is a fine girl—talented and good-hearted. Even when the whole city cursed me, she stood up for me! Knowing you'll marry her puts my mind at ease..."

“你为何要对我说这样的话？我想娶的人是你！你一直都知道！……淑真……”乔文仙抓住淑真握着暖炉的手，“我带你离开临安，我们去天涯海角，去哪里都行，只要能跟你在一起，在哪里都无所谓。”
"Why say things like that?! You're the one I want to marry—you've always known that! Shuzhen..." Catching her hands wrapped around the hand warmer, Qiao Wenxian pleaded, "Come away from Lin'an with me! To the ends of the earth, anywhere—as long as I'm with you, nothing else matters!"

这一刻，千里寻真的想答应，可理智再一次战胜情感，她抽回手，继续放在暖炉上，用平静的语气说道：“恐怕你以后会后悔。”
For one wild heartbeat Qianli Xun almost said yes—then reason clamped down hard. Pulling her hands back onto her hand warmer, she kept her voice flat: "You'd regret it sooner or later."

淑真抽回手，乔文仙更担心，他不想听到拒绝的话，果断说：“我永远不会后悔！”
Her pulled-back hands sent panic surging through him; refusing rejection, he swore, "I'll never regret it!"

千里寻装作冷笑的样子：“文渊，我今日才发现你是个极其自私的人，只顾自己开心，你有没有想过，离开后你的父母该怎么办？苗知瑜该怎么办？”
Qianli Xun forced a cold laugh: "Just realizing today how selfish you are, Wenyuan—thinking only of your own happiness! Did you ever stop to think what happens to your parents if you run off? Or to Miao Zhiyu?"

乔文仙靠近淑真，用微红的双眼盯着她：“那你说我们该怎么办？”
Stepping closer with reddened eyes locked onto hers: "Then tell me—what are we supposed to do?!"

千里寻不敢再看文渊，怕自己忍不住答应与他私奔。她狠心走向一边，瞧着神柳，从神柳的枝桠上落下一团雪，与地上的雪融为一体。
Unable to hold his gaze lest she cave and run off with him right now, Qianli Xun turned aside toward the Divine Willow, watching a clump of snow slip from a bough and vanish into the drift below.

“文渊，我们分手吧。”
"Let's break up, Wenyuan."

还是听到分开的话，乔文仙闭上双眼，两滴泪从眼角落下，他转过头，瞧向淑真：“我不同意！”
Hearing the word he dreaded most, Qiao Wenxian squeezed his eyes shut as two tears rolled down, then whipped his gaze back to her: "I don't agree!"

千里寻努力挤出笑容：“真可笑，你有什么资格不同意？你连婚约都有，难道想让我嫁给你做妾室不成？”
Qianli Xun forced a thin smile: "Hilarious—what right do you have to disagree? You're an engaged man! Expect me to marry into your house as your concubine?"

“你可愿意？”乔文仙充满期待，目前只有这个办法能解决所有问题。
"Would you?!" Hope flared sharp in his voice—it was the one bridge left!

千里寻努力做着心理建设，拒绝文渊是她今日来此的目的。感觉到自己的眼神足够坚定时，才大胆面向文渊：“我这辈子即使不嫁人，孤独终老，也绝不给人当妾室。”
Steeling every ounce of resolve—ending things clean was why she came today—she waited until her gaze felt cold as steel before facing him square: "Even if I never marry and grow old all alone, I will never be anyone's concubine."

乔文仙被刺痛，他失去理智：“淑真，你等着，我现在就去苗家退婚！”
Stung raw, reason snapped inside Qiao Wenxian: "Wait right here, Shuzhen—I'm marching straight to the Miao house to break off the betrothal right now!"

乔文仙说完，就往池塘对岸走去，南双不得已跟上，想要去扶自家公子，却被他用手甩开。
Spinning on his heel, he started off toward the far side of the pond. Nan Shuang hurried after to steady him, only to get shoved away.

池塘周围被雪覆盖，池塘里的水已结成厚厚的冰，冰封住水底的生命，也冰封住乔文仙的爱情。
Snow ringed the pond while thick ice sealed its surface—freezing the water beneath and freezing Qiao Wenxian's love along with it.

当文渊在雪中踉跄着靠近池塘岸边时，千里寻大喊道：“文渊！没用的！且不说你退不了婚，即使退了，我也不会嫁给你！”
As he stumbled across the snow toward the far bank, Qianli Xun cried out behind him: "It's useless, Wenyuan! Never mind you can't break it off—even if you did, I still wouldn't marry you!"

乔文仙停下踉跄的脚步，痛苦地转过身，大声问：“为什么？你为什么要说出这么狠心的话？”
His stumbling steps froze. Whipping back in raw agony, he shouted, "Why?! How can you say something so cruel?!"

千里寻努力抬起脚，踩着雪，靠近文渊：“因为……因为我不想为自己的幸福让他人痛苦，你若退婚，你的家人会痛苦，苗知瑜会更痛苦。……谢谢你文渊，谢谢你带给我一段美好的爱情，现在……是该结束的时候，你我……一别两宽，各自安好。”
Lifting heavy boots through the snow, Qianli Xun walked closer: "Because... I refuse to build my happiness on other people's pain! Break that betrothal and your family breaks, and Miao Zhiyu breaks even worse. Thank you, Wenyuan—thank you for giving me a beautiful romance. Now... it's time to end it. Let us part clean and wish each other peace."

千里寻说完，毅然转过身，泪如雨下，她控制住不去抽泣，怕文渊看到她颤抖的背影。
Turning away before he could answer, tears poured down her face like rain; she locked her shoulders tight against sobs so he would not see her back shaking.

两个丫鬟目睹自家小姐痛苦的表情，也是心如刀割。尤其是夏果，既为小姐，又为乔公子。小姐要离开，她只能与苦荞跟上。
Watching their mistress break apart tore at both maids—above all Xiaguo, aching for Miss and Young Master Qiao alike. When Miss walked away, they had no choice but to follow.

乔文仙听到“一别两宽，各自安好”的话语，再也忍不住，跪在雪地里，痛哭起来。
Hearing "part clean and wish each other peace" broke the last dam inside Qiao Wenxian: he collapsed to his knees in the snow and sobbed his heart out.

千里寻的背后留下文渊悲伤的哭声……
Behind Qianli Xun echoed Wenyuan's ragged, heartbroken weeping...
