## Chapter 237: The Servants Hold a Strategy Meeting

Watching his retreating back and glancing at the roses on the desk, Kuqiao murmured, "Miss... do you think Young Master really fell for you?"

Qianli Xun glared at the blooms. "Don't buy one word of it! Pure scheme—too bad it won't work on me! Think a handful of flowers and a creepy grin buys him a free pass? Not a chance!"

"Still, he looked like he was actually enjoying himself, not suffering at all..."

Qianli Xun tapped her chin. "Means I'm going too easy on him. Need to cook up something tougher."

Half agreeing, half doubtful, Kuqiao held her tongue as Qianli Xun snapped, "Toss those roses out."

Hugging the celadon vase, Kuqiao said, "Too pretty to toss! If you don't want them, I'll put them in Yuening Room."

Setting the roses in her room, Kuqiao stepped back outside—and Chouya rushed up grabbing her sleeve. "Sister Kuqiao, let's talk over something!"

"What?"

"Huge business!" Chouya whispered.

"Spill it or I'm leaving."

Leaning to her ear, Chouya breathed, "About Miss and Young Master's future happiness!"

Any talk of her mistress's happiness hooked Kuqiao immediately. "Where?"

"West Courtyard."

"Hang on—let me tell Miss first so she doesn't wonder where I ran off to."

"Meet you there!" Chouya dashed away.

Inside, seeing her mistress already buried in her huaben again, Kuqiao excused herself briefly and hurried to the West Courtyard. Headed toward Qin'an Lodge, she spotted Chouya waving from the doorway of the servants' dining hall.

"Thought it was just us two," Kuqiao laughed walking over. "Clearly not!"

"Everyone's coming except the three men Matron Wei took on errands."

"Funny—since when are you rallying the troops for Miss's happiness?"

Chouya blushed bright red. "You know why, Sister Kuqiao! Miss's happiness decides mine—Qinggua swore he won't be with me until Young Master and Miss make up!"

"Thought you were pure selfless—turns out you've got skin in the game!" Kuqiao teased, drawing a shy grin from Chouya.

Stepping across the threshold into the hall with its three dining tables, Kuqiao found two male servants already seated who jumped up chorusing, "Greetings, Sister Kuqiao!"

"Jiaxiang and Fulu—early birds! Where's the rest?"

"Sister Hongdou went rounding them up—right behind us!" Chouya answered just as Hongdou, Amei, Qinggua, and Asheng walked in.

Without shyness, Chouya pulled Qinggua onto the bench right beside her, leaving Qinggua watching helplessly as Hongdou settled next to Asheng.

Scanning the table, Kuqiao frowned. "Asheng and Qinggua both here—who's watching Young Master?"

"Sanbao alone is plenty," Asheng replied.

"Relax, Sister Kuqiao," Qinggua piped up. "Young Master's meek as a lamb now—Young Madam points east, he doesn't glance west! Right now he's buried deep in his books. Never once seen him study like this!"

"Wonderful!" Chouya cheered. "One little push and Miss and Young Master will bury the hatchet—then Qinggua and I can..." She trailed off blushing.

Qinggua finally realized his own excuse had sparked this whole summit—torn between rooting for his master and dreading falling into Chouya's clutches, especially seeing Hongdou smiling happily beside Asheng without giving Chouya a second glance.

"Every meeting needs a chair," Kuqiao noted. "Who leads?"

"Chouya called it—she chairs," Hongdou proposed.

Straightening up off Qinggua's shoulder—letting him breathe again—Chouya took charge. "Aside from Qinggua, we all belong to Miss. She treats us well, so we want her happy. Finding out Young Master spread those rumors, she started getting payback—ought to feel great, yet she looks gloomier than before. Sister Kuqiao knows best."

Kuqiao sighed. "Married to the guy who smeared your name—who could smile? Crucial question is helping Miss get past this hurdle. None of us want her going through a second divorce—in-laws as kind as Eldest Master and Eldest Madam are rare as gold!"

"Exact right! No second divorce!" everyone echoed.

Qinggua grinned. "Actually, Young Master and Young Madam already took a giant leap forward! Sister Kuqiao heard him confess his love to her today!"

Gasps erupted all around. "What?! Young Master confessed?!"

"True enough," Kuqiao admitted, "except Miss thinks he's faking and wants to turn up the heat. Honestly, even I can barely believe it."

"Same here!" the table chorused.

"It's for real!" Qinggua insisted. "He even confessed to her in his sleep!"

"If Qinggua says so, I believe it!" Chouya backed him up.

"I suggested he drop fighting her, use soft words, and copy Eldest Master's devotion to Eldest Madam—old Young Master would've kicked me across the room, yet he actually listened! If that isn't love, what is?"

"Guess he really did fall for her," Kuqiao conceded, thinking of the hidden weight in her mistress's heart. "Problem is, Miss doesn't feel the same right now."

Asheng cut straight to the core. "Young Master's already turned around; Miss is the key. If she falls for him too, smooth sailing for everyone!"

"Quiet as a mouse, then hits the bullseye!" Hongdou praised, trading a warm smile with him that made Qinggua sour: Couldn't have hit the bullseye without my setup!

Ignoring him, Hongdou snapped her fingers. "For Miss's happiness, let's help Young Master out! Starting today, keep an eye out for his good points and slip praise for him into Miss's ears every chance we get—especially you, Sister Kuqiao! Hear enough good things and her view of him will soften!"

"Will that even work?" Amei doubted. "Miss despises whoever spread those rumors—hard to let that go."

"No better plan on the table," Kuqiao sighed. "Worth a shot!"

"I'm in!" Chouya voted first, followed by unanimous nods.

Chouya summed up briskly: "Great meeting! Remember the three magic words: Praise Young Master! Qinggua, tell Young Master to step up his game so we actually have good points to praise!"

Locking eyes on Hongdou, Qinggua nodded. "Count on it."

Walking back to Boya Study with Asheng, Qinggua couldn't hold it in. "Asheng... do you like Hongdou?"

Hit square in the heart, Asheng owned up straight. "Y-yes, I do."

Bluffing bold, Qinggua muttered, "Hate to break it to you—Hongdou likes me."

Asheng blinked hard. "Wait—isn't Chouya the one who likes you? Practically glued to your arm back there!"

"Chouya liking me doesn't bar other girls from liking me! I'm still single!" Before his fib could unravel, Qinggua bolted inside Boya Study.

Left brooding outside the door, Asheng frowned: How could Hongdou like Qinggua?

Noticing his glum face, Sanbao asked, "What's eating you, Asheng?"

"Sanbao... think Hongdou likes Qinggua?"

"No way!" Sanbao scoffed instantly. "Chouya likes Qinggua—Hongdou likes me!"

Asheng's jaw dropped—stumbled straight onto a real rival! Better confess to Hongdou fast before anyone beats him to it!

Inside, Chen Haoyu sat memorizing with unprecedented focus. Pouring tea, Qinggua stood listening to classical Chinese sail clean over his head until his master slammed the book shut.

"Disappeared for ages—sneaking off with Chouya?"

"As if! If I sneak off, it's with Hongdou! Anyway, huge news, Young Master: every servant in Moxiang Courtyard just agreed to start singing your praises to Young Madam! Better bring out your A-game so they have ammunition!"

Chen Haoyu blinked. "Wait—aren't they all Zhu Shuzhen's die-hards? Why help me?"

"Because they love her and don't want her going through another divorce! Once I told them you fell for her, they want her to fall for you too!"

"Aha!" Chen Haoyu jumped up. "Come on—time to showcase my top talent right in front of Zhu Shuzhen!"

"Which talent?"

"Photographic memory!"

Stepping out of Boya Study, he waved at the two guards. "Quit shadowing me—I'm not running anywhere."

Still following out of duty—and hunting for praiseworthy traits—Asheng eyed him up and down and whispered to Sanbao, "Spot any good points on Young Master besides decent looks?"

Sanbao shook his head blankly. "Not a one."

"Does being handsome count?"

"Guess so?"

Waiting outside Suxin Thatched Hall, the guards watched Chen Haoyu stride inside—sinking for a second seeing the desk empty, then lighting up at spotting her seated at the tea table holding the bare-bottomed clay doll with a soft smile on her lips.

Stunned by how radiant she looked smiling, he thought: Gorgeous wife like this at home—why on earth did I ever roam outside?! Yesterday I cursed Zhu Xiyan; today I want to kiss his boots for handing me the Poetess of Jiangnan!

"Young Master is here," Kuqiao announced, alerting her mistress.

Ignoring Kuqiao, eyes glued to Shuzhen, he grinned. "Love clay dolls? Once I'm out I'll buy you hundreds—fill the whole hall!"

Handing the doll to Kuqiao to stow away, Qianli Xun rose and met him coldly. "Penniless as you are, buying them with what? Know your own wallet before bragging."

"Once you cool off and let me out, I'll ask Father to put me to work in the family business! Earn monthly wages and buy you all the dolls you want!"

Volunteering for family business after two days?! Shocked yet refusing to drop her guard, she fired back, "I've got more money than you—need you buying me toys? Besides, one doll is charming; filling the hall turns my home into a toy stall!"

"Mad again? My bad, all right? Cool down and sit—time to test my memorization!"

With zero temper left in him to push against, every sharp comeback jammed in her throat. Fine—let's hear him recite and shred him on the mistakes!
