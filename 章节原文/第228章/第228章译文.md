## Chapter 228: One Branch Worries While Another Laughs

While one wing fretted, inside Chen Jiangshu's quarters, his family of three laughed up a storm.

"Hahaha! Locked up tight!" Chen Haodong crowed. "No more roaming wild for Haoyu! Feels so good—why should I slave away earning silver for this house while he does nothing and spends like water? That tigress came through big-time!"

"Satisfying, yes," Chen Jiangshu mused, "yet it puzzles me why Old Madam Xia and First Sister-in-law haven't budged an inch. Haven't they heard?"

"Of course they heard!" Madam Bao said. "I was sitting right there in Old Madam's room when a maid ran in reporting it. You should've seen the looks on their faces!"

"Then why didn't they charge over to stop her? Nothing like them."

"My guess? They're terrified of the Zhu girl's fists and don't dare step in."

Chen Jiangshu shook his head. "Old Madam Xia has seen real storms in her life—no youngster scares her. Besides, didn't she march right over to make trouble when you and Third Sister-in-law egged her on last time? Wasn't scared of fists then."

"Put like that, I'm stumped too," Madam Bao admitted.

"I bet I know," Chen Haodong speculated. "The Zhu girl just won big points smoothing over relations between Grandmother and the elders, so Grandmother and First Aunt held back to save her face. Better for us if they don't step in—otherwise she might buckle under their pressure and let Haoyu out, blowing my plan."

"Makes sense," Chen Jiangshu nodded. "Only worry is they hold off today and cave tomorrow."

"Even if they try, they won't stop her," Madam Bao scoffed. "The Zhu girl's tongue is just as lethal as her fists. When Grandmother confronted her last time, dead in the wrong, she spun out argument after argument until she sounded spotless and left us speechless! Fearless as they come. Now that she knows Haoyu ruined her name, even Grandmother and First Sister-in-law combined won't talk her down!"

Father and son cracked up at Madam Bao's dead-on summary.

When the laughter eased, Chen Jiangshu praised his wife. "Spot on, my dear. Never again will I claim women are shortsighted!"

"Mother came from a scholarly family and read plenty of books," Chen Haodong chimed in. "No wonder she cuts straight to the bone."

Praised by the two men who mattered most to her, Madam Bao glowed with pride.

With the hour growing late, Chen Haodong bade his parents goodnight and returned to his own courtyard. Riding high on triumph, desire stirred in his gut; too late to head out to Chun Jinchan, he looked up at Ye Anya's darkened room. Asleep without waiting up for her husband—instant irritation flared.

He marched up and rapped hard. Knock, knock, knock!

Awake under the covers after sending Xiaocui off to bed, Ye Anya knew who stood outside and pulled the quilt over her head without stirring.

Knock, knock, knock! Still silence. Snapping, Chen Haodong kicked the door wide open, stormed past the screen in the dark, and yanked Ye Anya upright by the shoulder. "Why didn't you open the door?"

Numbed by endless beatings, Ye Anya showed zero fear. "Don't you know? Why ask?"

Smack! A hard slap cracked across her cheek. "You're my wife—supposed to wait up every night and attend me to bed! Instead you crawl under the covers early without a shred of respect! Behave half decent and I wouldn't lay a finger on you!"

Ye Anya gave a cold laugh. "Before our wedding I already heard about you cavorting with courtesans at Chunxiang Brothel. Since marrying you, the sight of you makes my stomach turn. Why would I wait on you?"

Chen Haodong shoved her flat onto the mattress and pinned her beneath him. She offered no struggle, just turned her head away, every line of her face frozen in moonlight with a chill that radiated straight through his ribs.

"I'm a man—what's wrong with playing around with a few women outside? Learn from Lin Jue's wife: gentle, dutiful, even used her own silver to buy Chun Jinqiao out—"

Spit hit his cheek square on. "Want me like that? Wait for your next life—no, not even then!"

Chen Haodong wiped the saliva off his face. "The colder you act, the more I want to claim you. I'm in a good mood tonight, so I'll go easy."

Closing her eyes, Ye Anya went numb while he forced himself on her. Not one happy night since her wedding—when would this nightmare end, and when could she be with the man she loved? Thoughts of Shuzhen rose in the dark, bold enough to haul Haoyu out of a brothel and lock him behind bars. Tomorrow she would go talk to her.

Over in Third Brother Chen Jiangmu's room, husband and wife chatted as they got ready for bed.

Removing her hairpins at the mirror, Madam Cui sighed. "Eldest Brother and Sister-in-law never should have sealed this match for Haoyu. Look at the poor boy suffering now."

Soaking his feet in a hot basin filled by a maid, Chen Jiangmu shrugged. "You know why—Prefect Zhu marched right to the gate. Eldest Brother had no choice. Married now; accept it."

Setting her ornaments in her box, Madam Cui crouched beside him and dipped her hands into the basin to wash his feet. "Still can't believe Haoyu started those rumors last year. Why on earth did he do that? Never heard of them crossing paths before."

Closing his eyes as his wife kneaded his arches, Chen Jiangmu murmured, "Haoyu roams outside day and night—who knows what trouble he gets into?"

"Thank goodness our Haofei is steady—never gave me a day of grief, nose always buried in his books."

"I'd rather he learned family trade, yet his heart's glued to scholarship."

Finishing up, Madam Cui stood, dried her hands on a towel handed by a maid, while another maid handed Chen Jiangmu a cloth to dry his feet. Once the maids withdrew and the lamp went out, both lay down.

"Haofei's two cousins each hold strong cards," Madam Cui reflected in the dark. "One charms Old Madam Xia, the other is a shark in trade. Neither you nor your son has the stomach to fight them for the family business. Studying for official exams is a smart separate path—merchants always rank beneath scholar-officials anyway. Once Haofei wins a degree, we become the parents of a court official! Talk about honor!"

Rolling over with his back to her, Chen Jiangmu murmured, "True enough. That's why I back him even if I'd prefer trade."

"Who do you think ends up running the Chen fortune?" she asked.

Chen Jiangmu yawned. "Count Haofei out."

"I mean between Haodong and Haoyu—who do you want?"

"Isn't it obvious?" he mumbled.

"You mean Haodong?" Hearing only a steady snore in answer, Madam Cui whispered to the ceiling, "As things stand, Haodong looks like a sure bet."

At dawn, Chen Haodong hurried off to the silk house, leaving Ye Anya breathing free air—wishing deep down he would roam outside forever like Haoyu. With heavy thoughts on her mind, she skipped sleeping in, ate an early breakfast, and walked to Moxiang Courtyard with Xiaocui.

Qianli Xun had just stepped to her courtyard gate, bound for Old Madam Xia's rooms to lay out her "discipline" plan, when she spotted Ye Anya approaching.

"Early visit, Eldest Sister-in-law! Must have something to talk over. My errand can wait—come chat!"

Ye Anya felt a rush of admiration at Shuzhen's resilience: furious enough yesterday to hog-tie her husband, yet bright and unclouded this morning. How did a spirit this sunny write such heartbreaking poetry? Perhaps pouring sorrow onto paper left her heart light in daily life.

"Am I interrupting anything?" Ye Anya asked hesitantly.

Taking her hand to lead her inside, Qianli Xun smiled. "Never! You're welcome anytime."

"Then let me sit in your room a moment and speak from the heart."

Sensing bedroom confidences coming, Qianli Xun led her straight into the inner parlor of Suxin Thatched Hall, where a woven rug lay under a low square tea table flanked by short stools that felt almost like sitting on the floor. Without being told, Kuqiao began whisking tea in the outer hall.

Seated, Ye Anya opened with yesterday's news. "I heard you locked Haoyu up! You have real nerve—aren't you worried Grandmother and Eldest Uncle and Aunt will chew you out?"

"If even you heard, they sure heard too," Qianli Xun observed. "Not a single one showed up at my door, which means either they know they're in the wrong, or they secretly approve."

Careless of the elders' motives, Ye Anya praised from the heart, "Bold, decisive, doing what you please—I envy you more than I can say."

Qianli Xun grinned at herself. "Don't you know folks call me a tigress behind my back?"

"Call it an insult if they like—to me it sounds like praise," Ye Anya smiled. "How many women in this world have the guts to march into a brothel, drag their husband home in ropes, and lock him up? Pure courage."

"You might be the only soul in Chen Manor who sees it that way."

Ye Anya paused, gathering every ounce of nerve. "Shuzhen, I wanted to ask your advice last time, yet felt too shy on our first real chat. Today I'm swallowing my pride to ask straight out—please don't hold back."

Remembering the embrace beneath the Divine Willow, Qianli Xun kept her tone careful. "No need to stand on ceremony, Eldest Sister-in-law. Speak your mind."

Ye Anya plunged right in. "You went through a mutual divorce once. Could you tell me how you managed it?"

Right then Kuqiao walked in carrying a tray with two bowls of whisked tea, catching the question mid-air. Hearing the dead seriousness behind it, the scene beneath the Divine Willow flashed through her mind. Awkward at overhearing, she set both bowls on the table without a word.

"Kuqiao, wait in the outer hall and keep everyone out," Qianli Xun ordered.

Ye Anya nodded to Xiaocui, who stepped outside behind Kuqiao.

Studying the raw hope in Ye Anya's eyes, Qianli Xun weighed her words with care—unwilling to wreck another woman's marriage carelessly without hearing the full truth. "A few days ago, I went out to the Divine Willow beyond the city... and stumbled onto an unexpected sight."

Catching the hint at once—and having already asked about divorce—Ye Anya dropped all pretense. "Since you saw, you already know why I'm asking."

"How long has it been?"

"We loved each other before I ever married into the Chen house. Because the Ye and Cao families have feuded for generations, my father forbade us to wed and betrothed me to the Chens instead. For a while I tried to accept my fate—until the very night before our wedding, when Xiaocui heard outside that Chen Haodong was spending the night with a brothel courtesan. Disgust took root in my bones, and I haven't gone one day without loathing him."

Ye Anya pushed back her sleeve, revealing angry purple-and-yellow bruises blotching her pale forearm. "Because I stay cold toward him, whenever Chen Haodong wants... his way, he uses his fists. My body is never free of bruises. I cannot live another day like this, so I thought of mutual divorce—only I have no idea where to start. Since you lived through it, I came to ask you."

Qianli Xun reached across and drew Ye Anya's sleeve back down over the bruises. "I had no idea you were going through hell, Eldest Sister-in-law. Are you certain you want to take the step of mutual divorce?"

Ye Anya nodded hard. "My mind is set, or I never would have dared ask."

Having survived the gauntlet herself and looking at fragile Ye Anya across the table, Qianli Xun feared she might shatter under the backlash. "Mutual divorce is brutal, Eldest Sister-in-law. Think long and hard before you leap."
