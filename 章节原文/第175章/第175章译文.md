## Chapter 175: At Last Speaking the Love in His Heart

When her brother vanished early from the wedding banquet without a word, anxiety gripped Qiao Mengrong tighter than ever before. Once Qiao Shouren's carriage delivered her back to Youlin Retreat, her first question to Butler Li was whether Brother had returned. Told he had not, she paced her chambers unable to sit still. Where had he gone? Had he slipped off behind her back to meet some beauty?

Seeing her mistress on edge, Xilan offered comfort. "Master Qiao said His Highness went to handle official business."

"Official business? When have you ever seen Brother care about official business?" Qiao Mengrong dismissed Qiao Shouren's excuse on the spot. "Think back for me, Xilan—since coming to Lin'an, has Brother treated any other woman differently?"

Xilan thought hard. "Does Youqi Jushi count?"

"Shuzhen? I do not see Brother treating her specially."

"Yet aside from Your Highness, Youqi Jushi is the only woman His Highness talks to at length."

"Shuzhen and I are close friends, so of course Brother talks more with her. Think harder—anyone besides Shuzhen?"

Xilan racked her brain again. "I truly cannot think of another woman His Highness treats out of the ordinary."

"I must be imagining shadows. Go watch the front gate and tell me the instant Brother gets back." A flash of the chuiwan match crossed Qiao Mengrong's mind—something between Brother and Shuzhen had felt slightly odd that day. Could Brother like Shuzhen? She brushed the notion off at once: why would Brother fancy a betrothed woman? And Shuzhen was her dearest friend—she would never betray her.

"Yes, Your Highness." Xilan withdrew to wait by the gate.

Nearly an hour later, Prince Yi's carriage rolled up at last.

Xilan hurried out to meet him. "Your Highness, Her Highness has been worried sick and had me wait at the gate."

Bai Feng sighed to himself: if the princess ever learned His Highness loved Youqi Jushi, how shattered would she be?

Stepping down from the carriage without a word, Shen Jianchen strode straight into the retreat. Bai Feng moved to follow, only for Xilan to catch his sleeve. "His Highness is heading straight for Her Highness. Let us hang back and not interrupt."

Agreeing—and praying His Highness would cherish the princess—Bai Feng held back.

Shen Jianchen walked directly to Mengrong's courtyard. To onlookers, it looked like a devoted betrothed rushing to ease her worry; only he knew he was going there to lay bare his heart.

Stepping through her courtyard gate, he saw Qiao Mengrong waiting under the moonlight. Catching sight of him, she ran straight up and threw her arms around his waist. "Where did you run off to, Brother?" The smell of wine rose from his robes—he had drunk plenty at the Qiao feast.

Just as always, Shen Jianchen did not wrap his arms around her. "Mengrong, I have something to tell you."

Pressing her cheek against his chest, Qiao Mengrong closed her eyes to savor the solid comfort of holding him. "Tell me anything, Brother."

After a quiet pause, Shen Jianchen braced himself. "Mengrong, after all this time together, you must have sensed I am not the Zhao Pu from before..."

Her eyes snapped open, the warmth gone in an instant, yet she refused to let go, terrified he would vanish if she loosened her grip. "Why bring that up, Brother?"

"I do not want to keep lying to you..."

Sensing dread ahead, Qiao Mengrong hugged him tighter. "Stop talking, Brother! You are my brother from before—you are Zhao Pu, His Majesty's thirteenth son!"

"Why keep fooling yourself? This body is Zhao Pu's, yes, yet the heart inside is not. I... I already have someone I love." Getting the truth out at last lifted a crushing stone from his chest.

Hearing the exact words she had dreaded so long drained the strength from Qiao Mengrong's arms. She pried her hands loose, tears brimming before she could speak. "Who is the woman Brother loves?"

Shen Jianchen did not answer directly. "Mengrong, I will always treat you like my own little sister, and I hope you will see me only as your brother."

Tears spilled down her cheeks as she pressed on. "Who is she?"

"I cannot tell you yet."

Grief overwhelmed her, and she threw her arms around him again. "Tell me you were joking, Brother! Tell me every word was a lie!"

Her sob-choked plea tore at Shen Jianchen's conscience, yet he refused to lie against his own heart. "I have held these words back for so long. Tonight I had to say them."

Forced to accept he meant every word, Qiao Mengrong released him again, sobbing through tear-streaked cheeks as she searched his face. Under the silver moonlight, he looked calm and relieved.

"What good does saying all this do, Brother? His Majesty decreed our marriage—you have no choice but to marry me."

Transmigrated into an unwanted imperial betrothal with zero way out, Shen Jianchen felt even more trapped than Qianli Xun; at least she could fight hers, whereas he had no opening at all.

Seeing him silent, Qiao Mengrong sobbed on. "No matter who you are, Brother, no matter whether I am in your heart, you are the man Qiao Mengrong is going to marry. Worst comes to worst, after we wed, I... I will let Brother take concubines! I will live in peace with the woman Brother loves..."

Looking at her fragile, weeping face, Shen Jianchen cursed himself inside even as he forced out the rest of the truth. "With the decree in place, we have no choice but to wed. I have thought it through: even after marriage, I will still treat you as a sister and never cross that line. And if you ever love someone else, I will set you free at any moment."

Every syllable cut deeper. So Brother had already planned out their whole future without her. Fresh tears poured down her face. "I will only ever love Brother in this lifetime, and I will stay by your side forever! Tell me who is in your heart right now, or I will swear you are lying to me!"

Shen Jianchen wanted her to know his heart, yet he could not expose Qianli Xun—not when she kept rejecting him and their two betrothals stood like mountains between them. "It is late. Get some rest."

Turning on his heel, he walked out of the courtyard.

Heartbreak crushed Qiao Mengrong, making her miss her old brother with every fiber of her being. Dragging heavy feet back inside, she threw herself across the bed and wept aloud.

Xilan hurried in to comfort her. "Do not cry so hard, Your Highness. Maybe His Highness was only testing whether your devotion is true!"

Qiao Mengrong sat up. "You heard everything?"

Xilan nodded. "Bai Feng and I stood outside the courtyard gate and heard every word."

"Xilan, pry it out of Bai Feng in private—who is the woman Brother loves?"

"I already asked him, and Bai Feng swore he had no idea. How could a guard read a prince's mind? I still bet His Highness was only teasing you."

"Bai Feng knows! He follows Brother everywhere—how could he not know where Brother goes? He just will not tell you."

"Then I will keep pressing him," Xilan promised. "Still, Your Highness has no need to despair. His Highness is the Emperor's son—of course there will not be only one woman around him, and taking a fancy to others is ordinary enough. You hold His Majesty's marriage decree and are guaranteed to be Prince Yi's primary consort. Once you hold the keys to the inner quarters, every other woman will walk on eggshells around you."

Xilan's sensible words cleared a corner of Qiao Mengrong's mind. With things already at this pass, crying changed nothing. Dabbing her cheeks with a silk handkerchief, she murmured, "You are right. Shuzhen told me the same thing once, and I brushed it off. Looks like she noticed Brother had someone else long ago—maybe she even knows who it is! I will visit Wangyue Pavilion tomorrow and ask her."

For all Xilan's soothing, Qiao Mengrong remained obsessed with one question: who held her brother's heart?
