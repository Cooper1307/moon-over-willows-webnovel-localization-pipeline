第280章 虚情假意陈浩东
## Chapter 280: Chen Haodong’s Crocodile Tears

陈浩宇被青瓜感动，他住在简陋的环境，青瓜还主动跑来陪伴，足以说明他是位义仆。以前他身边跟的那些人只因钱财围着他转，如果让他们来陪他，那些人估计跑得比兔子还快。
他为以前忽略青瓜感到惭愧，越是感动和惭愧，越说不出感激的话来，所有感情只能汇聚在平常说习惯的话中：“说你贱，你还真贱。”
同样骂人的话比往常任何时候听起来都顺耳，青瓜肯定地回应道：“我喜欢对公子贱。”
主仆二人腻歪的样子，陈浩东听不下去，何况他还要去瞧瞧隔壁那位，若青瓜跟着，实在不方便。
“行了，你们两个别再腻歪，我给差役说一声，大不了再给他些银子，这种小事他必然答应。”
青瓜高兴地说：“谢大公子！”
陈浩东不打算继续待下去：“东西和人都送到，我该走了，要不门外的差役该不高兴。”
陈浩东推门而出，顺手把门从外面关好，又从怀里掏出两锭银子塞给差役：“刚才的下人一直跟着浩宇，能否让他留下陪着？”
一下子跳进怀里两锭银子，差役更高兴得合不拢嘴：“又不是什么大事，尽管留。”
Touchiness softened into gratitude inside Chen Haoyu. Locked in this bare room, Qinggua still came running to keep him company—a truly loyal servant, unlike the fair-weather crowd who would have bolted faster than rabbits once the silver stopped flowing. Ashamed of how he had treated Qinggua before, he couldn’t bring himself to say anything sappy and fell back on his old teasing. “Call you shameless, and you prove me right.”

差役揣了银子，上前把门锁好，一转身见陈浩东还站在原地，心想人家一次给了两锭银子，应该还有事相求，便问道：“还有事？”
The familiar insult sounded sweeter than praise. Qinggua beamed. “I love being shameless for Young Master!”

“差役大哥好眼力。”陈浩东先夸赞一番，继续说，“隔壁关着的姑娘把浩宇的手臂给刺伤，狠着呢，我想进去劝她两句，不知可否方便？”
Sick of their sappy banter—and eager to visit the cell next door without Qinggua tagging along—Chen Haodong cut in. “All right, knock it off. I’ll talk to the constable and toss him extra silver if needed. Easy fix.”

差役犹豫片刻：“按说你不是她的家人，不能放你进去，看在银子的份上我偷偷给你行个方便，不能超过一刻钟。”
“Thank you, Eldest Young Master!” Qinggua cheered.

“多谢差役大哥。”
“Supplies and company delivered—time I got going before the guard loses patience.” Stepping out and shutting the door behind him, Chen Haodong slipped two more silver ingots into the constable’s hand. “That servant always waits on Haoyu. Mind letting him stay the night?”

差役打开大锁，推开门：“公子，请。”
Two ingots landing straight in his palm stretched the constable’s grin ear to ear. “No big deal—let him stay.” Locking the door, he turned to find Chen Haodong still lingering. Two ingots at once meant another favor coming. “Something else?”

陈浩东走进后，差役依旧将门关上。
“Sharp eyes, Brother!” Chen Haodong flattered. “That girl locked next door is a fierce one—she stabbed Haoyu’s arm. Think I could slip in a minute to talk some sense into her?”

还未来得及寻找春金兰，她已迫不及待站在他面前：“大公子可来了，刚才隔着门听到外面的动静，猜到大公子一准儿会来看我。”
The constable hesitated. “You’re not her kin, so it’s against the rules... tell you what, for the silver, I’ll look the other way. Fifteen minutes max.”

陈浩东装作一本正经的样子，他一直在春金兰面前都是这么装的。
“Much obliged, Brother.”

春金兰对男人恨之入骨，在青楼不愿接客，要不是春金沫罩着，估计在春香楼也待不下去。对付她只需装成正人君子，才能获得她的信任。
The padlock clicked open, the door swung wide, and Chen Haodong stepped inside before the constable shut it behind him.

陈浩东故意向后退出一步：“我一向见不得他人受苦，来看我二弟，也顺便看看金兰姑娘。”
Chun Jinlan was already waiting right inside the doorway. “You came, Eldest Young Master! Heard the noise next door and knew you’d drop in to see me.”

对方特意保持距离，春金兰更是从心底生出敬意：“金兰谢过大公子。”
Chen Haodong put on his straitlaced gentleman act—the only mask that worked on a woman who hated men so fiercely she refused clients at the brothel and only survived under Chun Jinmo’s wing. Stepping back a polite pace, he said, “I can’t stand seeing anyone suffer. Came to check on Second Brother, so I stopped in to see you too, Miss Jinlan.”

陈浩东继续美化自己：“我二弟做下错事，我当大哥的痛心不已，希望帮他找到一个改过自新的机会，才到处打听你的下落，苍天有眼，终于让我找到。我找到你，就是想让浩宇把你放在他身边，你既有个好去处，他也可弥补过错。”
That deliberate distance filled her with respect. “Thank you, Eldest Young Master.”

春金兰说：“大公子的好心我都明白。”
He piled on the noble act. “My brother’s wrongdoing broke my heart. Hoping to give him a chance to make amends, I tracked you down everywhere until Heaven let me find you—all so Haoyu would take you in, giving you a secure home and letting him right his wrong.”

“你不明白，今日我才意识到我错了。本以为你来闹上一闹，家人便可大事化小，劝说浩宇纳了你，谁知你和浩宇被关起来，都是我害得你们受罪。”
“I know your good intentions, Eldest Young Master.”

“大公子不必自责，不是你的错，你也是好心。”
“No—today I realized I blundered. I thought making a scene would get the elders to smooth things over and make Haoyu take you in. Instead both of you got locked up here. It’s my fault you’re suffering.”

陈浩东扫视一眼屋子，跟隔壁布置得一模一样，炕上只有草席，连个盖的都没有，他继续以情动人：“本来我是一个人前来，偷偷给你带了床薄被，可在司理院门口碰到浩宇身边的下人，被他一股脑搬到隔壁……”
“Don’t blame yourself—you meant well.”

陈浩东脱下身上的外衫，走到炕边放下：“还好天儿越来越热，晚上无需盖得太厚，我的衫子送你，睡觉时盖上肚子，省得着凉。”
Glancing around the twin cell—bare straw mat on the kang, not even a thin blanket—he cranked up the sympathy. “I came alone and smuggled a thin quilt for you, only Haoyu’s servant caught me at the gate and hauled everything into his room...” Shrugging off his own outer robe, he laid it on the kang. “Good thing the weather’s warming up. Take my robe to cover your stomach tonight so you don’t catch a chill.”

炕上的衫子令春金兰感动不已，她自从被卖到青楼，碰到的男人除了欺负她还是欺负她，第一次有男人见她毫不动色，还如此关切，她感动地跪下：“大公子是好人，既怜惜我，不如让我跟了你？”
Staring at the robe, Chun Jinlan’s eyes welled up. Ever since being sold to a brothel, every man she met had only used and abused her; never once had a man looked at her without lust and treated her with such kindness. Dropping to her knees, she pleaded, “You’re such a good man, Eldest Young Master! Since you pity me, why not let me belong to you instead?”

陈浩东这才意识到他的戏演得有些过，赶紧拒绝道：“万万不可！不是我嫌弃你，说到底是浩宇害了你，错误需他承担，再说我娘子也不允许我纳妾。”
Realizing he had laid the act on too thick, Chen Haodong recoiled. “Out of the question! Not that I look down on you, yet Haoyu’s the one who wronged you and must answer for it—besides, my wife would never allow a concubine.”

春金兰流下伤心的眼泪：“为何我总是遇不到好男人？好不容易遇见还成了亲，还对家里的娘子死心塌地。”
Tears spilled down her cheeks. “Why can’t I ever meet a good man? The one time I do, he’s already married and devoted to his wife!”

一刻钟的时间太短，情已扇够，是该说重点的时候：“你还是起来吧，我有要事与你说。”
With fifteen minutes ticking down and her emotions primed, it was time to plant the hook. “Get up—I’ve got something crucial to tell you.”

春金兰站起来，擦着眼角的泪水：“大公子请讲。”
Wiping her eyes, she rose. “Go ahead, Eldest Young Master.”

“浩宇的娘子心胸狭窄，容不下他身边有妾室，我估计她今晚会找到疤子和浩宇的狐朋狗友，用钱财买通他们，让他们明日做假证。”
“Haoyu’s wife is jealous and won’t tolerate a concubine. My bet is she’s out tonight buying off Scarface and Haoyu’s cronies to give false testimony tomorrow.”

“我该如何是好？”
“What do I do?”

“实在不行，只能以性命威胁。”
“If all else fails, threaten to take your own life.”

“我还不想死。”
“I don’t want to die!”

“没有让你真死，吓唬吓唬他们而已。仪王心善，见你连性命都不顾，自然不会轻易相信证人的话，必然会彻查此案，还你一个公道，然后让浩宇纳了你。有仪王出面为你做主，没有人敢反对。”
“Nobody’s asking you to die—just scare them! Prince Yi is soft-hearted. Seeing you put your life on the line, he won’t swallow those witnesses’ lies; he’ll dig to the bottom, get you justice, and force Haoyu to take you in. Once Prince Yi backs you, nobody dares object.”

春金兰疑惑道：“仪王真的会替我做主？”
Doubt flickered in her eyes. “Will Prince Yi really stand up for me?”

“仪王最是体察民情，善于为百姓考虑。懒人村的事你可听说过？”
“He cares about commoners more than anyone—heard about Lazybones Village?”

“听说了。”
“I did.”

“那还有什么可怀疑的？人家是贵人，屈尊来陈家办案，一点儿怨言都没有，你也是亲眼见过。再说温都头武功了得，绝对会在关键时刻救下你，你尽管假戏真做，才能让人信以为真。”
“Then what’s to doubt? A royal lowering himself to visit Chen Manor without a single complaint—you saw it with your own eyes! Plus Chief Constable Wen is a top martial artist; he’ll stop you at the last second for sure. You just have to make the act look real.”

春金兰狠狠心说：“好，我听大公子的。只是我的匕首被陈浩宇的娘子抢走……”
Hardening her resolve, Chun Jinlan nodded. “All right, I’ll do it. Only Chen Haoyu’s wife took my dagger...”

陈浩东取出提前藏在身上的一把匕首：“我已为你准备好，希望明日之后你能成为陈家的一员，为自己谋个好去处，也算给浩宇一次改邪归正的机会。”
Chen Haodong pulled a hidden blade from his clothes. “Already brought you a spare. Here’s hoping tomorrow makes you one of the Chens—a good home for you, and a chance for Haoyu to straighten up.”

春金兰接过匕首，跟之前的那把一模一样，她像上次一样藏在袖中。
Taking the twin dagger, she tucked it into her sleeve just like the first one.

蛊惑成功，陈浩东甚为满意，是该离开的时候，还需叮嘱几句：“若有人问起我今日见你说些什么，你只说我是劝你放过浩宇和陈家，其他一概不能说，我本为你好，只怕别人多心。”
Satisfied she was hooked, he added one last warning before leaving. “If anyone asks why I saw you tonight, just say I came urging you to drop the charges against Haoyu and the Chens. Not another word—I mean well, yet people love to gossip.”

春金兰点点头：“大公子放心，你是好人，我绝不会连累你。”
“Don’t worry, Eldest Young Master. You’re a good man—I’d never drag you down.”

陈浩东瞅向炕上的衫子，他再傻也知不能留下把柄，边靠近土坑，边说：“我突然觉得把我的衫子放在这里十分不妥，司理院的人个个精明，怕他们怀疑我们两个之间不清不楚，反而对你不利，我看我还是把它拿回去。”
His eyes fell on the outer robe on the kang. Foolish to leave physical proof behind. Walking over, he scooped it up. “Actually, leaving my robe here is a bad idea—these Judicial Office men are sharp, and if they suspect anything improper between us, it’ll hurt your case. Better I take it back.”

陈浩东已将外衫披在身上，春金兰失望之余只是认为他担心个人名节而已，叹口气：“唉，大公子想得周到，还是拿回去得好，你有这份心意我已心满意足。”
Slipping it back on, he left Chun Jinlan disappointed yet admiring his caution. “You think of everything, Eldest Young Master. Knowing you care is enough for me.”

陈浩东整理好衣装，心中嘲笑春金兰实在好哄。一切已安排妥当，他可高枕无忧。
Smoothing his sleeves, he sneered inside at how easy she was to play. Everything was in place.

陈浩东推开门，离开简陋阴暗之地。待差役将门锁好后，他又拿出一锭银子，塞到差役手中：“差役大哥，那女的实在不好劝说，我白白进去一趟，您就当今晚的事没发生过，别在他人面前提起，省得给仪王平添烦恼。”
Stepping back out into the yard while the constable relocked the door, Chen Haodong handed over one final silver ingot. “Stubborn woman—wasted my breath trying to talk her round. Pretend tonight never happened, Brother, and don’t breathe a word to anyone lest it bother Prince Yi.”

差役摸着银子，满口应下：“不是什么大事，提它作甚？您尽管把心放在肚子里。”
Pocketing the silver, the constable beamed. “Not worth mentioning—set your mind at ease!”

陈浩东这才大步流星离开司理院。
Chen Haodong strode out of the Judicial Office without a care in the world.
