## Chapter 280: Chen Haodong's Crocodile Tears

Touched by Qinggua's loyalty—far truer than every fair-weather crony who'd ditched him—Haoyu choked down emotion behind his usual gruff tease: "Call you stubborn, and you prove it!"

"Happy to be stubborn for you, Young Master!"

Sick of their touching reunion, Haodong stepped out, greased the constable's palm with two more silver ingots to let Qinggua stay—and buy fifteen quiet minutes inside the cell next door with Chun Jinlan.

Putting on his signature saintly act—stepping back modestly to win her trust—Haodong lamented getting her jailed, draped his own outer robe onto her bare kang as a blanket (bringing the gullible, mistreated girl to her knees begging to serve *him* instead!), and quickly declined before planting his poison: Haoyu's jealous wife would surely bribe Scarface and Haoyu's buddies into lying tomorrow! When things went south, Jinlan should stage a fake suicide threat in court—Chief Constable Wen was fast enough to save her, and kindhearted Prince Yi would force Haoyu to take her in!

Handing her an identical replacement dagger to tuck up her sleeve, Haodong swiftly thought better of leaving evidence and took his own robe right back ("Don't want sharp yamen eyes suspecting us"), slipped the constable another ingot to forget his visit ever happened, and strode into the night feeling untouchable.
