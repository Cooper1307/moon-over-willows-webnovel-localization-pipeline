第264章 还是交由我处理
## Chapter 264: Leave This to Me

阿升松口气，脸上露出轻松的笑容，有小姐在，红豆肯定没事。
村长又一挥手，所有人都围上来，男男女女里三层外三层。
看来今日必须打一架。
只听村长命令道：“大家叠罗汉，把这两个小娘儿们压住，压服了再用绳子绑起来！”
众人向千里寻和红豆逼近。
两人冷笑一声，使出连环拳打向围攻而来的一张张脏兮兮、可恶的脸上。
堵路口的八人见状，也跑过来，伸出拳头从外面向众人打去。
懒人村人虽多，都不会功夫，男人仗着劲儿大，用蛮力应对，女的打了一会儿，实在不好对付，一个个退出，缩到一边。
丑丫把老人送到医馆付了银子，刚好赶回来，瞧见打得激烈，气道：“竟然敢欺负我家小姐，看招！”使出拳头冲了过去。
懒人村的女人在一旁叹道：“这几个人真厉害，恐怕咱们要输。”
施粥棚的伙计在一旁边看边欢呼：“打得好！打得妙！打得懒人吱哇乱叫！”
被吓走的流浪之人也围拢过来看热闹，第一次见懒人村的人被揍得稀里哗啦，大家也都无比开心。
千里寻的身旁围着一群身强力壮的男子，包括村长在内。
本以为能轻松将眼前的小娘儿们给制服，没想到她的拳头如此厉害，搞得他们招架不住，节节败退，一个个被打得鼻青脸肿，浑身是伤。最后那娘儿们拳脚配合，他们全部被打倒在地上，周围人一阵欢呼。
千里寻落地站稳，指着倒地的人：“听好了！以后不许来施粥棚混吃混喝！来一次我打一次！”
红豆和丑丫也将其他人打趴下，站到小姐身后；阿升和阿美等八人见众人已被制服，停下拳脚，继续回路口处守着。
千里寻面向村长：“你是领头的？”
村长忍着身上的伤痛说：“俺是村长。”
千里寻指着他的鼻子骂道：“当村长的人不想着领全村人发家致富，却带着村民跑来和流浪的可怜人抢吃食，还有脸说自己是村长。”
村长说：“女侠有所不知，俺们也不想混吃混喝，怎奈村里的地只长草不长粮食，野菜到处都是，粮食却颗粒无收，便想到混吃混喝的注意。原本俺们村也不叫懒人村，混得久了，大家才这么叫。”
千里寻继续骂道：“你们有胳膊有腿，又有力气，难道不能出卖力气养活家人？”
村长继续解释：“干过，没用，靠力气挣的钱根本不够养活全村的人，再说临安城也要不了这么多人。”
千里寻正要接着骂，传来林觉的声音：“我说呢？怎么打起来？原来是陈家二少夫人在此。”
千里寻瞧去，林觉骑着马，身后跟着一群士兵，停在阿升堵的路口处。
林觉跳下马，穿过阿升等人，走过来：“都成亲了，不好好在家里待着，净跑出来惹事。”
千里寻没好气地说：“林统制早已不是练兵官，没事带士兵在街上瞎转悠什么？”
Asheng exhaled in relief—with Miss on deck, Hongdou was safe.

林觉一脸调侃的表情：“还真巧了，我今儿心血来潮出来转悠一圈儿，没承想碰上你，你说我们是不是很有缘分？”
The headman waved again, and a hundred men and women closed in rings three deep. No avoiding a brawl today.

千里寻很讨厌对方戏谑的表情：“我看你跟母猪最有缘分！作为临安统制，不知道带人帮陈家施粥棚把混吃混喝的人赶跑，却跟老娘说些不搭调的话，小心老娘的拳头不打他们专打你！”
"Dogpile 'em!" the headman roared. "Flatten the two wenches and tie 'em up!"

林觉无语，立刻恢复正常：“二少夫人，借一步说话。”
With matching cold smiles, Qianli Xun and Hongdou met the rush with blistering flurries of punches, slamming grimy, sneering faces left and right. Seeing the brawl erupt, the eight servants guarding the exits charged in from behind to box them in. The villagers had numbers, zero martial arts—the men relied on brute muscle while the women bailed out to the sidelines after taking a few hits.

千里寻从林觉的表情判断，他应该有正事要说，不如给他面子，便跟着走到一边。
Right then Chouya marched back from the clinic, took one look at the melee, and hollered, "How dare you gang up on my Miss? Take this!" Diving straight in swinging.

“有什么事非得将老娘叫到这边来说？”
From the sidelines, the village women sighed, "These folks can really fight—we're gonna lose."

“你有所不知，这些人我管不得。”
Meanwhile the congee-shed cooks cheered wildly—"Beat 'em! Thrash 'em! Make the lazybones squeal!"—and the chased-off beggars crept back to enjoy the glorious sight of Lazybones Village finally getting mopped across the dirt.

“为何？”
Surrounded by the village's burliest men, headman included, Qianli Xun made short work of their arrogance. Bruised, bloody-nosed, and reeling backward, every last man hit the ground under a crisp barrage of fists and kicks, drawing deafening cheers from the crowd.

“当今官家注重慈善，设置居养院、安济坊、漏泽园，目前临安这三处搁置起来，为安抚穷困百姓，才有了施粥棚，否则这些人把事闹大，传到官家耳朵里，知府大人吃不了兜着走。”
Landing steady, Qianli Xun pointed at the groaning heap. "Listen up! Never show your faces mooching at this congee shed again! Every time you step foot here, I'll beat you flat!"

林觉用朱晞颜开脱，千里寻甚是不满：“施粥棚是给真正需要的人设置，不是给那些混吃混喝的人，我是问你为何不管懒人村的人？少给我扯别的！”
Hongdou and Chouya finished dropping the rest and stepped behind their mistress while Asheng, Amei, and the others returned to guard the exits.

“你怎么知道懒人村的人不是真正需要的人？你就那么确定你眼睛看到的真正需要的人里面没有混吃混喝的？告诉你，所有人都是混，这也正是知府大人不启用那三处的原因，用这施粥棚打发打发应付应付得了，只要穷鬼们别闹事便好。”
Qianli Xun turned on the headman. "You're the leader?"

“难怪他们如此嚣张，都是官府给惯的。”
Groaning, he muttered, "I'm the village headman."

“我给你出个主意，回朱府跟知府大人说说，看他有没有什么更好办法。”
She jabbed a finger toward his nose. "A village headman who can't figure out how to lead his people to an honest living, and instead drags a whole village down here to rob starving beggars—you've got nerve calling yourself headman!"

千里寻清楚找朱晞颜说了也白搭，还会被他训斥一顿，不如把难题丢给林觉：“你这么喜欢巴结上司，我给你出一个巴结上司的好主意，不如把懒人村的男人编入临安的士兵里，既充实兵力，又有军饷可拿。有了军饷他们可养家糊口，省得到处混吃混喝。”
"You don't know our plight, Lady Hero," he wheezed. "Think we *want* to mooch? Our village soil grows nothing but weeds and wild greens—not a single grain of rice or wheat survives! We didn't even used to be called Lazybones Village; folks tagged us with it after we started coming down here to beg."

“你出的主意不是没想过，可他们不乐意，原因只有一个：怕死。”
"You've got arms, legs, and strong backs—can't you sell honest labor to feed your families?"

千里寻不信，冲着村长大喊：“喂！你过来！”
"Tried it! Wages barely feed one mouth, let alone a whole village—and Lin'an doesn't have work for a hundred of us anyway!"

村长从地上爬起来，上前问道：“女侠，有何吩咐？”
Before she could tear into him again, a familiar drawl drifted over. "Wondered who was brawling—turns out it's Second Young Madam Chen!"

千里寻直接问：“为什么不愿当兵？”
Qianli Xun glanced over to find Lin Jue on horseback with a troop of soldiers halted outside Asheng's post. Dismounting, Lin Jue sauntered through the guards. "Married woman and you still can't stay home—out here picking street fights?"

“当兵就得打仗，打仗就会没命，俺们村以前有几个人参军，后来去了汴梁，去年跟着攻打辽国，结果死了。所以俺们都不愿再参军，死了一家老小该怎么办？”
"Commander Lin isn't a drill officer anymore—why are you wandering the streets with soldiers?" she snapped back.

千里寻骂道：“真不愧是懒人村，给你们找什么出路都能找出一大堆理由来。带着你的村民赶紧滚！以后不准来混吃混喝！”
Lin Jue smirked playfully. "Just felt like patrolling today and bumped straight into you—guess fate really binds us together, eh?"

村长立刻跪下，后面所有的懒人村的人都跪下。
Sick of his teasing smirk, she fired back, "Fate binds you to a sow! You're the garrison commander of Lin'an—instead of clearing freeloaders out of the Chen congee shed, you're standing here spouting nonsense! Keep talking and my fists will skip them and head straight for you!"

村长带头说：“求女侠可怜一村的老人和孩子，今日俺们是吃饱了，可他们还饿着，就让俺们把粥和馒头给他们拉回去吧？俺们给您磕头了！”
His grin dropped into sober business. "Step aside for a word, Second Young Madam."

其他村民都齐声说：“求求女侠可怜可怜一村老小！”
Reading genuine seriousness in his face, she walked a few paces off with him. "Well? What's so hush-hush?"

面对此等情况，千里寻十分为难。
"I can't touch these people."

正为难之际，仪王出现：“此等小事，还是交由我处理！”
"Why not?"

原来申简辰回到司理院后，翻阅一会儿以前的卷宗，有些累想出去走走，想起陈家的施粥棚，也是司理参军管辖的地儿，不如过去瞧瞧。
"His Majesty prizes charity—Juyang Yuan, Anji Fang, Louze Yuan. Since Lin'an shut all three down, this congee shed is the only thing keeping paupers quiet. If they kick up a riot and word reaches the capital, Prefect Zhu's head is on the block."

走到时，发现千里寻在施粥棚正在与林觉说话。
Hearing him hide behind Zhu Xiyan only irritated her more. "The congee shed is for people who actually need it, not roving moochers! I asked why you won't handle Lazybones Village—stop dodging!"

再次见到她，已没有任何理由向她流露出充满爱意的眼神，说些充满感情的话，还是老老实实以仪王的身份对待他。
"How do you know they aren't genuinely desperate? Or that half the 'poor beggars' you pity aren't moochers too? Everybody's scraping by however they can—that's exactly why Prefect Zhu shelved those three institutions and threw a congee shed at them to keep the peace."

申简辰扫视一遍四周，应该是发生了什么。他吩咐白风将守在路口的阿升叫来。向阿升了解详情，又向一旁流浪之人问清懒人村的情况，接着目睹了懒人村的人跪下乞求的情景。不愿千里寻为难，此事还是交由他解决。
"No wonder they act like kings—the yamen spoils them rotten!"

仪王前来，林觉岂能错过巴结的机会？走上前拜道：“我本想着处理完此事去司理院拜见殿下，没想到殿下竟然前来。此等小事，殿下不必费心，还是交由属下处理。”
"Tell you what: go back to Zhu Manor and ask Prefect Zhu if he's got a brighter idea."

“林统制打算如何处理？”
Knowing Zhu Xiyan would just chew her out, Qianli Xun tossed the hot potato straight back. "Since you love kissing up to superiors, here's a gold-plated suggestion: draft the able-bodied men of Lazybones Village into Lin'an's garrison. Boosts troop numbers, gives them army pay to feed their families, and stops them freeloading!"

“将他们统统赶回懒人村，若不服，临安的牢狱还空着，关押几个不在话下。”
"Think we didn't try? They flat-out refused. One reason: scared of dying."

“临安的牢狱是用来关押犯人，他们可曾触犯刑法？”
Unconvinced, Qianli Xun barked at the headman, "Hey! Get over here!"

林觉急于表现，话没说到贵人心里，赶紧承认错误：“小人失言，望殿下海涵。”
Scrambling up, the headman shuffled over. "Yes, Lady Hero?"

千里寻也上前拜见，以后见到他必须知礼懂礼，保持距离：“仪王殿下。”
"Why won't you men join the army?"

申简辰不敢直视，他怕自己忍不住流露感情，那么屈服于现实的做法岂不前功尽弃？
"Soldiers fight wars, wars get you killed! A few lads from our village enlisted before, marched up to Bianliang, went off to fight the Liao last year, and never came back! If we die, who feeds our elders and kids?"

“少夫人不必多礼，今日让他们多带些吃食回去，我保证，明日起这些人不会再来。”
"Truly worthy of the name Lazybones Village—an excuse lined up for every honest path! Take your crew and get lost! Don't ever come mooching here again!"

对方充满距离感的表现和话语，千里寻虽不习惯，却觉甚好。只有如此梦容才不会难过，也才会拥有一个全心全意待她的哥哥。
At once the headman dropped to his knees, and every single villager behind him knelt in unison.

“是，谨遵仪王吩咐。”
"Have mercy on the old folks and little ones back home, Lady Hero!" the headman begged. "We ate our fill today, sure, yet they're still starving! Let us haul this gruel and buns back to them—we're begging on our knees!"

千里寻回完话后，去给伙计安排。
"Please, Lady Hero, spare our elders and children!" the whole crowd chorused.

申简辰则对跪着的众人说：“都起来吧，带着吃食统统回到懒人村，我会派人前去考察你们村的土质，帮你们解决种粮食的问题。”
Qianli Xun froze, caught in a genuine bind.

村长带领大家千恩万谢：“感谢大人！”
Right then a steady voice cut across the courtyard. "Leave a trifle like this to me."

待大家起身后，村长解释道：“其实俺们并不是真懒，如果地里能打粮食，俺们也愿劳作。外面不好混，又带了一村子的人，哪里都不愿意要俺们？只能混吃混喝，大人要是能帮忙解决问题，俺保证全村人再也不当懒人。”
Shen Jianchen had grown weary reading old dossiers at the Judicial Office and strolled over to inspect the congee shed under his jurisdiction—only to spot Qianli Xun speaking with Lin Jue. Having bowed to reality, he had no right to show longing in his eyes or warmth in his voice; he stayed strictly Prince Yi. Sending Bai Feng to question Asheng and a nearby vagrant filled him in on the whole brawl and Lazybones Village, and seeing Qianli Xun cornered made him step in.

“好！希望你们说到做到，可我暂时还无法信任你们。”申简辰转向林觉，“林统制，麻烦你派人护送他们回村，顺便让人把守村口，明日我会亲自带人前去懒人村。”
Never one to miss a chance to curry favor, Lin Jue hurried forward bowing. "I was just planning to call on Your Highness once this wrapped up—had no idea you'd come in person! No need to trouble yourself over a petty nuisance; leave it to me."

林觉立刻拱手：“谨遵殿下吩咐。”
"And how does Commander Lin plan to handle it?"

一大桶粥，整筐馒头已装好，林觉吩咐身边的一位练兵官带着前来的士兵押送懒人村的人离开。
"Chase the whole lot back to Lazybones Village—and if they resist, Lin'an's jail has plenty of empty cells to lock up a few!"

申简辰又对林觉说：“随我去见知府大人。”
"Jails hold criminals. What law did these people break?"

刚要走，又停住脚步，转过身对千里寻说：“二少夫人忙，我先行一步。”
Realizing he had missed the mark, Lin Jue bowed lower. "I spoke out of turn—forgive me, Your Highness."

千里寻低头行礼，并未去接触对方的眼神：“仪王客气。”
Qianli Xun stepped up and dipped a crisp curtsy, keeping proper etiquette and cool distance. "Your Highness Prince Yi."

申简辰只是盯着千里寻的头上的发饰看了一会儿，多亏她没抬眼，否则他担心自己忘记昨晚已与梦容圆房。
Not daring to meet her eyes—afraid one spark of feeling would undo last night's resolve—Shen Jianchen addressed the air near her hairpins. "No ceremony needed, Second Young Madam. Let them take extra food home today; I guarantee none of them will trouble you starting tomorrow."

以后还是少与她接触，感情藏在心底，不小心还会浮现出来，尤其是见到难以忘怀之人。
Stiff and formal as his tone felt, Qianli Xun silently approved—only this way would Mengrong stop hurting and keep a wholehearted husband. "As Your Highness commands." Withdrawing to instruct the cooks, she left the courtyard to him.

目送仪王与林统制离开，千里寻整理好心情，继续安排施粥棚之事。十个下人没必要再守住路口，继续维持秩序。
Shen Jianchen faced the kneeling villagers. "Get up. Take the food and head straight back to Lazybones Village. I'll send specialists to inspect your soil and find crops that will grow there."

流浪的可怜人又开始聚集而来，排着队，等候施粥。
The headman led a wave of kowtows. "Thank you, Your Honor!" Rising, he added earnestly, "Truth is, we aren't lazy by nature—if our land yielded grain, we'd happily farm it! Nobody in town wants a whole village of outsiders, so we had no choice but to mooch. If Your Honor solves our land problem, I swear nobody in our village will ever play lazybones again!"

千里寻在取粥棚处站着，瞧着伙计给大家盛粥，发馒头，那些流浪的人得了粥和馒头高兴不已。
"Good—hold to your word, though I don't take promises on blind faith yet." Turning to Lin Jue, he ordered, "Commander Lin, have troops escort them home and post guards at their village gate. Tomorrow I'll bring experts up to inspect the land myself."

一个老头佝偻着腰，一只手将碗伸出，伙计为他舀完两勺粥，给馒头时，他的另一只手拿着薄薄的书卷，不方便接，又不知放置何处。
"At once, Your Highness!"

千里寻伸出手：“老人家，我帮你拿。”
Once the barrel of porridge and baskets of buns were loaded, Lin Jue sent a drill officer and soldiers marching the villagers away.

老头将书卷递过去：“麻烦您了。”
"Come with me to see Prefect Zhu," Shen Jianchen told Lin Jue, then paused to nod toward Qianli Xun. "I'll leave you to your work, Second Young Madam."

千里寻接过书卷，见封皮上写着“幽栖居士徒有虚名”八个大字，不由想起梦容举行诗会之事，将书卷打开，上面大部分都是那日诗会时众人辱骂她的话语，还有一些是应是添油加醋加进去的。
Keeping her eyes lowered, Qianli Xun curtsied. "Safe travels, Your Highness."

看来梦容真的想毁了朱淑真江南女词人的美称。怎么办？千里寻第一次感到不知所措。
Shen Jianchen lingered half a heartbeat on the ornaments in her hair—thank goodness she never looked up, or he might have forgotten last night's bridal bed altogether. Better avoid seeing her altogether; buried love still surged to the surface at a single glimpse.

老头已将粥端到吃粥棚。
Once the prince and Lin Jue departed, Qianli Xun steadied herself and returned to organizing the shed. No need to block the exits now; the servants resumed keeping order as genuine paupers queued back up for gruel and buns.

千里寻跟上去：“老人家，你是从哪里得来这本书卷？”
Standing beside the serving table, Qianli Xun noticed a stooped old man holding out his chipped bowl with one hand while clutching a thin booklet in the other, fumbling awkwardly as the clerk handed him a bun.

老人接过，垫在屁股底下，坐好后，边吃边说：“我正在大街上乞讨，有人给发的，我也不识字，拿着上茅厕用也是好的。”
"Here, Grandpa, let me hold that for you," she offered.

“哪条街？”
"Much obliged, lady."

“城西那里。”
Taking the booklet, she glanced at the cover—eight bold characters: *Youqi Jushi: A Fraud Without Substance*. Mengrong's poetry salon flashed back at once. Flipping it open, she scanned page after page of the venom spat at her that day, spiced up even nastier. So Mengrong really meant to rip Zhu Shuzhen's Poetess of Jiangnan title to shreds! For the first time, Qianli Xun felt genuinely at a loss.

千里寻真想冲过去将所有书毁了：“红豆！”
Following the old man into the dining shed, she asked, "Where did you get this booklet, Grandpa?"

红豆立刻过来：“小姐，有何吩咐？”
Taking it back to sit on as a cushion, the old man munched his bun. "Begging in the street—folks were handing 'em out free. Can't read a word, figured it'll make fine latrine paper!"

她又犹豫起来，朱淑真江南女词人的美称后世都记着，怎会轻易丢掉？为了曾经的友情，不如忍了，让梦容发泄发泄也是好的。
"Which street?"

再说不忍又能如何？梦容怎么说也是仪王妃，身份尊贵，她一介草民即使功夫再高，也奈何不了她，还是认真管理施粥棚。
"Over in the west quarter."

“没事了，去忙吧。”
An impulse to charge over and torch every last copy flared up—"Hongdou!"

红豆觉得纳闷，小姐唤她的语气明明是有重大事情安排，怎么突然熄火？她不好多问，继续回到原处维持秩序。
Hongdou stepped right up. "Yes, Miss?"

千里寻走回休息处，在椅子上坐下，不断告诫自己：“忍一时风平浪静，退一步海阔天空。”
Then Qianli Xun stopped herself. History itself had cemented Zhu Shuzhen's name as Poetess of Jiangnan—could a batch of cheap pamphlets erase it? For the sake of their old friendship, let Mengrong vent. Besides, Mengrong was Princess Consort Yi; even with wicked fists, what could a commoner do against royalty? Better stick to running the congee shed. "Never mind—carry on."

她回想与梦容的过往，曾经的她们哪能想到关系会变成如今这样。她又开始自责，真不该悄悄喜欢上梦容的哥哥。
Baffled by the sudden retreat, Hongdou went back to her post.

千里寻决定要快些接纳陈浩宇，快些爱上他。只要梦容看到她与陈浩宇安心在一起，肯定不会再恨她，不会做出伤人伤己之事。
Sinking into her chair, Qianli Xun repeated an old proverb under her breath: *Step back once, and the sea and sky open wide.* Remembering how close she and Mengrong had once been, guilt pricked her again for ever falling for Mengrong's brother. She had to accept Chen Haoyu—and learn to love him—as fast as possible. Once Mengrong saw her truly settled with Haoyu, surely this self-destructive hatred would fade.

不如今日回到陈府与陈浩宇试试。
Maybe tonight, back at Chen Manor, she would try taking a real step with Chen Haoyu.
