## Chapter 167: A Hasty Exit from the Chuiwan Grounds

Qianli Xun and Qiao Mengrong were strolling along in cheerful conversation when Qiao Wenxian stepped into their path. The scent of wine clung to him, and his eyes locked straight onto Shuzhen.

Qiao Mengrong tugged Shuzhen's sleeve. "It is Young Master Qiao. You two talk—I will wait over there."

Reading the raw longing on Wenyuan's face frightened Qianli Xun; she feared softening if she stood alone with him. She caught Mengrong's wrist at once. "Stay right here with me as a witness, or folks might think Young Master Qiao and I are still tangled up."

Qiao Mengrong stayed put. From his gaze alone, she felt the depth of his love for Shuzhen and ached for the pair torn apart.

"Shuzhen," Qiao Wenxian began, "I will ask just one question. Had those rumors never spread and had our families sealed the betrothal, would you have married me willingly?"

After a beat of hesitation, Qianli Xun nodded. "Yes. Because back then, that was what I wanted."

"Let me ask one more..."

"You said just one question."

"I need to know... were your feelings for me back then real?"

"They were real," she answered with candor.

A pained smile touched Qiao Wenxian's lips. "I knew you loved me then. And... now?"

Three questions already. Unwilling to let him press further, Qianli Xun steeled her voice. "Now... I have let go of Wenyuan. I ask Young Master Qiao to let go of Shuzhen as well."

His brief smile vanished. "Must you address me so stiffly from now on? Casting aside even friendship? We once looked upon each other as kindred spirits."

"Shuzhen was naive before. I will have no more kindred spirits. Please stop thinking of Shuzhen as one."

"You... can be this ruthless? Leaving me not even... a last shred of hope?"

The tremor in his voice struck straight at Qianli Xun's chest. "Young Master Qiao jests. There was never any hope between us, let alone a last shred."

Struggling to steady his breathing, Qiao Wenxian forced his tone even. "It is all my fault... Can you promise me one thing? Even if we cannot be together, we remain friends. Even if you no longer call me a kindred spirit, call me Wenyuan, and let me still call you Shuzhen."

Unable to bear wounding him further, Qianli Xun nodded. "I promise. In return, promise me you will never seek me out alone again. Live your life in peace. If you live well, I will be glad."

"Alright. I will listen to you. No more talking alone, and I will live a steady life. Whatever makes you happy, I will do." Turning on his heel, Qiao Wenxian walked back toward the young gentlemen.

Mengrong noticed her brother glancing their way, likely worried about Young Master Qiao. "Ah, I truly feel for you and Young Master Qiao. I hope he recovers soon."

"Nothing to mourn," Qianli Xun replied. "I have made peace with it. Everyone has their own road to walk. Mine and Wenyuan's failed to intertwine, so we walk our separate paths. Once he grows used to his own road, he will let go."

Her words turned Qiao Mengrong's thoughts inward. "I wonder if Brother's road and mine will intertwine."

"Of course they will. Your betrothal binds your road tight to Prince Yi's."

"A betrothal is one thing, yet I fear Brother's heart is not with me. Just like Young Master Qiao—marrying Miao Zhiyu while his heart stays with you. I actually feel sorry for Lady Miao."

Mengrong spoke without intent, yet every word hit Qianli Xun like a stone. If Mengrong ever discovered Prince Yi held her own best friend in his heart, their friendship would shatter on the spot. Qianli Xun regretted teaming up with Shen Jianchen for chuiwan and tagging along to fly kites. Women were perceptive; Mengrong had surely sensed something off in Prince Yi's manner today. Better to leave now before Shen Jianchen pulled another stunt.

"Mengrong, I am not feeling well. I want to head home."

"You were fine a moment ago—what came over you?"

"I worry Wenyuan might come over again and hurt Lady Miao if she sees. I want to get back to Wangyue Pavilion soon."

"I was only rambling just now—do not take it to heart."

"It has nothing to do with you."

"Alright then. Once you have a free moment, come visit me at Youlin Retreat."

"I fear... I will not have time to visit Youlin Retreat for a while."

"Why?"

"The wedding date is closing in. I have to find a way to get our two families to cancel the betrothal. Once it is settled, I will... I will visit you at Youlin Retreat."

Pity crossed Qiao Mengrong's face. "You will have to rely on yourself this time, Shuzhen. Even Brother cannot help."

Qianli Xun caught the faint edge beneath her words: Mengrong did not want her brother involved in this. Just as well—neither did she. Leaving the remark untouched, she said, "I should be off. Go find Prince Yi before he blames me for hogging you all afternoon."

Parting from Mengrong with a tangled heart, Qianli Xun headed straight back toward the chuiwan field. Being busy with the betrothal was only an excuse; the real truth was that she meant to stop visiting Youlin Retreat and decline every invitation from Prince Yi from here on.

Spotting her mistress parting ways with the princess, Hongdou wound up the kite, jogged back to return it to the kite-maker, and sprinted to catch up.

After bidding her sister-in-law farewell at the pavilion, Qianli Xun boarded her carriage with Matron Wei and her maids for the ride back to Wangyue Pavilion.

Inside the carriage, Kuqiao drew the gold ruyi scepter from her sleeve. "Miss, Bai Feng said Prince Yi sent today's prize for you, as thanks for partnering with him in chuiwan."

"Put it away. When the Qiaos come to fetch Xiaguo, pack it in her dowry."

Hearing her name, Xiaguo asked with caution, "Miss, you do not blame me, do you?"

"Why would I blame you?"

"Young Master Qiao agreed to take me as his concubine..."

Far from blaming Xiaguo, Qianli Xun felt a twinge of envy. "You are overthinking it. Even as a concubine, you cannot arrive at Qiao Manor empty-handed. I will have Matron Wei prepare a proper dowry for you. It cannot match Miao Zhiyu's, of course—you are entering as a concubine, and my purse cannot compare with the Miaos'—yet it will be plenty to keep you comfortable there."

Tears spilled down Xiaguo's cheeks. "Miss, I will never forget your immense kindness as long as I live! What I cannot repay in this life, I will serve you as ox and horse in the next!"

"Cut out the 'next life' and 'ox and horse' nonsense. You owe me nothing. No matter how each of you three ended up with me, I treat you all like family. Every one of you will get a dowry when you wed—Kuqiao gets the biggest share, since she has stood by me longest."

All three maids blinked back tears, moved to the core.

Shen Jianchen watched Qianli Xun leave with clear eyes. With the woman he loved gone, he had no reason to linger. Rising to bid the scholars farewell, he walked over to meet Mengrong.

Seeing her brother walk straight toward her, Qiao Mengrong brightened, thinking his old warmth had returned. All her doubts vanished as she rose to meet him. "Brother, Shuzhen left, and I want to head home too."

"Why did Youqi Jushi hurry off instead of keeping you company?"

"Young Master Qiao scared her off! He did not care a bit that I was standing right there—said such bold things it made my ears burn."

Relief washed over Shen Jianchen. As long as he was not the reason she fled.

Then Qiao Mengrong recalled the kite incident. "Brother, why did you ignore me when I called you earlier?"

Part of him burned to spill the truth, yet this was neither the time nor the place. "Oh, I was lost in thought and never heard you. Brother's fault—it will not happen again."

The tension drained from Qiao Mengrong's face. "I thought Brother had someone else in his heart and no longer cared to talk to me."

Her words hit straight on the mark. Skipping a direct denial, Shen Jianchen steered elsewhere. "I wonder if that rascal Bai Feng handed the gold ruyi scepter to Kuqiao for Youqi Jushi. I would hate for folks to think me a stingy prince."

Noticing he had not hurried to deny it, Qiao Mengrong stiffened again. "Why does Brother care so much about where that scepter ended up?"

"You overthink everything—must be tired. Let us hurry back to Youlin Retreat." Taking her hand, Shen Jianchen led her toward the chuiwan grounds.

His gentle scolding and the warmth of his hand felt like reassurance enough, easing her heart a touch. Still, watching everyone else's wedding dates approach while hers dragged all the way to Mid-Autumn Festival felt far too long. What if they could marry this spring too?

She recalled back in Bianliang how Imperial Attendant Liang often visited Prince Yi's Manor on His Majesty's behalf, and she had always received him—slipping him silver on the side until they had grown quite chummy. Why not write to Imperial Attendant Liang and ask him to persuade His Majesty to move the wedding date forward, before delays bred fresh trouble?

Her mind made up, Qiao Mengrong followed her brother past the chuiwan grounds, through the gardens, and into their carriage back to Youlin Retreat.

Qiao Wenxian had watched Qianli Xun leave too, guessing her hasty farewell to the princess was on his account. If you want nothing to do with me, he told himself, I will grant you your wish.

Once Prince Yi left as well, Qiao Wenxian poured cup after cup for himself, downing six straight until a heavy buzz clouded his eyes. "Starting today, I am letting go of these feelings..."

"That is the spirit!" Dongfang Chenze applauded.

"...and keeping Shuzhen locked in my heart forever. Nobody will ever take her place."

The three friends stared at one another.

"If you are letting go of the feelings," An Mingxuan asked, "why keep the woman locked in your heart?"

"Is there a difference between the person and the feelings?" Cao Ban scratched his head.

Half-drunk, Qiao Wenxian sneered at them. "Neither of you has ever been in love—you know nothing."

Both turned to Dongfang Chenze. "Do you get it, Brother Dongfang?"

Shaking his head, Dongfang Chenze recited a couplet: "'Though master painters fill this boundless world, / Not one can paint a single broken heart.'" [TN: Translator's rendering]

"Why quote those two lines?" Cao Ban asked, baffled. "How does that answer our question?"

An Mingxuan explained, "Brother Dongfang means that even though he has a woman he loves, like those 'master painters,' he cannot capture Brother Qiao's sorrow."

"Oh, put like that, it makes sense." Cao Ban turned back to Qiao Wenxian. "We may not fathom the difference between the person and the feelings, Brother Qiao, yet so long as you figure out what to hold onto and what to release, the three of us can rest easy."

Drinking on with hollow eyes, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "She wants me to let go, so I let go. She wants me to stop bothering her, so I stop. She wants me to marry Miao Zhiyu, so I marry her. She wants me to take a concubine, so I take one. She wants me to live a good life, so I will live one..."

The three friends traded another helpless look. Letting go? He was just finding a brand-new way to torture himself.

Once Prince Yi departed the chuiwan grounds, the other guests drifted off in turn. Nobody needed to call an end to the Qiaos' tournament; it wound down on its own.

After nightfall and supper, Qiao Mengrong had Xilan lay out brush and ink and composed her letter—pleading with Liang Shicheng to talk His Majesty into advancing her wedding to Prince Yi. Which month should she ask for? Brush poised, she thought it over. Young Master Qiao's wedding fell in the third month, and Shuzhen's with Young Master Chen was in the third month too. Clearly the third month was auspicious; she would request the third month as well.

Stamping the page with Princess Jiahe's seal, she folded it into an envelope, tied the cord, and handed it to Xilan. "Take this to the relay station tonight so it rides out for Bianliang at first light."

Having glimpsed a few lines as her mistress wrote, Xilan knew the letter begged Imperial Attendant Liang to move the wedding up. Never mind the princess—Xilan herself could not wait another day. His Highness was far too fine a catch, and Lin'an's maidens far too charming. Only once the wedding was sealed could either of them breathe easy.
