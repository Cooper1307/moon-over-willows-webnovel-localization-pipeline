## Chapter 156: Chapter 156: First Performance Flops

千里寻回到闺房，吩咐苦荞去趟幽林别居，说她有重要的事情办，明日必然前去陪伴郡主。
苦荞去后，好不容易熬到可以出发的时间，千里寻只身一人来到朱府侧门外。
侧门紧闭，她站着欣赏了一会儿，想起第一次回朱府的情景……
那时带着好奇与期待来到这里，希望穿越而来能在朱府获得踏实与心安。的确获得过，亲情让她越来越愿意适应穿越而来的生活。
后来被朱晞颜赶出朱府，也是从这道门离开，走得心甘情愿，走得毫无眷恋，只因这道门里的亲人给了她足够的照顾和勇气。
父亲虽严厉，又喜欢毫无道理为她定亲，千里寻还是对这位父亲产生莫名其妙的亲情，甚至能理解父亲的举动。
毕竟穿越而来的时代不够先进，婚姻之事哪轮得上子女做主？再说有个离过婚，名声又不好，还被乔家放鸽子的女儿，父亲朱晞颜肯定心中不安，在他看来能定下陈家这门亲，已是上天眷顾。
能理解父亲，并不代表会接受他的想法，千里寻希望今日一闹，能产生些作用。
正想着，陈浩宇出现：“喂，朱淑真，站在自家门外发什么呆？”
千里寻转过头，瞧向与她有婚约之人，不仅不学无术，不求上进，还没事到处欺负人，一脸毫无因由得意的样子，她看着就来气。心想：“凭什么要嫁给这样的人？”
她懒得理会陈浩宇，自顾走到竹子后面躲起来。
陈浩宇叹道：“你也挺可怜，到家门口却进不去，还不如我，只要说找我姐，自然被放进去，知府大人也不管。”
见千里寻依然没理会，陈浩宇也走到竹子后面。
“你怎么不说话？”
“看到你就不爽，正在积攒力气一会儿打你。”
“说好了，不能真打。”
“哼，到时打起来，只怕我管不住自己的拳头。”
“你要是真打，我立刻离开，不跟你演戏。”
Back in her room, Qianli Xun dispatched Kuqiao to Youlin Retreat to tell Princess Jiahe urgent business came up and she would be there without fail tomorrow. Waiting out the afternoon hours, she headed solo to Zhu Manor's side gate.

千里寻没好气地说：“哄你的，谁让你一副欠揍的样子。”
Shut tight beneath swaying bamboo, the gate stirred memories of her very first visit back to Zhu Manor. Back then she had walked up anxious and hopeful, craving solid footing in an alien era—and found genuine family warmth that anchored her here. Even later, ordered out by Zhu Xiyan, she had stepped past this threshold steady and unafraid thanks to the love and courage those inside had given her. Strict and arbitrary about arranging her marriages as Zhu Xiyan was, an unexpected bond had grown inside her—even understanding his view. In an era where daughters held zero say in marriage, having a divorced, gossip-scarred daughter jilted by the Qiaos must have kept him awake nights; sealing a wealthy Chen match looked like sheer mercy to him.

陈浩宇第一次离朱淑真这么近，一股香气从她身上散发而来，心里不由想道：“母老虎身上竟然也有香气，还比春香楼姑娘身上的香气好闻，难以理解！明明是只母老虎，却生得貌美，要是性情好些，本公子勉为其难把你娶回家，也算替朱家解决个大麻烦，可谁让你拳头那么厉害，本公子是万万不能娶。”
Understanding him didn't mean surrendering to him—she hoped today's show shook him loose!

千里寻扭头瞧见陈浩宇露出色眯眯的表情，更是来气，没忍住，一拳打在他的脸上：“谁让你这么盯着老娘看！”
Right then Chen Haoyu walked up: "Hey, Zhu Shuzhen—zoning out outside your own door?"

陈浩宇的脸上立刻出现淤青，他捂着脸，不由自主从竹子后面走出：“打人不打脸！这个道理你难道不明白？”
Glancing at her unwanted fiancé—idle, spoiled, forever picking on folks, wearing a smug smirk for zero reason—her temper flared: On what planet do I marry THIS guy?! Ignoring him completely, she slipped behind a thick clump of bamboo to hide.

“老娘不明白！对你这种人最好照脸打！”
Chen Haoyu sighed mock-sympathetic: "Pretty pitiful, huh? Standing outside your own home barred from stepping in—beats me! All I do is say I'm visiting my sister and gates fly wide open; even the Prefect says nothing!"

千里寻刚骂完，听到哥哥朱景元的声音：“淑真，你站在竹子后面干什么？怎么浩宇也在？”
Meeting cold silence, he ducked behind the bamboo beside her. "Cat got your tongue?"

两人看到朱晞颜身着官服，一脸严肃地站在朱景元身旁，互相使了使眼色。
"Looking at your face ticks me off—saving up steam to punch you in a minute."

陈浩宇迅速来到朱晞颜面前，露出被打得紫青的脸：“朱伯伯，朱淑真打我，您瞧，我的脸被她打成什么样？”
"Agreed—no real punches!"

千里寻也冲过来：“陈浩宇！你给我爹告状也没用！再吃我一拳！”
"Hmph—once fists fly, can't promise I hold back!"

陈浩宇立刻躲到朱晞颜身后：“有朱伯伯在，看你还敢打我！”
"Throw one real punch and I walk straight off—no show!"

千里寻的拳头本就出得慢些，陈浩宇躲开后，拳头不偏不倚出现在朱晞颜面前。
"Relax, just messing with you—blame your punchable face!"

她赶紧收起拳头：“爹，您让开，我今日要好好收拾收拾陈浩宇，不把他收拾乖了，坚决不嫁到陈家。”
Standing inches from Zhu Shuzhen for the very first time, a crisp, clean fragrance drifted off her, surprising him: Tigress smells nicer than every girl in Chunxiang Brothel—how's that even work?! Fierce tigress wrapped in a stunner's face... if only her temper weren't lethal, wouldn't even mind marrying her to help the Zhus out—too bad her fists are deadly, zero chance I risk it!

朱晞颜什么大场面没见过，已猜出两个孩子的小心思，一脸平静：“景元，交给你处理，为父先回屋歇息。”
Glancing sideways to catch Chen Haoyu ogling her with a sleazy stare, Qianli Xun snapped and clocked him square in the cheek: "Who gave you permission to stare at me like that?!"

已有下人为老爷打开侧门，朱晞颜甩了甩袖子像什么都没发生一般离开。
An instant bruise darkened his cheek; clutching his face, he stumbled clean out from behind the bamboo: "Never hit the face—don't you know basic rules?!"

仅表演两下，没起到效果，千里寻很失望：“哥，爹怎么不理我们？”
"Not for you! Guys like you belong hit right in the face!"

朱景元笑道：“你们两个明显是故意表演给爹看，我都能瞧出来，何况爹？”
Words barely out when Zhu Jingyuan's voice rang down the lane: "Shuzhen—why are you hiding behind the bamboo? And Haoyu's here too?!"

陈浩宇委屈道：“哪里是表演？姐夫你瞧，我脸上是不是被你妹妹打得乌青。”
Turning to see stone-faced Zhu Xiyan in official robes standing beside Zhu Jingyuan, the two flashed a quick signal glance!

朱景元早已注意到陈浩宇脸上的伤：“跟我进去，让丫鬟给你上些药。”
Bolting straight up to Zhu Xiyan, Chen Haoyu thrust out his purple cheek: "Uncle Zhu! Zhu Shuzhen hit me! Look what she did to my face!"

千里寻埋怨道：“哥哥只顾关心别人，却把亲妹妹扔在一边。”
Charging right behind him, Qianli Xun yelled: "Tattling to my dad won't save you, Chen Haoyu—eat another fist!"

朱景元解释道：“我当然最关心你，浩宇那伤是你打的，我让他进府上药，无非是希望他别再埋怨你，你正好也跟着进去与娘坐坐。”
Ducking straight behind Zhu Xiyan's back, Chen Haoyu crowed: "Uncle Zhu's right here—dare hit me now?!"

千里寻很愿意见向夫人，尽管朱晞颜不允许她再进朱府，可都到家门口，不进去与娘聊两句，好像也说不过去。
Pulling her punch on purpose, Qianli Xun halted her fist right in front of Zhu Xiyan's chest as Chen Haoyu dodged, pulling back crisp: "Step aside, Father! Today I'm beating Chen Haoyu straight—until he's whipped into shape, zero chance I marry into the Chen house!"

“既如此，我们快进去，我想陪娘多说会儿话。”
Having weathered decades of official storms, Zhu Xiyan saw straight through two kids' amateur theater without blinking: "Jingyuan, handle them. Father is going in to rest." As gate servants swung the side door open, Zhu Xiyan flicked his sleeve and strolled inside as if nothing had happened!

千里寻正要走，被陈浩宇拉住：“与你娘聊天儿，什么时候都可以，我们还有重要的事情商议，改日再聊。”
Two whole punches and zero reaction—deflated, Qianli Xun groaned: "Brother, why'd Dad ignore us?!"

千里寻甩开陈浩宇的手：“别拉着老娘！”
Zhu Jingyuan chuckled: "Blatant stage play put on for Father—even I spotted it in one glance, let alone Father!"

“淑真，又说不文雅的话。”朱景元批评道，转向陈浩宇，“快跟我进去。”
"Stage play?!" Chen Haoyu yelped aggrieved. "Look at my face, Brother-in-law—does this purple shiner look fake to you?!"

陈浩宇心中有事，哪有心情上药：“这么点儿伤不碍事，我与朱淑真有事商议。”
Already noting the swelling, Zhu Jingyuan gestured inside: "Come on in and let maids put salve on it."

今日没成功，证明此办法无法撼动朱晞颜。千里寻寻思，与向夫人聊天儿有的是机会，不在乎一时半刻，还是尽快想想接下来该怎么办。
"Fussing over an outsider and leaving your own little sister hanging!" Qianli Xun pouted.

“哥，我改日再找娘聊。”千里寻向陈浩宇使眼色，两人没等朱景元说话，迅速登上各自的马车。
"Of course you come first! You gave him that bruise—getting him treated keeps him from holding a grudge! Come inside too and sit a spell with Mother."

朱景元自语道：“你们两个还是别瞎忙活，根本没用！”
Longing to visit Madam Xiang—banned from moving back or not, standing right at the door without saying hello to Mother felt wrong—she brightened: "Fine, let's go! Want to chat with Mother!"

两人来到街市，陈浩宇透过马车的窗户瞧到一家茶肆，探出脑袋，大声喊道：“喂！有家茶肆！进去坐下来商议！”
Just as she stepped forward, Chen Haoyu caught her arm: "Chat with your mother anytime—we've got urgent business to plot! Next time!"

千里寻本不愿与陈浩宇坐下喝茶，为退婚，勉强答应。
Yanking her arm free: "Keep your hands off me!"

两人跳下马车，走进茶肆，要壶茶。陈浩宇倒主动，提起茶壶，为二人分别倒上一盏。
"Mind your language, Shuzhen," Zhu Jingyuan chided, turning back to Chen Haoyu. "Come on inside."

千里寻盯着陈浩宇的动作，丝毫不觉得他是懂礼之人：“你出的主意实在烂！一点儿用都没有！”
With schemes burning in his head, who cared about ointment? "Tiny scrape, nothing to fuss over—got urgent business with Zhu Shuzhen!"

茶已斟完，陈浩宇放下茶壶：“怎么能怪我头上？我说明日去春香楼，你偏要在朱府门口堵你爹……”
With today's stunt flopping flat against Zhu Xiyan's iron wall, Qianli Xun figured she could visit Mother anytime—plotting Plan B came first. "Catch Mother another day, Brother!" Trading a quick look with Chen Haoyu, before Zhu Jingyuan could protest, both hopped straight into their waiting carriages.

“什么？春香楼？”
Left shaking his head at the gate, Zhu Jingyuan muttered, "Quit spinning your wheels, you two—total waste of time!"

陈浩宇才意识到口误，将聚香楼说成春香楼：“口误口误，谁让这两个地方名字只有一字之差？”
Rolling into the market quarter, Chen Haoyu spotted a teahouse out his window and leaned out yelling: "Hey! Teahouse right there—let's sit and talk!"

“差之毫厘，谬以千里。什么叫口误？你这种人满脑子只有春香楼。”
Loath to share a table with him yet desperate to kill the betrothal, Qianli Xun hopped down and followed him inside. Ordering a pot of tea, Chen Haoyu surprisingly played gentleman, pouring a bowl for each of them.

“别光顾着批评我，怎么样？明日再到聚香楼走一趟？”
Watching him pour without buying his manners for a second, Qianli Xun snapped: "Lousy idea you cooked up—total bust!"

“跟今日有什么区别？还不都是当着我爹的面把你揍一顿？”千里寻瞧着陈浩宇脸上的淤青，“老娘倒不介意在你另外半边脸上再来一拳。”
Setting down the teapot, Chen Haoyu protested: "Blaming ME?! I said stage it tomorrow at Chunxiang Brothel—YOU insisted ambushing your dad at his gate..."

陈浩宇摸摸淤青之处，感到疼痛，嘴角咧了一下：“区别大了，今日周围没外人，明日都是临安官员，说不定仪王也在，事闹大，你爹面子上挂不住，八成会主动到我爹娘面前要求退婚。”
"Wait—CHUNXIANG BROTHEL?!"

“怎么闹大？”
Catching his Freudian slip—Chunxiang Brothel instead of Juxiang Tower!—he scrambled: "Slip of the tongue! Both names sound practically identical!"

“明日你打我，我向所有官员诉说我的苦楚，包括仪王，他们见我可怜，见你凶悍，肯定会劝说你爹。”
"Off by a hair, off by a thousand miles! Slip of the tongue my foot—your head is stuffed full of Chunxiang Brothel!"

千里寻露出怀疑的目光：“在场的都是临安官员，我的名声岂不被你搞坏？”
"Quit lecturing—what do you say? Hit Juxiang Tower tomorrow?"

陈浩宇反问道：“你觉得名声被搞坏与嫁给我哪个更让你难以接受？”
"How's tomorrow any different from today—just decking you in front of my dad again?" Eyeing his bruised cheek, she smirked: "Not that I mind evening out the other side!"

千里寻仔细思忖，她的名声已被流言所坏，再坏些也没什么，只要不嫁给陈浩宇比什么都强。再说明日申简辰肯定在，凭他们的关系，他必然出手相助。
Touching his tender bruise with a wince, he explained: "Huge difference! Today nobody watched; tomorrow EVERY Lin'an official is packed in there—maybe even Prince Yi! Blow it up big so your dad loses face in front of the whole court, and odds are he marches straight to my parents calling off the wedding!"

“成交！明日聚香楼不见不散！”
"Blow it up how?"

朱淑真爽快答应，陈浩宇当然开心。一开心，竟然生起从未有过的怜悯之情。
"You whack me, I bawl out my misery to every official and Prince Yi! Seeing pitiful me and fierce you, they'll all push your dad to drop it!"

“其实我觉得你也挺可怜，嫁给林觉那小子，被他家暴不说，还闹到和离，又被乔文仙给骗了，好不容易跟我定亲，我又瞧不上你。实在不行，本公子勉为其难把你给娶了，你我之间只有一纸婚书，其他跟婚前一模一样，你过你的，我过我的，只要你别对我动拳头，什么都好商量……”
Qianli Xun squinted wary: "Every official in town watching—won't that trash my name?"

千里寻用拳头在陈浩宇面前晃晃：“老娘要钱有钱，要貌有貌，要才华有才华，要拳头有拳头，何须你可怜？你要有同情心，就同情同情那些被你欺负的良家妇女。别让老娘再见到你欺负人，见到必然一拳将你打趴下！”
Chen Haoyu shot straight back: "Which sounds worse—a trashed name, or marrying ME?!"

朱淑真的拳头在陈浩宇面前晃着，他瞅着粉白的拳头，小心避开，生怕真的打来，实在想不通，朱家的女词人什么时候变得这么厉害。
Weighing it one beat: her name was already dragged through rumor mud anyway; worse couldn't beat marrying Chen Haoyu! Plus Shen Jianchen would definitely be there tomorrow—given who they were to each other, he'd back her up! "Deal! Tomorrow at Juxiang Tower—don't flake!"

千里寻收起拳头，未喝一口茶，起身离开。
Thrilled she agreed fast, a bizarre flicker of pity crept into Chen Haoyu. "Truth is, you got a pretty raw deal yourself: wed that Lin Jue guy, beaten into divorce, played by Qiao Wenxian, now stuck engaged to me when I don't even want you! Tell you what—if all fails, I'll take one for the team and wed you in name only! Paper marriage, zero change from single life: you do you, I do me—long as you keep those fists to yourself, smooth sailing..."

陈浩宇才放心坐正，瞧着朱淑真走出茶肆的背影，不屑道：“哼！张狂个什么劲儿！拳头再厉害还不是被你爹想许给谁就许给谁！”
Waving a tight fist inches from his nose, Qianli Xun snarled: "Got cash, got looks, got talent, got fists—who asked for YOUR pity?! Save your sympathy for every decent girl you harass! Catch you bullying anyone again and I drop you cold!"

回望月轩的路上，千里寻坐在马车里，回想刚才陈浩宇说过的话……
Flashing back from her pale fist inches away, Chen Haoyu shook his head amazed—when on earth did Zhu Manor's refined poetess turn into a bruiser?! Pulling back her fist without touching one drop of tea, Qianli Xun rose and walked out.

当着陈浩宇的面并未在意，此时回想，也不由自顾怜悯起自己来。
Sitting straight once she cleared the door, Chen Haoyu sneered after her: "Act tough all you want! Iron fists and all, your dad still hands you over to whoever he pleases!"

她仔细思索，若不与林觉和离，也许就没后面的事，在外人看来，应该还算不错。可要是跟林觉继续过下去，她会觉得自己的人生连呼吸的力气都没有。她并不后悔和离，不管后面有多少不幸等待着她，她都会积极应对。
Riding back to Wangyue Pavilion alone, Chen Haoyu's words echoed back through Qianli Xun's mind. Shrugged off to his face, they stirred a quiet sting of self-pity in the hush of the carriage. Sure, had she never divorced Lin Jue, outsiders might have called her life smooth—yet staying trapped under Lin Jue would have suffocated every breath out of her! Zero regrets over divorcing; whatever storms waited ahead, she would meet every single one head-on.
