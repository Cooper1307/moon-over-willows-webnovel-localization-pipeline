第150章 复杂的感情纠葛
## Chapter 150: Tangled Hearts Under One Roof

有丫鬟进来，为来客摆上果品和茶水，又为每个人斟茶，然后悄声退下。
Maids filed in setting out fresh fruit and tea, pouring a cup for every guest before withdrawing in silence.

大家刚品几口茶，佳和郡主带着喜兰前来，众人见礼后，乔梦容说：“哥哥，后厨的酒菜已就绪，是先用饭？还是先品诗词？”
Barely had everyone taken a sip when Princess Jiahe arrived with Xilan. Once guests finished greeting her, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Brother, the kitchen's feast is ready—dine first, or appraise poetry first?"

申简辰举行诗会目的本不在诗会，故而说道：“已到饭点儿，等人到齐，先用饭，边用饭边欣赏歌舞，用过饭后，再听书，最后品鉴诗词。”
Never caring a whit about poetry in the first place, Shen Jianchen announced, "It's mealtime already. Once everyone's here, we eat first with music and dance, then listen to storytelling, and save poetry appraisal for last."

除陈浩宇，在场其他人难以理解，不是诗会吗？怎么品诗词成了附带？面对仪王，大家不敢说什么，只能任由安排。
Every guest except Chen Haoyu blinked in disbelief—a poetry salon where poetry got shoved to the dessert menu?! Before Prince Yi, nobody dared peep, nodding along.

陈浩宇总算明白，仪王为何请他来，原来品诗词不过是最后一项，吃过饭，再听听说书，到了品鉴诗词的环节，随便找个理由向仪王辞别，也省得在众人面前显得无所事事。
Chen Haoyu lit up—now he got why Prince Yi invited him! Feast first, listen to storytelling, then cook up an excuse to duck out before poetry started—zero awkwardness!

“好的，我这就安排。”乔梦容对喜兰说，“告诉后厨，人到齐后，立刻上酒菜。”
"Right away!" Qiao Mengrong instructed Xilan: "Tell the kitchen to bring out wine and dishes the second everyone is seated."

喜兰领命而去。
Xilan hurried off.

乔梦容很自然地在乔文仙对面的桌子坐下，扫视一遍在座的人，只有淑真还未到。
Taking the table straight across from Qiao Wenxian, Qiao Mengrong scanned the hall—only Shuzhen remained.

今日大家到得极早，就连哥哥到的也早。哥哥是身份尊贵的王爷，本应最后一个到，可乔公子一来，他便陪着乔公子坐在这里等待众人。
Everyone had turned up super early today—even Prince Yi himself! By rights a royal host arrived dead last, yet he had been sitting right here waiting since Young Master Qiao stepped in.

若不是因为乔公子，梦容绝对会误会哥哥想要早些见到某个参加诗会之人。
If not for Young Master Qiao keeping him company, Qiao Mengrong might almost have wondered if he was eager to see another guest.

正想着，淑真携两个丫鬟进来……
Right on cue, Shuzhen walked through the doorway with both maids trailing behind...

众人向幽栖居士瞧去。
Every pair of eyes swiveled toward Youqi Jushi.

乔梦容觉得淑真的气色比上次见时好许多，她应该已从伤痛中走出。
Qiao Mengrong smiled seeing bright color back in Shuzhen's cheeks—she had stepped out of the worst of her heartbreak.

她不由观察乔公子，在淑真进来那一刻，他的双眼依然放光，只是那光芒中带有些许失落。
Glancing across at Young Master Qiao, she saw his eyes flare bright at Shuzhen's entrance, shadowed by raw sorrow.

再观察苗知瑜，她瞧过淑真后，扭头看了一眼身旁的乔公子，然后低下头，拿起茶盏，装作喝茶的样子。
Glancing next at Miao Zhiyu, she watched her look from Shuzhen to Qiao Wenxian beside her, then lower her eyes and lift her tea bowl to hide behind a sip.

哥哥举办诗会，乔梦容开心，可想不通，哥哥为何要把有感情纠葛的人都叫到一起，难道是为看热闹？可哥哥从来不是喜欢看热闹的人，哪怕是失忆后的哥哥也并非是对热闹感兴趣之人。
Happy as Qiao Mengrong was about the salon, she could not fathom why Prince Yi had packed every tangled love rival into one room—he had never cared for cheap gossip, before amnesia or after.

乔梦容只顾关注他人，却没有认真观察他的哥哥。幽栖居士走进时，她的哥哥双眼中的光芒并不比乔文仙差。
So busy reading everyone else's faces, she missed her own fiancé's eyes—shining no less ardent than Qiao Wenxian's the instant Youqi Jushi walked in!

直到千里寻行礼问候：“仪王，佳和郡主，淑真这厢有礼。”
At last Qianli Xun stepped forward and bowed in greeting: "Greetings, Prince Yi, Princess Jiahe."

申简辰才担心大家看出他的心思，不得已收回眼神。
Afraid the whole room might read his heart, Shen Jianchen forced himself to pull his gaze away.

“幽栖居士最后一个来，比本王架子还大，今日既然是诗会，就罚幽栖居士现场做首词供大家鉴赏。”申简辰故意批评，只是想多与心爱的姑娘说句话而已
"Dead last to arrive, Youqi Jushi—bigger airs than a royal prince! Since this is a poetry salon, your penalty is composing a fresh ci on the spot for everyone!" Shen Jianchen teased harsh on purpose just to trade an extra sentence with her.

“淑真虽是最后一个到达，并未迟到，王爷不该罚我。既然是诗会，我作首词也是应该，不必王爷以此作为惩罚的借口。”
"Last to arrive doesn't mean late, Prince Yi—no grounds to penalize me! Still, at a poetry salon composing a ci is only proper; no need to dress it up as punishment."

千里寻心想：“仪王愿意早到，跟老娘有什么关系！其他人看你是王爷的份上，给你面子，早早来到，才显得我有些晚。你却抓住这点当众说我，实在过分！”
Qianli Xun fumed inside: "Not my fault you camped out at dawn! Everyone else kissed up to a prince showing up early—now you pick on me in public?!"

朱淑真被仪王批评，陈浩宇很开心，也佩服她竟然敢怼仪王。陈浩宇更觉得这个女子娶不得，一旦娶回家，天天被她怼，多没面子。
Watching Prince Yi needle Zhu Shuzhen delighted Chen Haoyu, though he had to admire her nerve talking back to royalty—making him even surer she was poison to marry! Imagine getting roasted like that by your own wife every day!

令陈浩宇没想到的是，仪王竟然一点儿不生气，让朱淑真挑位置坐下，还说很期待她的词作，跟那天打他时完全不一样。
To Chen Haoyu's shock, Prince Yi did not flare up one bit—inviting Zhu Shuzhen to sit and beaming that he looked forward to her ci! Night and day from the prince who had him beaten black and blue!

仪王既然吩咐坐下，千里寻自然归座，只剩下东方尘泽与梦容之间的座位，她没得挑，乖乖坐在那里。
Directed to take a seat, Qianli Xun found only one spot left—sandwiched between Dongfang Chenze and Mengrong—and settled in.

坐下时与梦容相视一笑，扭过头，自然看到代替她即将嫁给文渊的苗知瑜，千里寻以微笑与苗知瑜打招呼。
Trading a warm smile with Mengrong, she glanced across to see Miao Zhiyu, the bride taking her place beside Wenyuan, and offered a courteous smile.

千里寻觉得此时的微笑与刚才面对梦容的微笑不同，她尽量让自己显得并不介意、并不尴尬。
Very different from the smile she gave Mengrong—polished to look effortless, unbothered, free of awkwardness.

苗知瑜也以微笑回应，她看上去很开心，成功得到文渊，又怎能不开心。
Miao Zhiyu returned a bright smile—winning Wenyuan had her glowing!

还是无意中接触到不愿接触又难免要接触的目光，文渊直愣愣地盯着她，眼神中充满痛苦和不舍。
Then came the gaze Qianli Xun dreaded yet could not dodge: Wenyuan staring straight through her, eyes drenched in agony and longing.

千里寻不敢看下去，她怕自己会旧情复燃，赶紧低下头，盯着桌面的果品。
Afraid looking one second longer would reignite old ashes, Qianli Xun dropped her eyes straight onto her plate of fruit.

乔梦容担心大家尴尬，说道：“哥哥，人已到齐，该摆饭了。”
Sensing tension thickening the room, Qiao Mengrong nudged, "Everyone's here, Brother—time to serve lunch."

仪王竟然愣住心神，根本没听到梦容说话。
Lost in a daze, Prince Yi did not even hear her.

梦容从哥哥的眼神判断，他看向淑真。难道哥哥是帮乔公子观察淑真的心意？乔梦容大声唤道：“哥哥！”
Following his locked gaze straight to Shuzhen—assuming he was gauging Shuzhen's mood on Young Master Qiao's behalf—Qiao Mengrong called louder: "Brother!"

申简辰才大梦初醒，将目光从千里寻身上挪开：“哦，什么事？”
Snapping awake, Shen Jianchen tore his eyes off Qianli Xun: "Oh—what is it?"

乔梦容强调道：“人已到齐。”
Qiao Mengrong repeated: "Everyone is here."

申简辰说：“开饭吧。”
"Serve the feast, then!"

早有下人在门外听候，清楚地听到仪王的传令，迅速跑去后厨。没多久，丫鬟们开始在每位客人面前摆上酒菜。
Servants stationed outside heard the command and sprinted to the kitchen; moments later maids streamed in laying wine and dishes before every guest.

摆酒菜的过程，夏果在小姐身后一直观察乔公子，他胖了些，可瞧那样子，根本未放下小姐。
Standing behind her mistress during the service, Xiaguo kept her eyes glued to Young Master Qiao—he had gained a little weight, yet one look told her he still had not let Miss go.

南双见夏果一直盯着自家公子，没看他一眼，心中着实不满。
Watching Xiaguo stare nonstop at his master without sparing him half a glance burned Nan Shuang raw.

苦荞瞅瞅夏果，果然一进屋，眼中只有乔公子。她很不满，觉得夏果是丫鬟出身，能跟南双已不错，竟然把心思放在乔公子身上，实在不该。
Glancing over at Xiaguo, Kuqiao frowned too—sure enough, eyes glued straight on Young Master Qiao! Born a maid, Nan Shuang was already a fine match; chasing Young Master Qiao was sheer folly.

她就不会有不符身份的想法，安心喜欢白风。想到白风，不由向仪王身旁瞧去，白风也看向她。
Not like Kuqiao, happy with her own match—glancing toward Prince Yi's elbow, she caught Bai Feng looking right back at her!

三位公子面对尴尬的气氛，选择沉默不语。既然前来，主人怎么安排，就怎么享受，他们决定到品鉴诗词环节再开口不迟。
Sensing the electric awkwardness humming across the hall, the three young masters stayed mute, figuring they would enjoy the feast and wait until poetry time to speak up.

美食摆好后，申简辰对众人说：“大家随便用，不必拘束。”然后向一旁的白风示意。
Once every dish stood set, Shen Jianchen invited: "Dig in, everyone—no standing on ceremony!" Then he nodded toward Bai Feng.

白风明白王爷的意思，拍了拍手掌，立刻从两边走来舞伎，舞乐响起，舞伎们开始扭动腰肢跳起曼妙的舞姿。
Clapping his hands, Bai Feng signaled dancers to glide out from both wings as music struck up, slender waists swaying in graceful rhythm.

千里寻心想，仪王该不会不懂什么叫诗会？连个诗词的影子都没见到，又是美食，又是舞蹈，这不就是举行宴会吗？又何必以诗会的名头邀请大家前来？
Qianli Xun wondered if Prince Yi even knew what a poetry salon was—zero poetry, nonstop food and dancing! Why not just call it a banquet?!

吃了一会儿，欣赏一会儿舞蹈，千里寻不想再面对文渊的眼神，决定出去走走，透透气，她悄声对一旁的梦容说：“我出去方便一下。”
After eating a spell and watching a dance, eager to escape Wenyuan's burning stare for fresh air, Qianli Xun leaned close to Mengrong: "Stepping out to freshen up."

乔梦容小声说：“快去快回。”
Qiao Mengrong whispered back: "Don't take long."

千里寻点点头，起身往出走，苦荞自然跟上，拿上小姐的披风为她披好。夏果不愿离去，谁让乔公子就坐在对面。千里寻并不管她，带着苦荞从后门出去。
Nodding, Qianli Xun rose and headed out, Kuqiao hurrying right behind to drape her winter cape over her shoulders, while Xiaguo stayed planted—no way she was walking away from Young Master Qiao! Leaving Xiaguo behind, Qianli Xun slipped out the rear door with Kuqiao.

来到外面，呼吸到新鲜空气，心情舒畅许多。冬季，幽林别居的树木变得光秃秃，萧条的景色中倒有几分别致。
Stepping into crisp outdoor air cleared the heaviness from her lungs. Bare winter branches stretched stark over Youlin Retreat, lending bleak charm to the grounds.

光秃的树木无法阻挡冬日的阳光，时不时投射在千里寻的身上，她觉得冬日阳光带来的温暖比屋内火盆的暖意更加自然清新。
With leaves stripped away, pale winter sunlight poured down onto her shoulders, cleaner and gentler than indoor braziers.

她顺着小径，慢慢散步，享受冬日的光辉。走着走着，几株正在开放的梅花映入眼帘。幽林别居到处是树木，梅花倒成此处少有的景致。
Strolling unhurried along the garden path enjoying the sun, a cluster of blooming winter plum trees caught her eye—a rare splash of life amid the bare woods.

千里寻兴致萌生，上前欣赏起来，顺便将鼻子凑近，去嗅梅花清幽之香，不敢说沁人心脾，也令人头脑清晰。
Delighted, she walked closer and leaned in to breathe the crisp, cool plum fragrance, sharp enough to clear her mind.

她本以为已完全放下文渊，见到他，才发现完全放下没那么容易，可她必须装作已放下，不装又能如何？
She had fooled herself thinking she was over Wenyuan; one glimpse today proved letting go was not that simple—still, she had to keep acting over him. What else could she do?

肯定是时间太短，到明年春季，或者夏季，或者更长时间，她肯定能完全放下文渊。
Just needed more time—by spring, or summer, or a year down the line, she would get past him for good.

嗅完梅花的清香，千里寻继续行走在小径中。苦荞不愿打扰小姐，默默跟在身后……
Having drunk in the plum scent, she strolled further down the path while Kuqiao trailed quiet behind...

乔文仙见淑真出去，也起身往外走。申简辰分外紧张，尽力掩饰，装作轻松的样子享受美食和舞蹈。
Back indoors, watching Shuzhen step out, Qiao Wenxian rose and headed outside too. Tension gripped Shen Jianchen, though he forced an easy mask, keeping his eyes on the food and dancers.

陈浩宇在心中笑道：“你们两个最好旧情复燃，再闹出些事来，本公子就可以此为借口退婚。呵呵……”无意中瞥到失落的苗知瑜，举起酒杯，示意她干杯。
Chen Haoyu smirked inside: "Go on—rekindle that flame and cause a scandal so I get a free ticket out of this wedding! Heheh..." Catching sight of Miao Zhiyu's downcast face, he lifted his cup in a cheeky silent toast.

陈浩宇的动作，苗知瑜根本不理会，自顾忧虑。乔公子已与她有婚约，不仅见面一句话不说，还时刻用目光关照幽栖居士，见她出去，也跟着出去，令她苗知瑜情何以堪？
Ignoring Chen Haoyu's antics, Miao Zhiyu stewed in humiliation: engaged to her, yet Young Master Qiao had not spoken one word to her, kept staring at Youqi Jushi, and chased straight outside the second she left! Where did that leave her pride?!

三位公子也甚是担忧，又无可奈何，只好在心中祈祷，千万不要发生不该发生的事。
The three young masters traded anxious looks, praying no disaster broke out.

乔公子既然已离开，夏果没必要再站着，也走出去，尾随在乔公子身后，见周围没人，大胆唤道：“乔公子！”
The instant Young Master Qiao stepped out, Xiaguo saw no point standing indoors and slipped after him. Catching up where nobody else stood around, she called bold: "Young Master Qiao!"

乔文仙停下脚步，与南双转过身。南双问：“你唤我家公子干什么？”
Halting, Qiao Wenxian turned around with Nan Shuang, who snapped, "What do you want with my master?"

夏果并未理会南双，靠近后说：“乔公子已有婚约，不要再盯着我家小姐不放，再说我家小姐已将乔公子彻底放下，您就不要再给她制造麻烦。”
Ignoring Nan Shuang flat out, Xiaguo stepped close: "You're an engaged man now, Young Master Qiao—stop clinging to my mistress! Miss has already let you go for good; stop bringing her trouble!"

“制造麻烦？是淑真让你对我这么说的？”乔文仙显得很痛苦。
"Bringing her trouble? Did Shuzhen tell you to say that?" Pain twisted Qiao Wenxian's face.

夏果强调道：“我天天跟着小姐，她心里怎么想，我尽知。乔公子的未婚妻也在，您偏偏追着我家小姐出来，苗姑娘会怎么想？您有没有考虑过？”
Xiaguo pressed firm: "I'm beside Miss every hour—I know every thought in her head! Your fiancée is sitting right inside while you chase after my mistress—did you spare one thought for how Miss Miao feels?!"

“我管不了那么多，只想跟淑真说话。”乔文仙转身打算继续去追。
"I don't care about any of that—I just need to speak with Shuzhen!" Turning sharp, Qiao Wenxian started back down the path.

夏果快走几步，挡在面前：“乔公子，醒醒吧！您与我家小姐已不可能，放了她吧！放下，您轻松，我家小姐也轻松。”
Darting ahead to block him, Xiaguo cried, "Wake up, Young Master Qiao! You and Miss are impossible—let her go! Let go, and both you and Miss get peace!"

“放下？哪有那么容易？你让开！”
"Let go? Think it's that easy?! Step aside!"

夏果没有办法，只能将婚约道出：“乔公子可能还不知，我家小姐已与陈公子订亲。”
Out of ammo, Xiaguo dropped her trump card: "Maybe Young Master Qiao hasn't heard—Miss is already betrothed to Young Master Chen!"

“此事我已知，订亲又如何？我不也已订亲？”
"I already know! So what—aren't I betrothed too?!"

夏果见此法无用，瞎编道：“乔公子有所不知，我家小姐曾跟她的嫂嫂聊天时，说她已爱上陈公子，心甘情愿嫁到陈家……”
Seeing truth fail, Xiaguo spun a brazen lie: "What Young Master Qiao doesn't know is Miss told her sister-in-law she's already fallen in love with Young Master Chen and is marrying into the Chen house with all her heart..."

这个消息对乔文仙来讲如同晴空惊雷，他已顾不上去追淑真，原路返回，来到屋内。
A thunderbolt out of a clear sky! Forgetting all about chasing Shuzhen, Qiao Wenxian spun round and stormed straight back into the hall.

乐舞声掩盖住他的怒气，所有人并未注意到。直到他端起桌上的一盏茶，泼在陈浩宇的脸上，大家才惊讶不已。
Drowned out by music and dancing, nobody noticed his fury—until he snatched a tea bowl off the table and flung its contents straight into Chen Haoyu's face! Gasps froze the entire hall.

申简辰摆摆手，舞乐停下，舞伎们纷纷退出。
At a wave from Shen Jianchen, the music died and the dancers scurried out.

陈浩宇无缘无故被泼茶水，甚是生气：“乔文仙，你抽哪门子风？为何用茶水泼我？”
Drenched out of nowhere, Chen Haoyu exploded: "What the hell's gotten into you, Qiao Wenxian?! Splashing tea in my face?!"

乔文仙抓住陈浩宇的衣领：“谁让你跟淑真订亲？”
Seizing Chen Haoyu by the collar, Qiao Wenxian roared: "Who gave you the right to get betrothed to Shuzhen?!"

三位公子感到惊讶，幽栖居士竟然和纨绔子弟订婚，简直难以置信。
Shocked murmurs rippled through the three young masters—Youqi Jushi betrothed to a notorious rake?! Unthinkable!

“奇怪？刚才你已知，也不见打我，怎么出去一趟这么生气？”陈浩宇疑惑的同时，被乔文仙气到，也想气气他，继续笑道，“你跟苗知瑜都有婚约，有什么权利管我家淑真？我与淑真是越打感情越深，见你们不成，赶紧求我爹上朱家提亲，没成想一提一个准儿，知府大人满口应下，还夸我是乘龙快婿。”
"Weird—knew about it ten minutes ago and didn't touch me, step outside two minutes and now you go feral?!" Furious himself and itching to twist the knife, Chen Haoyu sneered: "You're engaged to Miao Zhiyu—what right do YOU have meddling with my Shuzhen?! Fighting just made Shuzhen and me fall harder for each other! Seeing you two flop, I begged my dad to propose to the Zhus—and boom, bull's-eye! The Prefect jumped at it and called me the son-in-law of his dreams!"

申简辰在心中冷笑道：“纨绔子弟！满嘴谎言！”
Shen Jianchen sneered inside: "Shameless dandy—lying through his teeth!"

乔文仙无法忍受，举起拳头就要打下去，被陈浩宇用手挡住。乔文仙的力气不如陈浩宇，拳头自然难以落下。
Snapping past reason, Qiao Wenxian cocked a fist to swing, only for Chen Haoyu to catch his wrist cold—stronger by far, locking Qiao Wenxian's punch dead in mid-air.

申简辰看不下去，大声说：“文渊是我兄弟，请陈公子担待些。”
Unable to sit by, Shen Jianchen barked loud: "Wenyuan is my brother—show some restraint, Young Master Chen!"

仪王发话，陈浩宇不好再与乔文仙敌对下去，一把推开他：“看在仪王的面子上，我不跟你计较，记着，朱淑真以后是我的人，你不要再惦记，你的未婚妻在那里，你的做法会令她伤心。”
At Prince Yi's command, Chen Haoyu could not push further and shoved Qiao Wenxian back: "For Prince Yi's sake I'll let this slide. Just remember: Zhu Shuzhen belongs to me now, so keep your eyes off her! Your own fiancée is sitting right there—acting like this only hurts her!"

乔文仙趔趄了一下，被南双扶住。当着仪王的面不好再闹下去，他怔怔地站在原地，痛苦地品味着陈浩宇刚才说的“淑真以后是我的人，你不要再惦记”，早已忘记陈浩宇说的那些不满朱陈两家订亲的话，他忽然之间觉得整个世界都失去色彩……
Staggering back into Nan Shuang's arms, Qiao Wenxian could not brawl before Prince Yi. Frozen where he stood, Chen Haoyu's taunt—"Shuzhen belongs to me now, keep your eyes off her"—hammered through his skull, wiping out every earlier complaint Chen Haoyu had made about hating the match until the whole world drained gray...

苗知瑜站起来，走到乔文仙身边，对陈浩宇说：“陈公子此言差矣，乔公子的做法并不会伤到知瑜，相反我很理解。幽栖居士与乔公子的事知瑜从头到尾尽知，知瑜也希望他们能走到一起，怎奈乔家与苗家已订亲，知瑜也毫无办法。”
Rising from her chair, Miao Zhiyu stepped beside Qiao Wenxian and faced Chen Haoyu with calm poise: "Young Master Chen is mistaken. Young Master Qiao's feelings don't hurt me at all—quite the opposite, I understand entirely. I know every chapter of Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao's bond and wished they could end up together; only because our families sealed this betrothal were our hands tied."

苗知瑜一段至情至理的话，令在场所有人对她刮目相看，就连乔文仙也对她投去赞赏的目光，莫名的愧疚感油然而生。
Her generous, gracious speech drew admiring glances from every corner of the room—even Qiao Wenxian looked at her with fresh respect, a quiet wave of guilt stirring in his chest.

夏果心想，苗知瑜通情达理，定是个好相处的主母，若成为乔公子的妾室，以后的日子应该好过些。
Watching from the side, Xiaguo beamed inside: reasonable and gracious—Miao Zhiyu would make an easy primary wife to live under once Xiaguo became Young Master Qiao's concubine!

苗知瑜见已俘获众人之心，对乔文仙说：“乔公子还是坐下继续享用美食，我想幽栖居士应该是出去方便，一会儿便回，你若有话与她说，待她回来，再与她讲。”
Knowing she had won every heart in the hall, Miao Zhiyu turned warm toward Qiao Wenxian: "Sit back down and finish lunch, Young Master Qiao. Youqi Jushi only stepped out to freshen up and will be right back—whatever you need to tell her, tell her once she returns."

面对此种状况，乔文仙只能坐回位置，本来心中有千言万语想对淑真说，被苗知瑜这么一劝，他倒难以启齿。
Backed into grace by Miao Zhiyu, Qiao Wenxian sank back into his chair—a thousand words that had burned to burst out at Shuzhen now caught silent in his throat.
