# 第64章 神柳之下的相遇
# Chapter 64: Meeting Beneath the Divine Willow

---

千里寻转过身，仪王出现在面前，她感到有些恍惚，为何此刻会对他产生莫名的熟悉感？

Qianli Xun turned around. Prince Yi appeared before her. She felt somewhat dazed—why did she feel this inexplicable sense of familiarity with him at this moment?

---

直到看见他身旁的乔姑娘，现在应该称她为佳和郡主，她手里拿着红绸，面带笑容盯着自己，千里寻才清醒过来。

Only when she saw the young lady Qiao beside him—now she should call her Princess Jiahe—holding red silk in her hand and watching her with a smile did Qianli Xun come to her senses.

---

看样子他们刚从后山下来，红绸应该是从尼姑庵求的。

Judging by appearances, they had just come down from the back mountain. The red silk was probably obtained from the nunnery.

---

千里寻屈膝行礼："仪王、佳和郡主，没想到在这里也能碰到二位贵人。"

Qianli Xun curtsied and bowed: "Prince Yi, Princess Jiahe, I didn't expect to run into Your Highnesses here."

---

乔梦容面带微笑："看来，你已知道我与哥哥的身份，知道也无妨，但不能因为我们的身份，你就生分，希望幽栖居士把我和哥哥当朋友一样看待。"

Qiao Mengrong smiled: "It seems you already know my brother's and my identity. Knowing is fine, but you shouldn't keep a distance because of who we are. I hope Youqi Jushi will treat my brother and me as friends."

---

穿越而来有了家人，还缺朋友，两位贵人愿意屈尊与她做朋友，千里寻很是乐意。

Having gained family after traveling through time, she still lacked friends. The two nobles were willing to condescend to be her friends, and Qianli Xun was more than happy.

---

"只要二位贵人不嫌弃，我当然愿意与你们成为朋友。"

"As long as Your Highnesses don't despise me, I'd naturally be willing to become friends with you."

---

乔梦容说："当然不嫌弃，是不是哥哥？"

Qiao Mengrong said: "Of course we don't despise you. Isn't that right, brother?"

---

申简辰回道："梦容不嫌弃，我又怎会嫌弃？再说幽栖居士是文渊的好友，也就是我与梦容的好友。"

Shen Jianchen replied: "If Mengrong doesn't despise her, how could I? Besides, Youqi Jushi is Wenyuan's good friend, which makes her my and Mengrong's good friend too."

---

两位贵人平易近人，千里寻轻松许多，刚刚因地位悬殊产生的距离感荡然无存，反而羡慕起佳和郡主与仪王的感情。

The two nobles were approachable, and Qianli Xun relaxed considerably. The sense of distance just created by their vast difference in status vanished completely. Instead, she found herself envying the relationship between Princess Jiahe and Prince Yi.

---

仪王的话语中流露出对佳和郡主迁就之意，在千里寻看来，只有一个男人深爱着一个女人，才懂得处处迁就对方。

Prince Yi's words revealed an accommodating attitude toward Princess Jiahe. In Qianli Xun's view, only a man who deeply loves a woman knows how to accommodate her in every way.

---

"你们是要在神柳上系红绸吗？"

"Are you going to tie red silk on the Divine Willow?"

---

乔梦容回道："是的，今日哥哥无事，专程陪我到尼姑庵请了条红绸，上面写下我和哥哥的名字，现在我与哥哥要一起把红绸系在神柳的枝条上。"

Qiao Mengrong replied: "Yes. Today my brother had nothing to do, so he specially accompanied me to the nunnery to get a red silk. We wrote our names on it, and now my brother and I are going to tie the red silk together on the branches of the Divine Willow."

---

申简辰心想，红绸上写着"赵朴"二字，他就当是替赵朴来挂红绸，希望赵朴在天有灵，到月老处走一趟，给他的妹妹重新牵一条婚姻的红线。

Shen Jianchen thought to himself: the red silk had "Zhao Pu" written on it. He would treat this as hanging the red silk on behalf of Zhao Pu. He hoped that if Zhao Pu had a spirit in heaven, he would visit the Matchmaker God and tie a new red thread of marriage for his sister.

---

千里寻目睹佳和郡主牵起仪王的手靠近神柳。

Qianli Xun watched Princess Jiahe take Prince Yi's hand and approach the Divine Willow.

---

喜兰先挑好一根柳枝，佳和郡主并不满意。仪王个高，用另一只手从较高处拉下一根。佳和郡主很满意，放开仪王的手，亲自将红绸在仪王拽下的柳枝上绑好，二人相视而笑。

Xilan first picked a willow branch, but Princess Jiahe wasn't satisfied. Prince Yi was tall, so he used his other hand to pull down a higher one. Princess Jiahe was very pleased. She let go of Prince Yi's hand and personally tied the red silk on the branch Prince Yi had pulled down. The two looked at each other and smiled.

---

千里寻叹道："二位的感情真令人羡慕。"

Qianli Xun sighed: "Your relationship is truly enviable."

---

乔梦容今日很开心，哥哥如此配合，她似乎找回从前的感觉。听到幽栖居士的慨叹，回应道："乔公子对幽栖居士的感情也令梦容羡慕不已。"

Qiao Mengrong was very happy today. Her brother was so cooperative that she seemed to have recaptured the feelings of the past. Hearing Youqi Jushi's sigh, she responded: "Young Master Qiao's feelings for Youqi Jushi also make Mengrong extremely envious."

---

申简辰放开柳枝，那柳枝向上浮去，将红绸带到较高处，可离最高处的红绸还差得很远。

Shen Jianchen let go of the willow branch. The branch floated upward, taking the red silk to a higher position, but it was still far from the highest red silk.

---

乔梦容望向高处："哥哥，为何有人将红绸挂得那么高？不知是什么人这么厉害？"

Qiao Mengrong looked up: "Brother, why did someone hang the red silk so high? I wonder who is so capable?"

---

听到佳和郡主问题，苦荞心想："当然是我家小姐，只有她有这等本事。"

Hearing Princess Jiahe's question, Kuqiao thought to herself: "Of course it's my young lady. Only she has such ability."

---

申简辰也瞧向高处："估计是不想被人摘去，才挂那么高。"

Shen Jianchen also looked up: "Probably hung it that high so no one would take it down."

---

乔梦容担心起自己刚挂的红绸："我们系的红绸会不会被别人拽走？"

Qiao Mengrong worried about the red silk they had just hung: "Will someone pull away the red silk we tied?"

---

申简辰宽慰道："应该不会，这么多红绸，也没见有人把它们拽走。你要是担心，可以每日过来检查一番，一旦被人拽走，大不了我再带你到尼姑庵求一条，下次绑得更高些。"

Shen Jianchen reassured her: "Probably not. There are so many red silks here, and I haven't seen anyone take them down. If you're worried, you can come check every day. If someone does take it down, at worst I'll take you back to the nunnery to get another one, and we'll tie it even higher next time."

---

"像那条红绸一样高，可以吗？"

"As high as that red silk, is that okay?"

---

"可以。"

"That's fine."

---

千里寻听着二人的对话，问道："仪王难道会轻功？"

Listening to their conversation, Qianli Xun asked: "Does Prince Yi know lightness skill?"

---

申简辰疑惑地问："你怎么知道我会轻功？"

Shen Jianchen asked suspiciously: "How do you know I know lightness skill?"

---

千里寻解释道："能把红绸挂得那么高，不会轻功的人很难实现。"

Qianli Xun explained: "Only someone who knows lightness skill could hang the red silk that high. It would be very difficult for someone without that skill."

---

申简辰稍有疑惑："难道幽栖居士也会轻功？"

Shen Jianchen was slightly puzzled: "Could it be that Youqi Jushi also knows lightness skill?"

---

千里寻隐瞒道："我……我……我一介女流，怎么可能会那玩意儿。"

Qianli Xun concealed the truth: "I... I... I'm just a woman. How could I possibly know that kind of thing?"

---

申简辰来到幽栖居士面前："可我听说幽栖居士偷偷练习功夫，为了和离，宁可被练兵官打伤，都不还一拳。"

Shen Jianchen came before Youqi Jushi: "But I heard that Youqi Jushi secretly practiced martial arts. For the sake of mutual divorce, you'd rather be beaten by the Military Training Officer than throw a single punch in return."

---

千里寻猜想，仪王肯定是从东方尘泽等人口中得知，看来隐瞒不下去："偷偷练过一些，只为防身，至于轻功，我可是一点儿都不会。"

Qianli Xun guessed that Prince Yi must have learned this from Dongfang Chenze and others. It seemed she couldn't hide it anymore: "I secretly practiced some, just for self-defense. As for lightness skill, I don't know any of it at all."

---

申简辰来了兴致："幽栖居士可否与我切磋一番？"

Shen Jianchen became interested: "Would Youqi Jushi spar with me?"

---

千里拳威力不小，只怕将仪王打趴下，即使不打趴下，赢了他，他面子上也挂不住。他是王爷，若败下阵来，难免以后会找麻烦，还是不比试得好。

Thousand Mile Fist was quite powerful. She was afraid she'd knock Prince Yi down. Even if she didn't knock him down, if she beat him, he'd lose face. He was a prince. If he lost, he'd inevitably cause trouble later. It was better not to spar at all.

---

再说仪王与文渊熟识，千里寻可不想让文渊知道她是一个能把男人打败的母老虎。文渊喜欢的应是像朱淑真那样又温柔又有才情的女子，故而，万万不可与仪王比试，不管输赢都都不可。

Besides, Prince Yi was familiar with Wenyuan. Qianli Xun didn't want Wenyuan to know she was a tigress who could beat men. Wenyuan should like gentle and talented women like Zhu Shuzhen. Therefore, she absolutely must not spar with Prince Yi, regardless of winning or losing.

---

"还是不了，我是弱女子，又怎能比得过仪王？"

"Better not. I'm a weak woman. How could I possibly compete with Prince Yi?"

---

对方拒绝，申简辰不好再继续要求，想起醉云楼诗会一事，说道："刚来临安时，我跟着文渊参加东方公子举行的诗会，拜读了幽栖居士的新词，一番品鉴下来，想着什么时候碰到幽栖居士，定要叮嘱一番。今日遇见，也算缘分，叮嘱的话还是要说的。"

Since she refused, Shen Jianchen couldn't press further. Remembering the poetry gathering at Drunken Cloud Tower, he said: "When I first came to Lin'an, I followed Wenyuan to attend the poetry gathering hosted by Young Master Dongfang. I read Youqi Jushi's new lyrics. After appreciating them, I thought about when I'd meet Youqi Jushi and definitely give some advice. Meeting you today is fate, so I still need to give that advice."

---

"仪王请讲。"

"Please speak, Prince Yi."

---

"以后写诗作词能不能不要写个人隐私？"

"In the future, when writing poems and lyrics, could you not write about personal privacy?"

---

千里寻愣住："仪王的话是何意？"

Qianli Xun was stunned: "What does Prince Yi mean?"

---

申简辰解释道："你把想要离开夫君的想法写到《断肠谜》中，被人品来品去，可觉得舒服？再说，以写诗作词的名头给别人提供八卦自己隐私的机会，似乎有欠妥当。被本朝人议论也就罢了，只怕幽栖居士美名远播，诗词会被后人一代又一代记住，你的隐私也会被一代又一代人议论，等你百年之后，在天之灵想要安息都难。"

Shen Jianchen explained: "You wrote your thoughts of wanting to leave your husband in 'Heartbreak Riddle.' People appreciate it and discuss it. Does that feel comfortable? Besides, using the name of writing poems and lyrics to give others the opportunity to gossip about your own privacy seems inappropriate. Being discussed by people of this dynasty is one thing, but I'm afraid Youqi Jushi's famous reputation will mean your poems and lyrics will be remembered by future generations, and your privacy will be discussed by generation after generation. After you pass away, even your spirit in heaven will find it hard to rest in peace."

---

仪王一番嘱咐不无道理，可千里寻依然要为朱淑真辩解几句。

Prince Yi's advice was not without reason, but Qianli Xun still wanted to say a few words in defense of Zhu Shuzhen.

---

"感谢仪王叮嘱，淑真受教。淑真生在闺阁，面对的只有闺阁之情，略微懂些写诗作词的技巧，有情无处可宣泄，便用诗词表达。如若我能像男儿一样立身朝堂，或征战沙场，或许诗词的内容会有所不同。可惜我的天地和战场在闺阁，这不是我的错，是由时代决定的。别的我不强求，只要大家能从我的诗词中看出这个时代女子的不易，我便心满意足。"

"Thank you for your advice, Prince Yi. Shuzhen has learned from it. Shuzhen was born in the boudoir. All she faced were the emotions of the boudoir. She slightly understood the techniques of writing poems and lyrics. With emotions but nowhere to vent them, she expressed them through poetry. If I could stand in the court like a man, or fight on the battlefield, perhaps the content of my poems would be different. But unfortunately, my world and battlefield are in the boudoir. This is not my fault—it's determined by the times. I don't ask for anything else. As long as people can see from my poems the difficulties of women in this era, I'll be completely satisfied."

---

朱淑真讲得至情至理，反而让申简辰觉得自己境界狭隘："幽栖居士既如此认为，我也不必多说什么，希望你以后写出更多诗词，让后人看到本朝闺阁女子的不易。"

Zhu Shuzhen spoke with such heartfelt emotion and sound reasoning that Shen Jianchen instead felt his own perspective was narrow: "Since Youqi Jushi thinks this way, I don't need to say more. I hope you'll write more poems and lyrics in the future, so future generations can see the difficulties of boudoir women in our dynasty."

---

很快将仪王的观念转变过来，千里寻对自己感到满意："那是自然。"

Having quickly changed Prince Yi's perspective, Qianli Xun felt satisfied with herself: "That's only natural."

---

乔梦容走过来邀请道："幽栖居士要不要去幽林别居坐坐？"

Qiao Mengrong walked over and invited: "Would Youqi Jushi like to visit Secluded Forest Villa?"

---

"幽林别居？"

"Secluded Forest Villa?"

---

乔梦容指着不远处的树林："就在那片树林里，我与哥哥住在里边，乔公子也在，幽林别居是乔家在城外的宅子。"

Qiao Mengrong pointed to the woods not far away: "It's right in those woods. My brother and I live inside. Young Master Qiao is also there. Secluded Forest Villa is the Qiao family's residence outside the city."

---

原来她用轻功飞身神柳之上看到的府宅是乔家的。

So the residence she saw when she used lightness skill to fly up the Divine Willow belonged to the Qiao family.

---

千里寻看看自己身上，出来也没有认真打扮过，决不能如此草率去见文渊。于是婉拒道："还是不了，明日文渊约我去西湖，自然见到，若今日见了，明日又见，那多没意思。"

Qianli Xun looked at herself. She hadn't bothered to dress up properly when she came out. She absolutely couldn't see Wenyuan so carelessly. So she politely declined: "Better not. Wenyuan invited me to West Lake tomorrow. We'll naturally meet then. If we meet today and meet again tomorrow, that would be too boring."

---

乔梦容笑道："有道理，改日幽栖居士也约乔公子一起到尼姑庵请条红绸挂在柳树上。"

Qiao Mengrong laughed: "That makes sense. Another day, Youqi Jushi should invite Young Master Qiao to the nunnery together to get a red silk and hang it on the willow tree."

---

"一定一定。"

"Definitely, definitely."

---

千里寻行完礼，目睹二位贵人离开神柳旁，沿着池塘走到对岸，身影消失在幽林中。

After Qianli Xun finished bowing, she watched the two nobles leave the Divine Willow, walk along the pond to the opposite shore, and their figures disappear into the secluded forest.

---

仪王刚刚出现时，千里寻产生错觉，误以为他是申简辰，此刻想来，甚觉可笑。他怎么可能是申简辰？肯定是她太想念申简辰的缘故，以至于见到有些类似的人，不由自主产生错觉。

When Prince Yi first appeared, Qianli Xun had an illusion and mistakenly thought he was Shen Jianchen. Thinking about it now, it was quite laughable. How could he be Shen Jianchen? It must be because she missed Shen Jianchen so much that when she saw someone somewhat similar, she couldn't help but have an illusion.

---

周围只剩下苦荞，千里寻才走到神柳跟前，伸开双臂，将神柳抱住，小声说："神柳，我已有喜欢的人，喜欢一个人的感觉很不错，让人觉得整个世界都变得无比美好。即便如此，也希望你帮我找到申简辰，他对我来说就像……"

With only Kuqiao around, Qianli Xun walked up to the Divine Willow, spread her arms and embraced it. She whispered: "Divine Willow, I already have someone I like. The feeling of liking someone is wonderful—it makes you feel the whole world has become incomparably beautiful. Even so, I still hope you'll help me find Shen Jianchen. To me, he's like..."

---

千里寻不知如何表达她对申简辰的感觉，好像用任何语言都不恰当，只好无奈地放开神柳："苦荞，我们回去吧。"

Qianli Xun didn't know how to express her feelings for Shen Jianchen. It seemed no language was appropriate, so she had no choice but to let go of the Divine Willow helplessly: "Kuqiao, let's go back."
