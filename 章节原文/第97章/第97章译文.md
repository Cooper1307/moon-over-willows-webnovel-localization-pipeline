## Chapter 97: The Nuns Remove the Red Silk Ribbons

Arriving at Juxiang Tower, they chose a private room. Lin Jue insisted on paying the bill, yet Prince Yi stood firm in refusing, leaving him no choice but to give in.

Allowed by Prince Yi to sit at the same table, Bai Feng was thrilled. Prince Yi was a man of status who would never wolf down his food, and Training Officer Lin came from money and wouldn't fight over dishes either. Today, Bai Feng could feast on this entire spread to his heart's content.

Once the waiter at Juxiang Tower laid out the wine and dishes, Training Officer Lin's presence saved Bai Feng even the chore of pouring wine for Prince Yi. He dug straight in with big mouthfuls; he never had to stand on ceremony in front of His Highness.

Lin Jue raised his wine cup. "I owe Prince Yi a huge debt of gratitude for helping me today. Let me drink three cups in a row; please drink at your leisure."

Lin Jue tossed back the first cup in one gulp, poured a second, and downed that too. As he reached for a third, Prince Yi stopped him.

"No matter how well you hold your liquor, Training Officer, you can't drink like that. If you get drunk, how am I supposed to talk business with you? Besides, I didn't do anything to help you." Shen Jianchen played dumb.

Lin Jue set down his cup. "Prince Yi coming to the Military Training Office helped me, and inviting me to dine with you helped me too. Without that, how would those three senior officers ever promote me?"

Shen Jianchen put on a look of sudden realization. "So that's how it is. Here I thought they had it all arranged beforehand and were just reporting it to me."

"Not at all. Half a dozen men had their eyes on the Commandant's post, and each of the three commanders wanted to slip one of his own people in. If Prince Yi hadn't shown up and treated me with such favor, they would never have promoted me on their own."

Eating as he listened to Lin Jue, Shen Jianchen caught the rot of Lin'an's officialdom in those few casual sentences. With an emperor who lived for pleasure and leaned on treacherous officials, how could the bureaucracy be anything but corrupt? When the very roots of the Great Song were rotting away, how healthy could its branches and leaves be?

"Who would have guessed I'd stumble into doing you a good turn without even trying? Since I've brought you some good fortune, Training Officer, I'll ask you to grant me one favor in return."

"Say the word, Prince Yi. I'd climb a mountain of blades or plunge into a sea of fire without a second thought."

"Don't make it sound so dramatic. It's a minor matter. I heard... you've taken a fancy to Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo. Is that true?"

So Prince Yi had come over Xiaguo! For a single maid to draw Prince Yi's attention, Zhu Xiyan must have put him up to it. No wonder the old man had dodged him yesterday.

Lin Jue dared not hide it, nor could he. "It's true. On the day Zhu Shuzhen and I went through our mutual divorce, I stated in front of everyone that I wanted to take Xiaguo as a concubine, and the Prefect gave his word in public too. Yet both Zhu Shuzhen and the maid refuse. Tell me, Prince Yi, why is my love life always so full of snags?"

"It's full of snags because you keep fixating on people who aren't right for you, or who were never yours to begin with..." Shen Jianchen thought of himself. The whole reason he was in pain was that he had fallen for someone he could not have, neither before crossing over nor after.

"Prince Yi's words have woken me straight from a dream. From now on, I need to change my mindset."

"Now you're talking sense. Let Xiaguo go. After all, Xiaguo's mistress is Youqi Jushi. Now that you've divorced her, stop tangling yourself up in her affairs and go live a good life of your own. That way, you're at peace and everyone else is at peace too."

Lin Jue had no choice but to heed Prince Yi's words. "I will follow Prince Yi's counsel. I... I'll let Xiaguo go."

"That's the spirit. Come on, eat up. Since you've done me this honor, Training Officer, from today on, you're one of my men."

Thrill surged through Lin Jue. Giving up one Xiaguo to have Prince Yi count him as one of his own? Not a loss at all. Unlucky in love, lucky in officialdom; Heaven was fair after all.

To a man, his career mattered most. As for romance, Lin Jue had already given up Zhu Shuzhen; letting go of a single Xiaguo was effortless. Once a man had wealth and rank, what kind of woman couldn't he have? Today, he made a clean break with Zhu Shuzhen in his heart and vowed never to pester her again.

Having persuaded two people in a single day, Shen Jianchen felt not a drop of triumph. After parting from Lin Jue, there was nowhere to go but Youlin Retreat, yet heading back meant facing Wenyuan's beaming excitement, and he had no desire to go home.

He told the coachman to drive to the foot of the hill, then sent him back ahead along the main road. With Bai Feng in tow, Shen Jianchen followed the trail at the hill's base toward the Divine Willow.

Walking along, Bai Feng asked, "Why is Your Highness heading to the Divine Willow again?"

Shen Jianchen did not answer, just kept walking ahead.

Bai Feng dared not ask again. When Prince Yi went quiet, something was weighing on his mind. Bai Feng could not figure it out: Prince Yi had no interest in meddling with military or civil affairs, had wealth and rank, and had Princess Jiahe by his side. With nothing to fret over and nothing lacking, what could he be dissatisfied with? Why was he unhappy?

When they reached the Divine Willow, four or five Buddhist nuns stood beneath it untying the red silk ribbons; more than half had already been taken down.

Curious, Shen Jianchen stepped forward and asked, "Venerable Sister, why are you taking down the red silk ribbons?"

One nun untied a ribbon, let the willow branch spring back, and placed the strip of silk into a bamboo basket on the ground.

"Amitabha. Benefactor may not know this, but too many red ribbons weigh down the Divine Willow. The Abbess instructs us to take them down every half year and bring them back to the nunnery to burn, while the Abbess recites sutras over them herself."

"I see. Still, won't the people who hung those ribbons feel heartbroken if they find out?" Shen Jianchen had already returned Qianli Xun's ribbon to her, yet Qiao Mengrong's was still up on the tree. If she learned it had been taken down, she was bound to be upset.

The nun replied, "Rest assured, Benefactor. The Divine Willow has a spirit of its own. The instant a ribbon touches its branches, it commits the wish of the one who hung it to memory. Leaving the silk behind afterward is just a formality."

Bai Feng figured the nun was spinning a yarn. How could a tree be that magical!

"Thank you for telling me, Venerable Sister." After thanking her, Shen Jianchen thought that if Mengrong ever asked, at least he now had a comforting answer to give her so she would not be too sad.

All the nuns were women, shorter than men, and could not reach the ribbons tied higher up.

One nun said, "Why isn't Old Man Li here yet? He's tall; we need him to reach the high ones."

Another replied, "Someone in Old Man Li's family fell ill, so he can't get away right now."

Seeing their predicament, Shen Jianchen said, "Bai Feng, come help these Venerable Sisters with me."

"Yes, Young Master." By now, Bai Feng knew down to a tee when he ought to call His Highness "Young Master."

The nuns could not thank them enough.

With two men lending a hand, the high ribbons were no longer a problem. Before long, every last ribbon was down, filling two whole bamboo baskets. After offering their thanks, the nuns lifted the baskets and left.

Stripped of the red ribbons, the willow before him convinced Shen Jianchen more than ever that this was the very Divine Willow he and Qianli Xun had seen before crossing over.

The Divine Willow stood unchanged, yet the people beneath it were no longer who they had been. He had Qiao Mengrong; Qianli Xun had Wenyuan. Fate had laid everything out in neat order. Why keep clinging on...

"Brother, what are you doing here?"

Qiao Mengrong's voice came from behind him. Shen Jianchen turned around to find Mengrong wearing a surprised expression.

"I should be asking you. What brought you all the way out here?"

"Brother was gone all day, and I got bored, so I came to take a walk beneath the Divine Willow you like so much. I never expected to run into you here! Young Master Qiao told me Brother went to see the Training Officer to settle Youqi Jushi's trouble." Before he could reply, Qiao Mengrong asked in alarm, "Where are the red ribbons on the willow? What happened to them?"

Shen Jianchen told her the truth. "Before you arrived, the nuns from the nunnery took every ribbon down to carry back and burn."

"What? The ones Brother and I hung together, with both our names written on them—just burned like that?" Sure enough, Qiao Mengrong's face fell.

Shen Jianchen comforted her. "No need to feel sad. The nun told me that the moment you tie on a ribbon, the Divine Willow commits your wish to memory. The ribbon itself is just a formality."

"Brother isn't just making that up to coax me?"

"The nun said it herself. If you don't believe me, ask Bai Feng. He heard it with his own ears."

Qiao Mengrong looked to Bai Feng, her eyes full of doubt.

Bai Feng replied, "That's what the nun said, word for word. His Highness isn't fooling you, Princess."

A smile lit up Qiao Mengrong's face. "As long as the Divine Willow remembers Brother's wish and mine, whether the ribbon stays up doesn't matter."

Bai Feng figured the nun had fooled His Highness, and now His Highness was fooling the Princess. Sigh! Sometimes people just loved hearing sweet lies, and even beamed over them.

"Brother, shall we head back?"

"You only just got here."

"I came because I was thinking of Brother. Now that I've found you, there's no need to look at the Divine Willow anymore."

Shen Jianchen nodded in agreement. He realized that facing Mengrong today no longer weighed so heavy on his heart. Qianli Xun was about to find her own happy home, and he had made up his mind to accept what fate had laid out for him.

Yet in his heart he still loved Qianli Xun. Letting go was no easy thing; all he could do was press those feelings down to the bottom of his heart, bit by bit.

As they crossed by the pond and approached the secluded grove, Shen Jianchen came to a halt. "Mengrong, at next year's Mid-Autumn Festival, I'll marry you and bring you home as my bride."

Though Qiao Mengrong had always known it, this was the first time since being brought back to life that Prince Yi had spoken of marrying her on his own. Overwhelmed with joy, she stepped forward and threw her arms around him.

"I'm so happy today!"

Shen Jianchen tried to make himself wrap his arms around the girl pressed against his chest, yet his raised hands refused to come down...

Both Xilan and Bai Feng turned their backs to give them privacy, happy for their two masters.

Just then, frogs began croaking from the pond: "Ribbit, ribbit, ribbit..."

Shen Jianchen remembered the night of his duel with Qianli Xun. The frogs had been croaking in that exact same way.
