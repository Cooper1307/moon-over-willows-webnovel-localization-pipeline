## Chapter 224: Hog-Tied and Locked Up

Too furious to care a fig for ladylike appearances, Qianli Xun hopped down from the carriage with ten servants behind her and stormed straight into Chunxiang Brothel. Seeing their mistress march in without blinking, Hongdou and the other two maids cast aside their shyness and followed right behind.

Inside, business hummed as usual—patrons watching dancers, sipping wine, flirting with courtesans. Every jaw dropped at the sight of a respectable young lady striding in flanked by servants, three of them young maids. Never before had a gentlewoman raided the hall; every head swiveled to stare.

Recognizing her on sight, the madam whispered, "Mercy me! Young Master Chen's tigress tracked him all the way here!" Putting on a nervous smile, she hurried over. "Looking for Young Master Chen, Young Madam?"

"Where is he?" Qianli Xun's face promised immediate bruised noses and black eyes.

Not daring to hide a husband from his wife, the madam pointed up. "Upstairs with Chun Jinmo."

"Lead the way." Qianli Xun's tone dripped ice over fire.

Trying to give her patron a heads-up, the madam spotted Scarface on the landing and yelled, "Scarface! Tell Young Master Chen his wife's here!"

Already spotting the raid, Scarface bolted into the private room to sound the alarm.

Only then did the madam gesture toward the stairs. "Right this way, Young Madam."

Before they reached the foot of the staircase, Chen Haoyu's shout boomed down. "Zhu Shuzhen! What the hell are you doing here? Go home!"

Looking up, Qianli Xun saw him descending the steps adjusting his rumpled robes, clearly fresh from a tryst, with the same three hired bodyguards trailing behind.

Thinking of last year's slander and staring at his smug, punchable face snapped her last nerve. Launching upward with light-foot skill, she soared straight up the staircase, seized him by the collar, dropped back to the main floor, and shoved him sprawling onto the floorboards.

Humiliated before a packed house, Chen Haoyu blustered from the floor, "Who do you think you're shoving? I won't stoop to fighting you in public—wait till we get home and see how I handle you!"

Having already tasted the seven servants' fists—and seeing from that leap that their mistress outclassed all three of them combined—the three bodyguards traded one glance and bailed. Stepping up to Chen Haoyu, they bowed quick. "We don't meddle in family matters, Young Master Chen. See you another day!"

Left high and dry as they bolted out the door, Chen Haoyu scrambled to his feet—just as Qianli Xun barked, "Tie him up!"

Asheng whipped out a length of rope, and with two men helping, trussed Chen Haoyu tight in three seconds flat.

"Zhu Shuzhen, you've gone too far!" he roared. "I'm your husband! Bullying your own husband in public—everyone's going to laugh at you!"

Sure enough, half the patrons craned their necks grinning at the spectacle.

Unwilling to linger another second in a brothel, Qianli Xun ordered, "Haul him out!"

Just as she spun toward the door, a woman's voice floated down behind her. "Please wait, Young Madam!"

Qianli Xun turned back. Descending the stairs came a slender, pretty woman swaying like a willow branch in the breeze—Chun Jinmo, no doubt.

Reaching her, Chun Jinmo curtsied deep. "Greetings, Young Madam."

"What do you want?"

"I have read your poems, Young Madam—gentle, poignant, full of tender feeling. Meeting you in person today, you look nothing like your verses."

"I'm in no mood to critique poetry. Goodbye." Qianli Xun turned to leave again.

Chun Jinmo stepped across her path. "Wait, Young Madam, I didn't come to talk poetry. I heard you and Young Master Chen signed a prenuptial contract promising not to meddle in each other's lives. Why barge in here today and treat your husband like this?"

Learning Chen Haoyu had blabbed their private contract to a courtesan made Qianli Xun's blood boil hotter. "Who the hell are you to stick your nose in my affairs? What prenuptial contract? Never heard of it! Quit wasting my breath!"

She turned a third time—and Chun Jinmo hurried forward, dropping to her knees in front of the whole crowd.

"I didn't lay a finger on you! What are you kneeling for?"

"Young Madam, ever since I was sold into Chunxiang Brothel, Young Master Chen has been my only man, looking out for me all along. If he stops coming, my life here will turn to hell. Have mercy and let Young Master Chen go—Jinmo will be forever grateful!"

Seeing her kneel melted Chen Haoyu's heart into mush. "Look how pitiful she is, Zhu Shuzhen! You hate my guts—you're happier without me, and the Chen family has plenty of other sons, yet Jinmo can't survive without me! I mean the world to her! Untie me right now and quit making a scene!"

With her grudge still scorching hot, Qianli Xun stepped right up to his face. "Starting today, the more you want something, the harder I'll make sure you don't get it. Want to look after her? I'll make sure you never lay eyes on her again!"

Turning back to Chun Jinmo, she spoke cold and clear. "Sure, you have a rough life, and I'm not heartless—I just don't have the bandwidth to care right now. Besides, how do you know I never needed pity? Where were you when I was drowning in misery, and what was your precious Chen Haoyu doing? I'm no saint and no savior. Kneeling to me buys you nothing!"

With that, she marched her crew out the door.

Dragged past the threshold, Chen Haoyu craned his neck back shouting, "Hang tight! I'll be back soon!"

Left crumpled on the floorboards, Chun Jinmo wept. With a wife that fierce, clinging to Young Master Chen was over. Would she really end up serving someone like Master Jiang?

The madam stepped over and helped her up. "Jinmo, you're a courtesan—face the truth. Any lord who calls your name and puts down silver gets served, unless you get lucky like Jinqiao and someone buys you out as a concubine. I hear you hinted to Young Master Chen plenty of times to redeem you, and he never gave a straight answer. Never meant to—just came here to play. With a tigress like that at home, forget him buying you out. Pick a new patron who might actually redeem you! Master Jiang isn't a bad choice—older and rough-looking, sure, yet dead serious about you. Look how many brawls he fought with Young Master Chen over you! Word is he doesn't even have a primary wife yet. Plenty of opportunity there."

Some of the madam's words sank in, and Chun Jinmo went upstairs dabbing her eyes.

Back in Moxiang Courtyard, Qianli Xun sat behind her desk glaring at Chen Haoyu trussed in a chair before her.

Playing cool, he smirked. "Why the staring? Never seen a handsome man before? Don't tell me you missed me these past days!"

"Hongdou! Slap him twice for me!"

Chen Haoyu bristled. "Zhu Shuzhen, are you out of your mind?"

Hongdou faltered. "Miss, I dare not—he's the young master."

"Touch me and I'll sell you straight to Chunxiang Brothel!" Chen Haoyu barked.

Hongdou froze.

"What's gotten into you today, Zhu Shuzhen?" Chen Haoyu demanded. "We had a prenuptial contract—no meddling! Did a dog eat your word?"

Qianli Xun lifted a sheet of paper from her desk. "Here's your contract. Watch close." Popping the lid off her bronze incense burner, she rolled the sheet tight and dropped it inside. Flames licked up and reduced it to gray ash in seconds.

Chen Haoyu's jaw dropped. Only when she clapped the lid back on did he find his voice. "Since you burned it, the deal's void! Hand back my properties, restore this courtyard and hall the way they were, and pack off to the back courtyard—I'm moving back in here!"

"Don't you feel ridiculous, Chen Haoyu? If I dared burn the contract, think I'm handing back one copper of the properties or one tile of this courtyard? Dream on!"

"Aren't you scared I'll tell Grandmother and my parents so they come teach you a lesson?"

"You have to make it out of this courtyard first!"

Sensing something had shifted—before, she had ignored him and preached peaceful coexistence; why go nuclear today?—Chen Haoyu damped his temper and asked, "Did you swallow gunpowder this morning? I haven't wronged you once since our wedding. Why pull this?"

That question lit the fuse all over again. "Forgotten the rotten filth you pulled, Chen Haoyu?"

A cold jolt hit his gut. Burning the contract, foaming at the mouth—had she found out about the rumors? He asked warily, "Did you hear something?"

Itching to smash his nose in, Qianli Xun rose, fists clenched, and stalked toward him. "Last year, you spread lies that wrecked my engagement and made me the laughingstock of Lin'an! Time to settle that tab!"

Terror iced his veins—she knew! I'm a dead man! Seeing her fist whistle straight toward his face, he squeezed his eyes shut. Crash! Splinters exploded beneath him as he hit the floorboards—she had punched the wooden chair into kindling instead.

Exhaling in relief, he cracked his eyes open and still tried to wriggle out. "Your own fault for bullying me nonstop! If you hadn't pushed me around, I never would've spread those rumors! Besides, people didn't just gossip because of me—you wrote 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed' yourself—"

"Hongdou! Slap him right now!" Qianli Xun barked.

Given a second direct order, Hongdou hesitated no longer. Stepping up, she declared, "Even if you sell me to a brothel, Young Master, I'm slapping you for Miss!"

Smack! Smack! Two ringing blows landed square on Chen Haoyu's cheeks.

Watching from the side, Kuqiao felt furious on her mistress's behalf yet winced at the sting on his face.

Hands tied behind him, unable to rub his throbbing cheeks, Chen Haoyu yelped, "You little wench! Why hit so hard?"

Qianli Xun crouched down and patted his reddened cheek. "I meant to beat you half to death, and downgraded it to two slaps. That's mercy—still complaining?"

Unmoved, Chen Haoyu spat, "Zhu Shuzhen, I'm going to divorce you!"

"Divorce me? Not a chance! From today on, I have two goals in Chen Manor: live a great life, and make yours miserable."

Fear flickered in his eyes. "W-what are you going to do to me?"

"Everything you enjoy, I'll forbid," Qianli Xun enunciated word by cold word. "Everything you hate, I'll force you to do."

Chen Haoyu sputtered speechless. "You... you..."

Qianli Xun stood. "Guards! Haul Chen Haoyu to Boya Study in the back courtyard and lock him up!"

Asheng and another servant hoisted him up, marched him to Boya Study, and untied his ropes. The instant his arms came free, Chen Haoyu swung at Asheng hoping to bolt—only for Asheng to block him solid. "Behave yourself, Young Master. Crossing Miss won't end well for you."

Outside, servants slammed the shutters and began nailing wooden planks across the windows.

Chen Haoyu stormed to the sill roaring, "Stop that! I own this courtyard!"

Behind him, Asheng stepped out and snapped a heavy padlock onto the door.

Chen Haoyu pounded the wood shouting, "Zhu Shuzhen, this isn't over! Just wait! This is Chen Manor, not Zhu Manor, and not your Wangyue Pavilion!"

A hushed voice drifted through a crack in the boards. "Young Master? Young Master, are you all right?"

For the first time in his life, Qinggua's voice sounded like heaven. Chen Haoyu pressed his face to the shutters. "Qinggua, thank goodness! The tigress is torturing me! Run straight to Old Madam Xia, Father, and Mother—tell them I'm repudiating her! Go!"

"Right away, Young Master!"

Qinggua sprinted toward the front yard to sound the alarm, yet had not even cleared the lotus pond before Qianli Xun's voice froze him in his tracks. "Qinggua! Where are you rushing? Playing messenger for your master?"

Skidding to a halt, Qinggua scrambled for a fib. "Not at all, Young Madam! I was just looking for an open space to practice Phantom Sidestep!"

"You already know it inside out—no need to practice. I worried you didn't have a bed, so from today on, you're rooming with Chen Haoyu in Boya Study." Taking zero chances on him running his mouth, she marched over, grabbed him by the collar, and hauled him toward the back courtyard.

When the padlock rattled open, Chen Haoyu beamed, sure Qinggua had summoned Grandmother to spring him—only for Qinggua to fly stumbling through the doorway before the door slammed shut again.

"Didn't want you lonely, Chen Haoyu," Zhu Shuzhen's voice drifted in, "so I sent you a roommate!"

Chen Haoyu hurled himself against the door, hammering the panels. "Zhu Shuzhen! The second I get out of here, I swear I'll divorce you!"
