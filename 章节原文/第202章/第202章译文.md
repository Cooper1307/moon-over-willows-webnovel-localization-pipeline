## Chapter 202: A Smooth Property Transfer

After Qianli Xun finished serving morning tea and withdrew, the Chen elders chatted briefly before dispersing. Moments later, Butler Wang came to Chen Jianglin's hall just as he and Yao Qingyan were setting up a chess game.

"Master, Madam, one delicate matter calls for your ruling," Butler Wang bowed. "Young Madam ordered every single maid in Second Young Master's courtyard cleared out to use her own people. Being her first day, I dared not cross her on the spot, yet thought it best to ask Master and Madam."

Placing a white stone casually on the board, Yao Qingyan snorted. "I could never stand that pack of maids Haoyu dragged in from who-knows-where anyway! Meant to tell him to clear them out ages ago—good riddance!"

Dropping a black stone, Chen Jianglin agreed. "Madam is right. Do exactly as Shuzhen wishes."

"What did Haoyu say about it?" Yao Qingyan asked.

"Naturally displeased, yet clearly helpless against Young Madam—gave in at the end."

"Proves we picked the right bride!" Chen Jianglin chuckled. "Obey Shuzhen's instructions, Butler Wang."

"One more tricky detail, Master," Butler Wang hesitated. "Half those maids want to stay inside Chen Manor. On the walk over I mentioned reassigning them to Second Master and Third Master—who jumped at the chance—then bumped into Second Madam and Third Madam, who flatly refused, calling Second Young Master's maids far too coquettish for their yards."

Holding a chess stone poised, Yao Qingyan slapped it decisively onto the board. "Dismiss the whole lot cleanly! Saves stirring trouble in every other courtyard!"

"Dismiss them all," Chen Jianglin backed her.

"Yes, Master." Butler Wang withdrew.

Playing on in relaxed warmth, Yao Qingyan teased him for letting her win every game, while Chen Jianglin grinned that watching her beam in victory was half the fun. Mentioning Haoyu, Yao Qingyan wondered aloud if Old Madam might kick up a fuss once she heard his maids were cleared—Chen Jianglin merely shrugged to wait and see.

After Yao Qingyan won, the pair strolled through Chen Manor's spring-bright gardens and rested in a kiosk—until Steward Fang, chief accountant of Chen commerce, came hurrying up. "Master! First thing this morning, Young Madam's Matron Wei came demanding every asset under Second Young Master's name be transferred into Young Madam's name! Too huge for me to decide solo, so I put her off to consult Master first."

Dismissing maids was peanuts; moving Chen assets required real thought. Telling Steward Fang to hold tight, they headed straight back inside and sent Yingxue to summon Matron Wei.

Entering moments later, Matron Wei found Yao Qingyan rising warmly to clasp her hands. "Now that you live at Chen Manor, Matron, drop by my rooms often!"

Curtsying smoothly despite her held hands, Matron Wei declined the seat of honor Chen Jianglin offered, settling politely on a lower chair even as both masters praised her fame—saying half Youqi Jushi's renown belonged to Matron Wei managing daily burdens so her genius could bloom. Chuckling warmly over tea, Matron Wei saved her hosts awkwardness by opening the topic herself: "Master and Madam summoned me over transferring Young Master's assets to my mistress, I assume?"

"Sharp as ever, Matron Wei," Chen Jianglin praised. "Nothing major, only sudden—we hoped to learn the background."

Drawing a folded sheet from her sleeve, Matron Wei let Yingxue pass it to Yao Qingyan, who read it with a sigh before handing it to Chen Jianglin. Knitting his brows as he read, Chen Jianglin exhaled. "Ah—so these terms are why Miss Zhu agreed to marry into our house."

"Do not feel saddened, Master and Madam," Matron Wei soothed smoothly. "Marriage follows Heaven's design—whatever brought her through your gate, Miss is a Chen now."

"Did they consummate the marriage last night?" Yao Qingyan asked softly.

"Neither wanted the match at first, yet Heaven still bound them together," Matron Wei answered tactfully. "Give them patience—nobody can foresee every twist ahead, yet good things will come."

Plainly not consummated yet—still, Matron Wei's steady calm smoothed Chen Jianglin's brow. "Once under one roof, consummation comes in due time. Since Haoyu signed this prenuptial pact himself, we elders cannot break his word—and besides, whether under Haoyu's name or our daughter-in-law's, Chen wealth stays inside Chen gates!" Glancing back at the contract, a wide grin broke across his face. "One clause here delights me most: allowing Haoyu only twenty taels of silver a month—and he STILL signed and thumbprinted it! Miracle!"

Handing the sheet back via Yingxue for Matron Wei to stow away, he heard her add smoothly, "Exactly as Master says—wealth stays in the family! Miss is no gold-digger; using property simply gave her peace of mind to stop fighting fate. Master's sharp eyes nailed the real truth: my young mistress is Young Master's sole match, and her presence brings pure gain to the Chens."

"Matron saw straight through why Jianglin and I insisted on this bride!" Yao Qingyan laughed at last. "Nothing left to hesitate over!"

Chen Jianglin nodded crisply. "Go back to Xinglong Pavilion and finalize it with Steward Fang, Matron—I will send word right ahead."

With yesterday's crimson wedding drapes cleared away, Xinglong Pavilion stood back in full commercial swing—hub of over two hundred managers and clerks housed along Chen Manor's west side with their own west gate, rarely crossing paths with Chen family members residing north and east. Stepping back onto the vast ground floor lit by daytime lanterns, Matron Wei walked past busy desks of copyists, runners, and ledger binders toward the innermost accounting bay humming with crisp abacus beads, where Steward Fang sat reviewing books.

"Back again, Steward Fang!"

Rising with a cheerful grin, Steward Fang greeted her: "Master's word just arrived—let us get this done right away!"

"Wait—I walked straight here without stopping and saw zero runners! Do you and Master speak through thin air?" Matron Wei teased.

"Just missed our fleet-footed lad by a hair!" Setting a chair out, Steward Fang pulled a thick ledger labeled *Property Registry* onto the desk and chatted while opening it. "Young Madam truly struck rare fortune marrying Second Young Master! By Song statute, a household's scattered properties register under a single household head—so all Chen assets sit in Eldest Master's name. Only because Old Madam dotes so fiercely on Second Young Master did she pull strings with Prefect Zhu to carve several holdings into his name—and now he hands the whole lot straight to his bride! Rare devotion!"

Already familiar with the single-household statute, Matron Wei felt deeper respect for the Chens' generosity. "Is transferring title into my mistress's name complicated?"

"Not a bit! Log it in this registry, stamp the Chen Firm grand seal plus Eldest Master's and Second Young Master's private seals, then register it down at the prefecture yamen." Dipping his brush, he recorded the entries cleanly and set the pen down. "Done on paper—I will head upstairs to the seal-keeper for the three stamps next." Glancing around, Matron Wei spotted a long wooden staircase climbing into upper floors she had never noticed before.

"One last detail to confirm, Matron—should Eldest Master's stewards keep managing those holdings, or will Young Madam install her own people?"

Smart enough to keep Chen Jianglin comfortable—leaving his stewards in charge ensured he felt assets remained safely in the family while sparing her own overburdened hands—Matron Wei smiled. "Keep the same stewards. I will drop by to look over accounts when free, and trust them when busy."

"Splendid!" Steward Fang beamed.
