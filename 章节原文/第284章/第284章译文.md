## Chapter 284: Princess Consort Yi Probes Chen Manor by Night

Arriving at the spotless, heavily staffed Chen country villa, Shen Jianchen inspected the exact room where Haoyu locked kidnapped girls—not a speck of dust left after a full year—yet one detail from Wen Xin's questioning snapped his focus sharp: on the exact day Haoyu left, *Chen Haodong* had sent Butler Wang to transfer every single servant back to town for three days, leaving only the estate steward—who conveniently got drunk and slept straight through noon!

Everything pointed straight at Haodong. Ordering Wen Xin to comb the grounds outside and check if *any* servant had skipped that transfer, Shen Jianchen hurried off toward Youlin Retreat, knowing Mengrong grew frantic when he ran late.

Sure enough, learning from a yamen runner that Brother had vanished solo all day had driven Qiao Mengrong half-mad with dread—convinced he was off trysting with Zhu Shuzhen! Ordering Xilan to hitch the carriage, she stormed straight to Chen Manor's gate, brushing aside offers to summon the masters and marching straight to Moxiang Courtyard.

Night-watch maid Chouya cheerfully said Miss was asleep—yet when Kuqiao stepped out of Suxin Thatched Hall alone, nervously shutting the door behind her and stalling with excuses about Miss being upset over Haoyu's case, Mengrong's blood boiled! *She's not even in there! She's out with Brother!*

Both maids knelt frantically stalling while Xilan accused them of insulting royalty—just as Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan hurried up, tipped off by the gatekeeper.

Accused of hiding an adulterous daughter-in-law, Yao Qingyan immediately ordered Kuqiao to unlock the door. Heart pounding out of her chest, Kuqiao stepped inside behind Eldest Madam and Mengrong—and nearly wept in sheer relief! The bed quilt lay rumpled around her mistress's sleeping form, only a tiny corner of men's silk poking out from beneath, which Kuqiao deftly tucked out of sight!

Blinking awake like a genuine sleeper, Qianli Xun scolded Kuqiao for nearly slighting royalty, slipped on a robe, and came out with Yao Qingyan to curtsy properly.

Seeing her rival undeniably safe in bed drained all the steam out of Mengrong; with a cold sniff—"Nothing to say, staying here bores me"—she swept straight out the gate.

Puzzled by the bizarre late-night visit, Yao Qingyan gently warned Shuzhen to stay extra cautious around jealous royal eyes—and teared up gratefully when Qianli Xun pledged faithfully: "Whether Haoyu's innocent or guilty, I'm his wife—if he's cleared, I'll stand beside him; if he falls, I'll stay a widow looking after Grandmother and you two forever."
