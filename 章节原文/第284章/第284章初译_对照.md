第284章 仪王妃夜探陈府
## Chapter 284: Princess Consort Yi’s Midnight Raid

申简辰见时不时有下人从身边经过，随口问道：“陈家的主人又没住在宅子，为何下人如此忙碌？”
“主人家虽不在，该忙活的还得忙活。”管家回完话，在一间屋子前停下，“大人，就是这间屋子，陈公子每次抓来的姑娘都关在这里。”
申简辰命令道：“打开。”
管家推开门，恭敬行礼：“大人，小人还有事要忙，就陪您到这里。”
“且慢！我还有话问你。”申简辰径直走进屋内。
温都头等人也都跟着进入。
管家不敢不听，站在门外耐心等候。
屋内布置精美，虽是晚上，一眼瞧去也有种窗明几净的感觉，还时不时传来一股熏香的味道。
申简辰进入里屋，瞧到一张软床，猜想去年春金兰被抓后，应该是被绑在那张床上。
他用手指在身边的木制家具上摸了摸，没有丝毫灰尘，如此整洁干净，恐怕一年前留下的证据早已被勤劳的下人当垃圾一样丢掉。
温信在一旁说：“白天已将屋内搜遍，找不到任何相关的痕迹。”
“可问过收拾屋子的下人？我指的是去年三月初四一早打扫屋子的丫鬟。”
“问过，丫鬟说时间太长，想不起来，既然想不起来说明印象不深刻，印象不深刻证明没有什么特别的发现。”
申简辰又将屋子扫视一遍：“其他人可查问过？”
“本想查问，管家说去年三月初三陈浩宇离开不久宅子里的下人都被临时调走。”
“临时调走？如此凑巧？”
“我也觉得太过凑巧。”
“把管家叫进来。”
温信到门口将管家唤进来，管家乖乖进入，拜道：“大人有何吩咐？”
申简辰转身面向管家：“去年三月初三为何宅子里的下人都被调走？”
管家老实回道：“主人家吩咐，小人也不敢多问，只管照做。听回来的下人说陈府要把城里的所有宅子打扫一遍，人手不够，才从乡下的宅子调人。”
“主人？陈家大老爷？”
“不是，是大公子。”
申简辰警觉起来，心想：“又是他，看来他的确十分可疑。”
“陈浩东？”
“是。”
“你呢？可留在宅子？”
“我倒是留着。”
申简辰走到椅子跟前坐下：“你既留着，应该知道那晚陈浩宇有没有回来？”
管家解释道：“我虽留着，多贪了几杯，睡过去，醒过来已是第二日午时，确实见到二公子，可头天晚上的状况我一点儿都不清楚。”
Watching servants hurry past even at night, Shen Jianchen asked, “No masters live here—why are the servants so busy?”

申简辰继续问：“陈浩东可亲自前来将下人调走？”
“Masters away or not, chores still have to get done.” Stopping outside a door, the caretaker said, “Here’s the room, Your Lordship—every girl Second Young Master snatched got locked in here.”

“那倒没有，是陈府的王管家过来将下人们带走，三日后才将所有下人送回来。”
“Open it.”

申简辰总觉得此事蹊跷，陈浩东偷情的宅子离此处不远，把人调走也是他所为，一切迹象都指向他，只是找不到证据。
Pushing the door wide, the caretaker bowed. “If you’ll excuse me, I’ve got duties to see to—”

“我已问完话，你去忙吧。”
“Hold on. I’ve got questions for you.” Shen Jianchen stepped inside, Wen Xin and the constables trailing while the caretaker waited outside.

管家退去，申简辰对温信说：“屋子里没什么可查的，趁夜色重点观察屋子周围，再把所有下人查一遍，问问有没有人三月初三那天没有被调走。明日再到陈府找管家确认下人被调走之事。”
Even at night, the room looked plush, bright, and scented with incense. Stepping into the inner chamber, Shen Jianchen eyed the soft bed where Chun Jinlan had been bound, then ran a finger along a wooden chest—zero dust. Any clue from a year ago had long since been scrubbed away by diligent maids.

温信回道：“是。”
“Combed every inch in daylight,” Wen Xin said. “Not a single trace.”

申简辰留下温信与差役，只身离开陈家的宅子，继续回幽林别居。
“Questioned the maid who cleaned up the morning of the fourth day of the third month last year?”

梦容对他越来越依赖，还需及时赶回去，否则她心中不安。
“Did. She said it’s too long ago to recall—which means nothing stood out.”

话说乔梦容眼巴巴等到天色逐渐暗下，却不见哥哥的身影。她担心不已，差幽林别居的下人前去司理院打探。
“What about the rest of the staff?”

下人回来后说仪王白天一个人出去，一直没有回司理院。
“Tried—caretaker says all the servants got pulled away right after Chen Haoyu left on the third day of the third month.”

乔梦容更是坐立难安，哥哥一个人出去，到底要做什么？难道背着她与人私会？
“Pulled away on that exact day? Awfully convenient.”

她越想心越不安：“喜兰，备马车，随我去趟陈府。”
“Thought so too.”

喜兰明白王妃的心思，乖乖备好马车，跟随王妃来到陈府。
“Call the caretaker in.”

乔梦容等不及喜兰先下马车扶她，第一个跳下车，冲向陈府的大门。
Summoned back, the caretaker bowed. “Your orders, Your Lordship?”

喜兰赶紧跟上，快走几步，上前敲门：咚咚咚！
“Why were all the servants transferred out on the third day of the third month last year?”

陈府的大门被打开，下人问道：“您是？”
“Master’s orders—not my place to ask. When the staff got back, they said Chen Manor was deep-cleaning all its city residences and ran short on hands.”

喜兰厉声说：“你面前这位是仪王妃，识相的把门开大些，让王妃进去！”
“Which master? Eldest Master Chen?”

下人一听是仪王妃，吓得把门开大跪在地上：“拜见仪王妃，小人这就去通报主人前来迎接贵人……”
“No, Your Lordship—Eldest Young Master.”

乔梦容尽量将语速放慢：“不必打扰他人，我来是找幽栖居士，也就是陈家二少夫人，你负责在前面带路。”
Alarm bells rang in Shen Jianchen’s head. *Him again!* “Chen Haodong?”

下人从地上爬起来：“二少夫人住在墨香苑，我这就带贵人过去。”
“Yes.”

乔梦容没有心情欣赏陈府的夜景，她来不过是想搞清楚朱淑真到底在不在？如果她不在，多半是出去与哥哥私会。
“And you stayed behind?”

她在心中祈祷朱淑真一定要在，否则她会疯掉。
“I did.”

乔梦容心中慨叹陈府挺大，比幽林别居还大，心中生出不满来，凭什么朱淑真既得到哥哥的心，还能嫁到这么有钱的人家？像她这种勾三搭四之人就该千夫所指，万人唾骂。
Taking a seat, Shen Jianchen pressed, “Then you’d know whether Chen Haoyu came back that night.”

在下人的引领下，终于来到墨香苑。
“I stayed, yes, only I drank a few cups too many and passed out cold—didn’t wake till noon the next day. Saw Second Young Master then, sure, yet I know nothing about the night before.”

借着月色看清墨香苑三个字时，乔梦容对下人说：“你去吧，不必惊动陈府其他人，我来找幽栖居士只为私事。”
“Did Chen Haodong come in person to transfer the staff?”

下人犹豫片刻后答应着退去。贵人前来，不告诉主人会被批评，还需通报一声。
“No—Butler Wang came from the main manor to fetch them and brought them back three days later.”

喜兰上前敲墨香苑的院门，开门的是丑丫，今晚她当值。
Too many coincidences—Chen Haodong’s secret tryst house sat right down the road, and he pulled the staff away that very day. Every arrow pointed straight at him, yet hard proof remained missing.

丑丫认得仪王妃，激动道：“郡主，不！仪王妃来找我家小姐？”
Dismissing the caretaker, Shen Jianchen told Wen Xin, “Nothing left inside. Comb the grounds outside under night cover, then question every servant to see if anyone missed that transfer on the third day of the third month. Tomorrow verify the transfer with Butler Wang at Chen Manor.”

喜兰帮着问：“幽栖居士可在？”
“Yes, Your Highness.”

丑丫回道：“在，当然在，这会儿应该刚睡下。仪王妃，里边请，我这就去给苦荞姐姐通报。”
Leaving Wen Xin and his squad at work, Shen Jianchen headed back to Youlin Retreat—Mengrong leaned on him more every day, and staying out too late would set her off.

乔梦容松口气，人在便好，不过还需亲眼看到方可安心。
Sure enough, back at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong had watched night fall with no sign of her brother. Sending a servant to check the Judicial Office only brought back word that Prince Yi had walked out alone during the day and never returned.

她走进墨香苑，院子不小，到处种着花，还有小池塘，里面长满荷叶与花骨朵，不远处还有一棵树，月色下判断不出是什么树。
Dread chewed at her nerves. Where had he gone alone—a secret rendezvous?

乔梦容无心多想，跟着丑丫来到素心草堂，看样子朱淑真应该住在这间屋子里。
“Xilan! Hitch the carriage—we’re going to Chen Manor!”

丑丫在门外向里说：“苦荞姐姐，仪王妃前来拜访。”
Reading her mistress’s mind, Xilan had the carriage ready in minutes. Pulling up outside Chen Manor, Qiao Mengrong didn’t even wait for Xilan’s arm—she vaulted down and marched straight for the main gate while Xilan sprinted ahead to pound the knocker.

乔梦容期待朱淑真能迅速推门而出，给她吃一颗定心丸。
A gatekeeper cracked the door. “Who is it?”

门是被打开，出来的却是苦荞，她看上去心神不安，还顺便把门带上。
“Princess Consort Yi stands before you!” Xilan snapped. “Open wide and let Her Highness in!”

乔梦容的心不由悬起来，难道她不在？
Terrified, the servant flung the gate wide and dropped to his knees. “Greetings, Your Highness! Let me summon my masters to welcome you—”

只见苦荞跪在地上，丑丫也跟着跪下。
“Don’t disturb anyone,” Qiao Mengrong said, forcing her voice slow and steady. “I’m here to see Youqi Jushi—Second Young Madam. Lead the way.”

苦荞说：“郡主前来，小姐本应出来迎接，可这几日不断有人找上门来要给姑爷当妾室，此事又惊动司理院，姑爷现在人还在司理院关着，小姐心中难过，刚刚睡下，恐怕……恐怕不能出来面见贵人。”
Scrambling up, the gatekeeper said, “Second Young Madam lives in Moxiang Courtyard. Right this way, Your Highness.”

乔梦容猜测定是陈浩东捣鼓出来的麻烦事，朱淑真不开心，她便开心。可苦荞的话疑点重重，乔梦容无法信服。再说司理院出面审理，哥哥更有机会与朱淑真接触，说不定哥哥没按时回幽林别居正是与她私会。
Blind to the manor’s nocturnal scenery, Qiao Mengrong cared about one thing alone: was Zhu Shuzhen home? If not, she had to be out meeting her brother—and Qiao Mengrong prayed she was here, or she’d lose her mind. Still, noticing Chen Manor dwarfed even Youlin Retreat sparked fresh venom: why did a shameless flirt like Zhu Shuzhen get both her brother’s heart and the richest family in Lin’an?

想到这里，乔梦容怒色道：“她既难过，我更应该见见她，说些安慰的话，你不过是个下人，哪有权利替幽栖居士做主？”
At the moonlit plaque of Moxiang Courtyard, Qiao Mengrong dismissed the guide. “Leave us—no need to alert anyone else. Private visit.” Hesitating, the servant bowed off—and went straight to alert his master anyway, knowing better than to hide a royal visit.

苦荞继续跪着不敢起来。
Xilan knocked on the courtyard gate. On night watch, Chouya opened it and gasped. “Princess—I mean, Princess Consort Yi! Here to see my miss?”

小姐白天女扮男装偷偷溜出去，天黑了还未归来。仪王妃此时跑来要见人，实在想不通她要做什么？凭上次诗会时她对小姐的态度，苦荞绝对不会认为她来是安慰小姐。难道是听到什么？来找小姐算账？
“Is Youqi Jushi in?” Xilan demanded.

苦荞十分紧张，不知说些什么话来应对。
“Yes, of course—she should’ve just gone to bed. Please come in, Your Highness, I’ll go tell Sister Kuqiao!”

丑丫猜到小姐应该不在，否则怎么敢慢待贵人？也帮着圆谎：“贵人不要怪罪，不是小姐不愿见您，实在是她今日身体不适，怕冲撞了您。”
Relief washed over Qiao Mengrong, though she wouldn’t trust it until she laid eyes on her. Crossing the flower-filled courtyard past the lotus pond and a shadowy tree, she followed Chouya to Suxin Thatched Hall.

苦荞也临时找到理由：“对，是怕冲撞了贵人，小姐心情不好，精神不济，出来见贵人，只怕不敬。”
Chouya called outside the door, “Sister Kuqiao, Princess Consort Yi is here to visit!”

喜兰替仪王妃说道：“幽栖居士躲着不出来才是对王妃大不敬！王妃好不容易来趟陈家找个人，你们都推三阻四，难道陈家人眼中容不下贵人？”
Expecting Zhu Shuzhen to step out right away, Qiao Mengrong watched the door crack open—only for Kuqiao to slip out looking rattled and pull it shut behind her.

传来陈江林的声音：“仪王妃驾到，有失远迎，陈家人的心中时刻放着贵人。”
Qiao Mengrong’s heart leaped into her throat. *She’s not here!*

原来下人只将此事告知大老爷，陈江林不想惊动老太太与两个弟弟，亲自带着姚轻琰前来，一路上他们十分纳闷，仪王妃为何大晚上跑到陈家？
Kuqiao dropped to her knees, Chouya kneeling beside her. “My miss ought to greet Your Highness in person, only women have been banging on the door these past days demanding to be Young Master’s concubines, and now Young Master is locked up at the Judicial Office. My miss was heartbroken and just fell asleep—I’m afraid she can’t come out to receive Your Highness.”

乔梦容转过身，不满道：“陈老爷嘴上说得好，可我来了一会儿，这两个丫鬟推三阻四，不想让我见幽栖居士，难道人根本不在房中？而是大晚上偷偷跑出去与人私会？”
Sure Chen Haodong’s scheme had made Zhu Shuzhen miserable, Qiao Mengrong still smelled a lie—what if the Judicial Office case had thrown her brother and Zhu Shuzhen together right now?

陈江林与姚轻琰不明所以，贵人生气，他们也都跪下。
Her face darkened. “If she’s heartbroken, all the more reason I should comfort her! Who gave a servant the right to speak for Youqi Jushi?”

姚轻琰解释道：“贵人息怒，千万不要误会，淑真肯定在，她没立刻出来，定是有原因，我这就让下人把门打开，亲自将淑真带出来面见贵人。”
Kuqiao stayed glued to her knees in cold sweat. Her miss had snuck out in men’s robes and still wasn’t back! Judging by how hostile the Princess Consort had been at the poetry salon, she sure hadn’t come to offer sympathy.

乔梦容又舒心一些：“都起来吧，搞得好像我欺负你们似的。”
Guessing her miss was out, Chouya chimed in to cover. “Please don’t be angry, Your Highness—Miss is feeling unwell today and fears offending you.”

众人起身，姚轻琰上前对苦荞说：“把门打开。”
Kuqiao grabbed the lifeline. “Yes! Low spirits and unwell—she’d hate to show disrespect.”

苦荞心想，这下完了，仪王妃见不到自家小姐，不知会如何编排？眼下情形，只能将门打开。
Xilan snapped, “Hiding indoors is real disrespect! Her Highness makes a rare trip to Chen Manor and you stall at every turn—do the Chens have zero regard for royalty?!”

苦荞小心推开门，姚轻琰走进去。
“Forgive our late welcome, Princess Consort Yi! The Chens hold royalty in utmost esteem.”

乔梦容已等不及，跟着进入，屋里的布置与望月轩的闺房很相似，亲切感袭上心头，她与淑真一起围坐火盆旁聊天儿的情景浮现在眼前……
Informed by the gatekeeper, Chen Jianglin had avoided waking Old Madam Xia or his brothers and hurried over with Yao Qingyan, baffled why the Princess Consort had come calling at night.

以前美好的回忆更让她恨朱淑真，都是他不顾闺中密友的情谊，偷走哥哥的心！
Qiao Mengrong wheeled on him. “Fine words, Master Chen—yet these two maids have stalled me at the door since I arrived! Is Youqi Jushi even inside, or did she sneak out after dark for a secret tryst?!”

苦荞越发紧张，大夫人正往里屋走，若看到床上空无一人，如何向仪王妃交代？
Alarmed by her fury, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan dropped to their knees.

苦荞心惊胆战跟在大夫人身后，竟然意外地发现闺床上的被子不是她出去前叠得整整齐齐的样子，小姐竟然躺在床上，只是被子盖得乱些，刚脱下的男装压在被子下面露出一角。
“Calm your anger, Your Highness—don’t misunderstand!” Yao Qingyan pleaded. “Shuzhen is definitely inside! There must be a reason she hasn’t come out. I’ll open the door and fetch her myself right now!”

回来就好，苦荞松口气，迅速上前为小姐盖好被子的同时，偷偷把男装向被子下面塞了塞。
Mollified, Qiao Mengrong waved them up. “Rise—making it look like I’m bullying you.”

姚轻琰上前轻声说：“淑真，贵人来看你，还是起来见一见。”
Getting to her feet, Yao Qingyan ordered Kuqiao, “Open the door.”

千里寻睁开紧闭的双眼，装作熟睡后刚醒来的样子：“谁？谁来看我？”
*We’re doomed,* Kuqiao thought, heart pounding as she pushed the door open.

姚轻琰强调道：“仪王妃。”
Yao Qingyan stepped inside with Qiao Mengrong right on her heels. The layout echoed Wangyue Pavilion’s bedchamber so much that a stab of nostalgia hit Qiao Mengrong—remembering nights huddled beside a brazier chatting with Shuzhen—only to curdle straight back into hatred over her betraying their friendship and stealing her brother’s heart.

千里寻立刻坐起来：“苦荞，为何不早些唤我？差点儿怠慢贵人！”
Trembling behind Madam as they entered the inner chamber, Kuqiao braced for an empty bed—and nearly collapsed in relief! Instead of the neat quilt she had folded, her miss lay tucked underneath, just one corner of her discarded men’s robe peeking out from beneath the covers.

苦荞装道：“小姐心里难过，好不容易睡个好觉，我不想打扰。”
Darting ahead, Kuqiao pulled the quilt smooth while jamming the men’s robe out of sight underneath.

千里寻故意批评道：“我没事，贵人重要，记着，以后只要是贵人来，哪怕我病着，也要立刻爬起来见，否则是对贵人大大的不敬。”
Yao Qingyan leaned over with a gentle call. “Shuzhen, Her Highness came to see you—best get up.”

姚轻琰露出满意的笑容。
Qianli Xun blinked open groggy eyes, playing freshly woken to perfection. “Who...? Who came to see me?”

苦荞假装做错事的样子：“记下了。”
“Princess Consort Yi,” Yao Qingyan stressed.

“我得赶紧穿了衣裳去见贵人。”
Qianli Xun shot upright. “Kuqiao! Why didn’t you wake me sooner? We nearly slighted Her Highness!”

千里寻走下床，在苦荞的伺候下穿好衣裳，与姚轻琰一起来到外厅。
Kuqiao played right along. “You were so upset, Miss, and had just fallen asleep—I hated to wake you.”

昔日的闺中密友出现在眼前，乔梦容本有的一脸担忧即刻消失得无影无踪。
“I’m fine—Her Highness comes first!” Qianli Xun scolded for show. “Remember: whenever Her Highness visits, even if I’m sick in bed, get me up right away!”

千里寻与婆婆一起拜见贵人
Yao Qingyan smiled in approval while Kuqiao bowed her head. “Yes, Miss.”

姚轻琰说：“让仪王妃久等。”
“Let me dress quick and greet Her Highness.”

千里寻则说：“仪王妃大驾光临，淑真受宠若惊，若有怠慢，还请见谅。”
Slipping out of bed with Kuqiao’s help, Qianli Xun came out to the main room with Yao Qingyan and curtsied beside her mother-in-law.

乔梦容轻松之后，很是不屑：“我来见你没什么事，你既然在，我待着也无趣。”
The sight of her former best friend standing right there wiped every trace of panic from Qiao Mengrong’s face.

乔梦容说完，拂袖而去。
“Forgive the wait, Your Highness,” Yao Qingyan said.

经过外面时，陈江林对着仪王妃的背影大声说：“恭送贵人！”
“Your Highness honors my humble room,” Qianli Xun added. “Forgive any slight.”

屋内，姚轻琰甚是疑惑：“这就走了？贵人闹这一出是何意？”
Relieved and back to frosty disdain, Qiao Mengrong rose. “Nothing important—now that I see you’re here, staying is boring.” With a flick of her sleeve, she swept out as Chen Jianglin called out from the yard, “Safe journey, Your Highness!”

千里寻多少已猜出，估计是找不到仪王给闹的。多亏她回来得及时，否则梦容绝对会怀疑她跟仪王在一起。她确实与仪王在一起，只不过不是她想的那样。
Inside, Yao Qingyan frowned in bafflement. “Leaving just like that? What was that whole stunt about?”

还需向婆婆解释：“贵人吗？仗着身份尊贵，喜欢瞎闹，只要我们没得罪她便好。”
Qianli Xun knew the score—she had lost track of Prince Yi and freaked out. Good thing she got home in time, or Mengrong would’ve sworn they were together—which, well, they had been, just not like that. “Royals throw whims around because they can, Mother-in-law. So long as we didn’t offend her, no harm done.”

姚轻琰说：“淑真啊，我知道因仪王抢亲的事，仪王妃对你有看法，以后你要多加注意，不要被人抓住把柄对你的名节说三道四。”
Yao Qingyan sighed. “Shuzhen, I know the Princess Consort holds a grudge over Prince Yi’s wedding raid. Be extra careful going forward so nobody finds an excuse to smear your reputation.”

抢亲之事陈家不计较，长辈还对她这么好，千里寻自是感动：“淑真记下，以后定会多加注意，不给公婆添麻烦。”
Touched that the Chens never blamed her for the wedding raid and still watched out for her, Qianli Xun nodded. “I’ll remember, Mother-in-law—I won’t bring trouble on you and Father-in-law.”

“说什么麻烦不麻烦的？都是一家人。浩宇的事……”姚轻琰担心因为商妓上门闹事，淑真不愿再接受儿子。
“Don’t talk about trouble—we’re family. As for Haoyu...” Yao Qingyan hesitated, worried the courtesan scandal had soured Shuzhen on her son.

千里寻打算给婆婆吃颗定心丸：“婆婆放心，陈浩宇再不济，也是我夫君，事情如果不是他做下，我会陪他一起渡过难关；若是他做下，我情愿为他守寡，跟着公婆还有奶奶生活一辈子。”
Deciding to set her mother-in-law’s heart at rest, Qianli Xun said, “Don’t worry, Mother-in-law. Flawed as he is, Chen Haoyu is my husband. If he’s innocent, I’ll stand by him through this storm; if he’s guilty, I’ll live out my life as his widow right beside Grandmother and you and Father-in-law.”

姚轻琰眼眶变红，握住淑真的手：“浩宇能娶到你，是他的福气。”
Eyes reddening, Yao Qingyan clasped her hand. “Marrying you was the greatest blessing of Haoyu’s life.”

“我能遇到奶奶和公婆这样的家人也是我的福气。”
“Having Grandmother and parents-in-law like you is my blessing too.”

婆媳二人说些表达感情的话，融洽的气氛令苦荞感动。
Watching the warm bond between mother-in-law and daughter-in-law brought tears to Kuqiao’s eyes.
