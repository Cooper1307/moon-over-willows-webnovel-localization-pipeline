# Chapter 65: Intercepting Qianli Xun on the Road

---

Shen Jianchen had just stepped into Secluded Forest Villa when he seemed to remember something. He said to Qiao Mengrong: "Mengrong, go back to your room first. I suddenly remembered something and need to go out."

---

"What is it? Can you tell me, brother?" Qiao Mengrong looked puzzled.

---

Shen Jianchen hesitated for a moment. He didn't want to express his true thoughts: "Bai Feng hasn't returned yet. I'll go look for him."

---

Qiao Mengrong gathered her courage to ask the question on her mind: "Why did you want to find the person who wrote the storytelling scripts after listening to just one storytelling session?"

---

"There's... there's nothing. I just want to meet him, that's all."

---

"Is the person who wrote the storytelling scripts the one you divined about, brother? Is it that young master named Qianli Xun?"

---

Shen Jianchen didn't want to explain further: "I need to leave quickly. I can't chat with you anymore."

---

Her brother quickly left Secluded Forest Villa.

---

Qiao Mengrong followed closely to the gate and watched her brother get into the carriage. He told the coachman to hurry, as if he had urgent business.

---

Qiao Mengrong was both puzzled and worried: "What do you think brother is going to do?"

---

Faced with the princess's question, Xilan felt at a loss: "The prince probably has urgent business."

---

"He doesn't even care about the position of Military Commissioner of Lin'an. What urgent business could he possibly have?"

---

Xilan didn't know how to answer: "Princess, you should go back to your room and rest. You've been climbing mountains and hanging red silk. You must be tired."

---

Mentioning the red silk brought a smile to Qiao Mengrong's puzzled face: "Let's go back. Brother is a man. There will always be things he can't conveniently tell women."

---

Xilan strongly agreed: "That's the right way to think, Princess."

---

Shen Jianchen took the carriage to outside the city gate and told the coachman to stop the carriage under a tree outside the city. He got off the carriage, looked up at the sky, then stared at the road leading to the Divine Willow.

---

Before long, he saw a carriage approaching from the distance. Shen Jianchen smiled.

---

When the carriage got closer, he stepped forward: "Youqi Jushi, I have something to ask you. Could you get off the carriage and chat with me for a while?"

---

Qianli Xun poked her head out of the carriage window. Seeing Prince Yi blocking her carriage, she was quite confused. He was a prince—how could she not obey? So she got off the carriage.

---

Qianli Xun looked around and didn't see Princess Jiahe. Obviously, Prince Yi had come alone to intercept her. She bowed and said: "I wonder what business Prince Yi has with me?"

---

Shen Jianchen pointed to the grassland outside the city: "Could you come over there and chat with me?"

---

Qianli Xun nodded and used her gaze to stop Kuqiao from following. Kuqiao understood and stood in place to wait.

---

Qianli Xun followed Prince Yi to the grassland. The grass grew thick and lush. Stepping on it felt soft and comfortable.

---

Prince Yi didn't bother with small talk. He asked directly: "I met you in the bamboo forest, which means you were living in the bamboo forest at that time. Your maid's younger brother happened to help Qianli Xun deliver his manuscripts to the storytellers. I think you must have met Qianli Xun. Your maid said he's nowhere to be found. Do you know where he went?"

---

So it was for this matter. Qianli Xun felt honored. Her novel that had been attacked online in her time was not only not accused of plagiarism in this era, but a prince was going to great lengths to dig out the author behind the scenes. It made Qianli Xun almost want to tell him the truth. But she held back.

---

"I did meet Qianli Xun in the bamboo forest and I've seen his manuscripts. The stories he writes are truly captivating. After reading them, I was completely impressed. I immediately concluded that Qianli Xun's stories will definitely be remembered for generations. What do you think, Prince Yi?"

---

Zhu Shuzhen only kept praising him without answering the question. Shen Jianchen emphasized: "Do you know where Qianli Xun went?"

---

After Qianli Xun's self-praise, she didn't get a response from him. She wasn't satisfied and didn't want to miss this good opportunity to hear others' comments: "Prince Yi, first evaluate the storytelling scripts written by Qianli Xun, then I'll tell you where he went."

---

Shen Jianchen was pleased. It seemed Zhu Shuzhen knew where Qianli Xun was. Since she appreciated Qianli Xun's storytelling scripts so much, he might as well say some nice things to make her happy, and she would definitely tell him the truth.

---

"I've also listened to storytelling in the capital. It was all clichés. Coming to Lin'an and hearing the stories written by Qianli Xun was refreshing. I dare not make a hasty conclusion about whether they'll be remembered for generations, but at least I enjoy listening to them. I see others enjoy listening too. As long as everyone likes them, it shows he writes well."

---

After all the praise, Qianli Xun found it very pleasing. Savoring the words "a breath of fresh air," she secretly rejoiced: "Heh, in the era where Qianli Xun lives, this kind of content isn't fresh at all. I didn't expect it to be a breath of fresh air when brought to the Song Dynasty. How interesting!"

---

"Please tell me where Qianli Xun went, Youqi Jushi."

---

Prince Yi's voice snapped Qianli Xun out of her daydream. Should she tell him the truth? Qianli Xun hesitated...

---

"Please hurry and tell me where Qianli Xun went, Youqi Jushi."

---

Prince Yi continued to press. She couldn't stay silent anymore: "Qianli Xun... he's a young master, not a young lady..."

---

Shen Jianchen wanted to hear more crucial information and said anxiously: "I know."

---

"When he left the bamboo forest, we were having tea together. He seemed to say... he seemed to say..." Qianli Xun thought about how to make up a destination. "Right! He said the Divine Willow was going to take him away. After that, I couldn't find him. Could he really have been taken away by the Divine Willow? Thinking of him today, I went to check by the Divine Willow, but I didn't see anything unusual."

---

Qianli Xun observed Prince Yi and found he looked very disappointed. She felt even more secretly pleased: "I didn't expect my die-hard fan to actually be a prince. This level of 'die-hard' is simply unmatched. I hurt him so badly—that's really cruel. I might as well comfort him."

---

"Prince Yi, don't be sad. Qianli Xun must be joking with me. I just studied the Divine Willow for a long time and didn't see that it has this ability. When you have time, go to the entertainment quarters and listen to storytelling. If you hear new stories written by Qianli Xun, it means he hasn't left."

---

Shen Jianchen felt sad for a while longer before saying: "It's good that he's left. It's just that I..." He couldn't continue. If Qianli Xun was gone, what meaning was there in leaving him alone in this era?

---

Before Prince Yi finished his words, he left the grassland dejectedly and walked toward the carriage under the tree.

---

Qianli Xun watched Prince Yi's back and didn't think too much about it. She still just treated him as a die-hard fan and thought: "In a few days, when the new manuscript is finished, I might as well invite Prince Yi to the entertainment quarters to listen and cheer him up. That's settled then."

---

On the way back to Secluded Forest Villa, Shen Jianchen looked through the carriage window at the distant shadow of the Divine Willow and said to himself: "Qianli Xun, if you've really gone back, I don't want to stay in this unfamiliar era either. I might as well go find the Divine Willow now and beg it to take me back."

---

Just as he was about to tell the coachman to turn the carriage around, Qiao Mengrong's gentle and expectant eyes floated into his mind. He couldn't bear to leave just like that: "I should go back and help Zhao Pu settle Mengrong first, then make plans."

---

Tomorrow was the date. Qiao Wenxian was very excited and planned to invite Prince Yi to bring Princess Jiahe along for a boat ride on the lake.

---

Bai Feng wasn't around, and he hadn't brought Shuang either. Qiao Wenxian walked into the main room, sat at the tea table, and personally started making tea.

---

He had just finished making the tea and taken a sip when Prince Yi came in from outside, looking utterly devastated.

---

Qiao Wenxian put down his teacup: "You're back. Why do you look unhappy? Let me make you a cup of tea to quench your thirst."

---

Shen Jianchen forced down his disappointment and squeezed out a smile: "How long have you been here?"

---

"Just enough time to make a cup of tea." Qiao Wenxian started making tea for Prince Yi: "You have Bai Feng go out on errands all day and have no one to attend to you. Why not have Butler Li arrange another servant for you? Preferably a pretty young maid. When you were at Prince Yi's Manor, you were surrounded by maids. After you recovered from your illness, you really changed your ways."

---

Shen Jianchen sat in a chair, looking very lonely: "Why do I need so many people to attend to me? Bai Feng alone is enough. When Bai Feng isn't around, I have hands and feet and can do everything myself. I'm not used to others attending to me."

---

"Your behavior is completely inappropriate for a prince's status."

---

"Today I'm a prince, tomorrow I might become a prisoner. One must never be deceived by the wealth and honor right before one's eyes. It's all fake."

---

"Tsk tsk tsk! How can you say such unlucky words? You're very off today. Did you quarrel with Princess Jiahe?"

---

"How could Mengrong quarrel with me? It would be better if she did quarrel with me."

---

"That's true. Princess Jiahe is gentle and dignified. She wouldn't quarrel with Prince Yi. What's wrong with you then? You look upset."

---

Shen Jianchen glanced at Wenyuan, who was carefully making tea: "I really don't understand. What's the point of making a cup of tea so meticulously? You continue. I'll go lie down in the inner room for a while."

---
