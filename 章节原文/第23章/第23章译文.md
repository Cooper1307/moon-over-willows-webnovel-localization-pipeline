# Chapter 23: Appreciating the Ci, Divorce, and the Seven Grounds

Lin Jue recited "The Heartbreak Riddle" in his mind no less than ten times. He couldn't grasp its meaning, let alone know the riddle's answer. He was completely lost in a tangled mess of words.

Qianli Xun saw Lin Jue sweating profusely and found it quite amusing. She thought Zhu Shuzhen marrying him was a tremendous loss.

"Lin Jue, if you can't understand the ci I wrote, then don't trouble yourself. Just let me leave the Lin family willingly, and I'll be grateful."

Lin Jue threw the paper with "The Heartbreak Riddle" toward the ground. It happened to land at Lin Zixiao's feet. He picked it up and began to carefully appreciate it.

Lin Jue sneered, "Hmph! I've always hated these pretentious literary things. The Old Ancestor probably knew I don't understand and deliberately brought it to trouble me. Using this as an excuse to make me agree to the divorce is simply unfair!"

Nanny Xiu Zhen criticized, "You young one are truly rude. The Old Ancestor has lived in the Zhu family's Buddhist shrine for years, never stepping outside. She knew nothing about you young ones. If the Zhu family girl hadn't been left with no other choice and begged the Old Ancestor for help, why would she come here today?"

The Old Ancestor raised her hand to stop Nanny Xiu Zhen. She fingered her prayer beads and said, "I originally didn't want to intervene for my granddaughter. But her ci moved me. It showed me both her talent and her determination to escape this marriage—not a momentary impulse, but a decision made after careful consideration. Since the young master can't guess the riddle's answer, perhaps he's indeed not suitable for my granddaughter. Please let go, so both can find peace."

Before Lin Jue and other clansmen could speak, Lin Zixiao blurted out, "Brilliant! Youqi Jushi's ci is truly brilliant!"

Lin Zixiao's enthusiasm for explaining the poem grew. He stood up. "Let me explain it to everyone..."

Someone said, "Second Brother, Lin Jue's marriage is about to end, and you still have the mood to explain poems? Hurry up and think of a way to help him protect his little family!"

Lin Jue really wanted to know what Zhu Shuzhen had written in the ci—something that moved the Old Ancestor to intervene and earned Second Uncle's praise. So he said, "Let Second Uncle explain it to everyone. I don't believe pretentious literary things can destroy my marriage."

Lin Zixiao temporarily set aside the divorce matter and spoke eloquently, "This ci is titled 'The Heartbreak Riddle.' It not only expresses the theme of heartbreak but also hides a puzzle of ten numbers..."

Lin Jue thought, "Ten numbers? How can I not see it?" He tried hard to recall the lines in the ci but still didn't understand. He blurted out, "Second Uncle, just tell us the answer."

Lin Zixiao continued, "Listen as I explain slowly. 'Coming down the stairs, a coin falls to divine'—the character '下' (down) with '卜' (divine) falling becomes '一' (one). 'Asking the blue sky, where is the person'—the character '天' (sky) with '人' (person) removed becomes '二' (two). 'Resenting the prince, he went straight away'—the character '王' (prince) with the straight stroke removed becomes '三' (three). 'Cursing the beloved, words are hard to keep'—the character '詈' (curse) with '言' (words) removed becomes '四' (four). 'Regretting at first, I lost my mouth'—the character '吾' (I) losing '口' (mouth) becomes '五' (five).

"'Having the top exchange, no bottom exchange'—the character '交' (exchange) with the top but no bottom becomes '六' (six). 'Why ask about black and white'—the character '皂' (black) with '白' (white) removed becomes '七' (seven). 'Parting without using a knife'—the character '分' (part) without using a knife becomes '八' (eight). 'From now on, don't rely on enemies'—the character '仇' (enemy) without a person relying becomes '九' (nine). 'A thousand miles of longing erased with a stroke'—the character '千' (thousand) with a stroke erased becomes '十' (ten). Everyone, isn't it brilliant?" [TN: This riddle is a Chinese character puzzle (拆字谜). Each line of the ci hides a number from one to ten by removing or modifying strokes of a Chinese character. Since this wordplay depends entirely on the visual structure of Chinese characters, the original characters are provided with explanations of how each transformation works.]

After Lin Zixiao finished explaining, Lin Jue suddenly understood. He indeed didn't know his wife before him. But he still couldn't grasp the "heartbreak" theme. He sneered, "So what? Just wrote ten numbers. What's so great about it?"

Some of the Lin family clansmen were convinced, while others, like Lin Jue, thought it was nothing special.

Lin Zixiao continued, "Lin Jue, nephew, you're wrong. Writing such a ci with hidden answers from beginning to end requires first choosing suitable characters, which takes effort. Then, after choosing the characters, selecting rhymes and putting them into rhyme also takes time and effort, and you must find ways to incorporate each character into the lines. Finally, and most importantly, you can't deviate from the main theme—you must infuse emotion into it. So, writing poetry is like building a tower—every brick and tile must be handled carefully, no easier than your military training. Youqi Jushi has a clever mind and extraordinary talent. Being able to write such a ci makes this old man admire her. Sigh! Every line in the ci is filled with dissatisfaction, everywhere complaining about parting ways. Especially this line 'From now on, don't rely on enemies'—Lin Jue, nephew, Youqi Jushi has already treated you as an enemy. If Second Uncle doesn't agree to the divorce, I'm afraid your future days won't be easy. Better let Youqi Jushi be free!"

Hearing this, Lin Jue sat in his chair with a lonely expression. Although he understood everything, he just didn't want to easily let Zhu Shuzhen leave the Lin family. Who made him like her? Because he liked her, he didn't want to see her throw herself into another man's arms in the future. What about Dongfang Chenze? What about Qiao Wenxian? They had been coveting his wife for more than a day or two. He absolutely couldn't let them succeed!

Qianli Xun was delighted. She didn't expect Zhu Shuzhen's ci to win over Lin Zixiao so quickly. She stood up and thanked him, "Second Uncle's analysis is truly thorough. Thank you for understanding and supporting me."

"Youqi Jushi, don't be polite. Could you give me this ci? I want to take it back and study it carefully."

"Second Uncle, please take it."

Lin Zixiao folded the paper carefully and put it in his bosom. He bowed to the Old Ancestor. "Old Ancestor, as a Lin family clansman, since I've agreed to the divorce, I shouldn't stay any longer. This junior will take his leave first."

The Old Ancestor nodded in agreement.

Lin Zixiao faced the clansmen again. "Brothers, three nephews, I'm leaving. You continue."

A clansman stood up to stop him. "Second Brother, if you leave, who will preside?"

Lin Zixiao said, "Third Brother is still here. Let him preside." With that, Lin Zixiao flicked his sleeves and left.

The Old Ancestor spoke, "Young master, I said earlier, if you can't guess it, please return my granddaughter's freedom. Since you didn't guess the riddle's answer, please sign the divorce papers."

Qianli Xun looked at Lin Jue with expectation.

Lin Jue's face was full of sorrow. He said in a disappointed tone, "Just relying on one ci to make me agree to the divorce is simply unreasonable. Out of respect for the Old Ancestor's age, I don't want to argue with you. Unless you persuade the elders here and my two brothers one by one, making them leave like Second Uncle did, only then will I agree to sign the divorce papers."

The Old Ancestor said nothing. She continued to half-close her eyes, fingering her prayer beads.

"Since you want me to preside, I'll sit in the main seat." The third uncle next to Lin Jue stood up, bowed to the Old Ancestor, and walked to sit in the main seat.

Qianli Xun looked at this third uncle. He was somewhat overweight, with a beard, eyes squinted into slits, and features close together. He looked quite comical. She thought about saying a few words to him, but on second thought, decided to listen to what he had to say first.

After Third Uncle sat down, he coughed twice. "Second Brother was easily swayed by you. That's just because he usually likes pretentious literary things. I don't like them. No matter how many ci you write, niece-in-law, it's useless to me. I advise you to save your energy. You're not young anymore. After divorce, who will want you? Stop thinking about poems and ci all day—those things can't be eaten. Living life is different from those things. Whoever you follow, at night it's still lights out, covers up, and those things under the covers..."

The Old Ancestor thought the Lin family's third uncle was too explicit. She coughed to remind him.

Nanny Xiu Zhen followed, "Third Uncle Lin, watch what you say."

Third Uncle immediately stopped. "As the saying goes, a woman's virtue lies in her lack of talent. A woman with talent easily becomes complicated in thought, thinks too much, demands too much, and makes the family unstable. Lin Jue, nephew, is the Military Training Officer in Lin'an. The soldiers he trains are responsible for guarding the entire Lin'an. He's made great contributions. Many women want to marry him, yet he specifically chose you, niece-in-law. That shows his true feelings for you. Third Uncle advises you—stop thinking about those Lin'an talents all day. They're just silver-tipped wax spears—look impressive but useless. Not like my Lin Jue nephew, trained in martial arts, strong and healthy, at night..."

The Lin family's third uncle kept talking and still couldn't control his mouth, bringing up those things under the covers again. Qianli Xun found it funny. The Lin family really had all kinds of people.

Hearing the Old Ancestor's cough again, Third Uncle didn't dare continue. He finally began to wrap up. "In short, Third Uncle is for your own good, niece-in-law. I hope you young couple can live your lives well together. A harmonious family brings prosperity. I'm done."

After hearing Third Uncle's words, Lin Jue's sense of loss from "The Heartbreak Riddle" immediately disappeared. He regained his previous confidence. One Second Uncle left, but there were still several clan uncles here to support him. The good show was yet to come.

Qianli Xun couldn't remain silent any longer. "Third Uncle's words are truly practical. I appreciate your good intentions. But my determination to leave Lin Jue is unshakable. No matter how the Lin family persuades me, I won't change my mind. If you elders don't agree to the divorce, then please have Lin Jue write a divorce letter on the spot. I'm not afraid of having a bad reputation after being repudiated, and I'm not afraid my family won't let me in the door!"

Qianli Xun's words shocked most people present. Although she had mentioned asking her husband to repudiate her, hearing it with their own ears made it hard to believe. How could a woman dare to ask her husband to repudiate her? It was unheard of. Only the Old Ancestor continued fingering her prayer beads, eyes half-closed.

Matchmaker Yan hurriedly stood up after hearing this. "Young Madam, you're a woman. You absolutely can't be repudiated by your husband's family. Besides, you haven't violated the 'Seven Grounds for Divorce.' The Lin family has no reason to repudiate you."

"Seven Grounds for Divorce"? Qianli Xun heard this for the first time. She didn't understand at all. She asked, "What are the 'Seven Grounds for Divorce'?"

Matchmaker Yan explained, "The 'Seven Grounds for Divorce' are the seven conditions for repudiating a wife: disobedience to parents-in-law, failure to bear a son, promiscuity, jealousy, afflicted with a severe illness, talkativeness, and theft."

She continued, "The Military Training Officer's parents passed away early, with no parents-in-law around, so disobedience to parents-in-law doesn't apply. Young Madam has been married for less than a year and is young. As long as you want to have children, it's easy, so failure to bear a son doesn't apply either. As for promiscuity and jealousy, they don't match Young Madam at all. Young Madam is healthy—we've all seen it—so afflicted with a severe illness doesn't apply."

"As for talkativeness, with Young Madam's upbringing and talent, you're definitely not the type who talks too much and gossips. Theft is even more out of the question. You don't meet a single condition. How could the Military Training Officer write a divorce letter?"

After Matchmaker Yan finished, she sat back in her seat.

With the "Seven Grounds for Divorce" blocking the way, the repudiation plan couldn't proceed either. Qianli Xun was stuck.

Lin Jue was happy, especially seeing Zhu Shuzhen's troubled expression. He thought, "Hmph! Want to leave me? Not that easy!"

Someone from the Lin family spoke again. "Sister-in-law, you want to leave Third Brother just because he beat you and found a merchant courtesan. As long as you live your life properly with him, Second Brother promises you here—when he beats you, come find me. I'll definitely break his legs. If he finds a merchant courtesan, also come find me. I'll chase him to the brothel and beat him in front of everyone, making him lose all face. If he dares bring the merchant courtesan home, I'll make him kneel outside the Lin residence gate for three days and three nights. I don't believe I can't control him! How about that?"

Qianli Xun thought, Lin Jue lived in the same residence as his two brothers, though in different directions. But she didn't believe his brothers didn't know about past events. They didn't manage it before, yet now they came to promise. Simply unbelievable. Anyway, she only had one thought—divorce. She might as well forcefully claim one of the "Seven Grounds." As long as it was somewhat relevant, that would do.

Qianli Xun decided to go all out. She didn't answer Lin Jue's second brother. Instead, she said something that shocked everyone even more. "The 'Seven Grounds for Divorce,' right? There's one that fits. Lin Jue says I'm flirting with Lin'an talents outside. Doesn't that match the 'promiscuity' condition?"

She saw the Lin family elders' eyes widen, looking at each other. Lin Jue's mouth slightly opened. This different wife was really different. The Old Ancestor's hand fingering the prayer beads immediately stopped. Nanny Xiu Zhen rubbed her hands, not daring to speak. Matchmaker Yan turned her head and stared at this young madam, as if seeing her for the first time. Kubiao was so worried she broke out in a cold sweat. Her miss after rebirth was truly bold, daring to say anything. Things that damage a woman's reputation—others would dodge them, yet her miss actually takes the initiative to claim them?
