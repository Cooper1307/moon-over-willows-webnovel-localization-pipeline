# Chapter 23: A Riddle to Break a Marriage

Lin Jue read "The Heartbreak Riddle" silently ten times over—grasping neither its depth nor its hidden answer, tangled up in a knot of words.

Watching sweat bead on his forehead, Qianli Xun almost laughed out loud—what a rotten deal Zhu Shuzhen had gotten marrying this guy!

"If you can't make sense of my lyric, Lin Jue, stop torturing yourself—let me leave the Lin family in peace, and I'll owe you one."

Lin Jue flung the sheet to the floor—right beside Lin Zixiao's feet, who scooped it up and began reading with delight.

"Hmph!" Lin Jue snapped. "Everyone knows I despise fancy word games! Did the Old Ancestor pick this on purpose knowing I couldn't solve it just to force me into divorce? Hardly fair!"

Matron Xiuzhen rebuked him sharply: "Watch your insolence, young man! The Old Ancestor stays shut in the Zhu Buddhist Shrine year round and knew nothing about you until Miss Zhu came begging with nowhere else to turn..."

Raising a hand to check Xiuzhen, the Old Ancestor resumed rolling her beads. "At first I refused to step in. Her lyric moved me—showing both her talent and that her wish to end this marriage is no passing whim, but a weighed decision. Since you cannot solve her riddle, you two are clearly mismatched. Let go so both of you can find peace."

Before Lin Jue or any elder could reply, Lin Zixiao burst out: "Brilliant! Utterly brilliant lyric, Youqi Jushi!"

Fired up to analyze verse, he sprang to his feet: "Allow me to unpack this for everyone!"

Someone groaned, "Second Brother, Nephew Lin Jue's marriage is falling apart and you're lecturing on poetry?! Help save his home!"

Curious what on earth Zhu Shuzhen had written to summon the Old Ancestor and dazzle his uncle, Lin Jue waved a hand. "Let Second Uncle explain—I don't believe some fancy word game can wreck my marriage!"

Setting the divorce aside, Lin Zixiao beamed and launched in: "Titled 'The Heartbreak Riddle,' this lyric not only conveys heartbreak—it conceals ten number riddles!"

Ten number riddles? Where? Lin Jue racked his brain over the lines and blurted out, "Tell us the answers first, Second Uncle!"

"Listen close," Lin Zixiao declared. "'Coming downstairs, golden coins drop for divination'—drop the 'bu' (卜) from 'xia' (下), leaving 'one' (一); 'I ask High Heaven, where has he gone?'—remove 'ren' (人) from 'tian' (天), leaving 'two' (二); 'Resenting the noble lord who walked straight away'—remove the vertical stroke from 'wang' (王), leaving 'three' (三); 'Cursing the heartless one—speaking of parting, hard to hold back'—remove 'yan' (言) from 'li' (詈), leaving 'four' (四); 'Regretting how I first misspoke'—remove 'kou' (口) from 'wu' (吾), leaving 'five' (五); 'There is upper union, no lower tie'—keep the top of 'jiao' (交) and drop the bottom, leaving 'six' (六); 'Why ask right from wrong?'—drop 'bai' (白) from 'zao' (皂), leaving 'seven' (七); 'Parting needs no knife'—drop the knife radical (刀) from 'fen' (分), leaving 'eight' (八); 'From this day on lean no more on a foe'—drop the person radical (亻) from 'chou' (仇), leaving 'nine' (九); 'A thousand miles of longing wiped away with one stroke'—drop the top flick from 'qian' (千), leaving 'ten' (十)! Tell me, everyone—isn't that sheer genius?!"

Understanding dawned on Lin Jue—he really didn't know his wife at all—yet the deeper heartbreak still sailed over his head. He sneered, "So what? Just ten numbers—big deal!"

Around the hall, half the Lin elders marveled while the rest shrugged alongside Lin Jue.

"You're wrong, Nephew," Lin Zixiao countered. "Composing an entire riddle lyric requires selecting exact characters, fitting strict rhyme schemes, weaving every glyph seamlessly into natural phrases, and never drifting from the emotional core! Writing verse is like raising a tower brick by brick—no easier than drilling armies! Youqi Jushi's wit and craft leave this old man in awe. Alas, every line brims with resentment and finality—above all 'from this day on lean no more on a foe.' She already regards you as an enemy, Lin Jue! If I still block this divorce, your future together will be poison. Give Youqi Jushi her freedom!"

Lin Jue slumped in his chair, gloom darkening his face. He understood every word—yet couldn't bear letting her walk out into another man's arms! Dongfang Chenze, Qiao Wenxian—those vultures had eyed his wife for ages; no way was he handing her over!

Thrilled that one poem had flipped Lin Zixiao so fast, Qianli Xun rose and bowed. "Spot-on reading, Second Uncle! Thank you for understanding and granting your blessing."

"Don't mention it, Youqi Jushi. Might I keep this copy to study more at home?"

"Yours to keep, Second Uncle!"

Folding the sheet carefully into his sleeve, Lin Zixiao bowed to the Old Ancestor. "As a Lin elder who now consents to the divorce, it's improper for me to stay. I take my leave."

The Old Ancestor gave a slight nod.

Turning to his kin, Lin Zixiao announced, "Brothers, nephews—I'm off. Carry on."

A clan brother sprang up. "Second Brother, you leave—who presides?!"

"Third Brother's right here—let him preside!" Flicking his sleeve, Lin Zixiao walked out.

The Old Ancestor spoke up: "Son-in-law, I said earlier—fail the riddle, grant my granddaughter her freedom. You failed. Sign the mutual divorce papers."

Qianli Xun looked at Lin Jue full of hope.

Wrinkling his brow in a glum look, Lin Jue pushed back: "Forcing me to divorce over one poem makes zero sense! Out of respect for your age I won't argue with you, Old Ancestor—yet unless you convince every elder here and both my brothers to walk out like Second Uncle, I'm not signing a thing!"

Without a word, the Old Ancestor closed her eyes and rolled her beads.

"Since I'm presiding, I'll take the main seat!" Third Uncle Lin rose from beside Lin Jue, bowed to the Old Ancestor, and settled into the head chair.

Qianli Xun eyed Third Uncle: plump frame, beard, eyes squinted to slits, features bunched close together—looked downright comical. Holding her tongue, she waited to hear his pitch.

Clearing his throat twice, Third Uncle began: "Second Brother caves easy because he loves fancy word games. Not me! Write a hundred lyrics, means zilch to me. Give it a rest, nephew's wife—you're not young anymore, who'd take a divorced woman? Quit dreaming of poems and songs—verse won't put rice on the table! Marriage is one thing, poetry's another. Whoever you marry, come nightfall you blow out the lamp, pull up the quilt, and it's all the same under the covers..."

Finding the remark crude, the Old Ancestor gave a warning cough.

Matron Xiuzhen snapped, "Third Uncle Lin, mind your language!"

Checking himself at once, Third Uncle pivoted: "As the saying goes, a woman's virtue is having no talent! Too much cleverness fills a woman's head with lofty demands and wrecks a home. Lin Jue is Lin'an's Training Officer guarding the whole city—plenty of girls would kill to marry him, yet he picked you out of true devotion! Stop pining for those Lin'an scholars—all flash and no steel, pretty to look at and useless in a fight! Not like my nephew Lin Jue, martial-trained, strong as an ox, come nightfall..."

There went his mouth slipping back under the covers again! Qianli Xun bit back a laugh—what a circus this Lin clan was.

Another cough from the Old Ancestor cut him short, forcing a wrap-up: "Point is, Third Uncle wants what's best for you. Stay together and make it work—harmony brings prosperity. Done!"

Third Uncle's speech swept away every bit of Lin Jue's gloom, puffing his confidence right back up. Lose one Second Uncle? Plenty more clan uncles stood behind him—the real show was just starting!

Done staying quiet, Qianli Xun spoke up: "Very practical advice, Third Uncle—appreciated! Yet my resolve to leave Lin Jue is ironclad; no lecture from the Lin clan will change my mind. If you elders refuse mutual divorce, then let Lin Jue write a bill of repudiation right here! I don't fear a ruined name, and I don't fear being barred from my maiden door!"

Shock rippled across the room. Rumors of her asking to be cast out were one thing—hearing a noble lady demand repudiation to their faces sounded unheard of! Only the Old Ancestor rolled her beads undisturbed.

Matchmaker Yan sprang up at once. "Young Madam, never talk of being cast out! Besides, you haven't violated any of the Seven Conditions for Repudiating a Wife—the Lin family has no grounds to cast you out!"

"Seven Conditions?" First time Qianli Xun had heard that phrase. "What are the Seven Conditions?"

Matchmaker Yan ticked them off: "Disobedience to parents-in-law, failure to bear a son, adultery, jealousy, severe illness, gossip, and theft! The Training Officer's parents passed away early, so disobedience doesn't apply. You've been wed less than a year and are young—bearing children is easy, so no failure there. Adultery and jealousy certainly don't fit. You're clearly healthy—no severe illness. With your poise and refinement, gossip is unthinkable—and theft is absurd! You don't fit a single ground; how could he even write a bill of repudiation?"

Finished, Matchmaker Yan sat back down.

Blocked cold by the Seven Conditions, even repudiation hit a wall! Qianli Xun felt boxed in.

Delighted at her stumped face, Lin Jue gloated inside: Hmph! Walking out on me won't be that easy!

Another Lin spoke up: "Sister-in-law, you only want to leave Third Brother because he hit you and saw courtesans. Stay with him, and Second Brother swears right here: if he hits you, come to me and I'll break his legs! If he visits courtesans, I'll haul him out of the brothel and thrash him in public! If he dares bring one home, I'll make him kneel outside Lin Manor's main gate for three days and nights! Fair enough?"

Living under the same roof—different courtyards or not—Qianli Xun didn't buy for one second his brothers knew nothing before. Now they suddenly play tough? Worthless promises! Only one thing mattered: getting divorced—even if she had to claim one of those Seven Conditions herself!

Going all-in, ignoring Second Brother, she dropped a bombshell: "Seven Conditions, right? One fits! Didn't Lin Jue accuse me of carrying on with Lin'an scholars outside? Doesn't that fit 'adultery'?!"

Eyes bulged all down the Lin row; Lin Jue's jaw dropped—this reborn wife stopped at nothing! Even the Old Ancestor's hand froze on her beads while Matron Xiuzhen wrung her hands in stunned silence. Matchmaker Yan stared as if seeing a stranger, and cold sweat drenched Kuqiao's brow: Reborn Miss is fearless—people run a mile from anything touching a woman's honor, and she's volunteering for it!
