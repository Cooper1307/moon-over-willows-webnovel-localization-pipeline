# Chapter 23: A Riddle to Break a Marriage

---

林觉在心里将《断肠谜》来来回回念了不下十遍，既品不出其中滋味，更不知谜底，他完全陷入一团乱如麻的文字谜中。

Lin Jue recited "The Heartbreak Riddle" in his mind no less than ten times. He couldn't grasp its meaning, let alone know the riddle's answer. He was completely lost in a tangled mess of words.

---

千里寻见林觉急得满头是汗，甚觉好笑，更认为朱淑真嫁给他实在是亏大发了。

Qianli Xun saw Lin Jue sweating profusely and found it rather amusing—and thought Zhu Shuzhen had gotten a raw deal marrying the man.

---

“林觉，既看不懂我写的词，就不要为难自己，乖乖放我离开林家，我将感激不尽。”

“Lin Jue, if you can't understand the ci I wrote, then don't torture yourself. Just let me go—peacefully, without a fuss—and I'll be ever so grateful.”

---

林觉将写有《断肠谜》的纸张向地上扔去，正好扔在林子孝的脚边，他顺手捡起，仔细品味起来。

Lin Jue threw the paper with "The Heartbreak Riddle" toward the ground. It happened to land at Lin Zixiao's feet. He picked it up and began to study it intently.

---

林觉则说：“哼！我平日最讨厌这些附庸风雅的玩意儿，老祖宗该不会知道我不懂，故意拿来为难我？想以此为由让我同意和离，实在不公平！”

Lin Jue sneered, "Hmph! I've always hated this pretentious poetry nonsense! The Old Ancestor probably knew I wouldn't understand and deliberately brought it out to trouble me. Using this as an excuse to force me into a divorce is simply unfair!"

---

秀珍嬷嬷批评道：“你这后辈着实无礼，老祖宗长年居住在朱家佛堂，大门不出二门不迈，对你这位小辈一无所知，要不是朱家姑娘无路可走，求到老祖宗面前，她今日怎会来此？……”

Matron Xiuzhen scolded, "You lack all manners, young man. The Old Ancestor has lived in the Zhu family's Buddhist shrine for years, never stepping outside. She knew nothing about any of you juniors. If the Zhu family girl hadn't been left with no other choice and begged the Old Ancestor for help, why would she come here today?"

---

老祖宗抬手阻止秀珍嬷嬷，边盘佛珠边说：“我本不愿为孙女出面，是她的词打动我，既让我看到她的才华，也让我明白她想要摆脱这段婚姻并不是一时冲动，而是经过认真思量后做出的决定。姑爷既猜不出谜底，看来的确不适合我孙女，还请放手，才能各自安好。”

The Old Ancestor raised her hand to stop Matron Xiuzhen. Thumbing her prayer beads, she said, “I never planned to step in for my granddaughter. But her ci moved me. It showed me her talent—and her resolve. This isn't some girl's whim. She's thought long and hard before making this choice. Since the young master can't solve the riddle—perhaps he truly isn't the right match for my granddaughter. Let her go. Let both of you find peace.”

---

未等林觉及其他族人说话，林子孝不由脱口而出：“妙！幽栖居士这首词写得实在是妙！”

Before Lin Jue and other clansmen could speak, Lin Zixiao blurted out, "Brilliant! Youqi Jushi's ci is truly a masterpiece!"

---

林子孝解诗的兴致大发，不由站起来：“我给诸位解解……”

Lin Zixiao's enthusiasm for explaining the poem grew. He stood up. "Let me explain it to everyone..."

---

有人说：“二哥，林觉侄儿这婚都要离了，你竟然还有心情解诗？还是赶紧想办法怎么帮他护住这个小家吧！”

Someone said, "Brother Zixiao, Lin Jue's marriage is about to end, and you're still in the mood to analyze poems? Hurry up and think of a way to help him protect his little family!"

---

林觉很想知道朱淑真在词中到底写些什么，既能打动老祖宗出面，还让二叔赞不绝口，于是说道：“就让二叔给大家解解，我就不信，附庸风雅的玩意儿能破坏我的婚姻？”

Lin Jue really wanted to know what Zhu Shuzhen had written in the ci—something that moved the Old Ancestor to intervene and earned Lin Zixiao's praise. So he said, “Let Second Uncle walk us through it. I don't believe fancy word games can destroy my marriage！”

---

林子孝暂时把和离之事放到一边，侃侃而谈：“这首词名为《断肠谜》，不仅抒发了断肠的主旨，还藏着十个数字之谜……”

Lin Zixiao temporarily set aside the separation matter and spoke eloquently, "This ci is titled 'The Heartbreak Riddle.' It not only expresses the theme of heartbreak but also hides a puzzle of ten numbers..."

---

林觉心想：“十个数字之谜？我怎么看不出？”他努力回想词中的句子，还是不明所以，不由脱口而出：“二叔先说说谜底。”

Lin Jue thought, "Ten numbers? How can I not see it?" He tried hard to recall the lines in the ci but still didn't understand. He called out, "Second Uncle, just tell us the answer."

---

林子孝继续讲道：“听我慢慢给大家解。'下楼来，金钱卜落'，'下'字'卜落'为一；'问苍天，人在何方'，'天'字'人'去为二；'恨王孙，一直去了'，'王'字去'一直'为三；'詈冤家，言去难留'，'詈'字'言'去为四；'悔当初，吾错失口'，'吾'字失'口'为五；'有上交，无下交'，'交'字'有上''无下'为六；'皂白何须问'，'皂'字去'白'为七；'分开不用刀'，'分'字'不用刀'为八；'从今莫把仇人靠'，'仇'字无'人靠'为九；'千里相思一撇消'，'千'字'一撇消'则为十。大家说说，妙不妙？”

Lin Zixiao continued, "Listen as I explain slowly. 'Coming downstairs, I cast coins to divine'—the character '下' (down) with '卜' (divine) removed becomes '一' (one). 'Asking the azure sky, where could he be?'—the character '天' (sky) with '人' (person) removed becomes '二' (two). 'Hating my lord, he has gone straight away'—the character '王' (prince) with the straight stroke removed becomes '三' (three). 'Cursing the faithless one, so hard to make him stay'—the character '詈' (curse) with '言' (words) removed becomes '四' (four). 'Regretting the past, I let careless words slip'—the character '吾' (I) losing '口' (mouth) becomes '五' (five).

---

'有上交，无下交'，'交'字'有上''无下'为六；'皂白何须问'，'皂'字去'白'为七；'分开不用刀'，'分'字'不用刀'为八；'从今莫把仇人靠'，'仇'字无'人靠'为九；'千里相思一撇消'，'千'字'一撇消'则为十。大家说说，妙不妙？"

'Ties severed above, with nothing below'—the character '交' stripped of its bottom strokes becomes '六' (six). 'Why even ask who is right and who is wrong?'—the character '皂' with '白' removed becomes '七' (seven). 'Separated without a knife'—the character '分' without using a knife becomes '八' (eight). 'From now on rely on no foe'—the character '仇' without a person relying becomes '九' (nine). 'A thousand kinds of longing, erased with one single stroke'—the character '千' with a stroke erased becomes '十' (ten). Everyone, isn't it brilliant?" [TN: This riddle is a Chinese character puzzle (拆字谜). Each line of the ci hides a number from one to ten by removing or modifying strokes of a Chinese character. Since this wordplay depends entirely on the visual structure of Chinese characters, the original characters are provided with explanations of how each transformation works.]

---

林子孝解完谜底，林觉恍然大悟，他确实不了解眼前的妻子，可“断肠”的主旨依然品不出来，冷笑道：“又能如何？不就是写了十个数字吗？有什么了不起？”

After Lin Zixiao finished his breakdown, Lin Jue suddenly understood. He indeed didn't know his wife before him. But he still couldn't grasp the "heartbreak" theme. He scoffed, "So what? She just hid ten numbers in a poem. What's so great about that?"

---

林家其他族人有的被折服，有的与林觉一样，认为没什么了不起。

Some of the Lin family clansmen were convinced, while others, like Lin Jue, thought it was nothing special.

---

林子孝接着讲：“林觉侄儿此言差矣，写这类从头到尾是谜底的词，首先要选适合的字，选字当需费一番功夫；其次，字选好了，选韵入韵亦需费时费力，还要想方设法将每一个字缀入词句；最后，也是最关键的，不能背离主旨，要将情感融入其中。所以，写诗作词好比建造楼阁，一砖一瓦都需谨慎，不比你练兵容易。幽栖居士心思巧妙，才华横溢，能写出这等词，令老夫佩服。唉！词中句句充斥着不满，处处诉说分道扬镳，尤其是这句'从今莫把仇人靠'，林觉侄儿，幽栖居士已将你视为仇人，如若二叔再不同意和离，只怕你们以后的日子不好过啊，还是放幽栖居士自由吧！”

Lin Zixiao continued, “Lin Jue, nephew, you're wrong. Writing such a ci with hidden answers from beginning to end requires painstaking effort—first, choosing the right characters. Then, matching the rhyme scheme and meter, and finding ways to weave each character into the lines. But the hardest part? You can't deviate from the main theme—you must pour real emotion into it. Writing poetry like this is like building a tower—every brick and tile must be laid with care. It's no easier than training an army! Youqi Jushi has a brilliant mind and extraordinary talent. Being able to write such a ci fills me with admiration. Alas! Every line in the ci bleeds with dissatisfaction, everywhere crying out for parting. Especially this line: 'From now on rely on no foe.' Lin Jue, nephew—Youqi Jushi already treats you as an enemy. If your uncle keeps opposing this split, I'm afraid your future days won't be easy. Better let Youqi Jushi have her freedom！”

---

听到这话，林觉坐在椅子上一脸落寞的表情，尽管他什么都明白，可就是不想轻易放朱淑真离开林家，谁让他喜欢她，因喜欢不愿看到她将来扑进其他男人的怀抱。什么东方尘泽，什么乔文仙，觊觎他的娘子不是一天两天，决不能让他们得逞！

Hearing this, Lin Jue sank into his chair, a look of defeat on his face. He understood everything now—but he just couldn't bring himself to let Zhu Shuzhen leave. Why did he have to fall for her? Because he loved her, the thought of her throwing herself into another man's arms drove him mad. Dongfang Chenze. Qiao Wenxian. Both had been circling his wife like vultures for months. He'd be damned if he let either of them swoop in!

---

千里寻一阵欣喜，没想到朱淑真的一首词这么快拿下林子孝。她起身谢道：“二叔的解析实在到位，感谢二叔理解和成全。”

Qianli Xun was thrilled. She didn't expect Zhu Shuzhen's ci to win over Lin Zixiao so quickly. She stood up and thanked him, "Your analysis was masterful. Thank you for your understanding and support."

---

“幽栖居士不必客气，能不能把这首词送给我？我想回去再仔细研究研究。”

"Youqi Jushi, don't be polite. Could you give me this ci? I want to take it back and study it carefully."

---

“二叔尽管拿去。”

"Second Uncle, please take it."

---

林子孝把纸张叠好，揣进怀里，面向老祖宗行礼：“老祖宗，我是林家族人，既已同意和离，不适合在此待下去，晚辈先告退。”

Lin Zixiao carefully folded the paper and tucked it into his robes. He bowed to the Old Ancestor. "Old Ancestor, as a Lin family clansman, since I've agreed to the separation, it's not fitting for me to stay and argue with my kin. I'll take my leave."

---

老祖宗点点头，表示同意。

The Old Ancestor nodded in agreement.

---

林子孝又面向族人：“各位兄弟，三位侄儿，我走了，你们继续。”

Lin Zixiao faced the clansmen again. "Brothers, nephews. I'm leaving. You continue."

---

一位族人站起来阻止道：“二哥，你走了，谁来主持？”

A clansman stood up to stop him. "Brother Zixiao, if you leave, who will preside?"

---

林子孝说：“三弟还在，让他主持。”说完，林子孝拂袖而去。

Lin Zixiao said, "Third Brother is still here. Let him preside." With that, Lin Zixiao flicked his sleeves and left.

---

老祖宗开口说：“姑爷，我刚才说过，你若猜不出，就请还我孙女自由。你既然没有猜出谜底，请在和离书上签字吧。”

The Old Ancestor turned her gaze back to Lin Jue. “Lin Jue, I said it clearly—if you can't guess the riddle, please return my granddaughter's freedom. Since you failed, sign the divorce papers.”

---

千里寻充满期待地看着林觉。

Qianli Xun looked at Lin Jue with expectation.

---

林觉满面愁容，用失落的语气说：“仅凭一首词就想让我同意和离，实在没有道理，晚辈念在老祖宗年长，不想跟您计较，除非您把这里的长辈和我两位哥哥一一说服，让他们也像二叔一样甩袖离去，我才会同意在和离书上签字。”

Lin Jue's face twisted with sorrow. “Relying on a single poem to force me into breaking this marriage is completely unreasonable. Out of respect for your age, Old Ancestor, I won't argue with you. But unless you persuade every elder here—and my two older brothers—to walk out just like he did... I will never sign those papers.”

---

老祖宗无言，继续微闭双目，盘着佛珠。

The Old Ancestor said nothing. She closed her eyes and resumed thumbing her prayer beads.

---

“既然让我主持，我还是坐在正位上。”林觉旁边的三叔站起来，向老祖宗行礼，走到正位跟前坐下。

"Since I am to preside, I shall take the main seat." The Third Uncle, sitting beside Lin Jue, stood up, gave a cursory bow to the Old Ancestor, and marched over to the seat of honor.

---

千里寻瞅着这位三叔，身形偏胖，留着胡须，眼睛迷成一条缝，五官离得较近，看上去很滑稽。本想与这位三叔说两句，仔细一想，还是先听听他如何说。

Qianli Xun studied this Third Uncle. He was rather rotund, sporting a wispy beard, eyes squinted into tiny slits, and features awkwardly bunched together. He looked quite comical. She debated cutting him off, but decided to hear what he had to say first.

---

三叔坐定后，咳了两声：“二哥能轻易被你们改变，只怪他平日喜欢附庸风雅的玩意儿。我可不喜欢，侄媳妇写多少词，对我来讲，毫无用处。我劝侄媳妇还是省省吧，年纪也不小，和离后谁还会要你？别整天想着什么诗啊词啊的，那玩意又不能当饭吃，过日子跟那玩意儿是两码事，跟了谁，到晚上还不是灯一熄，盖上被子，被窝里那点事儿……”

Once seated, Third Uncle cleared his throat. “Zixiao was easily swayed because he has a soft spot for that flowery, pretentious garbage. I don't. I don't care how many poems you write, Niece-in-law—to me, they're useless. I advise you to save your energy. You aren't young anymore. After a divorce, who else will want you? Stop filling your head with poetry and romance—you can't eat poems! Real life is nothing like those fantasies. No matter what man you follow, at the end of the day, when the lights go out and you're under the covers, a man's performance is all that matters...”

---

老祖宗觉得林家三叔说得露骨，咳了一声，以示提醒。

The Old Ancestor found the Third Uncle's words entirely too vulgar. She let out a sharp, warning cough.

Matron Xiuzhen scolded coldly, "Third Uncle Lin, watch your tongue."

---

三叔立刻收住：“常言说得好，女子无才便是德。女子有才华，思想容易变得复杂，想得多，要求的也多，搞得家庭不稳。林觉侄儿是临安城的练兵官，训练的士兵负责守卫整个临安，贡献极大，多少女子想嫁给他，可他却偏偏看上侄媳妇，也说明他对侄媳妇是真心。三叔劝你，别整日想着那些临安才子，他们不过是银枪蜡头，中看不中用，哪像我林觉侄儿，练武出身，身强体壮，到了晚上……”

Third Uncle immediately reeled it back. “Ahem! As the old saying goes, 'a woman's virtue lies in her lack of talent.' A woman too talented becomes overactive in thought—she overthinks, demands too much, and destabilizes the household! My nephew Lin Jue is the Military Training Officer of Lin'an. The soldiers he trains protect this entire city! He's a man of great contribution! Do you know how many women are dying to marry him? Yet he specifically chose you, Niece-in-law. That proves his feelings are genuine! Take my advice: stop fantasizing about those weak-kneed scholars. They're all flash and no substance—pretty to look at, useless in a fight! Nothing like my nephew Lin Jue—trained in martial arts, strong and strapping. When night falls...”

---

林家三叔说着说着还是没管住嘴，又扯到被窝里那些事。千里寻觉得好笑，林家真是什么人都有。

Unable to control his crude mouth, Third Uncle veered right back into the gutter. Qianli Xun almost laughed out loud. The Lin family truly had a diverse collection of clowns.

---

又听到老祖宗的咳嗽声，三叔才没敢往下说，终于开始收尾：“总之，三叔是为侄媳妇好，希望你们小两口能把日子好好过下去，家和万事兴嘛，我说完了。”

Hearing the Old Ancestor cough again, Third Uncle panicked and scrambled to wrap up. "In short! Third Uncle is looking out for your best interests, Niece-in-law! I hope you young couple can reconcile and live well together. A harmonious family brings prosperity! I've said my piece."

---

林觉听完三叔所说，《断肠谜》造成的失落感顿时消散，又恢复先前的自信。走一个二叔，还有好几位族叔在此为他撑腰，好戏还在后头。

Hearing his uncle's robust defense, the lingering defeat Lin Jue felt from "The Heartbreak Riddle" instantly vanished, replaced by his old arrogance. So what if Lin Zixiao left? He still had a room full of clan elders backing him. The real show was just beginning.

---

千里寻不能再沉默下去：“三叔讲得甚是实在，您的好意我心领，但我离开林觉之心坚不可摧，无论林家人怎么劝说，都不会改变想法，如果诸位长辈不同意和离，就请林觉当场写下休书，我不怕被休之后名声不好，也不怕娘家不让我进门！”

Qianli Xun refused to stay silent. "Third Uncle's words are certainly... practical," she said icily. "I appreciate your 'good intentions.' But my determination to leave Lin Jue is absolute. No matter what any of you say, my mind won't change. If the elders refuse an amicable parting, then I demand Lin Jue write me a divorce letter right here and now! I'm not afraid of the ruined reputation of being cast out, and I'm certainly not afraid of my own family turning me away!"

---

千里寻的话令在场大部分人感到震惊，尽管她说过主动要求丈夫休她之事，可大家亲耳听到，还是难以置信，竟然有女子敢于让丈夫休自己，真是闻所未闻。只有老祖宗依然盘着佛珠，双目微闭。

Her declaration sent shockwaves through the hall. Although she had mentioned wanting to leave before, hearing a woman actually demand a divorce letter unilaterally was staggering. Completely unheard of!

Only the Old Ancestor remained unfazed, her eyes lowered as she calmly turned her prayer beads.

---

闫媒婆听后，急忙站起来：“少夫人，您是女子，千万不能被婆家休了，再说您又没触犯'七出'，林家也没有理由休您。”

Matchmaker Yan leaped up in a panic. "Young Madam! You are a woman! You absolutely cannot allow yourself to be discarded! Furthermore, you haven't violated a single one of the 'Seven Grounds for Divorce.' The Lin family has no legal basis to send you away!"

---

“七出”？千里寻第一次听说，根本不明白，不由问道：“'七出'是什么？”

"Seven Grounds for Divorce"? Qianli Xun frowned. It was the first time she'd heard the term. "What exactly are the Seven Grounds?"

---

闫媒婆解释道：“'七出'是休妻的七大条件，分别是不孝公婆、无子、淫、善妒、身有恶疾、口多言、盗窃。练兵官父母早亡，无公婆在身边，谈不上不孝公婆；少夫人成婚不到一年，又年轻，只要想生孩子，再容易不过，所以无子也不符合；至于淫和善妒更与少夫人不相符；少夫人身体健康，我们都看在眼里，身有恶疾也不符合；至于口多言，凭少夫人的修养和才华，绝不是那种多言又爱搬弄是非之人；盗窃更谈不上。您一项都不符合，练兵官的休书岂能写得下去？”

Matchmaker Yan hurriedly laid out, "The Seven Grounds are the seven conditions under which a husband may send away his wife: disobedience to parents-in-law, failure to bear a son, unchastity, jealousy, severe illness, excessive talkativeness, and theft."

She continued, “The Military Training Officer's parents passed away long ago, so 'disobedience to parents-in-law' doesn't apply. You've been married less than a year and are still young—having children will be easy, so 'failure to bear a son' doesn't apply. You're clearly healthy, so 'severe illness' is out. With your upbringing and talent, you're certainly not a gossiping shrew—'talkativeness' doesn't fit. 'Theft' is entirely out of the question. As for jealousy and unchastity—those are completely absurd! You don't meet a single condition! How could he possibly write a divorce letter？”

Having delivered her frantic legal defense, Matchmaker Yan slumped back into her chair.

---

有“七出”挡在面前，休妻这一招也进行不下去，千里寻被难住。

With the "Seven Grounds for Divorce" acting as an impenetrable legal shield, the plan to be cast out was completely blocked. Qianli Xun was stuck.

---

林觉倒开心，尤其看到朱淑真为难的样子，心想：“哼！想离开我，没那么容易！”

Lin Jue, however, was thrilled. Seeing the frustrated look on Zhu Shuzhen's face filled him with a twisted sense of victory. "Hmph! Thought you could leave me? Not that easy!"

---

林家又有人说：“弟妹呀，你想离开三弟，无非是因为他打你，又找商妓，只要你跟他好好过日子，二哥在此向你保证，他打你时，你就来找我，我定打断他的腿；他要是找商妓，你也来找我，我会追到青楼当着众人的面揍他一顿，让他颜面全无，要敢把商妓带到家里，我就让他跪在林府的大门外，跪个三天三夜，不相信治不了他！你看可否？”

Another Lin elder chimed in. "Sister-in-law, you want to leave your husband because he beat you and visited a brothel. If you just settle down and live properly with him, your brother here promises you: if he ever touches you again, you come straight to me. I will personally break both his legs! If he visits another brothel, I'll drag him out into the street and thrash him senseless in front of the whole town! If he dares bring a courtesan into this house, I'll make him kneel outside the Lin estate gates for three days and three nights! I promise I'll whip him into shape! How does that sound?"

---

千里寻心想，林觉与他的两个哥哥同住在一个宅子，虽在不同的方位，但她不相信以前的事儿林觉的两位哥哥会不知？以前都不管，现在却来保证，实在不可信。反正她只有一个念头，就是离婚，不如在“七出”里硬拉一条，只要沾些边儿就行。

*Qianli Xun scoffed internally. Lin Jue lived in the exact same estate as his two brothers. She refused to believe they'd been blind to his domestic violence all this time. They hadn't lifted a finger before, but now they were making grand promises? Pure hypocrisy. Anyway, she had only one goal—getting away. If they were going to hide behind the “Seven Grounds，” she might as well forcefully claim one for herself. As long as it was even remotely relevant, that would do.*

---

千里寻打算豁出去，她没有回答林家二哥的问题，说出令所有人更加震惊的话：“'七出'是吧，倒有一条符合，林觉不是说我在外面跟临安才子勾勾搭搭，难道不符合'淫'这一条吗？”

Deciding to go scorched-earth, Qianli Xun ignored Lin Zixiao entirely. Looking around the room, she dropped a bombshell that left everyone paralyzed with shock. “The 'Seven Grounds,' you say? Actually, I think I fit one perfectly. Didn't Lin Jue just accuse me of running around the city, shamelessly carrying on with local scholars? Tell me—doesn't that perfectly satisfy the grounds of 'Unchastity'？”

---

只见林家长辈瞪大眼睛，面面相觑；林觉的嘴巴微微张开，这个不一样的娘子真的很不一样；老祖宗正在盘佛珠的手立刻停下，秀珍嬷嬷搓着手不敢言语；闫媒婆转头盯着这位少夫人，像是头一次见到她；苦荞担心得头上直冒冷汗，重生后的小姐真是胆大，什么话都敢说，有损女子清誉之事别人躲都躲不及，这位冤家怎么还大包大揽起来？

The Lin family elders' eyes widened in shock as they exchanged glances. Lin Jue's jaw dropped. This new version of his wife was genuinely terrifying.

The rhythmic clicking of the Old Ancestor's prayer beads abruptly stopped. Matron Xiuzhen clasped her hands nervously, too stunned to speak. Matchmaker Yan gaped at the Young Madam as if she had suddenly grown a second head. Cold sweat beaded on Kuqiao's forehead.

Her reborn Miss was far too reckless! She dared to say absolutely anything! Any other woman would rather die than be accused of unchastity—it was a stain that destroyed a woman's honor forever. Yet her impossible, chaotic Miss had just boldly claimed it for herself!
