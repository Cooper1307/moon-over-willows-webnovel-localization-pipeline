## Chapter 290: Ambush Beneath the Divine Willow

After Haoyu left, Kuqiao fretted two years sounded forever, yet Qianli Xun stood firm—only time tested a man's true metal, and after Jinlan's circus, no Chen elder would overrule her.

With the storm past, she felt a quiet pull to visit the Divine Willow. Stepping along the hillside path near Youlin Retreat—so close, yet worlds away now—she reflected on a full year since crossing millennia: family, love, betrayal, marriage, fame as a poetess and secret storyteller. Like the Divine Willow quietly wearing everyone's red ribbons without complaint, maybe accepting fate with open eyes was the real path to reshaping it.

Glancing up into the red-draped crown, she spotted one ribbon tied remarkably high. Leaping up with light-foot technique, she unfolded it—and froze at *Shen Jianchen / Zhao Pu* paired with *Qianli Xun / Zhu Shuzhen*! *Consummated with Mengrong and still tying our names together?! If this drops in her lap, I'll never wash it off!* Untying it swiftly, she tucked it into her waist pouch and dropped gracefully back down.

The second her boots hit grass, a ring of black-clad, masked swordsmen burst out of the brush with broadswords raised!

"Get behind the willow, Kuqiao!"

Empty-handed yet fearless, Qianli Xun wove like a darting dragon through flashing steel—parrying, punching chests, snapping wrists, kicking one attacker smashing straight into the willow trunk coughing blood! Even after a blade nicked her arm, her blistering fists battered half the squad flat until the survivors turned tail and bolted.

Binding her minor arm scratch with a handkerchief, Kuqiao marched over, booted the groaning assassin still pinned beneath the willow, ripped off his mask, and held his own broadsword at his nose until he squealed: "Master Jiang of the Qiantang Gang! Someone bought your life!"

A cold sadness gripped Qianli Xun—Mengrong really wouldn't stop. Turning silently to leave, she walked straight into Qiao Mengrong blocking the path with Matron Zou and Xilan.

"Useless trash—barely scratched your arm!" Mengrong spat coldly. "Today was just a warm-up."

Outraged, Kuqiao spoke up for her mistress, only for Mengrong to bark "Slap her!"—and Qianli Xun caught Xilan's wrist and shoved her clear: "Nobody touches my maid—not even royalty!"

Accusing Qianli Xun of lurking under the willow to seduce Brother—calling her a hypocrite putting up a virtue arch—Mengrong drew only calm, aching restraint from Qianli Xun, who bowed to leave before Matron Zou stepped in to lecture her on imperial marriage standards and warn her off royal fantasies.

"I want a quiet life—neither bothering you nor being dragged into your drama," Qianli Xun replied evenly. "And I won't set foot under the Divine Willow again." Turning once more after several paces to offer one final, silent curtsy, she walked away for good.

Watching her vanish left even Mengrong with a fleeting hollow pang—yet when Matron Zou urged letting go, Mengrong hardened like ice: "Look how she twisted even your heart in two minutes! She hooked Qiao Wenxian, hooked Brother, even tamed Chen Haoyu—she *must* die! Qiantang Gang is useless; go meet Chen Haodong at Yongtai Teahouse tomorrow!"
