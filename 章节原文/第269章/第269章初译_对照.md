第269章 也该陈浩东出手
## Chapter 269: Chen Haodong's Turn to Strike

三位公子离开后，白风心疼王爷的银子，一脸不满：“凭什么东方公子举办诗会，让王爷您掏银子？”
“回汴梁前承诺他的，不好反悔。”
“王爷承诺什么不好，偏偏承诺只管往外花银子却无法进帐的事？岂不亏大发？”
“你懂什么？有比银子更重要的。”
“王爷是有钱人，自然不觉得银子金贵，我就觉得银子是世上最重要的东西。”
“你觉得苦荞重要还是银子重要？”
王爷的问题还真把白风难住，想了一会儿说：“一样重要！”
提到苦荞，白风想起南双和喜兰说过的话，试探地问：“王爷，您说我娶苦荞王妃会不会不高兴？”
申简辰继续翻阅卷宗：“怎么会？”
“我娶苦荞是不是需仪王妃同意？”
“你是我的侍卫，我同意即可。”
“我若娶了苦荞能不能不住在幽林别居？”
申简辰放下卷宗盯着白风：“什么意思？”
“我想在外面置办房产，有自己独立的家，苦荞也能生活得舒坦些。当然，不会耽误伺候王爷的大事。”
申简辰继续拿起卷宗，边翻边说：“房产我给你置办。”
“谢王爷！”白风觉得自己很厉害，很快将难题解决，更体会到跟着王爷的好处。
一早哥哥悄无声息离开，乔梦容一点儿察觉都没有。
等她睁开眼时，身边已无哥哥的身影，她幸福之余又充满惆怅，如果每天醒来，第一眼看到哥哥那该多好。
哥哥生病时，衣食住行都离不开她，她能轻松自如地操控他的生活。起死回生的哥哥疾病全无，她难以掌控，也不敢掌控。
与哥哥有了肌肤之亲后，掌控哥哥的想法愈加强烈，又无从下手，担心稍有不慎哥哥反而会离她越来越远。
乔梦容在哥哥躺过的地方又趴了一会儿，通过枕头和被子再嗅一嗅哥哥身上的味道，那味道刺激着她的嗅觉，进而刺激她的大脑，昨晚的情景一幕一幕呈现在脑海……
哥哥对待她既温柔又粗鲁。温柔时像一只绵羊，在她耳边咩咩地叫着，声音低沉而轻柔，她整个身心就像平静的湖水不断泛起粼粼的水波。粗鲁时，像惊涛骇浪，带着她一起向岸边的岩石拍打而去，直到撞击出无数的水花……
正当乔梦容沉浸在昨晚情景的回味中时，喜兰将床幔挂起：“王妃，该起床了。”
乔梦容不舍地从哥哥睡过的地方爬起，用手摸摸枕头，仿佛只有如此才能将哥哥的气味留在她的指间。
喜兰见仪王妃懒懒的样子，心中觉得好笑，定是昨晚与王爷折腾得太累。
她一边为仪王妃整理衣装，一边说：“王爷关心王妃，走时吩咐奴婢让厨房给王妃熬了滋补粥，刚熬好，已放在桌上，王妃梳洗完毕，刚好食用。”
哥哥如此关心她，乔梦容更觉幸福，一扫懒懒的状态，变得精神起来。
梳洗完毕，整理好妆容后，坐在桌前用心享受哥哥为她安排的滋补粥，甜而不腻，还有股淡淡的中草药的味道，并不苦，反而更觉爽口。
享用完滋补粥后，乔梦容不想待在幽林别居，没有哥哥的地方实在无趣。
“喜兰，我今日无事，你陪我去趟司理院。”
正要起身带着喜兰去司理院找哥哥，有丫鬟进来禀报：“仪王妃，苗夫人和白夫人前来拜见。”
乔梦容心中埋怨二人来得不是时候，又一想她们应该是为书卷之事而来，也不知进展得如何，不妨接见接见，与她们聊完再去司理院不迟。
她继续坐好：“请二位夫人进来。”
丫鬟去后，两位夫人一起走进，向仪王妃请安之后，各自落座。
白梅若已打定主意，绝不先开口，一坐下便紧闭嘴巴。
苗知瑜发现白梅若一句话不说，总不能尴尬地坐着，于是先开口：“我与白夫人来幽林别居是有要事与王妃禀报。”
乔梦容问：“可是书卷之事？”
白梅若以为仪王已将昨日之事告知仪王妃，立刻插嘴道：“王妃已知昨日之事？”
乔梦容疑惑：“昨日之事？发生了什么？”
对方还不知，白梅若后悔主动问出，又不敢言语，尴尬地低下头。
苗知瑜瞧出白梅若的小心思，林觉帮王爷破坏王妃的计划，她担心被王妃批评，一直不敢主动说出昨日之事。她苗知瑜可不怕，再说只有讲出来，仪王妃不仅不会怪罪她们办事不力，还会将一切罪过归咎到幽栖居士身上，恐怕她在劫难逃，也好帮自己出口气。
苗知瑜主动说道：“诗会后，我与白夫人尽心竭力将诗会的内容整理在册，又添油加醋一番，找到书局刊印成书卷，又花钱找人在街上免费发放。此事不知怎么被仪王殿下发现，他派人将所有书卷收回，还抓了书局的管事。林统制也参与其中，白夫人最清楚不过。”
苗知瑜提到她，白梅若必须开口：“我家夫君只是个统制，官职不高，仪王的话不得不听。此事已被殿下破坏，毁掉江南女词人美名的计划恐怕得泡汤。”
计划会不会泡汤，已不是乔梦容关注的焦点，关键是哥哥竟然参与此事。他为何要这么做？
既然书局的管事被抓，哥哥应该已知背后操纵之人是她乔梦容，为何昨晚回来只字不提？还对她那么好？
哥哥若不爱她，又为何与她圆房？若爱她，为何要帮朱淑真？难道他的心里还装着女词人？
眼泪已在乔梦容的眼中打转，哥哥人已属于她，可心并未全在她的身上，想让哥哥心中只有她，必须让另一个人消失。
仪王妃眼眶变红，沉默不语。两位夫人更是紧张不已，担心贵人怪罪她们。
还是苗知瑜最有办法：“请王妃明示，下一步该怎么做？”
乔梦容略微平复心情，忍住即将掉下的泪水：“感谢二位夫人如实相告，计划失败与二位无关。哥哥既然把书局的管事抓了，必然已从他口中得知与此事相关之人。他昨晚回幽林别居只字未提，应该是看在我的面子上并不想将此事闹大。二位夫人也已暴露，还是收敛些好，你们先回去好生候着，若需要，我会差人上门去请。”
二位夫人再笨，也能听得出，仪王妃不想与她们继续聊下去，两人很知趣，起身行礼离开。
走出正院，白梅若才大胆问：“苗夫人，仪王妃到底什么意思？”
苗知瑜对白梅若刚才自保的举动甚是不满：“难道白夫人听不出？仪王妃是让我们暂时不要来打扰她。不打扰也好，省得白夫人为自保一言不发。”
被对方看出，白梅若一脸尴尬：“苗夫人别跟我计较，毕竟我家那位帮着破坏了王妃的计划，我担心贵人怪罪。没想到仪王妃通情达理，这下我就放心了。”
The moment the three scholars walked out, Bai Feng winced for his master's wallet. "Why are *you* footing the bill for Young Master Dongfang's salon?"

苗知瑜懒得再理会白梅若，继续往出走。
"Promised him before Bianliang—can't go back on my word."

二人来到幽林别居门口，白梅若没有上自己的马车，而是跟着进了苗知瑜的马车。
"Out of all things to promise, a money pit with zero return? Total loss!"

苗知瑜没好气地问：“为何上我的马车？”
"Some things matter more than silver."

白梅若坐好后，厚着脸皮说：“想和苗夫人多聊会儿，进了城我再下来不迟。”
"Easy for a rich prince to say! To me, silver's king."

苗知瑜懒得与她说话，装作闭目养神的样子。
"Oh? What's worth more to you—Kuqiao or silver?"

白梅若主动说：“我这人笨，还得苗夫人帮我分析分析仪王妃的意思。”
Stumped for three seconds, Bai Feng declared, "Equal!" Then, remembering Nan Shuang's and Xilan's warnings, he probed cautiously, "Your Highness... if I marry Kuqiao, will the Princess Consort object?"

苗知瑜继续闭着眼：“刚不都给你说过，怎么还问？”
"Why would she?" Shen Jianchen didn't look up from his dossier.

“你说仪王妃以后是不是不会再找幽栖居士麻烦？”
"Do I need the Princess Consort's permission?"

“不好说。”
"You're my guard—my permission is all you need."

“为何？”
"If I marry her, could we live outside Youlin Retreat? Buy our own little house so Kuqiao stays comfortable—without slacking on guarding you, of course!"

苗知瑜睁开双眼：“不找，像吃了只苍蝇，恶心着呢；找吧，又担心仪王插手。可仪王插手，王妃更会找幽栖居士麻烦，所以幽栖居士还是没好日子过。”
"I'll buy the house for you," Shen Jianchen said smoothly.

白梅若十分不解：“那为何不让我们两个来幽林别居？没有我们两个，谁帮仪王妃？”
"Thank you, Your Highness!" *Problem solved!* Bai Feng beamed—working for Prince Yi really paid off.

“你啊，别把自己想得太重要。她是贵人，找个人帮忙还不是轻轻松松的事儿？再说我们两个已暴露，稍有举动，仪王必然会猜到王妃的身上，我们还是老老实实在家待着。”
Back at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong woke to find Brother already gone without a sound. Bliss mixed with a hollow ache—how she wished his face were the first thing she saw every dawn. Back when he was sick, he couldn't eat, walk, or dress without her; she held every thread of his days. Now, healthy and strong, he slipped right past her grasp, and after tasting him last night, her craving to hold him tight only grew fiercer—terrified that pushing too hard would drive him away.

白梅若终于明白：“还是苗夫人聪慧，刚才的事就别跟我计较。”
Rolling onto his warm pillow, she breathed in his scent as last night's memories washed over her—his gentle murmurs rippling through her like breeze across a quiet lake, then his fierce, surging passion crashing over her like waves on rock...

“我若计较呢？”
Xilan hooked back the bed curtains. "Time to rise, Your Highness."

“算我错了，不如我请客，把你的闺中密友都叫上，一起去永泰茶楼。”
Lingering one last second to brush her fingertips over his pillow, Mengrong sat up. Smiling knowingly at her mistress's languid glow, Xilan helped her dress. "His Highness had the kitchen simmer nourishing porridge specially for you before he left—waiting hot on the table."

“还是算了，永泰茶楼现在生意不好，字画好久没更换过，听说陈家马上接手。先给你记在账上，等陈家接手后步入正轨我们再去不迟。”
That perked Mengrong right up. Washing, dressing, and savoring the lightly herbal, sweet porridge, she felt too restless to sit around an empty house. "Xilan, let's go visit the Judicial Office."

“好，就这么说定了。”
Just as she stood, a maid announced Madam Miao and Madam Bai. Irritated at the interruption yet curious about the pamphlets, Mengrong sat back down. "Show them in."

两位夫人走后，乔梦容继续伤心，已没有心情去司理院。她流了一会儿泪，吩咐喜兰将邹嬷嬷找来。
Both women curtsied and sat—Bai Meiruo promptly sealing her lips shut, leaving Miao Zhiyu to carry the ball.

邹嬷嬷来后，见仪王妃眼圈红红的，猜到与仪王有关，坐下说道：“王爷昨晚回来陪伴过王妃，为何你看上去仍旧不高兴？”
"We came to report something urgent, Your Highness."

“哥哥虽与我圆房，可他心里还装着女词人，我又如何能高兴？”
"The pamphlets?"

“王妃会不会要求得太多？”
"Wait—Your Highness already knows what happened yesterday?" Bai Meiruo blurted, immediately wishing she could swallow her tongue when Mengrong blinked in confusion.

“我只要求哥哥心中只有我一人，全心全意待我，难道多吗？”
*Coward!* Miao Zhiyu sneered silently, seizing control cleanly. "After the salon, Madam Bai and I compiled the critiques, spiced them up, printed them, and paid folks to hand them out. Somehow Prince Yi caught wind of it, confiscated every copy, and arrested the print manager—Commander Lin helped round them up, as Madam Bai knows best."

邹嬷嬷点点头：“是多了些，对男子可要求这，要求那，就是不能要求他心中只有你一人，何况还是王爷？王妃仔细想想，官家那些个成年的皇子哪个身边只有一个女子？”
Dragged into the spotlight, Bai Meiruo stammered, "My husband's just a garrison commander—couldn't refuse Prince Yi's order! Looks like ruining her reputation hit a snag."

“哥哥身边有其他女子我都能忍，唯独无法忍受他的心里还装着朱淑真。”
The ruined scheme didn't even register with Mengrong—*Brother* had intervened! If he arrested the print manager, he *knew* his own wife pulled the strings—so why hadn't he breathed a word last night while holding her so sweetly?! Did he still love Zhu Shuzhen?! Tears burned behind her eyes. She held his body, not his whole heart—and the only way to claim his heart was wiping that other woman off the map.

仪王妃说这话时面露愤恨之色，邹嬷嬷便知仪王妃眼中根本容不下这位女词人。
Seeing the Princess Consort's reddening eyes and icy silence, both visitors held their breath until Miao Zhiyu ventured, "What are your next orders, Your Highness?"

“该怎么做？仪王妃请说。”
Forcing her tears back down, Mengrong said evenly, "Thank you both for telling me straight. This failure isn't on you. Since Brother questioned the print manager, he already knows who was involved; staying silent last night means he doesn't want a scandal on my account. Since you two are exposed, lie low for now—wait at home until I send word."

“诗会后我让苗夫人和白夫人去做的事已失败，最可恨的，是因哥哥而失败。辛苦嬷嬷告诉陈家大公子，该他出场，让他帮忙与蒋爷搭上关系，我要借蒋爷之手除掉朱淑真。”
Taking the clear dismissal, both women curtsied and retreated.

邹嬷嬷完全理解仪王妃，毫不犹豫地答应：“我这就去找陈家大公子。”
Outside the main courtyard, Bai Meiruo whispered, "What did she mean by that?"

邹嬷嬷乘坐马车，来到西湖边的永泰茶楼。进入后发现里面生意惨淡，伙计都懒得招呼她。
"She means stay out of sight—just as well, since someone went mute to save her own skin!" Miao Zhiyu snapped.

邹嬷嬷径直走上二楼，来到一个包间外，只见门上有句诗“一览众山小”。邹嬷嬷虽识字，可对诗词没有研究，也不感兴趣，示意身边的丫鬟将门推开。
"Don't hold it against me—my husband helped wreck her plot! Good thing Her Highness is reasonable." Climbing uninvited right into Miao Zhiyu's carriage at the gate, Bai Meiruo pushed on despite Miao Zhiyu's closed eyes. "Think Her Highness will drop going after Youqi Jushi now?"

丫鬟乖乖上前推开门，一股茶香扑面而来，也传来一阵琴声。
"Hard to say." Miao Zhiyu cracked her eyes open. "Dropping it feels like swallowing a fly; moving again risks Prince Yi stepping in—and the more he steps in, the harder Her Highness will go after her. Either way, Youqi Jushi's in for rough weather."

邹嬷嬷走进后，瞧见陈浩东坐在桌前品茶，女茶师在茶桌旁做茶，男琴师则专注地抚琴。
"Then why bench us? Who else will help her?"

“陈大公子好雅兴！”
"Don't flatter yourself—she's royalty, she can snap her fingers for new hands anytime! We're burned; stay home and keep quiet."

陈浩东见邹嬷嬷进来，赶紧起身行礼：“嬷嬷大驾光临，有失远迎。”
"Smart as ever, Madam Miao! Let me treat you and your girlfriends to tea at Yongtai Teahouse to make up for today!"

邹嬷嬷毫不客气，坐在为她准备的桌旁：“大公子不必客气，你我坐下聊。”
"Pass—their art's stale and business is dead. Put it on my tab until the Chen family finishes taking over and freshens it up."

陈浩东继续坐下：“嬷嬷别急，先听听曲儿，品尝一下女茶师做的茶，再聊正事不迟。”
"Deal!"

要与陈浩东谈的事毕竟见不得光，有他人在场十分不便，不如等等。邹嬷嬷点点头，表示同意。她对乐曲一窍不通，也不感兴趣，倒是女茶师做茶的动作饶有趣味，她定睛瞧着……
Back inside, Mengrong wept briefly before summoning Matron Zou.

女茶师终于做完茶，最有趣的是，茶做完，乐曲刚好演奏完。女茶师示意身旁的丫鬟将茶盏为邹嬷嬷端到桌前摆好，才与琴师一起行礼离开，将门从外面关好。
"His Highness spent last night with you—why the red eyes?" Matron Zou asked gently.

邹嬷嬷瞧着茶盏中的画作，疑惑道：“茶师的手艺不错，为何茶楼的生意如此惨淡？”
"He bedded me, yet his heart still clings to that poetess! How can I be happy?"

陈浩东回道：“陈家正接手永泰茶楼，七日后这里会恢复如初。”
"Aren't you asking too much, Your Highness? Demand anything from a man—just never demand sole ownership of his heart, especially an imperial prince! Name one grown son of His Majesty who only has one woman."

“原来如此，难怪陈大公子约我来此。”邹嬷嬷端起茶盏品了一口，真是沁人心脾。
"I could stomach ten other women beside Brother—anyone except Zhu Shuzhen!" Venom hardened her face. "The pamphlet plan failed because *Brother* crushed it! Go tell Eldest Young Master Chen it's his turn—have him link us with Master Jiang to get rid of Zhu Shuzhen for good."

陈浩东笑道：“不是因陈家要接手永泰茶楼我才约嬷嬷来这里，以前我也常来，这个包间我常年包着，用来洽谈生意或是与朋友相聚。”
Unable to refuse her, Matron Zou rose at once. "I'll go find him right now."

邹嬷嬷放下茶盏：“你我闲话少叙，还是说正事。”
Matron Zou's carriage pulled up at Yongtai Teahouse by West Lake—so empty the waiter didn't even bother greeting her. Climbing straight to the second floor, she paused outside a booth marked *"Dwarfing All Other Peaks"* and nodded for her maid to push the door open.

“嬷嬷请讲。”
Tea aroma and guqin music drifted out. Inside sat Chen Haodong sipping tea while the female tea master whisked fresh bowls and a male musician strummed softly.

“王妃说该你出手。”
"How refined, Eldest Young Master Chen!"

“我早已安排好，只等王妃一声令下。”
Haodong rose bowing. "Forgive me for failing to greet you downstairs, Matron."

邹嬷嬷很是疑惑，她还未说如何做，对方竟然已准备好。
"No ceremony—let's sit." Agreeing to wait out the music before discussing dirty business, Matron Zou watched the tea master's crisp, fluid motions until the last note of the guqin faded right as the finished bowl landed on her table. Once the two performers bowed out and shut the door, Matron Zou admired the foam painting and took a fragrant sip. "Fine skill—why is this place so empty?"

“不知陈大公子如何安排？”
"The Chen family is taking over Yongtai Teahouse—in seven days it'll be booming again," Haodong chuckled. "Not that I picked it for that; I've kept this booth reserved year-round for private deals."

“上次嬷嬷说先给她制造些麻烦，我早已安排好，只等贵人的指令。”
Setting her cup down, Matron Zou came straight to the point. "The Princess Consort says it's your move."

邹嬷嬷摇摇头：“制造麻烦还不够，仪王妃想让她消失。”
"Already set up—just waiting for her word."

“消失？”陈浩东没想到仪王妃如此心狠，“上次嬷嬷说需徐徐图之，怎么又变了？”
"Wait—what did you set up?"

“仪王妃已等不及，眼中钉肉中刺不拔掉，她不舒服。需要陈大公子帮着找一人。”
"Two headaches for her, just like you asked last time."

“谁？”
Matron Zou shook her head. "Headaches aren't enough anymore. Her Highness wants her gone."

“蒋爷。”
"Gone?" Haodong blinked at the sudden ruthlessness. "Thought we were taking it slow?"

“我与此人打过交道，也有些交情，找他做此事再好不过。只是这位蒋爷认钱不认人。”
"Her Highness is out of patience. She needs you to hire one man: Master Jiang."

“只要认钱就好，银子不成问题。”
"I've dealt with him—we're on decent terms. Only Master Jiang answers to silver, nothing else."

邹嬷嬷向丫鬟示意，只见丫鬟拿出一个木盒，走上前放在陈浩东的桌上。
"Silver is no problem." At Matron Zou's nod, her maid placed a wooden box packed thick with banknotes onto Haodong's table.

陈浩东打开，里面是厚厚一沓银票。将盖子盖好后，说道：“嬷嬷转告仪王妃，此事交给我尽管放心。只是我费了些心思准备好两个麻烦事儿，总得让我尽兴一回。”
Peeking inside and snapping the lid shut, Haodong smiled. "Tell Her Highness to rest easy. Still, since I put real effort into those two little headaches, let me enjoy watching them play out first."

邹嬷嬷笑道：“你自行安排，只要不影响仪王妃关心的大事即可。”
"Suit yourself, so long as nothing delays Her Highness's main business."

“绝不会影响，反而锦上添花。”
"Quite the opposite—it'll be icing on the cake."

两人商议好后，陈浩东离开茶楼去忙制造麻烦之事，还需去钱塘帮走一遭。
Parting ways, Chen Haodong strode off to light his two fuses—and pay a call on the Qiantang Gang.
