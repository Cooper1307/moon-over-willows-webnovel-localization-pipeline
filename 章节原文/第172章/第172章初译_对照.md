第172章 望月轩外遇歹徒
## Chapter 172: Thugs Outside Wangyue Pavilion

苦荞回到望月轩，将遇到陈家老爷和夫人之事详细说明。
千里寻听后，甚是喜悦：“看来去陈家闹这步棋走得实在好，说不定今日退婚之事便成，等成功后，再去找我爹，想办法让他答应以后不要再随便给我定亲。”
要搁以前，苦荞肯定会劝小姐打消念头。而现在的小姐要胆量有胆量，要气魄有气魄，只要她想做的事，再难都会坚持。
“我相信小姐肯定能说服老爷。”
又转念一想，小姐打算以后一个人过，实在可怜：“不过，小姐还是要找一个互相喜欢，又能托付终身的人，我才放心。”
千里寻陷入沉思，文渊算是一个，可他今日已成亲，一段好好的缘分就这样没了，以后还有谁会是与她互相喜欢之人？
不知为何，她脑海中竟然出现仪王的身影。千里寻晃晃脑袋，想要将仪王的身影晃走。
﻿刚晃走，又出现。
苦荞见小姐不言语，只顾沉默，便去茶桌旁做茶。
千里寻认为一定是文渊成亲，她内心变得空虚，想找个人填补，才不由自主想到仪王。
自从知道仪王是申简辰后，千里寻对仪王的态度正悄悄发生变化，也许是因为她刚刚穿越而来时，心中只有申简辰的缘故。
想到梦容，觉得自己挂念与好友有婚约之人，实在不该。
她还是没忍住，在心中悄悄自问，若没有梦容，是否愿意接受仪王的感情？她强迫自己说出：“也许吧。”只怕不强迫答案会是肯定的。
穿越前，面对申简辰的表白，千里寻总是一副拒绝的姿态，甚至说出“除非猪上树，老娘才会嫁给你”的话。
穿越而来，她与申简辰想要走到一起，还真得等到猪能上树的那一日。仔细想来，人说话果然需三思而行，否则一不小心曾经说过的话便会成为谶语。
千里寻不由自主琢磨起仪王想要纳她为妾的想法。按说在这个时代，像朱淑真这等出身嫁给一位王爷做妾也不为过。可千里寻难以跨越心中的坎儿。
一方面，她不愿给人做妾。与其他女子共享一夫，每日为一个男人勾心斗角，想着都累，还有失尊严。
另一方面，她不想伤害梦容。梦容对仪王的爱深入骨髓，真是我见犹怜。千里寻甚至觉得她对仪王稍动心思都龌龊至极。
为了不再想下去，她坐回书桌前，提笔准备写话本。却发现心思越乱越难以编故事，话本是无法再写，朱淑真的诗却无意中蹦入脑海。
她微微一笑，心想，诗不愧是情感的集中体现，她心中有情想要宣泄，朱淑真的诗自然而然被召唤出来。
便提笔写下五首《恨春》:
其一
樱桃初荐杏梅酸，槐嫩风高麦秀寒。
惆怅东君太情薄，挽留时暂也应难。
其二
一瞬芳菲尔许时，苦无佳句记相思。
春光虽好多风雨，恩爱方深奈别离。
泪眼谢他花缴抱，愁怀惟赖洒扶持。
莺莺燕燕休相笑，试与单栖各自知。
其三
病酒厌厌日正方，一声啼鸟在花梢。
惊回好梦方萌蕊，唤起新愁却破包。
暗把后期随处记，闲将清恨倩诗嘲。
从今始信恩成怨，且与莺花作交谈。
其四
迟迟花日上帘钩，尽日无人独倚楼。
蝶使蜂媒传客恨，莺丝柳线织春愁。
碧云信断惟芳梦，红叶成诗想到秋。
几许别离多少泪，不堪重省不堪流。
其五
一篆烟消系臂香，闲看书册就牙床。
莺声冉冉来深院，柳色阴阴暗画墙。
眼底落红千万点，脸边新泪两三行。
梨花细雨黄昏后，不是愁人也断肠。
写诗期间，苦荞将做好的茶端来，见小姐写得认真，并未打扰，把茶盏轻轻放在桌上，站在一旁看小姐写诗。
小姐一句一句，一首一首写，苦荞心中暗自赞叹，重生后的小姐写诗作词并不比以前差，竟然一下子写了五首，实在厉害。
千里寻写完，内心郁结之情得到疏散，放下笔，端起茶盏，温度刚刚好，一口气喝完。
她放下茶盏，向四周瞧瞧：“红豆呢？”
“小姐您忘了？是您让红豆每日训练剩下的九个下人练习拳法，她此时正在后院。”
“瞧我这记性。”
门外传来小厮的声音：“姑娘，东方公子身边的化雨又来了。”
千里寻笑道：“化雨倒是来得及时，把这五首全部给他。”
苦荞很不情愿：“小姐辛苦写的，为何要全给他？依我看两首即可。”
“诗写出来要让人又读又品，才有意义，放在我身边只怕会发霉。”
小姐说得有道理，苦荞将五首全拿了，走出闺房，来到大门口。
化雨正乖乖站在门口向里张望，见苦荞出现，满脸堆笑：“幽栖居士和苦荞姐姐都是好人，从来不会让我久等。”
苦荞递上纸张：“你倒及时，每隔几日就来。今日乔家举行婚礼，怎么没跟你家公子一起去？”
化雨笑嘻嘻地从苦荞手中接过写有诗的纸张：“公子本要带我去，想起今日是该来望月轩讨要诗词的日子，便没让我跟着去。”
“你岂不是错过一次享用美食的机会？”
“没什么，我家公子说，只要我拿回幽栖居士的佳作，带我去聚香楼。”化雨只觉手中的纸张厚了些，仔细数数，竟然有五张纸：“没想到今日收获满满，幽栖居士送给我五首诗，公子肯定高兴，聚香楼是去定了。”
化雨离开后，苦荞向白风每次见她的地方瞟了一眼，心想，白风今日应该跟随仪王去乔府贺喜，故而没来找她。
正要准备回屋，听到车轮的声音，仔细瞧去，原来是陈家的马车。马车停下后，陈浩宇一个人从马车上下来，身边并未跟任何随从。
苦荞猜到他应该是来向小姐传达退婚的喜讯，便笑脸相迎。
陈浩宇走下马车，瞧见苦荞，正要喊，话还未出口，不知从哪里出现一群人，将陈浩宇团团围住……
眼前的阵仗将苦荞吓坏，她愣了一会儿，匆忙逃进望月轩，掩住大门，又因好奇，露出门缝向外瞧。
只见走来一个黑胖子，大腹便便，眯着眼睛，穿过众人，靠近陈浩宇。
苦荞自语道：“陈家公子平日不干好事，这下招灾了吧！有你罪受。”
她继续从门缝向外瞧……
当黑胖子靠近后，陈浩宇嬉皮笑脸相迎：“蔣爷，您怎么跑到这儿来？还带这么多人？”
蔣爷“哼”了一声：“旁边就是陈家的宅子，你小子常常在这一带出没，别以为我不知道？”
“蔣爷不愧是临安百事通，什么都知道。”
“没想到陈大公子也会说奉承话，既然态度这么好，今儿就答应我，不要再去找春金沫，大爷我瞧上她了。”
春金沫正是陈浩宇散播谣言之事被查出后，开荤的第一人，自此春金沫只认陈浩宇，而陈浩宇对春金沫也出手阔绰，处处照顾。
“春香楼女子多的是，蔣爷又何必跟我过不去？您要多少银子尽管说，我给就是。”
对方并没有相让之意，蔣爷冷笑道：“你们陈家有几个臭钱就了不起，今儿爷爷我就让你知道这天底下有钱解决不了的事儿，爷爷我偏要春金沫不要你的银子，看你能把我怎样？”
陈浩宇后悔为何今日出门不多带些人，对方来势汹汹，看来不打一架是不行。既然他还不想让出春金沫，不如打一架。
“蔣爷这边人多势众，我肯定把您怎样不了，不过我还不想把金沫随便让给别人。”
“给我打！打到他同意为止！”蔣爷一声令下，向后退去。
蒋爷的手下一起向陈浩宇攻击而来……
苦荞赶紧关好门：“活该！谁让你喜欢青楼女子！”
苦荞快跑着回到闺房：“小姐！不好了！外面打起来！”
几首诗疏散了情怀，内心安静许多，千里寻正提笔写话本，并未理会苦荞。
苦荞强调道：“小姐，外面打起来了！”
“打起来就打起来，与我们何干？”千里寻继续写话本。
“打的可是陈公子。”
“打他再好不过，他本就该打。”
千里寻写完一个字，突然停下笔：“陈浩宇来望月轩？”
苦荞点点头。
千里寻继续问：“他来会不会是找我说退婚的好消息？”
苦荞又点点头：“看样子是，不过还没说，就被一伙人围住，然后没说几句就打起来。”
千里寻撂下笔站起来：“看在他有可能给我带来好消息，不如帮帮他，你去后院，让红豆把人都给我带来，今日正好拿门外的人练练手。”
苦荞尽管讨厌陈浩宇，可小姐的嫂嫂人还是不错，看在她的面子上也应帮帮陈公子，她快步跑出闺房，跑到后院，唤了红豆，带上九名下人，来到院子与小姐会合。
千里寻已在院中听到外面的打斗声，命令小厮将门打开，然后与众人冲出门外……
陈浩宇一个人与众人对打，的确力不从心，为了面子，努力撑着。他会的三脚猫功夫，根本没用，打了没多会儿，撑不下去，被众人将手脚箍住，动弹不得。
蔣爷走过去，左右开弓在陈浩宇脸上扇了两巴掌：“爷爷我本来买了春金沫，结果你小子出的银子多，春香楼的妈妈便给了你，今日就让你知道，跟爷爷抢人，没好下场！”
陈浩宇并不服软：“此事只怪春香楼妈妈爱财，你有本事去打她，何必找我麻烦？”
“先收拾完你小子，我自然会收拾她。”
蔣爷话音刚落，听到身后的大门被打开，转身看去，从里面冲出一群人来，三女七男。接着走出一位漂亮姑娘。
蔣爷见到漂亮姑娘，顿时眉开眼笑：“哪里来这么俊俏的小娘子？”
抬头瞅到“望月轩”三个字：“小娘子难道是望月轩的主人？”
千里寻还未说话，陈浩宇像是见到救星：“朱淑真，快救救我，他们都是坏人！”
蔣爷听到“朱淑真”三个字，立刻恍然大悟：“哦，你就是那位江南女词人，长得真不赖……”
蔣爷边说边靠近：“听说你跟陈浩宇有婚约，他可不是什么好东西，整日往春香楼跑，不如舍了他，跟爷爷我，如何？嘿嘿……！”
千里寻哪会受这等奚落，再说对方色眯眯的样子实在恶心，她不由分说，一拳上去打在蔣爷的脸上：“让你见识见识老娘的厉害，以后跟我说话客气点！”
蔣爷的脸被打得火辣辣地疼，他咧着嘴，一脸怒气：“你们还愣着干什么？给我上！”
Back at Wangyue Pavilion, Kuqiao gave a full account of meeting Master and Madam Chen outside Zhu Manor.
Qianli Xun brightened at once. "Storming Chen Manor turned out to be a masterstroke! The betrothal might be dead by tonight. Once it is settled, I will go talk my father into swearing off arranging any more matches for me."
In the old days, Kuqiao would have urged her mistress to drop such wild notions. Yet her mistress now had nerve and grit to spare—once she set her mind on something, no obstacle could shake her. "I believe Miss can talk Master around," Kuqiao said, then added with a soft frown, "Still, I would feel much easier if Miss found someone who loves you back and whom you can rely on for life."
Qianli Xun drifted into thought. Wenyuan had been such a man, yet today was his wedding day—a good fate cut clean in half. Who else could ever share mutual love with her?
Out of nowhere, Prince Yi's image surfaced in her mind. She shook her head hard to fling it away—and the instant she stopped, he popped right back.
Seeing her mistress fall silent, Kuqiao stepped over to the tea table to whisk tea.
It must just be Wenyuan's wedding leaving an empty space inside her, Qianli Xun told herself, making her mind reach for someone to fill it. Ever since learning Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen, her attitude toward him had been shifting inch by inch—probably because when she had first transmigrated here, Shen Jianchen had been the only man in her thoughts.
Then Mengrong crossed her mind, and shame stung her. Harboring thoughts for a man betrothed to her closest friend was inexcusable. Still, she could not stop a quiet question from rising inside: without Mengrong in the picture, would she have accepted Prince Yi's love? She forced herself to answer, "Maybe"—afraid that without forcing it, the honest word would be "Yes."
Before transmigrating, she had brushed off Shen Jianchen's confessions every time, even declaring, "When pigs can climb trees, maybe I will marry you!" Now in this world, for the two of them to end up together really would take a pig climbing a tree. Truly, a person needed to watch her words before careless boasts turned into prophecy.
Her thoughts drifted to Prince Yi's wish to take her as concubine. By the standards of this era, a woman of Zhu Shuzhen's standing becoming a prince's concubine was no disgrace—yet Qianli Xun could never cross that line. First, she refused to share a husband and spend her days scheming against other women for one man's favor; exhausting and degrading both. Second, she could never break Mengrong's heart. Mengrong's devotion to Prince Yi ran bone-deep, touching anyone who witnessed it; even entertaining a stray thought about him felt sleazy to Qianli Xun.
To clear her head, she sat at her desk and picked up her brush to work on her huaben. Yet the messier her heart grew, the harder plotting a story became. No fiction would come—instead, Zhu Shuzhen's own poems rose unbidden in her mind.
A faint smile touched her lips. Poetry truly was the vessel of feeling; with emotion pressing for release, Zhu Shuzhen's verses answered the call on their own. Dipping her brush, she wrote out the five poems of *"Resenting Spring"* (*Hen Chun*):
One:
Early cherries are offered, apricots and plums still sour;
Tender locust leaves meet high winds, heading wheat feels the chill.
How melancholy that the Lord of Spring is so fickle of heart—
Even begging him to linger a moment proves hard indeed.
Two:
One fleeting flash of bloom, and all this time slips by,
Tormented that no fine lines capture my longing.
Bright as spring light may be, wind and rain come thick;
Just as deep love takes root, parting tears us apart.
With tear-filled eyes I thank the blossoms pressing close;
My heavy heart leans solely upon wine for support.
Orioles and swallows, do not laugh in pairs—
Try nesting all alone, and each of you will know.
Three:
Listless from last night's wine as the noon sun stands high,
A single bird cries out from the flowering bough.
Startled awake from sweet dreams just budding into bloom,
Fresh sorrow stirs and bursts open from the sheath.
In secret I mark future trysts wherever I turn;
At leisure I borrow verse to mock my pure regret.
From this day on I learn how kindness turns to grief,
And hold my quiet converse with the orioles and flowers.
Four:
Slowly the blossom-dappled sun climbs the curtain hook;
All day alone with no one near, I lean upon the tower rail.
Butterfly envoys and bee go-betweens carry a traveler's ache;
Oriole songs and willow threads weave spring's distress.
No word arrives from azure clouds—only fragrant dreams remain;
Red leaves inscribed with verse already call up autumn's thoughts.
How many partings, how many tears—
Unbearable to recall, unbearable to shed.
Five:
A coil of incense fades beside the scented sleeve sachet;
At leisure I read from scrolls upon my ivory-inlaid couch.
Oriole notes drift softly into the deep courtyard;
Willow shadows darken the painted garden wall.
Before my eyes, ten million petals of fallen red;
Along my cheeks, two or three fresh trails of tears.
Pear blossoms in fine drizzle after dusk—
Even one who knows no sorrow would feel their heart break.
[TN: Translator's rendering]
While she wrote, Kuqiao brought over the whisked tea. Seeing her mistress absorbed, she set the bowl down without a sound and watched from the side. Line by line, poem by poem, Kuqiao marveled inwardly: since her "rebirth," Miss's poetry lost nothing to her old genius—five whole poems in a single sitting!
Finishing the last character loosened the knot in Qianli Xun's chest. Setting down her brush, she lifted the bowl—just the right warmth—and drained it in one go. Glancing around, she asked, "Where is Hongdou?"
"Did Miss forget? You told Hongdou to drill the other nine servants in fist techniques every day. She is in the back courtyard."
"Look at my memory."
A page boy called from outside: "Miss, Huayu from Young Master Dongfang's manor is here again."
Qianli Xun smiled. "Timely as ever. Give him all five poems."
Kuqiao balked. "Miss worked so hard writing those—why hand over all five? Two ought to be plenty!"
"Poems exist to be read and savored. Lock them up in my room and they will just gather mold."
Conceding the point, Kuqiao gathered all five sheets and walked out to the gate.
Peering inside with an eager grin, Huayu beamed the moment she appeared. "Youqi Jushi and Sister Kuqiao are the kindest souls alive—never keeping me waiting!"
Handing over the sheets, Kuqiao teased, "You sure keep track of the calendar, showing up every few days like clockwork. Today is the Qiao wedding—why did you not go with your master?"
Accepting the papers with a grin, Huayu replied, "My master meant to bring me, then remembered today was the day to call at Wangyue Pavilion for verses, so he sent me here instead."
"Did you not miss out on a grand feast?"
"No sweat! My master promised that if I bring back fresh masterpieces from Youqi Jushi, he will treat me at Juxiang Tower!" Feeling the stack thicker than usual, Huayu counted the pages—five full sheets! "What a haul today! Five whole poems! My master will be over the moon—Juxiang Tower is in the bag!"
After Huayu skipped off, Kuqiao glanced down the lane toward the spot where Bai Feng usually waited. Today he must be attending Prince Yi at the Qiao wedding, so he had not come.
Just as she turned to go back inside, carriage wheels rumbled up—a Chen family carriage! It rolled to a stop, and Chen Haoyu climbed out all by himself without a single attendant.
Assuming he had come bearing good news about breaking off the engagement, Kuqiao stepped forward with a bright smile.
Chen Haoyu spotted her and opened his mouth to call out—when out of nowhere, a gang of rough men surged from the shadows and surrounded him tight.
Frozen in fright, Kuqiao bolted back inside Wangyue Pavilion, slammed the gate shut, and—unable to resist peeking—cracked it open an inch to watch.
Through the gap, she saw a swarthy, pot-bellied man with narrow squinting eyes shoulder through the thugs toward Chen Haoyu. "Serves Young Master Chen right for never doing any good!" Kuqiao muttered to herself. "Trouble caught up with you now!" She kept peeking.
As the burly man closed in, Chen Haoyu plastered on a cheeky grin. "Master Jiang! What brings you out here with such a big crew?"
Master Jiang gave a cold snort. "Your Chen estate sits right next door, and you haunt this neighborhood all the time. Think I did not know?"
"Master Jiang truly knows everything that moves in Lin'an!"
"Never figured Eldest Young Master Chen knew how to butter folks up! Seeing your polite attitude, give me your word right now: stay away from Chun Jinmo. This boss has his eye on her."
Chun Jinmo had been the very first courtesan Chen Haoyu had broken his celibacy with after the rumor scandal broke; since then, she received only him, and Chen Haoyu showered her with silver and favors in return.
"Chunxiang Brothel is overflowing with beauties—why pick a fight with me, Master Jiang? Name whatever sum of silver you want and it is yours."
Unmoved, Master Jiang sneered. "Think a pile of stinking Chen silver makes you king? Today your granddaddy will teach you there are things in this world money cannot buy! I want Chun Jinmo, not your silver—what are you going to do about it?"
Regretting he had left home without bodyguards, Chen Haoyu sized up the mob. No getting out without a scrap—and he was not handing Chun Jinmo over either. "With your numbers, Master Jiang, I cannot stop you—yet I am not giving Jinmo up on a platter."
"Beat him! Beat him till he says yes!" Master Jiang barked, stepping back as his thugs piled onto Chen Haoyu.
Kuqiao slammed the gate shut. "Serves him right for chasing brothel girls!" She sprinted back to her mistress's chamber. "Miss! Trouble! A brawl broke out outside!"
With her heart cleared by the poems, Qianli Xun was back at her huaben and did not look up.
"Miss, there is a fight right outside our gate!"
"Let them fight. What is it to us?" Qianli Xun kept writing.
"The one getting beaten is Young Master Chen!"
"Even better. He has had a beating coming for ages." Finishing a character, she froze mid-stroke. "Chen Haoyu came to Wangyue Pavilion?"
Kuqiao nodded.
"Could he have come to tell me good news about calling off the wedding?"
Kuqiao nodded again. "Looked like it—only before he got a word out, a gang boxed him in, traded two lines, and jumped him!"
Tossing her brush aside, Qianli Xun sprang up. "If he might be bringing good news, I suppose I should bail him out. Run to the back courtyard and tell Hongdou to bring everyone—let us test our training on the thugs outside!"
Much as Kuqiao loathed Chen Haoyu, Miss's sister-in-law was a sweetheart; on Young Madam's account alone, he deserved a hand. She dashed to the back courtyard, rounded up Hongdou and the nine trained servants, and met her mistress in the front yard.
Hearing the scuffle beyond the wall, Qianli Xun ordered the gate swung open and led her crew charging out.
Alone against a mob, Chen Haoyu had tried holding his own for face, yet his shoddy kung fu lasted all of ten breaths before thugs pinned his arms and legs tight.
Striding up, Master Jiang slapped him hard across both cheeks. "I already paid for Chun Jinmo, and you outbid me so the madam handed her over! Today you learn what happens to fools who steal from me!"
Unbowed, Chen Haoyu spat back, "Blame the brothel madam for chasing silver! Go beat her if you have the guts—why jump me?"
"Once I finish tuning you up, she is next!"
Hardly had the words left his mouth when the gate behind him banged open. Spinning around, Master Jiang saw ten people—three women and seven men—charge out, followed by a striking young lady.
Master Jiang's eyes lit up into a greasy grin. "Where did a pretty little miss like you pop out from?" Glancing up at the plaque reading *Wangyue Pavilion*, he chuckled. "Are you the mistress of Wangyue Pavilion?"
Before Qianli Xun could speak, Chen Haoyu hollered as if spotting a savior, "Zhu Shuzhen! Save me! These guys are thugs!"
At the name "Zhu Shuzhen," a bulb lit up in Master Jiang's head. "Aha! So you are that famous Jiangnan poetess! Pretty as a picture too..." Sidling closer, he leered. "Word is you are engaged to Chen Haoyu. He is no prize—haunting Chunxiang Brothel day and night! Why not dump him and come with me instead? Heheh!"
Qianli Xun had zero tolerance for sleazy remarks, and his greasy smirk turned her stomach. Without a word of warning, she drove a fist straight into Master Jiang's face. "Get a taste of my fist—and learn to keep your mouth clean when you talk to me!"
Clapping a hand to his burning cheek, Master Jiang bared his teeth in fury. "What are you idiots standing around for?! Get them!"
