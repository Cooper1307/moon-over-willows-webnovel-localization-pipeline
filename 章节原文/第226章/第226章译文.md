## Chapter 226: Hongdou Delivers Dinner to Boya Study

By dusk, Qinggua's voice had shrunk to a hoarse croak, yet he dared not stop whispering, "Zhu Shuzhen, I'm divorcing you."

Then a loud snore drifted from the inner bedroom. Qinggua lit up. "Young Master's asleep! Break time—my throat's dry as parchment. Need tea."

Padding to the wooden table, he poured a cup and tested it with his lips. "Cold already, and nobody brought a fresh pot? Oh well, thirsty enough to drink anything." Gulping it down, he glanced at the dim cracks between the boarded shutters. "Dark already and my stomach's growling. Where's dinner?"

Right then the padlock rattled outside. Qinggua darted to the door as it swung open and Hongdou walked in carrying a food carrier in one hand and a steaming teapot in the other.

"Hongdou! You're here!" Taking the teapot to set on the table, Qinggua eyed the open doorway thinking of bolting—just as the door clicked shut behind her.

Hongdou set down the food carrier and lifted out two plates of plain vegetables and two bowls of rice. "Miss sent dinner. Where's Young Master? Call him out to eat."

Woken by the clack of the padlock, Chen Haoyu strolled into the front room and glared at Hongdou. "The second I get out, I'm selling you off to pay you back for those two slaps!"

Having already swung on him, Hongdou had zero fear left with her mistress backing her. "Miss holds my deed of sale, Young Master. Nothing you can do to me."

Snapped at by a maid, Chen Haoyu bristled. "Go tell Zhu Shuzhen I'm divorcing her!"

"You had Qinggua shouting it all afternoon—every soul in Moxiang Courtyard heard, Miss included. No need for me to repeat it! Miss says whatever you want, she'll do the exact opposite. Divorcing her? Dream on!"

Chen Haoyu cocked a fist toward Hongdou—and Qinggua jumped right between them. "Don't take it out on Hongdou, Young Master! We depend on her to bring us food!"

Seeing his own servant jump in front of a maid made Chen Haoyu snap. Forgetting Qinggua's training, he swung a boot out of pure reflex.

Qinggua glided right past it. "Quit kicking me, Young Master—doesn't work anymore! Only reason I didn't shove you flat is because I care about you; anyone else would've hit the floor!"

Chen Haoyu knew he could not beat Hongdou, and now Zhu Shuzhen had ruined even his lone punching bag. Planting his feet, he clenched both fists and hissed, "Zhu Shuzhen, this isn't over!"

Amused, Hongdou said, "Better eat up, Young Master. I'm waiting to take the bowls back."

Too hungry to waste more steam on a losing battle, Chen Haoyu dropped onto a stool—and scowled at the table. "Two measly dishes? I never eat less than five dishes and a soup! Tell Zhu Shuzhen to send more!"

"Miss says every time you make a demand, we cut the portion further."

"Fine, forget I spoke!" Swallowing his rage, he picked up his chopsticks—and froze. "All vegetables? Not even a scrap of minced pork?"

"Miss says while you're locked up, it's vegetarian only. No meat."

Too livid to waste another word, Chen Haoyu dug in.

Starving himself, Qinggua eyed the second bowl of rice set out for him, took a bold seat across the table, and reached for his chopsticks.

"Who gave you permission to sit at my table?" Chen Haoyu barked.

Qinggua set the chopsticks down, stood up, and swallowed hard staring at the rice. "I yelled my lungs out for you all afternoon, Young Master—if not merit, at least pity! My belly's howling; let me have a couple bites?"

"Let Qinggua eat at the table with you, Young Master," Hongdou interposed. "Miss's orders."

"Hear that, Young Master? Young Madam's orders!" Qinggua chimed in.

Chen Haoyu had almost softened at Qinggua's puppy-dog eyes, yet invoking Zhu Shuzhen hardened him right back. "You're my servant, you obey me—Zhu Shuzhen doesn't call the shots! Sit down with me and see if I don't skin you!"

Backing off, Qinggua stood hovering at his elbow, heart twisting with every chopstickful his master lifted, terrified nothing would be left.

At last Chen Haoyu finished—leaving half of each vegetable dish intact. The instant his master shot Hongdou a glare and stomped back into the bedroom, Qinggua dropped onto the stool and wolfed everything down.

Once full, he helped Hongdou stack the dishes and seized the moment. "Hongdou, what do you think of me?"

Setting the empty bowls into the food carrier, Hongdou replied, "You're all right."

"What does 'all right' mean?" Qinggua pressed.

Stacking the plates inside, she said, "Not great, not terrible."

Unsatisfied, Qinggua pushed on. "So... do you like someone who's not great and not terrible?"

With the question laid bare, Hongdou felt zero embarrassment since she had zero interest. "What's rolling around in that head of yours, Qinggua? Plenty of maids in Chen Manor—bound to be someone who likes you."

Handing her the cold teapot, Qinggua said, "I don't care about anyone else. I only care if you like me."

Lifting the carrier in one hand and the teapot in the other, Hongdou drawled, "Do I like you..."

Qinggua's face lit up.

"...or not like you..."

His heart dropped.

"...truth is, I don't know."

Disappointed yet undeterred, Qinggua trailed her to the door. "I'm a really good guy! Spend more time with me and you'll see—marry me and I'll spoil my wife rotten!"

Hongdou laughed. "Since you're such a catch, I'll spread the word around Moxiang Courtyard—and all of Chen Manor if need be. Guaranteed we find you a bride!"

Before Qinggua could blink, the door opened and clicked padlocked from outside.

Qinggua called through the wood, "Hongdou! Will you be my wife?!"

No reply came from outside—only a mocking voice right behind him. "Have you no pride? Hongdou just shot you down cold and you're still howling shameless nonsense through the door! Even I'm embarrassed for you!"

Qinggua spun around. "Can you stop creeping up behind me like a ghost, Young Master? Nearly stopped my heart!"

"Daring to complain at me now? Sick of living?" Bored stiff in lockup and realizing anger bought him nothing, Chen Haoyu dropped onto a chair. "Come sit and chat."

Flattered, Qinggua hopped onto a stool. "What do you want to talk about, Young Master?"

"Chasing Hongdou like that won't work in a million years."

"Teach me how, Young Master!"

"Know why girls always fall over themselves for me?"

Stumped, Qinggua ransacked his memory. "Young Master, I've never once seen a girl actually like you! All I ever saw was you grabbing village maidens to serve in your courtyard—that's where every maid in Moxiang Courtyard came from before, plus other Chen properties..."

Chen Haoyu raised a fist. "Keep blabbing and eat knuckles!"

Qinggua leaned clear. "Not blabbing—just facts!"

Chen Haoyu lowered his fist. "Fine, I acted wild back then, yet I haven't touched a village girl in over half a year. Aren't I allowed to improve?"

"True enough—since last year you switched to haunting Chunxiang Brothel. Guess brothel girls suit you better. Do you have a favorite there, Young Master?"

Chun Jinmo crossed Chen Haoyu's mind. "Sort of. Wait—we were talking about you! How'd it turn onto me?"

"You asked why girls like you!" Qinggua protested. "If you ask me, they only flock to you for your silver."

"Bingo! You nailed it!" Chen Haoyu snapped his fingers. "That's why you need silver. Get rich, and you won't have to chase Hongdou—she'll throw herself at you!"

Qinggua shook his head. "Wrong, Young Master! You're loaded, yet Young Madam doesn't want you—even locked you up! Anyone chasing silver isn't sincere. Even if I had silver, I'd pretend to be broke so I'd know Hongdou likes me for real."

Those words left a bitter ache in Chen Haoyu's chest. Maybe that was why—knowing every coerced village girl and every smiling courtesan bent only for his silver—he had never truly fallen for anyone. Bought affection held zero real joy; bored with life, he had merely numbed himself with money.

Seeing his master fall quiet, Qinggua winced. "Put my foot in it again? Mad at me, Young Master?"

Instead, Chen Haoyu answered calm and low. "Looks like you know more about the heart than I do. Forget my advice—chase Hongdou your own way. Still, take it from me: she's not into you. Don't waste your breath."

"Thanks for the warning, Young Master, yet I haven't even tried for real yet—today was just a warm-up! Once we get out, I'll pull out all the stops. Sooner or later, Hongdou will be mine!" Qinggua declared with glowing confidence.

Not having the heart to burst his bubble further, Chen Haoyu turned back to escape. Night had fallen, yet neither Grandmother nor his parents had shown up. Could Zhu Shuzhen have kept him hidden? Impossible—he had spotted a maid from Old Madam Xia's own courtyard staring when he was dragged through the gates in ropes! Why hadn't Grandmother come storming to his rescue?

"Qinggua," he frowned, "why hasn't Grandmother come to spring me?"

"Maybe Old Madam doesn't know you're locked up?"

"One of her own maids saw Zhu Shuzhen march me in tied up! Normally Grandmother would've flown over here in a heartbeat. Did something big happen while I was away?"

Qinggua thought back. "Actually, yes—huge stuff!"

"Like what?"

"While you were gone, Young Madam hired a storyteller to come every single day and tell stories to Old Madam Xia—had her laughing nonstop! Today she even brought an old blind ballad singer, and Old Madam Xia loved it so much she ate extra bowls of rice! And the biggest thing of all—"

Unease prickled up Chen Haoyu's spine. "What's the biggest thing?"

"Young Madam smoothed things over between Old Madam Xia and Eldest Master and Madam! Now Second Master, Third Master, and their wives visit Old Madam Xia's courtyard every single day too! The maids say her courtyard is packed with laughter daily, and Old Madam Xia praises Second Young Madam to the skies—Eldest Master and Madam too!"

Chen Haoyu slammed his fist down onto the table. "Sneaky! Pure sneaky! Bought off my whole family the second my back was turned! No wonder nobody came for me! One clever bride walks in and suddenly Grandmother stops spoiling me and Mother forgets I exist?!"

Qinggua stifled a snicker.

"I'm fuming and you're laughing?" Chen Haoyu glared. "Asking for a beating?"

Qinggua jumped clear, making Chen Haoyu chuckle despite himself. "Relax, just messing with you. Sit back down and keep me from dying of boredom."

Settling back on his stool, Qinggua grinned. "Didn't mean to laugh, Young Master—you just called Young Madam a 'clever bride' for the first time ever!"

"Learn to listen! Was that a compliment? Quit stalling and help me get out of here. Wait—I've got a plan! Put your looks to work and seduce one of Zhu Shuzhen's maids into unlocking the door so I can slip out! Forget Kuqiao and Hongdou—she's got two other maids with plain looks who'll fall hook, line, and sinker!"

Qinggua pulled a sour face. "My looks don't hold a candle to yours, Young Master. Besides, I have zero interest in those two maids—and every maid under Young Madam is a tough cookie!"

"What, expecting me to step up and seduce them myself?"

"With your charm, Young Master, guaranteed success!"

Chen Haoyu raised a palm. "Want a smack?"

Qinggua ducked with a sheepish grin. "Hehe... my looks are really nothing special."

Chen Haoyu lowered his hand. "Sacrifice a sliver of your charm for me, and once we're out, I swear I'll help you win Hongdou over!"

Qinggua's eyes lit up like lanterns. "Really? Don't lie to me, Young Master!"

"A gentleman's word stands firm as four galloping horses!"

"Deal! I'll bite the bullet and do it for you!"

"Now you're talking!"
