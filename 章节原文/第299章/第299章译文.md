## Chapter 299: After the Dongfang Salon

Once Zhu Shuzhen left, Qiao Mengrong sat a few restless minutes, whispered an excuse to her brother, and departed too.

The Dongfang Poetry Salon didn’t miss a beat. Xiangrong sang several more of Youqi Jushi’s lyrics by popular demand, then came the live-writing round where Xiangrong brushed out Shuzhen’s fresh lyric alongside the scholars. Drawing lots to critique one another’s new works on the spot sent the hall into a frenzy—Chen Haofei shone bright enough to get his name remembered by every guest. Membership fees poured in for three-year terms, filling the salon’s coffers, and Prince Yi closed the event declaring the Dongfang Salon number one in Lin’an.

Back at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong didn’t even touch tea before summoning Matron Zou.

“Didn’t dare report yesterday with His Highness home,” Matron Zou said once seated. “Eldest Young Master Chen will keep helping, yet he wants Your Highness to pull strings in Bianliang for the Chen tea shops first.”

Qiao Mengrong fumed. “Hasn’t delivered a thing and wants upfront payment?! Tell him Bianliang waits until Zhu Shuzhen is dead!”

“Told him that exact thing, and he caved. Only Chen Manor picks its business heir next month, and he begged Your Highness to attend and back him—says royal presence seals the deal.”

Weighing it a beat, Qiao Mengrong asked, “Think I should go?”

“Commoners would kill for royalty gracing a family vote—depends if Your Highness feels like it.”

“Feels like it doesn’t matter—what matters is whether it serves me.” A smirk curved her lips. “I’ll go! Watching Youqi Jushi lose face sounds fun.”

Matron Zou blinked. “What’s the Chen inheritance got to do with her?”

“Chen Haoyu’s her husband—if he loses out, she’s humiliated. Her misery is my joy.”

“Fair enough—treat it like an outing. Chen Haodong said if you agree, he’ll approach the Shao Escort Agency—”

“Shao Escort Agency?”

“Biggest escort outfit in Lin’an—fighters just as tough as Youqi Jushi.”

“Escorts guard cargo—would they take a hit?”

“Chen Haodong says enough silver makes ghosts turn millstones.”

“Tell him to go—silver’s no object.”

Rising to leave, Matron Zou paused to plead, “Your Highness, Prince Yi dotes on you now—came home early yesterday, took you to the salon today, grants your every whim. Can’t we let the poetess go?”

Qiao Mengrong scoffed. “Tell her, Xilan.”

Xilan spilled the salon’s “Setting the Verse Straight” round and Prince Yi’s bankrolling—all to whitewash that shameless poetess.

Matron Zou frowned. “Why not host his own salon instead of funding Young Master Dongfang?”

“Cover story!” Qiao Mengrong hissed. “Now I know why Brother tried leaving me home!”

“How can His Highness still be so infatuated? Let me talk sense into him tonight—”

“Don’t you dare! He’ll think I put you up to it and call me petty! Just help me erase her in the dark.”

Sighing helplessly, Matron Zou walked out.

Shen Jianchen returned before sunset after finishing Judicial Office paperwork, relieved to find Mengrong calm and civil. Still wanting zero shadows between them, he took her hand and led her up the wooden terrace overlooking the sweeping hills to talk things through.

Determined to play unbothered so he wouldn’t watch her moves, Qiao Mengrong beat him to it. “Want to talk about the pamphlets, Brother?”

“How’d you guess?”

Turning to face him, she smiled soft. “Grew up together—I read your mind.”

Taking her hands, he said earnest, “My fault for hurting you and driving you to print them. Recalling the pamphlets and holding today’s round were only to fix my own mess.”

“Not your fault, Brother—I was petty.”

Pulling her into his embrace, he murmured, “Forgive how selfishly I chased my own feelings before and broke your heart. Never again—I’m devoted to you alone.”

*His own feelings—so this reborn Brother never really loved me at all.* “Do you love me, Brother?”

Stuck, he hedged. “We’re husband and wife—that says everything.”

“I want to hear you say it.”

“You’re the woman in my arms right now.”

“I’d rather you held someone else in your arms yet kept me in your heart.”

He forced a light laugh. “Then you’d demand my arms too! Quit poking at empty words—being right here with you is what counts.”

Hugging him tight, Qiao Mengrong felt cold inside: she held his body, never his heart—and the woman sitting inside his heart had to go.

That night, Chen Haodong came home from the tea shop riding high—clan votes lined up, Uncle leaning his way, Princess Consort Yi coming to back him next month! Feeling breezy, he strolled over to Ye Anya’s courtyard to share the news and warn her to behave.

Lamp glowing inside, he paused outside the door as Ye Anya’s voice floated out reciting love verses: “‘On high, we’d be two love birds flying wing to wing; on earth, two trees with branches twined from spring to spring.’ [TN: Translated by Xu Yuanchong] ‘Though my sash grows loose, I have no regret; for my beloved’s sake, I waste away gladly.’ ‘One day without seeing you, and longing drives me mad.’ ‘Playful and fond, unafraid of what others might guess, collapsing into his arms fully dressed.’ [TN: Translator’s rendering] Which lines fit best, Xiaocui?”

“Second Young Madam’s lines match your heart best, Miss!”

Love poems—and picking the boldest line of all?! Rage obliterated his good mood. Boom! He kicked the door clean open.

Ye Anya jumped to her feet while Xiaocui lunged to hide the paper—too late. Chen Haodong ripped the sheet from the desk, hands shaking as he saw a love letter addressed to “My Beloved.”

Shredding it in her face, he seized her collar. “Who’s your lover?! Who’s the bastard?!”

Knowing a beating was coming either way, Ye Anya spat straight back, “The man I love—certainly not you!”

Crack! His palm lashed across her cheek, splitting her lip. “Shameless whore!”

Clutching her bleeding mouth, she glared. “Look who’s talking! Think I don’t know the filth you do outside?!”

Xiaocui rushed to help, only for Chen Haodong to kick her down, drag her outside by the scruff, slam the door, and lunge back at Ye Anya. “Acting shameless for another man while playing ice queen with me?!”

“Because I don’t love you!”

“I’ll show you love tonight!” Dragging her into the bedchamber, he hurled her onto the bed.

“Animal!” she sneered. “All you know how to act like is a beast!”

Enraged, he grabbed a feather duster off the table and whipped her raw, welts bursting through her clothes while she screamed in agony—yet refused to beg. “I’ve had enough of you! Forget mutual divorce—write a divorce letter and kick me out right now!”

Exhausted and disgusted by the bloody welts, Chen Haodong flung the duster down. “With heir selection next month? Dream on! I won’t divorce you now, won’t divorce you ever, and never a mutual divorce—I’ll keep you locked beside me and torture you every day so you never fly off with your lover! Mood-killer!”

“Bastard!” she cried after his slamming door.

Rushing in to tend the bleeding welts with salve, Xiaocui begged in tears, “Just humor him next time, Miss, or you’re the one hurting!”

“I’d rather die than bend to him!” Eyes burning, Ye Anya thought of next month’s heir selection and hatched a desperate gamble. “Once these cuts heal, Xiaocui, invite Young Master Cao to Yunlai Inn.”

Xiaocui nearly dropped the salve jar. “An inn, Miss?!”

“Don’t ask—one last roll of the dice for my freedom! If Chen Haodong wrecks my life, I’ll wreck his too. Still got that medical prescription safe?”

“Safe and sound—why ask now?”

“Saved it for the exact day I needed it! You broke faith with me, Chen Haodong—don’t blame me for striking back!”

Steaming mad, Chen Haodong slipped out of Chen Manor to blow off steam with Chun Jinchan at their new hideout, sneaking back before dawn to pretend he’d slept at home. After breakfast and a quick swing by the tea shop, he headed to the Shao Escort Agency to call on Shao Zhengye.

Just back from a long escort run to Qingzhou, washed and sipping morning tea with his son Shao Zhize, Shao Zhengye welcomed Chen Haodong in assuming he had cargo to ship.

“Bringing me business, Eldest Young Master?”

“Huge business—name your own price!”

Trading suspicious glances with his son, Shao Zhengye asked, “What cargo?”

“Not cargo.” Chen Haodong drew a crisp finger across his throat. “Interested?”
