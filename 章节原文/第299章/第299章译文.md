## Chapter 299: After the Dongfang Poetry Salon

Finding the salon dull once Shuzhen left, Qiao Mengrong excused herself early while the gathering roared on to its peak—Xiangrong singing encore after encore, Chen Haofei dazzling the crowd in the random-draw critique round, and scholars lining up to pay three-year dues as Prince Yi crowned the Dongfang Poetry Salon number one in Lin'an.

Storming back into Youlin Retreat, Mengrong summoned Matron Zou—who reported Chen Haodong was willing to hire fighters from Lin'an's top martial firm, the **Shao Escort Agency**, if Princess Consort Yi graced next month's Chen heir election to back him up!

Eager to watch Youqi Jushi lose face when Haoyu lost—and furious all over again as Xilan told Matron Zou how Prince Yi had funded the salon solely to vindicate Shuzhen—Mengrong approved both backing Haodong and funding the Shao Escort Agency hit.

At sunset, Shen Jianchen returned and led Mengrong up onto the wooden viewing platform to clear the air openly about the pamphlets and salon, apologizing for past neglect and promising full devotion—yet when she pleaded *"Tell me you love me"*, he could only answer *"You're the woman in my arms"*, leaving her hugging him tight with fresh venom burning inside.

That same night, Chen Haodong returned home in high spirits and headed to Ye Anya's quarters—only to hear her testing passionate love lines with Xiaocui: Bai Juyi's *"On high, we'd be two lovebirds flying wing to wing; on earth, two trees with branches twined from spring to spring"* [TN: Translated by Xu Yuanchong], Liu Yong's *"My belt grows ever looser, yet I feel no regret; for my beloved I gladly waste away"*, *"One day without seeing you, and longing drives me mad"* [TN: Translator's rendering], and Zhu Shuzhen's *"Playful and unafraid of guesses, falling asleep fully dressed in his arms"*—with Xiaocui picking the last!

Kicking the door clean in, Haodong shredded her love letter to "Dearest," slapped blood across her lip, tossed Xiaocui into the yard, and whipped Anya raw with a feather duster—snarling that he would *never* divorce or repudiate her, trapping her beside him in misery forever!

Bleeding yet unbroken, Anya instructed weeping Xiaocui to invite Cao Ban to Yunlai Inn the moment she could walk—and keep that secret medical prescription safe: *If he won't let me live, I'm dragging him down with me!*

After venting his fury overnight with Chun Jinchan, Haodong called on Shao Zhengye—just back from an escort run to Qingzhou—and bluntly offered a blank check for murder!
