## Chapter 326: Scrambling to Save Her in Bianliang

Agreeing to split efforts at dawn—Haoyu making rounds of his Bianliang contact list while Bai Feng fetched top doctors for Mengrong—Shen Jianchen rode solo to Xihua Gate to petition His Majesty, only to hit a brick wall: **Grand Preceptor Tong Guan** (*Chuguo Gong*) now hovered beside the throne and sent back a brusque brush-off telling Prince Yi to sit tight until Liang Shicheng returned from Lin'an.

Restless and heartsick, Shen Jianchen bypassed an idle teahouse (where a storyteller was performing Qianli Xun's huaben!) and rode straight to the Court of Judicial Review—learning from Chief Minister Chen that trial awaited Liang Shicheng's arrival in seven or eight days, and that his own prince's waist token granted instant access to Bianliang's prison.

That evening, Haoyu staggered home utterly defeated after every single official slammed his door in terror of Liang Shicheng, dropping to his knees begging Prince Yi for help—and brightening at last over shared dinner when Shen Jianchen promised to take him into the prison tomorrow. Later, hearing top city doctors couldn't cure Mengrong, Shen Jianchen sent Bai Feng to summon an imperial physician from the Imperial Medical Bureau, still refusing to visit her himself.

Next morning, flashing his golden token at the bleak east-quarter prison, Shen Jianchen bent the rules to bring Haoyu (loaded with food and supply bundles) inside the foul women's wing. When the iron cell door swung wide, revealing pale Qianli Xun sitting shackled in hand and foot chains on a straw mat, her eyes lit with sudden tears at the sight of both men.
