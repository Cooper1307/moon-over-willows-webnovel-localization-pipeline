## Chapter 316: Shattered Mind After Miscarriage

Xilan sprinted straight to Prince Yi shrieking of blood; Shen Jianchen dispatched Bai Feng at a gallop for a physician and hurried to Mengrong's bedside alongside Matron Zou.

Taking Mengrong's pulse, Matron Zou broke the devastating blow—confirmed minutes later by the arriving physician: Mengrong had been over a month pregnant thanks to her tonics, and severe physical trauma yesterday had triggered a **miscarriage**!

Remembering the forced naked dance and thrashing under Liang Shicheng's needle, Mengrong cracked wide open—clamping both hands over her head and sobbing over and over: *"I hate myself! I hate myself!"*

Warning Shen Jianchen privately outside that losing her first pregnancy meant Mengrong would likely never conceive again—and that severe shock plus grief threatened total madness unless she avoided every stress—the physician recommended mental-illness specialist **Physician Guo** in south Lin'an once she stabilized.

Sticking close to comfort her, Shen Jianchen held her trembling shoulders and murmured tenderly as Zhao Pu: *"Zhao Pu only ever loves Mengrong."* Yet when he tried carrying her back to their main chamber, terror of his seeing the needle scars made her recoil back into her side room.

Once the doctor left and Xilan fed Mengrong her herbal purge, Shen Jianchen pulled Matron Zou outside to ask what had frightened Mengrong yesterday. Stepping back in to find Mengrong staring blankly, Matron Zou hauled Xilan outside and forced out the entire grotesque truth about Liu'an Villa! Heartbroken and blaming herself for finding the address, Matron Zou swore to hide the shame from Prince Yi to protect her mistress's dignity.
