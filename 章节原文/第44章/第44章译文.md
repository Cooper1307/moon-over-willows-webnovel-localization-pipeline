# Chapter 44: Listening to Stories at the Entertainment Precinct

Chen Haoyu ducked clean under Lin Jue's punch, and all three thugs piled straight onto Lin Jue, who scrambled to fend them off. Fearing flying fists, ground-floor diners bolted out the door; waiters ducked behind the counter peeking at the brawl while the manager winced praying his tables survived. Three against one, Lin Jue sagged fast toward defeat.

Watching from his seat, Shen Jianchen frowned: like him or not, Lin Jue was an imperial military officer! Letting local thugs beat an officer flat in public looked awful. Too green in Shen Fist yet for Bai Feng to handle three bruisers together, Shen Jianchen stepped in himself. Vaulting beside Lin Jue in one clean leap, he dropped all three thugs in three crisp blows and held up calm hands. "Talk things out like gentlemen—why swing fists?"

That sealed Lin Jue's hunch—definitely an imperial prince! Otherwise why step into another man's brawl alongside Qiao Wenxian instead of laughing? Protecting an imperial officer's face!

Reading Shen Jianchen's effortless power, Chen Haoyu knew picking a fight with him meant getting crushed—besides, he'd already stung Lin Jue and trashed Zhu Shuzhen's name, mission accomplished! Waving his three groaning thugs back, he sneered, "Not worth dirtying my hands on you, Lin Jue! One parting tip: cast Zhu Shuzhen out fast! She's a straight-up tigress—keep her around and she'll bite your head off! Haha...!" Roaring with laughter, he swaggered out with his crew.

Lin Jue cupped his hands grateful. "Thank you for stepping in, Young Master."

"Helped you so you stop misunderstanding Youqi Jushi and Wenyuan," Shen Jianchen murmured near his ear. "Wenyuan admires your wife, sure, yet everything between them stays spotless. Even if you don't trust Wenyuan, trust your own wife! Without basic trust, a marriage is already dead."

Hardly convinced yet polite before a presumed prince, Lin Jue bowed. "Wise counsel, Young Master, much obliged! Let me host a banquet at Lin Manor someday to repay your help."

"No need—putting today's meal on your tab squares us fine."

"Then Lin Jue takes his leave." Striding out of Juxiang Tower, Lin Jue walked home torn in knots: cast her out, grant mutual divorce, or keep dragging her along?

Finishing every last scrap on the table, Bai Feng and Nan Shuang sat back patting full bellies. Qiao Wenxian laughed shaking his head: "All you two ever do is eat, eat, eat!"

"Only thanks to Master's big heart we get feasts like this!" Nan Shuang grinned.

Rising, Shen Jianchen suggested, "Still want to check out Goulan Wasi—everyone game?"

All agreed. Stepping out of Juxiang Tower, Qiao Wenxian led them toward nearby Qiaotou Wa. Bright banners and lively crowds packed the entertainment district—storytelling, puppetry, shadow plays, acrobatics, song-and-dance, and opera performing inside separate Goulan theaters while peddlers hawked trinkets and snacks all around.

Flipping Bai Feng one tael of silver, Shen Jianchen said, "Pick whichever Goulan theater you two want. Wenyuan and I are hitting storytelling."

Catching the coin—"Thanks, Young Master!"—Bai Feng dragged Nan Shuang straight into a lively song-and-dance theater brimming with beauties.

Used to hitting Goulan Wasi with scholar friends for drinks, poetry, and occasional tales, Qiao Wenxian led the prince into a storytelling hall. Inside, a storyteller sat animated atop a low stage behind a wooden table holding a folding fan, gray teapot, and tea bowl. Packed rows of listeners clustered around small tables munching candied preserves and sipping tea as waiters darted through refilling pots; spotting newcomers, one called, "Two gentlemen—right this way!" Prime front seats all taken, they settled at a back table, where Qiao Wenxian ordered two plates of candied preserves and a pot of tea.

The storyteller's voice rang across the hall:

"Now Cui Ning returned home in low spirits, stepped into his chamber, and saw his wife seated upon the bed. Cui Ning cried, 'Mercy, sister—spare my life!' Xiuxiu replied, 'Because of you, the Prince had me beaten to death and buried in the rear garden! I hated Officer Guo for his loose tongue; today my vengeance is done, for the Prince had him flogged fifty strokes across the back! Now all know I am a ghost and I can linger no longer.' Speaking thus, she rose and gripped Cui Ning with both hands; one shriek, and he collapsed limp to the floor. When neighbors rushed in to see: both pulses stilled and sunk away, one soul gone down to yellow earth! Cui Ning too was dragged off to join her parents—four ghosts together..." (Excerpt from "The Jade Guanyin") [TN: Translator's rendering]

Clack! The storyteller struck his gavel block on the table, closing the tale, and stepped down off the stage while "Bravo!" thundered from the crowd.

Catching only the closing lines, Shen Jianchen blinked baffled. "What was that even about? Lost me!"

"Bright beauty Xiuxiu fell in love with jade carver Cui Ning, got killed by a cruel prince, and came back to make them ghost spouses in the underworld," Qiao Wenxian sighed. "How many devoted lovers denied in life can only cling to death or the next life—tragic, truly tragic!"

Clearly Wenyuan's own ache for Zhu Shuzhen colored his sigh. Unconcerned with Wenyuan's heartbreak, Shen Jianchen focused sharp: storytelling tied straight to the "mouth"! Maybe fate waited right here!

A second storyteller mounted the stage gripping a purple clay teapot, took a swig right off the spout, set it down, snapped open his folding fan, and bantered with the crowd: "Folks, just scored a brand-new huaben script—fresh off the brush! Care to hear something new?"

Voices shouted back from across the hall: "Heard those dusty classics a hundred times—give us fresh!" "Yeah, tell the new one!"

"You asked for it—listen close!" Snapping his fan shut, the storyteller launched smooth into the tale:

"When Goddess Nüwa mended the shattered heavens, a thunderbolt startled her into a shiver, shaking loose a wildflower tucked behind her ear! Carried a thousand miles on the wind, it drifted straight into the abode of the Flower Deity. Just then the Flower Deity was full-term with child, laboring in agony unable to deliver—when wind blew Nüwa's wildflower straight onto her belly and vanished! Instant baby wails rang out as the Flower Deity gave birth to a baby girl! 'Born from a nameless crimson wildflower radiating enchanting scent,' the Flower Deity declared, 'from this day on her name shall be Hongxiang...'"

Wait—wait a second! That plot struck like thunder in Shen Jianchen's head! Racking his memory, realization hit like lightning: that was one of Qianli Xun's web novels! Heart slamming against his ribs, he held his breath straining to confirm as the storyteller rolled on:

"The Flower Deity's palace was called Hundred-Blossom Palace. Pampered by flower fairies, Hongxiang grew into a radiant beauty, yet mischievous to the bone, forever sneaking out of the palace to play! One day she slipped away unseen and landed upon Cinnabar Cave Mountain, a wonderland sliced by a shimmering golden river. While admiring the peaks, a dazzling five-colored phoenix alighted on a boulder behind her and transformed into a breathtaking young man..."

Shen Jianchen's fingers trembled around his tea bowl as he whispered aloud, "Named Xuanying."

Right on cue from the stage: "The young man's name was Xuanying..."

Qiao Wenxian whipped his head round in shock. "First time anyone's ever heard this tale—how on earth did you know?!"

Shen Jianchen didn't hear a word. Staring at Prince Yi's frozen face—eyes locked unblinking onto the storyteller, hand setting down his tea bowl with visibly shaking fingers—Qiao Wenxian asked uneasy, "What's wrong with you?"

Still mute, Shen Jianchen rose to his feet, eyes glued to the stage. Wait—had Qianli Xun transmigrated into that storyteller?! Taking stiff steps toward the platform, he had zero clue how the man would react if he called out "Qianli Xun"! Thinking she had crossed into a short, slightly plump middle-aged man hit him with a wave of letdown, yet in the next heartbeat he swallowed it: fine, romance off the table, they could still be best friends for life! Blinking back hot tears, he kept closing in on the stage while Qiao Wenxian stared baffled from the table, wondering what had gotten into Prince Yi.

Up on stage the storyteller carried on: "Xuanying asked, 'Who are you, and what brings you to Cinnabar Cave Mountain?' Turning around, Hongxiang stole his breath away; never had he seen such beauty on the mountain! Hongxiang tossed the question back: 'Who are you, and why do you live here?' Xuanying answered, 'My name is Xuanying, and Cinnabar Cave Mountain is my home...'"

Seeing the young gentleman stride straight up, the storyteller figured he just wanted a closer seat—until Shen Jianchen choked out, "Qianli Xun!" Halting mid-sentence at the raw emotion on the stranger's face, the storyteller blinked. "You know Qianli Xun, young master?"

"I'm Shen Jianchen—I found you at last!" One hot tear broke free and slid down Shen Jianchen's cheek.

Utterly perplexed, the storyteller shook his head. "Never heard of Shen Jianchen. I know the name Qianli Xun alright, though I've never met the man myself!"

Relief washed over Shen Jianchen so hard he almost laughed at his own runaway meltdown! Cupping his hands urgent, he pleaded, "If you know Qianli Xun, sir, tell me where she is!"

"No idea where he lives! Only know he's a talented scholar who supplies me huaben scripts; every time, his household servant brings the new pages over."

Jeers broke out from the crowd: "Hey! Keep telling the story—we're waiting!" "Right—paid good silver to hear a tale, not chat!"

"Step aside till I finish my set, young master; we'll talk after," the storyteller urged, turning back to pick the tale right back up.

Returning to his seat, Shen Jianchen couldn't focus on another word, willing the clock to fly so he could pump the storyteller for leads. Studying Prince Yi with open curiosity, Qiao Wenxian asked, "Who is Qianli Xun?"

"A friend."

"Another friend from that 'other world'?"

Shen Jianchen nodded. Dropping further questions—whatever Prince Yi claimed happened after death sounded like fever dreams to Wenyuan, though that martial skill stayed undeniably uncanny—he sipped his tea quiet.

The instant the storyteller stepped down off the stage, Shen Jianchen darted straight over. "Sir, when does Qianli Xun's servant bring the next manuscript?"

Pondering a beat, the storyteller shrugged. "Never fixed—sometimes two or three days, sometimes five or six."

"Where do you live?"

"Why ask that?"

"Want to post my man outside your house waiting for him."

"No way, no way!" The storyteller waved hard.

Shen Jianchen pressed two taels of silver straight into his palm. "For your trouble—and more reward once I find Qianli Xun!"

Pocketing the silver with an instant grin, the storyteller spilled: "West Third Alley—very first house inside the lane!"

"Thank you, sir!" Joy sparked bright, then dimmed a shade as the storyteller's wording sank back in: a talented scholar... So Qianli Xun really did transmigrate into a man after all!

Clapping his shoulder from behind, Qiao Wenxian asked, "Lost in space—ready to head back?"

With a solid trail in hand, sightseeing was done. "Long day, tired—let's go home."

Rounding up Bai Feng and Nan Shuang, they hired a local carriage through busy markets out to the city gate, swapped into their own rig, and rolled back toward Youlin Retreat. Inside the carriage, Shen Jianchen issued crisp orders to Bai Feng: starting tomorrow morning, camp outside the first house inside West Third Alley until the manuscript courier showed up, and bring that courier straight back to him in person! Clueless what his master was chasing yet eager as ever, Bai Feng agreed on the spot.
