# 第44章 勾栏瓦肆听说书
# Chapter 44: Listening to Stories at the Entertainment Precinct

陈浩宇迅速闪过林觉打来的一拳，接着三个打手一起向林觉打去，他努力应对着。

Chen Haoyu dodged Lin Jue's punch and his three thugs attacked Lin Jue together. He struggled to defend himself.

---

一楼的食客见打得激烈，怕被伤到，纷纷离开；伙计们躲在柜台后面看热闹；掌柜则担心饭庄被破坏。

The diners on the first floor saw the intense fight and feared being hurt, so they left one after another. The attendants hid behind the counter to watch the show. The manager worried about the restaurant being damaged.

---

林觉应对三人越来越力不从心，快要败下阵来。

Lin Jue found it harder and harder to handle the three men. He was about to lose.

---

申简辰心想，林觉大小也是朝廷的地方官员，怎能眼睁睁看着他败在地痞流氓手中？

Shen Jianchen thought to himself, "Lin Jue is a local official of the court, after all. How can I stand by and watch him be defeated by thugs?"

---

白风练习申拳时间不长，三个打手联合起来他根本不是对手，申简辰决定亲自过去阻止。

Bai Feng hadn't practiced Shen Fist for long. He was no match for the three thugs combined. Shen Jianchen decided to intervene himself.

---

他迅速跳到林觉身边，三拳两脚制服打手，调解道：“诸位，有话好好说，何必动手？”

He quickly jumped to Lin Jue's side, subdued the thugs with a few swift strikes, and mediated, "Gentlemen, let's talk this out. Why resort to violence?"

---

林觉更加确信申简辰是朝廷派来的皇子。如若不是，他应跟着乔文仙继续看热闹，何必招惹是非？定是作为皇子不愿看到朝廷官员受制于人。

Lin Jue became even more convinced that Shen Jianchen was an imperial prince sent by the court. If not, why would he follow Qiao Wenxian to watch the show instead of staying out of trouble? He must be a prince who doesn't want to see a court official humiliated.

---

陈浩宇看出申简辰功力不低，要真与他计较，打起来绝不是对手，况且对方只是前来阻止。再说他陈浩宇的重点不是打林觉，而是羞辱他与朱淑真，让自己面上有光。他向三位打手挥挥手，示意撤到一边。

Chen Haoyu could tell Shen Jianchen's martial arts skills were not low. If he really fought him, he'd be no match. Besides, the other party was just here to stop the fight. Moreover, Chen Haoyu's focus wasn't on fighting Lin Jue, but on humiliating him and Zhu Shuzhen to save face. He waved to the three thugs, signaling them to step aside.

---

“林觉，本公子不想跟你计较，走之前再嘱咐一句，赶紧休了朱淑真，她就是只母老虎，放在身边，不小心会咬死你！哈哈……！”陈浩宇大笑着带众人离开。

“Lin Jue, I don't want to bother with you. One last word before I leave—hurry up and divorce Zhu Shuzhen. She's a tigress. Keep her around and she might bite you to death someday! Hahaha！” Chen Haoyu laughed loudly and led everyone away.

---

林觉谢道：“多谢公子相助。”

Lin Jue thanked him, "Thank you for your help, young sir."

---

“我帮你，是不想让你误解幽栖居士与文渊之间的关系。”申简辰靠近林觉耳边，“文渊爱慕你的夫人不假，可他们之间清清白白，你不信文渊也应信任你的夫人，如果夫妻之间连最起码的信任都没有，我看这婚姻也该走到头。”

"I helped you because I don't want you to misunderstand the relationship between Youqi Jushi and Wenyuan." Shen Jianchen leaned close to Lin Jue's ear. "It's true that Wenyuan admires your wife, but they're innocent. If you don't trust Wenyuan, you should at least trust your wife. If a married couple lacks even the most basic trust, I think this marriage should end."

---

林觉并不认同申简辰所说，可道谢的话还是不能少：“公子中肯相劝，林某感激不尽，今日相帮，改日还请到林府，我定设宴款待。”

Lin Jue didn't agree with what Shen Jianchen said, but he still expressed gratitude, "I'm grateful for your sincere advice, young sir. Thank you for your help today. Please visit Lin Manor another day, and I'll definitely host a banquet for you."

---

申简辰说：“不必客气，今日这桌已记在你账上，就算谢过。”

Shen Jianchen said, "No need to be polite. Today's table is on your account, so that's thanks enough."

---

“既如此，林某告辞。”林觉说完，走出聚香楼，一路上想着是休了朱淑真？还是和离？亦或是继续耗着她？他犹豫不决。

"In that case, I'll take my leave." Lin Jue finished and walked out of Juxiang Restaurant. On the way, he thought about whether to divorce Zhu Shuzhen, reach a mutual divorce, or just keep dragging it out. He couldn't decide.

---

白风与南双将饭菜吃个精光，一副满足的样子。

Bai Feng and Nanshuang finished all the food, looking satisfied.

---

乔文仙笑着批评道：“你们两个就知道吃！吃！吃！”

Qiao Wenxian criticized with a smile, "You two only know how to eat! Eat! Eat!"

---

南双说：“还不是因为公子人好，要不我们两个怎么可能吃到美食？”

Nanshuang said, "That's because our young sir is so kind. Otherwise, how could we have such delicious food?"

---

申简辰建议道：“我还想去勾栏瓦肆逛逛，你们意下如何？”

Shen Jianchen suggested, "I'd like to visit the entertainment precincts. What do you think?"

---

众人无不同意，走出聚香楼，在乔文仙的带领下往最近的桥头瓦走去。

Everyone agreed. They left Juxiang Restaurant and headed to the nearest Qiaotou Wasi under Qiao Wenxian's guidance.

---

瓦肆内装饰华美，热闹非凡，有说话、傀儡戏、皮影戏、杂技、歌舞、戏曲等，都在搭建的不同勾栏内演出，勾栏内外有许多小商贩售卖各种杂货与吃食。

The entertainment precinct was beautifully decorated and bustling with activity. There were storytelling, puppet shows, shadow plays, acrobatics, song and dance, and opera, all performed in different enclosed stages. Many small vendors sold various goods and snacks inside and outside the stages.

---

申简辰掏出一两银子：“你们两个想去哪个勾栏内自己定，我要与文渊去听说书。”

Shen Jianchen took out a tael of silver. "You two decide which stage you want to go to. Wenyuan and I are going to listen to storytelling."

---

白风接过银子：“谢谢公子！”拉起南双进入有美女和歌舞的勾栏内。

Bai Feng took the silver. "Thank you, young sir!" He pulled Nanshuang into a stage with beautiful women and song and dance performances.

---

乔文仙也常应邀来勾栏瓦肆，大多与文人雅士来此畅叙友情，吟诗作词，有时也会听听说书。仪王想听说书，他乐意奉陪。

Qiao Wenxian was often invited to the entertainment precincts, usually with literati and scholars to chat, write poems, and sometimes listen to storytelling. Since the prince wanted to hear stories, he was happy to accompany him.

---

他们走进一个说书的勾栏之地，说话艺人正在台上说得眉飞色舞，他前面有张木桌，上放折扇与灰色茶壶和茶盏。

They walked into a storytelling stage. The storyteller was on stage, speaking vividly. There was a wooden table in front of him with a folding fan, a gray teapot, and a tea bowl.

---

听书之人很多，三三两两坐在桌前，吃着蜜饯果子，品着茶。伙计提壶来回穿梭，忙着给客人添茶水，见有人来，迎上去：“二位！里面坐！”

Many people were listening to the story, sitting at tables in groups of two or three, eating candied fruits and sipping tea. Attendants carried teapots, shuttling back and forth to refill guests' tea. Seeing newcomers, an attendant approached, "Two gentlemen! Please sit inside!"

---

最佳位置已被占尽，他们只能找一张较偏远的桌子。

The best seats were already taken. They could only find a relatively remote table.

---

乔文仙要了两盘蜜饯果子和一壶茶，两人开始认真听书。

Qiao Wenxian ordered two plates of candied fruits and a pot of tea. The two began to listen attentively.

---

只听说话艺人讲道：

The storyteller spoke:

---

话说崔宁到家中，没情没绪，走进房中，只见浑家坐在床上，崔宁道：“告姐姐，饶我性命。”秀秀道：“我因为你，吃郡王打死了，埋在后花园里。却恨郭排军多口，今日已报了冤仇，郡王已将他打了五十背花棒。如今都知道我是鬼，容身不得了。”道罢，起身双手揪住崔宁，叫得一声，四肢倒地。邻舍都来看时，只见：两部脉尽总皆沉，一命已归黄壤下。崔宁也被扯去和父母四个一块儿做鬼去了。…… （节选自《碾玉观音》）

"Cui Ning returned home, feeling listless. He walked into his room and saw his wife sitting on the bed. Cui Ning said, 'Sister, please spare my life.' Xiuxiu said, 'Because of you, I was beaten to death by the Prince of the Commandery and buried in the back garden. I hated General Guo for his loose tongue. Today I've avenged myself. The Prince has given him fifty strokes on the back. Now everyone knows I'm a ghost. I can't stay here anymore.' With that, she stood up, grabbed Cui Ning with both hands, let out a cry, and collapsed on the ground. When the neighbors came to look, they saw: Both pulses gone, one life returned to the yellow earth. Cui Ning was also taken away, and he and his parents all became ghosts together..." (Excerpt from *The Jade-Carving Guanyin*)

---

说话艺人讲完，手拿醒木在桌上那么一拍，书就算说完，然后走下台去。

After finishing the story, the storyteller picked up his gavel and smacked it on the table. The story was over. He then stepped down from the stage.

---

下面听的人直喊：“好！”

The audience below shouted, "Excellent!"

---

刚进来，听了几句就结束，申简辰没听出名堂来：“他说的是什么？我根本没听明白。”

Having just entered and only heard a few lines before it ended, Shen Jianchen didn't understand, "What was he saying? I didn't understand at all."

---

“说的是聪明美貌的女子秀秀，爱上碾玉匠崔宁，后被郡王迫害，两人到地府作对鬼夫妻。这世间有多少痴男怨女无法在一起，只能寄情于死后或是来世，可悲！可叹！”

"It's about a clever and beautiful woman named Xiuxiu who fell in love with the jade carver Cui Ning. They were persecuted by the Prince and became ghost husband and wife in the underworld. How many passionate young men and women in this world can't be together and can only pin their hopes on death or the next life? How sad! How lamentable!"

---

文渊讲得感伤，应是想起他对朱淑真的感情。申简辰并不关心文渊的感情，他之所以来此，因此处与“口”有关，也许能碰到与千里寻有关之事。

Wenyuan spoke with emotion, probably thinking of his feelings for Zhu Shuzhen. Shen Jianchen didn't care about Wenyuan's feelings. He came here because this place was related to "speech," and perhaps he might encounter something related to Qianli Xun.

---

又一位说话艺人登台，手里端着紫砂壶，站到桌前，对着壶嘴喝了一口，放下紫砂壶，把手中的折扇打开，先与台下的人聊天儿：“列位，我刚得了新话本，故事新鲜着呢，不知列位想不想听？”

Another storyteller took the stage, holding a purple clay teapot. He stood at the table, took a sip from the spout, put down the teapot, opened his folding fan, and first chatted with the audience, "Ladies and gentlemen, I've just got a new story script. The story is fresh. I wonder if you'd like to hear it?"

---

有人说：“老掉牙的都听了无数遍，来些新鲜的才好！”

Someone said, "We've heard the old ones countless times. Bring us something fresh!"

---

“当然想听新鲜的！”其他人应和道。

"Of course we want to hear something new!" others echoed.

---

“好！既如此，听我道来！”说话艺人收起折扇，开始娓娓道来：

"Very well! Since that's the case, listen as I tell!" The storyteller closed his fan and began to narrate slowly:

---

话说女娲娘娘补天时，因天雷震动，一时被吓得打了个冷战，鬓角插的一朵野花掉落下来，被风吹到千里之外，来到花神居住之地。此时花神身怀六甲，腹中胎儿已足月，即将诞生，可怎么生都生不下来，正当众花仙焦急之际，女娲娘娘鬓角的野花被风使劲一吹，落在花神的腹部，消失不见。只听一阵婴儿的哭声传来，花神竟然诞下一个女婴。花神说道：“这孩子因朵不知名的野花而生，那野花呈红色，散发出诱人的奇香，以后就叫她红香……”

"When Goddess Nuwa was mending the sky, a thunderclap startled her. She shivered, and a wild flower from her hair fell, blown by the wind a thousand miles away to the land of the Flower Goddess. At this time, the Flower Goddess was pregnant, her child full-term and about to be born. But no matter how she tried, the baby wouldn't come. Just as the flower fairies were anxious, the wild flower from Goddess Nuwa's hair was blown hard by the wind and landed on the Flower Goddess's belly, disappearing. Then came the cry of a baby. The Flower Goddess had actually given birth to a baby girl. The Flower Goddess said, 'This child was born because of an unknown wild flower. The flower was red and emitted an alluring fragrance. From now on, she shall be called Hongxiang...'"

---

申简辰越听越觉得故事好像在哪里听过，他仔细回想，猛然想起正是在千里寻的小说中看到的，不由激动万分。为确认是千里寻曾写过的故事，他耐住激动的心情继续听。

The more Shen Jianchen listened, the more he felt the story seemed familiar. He thought carefully and suddenly remembered it was from Qianli Xun's novel. He was extremely excited. To confirm it was a story Qianli Xun had written, he restrained his excitement and continued listening.

---

说书艺人讲道:

The storyteller continued:

---

花神居住之地名百花宫，红香在百花宫众花仙的呵护下渐渐长大，出落得如花似玉，可十分淘气，最喜欢偷偷溜出百花宫到外界游玩。有一日，她趁众仙不注意，悄悄离开，来到丹穴山。红香落身丹穴山间，此处景色奇美，有一条呈金色的河流从山间流过。正当红香欣赏山间美景时，一只五彩斑斓的凤凰落在他身后的巨石上，化作美男子……

"The place where the Flower Goddess lived was called the Palace of a Hundred Flowers. Hongxiang grew up under the care of the flower fairies in the palace. She grew up looking like a flower, but she was very naughty and liked to sneak out to play in the outside world. One day, she took advantage of the immortals' inattention, quietly left, and came to Mount Danxue. The scenery there was extraordinarily beautiful, with a golden river flowing through the mountains. Just as Hongxiang was admiring the mountain scenery, a colorful phoenix landed on the giant rock behind her and transformed into a handsome man..."

---

申简辰握着茶盏的手开始轻微颤抖，口中说道：“美男子名叫玄英。”

Shen Jianchen's hand holding the tea bowl began to tremble slightly. He said, "The handsome man was named Xuanying."

---

果不其然说话艺人讲道：“美男子名叫玄英……”

Sure enough, the storyteller said, "The handsome man was named Xuanying..."

---

乔文仙感到奇怪：“我可是第一次听到这个故事，你以前听过？”

Qiao Wenxian was puzzled, "This is my first time hearing this story. Have you heard it before?"

---

仪王并未回应。

The prince didn't respond.

---

乔文仙发现仪王表情不太对劲，双眼一直盯着台上的说话艺人，手把茶盏放在桌上，手指因激动轻微颤抖。

Qiao Wenxian noticed the prince's expression was off. His eyes were fixed on the storyteller on stage. He put the tea bowl on the table, his fingers trembling slightly with excitement.

---

“你怎么了？”

"What's wrong with you?"

---

申简辰没有回答，他站起来，盯着说话艺人。

Shen Jianchen didn't answer. He stood up, staring at the storyteller.

---

难道千里寻穿越而来成为说话艺人？申简辰迈开步子向艺人走去，他无法预料如果叫出“千里寻”，说话艺人会如何反应？

Could Qianli Xun have transmigrated and become a storyteller? Shen Jianchen walked toward the artist. He couldn't predict how the storyteller would react if he called out "Qianli Xun."

---

申简辰以为千里寻穿越成为男子，还是位身体微胖的矮子，他失落之余，瞬间决定既然爱情无法寄托，不如与他做个好朋友。他忍住激动的泪水，继续靠近说话艺人……

Shen Jianchen thought Qianli Xun had transmigrated into a man, still a short, slightly chubby man. Feeling disappointed, he instantly decided that since love was hopeless, he might as well be good friends with him. He held back his excited tears and continued approaching the storyteller...

---

乔文仙不解，心想：“仪王这是怎么了？”

Qiao Wenxian was puzzled, thinking, "What's wrong with the prince?"

---

说话艺人继续讲道：

The storyteller continued:

---

玄英开口问：“你是何人？为何来到丹穴山？”当红香转过身时，玄英被红香的美貌吸引，他从未在丹穴山见过如此美貌的姑娘。红香反问道：“你又是谁？为何住在丹穴山？”玄英主动介绍道：“我叫玄英，家住丹穴山。”……

Xuanying asked, "Who are you? Why have you come to Mount Danxue?" When Hongxiang turned around, Xuanying was attracted by her beauty. He had never seen such a beautiful girl on Mount Danxue. Hongxiang asked back, "And who are you? Why do you live on Mount Danxue?" Xuanying introduced himself, "My name is Xuanying, and I live on Mount Danxue..."

---

说话艺人见申简辰走向自己，开始以为此人只不过想到近处听，直到申简辰叫出：“千里寻！”他才不得不停下来，发现走近的年轻人显得很激动。说话艺人疑惑地问：“这位公子，你认识千里寻？”

The storyteller saw Shen Jianchen walking toward him. At first, he thought this person just wanted to listen from closer. But then Shen Jianchen called out, "Qianli Xun!" He had to stop and noticed the young man approaching looked very excited. The storyteller asked in confusion, "Young sir, do you know Qianli Xun?"

---

“我是申简辰，我终于找到你！”一滴泪还是没忍住，从申简辰的眼中落下。

"I'm Shen Jianchen. I've finally found you!" A tear still couldn't be held back and fell from Shen Jianchen's eyes.

---

说话艺人很是不解：“申简辰我没听说过，千里寻的名讳倒是知道，只可惜我从未见过他本人。”

The storyteller was very confused, "I've never heard of Shen Jianchen, but I do know the name Qianli Xun. It's a pity I've never met him in person."

---

此话令申简辰变得轻松，刚才不知不觉生发的情感显得很可笑。他拱手相问：“听先生这话，应该知道千里寻，请告诉我，她在哪里？”

This made Shen Jianchen feel relieved. The emotions he had generated just now seemed ridiculous. He cupped his hands and asked, "From your words, you should know Qianli Xun. Please tell me, where is she?"

---

说话艺人回道：“他住在哪里我并不知，只知他是给我提供话本的一位才子，每次都是他家的下人将新的书稿送来。”

The storyteller replied, "I don't know where he lives. I only know he's a talented young man who provides me with story scripts. Every time, it's his servant who delivers the new manuscript."

---

有人开始不满：“喂！快说故事，我们都等着听呢！”“是啊！掏钱进来的，总不能让银子白花！”

Someone began to complain, "Hey! Hurry up and tell the story. We're all waiting to hear it!" "Yes! We paid to come in. We can't let our silver go to waste!"

---

说话艺人劝道：“公子先到一边等，等我讲完，再与你聊。”

The storyteller advised, "Young sir, please step aside for now. Let me finish the story, and then we'll talk."

---

说话艺人又继续开讲。

The storyteller continued his tale.

---

申简辰回到座位，已无心倾听，只希望时间过得快些，好打听千里寻的去处。

Shen Jianchen returned to his seat. He had no heart to listen anymore. He only hoped time would pass quickly so he could ask about Qianli Xun's whereabouts.

---

乔文仙一直研究仪王，终于忍不住问：“千里寻是谁？”

Qiao Wenxian had been studying the prince and finally couldn't help but ask, "Who is Qianli Xun?"

---

“一个朋友。”

"A friend."

---

“不会又是在另一个世界认识的？”

"Not someone you met in another world again?"

---

申简辰点点头。

Shen Jianchen nodded.

---

乔文仙不再追问，仪王死后经历了什么，他并不感兴趣，或者说在他看来都是仪王臆想出来的。不过，想到他会功夫之事还真的玄妙无比。

Qiao Wenxian stopped asking. He wasn't interested in what the prince had experienced after death, or rather, he thought it was all the prince's imagination. But thinking about him knowing martial arts was truly mysterious.

---

终于等到说话艺人走下台，申简辰一个箭步冲到面前：“先生，千里寻家的下人什么时候会来给您送书稿？”

Finally, the storyteller stepped down from the stage. Shen Jianchen rushed forward in one stride, "Young sir, when will the servant from Qianli Xun's home come to deliver the manuscript?"

---

说话艺人想想回道：“时间我也说不准，有时两三日，有时五六日。”

The storyteller thought and replied, "I can't say for sure. Sometimes two or three days, sometimes five or six days."

---

“您住哪里？”

"Where do you live?"

---

“问这干什么？”

"Why do you ask?"

---

“我想派人守在您家旁边。”

"I want to send someone to wait outside your house."

---

“不行不行！”说话艺人一口回绝。

"No, no!" The storyteller refused outright.

---

申简辰掏出二两银子：“这是酬劳，如若我找到千里寻，还会酬谢。”

Shen Jianchen took out two taels of silver. "This is your reward. If I find Qianli Xun, I'll reward you again."

---

说话艺人接过银子，变得和颜悦色：“我家住在西三胡同，进胡同第一家。”

The storyteller took the silver and became amiable. "My home is in West Third Alley, the first household entering the alley."

---

“感谢先生。”申简辰谢过，面露喜色，接着又开始失落，照说话艺人所讲，千里寻是位才子，看来她穿越而来真的成为男子。

"Thank you, young sir." Shen Jianchen thanked him, his face showing joy. Then he became depressed again. According to the storyteller, Qianli Xun was a talented young man. It seemed she had really transmigrated into a man.

---

乔文仙在申简辰身后拍了一下：“想什么呢？走不走？”

Qiao Wenxian patted Shen Jianchen from behind, "What are you thinking about? Are we leaving?"

---

找千里寻之事已有眉目，没有必要再逛下下去：“逛了一天，也累了，回去吧。”

They had some leads on finding Qianli Xun. No need to continue shopping. "We've been out all day and we're tired. Let's go back."

---

两人找到白风和南双，在闹市临时租辆马车把他们载到城门口，又换来时乘坐的马车，出了城，回幽林别居。

They found Bai Feng and Nanshuang, rented a carriage temporarily in the busy market to take them to the city gate, then switched to the carriage they came in, left the city, and returned to Youlin Villa.

---

马车上，申简辰吩咐白风从明日起到临安城西三胡同找第一户人家，在门口守着，直到遇见送书稿之人，要求他必须把送书稿之人亲自带到他面前。

On the carriage, Shen Jianchen ordered Bai Feng to go to the first household in West Third Alley, west of Lin'an city, starting tomorrow. Wait at the door until he meets the person delivering the manuscript. He must bring that person directly to him.

---

白风虽不懂王爷到底要干什么，还是爽快答应。

Although Bai Feng didn't understand what the prince was up to, he readily agreed.
