#### 问题1：重复用词
- **"storyteller"重复15次**：应适当替换为"the performer"、"the narrator"、"the artist"等。
- **"tea bowl"重复4次**：可简化为"the bowl"或"his cup"。
- **"Qianli Xun"重复8次**：部分可用代词"her"或"the author"替代。

#### 问题2：句式优化
- **长句需拆分**：
  - 原文："The entertainment precinct was beautifully decorated and bustling with activity. There were storytelling, puppet shows, shadow plays, acrobatics, song and dance, and opera, all performed in different enclosed stages."
  - 建议拆分为更短的句子，适合移动端阅读。

#### 问题3：文化细节
- **"两部脉尽总皆沉，一命已归黄壤下"**：这句诗翻译为"Both pulses gone, one life returned to the yellow earth"较为直译，可考虑更诗意的表达。
- **"背花棒"**：翻译为"strokes on the back"丢失了"花"的意象，可译为"fifty strokes with a flowered staff"。

#### 问题4：口语化程度
- 部分对话略显正式：
  - "I'm grateful for your sincere advice, young sir" → "Thanks for the advice, young sir"
  - "He couldn't decide" → "He was torn"

| storyteller | the performer / the narrator / the artist | 15次 |
| tea bowl | the bowl / his cup | 4次 |
| Qianli Xun | her / the author | 8次 |
| entertainment precinct | the venue / the precinct | 3次 |

### 3.2 句式拆分建议
**原文**：
> The entertainment precinct was beautifully decorated and bustling with activity. There were storytelling, puppet shows, shadow plays, acrobatics, song and dance, and opera, all performed in different enclosed stages. Many small vendors sold various goods and snacks inside and outside the stages.

**修订**：
> The entertainment precinct was beautifully decorated and bustling with activity. Different enclosed stages hosted storytelling, puppet shows, shadow plays, acrobatics, song and dance, and opera. Small vendors sold various goods and snacks inside and outside the stages.

### 3.3 文化细节优化

> Both pulses gone, one life returned to the yellow earth.

**修订**：
> Both pulses faded, one soul returned to the earth below.

**原文**：
> fifty strokes on the back

**修订**：
> fifty strokes with a flowered staff
