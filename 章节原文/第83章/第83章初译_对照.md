# 第83章 夜晚前来访别居

---

乔文仙来到幽林别居外，见淑真站在马车前等他，夜色下她依然那么美好。

Qiao Wenxian arrived outside Youlin Villa and saw Shuzhen standing in front of the carriage waiting for him. Even in the night, she was still so beautiful.

---

乔文仙来到淑真面前，关切而又兴奋地问："淑真，天都黑了，你怎么跑来？"

Qiao Wenxian came to Shuzhen's side and asked with concern and excitement: "Shuzhen, it's already dark. Why did you come here?"

---

千里寻毫不掩饰自己的感情："一日未见，甚是想念，忍不住过来看看你。"

Qianlixun didn't hide her feelings at all: "A day apart feels like an eternity. I couldn't help but come see you."

---

乔文仙抓住淑真的手："我也想你。"

Qiao Wenxian took Shuzhen's hand: "I miss you too."

---

苦荞与南双见二人亲密的样子，不好意思注视下去，都退到远处。

Kuqiao and Nanshuang, seeing the two being so intimate, felt embarrassed to keep watching and retreated to a distance.

---

两人执手互相对视，无须言语，便知对方心意。

The two held hands and gazed at each other. No words were needed to know each other's hearts.

---

申简辰到达别居门口时，他的腿脚无法挪动，明明猜到他们相见时会有亲密的举动，他却忍不住要跟着前来。

When Shen Jianchen arrived at the villa's gate, his legs couldn't move. He had clearly guessed they would be intimate when they met, yet he couldn't help but follow.

---

如果眼前的男子是别人，申简辰会一拳打过去，可偏偏是文渊。文渊是他来到这个时代认识的第一个朋友，他十分珍惜。

If the man before him were anyone else, Shen Jianchen would have thrown a punch. But it had to be Wenyuan. Wenyuan was the first friend he had made after coming to this era, and he cherished him greatly.

---

更令他伤心的是，千里寻喜欢着文渊，她看他的眼神与在另一个时代看他时完全不同。

What hurt him even more was that Qianlixun loved Wenyuan. The way she looked at him was completely different from how she had looked at him in the other era.

---

申简辰努力控制住情绪，不想让他们再这么亲密下去，脱口而出："文渊，你不是想让我帮你吗？我答应。"

Shen Jianchen struggled to control his emotions. Not wanting them to continue being so intimate, he blurted out: "Wenyuan, didn't you want me to help you? I agree."

---

文渊果然放开爱人的手，转身跑到门口抱住仪王："说话算话，不许骗我！"

Wenyuan, as expected, let go of his lover's hand, turned around, ran to the gate, and hugged the Prince of Yi: "A promise is a promise. No lying to me!"

---

申简辰看向千里寻，文渊放开她后，她显得失落。两人不再亲密，申简辰有些许快意，又后悔为何不假思索答应帮助文渊。话已出口，覆水难收。

Shen Jianchen looked at Qianlixun. After Wenyuan let go of her, she looked disappointed. The two were no longer intimate. Shen Jianchen felt a trace of satisfaction, but also regretted why he had agreed to help Wenyuan without thinking. The words were out. What's done cannot be undone.

---

"你再抱着我，我可要反悔。"

"If you keep hugging me, I'll change my mind."

---

"千万别，我与淑真的幸福全靠仪王您了。"乔文仙在仪王耳边说完，放开他。

"Please don't. My happiness with Shuzhen depends entirely on you, Your Highness." Qiao Wenxian whispered in the prince's ear, then let go.

---

千里寻上前行礼："仪王，淑真这厢有礼。"

Qianlixun stepped forward and bowed: "Prince of Yi, Shuzhen pays her respects."

---

"你见我不必如此多礼，像文渊一样，我才觉得舒服。"

"You don't need to be so formal with me. I only feel comfortable when you're like Wenyuan."

---

"仪王为何不早说？害得我见您行了好几次礼。"

"Why didn't you say so earlier? You made me bow to you several times."

---

"以前没想到，现在说也不晚。"申简辰有意靠近千里寻，"大晚上的，幽栖居士跑来见我家文渊，会不会显得太主动？姑娘家太主动似乎不大妥当。"

"I didn't think of it before. It's not too late to say it now." Shen Jianchen deliberately moved closer to Qianlixun. "Late at night, Youqi Jushi comes to see my Wenyuan. Won't that seem too forward? It doesn't seem proper for a young lady to be too forward."

---

千里寻的话还未出口，文渊主动说："我喜欢淑真主动。"

Before Qianlixun could speak, Wenyuan volunteered: "I like Shuzhen being forward."

---

千里寻得意地看着仪王："仪王刚说要帮文渊，是帮什么？"

Qianlixun looked at the Prince of Yi triumphantly: "You just said you'd help Wenyuan. Help with what?"

---

乔文仙怕淑真知道乔守仁没爽快答应求婚之事会难过，赶紧说："是我与仪王之间的事，你不必挂怀。"

Qiao Wenxian, afraid Shuzhen would be sad to know Qiao Shouren hadn't readily agreed to the proposal, quickly said: "It's something between me and the Prince of Yi. You don't need to worry about it."

---

千里寻装作生气的样子："你们两个之间竟然有秘密，文渊，我嫉妒了，我觉得你跟仪王比跟我关系好。"

Qianlixun pretended to be angry: "You two actually have secrets between you. Wenyuan, I'm jealous. I feel you're closer to the Prince of Yi than to me."

---

乔文仙靠近淑真，着急道："怎么会？别瞎想，我跟仪王除是亲人外，还是朋友，自然关系非他人能比，但与跟你的关系不一样，如果说仪王是我在这个世上最好的朋友，你就是我在这个世上最爱的人。"

Qiao Wenxian moved closer to Shuzhen and said anxiously: "How could that be? Don't think nonsense. Besides being family, the Prince of Yi and I are also friends. Naturally, our relationship can't be compared to others, but it's different from my relationship with you. If the Prince of Yi is my best friend in this world, you are the person I love most in this world."

---

如若没有仪王在身边，千里寻真想抱住文渊。仪王一直盯着他们，令她难以下手。心想："仪王怎么这么没眼色？没见人家与文渊情正浓，他却直勾勾盯着，让人难为情。"

If the Prince of Yi weren't around, Qianlixun would have loved to hug Wenyuan. The prince kept staring at them, making it hard for her to make a move. She thought: "Why is the Prince of Yi so clueless? Can't he see Wenyuan and I are in the middle of a tender moment? He keeps staring right at us, it's embarrassing."

---

文渊视他为亲人，申简辰十分感动，也让他感到羞愧，为何从文渊身边抢走千里寻的想法会如此强烈？他矛盾着。

Wenyuan regarded him as family. Shen Jianchen was deeply moved, but also ashamed. Why was the desire to take Qianlixun away from Wenyuan so strong? He was conflicted.

---

"文渊，能否让我与幽栖居士单独聊聊？"

"Wenyuan, could you let me talk to Youqi Jushi alone?"

---

乔文仙显然不乐意："为何？她可是来找我的。"

Qiao Wenxian was obviously unwilling: "Why? She came to see me."

---

"还想不想让我帮你？"

"Do you still want me to help you?"

---

"当然想。"

"Of course I do."

---

"既然想就听我的。"

"Then listen to me."

---

乔文仙见仪王语气坚定，心想，仪王已有佳和郡主，应该不会对淑真产生任何想法，既是为帮他，不如听他一回。他走到仪王身边，凑近后小声问："我能不能在旁边待着？"

Seeing the prince's firm tone, Qiao Wenxian thought: The Prince of Yi already has Princess Jiahe. He shouldn't have any thoughts about Shuzhen. Since it's to help him, why not listen to him once. He walked to the prince's side, leaned close, and whispered: "Can I stay nearby?"

---

"不行！"仪王声音虽不大，语气却坚定。

"No!" Though the prince's voice wasn't loud, his tone was firm.

---

乔文仙只好作罢："那好，我在院子里等，你跟淑真说话温柔些，不要吓着她。"

Qiao Wenxian had to give up: "Alright then. I'll wait in the courtyard. Be gentle when you talk to Shuzhen. Don't scare her."

---

"她会害怕？"申简辰反问道，靠近文渊耳边小声说，"看来你一点儿都不了解她。"

"She'll be scared?" Shen Jianchen retorted, then leaned close to Wenyuan's ear and whispered, "It seems you don't understand her at all."

---

"我当然了解。"乔文仙很自信，转向淑真，"我进去待会儿，一会儿我们再聊。"

"Of course I understand." Qiao Wenxian was very confident. He turned to Shuzhen. "I'll go in for a while. We'll talk later."

---

不等淑真答应，乔文仙已走进幽林别居。

Without waiting for Shuzhen's agreement, Qiao Wenxian had already walked into Youlin Villa.

---

南双跟上自家公子，到门口时，发现白风也跟来，一直在门口朝苦荞看。

Nanshuang followed his young master. At the gate, he found Baifeng had also followed and was staring at Kuqiao the whole time.

---

"你盯着苦荞姐姐做什么？"

"Why are you staring at Sister Kuqiao?"

---

白风拉住南双："她看着也不大，你怎么叫她姐姐？"

Baifeng pulled Nanshuang: "She doesn't look that old. Why do you call her sister?"

---

南双回道："她比我大三岁，我当然称呼她姐姐。"

Nanshuang replied: "She's three years older than me. Of course I call her sister."

---

"我比你大一岁，那她就比我大两岁，没想到她竟然比我大，不过大些也没关系。"白风像是自语。

"I'm one year older than you, so she's two years older than me. I didn't expect her to be older than me. But it's fine if she's a bit older." Baifeng seemed to be talking to himself.

---

南双听着没趣："我去陪我家公子，懒得跟你说话。"

Nanshuang found it boring: "I'm going to keep my young master company. I can't be bothered to talk to you."

---

白风继续盯着苦荞，他从未对一个女孩子有过像现在一样奇怪的感觉，这感觉使得他只想盯着对方看，甚至忘记关注王爷对幽栖居士说些什么。

Baifeng continued staring at Kuqiao. He had never felt such a strange feeling toward a girl before. This feeling made him want to keep staring at her, even forgetting to pay attention to what the prince was saying to Youqi Jushi.

---

再说王爷拉起幽栖居士，走到离他与苦荞较远的地方讲话，显然是不想让他听到。他干脆走近苦荞，站在她身边，感受着与她待在一起的感觉。

Meanwhile, the prince took Youqi Jushi's hand and walked to a place far from him and Kuqiao to talk, obviously not wanting him to hear. He simply walked closer to Kuqiao, stood beside her, and enjoyed the feeling of being with her.

---

申简辰拉起千里寻的手臂，把她拽到一边，站定后，依然不舍得放开，直到千里寻要求道："仪王，能不能放开我？"他才不得不放开。

Shen Jianchen took Qianlixun's arm and pulled her to the side. After they stopped, he still couldn't bear to let go, until Qianlixun requested: "Prince of Yi, could you let go of me?" Only then did he have to release her.

---

"仪王为何拉我到这边？难道想把你与文渊的秘密告诉我？"

"Why did you pull me over here? Do you want to tell me the secret between you and Wenyuan?"

---

千里寻问完，发现仪王看她的眼神很不对劲，蕴含的感情并不比文渊差。心想："怎么回事？他不是有佳和郡主吗？为何如此看我？该不会是因喜欢看我写的话本，偷偷喜欢上我？哎呀！不管是什么，我已有文渊，岂能接受他的感情？"

After Qianlixun asked, she noticed the prince's gaze was off — the emotion in it no less than Wenyuan's. She thought: "What's going on? Doesn't he have Princess Jiahe? Why look at me like that? Could he have fallen for me from reading my stories? No! I already have Wenyuan. I can't accept his feelings."

---

千里寻向后退了一步，继续问："仪王，不知你想对我说什么？"

Qianlixun stepped back and continued to ask: "Prince of Yi, what did you want to say to me?"

---

所爱之人有意与他保持距离，申简辰清醒许多，他收回眼神，抬起头，闭上眼睛，在心里叹口气，问道："你确定想嫁给文渊？"

Seeing the person he loved deliberately keeping her distance from him, Shen Jianchen became much more clear-headed. He withdrew his gaze, raised his head, closed his eyes, sighed inwardly, and asked: "Are you sure you want to marry Wenyuan?"

---

千里寻毫不犹豫："当然。"

Qianlixun didn't hesitate: "Of course."

---

"如若乔家人不同意，你是否依然坚持？"

"If the Qiao family doesn't agree, will you still persist?"

---

"那还用问！"

"Why even ask!"

---

"若你坚持，乔家人依然不同意，你会怎么做？"

"If you persist but the Qiao family still doesn't agree, what will you do?"

---

千里寻沉默了一会儿，说道："我会听文渊的，他若坚持，我便坚持，他若放弃，我……我不知道。"

Qianlixun was silent for a moment, then said: "I'll listen to Wenyuan. If he persists, I'll persist. If he gives up, I... I don't know."

---

申简辰对自己感到失望，穿越前不涉足爱情的她竟然在穿越后这么喜欢一个人，可悲的是这个人并不是他。

Shen Jianchen was disappointed in himself. Before transmigrating, she had never been interested in love. After transmigrating, she liked someone this much — and tragically, that person wasn't him.

---

"你……你以前可喜欢过其他人？"

"Have... have you ever liked anyone else before?"

---

千里寻不明白，仪王为何要问出这个问题？她还是照实回答："我失忆了，以前的事一点都记不起来，别人都说我喜欢过一位徐公子，可我连他长什么样都不记得。"

Qianlixun didn't understand. Why would the Prince of Yi ask this question? She still answered truthfully: "I have amnesia. I can't remember anything from before at all. Everyone says I once liked a Young Master Xu, but I can't even remember what he looked like."

---

"我听说人失忆是因为有新的灵魂住进身体里，不知幽栖居士体内新的灵魂是否还记得曾喜欢过谁？"申简辰半真半假的话语无非是想知道千里寻有没有爱过他。

"I've heard that amnesia happens because a new soul has moved into the body. I wonder if the new soul in Youqi Jushi's body still remembers who she once liked?" Shen Jianchen's half-true, half-false words were simply to find out if Qianlixun had ever loved him.

---

千里寻不由自主想到申简辰，用疑惑的目光盯着仪王，难道他知道了什么？

Qianlixun couldn't help but think of Shen Jianchen. She stared at the Prince of Yi with a puzzled gaze. Could he know something?

---

"这种说法我头一次听说。"

"This is the first time I've heard such a theory."

---

千里寻的回答没有说到申简辰心里，他叹口气："唉，幽栖居士既然如此喜欢文渊，我定会努力成全。也希望你认真想一想，有没有曾经喜欢过其他人，我是说失忆后的你，如果有，一定要告诉我。"

Qianlixun's answer didn't reach Shen Jianchen's heart. He sighed: "Sigh, since Youqi Jushi likes Wenyuan so much, I will definitely try to help you succeed. I also hope you think carefully about whether you've ever liked anyone else. I mean, after your amnesia. If there is someone, you must tell me."

---

申简辰说完，转身离去。走到门口见白风未跟过来，大声喊道："白风！还愣着干什么？"

After Shen Jianchen finished speaking, he turned and left. Walking to the gate, he saw Baifeng hadn't followed. He shouted loudly: "Baifeng! What are you standing there for?"

---

白风从怀里掏出一个泥人，塞进苦荞怀里："送给你。"

Baifeng pulled out a clay figurine from inside his coat and pressed it into Kuqiao's arms: "This is for you."

---

苦荞接住，想要拒绝已不可能，白风早迅速跟随仪王进入幽林别居。

Kuqiao caught it. It was already impossible to refuse. Baifeng had already quickly followed the prince into Youlin Villa.

---

乔文仙见仪王进来， 拉住他："淑真可走？"

Seeing the prince come in, Qiao Wenxian pulled him: "Can Shuzhen leave?"

---

"还在外面。"申简辰快速走开开，他怕自己忍不住冲出去抱住千里寻，那样不仅会破坏他与文渊之间的感情，也会听到千里寻毫不犹豫拒绝的话语。

"She's still outside." Shen Jianchen quickly walked away. He was afraid he couldn't resist rushing out to hug Qianlixun. That would not only destroy the friendship between him and Wenyuan, but he would also hear Qianlixun's words of refusal without hesitation.

---

乔文仙再次来到淑真面前，他并未询问仪王说些什么，担心太晚，坚持要护送淑真回去。

Qiao Wenxian came to Shuzhen again. He didn't ask what the prince had said. Worried it was too late, he insisted on escorting Shuzhen back.

---

千里寻没有拒绝，她本就希望与文渊多待些时间。他们二人同坐在朱府的马车内，后面跟着乔家的马车，里面坐着苦荞与南双。

Qianlixun didn't refuse. She had originally hoped to spend more time with Wenyuan. The two sat together in the Zhu family's carriage, followed by the Qiao family's carriage behind, with Kuqiao and Nanshuang inside.

---

一路上，他搂着她，不用说任何话，只需互相用心感受对方的心意。

Along the way, he held her in his arms. No words needed to be spoken. They only needed to feel each other's hearts with their own.
