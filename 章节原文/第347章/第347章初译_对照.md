第347章 尼姑庵请邵月婷
## Chapter 347: Fetching Shao Youting from the Convent

邵家镖局门外，申简辰匆忙跳下马，快速进入镖局。
Shen Jianchen sprang from his horse outside the Shao escort compound and hurried inside.

镖局的手下认得仪王：“殿下来了，我这就禀报邵当家。”
One of the escorts recognized Prince Yi. "Your Highness has come. I'll announce you to Master Shao at once."

“无须禀报，只需告诉我邵当家在哪里？”申简辰加快步伐。
"No need to announce me. Just tell me where Master Shao is." Shen Jianchen quickened his pace.

镖局的手下紧跟上：“在后院操练。”
The escort hurried after him. "Training in the back yard."

申简辰继续加快步伐向后院走。
Shen Jianchen strode faster toward the back yard.

邵正邺正在亲自操练几名刚入镖局的手下，冷不丁眼前跪下一人，仔细一瞧原来是仪王。邵正邺惶恐至极，上前去扶：“仪王这是何意？折煞我也！”
Shao Zhengye was drilling a few new escorts himself when a man dropped to his knees right in front of him. He looked closer and saw Prince Yi. Shao Zhengye was struck with alarm and stepped forward to help him up. "What is Your Highness doing? You'll be the death of me!"

申简辰坚决不起：“邵当家不答应，我便长跪不起。”
Shen Jianchen refused to rise. "Until Master Shao agrees, I shall stay on my knees."

“仪王快起来，你不起来我可什么都不答应。”
"Get up, Prince Yi. If you stay down, I'll promise you nothing."

“好，我起来。”
"Very well. I'll stand."

待仪王起身后，邵正邺对手下说：“都退下吧，我与仪王有话要说。”
Once Prince Yi was on his feet, Shao Zhengye said to his men, "All of you, go. I need a word with His Highness."

估摸着手下走远，邵正邺向仪王行礼：“殿下今日此举不知因何缘故？”
When he judged them out of earshot, Shao Zhengye bowed to Prince Yi. "May I ask what brings Your Highness here today?"

“想请邵当家的妹妹出面救人。”
"I've come to ask your sister to save a life."

“为何要请胞妹出面？”
"Why my sister?"

“我想救的人在完颜池手中……”
"The woman I want to save is in Wanyan Chi's hands..."

邵正邺犹豫道：“可以想想其他办法……”
Shao Zhengye hesitated. "Surely there's another way..."

“所有办法已想过，均无法撼动完颜池，无路可走才想到邵家。”
"I've tried every way. Nothing shakes Wanyan Chi. I came to the Shao family because I have nowhere left to turn."

“不知仪王所救何人？”
"Who is it Your Highness wants to save?"

“幽栖居士。”
"Youqi Jushi."

“听说人死在金军刀下。”
"I heard she died under the Jin blades."

“我刚刚得到消息她没死，应该还在完颜池手中。”
"I learned only now that she's alive. She must still be in Wanyan Chi's hands."

仪王与幽栖居士的事邵正邺听人议论过，仪王如此深情，实在令人感动。他刚才不顾王爷身份跪下，足以证明今日得不到他的应允绝不离开。
Shao Zhengye had heard people talk of Prince Yi and Youqi Jushi. Such devotion moved him. The fact that the prince had knelt despite his rank proved he would not leave without a promise.

“既是救仪王的人邵家义不容辞，只是我妹妹出家已久，恐怕难以说动。”
"If it's to save Prince Yi's woman, the Shao family can't refuse. But my sister has been a nun for years. I doubt I can persuade her."

“所以我才请邵当家出面。”
"That's why I came to you."

邵正邺犹豫片刻，果断答应：“请仪王随我去山上的尼姑庵。”
Shao Zhengye thought it over, then made up his mind. "Come with me to the convent up the mountain, Your Highness."

邵正邺独自陪同仪王来到山脚下，拴了马后，步行上山。
Shao Zhengye went alone with Prince Yi to the foot of the mountain. They tied their horses and walked up.

申简辰心里明白，邵正邺之所以单独陪他，是不想邵家与金人的关系让更多人知道。
Shen Jianchen understood why Shao Zhengye had come alone: he did not want word of the Shao family's ties to the Jin spreading.

到达尼姑庵外，负责传话的小尼姑出来后，邵正邺说：“小师傅，请转告净悟法师，我想见见她。”
At the convent gate, the young nun who handled messages came out. Shao Zhengye said, "Sister, please tell Reverend Jingwu I'd like to see her."

小尼姑认得邵正邺：“邵当家稍候。”
The young nun knew him. "Wait here, Master Shao."

没多久，小尼姑又来到门口：“净悟法师说让邵当家到客堂等候，二位请跟我来。”
Before long she returned to the gate. "Reverend Jingwu says to wait in the guest hall. Follow me, both of you."

二人来到客堂，小尼姑上好茶自行离去。
They entered the guest hall. The young nun served tea and left them alone.

两人品完一盏茶后，一位四十开外的女尼进入客堂。
They had finished one cup when a nun in her forties walked in.

申简辰站起来行礼：“阿弥陀佛。”
Shen Jianchen stood and bowed. "Amitabha."

邵月婷回礼道：“阿弥陀佛，施主请坐。”
Shao Youting returned the bow. "Amitabha. Please sit, benefactor."

邵月婷坐下主动问：“哥哥许久不来看我，好不容易来一趟却带一位陌生人，不知是何缘故？”
Shao Youting sat down and spoke first. "Brother, it's been ages since you visited. You finally come, and you bring a stranger. What's the reason?"

邵正邺解释道：“你也知镖局事务繁忙，再说哥哥不好上山打扰你清修。”
Shao Zhengye explained, "You know how busy the escort business is. Besides, I didn't wish to climb the mountain and disturb your meditation."

邵月婷看向仪王：“哥哥还是给我介绍一下你带来的年轻人。”
Shao Youting looked at Prince Yi. "Brother, introduce the young man you've brought."

邵正邺介绍道：“这位是仪王殿下。”
Shao Zhengye said, "This is His Highness Prince Yi."

邵月婷并未起身行礼，依旧端坐，态度平和：“原来是皇家人，难怪哥哥会带来见我。我佛向来主张众生平等，我乃出家人，恕我不能遵从俗世礼仪向贵人行礼。”
Shao Youting did not rise or bow. She sat straight, her tone even. "So, royalty. No wonder you brought him to see me. Buddhism teaches that all beings are equal. I'm a nun, so forgive me for not observing worldly customs and bowing to a noble."

申简辰并不介意：“我虽身是皇家人，却自视为普通百姓，法师只需将我看作普通众生即可。”
Shen Jianchen didn't mind. "Though I was born royal, I think of myself as an ordinary man. Reverend, you may treat me as just another being."

邵月婷赞道：“仪王乃智慧之人。”
Shao Youting praised him. "Prince Yi is a wise man."

两人说话间，邵正邺犹豫如何道出实情，也不知妹妹经过二十多年修行是否能超脱人间感情，从而原谅自己。
As they spoke, Shao Zhengye weighed how to tell the truth. He wondered whether twenty years of practice had freed his sister from earthly feeling enough to forgive him.

为救人只好鼓起勇气：“妹妹，有一事藏在哥哥心里，本想一直隐瞒下去，却事与愿违。我想应是妹妹修行感动上天，才逼得哥哥不得不上山向你道明实情，希望妹妹不要怪罪哥哥。”
To save the woman, he had to summon his courage. "Little sister, there's something I've kept in my heart. I meant to hide it forever, but things went otherwise. I think your practice moved Heaven, and it has forced me to climb this mountain and tell you the truth. Please don't blame me."

申简辰深感愧疚，自己的事逼得邵当家不得不说出隐瞒了二十多年的事。
Shen Jianchen felt deeply ashamed. His own trouble had forced Master Shao to reveal a secret kept for over twenty years.

邵月婷先是疑惑，紧接着品出些滋味来：“哥哥难道想跟我提二十多年前的事？”
Shao Youting was puzzled at first, then caught his meaning. "Brother, are you speaking of what happened more than twenty years ago?"

“正是。”
"Exactly."

“我修的是六根清净，哥哥又何必再提当年之事？”
"I practice detachment of the six senses. Why bring up the past now?"

“佛家讲救人一命胜造七级浮屠，若不是为救人，我又何必再提当年之事？”
"The Buddha says saving a life is better than building a seven-tiered pagoda. If it weren't to save a life, why would I speak of it again?"

“哥哥这话说得甚是蹊跷。”
"That's a strange thing to say, brother."

邵正邺站起来，踱步走到门口，把客堂的门关好。
Shao Zhengye stood, walked to the door, and shut the guest hall tight.

“哥哥这是何意？”
"What do you mean, brother?"

邵正邺转过身：“妹妹，哥哥骗了你，你的儿子还在人世，我也没有杀死完颜吴乞买，他现在是金国的皇帝……”
Shao Zhengye turned around. "Sister, I lied to you. Your son is alive. And I didn't kill Wanyan Wobuyan. He's the emperor of Jin now..."

突如其来的消息的确令邵月婷震惊，她紧紧捏住手中的佛珠，努力用平静的表情压服内心的情绪：“哥哥既然骗我，为何不继续欺骗？却要道出实情？”
The news stunned Shao Youting. She gripped her prayer beads, forcing her face to stay calm while her heart churned. "If you lied, brother, why not keep lying? Why tell me the truth?"

“我刚说过是为救人。”
"I just said: to save a life."

“与我何干？”
"What has that to do with me?"

“你的儿子现在是金国小王爷，名唤完颜池，他多次来临安找亲生娘亲，都被我打跑，有一次他甚至说只要能见娘亲一面，哪怕即刻死了也心甘情愿……”
"Your son is a young Jin prince now, named Wanyan Chi. He's come to Lin'an more than once looking for his birth mother, and each time I drove him away. Once he said that if he could only see his mother for a moment, he'd die content..."

眼泪从邵月婷的眼角滚落下来。
Tears rolled down Shao Youting's cheeks.

“仪王所爱之人被完颜池抓去，他想尽办法都无法从完颜池手中夺回爱人，才求到邵家镖局，跪在我面前。我被感动，更不希望身上流淌着邵家血液的人干出伤天害理之事，才决定说出实情，”
"The woman Prince Yi loves was seized by Wanyan Chi. He's tried every means and can't win her back, so he came to the Shao escort compound and knelt before me. I was moved. And I didn't want a man with Shao blood in his veins doing something so cruel. That's why I decided to tell you the truth."

“哥哥想让我做什么？”
"What does my brother want me to do?"

“跟仪王去趟燕京，见见完颜池，劝他放人。”
"Go with Prince Yi to Yanjing. See Wanyan Chi. Persuade him to let her go."

邵月婷脸上挂着泪痕沉默不语。
Shao Youting sat silent, tear streaks on her face.

申简辰豁出去，起身跪下，直接给邵月婷磕三个响头：“求法师帮我。”
Shen Jianchen threw caution aside. He stood, knelt, and knocked his head three times against the floor. "I beg you, Reverend, help me."

邵月婷略微平静一会儿，站起来：“我乃修行之人，向来不问世俗，仪王还是另寻他法，告辞。”
Shao Youting composed herself after a moment and stood. "I've left the world behind. I don't involve myself in secular matters. Prince Yi must find another way. Good day."

邵月婷既不顾仪王跪着，也不顾哥哥挡在面前，绕过邵正邺，推开客堂的门，走了出去，刚跨出门，忍了许久的泪水喷涌而出。
She paid no heed to the kneeling prince or to her brother blocking the way. She walked around Shao Zhengye, pushed open the door, and stepped out. The moment she crossed the threshold, the tears she'd held back for so long poured out.

邵正邺扶起仪王：“妹妹怨我欺骗了她，等她消气后自然会答应。”
Shao Zhengye helped Prince Yi up. "My sister resents my deception. Once her anger cools, she'll agree."

申简辰倍感惭愧：“为了我的事害邵当家不得已说出实情，我深感愧疚，这份恩情我会牢记在心。”
Shen Jianchen was filled with shame. "I'm sorry that my affair forced you to speak out. I'll remember this kindness all my life."

“仪王言重，瞒着妹妹二十多年对她太不公平，我早已想将实情告知，苦于没有好的契机，仪王所求之事算是让我找到借口。”
"Prince Yi, you honor me. Keeping the truth from my sister for twenty years was unfair to her. I've long wanted to tell her. I just lacked the right moment. Your request gave me the excuse."

“邵当家不担心与金人扯上关系连累邵家？”
"Aren't you afraid ties to the Jin will bring trouble on the Shao family?"

“当然怕。金人攻打大宋，多少大宋官员不战而降，邵家的事与他们比起来不值一提，他们都不怕百姓唾骂，我邵家更没资格怕。何况说出真相是为救人，若百姓知道说不定还会夸赞邵家义薄云天。”
"Of course I am. When the Jin attacked Song, how many Song officials surrendered without a fight? What our family did is nothing beside theirs. They weren't afraid of the people's scorn, so how can the Shaos claim to be? Besides, telling the truth is to save a life. If the people knew, they might even praise the Shao family for its loyalty."

“邵当家如此想，我会心安些。”
"If you see it that way, I can rest easier."

两人来到尼姑庵门外，邵正邺打算下山，申简辰则立足不前。
They came out to the convent gate. Shao Zhengye meant to head down the mountain, but Shen Jianchen stayed put.

“殿下先下山，等妹妹想通自然会答应。”
"Go down first, Your Highness. Once my sister has thought it through, she'll agree."

“也不知净悟法师何日能想通？我急着赶去燕京救人，必须今日得到答复。邵当家尽管离去，我要留在尼姑庵外耐心等待，希望用我的诚心打动法师。”
"When will Reverend Jingwu think it through? I must reach Yanjing to save her, and I need an answer today. Go back without me. I'll wait here at the gate and hope my sincerity moves her."

“唉，没有其他更好的办法，只能委屈仪王。”
"Ah. There's no better way. I'll leave Prince Yi to it."

“邵当家的忙已帮，剩下的交给我，你尽管下山。”
"You've done your part, Master Shao. Leave the rest to me and go down."

正是夕阳西下之时，尼姑庵的大门上染上夕阳的余晖，申简辰坐在门口的台阶上，享受着夕阳的温度。千里寻还活着，他的人生一下子充满希望。
The sun was setting. Its last light stained the convent gate. Shen Jianchen sat on the stone steps, warming himself in the glow. Qianli Xun was alive. His life was suddenly full of hope.

蔡靖将他骗的好苦，等到了燕京定要当面斥责。
Cai Jing had deceived him bitterly. When he reached Yanjing, he would confront the man to his face.

转念一想，要不是蔡靖欺骗他，他又如何能在陈浩宇面前表现得真实，让他相信他的娘子已不在，然后另觅新欢，现在连孩子都有了。
Then he thought again: if Cai Jing hadn't lied, how could he have acted so convincingly before Chen Haoyu, letting him believe his wife was gone and find someone new—a woman who now carried his child?

也许一切冥冥中注定，蔡靖欺骗的行为不过是为他清理掉障碍而已。神柳下女尼的话传入耳边：“守得云开见月明”，“随缘而不攀缘”。
Perhaps it was all fated. Cai Jing's lie had merely cleared the path for him. The nun under the willow came to mind: "When the clouds part, the moon shines," and "Follow fate, don't chase it."

那时的他看不到任何希望，无法理解女尼所说。回头想想过往的经历，猛然顿悟，人生只有随缘才可跨越一道道坎儿，最终拨云见日。
Back then he had seen no hope and could not grasp her words. Looking back now, it struck him: only by following fate could a man cross one hurdle after another and finally see the clouds break.

天黑时，小尼姑前来关门，见台阶上坐着个人，上前问好：“阿弥陀佛，施主为何还不下山？”
At dusk the young nun came to close the gate. Seeing someone on the steps, she went over and greeted him. "Amitabha. Why hasn't the benefactor gone down?"

申简辰起身有礼道：“请小师傅转达净悟法师，我在此等候她回话。”
Shen Jianchen rose and bowed. "Please tell Reverend Jingwu I'm waiting here for her answer."

小尼姑叹口气关上大门，将申简辰在外之事告知慈空，慈空又到山洞中说给住持。
The young nun sighed, shut the gate, and told Cikong about the man outside. Cikong went to the cave and told the abbess.

净光法师说：“请净悟到山洞来一趟。”
Reverend Jingguang said, "Ask Jingwu to come to the cave."

慈空出去不久，邵月婷独自一人进入山洞内，上了石炕，盘腿而坐。
Cikong left. Soon Shao Youting entered the cave alone, climbed onto the stone bed, and sat cross-legged.

“师姐唤我何事？”
"What does Sister want with me?"

净光法师边盘佛珠边说：“听说今日邵家人来看你，走时留下一位年轻人，他一直守在门外等你回话。”
Reverend Jingguang fingered her beads. "I hear your family came to see you today. They left a young man behind. He's been outside the gate ever since, waiting for your answer."

“该说的我已说，师姐不必为此费心。”
"I've said all I have to say. Sister needn't trouble herself."

“各人造下的业由个人承受，我自然不必费心。既然你唤我一声师姐，我有义务提醒你，出家人修行是要消去累生累世所造之业。既然业已找上门来，没有理由拒之门外，应该想方设法了结，方可清净。”
"Each man bears his own karma. You're right, I needn't. But since you call me sister, it's my duty to remind you: a nun practices to burn away the karma of this life and lives before. Now karma has come to your door. You can't shut it out. You must find a way to settle it, and only then will you be clean."

“师姐有所不知，我本以为自己的孩子不在人世，才看破红尘出家为尼，现在却知道他还活着，我怕见到他，二十多年修行的功夫化为泡影。”
"Sister doesn't understand. I thought my child was dead. That's why I saw through the world and became a nun. Now I learn he's alive. I'm afraid that if I see him, twenty years of practice will crumble."

“我问你，可放得下男女之情？”
"Tell me this: can you let go of desire between man and woman?"

“早已放下。”
"Long ago."

“最难的已做到，其他的不在话下，要相信自己。”
"The hardest is done. The rest is nothing. Trust yourself."

“我以为最难放下的是母子之情。”
"I thought the hardest was a mother's love for her child."

“既然难放下不如勇敢面对，面对了才真正能放下，总是躲避，又如何消去业障？”
"If it's hard to let go, face it. Only by facing it can you truly let go. Keep avoiding it, and how will you clear your karma?"

“我怕……”
"I'm afraid..."

“修行之人没有畏惧之心。”
"A person in practice knows no fear."

邵月婷被说服，终于答应：“师姐的境界师妹难及，一番开悟令我茅塞顿开，我这就让人告诉门外的王爷，同意随他前去救人。”
Shao Youting was won over. At last she agreed. "Sister, I can't match your understanding. Your words have opened my eyes. I'll tell the prince outside the gate that I'll go with him to save her."

净光法师露出笑容：“此业消除，师妹的境界自然会得到提升，我圆寂之后，尼姑庵可放心交到师妹手中。”
Reverend Jingguang smiled. "Once this karma is cleared, your understanding will deepen. After I pass from this world, you may take charge of the convent."

尼姑庵的大门被打开，邵月婷走出来。
The convent gate opened. Shao Youting walked out.

申简辰激动地站起来：“法师可答应与我一同前去救人？”
Shen Jianchen rose in excitement. "Reverend, will you come with me to save her?"

邵月婷点点头：“阿弥陀佛！让施主久等，明日一早请施主在山下等我。”
Shao Youting nodded. "Amitabha! I've kept you waiting. At first light tomorrow, wait for me at the foot of the mountain."

申简辰开心不已：“多谢法师！多谢法师！”
Shen Jianchen was overjoyed. "Thank you, Reverend. Thank you."

一大早两辆马车候在山下，邵月婷果不食言，收拾好包袱，跟随仪王前往燕京。
Early the next morning two carriages waited at the foot of the mountain. Shao Youting kept her word. She packed her things and set off with Prince Yi for Yanjing.

宋金的战事暂时搁置，大宋表面上恢复平静，连那位临阵逃脱的父皇听说已从镇江回到汴梁。他的回归会让本就千疮百孔的朝廷更加千疮百孔，他恐怕还没意识到几个月后汴梁也无法保住。
Fighting between Song and Jin had paused. On the surface Song was calm again. Even the emperor who had fled the battlefield—his father—was said to have returned from Zhenjiang to Bianliang. His return would make an already broken court more broken still. He likely had no idea that in a few months even Bianliang would be lost.

表面上的祥和也无法掩盖难民的数量，金军第一次南下，大宋的可怜人增加不少，一路上不断遇到因战乱失去家园之人，他们凭借双脚向南走，远离家乡乞讨生活。
The outward peace could not hide the number of refugees. The Jin's first push south had added to the ranks of Song's poor. All along the road they met families driven from home by war, walking south on foot, begging far from the places they called home.

两个难民说话时，申简辰无意中在马车上听到。
Shen Jianchen overheard two refugees talking from the carriage.

女的说：“都不打仗了，还是回去吧？到南边人生地不熟的，怎么过日子？”
The woman said, "The fighting's over. Why not go back? Down south we know nobody. How will we live?"

男的说：“头发长见识短，没听说金人不会善罢甘休吗？迟早还会打到汴梁，再回去，到时打起来刀剑无眼，吃亏的只能是老百姓。早些往南走，早些安顿下来，听我的准没错。”
The man said, "Short hair, short sight. Haven't you heard the Jin won't stop? Sooner or later they'll reach Bianliang. If we go back and fighting starts again, blades have no eyes. It's ordinary folk who get hurt. Go south early, settle early. Listen to me."

申简辰心想：“百姓看得明明白白的事，也不知朝堂的新旧官家是否明白。”
Shen Jianchen thought, "What common people see so clearly—do the court's new and old rulers see it too?"

...

路上行走了一个多月，终于来到燕京城外，城门口和城墙上均是把守的金人。
More than a month on the road, they finally reached the outskirts of Yanjing. Jin soldiers guarded the gate and lined the walls.

申简辰走下马车，用轻功跳上墙，打倒几个围攻的金兵，向上次嘲笑他的将领袭击而去，几拳将其打倒：“告诉完颜池，我带了他想见的人，他若愿意见，必须用我想要的人交换！”
Shen Jianchen stepped down, used his lightness skill to leap onto the wall, knocked down several Jin soldiers closing in, and struck the officer who had mocked him last time. A few punches brought the man down. "Tell Wanyan Chi I've brought the person he wants to see. If he'll see her, he must trade me the woman I want!"

被打伤的将领嘴上并不示弱：“你等着！小王爷来会要了你的命！”
The injured officer didn't yield in words. "Just you wait! When the young prince comes, he'll take your life!"

完颜池正在王府练习拳法，他的师傅纳河谷正在一旁指导。
Wanyan Chi was practicing boxing in the prince's residence. His master Na He Gu stood by, guiding him.

城墙上的将领在侍卫的带领下前来报告：“小王爷，大事不好，大宋的仪王又跑来要人，在城外候着。”
An officer led by a guard came to report. "Young Prince, bad news. Prince Yi of Song is here again, demanding the woman. He's waiting outside the city."

完颜池收起拳法：“真是好玩儿，他竟然还以为美人在我府中，那我就逗他一逗。”
Wanyan Chi drew back his fists. "This is fun. He still thinks the beauty is in my residence. I'll toy with him a little."

纳河谷说：“小王爷，既然大宋仪王的拳法与我创制的拳法一样，甚至很多招数比我的还精妙，不如趁此机会将他留下。”
Na He Gu said, "Young Prince, since Prince Yi of Song uses the same boxing I created—and many of his moves are finer than mine—why not take the chance to keep him here?"

“师傅既有此想法，徒儿定会满足。”完颜池又问哪位将领，“他还说了什么？”
"If that's your wish, Master, I'll see it done." Wanyan Chi turned to the officer. "What else did he say?"

将领回道：“他说他带了小王爷想见的人，小王爷若愿意见，需用他想要的人交换。”
The officer replied, "He says he's brought the person the Young Prince wants to see. If you'll see her, he wants the woman he wants in exchange."

“我想见的人？”完颜池轻声重复着，难道是他的娘亲？完颜池变得激动，“带上人随我去城外！”
"The person I want to see?" Wanyan Chi repeated under his breath. Could it be his mother? His pulse quickened. "Bring men and horses. We're going outside the city!"

陆远峰大声回道：“是！”
Lu Yuanfeng called out, "Yes!"

王府外，完颜池跳上马后，想起一事，向陆远峰吩咐：“你不必随我前去，去看着她，以防她与大宋的仪王碰上，坏我的大事。”
Outside the residence, Wanyan Chi mounted and remembered something. He turned to Lu Yuanfeng. "Don't come with me. Go and watch her. Make sure she doesn't run into Prince Yi of Song and ruin this."

“是。”陆远峰扭转马头朝另一个方向走去。
"Yes." Lu Yuanfeng turned his horse toward the other direction.
