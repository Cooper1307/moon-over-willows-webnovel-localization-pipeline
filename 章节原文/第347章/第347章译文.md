## Chapter 347: Seeking Shao Yueting at the Nunnery

Dropping straight onto his knees in Shao Zhengye's practice yard until the escort chief relented, Shen Jianchen hiked quietly up to the mountain nunnery where Shao Zhengye's sister lived under the Dharma name **Master Jingwu**.

Sitting serenely over tea until her brother bolted the parlor doors and confessed twenty years of lies—her son lived as Jurchen prince Wanyan Chi, desperately hunting for his mother, and now held Prince Yi's beloved hostage!—Shao Yueting wept silently yet stood firm as a nun refusing worldly entanglements, walking out even as Shen Jianchen kowtowed three loud times at her feet.

Refusing to leave, Shen Jianchen sat patiently on the nunnery steps through sunset until Master Jingguang summoned Master Jingwu into her meditation cave: *"Karma knocking at your door must be faced to be dissolved—running never clears a single debt."* Awakened, Shao Yueting stepped out at dusk and agreed to ride north at dawn.

Journeying a full month through swelling tides of southern refugees foreseeing a second Jin invasion, Shen Jianchen arrived outside Yanjing's walls, bounded onto the battlements knocking down guards, and shouted his challenge: *"Tell Wanyan Chi I brought the one person he longs to see—trade me the person I want!"*

Hearing the news while sparring with his martial master **Nahegu**—who urged trapping the rival master of his boxing style—Wanyan Chi's pulse spiked: *Mother?!* Sending Lu Yuanfeng off to keep Qianli Xun safely out of sight, he galloped straight to the city gate.
