## Chapter 311: The Heir Is Decided at Last

Shock rippled across the hall, leaving every face pale with disbelief.

Chen Haodong made one last desperate grab at denial. "Your Highness must be mistaken—how could I ever commit such a crime?!"

"Scarface already confessed—why bother lying?" Shen Jianchen held up the crisp banknote for all to see. "Right after leaving jail, Scarface collected one hundred and fifty taels of silver from you. Caught red-handed, he admitted taking silver to cover for you and spilled every detail of the third night of the third month last year. Clerk Han, read his confession aloud."

"Yes, Your Highness." Han Gu stepped forward. "According to Scarface, he regularly sold news of Second Young Master's outings to Chen Haodong for extra silver. On the third day of the third month last year, the moment Haoyu left the Chen country estate, Scarface ran straight to a private cottage nearby where Chen Haodong kept brothel trysts. Seeing his opening, Haodong hurried back into town and had Butler Wang pull every servant out on the pretext of spring-cleaning, while Scarface sent the other lackeys away and drugged the estate steward's wine. After nightfall, shrouded head to toe in a black cloak, Chen Haodong entered the villa, violated Chun Jinlan, and ordered Scarface to sell her to a Yangzhou brothel—then had Scarface spread rumors smearing Haoyu as a man who sold off abducted girls."

The strength drained out of Chen Haodong's knees. Before he could crumple on his own, Chen Jiangshu strode up and cracked a thunderous slap across his son's cheek. "You wretched animal! After all the years your uncle and I poured into raising you!"

Sprawled across the tiles, Haodong tasted the ashes of what should have been his proudest day. Squatting beside him, Chen Jiangshu clutched his head and wept—"All my fault!"—while Madam Bao threw herself onto her knees before Prince Yi begging for mercy.

Tucking the banknote away, Shen Jianchen spoke with cool finality. "These hundred and fifty taels go straight to the poor in the Judicial Office's name. A nation has its laws, a clan its rules. Chen Haodong broke Song law and must answer for Chun Jinlan's soul. Chief Constable Wen—bind him and haul him to the Judicial Office."

As constables tied Haodong and marched him out, Shen Jianchen cupped his hands in farewell and departed.

Chen Haoyu stared after them in a daze. "How on earth could it be Brother Dong?"

"Never guessed him either," Qianli Xun murmured. "Takes Prince Yi to flip the whole board upside down."

Ignoring Haoyu's puzzled blink, she watched Madam Bao sob at Old Madam Xia's knees—"Mother, save Haodong!"—until the matriarch wiped away tears and promised Chen Jianglin would spend whatever silver it took to spare his life after sentencing. Overwhelmed, Madam Bao fainted dead away; Qianli Xun darted forward and pinched her philtrum until she gasped back awake wailing her son's name, before Yao Qingyan had maids help her back to her quarters.

Bracing herself upright, Old Madam Xia resumed command. "We never foresaw today's blow, yet an election begun must finish. Two grandsons remain—which one do we choose?"

Rising from the floor, Chen Jiangshu spoke first, his voice raw with regret for ever feeding his son's toxic ambition. "Choose Haoyu, Mother. He was wild once, yet his heart has mended, and with Eldest Brother, Third Brother, and me teaching him, he'll master the business fast. Better still, we've all seen Shuzhen's true character—nothing like those street rumors."

Chen Jiangmu stood up next. "Haofei is bound for officialdom and sits the palace exam in Bianliang next spring—he's still an unmarried lad with no head for trade. Steward Qiu says Haoyu already outstrips senior shop clerks and flies through bookkeeping. Choose Haoyu!"

At Old Madam Xia's glance, Granduncle Lu huddled with the clan elders. Leaning close, Qianli Xun teased Haoyu, "No dodging fate now."

Still aching for his cousin—blaming his own foolish abduction of Jinlan for sparking the whole ruin—Haoyu shook his head. "Never dreamed the inheritance would drop on my head. If Brother Dong wanted a girl back then, all he had to do was ask me—why sneak around and sell her off?!" Catching his wife's sharp glare, he quickly added, "Back *then*, not now! With you, I'm spotless!"

"Not everyone does dirt out in the open like you used to," Qianli Xun remarked dryly. "Some folks wear saint masks in daylight and crawl through gutters at night."

Stepping forward, Granduncle Lu announced the elders' unanimous verdict: with Haofei pursuing officialdom, the mantle went to Haoyu. With a heavy heart, Chen Jianglin drafted a fresh appointment scroll; every elder pressed a red thumbprint—praying no fresh disaster walked through the door—before Chen Jianglin rolled it up and called his son forward.

Light as silk, the scroll felt heavy as stone in Haoyu's palms. "For the honor of the Chen clan, I accept this duty and swear to live up to every standard."

Rising beside him, Qianli Xun bowed to the elders. "As Chen Haoyu's wife, I give my word too: my family name is spotless, and from this day on I will watch my conduct and keep the inner household steady so my husband can devote himself wholly to the Chen enterprises."

Nodding in warm approval, Old Madam Xia wrapped up the assembly—sending Chen Jiangmu to notify Prefect Zhu and the Lin'an Guild, and Chen Jianglin to spare Haodong's neck at any price.

Under Song law, rape carried strangulation—yet Chen silver bought Haodong's life in redemption copper, leaving him locked behind prison bars for life. Devastated, Chen Jiangshu and Madam Bao fell ill for weeks, weeping anew at every prison visit over their broken son.

Rather than reflect on his sins, Haodong blamed sheer bad luck—and Prince Yi. Determined to drag the prince's mood straight into the mud alongside him, he asked the jailer to summon Prince Yi.

Fresh back from finishing the bamboo cottage in Zhuli Village, Bai Feng trailed his master into the foul-smelling jail, pinching his nose until they turned down a cleaner wing bought with Chen silver. Unlocking a cell, the jailer revealed disheveled Haodong lounging on his cot without bothering to stand.

"Since Your Highness ruined me," Haodong sneered, "I won't rest easy until I share a few secrets about Princess Consort Yi. She wanted Haoyu's wife dead—had me hire Master Jiang, try the Shao Escort Agency, and finally hand over Chen Manor's floor plan so Black-Fiend Tiger could strike! Since you love locking people up, Your Highness, why not arrest your own wife? She's obsessed—if she ever succeeds, will you behead her after the autumn assizes too?"

Shutting Bai Feng's protests down long enough to hear every poisoned syllable, Shen Jianchen turned on his heel and walked straight out to Haodong's echoing laughter, riding directly to Youlin Retreat.

There, Qiao Mengrong had just seen off a physician who diagnosed a "cold constitution" and prescribed three months of tonic to help her conceive. Beaming when Brother walked in early, her smile froze at his stony face as he dismissed Xilan and shut the door.

"Chen Haodong told me everything in prison today—"

Before he finished, knowing denial was dead, Mengrong dropped to her knees streaming tears. "I know, Brother! Hatred blinded me—I've regretted it every single day and swear I'll never touch her again! Thank heaven she wasn't hurt, or I could never live with myself!"

Face-to-face with her weeping frailty, every harsh rebuke melted off Shen Jianchen's tongue; blaming his own reckless wedding crash for breaking her, he gently lifted her up. "Knowing your wrong is enough—never pull anything like that again. Had Youqi Jushi lacked martial arts, she'd have died on Mount Tianmu—and as Judicial Investigator, I'd have traced it straight to you and enforced Song law without sparing family."
