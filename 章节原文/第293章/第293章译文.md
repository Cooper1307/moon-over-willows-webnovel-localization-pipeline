## Chapter 293: Mengrong Insists on Attending the Salon

Inside the Judicial Office's grim interrogation room, Scarface sat bound facing torture racks, turning on crocodile tears about his seventy-year-old mother—till Shen Jianchen threatened chili water!

Pinning Scarface's first lie using Aye's testimony that Jinlan had screamed in terror ("I'll kill you!"), Wen Xin dunked Scarface's head in a water basin until he gasped for air—and pulled a slick new lie out of his hat: claiming the black-cloaked rapist was **Black-Fiend Tiger** (real name Li Hu), notorious bandit chief of Mount Tianmu and Scarface's old neighbor!

Knowing Scarface was lying through his teeth to protect a rich paymaster—Han Gu noted Black-Fiend Tiger always dragged women he touched back up the mountain—Shen Jianchen played along smartly: sentencing Scarface to twenty boards and one month in jail for trafficking so they could tail him the day he walked out and catch his paymaster red-handed!

Stepping out, Shen Jianchen received Dongfang Chenze's salon invitation and rode home early to Youlin Retreat to tell Mengrong straight—hoping to go solo without hurting her feelings since the salon would publicly rebut her smear pamphlets.

Over lunch—Mengrong fibbing that Matron Zou had gone sightseeing at West Lake when she was actually meeting Chen Haodong—Shen Jianchen smoothly explained the salon wasn't a family banquet, only to hit Mengrong's laser radar: *Is Zhu Shuzhen going? Will you speak to her alone?*

Cornered by her ultimatum—"Skip it, or take me!"—Shen Jianchen sighed and caved: "Fine, I'll take you."
