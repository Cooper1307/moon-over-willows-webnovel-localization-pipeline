## Chapter 293: Mengrong Insists on Going

Inside the Judicial Office interrogation room, Shen Jianchen presided in person, determined to smoke out Chen Haodong.

Row upon row of torture gear lined the dim walls, chilling the blood at a glance. Bound to a chair with thick rope, Scarface hung his head while Han Gu poised brush and paper to take notes.

Wen Xin tapped a heavy whip against his palm. “Head up and answer His Lordship straight, or you get twenty lashes first!”

Eyeing the whip and irons, Scarface half regretted taking the fall—then pictured those hundred taels of silver and dug his heels in. Turn on Eldest Young Master Chen and he’d never see a copper!

Eyeing the prince—rumored across Lin’an as kindhearted and compassionate—Scarface tried turning on the waterworks. “Merciful Prince! I’m a poor lonely guy whose family can’t afford a wife! Seeing a pretty girl made me weak, so I slipped up with Chun Jinlan! I swear it was consensual—she loved it, just denied it hoping to snag Second Young Master! My only crime was selling her to a brothel! Punish me however you want, just spare my life—I’ve got a seventy-year-old mother to feed! Waaah!”

Cold steel rang in Shen Jianchen’s voice. “Drop the act. One more fake sob and we pour chili water down your nose.”

Scarface shut off the tears on the spot. “Ask whatever you want, Your Highness.”

“Who was the man in the black cloak?”

“Nobody! Aye made him up!”

“Aye has zero reason to lie—you, on the other hand, are spinning fairy tales.”

“Every word’s true—I’d never fool Your Highness!”

“Hmph! Two blatant lies. First, claiming it was consensual. Chun Jinlan’s dead, so she can’t face you, yet one detail blows your story sky-high. Bring Aye in.”

Wen Xin opened the door. Terrified by the torture rack, Aye stepped in with eyes glued to the floor.

Shen Jianchen softened his voice. “Don’t be scared. Tell me what Chun Jinlan’s screams sounded like that night.”

Puzzled, Aye replied, “Just... loud screaming...”

Scarface cut in, “Having fun with me, of course she screamed—”

Wen Xin flicked the whip. “One more peep and you taste leather!” Scarface clamped shut.

“What I mean,” Shen Jianchen clarified, “did she sound like she was enjoying herself, or terrified and furious?”

Aye got it at once. “Terrified and furious! I’ll never forget that voice—she was shrieking, ‘I’ll kill you!’”

“Why didn’t you say that in court?”

Aye shrank back. “Your Lordship... didn’t ask that detailed...”

*Blaming me now?* “All right, you may go.”

Opening the door for Aye, Wen Xin hid a grin—obviously the prince couldn’t grill a maid over bedroom noises in open court. Shutting the door again, he caught the prince’s look and signaled constables to bring in a tub of water.

Untying Scarface, Wen Xin dragged him to the tub and shoved his head straight under. Scarface held out a few seconds before lungs burned and he thrashed, gasping as Wen Xin hauled him up. “Mercy! I’ll talk! I’ll talk!”

Kicking the backs of his knees to drop him before Prince Yi, Wen Xin barked, “Talk straight!”

Confess to rape and he swung; rat out Chen Haodong and bye-bye hundred taels. Thinking fast, Scarface pulled a scapegoat out of thin air. “Fine—it wasn’t consensual, yet I didn’t touch her either! It was... it was...”

“Denying the cloaked man was lie number two! Out with it—who was he?”

“Black-Fiend Tiger!”

Shen Jianchen blinked. “Black-Fiend Tiger?”

“Bandit chief up on Mount Tianmu,” Wen Xin explained. “Leads a mob burning, looting, and murdering.”

Unconvinced, Shen Jianchen snapped, “Dragging in a mountain bandit to fool me? Fetch the thorn switches!”

Worse than paddles—Scarface scrambled to sell his lie. “Every word’s true! Black-Fiend Tiger’s real name is Li Hu—used to be my neighbor before he ran afoul of the law and turned bandit! On the first of the third month last year he slipped down the mountain, hid at my place, and demanded I get him a woman. Knowing the Chens’ country house sat empty on the third, I drugged the caretaker and snuck him in—he wore the black cloak so nobody’d spot him! Black-Fiend Tiger defiled Chun Jinlan, not me!”

*Brilliant!* Li Hu really had been his old neighbor and really had dropped by once. Blame an untouchable mountain bandit, keep Chen Haodong spotless, walk out alive, and pocket a hundred taels!

Skeptical, Shen Jianchen pressed, “Then why hide his name earlier?”

Scarface’s forehead kissed the floor. “Terrified you’d charge me with colluding with bandits!”

Airtight—zero thread linking back to Chen Haodong. Shen Jianchen had to hand it to Eldest Young Master Chen: not a single loose end, even holding a greedy rat like Scarface in check. Clearly Haodong had dangled a fat payout.

No point chasing ghosts up Mount Tianmu right away—better lock Scarface up a spell, turn him loose, and tail him straight to the silver payoff to catch Chen Haodong red-handed.

“If Black-Fiend Tiger did it, I’ll muster Lin’an’s garrison to wipe out Mount Tianmu! As his accomplice—plus trafficking a free citizen—you escape death yet not punishment: twenty strokes of the paddle and one month in jail.”

Collapsed on the floor, Scarface exhaled—alive! Once he got out, he’d hit Eldest Young Master up for extra hazard pay!

Once constables hauled Scarface out, Wen Xin frowned. “Chen Haodong stinks to high heaven, Your Highness—are we letting it drop here?”

Han Gu agreed. “Rumor says Black-Fiend Tiger drags every woman he touches back up Mount Tianmu. If true, Scarface is lying—the cloaked man is Chen Haodong.”

“Dead right,” Shen Jianchen confirmed. “Once Scarface gets out in a month, we put a tail on him. No way Chen Haodong stays clean.”

Both lit up. “Brilliant plan, Your Highness!”

Shen Jianchen added half-joking, half-grave, “Song penalties for human trafficking are way too soft—ought to carry the death penalty. Still, today it works for us: keeping Scarface alive gives us bait to reel in the big fish.”

Neither official commented on imperial law. Walking out of the interrogation room, a constable handed over an invitation card from Young Master Dongfang.

Opening it, Shen Jianchen chuckled—he had funded the salon and nearly forgot amid the case! He didn’t want to bring Mengrong tomorrow, yet hiding it would blow up worse if she heard later. With work slow, he headed back to Youlin Retreat at noon to tell her straight.

Surprised to see him home so early—a first since taking the Judicial Office post—Qiao Mengrong lit up, then noticed Bai Feng still missing. “Home early means work’s light—why didn’t Bai Feng come back with you?”

Shen Jianchen teased, “Now my feelings are hurt.”

“Why?”

“I walk through the door and all you ask about is Bai Feng—not one word about me?”

She dropped Bai Feng at once. “Don’t be silly—my heart’s all yours! Only asked because I worry nobody’s tending you.”

Taking her hand, he led her over. “Sit—got something to tell you.”

“Just in time for lunch—let’s eat and talk.”

Xilan had maids lay the table. Sitting beside her, Shen Jianchen asked, “Where’s Matron Zou?”

Matron Zou had gone to Yongtai Teahouse to see Chen Haodong, yet Qiao Mengrong covered smooth. “Still new to Lin’an and enchanted by the scenery—went out to West Lake this morning, won’t be back till dark.”

Letting it pass, Shen Jianchen ladled her a bowl of nourishing soup and pulled out the invitation. “Young Master Dongfang sent this for tomorrow’s poetry salon.”

Her eyes sparkled as she took it. “Taking me along, Brother?”

Seeing her excitement made his words catch. Choosing them with care, he said, “This salon’s different—strictly literary figures and Lin’an officials, not a social banquet. Bringing wives isn’t proper.”

Her smile vanished on the spot. *So that’s why he came home early—to ditch me!* Shutting the card, she handed it back. “If wives aren’t allowed, why even show me?”

“Young Master Dongfang aims to make his salon number one in Lin’an, so he’s limiting invites to poets and officials.”

She pouted. “Dongfang Chenze is just an ordinary gentleman—how’d he pull in every poet and official in town? Everybody’s going for your face, aren’t they?”

Caught dead-on, he admitted, “I put up the silver to back him.”

Suspicion sharpened her gaze. “You’re friendly with him, sure, not close enough to bankroll him like that. Why go to such lengths?”

Admitting the salon aimed to clear Zhu Shuzhen’s name over the pamphlets would detonate a bomb, so he told a white lie. “You know me—soft touch when friends beg.”

“All the more reason to take me! Young Master Dongfang wouldn’t dare mind.”

“Local poetry salons have strict traditions, Mengrong—even more so for me, I can’t be the one breaking rules. Try to understand.”

Unable to fault the logic, she picked up a piece of chicken with her chopsticks and set it in his bowl. “Is she going?”

Playing dumb, he asked, “Who?”

“Zhu Shuzhen.”

Setting a morsel in her bowl in return, he kept his face calm. “Most likely—she is Jiangnan’s poetess.”

Sipping her soup, Qiao Mengrong asked, “Will you talk to her alone there, Brother?”

He hesitated half a beat too long.

“Silence means yes?”

“Stop imagining things, Mengrong—we’ve consummated our marriage, and my heart is devoted to you.”

“I trust you, Brother—I don’t trust her. Either skip the salon, or take me with you.”

Cornered with no way out, Shen Jianchen gave in. “Fine. I’ll take you.”
