## Chapter 312: Time Flies Like a White Steed

A fresh pang stabbed Mengrong, bringing thicker tears. "Don't pin Mount Tianmu solely on me, Brother! Master Jiang's wife—that ex-courtesan Chun Jinmo—hated Zhu Shuzhen for taking Chen Haoyu and hiked up Mount Tianmu herself to recruit Black-Fiend Tiger! I only got foolish and asked Chen Haodong for the blueprint!"

The second Master Jiang's wife came up, Shen Jianchen dropped everything and strode straight out, shouting for Bai Feng—leaving Mengrong slumping onto the daybed knowing Brother still dropped her flat the instant Zhu Shuzhen's name appeared.

When Xilan stepped in urging her to stop going after the poetess, Mengrong wiped her staged tears coldly. "Drop it? Look how fast he sprinted off to Master Jiang for her! No more half-baked pawns—next time I pick the right blade and finish her clean. Fetch brush and paper—I'm writing to Imperial Attendant Liang in Bianliang."

Over at Master Jiang's waterfront residence, a month locked behind boarded windows had forced Chun Jinmo to face reality: Haoyu was gone forever, leaving Master Jiang as her sole safe harbor. Summoning her husband, she knelt weeping sincere repentance and swore to be a loyal wife—melting Master Jiang's heart right as he lifted her up—

*WHAM!* A fist slammed between Master Jiang's shoulder blades, sending him sprawling!

Whirling around to find furious Prince Yi towering over him, Master Jiang dropped straight to his knees beside trembling Jinmo. Hearing the prince list the Willow ambush and Mount Tianmu, Jiang rushed to explain he'd sent weaklings on purpose to spare Youqi Jushi, while Jinmo sobbed out full confession of climbing Mount Tianmu behind her husband's back—already beaten and locked away a full month for it!

Seeing their genuine terror and knowing Qianli Xun was safe cooled Shen Jianchen's rage. "My fists don't hit women outside a fair spar—yet if your wife ever plots harm again, I won't guarantee she won't taste them!"

Once the prince left, Master Jiang pulled weeping Jinmo into his arms, cheering her up with warm promises of a good life and a chubby son together.

Riding home, Shen Jianchen resolved to wipe the slate clean and cherish Mengrong so she'd never stray dark again—putting off visiting his brand-new bamboo cottage (asking Bai Feng to let Kujue watch over it) and scrapping plans to invite Haoyu and Shuzhen.

Calm months glided past. As the heir's wife, Qianli Xun studied household management under Yao Qingyan while turning out hit huaben stories now printed by bookshops and hailed across town—even weaving Ye Anya and Cao Ban's elopement into a tender tale. Haoyu threw himself into learning Chen trade under his father and uncles while still whisking daily foam-art tea for his wife. At Youlin Retreat, Mengrong sipped fertility tonics daily while Brother came home early and took her boating on West Lake—though no reply came from Bianliang, leaving her quietly waiting.

Past Mid-Autumn, both Miao Zhiyu and Xiaguo grew pregnant, Black-Fiend Tiger met the executioner's blade, and Lazybones Village's cottages finished—ending homelessness across Lin'an as the villagers thrived selling fresh and dried wild greens, letting the Chen congee shed close to citywide cheers for Prince Yi.

Then an imperial edict dropped like a thunderbolt: Prefect Zhu Xiyan was abruptly forced into early retirement and replaced by **Li Ke**, a crony of Imperial Attendant Liang!

Calling on Zhu Xiyan, Shen Jianchen learned the stark truth: refusing to skim silver or kiss Liang Shicheng's boots had made Zhu Xiyan the warning sacrifice. Resigning his own minor yamen post on his father's advice, Zhu Jingyuan greeted Qianli Xun when she hurried home to comfort her parents—finding her father surprisingly serene, letting all his house songstresses go with a full year's pay (thrilling Madam Xiang!) so he could retire to writing poetry.

"Honestly, Father, stepping down now is a blessing," Qianli Xun cracked lightly. "In a few years, the man sitting on that Dragon Throne might end up a prisoner himself—why slave for him?"

Alarmed, Zhu Xiyan and Zhu Jingyuan shushed her immediately—new Prefect Li Ke answered straight to Liang Shicheng and ignored even Prince Yi; one loose whisper could drag both the Zhu and Chen clans onto the chopping block!
