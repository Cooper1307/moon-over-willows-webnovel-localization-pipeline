## Chapter 176: Prince Yi Wants a Pig to Climb a Tree

申简辰走出闺房小院后，并未回自己屋中，带着白风往神柳处走去。
当白风在院门外听到王爷向郡主说的话后，既担心郡主，又可怜王爷。
他觉得王爷身份尊贵地位又高，却也有比不上自己的地方。他能与喜欢的人在一起，王爷却不能。
白风边跟随王爷穿越幽林，边琢磨王爷与幽栖居士的未来。怎么琢磨都只有一个结果，两人根本没机会在一起。
既然没有机会在一起，王爷又何必说出实情？搞得郡主难过。
白风仔细回想，到底王爷什么时候喜欢上幽栖居士？他猜测大概是王爷知道幽栖居士是千里寻时。唉，王爷也真是，没必要因为喜欢千里寻的话本，就喜欢上幽栖居士。
白风想来想去还是不明白，王爷自从知道幽栖居士就是千里寻后，没怎么听过说书，说他喜欢千里寻的话本好像也不对。难道王爷知道幽栖居士就是千里寻后，对她这个人更感兴趣？
以前白风认为王爷不近女色，现在看来，他完全错误，王爷是想家中红旗不倒，外面彩旗飘飘，现在喜欢上幽栖居士，谁知道以后又会喜欢上谁。郡主岂不是每次都要伤心一回，真可怜。
白风一路想着，已跟王爷来到神柳旁。
神柳的叶子长出许多，上面零零散散挂上红绸。夜色下，看不清红绸的颜色，能看到在夜晚春风的吹拂下，红绸随风摆动的姿态。
今晚不是满月，月亮的光线暗淡许多，还时不时被云遮住。
向梦容说出心里话之后，申简辰感到前所未有的轻松。
尽管婚约还在，尽管他必须娶梦容，可话说开后，身上的一道枷锁算是解开，自然会轻松许多。
他看着神柳，夜色下婆娑如画，回想着穿越前自由自在追求千里寻的过程，竟然发现回忆如此美。
包括千里寻说的话也显得美妙无比：“除非猪能上树，老娘才会答应嫁给你，老娘说到做到。”
申简辰露出笑容，心中想：“你既如此说，我便想方设法让猪爬上神柳，到时看你还如何抵赖。”
“白风！”
“在。”
Leaving Mengrong's courtyard, Shen Jianchen did not return to his own chambers, instead leading Bai Feng out toward the Divine Willow.

“交给你个任务。”
Having overheard his master's confession outside the gate, Bai Feng both pitied the princess and felt sorry for his prince. For all his master's royal blood and lofty rank, he was actually worse off than his own guard: Bai Feng could be with the girl he loved, while the prince could not.

“王爷请说。”
Following his master through the shadowy woods, Bai Feng mulled over Prince Yi and Youqi Jushi's odds and hit the same dead end every time: zero chance. If they could never be together, why blurt out the truth and break the princess's heart? When had His Highness even fallen for Youqi Jushi? Probably when he learned she was Qianli Xun. Really, why fall for a woman just because you enjoyed her huaben stories? No—wait—ever since finding out Youqi Jushi was Qianli Xun, His Highness had barely visited a single storyteller! So it was not the stories after all; maybe he had just gotten hooked on the woman herself. Bai Feng used to think his prince had zero interest in women; turns out he wanted a loyal wife holding the fort at home while chasing bright flags outside—Youqi Jushi today, who knew who tomorrow? Poor Princess Jiahe, doomed to weep again and again.

“搜罗聪明一些的猪，以后你每日抽时间到神柳下训练猪爬树。”
Lost in thought, Bai Feng arrived with his master beneath the Divine Willow. Fresh leaves draped the boughs, dotted here and there with red ribbons whose color melted into the dark as they fluttered on the spring night breeze. Half-moon light grew dim whenever clouds drifted past.

听到这个任务，白风直接傻眼，王爷该不会脑子有问题！难道没听说猪不能爬树的道理？
Having spoken his mind to Mengrong, Shen Jianchen felt lighter than he had in months. The betrothal remained and he would still have to wed her, yet laying the truth on the table had snapped one heavy chain off his shoulders.

“王爷，恐怕我做不到，猪天生不会爬树，训练也没用。”
Gazing up at the swaying willow in the dark, he found himself smiling over memories of chasing Qianli Xun back in their own world—even her blunt brush-off sounded sweet now: "When pigs can climb trees, I will marry you—and I mean every word!"

“世上无难事，只怕有心人。大不了我答应以后多给你些银子，再允许你随时去看苦荞。”
A sly grin tugged at his mouth. Since you laid down that condition, let us see how you wriggle out once I get a pig up the Divine Willow!

王爷的诱惑倒符合白风心意，他不好意思起来：“王爷让我做事，我自然不会拒绝，您没必要诱惑我。”
"Bai Feng!"

“这么说，你答应了？”
"Here, Your Highness."

“王爷对我好，我怎么可能拒绝？只是训练猪爬树，从未有人做过，只怕结果不如人愿。”
"I have a mission for you."

“答应了，就去做，别想那么多。”
"At your command, Your Highness."

“我能不能多问一句？”
"Hunt down a smart pig. Starting tomorrow, spend part of every day here under the Divine Willow training it to climb this tree."

“问吧。”
Bai Feng's jaw dropped. Had His Highness lost his mind? Everybody knew pigs could not climb trees! "Your Highness, I doubt I can pull that off. Pigs are not built to climb—training will not help."

“王爷为何要让猪爬树？”
"Nothing is impossible to a determined heart. Tell you what: I will raise your silver allowance and let you go visit Kuqiao whenever you please."

申简辰想了一会儿，回道：“我只是想让不可能的事变成可能而已。”
The bait struck dead center, leaving Bai Feng sheepish. "When Your Highness gives an order, I never refuse—no need to bribe me."

白风虽不大理解王爷的心思，也不好再追问：“那好吧，我就跟着王爷疯狂一回，做一次别人没做过的事情。”
"So that is a yes?"

申简辰很满意，又转向神柳，用心与它沟通：“神柳，你一定要帮我，也要保佑我与千里寻最终能走到一起。”
"Your Highness treats me well; how could I say no? Only nobody has ever trained a pig to climb a tree, so do not hold your breath on results."

话说陈浩宇要求千里寻的手下护送他，半道上还真把蔣爷给吓跑，他高兴得不行。
"You said yes, so get to it and stop overthinking."

马车到达正街时，陈浩宇从马车的窗户瞅见疤子带着一群小弟向他走来，才允许望月轩的下人离开。
"May I ask one question?"

红豆很不爽，二话不说，带人撤退。
"Go ahead."

疤子来到马车旁问：“陈公子，刚才那群人是谁？”
"Why does Your Highness want a pig climbing a tree?"

陈浩宇探出个脑袋：“问那么多干吗？赶紧的，你去帮本公子把陆远峰叫来。”
Shen Jianchen paused a beat. "I just want to turn the impossible into the possible."

疤子笑嘻嘻地说：“陈公子，这跑路费……”
Still bewildered yet knowing better than to pry further, Bai Feng shrugged. "Alright then—I will go crazy right alongside Your Highness and try what nobody has ever tried."

“本公子什么时候在钱方面含糊过？快去快回！”
Well pleased, Shen Jianchen turned back to the willow and spoke in his heart: Help me out here, Divine Willow, and see Qianli Xun and me together at last.

疤子小跑着离开，陈浩宇也走下马车，吩咐车夫回陈府不要等他，便带着小弟们边逛街边向春香楼而去。
Meanwhile, after Chen Haoyu borrowed Qianli Xun's servants to escort him, the squad really had scared Master Jiang off along the road, leaving him thrilled. Only once his carriage reached the main avenue and he spotted Scarface approaching with a pack of lackeys did he wave Wangyue Pavilion's crew off; disgusted, Hongdou led her team straight home without a word.

到达春香楼后，自然由春金沫陪伴，看了歌舞，玩一会儿打马，等陆远峰来时天已黑。
"Who was that crowd, Young Master Chen?" Scarface asked, jogging up to the carriage.

这位陆远峰正是在竹林与千里寻对决的高手，陈浩宇之所以把他请来，无非是怕蔣爷再到春香楼找他麻烦。
Poking his head out the window, Chen Haoyu snapped, "Quit asking questions! Go fetch Lu Yuanfeng for me on the double!"

陆远峰愿意帮陈浩宇是因他出的银子多，有钱赚何乐而不为？
Scarface rubbed his hands with a cheeky grin. "About the errand fee..."

在疤子的带领下陆远峰穿梭过热闹的一楼，顺着楼梯爬上二层，来到春金沫的房间外，疤子敲了敲门：“陈公子，我把陆远峰给您带来。”
"When have I ever been stingy with silver? Move!"

陈浩宇与春金沫刚好亲热完，春金沫帮他整理好衣装，人模狗样地坐在桌旁。
Scarface darted off. Climbing down, Chen Haoyu sent his driver back to Chen Manor and strolled toward Chunxiang Brothel with his cronies.

春金沫把门打开，也坐回桌旁嗑瓜子。
Inside, Chun Jinmo kept him company through song, dance, and a few rounds of dama—a Song board game—until night fell and Lu Yuanfeng arrived. This was the same martial expert who had sparred with Qianli Xun in the bamboo grove; Chen Haoyu had hired him purely to keep Master Jiang from crashing the brothel, and with generous silver on the table, Lu Yuanfeng had jumped at the work.

疤子带着陆远峰走进屋，瞧一眼春金沫，真是秀色可餐，难怪陈公子现在喜欢往春香楼跑。
Weaving through the noisy ground floor, Scarface led Lu Yuanfeng up to the second floor and knocked on Chun Jinmo's door. "Young Master Chen, I brought Lu Yuanfeng!"

疤子伸出手，一脸猥琐：“陈公子。”
Having just finished fooling around with Chun Jinmo, Chen Haoyu let her straighten his robes before sitting primly at the table. Chun Jinmo opened the door, then returned to cracking melon seeds.

陈浩宇掏出一锭银子，放在疤子手中：“滚！”
Stepping inside with Lu Yuanfeng, Scarface stole an appreciative look at Chun Jinmo—a real dish, no wonder Young Master Chen lived at the brothel these days—and held out a greedy palm. "Young Master Chen?"

疤子收好银子，眉开眼笑：“这就滚。”
Chen Haoyu slapped a silver ingot into his hand. "Scram!"

陈浩宇又拿出一袋银子，放在桌上：“陆远峰，这袋银子是你的，从现在开始，你要跟我形影不离，负责保护我。”
"Scramming right now!" Scarface pocketed it with a wide grin.

陆远峰倒不客气，伸手拿起桌上的一袋银子，塞进怀里：“有银子赚，好说话，以后有我在，没人敢欺负陈公子。”
Next, Chen Haoyu set a heavy pouch of silver on the table. "This pouch is yours, Lu Yuanfeng. Starting right now, stay glued to my side and keep me safe."

“好！要的就是你这句话。”
Without standing on ceremony, Lu Yuanfeng tucked the pouch into his sash. "Good silver buys good work. With me around, nobody lays a finger on Young Master Chen."

陈浩宇话音刚落，从楼下传来蔣爷的声音：“陈浩宇！还不快滚下来！别当缩头乌龟！爷爷我今日打得你服服帖帖！”
"Now that is what I like to hear!"

春金沫没有心情再继续嗑瓜子：“陈公子，该怎么办？”
Hardly had the words landed when Master Jiang's roar shook the floorboards from downstairs: "Chen Haoyu! Get your ass down here! Quit hiding like a turtle in its shell! Tonight I beat you into submission!"

有高手在一旁，陈浩宇自然不紧张：“放轻松，你在楼上安心待着，我下楼会会蔣爷。”
Chun Jinmo froze mid-seed. "What do we do, Young Master Chen?"

春金沫叮嘱道：“蔣爷是黑道中人，杀人不眨眼，你可要当心。”
With a master fighter right beside him, Chen Haoyu stayed cool. "Relax. Stay safe upstairs while I go meet Master Jiang."

陈浩宇虽谈不上有多喜欢春金沫，但她是目前为止让他最满意的人，既不像外面的黄花大姑娘扭扭捏捏，也不像春香楼其他商妓往谁的怀里都钻，对他一心一意，自然激起陈浩宇保护的欲望。
"Master Jiang runs the underworld and kills without blinking—be careful!"

“放心，有高手在，蔣爷算个球！”
Though Chen Haoyu was not head over heels for Chun Jinmo, she suited him better than anyone he had met—neither coy and prissy like sheltered maidens nor throwing herself into every customer's lap like other brothel girls. Her loyalty sparked a rare protective streak in him. "Relax. With a master here, Master Jiang does not amount to squat!"
