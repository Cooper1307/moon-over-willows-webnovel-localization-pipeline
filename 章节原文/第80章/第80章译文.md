# Chapter 80: Catching a Mistake Brings Joy

---

Chen Haoyu slept soundly and had a wonderful dream.

---

In the dream, he became a martial arts master. He fought Zhu Shuzhen, punched her to the ground, grabbed her clothes at the chest with one hand, and raised his fist with the other: "Zhu Shuzhen, do you admit defeat?"

---

Zhu Shuzhen cried and begged: "Master Chen! Grandfather Chen! Please spare me! I'll never dare again!"...

---

How satisfying! Chen Haoyu couldn't help but laugh out loud in his dream.

---

Just as he was laughing happily, he heard someone calling him: "Young Master Chen! Young Master Chen! Wake up quickly!"

---

Chen Haoyu opened his blurry eyes and found it was Cao Ban calling him. It turned out what he just experienced was only a dream.

---

Cao Ban said: "While you were sleeping just now, you were snoring and laughing loudly, disturbing everyone. If Young Master Chen is sleepy, you might as well go home and sleep."

---

Chen Haoyu rubbed his eyes and looked around, finding everyone staring at him. He said embarrassedly: "Please don't mind, everyone. It's just that your discussion of poetry was so fascinating that I was immersed in it and couldn't extricate myself. I accidentally fell asleep."

---

An Mingxuan leaned close to Chen Haoyu's ear: "There's no need for Young Master Chen to explain. Everyone knows what happened. You might as well go back. This place isn't suitable for you."

---

Having not yet heard Zhu Shuzhen's new ci poem, how could Chen Haoyu leave?

---

"Don't worry. I won't fall asleep again."

---

Dongfang Chenze stepped forward to ease the awkwardness: "Everyone! Continue! Continue! The fact that even Young Master Chen could attend the poetry gathering proves its wide influence."

---

Lin Zixiao also said: "Lord Sheng, Young Master Dongfang is right. Let's continue. No need to pay attention to Young Master Chen."

---

Lord Sheng was the organizer of this poetry gathering. He was nearly seventy years old, with white hair and beard, but he looked spirited.

---

"Just now, everyone was so absorbed in appreciating Dongpo Jushi's ci that they didn't notice Young Master Chen was here. If not for the snoring and laughter drowning out the voices of poetry appreciation, I wouldn't have known Young Master Chen would attend the poetry gathering. Hehe...! As Young Master Dongfang said, this old man's poetry gathering is quite attractive. Hehe...!"

---

The half-praising, half-mocking words made Chen Haoyu very unhappy. He picked up a cup of tea and pretended to drink, thinking: "What attraction! If I didn't need to deal with Zhu Shuzhen, I wouldn't bother coming to such a boring place!"

---

Lord Sheng continued: "Everyone, the segment of appreciating classic ci has ended. Now we enter the new ci recommendation segment. If anyone has obtained new ci poems recently, please recommend them for everyone to appreciate."

---

Chen Haoyu's spirits soared. He said to Dongfang Chenze: "Don't you have Zhu Shuzhen's newly written ci? Bring it out quickly!"

---

Dongfang Chenze said: "The seniors are here. Let's wait a moment."

---

Chen Haoyu didn't want to wait. Before anyone else could speak, he directly shouted: "I want to recommend Youqi Jushi's new ci!"

---

Everyone looked toward them.

---

Lin Zixiao said: "No wonder Young Master Chen came. It turns out he obtained Youqi Jushi's new ci. Please bring it out for everyone to appreciate."

---

Chen Haoyu pointed at Dongfang Chenze: "The new ci is with him."

---

Someone had already taken down Dongpo Jushi's "Water Melody."

---

Since Chen Haoyu had already shouted it out, Dongfang Chenze didn't decline. He ordered Huayu to hang the scroll in his hand on the screen for displaying ci poems.

---

Dongfang Chenze stood up and walked to the middle of the crowd: "The ci is still fresh. It was written by Youqi Jushi yesterday while touring West Lake, following the 'Qingpingle' tune. She only recited the lines and didn't name it. The two of us, Cao Ban and An Mingxuan, quietly memorized it. After returning, we wrote it on paper, but we struggled with having no title. After careful thought, since the Beginning of Autumn hasn't arrived yet and it's still summer, we boldly added the title 'Summer Tour of the Lake' to Youqi Jushi's new ci."

---

Everyone looked at Youqi Jushi's ci. The youngest child spoke first: "Is Young Master Dongfang certain it was written by Youqi Jushi yesterday?"

---

Before Dongfang Chenze could speak, An Mingxuan replied: "How could this be fake? The three of us heard it with our own ears and saw it with our own eyes."

---

The child felt puzzled: "But yesterday the weather was clear. Why does the ci write 'annoyed by mist, teasing fog' and 'a moment of plum rain drizzle'? Could it be that only the weather around West Lake was bad yesterday? Besides, the Beginning of Autumn is approaching, and the plum rain season has long passed. 'A moment of plum rain drizzle' is truly puzzling."

---

Someone said: "I toured West Lake yesterday. The weather was clear, and it didn't rain."

---

Hearing this, Chen Haoyu rejoiced inwardly: "Yesterday she fell into the water, looking like a drowned rat. Hehe, how could her mood be good? No matter how clear the weather, in her eyes it would be continuous rain. Hehe, my revenge was really well done, but it's not enough!"

---

When Chen Haoyu was happy, he voluntarily said: "You child don't understand writing poetry and ci at all. Poetry and ci don't necessarily have to be written according to actual circumstances. There's a term for it... what was it? Right! 'Imaginary description.' As long as it can express a person's inner emotions, that's enough."

---

Chen Haoyu actually voluntarily participated in appreciation, which everyone found novel. What he said wasn't without reason, so everyone nodded in agreement.

---

Having gained everyone's approval, Chen Haoyu was even more enthusiastic: "In my opinion, Zhu Shuzhen... no, Youqi Jushi, wrote about clear weather as rainy days because she accidentally fell into the water yesterday and was in a bad mood."

---

The three young masters immediately felt very embarrassed. Others showed mocking expressions. No one wanted to respond to Chen Haoyu's words.

---

Only the child asked: "Is Young Master Chen certain Youqi Jushi was in a bad mood yesterday?"

---

Chen Haoyu was very certain: "Of course. I saw her fall into the water with my own eyes. Right! And Qiao Wenxian fell into the water with her. If you don't believe me, when you see Qiao Wenxian, ask him and you'll know."

---

Cao Ban whispered a reminder: "Young Master Chen, don't say any more. The more you say, the more embarrassing it will be."

---

Chen Haoyu was puzzled. He felt there was nothing wrong with what he said. Why would Cao Ban say that? Anyway, he had nothing else to say. He might as well sit down honestly, listen to others, and patiently wait for the opportunity to deal with Zhu Shuzhen.

---

"Everyone, I've finished speaking. Please continue."

---

Someone stood up: "Let me say a few words. What Young Master Chen said about imaginary description naturally has reason, but to say Youqi Jushi was in a bad mood when writing this ci is completely unreasonable. The first line uses the character 'annoyed,' which seems to indicate a bad mood, but in my opinion, she was in a very good mood. Otherwise, how could there be 'hand in hand on the lake road among lotus flowers' and even 'sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed'?" After speaking, that person showed a teasing expression.

---

Though Chen Haoyu had appreciated incorrectly, he didn't blush. He cursed inwardly: "A group of pedantic literati. So what if you can appreciate a few lines of poetry? I don't care at all!"

---

Hearing "sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed," he looked at the scroll and indeed saw it. He rejoiced inwardly: "Zhu Shuzhen, oh Zhu Shuzhen, though you've been married, you don't need to be so unrestrained. You actually have no shame and lay in Qiao Wenxian's arms. Such an interesting line. I must make good use of it. I don't believe I can't bring you down."

---

Someone said: "I didn't expect Youqi Jushi could write such bold lines. It's truly embarrassing. Even I feel blushing after reading it. I wonder what everyone thinks?"

---

Another person said: "This truly lacks feminine virtue! Such ci poems shouldn't be brought out!"

---

Chen Haoyu secretly rejoiced on the side: "Zhu Shuzhen, oh Zhu Shuzhen, today I will witness with my own eyes how everyone scolds you! Hehe...!"

---

Seeing the ill intentions of those appreciating, Dongfang Chenze regretted bringing out the new ci. Since it was already brought out, he absolutely couldn't let everyone profane Youqi Jushi's new work.

---

"When Youqi Jushi wrote this ci, her mood was indeed quite good. The three of us can testify. Because her mood was good, she even drank a few more cups of wine and recited the lines while in a drunken state. The ci portrays a young man and woman, hand in hand walking by West Lake. The scenery is beautiful, and the characters' moods are also beautiful. The expression of the lines is indeed somewhat bold, but only in this way can the longing of boudoir women for emotional freedom be expressed. Everyone here understands poetry and ci. Please don't impose moral judgments on the poetry and ci."

---

Cao Ban hoped to redirect everyone's attention elsewhere. He stood up: "In Liu Yi Jushi's 'Youthful Roaming,' there is 'annoyed by mist, teasing fog, drinking drunk leaning on the west wind.' It writes about the beautiful feelings of the first garden tour with a partner. Youqi Jushi borrowed and used it creatively, resulting in 'annoyed by mist, teasing dew.' Liu Yi Jushi was a man, serving in the court, busy with matters of the world. His lines naturally have a man's sentiments. Youqi Jushi is a boudoir woman, whose vision is limited to boudoir feelings. Writing about the feelings between men and women in ci is perfectly normal. Moreover, Youqi Jushi is not the first to write about feelings between men and women in poetry and ci. The first piece of the Classic of Poetry, 'Ospreys,' writes about feelings between men and women. 'A modest maiden is a fit wife for a gentleman' has become a famous line through the ages. If we say Youqi Jushi's lines are bold, in my opinion, they don't compare to the lines in the Classic of Poetry's 'A Dead Deer in the Wild': 'A maiden in spring, a gentleman courts her.' 'Sleeping in someone's arms fully clothed' compared to this is not worth mentioning at all. Everyone, Confucius often discussed the Classic of Poetry with his younger brothers, and he didn't show criticism or disgust when seeing such lines. Why should everyone be narrow-minded? It's better to discuss ci based on the ci itself."

---
