## Chapter 130: Beating "Delayed" in Broad Daylight

The noodle-stall couple dashed up, clutching their boy tight and sobbing thanks: "Thank you, young sir! Thank you!"

Qianli Xun sized up the rider: about twenty, rugged and handsome, bearing an unmistakable air of high birth—matched only by his galling arrogance.

"You don't look like a Lin'an native, sir. Where are you from?"

"Up north. Where exactly is none of your business."

"If you love riding horses, go show off in the open countryside—why tear through a busy market scaring everyone half to death?"

"I ride where I please. Who are you to stop me?"

Even cockier than Chen Haoyu! Qianli Xun grinned—she had found someone begging for a beating at last! "Do what you like on your own time; endanger innocent folk and it becomes my business."

"Oh? And how do you plan to make it your business?"

"Get off that horse, apologize to the people you terrified, and get out of Lin'an."

"Why should I listen to you?"

"Because of these fists." Qianli Xun raised both fists. "Beat me and I won't say another word—run wild all you want. Lose, and you obey."

The black-clad rider vaulted down from his saddle, his four men dismounting behind him.

"Lead my horse aside and stand back—today I'll test this young gentleman's skills!"

His retainers led the horses clear and held back the gathering ring of spectators.

Too cautious to step close yet eager for a show, bystanders murmured worries for the slender youth—how could someone so slight stand up to a hulking giant like that?

Stepping into the open ring, Qianli Xun studied him closer—definitely not Han! Khitan? Jurchen? Or another northern tribe?

"Daydreaming? Throw your punches! Since you look delicate, I'll let you strike first."

Qianli Xun hoped he was Jurchen—pummeling a Jin rider today would count as her little contribution to resisting the Jin!

"A proud citizen of the Great Song needs no handicap from an outsider! Make your move!"

Realizing his origins had been spotted—no surprise, since Han clothes and hairstyle couldn't hide a northern build and bearing—the rider smirked:

"Big talk! Thought all Song folk were spineless weaklings—didn't expect a daredevil like you! Fine, I'll strike first!"

With a fierce surge, the rider launched a blistering barrage of punches...

Refusing to yield an inch, Qianli Xun met him with crisp, fluid technique.

A few exchanges in, a jolt of familiarity hit her; two more exchanges and shock flashed through her mind: "Why is this so close to Shen Fist?! Could he be Shen Jianchen?!"

Distracted for a split second, she caught a grazing blow that knocked her back two paces.

No—impossible! If Shen Jianchen had crossed over, he would have recognized Qianli Fist on contact! Not Shen Jianchen, though his style had to share roots with Shen Fist.

"Hate fighting opponents whose minds wander!" Already amazed by her razor-sharp skill over those few bouts, the rider marveled that a frame so slight packed fists every bit as fierce as his own.

Qianli Xun launched straight back into the fray...

Every blow the man threw carried crushing force like a storm gale, cracking the air with thunderous snaps.

Nimble and sharp, Qianli Xun wove past every strike and countered with deadly precision. Slender though she stood, astonishing power and spring flowed through every move, her forms linking like rushing water, swift and exact. Blending yielding force with pinpoint skill, she dissolved his brutal onslaught into thin air, landing crisp, light strikes straight into every opening he left.

Sensing her true strength as the bout wore on, the man poured full force into an all-out assault.

Calm and unruffled, Qianli Xun turned his own momentum against him, dodging and striking until every attack boomeranged back, tangling him on the defensive.

At last, one pinpoint Qianli Fist punch drove dead center into his chest.

Clutching his sternum, the man staggered backward and almost went down before a retainer caught his elbow.

Cheers erupted around the ring: "Bravo!"

Lowering her fists as she planted her stance, Qianli Xun smirked cold: "You lost. Apologize to the people and leave Lin'an."

Swallowing bitter pride before a watching crowd, the man bit down the pain, cupped his fists around the circle, and muttered, "My apologies, everyone."

Someone in the crowd shouted, "Stop galloping wild through city streets!"

Giving no answer, frustration burned in the man's gut—he had to strike back somehow. Mid-brawl he had already clocked she wasn't a man; why not expose her right in front of everyone?

Slipping a pebble from his belt, he flicked it with inner force...

Basking in victory and trading grins with the crowd, Qianli Xun never saw the pebble coming. It clipped dead center on her topknot, knocking her pearl hairpin to the cobbles as glossy black hair tumbled loose over her shoulders.

The man let out a mocking laugh: "Haha! A young woman all along—fooling everyone dressed as a man!"

Her jet-black hair cascading down her back, Qianli Xun spun around: "I dressed like this to stroll in peace, not fool anyone! Getting beaten by a girl—doesn't that cost you even more face?"

The laugh died on his lips—not from her retort, from her beauty. Frozen solid, an unexpected jolt stirred straight through his chest.

Forgetting his aching ribs, his feet carried him straight up to her: "My name is Yan Chi. Might young lady tell me your name and where you live? Once I finish my business, I'll come call on you."

Whiplashed by his sudden flip, Qianli Xun snapped, "Why on earth would I give you my name and address?!"

"Because I've fallen for you."

"Well, I haven't fallen for you!"

Most bystanders had drifted off, leaving a few stragglers pointing and murmuring. Afraid someone might recognize her, she needed an exit fast!

Right on cue, a Zhu Manor carriage came rolling down the street—Heaven be praised!

Shoving Yan Chi aside, Qianli Xun used light-foot technique to vault straight onto the moving Zhu carriage, whipped the door open, slid inside, and poked her head out the window: "What an awful name—Yan Chi, sounds just like yanchi, 'delayed'! Guess you're always late to everything, haha!"

More intrigued than ever by her beauty and fiery spirit, Yan Chi walked over, stooped to pick up the fallen pearl hairpin, and turned it over in his fingers: "We met together accidentally, and so my desire was satisfied" [TN: Translated by James Legge].

Tucking the hairpin inside his robe, he barked to his retainers, "Mount up! Out of the city!"

Vaulting back onto their steeds, the riders headed for the city gates—no longer charging reckless through the crowds, now that the girl who beat him occupied Yan Chi's mind.

Inside the carriage, Chen Wenxin stared wide-eyed at Shuzhen—hair tumbling wild, dressed in men's clothes, vaulting into a moving carriage out of thin air!

"Shuzhen, what on earth—how did you even jump in here?!"

"Skip the questions, Sister-in-law—take me back to Wangyue Pavilion!"

"Not until you spill everything!"

"Fine, fine! I was bored out of my skull at Wangyue Pavilion, went out for a walk disguised as a man so nobody'd recognize me, ran into a jerk begging for a beating on the street, and beat him up!"

Chen Wenxin's eyes went round as saucers: "What?! You got in a street fight?! The rumors barely died down and you're starting brawls—trying to prove the 'tigress' rumors right?!"

"Relax, Sister-in-law, nobody knew who I was! Besides, the guy had it coming—the whole crowd cheered! Great outing: clobbered a bully and blew off all my steam!"

Calling out to the driver, Chen Wenxin ordered, "Head to Wangyue Pavilion first."

"Yes, Young Madam!"

Qianli Xun asked, "Why are you out all alone, Sister-in-law—not even a maid with you?"

"Just felt like visiting my parents' house solo today—didn't bring anyone. Same as you leaving Kuqiao and Xiaguo behind!"

"Great minds think alike!"

Remembering the Qiao betrothal news, Chen Wenxin tested with care: "You heard about the Qiao family, right? What are you planning?"

The smile faded off Qianli Xun's face. After a quiet beat, she said, "My half-year isn't up yet. I'll keep waiting."

"He's engaged already, and you're still waiting?!"

"What else? Nobody's whisking me to an altar tomorrow—waiting or not waiting changes nothing. Might as well hold on to a little hope."

"Fair point—wait if you want. Meanwhile, Sister-in-law will keep her eyes peeled for you! Lin'an is full of fine young men—Qiao Wenxian isn't the only fish in the sea."

"Don't bother. If Wenyuan doesn't work out, I'll stay single till I'm old and gray."

"Bah, bite your tongue! Stay single for life?! Qiao Wenxian isn't worth that!"

"It's not for him. Love is sweet, sure, yet it cuts deep—I don't want to bleed again. Once I close my heart, how could I ever marry someone else? Staying solo is the only way."

"I don't get your theories! Your brother and I grew our love after getting married—who says you have to fall in love first? Marry first, live together, and affection grows on its own."

Qianli Xun managed a faint smile: "Not everyone has Sister-in-law's luck landing a gem like my brother."

"Plenty of good men out there! Look at my dad—spoils my mom rotten! If Mom points east, Dad wouldn't dare look west, haha! Makes even me jealous—figure Mom must've racked up mountains of good karma in a past life to land a husband who's both rich and doting."

"Plenty of good men, sure, yet meeting AND keeping them takes fate. Wenyuan is wonderful, only he and I..."

She broke off—unable to force out the words "fated to meet, never to stay."

Seeing sorrow darken Shuzhen's eyes, Chen Wenxin fell silent, and neither spoke for the rest of the ride.

Only after dropping Shuzhen off at Wangyue Pavilion did Chen Wenxin turn back toward Zhu Manor.
