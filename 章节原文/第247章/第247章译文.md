## Chapter 247: First Day at the Silk Shop

Still strolling the petal-strewn garden with Wenxin, Qianli Xun paused as a Chen maid hurried up. "Eldest Miss, Second Young Madam—Guard Bai from Prince Yi's household is at the gate asking for Kuqiao."

Chatting behind with Yuegui, Kuqiao had missed the news of Prince Yi's return and gasped bright-eyed: "Miss...!"

Eager herself to pick up scraps of news about Prince Yi and Mengrong, Qianli Xun waved her off. "Go on—I don't need you here."

"Thank you, Miss!" Kuqiao sprinted away.

"Look at that girl—one mention of Guard Bai and her mistress vanishes from her head!" Wenxin teased.

"My feet are tired, Sister-in-law—let's go sit at Grandmother's and catch the storyteller." Not really tired, just craving a noisy crowd to drown out Shen Jianchen's name.

"Heard hiring the storyteller by the month was your brainstorm!" Wenxin laughed. "Now half the rich houses in Lin'an hire him on retainer—sprints from manor to manor earning ten times what he made at Goulan Wasi! Chen Manor started a whole city trend!"

"Guess I ought to charge him a commission!"

"Second that!"

Arriving at Old Madam Xia's courtyard just as the gavel cracked—with maids taking Laibao off to play—the sisters-in-law settled in. Listening to her own plot performed live drowned out every stray thought until the tale ended and family chatter resumed, where Qianli Xun noticed Ye Anya missing once again.

Finding an opening, she asked, "Second Aunt, where's First Sister-in-law?"

Madam Bao's smile died. "Never likes crowds—prefers hiding in her room."

Madam Cui smirked inside: Everyone knows Anya only hides when Haodong's left fresh bruises!

Old Madam Xia turned stern on Madam Bao. "You and Jiangshu lecture Haodong—hitting a wife brings zero honor! Leading the Chen estate starts with putting your own house in order, and that starts with cherishing your wife. A wife is a man's treasure vault! Keep your vault bruised, weeping, and terrified, and how do you expect big fortune to stick?"

Unconvinced yet obedient, Madam Bao murmured, "Yes, Mother. We'll talk to him tonight."

"Not your ears—his heart needs to hear it!"

A wife is a man's treasure vault—Qianli Xun marveled at the phrase, picturing Chen Jianglin hovering devoted around Yao Qingyan while Chen wealth still swelled nonstop. A wild fleeting thought flickered—what if Chen Haoyu treated her like that? She snorted at herself at once; brothel-hopping slime turning into a dream husband? No thanks!

Once Old Madam Xia tired and Wenxin headed home with Laibao, Qianli Xun returned alone to Suxin Thatched Hall, where Kuqiao sat whisking tea with a glowing face.

Sitting on the floor cushion beside the tea table, Qianli Xun tested the waters casual. "Saw Bai Feng? Put on weight or lost some?"

Seeing right through the detour, Kuqiao handed over a bowl of tea. "Bai Feng says Prince Yi looks the same—only difference is he's wed to the princess now."

Taking a sip, Qianli Xun arched a brow. "Asked about Bai Feng—why bring up Prince Yi?"

Keep pretending! Kuqiao thought.

"Gone all that time—didn't Bai Feng bring you a souvenir?"

Hesitating—Bai Feng had brought a gift for her, nothing from Prince Yi for Miss, though both were married now anyway—Kuqiao admitted, "Yes—a Bianliang woodblock print."

"Let's see!"

Fetching it from Yuening Room, Kuqiao handed over a crisp carved portrait catching her own likeness to the life.

"Thoughtful guy," Qianli Xun murmured, struck by quiet envy—Kuqiao could love open and proud while she and Shen Jianchen stayed locked behind separate wedding vows.

Suddenly Kuqiao dropped straight to her knees, sobbing. "Miss... waaah!"

"Just saw your sweetheart—what's with the tears?!"

"Heartbroken for you, Miss! Waaah!"

"Save the tears—I'm thriving! Get up and sit!"

Wiping her eyes, Kuqiao sat down. "Terrified your Lin Manor misery is repeating all over again!"

"Not a chance—I'm not who I was back then."

Gathering her nerve, Kuqiao spoke straight. "Let Prince Yi go, Miss! You're both married—stop holding onto him. Chen Manor is wonderful; I'm praying you and Young Master work out!"

Realization hit Qianli Xun: she was standing one against the world—Chen elders, Zhu family, Matron Wei, every maid down to Kuqiao all rooting for Chen Haoyu! Resentment flared: why did two days of decent behavior turn everybody into his fan club while she got pressured to hand herself to a man she didn't love?!

"Meddling busybody—stay out of my heart!"

"I'm just a maid, Miss, yet I care about you too much to stay quiet! Even setting everything else aside, think of Princess Jiahe—what wife wants another woman holding her husband inside her heart?"

That arrow hit dead center. Qianli Xun's face turned grave as she stood up. "Pack that peeing clay doll out of my sight. I won't touch it again."

Nodding, Kuqiao felt a stab of guilt for snuffing out her mistress's last tiny keepsake.

Meanwhile, Chen Haoyu had stepped down at the silk shop with Chen Haodong, greeted by Manager Qiu. Showing off his turf, Chen Haodong headed straight into the guest room for a big southwest merchant deal while tossing a smooth nod to Manager Qiu: "Take good care of Haoyu for Eldest Uncle!" (sneering inside: learn all you want, still zerohow could they give me a hard time?"

Old Madam Xia gave him a laughing scold. "Talking nonsense again. Forgot why your father-in-law forced you to marry the Zhu girl in the first place?"

"A real hero doesn't bring up past glories! Don't mention that again, Grandmother, or if that tigress hears it, your grandson will be in for a world of pain."

To coax more silver out of her, he needed her in high spirits, so he switched tracks at once. "Grandmother, I always pray for your long life and good health. Today at Zhu Manor, I dug up the longevity secret of their Old Ancestor. Want to hear it?"

Certain he was just buttering her up, Old Madam Xia played along. "Of course I do."

Chen Haoyu leaned in with an air of mystery. "I paced outside the Zhu Buddhist shrine for ages and heard nothing but wooden fish tapping and sutra chanting inside. That must be why their Old Ancestor has lived so long. How about I buy you a wooden fish and a string of prayer beads tomorrow so you can give it a try?"

"Save it. Your grandmother isn't cut out for chanting sutras. Just come visit me every day and keep me company. When I'm happy, good health and long life take care of themselves."

"Deal, I'll spend more time with you from now on." Casting about for a way to sic his grandmother on Zhu Shuzhen, he put on his best pitiful face. "Grandmother, you have to help me."

"Help you with what?"

"Zhu Shuzhen is bullying me."

"How is she bullying you?"

"She kicked out my maids, seized my courtyard, hogged all my properties, and gives me only twenty taels of silver a month! I'm practically a pauper now. Nobody out on the street believes I'm a Chen."

Old Madam Xia chuckled. "That bride of yours has a razor-sharp tongue. Even I can't talk her down. Besides, who told you to get on your knees and beg her to marry you, then sign some prenuptial contract? Blame yourself for gambling with Master Jiang and near losing the whole Chen estate."

Chen Haoyu blinked in shock. "How did you know about that?"

"Your new bride told me. Your father and mother know too."

Zhu Shuzhen! You didn't leave me a shred of face in front of my own family! Just you wait! he fumed inside. Aloud, he said, "Gambling was my fault, Grandmother. I'll never touch it again. Still, you have to teach Zhu Shuzhen a lesson for me. She's walking all over my head now."

"How should I teach her a lesson?"

"Find excuses to give her a hard time whenever you get the chance."

"She's your wife now. Giving her a hard time means giving you a hard time."

"Grandmother, we're husband and wife in name only."

Old Madam Xia's face clouded with sorrow. "My grandson gets married at last, only to live like this. Just thinking about it breaks my heart."

Chen Haoyu rushed to cheer her up. "Don't be sad, Grandmother. Sooner or later I'll bring home a wife you approve of."

Her reply came straight from the heart. "I'm quite pleased with the wife you have right now."

With his scheme to set Grandmother on Zhu Shuzhen dead in the water, Chen Haoyu pivoted straight to silver. "Grandmother, Zhu Shuzhen won't give me spending money, and I don't dare ask Father or Mother. Could you spare me a little silver?"

Old Madam Xia never stinted on her grandson. She ordered a maid to fetch a wooden box and pulled out a five-hundred-tael silver note. "Spend it with care. Not a copper on courtesans or gamblers, you hear me?"

Overjoyed, Chen Haoyu snatched the note and planted kiss after kiss on her cheek until she beamed from ear to ear. After chatting a few minutes longer, he tucked the note into his sash, bolted out of the manor, and swept Scarface and his gang off for another round of carousing.
