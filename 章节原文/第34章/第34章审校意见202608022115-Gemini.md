### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 现代CEO心态转变的地道表达（对应第2段）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰觉得自己变了，穿越前是公司CEO，最是喜欢跟身份尊贵，有钱之人打交道，现在竟然变得无所谓，甚至从心里觉得麻烦。也许人只有死过一回，才能明白金钱名利如过眼云烟。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    ...Before transmigrating, he was a company CEO who loved dealing with people of noble status and wealth. Now he found himself indifferent, **even feeling it was a hassle from the bottom of his heart**. Perhaps one had to die once to understand that money, fame, and fortune were **like passing clouds**.
* **修改后 (After):**
    ...Before transmigrating, as a company CEO, he used to thrive on rubbing elbows with the wealthy and elite. Now, he felt completely indifferent, **even finding the whole charade utterly exhausting**. Perhaps it really took dying once to realize that money, fame, and status were **nothing but fleeting illusions**.
* **💡 修改理由:**
    "feeling it was a hassle from the bottom of his heart" 是严重的中式英语。改为 "finding the whole charade utterly exhausting"（觉得这种虚伪应酬极其令人疲惫），更符合前世CEO的心态。“过眼云烟”用意境更准的 "fleeting illusions" 替代平淡的 "passing clouds"。

#### 2. 发现老爹是宋徽宗时的内心理智与吐槽（对应第18、19段）
>
> **【原文】** 他在历史上是位昏君，重用蔡京、王黼...高俅...等人，这些人奸诈成性，横征暴敛，骄奢淫逸，要不怎么有会《水浒》一百零八个好汉被逼上梁山的故事？...申简辰既失落又轻松...

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Historically, he was an **incompetent emperor** who heavily relied on... These people were **treacherous by nature, exorbitantly taxing the people, extravagant and dissolute**. Otherwise, how would there be the story of *Water Margin* where 108 heroes were forced to Liangshan?
    Thinking of this, Shen Jianchen felt **both disappointed and relaxed**.
* **修改后 (After):**
    ***Historically, the man was a notoriously foolish ruler!*** *He heavily relied on corrupt officials like Cai Jing, Wang Fu... and Gao Qiu. These men were treacherous to the bone, bleeding the commoners dry to fund their extravagant, depraved lifestyles. Otherwise, why else would the 108 heroes of the Water Margin be forced to become outlaws on Mount Liang?*
    **A strange mix of disappointment and relief washed over Shen Jianchen.**
* **💡 修改理由:**
    1. **网文排版必须！** 男主脑海中关于历史的评价属于纯粹的心理活动，必须转换为**斜体 (Italics)**。
    2. 用词升级："bleeding the commoners dry" (榨干百姓血汗) 替代书面语 "exorbitantly taxing"；"both disappointed and relaxed" 过于直白，改为 "A strange mix of disappointment and relief washed over..." 情绪更饱满。

#### 3. 容貌直译的本土化修正（对应第43段：剑眉鼠目）
>
> **【原文】** 迎面走来一位臣子，身着紫袍，头戴官帽，留着胡须，剑眉鼠目，表情严肃。

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Walking towards them was an official wearing a purple robe, an official hat, and a beard. He had **sword-like brows and rat-like eyes**, with a serious expression.
* **修改后 (After):**
    An official strode toward them, dressed in a purple robe and official headpiece. He sported a beard, and beneath his **sharp, slanted brows darted a pair of beady, rat-like eyes**, framed by a severe expression.
* **💡 修改理由:**
    "剑眉鼠目" 是中文特有的面相描写，直接丢给外国读者 "sword-like brows and rat-like eyes" 会非常滑稽。精修版补充了动词 "darted" (乱转) 和 "beady" (如珠子般小而狡猾的)，完美复刻了影视剧中高俅那种阴险狡诈的奸臣形象。

#### 4. 内心核对身份时的震撼感（对应第47段）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰在心里琢磨"高太尉"的称呼，难道此人正是《水浒》中被描写成无恶不作的高俅高太尉？

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen **pondered the title "Grand Commandant Gao" in his heart**. Could this person be exactly Gao Qiu, the Grand Commandant Gao who was depicted as committing every evil in *Water Margin*?
* **修改后 (After):**
    ***"Grand Commandant Gao"?*** Shen Jianchen **mulled the title over in his head, his eyes widening slightly**. ***Could this actually be Gao Qiu? The exact same Grand Commandant Gao depicted as an absolute, unredeemable villain in the Water Margin?!***
* **💡 修改理由:**
    去掉生硬的 "in his heart"（心里），加入 "his eyes widening slightly"（微微睁大眼睛）作为微表情。用斜体和强烈的惊叹号表达出现代人突然看到“书里走出来的大反派”时的内心震动感。

#### 5. 男主脱口而出的网文感口语（对应第54段：大奸臣！）
>
> **【原文】** 申简辰不由脱口而出："大奸臣！"

* **修改前 (Before):**
    Shen Jianchen blurted out, "**A great treacherous official!**"
* **修改后 (After):**
    Shen Jianchen blurted out, "**That treacherous scumbag!**"
* **💡 修改理由:**
    "A great treacherous official" 太像文言文课本翻译。一个现代CEO看到大反派，脱口而出的自然是强烈的辱骂词汇，"That treacherous scumbag!" (那个阴险的败类/人渣！) 极其贴合此时的爽文语境。
