# 第34章 打听官家遇奸臣 / Inquiring About the Emperor and Encountering Treacherous Officials

---

内侍走后，申简辰瞧着手中的请帖，只觉麻烦，官家既然不重视仪王，为何还要送来请帖？搞得他还得留在汴梁。

After the imperial attendant left, Shen Jianchen looked at the invitation in his hand and felt only annoyance. If His Majesty didn't value Prince Yi, why send an invitation at all? Now he had to stay in Bianliang.

---

申简辰觉得自己变了，穿越前是公司CEO，最是喜欢跟身份尊贵，有钱之人打交道，现在竟然变得无所谓，甚至从心里觉得麻烦。也许人只有死过一回，才能明白金钱名利如过眼云烟。

Shen Jianchen felt he had changed. Before transmigrating, he was a company CEO who loved dealing with people of noble status and wealth. Now he found himself indifferent, even feeling it was a hassle from the bottom of his heart. Perhaps one had to die once to understand that money, fame, and fortune were like passing clouds.

---

又一想，新觅的徒弟白风还未前来报到，不如趁宴会多留一日，等他亲自送上门来。

Then again, his newly found disciple Bai Feng hadn't reported yet. Might as well stay one more day for the banquet and wait for him to come in person.

---

乔梦容高兴道："官家终于邀请哥哥！哥哥不必紧张，官家从未见过你，对以前的哥哥并不了解，你只需以真实的状态轻松应对即可。"

Qiao Mengrong said happily, "His Majesty has finally invited you, Brother! Don't be nervous. His Majesty has never met you and doesn't know the old you well. Just respond naturally and relaxed."

---

申简辰对乔梦容的体贴投以微笑。

Shen Jianchen smiled at Qiao Mengrong's thoughtfulness.

---

乔梦容又转向乔文仙："乔公子是官家宴会中的常客，对官家的喜好多少了解一些，不如给哥哥讲讲，让他好提前有个心里准备。"

Qiao Mengrong turned to Qiao Wenxian, "Young Master Qiao, you're a regular at His Majesty's banquets and know something of his preferences. Why not tell Brother so he can prepare mentally?"

---

"谨遵郡主吩咐。"乔文仙如数家珍般详细说明，"当今官家书法、绘画、诗词歌赋无一不通，极其推崇文人雅致，最喜举办宴会，不仅朝中大臣会参加，还会邀请有名的文人雅士。鄙人略懂书画歌赋，又能说上两句评论之语，也不知官家如何得知我的名头，在汴梁这段时日，只要宫中有活动，总会邀请我参加。"

"I obey the Commandery Princess's instructions." Qiao Wenxian explained in detail, listing things like treasures from his own home. "His Majesty excels at calligraphy, painting, poetry, and rhapsodies—he has no equal. He greatly admires literary elegance and loves holding banquets. Not only do court officials attend, but famous literati and scholars are also invited. I know a bit about calligraphy, painting, and poetry, and can offer a few words of criticism. I don't know how His Majesty learned of my reputation, but during my time in Bianliang, whenever there's a palace event, I'm always invited."

---

乔梦容赞道："乔公子过谦，你可是江南有名的才子，不仅在江南为人所知，到汴梁这段日子，也逐渐名声鹊起，足以证明你的才华。要说这江南真是人才辈出，我想起临安有位女词人，名叫朱淑真，她的诗词我读过一些，真是才华斐然，不知乔公子可见过她？"

Qiao Mengrong praised, "Young Master Qiao is too modest. You're a famous talent from Jiangnan, known not only there but also gradually gaining reputation in Bianliang—enough to prove your talent. Speaking of Jiangnan producing so many talents, I recall a female poet in Lin'an named Zhu Shuzhen. I've read some of her poems and ci—truly brilliant. Have you ever met her, Young Master Qiao?"

---

乔文仙叹道："唉，她正是乔某喜欢之人。"

Qiao Wenxian sighed, "Alas, she is precisely the one I admire."

---

乔梦容也叹道："原来乔公子喜欢的人是她。"

Qiao Mengrong also sighed, "So the one Young Master Qiao admires is her."

---

申简辰极少关注文学，他的文学知识仅限于上学期间所涉猎。上中学时，语文这门功课成绩还不错，但要站在专业角度鉴赏文学作品，他还是挺发憷的。

Shen Jianchen rarely paid attention to literature. His literary knowledge was limited to what he had learned during school. In middle school, his Chinese grades were decent, but to appreciate literary works from a professional perspective, he still felt intimidated.

---

后来，因为千里寻写网络小说，他才会捧着手机读那些无聊的文字，只为追求美人时，能找到除拳法以外的共同话题。

Later, because Qianli Xun wrote web novels, he would hold his phone and read those boring texts, just to find common topics besides martial arts when pursuing his beauty.

---

在爱情力量的驱使下，他硬着头皮把千里寻写过的小说统统看了个遍，也仔细思索和体味过，对于网络小说这种文学形式还能略微谈些见解。

Driven by the power of love, he gritted his teeth and read all the novels Qianli Xun had written, carefully thinking about and savoring them. For this literary form of web novels, he could still offer some slight insights.

---

可对于那些没学过没见过的古诗词，他绝对是门外汉，更别说书画之类，更是一窍不通，参加官家的宴会只怕会出丑，给赵朴脸上抹黑。

But for ancient poetry and ci he hadn't learned or seen, he was absolutely a layman. Not to mention calligraphy and painting—he knew nothing at all. Attending His Majesty's banquet, he was afraid he'd embarrass himself and disgrace Zhao Pu.

---

申简辰迅速打定主意，到时能躲多远便躲多远，那么多人，官家应该不会注意到他，毕竟赵朴不是官家在意的儿子。

Shen Jianchen quickly made up his mind: when the time came, he'd hide as far away as possible. With so many people, His Majesty probably wouldn't notice him. After all, Zhao Pu wasn't a son His Majesty cared about.

---

乔文仙看出仪王面色中有些许担忧，宽慰道："仪王不必担忧，到时你只需跟紧我，有什么不懂的，我悄悄告诉你。"

Qiao Wenxian noticed some worry on Prince Yi's face and reassured him, "Prince Yi need not worry. When the time comes, just stay close to me. If there's anything you don't understand, I'll quietly tell you."

---

乔梦容说："有乔公子照顾，哥哥尽管放宽心。"

Qiao Mengrong said, "With Young Master Qiao looking after you, Brother can rest assured."

---

申简辰先谢道："有劳文渊。"

Shen Jianchen thanked him first, "I'll trouble you, Wenyuan."

---

第二天，申简辰与乔文仙乘坐马车应邀前往皇宫，他对即将谋面的父皇实在不熟悉，还想从乔文仙处多了解些信息："文渊，我父皇叫什么名字？"

The next day, Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian took a carriage to the palace as invited. He was really unfamiliar with the father he was about to meet and wanted to learn more from Qiao Wenxian, "Wenyuan, what is my father's name?"

---

乔文仙凑近，小声说："官家的名讳本不应随便提起，仪王既问起，我就悄悄告诉你，当今官家大名赵佶。"

Qiao Wenxian leaned close and whispered, "His Majesty's taboo name shouldn't be casually mentioned. Since Prince Yi asked, I'll quietly tell you—the current Emperor's name is Zhao Ji."

---

穿越前申简辰虽不大关注过去的历史，可上学期间获得的历史知识还刻在脑中，宣和之类的年号他不甚熟悉，历代帝王的名讳还是有较为深刻的印象，赵佶不就是宋徽宗吗？

Before transmigrating, although Shen Jianchen didn't pay much attention to past history, the historical knowledge he gained during school was still etched in his mind. He wasn't very familiar with era names like Xuanhe, but he had a fairly deep impression of the taboo names of past emperors. Wasn't Zhao Ji exactly Song Huizong?

---

他在历史上是位昏君，重用蔡京、王黼、童贯、高俅、朱勔、梁师成等人，这些人奸诈成性，横征暴敛，骄奢淫逸，要不怎么有会《水浒》一百零八个好汉被逼上梁山的故事？

Historically, he was an incompetent emperor who heavily relied on Cai Jing, Wang Fu, Tong Guan, Gao Qiu, Zhu Mian, Liang Shicheng, and others. These people were treacherous by nature, exorbitantly taxing the people, extravagant and dissolute. Otherwise, how would there be the story of *Water Margin* where 108 heroes were forced to Liangshan?

---

想到这里，申简辰既失落又轻松，失落的是穿越而来，竟成为昏君的儿子；之所以轻松，是因为他从心里瞧不上昏君，既瞧不上便不会紧张，更不怕在昏君面前出丑。要知道昏君出的丑历史都记着呢！他申简辰是普通人物，出点儿丑最多被昏君和周围人笑笑，历史根本不会把他当回事。

Thinking of this, Shen Jianchen felt both disappointed and relaxed. Disappointed that after transmigrating, he actually became the son of an incompetent emperor. The reason he felt relaxed was that he looked down on the incompetent emperor from the bottom of his heart. Since he looked down on him, he wouldn't be nervous, let alone afraid of embarrassing himself in front of him. One must know that the embarrassments of incompetent emperors are recorded in history! He, Shen Jianchen, was an ordinary person. If he embarrassed himself a bit, at most the incompetent emperor and those around him would laugh. History wouldn't take him seriously at all.

---

乔文仙奇怪道："仪王听了官家的名讳，好像轻松许多，是何缘故？"

Qiao Wenxian said strangely, "After hearing His Majesty's name, Prince Yi seems much more relaxed. May I ask why?"

---

申简辰没有回答，继续问："不知文渊对我父皇，以及他管理的天下有何看法？"

Shen Jianchen didn't answer but continued asking, "I wonder what Wenyuan thinks of my imperial father and the realm he governs?"

---

乔文仙说："作为臣民，不应私下随便议论，不过仪王问起，我不妨说两句。"

Qiao Wenxian said, "As a subject, I shouldn't casually discuss this in private. But since Prince Yi asked, I might as well say a few words."

---

乔文仙又凑近仪王耳边："当今官家是书画大家，用才华横溢四字形容绝不为过，大宋的天下表面一派繁荣祥和，老百姓的日子过得也算富足，不说别的，这汴梁街市的繁华便可见一斑，有时间我带你晚上出来逛逛，那才叫一个热闹……"

Qiao Wenxian leaned close to Prince Yi's ear again, "His Majesty is a master of calligraphy and painting. Describing him as 'brimming with talent' is absolutely not an exaggeration. On the surface, the Great Song's realm appears prosperous and peaceful. The common people's lives are relatively well-off. Not to mention other things, the prosperity of Bianliang's markets alone gives a glimpse. When you have time, I'll take you out for a stroll at night. That's truly lively..."

---

没等乔文仙说完，申简忍不住问："你说表面一派繁荣祥和，实质上呢？"

Before Qiao Wenxian finished, Shen Jianchen couldn't help but ask, "You say on the surface it's prosperous and peaceful. What about in substance?"

---

乔文仙声音压得更低："实质上藏有隐患，外有北边金人虎视眈眈，内有起义军作乱，去年淮南盗生事，后被海州知州张叔夜打败，生擒义军副将，这股义军才不得不降。你可知淮南盗的首领是谁？"

Qiao Wenxian lowered his voice even more, "In substance, hidden dangers lurk. Outside, the Jin people to the north eye us like tigers. Inside, rebel armies cause chaos. Last year, the Huainan bandits caused trouble but were later defeated by Zhang Shuye, the Prefect of Haizhou. The rebel army's deputy general was captured alive, and only then did this rebel force have to surrender. Do you know who the leader of the Huainan bandits was?"

---

申简辰当然不知，摇摇头。

Shen Jianchen naturally didn't know and shook his head.

---

"是位乡里小民，名叫宋江。"

"He was a common man from the countryside, named Song Jiang."

[TN: Song Jiang was a real historical figure who led a rebellion during the Song Dynasty. He later became the protagonist of the classic Chinese novel *Water Margin*, where he was portrayed as a heroic outlaw.]

---

申简辰立刻来了兴致："宋江，我知道，他性情豪放，乐善好施，结识到不少英雄好汉，在百姓中有较高威望，是梁山起义的领袖，人称'及时雨'……"

Shen Jianchen immediately became interested, "Song Jiang, I know him. He was bold and generous, loved doing good deeds, and made many heroic friends. He had high prestige among the people and was the leader of the Liangshan uprising, known as 'Timely Rain'..."

---

正说着，见乔文仙用奇怪的目光看着他："宋江反的可是你们赵家的天下，你怎么说起他如此兴奋？"

Just as he was speaking, he saw Qiao Wenxian looking at him strangely, "Song Jiang rebelled against your Zhao family's realm. Why are you so excited talking about him?"

---

申简辰赶紧解释："我曾看过一本书，上面有位英雄豪杰，也叫宋江，没想到历史上真有宋江此人，不小心把两个人搞混了。"

Shen Jianchen quickly explained, "I once read a book where there was a hero also named Song Jiang. I didn't expect Song Jiang to really exist in history. I accidentally confused the two."

---

"真怀疑书上的宋江就是照着淮南盗首领宋江写的，要不你怎么说得那么像，是什么书？借我瞧瞧。"

"I really suspect the Song Jiang in the book was written based on the Huainan bandit leader Song Jiang. Otherwise, how could you describe him so accurately? What book is it? Let me take a look."

---

申简辰感到为难，又觉得好笑，宋代根本没有《水浒》，于是胡乱编道："是我死后在另一个世界看的，看完又活过来，其他不记得，只记得这本书。"

Shen Jianchen felt troubled but also found it funny. There was no *Water Margin* in the Song Dynasty at all, so he made something up, "I read it in another world after I died. After finishing it, I came back to life. I don't remember anything else, only this book."

---

"仪王该不会是骗我吧？能有这等神奇之事？"

"Prince Yi wouldn't be deceiving me, would he? Could such a miraculous thing happen?"

---

"我可是起死回生之人，难道不能证明世上有神奇之事？"

"I'm someone who came back from the dead. Doesn't that prove miraculous things exist in the world?"

---

乔文仙只能承认："嗯……，也对，你以前病成那样，我就是长九颗脑袋，也不会想到有一天你能健康地坐在马车里和我聊天儿，好吧，信你一回。"

Qiao Wenxian could only admit, "Hmm... you're right. You were so sick before. Even if I had nine heads, I wouldn't have thought that one day you'd be sitting healthily in a carriage chatting with me. Alright, I'll believe you this time."

---

申简辰又问："也不知官家今日因何举行办宴会？"

Shen Jianchen asked again, "I wonder why His Majesty is holding a banquet today?"

---

"官家举办宴会是常事，尤其在官家生日之际，场面更是宏大，而且要提前一个月准备。至于这次，如果我没猜错，应该是艮岳落成，庆祝庆祝。"

"His Majesty holding banquets is common, especially on his birthday, when the scene is even more grand and requires a month of advance preparation. As for this time, if I'm not mistaken, it should be to celebrate the completion of Genyue."

---

"艮岳是什么？"

"What is Genyue?"

---

"看来你真的什么都不记得，艮岳是官家建的风景园林，里面有天下之灵石，南方艳美珍奇之花木，设有雕阑曲槛，修葺着亭台楼阁，历经数十年时间才建成。"

"It seems you really don't remember anything. Genyue is a landscape garden built by His Majesty. It contains spiritual stones from across the realm, beautiful and rare flowers and trees from the south, with carved railings and winding pavilions, repaired towers and pavilions. It took decades to complete."

[TN: Genyue (艮岳) was an imperial garden built by Emperor Huizong of Song. It was famous for its elaborate landscaping and rare plants, but its construction drained the imperial treasury and burdened the people with heavy taxes.]

---

申简辰在心里批判道："奢侈！不知搜刮了多少民脂民膏！"

Shen Jianchen criticized in his heart, "Extravagant! Who knows how much of the people's blood and sweat was scraped together!"

---

两人继续聊着，已到皇宫门外。下了马车，抬头看到"西华门"三个大字，仪王府离西华门较近，从这里进皇宫倒也方便。

The two continued chatting and arrived at the palace gate. Getting off the carriage, they looked up and saw the three large characters "Xihua Gate." Prince Yi's Manor was relatively close to Xihua Gate, so entering the palace from here was convenient.

---

申简辰向皇宫门口的侍卫出示了请帖，侍卫看后，恭敬地说："仪王，乔公子，请。"

Shen Jianchen showed the invitation to the guards at the palace gate. After looking at it, the guard said respectfully, "Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao, please enter."

---

他们走进皇宫，面前是一条直直的用石砖铺就的大道，大道旁整齐地摆放着轿子，每个轿子跟前站着四名侍卫。

They walked into the palace. Before them was a straight road paved with stone bricks. Along the road, sedan chairs were neatly arranged, with four guards standing in front of each sedan chair.

---

一位内侍看到他们进来，迎上前："乔公子请上轿。"

An imperial attendant saw them enter and came forward, "Young Master Qiao, please board the sedan chair."

---

"这位是仪王。"乔文仙见内侍未招呼仪王，猜他并不认识，故而介绍道。

"This is Prince Yi." Qiao Wenxian saw that the imperial attendant hadn't greeted Prince Yi and guessed he didn't recognize him, so he introduced him.

---

内侍连忙行礼："奴才眼拙，没认出仪王，仪王这边请。"

The imperial attendant quickly bowed, "This slave is blind and didn't recognize Prince Yi. Prince Yi, please this way."

---

申简辰与乔文仙分别上了轿子，每个轿子由四名侍卫抬着，走过一段路，穿过右承天门，又行了较长一段，在垂拱殿门外停下。

Shen Jianchen and Qiao Wenxian each boarded sedan chairs. Each sedan chair was carried by four guards. They walked for a while, passed through Youchengtian Gate, and traveled a longer distance before stopping outside Chuigong Hall.

---

两人下了轿子后，侍卫很快将轿子抬走，毕竟垂拱殿之外庄严无比，不是放置轿子之处。

After the two got off the sedan chairs, the guards quickly carried them away. After all, outside Chuigong Hall was extremely solemn and not a place to leave sedan chairs.

---

迎面走来一位臣子，身着紫袍，头戴官帽，留着胡须，剑眉鼠目，表情严肃。

Walking towards them was an official wearing a purple robe, an official hat, and a beard. He had sword-like brows and rat-like eyes, with a serious expression.

---

乔文仙上前行礼道："高太尉，文渊这厢有礼了。"

Qiao Wenxian stepped forward and bowed, "Grand Commandant Gao, Wenyuan pays his respects."

---

高太尉瞧了瞧乔文仙："原来是乔公子，最近你可是深得官家喜爱，身边这位是？"

Grand Commandant Gao looked at Qiao Wenxian, "So it's Young Master Qiao. You've been deeply favored by His Majesty recently. Who is this beside you?"

---

乔文仙介绍道："仪王。"

Qiao Wenxian introduced, "Prince Yi."

---

高太尉严肃的表情立刻挤出笑容："原来是仪王，听说您起死回生，疾病全消，真是可喜可贺！老臣今日有幸见到仪王真容，也是幸运。仪王若不嫌弃，与老臣一起进入垂拱殿，官家要先在这里接受朝臣以及文人才子跪拜，然后才带大家去艮岳欣赏美景。请！"

Grand Commandant Gao's serious expression immediately broke into a smile, "So it's Prince Yi. I heard you came back from the dead and all your illnesses are gone. Truly cause for celebration! This old minister is fortunate to see Prince Yi's true appearance today. If Prince Yi doesn't disdain it, please enter Chuigong Hall with this old minister. His Majesty will first receive the kneeling bows of court officials and literary talents here, then lead everyone to Genyue to admire the beautiful scenery. Please!"

---

申简辰在心里琢磨"高太尉"的称呼，难道此人正是《水浒》中被描写成无恶不作的高俅高太尉？因高太尉就在身旁，不好向文渊请教，只能将好奇心暂时搁置。

Shen Jianchen pondered the title "Grand Commandant Gao" in his heart. Could this person be exactly Gao Qiu, the Grand Commandant Gao who was depicted as committing every evil in *Water Margin*? Since Grand Commandant Gao was right beside him, it wasn't appropriate to ask Wenyuan, so he could only temporarily set aside his curiosity.

---

走进垂拱殿的大门，并未真正到殿内，而是一个很大的院落，里面聚集许多人，看衣着，有文武百官，也有像文渊一样的才子在其中。

Walking through the gate of Chuigong Hall, they didn't actually enter the hall itself but a very large courtyard. Many people had gathered inside. Judging by their clothing, there were civil and military officials, as well as talents like Wenyuan.

---

有官员过来与高太尉打招呼，高太尉向大家说："你们猜猜这位是谁？"

An official came over to greet Grand Commandant Gao. Grand Commandant Gao said to everyone, "Guess who this is?"

---

大家都看向申简辰，一脸疑惑："从未见过，还请太尉大人介绍。"

Everyone looked at Shen Jianchen with confused faces, "We've never seen him before. Please introduce him, Grand Commandant."

---

高太尉说："官家的十三子仪王。"

Grand Commandant Gao said, "His Majesty's thirteenth son, Prince Yi."

---

围过来的臣子都听说过仪王死而复生之事，纷纷说道："我等终于有幸见到仪王真容。""能看到仪王健康地站在我等面前，实乃荣幸。"

The officials who gathered around had all heard about Prince Yi's resurrection and said one after another, "We are finally fortunate to see Prince Yi's true appearance." "To see Prince Yi standing healthily before us is truly an honor."

---

高太尉朝远处瞧了瞧，立刻说："仪王，失陪，老臣去那边瞧瞧。"

Grand Commandant Gao looked into the distance and immediately said, "Prince Yi, excuse me. This old minister will go check over there."

---

申简辰说："高太尉尽管去忙。"

Shen Jianchen said, "Grand Commandant Gao, please attend to your duties."

---

高太尉离开后，围过来的臣子也都纷纷离开。申简辰才得空向乔文仙询问："高太尉是否名叫高俅？"

After Grand Commandant Gao left, the officials who had gathered around also dispersed. Only then did Shen Jianchen have the chance to ask Qiao Wenxian, "Is Grand Commandant Gao named Gao Qiu?"

---

乔文仙说："没想到高太尉的大名你倒记得。"

Qiao Wenxian said, "I didn't expect you to remember Grand Commandant Gao's name."

---

申简辰不由脱口而出："大奸臣！"

Shen Jianchen blurted out, "A great treacherous official!"

---

乔文仙赶紧拦道："小声些，周围都是朝中大臣，被听去，传到高太尉耳朵里，对你不利。"

Qiao Wenxian quickly stopped him, "Keep your voice down. There are court officials all around. If they hear and it reaches Grand Commandant Gao's ears, it won't be good for you."

---

"我是官家的儿子，我怕他？"

"I'm His Majesty's son. Why should I fear him?"

---

"你是个不受宠的皇子，有时还比不上朝中的大臣。难道你没看出来大家都不认识你？"

"You're an unfavored prince. Sometimes you're not even as good as the court officials. Haven't you noticed that no one recognizes you?"

---

申简辰瞧向高俅："高太尉正与何人说话？"

Shen Jianchen looked at Gao Qiu, "Who is Grand Commandant Gao talking to?"

---

乔文仙小声说："是太子，你的大哥。"

Qiao Wenxian whispered, "It's the Crown Prince, your eldest brother."

---

申简辰又向另一边看去，也有一位被大臣们围着，看上去应该也是位皇子："他又是谁？"

Shen Jianchen looked to the other side. There was another person surrounded by officials who appeared to also be a prince, "And who is he?"

---

乔文仙朝仪王说的方向瞧去，继续小声说："是康王，你的九哥。"

Qiao Wenxian looked in the direction Prince Yi indicated and continued whispering, "It's Prince of Kang, your ninth brother."

---

有一名内侍朝这边走来，正是申简辰在仪王府第一次见到的人。他悄悄问乔文仙："向我们走过来的内侍叫什么？"

An imperial attendant was walking towards them—exactly the person Shen Jianchen had first seen at Prince Yi's Manor. He quietly asked Qiao Wenxian, "What's the name of the imperial attendant walking towards us?"

---

"梁师成，现任检校太傅，是个散官，但职位不低。"乔文仙尽量压低声音。

"Liang Shicheng, currently the Honorary Grand Tutor. It's a sinecure, but his rank isn't low." Qiao Wenxian lowered his voice as much as possible.

---

申简辰心想："又是一位奸臣！已经见到两位，接下来还不知会见到几位？"

Shen Jianchen thought, "Another treacherous official! Already seen two. Who knows how many more we'll see?"

---

梁师成已走近，乔文仙有礼道："内侍有何指教？"

Liang Shicheng had already approached. Qiao Wenxian said politely, "Does the imperial attendant have any instructions?"

---

梁师成回礼："官家一会儿就到，所有人即将进入大殿内，仪王第一次来，有些礼数不明白，下官前来说明。"

Liang Shicheng returned the courtesy, "His Majesty will arrive shortly. Everyone will soon enter the main hall. Since this is Prince Yi's first time, there are some protocols he may not understand. This official has come to explain."

---

申简辰说："内侍请讲。"

Shen Jianchen said, "Please explain, imperial attendant."

---

梁师成便将规矩详细说明："参加本次宴会的皇子加上仪王共二十六人，一会儿进入大殿内，仪王不能再与乔公子待在一处，要站到皇子处，与众位皇子一起拜见官家。还有，发生在仪王身上的事实属罕见，据下官猜测，官家定会问起，仪王要想好如何回答。就这些，下官已叮嘱完，还有其他事要安排，就不在此打扰。"

Liang Shicheng explained the rules in detail, "Including Prince Yi, there are twenty-six princes attending this banquet. Once we enter the main hall, Prince Yi can no longer stay with Young Master Qiao. You must stand with the other princes and greet His Majesty together with them. Also, what happened to Prince Yi is truly rare. This official guesses His Majesty will definitely ask about it. Prince Yi should think carefully about how to answer. That's all. This official has finished his instructions and has other matters to arrange, so I won't disturb you further."

---

梁师成走后，申简辰继续问："难道进入大殿后我都没办法跟你待在一处？"

After Liang Shicheng left, Shen Jianchen continued asking, "So after entering the main hall, I won't be able to stay with you at all?"

---

"放心，大殿内拜见官家，礼数还是要遵从的，离开大殿后比较自由，你我还能在一处。"

"Don't worry. When greeting His Majesty in the main hall, we must still follow protocol. But after leaving the main hall, it's more free. We can still be together."

---

"那便好。"

"That's good."

---

只听传来梁师成的声音："请各位进入大殿！"

They heard Liang Shicheng's voice announce, "Please everyone enter the main hall!"
