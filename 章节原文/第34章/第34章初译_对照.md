第34章 打听官家遇奸臣
## Chapter 34: Asking After His Majesty, Meeting Treacherous Officials

内侍走后，申简辰瞧着手中的请帖，只觉麻烦，官家既然不重视仪王，为何还要送来请帖？搞得他还得留在汴梁。
Once the eunuch left, Shen Jianchen stared at the invitation feeling annoyed—never favored Prince Yi before, why summon him now and stall the trip to Lin'an?

申简辰觉得自己变了，穿越前是公司CEO，最是喜欢跟身份尊贵，有钱之人打交道，现在竟然变得无所谓，甚至从心里觉得麻烦。也许人只有死过一回，才能明白金钱名利如过眼云烟。
Funny how he had changed: back as a CEO, he loved rubbing elbows with powerful, wealthy elites; now he couldn't care less and found court politics a headache. Guess dying once really showed how hollow cash and status were.

又一想，新觅的徒弟白风还未前来报到，不如趁宴会多留一日，等他亲自送上门来。
On the bright side, his new apprentice Bai Feng hadn't shown up yet—staying one extra day gave the kid time to report in.

乔梦容高兴道：“官家终于邀请哥哥！哥哥不必紧张，官家从未见过你，对以前的哥哥并不了解，你只需以真实的状态轻松应对即可。”
Qiao Mengrong beamed with pride: "His Majesty finally invited you, Brother! Don't be nervous—he's never met you before and doesn't know your old self, so just be natural and relaxed!"

申简辰对乔梦容的体贴投以微笑。
Shen Jianchen gave her a warm smile for her thoughtfulness.

乔梦容又转向乔文仙：“乔公子是官家宴会中的常客，对官家的喜好多少了解一些，不如给哥哥讲讲，让他好提前有个心里准备。”
Turning to Qiao Wenxian, Qiao Mengrong asked, "Young Master Qiao attends palace banquets all the time and knows His Majesty's tastes—please brief Brother so he knows what to expect."

“谨遵郡主吩咐。”乔文仙如数家珍般详细说明，“当今官家书法、绘画、诗词歌赋无一不通，极其推崇文人雅致，最喜举办宴会，不仅朝中大臣会参加，还会邀请有名的文人雅士。鄙人略懂书画歌赋，又能说上两句评论之语，也不知官家如何得知我的名头，在汴梁这段时日，只要宫中有活动，总会邀请我参加。”
"At your command, Princess," Qiao Wenxian smiled, rattling off details with ease. "His Majesty excels at calligraphy, painting, poetry, and lyrics alike—he adores scholarly elegance and throws banquets constantly, inviting both high ministers and noted literati. Dabbling a bit in art critique myself, somehow my modest name reached court; while living in Bianliang, I get invited to nearly every palace gathering."

乔梦容赞道：“乔公子过谦，你可是江南有名的才子，不仅在江南为人所知，到汴梁这段日子，也逐渐名声鹊起，足以证明你的才华。要说这江南真是人才辈出，我想起临安有位女词人，名叫朱淑真，她的诗词我读过一些，真是才华斐然，不知乔公子可见过她？”
"Too modest, Young Master Qiao!" Qiao Mengrong praised. "You're already celebrated across Jiangnan, and now your fame spreads through Bianliang too! Speaking of Jiangnan's rich talent, there's a female ci poet in Lin'an named Zhu Shuzhen—I've read several of her lyrics, truly brilliant! Have you ever met her?"

乔文仙叹道：“唉，她正是乔某喜欢之人。”
Qiao Wenxian let out a soft sigh. "She is the very woman I love."

乔梦容也叹道：“原来乔公子喜欢的人是她。”
Qiao Mengrong sighed in echo. "So it's her..."

申简辰极少关注文学，他的文学知识仅限于上学期间所涉猎。上中学时，语文这门功课成绩还不错，但要站在专业角度鉴赏文学作品，他还是挺发憷的。
Literature had never been Shen Jianchen's strong suit—his exposure stopped at high-school Chinese class; respectable grades back then, sure, yet professional literary critique terrified him.

后来，因为千里寻写网络小说，他才会捧着手机读那些无聊的文字，只为追求美人时，能找到除拳法以外的共同话题。
Only because Qianli Xun wrote web novels had he forced himself to scroll through her books on his phone—just to find shared topics beyond boxing while wooing her!

在爱情力量的驱使下，他硬着头皮把千里寻写过的小说统统看了个遍，也仔细思索和体味过，对于网络小说这种文学形式还能略微谈些见解。
Driven by love, he had slogged through every single novel she published, digesting them enough to toss out decent comments on web fiction.

可对于那些没学过没见过的古诗词，他绝对是门外汉，更别说书画之类，更是一窍不通，参加官家的宴会只怕会出丑，给赵朴脸上抹黑。
Classical poetry and ink painting? Total blank! Showing up at an emperor's artsy banquet risked embarrassing Zhao Pu flat.

申简辰迅速打定主意，到时能躲多远便躲多远，那么多人，官家应该不会注意到他，毕竟赵朴不是官家在意的儿子。
Plan set fast: keep a low profile, blend into the crowd—with dozens of guests and zero imperial favor, His Majesty wouldn't spare him a glance.

乔文仙看出仪王面色中有些许担忧，宽慰道：“仪王不必担忧，到时你只需跟紧我，有什么不懂的，我悄悄告诉你。”
Spotting worry on his face, Qiao Wenxian reassured him: "Don't sweat it, Prince Yi—stick close to me, anything unfamiliar I'll whisper in your ear."

乔梦容说：“有乔公子照顾，哥哥尽管放宽心。”
"With Young Master Qiao looking out for you, Brother, nothing to worry about," Qiao Mengrong added.

申简辰先谢道：“有劳文渊。”
"Much obliged, Wenyuan," Shen Jianchen thanked him.

第二天，申简辰与乔文仙乘坐马车应邀前往皇宫，他对即将谋面的父皇实在不熟悉，还想从乔文仙处多了解些信息：“文渊，我父皇叫什么名字？”
Next day, riding the carriage toward the palace with Qiao Wenxian, Shen Jianchen figured he ought to learn basic facts about the father he was about to meet: "Wenyuan, what's my father's name?"

乔文仙凑近，小声说：“官家的名讳本不应随便提起，仪王既问起，我就悄悄告诉你，当今官家大名赵佶。”
Leaning close, Qiao Wenxian whispered: "One shouldn't speak the sovereign's personal name aloud—since you asked, quietly: His Majesty is Zhao Ji."

穿越前申简辰虽不大关注过去的历史，可上学期间获得的历史知识还刻在脑中，宣和之类的年号他不甚熟悉，历代帝王的名讳还是有较为深刻的印象，赵佶不就是宋徽宗吗？
Rusty on reign titles like Xuanhe he might be, yet emperor names from school history still stuck—Zhao Ji was Emperor Huizong of Song!

他在历史上是位昏君，重用蔡京、王黼、童贯、高俅、朱勔、梁师成等人，这些人奸诈成性，横征暴敛，骄奢淫逸，要不怎么有会《水浒》一百零八个好汉被逼上梁山的故事？
One of history's most notorious incompetent emperors—elevating treacherous scoundrels like Cai Jing, Wang Fu, Tong Guan, Gao Qiu, Zhu Mian, and Liang Shicheng to bleed the people dry in luxury, sparking the whole Water Margin saga of the hundred and eight heroes driven to Mount Liang!

想到这里，申简辰既失落又轻松，失落的是穿越而来，竟成为昏君的儿子；之所以轻松，是因为他从心里瞧不上昏君，既瞧不上便不会紧张，更不怕在昏君面前出丑。要知道昏君出的丑历史都记着呢！他申简辰是普通人物，出点儿丑最多被昏君和周围人笑笑，历史根本不会把他当回事。
Mixed feelings washed over Shen Jianchen: bummed to wake up son to an incompetent emperor, yet oddly relaxed—zero respect meant zero nerves! Who cared if he slipped up in front of Huizong? History remembered Huizong's blunders, not some obscure thirteenth prince's gaffes!

乔文仙奇怪道：“仪王听了官家的名讳，好像轻松许多，是何缘故？”
Puzzled, Qiao Wenxian asked, "You look way more relaxed hearing His Majesty's name—why?"

申简辰没有回答，继续问：“不知文渊对我父皇，以及他管理的天下有何看法？”
Skipping the question, Shen Jianchen asked instead: "What do you honestly think of my father—and how he rules this empire?"

乔文仙说：“作为臣民，不应私下随便议论，不过仪王问起，我不妨说两句。”
"Subjects shouldn't gossip about court in private—yet since you ask, let me speak plain."

乔文仙又凑近仪王耳边：“当今官家是书画大家，用才华横溢四字形容绝不为过，大宋的天下表面一派繁荣祥和，老百姓的日子过得也算富足，不说别的，这汴梁街市的繁华便可见一斑，有时间我带你晚上出来逛逛，那才叫一个热闹……”
Lowering his voice further, Qiao Wenxian leaned close: "His Majesty is a towering master of calligraphy and painting—calling him gifted is no exaggeration. On the surface, the Great Song looks prosperous and peaceful, townsfolk live fairly well—just look at Bianliang's booming streets! Someday I'll take you out to see the night markets—dazzling..."

没等乔文仙说完，申简忍不住问：“你说表面一派繁荣祥和，实质上呢？”
Cutting right in, Shen Jianchen pressed: "On the surface—what about underneath?"

乔文仙声音压得更低：“实质上藏有隐患，外有北边金人虎视眈眈，内有起义军作乱，去年淮南盗生事，后被海州知州张叔夜打败，生擒义军副将，这股义军才不得不降。你可知淮南盗的首领是谁？”
Dropping to a bare murmur, Qiao Wenxian said, "Hidden perils everywhere. Up north the Jurchens of Jin eye us like tigers; inside, rebellions flare. Last year Huainan bandits rose up until Prefect Zhang Shuye of Haizhou defeated them and captured their deputy leader, forcing their surrender. Know who led those Huainan rebels?"

申简辰当然不知，摇摇头。
Shen Jianchen shook his head.

“是位乡里小民，名叫宋江。”
"A rural clerk named Song Jiang."

申简辰立刻来了兴致：“宋江，我知道，他性情豪放，乐善好施，结识到不少英雄好汉，在百姓中有较高威望，是梁山起义的领袖，人称‘及时雨’……”
Eyes lighting up, Shen Jianchen burst out: "Song Jiang—I know him! Bold, generous, rallying heroes across the land, beloved by common folk, leader of the Mount Liang uprising—nicknamed Timely Rain!..."

正说着，见乔文仙用奇怪的目光看着他：“宋江反的可是你们赵家的天下，你怎么说起他如此兴奋？”
Mid-rave, he caught Qiao Wenxian staring at him wide-eyed: "Song Jiang rebelled against YOUR Zhao dynasty—why are you glowing about him?!"

申简辰赶紧解释：“我曾看过一本书，上面有位英雄豪杰，也叫宋江，没想到历史上真有宋江此人，不小心把两个人搞混了。”
Scrambling to cover, Shen Jianchen lied fast: "Read a book once with a hero named Song Jiang—didn't realize there was a real historical Song Jiang! Mixed the two up!"

“真怀疑书上的宋江就是照着淮南盗首领宋江写的，要不你怎么说得那么像，是什么书？借我瞧瞧。”
"Sounds like that book copied the real Huainan bandit leader—how else match him so close? What book is it? Lend it to me!"

申简辰感到为难，又觉得好笑，宋代根本没有《水浒》，于是胡乱编道：“是我死后在另一个世界看的，看完又活过来，其他不记得，只记得这本书。”
Stuck—Water Margin wouldn't be written for centuries!—he spun on the fly: "Read it in the afterlife after I died! Woke back up remembering nothing else except that book."

“仪王该不会是骗我吧？能有这等神奇之事？”
"Pulling my leg, Prince Yi? How could anything that wild happen?"

“我可是起死回生之人，难道不能证明世上有神奇之事？”
"I literally came back from the dead—doesn't that prove wild things happen?"

乔文仙只能承认：“嗯……，也对，你以前病成那样，我就是长九颗脑袋，也不会想到有一天你能健康地坐在马车里和我聊天儿，好吧，信你一回。”
Qiao Wenxian had to concede: "Fair enough... frail as you were before, even with nine heads I never would've pictured you sitting healthy in a carriage bantering with me. Fine, I'll buy it."

申简辰又问：“也不知官家今日因何举行办宴会？”
Changing tracks, Shen Jianchen asked, "What's today's palace banquet even celebrating?"

“官家举办宴会是常事，尤其在官家生日之际，场面更是宏大，而且要提前一个月准备。至于这次，如果我没猜错，应该是艮岳落成，庆祝庆祝。”
"His Majesty throws banquets nonstop—especially his birthday, planned a full month ahead! Today, if I guess right, celebrates completing Genyue."

“艮岳是什么？”
"What's Genyue?"

“看来你真的什么都不记得，艮岳是官家建的风景园林，里面有天下之灵石，南方艳美珍奇之花木，设有雕阑曲槛，修葺着亭台楼阁，历经数十年时间才建成。”
"Truly wiped your memory clean! Genyue is His Majesty's grand imperial landscape park—rare scholar rocks hauled from across the empire, exotic southern trees and blooms, carved railings, pavilions and towers—took decades to build!"

申简辰在心里批判道：“奢侈！不知搜刮了多少民脂民膏！”
Shen Jianchen cursed under his breath: Pure excess—how much of the people's blood and sweat did that suck dry?!

两人继续聊着，已到皇宫门外。下了马车，抬头看到“西华门”三个大字，仪王府离西华门较近，从这里进皇宫倒也方便。
Chatting on, they pulled up outside the palace walls beneath the sign "Xihua Gate"—closest gate to Prince Yi's Manor, nice and convenient.

申简辰向皇宫门口的侍卫出示了请帖，侍卫看后，恭敬地说：“仪王，乔公子，请。”
Shen Jianchen showed his invitation to the gate guards, who bowed deep: "Enter, Prince Yi, Young Master Qiao."

他们走进皇宫，面前是一条直直的用石砖铺就的大道，大道旁整齐地摆放着轿子，每个轿子跟前站着四名侍卫。
Inside stretched a broad stone avenue lined with waiting sedan chairs, four guards stationed beside each.

一位内侍看到他们进来，迎上前：“乔公子请上轿。”
An attendant hurried forward: "Your sedan awaits, Young Master Qiao."

“这位是仪王。”乔文仙见内侍未招呼仪王，猜他并不认识，故而介绍道。
Seeing the eunuch ignore his companion, Qiao Wenxian introduced him: "This is Prince Yi."

内侍连忙行礼：“奴才眼拙，没认出仪王，仪王这边请。”
The eunuch dropped into a hasty bow: "Blind eyes, forgive this humble servant—Prince Yi, right this way!"

申简辰与乔文仙分别上了轿子，每个轿子由四名侍卫抬着，走过一段路，穿过右承天门，又行了较长一段，在垂拱殿门外停下。
Each boarding a sedan chair hoisted by four guards, they passed through Youchengtian Gate and rode a long stretch before halting outside Chuigong Hall.

两人下了轿子后，侍卫很快将轿子抬走，毕竟垂拱殿之外庄严无比，不是放置轿子之处。
The instant they stepped out, guards whisked the chairs away—the sacred forecourt of Chuigong Hall tolerated no clutter.

迎面走来一位臣子，身着紫袍，头戴官帽，留着胡须，剑眉鼠目，表情严肃。
Approaching them came a purple-robed official wearing court headgear—bearded, sharp slanted brows, beady rat eyes, stern expression.

乔文仙上前行礼道：“高太尉，文渊这厢有礼了。”
Qiao Wenxian bowed politely: "Grand Commandant Gao, Wenyuan pays respects."

高太尉瞧了瞧乔文仙：“原来是乔公子，最近你可是深得官家喜爱，身边这位是？”
Sizing up Qiao Wenxian, Grand Commandant Gao nodded: "Ah, Young Master Qiao—quite His Majesty's favorite of late! And your companion?"

乔文仙介绍道：“仪王。”
"Prince Yi," Qiao Wenxian announced.

高太尉严肃的表情立刻挤出笑容：“原来是仪王，听说您起死回生，疾病全消，真是可喜可贺！老臣今日有幸见到仪王真容，也是幸运。仪王若不嫌弃，与老臣一起进入垂拱殿，官家要先在这里接受朝臣以及文人才子跪拜，然后才带大家去艮岳欣赏美景。请！”
Instantly the official's stony face melted into a broad smile: "Prince Yi! Heard of your miraculous revival and full recovery—joyous news indeed! Meeting Your Highness in person honors this old minister! If you don't mind, enter Chuigong Hall with me—His Majesty receives the kowtows of court officials and scholars here first before leading everyone to tour Genyue. After you!"

申简辰在心里琢磨“高太尉”的称呼，难道此人正是《水浒》中被描写成无恶不作的高俅高太尉？因高太尉就在身旁，不好向文渊请教，只能将好奇心暂时搁置。
Grand Commandant Gao... could this be the infamous villain Gao Qiu from Water Margin?! With the man walking right beside him, Shen Jianchen held back from asking Wenyuan.

走进垂拱殿的大门，并未真正到殿内，而是一个很大的院落，里面聚集许多人，看衣着，有文武百官，也有像文渊一样的才子在其中。
Stepping through Chuigong Hall's outer gate opened into a vast courtyard teeming with officials in court robes and scholars like Wenyuan.

有官员过来与高太尉打招呼，高太尉向大家说：“你们猜猜这位是谁？”
Fellow ministers stepped up greeting Grand Commandant Gao, who beamed: "Guess who stands before you!"

大家都看向申简辰，一脸疑惑：“从未见过，还请太尉大人介绍。”
Every eye turned curious onto Shen Jianchen: "Never met him—please introduce us, Grand Commandant."

高太尉说：“官家的十三子仪王。”
"His Majesty's thirteenth son—Prince Yi!"

围过来的臣子都听说过仪王死而复生之事，纷纷说道：“我等终于有幸见到仪王真容。”“能看到仪王健康地站在我等面前，实乃荣幸。”
Having heard whispers of his resurrection, the ministers crowded in with smooth greetings: "At last we behold Prince Yi in person!" "An honor to see Your Highness standing hale before us!"

高太尉朝远处瞧了瞧，立刻说：“仪王，失陪，老臣去那边瞧瞧。”
Spotting someone across the yard, Grand Commandant Gao excused himself fast: "Pardon me, Prince Yi—let me step over there."

申简辰说：“高太尉尽管去忙。”
"Go right ahead, Grand Commandant."

高太尉离开后，围过来的臣子也都纷纷离开。申简辰才得空向乔文仙询问：“高太尉是否名叫高俅？”
The instant Gao left, the cluster of officials melted away too, finally leaving Shen Jianchen room to whisper: "Is Grand Commandant Gao's name Gao Qiu?"

乔文仙说：“没想到高太尉的大名你倒记得。”
"Well, well—you actually remember Grand Commandant Gao's name!" Qiao Wenxian marveled.

申简辰不由脱口而出：“大奸臣！”
"Major treacherous villain!" Shen Jianchen blurted out.

乔文仙赶紧拦道：“小声些，周围都是朝中大臣，被听去，传到高太尉耳朵里，对你不利。”
Qiao Wenxian hissed in panic: "Lower your voice! Court ministers everywhere—let word reach Grand Commandant Gao and you're in deep trouble!"

“我是官家的儿子，我怕他？”
"I'm the emperor's son—scared of him?!"

“你是个不受宠的皇子，有时还比不上朝中的大臣。难道你没看出来大家都不认识你？”
"Unfavored prince—sometimes lower in pull than senior ministers! Didn't you notice nobody here even recognized you?"

申简辰瞧向高俅：“高太尉正与何人说话？”
Glancing back toward Gao Qiu, Shen Jianchen asked, "Who's he talking to over there?"

乔文仙小声说：“是太子，你的大哥。”
"The Crown Prince—your eldest brother," Qiao Wenxian murmured.

申简辰又向另一边看去，也有一位被大臣们围着，看上去应该也是位皇子：“他又是谁？”
Looking the other way at another young man ringed by officials: "And him?"

乔文仙朝仪王说的方向瞧去，继续小声说：“是康王，你的九哥。”
"Prince of Kang—your ninth brother."

有一名内侍朝这边走来，正是申简辰在仪王府第一次见到的人。他悄悄问乔文仙：“向我们走过来的内侍叫什么？”
Just then an Imperial Attendant strode toward them—the very senior eunuch who had visited Prince Yi's Manor first! Shen Jianchen whispered: "Who's that eunuch walking over?"

“梁师成，现任检校太傅，是个散官，但职位不低。”乔文仙尽量压低声音。
"Liang Shicheng—Honorary Grand Tutor! Sinecure title, yet massive clout," Qiao Wenxian breathed low.

申简辰心想：“又是一位奸臣！已经见到两位，接下来还不知会见到几位？”
Another treacherous villain! Two down already—how many more today?!

梁师成已走近，乔文仙有礼道：“内侍有何指教？”
As Liang Shicheng arrived, Qiao Wenxian bowed: "What instructions, Attendant?"

梁师成回礼：“官家一会儿就到，所有人即将进入大殿内，仪王第一次来，有些礼数不明白，下官前来说明。”
Returning the bow, Liang Shicheng spoke smoothly: "His Majesty arrives shortly and everyone enters the hall. Since this is Prince Yi's first attendance, allow me to brief you on protocol."

申简辰说：“内侍请讲。”
"Go ahead, Attendant."

梁师成便将规矩详细说明：“参加本次宴会的皇子加上仪王共二十六人，一会儿进入大殿内，仪王不能再与乔公子待在一处，要站到皇子处，与众位皇子一起拜见官家。还有，发生在仪王身上的事实属罕见，据下官猜测，官家定会问起，仪王要想好如何回答。就这些，下官已叮嘱完，还有其他事要安排，就不在此打扰。”
Liang Shicheng ticked off the rules: "Including Prince Yi, twenty-six imperial princes attend today. Inside the hall, Your Highness cannot stand with Young Master Qiao—you join the princes' ranks to kowtow before His Majesty together. Also, your revival is extraordinary; I expect His Majesty will ask about it, so have your answer ready. That is all—excuse me, other duties call."

梁师成走后，申简辰继续问：“难道进入大殿后我都没办法跟你待在一处？”
Once Liang Shicheng withdrew, Shen Jianchen asked, "Wait—do we have to split up inside the hall?"

“放心，大殿内拜见官家，礼数还是要遵从的，离开大殿后比较自由，你我还能在一处。”
"Only for the formal kowtow inside—once we leave the hall, everyone mingles free and we stick together."

“那便好。”
"Good."

只听传来梁师成的声音：“请各位进入大殿！”
Liang Shicheng's sharp cry echoed across the yard: "All assemble inside the hall!"
