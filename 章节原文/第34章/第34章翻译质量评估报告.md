## 二、必须修改（2处）

### 问题1：中英混杂
**位置**：第341段（高俅对话）
**原文**：
> 高太尉严肃的表情立刻挤出笑容："原来是仪王，听说您起死回生，疾病全消，真是可喜可贺！
**当前译文**：
> Grand Commandant Gao's serious expression immediately squeezed into a smile, "So it's Prince Yi. I heard you came back from the dead and all your illnesses are gone. Truly可喜可贺!
**问题**：出现中文"可喜可贺"，属于严重的中英混杂错误
**修改建议**：
```
...all your illnesses are gone. Truly cause for celebration!
```

### 问题2：用词不当——"unofficial position"
**位置**：第461段（梁师成介绍）
**原文**：
> "梁师成，现任检校太傅，是个散官，但职位不低。"
**当前译文**：
> "Liang Shicheng, currently the Honorary Grand Tutor. He's an unofficial position, but his rank isn't low."
**问题**："散官"指无实权的官职，不是"unofficial position"（非官方职位）。应译为"sinecure"或"honorary position without real power"
**修改建议**：
```
"Liang Shicheng, currently the Honorary Grand Tutor. It's a sinecure, but his rank isn't low."
```

## 三、建议优化（4处）

### 优化1：重复用词——"imperial attendant"
**位置**：多处（内侍）
**问题**："内侍"在本章节多次出现，均译为"imperial attendant"，可适当变化
**建议**：首次用"imperial attendant"，后续可简化为"attendant"或"eunuch"交替使用

### 优化2：口语化表达——"squeezed into a smile"
**位置**：第341段
**原文**：
> 高太尉严肃的表情立刻挤出笑容
**当前译文**：
> Grand Commandant Gao's serious expression immediately squeezed into a smile
**建议**："squeezed into a smile"略显生硬，可改为"immediately broke into a smile"更自然

### 优化3：文化注释——"艮岳"
**位置**：第245、257段
**当前译文**：
> Genyue
**建议**：可增加简短译者注说明艮岳的历史意义：
```
[TN: Genyue (艮岳) was an imperial garden built by Emperor Huizong of Song. It was famous for its elaborate landscaping and rare plants, but its construction drained the imperial treasury and burdened the people with heavy taxes.]
```

### 优化4：历史人物注释——"宋江"
**位置**：第185、191段
**建议**：可增加简短译者注区分历史人物与小说形象：
```
[TN: Song Jiang was a real historical figure who led a rebellion during the Song Dynasty. He later became the protagonist of the classic Chinese novel *Water Margin*, where he was portrayed as a heroic outlaw.]
```
