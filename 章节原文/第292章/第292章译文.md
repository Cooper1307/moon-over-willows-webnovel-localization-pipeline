## Chapter 292: An Invitation to the Grand Poetry Salon

Taking her blunt truth to heart—vowing to turn the Qiantang Gang respectable for his wife's sake—Master Jiang brightened when Qianli Xun offered to bring Madam Jiang along to a poetry salon someday, only for Chun Jinmo to reject the offer coldly and shoo her husband away for a private word.

Learning Chen Haoyu had been acquitted and Chun Jinlan had killed herself, Jinmo turned bitter—mocking Qianli Xun's "royal affair" and warning that plenty of deadlier killers than her "spineless" husband existed for Princess Consort Yi's silver! Advising Jinmo one last time to cherish the man who genuinely loved her, Qianli Xun left feeling weirdly satisfied: at least Master Jiang was off Mengrong's payroll.

Back at Chen Manor's south gate, Dongfang Chenze's page Huayu stood waiting with an invitation to a grand new poetry salon at Juxiang Tower—exclusively for Lin'an's top poets and officials (even Miao Zhiyu didn't make the cut!), requesting a fresh lyric from Youqi Jushi.

Smart enough to consult the elders before attending a male-heavy salon, Qianli Xun walked straight to Old Madam Xia's hall—where the matriarch had just announced Chen Jianglin's decision to move the heir election up to this year (thrilling Madam Bao). Old Madam Xia and Yao Qingyan heartily blessed her going—and Madam Cui beamed with pride that Chen Haofei had been invited too, proudly revealing Prince Yi himself was bankrolling Dongfang Chenze's salon to make it Lin'an's number one!

Old Madam Xia also shared that she'd overruled Prefect Zhu's push to close the congee shed early—the Chen family would fund both the Lazybones Village resettlement *and* keep feeding Lin'an's paupers until the new cottages finished. Finally, Yao Qingyan gently assured Shuzhen the elders would stay wholly out of her and Haoyu's private pace—deepening Qianli Xun's quiet warmth for her new family.
