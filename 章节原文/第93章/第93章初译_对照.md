第93章 花心渣男大萝卜
## Chapter 93: A Shameless, Two-Timing Playboy

千里寻刚自语完，听到佳和郡主的声音：“幽栖居士怎么一个人在哥哥的院外？”
The words had no sooner left Qianli Xun's mouth than Princess Jiahe's voice rang out: "Why is Youqi Jushi standing alone outside Brother's courtyard?"

千里寻担心自言自语的内容被郡主听到：“佳和郡主有没有听到我刚才说的话？”
Worried the princess might have overheard her muttering, Qianli Xun asked, "Did Princess Jiahe hear what I just said?"

“你刚才说话了吗？我什么都没听到，再说只有你一个人，难道你是自说自话？”
"Were you speaking just now? I didn't hear a thing. Besides, you're all alone. Were you talking to yourself?"

看来对方没听到，千里寻总算放心：“我刚才是自语来着，感叹仪王真是老百姓的好王爷，我求他办事，他竟然一口答应，令我感动不已。”
Seeing she had not heard, Qianli Xun let out a quiet breath of relief. "I was talking to myself, yes, just marveling at how good Prince Yi is to the common folk—a true prince of the people. I came to ask him a favor, and he agreed on the spot. I'm moved beyond words."

“原来如此，幽栖居士既然没跟乔公子一起，不如陪我在园子里走走？”
"I see. Since Youqi Jushi isn't with Young Master Qiao right now, how about taking a walk with me through the garden?"

佳和郡主邀请，千里寻不好拒绝，不如跟她聊聊，顺便提醒她仪王是个靠不住的男人：“淑真不胜荣幸。”
With Princess Jiahe extending an invitation, Qianli Xun could not very well refuse. She might as well chat with her and drop a hint that Prince Yi was not a man to be trusted. "Shuzhen would be honored."

乔梦容本想找哥哥，有幸碰到幽栖居士，两人又十分投缘，加之她早想结交一位闺中密友，有事没事可与密友畅聊心事，她认为幽栖居士是绝佳人选。
Qiao Mengrong had meant to look for Prince Yi, only to run into Youqi Jushi instead. The two of them hit it off so well, and she had long wished for a close female confidante with whom she could share her heart whenever she pleased. To her mind, Youqi Jushi was the ideal choice.

两个女子行走在别居的小径，别居内尽是些高大的树木，立秋时节更显凉爽。
The two young women strolled along the retreat's paths. Tall trees lined the grounds, making the air all the cooler now that the Beginning of Autumn had arrived.

行了没几步，千里寻问：“听说郡主与仪王有婚约，还是官家赐婚？”
After only a few steps, Qianli Xun asked, "I heard you and Prince Yi are engaged, Princess, and by His Majesty's own imperial betrothal?"

乔梦容点点头：“我与哥哥青梅竹马，一起长大，官家感念哥哥从小失去母妃，我一直在他身边照料，才赐婚，其实官家不赐婚，我也非哥哥不嫁。”
Qiao Mengrong nodded. "Brother and I grew up together as childhood sweethearts. His Majesty was touched that I stayed by his side looking after him ever since he lost his mother, the Consort, as a child, so he granted us the marriage decree. Truth be told, even without His Majesty's decree, I would never marry anyone but Brother."

佳和郡主如此痴情，更令千里寻为她不值：“郡主有没有想过，男人一旦有钱又有地位，身边不会只有一个女人。我朝官员好养侍妾，不瞒你说，我爹养的那些歌伎舞伎，名义上是跳舞唱歌的，其实你懂的。仪王是王爷，身份更尊贵，恐怕也会受此风气影响，难以专情……”
Princess Jiahe's deep devotion made Qianli Xun feel even worse for her. "Have you ever considered, Princess, that once a man has wealth and status, he never stops at just one woman? Officials in our dynasty love keeping concubines. To be honest with you, the singing girls and dancing girls my father keeps are hired to sing and dance in name, though you know how that goes. Prince Yi is a royal prince of even higher rank. I'm afraid he might pick up the same habits and struggle to stay faithful..."

乔梦容赶紧解释：“哥哥不是那样的人，他病着时，心里眼里只有我……”
Qiao Mengrong hurried to defend him. "Brother isn't like that! Back when he was ill, he had room in his heart and eyes for no one but me..."

“好了呢？”
"And now that he's recovered?"

“好了……好了也是如此。”乔梦容不愿承认哥哥起死回生后面对她时的变化。
"Now... now it's just the same." Qiao Mengrong could not bring herself to admit how Prince Yi had changed toward her since being brought back to life.

千里寻心想：“可怜郡主如此痴情，却不知她的哥哥是个花心大萝卜，喜欢到处留情，连我这种名花有主之人都不放过，实在龌龊！”
Qianli Xun thought to herself, "Poor Princess, so head over heels for him, with no idea that 'Brother' of hers is a total playboy who flirts with every skirt he sees. He won't even spare a taken woman like me! Disgusting!"

她继续对郡主说：“郡主跟仪王有官家赐婚，必然要嫁给他。看在我与郡主十分投缘的份上，我给你出个主意，保准你以后的日子会好过些。”
She kept talking to the princess. "Since you and Prince Yi have an imperial betrothal from His Majesty, you're bound to marry him. Because you and I get along so well, let me give you some advice. I guarantee it'll make your life ahead much easier."

“幽栖居士请说。”
"Please speak, Youqi Jushi."

“你将来肯定是仪王府的当家主母，以后要把主母该有的权利抓在手中，还要多攒钱，趁仪王没纳其他女子时，想方设法从他身上多捞些钱财，最好是田产铺子之类，让他转到你名下。然后再跟他生个儿子，奠定你的地位。等那些乱七八糟的女人进来后，你也不要管那么多，你管得越多，他越烦，你不管，他还觉得你懂事，然后利用他对你的好印象，再多捞些钱财，钱财总比人靠得住。”
"You're bound to become the mistress of Prince Yi's Manor one day. Keep a tight grip on every bit of authority due to the primary wife, and stash away plenty of money. Before Prince Yi takes any other women, find every way you can to squeeze wealth out of him—landed estates and shops are best—and get them put in your name. Then bear him a son to lock down your standing. Once those messy other women start moving in, don't bother policing him too hard. The more you nag, the more annoyed he'll get. Leave him alone, and he'll think you're sensible and gracious, then use his goodwill to grab even more wealth. Money is always more reliable than a man."

千里寻说了许多，乔梦容听着很懵，还是解释道：“我不缺钱财，除官家赐给我郡主的身份时，顺带给的赏赐之物，加之仪王府的一半财产，够我花好几辈子的，我只想跟哥哥白头偕老。”
Qianli Xun rattled off all this advice while Qiao Mengrong listened in a daze, before explaining, "I'm not short on wealth. Between the gifts His Majesty bestowed when he granted me the title of Princess and half the property of Prince Yi's Manor, I have enough to spend for several lifetimes. All I want is to grow old together with Brother."

千里寻继续说：“既然你不缺钱，以后嫁给仪王就从心态这一块努力，把心态放好，不管他以后纳多少女子，你都不要当回事，也不要把仪王看得太重，否则受伤的只有自己。”
Qianli Xun pressed on. "Since you don't need money, then once you marry Prince Yi, work on your mindset instead. Keep a cool head. No matter how many women he takes in later, don't let it get to you, and don't invest your whole heart in Prince Yi, or you'll be the only one getting hurt."

“幽栖居士放心，哥哥曾说过，他只娶我一人，身边不会再有其他女子。”
"Don't worry, Youqi Jushi. Brother promised he will marry only me and never keep another woman by his side."

佳和郡主如此信任仪王，更令千里寻觉得仪王就是个渣男，一边欺骗温柔的妹妹，一边对其他女子放电：“唉，反正你记住我的话，这世上最靠不住的就是男人说过的话，你要多留个心眼儿。”
Seeing Princess Jiahe place such blind trust in Prince Yi convinced Qianli Xun all the more that he was a total scumbag, stringing along such a sweet, gentle girl with one hand while sending sparks at other women with the other. "Sigh. Just remember what I told you anyway. The least trustworthy thing in this world is a man's promise. Keep your wits about you."

乔梦容不认可幽栖居士所言，依然充满感激，幽栖居士所说都是为她考虑。第一次有人跟她畅聊男女之情，她倍感亲切，笑道：“幽栖居士可相信乔公子说过的话？”
Even though Qiao Mengrong did not agree with what Youqi Jushi said, she still felt grateful; she knew Youqi Jushi had her best interests at heart. For the first time, someone had shared such a frank heart-to-heart with her about matters between men and women, and she felt a warm closeness to her. Smiling, she asked, "Does Youqi Jushi believe what Young Master Qiao says?"

“我……我当然相信，文渊跟别的男人不一样！”千里寻发现她一味担心佳和郡主，忘记了文渊。
"I... of course I believe him! Wenyuan isn't like other men!" Qianli Xun realized she had been so busy worrying about Princess Jiahe that she had forgotten about Wenyuan.

“在我眼中，哥哥跟其他男人也不一样。”佳和郡主同样肯定着自己的哥哥。
"In my eyes, Brother isn't like other men either." Princess Jiahe stood by Prince Yi with the same certainty.

千里寻只觉恋爱中的女子智商均为零，佳和郡主如此，她是否也会如此？很快她否认自己的智商为零的想法。文渊是这个世界上最完美的男子，无懈可击，她很幸运，碰上他，还与他相爱。
Qianli Xun couldn't help thinking that women in love all had zero IQ. Princess Jiahe sure fit the bill—wait, could she be the same? In a heartbeat, she dismissed the thought that her own IQ had dropped to zero. Wenyuan was the most flawless man in the world, without a single blemish. How lucky she was to have met him and fallen in love with him.

游玩结束后，三位公子纷纷乘坐马车离开。乔文仙让淑真等一会儿，南双去帮她取衣裳。
Once the outing wrapped up, the three young masters boarded their carriages and departed one after another. Qiao Wenxian asked Shuzhen to wait a moment while Nan Shuang fetched her clothes.

原来是游湖那日放在裁缝铺的衣裳，千里寻差点儿忘记。
It turned out to be the garments left at the tailor shop on the day of their lake excursion; Qianli Xun had almost forgotten about them.

不一会儿，南双手中托着盒子走出大门，将盒子送到苦荞手中。
Before long, Nan Shuang came out through the main gate holding a box and handed it over to Kuqiao.

乔文仙说：“南双去裁缝铺取衣裳时，给你一同取回，我本想亲自送到朱府，正好你来，顺便带回去。”
Qiao Wenxian said, "When Nan Shuang went to the tailor shop to pick up the clothes, he brought yours back too. I'd meant to deliver them to Zhu Manor myself, so it's lucky you came; you can take them home with you now."

“让文渊费心了。”
"I've put you to too much trouble, Wenyuan."

“以后不许对我说这么客气的话。对了，你求仪王办事，他可答应？”
"Stop being so polite with me from now on. Oh, by the way, did Prince Yi agree to the favor you asked?"

千里寻点点头：“仪王倒是爽快，不过……”
Qianli Xun nodded. "Prince Yi agreed without hesitation, except..."

“不过什么？”
"Except what?"

千里寻大胆问道：“文渊，你真的认为仪王人很好吗？”
Qianli Xun worked up her nerve and asked, "Wenyuan, do you really think Prince Yi is a good person?"

“不然呢？他可是我最好的朋友。”
"Why wouldn't I? He's my best friend."

“你既如此认为，我也不多说什么，反正我对他印象不好。”
"If that's what you think, I won't say any more. Anyway, I don't have a good impression of him."

“你对他肯定有误会，没关系，以后接触时间长了，自然会清楚。”
"You must have misunderstood him. No matter—once you spend more time around him, you'll see for yourself."

千里寻不知该说什么，文渊一直拽着她的手，她不能立刻离开，也舍不得离开。
Qianli Xun did not know what to say. Wenyuan kept holding her hand; she could not leave right away, nor could she bear to.

“淑真，快到七夕节，我想在七夕节那日约你一起逛街。”
"Shuzhen, Qixi Festival is almost here. I'd like to take you out strolling through the streets on Qixi."

“好啊好啊！这么好的节日我怎么能一个人度过？一定要跟文渊在一起。”
"Yes, yes! How could I spend such a wonderful festival all alone? Of course I have to be with you, Wenyuan."

“我想把仪王和佳和郡主邀约上，我们四个一起。”
"I was thinking of inviting Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe along, so the four of us can go together."

“啊，还有他们。”
"Oh... them too?"

“怎么？你不愿意？”
"What's wrong? You don't want to?"

“我只想跟你在一起。”
"I just want to be with you."

“本来我也是这么想的，可仪王非要跟着，没办法，他是仪王，又帮我，也帮你，不好拒绝。你不用担心，有佳和郡主在，他们是一对儿，根本不会影响我们。”
"I felt the same way at first, but Prince Yi insisted on tagging along. What can I do? He's Prince Yi, and he's helping both me and you, so it's hard to turn him down. Don't worry—Princess Jiahe will be there too. They're a couple, so they won't get in our way at all."

仪王真是个渣男加电灯泡，总是破坏她的好事，连七夕节都不放过。文渊提到帮忙之事，千里寻不好再说什么，只能答应让仪王同去，就当是感谢他。
Prince Yi really was both a scumbag and a third wheel, always spoiling her good times, not even sparing Qixi Festival! Still, since Wenyuan brought up the favors he was doing them, Qianli Xun could not object further and had to agree to let Prince Yi come along, treating it as a way to thank him.

想起感谢一事，不由说道：“仪王很喜欢听书，不如到那日，我请仪王和佳和郡主去勾栏瓦肆听书，感谢他给我帮忙。”
Thinking of thanking him, she spoke up: "Prince Yi loves listening to storytellers. Why don't I treat Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe to a storytelling session at the Goulan Wasi that day to thank him for helping me?"

“主意不错，他确实喜欢听，尤其喜欢听一位叫千里寻的才子写的故事，也不知什么时候临安突然出现一位写话本的才子？”
"Great idea. He does love it—above all, he loves stories written by some gifted writer named Qianli Xun. No idea when a talented huaben author like that popped up out of nowhere in Lin'an."

千里寻有意问道：“他现在很出名吗？”
Qianli Xun probed on purpose: "Is he famous right now?"

“出不出名不知道，听仪王提起过。”
"I wouldn't know if he's famous; I only heard Prince Yi mention him."

“你说他未来的名气会不会盖过文渊你？”
"Do you think his fame might eclipse yours someday, Wenyuan?"

“那不至于，大家的喜好不一样，他以写话本为主，我则是专攻诗词品鉴，互不相扰。”
"I doubt it. People have different tastes. He focuses on writing huaben stories, while I specialize in poetry and ci criticism. Our paths don't cross."

“文渊喜不喜欢看话本？”
"Do you like reading huaben stories, Wenyuan?"

“我从不看话本，除非你写话本，我才会看，可你曾在我面前说，最瞧不上下里巴人之作，所以我从不会看话本，偶尔没事听听，消遣一下。”
"I never read huaben—unless you wrote one, then I'd read it. But you once told me yourself that you look down on lowbrow works more than anything, so I never read them. Now and then I'll listen to a storyteller when I have spare time, just to pass the hours."

原来文渊是受朱淑真影响，所谓的知己不过是文渊单方面的付出而已。
So Wenyuan had been shaped by Zhu Shuzhen's tastes all along; this supposed meeting of soulmates had just been Wenyuan giving everything from his side.

“可我现在认为，下里巴人之作也许会在未来得到主流人士的重视，就像词在唐代并不被大家重视，经过柳三变、东坡居士等人的努力，现已登上大雅之堂。以后，我不会再瞧不上落魄文人写的话本。”
"Now, though, I think lowbrow works might one day win respect from the mainstream, just like ci poetry got little respect back in the Tang dynasty, yet through the efforts of Liu Sanbian, Dongpo Jushi, and others, it has now ascended to the halls of refined literature. From now on, I won't look down on huaben stories written by down-and-out scholars anymore."

“既然你态度改变，我当然会顺着你。”
"Since you've had a change of heart, I'll follow your lead, of course."

两人依依不舍，苦荞见小姐还没有要走的意思，提醒道：“小姐，该回去了。”
Neither wanted to part. Seeing her mistress show no sign of leaving, Kuqiao stepped in to remind her: "Miss, it's time to head back."

千里寻只好与文渊分别，上了马车。
Qianli Xun had no choice but to bid Wenyuan farewell and climb into the carriage.

﻿苦荞手捧盒子，要登上马车时，发现白风来到大门口，向她张望。
Holding the box in her arms, Kuqiao was about to board the carriage when she spotted Bai Feng walking up to the main gate, peering out toward her.

苦荞心中埋怨：“我都要走，你才来。”接着后悔为何不晚些催促小姐，搞得自己想多看白风几眼都没时间。
Kuqiao grumbled to herself: "I'm just about to leave and now you show up." Then she kicked herself for not waiting a bit longer to hurry her mistress, leaving no time to steal a few more glances at Bai Feng.

“苦荞，你怎么不上马车？”千里寻从车窗探出脑袋。
"Kuqiao, why aren't you getting in?" Qianli Xun poked her head out of the carriage window.

苦荞向白风瞧了一眼，走上去。
Kuqiao cast one more glance at Bai Feng and climbed aboard.

千里寻又对文渊笑道：“七夕节见。”
Qianli Xun smiled at Wenyuan once more. "See you on Qixi Festival."

“下次见你时，我会带给你一个大大的好消息。”乔文仙与白风站在原地，目送马车渐行渐远。
"Next time I see you, I'll bring you a huge piece of good news." Qiao Wenxian and Bai Feng stood where they were, watching the carriage roll farther and farther into the distance.
