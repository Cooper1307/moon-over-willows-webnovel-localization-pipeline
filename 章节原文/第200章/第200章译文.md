## Chapter 200: A Morning Call at the Qiantang Gang

Frozen out for half an hour and barked to kneel on her first line? No way Qianli Xun was eating that! Staying upright, she offered a calm second bow. "Respectfully, Grandmother, do not take only Chen Haoyu's side of the story. I did not deny him breakfast—he slept past the hour for greeting the elders, refused to leave bed, and by the time he dressed, breakfast hour had long passed."

Dead set on breaking the new bride's pride on day one, Old Madam Xia refused to hear reason. "An elder orders you to kneel, and instead of dropping down you talk back?! Hear me plain: inside Chen Manor, my grandson sleeps late when he wants and does as he pleases—who dares police him while I breathe?! Bullying my grandson day one—what next?! I sentence you to kneel in this courtyard for the entire day!"

Chen Haoyu beamed—with Grandmother backing him, Zhu Shuzhen was cooked!

Disgusted by the blind coddling that had rotted Chen Haoyu into a useless rake, Qianli Xun dropped every ounce of meekness. Spotting teapot and bowls on a side table, she strolled calmly over, poured one cup, and carried it in both hands before Old Madam Xia. "Having married into the Chen family and called you Grandmother, I still owe you morning tea."

Old Madam Xia jerked her face away without reaching out.

Setting the bowl calmly on the low table beside her, Qianli Xun said, "Tea is served. Since Grandmother cannot stand the sight of me, I will excuse myself rather than spoil your mood."

With one sharp sweep of her hand, Old Madam Xia knocked the tea bowl crashing off the table, shattering it across the tiles in a splash of hot tea! "Did you go deaf?! I ordered you to kneel in my courtyard all day!"

"Heard you loud and clear, Grandmother—yet I committed zero wrong, so why kneel? Does seniority hand out a blank check to bully juniors at will?!"

Purple with fury, Old Madam Xia bellowed, "Servants!" Four or five maids rushed in. "Hold her down! Make her kneel!"

Remembering the splintered chair at the front gate, every maid froze in her tracks, not one daring to step forward.

Sweeping a cool look over the trembling girls, Qianli Xun scoffed, "Save the show, Old Madam! You already saw what my fists do—one punch drops all five of them, and you expect them to pin ME down? Laughable!" Spinning around, she snapped, "Come on, Kuqiao!"

Pausing at the threshold, she tossed one last volley over her shoulder: "Take three truths from me, Old Madam: ventures fail through laziness, men rot through idleness, and houses ruin through indulgence. If you truly love your grandson, quit spoiling him—lest the day he burns the entire Chen fortune to ash, you have nowhere left to cry!"

Striding out with head high, she left silence behind her.

Pointing at the empty doorway, Chen Haoyu cried, "Look how cocky she is, Grandmother—ignoring even YOU!"

To his utter shock, a warm chuckle broke across Old Madam Xia's face. "Weren't you the one begging to marry her yesterday? Turning sour already?"

Chen Haoyu blinked in total confusion. "Grandmother—you aren't mad?! You are LAUGHING?!"

"The whole tantrum was an act—just wanted to test her nerve and knock her down a peg so she goes easy on you! Listening to those sharp words, she has a clear head and will not bully you without cause. Better still, standing dead steady under my bullying—spitting image of me in my youth! I actually like her! Since you wanted her, settle down and live right with her, and stay out of filthy haunts!"

Floored that Zhu Shuzhen had won Grandmother over by wrecking her tea ritual, Chen Haoyu whined, "Got a granddaughter-in-law and stopped loving your grandson already?!"

"Nonsense—Grandmother loves you most."

"If she hits me, whose side are you on?"

"Yours, of course."

Chatting a few minutes more, Qianli Xun's warning about burning the Chen fortune jolted him back to Master Jiang's contract! Bidding Grandmother goodbye, he marched straight out to settle with the Qiantang Gang.

At Chen Manor's gate, spotting Qinggua trailing him, he whirled with a sudden kick straight into his belly! "Who asked you to tail me?! Beat it—important business!"

Doubled over clutching his gut, Qinggua wheezed, "Ow! Young Madam ordered me to stick with you—got to obey her!"

"Say that again! WHOSE orders do you take?!"

"Young Master's too, of course!"

"Then SCRAM!" Ditching Qinggua behind, Chen Haoyu swaggered out the gate straight into Scarface and his lackeys lurking outside.

Remembering them turning tail like cowards in the gambling house, Chen Haoyu snapped, "Got the nerve to show your faces after ditching me?! Find another boss!"

"Mercy, Young Master Chen!" Scarface grinned sheepishly. "Master Jiang had a whole army that night—we wanted to jump in yet dared not! Afterward we kicked ourselves and swore: from now on, say jump and we dive straight into fire!"

Unwilling to walk into the Qiantang Gang solo in case blows flew, Chen Haoyu barked, "Fine—earn back your spots! Right this second, drag Lu Yuanfeng PLUS a few extra musclemen to meet me at the Qiantang Gang gate!"

"On it!" The lackeys bolted in all directions while Chen Haoyu boarded his carriage for the eastern edge of town.

Pulling up outside the Qiantang Gang, Chen Haoyu squinted out the window in surprise—a second Chen Manor carriage already sat parked outside! Moments later, Chen Haodong strolled straight out of the gang's gates!

Panic shot through Chen Haoyu—had his gambling disaster leaked, sending Father's right-hand man Haodong here to mop up?! Hopping down, he braced himself as his cousin approached.

Spotting Haoyu gave Chen Haodong an instant start—must not let his cousin sniff out his secret deal with Master Jiang!—yet prepared with a ready cover, he flashed a smooth smile. "Haoyu? What brings you to the Qiantang Gang?"

*Smiling warmly—phew, he has no clue!* Relaxing at once, Chen Haoyu countered, "I was about to ask what brings Brother Haodong out here!"

Putting on a weary elder-brother sigh, Chen Haodong explained, "You stay clear of Chen commerce, so you miss the headaches of trade—got to stay friendly with officials on the bright side AND underworld bosses in the shadows, or trouble hits our shops. Dropped by to keep Master Jiang sweet so our business runs smooth."

Relieved beyond words, Chen Haoyu praised him. "Hard work on your shoulders, Brother Haodong—spares Father tons of grief."

"Do not flatter me—an empire this vast is too heavy for one pair of hands. Join in someday to share the load; Eldest Uncle and Grandmother would be thrilled!" Chen Haodong probed smoothly.

Totally blind to his cousin's guile, Chen Haoyu waved it off. "You know me—zero taste for trade! Long as silver keeps rolling and fun stays hot, I am set."

Satisfied completely, Chen Haodong smiled. "Still didn't say why you came out here."

"Ran into Master Jiang at a gambling den a few days back and he invited me to drop by—figured I had a free morning," Chen Haoyu fibbed.

Sneering inside yet nodding smoothly, Chen Haodong replied, "Good for the Chens if you stay chummy with Master Jiang. Carry on—I have books to check at the tea shop." Watching his cousin drive away, Chen Haoyu shook his head in awe—he could never match Brother Haodong in a million years.

With no hired muscle arriving yet, he lingered outside eyeing the burly gate guards—when another familiar carriage rolled up: Prince Yi! Elation surged in Chen Haoyu's chest! His tip during yesterday's raid had paid off! With Prince Yi's lethal guard—and surely an even deadlier prince—backing him, Master Jiang would cough up that contract in seconds!

Hurrying forward, he greeted him eagerly: "What luck running into Your Highness here!"

Glancing out at the worthless rake who had somehow married the woman he loved, Shen Jianchen felt pure bile rise and ignored him flat, stepping down with Bai Feng straight toward the gang's gate.

Guessing the prince held a grudge over the wedding, Chen Haoyu hustled alongside him. "Your Highness, one private word—about Zhu Shuzhen!"

Magic words—Shen Jianchen halted, eyed him once, and stepped aside out of Bai Feng's earshot.

Lowering his voice, Chen Haoyu spilled fast: "Like I told you yesterday, Master Jiang forced me into marrying Zhu Shuzhen—so our marriage is completely fake!"

"Invitations across Lin'an and a massive feast—and you expect me to call it fake?" Shen Jianchen scoffed.

"The feast was real, yet the marriage is pure paper! We signed terms beforehand—total non-interference! Last night I never even entered the bridal chamber; Zhu Shuzhen booted me straight to a back shack! Doubt me? Have Guard Bai ask Kuqiao!"

Relief spiked through Shen Jianchen alongside a sharp pang of pity for Qianli Xun: freed from one loveless cage only to climb into a sham marriage out of necessity. "Stubborn as she is, why would she marry you without caring for you?"

Recounting the entire gambling ambush, Chen Haoyu added, "Master Jiang rigged a bet to force me to wed her. Desperate, I dropped to my knees begging her—maybe she pitied me, plus I promised her sweet perks and we signed a written contract."

"A written contract?"

"Spelled out in black and white how we live apart—and transferring all my assets to her."

Pitying Qianli Xun yet suspicious of Chen Haoyu's sudden candor, Shen Jianchen narrowed his eyes. "Why tell me all this?"

Emboldened by the warmer reception, Chen Haoyu pitched straight: "Marrying the woman Your Highness loves made me sweat bullets! Wanted to clear the air—and beg Your Highness to help pry my contract out of Master Jiang's claws! I swear on my life I will never touch a hair on Zhu Shuzhen's head, and the day the moment ripens, I hand her straight back to Your Highness!"

Something smelled fishy as hell—an underworld boss turning down the whole Chen fortune? Definitely a scheme underneath worth digging out! A thin smile touched Shen Jianchen's lips. "For being so sensible, I will get your contract back."

*Pure genius!* Chen Haoyu beamed—flattered Prince Yi, crushed Master Jiang, AND laid a clean escape hatch from Zhu Shuzhen!

Leaving him behind, Shen Jianchen strode up to the gate. "Who are you? What business at Qiantang Gang?" the two guards barked.

Before the echoes faded, Bai Feng dropped both with two crisp punches. "Watch your mouths! Young Master Shen is here to see your boss!"

Strutting past the groaning guards behind them, Chen Haoyu crowed, "Heard that?! Young Master Shen! Going to wipe the floor with you lot!"

Stepping into the main yard, a ring of armed gangsters closed around all three with gleaming steel.
