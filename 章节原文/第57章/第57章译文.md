# Chapter 57: Successfully Persuading the Military Training Officer

---

Shen Jianchen thought his suspicion was ridiculous. He had seen how Zhu Shuzhen and Wenyuan exchanged flirtatious glances. The way she blushed shyly when she saw the man she liked was nothing like Qianli Xun, who would say "I'll marry you when pigs learn to climb trees."

---

Shen Jianchen sighed, shook his head, and felt quite foolish. He thought it better to wait for Bai Feng's news. Bai Feng had a mission today, so he couldn't bring him to the banquet. He'd treat him to a good meal at the restaurant later as a reward.

---

Shen Jianchen chose a secluded path toward the banquet area. The Zhu residence's greatest advantage was its connectivity—every path led to the banquet.

---

Upon arrival, Shen Jianchen found Zhu Xiyan and the officials still seated, enjoying the song and dance performances. Looking around, some were watching, some were chatting in groups, and others were wandering back like him.

---

Zhu Shuzhen and Miao Zhiyu also returned together, each sitting back in their places. The moment she sat down, she cast her gaze toward Wenyuan again. Meanwhile, Chen Haoyu whispered a few words to the Young Madam of the Zhu family, then quickly left, looking like he wouldn't be coming back.

---

Shen Jianchen remembered his promise to Wenyuan to help Zhu Shuzhen get a mutual divorce. Instead of returning to the male guest section, he walked over to a Zhu family servant, said a few words, then headed toward a crepe myrtle tree not far away.

---

The Zhu family servant found Lin Jue and whispered to him.

---

Lin Jue felt puzzled. He got up and looked in the direction the servant pointed. Shen Jianchen was standing under the flower tree, the crepe myrtle making him appear even taller and more upright.

---

Lin Jue couldn't guess why Young Master Shen was looking for him. Suspecting he was a prince, he left the male guest section and walked toward the crepe myrtle tree. Upon reaching him, he respectfully greeted: "Young Master Shen."

---

Shen Jianchen turned around: "You might as well call me Prince Yi."

---

Lin Jue was so startled he was about to kneel. Shen Jianchen stopped him: "No need to kneel. I don't want everyone at the banquet to know my identity. Otherwise, they'd all gather around to pay respects—wouldn't that be troublesome?"

---

Lin Jue remained standing, his waist already bent. He was quite confused. He had heard that Prince Yi was a sickly prince, yet the person before him looked very healthy. Could he be fooling him? Whether true or false, it wouldn't hurt to be careful.

---

Shen Jianchen said: "His Majesty sent me to Lin'an to serve as Military Commissioner. I originally planned to keep my identity hidden for a while, taking the opportunity to observe the people's lives throughout Lin'an. But now I have to reveal my identity early, using the title of Prince Yi and the position of Military Commissioner to ask the Military Training Officer to agree to one thing."

---

Hearing this, Lin Jue was certain he was a prince: "Prince Yi... no, Military Commissioner... no, Prince Yi, please speak. Your subordinate will agree to anything."

---

Persuading someone to agree to a mutual divorce always felt awkward for Shen Jianchen. After all, he was a prince—how did he end up doing this? But having already promised Wenyuan, he couldn't back out. So he gritted his teeth and said: "I'm not here for official business, but personal matters. When we met at Juxiang Lou that day, you already knew my relationship with Wenyuan and understood his feelings for Youqi Jushi. Since you and Youqi Jushi can't get along, why not let her go? That way, Wenyuan and Youqi Jushi can finally be together as lovers should."

---

So Prince Yi was here about the divorce. From his words, he must have been entrusted by Qiao Wenxian, which further proved that Qiao Wenxian had been coveting Zhu Shuzhen for quite some time. Though Lin Jue was angry inside, the person before him was Prince Yi, the Military Commissioner of Lin'an, who held power over his life and death. He still had to save face.

---

"Even without Your Highness saying so, I was already planning to mutually divorce Zhu Shuzhen. After the banquet today, I'll speak to my father-in-law about it. But I'm afraid he won't agree."

---

"That's fine. I'll personally meet with the prefect and persuade him to consider his daughter's future and happiness more." Lin Jue agreed so easily that Shen Jianchen actually felt guilty. He advised: "After the divorce, find a wife who suits you, preferably one who shares common interests with you."

---

"Thank you for your concern, Your Highness."

---

"Go now. Don't mention my identity to anyone at the banquet."

---

"Yes, sir."

---

Lin Jue bowed and left.

---

With royalty now involved, letting go of Zhu Shuzhen was inevitable. No matter how reluctant Lin Jue felt, he had to take action. Zhu Shuzhen's month in the countryside had given him more time to think. After this period of consideration, his unwillingness to divorce was not as firm as before.

---

Lin Jue sighed, sat back in his place, and looked toward Zhu Shuzhen. She was smiling, tasting the melons and fruits the maid had just served, while looking toward the male guest section. Following her gaze—who else could it be but Qiao Wenxian? Lin Jue cursed in his heart: "Shameless!"

---

Qiao Mengrong had been watching her brother all along. Finally catching sight of him, she saw him detour to the crepe myrtle tree, talk to someone, and only then return to the male guest section.

---

Those in charge of fruits and candied sweets had already set out the seasonal fruits and preserves. Everyone at the banquet returned to their seats and began tasting them. After her brother sat down, he only talked with Young Master Qiao, not even glancing at her. Qiao Mengrong was quite disappointed.

---

The singing and dancing on the wooden stage had stopped. Zhu Xiyan stepped forward: "Everyone, next, let our talented young men and women showcase their literary skills. Each person will write a ci poem. This year's theme is..."

---

Zhu Xiyan thought for a moment: "There's not much worth writing about these days. Let me think... in half a month it will be Qixi Festival. Why not make Qixi the theme? Each person writes one poem, and we'll select the best. My newly recruited female singers and dancers have the talent to compose music and choreography on the spot. They'll perform the best poem live for everyone. How does that sound?"

---

Many present were pleased and expressed their approval.

---

Zhu Xiyan continued: "Brush and ink are ready. Please proceed to the Long Corridor to showcase your talents."

---

Those who wanted to showcase their talents headed toward the Long Corridor.

---

Qianli Xun naturally couldn't let Zhu Shuzhen down, so she also got up and walked toward the Long Corridor.

---

Kuqiao was about to go forward to attend, but Xiaguo held her back: "Sister, stay and enjoy the fruits and candied sweets. I'll go attend to Young Lady."

---

Kuqiao said: "No, what would I be doing staying here? Let's go together."

---

Other maids, young servants, and book boys also followed, ready to attend to the young masters and young ladies of each family.

---

Kuqiao and Xiaguo went to their young lady's side. After entering the Long Corridor, they chose a table—one spread the paper, the other ground the ink.

---

Qianli Xun picked up her brush, thinking: "Zhu Shuzhen has a poem 'Magpie Bridge Immortal · Qixi.' This is the perfect opportunity to showcase it." She wrote:

---

*Light clouds adorn the evening, the west wind ends the heat,*
*Light rain falls from the sky as the moon sets.*
*The Cowherd and Weaver Girl have passed so many autumns—*
*How much sorrowful longing and tearful grief remains?*
*A cool breeze enters the sleeves, quiet joy fills the seats,*
*Heaven and earth, all is satisfied.*
*Why not be together morning and evening,*
*Instead of changing year after year?*

---

While her young lady was writing, Xiaguo still didn't forget to keep an eye on Young Master Qiao. Even though she could only see his back, it was enough to captivate her.

---

She couldn't understand how there could be such a perfect man in the world as Young Master Qiao—not only talented and from a good family, but most importantly, of good character. Although Xiaguo hadn't encountered anything that specifically demonstrated Young Master Qiao's character, she had met someone like Chen Haoyu. Comparing the two, Young Master Qiao's character must be excellent.

---

Young Master Qiao had just finished writing. He put down his brush and turned to look at their young lady. Xiaguo didn't dare keep watching and quickly withdrew her gaze.

---

Their young lady had finished writing. Xiaguo recognized most of the characters—thanks to Sister Kuqiao's careful teaching—but when combined, she couldn't understand the meaning. She thought about how she could never be worthy of Young Master Qiao and felt quite down.

---

Qiao Wenxian glanced at Shuzhen's back, then contentedly handed his finished poem to the maid in charge nearby.

---

The maid received it, bowed, and delivered it before the prefect and other officials.

---

All the poems were delivered one by one by the maids to the judging area.

---

Lin Zixiao was honored to be invited among those of status to judge the merits of each poem.

---

Among the young people, except for Lin Jue who didn't understand poetry and didn't participate, only Shen Jianchen remained seated. With a prince accompanying him, Lin Jue didn't feel too embarrassed.

---

The talented young men and women returned to their seats after finishing their poems.

---

Qianli Xun had just sat down when Chen Wenxin said: "At occasions like this, your poems always rank first. I don't even need to guess—it will be the same today. But let me speak from the heart, and don't be angry."

---

"Sister-in-law, go ahead. I won't be angry."

---

"Others say your poems are wonderful, but I don't like them. Reading them brings too much sorrow. I don't know where you get so much sadness from?"

---

Qianli Xun quite agreed with Chen Wenxin. Zhu Shuzhen's poems were indeed full of sorrow but lacked depth. Unfortunately, having traveled through time, while she could live Qianli Xun's life in Zhu Shuzhen's body in this era, she couldn't change Zhu Shuzhen's poems—they were already fixed by history and time.

---

"Can't you see, Sister-in-law? I'm a sheltered young lady, dealing only with domestic matters. Having encountered an unhappy marriage, how could I write joyful poems?"

---

Chen Wenxin nodded in agreement: "That's true."

---

Just as Chen Wenxin had said, Zhu Shuzhen's poem ranked first. When Lin Zixiao announced to everyone that Youqi Jushi's poem was the best, Qiao Wenxian smiled: "I knew it would be her!"

---

Shen Jianchen was quite dissatisfied: "Since you knew it would be her, why not just have her write alone? Why need so many others as foils?"

---

"I'm willing to be a foil."

---

"You may be willing, but others might not."

---

"I can't control others. As long as I'm willing, that's enough."

---

"Truly hopeless!"

---

"You have the nerve to talk? That... fascinating spirit you mentioned—is really ridiculously fascinating!"

---

Shen Jianchen remembered how Zhu Shuzhen used "I" in such a fiery tone when scolding Chen Haoyu: "You know what? You have good taste in people. Youqi Jushi is quite an interesting character."

---

Qiao Wenxian asked worriedly: "What do you mean?"

---

"Look at you, as if I'm going to steal her from you. Don't worry, I'm not interested in her." Shen Jianchen explained: "Today, I heard her use 'I' in such a fiery tone when scolding Chen Haoyu—she's quite something."

---

"What? She actually used vulgar language? She would never have done that before. She must have been forced into it after marrying Lin Jue. No, we must hurry and get her divorced." Qiao Wenxian grabbed Prince Yi's hand: "You must help me."

---

Shen Jianchen smiled: "I've already helped you halfway. Only half remains."

---

Qiao Wenxian happily asked: "What do you mean?"

---

Shen Jianchen whispered: "I just revealed my identity to Lin Jue and made the request. He's already agreed to the mutual divorce. Once we handle the prefect, everything will be settled."

---

Qiao Wenxian was even more excited: "Excellent!"

---

He looked at Zhu Shuzhen again. She was looking at the wooden stage. Qiao Wenxian also looked toward the stage. The female singer had quickly composed music for Youqi Jushi's poem, plucking the strings and displaying her beautiful voice. A dancer swayed her waist to the melody, performing a stunning dance that matched the poem's content.

---

Dongfang Chenze and others carefully savored the words.

---

Cao Ban said: "Youqi Jushi's poems remain beautifully sorrowful and graceful."

---

Dongfang Chenze affirmed: "Indeed, consistent with her usual style."

---

An Mingxuan said: "Ever since witnessing her prowess at Yongtai Teahouse, I've been thinking—Youqi Jushi's sorrowful and graceful style really doesn't match her performance that day."

---

Cao Ban said: "I was also puzzled, but later I realized it's because we all know too little about Youqi Jushi."

---

Dongfang Chenze nodded in agreement: "Makes sense."

---

The sound of strings and singing reached the Zhu family's Buddhist hall.

---

The Old Ancestor was fingering her prayer beads when she heard: "Why not be together morning and evening, instead of changing year after year." She couldn't help but open her eyes: "I wonder if there's been any progress on the Zhu family young lady's mutual divorce since that day?"

---

Nanny Xiuzhen replied: "The day before yesterday, I left the Buddhist hall to collect the Old Ancestor's monthly allowance from the Zhu family steward. I happened to run into the Zhu family young master. I asked a bit about the young lady's divorce, and he said it's still the same as before."

---

The Old Ancestor ordered: "Bring paper and brush."

---

Nanny Xiuzhen set out the paper and brush. The Old Ancestor picked up the brush and wrote a letter.

---

After finishing, she instructed: "Deliver this letter to the Zhu family master."

---

Nanny Xiuzhen received it, walked out of the Buddhist hall, asked for directions along the way, followed the sound of strings and singing to the banquet area, and saw Zhu Xiyan seated enjoying the performance. She came to the male guest section, bowed, and said: "Master, the Old Ancestor wrote you a letter and asked me to deliver it."

---

Hearing it was a letter from the Old Ancestor, Zhu Xiyan was very respectful: "I didn't expect the Old Ancestor to still remember me."

---

After he received the letter, Nanny Xiuzhen bowed again and left.

---

Zhu Xiyan opened the letter and read through it. His expression became grave. He beckoned to a servant nearby and instructed him to fetch the young master.

---

After Zhu Jingyuan came over, Zhu Xiyan whispered a few words to his son. Zhu Jingyuan nodded and said "good" after hearing them.

---

Zhu Xiyan then said to the officials beside him: "Everyone, please continue enjoying the performance. I'm feeling a bit unwell and will return to my room to rest for a while."

---

The officials all said: "Please, Prefect."
