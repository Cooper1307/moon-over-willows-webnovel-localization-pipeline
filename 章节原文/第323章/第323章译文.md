## Chapter 323: Bound for Bianliang Tomorrow

Stumbling back into Old Madam Xia's hall where the whole clan and Matron Wei waited in grim silence, Haoyu collapsed on his knees breaking the news: attempted murder, prison cart to Bianliang, five hundred thousand taels to spare her neck!

First thing next morning, Chen Jianglin and Haoyu hauled the half-million-tael chest straight back to Liu'an Villa—where Liang Shicheng pocketed every banknote smoothly with a hollow promise to "pull strings" in Bianliang, then released bound Zhu Xiyan at the gate (warning him to stay put while Haoyu vowed to ride straight to Bianliang himself).

Once the Chens departed, Liang Shicheng unlocked Prince Yi's prison room, fitted a concealed dagger into a wall slot to hoist the iron cage, and smugly informed him the poetess was already en route to Bianliang for attempted murder!

Shoving Liang Shicheng straight into the hall, Shen Jianchen snapped *"Hold the guards off till I'm done, Bai Feng!"*—pinned the shrieking eunuch flat, battered his face raw until Bai Feng's arrow wound nearly gave out, knocked Liang Shicheng cold with one final hook, and vaulted away with a chilling parting oath: *"If a hair on Youqi Jushi's head is harmed, I'll leave you without a burial plot!"*

Racing back to Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen found a shattering shock awaiting him: while he was locked up, Mengrong had overheard two loose-tongued maids gossiping that she could never bear children again—snapping her fragile mind completely! Dreaming of the real Zhao Pu calling from Heaven, she shrank back in terror clutching her pillow and screaming *"You're not my Brother!"*

Summoned in haste, mental-illness specialist Physician Guo sighed he couldn't cure her and urged taking her to Bianliang's imperial physicians. With Qianli Xun already jailed there anyway, Shen Jianchen gave the order: **depart for Bianliang tomorrow**, sending Bai Feng to invite Chen Haoyu to travel together.

Arriving at Chen Manor right as Qiao Wenxian finished landing frantic blows on unresisting Haoyu—before pledging a list of Bianliang contacts to help save Shuzhen—Bai Feng delivered the invitation and stole a tearful moment with Kuqiao, who promptly joined Haoyu's caravan to Bianliang.
