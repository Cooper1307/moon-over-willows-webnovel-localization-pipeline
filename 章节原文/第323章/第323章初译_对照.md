## Chapter 323: Deciding to Return to Bianliang Tomorrow

韦嬷嬷还没走出墨香院，青瓜从外面回来，见到韦嬷嬷，急切地问：“听门口的下人说少夫人被官兵带走，到底出了什么事？”
Matron Wei hadn’t even stepped out of Moxiang Courtyard when Qinggua hurried back from town and caught her. “I just heard from the gatekeepers that troops hauled Young Madam away—what on earth happened?”
Without answering, Matron Wei let out a heavy sigh and walked past.

韦嬷嬷没有回答，叹口气离开。
Qinggua rushed straight indoors to ask Chouya instead.

青瓜只好进屋找丑丫打听。
At the eldest master’s courtyard, servants told Matron Wei that Master and Madam had gone to Old Madam’s rooms, so she hurried there instead.

来到大老爷的院子，下人说大老爷和夫人去了老太太的屋子，韦嬷嬷又向老太太的院子拐去。
Shown inside Old Madam Xia’s chamber, she found the entire Chen family gathered in silence, every face grim and drawn. Clearly they had huddled together late into the night worrying over her young lady.

当她被允许进入老太太的屋子后，发现陈家所有人坐在里面，个个面色凝重，沉默不语。不用猜便知他们大晚上聚在一起，不过是替自家姑娘担心。
Deeply moved, Matron Wei bowed low to the elders. “Thank you all for losing sleep over Miss—let this servant thank every elder of the Chen household on her behalf.”

韦嬷嬷怀着感激之情上前向主人们行礼：“姑娘的事让大家费心，我替她谢过陈家所有长辈。”
Old Madam Xia sighed, “Shuzhen married into our house; naturally we worry for her. Pity the Chens hold no official rank to stand up to an imperial attendant—all we can do is sit and wait.”

夏老太太说：“淑真嫁到陈家就是陈家人，自然要为她费心。可惜陈家无权无势，拿梁内侍没办法，只能坐着等。”
Matron Wei asked urgently, “Do Prince Yi and Master Zhu know about this?”

韦嬷嬷问道：“仪王与朱家老爷可知此事？”
Chen Jianglin answered bleakly, “Both know—both are locked up inside Liu’an Villa right now. Imperial Attendant Liang didn’t even give Prince Yi face, let alone our in-law.”

陈江林解释道：“都已知，现在被关在柳岸别馆。梁内侍连仪王的面子都不给，何况亲家。”
Dread gripped Matron Wei. “If Madam Zhu finds out, she’ll faint dead away from weeping!”

韦嬷嬷担忧道：“朱家夫人若知道此事，还不得哭晕过去。”
Yao Qingyan said softly, “We’ve kept it from Madam Zhu and Wenxin for now. They know Master Zhu is being detained, though nothing about Shuzhen.”

姚轻琰说：“此事瞒着亲家夫人，包括文心，她们只知梁内侍将亲家公关起来，并不知淑真的事。”
“Best hide it as long as we can.” Having come hoping for some plan, yet seeing the whole family helpless, Matron Wei scanned the room and noticed the bridegroom missing. “Where is the Young Master?”

“能瞒一时是一时。”韦嬷嬷本想前来讨个主意，见众人如此，想必已毫无办法。她再将众人瞧一遍，发现姑爷不在，问道，“怎么没见姑爷？”
Madam Bao volunteered quickly, “Right after Shuzhen got taken away, Haoyu chased after her to Liu’an Villa.”

包氏帮着回答：“淑真被带走后，浩宇跟着去了柳岸别馆。”
Too sick at heart for chatter, Matron Wei sank quietly onto an empty side chair to wait alongside everyone else.

因心情沉重，韦嬷嬷不想说任何话，坐到一旁空的椅子上，与大家一起面色凝重等待有关姑娘的消息。
Stung that Matron Wei ignored her comment, Madam Bao fumed silently—*just a glorified maid putting on airs like a master, looking down her nose at me too!*

韦嬷嬷没有回应，包氏心里不舒服。心想，韦嬷嬷不过是有些身份的下人，竟然也学起主人来瞧不起自己。
Roughly half a shichen later, Chen Haoyu stumbled back through the doors, pale and unhinged, dropping straight to his knees before them.

大约过了半个多时辰，陈浩宇终于回来，跌跌撞撞失魂落魄地出现在众人面前，跪在地上。
“Father… Grandmother… save my wife! She’s… Imperial Attendant Liang locked her in a prisoner cart and sent her to Bianliang…”

“爹……奶奶……，救救我娘子，她……她被梁内侍用囚车送去汴梁……”
Shock rippled through the room.

众人一听，顿觉事态严重。
Chen Jianglin gasped, “On what charge?!”

陈江林吃惊地问：“定得什么罪？”
“Attempted murder… attempted murder of a court official…”

“谋杀……谋杀朝廷官员。”
Yao Qingyan burst into sobs. “My poor daughter-in-law! How could fate be so cruel?! Going to meet Liang once and getting slapped with a capital charge! Wuu…!”

姚轻琰哭起来：“可怜的儿媳妇啊！怎么这么命苦？去见一趟梁内侍就被定了这等罪！呜呜……！”
Every woman in the room reached for her handkerchief.

在场的女人们都开始抹眼泪。
Wiping a tear, Matron Wei asked tightly, “Is there any way to save her?”

韦嬷嬷擦完一滴泪后，问道：“可还有救？”
That was why Chen Haoyu had raced back. “Imperial Attendant Liang says saving her takes five hundred thousand taels of silver at minimum!”

陈浩宇回来就是为此事，赶紧说：“梁内侍说若想救人，最少也得五十万两银子。”
A spark of hope flared back across every face, drying tears fast.

众人又看到希望，不再难过。
Chen Jianglin decided instantly, “Tomorrow we pack the five hundred thousand taels in silver notes and go straight back to Liu’an Villa!”

陈江林立刻说：“明日把五十万两银票带上再去趟柳岸别馆。”
...

第二日，陈江林与陈浩宇像上次一样带着五十万两银票拜见梁内侍。
The very next morning, Chen Jianglin and Chen Haoyu brought the chest of five hundred thousand taels in silver notes back to Liu’an Villa.

梁师成毫不客气将银票收下，说些客套话：“女词人的罪过不小，按律当斩，看在你们陈家出手阔绰，等我回到汴梁定会上下打点，将女词人的罪行减轻，保住她的性命。”
This time Liang Shicheng swept the silver notes straight into his sleeve without blinking, rattling off oily assurances: “The poetess committed a grave crime—by law, execution. Still, seeing how generous you Chens are, once I return to Bianliang I’ll pull strings up and down to lighten her sentence and keep her head on her shoulders.”

陈浩宇本想问为何他的娘子来一趟柳岸别馆就得了谋杀朝廷官员的罪名，话到嘴边又咽了回去。他担心问得越多，对娘子越不利。
Chen Haoyu burned to demand how visiting Liu’an Villa once turned into “attempted murder of a court official,” yet bit his tongue hard—worried pushing too far would only hurt his wife worse.

父子二人被下人送出后，都觉得实在可笑，竟然要把希望寄托在害人的人身上。可陈家已被逼得无路可走，只好出此下策。
Shown out by servants, father and son felt sick at the absurdity: forced to pin all their hopes on the very villain who’d framed her. Backed into a corner with zero road left, what choice did the Chens have?

两人走到柳岸别馆的门口时，侍卫押着朱晞颜走来，他们瞧见后站在原地等候。
Reaching the villa’s front gate, they spotted guards marching Zhu Xiyan outside, so they waited.

朱晞颜被送至门口，侍卫警告道：“内侍大人说了，女词人谋杀朝廷官员，已被押往汴梁，你若再来寻事，别怪他不顾往日情谊。”
Tossed onto the street, Zhu Xiyan heard a guard bark, “Our Lord says the poetess attempted murder of a court official and is already en route to Bianliang. Come stirring trouble again, and don’t blame him for forgetting past favors!”

听到女儿被定下杀头的罪名，朱晞颜哭道：“可怜的淑真啊！”
Hearing his daughter carried a death-penalty charge broke Zhu Xiyan down on the spot. “My poor Shuzhen!”

陈江林上前安慰：“亲家别难过，先回家报个平安。我和浩宇已打点过梁内侍，他答应回到汴梁会上下疏通，减轻淑真的罪行。”
Stepping forward to steady him, Chen Jianglin whispered, “Take heart, In-law—go home first and let your household know you’re safe. Haoyu and I have already greased Liang’s palms; he promised to pull strings in Bianliang to commute Shuzhen’s sentence.”

朱晞颜并未责怪亲家没将女儿照顾好，他深知陈家必然会为淑真的事尽心尽力。他更了解梁师成，此人不达目的不罢休，任凭陈家怎么保护淑真，梁师成都有办法达到目的。
Zhu Xiyan didn’t dream of blaming the Chens—he knew they’d moved heaven and earth for her. Worse, he knew Liang Shicheng: a viper who never rested till he got his way, impossible for merchants like the Chens to outmaneuver.

“梁师成只手遮天，我等拿他毫无办法，只能回去等待消息。”
“Liang Shicheng blocks out the sky—there’s nothing men like us can do except go home and wait for word.”

陈浩宇说：“岳父大人放心，我会亲自去汴梁盯着娘子的事。”
Chen Haoyu promised firmly, “Rest easy, Father-in-law. I’m going to Bianliang myself to watch over my wife’s case.”

朱晞颜满意地点点头。
Zhu Xiyan nodded with heavy gratitude.

三人走后，梁师成来到关仪王的屋外，让人打开大锁。
...

梁师成走进后，先向坐在铁笼内的仪王行礼：“委屈殿下，今日便放您出去。”
Once the three left, Liang Shicheng strolled back to the room holding Prince Yi and ordered the heavy padlock unlocked.

申简辰站起来，厉声问：“快说！你将她怎么了？”
Stepping inside, he bowed mockingly toward Shen Jianchen sitting inside the iron cage. “Sorry for keeping Your Highness cooped up—we’re letting you out today.”

梁师成依然一副恭敬的样子：“我请女词人来不过是想与她畅聊诗词，她敬酒不吃吃罚酒，竟然想杀我。谋杀朝廷官员可是死罪，我只能派人用囚车将她送去汴梁。”
Springing upright, Shen Jianchen snapped, “Speak! What did you do with her?!”

申简辰一拳砸在铁笼上。
Maintaining his polite mask, Liang Shicheng sighed, “I merely invited the poetess over to discuss poetry—yet instead of appreciating kindness, she tried to murder me! Attempting to kill a court official carries a death sentence, so I had no choice but to ship her to Bianliang in a prisoner cart.”

梁师成装出关心的样子：“殿下要爱惜自己，别砸坏了手。”
Shen Jianchen slammed a fist hard into an iron bar.

梁师成说完，走到墙边。墙上有一个匕首形的凹槽。他从袖中取出一把匕首放进凹槽里，铁笼开始缓缓向上移动。
Feigning sympathy, Liang Shicheng clucked, “Mind your hands, Your Highness—wouldn’t want you bruising yourself.”

白风心想：“难怪我按榻上的机关毫无用处，找遍整个房间都没用，原来启动的关键是他袖中的匕首。”
Walking to the wall, he stopped before a dagger-shaped groove, drew a blade from his sleeve, and slid it into the slot. Slowly the heavy iron cage creaked upward toward the rafters.

等到能够从铁笼中出来时，申简辰迅速将梁师成推出门外。
*No wonder pressing the couch catch and tearing the whole room apart didn’t move a single bar,* Bai Feng thought—*the trigger was a dagger tucked up his sleeve!*

侍卫们围上来，白风警惕地盯着每个侍卫，负责保护好王爷。
The instant enough clearance opened beneath the bars, Shen Jianchen ducked out and shoved Liang Shicheng sprawling straight through the doorway into the yard.

梁师成笑道：“殿下还是省省吧，侍卫多得是，你根本杀不了我。”
Guards snapped blades up in a tight circle while Bai Feng hovered tense beside his master, ready to shield him.

“打你一顿也可暂时解我心头之恨！”申简辰说完，命令道，“白风，抵挡一会儿，等我打够了再走。”
Scrambling up on the couch outside, Liang Shicheng smirked, “Save your breath, Your Highness—I’ve got guards swarming everywhere; you’ll never kill me.”

“是！王爷！”白风的伤没完全好，倒也无大碍，帮王爷抵挡一会儿应该不成问题。
“Giving you a sound thrashing will do plenty to cool my rage!” Shen Jianchen barked. “Bai Feng, hold them back till I’ve beaten my fill!”

申简辰一拳将梁师成打倒，然后骑在他身上，左右拳开弓，在他的身上和脸上狂打一气。
“Yes, Your Highness!” Though still nursing his arrow wound, Bai Feng had plenty of fight left to buy his master time.

梁师成向侍卫们喊着：“快来救我！快来救我！……”
With one crushing punch, Shen Jianchen floored Liang Shicheng, straddled his chest, and hammered him left and right across face and ribs without holding back an ounce.

白风以灵巧的身姿竭尽全力阻挡侍卫，为王爷争取更多揍梁师成的时间。
Liang Shicheng shrieked, “Help! Get him off me! Help…!”

打斗激烈，他的伤口隐隐作痛，实在顶不住时，提醒道：“王爷！我快顶不住！”
Darting smoothly on light feet, Bai Feng deflected blade after blade, buying his prince extra breaths to pound the eunuch raw.

申简辰迅速用一拳将梁师成打晕，大喊一声：“走！”
Mid-brawl, Bai Feng’s wound ripped hot with pain; feeling himself about to buckle, he yelled, “Your Highness! I can’t hold much longer!”

二人用轻功从侍卫中脱身，待落地站稳后，申简辰大声说：“等他醒来，帮本王警告梁师成，若幽栖居士有个三长两短，我定让他死无葬身之地！”
Delivering one final knockout punch that left Liang Shicheng cold out, Shen Jianchen shouted, “Go!”

白风对着手执利剑的侍卫举起拳头，做出找打的动作，等王爷警告完众人，他跟上王爷扬长而去。
Vaulting cleanly over the guards’ heads with light-foot technique, both landed outside the wall. Turning back, Shen Jianchen roared across the courtyard, “Tell Liang Shicheng when he wakes up: if anything happens to Youqi Jushi, I’ll bury him without a grave!”

……
Shaking a fist tauntingly at the baffled swordsmen, Bai Feng dashed away after his prince.

邹嬷嬷见仪王消失两日后安全回来，甚是欣慰。可想到仪王妃，她又高兴不起来。
...

“王爷不在这两日，王妃按时吃药，身体好了许多，神志却越来越不清楚。要么发呆，要么说些大家听不懂的话，有时笑，有时又哭。”
Seeing Prince Yi return safe after vanishing for two days flooded Matron Zou with relief—only the state of Princess Consort Yi crushed that joy right away.

“难道她受了什么刺激？”
“While you were gone these two days, Your Highness, Her Highness took her medicine on schedule and her body mended nicely—yet her wits keep slipping worse. Either she stares blankly into space or babbles nonsense nobody understands, laughing one second and weeping the next.”

“应该不会，她每日待在幽林别居，哪里都没去。”
“Did something trigger another shock?”

申简辰来到梦容的屋子，她躺在床上睡得很香。申简辰在床边坐下，默默守着。
“Hardly—she stayed inside Youlin Retreat all day and never stepped out.”

熟睡中的乔梦容做了一个梦，她梦见自己来到一个奇怪的地方，到处金光闪闪，然后哥哥出现在她面前，唤着她的名字：“梦容！梦容！”
Walking to Mengrong’s chamber, Shen Jianchen found her sleeping peacefully on the bed and sat quietly on the edge watching over her.

梦中的她向哥哥走去，用手摸着哥哥的脸：“哥哥，是你吗？”
Deep in sleep, Qiao Mengrong dreamed she walked into a strange realm glowing bright gold everywhere—and right before her stood her brother, calling her name: “Mengrong! Mengrong!”

“是我，我才是你的哥哥。”
Hurrying forward in her dream, she cupped his face in both hands. “Brother… is it really you?”

乔梦容抱住哥哥：“哥哥，你不要喜欢淑真，只爱我好吗？”
“It’s me. I’m your real brother.”

“我的心里从来只有你，即使死了，心里也只有你。”
Throwing her arms around him, Qiao Mengrong cried, “Brother, don’t love Shuzhen—love only me, please?”

“哥哥别乱说，你没死，你还好好活着。”
“My heart has never held anyone besides you. Even dead, it holds only you.”

乔梦容怀中抱着的哥哥很快消失，只留下声音：“我才是你的哥哥，他不是……”
“Don’t talk nonsense, Brother—you aren’t dead, you’re right here alive!”

乔梦容从梦中惊醒，睁开眼睛，口中唤出：“哥哥！你别走！”
Yet the brother in her arms dissolved like mist, leaving only a fading echo: “I am your brother. He isn’t…”

申简辰凑上前：“哥哥没走，现在回来了。”
Jolting awake from the nightmare, Qiao Mengrong snapped her eyes open and cried, “Brother! Don’t leave me!”

乔梦容开始流泪：“你不是我哥哥，你不是我哥哥，……”
Shen Jianchen leaned closer. “I’m not leaving—I’m back now.”

申简辰抓起她的手：“我是，当然是。”
Tears spilled instantly down Qiao Mengrong’s cheeks. “You aren’t my brother! You aren’t my brother…!”

乔梦容甩开他的手，爬起来，抱起枕头，挨到床角，一副惊恐的样子：“你不是我哥哥，不是我哥哥，……”
Reaching for her hand, he insisted gently, “I am. Of course I am.”

邹嬷嬷说：“唉，从昨日起就变成这样，王妃还说要回汴梁找王爷，还不断地说她错了，让人看着难受。”
Flapping his hand away, she scrambled backward hugging a pillow tight against the far corner of the bed, eyes wide with terror. “You’re not my brother! Not my brother…!”

梦容的精神状态变得比两日前严重，申简辰厉声问道：“喜兰，老实说，我不在这两日到底发生了什么？为何王妃的病情会加重？”
Matron Zou sighed softly. “She’s been like this since yesterday, Your Highness—even begged to go back to Bianliang to look for you, repeating over and over that she was wrong. Breaks the heart to watch.”

喜兰跪下，老实交代：“昨日奴婢伺候王妃去茅厕，王妃出来时，还说身体里排出好些异物，觉得一身轻松。让奴婢陪她在园子里走走。走着走着，听到两个丫鬟议论，说王妃以后再也不能生孩子。王妃听后，当时没有任何表现，只要求回屋睡觉，醒来后就成这样。”
Alarmed by how sharply she had worsened in two days, Shen Jianchen snapped, “Xilan! Tell me the truth—what happened while I was gone? Why did Her Highness suddenly lose her mind like this?!”

邹嬷嬷生气道：“为何昨日不告诉我此事？”
Xilan dropped to her knees trembling. “Yesterday your servant helped Her Highness to the latrine. Coming out, she even remarked her belly felt much lighter after passing clots and asked me to walk her around the garden for fresh air. Walking along the path, we overheard two maids gossiping—saying Princess Consort Yi could never bear children again for the rest of her life. Right then Her Highness didn’t blink or cry—just asked to go back to bed and sleep. When she woke up… she was like this.”

喜兰辩解道：“嬷嬷没问，我便没提。”
Matron Zou flared up. “Why didn’t you tell me yesterday?!”

邹嬷嬷命令道：“你起来，告诉李管家，把那两个爱嚼舌的下人赶出去！”
Xilan pleaded weakly, “You didn’t ask, Matron, so I didn’t dare bring it up…”

喜兰回声“是”，起身去安排。
“Get up and tell Butler Li to throw those two loose-tongued wenches out of the estate immediately!”

申简辰瞧着靠在床角抱着枕头的梦容，她仿佛已经不认识他。
“Yes, Matron!” Scrambling up, Xilan hurried out to see it done.

“白风！”
Watching Mengrong huddled in the corner clutching her pillow like a frightened stranger who didn’t even recognize him, Shen Jianchen called out sharply, “Bai Feng!”

白风从外面进来：“王爷有何吩咐？”
Bai Feng stepped inside. “Your orders, Your Highness?”

“去城里把擅长治疗神志方面疾病的郭大夫请来。”
“Go into town and bring back Physician Guo, the specialist in mental disorders.”

等白风将郭大夫请来时，乔梦容已安静许多，她躺在床上，两只眼睛睁得老大，可又不知她在看着什么。
By the time Bai Feng returned with Physician Guo, Qiao Mengrong had calmed down, lying flat on her back with eyes stretched wide open, staring blindly at nothing at all.

郭大夫诊完脉后，叹口气：“我医术有限，治不了此病，开些安神之药，先吃着。建议你们去汴梁找大夫治，汴梁是京城，名医多。”
After taking her pulse, Physician Guo shook his head with a sigh. “My skill falls short—I cannot cure an affliction this deep. I’ll write a calming tonic to settle her spirits for now, though I advise taking her back to Bianliang. The capital has far better master physicians.”

申简辰本就打算回汴梁，现在千里寻已被囚车押往汴梁，他在临安待着没有任何意义，必须去汴梁救她。
Shen Jianchen had already meant to head back to Bianliang—and now that Qianli Xun had been hauled there in a prisoner cart, staying in Lin’an made zero sense. He had to go to Bianliang and save her.

等大夫写完药方离开后，申简辰对邹嬷嬷说：“明日就回汴梁。”
Once the physician wrote the prescription and left, Shen Jianchen told Matron Zou, “We leave for Bianliang tomorrow.”

他吩咐白风去趟陈家，告诉陈浩宇，若想救他的娘子，可结伴而行。
Then he sent Bai Feng straight to Chen Manor to tell Chen Haoyu that if he wanted to save his wife, they could travel north together.

白风来到陈府南门口时，乔家的马车停在一边，乔文仙带着南双站在门口向里张望。
...

他正想上前打招呼时，陈浩宇从大门走出。
Reaching Chen Manor’s south gate, Bai Feng saw the Qiao carriage parked nearby, with Qiao Wenxian and Nan Shuang standing outside peering eagerly toward the entrance.

乔文仙二话不说冲上前开打，边打边说：“早知淑真嫁给你会这样！我就该阻止她跟你成亲！”
Just as Bai Feng walked up to greet them, Chen Haoyu stepped out the main gate.

陈浩宇并未还手，像失去知觉一般任由乔文仙在他身上捶打。
Without saying a word, Qiao Wenxian charged straight up and started swinging at him, yelling between blows, “If I’d known marrying you would bring Shuzhen to this, I never would’ve let you wed her!”

乔文仙那两下子根本不会伤到陈浩宇，打累后，他用双手晃着陈浩宇，大声问：“你打算怎么办？”
Standing numb, Chen Haoyu didn’t even lift a hand to block, letting Qiao Wenxian hammer away against his chest.

陈浩宇回道：“我打算去汴梁救她。”
Not that Qiao Wenxian’s soft scholarly fists hurt much; panting once he tired out, he seized Chen Haoyu’s shoulders and shook him hard. “What are you going to do?!”

“算你还像个男人。”乔文仙推开陈浩宇，“我在汴梁待过一段时日，多少认识些人，回去后，我会把这些人的名字和住址一一写好，你前去只要报上我的名字，他们自会接见你，说不定还能帮到淑真。要不是家里的一妻一妾有身孕，我也会随你去汴梁。”
“I’m going to Bianliang to save her,” Chen Haoyu answered flatly.

陈浩宇将感激之情汇聚为两个字：“多谢。”
“At least you still act half a man.” Letting go, Qiao Wenxian added, “I lived in Bianliang a spell and know a few influential folks there. Once I get home, I’ll write down every name and address for you. Show up mentioning my name, and they’ll grant you a hearing—might even help get Shuzhen out. If my wife and concubine weren’t both pregnant right now, I’d ride north with you myself.”

白风走过来：“两位公子，我家王爷说了，他明日回汴梁，若陈公子想去汴梁救幽栖居士，可随我家王爷同行。”
Chen Haoyu condensed every ounce of gratitude into two words. “Thank you.”

乔文仙高兴道：“你跟着仪王会多一重保障，到汴梁也不用担心人生地不熟，救起淑真来更容易些。”
Stepping over, Bai Feng announced, “Gentlemen—my master bids me inform you he sets out for Bianliang tomorrow. If Young Master Chen wishes to save Youqi Jushi, you’re welcome to ride along with His Highness.”

陈浩宇也觉甚好：“辛苦白侍卫，帮我回复仪王殿下，明日不见不散。”
Qiao Wenxian brightened at once. “Riding with Prince Yi gives you solid protection—and you won’t be groping blind in Bianliang. Makes saving Shuzhen far easier!”

得到确切答复，白风并不着急走，而是向里瞧。
Seeing the wisdom in it too, Chen Haoyu nodded. “Thank you for bringing word, Guard Bai. Tell His Highness I’ll meet him bright and early tomorrow without fail.”

陈浩宇立刻明白，向门口的下人说：“去把苦荞叫出来。”
With a firm answer in hand, Bai Feng lingered anyway, craning his neck past the gateway.

白风很开心：“谢谢陈公子。”
Catching on fast, Chen Haoyu called to a gatekeeper, “Go tell Kuqiao to come out.”

乔文仙继续向陈浩宇嘱咐事情，他毕竟与朝中官员有所接触，还需将知道的以及该注意的事向陈浩宇说个明白，省得他到汴梁跟个无头苍蝇一般。
Bai Feng grinned wide. “Many thanks, Young Master Chen!”

南双则凑到白风跟前：“你要回汴梁？”
Drawing Chen Haoyu aside, Qiao Wenxian rattled off careful instructions on court etiquette and which officials to tread lightly around so he wouldn’t stumble blindly around the capital.

“对，明日就走。”
Meanwhile, Nan Shuangsidled over beside Bai Feng. “Heading back to Bianliang?”

“我们好久没见，见过苦荞后，来乔家找我，咱们好好聊聊。”
“Yep—leaving tomorrow.”

“好。”
“Haven’t caught up in ages. Once you finish talking with Kuqiao, drop by Qiao Manor and let’s have a real chat.”

叮嘱完后，乔文仙带着南双离开。
“Deal.”

陈浩宇则向白风拱手致谢后回府收拾东西。
Finishing his briefing, Qiao Wenxian departed with Nan Shuang.

苦荞终于出来，见到白风，眼泪不由流下来，哭道：“我家小姐被囚车拉去汴梁，生死未卜，该怎么办？”
Cupping his hands gratefully to Bai Feng, Chen Haoyu hurried back inside to pack.

白风安慰道：“王爷说明日回汴梁，如果我没猜错，他是为救幽栖居士才会这么快决定回汴梁。我来是告诉陈公子明日可与王爷同行。刚才乔公子还答应帮陈公子介绍些重要人物。所以，你不必担心，幽栖居士肯定没事。”
At last Kuqiao hurried out the gate. One look at Bai Feng, and tears burst right down her cheeks. “My Miss was hauled off to Bianliang in a prisoner cart—no telling if she’s dead or alive! What do we do?!”

苦荞露出笑容：“真的？”
Bai Feng hurried to reassure her. “His Highness leaves for Bianliang tomorrow—and if I’m not wrong, he’s only rushing north this fast to rescue Youqi Jushi! I just came to invite Young Master Chen to ride with us, and Young Master Qiao even promised letters introducing big shots in the capital! Don’t cry—Youqi Jushi’s going to be fine!”

“有这么多人关心她救她，怎么会有事？”
Hope lit a tearful smile on Kuqiao’s face. “Really?”

“这两日你去了哪里？”
“With this many people tearing down Heaven to save her, how could anything go wrong?”

“跟王爷一起被关在柳岸别馆。”
“Where were you these past two days?”

“你没事吧？”
“Locked up inside Liu’an Villa with His Highness.”

“没事。”白风怕苦荞问出身上的伤来，害她担心，赶紧转移话题，“要不你也跟着一起去汴梁？”
“Are you hurt?!”

苦荞二话不说答应：“好，我去，为了小姐我也要去。”
“Not a scratch!” Terrified she’d ask about his wound and worry sick, Bai Feng switched subjects fast. “Why don’t you come along to Bianliang with us?”

“太好了，我回去求王爷把你带上。”
Kuqiao agreed without blinking. “I’m coming! For Miss, I’d go anywhere!”

“为何要仪王带我？我家公子即可。”
“Brilliant! I’ll go beg His Highness to bring you along!”

“对对！给陈公子说说即可，看我高兴得都忘了你现在是陈家的丫鬟。”
“Why ask Prince Yi? My Young Master is going too!”

两人也只是片刻高兴，想到小姐的事，苦荞依旧愁眉不展。
“Oh—right, right! Just ask Young Master Chen! Haha, got so excited I forgot you’re a Chen maid now!”

白风又安慰一会儿，才与苦荞分别，跑去乔家找南双聊天儿。明日就要回汴梁，也不知何日还能见到他，走之前朋友之间还需阔别一番。
Their flicker of cheer faded fast as worry for Qianli Xun returned. After soothing her a bit longer, Bai Feng parted ways with Kuqiao and hurried off to Qiao Manor for one last catch-up with Nan Shuang before leaving town indefinitely.
