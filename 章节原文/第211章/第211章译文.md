## Chapter 211: Sending Servants to a Brothel to Fetch the Groom

Qianli Xun had set down only a handful of characters when Matron Wei walked in. Seeing her young mistress engrossed at her desk, the matron hated to interrupt, yet spoke up all the same. "Miss, why haven't you sent someone to bring the young master back?"

Having written so little, Qianli Xun had no worry that Matron Wei would see anything. She set down her brush and looked up. "Chen Haoyu and I have an agreement not to meddle in each other's affairs. Why would I bother looking for him?"

"Tomorrow is different," Matron Wei reminded her. "It is the third-day return visit to your maiden home. The groom cannot be absent, or people will call it bad luck. You had better bring him back, Miss. Besides, you promised Old Madam Xia, and you cannot go back on your word."

"Promising the old madam was just a stopgap. Going back to Zhu Manor without Chen Haoyu will let my father see for himself how unreliable his handpicked son-in-law is. As for good luck or bad luck, I could not care less."

Matron Wei understood her mistress, though she did not agree. "I understand how you feel, Miss, yet living in this world means you cannot think only of yourself. You have to weigh your surroundings and keep your actions within reason. Not for anything else, just to spare yourself trouble and buy some peace and quiet. Think it over: what will happen if you bring the young master back, versus if you don't?"

Qianli Xun turned it over in her head. If she left Chen Haoyu out there, Old Madam Xia would be back at her door making a scene. Showing up at Zhu Manor without him might send a pointed message to Zhu Xiyan, yet it would also hurt her parents and sour their opinion of the Chen family. Word might even spread among Lin'an's elite, whose wives and daughters had nothing better to do than chew over other households' gossip. Bringing Chen Haoyu back would nip every bit of that trouble in the bud.

Life left a person with so few clean choices, she reflected. Living in the mundane world meant playing by its rules, or inviting endless headaches.

In that instant, she saw Master Jingguang's words about "Right Thought and Right Mindfulness" in a new light. What counted as "right" depended on the situation, and right now, hauling Chen Haoyu home was the smartest move. "One word from you wakes the dreamer, Matron. I'll send someone to track him down right away."

Matron Wei smiled. "I'll leave you to it, Miss. It's been a tiring day, so I'll go rest."

Once Matron Wei left, Qianli Xun told Kuqiao to fetch Qinggua.

Stepping into the main room, Qinggua felt as if he had wandered into a stranger's house. The place looked nothing like before, and he dared not look around too much. This new young madam was formidable, unafraid to bully his master and bold enough to talk back to Old Madam Xia herself.

"Young Madam, you sent for me?"

With no mood left for her huaben and a runaway husband to hunt down, Qianli Xun turned the half-written sheet face down on the desk. "You heard Old Madam Xia tell me to bring Chen Haoyu back. You follow him around often, so you must have the best idea where he went."

Qinggua jumped straight into airing his grievances. "Young Madam, you just married in, so you wouldn't know. Young Master never takes me along when he goes out, and he loves kicking me for no reason. He only hangs around street hoodlums who tag along to sponge off his money, unlike me, who's devoted to him heart and soul. Yet he never appreciates it, and he doesn't even realize they're a bad influence..."

Qianli Xun had no patience for rambling. "I get that you have it rough. Right now, just tell me where to find Chen Haoyu."

Qinggua thought for a moment. "He never takes me with him, so it's hard to say what he gets up to outside or where he goes. Still, since last year he's been fond of haunting brothels. He might be in one right now."

Chunxiang Brothel sprang straight to Qianli Xun's mind. "Kuqiao, go call Hongdou."

Kuqiao fetched Hongdou at once.

"Hongdou," Qianli Xun instructed, "take seven of our male servants and head to Chunxiang Brothel. Bring Chen Haoyu back to me. If he refuses, tie him up with rope and haul him back."

Hongdou hesitated. "Miss, Chunxiang Brothel is a house of ill repute. I..."

"Why are you being so dense? You don't have to step inside. Just send the seven men in."

Relief washed over Hongdou's face. "Yes, Miss. I'll take them right now."

Once Hongdou left, Qinggua felt awkward lingering. "Young Madam, I'll go wait by the front gate for Young Master."

"Go on." Qianli Xun flipped her manuscript page back over and picked up her brush, meaning to resume writing, yet her mind stayed blank. She tossed the brush back down. "Too many people and too much drama in Chen Manor. It's killed my mood to write. Tomorrow will do."

Kuqiao stepped over and gathered the sheet with its few lines of ink. "I'll put this away for you, Miss. You can pick it back up when inspiration strikes."

After stowing the manuscript, Kuqiao walked back with a smile. "Miss, from now on you don't need to worry about Matron Wei seeing you write huaben."

"Why?" Qianli Xun asked, puzzled.

"She guessed ages ago that Master Qianli Xun is you, Miss. She just never called you out to your face."

"Nothing ever gets past Matron Wei. Just as well; now I won't have to hide it from her." A second thought crossed Qianli Xun's mind. "Does anyone else in this courtyard know?"

"You and I covered our tracks well, Miss. Aside from Matron Wei, nobody has a clue."

"That puts my mind at ease." Since she had no spark for fiction tonight, she might as well jot down a few of Zhu Shuzhen's poems for Kuqiao to keep on hand, so she would not have to scramble the next time Huayu came asking.

Meanwhile, Hongdou led seven martial-arts-trained male servants in two carriages to the doors of Chunxiang Brothel. She turned to one of them. "Asheng, take the boys inside and fetch the young master. I'll wait out here. Every last one of you listen up: don't let the women in there bewitch you and make you forget your errand!"

"Don't worry, Sister Hongdou," Asheng answered. "Even if we weren't scared of you, we're scared of Miss. Her fists don't mess around."

Hongdou cracked a smile. "Glad you remember. Go on."

Asheng and six others hopped down from the carriages and strode straight into Chunxiang Brothel, where the air reeked of perfume and rouge.

Spotting seven unfamiliar men dressed like household servants, the brothel madam stepped forward. "Don't recognize your faces, boys. Here for a good time, or looking for someone?"

"We're here for our young master, Young Master Chen," Asheng said.

"Ah, servants from the Chen household. Young Master Chen is upstairs enjoying himself in Chun Jinmo's room right now. I doubt he'll be coming back with you." Having no use for seven penniless servants, the madam turned to walk away.

Asheng shot a look to his companions, and three of them stepped straight across her path.

Asheng moved up. "Be so kind as to tell Young Master Chen that our young mistress requests his return. She also said that if he won't come down, we are to tie him up and drag him home."

The madam had never run into servants this brazen, yet she saw no sense in picking a fight. Spotting Chun Jinqiao finishing a performance on stage and heading for the stairs, she called out loud, "Jinqiao! Go upstairs and call Young Master Chen. Tell him someone's downstairs looking for him."

Lin Jue had not shown up today, leaving Chun Jinqiao with time to spare. She glanced at the men blocking the madam and called back, "Hold on, Madam, I'll go fetch him."

The madam forced a wide smile. "He'll be right down, boys. Step aside now; I've got work to do."

The three servants moved out of her way.

Before long, Chen Haoyu descended the stairs flanked by three martial-arts bodyguards, with Scarface and the rest of his lackeys trailing behind. He scanned the hall as he came down, seeing nothing but regular brothel patrons until his gaze snagged on seven men in servant livery who looked familiar.

Asheng recognized him at once. As soon as Chen Haoyu reached the bottom step, Asheng stepped forward and bowed. "Young Master, our young mistress asks you to return home."

Only then did Chen Haoyu place their faces, and a scowl crossed his brow. "She's sticking her nose in far too wide. Go tell her I'm not coming back yet. I'll go home once I've had my fill of fun."

"Tomorrow is the return visit to Zhu Manor, Young Master," Asheng pressed. "You must return tonight."

"Well now! Who the hell do you think you are, ordering me around? 'Must,' is it? Let me tell you something: I'm not going back, and let's see what you can do about it!"

In a crowded brothel, Chen Haoyu had no intention of earning a name as a henpecked husband. Besides, with three hired fighters at his back, seven servants meant nothing to him.

Asheng uncoiled a length of rope from behind his back. "Young Master, our mistress said if you refuse, we are to tie you up and carry you home."

Every head in the hall swiveled toward them; even the courtesans on stage stopped performing.

Chen Haoyu would never lose face before a crowd. He waved at his three bodyguards. "Teach them a lesson for me! Show them how proper servants behave!"

The three fighters held the seven servants in total contempt, assuming rich men's lackeys knew only how to pour tea and run errands. Each fighter figured he was worth two of them and could flatten all seven with ease. Snarling, they threw their fists forward.

The seven servants split into three squads on the spot, one group of three and two pairs, and met the fighters head-on.

Patrons scrambled as far back as they could. The madam ducked behind cover, shouting, "Stop fighting! Stop fighting! Break anything and you're paying for it!"

Seeing the seven servants unable to gain ground at first, Chen Haoyu crowed, "Relax! I'm right here. Whatever gets smashed, I'll pay full price!"

The madam shut her mouth and tucked herself into a corner to watch the show. With a deep-pocketed patron footing the bill, Chunxiang Brothel stood to lose nothing.

One-on-one, none of the servants could guarantee a win against these hired fighters, yet two or three against one was another story. Numbers told, and their months of fist training under Qianli Xun were no joke. Inch by inch, the seven servants seized the upper hand.

Within a quarter of an hour, all three bodyguards lay groaning on the floor with swollen faces and bruised noses. The seven servants had taken a few knocks themselves, nothing more than scrapes.

Asheng wiped a smear of blood from the corner of his mouth, unhitched the rope he had retied around his waist for the brawl, and faced Chen Haoyu. "Will you come home on your own two feet, Young Master, or should I tie you up?"

Chen Haoyu flipped his tune in a heartbeat. "Put that rope away! Why wouldn't I want to go home to my own house? I'm coming, I'm coming."

He strolled over to the three downed fighters and jeered, "You three need to practice harder instead of taking my silver for nothing. Can't even beat my own household servants, and you have the nerve to call yourselves masters. Hmph!"

One fighter winced through the pain. "Young Master Chen, we haven't seen a single wen for guarding you today. When do you plan to pay us?"

"When have I ever been stingy about money?" Chen Haoyu blustered. "I'll pay you another day, no question."

The brothel madam hurried over too. "Young Master Chen, look at the wreck your servants made of Chunxiang Brothel. When will you settle for the damages?"

"Relax, I'll pay you next time I'm here," Chen Haoyu declared without blinking.

Watching Chen Haoyu's retreating back, Scarface muttered to a crony, "What's gotten into Young Master Chen today? Going out without a scrap of silver? That's rare. Think that tigress he married has him on a tight leash and cut off his allowance?"

"Looks like it," the lackey agreed.

Chen Haoyu resented the whole affair, yet his feet stayed obedient as the seven servants escorted him out of Chunxiang Brothel. Along the way, he tried sweet-talking them. "Seven grown men like you—what's the fun in tagging along behind Zhu Shuzhen? Come work for me instead. I'll treat you to the best food and fun in Lin'an. Beats being someone's lackey."

"Thank you for thinking so high of us, Young Master," Asheng replied. "We belong to Miss's household, and every move we know we learned from her. She is both our mistress and our martial master. What kind of disciple abandons his master?"

Chen Haoyu gave up and climbed into one of the Chen carriages, only to spot Hongdou sitting inside. "Get out at once. I always ride alone."

As long as the groom came home without fuss, Hongdou obeyed as a servant should. She hopped down, crammed five of the men into the other carriage, and walked alongside Chen Haoyu's carriage with Asheng.

Sitting alone inside grew dull fast. Thinking back on how skilled Zhu Shuzhen's people were, Chen Haoyu wished he had loyal fighters like them at his beck and call.

He poked his head out the carriage window. "I have a soft heart, and I'd hate to see you walk yourselves ragged. I'll allow you two to ride inside with me."

Neither Hongdou nor Asheng stood on ceremony. Once the driver pulled up, both climbed aboard.

Chen Haoyu fished for an opening. "Like it or not, I'm your young master, half an owner of this house. From now on, shouldn't you follow me wherever I go so nobody bullies me? Take that Master Jiang, for instance. You clashed with his men last time, so you know him. Who knows when he might come hunting trouble with me again?"

"Protecting you is no problem, Young Master," Hongdou answered. "The moment Miss gives the word, we'll guard you."

"Forget I said anything." Chen Haoyu shut his mouth. He knew without asking that Zhu Shuzhen would never agree. He had hoped to poach one or two of them, only to get shut down before he even warmed up.

Still, he felt a pang of envy toward Zhu Shuzhen for keeping such devoted people around her. When would he ever have followers like that—obedient, tough, and not just hanging around for his silver?
