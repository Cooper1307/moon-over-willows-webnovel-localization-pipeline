## Chapter 333: Hunting for the Miracle Doctor in Yanjing

Arriving in eerily quiet Yanjing—still half-empty two years after the Jin stripped its youth and handed back a hollow shell—Shen Jianchen left Bai Feng and Xilan at the inn (where Xilan hinted shyly at liking Bai Feng, only to get cheerfully turned down in favor of Kuqiao!) and strolled out alone with Qianli Xun, secretly dragging out asking for Liu Miaoshou just to savor walking beside her incognito.

Spotting an apothecary right away and calling out his ploy, Qianli Xun quickly got directions—and paused when Shen Jianchen once more pressed whom she'd pick if completely free. *"I'd follow my heart,"* she admitted softly—before poking his chest sharply the second he grinned triumphantly: *"Both of us are married! Don't drag me into an affair!"* Stopping mid-sentence before swearing *"unless pigs can climb trees"* again, she left him beaming with renewed hope.

Turning west to **Liu Miaoshou's Clinic**, they caught apprentices frantically loading wagons to flee south to Yangzhou ahead of the coming Jin army! Inside, wiry old Physician Liu sipped straight from a small purple clay teapot, refusing patients—until an apprentice walked in carrying a lush pot of pure white chrysanthemums gifted by a grateful neighbor boy, giving Qianli Xun her instant opening!
