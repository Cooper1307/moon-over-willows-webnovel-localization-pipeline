# Chapter 82: Standing Outside for Long to Earn a Hug

---

Qiao Wenxian left the Qiao residence but didn't return directly to Youlin Villa. Instead, he went strolling on the streets.

---

Though Qiao Shouren hadn't explicitly refused the marriage proposal, Qiao Wenxian could hear in his father's voice that he was dissatisfied with Shuzhen. He feared the proposal would meet obstacles. As he strolled, he recalled his father's words and the expressions on his face while speaking. The more he thought about it, the more worried he became.

---

He had finally waited for Shuzhen to divorce and fall in love with him. He had thought that when he returned home to tell his parents, they would readily agree and immediately go to the Zhu family to propose. Who would have expected things to go against his wishes?

---

If in a few days he returned to the Qiao residence again and brought up the marriage proposal, only to be explicitly refused by his father once more, what should he do? Qiao Wenxian thought of the Prince of Yi. Why not ask him for help? He was a prince. If he stepped forward, his father would give some face.

---

Qiao Wenxian stopped wandering aimlessly. He boarded the carriage following behind him and returned to Youlin Villa.

---

As soon as he entered the villa's gate, Nanshuang came to meet him: "Young Master went out without taking me along, making me worry about you the whole time at the villa."

---

"What's there to worry about? I was returning to the Qiao residence, my own home. By the way, is the Prince of Yi here?"

---

"He is, but he drank too much and is sleeping in his room."

---

"Drank too much? Why?"

---

"Baifeng said he and the Prince of Yi went to Juxiang Restaurant for a meal. The wine there was so good that the prince had a few extra cups."

---

"Alright, in that case, I won't disturb him for now."

---

Qiao Wenxian headed toward the courtyard where he stayed. Reaching the courtyard gate, he saw the wooden platform. Why not go up and sit for a while? Gazing into the distance might help ease his mind: "Nanshuang, bring me a bottle of osmanthus wine. I want to sit up there, enjoy the view, and drink."

---

Nanshuang went back to get the wine. Qiao Wenxian climbed onto the wooden platform, sat at the table, and gazed into the distance. His field of vision broadened, but the worry in his heart didn't ease.

---

Nanshuang got the wine and also climbed onto the platform. He filled the prince's cup and reminded: "Young Master, don't get dead drunk like the Prince of Yi."

---

"Osmanthus wine doesn't have much kick. I won't get drunk."

---

Qiao Wenxian picked up his wine cup and slowly sipped. Rather than saying he was enjoying the scenery, it was more like he was killing time, waiting for the Prince of Yi to sober up.

---

After he finished a bottle of osmanthus wine, the sky gradually darkened. He estimated the Prince of Yi should be awake by now.

---

"Nanshuang, come with me to see the Prince of Yi."

---

The two climbed down from the platform and came to the courtyard outside the main building. Princess Jiahe was actually standing there, looking melancholy.

---

Qiao Wenxian stepped forward and asked: "Why is the princess standing at the gate?"

---

"I... I'm waiting for my brother to wake up." Qiao Mengrong said carefully.

---

Qiao Wenxian invited: "Would the princess like to come in with me?"

---

"No, I'm afraid my brother will be unhappy seeing me because I did something wrong." It turned out Qiao Mengrong still thought her brother was angry with her, disgusted that she had kissed him.

---

Seeing Princess Jiahe's pitiful and delicate appearance, Qiao Wenxian felt pity: "Why doesn't the princess go back? It's getting dark."

---

"Young Master Qiao doesn't need to worry about me. I want to wait here for my brother to wake up." Qiao Mengrong still persisted.

---

Qiao Wenxian stopped insisting. After bowing, he walked into the courtyard and headed straight for the main building. No one was in the outer hall, so he turned into the inner room.

---

The Prince of Yi lay on the bed, his eyes wide open, obviously sober.

---

Seeing Nanshuang come in, Baifeng, who was standing around bored, went over and pulled her: "Let's go out and play."

---

Nanshuang nodded in agreement. They held hands and left the main building, seeing Princess Jiahe in the courtyard.

---

After Qiao Wenxian entered the room, Qiao Mengrong boldly walked into the courtyard. She stood in the middle of the courtyard continuing to wait. Xilan placed a chair for her, but she refused to sit.

---

Seeing Baifeng and Nanshuang, Qiao Mengrong asked before they could bow: "Baifeng, is my brother awake?"

---

Baifeng replied: "The prince woke up long ago, but he just wants to lie down. I can't do anything about it."

---

"Is my brother happy?"

---

"Um... the prince neither laughed nor cried. He looks calm, so he should be fine."

---

Qiao Mengrong relaxed, indicating her brother was no longer angry with her: "I've finished asking. You two go play."

---

Baifeng and Nanshuang happily ran out.

---

Inside the room, Qiao Wenxian sat on the edge of the bed: "Since you're sober, get up quickly. Princess Jiahe has been standing outside all this time. Hurry up and coax her."

---

Of course Shen Jianchen cared for his sister, but the invisible pressure this sister caused him far exceeded the feeling of care. He turned around, his back to Wenyuan: "Help me persuade her to go back and rest."

---

"I've already tried persuading her. It was no use at all." Qiao Wenxian pulled the Prince of Yi with his hand. "Get up. Don't let down the beauty outside. If you go persuade her, she'll definitely listen."

---

Shen Jianchen pulled his hand back, still with his back to Wenyuan.

---

Qiao Wenxian took off his shoes and also lay on the bed: "Since you don't want to get up, I'll sleep with you."

---

Shen Jianchen still had his back to Wenyuan.

---

"Can you promise me something?"

---

"What?"

---

"In a few days, come back to the Qiao residence with me and persuade my father to go to the Zhu family to propose."

---

"Solve your own problems yourself."

---

"I've already made it clear to my father. Though he hasn't explicitly refused, I can feel he doesn't agree at all with proposing to the Zhu family."

---

Hearing this, Shen Jianchen actually felt a trace of relief. He turned around: "If your grandfather doesn't agree, what's the use of me going?"

---

"You're a prince. My father would never dare refuse your face. Just agree and help me." Qiao Wenxian continued to plead.

---

Shen Jianchen didn't speak and turned his back to Wenyuan again.

---

Qiao Wenxian hugged the Prince of Yi from behind: "If you don't agree, I'll keep hugging you and make Princess Jiahe jealous."

---

"Hug away if you want. Mengrong won't be so petty."

---

Shen Jianchen ignored him. He was conflicted. Should he help Wenyuan?

---

If he helped Wenyuan, in the future Qianlixun would be Wenyuan's person, and he, Shen Jianchen, would have not even a trace of hope.

---

If he didn't help, Wenyuan was still his best friend, and he also hoped Wenyuan could spend his life with the person he loved.

---

...

---

After dark, the garden of the Zhu residence became much brighter with lanterns hung everywhere. Qianlixun couldn't understand why Zhu Shuzhen liked such a dark courtyard. Wouldn't it be more beautiful if it were bright?

---

She walked around the courtyard several times, still not satisfied. She didn't expect the small courtyard to be even more beautiful at night, set off by the lantern light, than during the day.

---

After walking until tired, she sat in a rattan chair and appreciated it some more.

---

With such beautiful scenery, only Kuqiao accompanied her to appreciate it. If her lover were by her side, it would be even more perfect.

---

Thinking of this, Qianlixun felt the garden where Zhu Shuzhen lived was too lonely and cold.

---

"Kuqiao, I want to go to Youlin Villa."

---

"It's already dark. If the young lady goes out, Madam and Nanny Wei will definitely not agree."

---

"I'll sneak out secretly. If they don't know, isn't that fine?"

---

"Going out of the city at night will be very dangerous."

---

"Dangerous? In my eyes, no one is more dangerous than me!"

---

That's true. After her young lady was reborn, she became formidable and feared no one: "In that case, I'll go with you, young lady."

---

Qianlixun praised: "Good girl!"

---

The mistress and maid walked out of the garden, quietly sneaked out of the Zhu residence, hitched a carriage, and headed straight out of the city.

---

...

---

The sky grew darker and darker. Wenyuan was actually hugging the Prince of Yi and nearly fell asleep, until Nanshuang's voice came from outside the door: "Young Master, Youqi Jushi is waiting for you outside the villa."

---

Qiao Wenxian immediately sat up. He was just thinking of Shuzhen, and didn't expect she would come. They were really on the same wavelength! Qiao Wenxian excitedly put on his shoes and ran out.

---

Reaching the courtyard, he saw Princess Jiahe still standing there. Without time to ask, he gave a simple bow and hurriedly left.

---

Nanshuang couldn't figure it out. There was clearly a chair, so why wouldn't Princess Jiahe sit? As he thought, he followed his young master out.

---

Hearing Qianlixun had come, Shen Jianchen also immediately got up, his speed no less than Wenyuan's. He followed closely behind Wenyuan to the gate and found the servants had already lit the lanterns in the courtyard, making it quite bright. Qiao Mengrong stood in the courtyard, her legs and feet moving slightly, obviously having stood for a long time.

---

Shen Jianchen couldn't help but stop: "Mengrong, why are you still standing here?"

---

Hearing her brother's voice, Qiao Mengrong showed a smile: "I was waiting for my brother to wake up."

---

"Why didn't you come inside?"

---

"I didn't dare. I was afraid my brother would be disgusted with me."

---

Qiao Mengrong's appearance, so pitiful it broke his heart, made Shen Jianchen's heart ache: "How could I be disgusted with you?"

---

"Brother, I was wrong. I shouldn't have under the divine willow..."

---

Shen Jianchen had long forgotten about being forcibly kissed by Qiao Mengrong. It turned out she misunderstood that his bad mood was because of this: "No, you weren't wrong. It was your brother's fault."

---

"Brother doesn't resent me anymore?"

---

"I've never resented you."

---

Qiao Mengrong felt completely relaxed, and from the relaxation, she couldn't even stand. Xilan quickly supported the princess.

---

Shen Jianchen ordered: "Xilan, help the princess back to her room to rest. Mengrong, don't do this again in the future. Remember, your brother will never be angry with you."

---

Qiao Mengrong shed tears from happiness. She bid farewell to her brother and, with Xilan's support, walked outward. Because she had stood for so long, her legs and feet had gone numb, and walking was painfully sore.

---

Seeing this, Shen Jianchen stepped forward and picked up Mengrong in his arms: "I'll take you back to your room."

---

The sudden happiness left Qiao Mengrong with no time to think. She simply felt her brother's strong arms and chest. The scene she had fantasized about many times had finally come true. The discomfort her body had from standing for so long vanished in this moment.

---

Shen Jianchen carried Qiao Mengrong back to her boudoir, placed her on the bed, and said: "Rest well." Then, without the slightest hesitation, he walked quickly toward the villa's main gate.
