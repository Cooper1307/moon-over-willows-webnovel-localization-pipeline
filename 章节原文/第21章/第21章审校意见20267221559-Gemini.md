整体来看，第21章的翻译**依然非常流畅且趣味十足！**

这一章充满了戏剧性的反转和喜剧效果：女主用治淤青的药帮渣男“销毁了家暴（互殴）证据”，又用腐肌膏“加重了自己的伤势”准备在离婚谈判上卖惨。同时，渣男林觉竟然因为女主变凶了反而产生了“受虐倾向（好感）”，甚至偷偷开始看唐诗试图挽回她。你的翻译把这种黑色幽默和人物心理的转变拿捏得非常准。

针对你的审校需求，以下是几个细节上的优化建议，主要集中在**“拼写纠错”**、**“中式骂人俚语的地道化”**以及**“标题的网文感”**上：

### 1. 译文的标题是否足够吸引读者阅读兴趣？
*   **原译**：*Chapter 21: Questioned After the Poetry Gathering*
*   **点评**：有些平淡，像是在写流水账。这一章的爽点在于女主与渣男的交锋，以及渣男突然“犯贱”不想离婚了。
*   **建议修改**：**Chapter 21: The Scumbag’s Change of Heart**（渣男的回心转意） 或者 **Chapter 21: The Eve of the Divorce Showdown**（离婚大戏的前夕）。

### 2. 致命的拼写错误（重要！）
*   **纠错**：本章中，丫鬟苦荞的名字在多处被错误拼写成了 **Kubiao**（P2, P4, P49 等等）。英文读者对拼写非常敏感，这会引起阅读断层，我在下文的精修版中已为你全部修正回 **Kuqiao**。

### 3. 表达是否正确且地道？（核心修改点）

*   **P15 中式俗语的翻译（狗嘴里吐不出象牙）**：`You filthy mouth can't utter decent words...`
    *   **优化**：“狗嘴里吐不出象牙”是极具中国特色的俗语，直译老外很难get到骂人的爽感。这里可以直接用西方常见的互骂表达。
    *   **修改**：`"Nothing but pure filth comes out of that mouth of yours! Your mind is entirely in the gutter!"` （in the gutter 专门指满脑子龌龊、下流思想，极其地道）。
*   **P17 骂男人“婆婆妈妈”**：`you big man, acting like an old woman!`
    *   **优化**：在英文中骂男人婆婆妈妈，更常用 nag（唠叨）或者像老母鸡（old hen）。
    *   **修改**：`"You're a grown man, stop nagging like an old hen!"`
*   **P19 掂量掂量自己几斤几两**：`You don't even know what you're up against.`
    *   **优化**：原译意思是对的，但不够拽。
    *   **修改**：`"You'd better weigh your own strength before raising a hand against me."`
*   **P25 “我看你骨骼惊奇”的武侠梗**：`I felt you had good bone structure.`
    *   **优化**：`bone structure` 通常用于形容超模脸（骨相好），在武侠语境里“根骨好”应该说 martial arts frame 或 potential。
    *   **修改**：`"I can tell you've got an excellent frame for martial arts. How about this..."`
*   **P55 哑巴亏的精准翻译**：`The Zhu family can only swallow the bitter pill.`
    *   **点评**：**翻译得极好！** `swallow the bitter pill` 完美对应了“吃哑巴亏”。

### 4. 动作与节奏的优化
*   **P6 跳楼的动作张力**：`You jumped from the second floor to the first floor...`
    *   **修改**：`"You leaped straight off the second-floor balcony... "`（跳下二楼阳台，画面感更强）。
*   **P56 恶人先告状的细节**：`He had done his homework in advance...`
    *   **优化**：“提前做好功课”在这里是指渣男提前做局、恶人先告状。
    *   **修改**：`He had preemptively visited his father-in-law... playing the victim by crying...`

### 🌟 综合精修版英文参考 (Polished Version)

以下是吸收了上述建议后的精修版，修正了拼写错误，强化了男女主互怼时的火力，以及渣男偷偷看诗集的喜剧感：

**Chapter 21: The Eve of the Divorce Showdown**

On the way back, Qianli Xun felt quite satisfied with her performance. She could tell that while Zhu Shuzhen's four friends clearly noticed a shift in her personality, they didn't suspect for a second that she was an entirely different person. Her brilliant critique of Li Qingzhao's poem had flawlessly backed up Zhu Shuzhen's genius reputation. Thinking of this, a triumphant smile spread across her face.
Seeing her miss so happy, Kuqiao remarked, "You seem to be in a wonderful mood, Miss."
"Of course." Qianli Xun suddenly grew curious about how the original owner used to behave. "Kuqiao, when I met with those three gentlemen and Lady Miao in the past, was I talkative? Or did I still act all aloof and icy?"
Kuqiao smiled. "You only acted icy toward people who didn't understand poetry. You didn't even talk much with your own family. But whenever you met Young Master Dongfang and the rest of your literary circle, you would instantly light up and talk freely."
"Oh, so that's the kind of person I was," Qianli Xun nodded. "No wonder they didn't think much was out of the ordinary today."
"How could they not notice?!" Kuqiao gasped. "You leaped straight off the second-floor balcony to punch Young Master Chen! Young Master Dongfang and the others were so shocked their eyes nearly popped out of their heads. But fortunately, they just assumed you had been hiding your martial arts talents all along."
Qianli Xun chuckled. "Meeting those friends today actually made me realize something. I am *me*. There is absolutely no need for me to force myself to mimic the old Zhu Shuzhen. I should just act naturally and let my true self shine through."
Qianli Xun decided that from now on, the exhausting charade was over. Instead of pretending to be the old Zhu Shuzhen, she would simply absorb Zhu Shuzhen's legacy into her own identity.
As the carriage pulled up to the gates of the Lin residence, Qianli Xun remembered the Fuji Ointment in her pocket. It was time to apply it. She hurried straight toward the Zhulan Residence.
She had barely stepped into the western courtyard when Lin Jue blocked her path.
"And where has my wife been?" he demanded.
Qianli Xun immediately noticed that the dark bruise around his left eye had completely vanished. *That medicine from Jixiang Pharmacy really works wonders,* she rejoiced internally. With his bruise gone, he wouldn't be able to claim mutual combat during the divorce!
When she didn't answer, Lin Jue pressed harder. "Why aren't you speaking?"
"I went to a poetry gathering to catch up with some friends," Qianli Xun answered casually.
"Hmph!" Lin Jue sneered, his voice dripping with resentment. "More likely you used the poetry gathering as an excuse to fool around with those so-called scholars."
"Lin Jue! Nothing but pure filth comes out of that mouth of yours! Your mind is entirely in the gutter, and this queen has no time for you!" Furious at the baseless accusation, Qianli Xun turned on her heel to leave.
Lin Jue quickly darted forward, blocking her way. "I warned you not to go to those poetry gatherings. Why didn't you listen to me?"
Qianli Xun rolled her eyes impatiently. "You're a grown man, Lin Jue, stop nagging like an old hen! We're getting a divorce tomorrow anyway, so why are you sticking your nose in my business? And even if we weren't, you couldn't control me. I do whatever I want!"
Feeling utterly humiliated, Lin Jue raised his hand to slap her.
*Smack!* Qianli Xun caught his wrist mid-air effortlessly. 
"What? Want to try your hand at domestic violence again?" she challenged, her eyes turning lethal. "You'd better weigh your own strength before raising a hand against me."
Blocked by her terrifying, immovable strength, Lin Jue knew he didn't stand a chance against this bizarre new version of his wife. He forced himself to lower his hand, trying to soften his tone.
"One day, while I was training soldiers at the camp, I overheard them gossiping behind my back," he confessed grumpily. "They said you and Dongfang Chenze had something going on. They said you were making eyes at Qiao Wenxian. They said... well, it was incredibly ugly. I am your husband. I am a man. Hearing the whole town whisper about my wife like that—how could I not be furious? I warned you not to attend Dongfang Chenze's gatherings. You behaved for a while, but today you ran right back! Do you want the entire city of Lin'an to laugh at me?!"
Seeing Lin Jue's temper deflate, Qianli Xun decided not to escalate things further. She didn't want to make things irreparably tense on the eve of their divorce. 
"Your biggest flaw, Lin Jue, is that you lack even the most basic trust in your partner," she said earnestly. "If you ever remarry, do not repeat this mistake. To prove my innocence, the next time they invite me, I'll take you along so you can see exactly what we do. But by then, we'll likely be divorced. Doesn't matter, though. Once the marriage is over, we can still be friends. I'll take you as my friend."
Lin Jue was completely dumbfounded. In the past, whenever rumors like this flared up, Zhu Shuzhen refused to offer a single word of explanation, let alone invite him to her gatherings. To be brutally honest, she had always despised him deep down, viewing him as a crude brute who couldn't appreciate poetry, let alone romance. But now, this new Zhu Shuzhen was actually offering to bring him along? If she had been this communicative earlier, perhaps their relationship wouldn't have shattered like this.
"I have no desire to attend those pretentious gatherings," he muttered, looking away. "Qiao Wenxian and his lot are a bunch of sour, stinky pedants. I wouldn't fit in."
Qianli Xun looked Lin Jue up and down appraisingly. "You know, from the moment I first saw you, I could tell you have an excellent frame for martial arts. How about this: after we divorce, you can formally take me as your master, and I'll teach you the Qianli Fist."
Lin Jue's jaw practically hit the floor. The sheer absurdity of the offer further cemented his terrifying theory: the fragile Zhu Shuzhen had definitely died from his beating, and some eccentric martial arts spirit had possessed her corpse. A sudden wave of superstitious fear washed over him. He instinctively took two steps back, turned around, and practically fled the courtyard.
Qianli Xun watched his retreating back in confusion. "Hey! Where are you going?! Don't you want to be my disciple?"
Lin Jue didn't answer. He simply vanished from sight.
"What a weird guy," Qianli Xun muttered to herself.
Kuqiao sighed. "Miss, *you're* the weird one here. Taking your own husband as a martial arts disciple? Even I was stunned hearing it, let alone the young master."
Qianli Xun shrugged it off. Today, the name she had heard most frequently was 'Qiao Wenxian'. What exactly was his relationship with the original Zhu Shuzhen? She needed to get to the bottom of this.
"Kuqiao, come inside. Your Miss has some questions for you."
"Yes, Miss."
Once inside the Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun changed into comfortable loungewear. "I keep hearing people bring up Qiao Wenxian. Who exactly is he?"
As she folded her miss's discarded outer robes, Kuqiao explained, "He is a young master from the powerful Qiao family, born to one of Lord Qiao's concubines. Because he is the sole male heir destined to inherit the family's vast fortune, he was raised by the main wife. He has four older sisters. His eldest sister entered the imperial palace and became Consort Qiao. Unfortunately, her fate was tragic; she passed away shortly after giving birth to a prince."
"How do you know all this in such detail?" Qianli Xun asked, amazed that a simple maid knew the intricate politics of a noble house.
"The Qiao family's affairs are hardly a secret in Lin'an," Kuqiao smiled. "Because they produced an Imperial Consort, the public naturally gossips about them constantly. I heard all about it from the other maids back at the Zhu estate."
"Is Qiao Wenxian frequently invited to Dongfang Chenze's poetry gatherings?"
"He is. However, he never actually composes any poetry or *ci* himself. He only attends to appreciate *your* poetry, Miss. He has an incredibly sharp eye for literature and is the best at truly understanding your work. That is why you always considered him a soulmate."
"A soulmate? Wait... did I have feelings for him?"
"At that time, your heart was entirely consumed by Young Master Xu, Miss. You had no room for anyone else."
Qianli Xun let out an exasperated sigh. "It's entirely my parents' fault for not telling me the truth about the Xu family earlier, letting the original Zhu Shuzhen waste her feelings! If it were me today, I wouldn't bat an eye even if that cowardly Young Master Xu threw himself at my feet. I seriously question the old me's taste in men."
"Actually, Young Master Xu wasn't a bad person," Kuqiao defended mildly. "He was incredibly handsome, exceptionally talented, and shared all your interests. Plus, you grew up together as childhood sweethearts. It was only natural you fell for him. His only flaw was that he obeyed his parents too blindly. To put it nicely, he was filial. But in doing so, he treated you horribly unfairly."
"Well, enough about him. He's ancient history," Qianli Xun declared. "Let's focus on tomorrow's divorce. Bring me the Fuji Ointment."
Kuqiao reluctantly fetched the tiny porcelain vial. "I really can't fathom why you insist on harming yourself, Miss."
Qianli Xun took it, sat down before the bronze mirror, and dabbed a tiny speck of the ointment directly onto her healing wound. Instantly, the skin inflamed, making the injury look fresh, raw, and horrifyingly severe. "You'll see exactly why tomorrow. Now, go find Lin Jue and get my bruise-healing ointment back from him."
"Yes, Miss."

Kuqiao left the Zhulan Residence and walked to Lin Jue's quarters. Standing cautiously outside, she called out, "Young Master, the Miss asked me to retrieve the bruise ointment."
After a moment, a servant opened the door. "The Master tells you to come inside."
Walking in, Kuqiao was utterly bewildered to find Lin Jue sitting at his desk, intensely studying a book. *Since when does the young master read?!* she thought. 
Glancing closer, Kuqiao clearly saw the title on the cover: *Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng*. Having served her miss since childhood, Kuqiao recognized a few characters and knew that Wang Youcheng (Wang Wei) was a famous Tang Dynasty poet whose work her miss absolutely adored. She couldn't believe the brute was actually trying to read poetry!
Seeing Kuqiao enter, Lin Jue quickly placed the book face down on the desk, clearing his throat. "The bottle is on the table. Take it."
Kuqiao grabbed the bottle, curtsied, and was just turning to leave when Lin Jue's voice stopped her. "Wait. I have a question for you."
Kuqiao paused. "What is it, Young Master?"
"Your miss is acting entirely different. Is the Zhu family... truly not furious with me?"
*Does he really have the nerve to ask that?!* Kuqiao scoffed internally. *Just watch me.* 
"How could they not resent you?" she retorted coolly. "However, the concept of a soul being 'reborn' is simply too bizarre for the authorities to believe. Furthermore, while my Miss's temperament has drastically changed, she still physically looks exactly the same. Even if the Zhu family went to the magistrate to accuse you of beating their daughter to death, there is zero evidence. The Zhu family has no choice but to swallow this bitter pill."
Lin Jue still harbored some lingering fear of his father-in-law, the Prefect of Lin'an. Fortunately, he had acted preemptively. Before Zhu Shuzhen had even woken up, he had rushed to the Prefect's office, dropped to his knees, and played the ultimate victim. He tearfully claimed he had only struck Zhu Shuzhen because she had humiliated him by shamelessly flirting with scholars around the city, and the gossip had driven him mad. As for the brothel girls, he blamed it entirely on Zhu Shuzhen refusing to consummate their marriage. Thanks to his proactive, tearful performance, his father-in-law had actually taken his side and hadn't punished him.
"Tell me..." Lin Jue hesitated. "Do you prefer the way your miss is now, or the way she was before?"
Kuqiao flatly refused to give him the satisfaction of an honest answer. "As long as it is my Miss, I love her exactly the same, past or present."
"Well, I like her much better now," Lin Jue admitted surprisingly. "She knows martial arts, which makes her somewhat of a kindred spirit. But most importantly, she actually talks to me now, instead of freezing me out. Tell me... does she still like all that flowery, pretentious poetry nonsense?"
"I hate to disappoint you, Young Master," Kuqiao said, her tone dripping with subtle satisfaction. "But my Miss's literary genius has not faded one bit. Today at the gathering, she offered a brilliant critique of Yi'an Jushi's poetry and won the absolute admiration of every single scholar in the room."
A look of profound disappointment washed over Lin Jue's face. He glanced back down at the poetry book on his desk. He had stared at it for an hour, but the cryptic words just gave him a headache. He couldn't muster a shred of interest in it. 
"I seriously don't get it," he grumbled, tossing the book aside. "What is the point of liking this garbage? You can't eat it, and you can't drink it. Whatever. Just go. And tell your miss that getting a divorce from me won't be that easy."
Kuqiao bowed and stepped out. Once she was safely outside his door, she muttered under her breath, "My Miss is a force to be reckoned with now! She won't be bullied by you!"
Upon returning to the Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao relayed everything Lin Jue had said, word for word.
Qianli Xun locked onto a single sentence: *'getting a divorce won't be that easy.'*
She gazed at her horrific-looking reflection in the bronze mirror and smiled a cold, resolute smile. She had laid all the groundwork. The trap was set. Tomorrow's ultimate showdown was now entirely in the hands of fate.
