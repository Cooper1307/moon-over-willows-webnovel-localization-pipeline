# Chapter 21: Questioned After the Poetry Gathering

On the way back, Qianli Xun felt satisfied with today's performance. She could tell that Zhu Shuzhen's four friends thought she was different from before, but they didn't believe she wasn't the old Zhu Shuzhen. It must have been when she appraised Li Qingzhao's poem that she displayed talent worthy of Zhu Shuzhen. Thinking of this, a smile appeared on Qianli Xun's face.

Kubiao saw her miss was happy and said, "You look in a good mood, Miss."

"Of course." Qianli Xun suddenly wanted to know how Zhu Shuzhen usually behaved with her friends. "Kubiao, when I met the three young masters and Lady Miao before, was I talkative? Or was I still acting aloof?"

Kubiao smiled. "You only acted aloof with people who didn't understand poetry. You didn't talk much with family either. Only when you met Young Master Dongfang and the others would you beam with joy and speak freely."

"Oh, so that's the kind of person I was. No wonder they didn't notice anything different about me today."

"How could they not notice? You jumped from the second floor to the first floor to punch Young Master Chen. Young Master Dongfang and the others were so shocked their eyes nearly popped out. But fortunately, they only think you've been hiding your talents."

"Meeting friends today, I suddenly realized something—I am me. There's no need to try to be like the old Zhu Shuzhen. I should just be myself and show everyone my true side."

Qianli Xun decided that from now on, she would stop trying to pretend to be the old Zhu Shuzhen. She would let the old Zhu Shuzhen become part of who she was now.

When the carriage reached the Lin residence gate, Qianli Xun remembered the Fuji Ointment. It was time to apply it. She hurried back to the Zhulan Residence.

She had just entered the west courtyard when Lin Jue blocked her path.

"Where has Wife been?"

The bruise around Lin Jue's left eye had disappeared. Qianli Xun was secretly pleased. "The medicine from Jixiang Pharmacy really works."

Lin Jue continued to press. "Why aren't you speaking?"

Qianli Xun answered casually. "I went to a poetry gathering and met with some friends."

"Hmph! I bet you used the poetry gathering as an excuse to do shameful things with those so-called talents." Lin Jue's tone was full of resentment.

"Lin Jue! You filthy mouth can't utter decent words, always thinking dirty things. This queen has no time for you!" Qianli Xun was angered and planned to return to the Zhulan Residence, turning to the side.

Lin Jue quickly rushed forward, blocking her path. "I warned you not to go to the poetry gathering. Why didn't you listen?"

Qianli Xun was very impatient. "Lin Jue, you big man, acting like an old woman! We're getting a divorce anyway. Why do you care so much? Besides, even if we weren't divorced, you couldn't control me. I can do whatever I want!"

Lin Jue lost face and raised his hand to strike Zhu Shuzhen.

Qianli Xun blocked it with a punch. "What? You want to beat me again? Think you can take me on? You don't even know what you're up against."

Lin Jue was blocked by a force. He knew he couldn't beat this different wife at all. He had to lower his hand and try to soften his tone.

"Once when I was training soldiers, during their rest, I overheard them whispering about you. They said you and Dongfang Chenze had something going on. That you and Qiao Wenxian were flirting. They said... well, it was ugly. I'm your husband. A man. Hearing outsiders gossip about you like that—how could I not be angry? I warned you not to go to Dongfang Chenze's poetry gatherings. You behaved for a while, but today you went again. Do you want the whole city of Lin'an to laugh at me?"

Seeing Lin Jue's attitude soften, Qianli Xun was no longer angry. She didn't want to make things too tense—tomorrow's divorce was what mattered. She said earnestly, "Your biggest problem is you lack the most basic trust in your wife. When you remarry, don't be like this again. To prove my innocence, next time they invite me, I'll take you along. You can see for yourself what we do. But by then, we'll probably be divorced. Doesn't matter. Even after divorce, we can still be friends. I'll take you as a friend."

Lin Jue was immediately stunned. Before, when encountering such things, Zhu Shuzhen never made any explanations, let alone was willing to take him to poetry gatherings. More precisely, she looked down on him from the heart, just because he was a martial artist who didn't understand poetry and songs, let alone romance. But the Zhu Shuzhen before him now took the initiative to ask him to go to the poetry gathering. If she had been like this earlier, perhaps their relationship would have been different.

"I have no desire to go to such occasions. That group of people around Qiao Wenxian are all sour and stinky. They really don't get along with me."

Qianli Xun looked Lin Jue up and down. "From the first time I saw you, I felt you had good bone structure. How about after the divorce, you take me as your master and I'll teach you Qianli Fist?"

This made Lin Jue even more stunned, and it made him more certain that the old Zhu Shuzhen had been beaten to death by domestic violence. Lin Jue suddenly felt afraid and involuntarily took two steps back, turned around, and left with heavy steps.

Qianli Xun watched Lin Jue's back, feeling strange. "Hey! Why are you leaving? Don't you want to be my disciple?"

Lin Jue didn't answer. He left the courtyard and disappeared from Qianli Xun's sight.

Qianli Xun said to herself, "This person is really strange."

Kubiao said, "It's you who are strange, Miss. Wanting to take the young master as a disciple. Even I find it astonishing after hearing it, let alone the young master."

That day, the name she heard most was Qiao Wenxian. What exactly was his relationship with Zhu Shuzhen? Qianli Xun decided to find out.

"Kubiao, follow me back to the Zhulan Residence. Your miss has something to ask you."

Kubiao was very obedient and followed her miss back.

When they reached the Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun changed into casual clothes and began to inquire. "I keep hearing people mention Qiao Wenxian. Who exactly is he?"

Kubiao put away the clothes her miss had changed out of and said, "He's a young master from the Qiao family. He was born to a concubine of Lord Qiao. Because he's the only son, he'll inherit the Qiao family business in the future, so he was raised by the main wife. He has four older sisters above him. The eldest sister entered the palace and became Consort Qiao. Unfortunately, she had bad luck and died after giving birth to a son."

"How do you know so clearly?" Qianli Xun was curious. How could Kubiao, a maid, know so much about the Qiao family?

Kubiao explained, "The Qiao family's affairs are no longer a secret in Lin'an. Many people know. It's just because a Consort Qiao came from their family that everyone paid more attention and talked more about it. I only learned about it from chatting with other maids at the Zhu residence."

"Is Qiao Wenxian often invited by Young Master Dongfang to poetry gatherings?"

"That's right, but he never writes poems or ci. He only likes appreciating your poems and ci, Miss. And he's the best person at appreciating your poetry. That's why you consider him a soulmate."

"Soulmate? Does that mean I liked him?"

"At that time, your heart was filled with Young Master Xu. There was no room for anyone else."

Qianli Xun sighed. "It's all your parents' fault for not telling you the truth earlier, letting your miss sink so deep into it. If it were now, even if Young Master Xu threw himself at me, I might not even look at him. I really doubt your miss's taste from before. How could she fall for someone like Young Master Xu?"

"Actually, Young Master Xu isn't a bad person. He's both handsome and talented, shares common interests with you, and you grew up together. Naturally, you liked him. It's just that he listened to his parents too much. To put it nicely, he's filial. But it's just too unfair to you."

"Let's not talk about him. He's already in the past. Let's think about tomorrow's divorce instead. Bring me the Fuji Ointment."

Kubiao fetched the Fuji Ointment. "I really don't understand your thinking, Miss. You have to hurt yourself."

Qianli Xun took it, walked to the bronze mirror, and sat down. She poured out some ointment and applied it to the healing wound. "Tomorrow you'll know. Go find Lin Jue and get the bruise medicine back."

"Yes, Miss."

Kubiao left the Zhulan Residence and came to the outside of Lin Jue's room. She carefully said inside, "Young master, Miss asked me to get the bruise medicine."

After a while, a servant came out. "The young master wants you to go in."

Kubiao walked in and found the young master sitting at the desk, holding a book to read. She was puzzled. "Why is the young master reading?"

Lin Jue saw Kubiao come in and placed the book face down on the desk. Kubiao clearly saw the five characters "Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng." She had followed her miss since she was young. Not only did she recognize a few characters, but she also knew that Wang Youcheng (Wang Wei) was a famous Tang Dynasty poet. [TN: Wang Wei, a renowned Tang Dynasty poet, was also known by his official title Wang Youcheng.] Her miss loved his poems very much. She didn't expect the young master to actually read his poetry.

"The medicine bottle is on the table. Take it yourself."

Kubiao went to a nearby table and took the medicine bottle. After bowing, she was about to leave when the young master stopped her with words. "Wait a moment. I have something to ask you."

Kubiao stood still. "Please ask, Young Master."

"Your miss is different from before. Doesn't the Zhu family resent me?"

Kubiao thought to herself, *the young master still has the nerve to ask! Watch how I'll retort you.* "How could they not resent you? It's just that the story of rebirth is too bizarre to be believable. Besides, although your miss's temperament has changed greatly now, she looks exactly the same as before. Even if they go to the authorities to accuse you of beating my miss to death, they won't find any evidence. The Zhu family can only swallow the bitter pill."

Lin Jue was still somewhat afraid in his heart. After all, his father-in-law was the Prefect of Lin'an. Fortunately, he had done his homework in advance. Before Zhu Shuzhen, he visited his father-in-law, knelt down and tearfully recounted his experiences after marriage. He said he beat Zhu Shuzhen because she disregarded her husband's face and flirted with Lin'an talents outside. Because he heard others gossiping many times, he had no choice but to strike her. As for finding a merchant courtesan, it was because Zhu Shuzhen refused to consummate the marriage with him. In short, because of his tearful account and reasonable explanation, his father-in-law didn't blame him.

"Do you like your miss's appearance now, or before?"

Kubiao was unwilling to admit it in front of the young master. "As long as it's my miss, I like her whether it's the past or now."

"But I like her more now. She knows martial arts and is somewhat of a kindred spirit with me. The key is she still talks to me, not as cold as before. Does she still like those pretentious cultural things now?"

Kubiao said in a dissatisfied tone. "I'm sorry to disappoint you, Young Master. Miss's talent in poetry and ci is still there. Today at the poetry gathering, she appraised Yi'an Jushi's poem and received everyone's praise."

Lin Jue showed a disappointed expression. He looked at the poetry collection on the table. He had studied it for half a day but couldn't figure anything out, and he couldn't muster any interest at all. "I really don't understand. What's the use of liking this stuff? You can't eat it or drink it. You can go now. Tell your miss that divorce isn't that easy."

Kubiao withdrew. Outside the door, she quietly retorted to the room. "My miss is very powerful now! She's not easy to bully!"

Returning to the Zhulan Residence, Kubiao told her miss everything Lin Jue had said word for word.

Qianli Xun only focused on what Lin Jue said: "Divorce isn't that easy." She thought to herself that she had done everything she could. Tomorrow's result could only be left to heaven.
