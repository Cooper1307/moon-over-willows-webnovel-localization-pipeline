第21章 诗会之后被质问
## Chapter 21: Questioned After the Poetry Salon

回去的路上，千里寻为今日的表现感到满意。看得出，朱淑真的四位朋友虽觉得她与以往不同，可并未认为她不是以前的朱淑真。肯定是品鉴李清照的诗时，她展现出能够与朱淑真相媲美的才华。想到这些，千里寻的脸上浮现出笑容。
Riding home, Qianli Xun felt pleased with how today went. Zhu Shuzhen's four friends saw differences, sure, yet none suspected she wasn't the real Zhu Shuzhen—credit to her Li Qingzhao critique living up to the poet's fame. A satisfied smile tugged at her lips.

苦荞见小姐开心，说道：“小姐看上去心情不错。”
Noticing her bright mood, Kuqiao remarked, "You look real happy, Miss."

“那是当然。”千里寻突然想知道朱淑真与朋友在一起时到底如何表现，“苦荞，我以前见到三位公子和苗姑娘时爱不爱说话？该不会还是高冷的样子？”
"Course I am!" Curious how the real Zhu Shuzhen behaved among peers, Qianli Xun asked, "Kuqiao, did I talk much around those three gentlemen and Lady Miao before? Or did I play the ice queen there too?"

苦荞笑道：“您对不懂诗词的人才会表现出高冷，在家人面前话也不多，只有见到东方公子他们才会春风满面，侃侃而谈。”
Kuqiao chuckled. "You only acted frosty around folks who didn't understand poetry—and stayed quiet around family. Around Young Master Dongfang and the others, you beamed and talked nonstop!"

“哦，原来我是这样的人，难怪今日他们不觉得我有什么不同。”
"Aha—so that's how I was! No wonder they didn't bat an eye."

“怎么不觉得？您从二楼跳到一楼打陈公子，东方公子他们惊得个个眼睛瞪得老大。不过还好，他们只觉得小姐您深藏不露而已。”
"Didn't bat an eye? When you jumped off the second floor and beat up Young Master Chen, their eyes popped out of their skulls! Lucky they just figured you were hiding your talents."

“今日见见朋友，我突然明白一个道理，我就是我，没有必要想着还像以前一样，该怎样就怎样，把我自己最真实的一面展现在大家面前。”
"Meeting friends today taught me one thing: I am who I am. Done bending over backward pretending to be the old Zhu Shuzhen—from now on I show the world the real me!"

千里寻决定，以后再也不去想如何将自己伪装成以前的朱淑真，她要让以前的朱淑真成为现在的自己。
She made up her mind: stop squeezing herself into Zhu Shuzhen's mold, and let Zhu Shuzhen become Qianli Xun instead.

马车到达林府门口，千里寻想起腐肌膏，是该涂抹的时候，还是尽快回竹兰居。
Pulling up outside Lin Manor, Qianli Xun remembered the Corrosive Paste—time to apply it back at Zhulan Residence.

刚走进西院，被林觉堵住。
The moment she stepped into the West Courtyard, Lin Jue blocked her path.

“娘子这是去了哪里？”
"Where have you been, Wife?"

林觉左眼周围的淤青已消失，千里寻心里暗喜：“从吉祥药房买的药真不错。”
The bruise around his left eye had vanished clean. Qianli Xun smiled inside: That Jixiang Pharmacy salve really delivers!

林觉继续追问：“怎么不说话？”
"Why aren't you answering?" Lin Jue pressed.

千里寻轻松地答道：“我去参加诗会，与几位朋友聚聚。”
"Went to a poetry salon to catch up with friends," she replied breezy as air.

“哼！恐怕是以参加诗会为由，与所谓的才子行苟且之事吧？”林觉不满中带着些许恨意。
"Hmph! Probably using 'poetry salon' as a cover to carry on illicit affairs with those fancy scholars!" Bitter resentment edged his voice.

“林觉！你狗嘴里吐不出象牙，尽想些龌龊之事，老娘懒得理你！”千里寻被气到，打算回竹兰居，向一旁拐去。
"Lin Jue! Nothing decent ever comes out of your filthy mouth—your head is full of gutter trash! Not wasting breath on you!" Fuming, she sidestepped toward Zhulan Residence.

林觉快速冲上前，挡住她的去路：“我都警告过你，不要去参加诗会，为何不听？”
Lin Jue darted ahead to bar her way. "I already warned you to stay away from poetry salons—why didn't you listen?"

千里寻很不耐烦：“林觉，你个大男人，婆婆妈妈的！我们都要和离，管那么多干嘛？再说即使没离，你也管不着我，我想干什么就干什么！”
Exasperation flared. "Lin Jue, you're a grown man—quit nagging like an old busybody! We're getting divorced anyway, why do you care? Even if we weren't, you don't own me—I go where I please!"

林觉很没面子，伸出手向朱淑真打去。
Stung in his pride, Lin Jue swung a hand to strike her.

被千里寻一拳挡住：“怎么？还想家暴我？也不掂量掂量你有几斤几两，就想在我面前动手？”
Qianli Xun caught his blow cold with one fist. "What, trying domestic violence again? Didn't even weigh yourself before swinging at me?"

林觉被一股力量挡着，深知自己根本打不过眼前这个不一样的娘子，只好放下手，语气尽量放缓和。
Stopped dead by her force, knowing full well he stood no chance against this reborn wife, Lin Jue lowered his hand and softened his tone.

“有一次我练兵，士兵休息时，无意中听到他们悄悄议论你，说你跟东方尘泽不清不楚，还跟乔文仙眉来眼去，还说你们……，反正很难听，我是你夫君，是个男人，听到外面人如此议论你，能不生气吗？后来警告你不要去参加东方尘泽组织的诗会，你倒是老实一回，今日又跑去，难道想让整个临安城的人都看我笑话？”
"Once during a drill break, I overheard my soldiers whispering about you—saying you and Dongfang Chenze had something going on, that you and Qiao Wenxian exchanged flirtatious glances, saying all kinds of ugly things! I'm your husband, a man—how could I not lose my temper hearing folks talk like that? After I warned you off Dongfang Chenze's salons, you stayed home once, and now you go right back! Trying to make me the laughingstock of all Lin'an?"

千里寻见林觉态度缓和下来，也不再生气，她不想把关系闹得太僵，省得影响明日和离之事，语重心长地说：“你这人啊，最大的问题就是对自己的娘子缺乏最基本的信任，以后娶了妻，可不能再这样。为证明我以前的清白，等下次他们再邀请我时带你一起去，让你亲眼目睹我们都做些什么。不过，估计那时你我已经和离，没关系，婚离了，还是朋友，我以朋友的名义带你去。”
Seeing him cool down, Qianli Xun let her anger ebb too—no point souring things right before tomorrow's divorce. Speaking earnest and plain, she said, "Your biggest problem is having zero basic trust in your own wife. When you remarry, drop that habit. Just to clear my old name, next time they invite me I'll bring you along so you see with your own eyes what we actually do. Granted, by then we'll be divorced—no big deal, we can still be friends, and I'll bring you along as a friend."

林觉立刻傻眼。以前遇到此类事情，朱淑真从不做任何解释，更不愿意带他去参加诗会，更确切地说，她从心里瞧不起他，只因他是个武夫，不懂诗词歌赋，更不懂花前月下。而眼前的朱淑真却主动要求带他去诗会，如果她早如此，也许他们之间的关系会有所不同。
Lin Jue stood stunned. Whenever this came up before, Zhu Shuzhen never offered one word of explanation, never invited him along—down deep she despised him as an uncultured brute who knew nothing of verse or romance. Now she offered to take him herself! Had she acted like this from day one, might everything have turned out different?

“我才不稀罕去那种场合，乔文仙那帮人又酸又臭，跟我实在合不来。”
"Who wants to sit through that boring stuff? Qiao Wenxian and his crowd are pompous snobs—we'd never mix."

千里寻将林觉上下瞧瞧：“从我第一眼见你，就觉得你根骨不错，不如和离后，你拜我为师，我教你千里拳。”
Sizing him up from head to toe, Qianli Xun remarked, "First time I saw you, I noticed you've got solid bones for martial arts. Tell you what—after we divorce, become my apprentice and I'll teach you Qianli Fist!"

这话更让林觉傻眼，也让他更加确定以前的朱淑真已被家暴而亡。林觉突然感到害怕，不由后退两步，转过身，迈着沉重的步伐离开。
That froze Lin Jue colder still—clinching his dread that the old Zhu Shuzhen really had died by his hand. A chill crawled up his spine; taking two steps back, he spun around and stalked off with heavy strides.

千里寻看着林觉的背影，感到奇怪：“喂！怎么走了？难道不愿意当我的徒弟？”
Watching him leave, Qianli Xun blinked. "Hey! Where you going? Don't want to be my apprentice?"

林觉没有回答，出了院子，消失在千里寻面前。
Without answering, Lin Jue cleared the yard and vanished.

千里寻自语道：“这人真奇怪。”
"Weird guy," Qianli Xun muttered.

苦荞说：“是小姐奇怪才对，要收姑爷为徒弟，我听后都觉得诧异，更别说姑爷。”
"You're the weird one, Miss," Kuqiao piped up. "Offering to take Son-in-law as your apprentice—even my jaw dropped, let alone his!"

这一日，听到最多的名字就是乔文仙，他到底与朱淑真什么关系？千里寻决定搞搞清楚。
One name had echoed all day: Qiao Wenxian. What exactly was his tie to Zhu Shuzhen? Time to find out.

“苦荞，随我回竹兰居，你家小姐我有事要问你。”
"Back to Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao—got questions for you."

苦荞很听话，跟着小姐往回走。
Kuqiao trailed obedient behind her.

到了竹兰居，千里寻换了居家的衣裳后，开始打听：“老听人提到乔文仙，他到底是谁？”
Inside, after changing into lounge clothes, Qianli Xun asked straight out, "Everyone keeps mentioning Qiao Wenxian. Who is he?"

苦荞把小姐换下的衣裳放好后说：“他是乔家公子，是乔老爷纳的妾室所生，因是独苗，未来要继承乔家产业，所以养在夫人处，上面四个姐姐，大姐进了宫，成为乔贵妃，可惜命不好，生下儿子后就殁了。”
Folding the outer robes neatly, Kuqiao answered, "Young Master of the Qiao family—born to Master Qiao's concubine, yet as the sole male heir slated to carry on the Qiao estate, he was raised by Madam Qiao. He has four older sisters; the eldest entered the palace and became Consort Qiao—pity her fate was short, she passed away right after giving birth to a son."

“你怎么知道得这么清楚？”千里寻好奇，苦荞一个丫鬟怎么对乔家如此了解。
"How do you know all that?" Qianli Xun wondered how a maid had the full scoop.

苦荞解释道：“乔家的事在临安已不是秘密，很多人都知道，只因他家出了一位乔贵妃，大家关注得多，议论得多而已，我也是在朱府跟其他丫鬟聊天儿时才知道。”
"Nothing secret about Qiao family business in Lin'an—everyone knows because they produced Consort Qiao, so folks talk about them nonstop. I heard it chatting with other maids back at Zhu Manor."

“乔文仙是不是经常被东方公子邀请参加诗会？”
"Does Young Master Dongfang invite Qiao Wenxian to salons often?"

“是这样，不过他从来不做诗写词，只喜欢品鉴小姐您的诗词，而且是品鉴小姐诗词的绝佳之人，所以小姐视他为知己。”
"All the time! Though he never writes poems himself—just loves critiquing yours, and nobody reads your verse deeper than him, so you considered him a true confidant."

“知己？难道我喜欢他？”
"Confidant? Did I love him?"

“小姐那时心里装着徐公子，容不下他人。”
"Back then your heart belonged to Young Master Xu—nobody else could fit."

千里寻叹道：“都怪爹娘不早将实情说明，让你家小姐深陷其中，要搁现在，徐公子倒贴，我都不一定能看得上。真怀疑你家小姐以前的眼光，怎么能看上徐公子那样的人？”
Qianli Xun sighed. "Blame Mother and Father for hiding the truth and letting you pine for nothing! Put Young Master Xu in front of me today, I wouldn't take him for free! Wonder what your old self saw in him."

“其实，徐公子人不错，既长得风流倜傥，又才华横溢，与小姐有共同语言，加之青梅竹马，小姐自然喜欢，只是他太听从父母的话，往好了来讲是孝顺，就是对小姐太不公。”
"Young Master Xu wasn't bad, really—handsome, brilliant, spoke your language, plus childhood sweethearts. Only flaw was obeying his parents blindly—filial to them, cruel to you."

“不谈他了，他已经是过去式。还是想想明日和离之事，给我把腐肌膏拿来。”
"Enough about him—ancient history! Time to focus on tomorrow's divorce. Hand me the Corrosive Paste."

苦荞取来腐肌膏：“真不知道小姐是怎么想的，非要伤害自己。”
Fetching the vial, Kuqiao frowned. "Still can't fathom why you're hurting yourself on purpose, Miss."

千里寻接过，走到铜镜前坐下，倒出一些药膏涂在正在愈合的伤口上：“明天你就知道，去找林觉把治淤青的药拿回来。”
Taking a seat at the bronze mirror, Qianli Xun tipped salve onto her healing cut. "You'll see tomorrow. Now go fetch the bruise ointment back from Lin Jue."

“是，小姐。”
"Yes, Miss."

苦荞出了竹兰居，来到林觉住的屋外，小心向里面说：“姑爷，小姐让我来拿治淤青的药。”
Outside Lin Jue's quarters, Kuqiao called in politely: "Son-in-law, Miss sent me to retrieve the bruise medicine."

过了一会儿，出来一名下人：“主君让你进去。”
Moments later a servant stepped out. "Master says come inside."

苦荞走进去，发现姑爷坐在桌前，拿着一本书看，她纳闷：“姑爷怎么看起书来？”
Stepping in, Kuqiao saw Lin Jue sitting at the table reading a book—Wait, Son-in-law reading?!

林觉见苦荞进来，把书扣在桌上。苦荞清晰地看到“王右丞诗集”五个字，她从小跟着小姐，不仅认得几个字，而且知道王右丞是唐代著名诗人，他的诗小姐甚是喜爱，没想到姑爷竟然读他的诗。
Spotting her, Lin Jue flipped the book facedown. Growing up beside Zhu Shuzhen, Kuqiao recognized characters well enough to read "Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng" on the cover—her mistress's beloved Tang poet! Never dreamed Son-in-law would crack it open!

“药瓶在桌上，自己拿。”
"Vial's on the side table. Grab it."

苦荞到一旁的桌上拿了药瓶，行完礼，要离开，被姑爷用话语拦住：“你等会儿，我有事问你。”
Retrieving the bottle, Kuqiao bowed to leave, only for Lin Jue to halt her: "Hold on—a question."

苦荞站住：“姑爷请问。”
She paused. "Yes, Son-in-law?"

“你家小姐跟以前不一样，难道朱家人不怨我？”
"With your Miss acting nothing like before, doesn't the Zhu family resent me?"

苦荞心想，姑爷还有脸问！看我怎么怼你：“怎能不怨？只不过重生之说太过离奇，让人难以置信，再说现在的小姐性情虽大变，可长得跟以前一模一样，即使到官府告姑爷打死我家小姐，也找不到证据，朱家也只能吃哑巴亏。”
Kuqiao thought, The nerve of him asking! Watch me jab back: "Of course they resent you! Only tales of rebirth sound too wild for folks to believe, and since Miss still wears the exact same face despite changing inside, even if they dragged you to the yamen for beating her to death, there's zero proof—so the Zhus have to swallow it in silence."

林觉心里还是有些害怕，毕竟岳父大人是临安知府。还好他已提前做好功课，在朱淑真之前拜访了岳父大人，跪着哭诉婚后的经历，说自己打朱淑真是因为她不顾丈夫颜面，在外与临安才子勾勾搭搭，因多次听到他人议论，才不得已出手打她。至于找商妓，是因为朱淑真不愿与他圆房所致。总之，岳父大人因他哭诉，又说得在理，并未怪罪。
Unease pricked Lin Jue—his father-in-law was Prefect of Lin'an, after all. Lucky he had moved first, visiting Zhu Xiyan ahead of Shuzhen to kneel and weep out his side: claiming he only hit her because she flirted with Lin'an scholars and dragged his name through barracks gossip, and only saw courtesans because she refused his bed. Convinced by the tears and excuses, Prefect Zhu hadn't blamed him.

“你喜欢你家小姐现在的样子，还是从前？”
"Do you like your Miss better now, or how she was before?"

苦荞根本不愿在姑爷面前承认：“只要是我家小姐，不管过去还是现在我都喜欢。”
Refusing to hand him an inch, Kuqiao snapped, "Past or present, she's my Miss—I love her either way."

“可我更喜欢现在的她。又会功夫，跟我算是同道中人，关键是还跟我讲话，不像以前那么冷漠。现在的她可喜欢那些附庸风雅的玩意儿？”
"Well, I like her better now. Knows martial arts like one of my own kind, and actually talks to me instead of freezing me out. Does she still care for those fancy word games?"

苦荞用不满的语气说：“令姑爷失望，小姐的诗词才华依然还在，今日在诗会上品评易安居士的诗，得到所有人赞赏。”
Kuqiao answered with sharp relish: "Hate to disappoint you, Son-in-law—Miss's poetic gift hasn't faded a bit! At today's salon, her reading of Yi'an Jushi's poem won raves from everyone!"

林觉露出失落的表情，瞧了瞧放在桌上的诗集，他研究半天，研究不出所以然，也提不起一点儿兴致：“真想不通，喜欢这玩意儿有什么用？既不能吃又不能喝。你走吧，告诉你家小姐，和离没那么容易。”
Disappointment crossed Lin Jue's face as he glanced down at the poetry book on his desk; staring at it half the night hadn't made heads or tails of it or sparked an ounce of interest. "Don't see the point of that stuff—can't eat it, can't drink it. Go on, and tell your Miss: mutual divorce won't come easy."

苦荞退出，在门外小声对着屋内怼道：“我家小姐现在厉害着呢！不好欺负！”
Stepping outside, Kuqiao muttered back toward the closed door: "My Miss is tough now—nobody pushes her around!"

回到竹兰居，苦荞把林觉说的话原原本本告诉小姐。
Back at Zhulan Residence, she relayed every word to Qianli Xun.

﻿千里寻只关注林觉说的“和离没那么容易”，心想，自己该做的都做了，明日的结果只能交给老天。
Qianli Xun zeroed in on one line—"mutual divorce won't come easy." She had done everything she could; tomorrow was up to Heaven.
