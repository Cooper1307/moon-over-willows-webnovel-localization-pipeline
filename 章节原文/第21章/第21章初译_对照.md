# Chapter 21: Take Me as Your Master

---

回去的路上，千里寻为今日的表现感到满意。看得出，朱淑真的四位朋友虽觉得她与以往不同，可并未认为她不是以前的朱淑真。肯定是品鉴李清照的诗时，她展现出能够与朱淑真相媲美的才华。想到这些，千里寻的脸上浮现出笑容。

On the way back, Qianli Xun felt satisfied with today's performance. She could tell that Zhu Shuzhen's four friends thought she was different from before. But they didn't suspect for a second that she was an entirely different person. It must have been when she appraised Li Qingzhao's poem that she displayed talent worthy of Zhu Shuzhen. Thinking of this, a smile appeared on Qianli Xun's face.

---

苦荞见小姐开心，说道：“小姐看上去心情不错。”

Seeing her miss in such high spirits, Kuqiao remarked, "You seem to be in a wonderful mood, Miss."

---

“那是当然。”千里寻突然想知道朱淑真与朋友在一起时到底如何表现，“苦荞，我以前见到三位公子和苗姑娘时爱不爱说话？该不会还是高冷的样子？”

"Of course." Qianli Xun suddenly wanted to know how Zhu Shuzhen usually behaved with her friends. "Kuqiao, when I met the three young masters and Lady Miao before, was I talkative? Or was I still acting distant?"

---

苦荞笑道：“您对不懂诗词的人才会表现出高冷，在家人面前话也不多，只有见到东方公子他们才会春风满面，侃侃而谈。”

Kuqiao smiled. "You only acted aloof with people who didn't understand poetry. You didn't talk much with family either. Only when you met Young Master Dongfang and the others would you beam with joy and speak freely."

---

“哦，原来我是这样的人，难怪今日他们不觉得我有什么不同。”

"Oh, so that's the kind of person I was. No wonder they didn't notice anything different about me today."

---

“怎么不觉得？您从二楼跳到一楼打陈公子，东方公子他们惊得个个眼睛瞪得老大。不过还好，他们只觉得小姐您深藏不露而已。”

"How could they not notice? You leaped straight off the second-floor balcony to punch Young Master Chen. Young Master Dongfang and the others were so shocked their eyes nearly popped out. But fortunately, they only think you've been hiding your talents."

---

“今日见见朋友，我突然明白一个道理，我就是我，没有必要想着还像以前一样，该怎样就怎样，把我自己最真实的一面展现在大家面前。”

“Meeting friends today, I suddenly realized something—I am me. There's no need to try to be like the former Zhu Shuzhen. I should just be myself and show everyone my true side.”

---

千里寻决定，以后再也不去想如何将自己伪装成以前的朱淑真，她要让以前的朱淑真成为现在的自己。

Qianli Xun decided that from now on, she would stop trying to pretend to be the Zhu Shuzhen of before. She would let the Zhu Shuzhen of before become part of who she was now.

---

马车到达林府门口，千里寻想起腐肌膏，是该涂抹的时候，还是尽快回竹兰居。

When the carriage reached the Lin residence gate, Qianli Xun remembered the Corrosive Paste. It was time to apply it. She hurried back to the Zhulan Residence.

---

刚走进西院，被林觉堵住。

She had just entered the west courtyard when Lin Jue blocked her path.

---

“娘子这是去了哪里？”

"Where have you been, wife?"

---

林觉左眼周围的淤青已消失，千里寻心里暗喜：“从吉祥药房买的药真不错。”

The bruise around Lin Jue's left eye had disappeared. Qianli Xun was secretly pleased. *The medicine from Jixiang Pharmacy really works.*

---

林觉继续追问：“怎么不说话？”

Lin Jue continued to press. "Why aren't you speaking?"

---

千里寻轻松地答道：“我去参加诗会，与几位朋友聚聚。”

Qianli Xun answered casually. "I went to a poetry gathering and met with some friends."

---

“哼！恐怕是以参加诗会为由，与所谓的才子行苟且之事吧？”林觉不满中带着些许恨意。

"Hmph! I bet you used the poetry gathering as an excuse to do shameful things with those so-called talents." Lin Jue's tone was full of resentment.

---

“林觉！你狗嘴里吐不出象牙，尽想些龌龊之事，老娘懒得理你！”千里寻被气到，打算回竹兰居，向一旁拐去。

"Lin Jue! Nothing but pure filth comes out of that mouth of yours! Your mind is entirely in the gutter, and this queen has no time for you!" Qianli Xun was angered and planned to return to the Zhulan Residence, turning to the side.

---

林觉快速冲上前，挡住她的去路：“我都警告过你，不要去参加诗会，为何不听？”

Lin Jue quickly rushed forward, blocking her path. "I warned you not to go to the poetry gathering. Why didn't you listen?"

---

千里寻很不耐烦：“林觉，你个大男人，婆婆妈妈的！我们都要和离，管那么多干嘛？再说即使没离，你也管不着我，我想干什么就干什么！”

Qianli Xun was very impatient. "Lin Jue, you're a grown man, stop nagging like an old hen! We're getting a divorce anyway. Why do you care so much? Besides, even if we weren't divorced, you couldn't control me. I can do whatever I want!"

---

林觉很没面子，伸出手向朱淑真打去。

Lin Jue lost face and raised his hand to strike Zhu Shuzhen.

---

被千里寻一拳挡住：“怎么？还想家暴我？也不掂量掂量你有几斤几两，就想在我面前动手？”

Qianli Xun blocked it with a punch. "What? Want to try your hand at domestic violence again? You'd better weigh your own strength before raising a hand against me."

---

林觉被一股力量挡着，深知自己根本打不过眼前这个不一样的娘子，只好放下手，语气尽量放缓和。

Lin Jue was blocked by a force. He knew he couldn't beat this changed wife at all. He had to lower his hand and try to soften his tone.

---

“有一次我练兵，士兵休息时，无意中听到他们悄悄议论你，说你跟东方尘泽不清不楚，还跟乔文仙眉来眼去，还说你们……，反正很难听，我是你夫君，是个男人，听到外面人如此议论你，能不生气吗？后来警告你不要去参加东方尘泽组织的诗会，你倒是老实一回，今日又跑去，难道想让整个临安城的人都看我笑话？”

“Once when I was training soldiers, during their rest, I overheard them whispering about you. They said you and Dongfang Chenze had something going on. That you and Qiao Wenxian were flirting. They said... well, it was ugly. I'm your husband. A man. Hearing outsiders gossip about you like that—how could I not be angry? I warned you not to go to Dongfang Chenze's poetry gatherings. You behaved for a while, but today you went again. Do you want the whole city of Lin'an to laugh at me？”

---

千里寻见林觉态度缓和下来，也不再生气，她不想把关系闹得太僵，省得影响明日和离之事，语重心长地说：“你这人啊，最大的问题就是对自己的娘子缺乏最基本的信任，以后娶了妻，可不能再这样。为证明我以前的清白，等下次他们再邀请我时带你一起去，让你亲眼目睹我们都做些什么。不过，估计那时你我已经和离，没关系，婚离了，还是朋友，我以朋友的名义带你去。”

Seeing Lin Jue's attitude soften, Qianli Xun was no longer angry. She didn't want to make things too tense—tomorrow's divorce was what mattered. She said earnestly, “Your biggest problem is you lack the most basic trust in your wife. When you remarry, don't be like this again. To prove my innocence, next time they invite me, I'll take you along. You can see for yourself what we do. But by then, we'll probably be divorced. Doesn't matter. Even after divorce, we can still be friends. I'll take you as a friend.”

---

林觉立刻傻眼。以前遇到此类事情，朱淑真从不做任何解释，更不愿意带他去参加诗会，更确切地说，她从心里瞧不起他，只因他是个武夫，不懂诗词歌赋，更不懂花前月下。而眼前的朱淑真却主动要求带他去诗会，如果她早如此，也许他们之间的关系会有所不同。

Lin Jue was completely dumbfounded. Before, when encountering such things, Zhu Shuzhen never made any explanations, let alone was willing to take him to poetry gatherings. More precisely, she had always looked down on him from the bottom of her heart. He was just a martial artist who didn't understand poetry and songs, let alone romance. But the Zhu Shuzhen before him now took the initiative to ask him to go to the poetry gathering. If she had been like this earlier, perhaps their relationship would have been different.

---

“我才不稀罕去那种场合，乔文仙那帮人又酸又臭，跟我实在合不来。”

"I have no desire to go to such occasions. That group of people around Qiao Wenxian are all pretentious windbags. They really don't get along with me."

---

千里寻将林觉上下瞧瞧：“从我第一眼见你，就觉得你根骨不错，不如和离后，你拜我为师，我教你千里拳。”

Qianli Xun looked Lin Jue up and down. "From the first time I saw you, I could tell you have an excellent frame for martial arts. How about after the divorce, you take me as your master and I'll teach you Qianli Fist?"

---

这话更让林觉傻眼，也让他更加确定以前的朱淑真已被家暴而亡。林觉突然感到害怕，不由后退两步，转过身，迈着沉重的步伐离开。

This made Lin Jue even more stunned, and it made him more certain that the original Zhu Shuzhen had been beaten to death by domestic violence. Lin Jue suddenly felt afraid and instinctively took two steps back, turned around, and left with heavy steps.

---

千里寻看着林觉的背影，感到奇怪：“喂！怎么走了？难道不愿意当我的徒弟？”

Qianli Xun watched Lin Jue's back, feeling strange. "Hey! Why are you leaving? Don't you want to be my disciple?"

---

林觉没有回答，出了院子，消失在千里寻面前。

Lin Jue didn't answer. He left the courtyard and disappeared from Qianli Xun's sight.

---

千里寻自语道：“这人真奇怪。”

Qianli Xun said to herself, "This person is really strange."

---

苦荞说：“是小姐奇怪才对，要收姑爷为徒弟，我听后都觉得诧异，更别说姑爷。”

Kuqiao said, "It's you who are strange, Miss. Wanting to take the Young Master as a disciple. Even I find it astonishing after hearing it, let alone the Young Master."

---

这一日，听到最多的名字就是乔文仙，他到底与朱淑真什么关系？千里寻决定搞搞清楚。

That day, the name she heard most was Qiao Wenxian. What exactly was his relationship with Zhu Shuzhen? Qianli Xun decided to find out.

---

“苦荞，随我回竹兰居，你家小姐我有事要问你。”

"Kuqiao, follow me back to the Zhulan Residence. Your miss has something to ask you."

---

苦荞很听话，跟着小姐往回走。

Kuqiao obediently followed her back.

---

到了竹兰居，千里寻换了居家的衣裳后，开始打听：“老听人提到乔文仙，他到底是谁？”

When they reached the Zhulan Residence, Qianli Xun changed into casual clothes and began to inquire. "I keep hearing people mention Qiao Wenxian. Who exactly is he?"

---

苦荞把小姐换下的衣裳放好后说：“他是乔家公子，是乔老爷纳的妾室所生，因是独苗，未来要继承乔家产业，所以养在夫人处，上面四个姐姐，大姐进了宫，成为乔贵妃，可惜命不好，生下儿子后就殁了。”

Kuqiao put away the clothes her miss had changed out of and said, "He's a young master from the Qiao family. He was born to a concubine of Lord Qiao. Because he's the only son, he'll inherit the Qiao family business in the future, so he was raised by the main wife. He has four older sisters above him. The eldest sister entered the palace and became Consort Qiao. Unfortunately, she had bad luck and died after giving birth to a son."

---

“你怎么知道得这么清楚？”千里寻好奇，苦荞一个丫鬟怎么对乔家如此了解。

"How do you know so clearly?" Qianli Xun was curious. How could Kuqiao, a maid, know so much about the Qiao family?

---

苦荞解释道：“乔家的事在临安已不是秘密，很多人都知道，只因他家出了一位乔贵妃，大家关注得多，议论得多而已，我也是在朱府跟其他丫鬟聊天儿时才知道。”

Kuqiao explained, "The Qiao family's affairs are no longer a secret in Lin'an. Many people know. It's just because a Consort Qiao came from their family that everyone paid more attention and talked more about it. I only learned about it from chatting with other maids at the Zhu residence."

---

“乔文仙是不是经常被东方公子邀请参加诗会？”

"Is Qiao Wenxian often invited by Young Master Dongfang to poetry gatherings?"

---

“是这样，不过他从来不做诗写词，只喜欢品鉴小姐您的诗词，而且是品鉴小姐诗词的绝佳之人，所以小姐视他为知己。”

"That's right, but he never writes poems or ci. He only likes appreciating your poems and ci, Miss. And he's the best person at appreciating your poetry. That's why you consider him a soulmate."

---

“知己？难道我喜欢他？”

"Soulmate? Does that mean I liked him?"

---

“小姐那时心里装着徐公子，容不下他人。”

"Back then, your heart was full of Xu. There was no room for anyone else."

---

千里寻叹道：“都怪爹娘不早将实情说明，让你家小姐深陷其中，要搁现在，徐公子倒贴，我都不一定能看得上。真怀疑你家小姐以前的眼光，怎么能看上徐公子那样的人？”

Qianli Xun sighed. "It's all my parents' fault for not telling me the truth earlier, letting me sink so deep into it. If it were now, even if Xu threw himself at me, I might not even look at him. I really doubt my taste from before. How could I fall for someone like Xu?"

---

“其实，徐公子人不错，既长得风流倜傥，又才华横溢，与小姐有共同语言，加之青梅竹马，小姐自然喜欢，只是他太听从父母的话，往好了来讲是孝顺，就是对小姐太不公。”

"Actually, Xu isn't a bad person. He's both handsome and talented, shares common interests with you, and you grew up together. Naturally, you liked him. It's just that he listened to his parents too much. To put it nicely, he's filial. But it's just too unfair to you."

---

“不谈他了，他已经是过去式。还是想想明日和离之事，给我把腐肌膏拿来。”

"Let's not talk about him. He's already in the past. Let's think about tomorrow's divorce instead. Bring me the Corrosive Paste."

---

苦荞取来腐肌膏：“真不知道小姐是怎么想的，非要伤害自己。”

Kuqiao fetched the Corrosive Paste. "I really don't understand what you're thinking, Miss. Why do you have to hurt yourself?"

---

千里寻接过，走到铜镜前坐下，倒出一些药膏涂在正在愈合的伤口上：“明天你就知道，去找林觉把治淤青的药拿回来。”

Qianli Xun took it, walked to the bronze mirror, and sat down. She poured out some ointment and applied it to the healing wound. "Tomorrow you'll know. Go find Lin Jue and get the bruise medicine back."

---

“是，小姐。”

"Yes, Miss."

---

苦荞出了竹兰居，来到林觉住的屋外，小心向里面说：“姑爷，小姐让我来拿治淤青的药。”

Kuqiao left the Zhulan Residence and came to the outside of Lin Jue's room. She called cautiously into the room, "Young Master, Miss asked me to get the bruise medicine."

---

过了一会儿，出来一名下人：“主君让你进去。”

After a while, a servant came out. "The Young Master wants you to go in."

---

苦荞走进去，发现姑爷坐在桌前，拿着一本书看，她纳闷：“姑爷怎么看起书来？”

Kuqiao walked in and found the Young Master sitting at the desk, with a book in hand. She was puzzled. "Why is the Young Master reading?"

---

林觉见苦荞进来，把书扣在桌上。苦荞清晰地看到“王右丞诗集”五个字，她从小跟着小姐，不仅认得几个字，而且知道王右丞是唐代著名诗人，他的诗小姐甚是喜爱，没想到姑爷竟然读他的诗。

Lin Jue saw Kuqiao come in and placed the book face down on the desk. Kuqiao clearly saw the five characters “Collected Poems of Wang Youcheng.” She had followed her miss since she was young. Not only did she recognize a few characters, but she also knew that Wang Youcheng—the official title of the famous Tang Dynasty poet Wang Wei—was a name her miss adored. She didn't expect the Young Master to actually read his poetry.

---

“药瓶在桌上，自己拿。”

"The medicine bottle is on the table. Take it yourself."

---

苦荞到一旁的桌上拿了药瓶，行完礼，要离开，被姑爷用话语拦住：“你等会儿，我有事问你。”

Kuqiao went to a nearby table and took the medicine bottle. After bowing, she was about to leave when the Young Master stopped her with words. "Wait a moment. I have something to ask you."

---

苦荞站住：“姑爷请问。”

Kuqiao stood still. "Please ask, Young Master."

---

“你家小姐跟以前不一样，难道朱家人不怨我？”

"Your miss is different from before. Doesn't the Zhu family resent me?"

---

苦荞心想，姑爷还有脸问！看我怎么怼你：“怎能不怨？只不过重生之说太过离奇，让人难以置信，再说现在的小姐性情虽大变，可长得跟以前一模一样，即使到官府告姑爷打死我家小姐，也找不到证据，朱家也只能吃哑巴亏。”

Kuqiao thought to herself, *the Young Master still has the nerve to ask! Watch how I'll retort you.* "How could they not resent you? It's just that the story of rebirth is too bizarre to be believable. Besides, although your miss's temperament has changed greatly now, she looks exactly the same as before. Even if they go to the authorities to accuse you of beating my miss to death, they won't find any evidence. The Zhu family can only swallow the bitter pill."

---

林觉心里还是有些害怕，毕竟岳父大人是临安知府。还好他已提前做好功课，在朱淑真之前拜访了岳父大人，跪着哭诉婚后的经历，说自己打朱淑真是因为她不顾丈夫颜面，在外与临安才子勾勾搭搭，因多次听到他人议论，才不得已出手打她。至于找商妓，是因为朱淑真不愿与他圆房所致。总之，岳父大人因他哭诉，又说得在理，并未怪罪。

Lin Jue was still somewhat afraid in his heart. After all, his father-in-law was the Prefect of Lin'an. Fortunately, he had done his homework in advance. Before Zhu Shuzhen could complain, he had already visited his father-in-law. He knelt down and tearfully recounted his experiences after marriage. He said he beat Zhu Shuzhen because she disregarded her husband's face and flirted with Lin'an talents outside. Because he heard others gossiping many times, he had no choice but to strike her. As for finding a merchant courtesan, it was because Zhu Shuzhen refused to consummate the marriage with him. In short, because of his tearful account and reasonable explanation, his father-in-law didn't blame him.

---

“你喜欢你家小姐现在的样子，还是从前？”

"Do you prefer the way your miss is now, or the way she was before?"

---

苦荞根本不愿在姑爷面前承认：“只要是我家小姐，不管过去还是现在我都喜欢。”

Kuqiao was unwilling to admit it in front of the Young Master. "As long as it's my miss, I like her whether it's the past or now."

---

“可我更喜欢现在的她。又会功夫，跟我算是同道中人，关键是还跟我讲话，不像以前那么冷漠。现在的她可喜欢那些附庸风雅的玩意儿？”

"But I like her more now. She knows martial arts and is somewhat of a kindred spirit with me. The key is she still talks to me, not as cold as before. Does she still like those pretentious cultural things now?"

---

苦荞用不满的语气说：“令姑爷失望，小姐的诗词才华依然还在，今日在诗会上品评易安居士的诗，得到所有人赞赏。”

Kuqiao said in a dissatisfied tone. "I'm sorry to disappoint you, Young Master. Miss's talent in poetry and ci is still there. Today at the poetry gathering, she offered a brilliant critique of Yi'an Jushi's poetry and won the admiration of every scholar present."

---

林觉露出失落的表情，瞧了瞧放在桌上的诗集，他研究半天，研究不出所以然，也提不起一点儿兴致：“真想不通，喜欢这玩意儿有什么用？既不能吃又不能喝。你走吧，告诉你家小姐，和离没那么容易。”

Lin Jue showed a disappointed expression. He looked at the poetry collection on the table. He had studied it for hours but couldn't figure anything out, and he couldn't muster any interest at all. "I really don't understand. What's the use of liking this stuff? You can't eat it or drink it. You can go now. Tell your miss that divorce isn't that easy."

---

苦荞退出，在门外小声对着屋内怼道：“我家小姐现在厉害着呢！不好欺负！”

Kuqiao withdrew. Outside the door, she quietly retorted to the room. "My miss is very powerful now! She's not easy to bully!"

---

回到竹兰居，苦荞把林觉说的话原原本本告诉小姐。

Returning to the Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao told her miss everything Lin Jue had said word for word.

---

千里寻只关注林觉说的“和离没那么容易”，心想，自己该做的都做了，明日的结果只能交给老天。

Qianli Xun only focused on what Lin Jue said: "Divorce isn't that easy." She thought to herself that she had done everything she could. As for tomorrow's result, only heaven knew.
