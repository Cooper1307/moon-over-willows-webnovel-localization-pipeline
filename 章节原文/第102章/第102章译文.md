## Chapter 102: A Day in the Life of a Noble Lady

At the first hint of dawn, the servants of Zhu Manor were up and about. Yinxing and Lutao led several maids into the small courtyard, crowding around Kuqiao as they waited for her to open the wooden box, eager to see how the spider had spun its web overnight.

Xiaguo had no interest at all. With her mistress still asleep and nothing to do, she sat idling in the maids' quarters, daydreaming about Young Master Qiao.

When the maids lifted the lid and saw a big, round web inside, cheers erupted, and their laughter woke Qianli Xun.

She opened her eyes to daylight. Waking from that dream last night had left her tossing and turning; she had no idea when she had drifted back off.

Infected by the maids' bright laughter, Qianli Xun climbed out of bed ready to welcome a fine day. She wanted a look at the spiderweb too, for good luck.

Still in her nightgown, she stepped out of bed and opened the door.

At the sound of the door, the maids hushed their laughter and bowed to their mistress.

Qianli Xun said, "Carry on, don't mind me. Once you're done looking, bring the box over so I can see too."

"We've already seen it and need to get to work, so we won't disturb Miss." With a smile, Lutao led Yinxing and the other maids away.

Quiet returned to the courtyard.

Carrying the wooden box, Kuqiao stepped up to her mistress. "Look at it indoors, Miss. Autumn is here and the mornings are chilly—don't catch cold."

Qianli Xun took the box from Kuqiao, stepped inside, and opened it. Sure enough, tucked in the corner sat a web spun overnight—big and round for the size of the box.

"You girls sure know how to have fun, making a game out of spiderwebs."

Making the bed as she spoke, Kuqiao said, "Take a close look, Miss. The web is tight, fine, and round—anyone who sees it gains skill and cleverness."

Qianli Xun looked again; it really was. Setting the box aside, she said, "So I get skill too? Too bad I can't thread a needle to save my life, let alone embroider."

"Everyone's skill is different. For Miss, it's writing poetry and ci."

"Fair point."

Xiaguo carried in a basin of washing water, overheard their chat, set the basin on a stool, and stood ready with a towel in her hands.

"Miss was born clever and honed it through study—that's why she writes great poetry and ci. It has nothing to do with spiderwebs. Back in the countryside I saw webs twice as big and round, and nobody gained a lick of skill from them. Don't blow an ordinary spiderweb out of proportion, Sister Kuqiao."

Helping her mistress change, Kuqiao retorted, "What a killjoy you are! It's a custom handed down by our ancestors."

Xiaguo pressed on: "That custom makes zero sense and doesn't even leave the poor spider in peace. It'd be better if everyone forgot it."

Once dressed, Qianli Xun let Xiaguo help her wash her face.

As she washed, Qianli Xun said, "When a custom survives, it has its reason; if it fades away one day, that has its reason too. Enjoy it while it's here, and don't fret if it's gone. Go with the flow in life."

Drying her face, she handed the towel back to Xiaguo and sat down at the vanity while both maids styled her hair and makeup.

Her remarks left the two maids unable to bicker any further.

Once dressed and fed, Qianli Xun settled at her desk to work while Kuqiao whisked tea and Xiaguo watched to learn.

Whenever Kuqiao made tea, Xiaguo sat beside her studying every step. Today she wanted to try her own hand: "Sister Kuqiao, let me whisk a bowl for you in a moment?"

"Sounds wonderful! I make tea for Miss all day and never get a bowl made for me. I'll treat myself today."

After serving her mistress's tea at the desk, Kuqiao yielded the tea table to Xiaguo. Following the steps she had memorized one by one, Xiaguo turned out a respectable bowl.

Kuqiao took a sip and praised her: "Better than my very first try."

Xiaguo beamed. Getting praised on her very first bowl meant that with a bit more practice, she could surpass Kuqiao.

Thrilled, she asked her mistress for a book to read in the maids' quarters. Even though much of it went over her head, she pored over every line, driven by one wish: to be worthy of Young Master Qiao someday.

While Xiaguo was out of the room, Qianli Xun handed her latest finished manuscript to Kuqiao to deliver to West Third Alley. Kuqiao tucked it away, hitched a carriage, and set off.

With nothing pressing to do, Qianli Xun was about to go chat with her sister-in-law Chen Wenxin when Matron Wei arrived alone.

"Where are you headed, Young Lady?"

"Thought I'd go sit with Sister-in-law in her courtyard."

"Go in a bit. Madam sent me to confirm something with you."

"What is it? Come inside and talk, Matron."

Once seated inside, Matron Wei saw no maids around and asked, "Where are Kuqiao and Xiaguo? Not a soul in sight?"

"I sent Kuqiao out on an errand, and Xiaguo is reading in her room—I gave her permission."

Matron Wei chided her: "I don't mean to lecture you, Young Lady, but kind as you are, you shouldn't spoil maids until they forget their place. That girl Xiaguo is restless enough as it is; let her read books and soon she'll fancy herself a mistress!"

"You're making too much of it, Matron. I just want her to read a little so I can find her a good match later."

"Don't waste your energy on her. That girl aims sky-high—she turned her nose up at a Military Training Officer, so Heaven knows who she's set her sights on. Never mind her; let's talk real business. Tell me the truth, Young Lady: is the Qiao family planning to come propose marriage at Zhu Manor?"

Wenyuan had only told her yesterday—how had Matron Wei caught wind of it so fast?

"How did you hear about that, Matron?"

"So it's true!" Matron Wei burst into a grin. "While you were out strolling yesterday, Madam and I didn't sit idle either—we went to the Vice-Prefect's residence for Madam Qin's Qixi Tea Banquet. Over tea, someone mentioned the Qiao family had been picking a bride for their son and halted out of nowhere, telling the matchmaker to return all the young ladies' birth horoscopes because they had already settled on their future daughter-in-law."

Secret glee bubbled in Qianli Xun's chest. Even if the Qiao family's bride selection could not rival a prince choosing a consort, it was huge news in Lin'an, depending on both pedigree and luck—and she, Qianli Xun, had turned out to be the lucky one.

Matron Wei went on: "Later, Intendant An's wife whispered to Madam that she heard from the Qiao family's matchmaker that they plan to propose to the Zhu family. Madam couldn't believe her ears, and couldn't ask you in front of everyone last night, so she sent me over today."

Seeing no need to hide it from her family, Qianli Xun repeated every word Wenyuan had told her yesterday.

Matron Wei slapped her knee, roaring with laughter. "Haha! If you ask me, the Qiao family is in quite a rush! You only went through your mutual divorce a short while ago—waiting half a year would be just right, haha! Just goes to show how outstanding our Young Lady is; the Qiao family is afraid someone else will snatch you up first! Haha!"

Matron Wei's reaction made Qianli Xun realize a month or two was not long at all, and she felt far too shy to admit she wished the Qiao family would come tomorrow.

Matron Wei chatted a while longer without seeing hide or hair of Xiaguo, and before leaving reminded her mistress once more not to pamper the servants.

Qianli Xun snapped to attention—she had almost forgotten her appointment with Prince Yi from yesterday! Good thing she remembered in time. With lunch approaching, visiting her sister-in-law would have to wait for another day.

Only when Lutao and the other maids set out lunch on schedule did Xiaguo emerge from the maids' quarters to wait on her mistress.

Standing beside her mistress as she ate, bitterness welled up in Xiaguo's chest. Why was she born to serve while her mistress was born to be served? Heaven was so unfair.

Just then Kuqiao walked in from delivering the manuscript. Seeing her, Xiaguo snapped, "All you do is run off and play, Sister Kuqiao, only strolling back now! Don't you know Miss needs waiting on?"

Kuqiao shot back, "Miss sent me on an errand! Unlike certain people who grab a book and hide out in the maids' quarters acting like a noble lady."

"You!" Stung, Xiaguo burst into tears. "Miss gave me permission to read, and you know it! Fine, starting tomorrow I won't read a single page! Waaah!"

No peace even at lunch! Quarreling maids were twice the headache of male martial-arts disciples—she could neither hit them nor scold them harsh. Qianli Xun set down her chopsticks. "What's gotten into you two today—swallowed gunpowder? Looks like you two just can't get along. Tell you what, Xiaguo, why don't you go stay back at Wangyue Pavilion for now."

With a thud, Xiaguo dropped to her knees. "Please don't send me away, Miss! I'll never fight with Sister Kuqiao again—I'll wait on you with all my heart!"

Kuqiao chimed in: "Miss, I promise I won't bicker with Xiaguo anymore either."

"That's more like it. Behave yourselves and get along."

Ignoring them both, Qianli Xun went back to eating.

The two maids turned docile on the spot, one serving dishes and the other ladling soup.

When the time drew near, Qianli Xun prepared to head for the Divine Willow to meet Prince Yi. She took only Kuqiao, telling Xiaguo to stay behind, mind the courtyard, and get some more reading done.

Only after asking whether her mistress was going to meet Young Master Qiao and hearing a firm no did Xiaguo settle back in the courtyard, returning to the books she did not quite understand.
