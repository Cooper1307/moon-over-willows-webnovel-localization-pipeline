第174章 离开喜宴去朱府
## Chapter 174: Leaving the Wedding Banquet for Zhu Manor

乔文仙成亲，申简辰自然会去，仪王是乔家的外孙，怎么说也应前去撑场面。
迎亲的过程申简辰并未参与，他直接参加婚宴，亲眼见证一对儿新人拜天地的过程。
整个过程，文渊一脸平静，平静到大家根本发现不了他内心的不快。苗知瑜顶着盖头，虽看不到表情，申简辰却能想象到她是快乐的。
想到苗知瑜的快乐，不由想起千里寻，不知她是否伤心？
几次邀请她去幽林别居，她都婉言拒绝，要么说身体不适，要么说近期不宜出门，打算窝在望月轩。
申简辰知道千里寻是有意避开他，她越是有意，他越是伤心。
梦容也跟着前来参加文渊的婚礼，她近期将哥哥看得更紧，到哪里都要跟着。
拜天地的程序结束后，大家进入宴席，梦容才与哥哥分开坐到女宾区。与乔家的女眷坐在一起，面对大家的热情与恭敬，她暂时没有心思关注哥哥。
不被梦容关注，申简辰觉出一身轻松，大家敬酒来者不拒。
文渊过来敬酒时，申简辰已有几分醉意，还是端起酒盅一口饮下。
文渊也一干为敬，仪王是他第一个敬酒之人，他还没有醉意，可今日他已决定，要喝得酩酊大醉，只有这样才能忘记成亲时娶的不是心爱之人。
接着文渊到处敬酒，申简辰也一盅一盅继续喝，醉意越浓，心中越想千里寻。
他招来白风，悄悄说：“出去套好马车。”
白风不敢多问，乖乖离开宴席，出了乔府。
申简辰瞧了瞧文渊，他正在与众人敬酒，便向身边的乔守仁耳语道：“外公，我有要事，先行一步。结束后，烦请外公派人将梦容送回。”
乔守仁不便多问，自然同意：“仪王请便，结束后我定派人将佳和郡主安全送到。”
趁众人不注意，申简辰起身离开。
文渊成亲后，朱陈两家的婚事也快了，申简辰根本坐不住，在酒精的刺激下，他想去朱府走一趟。
马车到达朱府时，天色已暗下，申简辰从马车上下来，径直向朱府侧门而去。
白风不明所以，紧跟上，朱府侧门紧闭，他上前将门敲开。
开门的下人认识仪王，赶紧行礼，刚要说话，仪王却不管不顾走进府内，走了两步，不知该往哪个方向。
白风对朱府的下人说：“仪王来见知府大人，前面带路。”
下人说道：“仪王，您这边请。”
When Qiao Wenxian's wedding day arrived, Shen Jianchen naturally attended; as the Qiao family's maternal grandson, Prince Yi owed them his presence. Skipping the bridal procession, he arrived straight for the banquet and watched the new couple bow to heaven and earth.

朱府的下人把仪王带到会客厅，安顿好后，急急匆匆去通知老爷。
Throughout the rites, Wenyuan kept a calm, unreadable face—so steady that guests never guessed the misery beneath. Though the red veil hid Miao Zhiyu's features, Shen Jianchen could picture her quiet bliss. Thinking of Miao Zhiyu's joy drew his thoughts straight to Qianli Xun. Was she hurting today? Every time he invited her to Youlin Retreat lately, she sent polite excuses—feeling under the weather, or preferring not to go out and staying shut inside Wangyue Pavilion. Knowing she was dodging him on purpose only sharpened the ache in his chest.

朱晞颜吃过晚饭，正在院中溜达，听到下人报告，来不及换衣裳，匆忙往会客厅而去。
Mengrong had come to Wenyuan's wedding too, keeping a tighter watch on her brother than ever and sticking close wherever he went. Once the ceremony ended and the feast began, however, she moved to the women's section. Surrounded by warm, deferential Qiao ladies, she had no time to monitor her brother.

一路上思忖，乔文仙大婚，按说仪王应该在乔家，为何天刚暗下却突然造访？也不知是好事还是坏事。
Freed from Mengrong's watchful eyes, Shen Jianchen breathed easier and accepted every toast offered. By the time Wenyuan came around to toast him, Shen Jianchen already felt a warm buzz yet drained his cup in one swallow. Wenyuan matched him drop for drop. Prince Yi was the very first guest he toasted; still sober now, he had resolved to drink himself blind tonight so he could forget the bride waiting in his chambers was not the woman he loved.

当朱晞颜走进会客厅时，闻到一股酒气，便知仪王是从乔文仙的婚宴而来，看那脸色，略微泛红，应该喝了不少酒。
As Wenyuan moved from table to table, Shen Jianchen kept knocking back cup after cup, the wine only making him miss Qianli Xun harder. Beckoning Bai Feng over, he murmured, "Go hitch the carriage outside."

朱晞颜恭敬拜道：“让仪王殿下久候，是下官失职。”
Without asking questions, Bai Feng slipped out of the feast and exited Qiao Manor.

申简辰坐在椅子上，端起桌上的茶盏，喝起茶来，他想试试，会不会因为喝过几口茶，人变得清醒，便不想说出接下来的话。
Glancing over to see Wenyuan busy toasting guests, Shen Jianchen leaned toward Qiao Shouren and murmured, "Grandfather, urgent business calls me away. Once the banquet ends, please have someone escort Mengrong home."

可喝下茶后，他的想法依然未改变。
Qiao Shouren knew better than to pry. "Go right ahead, Your Highness. I will see Princess Jiahe safely home when it wraps up."

朱晞颜见仪王一言不发，只顾喝茶，不由紧张起来，难道近期政务出现什么问题被仪王知晓？
Slipping out unnoticed, Shen Jianchen left the hall. With Wenyuan wed, the Zhu-Chen wedding loomed right around the corner. Restless and emboldened by wine, he headed straight for Zhu Manor.

“下官若有什么错处，还请仪王明示。”
Dusk had fallen by the time the carriage rolled up. Alighting, Shen Jianchen marched straight to the side gate, with a baffled Bai Feng hurrying at his heels to knock.

只见仪王放下茶盏，抬起几分醉眼：“我过来是想求知府大人一件事。”
Recognizing Prince Yi, the gatekeeper dropped to his knees—only for the prince to stride straight past into the courtyard, then halt after two paces unsure which path to take.

朱晞颜心里的石头终于落地，原来不是为政务：“仪王请说，只要下官能办到，赴汤蹈火，在所不辞。”
"Prince Yi is here to see Prefect Zhu," Bai Feng told the gatekeeper. "Lead the way."

“知府大人言重，不是什么大事，也不是难事，就看知府大人是否乐意。”
"Right this way, Your Highness."

“乐意乐意，当然乐意，只要是仪王的事，下官哪有不乐意的道理。”
The servant ushered Prince Yi into the reception hall, saw him seated, and dashed off to summon Master Zhu.

“好，既如此，也没必要瞒着，我喜欢幽栖居士，想把她放在我身边，还请知府大人明日去陈家商议退婚之事。”
Having finished supper, Zhu Xiyan was strolling in his courtyard when the report reached him. Without stopping to change robes, he hurried toward the reception hall, wondering what could bring Prince Yi knocking right after dusk when he ought to be at Qiao Wenxian's wedding feast—good news or bad?

尽管早已猜到仪王对自家女儿有想法，此刻仪王如此明确提出，还是将朱晞颜给惊住。
Stepping inside, he caught the sharp whiff of wine and saw the flush across the prince's cheeks. Dropping into a deep bow, he said, "Keeping Your Highness waiting is this humble official's dereliction."

白风也被惊住。
Seated in his chair, Shen Jianchen lifted a tea bowl and drank. Part of him tested whether a few sips of tea would sober him up enough to hold back what he was about to say. It did not.

神柳之下，王爷与幽栖居士抱在一起时他虽已知道王爷的心思，可想着两人都有婚约，王爷应该会慢慢放下，没想到他如此疯狂，直接逼知府大人找陈家退婚，这跟抢亲有什么区别？
Watching the prince drink in tight-lipped silence rattled Zhu Xiyan—had some blunder in prefecture administration reached his ears? "If this humble official has erred in any way, please speak plainly, Your Highness."

吃惊之余，朱晞颜跪在地上，低下头，一言不发。
Setting the bowl down, Shen Jianchen raised wine-heavy eyes. "I came to ask Prefect Zhu for one favor."

申简辰垂下醉眼：“怎么？你不愿意？难道觉得我配不上幽栖居士？”
A boulder rolled off Zhu Xiyan's chest—not official business, then! "Speak, Your Highness! If it lies within my power, I will go through fire and water without hesitation!"

朱晞颜感慨，如果仪王早些出现那该多好。可现在他着实不想再折腾：“仪王乃人中龙凤，是淑真无福，配不上殿下。”
"No need for dramatics, Prefect. It is neither huge nor hard—only a matter of whether you are willing."

在喜酒的作用下，申简辰忍不住表露心迹：“可我看幽栖居士甚是顺眼。”
"Willing, willing, of course I am willing! How could I ever refuse anything for Your Highness?"

朱晞颜赶紧磕头：“仪王是官家之子，娶妻纳妾自然要征得官家同意，淑真不仅和离过一次，在临安名声也不好，只怕官家不会同意，还望仪王三思而行。”
"Good. Then I will not beat around the bush. I love Youqi Jushi and want her by my side. I ask Prefect Zhu to go to the Chen family tomorrow and call off their engagement."

申简辰再次抬起醉眼，见朱晞颜的头几乎挨到地面，不满道：“什么三思而行？我看是知府大人根本不想将女儿许给我。”
Even though Zhu Xiyan had half-suspected Prince Yi's feelings for his daughter, hearing it laid out so bluntly stunned him speechless.

朱晞颜的头这次真的挨到地面：“下官不敢，只是淑真的婚姻之路并不顺遂，好不容易与陈家订亲，婚事在即，作为她的父亲，不愿旁生枝节，更不敢奢望能攀上高枝，只愿她平平安安，一切顺利。”
Bai Feng froze in shock too. Seeing them embrace beneath the Divine Willow had tipped him off to his master's heart, yet with both of them betrothed, he had assumed the prince would let go in time. Never had he imagined His Highness going this far—pressuring the Prefect to cancel a wedding on the eve of the ceremony! How was this any different from snatching a bride?

申简辰变得沉默，千里寻拒绝的话回荡在耳边：“老娘说过，除非猪能上树，才答应嫁给你，这话依然有效。”
Reeling, Zhu Xiyan dropped straight to his knees and bowed his head in silence.

他叹口气，站起来，绕过朱晞颜向门外走去。白风也绕过朱晞颜，紧跟上王爷。
Shen Jianchen looked down through hazy eyes. "What? You refuse? Think I am not good enough for Youqi Jushi?"

仪王离开后，朱晞颜才抬起头，从地上爬起来，走到椅子跟前坐下，深深吸了一口气，又吐出：“唉，没想到淑真这么招人喜欢，连仪王都看上，可惜啊，我朱家没有成为皇亲国戚的命。”
If only Prince Yi had spoken up months ago, Zhu Xiyan lamented inwardly—yet now he could not bear another storm. "Your Highness is a dragon among men. It is Shuzhen who lacks the blessing to match Your Highness."

离开朱府，申简辰没有立刻回幽林别居，而是朝望月轩方向而去。
Emboldened by wedding wine, Shen Jianchen pressed his heart out into the open. "Yet Youqi Jushi suits my eyes."

到达后，并未下马车，呆呆地坐在车里。
Zhu Xiyan knocked his forehead to the floor. "Your Highness is His Majesty's own son; taking wife or concubine requires imperial assent. Not only has Shuzhen been divorced once, her name in Lin'an has been bruised. I fear His Majesty would never approve. I beg Your Highness to think thrice!"

白风不敢言语，更不敢去找苦荞，默默陪伴王爷。
Raising his eyes to see Zhu Xiyan's brow near the floorboards, Shen Jianchen frowned in irritation. "Think thrice? Sounds to me like Prefect Zhu simply refuses to give me your daughter."

大约过了两刻钟，韦嬷嬷带着一名丫鬟和小厮从外面回来。姑娘去陈家后，韦嬷嬷便去铺子里收租，收完租又与约好的朋友在外面喝茶，所以回来晚些。
Zhu Xiyan pressed his forehead flush against the floor. "This humble official would never dare! Only Shuzhen's road in marriage has been rocky from the start. After all this trouble, she is finally betrothed to the Chen family with the wedding close at hand. As her father, I want no fresh complications, nor dare I dream of climbing into imperial ranks. I wish only for her to live safe and smooth."

她走下马车后，一眼瞧见仪王的马车停在外面，以为佳和郡主来找自家姑娘，仪王又在外面等候，便走过去行礼道：“仪王殿下，可否去屋里坐，边品茶边等郡主？”
Shen Jianchen fell silent as Qianli Xun's sharp rejection rang in his ears: "I told you—when pigs can climb trees, maybe I will marry you. That still stands."

马车内并未传出仪王的声音，韦嬷嬷感到纳闷，向坐在马车边缘的白风问：“白侍卫，仪王殿下可在车上？”
Letting out a long breath, he rose, stepped around the kneeling Prefect, and walked out the door, Bai Feng hurrying right behind him.

白风见王爷不搭话，连忙掩饰：“王爷怎么可能在车上？是我想见苦荞，才偷偷把马车赶过来，见天色已晚，正犹豫要不要见她，嬷嬷就出现，我想我还是不见了，这就回去。”
Only after Prince Yi had gone did Zhu Xiyan lift his head, climb stiffly to his feet, and drop into a chair with a deep exhale. "Ah, who knew Shuzhen was so irresistible that even Prince Yi fell for her? Shame our Zhu family lacks the destiny to become imperial kin."

白风急忙赶起马车离开望月轩。
Leaving Zhu Manor, Shen Jianchen did not head straight back to Youlin Retreat, steering instead toward Wangyue Pavilion. Arriving outside its gate, he stayed inside the carriage staring blankly into the dark. Bai Feng dared neither speak nor slip off to see Kuqiao, sitting silent on the box beside his master.

韦嬷嬷立刻明白仪王肯定在马车上，而佳和郡主根本没来，看来这位仪王对自家姑娘确实不一般。韦嬷嬷决定将今晚所见烂在心里，不会在姑娘面前提起，省得令她心烦。
About half an hour passed before Matron Wei returned in a carriage with a maid and a page boy. After her young mistress had gone off to Chen Manor earlier, Matron Wei had collected shop rents and lingered over tea with old friends, arriving home late. Stepping down, she spotted Prince Yi's carriage parked outside and assumed Princess Jiahe was visiting inside while the prince waited. Walking over, she curtsied. "Your Highness, will you not step inside and wait for Her Highness over a bowl of tea?"

申简辰没有与韦嬷嬷搭话，并不是怕被人知道他对幽栖居士的心思，而是懒得说话。白风将马车赶走，他也未埋怨。
When no voice answered from inside, Matron Wei blinked and asked Bai Feng on the driver's bench, "Guard Bai, is Prince Yi inside?"

这个时间，梦容应该已从乔家回到幽林别居。申简辰想到梦容，心理重负再次出现，比往常任何时候都让他喘不过气来。
Seeing his master stay mute, Bai Feng scrambled to cover. "How could His Highness be inside? I only borrowed the carriage on the sly to see Kuqiao! Seeing how late it got, I was debating whether to knock when you showed up, Matron. Better I head back now!" Snapping the reins, he drove off in a hurry.

自捶丸场后，他几次想张口向梦容说明心中所爱，都因梦容充满爱意又让人不由产生怜惜的眼神，不得不将已到喉咙的话咽回去。
Matron Wei knew at once Prince Yi had been sitting inside—and Princess Jiahe was nowhere near Wangyue Pavilion. Prince Yi's feelings for her young mistress were far from ordinary. Resolving to bury tonight's sight deep in her chest, she decided not to breathe a word to Shuzhen and spare her fresh grief.

此刻，他坐在马车上，一阵醉意袭来，想要吐露真情的想法愈加强烈。
Inside the rolling carriage, Shen Jianchen had stayed silent not from fear of exposure, only from sheer weariness. He did not blame Bai Feng for driving away either. By this hour, Mengrong would be back at Youlin Retreat. One thought of her brought that suffocating weight crashing down on his chest harder than ever. Since the chuiwan grounds, he had tried telling her the truth more than once, only to choke every word back before her loving, vulnerable eyes. Now, with wine buzzing in his veins, the urge to lay the truth bare burned hotter than ever.
