第162章 不激烈的捶丸赛
## Chapter 162: A Mild Match of Chuiwan

仪王发话，众人不好再聊下去，各自散去。
林觉带白梅若回到凉亭下属于自己的位置，乔文仙与苗知瑜也来到长辈身边。
哥哥准备上场，乔梦容负责安排，跟上哥哥去帮他换装。
千里寻回到朱家所在的凉亭，不见韦嬷嬷的身影，猜她定是跑去与夫人们聊天儿拉关系。
苦荞见小姐回来，迎上去：“小姐可回来。”
千里寻笑着批评：“我只去逛了一会儿，说得好像我离开许久。”
“少夫人跑去捶丸，韦嬷嬷又去找人聊天儿，夏果刚还在，这会儿又不知去哪里，就剩我跟红豆，她又不爱说话，很没意思。”
千里寻瞧一眼红豆，她确实话少，估计是刚被提拔为贴身丫鬟还不适应。
“苦荞，你要是觉得无趣，去找白风玩，这里有红豆伺候。”
“真的可以吗？”
“有什么不可以？去吧。”
“谢谢小姐！”苦荞高高兴兴离开凉亭，去找白风。
红豆靠过来：“小姐，您需要我做什么？”
“我要去捶丸，给我绑好襻膊，再将球杆拿来。”
红豆很听话，拿出提前准备好的襻膊，为小姐绑好，取来球杆，双手递给小姐后，又不再说话，默默站在一边。
千里寻叮嘱道：“以后跟着我不要拘谨，要多说话，我喜欢话多的丫鬟。”
“是，小姐。”
Once Prince Yi spoke, the group broke off their chatter and scattered. Lin Jue led Bai Meiruo back to their seats in the pavilion, while Qiao Wenxian and Miao Zhiyu returned to their elders. Since her brother was taking the field, Qiao Mengrong followed him to see to his change of clothes.

“你要是无聊可以到处转转。”
Qianli Xun returned to the Zhu family's pavilion and found no sign of Matron Wei, guessing she had gone off to mingle and build ties with the other matrons.

“我不喜欢到处转，就在这里看小姐捶丸。”
Spotting her mistress, Kuqiao hurried up. "Miss, you are back at last."

千里寻并不强求，手拿球杆走下凉亭，来到捶丸场。申简辰已换好衣装，站着等她。
"I only went for a short stroll," Qianli Xun teased. "You make it sound like I vanished for hours."

千里寻走在薄薄的一层冒出新芽的枯草上，有些不忍心踩踏绿芽，她尽量绕开。又转念一想，今日已有许多人踩踏过，她再注意又有何用？再说一会儿比赛，肯定没有心思在意这些新生命，于是大胆踩上去。
"Young Madam went off to play chuiwan, Matron Wei went off chatting, and Xiaguo wandered off somewhere just a moment ago. That left only me and Hongdou, and she never says a word. I was bored to death."

被人踩踏过的青草，要么拼尽全力顽强生长，要么偃旗息鼓，化为尘土，不见踪迹。就像申简辰站的那一处，只剩下泥土，肯定是那里的草在命运的折磨下已丧生。
Qianli Xun glanced at Hongdou. The girl really was quiet, probably still adjusting after being promoted to personal maid. "Kuqiao, if you are bored, go hang out with Bai Feng. Hongdou can attend me here."

千里寻想到自己，会不会也要屈服命运，让它把自己玩弄于股掌之间……
"Can I really?"

“在想什么？”
"Why not? Go on."

申简辰主动打招呼，她不再想下去：“没什么，什么时候开始捶丸？”
"Thank you, Miss!" Kuqiao skipped out of the pavilion to find Bai Feng.

“过一会儿。”
Hongdou stepped closer. "Miss, what do you need me to do?"

千里寻瞧见嫂嫂和陈浩宇向他们走来：“对手过来了。”
"I am going to play chuiwan. Tie my sleeve-tying cord and fetch me a club."

申简辰故意调侃：“你与我一起跟自家人对打，会不会不太好？”
Obedient as ever, Hongdou took out the cord she had prepared in advance, tied back her mistress's sleeves, handed over the club with both hands, and fell back into silence at her side.

千里寻生气道：“嫂嫂是自家人，陈浩宇可不是。少在我面前瞎胡说，小心老娘到梦容面前说你坏话。”
"Do not be so stiff around me from now on," Qianli Xun told her. "Talk more. I like chatty maids."

千里寻不认为陈浩宇是自家人，申简辰很开心：“我可没做任何坏事。”
"Yes, Miss."

“故意引诱梦容求我跟你组队，就是坏事。”
"If you get bored, feel free to wander around."

刚说完，嫂嫂与陈浩宇已走到过来，一起向仪王行礼。
"I do not care to wander. I will stay right here and watch Miss play chuiwan."

陈文心说：“我听说仪王要跟我和浩宇对打，带他赶来拜见，一会儿捶丸时若有对不住之处，还请仪王见谅。”
Qianli Xun did not push her. Club in hand, she stepped down from the pavilion and walked onto the field, where Shen Jianchen already stood waiting in fresh sporting attire.

“捶丸场上无尊卑，一会儿文心嫂嫂可不要手下留情。”
Stepping across the thin mat of dry grass poked through with tender green shoots, Qianli Xun felt reluctant to crush the sprouts and tried stepping around them. Then she caught herself: dozens of people had trampled this turf all morning, so what difference would her caution make? Once the match started, she would have no mind to spare for new grass anyway. She planted her boots down and strode straight ahead.

陈浩宇行着礼，心中纳闷：“仪王又是跟朱淑真组队，又称姐姐为嫂嫂，难道他喜欢朱淑真？应该不会吧？母老虎我都瞧不上，王爷又如何能瞧得上？”
Trampled grass either fought with all its might to keep growing or surrendered, withered to dust, and vanished without a trace. Just like the bare patch of dirt under Shen Jianchen's feet, where the turf had given up under fate's relentless pounding. Would she too have to bow to fate and let it toss her around in the palm of its hand?

仪王这声“嫂嫂”称得陈文心甚是疑惑：“捶丸场上我自然不会留情，不过，仪王称呼草民为嫂嫂，好像不太……”
"What are you thinking about?"

千里寻瞪了一眼申简辰，心中不满：“都穿越成王爷，说话还如此不着调！”
Shen Jianchen's greeting snapped her out of it. "Nothing. When do we start?"

见到陈浩宇，申简辰不由自主想宣示主权，一不小心用一个亲密的称呼表露，被千里寻瞪一眼，赶紧解释：“梦容与幽栖居士是闺中密友，你是幽栖居士的嫂嫂也就是梦容的嫂嫂，我称呼一声嫂嫂也无妨。”
"In a moment."

陈浩宇松口气，继续想：“原来是这个原因，并不是喜欢朱淑真。”
Spotting her sister-in-law and Chen Haoyu walking up, Qianli Xun nodded toward them. "Here come our opponents."

又一想，自己紧张个什么劲儿？要是仪王喜欢上朱淑真，岂不是可以帮他解决掉大麻烦？
"Teaming up with me to play against your own family—are you sure that is alright?" Shen Jianchen teased.

传来乔家丫鬟的声音：“接下来的一场由陈家公子与仪王对战！彩头是我家夫人最喜欢的金如意！”
"My sister-in-law is family; Chen Haoyu is not," Qianli Xun snapped. "Cut the nonsense in front of me, or I will go tattle on you to Mengrong."

众人不管远近都朝丫鬟端的木盒中瞧去，果然有一把金灿灿的如意。
Hearing she did not count Chen Haoyu as family brightened Shen Jianchen's mood. "I have done nothing wrong."

有人议论：“要不是仪王捶丸，乔家夫人能拿出这么贵重的物件当彩头？”
"Baited Mengrong into begging me to be your partner—that counts as wrong."

另一人应和着：“说的可不是？”
Before he could reply, Chen Wenxin and Chen Haoyu arrived and bowed to Prince Yi.

裁判宣布：“本场比赛开始！仪王队，红旗！陈家公子队，黄旗！”
"I heard Your Highness wants to play a match against Haoyu and me, so I brought him right over to pay respects," Chen Wenxin said. "If we give offense during the game, please forgive us."

不能再聊下去，四人手拿球杆向第一处球基走去，裁判早已将球放置在球基处。
"There is no high or low on the chuiwan field," Shen Jianchen said. "Do not hold back on my account, Sister-in-Law Wenxin."

千里寻向远处瞧去，草丛中有的球窝旁插着黄色旗子，有的插着红色旗子，如果她没猜错，他们需要将球打入黄色旗子中才算赢。她目测球窝的距离，这一局应该很好打。
Mid-bow, Chen Haoyu frowned to himself. Prince Yi teams up with Zhu Shuzhen and calls my sister Sister-in-Law? Does he fancy Zhu Shuzhen? No way. Even I would not touch that tigress, so why would a prince?

捶丸开始，黄队先来。陈浩宇第一个走到球基跟前，摆好姿势，一杆下去，球乖乖地被打入插有红色旗子的球窝中。
The address bewildered Chen Wenxin as well. "I will certainly play my best, yet for Your Highness to call a commoner like me Sister-in-Law seems rather..."

第二个是陈文心，也顺利打入。
Qianli Xun shot Shen Jianchen a sharp glare, fuming inside: Transmigrated into a prince and you still talk without a filter!

第三个是申简辰，打这种球对他来讲毫无压力，轻轻一击，球顺利跳进插黄色旗子的球窝。
Seeing Chen Haoyu had triggered Shen Jianchen's urge to stake his claim, making an intimate title slip out. Under Qianli Xun's glare, he hurried to patch it over. "Mengrong and Youqi Jushi are closest friends. Being Youqi Jushi's sister-in-law makes you Mengrong's sister-in-law too, so calling you Sister-in-Law does no harm."

最后是千里寻，经过观察，确实与她打过的高尔夫球区别不大，对她来讲根本没有难度，一杆子下去，成功打入。
Chen Haoyu let out a breath. So that was all it was—he did not like Zhu Shuzhen after all. Then again, why was he even nervous? If Prince Yi really took a shine to Zhu Shuzhen, wouldn't that take a massive headache right off his hands?

陈文心赞道：“淑真，你的球技的确精进不少。”
A Qiao family maid called out, "Next match: Young Master Chen versus Prince Yi! The prize is our madam's favorite gold ruyi scepter!"

接着每人又在原处各捶两次，两队不分胜负，各获一筹。
Spectators near and far craned their necks toward the wooden box in the maid's hands, where a gleaming gold ruyi scepter rested.

四人又跟裁判向另一处球基走去。
"Would Madam Qiao ever put up a prize that lavish if Prince Yi were not playing?" someone muttered.

陈浩宇见仪王走在最前面，紧跟裁判，朱淑真则跟姐姐陈文心走在一起。他趁机靠近：“朱淑真，你会功夫，仪王也会功夫，你们两个组队，要是赢我们，胜之不武。”
"You can say that again," another voice agreed.

千里寻站住，没好气地盯着陈浩宇：“劝你没事多读些书，都不知道胜之不武是什么意思，净瞎胡用！”
The referee shouted, "Let the match begin! Prince Yi's side, red flags! Young Master Chen's side, yellow flags!"

“什么意思？”
Ending their chat, the four players walked club-in-hand to the first starting pad, where the referee had already placed the ball.

“懒得告诉你。”千里寻想起退婚一事，将陈浩宇拉到一边。
Qianli Xun squinted down the turf. Some holes in the grass bore yellow flags and some bore red. If she guessed right, their team had to sink balls into the yellow-flagged holes to score. Eyeballing the distance, she judged this opening round an easy shot.

陈文心并不理会，笑了笑，继续朝前走。
The yellow team went first. Chen Haoyu stepped up to the starting pad, set his stance, swung, and sent the ball rolling straight into a red-flagged hole. Chen Wenxin went second and sank hers with equal ease. Third came Shen Jianchen; a shot like this held zero pressure for him, and a light tap sent his ball hopping straight into a yellow-flagged hole. Last was Qianli Xun. Having watched closely, she found it little different from modern golf. One clean stroke, and her ball dropped straight in.

陈浩宇被拉到一边，甩开朱淑真的手：“你拉我干什么？”
"Your skill has come on in leaps and bounds, Shuzhen," Chen Wenxin praised.

千里寻悄悄问：“赶紧告诉老娘，你们家老太太是否还想耗着？”
Each player took two more shots from the same pad, leaving both teams dead even with one tally apiece.

“那还用说？”
The four followed the referee toward the next starting pad. Prince Yi strode out front alongside the referee while Zhu Shuzhen walked with Chen Wenxin. Seeing an opening, Chen Haoyu sidled up. "Zhu Shuzhen, you know martial arts and Prince Yi knows martial arts. If the two of you team up and beat us, that is an unfair victory."

“那便好，眼看快到成婚的日子，后面的事全靠你，可得上点心。”
Qianli Xun stopped dead and glared at him. "Do yourself a favor and crack open a book sometime. You do not even know what 'unfair victory' means, throwing phrases around at random!"

“放心，奶奶最疼我，什么都听我的。”
"Then what does it mean?"

申简辰已到第二个球基处，这一筹，由红队开始，他想让千里寻第一个捶丸，发现她并未跟上，朝后面一瞧，原来跟陈浩宇在一处说话，不由嫉妒起来。
"Cannot be bothered to teach you." Remembering their engagement, she grabbed Chen Haoyu and pulled him aside. Chen Wenxin smiled and kept walking without minding them.

“幽栖居士！赶紧过来！该你捶丸！”
Chen Haoyu shook off her hand. "What are you dragging me over here for?"

千里寻听到呼唤她的声音，甩下陈浩宇快步向第二个球基处走去。
"Tell me straight," Qianli Xun whispered. "Is your grandmother still planning to stall?"

陈文心在一旁偷偷观察仪王的表情，总觉哪里不对劲儿，又不敢往下想，她晃晃脑袋：“瞎想什么？毫无道理！”
"Of course she is."

第二处球基面对的球窝地势显然凹凸不平，想要把球打入球窝，有一定难度。可难不倒千里寻，她拿起球杆，一棒下去，球飞过凹凸不平之地，不偏不倚掉进对方的球窝……
"Good. The wedding date is right around the corner. Everything from here on rests on you, so stay sharp."

捶丸比赛不如打马球激烈，即使是仪王登场，也没有将所有人吸引住，该聊天儿的人照样聊天儿，该走动的照样走动。
"Relax. Grandmother dotes on me most and listens to everything I say."

只有像钱夫人那样喜欢捶丸的，或是像林觉那样需要巴结仪王的，抑或是像梦容那样不会捶丸但关心哥哥的，才会不断用目光照顾捶丸场上的人。
Having reached the second starting pad, where the red team had the first stroke, Shen Jianchen turned to invite Qianli Xun up—only to spot her huddled in private conversation with Chen Haoyu. Jealousy flared in his chest. "Youqi Jushi! Hurry over! It is your turn to strike!"

乔文仙关心淑真，自然也会瞧去。苗知瑜关心文渊，只会向他瞧的方向瞧去。
Hearing his call, Qianli Xun ditched Chen Haoyu and strode over to the second starting pad.

当千里寻顺利将球打进球窝里，钱氏赞道：“没想到朱家姑娘捶丸的技术这么好！”
Watching from the side, Chen Wenxin studied Prince Yi's expression. Something felt off, though she dared not follow the thought further. She shook her head. What wild nonsense am I imagining? It makes no sense at all!

米氏不屑道：“捶丸好，有什么用？女子关键要名声好。”
The turf stretching between the second pad and its target hole rose and dipped over uneven mounds, making a clean sink tricky. None of that fazed Qianli Xun. She raised her club and swung; the ball sailed over the bumps and dropped dead center into the opposing hole.

秦夫人也说：“米姨娘说得极是。”
Chuiwan lacked the roaring thrill of polo, so even with Prince Yi on the field, plenty of guests kept chatting and strolling as they pleased. Only die-hard chuiwan fans like Madam Qian, climbers eager to curry Prince Yi's favor like Lin Jue, or devoted onlookers like Mengrong kept their eyes fixed on the match. Qiao Wenxian watched because he cared about Shuzhen, and Miao Zhiyu watched only wherever Wenyuan's eyes went.

两人说完，瞧到钱氏脸色不好看，立刻意识到说错话，米氏赶紧解释：“姐姐别往心里去，我说的是朱家姑娘。”
When Qianli Xun sank her ball, Madam Qian clapped in admiration. "I never guessed Miss Zhu had such talent at chuiwan!"

秦夫人赶快改口：“女子捶丸好，名声好，会更好，就像夫人您。”
Concubine Mi sniffed. "What good is skill at chuiwan? For a woman, a good reputation is what counts."

钱氏这才露出笑容：“看他们捶丸，我也手痒，不如一会儿也去场上玩玩儿。”
"Concubine Mi speaks the truth," Madam Qin chimed in.

乔家人瞧不上幽栖居士，苗知瑜心里多少还是舒服些。
Both women saw Madam Qian's face darken and caught their slip at once. "Do not take it to heart, Sister," Concubine Mi scrambled to explain. "I meant Miss Zhu."

韦嬷嬷转了好几个凉亭，拉过一阵关系后，终于转到乔家，其实是她有意晚些过来。
Madam Qin hurried to mend her words too. "A woman with both chuiwan skill and a sterling reputation is best of all—just like you, Madam."

乔家那样对待自家姑娘，她故意晚些，既能表明态度，也可表达不满。
Only then did Madam Qian smile again. "Watching them play has my hands itching. I might step out for a round myself later."

“钱夫人是捶丸的好手，应该给年轻一辈露一手。”
Seeing the Qiao women look down on Youqi Jushi eased the tightness in Miao Zhiyu's chest a little.

大家向声音传来的方向看去，原来是韦嬷嬷。
After making the rounds of several other pavilions, Matron Wei at last strolled over to the Qiaos' pavilion. She had saved them for last on purpose; after how the Qiaos had treated her young mistress, showing up late made both her stance and her displeasure clear.

钱氏笑道：“我还在想，韦嬷嬷到处走动，为何不来与我们说话？可巧，这就来了，看座！”
"Madam Qian is a master at chuiwan," Matron Wei called out. "You really ought to give the younger generation a demonstration."

亭中人多，准备的凳子早已用完，丫鬟说：“夫人，没有凳子了。”
Everyone turned to see Matron Wei approaching. Madam Qian laughed. "I was just wondering why Matron Wei was visiting every pavilion except ours! Look how timely you are. Bring a seat!"

苗知瑜主动站起来：“我的凳子让与嬷嬷。”
The pavilion was crowded, and every stool was already taken. "Madam, there are no stools left," a maid reported.

韦嬷嬷客气道：“那怎么行？苗姑娘也是金尊玉贵的，赶紧坐下，没有凳子，我便站着与夫人说话。”
Miao Zhiyu stood up at once. "Let Matron Wei have mine."

“嬷嬷不必推让，知瑜坐了许久，想去走走。”
"Oh, I could never do that!" Matron Wei demurred. "Lady Miao is a precious noble daughter. Please sit back down. I can stand while I chat with Madam."

苗知瑜的确不想再坐下去，也不想看乔文仙一味盯着捶丸场的人，她想一个人到园子里透透气。向众人行过礼后，没经过同意，自作主张离开。
"Do not stand on ceremony, Matron. I have been sitting a long while and would like to go for a walk anyway."

丫鬟将凳子搬到钱夫人旁边，韦嬷嬷不再推让，上前坐下，瞧一眼乔公子，他根本不管苗家姑娘，盯着球场看，顺那目光瞧去，果然是自家姑娘。看来乔公子还未完全放下姑娘。
In truth, Miao Zhiyu could not bear sitting there another minute watching Qiao Wenxian stare nonstop at the playing field. She wanted fresh air in the gardens by herself. Dropping a curtsy to the elders, she left without waiting for leave.

韦嬷嬷重点不在乔公子，很快聊起来：“不知两家的婚礼定在何时？”
A maid moved the stool beside Madam Qian, and Matron Wei accepted it. One glance at Young Master Qiao showed he paid zero heed to Lady Miao, his eyes locked onto the field—and right onto her own young mistress. So Young Master Qiao had not let go after all.

秦夫人急忙说：“亲家着急，又提前一周，定在三月初三。”
Matron Wei kept her focus on the elders and struck up conversation. "May I ask when the wedding between your two families has been set?"

“算算日子也快到，到时我们望月轩定会送上一份厚礼。”韦嬷嬷如此说，不过是想向众人表明自家姑娘已放下乔家公子。
"Our in-laws are eager," Madam Qin jumped in, "so they moved it up another week, to the third day of the third month."

除了乔文仙，其他人听到此话，都露出会心的笑容。
"That is right around the corner," Matron Wei said. "Wangyue Pavilion will be sure to send a generous gift." She said it solely to signal that her mistress had moved on from Young Master Qiao.

钱夫人瞧着场上正在捶丸的陈浩宇：“朱陈两家亲上加亲，羡煞旁人，今日我怎么瞧着，都觉朱家姑娘与陈家公子实在相配，也不知他们两个的婚事安排在什么时候？”
Aside from Qiao Wenxian, every person in the pavilion smiled knowingly.

陈浩宇的口碑并不好，说自家姑娘与其相配，不就是暗含着自家姑娘名声不好？
Madam Qian looked out at Chen Haoyu swinging on the field. "A second marriage tie between the Zhu and Chen families—everyone is green with envy. The more I look today, the better matched Miss Zhu and Young Master Chen seem. When have the two families set their wedding?"

韦嬷嬷甚是不满，依然礼貌地回应：“确实令人羡慕，我听说陈公子未来要接替陈家家业，所以陈家长辈希望他能娶一位品貌绝佳，才学一流的女子。朱家姑娘文武双全，是陈家打着灯笼都难找的，因此着急娶回去，日子也定在三月，不过在乔公子之后。”
Chen Haoyu's name was mud across Lin'an; calling her young mistress a fine match for him was a barbed dig at her reputation. Irritation flared in Matron Wei's chest, yet her tone stayed polished. "Envied indeed. I hear Young Master Chen is slated to inherit the Chen family business, so his elders insisted on a bride of peerless virtue, beauty, and learning. My young mistress excels with both brush and fist—the sort of treasure the Chens could search for with a lantern and still not find. That is why they are in such a rush to welcome her home. Their date is in the third month too, just after Young Master Qiao's."

三人听出些不满来。
The sting in her words did not go unnoticed.

钱氏自知失言：“这个春天真是喜事连连，到时乔家也会送份大礼。”
Realizing she had spoken out of turn, Madam Qian smoothed things over. "What a spring of double blessings! The Qiao family will send a grand gift when the time comes."

米姨娘紧接着说：“孩子们越来越好，我们听着也高兴，嬷嬷以后可要常与我和姐姐说说话，回到朱府告诉向夫人，我与姐姐甚是欢迎她，以后可要常来参加姐姐举办的活动。”
Concubine Mi jumped right in. "Hearing the children are thriving delights us all. Matron, you must drop by to chat with Sister and me more often, and when you return to Zhu Manor, tell Madam Xiang she is always welcome at Sister's gatherings."

韦嬷嬷不好再与乔家人计较：“那是自然，我会专程回趟朱府，将二位的话转达给我家夫人。”
With nothing more to gain by pressing them, Matron Wei nodded. "Of course. I will make a special trip back to Zhu Manor to pass your message along to my madam."

乔文仙坐在一旁，听着拉家常的话，听出些许意味来，心中不满，一句话不说，起身离开。
Sitting beside them, Qiao Wenxian caught every undercurrent of the polite fencing. Fed up, he stood and walked off without a word.

米氏大声问：“文渊，你去哪里？”
"Wenyuan, where are you going?" Concubine Mi called out.

乔文仙并不理会。
He ignored her.

米氏埋怨道：“这孩子！”
"That boy!" Concubine Mi grumbled.

韦嬷嬷和秦夫人在，不好揭儿子的短，钱氏急忙掩饰：“他还能去哪里？定是去找知瑜，不要管他，聊我们的。”
Unwilling to air her son's faults in front of Matron Wei and Madam Qin, Madam Qian covered for him at once. "Where else could he be going? Off to find Zhiyu, of course. Leave him be and let us keep talking."

钱氏的解释令秦夫人高兴：“文渊这孩子，刚跟我家知瑜分开就不习惯，婚前感情好，婚后感情会更好。”
The excuse thrilled Madam Qin. "Wenyuan cannot bear being parted from my Zhiyu even for a moment. So fond before the wedding, they will be closer still once married."

钱氏与米氏更是高兴不已，都“呵呵”笑起来。
Madam Qian and Concubine Mi chuckled along in delight.

韦嬷嬷觉得可笑，这三个人可真会自欺欺人。
Matron Wei shook her head inside. What a trio of master self-deceivers.
