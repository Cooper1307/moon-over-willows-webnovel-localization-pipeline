# Chapter 87: Coaxing Father Zhu Xiyan

---

Zhu Xiyan returned from the prefect's office with a dark expression. He sent his son Zhu Jingyuan to fetch Shuzhen.

---

When Zhu Jingyuan arrived at the small courtyard, Qianlixun was writing story scripts.

---

With such a loyal fan as the Prince of Yi, she felt more motivated to create. Saying her pen moved as if by magic was no exaggeration.

---

Even though she was rewriting content she had written before, novels were different from poems — too long to remember every sentence. Fortunately, she still remembered the characters and important plot points. She only needed to reorganize the language and write according to the format of story scripts.

---

She was deeply engrossed in writing when her brother came. Ever since the divorce, her brother had been busy going out all day, with no time to chat. She hadn't seen her sister-in-law or Laibao either. The Zhu residence was so large that sometimes even family members couldn't meet.

---

Qianlixun put down her brush, stepped forward, and took her brother's hand: "Brother, I was just wondering when to visit your courtyard to chat with you and sister-in-law, and see Laibao. I didn't expect you to come here."

---

Zhu Jingyuan joked: "When do you have time for us? Now your heart and eyes are only on Qiao Wenxian. You're busy dating him — don't think I don't know."

---

"Don't tease me, brother. How about I go with you now to see sister-in-law and Laibao?" With that, she tried to pull Zhu Jingyuan out of the garden gate.

---

Zhu Jingyuan held his sister back: "Not now. Father asked me to find you."

---

"Father wants to see me?" Qianlixun guessed that Lin Jue must have played the villain and filed a complaint first, crying to the prefect. "What does Father want with me?"

---

"I don't know." Zhu Jingyuan shook his head. "But it doesn't look good. Father's face is dark!"

---

"Then I'd better not go. Just tell Father I'm not here." Qianlixun sat back at the desk, picked up her brush, and was about to write again.

---

Zhu Jingyuan went over, took the brush from her hand, and put it away. Worried that Father would scold his sister, he didn't pay attention to what was written on the paper: "Running away won't solve the problem. You'd better go."

---

Qianlixun quickly turned the written paper face down: "Alright, I'll listen to you, brother."

---

The act of turning the paper over aroused Zhu Jingyuan's curiosity: "What are you writing? Afraid your brother will see it? It's not a love letter to Qiao Wenxian, is it?"

---

"Don't guess wildly, brother. Let's go."

---

Qianlixun pulled Zhu Jingyuan and walked out. Kuqiao covered the paper with a silk handkerchief and hurried to catch up with her young lady.

---

Zhu Xiyan had changed into casual home clothes and was sitting on a couch tasting tea. A servant came in to report: "Master, the young lady and young master are waiting outside."

---

"Let them in." Zhu Xiyan put down his tea cup.

---

Qianlixun followed her brother in. The two paid their respects to their father and, with his permission, sat on chairs to the side.

---

Zhu Xiyan asked: "Do you know why I called you here?"

---

"Your daughter doesn't know."

---

"Don't know? Think carefully about what outrageous things you've done today!"

---

"I acted according to reason today. What outrageous things have I done?"

---

With a loud slap, Zhu Xiyan hit the wooden table on the couch. It didn't scare Shuzhen — instead, it made his own hand hurt. Enduring the pain, Zhu Xiyan said sternly: "You rushed into the Lin residence and snatched Lin Jue's concubine. And you still have the nerve to say you acted according to reason?"

---

Zhu Jingyuan was frightened by his father and also worried about his sister.

---

Qianlixun wasn't afraid at all: "Father, why do you always listen to one side of the story from Lin Jue? You did verbally promise to give Xiaguo to Lin Jue as a concubine, but Xiaguo's deed of sale is in my hands. Who she follows from now on is up to me. Xiaguo doesn't want to, and I don't want to either. Lin Jue is abducting an innocent woman! You don't even know — when I arrived at the Lin residence, Xiaguo was trussed up with rope, her mouth stuffed with cloth. Is that something a good person would do? The way he treated Xiaguo, how could I dare give Xiaguo to him?"

---

Zhu Xiyan continued sternly: "I won't stop you from being good to your maid. But that day during the divorce, I promised Lin Jue in front of everyone that I would give Xiaguo to him. Are you trying to make me break my word?"

---

"Father, tell me — is Xiaguo's happiness more important, or is your face more important?"

---

Zhu Xiyan was angered again: "What do you mean by that? Am I, as the head of the family and the prefect of Lin'an, worth less than a maid in your eyes?"

---

Seeing this, Zhu Jingyuan helped explain: "Father, Shuzhen doesn't mean that. She's just too kind and doesn't want to see her maid wronged."

---

"Am I, her father, worth less than a maid in her eyes? For the sake of a maid, she talks back to me and wants me to break my word! I... I..." Zhu Xiyan grabbed his tea cup and threw it on the ground.

---

The tea cup shattered, tea splashing on the ground. The maids nearby were so frightened they didn't dare step forward to clean it up.

---

Seeing this, Qianlixun wasn't afraid. When she was little, whenever her father got angry, she would hug his neck and kiss his cheek. His anger would vanish immediately. She decided to try the same trick on Zhu Shuzhen's father.

---

She stood up, walked to Zhu Xiyan, hugged him, and coaxed: "Father, why are you so angry? I just said the wrong thing. Of course Father is important. Father's face and the Zhu family's face are both important." With that, she kissed Zhu Xiyan on the cheek.

---

Zhu Xiyan had never been treated this way by his daughter before. He felt very unaccustomed and told himself: "She's not the Shuzhen from before."

---

Regardless, his daughter's sudden intimate gesture made Zhu Xiyan's anger vanish on the spot: "Where did you learn this? Hurry up and sit back down."

---

"No, I'll sit back down only when Father isn't angry anymore." Qianlixun continued hugging Zhu Xiyan.

---

Zhu Xiyan pretended to be unhappy: "Let go of me, and I won't be angry."

---

Qianlixun let go of Zhu Xiyan and turned to wink at her brother to the side.

---

Zhu Jingyuan was thinking about his sister's change and admiring how extraordinary she was. She actually used the technique of overcoming the hard with the soft. What man could resist that? Especially her own father.

---

Receiving his sister's signal, Zhu Jingyuan immediately said: "Father, we're a family. You can't side with outsiders. Lin Jue used to be the Zhu family's son-in-law, but now he has nothing to do with us. There's no need for you to be angry with your own daughter for his sake."

---

Looking at her father's expression, which seemed unhappy but had relaxed considerably, Qianlixun said: "Brother is right. Not to mention Lin Jue is no longer the Zhu family's son-in-law — just his behavior, though not exactly a bad person, isn't anything good either. Why side with him? You should side with your daughter. If you fall ill, it's your daughter and son who will take care of you by your bedside. Lin Jue won't care about any of that."

---

"Hmph! If I'm bedridden, I can't count on you." Zhu Xiyan's tone had softened considerably.

---

Qianlixun emphasized: "How can you not count on me? I'm your own daughter. I'll definitely take care of Father with all my heart."

---

Zhu Xiyan finally showed a rare smile: "But after all, I promised Lin Jue in public. How do you expect me to save face?"

---

Seeing her father smile, Qianlixun became spirited, her mind even more agile: "Father doesn't need to worry. Your daughter has already thought it through. When Lin Jue comes to you again, you can scold your daughter however you want in front of him. When you get home, just go about your business. Leave the rest to me. This way, not only will you maintain your image as a prefect whose promise is worth a thousand gold, but it also won't cause tension between us father and daughter."

---

Zhu Xiyan thought for a moment and scolded: "You're full of tricky ideas. Go, don't bother me here. Just seeing you makes me angry."

---

Qianlixun still wanted to ask if Father agreed or not, but Zhu Jingyuan pulled her: "Father, my sister and I are leaving. Get some good rest."

---

Being pulled by her brother, Qianlixun asked as she walked out: "Father, do you agree or not?"

---

Zhu Xiyan didn't pay her any attention.

---

Leaving her father's courtyard, Qianlixun shook off her brother's hand: "Brother, why did you pull me? Father hasn't agreed yet."

---

"You were so smart just now, why are you being silly now? Father has already agreed. No need to ask again."

---

"What? Father agreed? But he didn't say anything agreeing. He just kept scolding me."

---

"The idea you proposed is obviously to have Father deceive Lin Jue together with you. What kind of person is Father? How could he engage in such tricks with you? Even if he does it, he can't say it directly. He stopped being angry and let you leave — isn't that agreement?"

---

Qianlixun thought carefully and realized it seemed to be the case: "No wonder not everyone can be an official. Their minds are so complex, their words so implicit. They use all this on their own daughter. Fortunately, I have such a smart brother. Otherwise, I would have forced Father to give me a clear answer today."

---

Zhu Jingyuan said: "Though your brother isn't an official, I often deal with officials. I've seen a lot, so naturally I understand."

---

Seeing that the matter with Father had passed safely without incident, Qianlixun was happy and didn't want to return to the small courtyard: "Brother, I want to go to your courtyard, chat with sister-in-law, and see Laibao."

---

"If you want to go, come with me."

---

"Brother, wait a moment." Qianlixun pulled Kuqiao aside and whispered: "You don't need to follow. Go back and deliver the new story scripts I wrote to the storyteller."

---

Kuqiao nodded in agreement. After her young lady and young master left, she headed toward the small courtyard.
