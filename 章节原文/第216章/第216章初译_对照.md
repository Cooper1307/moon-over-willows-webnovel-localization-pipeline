第216章 哄夏老太太高兴
## Chapter 216: Winning Over Old Madam Xia

过了两日，陈府的花匠已将墨香苑池塘里的淤泥和杂质清理干净，放些养料肥沃的泥土进去，将莲藕种好，只等生根发芽长出荷叶与荷花来。
一切都已按照自己的喜好布置完毕，在陈家也有了自己的财产，公爹和婆婆对她态度又好，也无人前来寻事，千里寻心情大好。
自回门那日的晚上，千里寻就没见过陈浩宇，他去哪里浪荡，她毫不关心，倒是关心如何搞定老太太。
﻿不如先将写话本之事暂搁一旁，想想哄老太太的招数。
穿越前的她从小身边没个奶奶，还真没有跟老人相处的经验，怎么个哄法，还需动动脑筋。
她想到陈浩宇见到老太太那个骚劲儿，惹得老太太满脸是笑，看来陈浩宇是用那个骚劲儿哄老太太开心。可千里寻从来没跟人这么说话过，那骚劲儿她可学不来。
苦荞从外面进来，发现小姐坐在书桌前没有提笔书写，而是用手撑着下巴沉思。
“小姐，您在想什么？”
千里寻放下撑着下巴的手：“你来得正好，帮我想想用什么办法能把老太太哄高兴？”
苦荞想想说：“以前听我爹说，人越老越喜欢热闹，小姐不如想个热闹的法子？”
千里寻十分认同：“没想到你这丫头挺聪明，想到这么好的主意。正好有个现成的办法，不如把说话艺人请来，给老太太说回书？”
苦荞为难道：“好是好，只怕他认出我来，每次给他送话本的人都是我。”
“让红豆去不就行了？说话艺人来了，你待在墨香苑，我把红豆带在身边伺候即可。”
“小姐的主意不错，我这就去找红豆。”
刚要迈出步子，苦荞又想起件事：“小姐，还有一事需征求您的意见。”
“什么事？”
“韦嬷嬷说让我教红豆做茶，也好给小姐身边再准备个伺候的人。”
“既然是韦嬷嬷说的，不用理会我的意思，你自己安排即可。”
Two days later, the manor gardeners finished dredging the silt and weeds from the Moxiang Courtyard pond, laid down rich soil, and planted fresh lotus roots to sprout into leaves and blossoms.

“是。”
With every corner arranged to her taste, personal assets in her name, kind in-laws, and nobody knocking on her door looking for a fight, Qianli Xun was in high spirits.

苦荞来到西院，见红豆正在训练下人练习拳法，青瓜在一旁照猫画虎学习，一招一式实在不成样子，苦荞被逗得直想笑。
She had not laid eyes on Chen Haoyu since the evening of their return visit to Zhu Manor, nor did she care where he gallivanted. What did occupy her mind was winning over Old Madam Xia.

红豆见苦荞前来，吩咐大家继续练，她则迎上去：“苦荞姐姐过来，该不会是小姐有什么吩咐？”
Setting her huaben aside for the moment, she racked her brains for a way to win the old lady over. Having grown up without a grandmother before her transmigration, she had zero experience coaxing elders. She thought of the shameless sweet-talking act Chen Haoyu put on to make Old Madam Xia beam, and shuddered. She had never talked like that to anyone in her life; fawning was out of the question.

“小姐让你去西三胡同找说话艺人，把他请到陈府，给老太太说书。”
Kuqiao walked in to find her mistress not writing, just propping her chin on one hand in deep thought. "What's on your mind, Miss?"

“好，我这就去。”
Qianli Xun lowered her hand. "Just in time. Help me figure out how to cheer up Old Madam Xia."

红豆对众位下人说：“大家先散了，各忙各的，明日再继续练。”
Kuqiao pondered a second. "My father used to say the older folks get, the more they love a lively crowd. Why not come up with something lively for her?"

众人散去，红豆也离开。青瓜没法再照猫画虎练习拳法，便追上苦荞。
Qianli Xun brightened at once. "Didn't realize you were this sharp! That's a great idea, and we have the perfect fix ready to go. Why don't we invite the storyteller over to perform for Old Madam Xia?"

“苦荞，你能不能帮我给少夫人说说，让她也教我拳法？”
"Sounds wonderful, except he might recognize me," Kuqiao worried. "I'm the one who delivers your manuscripts to him every time."

苦荞边走边说：“你为何要练拳法？”
"Send Hongdou instead! When the storyteller comes, you stay here in Moxiang Courtyard and I'll bring Hongdou along to attend me."

青瓜一脸委屈：“公子让我练，我要练不成他就把我撵到西院。”
"Smart plan, Miss. I'll go find Hongdou right now." Turning to go, Kuqiao paused. "Oh, one more thing I need to ask you about, Miss."

苦荞站住：“既是姑爷让你练，就让姑爷亲自去找我家小姐说，我可不管你的破事。”
"What is it?"

苦荞继续往前走，青瓜继续跟上，装作可怜的样子。
"Matron Wei told me to teach Hongdou how to prepare whisked tea, so you'll have another maid trained to attend you."

“怎么能算破事？你就帮帮我吧？你跟着少夫人来到陈家，应该也看到，公子他经常踢我，少夫人如果能教我几招，我起码还可以躲过公子的腿脚。”
"If Matron Wei suggested it, don't bother asking me. Go ahead and arrange it."

苦荞见青瓜既可笑又可怜，答应道：“好吧，我去给小姐说说，她答不答应就看你的造化。”
"Yes, Miss."

青瓜把苦荞送回素心草堂，自己则待在外面充满希望地等待。
Heading to the West Courtyard, Kuqiao found Hongdou drilling the household servants in Qianli Fist, with Qinggua trailing off to the side mimicking every stance with hilariously sloppy form. Kuqiao nearly burst out laughing.

苦荞进入后，见小姐正坐到茶桌前做茶，动作依然很粗苯。
Spotting her, Hongdou told the others to keep practicing and walked over. "Sister Kuqiao? Does Miss have an errand for me?"

“小姐想喝茶，我来做，您没有必要亲自动手。”
"Miss wants you to head to West Third Alley, find the storyteller, and invite him to Chen Manor to tell stories for Old Madam Xia."

“我想着老太太的事，心中不清静，一点儿写故事的欲望都没有，也无心看书，想用做茶打发时间。本以为天天见你做茶，怎么着也能学个七分像，结果还是做得一塌糊涂。”千里寻第一次对自己感到失望。
"Got it. I'll go right away." Hongdou turned back to the group. "Take a break, everyone! Back to your chores, and we'll train again tomorrow."

小姐的话正说到苦荞心里：“小姐若想找事情打发时间，正好有一事，您肯定感兴趣。”
The servants dispersed and Hongdou hurried off. Left with nobody to copy, Qinggua jogged after Kuqiao.

千里寻放下茶筅：“什么事？快快说来。”
"Kuqiao, could you put in a word with Young Madam and ask her to teach me martial arts too?"

“我刚去西院，小姐猜我看到什么？”
"Why do you want to learn?" Kuqiao asked as she walked.

“别卖关子，赶紧说。”
Qinggua put on a mournful look. "Young Master ordered me to learn. If I can't pull it off, he'll banish me to the West Courtyard."

“我看到青瓜跟着学习拳法，练得一点儿都不标准，动作实在可笑。刚刚我回来时，他求我给您说说教他拳法，我本来想拒绝，见他可怜，便应下。”
Kuqiao halted. "If the young master told you to learn, have him come ask my mistress himself. Don't drag me into your mess."

千里寻的确来了兴致，站起来：“他竟然想学拳法？实在好玩儿，可他一点儿根骨都没有，想学下辈子吧。不过我闲来无事，就拿他消磨消磨时间。”
She walked on, and Qinggua trailed right behind, piling on the pity. "How is it a mess? Help me out, please? You've seen how Young Master kicks me every chance he gets. If Young Madam teaches me a few moves, at least I can dodge his boots."

苦荞管不了那么多，只要小姐答应，她也算尽了人情。小姐出去后，她开始收拾做残的茶汤。
Half amused and half sympathetic, Kuqiao relented. "Fine, I'll mention it to Miss. Whether she says yes is up to your luck."

千里寻走出素心草堂，青瓜的确站在门外，伸长脖子向里张望。
Qinggua escorted her back to Suxin Thatched Hall and waited outside full of hope.

青瓜见少夫人出来，满脸堆笑：“少夫人是不是答应教我拳法？”
Inside, Kuqiao found her mistress seated at the tea table whisking tea with stiff, clumsy strokes.

千里寻将青瓜上上下下瞧一遍，问道：“你为何要学拳法？”
"If you want tea, Miss, let me make it. No need to tire yourself."

青瓜心想，公子与少夫人水火不容，千万不能提是公子让学：“我见少夫人十分厉害，上不怕老太太，中不怕老爷和夫人，下不怕我家公子，就想跟您学几招，不图别的，起码在公子踢我时，能迅速躲开。”
"I've been mulling over Old Madam Xia's business, and my mind isn't settled enough to write or read. Figured making tea would pass the time. After watching you do it every day, I thought I could pull off at least a decent imitation, yet it still came out a total wreck." For the first time, Qianli Xun felt disappointed in herself.

千里寻想到一招：“你呀，谈不上根骨，我的拳法根本学不会，即使学会招数，也只能学个皮毛而已。不过我正好有拳法之外的一招，对根骨没什么要求，你要是学了，保准陈浩宇怎么踢你都能躲开，只要你愿意还可还他一脚。”
Her words handed Kuqiao the perfect opening. "If you want something fun to pass the time, Miss, I have just the thing."

青瓜先是失望，本想着跟随少夫人学习好拳法，以后可以跟着公子出去耀武扬威，谁承想自己没根骨，看来他天生没有跟着公子出去混的福气。干脆退而求其次，学会一招是一招，起码还能自保。
Qianli Xun set down the tea whisk. "What is it? Spill."

“少夫人，我学，您就教我吧。”
"I just came from the West Courtyard. Guess what I saw?"

千里寻说：“踢我一脚。”
"Quit stalling and tell me."

青瓜吓得说道：“我怎么敢踢少夫人？”
"Qinggua was trying to follow along with the martial arts drills, flailing every which way like a clown. On the way back, he begged me to ask if you'd teach him. I meant to turn him down, yet he looked so pitiful I agreed to ask."

千里寻命令道：“让你踢就踢！不踢我怎么教你？”
Qianli Xun's eyes lit up and she stood. "He actually wants to learn martial arts? That's hilarious. He doesn't have a single bone made for fighting—he'd have to wait for his next life to master Qianli Fist. Still, I've got time to kill. Might as well use him for entertainment."

青瓜大胆伸出一脚向少夫人踢去……
Having done her favor, Kuqiao stayed behind to clean up the botched tea while her mistress stepped outside.

只见少夫人迅速向侧方滑步闪身，同时身体微侧，他踢出的脚仅从少夫人身边掠过，接着他的身体出现短暂失衡。少夫人则迅速靠近他，用手拨开他的腿，用另一只手扭住他的手臂。
Sure enough, Qinggua stood right beyond the threshold, craning his neck indoors. The instant she appeared, he broke into a wide grin. "Young Madam, did you agree to teach me martial arts?"

“这招叫幻影闪移。”千里寻放开青瓜，“可记住招数？”
Qianli Xun looked him up and down. "Why do you want to learn?"

青瓜摇摇头：“没看明白。”
Knowing full well the bad blood between his master and mistress, Qinggua made sure not to mention Chen Haoyu's order. "I see how impressive you are, Young Madam—unafraid of Old Madam Xia above, Master and Madam in the middle, or Young Master below. I just want to pick up a few tricks so I can dodge when Young Master kicks me."

千里寻又让青瓜踢来，如此反复，演示了不下七八次，青瓜终于入门。她喊来阿升，让阿升带青瓜到西院帮着练习。
An idea clicked in Qianli Xun's head. "You don't have the build or aptitude for my fist style. Even if you memorized the forms, you'd only scratch the surface. Lucky for you, I know one standalone move that needs zero martial talent. Learn it, and no matter how Chen Haoyu kicks at you, you'll slip right past—and even kick him back if you feel bold."

刚安排好，红豆从外面回来。
Disappointment flickered over Qinggua's face; he had dreamed of mastering real kung fu and swaggering down the street behind his master. No such luck. Settling for second best, one self-defense move beat getting bruised black and blue. "I'll learn it, Young Madam! Teach me!"

“小姐，西三胡同的说话艺人已请来，在院外等候。”
"Kick me," Qianli Xun ordered.

“你带上说话艺人随我去找老太太。”
Qinggua paled. "How could I dare kick you, Young Madam?"

红豆很愿意跟着小姐，可平日这种事只有苦荞有机会，不由问道：“苦荞姐姐不去吗？”
"I told you to kick, so kick! How else can I demonstrate?"

“我给她安排了其他事，今日由你来陪。”
Steeling his nerve, Qinggua aimed a kick straight at her.

小姐边说边向外走，红豆赶紧跟上。
In a flash, Qianli Xun slid sideways, angling her torso so his boot sliced empty air right past her hip. The momentum pulled him off balance for a split second—all she needed to step inside, brush his leg aside with one hand, and lock his arm behind his back with the other.

说话艺人见主人家出来，上前拜见：“少夫人，不知让鄙人在何处表演？”
"This move is called Phantom Sidestep," she said, releasing him. "Catch all that?"

“跟着走便是。”
Qinggua shook his head blankly. "Moved too fast for me."

千里寻在前面带路，红豆带着说话艺人跟在后面。
She had him kick at her seven or eight more times until he grasped the footwork, then called Asheng over to drill him in the West Courtyard.

来到老太太的院外，千里寻让红豆与说话艺人在外面候着，她一个人先进去。
No sooner had she settled that than Hongdou returned. "Miss, I've brought the storyteller from West Third Alley. He's waiting outside the courtyard."

丫鬟们正扶着夏老太太在院子里散步，刚走出没几步，见孙媳妇进来，生气道：“太不懂礼数，没有通报就进来？还号称什么江南女词人！”
"Bring him along. We're going to Old Madam Xia's."

本来想学着陈浩宇说话，都已做好心理建设，被老太太没好气的话语击打得一点儿心情都没有。
Glad for the chance to accompany her mistress—a duty that usually went to Kuqiao—Hongdou asked, "Isn't Sister Kuqiao coming?"

“我是江南女词人，心胸自然比他人宽广，奶奶瞧我不顺眼，我不跟您计较，今日我带来一人，让您这冷清的院子也热闹热闹。”
"I gave her another task. You're with me today." Qianli Xun led the way out, with Hongdou trailing behind.

千里寻并不理会老太太，开始指挥丫鬟：“你们两个去搬张桌子来，你去搬把椅子。”
Spotting the young mistress emerge, the storyteller bowed low. "Young Madam, where would you like me to perform?"

一旁的丫鬟见老太太虽生气，却不言语，便知老太太并不反对，一个个忙碌起来。
"Just follow me."

桌椅摆好后，千里寻亲自去扶老太太：“奶奶，您再对我不满，今日把您肚子里的气先放一放，我扶您坐下。”
Qianli Xun walked ahead while Hongdou guided the storyteller behind her. At the gate of Old Madam Xia's courtyard, Qianli Xun told them to wait outside and entered alone.

孙媳妇前来，安排这，安排那，院子里变得热闹，老太太心里高兴，嘴上却不饶人：“那我就坐下，看你能耍什么花招。”
Maids were helping Old Madam Xia stroll around the yard. Barely a few steps in, the old lady spotted her granddaughter-in-law and barked, "What utter lack of manners, barging in without being announced! And they call you the Poetess of Jiangnan!"

扶老太太坐下后，千里寻向外面喊道：“红豆，把说话艺人带进来。”
Qianli Xun had hyped herself up to try a softer tone like Chen Haoyu's, only for that sharp rebuke to puncture her resolve on the spot. "Since I am the Poetess of Jiangnan, my tolerance runs wider than most. Even if you find me an eyesore, Grandmother, I won't hold it against you. I brought someone today to liven up this quiet courtyard of yours."

听到“说话艺人”的名称，老太太已装不下去，嘴角露出笑容。
Ignoring the old lady's scowl, Qianli Xun directed the maids on the spot. "You two, carry a table out here. You, fetch a chair."

千里寻用余光瞥到，心想：“这么快将你搞定，以后我在陈府日子就好过了。”
Seeing Old Madam Xia glare without stopping her, the maids realized she did not mind and hopped to work.

红豆把说话艺人带进来。
Once the table and chair stood ready, Qianli Xun stepped up to take the old lady's arm. "No matter how upset you are with me, Grandmother, set your temper aside for today and sit."

那说话艺人见到桌子倍感亲切，自然走过去，从褡裢里取出惊堂木和折扇放在桌上，再拿出紫砂壶，对一旁的丫鬟说：“麻烦，给沏壶茶。”
Watching her granddaughter-in-law bustle around ordering things set up made the courtyard feel alive, warming Old Madam Xia's heart even as her mouth stayed sharp. "Fine, I'll sit and see what trick you're pulling."

老太太脸上的笑容越来越多，主动说：“给先生沏壶好茶。”
Once the old madam was settled, Qianli Xun called toward the gate, "Hongdou, bring the storyteller in!"

丫鬟应答后，接过茶壶去忙活。
At the word "storyteller," Old Madam Xia's stern mask cracked, and the corners of her mouth curved up.

老太太瞅一眼站在身旁的孙媳妇：“你也坐下吧。”
Catching that flicker out of the corner of her eye, Qianli Xun grinned inside. That easy to win over? Smooth sailing in Chen Manor from here on out.

旁边的丫鬟听后，赶紧又去搬把椅子。
Hongdou ushered the storyteller inside. Sighting the table, he looked right at home, pulling his wooden clapper and folding fan from his cloth satchel, followed by a purple clay teapot. He handed the pot to a nearby maid. "Trouble you for a fresh brew of tea, miss."

千里寻坐下说：“奶奶，说话艺人说的是千里寻才子写的故事，您听了肯定喜欢。”
Old Madam Xia's smile widened. "Brew the master our finest tea."

“让你费心。”老太太说话的语气柔和许多。
The maid hurried off with the pot. Old Madam Xia glanced at Qianli Xun standing beside her. "You sit down too."

紫砂壶端上后，说话艺人先抿一口，丫鬟沏的茶不仅口感好，温度也合适，不烫嘴。他十分满意，放下紫砂壶：“谁不知陈府上下气象万千，富贵荣华满堂彩，老太太您是福寿双全，德高望重……”
Another maid scrambled off for a second chair.

说话艺人才说几句，被夏老太太笑着阻止：“行了，别说奉承话，赶快说故事，只要你说得好，我自然打赏。”
Taking her seat, Qianli Xun said, "Grandmother, the tale he's telling today was written by Master Qianli Xun. You're guaranteed to love it."

说话艺人说：“得了，听老太太您的。”拿起惊堂木一拍：
"Thoughtful of you," Old Madam Xia said, her voice much softer.

话说南天边有座琉璃塔，塔里困着一只玉面狐狸，足足困了上千年。那狐狸倒没闲着，以塔为家，以塔气为食，以塔土为衣，渐渐养得塔里塔气……
When the tea came back, the storyteller took a sip—fragrant and just the right temperature. Satisfied, he set the pot down and launched into his opener. "Who doesn't know that Chen Manor brims with splendor, wealth, and honor overflowing the halls, and that Old Madam Xia enjoys both fortune and longevity, revered by all—"

夏老太太被逗乐：“呵呵，第一次听‘塔里塔气’的说法，实在可乐。”
Old Madam Xia cut him off with a laugh. "Skip the flattery and get to the story. Tell it well, and I'll reward you handsomely."

说话艺人继续讲：
"Your wish is my command, Old Madam!" He slapped his wooden clapper sharp against the tabletop.

南天边有一神仙，名唤玄知，每日修行结束，都会盯着琉璃塔。玄知乃真神，目光如炬，充满神力。琉璃塔每日被真神注目，逐渐吸收神力。神力越积越多，琉璃塔无法忍受，只听南天边一阵巨响：“轰！”，琉璃塔被震成碎片。玉面狐狸终得自由，接触到一股仙气，化为一个女子，因长期待在塔内，满身灰尘，蓬头垢面。
"Now, at the southern edge of heaven stood a Glazed Pagoda, trapping a jade-faced fox spirit for a full thousand years. That fox didn't sit idle—she made the pagoda her home, fed on pagoda mist, dressed in pagoda dust, until every bit of her reeked of pure pagoda-ishness..."

这时真神玄知出现，女子被吓坏：“你谁呀？长得挺好看，就是脸看着不够喜庆。”
Old Madam Xia burst out laughing. "Heh! First time I've heard 'pagoda-ishness'! What a hoot!"

玄知将仙袖一挥，女子身上的灰尘全部消失，立刻变成一个美人。玄知说：“我救了你，你以后就是我的人。”
The storyteller rolled right on.

女子变得漂亮虽开心，可被玄知的话给气到：“凭什么说你救了我？我可是什么都没看到。”说完女子准备逃走，被玄知用仙力拉回……
"At the southern edge of heaven also dwelt a true deity named Xuanzhi. Every day after his cultivation, he gazed upon the Glazed Pagoda. Because Xuanzhi was a supreme god, his piercing gaze carried immense divine power. Day after day, the pagoda soaked up that energy until it could hold no more. With a thunderous boom across the southern skies, the Glazed Pagoda shattered to pieces! Free at last, the jade-faced fox touched a wisp of immortal aura and took human shape as a young woman—though after a millennium indoors, she stood coated in dust, unkempt and disheveled. "Right then, Xuanzhi appeared. Startled out of her wits, the girl demanded, 'Who are you? Decent-looking face, just way too grim and uncheerful!' "Xuanzhi swept one immortal sleeve, whisking every speck of dust off her and turning her into a stunning beauty on the spot. 'I saved you,' Xuanzhi announced. 'From now on, you belong to me.' "Delighted to look gorgeous yet furious at his nerve, the girl snapped back, 'Says who you saved me? I didn't see a thing!' With that, she turned to bolt, only for Xuanzhi to reel her straight back with his divine power..."

老太太边听边笑，自从儿子们成家，还没有人让自己这么开心过。
Old Madam Xia laughed nonstop. Not since her sons had grown up and started their own families had anyone made her this happy.

没过多久，说话艺人来陈府说书的事传开，陈浩飞听说，也跑来听，接着二婶、三婶和叶安雅也来到，院子里变得更热闹，老太太别提有多开心。
Word soon spread that a storyteller was performing inside Chen Manor. Chen Haofei hurried over to listen, followed by Second Aunt Madam Bao, Third Aunt Madam Cui, and Ye Anya. The courtyard filled with chatter and laughter, and Old Madam Xia could not have been more thrilled.
