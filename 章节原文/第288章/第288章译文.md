## Chapter 288: Second Young Master Cleared

Catching Haodong's microscopic nod across the courtroom, Scarface gritted his teeth for his hundred-tael payday and took the fall himself: confessing he'd slipped Sleep-Sage Powder into the steward's wine, sent his buddies off, and claiming Jinlan had *consensually* slept with him before he got greedy and sold her to Yangzhou—denying any black-cloaked man existed!

Shen Jianchen saw straight through the glaring holes—consensual lovers don't drug stewards or scream for murder!—yet without hard proof linking the black cloak to Haodong, at least Chen Haoyu stood completely cleared.

While everyone digested Scarface's twisted confession, Chun Jinlan spoke in dead calm: "If Chen Haoyu won't take me as concubine, I'll die right on this courtroom floor!"

Used to her empty threats, nobody—not even Wen Xin—moved a muscle, while Haodong watched with secret glee.

In one flash, Jinlan whipped Haodong's hidden dagger from her sleeve and plunged it straight into her own chest! Crumpling as stunned gasps tore through the hall, she locked fading eyes onto rushing Wen Xin and breathed her last bewildered question—"Why... didn't you save me?"—before going still forever.

Horrified guilt crushed Haoyu; innocent of the rape, he had still lit the fuse by kidnapping her.

Declaring Chen Haoyu acquitted and remanding Scarface for deeper interrogation, Shen Jianchen accepted Chen Jianglin's pledge to bury Jinlan decently and support her family—then cleared the courtroom except for the Chens and fixed cold eyes straight onto Chen Haodong.

"Will Eldest Young Master confess himself—or shall I tell your uncle for you?"

*THUD!* Haodong dropped onto his knees weeping crocodile tears, forcing kindhearted Chen Jianglin to swear forgiveness *before* spinning a saintly tale of bumping into poor Jinlan outside the country villa and "foolishly" advising her to confront Chen Manor out of pity for both her and Haoyu!

Unable to break his promise of pardon, Chen Jianglin could only sigh heavily, and guilt-ridden Haoyu even excused his cousin—leaving Shen Jianchen scoffing at Haodong's Oscar-worthy performance as he dismissed the family home.
