第288章 无罪释放二公子
## Chapter 288: Second Young Master Cleared

疤子抬头向陈浩东瞥一眼，陈浩东点了一下头，迅速将目光移到别处。
疤子心想：“一百两银子不少，不妨拼一拼。”
于是疤子决定承担下所有罪过：“去年三月初三的晚上，二公子的确没回宅子，春金兰也不是他糟蹋的。是我……是我见春金兰美貌，趁二公子不在想要与她亲热，怕其他兄弟看着眼馋先将他们支走，又担心管家发现，给酒里放了些睡圣散，他没喝几杯就睡着……”
听到这些，春金兰怒不可遏，打算向疤子扑去，被差役按住，只能大声吼道：“你胡说！明明是陈浩宇！”
疤子瞎编道：“我没胡说，你开始不愿意，我告诉你只要从了我，我会悄悄放你回家，以后也会照顾你的生活，你想通后便答应我。大人，我与她之间只能算是你情我愿。完事后，我又想多赚些银子，才又起歹心，将她打晕，卖到扬州青楼。”
再怒吼已经无用，被差役押着，春金兰没有机会掏出匕首以死威胁，只能暂且作罢，努力忍着，希望差役见她安静能很快放开她。
申简辰算是听出来，疤子想护住那位穿黑色披风的人：“穿黑色披风的人又是怎么回事？”
疤子继续编道：“没有此人，是丫鬟胡说。”
Scarface darted a glance at Chen Haodong, who gave one microscopic nod and looked away.

阿叶强调道：“我没有胡说，明明亲眼看到。”
*A hundred taels of silver—worth the gamble!*

疤子说：“天黑看错也正常。”
Taking the whole fall onto his own shoulders, Scarface blurted, “Second Young Master never came back that night, and he never touched Chun Jinlan! It was me! Saw how pretty she was, got itchy while Young Master was away, sent the boys off so they wouldn’t get jealous, and slipped Sleep-Sage Powder into the caretaker’s wine so he’d pass out...”

差役见春金兰老实许多，放开手站回原位。春金兰趁众人注意疤子之时，手伸入袖中，摸牢匕首。
Fury consumed Chun Jinlan. Lunging at Scarface before constables pinned her down, she screamed, “Liar! It was Chen Haoyu!”

申简辰心想，疤子的话矛盾重重，若真与春金兰你情我愿又何必使那么大力气？又是支人，又是睡圣散？阿叶听到喊叫声，显然也不是你情我愿该有的声音。若调走宅子的下人与此事相关，那位穿黑色披风之人定与疤子有勾结，而且此人一定是陈家人。陈浩宇已被排除在外，陈浩飞更与此事无关，一切迹象指向陈浩东，只是苦于没有证据。
Scarface spun his tale smooth. “Not lying! You fought at first, till I swore I’d sneak you home and look after you if you gave in—so you agreed! Your Lordship, it was consensual! Only afterward I got greedy for silver, knocked her out, and sold her to Yangzhou!”

申简辰又觉疤子实在狡诈，硬生生把强奸罪说成你情我愿，历来男女之事最不好断定，尤其对这种没有留下实锤证据的案子。好在已证明陈浩宇无罪，算是替千里寻解决一件忧虑之事。
Pinned tight, Chun Jinlan couldn’t reach her sleeve. Biting back her rage, she went limp, waiting for the constables to loosen their grip.

“你刚才还说穿黑色披风的人是陈浩宇，现在为何否认没有此人？”
Shen Jianchen saw straight through Scarface’s cover-up for the cloaked man. “And the man in the black cloak?”

“大人，小人前面说的都是假话，现在句句属实。”
“Never existed—the maid made him up!”

听了疤子的话，陈浩东又轻松许多。
“I didn’t make it up!” Aye cried. “I saw him with my own eyes!”

其他人沉浸在思考疤子的话哪句是真，哪句是假，以及穿黑色披风的人到底谁，并未注意到春金兰的变化。
“Easy to see shadows in the dark,” Scarface shot back.

直到春金兰说：“陈浩宇若不纳我为妾，我就死在公堂之上！”
Seeing Chun Jinlan calm down, the constables stepped back to their posts. While all eyes stayed on Scarface, Chun Jinlan slid her hand into her sleeve and wrapped her fingers around the dagger hilt.

春金兰张口闭口将死挂在嘴边，除了陈浩东其他人都未当回事，想着她只是说说而已，连差役也十分不屑。既是陈浩东的计谋，他更希望如此，自然不会阻拦。
Shen Jianchen spotted the holes in Scarface’s story a mile away: consensual, yet he sent his crew away, drugged the caretaker, and Aye heard terrified screams? Tie that to the transferred staff, and the cloaked man had to be Scarface’s accomplice—a Chen family insider. Haoyu was cleared, Haofei had no motive, every finger pointed straight at Chen Haodong—yet without proof, his hands were tied. Crafty rat that Scarface was, twisting rape into “consensual” made proving a capital charge impossible with no physical evidence. At least Chen Haoyu was cleared, lifting a huge stone off Qianli Xun’s chest.

谁知春金兰话音刚落，从袖中抽出匕首，刺向自己的胸前。当匕首真的被她刺入时，她盯着温信，心中疑惑，他为何不救她？还未搞清楚时，已向下倒去……
“A minute ago you swore the cloaked man was Chen Haoyu—now you claim he never existed?”

周围人被眼前的景象惊住。
“I was lying before, Your Lordship—every word now is gospel truth!”

申简辰看向陈浩东，他好像有种如释重负之感。
Relief washed over Chen Haodong. Everyone else sat mulling over the contradictions—missing the sudden shift in Chun Jinlan.

温信因未将春金兰的话当回事，慢了一步，等他冲到跟前，春金兰已倒在地上。他蹲下，把手指放在鼻息前，还有气息。
Until she cried out, “If Chen Haoyu won’t take me as his concubine, I’ll die right here in court!”

春金兰努力睁开眼，用几乎听不到的声音说：“你……为何……不救我？”
Having heard her cry wolf since yesterday, nobody took her seriously—not even the constables. And since Chen Haodong had planted the idea, he sure wasn’t stopping her.

春金兰使尽最后的力气说完心中疑惑，咽气而亡。
Before the echo faded, Chun Jinlan whipped the dagger from her sleeve and drove it straight into her own chest. As cold steel sank home, her eyes locked onto Wen Xin in stunned disbelief—*why didn’t he save me?*—before her knees gave out and she crumpled.

闹出人命，陈浩宇更是悔恨不已，要是当初没有将春金兰抓到宅子里，就没有后来的事，他虽无罪，却良心难安。
Gasps froze the hall. Shen Jianchen shot a look at Chen Haodong—catching a unmistakable flash of relief across his face.

温信吩咐两名差役将春金兰的尸体抬出大堂。
Caught half a step slow because he hadn’t believed her, Wen Xin reached her side as she hit the floor and checked her nose—still breathing faintly.

疤子认罪，春金兰自杀而亡，真正的罪人还逍遥法外，此事并未真正结束，但也需告一段落。
Forcing her eyelids open, Chun Jinlan whispered barely above a breath, “Why... didn’t you... save me?” With that final puzzled question, her breath stopped.

申简辰宣布道：“本案虽未完全了结，案情却已有眉目，陈家二公子陈浩宇无罪，将疤子暂且收押，等待司理院进一步审理。”
Blood on the floor crushed Chen Haoyu with remorse. Innocent of the crime, sure—yet if he hadn’t snatched her from that creek, she’d still be alive.

温信带着差役将疤子押了出去。
Wen Xin ordered two constables to carry the body out.

陈浩宇从地上爬起来，他无罪释放，却高兴不起来，刚才的一幕搞得他心情沉重，不想说一句话。
With Scarface confessing, Chun Jinlan dead, and the real mastermind still walking free, the case wasn’t over—yet this chapter had to close.

陈江林带着两个侄儿上前行礼：“感谢仪王替我儿洗去冤情，春金兰之事多少与浩宇有关，陈家愿承担她身后之事，也愿帮她安顿好家人。”
“While loose ends remain, the core facts stand clear,” Shen Jianchen announced. “Second Young Master Chen Haoyu is acquitted. Hold Scarface in custody pending further inquiry.”

“陈老爷此举也算是对春金兰有个交代。”申简辰说完，面向一旁的韩谷，“麻烦韩师爷带人退下，我有事与陈老爷和陈家公子说。”
Wen Xin and his constables hauled Scarface out. Chen Haoyu climbed to his feet, cleared of all charges yet too sick at heart to crack a smile.

韩谷应声“是”，将差役和证人带出大堂。
Chen Jianglin stepped forward with his two nephews and bowed. “Thank you for clearing my son’s name, Your Highness. Since Haoyu played a part in Chun Jinlan’s tragedy, the Chen family will pay for her burial and provide for her kin.”

申简辰看向陈浩东，强烈的第六感告诉他，穿黑色披风之人就是陈家大公子。唯一的证人疤子不肯道出实情，揽下所有罪责，除了继续审问疤子，动用刑罚逼他说出实情外，别无他法，而眼下只能放陈浩东一马。可他撺掇春金兰一事已是实锤，不如先让他在此事上吃些苦头。
“A decent gesture toward the dead, Master Chen.” Turning to Clerk Han, Shen Jianchen added, “Take the constables and witnesses outside. I need a word with Master Chen and the young masters.”

“大公子是打算亲口说呢？还是让本王替你说？”
Han Gu cleared the hall.

仪王的做法本就令陈江林疑惑，他的话更是令他不解。接着大侄子“扑通”一声跪在他面前，更是令他错愕，一时惊得忘记说话。
Shen Jianchen fixed his eyes on Chen Haodong. Every instinct screamed he was the cloaked man, yet with Scarface eating the blame, he’d have to break Scarface in interrogation later. Still, Chen Haodong’s meddling in bringing Chun Jinlan to Chen Manor was already proven—high time he tasted some heat.

陈浩宇依旧沉浸在后悔中，懒得关注发生的一切。
“Will Eldest Young Master tell him yourself, or shall I tell him for you?”

陈浩飞则惊讶地问：“东哥，你这是怎么了？为何要给大伯跪下？”
Before baffled Chen Jianglin could even process the question, his eldest nephew dropped flat to his knees with a loud thump, stunning him speechless. Haoyu stayed lost in guilt, while Haofei stared in shock. “Brother Dong, what’s going on? Why kneel to Eldest Uncle?”

陈江林这才说：“浩东，有话起来说，不必下跪。”
“Get up and speak, Haodong,” Chen Jianglin said. “No need to kneel.”

陈浩东声泪俱下：“大伯！我做了错事，您若不原谅侄儿，侄儿绝不起来，呜呜……！”
Tears poured down Chen Haodong’s cheeks. “Uncle! I did wrong! Until you forgive me, I’m not getting up! Waaah!”

申简辰心中骂道：“事儿还没说，就逼着别人原谅你，实在不地道！”
*Forcing forgiveness before even confessing—shameless!* Shen Jianchen fumed inside.

陈江林劝道：“在陈家小辈中你出类拔萃，从未做过错事，今日为何要如此说？”
“You’re the best of our younger generation and never put a foot wrong,” Chen Jianglin coaxed. “What’s all this?”

“呜呜，大伯先答应不怪我，我才说，呜呜……”
“Promise you won’t blame me first, Uncle, or I can’t say it!”

“好，我答应不责怪你。”
“All right, I promise I won’t blame you.”

陈浩东收住哭声：“有一次我去乡下的宅子，见春金兰在宅子外张望，上前训斥两句，她把我当成浩宇，向我袭击而来，还好从宅子里出来几名下人，将春金兰控制住，我告诉她我是浩宇的大哥，她才将她的经历告诉我。她实在太可怜，侄儿不由心生怜悯，加之一心认为是浩宇所为，想着如果她跟了浩宇，有好日子过，浩宇也不用担任何罪责，便给她出了到陈府闹的法子。都是我的错，我不该帮她，呜呜……”
Turning off the sobs on a dime, Chen Haodong spun his yarn. “One day at the country house, I caught Chun Jinlan peeking around outside. I scolded her, she mistook me for Haoyu and attacked, and servants restrained her. Once she learned I was Haoyu’s cousin, she told me her tragic story. My heart went out to her—and thinking Haoyu really did it, I figured if she joined his household, she’d have a good life and Haoyu would avoid prosecution, so I suggested she make a scene at Chen Manor. All my fault for trying to help!”

陈江林心情复杂，很想骂浩东两句，可已答应不责怪他，又一想只怪浩东太善良，要说错也是浩宇有错在先。陈江林只能用深深的一声叹息来回应：“唉！”
Stuck by his own promise—and blaming Haodong’s soft heart plus Haoyu’s own recklessness—Chen Jianglin could only let out a heavy sigh.

陈浩宇自责道：“东哥，你起来吧，此事不能怪你，都是我的错，我不该去乡下乱抢姑娘。”
Chen Haoyu spoke up in self-reproach. “Get up, Brother Dong—none of this is on you. I’m the idiot who went snatching girls in the countryside.”

申简辰看不下去，陈浩东演戏的水平实在高明：“陈大公子想哭，还是回到陈家，跪在长辈面前好好哭一场。”
Disgusted by the masterclass in theater, Shen Jianchen remarked dryly, “If Eldest Young Master wants a good cry, save it for kneeling in front of your elders at home.”

陈江林认为仪王是提醒他家事还需回家处理，行礼道：“让仪王见笑，我这就带陈家晚辈回去处理家事。”
Taking the cue to settle dirty laundry behind closed doors, Chen Jianglin bowed. “Forgive the spectacle, Your Highness. I’ll take the boys home to sort out family affairs.”

申简辰说：“陈老爷请便。”
“Go right ahead, Master Chen.”

陈浩飞扶起东哥，兄弟三人拜谢过仪王后，跟随陈江林离开司理院。
Chen Haofei helped his cousin up, and the three young men bowed to Prince Yi before trailing Chen Jianglin out.
