# 第55章 朱府宴会露真情
# Chapter 55: True Feelings Revealed at the Zhu Family Banquet

---

千里寻跟随母亲和嫂嫂走向迎宾处。

Qianli Xun followed her mother and sister-in-law toward the Reception Area.

---

迎宾处设在花园的长廊入口，长廊由几十根柱子撑着，既结实又宽敞，中间一个亭子，将长廊分为两部分。一部分摆上一排桌案，上面放置笔墨纸砚；另一部分正是迎宾处，铺有红色毯子，一直铺到亭中。亭子两侧也安排有数名丫鬟，恭敬地站立着，随时等待主人或宾客吩咐。

The Reception Area was set up at the entrance of the Long Corridor in the garden. The corridor, supported by dozens of pillars, was both sturdy and spacious. A pavilion in the middle divided it into two sections. One section had a row of tables with the Four Treasures of the Study—brush, ink, paper, and inkstone. The other section was the Reception Area itself, covered with a red carpet that extended all the way into the pavilion. Several maidservants stood respectfully on both sides of the pavilion, ready to serve the host or guests at any moment.

---

千里寻悄悄问陈文心：“嫂嫂，爹举办宴会用的都是朱家的银子吗？”

Qianli Xun whispered to Chen Wenxin: "Sister-in-law, does Father use the Zhu family's silver for all these banquets?"

---

“要用朱家的银子，朱家岂不是亏死？当然是府衙给拨的银两，只不过借朱府举办而已。”

“If the Zhu family had to pay for it, they'd be ruined! Of course it's the prefecture's funds—they just use the Zhu residence as the venue.”

---

陈文心讲完，已有人前来，三人赶紧笑脸相迎。

After Chen Wenxin finished explaining, guests began to arrive. The three of them quickly put on welcoming smiles.

---

首先到来的是苗知瑜与母亲秦夫人；接着是东方尘泽，身后跟着化雨；再接着，曹班跟随父亲曹经略使而来，安明轩则跟随父亲安提举和母亲前来。然后又有许多官员和家眷面带笑容走来。

First to arrive were Miao Zhiyu and her mother Madam Qin. Then came Dongfang Chenze, followed by Huayu. Next, Cao Ban arrived with his father Military Commissioner Cao, while An Mingxuan came with his father Intendant An and his mother. After that, many more officials and their families approached with smiles on their faces.

---

大家一一与向夫人、陈文心和幽栖居士行礼打招呼后，穿过长廊向宴饮之处而去，每个人都一派喜气。

Everyone greeted Madam Xiang, Young Madam Chen, and Youqi Jushi one by one, then passed through the corridor toward the banquet area, each person radiating joy.

---

千里寻又小声问嫂嫂：“父亲可请了乔家？”

Qianli Xun whispered to her sister-in-law again: "Did Father invite the Qiao family?"

---

陈文心悄悄回道：“当然要请，乔家可是皇亲国戚。”

Chen Wenxin whispered back: "Of course! The Qiao family is imperial relatives, after all."

---

千里寻心中偷乐，昨日与文渊见过一面就爱上他，没想到今日又能见到。即使不说话，只看对方一眼便心满意足，喜欢一个人的感觉竟然如此美好。

Qianli Xun secretly rejoiced. She had fallen in love with Wenyuan after meeting him just once yesterday, and she never expected to see him again today. Even without speaking, just one glance at him was enough to satisfy her. The feeling of liking someone was truly wonderful.

---

千里寻忽然觉得自己在另一个时代不谈恋爱实在是白白浪费了三十年的光阴。

Qianli Xun suddenly felt that not falling in love in her previous life had been a waste of thirty years.

---

正想着，心中所念之人向她走来。文渊在人群中如此出挑，让人不得不将目光聚焦在他的身上。

Just as she was thinking this, the person she was thinking of walked toward her. Wenyuan stood out so much in the crowd that people couldn't help but focus their attention on him.

---

千里寻既紧张又娇羞，她怨自己太没出息，有什么好害羞的？在另一个时代活了三十年，在这个时代连婚姻都有，怎么还跟个怀春少女一般？可依然忍不住红了脸。

Qianli Xun felt both nervous and shy. She scolded herself for being so useless—what was there to be embarrassed about? She had lived thirty years in another era, and in this era she was even married, so why was she still like a lovesick young girl? Yet she couldn't help blushing.

---

传来文渊温暖而充满磁性的声音：“向夫人、少夫人、幽栖居士，文渊这厢有礼。”

Wenyuan's warm, magnetic voice reached them: “Madam Xiang, Young Madam, Youqi Jushi—Wenyuan offers his greetings.”

---

千里寻跟着嫂嫂还礼，抬头的一刻正好与文渊四目相对，她的内心竟然怦怦乱跳。

Qianli Xun followed her sister-in-law in returning the greeting. The moment she looked up, her eyes met Wenyuan's, and her heart began to pound wildly.

---

向夫人问：“乔老爷和夫人怎么没来？”

Madam Xiang asked: "Why didn't Master Qiao and Madam Qiao come?"

---

乔文仙收回充满爱意的眼神：“今日家中有人上门提亲，家父与家母无法前来。”

Qiao Wenxian withdrew his loving gaze: "Someone came to seek a bride from our family today, so my father and mother couldn't make it."

---

听到提亲，千里寻一阵担心，难道是向文渊提亲？又一想，不对，这个时代一般都是男方到女方家里提亲，估计文渊有什么妹妹或姐姐之类。

Hearing about the marriage proposal, Qianli Xun felt a wave of worry—was someone proposing to Wenyuan? Then she thought again: no, in this era, it was usually the man who went to the woman's family to propose. Wenyuan probably had a younger sister or older sister or something like that.

---

向夫人继续问：“可是你妹妹的亲事？”

Madam Xiang continued to ask: "Is it for your sister's marriage?"

---

文渊回道：“正是。”

Wenyuan replied: "Yes, exactly."

---

千里寻松口气。

Qianli Xun breathed a sigh of relief.

---

向夫人瞧向文渊身旁：“这位是？”

Madam Xiang looked at the person beside Wenyuan: "And who is this?"

---

现在还不是表明身份的最佳时机，乔文仙介绍道：“这位是我在汴梁认识的朋友申公子，这位是我的远亲乔姑娘。”

Now was not the best time to reveal his identity, so Qiao Wenxian introduced: "This is Young Master Shen, a friend I met in Bianliang. And this is Young Lady Qiao, my distant relative."

---

千里寻听到“申公子”的介绍并未有任何怀疑，她一心关注文渊，哪有闲暇思索他人？只认出申公子正是在竹林遇到的人。

Qianli Xun didn't suspect anything when she heard "Young Master Shen" introduced. She was so focused on Wenyuan that she had no time to think about others. She only recognized Young Master Shen as the person she had met in the bamboo forest.

---

申简辰走过来时注意到幽栖居士，心想，原来正是在竹林与他有一面之缘的姑娘。

When Shen Jianchen walked over, he noticed Youqi Jushi and thought to himself: so this is the young lady I met briefly in the bamboo forest.

---

向夫人笑道：“哦，是从汴梁来的朋友，里边请！”

Madam Xiang smiled: “Oh, a friend from Bianliang—please come in！”

---

乔文仙经过淑真身边，向她露出充满爱意的笑容。即使一个字未说，千里寻已感受他满满的爱意。她在心里默默祈祷，希望今日韦嬷嬷与父亲谈判能顺利促成和离。

As Qiao Wenxian passed by Shuzhen, he gave her a loving smile. Even without saying a word, Qianli Xun could feel his love overflowing. She silently prayed in her heart, hoping that today Weimomo's negotiation with her father would successfully bring about the mutual divorce.

---

申简辰经过时，也瞟了一眼朱淑真，见她娇羞的样子，觉得好笑，心想：“恋爱中的女子真是可笑，哪怕经历过一场婚姻，遇见爱情，也是一幅无脑的样子。”

When Shen Jianchen passed by, he also glanced at Zhu Shuzhen. Seeing her shy expression, he found it amusing and thought: "Women in love are truly ridiculous. Even after going through one marriage, when they meet love, they still act brainless."

---

乔梦容第一次见幽栖居士，很开心，默默赞叹天下竟然真有才貌双全的女子。此时与幽栖居士搭话甚不方便，还是等宴饮之时再找机会与她聊两句，因而仅是礼貌性地从她身边走过。

This was Qiao Mengrong's first time meeting Youqi Jushi. She was very happy and silently marveled that there really was a woman in the world who was both talented and beautiful. It wasn't convenient to talk to Youqi Jushi at the moment, so she'd wait for the banquet to find a chance to chat with her. Thus, she merely passed by politely.

---

千里寻依然舍不得将目光从文渊身上挪开，哪怕盯着他的背影，都会有种心满意足的感觉。

Qianli Xun still couldn't bear to take her eyes off Wenyuan. Even just staring at his back gave her a sense of satisfaction.

---

正当沉浸在爱情的体验中，却听到最讨厌的声音：“伯母，姐姐。”

Just as she was immersed in the experience of love, she heard the most annoying voice: "Aunt, Sister."

---

千里寻扭过头，陈浩宇一副嬉皮笑脸的样子，身边一个人都没带。仔细想来，他平常身边不是一群听差遣的小弟，就是地痞流氓，这些人怎么可能进得了朱府？没带人倒十分正常。

Qianli Xun turned her head. Chen Haoyu was grinning from ear to ear, without a single person beside him. Thinking carefully, he was usually surrounded by either a group of lackeys or hooligans. How could such people enter the Zhu residence? It was perfectly normal that he came alone.

---

当陈浩宇礼貌性地唤了声“幽栖居士”，结果看到朱淑真露出拳头，想起她的第三个条件：“第三，以后见我要问好行礼，然后赶紧从我面前滚开。”

When Chen Haoyu politely called out "Youqi Jushi," he saw Zhu Shuzhen clench her fist and remembered her third condition: "Third, from now on, when you see me, greet me and then get lost quickly."

---

陈浩宇没等向夫人问话，急忙说：“我得赶紧过去，你们忙，你们忙。”说完迅速离开。

Before Madam Xiang could ask anything, Chen Haoyu hurriedly said: "I need to get going. You're busy, you're busy." Then he quickly left.

---

陈文心说：“这孩子，为何如此着急？”

Chen Wenxin said: "This child, why is he in such a hurry?"

---

向夫人很是理解：“估计是看到朋友，随他去吧。”

Madam Xiang was very understanding: "He probably saw a friend. Let him go."

---

千里寻心中训斥道：“这就是欺负老娘的下场，以后见到老娘能滚多快就滚多快！”

Qianli Xun scolded in her heart: "This is what happens when you mess with me! From now on, when you see me, roll away as fast as you can!"

---

林觉跟着族叔林子孝前来，林子孝先问好：“向夫人，少夫人，幽栖居士有劳了。”

Lin Jue came with his clan uncle Lin Zixiao. Lin Zixiao greeted first: “Madam Xiang, Young Madam, Youqi Jushi—much obliged.”

---

千里寻跟着嫂嫂还礼。

Qianli Xun followed her sister-in-law in returning the greeting.

---

向夫人笑道：“林家二叔来了，老爷等着跟你一起品鉴诗词。”

Madam Xiang smiled: "Second Uncle Lin is here. The master is waiting to appreciate poetry with you."

---

林子孝说：“承蒙知府大人厚爱。”

Lin Zixiao said: "I'm honored by the prefect's kindness."

---

林觉行礼道：“岳母，嫂嫂。”

Lin Jue bowed and said: "Mother-in-law, Sister-in-law."

---

向夫人因女儿之事，对这位姑爷着实不满，收起笑容：“姑爷既然来了，进去吧。”

Because of her daughter's situation, Madam Xiang was quite dissatisfied with this son-in-law. She put away her smile: "Since you're here, Young Master, go in."

---

林觉跟着林子孝向里走，经过朱淑真身边时，努力装道：“娘子走时也不给我说一声，我好跟你一起前来帮忙。”

Lin Jue followed Lin Zixiao inward. As he passed by Zhu Shuzhen, he tried to act concerned: "You didn't even tell me when you were leaving, wife. I could have come with you to help."

---

千里寻怼道：“林觉，装什么装？你我都这样，我不叫你才正常，再说朱家举行宴会，哪有你帮忙的份儿？”

Qianli Xun snapped back: “Lin Jue, stop pretending! We're like this now. It's normal that I didn't call you. Besides, this is the Zhu family's banquet—what business do you have helping？”

---

林觉气不过，想要当众说两句，被陈文心一句“姑爷慢走”给打住。

Lin Jue couldn't stand it and wanted to say something in public, but Chen Wenxin stopped him with a sentence: "Young Master, take care."

---

林子孝见情势不妙，拉起侄儿：“还不快跟我走！”

Seeing the situation was bad, Lin Zixiao pulled his nephew: "Hurry up and come with me!"

---

林觉只好悻悻地离开。

Lin Jue had no choice but to leave resentfully.

---

千里寻跟母亲和嫂嫂又接待了一会儿，才算结束，三人走到宴会之地，在女宾区专门为她们预留的位置坐下。

Qianli Xun continued receiving guests with her mother and sister-in-law for a while before it was finally over. The three of them walked to the banquet area and sat in the seats specially reserved for them in the female guest section.

---

所有人还未动筷，大家需等待知府大人讲完话方可享用美食，欣赏节目。

No one had picked up their chopsticks yet. Everyone had to wait for the prefect to finish his speech before they could enjoy the food and performances.

---

朱晞颜见大家已落坐，从男宾区起身，来到木台之上，环顾台下之人，冷冰冰的面孔上挂着少有的笑容。

Seeing everyone seated, Zhu Xiyan rose from the male guest section and went up to the wooden stage. He looked around at the people below, a rare smile on his usually cold face.

---

千里寻心想：“我爹真是奇怪，面对自己女儿连个笑脸都没有，对着大家倒是笑容可掬。”

Qianli Xun thought: "My father is so strange. He can't even smile at his own daughter, but he's beaming at everyone else."

---

只听朱晞颜讲道：“诸位同僚及其家眷，还有临安的文人才子们，大家每年能享有盛宴，首先要感谢官家，自官家即位，历经建中靖国、崇宁、大观、政和、重和，现至宣和，官家励精图治，为大宋创下这等丰亨豫大之象，真是国泰民安，上下一片祥和……”

Zhu Xiyan spoke: "Esteemed colleagues, your families, and the literary talents of Lin'an. Every year we can enjoy this grand banquet. First, we must thank His Majesty. Since his ascension, through the eras of Jianzhong Jingguo (1101), Chongning (1102-1106), Daguan (1107-1110), Zhenghe (1111-1118), Chonghe (1118-1119), and now Xuanhe (1119-1125), His Majesty has governed with diligence, creating such prosperity and grandeur for the Great Song. The country is prosperous and the people are at peace, with harmony throughout..."

---

申简辰心中很不屑：“好好吹吧，北宋很快就没了，到时哭都来不及。”

Shen Jianchen was disdainful in his heart: "Keep boasting. The Northern Song will be gone soon, and by then you won't have time to cry."

---

千里寻也想道：“现在是宣和四年，还有三年金军南下，汴梁陷落，我爹竟然意识不到，还在这里夸赞昏君，实在可笑。再看看周围这些人，都是一副无知的样子，还以为太平永随。要不告诉他们？……还是算了，说了也没人信，再说对这个时代的人来讲这是天机，常言道天机不可露，我还是少言语，不如仔细筹谋在即将动乱的年代如何过好自己的日子。”

Qianli Xun also thought: "It's now the fourth year of Xuanhe. In three years, the Jin army will march south, and Bianliang will fall. My father doesn't even realize it and is still here praising the incompetent emperor. It's ridiculous. Look at all these people around, all ignorant, thinking peace will last forever. Should I tell them? ...Forget it, no one would believe me anyway. Besides, for people in this era, this is a heavenly secret. As the saying goes, heavenly secrets must not be revealed. I'd better keep quiet and carefully plan how to live well in the coming turbulent times."

---

千里寻想完，向乔文仙看去，他也看过来，他们互相以微笑示意。

After thinking this, Qianli Xun looked at Qiao Wenxian. He was also looking at her. They smiled at each other.

---

朱晞颜继续讲道：“本官建议，先举起酒杯，面向汴梁，与官家同饮！”

Zhu Xiyan continued: "I suggest we first raise our cups, face Bianliang, and drink with His Majesty!"

---

一个下人用盘子端着酒杯呈给知府大人，朱晞颜拿起酒杯，先面向汴梁方向。所有人端起酒杯，站起来，学着知府大人，将酒杯双手举到头顶，片刻之后，一饮而下。

A servant presented a cup of wine to the prefect on a tray. Zhu Xiyan picked up the cup and first faced the direction of Bianliang. Everyone picked up their cups and stood up, following the prefect's example. They held their cups with both hands above their heads, and after a moment, drank it all in one gulp.

---

千里寻与申简辰心知肚明，又不能不随大流，也装模作样端起酒杯。

Qianli Xun and Shen Jianchen knew exactly what was going on, but they couldn't not follow the crowd, so they also put on an act and picked up their cups.

---

千里寻在心中说：“敬这群无知的人。”

Qianli Xun said in her heart: "Here's to this ignorant bunch."

---

申简辰则想道：“我还是敬神柳吧，希望它能帮我早日找到千里寻。”

Shen Jianchen thought: "I'd rather toast the Sacred Willow. I hope it can help me find Qianli Xun soon."

---

所有人饮完酒，坐回原位。

After everyone finished their wine, they sat back down.

---

朱晞颜把酒杯放在下人端的盘中：“其次，感谢各位同僚，本府任职临安，承蒙各位支持，临安的百姓才能有好日子过，百姓有好日子过，诸位也就有好日子过；最后，感谢各位文人才子，你们虽不参政，却以各自的才华装点临安，使得临安多次被官家夸赞，说临安人杰地灵，人才辈出。希望你们再接再厉，让临安的诗词书画更上一层楼！”

Zhu Xiyan put his cup on the tray held by the servant: "Second, I thank all my colleagues. I serve as prefect in Lin'an, and with your support, the people of Lin'an can live well. When the people live well, you all live well. Finally, I thank all the literary talents. Although you don't participate in politics, you adorn Lin'an with your respective talents, making Lin'an a place praised by His Majesty many times, saying Lin'an is renowned for its talented people, with talents emerging in large numbers. I hope you will continue to strive and take Lin'an's poetry, calligraphy, and painting to the next level!"

---

男宾区的官员们都鼓起掌：“知府大人说得好！”

The officials in the male guest section all applauded: "Well said, Prefect!"

---

父亲每次讲话，朱景元都会认真聆听，每次听了都佩服不已，大家喊好，他也跟着喊，声音很大。

Every time his father spoke, Zhu Jingyuan would listen attentively. Each time, he was filled with admiration. When everyone cheered, he joined in, his voice very loud.

---

女宾区的家眷们也都鼓起掌来。

The family members in the female guest section also applauded.

---

掌声结束后，朱晞颜发话：“宴会开始！”

After the applause ended, Zhu Xiyan announced: "The banquet begins!"

---

大家才正式享用美味佳肴。

Only then did everyone formally enjoy the delicious food.

---

朱晞颜坐回位置，与各位官员觥筹交错，不断示意朱景元向官员敬酒。

Zhu Xiyan sat back in his seat, exchanging toasts with the officials, constantly signaling Zhu Jingyuan to toast the officials.

---

台上的乐手演奏音乐，舞伎们上台跳起一曲《桃夭》，歌声从一旁传出：“桃之夭夭，灼灼其华。之子于归，宜其室家。……”

On the stage, musicians played music. The female dancers went on stage and performed "Peach Blossoms." A singing voice came from the side: "Peach blossoms so fair, glowing bright and clear. This lady goes to her new home, bringing harmony to her household..."

---

千里寻仔细瞧去，原来在木台的一侧坐着一位弹琵琶的女子，边弹边唱，琵琶声与其他乐器之音完美融合在一起，配上绝美的嗓音，令千里寻着迷……

Qianli Xun looked carefully. On one side of the wooden stage sat a woman playing the pipa, singing as she played. The sound of the pipa blended perfectly with the other instruments, accompanied by a beautiful voice, which fascinated Qianli Xun...

---

乔文仙顾不上欣赏歌舞，他的双眼一直没有离开淑真。男宾区和女宾区只隔着两排低矮的花卉，加之二人坐的位置较便利，只要稍微努力一下，可轻易瞧见对方。

Qiao Wenxian didn't have the mind to appreciate the singing and dancing. His eyes never left Shuzhen. The male and female guest sections were separated by only two rows of low flowers. Plus, their seats were conveniently located, so with just a little effort, they could easily see each other.

---

申简辰边吃菜边观察文渊，凑到耳边轻声说：“你的魂儿快被幽栖居士勾走。”

Shen Jianchen ate his dishes while observing Wenyuan. He leaned close to his ear and whispered: "Your soul is about to be stolen by Youqi Jushi."

---

乔文仙小声说：“你不觉得她很美吗？”

Qiao Wenxian whispered: "Don't you think she's beautiful?"

---

“是不错，不过那个丫鬟也挺美。”

"She's not bad, but that maidservant is also quite beautiful."

---

不远处的丫鬟正是夏果。

The maidservant not far away was Xia Guo.

---

夏果趁韦嬷嬷不注意，从房中溜出来，顺着歌舞声寻找而来，见苦荞等贴身丫鬟和小厮们挤在树下专为下人设置的用餐之处，便过去在苦荞和南双之间挤出一个位置，望向男宾区寻找着乔文仙的身影。

Xia Guo took advantage of Weimomo's inattention and slipped out of the room. Following the sound of singing and dancing, she came to where the personal maidservants and young servants were squeezed under the tree in the area specially set up for servants. She squeezed in between Kuqiao and Nanshuang and looked toward the male guest section, searching for Qiao Wenxian's figure.

---

乔文仙顺着仪王示意的方向瞧去，一眼看到夏果：“她是淑真的贴身丫鬟，是不错，可在我眼里，谁都没法与淑真相比。”

Qiao Wenxian looked in the direction the Prince indicated and saw Xia Guo at a glance: "She's Shuzhen's personal maidservant. She's not bad, but in my eyes, no one can compare to Shuzhen."

---

当乔文仙瞅到夏果时，夏果也一眼看到乔公子，见他看向自己，脸不由一红，低头装作吃菜的样子。

When Qiao Wenxian saw Xia Guo, Xia Guo also saw Young Master Qiao at a glance. Seeing him looking at her, she couldn't help but blush and lowered her head, pretending to eat.

---

苦荞奇怪道：“夏果，你脸红什么？”

Kuqiao asked strangely: "Xia Guo, why are you blushing?"

---

夏果掩饰道：“没……没什么。”

Xia Guo covered up: "No... nothing."

---

苦荞又说：“既然来了，与我在这里边吃边注意小姐，一旦小姐招手，定是有事，你我要随时过去。”

Kuqiao said again: "Since we're here, eat with me and keep an eye on the young lady. Once she waves, it must be for something. We need to be ready to go over at any time."

---

“小姐要是招手，我过去就行，苦荞姐姐只管放心享用美食。”夏果很乐意被小姐招呼，那样她就有理由光明正大站起来走到女宾区，让乔公子更加清楚地看到她。

"If the young lady waves, I'll go over. Sister Kuqiao, just enjoy the food in peace." Xia Guo was very happy to be called by the young lady. That way, she'd have a legitimate reason to stand up and walk to the female guest section, letting Young Master Qiao see her more clearly.

---

乔文仙的话更让申简辰意识到文渊对朱淑真的感情深厚，他想起千里寻。申简辰对千里寻的感情也是如此：“女子外表美又有何用？关键要有一个有趣的灵魂？”

Qiao Wenxian's words made Shen Jianchen realize even more how deep Wenyuan's feelings were for Zhu Shuzhen. He thought of Qianli Xun. His feelings for Qianli Xun were the same: "What's the use of a woman's outward beauty? The key is to have a fascinating spirit?"

---

乔文仙不解：“有趣的灵魂？什么意思？”

Qiao Wenxian was puzzled: "An interesting soul? What does that mean?"

---

申简辰说：“我曾喜欢一位姑娘，向她表白，她却对我说'除非猪能上树，老娘才会嫁给你！'你不觉得很有趣吗？”

Shen Jianchen said: "I once liked a young lady. I confessed to her, but she said to me, 'I'll marry you when pigs learn to climb trees!' Don't you find that interesting?"

---

乔文仙诧异：“有趣吗？”

Qiao Wenxian was surprised: "Interesting?"

---

申简辰肯定道：“当然。”

Shen Jianchen affirmed: "Of course."

---

“你的爱好真特别。”乔文仙进一步靠向仪王耳边，“难道佳和郡主有一个有趣的灵魂？”

"Your taste is truly special." Qiao Wenxian leaned closer to the Prince's ear. "Does County Princess Jiahe have an interesting soul?"

---

申简辰端起一杯酒饮下，叹道：“只可惜世间美女无数，有趣的灵魂并不多。”

Shen Jianchen picked up a cup of wine and drank it, then sighed: "It's just a pity that while there are countless beautiful women in the world, there aren't many interesting souls."
