## Chapter 285: Brother Comes Home Late

Once her in-laws left, Kuqiao fished the men's robe out from under the quilt with trembling hands. "You nearly gave me a heart attack—how did you even get in here?!"

"Perks of roof-leaping!" Qianli Xun laughed, pouring water. "Heard my in-laws coming from the eaves and dove straight through the back window!" Admitting she really *had* crossed paths with Prince Yi while tailing Haodong, she swore Kuqiao to absolute silence—especially not a peep to Bai Feng!

Riding home in her carriage, Mengrong's panic shifted into nagging paranoia—if not with Zhu Shuzhen, could Brother have gone to a brothel? Xilan quickly assured her His Highness would never stoop so low, and the instant they pulled into Youlin Retreat, the gatekeeper announced Prince Yi had walked in moments before them!

Sprinting down the lantern-lit paths with Xilan panting behind, Mengrong threw herself into Brother's arms outside their chamber. "I thought I'd lost you!"

Stroking her hair gently, Shen Jianchen smoothly covered his plain dark robe as undercover casework (dodging Haoyu's name) and brushed off Bai Feng's absence as staying at the yamen—never daring mention the bamboo grove cottage. Pulling her close, he softened her every worry until she whispered against his chest, "I want to bear you a child, Brother."

"Whatever makes you happy," he murmured, hoping his tenderness would finally melt her hatred for Qianli Xun.

Back at the Judicial Office first thing next morning, Han Gu delivered a key lead from Scarface's old crew: before they left the country villa that night, Scarface had produced a special pot of fine wine—claiming it was for Haoyu and Jinlan, though Shen Jianchen saw at once it had been drugged for the estate steward!

Right then Wen Xin marched in with a breakthrough of his own.
