第285章 哥哥归来天已晚
## Chapter 285: Returning Late at Night

大老爷和夫人走后，丑丫也回了西院。
苦荞把素心草堂的门关好，进到里屋，掀开被子，拿出男装，边整理边说：“可吓死我，差点儿穿帮，还好小姐回来得及时。不过，您到底是怎么进来的？”
千里寻从桌上放的茶壶里给自己倒杯水，喝完说道：“你家小姐我有飞檐走壁的本领，公婆出现时，我已在屋顶，迅速从后面的窗户进来，还好及时，没让你这丫头被主人打板子。”
苦荞已将男装整理好，放进收纳脏衣裳的竹筐：“老爷和夫人人好，绝对不会用板子打我。只是仪王妃来得突然，也不知她来做什么？”
千里寻坐到床边：“还能来干什么？肯定是找不到仪王怀疑到我头上。以前教她的话一句都没记在心上，就她那个紧张劲儿，有几个男人吓跑几个。”
苦荞走到小姐跟前：“仪王妃再怎么怀疑也不该怀疑到您头上。”
“她还真怀疑对了，今晚我的确跟仪王在一起。”
“啊！小姐，您可千万别做傻事！”
“不是你想的那样，我与仪王为陈浩宇的案子同时盯上一个人，结果给碰上，也是凑巧。”
“原来如此，恐怕仪王妃知道会瞎想。”
Once Eldest Master and Madam left and Chouya went back to the west wing, Kuqiao bolted the door, hurried into the inner room, and fished the men’s robe from beneath the quilt. “Nearly scared the soul out of me! One second later and we’d have been busted! How on earth did you get in?”

“仪王不说，我不说，你也不说，她怎么会知道？”
Pouring herself water from the teapot and gulping it down, Qianli Xun grinned. “Your miss walks roofs and vaults walls, remember? I was already on the roof when my in-laws showed up, slipped straight through the back window—just in time to save your hide from a paddling.”

“我会替小姐保密。”
Tossing the robe into the laundry basket, Kuqiao protested, “Master and Madam are kind—they’d never paddle me! Still, what got into Princess Consort Yi, barging in out of nowhere?”

“不能告诉白风！”
Sitting on the bed’s edge, Qianli Xun sighed. “What else? Lost track of Prince Yi and jumped straight to suspecting me. Not one word I taught her before stuck—clutching that tight will scare off any man.”

“坚决不会！”
“Suspect all she likes, she shouldn’t pin it on you!”

……
“Actually... she guessed right. I really was with Prince Yi tonight.”

离开陈府的路上，乔梦容的心情已经没有去陈府时那么紧张，可依然免不了胡乱猜测。
Kuqiao gasped. “Miss! Don’t do anything reckless!”

哥哥既然没有与朱淑真私会，他到底去了哪里？难道会去青楼？青楼的女子主动热情，什么不要脸的事都做得出来，没几个男人能招架得住，只怕哥哥也像其他男人一样。
“Not what you’re thinking! Both of us tailed the exact same suspect for Chen Haoyu’s case and bumped into each other—pure coincidence.”

“喜兰，你说哥哥会不会去青楼？”
“Oh! Still, if Princess Consort Yi found out, she’d lose her mind.”

喜兰果断否认：“肯定不会！王爷是何等人？身份尊贵，岂能去那种地方？”
“Prince Yi won’t talk, I won’t talk, you won’t talk—how could she ever know?”

乔梦容点点头：“你说得对，哥哥不是那样的人，是我多虑。”
“My lips are sealed!”

喜兰继续安慰：“郡主别瞎想，幽栖居士既然老老实实在家，王爷必然是去忙公务，忙完一准儿回幽林别居。”
“Not even to Bai Feng!”

乔梦容想起以前的朱淑真在幽林别居对她说过的话。
“Not a single syllable!”

那时她正与乔公子热恋，还未对哥哥动心，真心诚意劝她把心态放好，不要把哥哥看得太重，否则会受伤。
Meanwhile, riding home from Chen Manor, Qiao Mengrong felt the knot in her stomach loosen, yet fresh doubts crept in. If her brother wasn’t with Zhu Shuzhen, where was he? A brothel? Courtesans were brazen and shameless—few men resisted them.

谁知抢走哥哥心的人竟然是她，乔梦容便不再把她说过的话当回事。与哥哥圆房后，她将哥哥看得更加重要，越是重视，越怕失去，越想除去让她无法心安之人。
“Xilan, think Brother went to a brothel?”

陈浩东正在实施计划，按照乔梦容的意思，应该直接让蒋爷的人杀了朱淑真。可陈家大公子偏偏要先实施他准备好的两件麻烦事。以后还需依赖他跑腿，乔梦容不想计较太多，那就让朱淑真多活几日。
“Not a chance!” Xilan denied flat-out. “Who is His Highness? A man of his rank would never set foot in a place like that!”

回到幽林别居，乔梦容第一件事便是问守在门口的下人：“哥哥可回来？”
Qiao Mengrong nodded. “You’re right—Brother isn’t that type. I’m overthinking.”

下人回道：“王爷前脚刚回来，王妃您就回来了。”
“Don’t torture yourself, Princess,” Xilan soothed. “Since Youqi Jushi is sitting safe at home, His Highness must be tied up on official business and heading back to Youlin Retreat right now.”

乔梦容的脸上露出笑容，她已等不及，飞快地跑着，希望能追上哥哥。
Qiao Mengrong thought back to the old days when Zhu Shuzhen had urged her at Youlin Retreat not to cling so hard to her brother lest she get hurt. Back then she was head over heels for Young Master Qiao and hadn’t fallen for Brother yet. Once Zhu Shuzhen stole Brother’s heart, every word she ever said rang hollow. Consummating the marriage had only made Qiao Mengrong prize him more—and terrified of losing him, she wanted the threat erased for good.

幽林别居到处挂着灯笼，夜晚的道路看得清楚，下人也将道路清理干净，没有任何阻挡之物。喜兰不用担心王妃被绊倒，也跟着跑起来。
Chen Haodong was working his angle. Left to her, she’d have had Master Jiang’s men kill Zhu Shuzhen straightaway, yet Eldest Young Master Chen insisted on setting off his two traps first. Needing him as her errand boy for now, she let Zhu Shuzhen breathe a few extra days.

当乔梦容跑回院子，看到哥哥即将进屋的背影，她喘着气兴奋地喊声：“哥哥！”
Arriving at Youlin Retreat, Qiao Mengrong’s first question to the gatekeeper was, “Is Brother back?”

申简辰停下脚步，转过身，他感到纳闷，为何梦容一脸急切的样子？只见梦容飞快地扑进他的怀里，将他紧紧抱住。
“His Highness stepped through the gate the moment before you arrived, Your Highness!”

“哥哥为何回来这么晚？我以为要失去哥哥。”
Her face lit up. Unable to wait, she hiked her skirts and sprinted along the lantern-lit, freshly swept walkways toward his courtyard, Xilan jogging right behind.

梦容的样子显得十分可怜，申简辰用手在她的头上摸摸：“怎么会？再晚我都会回来。”
Spotting her brother’s back just about to cross his threshold, she cried out breathless, “Brother!”

“哥哥身上的衣衫料子实在普通。”
Turning in surprise at her urgency, Shen Jianchen barely caught his footing before she threw herself into his arms and clung tight. “Why are you back so late, Brother? I thought I’d lost you!”

“临时买的，装扮起来跟踪可疑之人才不容易被发现。”
Pity softening him, Shen Jianchen stroked her hair. “How could that happen? No matter how late, I’ll always come home.”

“哥哥是去办案？”
“The cloth on your robe is so coarse.”

“是。”
“Bought it on the fly—easier to tail a suspect without standing out.”

“可是陈浩宇的案子？”
“Working a case?”

因担心乔梦容胡思乱想，或者问东问西，申简辰开始编起瞎话：“不是，是其他案子。”
“Yes.”

乔梦容并不关心案子的事，陈浩东为了个人利益捣鼓出的事，又不能置朱淑真于死地，她才懒得理会。她放开哥哥，牵起他的手：“进屋，我帮哥哥换衣裳。”
“Chen Haoyu’s case?”

两位主人进屋之后，喜兰将门从外面关好。有王爷在，王妃心情好，他们会互相照顾对方，无须她在一旁添乱。她只需站在门外，若里面有需要，她再进屋伺候。
Not wanting to set off a storm of questions and suspicions, he fibbed. “No—a different one.”

卧房内，乔梦容为哥哥褪去衣衫：“为何没见白风？”
Uninterested in Chen Haodong’s minor harassment anyway since it wouldn’t kill Zhu Shuzhen, Qiao Mengrong let go and took his hand. “Come inside and let me help you change.”

白风去竹林之事绝对不能让梦容知道。竹林在幽栖居士的名下，建竹屋是因听苦蕨说竹林的东家想建，申简辰才有此想法，若照实说，恐怕梦容多疑，问来问去十分麻烦，干脆编个瞎话。
Once they stepped in, Xilan pulled the door shut from outside. When His Highness was home and Her Highness was happy, they didn’t need a third wheel hovering indoors; she’d wait outside in case they called.

“最近司理院忙，我不想让他来回跑，让他在司理院住上一段时日。”
Helping him out of his robe inside the bedchamber, Qiao Mengrong asked, “Where’s Bai Feng?”

“哥哥身边不能没人伺候。”
She could never know Bai Feng was out in the bamboo grove—that grove belonged to Youqi Jushi, and he had only started building the bamboo cottage after hearing Kujue mention the grove’s owner wanted one. Telling the truth would spark endless interrogation, so he lied smooth. “Judicial Office is swamped—I had him stay over there a while to save the commute.”

申简辰主动抱住梦容：“要不是你在幽林别居，我也不会回来，有你伺候我，根本不需要白风。”
“You can’t go without someone waiting on you.”

这招果然管用，梦容不再问东问西，脸贴在哥哥的胸前：“我想给哥哥生个孩子。”
Pulling her into his arms, Shen Jianchen murmured, “If not for you waiting at Youlin Retreat, I wouldn’t have come back tonight either. With you taking care of me, who needs Bai Feng?”

“你想怎样都行，我听你的。”申简辰只想让梦容开心，用他的爱化去她对千里寻的仇恨，也许有一日，她们的关系会恢复如初。
It worked like magic. Dropping the questions, she pressed her cheek against his chest. “I want to give you a child, Brother.”

喜兰见屋内的灯被熄灭，露出会心的笑容，她没必要再站着，安心回屋睡觉。
“Whatever you want, I’m yours.” All he hoped was to make her happy and melt away her hatred for Qianli Xun—maybe one day the two could even mend their bond.

……
Outside, seeing the bedchamber lamp go out, Xilan smiled to herself and went off to sleep.

天亮后，申简辰刚回到司理院，屁股还没坐热，韩谷走进来拜道：“王爷，昨日我办完事回来没见到您，今日一早来向您汇报查探的情况。”
At dawn, Shen Jianchen had barely sat down at the Judicial Office when Han Gu stepped in and bowed. “Your Highness, I missed you when I got back last night, so I came first thing to report.”

申简辰放下卷宗：“可查到重要线索？”
Setting down his dossier, Shen Jianchen asked, “Turn up any solid leads?”

韩谷摇摇头：“没有，不过那些人一口咬定是陈浩宇干的。”
Han Gu shook his head. “Nothing solid—Scarface’s cronies all swear Chen Haoyu did it.”

“他们可曾亲眼看到？”
“Did they see it with their own eyes?”

“那倒没有，他们说，陈浩宇回城里后，疤子给他们些银子，让他们去城里逛。”
“No. They said right after Chen Haoyu returned to town, Scarface handed them silver and sent them off to roam the city.”

“疤子很可疑。他们有没有提到陈家乡下宅子里的下人被调走之事？”
“Scarface reeks of guilt. Did they mention the staff being pulled away from the country house?”

“我正想向王爷汇报此事。跟着疤子的人说，他们还没离开时，陈家让把宅子里的下人全部调走，只留管家一人。此事与他们无关，也没多想，只想着陈公子回来见没有下人，肯定会生气。他们还提到一件事……”
“Right—before they left, orders came from Chen Manor pulling every servant out except the caretaker. They thought nothing of it except figuring Young Master Chen would be mad when he got back. One more detail...”

“什么事？”
“Out with it.”

“疤子准备了一壶好酒，他们几个本想尝尝，疤子不允许，说是给陈公子和春金兰预备的。”
“Scarface had a jug of fine wine ready. The boys wanted a taste, and Scarface snapped no—claimed it was saved for Young Master Chen and Chun Jinlan.”

“很有可能这壶酒是给管家预备的。”
“Odds are that wine was meant for the caretaker.”

话音刚落，温信带人回来，也将昨晚查探的情况一一汇报。
Right on cue, Wen Xin returned with his squad to deliver his full report from the night before.
