第171章 为了孩子操碎心
## Chapter 171: Hearts Worn Thin for Their Children

离开陈府，千里寻本想直奔朱府，当面告诉父亲陈家已同意退婚，又怕与父亲吵起来。
最终还是决定回望月轩亲笔写封信，将陈老太太说过的话一字不落表达清楚，再让苦荞将信送到朱府。既可避免与父亲正面冲突，又可仔细斟酌措辞，让父亲看后不得不面对陈家退婚的事实。
回到望月轩后，千里寻提起笔。给父亲写封信比写话本难度更大，主要是她太想两家退婚，因为太在意，总担心笔下的每个字每句话写得不到位，影响好不容易在陈家争取而来的口头承诺。
不知揉了多少团纸后，终于将信写好，折后装进信封，递给苦荞。
“去趟朱府，亲自将信交到我爹手中。”
苦荞从小姐郑重的表情中意识到信的重要性，谨慎接过，塞进怀里：“小姐放心，我这就去。”
苦荞怀揣小姐的亲笔信，乘坐马车来到朱府侧门外。
刚走下马车，发现陈江林与姚轻琰也从马车上下来，看那样子，是来拜访老爷和夫人。
苦荞知道小姐去陈家闹过，也知陈家同意退婚，更知信中写些什么，她猜想二位前来肯定是为退婚之事。
苦荞为小姐感到高兴，忍不住上前拜见：“陈老爷，夫人，我家小姐本来想亲自过来，怕跟老爷吵起来，所以让我送来书信，没承想碰上二位，有二位前来，小姐的心愿定能达成。”
陈江林与姚轻琰先愣一下，很快回过味儿来，原来这丫头以为他们是为退婚而来。
姚轻琰问：“你是朱家姑娘身边的丫鬟？叫什么名字？”
Leaving Chen Manor, Qianli Xun first thought of marching straight to Zhu Manor to tell her father the Chens had agreed to call off the engagement, then stopped herself lest they blow up into another shouting match. Better to return to Wangyue Pavilion, put every word Old Madam Xia had spoken down on paper, and send Kuqiao to deliver the letter. That spared a head-on clash while letting her weigh every phrase so her father could not brush off the Chens' refusal.

“我是小姐的贴身丫鬟，名叫苦荞。”
Back at her desk, she found writing to her father harder than drafting a huaben. Caring too much made her agonize over every word, afraid a clumsy line might ruin the hard-won concession she had wrung from Chen Manor. After crumpling a small mountain of drafts, she finished at last, folded the sheet into an envelope, and handed it to Kuqiao.

姚轻琰继续问：“你要送的书信可是为两家退婚之事？”
"Go to Zhu Manor and place this straight into my father's hands."

“是，小姐让我亲自送来。”
Reading the gravity on her mistress's face, Kuqiao tucked the letter with care inside her collar. "Leave it to me, Miss. I am on my way."

陈江林与姚轻琰四目相对，总算来得及时，朱家姑娘还未将老太太同意退婚的话传给知府大人。
Boarding the carriage, Kuqiao rode to Zhu Manor's side gate. Just as she stepped down, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan alighted from their own carriage right beside her, plainly come to call on Master and Madam.

姚轻琰说：“书信不必送了，我们来就是劝说知府大人退婚。”
Knowing her mistress had stormed Chen Manor and won their consent to cancel the match—and knowing what the letter contained—Kuqiao figured the Chen elders had come for the exact same purpose. Thrilled for her mistress, she hurried forward and curtsied. "Master Chen, Madam Chen! My mistress wanted to come in person yet feared quarreling with Master, so she sent me with a letter. What luck running into the two of you! With you here in person, my mistress's wish is sure to come true!"

“不行！小姐交代的任务，我必须完成。”
Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan blinked, then caught on at once: this maid thought they were here to call off the wedding.

姚轻琰见苦荞十分坚持，和颜悦色道：“不如把书信给我，一会儿我会面见知府大人，帮朱家姑娘送上。”
"Are you Miss Zhu's maid?" Yao Qingyan asked. "What is your name?"

苦荞犹豫后，果断拒绝：“小姐让我亲自交给老爷，我得听她的。”
"I am Miss's personal maid, Kuqiao."

陈江林想到一个好办法：“苦荞，朱家姑娘在信中写些什么，我们都知道，无非是说陈家同意退婚之事。可是想让知府大人同意，仅凭一封信根本无济于事。再说你一个丫头前去，把信一给，知府大人一看，不得气坏？朱家姑娘不在跟前，肯定拿你撒气，朱家家法甚言，打你几板子都是轻的。我们若帮你送进去，不仅事能办成，还帮你免去一顿板子，仔细想想，是不是这个理？”
"Is that letter about breaking off the betrothal between our families?"

陈家老爷一番说辞甚有道理，苦荞一路上也担心老爷看完信后一生气，动用家法惩罚她，既然有这么好的机会既能帮到小姐，又能避免自己被惩罚，何乐而不为？
"Yes, Miss told me to deliver it in person."

“既然二位是来商谈退婚一事，与我家小姐的目的一致，将信给你们也无妨。”苦荞从怀里掏出信，递给姚轻琰，“有劳陈家老爷和夫人。”
Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan exchanged a glance. Thank goodness they had arrived in time, before Old Madam Xia's outburst reached Prefect Zhu's ears!

姚轻琰接过，笑眯眯地应道：“放心，回去告诉朱家姑娘，我们二人定帮她办得妥妥当当。”
"No need to deliver it," Yao Qingyan said smoothly. "We came here to talk Prefect Zhu into canceling the match ourselves."

苦荞再次谢过二位后，上了望月轩的马车，心想：“陈公子的爹娘真好，要是陈公子人好些，小姐嫁到陈家也不错，可惜这么好的公婆与小姐无缘。”
"No! My mistress gave me an order, and I have to carry it out."

早已有下人打开朱府侧门，迎接陈家老爷和夫人。
Seeing the maid's stubbornness, Yao Qingyan put on a gentle smile. "Give the letter to me instead. I am about to see Prefect Zhu and can hand it to him on Miss Zhu's behalf."

二人走入后，陈江林从夫人的手上接过信，藏入怀中，悄悄说：“你我真是及时，正好拦下这封信，还是藏起来得好，决不能让知府大人知道。”
Kuqiao hesitated, then shook her head. "Miss told me to hand it to Master myself. I have to obey her."

姚轻琰点点头：“回到陈府后，赶紧把它烧了。”
Chen Jianglin thought fast. "Kuqiao, we both know what Miss Zhu wrote—only that the Chens agreed to break off the match. Yet a single sheet of paper will never sway Prefect Zhu. Worse, if a maid walks in and hands him that letter, imagine how livid he will be! With Miss Zhu not around, he is bound to vent his wrath on you. The Zhu household's discipline is strict; a round of the rod would be getting off light. Let us carry it inside, and not only does the job get done, you spare yourself a beating. Think about it—am I right?"

在朱府下人引领下，二人朝知府大人的院子走去。
His words hit straight on Kuqiao's secret dread; all the way over, she had fretted over Master flying into a rage and punishing her under house rules. Here was a chance to get Miss's task done and save her own skin—why not take it?

有下人已报告朱晞颜，他猜亲家前来定是商议两个孩子的婚事，让丫鬟将向夫人唤来，两人站在主屋门口笑脸相迎。
"Since you both came to discuss canceling the betrothal, matching my mistress's aim, letting you deliver it works fine." Pulling the envelope from her collar, she handed it to Yao Qingyan. "Much obliged, Master and Madam Chen."

当陈江林与姚轻琰来到朱府正院，见朱晞颜与向夫人满面春风，两人不由相视一番，面露愁容，估计一会儿知府大人不会笑得这么开心。
Yao Qingyan accepted it with a beaming smile. "Rest easy. Go tell Miss Zhu we will handle everything without a hitch."

两人很快掩饰住情绪，陈江林快走几步，露出笑容，行拱手礼：“有劳亲家相迎，我们二人没有事先告知，突然造访，还望亲家不要见怪。”
Thanking them once more, Kuqiao climbed back into Wangyue Pavilion's carriage, sighing to herself: Young Master Chen's parents are such kind folks! If only their son were half as decent, marrying into Chen Manor would not be bad at all. Shame Miss has no fate with in-laws this fine.

朱晞颜自然不会见怪：“亲家说的哪里话，朱府的大门随时为二位敞开，想什么时候来都行，不必事先告知。”
Servants had already swung Zhu Manor's side gate wide to receive the guests. Stepping inside, Chen Jianglin took the letter from his wife, tucked it into his robe, and whispered, "What timing! Intercepting this letter saved us. Hide it well—Prefect Zhu must never lay eyes on it."

向夫人则拉起姚轻琰的手：“妹妹，里边请。”
"We will burn it the second we get home," Yao Qingyan nodded.

姚轻琰客气地回道：“姐姐请。”
Guided by a servant, the pair walked toward Prefect Zhu's courtyard. Alerted ahead of time, Zhu Xiyan assumed his in-laws had come to finalize wedding details; summoning Madam Xiang, he waited at the main hall doorway with a broad smile.

大家进屋落座，丫鬟上了茶，一番寒暄过后，朱晞颜主动问：“亲家前来，可是为两个孩子的婚事？”
Seeing Zhu Xiyan and Madam Xiang beaming with spring cheer, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan traded a rueful glance—Prefect Zhu would not be smiling like that much longer. Masking their unease at once, Chen Jianglin hurried forward with cupped hands and a warm grin. "Forgive us for dropping in unannounced and troubling our in-laws to come welcome us."

陈江林装作坦然的样子：“是为两个孩子的婚事。”
"Nonsense! Zhu Manor's gates stand open to you both at any hour—no need for advance word," Zhu Xiyan laughed.

朱晞颜更是高兴：“婚期将近，淑真的嫁妆都已备齐，只等陈家来迎娶。至于婚礼怎么操办，全由陈家定，不用征求我们意见。”
Madam Xiang took Yao Qingyan's hand. "Come right inside, Sister."

陈江林依然装作坦然的样子：“婚礼前前后后已安排妥当，绝不比乔家的婚礼差，只等花轿上门将淑真接到陈家，以后也有人帮我们管那臭小子。”
"After you, Sister," Yao Qingyan replied with grace.

听到不比乔家的婚礼差，朱晞颜与向夫人都“呵呵”笑起来。
Once everyone was seated and maids served tea, Zhu Xiyan got straight to the point after pleasantries. "Did our in-laws come regarding the children's wedding?"

今日乔家举办婚礼，据说是近几年来临安城最盛大的婚礼，邀请了所有临安城有头有脸的人。当然也邀请了朱家，朱晞颜不愿亲自去，即使仪王去，他也不愿，让儿子领着儿媳代表朱家送上贺礼。
Chen Jianglin put on an easy air. "Indeed, regarding their wedding."

陈家不愧是与朱家亲上加亲，陈江林与夫人也没去，朱晞颜更加满意，也更觉得陈家是少有的好人家。
Zhu Xiyan beamed wider. "With the date close at hand, Shuzhen's dowry is packed and ready, awaiting only your sedan. Arrange the ceremony however the Chen family sees fit—no need to consult us."

见亲家笑得如此开心，陈江林不愿再隐瞒下去，今日来此，是为拖延婚期。他端起茶盏，啜一口茶，慢慢放下，待亲家停止笑声后，大胆说：“今日陈府发生一件大事……”
"Every detail of the wedding is already arranged," Chen Jianglin said smoothly. "It will not fall a hair behind the Qiao family's feast. We are just waiting to carry Shuzhen home so someone can finally rein in that rascal of ours."

朱晞颜笑得口干，也端起茶盏品了一口，饶有兴致地问：“哦？什么大事？说来听听。”
Hearing the wedding would rival the Qiaos' set Zhu Xiyan and Madam Xiang chuckling with satisfaction. Today's Qiao wedding was rumored to be Lin'an's grandest in years, drawing every notable family in the city. Zhu Manor had received an invitation too, yet Zhu Xiyan had refused to go—even with Prince Yi attending—sending his son and daughter-in-law with gifts instead. Showing solidarity as double in-laws, Chen Jianglin and his wife had skipped the Qiao feast too, raising the Chens higher than ever in Zhu Xiyan's esteem.

陈江林开始卖起关子：“亲家先答应我不生气我便说。”
Watching his host laugh in high spirits, Chen Jianglin hated bursting the bubble, yet postponing the wedding was why he had come. Lifting his bowl, he took a measured sip of tea, set it down, and waited for the laughter to ebb before diving in. "A major incident took place at Chen Manor today..."

朱晞颜敏锐地感觉到不对劲，面色变得平静：“我为何要生气？尽管说来听听。”
Throat dry from laughing, Zhu Xiyan took a sip of his own tea and perked up. "Oh? What sort of incident? Let us hear it."

陈江林看向夫人，见姚轻琰点点头，便不再卖关子：“今日朱家姑娘带一名丫鬟来陈府闹，打伤几名下人，还把石头和椅子砸烂，看她那样子，不退婚誓不罢休。”
"First promise not to lose your temper," Chen Jianglin hedged.

朱晞颜听后，心中生起怒火，当着亲家的面不好发作，强忍着：“亲家不必在意，我叮嘱过你，这俩孩子肯定会去陈府闹，果不其然，今日又闹开。”
Sensing trouble at once, Zhu Xiyan sobered. "Why would I lose my temper? Speak plainly."

陈江林和盘托出：“我与轻琰是不在意，还好言相劝，怎奈拗不过我家老太太，亲家也知道，老太太疼浩宇，不愿见他受半点儿苦，担心朱家姑娘那么厉害，浩宇娶了她肯定没好日子过，还说朱家姑娘什么时候改了性子什么时候娶她过门。亲家放心，婚事还在，只是得往后拖一拖。”
Glancing at his wife and catching her nod, Chen Jianglin laid it bare. "Miss Zhu marched up to Chen Manor today with a maid, beat several of our servants, and smashed a stone block and a chair to pieces. By the look of her, she will not rest until the betrothal is broken."

向夫人一直不敢多言，她羡慕陈江林亲切地称呼自己的夫人名讳，老爷从来没有如此称呼过她。
Fury surged in Zhu Xiyan's chest, though he choked it down before his guests. "Pay it no heed, my dear in-law. I warned you those two brats would bring their act to your door—and sure enough, she did."

听了亲家所说，她又没有心情再羡慕亲家母。女儿竟然跑到陈家闹，搞得陈家想要拖延婚期，令她担忧不已，怕老爷会撵到望月轩再鞭打女儿。
Chen Jianglin gave the full picture. "Qingyan and I paid it no mind and tried talking her down, yet we could not override our Old Madam. As you know, Mother dotes on Haoyu and hates seeing him suffer a scratch. Seeing how fierce Miss Zhu is, she fears Haoyu will know no peace married to her, and declared we will only welcome Miss Zhu home once she mends her temper. Rest assured, the betrothal stands—only the wedding must be pushed back a little."

朱晞颜此刻才明白，亲家前来确实为婚事，只是与他想的不一样。对方拖延婚期对朱家来讲无疑也是一种伤害，可自家女儿做出此等有失身份之事，对方不取消婚约已然不错。
Keeping quiet on the side, Madam Xiang felt a pang of envy hearing Chen Jianglin address his wife by her given name; her own husband had never once called her so warmly. Yet the next moment, envy vanished into sheer dread: her daughter had stormed Chen Manor and made them delay the wedding! What if Master stormed over to Wangyue Pavilion and whipped Shuzhen again?

“只怪我教女无方，养下这等粗鲁的女儿，我定收拾得她改掉那性子。”
Zhu Xiyan grasped the truth now: his in-laws had come about the wedding, only not in the way he had hoped. A postponed wedding stung Zhu pride, yet after his daughter's outrageous spectacle, it was already generous of the Chens not to cancel outright. "Blame my poor parenting for raising such an unruly daughter. I will discipline her until she mends that temper!"

老爷要收拾淑真，向夫人开始抹眼泪，姚轻琰在一旁看得甚是愧疚。
Hearing him vow to discipline Shuzhen, Madam Xiang began dabbing at her eyes, filling Yao Qingyan with guilt.

陈江林也担心自己一番说辞搞得朱家姑娘没好日子过，赶紧解释：“其实我很喜欢朱家姑娘的性子，不改也罢，她若嫁到陈家，定能将浩宇收拾得服服帖帖。只是……亲家您也知道，我不愿拗着老太太，她毕竟年纪大，身体也不好，要是因为这事气出个好歹来，我也不好过……”
Afraid his report might get Miss Zhu beaten, Chen Jianglin jumped in at once. "Truth be told, I like Miss Zhu's fiery spirit—no need to change a bit! Once she marries in, she will whip Haoyu right into line. Only... you understand my spot, in-law. I cannot go against Old Madam; she is frail and getting on in years, and if stress harms her health, I could never live with myself..."

听完这话，朱晞颜气焰消去一半儿：“亲家说，该如何是好？”
That took half the fire out of Zhu Xiyan's rage. "What do you propose, then?"

姚轻琰忍不住说道：“我与江林商量，淑真的性子挺好，无需改。但要给我们些时日，好慢慢说服老太太，最多将婚期推迟一个月，一个月后婚礼照常。”
Yao Qingyan spoke up without waiting. "Jianglin and I talked it over. Shuzhen's spirit is fine as it is—no need to change her. Just give us a little time to talk Old Madam around. We will push the wedding back a month at most; in one month, the ceremony goes on as planned."

向夫人不再抹眼泪，也大胆说：“这倒是个好办法。”
Drying her eyes, Madam Xiang dared chime in. "That sounds like a sensible plan."

朱晞颜瞪了向夫人一眼：“哪有你多嘴的份儿。”
Zhu Xiyan shot her a sharp glare. "Who asked you to butt in?"

向夫人不敢再言语，更是羡慕姚轻琰，说得比她多，也没见陈江林批评半句，足以显示在家中的地位有多高。
Madam Xiang shrank back into silence, envying Yao Qingyan more than ever: she spoke twice as much without a single sharp word from Chen Jianglin, proving how respected she stood in her own home.

朱晞颜也赞同姚轻琰所说：“两位不嫌弃淑真已是万幸，一个月就一个月，只是我们两家在临安是有头有脸的人家，两个孩子的婚期估计很多人都打听着，突然推迟，也需有个合理的由头，不至于被人笑话。”
Turning back, Zhu Xiyan nodded to Yao Qingyan's proposal. "That you two have not soured on Shuzhen is blessing enough. One month it is. Still, both our houses have faces to keep in Lin'an, and plenty of folks have been asking about the wedding date. Delaying out of the blue requires a decent pretext so neither family becomes a laughingstock."

朱晞颜重视清誉，陈江林早有所耳闻，临行前已将由头想好：“若有人问起，就说陈家过分看重这门婚事，计划大操大办，目前准备不充分，等待准备充分后，再举办婚礼。”
Knowing well how tightly Zhu Xiyan guarded his reputation, Chen Jianglin had come prepared. "If anyone asks, say the Chen family prizes this marriage so highly that we mean to throw a grander feast than ever, our preparations fell short of the mark, and we pushed the date back to do it full justice."

这个理由不错，两家都不失颜面，朱晞颜自然满意：“有劳亲家考虑周全，若需要我出面说服老太太，尽管开口。”
Face preserved for both houses, Zhu Xiyan smiled in approval. "Thank you for thinking so thoroughly. If you ever need me to help persuade Old Madam, just say the word."

“亲家忙得都是大事，这等家常小事我与轻琰应付得过来，如若实在应付不过去，再请亲家出面。”陈江林一番看似拒绝的话饱含着恭维，说得朱晞颜心中怒气全无。
"You have prefecture affairs to manage; Qingyan and I can handle a little family matter like this. If we truly hit a wall, we will ask for your help." Wrapping polite refusal in smooth flattery, Chen Jianglin swept away the last trace of Zhu Xiyan's anger.

两家商议完毕，统一意见后，陈江林与夫人没必要再待下去，辞别亲家，乘坐马车摇摇晃晃打道回府。
With both households aligned, Chen Jianglin and his wife took their leave and climbed back into their swaying carriage for the ride home.

回陈府的路上，姚轻琰还是充满担忧：“婚期只推迟一个月，真担心我们两个没能力说服老太太。还有，若家里其他人问起来，是该说只推迟一个月，还是按照老太太的意思说？”
Along the way, Yao Qingyan furrowed her brow. "Only one month's delay—I truly worry whether we can talk Mother around in time. And if the rest of the family asks, do we say it is postponed one month, or give Mother's version?"

陈江林感到为难：“若家里其他人问起，只说推迟，不要说那么细致，至于老太太那里，只能尽人事听天命，如果说服不了，把老太太送到乡下的别居住一段时间，等婚事成了，再把他接回来。”
"Tell the rest of the family only that it is delayed, without spelling out details," Chen Jianglin mused. "As for Mother, we do our best and leave the rest to Heaven. If she still will not budge, we send her out to the country villa for a spell and bring her back after the wedding is sealed."

“唉，也只能这么办。”姚轻琰叹口气，继续说，“要是别的姑娘嫁到我们陈家估计没这么多事，朱家姑娘与浩宇一个不愿嫁一个不愿娶，搞得我们跟着为难。”
"Ah, I suppose that is our only play," Yao Qingyan sighed. "Any other bride marrying into our house would never cause this much fuss. Miss Zhu refuses to marry, Haoyu refuses to wed, and we are the ones caught in the middle."

陈江林笑道：“你难道没听说过好事多磨？依我看她要是嫁到陈家，也是陈家的福气。”
Chen Jianglin chuckled. "Never heard the saying that good things meet many twists? To my mind, marrying her into the house will prove a blessing for the Chens."

姚轻琰来了兴致：“为何如此说？”
Yao Qingyan perked up. "How do you figure?"

“难道今日你没瞧出来？”
"Did you not notice today?"

“瞧出什么？”
"Notice what?"

“她根本不怕老太太。”
"She is not one bit afraid of Mother."

“的确是，当着老太太面，该怎样就怎样。”姚轻琰忽然之间明白过来，“你呀，孝顺，不敢违拗老太太，我自然也顺着老太太，搞得浩宇没人敢管，若把朱家姑娘娶回来，就有人敢管，这么想来的确是件好事。”
"True enough—stood right before Old Madam without flinching." Realization dawned on Yao Qingyan. "You are too filial to defy Mother, so I defer to her too, leaving nobody in the house brave enough to rein in Haoyu. Bring Miss Zhu in, and at last someone will! Put like that, it really is a blessing."

“还有，她也不怕你。”
"And she is not afraid of you either."

“什么意思？”
"What is that supposed to mean?"

“自己琢磨。”
"Think it over."

姚轻琰琢磨一会儿，琢磨出意味来，笑着说：“你的意思是我不让你管儿子？要我看，朱家姑娘也不怕你。她谁都不怕，嫁到我们家岂不是会闹翻天？”
Yao Qingyan mulled a second, caught the dig, and burst out laughing. "Saying I stop you from disciplining our son? If you ask me, Miss Zhu is not afraid of you either! Scared of nobody—will she not turn our whole house upside down?"

陈江林抓住夫人的手：“只要能把浩宇的天给翻过来，就是你我的福气。”
Chen Jianglin clasped her hand. "Long as she turns Haoyu's world upside down, that is our good fortune."

夫妻二人在马车上畅想着有人能帮他们管教儿子的美好场景，那时，他们便可以享受有个好儿子的幸福。
Riding home hand in hand, husband and wife pictured the bright day someone finally whipped their son into shape and let them taste the joy of having a decent heir.
