第100章 勾栏请客听说书
## Chapter 100: Treating Them to Storytelling at the Goulan

来到桥头瓦，他们向说书的勾栏走去。
Reaching Qiaotou Wa, they headed toward the Goulan storytelling theater.

快要到时，申简辰突然停下：“文渊，我能否跟幽栖居士单独说句话？”
Just before they reached the entrance, Shen Jianchen came to a stop. "Wenyuan, may I have a quick word in private with Youqi Jushi?"

文渊满口答应：“说多少句都行。”
Wenyuan agreed without hesitation. "Say as many words as you like."

千里寻根本不愿意：“申公子有什么话，就在这里说吧。”
Qianli Xun wanted no part of it. "Whatever Young Master Shen has to say, say it right here."

申简辰说出三个字：“千里寻……”
Shen Jianchen spoke three syllables: "Qianli Xun..."

千里寻立刻走到一边，她可不想让仪王道出她就是千里寻的秘密。申简辰露出得意的笑容，跟在她身后。
Qianli Xun stepped aside at once; the last thing she wanted was for Prince Yi to spill her secret that she was Qianli Xun. A smug smile crossed Shen Jianchen's face as he followed behind her.

乔梦容很疑惑，哥哥与幽栖居士有什么话不能当着众人面说？
Qiao Mengrong felt bewildered. What could Prince Yi have to say to Youqi Jushi that couldn't be spoken in front of everyone?

“乔公子，他们要说什么？为何避开我们？”
"Young Master Qiao, what are they going to talk about? Why step away from us?"

“乔姑娘不用担心，申公子自有道理，你我不必过问。”
"No need to worry, Young Lady Qiao. Young Master Shen has his reasons. There's no need for us to pry."

乔文仙如此放心，乔梦容也打消疑虑，只顾观察他们，猜测可能说的话。
Seeing Qiao Wenxian so at ease put Qiao Mengrong's doubts to rest, though she kept watching them and guessing what they might be saying.

小姐不在身边，只有夏果一人，她有意向乔公子靠近一些。她看着他，可他的眼中只有小姐。
With her mistress stepped away, Xiaguo stood alone and edged a little closer to Young Master Qiao on purpose. She gazed at him, yet his eyes held only her mistress.

确定安全后，申简辰问道：“你不是说手稿写好后，要先送给我看，为何没送？却直接请我来听说书？”
Once sure they were out of earshot, Shen Jianchen asked, "Didn't you promise to send me the manuscript to read first as soon as you finished it? Why didn't you, and instead brought me straight to a storytelling show?"

千里寻觉得仪王长得人高马大，心眼儿还挺小：“这等小事申公子就别计较。”
Qianli Xun thought that for a tall, broad-shouldered man, Prince Yi sure had a petty streak. "Don't sweat a tiny thing like that, Young Master Shen."

“怎么能算小事？说明你是个出尔反尔的人，在你心里根本没把我当回事。”
"How is that a tiny thing? It shows you go back on your word—and that I don't mean a thing to you."

千里寻急了，她不敢将嗓门放大，压着声音说：“本来我是想送到幽林别居，可你也知，文渊住在幽林别居，很容易被他发现，于是，我临时改变主意。您是仪王，又两次帮我，我再怎么着，也不会不把您放在眼里，我心里可是时时刻刻希望仪王您跟佳和郡主和和美美。”
Qianli Xun hurried to explain, keeping her voice low so no one would overhear. "I did plan to send it to Youlin Retreat, only as you know, Wenyuan stays there too, so he'd have caught on in no time. That's why I changed my mind at the last minute. You're Prince Yi, and you've helped me twice. No matter what, I would never brush you off. Every single moment, I'm wishing for you and Princess Jiahe to live in sweet harmony."

听到这话，申简辰有种想说出真实身份的冲动。周围吵吵闹闹，人流涌动，文渊与梦容又一直盯着，氛围实在不搭，还是等等再说。
Hearing that stirred an urge in Shen Jianchen to reveal who he truly was. Yet with the noisy crowd surging past and Wenyuan and Mengrong staring straight at them, the setting was all wrong; better to wait.

“你真的希望我跟梦容好？”
"Do you really want things to go well between Mengrong and me?"

“那还能有假？佳和郡主是好姑娘，您要懂得珍惜，千万不要因为您身份尊贵，又有钱就辜负她。”
"Could I be any more sincere? Princess Jiahe is a wonderful girl. Know how to treasure her, and whatever you do, don't let her down just because you have high rank and money."

申简辰喜欢千里寻的原因之一，便是因她善良，总是为他人考虑。为了能多跟千里寻待着，又故意问：“我若辜负呢？……”
One of the reasons Shen Jianchen loved Qianli Xun was her kind heart and the way she always thought of others. Wanting to linger beside her a little longer, he asked on purpose, "And what if I do let her down?..."

“我已视佳和郡主为闺中密友，你若敢辜负她，我可不忌惮你的身份，该为她出头，绝不当缩头乌龟。”
"I already consider Princess Jiahe my close confidante. If you ever dare let her down, I won't care a bit about your rank. I'll stand up for her and never shrink back like a coward."

千里寻的话说得坚决，申简辰既为乔梦容感动，又为自己难过：“梦容有你这样的朋友是她的幸运，可我……”
The steel in Qianli Xun's words touched Shen Jianchen for Qiao Mengrong's sake even as it broke his own heart. "Mengrong is lucky to have a friend like you, yet I..."

千里寻不喜欢藏着掖着，不如趁此机会将话挑明：“你这个人其实还不错，挺热心，跟我想象中的王爷不一样，……你再稍微注意一下会更好……”
Never one to beat around the bush, Qianli Xun seized the chance to lay it all out in the open. "To be honest, you're a pretty decent guy—warmhearted, not at all like the princes I used to imagine... You'd be even better if you just watched yourself a little..."

“注意什么？”申简辰不解。
"Watch myself how?" Shen Jianchen asked in puzzlement.

“不要随意用语言和您那双眼睛调戏美女，不是所有美女都会像我一样定力十足。您长得也不赖，身份地位又高，总是对美女放电，难免招惹到花花草草，会伤佳和郡主的心。”
"Stop flirting with pretty women left and right with your smooth talk and those eyes of yours. Not every beauty has my kind of self-control! You're good-looking and hold high rank; if you keep tossing sparks at pretty girls, you're bound to stir up messy entanglements and break Princess Jiahe's heart."

申简辰这才明白，时机不对，再对的事情都是错的，他面对千里寻不由自主表现出的爱意，竟然被她认为是调戏，真是极大的讽刺。
It dawned on Shen Jianchen at last: when the timing is wrong, even the truest thing turns wrong. The love he could not stop showing whenever he faced Qianli Xun had been misread by her as shameless flirting. What bitter irony.

“今日不谈此事，若幽栖居士想听我解释，明日酉时来神柳下，我会将实情告知。”
"We won't talk about this today. If Youqi Jushi wants my explanation, come beneath the Divine Willow tomorrow at dusk, at the Hour of the Rooster, and I'll tell you the whole truth."

申简辰说完，继续回到佳和郡主身边，拉起她朝说书的勾栏走去。
With that, Shen Jianchen walked back to Princess Jiahe's side, took her hand, and led her toward the Goulan theater.

“哼，说到痛处就跑，明日就明日，正好趁其他人不在，好好教育教育你。”千里寻想，最近只顾着谈恋爱，几乎忘记神柳，不如趁此机会去看看它。
"Hmph, running off the second I hit a nerve! Fine, tomorrow it is. I'll use the chance while nobody else is around to give you a proper lecture." Qianli Xun realized she had been so caught up in romance these days that she had all but forgotten the Divine Willow; she might as well take the chance to go see it.

乔文仙微笑着走来，拉起她的手：“在想什么？”
Qiao Wenxian walked up with a smile and took her hand. "What are you thinking about?"

“没什么。他们都进去了，我们快些跟上。”
"Nothing. They've already gone inside. Let's catch up."

今日说书的勾栏内，人真不少，仪王与佳和郡主已挑桌子坐下。乔文仙挑了一个离仪王近些的位置，两人在桌子的两侧坐下后，有伙计前来斟茶，摆蜜饯。
The storytelling theater was packed today. Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe had already taken a table, so Qiao Wenxian picked one close to theirs. Once he and Qianli Xun sat down on either side of the table, a waiter came over to pour tea and set out candied preserves.

千里寻担心夏果站着太累，吩咐伙计搬张椅子，让夏果坐在她身边，把有蜜饯的盘子放在她的手中：“这盘你端着吃。”
Worried Xiaguo would grow tired standing, Qianli Xun had the waiter bring over a chair so Xiaguo could sit beside her, then placed a dish of candied preserves in her hands. "Hold this plate and snack on these."

“小姐和乔公子怎么办？”
"What about Miss and Young Master Qiao?"

“桌上还有，放心吃，不够再拿。”
"There's more on the table. Go ahead and eat. We'll order more if we run out."

夏果很感激小姐照顾，她偷偷喜欢乔公子，小姐知道肯定不高兴。可她忍不住不喜欢，又时不时偷偷瞧他一眼。
Xiaguo felt touched by her mistress's kindness. She knew her mistress would be upset if she ever found out about her secret crush on Young Master Qiao, yet she could not stop her heart from beating for him, stealing shy glances his way every now and then.

台上说话艺人在说三国历史的动荡起伏，正讲到关云长刮骨疗毒的故事，听的人都被吸引住……
Up on stage, a storyteller was recounting the turbulent drama of the Three Kingdoms, right in the middle of the tale of Guan Yunchang having the poison scraped from his bone, holding the whole audience spellbound...

千里寻没见过接她手稿的说话艺人，也不知对方何时会表演她写的故事。她向四周瞅瞅，朝伙计招招手。
Qianli Xun had never met the storyteller who took her manuscript and had no idea when he would perform her tale. Looking around, she waved a waiter over.

伙计过来：“姑娘有何吩咐？”
The waiter hurried up. "How may I help you, Young Lady?"

“听说有位叫千里寻的，话本写得不错，他的话本常常在你们勾栏内表演，不知今日何时开始？”
"I heard there's a writer named Qianli Xun whose huaben stories are quite good, and they're often performed here at your theater. Any idea when today's session starts?"

“看来姑娘进门时没注意，外面的牌子上都写着呢，等台上的艺人把三国的历史说完，下一位就说千里寻写的故事。”
"Young Lady must not have noticed the signboard outside when you came in. It's all written right there. As soon as the performer on stage wraps up the Three Kingdoms, the next storyteller is up with Qianli Xun's tale."

“千里寻在你们这里很出名吗？”
"Is Qianli Xun really that famous around here?"

“只闻其名，不知其人，他的故事写得不错，大家都喜欢听。”
"Everyone knows the name, though nobody knows the man. He writes great stories, and the crowd loves hearing them."

千里寻很满意：“你去忙吧，有需要再叫你。”
Qianli Xun beamed with satisfaction. "Go on with your work. I'll call if we need anything."

千里寻看过好几遍三国演义的电视剧，每次看到华佗为关云长刮骨疗毒的情节时，她的心都会提到嗓子眼儿。
Qianli Xun had watched the "Romance of the Three Kingdoms" television drama several times, and every single time she reached the scene where Hua Tuo scraped the poison from Guan Yunchang's bone, her heart leaped into her throat.

她从小练习千里拳，倒也练出抗打的本领，可如果有人要给她刮骨疗毒，连麻药都不打，那是万万使不得，她认为自己还没被毒药毒死，肯定会被疼死。
Training in Qianli Fist since childhood had made her tough enough to take a beating, yet letting someone scrape poison off her bone without even a drop of anesthesia? Not in a million years! She figured she would die of the pain long before the poison ever got her.

今日在穿越而来的时代，听到说话艺人说起这段，内心对关云长又一次生起敬佩之情。
Hearing a storyteller perform the passage here in this bygone era she had crossed into filled her with a fresh wave of awe for Guan Yunchang.

乔文仙见淑真听得认真，本不想打扰，想起父亲答应提亲之事还未告知她，此刻时机最佳，不如趁此说了，让她高兴高兴。
Seeing how absorbed Shuzhen was in the tale, Qiao Wenxian had not meant to interrupt. Then he remembered he still had not told her his father had agreed to propose marriage—what better moment than right now to put a smile on her face?

乔文仙靠近小声说：“淑真，我父亲已答应一两个月后到朱府提亲。”
Leaning in close, Qiao Wenxian whispered, "Shuzhen, my father has agreed to come to Zhu Manor in a month or two to propose marriage."

千里寻上一秒还沉浸在体验关云长刮骨的疼痛中，听到文渊的话，痛苦全无，立刻露出笑脸，兴奋地看着文渊：“难道这就是你要告诉我的好消息？”
One second earlier, Qianli Xun had been wincing along with Guan Yunchang's bone-scraping agony; the moment Wenyuan spoke, every trace of pain vanished. A brilliant grin spread across her face as she looked at him in thrill: "Don't tell me this is the good news you promised to bring me?"

乔文仙点点头：“是的。”
Qiao Wenxian nodded. "It is."

“真是一个大大的好消息。”千里寻又装作伤心的样子，“一两个月，这么晚，如果是明天那该多好。”
"That really is a huge piece of good news!" Qianli Xun then put on a mock pout. "A month or two? That's so far away! If only it were tomorrow."

“没想到，你这么着急嫁给我。”
"Who knew you were in such a hurry to marry me."

“难道你不着急娶我？”
"Aren't you in a hurry to marry me too?"

“我当然着急，可父亲说，你刚和离，现在提亲不合适，让我等等，你耐心些，一两个月很快就过去，我答应你，多催催我父亲。”
"Of course I am! Only my father said since your mutual divorce was so recent, proposing right away wouldn't be proper, so he told me to wait. Be patient—a month or two will fly right by. I promise I'll keep nudging my father."

千里寻点点头，近期真是好事连连，不仅夏果的事情解决，她与文渊的婚事也快有眉目，在街上碰到陈浩宇带来的不快很快被忘记。
Qianli Xun nodded. Good news had been rolling in one piece after another these days: not only was Xiaguo's trouble settled, her own marriage to Wenyuan was taking shape too. The sour mood from running into Chen Haoyu on the street faded from her mind in no time.

夏果听到乔家要到朱府提亲，也替小姐高兴，更替自己高兴，只有小姐嫁到乔府，她才能离乔公子更近。
Hearing that the Qiao family would come to Zhu Manor to propose made Xiaguo happy for her mistress, and even happier for herself. Only once her mistress married into Qiao Manor could she draw closer to Young Master Qiao.

三国的故事终于讲完，下一位说话艺人走上台，正是接千里寻话本之人，啜了一口茶后，把扇子和茶壶放在桌上，开口说道：
At last the Three Kingdoms tale drew to a close. The next storyteller stepped onto the stage—the very man who had taken Qianli Xun's huaben manuscript. Taking a sip of tea, he set his fan and teapot on the table and began:

“诸位，上次讲到红香与玄英相遇相知又相爱，可他们之间的爱情得不到祝福，只因花神与凤凰有仇，他们若想在一起，必须从轮回崖跳下，脱离仙体，成为人，方可永远在一起。可成为人后，他们又会面临一大难题……”
"Ladies and gentlemen! Last time we left off with Hongxiang and Xuanying meeting, coming to know each other's hearts, and falling in love. Yet their love could receive no blessing, because a blood feud divided the Flower Deity and the Phoenix. To be together, they had to leap from Reincarnation Cliff, shed their immortal bodies, and become mortals so they could remain together forever. Yet once they became mortal, a grave trial awaited them..."

有人问：“什么难题？”
Someone in the crowd called out, "What trial?"

说话艺人回道：“若为人后，互不相识，要想相遇只能靠缘分。”
The storyteller replied, "Once reborn as mortals, they would no longer recognize each other; finding one another again would rest on fate alone."

又有人叹道：“可悲可叹，赶紧讲吧，我们都等着听呢！”
Another listener sighed, "How tragic! Hurry up and tell us—we're all on the edge of our seats!"

刚才说三国故事时，没见有人搭话，一讲自己写的故事，就有人搭话。千里寻很开心，说明大家真的爱听。
Nobody had chimed in during the Three Kingdoms tale just now, yet the instant her own story started, listeners were calling out. Qianli Xun glowed with pride; it proved people truly loved listening to it.

只听说话艺人讲道：
The storyteller launched into the tale:

话说红香与玄英相约来到轮回崖，望着深不可测的崖底，玄英从身上拔下一根凤凰的羽毛，说道：“你拿着它，成为人后，带着它来找我。”红香用仙力变出一片金色的花瓣，送到玄英手中：“这是我送给你的，记着我，你我到人间再续前缘。”
By mutual vow, Hongxiang and Xuanying met at Reincarnation Cliff. Gazing down into the bottomless abyss, Xuanying plucked a single phoenix feather from his own body and said, "Take this. Once you are reborn as a mortal, bring it with you and come find me." With her immortal power, Hongxiang conjured a golden petal and pressed it into Xuanying's hand: "This is my gift to you. Remember me, and let us renew our destined bond in the mortal world."

他们都把对方给的信物用神力藏在身上，牵着手，从轮回崖跳下。跳下的过程中，他们被一股无形的力量分开，无法看到对方，不知在黑暗中飘了多久，终于来到人间。
Using their immortal power, each concealed the other's keepsake upon their body. Hand in hand, they leaped from Reincarnation Cliff. As they fell, an unseen force tore them apart until neither could see the other. After drifting through the darkness for who knew how long, they reached the mortal world at last.

玄英成为武烨城主的儿子，名叫武玄英，出生时，胸前有一胎记，呈花瓣形；红香出生在山村里一个姓华的人家，名叫华红香，出生时手臂上有一胎记，外形像凤凰的羽毛。他们都已忘记前尘往事……
Xuanying was reborn as the son of the Lord of Wuye City and named Wu Xuanying, bearing a petal-shaped birthmark upon his chest; Hongxiang was born to a mountain-village family surnamed Hua and named Hua Hongxiang, bearing a birthmark shaped like a phoenix feather upon her arm. Both had forgotten every trace of their past lives...

此故事申简辰再熟悉不过，并不觉得什么。乔梦容倒是感慨不已，本来相爱的两个人，就这样被分开，再次见面也不知要经历多少千难万险。
Shen Jianchen knew this story inside and out, so it stirred nothing new in him. Qiao Mengrong, however, felt swept up in emotion: two people in love, torn apart just like that—who knew how many trials and hardships they would have to endure before meeting again?
