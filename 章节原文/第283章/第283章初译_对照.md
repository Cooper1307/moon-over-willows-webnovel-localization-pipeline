第283章 东哥偷情被暴露
## Chapter 283: Caught in the Act

千里寻并不去看，从传出的声音足以判断二人正在做着什么。她听得脸红心跳，后悔不该答应留下来。想走，身体不听使唤，屋里的声音仿佛有种魔力，将她的身体吸住，无法挪动。
她看向申简辰，他旁若无人，透过屋顶的小洞向下瞧着，既不脸红，心也不跳，仿佛看热闹似的。
千里寻红着脸，还好被丝帕遮住，不容易被人发现。她小声奚落：“哼！你如此专注，真不愧是圆过房的人。”
申简辰将目光移开，不好意思起来：“我是担心遗漏掉重要的话。”
千里寻继续小声奚落：“哼！也不用脑子想想，他们两个只顾亲热，哪有心情聊重要的话？你自己想看，不如老实承认，还显得你是真诚之人。”
申简辰更不好意思起来：“好了好了，不看就是。”
他坐正后，抬头看天上的一轮明月。刚才千里寻提到他圆过房，应该是白风说给苦荞，她从苦荞那里得知。
为了不被屋里的人听到，申简辰尽量将声音压低：“你会不会怪我？”
对方突如其来的问题，千里寻搞不明白，露出疑惑的表情。
申简辰将目光移到千里寻的脸上，借着月光清楚地接触到她不解的目光：“我食言了，没有守身如玉。”
原来是为此事，他回汴梁前说出这四个字，千里寻多少感动过，偶尔也会回想一番。可因并不现实，她从未真正放在心上。
“我并未当回事，你不算食言。”
申简辰不知自己是该失落还是该轻松，他苦笑一下：“你和陈浩宇呢？是不是也已圆房？”
不愿对方再抱任何希望，千里寻哄骗道：“当……当然。”因说谎，心中稍有紧张，手下意识从腿上挪开，一不小心碰到揭开的瓦片。
那瓦片顺着屋顶掉落在院内，只听“咣当”一声，瓦片掉在地上被摔成几瓣。
屋里传出陈浩东的声音：“谁？”
Qianli Xun refused to look—the sounds rising through the gap left zero doubt what was happening below. Heart hammering and face burning, she kicked herself for staying, yet her feet felt nailed to the tiles.

申简辰急中生智，学了声猫叫：“喵！”
Glancing over, she saw Shen Jianchen peering right through the hole without blinking, neither blushing nor flustered, cool as a spectator at a fair.

接着传来春金蝉的声音：“原来是只野猫，大公子别理会，我们继续。”
Thank goodness the handkerchief hid her red face. She whispered with a scoff, “Hmph! Staring so intent—spoken like a man who’s already consummated his marriage.”

屋里又传出一阵春波荡漾的笑声。
Shen Jianchen jerked his gaze away, sheepish. “I didn’t want to miss anything important they might say.”

两人不再说话，默默坐在屋顶，看似欣赏月光，其实各怀心思。
“Hmph! Use your head—they’re busy rolling around, not sharing secrets! If you want to watch, own up to it like an honest man.”

千里寻埋怨屋里的二人闹得太火热，搞得她坐在屋顶听到不该听的，同时为大嫂叶安雅感到不值。
Flustered, he gave in. “Fine, fine—I won’t look.”

叶安雅刚给她说想要和离时，她替她担忧过，现在却认为叶安雅离开陈浩东才是正确的选择。
Sitting straight, he tilted his face up at the bright moon. She knew about his consummation—Bai Feng must have told Kuqiao, who told her.

申简辰则为二人都已圆房而伤感，世间之事不如意者十有八九，他很想用感情去换其他如意之事，可生活偏偏不让他如意。
Keeping his voice barely a breath so nobody below heard, he asked, “Do you blame me?”

屋里的躁动终于告一段落，陈浩东一脸享受的样子，闭着眼躺在床上；春金蝉则靠在他的身上。
Caught off guard, she shot him a puzzled look.

“大公子雄风十足，会令不少女人神魂颠倒，也不知你家娘子是怎么想的，竟然一点儿都不珍惜？”
Meeting her eyes in the moonlight, he murmured, “I broke my word. I didn’t keep myself chaste.”

“你不是不喜欢提她吗？”
Oh—that vow he had made before returning to Bianliang. It had touched her at the time, even lingered in her thoughts now and then, yet it had never been realistic, so she hadn’t held him to it. “I never took it seriously—so you didn’t break your word.”

“偶尔提提也无妨。”
Unsure whether to feel crushed or relieved, he gave a wry smile. “What about you and Chen Haoyu? Have you consummated too?”

“等我成了陈家产业的继承人，到时找机会让你好好教教她如何伺候男人。”
Determined to snuff out any lingering hope in him, she fibbed. “Of—of course.” Nerves over the lie made her shift her hand off her knee—bumping the loosened roof tile.

“我可不教！省得她学会后大公子不再来找我。”
Clatter! The tile slid down the eaves and shattered into shards in the yard.

“怎么会？你这股子骚劲儿无人能比，我可舍不得。”
“Who’s out there?!” Chen Haodong barked from inside.

春金蝉开心地笑了一会儿，问道：“金兰给陈浩宇当妾室可有希望？”
Quick as lightning, Shen Jianchen let out a crisp “Meow!”

终于扯到重要话题，两人不约而同将头凑到小洞前，结果他们的头不小心撞到一起。两人各自捂住撞到的额头，忍了一会儿。
Chun Jinchan’s voice followed. “Just a stray cat, Eldest Young Master—ignore it and keep going.”

申简辰不再去抢，把地方让给千里寻。屋里的二人说话声音够大，不凑近，也能听到七八分。
Lush, giggling laughter floated back up from inside.

千里寻毫不客气，将耳朵凑到小洞跟前……
Neither spoke after that, sitting side by side on the roof beneath the moon, lost in private thoughts. Qianli Xun fumed at the racket below and felt awful for Ye Anya—earlier she had worried over her sister-in-law wanting a divorce, yet walking away from Chen Haodong was clearly the smartest move possible. Meanwhile, Shen Jianchen ached over thinking both of them had moved on; nine times out of ten life denied you what you wanted most.

只听陈浩东回道：“还很难说，今日审案不算顺利。疤子什么都承认，浩宇的两个朋友却说他那天晚上与他们在一起。证词不一致，参军大人还需寻找新的证据，只怕结果不如人意。”
At last the commotion below quieted. Chen Haodong lay back with his eyes shut in smug bliss while Chun Jinchan draped herself over him.

“大公子希望谁说的是假话？”
“Eldest Young Master’s vigor could sweep half the women in town off their feet,” she purred. “What’s wrong with your wife, not appreciating what she’s got?”

“你明明知道，还来问我？”
“Thought you didn’t like bringing her up?”

“你昨晚找疤子就是为此事？”
“Now and then doesn’t hurt.”

“当然，我担心疤子向着我堂弟，到公堂上作假证，便找他将利害关系说了，然后晓之以理动之以情，他终于答应照实说。”
“Once I inherit the Chen family business, I’ll find a chance for you to teach her how to serve a man properly.”

“这么说来那两位公子说的是假话？”
“No way! What if she learns and you stop coming to see me?”

“也能理解，浩宇常常与他们玩儿在一起，感情自然深些，帮他做假证再正常不过。”
“Never—nobody matches your fire. I couldn’t give you up.”

“不如大公子向司理参军说说，就说那两名公子作假证。”
Giggling in delight, Chun Jinchan asked, “Any chance Jinlan becomes Chen Haoyu’s concubine?”

“现在的司理参军是仪王，我哪有那么大的面子？再说什么事都得讲个证据，凭我空口白牙没人信。”
Finally—the real goods! Both leaned over the hole at the same instant and knocked foreheads with a dull thud. Wincing, they clutched their brows until the sting faded, and Shen Jianchen yielded the spot to her—the voices below carried loud enough anyway.

听了半天，可以确定春金兰闹事的确与陈浩东有关，还是听不出与疤子做假证相关之事。
Pressing her ear close, Qianli Xun caught Chen Haodong’s answer. “Hard to say. Today’s hearing didn’t go smooth. Scarface confessed everything, yet two of Haoyu’s friends swore he was with them all night. With clashing testimonies, the Judicial Investigator is hunting more proof—might not go my way.”

屋里的二人又开始闹腾，千里寻实在听不下去。她偷偷溜出来，还需尽快回去，只怕长辈无意中去墨香苑发现她不在，不好交代。
“Who do you hope is lying, Eldest Young Master?”

“没什么好听的，剩下的交给你，我先行一步。”
“You already know—why ask?”

“马车归你。”
“That’s why you went looking for Scarface last night?”

千里寻并未拒绝，立刻用轻功离开。
“Of course. Scared Scarface would side with my cousin and perjure himself, so I laid out the stakes, reasoned with him, and got him to swear he’d tell the truth.”

剩下申简辰一人，他决定给陈浩东来个措手不及，逼他说出实情。
“So those two young masters lied?”

他用轻功跳入院内，将脸上的黑布取下，塞进怀里，靠近闭着的屋门。
“Makes sense—they party with Haoyu all the time. Covering for a buddy is normal.”

里边的二人应该以为此处只有他们，并未将门关紧，还露出一条清晰的缝隙。申简辰上前轻轻将门推开，木门发出“咯吱”的声音。
“Why not tell the Judicial Investigator those two are lying?”

“谁？”陈浩东已无心闹腾，惊恐地问道。
“Prince Yi is the Judicial Investigator—think I carry that kind of pull? Besides, courts run on proof, not hot air.”

申简辰不想再往里走，站在外厅大声说：“陈大公子外出偷 情，真是快活！”
So Chen Haodong was definitely behind Chun Jinlan’s outburst, yet nothing proved he paid Scarface to lie.

声音如此熟悉，沉稳中颇具力度，陈浩东反应了一会儿，吓出一身冷汗。迅速从春金禅的身上爬起来，抓起衣衫，胡乱穿着。他只觉双腿无力，脑袋一片空白，踉跄着来到外厅。
Below, the pair started getting rowdy all over again. Too embarrassed to keep listening—and needing to get back before any Chen elder noticed her missing from Moxiang Courtyard—Qianli Xun stood. “Nothing left worth hearing. Leave the rest to you—I’m out.”

果然是仪王，他吓得跪在地上，全身颤抖：“殿……殿下……怎会……来此？”
“Take the carriage.”

申简辰半真半假地说：“我闲来无事，出来转转，没想到碰见陈大公子在外偷 情，还听到些不该听的。”
She accepted without fuss and vaulted off into the night.

陈浩东头上直冒冷汗：“我娘子……对我十分冷淡，不得已才……才把春香楼的商妓叫到这里，还……还请殿下替我保密。”
Left alone, Shen Jianchen decided to catch Chen Haodong off guard and shake the truth out of him. Dropping into the yard, he pocketed the black mask and stepped up to the door. Assuming they had the house to themselves, the pair hadn’t even latched it—a clear crack showed light inside.

“我才懒得管你们夫妻之事，之所以打断陈大公子的雅兴，是想问你几个问题。”
He pushed the wooden door open with a slow creak.

“殿下尽管问。”
“Who’s there?!” Chen Haodong yelped in panic.

“春金兰可否是你找来？然后撺掇她到陈家闹事？”
Stopping in the outer hall, Shen Jianchen spoke loud and cool. “Sneaking out for a tryst—having a grand time, Eldest Young Master Chen?”

看来与春金蝉一番对话已被仪王听去，没法再瞒下去，陈浩东老实交代：“是……是我撺掇的。”
That steady, unmistakable voice sent cold sweat pouring down Chen Haodong’s back. Scrambling off Chun Jinchan, he yanked his clothes on crooked, mind blank and knees like water, and stumbled into the outer room.

“为了陈家产业？”
Prince Yi stood right there. Dropping to his knees, Chen Haodong shook head to toe. “Your—Your Highness... what brings you here?”

“正是。不过，我也是一片好心，既帮浩宇改过自新，也能让金兰姑娘有个好去处，她实在太苦，我看着心中难安。当然，对我也有好处。”
Shen Jianchen gave a half-truth. “Out for an evening stroll when I stumbled right onto your tryst—and overheard a few things I shouldn’t have.”

“哼！你倒挺会找理由。”
Cold sweat beaded Chen Haodong’s brow. “My wife... treats me like ice, so I had no choice but to call a courtesan from Chunxiang Brothel here... Please keep this quiet, Your Highness!”

“不是理由，是发自肺腑之言。”
“I couldn’t care less about your bedroom affairs. I interrupted your fun to ask a few questions.”

陈浩东已没刚才紧张，被仪王发现的不过是私情和想要继承陈家产业的野心，还不至于丢掉小命。
“Ask anything, Your Highness.”

“昨晚你找过疤子？”
“Did you dig up Chun Jinlan and put her up to making a scene at Chen Manor?”

“找过。”
No hiding it after what the prince overheard. “Yes... I egged her on.”

“你找他都说些什么？”
“Over the Chen family business?”

陈浩东思考片刻说道：“我担心他在公堂上做假证，告诉他作假证迟早会被查出，到时罪过可就大了，不如照实说，陈家为保住浩宇性命，必然同意把春金兰放他在身边，两全其美。”
“Yes—though I meant well too! Giving Haoyu a chance to reform and finding poor Miss Jinlan a home—her suffering tore at my heart. Plus, sure, it helped me out.”

“你认为他今日在公堂上所言是真是假？”
“Hmph! Slick excuses.”

“真实不虚。”
“Not excuses—straight from the heart!” Catching his breath, Chen Haodong calmed down; being caught cheating and scheming for the family business was embarrassing, not fatal.

“难道你想要了陈浩宇的命？奸淫妇女可是死罪，宋律上写得明明白白。”
“Did you see Scarface last night?”

陈浩东吓得头快要碰到地上：“我们是一家人，我怎会有这心思？找春金兰到陈家闹事，只想逼浩宇纳商妓为妾，未曾想会闹到司理院，还请殿下劝说陈家长辈将此事私了。”
“I did.”

“陈家长辈愿意交给司理院，一来是信任我，二来也是不愿纵容陈家子弟。这份魄力，本王相当佩服，又岂会反过来劝陈家长辈私了？”
“What did you say to him?”

陈浩东假装哭道：“呜呜……，这么说来，浩宇必然要被判死刑，岂不是我的罪过？还请殿下饶他一命，呜呜……”
Thinking a beat, Chen Haodong answered smooth. “I feared he’d perjure himself in court and warned him lying would get him harsher punishment once exposed. Told him to tell the truth—that way the Chens would let Haoyu take Chun Jinlan in to save his neck. Best outcome for all.”

通过一番问话，申简辰能够确定，陈浩东为了继承陈家产业费尽心机，连一点兄弟之情都不顾，这种人若继承陈家产业，陈家的未来十分堪忧。
“And do you believe his testimony today was true?”

申简辰不想再问下去，对方说话滴水不漏，也问不出什么：“陈大公子不当戏子实在屈才，今日就聊到这里。”
“Every word.”

仪王要离开，陈浩东松口气，面向仪王出去的背影：“今日之事，还请殿下替草民保密！”
“Are you trying to get Chen Haoyu executed? Rape is a capital crime under Song law.”

申简辰很是不屑，心想，自己做下丑事，还想让别人替你保密，实在可笑！
Chen Haodong’s forehead nearly grazed the floor. “We’re family—how could I wish him dead?! I only pushed Chun Jinlan to force him to take a courtesan as concubine, never dreamed it would land at the Judicial Office! Please urge the Chen elders to settle this privately, Your Highness!”

走出宅子，申简辰利用轻功跳上一棵高树。
“The Chen elders came to the Judicial Office because they trust me and refuse to coddle a guilty kinsman. I admire their integrity—why would I urge them to sweep it under the rug?”

千里寻应该已乘坐马车回城，他打算踩着乡下的高树，边欣赏夜色，边朝幽林别居的方向而去。
Chen Haodong broke into crocodile tears. “Waaah... if Haoyu gets sentenced to death, it’ll be on my head! Spare his life, Your Highness!”

刚踩了十来棵树，竟然看到温信带着司理院的几名差役从树下经过。
Every word confirmed Chen Haodong would stab his own cousin in the back for the family fortune—handing the Chen empire to a snake like him spelled disaster. Still, his answers were airtight for now. “Shame you didn’t become an actor, Eldest Young Master Chen. We’re done here.”

于是用轻功落在他们面前：“这么晚温都头还在办差？”
Relieved, Chen Haodong called after his retreating back, “Please keep tonight’s matter confidential, Your Highness!”

仪王突然从天而降，大家吃惊的同时，赶紧行礼：“拜见王爷！”
Shen Jianchen scoffed to himself—doing dirty deeds and begging witnesses to keep quiet!

申简辰礼貌性地说道：“免礼，你们这是要去哪里？”
Vaulting up into a tall tree outside, knowing Qianli Xun had taken the carriage back to town, he leaped treetop to treetop toward Youlin Retreat under the moonlight—only to spot Wen Xin marching beneath him with a squad of constables after ten trees.

温信回道：“陈家在乡下的宅子就在附近，白天来过一回，没查出什么。属下想，既然事情是在晚上发生，不如趁天黑过再查探一番，也许会有新的发现。”
Dropping right in front of them, he asked, “Still on duty this late, Chief Constable Wen?”

既然碰到，申简辰决定亲自前往：“本王随你们去看看。”
Startled by the prince dropping from the sky, the men bowed. “Greetings, Your Highness!”

……
“At ease. Where are you headed?”

仪王离开后，春金蝉整理好衣装，出来扶起陈浩东：“你我刚才亲热时不会被他瞧见？”
“The Chens’ country house is right nearby,” Wen Xin explained. “Turned up nothing in daylight, so since the crime happened at night, I figured we’d inspect it after dark—might catch something fresh.”

陈浩东走到椅子跟前坐下：“八成是。”
“I’ll tag along and take a look.”

春金蝉笑道：“呵呵，我倒没什么，就当伺候大公子的同时，顺带伺候了一位王爷。”
Back at the tryst house, Chun Jinchan straightened her clothes and helped Chen Haodong up. “Think he saw us going at it?”

“亏你还笑得出！”陈浩东将屋子瞧一遍，“此处已被人发现，明儿我找人把这宅子给卖了。”
Collapsing into a chair, Chen Haodong muttered, “Odds are yes.”

春金蝉不满道：“是该卖了，总没人打扫，都是灰尘，老跟你这么偷偷摸摸的，什么时候是个头？”
Chun Jinchan giggled. “Doesn’t bother me—serving a prince along with Eldest Young Master is a bonus!”

陈浩东并未回答，掏出几张银票：“拿着，你先回城里，我再待会儿。”
“How can you still laugh?!” Glancing around, he snapped, “This place is burned—I’ll have someone sell it tomorrow.”

见到银票，春金兰喜笑颜开，接过来塞进怀里：“大公子出手就是阔绰，找好新地儿，差人告诉我。别再找乡下的地儿，太远。”然后在陈浩东的额头亲了一口，扭着腰肢离开。
“Good riddance—dusty, never cleaned, and sneaking around like rats gets old fast.”

陈浩东坐在椅子上，将自己与春金蝉亲热完说过的每一句话回想一遍，又把刚才与仪王的对话也回顾一回，确认没有说错一句，才深深吐口气，自语道：“仪王突然出现，说明他已盯上我。没想到这位王爷如此厉害，这么快怀疑到我头上。多亏我掩饰得好，加之一切处理得干净，他肯定找不出任何线索。”
Ignoring her gripe, he tossed out several bank notes. “Take these and head back to town. I’ll sit a while longer.”

……
Eyes lighting up at the silver notes, Chun Jinchan tucked them inside her bodice. “Generous as ever! Send word once you find a new spot—somewhere closer to town, not out in the sticks.” Planting a kiss on his forehead, she swayed out.

陈家在乡下的宅子虽比不上城里的陈府，却也有幽林别居那么大，不愧是有钱人家，乡下的宅子不住人，也收拾得整洁干净。
Alone in the chair, Chen Haodong replayed every word he had spoken to Chun Jinchan and Prince Yi—not a single slip. Exhaling long, he muttered, “Prince Yi showing up proves he’s onto me. Sharp prince—locked on this fast! Good thing I covered my tracks clean; he won’t dig up a shred of proof.”

司理院的人大晚上又来宅子，管家亲自迎接，见多了位年轻人，衣着虽普通，气度却不凡，温都头对他又恭敬，猜想应是参军大人，行礼道：“拜见大人。”
Meanwhile, the Chens’ country residence loomed ahead—smaller than the main manor in town, yet rivaling Youlin Retreat in size and kept spotless despite sitting empty.

温信发话道：“带我和大人再去陈公子关人的屋子走一趟。”
Greeting the constables at the gate, the caretaker noticed the plainly dressed young man whose presence commanded Wen Xin’s deference, guessed it was the Judicial Investigator, and bowed low. “Greetings, Your Lordship.”

管家说：“大人请。”
“Take His Lordship and me back to the room where Young Master Chen locked people up,” Wen Xin ordered.

申简辰边走边问：“宅子里有多少下人？”
“Right this way, Your Lordship.”

管家回道：“不多，五六十个。”
Walking along, Shen Jianchen asked, “How many servants work here?”

“温都头白天来过，你应该知道是为何事？”
“Not many—fifty or sixty.”

“小人知道，还不是二公子惹下的事？”
“Chief Constable Wen came by today, so you know why we’re here?”

“他常带人来此？”
“Yes, Your Lordship—Second Young Master’s old mess.”

“以前常来，不知道从哪里搞来些姑娘家，放在宅子里当丫鬟，听说陈家别的宅子也有。两日前，有几个丫鬟还跑去城里的陈府闹事，逼着二少夫人帮着找婆家。二少夫人心善，都给找了，也算圆满。”
“Did he bring girls here often?”

申简辰心想，被陈浩宇抢来的姑娘还真不少，还好那些他欺负过的姑娘没有告到官府，若一个个告，他不知得死多少回。
“Used to—no idea where he found them, turning them into maids here and at other outside properties. Two days ago a bunch marched over to the main manor demanding Second Young Madam find them husbands. Good-hearted lady that she is, she sorted every one out.”

想到这里，替千里寻感到不值。埋怨命运实在无理，为何要给二人设置两条难以相交的道路。
Shen Jianchen felt a twinge for Qianli Xun—Chen Haoyu had snatched so many girls he’d have lost his head ten times over if they had sued. How unfair of fate to lay such mismatched paths for the two of them.
