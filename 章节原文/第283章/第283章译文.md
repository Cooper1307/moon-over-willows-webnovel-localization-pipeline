## Chapter 283: Haodong's Secret Tryst Exposed

Refusing to look down yet frozen by the unmistakable sounds rising through the gap, Qianli Xun flushed behind her silk veil and hissed at Shen Jianchen peering calmly down through the hole: "Sure staring hard—spitting image of a married man who's consummated his marriage!"

Flushing sheepishly, he sat back under the bright moon. "Sorry—just didn't want to miss clues." After a quiet beat, he murmured, "Do you blame me? For breaking my vow to stay chaste?"

"I never held you to it."

A wry smile tugged his mouth. "What about you and Chen Haoyu—did you consummate too?"

Determined to kill his last shred of hope, she lied: "Of... of course." Tense from lying, her hand shifted and nudged the loose roof tile—*CRASH!*

"Who's out there?!" Haodong yelled inside.

"Meow!" Shen Jianchen improvised instantly.

"Just a stray cat—keep going, Eldest Young Master!" Chun Jinchan giggled.

Sitting silent under the moonlight while the room below rocked on, Qianli Xun felt an ache of outrage for Ye Anya—divorcing this pig was the smartest move Anya could make—while Shen Jianchen mourned the cruel irony of life.

At last the clamor ceased, and Chun Jinchan brought up Chun Jinlan. Both rooftop spies dove for the peephole at once, cracking foreheads with a muffled wince before Shen Jianchen yielded the spot. Down below, Haodong bragged cautiously about visiting Scarface—still keeping his wording vague enough not to admit faking evidence—before grabbing Jinchan for round two.

Having heard enough and eager to slip back into Chen Manor before anyone noticed her gone, Qianli Xun took his carriage and bounded off into the night.

Remaining behind, Shen Jianchen dropped into the yard, pocketed his mask, and pushed the unlatched front door wide with a crisp creak. "Having a grand time sneaking around, Eldest Young Master Chen?"

Half-naked and trembling like a leaf, Haodong stumbled out and hit his knees. Cornered, he smoothly confessed to his affair, to egging Jinlan on out of "pity" plus ambition for the inheritance, and to lecturing Scarface to "tell the truth"—never once slipping on the actual rape, even turning on fake tears begging Prince Yi to spare Haoyu's neck!

"Wasted talent not joining an opera troupe," Shen Jianchen scoffed, walking out empty-handed yet 100% convinced Haodong was rotten to the core.

Bounding across treetops toward Youlin Retreat, Shen Jianchen spotted Wen Xin and constables marching below—headed back to the Chen country estate for a nighttime re-inspection—and dropped straight down to join them. Meanwhile, back in his secret love nest, Haodong paid off Chun Jinchan with banknotes, resolved to sell the compromised cottage tomorrow, and exhaled in relief that he'd left zero hard proof behind.
