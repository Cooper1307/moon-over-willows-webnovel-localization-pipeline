第245章 姑嫂聊天谈贵人
## Chapter 245: Sisters-in-Law Talk of Royal Guests

写完朱淑真的词，千里寻从头到尾品味一番。很明显，朱淑真的悲戚之情与她相比更甚。从词中看得出，她应该是生病了。人生病时，往往最脆弱，需要有人相伴左右，可身边没有爱人能给予她些许温暖，唯有泪相伴。
千里寻想起穿越前一次生病的经历。
千里寻一年到头很少生病，一旦感冒，比别人严重许多，除了鼻塞流鼻涕咳嗽以外，发起烧来浑身难受。
有一段时日，千里拳庄的徒弟们接下商演，倾巢出动，去外地拍戏，要整整一个月才能回来，她正好清闲下来，一个人住在二十二层，宅在家里写小说。
谁知重感冒袭来，身上肌肉酸痛，无力再写小说，便收了笔记本，拿出体温计，夹在胳肢窝下，倒在床上。
估摸着时间差不多时，将体温计拿出瞧了瞧，三十八度六，果然发烧。
她撑着起来，想将体温计中的水银甩下去，都觉无力，只能将体温计放在床边的柜子上，打开抽屉，还有一盒降温药，可杯子和水没在旁边。后悔没留下一位女弟子，否则此时一个电话便有人前来照料。
没办法，只能自己动手。她努力爬起，全身的肌肉更加酸痛，脑袋晕晕乎乎，平常练习拳法积攒的功力在疾病面前根本没用，走出两步，便倒在地上。她的意识非常清晰，只是浑身无力，干脆趴在地上休息一会儿。
电话铃声响起，本以为是哪位徒弟打来，不如趁机叫回一个。手机放在床边的柜子上，她努力爬到柜子跟前，却看到申简辰的名字。
她太需要人照顾，抓住一个是一个，伸出手将绿色的通话键划向一边，再点开免提。
“喂，千里寻，听说你的弟子们都去了外地，你一个人太无聊，不如我去陪你？”
“我……我生病了，你……来吧，密码是……我生日。”
“啊，你病了，等着，我马上到。”
那边挂掉电话后，千里寻心里变得坦然许多，第一次觉出申简辰的好来。她放心地坐在地上，趴在床边休息，谁让她连起来的力气都没有。
等千里寻睁开眼睛时，她已躺在床上，身上盖着被子，额头上贴着物理降温贴，一股饭香扑鼻而来。
仔细瞧去，申简辰身着围裙，双手捧着一碗面出现在她面前。
“醒了，正好做了西红柿鸡蛋面，最适合生病的人吃。”申简辰说着，把碗放在床边的矮柜上，他则挨床边坐下，“我第一次见人感冒这么严重，多亏有我在。”
“谢谢你，抽屉里有药，你帮我倒杯水，我吃完药就好。”
“我已看过，抽屉里的药全部过期，我重新买了药，已帮你服下。”
“什么？我已吃过药？怎么一点儿印象都没有？”
“我来时，你坐在地上，趴在床边，昏昏沉沉，被人卖了都不知道。是我把你抱起来放到床上，发现你的药全部过期，便跑到楼下的药店买来新药。你虽昏昏沉沉倒挺配合，让张嘴就张嘴，我顺利帮你把药吃下。”
申简辰取掉退烧贴，用手摸摸她的额头：“烧退了一些，快坐起来，把面吃了，一会儿还要吃药，总不能空着肚子。”
Reading back over Zhu Shuzhen's lyric, Qianli Xun felt the deeper abyss beneath the original poetess's grief—clearly ill on top of lonely, crying through cold nights without one warm hand beside her.

身上已没有之前难受，千里寻坐起来，在床头靠好。她忽然觉得申简辰很不错，如果她想恋爱想结婚可以找他这样的。
A memory from her own previous life surfaced sharp and clear. Rarely sick, whenever a heavy flu caught Qianli Xun, it floored her completely—fever, chills, aching bones. One month all her disciples at Qianli Fist Manor had gone out of town for a film stunt gig, leaving her solo in her twenty-second-floor apartment writing web fiction.

申简辰将碗递到她面前：“怎么不接着？难道想让我喂你吃？我倒是想，怕你不乐意。”
A brutal fever hit overnight. Too weak to type, she slipped a thermometer under her arm and collapsed onto the mattress—thirty-eight point six degrees. Too shaky even to shake the mercury down, she spotted fever pills in her nightstand drawer with zero water nearby, cursing herself for not keeping one female student behind. Pushing herself off the bed for water, martial arts strength useless against a virus, she took two dizzy steps and crumpled onto the floorboards—mind clear, limbs lead.

千里寻接过碗：“老娘自己会吃，哪需要你喂？”
Her phone rang on the nightstand. Crawling over hoping it was a student, she saw Shen Jianchen's name glowing on the screen. Desperate for any help, she swiped the green answer button and tapped speaker.

……
"Hey, Qianli Xun! Heard your whole squad left town—bored solo? Want company?"

正沉浸在回忆中，陈文心进来，身后跟着丫鬟月桂。
"I'm... sick... come over... door code's my birthday..."

“淑真，快别待在书桌前，我好不容易来一趟，陪我聊聊天儿。”
"Whoa—hang tight, I'm on my way!"

陈文心的声音将千里寻从回忆拉回现实，自从她嫁过来后，嫂嫂还是第一次来。
The line clicked dead, and an odd rush of safety washed over her—the very first time she realized Shen Jianchen was someone she could lean on. Resting her cheek against the bedframe, she drifted out.

千里寻迅速整理好心情，起身迎上去：“嫂嫂许久没来，淑真甚是想念。”
When she opened her eyes, she lay tucked under her quilt with a cooling patch on her forehead, rich tomato-and-egg aroma drifting in as Shen Jianchen walked up in an apron carrying a steaming bowl of noodles.

陈文心将屋子瞧上一遍：“我一进院子就觉出不同来，没想到屋子的布置也跟以前大不相同，难怪浩宇这么快被你改变过来。”
"Awake! Whipped up tomato-egg noodles—best thing for a fever!" Setting the bowl on the nightstand, he perched beside her. "Never saw anyone catch a cold this hard—lucky I called!"

听此话，嫂嫂应该见过长辈。
"Thanks... grab me water for the pills in the drawer?"

“莱宝呢？怎么没一起带来？”
"Checked already—every box in there was expired! Ran down to the pharmacy and got fresh medicine—already gave you a dose."

“在老太太院子里玩儿，有长辈们和丫鬟陪着，不缺我一个，正好抽空来看看你。没想到你嫁到陈家没多久，陈家的气象与从前大不相同。以前长辈们很少去奶奶的院子，现在倒好，我娘还有两位婶婶都聚集在那里，奶奶更是满脸堆笑。她们都告诉我是你的功劳。你倒好，反而一个人待在屋子里，冷冷清清的。”
"I took medicine? Don't remember a thing!"

“奶奶有人陪，不缺我一个，我也好趁此机会写诗作词，省得辜负了女词人的美称。”
"Found you slumped on the floor half out of it—anyone could've kidnapped you! Carried you to bed, grabbed fresh meds downstairs, and you opened right up like a good patient!" Peeling off the patch, he tested her brow with his palm. "Fever's coming down. Sit up and eat before your next dose—can't take pills on an empty stomach."

“又写新词？”陈文心好奇地走到书桌前将淑真写的词仔细瞧一遍，批评道，“怎么还写这么悲悲戚戚的词？好像陈家慢待你似的？”
Feeling strength return as she propped herself against the headboard, a quiet thought crossed her mind: Guy's actually decent—if I ever decide to date or marry, someone like him wouldn't be bad.

又走过去拉起淑真的手：“让我瞧瞧，脸色白里透红，眉头也挺舒展，精神着呢？为何要写‘愁病’二字？依我看你就是无病呻 吟。”
"Not taking the bowl?" he teased. "Want me to spoon-feed you? Gladly, just scared you'll deck me!"

“无病呻 吟”四个字将千里寻逗乐，扑哧笑出声来：“随便写写，就当我是无病呻 吟。嫂嫂别站着，坐下说话。”
Snatching the bowl, she snapped, "I've got two hands—feed yourself!"

“整日不是躺着就是坐着，多没趣，我们到园子里走走，大好春光，可不要辜负。”
"Shuzhen! Quit hiding at your desk—finally got free to visit, come chat!"

千里寻点头同意。
Chen Wenxin's cheerful voice yanked Qianli Xun back to Song-dynasty Lin'an, walking in with Yuegui—her sister-in-law's first call since the wedding.

二人来到南边的园子，这里的园子多种些花树，有开满白色小花的流苏树，有满树红艳艳的木棉树，还有黄花风铃木和娇艳玉兰花。
Brushing off her nostalgia, Qianli Xun rose warmly. "Missed you, Sister-in-law!"

千里寻一边嗅着花香，一边听嫂嫂说话。苦荞与月桂跟在后面聊着下人们之间的事。
Scanning the room, Chen Wenxin marveled, "Whole courtyard feels fresh, and this room looks totally transformed! No wonder Haoyu shaped up overnight!"

她们穿过小径，洒满从树上掉落下来的花瓣，即使不忍心踩，也不得不踩，满地都是，若不去踩，哪来的落脚之处？千里寻猛然想起陆游的两句词：“零落成泥碾作尘，只有香如故。”尽管是写梅花，此时倒也应景。
"Where's Laibao?"

花开花落，不就是人生的写照。花开时，美艳无比；花落后，被人踩在脚下，化作尘土，就像人死后亦入土为安，成为尘土的一部分。她忽然觉得女娲娘娘用泥土造人的神话并非神话，也许人真的由泥土而来，最终回归泥土。
"Playing in Grandmother's yard with plenty of elders and maids watching, so I slipped over to see you. Hard to believe how much Chen Manor brightened since you arrived—Mother and both aunts all gathering around a beaming Grandmother, everyone crediting you, while you hide solo in here!"

花瓣化为泥土前，在世间留下一缕清香，她千里寻在短暂的人生中又会留下什么？她有幸经历两段人生，尽管到现在都无法分清哪一段是真，哪一段是假？可却有清清楚楚的记忆。
"Grandmother's got plenty of company—gives me quiet to write verses and earn my poetess title."

穿越前的她留下被大家诟病的网络小说和一群练过千里拳的弟子，足以印证她来过世间一趟；穿越后，大家只知她是朱淑真，她更要努力让千里寻在这个时代留下些什么，哪怕只像脚下踩的花瓣留下一段瞬间即逝的清香，也会让她觉得没有白来一遭。
"New lyric?" Stepping to the desk, Chen Wenxin read the fresh sheet and chided, "Why still writing mournful stuff like the Chens mistreat you?" Catching Shuzhen's hand, she eyed her rosy cheeks. "Glowing skin, smooth brow, bursting with health—writing 'sorrow and sickness'? Pure melodrama!"

“淑真，谢谢你把浩宇管教好。”
"Pure melodrama" cracked Qianli Xun up. "Just scribbling—call it melodrama! Sit down!"

嫂嫂感谢的话打断千里寻关于人生的思索：“这两日长辈们感谢得够多，嫂嫂又来，我怕自己承受不起，说不定过上几日大家都会失望。”
"Sitting indoors wastes gorgeous spring weather—let's stroll the garden!"

陈文心不解：“什么意思？”
Agreeing, they wandered into the south flower garden past white fringe trees, blazing crimson kapoks, golden trumpet blossoms, and creamy magnolias while Kuqiao and Yuegui trailed gossiping behind.

“只怕陈浩宇坚持不了几日。”这种打击人的话千里寻不好在长辈面前说，面对嫂嫂倒可直言不讳。
The path lay carpeted in fallen petals impossible to avoid stepping on, bringing Lu You's famous line to mind: "Fallen into mud and ground to dust, only its fragrance remains." [TN: Translator's rendering]

陈文心笑道：“我的亲弟弟我最了解，他一旦变好，便是真的好，不会轻易改变。”
Petals blooming bright, crushed to earth yet leaving scent behind—human life mirrored in miniature. She had lived two vivid lives—modern web novelist and martial master leaving books and disciples behind; Song poetess borrowing Zhu Shuzhen's name. Surely Qianli Xun herself could leave one bright trace of fragrance in this century too.

嫂嫂对自己的弟弟毫无道理的信任，千里寻懒得再批判，还是让时间检验一切。她对韦嬷嬷的判断深信不疑，也相信陈浩宇是变好，但还是认为陈浩宇坚持不了多久，毕竟不良习气早已养成，想要彻底改变没那么容易。
"Thank you for reforming Haoyu, Shuzhen," Chen Wenxin broke her reverie.

陈文心接着说：“浩宇现在变好，你要试着接纳他，跟他好好过日子。我嫁给你哥时，也谈不上喜欢，现在我们不是过得挺好？所以，不要把感情看得太重，人好是关键，感情婚后也可以慢慢培养。”
"Too many thank-yous already—scared everyone will end up disappointed in a few days."

千里寻算是听出来，嫂嫂是替长辈来劝说她，不如给她个拖延时间的答复：“嫂嫂也说人好是关键，陈浩宇好不好仍需时间检验，等到我觉得他好时，再谈要不要接纳他。”
"How so?"

陈文心高兴道：“有你这句话就行。”
"Bet Chen Haoyu doesn't last a week," she said bluntly—safe to speak candidly with Wenxin.

拖延时间的答复果然奏效，嫂嫂不再提及刚才的话题，与她沉默着欣赏周围的风景，又踩踏一会儿花瓣，才问道：“你知道我今日回陈府所为何事？”
Chen Wenxin laughed. "Nobody knows my kid brother better than me—once he truly flips, he stays flipped!"

“嫂嫂不说，我怎么知道？”
Leaving time to prove who was right, Qianli Xun held her tongue as Chen Wenxin added, "Since he's turning good, open your heart to him. I wasn't madly in love with your brother on our wedding day either, yet look how happy we are! Good character comes first; love grows after marriage."

“公爹派我来的？”
Recognizing a gentle lobby from the elders, Qianli Xun gave a neat stall: "Character takes time to prove. Once I see he truly holds steady, we'll talk."

“我爹？嫂嫂回娘家怎么还需我爹派遣？”
"Fair enough!" Satisfied, Chen Wenxin dropped it, crunching petals a moment before asking, "Know why I really came home today?"

“临安又要举行大的宴会，邀请陈家参加，公爹派我回来转达他的意思，当然，我也顺便回娘家一趟。”
"Tell me."

“这倒奇怪，往常举办宴会发帖子即可，这次倒让嫂嫂亲自来说，宴会与往常有何不同？”
"Father-in-law sent me."

“难道你没听说？”
"My father sent you to visit your own maiden home?"

“听说什么？”
"Big banquet coming up in Lin'an—Chen family's invited, so Father-in-law sent me personally to relay his message while visiting home."

“仪王和佳和郡主从汴梁回来了。”
"Usually an invitation card does the trick—what's so special about this banquet?"

终于回来，千里寻既高兴又忧虑，不知该如何回应，半晌才问出：“他们何时回来的？”
"Haven't heard?"

“今日一早刚到，两人走时十分低调，回来仍旧低调，多亏公爹多了个心眼儿，早早给幽林别居的李管家打过招呼，贵人一到，李管家便差人送来信儿，公爹才知他们回来。一早带领临安官员去看望贵人，明日便举办宴会，一来为贵人接风洗尘；二来算是给两位贵人在临安补办婚宴。”
"Heard what?"

“补办婚宴。”千里寻心中嘀咕着，他们再次回到临安，已是夫妻，以后见到梦容恐怕得称呼仪王妃。
"Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe just got back from Bianliang!"

“即使是为仪王补办婚宴，只要差人送来请帖，陈家也不敢怠慢，真想不通我爹为何如此谨慎？还让嫂嫂来传话？”
Back already! Joy and dread tangled instant in Qianli Xun's chest before she managed, "When did they arrive?"

陈文心小心提醒：“你认真想想便知。”
"First thing this morning—slipped in quiet as they left! Lucky Father-in-law thought ahead and tipped off Butler Li at Youlin Retreat to send word the minute they arrived. Led all the Lin'an officials to pay respects at dawn, and tomorrow they're throwing a grand banquet—half welcome-home, half makeup wedding feast!"

千里寻先疑惑片刻，便立刻明白，后悔不该追问嫂嫂前来传话之事。
Makeup wedding feast—married for real now. From today on, Mengrong was Princess Consort Yi. "Even for a royal wedding feast, why send you in person instead of a card?"

她成婚前申简辰到朱府阻挠婚事，朱晞颜冒着丢掉乌纱帽的危险带领家人下跪阻止；成亲当日申简辰又抢亲，梦容还因此受伤，陈家担心贵人怪罪，肯定无不小心。朱陈两家必然更加重视这场宴会。
"Think a second and you'll know," Chen Wenxin hinted gently.

陈文心接着说：“举办宴会所有的花销均由陈家出。”
Realization stung instant: Prince Yi barging into Zhu Manor before the wedding forcing Prefect Zhu to kneel, then raiding the bridal procession leaving Mengrong wounded—both families walked on eggshells terrified of royal wrath!

千里寻这才觉出愧疚来，她惹下的事却让陈家买单，心中着实不安：“其实仪王人很好，他不会跟朱陈两家计较。再说那些事都是他一厢情愿为之，更怪不到两家的头上来。”
"Chen family's footing the entire banquet bill," Chen Wenxin added.

“话虽如此，仪王毕竟是贵人，面子还是要给的。捶丸场时我就看出来，他对你有意思，仔细想来，陈家可是与仪王抢人，而你却嫁到陈家，仪王肯定面子上挂不住。我爹和公爹倍加小心，仪王刚走时就开始差人筹备宴会，只等人一回来就举办，他们不过是希望仪王不要因为没有得到你而怪罪两家。”
Guilt pricked sharp. "Prince Yi isn't petty—he wouldn't punish either family, especially since he acted on his own!"

千里寻担心宴会也邀请自己，她还没有做好面对梦容的准备：“不知宴会都邀请哪些人参加？”
"Royal pride still matters," Chen Wenxin explained. "Even back at the chuiwan match I saw he had eyes for you—technically the Chens snatched the bride right out from under a prince! Father and Father-in-law started prepping this feast the day Prince Yi left just to smooth things over and keep both families safe."

“临安的官员和有身份之人，只准携带正室参加，我是借了你哥哥的光，才能有幸参加，而你与浩宇根本不在被邀请之列。哦，有一个例外，乔家除乔老爷和夫人外还邀请了乔文仙，他会带苗知瑜参加。”
Dreading an invite before she felt ready to face Mengrong, Qianli Xun asked carefully, "Who's on the guest list?"

千里寻在内心长舒一口气，还好她不必参加。尽管见梦容是迟早的事，但不是现在。
"Lin'an officials and dignitaries with primary wives only—I'm only going on your brother's coattails. You and Haoyu aren't even invited. One exception: besides Master and Madam Qiao, Qiao Wenxian's invited with Miao Zhiyu."

千里寻又埋怨自己为何如此小心谨慎？她没有做过任何对不起梦容之事。可接着她又反问自己时不时想起申简辰，难道就对得起梦容？
Relief washed through Qianli Xun—spared for now. Meeting Mengrong was inevitable eventually, just not tomorrow. Yet guilt jabbed back: I did nothing wrong to Mengrong—wait, silently missing Shen Jianchen every other day, is that really clean?
