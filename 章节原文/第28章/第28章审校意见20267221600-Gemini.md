### 🎯 核心修改段落对比 (Targeted Revisions)

#### 1. 文化背景的地道转换（对应第9段：给名字画框）
> **【原文】** 想起读书时，偶尔看到一本书编者很多，其中有编者已去世，会在此人的名字外加一个方框。千里寻学着，在"朱淑真"三个字的外面画了一个长形的方框。完成后，搁下笔，拿起红绸看了看，十分满意。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    She recalled a book from her student days with many editors. When one had passed away, a box was drawn around their name. Following this custom, Qianli Xun drew a rectangular frame around the three characters "Zhu Shuzhen." Setting down the brush, she held up the silk and examined her work with satisfaction.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Remembering a **modern publishing convention** where **deceased authors** had a black box drawn around their names, Qianli Xun traced a neat rectangle around the characters for "Zhu Shuzhen." Satisfied with her work, she set the brush down and admired the silk ribbon.
*   **💡 修改理由:** 原译文描述略显拖沓。精修版直接用 "modern publishing convention"（现代出版惯例）进行归纳总结，降低了跨文化理解门槛。

#### 2. 武侠动作描写的张力强化（对应第34段：轻功飞上神柳）
> **【原文】** 千里寻没说话，攥紧红绸，启动轻功，"嗖"地一声蹿到神柳的高处，踩在柔软的柳枝上，如同步履平地。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Without a word, Qianli Xun gripped the silk and **activated her qinggong**. With a soft whoosh, she shot upward, landing among the high branches. She stepped onto the swaying willow limbs as though they were solid ground.
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Without a word, Qianli Xun gripped the silk, **channeled her inner energy, and executed her lightweight *qinggong***. ***Swoosh!*** She launched herself into the high canopy, landing gracefully on a slender branch. She balanced on the swaying willow limbs as effortlessly as if she were walking on solid ground.
*   **💡 修改理由:** "activated her qinggong" 听起来像按下了机器开关，缺乏古风感。改为 channel inner energy 更有武侠内功的韵味，并强化了拟声词格式。

#### 3. 女主霸气人设及网文独白规范（对应第41、46段）
> **【原文】** 千里寻根本没将林觉放在眼里："林觉算老几？老娘的事老娘做主！"
> **【原文】** "桃花饭？"千里寻嘀咕着，她想起陆游的两句诗...果然陆放翁不欺后人...嘿嘿，还是不要把他的诗句念出来为好。

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Qianli Xun **didn't even put Lin Jue in her eyes**. "Who does Lin Jue think he is? **I** decide my own affairs!"
    ...
    "Peach blossom rice?" Qianli Xun muttered. She remembered two lines from Lu You's poem... **Hehe, it's better not to recite his poem.**
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Qianli Xun **snorted, dismissing Lin Jue entirely**. "Who does Lin Jue think he is? **Yours truly** makes her own decisions!"
    ...
    ***"Peach blossom rice?"*** Qianli Xun muttered to herself. She immediately recalled two lines from a poem by Lu You... ***Hehe, it's best not to recite his poem out loud.***
*   **💡 修改理由:** 修正了中式英语 "put in her eyes"，并严格执行术语表中“老娘 = Yours truly”的设定。**将内心的吐槽和心理活动严格统一为斜体 (Italics)**，这是英文网文增强代入感的核心格式。

#### 4. 夫妻对峙时的火药味与地道对骂（对应第55段：圆你个头）
> **【原文】** 本来心情挺好，被这家伙两句调侃的话搞得炸毛，千里寻生气道："圆你个头！老娘过来有事通知你，还是进去说。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Her mood was originally quite good, but this guy's two teasing sentences made her furious. Qianli Xun snapped, "**Consummate this!** I came to notify you of something. Let's talk inside."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Qianli Xun had been in a decent mood, but this bastard's arrogant teasing instantly grated on her nerves. "**Consummate my foot!**" she snapped. "Yours truly is just here to inform you of something. Let's talk inside."
*   **💡 修改理由:** "Consummate this" 杀伤力不够。英文中常用来表达“……个鬼/个头”的经典地道用法是 "**My foot!**"，完美还原了千里寻翻白眼怒怼的语气。

#### 5. “野男人/野女人”的精准对仗（对应第62、63段）
> **【原文】** 林觉松口气："只要不是跟野男人在一起就行。"
> "野男人"三个字直接伤到千里寻的自尊："你都把野女人带到家里，我即使跟野男人在一起，你也管不着，还是先管好你自己。"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Lin Jue breathed a sigh of relief. "As long as you're not with a **wild man**, it's fine."
    The words "**wild man**" struck Qianli Xun's pride. "You've brought a **wild woman** home. Even if I'm with a **wild man**, you can't control me. Better take care of yourself first."
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Lin Jue visibly breathed a sigh of relief. "As long as you're not sneaking around with some **wild lover**, fine."
    The phrase ***wild lover*** severely insulted Qianli Xun's pride. "You have the nerve to bring a **wild harlot** into this house, yet you try to police me? Even if I *were* with another man, you have no right to interfere. Better take care of your own mess first."
*   **💡 修改理由:** "Wild man" 在英语里容易让人联想到“野人/山顶洞人”。这里是指出轨对象，改为 "**wild lover**"（野男人/情夫）和 "**wild harlot**"（野妓女/野女人）对仗，更能凸显针锋相对的辱骂感。

#### 6. 绿茶女配“春金巧”的神态刻画（对应第65段）
> **【原文】** 春金巧把门关上，走到林觉跟前，夹着嗓子，劝道："练兵官，你这过得什么日子！不如同意和离，放她走吧？"

*   **修改前 (Before):**
    Chun Jinqiao closed the door, walked to Lin Jue, and spoke **in an affected voice**. "Military Training Officer, what kind of life are you living! Why not agree to the divorce and let her go?"
*   **修改后 (After):**
    Chun Jinqiao closed the door properly and **sashayed** up to Lin Jue, **feigning a sweet, whiny voice**. "Military Training Officer, what kind of miserable life are you living? Why not just agree to the divorce and let her go?"
*   **💡 修改理由:** 强化反派角色的神态。用 "sashayed" (扭着腰走) 替换平淡的 "walked"，用 "feigning a sweet, whiny voice" (假装甜腻哀怨的嗓音) 翻译“夹着嗓子”，人物画面感跃然纸上。
