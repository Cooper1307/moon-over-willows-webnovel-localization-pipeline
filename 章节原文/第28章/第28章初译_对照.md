第28章 又一棵千年神柳
## Chapter 28: Another Millennial Divine Willow

苦荞见小姐拿着红色的绸带从山洞出来，立刻站起来迎上去：“小姐，可有收获？”
千里寻点点头：“随我去正殿。”
她们原路返回，来到尼姑庵的正殿，又传来连续不断的念佛声。有人正在跪拜，女尼忙着敲木鱼。千里寻不便打扰，与苦荞站在一旁耐心等待。
拜佛人起身从正殿后门离开后，女尼抬眼看到千里寻手中的红绸，主动走上前，双手合十：“阿弥陀佛，施主请跟我来。”
千里寻带苦荞跟随女尼拐到正殿后面。
女尼说：“这里有笔墨，施主先写下名字，我再带您去找神柳。”
正殿后面有张桌子，放着笔墨。千里寻走到跟前，将红绸铺好，拿起旁边的笔，要写下名字时犹豫起来。
尽管她认为自己是千里寻，可周围的人认为她是朱淑真，也只会用这个名字称呼她。到底该写哪一个？犹豫半晌，最终决定两个名字都写。她用小楷先写“千里寻”，再写下“朱淑真”。
想起读书时，偶尔看到一本书编者很多，其中有编者已去世，会在此人的名字外加一个方框。千里寻学着，在“朱淑真”三个字的外面画了一个长形的方框。完成后，搁下笔，拿起红绸看了看，十分满意。
“小师傅，我已写好，麻烦前面带路。”
“请跟我来。”
出了正殿，从西边的门向尼姑庵后面拐去。尼姑庵并不大，很快来到后门，跨出后门，便是后山。
“我送施主到此，神柳在后山之下，您顺着这条路一直往下走，会看到一个池塘，神柳在池塘边上，善男信女喜欢把红绸挂在神柳上，找起来十分容易。”
说完，女尼从后门进了尼姑庵，顺手把门关上。
千里寻急道：“门怎么关了？一会儿我们还要回去！”
苦荞说：“小姐不用担心，我们待会儿下山，直接从山脚下绕到上山的入口处，不就行了。”
千里寻恍然大悟：“说的有道理，赶紧下山。”
苦荞从小姐手中接过红绸：“还是我替小姐装着。”
千里寻没有拒绝，松手给了苦荞，看着她把红绸卷好，塞进袖子里。
主仆二人顺着山路一直向下走。
后山的景色相当宜人，千里寻却无心欣赏，她一直挂念神柳，关键是这棵神柳也生长在池塘边，会不会让她产生一种故地重游之感？
下山的路很好走，看样子从尼姑庵去神柳处挂红绸的人不少，她猜测定以年轻人居多，因为山路被踩得相当结实。
下山比上山要容易许多，不知不觉已到山脚下，周围都是石头和疯长的野草，有的野草甚至与千里寻一样高。还好，野草中也被踏出一条路，苦荞走在前面探路，千里寻一边拨动身旁的野草，一边向外走。
苦荞先走了出去：“小姐，果然有个池塘。”
千里寻用手拨开最后挡住视线的野草，将步子迈出去，眼前出现一片池塘，不是很大，里面生长着荷花，一只青蛙蹲在荷叶上，没有发出叫声，瞪着两只鼓鼓的眼睛观察周围，没多时，纵身跃起，跳入水中，水中绿色的浮萍立刻向四周散去。
千里寻只觉一切似曾相识。她快走几步，来到池塘边寻找神柳。
的确很好找，池塘边只有一棵柳树，关键是树上挂着许多红绸，映着绿叶，使整棵树成熟中更显娇艳。
千里寻绕着池塘向神柳走去，她的心跳加快，到底是梦？还是她又回到穿越之前？眼前的神柳与地震时倾倒的那棵一模一样，唯一不同的是它的枝条上挂着许多红绸。
Seeing her mistress step out of the cave holding a red silk ribbon, Kuqiao jumped up at once. "Did it go well, Miss?"

来到树下，千里寻抑制不住激动的心情，泪水从眼中滚落下来，她忍不住上前抱住神柳粗壮的树干，像抱住亲人一般，轻声说：“带我回去吧，我不喜欢这个时代，尤其不喜欢莫名其妙的婚姻。”
Qianli Xun nodded. "Back to the main hall."

她闭上眼睛，用心感受一会儿，希望神柳能给予她肯定的答复。
Retracing their steps into the nunnery's main hall, they heard unbroken Buddhist chanting. Pilgrims knelt in prayer while the young nun tapped her wooden fish, so Qianli Xun and Kuqiao waited to the side rather than intrude. Once the worshipers rose and left through the rear exit, the young nun spotted the red silk ribbon in Qianli Xun's hand, pressed her palms together, and stepped over. "Amitabha. Come with me, benefactor."

可神柳没有任何反应，冷冰冰的树干也没有一丝温度。千里寻睁开双眼，一脸失落。
She led Qianli Xun and Kuqiao behind the main hall, where brush and ink waited on a small table. "Write your name first, benefactor, then I'll show you the way to the Divine Willow."

她慢慢放开神柳，向后退去，抬头望着繁茂的枝叶和随风轻轻摆动的红绸，更加确信它就是地震后带她来到这个时代的神柳。
Spreading the red silk flat and lifting the brush, Qianli Xun paused. Deep down she was Qianli Xun, yet everyone around her knew her only as Zhu Shuzhen. Which name belonged on the silk? After a long beat, she chose both, writing "Qianli Xun" in crisp small regular script and "Zhu Shuzhen" beside it. Remembering how modern books drew a neat box around the name of a deceased co-author, she traced a slim rectangle around "Zhu Shuzhen." Setting down the brush, she lifted the ribbon and nodded in satisfaction.

她转过身，想要寻找她与申简辰决斗的场地。周围全是杂草和石头，不远处只有山，除了池塘和神柳，其他完全不一样。千里寻清醒许多，这是一千多年前神柳所在之地，又如何能找到一千多年后的痕迹？
"All done, Little Master. Lead the way."

更奇怪的是，穿越前的时代神柳在另一个城市，穿越之后它却在临安地界，一切到底到底是做梦还是现实？
"Right this way." Exiting the west side of the main hall, they crossed the compact nunnery to its back gate, which opened straight onto the rear slope of the hill. "This is as far as I escort you, benefactor. The Divine Willow stands at the foot of the rear mountain. Follow this trail straight down until you reach a pond; the willow grows right on its bank, easy to find from all the red ribbons devotees hang on its boughs."

苦荞从袖中取出红绸：“小姐，我帮您把红绸挂上去吧？”苦荞找到一条柳枝，准备将红绸绑上去。
With that, the young nun stepped back through the gate and pulled it shut.

千里寻从迷惑中清醒过来，阻止道：“净光法师说要挂在西面的枝条上。”
"Hey, why'd she shut the door?! How do we get back?" Qianli Xun yelped.

苦荞只好住手，抬头望望太阳，刚刚偏西，她走到太阳的方向：“小姐，这里是西面，我找一条柳枝挂上去。”
"No worries, Miss," Kuqiao reassured her. "Once we're at the bottom, we just skirt the foot of the hill back around to the front trailhead."

千里寻瞧着神柳，发现凡是人能够到的枝条一大半已挂上红绸，她不想与众人的红绸挤在一起。再说这么低，很容易被人伸手拽走。
"Makes sense! Let's head down."

她向神柳高处望去：“我打算挂在那里。”
"Let me hold the ribbon for you, Miss." Handing it over, Qianli Xun watched Kuqiao roll the silk neat and tuck it inside her sleeve before they started down the rear slope.

苦荞顺着小姐的目光望去：“啊！小姐，这么高，怎么上去？您不是为难我吗？”
Lovely as the hillside looked, Qianli Xun had eyes for one thing alone: this Divine Willow also stood beside a pond! Would seeing it feel like stepping back into her old life? The downhill trail ran smooth and packed solid—clearly crowds of young people trekked down from the nunnery to tie ribbons. Heading down took half the effort of climbing; before long they reached the base amid boulders and wild grass growing tall as Qianli Xun's shoulders. Good thing a trodden path cut through the weeds; Kuqiao parted the brush in front while Qianli Xun pushed stalks aside behind her.

千里寻伸出手：“拿来！”
Stepping into the clear first, Kuqiao called out, "Miss, there really is a pond!"

苦荞乖乖地把红绸放到小姐手中：“小姐，这么高，您能上去吗？”
Brushing past the last tangle of tall grass, Qianli Xun stepped out before a modest pond filled with lotuses. A frog squatted mute on a lotus leaf, bulging eyes surveying the bank before springing into the water and scattering green duckweed across the ripples. Déjà vu hit like a wave. Hurrying along the bank, she scanned for the willow.

千里寻没说话，攥紧红绸，启动轻功，“嗖”地一声蹿到神柳的高处，踩在柔软的柳枝上，如同步履平地。
Impossible to miss: a lone ancient willow stood by the water, draped top to bottom in red silk ribbons glowing bright against emerald leaves, lending the grand tree a striking charm. Rounding the pond toward the trunk, Qianli Xun felt her heart hammer. Was this a dream, or had she slipped back before the quake? Trunk for trunk, bough for bough, this willow mirrored the exact tree that had toppled during the earthquake, save for the ribbons fluttering across its branches.

苦荞不由赞道：“我家小姐真厉害！”
Reaching the trunk, emotion broke loose; tears rolled down her cheeks as she threw both arms around the rough bark like hugging long-lost kin, whispering, "Take me home... I hate this era, especially this ridiculous marriage!"

千里寻在高处找到一条柳枝，将红绸绑好，刚要跳下时，发现离池塘不远处的密林中藏着一座府宅，不像是普通人家的住的宅子。
Shutting her eyes, she waited in tense silence, willing the willow to answer. Nothing stirred—only cold, lifeless bark under her palms. Opening her eyes, hollow letdown flooded her chest. Stepping back to gaze up at lush fronds and ribbons swaying in the breeze, she knew beyond doubt this was the same willow that had hurled her across time.

她跳下后指着府宅的方向：“那里竟然有座宅子，你知不知道是谁家的？”
Turning around, she looked for the open ground where she and Shen Jianchen had fought. Nothing stood there but weeds, stones, and green hills; aside from pond and willow, not one landmark matched. Reality settled back in: this was the willow a thousand years earlier; how could modern traces exist here? Stranger still, in her own era the willow had grown in another city altogether, yet here it sat right outside Lin'an. Was any of this real, or one long dream?

苦荞朝小姐指的方向望去，只看到一片茂密的树林，根本看不到宅子。
Drawing the ribbon from her sleeve, Kuqiao stepped toward a low willow limb. "Let me tie it up for you, Miss."

“看来你也不知道，算了，跟我们又没什么关系，还是赶紧回去吧。”
Snapping out of her daze, Qianli Xun stopped her. "Master Jingguang said tie it on a western branch!"

千里寻又看了一眼神柳，她打算以后常来此处坐坐，在陌生的时代神柳令她倍感亲切。
Checking the afternoon sun slanting west, Kuqiao walked around to the western side. "West side right here, Miss. Let me find a good branch."

她与苦荞环绕山脚走着，一路上也是杂草和顽石相伴。想起净光法师的建议，千里寻问道：“苦荞，你可知哪里有竹林？”
Looking up, Qianli Xun saw almost every limb within reach crowded thick with red silk. No way was she wedging hers down low where anyone could yank it off! Pointing high toward the upper canopy, she said, "I'm tying mine up there."

苦荞在小姐前面边走边说：“我们村的人以种竹子为生，周围有一大片竹林。小姐问这个干吗？”
Following her finger, Kuqiao gasped, "Whoa, Miss! How do we climb way up there? You're messing with me!"

“还不是净光法师给我出的主意，说竹子对我好，让我找个有竹林的地方住上一个月。”
"Hand it here."

“太好了！小姐就去我家，我也正好回家陪爹娘住一段时日。”苦荞很开心，很快又担忧道，“只怕姑爷不会同意。”
Placing the ribbon in her palm, Kuqiao blinked. "You can really jump that high, Miss?"

千里寻根本没将林觉放在眼里：“林觉算老几？老娘的事老娘做主！”
Without a word, Qianli Xun clenched the silk, kicked off with light-foot skill, and shot straight up into the high willow boughs, balancing on supple branches light as air.

主仆二人聊着天儿，不知不觉来到大山入口处。林家的车夫正坐在石头上，拿着饼子大嚼大咽，见夫人回来，收起半个饼子，走到马车前，做好回城的准备。
"My Miss is awesome!" Kuqiao cheered from the bank.

到达林府时，天刚黑。
Fastening the ribbon tight to a high western limb, Qianli Xun paused just before dropping down—spotting an estate hidden deep inside dense woods not far past the pond, far too grand for ordinary folk. Dropping light beside Kuqiao, she pointed toward the trees. "There's a manor tucked over there. Know whose it is?"

林觉早已从下人口中得知朱淑真出去一整天，待她回到竹兰居后，派人把车夫叫来，问道：“她今日去了哪里？”
Squinting where she pointed, Kuqiao saw only solid forest canopy and no rooftop at all. "Guess you don't know either," Qianli Xun shrugged. "Forget it, none of our business. Let's head back."

“回主君，夫人去了尼姑庵。”
Casting one last look back at the willow—she meant to visit often, drawn by its familiar comfort in a foreign age—she skirted the mountain base with Kuqiao amid weeds and boulders. Remembering Master Jingguang's parting counsel, she asked, "Kuqiao, know anywhere with a bamboo grove?"

“尼姑庵？去那里做什么？”
Walking ahead, Kuqiao brightened. "My home village grows bamboo for a living! We're surrounded by a massive bamboo grove. Why ask, Miss?"

“小的只负责赶车，没跟着上去，夫人到底做些什么，我不大清楚。据我猜测应该是拜拜佛，求神佛保佑。”
"Master Jingguang's advice. Says bamboo brings me luck and told me to live near a bamboo grove for one month."

“谁不知道！要你说？下去吧！”林觉是想知道娘子有没有借着拜佛跟哪位才子私会。
"Awesome! Come stay at my home, Miss—I get to spend time with my parents too!" Joy flashed, then dimmed with worry. "Only, Son-in-law probably won't agree."

千里寻回到竹兰居，看到里面栽种的竹子，又想起净光法师的建议，心想，这里也有竹子，难道住这里不行吗？非要找个竹林？很快她想通，肯定是竹兰居的竹子太少，对人的影响不够大。
Qianli Xun scoffed. "Who cares what Lin Jue thinks? My life, my call!"

千里寻换完衣裳，感到腹中饥饿：“苦荞，我饿了，弄些吃的来。”
Chatting along, they rounded the hill back to the front trailhead, where the Lin coachman sat on a boulder munching flatbread; spotting Madam, he stowed the rest and climbed onto the box ready for town.

苦荞去厨房安排，不一会儿将吃的端来，两道菜，一碗鸡汤，倒没有什么特别之处。
Dusk had just fallen when they rolled back to Lin Manor. Already told by servants that Zhu Shuzhen had been gone all day, Lin Jue summoned the coachman the moment she returned to Zhulan Residence. "Where did she go today?"

当苦荞将一碗饭放下后，千里寻被吸引，只见饭上放着些桃花的花瓣：“这是什么饭？如此别致？”
"To the nunnery, Master."

“桃花饭。”
"The nunnery? Doing what?"

“桃花饭？”千里寻嘀咕着，她想起陆游的两句诗，“软炊香甑桃花饭,浅酌清尊竹叶醅。”果然陆放翁不欺后人，仔细想来，现在是宣和四年，陆放翁还未出生，嘿嘿，还是不要把他的诗句念出来为好，“早已过了桃花开放的季节，哪里来的桃花？”
"I stayed by the carriage at the foot of the hill, Master, so I couldn't say for sure. Guessing she went to burn incense and pray for blessings."

“小姐难道没听过地桃花？”
"Anyone could guess that! Get out!" All Lin Jue wanted to know was whether she had used prayer as cover to meet some young scholar.

千里寻摇摇头：“没听过。”
Back in Zhulan Residence, eyeing the clumps of bamboo planted in her courtyard, Qianli Xun wondered why staying right here didn't count, then figured a handful of garden stalks couldn't match a real forest. Changing into lounge clothes, hunger hit. "Starving, Kuqiao. Fetch me dinner!"

“林府的路边种的到处都是，小姐肯定见过，只是不知道它们是地桃花而已。”
Kuqiao hurried to the kitchen and returned soon with two dishes and a bowl of chicken soup, nothing unusual until she set down a bowl of rice dotted with pink petals. "What's this fancy rice?" Qianli Xun asked.

的确在林府见过，地桃花大约一米来高，花朵外形很像桃花。自己真是孤陋寡闻，什么都不懂。千里寻拿起筷子夹一口桃花饭放入口中，香甜软糯：“不错，还有红枣的香味。”
"Peach Blossom Rice."

“蒸桃花饭时，必须放红枣，小姐再吃几口就能看到红枣。”
"Peach Blossom Rice?" Qianli Xun murmured, recalling Lu You's famous couplet: "Soft-steamed fragrant rice with peach blossoms, light sips from clear cups of bamboo-leaf brew." [TN: Translator's rendering] Lu You hadn't lied to later ages—good thing she didn't speak it aloud, since in the fourth year of Xuanhe Lu You wasn't even born yet! "Peach season passed ages ago. Where'd peach blossoms come from?"

千里寻用完晚饭，安排苦荞今晚收拾好东西，明日一早去有竹林的地方居住。想到林觉，不如知会他一声，毕竟她住在林府，名誉上还是他的妻子。
"Haven't you heard of Caesarweed, ground peach blossom, Miss?"

千里寻独自一人往林觉住的屋子而来，刚走到门外，听到里面传出女子娇滴滴的笑声，猜到林觉又把商妓带到家里。
Qianli Xun shook her head. "Never."

本打算转身回去，转念一想，她本不在乎林觉，又何必忌讳面对此事，不如让他看到自己无所谓的态度，也许会更加后悔没有同意和离。再说院子里不时有下人经过，见她来了又走，岂不是会偷偷笑她太软弱。
"Grows all along the paths in Lin Manor! You've seen plenty, just didn't know its name."

千里寻叫住一名下人：“你，去敲门，告诉林觉，我有事与他说。”
Right, those waist-high bushes with blooms shaped just like peach blossoms! So much to learn here. Lifting her chopsticks, she tasted a mouthful—sweet, soft, and sticky. "Delicious! Smells like red dates too!"

下人走到门前，向里面报告：“主君，夫人来了，有事与您说。”
"You always steam Peach Blossom Rice with red dates, Miss. Dig deeper and you'll hit them."

里面的笑声立刻停止，过了一会儿，门被打开。下人瞅准机会赶紧离开，恐怕主君与夫人要干一架，还是躲远些，别伤到自己。
After supper, Qianli Xun told Kuqiao to pack tonight so they could leave for the bamboo grove first thing tomorrow. Thinking of Lin Jue—well, she lived under his roof and still wore his wife's title; might as well drop him a courtesy notice.

林觉站在门口：“夫人不是说井水不犯河水吗？怎么前来找我？难道是想通，来找为夫圆房？”
Walking alone to Lin Jue's quarters, she heard a woman's coy giggle drifting through the closed door—another courtesan! Half tempted to turn back, she checked herself. She didn't care two cents about Lin Jue anyway; why act shy? Showing him she couldn't care less might make him regret refusing divorce sooner, and with servants passing through the yard, backing off would make them smirk at her for being timid.

本来心情挺好，被这家伙两句调侃的话搞得炸毛，千里寻生气道：“圆你个头！老娘过来有事通知你，还是进去说。”
Flagging down a passing servant, she ordered, "Go knock and tell Lin Jue I have something to tell him."

千里寻快步向屋里走去，到门口用肩膀将林觉撞到一边。她虽不介意，可在好奇心的驱使下想要看看里面的女子是何等货色，结果一个人影儿都没有。
Stepping to the door, the servant announced, "Master, Madam is here to speak with you." The giggling cut off dead; moments later the door cracked open, and the servant bolted fast, sure a brawl was about to break out.

千里寻转过身笑道：“呵呵，林觉，何必把人藏起来？老娘有那么可怕吗？再说老娘根本不介意你带商妓回家，现在就收回约法三章的第二条，以后你想带多少商妓回林府都可以，只要你喜欢。”
Standing in the doorway, Lin Jue sneered, "Didn't you swear we stay out of each other's hair? What brings you to my door? Changed your mind and came to consummate our marriage?"

春金巧从里面出来：“练兵官，我说没事，你偏让我躲起来。”
That jab lit her fuse on the spot. "Consummate my foot! I'm here to give you notice. Let's talk inside!" Shouldering Lin Jue aside, she marched in, half curious to inspect his guest, only to find the front chamber empty. Spinning back with a mocking laugh, she teased, "Ha! Why hide her, Lin Jue? Am I that scary? Relax—I don't care one bit who you drag home! Consider Rule Two revoked: bring as many courtesans into Lin Manor as your heart desires!"

千里寻盯着浓妆艳抹的春金巧，继续笑道：“呵呵，林觉，没想到你找商妓倒挺专一。”
Chun Jinqiao sauntered out from the inner room. "Told you it was fine, Officer Lin; you're the one who made me hide!"

林觉没好气地问：“你到底找我什么事？”
Eyeing heavily rouged Chun Jinqiao, Qianli Xun laughed again. "Well, well—loyal to one courtesan at least!"

“我是来通知你，从明天起，我要到乡下住一段时日，至于什么时候回来，看心情。”
Irritated, Lin Jue snapped, "What do you actually want?"

“是你一个人还是有别人？”
"Just giving notice: starting tomorrow I'm moving out to the countryside for a spell. When I come back depends on my mood."

“当然不是我一个人，还有苦荞。”
"Alone, or with somebody else?"

林觉松口气：“只要不是跟野男人在一起就行。”
"Not alone. Kuqiao's coming."

“野男人”三个字直接伤到千里寻的自尊：“你都把野女人带到家里，我即使跟野男人在一起，你也管不着，还是先管好你自己。”
Relief relaxed his jaw. "Long as you aren't off with some wild man."

说完，千里寻摔门而出。
"Wild man" pricked her pride. "You haul wild women straight into the house! Even if I went off with a wild man, none of your business! Tend your own mess first!" Slamming the door hard behind her, she marched out.

春金巧把门关上，走到林觉跟前，夹着嗓子，劝道：“练兵官，你这过得什么日子！不如同意和离，放她走吧？我不指望你为我赎身把我留在身边，只要能娶一位知你疼你的夫人，我也就心满意足。”
Latching the door, Chun Jinqiao sidled up and purred in a sugary voice, "Officer Lin, what kind of life is this? Just agree to mutual divorce and let her go! I don't expect you to buy my freedom—just want you wed to a sweet wife who loves and pampers you!"

林觉无法抵抗春金巧撒娇的声音，把她抱起来：“我暂时还不想放她走，等想通的时候再说。”然后抱着春金巧进了里屋。
Helpless against her cooing, Lin Jue scooped her right off her feet. "Not letting her go just yet. Wait till I'm ready." Carrying Chun Jinqiao, he strode into the inner chamber.
