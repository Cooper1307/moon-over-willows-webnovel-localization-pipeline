# Chapter 28: Another Millennial Divine Willow

---

苦荞见小姐拿着红色的绸带从山洞出来，立刻站起来迎上去："小姐，可有收获？"

Kubiao saw her miss come out of the cave holding a red silk ribbon. She immediately stood up and went to greet her. "Miss, did you get what you wanted?"

---

千里寻点点头："随我去正殿。"

Qianli Xun nodded. "Come with me to the main hall."

---

她们原路返回，来到尼姑庵的正殿，又传来连续不断的念佛声。有人正在跪拜，女尼忙着敲木鱼。千里寻不便打扰，与苦荞站在一旁耐心等待。

They returned the way they came to the nunnery's main hall. The continuous sound of chanting Buddha's name could be heard again. Someone was kneeling and bowing, while the nun was busy striking the wooden fish. Qianli Xun didn't want to disturb them, so she stood to the side with Kubiao and waited patiently.

---

拜佛人起身从正殿后门离开后，女尼抬眼看到千里寻手中的红绸，主动走上前，双手合十："阿弥陀佛，施主请跟我来。"

After the worshipper stood up and left through the back door of the main hall, the nun looked up and saw the red silk in Qianli Xun's hand. She took the initiative to walk over, palms together. "Amitabha. Donor, please follow me."

---

千里寻带苦荞跟随女尼拐到正殿后面。

Qianli Xun led Kubiao to follow the nun as they turned to the back of the main hall.

---

女尼说："这里有笔墨，施主先写下名字，我再带您去找神柳。"

The nun said, "There are brush and ink here. Donor, please write your name first, then I'll take you to find the divine willow."

---

正殿后面有张桌子，放着笔墨。千里寻走到跟前，将红绸铺好，拿起旁边的笔，要写下名字时犹豫起来。

Behind the main hall was a table with brush and ink. Qianli Xun walked over, spread out the red silk, picked up the brush beside it, and hesitated when she was about to write her name.

---

尽管她认为自己是千里寻，可周围的人认为她是朱淑真，也只会用这个名字称呼她。到底该写哪一个？犹豫半晌，最终决定两个名字都写。她用小楷先写"千里寻"，再写下"朱淑真"。

Although she considered herself Qianli Xun, the people around her thought she was Zhu Shuzhen and would only call her by that name. Which one should she write? After hesitating for a while, she finally decided to write both names. She wrote "Qianli Xun" first in small regular script, then "Zhu Shuzhen."

---

想起读书时，偶尔看到一本书编者很多，其中有编者已去世，会在此人的名字外加一个方框。千里寻学着，在"朱淑真"三个字的外面画了一个长形的方框。完成后，搁下笔，拿起红绸看了看，十分满意。

She remembered when she was studying, she occasionally saw a book with many editors. If one of the editors had passed away, a box would be drawn around that person's name. Qianli Xun followed this practice and drew a rectangular box around the three characters "Zhu Shuzhen." After finishing, she put down the brush, picked up the red silk and looked at it, very satisfied.

---

"小师傅，我已写好，麻烦前面带路。"

"Young sister, I've finished writing. Please lead the way."

---

"请跟我来。"

"Please follow me."

---

出了正殿，从西边的门向尼姑庵后面拐去。尼姑庵并不大，很快来到后门，跨出后门，便是后山。

They left the main hall and turned from the west door to the back of the nunnery. The nunnery wasn't large. They soon reached the back door. Stepping out the back door was the back mountain.

---

"我送施主到此，神柳在后山之下，您顺着这条路一直往下走，会看到一个池塘，神柳在池塘边上，善男信女喜欢把红绸挂在神柳上，找起来十分容易。"

"I'll send the donor this far. The divine willow is at the foot of the back mountain. Follow this road all the way down. You'll see a pond. The divine willow is by the pond. Good men and faithful women like to hang red silk on the divine willow. It's very easy to find."

---

说完，女尼从后门进了尼姑庵，顺手把门关上。

After speaking, the nun entered the nunnery through the back door and closed it behind her.

---

千里寻急道："门怎么关了？一会儿我们还要回去！"

Qianli Xun said anxiously, "Why did you close the door? We need to go back later!"

---

苦荞说："小姐不用担心，我们待会儿下山，直接从山脚下绕到上山的入口处，不就行了。"

Kubiao said, "Don't worry, Miss. Later when we go down the mountain, we can just go around from the foot of the mountain to the entrance where we came up. That'll work."

---

千里寻恍然大悟："说的有道理，赶紧下山。"

Qianli Xun suddenly understood. "That makes sense. Let's hurry down the mountain."

---

苦荞从小姐手中接过红绸："还是我替小姐装着。"

Kubiao took the red silk from her miss's hand. "Let me hold it for you, Miss."

---

千里寻没有拒绝，松手给了苦荞，看着她把红绸卷好，塞进袖子里。

Qianli Xun didn't refuse. She let go and gave it to Kubiao, watching her roll up the red silk and stuff it into her sleeve.

---

主仆二人顺着山路一直向下走。

The mistress and maid walked down the mountain road together.

---

后山的景色相当宜人，千里寻却无心欣赏，她一直挂念神柳，关键是这棵神柳也生长在池塘边，会不会让她产生一种故地重游之感？

The scenery of the back mountain was quite pleasant, but Qianli Xun had no mind to appreciate it. She was preoccupied with the divine willow. The key was that this divine willow also grew by a pond. Would it give her a feeling of revisiting an old place?

---

下山的路很好走，看样子从尼姑庵去神柳处挂红绸的人不少，她猜测定以年轻人居多，因为山路被踩得相当结实。

The road down the mountain was easy to walk. It seemed that many people went from the nunnery to the divine willow to hang red silk. She guessed it was mostly young people because the mountain road had been trodden quite solid.

---

下山比上山要容易许多，不知不觉已到山脚下，周围都是石头和疯长的野草，有的野草甚至与千里寻一样高。还好，野草中也被踏出一条路，苦荞走在前面探路，千里寻一边拨动身旁的野草，一边向外走。

Going down the mountain was much easier than going up. Unaware, they had reached the foot of the mountain. All around were stones and wildly growing grass. Some grass was even as tall as Qianli Xun. Fortunately, a path had been trodden through the grass. Kubiao walked ahead to scout the way. Qianli Xun pushed aside the grass beside her as she walked outward.

---

苦荞先走了出去："小姐，果然有个池塘。"

Kubiao walked out first. "Miss, there really is a pond."

---

千里寻用手拨开最后挡住视线的野草，将步子迈出去，眼前出现一片池塘，不是很大，里面生长着荷花，一只青蛙蹲在荷叶上，没有发出叫声，瞪着两只鼓鼓的眼睛观察周围，没多时，纵身跃起，跳入水中，水中绿色的浮萍立刻向四周散去。

Qianli Xun pushed aside the last grass blocking her view with her hand and stepped out. A pond appeared before her. It wasn't very large. Lotus grew inside it. A frog squatted on a lotus leaf, not making a sound, staring with two bulging eyes observing the surroundings. After a while, it leaped up and jumped into the water. The green duckweed in the water immediately scattered in all directions.

---

千里寻只觉一切似曾相识。她快走几步，来到池塘边寻找神柳。

Qianli Xun felt that everything seemed familiar. She walked a few steps quickly and came to the edge of the pond to look for the divine willow.

---

的确很好找，池塘边只有一棵柳树，关键是树上挂着许多红绸，映着绿叶，使整棵树成熟中更显娇艳。

It was indeed easy to find. There was only one willow tree by the pond. The key was that many red silk ribbons hung on the tree. Against the green leaves, the whole tree looked even more charming in its maturity.

---

千里寻绕着池塘向神柳走去，她的心跳加快，到底是梦？还是她又回到穿越之前？眼前的神柳与地震时倾倒的那棵一模一样，唯一不同的是它的枝条上挂着许多红绸。

Qianli Xun walked around the pond toward the divine willow. Her heartbeat quickened. Was this a dream? Or had she returned to before her transmigration? The divine willow before her was exactly the same as the one that had fallen during the earthquake. The only difference was that many red silk ribbons hung on its branches.

---

来到树下，千里寻抑制不住激动的心情，泪水从眼中滚落下来，她忍不住上前抱住神柳粗壮的树干，像抱住亲人一般，轻声说："带我回去吧，我不喜欢这个时代，尤其不喜欢莫名其妙的婚姻。"

Coming under the tree, Qianli Xun couldn't suppress her excitement. Tears rolled down from her eyes. She couldn't help but step forward and hug the thick trunk of the divine willow, like hugging a family member. She said softly, "Take me back. I don't like this era, especially this inexplicable marriage."

---

她闭上眼睛，用心感受一会儿，希望神柳能给予她肯定的答复。

She closed her eyes and felt with her heart for a while, hoping the divine willow would give her an affirmative response.

---

可神柳没有任何反应，冷冰冰的树干也没有一丝温度。千里寻睁开双眼，一脸失落。

But the divine willow had no reaction. The cold trunk had no warmth at all. Qianli Xun opened her eyes, her face full of disappointment.

---

她慢慢放开神柳，向后退去，抬头望着繁茂的枝叶和随风轻轻摆动的红绸，更加确信它就是地震后带她来到这个时代的神柳。

She slowly let go of the divine willow and stepped back. Looking up at the lush branches and leaves and the red silk ribbons gently swaying in the wind, she became even more convinced that this was the divine willow that had brought her to this era after the earthquake.

---

她转过身，想要寻找她与申简辰决斗的场地。周围全是杂草和石头，不远处只有山，除了池塘和神柳，其他完全不一样。千里寻清醒许多，这是一千多年前神柳所在之地，又如何能找到一千多年后的痕迹？

She turned around, wanting to find the place where she had dueled with Shen Jianchen. All around were weeds and stones. Not far away were only mountains. Except for the pond and the divine willow, everything else was completely different. Qianli Xun became much more sober. This was the place where the divine willow was located over a thousand years ago. How could she find traces from over a thousand years later?

---

更奇怪的是，穿越前的时代神柳在另一个城市，穿越之后它却在临安地界，一切到底到底是做梦还是现实？

What was even stranger was that before the transmigration, the divine willow was in another city. After the transmigration, it was in the Lin'an region. Was all this a dream or reality?

---

苦荞从袖中取出红绸："小姐，我帮您把红绸挂上去吧？"苦荞找到一条柳枝，准备将红绸绑上去。

Kubiao took out the red silk from her sleeve. "Miss, shall I help you hang the red silk?" Kubiao found a willow branch and prepared to tie the red silk to it.

---

千里寻从迷惑中清醒过来，阻止道："净光法师说要挂在西面的枝条上。"

Qianli Xun came to her senses from her confusion and stopped her. "Master Jingguang said to hang it on the branches on the west side."

---

苦荞只好住手，抬头望望太阳，刚刚偏西，她走到太阳的方向："小姐，这里是西面，我找一条柳枝挂上去。"

Kubiao had to stop. She looked up at the sun, which had just moved to the west. She walked in the direction of the sun. "Miss, this is the west side. I'll find a willow branch to hang it on."

---

千里寻瞧着神柳，发现凡是人能够到的枝条一大半已挂上红绸，她不想与众人的红绸挤在一起。再说这么低，很容易被人伸手拽走。

Qianli Xun looked at the divine willow and found that more than half of the branches within people's reach already had red silk hung on them. She didn't want to crowd together with everyone else's red silk. Besides, being so low, it would be easy for someone to reach out and pull it away.

---

她向神柳高处望去："我打算挂在那里。"

She looked up to the higher part of the divine willow. "I plan to hang it there."

---

苦荞顺着小姐的目光望去："啊！小姐，这么高，怎么上去？您不是为难我吗？"

Kubiao followed her miss's gaze. "Ah! Miss, it's so high. How can you get up there? Aren't you making things difficult for me?"

---

千里寻伸出手："拿来！"

Qianli Xun reached out her hand. "Give it here!"

---

苦荞乖乖地把红绸放到小姐手中："小姐，这么高，您能上去吗？"

Kubiao obediently put the red silk in her miss's hand. "Miss, it's so high. Can you get up there?"

---

千里寻没说话，攥紧红绸，启动轻功，"嗖"地一声蹿到神柳的高处，踩在柔软的柳枝上，如同步履平地。

Qianli Xun didn't speak. She gripped the red silk tightly, activated her lightness skill, and with a "whoosh" sound, she shot up to the high part of the divine willow. She stepped on the soft willow branches as if walking on flat ground.

---

苦荞不由赞道："我家小姐真厉害！"

Kubiao couldn't help but praise. "My miss is truly amazing!"

---

千里寻在高处找到一条柳枝，将红绸绑好，刚要跳下时，发现离池塘不远处的密林中藏着一座府宅，不像是普通人家的住的宅子。

Qianli Xun found a willow branch at a high place and tied the red silk. Just as she was about to jump down, she discovered a mansion hidden in the dense forest not far from the pond. It didn't look like an ordinary person's residence.

---

她跳下后指着府宅的方向："那里竟然有座宅子，你知不知道是谁家的？"

After jumping down, she pointed in the direction of the mansion. "There's actually a mansion over there. Do you know whose it is?"

---

苦荞朝小姐指的方向望去，只看到一片茂密的树林，根本看不到宅子。

Kubiao looked in the direction her miss pointed. She only saw a dense forest and couldn't see the mansion at all.

---

"看来你也不知道，算了，跟我们又没什么关系，还是赶紧回去吧。"

"It seems you don't know either. Forget it. It has nothing to do with us anyway. Let's hurry back."

---

千里寻又看了一眼神柳，她打算以后常来此处坐坐，在陌生的时代神柳令她倍感亲切。

Qianli Xun took another look at the divine willow. She planned to come here often in the future to sit. In this unfamiliar era, the divine willow made her feel very much at home.

---

她与苦荞环绕山脚走着，一路上也是杂草和顽石相伴。想起净光法师的建议，千里寻问道："苦荞，你可知哪里有竹林？"

She and Kubiao walked around the foot of the mountain. Along the way, they were accompanied by weeds and stubborn stones. Remembering Master Jingguang's suggestion, Qianli Xun asked, "Kubiao, do you know where there's a bamboo forest?"

---

苦荞在小姐前面边走边说："我们村的人以种竹子为生，周围有一大片竹林。小姐问这个干吗？"

Kubiao walked ahead of her miss and said, "The people in our village make a living by growing bamboo. There's a large bamboo forest around. Why do you ask, Miss?"

---

"还不是净光法师给我出的主意，说竹子对我好，让我找个有竹林的地方住上一个月。"

"It's Master Jingguang's idea. She said bamboo is good for me and told me to find a place with a bamboo forest and live there for a month."

---

"太好了！小姐就去我家，我也正好回家陪爹娘住一段时日。"苦荞很开心，很快又担忧道，"只怕姑爷不会同意。"

"That's great! Miss, just come to my house. I also happen to go home and stay with my parents for a while." Kubiao was very happy, but soon worried. "I'm afraid the young master won't agree."

---

千里寻根本没将林觉放在眼里："林觉算老几？老娘的事老娘做主！"

Qianli Xun didn't even put Lin Jue in her eyes. "Who does Lin Jue think he is? This queen decides her own affairs!"

---

主仆二人聊着天儿，不知不觉来到大山入口处。林家的车夫正坐在石头上，拿着饼子大嚼大咽，见夫人回来，收起半个饼子，走到马车前，做好回城的准备。

The mistress and maid chatted as they walked. Unaware, they reached the entrance of the mountain. The Lin family's coachman was sitting on a stone, chewing on a big piece of flatbread. Seeing the madam return, he put away half the flatbread, walked to the carriage, and prepared to return to the city.

---

到达林府时，天刚黑。

When they arrived at the Lin residence, it had just gotten dark.

---

林觉早已从下人口中得知朱淑真出去一整天，待她回到竹兰居后，派人把车夫叫来，问道："她今日去了哪里？"

Lin Jue had long learned from the servants that Zhu Shuzhen had been out all day. After she returned to the Zhulan Residence, he sent someone to call the coachman and asked, "Where did she go today?"

---

"回主君，夫人去了尼姑庵。"

"Reporting to the young master, the madam went to the nunnery."

---

"尼姑庵？去那里做什么？"

"The nunnery? What did she go there for?"

---

"小的只负责赶车，没跟着上去，夫人到底做些什么，我不大清楚。据我猜测应该是拜拜佛，求神佛保佑。"

"This humble one was only responsible for driving the carriage. I didn't go up with her. I'm not very clear on what the madam did. I guess she probably worshiped Buddha and prayed for the Buddha's blessing."

---

"谁不知道！要你说？下去吧！"林觉是想知道娘子有没有借着拜佛跟哪位才子私会。

"Who doesn't know that! Do I need you to tell me? Get out!" Lin Jue wanted to know if his wife had used worshiping Buddha as an excuse to meet secretly with some talented scholar.

---

千里寻回到竹兰居，看到里面栽种的竹子，又想起净光法师的建议，心想，这里也有竹子，难道住这里不行吗？非要找个竹林？很快她想通，肯定是竹兰居的竹子太少，对人的影响不够大。

Qianli Xun returned to the Zhulan Residence. Seeing the bamboo planted inside, she remembered Master Jingguang's suggestion again. She thought, "There's bamboo here too. Isn't it okay to live here? Do I have to find a bamboo forest?" She soon figured it out. It must be that there's too little bamboo in the Zhulan Residence, not enough to have a big impact on a person.

---

千里寻换完衣裳，感到腹中饥饿："苦荞，我饿了，弄些吃的来。"

After changing clothes, Qianli Xun felt hungry. "Kubiao, I'm hungry. Get me something to eat."

---

苦荞去厨房安排，不一会儿将吃的端来，两道菜，一碗鸡汤，倒没有什么特别之处。

Kubiao went to the kitchen to arrange it. After a while, she brought the food. Two dishes and a bowl of chicken soup. Nothing particularly special.

---

当苦荞将一碗饭放下后，千里寻被吸引，只见饭上放着些桃花的花瓣："这是什么饭？如此别致？"

When Kubiao put down a bowl of rice, Qianli Xun was attracted. She saw peach blossom petals on the rice. "What kind of rice is this? So elegant?"

---

"桃花饭。"

"Peach blossom rice."

---

"桃花饭？"千里寻嘀咕着，她想起陆游的两句诗，"软炊香甑桃花饭,浅酌清尊竹叶醅。"果然陆放翁不欺后人，仔细想来，现在是宣和四年，陆放翁还未出生，嘿嘿，还是不要把他的诗句念出来为好，"早已过了桃花开放的季节，哪里来的桃花？"

"Peach blossom rice?" Qianli Xun muttered. She remembered two lines from Lu You's poem, "Softly steamed fragrant peach blossom rice, lightly sipped clear bamboo leaf wine." Indeed, Lu Fangweng didn't deceive later generations. Thinking carefully, it's now the 4th Year of Xuanhe. Lu Fangweng hasn't been born yet. Hehe, it's better not to recite his poem. "The peach blossom season has long passed. Where did the peach blossoms come from?"

---

"小姐难道没听过地桃花？"

"Miss, haven't you heard of ground peach blossoms?"

---

千里寻摇摇头："没听过。"

Qianli Xun shook her head. "Never heard of it."

---

"林府的路边种的到处都是，小姐肯定见过，只是不知道它们是地桃花而已。"

"They're planted everywhere along the roads in the Lin residence. Miss must have seen them. You just didn't know they were ground peach blossoms."

---

的确在林府见过，地桃花大约一米来高，花朵外形很像桃花。自己真是孤陋寡闻，什么都不懂。千里寻拿起筷子夹一口桃花饭放入口中，香甜软糯："不错，还有红枣的香味。"

Indeed, she had seen them in the Lin residence. Ground peach blossoms were about a meter tall, and the flowers looked very much like peach blossoms. She was truly ignorant and didn't understand anything. Qianli Xun picked up her chopsticks, took a bite of peach blossom rice, and put it in her mouth. It was sweet, fragrant, soft, and glutinous. "Not bad. There's also the fragrance of red dates."

---

"蒸桃花饭时，必须放红枣，小姐再吃几口就能看到红枣。"

"When steaming peach blossom rice, red dates must be added. Miss, if you eat a few more bites, you'll see the red dates."

---

千里寻用完晚饭，安排苦荞今晚收拾好东西，明日一早去有竹林的地方居住。想到林觉，不如知会他一声，毕竟她住在林府，名誉上还是他的妻子。

After finishing dinner, Qianli Xun arranged for Kubiao to pack their things tonight and go to the place with the bamboo forest early tomorrow morning. Thinking of Lin Jue, she might as well let him know. After all, she lived in the Lin residence and was still nominally his wife.

---

千里寻独自一人往林觉住的屋子而来，刚走到门外，听到里面传出女子娇滴滴的笑声，猜到林觉又把商妓带到家里。

Qianli Xun walked alone toward the room where Lin Jue lived. Just as she reached the door, she heard the coquettish laughter of a woman coming from inside. She guessed that Lin Jue had brought a merchant courtesan home again.

---

本打算转身回去，转念一想，她本不在乎林觉，又何必忌讳面对此事，不如让他看到自己无所谓的态度，也许会更加后悔没有同意和离。再说院子里不时有下人经过，见她来了又走，岂不是会偷偷笑她太软弱。

She originally planned to turn around and go back, but then thought again. She didn't care about Lin Jue anyway. Why should she avoid facing this? It's better to let him see her indifferent attitude. Maybe he'll regret even more not agreeing to the divorce. Besides, servants passed by the courtyard from time to time. If they saw her come and then leave, wouldn't they secretly laugh at her for being too weak?

---

千里寻叫住一名下人："你，去敲门，告诉林觉，我有事与他说。"

Qianli Xun called out to a servant. "You, go knock on the door and tell Lin Jue I have something to say to him."

---

下人走到门前，向里面报告："主君，夫人来了，有事与您说。"

The servant walked to the door and reported inside. "Young master, the madam is here. She has something to say to you."

---

里面的笑声立刻停止，过了一会儿，门被打开。下人瞅准机会赶紧离开，恐怕主君与夫人要干一架，还是躲远些，别伤到自己。

The laughter inside immediately stopped. After a while, the door was opened. The servant seized the opportunity to leave quickly, afraid that the young master and madam would have a fight. Better to stay far away so as not to get hurt.

---

林觉站在门口："夫人不是说井水不犯河水吗？怎么前来找我？难道是想通，来找为夫圆房？"

Lin Jue stood at the door. "Didn't the madam say to keep your well water from touching my river water? Why did you come to find me? Have you thought it through and come to find your husband to consummate the marriage?"

---

本来心情挺好，被这家伙两句调侃的话搞得炸毛，千里寻生气道："圆你个头！老娘过来有事通知你，还是进去说。"

Her mood was originally quite good, but this guy's two teasing sentences made her furious. Qianli Xun said angrily, "Consummate your head! This queen came to notify you of something. Let's talk inside."

---

千里寻快步向屋里走去，到门口用肩膀将林觉撞到一边。她虽不介意，可在好奇心的驱使下想要看看里面的女子是何等货色，结果一个人影儿都没有。

Qianli Xun walked quickly into the room. At the door, she used her shoulder to bump Lin Jue to the side. Although she didn't mind, curiosity drove her to see what kind of woman was inside. But there wasn't a single person in sight.

---

千里寻转过身笑道："呵呵，林觉，何必把人藏起来？老娘有那么可怕吗？再说老娘根本不介意你带商妓回家，现在就收回约法三章的第二条，以后你想带多少商妓回林府都可以，只要你喜欢。"

Qianli Xun turned around and smiled. "Hehe, Lin Jue, why hide the person? Is this queen that scary? Besides, this queen doesn't mind at all if you bring a merchant courtesan home. I'll withdraw the second point of the Three-Point Agreement right now. In the future, you can bring as many merchant courtesans back to the Lin residence as you want, as long as you like."

---

春金巧从里面出来："练兵官，我说没事，你偏让我躲起来。"

Chun Jinqiao came out from inside. "Military Training Officer, I said it was fine, but you insisted I hide."

---

千里寻盯着浓妆艳抹的春金巧，继续笑道："呵呵，林觉，没想到你找商妓倒挺专一。"

Qianli Xun stared at the heavily made-up Chun Jinqiao and continued to smile. "Hehe, Lin Jue, I didn't expect you to be quite dedicated in finding merchant courtesans."

---

林觉没好气地问："你到底找我什么事？"

Lin Jue asked in a bad mood. "What exactly did you come to find me for?"

---

"我是来通知你，从明天起，我要到乡下住一段时日，至于什么时候回来，看心情。"

"I came to notify you. Starting tomorrow, I'm going to live in the countryside for a while. As for when I'll come back, it depends on my mood."

---

"是你一个人还是有别人？"

"Are you going alone or with others?"

---

"当然不是我一个人，还有苦荞。"

"Of course not alone. Kubiao is coming too."

---

林觉松口气："只要不是跟野男人在一起就行。"

Lin Jue breathed a sigh of relief. "As long as you're not with a wild man, it's fine."

---

"野男人"三个字直接伤到千里寻的自尊："你都把野女人带到家里，我即使跟野男人在一起，你也管不着，还是先管好你自己。"

The three words "wild man" directly hurt Qianli Xun's pride. "You've brought a wild woman home. Even if I'm with a wild man, you can't control me. Better take care of yourself first."

---

说完，千里寻摔门而出。

After speaking, Qianli Xun slammed the door and left.

---

春金巧把门关上，走到林觉跟前，夹着嗓子，劝道："练兵官，你这过得什么日子！不如同意和离，放她走吧？我不指望你为我赎身把我留在身边，只要能娶一位知你疼你的夫人，我也就心满意足。"

Chun Jinqiao closed the door, walked to Lin Jue, and spoke in a affected voice. "Military Training Officer, what kind of life are you living! Why not agree to the divorce and let her go? I don't expect you to redeem me and keep me by your side. As long as you can marry a wife who knows and cares for you, I'll be content."

---

林觉无法抵抗春金巧撒娇的声音，把她抱起来："我暂时还不想放她走，等想通的时候再说。"然后抱着春金巧进了里屋。

Lin Jue couldn't resist Chun Jinqiao's coquettish voice. He picked her up. "I don't want to let her go for now. We'll talk about it when I've thought it through." Then he carried Chun Jinqiao into the inner room.
