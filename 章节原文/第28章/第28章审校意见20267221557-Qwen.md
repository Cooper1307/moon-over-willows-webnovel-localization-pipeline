## 四、表达正确性与地道性

### 1. 开篇

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Kuqiao saw her miss emerge from the cave, red silk ribbon in hand. She rose at once. "Miss, did you get what you wanted?"* |
| **修改后** | *Kuqiao was on her feet before her miss even cleared the cave mouth. "Well? Did it work?"* |
| **理由** | "She rose at once" 偏叙述体。"on her feet before" 更有画面动感。"Did you get what you wanted" 过于正式，丫鬟对小姐应更急切、更口语 |

---

### 2. "Mistress and maid"（出现 2 次）

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Mistress and maid walked down the mountain road together.* |
| **修改后** | *The two of them walked on.* 或 *They descended together, shoulders almost touching.* |
| **理由** | "Mistress and maid" 是中文"主仆二人"的直译，英文中极不自然（像维多利亚小说）。网文应用更轻的处理 |

---

### 3. "qinggong"

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Qianli Xun gripped the silk and activated her qinggong. With a soft whoosh, she shot upward, landing among the high branches.* |
| **修改后** | *She gripped the ribbon, bent her knees—and launched. The ground fell away. Wind roared past her ears. Then: stillness. She stood on a swaying willow branch as if it were a stone path.* |
| **理由** | "activated her qinggong" 像游戏技能描述。网文英文读者（尤其非武侠核心读者）对 qinggong 未必熟悉。用动作描写替代术语，更有沉浸感。若保留 qinggong，至少首次加 [TN: lightness skill] |

### 5. 陆游诗引用段

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *She remembered two lines from Lu You's poem, "Softly steamed fragrant peach blossom rice, lightly sipped clear bamboo leaf wine." Indeed, Lu Fangweng didn't deceive later generations. ## 七、吸引力评估

**做得好的：**
- 神柳前的情感爆发（抱树哭泣）翻译有感染力 ✓
- "Take me back" 的简短台词有冲击力 ✓
- 千里寻用轻功上树的画面感好 ✓
- 林觉"wild man" vs 千里寻"wild woman"的对仗保留 ✓
- 桃花饭的世界观细节有趣 ✓
- 结尾"门关上"的留白处理（修改后）✓

**可加强：**
- **抱树哭泣是本章情感最高潮**，但现译处理偏平：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Beneath the tree, emotion overwhelmed Qianli Xun. Tears spilled down her cheeks. She threw her arms around the thick trunk as if embracing a long-lost kin. "Take me back," she whispered. "I don't like this era—especially this inexplicable marriage."* |
| **修改后** | *She reached the trunk. Pressed her palms flat against the bark. <br><br> Cold. Just cold. <br><br> Then her arms were around it—hugging the wood like a person, like a relative she hadn't seen in a thousand years. Her face pressed into the rough bark. Tears came. She couldn't stop them. <br><br> "Take me back," she whispered. "Please. I hate it here. I hate this marriage. I hate that I can't—" <br><br> She swallowed. Tightened her grip. The tree said nothing. It was just a tree.* |
| **理由** | 原文"as if embracing a long-lost kin"是比喻，修改后用动作展示（hugging like a person）。加入"cold bark"的触觉、"tears came, she couldn't stop them"的失控感。"I hate that I can't—"用破折号截断，暗示她说不出口的更多委屈。最后"The tree said nothing. It was just a tree."单独成段，制造落空感 |

---

- **千里寻发现"这是千年前的同一个地点"的震撼**，现译偏叙述体：

| | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **修改前** | *Reality crashed over Qianli Xun. This was where the tree had stood over a thousand years ago. How could any trace of her time remain?* |
| **修改后** | *A thousand years. <br><br> This spot—this exact patch of dirt and weeds—had been a city park in her time. A parking lot. A convenience store. All of it, a thousand years in the future. <br><br> Of course there was nothing left. Of course.* |
| **理由** | 加入"parking lot, convenience store"等现代意象，让穿越女的时空错位感更具体、更有冲击力。"Of course." 重复两次，传达"理智上知道，情感上不接受"的矛盾 |
