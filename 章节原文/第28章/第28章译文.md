# Chapter 28: Another Millennial Divine Willow

Kuqiao saw her miss emerge from the cave, red silk ribbon in hand. She rose at once. "Miss, did you get what you wanted?"

Qianli Xun nodded. "Come with me to the main hall."

They retraced their steps to the nunnery's main hall, where the steady rhythm of chanting filled the air once more. A worshipper knelt in prostration while a nun struck the wooden fish. Not wishing to interrupt, Qianli Xun and Kuqiao stood aside and waited.

When the worshipper rose and departed through the rear exit, the nun glanced up, spotted the red silk in Qianli Xun's hand, and approached. Pressing her palms together, she intoned, "Amitabha. Donor, please follow me."

Qianli Xun led Kuqiao to follow the nun as they turned to the back of the main hall.

The nun spoke, "There are brush and ink here. Donor, please write your name first, then I'll take you to find the divine willow."

Behind the main hall stood a table bearing brush and ink. Qianli Xun approached, spread the red silk flat, and lifted the brush. But as she poised to write, she hesitated.

Though she was Qianli Xun at heart, everyone else saw her as Zhu Shuzhen. Which name should she write? After a moment's hesitation, she decided on both. In small regular script, she wrote "Qianli Xun" first, then "Zhu Shuzhen."

She recalled a book from her student days with many editors. When one had passed away, a box was drawn around their name. Following this custom, Qianli Xun drew a rectangular frame around the three characters "Zhu Shuzhen." Setting down the brush, she held up the silk and examined her work with satisfaction.

"Young sister, I've finished writing. Please lead the way."

"Please follow me."

They exited the main hall through the western gate and turned toward the rear of the nunnery. The compound was small, and they soon reached the back gate. Beyond it lay the mountain.

"I'll take you this far, Donor. The sacred tree is at the foot of the back mountain. Follow this road all the way down. You'll see a pond. The willow is by the pond. The devout like to hang red silk on it. It's very easy to find."

With that, the nun entered the nunnery through the back door and closed it behind her.

Qianli Xun demanded, "Why did you close the door? We need to go back later!"

Kuqiao reassured her, "Don't worry, Miss. When we head down, we can circle around from the mountain's base to where we entered. That'll work."

Qianli Xun's eyes lit up with understanding. "That makes sense. Let's hurry down."

Kuqiao took the red silk from her miss's hand. "Let me hold it for you, Miss."

Qianli Xun didn't refuse. She let go and watched Kuqiao roll up the silk and tuck it into her sleeve.

Mistress and maid walked down the mountain road together.

The back mountain's scenery was pleasant enough, but Qianli Xun had no eye for it. Her thoughts were fixed on the divine willow—and the fact that it, too, grew beside a pond. Would it stir a sense of déjà vu?

The descent was easy. Clearly, many pilgrims made the trek from the nunnery to the divine willow to hang their silks—mostly the young, judging by how firmly the path had been trodden.

The descent was far easier than the climb. Before they knew it, they had reached the mountain's base. Stones and wild grass surrounded them—some blades growing as tall as Qianli Xun herself. Fortunately, a path had been trampled through the undergrowth. Kuqiao led the way, while Qianli Xun parted the grass as she followed.

Kuqiao pushed through first. "Miss, there really is a pond."

Qianli Xun brushed aside the last blades blocking her view and stepped through. A small pond lay before her, dotted with lotus. A frog crouched on a leaf, silent, its bulging eyes scanning the surroundings. Then it leaped into the water, sending green duckweed scattering in all directions.

A wave of déjà vu washed over Qianli Xun. She quickened her pace to the pond's edge, searching for the divine willow.

It was easy to spot—only one willow stood by the pond, its branches heavy with silk ribbons. Against the green leaves, the red silks made the tree look strikingly vibrant.

Qianli Xun circled the pond toward the sacred tree, her heart pounding. Was this a dream? Had she somehow returned to before the transmigration? The willow before her was identical to the one that had toppled during the earthquake—save for the silk ribbons adorning its branches.

Beneath the tree, emotion overwhelmed Qianli Xun. Tears spilled down her cheeks. She threw her arms around the thick trunk as if embracing a long-lost kin. "Take me back," she whispered. "I don't like this era—especially this inexplicable marriage."

She closed her eyes and reached out with her senses, hoping the sacred tree would answer.

But the tree gave no sign. Its trunk was cold, devoid of any warmth. Qianli Xun opened her eyes, her face falling.

She slowly released her grip and stepped back. Looking up at the lush branches and the silk ribbons swaying gently in the breeze, she grew even more convinced—this was the tree that had brought her to this era after the earthquake.

She turned, searching for the spot where she had dueled with Shen Jianchen. Weeds and stones surrounded her; mountains rose in the distance. Save for the pond and the willow, everything was utterly different. Reality crashed over Qianli Xun. This was where the tree had stood over a thousand years ago. How could any trace of her time remain?

Stranger still—in her original time, the divine willow had stood in another city. Now, in this era, it was in the Lin'an region. Was any of this real, or merely a dream?

Kuqiao drew the silk from her sleeve. "Miss, shall I help you hang it?" She reached for a low willow branch, ready to tie the ribbon.

Qianli Xun shook off her reverie and stopped her. "Master Jingguang said to hang it on the western branches."

Kuqiao halted. Glancing at the sun, now tilting west, she moved in that direction. "Miss, this is west. I'll find a branch up there."

Qianli Xun studied the tree. More than half the branches within reach were already draped in silk. She had no desire to crowd among strangers' ribbons—and hung so low, hers would be easy for any passerby to snatch.

She looked up to the higher branches. "I plan to hang it there."

Kuqiao followed her miss's gaze. "Ah! Miss, it's so high. How can you get up there? Aren't you making things difficult for me?"

Qianli Xun held out her hand. "Give it here!"

Kuqiao obediently placed the silk in her miss's hand. "Miss, it's so high. Can you get up there?"

Without a word, Qianli Xun gripped the silk and activated her qinggong. With a soft whoosh, she shot upward, landing among the high branches. She stepped onto the swaying willow limbs as though they were solid ground.

Kuqiao gasped in admiration. "My miss is truly amazing!"

Qianli Xun found a high branch and secured the silk. As she prepared to leap down, she spotted something—a mansion nestled in the dense forest beyond the pond. It didn't look like any common dwelling.

After jumping down, she pointed toward the mansion. "There's actually a mansion over there. Do you know whose it is?"

Kuqiao peered in the direction her miss indicated. She saw only dense forest—no mansion in sight.

"It seems you don't know either. Forget it. It has nothing to do with us anyway. Let's hurry back."

Qianli Xun cast one last glance at the sacred tree. She would come back often, she decided. In this unfamiliar era, the willow was the only thing that felt like home.

Mistress and maid circled the mountain's base, their path lined with weeds and stubborn rocks. Remembering Master Jingguang's advice, Qianli Xun asked, "Kuqiao, do you know where there's a bamboo grove?"

Kuqiao walked ahead of her miss and replied, "The people in our village make a living by growing bamboo. There's a large bamboo forest around. Why do you ask, Miss?"

"It's Master Jingguang's idea. She said bamboo is good for me and told me to find a place with a bamboo forest and live there for a month."

"That's great! Miss, just come to my house. I also happen to go home and stay with my parents for a while." Kuqiao was very happy, but soon worried. "I'm afraid Master Lin won't agree."

Qianli Xun snorted, dismissing Lin Jue entirely. "Who does Lin Jue think he is? Yours truly makes her own decisions!"

The mistress and maid chatted as they walked. Unaware, they reached the entrance of the mountain. The Lin family's coachman was sitting on a stone, wolfing down half a flatbread. Seeing the madam return, he put away half the flatbread, walked to the carriage, and prepared to return to the city.

When they arrived at the Lin residence, it had just gotten dark.

Lin Jue had long learned from the servants that Zhu Shuzhen had been out all day. After she returned to the Zhulan Residence, he sent someone to call the coachman and asked, "Where did she go today?"

"Reporting to Master Lin, the madam went to the nunnery."

"The nunnery? What did she go there for?"

"I only drove the carriage, Master Lin. Didn't go up. Didn't see a thing. I guess the madam worshiped Buddha and prayed for blessings."

"Who doesn't know that! Do I need you to tell me? Get out!" Lin Jue wanted to know if his wife had used worshiping Buddha as an excuse to meet secretly with some talented scholar.

Qianli Xun returned to the Zhulan Residence. Seeing the bamboo planted inside, she remembered Master Jingguang's suggestion again. *There's bamboo right here. Isn't this enough? Do I really need a whole forest?* She soon figured it out—too little bamboo in the Zhulan Residence, not enough to make a difference.

After changing clothes, Qianli Xun felt hungry. "Kuqiao, I'm hungry. Get me something to eat."

Kuqiao went to the kitchen to arrange it. After a while, she brought the food. Two dishes and a bowl of chicken soup. Nothing particularly special.

When Kuqiao put down a bowl of rice, Qianli Xun was attracted. She saw peach blossom petals on the rice. "What kind of rice is this? So elegant?"

"Peach blossom rice."

"Peach blossom rice?" Qianli Xun muttered. She remembered two lines from Lu You's poem, "Softly steamed fragrant peach blossom rice, lightly sipped clear bamboo leaf wine." Indeed, Lu Fangweng didn't deceive later generations. Thinking carefully, it's now the 4th Year of Xuanhe. Lu Fangweng hasn't been born yet. Hehe, it's better not to recite his poem. "The peach blossom season has long passed. Where did the peach blossoms come from?"

"Miss, haven't you heard of ground peach blossoms?"

Qianli Xun shook her head. "Never heard of it."

"They're planted everywhere along the roads in the Lin residence. Miss must have seen them. You just didn't know they were ground peach blossoms."

Indeed, she had seen them in the Lin residence. Ground peach blossoms were about a meter tall, and the flowers looked very much like peach blossoms. She was truly ignorant and didn't understand anything. Qianli Xun picked up her chopsticks, took a bite of peach blossom rice, and put it in her mouth. It was sweet, fragrant, soft, and glutinous. "Not bad. There's also the fragrance of red dates."

"When steaming the dish, red dates must be added. Miss, if you eat a few more bites, you'll see them."

After finishing dinner, Qianli Xun arranged for Kuqiao to pack their things tonight and go to the place with the bamboo forest early tomorrow morning. Thinking of Lin Jue, she might as well let him know. After all, she lived in the Lin residence and was still nominally his wife.

Qianli Xun walked alone toward Lin Jue's quarters. Just as she reached the door, a woman's giggle drifted out—high, sugary, performative. *Of course. Another merchant courtesan. Because why not?*

She turned to leave. Then stopped. *Why am I avoiding this? I don't care about him. Let him SEE that I don't care. Maybe then he'll regret not signing those divorce papers.* Besides—servants passed through this courtyard all day. If they spotted her sneaking away from his door, the gossip would write itself. No. She knocked.

Qianli Xun called out to a servant. "You, go knock on the door and tell Lin Jue I have something to say to him."

The servant walked to the door and reported inside. "Master Lin, the madam is here. She has something to say to you."

The laughter inside immediately stopped. After a while, the door was opened. The servant seized the opportunity to leave quickly, afraid that Master Lin and madam would have a fight. Better to stay far away so as not to get hurt.

Lin Jue stood at the door. "Didn't the madam say to keep your well water from touching my river water? Why did you come to find me? Have you thought it through and come to find your husband to consummate the marriage?"

Her mood was originally quite good, but this guy's two teasing sentences made her furious. Qianli Xun snapped, "Consummate my foot! I came to notify you of something. Let's talk inside."

Qianli Xun walked quickly into the room. At the door, she used her shoulder to bump Lin Jue to the side. Although she didn't mind, curiosity drove her to see what kind of woman was inside. But there wasn't a single person in sight.

She laughed—short, sharp, amused. "Lin Jue, why hide her? Am I that frightening? Besides, I don't mind at all if you bring a courtesan home. I'll withdraw the second rule of the Three Rules right now. In the future, you can bring as many back to the Lin residence as you want, as long as you like."

Chun Jinqiao came out from inside. "Military Training Officer, I said it was fine, but you insisted I hide."

Qianli Xun stared at the heavily made-up Chun Jinqiao and continued to smile. "Hah, Lin Jue, I didn't expect you to be quite dedicated in finding merchant courtesans."

Lin Jue demanded. "What exactly did you come to find me for?"

"I came to notify you. Starting tomorrow, I'm going to live in the countryside for a while. As for when I'll come back, it depends on my mood."

"Are you going alone or with others?"

"Of course not alone. Kuqiao is coming too."

Lin Jue breathed a sigh of relief. "As long as it's not some random man, that's fine."

The words struck Qianli Xun's pride. "You have the nerve to bring a random woman into this house, yet you try to police me? Even if I were with another man, you have no right to interfere. Better take care of your own mess first."

After speaking, Qianli Xun slammed the door and left.

Chun Jinqiao shut the door. She drifted to Lin Jue's side, fingers trailing his sleeve. "Officer Lin," she murmured, voice dripping honey, "what a miserable life you're living. Why not just let her go? I don't need you to buy my freedom. I don't need a place at your side. Just... marry someone who'll actually cherish you. That's all I ask."

Lin Jue's jaw tightened. Then loosened. He scooped her up. "Not yet," he said. "I'm not done thinking." He carried her into the inner room. The door closed.
