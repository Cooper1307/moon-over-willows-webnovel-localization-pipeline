#TITLE: Chapter 2: A Duel, an Earthquake, and Transmigration
Before she could work out her next move, her stomach let out a loud growl. Half a glass of watered-down milk did nothing to fill her up. Qianli Xun walked back into the dining area and pulled the refrigerator open. Nothing sat inside except milk.
She dialed her apprentice. "Hey, Er'gang, your master is getting canceled online and stuck inside with reporters camping downstairs. Buy me a box of steamed rice rolls, a cup of soy milk, and a bowl of tofu pudding, and tell them to pile extra chili oil on the pudding."
"That much? Can you finish all of it?"
"The whole internet is gunning for me. Don't I need a full belly to fight back? Oh, and bring a set of ancient women's robes and a veiled hat when you come."
"What do you want those for?"
"I'm heading back to Qianli Fist Manor. With all those reporters downstairs, I need a disguise so nobody spots me."
"Good thing folks only know you as a web novelist and have no clue about Qianli Fist Manor. Lying low at the manor is smart. Sit tight, I'll be right there."
About half an hour later, Er'gang pulled up outside the apartment complex on his motorcycle, carrying breakfast and a large paper bag. He shot one glance at the reporters, walked into the building, murmured a quick word to the security guard, and headed to the elevator. When the doors slid open, he stepped in and pressed the button for twenty-two. The reporters paid him no mind. Wearing a black martial arts uniform that showed off his muscled arms, he looked like the last person tied to Qianli Xun.
Up on the twenty-second floor, Er'gang knocked on his master's door. Once inside, he tossed the paper bag onto the sofa and carried breakfast to the kitchen table, grumbling as he walked. "You never should have written web novels, Master. Being the 508th-generation heir to Qianli Fist gives you plenty to be proud of, yet here you are getting torn apart online. Might be for the best, though. Stay put at the manor from now on, take commercial gigs when you feel like it, make a comfortable living, find a husband, and call it a happy life."
"I'm the master here. Since when does an apprentice lecture his master?" Trailing behind him, Qianli Xun took a seat at the table, unpacked the plastic bags, and started eating.
"Take your time, Master. I'll wait outside the complex gate."
Without replying, Qianli Xun picked up a steamed rice roll. Listening to the front door click shut behind him, she muttered to herself, "These apprentices have less respect for me every day. Wait till I get back to the manor and whip them into shape."
Her appetite proved every bit as big as her order. She polished off every last bite, wiped her mouth with a tissue, tossed it into the trash, and gave a wide stretch before going to the bathroom to brush her teeth, wash her face, and comb out her long hair. Back in the living room, she pulled the outfit and veiled hat from the paper bag, shed her pajamas, and changed. Not bad at all; she looked like a classic swordswoman from a period drama. She tucked her phone and essentials into a crossbody bag, then settled the veiled hat onto her head. Light gauze drifted down around her face, hiding her features well.
Slipping on casual sneakers by the door, she rode the elevator down to the lobby, an odd flicker of tension tightening her chest. As she walked past the security guard, he was watching short videos on his phone; judging by the voice blasting out, another clip was roasting Qianli Xun. Too absorbed in his screen to notice anyone walking out, he kept watching with relish.
Stepping through the main doors, Qianli Xun peered through the gauze and saw every reporter swing around to look her way. Her pulse sped up. A female reporter stepped forward first. "Hello, miss. Which floor of this building do you live on?"
"Twenty-second."
At the words "twenty-second," the entire pack surged around her. "You live on the same floor as Qianli Xun? Know how she's doing?"
Qianli Xun kicked herself for not naming any other floor. Nerves had wrecked her wits. "I don't know her well. All I know is she lives across the hall, and this morning awful crying came out of her apartment, probably scared half to death by all the online hate. Excuse me, I have errands."
Breaking free from the reporters, she hurried out to the complex gate and breathed under her breath, "Thank goodness nobody recognized me."
"Master! Over here!" Er'gang called.
She hurried over, swung onto the back of the motorcycle, and wrapped her arms around Er'gang's waist. With a sharp rev of the engine, they shot down the avenue toward Qianli Fist Manor.
Built out in the city suburbs, Qianli Fist Manor trained over a hundred apprentices, men and women alike. When the motorcycle rolled to a stop outside the gates, Shen Jianchen stood there in a crisp suit, leading his Shen Fist disciples to challenge the manor once more. Judging by the tense standoff, no blows had been exchanged yet.
Pulling off her veiled hat and tossing it into Er'gang's chest, Qianli Xun shouted, "Shen Jianchen! Isn't our sparring match on the twenty-fifth of each month? Why show up early?"
Shen Jianchen turned around. Seeing Qianli Xun dressed like a striking swordswoman, he held her in a deep gaze and let a faint smile cross his lips. "Woke up early to find you trending across the whole internet, so I came over to check on you."
Hanging the veiled hat off his handlebars, Er'gang strolled over beside Shen Jianchen and teased, "You're CEO of a huge company. Ditching your office every month to drag your Shen Fist students out here for matches... sure you aren't after my master?"
Eyes anchored on Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen answered straight, "After your master, yes; here to spar, also yes. You apprentices ought to talk sense into her. Already an old maid of thirty; wait any longer and nobody will want her."
Already simmering from the morning's cyberbullying with nowhere to vent, hearing him poke at her age lit Qianli Xun's fuse on the spot. "I don't turn thirty until tomorrow!"
Shen Jianchen chuckled. "Quit calling yourself 'this old lady' every other breath. You aren't twenty anymore; talking like that ages you faster."
Fury spiked higher. "Listen up, Shen Jianchen! Today I'm challenging you to a real duel. Lose, and you never set foot near Qianli Fist Manor again!"
"And if you lose?"
"Lose is lose; what could you do about it? Besides, no way I'm losing!"
"If you lose, marry me."
Hearing that, the Qianli Fist disciples exchanged whispers. "CEO Shen has everything going for him except zero game with girls. Winding Master up like this, how on earth would she ever marry him?"
"Keep dreaming!" Qianli Xun shot back. "Only when pigs climb trees will I consider marrying you! Tonight at seven under the Millennial Divine Willow. Come alone; bring nobody else!"
"Copy that!" Looking thrilled beyond words, Shen Jianchen waved to his disciples. "Move out!"
As the Shen Fist disciples followed their master toward a parked Rolls-Royce, spotting the broad grin plastered across his face, one apprentice asked in puzzlement, "Master, you're about to get beaten up. Why are you grinning like that?"
"Beaten up? That's a date! Nighttime, a bright moon rising beside the Millennial Divine Willow... brings to mind two classic lines: 'The moon rises above the willow tips; our tryst is set past dusk.'" Lost in a daydream, Shen Jianchen showed not a shred of worry.
Watching him climb into the luxury car, the disciples traded baffled mutters. "How did Master ever become a CEO with a head like that? How does he run a giant company and drive a car this fancy?"
At dusk beneath the suburban Millennial Divine Willow, Qianli Xun stood with her hair tied in a high ponytail, dressed in a white T-shirt, casual jeans, and running shoes. Fresh and bright, she looked nothing like a woman turning thirty tomorrow. Shen Jianchen had changed into casual clothes, relaxed yet sharp, watching her with open affection in his eyes. Frogs croaked in bursts from the pond nearby, breaking the quiet between them.
When the noisy frogs paused, Shen Jianchen gazed up at the moon. Near the fifteenth of the lunar month, it was almost full. He glanced at the thick trunk of the Divine Willow along the bank and laughed. "Hey, Qianli Xun, if you wanted a date with me, just say so. Why dress it up as a duel?"
"Which ear of yours heard me say date? I came here to fight you!"
With that, Qianli Xun clenched her fists and lunged straight at him. Shen Jianchen sidestepped clean. Unwilling to give ground, she raised the pace, unleashing a fierce barrage of boxing combinations to pin him down. Shen Jianchen wove through her assault, using dodges, counter-grapple techniques, and supple deflections to drain her momentum. The instant she fired Quick Fist, he poured full power into his own punch and met hers head-on. The clash drove both of them more than two meters back.
"Why push yourself this hard, Qianli Xun? You're a girl; why not marry me? Look at me: 360th-generation heir to Shen Fist, CEO of a major corporation, a catch hard to find even with a lantern! Crowds of women would line up to marry me, yet you're the only one I want. Marry me, quit writing web novels, and I'll support you."
Maybe because he called her a "girl," her sour mood lifted a notch, stirring an unexpected warmth in her chest, though her tongue refused to soften. "Keep dreaming! I told you: only when pigs climb trees will I marry you, and I keep my word!"
No sooner had she spoken than the earth beneath their feet heaved in huge jolts. Knocked off balance by the brutal tremors, both hit the ground and watched nearby buildings collapse into rubble in seconds. Only then did realization strike: an earthquake! Humans stood fragile as dust before nature's fury.
Rocking back and forth under the quake, the Millennial Divine Willow wrenched free from the soil and began to topple straight toward Qianli Xun standing underneath. Spotting the falling trunk, Shen Jianchen scrambled up off the tilting ground and sprinted toward her. The split second he threw his body over hers, the ancient willow crashed down atop them both, crushing them into the heaving dirt as blood soaked into the earth.
Ten minutes later, stillness fell over the wreckage. Qianli Xun felt herself peel loose from her body and drift into the air. Looking down at her mangled corpse pinned under the willow tore at her heart with searing grief. Then she saw Shen Jianchen separate from his body too. "Shen Jianchen! Shen Jianchen!" she shouted, yet he could not hear a single sound.
When Shen Jianchen floated high enough, their eyes met. She could tell he was calling her name with everything he had, and she strained to answer, yet silence walled them off from each other. Then an immense, unearthly force surged from the Divine Willow's massive trunk and swept both spirits away. Pitch darkness swallowed Qianli Xun's sight, wrapping her in terror...

#TITLE: Chapter 70: A Confession in the Tailor Shop
Stepping ashore on West Lake's northern bank, Nan Shuang bowed for instructions. "Your carriage is parked not far from here, Young Master. I'll go stow the food carrier first, then meet you at the tailor shop."
At Qiao Wenxian's nod, Nan Shuang jogged off.
The tailor shop still lay a short walk away. As a man, having soaked clothes cling to his frame did not bother Qiao Wenxian, yet seeing Shuzhen drenched through made his heart ache. Scanning the bank, he spotted a young woman wearing a gossamer-thin cloak. "Wait here a moment, Shuzhen. I'll be right back."
Before Qianli Xun could nod, Wenyuan walked away straight toward the stranger. A pinch of hurt pricked her chest as she muttered under her breath, "Sees a pretty girl and walks right off on me. Does he know her?" Kuqiao looked just as puzzled over what Young Master Qiao had in mind.
Watching from afar, they saw Wenyuan bow to the woman and speak a few words. At first she shook her head, until Wenyuan gestured back toward Shuzhen, pointed down another path, and spoke again. Only then did the woman nod, unfasten her cloak, and hand it over. Understanding hit Qianli Xun at last: Wenyuan had gone to borrow a cloak for her.
Returning, Wenyuan draped the cloak over her shoulders. Instant warmth flooded her chest, chasing away every trace of chill. Taking her hand, he led her along winding paths out past the lakeside grounds until a tailor shop came into view.
Just as they neared the door, Miao Zhiyu walked out of the tailor shop with a group of close female friends. Spotting Young Master Qiao holding Youqi Jushi's hand in plain sight, Miao Zhiyu meant to pretend she saw nothing and slip past, until one young lady spoke up. "Look! That's Young Master Qiao and Youqi Jushi, hand in hand! Guess those rumors about them being together are true after all!"
Cornered, Miao Zhiyu had no choice but to step up and greet them. "Young Master Qiao, Youqi Jushi, how did your clothes get drenched?"
Worried the sight might hurt Miao Zhiyu, Qianli Xun tried to slip her hand free. Instead, Wenyuan gripped her fingers tighter and explained smooth, "Shuzhen and I went boating on the lake and bumped into another craft by mishap, turning us both into drowned rats."
Qianli Xun added, "Heard there's a tailor shop nearby, so we came over to pick out ready-made clothes to change into."
Forcing a smile, Miao Zhiyu said, "Their fabrics are fine and the cuts look lovely. Go right ahead; Zhiyu still has shopping to do with her friends."
"Enjoy your outing, Lady Miao." Still holding Shuzhen's hand, Qiao Wenxian led her toward the shop door.
A sharp pang gripped Miao Zhiyu. Gazing at his retreating back, she could not keep from calling out, "Young Master Qiao!"
Halting, Qiao Wenxian glanced back over his shoulder. "Something else, Lady Miao?"
Qianli Xun stopped beside him without turning around, unwilling to face Miao Zhiyu's downcast look, listening sharp instead.
"Young Master Dongfang and the others are gathering at a pavilion not far off," Miao Zhiyu said. "Will you go over in a bit?"
"Brother Dongfang invited me yesterday, and I already declined since Shuzhen and I had plans first," Qiao Wenxian answered clear. "Today I'm staying with Shuzhen alone. No gatherings for me."
Miao Zhiyu had only wanted to keep him talking a moment longer; his blunt answer left nothing more to say. She curtsied and walked off, glancing back over her shoulder as the two stepped hand in hand inside the shop. Letting out a sigh, she fell mute as she wandered along beside her friends.
"Zhiyu, don't tell me you fell for Young Master Qiao?" one girl asked.
Miao Zhiyu stayed quiet, which said yes plain enough.
"With Young Master Qiao's lineage, character, and looks, who wouldn't like him?" another girl chimed in. "Dare any of you swear you don't?"
The others all confessed together, "We do!"
Miao Zhiyu sighed again. "Pity none of us have Youqi Jushi's luck. Saw how tight Young Master Qiao held her hand, as if afraid she might vanish? Makes anyone envious."
Another young lady echoed with a sigh, "All we can pin our hopes on is the Qiao family's bride selection. If one of us gets chosen, even if Young Master Qiao doesn't dote on me quite that much, just staying by his side would be enough." Those words struck straight to Miao Zhiyu's heart; as things stood, that bride selection was her sole remaining hope.
Inside the tailor shop, once both chose ready-made outfits, Qiao Wenxian bought two large clean cloths from the clerk and handed one to Kuqiao. Taking the cloth, Kuqiao escorted her mistress into the shop's inner fitting room, helped her peel off the wet robes, dried her skin, dressed her in crisp new garments, and smoothed her hair back in place.
The moment Qianli Xun stepped back out into the front room, Nan Shuang arrived right on cue and escorted his master inside to change. Just then a young woman walked in and approached Qianli Xun. "Excuse me, miss. May I have my cloak back?"
Qianli Xun smiled warm. "Thank you so much, miss."
Kuqiao handed the cloak back. Accepting it, the girl beamed, "Your husband treats you so well!" With that, she left carrying her cloak.
Qianli Xun turned the word "husband" over in her mind. Back when she was tied to Lin Jue, that word had felt like a dead weight she could not wait to shed; savoring it now brought an altogether different sweetness.
Eyeing the damp garments on the table, the shop clerk offered, "Miss, touring West Lake lugging wet clothes is a hassle. Why not leave them at our shop? I'll have someone wash them clean and press them flat, and you can send a servant to pick them up."
Qianli Xun chuckled. "Didn't know tailor shops ran laundry service too."
"These days, if a customer needs it and there's silver to earn, why not?"
At Qianli Xun's glance, Kuqiao counted out silver and handed it to the clerk. Taking the coin, the clerk asked, "Include that young master's wet clothes too?"
"Of course," Kuqiao replied.
"Looks like you're all one family," the clerk remarked offhand.
"One family" warmed Qianli Xun straight through. Longing to build a real home with Wenyuan, she drifted into daydreams of married life: loving each other deep, a son and daughter running around their feet...
"Shuzhen, what are you thinking about?"
Wenyuan's voice pulled her back to earth. Freshly changed into new robes, his features looked even crisper, his complexion glowing pale and smooth. Envying skin like that, Qianli Xun reached right up to touch his cheek. "Your skin is so smooth. How many girls must envy you?"
Catching her hand in his palm, Qiao Wenxian murmured, "No need to envy me, because I belong to you."
Such a blunt confession made everyone within earshot blush and look aside. To Qianli Xun, his words went straight to her head stronger than fine wine; floating in a happy daze, she let him clasp her hand and lead her back out of the tailor shop. All down the lane she stayed wrapped inside "I belong to you," breaking into silly grins every few steps, while Wenyuan read her mood well enough not to interrupt, keeping her hand locked in his.
Reaching the lakeshore again, they strolled in comfortable hush, soaking in a bond where words felt needless. Along the walk, a woman's clear singing floated over the breeze, sweet and lyrical.
At last Qiao Wenxian spoke. "Those lyrics are your 'Pusaman.'"
Listening close, Qianli Xun caught the opening lines: "Hill pavilions and waterside halls stand at mid-autumn; lone behind phoenix curtains, no one keeps me company. Fresh sorrow wells anew, while my moth brows knit in the same old frown..."
Drawn by the music, the two followed a willow-lined path until a vast spread of lotus leaves opened ahead. Some blossoms had faded, some stood in full bloom, and others remained tight buds. Just beyond rose a lakeside pavilion ringed with listeners, a singing girl seated at its center strumming a zither as she sang: "Rising to stand by the embroidered door, sparse fireflies drift past now and then. I thank the moon for pitying me—tonight it cannot bear to shine full." [TN: Translator's rendering]
"Who set that to music?" Qianli Xun breathed in wonder. "What a lovely tune!"
"Who else? Brother Dongfang, of course," Qiao Wenxian smiled. "Beyond loving poetry, he's a master at composing melodies, and setting your lyrics to music is his favorite pastime. He must have written a new score today and invited everyone to hear it. Since we're right here, let's go say hello."
Glancing toward the pavilion, Qianli Xun spotted Dongfang Chenze's circle alongside several unfamiliar young gentlemen. Since Wenyuan suggested it, she nodded without hesitation.

#TITLE: Chapter 151: Fleeing After a Confession
With Wenyuan calm again and Chen Haoyu letting the clash drop, Shen Jianchen had no further fight to worry about. His mind stayed anchored on Qianli Xun, yet to avoid drawing suspicion, he told Bai Feng to summon the storyteller.
Servants had already set up a small table. Moments later the storyteller entered holding his purple clay teapot, took a sip, set the pot down, and greeted the hall. "Honored guests, this is the first time in my life anyone invited me into a grand home to tell tales. Feels a bit odd, so forgive any slips!"
"Cut the chatter and start the story. We're all waiting!" Cao Ban called out.
"Patience, young masters, right away!" Clapping his gavel block crisp on the wood, the storyteller launched in. "Last time we left off with heroic Wu Xuanying taking command of the army in his father's place to crush the rebellion! Mounted astride his crimson Red Hare steed and gripping the Xuanyuan Sword, Wu Xuanying led fifty thousand troops south in a thunderous march..."
Having no patience to sit through the set, Shen Jianchen beckoned Xilan over. She stepped up at once and nodded along as Prince Yi whispered instructions in her ear, then hurried back to murmur beside Princess Jiahe. "His Highness needs to step out to the latrine and asks Princess to look after the guests. Above all, don't let Young Master Qiao and Young Master Chen clash again."
Qiao Mengrong glanced over and gave her brother a quiet nod. Answering with a reassuring smile, Shen Jianchen rose and walked out. When Bai Feng started to follow, Prince Yi halted him. "Just heading to the latrine. Stay here and tend the guests for me." Bai Feng had no choice but to stay behind.
Shen Jianchen was nowhere near heading to a latrine; he was hunting Qianli Xun. Stepping out the main doors, luck smiled on him: right past the courtyard stood Kuqiao beneath the wooden tree platform, and looking up, he spotted Qianli Xun standing atop the platform gazing over the forest.
Spotting Prince Yi, Kuqiao stepped forward and curtsied. "Your Highness."
"Bai Feng is waiting for you in the back courtyard. Why are you idling out here?" Shen Jianchen fibbed without blinking; sending Kuqiao off cleared his way up the platform.
Hearing Bai Feng waited for her, Kuqiao hesitated. "I'm supposed to attend Miss. I can't run off to see Bai Feng."
As if that could stop Shen Jianchen! "Go right ahead. If Youqi Jushi asks later, I'm right here; she won't blame you."
With royal backing, Kuqiao lit up at once. "Thank you, Your Highness!" Off she skipped toward the back courtyard.
Climbing the wooden steps fast, Shen Jianchen hurried upward, eager to catch Qianli Xun alone at last. Just the two of them high above the woods, facing the very Divine Willow that had carried them across a thousand years.
Up on the platform, Qianli Xun gazed toward the distant willow, turning her tangled fate over in silence. Divine Willow, oh Divine Willow, I swore off romance and just wanted a quiet solo life, yet fate plays a cruel joke tying me to a ridiculous betrothal. Is Chen Haoyu really my ending? No, never! Tell me, Divine Willow, where on earth do I belong?
Too far away to answer, the willow gave no sign, yet right behind her sounded a clear voice: "Qianli Xun!"
Spinning around to find Prince Yi standing right there, Qianli Xun froze in a daze, a wild thought flashing across her mind: Is he where I belong? In the next breath she shook herself awake. No, impossible! He's the man Mengrong loves. I could never betray Mengrong!
Skipping formal greetings, she scolded him sharp. "I already warned Your Highness not to blurt out that name. Why say it again?"
"Because I like calling you by it. Nobody's around; what harm?"
Warmth and open devotion filled Prince Yi's eyes. Qianli Xun cursed under her breath—Trying to seduce me again!—and glanced over the rail only to find Kuqiao gone. "Since Your Highness came up for the view, I'll head down and leave you the platform." She turned toward the stairs.
Shen Jianchen baited her on purpose. "Has Youqi Jushi fallen for me already, fleeing in panic?"
Halting her steps, she snapped back, "Who's fleeing?! I've been out long enough and ought to get back inside for the dances! Besides, I have zero feelings for Your Highness. Quit saying things like that before Mengrong hears and gets hurt!"
Turning his gaze toward the distant willow, Shen Jianchen murmured, "I love one girl with all my heart and long for her to love me back. See over there? That Divine Willow stands witness to our love."
Remembering how Prince Yi and Mengrong had hung red ribbons beneath that willow together, Qianli Xun frowned. Did he mean Mengrong? Didn't sound like it! Ready to dress down a playboy prince on Mengrong's behalf, she shot back, "Your Highness's greatest gift is flirting with other women behind Mengrong's back! Which other girl did you sneak under the Divine Willow to tie ribbons with? Poor Mengrong still thinks her brother is loyal to the bone!"
Stepping closer, eyes brimming with earnest feeling, Shen Jianchen spoke plain. "The girl I love is Youqi Jushi, Qianli Xun! Tell me, do you love me?"
Even having heard his confession before, Qianli Xun froze and stepped back a pace. "I never tied ribbons under the Divine Willow with you..."
Taking a step forward to close the gap, Shen Jianchen spoke with a tone bordering on command. "Stop loving Wenyuan, and do not marry Chen Haoyu! You belong to me alone!"
Her clenched fist stayed pinned at her side. Strange—today the warmth in Prince Yi's eyes felt like winter sunshine melting away her chill, and his bossy decree did not even grate on her nerves. Why? Alarm jolted her chest. Thinking of Mengrong, she dared not linger another second; Prince Yi knew how to shake her heart too well, and staying risked losing her head. Spotting the stairway opening, she shoved Prince Yi aside and fled down the steps.
Before he could utter the crucial words, she was gone! Leaning over the railing watching her hurry down the stairs, Shen Jianchen called aloud, "After the salon, meet me under the Divine Willow! I have something vital to tell you!"
"Don't hold your breath, Your Highness. I'm not going!" she tossed back without breaking stride.
"Skip it, and I tell Mengrong straight to her face that I love you!"
That hit dead-center. Qianli Xun skidded to a halt and glared straight up at his smug grin. "Low blow! Fine, I'll show up! Don't dream of me falling for you, and don't whine if you catch a beating!"
"Just looking for a good sparring partner. Sparring with the woman I love couldn't be sweeter!" Since he meant to reveal that Zhao Pu was Shen Jianchen, what better proof than Shen Fist itself?
Hurrying down the remaining stairs and straight back into the courtyard, Qianli Xun refused to let Prince Yi catch up and spark gossip by walking in together.
Inside the hall, lunch platters had been cleared for fruit and tea snacks. The dancers were gone, and the storyteller was mid-set spinning Hongxiang and Xuanying's tale—none other than the storyteller from West Third Alley! Scanning for Kuqiao and finding her absent brought huge relief; if Kuqiao walked in here, her secret pen name would blow wide open.
Slipping back into her seat, she met Qiao Mengrong leaning close to whisper, "What kept you out so long?"
"Youlin Retreat's views looked too lovely; I wandered a few extra steps."
Smiling without pushing, Qiao Mengrong turned back to the gripping tale. Qianli Xun beckoned to Xiaguo standing behind her, yet Xiaguo stayed glued on Young Master Qiao without noticing until Huayu poked her arm. Jolted awake, Xiaguo leaned down. "Your orders, Miss?"
"Where's Kuqiao?"
"She went out with you and never came back inside."
Qianli Xun nodded. Seeing Bai Feng standing right at his post, she assumed Kuqiao had spotted the storyteller and hidden away to keep her cover safe. Settling back, she gave herself over to the story; hearing her own writing come alive before an enthralled crowd felt wonderful.
Standing behind his table, the storyteller delivered every twist with gripping drama. Returning victorious from battle, Wu Xuanying searched high and low for Hua Hongxiang without a trace. Torn apart—having struggled so hard to be together, could they lose each other now?—Wu Xuanying marched straight to his father the City Lord demanding, "Where did Hongxiang go?!"
Right then Shen Jianchen returned from outside, took his seat, and cast a warm glance across at Qianli Xun, still chuckling inside at her hasty flight off the platform.
The storyteller pressed on: "The City Lord said, 'Forget Hongxiang, my son.' 'Never!' Wu Xuanying dropped to his knees. 'Father, tell me where you hid her!' The City Lord answered, 'Why would I hide her? She has vanished from this world and will never return.' 'Vanished?' Crushed beyond bearing, Wu Xuanying broke into piercing sobs and fainted cold onto the floor!" Clack! Down came the gavel block. "To learn what follows next, stay tuned for our next session!"
Having written that chapter during a dark mood, Qianli Xun had laid the tragedy on thick, leaving nearly the whole room sighing in sympathy. Qiao Wenxian felt every word cut to the bone, Shuzhen lost to him just as Hongxiang had vanished. Having walked in late and still glowing from his moment on the platform, Prince Yi felt none of the gloom, while Chen Haoyu scoffed at melodramatic romance: what real man faints crying over a girl?
As the storyteller bowed to leave, Dongfang Chenze halted him. "Wait a moment, sir! What happens next?"
"My lips are sealed, young master! Want the next chapter, come to Qiaotou Wa tomorrow!" Bowing to all, the storyteller withdrew.
Qiao Wenxian's eyes drifted back to Shuzhen—seated mere paces away, yet speaking one word to her felt impossible—then shifted onto Chen Haoyu's cocky slouch. How could a dandy like that deserve Shuzhen? Ache crushing his chest, he told Nan Shuang, "Go fetch me a pot of wine." Nan Shuang obeyed at once.
Miao Zhiyu sighed in admiration. "Young Master Qianli Xun writes with such piercing feeling. Tomorrow I'll bring my close friends to hear the next part!"
"Funny thing," Cao Ban mused aloud. "I always get the hunch Qianli Xun is a young lady, even though street rumors insist he's a young gentleman. What do you all think?"
At that remark, Shen Jianchen shot a sly glance at Qianli Xun, hiding a grin.
"Same hunch," An Mingxuan agreed. "Word is Young Master Qianli Xun was spotted once in the market dressed as a man, yet graceful and pretty as a girl."
Chen Haoyu snorted in scorn. "Bet Qianli Xun's just some soft sissy. How else write a spineless tale like that? Who on earth faints crying like Wu Xuanying?! Qiao Wenxian, you've cried before; ever pass out?"
Being called a sissy did not faze Qianli Xun one bit; she was a woman disguised as a guy! Yet Qiao Wenxian's blood boiled. On what merit did Chen Haoyu get to marry Shuzhen? "Quit gloating after stealing a prize, Chen Haoyu! Whether I cried or fainted is none of your business!"
Chen Haoyu smirked back. "Hear that? Even Qiao Wenxian doesn't pass out crying. Proves Qianli Xun is a sissy!"

Spotting Shuzhen, Mengrong jumped up. "Going to grab Shuzhen, Brother!"
"Give them a minute; the Qiaos need to greet them first." Sure enough, Qiao elders stepped up, and only after Wenyuan strode over and stalked off did restless Shen Jianchen let Mengrong go fetch her.
Since revealing his identity to Qianli Xun, joy and ache tugged him in two. He felt thrilled she talked breezy and real with him like old times, zero stiffness or misunderstandings, yet ached because her iron line beneath the Divine Willow had not budged an inch. With Mengrong standing between them, he could not chase her in the open, yet could not tear her out of his heart for a single breath.
Watching Mengrong lead Qianli Xun off into the gardens and linger long made him antsy. What decent excuse got him over to talk? Right on cue, Madam Qian's maid stepped forward calling loud: "Attention all players! Warm-ups done—official matches start now! Madam sets teams of two, men or women mixed free, prizes courtesy of Madam! Round One prize: one fine emerald jade bangle!" A servant held up a glowing jade tray, pocket change to wealthy guests, pure sport.
Perfect opening to invite Qianli Xun onto the lawn! Telling Bai Feng, "Stay put, I'm going to fetch Princess," Shen Jianchen strolled off. Knowing an empty royal pavilion looked like they had departed, Bai Feng held the post—and seized the golden moment with both masters away to grin and wave right across the grass at Kuqiao! Catching Kuqiao sneaking a shy wave right back, Matron Wei smiled warm. "Solid lad—good eyes you've got!" Turning bright red to her ears, Kuqiao beamed inside.
Then Matron Wei's gaze hit Xiaguo standing behind her and iced straight over, cut off before scolding as Chen Haoyu strolled up gripping a wooden club and grinning ear to ear beneath the pavilion steps. "Sis! Team up with me!"
"When'd you slink in without even saying hello?!" Chen Wenxin laughed scolding. "Almost a married man and still zero manners!"
"Family—skip the rules! Come down and swing clubs with me!"
Standing up, Chen Wenxin slipped off her cloak while her maid bound her wide sleeves tight with a sleeve-tying cord. Grabbing her club, she trotted down onto the lawn beside her brother. Facing Qiao Wenxian's younger sister Wenxiu paired with a Qiao cousin, both sides teed off from the starting mark taking turns driving balls toward the flagged hole. Far calmer than polo, chuiwan served most guests as a social playground; few kept score, crowds strolling pavilion to pavilion sealing friendships and deals, while players on the grass drowned out everything in fierce focus.
Indifferent to the match, once Young Madam left, Matron Wei turned crisp to Xiaguo. "Didn't speak in front of Young Madam earlier. Passing Miss's word straight to you now: Young Master Qiao asked for you as concubine, Master Qiao consented, and Miss won't block you. Got your wish—on the Qiao wedding day, their sedan chair comes to Wangyue Pavilion for you."
Even knowing the outcome, hearing it confirmed made Xiaguo beam. "Thank you, Miss! Thank you, Matron!"
"Save your thanks for your own scheming, winding Young Master Qiao up with two slick lies till he blurted it out! Bet he's kicking himself blind right now!"
Brushing off the jab—long as she won the man she wanted, nothing else mattered, and Young Master Qiao's lineage, grace, and learning crushed Lin Jue by a hundred miles—Xiaguo's eye caught a familiar man escorting a young woman toward an opposite pavilion. "Matron, who's that lady beside Training Officer Lin?"
"Training Officer? Jumped two ranks—Division Commander Lin now! And that's his new bride."
Kuqiao blinked curious. "New bride? From which house?"
"Distant relative from outside Lin'an, word says."
"They look happy enough. Guess Division Commander Lin quit hauling Chun Jinqiao home?" A quiet sting pricked Kuqiao for Miss: even Lin Jue had remarried smiling while Miss fought tooth and nail to escape an unwanted match.
Matron Wei scoffed. "Ever heard the old saying?..."
Xiaguo finished crisp, "A dog can't quit eating filth!"
For the very first time, a genuine smile cracked Matron Wei's face toward Xiaguo. "Fair point—said one line I like at last!"
Across the lawn, Madam Qian and Concubine Mi sat chatting warm with Madam Qin while poised Miao Zhiyu stayed demure beside her mother, drawing fond grins from her future mother-in-law and beaming smiles from Concubine Mi. Glancing over toward the Zhu pavilion and noticing Miss Zhu absent, Madam Qin asked offhand, "Hear the Zhus betrothed that ci poet to the Chen family, in-law?"
"Who hasn't heard?" Madam Qian sniffed. "No clue what Prefect Zhu's thinking. Wealthy house, sure, yet their second son's a hopeless scamp! Marry her over there and they'll end up in mutual divorce all over again!"
Concubine Mi chimed in, "Bet Prefect Zhu settled just because her reputation's shot!"
"Whatever their reason, long as she stays clear of Wenyuan!" Madam Qian's words warmed Madam Qin straight through. Glancing fond at Miao Zhiyu, Madam Qian told a maid, "Go fetch Young Master over."
Meanwhile, cooling down after his rash fit demanding Xiaguo, bitter regret choked Qiao Wenxian. Shunning every guest, he sat hunched alone on a boulder at the lawn's edge while heart-sore Nan Shuang stood silent beside him, refusing to speak after Master claimed the very girl Nan Shuang liked.
Madam Qian's maid hurried up. "Young Master, Madam summons you."
Silence. Repeating once more—"Young Master, Madam calls for you"—she watched him rise mute and stalk straight toward Madam Qian's pavilion, glum Nan Shuang trailing behind swearing to wipe Xiaguo clean from his heart. Used to Young Master's bleak gloom ever since the Miao betrothal, the maid did not bat an eye.
Stepping into Madam Qian's pavilion, Qiao Wenxian bowed stiff. "You called for me, Mother?"
Madam Qian smiled warm. "Zhiyu's been sitting long enough. Lovely spring gardens out there—walk her around!"
Skipping any reply to that, Qiao Wenxian announced flat, "One matter to report, Mother: your son has decided to take Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo as concubine, and Father already consented."
Frozen a split second, Madam Qian glanced over at Concubine Mi, who forced an awkward chuckle. "Thought it was big news! Taking a concubine or two is nothing, right, Madam Qin?"
Burning inside yet swallowing pride before imperial kin—marrying into the Qiao house lifted the Miaos high—Madam Qin pasted on a polite smile. "No harm in a gentleman taking a concubine or two. Only hope she's sweet-tempered as Concubine Mi so my Zhiyu won't suffer grief!"
"Zhiyu enters as future head matriarch of Qiao Manor," Madam Qian declared firm. "Whoever dares cross her answers to me first!"
Stung sour having another woman claim a slice of her groom before she even crossed his threshold, Miao Zhiyu felt Madam Qian's iron promise soothe at least a fraction of her chill.
