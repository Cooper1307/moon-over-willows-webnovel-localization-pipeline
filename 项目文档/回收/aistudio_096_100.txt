#TITLE: Addressing the Troops on the Drill Ground
Seeing Prince Yi fall silent, Qiao Shouren assumed the prince had no wish to see a shrew become the Qiao family's daughter-in-law either, and secret glee warmed his chest.
Panic seized Qiao Wenxian. He set down his tea bowl. "Prince Yi, why aren't you saying anything?"
Looking into Wenyuan's pleading eyes, Shen Jianchen thought, "If Qianli Xun were here, she'd be looking at me with that exact same longing. I love her, yet I can't have her. Better to give them my blessing."
Shen Jianchen steadied his mind and forced himself to speak. "Grandfather must have heard of Young Master Chen's conduct and reputation. When Miss Zhu taught him a lesson, first, she was disciplining a junior on behalf of her brother and sister-in-law; second, Young Master Chen must have done something that begged for a beating before she lifted a hand. That proves Miss Zhu stands up for justice and fears no bully. Had I been there, I would have stepped in too. A young woman who can beat Young Master Chen in a fight is all the more the one and only match for Wenyuan. Think about it, Grandfather: the Qiao family holds vast estates, and Wenyuan hasn't the strength to truss up a chicken. If he marries some frail mistress of the household on top of that, how will they ever hold this estate together in the future? Please give it careful thought, Grandfather."
Qiao Shouren recalled a time he had sent servants to the countryside to collect rent, only for them to run into local thugs. The rent collector came back empty-handed, his leg beaten until it was crippled.
Glancing back at his son sipping tea, he admitted that while Wenyuan might not be quite "too weak to truss up a chicken" as Prince Yi put it, carrying such a massive family enterprise alone would indeed be a heavy burden. Marrying a tough, capable woman might not be a bad idea after all.
Besides, Prince Yi had come in person and argued with such earnest conviction. If his words had been hollow, that would be one thing, yet every point made complete sense. Turning him down now would be a slap in Prince Yi's face.
"Prince Yi makes a compelling case. I have no further objection. I agree."
Qiao Wenxian gulped down the rest of his tea and asked before even setting the bowl down, "Father! You agree to send a marriage proposal to Zhu Manor?"
Qiao Shouren nodded. "I agree. Hold your horses, though. Miss Zhu only went through her mutual divorce a short while ago, so calling on them to propose right now would look unseemly. Give it a month or two, and then I'll take your mother to Zhu Manor to pay a call on the Prefect."
Wenyuan rose and bowed to both his father and Prince Yi. "Thank you, Prince Yi! Thank you, Father!"
Shen Jianchen, however, could feel no joy. After all the trouble it took to find Qianli Xun, he had just found her, had not even told her that Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen, and now he was about to lose her just like that.
Leaving Qiao Manor, Shen Jianchen remembered the favor Qianli Xun had asked of him. With no plans to go back to Youlin Retreat alongside Wenyuan, he parted ways with him on the street.
Since a coachman was driving today, Bai Feng was not needed up front and rode inside the carriage with His Highness.
Seeing how downcast His Highness looked, Bai Feng dared not disturb him. He sat in silence at his side, thinking of Kuqiao. Recalling how he had confessed his feelings to Kuqiao inside Zhu Manor's carriage, he found himself both bold and ridiculous, and a laugh slipped out before he could stop it.
The laugh drew Shen Jianchen's attention to Bai Feng. "What are you laughing about?"
"N-nothing." Bai Feng felt too embarrassed to say.
"Did you go hang out with Nan Shuang just now?"
"He showed me around Qiao Manor."
"Are you hungry?"
"A bit. Why didn't Your Highness stay for lunch at Qiao Manor before leaving? I saw they made Dongpo pork today."
Shen Jianchen forced a smile. "I thought you only had eyes for Youqi Jushi's maid now. Didn't expect you to still be thinking about Dongpo pork."
"I'm interested in both."
"Once I wrap up my errand, I'll take you out to eat."
"Yes, yes!" Delighted, Bai Feng asked, "Your Highness, when is Youqi Jushi coming back to Youlin Retreat?"
"Well... hard to say." Shen Jianchen teased Bai Feng on purpose, hoping a little banter might ease the ache in his own chest.
"Sigh. Right now I'd love to trade places with Nan Shuang. Whenever Young Master Qiao goes out with Youqi Jushi, he gets to see Kuqiao, while I have no such luck."
Putting on a cheerful front, Shen Jianchen said, "How about we swap you with Nan Shuang tomorrow?"
"No way! I'm Your Highness's personal bodyguard, and I still have to learn martial arts from you. I was just sighing over my luck."
"Qixi Festival is almost here. Wenyuan is taking Youqi Jushi out to stroll the streets, and I'm going too, so you'll get to see the person you've been missing."
Bai Feng counted the days on his fingers and broke into a grin. "Five days left."
The carriage rolled up to the Military Training Office.
Located in the west of the city, the Military Training Office fell under Lin Jue's charge. Above him stood the Commandant, the Division Commander, the Vice Commander-in-Chief, the Commander-in-Chief of All Forces, and at the very top, the Lord Military Commissioner.
The only reason Lin Jue dared bypass the chain of command to approach the Military Commissioner himself was the tie he had forged through his mutual divorce. Growing closer to him would also make other officials think twice before crossing Lin Jue. Looked at that way, divorcing Zhu Shuzhen had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.
Shen Jianchen stepped down from the carriage. Before him stood the gate of the Military Training Office, not grand yet solemn enough, with three large characters reading "Military Training Office" inscribed above.
Bai Feng walked up to the soldier stationed at the gate. "The Lord Military Commissioner is here to see Training Officer Lin. Go tell him to come out and receive His Lordship."
Hearing it was the Military Commissioner, the soldier dropped into a bow at once. "Please wait a moment, Your Lordship. I will report to the Training Officer right away."
Moments after the soldier hurried off, Lin Jue rushed out. "I did not know Your Lordship was coming! Please forgive my failure to greet you!"
Shen Jianchen said, "I just happened to recall how you invited me to address the troops last time, so I thought I'd drop by for a look."
Lin Jue felt even more honored. He had only tossed out a casual invitation, yet Prince Yi had taken it to heart. With a patron this mighty behind him, his rise through the ranks was assured.
The post of Commandant had just fallen vacant, and Lin Jue had his heart set on climbing into it. He had meant to ask Zhu Xiyan for help, yet over the whole Xiaguo affair, Zhu Xiyan had gone out of his way to dodge him yesterday. Prince Yi's arrival handed Lin Jue a golden opportunity; he had to put on a stellar show.
"Please come inside, Lord Military Commissioner. Have a cup of tea first, and then I'll escort you to the drill ground."
"Skip the tea. Take me straight to the drill ground. After I see the troops, I have something to discuss with you."
Secret delight surged in Lin Jue's chest. If Prince Yi had business to discuss with him, that meant the prince already viewed him as one of his own. "Very well, I'll take Your Lordship straight to the drill ground. This way, please."
The drill ground lay only a few steps from the Military Training Office. Upon arriving, Lin Jue invited Prince Yi up onto the reviewing platform and ordered a captain to assemble the soldiers.
Lin'an had quite a force: a dense, dark sea of soldiers stretching across the ground.
Lin Jue bowed to Prince Yi first. "Lord Military Commissioner, let me address the troops first, and then you can give your speech."
Shen Jianchen nodded.
Lin Jue stepped forward and boomed, "Men! You shoulder a heavy duty, guarding the safety of Lin'an's people. His Majesty knows this well. Sending Prince Yi to serve as our Military Commissioner shows just how much His Majesty cares for every one of you. Now, let us welcome the Lord Military Commissioner to address the ranks!"
Lin Jue stepped back, leaving Shen Jianchen no choice but to step forward. Thinking of that incompetent emperor drained him of any desire to speak, yet facing row after row of expectant faces lit a spark of fire inside him.
"Men, I am Zhao Pu, Prince Yi, thirteenth son of His Majesty, and Lin'an's new Military Commissioner. From this day on, remember this: you are not just soldiers of Lin'an—you are soldiers of the Great Song! With men like you, the day will come when the Song no longer needs to ally with the Jin to strike the Liao. By our own strength alone, we will reclaim the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun!"
"The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun! The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun! The Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun!"
Shen Jianchen knew history could not be undone, and the Song-Jin alliance was a disastrous blunder, yet he could not pour cold water on a solemn gathering like this. Now that he had stirred their spirits, he needed to leave them with a word of warning as well.
"On the surface, the Jin ally with the Great Song, yet beneath it they harbor sinister designs. We must stay on our guard at every moment and never let the Jin fool us: use them where they serve us, and guard against them where we must. Only by sharpening our prowess on the battlefield can we protect our homes and defend our nation!"
"Protect our homes and defend our nation! Protect our homes and defend our nation! Protect our homes and defend our nation!"
Finished speaking, Shen Jianchen took a step back.
Lin Jue bowed again and stepped forward. "Carve every word of the Military Commissioner's speech into your hearts—do you hear me?!"
"Yes, sir! Yes, sir! Yes, sir!"
"Good! Back to your drills!"
The soldiers resumed their drills.
Amid the clamor of the drilling troops, Shen Jianchen stepped down from the reviewing platform and walked out of the drill ground.
Three senior officers came striding toward him: the Commander-in-Chief of All Forces, the Vice Commander-in-Chief, and the Division Commander.
Shen Jianchen had met them before. He had only come to the drill ground because he had private business to settle; who would have guessed word would leak so fast and bring all three rushing over?
The three officers hurried up to greet him, bowing low.
The Commander-in-Chief of All Forces spoke first. "When the Lord Military Commissioner pays a visit, you ought to send word ahead so we can come welcome you in advance and avoid neglecting so honored a guest."
Shen Jianchen said, "I just dropped by the Military Training Office on a whim. I didn't mean to stir up all three of you."
The Commander-in-Chief said, "The Training Officer sent a runner to notify us, so we hurried right over. If Your Lordship doesn't mind, why not step into our headquarters for a seat? It's far more comfortable than standing out here."
Shen Jianchen turned them down. "No need. I have some private matters to discuss with the Training Officer today. I'll pay a visit to your headquarters another day."
Lin Jue beamed with pride. On the strength of that single remark from Prince Yi, the three senior commanders were bound to summon him tomorrow. The Commandant's post was as good as in the bag.
Sure enough, the three commanders exchanged a knowing look.
The Commander-in-Chief added, "Since Prince Yi has business with the Training Officer, we won't intrude any further. There is just one matter we must report to Your Lordship: the post of Commandant is vacant at the moment, and all three of us intend to recommend Training Officer Lin. What are Your Lordship's thoughts?"
He had just guessed tomorrow; he never dreamed it would happen this fast! Overjoyed, Lin Jue thought how rewarding it was to stay close to Prince Yi.
Eyeing Lin Jue's delight, Shen Jianchen thought, "Didn't expect my visit to land him a promotion. Fine by me—that'll make it all the easier to talk him into giving up Xiaguo in a moment."
He told the three officers, "I leave it all to your discretion."
The Commander-in-Chief led the reply once more: "We shall abide by the Lord Military Commissioner's decision. We won't keep you from your business. We take our leave."
After the three withdrew, Shen Jianchen turned to Lin Jue. "My personal bodyguard loves the food at Juxiang Tower, and it's just about mealtime. Care to join me, Training Officer Lin?"
How could Lin Jue turn down an honor like that? He agreed without missing a beat. "Dining with Prince Yi is the highest honor this humble officer could ask for!"

Arriving at Juxiang Tower, they chose a private room. Lin Jue insisted on paying the bill, yet Prince Yi stood firm in refusing, leaving him no choice but to give in.
Allowed by Prince Yi to sit at the same table, Bai Feng was thrilled. Prince Yi was a man of status who would never wolf down his food, and Training Officer Lin came from money and wouldn't fight over dishes either. Today, Bai Feng could feast on this entire spread to his heart's content.
Once the waiter at Juxiang Tower laid out the wine and dishes, Training Officer Lin's presence saved Bai Feng even the chore of pouring wine for Prince Yi. He dug straight in with big mouthfuls; he never had to stand on ceremony in front of His Highness.
Lin Jue raised his wine cup. "I owe Prince Yi a huge debt of gratitude for helping me today. Let me drink three cups in a row; please drink at your leisure."
Lin Jue tossed back the first cup in one gulp, poured a second, and downed that too. As he reached for a third, Prince Yi stopped him.
"No matter how well you hold your liquor, Training Officer, you can't drink like that. If you get drunk, how am I supposed to talk business with you? Besides, I didn't do anything to help you." Shen Jianchen played dumb.
Lin Jue set down his cup. "Prince Yi coming to the Military Training Office helped me, and inviting me to dine with you helped me too. Without that, how would those three senior officers ever promote me?"
Shen Jianchen put on a look of sudden realization. "So that's how it is. Here I thought they had it all arranged beforehand and were just reporting it to me."
"Not at all. Half a dozen men had their eyes on the Commandant's post, and each of the three commanders wanted to slip one of his own people in. If Prince Yi hadn't shown up and treated me with such favor, they would never have promoted me on their own."
Eating as he listened to Lin Jue, Shen Jianchen caught the rot of Lin'an's officialdom in those few casual sentences. With an emperor who lived for pleasure and leaned on treacherous officials, how could the bureaucracy be anything but corrupt? When the very roots of the Great Song were rotting away, how healthy could its branches and leaves be?
"Who would have guessed I'd stumble into doing you a good turn without even trying? Since I've brought you some good fortune, Training Officer, I'll ask you to grant me one favor in return."
"Say the word, Prince Yi. I'd climb a mountain of blades or plunge into a sea of fire without a second thought."
"Don't make it sound so dramatic. It's a minor matter. I heard... you've taken a fancy to Youqi Jushi's maid Xiaguo. Is that true?"
So Prince Yi had come over Xiaguo! For a single maid to draw Prince Yi's attention, Zhu Xiyan must have put him up to it. No wonder the old man had dodged him yesterday.
Lin Jue dared not hide it, nor could he. "It's true. On the day Zhu Shuzhen and I went through our mutual divorce, I stated in front of everyone that I wanted to take Xiaguo as a concubine, and the Prefect gave his word in public too. Yet both Zhu Shuzhen and the maid refuse. Tell me, Prince Yi, why is my love life always so full of snags?"
"It's full of snags because you keep fixating on people who aren't right for you, or who were never yours to begin with..." Shen Jianchen thought of himself. The whole reason he was in pain was that he had fallen for someone he could not have, neither before crossing over nor after.
"Prince Yi's words have woken me straight from a dream. From now on, I need to change my mindset."
"Now you're talking sense. Let Xiaguo go. After all, Xiaguo's mistress is Youqi Jushi. Now that you've divorced her, stop tangling yourself up in her affairs and go live a good life of your own. That way, you're at peace and everyone else is at peace too."
Lin Jue had no choice but to heed Prince Yi's words. "I will follow Prince Yi's counsel. I... I'll let Xiaguo go."
"That's the spirit. Come on, eat up. Since you've done me this honor, Training Officer, from today on, you're one of my men."
Thrill surged through Lin Jue. Giving up one Xiaguo to have Prince Yi count him as one of his own? Not a loss at all. Unlucky in love, lucky in officialdom; Heaven was fair after all.
To a man, his career mattered most. As for romance, Lin Jue had already given up Zhu Shuzhen; letting go of a single Xiaguo was effortless. Once a man had wealth and rank, what kind of woman couldn't he have? Today, he made a clean break with Zhu Shuzhen in his heart and vowed never to pester her again.
Having persuaded two people in a single day, Shen Jianchen felt not a drop of triumph. After parting from Lin Jue, there was nowhere to go but Youlin Retreat, yet heading back meant facing Wenyuan's beaming excitement, and he had no desire to go home.
He told the coachman to drive to the foot of the hill, then sent him back ahead along the main road. With Bai Feng in tow, Shen Jianchen followed the trail at the hill's base toward the Divine Willow.
Walking along, Bai Feng asked, "Why is Your Highness heading to the Divine Willow again?"
Shen Jianchen did not answer, just kept walking ahead.
Bai Feng dared not ask again. When Prince Yi went quiet, something was weighing on his mind. Bai Feng could not figure it out: Prince Yi had no interest in meddling with military or civil affairs, had wealth and rank, and had Princess Jiahe by his side. With nothing to fret over and nothing lacking, what could he be dissatisfied with? Why was he unhappy?
When they reached the Divine Willow, four or five Buddhist nuns stood beneath it untying the red silk ribbons; more than half had already been taken down.
Curious, Shen Jianchen stepped forward and asked, "Venerable Sister, why are you taking down the red silk ribbons?"
One nun untied a ribbon, let the willow branch spring back, and placed the strip of silk into a bamboo basket on the ground.
"Amitabha. Benefactor may not know this, but too many red ribbons weigh down the Divine Willow. The Abbess instructs us to take them down every half year and bring them back to the nunnery to burn, while the Abbess recites sutras over them herself."
"I see. Still, won't the people who hung those ribbons feel heartbroken if they find out?" Shen Jianchen had already returned Qianli Xun's ribbon to her, yet Qiao Mengrong's was still up on the tree. If she learned it had been taken down, she was bound to be upset.
The nun replied, "Rest assured, Benefactor. The Divine Willow has a spirit of its own. The instant a ribbon touches its branches, it commits the wish of the one who hung it to memory. Leaving the silk behind afterward is just a formality."
Bai Feng figured the nun was spinning a yarn. How could a tree be that magical!
"Thank you for telling me, Venerable Sister." After thanking her, Shen Jianchen thought that if Mengrong ever asked, at least he now had a comforting answer to give her so she would not be too sad.
All the nuns were women, shorter than men, and could not reach the ribbons tied higher up.
One nun said, "Why isn't Old Man Li here yet? He's tall; we need him to reach the high ones."
Another replied, "Someone in Old Man Li's family fell ill, so he can't get away right now."
Seeing their predicament, Shen Jianchen said, "Bai Feng, come help these Venerable Sisters with me."
"Yes, Young Master." By now, Bai Feng knew down to a tee when he ought to call His Highness "Young Master."
The nuns could not thank them enough.
With two men lending a hand, the high ribbons were no longer a problem. Before long, every last ribbon was down, filling two whole bamboo baskets. After offering their thanks, the nuns lifted the baskets and left.
Stripped of the red ribbons, the willow before him convinced Shen Jianchen more than ever that this was the very Divine Willow he and Qianli Xun had seen before crossing over.
The Divine Willow stood unchanged, yet the people beneath it were no longer who they had been. He had Qiao Mengrong; Qianli Xun had Wenyuan. Fate had laid everything out in neat order. Why keep clinging on...
"Brother, what are you doing here?"
Qiao Mengrong's voice came from behind him. Shen Jianchen turned around to find Mengrong wearing a surprised expression.
"I should be asking you. What brought you all the way out here?"
"Brother was gone all day, and I got bored, so I came to take a walk beneath the Divine Willow you like so much. I never expected to run into you here! Young Master Qiao told me Brother went to see the Training Officer to settle Youqi Jushi's trouble." Before he could reply, Qiao Mengrong asked in alarm, "Where are the red ribbons on the willow? What happened to them?"
Shen Jianchen told her the truth. "Before you arrived, the nuns from the nunnery took every ribbon down to carry back and burn."
"What? The ones Brother and I hung together, with both our names written on them—just burned like that?" Sure enough, Qiao Mengrong's face fell.
Shen Jianchen comforted her. "No need to feel sad. The nun told me that the moment you tie on a ribbon, the Divine Willow commits your wish to memory. The ribbon itself is just a formality."
"Brother isn't just making that up to coax me?"
"The nun said it herself. If you don't believe me, ask Bai Feng. He heard it with his own ears."
Qiao Mengrong looked to Bai Feng, her eyes full of doubt.
Bai Feng replied, "That's what the nun said, word for word. His Highness isn't fooling you, Princess."
A smile lit up Qiao Mengrong's face. "As long as the Divine Willow remembers Brother's wish and mine, whether the ribbon stays up doesn't matter."
Bai Feng figured the nun had fooled His Highness, and now His Highness was fooling the Princess. Sigh! Sometimes people just loved hearing sweet lies, and even beamed over them.
"Brother, shall we head back?"
"You only just got here."
"I came because I was thinking of Brother. Now that I've found you, there's no need to look at the Divine Willow anymore."
Shen Jianchen nodded in agreement. He realized that facing Mengrong today no longer weighed so heavy on his heart. Qianli Xun was about to find her own happy home, and he had made up his mind to accept what fate had laid out for him.
Yet in his heart he still loved Qianli Xun. Letting go was no easy thing; all he could do was press those feelings down to the bottom of his heart, bit by bit.
As they crossed by the pond and approached the secluded grove, Shen Jianchen came to a halt. "Mengrong, at next year's Mid-Autumn Festival, I'll marry you and bring you home as my bride."
Though Qiao Mengrong had always known it, this was the first time since being brought back to life that Prince Yi had spoken of marrying her on his own. Overwhelmed with joy, she stepped forward and threw her arms around him.
"I'm so happy today!"
Shen Jianchen tried to make himself wrap his arms around the girl pressed against his chest, yet his raised hands refused to come down...
Both Xilan and Bai Feng turned their backs to give them privacy, happy for their two masters.
Just then, frogs began croaking from the pond: "Ribbit, ribbit, ribbit..."
Shen Jianchen remembered the night of his duel with Qianli Xun. The frogs had been croaking in that exact same way.

#TITLE: Qixi Arrives and the Streets Come Alive
On Qixi Festival, the servants of Zhu Manor rose at dawn and got straight to work, eager to finish their chores and head out to roam the streets. Once dusk closed in, they would hurry back to set up the Qiqiao Banquet, the Qixi feast for praying for skill.
Some fetched small wooden boards, spread soil over them, and planted millet seeds; some carved fine, clever patterns into melons of every kind; some shaped all manner of pastries from flour, oil, and honey into endless designs and forms; and others bundled tall mung bean sprouts with red and blue silk threads and arranged them on plates.
Still other servants hung lanterns and silk festoons in Zhu Manor's largest courtyard, set up incense tables, and laid out Mohouluo clay dolls, carved flower-melons, fine wine, the Four Treasures of the Study, needles and thread, and more.
Once everything stood ready, the servants—men and women alike—changed into new clothes and headed out in twos and threes to stroll the streets.
Dressed in her new outfit, Lutao invited Kuqiao to go out and have fun on the streets, only for Kuqiao to turn her down, saying she had to attend her mistress. Kuqiao had half wanted to go with the other maids, yet going with them meant missing Bai Feng, and Qixi Festival was meant to be spent with the one you liked.
Thanks to Prince Yi's help, Lin Jue no longer pestered Xiaguo. Xiaguo had ventured back from Wangyue Pavilion, and she was even less willing to leave her mistress's side, because staying with her mistress was the only way she could see Young Master Qiao.
Each of the two maids had her own private motive, and Qianli Xun saw no point saying much. If they wanted to tag along, let them.
Changing into their fresh, custom-made outfits, the three walked out of Zhu Manor and boarded the carriage. Nan Shuang had stopped by yesterday with the meeting time and place; setting off now would bring them to Seven Star Bridge in Lin'an right on time, neither early nor late.
Qianli Xun reminded her, "Xiaguo, Prince Yi will be there today too. The moment you see him, make sure you give him a few proper kowtows."
"Don't worry, Miss. As soon as I see Prince Yi, I'll drop to my knees and kowtow without a word."
"Good."
With Bai Feng on her mind, Kuqiao stayed quiet the whole way.
Qianli Xun asked, "Why are you so quiet today?"
Kuqiao tried to cover it up. "I'm always quiet. Is Miss just noticing now?"
Qianli Xun studied Kuqiao's face. "No way. Your quiet today isn't like your usual quiet. Fess up: is there someone you like? Don't tell me it's Nan Shuang! I've seen you hanging around him a lot, haha...!"
Kuqiao flushed crimson. "Miss just loves teasing me! Nan Shuang is still a kid. How could I like him? The only reason I got close to him is because Miss sees Young Master Qiao all the time, so I couldn't help getting friendly with him."
Thinking it over, Qianli Xun figured Kuqiao had a point and stopped teasing her.
Kuqiao's mention of Young Master Qiao stirred a strange ache inside Xiaguo.
During her stay at Wangyue Pavilion, her mistress and Young Master Qiao had grown close in leaps and bounds. Xiaguo dared not dream of Young Master Qiao liking her back; as long as she stayed by her mistress and got to see him, she would be content. And if her mistress married Young Master Qiao someday, seeing him every day might even bring her the chance to wait on him up close.
She had heard of plenty of wealthy households in Lin'an taking a mistress's maid as a concubine. Xiaguo wanted no part of being Lin Jue's concubine, yet if Young Master Qiao ever wanted her, she would become his concubine with all her heart.
Since the lane leading to Seven Star Bridge was too narrow for a carriage, they stepped out early and continued on foot. Crowds streamed past in both directions, everyone dressed in new clothes and beaming with cheer, as festive as New Year's Day.
Qianli Xun marveled at how these ancient folk turned an ordinary Qixi Festival into such a jubilant, bustling spectacle.
Weaving through the crowd, the mistress and her two maids reached Seven Star Bridge. Qianli Xun scanned the area, yet saw no sign of Wenyuan.
Just as she was about to grumble, Kuqiao pointed off to the side. "Miss, Young Master Qiao is over there! He's waving at us!"
Qianli Xun looked over, and sure enough, there sat Wenyuan inside a teahouse, waving his arm. Breaking into a smile, she waved back and led her maids toward the teahouse.
This teahouse differed from most, built half open to the air beneath an exquisite awning; sitting under its shade over tea gave an unobstructed view all around.
Qianli Xun spotted Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe sitting at the table too. Stepping forward, she bowed in greeting to the two noble guests, careful not to utter their titles aloud in case they preferred to keep their identities hidden while strolling the streets.
Without a second thought, Xiaguo dropped to her knees and kowtowed at once. "Thank you, Your Highness! Thank you, Your Highness!"
Shen Jianchen knitted his brows. "What 'Highness'? Today there is only Young Master Shen."
"Thank you, Young Master Shen! Thank you, Young Master Shen!"
"Just thank your mistress. As for me, it's your mistress who owes me thanks." Shen Jianchen spoke those words on purpose, shooting Qianli Xun a glance. Dressed up today, she looked stunning, and his heart could not help racing.
Prince Yi's double meaning was plain as day, and that look in his eyes was anything but innocent. "Flirting with me again!" Qianli Xun thought.
Glancing over at Princess Jiahe, she saw the princess still smiling away. Couldn't she hear the flirtation laced into Prince Yi's tone? And that gaze! The princess was way too naive. Couldn't she read the room at all?
Looking at Wenyuan, he looked just as cheerful, without a shred of guard up against Prince Yi.
"Xiaguo, since Young Master Shen said so, get up. No need to stay kneeling." Qianli Xun took a seat beside Wenyuan. "Rest assured, Young Master Shen, I will thank you today."
"And how do you plan to thank me?" Even though he already knew, Shen Jianchen wanted to hear Qianli Xun say it herself. Even though he meant to let go of his feelings for her, the moment he saw her, he could not stop himself from wanting to draw out every word with her.
Qianli Xun asked, "Didn't Wenyuan tell you?"
"What he says is his business. I want to hear it from Youqi Jushi's own lips."
Irritation flared in Qianli Xun's chest. "Still got the nerve to flirt right out in the open? Watch me kill that wishful thinking right now!"
Qianli Xun rested her head against Wenyuan's shoulder. "Wenyuan can speak for me anytime. Him saying it is no different from me saying it."
Envy flickered across Qiao Mengrong's face; she wished she could lean her head on Prince Yi's shoulder too. Yet his expression turned stern and his eyes dropped, leaving her too timid to try. A sigh escaped her lips: "Youqi Jushi and Young Master Qiao really make people envious."
Qiao Wenxian murmured, "Princess Jiahe and Prince Yi make people just as envious."
Unable to stomach watching the two of them act so cozy, Shen Jianchen took Qiao Mengrong by the hand. "Mengrong, let's go stroll the streets."
With Prince Yi reaching for her hand on his own, bliss washed over Qiao Mengrong. Like a docile lamb, she followed him out of the teahouse.
Qianli Xun sat up from Wenyuan's shoulder in delight. "That's how it should be! When you've got such a sweet girl, cherish her—don't eat from your own bowl while eyeing the pot."
Qiao Wenxian blinked in confusion. "What do you mean?"
"Nothing. Let's go stroll too!" Unable to explain to Wenyuan, Qianli Xun took Qiao Wenxian's hand and led him out of the teahouse as well.
Xilan had already hurried after the princess.
Watching Kuqiao and Bai Feng the whole time, Nan Shuang grew all the more certain there was something special between those two.
Bai Feng hated tearing himself away from Kuqiao, yet with Prince Yi leaving, he had to follow and could only leave her behind.
Xiaguo paid no attention to anyone else; her eyes revolved around Young Master Qiao alone.
When their masters rose to leave, Kuqiao, Xiaguo, and Nan Shuang all followed behind.
Kuqiao walked fastest of all and soon caught up to Bai Feng.
Qianli Xun did not bother reining her in; today was a holiday anyway, so let her do as she pleased.
Xiaguo, meanwhile, stuck close to her mistress's heels, which felt just like staying right beside Young Master Qiao.
Ahead of him walked couples in pairs, and all around him passersby strolled in pairs too; above all, watching Bai Feng and Kuqiao blossom with quiet affection made Nan Shuang's heart itch. He weighed his options: was Xilan better, or Xiaguo?
Before long he sorted it out: Xilan was the princess's maid, way out of his league. Better to go with Xiaguo. Besides, Xiaguo was prettier than Xilan anyway.
Nan Shuang tugged Xiaguo's sleeve. "Sister Xiaguo, slow down. I have something to tell you."
Eyes glued to Young Master Qiao's back, Xiaguo snapped with impatience, "What is it? Spit it out."
A shy blush crept up Nan Shuang's cheeks. "Sister Xiaguo, spend Qixi Festival with me, won't you?"
Xiaguo shot back, "Aren't I walking with you right now?"
Nan Shuang explained, "What I mean is, could you and I like each other the way Bai Feng and Sister Kuqiao do?"
"You little kid, what nonsense is running through your head? Worry about waiting on your master instead!" With that, Xiaguo shook Nan Shuang off, caught up to their two masters, and resumed walking beside her mistress while watching Young Master Qiao.
Nan Shuang hurried after her with a sulky scowl, gazing with deep envy at how happy Bai Feng and Kuqiao looked up ahead.
Holding Wenyuan's hand, Qianli Xun walked behind Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe. When she noticed Prince Yi let go of Princess Jiahe's hand, the pang of disappointment in her chest was no smaller than Qiao Mengrong's.
From the bottom of her heart, Qianli Xun hoped Prince Yi would devote himself to Princess Jiahe with all his heart and that the two of them would share a happy life together. To her mind, offering a blessing filled with sincere goodwill was the finest way to thank Prince Yi.

#TITLE: Rumors Begin to Spread
Several couples passed by, each young woman holding two lotus buds that looked as though they grew from a single stem.
Curiosity sparked in Qianli Xun's eyes. "Wenyuan, what is that?"
Qiao Wenxian laughed. "After losing your memory, you really don't remember a thing! That's a twin-headed lotus. It's a symbol of good fortune."

Holding it in her hand as a novel keepsake, Qianli Xun gave it a closer look and saw it had been fashioned by joining two lotus buds together, not a real twin-headed lotus at all.
Turning his head, Shen Jianchen spotted the twin-headed lotus in Qianli Xun's hand. Without knowing what it was called, he stepped up to a passing stall and bought one for Qiao Mengrong as well.
Taking it into her hands, Qiao Mengrong felt a rush of emotion. This was her first time spending Qixi Festival out in the city with Prince Yi, and his first time giving her a twin-headed lotus. Tears welled in her eyes. "Brother, do you know what a twin-headed lotus stands for?"
Shen Jianchen shook his head, a fresh weight of pressure settling on him at Qiao Mengrong's reaction. He had seen Qianli Xun holding one and knew he had no chance of buying one for her, so he could only give it to Mengrong instead.

"I'll treasure the twin-headed lotus Brother gave me with all my care."
"It's bound to wilt sooner or later."

Shen Jianchen glanced back at Qianli Xun. She was smiling so bright; no doubt she had long since forgotten Shen Jianchen, so why keep clinging to her?

Qiao Mengrong caught his meaning plain as day: he meant to grow old together with her. A radiant smile broke across her face as she continued strolling the streets beside him.
Walking along, Qianli Xun breathed in the scent of her twin-headed lotus while taking in the crowds and street stalls around her. As she looked about, the person she loathed most drifted into view. She turned her head away and pretended not to see him.
Chen Haoyu had already spotted her and strolled right over on purpose with a handful of lackeys at his heels. "What fate brings my dear in-law and me together! Even out strolling the streets, we bump into each other."
"Chen Haoyu, if you know what's good for you, stay back before I beat you up!" Qianli Xun thrust her twin-headed lotus into Xiaguo's hands and raised her fist in warning.
"I only came over to say hello. Why act like a ferocious she-demon?" Chen Haoyu clapped Qiao Wenxian on the shoulder. "Qiao Wenxian, I feel sorry for you. If you marry her someday, every day of your life is going to be misery."
Qianli Xun snapped in anger, "One more second and I'm swinging!"
Qiao Wenxian stepped in to soothe her. "Shuzhen, no need to lose your temper with him. He's just running his mouth."
Chen Haoyu said, "Qiao Wenxian knows me best! I've had my fun running my mouth, so I'm off. Won't disturb the two of you any longer." With that, he led his lackeys away without a fuss.
Qianli Xun unclenched her fist and held out her palm, waiting for Xiaguo to hand back the twin-headed lotus.
Xiaguo hesitated for a beat before placing it back in her mistress's hand. Young Master Qiao had bought that twin-headed lotus, and she hated letting go of it.
Taking back the twin-headed lotus, Qianli Xun asked, "Do you like twin-headed lotuses too?"
Xiaguo nodded.
"It wouldn't be right for me to buy you one of these. You'll just have to wait until you find someone you like and let him buy one for you."
Noticing a stem in Kuqiao's hand up ahead, Qianli Xun realized Kuqiao had fallen for Bai Feng; no wonder the two of them had been walking side by side laughing and chatting the whole time.
Nan Shuang piped up, "If Youqi Jushi approves, I can buy one for Sister Xiaguo."
Qianli Xun laughed. "Whether I approve doesn't matter. What matters is whether Xiaguo agrees."
Xiaguo cut in at once: "I don't want you buying me anything!"
Qianli Xun chided her with a laugh, "Even if you don't want it, don't be so blunt. You'll hurt Nan Shuang's feelings."
Sure enough, Nan Shuang stormed off in a huff and hurried ahead to join Kuqiao and Bai Feng.
Qiao Wenxian chuckled. "Look at my Nan Shuang, all grown up and tasting his first crush. Xiaguo, won't you consider him?"
Hearing the man she loved play matchmaker for her stung Xiaguo's heart; it proved Young Master Qiao did not have her in his heart at all.
"No need to persuade me, Young Master Qiao. There's already someone I like..."
Qianli Xun lit up with delight. "Who is it?"
"I like him, though he might not like me back. Better not say; it would only be embarrassing." Xiaguo lowered her head and refused to say another word.
Since Xiaguo did not want to tell, Qianli Xun could not press her further.
Remembering their plan to treat Prince Yi to a storytelling show, Qiao Wenxian said, "Shuzhen, Qiaotou Wa is just up ahead."
"We're here already? I still wanted to stroll around with you a bit longer."
"After the storytelling, I'll stroll with you some more."
"All right."
Quickening their pace, they caught up to Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe.
Their masters gave the servants leave to go off on their own. Kuqiao and Bai Feng were over the moon, Xilan wanted to wander the streets on her own too, and Nan Shuang jumped at the chance to act as Xilan's guide. Only Xiaguo stayed behind with her mistress; after all, the man she loved stayed right by her mistress's side.
Meanwhile, Chen Haoyu's lackeys were bored stiff trailing him through the streets. One of them spoke up: "Young Master Chen, this Qixi Festival is a total bore. We'd be better off heading over to Chunxiang Brothel."
Chen Haoyu let out a sigh. "I know it's boring, all right? I only came out to gauge how well my rumors are spreading. Today's got the biggest crowds on the streets. Have any of you heard people talking about Zhu Shuzhen?"
The lackeys shook their heads. "Not a word."
"Why is it taking so long? I'm losing my patience! You!" Chen Haoyu pointed at one of his lackeys. "Go tell the rumor-mongers to pick up the pace and spread it twice as hard. I'll pay them extra taels of silver."
"Yes, sir!" The lackey took his orders and scurried off.
Another lackey said, "Don't get too impatient, Young Master Chen. Rumors travel by word of mouth, so they're bound to take time. Besides, it's been less than ten days. Give it a month, then see what happens."
"Fair point, no use rushing. Still, how can I not be impatient? You all saw it just now: the way Zhu Shuzhen glared daggers at me made my blood boil. Until I knock her down a few pegs, I've got a knot sitting right here." Chen Haoyu rubbed his chest in mock agony.
One lackey said, "We've got knots in our chests too! The whole street is packed with couples, while you and the rest of us are all bachelors. We stick out like sore thumbs in this crowd. We really ought to hit Chunxiang Brothel."
Chen Haoyu pulled out a pouch of silver and tossed it into one man's arms. "Go on if you want. My vow of abstinence isn't up yet, and I won't step a single inch over that line."
"Thank you, Young Master Chen!" Clutching the silver, the lackeys grinned from ear to ear and headed off toward Chunxiang Brothel.
Left all alone, Chen Haoyu watched the couples drifting up and down the street and could not help wondering when he might find someone who suited his heart too.
Chen Haoyu had dallied with plenty of women, yet not a single one had found her way into his heart so far.
Perhaps he had played around with too many women to ever feel for one the way Qiao Wenxian did, or the way these men on the street did—men who knew nothing of what women were like.
At times he even envied those naive men, who could still look at women through misty, starry eyes and feel emotions beyond words, whereas to this day he still had no idea what love was.
He had thought before that if any woman ever stirred real feelings in him, he would marry her and mend his ways for good. Yet he figured that notion was nothing more than a pipe dream.
Before he knew it, he had wandered into an ordinary teahouse, the sort of place he never set foot in. Breaking his own habit today had only one purpose: to listen in and hear how the rumors were spreading.
Chen Haoyu picked a seat, ordered a pot of tea at random, filled his cup, and strained his ears. Customers came and went, chatting for the most part of household trivia and romance; after listening for ages, he caught not a single word of his rumors.
Two young couples walked in, and the waiter happened to seat them at the table right beside Chen Haoyu. He was just about to leave when one of the young women asked, "Have you heard the ci lyric Youqi Jushi wrote the other day?"
At last, someone was talking about Zhu Shuzhen! Not about to miss a word, Chen Haoyu pricked up his ears.
One of the men asked, "Which ci?"
The young woman answered, "'Qingping Yue: Summer Excursion on the Lake.'"
The other man said, "I've heard it. One line in it is bold beyond belief."
The second woman chimed in, "It really is! How could she be so brazen as to write 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed'? Just reading it makes me blush."
A wave of glee washed over Chen Haoyu. "Hmph!" he thought. "Before my rumors even make the rounds, your own ci has already spread far and wide. You reap what you sow, all right! Heheh..."
The first woman went on, "I've also heard ugly rumors about Youqi Jushi these past days—calling her a tigress, saying she had affairs and traded secret glances with men, even saying she deserves to be pointed at by a thousand fingers and spat on by ten thousand. It's awful to hear."
One of the men looked skeptical. "Could someone be making all that up?"
The second woman said, "There's no smoke without fire. Someone who can write 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed' is capable of just about anything."
The other three nodded in agreement.
Chen Haoyu gloated all the more to himself: "Just you wait, Zhu Shuzhen! Before long, the whole city will be buzzing with scandals about you. No matter how tough your fists are, who're you going to punch when it's everyone talking? Hahaha...!"

#TITLE: Treating Them to Storytelling at the Goulan
Reaching Qiaotou Wa, they headed toward the Goulan storytelling theater.
Just before they reached the entrance, Shen Jianchen came to a stop. "Wenyuan, may I have a quick word in private with Youqi Jushi?"
Wenyuan agreed without hesitation. "Say as many words as you like."
Qianli Xun wanted no part of it. "Whatever Young Master Shen has to say, say it right here."
Shen Jianchen spoke three syllables: "Qianli Xun..."
Qianli Xun stepped aside at once; the last thing she wanted was for Prince Yi to spill her secret that she was Qianli Xun. A smug smile crossed Shen Jianchen's face as he followed behind her.
Qiao Mengrong felt bewildered. What could Prince Yi have to say to Youqi Jushi that couldn't be spoken in front of everyone?
"Young Master Qiao, what are they going to talk about? Why step away from us?"
"No need to worry, Young Lady Qiao. Young Master Shen has his reasons. There's no need for us to pry."
Seeing Qiao Wenxian so at ease put Qiao Mengrong's doubts to rest, though she kept watching them and guessing what they might be saying.
With her mistress stepped away, Xiaguo stood alone and edged a little closer to Young Master Qiao on purpose. She gazed at him, yet his eyes held only her mistress.
Once sure they were out of earshot, Shen Jianchen asked, "Didn't you promise to send me the manuscript to read first as soon as you finished it? Why didn't you, and instead brought me straight to a storytelling show?"
Qianli Xun thought that for a tall, broad-shouldered man, Prince Yi sure had a petty streak. "Don't sweat a tiny thing like that, Young Master Shen."
"How is that a tiny thing? It shows you go back on your word—and that I don't mean a thing to you."
Qianli Xun hurried to explain, keeping her voice low so no one would overhear. "I did plan to send it to Youlin Retreat, only as you know, Wenyuan stays there too, so he'd have caught on in no time. "Could I be any more sincere? Princess Jiahe is a wonderful girl. Know how to treasure her, and whatever you do, don't let her down just because you have high rank and money."

"I already consider Princess Jiahe my close confidante. If you ever dare let her down, I won't care a bit about your rank. I'll stand up for her and never shrink back like a coward."

It dawned on Shen Jianchen at last: when the timing is wrong, even the truest thing turns wrong. The love he could not stop showing whenever he faced Qianli Xun had been misread by her as shameless flirting. What bitter irony.

Qianli Xun realized she had been so caught up in romance these days that she had all but forgotten the Divine Willow; she might as well take the chance to go see it.
Qiao Wenxian walked up with a smile and took her hand. "What are you thinking about?"
"Nothing. They've already gone inside. Let's catch up."

"What about Miss and Young Master Qiao?"

Xiaguo felt touched by her mistress's kindness. She knew her mistress would be upset if she ever found out about her secret crush on Young Master Qiao, yet she could not stop her heart from beating for him, stealing shy glances his way every now and then.
Up on stage, a storyteller was recounting the turbulent drama of the Three Kingdoms, right in the middle of the tale of Guan Yunchang having the poison scraped from his bone, holding the whole audience spellbound...
Qianli Xun had never met the storyteller who took her manuscript and had no idea when he would perform her tale. Looking around, she waved a waiter over.
The waiter hurried up. "How may I help you, Young Lady?"
"I heard there's a writer named Qianli Xun whose huaben stories are quite good, and they're often performed here at your theater. Any idea when today's session starts?"
"Young Lady must not have noticed the signboard outside when you came in. It's all written right there. As soon as the performer on stage wraps up the Three Kingdoms, the next storyteller is up with Qianli Xun's tale."
"Is Qianli Xun really that famous around here?"
"Everyone knows the name, though nobody knows the man. He writes great stories, and the crowd loves hearing them."
Qianli Xun beamed with satisfaction. "Go on with your work. I'll call if we need anything."
Qianli Xun had watched the "Romance of the Three Kingdoms" television drama several times, and every single time she reached the scene where Hua Tuo scraped the poison from Guan Yunchang's bone, her heart leaped into her throat.
Training in Qianli Fist since childhood had made her tough enough to take a beating, yet letting someone scrape poison off her bone without even a drop of anesthesia? Not in a million years! She figured she would die of the pain long before the poison ever got her.
Hearing a storyteller perform the passage here in this bygone era she had crossed into filled her with a fresh wave of awe for Guan Yunchang.
Seeing how absorbed Shuzhen was in the tale, Qiao Wenxian had not meant to interrupt. Then he remembered he still had not told her his father had agreed to propose marriage—what better moment than right now to put a smile on her face?
Leaning in close, Qiao Wenxian whispered, "Shuzhen, my father has agreed to come to Zhu Manor in a month or two to propose marriage."
One second earlier, Qianli Xun had been wincing along with Guan Yunchang's bone-scraping agony; the moment Wenyuan spoke, every trace of pain vanished. A brilliant grin spread across her face as she looked at him in thrill: "Don't tell me this is the good news you promised to bring me?"
Qiao Wenxian nodded. "It is."
"That really is a huge piece of good news!" Qianli Xun then put on a mock pout. "A month or two? That's so far away! If only it were tomorrow."
"Who knew you were in such a hurry to marry me."
"Aren't you in a hurry to marry me too?"
"Of course I am! Only my father said since your mutual divorce was so recent, proposing right away wouldn't be proper, so he told me to wait. Be patient—a month or two will fly right by. I promise I'll keep nudging my father."
Qianli Xun nodded. Good news had been rolling in one piece after another these days: not only was Xiaguo's trouble settled, her own marriage to Wenyuan was taking shape too. The sour mood from running into Chen Haoyu on the street faded from her mind in no time.
Hearing that the Qiao family would come to Zhu Manor to propose made Xiaguo happy for her mistress, and even happier for herself. Only once her mistress married into Qiao Manor could she draw closer to Young Master Qiao.
At last the Three Kingdoms tale drew to a close. The next storyteller stepped onto the stage—the very man who had taken Qianli Xun's huaben manuscript. Taking a sip of tea, he set his fan and teapot on the table and began:
"Ladies and gentlemen! Last time we left off with Hongxiang and Xuanying meeting, coming to know each other's hearts, and falling in love. Yet their love could receive no blessing, because a blood feud divided the Flower Deity and the Phoenix. To be together, they had to leap from Reincarnation Cliff, shed their immortal bodies, and become mortals so they could remain together forever. Yet once they became mortal, a grave trial awaited them..."
Someone in the crowd called out, "What trial?"
The storyteller replied, "Once reborn as mortals, they would no longer recognize each other; finding one another again would rest on fate alone."
Another listener sighed, "How tragic! Hurry up and tell us—we're all on the edge of our seats!"
Nobody had chimed in during the Three Kingdoms tale just now, yet the instant her own story started, listeners were calling out. Qianli Xun glowed with pride; it proved people truly loved listening to it.
The storyteller launched into the tale:
By mutual vow, Hongxiang and Xuanying met at Reincarnation Cliff. Gazing down into the bottomless abyss, Xuanying plucked a single phoenix feather from his own body and said, "Take this. Once you are reborn as a mortal, bring it with you and come find me." With her immortal power, Hongxiang conjured a golden petal and pressed it into Xuanying's hand: "This is my gift to you. Remember me, and let us renew our destined bond in the mortal world."
Using their immortal power, each concealed the other's keepsake upon their body. Hand in hand, they leaped from Reincarnation Cliff. As they fell, an unseen force tore them apart until neither could see the other. After drifting through the darkness for who knew how long, they reached the mortal world at last.
Xuanying was reborn as the son of the Lord of Wuye City and named Wu Xuanying, bearing a petal-shaped birthmark upon his chest; Hongxiang was born to a mountain-village family surnamed Hua and named Hua Hongxiang, bearing a birthmark shaped like a phoenix feather upon her arm. Both had forgotten every trace of their past lives...
Shen Jianchen knew this story inside and out, so it stirred nothing new in him. Qiao Mengrong, however, felt swept up in emotion: two people in love, torn apart just like that—who knew how many trials and hardships they would have to endure before meeting again?
