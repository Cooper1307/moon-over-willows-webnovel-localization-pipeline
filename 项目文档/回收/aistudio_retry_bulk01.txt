#TITLE: Chapter 9: Whisked Tea, Talk, and the Road to Zhu Manor
When Qianli Xun returned to Zhulan Residence, Kuqiao sat at the tea table making tea. Her curiosity sparked, so she pulled up a chair to watch, pushing serious errands to the back of her mind for a moment.
"Making tea again?" she asked.
Whisking brisk waves inside the tea bowl with a bamboo tool, Kuqiao answered, "You drink a bowl after breakfast every morning, Miss."
"What do you call that gadget in your hand?"
"A tea whisk." Kuqiao lifted the purple clay teapot beside her, poured a splash of hot water along the rim of the bowl, and whisked again until fine froth began to rise.
"That's magic!" Qianli Xun marveled.
Kuqiao added hot water and whisked again, seven infusions in all, until thick creamy foam surged to the very lip of the bowl. Setting the whisk down, she lifted the tea bowl and presented it. "Ready to taste, Miss."
This time Qianli Xun did not gulp it down in one swallow. She took a small sip, lingered over the fresh fragrance, then took a second. Refinement, she thought, had nothing to do with modern gadgets; back in her high-tech era, she had never lived half this refined.
With the tea served, Kuqiao moved to the study desk to straighten up, laying out brush and paper before grinding ink. Finishing her last mouthful, Qianli Xun set the bowl down and asked, "What are you up to now, Kuqiao?"
"Every morning after tea, you sit here to read or write poetry and ci, Miss. I'm getting everything ready," Kuqiao replied without pausing the inkstick. These daily rituals were habits she refused to drop, never once asking her reborn mistress what she preferred, because deep down she still could not bear admitting her old mistress was gone.
Qianli Xun walked over and took a seat at the desk. She had never owned a real study desk before. During her school years, desks belonged to her parents or classrooms, and she had disliked them all. Later, when she bought her own apartment and the designer asked whether to include a study desk, she waved it off and asked for a simple computer desk instead. Even that gathered dust; whenever she wrote web novels, she propped herself against bed pillows, hugged a giant Hello Kitty plush, and tapped out chapters on her phone. Only after transmigrating into Zhu Shuzhen did she both own and need a study desk.
She eyed the Four Treasures of the Study spread before her. Her fingers remembered keyboards and touchscreens, whereas Zhu Shuzhen's hands knew only the brush. Good thing Qianli Xun had practiced brush calligraphy from middle school through college, putting in seven or eight solid years.
Out of the corner of her eye, she spotted a square wooden tub on the floor stuffed with rolled sheets of calligraphy. Pulling one out and smoothing it flat, she found a ci lyric penned in graceful strokes:
"Spring night, my jade body is soft as a gold hairpin as, back to the lamp, I unfasten my silk skirt. But the quilt and pillow are cold. The night's fragrance is gone.
Spring is a deep courtyard of many locked doors. Petals falling in falling rain make the night seem forever. Regret comes to me in dream. There is no escape." ("Huanxisha: Spring Night") [TN: Translated by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping]
Qianli Xun had read the lyric before, yet opening it now in Zhu Shuzhen's own shoes, she felt the poet's ache far deeper: the quiet resentment, loneliness, and frustration of a loveless marriage, and the fierce hunger for true love. She lifted the brush, soaked the tip in ink, and copied the entire poem stroke for stroke beside the original hand.
Once she laid the brush down, Kuqiao leaned over to admire the sheet. "Your writing is already seven or eight parts like before, Miss! Keep practicing and nobody could tell the difference."
That reminded Qianli Xun of her real business. "Kuqiao, I have to make a trip back to Zhu Manor. Give me the rundown on the household so I know what I'm walking into."
Kuqiao scratched her head. "Zhu Manor? Asking out of the blue, I don't know where to start. Could you ask something specific, Miss?"
Thirst crept back into Qianli Xun's throat. "My mouth's dry. Make me another bowl of tea, and we'll talk while you work."
"Right away!" Returning to the tea table, Kuqiao opened the small porcelain jar for tea powder. "Out of tea powder, so I'll grind some fresh. Ask whatever you like, Miss; I'll tell you all I know."
Watching Kuqiao drop roasted tea leaves into a stone mortar and pound them with a stone pestle, Qianli Xun asked, "First, who lives in Zhu Manor?"
"Master, Madam, Eldest Young Master, and Young Madam, your sister-in-law," Kuqiao replied as she worked the pestle. "When you married into the Lin family, Young Madam was already eight months pregnant, so the baby must be born by now."
"I've been married over half a year and never went back home once?"
Kuqiao transferred the crushed leaves into a tea roller and ground them back and forth. "Master agreed to the match on his own. You hated the idea, yet the betrothal was sealed, so you had no choice but to nod and go through with the wedding. On the third day, when it was time for the return visit to your maiden home, you were too furious to go."
"Did I leave things real tense with my parents?" Qianli Xun worried a bitter fallout would ruin her chances at mutual divorce.
"Tense at the time, sure," Kuqiao said, keeping the roller moving. "Still, what parent holds an overnight grudge against their own child? Besides, Madam ran into me on the street several times and asked after you every time."
"True enough. Parents don't stay mad at their kids forever..."
Her own parents flashed across Qianli Xun's mind. Growing up wild as a boy, she had earned endless scoldings and once even ran away from home; the moment they found her, she threw her arms around her mother in tears and every bit of anger vanished on the spot. Then a car crash took her mother when Qianli Xun was twelve. Her father passed every technique of Qianli Fist on to her, charged her with keeping the school alive, and shaved his head to become a wandering monk right around her college graduation, leaving her to run Qianli Fist Manor alone. Had Qianli Fist Manor even survived the earthquake? Grief tightened her chest, and a heavy sigh escaped her.
Having ground the leaves into fine dust and brushed them into a tea sieve, Kuqiao heard the sigh and tried to lift her spirits. "If you miss Master and Madam, I'll go back with you to visit them, Miss. They'll be thrilled to see you."
"What I don't get is this," Qianli Xun said. "If a daughter skips her return visit, her father holds a high post. Couldn't he question the son-in-law he handpicked? Don't tell me Lin Jue dares snub the Prefect of Lin'an."
Sifting the tea powder, Kuqiao answered, "I'm only a maid, so I don't know what goes on between Master and Son-in-law. All I know is Master guards family honor above everything."
A father obsessed with reputation. Would he step forward to back a mutual divorce? No sense pumping Kuqiao for guesses; better to meet Zhu Shuzhen's parents face to face and size them up herself.
"Kuqiao, finish that bowl of tea, then come with me to Zhu Manor."
"Yes, Miss." Done sifting, Kuqiao spooned green powder into the tea bowl and swept the surplus into the porcelain jar for later. She added hot water and whisked through seven infusions, then carried the finished bowl to her mistress. "Please drink, Miss."
Taking the bowl, Qianli Xun sighed in admiration. "Watching you pour so much care into every single step makes me almost hate to drink it."
"Only if you drink it is all that work worthwhile, Miss."
"Good with tea and smooth with words too. Just looking at you tells me what your old mistress was like: meticulous about every detail, nothing like sloppy me..."
Kuqiao flared up at once. "What talk is that, Miss?! How many times do I have to say it? You are my one and only mistress!"
Ignoring the scolding, eager to reach Zhu Manor and cut loose from a marriage she had zero use for, Qianli Xun drained the tea in one gulp, set down the bowl, and stood up. "Kuqiao, let's go to Zhu Manor!"
They walked out of Lin Manor, had the Lin coachman hitch a carriage, and climbed aboard one after the other. Her very first ride in a horse-drawn carriage thrilled Qianli Xun even more than her first ride in Shen Jianchen's Rolls-Royce. To her, that Rolls-Royce had been nothing more than a prop for flashing wealth, and she loathed men who waved cash in front of girls like clueless fools. Yet the instant the carriage lurched forward over uneven cobblestones, she had to admit the Rolls-Royce won hands down for comfort, bracing both hands to stay steady.
Soon loud street clamor swelled outside. Lifting the cloth curtain, she looked out over Lin'an's crowded avenues, lined shoulder to shoulder with storefronts. As the carriage threaded through throngs of pedestrians, snippets of banter and haggling drifted in; teahouses and taverns overflowed with guests, snatches of storytelling carrying out onto the street.
Dropping the curtain, Qianli Xun remarked, "Lin'an's markets are livelier than I expected."
"This is nothing. Bianliang, the Eastern Capital, is way livelier!"
"You've been there?"
"No, you told me before, Miss. A Lady Wei from Bianliang wrote inviting you up there to make friends through poetry, and you promised to take me sightseeing in the capital someday."
"Lady Wei? Is her name Wei Wan?"
"No idea. You never told me her given name."
Must be Wei Wan, Qianli Xun thought. Later historical notes recorded Zhu Shuzhen corresponding with Wei Wan, with surviving poems to prove it. Right now she had no patience to dig through old verse; figuring out how to win over Zhu Shuzhen's parents came first.
Lost in thought, she felt the clamor hush as the carriage rolled to a stop. "We're here, Miss," Kuqiao announced.
Nerves fluttered in Qianli Xun's stomach. "Here already? I haven't even figured out what to say or what face to put on when I meet my parents!"
"Don't panic, Miss. You grew up right here."
"Does it show that much? Even you noticed."
Kuqiao smiled without answering, hopped down first, and stood ready beside the step. After a brief hesitation inside, Qianli Xun braced herself and climbed down, taking Kuqiao's steadying hand. Looking up, she saw massive vermilion gates shut tight beneath a plaque reading "Zhu Manor," flanked by imposing stone lions that gave the entrance grand weight.
"Kuqiao, go knock."
"We don't enter through the main gate, Miss."
"Not the main gate? The back gate, then?"
"The side gate. Follow me, Miss."
"Weird. Why build such a grand gate if nobody walks through it? What a waste!"
"You wouldn't know, Miss. The main gate stays shut unless honored guests visit. Family members use the side gate; even Master uses the side gate every day."
Following Kuqiao around the left wall, Qianli Xun soon reached a far more modest side gate framed by slender bamboo groves, quiet and tasteful.

#TITLE: Chapter 10: Home to Zhu Manor, Meeting Her Mother
Kuqiao knocked on the side gate for a moment. Soon the servant on duty opened up, recognized Kuqiao, realized the young mistress was home, and swung the door wide. "Miss is back! Madam has been talking about you nonstop!"
"Where are Master and Madam?" Kuqiao asked. "Miss came to pay her respects."
"Master's still at the prefecture office. Madam is in the garden feeding the fish," the servant replied, stepping aside to clear the way. Qianli Xun crossed the threshold with Kuqiao right behind her. Once they were inside, the servant added, "Wait indoors, Miss; I'll run report to Madam." He jogged off toward the garden.
"Where do we go now?" Qianli Xun asked.
"Madam's quarters. Just stay right behind me, Miss."
Kuqiao led the way while Qianli Xun trailed after her. Zhu Shuzhen's childhood home felt like stepping into a labyrinth, though a scenic one that pleased the eye at every turn. With her hopeless sense of direction, Qianli Xun knew she would lose her bearings in seconds without a guide.
"Kuqiao, stick beside me the whole time in Zhu Manor. This estate is massive and twists every which way; I'll get lost for sure."
"Don't worry, Miss. I won't leave your side for a second."
After more twists than she could count, a courtyard gate appeared ahead. "Madam lives here, Miss. Come inside," Kuqiao said.
Stepping into the yard, they met a maid hurrying toward them. "Miss is home! Quick, come sit inside; I'll send someone to fetch Madam!"
"No need, Sister. The gatekeeper already went," Kuqiao replied, turning to introduce her. "Miss, this is Yinxing, a maid from Madam's rooms."
Before Qianli Xun could speak, Yinxing chided her, "Since when does Miss need you introducing me?" Taking Qianli Xun by the arm, she guided her indoors, chattering as they walked. "Miss, you haven't set foot home since marrying into the Lin house. If Master hadn't held her back, Madam would have marched right over to Lin Manor for you!"
Rarely this quiet, Qianli Xun clamped her lips shut lest one slip give her away. Inside, she picked a chair and sat while Yinxing sent other maids to brew tea. Once tea was laid out, Yinxing drew Kuqiao aside and asked in a hushed voice, "What's gotten into Miss? Her expression looks odd, nothing like before."
"Quit asking silly questions, Sister. I'm waiting on her here; go tend to your chores," Kuqiao brushed her off and resumed her post beside her mistress.
Yinxing turned back to Qianli Xun. "Sit tight, Miss. I'll step out to the doorway and watch for Madam."
Qianli Xun nodded. "Go ahead, no need to hover."
Once Yinxing left, Qianli Xun let out a quiet breath. About to face Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother, she wondered if she ought to squeeze out a few tears to look sincere, then thought better of it. Faking the original Zhu Shuzhen would look phony; better to stay natural and read the room as it came.
No sooner had she decided than a woman in her forties hurried in with reddened eyes, ringed by four or five maids. Guessing this was Zhu Shuzhen's mother, Qianli Xun rose from her seat.
Madam Xiang had been feeding the garden fish when a page boy ran up with word of her daughter's return; dropping everything, she rushed straight back with her maids. Stepping through the doorway and laying eyes on the daughter she had missed for over half a year, she felt her nose sting and tears well up. Seeing Shuzhen stand, she hurried over and seized both her hands. "Shuzhen, Mother thought you'd never come back to see me..."
"You're Zhu Shuzhen's birth mother?" Qianli Xun asked straight out. "When I woke up, Kuqiao told me I'm Zhu Shuzhen, daughter of Zhu Manor, so I had her bring me home today to meet my family."
That single speech froze Madam Xiang and every maid in the room. Staring through eyes brimming with tears, Madam Xiang looked her daughter over from head to toe. "Shuzhen, what on earth are you saying?"
Thump! Kuqiao dropped straight to her knees and burst into sobs before getting a word out. Good teammate, Qianli Xun applauded in silence!
Letting go of her daughter and stepping back, Madam Xiang sensed grave trouble. Dabbing the corners of her eyes with a silk handkerchief, she ordered the other maids, "All of you out, and shut the door." Once every maid except Kuqiao filed out and the door clicked shut, Madam Xiang sank into a chair. "What kind of scene are you two putting on?"
Knowing Kuqiao would lay out the whole tale, Qianli Xun sat back down and watched how Madam Xiang would take it.
Weeping hard, Kuqiao poured it all out. "Madam, Miss hasn't had one peaceful day since marrying Son-in-law! He beats her over nothing and drags brothel courtesans right into the house! Just yesterday he brought one home again, beat Miss, and shoved her straight into a pillar! She fainted dead away, and when she woke up, she couldn't remember a thing, like a whole different person! Sob..."
Fury surged across Madam Xiang's face. "How dare he! Why didn't you come home and tell Master and me sooner?"
Wiping her cheeks, Kuqiao sobbed, "Miss forbade me! Said telling you wouldn't help, might as well let Son-in-law beat her to death! Sob..."
Madam Xiang looked close at her daughter and spotted the wound across her forehead; overwhelmed at seeing her, she hadn't even noticed. Rising, she stepped over and touched the cut light as a feather with her handkerchief. "Does it hurt?"
Starved for motherly warmth since losing her own mother in that crash, Qianli Xun felt heat bloom inside her chest. "Doesn't hurt. Are you really my mother?"
Madam Xiang nodded. "You are my daughter Shuzhen."
"Mother, I woke up remembering nothing. If I'm not like the daughter you knew, please don't disown me."
Fresh tears rolled down Madam Xiang's cheeks. "Had I known you'd suffer like this in the Lin house, I never would have agreed to marry you to Lin Jue!"
What a clean start, Qianli Xun thought—already pulled Zhu Shuzhen's mother onto her side!
"Get up and speak, Kuqiao," Madam Xiang ordered. Drying her eyes, Kuqiao climbed to her feet. Sitting back down, Madam Xiang pressed on, "Tell me, Kuqiao: how is Shuzhen different from before?"
Glancing at her mistress for permission and catching a nod, Kuqiao spoke up. "Aside from her face and figure, everything's different! Now she speaks loud, talks nonstop, can't even whisk tea, and best of all, she knows martial arts! Even Son-in-law couldn't beat her, and she demanded he cast her out! Madam, I think Son-in-law knocked her cold and she was reborn!"
"What? Reborn?" Madam Xiang shot up in shock, then dropped back down, slapping the table as she wailed, "My poor daughter! Did Lin Jue beat you to death?! Sob..."
Unable to bear seeing her weep like that, Qianli Xun spoke up. "Mother, I'm alive, not dead, just a bit different from before."
Yes, same voice, same face, living and breathing before her eyes. Madam Xiang choked back her wails. "Yes, my Shuzhen is alive! Come here, let Mother get a good look at you."
Qianli Xun walked over, crouched down, and rested her head against her mother's knees. "Mother!"
Stroking her hair, Madam Xiang swore, "Mother almost lost you! No, I will never let you go back to that Lin house!"
Lifting her head, Qianli Xun said, "Mother, I may not remember the past, yet even after I woke up Lin Jue tried hitting me, and I saw that courtesan right there in the house. Out of anger I told him to write a bill of divorce, till I learned a cast-out wife loses her good name. So I pushed for mutual divorce. Lin Jue agreed and told me to bring Zhu family elders in three days to settle it."
Madam Xiang helped her stand. "Rest easy; Mother won't leave you trapped in that fiery pit! Good thing Lin Jue has the sense to agree to mutual divorce. My daughter did nothing wrong; he has zero grounds to cast you out anyway!"
"Mother, I want to stay home for a couple of days. I don't want to go back to Lin Manor."
"Silly girl, this is your home. Stay as long as you wish!" Madam Xiang called toward the door, "Yinxing!"
Yinxing pushed the door open. "Your orders, Madam?"
"Go to Lin Manor in person and inform the Lin family that Miss is staying at Zhu Manor. When she returns, we don't know yet."
"Yes, Madam." Yinxing bowed and went off on her errand.
A rush of joy warmed Qianli Xun. Having real family backing her anchored her in this strange century.
"Once your father gets home, I'll explain everything before you pay your respects," Madam Xiang told her. "Mother believes he'll consent to mutual divorce too."
"Is Father fierce?"
The question pierced Madam Xiang's heart, proving her daughter truly recalled nothing. "Not fierce, just stern, and strict at times. Nothing to fear. Your old courtyard is still kept for you. Have Kuqiao take you over and walk you around the estate so you can get your bearings; maybe your memories will come back bit by bit."
Bidding Madam Xiang farewell, Qianli Xun followed Kuqiao through winding lane after winding lane deeper into Zhu Manor. Impossible to guess how sprawling the grounds were; it felt as if they could walk forever without hitting a wall.
Along the way, singing drifted across the air. Qianli Xun halted and looked toward the sound, spotting another walled courtyard whose high walls hid the halls within. "Kuqiao, who lives in there? Why is someone singing?"
"Those are the singing girls and dancing girls Master keeps."
"What? Singing and dancing girls? Why does my father keep entertainers?"
"Most high officials' estates keep musicians and dancers to entertain honored guests. Every year Master hosts a poetry banquet for Lin'an's noted scholars, and entertainers help liven up the occasion."
"Did I attend those banquets?"
"Every year since you were ten, Miss! Many of your finest poems and lyrics were composed right at those banquets and passed from hand to hand by scholars. That's how you earned the title Poetess of Jiangnan."
"The Poetess of Jiangnan," Qianli Xun murmured. Web novels she could spin in her sleep, yet classical verse was not her craft. Wearing Zhu Shuzhen's name meant defending her place in the literary world, and pressure pricked at her chest until she remembered she knew Zhu Shuzhen's poems by heart. Exhaling, she asked, "Kuqiao, isn't there a single straight path in Zhu Manor? Everything twists like a maze!"
"Not one! Heard Master built this manor around the poetic ideal of winding paths leading to secluded spots. Isn't it poetic?"
"Poetic, sure, and it's making me dizzy! Where on earth is my courtyard?"
"Patience, Miss. Two more bends and we're there."
Two turns later, a small lake opened before them, over half its surface blanketed with lotus leaves dotted with upright lotus buds. A little wooden bridge spanned the water, leading at last into Zhu Shuzhen's own secluded garden.

#TITLE: Chapter 13: Facing Her Father, Smashing the Table
By the time they reached Zhu Manor's Main Courtyard, night had fallen thick. Lanterns hung at even intervals all around the yard, their bright pools of glow pushing back the dark. Yinxing raised her handheld lantern, blew out the flame, and led Qianli Xun and Kuqiao down a long corridor into the main hall's paved stone courtyard. By the lantern light, three magnolia trees stood clear on each side in full bloom, white, purple, and yellow.
Drawn by the blossoms, Qianli Xun paused to take them in. Yinxing nudged her along: "If you like them, Miss, come look in daylight tomorrow; they shine even brighter under the sun. Better hurry to dine with Master and Madam before Master grows displeased."
Resuming her pace, Qianli Xun weighed her plan: act like the old Zhu Shuzhen before her father, or stay Qianli Xun? Better start in Zhu Shuzhen's mode, watch the room first, and adapt. She coached herself in silence: bow first, then stay quiet.
Soon they reached the main dining hall, where maids bustled indoors bearing platters. Halting at the threshold, Yinxing announced, "Master, Madam, Miss has arrived."
A man's voice answered from inside, cool and remote: "Let her in."
"Master says you may enter, Miss," Yinxing murmured.
Two steps in, Qianli Xun saw Kuqiao staying back and glanced over her shoulder. "Aren't you coming?"
"I'm not permitted inside, Miss. I'll wait right out here."
"Don't worry, Miss," Yinxing added. "I'll take Kuqiao to eat in the maids' quarters."
Qianli Xun sighed. "Fine. Looks like I'm walking in solo to meet my dad."
Stepping past the doorway, she met a massive standing screen blocking the interior. Pausing a beat, she reminded herself: Keep calm, mind manners, hold your tongue. A maid stepped out and murmured, "This way, Miss." Rounding the screen behind her, Qianli Xun took in the hall at a glance.
Straight ahead stood a wide couch with a low square table set across it. On one side sat Madam Xiang, greeting her daughter with a quiet smile and no words; across from her sat Zhu Shuzhen's father, absorbed in a volume without lifting his eyes once. Qianli Xun studied him: forties, neatly kept beard, bold brows, clear eyes, clean jawline, good complexion. He must have been quite handsome in his youth. So Zhu Shuzhen got her looks from her father, she mused.
Stepping up, she bowed. "Father. Mother."
Madam Xiang nodded without speaking. Without raising his gaze from the book, Zhu Xiyan ordered, "Sit."
Qianli Xun took a seat, chanting inside her head: Stay quiet, stay quiet. Minutes dragged in stiff silence while maids shuttled in and out laying dishes. All this spread for three people? Talk about wasteful!
Madam Xiang had not yet found an opening to brief her husband, never daring to speak out of turn before him. Watching Shuzhen walk in with poised grace and sit still without a peep, a relieved smile lit her face.
Then her daughter opened her mouth: "Father, Mother, food's all set! Let's go eat. Eating too late messes up your digestion and puts on weight..."
A single tear slid down Madam Xiang's cheek: this was no longer her old Shuzhen. She turned her face aside to blot it with her handkerchief. Startled, Zhu Xiyan raised his eyes and shot a sharp look at his daughter. A chill ran down Qianli Xun's spine under that stern glare; biting off the rest, she ducked her head and went mute again.
A maid stepped forward with a bow. "Master, Madam, Miss, dinner is served."
At last! Qianli Xun started up first, only to catch Madam Xiang shaking her head and waving her back down. Sitting again, she sneaked a look as Zhu Xiyan laid down his book, stepped off the couch, and walked past her. Madam Xiang rose too, took her daughter's hand, trailed behind Zhu Xiyan to the dining table, and guided Shuzhen into her proper seat.
Never in her life had Qianli Xun endured such a suffocating meal. Fed up with the chill, she ditched silence. The instant Zhu Xiyan said, "You may begin," she picked up a slice of duck and dropped it into Madam Xiang's bowl. "Mother, try this."
Madam Xiang shook her head again. Ignoring the hint, Qianli Xun lifted a second piece of duck toward Zhu Xiyan's bowl, starting "Father..." before pulling back at his thunderous scowl. "Don't want it? I'll eat it!" She popped it straight into her own mouth and chewed away.
A maid leaned down and whispered in her ear, "Miss, no talking at the table, and watch your posture."
Irritation simmered in Qianli Xun: Can't even eat in peace in my own home, rules for this and rules for that! Fine, I need a favor from him. Look at him though, a grown man nibbling without a sound, zero manly appetite! Glancing at Madam Xiang, she saw dainty chopsticks, tiny morsels, quiet chewing. Qianli Xun tried copying her, then almost laughed at herself; this wasn't her at all. Raised practicing Qianli Fist among martial artists, she had always eaten bold and hearty. Refusing to torture herself, she dropped the act and dug in with big bites.
Madam Xiang froze mid-bite in shock. This was not her refined Shuzhen; she ate like a country bumpkin! Tears welled again as she reached for her handkerchief, while serving maids traded stunned smirks.
Smack! Zhu Xiyan slammed his chopsticks down on the table, making everyone jump.
Mid-reach for a drumstick, Qianli Xun jerked her hand back, clapped a palm to her forehead, and put on a show. "Ow! My forehead wound hurts so bad!"
Alarmed, Madam Xiang cried, "Shuzhen, are you alright? Somebody fetch wound salve at once!" A maid rushed over and dabbed ointment onto her forehead. "Does it still hurt?" Madam Xiang asked in anxiety.
"Much better," Qianli Xun said, hoping the stunt might stir a father's pity or at least crack open the subject she needed.
To her surprise, it worked. Zhu Xiyan spoke at last, face still carved in stone. "I heard you want a mutual divorce from Lin Jue. Is that true?"
With the door cracked open, Qianli Xun pushed straight through. "Father, look at the cut on my forehead; Lin Jue gave me that! And you can tell I'm nothing like your old daughter because he beat her unconscious, and when she woke up she became me, with zero memory of the past! On top of that, he brings brothel courtesans right into the house! Isn't that outrageous? Keeping a man like that as your son-in-law shoves your daughter into a fiery pit! Father, please step forward and arrange a mutual divorce!"
"Are you still clinging to Young Master Xu?" Without even glancing at her, Zhu Xiyan spoke flat and cold. "I may as well tell you today: the Xu family moved north, and Young Master Xu married long ago. He already has a son and a daughter."
Recalling Kuqiao's story about that childhood sweetheart, a total stranger to Qianli Xun, she brushed it off. "Young Master who? Lin Jue beat the memory out of me; I don't remember anyone!"
"Better that you don't. Then settle down and live your married life, and forget this nonsense about mutual divorce."
Qianli Xun gaped. His own daughter came home battered, and he didn't even blink? What kind of father was this? "Father, he beats me and fools around with courtesans! How can I keep living with him?"
"Hmph! Think I'm in the dark? Lin Jue came to the prefecture office today, laid out the whole affair, and knelt to admit his fault. He injured you, yet you beat him just as hard! As for bringing a courtesan home, are you blameless? Had you consummated the marriage when you should have, none of this would have happened."
So Lin Jue had beaten her to the punch with his own twisted story! Fury boiled over as Qianli Xun shot to her feet. "Father, you can't listen only to his side of the story!"
"And I'm supposed to listen only to yours?"
Stung by his warped logic, Qianli Xun pressed harder. "Father, Lin Jue beat your original daughter to death! I'm a reborn Zhu Shuzhen borrowing her body, not your real daughter! I don't hold a single shred of her memories; haven't you noticed? On that alone, you should charge Lin Jue with murder and lock him up!"
Zhu Xiyan raised his eyes to study her: different in manner, yes, yet identical in face and frame. Dropping his gaze again, he scoffed, "Tales of rebirth are rustic superstition made up to fool simpletons. How could anyone take that as truth? If you aren't Shuzhen, who else could you be?"
Qianli Xun raised a clenched fist. "I know martial arts; did the old Zhu Shuzhen? I beat Lin Jue flat; the old Zhu Shuzhen could only take his fists! Do you still refuse to believe he killed her?"
Beside them, Madam Xiang burst into loud sobs. "Sob... Shuzhen! What a tragic death!" Stunned by the clash, the maids kept their heads down, not daring to whisper.
Zhu Xiyan spoke with calm, iron weight: "What are you crying for? Joining the uproar without knowing the facts!" Madam Xiang choked back her wails at once and dabbed her tears in silence.
Then Zhu Xiyan delivered the last words Qianli Xun ever expected: "Since you claim you are not my daughter Shuzhen, I have even less reason to step forward for your divorce. Leave; your presence spoils my meal."
