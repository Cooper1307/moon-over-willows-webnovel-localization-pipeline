#TITLE: Waiting Outside the Gate for His Beloved
Expecting Prince Yi to intervene, Qiao Shouren came prepared, stepping up with a deferential bow: "Your Highness Prince Yi, I'm here to bring Wenyuan back to Qiao Manor. Please do not stand in my way."
Shen Jianchen reasoned, "Of course I won't stop Grandfather from taking Wenyuan home, yet tying him up makes no sense. Wenyuan isn't some wild dandy like Young Master Chen—why treat him like a criminal? Untie him."
Qiao Shouren stood ironclad: "Prince Yi speaks sense, yet without ropes he won't come home quiet. I'm taking him back to lock him up until he willingly lets go of Miss Zhu."
Qiao Wenxian shouted, "Save me, Prince Yi! I don't want Father locking me up!"
Shen Jianchen tried again: "Grandfather can lock up his body, not his heart."
Qiao Shouren countered, "The heart sits inside the body—lock up the body long enough, and the heart gives in too."
Unable to watch Wenyuan suffer, Shen Jianchen pushed: "And what if I stop you by force?"
Seeing Prince Yi wouldn't back down, Qiao Shouren blamed every ounce of this family turmoil on Miss Zhu—if he didn't cut it off now, Wenyuan would sink beyond saving.
Drawing a dagger from his sleeve, Qiao Shouren pressed the blade to his own throat: "If Prince Yi stops me by force, today becomes the day I die!"
Never expecting Qiao Shouren to pull that stunt, Shen Jianchen felt equal parts alarm for his grandfather's life, pity for Wenyuan, and—truth be told—a handy excuse to step back.
"Why resort to something so extreme, Grandfather? I won't interfere."
Torn by anguish, Qiao Wenxian cried, "Don't push Prince Yi, Father—I'll go home with you."
Sheathing his dagger, Qiao Shouren bowed with full respect once more: "Forgive this old man, Prince Yi. I'll take Wenyuan away at once. As of today, I gift Youlin Retreat to Your Highness—Wenyuan won't set foot here again."
With that, Qiao Shouren led his men away, hauling Wenyuan off in haste.
Left standing in the empty yard, Shen Jianchen felt this tucked-away retreat grow lonelier than ever with Wenyuan gone—and the lonelier it felt, the stronger his longing burned to be beside the woman he loved.
Seeing His Highness stride toward Princess Jiahe's courtyard, Bai Feng trailed behind in silence, daring not say a word.
At the gate of her small courtyard, the wooden doors still stood bolted shut.
Standing on tiptoe to peer over the wall, Bai Feng saw the chamber door closed too, with not a maid in sight.
"Princess must still be asleep, Your Highness. Want to head back first?"
"No need. I'll wait outside a bit." Shen Jianchen figured Qianli Xun and Mengrong had stayed up talking past midnight and were sleeping late into the morning.
His mind drifted back before crossing over, to the morning he had hunted down Qianli Xun's apartment in the city, bought a bouquet of roses at dawn, drove his luxury car to her high-rise, and rode the elevator up to the twenty-second floor.
Finding her door, he knocked: *Knock, knock, knock!*
Dead silence inside. Puzzled—his intel swore she was home today—why wasn't anyone answering?
He knocked again—*knock, knock, knock!*—still nothing. Four or five rounds later, Qianli Xun's groggy, sleep-thick voice finally yelled through the door: "Who the hell is hammering like a ghost at crack of dawn?!"
Hearing her voice, Shen Jianchen called out in high spirits: "It's me!"
"What deity on earth disturbs my beauty sleep this early?!"
"Me! Shen Jianchen!"
"Scram! I was writing until dawn and I'm dead tired—quit bugging me!"
Then dead silence again.
Knowing she was home put him at ease. Setting the roses on the floor, he slumped down against the hallway wall, pulled out his phone, and played games while waiting for her to wake up.
He had cleared his whole work schedule today for chasing Qianli Xun—no leaving until he saw her in person.
Around noon, the faint shuffle of slippers sounded inside. Tucking his phone away, he scooped up the roses, smoothed his suit, cleared his throat, and knocked again: *Knock, knock, knock!*
The door swung open to reveal Qianli Xun with wild bedhead, glaring out of wide eyes crusted with eye boogers: "You're still here?!"
"Well? Pretty, right?" Shen Jianchen thrust the bouquet under her nose.
"So tacky! All you ever bring are roses!"
"Tacky? I don't think so. After waiting outside for hours, can't you reward my persistence and let me in instead of blocking the door?"
Qianli Xun swung the door wider. "Come in, put the flowers on the table, and never bring me flowers again—I hate cheesy stuff like that!"
Setting the bouquet down, Shen Jianchen glanced around the apartment: "Way too bare in here. One of these days I'll hire designers to renovate it for you."
"Are you nuts?! Renovating takes time, money, and weeks airing out formaldehyde—when am I supposed to live in it?!"
"Fine, I'll buy you a luxury turnkey condo—just bring your suitcase. Deal?"
"Think having a little cash makes you king, Shen Jianchen? I'm not broke—who asked you to buy me a place?!"
Shen Jianchen realized Qianli Xun was no ordinary girl money could win over.
"Go freshen up—I'm taking you out for a fancy feast."
"Fancy feast, my foot! Acting like I've never eaten decent food! Hate to break it to you, I despise fancy restaurants—save that routine!"
Facing the toughest challenge of his life, Shen Jianchen yielded: "Whatever you want to eat—your call."
Pleased with shutting him down and amused at his helpless look, Qianli Xun cracked a grin: "Haha, I don't feel like going out today. Since you suffered waiting outside so long, I'll cook you a bowl of instant noodles with a poached egg."
"Instant noodles? My specialty! Sit tight—I'll cook." Making her instant noodles with his own hands beat a five-star restaurant by a mile.
Qianli Xun agreed at once and went off to wash up in the bathroom, leaving him the kitchen.
Opening her fridge to find two cartons of milk, a few packs of instant noodles, greens, and eggs, he muttered, "What a Spartan life—get with me and I'll pack your fridge to bursting."
Grabbing the ingredients, he got cooking. By the time Qianli Xun came out fresh and tidy, two steaming bowls of noodles topped with poached eggs sat ready on the table.
Slurping away, Qianli Xun praised his noodles as even better than hers.
Watching the girl he loved eat with such relish, Shen Jianchen seized the moment to confess: "I'd love to cook instant noodles for you for the rest of our lives."
Qianli Xun laughed right back: "Haha, trying to make me die of boredom eating noodles?!"
Remembering that day curved Shen Jianchen's lips into a warm smile—the longest, happiest afternoon he had ever spent with Qianli Xun.
Watching his master beam at thin air baffled Bai Feng, though he dared not ask.
Tiptoeing to peek over the wall again, Bai Feng spotted Kuqiao stepping out of the maids' quarters and waved frantic hands in a stage whisper: "Kuqiao! Kuqiao! Over here! Look here!"
Catching his whisper, Kuqiao glanced around until she spotted Bai Feng's head bobbing above the wall.
Walking over, she unbolted the courtyard gate—finding Prince Yi standing right outside.
Kuqiao dropped into a bow at once: "Prince Yi! Princess and my mistress are still asleep—I'll go wake them."
"No need. I'm fine waiting right here." Shen Jianchen savored the wait; it brought back those bright days from before.
"Let me bring Prince Yi a chair—"
"No need, I'd rather stand. Go play with Bai Feng and leave me be."
"Thank you, Your Highness!" Needing no second invitation, Bai Feng grabbed Kuqiao's hand and vanished around the corner.
Shutting the gate himself, Shen Jianchen stood guard outside with a willing heart, hoping he would be the very first man Qianli Xun saw when she woke.
Stepping out of her own room, Xilan squinted up at the high sun—almost noon! Time to wake the princess.
With the princess and Youqi Jushi sharing one bed, waking one meant waking both; Xilan only cared about tending her own mistress anyway. Pushing open the maids' quarters door, she saw no Kuqiao, only Xiaguo dead to the world, snoring loud.
Thrilled yet stung after confessing last night, Xiaguo had lain awake half the night before crashing hard toward dawn.
Xilan called out, "Xiaguo, get up! Come help me wake the mistresses!"
Zero response—Xiaguo kept snoring away.
Reaching under the quilt, Xilan gave Xiaguo's arm a sharp pinch.
Jolting awake with a yelp, Xiaguo was about to curse when her blurry eyes made out Xilan—the princess's personal maid outranked her, so she swallowed her anger and sat up: "If Sister Xilan wants to wake me, no need to pinch—that hurt!"
"Called you five times and you didn't budge, and Kuqiao's nowhere to be found! Works like magic, doesn't it? Drag yourself out of bed—it's almost noon!"
Letting out a huge yawn, Xiaguo crawled out of bed and threw on her clothes with zero time to tidy up, hurrying off to serve her mistress—resenting her lot in life more than ever. When would she shed this maid's skin and become a mistress waited on by others?
