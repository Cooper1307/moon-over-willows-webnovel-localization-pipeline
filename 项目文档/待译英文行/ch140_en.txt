#TITLE: Cracking the Whip on Chen Haoyu
That day Chen Haoyu slipped out of Chen Manor solo without a single lackey, worried a crowd would attract attention now that Lin Jue was digging harder—better keep a low profile.
Halfway to a gambling den, a burlap sack dropped out of nowhere over his head, plunging him into pitch black as strong ropes bound him tight.
Chen Haoyu yelled, "Who the hell are you?! Ask around before tying up Grandpa Chen—my boys'll break your arms and legs!"
Then Lin Jue's voice answered cool: "Save your lungs, Young Master Chen. His Lordship the Prefect requests your presence."
Realizing his smear campaign had blown wide open, Chen Haoyu shut his mouth and let them hoist him onto the carriage, racking his brains how to wriggle past Zhu Xiyan without getting flayed.
Drawing a blank after a minute, he called out, "Lin Jue—you in here?"
"What if I am?"
"Pull this sack off my head! We know each other—what's the point keeping me blindfolded?"
Reaching over, Lin Jue yanked the sack off and tossed it aside.
Squinting until his eyes adjusted to the dim light, Chen Haoyu asked, "Where are you hauling me?"
"To see His Lordship the Prefect."
"What's he going to do to me?"
"No idea. First tell me why you smeared Zhu Shuzhen."
Chen Haoyu smirked: "Bet you've eaten plenty of dirt from her too, Lin Jue! Look at it this way—I avenged you! Shouldn't you be thanking me?"
Remembering the day and night Zhu Shuzhen had kept him trussed in ropes, Lin Jue muttered, "Sure, I got burned by her too—yet even I wouldn't sink as low as you, getting the whole city cursing her and wrecking her marriage so nobody wants her."
Chen Haoyu poked at him: "Funny—you sound like you're secretly enjoying it!"
"Me?! What makes you think that? If I were enjoying it, would I be hauling you to the Prefect?"
"Come on—stuck in officialdom chasing promotions, of course you jump when Zhu Xiyan snaps his fingers! Not me—I'm a free commoner with zero fear of Zhu Xiyan. Let's cut a deal: you love power, so you must love silver too. I'll pay you off—plus I avenged you against Zhu Shuzhen! Cash plus favors—how about letting me walk?"
"Hmph! Sure I love power and silver, and sure you gave Zhu Shuzhen a taste of hell—yet I'm still not letting you go! Because while smearing Zhu Shuzhen, you made a laughingstock out of ME too!"
Chen Haoyu blinked: "When did my rumors mock you?!"
"Claiming Zhu Shuzhen had affairs behind my back—isn't that mocking me as a spineless husband who couldn't control his wife?!"
Chen Haoyu chuckled: "Big tough guy with delicate feelings! Fair point—I was so busy sticking it to Zhu Shuzhen I forgot about you. My bad!"
"Hmph! What good's 'my bad'? Wait till you face the Prefect—you're in for a world of pain!"
For several days Zhu Xiyan had turned the Chen angle over with utmost care: not only were the two families in-laws, the Chens were Lin'an's wealthiest clan, controlling half the city's commerce—rattle the Chens and Lin'an's entire economy shook.
Everything about the Chen house was sterling except one rotten son—wrecking his own house was their problem, yet wrecking the Zhu house crossed every line. Both Chen Haoyu and the Chen clan had to pay!
Decision made, the instant Lin Jue sent word, Zhu Xiyan changed robes, grabbed a whip, left every servant behind without breathing a word to his family, and rode solo to the abandoned dark house to wait for Chen Haoyu.
Leaning against the wall stood a bulging burlap sack—looked stuffed with old straw.
Walking over to lay the sack flat on the floor, Zhu Xiyan felt its heft: grain, not straw! Odd to find a sack of grain left in a deserted shack.
Before he could wonder further, footsteps sounded outside. Zhu Xiyan stepped back into his chair and sat rigid, face dark with magisterial sternness.
The door swung open; Lin Jue shoved Chen Haoyu inside and closed it again.
Peering through the gloom, Chen Haoyu made out Zhu Xiyan sitting stone-faced in his chair.
Putting on a breezy front, Chen Haoyu said, "If Lord Prefect wanted to see me, just send my sister to fetch me from Chen Manor—why have Lin Jue haul me into a shack?"
Ignoring him, Zhu Xiyan turned to Lin Jue: "Go attend to your duties, Commandant Lin—I'll take it from here."
Reading between the lines that this was now private family business—and eager to go report the breakthrough to Prince Yi—Lin Jue bowed:
"I'll take my leave, Lord Prefect. Send word to Lin Manor anytime you need me." Bowing out, he shut the door behind him.
In that brief flash of daylight as the door opened, Chen Haoyu caught the volcanic rage on Zhu Xiyan's face and thought, "Cooked! Wonder what punishment he's cooking up..."
"Kneel!"
Soft-spoken, yet heavy as a gavel.
Guilty to the bone, Chen Haoyu dropped straight to his knees without a peep.
"Our two houses are in-laws on warm terms—why on earth would you sink to gutter tricks to ruin Shuzhen's honor and smear the Zhu name?!"
"Uncle Zhu!" Chen Haoyu turned on the charm fast. "I never meant to touch the Zhu family's name! Your daughter bullied me too hard, so I lost my head wanting to mess with her—didn't dream it'd blow up so huge! You know how rumors are—once loose, nobody can stop them..."
Rage boiling over, Zhu Xiyan shot up and cracked his whip straight across the burlap sack beside him: "So you KNEW rumors can't be stopped?! Then why pull a dirty stunt like that?! Begging for a beating, aren't you!"
Two more ferocious cracks lashed the grain sack.
Even untouched, Chen Haoyu flinched at the sheer whistling force of that whip—one hit on bare flesh would rip skin clean off!
"Blame Zhu Shuzhen for picking on me! If she hadn't bullied me, I never would've hit back! Want to whip someone, go home and whip your own daughter!"
Furious, Zhu Xiyan whipped the sack again until the burlap split open, spilling moldy grain across the dirt floor with a sharp musty stench.
Terror froze Chen Haoyu's tongue—shredding a heavy burlap sack like paper! One stroke on him would kill him dead! He clamped his mouth shut tight.
Winded from swinging, Zhu Xiyan sank back into his chair with a heavy sigh: "Whatever set you off, the damage is done. If you're a real man, think how you're going to fix the mess you made."
Baffled, Chen Haoyu ventured cautious: "What do you mean, Uncle Zhu?"
Lifting sharp eyes to him, Zhu Xiyan asked, "First tell me—do you want to clean up the mess your rumors brought on Shuzhen and the Zhu family?"
In truth, Chen Haoyu couldn't be happier watching Zhu Shuzhen crash and burn, and couldn't care less about the Zhu house—yet kneeling under a raised whip, he wasn't suicidal!
"Yes! Of course I want to fix it! Good news is nobody cares about the rumors anymore, so Shuzhen's and the Zhu family's trouble is already over! No need to stress, Uncle Zhu—let me off this once, and I swear I'll never pull a stunt like that again!"
"Hmph! Let you off? Dream on! Sure the gossip died down, yet you blew up Shuzhen's fine match with the Qiao family, forced me to cast her out alone to weep her eyes out, and left her with zero prospects for a decent marriage!" Two genuine tears rolled down Zhu Xiyan's cheeks.
Seizing the opening, Chen Haoyu offered smooth: "Easy fix, Uncle Zhu—hear my plan!"
Hope flickered in Zhu Xiyan: "What plan?"
"Bring Zhu Shuzhen back home—with family around she'll cheer right up! And with your clout as Prefect of Lin'an, landing your daughter a fine match won't be hard at all..."
That wasn't the line Zhu Xiyan wanted. Jumping up, he swung the whip toward Chen Haoyu, checked himself mid-air, and slashed the split grain sack again, sending another flood of moldy grain pouring across the floor.
Jolted half out of his skin—what had he said wrong?!—Chen Haoyu yelped:
"I'm genuinely repenting and offering ideas, Uncle Zhu—why swing the whip again?!"
Pointing the whip handle straight at Chen Haoyu's nose, Zhu Xiyan declared: "Since you wrecked Shuzhen's marriage, man up and MARRY HER YOURSELF! Restore both Shuzhen's and the Zhu family's honor!"
Over the past few days, Zhu Xiyan had cooked up this masterstroke: double the tie between in-laws, force Chen Haoyu to own his crime, AND secure a wealthy home for his daughter—three birds with one stone!
Hauling Chen Haoyu into this dark shack was pure shock-and-awe to scare him into agreeing first.
Chen Haoyu's eyes nearly popped out of his skull—what a sly old fox, trying to dump his unwanted daughter straight onto him! Not in a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand years!
"Don't flatter me, Uncle Zhu—I'm no real man! Can't possibly marry your daughter! Besides, Zhu Shuzhen would never say yes!"
Expecting refusal, Zhu Xiyan had his next move ready. Rolling up his right sleeve, whip gripped in his left hand, he seized Chen Haoyu by the scruff: "Whether you agree or not isn't your call! Come with me to face your parents right now and seal your betrothal to Shuzhen before your elders!"
Trussed helpless in ropes, Chen Haoyu wailed: "Uncle Zhu! Lord Prefect! Master Zhu! I admit I'm scum—beat me raw with that whip, lock me in Lin'an's dungeon for a year, anything—just don't force my parents to betroth me to Zhu Shuzhen! I'm begging you!"
Deaf to his pleas, Zhu Xiyan kicked the door open, hauled Chen Haoyu out, heaved him into the carriage with his driver's help, and barked: "To Chen Manor!"
Once the wheels rolled, Chen Haoyu fell silent. Looking at Zhu Xiyan's iron jaw, the old fox really meant to bully the Chens into a betrothal today! Regret bit hard—had he seen this coming, he'd never have paid a single copper for rumors!
With a few blocks left to Chen Manor, he racked his brains for any trick to stop Zhu Xiyan shoving his unwanted daughter down his throat.
