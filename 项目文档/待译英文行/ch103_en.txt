#TITLE: A Rendezvous Beneath the Millennial Divine Willow
Walking down the hillside path toward the Divine Willow, Qianli Xun felt a pang of guilt.
The Divine Willow stood not far from Youlin Retreat. Yet here she was, not sneaking out for a tryst with Wenyuan, but meeting Prince Yi in secret. If Wenyuan ever found out, he was sure to be upset.
And then there was Princess Jiahe, whom Qianli Xun already regarded as a close confidante. Devoted as the princess was to Prince Yi, how heartbroken would she be if she knew he had invited Qianli Xun out here?
The red silk ribbons were gone from the willow. Bare of ribbons, the tree reminded her all the more of her brawl with Shen Jianchen beneath it before crossing over.
Thinking of Shen Jianchen while gazing at the figure standing with his back to her under the willow left Qianli Xun in a trance. If not for the Song robes and Bai Feng standing beside him, she would have sworn Prince Yi was Shen Jianchen.
Bai Feng proved far livelier than his master, stepping forward with a bow. "Youqi Jushi, may I take Kuqiao off to play?"
Qianli Xun looked Bai Feng up and down. "If you ever dare let Kuqiao down, I won't spare you!"
"Put your mind at ease—I treat Kuqiao like gold! Just promise me you won't marry her off to anyone else."
Qianli Xun warned him, "Until I agree to let you marry Kuqiao, no holding hands and nothing closer—hear me? If word reaches my ears, I don't care who your master is, I'll still beat you up!"
"Mercy, I surrender!" Bai Feng stuck out his tongue and winked at Kuqiao, and the two headed off to sit on the stones by the pond and chat.
While Bai Feng spoke, Shen Jianchen had already turned around, drinking in the sight of the woman he loved, searching that familiar face for glimpses of the lively soul he knew.
Seeing Prince Yi stay silent and just flirt with his eyes, Qianli Xun frowned. "Here you go again, Prince Yi. Nobody else is around today, so let me give you a piece of my mind. I have someone I love, and you have someone too—why do you keep staring at me like that? Don't you think it's shameless?"
"Is that how you see me?"
Qianli Xun dodged his gaze. "Of course I do! Eating from your own bowl while eyeing the pot—that's pure greed. Since you asked me here today, let's put our cards on the table. I'm grateful for your help, sure, but one thing is separate from another. You can't expect me to ditch Wenyuan for you just because you did me a favor. I'm a one-man woman: I love one person for life and marry one person for life."
Every word struck straight at Shen Jianchen's heart. The truth rose to his lips, only for him to swallow it back down.
After a silence, he said, "Youqi Jushi misjudges me. I'm devoted to one person too. The pity is that I don't love the one who loves me, while the one I love is about to marry another man—and by my own hand. Can you understand that pain?"
Qianli Xun had to hand it to him: in two sentences he painted himself as the tragic victim.
"Did Prince Yi summon me here just to claim you're a faithful man? Haha, spin that tale to Princess Jiahe and she might buy it, but my sharp eyes see right through you—you're a fickle playboy."
Shen Jianchen stepped closer. "Does Youqi Jushi dare look me in the eye?"
"What's there to dare? I just can't be bothered staring at you..." Qianli Xun backed away.
Shen Jianchen baited her: "Don't tell me you've fallen for me and feel too guilty to admit it?"
"Me, fall for you? Hilarious! Look you in the eye? Fine—what are you going to do, eat me?" Qianli Xun locked eyes with Prince Yi—and a jolt like an electric shock shot through her whole body. Why had she never felt anything like this with Wenyuan?
"Qianli Xun, will you be with me?" Speaking through Zhao Pu's lips, Shen Jianchen voiced his heart once more.
Dazed all over again, Shen Jianchen's name flashed across her mind. Blinking into focus, all she saw was a fickle royal prince—and in a rush of panic, her fist flew out, landing smack on Prince Yi's cheek as she shouted, "Shameless!"
Hearing the shout, Kuqiao and Bai Feng shot up and came running.
Seeing the bruise blooming on his master's left cheek, Bai Feng cried, "Your Highness, are you all right?"
Seeing her mistress had punched a prince, Kuqiao dropped to her knees at once. "Your Highness, spare her!"
A wave of delayed dread hit Qianli Xun too. She had just punched an imperial prince—one who had helped her, no less! If he pressed the issue, Zhu Xiyan could be dragged down with her.
Yet Prince Yi spoke up: "Get up, go further off with Bai Feng, and pay us no mind. Youqi Jushi and I still have things to discuss."
Amazed at Prince Yi's magnanimity, Kuqiao sighed in relief for her mistress, rose, and walked with Bai Feng to the far side of the pond to sit on the rocks and talk love.
Qianli Xun said, "If Prince Yi hadn't said something so flippant, I wouldn't have hit you."
"Sooner or later you'll understand: I wasn't being flippant. Every word came from my heart."
"My advice is to treasure Princess Jiahe. The Qiao family will be coming to Zhu Manor to propose soon—I hope Prince Yi never breathes another word of what you said today."
"I know it's impossible now. I just wanted you to know my heart—to know someone loves you in silence..."
"What good does knowing do? It only brings headaches."
"Has Youqi Jushi remembered my question yet?"
"What question?"
Dejection crossed Shen Jianchen's face. "You never take my words to heart. Fine, I won't hold it against you. I'll ask again: after Youqi Jushi lost her memory, does the new soul inside her remember ever loving someone?"
Qianli Xun paused to think. Could Prince Yi have guessed she crossed over? Impossible—he didn't look like some prophet who saw past and future. Last time he had mentioned the theory that amnesia meant a new soul entering the body; who knew where he picked up such a plausible idea?
Settling her thoughts, Qianli Xun shot back, "Why does Prince Yi keep harping on that? Whoever I loved has nothing to do with you."
Stepping closer still, Shen Jianchen gathered his nerve. "I want to know who Qianli Xun ever loved."
Dazed once more by a wave of uncanny familiarity, she breathed out three syllables without meaning to: "Shen... Jian... chen?"
A satisfied smile lit Shen Jianchen's face. "Is he the one you used to love?"
Trapped in that haze of familiarity, Qianli Xun searched Prince Yi's features for traces of Shen Jianchen, half deaf to his question.
Shen Jianchen went on: "No need to look at me like that. I am—"
Before he could finish, Qiao Mengrong's voice cut across the clearing: "Why is Brother here with Youqi Jushi?"
Qianli Xun snapped wide awake in an instant. Missing Shen Jianchen so much while standing beneath the Divine Willow must have made her hallucinate and mistake Prince Yi for him!
How on earth could Prince Yi be Shen Jianchen? Shen Jianchen's cocky swagger didn't fit a noble imperial prince at all. Blame Prince Yi for being such a smooth seducer that even her own self-control had wobbled today.
With the moment shattered by Qiao Mengrong, Shen Jianchen had no right to complain and turned around. "Mengrong, what brings you here?"
Spotting the bruise on his cheek, Qiao Mengrong gasped, "What happened to Brother's face? Does it hurt?"
"It's nothing. I bumped into a tree branch walking through the woods." Shen Jianchen tossed off an excuse.
Qiao Mengrong sensed at once that her arrival was unwelcome. "Doesn't Brother want to see me?"
Shen Jianchen did not know what to say; he hated lying to her.
When he offered no denial, tears welled in Qiao Mengrong's eyes. "Brother really didn't want to see me?"
Seeing the impasse, Qianli Xun stepped in and bowed to Princess Jiahe. "Please don't misunderstand Prince Yi, Princess. I came to the Divine Willow today to check on my red ribbon before heading to Youlin Retreat to see Wenyuan, only to find every ribbon gone! Just as I was about to go ask the nunnery what happened, Prince Yi happened to walk up. Since we know each other, we chatted a bit, and Prince Yi was just talking about you..."
Qiao Mengrong brightened in an instant. "Really?"
Qianli Xun kept spinning: "Of course! Prince Yi said the greatest fortune of his life was meeting Princess Jiahe, and he even invited me to your wedding."
Shen Jianchen thought, "Crossing over sure sharpened your lying skills. Do you really think fooling Mengrong is for her own good?"
Qiao Mengrong stepped up beside him, a single tear rolling from the corner of her eye. "Brother, do you really think meeting me is the greatest fortune of your life?"
Still silent, Shen Jianchen pulled out a handkerchief and wiped the tear from her cheek. Qiao Mengrong's arrival reminded him yet again that he had no right to love Qianli Xun.
Finishing, he glanced up at the rising moon and realized dusk was closing in.
"Is Youqi Jushi still going to see Wenyuan?"
Without missing a beat, Qianli Xun answered, "I'd better head back to Zhu Manor."
Qiao Mengrong blinked. "Didn't you plan to see Young Master Qiao? Why not go?"
With Prince Yi wrecking her mood, Qianli Xun feared Wenyuan would notice something off. "I did mean to see Wenyuan, then remembered my father wants to talk to me tonight. I don't want to get back late and upset him."
"As Youqi Jushi wishes." With that, Shen Jianchen took Qiao Mengrong's hand and walked away.
Having parted from Bai Feng, Kuqiao walked back from the far side of the pond.
Watching Prince Yi and Princess Jiahe disappear down the path, Qianli Xun sighed. "Cherish what you have in front of you! Stop stirring up trouble when you're bored! I swear I will never meet you alone again—mark my words!"
