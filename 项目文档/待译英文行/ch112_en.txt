#TITLE: Charging Lin Jue and Carrying Word
Leaving Wangyue Pavilion, Shen Jianchen did not head straight back to Youlin Retreat; he told Bai Feng to drive to Lin Manor.
All the way there, those rumors nagged at him. Spreading across the whole city like wildfire meant someone was pulling strings behind the scenes.
Who in Lin'an had so much time on their hands as to drag Youqi Jushi through the mud? Most likely an enemy of the Zhu family who hated seeing them thrive.
Shen Jianchen recalled Qianli Xun's expression just now. Even wearing Zhu Shuzhen's face, he could feel how heavy her heart was.
How could anyone stay cheerful after a blow like that?
When Qianli Xun hurt, how could Shen Jianchen rest easy? He was going to stamp out these rumors across Lin'an for her.
Outside Lin Manor, Bai Feng stated their purpose to a gatekeeper, who looked awkward: "You picked a bad time—Third Master isn't home at this hour."
Bai Feng asked, "Where did he go?"
The servant didn't even blink: "Chunxiang Brothel."
Shen Jianchen directed them straight to Chunxiang Brothel.
Even after nightfall, Lin'an stayed lively—no quieter than the capital.
Thinking about it, since crossing over, he hadn't even toured Bianliang's night markets before rushing down to Lin'an. He had now spent longer in Lin'an than in Bianliang—proof fate still tied him to Qianli Xun. Wasn't the Qiao proposal collapsing proof too?
No sooner did that spark of cheer rise than Qiao Mengrong's voice calling "Brother" echoed in his ears.
Shen Jianchen grumbled at Zhao Pu in his head: "Zhao Pu, oh Zhao Pu, why did you have to check out the exact second I crossed over, dumping me in your body to babysit your little fiancée?"
He let out a sigh. "Ah, stop overthinking—we'll cross that bridge when we come to it."
Hearing his master speak without catching the words, Bai Feng asked, "Did Your Highness say something? I didn't catch it."
"Nothing. Between Bianliang and Lin'an, which has livelier nights?"
"Didn't Your Highness ever stroll Bianliang's streets at night?"
"I was sick the whole time—where would I get the energy or mood to go out? And the second I recovered, I came down to Lin'an before getting a night out, so I wouldn't know." Pausing, he wondered why he was explaining so much to Bai Feng. "Why all the questions? Answer what I ask and quit prying!"
"Yes, yes, I won't pry into Your Highness's business." Driving on, Bai Feng puffed out his chest: "Bianliang is the capital, so of course it's grander—nights are livelier than days! Lin'an can't hold a candle to it! Once Your Highness returns to the capital, I'll take you out at night to Fanlou Tower and the Goulan Wasi—the food and entertainment blow Lin'an out of the water!"
Hearing the hometown pride of an ordinary Bianliang lad, Shen Jianchen scoffed, "Sounds like common folk are mighty happy with life these days—just because the eating and playing are good?"
"Well, there's plenty they're unhappy about too."
"Go on—tell me what they're unhappy about."
"If I say it, Your Highness can't punish me."
"Speak your mind."
"Bianliang looks dazzling, sure, yet common folk are still dirt poor. All that glitter belongs to the rich, nothing to do with little folk like us. If common folk were happy with life, nobody would be rising in rebellion."
"Well said! For that remark alone, once we're back in Bianliang I'm treating you to a feast at Fanlou Tower!"
"Thank you, Your Highness!" Thrilled yet baffled, Bai Feng wondered if His Highness was really the Emperor's son—he had just criticized flaws in His Majesty's realm, and His Highness loved it! What an unusual prince.
Chatting along, they pulled up outside Chunxiang Brothel.
Unwilling to step inside, Shen Jianchen stayed in the carriage and sent Bai Feng in to summon Lin Jue.
It was Bai Feng's first time stepping into a house of pleasure, leaving him equal parts curious and nervous.
No sooner had he crossed the threshold than the madam swept up. "Never seen this young master before—stranger the first time, regular the second! Every girl in my house is top-notch. What type strikes your fancy?"
Uncomfortable, Bai Feng stepped back: "I have someone I like—I'm not here for girls. I'm looking for Lin Jue."
"A faithful lad, eh?" The madam pointed across the room. "There—Commandant Lin is right over there."
Chun Jinqiao sat pressed up beside Lin Jue, watching dancers on stage.
Awkwardness pricked Bai Feng. "No wonder Youqi Jushi divorced Lin Jue—looks like he's a fixture at Chunxiang Brothel."
Bai Feng stepped over to Lin Jue. "Commandant Lin, Prince Yi requests your presence."
Absorbed in his pleasure, Lin Jue shot up at the mention of Prince Yi, recognizing the prince's personal bodyguard.
"Where is Prince Yi?"
"Waiting in his carriage outside."
Ditching Chun Jinqiao on the spot, Lin Jue hurried out of Chunxiang Brothel.
A courtesan stepped into Bai Feng's path: "Stay and have a little fun with me, handsome?"
"Get off, get off—I'm on duty!" Pushing past her, Bai Feng headed out too.
The courtesan huffed, "What a prude! Why step into Chunxiang Brothel if you're going to act saintly?"
Chun Jinqiao chuckled, "Pick your targets better, sister. One look tells you that kid's a first-timer—pouncing on him like that was bound to scare him off!"
"Easy for Sister to say, hogging Commandant Lin so the rest of us don't get a look-in! Hmph!" The second fresh customers walked in, her pout flipped straight into a beaming welcome.
Spotting the carriage parked on the street, Lin Jue stepped up and bowed. "Greetings, Prince Yi."
"Climb inside. I have a word for you."
Lin Jue climbed aboard and sat across from Prince Yi, tense lest the prince scold him for visiting a brothel.
"I came to ask a favor."
Relieved Prince Yi skipped mention of Chunxiang Brothel, Lin Jue exhaled. "Name it, Prince Yi—anything you ask."
Shen Jianchen said, "The whole city is slandering Youqi Jushi, and something smells off about it. I want you to investigate the source of these rumors on the quiet and bring whoever started them straight to me."
Prince Yi had come over this too! Entrusted in a single day by the two highest-ranking men in Lin'an filled Lin Jue with pride.
Still, he wondered: Zhu Xiyan was acting for the Zhu family, so why did Prince Yi care? Probably on Qiao Wenxian's behalf—everyone of standing in Lin'an knew how tight those two were.
"Rest easy, Prince Yi. His Lordship the Prefect came to me today too, and I've already sent men out. We'll drag the mastermind into the light."
"Good. Go back to your evening, Commandant Lin."
Embarrassed by the parting remark, Lin Jue bowed: "I take my leave."
Once Lin Jue exited, Bai Feng didn't need to ask—he drove straight back to Youlin Retreat.
Outside Youlin Retreat's gate, Qiao Wenxian stood craning his neck with Nan Shuang beside him.
Butler Li had urged his young master indoors several times to no avail before giving up, ordering Nan Shuang to bring out a thick cape against the autumn night chill, then heading off to bed himself.
Just as Nan Shuang draped the cape over his master's shoulders, the creak of carriage wheels sounded down the road.
Qiao Wenxian bolted out into the lane, forcing Bai Feng to haul the horse to a stop.
Before Prince Yi could step down, Qiao Wenxian scrambled into the carriage and clutched his arm. "Did my father agree?!"
Shen Jianchen wouldn't lie to him: "Grandfather threatened his own life. There was nothing I could do."
Strength drained from Qiao Wenxian's hands. His eyes turned red and his voice shook: "Everything was going fine—why did it turn out like this?! Tell me, Prince Yi... tell me this is all a bad dream!"
Ignoring the plea, Shen Jianchen said, "Don't worry—I'm going to track down whoever started these rumors and haul him right in front of Grandfather. Maybe then he'll change his mind."
Hope flared again, and Qiao Wenxian threw his arms around Prince Yi. "You really are my best brother..."
Unable to watch Wenyuan suffer, Shen Jianchen added a bright note: "Here's some good news—I found Youqi Jushi. She moved to the east side of town, a place called Wangyue Pavilion, and asked me to tell you to meet her beneath the Divine Willow tomorrow."
The best news since the proposal collapsed! Qiao Wenxian hugged him even tighter. "Back when you were sick, I never knew how much you'd mean to me; who knew once you recovered, you'd become my lifeline? I can never repay this debt—I'll serve you like an ox or a horse in the next life!"
Wenyuan's gratitude stabbed Shen Jianchen with guilt, knowing his own motives weren't pure. "Cut the melodrama—you don't owe me anything. Since when do we stand on ceremony? Keep thanking me and I won't help next time."
A smile broke across Qiao Wenxian's face at last, and he let go. "Princess Jiahe came looking for you several times—go see her."
That familiar leaden weight dropped back onto Shen Jianchen's chest. Without another word, he stepped out of the carriage and walked into the retreat—toward the duty he could not escape.
