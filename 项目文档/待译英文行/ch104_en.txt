#TITLE: A Sudden Turn of Fortune
Time flew past, and over a month slipped by. The air grew brisk, and women across Lin'an changed into autumn attire, Qianli Xun included.
For over a month, her days had rolled along smooth as silk: when not with her family, she was out on dates with Wenyuan or attending poetry salons with him.
Since that meeting beneath the Divine Willow, she had not seen Prince Yi once. He seemed to be avoiding her on purpose, which suited Qianli Xun just fine.
That was more like it! Her face-to-face lecture must have worked wonders. Turning a royal prince around—Qianli Xun felt pretty impressed with herself.
Even better, she had not run into Chen Haoyu once at Zhu Manor all month. She had visited her sister-in-law several times without spotting him, nor had she crossed paths with him on the streets; he seemed to have vanished from her life overnight. Qianli Xun thanked her lucky stars—Heaven must be skimming the trash out of her life.
Yet when days went too smooth, unease crept in, and she kept waiting for the other shoe to drop.
With Mid-Autumn Festival nearing, Qiao Shouren made up his mind at last to propose at Zhu Manor. He spent five days preparing betrothal gifts, instructed the matchmaker, fixed a date, and sent an invitation card over to Zhu Manor.
Seeing the card, Zhu Xiyan grinned from ear to ear.
When the appointed day arrived, the Zhu Manor servants scrubbed the estate spotless from front to back, and Zhu Xiyan invited his clansmen over. Yet they waited and waited until noon, with no sign of the Qiao family.
Madam Xiang fretted: "Husband, shouldn't we send someone to Qiao Manor to check what happened?"
Zhu Xiyan nodded and dispatched a servant to inquire.
No sooner had the servant stepped out the side gate of Zhu Manor than he ran into the Qiao family's matchmaker. Grabbing her, he asked, "The Qiao family promised to come propose today—why aren't they here yet? The whole house is waiting on pins and needles!"
The matchmaker said, "What proposal? Since you're here, you'll save me a trip facing the Prefect. Pass on word to His Lordship: Miss Zhu's reputation outside is ruined, so the Qiao family won't be coming to propose anymore."
The servant seized her arm. "You matchmaker—how dare you slander our Young Lady! I'm dragging you straight to my master!"
Wrenching free, the matchmaker bolted, leaving the servant no choice but to return and report the truth.
Zhu Xiyan slammed the table in fury: "The Qiao family has gone too far! They asked to propose, picked the date, stood us up, and cooked up this excuse! Don't think being imperial relatives makes them gods—I'm the Prefect of Lin'an, and my word still rules this city!"
Madam Xiang sat weeping beside him. How had such a happy day fallen apart in an instant?
Matron Wei urged, "Don't lose your temper yet, Master—wait until we get to the bottom of this. Even if the Qiao family did hear wild gossip and backed out, it only proves they're shortsighted and gullible, ready to swallow any rumor. I wouldn't feel safe letting our Young Lady marry into a house like that anyway."
Just then Zhu Jingyuan walked in from outside, stepped up to his father, and whispered into his ear.
Zhu Xiyan's face went dark as thunder. "Go tell the clansmen to head home."
Glancing at Madam Xiang dabbing her eyes, Zhu Jingyuan shook his head and went out to handle it.
Zhu Xiyan snapped, "What are you crying for? Look at the fine daughter you raised!"
Madam Xiang sobbed all the harder: "Waaah! Why is my Shuzhen's fate so bitter! A mutual divorce from the Lin family, and now treated like this by the Qiaos! Waaah!"
Matron Wei steadied her. "Don't cry, Madam. Master knows what to do—let me help you back to your room."
Word that the Qiao family had canceled the proposal spread across Zhu Manor in no time, and Kuqiao rushed in to tell her mistress.
Qianli Xun's brush slipped from her fingers onto the paper. "Why hasn't the Qiao family come yet?"
"Servants are saying it's because Miss's reputation outside is ruined." Outrage burned on Kuqiao's face. "Pure nonsense! Miss is beautiful and kind—the Qiao family must just be making up excuses because they didn't want to propose!"
Hearing the Qiao family would not propose, Xiaguo wept harder than her mistress, slumping over the tea table: "Waaah! Does this mean Miss can't marry into the Qiao family anymore?! Waaah!"
With no energy to mind Xiaguo, Qianli Xun racked her brains over how her reputation had been ruined. Could it be that line, "falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed"?
"Kuqiao, go fetch Eldest Brother. He's bound to know the full story."
After Kuqiao left, Xiaguo kept sobbing. Qianli Xun said, "Who knew you'd take it harder than me? Fine—cry all the tears I'm supposed to cry so I can think straight about what to do next."
Xiaguo still would not stop, fraying Qianli Xun's nerves. "Go back to your room to cry. Eldest Brother will be here in a minute to talk to me."
Still sobbing, Xiaguo rose, returned to the maids' quarters, flung herself onto her bed, and wept even harder: "If Miss can't marry Young Master Qiao, what am I going to do?! Waaah!"
Before long, Zhu Jingyuan entered and told Kuqiao to shut the door.
Her brother's grim manner tightened the knot in Qianli Xun's chest. "Brother, what happened?"
"Don't take it too hard when you hear this, Sister. It looks like things between you and Qiao Wenxian are over." Zhu Jingyuan tried to tread with care, yet the truth hurt no matter how gentle he made it.
"What... what did the Qiao family hear... to call off the proposal out of nowhere?" For the first time, Qianli Xun heard her own voice tremble.
"Did you write a certain line of ci?"
Just as she feared. Qianli Xun nodded, steadying herself. "I did, back when Wenyuan and I were together. I wrote it for him."
"Nobody cares about that. Everyone is condemning you for lacking wifely virtue and corrupting morals—even calling you unchaste..."
"Does Brother think I'm to blame too?"
"My opinion doesn't matter anymore. The point is everyone thinks so. Stop writing poetry and ci from now on, Sister, or you'll grow old alone inside Zhu Manor."
Qianli Xun remembered how the historical Zhu Shuzhen had died of grief inside Zhu Manor. Was her life doomed to the same end? Never! She pushed herself to her feet. "Don't worry, Brother. I'll never let myself die an old maid inside Zhu Manor."
With that, she started for the door.
"Where are you going?"
She did not even know—she just needed out of Zhu Manor. "For a walk."
"Don't go out! The streets are overflowing with nasty rumors about you."
"Rumors?" Holding back the tears pressing at her eyes, Qianli Xun spun around. "One line of ci wasn't enough—there are rumors too?"
"Yes."
"Tell me what they're saying."
"Better not. Stay indoors and don't go anywhere. Father is furious right now—don't cross his path either."
"If Brother won't tell me, I'll go out and hear for myself."
"Fine, I'll tell you! Folk all over town are saying you're a tigress, that you had affairs while married to the Lin family and traded secret glances with Lin'an's scholars, and that you deserve to be pointed at by a thousand fingers and spat on by ten thousand..."
Fury exploded in Qianli Xun. She slammed her fist down on the chair beside her; the wood cracked clean through, scraping her knuckles raw.
Kuqiao rushed forward and grabbed her hand. "Miss, are you hurt?!"
"See what I mean? You're this furious indoors—step outside and you'll punch the first person you see, proving the 'tigress' rumors right! Listen to Brother: stay put and don't go anywhere. Father and I will handle this." Trying to comfort her, Zhu Jingyuan drew a bottle of salve from his sleeve. "I happened to buy medicine. Use this first."
Kuqiao took the bottle, and once Eldest Master left, sat her mistress down and applied the salve.
Seeing her mistress neither weep nor rage frightened Kuqiao even more, and the more scared she got, the faster she talked.
"Don't worry, Miss. Rumors come fast and fade fast. Young Master Qiao's heart belongs to you—if the Qiao family didn't come today, they'll come another day. I'll stay right here with you and wait."
When her mistress stayed silent, Kuqiao rattled on: "Back in our village, folks once spread rumors I wasn't my mother's real child, claiming she picked me up beside a ditch. My family paid it no mind, and after a while everyone got bored and forgot. I swear, within half a month nobody will breathe another word—"
Before Kuqiao could finish, a throng of servants burst through the door, sweeping manuscripts off the desk and poems and paintings out of wooden boxes, ransacking chests and drawers and hauling out every sheet of paper with ink on it into the yard.
Frozen in shock, Qianli Xun stood watching them like a band of looters stripping away every poem and painting of Zhu Shuzhen's.
Kuqiao barked, "Have you all lost your minds?! How dare you ransack Miss's room!"
One servant said, "Forgive us, Miss. Master ordered us to haul every single one of your manuscripts out into the yard and burn them."
"Burn them?" Qianli Xun repeated the words under her breath. In her memory, Zhu Shuzhen's manuscripts were burned only after she died of heartbreak past the age of forty.
Yet here she was, crossed over at just twenty, watching it happen right now. Could today be the turning point where Zhu Shuzhen's fate ended and Qianli Xun's true fate began?
