#TITLE: Stepping Up for Wenyuan Once More
Unable to stomach another word, Qiao Wenxian stormed up, face dark with rage, and shouted, "Every word out of your mouths is garbage! Shuzhen isn't like that! Say one more word and I'll flip your vegetable stall!"
Terrified, the vendor shrank back: "No more! I won't say another word! Don't overturn my stall, Young Master!"
Clutching her basket, the buyer fled, muttering, "Where did this lunatic pop out from?"
Unfazed, the old bachelor sneered, "Looks like Young Master knows Zhu Shuzhen! Did she toss secret glances at you too? Fall asleep in your arms too?"
Qiao Wenxian seized the old bachelor by the collar. "Say one more word and I'll rip your mouth apart!"
Spittle flying, the old bachelor barked back, "What's the use bullying me? Every soul on this street is saying the same thing—go listen for yourself if you don't believe me!"
Qiao Wenxian whipped his head around in disgust. People on all sides were pointing at him, whispering behind their hands...
He could punch one man and curse a dozen, yet he could never silence a whole city's tongues. Heartbreak drained the strength from his fingers, and the old bachelor slipped free and ran.
Qiao Wenxian stumbled down the street, wilted like a frostbitten eggplant.
Were he and Shuzhen never going to be together? Would all his dreams of growing old beside her remain nothing but empty dreams?
He decided to go find Shuzhen—she had to be suffering inside Zhu Manor.
Nan Shuang urged him to hire a carriage, yet Qiao Wenxian refused; only walking until his legs gave out could numb the pain in his chest.
When he reached Zhu Manor, a gatekeeper informed him, "Young Master Qiao came too late. Master has already driven our Young Lady out of the house."
"Where did she go?"
The servant shook his head. "No idea."
Grief crashed over Qiao Wenxian. Cast out by her own family, how would Shuzhen get by? He could picture her walking out of Zhu Manor with tears streaming down her cheeks like rain on pear blossoms.
The more he imagined it, the sharper his chest ached. With her cast out and no elders to arrange their marriage, how could they ever be wed with honor?
Qiao Wenxian meant to go see the Prefect and swear his resolve to marry Shuzhen.
The gatekeeper blocked his path: "Master's orders—no one from the Qiao family is allowed inside."
Qiao Wenxian backed away with heavy steps. What could he do now? Only one person came to mind: Prince Yi. He had to beg him for help again.
He could not walk all the way back to Youlin Retreat on foot. With no Qiao carriage nearby, Nan Shuang hired a carriage on the street to carry them beyond the city gates.
All the way out, his master said not a single word.
Nan Shuang felt heartbroken for his master and Youqi Jushi too. He could not fathom why everyone was talking about her—and saying such ugly things.
"Young Master, could someone have spread those rumors on purpose to wreck the proposal?"
His master stayed silent, deaf to the world around him.
Nan Shuang kept guessing: "My bet is it's some girl who likes you, Young Master. If you proposed to Youqi Jushi, she'd lose her shot, so she cooked up this dirty trick."
Qiao Wenxian still did not answer. Whoever slandered Shuzhen didn't matter to him right now—all he cared about was what the two of them would do next.
"Blame it on you being too outstanding, Young Master, and the Qiao family being imperial kin—so many ladies have their eyes on you. Cheer up, this will blow over." Nan Shuang kept trying to comfort him as he speculated.
...
Ever since meeting Qianli Xun beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen had not stepped out of Youlin Retreat, keeping his walks to the grounds nearby, going no further than the willow.
Today was the Qiao family's proposal day, and Shen Jianchen discovered that hiding from Qianli Xun did nothing to help him forget her. With her marriage to Wenyuan about to become fact, the ache inside him only deepened.
Since Wenyuan left at dawn, Shen Jianchen had holed up in his room. At noon Bai Feng laid out lunch, yet he had zero appetite and never lifted his chopsticks, leaving Bai Feng to carry the dishes back untouched.
Qiao Mengrong stopped by several times, only to be turned away at the door. He could not bear letting her see him moping over another woman, and feared seeing her would push harsh words out of his mouth. He only claimed he was busy thinking through Lin'an's military and civil affairs and wanted no interruptions.
Just as Shen Jianchen sat nursing his own misery, the door flew open. Wenyuan stumbled in, dropped to his knees before him, and begged in a choked voice: "Prince Yi, help me—only you can help me..."
Startled, Shen Jianchen asked, "What happened? Isn't today the proposal?"
Wenyuan was too choked with grief to get the words out.
Nan Shuang spoke up for his master: "Prince Yi doesn't know—the streets are flooded with filthy rumors about Youqi Jushi! When Master found out, he called off the proposal to the Zhu family, and the Prefect even threw Youqi Jushi out of the house—nobody knows where she went!"
"What?!" Realization snapped Shen Jianchen straight to attention, fear for Qianli Xun surging through him. "Something like that happened?"
Weeping, Wenyuan cried, "Help me, Prince Yi! If I can't be with Shuzhen, what's the point of living?!"
Shen Jianchen stepped forward and hauled Wenyuan to his feet. "Pull yourself together. Stay here at the retreat while I find out what's going on and decide what to do."
Qiao Wenxian wrapped his arms around Prince Yi. "You have to persuade my father again! He only listens to you!"
Shen Jianchen reassured him: "I'm heading to Qiao Manor to see Grandfather right now."
Qiao Wenxian hugged him tighter. "You're the finest prince under heaven! Once this works out, I'll hang an inscribed plaque in your honor!"
"All right, let go of me—how am I supposed to get to Qiao Manor with you clinging to me?" Shen Jianchen thought something had to be twisted in his own head: half of him wanted to help Wenyuan, while the other half felt glad the proposal had fallen through.
Qiao Wenxian released him and wiped the tear streaks from his cheeks.
Shen Jianchen ordered, "Nan Shuang, take your master to rest, and go fetch Bai Feng."
Standing right outside the door, Bai Feng stepped in at once. "No need to fetch me, Your Highness—I've been waiting right outside."
"Hitch the carriage. We're going to Qiao Manor."
"Awesome! Your Highness is leaving the house at last! I get to step out of Youlin Retreat too!"
"Less chatter—get moving!"
Watching Prince Yi leave with Bai Feng, hope flickered back to life in Qiao Wenxian's eyes.
On the road to Qiao Manor, Bai Feng asked from the driver's box, "Your Highness, I haven't seen Kuqiao in over a month. Can I see her today?"
Not just Bai Feng—Shen Jianchen hadn't seen Qianli Xun in over a month either. With all this crashing down on her, how was she holding up? Nan Shuang said she'd been thrown out of Zhu Manor; where had she gone?
"Sure. Once we find Youqi Jushi, you'll see Kuqiao."
"Find her? Did Youqi Jushi go missing?"
"Stop asking questions and drive faster to Qiao Manor."
Bai Feng stuck out his tongue. Here was a prince who never visited the prefecture office and cared nothing for government business, yet threw himself into Young Master Qiao's romance—what an odd prince.
Once inside the city gates, Shen Jianchen went straight to Qiao Manor.
Hearing Prince Yi had arrived, Qiao Shouren led Madam Qian and Concubine Mi out to welcome him.
Shown into the main hall, Qiao Shouren beat Prince Yi to the punch: "Did Prince Yi come over the proposal?"
Shen Jianchen nodded. "Wenyuan told me it fell through. I came to ask Grandfather—are you calling off the proposal to Zhu Manor over nothing more than street rumors about Miss Zhu?"
Qiao Shouren answered, "Precisely. Last time Prince Yi talked me round, and the Qiao family agreed to the match with all our hearts. Who knew folk outside were spreading such foul talk about Miss Zhu? How could the Qiao house take in a bride of such disrepute?"
"Has Grandfather considered they're just rumors?"
"Of course I have—yet flies don't buzz around a seamless egg! Why didn't rumors target anyone else, only Miss Zhu? Clearly she brought it on herself."
Shen Jianchen heard the iron resolve in his grandfather's tone. To test how firm he was, he probed: "Wenyuan has eyes only for Youqi Jushi. He even said life has no meaning without her."
Concubine Mi panicked: "Master, if Wenyuan is talking like that, what do we do?!"
Qiao Shouren barked at her: "Know your place! When I'm speaking with Prince Yi, who gave you leave to butt in? Withdraw at once!"
Displeasure flashing across her face, Concubine Mi bowed to Prince Yi and left with her maid.
Only then did Qiao Shouren say, "Don't take Wenyuan's words to heart, Prince Yi. He's young and bound to talk like a lovesick fool; once he marries, he'll get over it."
Shen Jianchen pushed further: "What if I went to His Majesty and asked for an imperial marriage decree for Wenyuan..."
Before he could finish, Qiao Shouren shot up and dropped to his knees, with Madam Qian kneeling beside him.
"If Prince Yi asks for an imperial decree, I'll smash my head open right in front of you! Even at the cost of this old life, I will never let Miss Zhu step across the Qiao threshold!"
Shen Jianchen rose and let out a sigh. "Get up. This is Qiao family business—I won't meddle in it again."
Exhaling in relief, Qiao Shouren and Madam Qian climbed to their feet and called out to Prince Yi's retreating back in unison: "We humbly see Prince Yi off!"
