#TITLE: Returning to Chen Manor to See the Family
With the whole city gossiping about Shuzhen and dragging the Zhu family down too, her father-in-law Zhu Xiyan stayed in a black mood, smothering Zhu Manor in gloom. Afraid of crossing her father-in-law or making her mother-in-law cry more, Chen Wenxin decided to go stay at her maiden home for a few days.
With Zhu Jingyuan's blessing, she bade her in-laws farewell, claiming her grandmother was getting on in years, missed Laibao, and had asked her back for a short visit.
Neither Zhu Xiyan nor Madam Xiang had reason to refuse, only asking her to pass their regards to the Chen elders.
Stepping into the room to find her brother standing at attention, Chen Wenxin guessed Father was lecturing him and teased, "Well, well! Look who's behaving at home today! Did you make Father mad again?"
Chen Haoyu grumbled, "What's with that tone, Sister? Acting like you never see me! Even when I don't come back to Chen Manor, I stop by Zhu Manor all the time to see you, Brother-in-law, and Laibao."
"Fair enough." Stepping beside her father, Chen Wenxin said, "Don't waste anger on him, Father—it's rare to have the whole family together."
Seeing his daughter lit up Chen Jianglin's face: "For your sake, I'll let the brat off today and settle accounts another time."
Yao Qingyan beckoned the maid to bring Laibao over to her lap.
Charmed by their adorable grandson, both grandparents forgot all about Chen Haoyu.
Delighted his sister had saved him a trip to Zhu Manor, Chen Haoyu tugged Chen Wenxin's sleeve: "Come outside a sec, Sister—got something to ask you."
Chen Wenxin followed him out, with Qinggua trotting right behind—inside Chen Manor, Young Master had no excuse to shoo him off.
Out in the courtyard, Chen Wenxin asked, "Why drag me out here?"
"I was going to swing by Zhu Manor after seeing Father and Mother, so you saved me the trip."
Suspicion narrowed Chen Wenxin's eyes. "Since when did you grow so thoughtful, coming home to see our parents?"
"I've always been thoughtful! Decided to stay put at Chen Manor for a while and keep the family company."
Suspicion sharpened further: "The sun really is rising in the west—why does this feel fake? Don't tell me you pulled some colossal stunt and ran home for shelter!"
Chen Haoyu marveled at how well his sister read him, though he'd never admit a thing: "No way! Hanging around outside gets boring."
"Spit it out—what did you want to ask?"
Chen Haoyu probed with care: "Sister, why is everyone outside gossiping about Zhu Shuzhen? Saying such ugly stuff I can't even stand listening!"
Indignation blazed on Chen Wenxin's face: "Even you can't stand it—imagine my father-in-law and mother-in-law! Who knows who Shuzhen offended to get smeared like this, dragging the whole Zhu family down with her! Father-in-law suspects foul play and has men investigating. Hey, Haoyu—you roam outside all the time and know loads of people. Help sniff around for whoever's behind this! Catch that rat and I'll slap their face raw and kick them black and blue!"
Chen Haoyu practically felt the slaps and kicks landing on his own hide: "Uh, Sister, isn't that a bit harsh?"
"Hmph, that's getting off light! If Father-in-law catches them, he'll flay them alive!" Scowl fierce, Chen Wenxin looked ready to shred the culprit on the spot.
Nerves jangling, Chen Haoyu put on a cool front: "If you ask me, the Prefect shouldn't waste his energy. What rumor-monger? Zhu Shuzhen brought it on herself with that line of verse! If she hadn't written trash and hung out with Lin'an's scholars all the time, would her name stink? Careful the Prefect digs and digs only to land right back on his own daughter—what a laughingstock that'd be!"
Studying her brother's face, Chen Wenxin frowned: "One second ago you were defending Shuzhen—why flip your tune so fast?"
Chen Haoyu scrambled to cover: "You know me, I speak my mind! Quit staring at me like that, Sister—it's embarrassing."
Chen Wenxin poked his forehead. "Since when does a rascal like you know embarrassment? Now that you're home, go see Grandmother—she's been missing you nonstop."
"I'm going, I'm going—just tell me one more thing first."
"What?"
"How badly did the rumors hit Zhu Shuzhen?" Though he had already heard, he wanted confirmation straight from his sister.
"How badly? Catastrophic! The Qiao family canceled the proposal, and Father-in-law threw Shuzhen out of the house—what do you think?!"
Elation burst inside Chen Haoyu. "Ha! Zhu Shuzhen, try messing with me again—how does defeat taste?! Hahaha!"
Chen Wenxin eyed him askance: "Haoyu, why do you look like you're gloating?"
Chen Haoyu hurried to mask his grin: "Me? No way! Going to see Grandmother now!"
Chen Wenxin caught his sleeve. "I'm warning you—no matter how much you clash with Shuzhen, don't you dare pick on her while she's down! She's suffered enough—hear me?"
"Heard you, heard you! I'm staying home anyway, so I won't even cross paths with her—how could I pick on her? Besides, whenever we cross paths, she's the one beating me up!" Breaking free, Chen Haoyu bolted off in a flash.
"That little menace!"
Qinggua offered, "Eldest Miss, I'll go keep Young Master company."
"Go on, and keep a close eye on him so he doesn't cause trouble."
Qinggua nodded and scampered off.
Returning inside with a smile, Chen Wenxin sent the maid out to play with Laibao, then sat down with her parents to talk over the Zhu family's troubles.
"Father, Mother, you must have heard what happened to the Zhu family."
Yao Qingyan sighed, "How could we miss it? Gossip's flying everywhere—poor Miss Zhu."
Chen Jianglin asked, "How does your father-in-law plan to handle it?"
"Jingyuan told me Father-in-law sent men to track down whoever started the rumors."
Chen Jianglin shook his head. "How can you trace that? A rumor passes from one mouth to the next until nobody remembers who spoke first. Better for your father-in-law to ignore it—rumors flare fast and die fast. Give it time and folks will forget."
"I just worry about poor Shuzhen getting stood up by the Qiaos—how will she find a husband now? Why is her fate so hard? First an unhappy marriage, and just when a fine match comes along, it falls apart!" Grief misted Yao Qingyan's eyes as if it were her own daughter.
Chen Jianglin smiled: "Your mother's too soft-hearted—can't bear seeing anyone suffer."
"Not just anyone—our in-laws' daughter!" Yao Qingyan insisted. "Why don't we pay the Zhus a visit in a day or two to comfort Wenxin's in-laws?"
Chen Wenxin waved her hands at once: "Better not! Going right now would be awkward—wait a bit."
Chen Jianglin agreed: "True. Wait until the gossip dies down and the Zhus calm down and sort things out, then we'll call on them."
Weighing their words, Yao Qingyan saw the sense in it and nodded.
Meanwhile, a maid had already carried news of Second Young Master's return to Old Madam Xia. Grabbing her cane, the old woman started out to find her grandson, her maid hurrying to steady her.
Right at the doorway, they bumped into Chen Haoyu walking up.
Chen Haoyu threw himself into her arms: "Grandmother, I missed you to death!"
Old Madam Xia laughed while scolding him: "Sweet-talking me again! Missing me while staying away all the time—good thing the Chen family owns houses everywhere, or you'd be sleeping in the streets!"
Chen Haoyu steadied her elbow: "Let me help you inside, Grandmother—we'll talk indoors."
Once inside, the maid settled Old Madam Xia onto the couch.
Chen Haoyu kicked off his shoes, climbed up cross-legged, and barked at a maid: "I'm parched—go brew a pot of tea."
The maid hurried off while Qinggua stood beside them listening to grandmother and grandson chat.
Holding her grandson's hand, Old Madam Xia peered close: "Let Grandmother look at you—you've lost weight! Have fun all you want, just take care of your health."
"Don't worry, Grandmother, I'm fit as a fiddle."
"Tell Grandmother the truth: what keep you busy out there?"
How could he tell his grandmother about his real antics? Chen Haoyu spun a yarn: "Making friends, attending poetry salons now and then with Lin'an's scholars."
"Splendid! My grandson even attends poetry salons! I heard not just anyone gets invited—my boy's turning into a real scholar! Maybe one day you'll pass the exams and bring honor to our house!"
Guilt prickled Chen Haoyu under her praise. Just in time, the maid brought tea and poured a cup; Chen Haoyu lifted it to his lips to hide his sheepish face.
Doting eyes glued to her favorite grandson, Old Madam Xia noticed nothing and rattled on: "Your older cousin is already wed—when are you bringing Grandmother home a granddaughter-in-law? I'm waiting to hold a great-grandson!"
Glad for the change of subject, Chen Haoyu set his cup down. "Don't worry, Grandmother—sooner or later I'll bring you home a granddaughter-in-law you'll adore."
Old Madam Xia beamed brighter: "Do you have a girl in mind already?"
"Not yet. The second I do, Grandmother will be the first to know."
"Since you don't, I'll push your parents to get serious and look over Lin'an's noble daughters for you."
"Pass! Those highborn ladies in Lin'an are stuck-up prudes who talk all coy and think they're hot stuff—total snoozefests."
"Sounds like my boy has someone in mind after all, haha!" Old Madam Xia squinted with glee.
Chen Haoyu had never given marriage a single thought: "No way! I haven't had my fill of fun yet—why would I be dumb enough to shackle myself?"
"I heard you've been buying up maids and visiting brothels—don't you dare bring home a maid or a courtesan! Much as I spoil you, I'll never allow that." Even while lecturing, her face overflowed with affection; she could never get enough of her second grandson.
Mixing half-truth with lie, Chen Haoyu vowed, "Don't listen to gossip, Grandmother. I didn't buy maids and I don't haunt brothels. When I marry, it'll be a girl I love from a solid family—maids and courtesans are only for passing the time."
"Good that you know! Don't go wild!"
"Relax, Grandmother, I know the score."
Old Madam Xia swallowed every word without a trace of doubt, convinced her grandson was every bit as sharp and sensible as his late grandfather.
