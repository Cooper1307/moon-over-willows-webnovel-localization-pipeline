#TITLE: Bullying the Groom After the Feast
@@G@@ 1-25
Once the banquet ended and the last guests departed, Chen Jiangmu drew Second Brother Chen Jiangshu aside into a spring-blooming garden pavilion.
@@G@@ 26-26
"What is the secret, Third Brother?" Chen Jiangshu asked.
@@G@@ 27-27
"Huge trouble, Second Brother—the Chens might have crossed royalty!"
@@G@@ 28-28
"Prince Yi?"
@@G@@ 29-29
"Yes! Haofei marched with the bridal escort and watched with his own eyes as Prince Yi brought soldiers to snatch the bride mid-road! Had Princess Jiahe not put her own life on the line, Miss Zhu would never have reached Chen Manor!"
@@G@@ 30-30
Alarm shot through Chen Jiangshu—offending royalty was no joke, and now Prince Yi's absence made total sense!
@@G@@ 31-31
Honest and cautious by nature, Chen Jiangmu always leaned on his elder brothers. "I froze when Haofei told me—was not sure whether to tell Eldest Brother, so I came to you first."
@@G@@ 32-32
"No hiding something this huge—Eldest Brother will find out sooner or later, maybe Haoyu already told him. Come with me to see Eldest Brother right now."
@@G@@ 33-33
In the main courtyard, Chen Jianglin and Yao Qingyan had just sat down for quiet tea when the two brothers arrived; sensing serious talk, Yao Qingyan excused herself to the inner room.
@@G@@ 34-34
Pouring tea and inviting his brothers to sit—Chen Jiangshu opposite on the couch, Chen Jiangmu on a chair—Chen Jianglin had barely handed cups out when Chen Jiangshu cut straight in. "Big trouble, Eldest Brother—did Haoyu tell you?"
@@G@@ 35-35
"Tell me what?"
@@G@@ 36-36
"Did you not wonder why Prince Yi skipped the feast?"
@@G@@ 37-37
"Heard something?" Chen Jianglin tensed.
@@G@@ 38-38
Chen Jiangmu spoke up straight. "Haofei whispered to me that Prince Yi raided the bridal procession today! Princess Jiahe forced him back at risk of her own life and got wounded before he withdrew."
@@G@@ 39-39
Reaching for his tea bowl, Chen Jianglin jerked in shock, tipping the cup clean across the tabletop. As a maid scrambled to wipe the spill, he barked, "Go fetch Haoyu this instant!"
@@G@@ 40-40
A servant sprinted off.
@@G@@ 41-41
Meanwhile, after the feast wrapped up, Chen Haoyu figured Zhu Shuzhen had not eaten all day and had Qinggua pack a food carrier—not from romance, purely keeping peace under one roof. Glancing at the sinking sun reminded him of his wedding night; no way he dared lay a hand on that tigress, so he planned to drop off the meal and head straight to Chun Jinmo.
@@G@@ 42-42
Reeking of wine as he stepped into his courtyard, he stopped dead: twenty or thirty maids stood lined up in formation while Zhu Shuzhen—already out of her bridal gown—addressed them like a general!
@@G@@ 43-43
Waking from a one-hour nap hungry, Qianli Xun had eaten the meal Matron Wei sent over, then decided to inspect the household staff to shake off thoughts of Prince Yi's ambush. Strolling the courtyard with Kuqiao revealed an absurd crowd of pretty young maids—even the sweepers looked handpicked for looks! Disgusted by this rake keeping a private harem while never even sleeping home, she summoned all twenty or thirty maids alongside Chen Manor's Butler Wang to clear house.
@@G@@ 44-44
Spotting Chen Haoyu walk in flushed from wine, she greeted him briskly. "Good timing! Just letting you know I need zero of these maids and am clearing them out. Already asked each one: half want their deeds of sale back to leave, half want to stay on at Chen Manor, so Butler Wang will reassign those to other courtyards."
@@G@@ 45-45
Half his wine buzz evaporated in sheer outrage as Chen Haoyu stabbed a finger at her. "You—you have gone way too far!"
@@G@@ 46-46
Rising to her feet, Qianli Xun snapped, "Point that finger somewhere else! This courtyard belongs to me now, and it runs my way!"
@@G@@ 47-47
Unable to contradict her, he tried bargaining. "At least leave me a couple pretty ones!"
@@G@@ 48-48
"You barely sleep here—what for?" Flicking a look at Qinggua, she asked, "Your name?"
@@G@@ 49-49
"Qinggua, Young Madam."
@@G@@ 50-50
"Qinggua looks handy enough. One attendant is plenty for you."
@@G@@ 51-51
Choking down fury, Chen Haoyu sneered, "A real man does not wrestle women—have it your way! Still, boot everyone out and who sweeps the courtyard? Working Kuqiao to death?!"
@@G@@ 52-52
Right on cue, ten familiar figures marched through the courtyard gate—the exact crew who had flattened Master Jiang's gang outside Wangyue Pavilion!
@@G@@ 53-53
Hongdou stepped forward to report: "Miss, Matron Wei sent us ahead. Carts loaded with all your books are waiting outside the gate—move them in now or wait till tomorrow?"
@@G@@ 54-54
"See? Plenty of help!" Qianli Xun smirked, then told Hongdou, "Park the carts inside the manor and wait until Matron Wei finishes redoing the hall before unloading the books."
@@G@@ 55-55
"Yes, Miss!" Dismissing the nine others to settle in, Hongdou turned back out to direct the carts.
@@G@@ 56-56
Chen Haoyu turned purple. "Zhu Shuzhen! Every stick of furniture in that hall is rare hardwood! Redoing the room—are you hauling MY furniture out too?!"
@@G@@ 57-57
"Whatever I do not want goes; whatever fits stays. Oh, one more thing: the side room sits close to me, so Kuqiao and Hongdou move in there. Out back sit two rooms—Matron Wei takes the left, you take the right. Kuqiao already made your bed nice and cozy, just a bit plain."
@@G@@ 58-58
Chen Haoyu's lips trembled with rage. "Zhu... Zhu Shuzhen! Too far! Here I felt sorry for you and brought you dinner—I would rather feed it to pigs than give you a bite!"
@@G@@ 59-59
Glancing at the food carrier in Qinggua's hand, she smiled coolly. "Thanks for the thought, yet I already ate. Wait for your leftovers and I would starve to death."
@@G@@ 60-60
Just then a servant hurried in from the master's wing: "Young Master, Master summons you!"
@@G@@ 61-61
Desperate to vent, Chen Haoyu swung a kick straight into Qinggua's food carrier, sending bowls and dishes smashing across the flagstones—and stubbing his own toes so hard he hopped yelling "Ow! Ow!" Starting out the gate, he slammed straight into Qianli Xun blocking his path.
@@G@@ 62-62
"Clean it up before you leave!"
@@G@@ 63-63
Terrified a brawl was about to erupt, Qinggua scrambled onto his knees. "I will clean it, Young Madam! Leave it to me!"
@@G@@ 64-64
Only then did Qianli Xun step aside; fists clenched tight, Chen Haoyu stormed out. Sweeping shattered bowls into the tray, Qinggua mused to himself: *Young Master finally met his nemesis—wonder if that is blessing or curse!*
@@G@@ 65-65
Swallowing his anger, Chen Haoyu reached his father's hall, saw both uncles seated inside, and knew at once word of the ambush had leaked. Bowing to all three, he braced himself.
@@G@@ 66-66
"Tell me straight," Chen Jianglin demanded. "Did Prince Yi try to snatch your bride?"
@@G@@ 67-67
"Yes, Father."
@@G@@ 68-68
"How bad is Princess Jiahe's wound?"
@@G@@ 69-69
"Missed vital organs—she will pull through."
@@G@@ 70-70
"Thank Heaven. Do you think this spells trouble for the Chen family?"
@@G@@ 71-71
Fresh off being steamrolled by Zhu Shuzhen, Chen Haoyu leaped at the chance to paint her black. "None of this is the Chens' fault! Blame Zhu Shuzhen—carrying on with Prince Yi behind closed doors, then dragging that disaster into Chen Manor onto my head!"
@@G@@ 72-72
Privately thrilled—the messier Haoyu's marriage, the cleaner Haodong's path to the empire!—Chen Jiangshu chimed in smoothly, "Haoyu speaks true, Eldest Brother. None of this falls on us. Besides, Prince Yi is known for fairness and will hardly punish the Chens over private heartache."
@@G@@ 73-73
Steering clean clear of gossip about his brand-new daughter-in-law, Chen Jianglin nodded. "Fair point. Wait a beat, then we call on Prince Yi in person to apologize."
@@G@@ 74-74
"Apologize for what?!" Chen Haoyu balked. "What crime did we commit?!"
@@G@@ 75-75
"Crime or not, it unfolded during our bridal escort," Chen Jianglin explained. "Showing humility saves royal face, and a fair prince will not hold a grudge once he sees our respect."
@@G@@ 76-76
Both uncles agreed. Chen Haoyu opened his mouth to trash Zhu Shuzhen further when his father waved him off. "Your wedding night—do not leave your bride waiting. Head back."
@@G@@ 77-77
Bowing out into the dark, Chen Haoyu had zero desire to crawl back to his hijacked courtyard and steered straight for the front gate toward Chunxiang Brothel—only to get boxed in halfway across the grounds by Hongdou and two sturdy maids!
@@G@@ 78-78
"Got a death wish, blocking this young master inside his own manor?!"
@@G@@ 79-79
"No offense, Young Master," Hongdou answered politely. "Matron Wei reminded us you must accompany Miss at dawn to serve morning tea to the Chen elders. Skipping out breaks rite, so Miss sent us to escort you back."
@@G@@ 80-80
"And if I refuse?!"
@@G@@ 81-81
"Miss said carry you back over our shoulders if we have to."
@@G@@ 82-82
Before he blinked, the two martial-trained maids hooked his arms from either side and frog-marched him straight back despite his thrashing! Dumping him inside the yard, Hongdou latched the gate before letting him go.
@@G@@ 83-83
"Zhu Shuzhen! Oath-breaker!" Chen Haoyu roared. "Signed in black and white zero interference with my freedom—going back on your word night one?!"
@@G@@ 84-84
Stepping onto the porch, Qianli Xun shot back, "Quit howling! Like I care about your freedom! Look at what you are wearing—a groom bolting on his wedding night makes both of us a joke!"
@@G@@ 85-85
"Oh?" Chen Haoyu leered slyly. "Hoping to consummate the marriage after all?"
@@G@@ 86-86
"Consummate my foot! Tomorrow morning you escort me to greet your elders—leave me going solo? Relax, just tonight! Starting tomorrow afternoon, go rot in any gutter you want and I will not lift a finger!"
@@G@@ 87-87
Turning back inside, she shut the door, leaving Chen Haoyu standing lonely under the stars. "Thank heaven I had the brains to sign a contract first, or living under this roof would be hell!" Muttering, he shuffled off toward the back courtyard.
