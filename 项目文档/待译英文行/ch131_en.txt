#TITLE: A Lead Surfaces on the Rumor Trail
Meanwhile, bored stiff cooped up at Chen Manor, Chen Haoyu had snuck out for a turn at a gambling den—not caring whether he won or lost, just chasing the rush before the dice settled.
Having scratched that itch, he stepped out planning to treat his cronies to a feast before heading back to play the good son at Chen Manor.
With Lin Jue poking around on the quiet for the rumor-mongers, Chen Haoyu still carried a twinge of nerves and meant to stay on his best behavior—already fooling his family into thinking he had turned over a new leaf.
Barely down the street from the gambling den, a gang boss poked his head out of a side alley and waved frantic hands at him.
Chen Haoyu almost ignored him—what if Lin Jue's men were tailing these thugs? Then again, what if it was an emergency? Better hear him out.
Checking the street for tails and spotting none, he strolled over.
Finding the alley empty, Chen Haoyu stationed two cronies at the mouth as lookouts, stepped deeper inside, and snapped, "Didn't I tell you never to come looking for me? Why wave me down in broad daylight?!"
The boss hurried to reassure him: "Relax, Young Master Chen—I checked the whole block, zero sign of Commandant Lin's men before I dared call you over."
"Spit it out—what's going on?"
"Got two pieces of news for you, Young Master Chen: one good, one bad. Which do you want first?"
Without hesitation: "Good news!"
"Good news is Commandant Lin's men haven't traced anything to you yet."
"Duh! If they had, would I be standing here talking to you? What's the bad news?"
"Bad news is Commandant Lin's men dug up several of those written rumor sheets—and yesterday they dragged one of my boys in for questioning!"
"What?! They pulled in one of your boys—and you came straight to me?! Get the hell away from me!" Panic spiked in Chen Haoyu—since when did Lin Jue work this fast?!
"Don't panic, Young Master Chen! They only questioned him, he played totally dumb, and they let him walk! I just came to make sure that if we ever do get caught, you'll pull strings to bail us out..."
"Yeah, yeah, fine! Commandant Lin won't crack it—just tell your boys to stay sharp and keep their mouths shut! Where on earth did they even find those rumor sheets?"
"Beats me—fished them out of a latrine maybe? Imagine the stink!"
"Hope the stink chokes Lin Jue's men dead! Beat it, and never come looking for me again!" Pulling a pouch of silver from his robe, Chen Haoyu tossed it over: "Take it!"
Catching the pouch with a greasy grin: "Count on us, Young Master Chen—won't bother you again!"
Slipping out of the alley, Chen Haoyu canceled his feast, hurried straight home to lie low at Chen Manor, and warned his cronies to stay away for a while.
Lin Jue's soldiers had indeed turned up three rumor sheets—one crumpled tight, one torn in half, and one caked in mud.
Holding those sheets confirmed beyond doubt that the smears against Zhu Shuzhen had been orchestrated. Lin Jue had his men bring in locals from around where the sheets were found to fish for clues.
Out of dozens questioned, some swore they'd never seen the sheets, some swore they never gossiped, and others shook too hard to utter a word.
Only one passerby recalled seeing a man reading aloud from an identical sheet on the street—though he couldn't remember the man's face.
Stationing men to stake out that neighborhood, Lin Jue took the sheets straight to the Prefect.
Receiving Lin Jue at the prefecture office, Zhu Xiyan's face went black as he examined the crumpled sheets: "Clear as daylight—someone targeted the Zhu house on purpose! Keep digging, Commandant Lin."
"Rest easy, Lord Prefect. One witness saw someone reading from these sheets on the street; I already have men watching that quarter."
"Much obliged."
Lin Jue hesitated a beat: "Lord Prefect, tracking down the mastermind will be mighty tough. Since folk have stopped talking about the rumors anyway, why not drop the probe? Dig too hard and we might stir the gossip right back up."
"You have a point, Commandant Lin, yet whoever pulled this filthy stunt is intolerable—I won't rest until he's dragged into the light! Keep digging, just keep it quiet."
Left with no counter, Lin Jue bowed: "Rest easy, Lord Prefect—sooner or later I'll drag the mastermind out for you."
Leaving the prefecture office, Lin Jue rode straight out of town to Youlin Retreat—having found a clue, he owed a report to Prince Yi, the true patron he wanted to anchor himself to.
Having gifted Youlin Retreat—servants and all—to Prince Yi, Butler Li escorted Lin Jue inside to Prince Yi's courtyard.
Stepping into Youlin Retreat for the first time, Lin Jue marveled at Qiao Shouren's clever taste in tucking a whole residence inside a secluded forest.
Outside the door, Butler Li announced, "Your Highness, Commandant Lin is here to pay a call."
Indoors, Shen Jianchen was bored out of his skull—no Wenyuan to pester him, forbidden from visiting Wangyue Pavilion, only Mengrong dropping by, leaving endless empty hours itching to head for Wangyue Pavilion until he forced himself back down.
Lin Jue's visit broke the monotony; he nodded to Bai Feng.
Opening the door, Bai Feng called out, "His Highness invites Commandant Lin inside."
Striding in, Lin Jue found Prince Yi seated at a desk reading a book.
Truth be told, Shen Jianchen had just grabbed the scroll a second ago so he wouldn't look like an idle bum.
Bowing low, Lin Jue reported, "Your Highness, we've turned up a lead in the rumor investigation, so I hurried to report."
Setting the book down with princely poise, Shen Jianchen asked, "Good work, Commandant Lin. What did you find?"
Lin Jue pulled out the torn half-sheet: "Please inspect this, Your Highness."
Bai Feng took it and handed it to his master.
Scanning the lines, Shen Jianchen got the full picture at once.
"So someone really did spread rumors against Youqi Jushi on purpose. Does the Prefect know?"
"He does, Your Highness. I came to report—and ask whether Your Highness wishes us to halt the search?" Lin Jue had already decided: if Prince Yi said stop, he'd ignore Zhu Xiyan!
"Why halt?"
"Hunting down a rumor-monger is like fishing a needle out of the sea. Nobody cares about the gossip anymore—keep poking around and folk might start talking again."
Shen Jianchen saw straight through Lin Jue: the man was just dodging tedious work! No chance—nobody smeared the woman he loved and walked away clean; he meant to catch the mastermind and pound him into understanding who was off-limits!
"I know it's a needle in a haystack, Commandant Lin—no matter how tough, drag the culprit out for me. I'll wait as long as it takes. As for stirring gossip back up, quite the opposite: once folks see authorities investigating, they'll clamp their mouths twice as tight. Keep your men digging."
With Prince Yi's word laid down, Lin Jue had zero wiggle room left—keep digging it was.
One thing dawned clear as glass on Lin Jue now: Prince Yi cared way too much about the Zhu family for it to be just about Qiao Wenxian!
"I will carry out Prince Yi's orders to the letter!"
Bowing out and climbing into his carriage, Lin Jue racked his brains all the way back to town: why on earth was Prince Yi this devoted to the Zhu house?
Qiao Wenxian was already betrothed to someone else, so Prince Yi had zero reason to care about Zhu Shuzhen or old rumors—yet he stayed dead set on hunting the culprit! And Prince Yi had never had ties to Zhu Xiyan before!
Lin Jue thought until his head throbbed without cracking the puzzle. Still, knowing the Qiaos had dumped Zhu Shuzhen gave him a petty thrill: leaving him hadn't gotten her far—nobody wanted her now!
Every single day Shen Jianchen itched to go see Qianli Xun; more than once his carriage got halfway to town before he ordered it turned back, leaving Bai Feng baffled yet silent.
Then an idea sparked, and he strolled over to Qiao Mengrong's courtyard.
Just stepping out to visit him herself, Qiao Mengrong lit up at meeting him in the yard, thinking he had missed her: "I was just coming to find Brother, and here you are!"
"You've been cooped up in Youlin Retreat forever without going out—today I'll take you out strolling the streets."
"Brother read my mind!"
Joy bubbled over in Qiao Mengrong—they were growing more in sync every day!
Riding into the city's bustling heart, Qiao Mengrong strolled in pure delight.
Mind locked onto Qianli Xun, Shen Jianchen had zero heart for shopping. Picking his moment, he said, "Here we are having fun, while poor Wenyuan is miserable."
"Didn't Brother say Young Master Qiao is allowed out again?"
"Only because the Qiao family engaged him to someone else."
"To Shuzhen?"
"To the Miao family's daughter, Miao Zhiyu."
"Won't Shuzhen be heartbroken?!"
"Right—I didn't even dare send Bai Feng to tell her for fear of hurting her. Still, she's bound to hear sooner or later. Since you two are close, why not go break the news gently and comfort her?"
"Come to think of it, I haven't seen her in ages—I should visit! Let's go today!"
Equal parts glad and disgusted with himself for playing Mengrong's kind heart like a pawn, Shen Jianchen couldn't stop himself.
Sending Bai Feng never gave him enough—the lad only dropped off messages and spent the rest of the time flirting with Kuqiao, bringing back useless scraps.
Only Mengrong, Qianli Xun's close confidante, could draw out every real thought hiding inside Qianli Xun's heart.
