#TITLE: Returning to Zhu Manor to Check on the Betrothal
True to Prince Yi's training, Bai Feng dodged every vital spot while pounding Chen Haoyu black, blue, and swollen from head to toe—a total wreck!
His temper cooled at last, Shen Jianchen called off the beating: "Enough—keep going and you'll cripple him. Just make sure he remembers crossing me doesn't end well. Open your eyes wider next time, watch who you mess with, and don't step on a tiger's tail."
Lowering his fists, Bai Feng beamed—his first time pummeling a rich dandy, and it felt fantastic!
Sprawled groaning on the frozen dirt, aching in every bone, Chen Haoyu still had zero clue how he had offended Prince Yi.
Gasping through swollen lips, he asked, "I've got... no grudge... with Prince Yi... Why have your man... beat me?!"
Stepping close, Shen Jianchen looked down cold: "Think back on the filthy stunts you pulled lately and who you bullied—you'll figure it out."
The rumor campaign flashed into Chen Haoyu's head: "Because... I spread... rumors about... Zhu Shuzhen?!"
"Not as dumb as you look—bull's-eye."
Careful not to let Chen Haoyu suspect his own feelings for Qianli Xun and risk hurting her, Shen Jianchen added a cover: "Because of your smears against Miss Zhu, Wenyuan lost the woman he loves and suffered hell—beating you is getting off light!"
"So... Your Highness beat me... for Qiao Wenxian... Thought it was... for Zhu Shuzhen..."
"Hmph! For Wenyuan—and teaching you a lesson for Miss Zhu too! You wrecked an innocent girl's life and marriage prospects; you deserved twice this beating!"
Chen Haoyu forced a bloody grimace: "Her marriage... Prefect Zhu's already... handled that... I'm the poor sucker... beaten by Your Highness... AND forced to..."
Every rib throbbing, Chen Haoyu gave up talking.
Shen Jianchen warned him cold: "Breathe a single word that I had you beaten today, and I'll beat you every single time I lay eyes on you."
"Relax... kill me... and I still won't... brag about... something this humiliating..."
"Smart boy. Come on, Bai Feng—leave Young Master Chen out here alone to reflect on his sins."
Prince Yi strode off with Bai Feng, leaving Chen Haoyu flat on the freezing ground. Shivering against the cold earth, he gritted his teeth and fought to stand—collapsing three or four times before dragging himself upright and hobbling forward, every limb heavy as lead.
Stumbling on until twilight darkened the hills, he finally reached the road back toward town—tripping over a stone and crashing down without even feeling the fresh scrape over the symphony of pain already screaming across his body.
Crawling back up, he limped onward, swallowing a choke of bitter rage he dared neither speak aloud nor avenge.
"Young Master! Found you at last!"
Qinggua's voice rang out of the dusk—making Qinggua look downright angelic to Chen Haoyu for the first time in his life!
After watching his master snatched off the street, Qinggua had sat stunned for half a minute before snapping out of it, hopping into the driver's seat, and whipping the horses in pursuit.
Betting the kidnappers headed outside the city gates, he had driven straight out—uncertain which road they took, searching trail after trail until reaching the foot of the hills.
With dusk closing fast and hope nearly gone, he spotted a limping silhouette in the gloom—and sure enough, it was his master!
At the sight of Qinggua, Chen Haoyu's last scrap of grit snapped and he pitched forward.
Qinggua lunged to catch him: "Who had the nerve to beat you like this, Young Master?!"
"Quit... asking... Take me to... Yongning... Residence... Won't go home... to Chen Manor... till I heal..."
Relieved at least to have found his master alive so he wouldn't face Master's wrath empty-handed, Qinggua hoisted him into the carriage and drove back into town to Yongning Residence.
Helping him into bed at Yongning Residence, Qinggua sent servants running for a physician, who prescribed both herbal drafts and topical salves.
Once maids smoothed cooling ointment across his bruises, relief eased his groans. Lying face-down on the mattress, he called, "Qinggua, come here."
Stepping to the bedside: "Yes, Young Master?"
"Go back to Chen Manor and tell Father I'm staying at Yongning Residence for a spell. Not a word about today."
Promising he would, Qinggua bristled: "The second you heal, Young Master, we're going to hunt down whoever beat you and pay them back!"
"Shut it—never mention today again."
Settling back onto the pillows, Chen Haoyu savored the cool sting of the salve—never in his life had plain comfort felt this sweet.
Not daring another peep, Qinggua rode back to Chen Manor and relayed his master's message word for word to Chen Jianglin.
Exasperated at his son running off again yet helpless to stop him, Chen Jianglin cheered right up thinking of the Zhu betrothal—eager to welcome Miss Zhu into Chen Manor to whip his boy into shape.
Meanwhile, after Chen Haoyu left Wangyue Pavilion, Qianli Xun could not shake the dread that this insane betrothal might actually be real, deciding to go straight to Zhu Manor for the truth.
It was her first time setting foot back inside Zhu Manor since being driven out—after all, why return to a house that threw her out?
Yet if Zhu Xiyan was arranging her marriage again, he still counted her as his daughter—giving her every right to demand answers!
Arriving at Zhu Manor, she skipped having servants announce her and walked straight to Madam Xiang's quarters.
Dozing on her couch, Madam Xiang opened her eyes at the call of "Mother" to find her daughter standing before her.
Sitting up at once: "You're back, Shuzhen? Did your father summon you?"
Skipping pleasantries, Qianli Xun cut straight in: "Mother, do you know anything about Father betrothing me to the Chen family?"
Madam Xiang blinked in genuine surprise: "Betrothing you to the Chen family? Never heard a word! Where did you pick that up?"
Her mother's bewildered face eased the tight spring in Qianli Xun's chest—had Chen Haoyu been messing with her?
Sitting beside Madam Xiang, she explained, "Chen Haoyu showed up at Wangyue Pavilion today claiming our two families are getting engaged—it sounded so wild I came straight here to check."
"Young Master Chen said that? Your father hasn't breathed a word to me!" Unease flickered in Madam Xiang lest her husband was hiding something.
"Mother, could you go ask Father for me?"
"Of course—come along with me and see your father too."
Fearing two sentences with Zhu Xiyan would spark another shouting match, Qianli Xun shook her head: "Better I wait here in Mother's room so Father doesn't yell at me for stepping back into Zhu Manor."
"Wait right here then; Mother will get to the bottom of it."
Taking a maid, Madam Xiang headed to Zhu Xiyan's study, finding him reading a book in peace.
Rumors crushed, a rich marriage lined up for his daughter, Zhu honor restored—Zhu Xiyan hadn't felt this chipper in months!
He even fancied Chen Haoyu's smear campaign had been heaven-sent; without it, what leverage would he ever have had to make the Chens take his daughter?
Determined to keep the betrothal under wraps until sealed—knowing Shuzhen would raise hell and wreck it if wind reached her—he hadn't even breathed a word to Madam Xiang.
Afraid his daughter-in-law Chen Wenxin might visit Chen Manor and spill the beans to Shuzhen, the minute Zhu Xiyan got home from Chen Manor he had summoned Zhu Jingyuan and loaded him up with a tall stack of books for Chen Wenxin to copy out in her neat hand.
Mystified, Zhu Jingyuan had carried them over anyway.
Copying that stack would tie Chen Wenxin down for two weeks—zero free hours to visit Chen Manor or hear about the engagement!
He had also ordered gatekeepers to bar Chen Haoyu from setting foot inside Zhu Manor.
With every loophole sealed, all left was waiting for the Chens to arrive with betrothal gifts.
Stepping into the study, Madam Xiang announced, "Husband, Shuzhen came back..."
"What's she doing back here?" Without setting down his book, Zhu Xiyan put on a frosty scowl.
"She came asking whether the Chen family is coming to propose."
Lowering his book, sharp alertness flashed in Zhu Xiyan's eyes: "Has Madam heard anything about that?"
Madam Xiang shook her head: "Not a word—Young Master Chen told Shuzhen himself."
"That boy is a wild prankster—how can anyone trust a word out of his mouth?" Lifting his book again, Zhu Xiyan tested casually, "If it were true, would Madam want Shuzhen marrying into the Chen family?"
"If it were true, it wouldn't be bad at all—only Shuzhen would probably fight it. Wenxin told me Shuzhen and her brother clash like cats and dogs, ready to throw punches the second they meet."
Pleased his secrecy had paid off, Zhu Xiyan waved a hand: "Go tell Shuzhen a Chen betrothal is pure hot air—send her back and tell her not to wander into Zhu Manor without cause."
"Yes, Husband."
Returning to her chamber, Madam Xiang passed the denial straight to her daughter.
Still half skeptical—even a reckless dandy like Chen Haoyu wouldn't joke about his own wedding!—Qianli Xun decided to double-check with her sister-in-law.
Walking over to her brother's courtyard, she found Chen Wenxin buried at her desk writing nonstop, beckoning Shuzhen to sit without lifting her brush.
"What keeps Sister-in-law writing so furiously?"
"Sigh! Out of nowhere Father-in-law dumped this mountain of books on me to copy! Look at this stack—can't even visit you at Wangyue Pavilion for weeks, so don't hold it against me!"
"How could I? Where's my brother?"
"Always inseparable—asking for your brother the second you walk in! Just stepped out on errands; too buried in copying to ask where."
"Say, Sister-in-law—have you heard a certain piece of news?"
"What news?" Chen Wenxin asked, brush still flying.
"Chen Haoyu showed up at Wangyue Pavilion today claiming the Chens and Zhus are getting engaged..."
Her brush froze mid-stroke as Chen Wenxin looked up beaming: "Is there really news that wonderful?!"
Irritation spiked in Qianli Xun: "Looks like you know nothing—meaning that brother of yours made the whole thing up just to mess with me! Wait till I get my hands on him!"
Resuming her copying, Chen Wenxin grinned: "Maybe Haoyu actually likes you and cooked that up to test your reaction!"
"Now Sister-in-law's talking crazy! Chen Haoyu liking me?! If he ever likes me I'll jump straight into the Qiantang River!"
"Is my brother really that awful?"
"Worse than awful!"
Unbothered, Chen Wenxin kept writing: "Relax—I haven't heard a syllable about any betrothal. Next time I catch Haoyu, I'll box his ears for teasing you with nonsense like that."
Weight lifted off her shoulders at last, Qianli Xun smiled: "Hearing Sister-in-law say that puts my mind at rest. I'll head back to Wangyue Pavilion before Father spots me and blows his top."
"Too swamped to see you out—I'll visit Wangyue Pavilion once I dig out from under these books."
Smiling goodbye, Qianli Xun rose and left.
Riding home in the carriage, Xiaguo wondered aloud, "Why would Young Master Chen lie to you about something like that, Miss?"
"Who knows what rattles inside his skull? Better hope he doesn't cross my path again, or one punch puts him flat on the pavement."
Beside her, Kuqiao hid a quiet giggle.
Qianli Xun raised a brow: "What are you snickering at?"
Kuqiao beamed: "Miss sounds just like her old self again—makes me happy!"
Qianli Xun fell silent—not from fresh agony over Wenyuan, but pausing to take her own pulse at Kuqiao's words. The ache still lingered, yet its jagged edge had softened.
Time truly was the finest medicine, sanding down even the sharpest scars; before long, she trusted the pain would fade away for good.
