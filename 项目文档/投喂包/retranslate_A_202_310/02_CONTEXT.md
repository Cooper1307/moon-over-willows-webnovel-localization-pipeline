Seventeen thumbprints down, only *three* elders left—when Han Gu's voice echoed across the hall:

*"The Judicial Investigator arrives!"*

Leaving Chen Manor earlier, Qiao Mengrong had dropped by the Judicial Office and gleefully dished the whole Chen scandal to Brother—prompting Shen Jianchen to realize there was zero Chen face left to spare, and march straight over before the clan crowned a rapist! (Riding home afterward, Mengrong sighed over losing another pawn, eyed Imperial Attendant Liang as a dream trump card—and suddenly wondered why Bai Feng had vanished for weeks, sparking fresh paranoia about Shuzhen!)

Striding into Xinglong Pavilion with Han Gu, Wen Xin, and armed constables, Shen Jianchen smiled coolly at stunned Chen Jianglin: "Looks like I arrived just in time."

Stopping straight before sweating, white-faced Chen Haodong, Shen Jianchen proclaimed loud enough for every elder in the room to hear:

"Remember Chun Jinlan? On the third night of the third month last year, maid Aye saw a man shrouded head-to-toe in a black cloak walk out of that room. That man was *you*—Chen Haodong, you are the rapist who violated Chun Jinlan!"
