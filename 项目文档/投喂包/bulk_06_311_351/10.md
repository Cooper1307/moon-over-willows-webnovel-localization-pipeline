Sending Bai Feng to escort Matron Zou safely to Lin'an's bamboo cottage, Shen Jianchen rode north through winter storms alone with **ten million taels** in banknotes.
Stunned when Cai Jing announced Prince Yi had actually raised ten million taels, jealous Wanyan Chi hatched a vicious double-cross: inviting Shen Jianchen into his hall the next day, inspecting the bursting chest of banknotes—then claiming coldly that Qianli Xun had stabbed herself to death last night and been tossed in the pauper's pit!
Crushing Wanyan Chi's ambush squad and smashing Wanyan Chi's jaw bloody, Shen Jianchen faltered for one fatal heartbeat when Wanyan Chi taunted that her dying thoughts had been of him—taking a treacherous Jin sword straight through the back and blacking out as Wanyan Chi stole his fortune and dumped him outside the city wall!
Waking bandaged in a farmhouse cared for by Cai Jing—who spun a merciful lie and even staged a fresh dummy grave outside town so the ruined prince wouldn't throw his life away storming Wanyan Chi's garrison!—Shen Jianchen collapsed weeping across the fresh earth. Warned by Cai Jing not to exhume her during the forty-nine-day *bardo* spirit transition, Shen Jianchen hired a carriage straight back to Lin'an's Divine Willow.
Standing before bare winter branches, he pressed a lethal fist against his own heart to follow her—only for **Master Jingguang** to halt him cold: *"Suicide breeds dark karma that rebounds straight onto the dead! Live on and build merit in her name!"* Awakened from despair, Shen Jianchen bowed deep, resolving to spend his remaining years doing charity under *Qianli Xun*'s name.
#CH 340
#TITLE: Prince Yi Brings News of Her Death to Lin'an
Marching straight to Chen Manor, Shen Jianchen absorbed Haoyu's furious punches without flinching before breaking the shattering news that she was dead. When weeping Haoyu swore to fetch her bones home to Chen ground, possessive grief made Shen Jianchen lie bluntly—claiming he'd told her about the edict and consummated with her in Yanjing, leaving Haoyu shattered and retreating inside Chen Manor empty-handed.
Walking numbly out of town toward his lonely bamboo cottage, Shen Jianchen ran into Qiao Wenxian's carriage and broke down sobbing in his friend's arms. Back at Youlin Retreat—where Miao Zhiyu and Xiaguo now raised a toddler boy and girl—Wenxian poured warm rice wine beside a roaring brazier, sharing two years of Lin'an changes: the Dongfang Salon shuttered by Prefect Li Ke, Cao Ban and Ye Anya finally welcomed home by the Caos with their two-year-old son, Chun Jinmo dying in childbirth to save Master Jiang's boy, and Chun Jinqiao dying of a strange illness while Lin Jue reformed and fathered a daughter with Bai Meiruo.
Moments later, flushed from winter wind, Lin Jue arrived to kneel in sincere repentance before marching north tomorrow to fight the Jin—sharing one quiet farewell cup with his old rivals. Afterward, hearing the tragic news from Wenxian, both Xiaguo and Miao Zhiyu burst into tears as Wenxian vowed never to attend another literary gathering unless Shuzhen came back from the dead.
#CH 341
#TITLE: Coaxing Her with Drugged Osmanthus Wine
Back inside Yanjing's Under-Manor, Wanyan Chi lied that Prince Yi had never shown up with the ransom, trapped furious Qianli Xun under a ceiling net triggered by floor switches, and bragged of shaman drugs arriving from Huining—leaving her fuming and puzzled why the traps obeyed only his footsteps.
Pumping her chatterbox Jurchen maid for intel over five quiet days, Qianli Xun learned Wanyan Chi's surprisingly tragic backstory: son of the Jin Emperor and a Han mother from Lin'an (Shao Zhengye's sister!), mocked as a half-breed, forced into loveless political marriages after his father murdered his first love—who looked hauntingly like Qianli Xun!
On day six, Wanyan Chi arrived with a pot of osmanthus wine, playing his deepest card with genuine red-eyed grief—saying today was the anniversary he celebrated for the mother he'd never met, begging her to share just two cups before he released her from the Under-Manor.
Touched by his motherless ache and watching him drink first without flinching, Qianli Xun dropped her guard and sipped—only for her vision to spin dark mid-sentence as she pitched forward unconscious into his grinning arms.
#CH 342
#TITLE: Tears Beneath the Bridal Candles
Waking to daylight inside a crimson wedding chamber, Qianli Xun tried jumping off the bed only to find every muscle limp as boiled noodles—her martial strength completely drained by a Jin shaman's drug!
Swearing she'd bite off her own tongue if he touched her, she bit Wanyan Chi's lip bloody when he forced a kiss—causing him to laugh darkly, send maids to clear out every blade and pair of scissors, and post armed guards outside the locked doors.
Getting nowhere trying to wheedle an antidote out of terrified maid **Huilanna**, Qianli Xun drifted into a fevered dream beneath the Divine Willow—where the spirit tree spoke of karma binding her travels before visions of Shen Jianchen, Haoyu, and knife-wielding Mengrong jolted her awake to glowing red wedding candles.
Refusing the Jurchen bridal gown outright, she watched Wanyan Chi shrug off his own groom's robe and climb onto the bed. Firing one desperate kick straight to his groin—only for him to tap her immobilizing acupoint before she could strike again—she lay paralyzed as silent tears of humiliation slipped down her cheeks.
#CH 343
#TITLE: Stripped of Honor, Too Ashamed to Go Home
Waking before dawn once the acupoint wore off, Qianli Xun huddled weeping in the corner while smug Wanyan Chi gloated over being her first man—swearing he'd stay faithful and take her back to Lin'an someday, until she yanked the whole quilt off him in fury to freeze him out. Leaving sheepishly at sunrise promising never to touch her uninvited again, he gave her free run of the manor.
Strolling out at noon with Huilanna into the stark northern winter, she paused by a round pond strewn with red maple leaves beside withered chrysanthemums and bright yellow courtyard citrus, murmuring Zhu Shuzhen's ***Writing My Thoughts in Early Winter***:
*"My eyes meet the round pond's winter scene— / lotus withered, chrysanthemums gone wild. / Chill winds invade my midnight pillow; / biting frost makes me shrink from morning rouge. / Crimson maple leaves flip across the river gale; / yellow citrus hangs before the courtyard hall. / I brush a poem hoping to ease this gloom, / yet facing the view only doubles my grief."* [TN: Translator's rendering]
Clapping warmly from behind, Wanyan Chi met her blistering hatred by handing her the real **antidote pill** outright—*"Kill me if you hate me that much, just lead my mother to my grave!"*
Swallowing the pill and feeling power surge back into her fists, Qianli Xun smashed a full-strength punch straight into his unguarded chest, sending him kneeling coughing blood—yet froze inches from his skull when weeping Huilanna clung to her knees pleading for the generous prince who'd saved her father's life, surrounded by loyal guards swearing blood vengeance across generations if he died.
Dropping her fist in disgusted defeat—too ashamed now to face either Haoyu or Prince Yi—she marched straight toward the front gates to vanish on her own.
#CH 344
#TITLE: Grieving in a Crumbling Era
Flattening every blocking gate guard, Qianli Xun blasted Wanyan Chi's twisted "love" speech—stopping dead in her tracks only when he called after her in sudden decency: *"Prince Yi brought ten million taels for you! I lied you were dead and robbed him—go find him!"*
Tears spilling down her cheeks, she walked straight out into the winter streets without looking back: better let Shen Jianchen believe her dead and move on clean, while Wanyan Chi quietly sent Lu Yuanfeng to trail and protect her from afar.
Back in Lin'an, the fake death news broke Old Madam Xia's frail heart, and the Old Ancestor at Zhu Manor peacefully passed two days later—leaving Haoyu shattered inside Suxin Thatched Hall, refusing to let graying Matron Wei or weeping Kuqiao move back to Zhu Manor while young Chen Haofei resigned his corrupt capital post to learn Chen trade.
Meanwhile, history roared south: Emperor Zhao Ji panicked and dumped the throne onto weeping **Zhao Huan** (opening the **first year of Jingkang**), while hero **Li Gang** rallied Bianliang's defenders against Wanyan Zonghan until appeasers bought a fragile truce by ceding Zhongshan, Hejian, and Taiyuan!
Spring thawed the frost, yet Haoyu still sat drinking in Moxiang Courtyard reciting Zhu Shuzhen's ***Busuanzi: Ode to the Plum Blossom***:
*"One bough leans sideways deep in the bamboo grove, / lending the quiet woods a stillness deeper still. / After rain, pure beauty no brush could ever paint— / sparse shadows stretched across the shallow water. / A flute plays the Little Chanyu tune clean through, / stirring old sorrows back to mind once more. / Faint fragrance drifts across the gentle breeze, / while chill moonlight wraps the blossoms cold."* [TN: Translator's rendering]
Unable to stomach seeing his master waste away indoors, Qinggua talked him into visiting Qiaotou Wa (where the storyteller was sick)—then pulled out his ace: **Xiangrong** at Huayun Pavilion, who sang all of Second Young Madam's lyrics set to Dongfang Chenze's melodies!
#CH 345
#TITLE: Longing Set to Song, a New Bond Forged
Inside a quiet Huayun Pavilion booth, Xiangrong swept her strings into Zhu Shuzhen's ***Dielianhua***—*"Slanting wind and fine rain chill the spring. / Before the wine cup recalling vanished joys, / I once leaned lonely by pear blossoms streaked with tears..."* [TN: Translator's rendering]—before breaking house rules to sing a sixth piece she swore was written for Haoyu: the legendary ***Circle Lyric*** (*Quan'er Ci*)!
Tears blurring his eyes as Xiangrong handed him the authentic page of inked circles and dots, Haoyu clutched her final keepsake to his chest—barely noticing outside as Qinggua helped **Amei** return a wandering toddler wearing a *Jiang* longevity lock straight back to **Master Jiang** (sparking love at first sight, with Master Jiang proposing marriage the very next day!), and pointed out free steamed buns at a shop hung with *"Qianli Buns and Qianli Congee"* funded monthly by Prince Yi from Zhuli Village!
Seeing Haoyu perk up visiting Huayun Pavilion daily for half a month, Yao Qingyan convinced Chen Jianglin to break clan precedent, redeem Xiangrong for three thousand taels, and bring her home as Haoyu's concubine. Moved when Xiangrong humbly vowed only to sing Shuzhen's lyrics with him rather than replace her, Haoyu settled her in a cozy side courtyard—until one lonely drunken night listening to *"I walk alone, sit alone, sing alone, drink alone, and sleep alone..."* [TN: Translated by Tony Barnstone and Chou Ping], he blurred Xiangrong into his lost wife and pulled her into his arms.
#CH 346
#TITLE: A Storyteller Brings Miraculous News
Waking in Xiangrong's bed, Haoyu vowed no second wedding yet promised lifelong loyalty to her alone—and within weeks, news of Xiangrong's pregnancy pulled Haoyu straight back into leading the Chen empire, though he still refused Matron Wei's offer to vacate Moxiang Courtyard.
Out in Zhuli Village, bearded, weathered Shen Jianchen lived simply while nervous Prefect Li Ke—whose patron Liang Shicheng had just been strangled on Emperor Zhao Huan's orders!—showered him with tribute silver that Shen Jianchen poured right into village *"Qianli Feasts"* (making everyone shout *"Qianli Xun!"* ten times just to hear her name alive in the air), promising Bai Feng his own bamboo house so he could finally marry Kuqiao and bring Matron Wei over too.
Slipping into town to Qiaotou Wa's storytelling hall after lunch with his wine gourd, Shen Jianchen froze dead in his tracks when the storyteller announced a **brand-new huaben from Master Qianli Xun**, smuggled out of Yanjing just ten days ago—with promises of more to come!
*"She's alive! She's alive!"* Bursting back to his bamboo cottage in dizzy joy, he crashed straight into Qiao Wenxian bearing twin proof: a scholar returning from Yanjing had spotted a brand-new lyric by Youqi Jushi hung in a Yanjing teahouse, signed **sixth day of the first month, first year of Jingkang**!
Cooling Shen Jianchen's impulse to rush blindly north without leverage, Wenxian swore all his friends to keep her survival quiet from newly expectant Haoyu so Shen Jianchen finally got his fair shot! Shaving clean while weeping Matron Zou combed his hair, Shen Jianchen mounted up and galloped straight to the **Shao Escort Agency**—holding the one trump card Wanyan Chi could never refuse.
#CH 347
#TITLE: Seeking Shao Yueting at the Nunnery
Dropping straight onto his knees in Shao Zhengye's practice yard until the escort chief relented, Shen Jianchen hiked quietly up to the mountain nunnery where Shao Zhengye's sister lived under the Dharma name **Master Jingwu**.
Sitting serenely over tea until her brother bolted the parlor doors and confessed twenty years of lies—her son lived as Jurchen prince Wanyan Chi, desperately hunting for his mother, and now held Prince Yi's beloved hostage!—Shao Yueting wept silently yet stood firm as a nun refusing worldly entanglements, walking out even as Shen Jianchen kowtowed three loud times at her feet.
Refusing to leave, Shen Jianchen sat patiently on the nunnery steps through sunset until Master Jingguang summoned Master Jingwu into her meditation cave: *"Karma knocking at your door must be faced to be dissolved—running never clears a single debt."* Awakened, Shao Yueting stepped out at dusk and agreed to ride north at dawn.
Journeying a full month through swelling tides of southern refugees foreseeing a second Jin invasion, Shen Jianchen arrived outside Yanjing's walls, bounded onto the battlements knocking down guards, and shouted his challenge: *"Tell Wanyan Chi I brought the one person he longs to see—trade me the person I want!"*
Hearing the news while sparring with his martial master **Nahegu**—who urged trapping the rival master of his boxing style—Wanyan Chi's pulse spiked: *Mother?!* Sending Lu Yuanfeng off to keep Qianli Xun safely out of sight, he galloped straight to the city gate.
#CH 348
#TITLE: Rescuing Her Outside Yanjing's Gates
After walking out of Wanyan Chi's manor penniless, Qianli Xun had busked on Yanjing's street performing her custom martial routine to Zhu Shuzhen's *Niannu Jiao* until Lu Yuanfeng showed up—claiming he'd bumped into her by coincidence—installed her in a modest lane house, lined up letter-writing and teahouse poem commissions (plus smuggling fresh huaben out through a sympathetic storyteller!), and even had her teaching six Jurchen children the *Analects* so she could peacefully "Sinicize" the next generation! On the bright new-moon night of **the sixth day of the first month, first year of Jingkang**, defying Jin calendar bans, she had penned Zhu Shuzhen's ***Yi Qin'e*** for the teahouse:
*"Curved and slender, / the New Year's new moon hooks cold jade. / Hooks cold jade— / tiny phoenix slippers, / furrowed emerald brows. / Festive moths and snow-willows crown her hair; / fiery dragon-lanterns race bright trees of flame. / Race bright trees of flame— / yet Lantern Night's full fifteenth / pales beside this sixth!"* [TN: Translator's rendering]
While Lu Yuanfeng steered her down quiet back streets buying ink to keep her far from Main Street, Wanyan Chi stared down from Yanjing's battlements at Shen Jianchen's two carriages—until a gentle voice inside asked Shen Jianchen to name the **red birthmark behind Wanyan Chi's left ear**!
Breaking into sobs, Wanyan Chi sprinted down off the wall and dropped onto his knees weeping *"Mother!"* outside the carriage. Refusing to step down until he freed the girl, Shao Yueting won his instant oath—though crafty Wanyan Chi still slipped a slick condition onto surrounded Shen Jianchen: *I'll send her safely home to Lin'an today without your seeing her, while you stay one full year teaching me and my master your boxing!*
Seeing no safer move—and eager to uncover *Shen Fist*'s origins—Shen Jianchen agreed, and Shao Yueting rode into the manor with her long-lost son.
#CH 349
#TITLE: Reunited with His Mother, Freeing the Beauty
Inside the manor, touching the tall, weathered face of the son she'd mourned for twenty years, Shao Yueting refused to leave her vows yet agreed to stay fifteen days—making Wanyan Chi swear never to tell the Jin Emperor she lived.
Handing Shen Jianchen brush and paper to write Qianli Xun an urgent farewell letter—unsigned except for a single **"Shen"**—Wanyan Chi sent a maid straight to Lu Yuanfeng's house.
Unmasking Lu Yuanfeng's whole covert caretaking act, Qianli Xun tore open the letter and read with brimming eyes: how Shen Jianchen had learned she lived from her stories and poem, brought Wanyan Chi's mother to trade her free, vowed to stay one year sparring with Nahegu, asked her to bring Mengrong's and Xilan's bones home to Lin'an—and finally revealed the imperial marriage edict plus Haoyu's new family with pregnant Xiangrong, begging her simply to wait for him in their bamboo cottage!
Packing her manuscripts and the refunded rent silver, she hurried outside the east gate to Mengrong's grave—where Wanyan Chi waited with diggers and the bound Jin soldier who had driven Mengrong to suicide (already condemned for murdering his superior officer). When Qianli Xun hesitated to execute a bound man, Wanyan Chi snapped the killer's neck with one blow, oversaw exhuming both coffins cleanly, and bade her a wistful, empty-handed farewell as a fresh carriage waited down the road.
Returning to the heavily guarded chamber where Shen Jianchen sat calmly drinking, Wanyan Chi couldn't resist dropping one sharp barb: *"I was her first man!"*
Drenched in tossed wine and floored under furious fists—not out of regret, Shen Jianchen roared, only raw agony over failing to save her from his filth!—Wanyan Chi wiped his split lip and burst out laughing when Shen Jianchen snarled, *"I don't care a damn! Say it again and I'll kill you!"*
"Exactly what I needed to hear," Wanyan Chi chuckled from the floor. "Take good care of her."
#CH 350
#TITLE: Returning to Lin'an, Ending an Old Tie
Traveling south over a gentle month and a half—pausing at scenic towns to describe the wide world softly to the red wooden casket holding Mengrong's bones beside gray-boxed Xilan—Qianli Xun bypassed Lin'an straight to Zhuli Village, greeted by ecstatic Kujue, Bai Feng, and tearfully grateful Matron Zou.
Laying Mengrong and Xilan to rest amid blooming mountain wildflowers outside the bamboo grove, Qianli Xun marveled at the sprawling, book-lined bamboo residence Shen Jianchen had built to her exact dreams, happily blessed Bai Feng and Kuqiao's wedding at last, and rode into town to Zhu Manor—where Zhu Xiyan dropped his teacup in joyful tears and Madam Xiang, Zhu Jingyuan, and Chen Wenxin wept hugging their miracle daughter back to life.
Right after their reunion lunch, Chen Haoyu walked straight through the doorway alongside Kuqiao, Matron Wei, and four-months-pregnant Xiangrong—just in time to overhear Qianli Xun declare she'd wait in the bamboo cottage for Prince Yi.
Clearing the room gracefully (sending Kuqiao off to Bai Feng and caring for pale Xiangrong), the family left the former couple alone.
Tears rolling down his cheeks, Haoyu pulled her briefly close before stepping back at her soft reminder she was no longer his wife—laughing bitterly through his tears at how "shamelessly" Prince Yi had knelt in Bianliang, sold his entire estate for ten million taels, and traded himself in Yanjing so Haoyu couldn't even hate him!
When Qianli Xun firmly declined trying to annul the old edict, Haoyu locked red-rimmed eyes onto hers for one final truth: *"In that Bianliang prison cell, when you kissed me... did you love me?"*
Unable to wound the man who had grown up for her sake—and remembering that honest moment of shelter—she whispered gently, *"In that moment... yes, I think I did."*
Smiling through his tears—*"Knew you loved me once..."*—Chen Haoyu walked quietly out the door, closing their chapter forever.
#CH 351
#TITLE: Moon Above the Divine Willow, a Lover Returns (The End)
Letting Haoyu walk off his heartbreak alone, Qianli Xun rode with Xiangrong and Chen Wenxin to Chen Manor—reassuring Xiangrong with warm sincerity that she was Haoyu's true match before kneeling tearfully at Old Madam Xia's spirit tablet. Asking Yao Qingyan to take her as a goddaughter—*"No goddaughter—a real daughter!"*—she bowed to Chen Jianglin as "Father" too, receiving back every deed, shop, and Zuiyun Tower (which Matron Wei had quietly returned when she'd been presumed dead) plus a jade bangle from Yao Qingyan.
Walking in composed at last, Haoyu settled Xiangrong gently in her seat and granted Qianli Xun's request to move Moxiang Courtyard's staff and belongings out that very evening, sending Qinggua to help load the carts while masking one last quiet ache.
After paying peaceful respects to the Old Ancestor at Zhu Manor's shrine and spending several joyful days surrounded by her beaming parents, Qianli Xun rode out to her bamboo cottage alongside Matron Wei—who blushed happily admitting she and Steward Fang had fallen for each other during the dark years, agreeing to marry yet stay right beside her mistress until they retired together to the bamboo grove.
Life blossomed at the cottage: joyous weddings for Kuqiao and Bai Feng, Asheng and Hongdou, and Qinggua and Chouya; visits from happily married Amei, glowing Ye Anya and Cao Ban, Wenxian with his thriving household, and even mellowed Lin Jue back from the front with Bai Meiruo—while Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo apologized sincerely and became Qianli Xun's monthly tea-date bosom friends at Yongtai Teahouse! Meanwhile, quiet visits to Master Jingwu at the nunnery brought precious snippets of news about Shen Jianchen through Wanyan Chi's letters.
Months rolled by as history struck its cruelest blow: the second Jin invasion crashed through Bianliang in the first month of Jingkang's second year, stripping Emperors Huizong and Qinzong of their dragon robes into northern captivity as the Northern Song fell—and Prince of Kang Zhao Gou took the throne in Yingtian Prefecture, founding the Southern Song. Amid the shifting times, Xiangrong bore Haoyu a healthy son and became primary wife, while Chen silver quietly bought chastened, subdued Chen Haodong out of prison to live humbly beside his parents.
Past a full year, longing sharpened in Qianli Xun's chest. Visiting Youlin Retreat one afternoon with Miao Zhiyu and Bai Meiruo, she stood beneath the lush, red-ribboned Divine Willow while their children chased butterflies, silently praying ten times for Shen Jianchen's safe return—
"Bet you'd be under the Divine Willow—nailed it!"
Thinking longing was playing tricks on her ears until Miao Zhiyu nudged her smiling, Qianli Xun spun around—and saw him standing tanned and whole before her as her friends tactfully whisked their children away!
"Liar—said one year, and you're over two months late!"
"Got held up in Yingtian Prefecture—His Majesty insisted I stay a spell. Wait... don't tell me you're still thinking of turning me down?"
Fighting back a grin to tease him one last time, she turned her back playfully. "What if I do?"
"Chubby!" Shen Jianchen called out.
*Snort, snort!* Before she could blink, a sleek, jet-black piglet tore past her ankles, hit the Divine Willow at a dead sprint, scrambled effortlessly straight up the vertical trunk on all four hooves, and sprawled smugly across the thickest bough!
Jaw hitting the grass in speechless shock, Qianli Xun heard him step beside her: *"Let's see you turn me down now!"*
Throwing her arms clean around his neck, she laughed through happy tears. "You giant idiot—I was teasing! Even if pigs never climbed trees, I'd still say yes!"
"Only for you do I turn into an idiot," Shen Jianchen whispered, pulling her tight at last into a deep, unguarded kiss beneath a rising full moon turning the willow bright as day.
Catching her breath afterward, she laughed learning he'd paid a hundred taels to a street performer in Huining for Chubby while traveling north with Master Nahegu—confirming *Shen Fist* actually predated *Qianli Fist* by four or five centuries!
"Older or not, you're still my beaten opponent!"
"Happily your beaten opponent," Shen Jianchen smiled, holding her close under the silver moon. "Forever."
(The End)
