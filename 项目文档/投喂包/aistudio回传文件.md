#TITLE: An Apology from the Three Young Masters
The Dongfang family's dancers soon entered and began to dance. Everyone ate while watching the performance, while the servants they had brought stood off to the side to wait on them; they would only get to enjoy the food once their masters finished.
Today's spread held no appeal for Bai Feng at all. Standing beside Prince Yi, he kept his eyes fixed on Kuqiao.
Spotting Dongpo Pork on the table, Shen Jianchen knew how much Bai Feng loved it. He did not even need to look to guess how long his guard's drool must be by now. He picked up a piece with his chopsticks, placed it in a small porcelain bowl, lifted it, and held it out to the side.
When no one took it after a long moment, Shen Jianchen turned his head and saw that Bai Feng had paid no attention to the pork at all. Puzzled, he wondered why the guy had zero interest in Dongpo Pork. Had he eaten too much of it and grown sick of it?
Taking a closer look, he saw a smile hanging on Bai Feng's face, his eyes locked straight in one direction.
Shen Jianchen pulled the small porcelain bowl back, set it on the table, and followed Bai Feng's gaze. So he had someone he liked now.
A knowing smile curved Shen Jianchen's lips. "I like Qianli Xun, and you like her maid," he thought. "That's quite amusing."
Shen Jianchen had no interest in Kuqiao. Now that he was already looking in Qianli Xun's direction, he could not stop himself from looking at her.
Between watching the dance and enjoying the food, she had eyes only for Wenyuan, sharing cups of wine and trading glances. They looked so close. A smile Shen Jianchen had never seen before lit up Qianli Xun's face, and he could feel how happy she was in this moment.
"Brother," came Qiao Mengrong's voice.
Shen Jianchen looked over at Mengrong. She was raising her wine cup toward him with a smile.
He picked up his cup from the table, gestured a toast to Mengrong, and drank it down. He dared not keep watching Qianli Xun, afraid he might lose control, march right over, and demand of her: "Why did you have to fall in love with Wenyuan? Why didn't you wait for me?"
The three young masters had been watching Youqi Jushi's expression the whole time. Sure enough, seeing Brother Qiao had put her in high spirits, and the three exchanged a glance.
Once the dancers finished and withdrew, Dongfang Chenze filled his cup and raised it. "Youqi Jushi, besides paying our respects to Prince Yi and having a good time with Brother Qiao, the three of us came to Secluded Forest Villa today for another important matter."
Qianli Xun was puzzled. "What important matter? Why bring it up to me in particular?"
The other two young masters raised their cups as well.
Dongfang Chenze said, "The three of us owe Youqi Jushi an apology!"
All three spoke in unison: "We hope Youqi Jushi will show us forbearance!"
With that, they drained their cups together.
Qianli Xun was even more baffled than before. "Why apologize to me? I don't recall you doing anything to offend me."
Dongfang Chenze set down his cup. "Does Youqi Jushi still remember the night we went boating on West Lake, when you got drunk and composed a new ci lyric at Wangjing Pavilion?"
Qianli Xun nodded. "Of course I remember. Is something wrong with it?"
"The three of us wrote down your new ci. We all knew the tune title, of course, but since it had no subtitle, we talked it over and came up with a fitting title..."
"What title?" Qianli Xun felt a pang of worry. Later generations already knew this ci lyric; could the three of them have given it some other title?
"'Summer Excursion on the Lake.'"
Qianli Xun broke into a gentle smile. History could not be altered on a whim; as if guided by fate, the three young masters had named it with those exact four characters, "Summer Excursion on the Lake." "It's a great title! You did a good thing, so why apologize?"
"Nothing delights the three of us more than spreading Youqi Jushi's poetry, so once we got our hands on a new ci, we couldn't wait to share it. Yet while people were appraising it, some took issue with the line 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed.' We fear that once this lyric spreads, it could harm Youqi Jushi's reputation. Afterward we regretted putting it out for everyone to critique, but what's done is done. People already know it, and there's no taking it back. If unfavorable comments ever reach your ears, Youqi Jushi, please don't hold it against us."
Qianli Xun still could not figure out why she had recited Zhu Shuzhen's "Qing Ping Yue: Summer Excursion on the Lake" when she got drunk that night. Thinking back on it, she must have been too happy that day, boating on West Lake and going on a date, so the lyric had just come to mind on its own.
Now that she had recited it, she was not afraid of people passing it around; there was no need to fret so much. Besides, a thousand years later, history still remembered this ci. Everyone had given it a fair appraisal, and nobody had condemned Zhu Shuzhen for it.
"I thought it was something serious! All I did was recite 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed.' If I dared say it aloud, I'm not afraid of people knowing. You were all right there at the time, so you saw with your own eyes I didn't do anything improper. As for sitting in Wenyuan's lap, what's the harm? You all know what Wenyuan and I are to each other. Relax. Even if people gossip, I'd never blame you. You spread my ci out of goodwill. People who read poetry are all over the map in taste, so of course you'll hear every sort of chatter. Why take the blame on yourselves? Besides, up to now, I haven't heard a single peep of criticism, haha..."
At last, the three young masters felt relieved.
Cao Ban said, "Hearing Youqi Jushi see it that way puts our minds at ease at last."
Qiao Wenxian turned to face Shuzhen. "I love 'Summer Excursion on the Lake,' and I'll remember it for the rest of my life. Anyone who slanders it slanders me, and I won't sit by and let that happen. As long as I'm here, you have nothing to worry about."
Qiao Wenxian's words nourished her heart like spring rain. Qianli Xun turned to look at Wenyuan; his gaze was so warm she felt ready to melt. If she had always had someone like Wenyuan by her side, watching over her at every turn, perhaps she would never have inherited Qianli Fist at all.
After her mother passed away, her father loved her too, yet all he ever told her was that a girl had to stand on her own two feet and hold up her own sky, because men could never be trusted. That mindset had guided her whole life. Not only had she taken up Qianli Fist of her own free will, she had worked hard to become a web novelist, pouring her whole heart into her career and refusing to touch romance or marriage.
In this moment, Qianli Xun felt what it was like to have someone beside her speaking warm words. It did not just warm her heart; it took away any fear of the gossip that might come, because now she had someone in her life to face everything with her.
Just as Qianli Xun lost herself in Wenyuan's warm gaze, Prince Yi's voice cut in.
"Which brings me back to what I said before. From now on, Youqi Jushi should stop putting private affairs into poetry for the whole world to see. If you and Wenyuan are together, it's enough that the two of you know. Stop parading it in front of others, let alone writing it into verse. What a pity: you didn't even mention Wenyuan's name. Who's to know whether it was Wenyuan's arms you fell into, or some other man's? I'm afraid gossip is inevitable."
Shen Jianchen could not stand watching the two of them trade tender gazes, and a distinct edge of criticism crept into his tone.
Qianli Xun bristled at that, yet since the speaker was Prince Yi, she dared not talk back. More important, she needed a favor from him, so she had to swallow her annoyance. "Thank you for your guidance, Prince Yi..." She had half a mind to explain herself, then thought better of risking his displeasure and bit the words back.
Qianli Xun stopped looking at Wenyuan and stabbed at the dishes on her plate with her chopsticks, taking out her frustration as if she meant to crush the food to bits.
Shen Jianchen felt delighted. As long as she was not locking eyes with Wenyuan, he was happy. Smiling, he lifted his wine cup and took a slow, savoring sip.
Qiao Mengrong saw how displeased Youqi Jushi looked after her brother's remarks. Worried she might harbor resentment toward him, she spoke up. "I've read many of Youqi Jushi's poems and ci, and every one of them speaks straight to my heart. 'Summer Excursion on the Lake' is bound to be the same. From the line Young Master Dongfang just quoted, 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed,' anyone can tell Youqi Jushi is a woman of true feeling, living with candor and without pretense, free of the coy affectations of sheltered boudoir ladies. Mengrong both appreciates and admires that."
Princess Jiahe sure knew how to talk so much better than Prince Yi. Qianli Xun set down her chopsticks and offered a genuine smile. "Thank you for your appreciation, Princess Jiahe."
With that, she shot a glance at Prince Yi.
"She looked at me on her own at last." Shen Jianchen felt overjoyed; even if there was not a trace of affection in her gaze, he was happy all the same.
Qianli Xun tore her eyes away in a hurry, seeing Prince Yi wear an expression of sheer bliss.
Remembering the look Prince Yi had given her outside Secluded Forest Villa, she felt a chill run down her spine. "What a fickle flirt Prince Yi is!" she thought. "Princess Jiahe is this wonderful and he's still not satisfied? He even has ideas about me? If you weren't a prince, hadn't helped me before, and I didn't need a favor from you right now, I'd call you out right in front of everybody for the total playboy you are!"
On second thought, if she really called him out, Prince Yi could deny the whole thing. After all, he had not said a word; it had only been in his eyes.
Seeing everyone fall quiet, An Mingxuan spoke up. "Youqi Jushi, there is one thing I don't quite understand. Might I ask you to clear it up for me?"
"Please go ahead." Qianli Xun thought An Mingxuan's question had come at the best possible moment. It kept anyone from noticing Prince Yi staring at her; above all, she worried Wenyuan might catch on and be hurt. If Wenyuan felt sad, she would not be happy either.

An Mingxuan said, "On the day Youqi Jushi and Brother Qiao went boating on the lake, the sky was clear and bright. Why, then, did your ci say 'vexing mist and teasing dew,' and since the plum rain season is long past, why write 'a fleeting shower of plum rain drizzle' [TN: Translator's rendering]?"
Why write that? How on earth would Qianli Xun know! Most likely, when the real Zhu Shuzhen went on a date with the man she loved, it had been the plum rain season, with dreary weather and a spell of plum rain drizzle. Qianli Xun had just recited Zhu Shuzhen's ci to voice her own feelings. How was she supposed to give Young Master An a plausible explanation?
Just as she was racking her brains, Wenyuan spoke up. "I wonder if you all remember: before Shuzhen was married, we once went on an outing to West Lake together right in the middle of the plum rain season, when the weather was miserable. I remember dark clouds piling up overhead, damp air, and the odd gust of cool wind. Then it started to rain, and none of us had brought umbrellas. The three of you dragged me into a pavilion for shelter, while Young Master Xu took Shuzhen by the hand and led her off the other way. Watching their retreating backs left me feeling so downcast..."
At the word "downcast," a look of genuine dejection crossed Qiao Wenxian's face.
Hearing Wenyuan bring up Young Master Xu, Qianli Xun worried it would upset him. Sure enough, sadness crept over his face, and she offered him gentle comfort. "Why dig up old history? I don't remember a thing about it."
Qiao Wenxian's smile returned in an instant. "I'm fine. If I had to guess, you recited 'vexing mist and teasing dew' and 'a fleeting shower of plum rain drizzle' to commemorate that day..."
"That's not it at all! When writing a ci, how could every detail be literal fact? In my view, as long as the feeling is sincere, everything else can come from the imagination. I wrote of the plum rain season just to use the vexing weather as a foil for the young woman's joy." Qianli Xun refused to go along with Wenyuan's guess; besides, Zhu Shuzhen's past had nothing to do with her. "Keep two things in mind, everyone. First, a ci lyric doesn't have to be all literal fact; imagined description is essential. Second, once I compose a ci, it no longer belongs to me. It belongs to those who read and savor it. There's no need to dissect my private life and feelings through it; instead, find your own feelings within it."
Hearing Shuzhen deny that the young couple in the ci were her and Young Master Xu, Qiao Wenxian felt immediate relief and looked at her. "You're right. During the Wei and Jin periods, Lu Ji wrote in his Rhapsody on Literature, 'Lyric poetry springs from feelings and is exquisitely ornate' [TN: Translated by David R. Knechtges]. Feeling is the very root of poetry and ci. All we need to do is savor the emotion and the beauty of scene and feeling blending into one. As for prying into a poet's private life through their verse, that is just a petty trick of literary criticism. Even if we catch glimpses of the poet's experiences, that is only what we ourselves perceive. It need not match what the poet saw or heard, because poetry and ci always contain some measure of imagined description."
Winning Wenyuan's agreement made Qianli Xun even happier.
Dongfang Chenze said, "I've learned something today. How narrow-minded of us to get hung up on the weather that day on the lake."
Shen Jianchen could tell Qianli Xun's words had been aimed straight at him. He might as well say something pleasant to coax her into a better mood. "What Youqi Jushi and Wenyuan just said has opened my eyes. From now on, I won't harp on whether Youqi Jushi writes about her personal life in her ci. Still, I have to scold everyone here: the last time you all analyzed Youqi Jushi's verse, you used the exact 'petty trick' Wenyuan just mentioned. Even Wenyuan himself did. Don't do that again."
Wenyuan and the three young masters cupped their hands in salute and answered in unison: "We shall heed Prince Yi's guidance."
Qianli Xun had no choice but to turn toward Prince Yi. "Thank you for understanding, Prince Yi."
Shen Jianchen crowed to himself: "How about that? Pretty clever of me, right? Got you facing me again, so you can't spend all day staring at Wenyuan."
Once the banquet ended, the servants went off to eat, and Shen Jianchen returned to the main courtyard alone.
Since it was the three young masters' first visit to Secluded Forest Villa, they wanted to stroll around and see the grounds. Qiao Wenxian went along as their host, and wherever Qiao Wenxian went, Qianli Xun was sure to follow.
With her brother gone, Qiao Mengrong had no desire to tag along on their walk. Besides, Youqi Jushi had eyes only for Young Master Qiao, so intruding on them would be awkward. With Xilan off eating, she went back to her own courtyard alone.
After strolling around Secluded Forest Villa with Wenyuan and the three young masters for a while, Qianli Xun remembered Xiaguo's trouble. Better to ask Prince Yi for help as soon as possible. "Wenyuan, stay with the three young masters. I'm going to pay a call on Prince Yi and ask him for a small favor."
"What favor?"
"Lin Jue has set his sights on my maid Xiaguo, but Xiaguo doesn't want any part of it. Yet my father gave his word in front of everyone..."
"If Lin Jue has taken a fancy to Xiaguo, why not let him have her? Entering the Lin family would be a decent outcome for her."
"The point is, I'm not having it. You don't know—Lin Jue only wants Xiaguo because her figure looks a bit like mine. Just thinking about it makes my stomach turn. Besides, Xiaguo said no. I can't force her against her will."
"All right, I'll follow your lead."
"Tell me, if I go ask Prince Yi, will he agree to help me?"
"Prince Yi isn't like other princes. He's kind and warmhearted, and he loves helping people out. Go ask him; he's bound to say yes. I need to stay with those three, so go ahead first. If it doesn't work, I'll pester Prince Yi myself until he agrees."
"Pester him?"
"Prince Yi and I are close. He can never hold out when I pester him. He'll say yes on the spot."
Qianli Xun could not imagine a refined scholar pestering a royal prince. She glanced at the three young masters walking ahead. "All right, I'm off."
Qiao Wenxian pointed in one direction. "Prince Yi stays in the courtyard over that way. Follow this path, and if you lose your way, just ask any servant passing by."
Qianli Xun nodded, parted ways with Wenyuan, and headed down the path he had indicated.
Secluded Forest Villa was not large, so finding it was easy. After asking just one maid for directions, Qianli Xun located Prince Yi's courtyard. It had to be the main courtyard; both the yard and the residence itself were far larger than where Wenyuan stayed.
The courtyard gate stood open. Qianli Xun stepped inside with care; not a single servant was in sight across the empty yard.
She muttered under her breath, "In the novels I write, a prince always has a whole crowd of people waiting on him. Doesn't reality work like that? Or does Prince Yi just hate being waited on?"
"What is Youqi Jushi muttering about under her breath?" Bored sitting indoors and eager to see Qianli Xun again, Shen Jianchen had decided to head out and find Wenyuan and the others. Reaching the doorway, he was delighted to spot her right there in the courtyard, yet he still put on his princely airs.
Qianli Xun stepped forward and bowed in greeting. "Greetings, Prince Yi."
"Bowing again?"
"You are a prince, and I am a commoner. A commoner must still observe proper etiquette before a prince."
"Why aren't you with Wenyuan, instead of sneaking into my courtyard?"
"Sneaking?" Qianli Xun's temper flared. How could Prince Yi have such a terrible way with words? Never mind, better keep her tone polite; after all, she needed a favor from him. "Hearing Prince Yi call it 'sneaking' makes Shuzhen uneasy. I came to call on you fair and square, in broad daylight."
"Don't tell me you remembered my question and hurried over to give me an answer?"
As for that question, Qianli Xun still had no memory of it. She had not paid much attention when Prince Yi asked it, and it had slipped her mind afterward. "Prince Yi's question requires careful thought before I can give you a proper answer. I came looking for you now because I have a favor to ask."
"A favor to ask?" A smile tugged at the corner of Shen Jianchen's mouth. When had Qianli Xun ever asked him for a favor? This was a true first, like a new bride climbing into her wedding sedan. He loved the feeling of her coming to trouble him. Walking up to her, he asked, "What is it?"
Qianli Xun looked up, and her eyes met Prince Yi's. A jolt of shock ran through her. What was going on? Why did an electric current just shoot straight into her heart? She dropped her gaze at once and cursed him in her mind: "Total playboy!"
Aloud, however, she said, "Prince Yi, you hold an exalted rank. Everyone in Lin'an listens to you; even my father does. This commoner has one small problem you can settle with a single word."
"Well, what is it?" That Qianli Xun had ducked away from his gaze meant she had caught on to his feelings, and Shen Jianchen felt rather pleased.
"After Lin Jue and I went through our mutual divorce, he had to go and set his eyes on my maid Xiaguo. Xiaguo refuses to go, and more important, I refuse too. But my father agreed to it in front of everyone, so he can't very well turn Lin Jue down for me, and Lin Jue keeps using it to stir up trouble..."
"So you want me to persuade Lin Jue to let Xiaguo go, solving Xiaguo's problem while saving your father's face?"
"Prince Yi is sharp as ever. That's right."
"Hmph! You sure know how to work every angle."
Afraid Prince Yi might refuse, Qianli Xun said, "If you won't agree, I'll drop to my knees and kowtow to you right now."
"It's your maid Xiaguo who ought to come kowtow to me. Since this is the first time you've asked me for anything, I agree."
Prince Yi's ready agreement caught Qianli Xun off guard, though she still did not skimp on etiquette. "Many thanks, Prince Yi!"
Shen Jianchen stepped forward and steadied Qianli Xun by the arms. "I'm glad you came to me for help."
His gentle tone, the touch of his hands steadying her, and that gaze she dared not meet sent Qianli Xun's thoughts spinning in flustered panic. "I'll leave Your Highness to your business. I have things to attend to."
She fled in a panic.
Watching Qianli Xun's retreating back bolt out of the courtyard, Shen Jianchen let out a chuckle. "Heh. Who would've thought you could get shy?"
Once out of the main courtyard, Qianli Xun checked that no one was around and let loose a bold tirade. "Don't think just because you're a prince you can flirt with me like that! If I didn't need a favor from you, I wouldn't care who you are. Get any crooked ideas about me, and one punch will split your skull wide open!"

Prince Yi really was both a scumbag and a third wheel, always spoiling her good times, not even sparing Qixi Festival! Still, since Wenyuan brought up the favors he was doing them, Qianli Xun could not object further and had to agree to let Prince Yi come along, treating it as a way to thank him.

"I doubt it. People have different tastes. He focuses on writing huaben stories, while I specialize in poetry and ci criticism. Our paths don't cross."
"Do you like reading huaben stories, Wenyuan?"

So Wenyuan had been shaped by Zhu Shuzhen's tastes all along; this supposed meeting of soulmates had just been Wenyuan giving everything from his side.
"Now, though, I think lowbrow works might one day win respect from the mainstream, just like ci poetry got little respect back in the Tang dynasty, yet through the efforts of Liu Sanbian, Dongpo Jushi, and others, it has now ascended to the halls of refined literature. From now on, I won't look down on huaben stories written by down-and-out scholars anymore."
"Since you've had a change of heart, I'll follow your lead, of course."
Neither wanted to part. Seeing her mistress show no sign of leaving, Kuqiao stepped in to remind her: "Miss, it's time to head back."
Qianli Xun had no choice but to bid Wenyuan farewell and climb into the carriage.
Holding the box in her arms, Kuqiao was about to board the carriage when she spotted Bai Feng walking up to the main gate, peering out toward her.
Kuqiao grumbled to herself: "I'm just about to leave and now you show up." Then she kicked herself for not waiting a bit longer to hurry her mistress, leaving herself no time to steal a few more glances at Bai Feng.
"Kuqiao, why aren't you getting in?" Qianli Xun poked her head out of the carriage window.
Kuqiao cast one more glance at Bai Feng and climbed aboard.
Qianli Xun smiled at Wenyuan once more. "See you on Qixi Festival."
"Next time I see you, I'll bring you huge good news." Qiao Wenxian and Bai Feng stood where they were, watching the carriage roll farther and farther into the distance.
#CH 94
#TITLE: Two Willow Leaves
Once Shuzhen's carriage had vanished from sight, Qiao Wenxian turned around and saw Bai Feng standing by the gate, gazing off into the distance. He thought nothing of it.
"What is Prince Yi doing?"
With Young Master Qiao asking, Bai Feng could not keep craning his neck. "His Highness is inside his room."
"Come with me to see Prince Yi."
Bai Feng stole one last glance down the road before following Qiao Wenxian back into the villa. When they entered the main courtyard's room, Prince Yi was sitting in a chair in a daze, breaking into a smile every now and then.
Qiao Wenxian stepped closer and waved a hand in front of Prince Yi's eyes, yet Prince Yi did not react at all.
Qiao Wenxian turned his head to ask Bai Feng, "What's wrong with Prince Yi?"
Bai Feng shook his head.
Shen Jianchen was lost in savoring the memory of Qianli Xun coming to ask him for a favor, above all the moment he steadied her and she bolted in a panic. Startled to notice someone standing right in front of him, he snapped back to his senses. "Oh, Wenyuan. What brings you here?"
Qiao Wenxian picked a chair and sat down. "What was Prince Yi thinking about so hard?"
"Nothing."
Facing Wenyuan, Shen Jianchen felt a rush of guilt rise in his chest. If Wenyuan ever found out they were in love with the same woman, what would he think?
Before long, Shen Jianchen came up with a neat excuse for himself: Wenyuan loved Zhu Shuzhen, while he loved Qianli Xun.
Yet thinking it through, the Zhu Shuzhen standing here today was Qianli Xun, and Qianli Xun was Zhu Shuzhen. There was no telling them apart, just as he could no longer tell whether Shen Jianchen was Zhao Pu, or Zhao Pu was Shen Jianchen.
Qiao Wenxian said, "I'd like to ask Your Highness to come back to Qiao Manor with me tomorrow."
"Back to Qiao Manor?" Shen Jianchen guessed at once that Wenyuan wanted him to persuade the Qiao family elders to send a marriage proposal to Zhu Manor.
In an earnest tone, Qiao Wenxian said, "I want to marry Shuzhen soon, and I'm hoping Prince Yi will help me convince my father."
Deep down, Shen Jianchen hated the idea, yet reality left him no choice but to agree. For one thing, he had already volunteered to help Wenyuan. For another, he and Qianli Xun had no chance anyway. Never mind His Majesty's marriage decree standing in the way, Qianli Xun's head-over-heels devotion to Wenyuan was enough to stop Shen Jianchen in his tracks.
Still, the thought of Qianli Xun becoming another man's wife was more than Shen Jianchen could bear. What was he supposed to do? He sat torn in two.
Qiao Wenxian looked worried. "Prince Yi isn't thinking of going back on his word, is he?"
Shen Jianchen wanted to take it back so bad, yet the thought of his engagement to Mengrong caught the words in his throat, and he forced them back down with an ache in his chest.
Bound by an engagement he could not break, how could he ever bring Qianli Xun happiness? Better to help her and Wenyuan be together. As long as she was happy, that would be enough.
"How could I go back on words I've already spoken? You have my word—I'll go back to Qiao Manor with you tomorrow."
Qiao Wenxian shot to his feet in excitement and threw his arms around Prince Yi. "I knew you were the best!"
Shen Jianchen could not muster a shred of cheer, heavy with sorrow that he had no right to take Qianli Xun away from Wenyuan.
"All right, all right, let go of me! Both you and Youqi Jushi come running to me for favors. Here I am, a dignified royal prince, reduced to your personal errand boy to be ordered around. It's beneath my station! I've made up my mind: from now on, no matter what favors you two ask of me, the answer is a flat no."
Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi with a laugh. "Don't worry. After this, I won't bother you again."
"You said it—don't you dare break your word!" Shen Jianchen forced a smile onto his face.
Qiao Wenxian studied Prince Yi's face. "You look out of sorts. What's going on?"
"Both you and Youqi Jushi keep pestering me for favors. It's driving me nuts. How could I be in a good mood?" Shen Jianchen stood up and pushed Wenyuan toward the door. "Quit asking questions, get back to your own room, and give me some peace and quiet."
Shoved out the door, Qiao Wenxian cupped his hands in salute. "My thanks, Prince Yi. On Qixi Festival, Shuzhen wants to treat you to a storytelling show. It ought to be one of Qianli Xun's huaben stories—you're sure to love it."
Another chance to see her filled Shen Jianchen with eager anticipation. "Tell her she's not allowed to back out."
"Shuzhen never breaks her word." A puzzled look crossed Qiao Wenxian's face. "There's one thing I don't understand, though. Why did you stop having Bai Feng search for Qianli Xun? Has Prince Yi found him already?"
Shen Jianchen had promised Qianli Xun to keep it a secret, so of course he would not tell Wenyuan. This secret was the only thing he and Qianli Xun shared. How could he share it with anyone else?
"I already told you to leave me alone! Why are you still jabbering?"
Prince Yi had already agreed to help, so there was no need to keep badgering him. If he wanted some quiet time, better not keep bothering him. Qiao Wenxian smiled, turned, and left the main courtyard.
After Wenyuan left, Shen Jianchen walked out into the courtyard and stood on the exact spot where Qianli Xun had stood when she asked for his help. As if her scent still lingered there, he closed his eyes and breathed it in for a quiet moment...
Once he had his fill, he opened his eyes and found Bai Feng standing beside him, looking as if he had something to say.
"You need something?"
"Your Highness, I... I like Kuqiao?"
"Youqi Jushi's personal maid?"
"Yes. Could Your Highness put in a marriage proposal for me?"
Shen Jianchen chided him: "Two people have already come begging favors of me today, and now you too?"
"I'm afraid Youqi Jushi might marry Kuqiao off to someone else." Bai Feng looked downright aggrieved.
Shen Jianchen reassured him. "It's still too soon to propose. Once the time is right, I'll handle it for you. And don't fret—Youqi Jushi is open-minded. She'd never marry off her own maid without asking what the girl wants. From what I've seen, that maid Kuqiao has feelings for you too, so she won't end up with anyone else."
Bai Feng pleaded, "You have to promise me, Your Highness. As soon as you and the Princess are married, you'll propose on my behalf."
At the mention of his marriage to Mengrong, gloom settled back over Shen Jianchen's face. He let out a sigh. "Ah, eight or nine things out of ten in life never go the way we want. Fine, you have my promise."
Bai Feng could not fathom what life's disappointments had to do with proposing marriage for him, though as long as His Highness agreed, he was thrilled. "Thank you, Your Highness!"
"Come with me to see the Divine Willow." Shen Jianchen wanted to sit in peace before the Divine Willow and think things through.
"Yes, Your Highness."
On the way to the Divine Willow, Bai Feng kept wondering why His Highness always loved sitting beneath it. He could not see anything special about that thick-trunked willow. Aside from a messy tangle of red silk ribbons hanging all over its branches, it looked plain as dirt. Even if it had stood for a thousand years, it was still just a tree.
Arriving beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen sat back down on the boulder and leaned against the trunk. The chill in the air felt much sharper than before; it was past the Beginning of Autumn, after all.
Autumn had only just arrived, so the Divine Willow's leaves showed no sign of yellowing yet. Still, he could picture how, before long, the whole canopy of green would wither, turn yellow, and fall away.
If only his love for Qianli Xun could wither and fall away like those leaves! Then he would not hurt like this; he could accept everything this new destiny had arranged for him with a willing heart.
Quite the opposite: the more out of reach Qianli Xun remained, the fiercer his love for her burned, and the harder it became to let go. On top of that, he had to swallow the pain and help Wenyuan win her. How many people could ever fathom what he was going through!
In silence, Shen Jianchen spoke to the Divine Willow in his mind: "Divine Willow, you helped me find Qianli Xun, yet she fell in love with Wenyuan, and I'm tied down by an engagement. Even so, I still love her, more than I ever have. What should I do? Should I try every means I have to make her love me and be with me, or should I let go right here and wish each other well? If it's the first, please drop one leaf. If it's the second, please drop two."
Shen Jianchen held out his open palm, waiting in hope for a leaf to drift into his hand...
Standing not far off, Bai Feng found it bizarre to see Prince Yi holding out his palm and muttered to himself, "Has His Highness lost his marbles? Why is he sticking his hand out? Does he expect the Divine Willow to drop gold ingots? The Divine Willow isn't a money tree, and His Highness isn't short on cash either. What on earth is going on?"
Bai Feng watched a while longer. Just as Prince Yi's arm grew sore from holding it out and he was about to pull it back, a single willow leaf drifted down and landed smack in the middle of his palm.
Prince Yi sprang to his feet in delight and shouted, "One! Just one! So you're on my side too, telling me to fight to be with her! Wonderful!"
Shen Jianchen pinched the willow leaf between his fingers and studied it from every angle, as if he had just found a priceless treasure.
Just then, Bai Feng saw another leaf drift down and land right on Prince Yi's shoulder. "Your Highness, another one fell—right on your shoulder."
Bai Feng's voice acted like a mighty broom, sweeping away the burst of joy that had just reached Shen Jianchen. Refusing to accept it, he snapped, "Nonsense! There was only one! What second leaf?!"
Bai Feng stepped over, plucked the second leaf off his master's shoulder, and held it out in front of him. "Here, look for yourself, Your Highness."
Staring at the second leaf he had prayed not to see, Shen Jianchen took it from Bai Feng's fingers with a heavy hand, a crushing weight sinking over his heart.
"Let go... let go... So you're telling me to let go..." Shen Jianchen murmured to himself, raw pain etched across his face.
Bai Feng froze in alarm. It was just one extra willow leaf; why did Prince Yi look on the verge of tears, with both eyes rimmed red?
"Your Highness, did I do something wrong?"
Shen Jianchen said nothing. He stumbled back along the path as the two willow leaves slipped from his fingers and drifted down beneath the tree. Walking on, he murmured to himself, "Fine. Since you tell me to let go, I'll let go. May we each find our own peace."
Bai Feng trailed after him, not daring to utter a single word.
#CH 95
#TITLE: Trying to Convince Qiao Shouren
Returning to Qiao Manor once more, Qiao Wenxian had Prince Yi at his side. His father, Qiao Shouren, received Prince Yi with full deference and even stepped up to the tea table himself to prepare whisked tea for the prince.
Shaken by those two willow leaves, Shen Jianchen had spent a restless night and felt low on energy, yet he had sorted everything out in his head. He had to accept the reality of those two falling willow leaves.
"Wenyuan, you must not have taken proper care of Prince Yi," Qiao Shouren scolded as he prepared the tea.
Qiao Wenxian hurried to protest. "What are you talking about, Father? I couldn't treat Prince Yi any better. I even prepare tea for him myself all the time."
"Then why does Prince Yi look a bit worn out?" Qiao Shouren pressed on.
Qiao Wenxian looked at Prince Yi. "I was puzzled myself this morning. You were fine yesterday; why do you look so haggard today?"
Shen Jianchen explained, "Don't blame Wenyuan, Grandfather. I just didn't rest well last night."
Qiao Wenxian asked, "Prince Yi always sleeps like a log. Why suffer insomnia last night of all nights?"
Shen Jianchen tossed off a quick answer: "I had official business on my mind."
Qiao Wenxian blinked in disbelief. "Since when has Prince Yi cared about official business?"
Qiao Shouren rebuked him again. "Wenyuan, is that any way to speak to Prince Yi? As Military Commissioner of Lin'an, Prince Yi outranks the Prefect of Lin'an. Of course he has plenty on his plate."
Qiao Wenxian smiled and kept quiet.
Shen Jianchen made no attempt to hide it. "Grandfather, Wenyuan is right. Before I got here, Lin'an's military and civil affairs were running without a hitch. Why should I stick my nose in the moment I arrive? So I stay out of all of it and let the officials carry on as they always have."
As he prepared the tea, Qiao Shouren said, "Staying hands-off has its merits, yet I fear His Majesty might be displeased. His Majesty must have had deep intentions in appointing Prince Yi as Military Commissioner of Lin'an."
Shen Jianchen laughed. "You're overthinking it, Grandfather. I only got the post because I flattered His Majesty well and beat Grand Commandant Gao at cuju, so His Majesty put me in charge as Military Commissioner of Lin'an in a fit of good humor. What deep intentions could there be? As for displeasing His Majesty, put your mind at ease. As long as Lin'an's officials are happy, they'll never let word of my real habits reach His Majesty. How could he be displeased?"
"Fair enough." Having finished preparing the tea, Qiao Shouren instructed a maid beside him to serve it to Prince Yi, then began making a bowl for Wenyuan.
"Let me do that, Father." Qiao Wenxian stepped up to the tea table. "How could your son trouble you to make tea for me? Go sit and chat with Prince Yi. He has something important to discuss with you today."
Qiao Shouren rose with a smile, yielding the tea table to Wenyuan.
Prince Yi sat in the seat of honor, so Qiao Shouren took the lower seat to the left as a matter of course. Seeing this, a maid set the steeped Jiuqu Hongmei before him.
Sipping the tea his grandfather had prepared with his own hands, Shen Jianchen mulled over how to broach the subject and convince him to propose marriage to the Zhu family.
Qiao Shouren asked, "How is the tea I made? Does it suit Prince Yi's taste?"
"Smooth and delicate on the palate, better than Wenyuan's."
"I'm glad Prince Yi enjoys it."
"Truth be told, I didn't miss sleep last night over official business. I just said that off the cuff." Shen Jianchen studied Qiao Shouren's face.
Qiao Shouren asked, "May I ask what kept Prince Yi from resting well?"
"I couldn't sleep last night because Wenyuan came asking for my help, and Youqi Jushi came asking for my help too. I tossed and turned all night trying to figure out how to help them both, which is why I look so worn out today."
Hearing the name "Youqi Jushi," Qiao Shouren guessed the Zhu family's daughter must have visited Secluded Forest Villa, and he feared her bond with Wenyuan was growing deeper by the day.
"Young folk don't know their manners; please forgive them, Prince Yi. Don't take their trifles to heart either. Those are all minor matters. Prince Yi ought to focus on Lin'an's grand affairs instead."
"You're mistaken, Grandfather. Isn't Wenyuan's marriage a grand affair?"
Qiao Shouren understood in an instant: Prince Yi had come today on behalf of Wenyuan's marriage arrangement.
"Wenyuan's marriage is only a big matter for the Qiao family. Compared with the grand affairs of Lin'an, it isn't worth a mention."
"I have to disagree, Grandfather. The Qiao family treats me as one of their own, so I'm a member of the Qiao family too. That makes Wenyuan's marriage a major affair to me as well."
Hearing Prince Yi call himself a member of the Qiao family pleased Qiao Shouren to no end. "Thank you for caring so much about Wenyuan, Prince Yi."
"No need to stand on ceremony with me, Grandfather." Shen Jianchen cut straight to the point: "You already know Wenyuan is in love with the Zhu family's daughter. Where do you stand on it?"
Qiao Shouren fell silent for a moment before answering. "To be blunt with Prince Yi, I'm not too thrilled with Miss Zhu. She has been through a mutual divorce, whereas Wenyuan has never been married. Wouldn't marrying her be a huge loss for him? Once word gets out, everyone will make a laughingstock of the Qiao family."
Panic hit Qiao Wenxian. He set down the tea whisk, losing all heart for making tea. "Father, why care about what the world thinks? What matters most is that Shuzhen and I love each other."
"What good is love?! When you reach my age, you'll realize all that youthful romance is utter nonsense! Be realistic, for heaven's sake." Forgetting that Prince Yi sat right there in his main hall, Qiao Shouren tore into his son with even harsher sternness.
His grandfather's attitude gave Shen Jianchen a flicker of secret satisfaction.
He felt sure he had split into two different people. One told him to let Qianli Xun go and help her find happiness with Wenyuan; the other refused, wanting to keep Qianli Xun all to himself. The instant he let his guard down, the resolve those two willow leaves had given him threatened to crumble to dust.
Wenyuan shot him a pleading glance. Shen Jianchen worked hard to shore up the walls of his reason, telling himself over and over in his heart: as long as Qianli Xun is happy, that's all that matters.
"To be honest with you, Grandfather, I played a hand in helping Miss Zhu obtain her mutual divorce, so I know the whole story from start to finish. The only one to blame is Zhu Xiyan for having awful judgment when picking a son-in-law. You must have heard what Miss Zhu went through after her wedding. Divorcing Lin Jue by mutual consent was her only way out; none of it was her fault."
"Even so, Lin'an is full of young ladies from fine families. Why must Wenyuan marry a divorced woman? If Wenyuan had an older or younger brother, I wouldn't care quite so much. But he's the sole heir of the Qiao line! The whole household has been busy choosing a bride for him, and if he ends up marrying a divorced woman, people will laugh us out of town."
"There you're wrong, Grandfather. You live your life for yourself, not as a show for outsiders. Living every day just to please outsiders—isn't that an exhausting way to live? Miss Zhu is unmatched in talent, virtue, character, and looks. If she marries into Qiao Manor, she will honor her in-laws above, support her husband and raise their children below, and keep the whole Qiao household in spotless order. A daughter-in-law this fine is hard to find even with a lantern."
Hearing Prince Yi sing Shuzhen's praises so high cheered Wenyuan right up. He picked up the tea whisk again to finish making the tea: two more pours of hot water, a round of brisk whisking, and it would be ready. Whisking away, he listened for his father's response.
"Miss Zhu is fine enough. Had she never been married before, she'd be the Qiao family's first choice without question. What a pity..."
The one hurdle Qiao Shouren still could not get past was the mutual divorce. Just then, Shen Jianchen remembered the story Qianli Xun had once told him about Liu E.
"Grandfather, have you heard of Empress Zhangxian Mingsu?"
"Of course I know of her."
"She went through a mutual divorce too, yet Emperor Zhenzong never looked down on her for it. He brought her into the palace, and she rose to become empress, the paragon of maternal virtue across the empire. During Emperor Renzong's reign, she ruled as regent from behind the curtain with an illustrious record of governance, laying a solid foundation for the golden age of Renzong's rule. If even an emperor didn't mind a woman who had divorced, why should Grandfather get hung up on it?"
With Prince Yi invoking Empress Zhangxian Mingsu, Qiao Shouren dared not make an issue of the divorce anymore; pressing it further would show disrespect to the imperial family. "Prince Yi makes a sound point. I can look past the mutual divorce. Still, I've heard Miss Zhu is... is a tigress, and that's the only reason Lin Jue agreed to the mutual divorce."
Having finished preparing the tea, Qiao Wenxian stayed seated by the tea table, admiring Prince Yi even more. Who would have thought to bring up Emperor Zhenzong's Empress Liu E and push his father back such a huge step in one stroke?
Yet hearing his father call Shuzhen a tigress rattled him again. He set down the tea bowl, ready to argue back, when he caught Prince Yi shooting him a warning glance to hold his tongue, so he kept quiet.
At his grandfather's mention of "tigress," Shen Jianchen chuckled to himself. "The old Zhu Shuzhen could only grit her teeth and take a beating, but since when would Qianli Xun ever let anyone lay a hand on her? Not beating Lin Jue up would be out of character for her. No wonder she earned a name as a tigress."
Shen Jianchen said, "Calling her a tigress goes too far. I've met Miss Zhu. She looks gentle and lovely, nothing like a tigress at all. If that rumor got around, I'd wager she just couldn't stand Lin Jue's domestic abuse anymore and fought back. Think about it, Grandfather: if Wenyuan married some fragile, timid girl, how could she ever stand on her own and help him manage an estate as big as the Qiao family's?"
Qiao Shouren still would not budge. "I heard she even beat up Young Master Chen out in public! She's an outright shrew! If she marries Wenyuan, will he ever know a day of peace?"
Having argued this far, Shen Jianchen knew that if he pushed just a little more, his grandfather was bound to give in, which meant handing Qianli Xun over to Wenyuan for good.
Shen Jianchen began to hesitate over whether to speak the words already formed in his mind...

#CH 91
#TITLE: An Apology from the Three Young Masters
The Dongfang family's dancers soon entered and began their dance. Everyone ate as they watched the performance, while the servants they had brought stood off to the side waiting on them; only after their masters finished would they get to enjoy the feast.
Today's spread held no appeal for Bai Feng at all. Standing beside Prince Yi, he kept his eyes fixed on Kuqiao.
Spotting Dongpo pork on the table, Shen Jianchen knew how much Bai Feng loved it. He did not even need to look to guess how long Bai Feng's drool must be by now. He picked up a piece with his chopsticks, placed it in a small porcelain bowl, lifted it, and held it out to the side.
When no one took it after a long moment, Shen Jianchen turned his head and saw that Bai Feng had paid no attention to the pork at all. Puzzled, he wondered why the guy had zero interest in Dongpo pork. Had he eaten too much of it and grown sick of it?
Taking a closer look, he saw a smile hanging on Bai Feng's face, his eyes locked straight in one direction.
Shen Jianchen pulled the small porcelain bowl back, set it on the table, and followed Bai Feng's gaze. So Bai Feng had someone he liked now.
A knowing smile curved Shen Jianchen's lips. "I like Qianli Xun, and you like her maid," he thought. "That's quite amusing."
Shen Jianchen had no interest in Kuqiao. Now that he was already looking in Qianli Xun's direction, he could not stop himself from looking at her.
Between watching the dance and enjoying the food, she had eyes only for Wenyuan, sharing cups of wine and trading glances. They looked so close. A smile Shen Jianchen had never seen before lit up Qianli Xun's face, and he could feel how happy she was in this moment.
"Brother," came Qiao Mengrong's voice.
Shen Jianchen looked over at Mengrong. She was raising her wine cup toward him with a smile.
He picked up his cup from the table, mimed clinking cups with Mengrong, and drank it down. He dared not keep watching Qianli Xun, afraid he might lose control, march right over, and demand of her: "Why did you have to fall in love with Wenyuan? Why didn't you wait for me?"
The three young masters had been watching Youqi Jushi's expression the whole time. Sure enough, seeing Brother Qiao had put her in high spirits, and the three exchanged a glance.
Once the dancers finished and withdrew, Dongfang Chenze filled his cup and raised it. "Youqi Jushi, besides paying our respects to Prince Yi and having a good time with Brother Qiao, the three of us came to Youlin Retreat today for another important matter."
Qianli Xun was puzzled. "What important matter? Why bring it up to me in particular?"
The other two young masters raised their cups as well.
Dongfang Chenze said, "The three of us owe Youqi Jushi an apology!"
All three spoke in unison: "We hope Youqi Jushi will be magnanimous and forgive us!"
With that, they drained their cups together.
Qianli Xun was even more baffled than before. "Why apologize to me? I don't recall you doing anything to offend me."
Dongfang Chenze set down his cup. "Does Youqi Jushi still remember the night we went boating on West Lake, when you got drunk and composed a new ci lyric at Wangjing Pavilion?"
Qianli Xun nodded. "Of course I remember. Is something wrong with it?"
"The three of us wrote down your new ci. We all knew the tune title, of course, but since it had no title of its own, we talked it over and came up with a fitting one..."
"What title?" Qianli Xun felt a pang of worry. Later generations already knew this ci lyric; could the three of them have given it some other title?
"'Summer Excursion on the Lake.'"
Qianli Xun broke into a gentle smile. History could not be altered on a whim; as if guided by fate, the three young masters had named it with those exact four characters, "Summer Excursion on the Lake." "It's a great title! You did a good thing, so why apologize?"
"Nothing delights the three of us more than spreading Youqi Jushi's poetry, so once we got our hands on a new ci, we couldn't wait to share it. Yet while people were appraising it, some took issue with the line 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed.' We fear that once this lyric spreads, it could harm Youqi Jushi's reputation. Afterward we regretted putting it out for everyone to critique, but once it was out, others already knew it, and there's no taking it back. If unfavorable comments ever reach your ears, Youqi Jushi, please don't hold it against us."
Qianli Xun still could not figure out why she had recited Zhu Shuzhen's "Qingping Yue: Summer Excursion on the Lake" when she got drunk that night. Thinking back on it, she must have been too happy that day, boating on West Lake and going on a date, so the lyric had just come to mind on its own.
Now that she had recited it, she was not afraid of people passing it around; there was no need to fret so much. Besides, a thousand years later, history still remembered this ci. Everyone had given it a fair appraisal, and nobody had condemned Zhu Shuzhen for it.
"I thought it was something serious! All I did was recite 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed.' If I dared say it aloud, I'm not afraid of people knowing. You were all right there at the time, so you saw with your own eyes I didn't do anything improper. As for sitting in Wenyuan's lap, what's the harm? You all know what Wenyuan and I are to each other. Relax. Even if people gossip, I'd never blame you. You spread my ci out of goodwill. People who read poetry are all over the map in taste, so of course you'll hear every sort of chatter. Why take the blame on yourselves? Besides, up to now, I haven't heard a single peep of criticism, haha..."
At last, a weight lifted from the three young masters.
Cao Ban said, "Hearing Youqi Jushi see it that way puts our minds at ease—now we can rest easy."
Qiao Wenxian turned to face Shuzhen. "I love 'Summer Excursion on the Lake,' and I'll remember it for the rest of my life. Anyone who slanders it slanders me, and I won't sit by and let that happen. As long as I'm here, you have nothing to worry about."
Qiao Wenxian's words nourished her heart like spring rain. Qianli Xun turned to look at Wenyuan; his gaze was so warm she felt ready to melt. If she had always had someone like Wenyuan by her side, watching over her at every turn, perhaps she would never have inherited Qianli Fist at all.
After her mother passed away, her father loved her too, yet all he ever told her was that a girl had to stand on her own two feet and hold up her own sky, because men could never be trusted. That mindset had guided her whole life. Not only had she taken up Qianli Fist of her own free will, she had worked hard to become a web novelist, pouring her whole heart into her career and refusing to touch romance or marriage.
In this moment, Qianli Xun felt what it was like to have someone beside her offering gentle comfort. It did not just warm her heart; it freed her from fearing any gossip that might come, because now she had someone in her life to face everything with her.
Just as Qianli Xun lost herself in Wenyuan's warm gaze, Prince Yi's voice cut in.
"Which brings me back to what I said before. From now on, Youqi Jushi should stop putting private affairs into poetry for the whole world to see. If you and Wenyuan are together, it's enough that the two of you know. Stop parading it in front of others, let alone writing it into verse. What a pity: you didn't even mention Wenyuan's name. Who's to know whether it was Wenyuan's arms you fell into, or some other man's? I'm afraid gossip is inevitable."
Shen Jianchen could not stand watching the two of them trade tender gazes, and a distinct edge of criticism crept into his tone.
Qianli Xun bristled at that, yet since the speaker was Prince Yi, she dared not talk back. More important, she needed a favor from him, so she had to swallow her annoyance. "Thank you for your guidance, Prince Yi..." She had half a mind to explain herself, then thought better of risking his displeasure and bit the words back.
Qianli Xun stopped looking at Wenyuan and stabbed at the dishes on her plate with her chopsticks, taking out her frustration as if she meant to crush the food to bits.
Shen Jianchen felt delighted. As long as she was not locking eyes with Wenyuan, he was happy. Smiling, he lifted his wine cup and took a slow, savoring sip.
Qiao Mengrong saw how displeased Youqi Jushi looked after Prince Yi's remarks. Worried she might hold resentment toward him, she spoke up. "I've read many of Youqi Jushi's poems and ci, and every one of them speaks straight to my heart. 'Summer Excursion on the Lake' is bound to be the same. From the line Young Master Dongfang just quoted, 'falling asleep in his arms, fully dressed,' anyone can tell Youqi Jushi is a woman of true feeling, living with candor and without pretense, free of the coy affectations of sheltered ladies of the inner chambers. You have both Mengrong's appreciation and admiration."
Princess Jiahe sure knew how to talk so much better than Prince Yi. Qianli Xun set down her chopsticks and offered a genuine smile. "Thank you for your appreciation, Princess Jiahe."
With that, she shot a glance at Prince Yi.
"She looked at me on her own at last." Shen Jianchen felt overjoyed; even if there was not a trace of affection in her gaze, he was happy all the same.
Qianli Xun tore her eyes away in a hurry, seeing Prince Yi wear an expression of sheer bliss.
Remembering the look Prince Yi had given her outside Youlin Retreat, she felt a chill run down her spine. "What a fickle flirt Prince Yi is!" she thought. "Princess Jiahe is this wonderful and he's still not satisfied? He even has ideas about me? If you weren't a prince, hadn't helped me before, and I didn't need a favor from you right now, I'd call you out right in front of everybody for the total playboy you are!"
On second thought, if she really called him out, Prince Yi could deny the whole thing. After all, he had not said a word; it had only been in his eyes.
Seeing everyone fall quiet, An Mingxuan spoke up. "Youqi Jushi, there is one thing I don't quite understand. Might I ask you to clear it up for me?"
"Please go ahead." Qianli Xun thought An Mingxuan's question had come at the best possible moment. It kept anyone from noticing Prince Yi staring at her; above all, she worried Wenyuan might catch on and be hurt. If Wenyuan felt sad, she would not be happy either.

Why write that? How on earth would Qianli Xun know! Most likely, when the real Zhu Shuzhen went on a date with the man she loved, it had been the plum rain season, with dreary weather and plum rain drizzle falling. Qianli Xun had just recited Zhu Shuzhen's ci to voice her own feelings. How was she supposed to give Young Master An a plausible explanation?
Just as she was racking her brains, Wenyuan spoke up. "I wonder if you all remember: before Shuzhen was married, we once went on an outing to West Lake together right in the middle of the plum rain season, when the weather was miserable. I remember dark clouds piling up overhead, damp air, and the odd gust of cool wind. Then it started to rain, and none of us had brought umbrellas. The three of you dragged me into a pavilion for shelter, while Young Master Xu took Shuzhen by the hand and led her off the other way. Watching their retreating backs left me feeling so downcast..."
At the word "downcast," a look of genuine dejection crossed Qiao Wenxian's face.
Hearing Wenyuan bring up Young Master Xu, Qianli Xun worried it would upset him. Sure enough, sadness crept over his face, and she offered him gentle comfort. "Why dig up old history? I don't remember a thing about it."
Qiao Wenxian's smile returned in an instant. "I'm fine. If I had to guess, you recited 'vexing mist and teasing dew' and 'a passing spell of plum rain drizzle' to commemorate that day..."
"That's not it at all! When writing a ci, how could every detail be literal fact? In my view, as long as the feeling is sincere, everything else can come from the imagination. I wrote of the plum rain season just to use the vexing weather as a foil for the young woman's joy." Qianli Xun refused to go along with Wenyuan's guess; besides, Zhu Shuzhen's past had nothing to do with her. "Keep two things in mind, everyone. First, a ci lyric doesn't have to be all literal fact; imagined description is essential. Second, once I compose a ci, it no longer belongs to me. It belongs to those who read and savor it. There's no need to dissect my private life and feelings through it; instead, find your own feelings within it."
Hearing Shuzhen deny that the young couple in the ci were her and Young Master Xu, Qiao Wenxian felt immediate relief and looked at her. "You're right. During the Wei and Jin periods, Lu Ji wrote in his Rhapsody on Literature, 'Lyric poetry springs from feelings and is exquisitely ornate' [TN: Translated by David R. Knechtges]. Feeling is the very root of poetry and ci. All we need to do is savor the emotion and the beauty of scene and feeling blending into one. As for prying into a poet's private life through their verse, that is just a petty trick of literary criticism. Even if we catch glimpses of the poet's experiences, that is only what we ourselves perceive. It need not match what the poet saw or heard, because poetry and ci always contain some measure of imagined description."
Winning Wenyuan's agreement made Qianli Xun even happier.
Dongfang Chenze said, "Thank you for enlightening us. How narrow-minded of us to fixate on the weather from that day on the lake."
Shen Jianchen could tell Qianli Xun's words had been aimed straight at him. He might as well say something pleasant to coax her into a better mood. "What Youqi Jushi and Wenyuan just said has opened my eyes. From now on, I won't harp on whether Youqi Jushi writes about her personal life in her ci. Still, I have to scold everyone here: the last time you all analyzed Youqi Jushi's verse, you used the exact 'petty trick' Wenyuan just mentioned. Even Wenyuan himself did. Don't do that again."
Wenyuan and the three young masters cupped their hands in salute and answered in unison: "We shall heed Prince Yi's guidance."
Qianli Xun had no choice but to turn toward Prince Yi. "Thank you for understanding, Prince Yi."
Shen Jianchen crowed to himself: "How about that? Pretty clever of me, right? Got you facing me again, so you can't spend all day staring at Wenyuan."
Once the banquet ended, the servants went off to eat, and Shen Jianchen returned to the main courtyard alone.
Since it was the three young masters' first visit to Youlin Retreat, they wanted to stroll around and see the grounds. Qiao Wenxian went along as their host, and wherever Qiao Wenxian went, Qianli Xun was sure to follow.
With Prince Yi gone, Qiao Mengrong had no desire to tag along on their walk. Besides, Youqi Jushi had eyes only for Young Master Qiao, so intruding on them would be awkward. With Xilan off eating, Mengrong returned to her own courtyard alone.
After strolling around Youlin Retreat with Wenyuan and the three young masters for a while, Qianli Xun remembered Xiaguo's trouble. Better to ask Prince Yi for help as soon as possible. "Wenyuan, stay with the three young masters. I'm going to pay a call on Prince Yi and ask him for a small favor."
"What favor?"
"Lin Jue has set his sights on my maid Xiaguo, but Xiaguo doesn't want any part of it. Yet my father gave his spoken word in front of everyone..."
"If Lin Jue has taken a fancy to Xiaguo, why not let him have her? Entering the Lin family would be a decent outcome for her."
"The point is, I'm not having it. You don't know—Lin Jue only wants Xiaguo because her figure looks a bit like mine. Just thinking about it makes my stomach turn. Besides, Xiaguo said no. I can't force her against her will."
"All right, I'll follow your lead."
"Tell me, if I go ask Prince Yi, will he agree to help me?"
"Prince Yi isn't like other princes. He's kind and warmhearted, and he loves helping people out. Go ask him; he's bound to say yes. I need to stay with those three, so go ahead first. If it doesn't work, I'll pester Prince Yi myself until he agrees."
"Pester him?"
"Prince Yi and I are close. He can never hold out when I pester him. He'll say yes on the spot."
Qianli Xun could not imagine a refined scholar pestering a royal prince. She glanced at the three young masters walking ahead. "All right, I'm off."
Qiao Wenxian pointed in one direction. "Prince Yi stays in the courtyard over that way. Follow this path, and if you lose your way, just ask any servant passing by."
Qianli Xun nodded, parted ways with Wenyuan, and headed down the path he had indicated.
Youlin Retreat was not large, so finding it was easy. After asking just one maid for directions, Qianli Xun located Prince Yi's courtyard. It had to be the main courtyard; both the courtyard and the buildings were far larger than where Wenyuan stayed.
The courtyard gate stood open. Qianli Xun stepped inside with care; not a single servant was in sight across the empty yard.
She muttered under her breath, "In the novels I write, a prince always has a whole crowd of people waiting on him. Doesn't reality work like that? Or does Prince Yi just hate being waited on?"
"What is Youqi Jushi muttering about under her breath?" Bored sitting indoors and eager to see Qianli Xun again, Shen Jianchen had decided to head out and find Wenyuan and the others. Reaching the doorway, he was delighted to spot her right there in the courtyard, yet he still put on his princely airs.
Qianli Xun stepped forward and bowed. "I pay my respects to Prince Yi."
"Bowing again?"
"You are a prince, and I am a commoner. A commoner must still observe proper etiquette before a prince."
"Why aren't you with Wenyuan, instead of sneaking into my courtyard?"
"Sneaking?" Qianli Xun's temper flared. How could Prince Yi have such a terrible way with words? Never mind, better keep her tone polite; after all, she needed a favor from him. "Hearing Prince Yi call it 'sneaking' makes Shuzhen uneasy. I came to call on you fair and square, in broad daylight."

As for that question, Qianli Xun still had no memory of it. She had not paid much attention when Prince Yi asked it, and it had slipped her mind afterward. "A favor to ask?" A smile tugged at the corner of Shen Jianchen's mouth. When had Qianli Xun ever asked him for a favor? This was a true first, like a new bride climbing into her wedding sedan. He loved the feeling of her coming to trouble him. Walking up to her, he asked, "What is it?"
Qianli Xun looked up, and her eyes met Prince Yi's. A jolt of shock ran through her. What was going on? Why did an electric current just shoot straight into her heart? She dropped her gaze at once and cursed him in her mind: "Total playboy!"

"Prince Yi is sharp as ever. That's right."

"It's your maid Xiaguo who ought to come kowtow to me. Since this is the first time you've asked me for anything, I agree."
Prince Yi's ready agreement caught Qianli Xun off guard, though she still did not skimp on etiquette. "Many thanks, Prince Yi!"
Shen Jianchen stepped forward to steady Qianli Xun by the arms. "I'm glad you came to me for help."
His gentle tone, the touch of his hands steadying her, and that gaze she dared not meet left Qianli Xun's heart racing in flustered confusion. "I'll leave Your Highness to your business. I have things to attend to."
She fled in a panic.
Watching Qianli Xun race out of the courtyard, Shen Jianchen let out a chuckle. "Heh. Who would've thought you could get shy?"
Once out of the main courtyard, Qianli Xun checked that no one was around and let loose a bold tirade. "Don't think just because you're a prince you can flirt with me like that! If I didn't need a favor from you, I wouldn't care who you are. Get any crooked ideas about me, and one punch will split your skull wide open!"

So Wenyuan had been shaped by Zhu Shuzhen's tastes all along; this supposed meeting of soulmates had just been Wenyuan giving everything from his side.

Qianli Xun had no choice but to bid Wenyuan farewell and climb into the carriage.
Holding the box in her arms, Kuqiao was about to board the carriage when she spotted Bai Feng walking up to the main gate, peering out toward her.
Kuqiao grumbled to herself: "I'm just about to leave and now you show up." Then she kicked herself for not waiting a bit longer to hurry her mistress, leaving no time to steal a few more glances at Bai Feng.

Kuqiao cast one more glance at Bai Feng and climbed aboard.

"Next time I see you, I'll bring you a huge piece of good news." Qiao Wenxian and Bai Feng stood where they were, watching the carriage roll farther and farther into the distance.

Never mind His Majesty's marriage decree standing in the way, Qianli Xun's head-over-heels devotion to Wenyuan was enough to stop Shen Jianchen in his tracks.
Still, the thought of Qianli Xun becoming another man's wife was more than Shen Jianchen could bear. What was he supposed to do? He sat torn in two.
Qiao Wenxian looked worried. "Prince Yi isn't thinking of going back on his word, is he?"
Shen Jianchen longed to take it back, yet the thought of his engagement to Mengrong caught the words in his throat, and he forced them back down with an ache in his chest.
Bound by an engagement he could not break, how could he ever bring Qianli Xun happiness? Better to help her and Wenyuan be together. As long as she was happy, that would be enough.
"How could I go back on words I've already spoken? You have my word—I'll go back to Qiao Manor with you tomorrow."
Qiao Wenxian shot to his feet in excitement and threw his arms around Prince Yi. "I knew you were the best!"
Shen Jianchen could not muster a shred of cheer, heavy with sorrow that he had no right to take Qianli Xun away from Wenyuan.
"All right, all right, let go of me! Both you and Youqi Jushi come running to me for favors. Here I am, a dignified royal prince, reduced to your personal errand boy to be ordered around. It's beneath my station! I've made up my mind: from now on, no matter what favors you two ask of me, the answer is a flat no."
Qiao Wenxian let go of Prince Yi with a laugh. "Don't worry. After this, I won't bother you again."
"You said it—don't you dare break your word!" Shen Jianchen forced a smile onto his face.
Qiao Wenxian studied Prince Yi's face. "You look out of sorts. What's going on?"
"Both you and Youqi Jushi keep pestering me for favors. It's driving me nuts. How could I be in a good mood?" Shen Jianchen stood up and pushed Wenyuan toward the door. "Quit asking questions, get back to your own room, and give me some peace and quiet."
Shoved out the door, Qiao Wenxian cupped his hands in salute. "My thanks, Prince Yi. On Qixi Festival, Shuzhen wants to treat you to a storytelling show. It ought to be one of Qianli Xun's huaben stories—you're sure to love it."
Another chance to see her filled Shen Jianchen with eager anticipation. "Tell her she's not allowed to back out."
"Shuzhen never breaks her word." A puzzled look crossed Qiao Wenxian's face. "There's one thing I don't understand, though. Why did you stop having Bai Feng search for Qianli Xun? Has Prince Yi found him already?"
Shen Jianchen had promised Qianli Xun to keep it a secret, so of course he would not tell Wenyuan. This secret was the only thing he and Qianli Xun shared. How could he share it with anyone else?
"I already told you to leave me alone! Why are you still jabbering?"
Prince Yi had already agreed to help, so there was no need to keep pestering him. If he wanted some peace and quiet, better to leave him be. Qiao Wenxian smiled, turned, and left the main courtyard.
After Wenyuan left, Shen Jianchen walked out into the courtyard and stood on the exact spot where Qianli Xun had stood when she asked for his help. As if her scent still lingered there, he closed his eyes and breathed it in for a quiet moment...
Once he had his fill, he opened his eyes and found Bai Feng standing beside him, looking as if he had something to say.
"You need something?"
"Your Highness, I... I like Kuqiao?"
"Youqi Jushi's personal maid?"
"Yes. Could Your Highness ask for her hand on my behalf?"
Shen Jianchen chided him: "Two people have already come begging favors of me today, and now you too?"
"I'm afraid Youqi Jushi might marry Kuqiao off to someone else." Bai Feng looked downright aggrieved.
Shen Jianchen reassured him. "It's still too soon to ask for her hand. Once the time is right, I'll handle it for you. And don't fret—Youqi Jushi is open-minded. She'd never marry off her own maid without asking what the girl wants. From what I've seen, that maid Kuqiao has feelings for you too, so she won't end up with anyone else."
Bai Feng pleaded, "You have to promise me, Your Highness. As soon as you and the Princess are married, you'll ask for her hand on my behalf."
At the mention of his marriage to Mengrong, gloom settled back over Shen Jianchen's face. He let out a sigh. "Ah, eight or nine things out of ten in life never go the way we want. Fine, you have my promise."
Bai Feng could not fathom what life's disappointments had to do with asking for Kuqiao's hand for him, though as long as His Highness agreed, he was thrilled. "Thank you, Your Highness!"
"Come with me to see the Divine Willow." Shen Jianchen wanted to sit in peace before the Divine Willow and think things through.
"Yes, Your Highness."
On the way to the Divine Willow, Bai Feng kept wondering why His Highness always loved sitting beneath it. He could not see anything special about that thick-trunked willow. Aside from a messy tangle of red silk ribbons hanging all over its branches, it looked plain as dirt. Even if it had stood for a thousand years, it was still just a tree.
Arriving beneath the Divine Willow, Shen Jianchen sat back down on the boulder and leaned against the trunk. The chill in the air felt much sharper than before; it was the Beginning of Autumn, after all.
Autumn had only just arrived, so the Divine Willow's leaves showed no sign of yellowing yet. Still, he could picture how, before long, the whole canopy of green would wither, turn yellow, and fall away.
If only his love for Qianli Xun could wither and fall away like those leaves! Then he would not hurt like this; he could accept everything this new destiny had arranged for him with a willing heart.
Quite the opposite: the more out of reach Qianli Xun remained, the fiercer his love for her burned, and the harder it became to let go. On top of that, he had to swallow the pain and help Wenyuan win her. How many people could ever fathom what he was going through!
In silence, Shen Jianchen spoke to the Divine Willow in his mind: "Divine Willow, you helped me find Qianli Xun, yet she fell in love with Wenyuan, and I'm tied down by an engagement. Even so, I still love her, more than I ever have. What should I do? Should I try every means I have to make her love me and be with me, or should I let go right here and wish each other well? If it's the first, please drop one leaf. If it's the second, please drop two."
Shen Jianchen held out his open palm, waiting in hope for a leaf to drift into his hand...
Standing not far off, Bai Feng found it bizarre to see Prince Yi holding out his palm and muttered to himself, "Has His Highness lost his mind? Why is he sticking his hand out? Does he expect the Divine Willow to drop gold ingots? The Divine Willow isn't a money tree, and His Highness isn't short on cash either. What on earth is going on?"
Bai Feng watched a while longer. Just as Prince Yi's arm grew sore from holding it out and he was about to pull it back, a single willow leaf drifted down and landed smack in the middle of his palm.
Prince Yi sprang to his feet in delight and shouted, "One! Just one! So you're on my side too, telling me to fight to be with her! Wonderful!"
Shen Jianchen pinched the willow leaf between his fingers and studied it from every angle, as if he had just found a priceless treasure.
Just then, Bai Feng saw another leaf drift down and land right on Prince Yi's shoulder. "Your Highness, another one fell—right on your shoulder."
Bai Feng's voice acted like a mighty broom, sweeping away the burst of joy that had just reached Shen Jianchen. Refusing to accept it, he snapped, "Nonsense! There was only one! What second leaf?!"
Bai Feng stepped over, plucked the second leaf off his master's shoulder, and held it out in front of him. "Here, look for yourself, Your Highness."
Staring at the second leaf he had prayed not to see, Shen Jianchen pinched it from Bai Feng's fingers in bitter reluctance, a crushing weight sinking over his chest.
"Let go... let go... So you're telling me to let go..." Shen Jianchen murmured to himself, raw pain etched across his face.
Bai Feng froze in alarm. It was just one extra willow leaf; why did Prince Yi look on the verge of tears, with both eyes rimmed red?
"Your Highness, did I do something wrong?"
Shen Jianchen said nothing. He stumbled back along the path as the two willow leaves slipped from his fingers and drifted down beneath the tree. Walking on, he murmured to himself, "Fine. Since you tell me to let go, I'll let go. May we each find our own peace."
Bai Feng trailed after him, not daring to utter a single word.

Returning to Qiao Manor once more, Qiao Wenxian had Prince Yi at his side. His father, Qiao Shouren, received Prince Yi with full deference and even stepped up to the tea table himself to prepare whisked tea for the prince.
