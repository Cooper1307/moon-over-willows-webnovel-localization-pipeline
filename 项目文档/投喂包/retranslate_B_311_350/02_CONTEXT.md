## 4. 上章结尾口吻参照

（来源：第350章末 6 段）

Right after their reunion lunch, Chen Haoyu walked straight through the doorway alongside Kuqiao, Matron Wei, and four-months-pregnant Xiangrong—just in time to overhear Qianli Xun declare she'd wait in the bamboo cottage for Prince Yi.

Clearing the room gracefully (sending Kuqiao off to Bai Feng and caring for pale Xiangrong), the family left the former couple alone.

Tears rolling down his cheeks, Haoyu pulled her briefly close before stepping back at her soft reminder she was no longer his wife—laughing bitterly through his tears at how "shamelessly" Prince Yi had knelt in Bianliang, sold his entire estate for ten million taels, and traded himself in Yanjing so Haoyu couldn't even hate him!

When Qianli Xun firmly declined trying to annul the old edict, Haoyu locked red-rimmed eyes onto hers for one final truth: *"In that Bianliang prison cell, when you kissed me... did you love me?"*

Unable to wound the man who had grown up for her sake—and remembering that honest moment of shelter—she whispered gently, *"In that moment... yes, I think I did."*

Smiling through his tears—*"Knew you loved me once..."*—Chen Haoyu walked quietly out the door, closing their chapter forever.
