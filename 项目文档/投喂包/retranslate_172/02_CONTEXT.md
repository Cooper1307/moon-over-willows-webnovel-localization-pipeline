"And she is not afraid of you either."

"What is that supposed to mean?"

"Think it over."

Yao Qingyan mulled a second, caught the dig, and burst out laughing. "Saying I stop you from disciplining our son? If you ask me, Miss Zhu is not afraid of you either! Scared of nobody—will she not turn our whole house upside down?"

Chen Jianglin clasped her hand. "Long as she turns Haoyu's world upside down, that is our good fortune."

Riding home hand in hand, husband and wife pictured the bright day someone finally whipped their son into shape and let them taste the joy of having a decent heir.
