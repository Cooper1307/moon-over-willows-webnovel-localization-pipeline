Leaving him behind, Shen Jianchen strode up to the gate. "Who are you? What business at Qiantang Gang?" the two guards barked.

Before the echoes faded, Bai Feng dropped both with two crisp punches. "Watch your mouths! Young Master Shen is here to see your boss!"

Strutting past the groaning guards behind them, Chen Haoyu crowed, "Heard that?! Young Master Shen! Going to wipe the floor with you lot!"
