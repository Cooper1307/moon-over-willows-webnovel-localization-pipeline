## 4. 上章结尾口吻参照

（来源：第300章末 6 段）

Catching his slip, Shen Jianchen eased back. “Relax—just talking, didn’t mean to rattle you. Past is past; forward is the only road.” Chatting neutrally a few minutes more, he saw Chen Haoyu off—then saddled a horse and rode straight to the Qiantang Gang alone, determined to show Master Jiang he’d bet on the exact right person.

Minutes after Chun Jinmo stepped out, a runner burst in reporting Prince Yi waiting alone on horseback at the gate. Overjoyed, Master Jiang didn’t even jam on shoes—he sprinted out barefoot and dropped to his knees. “Your Highness honors our humble gang!”

Eyeing his bare feet, Shen Jianchen teased, “No shoes—that how you welcome guests?”

“So thrilled to hear Your Highness arrived I forgot shoes existed—bare feet prove how eager I am to greet you!”

Slick talker for a rough customer. “You asked someone whose face I prize very much to bridge us—so here I am. Going to invite me inside?”

*Betting on the poetess was the smartest move of my life!* Master Jiang sprang up, ordered his men to tend the horse, and ushered Prince Yi inside.
