## 英文润色风格规则（用户明确要求）
- **禁用 -ly 副词修饰动词**（英文忌用副词堆砌弱动词）：优先换强动词或重组句式。例如 `finally came to an end→drew to a close`、`blooming defiantly→stood in full bloom, defying`、`rarely saw→a rare sight`、`suddenly burst→burst`、`would surely recover→was sure to recover`、`immediately bowed deeply→bowed low at once`、`finally understood→understood at last`、`bitterly cold→biting`、`barely containing→a joy she could not quite contain`。
- 允许的 -ly：程度/系表修饰（really, perfectly, truly, entirely）、话语标记（Miraculously）属正常，非"副词修饰动词"范畴。
- 逐章先做"重复表达排查 + 副词核查"再润色；专有名词（人名/地名/官衔）高频重复属忠实必要，不强行换代词以免歧义。
